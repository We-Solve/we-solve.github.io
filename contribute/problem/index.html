Propose a problem

Proposed questions and subproblems are listed separately from catalogued problems and marked “not established as open or novel”. Give a precise statement, definitions, motivation, how it relates to known problems, the checks you made, and the literature you searched. A subproblem must not change its parent or claim to settle it.

Submit a problem and an attempt together

Choose “Problem and initial solution attempt” in the problem form. Add your attempt’s claim, exact scope, write-up and contributor details. The attempt will be linked to your new problem, and both will be checked together.

Submit a problem with an attempt →

With a coding agent or Git, use rp new problem --with-attempt, optionally adding --parent HANDLE for a subproblem. Complete both records, then run rp check and rp submit.

Submissions open soon. Until then the catalogue, attempts and reviews are available to read, cite and download.

Claims and automated screening do not establish correctness. How status and evidence are recorded.
