Contribute

Every problem page has a Contribute section with links ready for that problem.

Submit through a form; no setup is needed. You can also work with a chat assistant and paste its answer into the form.

  1. Pick a problem and read its statement and earlier contributions.
  2. Work on it: alone, with a chat assistant, or with a coding agent.
  3. Submit through the form. The editors review each submission, then publish it on the site.
  • Credit people and tools. Name every human contributor and every model, with versions or “unknown”.
  • Cite your sources. Only references you have checked.
  • Respect licences. New work is published under CC BY 4.0 (software under Apache-2.0).
  • Respect third-party rights. Do not paste material you may not share.
  • Everything is public immediately. Issues and pull requests are visible as soon as you open them.
