Sharp Spectral-Cluster Density Bounds on Manifolds

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
Write $n=\dim M\geq2$ and take $\Delta_g$ nonnegative. The source exponents are $s(p)=\frac{n-1}{2}(\frac12-\frac1p)$ for $2\leq p\leq\frac{2(n+1)}{n-1}$ and $s(p)=n(\frac12-\frac1p)-\frac12$ for $\frac{2(n+1)}{n-1}\leq p\leq\infty$. The second exponent is determined by $2s(p)+(n-1)/\alpha(p)=n-1$, allowing $\alpha(\infty)=\infty$. The space $E_\lambda$ is the displayed frequency cluster. Intermediate rank means $r_\lambda\to\infty$ and $r_\lambda/\dim E_\lambda\to0$. For an orthonormal basis $(u_j)$ of $Q$, its basis-independent density is $\rho_Q(x)=\sum_j|u_j(x)|^2$. The constants in the lower bound are independent of $\lambda$.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Let $M$ be smooth, compact, and boundaryless, let $E_\lambda=\mathbf{1}_{\{x:\lambda^2\leq x<(\lambda+1)^2\}}(\Delta_g)L^2(M)$, and suppose $1\ll r_\lambda\ll\dim E_\lambda$. For $2\leq p\leq\infty$, do there exist subspaces $Q_\lambda\subset E_\lambda$ with $\dim Q_\lambda\sim r_\lambda$ such that $\lVert\rho_{Q_\lambda}\rVert_{L^{p/2}(M)}\geq c\lambda^{2s(p)}r_\lambda^{1/\alpha(p)}$ for some $c>0$ independent of $\lambda$, where $\rho_Q$ is the density of an orthonormal basis of $Q$?

The rank endpoint and a point-concentration construction.
Fix a nonzero cluster $E_\lambda$ of dimension $D$, with orthonormal basis $(\phi_j)_{j=1}^D$, and write \[ K(x,y)=\sum_{j=1}^D\phi_j(x)\overline{\phi_j(y)}. \] For any rank-$r$ subspace $Q\subset E_\lambda$, $1\leq r\leq D$, its density satisfies $\int_M\rho_Q=r$. At $p=2$ the source exponents give $s(2)=0$ and $\alpha(2)=1$, so this exactly equals the required lower bound. This endpoint is an identity, not new sharpness progress.

For any $x$ with $K(x,x)>0$, the vector \[ v_x(y)=K(y,x)/\sqrt{K(x,x)} \] has norm one and $|v_x(x)|^2=K(x,x)$. Extend it to an orthonormal $r$-tuple in $E_\lambda$ and let $Q_x$ be its span. The other vectors vanish at $x$: their inner product with $K(\cdot,x)$ is their evaluation at $x$, up to the chosen inner-product convention. Consequently \[ \rho_{Q_x}(x)=K(x,x),\qquad \max_{\substack{Q\subset E_\lambda\\\dim Q=r}}\rho_Q(x)=K(x,x). \] The upper bound follows by extending a basis of $Q$ to one of $E_\lambda$ and dropping nonnegative terms. Since $\int_M K(x,x)=D$, some point has $K(x,x)\geq D/\operatorname{vol}(M)$. We obtain $\|\rho_{Q_x}\|_\infty\geq D/\operatorname{vol}(M)$ for every allowed rank.

Comparison with the desired frequency scale.
At $p=\infty$, the requested scale is $\lambda^{n-1}$, independent of $r$, because $2s(\infty)=n-1$ and $1/\alpha(\infty)=0$. The preceding construction reaches that scale on any chosen sequence satisfying the additional estimate $D\geq c_0\lambda^{n-1}$. We have not proved that estimate for the source's frequency windows, and the intermediate-rank assumption alone does not imply it. The construction applies without it but gives only the stated bound in terms of $D$.
Why endpoints do not fill the gap.
Large value at one point does not bound the volume on which the density is large. For $2<p<\infty$, that volume and the arrangement of $r$ orthogonal modes determine the $L^{p/2}$ norm. Interpolation of upper bounds cannot supply a missing lower bound. A construction with quantified spatial concentration and the specified rank scaling is still required on general compact manifolds. The source-known two-sphere result is retained rather than replaced by these endpoint identities.
