The Toda lattice with random initial data

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let $n\ge2$ and let $J_0$ be a symmetric tridiagonal matrix with independent entries: diagonal $a_i\sim N(0,2)$ and positive off-diagonal $b_j\sim\chi_{n-j}$, where $\chi_r$ is the Euclidean length of $r$ independent standard real Gaussians. This fixes the GOE normalization with off-diagonal variance one before Householder tridiagonalization. Evolve $J(t)$ by $\dot J=[B,J]$ with $J(0)=J_0$, where $B_{i,i+1}=b_i$, $B_{i+1,i}=-b_i$, and all other entries are zero. Define \[\tau_\varepsilon=\inf\{t\ge0:\max_{1\le j<n}b_j(t)<\varepsilon\},\qquad \tau_\varepsilon^{\rm edge}=\inf\{t\ge0:b_{n-1}(t)<\varepsilon\}.\] For the standard unshifted QR iteration, use the normalization $J_r=Q_rR_r$ with $R_r$ upper triangular with positive diagonal (almost surely), and set $J_{r+1}=R_rQ_r$. Define the step count with the same off-diagonal tolerance by $m_\varepsilon=\inf\{r\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}:\max_{1\le j<n}|(J_r)_{j,j+1}|<\varepsilon\}$. At stopping, the diagonal entries approximate the eigenvalues to $O(\varepsilon)$; the sign and stopping rules specify the convention for this QR formulation.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given $n$ and $\varepsilon>0$, determine $\mathbb E\tau_\varepsilon$, the average time for the Toda particles to become free to this accuracy. Also determine the edge-deflation expectation $\mathbb E\tau_\varepsilon^{\rm edge}$, and $\mathbb E m_\varepsilon$, the corresponding average number of QR steps under the stated normalization and tolerance for the same tridiagonal Gaussian-ensemble data. [S2]

Short English statement

Determine the average diagonalization time of a Toda flow started from tridiagonal GOE data, including edge deflation and the related QR iteration question.

Exact two-particle Toda stopping time.
Take $n=2$. Put $x=(a_1-a_2)/2$, $b=b_1>0$. Direct multiplication of the stated commutator gives $\dot x=2b^2$, $\dot b=-2xb$. Hence $R=(x^2+b^2)^{1/2}$ is constant, and, with $u=\operatorname{artanh}(x(0)/R)$, \[x(t)=R\tanh(2Rt+u),\qquad b(t)=R\operatorname{sech}(2Rt+u).\] If $b(0)<\varepsilon$, the stopping time as defined is zero, even if $b$ later increases. If $b(0)>\varepsilon$, its first descent below the threshold has infimum \[\tau_\varepsilon=\frac{\operatorname{arcosh}(R/\varepsilon)-u}{2R}.\] The equality boundary has probability zero in this ensemble. Edge and full stopping coincide for two particles.
An integral for the expectation.
The initial $x$ is $N(0,1)$, and $b$ is an independent absolute standard normal. Their joint density on $b>0$ is $\pi^{-1}e^{-(x^2+b^2)/2}$. Reflection $x\mapsto-x$ cancels the term containing $u$ on $b>\varepsilon$. Using $x=r\cos\theta,b=r\sin\theta$, $0<\theta<\pi$, yields \[\mathbb E\tau_\varepsilon=\frac12\int_\varepsilon^\infty e^{-r^2/2}\operatorname{arcosh}(r/\varepsilon) \left(1-\frac2\pi\arcsin(\varepsilon/r)\right)\,dr.\] This integral is finite for every $\varepsilon>0$: the factor in parentheses lies in $[0,1]$, and $\operatorname{arcosh}(r/\varepsilon)\le\log(2r/\varepsilon)$. At the lower endpoint both angular width and inverse hyperbolic factor tend to zero.
Scope.
This is a reconstructible elementary $n=2$ calculation, not an asymptotic random-matrix result. For $n>2$ the individual gaps and norming constants interact, so the one-radius reduction no longer applies. The unshifted discrete QR question has a different stopping law and has not been reduced to this formula. Thus neither the all-$n$ expectations nor the bundled QR expectation is determined here.
