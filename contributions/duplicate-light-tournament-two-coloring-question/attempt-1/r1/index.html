Testing first-fit on the actual light class

This repeated question concerns the same primary definition: an arc $u\to v$ is light when $\{w:v\to w\to u\}$ induces an acyclic tournament. The attempted algorithm processes vertices in a fixed order and gives each vertex the first of two colors that leaves its color class acyclic, without recoloring. Consider vertices $0,1,2,3,4,5$, with the following fifteen arcs (exactly one for each pair): \[ 01,04,05,12,20,25,30,31,32,41,42,43,51,53,54, \] where $ij$ denotes $i\to j$.
Proposition: This tournament is light and two-colorable, but first-fit in the displayed vertex order fails at vertex five.
Proof: The complete list of directed triangles, recorded as unordered triples, is \[ 012,024,034,035,125,235,245. \] This list follows by checking the twenty triples: a triple is cyclic exactly when each vertex has one outgoing arc within it. From the arc list, the neighborhoods of $01,04,05,12,20,25,30,31,32,41,42,43,51,53,54$ respectively have the following topological orders: \[ (2),(3,2),(3),(0,5),(4,1),(4,3,1),(5,4),(),(5),(),(0,5),(0),(2),(2,0),(2). \] Every earlier listed vertex points to every later listed vertex in its neighborhood. Hence all neighborhoods are acyclic and the tournament is light. First-fit places $0,1$ in color zero. Triangle $012$ forces $2$ into color one. Vertex $3$ joins color zero, since $\{0,1,3\}$ is acyclic. Vertex $4$ cannot join it because of $034$, and joins $\{2\}$ in color one. Finally $5$ creates triangle $035$ in color zero and triangle $245$ in color one, so both choices fail. Nevertheless $\{0,2\}$ and $\{1,3,4,5\}$ are acyclic: neither contains any of the seven listed triangles. They give a valid two-coloring. ∎
Thus lightness plus a two-colorability promise does not justify irreversible first-fit. The example is not a hardness proof and does not exclude recoloring or a special ordering.

Verification and remaining gaps

The example was found by enumerating labeled tournaments on five and then six vertices; the search found none on five for this fixed first-fit failure condition and found the six-vertex mask 2105. Independent direct checks verified all arc neighborhoods, all twenty triples, the failed coloring sequence and the successful partition. No global two-color algorithm is obtained.
