Aubin–Talenti Optimizers for Critical Dirac Potentials

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

For fixed mass $m>0$, the massive Dirac operator $D_m$ has essential spectral gap $(-m,m)$. Take $d>1$ and $p\geq d$, and let $V\in L^p(\mathbb R^d)$ be nonnegative. Write $\lambda_D(V)$ for the lowest eigenvalue of the perturbed operator $D_m-V$ in the gap, in the admissible regime of the source, and $\Lambda_D(\alpha,p)=\inf_{\|V\|_p=\alpha}\lambda_D(V)$. The critical value $\alpha_*(p,d)$ is the endpoint where the optimized gap eigenvalue reaches $-m$ as an eigenvalue or resonance. The proposed Aubin--Talenti profile has positive scale constants $A,B$, and may be translated.

Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
In dimensions $d>1$ at the critical parameter $\alpha=\alpha_*(p,d)$, is the optimizing nonnegative potential for the Dirac spectral-gap problem of Aubin–Talenti form $V(x)=A/(1+B|x|^2)$?
Scope and source qualification.
The admissible exponent range and critical parameter are those of the cited spectral problem, not arbitrary choices of $p$ or potential norm.

Determine whether the critical optimizing Dirac potential in every dimension greater than one has the proposed Aubin--Talenti form.

Normalization and a necessary interior stationarity condition.
For the proposed profile $V(x)=A/(1+B|x|^2)$, $A,B>0$, change variables $y=\sqrt Bx$. Then \[ \|V\|_p^p=A^p B^{-d/2}C_{d,p},\qquad C_{d,p}=|S^{d-1}|\int_0^\infty\frac{r^{d-1}}{(1+r^2)^p}\,dr. \] The integral is finite exactly when $2p>d$, which includes the stated $p\geq d>1$. Fixing the norm therefore fixes $AB^{-d/(2p)}$, not the scale $B$ separately.
Variation calculation.
Suppose, only for this test, that an interior optimizer has a normalized simple isolated gap eigenvector $\psi$, that its eigenvalue is differentiable under compactly supported potential variations, and that the constraint admits a Lagrange multiplier $\eta$. Differentiating $(D_m-V)\psi=\lambda\psi$, pairing with $\psi$, and cancelling the eigenvector derivative gives \[ \delta\lambda=-\int|\psi|^2\delta V, \qquad |\psi|^2=\eta pV^{p-1} \] on regions of strictly positive $V$. Hence the proposed profile would require $|\psi(x)|^2$ proportional to $(1+B|x|^2)^{-(p-1)}$. For a nonzero normalized eigenvector this necessary tail is integrable only if $2(p-1)>d$.
