Definitions and notation.

In four dimensions a gravitational instanton is a complete noncompact Ricci-flat geometry in the source’s self-dual setting. N-body analytical methods organize asymptotic regions by clusters separating from one another and analyze operators or geometry in those regimes. Their application here requires compatible cluster decompositions and quantitative estimates on the moduli-space ends.

A quantitative cluster replacement.
Partition positive point charges into clusters $C_\alpha$. For each cluster choose its weighted center $c_\alpha=M_\alpha^{-1}\sum_{j\in C_\alpha}m_ja_j$, total mass $M_\alpha$, radius $R_\alpha=\max_{j\in C_\alpha}|a_j-c_\alpha|$, and second moment $Q_\alpha=\sum_{j\in C_\alpha}m_j|a_j-c_\alpha|^2$. At a point with $d_\alpha=|x-c_\alpha|>R_\alpha$, Taylor's theorem and the Coulomb Hessian norm $2/|y|^3$ give \[\left|\sum_{j\in C_\alpha}\frac{m_j}{|x-a_j|}-\frac{M_\alpha}{d_\alpha}\right|\le\frac{Q_\alpha}{(d_\alpha-R_\alpha)^3}. \] The linear term cancels by the center definition. Summing gives a uniform error for replacing all sufficiently distant clusters by their centers. If $d_\alpha\ge2R_\alpha$, the corresponding term is at most $8Q_\alpha d_\alpha^{-3}$.
A genuine multiscale limitation.
A point close to one cluster may be far from the others. The estimate then permits replacing only the distant clusters while retaining the nearby potential exactly. Replacing every cluster at once would fail near a cluster center, where the denominator vanishes and the individual poles remain resolved. This is the elementary cluster hierarchy needed before an N-body-style partition of the ends can work.
Gap.
The bound is for scalar Coulomb potentials, not the Laplacian or Hodge operator of a general toric hyper-Kahler quotient. To use it in cohomology one must construct compatible coordinate/gauge charts in overlapping cluster regions, prove metric derivative bounds, and control operator domains and thresholds there. None of those analytical steps follows from the multipole estimate. The source's methodological programme is therefore unresolved; a Gibbons--Hawking toy model is explicitly kept separate from the full quotient class.
