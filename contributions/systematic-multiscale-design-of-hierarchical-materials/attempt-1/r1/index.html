Systematic Multiscale Design of Hierarchical Materials

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A hierarchical material has structural features at several characteristic length scales $\ell_1<\cdots<\ell_m$, rather than a single microstructure scale. Effective properties, such as stiffness, strength, and flaw tolerance, are macroscopic responses derived from the constitutive and geometric behavior at finer scales. In the report, flaw tolerance means that a pre-existing crack does not propagate before the surrounding material approaches its limiting strength.

A scale-selection obstruction in an exact model.
Consider a one-dimensional elastic bar with positive modulus $E(x)$, length $L$, and imposed end displacement $u(L)-u(0)=eL$. Its energy is $\frac12\int_0^L E(x)|u'(x)|^2dx$. Assume $0<S=\int_0^LE(x)^{-1}dx<\infty$. Cauchy--Schwarz gives $(eL)^2\leq S\int E|u'|^2$, with equality precisely when $u'(x)=eL/(SE(x))$. Therefore \[ \min\tfrac12\int_0^L E|u'|^2=\tfrac12LE_{\rm eff}e^2, \qquad E_{\rm eff}^{-1}=L^{-1}\int_0^L E^{-1}. \] For two constituents in fractions $\theta,1-\theta$, the inverse modulus is $\theta/E_1+(1-\theta)/E_2$, independent of their ordering and absolute layer thicknesses. Applying the same identity inside each hierarchical block and then between blocks simply averages the leaf compliances with their total volume fractions.
Consequences for the proposed design route.
This gives a rigorous bottom-up bridge for the selected elastic response but also proves that stiffness optimization alone cannot select the hierarchy lengths: any rescaling of layers preserving fractions has exactly the same energy. Order the distinct constituent moduli as $0<E_1<E_2$. For a target $E_*\in[E_1,E_2]$, solving $\theta=(E_*^{-1}-E_2^{-1})/(E_1^{-1}-E_2^{-1})$ fixes a fraction, not a length scale. Targets outside that interval are impossible in this series-bar model.
