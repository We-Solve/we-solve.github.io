Furstenberg No-Bigeodesics (Planar FPP, Standard Minimum Moment)

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Planar iid nonatomic first-passage weights are claimed to admit no doubly infinite minimizing path when the minimum of four weights has a finite second moment.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Give the nearest-neighbor edges of $\mathbb Z^2$ independent nonnegative passage times with an atomless common law $G$, and let $T(x,y)$ be the minimal passage cost. Assume \[ \mathbb E[\min\{X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4\}^2]<\infty, \qquad X_i\text{ independent with law }G. \] The assertion is that almost surely no doubly infinite self-avoiding adjacent sequence $(v_i)_{i\in\mathbb Z}$ satisfies \[ \sum_{k=i}^{j-1}t_{\{v_k,v_{k+1}\}}=T(v_i,v_j) \quad\text{for all integers }i<j. \] Every finite segment would otherwise be geodesic.

Outline of the argument

Busemann labels reduce potential bigeodesics to compact sectors. Finite increases of passage times create marked paths with a definite extra cost; deterministic label bins and telescoping Busemann comparisons impose a finite budget that cannot support all the marks, including environment-selected exceptional directions.
