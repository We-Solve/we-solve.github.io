interacting particle systems and KPZ

Definitions and mathematical statement

Definitions and notation.
For a random growing interface with height $h(x,t)$, KPZ scaling means time, space and centered height scale in proportions $3:2:1$: $t\sim L^3$, $x\sim L^2$ and fluctuations $\sim L$. A multi-point scaling limit concerns joint distributions or a specified process topology, not just one marginal. Last-passage percolation maximizes sums of random weights over directed paths. ASEP is an exclusion process with asymmetric jump rates.

A scaling calculation with its singular limit exposed.
Formally write KPZ as $h_t=\nu h_{xx}+(\lambda/2)h_x^2+\sqrt D\,\xi$, with positive $\nu,D$ and space-time white noise $\xi$. The formula is used only at a smooth regularization level; the square requires renormalization in the rough equation. Set $H_L(x,t)=L^{-1}h(L^2x,L^3t)$. Chain-rule differentiation gives \[(H_L)_t=(\nu/L)(H_L)_{xx}+(\lambda/2)(H_L)_x^2+\sqrt D\,L^{-1/2}\xi_L, \] where $\xi_L(x,t)=L^{5/2}\xi(L^2x,L^3t)$ again has white-noise covariance. Indeed the spatial and temporal delta functions scale by $L^{-2}$ and $L^{-3}$, exactly canceled by $L^5$. Any deterministic centering term must be transformed along with this equation.
Why coefficient limits do not prove universality.
Both the viscosity and noise amplitudes vanish in these coordinates, whereas the nonlinear coefficient remains fixed. Dropping the vanishing terms before proving regularity and tightness would produce a deterministic first-order equation and erase the fluctuations sought by the problem. Their effect can persist through rough gradients; the scaling identity contains no uniform bound on those gradients. Thus dimensional scaling by itself does not identify a random limiting process.
