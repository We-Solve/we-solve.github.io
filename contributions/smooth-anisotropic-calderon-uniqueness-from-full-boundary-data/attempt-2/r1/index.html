Smooth anisotropic Calderón uniqueness from full boundary data

OpenAI's claim

Recovery of a metric and connection: Zero-frequency measurements with input and observation on one arbitrary open boundary patch are claimed to recover both a smooth metric and a unitary rank-two connection; recovery is up to a diffeomorphism and unitary gauge that fix the measured patch.

One-patch anisotropic uniqueness: The paper claims determination of a smooth Riemannian metric in dimension at least three from its zero-frequency energy form on any one open boundary patch; the only ambiguity is a diffeomorphism equal to the identity on that patch.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Fix $n\ge3$ and a smooth connected manifold $M$ of that dimension, assumed compact with $\partial M$ smooth and nonempty. Choose a relatively open $\Gamma\subset\partial M$ which is nonempty and proper. On the trivial Hermitian rank-two bundle, set $d_A=d+A$, $A\in C^\infty(T^*M\otimes\mathfrak u(2))$, and define the local Dirichlet energy form using solutions of $d_A^{*g}d_Au=0$ with boundary data supported in $\Gamma$. If two smooth metrics and connections give the same form for every $f,h\in C_c^\infty(\Gamma;\mathbb C^2)$, the claim supplies smooth $\Phi:M\to M$ and $U:M\to U(2)$ with \[\Phi|_\Gamma=\operatorname{id},\quad U|_\Gamma=I,\quad g_2=\Phi^*g_1,\quad A_2=U^{-1}(\Phi^*A_1)U+U^{-1}dU.\] Thus the ambiguity in recovering the two geometric unknowns consists of the displayed diffeomorphism and gauge, each trivial on the observation patch.
Mathematical claim 2.
Fix a dimension $n\ge3$. Assume the smooth $n$-manifold $M$ is compact and connected, with smooth nonempty boundary. For a proper relatively open observation patch $\Gamma\ne\varnothing$, the claim asserts \[\Lambda_{g_1,\Gamma}=\Lambda_{g_2,\Gamma}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g_2=\Phi^*g_1,\qquad\Phi|_\Gamma=\operatorname{id},\] for some diffeomorphism smooth through the boundary. Both metrics are smooth, and the local maps are the harmonic Dirichlet energy forms for data supported in $\Gamma$. Equality for all full-boundary data gives a diffeomorphism fixing the entire boundary. This concerns smooth anisotropic metrics, not arbitrary bounded measurable scalar conductivities.

Outline of the argument

Recovery of a metric and connection: Boundary factorization identifies normal-gauge jets, and a common exterior cap starts equality of Green matrices. Source solutions provide separating points, harmonic jets, and bundle frames. Mixed-kernel continuation in the product, followed by moving-sphere transfer and angular coercivity, extends the maximal local metric-and-bundle identification throughout the manifold.

One-patch anisotropic uniqueness: Boundary jets and a common exterior cap identify actual Green kernels in an initial region. Remote source solutions produce harmonic coordinates and enough second-order jets. A mixed Green-kernel continuation theorem extends metric equality across a smooth frontier, so the maximal paired coordinate region cannot stop in the interior.
