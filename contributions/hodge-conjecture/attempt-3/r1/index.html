Hodge Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

Manuscript 1 (family 032, 2026-09-10): A nonzero algebraic map from generic primitive K3 cohomology to an abelian target is claimed to recover the exact Kuga--Satake correspondence; existence of that starting map remains an assumption; Manuscript 2 (family 032, 2026-09-30): For tensor-Hodge-generic abelian-cover Jacobians and the specified very general diagonal complete intersections, OpenAI claims the rational Hodge assertion on every self-power; Manuscript 3 (family 032, 2026-09-30): On the specified quadratic-lattice locus of projective K3 surfaces, all rational Hodge classes on every power are claimed to be algebraic; an exact algebraic Kuga--Satake tensor also gives a conditional criterion beyond that locus. Manuscript 4 (family 032, 2026-10-03): For every projective complex K3 surface, the precisely normalized Kuga--Satake embedding into the full even-Clifford abelian target is claimed to come from an algebraic cycle; Manuscript 5 (family 032, 2026-09-18): Every rational Weil class of a split eight-dimensional abelian variety is claimed to have an algebraic representative, uniformly over imaginary-quadratic fields and compatible polarizations; Manuscript 6 (family 032, 2026-09-30): The manuscripts claim algebraicity of rational Hodge classes on CM abelian varieties, with consequences for generalized Hodge classes and finite-field Tate classes. Manuscript 7 (family 032, 2026-10-04): Rational Hodge classes on any finite product of projective complex K3 surfaces are claimed to be algebraic, even when the factors have unrelated periods or extra endomorphisms; Manuscript 8 (family 032, 2026-09-30): Algebraicity is claimed for Hodge classes on powers of split Weil sixfolds and of abelian varieties of dimension at most five carrying an imaginary-quadratic action, including special periods; Manuscript 9 (family 040, 2026-09-24): For every smooth complex projective surface with $p_g=0$, OpenAI claims that the Albanese map on integral degree-zero cycles is an isomorphism.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $(S,h)$ be a primitively amply polarized projective complex K3 surface, put $V=h^\perp\subset H^2(S,\mathbb Q)$ with cup-product form $q$, and assume $\operatorname{Hg}(V)=\operatorname{SO}(V,q)$. This hypothesis implies $T(S)=\operatorname{NS}(S)_{\mathbb Q}^{\perp}=V$. For any stipulated rational quadratic polarization and full standard even-Clifford Kuga--Satake construction, including parity, weight-one polarization and isogenous realization, write $A_S$ for its abelian variety and $\kappa_S:V\to H^2(A_S^2,\mathbb Q)$ for the prescribed map. OpenAI asserts equivalence of these three existence statements: (i) some abelian variety $B$ admits a rational codimension-two cycle on $S\times B$ inducing a nonzero map from $V$ to $H^2(B,\mathbb Q)$; (ii) a smooth connected projective surface $Y$ admits a dominant generically finite map $f:Y\to S$ with \[ f_*\operatorname{im}\left(\bigwedge^2 H^0(Y,\Omega_Y^1)\longrightarrow H^0(Y,\Omega_Y^2)\right)\ne0; \] (iii) some $\Gamma\in\operatorname{CH}^2(S\times A_S^2)_{\mathbb Q}$ satisfies $\Gamma_*|_V=\kappa_S$. Thus algebraicity is conditional on either equivalent geometric input; this theorem does not assert that every K3 surface has that input.
Manuscript 2.
Fix $n\ge2$, $d\ge0$, $c\in\{0,1,2\}$ and $m=d+c+1$. For a rank-$c$ complex matrix $A=(a_{\alpha i})$ with every maximal coordinate minor nonzero, define the smooth diagonal complete intersection \[ X_A=\{[x_1:\cdots:x_m]\in\mathbb P^{m-1}:\ \sum_i a_{\alpha i}x_i^n=0\quad(1\le\alpha\le c)\}. \] OpenAI asserts surjectivity of $\operatorname{CH}^p(X_A^u)_{\mathbb Q}\to H^{2p}(X_A^u,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X_A^u)$ for every $u\ge1$ and every $p$, outside a union, indexed by a countable set, of proper algebraic closed subsets of this smooth parameter space. The exceptional set is unnecessary when $c\le1$ or $d=0$. A second assertion concerns a fixed connected finite abelian topological cover of a compact oriented surface with specified branch monodromy, allowing unbranched and trivial covers. Vary the full marked complex structure and branch points. At any parameter $s$ for which each rational tensor of type $(a,a)$ in $H^1(C_s,\mathbb Q)^{\otimes 2a}$ keeps its Hodge type at every point of the marked family, the same cycle-class surjectivity holds on \[ (\operatorname{Jac}(C_s)\times M)^N\quad(N\ge1),\qquad C_s^a\times M^b\quad(a,b\ge0), \] for every complex CM abelian variety $M$. CM means that its degree-one Hodge group is a torus. This is a Hodge-generic assertion, with the generic locus chosen independently of $M$.
Manuscript 3.
For a projective complex K3 surface $S$, write $T_S=\operatorname{NS}(S)_{\mathbb Q}^{\perp}$ with cup-product quadratic form $q$. Assume a rational isometric embedding \[ (T_S,q)\hookrightarrow\mathbb U_{\mathbb Q}^{\oplus2}\perp\langle-1\rangle^4, \] where $\mathbb U$ is the hyperbolic plane with Gram matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Choose any full even-Clifford realization $H^1(A,\mathbb Q)=C^+(T_S,q)$, any $w\in T_S$ with $q(w)\ne0$, and any rational weight-one polarization. The Kuga--Satake map is formed from $v\mapsto(a\mapsto vaw)$ and this polarization. OpenAI asserts existence of $\Gamma\in\operatorname{CH}^2(S\times A^2)_{\mathbb Q}$ inducing this exact prescribed map on $T_S$, also after transporting it to an isogenous realization. It further claims rational Hodge and generalized Hodge conjectures in all degrees of $S^m$ for every $m\ge1$. The quadratic embedding is a hypothesis, so this theorem alone is restricted to that locus.
Manuscript 4.
Let $S$ be any smooth projective complex K3 surface and let $T(S)=\operatorname{NS}(S)_{\mathbb Q}^{\perp}$ in $H^2(S,\mathbb Q)$. Fix the full even-Clifford Kuga--Satake construction, including the rational quadratic polarization, parity identification and rational weight-one polarization, with associated abelian variety $A_S$. These choices specify $\kappa_S:T(S)\to H^2(A_S^2,\mathbb Q)$. OpenAI claims \[ \exists\Gamma_S\in\operatorname{CH}^2(S\times A_S^2)_{\mathbb Q}\quad (p_{A_S^2})_*\left(\operatorname{cl}(\Gamma_S)\smile p_S^*x\right)=\kappa_S(x) \quad\text{for all }x\in T(S). \] Here $\operatorname{CH}^2$ is the group of rational codimension-two cycles modulo rational equivalence and $p_S,p_{A_S^2}$ are the projections. The assertion includes every isogenous realization with the prescribed map transported by its rational Hodge isomorphism.
Manuscript 5.
Let $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$ be imaginary quadratic, $A$ a complex abelian variety of dimension eight with a $K$-action, and $\lambda$ a compatible polarization of Weil type, with eigenspace multiplicities $(4,4)$. Put $V=H_1(A,\mathbb Q)$, let $\psi$ be the rational Riemann form and $J$ multiplication by $\sqrt{-d}$. Assume the associated Hermitian form \[ h(x,y)=\psi(Jx,y)+\sqrt{-d}\,\psi(x,y) \] vanishes on a four-dimensional $K$-subspace of $V$ (the split condition). Define the rational Weil space $W_K(A)=\bigwedge_K^8H^1(A,\mathbb Q)\subset H^8(A,\mathbb Q)$ using the two conjugate $K$-eigenspace exterior powers. OpenAI claims \[ W_K(A)\subset\operatorname{im}\left(\operatorname{CH}^4(A)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{cl}}H^8(A,\mathbb Q)\right). \] The field and compatible polarization type are arbitrary; extra endomorphisms are allowed. The statement concerns these Weil classes, rather than all Hodge classes on all varieties.
Manuscript 6.
A complex abelian variety $A$ is CM if $\operatorname{End}(A)\otimes\mathbb Q$ contains a commutative semisimple algebra of dimension $2\dim A$. For every such $A$ and every $p\ge0$, OpenAI claims \[ \operatorname{CH}^p(A)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow H^{2p}(A,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A) \quad\text{is surjective}. \] This also applies to arbitrary finite products of CM abelian varieties, including their powers. If $W\subset H^k(A,\mathbb Q)$ is a rational Hodge substructure and $W(r)$ is effective (all Hodge bidegrees are nonnegative), it claims a closed algebraic $Z\subset A$ of codimension at least $r$ with $W\subset\ker[H^k(A,\mathbb Q)\to H^k(A\setminus Z,\mathbb Q)]$. The manuscript additionally asserts that, for every abelian variety $A/\mathbb F_q$, every $r\ge0$ and prime $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q$, \[ \operatorname{CH}^r(A)\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell\longrightarrow H^{2r}_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(A_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell(r))^{\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb F}_q/\mathbb F_q)} \quad\text{is surjective}. \] Numerical and $\ell$-adic homological equivalence are claimed to agree on rational cycles. Over any algebraically closed field, for an abelian $g$-fold and rational ample class $h$, the form $(-1)^r\deg(xyh^{g-2r})$ is claimed positive definite on the real extension of $\ker[h^{g-2r+1}:A^r_{\mathrm{num}}\to A^{g-r+1}_{\mathrm{num}}]$ for $0\le r\le g/2$.
Manuscript 7.
Choose any integer $m\ge1$ and any projective complex K3 surfaces $S_1,\ldots,S_m$, allowing repeated factors. For $X=\prod_{j=1}^m S_j$, OpenAI claims \[ \operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\operatorname{cl}} H^{2p}(X,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X) \quad\text{is surjective for every }0\le p\le2m. \] There are no Picard-number, period or endomorphism-field restrictions. The quantified varieties are products of K3 surfaces, so this statement has smaller scope than the Hodge conjecture for arbitrary smooth projective complex varieties.
Manuscript 8.
Let $E=\mathbb Q(u)$ be imaginary quadratic with $u^2=-d$, $d>0$, and let $A$ be a complex abelian variety with a unital $E$-action. OpenAI claims rational cycle-class surjectivity \[ \operatorname{CH}^p(A^m)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow H^{2p}(A^m,\mathbb Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A^m) \quad(m\ge1,\ p\ge0) \] in either of these two cases: $\dim A\le5$; or $\dim A=6$ and the rational Hermitian form associated to a compatible polarization is hyperbolic with signature $(3,3)$. In the second case, with rational Riemann form $e$, this means $h_E(x,y)=e(x,uy)+u e(x,y)$ on $H_1(A,\mathbb Q)$ has a totally isotropic three-dimensional $E$-subspace. Compatibility means $e(ax,y)=e(x,\bar a y)$ for all $a\in E$.
Manuscript 9.
For a smooth connected projective complex surface $S$ put $p_g=h^0(S,\omega_S)$ and $q=h^1(S,\mathcal O_S)$. OpenAI asserts the integral statements \[ p_g=q=0\ \Longrightarrow\ \deg:\operatorname{CH}_0(S)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb Z, \qquad p_g=0\ \Longrightarrow\ \operatorname{alb}_S:\operatorname{CH}_0(S)^0\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Alb}(S)(\mathbb C), \] where $\operatorname{CH}_0(S)^0$ is the subgroup of degree-zero zero-cycles modulo rational equivalence. Under $p_g=0$, it also claims that the rational Chow motive is finite-dimensional in Kimura's sense and has zero transcendental degree-two summand. When $p_g=q=0$ it claims \[ h(S)_{\mathbb Q}\simeq\mathbf1\oplus\mathbb L^{\oplus b_2(S)}\oplus\mathbb L^2, \] where $\mathbb L$ is the Lefschetz motive. Under the joint hypothesis $p_g=q=0$, for each power $Y=S^m$ and each Hilbert scheme $Y=S^{[n]}$, the corresponding rational motive is pure Tate and $\operatorname{CH}^i(Y)_{\mathbb Q}\xrightarrow{\sim}H^{2i}(Y,\mathbb Q)$, with $H^{2i+1}(Y,\mathbb Q)=0$, for every $i\ge0$.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 032, 2026-09-10): Acting independently on each weight-one factor reconstructs the Clifford multiplicities, and rational descent treats nonsplit algebras. Hilbert parameter spaces carry the specified flat cycle class to special periods, while signed square-root comparisons keep the polarization normalization fixed. Manuscript 2 (family 032, 2026-09-30): Deck-character projections and equivariant period calculations identify the necessary monodromy invariants of curve covers. Correspondences dominated by CM sources supply determinant tensors, while Gale duality transfers the character-level construction to diagonal complete intersections with the required supports and degree shifts. Manuscript 3 (family 032, 2026-09-30): Clifford multiplication carries exterior powers of the transcendental space into degree-two cohomology of an abelian target. Transposed cycles and a polynomial inverse on the finite-dimensional image recover these summands, so divisor algebraicity supplies the required classes without an inverse Lefschetz correspondence.

Manuscript 4 (family 032, 2026-10-03): The construction engineers a Lagrangian whose second cohomology has a nineteen-dimensional deficit, yielding a corresponding bound on self-extensions after mirror realization. The resulting characteristic class is transported through K3 moduli by semiregularity and specialization, retaining the prescribed Clifford tensor. Manuscript 5 (family 032, 2026-09-18): An embedded spin Lagrangian detects the exceptional middle-cohomology component and makes two homological obstruction maps injective. Finite-cutoff weighted Floer operations are assembled into algebraic module identities; cancellation in alternating specialization then transports the desired Chern character to an algebraic cycle. Manuscript 6 (family 032, 2026-09-30): The argument realizes four-factor balanced tensors using unitary theta constructions. Elementary switches between equal-sum lists, followed by algebraic polarization contractions and descent of coefficients, build arbitrary balanced CM tensors from these elementary cycle classes.

Manuscript 7 (family 032, 2026-10-04): A Lagrangian immersion is used to produce prescribed Euler pairings while bounding the second self-extension space of the associated complex. Clifford-algebra calculations then identify its transcendental cohomology component; algebraic Kuga--Satake tensors and their contractions supply the classes needed on products. Manuscript 8 (family 032, 2026-09-30): A coherent seed and its Fourier--Mukai transform produce the split-sixfold Weil plane through an obstruction calculation and deformation. A determinant description of the actual Hodge group, combined with the CM result, extends these algebraic generators to powers and to the stated low-dimensional cases. Manuscript 9 (family 040, 2026-09-24): A determinant-constrained Quot obstruction theory and torus localization produce a Chow relation involving the diagonal. A nested-Hilbert recursion leaves two surface factors unintegrated, retaining an actual correspondence identity; its external-product terms annihilate degree-zero cycles, and the diagonal coefficient gives the required vanishing after the standard reductions.
