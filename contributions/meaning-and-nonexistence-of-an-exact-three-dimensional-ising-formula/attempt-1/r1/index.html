Meaning and Nonexistence of an Exact Three-Dimensional Ising Formula

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
For Ising spins $\sigma_x\in\{-1,1\}$ on a lattice, a standard finite-volume Hamiltonian is $H_\Lambda=-J\sum_{\langle x,y\rangle}\sigma_x\sigma_y$ and the partition function is $Z_\Lambda(\beta)=\sum_\sigma e^{-\beta H_\Lambda(\sigma)}$. A thermodynamic free energy is obtained from the volume-normalized logarithm of $Z_\Lambda$. Onsager’s solution motivates the comparison in two dimensions. The admissible class of an "exact formula" is deliberately not defined in the source: providing that definition is part of the problem.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Give a mathematically precise meaning to an exact formula comparable to Onsager's formula for the two-dimensional Ising model, and prove or disprove that no such formula exists for the three-dimensional Ising model.
Scope and source qualification.
The source requires both a definition and a proof or disproof. Choosing an arbitrarily narrow class of expressions does not settle the intended programme; no universal meaning of exact formula is imposed here.

Short English statement

Define what an exact three-dimensional Ising solution would mean, then establish or refute an impossibility theorem.

Testing a computability interpretation of exactness.
For a cubic box of side $L$, let $p_L(\beta)=L^{-3}\log Z_L(\beta)$ with free boundary and nearest-neighbour coupling $J$. If a large cube of side $mL$ is split into $m^3$ boxes, removing the bonds between boxes changes every Hamiltonian by at most $|J|B$, where $B=3(m-1)m^2L^2$. Hence \[e^{-\beta|J|B}Z_L^{m^3}\le Z_{mL}\le e^{\beta|J|B}Z_L^{m^3},\quad |p_{mL}-p_L|\le 3\beta|J|/L\] for $\beta\ge0$. Along compatible box sizes this is a quantitative boundary-error estimate. For two sizes $L,K$, compare both with a common multiple and let that multiple grow; their pressures differ by at most $3\beta|J|(L^{-1}+K^{-1})$. Thus the pressure sequence is Cauchy and its limit $p$ satisfies $|p-p_L|\le3\beta|J|/L$. Periodic boundaries differ by at most $3\beta|J|/L$ as well.
Consequence and limitation.
For computable fixed $\beta,J$, finite enumeration of $2^{L^3}$ spin configurations and the displayed error yield certified approximations to $p$ at any requested tolerance. Exponential cost is allowed in this computability interpretation. At $\beta=0$, $p=\log2$ directly. Thus impossibility cannot mean that no terminating arbitrary-precision approximation procedure exists for this model.
First gap.
An Onsager-comparable formula imposes expressive or structural restrictions not captured by computability. The estimate neither selects that class nor excludes any proposed representation. It is also about a uniform ferromagnetic or signed constant coupling, not a complexity reduction from arbitrary instance-dependent couplings. Inferring nonexistence of an exact homogeneous-model formula from the expense of brute-force enumeration would be invalid. The source's definition requirement and impossibility question both remain unresolved; this known boundary argument only rejects one overbroad interpretation.
