For particle positions $X_i(t)$, the empirical measure is $\mu_N(t)=N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N\delta_{X_i(t)}$. The source uses $V_\eta=Z_\eta*Z_\eta$, a smooth normalized symmetric compactly supported convolution-square potential, with algebraic interaction length $\eta=N^{-\beta}$. Its intermediate nonlocal density $u^\eta$ solves the report's aggregation--diffusion equation. Smoothed measures are $f^{N,\eta}=\mu^{N,\eta}*Z_\eta$ and $g^\eta=u^\eta*Z_\eta$. Intermediate fluctuations are $\sqrt N(\mu^{N,\eta}-u^\eta)$, distinct from fluctuations around the final local limit $u$.

The independent sampling baseline.
Let $X_1,\ldots,X_N$ be independent samples of a probability density $\rho$, and put $Z_\eta(x)=\eta^{-d}Z(x/\eta)$ for a normalized nonnegative $L^2$ smoothing kernel. With $f_N=N^{-1}\sum_iZ_\eta(\cdot-X_i)$ and $g=\rho*Z_\eta$, expansion of the squared norm and independence give \[ \mathbb E\|f_N-g\|_2^2 =\frac1N\left(\|Z_\eta\|_2^2-\|g\|_2^2\right) =\frac1N\left(\eta^{-d}\|Z\|_2^2-\|\rho*Z_\eta\|_2^2\right). \] The cross terms for distinct particles vanish after centering, and translation leaves each kernel norm unchanged. If $\rho\in L^2$, Jensen's inequality for normalized nonnegative $Z_\eta$ bounds $\|\rho*Z_\eta\|_2\le\|\rho\|_2$. For $\eta=N^{-\beta}$, $\beta>0$, the leading mean-square scale is therefore $N^{-1+d\beta}$.
