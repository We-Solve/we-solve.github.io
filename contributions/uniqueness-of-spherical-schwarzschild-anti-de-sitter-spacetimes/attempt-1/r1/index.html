Uniqueness of Spherical Schwarzschild–Anti-de Sitter Spacetimes

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

A static spacetime has a hypersurface-orthogonal timelike Killing field. Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter is the spherically symmetric black-hole family with negative cosmological constant. A uniqueness theorem compares static solutions subject to an asymptotic conformal boundary, horizon conditions and regularity assumptions.

Solving the spherical ansatz.
Restrict to a four-dimensional static spherically symmetric exterior \[ g=-e^{2\Phi(r)}F(r)dt^2+F(r)^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2, \qquad F>0, \] with $\Lambda=-3/\ell^2$, $\ell>0$. Direct computation of the mixed Einstein tensor gives $G^t{}_t=(rF'+F-1)/r^2$ and $G^r{}_r-G^t{}_t=2F\Phi'/r$. The vacuum equations $G^a{}_b+\Lambda\delta^a_b=0$ therefore imply \[ (rF)'=1+3r^2/\ell^2,\qquad \Phi'=0. \] Integration gives $F=1-2M/r+r^2/\ell^2$; a constant change of the time coordinate removes $\Phi$. Substitution in the angular equation verifies it as well: with constant $\Phi$, $G^\theta{}_\theta=F'/r+F''/2=3/\ell^2$.
Boundary and horizon test.
For $M>0$, $F'=2M/r^2+2r/\ell^2>0$, while $F\to-\infty$ at zero and $F\to+\infty$ at infinity. There is exactly one positive simple zero, delimiting this static exterior. For $M=0$, no such positive horizon occurs. The normalization of time still has to match the specified conformal boundary.
First uniqueness gap.
Spherical symmetry was assumed, whereas the source seeks uniqueness among the relevant static Einstein competitors. The calculation cannot derive spherical symmetry from a spherical conformal boundary and the appropriate horizon/regularity hypotheses; nor does it specify all global topologies. That rigidity step, with exact source boundary data, is the first gap. The familiar ansatz calculation is not a full static uniqueness theorem.
