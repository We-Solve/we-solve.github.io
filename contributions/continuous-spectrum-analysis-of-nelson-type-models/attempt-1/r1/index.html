Definitions and conventions.
$H$ is the selfadjoint interacting Hamiltonian of the report's Nelson or nonrelativistic-QED model; $\sigma(H)$ is its spectrum and $\sigma_{\mathrm{pp}}(H)$ the set of its eigenvalues. $H_{\mathrm{el}}$ is the electronic Hamiltonian and $\Sigma_{\mathrm{el}}=\inf\sigma(H_{\mathrm{el}})$ is the threshold named in the report. A limiting-absorption principle establishes suitably weighted boundary values of $(H-E\mp i\varepsilon)^{-1}$ as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ on continuous spectral energies; local-decay estimates control the corresponding time evolution.

A continuous-spectrum threshold countertest.
Let $H$ act on $L^2(-1,1)$ by $(Hf)(x)=x^2f(x)$, and let $u(x)=1/\sqrt2$. The energy zero belongs to its spectrum but is not an eigenvalue, because an eigenfunction there would be supported on the null set $\{0\}$. Nevertheless \[ \operatorname{Im}\langle u,(H-i\epsilon)^{-1}u\rangle =\tfrac12\int_{-1}^1\frac{\epsilon}{x^4+\epsilon^2}\,dx =\epsilon^{-1/2}\int_0^{\epsilon^{-1/2}}\frac{dt}{1+t^4}. \] The last integral is bounded below by its positive integral on $[0,1]$ for $0<\epsilon\leq1$. Thus this rank-one compressed resolvent diverges at least as $c\epsilon^{-1/2}$. Removing point spectrum does not remove a threshold singularity.
A decay consequence in the same model.
The scalar evolution is $\langle u,e^{-itH}u\rangle=\int_0^1e^{-itx^2}dx=t^{-1/2}\int_0^{\sqrt t}e^{-iy^2}dy$. Without needing its exact asymptotic constant, the rescaling identifies the threshold time scale and shows why a proof uniform through zero cannot simply reuse an estimate bounded away from threshold.
Source qualification and missing step.
This is not a Nelson or QED counterexample, and it imposes no claim about the source's particular conjugate-operator weights. It diagnoses a necessary threshold analysis before extending the method to the entire continuous spectrum. For QED the primary report asks for energies below $\Sigma_{\rm el}$, whereas the retained catalogue removes that interval. The calculation settles neither range and preserves that difference.
