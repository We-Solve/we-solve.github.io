Finiteness of Atypical Hodge Subvarieties

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 016; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that non-CM elliptic-square points on Hodge-generic curves in $\mathcal A_2$ are finite, and combining the three endomorphism branches gives the full curve-level Zilber--Pink assertion there.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $\mathcal A_2$ be the moduli space of principally polarized abelian surfaces over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$, and let $C$ be any nonempty reduced irreducible closed Hodge-generic algebraic curve therein. The complete asserted conclusion is \[ \#\left(C(\overline{\mathbb Q})\cap \bigcup_{\substack{Z\subset\mathcal A_2\ \mathrm{special}\\\dim Z\le1}} Z(\overline{\mathbb Q})\right)<\infty. \] In particular the points whose surface is isogenous to an elliptic square are finite. Two additional assertions give finiteness for surfaces isogenous to $E_1\times E_2$ with a CM factor, and for surfaces having a full rational endomorphism algebra that is quaternionic, a division algebra and indefinite. There are no bounds on CM fields, isogeny degrees, endomorphism orders or polarizations, and no height, boundary or integrality restrictions.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 016; 2026-09-24), two auxiliary markings control bad-center proximity in a five-dimensional orthogonal variation. Common-parameter interpolation gives arithmetic height bounds, and orbit counting handles elliptic squares; classification of special curves then joins this branch to the CM-product and quaternionic branches.
