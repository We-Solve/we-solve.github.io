The numerical spacetime enclosing-area inequality in every dimension

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: In dimensions three and four, trapped exteriors are claimed deformable to nonnegative-scalar-curvature metrics with protected enclosing area and arbitrarily small ADM-energy loss; The proposed neutral spacetime Penrose inequality compares invariant ADM mass with minimum enclosing area in all dimensions $n\geq3$, under the listed energy, decay, and weak future-trapping hypotheses. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $\Omega^n$, $n\ge3$, be smooth, connected and orientable, with nonempty compact smooth boundary $S$, complete with that boundary included, and exactly one end, with Euclidean coordinates and compact complement. The tensors $g,K$ extend smoothly to $S$. With $\tau=\operatorname{tr}_gK$, use \[ 2\mu=R_g+\tau^2-|K|_g^2,\qquad J=\operatorname{div}_g(K-\tau g),\qquad \mu\ge|J|_g,\quad\mu,|J|_g\in L^1(\Omega,dV_g). \] The finite ADM fluxes use $\omega_{n-1}=|\mathbb S^{n-1}|$ and \[ E=\frac1{2(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|x|=R} (\partial_jg_{ij}-\partial_ig_{jj})n_\delta^i\,dA_\delta,\quad P_i=\frac1{(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|x|=R}(K_{ij}-\tau g_{ij})n_\delta^j\,dA_\delta. \] Orient boundary normals towards the end, with $\theta_+=H+\operatorname{tr}_SK$. Here $O_j(r^{-b})$ controls coordinate derivatives through order $j$, of respective sizes $O(r^{-b-i})$. Choose a connected smooth end-side region $D\subset\Omega$, closed in $\Omega$, containing the distant end and having its manifold interior in $\operatorname{int}\Omega$. A full cut consists of all of its intrinsic boundary $\Gamma=\partial D$; require this boundary to be compact, smoothly embedded and two-sided. All components and portions coinciding with $S=\partial\Omega$ count. Put $a_g(S)=\inf_\Gamma\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)$. In dimension three, suppose $g-\delta=O_2(r^{-q})$, $K=O_1(r^{-1-q})$ for $q>1/2$, and $\theta_+\le0$ on every boundary component. If $E>|P|$, the exterior theorem asserts \[ \sqrt{E^2-|P|^2}\ge\sqrt{a_g(S)/(16\pi)}. \] The preparatory output treats the stronger subclass with compactly supported $K$, $g-\delta=O_j(r^{-1})$ for every fixed $j$, $P=0$, $E>0$, $\theta_+<0$, $A_*=a_g(S)>0$, and, for a smooth radius extension $\varrho\ge1$, \[ R_g=O(r^{-3-\delta}),\qquad \mu-|J|_g\ge c\varrho^{-3-\delta}\quad(c>0,\ 0<\delta<1). \] For sufficiently small fixed $\epsilon>0$ and large $N$, a smooth one-ended subexterior has a complete boundary-included metric $\widehat g_{\epsilon,N}$ satisfying \[ R_{\widehat g}\ge0,\quad H_{\partial\Omega}(\widehat g)<0,\quad \widehat g\ge e^{-4\epsilon}g,\quad E_{\widehat g}\le E+\eta_{\epsilon,N},\quad |T|_{\widehat g}\ge e^{-4\epsilon}A_* \] for every full cut $T$. At fixed $\epsilon$, $0\le\eta_{\epsilon,N}\le C(1+N)^a(e^{-\epsilon N}+e^{-\epsilon N/2})\to0$. The output metric has $O_2(r^{-1})$ decay and scalar curvature $O(r^{-3-\delta'})$ for some $\delta'>0$.
Result 2.
Let $\Omega^n$, $n\ge3$, be smooth, connected and orientable, with nonempty compact smooth boundary $S$, complete with that boundary included, and exactly one end, with Euclidean coordinates and compact complement. The tensors $g,K$ extend smoothly to $S$. With $\tau=\operatorname{tr}_gK$, use \[ 2\mu=R_g+\tau^2-|K|_g^2,\qquad J=\operatorname{div}_g(K-\tau g),\qquad \mu\ge|J|_g,\quad\mu,|J|_g\in L^1(\Omega,dV_g). \] The finite ADM fluxes use $\omega_{n-1}=|\mathbb S^{n-1}|$ and \[ E=\frac1{2(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|x|=R} (\partial_jg_{ij}-\partial_ig_{jj})n_\delta^i\,dA_\delta,\quad P_i=\frac1{(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|x|=R}(K_{ij}-\tau g_{ij})n_\delta^j\,dA_\delta. \] Orient boundary normals towards the end, with $\theta_+=H+\operatorname{tr}_SK$. Here $O_j(r^{-b})$ controls coordinate derivatives through order $j$, of respective sizes $O(r^{-b-i})$. Choose a connected smooth end-side region $D\subset\Omega$, closed in $\Omega$, containing the distant end and having its manifold interior in $\operatorname{int}\Omega$. A full cut consists of all of its intrinsic boundary $\Gamma=\partial D$; require this boundary to be compact, smoothly embedded and two-sided. All components and portions coinciding with $S=\partial\Omega$ count. Put $a_g(S)=\inf_\Gamma\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)$. For $g-\delta=O_6(r^{-q})$, $K=O_5(r^{-1-q})$ with $(n-2)/2<q<n-2$, $\theta_+\le0$, $a_g(S)>0$ and $E>|P|$, the numerical bound is \[ \sqrt{E^2-|P|^2}\ge\frac12\left(\frac{a_g(S)}{\omega_{n-1}}\right)^{(n-2)/(n-1)}. \] Its endpoint corollary derives $E>|P|$ from the other hypotheses. In $n\in\{3,4\}$, the lower-regularity theorem only requires $g-\delta=O_2(r^{-q})$, $K=O_1(r^{-1-q})$ with $q>(n-2)/2$; it proves $a_g(S)>0$ and $E>|P|$ as well as the same inequality. The coefficient is sharp. Numerical assumptions do not require connected boundary, maximality, vacuum, outermostness, spin, or outer area minimization. There is also a complete-data formulation on a smooth connected orientable boundaryless three-manifold $M$, with complete $g$, a compact core and finitely many AF ends. Each end has $g-\delta=O_2(r^{-q})$, $K=O_1(r^{-1-q})$, $q>1/2$, and finite ADM limits; the constraint densities are globally integrable and satisfy DEC. Choose an end-side open region $D^+$, possibly disconnected or containing other ends, whose full boundary $S$ is nonempty, compact, smooth, embedded and two-sided, with $\theta_+\le0$ towards $D^+$. Define \[ A_{\min}(S)=\inf_{D'^+\subseteq D^+}\operatorname{Area}_g(\partial D'^+), \] where smooth open competitors contain the distant designated end and have compact smooth full boundary, permitting disconnected competitors, other retained ends, and coincidence with $S$. The conclusion is $E>|P|$ and $\sqrt{E^2-|P|^2}\ge\sqrt{A_{\min}(S)/(16\pi)}$.

Outline of the argument

Collars and weak boundary supports set the graph barrier. Elliptic estimates exclude contact with the floor and solve the graph-conformal deformation; its negative inner mean curvature supplies the boundary route to the neutral mass-area bound.

End preparation removes ADM momentum before a coupled graph and conformal scalar construction. Filled-equation estimates and height separation protect enclosing cuts, while the scalar identity bounds the change in mass; the Riemannian comparison then returns the inequality to the original data.
