Current Criteria for Phase Separation in Driven Systems

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A one-dimensional driven conserving system has locally moving particles, a fixed total density, and a nonequilibrium stationary current. Phase separation means macroscopic domains with distinct densities rather than a homogeneous stationary state. If $J_\ell$ is the outgoing steady current of a domain of length $\ell$, its large-$\ell$ dependence can be compared with condensation criteria in an associated zero-range process. A jamming transition is the formation of a persistent macroscopic congested region in the traffic interpretation.

The zero-range current calculation.
In a selected zero-range surrogate let the departure rate at occupation $n\geq1$ be $u(n)=u_\infty(1+b/n)$, with $u_\infty>0$ and $b>0$. Its product-form single-site weight, after absorbing $u_\infty$ into fugacity, is \[ f(0)=1,\qquad f(n)=\prod_{k=1}^n(1+b/k)^{-1}. \] Since $\log(1+b/k)=b/k+O(k^{-2})$ and the error is summable, $\log f(n)=-b\log n+O(1)$. Hence positive constants bound $f(n)$ above and below by multiples of $n^{-b}$.
A sharp critical-mean test in this surrogate.
At the limiting fugacity one, $Z=\sum_nf(n)$ is finite exactly when $b>1$. When normalized, its mean $\rho_c=Z^{-1}\sum_nnf(n)$ is finite exactly when $b>2$. The endpoints matter: $b=1$ gives harmonic divergence of $Z$, and $b=2$ gives harmonic divergence of the first moment. These statements follow directly from the comparison series, without invoking a condensation theorem.
The source-level gap.
Thus a current correction of order $b/n$ can determine whether the surrogate has finite background carrying capacity. It does not establish macroscopic condensation, which additionally needs a canonical large-deviation argument, and it does not prove that domain lengths of a driven traffic system form this zero-range process. Correlations between domains can change the stationary weights. The first missing step for the original problem is a justified current-to-domain-process reduction; even after it, the relation between excess mass, phase-separated domains and traffic jams must be proved. This reconstructs the mechanism behind a criterion, not a universal necessary-and-sufficient theorem.
