An exact vertical balance and its ambiguity.
On $0\leq z\leq H$, prescribe constant upward velocity $w$, diffusivity $\kappa>0$, buoyancy endpoints $b(0)=b_0$, $b(H)=b_1$, and steady balance $wb'=\kappa b''$. For $w\ne0$ integration gives \[ b(z)=b_0+(b_1-b_0)\frac{e^{wz/\kappa}-1}{e^{wH/\kappa}-1}. \] For $w=0$ its continuous limit is the linear profile. Differentiation verifies the equation and endpoints. The stratification $N^2=b'$ is positive when $b_1>b_0$, regardless of the sign of $w$, because numerator and denominator signs cancel.
A concrete identification obstruction.
The profile depends on $w$ and $\kappa$ only through $w/\kappa$. Multiplying both by any $c>0$ leaves $b$ and $N^2$ unchanged while multiplying vertical transport by $c$. Thus stratification data alone cannot identify overturning in this model. Even full knowledge of the profile and boundary values does not resolve the ambiguity without an independent transport or mixing measurement.
Source scope.
The actual source has a basin and a reentrant southern channel, with competing mean and eddy overturning. Deriving that closure and its basin/channel matching is the first gap; neither a universal ocean formula nor a unique inference of overturning from stratification has been proved.
