Spectral scalar-curvature control of width in dimension three

OpenAI's claim

Spectral width in dimension three: A complete three-manifold satisfying $-4\Delta+\mathrm{Scal}\ge\lambda>0$ is claimed to map continuously to a graph with full fiber diameter at most $500/\sqrt\lambda$. No global bounded-geometry condition is assumed.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $(M^3,g)$ be connected, complete, smooth and without boundary. If $\lambda>0$ and \[\int_M(4|\nabla\phi|^2+\operatorname{Scal}_g\phi^2)\,dV_g\ge\lambda\int_M\phi^2\,dV_g\quad(\phi\in C_c^\infty(M;\mathbb R)),\] the claim supplies a simplicial complex $K$ satisfying $\dim K\le1$ and a map $f:M\to K$ which is continuous with $\operatorname{diam}_g f^{-1}(y)\le500/\sqrt\lambda$ for every fiber. Compactness, orientability, spin and bounded geometry are not required.

Outline of the argument

Spectral width in dimension three: A positive supersolution is converted into a drift-weighted scalar inequality without changing the original distance. Weighted stable surfaces inherit a two-dimensional inequality, whose geodesic index estimate bounds their intrinsic diameter. Local minimization and smoothing in compact regions then organize these surfaces into a graph-valued decomposition with a uniform bound on each complete fiber.
