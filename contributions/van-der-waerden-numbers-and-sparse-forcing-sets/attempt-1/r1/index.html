van der Waerden numbers and sparse forcing sets

OpenAI's claim

Superexponential van der Waerden growth: The claimed lower bound is $W_r(k)>k^{ck\lfloor\log_2 r\rfloor}$ for one absolute positive $c$ once $k$ is large enough. In particular, for each fixed color count at least two, the $k$th root of $W_r(k)$ is unbounded.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $W_r(k)$ be the least $N$ such that every $r$-coloring of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has a monochromatic nonconstant $k$-term arithmetic progression. The claimed lower bound is \[W_r(k)>k^{10^{-5}k\lfloor\log_2r\rfloor}\quad(k\ge K_0,\ r\ge2),\] where $K_0$ is absolute. The quantifier is uniform over all integer color counts $r\ge2$, but the estimate does not give exact van der Waerden numbers.

Outline of the argument

Superexponential van der Waerden growth: A cyclic-group coloring uses two circle-coordinate meshes related by an arithmetically chosen dilation. Counting anchored progression patterns gives a balanced-color versus affine-coordinate alternative uniformly in the growing dimension. A norm-dependent perturbation excludes the affine alternative, after which interval and product-color transfers give the stated bounds for all color counts.
