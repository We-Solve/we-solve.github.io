B-semiampleness for compact Kähler log-smooth fibrations

OpenAI's claim

The threshold-moduli part of a compact log-smooth Kähler fibration is claimed to become globally generated on a suitable modification, even with horizontal coefficient-one divisors. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $f:Y\to X$ be a connected-fiber surjective holomorphic map of smooth compact connected Kähler manifolds, with effective rational simple-normal-crossing $\Delta$ of coefficients in $[0,1]$, and $K_Y+\Delta\sim_{\mathbb Q}f^*L$ for $L\in\operatorname{Pic}(X)_{\mathbb Q}$. On a smooth compact Kähler modification $\mu:X'\to X$, resolve the main fiber product to $f':Y'\to X'$ and $h:Y'\to Y$, and set $\Delta'=h^*\Delta-K_{Y'/Y}$. Define \[ t_P=\inf_{E\mapsto P}\frac{a(E;Y',\Delta')}{\operatorname{ord}_E((f')^*P)},\quad B_{X'}=\sum_P(1-t_P)P,\quad M_{X'}=\mu^*L-K_{X'}-B_{X'}. \] The infimum is over divisors dominating $P$ with positive denominator. OpenAI claims a smooth compact Kähler modification $S\to X$ for which every further smooth compact Kähler $\nu:S_1\to S$ has $M_{S_1}=\nu^*M_S$ in rational Picard groups and some actual positive integral multiple of $M_S$ is globally generated. Point bases and relative dimension zero are included, and no projectivity assumption on the spaces or morphism is made.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 033, 2026-09-10): Cyclic roots and iterated residues identify the relevant pure Hodge line with a deepest boundary stratum. Compact Douady spaces parameterize product covers of the fibers, and norm estimates extend the precise line identity across the boundary; finite analytic norms then descend one generating exponent.
