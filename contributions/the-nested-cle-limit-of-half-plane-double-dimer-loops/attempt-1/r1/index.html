The nested CLE limit of half-plane double-dimer loops

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. All macroscopic half-plane Temperleyan double-dimer loops are claimed to converge to the nested $\mathrm{CLE}_4$ ensemble along the full mesh limit.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
On the upper-half-plane square lattice, use the Temperleyan matching law obtained by exhausting with coarse-grid simple polygons, deleting a boundary root escaping to infinity, and taking local limits of uniform perfect matchings. Superpose two independent samples and remove doubled edges, leaving loops $\mathcal L_\delta$. For $F(z)=(z-i)/(z+i)$, the source claims tightness and \[ F(\mathcal L_\delta)\Longrightarrow F(\mathcal L), \] where $\mathcal L$ is nested $\operatorname{CLE}_4$ in the upper half-plane, normalized by Brownian-loop-soup central charge one. Convergence matches all loops above every positive diameter threshold in uniform curve distance modulo circle reparametrizations, retaining multiplicity and nesting, along the full mesh limit.

Outline of the argument

Rectangle transfer is expressed in particle sectors and controlled by singular modes with marked boundary caps. Traversal and short-segment bounds provide curve tightness; the resulting geometric control upgrades the dimer field information to matching of every macroscopic loop.
