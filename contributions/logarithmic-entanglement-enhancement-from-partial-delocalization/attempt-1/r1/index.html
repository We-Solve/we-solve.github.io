Logarithmic Entanglement Enhancement from Partial Delocalization

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a bipartite quantum state, entanglement entropy is the von Neumann entropy of a reduced state. In a free-fermion ground state it is computed from the restriction of the one-particle correlation operator to a spatial region. An area law grows like boundary area; logarithmic enhancement adds a factor comparable to the logarithm of region size. Delocalization concerns spatial spread or transport of one-particle states and is not identical to absolute continuity of the spectral measure.

For entanglement entropy in disordered free-fermion systems, how much delocalization of the one-particle eigenvalues and eigenfunctions is needed to produce a logarithmic enhancement of the area law, even when the spectrum is not absolutely continuous?

A kernel criterion for a lower entropy bound.
Let $P$ be an orthogonal projection on $\ell^2(\mathbb Z)$, and $C=\mathbf1_A P\mathbf1_A$ on a finite interval $A$. Its spectrum lies in $[0,1]$. For $h(t)=-t\log t-(1-t)\log(1-t)$, the elementary inequalities $-\log t\ge1-t$ and $-\log(1-t)\ge t$ give $h(t)\ge2t(1-t)$, including endpoints by continuity. Therefore \[ S(A)=\operatorname{Tr}h(C)\ge2\operatorname{Tr}(C-C^2) =2\sum_{x\in A,y\notin A}|P(x,y)|^2. \] The last identity uses $P^2=P$, hence $P(x,x)=\sum_y|P(x,y)|^2$.
A conditional logarithm.
Suppose additionally $|P(x,y)|^2\ge c/(y-x)^2$ whenever $x\in A=\{1,\ldots,L\}$, $y>L$, and $1\le y-x\le L$. There are exactly $r$ such crossing pairs at separation $r\le L$. It follows that $S(A)\ge2c\sum_{r=1}^Lr^{-1}\ge2c\log(L+1)$.
Gap and scope.
