Toms–Winter Regularity Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

Manuscript 1 (family 291, 2026-09-23): A separable nuclear C*-algebra that is simple and non-elementary is claimed to absorb Jiang--Su under extended-functional strict comparison, including nonunital and unbounded-trace cases; semigroup divisibility also yields a nonsimple criterion; Manuscript 2 (family 291, 2026-09-23): Finite nuclear dimension is claimed to characterize Jiang--Su stability for separable nuclear algebras lacking elementary ideal subquotients; moreover, Jiang--Su stabilization always lowers nuclear dimension to at most one. Manuscript 3 (family 291, 2026-09-23): The paper claims uniform Gamma from real rank zero in the tracial-completion ultrapower for simple unital nuclear stably finite algebras. Comparison gives absorption, also under specified bounded-trace hypotheses in the stably projectionless setting; Manuscript 4 (family 302, 2026-09-25): Whenever an infinite compact metrizable system is minimal under a homeomorphism, its crossed-product comparison radius is claimed to be half its mean dimension, including infinite values; zero forces nuclear dimension at most one.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $\operatorname{Cu}(A)$ be the ordered Cuntz semigroup of positive elements of $A\otimes\mathcal K$, modulo mutual norm-approximation comparison. For separable simple nuclear non-elementary $A$, OpenAI claims $A\simeq A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z$ if, for every $x,y\in\operatorname{Cu}(A)$, inequalities $\varphi(x)\le\varphi(y)$ for all additive order/supremum-preserving functionals $\varphi:\operatorname{Cu}(A)\to[0,\infty]$, strict whenever $0<\varphi(y)<\infty$, imply $x\le y$. It further claims absorption for every nonzero separable nuclear $A$ whose Cuntz semigroup satisfies both \[ (l+1)x\le ly\ \Longrightarrow\ x\le y\quad(l\ge1),\qquad \forall x,\ N\ge1\ \exists u:\ Nu\le x\le(N+1)u. \] In that case a unital $\mathcal Z\to (A_\omega\cap A')/\operatorname{Ann}(A)$ is asserted for every free ultrafilter, where $A_\omega$ is the norm ultrapower and $\operatorname{Ann}(A)=\{z:zA=Az=0\}$. Finally it claims \[ A\simeq A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Cu}(A)\simeq\operatorname{Cu}(A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z) \] for all separable nuclear $A$, allowing an abstract ordered-semigroup isomorphism rather than only the canonical first-factor map. Here $\mathcal Z$ is the Jiang--Su algebra, and non-elementary excludes compact-operator algebras.
Manuscript 2.
For a separable nuclear complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ with no ideal subquotient that is nonzero and elementary $I/J\simeq\mathcal K(H)$, OpenAI claims \[ \dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}A<\infty\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\simeq A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}A\le1. \] Nuclear dimension is defined by finite-dimensional completely positive approximations with order-zero color decomposition; $\mathcal Z$ is the Jiang--Su algebra. The conditions are also claimed equivalent to either a canonical or an abstract isomorphism of $\operatorname{Cu}(A)$ with $\operatorname{Cu}(A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z)$. Without the subquotient restriction it asserts $\dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}(A_0\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z)\le1$ for every separable nuclear $A_0$. Separately, for every nonzero unital characterless $D$ it claims a finite maximal tensor power containing two full orthogonal positive elements; full means generating the whole algebra as a closed ideal.
Manuscript 3.
Let $A$ be unital, separable, simple, infinite-dimensional, nuclear and stably finite, with nonempty tracial state space $T(A)$. Let $B=\overline A^{T(A)}$ be its completion on the norm-bounded unit ball for $\|a\|_{2,T(A)}=\sup_\tau\tau(a^*a)^{1/2}$, and $B^\omega$ its uniform-tracial ultrapower for a free ultrafilter. If $B^\omega$ has real rank equal to zero when given the quotient $C^*$-norm, OpenAI claims a central projection $p$ satisfying \[ \lambda(px)=\tfrac12\lambda(x)\qquad(x\in B,\ \lambda\in\Lambda_\omega), \] where $\Lambda_\omega$ consists of ultralimits of sequences of designated trace extensions. It also claims every tracial state on $B$ is the unique extension of a trace of $A$, with $|\sigma(b)|\le\|b\|_{2,T(A)}$. For a unital nuclear inclusion $A\subset D$ with $D$ real rank zero and nonempty traces, assume arbitrarily small uniformly tracial full projections in $D$. It claims finite-set approximate central halving projections: given finite $K\subset\mathcal U(A)$ and $\varepsilon>0$, some projection $P\in D$ satisfies $\sup_\tau\|[P,x]\|_{2,\tau}<\varepsilon$ and $\sup_\tau|\tau(Px)-\tau(x)/2|<\varepsilon$ for $x\in K$. The independent unital result takes an infinite-dimensional nuclear complex $C^*$-algebra $A$, assumed simple, separable and unital. For positive $a,b\in A\otimes\mathcal K$, define $d_\tau(a)=\lim_{n\to\infty}(\tau\otimes\operatorname{Tr})(a^{1/n})$, using the unnormalized matrix trace. Assume \[ b\ne0,\quad d_\tau(a)<d_\tau(b)\quad(\tau\in T(A))\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a\precsim b. \] When $T(A)$ is empty, this premise still requires $a\precsim b$ for every positive $a$ and every nonzero positive $b$. OpenAI claims $A\simeq A\otimes\mathcal Z$; when traces exist it also constructs uniform property $\Gamma$. The separate nonunital theorem starts with a nonzero nuclear complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ that is simple and separable, with no nonzero projection in $A\otimes\mathcal K$. Write $T_{\mathrm{lsc}}(A)$ for densely finite lower-semicontinuous traces, and $T_1(A)$ for those bounded traces having norm one. Require every trace in $T_{\mathrm{lsc}}(A)$ to be bounded on the positive unit ball, and require $T_1(A)$ to be nonempty and weak-$*$ compact. The precise comparison premise is \[ \forall a,b\in(A\otimes\mathcal K)_+,\quad b\ne0,\quad \left[d_\tau(a)<1\text{ for every }\tau\in T_{\mathrm{lsc}}(A)\text{ with }d_\tau(b)=1\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ a\precsim b. \] Under these hypotheses it claims uniform property $\Gamma$ and $A\simeq A\otimes\mathcal Z$. Here $a\precsim b$ means $x_n^*bx_n\to a$ in norm for stabilized elements $x_n$, and $\mathcal Z$ is the Jiang--Su algebra.
Manuscript 4.
Let $h$ be a homeomorphism of $X$ whose orbits are all dense, where $X$ is compact, infinite and metrizable. OpenAI claims \[ \operatorname{rc}(C(X)\rtimes_h\mathbb Z)=\tfrac12\operatorname{mdim}(X,h)\quad\text{in }[0,\infty]. \] For positive stabilized elements define $d_\tau(a)=\lim_m\tau(a^{1/m})$. The radius of comparison is the infimum of $r\ge0$ such that $d_\tau(a)+r<d_\tau(b)$ for every normalized trace implies Cuntz subequivalence $a\precsim b$. Mean dimension is the asymptotic covering dimension per iterate, defined from joins of finite open covers. Both finite and infinite mean dimensions are included.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 291, 2026-09-23): Trace transfer into small hereditary supports followed by spectral rounding establishes full Cuntz-semigroup divisibility. Pure-state compression produces norm-visible matrix cones, while localized nuclear approximations produce trace-visible ones. Comparison packs their defects into a central corner, constructing dimension-drop maps whose lifted intertwining relations give Jiang--Su absorption. Manuscript 2 (family 291, 2026-09-23): Localized order-zero approximations control ranks even when traces are infinite on some ideals. A supporting map and comparison yield two-color approximations that survive stabilization and hereditary passage. Conversely, Gaussian sign tensors and pure-state excision construct two full orthogonal central elements from the absence of characters, supplying the absorption criterion.

Manuscript 3 (family 291, 2026-09-23): Nuclear averaging and scalar block compressions preserve coefficient traces while packing small projections with controlled commutators. A separate comparison construction rounds ranks, forms clipped sums of centered symmetries, and extracts Gaussian trace partitions. In the projectionless case, a combined overlap-and-model cost controls small splittings before reindexing amplifies them to exact central partitions. Manuscript 4 (family 302, 2026-09-25): Finite-support comparison witnesses are converted to bundle embeddings, which feed the companion's boundary-preserving compression theorem. Packet reconstruction translates that compression into a smaller cover without introducing absent colors. Applying the construction on aligned orbit blocks contradicts any larger cover-growth rate, providing the missing lower bound; established upper bounds and regularity results finish the equality and zero-dimensional consequences.
