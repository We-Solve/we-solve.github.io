An explicit high-entropy two-source benchmark

The source seeks better error and output length at near-optimal entropy for specific seedless source classes. A recent October2026 primary abstract claims much stronger affine-extractor parameters in higher-entropy regimes; we do not treat those claims as a proof of the low-entropy target. We analyze a classical one-bit construction to expose the entropy and independence losses directly. Let independent $X,Y\in\mathbb F_2^n$, $n\ge1$, have min-entropies at least $k_1,k_2$, meaning every point probability is at most $2^{-k_1}$ and $2^{-k_2}$ respectively. Define $Z=\langle X,Y\rangle\pmod2$.
Proposition: Its distance from a uniform bit satisfies \[ \|\mathcal L(Z)-U_1\|_{\rm TV} \le\frac12\,2^{(n-k_1-k_2)/2}. \]
Proof: Let $H$ be the $2^n\times2^n$ real matrix $H_{x,y}=(-1)^{\langle x,y\rangle}$. For $x\ne x'$, the sum of $(-1)^{\langle x+x',y\rangle}$ over $y$ is zero: translation by a unit vector in a nonzero coordinate of $x+x'$ pairs opposite signs. For $x=x'$ the sum is $2^n$. Thus $HH^T=H^TH=2^nI$. If $p,q$ are the probability vectors of the two sources, independence gives bias $\mathbb E(-1)^Z=p^THq$. By Cauchy--Schwarz, \[ |p^THq|\le2^{n/2}\|p\|_2\|q\|_2 \le2^{(n-k_1-k_2)/2}, \] because $\sum p_x^2\le(\max p_x)\sum p_x\le2^{-k_1}$, and similarly for $q$. A bit with bias $b$ has probabilities $(1+b)/2,(1-b)/2$, hence total-variation distance $|b|/2$ from uniform. ∎
The construction gives small error only when $k_1+k_2$ substantially exceeds $n$; inserting $k_1,k_2=O(\log n)$ yields no useful estimate. Repeating this bit without additional analysis does not produce jointly uniform output bits. Independence is indispensable: for $n\ge2$, take $X$ uniform on the even-parity subspace and put $Y=X$. Both marginals have min-entropy $n-1$, yet $\langle X,X\rangle=\sum_iX_i=0$, so the output is constant. More generally, no deterministic function with a nonempty bit output extracts from all sources of min-entropy at least $n-1$: one fiber of its first output bit has at least $2^{n-1}$ inputs. The uniform law on that fiber has the required entropy but a fixed first bit, giving total-variation distance at least $1/2$. Additional source structure in the workshop is therefore substantive.
