A directional zero–one law for finite-range-dependent random environments

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The directional zero--one conclusion is claimed for uniformly elliptic stationary finite-range-dependent environments in dimensions at least three, including irrational fixed directions.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $d\ge3$ and consider nearest-neighbor transition rows on $\mathbb Z^d$ under a translation-stationary ergodic environment law $Q$. Assume a deterministic ellipticity lower bound $\omega(x,e)\ge\kappa>0$ almost surely and a deterministic dependence range $R$: row sigma-fields for any two deterministic sets, including infinite sets, are independent when their $\ell^\infty$ distance exceeds $R$. For the annealed law $P_0=\int P_{0,\omega}\,dQ$, the claimed conclusion is \[ \forall\ell\in\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\text{ fixed},\qquad P_0(X_n\cdot\ell\to+\infty)\in\{0,1\}. \]

Outline of the argument

The proof uses independent sigma-fields of separated transition rows rather than an iid-label representation. Buffered regeneration pieces keep reveals beyond the dependence range, allowing the directional contact and escape contradiction to survive the finite-range correlations.
