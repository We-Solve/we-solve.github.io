Factor-two hardness of Vertex Cover

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 102; 2026-09-23), the asserted result is that every fixed approximation factor strictly below two for Vertex Cover is NP-hard on simple unweighted graphs.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For every fixed factor $1\le\alpha<2$, the manuscript claims NP-hardness of approximating minimum Vertex Cover within factor $\alpha$. In particular, \[ (\exists\text{ deterministic polynomial-time }(2-\varepsilon)\text{-approximation}) \Rightarrow\mathrm P=\mathrm{NP}\quad\text{for every fixed }\varepsilon\in(0,1]. \] The optimum is minimum number of selected vertices meeting every edge in the unweighted graph.
