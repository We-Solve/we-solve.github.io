Problem 23: extending a certified cut through a small exceptional set

1) OBJECTIVE AND SOURCE REVIEW

Attempt dated 2026-09-23. The conjecture is $\tau_B(G)\le |V(G)|^2/25$ for triangle-free graphs, where $\tau_B$ is the fewest edges deleted to obtain a bipartite graph. I read 23.tex, 23_v2.tex, 23_v3.tex, and 23_v4.tex in full: they respectively use universal bounds, $C_5$ homomorphisms, longer odd-cycle homomorphisms, and a cited common-neighborhood theorem. I investigate graphs close to the certified homomorphism class.

2) WORK

Let $S\subseteq V(G)$ have $t$ vertices, and suppose $H=G-S$ maps to $C_5$. Write $b=e(S,V(G)\setminus S)$ and $h=e(G[S])$. Then \[ \tau_B(G)\le\frac{(N-t)^2}{25}+\frac{b+h}{2},\qquad N=|V(G)|. \] For completeness, the homomorphism fibers have sizes $a_0,\ldots,a_4$ with sum $N-t$. A cut leaving only fiber pair $i,i+1$ uncut costs at most $a_ia_{i+1}$. Their minimum is at most $(N-t)^2/25$: their product is $(\prod a_i)^2\le((N-t)/5)^{10}$. Fix this cut on $H$ and assign each vertex in $S$ independently to either side with probability $1/2$. Each edge incident with $S$, including an edge inside $S$, is uncut with probability $1/2$. Some assignment costs no more than the expectation, proving the claim.

In particular the conjectured bound holds whenever \[ b+h\le\frac{4Nt-2t^2}{25}. \] For $t=1$, a vertex of degree at most $(4N-2)/25$ can be restored to any graph already satisfying the $(N-1)^2/25$ bound. Consequently a counterexample with the smallest possible number of vertices must have minimum degree strictly greater than $(4N-2)/25$. This last observation applies without a homomorphism assumption, by minimality.

3) ADVERSARIAL VERIFICATION

The cut on $S$ is optimized after the cut on $H$ is fixed, so no independence of different edges is asserted or needed. The number $b+h$ counts edges inside $S$ once. No structural theorem saying that every graph has a suitable $S$ has been established.

There is also a scope correction to 23_v3.tex: every odd cycle of length at least 5 maps to $C_5$. A closed walk of length $5+2j$ is obtained by one circuit of $C_5$ and $j$ backtracks. Thus allowing these longer cycle targets does not enlarge the class of graphs covered by $C_5$ colorability for the original $N^2/25$ question, although the length-dependent numerical bound can be sharper.

4) FINAL

UNRESOLVED. The result gives an explicit certificate when a small exceptional set has few incident edges. The missing step is to find such a certificate, or an equally strong cut, in every triangle-free graph.

5) SOURCES CHECKED

All four supplied versions. Balogh--Clemen--Lidický, Max Cuts in Triangle-free Graphs, https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14179, checked 2026-09-23, confirms the conjecture and the published $N^2/23.5$ bound. The extension lemma is proved here; it is not presented as an improvement of that published universal bound.
