Rooted-Tree Expansions of Renormalized Two-Point Functions

Definitions and conventions.
The source model is the self-dual noncommutative $\phi^4_4$ theory in its planar sector. Its renormalized connected two-point function is expanded formally in the coupling $\lambda$, $G_{\alpha\beta}=\sum_{n\ge0}\lambda^nG^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta}$, with $0\le\alpha,\beta<1$. Write $A=(1-\alpha)/(1-\alpha\beta)$ and $B=(1-\beta)/(1-\alpha\beta)$. Rooted trees index nested integrals arising in the source's integral-equation recursion. The first examples are $I(z)=\int_0^1 z(1-zx)^{-1}\,dx=-\log(1-z)$ and $I_\bullet(z)=\int_0^1 zI(x)(1-zx)^{-1}\,dx$. Polynomiality at every order means that each coefficient $G^{(n)}$ lies in the algebra over $\mathbb Q$ generated by the specified variables and source iterated integrals.

An exact first nested integral.
For $0\leq z<1$, the source gives $I(z)=-\log(1-z)$ and \[ I_\bullet(z)=\int_0^1\frac{z[-\log(1-x)]}{1-zx}\,dx. \] Expanding the nonnegative geometric series and integrating termwise gives $I_\bullet(z)=\sum_{m\geq1}H_mz^m/m$, where $H_m=\sum_{k=1}^m1/k$. To verify the coefficient, write $-\log(1-x)=\sum_{k\geq1}x^k/k$ and use $\sum_{k\geq1}1/[k(k+m)]=H_m/m$, a telescoping difference.
Reduction to familiar functions.
Define $\operatorname{Li}_2(z)=\sum_{m\geq1}z^m/m^2$. Differentiating convergent series on compact subsets of $[0,1)$ gives \[ \frac d{dz}\sum_{m\geq1}\frac{H_m}{m}z^m =\frac{-\log(1-z)}{z(1-z)}. \] The same derivative is obtained from $\operatorname{Li}_2(z)+\tfrac12\log^2(1-z)$, and both vanish at zero. Thus $I_\bullet(z)=\operatorname{Li}_2(z)+\tfrac12\log^2(1-z)$. The logarithmic divergence at $z\uparrow1$ shows that endpoint substitutions need explicit renormalized constants rather than unqualified evaluation.
