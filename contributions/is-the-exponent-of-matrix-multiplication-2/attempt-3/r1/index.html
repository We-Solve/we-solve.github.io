Is the exponent of matrix multiplication 2

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 107; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that characteristic-zero matrix multiplication has square exponent below $2.258$, dual exponent above $0.465$, and rectangular exponent at aspect $0.709$ below $2.092$, with the stated finite-characteristic exceptions; In manuscript 2 (family 107; 2026-10-02), the asserted result is that the complex matrix-multiplication exponent is at most $9/4$; In manuscript 3 (family 107; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that the square multiplication exponent is below $2.371054886006746$ over every fixed field, including every positive characteristic.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
In exact complex arithmetic, let $\omega$ be the square matrix-multiplication exponent, $w(k)$ the exponent for multiplying an $n\times n^k$ matrix by an $n^k\times n$ matrix, and $\alpha=\sup\{k:w(k)=2\}$. The claimed bounds are \[ \omega<1129/500=2.258,\qquad\alpha>0.465,\qquad w(0.709)<2.092. \] These are arithmetic exponents, rather than bit-complexity or finite-precision bounds.
Manuscript 2.
For every $\varepsilon>0$, the claim gives an exact complex-arithmetic algorithm multiplying two $n\times n$ matrices in \[ O_{\varepsilon}(n^{9/4+\varepsilon})\quad\text{operations},\qquad\omega\le9/4. \] Its associated tensor inequality uses $T=\sum_{i=1}^sT_i$ with every $T_i\ne0$, a shared first tensor factor and matched disjoint sectors on the other two. A tensor character $\lambda$ takes nonnegative real values and is additive, multiplicative and restriction-monotone, with $\lambda(1)=1$; define $p_X$ by $\lambda(x\sum_{j=1}^my_jz_j)=m^{p_X}$. For every probability vector $q$ the claimed inequality is \[ \lambda(T)\ge e^{p_XH(q)}\prod_i\lambda(T_i)^{q_i},\qquad H(q)=-\sum_iq_i\log q_i, \] with $0\log0=0$. These are exact arithmetic and tensor-character statements, without finite-precision assumptions.
Manuscript 3.
For every fixed field $F$, the claimed arithmetic exponent of square matrix multiplication satisfies \[ \omega_F<2.371054886006746<2.371054887. \] The exponent is the infimum of asymptotic powers achievable by exact field operations, allowing an arbitrarily small additive exponent slack. Positive and zero characteristics are both included.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 107; 2026-09-24), an auxiliary tensor supplies a conditional label to the missing third leg while retaining a dot-product block. Recursive completion trees and finite-family entropy optimization produce certified parameters; a strict comparison argument verifies the advertised boundaries.

For manuscript 2 (family 107; 2026-10-02), tensor degenerations separate blocks that already expose two independent variable legs. Character inequalities for polynomial multiplication, a determinant filtration, and discrete growth bound the detecting character values for matrix multiplication at the claimed exponent.

For manuscript 3 (family 107; 2026-09-24), a joint extraction from several tensor depths uses entropy and a common modular hash. Sequential deletion assigns variables compatibly, and exact integer tables repair inherited masks; the resulting direct sum is converted to an exponent bound by the rank comparison.
