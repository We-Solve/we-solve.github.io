Definitions and conventions.

Configuration-space equidistribution means $\int_M a|\varphi_j|^2\,d\mathrm{vol}_g\to \mathrm{vol}_g(M)^{-1}\int_M a\,d\mathrm{vol}_g$ for every continuous test function $a$. The standard stronger microlocal QUE formulation concerns convergence of semiclassical measures on the unit cotangent bundle to normalized Liouville measure.

Testing the density-one route.
A common attempted upgrade starts from an averaged variance estimate for a fixed observable. Abstractly, let $a_j\ge0$ be its squared equidistribution error and suppose $N^{-1}\sum_{j\le N}a_j\to0$. For every $\delta>0$, the number of $j\le N$ with $a_j\ge\delta$ is at most $\delta^{-1}\sum_{j\le N}a_j=o(N)$. This establishes a zero-density exceptional set for each fixed threshold, but not its absence.
An exact obstruction to the upgrade.
Set $a_j=1$ when $j$ is a perfect square and zero otherwise. Then \[ \frac1N\sum_{j\le N}a_j=\frac{\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor}{N}\longrightarrow0, \qquad a_{n^2}=1. \] The same issue can be expressed by probability measures: take normalized volume measure except at square indices, where take a fixed point mass. For a continuous observable separating that point from its volume average, its errors behave exactly in this sparse way.
