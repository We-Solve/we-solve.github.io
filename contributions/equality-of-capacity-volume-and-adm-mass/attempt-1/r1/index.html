Equality of Capacity–Volume and ADM Mass

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

An asymptotically flat three-manifold approaches Euclidean space on its end. The ADM mass is the asymptotic flux of first derivatives of the metric. The normalized capacity of a compact region $K$ is the infimum of $(4\pi)^{-1}\int_{M\setminus K}|\nabla u|^2dV_g$ over admissible functions with boundary value one on $K$ and value zero at infinity. Writing $|K|$ for Riemannian volume, Jauregui's capacity--volume mass is \[ m_{\mathrm{CV}}(M,g)=\sup_{\{K_j\}}\limsup_{j\to\infty} \left[\left(\frac{3|K_j|}{4\pi}\right)^{1/3}-\operatorname{cap}(K_j)\right], \] where the supremum is over the admissible compact exhaustions in the source.

Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For every asymptotically flat Riemannian three-manifold $(M^3,g)$, does the capacity–volume mass equal the ADM mass, $m_{\mathrm{CV}}(M,g)=m_{\mathrm{ADM}}(M,g)$?
Scope and source qualification.
The source reports equality for harmonically flat ends under additional hypotheses, and asks about general asymptotically flat manifolds. An exact proof must use the report's definition of the exhaustion supremum, rather than a single chosen family of regions.

A harmonic-end exhaustion calculation.
Let $g=(1+a/r)^4\delta$ outside a compact set in three dimensions, with $a\ge0$. For the coordinate sphere $r=R$, a radial capacitary potential $v$ satisfies $((r+a)^2v')'=0$, $v(R)=1$, $v(\infty)=0$. Thus $v(r)=(R+a)/(r+a)$. Its normalized energy is \[ \operatorname{cap}(B_R)=\int_R^\infty(r+a)^2|v'|^2dr=R+a. \] It minimizes the energy: for any zero-boundary perturbation $w$, the cross term vanishes by the radial harmonic equation and integration by parts, leaving the nonnegative energy of $w$. Meanwhile the compact interior contributes only a bounded volume correction, and expansion of $r^2(1+a/r)^6$ gives \[ |B_R|=4\pi(R^3/3+3aR^2+O(R)),\qquad (3|B_R|/(4\pi))^{1/3}=R+3a+O(R^{-1}). \] The capacity--volume difference along this exhaustion tends to $2a$. The ADM flux also equals $2a$: since $g_{ij}=(1+4a/r+O(r^{-2}))\delta_{ij}$, its flux integrand is $-2\partial_r(1+a/r)^4$, and division by $16\pi$ gives the stated limit.
