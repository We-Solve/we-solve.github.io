Infrared Equivalence of Stack Presentations

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a physically realizable presentation $[X/G]$ of a stack, the associated worldsheet theory is the report's $G$-gauged sigma model on $X$. Renormalization-group flow changes its couplings with scale; its infrared theory describes the long-distance limit. Two infrared theories being the same means physical equivalence in the report's sense, not equality of their microscopic fields, gauge groups, or bare actions.
Source scope and qualifications.
The source restricts to physically realizable presentations and formulates infrared equivalence as a conjecture. It neither supplies a fully defined universal RG flow nor asserts equivalence for arbitrary stack presentations; the qualification is essential.

An exact finite presentation check.
A finite groupoid has finitely many objects and morphisms. Its weighted cardinality is $|\mathcal G|=\sum_{[x]}1/|\operatorname{Aut}(x)|$, the sum over isomorphism classes. If a finite group $G$ acts on a finite set $X$, the action groupoid has \[ |X//G|=\sum_{\text{orbits }O}1/|G_x| =\sum_O|O|/|G|=|X|/|G|. \] The middle equality follows from the bijection $G/G_x\to O$. An equivalence of finite groupoids bijects isomorphism classes and gives isomorphic automorphism groups, so this weighted cardinality is presentation independent.
A concrete change of presentation.
Let a finite group $H$ act freely on itself and trivially on $X$. Then $[X\times H/(G\times H)]$ presents the same finite quotient as $[X/G]$, with the obvious independent actions. The unnormalized counts differ by $|H|$, but the normalized zero-dimensional partition sum agrees: \[ \frac{|X\times H|}{|G\times H|}=\frac{|X|}{|G|}. \] The same proof works for any weight constant on groupoid isomorphism classes and pulled back under the equivalence.
Why this is not an RG theorem.
This exact calculation checks the gauge-volume normalization in a finite toy model. A worldsheet sigma model has fields, kinetic terms, possible anomalies and scale-dependent couplings, none of which is represented by the count. Stack equivalence alone does not identify the chosen measures and renormalized actions. The first unresolved step is a correspondence between physically realizable presentations preserving those data along a defined RG flow. No conclusion for arbitrary presentations, and no infrared equivalence for the source theories, follows from the counting identity.
