The Unit Distance Problem

OpenAI's claim

Planar unit-distance power saving: The unit-distance manuscript claims an absolute exponent $\beta<4/3$ bounding the number of unordered unit pairs by $C n^\beta$. The point configuration is otherwise arbitrary.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For $P\subset\mathbb R^2$ of $n$ distinct points let $u(P)$ count unordered pairs at Euclidean distance $1$, and let $u(n)=\max_{|P|=n}u(P)$. The claim is that absolute constants $C<\infty$ and $1\le\beta<4/3$ satisfy \[u(n)\le Cn^\beta\quad(n\ge0).\] The unspecified saving $4/3-\beta>0$ improves the known exponent but does not establish every proposed near-linear bound.

Outline of the argument

Planar unit-distance power saving: A near-extremal incidence sequence is transferred to algebraic configurations and analyzed using distance identities over a negligible-height coefficient field. After differentiating those identities, valuations distinguish the individual radicands and allow independent square-root sign changes. One unused sign change contradicts the differentiated system; a separate exclusion of popular unit circles handles the possible square-class failure, preventing approach to exponent $4/3$.
