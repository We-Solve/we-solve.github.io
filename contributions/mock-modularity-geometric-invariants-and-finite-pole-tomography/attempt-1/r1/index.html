Definitions and notation.
Gromov--Witten invariants are virtual counts of stable maps into a specified target. Donaldson-type invariants depend on the relevant geometric/gauge-theoretic setting. Mock modular forms are holomorphic parts of modular completions. The OSV conjecture relates a black-hole counting ensemble to topological-string data under particular physical and normalization assumptions.

Finite coefficients cannot certify a mock-modular connection.
Suppose a geometric calculation supplies only $a_0,\ldots,a_N$ of a holomorphic generating series. For any holomorphic function $G(q)$ on the unit disk, the series \[F_G(q)=\sum_{n=0}^Na_nq^n+q^{N+1}G(q) \] has the same supplied data. Taking $G=0$ and $G=(1-q)^{-1}$, for example, gives different singular behavior at $q=1$ while preserving every checked coefficient. Even an asserted finite list of polar coefficients at one cusp leaves analogous freedom unless a function space and transformations at all relevant cusps are fixed.
A useful uniqueness condition, stated conditionally.
If a specified space $\mathcal V$ of candidate completed modular objects is finite dimensional and a coefficient map $E:\mathcal V\to\mathbb C^{N+1}$ is injective, then agreement of those coefficients does imply equality within $\mathcal V$. This is immediate from $E(F_1-F_2)=0$. The substantial work is proving membership in $\mathcal V$, the same shadow and growth conditions, and injectivity of $E$; none follows from the numerical match itself.
