All-pairs diamond distortion in tree-segment Banach spaces

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 331; 2026-09-27), the asserted result is that countably branching depth-$k$ diamonds incur $D^2\ge1+k/4$ in the selected segment spaces, with further power-type bounds for path and recursive norms.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
On a countably branching tree, give finitely supported arrays the disjoint-segment norm \[ \rho(a)=\sup_{S_1,\ldots,S_m}\left(\sum_{j=1}^m|\sum_{s\in S_j}a_s|^2\right)^{1/2}, \] where segments are ancestral intervals and are pairwise disjoint. Complete this norm on the forest $\coprod_{h\ge1}\mathbb N^{\le h}$ to obtain $J$ and set $X=J^*$. On $\mathbb N^{<\omega}$ obtain $J_{\infty}$ in the same way, and let $B_{\infty}$ be the closed span of coordinate evaluations in $J_{\infty}^*$. Form $\mathcal D_k$ by replacing each edge by countably many two-edge paths, halving the edge lengths at each stage. The claimed bound for every bi-Lipschitz embedding into either target is \[ D^2\ge1+k/4\quad(k\ge0), \] where $D$ is the ratio of upper and lower Lipschitz constants with respect to shortest-path distance.
