Erdős Problem #60: independent continuation

Date: 23 September 2026. Source versions read in full: 60.tex in gpt_pro_5.4.

1) OBJECTIVE AND SOURCE AUDIT

The target is a lower bound of order $\sqrt n$ for the number of four-cycles in an $n$-vertex graph with $\operatorname{ex}(n,C_4)+1$ edges. The supplied file correctly demonstrates a limitation of replacing all degree information by its average. I retain the missing local quantities instead.

2) AN EXACT DEFECT IDENTITY

For unordered vertex pairs let $c(u,v)$ be their common-neighbour count. Write \[ M=\sum_v\binom{d(v)}2,\quad N=\binom n2,\quad Z=|\{\{u,v\}:c(u,v)=0\}|, \quad T=\sum_{c(u,v)\ge3}\binom{c(u,v)-1}{2}. \] For every positive integer $c$, $\binom c2=(c-1)+\binom{c-1}2$. Also $\sum c(u,v)=M$. Since each four-cycle is counted by its two opposite pairs, \[ \boxed{2\#C_4=M-N+Z+T.} \tag{1} \] This is an equality, rather than merely the source's surplus inequality. Let $m=e(G)$, $\overline d=2m/n$, and $V=\sum_v(d(v)-\overline d)^2$. Then \[ M=2m^2/n-m+V/2. \] With $R=\frac n4(1+\sqrt{4n-3})$, $D=R-\operatorname{ex}(n,C_4)$, and $m=\operatorname{ex}(n,C_4)+t$, $1$ becomes \[ 2\#C_4=\sqrt{4n-3}(t-D)+\frac{2(t-D)^2}{n}+V/2+Z+T. \tag{2} \]

3) THE MISSING STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE

Equation $2$ pinpoints what a repair of the global method needs. At $t=1$, it would suffice to prove that $V/2+Z+T$ exceeds the negative quadratic defect by $c\sqrt n$. Degree variance alone need not be large enough. In a $C_4$-free graph, all codegrees are zero or one, so already $M-N+Z=0$: the zero-codegree term precisely compensates the apparent global deficit. A supersaturation proof must control how that compensation changes after crossing the extremal edge count. As a direct check, adding a nonedge $uv$ to a $C_4$-free graph creates exactly as many four-cycles as there are length-three paths from $u$ to $v$ in the original graph. Nothing in the edge count alone guarantees the needed path count for an arbitrary near-extremal graph.
