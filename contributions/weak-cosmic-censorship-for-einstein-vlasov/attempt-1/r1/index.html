Weak Cosmic Censorship for Einstein–Vlasov

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
The Einstein--Vlasov system couples $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi T_{\mu\nu}[f]$ to collisionless transport of a nonnegative phase-space density $f$ along the spacetime geodesic flow on the particle mass shell. The stress-energy tensor is obtained by integrating particle momenta against $f$. Asymptotically flat initial data describe an isolated system; spherical symmetry reduces the metric and matter fields to radial variables. Weak cosmic censorship asks for complete future null infinity for generic admissible data. Strong cosmic censorship concerns inextendibility of the maximal globally hyperbolic development in a specified regularity class.
Source scope and qualifications.
Genericity, data topology, matter regularity, and the precise completeness formulation must be fixed in a proof. The source emphasizes that dust and scalar-field results cannot be transferred automatically to Vlasov matter; this is not a theorem for every initial datum without exceptional sets.

A matter inequality from the Vlasov representation.
At a spacetime point choose an orthonormal frame, particle mass $m_0\geq0$ and future momentum $p^0=\sqrt{m_0^2+|p|^2}$. For a nonnegative integrable distribution $f(p)$ define $T^{ab}=\int f(p)p^ap^b\,dp/p^0$, with the usual exclusion of the zero momentum singularity in the massless case. For any null vector $k$, \[ T_{ab}k^ak^b=\int f(p)(p_ak^a)^2\,dp/p^0\geq0. \] This proves the null energy inequality directly whenever the integral is finite; it does not depend on dust or scalar-field analogies.
A conditional focusing estimate.
For a hypersurface-orthogonal null congruence in four dimensions with expansion $\theta$, Raychaudhuri's equation and this inequality would give $d\theta/d\lambda\leq-\theta^2/2$ along an affinely parameterized generator. If $\theta(0)<0$ and the inequality remains valid before a caustic, integration of $d(1/\theta)/d\lambda\geq1/2$ forces blowup or breakdown by affine time at most $2/|\theta(0)|$. This is a local focusing consequence; the Raychaudhuri identity is stated as an explicit geometric input, not independently rederived.
Why censorship does not follow.

The calculation neither transfers a scalar-field theorem nor proves the assertion for every spherically symmetric datum.
