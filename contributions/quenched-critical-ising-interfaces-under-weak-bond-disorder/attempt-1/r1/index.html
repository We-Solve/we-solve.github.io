Quenched critical Ising interfaces under weak bond disorder

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: For small binary ferromagnetic bond disorder, critical Ising interfaces are claimed to converge quenched in probability to chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_3$; The quenched $\mathrm{SLE}_3$ conclusion is also claimed for any fixed bounded nondegenerate mean-zero iid bond law at sufficiently small disorder strength. Together they provide the following support within the scopes described below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
On square-lattice bonds let $J_e=1+\varepsilon\xi_e$, with independent fair signs $\xi_e$. Define the physical critical inverse temperature by \[ \beta_c(\varepsilon)=\inf\left\{\beta\ge0: \mathbb E_{\mathrm{env}}\lim_{m\to\infty}\langle\sigma_0\rangle^+_{[-m,m]^2,\beta,J}>0\right\}, \] normalizing each Gibbs law before environmental averaging. Use a bounded Jordan domain with two marked boundary points and deterministic square-lattice approximations with uniformly converging marked boundary homeomorphisms. Their contour space has precisely two odd-degree dual boundary marks, completed by exterior stubs and a finite exterior contour with the same odd-degree marks. The conditional contour law is proportional to \[ \exp\left[-2\beta_c(\varepsilon)\sum_{e\in P}J_{e^\bullet}\right], \] where $e^\bullet$ crosses the dual edge $e$; bonds crossing the spin-domain boundary are included. Resolve degree four by the north-east/south-west rule and let $Q_{n,\omega}$ be the physical oriented interface law. There exists $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that for fixed $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0$ and each $\eta>0$, \[ \mathbb P_{\mathrm{env}}\{d_{\mathrm{BL}}(Q_{n,\omega},\mathrm{SLE}_3(D;a,b))>\eta\}\to0. \] The metric uses uniform curve distance modulo increasing reparametrization, and the environment remains fixed while sampling each interface. The completion satisfies $P_o\subset E\setminus E_V$, where $E_V$ comprises dual edges crossing bonds incident to the spin domain $V$. The prescribed interior contour class is required to be nonempty.
Result 2.
Let $\rho$ be a probability law supported on $[-1,1]$ with mean zero and positive variance, take independent $\xi_e\sim\rho$, and set $J_e=1+\varepsilon\xi_e$. Define \[ \beta_c^\rho(\varepsilon)=\inf\left\{\beta\ge0: \mathbb E_\rho\lim_{m\to\infty}\langle\sigma_0\rangle^+_{[-m,m]^2,\beta,J}>0\right\}. \] On deterministic filled square-lattice Jordan approximations to any bounded marked Jordan domain $(D;a,b)$, use Dobrushin signs and the north-east/south-west contour resolution. Assume mesh tends to zero and marked boundary homeomorphisms tend uniformly to the identity. There is $\varepsilon_0(\rho)>0$ such that for fixed $0<\varepsilon<\min(1,\varepsilon_0(\rho))$, \[ d_{\mathrm{BL}}(Q_{n,\omega},\mathrm{SLE}_3(D;a,b))\xrightarrow{\mathbb P_\rho}0, \qquad \beta_c^\rho(\varepsilon)=\tfrac12\log(1+\sqrt2)+O_\rho(\varepsilon^2). \] Each $Q_{n,\omega}$ uses its own Gibbs normalization and uniform oriented-curve topology. The disorder bound depends on $\rho$ but is independent of domain and approximation.

Outline of the argument

Disorder comparison is performed for normalized Gibbs laws in a fixed environment before averaging. Protected local interface estimates and control of the disorder-induced critical shift reduce the conditional curve laws to the critical reference; tightness then identifies the full SLE law.

The binary-disorder comparison is extended by controlling general bounded bond increments. Uniform critical-reference and conditional-interface estimates identify the spontaneous-magnetization threshold and show that environment-dependent errors vanish in probability for the complete curve law.
