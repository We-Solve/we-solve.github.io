Sharp Upper Bound in Yau's Conjecture on Nodal Sets of Laplace Eigenfunctions

OpenAI's claim

Power-law failure of nodal upper bounds: The manuscript claims a fixed smooth metric on $S^4\times S^1$ with eigenfunctions whose nodal four-volume exceeds the square-root scale by a fixed positive power of the eigenvalue; this rules out even an arbitrary-small-power-loss version of the proposed general upper bound.

Nodal upper length on surfaces: The paper claims nodal length at most $C\sqrt\lambda$ for every real nonzero Laplace eigenfunction on a fixed smooth closed surface; combined with the established lower estimate, this settles the two-dimensional smooth setting.

Nodal upper-bound failures in dimensions three and four: The companion claims fixed smooth metrics on $S^3$ and $S^2\times\mathbb T^2$ with unbounded nodal-measure-to-$\sqrt\lambda$ ratios along exact eigenfunction sequences; the three-sphere metric may be chosen as close to round as prescribed in the smooth topology.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
On one fixed smooth metric $g$ on $S^4\times S^1$, the claim gives $\epsilon_0>0$ and nonzero real smooth eigenfunctions $u_j$ with \[-\Delta_gu_j=\lambda_ju_j,\qquad\lambda_j\to\infty,\qquad\frac{\mathcal H_g^4(\{u_j=0\})}{\lambda_j^{1/2+\epsilon_0}}\to\infty.\] Thus even a fixed positive power beyond the square-root nodal upper scale is exceeded along this sequence.
Mathematical claim 2.
Fix a smooth metric $g$ on a closed connected surface $M$. One finite $C(M,g)$ is asserted to work simultaneously for all nonzero real eigenfunctions solving $-\Delta_gu=\lambda u$, $\lambda>0$, satisfies \[\mathcal H_g^1(\{u=0\})\le C(M,g)\sqrt\lambda.\] The constant is shared by all eigenfunctions in all positive eigenspaces; the assertion is specific to surfaces.
Mathematical claim 3.
Every $C^\infty$ neighborhood of the round metric on $S^3$ is claimed to contain a smooth metric $g_\infty$ and real eigenfunctions $u_j$ with \[-\Delta_{g_\infty}u_j=\lambda_ju_j,\quad\lambda_j\to\infty,\quad\mathcal H_{g_\infty}^2(\{u_j=0\})/\sqrt{\lambda_j}\to\infty.\] The metric is fixed for the sequence. Taking its product with a fixed circle and extending $u_j$ constantly along that factor gives the stated corresponding four-dimensional failure.

Outline of the argument

Power-law failure of nodal upper bounds: A logarithmic envelope on the four-sphere is corrugated through a number of generations proportional to the logarithm of frequency, producing a power gain in its gradient integral. Complex phase and transport jets create highly accurate localized waves beneath that envelope. Exact metric and spectral corrections then realize genuine eigenfunctions while preserving the growing nodal score on one limiting metric.

Nodal upper length on surfaces: Subharmonic profile limits control local growth under affine subdivision. High-growth excursions along grid paths produce witness regions on which growth persists to terminal scale. A packing argument pays for witness regions outside their entry squares using summable collars, bounding the terminal average growth and hence nodal length.

Nodal upper-bound failures in dimensions three and four: Each construction starts with explicit high-frequency background modes and an untouched region that anchors spectral control. Corrugated logarithmic envelopes and finite complex-wave jets create quasimodes with a stable lower score from opposite-sign tests. Metric corrections make the waves exact, and a persistence argument passes to one fixed smooth metric without losing the score.
