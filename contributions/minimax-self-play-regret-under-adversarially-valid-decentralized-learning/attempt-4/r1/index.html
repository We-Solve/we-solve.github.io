Constant-regret literature update and the remaining catalogue gap

Status checked: 17 October 2026

Target and claim

This is a literature report for ECON-C73-63, Minimax self-play regret under adversarially valid decentralized learning. No new algorithm or full solution is claimed.

Game, feedback, and regret

Fix integers $N\geq 2$ and $K\geq 2$. A game has labelled players $i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}$, action sets $A_i=\{1,\ldots,m_i\}$ with $1\leq m_i\leq K$, and payoffs $u_i:\prod_j A_j\to[0,1]$. Payoffs extend multilinearly to $\Sigma=\prod_i\Delta(A_i)$. At round $t\geq1$, all players choose mixed strategies simultaneously. After play, player $i$ receives its own exact vector $v_i^t(a)=u_i(a,x_{-i}^t)$ for every $a\in A_i$. An uncoupled update uses its own past vectors, its state, its own label and the public dimensions, without access to any player's payoff table or to precomputed equilibria. All logarithms are natural. Set \[ R_i(T)=\max_{a\in A_i}\sum_{t=1}^T \left(v_i^t(a)-\langle x_i^t,v_i^t\rangle\right), \qquad [r]_+=\max\{r,0\}. \] The comparator is one fixed action. A horizon-uniform bound concerns $\sup_T R_i(T)$, rather than $\sup_T|R_i(T)|$ or a comparator that changes actions each round. This is expected-payoff feedback and mixed-payoff regret, rather than realized regret after action sampling.

Reported advances

Liu--Farina--Ozdaglar introduce ECHO-OFTRL and report deterministic, uncoupled, horizon-independent individual regret; their dimensional rate is $O(N^{21}\log^4K)$ as compared in HOOD's introduction [echo,hood]. Abbadi--Laraki--Mertikopoulos report for HOOD \[ R_i(T)\leq45(N+1)^3(16+4\log K+\log^2K),\qquad i,T, \] in the same expected-payoff self-play model [hood]. Soleymani--Piliouras report the stronger dimensional bound [morm, Theorem 4.10] \[ R_i(T)\leq A(N,K):=96\sqrt N(2+\log K),\qquad i,T, \] with constant rate $\eta=1/(32\sqrt N)$. Their safeguarded rule leaves fixed-game self-play unchanged and has the adversarial guarantee \[ R_i(T)\leq A(N,K)+21\sqrt{T(2+\log K)}. \] These are attributed preprint theorem claims, not independently certified proofs or results originating in this contribution.

Why the existing target needs a separate admissibility argument

The catalogue fixes $C_0$ and a declared polynomial per-round budget. For each player, admissibility requires expected positive regret at most $C_0\sqrt{T\log m_i}$ against every allowed nonanticipating payoff-vector sequence. Its minimax quantity is \[ \mathcal R_{N,m}(T)=\inf_{\mathcal A\in\mathfrak L(C_0)}\sup_G \max_i\mathbb E[(R_i^{\mathcal A,G}(T))_+]. \] An adversarial rate with a dimension-dependent additive constant is not, by itself, a proof of membership in this fixed class. In particular, the displayed MORM bound does not identify the minimum prescribed $C_0$ for which that membership holds. Any use of an exact-arithmetic algorithm must also match the target's declared computational model. These requirements cannot be dropped when importing the new literature.

For illustration, using the trivial pathwise bound $R_i(T)\leq T$, \[ [R_i(T)]_+\leq \min\{T,A+21\sqrt{T(2+\log K)}\}. \] With $L_i=\log m_i>0$, $\min\{T,A\}\leq\sqrt{AT}$ implies \[ [R_i(T)]_+\leq \left(\sqrt{A/L_i}+21\sqrt{(2+\log K)/L_i}\right) \sqrt{TL_i}. \] This elementary comparison supplies a sufficient, dimension-dependent budget coefficient for the reported safeguarded bound, without proving the required computational admissibility or covering a smaller fixed $C_0$. Players with $m_i=1$ have regret zero. It is not a matching lower bound or a proof of optimality.

Existing contributions and relation

The three existing contributions were read at the pinned source revision recorded in the manifest. Attempt A1 gives an initial-information lower bound and a constant-vector specialization. A2 extends a wrapper to a persistent maximizing action and weak-dominance situations. A3 treats iterated strict dominance with game-dependent constants and discusses finite precision. Their stated scopes do not give a dimension-uniform bound over all games; none is superseded by a claim of a full solution here. This report adds the general-game preprint context.

Equilibrium consequence and scope

For $\mu_T=T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^T\bigotimes_jx_j^t$, define the nonnegative CCE gap by taking the positive part of the maximum fixed-action gain. Linearity gives \[ \operatorname{Gap}_{\rm CCE}(\mu_T) =T^{-1}\max_i[R_i(T)]_+. \] This concerns the time mixture of product distributions. It does not prove last-iterate Nash convergence, constant realized-action regret, or the Markov-game target ECON-C73-113. The stochastic-game payoff target ECON-C73-1 is also a different question.
