The all-temperature pressure of orthogonally invariant Ising disorder

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Haar-orthogonal Ising spin glasses without spectral outliers are claimed to have an all-temperature variational pressure formula, with admissible deterministic fields and a zero-temperature ground-energy limit.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $\Lambda_N$ be deterministic real diagonal matrices, with empirical eigenvalue measures tending weakly to a compactly supported probability law $\mu$ and extreme eigenvalues tending to the edges of its support. For Haar $U_N\in O(N)$, define \[ P_N=N^{-1}\log\left(2^{-N}\sum_{\sigma\in\{-1,1\}^N} e^{\sigma^{\mathsf T}U_N^{\mathsf T}\Lambda_NU_N\sigma/2}\right). \] Write $\lambda_+=\max\operatorname{supp}\mu$. For $x>0$, choose $b(x)>\lambda_+$ solving $\int(b(x)-\lambda)^{-1}\mu(d\lambda)=x$ when such a solution exists, and otherwise set $b(x)=\lambda_+$. Put $R(x)=b(x)-1/x$ and $R(0)=\int\lambda\,\mu(d\lambda)$. Let $\mathcal P$ consist of nondecreasing measurable $p:(0,1)\to[0,1]$, and set \[ D_p(r)=1-r-\int_0^1(p(s)-r)_+\,ds,\qquad G_1(p)=\frac12\int_0^1R(D_p(r))\,dr. \] The entropy term is defined as follows. For a finite nonnegative nondecreasing step field $h$, with values $h_i$ on $\zeta_i<u<\zeta_{i+1}$, $0=\zeta_0<\cdots<\zeta_k=1$, set $h_{-1}=0$ and \[ T_{a,v}f(z)=\begin{cases}a^{-1}\log\mathbb E e^{af(z+\sqrt vG)},&a>0,\\ \mathbb Ef(z+\sqrt vG),&a=0,\end{cases}\quad G\sim N(0,1). \] Starting with $F_{k-1}(z)=\log\cosh z$, recursively put $F_{i-1}=T_{\zeta_i,h_i-h_{i-1}}F_i$ for $i=k-1,\ldots,0$. Then \[ \ell(h)=F_{-1}(0)-h_{k-1}/2,\qquad S(p)=\sup_h\left(\ell(h)+\frac12\int_0^1p(u)h(u)\,du\right). \] At each fixed temperature, absorbed into $\Lambda_N$, the limiting pressure satisfies \[ \lim_N\mathbb EP_N=\lim_NP_N=\inf_{p\in\mathcal P}\{S(p)+G_1(p)\}, \] with the second limit almost sure, without requiring independence across $N$.

Outline of the argument

Finite spectral alphabets permit spectral-coordinate overlap identities and cascade calculus. Concavity of the Ising field functional and rotation identities give matching variational bounds; continuity extends the result to compact spectral laws and first-moment field limits, and temperature passage yields ground energy.
