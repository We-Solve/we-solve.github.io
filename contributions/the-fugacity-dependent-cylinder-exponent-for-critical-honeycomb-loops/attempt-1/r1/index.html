The fugacity-dependent cylinder exponent for critical honeycomb loops

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: Critical honeycomb loop partition exponents are claimed at every positive fugacity; fugacity two gives cylinder exponent $1/6$ and planar nesting exponent $1/12$; Signed propagation is claimed to recover the fugacity-two nesting exponents and macroscopic first-exit chord mass and length laws, together with a squared-polygon-length bound. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
On the balanced honeycomb cylinder of even circumference parameter $N$, sum over finite vertex-disjoint polygon families separating its two prescribed markers. With $\rho=(2+\sqrt2)^{-1/2}$, set $Z_N(\chi)=\sum_{\mathcal P}\prod_{P\in\mathcal P}\chi\rho^{|P|}$, including the empty family. For each fixed $\chi>0$, the source claims \[ Z_N(\chi)=N^{\tau(\chi)+o(1)},\qquad \tau(\chi)=\begin{cases} \frac16-\frac{2}{3\pi^2}\arccos(\chi/2)^2,&0<\chi\le2,\\ \frac16+\frac{2}{3\pi^2}\operatorname{arcosh}(\chi/2)^2,&\chi\ge2. \end{cases} \] Separation includes winding loops and contractible loops enclosing exactly one marker. Use the quotient of the embedded honeycomb plane by the translation $iN\sqrt3/2$, with $N=2m$. The two markers are $0$ and $m e^{2\pi i/3}$.
Result 2.
For the honeycomb cylinder polygon partition $G_m(g)$, each separating loop has weight $g x^{|\lambda|}$, $x=(2+\sqrt2)^{-1/2}$, with vertex-disjoint loop families. Along even $m$, the source asserts \[ G_m(2)=m^{1/6+o(1)},\qquad -\left.\frac{d^2}{dg^2}\log G_m(g)\right|_{g=0}=O(\log m), \] where the latter counts weighted ordered incompatible cycle pairs, including self-pairs. Locally uniformly for complex $\Theta$ with $|\operatorname{Re}\Theta|<\pi$, \[ |G_m(2\cos\Theta)|\le m^{\operatorname{Re}(1/6-2\Theta^2/(3\pi^2))+o(1)}. \] For disjoint polygons surrounding the central face of a regular side-$R$ hexagon, fugacity two gives $\mathcal Z(R)=R^{1/12+o(1)}$. The cylinder identifies horizontal coordinates modulo $2m$, and its markers are $(0,0)$ and $(m,0)$, with $m$ even.

Outline of the argument

Contour sectors are centered and compared with a coercive fluctuation spectrum. Compensated quadratic integration and an electric contour integral continue the imbalance sum, determining cylinder growth; winding pressure and separating-loop bounds transfer it to planar nesting.

Boundary-balance identities define a two-puncture spin observable. Angular transfer and bosonization control the horizontal gas, including possible Jordan blocks, while a Pfaffian normalization fixes the physical amplitude; a separate tilted two-bond estimate gives polygon moment control.
