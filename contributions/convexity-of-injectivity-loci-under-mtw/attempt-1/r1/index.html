Convexity of Injectivity Loci Under MTW

OpenAI's claim

Weak MTW support and injectivity-domain convexity: On a compact smooth manifold, weak MTW curvature is claimed to imply convexity of every tangent injectivity domain; the paper also gives global supporting mountains for ordinary subgradients of squared-distance potentials, without a density assumption.

Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW: For a fixed compact weak-MTW manifold, the manuscript claims one Hölder modulus for optimal maps and their inverses over all density pairs with common positive lower and finite upper bounds; no density smoothness or absence of conjugate cut points is assumed.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $(M,g)$ be smooth, connected, compact and boundaryless with $\dim M\ge2$. Set $c(x,y)=d(x,y)^2/2$ and $I(x)=\{v\in T_xM:d(x,\exp_x(av))=a|v|\text{ for some }a>1\}$. Assume the weak MTW inequality \[-\frac32\left.\frac{\partial^4}{\partial s^2\partial t^2}c(\exp_x(t\xi),\exp_x(v+s\eta))\right|_{s=t=0}\ge0\quad(v\in I(x),\ \langle\xi,\eta\rangle=0).\] Then the claimed conclusion is $v_0,v_1\in I(x)\Longrightarrow(1-\theta)v_0+\theta v_1\in I(x)$ for every $0\le\theta\le1$. No strict MTW bound, nonfocality or prior convexity is assumed.
Mathematical claim 2.
On a smooth connected compact boundaryless $n$-manifold, $n\ge2$, use $c=d^2/2$ and the weak MTW assumption $-\tfrac32\partial_s^2\partial_t^2c(\exp_x(t\xi),\exp_x(v+s\eta))|_0\ge0$ for every $v$ in the open pre-cut domain and $\xi\perp\eta$. Fix $0<\lambda\le\Lambda<\infty$ such that normalized densities $\lambda\le\rho_i\le\Lambda$ exist. There are $0<\alpha\le1,C<\infty$, depending only on $(M,g),\lambda,\Lambda$, such that the almost-everywhere optimal map for $c$ from $\rho_0dV$ to $\rho_1dV$ has a homeomorphic representative $T$ with \[d(Tx,Tx^\prime)\le Cd(x,x^\prime)^\alpha,\qquad d(T^{-1}y,T^{-1}y^\prime)\le Cd(y,y^\prime)^\alpha.\] The two densities are merely measurable, have total mass one and obey the bounds almost everywhere. Its geometric convexity result also asserts convexity of every open tangent injectivity domain.

Outline of the argument

Weak MTW support and injectivity-domain convexity: Proper radial shortening gives smooth distance supports and strict time monotonicity of the branch Hessian. The full ordinary-subgradient graph is represented locally by convex combinations of all active minimizing logarithms. At the first scale where support could fail, limiting convex hulls remain minimizing; a conjugacy-kernel analysis and the radial Hessian identity rule out the alleged failure.

Uniform bi-Hölder transport under weak MTW: Lifted gap sections of transport potentials are studied in the tangent injectivity domain. Convexity turns a uniform power estimate on dual-potential oscillation into a diameter estimate on their endpoint images. Taking the supremum over all admissible densities gives one class-wide bound, and comparison at nearby poles supplies Hölder continuity in both transport directions.
