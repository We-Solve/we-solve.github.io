Lee–Huang–Yang Upper Bound for Hard-Sphere Bosons

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

A hard-sphere Bose gas is described by symmetric wave functions vanishing whenever two particle centers are closer than the hard-core distance. Let $a$ be the scattering length and $\rho=N/L^3$ the density. Its thermodynamic energy density is $e(\rho)=\lim E_0(N,L)/L^3$ at fixed $\rho$, followed by the dilute limit $\rho a^3\to0$. Units are chosen so that the kinetic operator is $-\Delta$.

Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For a dilute hard-sphere Bose gas in the thermodynamic limit, prove the second-order upper bound matching the Lee–Huang–Yang expansion $e(\rho)=4\pi a\rho^2\left(1+128(\rho a^3)^{1/2}/(15\sqrt\pi)+o((\rho a^3)^{1/2})\right)$.
Scope and source qualification.

Truncated two-body scattering test.
For hard-core radius $a>0$ and cutoff $b>a$, consider the radial correlation \[ f_b(r)=0\ (r\leq a),\qquad f_b(r)=\frac{1-a/r}{1-a/b}\ (a<r<b),\qquad f_b(r)=1\ (r\geq b). \] On the annulus its derivative is $a/(r^2(1-a/b))$; consequently \[ \int_{\mathbb R^3}|\nabla f_b|^2 =\frac{4\pi a^2}{(1-a/b)^2}\int_a^b r^{-2}dr =\frac{4\pi a}{1-a/b}. \] The relative truncation excess over $4\pi a$ is $a/(b-a)$.
Testing a simple dilute-pair strategy.
Write $\delta=\rho a^3$. A strategy that requires the truncation error to be $o(\sqrt\delta)$ forces $b/a\gg\delta^{-1/2}$. A strategy simultaneously treating the correlation balls as rarely overlapping requires $\rho b^3=o(1)$, or $b/a\ll\delta^{-1/3}$. The requirements are incompatible as $\delta\to0$: the first already makes $\rho b^3\gg\delta^{-1/2}$. This is a contradiction between the two proposed error controls, not a lower bound on all Jastrow trial states.
