Rigidity and Assembly Conjectures for Discrete Groups

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: The Bass trace restriction is claimed for complex group rings of arbitrary discrete groups, implying triviality of scalar idempotents for torsion-free groups over characteristic-zero domains; A hyperbolic group with two-sphere boundary is claimed to act geometrically on hyperbolic three-space with finite kernel; Two homotopy-equivalent closed aspherical four-manifolds with hyperbolic fundamental group are claimed nonhomeomorphic, with an additional nongeometric self-homotopy equivalence. Together they provide the following support within the scopes described below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For an arbitrary discrete group $G$, let $\operatorname{HS}_G$ be the Hattori--Stallings trace, whose value at a conjugacy class sums the coefficients on that class. For every $x\in K_0(\mathbb C[G])$, \[ |g|=\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\operatorname{HS}_G(x)([g])=0. \] The torsion-free corollary states that, for every $q\ge1$ and idempotent $e\in M_q(\mathbb C[G])$, its identity-coefficient trace equals the rank of its augmentation matrix: \[ \tau_{G,q}(e)=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb C}(\epsilon_{G,q}(e)). \] For such $G$, any commutative unital domain $R$ of characteristic zero satisfies \[ e\in R[G],\ e^2=e\quad\Longrightarrow\quad e\in\{0,1\}. \] The characteristic-zero qualification does not cover positive-characteristic group rings.
Manuscript 2.
Let $G$ be word-hyperbolic with Gromov boundary $\partial G\cong S^2$. The source claims a homomorphism \[ \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^3) \] whose action on hyperbolic three-space is proper and cocompact, and whose kernel is finite. With the additional assumption that $G$ is torsion-free, the corollaries identify $G\cong\pi_1(M)$, where $M$ is closed and has a hyperbolic metric in dimension three, and supply a finite-index subgroup \[ H\cong\pi_1(\Sigma)\rtimes\mathbb Z, \] where $\Sigma$ is a closed connected orientable surface. They also assert residual finiteness, virtual compact specialness, and positive virtual first Betti number in that torsion-free case.
Manuscript 3.
The construction claims two closed connected aspherical topological four-manifolds $M,N$ with \[ M\simeq N,\qquad M\not\cong N\text{ by homeomorphism},\qquad \pi_1M\cong\pi_1N\text{ word-hyperbolic}. \] Thus homotopy equivalence and hyperbolicity do not force topological homeomorphism for this claimed four-dimensional pair.

Outline of the argument

An idempotent is encoded by finite cyclic path configurations whose weighted deletion relations generate cycles. Exterior differential forms and a cyclic Pfaffian identity detect forbidden conjugacy support; trace-rank arguments then turn the complex trace conclusion into the stated idempotent consequence.

The argument applies a boundary modulus criterion. Unbounded modulus produces scalar limits analyzed by exponential records or subexponential probability flows; comparing same-depth expansions forces the required measure control and the three-dimensional hyperbolic action.

Reflected cyclic covers realize the relevant symmetries while preserving asphericity. A parity-chamber construction and cyclic-cover criteria distinguish manifold models despite the common homotopy type, and the realized symmetry gives the self-equivalence obstruction.
