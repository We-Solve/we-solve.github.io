Stability of Atomic Ground-State Quantum Numbers

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For nuclear charge $Z>0$ and $N\ge1$ electrons, the source uses the nonrelativistic atomic Hamiltonian in atomic units, \[ H_{N,Z}=\sum_{i=1}^N\left(-\tfrac12\Delta_{x_i}-\frac{Z}{|x_i|}\right)+ \sum_{1\le i<j\le N}\frac1{|x_i-x_j|}, \] on antisymmetric wavefunctions in $L^2((\mathbb R^3\times\{\uparrow,\downarrow\})^N)$. The nucleus is fixed in the Born--Oppenheimer approximation. Total orbital angular momentum, total spin, and parity commute with this Hamiltonian. Their labels on symmetry eigenspaces are $L,S,p$, with eigenvalues $L(L+1)$ and $S(S+1)$ for the squared angular-momentum operators. The isoelectronic parameter $N$ remains fixed while $Z$ decreases from the large-charge regime to $N$, the neutral value. Ground-state dimension is the multiplicity of the bottom eigenvalue; the source compares its large-Z symmetry labels with those of neutral atoms.
Source scope and qualifications.
The quantum numbers obtained theoretically refer to the electron numbers and ion sequences analyzed in the source. This is not a warrant to assert constancy for every N with an unspecified starting eigenspace. Crossings and changes in multiplicity are precisely what the required uniform continuation must rule out.

A finite-dimensional gap criterion.
Let a compact symmetry group act unitarily on a finite-dimensional space, and let $H_0$ commute with it. Assume its ground eigenspace is a single irreducible representation, separated from the rest by gap $\Delta>0$. For a symmetry-preserving self-adjoint perturbation $V$ with $\|V\|<\Delta/2$, the eigenvalue variational characterization gives shifts of at most $\|V\|$ along $H_s=H_0+sV$, $0\leq s\leq1$. Hence the original ground cluster stays isolated with gap at least $\Delta-2\|V\|$.
Why the representation is unchanged.
Its spectral projections $P_s$ vary continuously: integrate $(z-H_s)^{-1}$ over a contour enclosing the isolated cluster locally in $s$, and use the resolvent identity. For nearby parameters, $\|P_s-P_t\|<1$, so $P_t$ restricted to $\operatorname{ran}P_s$ is injective and, by equal finite rank, bijective. It intertwines the group action. Chaining these local isomorphisms shows the ground cluster carries the same irreducible representation throughout. Any self-adjoint operator commuting with an irreducible representation is scalar, since each eigenspace would otherwise be a nontrivial invariant subspace. Thus the cluster cannot split internally and remains the ground multiplet with unchanged labels and dimension.
The atomic obstruction.
This argument identifies the needed protection against level crossings, but the actual Coulomb perturbation under changing $Z$ is unbounded and no uniform gap estimate down to $Z=N$ has been proved here. Large-$Z$ labels alone supply neither bound. The irreducibility hypothesis also matters if an initial ground space is a sum of symmetry types. Extending a certified isolated-multiplet continuation across the full source ion sequence is the first remaining gap.
