Open Structures in Compactified Imaginary Liouville Theory

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

Imaginary Liouville theory uses an imaginary exponential coupling of a scalar field, with parameter $\beta$. Compactification identifies field values periodically. A logarithmic conformal theory may have nonsemisimple dilatation action and logarithmic correlations. CILT refers to the compactified imaginary Liouville theory defined in the report. BPZ minimal models are conformal field theories with central charge $c=1-6(p-q)^2/(pq)$ for coprime integers $p>q$. Felder's BRST reduction extracts the minimal-model state spaces from Coulomb-gas representations.

The renormalization threshold in a local test.
Assume a regularized real Gaussian field on a bounded planar region has covariance $C_\epsilon(x,y)$ approaching $-\log|x-y|+O(1)$ off the diagonal. For $Z_\epsilon(x)=\exp(i\beta X_\epsilon(x)+\beta^2C_\epsilon(x,x)/2)$, direct Gaussian integration gives \[ \mathbb E\left[Z_\epsilon(x)\overline{Z_\epsilon(y)}\right] =\exp(\beta^2 C_\epsilon(x,y)). \] The candidate second-moment kernel is therefore comparable locally to $|x-y|^{-\beta^2}$. Its radial integral near the diagonal is $2\pi\int_0^1r^{1-\beta^2}dr$, finite exactly when $\beta^2<2$, logarithmically divergent at equality. This diagnoses why a plain second-moment construction stops at $\sqrt2$; it does not rule out other renormalizations beyond that point. Rationality of $\beta^2$ plays no role in this local integrability test.
Jordan cells and logarithms.
If a dilatation operator has a block $H=hI+N$ with $N^2=0$ but $N\ne0$, then for $r>0$ \[ r^{-H}=r^{-h}\left(I-(\log r)N\right). \] This is the exact truncated exponential series. A correlation sees a logarithm only if its chosen bra and ket couple to $N$. Non-self-adjointness alone proves no such block: $\operatorname{diag}(i,2i)$ is non-self-adjoint and diagonalizable.
