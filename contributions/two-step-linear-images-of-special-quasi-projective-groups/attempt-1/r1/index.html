Two-step linear images of special quasi-projective groups

OpenAI's claim

Every complex linear representation of a smooth special quasi-projective fundamental group is claimed to have virtually two-step-nilpotent image, while the paper also constructs nonspecial general quasi-Albanese fibers. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For a smooth complex quasi-projective $V$, specialness requires the following after any proper birational modification: each dominant algebraic fibration onto a normal quasi-projective base $Z$ with $\dim Z>0$ and connected general fiber has $\kappa(Z,\Delta_f)<\dim Z$, where $\Delta_f$ denotes the induced orbifold boundary. The orbifold dimension is computed on smooth compactifications with infimum multiplicities, and removed divisors have infinite multiplicity. For every connected such $V$, every $d\ge1$ and every $\rho:\pi_1(V)\to\operatorname{GL}_d(\mathbb C)$, OpenAI claims \[ \exists H\le\rho(\pi_1V):\quad[\rho(\pi_1V):H]<\infty,\qquad[H,[H,H]]=1. \] Thus every complex linear image is virtually nilpotent of class at most two. Virtual abelianity of the entire open fundamental group is not the stated conclusion.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 057, 2026-09-24): At each canonical nilpotent stage, the fiber Lie algebra equals the commutator algebra. Its abelianization has only weight minus two, making the next conjugation bracket incompatible with the allowed weights; universality then transfers the resulting class-two bound to arbitrary unipotent representations.
