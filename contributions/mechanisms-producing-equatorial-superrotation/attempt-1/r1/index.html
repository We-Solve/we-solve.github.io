Mechanisms Producing Equatorial Superrotation

Definitions and conventions.
Equatorial superrotation is an eastward equatorial mean flow with axial angular momentum exceeding the local solid-body reference. Planetary-scale heating forces the large-scale atmospheric circulation; upscale momentum flux transfers momentum from finer-scale fluctuations to the mean flow. In the report's balanced first-baroclinic calculation, heating amplitude $S$ and momentum-flux amplitude $F$ produce a near-equatorial meridional momentum flux proportional to $-ySF e^{-y^2}$.

The source flux in a damped mean-flow balance.
Take the reported near-equatorial flux $F_m(y)=-C SF\,y e^{-y^2}$ with $C>0$, heating amplitude $S$ and upscale-flux amplitude $F$. In the deliberately reduced balance $\partial_tU=-\partial_yF_m-rU$, where $r>0$ is linear drag, differentiation gives \[ -\partial_yF_m=C SF(1-2y^2)e^{-y^2},\qquad U(0,t)=e^{-rt}U(0,0)+\frac{CSF}{r}(1-e^{-rt}). \] For zero initial equatorial flow, the sign is exactly the sign of $SF$ for every $t>0$. The forcing converges momentum toward the equator for $SF>0$ and is westward outside $|y|>1/\sqrt2$ in this nondimensional coordinate. Its integral over the entire real line is zero because $F_m$ decays at both ends: the mechanism redistributes momentum rather than creating net zonal momentum in this model.
Counterchecks.
If either amplitude is zero, the model produces no equatorial acceleration. If $r=0$, the correct limit is $U(0,t)=U(0,0)+CSFt$, not a finite steady state. A westward initial flow can stay westward for a finite time despite $SF>0$, so positive forcing and existing superrotation are distinct assertions.
Full-source obstruction.
The sign test is sufficient only within the chosen closed balance. Mean meridional transport, vertical fluxes and other torques can alter it, and deriving $S,F,C$ from the coupled atmospheric equations is not done. Hence $SF>0$ is not established as a universal necessary feature of planetary heating or superrotation. The first gap is a controlled derivation and closure of the full angular-momentum budget for the regime in question.
