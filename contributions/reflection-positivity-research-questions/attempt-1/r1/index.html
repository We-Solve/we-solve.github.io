Reflection-Positivity Research Questions

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
In its basic form, reflection positivity concerns a Hilbert space $\mathcal E$, a unitary involution $\theta$ and a subspace $\mathcal E_+$ such that $\langle\theta v,v\rangle\geq0$ for $v\in\mathcal E_+$. Quotienting by the null space produces a physical Hilbert space. Osterwalder--Schrader axioms describe Euclidean correlation functions; Wightman axioms describe relativistic fields. A Borchers triple consists of an operator algebra, translation representation and vacuum with compatible wedge-locality properties. Super versions incorporate a $\mathbb Z_2$ grading.

A common positive-kernel mechanism.
Let $\nu$ be a finite positive measure on $[0,\infty)$ and define $C(t)=\int e^{-m|t|}d\nu(m)$. For positive times $t_i$ and complex coefficients $c_i$, reflection in time produces \[ \sum_{i,j}\overline c_i c_j C(t_i+t_j) =\int\left|\sum_jc_je^{-mt_j}\right|^2d\nu(m)\geq0. \] Thus the reflected kernel is positive, and its null vectors are precisely combinations whose Laplace-polynomial representative vanishes $\nu$-almost everywhere. The quotient pre-Hilbert space embeds isometrically in $L^2(\nu)$ by sending a time symbol to $m\mapsto e^{-mt}$. For a single mass it is one-dimensional; several masses give multiple directions.
Positivity of the measure matters.
For two distinct masses with weights $w_1>0>w_2$, the determinant of the reflected $2\times2$ matrix at distinct positive times is \[ w_1w_2\left(e^{-m_1t_1-m_2t_2}-e^{-m_1t_2-m_2t_1}\right)^2<0. \] So a signed or complex spectral weight is not a harmless replacement of the positive one.
Limits of the common core.
This kernel calculation does not construct graded massive fields, settle antiunitary involutions, reconstruct Borchers triples or AQFTs, or remove OS--Wightman growth/regularity assumptions. Diffeomorphism and noncommutative examples, complex measures, stochastic quantization, number/string/conformal links, multiparticle cluster spectra, modular/BMV connections, Lax--Phillips normal forms, absent Euclidean Hilbert spaces and multiple-involution duality remain separate source directions. The first gap is extending this elementary kernel positivity to the required field/domain/reconstruction structures without assuming the omitted regularity.
