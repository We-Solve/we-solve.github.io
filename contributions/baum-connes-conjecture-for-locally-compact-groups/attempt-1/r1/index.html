Baum–Connes Conjecture for Locally Compact Groups

OpenAI's claim

Manuscript 1 (family 285, 2026-09-23): For one finitely generated group without torsion, the coefficient-free reduced assembly map annihilates an infinite-order degree-zero K-homology class; this is claimed to refute rational injectivity. Manuscript 2 (family 285, 2026-09-23): OpenAI claims the existence of a nontrivial scalar projection inside the reduced C*-algebra of a finitely generated torsion-free group, contradicting the Kadison--Kaplansky assertion. Manuscript 3 (family 285, 2026-09-23): An irrational-trace scalar projection is constructed for a finitely generated discrete group, yielding a K_0 class that reduced Baum--Connes assembly with trivial coefficients cannot reach.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
OpenAI claims a discrete group, torsion-free and finitely generated, $G$, an injection $i:\mathbb Z^2\hookrightarrow G$, and $c\in H^2(BG;\mathbb Z)$ such that \[ \langle(Bi)^*c,[T^2]\rangle=1,\qquad i_*\beta=0\text{ in }K_0(C_r^*(G)), \] here $\beta\in K_0(C_r^*(\mathbb Z^2))$ is the Bott generator chosen to have rank zero. The class $h=(Bi)_*[D_{T^2}]\in K_0(BG)$ has infinite order but satisfies $\mu_G^r(h)=0$. Here $\mu_G^r:K_*(BG)\to K_*(C_r^*(G))$ is reduced coefficient-free analytic assembly and $[D_{T^2}]$ is the oriented torus spin-Dirac class. This asserts failure of reduced assembly injectivity without coefficients, even rationally.
Mathematical claim 2.
OpenAI claims a discrete group, torsion-free and finitely generated, $G$ whose reduced group $C^*$-algebra contains a scalar projection $e=e^*=e^2$ with \[ 0<\tau_G(e)<\tfrac12,\qquad\tau_G(x)=\langle x\delta_1,\delta_1\rangle. \] Thus $e$ is neither $0$ nor $1$. This concerns a scalar projection in the operator-norm completion of the left regular group algebra, and supplies a counterexample to the torsion-free projection assertion. The manuscript also uses the integral trace restriction on reduced assembly for torsion-free groups to place this projection class outside the assembly image.
Mathematical claim 3.
For some finitely generated discrete group $G$, OpenAI claims a projection $b_0$ in its reduced group $C^*$-algebra satisfying \[ \tau_G(b_0)\notin\mathbb Q,\qquad [b_0]\notin\mu_0^G\left(K_0^G(\underline EG)\right)\subset K_0(C_r^*(G)). \] Here $\tau_G$ is the canonical trace, $\underline EG$ the universal proper $G$-space and $\mu_0^G$ reduced coefficient-free analytic assembly. The obstruction uses the rational trace of every assembly class. No torsion-free hypothesis is asserted for this group.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 285, 2026-09-23): The construction embeds a torus subgroup with an integral cohomology detector while arranging disappearance of its Bott class analytically. Operators assembled on almost disjoint Cayley-graph sheets retain controlled spectral supports; estimates for the leftover forest permit the required homotopies in the reduced completion. A finite witness then restricts the construction to a finitely generated subgroup.

Manuscript 2 (family 285, 2026-09-23): A magnetic tower model produces a separated spectral band carrying a matrix projection of positive trace below one half. Graph sampling and small-cancellation recovery preserve this band after the model is represented on the group, with intersection losses bounded by trace estimates. A free-product compression converts the matrix projection into a scalar one while retaining torsion-freeness.

Manuscript 3 (family 285, 2026-09-23): Finite-field rules generate graph components on which a group-ring operator has kernels of explicitly counted pattern probabilities. Expander estimates separate those kernels from the remaining spectrum, putting their projection in the reduced C*-algebra rather than merely the von Neumann completion. Its irrational trace conflicts with the rational trace values available to assembly classes.
