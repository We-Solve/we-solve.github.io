Metric k-median strictly below factor two

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 125; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that metric $k$-median admits exact-budget recovery from nearly complete proxy anchors, and a compatible bounded-price argument yields an absolute factor below two on arbitrary rational metrics.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For finite rational metric $k$-median with clients $D$, facilities $F$, and $1\le k\le|F|$, define $\operatorname{cost}(S)=\sum_{j\in D}d(j,S)$ and $\operatorname{opt}_k$ as its minimum over $|S|\le k$. The claim gives an absolute $\sigma>0$ such that for every fixed $a>0$ a randomized polynomial-bit-time algorithm always returns a feasible $S$ and satisfies \[ \Pr[\operatorname{cost}(S)\le(2-\sigma)\operatorname{opt}_k] \ge1-(|D|+|F|+2)^{-a}. \] It can additionally achieve $\mathbb E\operatorname{cost}(S)\le(2-\sigma)\operatorname{opt}_k$. Polynomial constants may depend on fixed parameters.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 125; 2026-09-24), sampled balls are completed using a partition-constrained submodular maximization that separates destinations without sampled proxies. A stability dichotomy combines recovery with one certified surplus execution, paying for removals while opening no more than the original facility budget.
