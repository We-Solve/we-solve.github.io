Griffiths Positivity Conjecture for Ample Vector Bundles

OpenAI's claim

On the quadric surface, a family of rank-two ample bundles is claimed to admit no smooth strictly Griffiths-positive metric for all sufficiently large coordinatewise power pullbacks. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
On $S=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ let $P=\mathcal O_S(1,1)$ and let $f_m$ raise homogeneous coordinates in both factors to the $m$th power. OpenAI claims a rank-two algebraic bundle $G$ such that \[ E_m=f_m^*G\otimes P\text{ is ample for every }m\ge1, \] while for every sufficiently large $m$, no smooth Hermitian metric on $E_m$ has strictly Griffiths-positive Chern curvature. Strict positivity means positive curvature in each nonzero tangent and fiber direction. The single fixed $G$ produces all members of the sequence.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 050, 2026-09-24): Compatible graph identifications use finite-order transformations for gluing but an expanding product for the obstruction. Explicit symmetric-power sections certify ampleness; a limiting positive-metric argument would produce a measure invariant under the graph transformations, contradicting the expanding dynamics.

Formal verification

For family 050, the scope page documents the following selections. The selected power-pullback family is ample in every degree but lacks a smooth strictly positive Griffiths metric beyond a threshold. The additional even-exponent very-ampleness certificate is not selected. The documented statement covers the main claim of manuscript 1.
