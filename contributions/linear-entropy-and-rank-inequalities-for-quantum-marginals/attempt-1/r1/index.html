Linear Entropy and Rank Inequalities for Quantum Marginals

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a finite-dimensional density operator $\rho$, the marginal on subsystem $X$ is $\rho_X=\operatorname{Tr}_{X^c}\rho$. Its von Neumann entropy is $S(X)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_X\log\rho_X)$, with $0\log0=0$, and its rank is $r_X=\operatorname{rank}\rho_X$. Linear entropy inequalities are linear combinations of the values $S(X)$; log-rank inequalities are linear combinations of $\log r_X$. The source's entropy subquestion concerns Yeung--Zhang inequalities and their higher-order extensions, originally known for Shannon entropy.

A pure-state rank test.
Let $|\psi\rangle$ be a unit vector on finite-dimensional $A\otimes B\otimes C$. If $P_A,P_B$ project onto the supports of its marginals, then \[ \|((I-P_A)\otimes I)\psi\|^2=\operatorname{Tr}((I-P_A)\rho_A)=0, \] and similarly for $B$. Hence $\rho_{AB}$ is supported in $\operatorname{ran}P_A\otimes\operatorname{ran}P_B$, giving $r_{AB}\le r_A r_B$. Across a bipartition the coefficient matrix of a pure vector yields reduced matrices $TT^*$ and the transpose of $T^*T$; they have the same nonzero eigenvalues, by mapping a positive-eigenvalue vector with $T$ or $T^*$. Therefore \[ r_{AB}=r_C,\qquad r_{AC}=r_B,\qquad r_{BC}=r_A, \] so the source inequality $r_{AB}\le r_{AC}r_{BC}$ holds in this pure tripartite subcase.
Boundary and scope.
All ranks are at least one for normalized states; product vectors give equality. The same coefficient-matrix argument gives equal complementary von Neumann entropies for pure states, but does not supply the named Yeung--Zhang family. A tripartite reduction of a four-party mixed or entangled state need not be pure, so complement equalities used above cannot simply be inserted into the full problem. The supplied statement records existing progress on the rank question; this elementary subcase is not claimed as new. A general mixed-state argument and the full four-party entropy/log-rank region remain outside this attempt.
