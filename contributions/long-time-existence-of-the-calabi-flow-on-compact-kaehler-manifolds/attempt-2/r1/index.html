Long-Time Existence of the Calabi Flow on Compact Kaehler Manifolds

OpenAI's claim

Finite-time Calabi-flow singularity: The manuscript claims a smooth Kähler metric in the standard class on $\mathbb {CP}^{10}$ whose scalar curvature becomes unbounded during Calabi evolution before a finite time. Thus the failure can occur in a class that also contains a constant-scalar-curvature metric.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
The claimed initial Kähler form $\omega_0$ lies in the Fubini--Study class on $\mathbb{CP}^{10}$, is smooth, and is invariant under $U(10)$ whose maximal smooth Calabi flow has finite lifespan $0<T_*<\infty$. At some fixed point $p$ and with $a>0$, \[S(\omega(t))(p)\sim a(T_*-t)^{-1/2}\quad(t\uparrow T_*).\] Here the flow is $\partial_t\omega=i\partial\bar\partial S(\omega)$, and $S(\omega)=\operatorname{tr}_{\omega}\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)$, using the complex Kähler trace convention. This refutes universal smooth long-time existence.

Outline of the argument

Finite-time Calabi-flow singularity: A radial momentum ansatz reduces the geometric flow to a fourth-order scalar equation with smooth endpoint conditions. An exact shrinking solution of the flat model is constructed first. A correction on the compact projective manifold preserves the shrinking mechanism and the positivity of the metric, giving the finite-time curvature singularity.
