Affine–Virasoro Derivation of Nekrasov Blow-Up Identities

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

Write $\Psi(a,m;w,q;\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$ for the instanton partition function with surface defect and $Z(a,m;q;\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)$ for the function without that defect. Here $a$ is the Coulomb parameter, $m$ the four masses, $w$ the defect parameter, $q$ the gauge coupling, and $\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2$ the deformation parameters. The source blow-up relation reads \[ \Psi(a,m;w,q;\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z} \Psi(a+\epsilon_1n,m;w,q;\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2-\epsilon_1) Z(a+\epsilon_2n,m;q;\epsilon_1-\epsilon_2,\epsilon_2). \] An affine--Virasoro identity decomposes affine Lie-algebra representations into factors involving Virasoro representations.

A formal normalization obstruction.
Test the displayed blow-up formula under the common but potentially incorrect convention that each instanton-only factor has expansion $1+O(q)$, independent of the shifted integer $n$. Then every summand on the right has constant coefficient one. An infinite sum of formal power series is defined coefficientwise only when each coefficient receives finitely many nonzero contributions, or when an additional convergence structure is supplied. Here the putative constant coefficient is \[ \sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}1, \] so that normalization cannot make the displayed identity meaningful in the ordinary formal power-series ring. Classical factors, lattice-sector prefactors or a different summation convention must be retained. For example exponents $q^{c_n}$ with $c_n\to+\infty$ make coefficientwise local finiteness possible, although they do not prove the identity.
Parameter countercheck.
From the stated relation, with $\epsilon_2\ne0$, $k\ne-2$, algebra gives \[ \frac{\epsilon_1}{\epsilon_2}=-\frac1{k+2}. \] Thus finite $k$ also requires $\epsilon_1\ne0$. The exceptional $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2$ corresponds to $k=-3$ and $b^2=0$, where generic conformal-block expressions may require separate limiting treatment.
