Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $\Omega^n$, $n\ge3$, be smooth, connected and orientable, with nonempty compact smooth boundary $S$, complete with that boundary included, and exactly one end, with Euclidean coordinates and compact complement. The tensors $g,K$ extend smoothly to $S$. With $\tau=\operatorname{tr}_gK$, use \[ 2\mu=R_g+\tau^2-|K|_g^2,\qquad J=\operatorname{div}_g(K-\tau g),\qquad \mu\ge|J|_g,\quad\mu,|J|_g\in L^1(\Omega,dV_g). \] The finite ADM fluxes use $\omega_{n-1}=|\mathbb S^{n-1}|$ and \[ E=\frac1{2(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|x|=R} (\partial_jg_{ij}-\partial_ig_{jj})n_\delta^i\,dA_\delta,\quad P_i=\frac1{(n-1)\omega_{n-1}}\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{|x|=R}(K_{ij}-\tau g_{ij})n_\delta^j\,dA_\delta. \] Orient boundary normals towards the end, with $\theta_+=H+\operatorname{tr}_SK$. Here $O_j(r^{-b})$ controls coordinate derivatives through order $j$, of respective sizes $O(r^{-b-i})$. Choose a connected smooth end-side region $D\subset\Omega$, closed in $\Omega$, containing the distant end and having its manifold interior in $\operatorname{int}\Omega$. A full cut consists of all of its intrinsic boundary $\Gamma=\partial D$; require this boundary to be compact, smoothly embedded and two-sided. All components and portions coinciding with $S=\partial\Omega$ count. Put $a_g(S)=\inf_\Gamma\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)$. Suppose $g-\delta=O_6(r^{-q})$, $K=O_5(r^{-1-q})$, $(n-2)/2<q<n-2$, and $E>|P|$. Require connected $S$, $\theta_+(S)=0$, and $\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)\ge A=|S|_g>0$ for every full cut. Exclude every compact smooth embedded two-sided full enclosing hypersurface wholly in $\operatorname{int}\Omega$ with $\theta_+\le0$, allowing disconnected excluded surfaces. If \[ m=\sqrt{E^2-|P|^2}=\frac12(A/\omega_{n-1})^{(n-2)/(n-1)}, \] then the entire original pair $(g,K)$, including the boundary and future normal, is induced by a smooth spacelike embedding into Schwarzschild--Tangherlini of mass $m$, using its regular extension across the future horizon. The interior lies in its domain of outer communication, the end reaches spatial infinity, and $S$ maps to a full future-horizon section or the bifurcation sphere. Conversely, admissible spacelike model slices with these same intrinsic hypotheses attain equality and have $A=\omega_{n-1}(2m)^{(n-1)/(n-2)}$. Time-symmetric and nonzero-$K$ examples exist in each $n\ge3$. In the three-dimensional complete-data equality theorem, use a smooth connected orientable boundaryless $M$ with complete metric, compact core and finitely many AF ends, each obeying $O_2/O_1$ decay with $q>1/2$, finite ADM limits, integrable densities and DEC. Take $S=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{\ell}S_i$, $1\le\ell<\infty$, with disjoint connected compact smooth two-sided components bounding a connected closed exterior $\Omega$ with exactly the designated end. Require $\theta_+(S_i)=0$ and $\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)\ge A=\sum_i|S_i|_g>0$ for every full enclosing cut. Exclude distinct enclosing cuts whose components each coincide with an entire $S_i$ or lie wholly outside, with at least one component of the latter kind, and with $\theta_+\le0$ on all components. If $E>|P|$ and $\sqrt{E^2-|P|^2}=\sqrt{A/(16\pi)}$, then $\ell=1$, $S\cong S^2$, and the entire original exterior admits a proper smooth spacelike Schwarzschild embedding of mass $\sqrt{E^2-|P|^2}$ inducing $g,K$ and the prescribed future normal. Its end reaches spatial infinity and its boundary is a full future-horizon section or bifurcation sphere, with smooth continuation through that boundary. For connected $S$, the connected-horizon corollary replaces the cut exclusion by exclusion of every wholly exterior weakly future-trapped enclosing surface, including disconnected ones. Thus $\ell\ge2$ forces strict inequality in the finite-component subclass.

Outline of the argument

Minimizing enclosures are treated through their area envelope. Saturation yields a constrained minimum in the original data, whose static rigidity equations produce the spacetime embedding and smooth horizon attachment; in three dimensions the analysis also rules out equality for disconnected horizons.
