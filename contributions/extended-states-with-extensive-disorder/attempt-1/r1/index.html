Extended States with Extensive Disorder

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. For uniform iid Anderson site disorder in every fixed dimension at least three, sufficiently small fixed disorder is claimed to give purely absolutely continuous spectrum on a nonempty open interval.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For independent $V_x$ uniformly distributed on $[-1,1]$, consider \[ (H_{\lambda,\omega}\psi)(x)=-\sum_{j=1}^d[\psi(x+e_j)+\psi(x-e_j)]+\lambda V_x\psi(x) \] on $\ell^2(\mathbb Z^d)$. The claimed fixed constant $e_2\in(0,1/100)$ and thresholds $\lambda_d>0$ give, for each $d\ge3$ and $0<\lambda<\lambda_d$, almost surely \[ I_d\subset\sigma(H_{\lambda,\omega}),\qquad \chi_{I_d}(H_{\lambda,\omega})\ell^2(\mathbb Z^d)\subset\mathcal H_{\mathrm{ac}}(H_{\lambda,\omega}), \] where \[ I_3=(-6+e_2/2,-6+e_2),\qquad I_d=-2d+(1/200,1/100)\quad(d\ge4). \] The projection is nonzero; the assertion is purely absolutely continuous spectrum on these windows, for this uniform-distribution model.

Outline of the argument

A finite-volume matrix-state certificate is propagated through controlled ordinary sweeps. Local trials and clearing of pending components maintain the comparison forms needed for resolvent bounds, which identify an interval with nonzero absolutely continuous weight.
