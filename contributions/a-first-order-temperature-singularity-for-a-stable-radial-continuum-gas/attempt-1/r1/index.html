A first-order temperature singularity for a stable radial continuum gas

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: A stable three-dimensional radial pair potential with a divergent core and integrable tail is claimed to exhibit one finite-temperature canonical derivative jump throughout a positive density interval; A bounded continuous stable radial potential with decay $O(r^{-3-1/32})$ is claimed to give the same common-temperature first-order singularity over an open density interval. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
There exists a radial Borel pair potential $\Phi(x)=\phi(|x|)$ on $\mathbb R^3$ with $\phi(0)=+\infty$, finite locally bounded values on $(0,\infty)$, and \[ \phi(r)\to+\infty\ (r\downarrow0),\quad \phi(r)\le-a\ (r\in[r_1,r_2]),\quad \int_R^\infty r^2|\phi(r)|\,dr<\infty,\quad \phi(r)=o(r^{-3}), \] for suitable $a>0$, $0<r_1<r_2$ and finite $R$. Its interaction satisfies $U_N=\sum_{i<j}\Phi(x_i-x_j)\ge-BN$ on every finite configuration. For free cubes $\Lambda_L=[-L/2,L/2]^3$, let \[ Z_{L,N}(\beta)=\frac1{N!}\int_{\Lambda_L^N}e^{-\beta U_N}\,dx_1\cdots dx_N,\qquad f_\Phi(\beta,\rho)=-\beta^{-1}\lim_{L\to\infty}L^{-3}\log Z_{L,\lfloor\rho L^3\rfloor}(\beta). \] This limit exists and is finite for every $\beta,\rho>0$. For some nonempty open density interval $I$ and a single $\beta_c\in(1/2,3/2)$, \[ D^-_\beta f_\Phi(\beta_c,\rho)>D^+_\beta f_\Phi(\beta_c,\rho)\quad(\rho\in I), \] with finite one-sided derivatives. Position integration uses temperature-independent Lebesgue measure.
Result 2.
A second construction gives a bounded continuous radial pair potential $\phi$ on $\mathbb R^3$ and finite $B,C>0$ satisfying \[ \sum_{i<j}\phi(|x_i-x_j|)\ge-BN,\qquad |\phi(r)|\le Cr^{-3-1/32}\quad(r\ge1). \] Let $p=\lim_{L\to\infty}L^{-3}M_L$, where $M_L$ is the maximum number of points in $[0,L]^3$ with pairwise separation at least one. For the free-cube partition function \[ Z_{L,N}^\phi(\beta)=\frac1{N!}\int_{([0,L]^3)^N} e^{-\beta\sum_{i<j}\phi(|x_i-x_j|)}\,dx_1\cdots dx_N, \] the limit $f(\beta,\rho)=-\beta^{-1}\lim_LL^{-3}\log Z_{L,\lfloor\rho L^3\rfloor}^\phi(\beta)$ is finite for every $\beta>0$ and every $\rho$ in an open interval $I$ centred at $5p/3$. At a common $\beta_c\in[7/8,9/8]$, \[ f'_-(\beta_c,\rho)>f'_+(\beta_c,\rho)\quad(\rho\in I), \] with both derivatives finite.

Outline of the argument

Four-particle cluster bounds give stability and canonical thermodynamic limits. Successive Kac-type attractions preserve a triple of supporting slopes; perturbation estimates keep the slope separation while the range grows, yielding one potential and one common singular temperature.

Packing bounds control cell occupations and ensure stability of the bounded core. Long-range attraction is compared quantitatively with an ideal density transform, and pressure supports are preserved through the range iteration; the chosen scales produce the explicit algebraic tail.
