A uniform gap in the periodic spin-one antiferromagnetic chain

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The pure spin-one antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chain is claimed to have a size-independent positive gap on even periodic rings.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For spin-one matrices satisfying $[S^a,S^b]=i\varepsilon_{abc}S^c$, $\sum_a(S^a)^2=2I$, use the even periodic ring \[ H_L=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}\boldsymbol S_j\cdot\boldsymbol S_{j+1},\qquad\boldsymbol S_L=\boldsymbol S_0. \] Let $\gamma_L$ be the difference between its first two distinct energies. The source claims a unique ground state for even $L\ge60$, and \[ \gamma_L>\frac4{105}\log\frac{80}{79}\quad(L\ge60),\qquad \gamma_L>\frac{\log20}{784}\quad(L\ge2304), \] for even lengths. Therefore $\liminf_{L\to\infty,\ L\text{ even}}\gamma_L\ge\log20/784>0$. Simultaneously similar realizations with the same spin normalization have the same spectral conclusion.

Outline of the argument

Auxiliary twists express partition functions as spatial moments. Two purity comparisons yield a gap criterion, and two finite initializations start an iterative improvement that makes the criterion uniform in ring size.
