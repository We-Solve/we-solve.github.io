Exact consequence of a defect intertwiner.
Suppose $H_L,H_R$ are finite-dimensional self-adjoint Hamiltonians and $D$ is a map with $DH_L=H_RD$. Induction gives $DH_L^n=H_R^nD$; the norm-convergent exponential series then gives \[ De^{-itH_L}=e^{-itH_R}D. \] If $D^*D=P$ is a projection and $P\psi=\psi$, then $\|D\psi\|=\|\psi\|$. Taking the adjoint intertwining relation shows $P$ commutes with $H_L$, so this norm-preserving subspace is dynamically invariant. Neither statement requires $D$ to be an invertible symmetry on the full Hilbert space.
