Hyperkähler SYZ (Lagrangian Fibration) Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

On a compact Kähler IHS manifold, a holomorphic line is claimed to be semiample whenever its Chern class is nonzero, nef, and isotropic, regardless of deformation type. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $X$ be an irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold, required to be compact and Kähler, of complex dimension $2n$, $n\ge1$. Write $q_X$ for its Beauville--Bogomolov form and $\overline{\mathcal K_X}$ for the closed Kähler cone. For a holomorphic line bundle $L$ with nonzero $c_1(L)\in\overline{\mathcal K_X}$ and $q_X(c_1(L))=0$, OpenAI claims semiampleness and, more precisely, \[ L^m\simeq f^*A,\qquad f:X\twoheadrightarrow B,\qquad\dim_{\mathbb C}B=n, \] with $m>0$, connected fibers, normal projective $B$ and ample $A$. Every fiber component has dimension $n$ and the holomorphic symplectic form vanishes on its smooth locus. A separate corollary claims finitely many smooth deformation types in each dimension $2n$ among these manifolds with $b_2\ge5$.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 041, 2026-09-23): The prescribed lattice vector is retained through a degeneration that produces a special Lagrangian torus. Rotation makes that torus holomorphic on a nonprojective deformation, where the torus-fibration theorem supplies semiampleness; the fixed-class deformation result brings the conclusion back to the original manifold.
