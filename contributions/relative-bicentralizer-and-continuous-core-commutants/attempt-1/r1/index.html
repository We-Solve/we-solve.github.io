Relative bicentralizer and continuous-core commutants

OpenAI's claim

An expected inclusion with separable preduals is claimed to contain an expected amenable subalgebra of its lower algebra preserving the relative commutant inside the upper algebra's continuous core. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For a unital inclusion $N\subset M$ of von Neumann algebras whose preduals are separable and a conditional expectation that is faithful and normal $E_N:M\to N$, OpenAI claims an injective (amenable) unital $P\subset N$ and conditional expectation that is faithful and normal $F:N\to P$ satisfying \[ P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M). \] The continuous core is $c(M)=M\rtimes_{\sigma^\psi}\mathbb R$ for a faithful normal state $\psi$, with the canonical embedding of $M$. Equality is inside this whole core, not only inside $M$.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 290, 2026-09-23): Absolute bicentralizer reduction establishes multiplication isometries through modular averaging and a spectral contradiction. In the relative setting, completely positive packaging of long words gives estimates uniform in tensor length and modular shifts. Repeated randomized comparisons would exhaust the available norm if an error remained, identifying the core fixed algebra and invoking the amenable-subalgebra criterion.
