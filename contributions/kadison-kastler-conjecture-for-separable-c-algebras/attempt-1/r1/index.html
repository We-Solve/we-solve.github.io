Kadison–Kastler Conjecture for Separable \$C^*\$-Algebras

OpenAI's claim

Manuscript 1 (family 289, 2026-10-05): Arbitrarily close, norm-separable unital C*-algebras with a shared von Neumann closure are claimed to exist without any unitary conjugacy, on one separable complex Hilbert space. Manuscript 2 (family 289, 2026-09-23): For any prescribed small unitary displacement, the paper claims one uniform Kadison--Kastler tolerance guaranteeing conjugacy of close unital von Neumann algebras on any complex Hilbert space.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every $\varepsilon>0$, OpenAI claims a separable complex Hilbert space $H$ and norm-separable unital $C^*$-algebras $A,B\subset B(H)$ with the same identity such that \[ d_{\mathrm{KK}}(A,B)<\varepsilon,\qquad A''=B'',\qquad u Au^*\ne B\text{ for every unitary }u\in B(H). \] Here $d_{\mathrm{KK}}$ is the Hausdorff distance between norm unit balls, and $A'',B''$ are von Neumann closures. This is the norm-separable $C^*$-algebra spatial-conjugacy assertion; it does not contradict the separate von Neumann theorem.
Mathematical claim 2.
For every $\varepsilon>0$, OpenAI claims a single $\delta(\varepsilon)>0$ valid for every complex Hilbert space $H$ and all unital von Neumann algebras $M,N\subset B(H)$ with common identity: if their norm-unit-ball Hausdorff distance $d(M,N)<\delta$, then \[ \exists u\in B(H):\quad u^*u=uu^*=I_H,\quad uMu^*=N,\quad\|u-I_H\|<\varepsilon. \] No separability, type or injectivity hypothesis is imposed on these von Neumann algebras.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 289, 2026-10-05): Close free-group deformations yield two inclusions distinguished by a relative tensor-norm identity. A convergent-sequence algebra turns this positional difference into an invariant under every spatial conjugacy: central coordinate projections force permutations and coordinatewise normalizers. The tensor identity survives such normalizers and finite-family norm limits, ruling out conjugacy despite the small gap.

Manuscript 2 (family 289, 2026-09-23): For finite factors, free-word estimates control amplification while boundary averaging corrects nearby unitaries into a measurable cocycle. Double ergodicity makes the resulting correspondence multiplicative and yields a spatial implementation. Properly infinite algebras are handled through matrix amplification and modular preparation; alignment of centers and limits over separable restrictions assemble the universal conclusion.
