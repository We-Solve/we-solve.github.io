Generic C2 future inextendibility near rotating subextremal Kerr

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: Near each fixed rotating subextremal two-ended Kerr bridge, a dense $G_\delta$ class of smooth vacuum data is claimed to have no future $C^1$ extension; In the same near-Kerr setting, data permitting a continuous nondegenerate future extension with locally square-integrable weak connection are claimed meagre; Near every fixed rotating subextremal Kerr bridge, data with future $C^2$-extendible full developments are claimed meagre, without a symmetry restriction. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Fix a complete two-ended Kerr bridge datum $d_*=(h_*,K_*)$ on $\Sigma\cong\mathbb R\times S^2$, of mass $M>0$ and subextremal nonzero rotation $\mathfrak a$. With a smooth end-radius weight $\rho\ge1$, define \[ p_m(u,v)=\sum_{j=0}^m\left(\sup_\Sigma\rho^{1+j}|\nabla_*^ju|_*+ \sup_\Sigma\rho^{2+j}|\nabla_*^jv|_*\right). \] The data space $\mathcal D$ consists of smooth complete positive-definite $h$, with all $p_m(d-d_*)$ finite, satisfying \[ R(h)+(\operatorname{tr}_hK)^2-|K|_h^2=0,\qquad \operatorname{div}_hK-d(\operatorname{tr}_hK)=0. \] Use the relative topology of all these seminorms and $\mathcal U_\varepsilon=\{d:p_{10}(d-d_*)<\varepsilon\}$. An extension has a smooth time-oriented isometric embedding of the full maximal globally hyperbolic development with image open and strictly contained in a connected Lorentzian four-manifold, and a future timelike $C^1$ curve that exits its image at its endpoint. The ambient extension need not satisfy the vacuum equations or global hyperbolicity. For $0<\mathfrak a<M$, the theorem claims some $\varepsilon_0>0$ and a dense $G_\delta$ subset $\mathcal G\subset\mathcal U_{\varepsilon_0}$ whose data have no such future extension with nondegenerate $C^1$ metric. This is Baire genericity in the stated near-Kerr topology.

An extension has a smooth time-oriented isometric embedding of the full maximal globally hyperbolic development with image open and strictly contained in a connected Lorentzian four-manifold, and a future timelike $C^1$ curve that exits its image at its endpoint. The ambient extension need not satisfy the vacuum equations or global hyperbolicity. For $0<|\mathfrak a|<M$, the source claims an $\varepsilon>0$ for which \[ \{d\in\mathcal U_\varepsilon:\text{a future extension with }\widetilde g\in C^0\cap W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}\text{ exists}\} \text{ is meagre}. \] The weak metric is continuous and nondegenerate, and its chart components have square-integrable distributional first derivatives. Its weak Christoffel coefficients are locally $L^2$.

No symmetry condition is imposed on the perturbation.

Outline of the argument

Closed transport tests reduce arbitrary exits to selected interior branches. Exact constrained signed packets are propagated nonlinearly through a double-null region and attached to the full development; the observing geodesic records an obstruction to continuously differentiable extension.

Countably many finite-branch tests cover arbitrary weak exits. Exact-data preparation and a nonlinear double-null comparison produce curvature signals along matched observing states; averaged holonomy detects the weak-connection obstruction and yields the category conclusion.

Closed finite-observation tests are coupled to microlocally separated Kerr packets. Stationary gauges and causal modulation control propagation across compact and far regions, then the two-ended interior estimates produce curvature behavior incompatible with a twice differentiable extension.
