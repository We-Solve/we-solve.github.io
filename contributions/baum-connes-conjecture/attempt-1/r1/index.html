Baum-Connes Conjecture

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\underline EG$ be a classifying space for proper $G$-actions: fixed-point sets for compact subgroups are contractible and those for noncompact subgroups are empty. Topological $K$-theory here is the compact-support/direct-limit equivariant $K$-homology of $\underline EG$. The reduced group $C^*$-algebra $C_r^*(G)$ is the operator-norm completion of convolution operators in the left regular representation. The assembly map is \[ \mu_G:K_*^{\rm top}(G)\longrightarrow K_*(C_r^*(G)),\qquad *=0,1. \] The conjecture says it is an isomorphism for every second-countable locally compact $G$. This is the coefficient-free target; adding arbitrary coefficient algebras changes the conjecture.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Does geometric equivariant $K$-homology compute the operator-algebraic $K$-theory of every group in the stated class?
