Uniform Rectifiability from Bounded Riesz Transforms

OpenAI's claim

Higher-codimension Riesz rectifiability: The claimed result turns uniform $L^2$ control of all positive hard truncations of the Riesz transform into quantitative uniform rectifiability. It concerns $n$-regular measures in $\mathbb R^d$ with $d\ge4$ and $2\le n\le d-2$.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $d\ge4$ and $2\le n\le d-2$. Suppose a Radon measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb R^d$ satisfies $C^{-1}r^n\le\mu(B(x,r))\le Cr^n$ for $x\in\operatorname{supp}\mu$ and $0<r\le\operatorname{diam}(\operatorname{supp}\mu)$. If \[R_{\mu,\epsilon}f(x)=\int_{|x-y|>\epsilon}\frac{x-y}{|x-y|^{n+1}}f(y)\,d\mu(y),\qquad\sup_{\epsilon>0}\|R_{\mu,\epsilon}\|_{L^2(\mu)\to L^2(\mu;\mathbb R^d)}\le C_R,\] then there are $\theta>0$ and $M<\infty$, depending only on $d,n,C,C_R$, such that each admissible $B(x,r)$ contains at least $\theta r^n$ of $\mu$ on an $M$-Lipschitz image of the radius-$r$ ball in $\mathbb R^n$. This is uniform $n$-rectifiability; pointwise principal values are not assumed.

Outline of the argument

Higher-codimension Riesz rectifiability: After a flat blowup, the normal height is renormalized by the initial excess so that relative improvement can be detected. A fractional-energy test yields compactness and a limiting equation whose integrability forces the height to be affine. Dyadic packing then counts failures of excess improvement, leading to large Lipschitz pieces at every support scale.
