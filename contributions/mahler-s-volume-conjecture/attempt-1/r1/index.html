Mahler’s Volume Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 087; 2026-09-22), the asserted result is that general convex bodies satisfy the simplex lower bound $(n+1)^{n+1}/(n!)^2$ for their centered volume product, with simplex equality; In manuscript 2 (family 087; 2026-09-22), the asserted result is that origin-symmetric convex bodies in dimension $n$ have volume product at least $4^n/n!$, with equality precisely for linear images of Hanner polytopes.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For a full-dimensional convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$, let $s(K)$ be its Santaló point, the minimizer of $|(K-z)^{\circ}|$ over interior $z$, and define $P(K)=|K|\,|(K-s(K))^{\circ}|$. The claimed nonsymmetric Mahler bound is \[ P(K)\ge\frac{(n+1)^{n+1}}{(n!)^2}\quad(n\ge1), \] Equality characterizes simplices among these convex bodies. No central-symmetry hypothesis is imposed; the polar is taken after Santaló centering.
Manuscript 2.
In each dimension $n\ge1$, take any convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$ with $K=-K$. Define $K^{\circ}=\{y:x\cdot y\le1\text{ for all }x\in K\}$. The manuscript claims \[ |K|\,|K^{\circ}|\ge\frac{4^n}{n!},\qquad |K|\,|K^{\circ}|=\frac{4^n}{n!}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ K\text{ is a linear Hanner body}. \] Here Hanner bodies are built from one-dimensional symmetric intervals by successive $\ell_1$ and $\ell_{\infty}$ sums, and a linear Hanner body is an invertible linear image of one. The bound and the complete equality classification hold in every dimension.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 087; 2026-09-22), lifting the body to a cone converts polarity into paired Laplace integrals. Biased projection coordinates and an entropy inequality control their product, and change of variables returns the geometric lower bound and rigidity.

For manuscript 2 (family 087; 2026-09-22), a functional inequality is obtained from a conformal-lens construction and a real simplex identity. Feasible bases and a common equality witness identify the extremizers, while approximation transfers the sharp estimate from polytopes to arbitrary bodies.
