Recovering Spatial Tensor Systems by Intersection

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a unital coefficient algebra $B$, a Hilbert $B$-bimodule is a right Hilbert $B$-module with a compatible left action. $\mathcal B^a(E)$ denotes its adjointable operators. A pointed free product system $(E_t,\omega_t)$ has central reference vectors and associative identifications $E_s*E_t\simeq E_{s+t}$, using the source's reduced free product over $B$. A tensor product subsystem instead has $E_s\odot E_t\simeq E_{s+t}$ with $\odot$ the internal tensor product over $B$. An $E_0$-semigroup is a continuous semigroup of unital $*$-endomorphisms. A unit is a section whose values multiply according to the product-system structure. Starting with a spatial tensor product system $E^\odot$ and its reference unit, the source forms free products over time decompositions and takes an inductive limit to obtain a generated free product system $E^*$. Recovery asks whether intersecting all embedded tensor products at fixed total time gives exactly the original $E^\odot$, rather than a larger tensor subsystem.
Source scope and qualifications.
Generation by the starting system is a theorem of the construction in this report. Recovering the starting tensor system by intersection, and characterizing free systems admitting that description, are the unresolved questions. This reverses a construction, whereas ID 30001178 asks generation inside a given free system.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Given a spatial tensor product subsystem $E^{\odot}$ of a free product system, is $E^{\odot}$ recovered by the intersection procedure over all decompositions of each time parameter? More generally, characterize when a free product system can be obtained in this way from a spatial tensor product subsystem.
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The report gives a positive construction in a spatial setting but leaves open when the intersection procedure recovers the original tensor product system in general. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Determine when constructing a free product system from a spatial tensor system and then intersecting tensor products recovers that original system.

A Hilbert-space intersection reduction.
Suppose a countable cofinal family of time refinements gives decreasing closed candidate subspaces $F_1\supset F_2\supset\cdots$ of a fixed Hilbert space, and let $P_n$ be their orthogonal projections. For $m\geq n$, $P_nP_m=P_m$, so \[ \|P_n\xi-P_m\xi\|^2=\|P_n\xi\|^2-\|P_m\xi\|^2. \] The decreasing nonnegative norms converge, making $P_n\xi$ Cauchy. Its limit belongs to every $F_n$, and the residual is orthogonal to their intersection. Thus $P_n$ converges strongly to the projection onto $\bigcap_nF_n$.
A usable equality criterion.
If the starting tensor fiber $E_t$ is contained in every $F_n$, equality $\bigcap_nF_n=E_t$ would follow from a uniform bound \[ \|P_n\xi\|\leq\epsilon_n\|\xi\|\quad(\xi\perp E_t),\qquad\epsilon_n\to0. \] Indeed a vector in the intersection and orthogonal to $E_t$ is fixed by every projection, forcing it to vanish. Mere containment gives no such estimate: taking every $F_n=E_t\oplus\mathbb C v$ leaves a permanent extra vector in the intersection.
Scope and obstruction.
This provides a concrete norm-decay route, not the requested recovery theorem. The actual source is a Hilbert-module construction, where closed submodules need not be orthogonally complemented, and even the scalar case requires cofinality and a control on reduced-word components. Those hypotheses have not been established. Generation of the free system by the starting tensor system, already in the source, does not imply decay of unwanted vectors under intersection. Recovery and the broader characterization of free systems obtainable this way both remain unresolved.
