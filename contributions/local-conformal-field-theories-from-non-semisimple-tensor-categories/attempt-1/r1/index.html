Local Conformal Field Theories from Non-Semisimple Tensor Categories

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A finite tensor category is a rigid abelian tensor category with finite-length and finite-dimensionality properties; nonsemisimple means objects need not split into simple summands. Chiral data describe one holomorphic sector of a two-dimensional conformal theory. A bulk object combines the two chiral sectors. Sewing compatibility requires correlators to agree under gluing surfaces; mapping-class-group invariance requires compatibility with the topological symmetries of those surfaces.

For a nonsemisimple finite tensor category $\mathcal C$, do full local conformal field theories with chiral data $\mathcal C$ exist, and how can their bulk objects and sewing-compatible, mapping-class-group-invariant correlators be constructed?

A nonsemisimple sewing-algebra test.
Let $A=\mathbb C[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ and define $\tau(1)=0$, $\tau(\epsilon)=1$. The bilinear pairing $B(a,b)=\tau(ab)$ has matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ in the basis $1,\epsilon$, so is nondegenerate although the algebra is not semisimple. Associativity gives $B(ab,c)=B(a,bc)$; therefore the elementary four-point contractions determined by multiplication and this pairing have the same value $\tau(abcd)$ in the two parenthesizations.
A concrete handle calculation.
The dual basis to $1,\epsilon$ is $\epsilon,1$, so the algebraic handle element is $e=1\epsilon+\epsilon1=2\epsilon$. Hence $e^2=0$, $\tau(e)=2$, and $\tau(e^g)=0$ for $g\geq2$. These are internally consistent finite algebraic contractions. With the involution fixing $\epsilon$, however, the form $\tau(a^*a)$ is not positive: for $a=1-\epsilon$ it equals $-2$. Algebraic gluing identities alone therefore do not produce a positive Hilbert realization.
What this does and does not obstruct.
Logarithmic conformal theories need not be unitary, so lack of this positivity is not a no-go theorem for the source. Conversely, a nondegenerate Frobenius pairing is not a construction of analytic local conformal correlators with prescribed chiral category. One still needs braided/nondegenerate categorical data, bulk objects, all sewing identities and mapping-class-group compatibility, plus analytic field control. The first gap is upgrading such algebraic candidates to the full source realization without silently adding hypotheses to every finite tensor category.
