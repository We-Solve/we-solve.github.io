Logarithmic correspondence-coloring bound for fixed forbidden cliques

OpenAI's claim

Correspondence coloring with a forbidden clique: For each fixed $r\ge4$, the paper claims correspondence chromatic number $O_r(\Delta/\log\Delta)$ in graphs with no $K_r$ subgraph and large maximum degree. Ordinary and list-coloring versions follow, and fixed forbidden subgraphs are also covered.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every fixed integer $r\ge4$ there are $C_r>0,\Delta_r\ge3$ with the following implication for a finite simple undirected graph $G$, writing $\Delta=\Delta(G)$: if $\Delta\ge\Delta_r$ and no $K_r$ occurs, then \[\chi_{\mathrm{DP}}(G)\le\left\lceil C_r\frac\Delta{\log\Delta}\right\rceil.\] The DP, or correspondence, chromatic number permits a matching of forbidden color pairs across each edge rather than identical color names. The stated corollary replaces $K_r$-freeness by noninduced $F$-freeness for any fixed finite graph $F$, with constants depending on $F$. It does not prescribe the optimal leading constant or strong edge-coloring bounds.

Outline of the argument

Correspondence coloring with a forbidden clique: Random partial-coloring rounds act directly on the graph of available list entries and forbidden matching pairs. Weights track total list supply, individual probabilities, and conflicting edge mass. An entropy-and-mass potential pays for losses when neighboring vertices are colored, including local adjustments for triangles, until the residual lists can be completed.
