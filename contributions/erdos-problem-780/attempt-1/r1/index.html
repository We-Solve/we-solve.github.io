Erdős Problem #780: disjoint edges in one colour

Statement and outcome

Let the $r$-subsets of $[n]$ be coloured with $t$ colours. If \[ n\geq kr+(t-1)(k-1), \] must there be $k$ pairwise disjoint $r$-sets of one colour? Yes. We reconstruct a consensus-division proof for prime $k$, then give an elementary product reduction and the sharp lower construction. Attempt status: solved relative to the explicitly stated consensus-division theorem.

The topological input

We use the following consensus-division theorem for a prime $p$. Given $t$ real-valued functions on measurable subsets of $[0,1]$, each uniformly continuous for the metric \[ d(E,F)=\mu(E\mathbin{\triangle}F), \] there is a partition into $p$ pieces, each a union of intervals, with at most $(p-1)t$ cuts, such that every function takes the same value on every piece. The functions need not be additive measures. This theorem is an external input, not proved here.

Prime number of disjoint edges

Suppose $k=p$ is prime and the colouring is $c:\binom{[n]}r\to[t]$. Divide $[0,1]$ into $n$ equal open cells $I_j=((j-1)/n,j/n)$, ignoring endpoints of measure zero. For each colour $i$, define on $[0,1]^n$ \[ u_i(x)=\max_{\substack{B\in\binom{[n]}r\\c(B)=i}}\ \min_{j\in B}x_j, \] with an empty maximum equal to zero. The finite maximum of these coordinate minima is $1$-Lipschitz for the supremum norm. For measurable $E$, set \[ x_j(E)=n\mu(E\cap I_j),\qquad v_i(E)=u_i(x(E)). \] Then $|v_i(E)-v_i(F)|\leq n\mu(E\triangle F)$, so the theorem applies.

Let $P_1,\ldots,P_p$ be the resulting pieces. At most $(p-1)t$ open cells contain a cut. Every remaining cell lies, up to endpoints, wholly in a single piece. The hypothesis gives \[ n-(p-1)t\geq p(r-1)+1. \] By pigeonhole some piece contains at least $r$ whole cells, indexed by a set $B$. If $c(B)=i$, then $v_i(P_s)=1$ for that piece, and equality of values gives $v_i(P_j)=1$ for every $j$. By the definition of the finite maximum, each $P_j$ therefore contains full measure in all cells indexed by an $r$-set $B_j$ of colour $i$. The $B_j$ are pairwise disjoint: a cell of positive measure cannot lie with full measure in two disjoint pieces. These are the required $p$ edges.

An elementary multiplication step

Denote the assertion for $k$ by $P(k)$, including all positive integers $r,t$. Suppose $P(a)$ and $P(b)$ hold. Given a colouring of $r$-sets with \[ n\geq ab\,r+(t-1)(ab-1), \] put $R=ar+(t-1)(a-1)$. Colour each $R$-subset $Y$ by one colour in which $Y$ contains $a$ disjoint original $r$-sets; this is possible by $P(a)$. Now \[ bR+(t-1)(b-1)=ab\,r+(t-1)(ab-1). \] By $P(b)$, there are $b$ pairwise disjoint $R$-sets bearing the same new colour. Their internal $a$-matchings together give $ab$ pairwise disjoint original edges of that colour. Thus $P(ab)$ follows. Factoring any $k\geq2$ into primes proves the assertion; $k=1$ is immediate.

Sharpness

For \[ n=kr-1+(t-1)(k-1), \] partition $[n]$ into $X_1$ of size $kr-1$, and $X_2,\ldots,X_t$ each of size $k-1$. Colour an $r$-set by the least $i\geq2$ whose $X_i$ it meets, if such an $i$ exists; otherwise use colour 1. A matching of colour 1 lies in $X_1$, too small for $k$ disjoint $r$-sets. A matching of colour $i\geq2$ has at most $|X_i|=k-1$ members, since every edge meets $X_i$ and the edges are disjoint. This proves the exact threshold.

Attribution and scope

The result is the Alon--Frankl--Lovász theorem. The prime-case reconstruction follows I. Haviv, The Chromatic Number of Kneser Hypergraphs via Consensus Division, arXiv:2311.09016v2, Theorem 2.4 and Section 3, https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09016v2. The consensus-division theorem stated there is due to Filos-Ratsikas and collaborators. Its hypotheses, the discrete extraction, multiplication step and sharp colouring are all explicitly checked here. Statement checked at https://www.erdosproblems.com/780 on 24 September 2026.
