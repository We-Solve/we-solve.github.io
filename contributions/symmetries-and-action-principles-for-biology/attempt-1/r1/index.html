Symmetries and Action Principles for Biology

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
A symmetry is a transformation preserving a model’s governing equations or relevant observables. An action principle selects trajectories by stationarity of a functional, commonly an integral of a Lagrangian. Robustness concerns persistence under perturbations; modularity concerns interacting subsystems; evolvability concerns accessible heritable changes; variability concerns differences among states or individuals. These concepts require model-specific definitions before a theorem can be posed.

Testing whether an action principle constrains a model.
Let $F:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d$ be smooth. Any differential equation $\dot x=F(x)$ can be embedded in an action principle by introducing an auxiliary variable $p\in\mathbb R^d$: \[\mathcal A[x,p]=\int_0^T p(t)\cdot(\dot x(t)-F(x(t)))\,dt.\] With fixed endpoints for $x$ and unrestricted interior variations, integration by parts gives the Euler--Lagrange equations $\dot x=F(x)$ and $\dot p=-DF(x)^Tp$. In particular every original trajectory lifts with $p=0$. Thus mere existence of an action of this kind is unable to distinguish robust from fragile biological dynamics.
A concrete robustness comparison.
In one dimension take $F_-(x)=-x$ and $F_+(x)=x$. Both admit the preceding action and both commute with the sign symmetry $x\mapsto-x$. Their trajectories, however, satisfy $|x_-(t)|=e^{-t}|x_-(0)|$ and $|x_+(t)|=e^t|x_+(0)|$. For $\dot x=-x+u(t)$, variation of constants yields \[|x(t)|\le e^{-t}|x(0)|+(1-e^{-t})\|u\|_{L^\infty(0,t)},\] whereas a constant nonzero input to $\dot x=x+u$ produces exponential amplification. The same formal symmetry and multiplier construction therefore coexist with incompatible stability properties.
First obstruction to the programme.
The auxiliary action has no supplied biological interpretation or positive cost, and its stationarity supplies no modularity, heritability or evolvability criterion. To turn variational language into an explanatory constraint, one must restrict admissible variables and actions and relate their coefficients to observable biology. The explicit comparison rules out the attempted inference from unrestricted stationarity and symmetry to robustness. It does not refute the broad source objective, and the other named traits remain unaddressed. This is a methodological counterexample, with no claim of new biological theory or fresh source verification.
