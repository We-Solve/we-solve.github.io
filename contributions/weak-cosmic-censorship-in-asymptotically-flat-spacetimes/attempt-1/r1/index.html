Weak Cosmic Censorship in Asymptotically Flat Spacetimes

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
Asymptotic flatness describes an isolated gravitating system whose far-field geometry approaches Minkowski space in the prescribed sense. Future null infinity is the asymptotic endpoint of outgoing null rays. Weak cosmic censorship asserts, in an appropriate generic initial-data class, that singularities formed by evolution are hidden from distant observers; completeness of future null infinity is a standard precise ingredient. The black-hole region is the complement of the causal past of future null infinity.

An exact exterior escape calculation.
For the four-dimensional Schwarzschild exterior with parameter $M>0$, take \[ ds^2=-f(r)dt^2+f(r)^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2,\qquad f(r)=1-2M/r,\quad r>2M. \] For a radial null geodesic the conserved quantity $E=f\dot t>0$, together with the null equation, gives $\dot r=E$ on the outgoing branch; the dot denotes an affine parameter derivative. Hence $r=r_0+E(\lambda-\lambda_0)$, and reaching arbitrarily large radius requires an unbounded affine interval. Coordinate time obeys \[ t-t_0=r-r_0+2M\log\frac{r-2M}{r_0-2M}, \] obtained by integrating $dt/dr=f^{-1}$. Every outgoing ray starting at $r_0>2M$ escapes, and the divergence as $r_0\downarrow2M$ is a coordinate-time effect in this formula.
Why this is insufficient.
This verifies one elementary exterior completeness mechanism for an exact metric. It does not construct a collapse spacetime, control all nonradial geodesics, establish conformal null infinity, or prove persistence under perturbations. In particular it cannot exclude a singularity whose evolving exterior ceases to resemble this metric. The source asks for appropriate geometric/dimensional hypotheses and genericity, none of which is fixed by this calculation. The first missing step is an a priori global exterior estimate for a specified evolution and data class. No statement that censorship holds for every matter model or every asymptotically flat data set is inferred.
