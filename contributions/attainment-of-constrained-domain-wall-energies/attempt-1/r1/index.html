Attainment of Constrained Domain-Wall Energies

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
In the report, $\Omega=\mathbb R\times(-1,1)$ has coordinates $(x_1,x_3)$, and magnetizations $m=(m_1,m_2,m_3)$ take values in $S^2$ with finite Dirichlet energy. Put $m'=(m_1,m_3)$ and $m_\theta^\pm=(\cos\theta,\pm\sin\theta,0)$. The class $\mathcal M_\theta$ has $\operatorname{div}m'=0$ in $\Omega$, $m_3=0$ on its boundary, and the source far-field condition $m(\pm\infty,\cdot)=m_\theta^\pm$. Its energy is $\int_\Omega|\nabla m|^2\,dx$. The two topological constraints are $D(m)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_\Omega m\cdot(\partial_1m\times\partial_3m)\,dx$ and $W(m)=\pi^{-1}\int_\Omega\nabla m_1\times\nabla m_2\,dx$, where the last cross product is the determinant in the $(x_1,x_3)$ plane. The infimum is taken over $m\in\mathcal M_\theta$ with either $D(m)=q$ or $W(m)=q$.

Topological charge gives a rigorous energy floor.
For a smooth finite-energy admissible magnetization $m:\Omega\to S^2$, $\partial_1m$ and $\partial_3m$ are tangent to the sphere. Cauchy--Schwarz and $2ab\leq a^2+b^2$ give \[ |m\cdot(\partial_1m\times\partial_3m)| \leq\tfrac12(|\partial_1m|^2+|\partial_3m|^2). \] With the source normalization of $D$, integration yields $\int_\Omega|\nabla m|^2\geq4\pi|D(m)|$. Likewise $|\nabla m_1\times\nabla m_2|\leq\tfrac12(|\nabla m_1|^2+|\nabla m_2|^2)$ gives $\int|\nabla m|^2\geq2\pi|W(m)|$. These bounds preserve the different normalizations of degree and winding.
Why coercivity does not ensure attainment.
The energy, divergence constraint, strip boundary condition and either charge are invariant under translations $m(x_1,x_3)\mapsto m(x_1-a,x_3)$. If an admissible wall with the intended far-field convergence is shifted to $a\to+\infty$, its local limit can be the negative-end constant state, losing the opposite far field while preserving each translated wall's energy and charge. This is a conditional compactness obstruction; existence of a wall in every charge class is not asserted.
Remaining gap.
Translation recentering is necessary but may not prevent splitting into several separated charge-carrying walls or charge loss under weak convergence. One needs admissible constructions and a concentration-compactness argument respecting $\operatorname{div}m'=0$, both topology constraints and all $0<\theta\leq\pi/2$, including all integer $q$. The source's questionable sign in an integrability line is not used to make the class empty. Neither energy lower bound proves attainment.
