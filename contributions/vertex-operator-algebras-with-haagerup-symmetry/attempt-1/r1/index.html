Vertex Operator Algebras with Haagerup Symmetry

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

A vertex operator algebra packages chiral fields and their operator products. Its representation category records modules and tensor or fusion structure where defined. The Haagerup fusion category has the exceptional fusion data of the corresponding subfactor construction. Containing this symmetry data must be specified as a suitable categorical realization, rather than only a numerical coincidence of dimensions.

Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Construct a vertex operator algebra arising in conformal field theory whose representation category contains the Haagerup fusion category or its symmetry data, or prove that no such vertex operator algebra exists.

A restricted categorical obstruction.
The six-simple-object Haagerup category $\mathcal H$ in [E1, p. 1053] has distinct simples $1,\alpha,\alpha^2,\rho,\alpha\rho,\alpha^2\rho$, with $\alpha^3=1$ and $\alpha\rho=\rho\alpha^2$. Multiplying the latter relation on the left by $\alpha^2$ and on the right by $\alpha$ gives $\rho\alpha=\alpha^2\rho$. Therefore \[ \alpha\otimes\rho\not\cong\rho\otimes\alpha. \] There cannot be a fully faithful strong monoidal functor from this $\mathcal H$ into a braided category: the target braiding would give an isomorphism between the two image products, and full faithfulness would pull it back to the forbidden isomorphism. Indeed fullness lifts both it and its inverse, and faithfulness proves their composites are identities.
Scope of the obstruction.
Under a VOA framework whose relevant module category is braided, literal full tensor-subcategory containment of this six-object category is consequently impossible. This is an elementary reconstruction of a known nonbraided fusion-ring obstruction, not a new nonexistence theorem for Haagerup symmetry. It uses the specific six-object category, not an unspecified Morita-relative realization.
