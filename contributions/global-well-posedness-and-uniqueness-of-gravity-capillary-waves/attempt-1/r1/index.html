Definitions and conventions.
The gravity--capillary water-wave problem describes irrotational incompressible fluid motion with a free surface subject to gravity and surface tension. The source uses surface elevation $\eta$ and surface velocity potential $\xi$, an energy functional, and a conserved momentum or impulse constraint to obtain solitary-wave minimizers. Global well-posedness means existence for all time, uniqueness, and continuous dependence in its variational energy space. Uniqueness up to translations means that any two minimizers in the specified family differ only by a spatial shift.

Testing energy control of graph slope.
Consider the gravitational and capillary surface terms for a one-dimensional graph, \[ E_s(\eta)=\frac g2\int\eta^2dx+\tau\int(\sqrt{1+|\eta'|^2}-1)dx, \quad g,\tau>0. \] Choose a nonconstant smooth compactly supported $f$, set $\eta_\epsilon(x)=\epsilon^{1/2}f(x/\epsilon)$, and take zero surface potential. Then $\|\eta_\epsilon'\|_\infty=\epsilon^{-1/2}\|f'\|_\infty\to\infty$, whereas \[ \int\eta_\epsilon^2=\epsilon^2\int f^2,\qquad \int(\sqrt{1+|\eta_\epsilon'|^2}-1) \leq\int|\eta_\epsilon'|=\epsilon^{1/2}\int|f'|\to0. \] The inequality uses $\sqrt{1+s^2}-1\leq|s|$. Hence these conserved-energy terms cannot by themselves control a Lipschitz continuation criterion.
Why this is only an obstruction to a proof.
The sequence consists of smooth graphs and does not construct a singular water-wave solution. It also does not preserve the report's fixed nonzero impulse constraint, so it cannot refute existence or uniqueness of its minimizers. Its point is narrower: an argument "energy is conserved, therefore all higher geometric norms remain bounded" is invalid without an additional estimate. Replacing the exact capillary energy by its quadratic small-slope approximation would hide this issue precisely where slopes become large.
Both missing hypotheses remain.
Global evolution in the actual variational energy space requires a continuation mechanism beyond this surface-energy bound. Uniqueness up to translation of the constrained solitary-wave minimizer is a separate variational question; energy boundedness yields no strict convexity after the nonlinear constraint. Neither source hypothesis, and thus no unconditional orbital-stability conclusion, is proved here.
