Scaling Limits of Tensor Products in AQFT

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
An algebraic quantum field theory net assigns an algebra $\mathcal F(O)$ to each spacetime region, together with vacuum and translation data. The tensor-product net has local algebras $\mathcal F_1(O)\overline\otimes\mathcal F_2(O)$. A scaling limit is obtained by rescaling regions $\lambda O$ and taking a scaling-algebra state limit as $\lambda\downarrow0$. Commutation means that the corresponding limit net is isomorphic to $\mathcal F_{1,0}\overline\otimes\mathcal F_{2,0}$, with compatible choices of limit states.
Source scope and qualifications.
The report proves commutation under a uniform small-p nuclearity bound and twisted Haag duality. The open request is for more general conditions, not to reprove that stated sufficient case or to assert commutation without phase-space assumptions.

What product convergence really gives.
Suppose states $\omega_{i,\lambda}$ on fixed unital $C^*$-algebras $A_i$ converge pointwise to $\omega_{i,0}$ along the same subnet. On a fixed finite sum $z=\sum_{j=1}^r a_j\otimes b_j$, \[ (\omega_{1,\lambda}\otimes\omega_{2,\lambda})(z) =\sum_j\omega_{1,\lambda}(a_j)\omega_{2,\lambda}(b_j) \longrightarrow(\omega_{1,0}\otimes\omega_{2,0})(z). \] Since states have norm one, norm approximation extends this to every element of the spatial tensor product. This elementary argument justifies convergence on the fixed product algebra.
The scaling-dependent obstruction.
It says nothing about families requiring an increasing number of tensor terms. Let $e_1,\ldots,e_d$ be an orthonormal basis and $\Omega_d=d^{-1/2}\sum_je_j\otimes e_j$. Any vector $v$ which is a sum of at most $r$ simple tensors has its first factor in a subspace $V$ of dimension at most $r$. If $P_V$ is the orthogonal projection, then \[ \|\Omega_d-v\|^2\geq\|((I-P_V)\otimes I)\Omega_d\|^2 =1-\dim(V)/d\geq1-r/d. \] Thus bounded tensor rank does not approximate these unit vectors uniformly as $d\to\infty$.
Attempt outcome.
The example is finite-dimensional linear algebra, not a counterexample net. It isolates the missing uniform phase-space approximation: scaling families in the full product net need not be approximable by a fixed number of controlled factor families merely because fixed tensors converge. The source's small-$p$ nuclearity and twisted-duality assumptions address stronger structure; broadening them requires a uniform estimate not established here. Compatible scaling states are necessary but do not by themselves identify the full limiting local algebras.
