Attempt and results

The source leaves the ring, minimality, and output meaning of a resolution step unspecified. We test random access on an explicit infinite minimal resolution. This gives a concrete positive special case and a size obstruction to an unrestricted fast materialization claim, not a replacement target.
A directly indexed infinite resolution.
Let $e\ge1$, $k$ be a field, and $R=k[x_1,\ldots,x_e]/(x_i x_j:1\le i,j\le e)$. Its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ has $k$-basis $x_1,\ldots,x_e$ and $\mathfrak m^2=0$. Let $F_n$ be the free $R$-module on all length-$n$ words $w=(i_1,\ldots,i_n)$ in $\{1,\ldots,e\}$; $F_0=R$ has the empty word. Define \[ d_n(e_{i_1\cdots i_n})=x_{i_1}e_{i_2\cdots i_n},\qquad n\ge1, \] and augment $F_0$ by $R\to k=R/\mathfrak m$. The composition of two successive maps is zero because every product $x_i x_j$ is zero. To prove exactness, write an element of $F_n$ as $\sum_w(c_w+u_w)e_w$ with $c_w\in k$, $u_w\in\mathfrak m$. For each suffix $v$ of length $n-1$, its image coefficient is $\sum_i c_{iv}x_i$. Linear independence of the $x_i$ gives $d_n(\sum_w(c_w+u_w)e_w)=0$ exactly when every $c_w=0$. Hence $\ker d_n=\mathfrak m F_n$, and $\operatorname{im}d_{n+1}$ equals this same module, since every $x_i e_w$ is the image of $e_{iw}$. Exactness at $F_0$ is $\operatorname{im}d_1=\mathfrak m=\ker(R\to k)$. We have proved the entire resolution and its rank $\operatorname{rank}F_n=e^n$. All matrix entries lie in the maximal ideal, which is the usual local minimality criterion; if minimality is defined as this condition, the resolution is minimal. One can also see why a free cancellation cannot occur: a cancellable rank-one identity block would require a unit coefficient after invertible basis changes, but any such changes preserve the property that all entries lie in $\mathfrak m$.
What random access does and does not save.
For a supplied word $w$, the corresponding column of $d_n$ has exactly one nonzero, at the row indexed by its suffix, with coefficient $x_{i_1}$. This is obtained by reading the word and deleting its first letter; none of $F_1,\ldots,F_{n-1}$ needs to be materialized. The same formula gives a lazy matrix representation using $e$ and $n$ with an explicit indexing convention. However, explicitly listing all nonzero column entries costs at least $e^n$ output symbols, because there are $e^n$ distinct columns and each has one nonzero. For fixed $e=2$, this is exponential in $n$ and more than exponential in the binary length of $n$. This is only an output bound for the stated explicit format; it does not prevent the short formula or query access.
Why a rank oracle is insufficient for the general map request.
Over $S=k[x,y]$, the modules $S/(x)$ and $S/(y)$ both have free resolutions $0\to S\to S\to M\to0$ of ranks one and one, using maps multiplication by $x$ and $y$ respectively. Exactness follows because each multiplication is injective in a domain and its cokernel is the indicated quotient. But the modules are not isomorphic as $S$-modules: the annihilator of the first is $(x)$ and that of the second is $(y)$, and isomorphic modules have equal annihilators. Thus identical rank or Betti-number data cannot determine the requested differential and its cokernel. A route that computes a term rank and then declares the map recovered has a genuine missing step.
