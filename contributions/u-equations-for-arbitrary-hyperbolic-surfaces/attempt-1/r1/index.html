An infinite-arc obstruction to a finite-type shortcut.
Consider an annulus with one marked point on each boundary. Its universal cover is a strip $\mathbb R\times[0,1]$, with deck translation $(x,y)\mapsto(x+1,y)$. For each integer $n$, project the straight segment joining $(0,0)$ to $(n,1)$. It is an embedded arc joining the marked points: its interior has strictly increasing second coordinate, so two distinct interior parameters cannot differ by a deck translation. These arcs are distinct up to endpoint-fixed homotopy. A homotopy lifts with initial endpoint $(0,0)$; the terminal endpoint must stay in the discrete set $\mathbb Z\times\{1\}$, hence its integer label is constant. Thus \[ \{\text{relative arc classes}\}\supseteq\{\gamma_n:n\in\mathbb Z\}. \]
Consequence for an attempted extension.
A construction that assigns one variable to every arc cannot be extended to this boundary type by simply retaining a finite catalogue of all arc labels. A finite seed might still generate infinitely many variables, or a completion may handle infinite relations, but that is additional structure. Counting a finite triangulation alone does not remove the winding classes.
