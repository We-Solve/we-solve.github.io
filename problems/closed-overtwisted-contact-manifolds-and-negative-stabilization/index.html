Math-1531

Closed overtwisted contact manifolds and negative stabilization

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Question 1.7 records Paul Biran's request, following Emmanuel Giroux's results, for a definition of higher-dimensional overtwistedness in terms of supporting open books. It notes that Giroux proposed a definition and refers to the sixth day's discussion, which is not reproduced in the canonical question.

For the aid's modern formulation, let $(Y^{2m+1},\xi)$ be a closed cooriented contact manifold with $m\ge2$. An abstract supporting open book $\operatorname{OB}(W,\phi)$ has a $2m$-dimensional suitable exact symplectic/Weinstein page $W$ and an exact symplectic monodromy $\phi$, equal to the identity near the boundary; the associated contact structure is supported by the open book. A negative stabilization is the handle-and-twist operation: attach the appropriate Weinstein $m$-handle along the boundary of a properly embedded Lagrangian disk, form the Lagrangian sphere from that disk and the handle core, extend the old monodromy, and compose with a left-handed Dehn--Seidel twist about this newly created sphere. Composition and parametrization conventions must match the chosen stabilization definition.

The aid attributes intrinsic overtwisted disks to Borman--Eliashberg--Murphy and the following characterization to Casals--Murphy--Presas: \[ (Y,\xi)\text{ is overtwisted} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{some supporting open book for }(Y,\xi) \text{ is a negative stabilization}. \] The existence quantifier is over supporting open books; it does not say every supporting open book displays such a stabilization. The record supplies no full local disk model or complete theorem citation, so "overtwisted" here uses the cited intrinsic notion rather than inventing its model. The aid identifies Giroux's sixth-day proposal with the handle-and-left-twist operation.

Its cancellation family distinguishes actual stabilization from a negative factor on a fixed page. For every $m\ge2$, take the disk cotangent page $W=D^*S^m$, with zero-section Lagrangian sphere $L=S^m$. The aid reports that \[ \operatorname{OB}(D^*S^m,\operatorname{id}) \] is Weinstein fillable (by the usual product-page construction), yet its monodromy has the signed factorization \[ \operatorname{id}=\tau_L\circ\tau_L^{-1}. \] Thus a left-handed twist factor can occur in a fillable open book. Merely exhibiting such a factor, or composing an arbitrary existing monodromy with an inverse twist, is not the stabilization criterion. In negative stabilization the added handle and the particular handle-created Lagrangian sphere are essential. This counterexample family tests the logical formulation, not the cited existence characterization.

Short English statement

The source asks for an open-book definition of higher-dimensional overtwistedness. The aid reports an existence characterization by negative stabilization and explains why a negative monodromy factor alone is too weak: it can be inserted into a cancelling pair in a Weinstein-fillable identity open book.
