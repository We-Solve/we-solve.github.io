Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.38

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be an odd prime, let ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ be the field with $p$ elements, and equip the formal power series ring ${\mathbb{F}}_p[[t]]$ with its $t$-adic topology. Define \[ u_p=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad v_p=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\t&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad H_p=\overline{\langle u_p,v_p\rangle}\leq \operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p[[t]]), \] where the bar denotes closure in the induced matrix topology. A pro-$p$ group is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. A group $G$ is soluble if its derived series $G^{(0)}=G$, $G^{(j+1)}=[G^{(j)},G^{(j)}]$ reaches the trivial group after finitely many steps. Write $\mathcal V_p(G)$ for the variety of pro-$p$ groups generated by $G$, using the convention that this is the smallest class of pro-$p$ groups containing $G$ and closed under closed subgroups, continuous quotient groups, and arbitrary direct products.

The supplied source attributes to A. N. Zubkov the following result for $2\times2$ matrices: for every odd prime $p$ and every pro-$p$ group $G$ of such matrices, \[ G\text{ is soluble}\quad\text{or}\quad H_p\in\mathcal V_p(G). \] Here the matrix coefficient domain and its topology are not specified in the supplied record.

Question (Kourovka Notebook 14.38). Does the same conclusion hold in every matrix size $n\geq3$? More explicitly, for every odd prime $p$, every integer $n\geq3$, and every pro-$p$ group $G$ of $n\times n$ matrices in the class intended by the source, must \[ G\text{ be soluble}\quad\text{or}\quad H_p\in\mathcal V_p(G)? \] The comparison group remains the explicitly displayed group $H_p\leq\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p[[t]])$. The quantification over matrix groups cannot be made fully explicit from this record: the permitted coefficient fields or rings, the ambient matrix topology, and the requirements on the matrix realization must first be specified. The definition of $\mathcal V_p(G)$ above states the standard closure convention used in this draft; the source does not expand that terminology.

Short English statement

For an odd prime $p$, the source reports that a pro-$p$ group of $2\times2$ matrices is either soluble or generates a variety containing the specified two-generator closed subgroup of $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p[[t]])$. Zubkov asks whether this also holds for every matrix size at least $3$. The coefficient domain and topology of the matrix groups are unspecified. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied status information, not an independently verified assessment.
