Math-7378

Definable Maximal Almost Disjoint Families

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, and let $[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega$ denote its infinite subsets, identified with their characteristic functions and equipped with the subspace topology inherited from $2^{{\mathbb{N}}}$. An infinite family $\mathcal A\subseteq[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega$ is a maximal almost disjoint family (a MAD family) if distinct members have finite intersection and \[ \forall X\in[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega\ \exists A\in\mathcal A\quad |X\cap A|=\aleph_0. \] Thus maximality is with respect to adding infinite subsets of ${\mathbb{N}}$.

For $f,g\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}$, write $f\leq^*g$ if $f(n)\leq g(n)$ for all sufficiently large $n$. The bounding number is \[ \mathfrak b=\min\left\{|B|:B\subseteq{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}\text{ and }\forall g\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}\ \exists f\in B\ (f\not\leq^*g)\right\}. \]

The supplied record does not define $a_{\mathrm{Borel}}$ or $a_{\mathrm{closed}}$. Their intended minimization problems and ambient topological spaces must therefore be recovered from the cited source before these parts can receive a complete mathematical formulation. In particular, this draft does not identify them with the cardinalities of Borel or closed MAD families.

The projective notation also requires a convention about real parameters. Under the boldface interpretation, a $\Pi^1_1$ subset of $[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega$ is coanalytic, meaning that its complement is the projection of a Borel subset of $[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega\times{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}$. A $\Sigma^1_2$ subset is the projection of a coanalytic subset of that product. These definitions allow real parameters. The record does not explicitly distinguish this interpretation from the lightface, effective interpretation. For either fixed interpretation $\Gamma$, let \[ P_{\Gamma}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists\mathcal A\subseteq[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega\ \left(\mathcal A\text{ is a MAD family and }\mathcal A\in\Gamma\right). \] The same parameter convention must be used throughout.

For a set $S\subseteq[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega$, use the homogeneous-set formulation of the Ramsey property: \[ \forall X\in[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega\ \exists Y\in[X]^\omega\quad \left([Y]^\omega\subseteq S\ \lor\ [Y]^\omega\cap S=\varnothing\right). \] Write $R_{\Sigma^1_2}$ for the assertion that every $\Sigma^1_2$ subset of $[{\mathbb{N}}]^\omega$ has this property. This states the convention used here; the source record does not specify its precise Ramsey-property convention.

The supplied problem bundles the following questions.

  1. Determine the intended equality or consistency question involving \[ a_{\mathrm{Borel}}=a_{\mathrm{closed}}, \] and the consistency question involving \[ a_{\mathrm{Borel}}>\mathfrak b. \] The original wording, "Are $a_{\mathrm{Borel}}=a_{\mathrm{closed}}$ and is $a_{\mathrm{Borel}}>\mathfrak b$ consistent?", can ask whether the equality holds and whether the strict inequality is consistent. The cleaned wording can instead ask consistency of each assertion, or of their conjunction. These readings are retained as unresolved alternatives.
  2. Is it consistent that \[ \mathfrak b>\aleph_1\quad\text{and}\quad P_{\Pi^1_1}? \]
  3. Is the existence equivalence \[ P_{\Pi^1_1}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ P_{\Sigma^1_2} \] valid?
  4. Does \[ R_{\Sigma^1_2}\ \Longrightarrow\ \neg P_{\Sigma^1_2} \] hold?
The background theory for consistency and validity is not stated in the record. Under the usual ZFC reading, question 2 asks about consistency of $\mathrm{ZFC}+(\mathfrak b>\aleph_1)+P_{\Pi^1_1}$, and questions 3 and 4 ask whether their displayed implications or equivalence hold in every model of ZFC, with the chosen projective conventions. This is an explicit conditional interpretation, not an additional hypothesis supplied by the source. The undefined cardinal invariants and the alternative readings in question 1 remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The problem asks about comparisons between two unspecified definable-MAD invariants and the bounding number, the coexistence of a large bounding number with a coanalytic MAD family, equivalence of two projective MAD existence assertions, and whether projective Ramsey regularity rules out projective MAD families. The invariant definitions, part of the first question, and the logical and definability conventions require source clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft makes no independent claim about which questions remain open.
