Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 13.6

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite-rank free group $F_n=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle$ and a word $w\in F_n$, the corresponding one-relator group is \[ G=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\mid w\rangle=F_n/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle, \] where $\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle$ denotes normal closure. A group is coherent if every finitely generated subgroup has a finite presentation. The question is the universal assertion \[ \forall n\ge1\ \forall w\in F_n\ \forall H\le F_n/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle, \qquad H\text{ finitely generated}\ \Longrightarrow\ H\text{ finitely presented}. \] No torsion-free condition or restriction excluding proper-power relators is imposed. The supplied research summary reports an affirmative resolution by Jaikin-Zapirain and Linton (2025), describing the question as Baumslag's problem from 1973/74. That is a supplied literature assessment, not a new proof or independently performed source verification.

Short English statement

Does every finitely generated subgroup of a one-relator group admit a finite presentation? The supplied research summary reports that all one-relator groups are coherent.
