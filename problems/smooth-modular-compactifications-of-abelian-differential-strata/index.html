Math-4448

Smooth Modular Compactifications of Abelian-Differential Strata

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A nonzero holomorphic abelian differential on a smooth connected complex curve $C$ of genus $g$ is a section $0\ne\omega\in H^0(C,\Omega_C^1)$. Its zero divisor has degree $2g-2$. A zero signature $\mu=(m_1,\ldots,m_n)$ records the multiplicities of its zeros, so that $\sum_{i=1}^n m_i=2g-2$. Entries recording actual zeros are positive; allowing entries equal to zero instead records additional marked regular points. The source does not specify which convention or range of signatures is intended.

Under the conventional interpretation over ${\mathbb{C}}$ with ordered marked points, the projectivized stratum $\mathbb P\Omega\mathcal M_g(\mu)$ parametrizes isomorphism classes of tuples \[ (C,p_1,\ldots,p_n,[\omega]),\qquad [\omega]\in\left(H^0(C,\Omega_C^1)\setminus\{0\}\right)/{\mathbb{C}}^*, \qquad \operatorname{div}(\omega)=\sum_{i=1}^n m_i p_i, \] where $C$ is smooth, connected and projective of genus $g$, and the $p_i$ are pairwise distinct. Isomorphisms preserve the marked points and the differential up to a nonzero scalar. This describes a conventional marked moduli problem; the supplied statement does not specify the base field, ordered versus unordered zeros, or whether the stratum denotes a stack or its coarse moduli space.

Requested construction. For the genus $g$ and signature $\mu$ intended in the source, construct a compactification $\overline{\mathcal H}_{g,\mu}$ together with an open immersion \[ j:\mathbb P\Omega\mathcal M_g(\mu)\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathcal H}_{g,\mu} \] whose image is dense, such that $\overline{\mathcal H}_{g,\mu}$ is proper and smooth over the intended base and has a natural modular interpretation. In the conventional complex interpretation, properness and smoothness are relative to $\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}$. A modular interpretation should specify the geometric objects and their families, including boundary objects, whose moduli recover the compactification and restrict to the abelian-differential moduli problem on the open stratum.

The intended range of $(g,\mu)$, the ambient category in which properness and smoothness are required, and the precise meaning of a natural modular interpretation remain unspecified. Consequently, the displayed construction is a conditional formulation of the supplied target, rather than a claim that these choices have been resolved.

Short English statement

Construct a proper, smooth compactification of the projectivized stratum of holomorphic abelian differentials with prescribed zero multiplicities, and describe its points and families by a natural moduli problem extending the interior one. The source leaves the parameter range, base, marking convention, category of moduli objects, and required boundary interpretation unresolved. Its partially_solved label is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
