Characterization of Integral Models of Shimura Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integral domain $R$ with fraction field $F$, and a Shimura variety $V$ defined over $F$, the generic-fibre condition for an integral model consists of an $R$-scheme $\mathcal V$ together with an isomorphism \[ \mathcal V\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}F\;\cong\;V. \] This supplies terminology only: the source does not specify the allowable rings, Shimura varieties, levels, or additional conditions on $\mathcal V$, such as flatness, finiteness, or an extension property.

Problem. Find a good general characterization of integral models of Shimura varieties.

The intended characterization must go beyond merely restating the generic-fibre condition, but the supplied record does not specify its desired form or the class of models to be characterized. Consequently, a uniquely determined quantified mathematical statement cannot be extracted without additional hypotheses. In particular, the record does not specify whether the intended bases are global or local, which reduction conditions are allowed, or which properties distinguish the intended models.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a general characterization of integral models of Shimura varieties. The source leaves the base, the class of models, and the required characterizing properties unspecified, so the precise scope requires mathematical review.
