Math-4398

Teichmüller Projections of Hitchin Components

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the conventional setting of this question, let $\Sigma_g$ be a closed, connected, oriented surface of genus $g\geq 2$, and let $n\geq 2$ be an integer. These surface hypotheses and parameter ranges are not explicitly supplied in the source; the formulation below is conditional on these conventions.

Let $T(\Sigma_g)$ be the Teichmüller space of marked complex structures on $\Sigma_g$, and let $\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g)$ be the group of isotopy classes of orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms of $\Sigma_g$. Write $\operatorname{Hit}_n(\Sigma_g)$ for a Hitchin component of the $\operatorname{PSL}(n,{\mathbb{R}})$ character variety of $\pi_1(\Sigma_g)$: a connected component containing a representation obtained by composing a discrete faithful Fuchsian representation into $\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{R}})$ with the principal homomorphism into $\operatorname{PSL}(n,{\mathbb{R}})$. Equip Teichmüller space and the Hitchin component with their natural mapping class group actions induced by changing markings.

For a marked Riemann surface $X\in T(\Sigma_g)$, let $K_X$ be its holomorphic cotangent line bundle, let $K_X^i=K_X^{\otimes i}$, and let $H^0(X,K_X^i)$ be the complex vector space of holomorphic sections of $K_X^i$. Set \[ V_X=\bigoplus_{i=3}^{n}H^0(X,K_X^i). \] For $n=2$, this is the zero vector space by the empty direct sum convention.

The supplied question asks whether there exists a projection \[ p:\operatorname{Hit}_n(\Sigma_g)\longrightarrow T(\Sigma_g) \] that is surjective and mapping-class-group-equivariant, meaning \[ p(\gamma\cdot\rho)=\gamma\cdot p(\rho) \qquad \text{for every }\gamma\in\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g) \text{ and }\rho\in\operatorname{Hit}_n(\Sigma_g), \] and whose fibers satisfy \[ p^{-1}(X)\cong V_X \qquad\text{for every }X\in T(\Sigma_g). \] Here $\cong$ records the requested fiber identification without resolving its category. The source does not specify whether the projection must be continuous, smooth, locally trivial, or satisfy another regularity condition; nor does it specify whether the fiber identifications are homeomorphisms, diffeomorphisms, or identifications with additional structure. It also does not specify compatibility of those identifications as $X$ varies. Thus the displayed conditions preserve the mathematical target, but a fully precise existence statement requires these qualifications to be settled. No section, retraction property, or vector bundle structure is asserted.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Hitchin component admits a mapping-class-group-equivariant projection to Teichmüller space such that the fiber over every marked Riemann surface $X$ is identified with the space of holomorphic differentials of degrees $3,\ldots,n$ on $X$. The surface hypotheses, parameter quantifiers, regularity of the projection, and meaning of the fiber identification require clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
