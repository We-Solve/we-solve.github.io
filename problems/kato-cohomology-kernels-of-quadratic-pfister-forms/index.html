Kato Cohomology Kernels of Quadratic Pfister Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for the following equality of Kato-cohomology groups. Its characteristic and notation conventions are not explicitly stated. The formulation below uses the conventional characteristic-two interpretation; these conventions, including the ranges of the integer parameters, require confirmation against the cited report.

Let $F$ be a field of characteristic $2$, let $m,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$, let $a_1,\ldots,a_m\in F^\times$, and let $b\in F$. Write $\langle 1,a_i\rangle$ for the symmetric bilinear form with diagonal matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1,a_i)$, and write $[1,b]$ for the quadratic form $(x,y)\mapsto x^2+xy+by^2$. Suppose that the quadratic Pfister form \[ Q=\langle 1,a_1\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes\langle 1,a_m\rangle\otimes[1,b] \] is anisotropic, meaning that $Q(v)=0$ implies $v=0$. Let $F(Q)$ denote the function field of the projective quadric defined by $Q=0$.

For any field $K$ of characteristic $2$, let $\Omega_K^r=\bigwedge_K^r\Omega^1_{K/{\mathbb{F}}_2}$ be the space of absolute differential $r$-forms, with $\Omega_K^0=K$ and $\Omega_K^{-1}=0$. Write $\mathrm{dlog}(u)={\,\mathrm{d}} u/u$ for $u\in K^\times$. The standard Kato operator \[ \wp_r:\Omega_K^r\longrightarrow\Omega_K^r/{\,\mathrm{d}}\Omega_K^{r-1} \] is the additive map characterized on logarithmic expressions by \[ \wp_r\!\left(x\,\mathrm{dlog}(u_1)\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{dlog}(u_r)\right) =(x^2-x)\,\mathrm{dlog}(u_1)\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{dlog}(u_r) \pmod{{\,\mathrm{d}}\Omega_K^{r-1}}. \] Define \[ \nu_K(r)=\ker(\wp_r),\qquad H_2^{r+1}(K)=\operatorname{coker}(\wp_r). \] In degree zero, the logarithmic product is empty, so $\wp_0(x)=x^2-x$ and $\nu_K(0)={\mathbb{F}}_2$. For an extension $L/K$, define \[ H_2^{r+1}(L/K)=\ker\!\left(H_2^{r+1}(K)\longrightarrow H_2^{r+1}(L)\right), \] where the map is induced by extension of scalars on differential forms.

Under these conventions, is it true for every such choice of data that \[ H_2^{n+1}(F(Q)/F)= \begin{cases} \left\{\left[b\,\mathrm{dlog}(a_1)\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{dlog}(a_m)\wedge\eta\right]:\eta\in\nu_F(n-m)\right\},&n\geq m,\\[4pt] \{0\},&n<m, \end{cases} \] with brackets denoting classes in $H_2^{n+1}(F)$? Empty products are interpreted as $1$ if $m=0$; whether that boundary case is included in the report's intended question is unresolved from the supplied record.

The corrected statement supplied in the registry uses $H_2^{n+1}$. The field named original_statement instead prints $H^{2n+1}$ and leaves the symbol unspecified. This draft follows the supplied corrected version, whose verification is reported by the registry and has not been independently checked here.

Short English statement

For an anisotropic quadratic Pfister form, ask whether the kernel of Kato cohomology after passage to its quadric's function field consists exactly of the displayed differential-symbol classes when $n\geq m$, and vanishes when $n<m$. This draft uses the conventional characteristic-two definitions; the source record omits explicit characteristic and parameter-range hypotheses. The supplied status is open, subject to its dated curation and human mathematical review.
