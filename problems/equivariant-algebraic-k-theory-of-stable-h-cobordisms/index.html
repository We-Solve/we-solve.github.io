Equivariant Algebraic K-Theory of Stable h-Cobordisms

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact smooth $G$-manifold, as specified in the supplied source excerpt. The class of admissible groups $G$, the boundary conditions on $M$, and the conventions for equivariant cobordisms are not specified in the record.

An ordinary smooth $h$-cobordism between manifolds $M_0$ and $M_1$ is a compact smooth cobordism $W$ whose boundary inclusions $M_i\hookrightarrow W$ are homotopy equivalences. For manifolds with boundary, additional boundary and corner conventions are needed. An equivariant version includes compatible $G$-actions; the record does not specify the precise equivalence conditions, boundary identifications, or model of its moduli space.

Write $H_G^\infty(M)$ for the source's stable moduli space of equivariant $h$-cobordisms on $M$. The excerpt specifies stabilization with respect to representation disks: for a finite-dimensional real $G$-representation $V$ with an invariant inner product, $D(V)$ denotes its unit disk. The representation system and stabilization maps defining $H_G^\infty(M)$ are not supplied.

Problem. For compact smooth $G$-manifolds $M$ satisfying the intended, but incompletely supplied, hypotheses, describe $H_G^\infty(M)$ in terms of equivariant algebraic $K$-theory. A precise formulation requires specifying the equivariant algebraic $K$-theory object and the comparison construction or equivalence being requested.

Supplied context and unresolved qualifications. The excerpt reports that stable isovariant and equivariant $h$-cobordism spaces agree. It also prints a sequence of $G$-spectra, in the following order, \[ A_G(M)\longrightarrow \Sigma_G^\infty M\longrightarrow \operatorname{Wh}_G(M), \] and an equivalence written as \[ \Omega^{\infty+1}\left(\operatorname{Wh}_G(M)^G\right) \simeq H_G^\infty(M). \] Here $A_G(M)$ and $\operatorname{Wh}_G(M)$ are the source's algebraic $K$-theory and Whitehead-spectrum notation. Their constructions, the maps, the suspension-spectrum basepoint convention, the meaning of fixed points, and the intended exactness properties are not supplied. The placement of fixed points in the second display is a transcription interpretation. These displays are retained as supplied contextual claims, not independently established identifications; their relationship to the still-posed question requires review. The excerpt attributes the sequence to results combined with an unidentified reference $[16]$, and calls the resulting identification a significant step toward its Question 1.

The excerpt also attributes a splitting theorem, numbered Theorem 4, to "Goodwillie--Igusa--Malkiewich--M." Its corrupted product display appears to suggest \[ H_G^\infty(M)\simeq \prod_{(H)\leq G} H^\infty\!\left((M^H)_{hW_GH}\right). \] This is a tentative parsing, not a verified reconstruction. In that parsing, $(H)$ ranges over conjugacy classes of subgroups, $M^H=\{m\in M:hm=m\text{ for every }h\in H\}$, $W_GH=N_G(H)/H$ is the Weyl group, and $X_{hK}=EK\times_K X$ denotes homotopy orbits. The admissible subgroups, the scope of the theorem, and the meaning of $H^\infty$ on these homotopy-orbit spaces must be checked in the source. No unstated restriction on $G$, extension of the ordinary $h$-cobordism functor, or corrected theorem is asserted here.

Short English statement

Describe the stable space of equivariant $h$-cobordisms on a compact smooth $G$-manifold using equivariant algebraic $K$-theory. Stabilization uses representation disks. The supplied record labels the problem open and supplies related identifications and a splitting, but incomplete hypotheses, undefined comparison objects, and a corrupted formula prevent a fully precise reconstruction without mathematical review.
