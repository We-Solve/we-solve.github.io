Math-7213

Cost and First $L^2$ Betti Numbers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Regard a finitely generated group $\Gamma$ as a discrete group. For an essentially free probability-measure-preserving action $\Gamma\curvearrowright(X,\mu)$ on a standard nonatomic probability space, let $R_\Gamma$ denote its orbit equivalence relation. A graphing of $R_\Gamma$ is a countable family $\Phi=(\varphi_i:A_i\to B_i)_i$ of measure-preserving partial isomorphisms whose graphs generate $R_\Gamma$, modulo null sets. Define \[ \operatorname{cost}(R_\Gamma)=\inf_{\Phi}\sum_i\mu(A_i). \] One standard convention for group cost is \[ \operatorname{cost}_{\inf}(\Gamma)=\inf_{\Gamma\curvearrowright(X,\mu)}\operatorname{cost}(R_\Gamma), \] where the infimum ranges over all such essentially free actions. Let $\mathcal N\Gamma$ be the group von Neumann algebra with its canonical trace. The first $L^2$-Betti number is \[ \beta_1^{(2)}(\Gamma)=\dim_{\mathcal N\Gamma}H_1\!\left(\mathcal N\Gamma\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}\Gamma}C_*(E\Gamma;{\mathbb{Z}})\right), \] where $E\Gamma$ is a contractible free $\Gamma$-CW complex and $\dim_{\mathcal N\Gamma}$ is von Neumann dimension.

Supplied question, conditional on the group-cost convention above. Is it true that \[ \forall\,\Gamma\text{ finitely generated},\qquad \operatorname{cost}_{\inf}(\Gamma)=1+\beta_1^{(2)}(\Gamma)? \] Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define group cost or specify whether the intended assertion concerns its infimum over actions or the cost of every essentially free probability-measure-preserving action. These quantifiers must be distinguished. Moreover, the supplied quantifier includes finite groups. Under the convention above, a finite group has cost $1-|\Gamma|^{-1}$ and first $L^2$-Betti number zero, so the displayed unrestricted formulation is incompatible with that boundary case. An infinitude hypothesis or an altered formula would change the supplied statement; neither is silently inserted here. The intended formulation therefore requires source clarification.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every finitely generated group has cost equal to one plus its first $L^2$-Betti number. Its intended meaning requires clarification of the cost convention, the quantifier over actions, and the inclusion of finite groups.
