Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Question 33

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F=K(B)$ be the function field of an integral algebraic curve $B$ over a field $K$. Let $f:{\mathbb{P}}^N_F\to{\mathbb{P}}^N_F$ be a morphism of algebraic degree $d\geq 2$, and let $P\in{\mathbb{P}}^N(F)$. Here algebraic degree means that $f^*\mathcal O(1)\cong\mathcal O(d)$.

To make the height explicit under the standard geometric function-field interpretation, choose a normal projective curve $C$ over $K$ with function field $F$. Each $Q\in{\mathbb{P}}^N(F)$ extends to a morphism $q_Q:C\to{\mathbb{P}}^N_K$. Define \[ h_C(Q)=\deg_K\left(q_Q^*\mathcal O(1)\right), \qquad \hat h_f(P)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{h_C(f^{\circ n}(P))}{d^n}, \] where $f^{\circ n}$ denotes the $n$-fold iterate of $f$, with $f^{\circ 0}$ the identity.

Question. Is $\hat h_f(P)\in{\mathbb{Q}}$ always true for the function fields, morphisms, and points under consideration? If it is not always true, determine necessary and sufficient conditions on the data $(K(B),f,P)$ for \[ \hat h_f(P)\in{\mathbb{Q}}. \]

Unresolved scope qualification. The supplied excerpt does not specify the characteristic or other hypotheses on $K$, additional assumptions on $B$, or the normalization and precise meaning of the function-field height. Short English statement

For a morphism of projective space of degree at least two over a curve's function field, ask whether every rational point has rational canonical height. If this fails, characterize exactly when the canonical height is rational. The intended base-field hypotheses and height conventions remain unspecified in the supplied excerpt.
