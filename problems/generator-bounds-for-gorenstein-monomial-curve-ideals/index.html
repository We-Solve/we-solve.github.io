Math-7005

Generator Bounds for Gorenstein Monomial Curve Ideals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $k$, an integer $n\geq 1$, and positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$, consider the homomorphism \[ \varphi_{\mathbf a}:k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\longrightarrow k[t],\qquad x_i\longmapsto t^{a_i}. \] Under the standard positive-exponent interpretation, the associated affine monomial curve is \[ C_{\mathbf a}=\operatorname{Spec}k[t^{a_1},\ldots,t^{a_n}]\subseteq\mathbb A_k^n, \] and its defining ideal is $I_{\mathbf a}=\ker\varphi_{\mathbf a}$. The curve is called Gorenstein when its coordinate ring is Gorenstein: every localization at a prime ideal is a Gorenstein local ring, meaning a Noetherian local ring of finite injective dimension as a module over itself. Define the minimal number of ideal generators by \[ \mu(I_{\mathbf a})=\min\left\{r\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}:I_{\mathbf a}=(f_1,\ldots,f_r)\text{ for some }f_i\in k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\right\}. \] Question, retaining the supplied universal quantifier. For a fixed field $k$, does there exist an integer $B\geq0$, independent of both $n$ and the curve, such that \[ \forall n\geq1\ \forall(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\in\mathbb Z_{>0}^{\,n},\qquad k[t^{a_1},\ldots,t^{a_n}]\text{ Gorenstein} \ \Longrightarrow\ \mu(I_{\mathbf a})\leq B? \] This is a conditional formalization using the usual positive-exponent convention. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, whether the parametrization must be minimal, or whether the bound must also be independent of the field. No minimality restriction is imposed in the displayed formulation. In particular, the phrase "independently of $n$" has been retained literally; its compatibility with the intended source question requires mathematical review. The supplied open-status assessment is not an independently verified conclusion.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether one constant bounds the minimum number of equations generating the defining ideal of every affine Gorenstein monomial curve, uniformly over all ambient dimensions. The ground-field convention, permitted parametrizations, and intended uniformity in dimension require source review.
