Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt does not explicitly specify the ambient manifold, the volume form, or the solution convention. Under the customary compact Kähler interpretation, let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension $n\geq 1$, let $dV$ be a smooth positive volume form, and let $\omega$ be a smooth closed real $(1,1)$-form. These ambient assumptions require confirmation against the full source. Assume that $\omega$ is semipositive, meaning $\omega_x(v,\overline v)\geq 0$ in the Hermitian interpretation, and big in the sense explicitly used in the supplied record: \[ \int_X\omega^n>0. \] An $\omega$-plurisubharmonic function $\varphi$ is an upper semicontinuous, locally integrable real-valued function for which $\omega+dd^c\varphi$ is a positive current. Here $dd^c$ is the real complex Hessian operator, with one fixed conventional normalization used throughout.

For $p>1$, let $f\in L^p(X,dV)$ be nonnegative and satisfy the necessary mass compatibility condition \[ \int_X f\,dV=\int_X\omega^n. \] Consider a continuous $\omega$-plurisubharmonic solution $\varphi$ of \[ (\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n=f\,dV, \] where the Monge--Ampère measure is interpreted in the Bedford--Taylor sense for locally bounded potentials. Nonnegativity, mass compatibility, and this solution convention are implicit in this reconstruction, rather than explicitly stated hypotheses of the supplied excerpt.

The question attributed to Eyssidieux--Guedj--Zeriahi is whether every such solution is Hölder continuous on all of $X$: for a fixed smooth Riemannian distance $d$ on $X$, must there exist $\alpha\in(0,1]$ and $C<\infty$ such that \[ |\varphi(x)-\varphi(y)|\leq C\,d(x,y)^\alpha \qquad\text{for all }x,y\in X? \] No uniform exponent or quantitative dependence of $\alpha$ and $C$ is specified in the supplied question. Adding a constant to $\varphi$ does not affect this property.

The supplied background attributes continuity for these $L^p$ equations, obtained using viscosity techniques, to the reference labeled $[\mathrm{EGZ11}]$, and states uniqueness up to an additive constant. The ambient and connectedness hypotheses for that uniqueness assertion are not supplied. Its description of further regularity as largely open, and the registry's open status, are supplied source assessments rather than independently verified current literature conclusions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether solutions of the complex Monge--Ampère equation with an $L^p$ density, $p>1$, are Hölder continuous everywhere when the background form is semipositive and has positive top self-intersection. The supplied background asserts continuity and uniqueness up to an additive constant. The precise ambient hypotheses and solution convention need confirmation from the full source.
