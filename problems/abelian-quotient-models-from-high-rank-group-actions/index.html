Math-3398

Abelian-Quotient Models from High-Rank Group Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n=\dim X$. In the usual characteristic-zero terminology, a projective minimal terminal variety is a normal projective variety with terminal singularities and nef $\mathbb Q$-Cartier canonical divisor $K_X$. Write \[ \operatorname{NS}_{\mathbb C}(X)=\operatorname{NS}(X)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb C, \] where $\operatorname{NS}(X)$ is the group of line bundles modulo algebraic equivalence. A group is virtually solvable if it has a solvable subgroup of finite index.

Let $X$ be a projective minimal terminal variety of dimension $n$, and let $G\leq\operatorname{Aut}(X)$. Assume that the image of the induced representation \[ G\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}\left(\operatorname{NS}_{\mathbb C}(X)\right) \] is not virtually solvable. Write $R(G)$ for the radical referred to in the supplied record; its precise definition is not supplied. Reading passage to finite index followed by quotient by $R(G)$ as taking the image of a finite-index subgroup in $G/R(G)$, assume that there exist $G_0\leq G$ with $[G:G_0]<\infty$, an almost simple real linear algebraic group $H$, and an injective homomorphism \[ \iota:G_0/(G_0\cap R(G))\hookrightarrow H(\mathbb R) \] whose image is Zariski dense in $H$, such that \[ \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb R}H=n-1. \] Here real rank is the dimension of a maximal $\mathbb R$-split torus.

Question. Must $X$ be birational to a quotient of an abelian variety of dimension $n$, without imposing $n=3$? Under the conventional interpretation of "quotient" as a finite group quotient, the requested conclusion is the existence of an abelian variety $A$ and a finite subgroup $F\leq\operatorname{Aut}(A)$ such that \[ \dim A=n,\qquad X\dashrightarrow A/F\quad\text{is birational}. \]

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied formulation does not explicitly specify the ground field, define $R(G)$, distinguish the displayed quotient from quotienting $G_0$ by its own radical, or specify the kind of quotient of an abelian variety intended. The finite-group conclusion above is therefore conditional on that interpretation. The original question invokes the hypotheses of Theorem 3, whose text is not included. Although the registry reports that those hypotheses were restored, their completeness cannot be checked from the supplied record. These qualifications prevent treating this draft as an independently verified, fully specified formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether a projective minimal terminal variety with the stated nonsolvable automorphism action and a Zariski-dense almost simple quotient of real rank $\dim X-1$ must be birational to an abelian-variety quotient in arbitrary dimension. The ground field, radical, quotient convention, and completeness of the restored Theorem 3 hypotheses require review.
