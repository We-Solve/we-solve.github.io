Essential Images in p-Adic Simpson Theory

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the geometric hypotheses on $X$, its coefficient field, the convention for Higgs fields, or the construction and choices underlying $\mathcal H_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}$. The following is therefore a source-relative formulation, conditional on recovering that setting from the cited report.

For orientation, in the ordinary untwisted convention a Higgs bundle is a finite-rank locally free sheaf $E$ together with an $\mathcal O_X$-linear map $\theta:E\to E\otimes\Omega_X^1$ satisfying $\theta\wedge\theta=0$. If $X$ is a smooth proper curve, a nonzero Higgs bundle is slope semistable when every nonzero proper $\theta$-invariant subbundle $F\subset E$ satisfies \[ \frac{\deg F}{\operatorname{rk}F}\leq\frac{\deg E}{\operatorname{rk}E}. \] These conventions are explanatory; the supplied excerpt does not establish that they give the precise setting of the question. In particular, any twist in the Higgs field and any additional geometric hypotheses must be recovered from the source.

Let $\operatorname{HB}(X)$ denote the category of Higgs bundles in the intended source setting. Write \[ S_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}:\mathcal D_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}\longrightarrow\operatorname{HB}(X) \] as a placeholder for the intended $p$-adic Simpson functor; neither its domain nor its construction is specified in the record. Its essential image consists of Higgs bundles isomorphic to $S_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}(V)$ for some object $V$ of its domain. Interpreting $\mathcal H_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}$ as this essential image, as the supplied clean statement does, the question is whether \[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every }(E,\theta)\in\operatorname{HB}(X),\\ (E,\theta)\text{ semistable and }\deg E=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists V\in\mathcal D_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}\; (E,\theta)\simeq S_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}(V). \end{gathered} \] The isomorphism here is an isomorphism of Higgs bundles. The question includes the case $\theta=0$.

The supplied excerpt also asserts that its Theorem 4 reduces the question to membership in a category whose corrupted notation appears to be $\operatorname{HB}^{\mathrm{pDW}}_{X,\mathrm{Exp}}(X)$, and states that every degree-zero Higgs line bundle belongs to that category. Neither this category nor the hypotheses of the reduction are supplied, so the asserted reduction cannot be made precise here. The supplied dated assessment describes the general question, including the zero-Higgs-field case, as widely open and says that rank-one results and restricted rank-two nilpotent cases do not settle it. These are supplied source assessments, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

In the intended geometric setting, must every semistable degree-zero Higgs bundle arise, up to isomorphism, from the specified $p$-adic Simpson correspondence? This includes bundles with zero Higgs field. The supplied record omits the hypotheses on $X$ and the precise correspondence, so the formulation remains conditional on recovering those definitions.
