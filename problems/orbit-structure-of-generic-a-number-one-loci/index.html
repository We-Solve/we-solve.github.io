Math-14569

Orbit Structure of Generic A-Number-One Loci

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a prime $p$ and a $p$-divisible group $H$ over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p$, the usual $a$-number is \[ a(H)=\dim_k\operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-group schemes}}(\alpha_p,H[p]), \qquad \alpha_p=\ker(F:\mathbb G_{a,k}\longrightarrow\mathbb G_{a,k}), \] where $F$ is Frobenius. This explains the standard numerical invariant without selecting the unspecified parameter space in the source.

The supplied question concerns strata associated with an affine Deligne--Lusztig invariant denoted $f_g$, and an action denoted $J_b(F)$. It asks whether the locus with generic $a$-number $1$ is a single orbit of strata after removing the hypothesis that $b$ is basic.

The intended equality can be expressed conditionally as follows. Once an ambient parameter space $\mathscr X$, its collection $\mathscr S$ of $f_g$-strata, the action of $J_b(F)$ on those strata, and the intended locus $\mathscr L_1\subseteq\mathscr X$ have been specified, ask whether \[ \exists S_0\in\mathscr S\qquad \mathscr L_1=\bigcup_{j\in J_b(F)}j\cdot S_0 \] continues to hold for nonbasic $b$ in the intended class of data. Here the right-hand side is the union of the strata belonging to one orbit; it does not assert transitivity on individual points.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define $f_g$, the ambient parameter space, the strata, $F$, $b$, the underlying group, or the action. It also does not specify whether generic $a$-number $1$ is evaluated on each stratum, each irreducible component, or another family. Thus $\mathscr L_1$ and the class of data quantified over remain unspecified, and the displayed equality is a template for the supplied question, not a fully determined conjecture. The treatment of an empty locus is also unspecified. The supplied curation reports that the equality is proved when $b$ is basic and labels the extension open; these are supplied literature claims, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the strata representing the intended generic $a$-number-one locus form exactly one $J_b(F)$-orbit even for nonbasic $b$. The source record omits the invariant, parameter space, action, and meaning of genericity needed to make this precise. Its reported basic-case result and open status require independent review.
