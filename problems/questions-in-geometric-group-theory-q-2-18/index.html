Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 2.18

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A metric space $M$ is proper if its closed bounded balls are compact. It is CAT$(0)$ if it is geodesic and distances in each geodesic triangle are bounded above by the corresponding distances in its Euclidean comparison triangle. The action of $\operatorname{Isom}(M)$ is cocompact if there is a compact set $K\subseteq M$ with $\operatorname{Isom}(M)K=M$.

An infinite geodesic ray from $a$ is an isometric embedding $\gamma:[0,\infty)\to M$ with $\gamma(0)=a$. The source defines $M$ to be almost geodesically complete if \[ \exists R\geq0\ \forall a,b\in M\ \exists\gamma:[0,\infty)\to M\ \exists t\geq0: \quad \gamma(0)=a,\quad d(\gamma(s),\gamma(u))=|s-u|\ (s,u\geq0),\quad d(\gamma(t),b)\leq R. \] The constant $R$ is uniform in $a,b$, while the ray may depend on both points.

Ross Geoghegan's question as transcribed asks whether every proper CAT$(0)$ space $M$ with cocompact isometry group has this property. The transcribed question omits a noncompactness hypothesis. The supplied reported affirmative theorem instead states: \[ \text{If $M$ is noncompact, proper, CAT$(0)$, and $\operatorname{Isom}(M)$ acts cocompactly, then $M$ is almost geodesically complete.} \] These scopes must be distinguished: a nonempty compact metric space cannot contain an isometrically embedded infinite ray, so it cannot satisfy the displayed definition. Noncompactness cannot silently be removed from the cited affirmative statement or inserted into the historical wording.

The supplied research summary attributes the noncompact assertion to Theorem 5 of Geoghegan--Ontaneda (2007), combining nonvanishing compactly supported integral cohomology $H_c^d(M;{\mathbb{Z}})$ in a top degree $d$ with Ontaneda's earlier homological criterion. It also reports that the special case of CAT$(0)$ groups, acting geometrically with discrete orbits, was already settled by Ontaneda's Theorem B (2005). These literature statements are supplied assessments, rather than independently verified proofs here.

Short English statement

Almost geodesic completeness means that every ordered pair of points can be connected up to one uniform error by an infinite geodesic ray starting at the first point. The supplied literature reports this for noncompact proper CAT$(0)$ spaces with cocompact isometry group. The source transcription lacks noncompactness, which is incompatible with compact spaces and requires correction or qualification.
