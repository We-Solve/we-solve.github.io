A cut-critical kernel for the vertexwise rainbow-cycle conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The literal record is only "Conjecture 6.9. Under the hypotheses of". Neither the antecedent nor conclusion is present. The following vertexwise-load statement is recovered by the attached research aid and remains a qualified reconstruction despite the registry's exact label.

Let $D$ be a nonempty finite simple loopless digraph on $n$ vertices, and let each ordered arc $vw$ have a finite label set $S_{vw}$ (empty sets can be treated as absent arcs). Write \[ \ell(v)=\sum_{w\ne v}|S_{vw}|. \] A rainbow directed cycle admits distinct representatives from its arc label sets, including a digon when present. The recovered Conjecture 6.9 asserts that $\ell(v)\geq n$ for every $v$ forces a rainbow cycle.

Reported cut-critical reduction. If a counterexample exists, the aid proves that deleting vertices and label incidences yields a counterexample on $m\geq2$ vertices with exact load \[ \ell(v)=m\qquad\text{for every vertex}, \] and, for every nonempty proper $X\subset V$, the strict cut certificate \[ \max_{v\notin X}\sum_{x\in X}|S_{vx}|\geq|X|+1. \] The maximum is existential in the outside vertex; the assertion is not that every outside vertex has this load into $X$. All operations retain a rainbow-cycle-free instance.

In particular the overlap core, consisting of arcs with $|S_{vw}|\geq2$, is strongly connected. On every directed cycle in that core, Hall's distinct-representative condition fails: there is a subset $F$ of at least three cycle arcs with \[ \left|\bigcup_{e\in F}S_e\right|<|F|. \] The lower size three follows because each core arc already has two available labels. This gives necessary structure for hypothetical counterexamples, not an example proving they exist.

Reported threshold and hypothesis distinctions. A directed $m$-cycle whose arcs all share $m-1$ labels has load $m-1$ everywhere and no rainbow cycle. Thus the pointwise threshold $m$ cannot simply be replaced by $m-1$.

For layers $E_i=\{vw:i\in S_{vw}\}$, the aid distinguishes the stronger hypothesis of the preceding recovered Conjecture 6.8, \[ \sum_i\min_v d^+_{E_i}(v)\geq n, \] from the more permissive vertexwise condition \[ \min_v\sum_i d^+_{E_i}(v)\geq n. \] The first implies the second, but the report supplies a directed-cycle family showing no converse; the exact family is not printed in the summary. Proving a result only under the sum-of-minima hypothesis therefore does not prove the full vertexwise conjecture. The cut-critical theorem leaves the latter unresolved.

Short English statement

The source conjecture is truncated. For the aid's vertexwise rainbow-cycle reconstruction, any counterexample has a smaller exact-load kernel with strict incoming-cut certificates, a strongly connected multi-label core, and Hall obstructions on its cycles. These are conditional restrictions, not a proof or a counterexample to the conjecture.
