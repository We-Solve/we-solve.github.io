Biembeddability and the Golden-Ratio Thompson Group

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H$ denote the ambient group written $\mathrm{PL}_{o}(I)$ in the supplied record, and put \[ X=\{G\leq H: G\text{ is finitely generated}\}. \] For groups $G,K$, write $G\hookrightarrow K$ if there exists an injective group homomorphism from $G$ to $K$. Define biembeddability by \[ G\sim K\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\hookrightarrow K\ \text{and}\ K\hookrightarrow G. \] Write $[G]$ for the equivalence class of $G$ and define the partially ordered set \[ [X]=X/{\sim},\qquad [G]\preceq[K]\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\hookrightarrow K. \] Here embeddings are read as abstract group embeddings; the record does not specify any additional compatibility with the actions on $I$.

The supplied extraction combines two questions:

  1. Can the structure of the biembeddability poset $([X],\preceq)$ be understood further?
  2. Does Cleary's golden-ratio Thompson group $F_{\tau}$ embed in Thompson's group $F$? In symbols, does there exist an injective homomorphism \[ \varphi:F_{\tau}\longrightarrow F? \]
The first question specifies no particular structural property to establish and therefore does not determine a precise conjecture. The record does not define $I$, the notation $\mathrm{PL}_{o}(I)$, or the groups $F$ and $F_{\tau}$ by presentations or actions. The poset construction above is consequently relative to the intended ambient group, and the second question retains the named groups without supplying missing definitions.

The supplied statement-verification note reports that these are independent numbered questions merged during extraction, and marks the combined statement as unrecoverable. The supplied dated literature assessment reports a negative answer to the embedding question by Hyde--Moore, while retaining an open label for the combined record because the structural question is incomplete. These are supplied curation claims, not independently verified mathematical or literature conclusions.

Short English statement

The record combines a broad question about the ordering of finitely generated subgroups by mutual embeddability with the question whether Cleary's golden-ratio Thompson group embeds in Thompson's group $F$. The structural target and several definitions are missing. Supplied dated curation reports that the embedding question has a negative answer, but keeps the combined record labeled open; this draft requires human mathematical and source review.
