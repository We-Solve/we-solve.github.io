No constraint equations, class of initial data, localisation conditions, or fall-off rates are specified.
  • Anna Sakovich described recent results concerning the definition and geometry of the mass aspect function for asymptotically hyperbolic, rather than asymptotically Euclidean, initial data sets. The passage suggests studying such data under very weak decay assumptions, with possible relevance to stability of geometric inequalities such as the positive mass theorem. It gives neither precise decay assumptions nor a stability assertion.
  • Markus Wolff described a geometric flow along null hypersurfaces that allows construction of foliations by surfaces of constant spacetime mean curvature in null cones near null infinity. The passage relates this notion to foliations considered by Carla Cederbaum and Anna Sakovich in initial data sets. It supplies no definition of the curvature, flow equation, spacetime hypotheses, or meaning of the neighbourhood of null infinity.
  • Annegret Burtscher described a characterisation of global hyperbolicity via completeness of the null distance, presented as a Lorentzian analogue of the Hopf--Rinow theorem. The passage does not specify the construction of the null distance or the hypotheses needed for the characterisation.
  • James Lucietti described results fully characterising the near-horizon geometry of stationary spacetimes hosting a degenerate Killing horizon, together with their use in proving rigidity. The spacetime dimension, field equations, regularity, and other hypotheses are not given.
  • Jan Sbierski described a result showing Lipschitz inextendibility of weak null singularities, which the passage says are expected to form in the interior of generic rotating black holes. It states that such an inextendibility statement would be necessary to deduce versions of Penrose's strong cosmic censorship conjecture. Neither the relevant class of spacetimes, the meaning of genericity, the extension category, nor a particular version of the conjecture is specified.
