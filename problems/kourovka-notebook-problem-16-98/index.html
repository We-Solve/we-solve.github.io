Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.98

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite solvable group and let $A\leq \operatorname{Aut}(G)$ satisfy $\gcd(|A|,|G|)=1$. Define the common fixed-point subgroup by \[ C_G(A)=\{g\in G: a(g)=g\text{ for every }a\in A\}. \] For a finite solvable group $H$, its Fitting height $h(H)$ is the least integer $r\geq 0$ for which there is a series \[ 1=H_0\leq H_1\leq\cdots\leq H_r=H, \] with every $H_i$ normal in $H$ and every factor $H_i/H_{i-1}$ nilpotent. In particular, $h(1)=0$. Write $l(A)$ for the maximum number of strict inclusions in a subgroup chain \[ 1=A_0<A_1<\cdots<A_{l(A)}=A. \] Thus $l(1)=0$; this explicitly fixes the chain-length convention, which is not specified in the supplied wording.

Question (A. Turull, Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.98). Does there exist, for every finite group $A$ and every integer $c\geq 0$, an integer $b(A,c)\geq 0$, depending only on the isomorphism type of $A$ and on $c$, such that every finite solvable group $G$ admitting a faithful action of $A$ by automorphisms with $\gcd(|A|,|G|)=1$ satisfies \[ h(C_G(A))=c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h(G)\leq b(A,c)? \] More strongly, does there exist a function \[ f:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0} \] such that every pair $(G,A)$ as above satisfies \[ h(G)\leq f\left(l(A),h(C_G(A))\right)? \] Here faithfulness expresses the source hypothesis that $A$ is a group of automorphisms of $G$.

Supplied background qualification. The source attributes to A. Turull the result that, when $A$ is solvable, \[ h(G)\leq h(C_G(A))+2l(A), \] and states that this bound is best possible when $h(C_G(A))>0$. The supplied record does not specify the precise parameterwise meaning of this optimality assertion. It also refers to results of H. Kurzweil for nonsolvable $A$, without stating their hypotheses or conclusions. These are supplied literature descriptions, not independently verified assessments.

Short English statement

For a finite solvable group acted on by a group of automorphisms of coprime order, ask whether its Fitting height is bounded by the acting group and the Fitting height of the common fixed-point subgroup. Ask further whether only the longest subgroup-chain length of the acting group is needed. The supplied source records a sharp bound for solvable acting groups and references partial results for nonsolvable ones.
