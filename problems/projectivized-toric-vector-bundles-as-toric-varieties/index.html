Projectivized Toric Vector Bundles as Toric Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote a ground field, whose characteristic and algebraic closure assumptions are not specified in the supplied record. A toric variety $X$ has an algebraic torus $T$ as a dense open subset, with the multiplication action extending to $X$; the precise convention concerning splitness and normality must be fixed for a definitive formulation. A toric vector bundle $\mathcal E$ on $X$ is a finite-rank locally free sheaf equipped with a lift of the $T$-action that acts linearly on the fibers of its associated vector bundle. For positive rank, the quotient convention for projectivization is \[ \pi\colon Y={\mathbb{P}}_X(\mathcal E)=\operatorname{Proj}_X\!\left(\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal E\right)\longrightarrow X, \] which parametrizes one-dimensional quotients of the fibers of $\mathcal E$. The alternative convention parametrizes one-dimensional subspaces and replaces $\mathcal E$ by $\mathcal E^\vee$ in this formula. The source does not select a convention.

Research question, with unresolved scope. For projectivizations $Y$ of toric vector bundles, characterize the structures, invariants, and vanishing properties that can be compared with those of smooth projective toric varieties, and determine the hypotheses under which each proposed comparison holds.

This is a research program rather than a fully specified conjecture. The record does not identify particular structures, invariants, cohomology groups, or vanishing assertions. It also does not specify whether $X$ is smooth, complete, or projective, the allowed ranks of $\mathcal E$, or whether $Y$ is required to be smooth or projective. The original wording uses "a (toric) vector bundle," leaving the possible inclusion of bundles without a torus-equivariant structure unresolved. Consequently, no universal comparison theorem, precise vanishing statement, or assertion that $Y$ itself is toric can be extracted without additional choices.

Short English statement

The question asks which features of smooth projective toric varieties also occur for projectivized toric vector bundles, and under which hypotheses. The supplied wording leaves the comparison properties and the class of bundles and base varieties unspecified, so it does not determine a single precise conjecture.
