Geometry of Cones of $q$-Ample Line Bundles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for a useful geometric description of the generally nonconvex cone of $q$-ample line bundles on a variety. It does not specify the ground field, projectivity hypotheses, range of $q$, ambient space of the cone, or the meaning of a useful description.

To explain the standard cohomological terminology in a conditional projective setting, let $X$ be a projective variety over a field $k$, and let $q\geq 0$ be an integer. A line bundle $L$ on $X$ is $q$-ample if, for every coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$, there exists an integer $m_0=m_0(\mathcal F,L)\geq 0$ such that \[ H^i\!\left(X,\mathcal F\otimes L^{\otimes m}\right)=0 \qquad\text{for every integer }i>q\text{ and every integer }m\geq m_0. \] If $q\geq\dim X$, this condition is automatic in this projective setting; the potentially nontrivial range is $0\leq q<\dim X$. These projective hypotheses supply a conventional explanatory framework and are not stated hypotheses of the supplied question.

In this setting, numerical equivalence of line bundles means equality of their degrees on every integral curve in $X$. Write \[ N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{R}}} =\left(\operatorname{Pic}(X)/{\equiv_{\mathrm{num}}}\right) \otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}, \qquad \mathcal S_q(X)=\{[L]_{\mathrm{num}}:L\text{ is a }q\text{-ample line bundle on }X\}. \] The numerical classes in $\mathcal S_q(X)$ explain the line-bundle data underlying the customary numerical-cone interpretation. The supplied record does not specify how its cone is defined for real classes, so no particular extension from line bundles to real classes is imposed here. A generally nonconvex cone must not be replaced by the convex cone generated by its elements.

Problem, retaining the source's unresolved scope. Give a useful geometric description of the generally nonconvex cone of $q$-ample line bundles on a variety. Under a projective numerical interpretation, this asks for a geometric characterization of the $q$-ample locus in $N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{R}}}$, with the real-class convention and hypotheses still to be specified. The source does not prescribe a concrete criterion or a precise standard for what counts as a useful description. Its toric background does not explicitly restrict the question to toric varieties.

Short English statement

A line bundle is $q$-ample when sufficiently high positive powers kill coherent-sheaf cohomology in every degree greater than $q$. The question asks for a geometric description of the resulting generally nonconvex cone. The source leaves the class of varieties, the real-class convention, and the requested form of description unspecified.
