Stanley's Positivity Problems in Algebraic Combinatorics — Problem 14: Combinatorial symmetry and Schur-positivity of LLT ribbon polynomials

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $\mu\subseteq\lambda$ be partitions. Their skew Young diagram is \[ \lambda/\mu=\{(i,j):\mu_i<j\leq\lambda_i\}. \] A $k$-ribbon is an edge-connected skew Young diagram consisting of $k$ cells and containing no $2\times2$ block. Assume that $\lambda/\mu$ admits a tiling by $k$-ribbons, and put $n=(|\lambda|-|\mu|)/k$.

The supplied record refers to LLT $k$-ribbon tableaux but does not specify their labeling and semistandard admissibility conditions. Denote the intended family by $\mathcal T^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu}$. For a tableau $T$ whose ribbons carry positive integer labels, let $m_i(T)$ count the ribbons labeled $i$, and set \[ x^T=\prod_{i\geq1}x_i^{m_i(T)}, \qquad H^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu}(x;q) =\sum_{T\in\mathcal T^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu}}q^{s(T)}x^T. \] Here $s(T)$ is the spin statistic intended by the source. Its normalization is not specified. For example, if the height of a ribbon is the number of rows it occupies, the sum of ribbon heights minus one and half of that sum give different exponent conventions. The latter can introduce half-integral powers of $q$. The intended convention must therefore be fixed before interpreting polynomiality in $q$.

Requested statement, subject to these unresolved conventions. For every admissible $k$ and shape, establish the following assertions for the intended LLT ribbon generating function:

  1. (a) Give a combinatorial proof of symmetry: for every permutation $\sigma$ of the positive integers that fixes all but finitely many indices, \[ H^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu}(x_{\sigma(1)},x_{\sigma(2)},\ldots;q) =H^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu}(x_1,x_2,\ldots;q). \]
  2. (b) Writing its homogeneous degree-$n$ Schur expansion as \[ H^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu}(x;q) =\sum_{\nu\vdash n}c^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu,\nu}(q)s_\nu(x), \] where $s_\nu$ is the Schur symmetric function, determine whether \[ c^{(k)}_{\lambda/\mu,\nu}(q)\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}[q] \qquad\text{for every }\nu\vdash n. \]
The skew notation above makes the opening hypothesis explicit. The supplied statement subsequently uses $H^{(k)}_\lambda$ and tableaux of shape $\lambda$, so it does not settle whether the intended scope includes arbitrary skew shapes or only straight shapes $(\mu=\varnothing)$. This scope requires confirmation.

The registry labels the problem partially solved. Its supplied background reports that part (b) was proved using Kazhdan--Lusztig theory of affine Hecke algebras, attributing this to Leclerc--Thibon (2000) and Grojnowski--Haiman (2007), while direct combinatorial bijective proofs of symmetry and general Schur positivity remain partially open. This is a supplied literature assessment, not an independently verified conclusion; its exact scope and conventions require review.

Short English statement

For LLT generating functions of labeled ribbon tableaux, seek a combinatorial proof that permuting the variables leaves the function unchanged, and ask whether every Schur coefficient is a polynomial in $q$ with nonnegative integer coefficients. The supplied record reports a proof of the positivity assertion but retains combinatorial proof questions. Tableau admissibility, spin normalization, and the intended straight or skew shape scope remain unspecified.
