Math-4456

Stationary Reflection and Large Power at Aleph Omega

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Work in $\mathsf{ZFC}$. For an infinite cardinal $\kappa$, write $\kappa^+$ and $\kappa^{++}$ for its first and second successor cardinals. Say that $\kappa$ is strong limit if $2^\lambda<\kappa$ for every cardinal $\lambda<\kappa$, and singular if $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)<\kappa$. Here $2^\lambda$ denotes the cardinality of the power set of $\lambda$.

For a limit ordinal $\delta$, a set $C\subseteq\delta$ is club if it is unbounded in $\delta$ and closed under limits below $\delta$. A set $S\subseteq\delta$ is stationary in $\delta$ if it meets every club subset of $\delta$. Under the convention used here, stationary sets $S_0,\ldots,S_{n-1}\subseteq\theta$, where $\theta$ is regular uncountable, reflect simultaneously if there exists $\alpha<\theta$ with $\operatorname{cf}(\alpha)>\omega$ such that each $S_i\cap\alpha$ is stationary in $\alpha$.

Using this convention, simultaneous finite stationary-set reflection at $\theta$ means \[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every integer }n\geq 1\text{ and every sequence }\langle S_i:i<n\rangle \text{ of stationary subsets of }\theta,\\ \text{there exists }\alpha<\theta\text{ with }\operatorname{cf}(\alpha)>\omega \text{ such that }S_i\cap\alpha\text{ is stationary in }\alpha \text{ for every }i<n. \end{gathered} \] Consistency question. Can the consistency result referred to in the supplied record be obtained with $\kappa=\aleph_\omega$? Under the convention above, its requested target is a model of $\mathsf{ZFC}$ in which \[ \kappa=\aleph_\omega,\qquad \operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\omega,\qquad (\forall\lambda<\kappa\text{ a cardinal})\;2^\lambda<\kappa, \qquad 2^\kappa=\kappa^{++}, \] and simultaneous finite stationary-set reflection holds at $\kappa^+=\aleph_{\omega+1}$.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define its reflection principle. The displayed formulation uses all stationary subsets of $\kappa^+$ and requires a common reflection point of uncountable cofinality; whether the intended principle restricts the stationary sets or the cofinality of the reflection point must be checked against the source. The record calls the referenced result a $\Sigma$-Prikry consistency result, but supplies neither its exact theorem nor its large-cardinal hypotheses. Consequently, this draft does not specify a relative consistency strength or assert that a particular forcing construction is required.

Short English statement

The question asks whether $\aleph_\omega$ can be a strong-limit singular cardinal whose power set has size $\aleph_{\omega+2}$, while every finite family of stationary subsets of $\aleph_{\omega+1}$ reflects at a common ordinal of uncountable cofinality. This formulation uses an explicit reflection convention whose agreement with the intended source principle requires review. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified here.
