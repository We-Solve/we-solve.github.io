Math-7028

Rank-One Kernel Boundary Points of Free Spectrahedra

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard complex-Hermitian convention, let $g,d\geq 1$ be integers and let $A_1,\ldots,A_g\in M_d({\mathbb{C}})$ satisfy $A_j^*=A_j$. A Hermitian monic linear pencil is \[ L(x)=I_d+\sum_{j=1}^g A_jx_j. \] For $n\geq 1$, write $\mathcal H_n=\{T\in M_n({\mathbb{C}}):T^*=T\}$. For a tuple $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_g)\in\mathcal H_n^g$, define \[ L(X)=I_d\otimes I_n+\sum_{j=1}^g A_j\otimes X_j, \qquad D_L(n)=\{X\in\mathcal H_n^g:L(X)\succeq0\}. \] The free spectrahedron is the graded set $D(L)=(D_L(n))_{n\geq1}$. Taking boundary level by level, let $\partial D_L(n)$ denote the topological boundary in the real vector space $\mathcal H_n^g$, and set \[ \partial^1D(L)=\bigsqcup_{n\geq1} \{X\in\partial D_L(n):\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\ker L(X)=1\}. \] Here the kernel is that of the evaluated $dn\times dn$ matrix; the superscript $1$ refers to kernel dimension, not matrix level.

Extracted question. For every Hermitian monic linear pencil $L$ covered by the intended source hypotheses, must there exist an integer $n\geq1$ and a tuple $X\in\partial D_L(n)$ such that \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\ker L(X)=1? \] Equivalently, must $\partial^1D(L)\neq\varnothing$?

Unresolved qualifications. The definitions above explicitly adopt complex-Hermitian tuples and levelwise topological boundary. The supplied record does not specify the scalar field, the boundary convention, or whether the matrix level is unrestricted. A real-symmetric interpretation would instead use symmetric real matrices and real kernel dimension. The record states no minimality, irreducibility, nonconstancy, boundedness, or other nondegeneracy hypothesis on $L$. In particular, the question is expressed in terms of the chosen pencil, and the record does not say whether redundant or repeated blocks are excluded. These potentially material restrictions must be checked before treating the unrestricted formulation as the intended conjecture; none is imposed here as an established source hypothesis.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a free spectrahedron has, at some matrix size, a boundary tuple at which its defining pencil has a one-dimensional kernel. The supplied record labels the question open, but its intended hypotheses and matrix-domain conventions require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
