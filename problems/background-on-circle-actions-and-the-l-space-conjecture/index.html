Background on Circle Actions and the L-Space Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed, connected, oriented, irreducible rational homology $3$-sphere. Here rational homology sphere means that $H_k(M;{\mathbb{Q}})\cong H_k(S^3;{\mathbb{Q}})$ for every $k$, and irreducibility means that every embedded $2$-sphere bounds a $3$-ball. Write $S^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}(S^1)$ and $\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}({\mathbb{R}})$ denote the groups of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms. Define \[ \operatorname{Homeo}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}({\mathbb{R}})=\{f\in\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}({\mathbb{R}}): f(x+1)=f(x)+1\text{ for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}\}. \] The supplied exposition identifies this group as the universal covering group of $\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}(S^1)$. Its projection $p$ is given by $p(f)(x+{\mathbb{Z}})=f(x)+{\mathbb{Z}}$.

For a nontrivial group homomorphism \[ \rho:\pi_1(M)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}(S^1), \] a lift means a group homomorphism $\widetilde\rho:\pi_1(M)\to\operatorname{Homeo}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}({\mathbb{R}})$ satisfying $p\circ\widetilde\rho=\rho$. The source describes an Euler class $e(\rho)\in H^2(M)$ as the obstruction to such a lift. It does not specify the cohomology coefficients; under the usual integral Euler-class convention, this notation would mean $e(\rho)\in H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})$. This coefficient interpretation requires confirmation.

A group $G$ is left-orderable if it admits a strict total order $<$ such that $g<h$ implies $ag<ah$ for every $a,g,h\in G$. The excerpt discusses representations arising from foliations and flows on $M$, their use in establishing left-orderability of $\pi_1(M)$ in unspecified situations, and analogous existence results for taut foliations in connection with the L-space conjecture.
