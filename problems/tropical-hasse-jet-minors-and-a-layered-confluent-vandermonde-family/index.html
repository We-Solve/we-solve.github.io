Math-223

Tropical Hasse-jet minors and a layered confluent-Vandermonde family

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Ravi Vakil proposes a tropical approach to interpolation of plane curves with specified multiple points: add conditions until an expected one-dimensional system is reached, then seek an isolated, non-deforming tropical solution that lifts. The supplied verification identifies the trailing extracted "7" as the next problem heading, so it is not part of this question.

For degree $d\ge0$, multiplicities $m_i\ge0$, and general distinct points $p_i\in{\mathbb{P}}^2$, write \[ V=H^0\left({\mathbb{P}}^2,\mathcal O(d)\otimes\bigcap_i\mathcal I_{p_i}^{m_i}\right),\quad N=\binom{d+2}{2},\quad M=\sum_i\binom{m_i+1}{2}. \] The naive expected vector-space dimension is $\max(0,N-M)$; projective dimension is one less when $V\ne0$. The source calls the intended statement SHGH and says positive expected dimension should give the right number of solutions, but does not state the exceptional-special-system qualifications of that conjecture. A blanket assertion that all positive-expected-dimension systems impose independent conditions is not a precise substitute for SHGH. The meaning of "expected dimension 1" must also distinguish vector dimension one, a single projective parameter point, from a projective pencil.

The attached unreviewed aid proposes a sufficient tropical-linear certificate for independence. Use Hasse jets, the coefficient functionals in local Taylor expansions of orders below $m_i$, rather than characteristic-dependent ordinary derivatives. If $M\le N-1$, add $N-1-M$ disjoint simple point-evaluation rows to their coefficient matrix. A noncancelling initial determinant of an $(N-1)$-square minor certifies augmented rank $N-1$, hence one reduced projective parameter point and independence of the original rows. The valued field, valuation and noncancellation/lifting conventions require specification; this is not a stable-map correspondence theorem.

For the explicit range $\sum_i m_i\le d+1$, the aid supplies a characteristic-free layered confluent-Vandermonde minor, with distinct parameters $a_i$, whose determinant is \[ \pm\prod_{b\ge0}\prod_{i<j}(a_j-a_i)^{(m_i-b)_+(m_j-b)_+},\qquad (t)_+=\max(t,0). \] The product is finite. It reports a unit-initial certificate and extension of independent original rows by distinct disjoint simple evaluations to rank $N-1$ when the expected vector dimension is positive. This restricted family and criterion do not establish general SHGH or provide the missing tropical moduli-space and liftability theorem requested by the proposal.

Short English statement

Seek isolated liftable tropical certificates for plane interpolation with multiple points. The aid gives a matrix-rank certificate and a restricted characteristic-free determinant family; the original SHGH shorthand and dimension normalization need qualification.
