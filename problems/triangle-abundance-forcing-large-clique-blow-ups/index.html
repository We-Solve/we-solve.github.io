Triangle Abundance Forcing Large Clique Blow-Ups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual convention, let a graph mean a finite simple undirected graph. For such a graph $G$, let $\tau(G)$ be the number of unordered three-element subsets of $V(G)$ that span a triangle. Write $\log$ for the natural logarithm. For an integer $t\geq 1$, a $t$-blow-up of $K_3$ is the complete tripartite graph $K_{t,t,t}$. Containment here is interpreted as ordinary subgraph containment: there are pairwise disjoint sets $A,B,D\subseteq V(G)$, each of size $t$, with every edge between distinct sets present; edges within these sets are unrestricted. These conventions are not explicitly specified in the supplied record and require source confirmation.

The supplied clean and current statements ask whether \[ \begin{gathered} \forall C>0\ \exists\gamma=\gamma(C)>0\ \exists n_0=n_0(C)\in{\mathbb{N}},\quad n_0\geq 3,\\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{N}},\ n\geq n_0,\quad \forall G\text{ with }|V(G)|=n,\\ \tau(G)\geq \frac{n^{3/2}}{\exp(C\sqrt{\log n})} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists t\in{\mathbb{N}}:\quad t\geq\gamma\sqrt{\log n} \ \text{ and }\ K_{t,t,t}\subseteq G. \end{gathered} \] Here $\subseteq$ denotes subgraph containment, without an induced-subgraph requirement.

There is an unresolved discrepancy in the supplied evidence: the field original_statement instead asks for \[ t\geq\gamma\log n,\qquad \gamma=\gamma(C), \] under the same triangle-count hypothesis. With the positive-constant and sufficiently-large-$n$ quantifiers made explicit as above, this is a stronger target than the square-root-logarithmic target. The original field does not itself explicitly state all of those quantifiers or the positivity of $\gamma$. The record labels the clean statement corrected_verified and reports that it was checked against the official report; that is supplied verification metadata, not independent verification in this draft. Both targets are retained here, and their discrepancy requires human source review.

Short English statement

The supplied corrected formulation asks whether, for every fixed $C>0$, sufficiently many triangles force a complete tripartite subgraph with each part of size at least $\gamma(C)\sqrt{\log n}$, where $\gamma(C)>0$. The recorded original formulation instead requires $\gamma(C)\log n$. The supplied status is open; the conflicting targets and implicit graph conventions remain subject to human review.
