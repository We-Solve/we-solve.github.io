Math-3638

Averaging Times for $H^p$ Dirichlet Polynomials

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N\in{\mathbb{N}}$, $N\geq1$, and $C>1$. A Dirichlet polynomial supported on $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ is a function \[ f(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n n^{-s},\qquad a_n\in{\mathbb{C}},\quad s\in{\mathbb{C}}. \] This is the meaning of "degree at most $N$" used in the supplied formula.

The supplied record does not define $\|f\|_p$. Under the conventional Dirichlet Hardy interpretation, for finite $p>0$ one uses \[ \|f\|_p^p=\lim_{R\to\infty}\frac1R\int_0^R|f(it)|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] The statement below is conditional on this interpretation of the norm; its agreement with the report's definition requires review.

For $1\leq p<2$, define the set of admissible averaging times by \[ \mathcal A_p(N,C)=\left\{T>0:\ \frac1T\int_0^T\left|\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n n^{-it}\right|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t \leq C\left\|\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n n^{-s}\right\|_p^p \ \text{for every }(a_1,\ldots,a_N)\in{\mathbb{C}}^N\right\}. \] The supplied formulation calls $T_p(N,C)$ the least element of $\mathcal A_p(N,C)$. Thus, wherever that least element exists, its literal definition is \[ T_p(N,C)=\min\mathcal A_p(N,C). \] There is an unresolved boundary qualification: for $N=1$, every polynomial is constant and $\mathcal A_p(1,C)=(0,\infty)$, which has no least element. The record also does not specify whether an infimum or a threshold valid for every larger averaging time was intended. Neither convention is silently substituted here.

Problem, subject to clarification of the averaging-time definition. Determine the dependence of $T_p(N,C)$ on $p$. In particular, is it true that, for each fixed $C>1$ and each fixed $p\in[1,2)$, there exist constants $K(p,C)>0$ and $N_0(p,C)\in{\mathbb{N}}$ such that \[ T_p(N,C)\leq K(p,C)N\qquad\text{for every integer }N\geq N_0(p,C)? \] This spells out the supplied target $T_p(N,C)=O(N)$ with dependence of the implicit constant on $p$ and $C$ allowed. The general question does not specify a domain for $p$ beyond the explicitly stated range in this particular target. The original statement refers to a definition in connection with the triangle Hilbert integral; that definition is not reproduced in the supplied record.

Short English statement

For every Dirichlet polynomial supported on the first $N$ positive integers, compare its average $p$-th power on an initial vertical interval with its Dirichlet Hardy mean. The question asks how the required averaging time depends on $p$, especially whether it is $O(N)$ for each fixed $1\leq p<2$ and $C>1$. The norm convention and the meaning of "least averaging time" require clarification before this becomes an unconditional precise statement.
