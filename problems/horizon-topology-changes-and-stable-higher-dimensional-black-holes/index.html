Definitions and conventions.
The Gregory--Laflamme instability is an instability of extended higher-dimensional black objects associated with long-wavelength perturbations. Horizon topology is the topology of a horizon cross-section; a topology-changing evolution must distinguish apparent and event horizons. Myers--Perry metrics are the higher-dimensional rotating vacuum black-hole family. Stability requires a chosen perturbation class. A scaling test of the quoted spin bound.
Keep the source expression $J/M^{D-3}$ exactly as written. Under a vacuum length rescaling by $s>0$, the usual asymptotic mass and angular momentum coefficients scale as $M\mapsto s^{D-3}M$, $J\mapsto s^{D-2}J$: these are the powers of the leading mass and rotation terms in the asymptotic metric. Consequently \[ \frac{J_s}{M_s^{D-3}}=s^{D-2-(D-3)^2}\frac{J}{M^{D-3}}. \] For every integer $D>4$, the exponent is negative: it is $-1$ at $D=5$ and decreases thereafter. Thus, conditional on a scale-invariant stable family with nonzero spin, this ratio has no uniform scale-independent upper bound as $s\downarrow0$. In contrast $J/M^{(D-2)/(D-3)}$ is invariant under this calculation.
