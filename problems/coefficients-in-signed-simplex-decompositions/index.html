It does not specify the admissible sets $P$, the ambient dimension, the normalization of the measures, or the construction that determines the simplices and coefficients.

Short English statement

In the intended signed simplex decomposition of the uniform measure on $P$, must every nonzero simplex coefficient equal $1$ or $-1$? If not, which nonzero coefficients can occur?
