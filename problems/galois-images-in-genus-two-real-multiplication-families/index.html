Galois Images in Genus-Two Real-Multiplication Families

Definitions and mathematical statement

Put \[ \mathcal O=\mathbb Z\!\left[\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right],\qquad f_t(x)=x^5-5x^3+5x+t. \] The supplied source considers the one-parameter family $C_t:y^2=f_t(x)$ and attributes real multiplication by $\mathcal O$ to its Jacobians. A genus-two curve here must mean the smooth projective model of this affine equation at a parameter for which the polynomial is squarefree. Singular parameters cannot be included as smooth genus-two fibers.

The source does not specify a base field or the intended range of parameters. To define the arithmetic objects conditionally, let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $t\in K$ satisfy the squarefreeness condition. Write $J_t=\operatorname{Jac}(C_t)$, choose an algebraic closure $\overline K$, and put $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K/K)$. These are auxiliary choices for explaining the question, not a claim that the source quantifies over all such fields and parameters. Real multiplication means an embedding \[ \iota:\mathcal O\hookrightarrow\operatorname{End}_{\overline K}(J_t). \] Whether the intended embedding is defined over $K$, and what compatibility with the polarization is required, are not specified in the supplied record.

For a positive integer $N$, define \[ J_t[N](\overline K)=\{P\in J_t(\overline K):NP=0\},\qquad \rho_{t,N}:G_K\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z}\left(J_t[N](\overline K)\right) \] by the natural Galois action. For a prime $\ell$, the $\ell$-adic Tate module and its action are \[ T_\ell(J_t)=\varprojlim_n J_t[\ell^n](\overline K),\qquad \rho_{t,\ell^\infty}:G_K\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(T_\ell(J_t)), \] where the transition maps are multiplication by $\ell$. An alternative possible meaning of the source's full Tate-module question is the action on $\prod_{\ell}T_\ell(J_t)$; the source does not distinguish these targets.

Source question. Inside the two-dimensional real-multiplication moduli space referred to by the source, determine in what sense the image of this one-dimensional family is special, and determine which level structure the family fixes. Determine whether an exceptional positive integer $N$ can force the mod-$N$ Galois image to be smaller than expected, and determine the image on the intended Tate module.

A fully specified arithmetic question still requires a choice of base field, generic fiber or specialization, parameter quantifiers, and comparison group for the expected image. The comparison group must account for the intended real-multiplication and polarization data and their fields of definition. The source supplies none of these choices explicitly. In particular, the displayed conditional representations do not resolve whether the question concerns arithmetic Galois images, geometric monodromy, or both; no particular interpretation or expected maximal group is asserted here.

Short English statement

For the smooth genus-two members of $y^2=x^5-5x^3+5x+t$, whose Jacobians are stated to have real multiplication by $\mathbb Z[(1+\sqrt5)/2]$, describe the family's special position in the two-dimensional real-multiplication moduli space, its fixed level structure, and its torsion and Tate-module Galois images. The base field, parameter quantifiers, expected image, and meaning of the Tate-module target remain unspecified.
