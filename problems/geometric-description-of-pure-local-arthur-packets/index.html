Geometric Description of Pure Local Arthur Packets

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a group denoted by $G$ over a field denoted by $F$, but does not specify their admissible class. Its notation $\Pi^{\mathrm{pure}}(G/F)$ denotes the collection of representation labels $[\pi,\delta]$ across the pure forms under consideration. The equivalence relation on these labels and the precise meaning of the pure-form datum $\delta$ are not supplied. For an Arthur parameter $\psi$ of $G$, the notation $\Pi^{\mathrm{pure},\psi}(G/F)$ denotes the pure local Arthur packet whose geometric description is sought. The symbols $P(\pi,\delta)$, $\operatorname{Ev}^{0}_{\psi}$, and $A_{\psi}$ denote, respectively, the associated geometric object, the evaluation operation, and the group acting on the resulting object in the intended construction; their definitions and normalization conventions are absent from the supplied record.

For readability, put \[ V_{\psi}(\pi,\delta):=\operatorname{Ev}^{0}_{\psi}\left(P(\pi,\delta)\right). \] The proposed trace identity presupposes that this object carries an $A_{\psi}$-action for which the displayed trace is defined. This is a requirement of the intended formulation, not a construction established by the supplied record.

Proposed statement. For every Arthur parameter $\psi$ of $G$, is it true that \[ \Pi^{\mathrm{pure},\psi}(G/F) = \left\{[\pi,\delta]\in\Pi^{\mathrm{pure}}(G/F): V_{\psi}(\pi,\delta)\neq 0\right\}, \] and that, for every $[\pi,\delta]\in\Pi^{\mathrm{pure},\psi}(G/F)$ and every $a\in A_{\psi}$, \[ \langle a,[\pi,\delta]\rangle_{\psi} = \operatorname{Trace}\!\left(V_{\psi}(\pi,\delta)(a)\right)? \] Here the pairing on the left is the intended Arthur-packet pairing, whose normalization is likewise unspecified. These identities retain the supplied mathematical target, but a standalone precise conjecture requires the missing hypotheses on $F$ and $G$, the parameter conventions, and the definitions and normalizations of the packet, geometric construction, action, and pairing. The supplied literature assessment describes a universal target involving all pure forms and reductive groups; it does not provide the hypotheses needed to formalize that scope.

Short English statement

The proposed identities characterize membership in a pure local Arthur packet by nonvanishing of a geometric evaluation and recover the packet pairing as the trace of the resulting group action. The source leaves the field, group hypotheses, and geometric and normalization conventions unspecified, so this is a qualified statement-only draft requiring mathematical review. The supplied label partially_solved is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
