Poonen-Rains Selmer Distributions in Twist Families

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record describes a distribution problem but does not specify enough hypotheses to determine a unique conjecture. The following is a precise template for its number-field interpretation, suggested by the norms to ${\mathbb{Q}}$ appearing in the excerpt; the record does not explicitly specify the intended domain of $K$.

Let $K$ be a number field, let $E/K$ be an elliptic curve, and let $p$ be a prime. For each place $v$ of $K$, write $K_v$ for the completion. Write $E[p]$ for the kernel of multiplication by $p$, and define \[ \operatorname{Sel}_p(E/K) :=\ker\!\left( H^1(K,E[p])\longrightarrow \prod_{v\text{ a place of }K}H^1(K_v,E)[p] \right), \] where the maps are localization followed by the maps induced by $E[p]\hookrightarrow E$, and $[p]$ denotes the subgroup killed by $p$. This is a finite-dimensional ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector space. Its dimension bounds above the Mordell--Weil rank of $E(K)$.

In the quadratic-twist setting explicitly discussed in the excerpt, a nontrivial square class $d\in K^\times/(K^\times)^2$ determines a quadratic extension $K(\sqrt d)/K$ and a quadratic twist $E_d/K$. Let $\mathcal F(X)$ denote the intended finite family of such extensions with bound $X$. Its defining restrictions and ordering are absent from the supplied record. Once these data and the necessary hypotheses on $E,K,p$ have been specified, the stated distribution target is that, for every integer $j\geq 0$, \[ \lim_{X\to\infty} \frac{\#\{K(\sqrt d)\in\mathcal F(X): \dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}\operatorname{Sel}_p(E_d/K)=j\}} {\#\mathcal F(X)} =\operatorname{PR}_p(j), \qquad \operatorname{PR}_p(j) =\left(\prod_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1+p^{-k}}\right) \left(\prod_{k=1}^{j}\frac{p}{p^k-1}\right), \] with the second product equal to $1$ when $j=0$. The probability formula is a reconstruction of the damaged formula in the supplied excerpt and requires source verification. The intended ordering must make the denominator nonzero for sufficiently large $X$ and permit the limiting question. This template does not assert the formula for every elliptic curve or every choice of twist family or ordering. The cleaned statement asks for the full natural family beyond currently proved primes, fields, and conditional regimes, but does not identify that family or those regimes; its scope may also extend beyond quadratic twists.

The excerpt supplies the following separate conditional background assertion. For an odd prime $p$, define \[ \mathcal F(p,X)=\{L/K:\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}, \ N_{K/{\mathbb{Q}}}(\operatorname{rad}(\Delta_{L/K}))\leq X\}, \] where $\Delta_{L/K}$ is the relative discriminant ideal and the radical of a nonzero integral ideal is the product of its distinct prime ideal divisors. Under the extended Riemann hypothesis and the condition $\operatorname{Gal}(K(E[p])/K)\supseteq\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p)$, interpreted through the action on $E[p]$, the excerpt reports that for every integer $j\geq0$, \[ \liminf_{X\to\infty} \frac{\#\{L\in\mathcal F(p,X): \operatorname{rank}E(L)-\operatorname{rank}E(K)\leq(p-1)j\}} {\#\mathcal F(p,X)} \geq\sum_{i=0}^{j}\operatorname{PR}_p(i). \] This is supplied background attributed to joint work with Daniel Keliher, not an independently verified theorem here and not the requested full Selmer distribution. The excerpt also introduces superelliptic equations $C_d:dy^p=f(x)$, with $p$ odd, $f\in K[x]$ of degree $3$, and bound $N_{K/{\mathbb{Q}}}(\operatorname{rad}((d)))\leq X$; it supplies no corresponding conclusion and leaves the parameter conventions and smoothness hypotheses unspecified.

Short English statement

The intended problem asks for the Poonen--Rains probabilities for every possible $p$-Selmer dimension in the full intended twist family. The family, ordering, field domain, and necessary hypotheses are missing, so the displayed limit is a formulation template requiring review. The supplied conditional cyclic-extension rank bound is separate background. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified assessment.
