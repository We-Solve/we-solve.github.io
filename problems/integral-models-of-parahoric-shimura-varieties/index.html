Math-4410

Integral Models of Parahoric Shimura Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(G,X)$ denote a Shimura datum, where $G$ is a connected reductive group over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $E=E(G,X)$ be its reflex field. For a compact open subgroup $K\subset G({\mathbb{A}}_f)$, write $\operatorname{Sh}_K(G,X)$ for the Shimura variety over $E$; after choosing an embedding $E\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$, its complex points have the description \[ \operatorname{Sh}_K(G,X)({\mathbb{C}}) =G({\mathbb{Q}})\backslash\left(X\times G({\mathbb{A}}_f)/K\right), \] where ${\mathbb{A}}_f$ denotes the finite adeles of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. Parahoric level at a prime $p$ refers to a factorization $K=K_pK^p$, where $K_p\subset G({\mathbb{Q}}_p)$ is a parahoric subgroup in the sense of Bruhat--Tits theory and $K^p\subset G({\mathbb{A}}_f^p)$ is a compact open subgroup away from $p$. The precise convention for the parahoric subgroup is not specified in the supplied record.

To display the missing formulation choices, suppose a place $v\mid p$ of $E$ is chosen and put $B=\mathcal O_{E,v}$. In a scheme formulation, a flat integral model over $B$ consists of a flat $B$-scheme $\mathscr S$ together with an identification \[ \iota:\mathscr S\times_B E\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Sh}_K(G,X). \] This describes one possible local framework; the supplied record does not choose this base, the category of models, or additional conditions such as finite type, separatedness, normality, or an extension property.

Statement requested by the source. Determine the existence of integral models of Shimura varieties with parahoric level structure, and characterize their uniqueness. A precise version would have to specify the allowed Shimura data, primes, levels, bases, and a defining property $\mathcal P$ of the models, then determine for which allowed data a pair $(\mathscr S,\iota)$ satisfying $\mathcal P$ exists and in what sense it is unique. For example, uniqueness up to an isomorphism compatible with the generic-fibre identifications asks, for two such models, for a $B$-isomorphism $f:\mathscr S_1\to\mathscr S_2$ with \[ \iota_2\circ f_E=\iota_1; \] uniqueness of this compatible isomorphism is a further possible requirement. The source does not distinguish these meanings or supply $\mathcal P$. Thus the supplied question is a broad existence-and-characterization target, not a fully quantified assertion of existence or uniqueness for every Shimura datum.

Short English statement

The problem asks when Shimura varieties with parahoric level admit integral models and which conditions characterize those models uniquely. The supplied statement leaves the permitted data, base, category of models, defining properties, and meaning of uniqueness unspecified, so it requires clarification before it can become a single precise conjecture.
