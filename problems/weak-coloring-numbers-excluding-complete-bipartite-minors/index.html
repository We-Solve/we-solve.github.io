Weak Coloring Numbers Excluding Complete Bipartite Minors

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a conventional formulation, let $G$ be a nonempty finite simple undirected graph, let $r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$, and let $t\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}$. These domain conventions are not explicitly specified in the supplied source. Let $K_{3,t}$ denote the complete bipartite graph with parts of sizes $3$ and $t$. A graph is a minor of $G$ if it can be obtained by deleting vertices, deleting edges, and contracting edges, with loops removed and parallel edges identified. Say that $G$ excludes $K_{3,t}$ as a minor if no minor of $G$ is isomorphic to $K_{3,t}$.

For a linear order $\preceq$ on $V(G)$, define \[ \operatorname{WReach}_r(G,\preceq,v) =\left\{u\in V(G):\begin{array}{l}\text{there is a path }P\text{ from }v\text{ to }u\text{ of length at most }r,\\ u\preceq x\text{ for every }x\in V(P)\end{array}\right\}. \] Paths of length zero are allowed, so $v\in\operatorname{WReach}_r(G,\preceq,v)$. The $r$-th weak coloring number is \[ \operatorname{wcol}_r(G) =\min_{\preceq}\max_{v\in V(G)} \left|\operatorname{WReach}_r(G,\preceq,v)\right|, \] where the minimum is over all linear orders on $V(G)$. Define the extremal function, with its supremum understood in the extended nonnegative reals, by \[ F_t(r)=\sup\left\{\operatorname{wcol}_r(G):G\text{ is a nonempty finite simple undirected graph excluding }K_{3,t}\text{ as a minor}\right\}. \] Problem. Determine an asymptotically sharp bound for $F_t(r)$. Under the interpretation that $t$ is fixed and $r\to\infty$, this asks for an explicit positive function $f_t$ and constants $c_t,C_t>0$ and $r_t\geq 0$ such that, for every integer $r\geq r_t$, \[ c_t f_t(r)\leq F_t(r)\leq C_t f_t(r). \] The upper bound must hold for every graph in the indicated class, and the lower bound describes extremal growth over that class; the witnessing graphs may depend on $r$. The source does not specify the intended range of $t$, whether $t$ is fixed, or whether sharp dependence on both $t$ and $r$ is required. Thus the fixed-$t$ formulation above is an explicitly qualified interpretation requiring review, rather than a resolved choice of asymptotic regime.

Short English statement

Determine the largest possible $r$-th weak coloring number among graphs with no $K_{3,t}$ minor, up to asymptotically matching upper and lower bounds. The intended range of $t$ and the dependence required on $t$ remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; that label is not independently verified here.
