Math-3758

Fundamental Groups of Five-Dimensional Log-Canonical Singularities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $G$ is finitely presented if there exist finite sets of generators and relations such that $G\cong\langle g_1,\ldots,g_r\mid R_1,\ldots,R_s\rangle$.

For a conventional complex, boundary-free interpretation, a log-canonical singularity is a normal complex germ $(X,x)$ whose canonical divisor $K_X$ is ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier and whose discrepancies are at least $-1$. More explicitly, for every log resolution $f:Y\to X$, writing \[ K_Y=f^*K_X+\sum_{E\text{ exceptional}}a(E,X)E, \] one requires $a(E,X)\geq -1$ for every exceptional prime divisor $E$. This is the log-canonical condition for the pair $(X,0)$.

The supplied question asks whether \[ \forall G\text{ finitely presented},\quad \exists (X,x)\text{ log-canonical with }\dim X=5 \quad\text{such that}\quad \pi_1^{\mathrm{loc}}(X,x)\cong G. \] Here dimension is complex dimension under the conventional complex interpretation above. The notation $\pi_1^{\mathrm{loc}}$ requires a source convention that is not specified in the supplied extract. In particular, two possible topological meanings are \[ \pi_1(U\setminus\{x\},b) \qquad\text{and}\qquad \pi_1(U\cap X_{\mathrm{reg}},b), \] where $U$ is a sufficiently small representative neighborhood of $x$, $X_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is the smooth locus, and $b\in U\cap X_{\mathrm{reg}}$. The first is the punctured-neighborhood interpretation, equivalently the fundamental group of the link in the usual complex analytic setting. The second removes the entire singular locus. They coincide for an isolated singularity but need not coincide in general.

Unresolved qualifications. The record imposes no isolated-singularity hypothesis and does not specify which local fundamental group is intended, whether the category is algebraic or analytic, or the ground field explicitly. The complex, boundary-free definition above is therefore a conditional interpretation, not a verified expansion of the source. The realization question must retain these unresolved conventions pending source review; no isolatedness assumption or boundary divisor is added.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every finitely presented group can be realized as the local fundamental group of a log-canonical singularity of dimension five. The meaning of local fundamental group, the geometric category, and the precise singularity conventions require confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
