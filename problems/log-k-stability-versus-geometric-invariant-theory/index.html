Log K-Stability versus Geometric Invariant Theory

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the supplied permanent UnsolvedMath record $30004174$, let $X$ be a K-polystable Fano variety. The ground field and permitted singularities of $X$ are not specified. For a positive integer $l$ such that $-lK_X$ is Cartier, write \[ V_l=H^0\left(X,\mathcal O_X(-lK_X)\right). \] A nonzero section $s_D\in V_l$ defines an effective divisor $D=\operatorname{div}(s_D)\in|-lK_X|$; its scalar class is $[s_D]\in\mathbb P(V_l)$. This explains the usual section--divisor interpretation of the source notation $H^0(X,K_X^{-l})$, but the source does not specify the allowed integers $l$ or explicitly state the divisor hypothesis.

K-polystability and log K-polystability refer to the corresponding test-configuration stability notions: the relevant Donaldson--Futaki invariant is nonnegative, with equality permitted only for product configurations, subject to the conventions and admissibility hypotheses of the chosen setting. GIT stability means geometric invariant theory stability for a specified group action and linearization. The source names the natural $\operatorname{Aut}(X)$-action on plurianticanonical sections, but does not specify the linearization or whether stability is taken in $V_l$ or $\mathbb P(V_l)$.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such K-polystable Fano variety $X$ and for sufficiently large $\beta$, the equivalence \[ (X,(1-\beta)D)\text{ is log K-polystable} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{a section defining }D\text{ is GIT-stable under }\operatorname{Aut}(X) \] holds. This is a statement of the intended target, with unresolved parameters, rather than a fully quantified conjecture: the source gives neither the range of $\beta$, the quantification over $l$ and $D$, nor the permitted dependence of the threshold for $\beta$. In particular, interpreting "sufficiently large" as approaching $1$ from below would require an additional convention absent from the record.

There is also an unresolved normalization issue. If $D\in|-lK_X|$ is used without rescaling, then \[ -\left(K_X+(1-\beta)D\right) \sim_{\mathbb R}\left(1-l(1-\beta)\right)(-K_X). \] Thus the usual effective-boundary log Fano interpretation requires appropriate coefficient, singularity, and positivity hypotheses. Replacing $D$ by $D/l$ would change the displayed pair and is not authorized by the supplied statement. Finally, the source explicitly compares log K-polystability with GIT stability; it does not resolve whether this distinction is intentional.

Short English statement

The question asks whether adding a sufficiently small divisor boundary to a K-polystable Fano variety makes log K-polystability equivalent to GIT stability of the section defining that divisor. The phrase "sufficiently small boundary" would correspond to $\beta$ near $1$ from below, an interpretation that the source does not explicitly specify. Divisor normalization, parameter quantifiers, and the precise GIT notion require clarification before a fully precise conjecture can be stated.
