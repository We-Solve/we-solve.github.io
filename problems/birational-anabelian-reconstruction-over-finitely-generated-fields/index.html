Birational Anabelian Reconstruction over Finitely Generated Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a finitely generated field, understood in the usual sense as finitely generated over its prime field. The supplied question concerns every pair of $K$-varieties $X,Y$ with $\dim X\geq 1$ and $\dim Y\geq 1$. To interpret their function fields, suppose that "variety" means an integral separated scheme of finite type over $K$; the record does not explicitly specify this convention or impose geometric integrality, smoothness, or normality. Write $L_X=K(X)$ and $L_Y=K(Y)$.

Define $\operatorname{Isom}_K(L_X,L_Y)$ to be the set of field isomorphisms $L_X\to L_Y$ fixing $K$. Let $\operatorname{Bir}_K(Y,X)$ be the set of birational maps $Y\dashrightarrow X$ defined over $K$, with rational maps identified when they agree on a common dense open subset. Pullback reverses direction: a birational map $f:Y\dashrightarrow X$ induces $f^*:L_X\to L_Y$. This reversal must be accounted for when interpreting the source's first canonical map.

For a field $L$, choose a separable closure $L^{\mathrm{sep}}$ and write \[ G_L=\operatorname{Gal}(L^{\mathrm{sep}}/L), \] with its profinite topology. One possible meaning of an outer Galois-group isomorphism is an element of \[ \operatorname{OutIsom}(G_{L_Y},G_{L_X}) = \operatorname{Isom}_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_{L_Y},G_{L_X}) \big/\operatorname{Inn}(G_{L_X}), \] where the quotient identifies isomorphisms differing by conjugation in the target. A field isomorphism $L_X\to L_Y$ induces such an outer isomorphism in the displayed reverse direction.

Question recorded by the source. For every $K,X,Y$ as above, are the canonical maps from $K$-isomorphisms of function fields to $K$-birational isomorphisms and then to the corresponding outer Galois-group isomorphisms bijective? With directions made explicit, the requested pattern is \[ \operatorname{Isom}_K(L_X,L_Y) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Bir}_K(Y,X) \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_K(G_{L_Y},G_{L_X}), \] where $\mathcal{O}_K$ denotes the intended target in the source, whose definition is unresolved. In particular, the supplied record does not say whether this target is the full outer-isomorphism set defined above or a relative version requiring compatibility with restriction to $G_K$. If compatibility is intended, the required equality or equality up to conjugacy, and the permitted inner conjugations, must be specified. Thus the display records the requested assertion but does not supply a fully determined final target.

The record also leaves positive-characteristic and inseparability qualifications unspecified: it does not say whether ordinary function fields or their perfect closures are intended, or whether additional restrictions on $K$, $X$, and $Y$ are required. These omissions prevent a unique precise formulation of the full conjecture. The background additionally mentions broader anabelian-variety and generic-point assertions, but supplies no mathematical formulations of them; they are not additional statements that can be formalized from this record.

Short English statement

The source asks whether function-field isomorphisms, birational maps, and suitable outer Galois-group isomorphisms determine one another for positive-dimensional varieties over finitely generated fields. A precise final conjecture requires clarification of the Galois-group target, compatibility with the base field, and inseparability conventions. The supplied partially_solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
