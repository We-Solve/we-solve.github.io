Math-7291

Isoperimetric-Defect Corrections to Faber–Krahn Bounds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ be a nonempty open convex domain with finite positive area $A=|\Omega|$ and finite perimeter $L=\mathcal H^1(\partial\Omega)$. Interpret $\lambda_1(\Omega)$ as the first Dirichlet eigenvalue of the Laplacian, namely \[ \lambda_1(\Omega)=\inf_{0\ne u\in H_0^1(\Omega)}\frac{\int_\Omega |\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}{\int_\Omega |u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}, \] where $H_0^1(\Omega)$ is the closure of $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ in the Sobolev space $H^1(\Omega)$. Let $j_{0,1}$ denote the first positive zero of the Bessel function $J_0$ of the first kind.

The question is whether \[ \exists K>0\quad\forall\Omega\text{ as above},\qquad \lambda_1(\Omega)\geq\frac{\pi j_{0,1}^2}{A}+K\frac{L^2-4\pi A}{A^2}. \] The constant $K$ must be independent of $\Omega$. The supplied original statement attributes to the report the contrary conjecture: for every $K>0$, there exists such a convex domain $\Omega$ satisfying \[ \lambda_1(\Omega)<\frac{\pi j_{0,1}^2}{A}+K\frac{L^2-4\pi A}{A^2}. \] In the terminology of the supplied record, this asks whether the optimal coefficient of the isoperimetric defect $L^2-4\pi A$ is zero.

The Dirichlet boundary condition and the definitions of $\lambda_1$ and $j_{0,1}$ are the standard interpretation suggested by the supplied Faber--Krahn context; they are not explicitly specified in the supplied statement and require source confirmation. The domain conventions above make the quantities precise. The original statement begins with a simply connected planar domain but explicitly restricts the proposed inequality to convex domains; convex domains are simply connected, so the question here retains the convex restriction.

Short English statement

Does the first Dirichlet eigenvalue of every planar convex domain exceed the disk's equal-area eigenvalue by a universal positive multiple of its perimeter defect divided by the square of its area? The supplied original record attributes the conjecture that no positive coefficient works to the report. The boundary-condition interpretation requires confirmation, and the supplied open label is not independently verified.
