Math-1010

Distributions of Class-Group Ranks and $S_5$ Extensions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime $p$. For a positive integer $D$, let $K_D={\mathbb{Q}}(\sqrt{-D})$ and define \[ r_p(D)=\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}\left(\operatorname{Cl}(K_D)/p\operatorname{Cl}(K_D)\right), \] using additive notation for the ideal class group. The first group of questions asks, as $0<D<X$ and $X\to\infty$, for the mean of $p^{r_p(D)}$, the distribution of $r_p(D)$, and upper bounds depending on $D$.

If $\mathcal I(X)$ denotes the intended sample of those integers, the quantities to determine include \[ \frac{1}{|\mathcal I(X)|}\sum_{D\in\mathcal I(X)}p^{r_p(D)}, \qquad \frac{|\{D\in\mathcal I(X):r_p(D)=j\}|}{|\mathcal I(X)|} \quad(j\geq0), \] including their limiting behavior where limits exist, and pointwise upper estimates for $r_p(D)$. The source does not say whether the sample comprises all positive integers, squarefree radicands, or a discriminant-restricted family. In particular $D$ as written is a radicand parameter, not automatically the absolute field discriminant; repeated radicands can represent the same quadratic field.

For the second group, retain $n(D)$ as the source's count of "$S_5$-extensions of ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with squarefree discriminant $D$". For the intended sample $\mathcal J(X)\subset\{D\in{\mathbb{Z}}:0<|D|<X\}$, the requested mean and distribution are \[ \frac{1}{|\mathcal J(X)|}\sum_{D\in\mathcal J(X)}n(D), \qquad \frac{|\{D\in\mathcal J(X):n(D)=j\}|}{|\mathcal J(X)|} \quad(j\geq0), \] together with pointwise upper bounds for $n(D)$ in terms of its discriminant size. It is not stated whether $S_5$ means a quintic field whose normal closure has Galois group $S_5$, or a Galois extension itself with group $S_5$; these are distinct degrees and discriminants. Counting up to ${\mathbb{Q}}$-isomorphism and whether nonsquarefree integers are excluded or assigned count zero also require confirmation. No predicted probability law, mean constant, or bound is invented.

Short English statement

Determine means, distributions and pointwise bounds for quadratic class-group $p$-ranks and, separately, counts of $S_5$ fields with a given squarefree discriminant. The sampling conventions and the meaning of $S_5$-extension are not specified.
