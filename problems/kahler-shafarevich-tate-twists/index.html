Kähler Shafarevich-Tate Twists

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be an irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold: a compact, simply connected Kähler manifold with $H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)={\mathbb{C}}\sigma$, where $\sigma$ is everywhere nondegenerate. Write $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X=2n$. Let $\pi:X\to B$ be a Lagrangian fibration, meaning a proper surjective holomorphic map with connected fibres whose smooth fibres have complex dimension $n$ and on which $\sigma$ restricts to zero.

A Shafarevich--Tate twist $\pi^{\phi}:X^{\phi}\to B$ is obtained by gluing local copies of this fibration using the fibrewise automorphisms admitted by the Shafarevich--Tate construction. Denote its group of twist parameters by $\mathcal T_{\pi}$. This notation is a placeholder: the supplied record does not define the automorphism sheaf, parameter group, or equivalence relation used in that construction. The notation rendered as $X$ and $X_0$ in the extracted parameter-group expressions is denoted here by $\mathcal T_{\pi}$ and $\mathcal T_{\pi,0}$, respectively, to distinguish it from the manifold $X$. The meaning of the subgroup $\mathcal T_{\pi,0}$ must be recovered from the source.

A complex manifold is Kähler if it admits a closed real $(1,1)$-form that is positive definite. The requested problem is: for each fibration $\pi$ as above, give necessary and sufficient conditions on every admissible class $\phi\in\mathcal T_{\pi}$ for $X^{\phi}$ to be Kähler; equivalently, characterize \[ \mathcal K_{\pi}=\{\phi\in\mathcal T_{\pi}:X^{\phi}\text{ is K\"ahler}\}. \]

The supplied excerpt reports the following partial results, subject to the source's definitions and any hypotheses omitted from the extraction. If there exists $r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$ such that $r\phi\in\mathcal T_{\pi,0}$, then $X^{\phi}$ is Kähler. It also uses the Leray differential \[ d_2^{0,2}:H^0(B,R^2\pi_*{\mathbb{Q}}_X)\longrightarrow H^2(B,R^1\pi_*{\mathbb{Q}}_X) \] and a supplied identification \[ H^2(B,R^1\pi_*{\mathbb{Q}}_X)\simeq(\mathcal T_{\pi}/\mathcal T_{\pi,0})\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}. \] Let $\bar\phi$ denote the image of $\phi$ under this identification. Writing $b_2(Y)=\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H^2(Y,{\mathbb{Q}})$, the excerpt reports:

  • If $\bar\phi\in\operatorname{im}(d_2^{0,2})$, then $b_2(X^{\phi})=b_2(X)$, and there is a degree-two cohomology class on $X^{\phi}$ whose restriction to a smooth fibre is ample.
  • If $\bar\phi\notin\operatorname{im}(d_2^{0,2})$, then $b_2(X^{\phi})=b_2(X)-1$, all degree-two cohomology classes on $X^{\phi}$ restrict trivially to a smooth fibre, and $X^{\phi}$ is not Kähler.
The coefficients for the degree-two classes in these two assertions, and the precise meaning of an ample restriction, are not specified in the excerpt. These assertions are preserved as supplied source claims, not independently verified theorems. In particular, the excerpt does not assert that membership of $\bar\phi$ in $\operatorname{im}(d_2^{0,2})$ is sufficient for Kählerness. The missing construction and hypotheses prevent this draft from fixing a fully specified parameter domain.

Short English statement

For a fixed Lagrangian fibration on an irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold, characterize exactly which Shafarevich--Tate twists have Kähler total spaces. The supplied source reports sufficient and obstructing conditions, but its extracted text omits definitions needed to specify the twist parameters precisely. Its partially solved status and literature assessment are supplied dated curation.
