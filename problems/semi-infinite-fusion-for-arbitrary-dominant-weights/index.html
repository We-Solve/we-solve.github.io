Math-14573

Semi-Infinite Fusion for Arbitrary Dominant Weights

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks to extend its semi-infinite fusion construction from weights proportional to $\Lambda_0$ to every dominant weight, uniformly for the intended class of simple Lie algebras.

The notation $\Lambda_0$ suggests the usual affine framework: starting with a finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, form its associated affine Lie algebra $\widehat{\mathfrak g}$. Relative to affine simple coroots $h_i$, a dominant integral weight $\lambda$ satisfies $\lambda(h_i)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ for every $i$, and the zeroth fundamental weight satisfies $\Lambda_0(h_i)=\delta_{0i}$. The weights $m\Lambda_0$ are a distinguished subfamily of these weights. The affine type and normalization, including any degree-coordinate convention, must match the actual construction.

In that interpretation, the proposed extension has the quantifiers \[ \text{for every intended simple }\mathfrak g \text{ and every }\lambda\in P_+(\widehat{\mathfrak g}), \quad\text{the source's semi-infinite fusion construction works at }\lambda. \] Here $P_+$ denotes dominant integral affine weights. This is a quantified description of the target, not a definition of "works": the extract supplies neither the finite fusion factors, filtration, connecting maps or semi-infinite limit, nor the module and structural properties that the construction is meant to realize. It also does not explicitly distinguish finite-dimensional from affine dominant weights or state the ground field, affine type and allowable levels. Thus the common affine interpretation is exposed as contextual rather than silently turned into a verified construction or a claimed module isomorphism.

Short English statement

Extend the intended semi-infinite fusion construction beyond multiples of the zeroth fundamental weight to every dominant weight for every intended simple Lie algebra. The construction, weight conventions and success criterion are missing from the extract.
