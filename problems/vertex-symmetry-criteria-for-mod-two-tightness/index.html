Vertex-Symmetry Criteria for Mod-Two Tightness

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a simplicial complex with vertex set $V(X)$ and dimension $2$; thus every simplex has at most three vertices and at least one simplex has three vertices. For $W\subseteq V(X)$, define the induced subcomplex by \[ X[W]=\{\sigma\in X:\sigma\subseteq W\}. \] No finiteness, purity, manifold, or connectedness hypothesis is explicitly supplied.

The source uses a quantity $c_Y(y_1,y_2)$ associated with an induced subcomplex $Y$ and an ordered pair of distinct vertices of $Y$. It supplies neither a definition nor a codomain for this quantity. Consequently, its symmetry condition can only be retained symbolically.

Question as supplied. For every two-dimensional simplicial complex $X$, does the implication \[ \left[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every }W\subseteq V(X)\text{ and all }y_1,y_2\in W,\\ y_1\ne y_2\ \Longrightarrow\ c_{X[W]}(y_1,y_2)=c_{X[W]}(y_2,y_1) \end{gathered} \right] \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\text{ is }\mathbb Z_2\text{-tight} \] hold?

For clarity, a conventional homological interpretation of $\mathbb Z_2$-tightness requires $X$ to be connected and every inclusion $X[W]\hookrightarrow X$ to induce injective maps \[ H_i(X[W];\mathbb F_2)\longrightarrow H_i(X;\mathbb F_2) \qquad(W\subseteq V(X),\ i\geq 0), \] where $\mathbb F_2=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$. This is an explicitly qualified possible interpretation: the supplied record does not define tightness or specify whether finiteness or other ambient conventions are assumed. The intended definition of $c_Y$, and the applicable tightness conventions, must be recovered before the displayed question constitutes a fully specified mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether symmetry of $c_Y$ on every ordered pair of distinct vertices in every induced subcomplex forces a two-dimensional simplicial complex to be $\mathbb Z_2$-tight. The quantity $c_Y$ is undefined in the record, so the question remains incomplete.
