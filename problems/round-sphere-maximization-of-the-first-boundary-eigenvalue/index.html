Round-Sphere Maximization of the First Boundary Eigenvalue

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether the round sphere maximizes the normalized first nontrivial Laplace--Beltrami eigenvalue among convex hypersurfaces in every dimension $m\geq 3$. It does not explicitly specify compactness, regularity, or whether hypersurfaces must have no boundary. The following precise formulation is conditional on interpreting the admissible hypersurfaces as smooth boundaries of compact convex bodies with nonempty interior.

For an integer $m\geq 3$, let $K\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{m+1}$ be a compact convex set with nonempty interior and smooth boundary $\Sigma=\partial K$. Equip $\Sigma$ with its induced Riemannian metric, and write \[ |\Sigma|=\int_\Sigma 1\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_\Sigma. \] Let $\lambda_1(\Sigma)$ denote the first positive eigenvalue of the nonnegative operator $-\Delta_\Sigma$. Equivalently, \[ \lambda_1(\Sigma)=\inf_{\substack{u\in C^\infty(\Sigma;{\mathbb{R}}),\ u\not\equiv0\\ \int_\Sigma u\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_\Sigma=0}} \frac{\int_\Sigma |\nabla_\Sigma u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_\Sigma}{\int_\Sigma u^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_\Sigma}. \] Let $S^m=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m+1}:|x|=1\}$ with its round metric. Under the stated interpretation, the question is whether, for every integer $m\geq3$ and every such $K$, \[ \lambda_1(\partial K)|\partial K|^{2/m} \leq \lambda_1(S^m)|S^m|^{2/m} = m|S^m|^{2/m}. \] This asks for a global maximum attained by round spheres. A uniqueness assertion is not included. The admissible class must be confirmed against the underlying report: the smoothness, compactness, and absence of boundary used here are explicit interpretive assumptions, not additional hypotheses verified from that report. If other hypersurfaces are intended, their spectral realization and any necessary boundary conditions remain unspecified.

Short English statement

For each integer $m\geq3$, is the first positive Laplace--Beltrami eigenvalue multiplied by the $2/m$ power of hypersurface area always at most its value on a round sphere? The displayed formulation assumes smooth closed boundaries of compact convex bodies; the supplied record does not explicitly settle the admissible regularity or boundary conditions.
