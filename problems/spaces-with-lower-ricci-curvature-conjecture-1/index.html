Spaces with Lower Ricci Curvature — Conjecture 1

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be a fixed integer, and let $(M_i^n,g_i,p_i)$ be a sequence of pointed Riemannian $n$-manifolds with a pointed metric limit $(X,d,p)$. The supplied record does not specify the convergence topology; the formulation below uses the conventional interpretation of pointed Gromov--Hausdorff convergence: \[ (M_i^n,d_{g_i},p_i)\xrightarrow{\mathrm{pGH}}(X,d,p). \] For fixed constants $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$ and $v>0$, suppose that, for every $i$, \[ \operatorname{Ric}_{g_i}(u,u)\geq-\lambda g_i(u,u) \quad\text{for every tangent vector }u, \qquad \operatorname{Vol}_{g_i}\left(B_1^{g_i}(p_i)\right)>v, \] where $d_{g_i}$ is the Riemannian distance and $B_1^{g_i}(p_i)=\{q\in M_i:d_{g_i}(q,p_i)<1\}$.

Conjectural conclusion (Naber, Conjecture 1, 2020). The supplied assertion is that $X$ is homeomorphic to a manifold away from a set of codimension three. If this means Hausdorff codimension at least three and an $n$-dimensional topological manifold without boundary, its precise formulation is \[ \exists S\subseteq X:\qquad \dim_{\mathrm H}^{d}S\leq n-3, \qquad X\setminus S\text{ is a topological }n\text{-manifold}. \] Here $\dim_{\mathrm H}^{d}$ denotes Hausdorff dimension in the metric $d$, and a topological $n$-manifold means a Hausdorff, second-countable space locally homeomorphic to ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, with the subspace topology on $X\setminus S$. For this interpretation in dimensions $n<3$, use $\dim_{\mathrm H}\varnothing=-\infty$, so the displayed bound forces $S=\varnothing$.

Unresolved qualifications. The convergence topology, completeness and boundary assumptions on the approximating manifolds, the meaning of codimension, the intended dimension and boundary convention for the resulting manifold, and any restriction on $n$ are not explicit in the supplied record. The displayed conclusion is therefore a qualified interpretation, not a uniquely determined transcription. No closedness requirement on $S$ has been added; whether the intended conclusion requires $X\setminus S$ to be open in $X$ remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Naber's supplied conjecture says that a limit of Riemannian $n$-manifolds with a uniform Ricci lower bound and a uniform positive lower bound for the volumes of unit balls at the basepoints is a topological manifold outside an exceptional set of codimension three. A pointed Gromov--Hausdorff and Hausdorff-codimension interpretation is given above, with unresolved source conventions stated explicitly. The registry labels the problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
