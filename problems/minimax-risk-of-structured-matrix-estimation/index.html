Math-15009

Minimax Risk of Structured Matrix Estimation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns an unknown matrix $\theta=(\theta_{ij})\in\Theta$ and observations \[ Y_{ij}=\eta_{ij}(\theta_{ij}+W_{ij}), \] where $\eta_{ij}$ are indicators of independently sampled entries and the noise variables $W_{ij}$ are independent and sub-Gaussian. The matrix dimensions, the structured parameter class $\Theta$, and its outer factor classes are not defined in the supplied record. Independence between the sampling indicators and the noise, noise centering, and a uniform sub-Gaussian scale are also not explicitly specified.

For a matrix $A$, write $\|A\|_F^2=\sum_{i,j}|A_{ij}|^2$. Once the dimensions, parameter class, and observation experiment $\mathcal E$ have been specified, the unnormalized squared Frobenius minimax risk is \[ \mathcal R(\Theta;\mathcal E) =\inf_{\widehat\theta}\sup_{\theta\in\Theta} \mathbb E_{\theta,\mathcal E} \left[\|\widehat\theta-\theta\|_F^2\right], \] where the infimum ranges over measurable estimators based on the available observations. The record does not specify whether the sampling indicators are themselves observed or whether the intended loss is normalized by the number of matrix entries; the displayed functional therefore gives a conditional formulation using unnormalized squared loss.

Requested determination. Determine the minimax Frobenius-risk rate over the full structured factor class, beyond the finite-alphabet cases. In the notation attributed by the record to Theorem 1 of the cited report, the proposed comparison is formally \[ \mathcal R(\Theta;\mathcal E) \asymp \frac{R_X+R_B+R_Z}{p}. \] Here $\asymp$ denotes comparison up to positive multiplicative constants over the intended parameter regime. The quantities $R_X,R_B,R_Z$, the permitted dependence of those constants, and that regime are not supplied. If $p$ is a common sampling probability, this expression requires $0<p\leq1$; the record does not explicitly give the sampling law or address the boundary $p=0$.

The question also asks whether the contribution $R_X$, respectively $R_Z$, disappears when the corresponding outer factor class consists only of the identity matrix. Formally, these proposed simplifications are $(R_B+R_Z)/p$, respectively $(R_X+R_B)/p$, subject to the still unspecified factor dimensions and definitions. These expressions record the intended question, not established conclusions. Without the definition of $\Theta$ and the statement of the cited Theorem 1, the supplied evidence does not determine a complete mathematical conjecture.

Short English statement

Determine the optimal worst-case squared Frobenius error for estimating a structured matrix from independently sampled entries with independent sub-Gaussian noise. The supplied question asks whether the lower-bound expression $(R_X+R_B+R_Z)/p$ is the correct rate for the full factor class and whether identity outer factors remove their corresponding terms. Essential definitions and hypotheses are missing, so this remains a conditional statement requiring mathematical review.
