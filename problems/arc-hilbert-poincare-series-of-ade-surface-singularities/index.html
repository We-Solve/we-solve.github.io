Arc Hilbert–Poincaré Series of ADE Surface Singularities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $q$ be a formal variable, and define \[ P(q)=\frac{1}{(1-q)^3}\prod_{i=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(1-q^i)^2}\in{\mathbb{Z}}[[q]]. \] The product is interpreted formally: each coefficient depends on only finitely many factors. Write $\operatorname{AHP}_{X,O}(q)$ for the arc Hilbert--Poincaré series of a pointed singularity $(X,O)$, as named in the supplied source. The record does not define this invariant or specify the algebra, grading, or filtration used to construct it; consequently, its precise definition remains to be supplied from the underlying report.

The source identifies ADE surface singularities with rational double point surface singularities. It reports, as Theorem 1 attributed to its reference [7], that every such singularity satisfies \[ \operatorname{AHP}_{X,O}(q)=P(q). \] Question, with unresolved scope. Does equality with this series characterize the class of ADE surface singularities? More explicitly, once the intended class $\mathcal C$ of pointed surface singularities and the definition of $\operatorname{AHP}$ have been fixed, is the following implication true? \[ \forall (X,O)\in\mathcal C,\qquad \operatorname{AHP}_{X,O}(q)=P(q) \ \Longrightarrow\ (X,O)\text{ is an ADE surface singularity}. \] Together with the reported forward implication, this would give an equivalence on $\mathcal C$. The record does not specify $\mathcal C$, the ground field or its characteristic, the algebraic, analytic, or formal category, or hypotheses such as reducedness, normality, or isolation of the singularity. This is therefore a formulation schema requiring those qualifications, rather than a fully specified universally quantified conjecture. Because the reported series is the same for every ADE singularity, the proposed characterization concerns membership in the ADE class; the displayed formula cannot distinguish individual ADE types.

For context, the source says that this invariant detects regularity and contains ingredients relevant to singularity theory, referring to its reference [8], and that its computation is generally difficult. It also reports, as Theorem 3 attributed to its reference [5], that for the dual-number scheme \[ X=\operatorname{Spec}\left(K[x]/(x^2)\right),\qquad O=(x), \] one has \[ \operatorname{AHP}_{X,O}(q) =\prod_{\substack{i\geq 1\\ i\equiv 1,4\pmod 5}}\frac{1}{1-q^i}. \] Here $K$ is the unspecified field appearing in the source; no characteristic hypothesis is supplied. This contextual example is not a surface singularity and does not specify the comparison class in the question. All reported formulas and regularity claims require checking against the original definitions and hypotheses.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a surface singularity whose arc Hilbert--Poincaré series equals the displayed common ADE series must be a rational double point. The source reports the forward implication. The invariant's definition, the ground field, and the class of singularities under comparison are missing, so the converse remains a statement schema requiring human review. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified assessment.
