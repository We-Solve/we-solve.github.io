Math-3550

Complex Extension Conjecture for Polyhedral Kähler Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$, and let $M^{2n}$ be a polyhedral Kähler manifold in the sense intended by the supplied source. Let $S_{\mathrm{met}}\subset M$ be its metric singular locus, namely the points without a neighborhood isometric to a Euclidean open set, and put $U=M\setminus S_{\mathrm{met}}$. Denote the given flat complex structure on $U$ by $J$; it is parallel for the flat metric and makes $U$ a complex manifold of complex dimension $n$. The supplied record does not give the full definition of a polyhedral Kähler manifold, including any additional conditions imposed along singular strata. These hypotheses must therefore be understood in the source's sense and checked before treating this as a complete standalone formulation.

Complex extension conjecture. For every such $M^{2n}$, does there exist a normal complex analytic variety $X$ of pure complex dimension $n$ and a homeomorphism $h:M\to X$ such that $h(U)\subset X_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is open and $h|_U:(U,J)\to h(U)$ is biholomorphic, with the further source requirement that $X$ have "complex singularities in codimension $3$"? Here $X_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is the complex manifold locus and $\operatorname{Sing}(X)=X\setminus X_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is the complex analytic singular locus. Normality means that every local analytic ring $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is an integrally closed domain. The metric singular locus and the complex analytic singular locus are distinct notions; the extension requirement implies only $\operatorname{Sing}(X)\subset h(S_{\mathrm{met}})$.

Unresolved codimension qualification. If the quoted phrase means complex codimension at least $3$, the additional requirement is \[ \operatorname{codim}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(Z,X)\geq 3 \quad\text{for every irreducible component }Z\subset\operatorname{Sing}(X). \] Equivalently, every such component has complex dimension at most $n-3$; an empty singular locus satisfies this condition. In particular, this reading requires $X$ to be nonsingular when $n\leq 2$. The supplied original statement does not explicitly say "at least," while the normalized statement says that the singularities "have codimension $3$." An exact-codimension requirement, including whether it applies to every component and whether an empty singular locus is allowed, is therefore not settled by the record. The displayed inequality is a conditional clarification, not a silently imposed correction.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the flat complex structure on the metrically regular part of every polyhedral Kähler manifold extends to a normal complex analytic structure on the entire underlying space, with complex singularities restricted by the source's codimension-$3$ condition. The precise polyhedral Kähler hypotheses and the meaning of that codimension condition require source review. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved.
