Math-3669

Diagrammatic and Geometric Maximality

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $c(K)$ denote the crossing number of a knot or link $K$, namely the minimum number of crossings among its diagrams. Let $v_8$ denote the hyperbolic volume of a regular ideal octahedron in hyperbolic three-space of sectional curvature $-1$. Write $\log$ for the natural logarithm.

Under the usual interpretation of knots and links in $S^3$, the determinant $\det(K)$ can be defined as the order of $H_1(\Sigma_2(K);{\mathbb{Z}})$ when this group is finite, and as zero otherwise, where $\Sigma_2(K)$ is the double cover of $S^3$ branched over $K$. Thus the real expression $\log\det(K)$ requires $\det(K)>0$. When $S^3\setminus K$ admits a complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric, $\operatorname{vol}(K)$ denotes its hyperbolic volume. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the ambient category or a volume convention for nonhyperbolic knots and links.

For a sequence $(K_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ of knots or links with $c(K_n)\to\infty$, call the sequence geometrically maximal when the volume terms are defined and \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{vol}(K_n)}{c(K_n)}=v_8, \] and diagrammatically maximal when the determinant logarithms are defined and \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{2\pi\log\det(K_n)}{c(K_n)}=v_8. \] Since $c(K_n)\to\infty$, the denominators are positive for all sufficiently large $n$; these limits may be read on that tail.

The supplied question asks whether every diagrammatically maximal sequence is geometrically maximal and whether every geometrically maximal sequence is diagrammatically maximal. On a common domain where both expressions are defined for all sufficiently large $n$, the precise equivalence being asked is: for every such sequence with $c(K_n)\to\infty$, \[ \left(\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{2\pi\log\det(K_n)}{c(K_n)}=v_8\right) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left(\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{vol}(K_n)}{c(K_n)}=v_8\right). \] This common-domain formulation does not settle the intended scope of the original unrestricted question. In particular, the record does not say whether all terms must be hyperbolic or whether volume is extended to nonhyperbolic links, nor how sequences containing infinitely many zero determinants are treated. These qualifications require source clarification rather than an implicit restriction of the problem.

Short English statement

For sequences of knots or links whose crossing numbers tend to infinity, does maximal normalized logarithmic determinant occur exactly when maximal normalized hyperbolic volume occurs, both with limiting value $v_8$? The supplied formulation leaves the treatment of nonhyperbolic links and zero determinants unspecified.
