Brauer Groups of Smooth Models of Symmetric Powers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a variety. For a nonnegative integer $m$, the symmetric power, when the relevant quotient exists, is \[ X_m=\operatorname{Sym}^{m}(X)=X^{m}/\mathfrak S_m, \] where $\mathfrak S_m$ acts by permuting the factors. This recalls the usual construction; the supplied record does not specify the intended range of $m$, the base field, or the class of varieties under consideration. Let $X'_m$ be a smooth model of $X_m$. Under the usual birational interpretation, this means a smooth variety birational to $X_m$; the record does not specify whether the model must be proper or projective, or must carry a resolution morphism to $X_m$.

The supplied question is: can the Brauer group \[ \operatorname{Br}(X'_m) \] be estimated in terms of $\operatorname{Br}(X)$?

The meaning of $\operatorname{Br}$ is not specified. Standard possibilities include the Azumaya Brauer group, consisting of Morita-equivalence classes of Azumaya algebras with group operation induced by tensor product, and the cohomological Brauer group \[ \operatorname{Br}'(Y)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Y,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}. \] These conventions are listed as alternatives; their identification is not assumed here. The record also does not identify the invariant to be estimated, the form of the estimate, or its permitted dependence on $m$, the base field, the dimension of $X$, or the chosen smooth model. Thus the displayed question preserves the supplied target, but the missing qualifications prevent its conversion into a uniquely specified quantified bound or theorem. The supplied literature assessment additionally mentions a general vanishing theorem without stating its hypotheses or conclusion; no vanishing assertion is inferred.

Short English statement

The question asks how the Brauer group of a smooth model of a symmetric power of $X$ can be controlled by the Brauer group of $X$. The base field, hypotheses on $X$, range of $m$, meaning of smooth model and Brauer group, and desired estimate remain unspecified. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified status.
