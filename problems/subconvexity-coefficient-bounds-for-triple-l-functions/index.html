Subconvexity Coefficient Bounds for Triple $L$-Functions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix the Maass forms $\phi$ and $\phi'$ occurring in the source, and let $(\phi_i)$ be the intended family of Maass forms on the same space $Y$, with spectral parameters $\lambda_i$ and measure ${\,\mathrm{d}} v$. Define \[ c_i=\int_Y\phi\phi'\phi_i\,{\,\mathrm{d}} v, \qquad d_i=\gamma(\lambda_i)|c_i|^2, \] where $\gamma$ denotes the source's standard gamma-factor normalization. The integral is reproduced without inserting complex conjugation. The conjectural bound is \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\quad d_i\ll_{\phi,\phi',Y,\varepsilon}|\lambda_i|^\varepsilon \qquad (|\lambda_i|\longrightarrow\infty). \] Thus, for each $\varepsilon>0$, with the background data fixed, there should exist $C_\varepsilon>0$ and $T_\varepsilon>0$ such that $d_i\le C_\varepsilon|\lambda_i|^\varepsilon$ for every index $i$ with $|\lambda_i|\ge T_\varepsilon$. This expresses the usual asymptotic reading of $\ll$; it does not impose a bound at a possibly zero spectral parameter.

The supplied record does not specify $Y$, the automorphy group, the measure normalization, the normalization and completeness of $(\phi_i)$, the relation between $\lambda_i$ and the Laplace eigenvalue, or the formula for $\gamma$. These are essential unresolved parts of the formulation. In particular, an $L^2$ normalization or an explicit gamma factor must be recovered from the report rather than invented here.

The original excerpt places this conjecture after a bound with exponent $5/3$, comparing it with a convexity exponent $2$ for a degree-eight triple $L$-function. It describes the method as explicit principal-series calculations in a circle model followed by stationary phase, refers to an unidentified reference [1], and discusses difficulties for higher rank and discrete series. Its printed group is $PGL(2,{\mathbb{Z}})$; this potentially corrupted group specification is not silently replaced by a real Lie group. The excerpt's historical claims about the first degree-eight subconvexity bound and earlier results of degree at most four are retained as source context, not independently established assertions.

Short English statement

For fixed Maass forms, the conjecture asks whether their gamma-normalized squared triple periods grow more slowly than every positive power of the varying spectral parameter. The restored bound is precise only after the geometric, spectral, and gamma-factor conventions have been recovered from the source.
