Math-6811

Positivity Criterion for Quantum Greedy Bases

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied clean statement concerns a quantum greedy basis with parameters $\delta_1,\delta_2$. The extracted report appears to display the factorization \[ \Delta=\begin{pmatrix}\delta_1&0\\0&\delta_2\end{pmatrix},\qquad \Omega=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=\Delta\Omega=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\delta_1\\\delta_2&0\end{pmatrix}. \] This is a reconstruction of damaged matrix formatting and requires confirmation against the report. The record does not explicitly specify the domain of the $\delta_i$; positive integers are a natural candidate domain for the divisibility condition, but this is an additional interpretation.

Intended conjectural implication, with unresolved conventions. If $\mathcal G_q(B)$ denotes the quantum greedy basis intended by the supplied clean statement, the requested implication is \[ \left(\delta_1\mid\delta_2\ \text{or}\ \delta_2\mid\delta_1\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal G_q(B)\text{ has the intended positivity property}. \] The construction of $\mathcal G_q(B)$, its coefficient ring, the compatible quantum data, and the meaning of positivity are not defined in the supplied excerpt. Consequently this implication is not yet a completely specified mathematical conjecture.

One possible interpretation is universal Laurent positivity: every basis element, when expanded in the quantum Laurent monomials of every cluster, has coefficients that are finite sums of allowed quantum-parameter monomials with nonnegative integer coefficients. Under that interpretation, the intended quantifiers would be: for every admissible pair $(\delta_1,\delta_2)$ satisfying the divisibility condition, every element of the corresponding quantum greedy basis is positive in every cluster. This interpretation, including the admissible pairs, coefficient conventions, and monomial normalization, requires source confirmation. The phrase "positivity is preserved" does not itself identify an operation under which positivity is preserved.

The original excerpt instead places the conjecture in a discussion of cluster scattering diagrams. It introduces $N={\mathbb{Z}}^2$, a set $N^+$ of positive elements whose exact definition is not supplied, and the skew-symmetric form determined by $\Omega$. It describes a Lie algebra with generators $X_n$, $n\in N^+$, and relations \[ [X_n,X_{n'}]=[\{n,n'\}]_qX_{n+n'}, \qquad [a]_q=\frac{q^a-q^{-a}}{q-q^{-1}}. \] The displayed formula for $[a]_q$ is the apparent restoration of corrupted extraction. The excerpt then uses a degree completion $\widehat{\mathfrak g}_q$, the BCH exponential group $G_q=\exp(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_q)$, and, for positive rational parameters $a,b$, elements apparently given by \[ \Psi_{a,b}[n]=\exp\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{j+1}q^{jb}}{j[ja]_q}X_{jn}\right). \] The coefficient field, interpretation of rational powers of $q$, degree, and completion conventions are not specified in the record. The excerpt states that if $\{n_2,n_1\}=c>0$, then, for "any" $b_i$, \[ \Psi_{c,b_2}[n_2]\Psi_{c,b_1}[n_1] = \Psi_{c,b_1}[n_1]\Psi_{c,b_1+b_2}[n_1+n_2]\Psi_{c,b_2}[n_2]. \] The scope of "any" is unclear relative to the earlier requirement that $b$ be positive rational. The excerpt says that the classical case is obtained as $q\to1$, with $\Psi_{a,b}[n]$ reducing to $\Psi[n]^{1/a}$, and that the same divisibility condition emerges through the pentagon relation. It does not explicitly identify the positivity in this discussion with positivity of a quantum greedy basis; that connection remains to be checked.

Short English statement

The intended question asks whether the quantum greedy basis is positive whenever either parameter divides the other. A precise final formulation requires confirmation of the parameter domain, quantum cluster algebra conventions, and meaning of positivity. The original excerpt discusses scattering diagrams without explicitly identifying its positivity property with quantum greedy-basis positivity.
