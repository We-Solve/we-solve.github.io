Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.16

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $r$ be a positive integer. This is the range supported by the supplied recursive notation; the source does not explicitly state the range of $r$ or define a zeroth iterated commutator. For this draft, use the convention $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$, and define \[ [x,{}_1 y]=[x,y],\qquad [x,{}_{i+1}y]=[[x,{}_i y],y]\quad(i\geq 1). \] The source does not specify its convention for the basic commutator, so this convention requires confirmation against the original source. Define \[ V_r=\left\{G:\ G\text{ is a finite group and }\exists s\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0},\ s>r,\ \forall x,y\in G,\ [x,{}_r y]=[x,{}_s y]\right\}. \] Here $s=s(G)$ may depend on $G$, with $r$ fixed, but must be independent of $x$ and $y$.

For a finite soluble group $G$, its Fitting length $h(G)$ is the least integer $n\geq 0$ for which there is a series \[ 1=G_0\trianglelefteq G_1\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq G_n=G \] with every $G_i$ normal in $G$ and every factor $G_i/G_{i-1}$ nilpotent. In particular, $h(1)=0$.

  1. (a) Does there exist a function $f:{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$ such that \[ \forall r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\ \forall G\in V_r,\qquad G\text{ soluble}\ \Longrightarrow\ h(G)<f(r)? \] The integer-valued formulation expresses the requested uniform bound on the integer-valued Fitting length. For $r<3$, the supplied source directs the reader to the 1983 paper by R. Brandl listed below; it does not state the precise result of that paper.
  2. (b) For every fixed positive integer $r$, are there only finitely many isomorphism classes of nonabelian finite simple groups belonging to $V_r$? Equivalently, is \[ \left\{[S]_{\cong}:\ S\in V_r\text{ and }S\text{ is nonabelian simple}\right\} \] finite for every such $r$? Counting up to isomorphism is the interpretation used here for the source's phrase "finitely many nonabelian simple groups." The supplied source states that this holds for $r<4$; that assertion is retained as a source qualification and has not been independently verified.

Short English statement

For each fixed positive integer $r$, consider finite groups whose $r$-fold iterated commutator equals a later iterated commutator identically, with the later index allowed to depend on the group. The questions ask for a bound depending only on $r$ on the Fitting length of soluble members, and for finiteness up to isomorphism of the nonabelian simple members. The supplied source refers to Brandl's work for part (a) when $r<3$ and reports part (b) as true when $r<4$.
