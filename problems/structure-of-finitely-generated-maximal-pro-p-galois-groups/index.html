Math-788

Structure of Finitely Generated Maximal Pro-$p$ Galois Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $F$ be a field with $\operatorname{char}F\ne p$ containing all $p$-th roots of unity. Fix a separable closure $F^{\mathrm{sep}}$, and let $F(p)$ be the compositum therein of the finite Galois extensions of $F$ of $p$-power degree. Write \[ G_F(p)=\operatorname{Gal}(F(p)/F). \] This is the maximal pro-$p$ Galois group, rather than an assumption that the full absolute Galois group is pro-$p$. Suppose $G_F(p)$ is topologically finitely generated: some finite subset generates a dense subgroup.

For a valuation $v$ of $F$ and an extension $w$ to $F(p)$, define its decomposition subgroup and inertia subgroup by \[ D_w=\{\sigma\in G_F(p):w\circ\sigma=w\}, \qquad I_w=\ker\!\left(D_w\longrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(\kappa(w)/\kappa(v))\right), \] using the residue-field action. Different extensions produce conjugate subgroups in the usual valuation-theoretic convention. The source does not state any further restrictions on the valuations.

The conjecture asks whether there exists a free pro-$p$ product decomposition \[ G_F(p)\cong\mathop{\ast}\limits_{j\in J}^{\mathrm{pro}\text{-}p}H_j, \] where each factor is isomorphic to one of \[ {\mathbb{Z}}_p,\qquad {\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}\ \text{(allowed only when }p=2\text{)}, \qquad D_w\ \text{for a valuation with }I_w\ne1. \] Here ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ is the additive group of $p$-adic integers and the free product is the coproduct in the category of pro-$p$ groups and continuous homomorphisms, not the abstract discrete free product. The statement concerns topological group isomorphism to the allowed factors; it does not require a specified internal embedding of every factor. An empty-product convention would accommodate a trivial group, but the source does not discuss that degenerate case.

The range of valuation types, residue characteristics, and whether the decomposition groups are meant precisely inside $G_F(p)$ for valuations of $F$ must be confirmed in the report. No rank-one, henselian, tame, or residue-characteristic restriction is silently introduced, and no number or uniqueness of factors is claimed.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether a finitely generated maximal pro-$p$ Galois group over a field containing the $p$-th roots of unity splits as a free pro-$p$ product of procyclic factors, order-two factors when $p=2$, and valuation decomposition groups with nontrivial inertia.
