Derived Invariants from Gentle Quivers with Relations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote the ground field required by the source theorem. For a quiver $Q$, its path algebra $kQ$ has directed paths as a basis, with multiplication given by concatenation when defined and zero otherwise. An ideal of relations $I\subseteq kQ$ gives the presented algebra $kQ/I$. Write \[ A(\Delta)=kQ(\Delta)/I(\Delta). \] The input is the quiver with relations $(Q(\Delta),I(\Delta))$, promised to define a gentle algebra in the class covered by the source's Theorem 3.3. The notation $\Delta$ does not supply additional geometric input.

In the usual finite-dimensional setting, derived equivalence means equivalence of the bounded derived categories of finite-dimensional modules as $k$-linear triangulated categories. The supplied record does not reproduce the ground-field assumptions, the precise definition and scope of gentle algebras used in Theorem 3.3, or that theorem's convention for derived equivalence; these must be recovered before fixing the domain precisely.

Requested algorithm, conditional on the missing theorem specification. Let $\mathcal C_{3.3}$ denote the intended class of gentle algebras and let $\operatorname{Inv}_{3.3}(A)$ denote the complete derived-equivalence invariant of the referenced Theorem 3.3. These symbols are placeholders for the missing theorem data. Construct an algorithm which, for every valid input presentation $(Q,I)$ with $kQ/I\in\mathcal C_{3.3}$, terminates and computes $\operatorname{Inv}_{3.3}(kQ/I)$ using only that presentation. Its output must include all of the theorem's genus, puncture and boundary data, winding-number invariants, and remaining parity or greatest-common-divisor invariants, with their applicable case distinctions and identifications. In particular, after the theorem's output conventions have been specified, completeness requires \[ \operatorname{Inv}_{3.3}(A)\sim\operatorname{Inv}_{3.3}(B) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\text{ and }B\text{ are derived equivalent} \qquad(A,B\in\mathcal C_{3.3}), \] where $\sim$ means agreement under the identifications prescribed by that theorem.

Unresolved specification. The supplied record does not reproduce Theorem 3.3 or unambiguously locate it within a particular supplied reference. Consequently, it does not determine the exact output tuple, winding-number conventions, boundary and puncture conventions, parity and greatest-common-divisor formulas, or their case restrictions. The displayed requirement records the intended target; it is not a fully specified computational problem until these omissions are resolved.

Short English statement

For UnsolvedMath ID $30004365$, construct a terminating procedure that extracts the complete derived-equivalence invariant of a gentle algebra directly from its quiver and relations. It must recover the specified topological, winding-number, parity and greatest-common-divisor data without additional geometric input. The referenced Theorem 3.3 is absent, so its hypotheses and exact output specification remain unresolved. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
