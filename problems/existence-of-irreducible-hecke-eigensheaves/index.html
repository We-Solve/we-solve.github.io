Math-60

Existence of Irreducible Hecke Eigensheaves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be the smooth projective curve and $G$ the reductive group intended in the source, and write $\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)$ for the moduli stack of principal $G$-bundles on $X$. Write $\check G$ for the Langlands dual group, whose root datum is dual to that of $G$ in the connected reductive case. The supplied literature assessment places the question in the finite-field $\ell$-adic setting: the base field is a finite field $k$, and $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(k)$. The record does not specify the remaining hypotheses on $X$ and $G$, the coefficient field, or the descent conventions; these remain qualifications on the formulation below.

A $\check G$-local system $E$ associates to a finite-dimensional representation $V$ of $\check G$ a local system $E_V$ on $X$, compatibly with tensor operations. Let $\operatorname{Loc}_{\check G}^{\mathrm{irr}}(X)$ denote the local systems satisfying the source's intended irreducibility condition. That condition is not defined in the supplied record. In particular, whether it means that monodromy is not contained in a proper parabolic subgroup, and whether this is tested arithmetically or geometrically, must be clarified.

A Hecke eigensheaf with eigenvalue $E$ is a sheaf complex $F$ on $\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)$, together with Hecke eigenisomorphisms, schematically \[ \mathsf H_V(F)\simeq E_V\boxtimes F \qquad\text{on }X\times\operatorname{Bun}_G(X), \] for every finite-dimensional representation $V$ of $\check G$, satisfying the tensor, unit, and iterated-Hecke compatibilities. Here $\mathsf H_V$ denotes the Hecke functor attached to $V$. This display presupposes compatible choices of Hecke normalization; the shifts, twists, and ambient sheaf category are not specified by the record.

Source question, subject to these unresolved qualifications. For every $E\in\operatorname{Loc}_{\check G}^{\mathrm{irr}}(X)$, does there exist a nonzero Hecke eigensheaf $F$ on $\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)$, with eigenvalue $E$, such that \[ \left.F\right|_C\text{ is a nonzero simple object of }\operatorname{Perv}(C) \qquad\text{for every irreducible component }C\subseteq\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)? \] Here $\operatorname{Perv}(C)$ denotes the intended category of perverse $\ell$-adic sheaves on $C$; "irreducible perverse sheaf" means a nonzero simple object. The restriction requirement applies to every irreducible component, including components other than any chosen neutral component. A fully fixed mathematical statement requires the omitted hypotheses and categorical conventions to be recovered from the source.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every irreducible Langlands-dual local system on the curve has a nonzero Hecke eigensheaf whose restriction to every irreducible component of the bundle stack is an irreducible perverse sheaf. The supplied curation places this in the finite-field $\ell$-adic setting, but leaves several hypotheses and conventions unspecified.
