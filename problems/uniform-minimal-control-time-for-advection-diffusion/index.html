Uniform Minimal Control Time for Advection–Diffusion

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a length $L>0$ and a nonzero constant drift $M$. Let $H_0^1(0,L)$ be the Sobolev space of functions with square-integrable weak first derivative and zero endpoint traces, and let $H^{-1}(0,L)=(H_0^1(0,L))'$ be its continuous dual. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the scalar field or state that $M$ is real; these conventions require confirmation from the source.

For a candidate control time $T>0$, diffusion parameter $\varepsilon>0$, initial datum $y_0\in H^{-1}(0,L)$, and boundary control $v\in L^2(0,T)$, consider \[ \begin{cases} \partial_t y-\varepsilon\partial_{xx}y+M\partial_xy=0, & (x,t)\in(0,L)\times(0,T),\\ y(0,t)=v(t),\qquad y(L,t)=0, & t\in(0,T),\\ y(\cdot,0)=y_0. \end{cases} \] For these data, the boundary conditions and solution must be interpreted weakly. A standard transposition interpretation is intended in the formulation below; the supplied record does not specify its precise solution convention. A control is a null control if its corresponding solution satisfies $y(\cdot,T)=0$ in $H^{-1}(0,L)$.

To make uniform control cost precise, use the normalized worst-case cost \[ \mathcal C_{\varepsilon}(T;L,M) :=\sup_{\substack{y_0\in H^{-1}(0,L)\\ \|y_0\|_{H^{-1}(0,L)}=1}} \inf\left\{\|v\|_{L^2(0,T)}: v\text{ is a null control for }y_0\right\}, \] where the infimum of an empty set is $+\infty$. This is the standard operator-norm interpretation of the supplied phrase "cost uniformly bounded"; the record does not itself give an explicit cost formula. Uniform null controllability as $\varepsilon\to0^+$ means that there exist $\varepsilon_0>0$ and $C<\infty$, independent of $\varepsilon$ and the initial datum, such that for every $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0$ and every $y_0^{\varepsilon}\in H^{-1}(0,L)$, a null control $v^{\varepsilon}\in L^2(0,T)$ exists with \[ \|v^{\varepsilon}\|_{L^2(0,T)} \le C\|y_0^{\varepsilon}\|_{H^{-1}(0,L)}. \] In particular, the initial datum may depend on $\varepsilon$.

With $L$ fixed, define the threshold denoted by $T_M$ in the source by \[ T_M:=\inf\left\{T>0: \exists\,\varepsilon_0>0\quad \sup_{0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0} \mathcal C_{\varepsilon}(T;L,M)<\infty\right\}, \] again using $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. The dependence on the fixed length $L$ is suppressed in the source notation.

Problem. Determine this minimal uniform null-controllability time $T_M$. The use of an infimum does not assert that uniform controllability holds at $T=T_M$; the supplied record does not clarify whether its word "minimal" includes attainment of the threshold.

Short English statement

Determine the least time threshold for driving every initial state to zero by controlling the left boundary of the stated advection--diffusion equation, with control norm bounded by a constant times the initial-state norm uniformly as $\varepsilon\to0^+$. The right boundary remains zero, and the drift may have either sign in the intended real setting. The precise scalar-field, weak-solution, cost, and threshold-attainment conventions require source confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
