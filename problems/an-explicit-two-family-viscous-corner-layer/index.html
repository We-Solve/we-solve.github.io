An explicit two-family viscous corner layer

Definitions and mathematical statement

Primary question: interacting viscous shocks. The supplied verification identifies M. Williams, "Two Interacting Shocks in 1D", as the source context. Construct explicit solutions of the viscous problem (B), possibly using fast variables $t/\epsilon,x/\epsilon$, converging in a suitable sense to the inviscid problem (A), and explicitly resolve the corner where the states $U_l,U_r$ meet in the $(x,t)$-plane. The workshop says abstract convergence is already available in A. Bressan's C.I.M.E. notes. However, the supplied record does not reproduce (A), (B), their flux, data or full diagram, and its verification field ends at "The setup is". The following is the aid's special class, not a recovery of a general coupled system.

Reported affine-diagonal Burgers corner. Let $P\in\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{R}})$, $b\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$ be constant, and put \[ U=b+Pw,\qquad F(U)=P\begin{pmatrix}w_1^2/2\\w_2^2/2\end{pmatrix}. \] Then $U_t+\partial_xF(U)=\epsilon U_{xx}$ is the constant affine conjugate of two scalar viscous Burgers equations. For each $i=1,2$, choose $L_i>R_i$, set $d_i=L_i-R_i$, $s_i=(L_i+R_i)/2$, and assume $s_1<s_2$. The reported exact global fast-variable solution, for $\epsilon>0$, is \[ w_i^\epsilon(t,x)=s_i-\frac{d_i}{2} \tanh\left(\frac{d_i(x-s_it-\epsilon\theta_i)}{4\epsilon}\right), \qquad (t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2, \] where $\theta_i\in{\mathbb{R}}$ are fixed phases. For zero phases its inviscid step limits are $L_i$ for $x<s_it$, $R_i$ for $x>s_it$. For $t>0$, the sectors are \[ U_l=b+P(L_1,L_2)^T,\quad U_m=b+P(R_1,L_2)^T,\quad U_r=b+P(R_1,R_2)^T, \] separated by $x=s_1t,s_2t$. For $t<0$, the intermediate state is instead $b+P(L_1,R_2)^T$. This gives the reported four-sector eternal corner and its forward upper two-shock fan; matching any omitted source diagram remains a supplied aid assertion.

In the adapted norm $|v|_P=|P^{-1}v|_1$, the aid reports, for centered phases and the corresponding step solution $U^0$, \[ \int_{\mathbb{R}}|U^\epsilon(t,x)-U^0(t,x)|_P\,\mathrm dx =8\epsilon\log2\quad(t\in{\mathbb{R}}). \] Each centered component has zero signed mass difference. A shift $\epsilon\theta_i$ has component mass, relative to the centered inviscid step, \[ m_i=\int_{\mathbb{R}}(w_i^{\epsilon,\theta_i}-w_i^0)\,\mathrm dx =\epsilon d_i\theta_i, \] so its phase is recovered by $\theta_i=m_i/(\epsilon d_i)$. The same exact norm-error identity applies when each step is aligned with its shifted viscous profile; it is not asserted for unaligned nonzero phases. No analogous formula for a general coupled $2\times2$ flux is supplied.

The extracted equations are $v_t-u_x=0$, $u_t+\partial_xp(v)=\partial_x(b(v)u)$, with $p'<0$, $p''>0$.   • K. Promislow asks for the Evans function and perturbed-front dynamics in an optical-resonance model. Short English statement

The aid supplies an explicit two-family corner only for affine conjugates of diagonal Burgers equations, with exact adapted $L^1$ error and phase/mass laws. The omitted general viscous/inviscid setup is not invented. Three subsequently concatenated questions about strong shocks, optical fronts and Evans branch roots are retained separately with their extraction gaps.
