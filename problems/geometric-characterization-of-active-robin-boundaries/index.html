Math-3400

Geometric Characterization of Active Robin Boundaries

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $D\subset{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be a bounded domain containing a closed ball $B^*\subset D$, and let $u$ be the intended solution of \[ \Delta u=0\quad\text{in }D\setminus B^*,\qquad u=1\quad\text{on }\partial B^*,\qquad \frac{\partial u}{\partial n}=u\quad\text{on }\partial D. \] The question is to give necessary and sufficient geometric conditions on the domain (with the fixed interior ball data as relevant) for \[ \inf_{x\in D\setminus B^*}u(x)>0. \] The source calls this equivalent to the whole boundary being active; that terminology is retained with the supplied lower-bound criterion, not replaced by another boundary-activity definition. This is uniform positivity, stronger than merely $u(x)>0$ at each interior point.

The normal orientation is not given. With $n$ pointing inward to $D$, the stated outer-boundary equation is the usual positive Robin condition $\partial_{n_{\mathrm{out}}}u+u=0$. If $n$ points outward, it is instead $\partial_{n_{\mathrm{out}}}u-u=0$, a different boundary problem whose solvability and positivity cannot be assumed identical. This sign convention must be resolved from the source; it is not silently changed.

For arbitrary bounded domains a classical normal and boundary trace need not exist. A precise formulation therefore needs the admissible geometric class, solution space, boundary measure and trace or reflecting-process interpretation. Existence, uniqueness and the representative of $u$ underlying the infimum must be fixed before an all-domains characterization is meaningful. The excerpt does not supply these, and no smoothness or Lipschitz restriction is imposed merely to make the question easier. Nor is independence from the particular interior ball asserted without a source result.

The supplied literature triage checked 21 August 2026 reports substantial Robin/reflection results for Lipschitz, fractal and approximating domains, while retaining unresolved general questions, including construction on arbitrary Euclidean closures. That broader reflecting-process context does not specify the missing Robin conventions or constitute an exact necessary-and-sufficient geometric characterization here.

Short English statement

Which bounded domains have a Robin harmonic solution bounded uniformly away from zero outside a fixed interior ball, so that the whole boundary is active? The normal direction and rough-boundary solution/measure conventions are essential and are missing. The supplied assessment reports substantial special-domain results, without a general characterization.
