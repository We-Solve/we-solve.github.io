Definitions and mathematical statement

The source does not specify the ambient class of spaces or its conventions for covers. The following gives a formulation using standard countable-cover conventions; agreement with the cited source requires review.

For a topological space $X$, a cover is a countable family $\mathcal U$ of subsets of $X$ such that $\bigcup\mathcal U=X$ and $X\notin\mathcal U$. It is a $\gamma$-cover if it is infinite and each point of $X$ belongs to all but finitely many members. It is a $\tau$-cover if each point belongs to infinitely many members and, for every $x,y\in X$, at least one of the sets \[ \{U\in\mathcal U:x\in U,\ y\notin U\},\qquad \{U\in\mathcal U:y\in U,\ x\notin U\} \] is finite. Write $\Gamma(X)$ and $\mathrm T(X)$ for the families of open $\gamma$-covers and open $\tau$-covers, respectively. Write $\mathcal B_\Gamma(X)$ and $\mathcal B_{\mathrm T}(X)$ for the corresponding families whose members are Borel subsets of $X$.

For cover families $\mathcal A(X)$ and $\mathcal B(X)$, define the following properties:

  • $\mathsf S_1(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$: for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ in $\mathcal A(X)$, there are choices $U_n\in\mathcal U_n$ such that $\{U_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\}\in\mathcal B(X)$.
  • $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$: for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ in $\mathcal A(X)$, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that $\bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}\mathcal F_n\in\mathcal B(X)$.
  • $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$: for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ in $\mathcal A(X)$ such that no $\mathcal U_n$ has a finite subcover, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that $\{\bigcup\mathcal F_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\}\in\mathcal B(X)$.
Here ${\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and each displayed output family is treated as a set of subsets, without counting repetitions.

Question (Tsaban's list, Problem 15; context [AddQuad]). For each of the properties \[ \mathsf S_1(\mathrm T,\mathrm T),\quad \mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathrm T,\mathrm T),\quad \mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\mathrm T),\quad \mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\mathrm T),\quad \mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\mathrm T), \] and each corresponding Borel version obtained by replacing $\Gamma$ and $\mathrm T$ by $\mathcal B_\Gamma$ and $\mathcal B_{\mathrm T}$, is that property preserved under finite unions?

Explicitly, for each such property $P$, ask whether, for every positive integer $m$ and every union $X=\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}X_i$ allowed by the intended domain, with each $X_i$ carrying the subspace topology from $X$, \[ \left(\forall i\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\;X_i\text{ satisfies }P\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\text{ satisfies }P. \] The intended domain of these unions is not specified in the supplied record. In particular, whether the question concerns sets of reals, a specified class of spaces, or arbitrary topological spaces remains unresolved; the displayed implication is a formulation template pending that specification.
