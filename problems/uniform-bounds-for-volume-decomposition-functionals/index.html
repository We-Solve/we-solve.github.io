Math-15909

Uniform Bounds for Volume-Decomposition Functionals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer and let $k\in\{1,\ldots,n-1\}$. For a polytope $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$, let $V_n(P)$ denote its $n$-dimensional Lebesgue volume. When $V_n(P)>0$, define its volume centroid by \[ g(P)=\frac{1}{V_n(P)}\int_P x\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] The supplied record refers to volume-decomposition functionals $X_k$, but supplies neither their definitions nor the decomposition identity mentioned in its original excerpt. Consequently, $X_k(P)$ below is an unresolved source-specific quantity, whose definition and normalization must be recovered before this becomes a self-contained mathematical statement.

The question in the supplied clean statement is whether \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ n\geq2,\quad \forall k\in\{1,\ldots,n-1\},\quad \exists c(n,k)\in{\mathbb{R}}\quad \forall P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n\text{ a polytope with }V_n(P)>0\text{ and }g(P)=0, \qquad \frac{X_k(P)}{V_n(P)}\leq c(n,k). \] Here the constant must be finite and depend only on $n$ and $k$. The requirement $V_n(P)>0$, equivalently full dimensionality for a polytope in ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, is necessary for this quotient and the displayed centroid formula; it is not stated explicitly in the supplied problem and requires confirmation against the intended source conventions.

Unresolved normalization. The supplied original excerpt instead asks for $X_k(P)\leq c(n,k)$, without a volume denominator. No definition or normalization is supplied that reconciles these formulations. The displayed quantified question records the clean statement's target, without asserting that the original excerpt is equivalent to it.

Supplied context. The excerpt relates the decomposition functionals to the Lutwak--Yang--Zhang $U$ functional and describes a 2001 conjecture concerning $X_n$ and volume, with equality precisely for parallelotopes, as subsequently settled. Its displayed inequality is corrupted in the extraction and cannot be restated reliably from this record. The excerpt also says that Problem $X$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ was satisfactorily solved by Liu--Sun--Xiong; the scope of that assertion relative to the normalized question above is not specified.

Short English statement

For each dimension $n\geq2$ and each $1\leq k<n$, the clean statement asks whether $X_k(P)/V_n(P)$ has a finite upper bound depending only on $n,k$ over polytopes with volume centroid at the origin. The definition of $X_k$ is missing, and the original excerpt omits the volume denominator. Positive volume is required to interpret the quotient. These issues require human mathematical review.
