Math-936

Classification of Four-Dimensional Ricci-Flow $\kappa$-Solutions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A four-dimensional Ricci flow is a family of Riemannian metrics $g(t)$ on a smooth manifold $M$ with $\dim M=4$, satisfying \[ \frac{\partial g}{\partial t}=-2\operatorname{Ric}_{g(t)}. \] An ancient flow is one defined for all sufficiently negative times. Parabolic scaling by $c>0$ replaces $g(t)$ by $c\,g(t/c)$, on the correspondingly rescaled time interval. Write $S^n$ for the $n$-sphere, $\mathbb{CP}^2$ for the complex projective plane, $O(3)$ for the orthogonal group of ${\mathbb{R}}^3$, and ${\mathbb{Z}}_2={\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$.

Proposed classification in the supplied statement. Every four-dimensional Ricci-flow $\kappa$-solution is asserted, up to scaling and finite quotients, to belong to the following list:

  1. Shrinking solitons on \[ S^4,\qquad \mathbb{CP}^2,\qquad S^2\times S^2,\qquad {\mathbb{R}}\times S^3,\qquad {\mathbb{R}}^2\times S^2. \]
  2. The four-dimensional Bryant soliton; the three-dimensional Bryant soliton times a line; and Lai's one-parameter family of ${\mathbb{Z}}_2\times O(3)$-symmetric steady solitons.
  3. The known four-dimensional ovals; products of three-dimensional ovals with a line; and the one-parameter family of ${\mathbb{Z}}_2^2\times O(3)$-symmetric ovals.
Here a shrinking soliton evolves by pullback under diffeomorphisms and an overall decreasing scale, whereas a steady soliton evolves by pullback without changing its overall scale. The named solitons and oval families above are retained as supplied identifiers; the record provides neither their defining metrics nor constructions or parameter ranges.

Unresolved scope and provenance. This is a transcription of the proposed classification, not a fully specified conjecture. The source does not define a $\kappa$-solution or specify the quantifier on $\kappa>0$, completeness, curvature hypotheses, curvature bounds, the noncollapsing condition, or the time interval and endpoint conditions. These choices are therefore left unresolved. Likewise, the precise metrics on the displayed manifolds, the meaning of "known four-dimensional ovals," the allowed finite quotient actions, and the intended equivalence of flows require clarification.

There is also a material conflict within the supplied record: the statement fields give the explicit list above, but the original passage only announces "Conjecture 1 ([11])" for the related four-dimensional Ricci-flow problem. The supplied literature assessment says that the extracted passage does not state the conjecture's mathematical content. Consequently, the list cannot be authenticated from that passage, and neither its exhaustiveness nor the admissibility of every listed family under the intended hypotheses can be treated as verified.

Short English statement

The supplied statement proposes that every four-dimensional Ricci-flow $\kappa$-solution belongs, up to scaling and finite quotients, to the listed shrinking, steady, or oval families. The defining hypotheses and several family descriptions are missing. Moreover, the supplied original excerpt merely announces a conjecture, so it does not substantiate the explicit classification. This statement-only draft requires human mathematical and source review.
