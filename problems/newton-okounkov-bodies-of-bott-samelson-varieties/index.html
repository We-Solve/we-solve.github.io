Newton–Okounkov Bodies of Bott–Samelson Varieties

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Newton--Okounkov body requires both a divisor or graded linear series and a valuation, often supplied by an admissible flag. For example, under the usual complete-linear-series convention, let $X$ have dimension $m$, let $D$ be a big Cartier divisor, and let \[ Y_\bullet:\quad X=Y_0\supset Y_1\supset\cdots\supset Y_m=\{x\} \] be a flag of irreducible subvarieties with $\operatorname{codim}_X(Y_r)=r$, each nonsingular at $x$. The flag valuation $\nu_{Y_\bullet}(s)$ records successive orders of vanishing along the flag, after dividing out the preceding vanishing orders and restricting. In this convention, \[ \Delta_{Y_\bullet}(X,D) =\overline{\operatorname{conv}}\left( \bigcup_{k\geq 1} \left\{\frac{\nu_{Y_\bullet}(s)}{k}:0\ne s\in H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(kD))\right\} \right)\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^m. \] These are explanatory conditional definitions; the supplied record does not specify that this divisor convention or these hypotheses are the intended ones.

The recorded question concerns a Bott--Samelson variety $X=R(i_1,\ldots,i_l)$ associated with $\operatorname{GL}_n$. It asks whether its Newton--Okounkov body for the source's "indicated flag" equals \[ P=A_{i_1}T_{v_1}A_{i_2}\cdots T_{v_{l-1}}A_{i_l}(p) \] for suitable parameters \[ p\in{\mathbb{R}}^d,\qquad v_j\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d_{i_j}}\quad(1\leq j\leq l-1). \] Here $A_i$, $T_v$, $d$, and $d_i$ are retained exactly as source notation, without assigned definitions. The supplied record omits the indicated flag, the divisor or graded linear series, and the definitions and domains of these operators. It also does not specify the ground field or the hypotheses on the index sequence. Consequently, the displayed equality records the proposed target, but the available information does not determine a fully quantified mathematical conjecture or identify the two sides in a common ambient space.
