Reconstruction Threshold for Critical Ising Models on the Torus

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, let $\mathbb Z_n={\mathbb{Z}}/n{\mathbb{Z}}$ and let $T_n=\mathbb Z_n^2$ be the two-dimensional discrete torus, with periodic boundary conditions. An Ising spin configuration on $T_n$ is an element $\sigma\in\{-1,+1\}^{T_n}$.

Problem as supplied. Determine the exact reconstruction threshold for the critical Ising model on $T_n$.

Unresolved formulation. Short English statement

The question asks for an exact reconstruction threshold for the critical Ising model on a two-dimensional torus.
