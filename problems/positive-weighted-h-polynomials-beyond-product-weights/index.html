Positive Weighted $h^*$-Polynomials beyond Product Weights

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $P\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^N$ be a nonempty lattice polytope, meaning the convex hull of finitely many points of ${\mathbb{Z}}^N$, and put $d_P=\dim P$. Let $w\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_N]$ be homogeneous of degree $m\geq0$. For every integer $n\geq0$, define \[ \operatorname{ehr}(nP,w)=\sum_{x\in nP\cap{\mathbb{Z}}^N}w(x). \] Using the degree-based normalization, define the weighted numerator by \[ \sum_{n\geq0}\operatorname{ehr}(nP,w)t^n =\frac{h^*_{P,w}(t)}{(1-t)^{d_P+m+1}}, \qquad h^*_{P,w}(t)=\sum_{i\geq0}h^*_{P,w,i}t^i. \] The supplied excerpt asserts weighted polynomiality of degree at most $d_P+m$, but ends before displaying its generating-function formula. Consequently, this explicit normalization requires confirmation against the complete report. Coefficients beyond the degree of a numerator are understood to be zero.

The source describes $R_P$ as sums of products of linear forms whose individual factors are nonnegative on $P$. It describes $S_P$ as "nonnegative sums of products of linear forms," without specifying precisely where nonnegativity is required or whether it applies to coefficients, products, or their sum. We therefore retain $S_P$ as an incompletely specified source notation. The source asserts $R_P\subseteq S_P$, with strict inclusion in general, and supplies the example \[ P=\operatorname{conv}(0,e_1,e_2)\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^2, \qquad w(x_1,x_2)=(x_1-x_2)^2\in S_P\setminus R_P, \] where $e_1,e_2$ are the standard basis vectors.

Characterization problem. Characterize classes of homogeneous polynomial weights extending beyond $R_P$, particularly within the intended class $S_P$, that have both of the following properties: \[ h^*_{P,w,i}\geq0\qquad\text{for every integer }i\geq0, \] and coefficientwise monotonicity, namely, for each eligible inclusion of lattice polytopes $P\subseteq Q$ in a common ambient lattice, using the same weight $w$, \[ h^*_{P,w,i}\leq h^*_{Q,w,i} \qquad\text{for every integer }i\geq0. \] Here "eligible" remains unresolved: the record does not state the required membership condition for $w$ relative to $P$ and $Q$, or whether inclusions of different dimensions are intended. Thus these inequalities specify the desired properties for any fixed admissible data, but a fully quantified characterization requires the missing definitions and inclusion hypotheses. No complete definition of $S_P$ or admissibility condition is inferred from the example alone.

The restriction to homogeneous weights is material: the excerpt reports examples of nonhomogeneous weights that are nonnegative at every point of the polytope but have weighted numerators with negative coefficients.

Short English statement

Determine which homogeneous polynomial weights, especially weights in the source's class $S_P$ beyond $R_P$, produce weighted $h^*$-polynomials with nonnegative coefficients and coefficients that increase under suitable polytope inclusions. The exact definition of $S_P$, the numerator normalization, and the hypotheses governing inclusions require confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
