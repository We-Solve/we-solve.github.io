Structure Groups of Anisotropic Bilinear Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a field and let $E=K^n$. Let $A\in M_n(K)$ define the $K$-bilinear form \[ b(v,w)=v^{\mathsf T}Aw. \] Assume that $b$ is anisotropic, meaning that \[ b(v,v)\ne 0\qquad\text{for every }v\in E\setminus\{0\}. \] Symmetry of $A$ is not assumed. Let $L(K,n)$ denote the lattice of linear subspaces of $E$. For a subspace $U$, use the right orthogonal complement \[ U^{\perp_b}=\{z\in E: b(u,z)=0\text{ for every }u\in U\}. \] The source associates to $b$ an $L$-algebra $X(b)$ on this lattice by the Sasaki operation \[ U\mathbin{\cdot}W=U^{\perp_b}+(U\cap W), \qquad U,W\in L(K,n), \] where $+$ is the sum of subspaces. Here the orthogonal complement is interpreted in the ambient space $E$: the extracted source instead contains an unclear membership condition involving $V$. This interpretation requires checking against the original notation.

Write $G(X(b))$ for the structure group attached to this $L$-algebra in the source's convention. The supplied excerpt does not give its defining presentation or the general definition of an $L$-algebra, so these conventions remain to be supplied for a fully self-contained formulation.

For comparison, when $A=A^{\mathsf T}$, the supplied excerpt states, as Theorem 7 with reference label [1], that \[ G(X(b))\cong\operatorname{PPU}(b), \] where \[ \operatorname{PPU}(b)= \left\{\varphi(t)\in\operatorname{GL}_n\left(K[t,t^{-1}]\right): \begin{aligned} \varphi(t^{-1})^{\mathsf T}A\varphi(t)&=A,\\ \varphi(1)&=I_n \end{aligned} \right\}. \] Here $t$ is a formal indeterminate, $K[t,t^{-1}]$ is the Laurent polynomial ring, and the displayed transpose and substitution do not introduce an involution on $K$.

Problem 8. For an arbitrary anisotropic $K$-bilinear form $b$ as above, with $A$ not necessarily symmetric, find a representation or concrete description of $G(X(b))$ analogous to the displayed pure paraunitary description. The source does not specify the class of admissible representations or a precise criterion for being analogous; in particular, it does not assert that the same displayed matrix group describes the nonsymmetric case.

Immediately following Problem 8, the extraction lists: (1) $n\in\{1,2\}$, marked "trivial"; (2) existence of an anisotropic hermitian bilinear form on $K^n$, with reference label [3]; and (3) $K$ a finite field, with reference label [2]. It then states that a Desarguesian lattice without a brick structure must be infinite. Short English statement

Given an anisotropic bilinear form over a field, describe the structure group of its associated subspace $L$-algebra when the form may be nonsymmetric. The requested description should be analogous to the pure paraunitary model supplied for symmetric forms. The structure-group convention, the precise meaning of an analogous representation, and several extracted contextual qualifications require human review.
