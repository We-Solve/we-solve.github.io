Hirzebruch's 1954 Problem List — Problem 6

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M_n$ denote the smooth manifold underlying complex projective space $\mathbb{CP}^{n}$, equipped with its usual differentiable structure. Here $n$ is the complex dimension, hence a nonnegative integer; the source does not specify its intended range or whether dimension zero is included.

A complex structure on $M_n$ means a smooth integrable bundle endomorphism $J:TM_n\to TM_n$ satisfying $J^2=-\operatorname{id}$. Integrability means that $J$ is induced by an atlas of complex coordinate charts with holomorphic transition maps. Write \[ \mathcal{J}(M_n)=\{J:TM_n\to TM_n\mid J\text{ is a smooth integrable complex structure}\}. \] A complex structure $J$ admits a Kähler metric if there exists a Riemannian metric $g$ on $M_n$ such that \[ g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y),\qquad \omega(X,Y)=g(JX,Y),\qquad \mathrm{d}\omega=0 \] for all smooth tangent vector fields $X,Y$. Define \[ \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{K}}(M_n)=\{J\in\mathcal{J}(M_n)\mid (M_n,J)\text{ admits a K\"ahler metric}\}. \] Problem. Determine all complex structures on $M_n$, and in particular determine all those admitting a Kähler metric; that is, determine $\mathcal{J}(M_n)$ and its subset $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{K}}(M_n)$, in the classification sense intended by the source.

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify the equivalence relation for this classification. The usual interpretation would identify $J$ and $J'$ when there is a diffeomorphism $f:M_n\to M_n$ satisfying $\mathrm{d}f\circ J=J'\circ\mathrm{d}f$, equivalently a biholomorphism between the resulting complex manifolds; this interpretation requires confirmation. The source also says "See Problem 14" and labels the Kähler subquestion $(28*)$. Neither the referenced problem nor an explanation of that label is supplied.

Short English statement

Classify complex structures on the usual smooth manifold underlying $\mathbb{CP}^{n}$, including the subcollection that admits a Kähler metric. The source leaves the dimension range and classification equivalence unspecified. Its cross-reference to Problem 14 and label $(28*)$ remain unresolved.
