Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied source asks: "Solve $\bar\partial$ with $L^p$ estimates, $1<p<\infty$, for smooth convex domains in ${\mathbb{C}}^n$."

The usual estimate can be expressed by the following template. Fix a positive integer $n$, a convex domain $\Omega\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}^n$ with smooth boundary, an exponent $1<p<\infty$, and a form degree $q$ with $1\leq q\leq n$. Here a domain is a connected open set. Write $L^p_{0,q}(\Omega)$ for the measurable $(0,q)$-forms whose coefficients have finite $L^p$ norm with respect to Euclidean volume. For a form $v$, the operator $\bar\partial$ is interpreted distributionally as \[ \bar\partial v=\sum_{j=1}^{n}d\bar z_j\wedge\frac{\partial v}{\partial\bar z_j}. \] One seeks a finite constant $C_{\Omega,p,q}$ such that \[ \forall f\in L^p_{0,q}(\Omega),\qquad \bar\partial f=0 \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists u\in L^p_{0,q-1}(\Omega):\quad \bar\partial u=f,\qquad \|u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}\leq C_{\Omega,p,q}\|f\|_{L^p(\Omega)}. \] The constant must be independent of $f$; the displayed template allows dependence on the domain, exponent, and form degree.

This template explains a standard meaning of the requested estimate, but the supplied statement does not fully determine its scope. It does not specify the form degree or whether all degrees are intended, whether domains must be bounded, the precise boundary regularity meant by smooth, or whether the estimates are global or local. It also does not prescribe boundary conditions, require a linear solution operator, or state any uniformity across domains. These qualifications remain unresolved; the template is not an assertion that the source explicitly imposes each of its conventions.

Short English statement

For each $1<p<\infty$, seek solutions of the $\bar\partial$ equation on smooth convex domains in ${\mathbb{C}}^n$ whose $L^p$ norms are controlled by the data. The source leaves the form degrees, boundedness assumptions, and precise scope of the estimates unspecified.
