Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(W,L,\rho)$ be any triple admissible for the source's quantum hyperbolic invariants (QHI). Let $K_N(W,L,\rho)$ denote its invariant at the admissible positive integer parameter $N$, and let $c_I(W,L,\rho)$ and $R$ be the source's class and regulator-type expression. The real volume conjecture attributed to S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti is \[\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N^2}\log\left|K_N(W,L,\rho)\right| =\operatorname{Im}R\left(c_I(W,L,\rho)\right)\] for every such triple. The logarithm is the real natural logarithm of the absolute value, and the denominator is $N^2$, exactly as supplied. This is an ordinary limit along the parameters for which the source defines $K_N$, not an optimistic limit or a logarithm with complex branch choices. Root-of-unity multiplication does not change the absolute value.

If $V=\operatorname{Im}R(c_I(W,L,\rho))$ is a well-defined finite real number, the finite-limit reading means that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $N_0$ such that for every admissible $N\geq N_0$, the logarithm is defined and \[\left|\frac{2\pi}{N^2}\log|K_N(W,L,\rho)|-V\right|<\varepsilon.\] The source does not specify how zero invariant values are treated; an extended convention $\log0=-\infty$ would still require addressing them for a finite limit. The domain and equivalence data of the triples, allowed $N$, definition of $c_I$, domain and ambiguity of $R$, and well-definedness of its imaginary part must be supplied by the surrounding theory. The formula does not assert that every $W$ is hyperbolic or replace its right side by a particular geometric volume without those definitions.

Short English statement

The absolute values of the QHI invariants should have logarithmic growth $N^2/(2\pi)$ times the imaginary regulator value, for every admissible triple. The omitted QHI definitions and possible zero values require clarification.
