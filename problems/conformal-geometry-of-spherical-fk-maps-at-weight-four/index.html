Conformal geometry of spherical FK maps at weight four

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For rooted $n$-edge spherical FK maps at $q=4$, weight map/subset pairs by $4^{\ell/2}$, where $\ell=2k(A)+|A|-|V|$. Uniformize the equilateral flag-triangle sphere by three independent area marks sent to $0,1,\infty$; denote the embedding $\phi_n$, normalized flag area $m_n$, full embedded distance graph $K_n(a_n)$, and nested interfaces $\Gamma_n$. The source claims deterministic $a_n\to0$ with \[ ((\phi_n)_*m_n,K_n(a_n),\Gamma_n) \Longrightarrow(\mu_h^{\mathrm{crit}},\{(z,w,D_h(z,w))\},\Gamma). \] The target is its area-one critical $\gamma=2$ quantum-sphere law and nested $\operatorname{CLE}_4$. Topologies are weak area, Hausdorff distance graphs, and uniform loop matching with nesting/multiplicities. In the same joint limit, the maximum spherical diameter of any embedded flag triangle tends to zero in probability. The limiting nested whole-plane $\operatorname{CLE}_4$ is independent of the entire marked quantum surface. The distance graph is \[ K_n(a)=\{(\phi_n(u),\phi_n(v),a d_n(u,v)):u,v\in V(M_n)\}, \] where $d_n$ counts all primal map edges.

Outline of the argument

The field and area law are first obtained by taking the Liouville parameter to its critical value, with area-mark normalization and norm convergence. Critical contour sewing and local reconstruction then couple the embedded map metric, measure, and all macroscopic loop interfaces to this surface.
