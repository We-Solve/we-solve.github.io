Semicuspidal and Schur Algebras of Affine Quivers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $Q$ be a quiver of affine type, and let $Q_0$ denote its vertex set. Let $k=\mathbb F_q$, where $q$ is a prime power. The notation $\delta$ conventionally denotes the minimal positive imaginary root of the corresponding affine root system; the supplied excerpt does not explicitly define it or specify the precise class of affine quivers allowed.

The supplied record calls $C(n\delta)$ the semicuspidal algebra. Its construction, coefficient conventions, and auxiliary choices are not supplied. The excerpt also mentions a KLR algebra $R(n\delta)$ and an ideal $I_\mu(n\delta)$, without defining either or explicitly giving the quotient defining $C(n\delta)$. The notation $S_n(\mathbb Z^{Q_0})$ denotes the proposed Schur algebra in the cleaned record, but neither this construction nor the mathematical object represented by $\mathbb Z^{Q_0}$ is defined in the supplied material.

Conjecture 6, as represented by the supplied cleaned record. For every quiver $Q$ of affine type and every finite coefficient field $k=\mathbb F_q$, the proposed identification is \[ C(n\delta)\simeq S_n(\mathbb Z^{Q_0})\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb F_q. \] Here $n$ is the parameter occurring in the source. Its permitted range, including whether $n=0$ is included, is not stated. The tensor product presupposes an integral algebra structure on the proposed Schur algebra, whose definition is absent. The intended assertion is an algebra isomorphism; whether it must preserve a grading or additional structure is unspecified.

Unresolved source qualification. The extracted original writes the right-hand algebra as Sen (ZQ0), whereas the cleaned statement writes $S_n(\mathbb Z^{Q_0})$. The excerpt separately distinguishes $S_n(\mathbb P^1)$ from an image algebra also extracted with the notation Sen. Consequently, a potentially meaningful decoration on the conjectured Schur algebra cannot be resolved from this record. The displayed formulation records the cleaned target conditionally; it does not identify these potentially distinct algebras or supply their missing definitions.

The proposed geometric argument is explicitly unfinished in the supplied excerpt. Its subsequent Steinberg-variety assertion is labelled a theorem in progress, and is not supplied as an established proof of this conjecture.

Short English statement

For an affine quiver, the conjecture proposes an isomorphism between its semicuspidal algebra at dimension $n\delta$ and the reduction of a proposed integral Schur algebra modulo the characteristic of $\mathbb F_q$. The supplied excerpt leaves essential definitions, the range of $n$, and a potentially significant Schur-algebra decoration unresolved. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
