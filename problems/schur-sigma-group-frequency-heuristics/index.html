Schur $\sigma$-Group Frequency Heuristics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime. A finite $p$-group is a finite group whose order is a power of $p$. For a finite $p$-group $H$, its generator rank is \[ d(H)=\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}H/\left(H^p[H,H]\right), \] where $H^p$ is the subgroup generated by the $p$th powers and $[H,H]$ is the commutator subgroup. Write $\operatorname{Aut}(H)$ for its automorphism group.

The supplied conjecture concerns a finite $p$-group $G$ that is either a Schur $\sigma$-group of rank $g$, or a maximal $p$-class-$c$ quotient of such a group. It considers imaginary quadratic fields $K$ for which $A_K$ has rank $g$. Retaining the source notation, put \[ M(G;g,h,p)=\frac{z(G)^g}{|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|}\,p^{-gh} \prod_{k=1}^{g}\left(p^g-p^{g-k}\right) \prod_{k=1}^{h}\left(p^g-p^{h-k}\right). \] The source asks whether the probability that $G_K\cong G$, in the Schur $\sigma$-group case, equals $M(G;g,h,p)$, and whether the probability that the maximal $p$-class-$c$ quotient of $G_K$ is isomorphic to $G$, in the quotient case, equals the same expression. Both probabilities are conditioned on $A_K$ having rank $g$. The source also describes the proposed probability as a limiting frequency.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture predicts a particular frequency for a finite Schur $\sigma$-group, or a specified quotient of one, among groups attached to imaginary quadratic fields with fixed class-group rank in the source notation. Several essential symbols, group conventions, and the meaning of limiting frequency are unspecified, so the formula is preserved with these gaps explicit.
