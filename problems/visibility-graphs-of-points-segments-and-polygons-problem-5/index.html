Visibility Graphs of Points, Segments and Polygons — Problem 5

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt does not define blockers or $b(n)$, and does not explicitly specify the ambient space. The following is a precise candidate formulation under a planar interpretation, with $b(n)$ interpreted as a minimum over point configurations. These interpretations require confirmation from the complete source.

For an integer $n\geq 2$, let $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ consist of $n$ distinct points in general position, meaning that no three points of $P$ are collinear. For distinct $p,q\in P$, define the open segment \[ (p,q)=\{(1-t)p+tq:0<t<1\}. \] A finite set $B\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2\setminus P$ blocks $P$ if \[ B\cap(p,q)\neq\varnothing \qquad\text{for every distinct }p,q\in P. \] Under the interpretation above, set \[ b(P)=\min\{|B|:B\text{ is a finite set blocking }P\}, \qquad b(n)=\min_{\substack{P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2,\ |P|=n\\\text{no three points of }P\text{ collinear}}}b(P). \] The requested conjecture is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{b(n)}{n}=+\infty. \] Equivalently, for every real $M>0$ there exists an integer $N\geq2$ such that, for every integer $n\geq N$, every such configuration $P$ of $n$ points, and every finite set $B$ blocking $P$, one has $|B|\geq Mn$. The threshold $N$ must be uniform over all these configurations.

For the accompanying source context, define \[ \mu(P)=\left|\left\{\frac{p+q}{2}:p,q\in P,\ p\neq q\right\}\right|, \qquad \mu(n)=\min_{\substack{P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2,\ |P|=n\\\text{no three points of }P\text{ collinear}}}\mu(P). \] Thus coincident midpoints are counted only once. The excerpt states $b(n)\leq\binom n2$ and attributes to Stanchescu and Pach, independently, the bounds \[ b(n)\leq\mu(n)\leq n\,2^{c\sqrt{\log n}}, \] where $c$ is an absolute constant, using Freiman's theorem on set addition. Here $\log$ may be read as the natural logarithm, with its fixed base absorbed into $c$; the excerpt does not specify the range of $n$ for the displayed estimate. Its accompanying observation is that any failure of $\mu(n)=O(n)$ is constrained by this upper bound to slightly superlinear growth. These bounds and attributions are supplied background, not independently verified results in this draft. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied curation rather than an independent assessment.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly stated planar interpretation, prove that the minimum number of added points needed to block every segment joining two of $n$ points, with no three original points collinear, grows faster than every constant multiple of $n$, uniformly over all configurations. The excerpt leaves the definitions of blockers and $b(n)$ implicit, so this interpretation requires source confirmation.
