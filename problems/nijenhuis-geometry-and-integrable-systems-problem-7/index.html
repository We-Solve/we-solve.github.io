Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the conventional real-manifold interpretation, let $M$ be a two-dimensional manifold and let $L\colon TM\to TM$ be a field of endomorphisms. The supplied excerpt does not specify the differentiability category. Its Nijenhuis torsion is \[ N_L(X,Y)=[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]+L^2[X,Y] \] for vector fields $X,Y$. The operator $L$ is Nijenhuis if $N_L(X,Y)=0$ for all $X,Y$. It is $\mathrm{gl}$-regular if, at every $p\in M$, the minimal polynomial of $L_p$ has degree two; equivalently, $L_p$ is not a scalar multiple of the identity.

For precision, the conventional differential interpretation of geodesic compatibility is recorded here. A nondegenerate symmetric metric tensor $g$, with Levi--Civita connection $\nabla$, is geodesically compatible with $L$ if \[ g(LX,Y)=g(X,LY) \] and \[ (\nabla_XL)Y=\frac12\left({\,\mathrm{d}}(\operatorname{tr}L)(Y)X+g(X,Y)\operatorname{grad}_g(\operatorname{tr}L)\right) \] for all vector fields $X,Y$, where $g(\operatorname{grad}_g h,Z)={\,\mathrm{d}} h(Z)$. This convention permits indefinite metrics; the excerpt does not specify the intended signature or provide its own definition of compatibility.

Requested construction. For every $\mathrm{gl}$-regular Nijenhuis operator $L$ in dimension two, construct explicitly a metric $g$ geodesically compatible with $L$.

The supplied text suggests first obtaining a regular conservation law $f$, then constructing companion coordinates and applying an explicit formula cited as [nijappl5]. This is a suggested route, not an additional hypothesis on $L$. The excerpt supplies neither the definitions needed for that route nor the cited formula. It also does not determine whether the requested construction is local near every point or global on $M$, the permitted metric signatures, or the regularity category. These qualifications remain unresolved; the displayed compatibility equations express a conventional interpretation requiring confirmation against the complete source context.

Short English statement

For each two-dimensional $\mathrm{gl}$-regular Nijenhuis operator, explicitly construct a geodesically compatible metric.
