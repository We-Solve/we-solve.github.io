Math-61

Embedded Resolution of Singularities in Positive Characteristic

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a scheme $Y$, regularity means that every local ring $\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ is a regular local ring. A regular center is a closed subscheme that is regular. Given a closed immersion $X_i\hookrightarrow Z_i$ and a regular center $C_i\hookrightarrow Z_i$, write \[ \pi_i:Z_{i+1}=\operatorname{Bl}_{C_i}(Z_i)\longrightarrow Z_i \] for the blowup. The strict transform $X_{i+1}\hookrightarrow Z_{i+1}$ is the scheme-theoretic closure of $\pi_i^{-1}(X_i\setminus C_i)$ in $Z_{i+1}$. To use these strict transforms as birational modifications of all components of $X_i$, suitable restrictions on the centers and on $X_i$ must be specified; the source does not provide them.

Source question, with unresolved scope. Does embedded resolution exist in dimension three or higher? Its explicitly stated core asks whether, for every admissible closed immersion $X=X_0\hookrightarrow Z=Z_0$, there exist a finite integer $r\geq 0$ and blowups \[ Z_r\xrightarrow{\pi_{r-1}}Z_{r-1}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow Z_1\xrightarrow{\pi_0}Z_0, \qquad Z_{i+1}=\operatorname{Bl}_{C_i}(Z_i), \] with regular centers $C_i$, such that the successive strict transforms $X_i\hookrightarrow Z_i$ culminate in a regular $X_r$, while retaining the ambient embeddings.

This expresses the core requested construction, but does not specify a complete embedded-resolution assertion. The source leaves the class of admissible pairs undefined: it does not specify a base field or base scheme, finite-type or reducedness hypotheses, regularity of the ambient space, whether dimension refers to $X$ or $Z$, or conditions ensuring that the modification preserves the regular locus. It also does not state whether the exceptional divisor must have simple normal crossings or how it must meet $X_r$. No such requirements are silently imposed here.

The title and supplied literature assessment indicate positive characteristic as the intended unresolved setting, whereas the historical question itself has no characteristic hypothesis. Thus the characteristic restriction, the meaning of dimension three or higher, and the full endpoint conditions require clarification before this can be treated as a precise universally quantified conjecture. The supplied assessment describes characteristic-zero resolution as known and qualifies its positive-characteristic open-status claim by excluding known low-dimensional cases; those cases and hypotheses are not identified in the record.

Short English statement

The question asks whether an embedded singular space can be resolved through finitely many ambient blowups with regular centers. The supplied title points to positive characteristic, but the historical wording leaves the domain, dimension convention, and required exceptional-divisor behavior unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review; the supplied open-status assessment is dated curation.
