Quadrilaterals of Subfactors with Two Intermediate Factors

Definitions and mathematical statement

A factor is a von Neumann algebra whose center consists of the scalar multiples of its identity. The supplied configuration is a quadruple of factors $(N,P,Q,M)$ with inclusions \[ N\subseteq P\subseteq M,\qquad N\subseteq Q\subseteq M. \] Thus $P$ and $Q$ are intermediate subfactors of $N\subseteq M$. The cleaned source formulation imposes finite index, written symbolically as \[ [M:N]<\infty. \] The factor type and the index convention are not specified in the supplied record; this notation records the stated requirement without resolving those choices. The record also does not explicitly specify properness of the inclusions, distinctness of $P$ and $Q$, irreducibility, or the conditions $P\cap Q=N$ and $P\vee Q=M$, where $P\vee Q$ denotes the von Neumann algebra generated by $P$ and $Q$. These additional conditions are therefore not imposed here.

Problem, as a research programme. Develop a planar-algebra theory for finite-index configurations $N\subseteq P,Q\subseteq M$ that accounts for the relative position of the two intermediate subfactors, extending the treatment beyond the commuting-square and restricted cocommuting cases mentioned in the cleaned source formulation.
