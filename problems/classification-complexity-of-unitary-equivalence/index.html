Classification Complexity of Unitary Equivalence

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A $C^*$-algebra $A$ is separable if its norm topology has a countable dense subset. For equivalence relations $E$ on a standard Borel space $X$ and $F$ on a standard Borel space $Y$, write $E\leq_B F$ if there is a Borel map $f:X\to Y$ such that \[ x\mathrel{E}x'\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(x)\mathrel{F}f(x') \qquad(x,x'\in X). \] The relation $E$ is smooth if $E\leq_B {=}_{{\mathbb{R}}}$. It is classifiable by countable structures if there exists a countable first-order language $L$ such that $E\leq_B\cong_L$, where $\cong_L$ denotes isomorphism on the standard Borel space $\operatorname{Mod}_{{\mathbb{N}}}(L)$ of $L$-structures with underlying set ${\mathbb{N}}$.

The supplied question asks whether, for every separable $C^*$-algebra $A$, \[ E_A^{\mathrm{u.e.}}\not\leq_B {=}_{{\mathbb{R}}} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{for every countable language }L,\quad E_A^{\mathrm{u.e.}}\not\leq_B\cong_L. \] Here $E_A^{\mathrm{u.e.}}$ is called a unitary-equivalence relation in the supplied statement. However, the record does not specify the objects being compared, their standard Borel parameter space, or the precise meaning of unitary equivalence. The displayed implication is therefore a formulation schema, conditional on supplying those definitions, and is not a fully determined mathematical assertion. In particular, the linked reference concerning automorphisms does not by itself identify the intended domain. No unitality or continuous-trace hypothesis is stated in the question.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every nonsmooth unitary-equivalence relation associated with a separable $C^*$-algebra fails to admit classification by countable structures through Borel maps. The record leaves the relation and its parameter space unspecified, so its intended assertion requires clarification.
