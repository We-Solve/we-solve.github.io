Math-779

Large-Data Scattering for Energy-Critical Schrödinger Maps

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write spacetime as ${\mathbb{R}}_t\times{\mathbb{R}}_x^2$ and let $S^2=\{v\in{\mathbb{R}}^3:|v|=1\}$. In the usual extrinsic convention, a Schrödinger map is a map $u:I\times{\mathbb{R}}^2\to S^2$ satisfying \[ \partial_tu=u\times\Delta_xu,\qquad u(0,x)=u_0(x), \] where $I$ is a time interval containing $0$, $\times$ is the cross product in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$, and $\Delta_x=\partial_{x_1}^2+\partial_{x_2}^2$. This equation makes explicit the conventional interpretation of the term Schrödinger map; the supplied statement does not display the equation or its sign convention. Use the Dirichlet-energy normalization \[ E(v)=\frac12\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^2}\left(|\partial_{x_1}v|^2+|\partial_{x_2}v|^2\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] For a smooth map $v$ extending continuously to the one-point compactification ${\mathbb{R}}^2\cup\{\infty\}\simeq S^2$, its homotopy class is described by its degree, with \[ \deg(v)=\frac{1}{4\pi}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^2}v\cdot(\partial_{x_1}v\times\partial_{x_2}v)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] Thus, in this compactified setting, zero homotopy class means $\deg(v)=0$. Continuous extension at infinity is an explicit sufficient setting for this interpretation, not a boundary condition stated in the supplied record; the intended treatment of more general finite-energy maps is unspecified.

Question (permanent catalogue entry). For every initial map $u_0:{\mathbb{R}}^2\to S^2$ in the intended admissible class, with zero homotopy class and \[ E(u_0)<2E(Q_1)=8\pi, \] does the Schrödinger-map initial-value problem have global well-posedness and scattering? Global well-posedness asks for existence for all times $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$, uniqueness in the intended solution class, and continuous dependence on the initial data in the intended topology. Scattering asks for the asymptotic behavior specified by the intended scattering framework.

The strict threshold excludes $E(u_0)=8\pi$. The record supplies the equality $E(Q_1)=4\pi$ but does not define $Q_1$. It also does not specify the initial-data regularity, topology for continuous dependence, uniqueness class, scattering variables, comparison evolution, convergence norm, or whether scattering is required in both time directions. Consequently, the displayed question preserves the supplied target but is not yet a fully specified analytic theorem statement; those choices require source clarification and human mathematical review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every admissible degree-zero Schrödinger map from two-dimensional space into $S^2$, with initial energy strictly below $8\pi$, evolves globally with uniqueness, continuous dependence, and scattering. The source leaves the precise data class, boundary behavior, and meaning of scattering unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved; that status is dated curation rather than an independently verified conclusion.
