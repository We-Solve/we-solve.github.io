Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.7

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $g\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $g\geq 2$. The source denotes by $\Gamma_g$ the fundamental group of a closed surface of genus $g$, without explicitly specifying orientability. Under the usual interpretation as a closed connected orientable surface $\Sigma_g$, set $\Gamma_g=\pi_1(\Sigma_g)$. The formulation below uses this interpretation; the orientability qualification requires confirmation from the source.

For every finite subset $S\subseteq\Gamma_g$ satisfying $\langle S\rangle=\Gamma_g$, write $S^{-1}=\{s^{-1}:s\in S\}$. For each integer $n\geq 0$, define \[ B_S(n)=\{s_1\cdots s_m:0\leq m\leq n,\ s_i\in S\cup S^{-1}\}, \qquad \beta_S(n)=|B_S(n)|, \] where the empty product is the identity. Define \[ \omega(\Gamma_g,S)=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\beta_S(n)^{1/n}, \qquad \omega(\Gamma_g)=\inf_{\substack{S\subseteq\Gamma_g\text{ finite}\\\langle S\rangle=\Gamma_g}}\omega(\Gamma_g,S). \] The problem is to determine the exact value of $\omega(\Gamma_g)$ for every integer $g\geq 2$.

As supplied background, the source states that for the free group $F_k$ of rank $k\geq 2$, the corresponding infimum over all finite generating sets is $\omega(F_k)=2k-1$, citing M. Gromov, Exercise 5.13. It further states that every finite generating set of $\Gamma_g$ contains a subset of $2g-1$ elements generating an infinite-index subgroup whose abelianization is ${\mathbb{Z}}^{2g-1}$, hence a free subgroup of rank $2g-1$. The source consequently records the lower bound \[ \omega(\Gamma_g)\geq 4g-3. \] These are supplied background assertions, not an independently verified assessment or a determination of the requested infimum.

Short English statement

For each genus $g\geq 2$, determine the smallest exponential growth rate obtainable from finite generating sets of the closed surface group. The supplied lower bound is $4g-3$. The source does not explicitly specify orientability; this draft uses the usual orientable interpretation subject to confirmation. The registry labels the problem open, without independent verification here.
