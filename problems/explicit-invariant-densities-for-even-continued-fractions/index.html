Math-6531

Explicit Invariant Densities for Even Continued Fractions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an irrational number $x\in[0,1]$, write its regular continued fraction as \[ x=[0;a_1(x),a_2(x),\ldots],\qquad a_n(x)\in\{1,2,3,\ldots\}. \] Define \[ E_{\mathrm{even}}=\{x\in[0,1]\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}: a_n(x)\in\{2,4,6,\ldots\}\text{ for every }n\geq1\}, \qquad h=\dim_{\mathrm H}E_{\mathrm{even}}. \] The Gauss map on irrational points of $(0,1)$ is \[ T(x)=\frac1x-\left\lfloor\frac1x\right\rfloor. \] It maps $E_{\mathrm{even}}$ into itself. Interpret the source's $P_h$ as the $h$-dimensional packing measure restricted to $E_{\mathrm{even}}$; its normalization is not specified. The source refers to a measure $\mu$ on this set satisfying \[ \mu(T^{-1}A)=\mu(A),\qquad \mu(A)=0\Longleftrightarrow P_h(A)=0 \] for every Borel set $A\subseteq E_{\mathrm{even}}$, where the inverse image is taken under the restricted map. The record does not specify a normalization or a uniqueness characterization of $\mu$, or supply the measure-theoretic hypotheses supporting the stated Radon--Nikodym derivative.

Recorded request, subject to unresolved qualifications. Find a closed explicit formula for the real-analytic extension of $\mathrm d\mu/\mathrm dP_h$ to $[0,1]$, or prove that no such formula exists. Under the usual neighborhood interpretation of real analyticity on a closed interval, the extension sought would be a real-analytic function $F:U\to{\mathbb{R}}$, for some open set $U\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$ containing $[0,1]$, such that \[ F(x)=\frac{\mathrm d\mu}{\mathrm dP_h}(x) \quad\text{for }P_h\text{-almost every }x\in E_{\mathrm{even}}. \] This interpretation of endpoint analyticity requires confirmation. The wording presupposes such an extension but does not supply its existence or hypotheses ensuring it. Moreover, "closed explicit formula" has no specified class of admissible expressions, so its nonexistence alternative is not a precise mathematical assertion as supplied.

Short English statement

The recorded request concerns an analytic formula for the density of a Gauss-map invariant measure on numbers whose regular continued-fraction entries are all even. The measure normalization, endpoint analyticity convention, and meaning of "closed explicit formula" require clarification. The supplied curation also reports a truncated, duplicated extraction involving a different intended question, so this draft requires source and mathematical review.
