Math-6843

Strong Geography for Rational Homology Spheres

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A rational homology three-sphere is a closed connected three-manifold $Y$ satisfying $H_*(Y;{\mathbb{Q}})\cong H_*(S^3;{\mathbb{Q}})$. The notation $HF^-(Y)$ denotes minus Heegaard Floer homology, regarded as a module over ${\mathbb{F}}[U]$, and $HF^-_{\mathrm{red}}(Y)$ denotes its reduced part. Here $U$ acts by the module structure. The supplied record does not specify the coefficient field ${\mathbb{F}}$, orientation conventions, the precise reduced-homology convention, or whether direct summands must respect gradings.

For a nonzero reduced module annihilated by a power of $U$, let \[ \ell=\min\{n\geq 1: U^nHF^-_{\mathrm{red}}(Y)=0\}. \] For each positive integer $j$, the module ${\mathbb{F}}[U]/(U^j)$ is the quotient by the ideal generated by $U^j$.

Supplied question (strong geography restriction). For every rational homology three-sphere $Y$, does $HF^-(Y)$ contain a direct summand \[ \bigoplus_{j=1}^{\ell}{\mathbb{F}}[U]/(U^j) = {\mathbb{F}}[U]/(U^\ell)\oplus{\mathbb{F}}[U]/(U^{\ell-1})\oplus\cdots\oplus{\mathbb{F}}[U]/(U)? \] In the category of ungraded ${\mathbb{F}}[U]$-modules, this asks whether there exists a module $M$ such that \[ HF^-(Y)\cong\left(\bigoplus_{j=1}^{\ell}{\mathbb{F}}[U]/(U^j)\right)\oplus M. \] This expresses the ungraded reading only; a graded interpretation would require additional conventions concerning grading shifts.

Unresolved qualifications. The source defines $\ell$ as the least integer annihilating the reduced part, without explicitly specifying the allowed integers. If $HF^-_{\mathrm{red}}(Y)=0$, the least nonnegative exponent is $0$, whereas the least positive exponent is $1$; these give different interpretations of the requested summand. The record does not settle this boundary case, so the displayed positive-length formulation does not resolve the full universal question. The source also does not explicitly state the nilpotence and finiteness conventions underlying the definition of $\ell$.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether the minus Heegaard Floer homology of every rational homology three-sphere contains cyclic ${\mathbb{F}}[U]$-summands of every length from $1$ through the nilpotence length of its reduced part. Coefficients, grading conventions, and the case of vanishing reduced homology require clarification. The supplied open label and verification claims are recorded as curation, not independently established facts.
