Persistence and Exit Times of Integrated Self-Similar Processes

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses a family of questions about integrals of self-similar Markov processes, but does not specify enough hypotheses to determine a unique mathematical statement. The following notation describes a conditional formulation; the choices introduced here are not asserted to be those intended by the source.

For a real-valued Markov family $(Y,\mathbb P_y)_{y\in E}$, where $E\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$ is invariant under positive dilations, self-similarity with index $H>0$ means that, for every $c>0$ and $y\in E$, \[ (Y_{ct})_{t\geq0}\text{ under }\mathbb P_y \ \stackrel{\mathrm{law}}{=}\ (c^H Y_t)_{t\geq0}\text{ under }\mathbb P_{c^{-H}y}. \] This definition presupposes that the processes are defined for the indicated times; any killing, lifetime, or boundary convention must also be specified. If $Y$ has locally time-integrable paths, define its integral, with initial integral value $z\in{\mathbb{R}}$, by \[ I_t=z+\int_0^t Y_s\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s. \] The initial data for the integrated process generally include both $I_0=z$ and $Y_0=y$; write $\mathbb P_{z,y}$ for such a joint initial condition when defined.

For example, if the intended exit problem concerns a specified finite interval $(a,b)$, with $a<z<b$, one could define \[ \tau_{(a,b)}=\inf\{t\geq0:I_t\notin(a,b)\}, \qquad \inf\varnothing=\infty. \] If $I$ is continuous and defined for all relevant times, and if $T_u=\inf\{t\geq0:I_t=u\}$, then $\tau_{(a,b)}=T_a\wedge T_b$. The source does not define $T_a$, $T_b$, or the subscript $x$, so this interpretation remains conditional.

Requested problems. Determine persistence and exit-time asymptotics for the integral of a general self-similar Markov process. In particular, the supplied expression to be studied is \[ \mathbb P_x(T_a\wedge T_b>t),\qquad t\longrightarrow\infty. \] Under the interval interpretation above, this would ask for the large-time behavior of $\mathbb P_{z,y}(\tau_{(a,b)}>t)$, for the specified process, boundary conditions, and admissible initial data. The source does not state whether an exponent, an asymptotic equivalent, a leading constant, or another description is required, and does not separately define the persistence event.

The record also asks for the analogous exit-time problem when the driving process $Y$ is a stable Lévy process, and asks to compute the area under a general stable excursion. If $e$ denotes a specified excursion with lifetime $\zeta$, its signed area, when the integral exists, would be \[ A(e)=\int_0^{\zeta}e(s)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s. \] Neither the underlying stable process, the excursion law and normalization, nor the meaning of "compute" is supplied. In particular, the source does not determine whether this is a signed or nonnegative area, or whether the desired output is its distribution, transform, moments, or asymptotics. These qualifications must be resolved before these questions become fully specified mathematical problems.

Short English statement

The problem asks for long-time survival probabilities for integrated self-similar Markov processes, including stable Lévy processes, and for the area law of a general stable excursion. The source leaves the process class, initial data, boundary conventions, and excursion normalization unspecified. The displayed definitions therefore give only a conditional framework requiring human mathematical review.
