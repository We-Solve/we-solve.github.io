Polynomial Subalgebras Admitting SAGBI Bases

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: Which subalgebras of a polynomial ring have a SAGBI basis?

To specify the usual terminology over a field, let $k$ be a field, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $A\subseteq k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ be a unital $k$-subalgebra. Fix a monomial order $\prec$, meaning a total well-order on monomials, compatible with multiplication and with $1$ least. For a nonzero polynomial $f$, write $\operatorname{LM}_{\prec}(f)$ for its largest monomial under $\prec$. Define the initial algebra by \[ \operatorname{in}_{\prec}(A) = k\left[\operatorname{LM}_{\prec}(f):f\in A\setminus\{0\}\right]. \] A subset $B\subseteq A\setminus\{0\}$ is a SAGBI basis of $A$ with respect to $\prec$ if \[ \operatorname{in}_{\prec}(A) = k\left[\operatorname{LM}_{\prec}(b):b\in B\right]. \] It is a finite SAGBI basis when $B$ is finite.

The supplied literature assessment interprets the target as a characterization of subalgebras admitting finite SAGBI bases. Under that interpretation, for a specified $k$, $n$, and $\prec$, the characterization problem concerns the condition \[ \exists\,B\subseteq A\setminus\{0\},\quad |B|<\infty, \qquad \operatorname{in}_{\prec}(A) =k\left[\operatorname{LM}_{\prec}(b):b\in B\right]. \] If the order is instead allowed to vary with $A$, the condition becomes \[ \exists\,\prec\ \exists\,B\subseteq A\setminus\{0\},\quad |B|<\infty, \qquad \operatorname{in}_{\prec}(A) =k\left[\operatorname{LM}_{\prec}(b):b\in B\right]. \] The record does not determine which order quantifier is intended. It also does not specify the coefficient domain, the number of variables, or whether the subalgebras under consideration must be finitely generated. The field-based definitions above therefore explain a conventional setting rather than supply missing source hypotheses. Moreover, the question itself omits finiteness: if arbitrary infinite SAGBI bases are permitted under the displayed definition, $A\setminus\{0\}$ is such a basis by construction. Thus the finite interpretation comes from the supplied assessment and requires confirmation against the source context.

Short English statement

Characterize polynomial subalgebras whose initial algebras are generated by the leading monomials of finitely many of their elements. Finiteness is indicated by the supplied literature assessment but is absent from the quoted question. The coefficient domain, restrictions on the subalgebras, and whether the monomial order is fixed or may be chosen remain unresolved.
