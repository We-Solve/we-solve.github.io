Math-4908

Preservation of Positive-Scalar-Curvature Obstructions Under Surgery

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a smooth manifold $M$, under the convention that no completeness or boundary conditions are imposed, let \[ \mathcal R_{>0}(M)=\{g:\ g\text{ is a smooth Riemannian metric on }M,\ \operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0\text{ for every }x\in M\}. \] Here $\operatorname{Scal}_g$ denotes scalar curvature. The source does not specify whether metrics must be complete, whether manifolds are closed, or what conditions apply at a boundary; thus this convention is provisional.

To express the question for ordinary framed interior surgery, let $M$ have dimension $n$, let $0\leq p\leq n-1$, and let \[ \iota:S^p\times D^{n-p}\hookrightarrow\operatorname{int}(M) \] be a smooth embedding specifying a framed tubular neighborhood. Here $S^k$ and $D^k$ denote the standard sphere and closed disk. The manifold obtained by this surgery is \[ M_\iota= \left(M\setminus\iota\left(S^p\times\operatorname{int}(D^{n-p})\right)\right) \mathop{\cup}_{S^p\times S^{n-p-1}} \left(D^{p+1}\times S^{n-p-1}\right), \] where the gluing uses the boundary identification specified by $\iota$, with the usual smoothing. The surgery sphere has codimension $n-p$.

Problem. Determine which kinds of surgery preserve nonexistence of positive scalar curvature metrics: find conditions on the dimension, surgery sphere, framing, embedding, and any necessary manifold hypotheses that ensure \[ \mathcal R_{>0}(M)=\varnothing \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal R_{>0}(M_\iota)=\varnothing \] for every manifold and every surgery satisfying those conditions. Ordinary framed interior surgery gives one standard interpretation of the source question; the source does not specify the intended range of surgeries or manifold hypotheses. It therefore supplies a broad classification question rather than a single fully specified universal conjecture.

Short English statement

Determine which surgeries preserve the property that a smooth manifold admits no Riemannian metric with scalar curvature strictly positive everywhere. The source leaves the dimension, manifold class, metric completeness, boundary conditions, and permitted surgeries unspecified, so these require clarification before a definitive formulation can be fixed.
