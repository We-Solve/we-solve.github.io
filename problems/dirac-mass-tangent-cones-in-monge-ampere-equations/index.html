Dirac-Mass Tangent Cones in Monge–Ampère Equations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns singular convex solutions of the displayed equation \[ \det D^2u=1+\sum_{i\in I}a_i\delta_{p_i}, \] where $\delta_{p_i}$ denotes the unit Dirac measure at $p_i$. The dimension, domain, index set, coefficients, boundary conditions, and particular family of examples are not specified.

A standard conditional interpretation is the Aleksandrov Monge--Ampère equation. To describe this interpretation, let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be open and convex and let $u:\Omega\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be finite and convex. Define \[ \partial u(x)=\{q\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:u(y)\ge u(x)+q\cdot(y-x)\text{ for every }y\in\Omega\}, \] and, for Borel sets $E\subset\Omega$, define its Monge--Ampère measure by \[ \operatorname{MA}_u(E)=\mathcal L^n\!\left(\bigcup_{x\in E}\partial u(x)\right), \] where $\mathcal L^n$ is Lebesgue measure. In this interpretation the displayed equation means \[ \operatorname{MA}_u=\mathcal L^n\!\restriction_\Omega+\sum_{i\in I}a_i\delta_{p_i} \quad\text{as measures on }\Omega, \] provided the atomic expression defines a nonnegative locally finite measure. This replaces the scalar $1$ by Lebesgue measure and interprets the determinant in a measure sense. These are explicit interpretive conventions, not hypotheses or a solution concept confirmed by the supplied record.

For an interior point $p$ of such a convex function, a standard first-order tangent profile is \[ T_pu(v)=\lim_{t\downarrow0}\frac{u(p+tv)-u(p)}{t},\qquad v\in{\mathbb{R}}^n. \] Its graph $\{(v,T_pu(v)):v\in{\mathbb{R}}^n\}$ describes a graph tangent cone translated to the origin; the corresponding epigraph cone is $\{(v,s):s\ge T_pu(v)\}$. The source does not specify which convention, or which normalization of tangent cones, is intended.

Problem, with unresolved scope. For the source-intended singular convex Monge--Ampère examples satisfying the displayed equation, determine precise descriptions of the tangent cones at each point $p_i$ carrying a Dirac mass. The original statement additionally asks for analogous investigations when the obstacle graphs are not necessarily convex polytopes. The obstacles, their relation to $u$, and the admissible replacement class are not supplied, so this additional target cannot be formulated more precisely from the record. It must be retained even though it is absent from the clean statement. No universal quantification over all convex solutions, prescribed dimension, boundary value problem, or obstacle construction is justified by the supplied text.

Short English statement

Describe the tangent cones at the atomic singularities of the intended convex Monge--Ampère examples, and investigate the analogous question for obstacle graphs that need not be convex polytopes. The examples, solution concept, cone convention, and obstacle hypotheses require clarification. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
