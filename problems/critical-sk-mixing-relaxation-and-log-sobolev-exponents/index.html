Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For independent standard Gaussian $J_{ij}$, $i<j$, with $J_{ji}=J_{ij},J_{ii}=0$, use the zero-field SK measure \[ \pi_{n,\beta}^J(x)=Z^{-1}\exp\left(\frac\beta{\sqrt n}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j\right),\qquad x\in\{-1,1\}^n. \] Continuous heat-bath dynamics refreshes every site at rate one; a discrete step refreshes one uniformly selected site, including attempts that retain its spin. At $\beta=1$, let $Q_i$ be conditional expectation given all other spins, and define $\mathcal D_J(f)=\sum_i\pi[\operatorname{Var}(f\mid x_{-i})]$. Let $t_{\mathrm{mix}}(\varepsilon)$ and $k_{\mathrm{mix}}(\varepsilon)$ be worst-start total-variation mixing times for the respective clocks. Also put \[ R_J=\sup_{\operatorname{Var}_\pi f>0}\frac{\operatorname{Var}_\pi f}{\mathcal D_J(f)},\qquad C_{\mathrm{LS},J}=\sup_{\operatorname{Ent}_\pi(f^2)>0}\frac{\operatorname{Ent}_\pi(f^2)}{\mathcal D_J(f)}, \] where $\operatorname{Ent}_\pi(g)=\pi(g\log g)-\pi(g)\log\pi(g)$. For fixed $0<\varepsilon<1/2$, in disorder probability, \[ t_{\mathrm{mix}}(\varepsilon)=n^{2/3+o(1)},\quad k_{\mathrm{mix}}(\varepsilon)=n^{5/3+o(1)},\quad R_J=n^{2/3+o(1)},\quad C_{\mathrm{LS},J}=n^{2/3+o(1)}. \] Each exponent assertion means upper and lower bounds with every fixed positive exponent error on events of probability tending to one.
