Undetermined Coefficients in Universal Residual Polynomials

Definitions and mathematical statement

Source-level notation. The supplied record denotes by $R_n(u,c_1,c_2)$ the Kleiman--Piene universal residual polynomials. It does not specify their defining construction, coefficient ring, normalization, admissible monomials, or the meanings and domains of $u,c_1,c_2$. It also does not provide the proposed linear-system algorithm, its equations, its unknown coefficients, or its input data. Accordingly, this notation identifies the objects named by the source but does not constitute a complete definition.

Requested determination. For every integer $n\geq 20$, determine the coefficients of $R_n(u,c_1,c_2)$ that remain undetermined by the proposed linear-system algorithm, and explain the loss of full rank reported for that system.

Unresolved qualifications. A fully specified mathematical problem requires the definitions and normalization of $R_n$, the coefficient domain, and the exact linear systems for the stated range of $n$. The record does not specify which notion of full rank is intended, the expected rank, or whether the reported deficiency occurs uniformly for every $n\geq20$. These details cannot be reconstructed from the supplied fragment. The supplied dated assessment describes the coefficient computation as incomplete and states that later Kleiman--Piene work proves a relevant universality theorem under precise hypotheses; those hypotheses and the theorem's exact relationship to this computation are not supplied.

Short English statement

For integers $n\geq20$, determine the coefficients left unresolved by the cited algorithm for the Kleiman--Piene universal residual polynomials and explain its reported rank deficiency. The polynomial definitions and linear systems are missing, so this is a source-level target requiring further specification.
