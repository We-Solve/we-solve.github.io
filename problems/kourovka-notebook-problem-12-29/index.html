Kourovka Notebook Problem 12.29

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $G$ is locally finite if every subgroup generated by finitely many elements of $G$ is finite. Its complex group algebra is \[ {\mathbb{C}}[G]=\left\{\sum_{g\in G}a_g g : a_g\in{\mathbb{C}},\quad a_g=0\text{ for all but finitely many }g\right\}, \] with multiplication induced by the group operation and extended bilinearly. The augmentation homomorphism and augmentation ideal are \[ \varepsilon:{\mathbb{C}}[G]\longrightarrow{\mathbb{C}},\qquad \varepsilon\left(\sum_{g\in G}a_g g\right)=\sum_{g\in G}a_g, \qquad I(G)=\ker\varepsilon. \] Regard $I(G)$ as a ring with its inherited addition and multiplication. Here a simple ring means a nonzero ring whose only two-sided ideals are zero and the whole ring; a multiplicative identity is not required. A two-sided ideal means an additive subgroup closed under multiplication on either side by every ring element.

Classification problem. Classify, up to group isomorphism, all locally finite groups $G$ such that $I(G)$ is a simple ring. Equivalently, determine precisely which locally finite groups satisfy \[ I(G)\ne\{0\},\qquad \forall J\triangleleft I(G),\quad J=\{0\}\ \text{or}\ J=I(G), \] where $J\triangleleft I(G)$ denotes a two-sided ring ideal.

The source does not explicitly specify its convention for simplicity of rings without an identity or for the zero ring. The convention above excludes the trivial group, whose augmentation ideal is zero; this convention requires confirmation against the intended source formulation.

Short English statement

Classify the groups in which every finitely generated subgroup is finite and the augmentation ideal of the complex group algebra is a nonzero simple ring, allowing rings without an identity. The supplied registry labels this problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
