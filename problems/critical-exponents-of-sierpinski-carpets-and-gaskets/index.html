Math-3403

Critical Exponents of Sierpiński Carpets and Gaskets

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,d)$ be either the standard Sierpiński carpet, obtained by repeatedly deleting the middle open subsquare in each retained square, or the standard Sierpiński gasket, obtained from a triangle by retaining its three half-scale corner triangles, with the induced Euclidean metric.

A homeomorphism $F:(X,d)\to(Y,d_Y)$ is quasisymmetric if some homeomorphism $\eta:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ satisfies \[ \frac{d_Y(F(x),F(a))}{d_Y(F(x),F(b))} \le\eta\!\left(\frac{d(x,a)}{d(x,b)}\right) \] for all distinct $x,a,b$. The ordinary conformal dimension is \[ \operatorname{Cdim}(X,d)=\inf\{\dim_HY:Y\text{ is quasisymmetrically equivalent to }(X,d)\}. \] An Ahlfors-regular conformal-dimension variant restricts the admissible images to those carrying a measure with ball masses comparable to $r^Q$; the two conventions are not silently identified.

The source asks to determine conformal-dimension and $\ell^p$-cohomology critical-exponent behavior for both named spaces, especially its quantities \[ p(\mathcal J(X,d)). \] The extract does not define the associated object $\mathcal J(X,d)$, the particular cohomology degree, reduced versus unreduced cohomology, or whether the critical exponent records nonvanishing, separation of boundary points, or another threshold. Broadly, graph-based $\ell^p$ cohomology uses cocycles with $p$-summable values, modulo the specified coboundaries; the construction and exact threshold must be recovered from the report. No equality between a guessed cohomological threshold and a conformal dimension is asserted without those hypotheses, and endpoint behavior is part of the requested determination.

The literature assessment checked on 21 August 2026 reports conformal dimension one for the gasket and substantial bounds and analytic criteria for the carpet, whose exact conformal dimension it calls open. It does not provide the corresponding values of every $p(\mathcal J(X,d))$ variant or resolve their endpoints. Those are supplied dated partial-progress claims requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

Determine the conformal dimensions and associated $\ell^p$-cohomology thresholds for the standard carpet and gasket. The gasket value reported as one does not by itself determine the source's undefined cohomological critical exponents or their endpoints.
