Let $\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}$ denote the ring of adeles of $\mathbb Q$. The supplied statement uses a symbol $D$, its group of units $D^\times$, and the quotient \[ X=D^\times(\mathbb Q)\backslash D^\times(\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q}). \] The record does not define $D$ or specify hypotheses on it. If $f$ is a function on $X$, interpret $f(y)$, for $y\in D^\times(\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q})$, as its value at the coset represented by $y$. The source asks whether there exists $\delta>0$ such that, for $y\in D^\times(\mathbb A_{\mathbb Q})$ and every relevant Hecke--Laplace eigenfunction $f$ of eigenvalue $\lambda$, \[ f(y)=\int_X f(x)\,\mathrm d\mu(x) +O\!\left(\lambda^{1/4-\delta}\right). \] The single existential quantifier for $\delta$ precedes the quantifiers over $y$ and $f$. Short English statement

The supplied question asks for a fixed positive power saving in the difference between a Hecke--Laplace eigenfunction's value at an adelic point and its integral over the displayed quotient. The algebra $D$, eigenfunction normalization, operators, measure, convergence conditions, and error-term uniformity are unspecified, so the intended conjecture requires clarification.
