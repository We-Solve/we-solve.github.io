Math-7628

Shafarevich Freeness Conjecture over Qab

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra pro Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}}$ be the union, in $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, of all finite abelian extensions of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The target is \[ \operatorname{Gal}(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}/{\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}})\cong\widehat F_\omega \] as profinite groups, where $\widehat F_\omega$ denotes the free profinite group on a countably infinite basis converging to the identity. Its universal property extends maps from that basis into any profinite group, when the images converge to the identity, uniquely to continuous homomorphisms. This topological basis convention matters for infinite rank. The claim is not freeness as an abstract discrete group or merely freeness of every finite quotient.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

After adjoining every abelian extension of the rationals, is the remaining absolute Galois group free profinite of countably infinite rank?
