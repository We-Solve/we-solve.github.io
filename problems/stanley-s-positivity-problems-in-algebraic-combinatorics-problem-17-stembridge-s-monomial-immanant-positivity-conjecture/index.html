Stanley's Positivity Problems in Algebraic Combinatorics — Problem 17: Stembridge's monomial immanant positivity conjecture for totally nonnegative matrices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $\lambda\vdash n$ mean that $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_\ell)$ is a weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers with sum $n$. Let $S_n$ be the symmetric group on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. For $w\in S_n$, write $\rho(w)$ for the partition consisting of its cycle lengths. In commuting indeterminates $x_1,x_2,\ldots$, put \[ p_r=\sum_{i\geq 1}x_i^r,\qquad p_\mu=\prod_{j=1}^{\ell(\mu)}p_{\mu_j}. \] Let $m_\lambda$ be the monomial symmetric function: the sum of all distinct monomials whose positive exponents, arranged in decreasing order, are the parts of $\lambda$.

A class function on $S_n$ is a function constant on conjugacy classes. Its Frobenius characteristic is \[ \operatorname{ch}(f)=\frac{1}{n!}\sum_{w\in S_n}f(w)p_{\rho(w)}. \] Define $\phi^\lambda$ to be the unique class function satisfying $\operatorname{ch}(\phi^\lambda)=m_\lambda$. For a real matrix $A=(a_{ij})_{1\leq i,j\leq n}$, define \[ \operatorname{Imm}_{\phi^\lambda}(A) =\sum_{w\in S_n}\phi^\lambda(w)\prod_{i=1}^{n}a_{i,w(i)}. \] The matrix $A$ is totally nonnegative if, for every $1\leq k\leq n$ and all increasing index sequences $i_1<\cdots<i_k$ and $j_1<\cdots<j_k$, \[ \det\left(a_{i_r,j_s}\right)_{r,s=1}^{k}\geq 0. \] Zero minors and singular matrices are allowed.

Conjecture (attributed in the supplied record to John R. Stembridge; Stanley's Problem 17). For every integer $n\geq1$, every partition $\lambda\vdash n$, and every totally nonnegative matrix $A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n\times n}$, \[ \operatorname{Imm}_{\phi^\lambda}(A)\geq0. \] The supplied registry labels this conjecture open; that label is supplied curation and is not an independently verified assessment of its current status.

Short English statement

Stembridge's conjecture asks whether every immanant associated with a monomial symmetric function is nonnegative on every real totally nonnegative square matrix. The claim includes matrices with zero minors. It is listed as Problem 17 in Stanley's 1999 collection and marked open in the supplied registry.
