Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $W_{{\mathbb{Q}}_p}$ denote the Weil group of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$. The supplied excerpt considers a group $G$, a torus $T$, a toral parameter $\phi_T$, and a parameter $\phi$ induced from $\phi_T$. It does not specify the full hypotheses on these objects, the coefficient field, or the precise parameter maps. These data must therefore remain unresolved rather than being assigned additional hypotheses.

For each coroot $\alpha$ occurring in the source's convention, write $\chi_\alpha=\alpha\circ\phi_T$ for the resulting character of $W_{{\mathbb{Q}}_p}$. The source defines $\phi_T$ to be generic when \[ \forall\alpha,\qquad \chi_\alpha\not\cong\mathbf{1}\quad\text{and}\quad\chi_\alpha\not\cong|\cdot|, \] where $\mathbf{1}$ is the trivial character and $|\cdot|$ is the norm character in the source's normalization. The source also states the equivalent condition \[ \forall\alpha,\qquad R\Gamma(W_{{\mathbb{Q}}_p},\chi_\alpha)\simeq 0. \] The relevant coroot convention, coefficient category, and norm normalization are not specified in the excerpt; the asserted equivalence is retained as a source assertion subject to those missing conventions.

Intended assertion, recoverable only schematically. For a parameter $\phi$ induced from a generic toral parameter $\phi_T$, the proposed sheaf $S_\phi$ should be a Hecke eigensheaf with eigenvalue $\phi$. Here the eigensheaf condition requires compatible Hecke eigenisomorphisms, but neither the relevant Hecke functors nor the construction of $S_\phi$ is supplied. Thus this sentence does not specify a complete mathematical conjecture.

The excerpt explicitly calls the unrestricted assertion naive: representations of non-quasi-split groups can have semisimplified parameters factoring through a maximal torus and are expected to contribute to eigensheaf stalks. It gives the trivial representation of the units of a quaternion division algebra as an example, although the extracted algebra notation is damaged. It further asserts that genericity excludes $\phi$ from being the semisimplification of a parameter with nontrivial monodromy. Its restriction on possible eigensheaf stalks additionally assumes expected properties of the Fargues--Scholze correspondence and refers to an earlier stalk description absent from the excerpt.

Conditional result reported by the source. The excerpt attributes to its reference [6, Theorem 1.17] the eigensheaf assertion for generic toral parameters, assuming certain properties of the Fargues--Scholze local Langlands correspondence and possibly additional constraints on $\phi_T$. Those properties and constraints are not enumerated here, so a precise conditional theorem cannot be reconstructed.

The discussion uses the moduli space $\operatorname{Bun}_B$ of parabolic $B$-structures on $G$-bundles, a Drinfeld compactification, and Eisenstein constructions applied to the toral eigensheaf $S_{\phi_T}$. It distinguishes pushforward from the open space from pushforward from its compactification, the latter requiring an intersection-cohomology kernel. The extraction loses the distinguishing notation and ends mid-sentence. Consequently it does not determine which construction defines $S_\phi$, or a standalone unresolved assertion about it. The registry explicitly marks the statement as unrecoverable; this draft preserves that limitation.

Short English statement

The intended claim is that a sheaf associated with a generic toral parameter is a Hecke eigensheaf for the induced parameter. The excerpt rejects an unrestricted naive formulation and reports a conditional theorem, but omits the sheaf construction and essential hypotheses. It therefore does not support a precise standalone open problem without recovery and mathematical review of the source.
