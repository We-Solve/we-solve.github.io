Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For the three-dimensional periodic hard-sphere Bose gas of exclusion distance $a>0$, let $\mathcal G_{N,L,a}$ be all trace-one states supported on the lowest eigenspace, and $n_{\Gamma,L}(k)$ the occupation of $L^{-3/2}e^{ik\cdot x}$. Define \[ g(t)=\frac{|t|^2+1}{2\sqrt{|t|^4+2|t|^2}}-\frac12,\qquad d\nu_{\mathrm{Bog}}(t)=\frac{(8\pi)^{3/2}}{(2\pi)^3}g(t)\,dt. \] For each bounded continuous real $f$ and $\varepsilon>0$, the source gives $\rho_0(a,f,\varepsilon)>0$. If $0<\rho<\rho_0$, $\eta=\rho a^3$, and $N_j,L_j\to\infty$, $N_j/L_j^3\to\rho$, then \[ \limsup_j\sup_{\Gamma\in\mathcal G_{N_j,L_j,a}} \left|\frac{\sum_{k\in(2\pi/L_j)\mathbb Z^3,\ k\ne0} f(k/\sqrt{8\pi\rho a})n_{\Gamma,L_j}(k)}{N_j\sqrt\eta} -\int f\,d\nu_{\mathrm{Bog}}\right|\le\varepsilon. \] The measure has total mass $8/(3\sqrt\pi)$.
