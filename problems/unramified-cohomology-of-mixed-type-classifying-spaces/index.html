Math-4360

Unramified Cohomology of Mixed-Type Classifying Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and let $G$ be a semisimple algebraic group over $k$. The source imposes no restriction on $\operatorname{char}(k)$. The Dynkin type of $G$ records the irreducible components of its root system. We interpret "mixed Dynkin types" provisionally as requiring at least two distinct irreducible Dynkin types among these components; the source does not explicitly define this term or restrict the central isogeny type of $G$.

The notation $BG$ denotes the classifying space of $G$. The source writes $F(BG)$, but does not define $F$, its relationship to $k$, or the construction of the classifying-space function field. One usual interpretation uses the invariant field $k(V)^G$ of a generically free linear representation $V$ of $G$; this interpretation is not specified in the record.

For a field extension $K/k$, once a coefficient object $M$, a cohomology theory, and residue maps have been fixed, unramified degree-three cohomology consists of the classes in $H^3(K,M)$ whose residues vanish at the specified valuations. For example, under the convention using all discrete rank-one valuations trivial on $k$, write \[ \mathcal V(K/k)=\{v: v\text{ is a discrete rank-one valuation of }K\text{ trivial on }k\}. \] When the relevant residue maps $\partial_v$ are defined, this convention gives \[ H^3_{\mathrm{nr}}(K/k;M) =\bigcap_{v\in\mathcal V(K/k)}\ker\!\left(\partial_v:H^3(K,M)\longrightarrow\text{the residue group at }v\right). \] This explains the terminology without selecting coefficients or a residue theory for the source question; these choices, including their meaning in positive characteristic, remain unresolved.

The supplied question is whether, for every algebraically closed field $k$ and every semisimple $k$-group $G$ of mixed Dynkin types, \[ H_{\mathrm{nr}}^3(F(BG))=0. \] Here triviality means that every unramified degree-three class is zero. This is a statement-only formulation of the supplied target. It is not a fully specified cohomological assertion until the meaning of $F(BG)$, the coefficients, the residue convention, and the intended mixed types are fixed.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every unramified degree-three cohomology class of the classifying-space function field of a semisimple group with mixed Dynkin types vanishes over an algebraically closed field. The source leaves the function-field construction, coefficients, and characteristic-dependent conventions unspecified. Its open-status label is supplied curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
