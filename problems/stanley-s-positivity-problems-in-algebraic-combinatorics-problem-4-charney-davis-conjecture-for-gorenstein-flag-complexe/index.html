Stanley's Positivity Problems in Algebraic Combinatorics — Problem 4: Charney-Davis conjecture for Gorenstein* flag complexes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $e\geq 1$ be an integer. Let $\Delta$ be a finite abstract simplicial complex of dimension $2e-1$, so its largest faces have $2e$ vertices. A minimal non-face is a set of vertices that is not a face although every proper subset is a face. The complex is flag if every minimal non-face has exactly two vertices.

To make the coefficient convention explicit, fix a field $k$. For a face $\sigma\in\Delta$, define \[ \operatorname{lk}_{\Delta}(\sigma)=\{\tau\in\Delta:\tau\cap\sigma=\varnothing,\ \tau\cup\sigma\in\Delta\}. \] Use the standard homological formulation of Gorenstein* over $k$: the complex is pure of dimension $2e-1$, and for every face $\sigma$, including the empty face, \[ \widetilde H_i(\operatorname{lk}_{\Delta}(\sigma);k)\cong \begin{cases} k,&i=2e-1-|\sigma|,\\ 0,&i\neq 2e-1-|\sigma|. \end{cases} \] Here reduced simplicial homology includes degree $-1$, with $\widetilde H_{-1}(\{\varnothing\};k)\cong k$.

Let $f_{j-1}$ count the faces with $j$ vertices, with $f_{-1}=1$. Define the $h$-vector by \[ h_{\Delta}(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{2e}h_i t^i =\sum_{j=0}^{2e}f_{j-1}t^j(1-t)^{2e-j}. \] The proposed assertion is that every such Gorenstein* flag complex satisfies \[ (-1)^e h_{\Delta}(-1) =(-1)^e\sum_{i=0}^{2e}(-1)^i h_i\geq 0. \] The supplied record does not specify a coefficient field or its quantification. The formulation above is relative to a fixed field $k$; the intended field scope requires confirmation. Finiteness is the conventional assumption underlying the supplied face-counting $h$-vector. The record also leaves the range of $e$ implicit; the statement above covers positive odd dimensions. If $e=0$ and the dimension $-1$ complex $\{\varnothing\}$ is admitted, its $h$-polynomial is $1$, and the displayed inequality holds.

Short English statement

The Charney--Davis conjecture in Stanley's Gorenstein* flag formulation asks whether the alternating sum of the $h$-vector has sign $(-1)^e$ or is zero in dimension $2e-1$. The coefficient-field scope is unspecified in the supplied record and requires review.
