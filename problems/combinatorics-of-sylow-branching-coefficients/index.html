Combinatorics of Sylow Branching Coefficients

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks for a combinatorial description of the map \[ Z^{\mathbf 1_{P_n}}:\mathcal P(n)\longrightarrow\mathbb N_0, \qquad \lambda\longmapsto Z^{\mathbf 1_{P_n}}_\lambda, \] where $\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. The record does not explicitly define $P_n$, identify its ambient group, or specify the relevant prime or range of $n$. Consequently, the following gives a conditional interpretation of the notation, rather than an unqualified completion of the missing hypotheses.

Under the usual symmetric-group interpretation, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, let $p$ be a prime, let $S_n$ be the symmetric group on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and let $P_n\leq S_n$ be a Sylow $p$-subgroup. Define \[ \mathcal P(n)=\left\{(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_\ell):\ell\geq 1,\ \lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_\ell\geq 1,\ \sum_{i=1}^{\ell}\lambda_i=n\right\}. \] For $\lambda\in\mathcal P(n)$, let $V^\lambda$ be the irreducible complex representation of $S_n$ indexed by $\lambda$, with character $\chi^\lambda$. Let $\mathbf 1_{P_n}$ denote the trivial character of $P_n$. The corresponding Sylow branching coefficient is \[ Z^{\mathbf 1_{P_n}}_\lambda =\left\langle\operatorname{Res}^{S_n}_{P_n}\chi^\lambda,\mathbf 1_{P_n}\right\rangle_{P_n} =\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}(V^\lambda)^{P_n}, \] where \[ \langle\alpha,\beta\rangle_{P_n} =\frac{1}{|P_n|}\sum_{g\in P_n}\alpha(g)\overline{\beta(g)}, \qquad (V^\lambda)^{P_n}=\{v\in V^\lambda:gv=v\text{ for every }g\in P_n\}. \] Thus, under this interpretation, the requested task is to describe the full multiplicity map combinatorially: give a combinatorial rule determining $Z^{\mathbf 1_{P_n}}_\lambda$ for every $\lambda\in\mathcal P(n)$, for the intended values of $n$ and the intended Sylow prime. The source does not specify a required form of combinatorial rule. The choice or quantification of $p$, the intended range of $n$, and confirmation of this interpretation remain unresolved; no particular prime, extension to $n=0$, or assertion that all primes are intended is imposed here.

Short English statement

Describe combinatorially the multiplicity of the trivial representation of $P_n$ in each irreducible representation indexed by a partition of $n$. Under the conventional symmetric-group interpretation, these are dimensions of $P_n$-fixed subspaces. The supplied record leaves the Sylow prime, the range of $n$, and the explicit ambient-group convention unspecified.
