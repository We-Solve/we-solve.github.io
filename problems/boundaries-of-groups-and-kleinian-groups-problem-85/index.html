Math-13047

Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 85

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Take the finite presentation appearing in the source, \[ G=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_k\mid R_1,\ldots,R_m\rangle, \qquad \ell_j=|R_j|, \] where word length counts letters in the generators and their inverses. For a representation $\rho:G\to\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$, with the usual curvature-$-1$ hyperbolic metric $d$, define \[ B_n([\rho])=\inf_{x\in\mathbb H^n}\max_{1\le i\le k} d(x,\rho(g_i)x). \] Conjugating $\rho$ by a hyperbolic isometry leaves this value unchanged, explaining the notation $[\rho]$. No attainment of the infimum is assumed.

The source asks for a constructive proof of an "above theorem", more precisely an explicit finite constant depending only on the displayed presentation parameters, \[ C=C(n,k,m,\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_m),\qquad \forall[\rho]\in D_n(G):\quad B_n([\rho])\le C. \] The bound is independent of the individual representation and uses the chosen generating set. "Explicit" should in particular permit evaluation from the allowed finite data, rather than merely asserting existence of an unspecified supremum.

The referenced theorem is absent, as are its hypotheses on $G$ and the definition of $D_n(G)$. Thus the admissible representations and group class cannot be inferred: $D_n(G)$ is not silently replaced by all representations or even by all discrete faithful representations. The missing restrictions are essential to a boundedness claim. The word-length convention and range of dimensions also need confirmation in the original source.

The summary labels the problem open and mentions domains of discontinuity, stable translation, Margulis invariants, and effective linearization, without giving a precise connection to the displayed bound or a theorem citation. These descriptions do not restore the missing theorem. The historical problem label and later open assessment remain unverified source information.

Short English statement

Give a computable uniform bound, in terms of dimension and presentation sizes, for the least possible maximal displacement of the generators over the intended deformation space. The preceding theorem and the definition of that deformation space are missing, so the admissible groups and representations cannot yet be fixed.
