Polynomial deterministic two-way simulation of one-way NFAs

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 129; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that one-way relational liveness has small nondeterministic recognizers but exponentially large deterministic two-way recognizers, uniformly over the growing finite alphabets.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For each $h\ge2$, use all binary relations on $[h]$ as letters and set \[ \operatorname{OWL}_h=\{R_1\cdots R_k:R_1\cdots R_k\ne\varnothing\}. \] Composition follows paths from left to right; the identity empty product makes the empty word accepted. The language is claimed recognizable by an automaton using nondeterministic transitions, $h+3$ states and only right or stay moves. By contrast, every deterministic two-way automaton recognizing it with $s$ states obeys \[ 4(s+2)^2\ge2^{\lfloor(h-2)/31\rfloor}. \] The target may use stay moves, partial transitions and nonaccepting infinite runs. If an accepting initial configuration counts as success, the sharper $s+1$ replaces $s+2$. The alphabet is not fixed independently of $h$.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 129; 2026-09-25), deterministic computations are represented by local matching diagrams and a sink tour. Composition passes to relation-monoid corners, where lifted permutations and rank-loss amplification bound the size of any image that separates live and empty products.
