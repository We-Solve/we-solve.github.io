Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.59

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ act on a set $\Omega$ with $|\Omega|\geq 3$; no finiteness assumption is imposed. For distinct points $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_k\in\Omega$, write \[ G_{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_k}=\{g\in G:g\alpha_i=\alpha_i\text{ for every }i\}. \] Here stabilizers are interpreted as pointwise stabilizers. Assume that the action is triply transitive: for any two ordered triples of pairwise distinct points, there is an element of $G$ carrying the first triple to the second. Assume also that, for every pair of distinct points $\alpha,\beta$, \[ \{g\in G_{\alpha,\beta}:g^2=1\}=\{1\}, \] and that $G_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}=\{1\}$ for every triple of pairwise distinct points. Thus the action is sharply triply transitive and faithful.

For a field $P$, let \[ \operatorname{PGL}_2(P)=\operatorname{GL}_2(P)/\{\lambda I_2:\lambda\in P^\times\}. \] Its natural action on $\mathbb P^1(P)=P\cup\{\infty\}$ is the action on one-dimensional subspaces of $P^2$, identifying $x\in P$ with $[x:1]$ and $\infty$ with $[1:0]$.

Question (V. D. Mazurov, Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.59). Must there exist a field $P$ of characteristic $2$, a group isomorphism $\varphi:G\to\operatorname{PGL}_2(P)$, and a bijection $f:\Omega\to\mathbb P^1(P)$ such that \[ f(g\alpha)=\varphi(g)f(\alpha) \qquad(g\in G,\ \alpha\in\Omega)? \] This interprets the source's word "similar" as isomorphism of permutation actions. The source does not explicitly specify these stabilizer and similarity conventions, so they require confirmation against the original text.

The supplied statement reports an affirmative answer under the additional hypothesis that a two-point stabilizer is periodic, meaning that every element of that stabilizer has finite order. No uniform bound on these orders is stipulated. This special-case assertion is retained as supplied source information, without independent verification.

Short English statement

Must every sharply triply transitive permutation group whose pointwise two-point stabilizers contain no involutions be permutation-isomorphic to the natural action of $\operatorname{PGL}_2(P)$ for some field $P$ of characteristic $2$? The supplied source reports that this holds when a two-point stabilizer is periodic.
