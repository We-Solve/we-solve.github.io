Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every metric space with at least k+1 points, $k\geq 2$ and initial labeled server tuple, does there exist one randomized online policy whose expected cost on every oblivious finite request sequence is $\leq C(\log (k+1))^{2}OPT+B$, with a metric-independent absolute C and a finite B independent of sequence and horizon? Can B be zero when initial positions are distinct?
