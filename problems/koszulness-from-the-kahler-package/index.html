Koszulness from the Kähler Package

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: Is every Artinian quadratic Gorenstein algebra with the Kähler package Koszul? The coefficient field and the precise meaning of the Kähler package are not specified in the supplied excerpt. The definitions below make the usual algebraic framework explicit and describe a possible real interpretation of the package; that interpretation requires confirmation against the source context.

Let $k$ be a field and let $A=\bigoplus_{j\geq 0}A_j$ be a commutative standard graded $k$-algebra: $A_0=k$, $A_1$ is finite dimensional, and $A$ is generated by $A_1$. Write $A_+=\bigoplus_{j>0}A_j$. In this framework, $A$ is Artinian if it is finite dimensional over $k$, and it is quadratic if the kernel of the natural surjection $\operatorname{Sym}_k(A_1)\twoheadrightarrow A$ is generated by homogeneous elements of degree two. It is Gorenstein if its socle \[ \operatorname{Soc}(A)=\{a\in A:A_+a=0\} \] is one dimensional over $k$. If $d=\max\{j:A_j\ne0\}$, this socle is $A_d$, and a choice of isomorphism $\deg:A_d\to k$ gives perfect multiplication pairings $A_i\times A_{d-i}\to k$, $(a,b)\mapsto\deg(ab)$.

The algebra $A$ is Koszul if the graded $A$-module $k=A/A_+$ has a linear minimal graded free resolution, equivalently if \[ \operatorname{Tor}^{A}_{p}(k,k)_q=0 \qquad\text{for all }p,q\geq0\text{ with }q\ne p. \] Here $p$ is homological degree and $q$ is internal degree.

For clarity, one customary real, single-class interpretation of the Kähler package is the following additional structure. Take $k={\mathbb{R}}$, a degree isomorphism $\deg:A_d\to{\mathbb{R}}$, and an element $\ell\in A_1$. Besides the perfect pairings above (Poincaré duality), require, for every integer $0\leq i\leq\lfloor d/2\rfloor$, the hard Lefschetz isomorphism \[ \ell^{d-2i}:A_i\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }A_{d-i} \] and the Hodge--Riemann positivity condition that \[ Q_{\ell,i}(a,b)=(-1)^i\deg\left(a\ell^{d-2i}b\right) \] is positive definite on \[ P^i_{\ell}=\ker\left(\ell^{d-2i+1}:A_i\longrightarrow A_{d-i+1}\right), \] where graded pieces above $d$ are zero. Under this particular interpretation, the question becomes whether every commutative standard graded Artinian quadratic Gorenstein ${\mathbb{R}}$-algebra admitting such $\deg$ and $\ell$ is Koszul.

Unresolved qualification. The excerpt does not establish that this real, single-class interpretation is the intended one. It may instead presuppose another coefficient field followed by extension to ${\mathbb{R}}$, a specified cone of Lefschetz classes, or further compatibility conditions. Consequently, the original universal question is retained with its source-dependent phrase "with the Kähler package"; its exact domain and package hypotheses require human review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Kähler package forces every Artinian quadratic Gorenstein algebra in the intended class to be Koszul, meaning that its residue field has a linear graded free resolution. The supplied excerpt leaves the coefficient field and the precise package hypotheses unspecified.
