Math-6574

Rationality and Computation of Free-Group Rotation Suprema

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F=\langle a,b\rangle$ be the free group of rank two. The supplied record does not specify the target of the representations or the choice of lifts. To make the notation explicit, consider the conventional lifted circle-action interpretation \[ \widetilde{\operatorname{Homeo}}^{+}(S^{1}) =\{f:{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{R}}\mid f\text{ is an increasing homeomorphism and }f(x+1)=f(x)+1\text{ for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}\}, \qquad S^{1}={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}. \] For an element $f$ of this group, define its lifted rotation number, also called its translation number, by \[ \tau(f)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f^{n}(x)-x}{n}, \] where the limit is independent of $x\in{\mathbb{R}}$. Its reduction modulo ${\mathbb{Z}}$ is the rotation number of the induced circle homeomorphism. Under this interpretation, for $w\in F$ and $r,s\in{\mathbb{R}}$, set \[ \mathcal H(r,s)=\left\{\rho:F\longrightarrow\widetilde{\operatorname{Homeo}}^{+}(S^{1})\ \middle|\ \rho\text{ is a group homomorphism},\ \tau(\rho(a))=r,\ \tau(\rho(b))=s\right\}, \] \[ R(w,r,s)=\sup_{\rho\in\mathcal H(r,s)}\tau(\rho(w)). \] This is an explicit conventional interpretation, not a representation target independently confirmed from the cited report.

The supplied question asks whether \[ \text{for every }w\in F\text{ and every }r,s\in{\mathbb{R}},\qquad R(w,r,s)\in{\mathbb{Q}}, \] and asks for a practical method to compute or approximate $R(w,r,s)$.

Unresolved formulation qualification. Under the interpretation above, the generator constraints themselves give $R(a,r,s)=r$ and $R(b,r,s)=s$. Thus the universal rationality clause, which includes generator words and arbitrary irrational inputs, is incompatible with these definitions. The intended restrictions on the words or inputs, or the intended alternative rationality assertion, must be checked against the original source. No replacement conjecture is selected here. The computational request also leaves unspecified how arbitrary real inputs are represented, what constitutes a practical method, and what approximation error or guarantee is required.

Short English statement

The supplied problem concerns the largest lifted rotation number obtainable for a word in two free generators when their lifted rotation numbers are prescribed. It asks about rationality and practical computation or approximation. The representation target and lift conventions require confirmation, and the stated rationality claim for all real inputs conflicts with the generator cases under the conventional interpretation.
