Scalar Curvature Question [?76]: Stability of Periodic Slabs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The main conjecture, "Stability of Periodic Slabs," says that the only ${\mathbb{Z}}^{n-3}$-invariant mean-convex domains in ${\mathbb{R}}^n$ with disconnected boundaries are slabs between parallel hyperplanes. In the usual translation-periodic reading, $n\ge3$ and a rank-$(n-3)$ lattice of translations preserves a domain $Y$; the proposed conclusion is \[ Y=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:a<\langle x,v\rangle<b\} \] for a unit vector $v$ and real $a<b$, with the appropriate boundary convention. The source does not specify the periodic action, boundary regularity, or sign and non-strict/strict meaning of mean convexity. Translation periodicity is an explicit conventional reading, not a supplied definition.

The passage then discusses a different class of "thick" mean-convex-domain constructions. It considers a complete Riemannian $n$-manifold $X$ satisfying three conditions:

  1. $X$ is connected at infinity; the precise end convention is not stated.
  2. Its boundary at infinity has infinite $(n-1)$-volume in the source's stated sense: every proper continuous $f:X\to{\mathbb{R}}_+$ satisfies \[ \limsup_{t\to\infty}\operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(f^{-1}(t))=\infty. \] The measure of level sets of arbitrary continuous functions needs an explicit convention.
  3. $X$ is locally "$(n-2)$-thick": there exist $\varepsilon>0$, $\alpha>1$, and $c>0$ such that every $(n-2)$-cycle $B$ with \[ \operatorname{diam}(B)\le\varepsilon,\qquad \operatorname{vol}_{n-2}(B)\le\varepsilon^{n-2} \] bounds an $(n-1)$-chain $C$ satisfying \[ \partial C=B,\qquad \operatorname{vol}_{n-1}(C)\le c\left(\operatorname{vol}_{n-2}(B)\right)^\alpha. \] Cycle coefficients, chain category, and volume definitions are not supplied.
Under these three conditions the source asserts that every compact $Y_0\subset X$ is contained in a smooth compact mean-convex domain $Y_1\subset X$. It contrasts these thick domains with thin/narrow ones surrounding codimension-two subsets. This construction statement is retained as adjacent context, not a proof of the periodic-slab conjecture.

The source says that manifolds uniformly bi-Lipschitz homeomorphic to ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, or to "any Riemannian homogeneous space," satisfy all three conditions. The latter blanket phrase needs qualifications: completeness and homogeneity alone do not specify noncompactness or connectedness at infinity, and compact homogeneous spaces do not have the stated unbounded-level-set property. No intended restriction is guessed. The extraction ends at "Sketch of the," so the asserted construction's proof is absent.

Short English statement

Must a periodic mean-convex Euclidean domain with disconnected boundary be a slab? The source also includes a separate compact mean-convex-enclosure assertion under end, level-set-area, and local filling assumptions. Both statements are retained with their distinct hypotheses.

The supplied summary reports no resolution of periodic-slab rigidity and gives only general mean-curvature/topology context. The OPEN-TRIAGE label and 2017 passage are retained with current status marked for review. The adjacent homogeneous-space claim and truncated proof do not independently verify either statement.
