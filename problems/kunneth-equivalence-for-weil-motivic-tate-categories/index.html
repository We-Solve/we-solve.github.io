Künneth Equivalence for Weil Motivic Tate Categories

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $X_1,X_2$ be algebraic varieties over the finite field ${\mathbb{F}}_p$. Write $X_i^{\mathrm{Weil}}$ for the Weil object associated with $X_i$, and $\operatorname{DTM}(Y)$ for the Weil-motivic Tate category attached to an admissible object $Y$, using the source's intended conventions. The supplied record does not define these constructions, their coefficients, or the categorical framework for the relative tensor product; consequently these symbols cannot be given complete definitions from the supplied evidence alone.

The intended question is whether, for every such pair $X_1,X_2$, exterior product induces an equivalence \[ \boxtimes:\operatorname{DTM}(X_1^{\mathrm{Weil}}) \otimes_{\operatorname{DTM}({\mathbb{F}}_p)} \operatorname{DTM}(X_2^{\mathrm{Weil}}) \longrightarrow \operatorname{DTM}\!\left(X_1^{\mathrm{Weil}}\times_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}X_2^{\mathrm{Weil}}\right). \] Here the tensor product is intended to be relative to the displayed base category $\operatorname{DTM}({\mathbb{F}}_p)$. The necessary module structures, the meaning of the fiber product of Weil objects, and the existence and precise construction of this functor require the source's missing conventions. The target is retained exactly as supplied; no identification with $(X_1\times_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}X_2)^{\mathrm{Weil}}$ is assumed.

As background, the supplied excerpt labels an analogous exterior-product equivalence for constructible $\ell$-adic sheaves as Theorem 2, attributed to reference [HRS20]. This is supplied background attribution, not an independently verified theorem here; its coefficient conventions and precise hypotheses are absent. It does not resolve the displayed motivic question.

Short English statement

For every pair of algebraic varieties over ${\mathbb{F}}_p$, does exterior product identify the relative tensor product of their Weil-motivic Tate categories with the category on the displayed fiber product of Weil objects? The supplied record labels this question open, but essential categorical definitions and conventions remain unspecified.
