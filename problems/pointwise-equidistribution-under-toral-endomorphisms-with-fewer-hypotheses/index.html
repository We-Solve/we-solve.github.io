Math-4543

Pointwise Equidistribution Under Toral Endomorphisms with Fewer Hypotheses

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\geq 1$, let $\mathbb T^d={\mathbb{R}}^d/{\mathbb{Z}}^d$, and let $A,B\in M_d({\mathbb{Z}})$ satisfy $\det A\neq 0$ and $\det B\neq 0$. They induce toral endomorphisms $T_A(x)=Ax\pmod{{\mathbb{Z}}^d}$ and $T_B(x)=Bx\pmod{{\mathbb{Z}}^d}$. Let $\mu$ be a Borel probability measure on $\mathbb T^d$ that is $T_A$-invariant and ergodic, with positive measure-theoretic entropy $h_\mu(T_A)>0$.

Interpret the source's irreducibility requirement as \[ \det(tI_d-B^n)\text{ is irreducible in }{\mathbb{Q}}[t] \qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq 1. \] The source also requires that no power of $B$ be conjugate to a power of $A$. Under the positive-power interpretation, this excludes conjugacy between $B^n$ and $A^m$ for every $m,n\geq 1$. The source does not specify the field or group in which conjugacy is taken, so this hypothesis remains unresolved.

For a Borel probability measure $\nu$ on $\mathbb T^d$, define its set of points generic under $T_B$ by \[ G_B(\nu)=\left\{x\in\mathbb T^d: \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}f(T_B^k x) =\int_{\mathbb T^d}f\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\nu \text{ for every }f\in C(\mathbb T^d,{\mathbb{R}})\right\}. \] Question, with unresolved source qualifications. Under the preceding assumptions, must $\mu(G_B(\nu))=1$ hold for the intended equidistribution measure $\nu$, without the additional spectral and eigenspace hypotheses referred to in the source as $(3a)$--$(3c)$?

The supplied record neither specifies $\nu$ nor reproduces $(3a)$--$(3c)$. If equidistribution is intended with respect to normalized Haar probability measure $m_d$ on $\mathbb T^d$, the proposed conclusion is precisely \[ \text{for }\mu\text{-almost every }x,\quad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}f(B^k x) =\int_{\mathbb T^d}f\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m_d \quad\text{for every }f\in C(\mathbb T^d,{\mathbb{R}}). \] This Haar formulation is conditional on confirming the intended reference measure. The missing hypotheses and unspecified conjugacy relation prevent a fully determined formulation from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

The question asks whether positive entropy for an ergodic $A$-invariant measure, irreducibility of the characteristic polynomials of all positive powers of $B$, and the stated exclusion of conjugate powers suffice for almost-everywhere equidistribution under $B$. The reference measure, the meaning of conjugacy, and the omitted conditions $(3a)$--$(3c)$ require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
