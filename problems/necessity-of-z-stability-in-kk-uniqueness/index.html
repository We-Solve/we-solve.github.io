Math-3362

Necessity of Z-Stability in KK-Uniqueness

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C,D,E$ be separable $C^*$-algebras, let $D\triangleleft E$ be a closed two-sided ideal, and let $q:E\to E/D$ be the quotient map. Let $\Phi,\Psi:C\to E$ be $*$-homomorphisms agreeing modulo $D$, meaning \[ q\circ\Phi=q\circ\Psi, \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad \Phi(c)-\Psi(c)\in D\quad(c\in C). \] Write $[\Phi,\Psi]_{KK}\in KK(C,D)$ for the associated relative Kasparov class, as in the supplied source. Assume that both maps are absorbing relative to $D$. The supplied excerpt does not define this absorption condition; its precise source-specific meaning must be recovered before this draft is a fully specified statement.

Let $\mathcal Z$ denote the Jiang--Su algebra. The condition of $\mathcal Z$-stability is \[ E\cong E\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z. \] Under the usual interpretation, asymptotic unitary equivalence through $\widetilde D$ means that there exists a norm-continuous path \[ u:[0,\infty)\longrightarrow\mathcal U(\widetilde D) \] such that \[ \lim_{t\to\infty}\|u(t)\Phi(c)u(t)^*-\Psi(c)\|=0 \qquad\text{for every }c\in C. \] Here $\mathcal U(\widetilde D)$ denotes the unitary group of the unitization used in the source, acting on the maps through its natural image in an ambient unitization of $E$. The excerpt does not specify the unitization convention or whether an initial-value condition such as $u(0)=1$ is intended. The displayed interpretation imposes no initial-value condition and requires confirmation against the full theorem.

Question. For every such $C,D,E,\Phi,\Psi$, with the source's relative absorption hypothesis retained, does the equivalence \[ [\Phi,\Psi]_{KK}=0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \Phi\text{ and }\Psi\text{ are asymptotically unitarily equivalent through }\widetilde D \] remain valid without assuming $E\cong E\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z$? The supplied excerpt asserts this equivalence under $\mathcal Z$-stability; the question concerns removing that hypothesis.

The surrounding excerpt also gives a schematic uniqueness assertion for maps $\phi,\psi$ and a classification proof sketch involving the UCT, adjustments to $\phi$, reindexations, and intertwining. It explicitly qualifies that sketch with informal omissions. Those maps and their hypotheses are not fully supplied, so that surrounding discussion is not formulated here as an additional theorem.

Short English statement

For separable $C^*$-algebras and two relatively absorbing $*$-homomorphisms that agree modulo a closed ideal $D$, ask whether vanishing of their relative $KK$-class is equivalent to asymptotic unitary equivalence through $\widetilde D$, without assuming $\mathcal Z$-stability of the ambient algebra. The source's absorption definition and path conventions still need confirmation.
