Comparison Maps in Motivic Cohomology of Central Simple Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote the unspecified base field and let $A$ be a central simple $k$-algebra: a finite-dimensional associative unital $k$-algebra with center $k$ and no nonzero proper two-sided ideals. Its reduced norm defines the algebraic group \[ \operatorname{SL}_1(A)=\ker\left(\operatorname{Nrd}_{A/k}:\operatorname{GL}_1(A)\longrightarrow\mathbb G_m\right). \] Generalized Severi--Brauer varieties associated with $A$ parametrize ideals of prescribed reduced dimension; the supplied record does not specify the dimension parameters or the ideal convention involved.

The source asks for a comparison of three motivic-cohomology maps associated with $A$, arising respectively from the étale Bloch--Lichtenbaum spectral sequence, generalized Severi--Brauer varieties, and $\operatorname{SL}_1(A)$. Under which hypotheses, and after which identifications of their sources and targets, do these constructions give the same map?

More explicitly, if the intended constructions and identifications produce homomorphisms \[ f_{\mathrm{et}},\ f_{\mathrm{SB}},\ f_{\mathrm{SL}_1}:M\longrightarrow N, \] the requested comparison is whether, and under what conditions, \[ f_{\mathrm{et}}=f_{\mathrm{SB}}=f_{\mathrm{SL}_1}. \] Here $M$, $N$, and the three homomorphisms are schematic placeholders, not objects specified by the supplied record. The record omits their definitions, cohomological degrees, coefficients, and comparison identifications, as well as any restrictions on $k$ or $A$. Consequently, it does not determine a precise equality of defined maps or a fully quantified conjecture. These missing data require source clarification and human mathematical review.

Short English statement

For a central simple algebra, compare the motivic-cohomology maps obtained from the étale Bloch--Lichtenbaum spectral sequence, generalized Severi--Brauer varieties, and $\operatorname{SL}_1(A)$. The supplied question asks when they agree, but does not define the maps or their common source and target, so a precise equality remains unresolved.
