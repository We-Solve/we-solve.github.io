Optimal Constructions for Cycle Saturation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For reference, under the usual convention of finite simple undirected graphs, let $C_k$ denote the cycle on $k\geq 3$ vertices. A graph $G$ is $C_k$-saturated if it contains no subgraph isomorphic to $C_k$, but adding any missing edge creates such a subgraph. A graph $G$ is $C_k$-semisaturated if adding any missing edge creates a new copy of $C_k$; existing copies are allowed. Copies here need not be induced. With these conventions, define \[ \operatorname{sat}(n,C_k)=\min\{|E(G)|:|V(G)|=n,\ G\text{ is }C_k\text{-saturated}\}, \] \[ \operatorname{ssat}(n,C_k)=\min\{|E(G)|:|V(G)|=n,\ G\text{ is }C_k\text{-semisaturated}\}. \] These definitions provide conventional terminology; the supplied record does not establish the precise graph conventions or the intended range of $k$.

The retained conjecture says that both constructions referred to in the source attain their respective minima whenever $n>n_0(k)$. If those constructions are denoted provisionally by $G^{\mathrm{sat}}_{n,k}$ and $G^{\mathrm{ssat}}_{n,k}$, its intended logical form is: for every $k$ in the source's unspecified admissible range, there exists a positive integer $n_0(k)$ such that, for every positive integer $n>n_0(k)$, the two constructions are defined, have $n$ vertices, have their respective saturation properties, and satisfy \[ |E(G^{\mathrm{sat}}_{n,k})|=\operatorname{sat}(n,C_k),\qquad |E(G^{\mathrm{ssat}}_{n,k})|=\operatorname{ssat}(n,C_k). \] This is only a conditional schema: the supplied record does not specify either construction or its edge count. Its statement-verification field describes the constructions as diagram-dependent and unrecoverable in standalone text, and its statement status is unrecoverable. Consequently, an explicit, independently interpretable optimality conjecture cannot be recovered from this record alone; no construction or extremal formula is supplied here.

Short English statement

The source conjectures that two particular constructions attain the cycle saturation and cycle semisaturation minima for all sufficiently large $n$, with the threshold depending on $k$. The constructions and the intended range of $k$ are missing, so only a conditional formulation is possible. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified status.
