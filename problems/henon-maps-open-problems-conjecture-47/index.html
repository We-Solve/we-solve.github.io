Henon Maps: Open Problems — Conjecture 47

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a number field, that is, a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $\overline K$ be an algebraic closure. Let $B\geq 1$ and let $d\geq 2$ be an integer. Write $\mathcal H_d(K)$ for the class of generalized Hénon maps of degree $d$ over $K$ intended in the supplied source. These are polynomial automorphisms of the affine plane. The supplied record does not define this class precisely: in particular, it does not specify whether it includes compositions of generalized Hénon maps. This qualification remains unresolved, and no convention for that class is imposed here.

For $f\in\mathcal H_d(K)$, interpret the periodic points relevant to the stated field-degree condition as \[ \operatorname{Per}(f)=\{P\in{\mathbb{A}}^2(\overline K): f^m(P)=P\text{ for some integer }m\geq 1\}. \] For $P=(x,y)$, put $K(P)=K(x,y)$. Thus individual algebraic periodic points are counted, without identifying points in the same orbit or points conjugate over $K$.

Conjecture 47 (Berger and Bedford, as attributed in the supplied record). There exists a finite bound depending only on $[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]$, $d$, and $B$ for \[ \#\{P\in\operatorname{Per}(f):[K(P):K]\leq B\}. \] More explicitly, for every integer $n\geq 1$, every integer $d\geq 2$, and every $B\geq 1$, there exists a finite constant $C(n,d,B)$ such that for every number field $K$ with $[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]=n$ and every $f\in\mathcal H_d(K)$, \[ \#\{P\in\operatorname{Per}(f):[K(P):K]\leq B\}\leq C(n,d,B). \] There is no restriction on the periods of the counted points. This formulation remains conditional on clarifying the source's intended class $\mathcal H_d(K)$.

Short English statement

The conjecture predicts a uniform bound on the number of algebraic periodic points of bounded field degree for generalized Hénon maps. The bound depends only on the degree of the number field, the degree of the map, and the field-degree cutoff $B$. The precise convention for generalized Hénon maps requires clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that label is not an independently verified status assessment.
