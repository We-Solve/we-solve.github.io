Math-3541

Étale Covers of Smooth Loci of Singular Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a singular complex algebraic variety, let $U=X_{\mathrm{reg}}$ denote its smooth locus, and let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be the inclusion. The source imposes no explicit normality, irreducibility, connectedness, or properness hypothesis; none is added here.

For a complex algebraic variety $T$, let $\operatorname{FinEt}(T)$ be the category of finite étale morphisms to $T$, with morphisms over $T$. Given a finite étale cover $p:V\to U$, an extension as a finite étale cover of $X$ consists of a finite étale morphism $\overline p:Y\to X$ and an isomorphism \[ Y\times_X U\cong V \] over $U$. Thus restriction defines a functor \[ j^*:\operatorname{FinEt}(X)\longrightarrow\operatorname{FinEt}(U). \] For a geometric base point $\bar x$ of $U$, the étale fundamental group of a connected component containing $\bar x$ is the profinite automorphism group of the geometric-fiber functor on its finite étale covers. Writing $U_{\bar x}$ and $X_{\bar x}$ for the respective connected components, inclusion induces a continuous homomorphism \[ j_*:\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute et}}(U_{\bar x},\bar x) \longrightarrow \pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute et}}(X_{\bar x},\bar x). \]

Problem. Characterize the essential image of $j^*$: determine the obstructions to a finite étale cover of $X_{\mathrm{reg}}$ admitting an extension as a finite étale cover of $X$. Describe how the singularities of $X$ affect the comparison homomorphisms $j_*$, including their kernels and images, and explain their relationship to these extension obstructions. If the smooth locus has several connected components, the extension question concerns the full restriction functor, including compatibility across those components.

This is a formulation of the source's open-ended comparison question, not a proposed theorem. Extension is interpreted here as extension that remains finite étale over all of $X$; the source does not explicitly specify whether a broader class of extensions was intended. Allowing ramification over the singular locus, including quasi-étale extensions in an appropriate normal setting, is a different extension question. The intended generality of $X$, the permitted extensions, and the desired form of an obstruction theory require source clarification.

Short English statement

For a singular complex algebraic variety, determine which finite étale covers of its smooth locus extend to finite étale covers of the whole variety, and describe the induced comparison of étale fundamental groups. The source leaves the hypotheses and the intended obstruction theory unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
