Math-3717

Congruence from Complete Robin and Dirichlet Spectra

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual Euclidean interpretation, let $\Omega_1,\Omega_2\subset{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be Lipschitz domains in the same dimension. The supplied record does not specify the dimension, boundedness, or hypotheses ensuring discrete spectra. The formulation below is therefore conditional on the Laplacians having complete discrete eigenvalue sequences as presupposed by the source; boundedness is not added as a source hypothesis.

For $i\in\{1,2\}$ and a constant $\beta\geq0$, let $A_{i,\beta}$ denote the Robin realization of $-\Delta$ on $L^2(\Omega_i)$, with boundary condition \[ \partial_{\nu_i}u+\beta u=0\quad\text{on }\partial\Omega_i, \] where $\nu_i$ is the outward normal and the condition is understood in the weak sense appropriate to a Lipschitz boundary. This uses the conventional nonnegative Robin parameter sign; $\beta=0$ gives the Neumann condition. Let $A_{i,D}$ denote the Dirichlet realization, with zero boundary trace. Write their eigenvalues in nondecreasing order, repeated according to multiplicity, as \[ \left(\lambda_k(\Omega_i,\beta)\right)_{k\geq1},\qquad \left(\lambda_k^D(\Omega_i)\right)_{k\geq1}. \] Here completeness of these sequences means that they exhaust the corresponding spectra.

The supplied question asks whether \[ \begin{aligned} \lambda_k(\Omega_1,\beta)&=\lambda_k(\Omega_2,\beta) &&\text{for every }\beta\in[0,\infty)\text{ and every }k\geq1,\\ \lambda_k^D(\Omega_1)&=\lambda_k^D(\Omega_2) &&\text{for every }k\geq1 \end{aligned} \] necessarily implies Euclidean congruence: \[ \Omega_2=Q\Omega_1+a \quad\text{for some }Q\in{\mathbb{R}}^{d\times d},\ Q^{\mathsf T}Q=I_d, \text{ and }a\in{\mathbb{R}}^d. \] Equality is required at each common Robin parameter, together with Dirichlet spectral equality. The intended dimension and spectral hypotheses require source clarification before this conditional reconstruction can be regarded as a fully specified statement.

Short English statement

The question asks whether two Lipschitz domains with identical Robin spectra, including multiplicities, at every constant parameter $\beta\geq0$, and identical Dirichlet spectra must be related by a Euclidean rigid motion. The supplied formulation leaves the dimension and assumptions guaranteeing complete discrete eigenvalue sequences unspecified. Its open status is supplied curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
