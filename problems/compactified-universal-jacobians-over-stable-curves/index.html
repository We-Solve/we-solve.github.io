Compactified Universal Jacobians over Stable Curves

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source specifies a degree $d$, but does not specify the genus, number of markings, base field or base scheme, or category of moduli spaces. The following notation describes the conventional interpretation conditionally on those choices; it does not select a particular compactification.

Fix integers $g,n\geq 0$ with $2g-2+n>0$, and $d\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. After choosing a base, let $\mathcal M_{g,n}$ denote the moduli stack of smooth, proper, geometrically connected genus-$g$ curves with $n$ ordered, distinct marked points. Let $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}$ denote the moduli stack of stable marked curves: the curves may have nodes, the markings are distinct smooth points, and the marked curves have finite automorphism groups. Write $j:\mathcal M_{g,n}\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}$ for the smooth locus. Write $\mathcal J^d_{g,n}\to\mathcal M_{g,n}$ schematically for the degree-$d$ universal Jacobian moduli problem, whose fiber over a smooth marked curve $C$ is $\operatorname{Pic}^d(C)$, parametrizing isomorphism classes of degree-$d$ line bundles. A precise construction must specify whether this notation denotes a relative Picard space, a rigidified stack, or the stack retaining scalar automorphisms of line bundles.

For a geometric stable curve $C$, a possible replacement for a line bundle is a coherent sheaf $F$ that is torsion-free and has rank one at the generic point of every irreducible component. Its total degree is \[ \deg_C(F)=\chi(C,F)-\chi(C,\mathcal O_C). \] If $C_1,\ldots,C_r$ are the irreducible components of $C$, the multidegree of a line bundle $L$ is \[ \underline{\deg}(L)=\left(\deg_{C_1}(L|_{C_1}),\ldots,\deg_{C_r}(L|_{C_r})\right),\qquad \sum_{i=1}^{r}\deg_{C_i}(L|_{C_i})=d. \] For a torsion-free sheaf, component degrees can instead be defined using the restrictions modulo torsion; their relation to total degree must account for failure of local freeness at nodes. The source does not prescribe a sheaf model or a multidegree convention for such boundary objects.

Problem. Determine how to construct an extension \[ \overline\pi:\overline{\mathcal J}^{\,d}_{g,n}\longrightarrow\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}, \qquad \overline{\mathcal J}^{\,d}_{g,n}\times_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}}\mathcal M_{g,n} \simeq\mathcal J^d_{g,n}, \] whose fibers over stable marked curves are well-behaved compactified Jacobians, and determine which degenerations of line bundles and which multidegrees belong in these fibers. Specify the auxiliary choices and conditions needed to make this construction precise, including any stability condition or polarization used to select boundary objects.

The phrase "well-behaved" is not defined in the supplied record. In particular, the record does not specify the required properness, separatedness, flatness, representability, universal-family, or base-change properties, nor the precise locus that must be dense in a compactified fiber. Consequently, this is a formulation of the supplied construction question with explicit unresolved parameters, rather than a uniquely specified existence assertion.

Short English statement

Extend the degree-$d$ universal Jacobian from smooth marked curves to stable marked curves, and determine the boundary objects and multidegrees to include. The source leaves the base, moduli category, stability choices, and meaning of "well-behaved" unspecified, so these remain part of the formulation requiring review.
