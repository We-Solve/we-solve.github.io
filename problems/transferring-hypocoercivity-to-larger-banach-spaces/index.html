Math-4818

Transferring Hypocoercivity to Larger Banach Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and let $L_H:D(L_H)\subseteq H\to H$ denote the operator called $L$ in the supplied record. Write $\sigma_H(L_H)$ for its spectrum and $\ker L_H=\{f\in D(L_H):L_Hf=0\}$. Let $T_H(t)=e^{tL_H}$ denote the semigroup appearing in the source assertion, and let $\Pi_H$ denote the operator written there as $\Pi$. The record does not explicitly define this operator or specify the operator-domain and semigroup-generation hypotheses.

The supplied Hilbert-space assumptions are that there exist $a<0$ and a finite constant $C_a>0$ such that \[ \sigma_H(L_H)\cap\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:\operatorname{Re}z>a\}=\{0\}, \qquad \dim\ker L_H<\infty, \] and, with the usual forward-time interpretation of the decay assertion, \[ \|T_H(t)f-\Pi_Hf\|_H\leq C_a e^{at}\|f\|_H \qquad(f\in H,\ t\geq0). \] The source does not specify whether the scalar field is complex or whether complexification is intended when defining the spectrum of a real operator. The role of $\Pi_H$ as an equilibrium projection, including its range and whether it is a spectral or orthogonal projection, requires clarification from the source.

Let $E$ be a Banach space containing $H$, and let $L_E:D(L_E)\subseteq E\to E$ extend $L_H$. Under the identification $H\subseteq E$, operator extension means \[ D(L_H)\subseteq D(L_E),\qquad L_Ef=L_Hf\quad(f\in D(L_H)). \] No continuity or density assumption on the inclusion $H\subseteq E$ is supplied.

Extension question. Determine additional hypotheses on the inclusion, the operators and their semigroups under which the Hilbert-space spectral and decay conclusions transfer to $E$. A literal same-rate formulation of the requested conclusions is \[ \sigma_E(L_E)\cap\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:\operatorname{Re}z>a\}=\{0\}, \qquad \dim\ker L_E<\infty, \] together with a semigroup $T_E(t)=e^{tL_E}$, an appropriate bounded equilibrium projection $\Pi_E$, and a finite constant $C_{a,E}>0$ satisfying \[ \|T_E(t)f-\Pi_Ef\|_E\leq C_{a,E}e^{at}\|f\|_E \qquad(f\in E,\ t\geq0). \] The intended compatibility conditions would need to specify whether \[ T_E(t)|_H=T_H(t),\qquad \Pi_E|_H=\Pi_H, \qquad \operatorname{Ran}\Pi_E=\ker L_E \] are required. These conditions express a possible precise transfer target; they are not additional hypotheses explicitly supplied by the record. Likewise, the record does not settle whether the same exponent $a$ is required or whether transfer with a different negative exponent is sufficient. The question asks for sufficient hypotheses, rather than asserting transfer for every extension. The supplied verification note says that the report gives sufficient splitting conditions and applications, but those conditions are not included in the supplied record and cannot be reconstructed here.

Short English statement

The problem asks when spectral isolation of zero and exponential convergence to equilibrium for an operator on a Hilbert space persist after extending the operator to a larger Banach space. The embedding, generator assumptions, equilibrium projection and intended decay rate need clarification. The supplied curation labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
