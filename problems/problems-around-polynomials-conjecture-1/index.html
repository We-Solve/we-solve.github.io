Problems Around Polynomials — Conjecture 1

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N\geq1$, let $x_1,\ldots,x_N\in{\mathbb{R}}^3$ be distinct fixed charge locations, and let $\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_N$ be their real charge strengths. On $\Omega={\mathbb{R}}^3\setminus\{x_1,\ldots,x_N\}$ define \[ E(x)=\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{\xi_i(x-x_i)}{\|x-x_i\|^3},\qquad \Phi(x)=\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{\xi_i}{\|x-x_i\|},\qquad E=-\nabla\Phi. \] An equilibrium is a point $x\in\Omega$ with $E(x)=0$. The source assumes the set of equilibria is finite and asks whether \[ \#\{x\in\Omega:E(x)=0\}\leq(N-1)^2. \] The count is of distinct points, not multiplicities, and no nondegeneracy assumption is in the conjecture. The source imposes no sign restriction on the strengths; it does not explicitly discuss zero strengths. Charge locations themselves are excluded from the field domain.

The supplied research summary reports a refutation by Arathoon--Ball--Kvalheim, preprint version 1 of July 29, 2026. Its described configuration has $N=5$: unit charges at the vertices of an equilateral triangle and two equal small charges at axial positions $\pm\varepsilon$, with \[ q_\varepsilon=\frac34\varepsilon^3-\frac5{32}\varepsilon^5. \] For sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$, the summary reports at least $24$ nondegenerate equilibria, exceeding $(5-1)^2=16$, and a generic perturbation of the charge strengths yielding a Morse potential with $k\geq24$ critical points. Nondegeneracy means $\det D^2\Phi\ne0$ at a critical point. The full coordinate normalization and the global finiteness argument are not reproduced in this record and must be checked against the finite-equilibrium hypothesis of the conjecture.

The summary additionally claims its author analytically and numerically rechecked a computational lemma saying that a degree-$4$ limit potential $\Phi_0$ has exactly $21$ nondegenerate critical points. That limit potential and its calculation are not supplied. The $21$-point auxiliary claim is distinct from the $24$-equilibrium claim for the charge configuration. The summary explicitly qualifies the claimed refutation as a very recent, not yet peer-reviewed preprint; its asserted high confidence and independent re-verification belong to the supplied summary, not to this draft.

Short English statement

Maxwell's conjecture in the source bounds a finite set of electrostatic equilibria of $N$ point charges by $(N-1)^2$. The supplied summary reports a July 2026 five-charge construction with at least $24$ equilibria, against the bound $16$, but explicitly identifies the claimed refutation as an unrefereed preprint.
