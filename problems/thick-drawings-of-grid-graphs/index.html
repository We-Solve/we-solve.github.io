Math-14727

Thick Drawings of Grid Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a graph $G$, let $V(G)$ denote its vertex set and let $n=|V(G)|$ be its number of vertices. The supplied corrected statement asks whether there exists an absolute constant $C>0$, independent of both $G$ and $n$, such that every grid graph with $n$ vertices has a $1$-thick drawing contained in a square of side length $C\sqrt n$.

The supplied record does not define either "grid graph" or "$1$-thick drawing". Accordingly, let $\mathcal G_{\mathrm{grid}}$ provisionally denote the intended class of grid graphs, and let $\mathcal T_1(G)$ provisionally denote the intended collection of $1$-thick drawings of $G$. For a drawing $D$, write $\operatorname{im}(D)$ for its geometric image. With these unresolved conventions, the question for positive vertex counts has the form \[ \exists C>0\;\forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\;\forall G\in\mathcal G_{\mathrm{grid}},\qquad |V(G)|=n\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists D\in\mathcal T_1(G)\quad \operatorname{im}(D)\subseteq[0,C\sqrt n]^2. \] Here the coordinate square represents the square in the question. No additional clearance from its boundary is specified in the supplied record.

Unresolved definitions. The record does not specify whether grid graphs are arbitrary finite subgraphs of a lattice graph, induced subgraphs, or rectangular grid graphs, nor does it specify the lattice dimension. It also does not specify the permitted edge geometry, intersection rules, or distance conditions defining thickness, including how incident vertices and edges are treated. The notation above therefore records the quantifiers and size bound without supplying these missing definitions; it is not a complete intrinsic formulation until those conventions are established. The source does not separately address the empty graph.

The bound $C\sqrt n$ follows the supplied corrected statement. The original extraction has a displaced square-root symbol and extensive unrelated text; it is not a reliable independent rendering of that bound.

Short English statement

Is there a universal constant $C>0$ such that every grid graph on $n$ vertices has a $1$-thick drawing inside a square of side length $C\sqrt n$? The intended meanings of grid graph and thickness must be established before this becomes a fully specified mathematical question.
