Math-7135

Disconnected Preimages of Subsurfaces Under Finite Covers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The notation for the surfaces is not explicitly defined in the supplied record. Under the conventional interpretation, let $S_j$ denote a closed, connected, orientable surface of genus $j$, and let a finite cover mean an unbranched covering map with finitely many sheets. The formulation below is conditional on these conventions; whether they match the source requires review.

For $k\in\{3,4\}$, a $k$-holed sphere in $S_g$ is a compact embedded subsurface $H\subset S_g$ homeomorphic to a sphere with the interiors of $k$ pairwise disjoint closed disks removed. Require every component of $\partial H$ to be essential in $S_g$, meaning that it is not null-homotopic in $S_g$. No requirement that distinct boundary components be nonisotopic is added. For a subset of a surface, $\operatorname{int}_{S_j}$ denotes its interior in that surface.

For each $k\in\{3,4\}$, consider the assertion \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every pair of integers }h>g\geq 2 \text{ and every finite covering map }f:S_h\longrightarrow S_g,\\ \text{there exists a }k\text{-holed sphere }H\subset S_g \text{ whose boundary components are essential in }S_g\\ \text{such that }\operatorname{int}_{S_h}\!\left(f^{-1}(H)\right) \text{ is disconnected.} \end{gathered} \] The source asks whether this assertion holds for $k=4$, and asks the corresponding question for $k=3$. The subsurface $H$ may depend on the covering map $f$. These are questions, not asserted conclusions.

Short English statement

For every finite cover $S_h\to S_g$ with $h>g\geq2$, must there be an embedded four-holed sphere with essential boundary whose full preimage has disconnected interior? Ask the same question for a three-holed sphere. This formulation uses the usual closed, connected, orientable surface convention, which the supplied record does not explicitly specify.
