Math-6836

Uniform Determinant Integrals for Row Contractions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Using the standard complex-matrix and probability-Haar conventions, let $M_k({\mathbb{C}})$ denote the complex $k\times k$ matrices, and define the row norm of $A=(A_1,\ldots,A_d)\in M_k({\mathbb{C}})^d$ by \[ \|A\|_{\mathrm{row}}:=\left\|\sum_{j=1}^d A_jA_j^*\right\|^{1/2}, \] where $^*$ denotes conjugate transpose and the norm is the operator norm on ${\mathbb{C}}^k$. For each positive integer $N$, let \[ U(N):=\{V\in M_N({\mathbb{C}}):V^*V=VV^*=I_N\}, \] and equip $U(N)^d$ with product normalized Haar measure ${\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_N^{\otimes d}(U)$. For $U=(U_1,\ldots,U_d)$, put \[ A\otimes U:=\sum_{j=1}^d A_j\otimes U_j\in M_{kN}({\mathbb{C}}), \] where the tensor products on the right are Kronecker products. These standard conventions make explicit notation left undefined in the supplied excerpt; their agreement with the report requires review.

Conjecture 12 (supplied formulation). For every real $r$ with $0\le r<1$ and every integer $k\ge1$, there exists a finite constant $C(r,k)\ge0$ such that, for every integer $d\ge1$ and every $A\in M_k({\mathbb{C}})^d$ satisfying $\|A\|_{\mathrm{row}}\le r$, \[ \sup_{N\in\{1,2,\ldots\}} \int_{U(N)^d} \left|\det\!\left(I_{kN}-\sum_{j=1}^d A_j\otimes U_j\right)\right|^2 \,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_N^{\otimes d}(U) \le C(r,k). \] In particular, the same constant must work independently of $d$, $N$, and $A$.

Source qualifications. The excerpt presents this bound as a weaker conjecture whose proof would make rigorous a formal argument, inspired by a strong Szegő limit theorem proof attributed to Bump and Diaconis, for a preceding conjecture. The included Conjecture 10 concerns polynomials $p,q$ in noncommuting indeterminates $x_1,\ldots,x_d$, with $p(0)=q(0)=1$, assumed stable and admitting determinantal representations $\det p(X)=\det(I-A\otimes X)$ and $\det q(X)=\det(I-B\otimes X)$. Its transcribed limit formula has unclear conjugation, and stability is not defined in the supplied record, so that contextual formula is not reconstructed here.

The excerpt reports that a related conjecture holds for all $d$ when $k=1$, and for all $k$ when $d=1$; the referent of that sentence is unclear because both Conjectures 12 and 10 appear in the transcription. It also proposes a particular bound involving $1-r^2$, but the exponent is corrupted by a detached numeral $2$, so no precise candidate constant is asserted here. The source further suggests that, for fixed $d$ and $A$, these integrals should be nondecreasing in $N$, and reports that both the proposed explicit bound and this monotonicity hold when $k=1$. These are supplied contextual claims, not additional requirements of the displayed conjecture or independently verified facts.

Short English statement

For each $0\le r<1$ and matrix size $k\ge1$, the conjecture asks for a finite bound on the squared determinant averaged over independent Haar unitary matrices, uniform in their size $N$, the number $d$ of matrices, and every tuple with row norm at most $r$. Standard notation and measure conventions are made explicit above; source ambiguities and reported special cases remain subject to human review.
