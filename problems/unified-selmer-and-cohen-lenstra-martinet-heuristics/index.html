Unified Selmer and Cohen-Lenstra-Martinet Heuristics

Its ordinary ideal class group is \[ \operatorname{Cl}(K)=I_K/P_K, \] where $I_K$ is the group of nonzero fractional ideals of the ring of integers $\mathcal O_K$, and $P_K$ is its subgroup of nonzero principal fractional ideals.
