Math-7121

Finiteness Properties of Lodha–Moore Kernels

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $H$, a classifying space $K(H,1)$ is a connected CW complex with fundamental group $H$ and contractible universal cover. The group $H$ is of type $F_\infty$ if it admits a classifying space whose $n$-skeleton is finite for every integer $n\geq 0$. Here the source notation $F^{\infty}$ is interpreted as the standard finiteness property $F_\infty$; this interpretation requires review.

The group $H$ is of type $FP_2$ over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ if the trivial left ${\mathbb{Z}} H$-module ${\mathbb{Z}}$ has a projective resolution \[ \cdots\longrightarrow P_2\longrightarrow P_1\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}\longrightarrow 0 \] in which $P_0,P_1,P_2$ are finitely generated. The coefficient ring is taken to be ${\mathbb{Z}}$, following the usual convention for an unqualified $FP_2$; the supplied statement does not specify coefficients. A group is finitely presentable if it admits a presentation with finitely many generators and finitely many defining relations.

The supplied record combines two questions, which must be kept separate.

  1. For the intended Lodha--Moore groups $G$, equipped with the source's designated natural homomorphism $\chi_G:G\to{\mathbb{Z}}$, is \[ \ker(\chi_G)=\{g\in G:\chi_G(g)=0\} \] of type $F_\infty$? This is only a schematic formulation: the supplied record neither defines the intended groups nor specifies $\chi_G$. It also does not explicitly state that $\chi_G$ is surjective. Thus neither the precise range of $G$ nor the kernel is determined by the supplied statement.
  2. Let $\mathcal T$ denote the intended family of Thompson-like groups. Does there exist a group $H\in\mathcal T$ such that $H$ is of type $FP_2$ over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ and is not finitely presentable? The supplied record gives no definition of $\mathcal T$, so this existential question has no fully specified domain here.

The supplied verification note states that the extraction merges two independent numbered questions in the report. Their separate original numbers and complete formulations are not supplied. Consequently these are qualified reconstructions, not a recovered single precise problem. The registry marks the statement as unrecoverable and the overall literature status as partially_solved; those are supplied curation labels.

Short English statement

The record combines two independent questions: whether certain unspecified Lodha--Moore kernels have finite skeleta in every dimension in a classifying space, and whether the intended family of Thompson-like groups contains a group of type $FP_2$ that is not finitely presentable. The groups, natural homomorphisms, and intended family need specification. Supplied dated curation reports the first question resolved and the second open; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
