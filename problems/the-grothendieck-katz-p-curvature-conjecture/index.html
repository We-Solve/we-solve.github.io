The Grothendieck–Katz p-Curvature Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer and let $A\in M_n({\mathbb{Q}}(z))$. Write $\partial={\,\mathrm{d}}/{\,\mathrm{d}} z$ and consider the differential system \[ (\partial-A)\mathbf f=0. \] Here, interpret "all solutions are algebraic" in the usual complex local sense: at every point $z_0\in{\mathbb{C}}$ where $A$ has no pole, every local holomorphic solution germ $\mathbf f=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)^\mathsf T$ has each component algebraic over ${\mathbb{C}}(z)$. Explicitly, for each $i$ there is a nonzero polynomial $P_i(T)\in{\mathbb{C}}(z)[T]$ such that $P_i(f_i)=0$ as a germ of a meromorphic function. This interpretation allows arbitrary complex initial values; the source does not explicitly specify the field over which solutions are to be algebraic.

To define reduction, choose $B\in M_n({\mathbb{Z}}[z])$ and a nonzero $q\in{\mathbb{Z}}[z]$ with $A=B/q$. For each prime $p$ such that $\overline q\neq 0$, put \[ A_p=\frac{\overline B}{\overline q}\in M_n({\mathbb{F}}_p(z)),\qquad D_p:{\mathbb{F}}_p(z)^n\longrightarrow{\mathbb{F}}_p(z)^n,\qquad D_p(\mathbf v)=\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}\mathbf v}{{\,\mathrm{d}} z}-A_p\mathbf v. \] The power $D_p^p$ means composition of $D_p$ with itself $p$ times. Since $({\,\mathrm{d}}/{\,\mathrm{d}} z)^p=0$ on ${\mathbb{F}}_p(z)$, this composition is ${\mathbb{F}}_p(z)$-linear and is the $p$-curvature evaluated on ${\,\mathrm{d}}/{\,\mathrm{d}} z$. Thus the vanishing condition means that $D_p^p$ is the zero endomorphism of ${\mathbb{F}}_p(z)^n$, rather than merely vanishing on horizontal solutions.

Conjectural statement (Grothendieck--Katz $p$-curvature conjecture). For every such $n$ and $A$, is the following equivalence true? \[ \begin{gathered} \text{Every local holomorphic solution of }(\partial-A)\mathbf f=0 \text{ is algebraic over }{\mathbb{C}}(z) \\[2pt] \Longleftrightarrow \\[2pt] \exists\text{ a finite set of primes }S\quad \forall\text{ primes }p\notin S:\quad \overline q\neq 0\ \text{ and }\ D_p^p=0. \end{gathered} \] The finite exceptional set includes primes where the chosen presentation cannot be reduced.

Unresolved source qualification. The supplied clean statement specifies this linear differential-system conjecture. The supplied original excerpt also contains an incomplete formulation of a stronger nonlinear converse to Eisenstein's theorem, with missing conditions and an undefined function $\omega(p)$. That additional conjecture cannot be stated precisely from this record and is not reconstructed here.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every solution of a rational linear differential system is algebraic exactly when its $p$-curvature vanishes for all but finitely many primes. Algebraicity is interpreted here for complex local solution germs over ${\mathbb{C}}(z)$. The supplied record labels the conjecture open; its incomplete nonlinear Eisenstein-related formulation requires separate clarification.
