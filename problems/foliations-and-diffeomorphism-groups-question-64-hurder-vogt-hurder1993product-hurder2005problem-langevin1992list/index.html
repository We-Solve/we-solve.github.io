Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 64 (Hurder-Vogt [hurder1993product,hurder2005problem,langevin1992list])

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $q$ denote the codimension parameter in the source question; the supplied record does not specify its allowed range. Write \[ SO(q)=\{A\in M_q({\mathbb{R}}):A^{\mathsf T}A=I_q,\ \det A=1\}, \] with its canonical action on ${\mathbb{R}}^q$ given by $(A,v)\mapsto Av$. A codimension-$q$ microfoliation on a manifold $M$ is a germ, along the zero section of a rank-$q$ vector bundle over $M$, of a foliation transverse to the bundle fibers.

Let $\gamma$ be the $\bar\Gamma_q$-structure on $SO(q)$ described in the source: its microfoliation is the suspension of this canonical action. Let \[ c_\gamma\colon SO(q)\longrightarrow B\bar\Gamma_q \] be a classifying map for this structure. The question is whether $c_\gamma$ is null-homotopic. Explicitly, does there exist a point $b\in B\bar\Gamma_q$ and a continuous map \[ H\colon SO(q)\times[0,1]\longrightarrow B\bar\Gamma_q \] such that, for every $A\in SO(q)$, \[ H(A,0)=c_\gamma(A),\qquad H(A,1)=b? \] The notation $\bar\Gamma_q$, its regularity and normal-bundle conventions, and the suspension construction are retained from the source. The supplied record does not define these conventions or provide enough construction details to specify them independently. The question is therefore conditional on those intended conventions; no additional choices are imposed here.

Short English statement

Does the structure on $SO(q)$ obtained by suspending its standard action on ${\mathbb{R}}^q$ have a classifying map homotopic to a constant map in $B\bar\Gamma_q$? The intended range of $q$ and the precise structure and suspension conventions require clarification from the source.
