Classification of Stable Allen-Cahn Configurations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a classification of entire stable Allen--Cahn configurations in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$. It does not specify the potential, the admissible class of configurations, or the precise macroscopic conclusions sought. The following gives a conditional variational framework without supplying those missing hypotheses.

In a scalar formulation, let $W\in C^2({\mathbb{R}})$ denote the intended potential and let $\mathcal U\subseteq C^2({\mathbb{R}}^3;{\mathbb{R}})$ denote the intended admissible class. Both must be specified to obtain a definite classification problem. For a bounded open set $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^3$, define the local energy \[ E_W(u;\Omega)=\int_\Omega\left(\frac12|\nabla u|^2+W(u)\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] An entire critical point satisfies \[ -\Delta u+W'(u)=0\qquad\text{in }{\mathbb{R}}^3. \] Under the usual compactly supported variation convention, it is stable if \[ \int_{{\mathbb{R}}^3}\left(|\nabla\varphi|^2+W''(u)\varphi^2\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\geq0 \qquad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^3). \] Thus the conditional classification target is \[ \mathcal S(W,\mathcal U)=\left\{u\in\mathcal U: -\Delta u+W'(u)=0\text{ in }{\mathbb{R}}^3,\quad \int_{{\mathbb{R}}^3}\left(|\nabla\varphi|^2+W''(u)\varphi^2\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\geq0 \text{ for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^3) \right\}. \] For comparison, an absolute minimizer under compact perturbations satisfies \[ E_W(u;\Omega)\leq E_W(u+\psi;\Omega) \] for every bounded open $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^3$ and every $\psi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$, subject to any additional admissibility restrictions in the intended formulation. These local definitions do not require finite total energy on ${\mathbb{R}}^3$.

Requested problem, with unresolved qualifications. Specify the intended $W$ and $\mathcal U$, and classify every member of $\mathcal S(W,\mathcal U)$. In particular, determine whether the macroscopic regularity and minimal-surface behavior referred to in the source for absolute minimizers extend to every stable configuration. The supplied excerpt does not identify those results or define their scaling, topology of convergence, limiting objects, or regularity conclusions. Consequently this last request cannot yet be expressed as a precise quantified assertion. The framework above does not assert that the problem is posed for every $C^2$ potential or every entire scalar solution.

Short English statement

The problem asks which entire Allen--Cahn configurations in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ have nonnegative second variation, and whether they share the large-scale regularity and minimal-surface behavior attributed to absolute minimizers. The potential, admissibility assumptions, and exact large-scale conclusions remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
