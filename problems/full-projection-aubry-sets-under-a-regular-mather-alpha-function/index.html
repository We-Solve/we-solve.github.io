Full-projection Aubry sets under a regular Mather alpha function

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Gonzalo Contreras's Question 6 asks whether, "under the same condition on $\alpha_L$," every Aubry set $\mathcal A_c$ covers the configuration torus $T^n$. The preceding condition is not included in this source record. Consequently the literal implication has a missing premise. The aid treats an analytic time-periodic Tonelli Lagrangian and an everywhere positive-definite Hessian of its analytic Mather $\alpha$-function; that is an explicit conditional reconstruction, not a recovered quotation of the omitted premise.

For $L:TT^n\times\mathbb T\to{\mathbb{R}}$, $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, assume fiberwise strict convexity, superlinearity, complete Euler--Lagrange flow, and the indicated analyticity. Identify $H^1(T^n;{\mathbb{R}})$ with ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, and define \[ \alpha_L(c)=-\inf_\mu\int(L-c\cdot v)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu, \] where $\mu$ ranges over invariant probability measures with finite action. With $L_c=L-c\cdot v+\alpha_L(c)$, integer-time minimal actions define the Peierls barrier $h_c(x,y)$, and the time-zero projected Aubry set is $\mathcal A_c^0=\{x:h_c(x,x)=0\}$. The reconstructed target is $\mathcal A_c^0=T^n$ for every $c$; a statement at every time slice needs the corresponding time-periodic interpretation.

The aid reports three scoped consequences. If $\alpha_L$ is analytic and $D^2\alpha_L(c)$ is positive definite for every $c$, its superlinearity makes $\nabla\alpha_L:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to{\mathbb{R}}^n$ a global analytic diffeomorphism, and its Legendre dual \[ \beta_L(h)=\sup_c(c\cdot h-\alpha_L(c)) \] is analytic. The aid invokes Massart--Sorrentino for an affirmative autonomous two-torus case under these hypotheses, without a full theorem citation. Also, an intersection of the lifted phase-space Aubry sets for two distinct classes $c_0,c_1$ forces $\alpha_L$ to be affine on $[c_0,c_1]$, hence cannot occur under strict convexity. This means a common velocity-and-time phase point, not merely intersecting projections in the base torus. These results do not establish the arbitrary-dimensional time-periodic implication.

The explicit time-dependent family supplied by the aid is \[ L(x,v,t)=\tfrac12(v-b(t))^{\mathsf T}A(v-b(t)) +\partial_t\phi(x,t)+D_x\phi(x,t)\cdot v, \] where $A$ is a constant symmetric positive-definite matrix, $b$ is real analytic and one-periodic, and $\phi$ is real analytic and periodic in space and time. Write $\bar b=\int_0^1b(t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t$. The aid's formulas are \[ \alpha_L(c)=c\cdot\bar b+\tfrac12c^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}c,\qquad \nabla\alpha_L(c)=\bar b+A^{-1}c. \] For lifts $X,Y$ of $x,y$ and a positive integer $N$, the normalized action is \[ h_c^N(x,y)=\phi(y,0)-\phi(x,0) +\frac1{2N}\min_{\ell\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n} (Y-X+\ell-N(\bar b+A^{-1}c))^{\mathsf T} A(Y-X+\ell-N(\bar b+A^{-1}c)). \] The quadratic remainder lies between zero and $\lambda_{\max}(A)n/(8N)$, so \[ h_c(x,y)=\phi(y,0)-\phi(x,0),\quad \mathcal A_c^0=T^n, \qquad p=c+D_x\phi(x,t) \] are the barrier, full projection, and dual Aubry graph in the physical cotangent variable $p=\partial_vL$. These graphs foliate each cotangent fiber as $c$ varies, with calibrated velocity $v=b(t)+A^{-1}c$. Nonconstant $b$ gives genuinely time-dependent coefficients, but the example remains a special drift-and-gauge family. The general analytic time-periodic question is reported open in the literature checked by the aid, not independently checked here.

Short English statement

The source omits the preceding hypothesis on Mather's $\alpha$-function. Under the aid's positive-Hessian analytic interpretation, it reports several restricted consequences and an exact time-dependent drift-and-gauge family with full Aubry projection in every class, while retaining the general question as open.
