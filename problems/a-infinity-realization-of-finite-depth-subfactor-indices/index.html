A-Infinity Realization of Finite-Depth Subfactor Indices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the customary interpretation in terms of finite-index subfactors of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors, let $N\subset M$ be a unital inclusion and let $[M:N]=\dim_N L^2(M)$ denote its Jones index. The inclusion is irreducible if $N'\cap M={\mathbb{C}} 1$. A factor is hyperfinite if it is the weak operator closure of a directed increasing union of finite-dimensional unital $*$-subalgebras. Finite depth means that only finitely many isomorphism classes of irreducible bimodules occur in the standard invariant generated by the inclusion, equivalently that its principal graph is finite.

For the purpose of stating a candidate interpretation, let an $A_\infty$-subfactor mean a finite-index inclusion $P\subset Q$ of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors whose principal graph is the rooted bipartite graph \[ V=\{0,1,2,\ldots\},\qquad E=\left\{\{n,n+1\}:n\geq 0\right\}, \] rooted at $0$, with bipartition given by parity. The supplied record does not define this terminology, so this interpretation requires confirmation.

Supplied Conjecture 1, under this interpretation. For every finite-index inclusion $N\subset M$ of hyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors, if $N'\cap M={\mathbb{C}}1$ and $N\subset M$ has finite depth, then there exist type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors $P\subset Q$ such that \[ \Gamma(P\subset Q)\cong A_\infty, \qquad [Q:P]=[M:N], \] where $\Gamma$ denotes the rooted principal graph.

Unresolved scope qualifications. The source does not explicitly specify the factor type, separability conventions, or whether the realizing inclusion $P\subset Q$ must itself be hyperfinite. The displayed formulation leaves hyperfiniteness of the realizing inclusion unresolved. More substantially, the supplied universal assertion includes no lower bound on $[M:N]$. Under the graph-based meaning of $A_\infty$, compatibility with low-index cases requires clarification; a restriction such as $[M:N]>4$ must not be inserted without source confirmation. Consequently, the displayed assertion is a transcription of the supplied target under stated conventions, not a mathematically certified formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every index of an irreducible hyperfinite finite-depth subfactor can also occur for a subfactor with principal graph $A_\infty$. Its intended index range and the hyperfiniteness requirement for the realizing subfactor need clarification. The supplied partially solved status and exceptional-index claims are dated curation, not independently verified results.
