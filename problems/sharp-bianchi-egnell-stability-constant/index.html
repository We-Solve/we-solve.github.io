Sharp Bianchi-Egnell Stability Constant

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for the sharp Bianchi--Egnell stability constant for the classical Sobolev inequality. Its extracted original statement truncates the stability inequality before its right-hand side. The following gives the conventional formulation explicitly; the dimension, scalar field, and distance normalization must be checked against the complete cited report.

Fix an integer $n\geq 3$, and adopt the real-valued setting. Let $2^*=2n/(n-2)$, and let $\dot H^1({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})$ be the completion of $C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})$ under the norm \[ \|u\|_{\dot H^1}=\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right)^{1/2}, \] identified with its representatives in $L^{2^*}({\mathbb{R}}^n)$. Define the sharp Sobolev constant by \[ S_n=\inf_{\substack{u\in\dot H^1({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})\\u\ne0}} \frac{\displaystyle\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x} {\displaystyle\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|u|^{2^*}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right)^{2/2^*}}. \] For $z\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$ and $\lambda>0$, put \[ B_{z,\lambda}(x)=\lambda^{(n-2)/2}\left(1+\lambda^2|x-z|^2\right)^{-(n-2)/2}, \qquad \mathcal M_n=\{aB_{z,\lambda}:a\in{\mathbb{R}},\ z\in{\mathbb{R}}^n,\ \lambda>0\}. \] The supplied excerpt identifies constant multiples of these bubbles as the Sobolev optimizers. Set \[ \delta_n(u)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x -S_n\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|u|^{2^*}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right)^{2/2^*}, \qquad d_n(u)^2=\inf_{v\in\mathcal M_n}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|\nabla(u-v)|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] With this distance convention, define \[ c_{\mathrm{BE}}(n)=\sup\left\{c\geq0: \delta_n(u)\geq c\,d_n(u)^2 \text{ for every }u\in\dot H^1({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})\right\}. \] Equivalently, \[ c_{\mathrm{BE}}(n)= \inf_{\substack{u\in\dot H^1({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})\\d_n(u)>0}} \frac{\delta_n(u)}{d_n(u)^2}. \] The problem is to determine the exact value of $c_{\mathrm{BE}}(n)$. The source writes $c_{\mathrm{BE}}$ without selecting a dimension; the dimension-dependent notation here makes that unresolved parameter explicit. The formulation uses all of ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, finite homogeneous Sobolev energy, and no finite boundary. The real-valued domain and the displayed distance normalization are explicit reconstruction conventions, since the supplied extraction does not contain the hypotheses and right-hand side of the stability inequality.

The record attributes the existence of a positive stability constant to Bianchi and Egnell. Its supplied dated literature assessment reports improved bounds and no known exact value. These are source-reported claims and status labels, not an independent verification in this draft.

Short English statement

For each dimension $n\geq3$, determine the largest constant controlling the Sobolev deficit by the squared gradient distance to the family of bubble optimizers. The displayed formulation adopts the conventional real-valued homogeneous Sobolev setting; the incomplete source extraction leaves its exact domain and normalization to be checked. The supplied record labels the problem open.
