Brauer–Manin Obstruction for Rationally Connected Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied statement does not specify the ground field. The formulation below explicitly adopts the usual number-field interpretation of the Hasse principle and Brauer--Manin obstruction; this domain is an interpretation requiring confirmation, not a hypothesis explicitly present in the source.

Let $k$ be a number field, let $\bar{k}$ be an algebraic closure, and let $\Omega_k$ be the set of all places of $k$, including the archimedean places. Write $k_v$ for the completion at $v$. Let $X$ be a smooth, proper, geometrically integral variety over $k$. Here geometrically integral means that $X_{\bar{k}}$ is integral. Interpret rational connectedness geometrically: there is a dense open subset $U\subseteq X_{\bar{k}}$ such that, for any $x,y\in U(\bar{k})$, there is a morphism $f:{\mathbb{P}}^1_{\bar{k}}\to X_{\bar{k}}$ whose image contains both $x$ and $y$.

Let ${\mathbb{A}}_k$ denote the adele ring of $k$. Since $X$ is proper, its adelic points can be identified with \[ X({\mathbb{A}}_k)=\prod_{v\in\Omega_k}X(k_v). \] Define the cohomological Brauer group by \[ \operatorname{Br}(X)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}. \] For $\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(X)$ and $P_v\in X(k_v)$, evaluation gives $\alpha(P_v)\in\operatorname{Br}(k_v)$. With $\operatorname{inv}_v:\operatorname{Br}(k_v)\to{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$ the local invariant map, define \[ X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}= \left\{(P_v)_v\in X({\mathbb{A}}_k): \sum_{v\in\Omega_k}\operatorname{inv}_v\left(\alpha(P_v)\right)=0 \text{ for every }\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(X)\right\}. \] For each fixed $\alpha$ and adelic point, only finitely many summands are nonzero. Global reciprocity puts the diagonal image of $X(k)$ inside this set. A failure of the Hasse principle means that $X(k_v)\ne\varnothing$ for every $v$, but $X(k)=\varnothing$; a Brauer--Manin obstruction to rational points occurs when $X({\mathbb{A}}_k)\ne\varnothing$ but $X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}=\varnothing$.

Under the stated number-field and geometric rational-connectedness interpretations, the conjectural assertion is: for every number field $k$ and every smooth, proper, geometrically integral, rationally connected $k$-variety $X$, \[ X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}\ne\varnothing \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X(k)\ne\varnothing. \] Thus the Brauer--Manin obstruction is asserted to be the only obstruction to the Hasse principle. No assertion about weak approximation is included. The supplied curation identifies this assertion with the Colliot--Thélène conjecture and labels it open; these are supplied attribution and status information, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly provisional number-field interpretation, every smooth, proper, geometrically integral, geometrically rationally connected variety with an adelic point orthogonal to its entire Brauer group should have a rational point. The source omits the ground field, so the intended domain requires confirmation. The supplied record labels the conjecture open.
