A quotient-root formula for every parking/toppling Betti number

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Sam Hopkins's source question starts from a Venkatesh--Viswanath result, reference [34], relating maximal graph-parking functions to the full-support root space of the Kac--Moody algebra with Cartan matrix $2I-A(G)$. Hopkins asks whether all parking functions occur somewhere and whether the sandpile group acts on such a root space, and notes a natural maximal-parking-function basis labeling. Anton Dochtermann notes matching counts of last syzygies of the toppling ideal and chambers of a slice of the graphic arrangement, asking for structural links. The record supplies no full description of that arrangement slice.

For a precise version of the aid's formula, let $G$ be a connected finite simple graph with $n$ vertices and choose a sink $q$. A parking function is $f:V\setminus\{q\}\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ such that every nonempty $U\subseteq V\setminus\{q\}$ has a vertex $v$ with $f(v)<\operatorname{outdeg}_U(v)$. Maximal means maximal coordinatewise among parking functions. In $S_0={\mathbb{C}}[x_v:v\neq q]$, the parking ideal is \[ M_{G,q}=\left\langle\prod_{v\in U}x_v^{\operatorname{outdeg}_U(v)}: \varnothing\neq U\subseteq V\setminus\{q\}\right\rangle. \] Its standard monomials are exactly $x^f$ for all parking functions. For the homogeneous toppling ideal, put $S={\mathbb{C}}[x_v:v\in V]$ and \[ I_G=\langle x^{u_+}-x^{u_-}:u\in\operatorname{im}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\Delta_G\rangle, \] where $u=u_+-u_-$ and $\Delta_G$ is the graph Laplacian. Extending $M_{G,q}$ to $S$ preserves its total Betti numbers, but the finite standard-monomial basis statement concerns $S_0/M_{G,q}$, not an added free sink variable.

Let $\Pi$ run over partitions into $r$ nonempty blocks, each inducing a connected subgraph. Contract the blocks, discard loops and replace parallel edges by a single edge to obtain the simple support graph $Q_\Pi$. Let $\mathfrak g(2I-A(Q_\Pi))$ be its Kac--Moody algebra and $\delta_\Pi$ the sum of its $r$ simple roots. With total Betti numbers, the aid reports \[ \beta_{r-1}^{S_0}(S_0/M_{G,q})=\beta_{r-1}^{S}(S/I_G) =\sum_{\substack{\Pi\ \mathrm{connected}\ r\text{-}\mathrm{partition}}} \dim\mathfrak g(2I-A(Q_\Pi))_{\delta_\Pi},\qquad1\leq r\leq n. \] These are total, not multigraded, equalities. At $r=n$, the partition into singletons recovers the last-syzygy/root-space coincidence for $G$ itself. At other degrees the formula uses root spaces for connected quotient partitions, not one single root space containing every parking function.

The full parking-function set is also a torsor for the sandpile group \[ K(G,q)={\mathbb{Z}}^{n-1}/\Delta_{G,q}{\mathbb{Z}}^{n-1}, \] using its usual reduced-divisor/parking representative action. The aid states that this transitive action preserves the subset of maximal parking functions exactly for trees. For a connected graph with a cycle that subset is proper, so this usual action cannot restrict to the maximal-parking-function root basis. This does not rule out other representations of the sandpile group on a vector space; in particular it is not a nonexistence theorem for every possible root-space action. The all-degree numerical formula and this obstruction leave the broader requested structural Lie/algebra/arrangement connections open.

Short English statement

The aid extends the last-syzygy count to every total Betti number by summing root multiplicities over connected quotient partitions. All parking functions have a standard-monomial and torsor interpretation. The usual sandpile action, however, does not preserve the maximal-parking subset for a graph with a cycle, so it cannot simply act on that root basis by restriction.
