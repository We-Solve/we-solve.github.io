Math-9945

Rational Hodge classes on CM abelian varieties

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
On every complex abelian variety of CM type, is each rational class in $H^{2p}$ of Hodge type (p,p) the class of a rational algebraic codimension-p cycle? All p and all finite products and powers of CM abelian varieties are included.

Short English statement

On every complex abelian variety of CM type, is each rational class in $H^{2p}$ of Hodge type (p,p) the class of a rational algebraic codimension-p cycle? All p and all finite products and powers of CM abelian varieties are included.
