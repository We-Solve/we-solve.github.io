Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A zero framing of an oriented knot $K\subset S^3$ is a framing for which a push-off has linking number zero with $K$. For a geometric representative $\gamma$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$, the self Gauss integral is conventionally the writhe integral \[ \operatorname{Gauss}(\gamma)=\frac1{4\pi} \int_{S^1\times S^1\setminus\Delta} \frac{(\gamma'(s)\times\gamma'(t))\cdot(\gamma(s)-\gamma(t))} {\|\gamma(s)-\gamma(t)\|^3}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] Zero framing and vanishing of this geometric integral are distinct conditions and are both retained.

C. Lescop asks whether, for the same zero-framed knot represented with $\operatorname{Gauss}(\gamma)=0$, the Kontsevich integral equals the Chern--Simons series obtained from configuration-space integrals: \[ Z_{\mathrm{Kont}}(K)=Z_{\mathrm{CS}}(\gamma). \] The intended equality is of the full diagram-valued formal series in a common degree-completed diagram space, hence coefficientwise in every degree. Equality only after a chosen Lie-algebra weight system or an unspecified reparametrization would be a different assertion.

The excerpt does not define the particular configuration-space series, its anomaly or correction conventions, the diagram coefficients, or normalization of either side. These must be recovered from the source; they cannot be freely adjusted to force equality. The Gauss-integral display gives its conventional geometric meaning, which also needs confirmation against the source conventions.

Short English statement

For a zero-framed knot with vanishing Gauss integral, is its Kontsevich integral exactly the Chern--Simons configuration-space integral series? The precise series and normalizations need source definitions, and zero framing must not be conflated with zero writhe.
