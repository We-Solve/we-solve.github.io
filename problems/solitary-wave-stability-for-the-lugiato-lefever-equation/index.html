Solitary-Wave Stability for the Lugiato–Lefever Equation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\psi$ denote a complex-valued field, let $i^2=-1$, and let $|\psi|$ denote its complex modulus. The supplied equation is the Lugiato--Lefever equation \[ \partial_t\psi=-i\beta\,\partial_x^2\psi-(1+i\alpha)\psi+i|\psi|^2\psi+F. \] Here $t$ and $x$ are the time and spatial variables, respectively, and $\alpha,\beta,F$ are parameters. The source record does not specify their admissible values or whether each parameter is real or complex; it also does not specify the spatial domain or boundary conditions.

Requested mathematical target. Determine the stability or instability of the solitary-wave solutions of this equation throughout the intended parameter and solution family.

This target remains underdetermined by the supplied record. In particular, it does not define a solitary wave, specify whether the waves are stationary or traveling, prescribe their asymptotic background or decay, or identify the perturbation class and notion of stability. Spectral, linear, nonlinear, orbital, and asymptotic stability are distinct possible targets, and none is selected here. A precise quantified stability assertion requires these choices, including the solution regularity, topology, time direction, and treatment of any symmetries. No particular choice of these missing hypotheses is imposed in this draft.

Short English statement

Determine which solitary waves of the displayed Lugiato--Lefever equation are stable or unstable. The supplied record leaves the parameter regime, spatial setting, solitary-wave family, and meaning of stability unspecified, so it does not determine a unique precise stability problem.
