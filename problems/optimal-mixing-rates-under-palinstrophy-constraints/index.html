Optimal Mixing Rates Under Palinstrophy Constraints

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns passive-scalar mixing under a uniform palinstrophy or Sobolev $H^2$ budget. It does not specify enough data to determine a unique optimization problem. The following notation describes its mathematical content conditionally, without selecting the missing hypotheses.

Let $\Omega$ denote a spatial domain whose dimension, geometry, and boundary conditions remain unspecified. Under the interpretation of passive-scalar transport as transport without diffusion, a scalar $\theta$ and velocity $u$ satisfy \[ \partial_t\theta+u\cdot\nabla\theta=0, \qquad \theta(0,\cdot)=\theta_0. \] The initial datum $\theta_0$, the solution concept, the regularity of $u$, and whether $\nabla\cdot u=0$ is required are not supplied. This equation is therefore a conditional interpretation, not a complete prescribed initial-boundary value problem.

On a Euclidean domain, the full Sobolev norm for a vector field can be written as \[ \|u\|_{H^2(\Omega)}^2 =\sum_{|\alpha|\leq 2}\int_\Omega |D^\alpha u(x)|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] A uniform full $H^2$ budget would take the form \[ \sup_{t\geq0}\|u(t,\cdot)\|_{H^2(\Omega)}\leq B, \qquad 0<B<\infty. \] For comparison, in two spatial dimensions, with $\omega=\partial_1u_2-\partial_2u_1$, the usual palinstrophy quantity is, up to normalization, \[ \mathcal P(u)=\int_\Omega |\nabla\omega|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] A uniform bound on this quantity and a uniform bound on the full $H^2$ norm are distinct formulations unless additional hypotheses justify their relationship. The record does not specify the dimension, normalization, boundary conditions, or control of lower-order components needed to resolve its use of these terms.

For a chosen scalar norm $\|\cdot\|_X$, write \[ M_X(t)=\|\theta(t,\cdot)\|_X. \] Here $X$ is a placeholder for the unspecified mix-norm; the record does not determine whether the scalar must first have its mean removed or satisfy a mean-zero condition.

The requested problem is to determine the sharp asymptotic decay of the specified mix-norm as $t\to\infty$, among scalar evolutions driven by velocities satisfying the intended uniform budget, and to decide whether the optimal decay is polynomial or exponential. A precise quantified statement still requires the domain, boundary conditions, admissible velocities and scalar data, exact budget, and exact mix-norm. It must also specify whether optimization permits a different velocity for each terminal time, requires one velocity to attain the asymptotic rate, and requires bounds uniform over an initial-data class. No choice among these formulations is made here.

Short English statement

Determine the fastest possible long-time mixing of a passively transported scalar when the velocity has a uniform palinstrophy or $H^2$ budget, including whether the sharp decay is polynomial or exponential. The supplied record leaves the domain, mix-norm, admissible data, budget interpretation, and optimization quantifiers unresolved, so it does not yet specify a unique mathematical problem.
