Squarefree Kernel Counts Outside the Established Range

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual interpretation of squarefree kernel as the product of the distinct prime divisors, put \[ k(n)=\prod_{p\mid n}p\quad(n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}),\qquad k(1)=1, \] where the product is over primes. Define \[ N(x,y)=\#\{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}:n\leq x,\ k(n)\leq y\}. \] The positive-integer counting domain and the product-of-distinct-primes convention make explicit the customary interpretation; the supplied record does not expressly define either convention.

For sufficiently large real $x$, write the source's region as \[ H_{\varepsilon}=\left\{(x,y):\exp\left((\log\log x)^{\varepsilon}\right)\leq y\leq x\right\}, \] where $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm. The source asks for the asymptotic behavior of $N(x,y)$ outside $H_{\varepsilon}$, apart from a known small-$y$ range.

This is an incompletely specified asymptotic request. The record supplies neither the admissible values and quantification of $\varepsilon$, nor the ambient parameter domain whose complement is intended, nor the boundaries of the excluded small-$y$ range. It also leaves unspecified how $y$ may depend on $x$, the required uniformity, and the intended form of an asymptotic estimate. Consequently, the displayed request cannot be made into a uniquely quantified asymptotic assertion from this record alone. The descriptions of established and known ranges are supplied source qualifications, not independently verified results.

Short English statement

Count positive integers up to $x$ whose product of distinct prime divisors is at most $y$. The source asks for asymptotics beyond the displayed established region, excluding a known small-$y$ range. The omitted parameter conditions and exclusion boundaries require clarification before a precise asymptotic target can be stated.
