Analytic Structures for Nonformal Deformation Quantization

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A formal deformation quantization replaces multiplication by $f\star g=fg+\sum_{j\geq1}\hbar^j C_j(f,g)$ in formal power series, with the first-order commutator encoding the Poisson bracket. Non-formal quantization instead requires actual parameter values and analytic control of products or representations. A Hilbert algebra combines an involutive algebra with a compatible inner product; a von Neumann algebra is a weakly closed operator algebra. These analytic structures require domains, positivity and completion conventions.

Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
What are the essential ingredients of a non-formal deformation quantization, including the associated Hilbert-algebra or von Neumann-algebra structure?
Source scope and qualification.
This is a foundations programme, not a claim that every Poisson manifold admits a specified strict quantization. The desired notion of non-formal quantization must be made explicit in any proposed answer.

Short English statement

Identify the analytic ingredients that turn formal deformation quantization into an actual Hilbert- or operator-algebra theory.

A concrete nonformal torus algebra.
For $k,l\in\mathbb Z^2$ write $\omega(k,l)=k_1l_2-k_2l_1$. On finitely supported sequences define \[ (a\star_\hbar b)_n=\sum_{k+l=n}a_kb_l e^{\pi i\hbar\omega(k,l)},\qquad a^*_n=\overline{a_{-n}},\quad\hbar\in\mathbb R. \] Bilinearity of $\omega$ gives $\omega(k,l)+\omega(k+l,r)=\omega(l,r)+\omega(k,l+r)$, proving associativity. Antisymmetry verifies $(a\star b)^*=b^*\star a^*$. The coefficient at zero defines $\tau(a)=a_0$ and $\tau(a^*\star a)=\sum_k|a_k|^2\geq0$.
An actual operator completion.
Left multiplication by a basis element is a shift with phases of modulus one on $\ell^2(\mathbb Z^2)$, hence unitary. By the triangle inequality, $\|L_a\|\leq\sum_k|a_k|$ for finite support, extending to $\ell^1$ coefficients. The weak operator closure of the represented unital $*$-algebra is therefore a concrete von Neumann algebra. All formulas make sense at each real $\hbar$, with no convergence of a formal power series required. At $\hbar=0$ the product is ordinary convolution of Fourier coefficients.
Scope.
This elementary twisted-group-algebra realization supplies positivity, domains and bounded representations for one noncommutative-torus example. It does not show that a general formal star product admits such a norm, positive trace or analytic completion, nor classify the appropriate structures for arbitrary Poisson manifolds. The first gap is controlling the proposed product in a topology admitting faithful positive representations beyond this special Fourier setting.
