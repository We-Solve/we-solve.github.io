The Twisted Fourier–Jacobi Gan–Gross–Prasad Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a local or global field, and let $E$ and $K$ be quadratic étale $F$-algebras. Write $a\mapsto\bar a$ for the canonical involution of $E/F$. Under the usual interpretation of a skew-Hermitian space, $V$ is a finite-dimensional $E$-module with a nondegenerate sesquilinear form $h$ satisfying \[ h(av,bw)=a\,h(v,w)\,\bar b, \qquad h(w,v)=-\overline{h(v,w)}. \] Its unitary group consists of the $E$-linear automorphisms preserving $h$. Interpret \[ V_K=V\otimes_F K \] as a skew-Hermitian space over $E\otimes_F K$, with the scalar-extended form and involution $\bar{\phantom{a}}\otimes\operatorname{id}_K$. Scalar extension gives the natural inclusion of $U(V)$ into the group denoted $U(V_K)$ in the source. These are conventional interpretations of notation that the supplied record does not explicitly define.

For local $F$, let $\Pi$ be an irreducible representation of $U(V_K)$, and let $\omega_{V,\psi,\mu}$ denote the Weil representation of $U(V)$ specified in the source. Once an appropriate representation category and the auxiliary data $\psi,\mu$ have been fixed, define the branching space and its multiplicity by \[ \mathcal H_V(\Pi;\psi,\mu) =\operatorname{Hom}_{U(V)} \left(\Pi|_{U(V)},\omega_{V,\psi,\mu}\right), \qquad m_V(\Pi;\psi,\mu) =\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\mathcal H_V(\Pi;\psi,\mu). \] Here the Hom space consists of intertwining maps in the chosen category; the record does not specify that category or establish finiteness of its dimension.

Requested local and global assertions. For the data to which the twisted Fourier--Jacobi Gan--Gross--Prasad conjectures apply, determine $m_V(\Pi;\psi,\mu)$ locally and determine the corresponding global period when $F$ is global, in accordance with the conjectural twisted GGP formulas. The supplied record does not reproduce those formulas or define the global period. It also omits the restrictions on the fields, representations, and auxiliary characters needed for their precise formulation. Consequently, this is a statement of the supplied research target, not a complete conjectural identity: neither a specific multiplicity formula nor a specific global period identity can be recovered from this record alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks for the twisted Fourier--Jacobi Gan--Gross--Prasad formulas describing the restriction of $\Pi$ from $U(V_K)$ to $U(V)$, measured against a Weil representation, and the corresponding global period. The supplied record omits the formulas and several necessary hypotheses, so the exact conjectures require human mathematical review.
