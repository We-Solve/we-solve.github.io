Semiorthogonal Decompositions in Algebraic Families

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal D$ be a triangulated category, with shift functor $[1]$. A finite semiorthogonal decomposition \[ \mathcal D=\langle\mathcal A_1,\ldots,\mathcal A_n\rangle, \qquad n\geq 1, \] consists of full triangulated subcategories that generate $\mathcal D$ as a triangulated category and satisfy \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(B,A[k])=0 \quad\text{for every }j>i,\ A\in\mathcal A_i,\ B\in\mathcal A_j,\ k\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] A full triangulated subcategory is called admissible if its inclusion has both a left adjoint and a right adjoint. Whether admissibility is required in the intended decompositions is not specified by the source record.

Source question. How do semiorthogonal decompositions of derived categories behave in algebraic families?

This is a broad research question rather than a specified universally quantified conjecture. The supplied statement does not identify the objects parametrized by a family, the ground field, the base, hypotheses on the family, or the version of the derived category. In particular, it does not choose between bounded derived categories of coherent sheaves and categories of perfect complexes. It also does not specify the intended meaning of behavior in families, such as extension of a decomposition from a fiber, specialization, compatibility with base change, or a moduli description. These are possible interpretations, not additional assertions of the source. Consequently, no precise extension, specialization, or classification assertion can be extracted without further hypotheses and a specified target.

Short English statement

The question asks how semiorthogonal decompositions vary in algebraic families. Such a decomposition expresses a triangulated category through ordered subcategories with vanishing morphisms in one direction. The source leaves both the class of families and the desired behavior unspecified, so this draft preserves the research question and records the missing specifications.
