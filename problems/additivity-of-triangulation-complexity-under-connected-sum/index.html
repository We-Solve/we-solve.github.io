Math-6558

Additivity of Triangulation Complexity under Connected Sum

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix an integer $d\geq 4$. For a finite simplicial triangulation $\Delta$ of a compact $(d-1)$-dimensional manifold $M$, let $f_j(\Delta)$ denote its number of $j$-dimensional faces, with $f_{-1}(\Delta)=1$. Define its $h$-vector by \[ \sum_{i=0}^{d}h_i(\Delta)t^{d-i} =\sum_{i=0}^{d}f_{i-1}(\Delta)(t-1)^{d-i}. \] Let $f_0^{\mathrm{int}}(\Delta)$ be the number of vertices whose images lie in $M\setminus\partial M$. Write $|\Delta|\cong M$ for a triangulation whose geometric realization is homeomorphic to $M$.

For a manifold with boundary, the supplied record gives the definition \[ \Gamma(M) =\min_{|\Delta|\cong M} \left(h_2(\Delta)-h_{d-1}(\Delta)-d\,h_d(\Delta)\right) =\min_{|\Delta|\cong M} \left(h_2(\Delta)-f_0^{\mathrm{int}}(\Delta)\right). \] The equality of these two minimum formulas is part of the supplied formulation. The record does not explain why it holds for the entire stated class of compact manifolds with boundary, or identify any additional hypotheses needed for it. This draft therefore retains the asserted equality as a source qualification requiring review, rather than establishing that the two expressions define the same invariant. The record also does not specify a category of triangulations or hypotheses ensuring that the triangulation class is nonempty and that the minima exist as finite values.

For a closed manifold, the supplied extension is \[ \Gamma(M)=\Gamma(M\setminus B^{d-1}). \] Here the notation for the removed ball is unresolved. To obtain a compact manifold with boundary, the customary puncturing construction removes the interior of an embedded closed $(d-1)$-ball. The record does not explicitly specify this convention, the embedding category, or independence of the choice of ball.

Supplied additivity question. For compact manifolds $M_1,M_2$ of the same dimension $d-1$, with $d\geq4$, does \[ \Gamma(M_1\mathbin{\#}M_2) =\Gamma(M_1)+\Gamma(M_2) \] hold for every admissible pair? The intended class of admissible pairs and the meaning of $\#$ require clarification: the record does not specify whether the target concerns closed manifolds, manifolds with boundary, or both, nor whether a boundary case uses interior connected sum or boundary connected sum. Connectedness, orientability, and gluing conventions are likewise unstated. Thus the universal target is preserved, but its precise domain and the well-definedness of $\Gamma$ remain subject to the qualifications above.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether a minimum triangulation quantity $\Gamma$ is additive under connected sum in dimensions at least three. It gives two asserted formulas for $\Gamma$ and extends it to closed manifolds by puncturing. The equivalence of the formulas, admissible triangulations, puncturing convention, and scope of connected sum require clarification. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
