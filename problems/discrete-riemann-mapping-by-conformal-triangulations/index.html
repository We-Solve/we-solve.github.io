Math-14489

Discrete Riemann Mapping by Conformal Triangulations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{C}}$ be a Jordan domain, meaning the bounded domain enclosed by a Jordan curve, and let $p,q,r\in\partial\Omega$ be pairwise distinct. For each mesh size $\varepsilon>0$, the source prescribes a maximal simply connected region $\Omega_\varepsilon\subset\Omega$ triangulated by equilateral Euclidean triangles of side length $\varepsilon$, with boundary vertices $p_\varepsilon,q_\varepsilon,r_\varepsilon$ satisfying \[ |p_\varepsilon-p|\leq 2\varepsilon,\qquad |q_\varepsilon-q|\leq 2\varepsilon,\qquad |r_\varepsilon-r|\leq 2\varepsilon. \] Here the distances are Euclidean. The admissible class relative to which the region is maximal is not specified in the supplied record.

For a triangulated region with disk topology, its metric double is obtained from two isometric copies by identifying corresponding boundary points, equipped with the induced intrinsic length metric. Denote the resulting doubled surface by $S_\varepsilon$, and denote the images of the three selected boundary vertices by $P_\varepsilon,Q_\varepsilon,R_\varepsilon$. On a Euclidean polyhedral surface, the curvature at a vertex $v$ is its angle defect \[ K(v)=2\pi-\sum_{f\ni v}\theta_f(v), \] where $\theta_f(v)$ is the angle of an incident triangular face at $v$.

The source asks for a discrete conformal uniformization of $S_\varepsilon$ to a Euclidean polyhedral sphere with metric $\widehat d_\varepsilon$ such that \[ \operatorname{Area}(S_\varepsilon,\widehat d_\varepsilon)=\frac32, \qquad K(P_\varepsilon)=K(Q_\varepsilon)=K(R_\varepsilon)=\frac{4\pi}{3}, \] with zero curvature at every other vertex. The supplied record does not define the discrete conformal equivalence relation or specify the existence and uniqueness hypotheses for this uniformization; these remain part of the unresolved formulation.

Conjecture as supplied, with unresolved qualifications. For every Jordan domain and every triple of distinct boundary points as above, do the resulting uniformized polyhedral spheres converge, as $\varepsilon\to0$, to the metric double of the unit equilateral triangle?

The source does not specify the notion of convergence, whether markings or identifications with the original domain are retained, or whether the assertion must hold for every admissible choice of triangulations and boundary vertices. Its prescription for every $\varepsilon>0$ also lacks an existence qualification for the approximating regions; a formulation restricted to sufficiently small admissible mesh sizes would require clarification.

There is an additional normalization issue: if "unit equilateral triangle" means side length $1$, its metric double has Euclidean area $\sqrt{3}/2$, whereas the prescribed approximating spheres have area $3/2$. The intended meaning of "unit," the area normalization, and the convergence notion must therefore be clarified together. Both source normalizations are retained here without correction.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture starts with equilateral triangulations inside a Jordan domain, doubles each triangulated region across its boundary, and applies discrete conformal uniformization so that curvature is concentrated equally at three selected boundary points. It asks whether these spheres converge to a doubled equilateral triangle. The meaning of maximal approximation, discrete conformality, convergence, and the target scale remains unresolved. The source labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
