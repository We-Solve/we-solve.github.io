Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.107

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $S_H$ consist of injective, sense-preserving harmonic functions on $\mathbb D$ with the expansion \[f(z)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_nz^n+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{-n}\overline z^{\,n},\qquad a_1=1.\] Harmonicity means that the real and imaginary parts satisfy the planar Laplace equation. Writing $f=h+\overline g$ with $h,g$ holomorphic, sense preservation is $|h'(z)|^2-|g'(z)|^2>0$ throughout $\mathbb D$. The subclass $S_H^0$ is defined by $a_{-1}=0$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every $f\in S_H^0$ and integer $n\geq2$, does \[\left||a_n|-|a_{-n}|\right|\leq n\] hold? The outer absolute value is part of the assertion. [S2] [S3]

Short English statement

Is the absolute difference between the magnitudes of the analytic and coanalytic nth coefficients at most n?
