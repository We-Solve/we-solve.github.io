Integral Geometrization with Nilpotent Singular Support

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied formulation is a categorical geometrization conjecture in the local setting of the cited report. Its statement depends on conventions and hypotheses omitted from the supplied passage; the following records its intended assertion while keeping these omissions explicit.

Let $G$ be quasi-split, let $B\subseteq G$ be a Borel subgroup, and let $U$ be its unipotent radical. Write $E$ for the field occurring in $U(E)$, $\ell$ for the coefficient prime, and $\widehat G$ for the dual group. The supplied record does not specify the hypotheses on $E$, the full assumptions on $G$, or the restrictions on $\ell$.

Let $\psi$ be the nondegenerate character used to construct the Whittaker sheaf. The extracted passage prints $\psi:U(E)\to\mathbb Z_\ell$, without specifying a group law or resolving whether the coefficient target has been corrupted. Consequently, the coefficient-valued character and the precise meaning of nondegeneracy remain to be fixed from the report. Subject to those conventions, its displayed construction is \[ W_\psi=(i_1)_!\left(\operatorname{c\!\operatorname{-}Ind}_{U(E)}^{G(E)}\psi\right). \] Here compact induction is the construction indicated by the source, and $i_1$ is the map used there to place the resulting object on $\operatorname{Bun}_G$. The supplied passage does not define $i_1$, its domain, or the sheaf-theoretic realization of compact induction.

Put \[ \mathcal X=\operatorname{LocSys}_{\widehat G}/\mathbb Z_\ell, \qquad \mathcal D=D_{\mathrm{lis}}(\operatorname{Bun}_G,\mathbb Z_\ell). \] These denote the spectral parameter stack and the automorphic category of the report; their constructions and categorical conventions are not supplied. Let $\operatorname{Perf}(\mathcal X)$ denote its category of perfect complexes, and write $F*A$ for the supplied spectral action on $\mathcal D$. The notation $\operatorname{Coh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal X)$ means the source's category of coherent complexes satisfying the Arinkin--Gaitsgory singular-support condition \[ \operatorname{SS}(F)\subseteq\operatorname{Nilp}, \] where $\operatorname{Nilp}$ is its nilpotent cone in the space governing singular support. The source identifies the zero-section support condition with the perfect subcategory. The precise derived model, support space, and conventions for the coherent category are not specified in the record.

Conjectural assertion. With all these objects interpreted in the conventions of the report, the functor \[ \Phi_0:\operatorname{Perf}(\mathcal X)\longrightarrow\mathcal D, \qquad F\longmapsto F*W_\psi, \] extends along the inclusion of perfect complexes to an equivalence \[ \Phi:\operatorname{Coh}_{\mathrm{Nilp}}(\mathcal X) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\mathcal D \] compatible with the spectral action. The source further states that the nilpotent singular-support condition becomes automatic after inverting $\ell$, whereas it is needed integrally. This qualification is retained as a source assertion; its precise scope depends on the omitted categorical conventions. These omissions prevent treating the displayed formulation as a fully specified standalone conjecture.

Short English statement

The conjecture says that acting on a Whittaker sheaf should identify the integral spectral category with nilpotent singular support with the automorphic category on $\operatorname{Bun}_G$, compatibly with the spectral action. The source says the support restriction becomes automatic after inverting $\ell$. Field hypotheses, character coefficients, and category conventions require clarification. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved while describing the full equivalence as conjectural; this draft does not independently verify either assessment.
