Conformal nets and representation equivalence for strongly rational unitary VOAs

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Simple strongly rational unitary VOAs are claimed to generate completely rational nets, with unitary modules and a braided unitary equivalence to finite-index sectors.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Take a vertex operator algebra $V$ over $\mathbb C$; require simplicity, unitarity and strong rationality. Then the source claims polynomial energy bounds and strong locality of the smeared fields, producing an irreducible conformal net $A_V$. It also asserts complete unitarity of $V$, strong integrability for unitary grading-restricted finite-length modules, and complete rationality of $A_V$. With invariant positive Hermitian forms on simple modules, the representation categories are claimed to obey \[ \operatorname{Rep}(V)\simeq\operatorname{Rep}^{u}(V) \xrightarrow{F_{\mathrm{CWX}}^{V}}\operatorname{Rep}^{f}(A_V), \] a braided unitary tensor equivalence. The net category uses Connes fusion and DHR braiding with matching circle orientation. Strong rationality here includes simplicity, CFT type, self-contragredience, rationality, and $C_2$-cofiniteness; the source does not extend this theorem to every unitary VOA.

Outline of the argument

Energy bounds establish strong locality of the generating fields. Annular charged-field estimates integrate modules, positive fusion forms give unitary tensor structure, and rationality identifies net sectors; the cited extension theorems then relate local net extensions to conformal VOA extensions.
