Math-898

Conditions Imposed by Fat Points on Curve Systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem does not specify the ambient space, the ground field, or whether the linear system is complete. Consequently, its numerical target can be expressed precisely only relative to these additional data.

For any specified geometric setting in which curves of degree $d$ are represented by sections of a line bundle $L_d$ on an ambient space $X$ over a field $k$, let $Z=\{Q_1,\ldots,Q_s\}$ be the prescribed finite set. If multiplicities are prescribed, write $a_j\geq 1$ for the multiplicity at $Q_j$; otherwise put $a_j=1$. Define the base subscheme $B$ by \[ \mathcal I_B=\bigcap_{j=1}^{s}\mathcal I_{Q_j}^{a_j}. \] Represent the given linear system by $\mathbb P(V)$, where $V$ is a specified finite-dimensional $k$-linear subspace of $H^0(X,L_d\otimes\mathcal I_B)$. The complete system corresponds to equality, but completeness is not stated in the source.

For a specified number $r\geq 1$ and specified positive integers $m_1,\ldots,m_r$, a collection of fat points supported at distinct points $P_1,\ldots,P_r$ is the subscheme $F$ with \[ \mathcal I_F=\bigcap_{i=1}^{r}\mathcal I_{P_i}^{m_i}. \] The number of independent conditions that $F$ imposes on $V$ is \[ c_V(F)=\operatorname{rank}_k\!\left(V\longrightarrow H^0(F,L_d|_F)\right) =\dim_k V-\dim_k\!\left(V\cap H^0(X,L_d\otimes\mathcal I_F)\right). \] In particular, imposing both sets of conditions uses $\mathcal I_B\cap\mathcal I_F$, including when their supports overlap.

Problem, conditional on specifying the missing geometric data. Determine $c_V(F)$ for general choices of the supports $P_i$, as a function of the degree, the prescribed base conditions, the chosen linear system, and the fat-point multiplicities. Here a general choice means a choice in a nonempty Zariski-open subset of the specified irreducible parameter space of allowed configurations. The intended parameter space, any restriction excluding $Z$, and the permitted geometric data must be supplied before this becomes a unique classification problem. This formulation records the requested rank; it does not assert that the rank equals an expected dimension.

Short English statement

Determine how many independent linear conditions general fat points impose on a fixed-degree system of curves already passing through a prescribed finite set $Z$, possibly with specified multiplicities at $Z$. The ambient geometry, field, admissible configurations, and precise linear system remain unspecified in the supplied record.
