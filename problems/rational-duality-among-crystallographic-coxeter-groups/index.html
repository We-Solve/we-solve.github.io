Math-3877

Rational Duality among Crystallographic Coxeter Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system, so that \[ W=\langle S\mid (st)^{m_{st}}=1\text{ whenever }m_{st}<\infty\rangle, \] where $m_{ss}=1$ and $m_{st}=m_{ts}\in\{2,3,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}$ for distinct $s,t\in S$. An entry $m_{st}=\infty$ imposes no relation on $st$.

Regard $W$ as a discrete group and write ${\mathbb{Q}} W$ for its group algebra. The rational cohomological dimension $\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(W)$ is the projective dimension of the trivial left ${\mathbb{Q}} W$-module ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The group is of type $\mathrm{FP}$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ if ${\mathbb{Q}}$ has a finite resolution by finitely generated projective left ${\mathbb{Q}} W$-modules. In the standard discrete-group sense, a rational duality group of dimension $d$ is a group of type $\mathrm{FP}$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ such that \[ \operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(W)=d,\qquad H^i(W,{\mathbb{Q}} W)=0\quad\text{for every }i\geq0\text{ with }i\neq d, \qquad H^d(W,{\mathbb{Q}} W)\neq0. \] Here $H^i(W,M)=\operatorname{Ext}^i_{{\mathbb{Q}} W}({\mathbb{Q}},M)$, with ${\mathbb{Q}} W$ carrying its left regular action. No requirement that $H^d(W,{\mathbb{Q}} W)$ be one-dimensional is imposed.

Classification problem. Determine all crystallographic Coxeter groups $W$ that satisfy these rational duality conditions for some integer $d\geq1$, and determine the corresponding dimension $d$.

Unresolved scope qualification. The supplied record does not define "crystallographic" or specify a rank convention. A lattice-based interpretation uses a faithful real reflection realization preserving a full lattice, meaning a discrete subgroup spanning its finite-dimensional real representation space. The record does not identify the intended realization or establish that this interpretation is its convention. Thus the rational duality condition above is explicit, but the precise class of Coxeter groups to which the classification applies requires confirmation from the source. No finite-rank, irreducibility, affine, or hyperbolic restriction is added here.

Short English statement

Classify the crystallographic Coxeter groups whose rational group-ring cohomology is nonzero in exactly one degree $d\geq1$, subject to the finite projective resolution condition stated above. The intended meaning and rank scope of "crystallographic" require source confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
