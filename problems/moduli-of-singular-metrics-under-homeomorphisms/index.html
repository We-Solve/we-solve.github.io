Math-15063

Moduli of Singular Metrics Under Homeomorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For context, if $M$ is a smooth manifold, let $\operatorname{Riem}(M)$ denote the set of smooth positive-definite symmetric covariant $2$-tensor fields on $M$, and let $\operatorname{Diff}(M)$ denote its group of smooth diffeomorphisms. The usual equivalence relation on metrics is \[ g_1\sim g_2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad g_1=\phi^*g_2\text{ for some }\phi\in\operatorname{Diff}(M), \] with quotient $\operatorname{Riem}(M)/\operatorname{Diff}(M)$. This is contextual notation; the source does not specify a manifold, dimension, compactness assumption, or boundary condition.

The supplied research question is: Can diffeomorphisms be replaced by homeomorphisms and Riemannian metrics by singular metrics in the study of moduli spaces?

A schematic formulation would require a specified underlying topological space $X$, a specified class $\mathcal S(X)$ of singular metric structures, and a rule for pulling these structures back by every element of $\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$, the group of self-homeomorphisms of $X$. If such a rule is defined and preserves $\mathcal S(X)$, the proposed equivalence relation would be \[ s\sim t\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s=h^*t\text{ for some }h\in\operatorname{Homeo}(X), \qquad s,t\in\mathcal S(X), \] and the corresponding quotient would be $\mathcal S(X)/\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$.

The question asks whether an appropriate moduli theory can be developed in this setting. The source does not define singular metrics, the admissible spaces $X$, the pullback rule, the relevant topologies, or the properties that such a theory must establish. In particular, existence of the displayed orbit set alone does not specify the intended mathematical goal. These choices remain unresolved, so the supplied question is a research programme rather than a fully quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

Can the study of metric moduli spaces be extended to singular metric structures, with equivalence given by homeomorphisms? The source leaves the allowed singularities, underlying spaces, topology, and desired conclusions unspecified, preventing a unique precise conjecture.
