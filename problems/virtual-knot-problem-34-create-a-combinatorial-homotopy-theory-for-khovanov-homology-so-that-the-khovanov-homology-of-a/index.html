Virtual-knot problem 34 — Create a combinatorial homotopy theory for Khovanov homology so that the Khovanov homology of a knot or link is equiv…

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Kh}^{i,j}(L;R)$ denote Khovanov homology of a classical link $L$, with homological degree $i$, quantum degree $j$, and coefficient ring $R$. The source does not fix $R$, reduced versus unreduced theory, or grading conventions.

Problem-list item 34 asks for a combinatorial homotopy theory assigning to each knot or link an abstract complex or category whose homotopy type refines and recovers its Khovanov homology, in the spirit of Bar-Natan's categorical chain-complex reformulation. The assignment should be invariant up to the appropriate homotopy equivalence under the knot or link equivalences, and should also work for virtual knots and links. The source seeks a deeper connection than the progress it attributes to Lifshitz and Sarkar.

A possible form of the recovery requirement, if the chosen model is a family of pointed spaces or spectra $X_j(L)$ and uses cohomology, is \[ \widetilde H^i(X_j(L);R)\cong \operatorname{Kh}^{i,j}(L;R). \] This is an illustrative interpretation, not a specific model or normalization prescribed by the source. A graded homology group is not literally a homotopy type; the source's word "equivalent" needs a specified recovery or comparison construction. In particular, the request must specify what additional structure constitutes the desired deeper theory, and which virtual-link version of Khovanov homology and which virtual moves it should respect.

Short English statement

Construct a combinatorial homotopy refinement of Khovanov homology for knots and links, including virtual ones, with an explicit procedure recovering the graded homology and a precise notion of invariance.
