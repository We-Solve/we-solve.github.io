Packing and Covering for Infinite Matroids

OpenAI's claim

Unrestricted infinite matroid obstruction: Two self-dual partitional matroids on a countable common ground set are claimed to admit no witness for intersection and no partition satisfying packing/covering. They lie outside the finitary and cofinitary classes.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
In ZFC the claimed construction gives two matroids $M_0,M_1$ on a countably infinite set $E$, each equal to its dual on that same labelled ground set, for which \[I_0\cup I_1\ne E\quad\text{for every }I_i\text{ independent in }M_i.\] No partition $E=P\mathbin{\dot\cup}C$ has disjoint spanning sets in the restrictions to $P$ and independent sets covering $C$ in the contractions to $C$. The intersection corollary also states that, for every common independent $J$ and partition $J=J_0\mathbin{\dot\cup}J_1$, $\operatorname{cl}_{M_0}(J_0)\cup\operatorname{cl}_{M_1}(J_1)\ne E$.

Outline of the argument

Unrestricted infinite matroid obstruction: An ultrafilter carries an ordinal-valued rank on large subsets. A two-sided self-dual matroid is built from rapidly growing blocks and probe limits so that any relevant basis forces a strict rank decrease from one side to the other. Alternating copies along an infinite bundle path would turn a supposed covering into an infinite descending ordinal chain.
