Median Actions and Kazhdan Property for Brin Group 2V

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C=\{0,1\}^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ be the Cantor space with its product topology. For a finite binary word $u$, let $uC$ denote the cylinder consisting of infinite binary sequences beginning with $u$. Brin's higher-dimensional Thompson group $2V$ can be described as the group of homeomorphisms of $C^2$ given by finite prefix replacements: there are two finite partitions \[ C^2=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{m}(u_iC\times v_iC) =\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{m}(p_iC\times q_iC) \] such that the homeomorphism sends \[ (u_i\alpha,v_i\beta)\longmapsto(p_i\alpha,q_i\beta) \qquad(\alpha,\beta\in C). \] The finite words may be empty, and the group operation is composition.

For a connected, undirected graph $M$, equip its vertex set with the graph distance $d_M$, and define \[ I(x,y)=\{z\in V(M):d_M(x,z)+d_M(z,y)=d_M(x,y)\}. \] The graph is a median graph if, for every three vertices $x,y,z$, the intersection \[ I(x,y)\cap I(y,z)\cap I(z,x) \] contains exactly one vertex. Interpret an action on a median graph as an action by graph automorphisms. Such an action has an unbounded orbit if \[ \exists x\in V(M)\quad \sup_{g\in 2V}d_M(x,gx)=\infty. \] For an action on a connected graph, unboundedness of one vertex orbit implies unboundedness of every vertex orbit. No local finiteness, finite-dimensionality, or properness condition is imposed in the supplied question.

A discrete group $G$ has Kazhdan's property $(T)$ if every unitary representation $\pi:G\to\mathcal U(\mathcal H)$ on a complex Hilbert space that has almost invariant unit vectors also has a nonzero invariant vector. Here almost invariant unit vectors means \[ \forall F\subseteq G\text{ finite}\;\forall\varepsilon>0\;\exists\xi\in\mathcal H: \quad \|\xi\|=1,\qquad \max_{g\in F}\|\pi(g)\xi-\xi\|<\varepsilon, \] with the condition on $F$ understood vacuously when $F$ is empty. An invariant vector $\eta\ne0$ satisfies $\pi(g)\eta=\eta$ for every $g\in G$.

Questions. For Brin's group $2V$:

  1. Does there exist a nonempty connected median graph $M$ and an action $2V\curvearrowright M$ by graph automorphisms with an unbounded vertex orbit?
  2. Does $2V$ have Kazhdan's property $(T)$?
These are retained as two questions from the supplied cleaned statement; no equivalence between them is asserted. The supplied literature assessment specifically identifies the property-$(T)$ question as part of Problem 9(i). The raw excerpt begins with an unresolved pronoun and then includes other problems, so the identification of its subject with $2V$, and the inclusion of the first question, rely on the supplied cleaned statement and curation.

Short English statement

The questions ask whether Brin's group $2V$ can act on a median graph with an unbounded orbit, and whether every unitary representation of $2V$ with almost invariant unit vectors must have a nonzero invariant vector. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
