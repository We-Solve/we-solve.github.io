Several Complex Variables Problem List — Problem 71

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{P}}^k({\mathbb{C}})$ denote complex projective space of complex dimension $k$. Consider a holomorphic self-map $f\colon{\mathbb{P}}^k({\mathbb{C}})\to{\mathbb{P}}^k({\mathbb{C}})$ and a fixed point $p$, so that $f(p)=p$. The source also asks for the local version, in which $f$ is defined only on an open neighborhood $U$ of $p$ in ${\mathbb{P}}^k({\mathbb{C}})$ and satisfies $f(p)=p$. In local coordinates centered at $p$, this gives a holomorphic germ $F\colon({\mathbb{C}}^k,0)\to({\mathbb{C}}^k,0)$ with $F(0)=0$. Two representatives define the same germ if they agree on some neighborhood of $0$. Iteration is considered wherever the successive compositions are defined; no invariant neighborhood is assumed.

Classification problem. Classify the dynamics near the fixed point $p$ of such a holomorphic map, both for maps defined on all of ${\mathbb{P}}^k({\mathbb{C}})$ and for maps defined only near $p$.

Unresolved scope. The source does not specify a range of dimensions $k$, an equivalence relation for the classification, or the dynamical features to be classified. In particular, it does not say whether classification is intended up to holomorphic conjugacy, topological conjugacy, or another relation. It imposes no stated hypotheses on the derivative at the fixed point or on local invertibility. The local formulation above interprets the neighborhood version as a holomorphic self-map germ of the same ambient complex manifold; the local codomain is not separately stated in the source. Thus the supplied request is a broad classification program rather than a uniquely specified theorem or conjecture.

Short English statement

Describe and classify the behavior near a fixed point of a holomorphic map of complex projective space, including the case where the map is defined only near that point. The source leaves the meaning and scope of classification unspecified. Its supplied status is open; that label has not been independently verified.
