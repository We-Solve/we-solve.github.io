Kähler invariance of plurigenera: current boundary and a pseudoeffective-chamber reduction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Delta=\{t\in{\mathbb{C}}:|t|<1\}$, and interpret a holomorphic family as a proper holomorphic submersion $\pi:\mathcal X\to\Delta$ with compact connected Kähler fibers $X_t$ of complex dimension $n$. For every integer $m\geq1$, define the plurigenus \[ P_m(t)=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}H^0(X_t,K_{X_t}^{\otimes m}). \] Siu's question asks whether $P_m(t)$ is independent of $t$ for every $m$. The original assumes Kähler fibers, not explicitly a Kähler total space. It points to the smooth projective invariance theorem, source reference [7], and explains the difficulty that an auxiliary ample line bundle used there need not be available in the Kähler case.

The attached aid reports the cases $m=1$, fiber dimensions at most two, and projective or fiberwise Moishezon families as settled. It additionally reports a March 2026 Hacon--Li--Rao preprint establishing invariance for smooth compact Kähler threefold families, while describing fiber dimensions at least four as still open in general. This is its dated literature assessment, without publication details or new source verification here.

For fixed $m$, write $h_m^q(t)=\dim H^q(X_t,K_{X_t}^{\otimes m})$. The aid reports constancy of the Euler characteristic in a smooth proper family and the exact jump identity relative to generic fiber values: \[ P_m(t)-P_m(\mathrm{gen})= \sum_{q=1}^n(-1)^{q+1}\left(h_m^q(t)-h_m^q(\mathrm{gen})\right). \] Thus a special plurigenus jump requires that alternating higher-cohomology jump. If $h_m^q(t_0)=0$ for all $q>0$ on one fiber, the aid concludes local constancy of $P_m$ near $t_0$, not automatically constancy on the entire disk.

A line bundle is pseudoeffective in the compact Kähler setting when its first Chern class has a closed positive-current representative. The aid conditionally assumes deformation invariance of pseudoeffectivity of $K_{X_t}$. It cites a proper holomorphic submersion with Kähler total space as a setting where this follows from results attributed to Fujiki--Levine and Ou. In such a family the non-pseudoeffective branch has \[ P_m(t)=0\quad\text{for every }m\geq1, \] reducing its unresolved part to the pseudoeffective chamber. The additional total-space hypothesis and exact scope of the named theorems are not supplied in full, so the reduction is not silently extended to every fiberwise Kähler family.

Short English statement

Are all plurigenera constant in smooth proper families of compact Kähler manifolds? The attached aid reports known lower-dimensional cases and a March 2026 threefold preprint, then gives a higher-cohomology jump identity and a conditional reduction to pseudoeffective canonical bundles, leaving its stated higher-dimensional gap.
