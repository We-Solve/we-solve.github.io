Let $\mathcal P=\{p\in{\mathbb{N}}:p\text{ is prime}\}$. The supplied record asks whether a characterization of the UCT problem remains valid when its quantifier over $\mathcal P$ is restricted to one prime, especially $2$. It does not supply the characterization, the prime-dependent condition, or a definition of the UCT problem in the intended setting.

Short English statement

The question asks whether testing the prime-dependent condition in a characterization of the UCT problem at just $p=2$, or at another fixed prime, suffices. The characterization and its hypotheses are absent from the supplied record, so the target can only be stated schematically pending source review.
