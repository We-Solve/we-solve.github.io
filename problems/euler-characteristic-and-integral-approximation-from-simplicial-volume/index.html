Math-1069

Euler Characteristic and Integral Approximation from Simplicial Volume

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be an oriented, closed, connected manifold of dimension $n$, where closed means compact without boundary. No dimension restriction is supplied. The manifold is aspherical if its universal covering space is contractible. For $A\in\{{\mathbb{Z}},{\mathbb{R}}\}$, write $[M]_A\in H_n(M;A)$ for its fundamental class. For a finite singular chain written with distinct singular simplices as $c=\sum_{j=1}^r a_j\sigma_j$, define $\lVert c\rVert_1=\sum_{j=1}^r|a_j|$. Define the real and integral simplicial volumes by \[ \lVert M\rVert=\inf\{\lVert c\rVert_1:c\in C_n(M;{\mathbb{R}}),\ \partial c=0,\ [c]=[M]_{{\mathbb{R}}}\}, \] \[ \lVert M\rVert_{{\mathbb{Z}}}=\inf\{\lVert c\rVert_1:c\in C_n(M;{\mathbb{Z}}),\ \partial c=0,\ [c]=[M]_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\}. \] The stable integral simplicial volume is \[ \lVert M\rVert_{{\mathbb{Z}}}^{\infty} =\inf_{p:N\to M}\frac{\lVert N\rVert_{{\mathbb{Z}}}}{\deg(p)}, \] where the infimum ranges over all connected finite-sheeted covering maps, including the identity covering, and $N$ carries the orientation induced from $M$, so that $\deg(p)$ is the positive number of sheets. Write \[ \chi(M)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H_k(M;{\mathbb{Q}}). \] A group $G$ is residually finite if, for every $g\in G\setminus\{1\}$, there is a homomorphism $q:G\to F$ to a finite group such that $q(g)\ne1$.

First question. For every oriented, closed, connected, aspherical manifold $M$, is the implication \[ \lVert M\rVert=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\chi(M)=0 \] valid?

Integral approximation question. For every such $M$ satisfying $\lVert M\rVert=0$ and having residually finite fundamental group, does \[ \lVert M\rVert_{{\mathbb{Z}}}^{\infty}=\lVert M\rVert=0 \] hold?

The supplied source describes the second question as "more strongly." Its vanishing hypothesis is retained from the opening sentence; no assertion about integral approximation for manifolds of positive simplicial volume is added. The source does not specify a manifold category or an explicit dimension range, and these qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

Must every oriented, closed, connected, aspherical manifold with zero real simplicial volume have zero Euler characteristic? Under the additional assumption that its fundamental group is residually finite, must its stable integral simplicial volume also vanish? Both questions are supplied as open; this draft does not independently verify their status.
