Math-5580

What is Σ_(n≥1 )φ(n)/2^(n), where φ(n) is the Euler phi (totient) function, counting the number of integers less than n

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\varphi(n)$ be Euler's totient function, \[ \varphi(n)=\#\{a:1\leq a\leq n,\ \gcd(a,n)=1\}, \] so $\varphi(1)=1$ and for $n\geq2$ this is the number of positive integers less than $n$ coprime to $n$. Define \[ S=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\varphi(n)}{2^n}. \] The series converges absolutely since $0\leq\varphi(n)\leq n$ and $\sum n2^{-n}<\infty$. The convention at $n=1$ matters for the numerical value and is fixed by the standard totient function.

Questions attributed to Erdős. Determine $S$, with a closed-form answer desired. Is $S$ irrational, that is, $S\notin{\mathbb{Q}}$? The closed-form request and irrationality question are separate: a numerical approximation or a different infinite-series representation does not by itself provide the requested closed form. The source specifies no class of permitted constants or operations for such a form.

The registry status is open and its classification is OPEN-TRIAGE. The supplied research summary instead claims irrationality known, says no simple elementary closed form is available, and recommends partial-progress wording. It tentatively names Erdős--Borwein--Chamayou with a question mark, later names Borwein--Chamayou, and refers to generalized continued-fraction irrational sums and an unspecified average-order expression. It supplies no precise theorem, article, or actual expression verifying those statements. Therefore the irrationality claim remains explicitly attributed and unverified; it is not treated as a settled conclusion of this draft.

Short English statement

Find a closed form for $\sum_{n\geq1}\varphi(n)/2^n$ and determine whether it is irrational. The standard convention is $\varphi(1)=1$. The summary claims irrationality known but gives tentative attribution and no verifiable theorem reference, conflicting with the open registry status.
