Multiplicity Formula for Elliptic Arthur Parameters

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks about parameters described as elliptic Arthur parameters of Ramanujan type. It uses an auxiliary symbol $\psi_N$, an assignment $\phi\mapsto\pi^{\psi_N}(\phi)$, coefficients $c_{\pi}^{\psi_N}$, and objects $S_\phi$, with $|S_\phi|$ denoting cardinality. The record does not define these objects or specify their ambient setting, so these descriptions preserve the source notation without supplying missing definitions.

The proposed identity is \[ c_{\pi^{\psi_N}(\phi)}^{\psi_N}=|S_\phi| \qquad\text{for every elliptic Arthur parameter $\phi$ of Ramanujan type.} \] The question is whether this identity holds for every such $\phi$ in the intended source setting. A fully specified mathematical formulation requires the underlying group and field, the local or global setting, the meaning of Ramanujan type and ellipticity, the admissible choice and quantification of $\psi_N$, the construction of $\pi^{\psi_N}(\phi)$, the definition and normalization of $c_{\pi}^{\psi_N}$, and the definition of $S_\phi$, including any quotient conventions and finiteness assumptions. None of these missing specifications is imposed here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the coefficient attached to $\pi^{\psi_N}(\phi)$ equals $|S_\phi|$ for every elliptic Arthur parameter of Ramanujan type. The supplied record labels this claim open, but does not provide the definitions and hypotheses needed for a self-contained formulation.
