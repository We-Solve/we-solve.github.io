Shadowing Without Bounded Distortion

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether a shadowing characterization for dissipative composition operators extends when bounded distortion is no longer assumed. It also asks specifically whether generalized hyperbolicity and shadowing remain equivalent. The precise underlying function space, measure-theoretic hypotheses, and characterization referred to as [1] are not reproduced in the record; consequently, the complete original formulation cannot be recovered from the supplied text alone.

The following definitions give a conditional formulation under the usual invertible Banach-space conventions. They do not establish that these are exactly the conventions of the cited report. Let $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ be a measure space, let $E$ be a Banach space of scalar-valued measurable functions modulo equality almost everywhere, and let $f\colon X\to X$ induce a well-defined bounded invertible composition operator \[ T=C_f\colon E\to E,\qquad C_f\varphi=\varphi\circ f. \] The record does not specify $E$, its scalar field, or the hypotheses on $f$ ensuring these properties. In a common invertible dissipative setting, there is a measurable wandering set $W$ such that the sets $f^n(W)$, $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, are pairwise disjoint modulo null sets and \[ X=\bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}}f^n(W)\quad\text{modulo null sets}. \] Any additional requirements on $W$, such as finiteness or positivity of its measure, must be recovered from the source.

For a bounded invertible operator $T$ on a Banach space $E$, call $T$ generalized hyperbolic if there are closed subspaces $M,N\subseteq E$, constants $K\geq1$ and $0<r<1$, such that \[ E=M\oplus N,\qquad T(M)\subseteq M,\qquad T^{-1}(N)\subseteq N, \] and, for every integer $n\geq0$, \[ \|T^n u\|\leq Kr^n\|u\|\quad(u\in M),\qquad \|T^{-n}v\|\leq Kr^n\|v\|\quad(v\in N). \] Under the two-sided convention, $T$ has the shadowing property if for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $\delta>0$ such that every sequence $(x_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}}\subseteq E$ satisfying \[ \|Tx_n-x_{n+1}\|<\delta\qquad(n\in{\mathbb{Z}}) \] admits $x\in E$ with \[ \|T^n x-x_n\|<\varepsilon\qquad(n\in{\mathbb{Z}}). \] The record does not specify whether this two-sided convention is intended.

With the original ambient hypotheses and conventions fixed, let $\mathcal D$ denote the intended class of dissipative composition operators after removal of the bounded-distortion hypothesis. The requested equivalence is \[ \text{For every }C_f\in\mathcal D,\qquad C_f\text{ is generalized hyperbolic} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad C_f\text{ has the shadowing property}. \] Here removal of the hypothesis permits operators without bounded distortion; it need not mean restricting to operators that fail bounded distortion. The source wording leaves this distinction unresolved. The additional question asks whether the full characterization of shadowing cited as [1] remains valid in this enlarged setting. Since that characterization and the identification of [1] are not explicitly supplied, this additional question cannot be stated as an explicit mathematical equivalence from the record alone.

Short English statement

For the dissipative composition operators intended in the source, does removing the bounded-distortion assumption preserve the equivalence between generalized hyperbolicity and shadowing? The original passage also asks whether an earlier full characterization of shadowing extends. The supplied record omits the exact ambient hypotheses and that characterization, so the displayed formulation is conditional on recovering them.
