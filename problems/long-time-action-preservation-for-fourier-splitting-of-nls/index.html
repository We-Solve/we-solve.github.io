Long-Time Action Preservation for Fourier Splitting of NLS

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a prime discretization size, let $\tau>0$ denote the time step, and let $\xi_j^n$ denote the numerical Fourier coefficients produced by the fully discrete Fourier splitting method for the one-dimensional cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation referred to in the supplied record. Here $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ is the time-step index and $j$ ranges over the retained Fourier modes, whose index set is not specified. Define the numerical actions by \[ I_j^{K,n}=|\xi_j^n|^2. \] The supplied record states that an estimate \[ \left|I_j^{K,n}-I_j^{K,0}\right|\leq C\varepsilon \qquad\text{for }n\tau\leq T/\varepsilon \] is known in the underlying regime. The meaning of the positive parameter $\varepsilon$, the hypotheses of this estimate, and the permitted dependence of $C$ and $T$ are not supplied.

Long-time preservation question. Under the hypotheses intended for that estimate, does comparable preservation of the numerical actions persist to substantially longer times, such as times of order $\varepsilon^{-r}$ for large $r$? A same-order-error formulation of the requested extension would ask whether suitable positive constants $C_r,T_r$ give \[ \left|I_j^{K,n}-I_j^{K,0}\right|\leq C_r\varepsilon \qquad\text{whenever }n\tau\leq T_r\varepsilon^{-r}. \] This display describes a possible precise target, rather than a fully quantified conjecture supplied by the record. The record does not specify whether the extension is sought for every sufficiently large fixed $r$, for arbitrarily large $r$ under additional hypotheses, or for some particular large exponent. It also leaves unresolved the equation's spatial domain and boundary conditions, the precise splitting and Fourier discretization, the initial-data assumptions, restrictions on $\tau$ and $K$, and the required uniformity over modes and discretization parameters. These qualifications must be recovered before a complete mathematical statement can be fixed.

Short English statement

For a fully discrete Fourier splitting approximation of the one-dimensional cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation with prime discretization size $K$, the supplied question asks whether numerical actions that remain within $C\varepsilon$ of their initial values until times of order $\varepsilon^{-1}$ enjoy comparable preservation until times of order $\varepsilon^{-r}$ for large $r$. The numerical scheme, parameter regime, and necessary quantifiers remain unspecified.
