Math-353

Four-dimensional finiteness and a uniform Gromov--Hausdorff gap

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For fixed $\Lambda\geq0$, $D>0$ and $V>0$, consider the intended class of closed connected smooth Riemannian $4$-manifolds \[ \mathcal M(\Lambda,D,V)=\{(M,g): \sec_g\geq-\Lambda^2,\ \operatorname{diam}_gM\leq D, \ \operatorname{Vol}_gM\geq V\}. \] The question asks whether only finitely many diffeomorphism types occur. Closedness is an intended convention identified in the aid, not written explicitly in the extracted sentence; no assertion about arbitrary incomplete manifolds or uncontrolled boundary is made.

The supplied assessment attributes an affirmative answer to Pro--Wilhelm's detailed 2020 preprint, Theorem A, and a stronger sequential diffeomorphism-stability statement to its Theorem B. It explicitly says no journal version, correction or refutation was located in that research check. The solved label therefore carries a preprint-only caveat, and the stability theorem's exact hypotheses require checking in that preprint.

Using that stronger sequential stability, the aid proposes the following nonconstructive uniform-gap corollary. For every $a\geq0$ and $\nu>0$, there is $\delta(a,\nu)>0$ such that any two nondiffeomorphic members of \[ \mathcal M_{a,\nu}= \{(M^4,g)\text{ closed connected}:\sec_g\geq-a^2, \ \operatorname{diam}_gM\leq1, \ \operatorname{Vol}_gM\geq\nu\} \] satisfy \[ d_{\mathrm{GH}}((M,g),(N,h))\geq\delta(a,\nu). \] Here $d_{\mathrm{GH}}$ is Gromov--Hausdorff distance between compact metric spaces. If the class is empty the assertion is vacuous. The dependence is on the fixed bounds, not on the individual metrics, and no explicit computable formula for $\delta$ is supplied.

The stated reasoning uses Gromov--Hausdorff precompactness and noncollapse: failure of a gap gives nondiffeomorphic pairs at distance tending to zero, a subsequence of pairs converging to the same four-dimensional limit, and hence an interleaved convergent sequence. The quoted sequential stability would make its late terms diffeomorphic, a contradiction. Mere finiteness of the number of smooth types is not by itself enough for this gap; the stronger stability input is essential.

Rescaling by the common diameter bound, $g'=D^{-2}g$, gives the dimensionless parameters \[ a=\Lambda D,\qquad\nu=V/D^4. \] Thus the unnormalized gap is $D\,\delta(\Lambda D,V/D^4)$. A finite Gromov--Hausdorff covering of $\mathcal M_{a,\nu}$ by balls of radius $\delta/3$ has at most one diffeomorphism type in each ball, since two of its members have distance less than $\delta$. This bounds the number of types by a finite covering number, without giving a numerical bound.

Short English statement

Are there finitely many smooth types of closed $4$-manifolds under a lower sectional-curvature bound, upper diameter bound and positive lower volume bound? The supplied assessment cites an affirmative 2020 preprint, with a preprint-only caveat. Its stronger sequential stability would also give a uniform Gromov--Hausdorff gap between different smooth types after rescaling.
