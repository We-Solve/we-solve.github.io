Math-882

Minimal Dilatations of Pseudo-Anosov Maps

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a pseudo-Anosov map $f$, its dilatation $\lambda(f)>1$ is the factor by which it expands one of its invariant transverse measured singular foliations; it contracts the other by $\lambda(f)^{-1}$. Write $L(f)=\log\lambda(f)$.

The supplied problem does not define the class of surfaces or maps under consideration, or the length $\ell_g$. In particular, it does not specify whether surfaces are closed, punctured, or have boundary, whether maps preserve orientation, or whether maps are considered up to isotopy or conjugacy. Consequently, the following is a conditional formulation, pending specification of these conventions.

For each admissible genus $g$, let $\mathcal P_g$ denote the intended class of pseudo-Anosov maps. For every $g$ for which this class is nonempty, define \[ m_g=\inf_{f\in\mathcal P_g}\log\lambda(f), \qquad \mathcal M_g=\{f\in\mathcal P_g:\log\lambda(f)=m_g\}. \] If the intended length is logarithmic dilatation, then the intended identification is $\ell_g=m_g$. Under this interpretation, the problem asks for the exact values of $m_g$ and a description of the qualitative properties of the maps in $\mathcal M_g$. This definition of the infimum does not itself assert that $\mathcal M_g$ is nonempty.

The broader question concerns families $(f_g)_{g\in I}$, where $I$ is an unbounded set of admissible genera and $f_g\in\mathcal P_g$, satisfying \[ \exists\,c,C>0\ \exists\,g_0\in{\mathbb{N}}\ \forall\,g\in I, \qquad g\ge g_0\ \Longrightarrow\ \frac{c}{g}\le\log\lambda(f_g)\le\frac{C}{g}. \] Here the constants are independent of $g$, though they may depend on the family. This spells out the asymptotic condition $\log\lambda(f_g)\asymp g^{-1}$. The source asks for qualitative properties of such maps, but does not specify which properties, what equivalence relation should govern their classification, or whether the family must be defined for every sufficiently large genus rather than an unbounded subset. These remain unresolved parts of the intended formulation.

Short English statement

Determine the minimum logarithmic pseudo-Anosov dilatation in each genus and describe the maps attaining it; more broadly, describe families whose logarithmic dilatation is comparable to $1/g$. This interpretation is conditional: the source leaves the surface conventions, the definition of $\ell_g$, and the requested qualitative properties unspecified.
