Math-6982

Extremal Graphs Maximizing Non-Triangular Edges

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the conventional interpretation that a graph is finite, simple, and undirected; these conventions are implicit rather than explicit in the supplied record. An edge of a graph is nontriangular if it belongs to no triangle. Write \[ q(H)=\left|\{uv\in E(H):\text{there is no }w\in V(H)\setminus\{u,v\}\text{ with }uw,vw\in E(H)\}\right|. \] For nonnegative integers $a,b,c$, let $G(a,b,c)$ have a vertex partition $A\sqcup B\sqcup C$, where $|A|=a$, $|B|=b$, and $|C|=c$. The set $A$ induces a clique, $B$ and $C$ are independent, every vertex of $B$ is adjacent to every vertex of $A\cup C$, and there are no edges between $A$ and $C$. Thus \[ |E(G(a,b,c))|=\binom{a}{2}+ab+bc. \] Conjecture (feasible-parameter formulation). For every pair of integers $n,e$ with $n\geq 0$ and $0\leq e\leq\binom{n}{2}$, there exist nonnegative integers $a,b,c$ such that \[ a+b+c=n,\qquad \binom{a}{2}+ab+bc\geq e, \] and, for every graph $H$ with $|V(H)|=n$ and $|E(H)|\geq e$, \[ q(H)\leq q(G(a,b,c)). \] The source says "for every $n$ and $e$" without specifying their ranges. The displayed ranges make the optimization feasible under the graph conventions above; including $n=0$ uses the empty-graph convention. The threshold is at least $e$ edges, not exactly $e$ edges.

The supplied curation labels the problem partially solved and reports a sufficiently-large-$n$ result of Gruslys--Letzter while retaining the unrestricted all-$n$ formulation as open. This is a supplied literature assessment, not an independently verified theorem or status determination.

Short English statement

For every feasible vertex count and edge threshold, the conjecture asks whether some graph of the specified three-part form maximizes the number of edges lying in no triangle among all graphs with that vertex count and at least that many edges. The supplied dated curation reports a result for sufficiently large vertex counts and leaves the all-size formulation open.
