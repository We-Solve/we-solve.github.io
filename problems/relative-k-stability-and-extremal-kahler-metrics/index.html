Math-753

Relative K-Stability and Extremal Kähler Metrics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth complex projective manifold and let $L$ be an ample holomorphic line bundle on $M$. Such a pair $(M,L)$ is called a polarized projective manifold. Write $c_1(L)\in H^2(M,{\mathbb{R}})$ for the real first Chern class, and let $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ denote the group of holomorphic automorphisms of $M$. An algebraic torus is an algebraic group isomorphic to $({\mathbb{C}}^*)^r$; maximality here means maximality under inclusion among algebraic tori in $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$.

A Kähler form $\omega$ represents the polarization class when $[\omega]=c_1(L)$. Its associated Kähler metric is called extremal if the $(1,0)$-gradient of its scalar curvature $S_\omega$ is a holomorphic vector field: \[ \bar\partial\left(\operatorname{grad}^{1,0}_{\omega}S_\omega\right)=0. \]

The supplied question asks whether, for every polarized projective manifold $(M,L)$ and every maximal algebraic torus $T\subseteq\operatorname{Aut}(M)$, the following implication holds: \[ \begin{gathered} (M,L)\text{ is $K$-stable relative to }T \\[2pt] \Longrightarrow \\[2pt] \exists\,\omega\text{ a Kähler form on }M:\quad [\omega]=c_1(L),\qquad \bar\partial\left(\operatorname{grad}^{1,0}_{\omega}S_\omega\right)=0. \end{gathered} \]

Unresolved qualification. The supplied record does not define its notion of relative $K$-stability. In particular, it does not specify the admissible test configurations, the modified invariant and its normalization, or the equality condition. It also places $T$ in $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ without specifying preservation of the polarization or a lift of the torus action to $L$ or a tensor power. The hypothesis above retains the source wording; these conventions require source verification before the implication is a fully specified mathematical conjecture.

Short English statement

Does relative $K$-stability of a polarized complex projective manifold, with respect to a maximal algebraic torus of automorphisms, guarantee an extremal Kähler metric in $c_1(L)$? The precise stability convention and the compatibility of the torus with the polarization remain unspecified in the supplied record.
