Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 36 (II$_1$ factor Tarski's problem)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 2$, let $\mathbb{F}_n$ be the free group on $n$ generators. Its group von Neumann algebra is \[ L(\mathbb{F}_n)=\{\lambda_n(g):g\in\mathbb{F}_n\}'', \] where $\lambda_n(g)\delta_h=\delta_{gh}$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb{F}_n)$, and the double commutant is taken in $B(\ell^2(\mathbb{F}_n))$. Equip this $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with its canonical normalized trace \[ \tau_n(x)=\langle x\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle, \] where $e$ is the group identity. Interpret elementary equivalence in the standard continuous first-order language of tracial von Neumann algebras: two such structures are elementarily equivalent if every sentence has the same value in both.

The finite-rank formulation of the supplied question is: \[ \text{For every }n,m\in\mathbb{N}\text{ with }n,m\geq 2\text{ and }n\ne m, \qquad (L(\mathbb{F}_n),\tau_n)\equiv(L(\mathbb{F}_m),\tau_m)? \] Equivalently, must $\varphi^{(L(\mathbb{F}_n),\tau_n)}=\varphi^{(L(\mathbb{F}_m),\tau_m)}$ hold for every sentence $\varphi$ and every such pair of ranks?

The source does not explicitly specify the range of $n$ or the logical language. The displayed formulation uses finite nonabelian ranks and the standard tracial continuous language. Whether the intended question also includes the countably infinite rank $n=\infty$ remains unresolved from the supplied record. Rank $1$ does not give a $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor and is excluded from this formulation.

Short English statement

Do all finite-rank nonabelian free group factors satisfy exactly the same continuous first-order sentences as tracial von Neumann algebras? The supplied record calls this the $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor Tarski problem and labels it open. Its intended rank range and logical language require confirmation.
