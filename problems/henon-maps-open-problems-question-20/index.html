Henon Maps: Open Problems — Question 20

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal H$ denote the class of Hénon maps intended in Question 20 of the supplied source. For $f\in\mathcal H$, let $F(f)$ denote its forward Fatou set, namely the normality locus of the family of iterates $\{f^n:n\geq 0\}$, using the ambient space and normality convention intended by that source. A Fatou component is a connected component of $F(f)$. Such a component $U$ is wandering if \[ f^m(U)\cap f^n(U)=\varnothing \qquad\text{for every pair of integers }0\leq m<n. \] Here $f^0$ is the identity and $f^n$ is the $n$-fold iterate for $n\geq1$.

Question 20 (Berger, Bedford, 2023). Does every quasi-hyperbolic map $f\in\mathcal H$ have no wandering Fatou component? Equivalently, with the preceding conventions, is it true that \[ \forall f\in\mathcal H,\quad f\text{ is quasi-hyperbolic} \ \Longrightarrow\ \forall U\in\pi_0(F(f)),\ \exists\,0\leq m<n:\quad f^m(U)\cap f^n(U)\neq\varnothing, \] where $\pi_0(F(f))$ denotes the set of connected components of $F(f)$?

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied excerpt does not specify the precise class $\mathcal H$, its ambient space or parameter restrictions, the definition of quasi-hyperbolicity, or the normality convention defining the Fatou set. These remain source-dependent terms in this formulation; the excerpt alone does not determine a fully specified mathematical assertion.

Short English statement

Berger and Bedford ask whether quasi-hyperbolicity rules out wandering Fatou components for the Hénon maps under consideration. The supplied excerpt leaves the map class and several defining conventions unspecified, so this statement requires review against the source.
