Question (Babenko; incomplete source formulation). With the missing data fixed as intended in the source, does there exist a continuous or smooth family \[ [0,1]\longrightarrow\mathcal G,\qquad t\longmapsto g_t, \] such that \[ g_0=g_{\mathrm{rd}},\qquad g_1=g_{\mathrm{ex}}, \] and $t\mapsto\sigma(g_t)$ is decreasing? Here $[0,1]$ records the interpolation between the two stated endpoint parameters. The source excerpt does not specify whether "decreasing" means \[ \sigma(g_t)\leq\sigma(g_s)\qquad(0\leq s<t\leq1) \] or the strict condition \[ \sigma(g_t)<\sigma(g_s)\qquad(0\leq s<t\leq1). \] Neither interpretation is selected in this draft. Likewise, continuity and smoothness remain alternative requested regularities whose meanings depend on the missing structure on $\mathcal G$; the round endpoint must be allowed in that space.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the round sphere can be joined to a particular example by a continuous or smooth family of metrics with conical singularities along which $\sigma$ decreases. The example, the meaning of $\sigma$, the family regularity, and the precise monotonicity requirement are not determined by the supplied excerpt.
