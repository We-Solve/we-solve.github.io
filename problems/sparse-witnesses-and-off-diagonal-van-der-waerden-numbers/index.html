Sparse witnesses and off-diagonal van der Waerden numbers

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $n\ge2$, interpret $W(n)$ as the least $N$ such that every two-coloring of $[N]$ contains a monochromatic nonconstant $n$-term arithmetic progression. The supplied record does not define $W^*(n)$; the aid's cardinality-witness analysis uses the interpretation \[ W^*(n)=\min\{|S|:S\subset{\mathbb{Z}}\text{ finite and every two-coloring of }S \text{ contains a monochromatic }n\text{-term progression}\}. \] There is no diameter restriction on $S$. Under this explicit interpretation, the first source question is \[ W(n)-W^*(n)\longrightarrow\infty\quad(n\to\infty), \] meaning that for every $M>0$, the difference is at least $M$ for every sufficiently large $n$. The aid calls this apparently open and does not settle it.

For a cardinality-minimal witness $S$ with $|S|=W^*(n)$, the aid reports that every $v\in S$ belongs to two $n$-term progressions whose intersection is exactly $\{v\}$, arising from the two extensions of an avoiding coloring of $S\setminus\{v\}$. It also reports that $S$ contains at least $2^{n-1}$ distinct $n$-term progressions. Together with the fact that a nonconstant $n$-term progression is determined by its minimum and maximum, the reported necessary bound is \[ W^*(n)\ge\max\left\{2n-1, \left\lceil\frac{1+\sqrt{1+2^{n+2}}}{2}\right\rceil\right\}. \] These constraints give local pruning certificates for witnesses, not a sufficient test of the Ramsey property or a solution of the difference-limit question.

The record then turns to off-diagonal numbers. Define $W(a,b)$ as the least $N$ such that every red--blue coloring of $[N]$ has a red $a$-term progression or a blue $b$-term progression; color exchange gives $W(a,b)=W(b,a)$. It suggests quadratic growth of $W(k,3)$ from computed values and prints \[ k^{2-o(1)}<W(k)<\exp(O(k\log^5k)). \] The supplied verification reconstructs $W(k)$ in this display as $W(k,3)$, using the heading, the Brown--Landman--Robertson lower bound, and the next record's separate diagonal-growth question. This remains reconstructed_unverified; the diagonal notation is not silently equated with the off-diagonal number. The aid says Green and Hunter have refuted the quadratic-growth speculation, without stating their quantitative lower bound.

Let $r_3(N)$ be the largest size of a subset of $[N]$ with no nonconstant three-term progression. The aid reports the implication \[ N\ge2k,\quad r_3(N)<N/(2k) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad W(3,k)\le N. \] It then reports the transferred published-density estimate \[ W(3,k)\le\exp(O((\log k)^{12})), \] and, conditionally on the claimed March 2026 Raghavan preprint estimate, the improvement \[ W(3,k)\le\exp(O((\log k)^6(\log\log k)^6)). \] These statements concern sufficiently large $k$, with constants and hypotheses needing reference verification. They address the off-diagonal problem separately and do not answer the first source question.

Short English statement

The source combines a limit question comparing interval and sparse-set van der Waerden witnesses with off-diagonal growth estimates. The supplied aid gives necessary structural bounds for minimal sparse witnesses and transfers progression-free density bounds to off-diagonal numbers, while keeping the notation reconstruction and preprint qualification explicit.
