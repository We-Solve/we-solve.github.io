Linear Systems and Eigenvalue Problems — Problem 27

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $B=(b_{ij})$ be an $n\times n$ upper bidiagonal matrix, meaning that $b_{ij}=0$ unless $j=i$ or $j=i+1$. The source does not specify whether the entries of $B$ are real or complex. For $x\in{\mathbb{C}}^n$, let $\|x\|_2=(\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2)^{1/2}$; for a matrix, $\|\cdot\|_2$ denotes the induced operator norm. Write $x^H$ for the conjugate transpose of $x$, and let $\epsilon$ denote machine precision.

For a requested integer number $k$ of triples, is there an algorithm using $O(kn)$ operations that produces $(\sigma_i,v_i,u_i)$, $1\leq i\leq k$, satisfying \[ \sigma_i\geq0,\qquad v_i,u_i\in{\mathbb{C}}^n,\qquad \|v_i\|_2=\|u_i\|_2=1, \] \[ \|Bv_i-\sigma_i u_i\|_2=O(\epsilon n\|B\|_2) \qquad(1\leq i\leq k), \] and \[ |v_i^Hv_j|=O(\epsilon n),\qquad |u_i^Hu_j|=O(\epsilon n) \qquad(1\leq i\neq j\leq k)? \] The admissible range of $k$, the numerical computation model, and the parameter dependence and uniformity of the constants in the three $O$-bounds are not specified in the source. Thus the intended universal quantification over inputs requires clarification. The displayed requirements include only the residual $Bv_i-\sigma_i u_i$; no bound for $B^Hu_i-\sigma_i v_i$, nor a rule selecting particular singular values, is supplied.

Short English statement

Can an algorithm produce $k$ nonnegative scalars and pairs of unit vectors for an upper bidiagonal matrix in $O(kn)$ operations, with the stated small residuals and approximate orthogonality within each vector family? The matrix field, allowable $k$, and numerical complexity conventions remain unspecified.
