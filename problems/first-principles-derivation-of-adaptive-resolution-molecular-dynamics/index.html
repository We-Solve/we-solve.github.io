First-Principles Derivation of Adaptive-Resolution Molecular Dynamics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
Adaptive-resolution molecular dynamics couples an atomistic region and a coarse-grained region using a switching function $w\in[0,1]$. For molecular centers $X_\alpha,X_\beta$, the report's force interpolation has atomistic weight $w(X_\alpha)w(X_\beta)$ and coarse-grained weight $1-w(X_\alpha)w(X_\beta)$. A potential interpolation generally yields additional derivatives of the spatial weights, hence drift forces. A first-principles derivation would obtain the prescribed interpolation and statistical ensemble from microscopic statistical mechanics rather than choosing them ad hoc.
Source scope and qualifications.
The report supplies an argument against a particular potential-interpolation scheme and leaves a first-principles force derivation open. It does not establish that every conceivable energy-based coarse-graining is impossible; that stronger conclusion is not part of the target.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For the adaptive-resolution interpolation formula in molecular dynamics, why is it not naturally formulated using potentials, and can the force-based interpolation formula be formally derived from first principles of statistical physics?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The potential-based coupling is ruled out by an overdetermined drift system in the report, while a first-principles derivation of the force interpolation formula is explicitly left as a challenge. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Explain why the adaptive-resolution coupling is force based and derive that force interpolation from statistical mechanics.
