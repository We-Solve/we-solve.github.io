Local Discrete Holomorphic Dynamics — Open Problem OP3

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{End}({\mathbb{C}},0)$ denote the set of germs at $0$ of holomorphic maps $f$ satisfying $f(0)=0$. The multiplier of such a germ is $\lambda_f=f'(0)$. A Cremer fixed point is an irrationally indifferent fixed point at which the germ is not holomorphically linearizable: $\lambda_f=e^{2\pi i\theta_f}$ for some $\theta_f\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}$, and there is no local biholomorphic change of coordinates $\phi$, with $\phi(0)=0$, satisfying \[ \phi\circ f\circ\phi^{-1}(z)=\lambda_f z \] as germs at $0$.

A local topological conjugacy between germs $f$ and $g$ is a germ of a homeomorphism $h$ between neighborhoods of $0$, fixing $0$, such that $h\circ f=g\circ h$ wherever these compositions are defined sufficiently near $0$. The supplied excerpt does not specify whether the allowed homeomorphisms must preserve orientation; this convention must be fixed before asserting that the multiplier is a topological invariant.

Problem OP3 (Abate, 2011). Are the multiplier and the homeomorphism class of the stable set a complete set of topological invariants for germs in $\operatorname{End}({\mathbb{C}},0)$ having a Cremer fixed point at the origin?

More explicitly, write $\mathcal S(f)$ for the stable set intended by the source and $[\mathcal S(f)]_{\mathrm{homeo}}$ for its intended homeomorphism class. Subject to fixing those meanings and the conjugacy convention, the question asks whether, for every pair of Cremer germs $f,g\in\operatorname{End}({\mathbb{C}},0)$, \[ f\text{ and }g\text{ are locally topologically conjugate} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left(\lambda_f=\lambda_g\ \text{ and }\ [\mathcal S(f)]_{\mathrm{homeo}}=[\mathcal S(g)]_{\mathrm{homeo}}\right). \]

Unresolved stable-set qualification. The supplied record does not define the stable set. For a representative of $f$ defined on a neighborhood $W$ and a neighborhood $U\subseteq W$ of $0$, one possible local construction is \[ K_U(f)=\{z\in U: f^n(z)\text{ is defined and belongs to }U\text{ for every }n\geq 0\}. \] The excerpt does not establish whether this construction, a germ of such sets, or another construction is intended. Nor does it specify whether the homeomorphism class is abstract, pointed at $0$, or taken with respect to the embedding in ${\mathbb{C}}$. Thus $\mathcal S(f)$ remains a placeholder for the source's intended definition; no choice or independence from $U$ is asserted here.

Short English statement

Abate's Problem OP3 asks whether two holomorphic germs with Cremer fixed points are locally topologically conjugate exactly when their multipliers agree and their stable sets have the same homeomorphism class. The supplied excerpt leaves the stable-set definition and the relevant homeomorphism conventions unspecified. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied metadata, not an independently verified assessment.
