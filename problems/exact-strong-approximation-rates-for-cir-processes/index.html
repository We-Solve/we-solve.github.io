Math-7017

Exact Strong Approximation Rates for CIR Processes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $a,\sigma>0$ and $b,x\geq0$. Let $W$ be a standard real Brownian motion, and let $X$ be the nonnegative Cox--Ingersoll--Ross process satisfying \[ X_t=x+\int_0^t(a-bX_s)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s+\int_0^t\sigma\sqrt{X_s}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} W_s. \] The stochastic integral is understood in the Itô sense. The parameter range includes $x=0$ and $b=0$; no condition excluding attainment of the boundary $0$ is imposed.

For an explicit interpretation of the approximation question, fix a deterministic terminal time $T>0$. For $N\in{\mathbb{N}}$, define the optimal absolute-mean error from equidistant Brownian observations by \[ e_N(T;a,b,\sigma,x) :=\inf_{\substack{\varphi:{\mathbb{R}}^N\to{\mathbb{R}}\\ \varphi\text{ Borel measurable}}} {\mathbb{E}}\!\left[\left|X_T-\varphi\left(W_{T/N},W_{2T/N},\ldots,W_T\right)\right|\right]. \] The approximation map may depend on the fixed parameters and on $N$. One precise convention for its optimal algebraic convergence order is \[ \rho(T;a,b,\sigma,x) :=\sup\left\{r\geq0:\sup_{N\geq1}N^r e_N(T;a,b,\sigma,x)<\infty\right\}. \] Under these explicit conventions, the question is whether, for every fixed $T>0$, $a,\sigma>0$, and $b,x\geq0$, \[ \rho(T;a,b,\sigma,x) =\min\left\{\frac{2a}{\sigma^2},1\right\}. \] Here the quantifiers concern each fixed parameter choice; constants in error estimates may depend on those parameters and on $T$.

Unresolved formulation qualification. The original statement refers to an approximation error in equation (4), but that equation is absent from the supplied record. In particular, the record does not specify whether the intended claim is only the equality of algebraic orders above or the stronger assertion that, writing $r=\min\{2a/\sigma^2,1\}$, there exist constants $0<c\leq C<\infty$ and $N_0$ such that \[ cN^{-r}\leq e_N(T;a,b,\sigma,x)\leq CN^{-r} \qquad(N\geq N_0). \] These assertions are not interchangeable without further information. The positive terminal time and the exact observation grid also require confirmation against the missing equation.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the best mean absolute error for approximating a CIR process at a fixed positive time from $N$ equally spaced Brownian observations has algebraic order $\min\{2a/\sigma^2,1\}$ for every $a,\sigma>0$ and $b,x\geq0$. The missing equation (4) leaves the precise error class and meaning of exact order unresolved. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
