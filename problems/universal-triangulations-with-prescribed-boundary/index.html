Universal Triangulations with Prescribed Boundary

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\geq 2$ be an integer, covering the dimensional cases explicitly mentioned in the supplied statement. A finite simplicial $(d-1)$-sphere is a finite simplicial complex $T$ whose geometric realization $|T|$ is homeomorphic to $S^{d-1}$. A linear embedding $f:|T|\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^d$ is an embedding whose restriction to each simplex is affine. A simplicial $d$-ball is a finite simplicial complex $U$ triangulating a closed $d$-dimensional ball; prescribing boundary $T$ requires an identification $\partial U\cong T$ as simplicial complexes.

Requested problem, with unresolved specifications. Given such an embedded sphere $f:|T|\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^d$, characterize the triangulations called universal in the source, give an algorithm constructing a universal simplicial $d$-ball $U$ with prescribed boundary $T$, and establish bounds on its size. The supplied record does not define universality: neither the class of objects over which its quantifiers range nor the required extension, realization, or comparison property is specified. It also does not specify whether $U$ must admit a linear embedding extending $f$, whether boundary subdivision is permitted, which combinatorial quantity measures size, or the parameters controlling the requested bound. Consequently, this is an incomplete problem specification, not a fully quantified mathematical assertion. The boundary identification above expresses the literal prescribed-boundary requirement; any permission to subdivide it needs clarification.

The supplied record reports that the case $d=2$ is known and that higher-dimensional cases remain open. These are supplied status claims, not independently verified mathematical facts. The record does not explicitly address dimensions below $2$. Its analytic-torsion literature assessment concerns a different mathematical problem and cannot supply the missing definition of universality.

Short English statement

For a triangulated sphere embedded linearly in ${\mathbb{R}}^d$, the requested task is to characterize universal triangulations, algorithmically construct a universal triangulated ball with that boundary, and bound its size. The meaning of universal and the size measure are missing, so a precise conjecture cannot yet be stated. The reported planar result, higher-dimensional open status, and unrelated analytic-torsion assessment require source review.
