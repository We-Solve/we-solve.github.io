Convex-Hull Decomposition for Mixed-Integer Bipartite Systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

For a subtree $T$, its constraints and integral coordinates are restricted from the graph. The conjectured equality compares the convex hull of all mixed-integer solutions with the simultaneous convex-hull restrictions from the specified subtrees.

An integer program imposes integral coordinates on a system of constraints. A mixed-integer program allows both integral and real coordinates; $\operatorname{conv}(S)$ denotes the set of finite convex combinations of feasible points of $S$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Let $G$ be bipartite, let $I$ be the set of integral-coordinate vertices, and let $S(G,I)=\{x:x_i+x_j\geq b_{ij}\text{ for }ij\in E,\ x_i\in\mathbb{Z}\text{ for }i\in I\}$. If $T$ ranges over subtrees of $G$ for which $I_T=V(T)\cap I$ is the set of leaves, is $\operatorname{conv}(S(G,I))=\bigcap_T\operatorname{conv}(S(T,I_T))$? The conjecture is known for denominator parameter $k=2$ and open for $k\geq3$. [S2]
Scope qualification.
The denominator parameter $k$ is not defined in the supplied question; its arithmetic assumptions are those of the report.

Short English statement

Determine whether all the stated leaf-integral subtree constraints describe the full mixed-integer convex hull.
