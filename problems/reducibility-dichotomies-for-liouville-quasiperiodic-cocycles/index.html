Math-3376

Reducibility Dichotomies for Liouville Quasiperiodic Cocycles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, let $\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}$ and let $A\in C^\infty(\mathbb T,\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}}))$. The cocycle acts by $(x,v)\mapsto(x+\alpha,A(x)v)$. For $\theta\in\mathbb T$ put \[ R_\theta=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(2\pi\theta)&-\sin(2\pi\theta)\\ \sin(2\pi\theta)&\cos(2\pi\theta)\end{pmatrix}, \qquad A_\theta(x)=R_\theta A(x). \] An irrational $\alpha$ is Liouville if for every positive integer $N$ it has rational approximations $p/q$ with arbitrarily large $q$ and $0<|\alpha-p/q|<q^{-N}$.

The source first asks whether, for every Liouville $\alpha$ and every such smooth $A$, for Lebesgue-almost every $\theta$ the cocycle $(\alpha,A_\theta)$ is either "nonuniformly hyperbolic" or "rotations-reducible". A rotation-valued reduction has the form \[ B(x+\alpha)^{-1}A_\theta(x)B(x)\in\operatorname{SO}(2) \qquad(x\in\mathbb T) \] for an allowed invertible conjugacy $B$. The regularity, real/projective target and possible covering period of $B$ are not specified. Rotation-valued reduction is not automatically reduction to one constant rotation, especially at Liouville frequencies.

The hyperbolicity convention is material. For $F=A_\theta$, write $F_n(x)=F(x+(n-1)\alpha)\cdots F(x)$ and \[ L(\alpha,F)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\int_{\mathbb T}\log\|F_n(x)\|\,dx. \] Positive Lyapunov exponent is sometimes the intended nonuniform-hyperbolicity condition, without excluding uniform hyperbolicity. A stricter usage requires positive exponent and failure of uniform hyperbolicity; then uniformly hyperbolic parameters are a distinct case needing explicit treatment in any exhaustive dichotomy. The excerpt does not resolve this convention, so neither meaning is silently selected.

The second question extends to every irrational $\alpha$ the corresponding almost-rotations-reducibility dichotomy, retaining the almost-every-$\theta$ qualifier. Almost reduction requires a specified closure/topology of the conjugacy orbit and target class. A typical smooth interpretation asks whether that closure meets the rotation-valued cocycles; approximation by constants is a different condition. The source gives neither the exact topology nor the required regularity and period of the conjugacies, so these remain explicit qualifications.

The supplied literature assessment checked 21 August 2026 reports major analytic one-frequency almost-reducibility results, including Liouville frequencies, but says they do not settle the broader extracted question. Analytic and arbitrary smooth hypotheses, exact and almost reduction, and rotation-valued versus constant targets must not be conflated.

Short English statement

For almost every rotation parameter, must a smooth Liouville-frequency cocycle be hyperbolic in the intended sense or conjugate to rotations? Does an almost-reduction version hold for every irrational frequency? Hyperbolicity and conjugacy conventions are missing, and the supplied analytic progress is narrower than the smooth question.
