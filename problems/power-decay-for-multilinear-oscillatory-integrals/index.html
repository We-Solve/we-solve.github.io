Power Decay for Multilinear Oscillatory Integrals

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a multilinear oscillatory integral denoted by $I_\lambda(f_1,\ldots,f_n)$, with a polynomial phase described as nondegenerate. It does not give the formula for this integral, its integration domain, its amplitude or cutoff, the arguments at which the functions $f_j$ are evaluated, or a definition of nondegeneracy.

To display the requested estimate, write $(X_j,\mu_j)$ for the unspecified measure space on which $f_j$ is defined, and use \[ \|f_j\|_{L^\infty(X_j,\mu_j)} =\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{x\in X_j}|f_j(x)|. \] These spaces are placeholders for missing source data, not additional universally quantified choices.

The question recorded is whether, for each fixed admissible configuration defining $I_\lambda$ and its nondegenerate polynomial phase, there exist constants $\delta>0$, $C>0$, and $\lambda_0>0$ such that \[ \left|I_\lambda(f_1,\ldots,f_n)\right| \le C|\lambda|^{-\delta} \prod_{j=1}^{n}\|f_j\|_{L^\infty(X_j,\mu_j)} \] for every allowed parameter $\lambda$ with $|\lambda|\ge\lambda_0$ and every allowed tuple of essentially bounded functions $f_j$. The constants must be independent of $\lambda$ and of the functions $f_j$. The allowed configurations, parameter range, function classes, and permitted dependence of the constants on the fixed data are not specified in the record. Consequently, this is a quantified statement of the requested estimate, but the supplied information does not determine a complete mathematical formulation.

Short English statement

Does nondegeneracy of the polynomial phase guarantee power decay in $|\lambda|$, uniformly over bounded input functions after multiplying by their $L^\infty$ norms? The integral and the meaning of nondegeneracy are missing, so the intended scope requires clarification from the cited source. The supplied open status is a dated curation claim.
