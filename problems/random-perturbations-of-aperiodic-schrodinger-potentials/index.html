Random Perturbations of Aperiodic Schrödinger Potentials

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\nu$ be a compactly supported probability measure on ${\mathbb{R}}$, let $S=\operatorname{supp}\nu$ be its topological support, and assume $\#S\geq 2$. Set \[ \Omega=S^{{\mathbb{Z}}},\qquad W_\omega(n)=\omega_n\quad(\omega\in\Omega,\ n\in{\mathbb{Z}}). \] Thus the noise is indexed by the entire integer line. Its multiplication operator on $\ell^2({\mathbb{Z}})$ is \[ (M_\omega u)(n)=\omega_nu(n). \] Compactness of $S\subset{\mathbb{R}}$ makes this a bounded self-adjoint operator. If an unperturbed self-adjoint operator $H_x$ on $\ell^2({\mathbb{Z}})$ is specified, its additive perturbation can therefore be written conditionally as \[ H_{x,\omega}=H_x+M_\omega,\qquad \mathcal D(H_{x,\omega})=\mathcal D(H_x). \] This conditional notation supplies an interpretation of adding the displayed noise; it does not recover the missing definition of $H_x$.

The excerpt describes an ergodic-potential setting and states that there is a compact set $\Sigma_0$ such that \[ \sigma(H_x)=\Sigma_0\qquad\text{for }\mu\text{-almost every }x\in X. \] However, it does not supply the definitions or full hypotheses for $X$, $\mu$, the base transformation $T$, the sampling function, or $H_x$. This almost-sure spectral assertion is consequently retained as supplied context, without asserting that it follows from hypotheses fully recorded here. The law of $\omega$ on $\Omega$, its independence properties, and its relation to the base variable $x$ are also not explicitly specified.

The source's question is what happens when random noise is added to an aperiodic potential, or more generally to a given ergodic potential. Its examples include periodic potentials with $X$ finite and $T$ a cyclic permutation; almost Mathieu potentials with $X=S^1$, $T$ an irrational rotation, and $f(x)=2\lambda\cos(2\pi x)$; Fibonacci substitution potentials; and Sturmian potentials with a Cantor base. The excerpt gives no parameter range for $\lambda$. It also says that one-dimensional aperiodic structures can be included through sequence hulls, but does not specify the required invariant measure or ergodicity assumptions.

Unresolved formulation. No precise conjecture or requested conclusion can be recovered from this record. The excerpt ends at It turns out that, before the subsequent conclusions. The supplied verification field labels the statement unrecoverable and reports that the introductory question is followed by localization and spectral-structure theorems, with a remaining Fibonacci case recorded separately. That separate formulation is not supplied here. Accordingly, this draft records the noise construction and the introductory question, without turning them into an asserted open conjecture.
