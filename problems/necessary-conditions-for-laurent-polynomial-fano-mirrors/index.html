Necessary Conditions for Laurent-Polynomial Fano Mirrors

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $k$ and a specified number $n$ of variables, a Laurent polynomial is an element \[ f\in k[x_1^{\pm1},\ldots,x_n^{\pm1}],\qquad f=\sum_{m\in A}c_m x_1^{m_1}\cdots x_n^{m_n}, \] where $A\subset{\mathbb{Z}}^n$ is finite and $c_m\in k$. Under the usual algebraic convention, a Fano manifold is a smooth projective geometrically integral variety $X$ whose anticanonical line bundle $\mathcal O_X(-K_X)$ is ample. The source does not specify the base field, the number of variables, the dimension of $X$, or any additional restrictions on $f$ or $X$; these conventions therefore do not resolve the intended geometric setting.

The source question is: What necessary conditions must a Laurent polynomial $f$ satisfy in order to be mirror to a Fano manifold?

Once a geometric setting and a precise mirror relation $\mathcal M(f,X)$ have been specified, the requested conditions can be expressed as properties $P$ of Laurent polynomials for which \[ \forall f\in k[x_1^{\pm1},\ldots,x_n^{\pm1}],\qquad \left(\exists X\text{ a Fano manifold with }\mathcal M(f,X)\right) \Longrightarrow P(f). \] Here $\mathcal M$ is only a placeholder for the unspecified meaning of being mirror; no definition of that relation is supplied or asserted. The class of admissible $X$, including any requirement that $\dim X=n$, must also be specified before this schema becomes a determinate mathematical problem. The question asks for necessary conditions, without asserting their sufficiency or asking explicitly for a complete characterization.

Short English statement

Determine restrictions that every Laurent polynomial mirror to a Fano manifold must satisfy. The supplied question leaves the meaning of mirror and the geometric setting unspecified, so a precise version requires those choices. Its supplied status is partially solved; this is a dated curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
