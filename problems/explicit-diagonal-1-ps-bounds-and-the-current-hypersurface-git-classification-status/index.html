Math-5076

Explicit diagonal 1-PS bounds and the current hypersurface GIT classification status

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Determine the GIT stable and semistable degree-five surfaces in ${\mathbb{P}}^3$, degree-four threefolds in ${\mathbb{P}}^4$, and degree-five threefolds in ${\mathbb{P}}^4$. Also, for fixed hypersurface dimension and degree, bound the exponents of diagonal one-parameter subgroups needed for testing. The original says smaller cases had been checked by various authors, without references.

In the usual complex GIT interpretation, use the $\operatorname{SL}_{N+1}$ action and standard linearization on ${\mathbb{P}}(\operatorname{Sym}^d({\mathbb{C}}^{N+1})^*)$. A diagonal $1$-PS has integer weights $a=(a_0,\ldots,a_N)$, $\sum_i a_i=0$, and is primitive if their gcd is one. Under the chosen coordinate-weight convention, a monomial $x^\alpha$ of total degree $d$ has weight $a\cdot\alpha$. With $w_a(F)=\min_{c_\alpha\ne0}a\cdot\alpha$, semistability requires $w_a(gF)\le0$ and stability requires $w_a(gF)<0$ for every nontrivial $a$ and every required change of coordinates $g$. Thus weak witnesses $w_a\ge0$ detect failure of stability, while strict witnesses $w_a>0$ detect instability. Bounds on diagonal weights do not remove the coordinate-change quantifier.

The attached unreviewed aid proposes \[ D_{N,d}=\left\lceil\sqrt N\,d^{N-1}\right\rceil,\qquad B_{N,d}=N D_{N,d}. \] It claims every weak Hilbert--Mumford witness can be replaced, after the required coordinate change, by a primitive diagonal witness with $\max_i|a_i|\le D_{N,d}$. It further claims every diagonal $1$-PS has a primitive representative with identical signs on all degree-$d$ monomial weights and $\max_i|a_i|\le B_{N,d}$, giving the latter bound for strict witnesses. Its reported stability/semistability bounds are respectively $44/132$ for quintic surfaces, $128/512$ for quartic threefolds and $250/1000$ for quintic threefolds.

The aid reports algorithmic finite-state descriptions for all three cases but says their full geometric interpretation remains open or at announcement level in important parts. This is the supplied literature assessment, not a fresh status determination. The exact algorithms, classification strata and proofs of the proposed bounds are not supplied; numerical witness bounds alone do not classify the geometric stable and semistable loci.

Short English statement

Classify the three specified hypersurface GIT loci and bound the one-parameter subgroup weights needed for testing. The aid gives separate proposed bounds for weak and strict witnesses, while retaining all coordinate changes and the missing geometric classification.
