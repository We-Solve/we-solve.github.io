Monodromy of Universal Plane-Curve Bundles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a maximal-monodromy statement for every integer $d\geq 4$. It does not explicitly specify the ground field or the parameter space of the universal bundle. The formulation below spells out the customary interpretation using smooth complex plane curves and their projective parameter space; these conventions require confirmation against the cited report.

For an integer $d\geq 4$, put \[ r=d-3,\qquad g=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}{2}. \] Under this interpretation, let \[ U_d=\left\{[F]\in{\mathbb{P}}(H^0({\mathbb{P}}^2_{{\mathbb{C}}},\mathcal O(d))): V(F)\text{ is smooth}\right\}, \] and let \[ \pi_d:\mathcal C_d\longrightarrow U_d,\qquad \mathcal C_d=\{([F],x)\in U_d\times{\mathbb{P}}^2_{{\mathbb{C}}}:F(x)=0\}, \] be the universal family. Choose a base point $[F_0]\in U_d$, write $C_0=V(F_0)$, and choose an orientation-preserving identification of the underlying surface of $C_0$ with a closed connected oriented surface $\Sigma_g$ of genus $g$. Write \[ \operatorname{Mod}_g=\pi_0\operatorname{Diff}^{+}(\Sigma_g). \] The bundle monodromy gives a homomorphism \[ \rho_d:\pi_1(U_d,[F_0])\longrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}_g, \qquad \Gamma_d=\operatorname{im}(\rho_d). \]

An $r$-spin structure here means an isomorphism class of a topological complex line bundle $L$ together with an isomorphism $L^{\otimes r}\cong K$, where $K$ is the complex cotangent line bundle of the oriented surface equipped with a compatible complex structure. The associated topological classes carry the natural pullback action of $\operatorname{Mod}_g$. Adjunction gives \[ K_{C_0}\cong\mathcal O_{C_0}(d-3), \] so $\mathcal O_{C_0}(1)$, with its induced $r$-th-power identification, determines the associated topological $r$-spin structure $\phi_d$. Define its stabilizer by \[ \operatorname{Mod}_g[\phi_d] =\{[f]\in\operatorname{Mod}_g:f^*\phi_d=\phi_d\}. \] Both groups are expressed using the same identification with $\Sigma_g$; changing that identification conjugates both groups simultaneously.

The proposed assertion is \[ \text{for every integer }d\geq 4,\qquad \Gamma_d=\operatorname{Mod}_g[\phi_d]. \] The boundary case $d=4$ is included, with $r=1$. The complex parameter-space convention and the interpretation of $\phi_d$ as a topological spin structure are explicit interpretive qualifications, rather than details verified from the report. The supplied record labels the assertion open; no proof or independent status determination is asserted here.

Short English statement

For every degree $d\geq 4$, does monodromy of the universal family of smooth plane curves realize every orientation-preserving mapping class that preserves the associated $(d-3)$-spin structure? The displayed formulation uses the customary complex parameter space and the spin structure induced by adjunction, subject to source confirmation.
