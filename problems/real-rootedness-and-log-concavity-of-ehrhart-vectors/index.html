Real-Rootedness and Log-Concavity of Ehrhart Vectors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M\cong{\mathbb{Z}}^d$ be a lattice, let $M_{{\mathbb{R}}}=M\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}$, and let $P\subset M_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ be a full-dimensional lattice polytope. Its Ehrhart polynomial counts lattice points in dilates: $L_P(n)=|nP\cap M|$ for integers $n\geq0$. Define its Ehrhart $h^*$-polynomial and $h^*$-vector by \[ \sum_{n\geq0}L_P(n)t^n=\frac{h_P^*(t)}{(1-t)^{d+1}},\qquad h_P^*(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{s}h_i^*t^i,\qquad (h_0^*,\ldots,h_s^*), \] where $s=\deg h_P^*\leq d$. The coefficients are nonnegative integers and $h_0^*=1$. The polynomial is real-rooted if every complex zero is real; the constant polynomial satisfies this condition vacuously. Its coefficient vector is log-concave if \[ (h_i^*)^2\geq h_{i-1}^*h_{i+1}^*\qquad(1\leq i\leq s-1). \] It is unimodal if there exists $k\in\{0,\ldots,s\}$ such that $h_0^*\leq\cdots\leq h_k^*\geq\cdots\geq h_s^*$.

For context, standard lattice-relative meanings of the geometric properties mentioned in the source are as follows. The polytope is smooth if, at every vertex, the primitive lattice directions of its incident edges form a basis of $M$. It is spanning if \[ \operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\{x-y:x,y\in P\cap M\}=M. \] It is very ample if, for every vertex $v$, \[ \left\{\sum_{x\in P\cap M}a_x(x-v):a_x\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\right\} =\operatorname{cone}(P-v)\cap M, \] where $\operatorname{cone}(P-v)$ denotes the nonnegative real cone generated by $P-v$.

Source question. Can the implication diagram relating Ehrhart $h^*$-vector properties, including unimodality, to geometric properties such as smoothness, very ampleness, and spanning be extended to include real-rootedness of $h_P^*(t)$ or log-concavity of its coefficients?

Short English statement

The question asks how real-rootedness and log-concavity of Ehrhart $h^*$-polynomials fit into an existing diagram connecting coefficient properties and geometric properties of lattice polytopes.
