Simplicity of Twisted Hopf Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 3$ and consider the group data specified in the supplied record: \[ G=\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left(D_i\rtimes_{\varphi_i}M_i\right), \qquad M=\prod_{i=1}^{n}M_i. \] Each factor $D_i\rtimes_{\varphi_i}M_i$ is required to be a Frobenius group, each $M_i$ is abelian of prime-power order, and $|M|$ is a perfect square. No requirement that the primes associated with different $M_i$ coincide is supplied. In the usual finite Frobenius-group interpretation, $D_i$ is the Frobenius kernel and $M_i$ is a complement whose nonidentity elements fix no nonidentity element of $D_i$ under the specified action. The record does not explicitly state finiteness of the $D_i$, although its surrounding discussion concerns finite-dimensional Hopf algebras.

For a field $K$, the group algebra $KG$ has coproduct $\Delta(g)=g\otimes g$, counit $\varepsilon(g)=1$, and antipode $S(g)=g^{-1}$ for $g\in G$. An invertible element $J\in KG\otimes_K KG$ is a twist, with the convention in the supplied excerpt, if \[ (1\otimes J)(\operatorname{id}\otimes\Delta)(J) =(J\otimes1)(\Delta\otimes\operatorname{id})(J), \qquad (\varepsilon\otimes\operatorname{id})(J) =(\operatorname{id}\otimes\varepsilon)(J)=1. \] The twisted Hopf algebra $(KG)^J$ has the same multiplication and counit as $KG$, and coproduct \[ \Delta^J(h)=J\Delta(h)J^{-1}. \] The record requires that $J$ arise from a $2$-cocycle on the character group $\widehat M$. For a specified character group and coefficient field, a normalized multiplicative $2$-cocycle is a map $\alpha:\widehat M\times\widehat M\to K^\times$ satisfying \[ \alpha(\chi,\psi)\alpha(\chi\psi,\rho) =\alpha(\psi,\rho)\alpha(\chi,\psi\rho), \qquad \alpha(1,\chi)=\alpha(\chi,1)=1. \] However, the record does not specify the character field, the hypotheses on $K$, the construction of $J$, or any nondegeneracy condition on the cocycle. The square-order assumption is retained without treating it as a supplied nondegeneracy hypothesis.

Under the usual Hopf-algebra meaning, a Hopf algebra $H$ is simple if its only normal Hopf subalgebras are $K1$ and $H$. Here a Hopf subalgebra $A\subseteq H$ is normal when, for all $h\in H$ and $a\in A$, both \[ \sum h_{(1)}aS(h_{(2)})\in A, \qquad \sum S(h_{(1)})ah_{(2)}\in A, \quad \Delta(h)=\sum h_{(1)}\otimes h_{(2)}. \] The supplied record does not explicitly define simplicity.

Question as supplied, with unresolved qualifications. For the above data and $n\geq3$, is $(KG)^J$ simple? Read universally, this asks whether simplicity holds for every choice of the stated group data and every allowed cocycle twist. The missing field hypotheses, cocycle restrictions, finiteness convention, and intended meaning of simplicity must be resolved before this can be regarded as a fully specified conjecture. The supplied original excerpt introduces twisting and Hopf orders but does not itself contain this question.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether twisting the group algebra of a product of at least three specified Frobenius groups produces a simple Hopf algebra. The assumptions on the field and cocycle, and the intended quantification over twists, remain incomplete. The record labels the problem open; that label and its claimed source verification are supplied curation, not independent verification.
