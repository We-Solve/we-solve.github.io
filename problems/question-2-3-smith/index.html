Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}$ denote the source's mapping class group of a compact oriented genus-$g$ surface with boundary, with boundary fixed pointwise, and let $\delta$ be its specified boundary twist. For one boundary component the usual convention is $\delta=T_{\partial}$; with several boundary components the excerpt does not specify whether $\delta$ is one twist or a boundary multitwist. Positive twists are right Dehn twists about the allowed essential curves, whose exact class must be fixed.

Question 2.3 (Smith). Is there an a priori upper bound on the length $N$ of every factorization \[ \delta=T_{c_1}\cdots T_{c_N} \] into positive Dehn twists in $\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}$? The source's geometric version asks for a bound depending only on genus on the number of singular fibers of a Lefschetz fibration admitting a section of self-intersection $-1$. In the usual sphere-base, one-boundary monodromy interpretation, the target has the quantifier form \[ \exists B(g)<\infty\quad\forall\text{ such genus-}g\text{ fibrations }p, \qquad\#\{\text{singular fibers of }p\}\le B(g). \] Generic distinct critical values identify the number of factors with the number of singular fibers. Base, relative-minimality, allowed-curve, and multiple-boundary conventions needed for the stated equivalence are not spelled out in the excerpt. The question is an upper bound over all positive factorizations, not a lower bound or a bound for the shortest factorization. The source separately mentions existing lower bounds [28] and the difficulty of measuring Dehn-twist rotation on the boundary of the hyperbolic disc in approach [26].

The appended discussion considers arbitrary $T\in\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}^+$, understood as the positive-twist monoid, and positive factorizations with total product $T$. These encode Lefschetz fibrations over a disc with bounded fibers and prescribed boundary monodromy. The boundary carries the corresponding contact/open-book structure $Y$ [10]. The source links classification of such factorizations to a subset of the Stein-filling classification of $Y$ [1,10,15], and cites filling results for lens spaces or singularity links [14,18] and infinitely many inequivalent-filling constructions [20]. The claim that total spaces are Stein needs the appropriate allowable-vanishing-cycle and other hypotheses; positivity alone is not silently asserted to supply every missing filling condition. No bijection with all Stein fillings is claimed. The final "Hence we may ask" introduces a question not included here.

Short English statement

Are positive Dehn-twist factorizations of the boundary twist uniformly bounded in length, or equivalently, under the standard fibration conventions, are genus-fixed Lefschetz fibrations with a square-$-1$ section bounded in singular-fiber count? The source relates broader factorization questions to Stein fillings.
