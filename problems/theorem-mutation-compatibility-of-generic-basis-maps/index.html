Theorem: Mutation Compatibility of Generic-Basis Maps

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a specified algebra $A$, the notation $\operatorname{proj}A$ conventionally denotes the category of finitely generated projective $A$-modules. Its Grothendieck group $K_0(\operatorname{proj}A)$ is generated by isomorphism classes $[P]$, with relations $[P\oplus P']=[P]+[P']$. The supplied record does not specify $A$, its base, or the module-side convention.

The retained source extraction concerns a map \[ I:K_0(\operatorname{proj}A)\longrightarrow\mathcal A_Q^+. \] It asserts that this map is canonical and commutes with mutations, and that, in an unspecified example, its image is the generic basis. The extraction further asserts that these results establish the Fock--Goncharov conjecture in that example.

Unresolved formulation. The assertion about the conjecture is retained solely as a supplied source claim, with its scope restricted to the unidentified example.
