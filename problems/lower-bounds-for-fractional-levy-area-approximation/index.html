Lower Bounds for Fractional Lévy-Area Approximation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a lower bound for fractional Brownian Lévy-area approximation, but does not specify the component covariance, area normalization, or exact sampling convention. The following is a precise candidate formulation under explicitly stated conventional assumptions; these assumptions require confirmation against the source.

Fix $H\in(1/4,1)$. Let $B=(B^1,B^2)$ be a centered Gaussian process with independent standard fractional Brownian components, so that \[ {\mathbb{E}}[B^i_sB^j_t]=\frac{\delta_{ij}}2\left(s^{2H}+t^{2H}-|t-s|^{2H}\right),\qquad s,t\geq0,\quad i,j\in\{1,2\}. \] For $T>0$, let $B^{(m)}$ be the piecewise linear interpolation of $B$ on the dyadic partition of $[0,T]$ into $2^m$ intervals. Use the signed-area convention \[ A_T=\lim_{m\to\infty}^{L^2}\frac12\left(\int_0^T B^{1,(m)}_t\,{\,\mathrm{d}} B^{2,(m)}_t-\int_0^T B^{2,(m)}_t\,{\,\mathrm{d}} B^{1,(m)}_t\right), \] where the integrals before taking the limit are ordinary integrals along piecewise linear paths. For a positive integer $n$, define \[ t_k=\frac{kT}{n}\quad(0\leq k\leq n),\qquad \mathcal G_{n,T}=\sigma\left(B^i_{t_k}:i\in\{1,2\},\ 0\leq k\leq n\right),\qquad \widehat A_{n,T}={\mathbb{E}}[A_T\mid\mathcal G_{n,T}]. \] Thus this convention uses $n$ subintervals and $n$ nonzero observation times, together with the deterministic starting value.

Under these conventions, the candidate lower-bound question is whether \[ \forall H\in(1/4,1)\ \exists c_H>0\ \forall T>0\ \forall n\in\{1,2,\ldots\}:\qquad \left({\mathbb{E}}\left[|A_T-\widehat A_{n,T}|^2\right]\right)^{1/2} \geq c_H T^{2H}n^{-2H+1/2}. \] Here $c_H$ may depend on $H$, but is independent of $T$ and $n$. The supplied record does not resolve whether the intended bound holds for every positive integer $n$, as displayed, or only for all sufficiently large $n$. It also does not establish that the independent planar model and signed-area convention above are exactly those of the report. These qualifications remain unresolved; the displayed formulation is conditional on them.

Short English statement

Can the root mean square error in estimating fractional Brownian Lévy area from equally spaced observations be bounded below by $c_HT^{2H}n^{-2H+1/2}$, for each $H\in(1/4,1)$? The supplied curation labels this question open and describes a matching upper rate. The precise process, area convention, sampling count, and range of $n$ need source confirmation.
