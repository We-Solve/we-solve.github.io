Chern and Degeneracy Classes of Coinvariant Bundles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses a collection of questions, rather than a single fully specified conjecture. Its hypothesis is that the vertex algebra $V$ is not of CohFT-type, where CohFT means cohomological field theory. The excerpt does not specify the definition of CohFT-type, the admissible vertex algebras and module data, or the base parameter space. These hypotheses must be recovered before a definitive formulation is possible; being outside the stated CohFT-type class should not silently be replaced by another condition concerning cohomological field theories.

Let $X$ denote a base parameter space intended by the source, and let $\mathcal E$ be an associated sheaf of coinvariants. Whenever $\mathcal E$ is locally free of finite rank $r$, it is a vector bundle, with total Chern class \[ c(\mathcal E)=1+c_1(\mathcal E)+\cdots+c_r(\mathcal E). \] Here the classes must be interpreted in a specified intersection or cohomology theory on $X$. The source does not select that theory or its coefficients. If the chosen theory contains a specified tautological subring $R^*(X)$, tautologicality of $c_i(\mathcal E)$ means \[ c_i(\mathcal E)\in R^i(X). \] Neither $X$ nor this tautological subring is identified in the supplied excerpt. In a specified cycle theory, an effective class means a class represented by a cycle with nonnegative coefficients; the coefficient convention and any passage to numerical equivalence must also be fixed.

Subject to these unresolved choices, the source asks the following questions for coinvariants associated to $V$ outside the CohFT-type setting.

  1. Compute the Chern classes of the associated bundles. Determine when the higher Chern classes $c_i(\mathcal E)$, for $2\leq i\leq r$, are tautological.
  2. Find sufficient conditions ensuring that the Chern classes $c_i(\mathcal E)$, for $1\leq i\leq r$, are effective. The original question requests sufficient conditions, without asserting that effectiveness always holds.
  3. The excerpt attributes to the reference label $[\mathrm{CLTU23}]$ the existence of infinitely many extremal rays of an effective cone. For each ray $\rho$ in that intended family, determine whether there is a bundle of coinvariants $\mathcal E$ whose first Chern class spans $\rho$. Explicitly, in the common real class space in which that cone is defined, the requested realization is \[ \rho={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\,c_1(\mathcal E),\qquad c_1(\mathcal E)\neq 0. \] A ray $\rho$ of a cone $C$ is extremal if $u,v\in C$ and $u+v\in\rho$ imply $u,v\in\rho$. The space, cone, and family of rays attached to $[\mathrm{CLTU23}]$ are not identified in the supplied record. The distinction between a cone of effective classes and its closure is also unresolved.
  4. Determine the degeneracy loci and their classes for the natural maps between sheaves of coinvariants referred to in the source. In the locally free case, for a specified map $\phi:\mathcal E\to\mathcal F$ of bundles of ranks $e$ and $f$, and an integer $0\leq k<\min(e,f)$, its rank degeneracy locus is \[ D_k(\phi)=\{x\in X:\operatorname{rank}(\phi_x)\leq k\}, \] with scheme structure given locally by the $(k+1)$-minors. The relevant maps, rank thresholds, and meaning of their degeneracy classes remain unspecified. This bundle definition does not settle the source's broader question about maps of sheaves that may fail to be locally free.
No local freeness, expected-codimension hypothesis, or extension of Chern classes to arbitrary coherent coinvariant sheaves is asserted by this formulation. Such hypotheses or constructions require separate specification.

Short English statement

For vertex algebras outside the source's CohFT-type setting, study the Chern classes of associated bundles of coinvariants, identify when higher classes are tautological, and give sufficient conditions for effectiveness. Ask whether the extremal effective rays attributed to $[\mathrm{CLTU23}]$ can be realized by first Chern classes, and study degeneracy loci and classes of natural maps between coinvariant sheaves. The base spaces, finiteness hypotheses, cycle theory, and cited family of rays are insufficiently specified for a definitive conjecture.
