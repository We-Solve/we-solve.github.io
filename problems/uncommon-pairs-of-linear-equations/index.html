Uncommon Pairs of Linear Equations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k\geq 6$ be even, let $q$ be a prime power, and let $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_k)$ and $b=(b_1,\ldots,b_k)$ belong to ${\mathbb{F}}_q^k$. Put \[ L=\begin{pmatrix}a_1&\cdots&a_k\\b_1&\cdots&b_k\end{pmatrix}. \] The hypothesis on the scalar solution space is \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}\ker L=k-2, \] equivalently, $\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}L=2$. For each positive integer $n$, define \[ S_L(n)=\left\{(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\in({\mathbb{F}}_q^n)^k: \sum_{i=1}^k a_i x_i=0,\quad \sum_{i=1}^k b_i x_i=0\right\}. \] For $f:{\mathbb{F}}_q^n\to[0,1]$, let \[ M_L(f)=\frac{1}{|S_L(n)|}\sum_{(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\in S_L(n)} \left(\prod_{i=1}^k f(x_i)+\prod_{i=1}^k(1-f(x_i))\right). \] Thus the average is uniform over all solutions, with repeated coordinates permitted. Following the supplied definition, the pair is common with respect to two colours over ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ if \[ \forall n\geq1\;\forall f:{\mathbb{F}}_q^n\to[0,1],\qquad M_L(f)\geq 2^{1-k}. \] It is uncommon if \[ \exists n\geq1\;\exists f:{\mathbb{F}}_q^n\to[0,1],\qquad M_L(f)<2^{1-k}. \] Source question (Conjecture 16). For every even $k\geq6$, does the rank-two hypothesis imply that the pair is uncommon over every sufficiently large finite field? For a fixed field and coefficient pair, the proposed conclusion is precisely the last displayed strict inequality for some $n$ and $f$.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The record does not specify how the coefficients are fixed or transported as the field varies, or whether the threshold for $q$ may depend on the coefficient pair as well as on $k$. Consequently, a unique quantifier order for the large-field assertion cannot be recovered. The supplied formulation imposes only the rank condition: it gives no additional restrictions on zero coefficients, equation supports, or linear combinations of the two equations. No such restrictions are added here; whether further hypotheses were intended requires mathematical review.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every pair of independent homogeneous linear equations in an even number $k\geq6$ of variables is uncommon over sufficiently large finite fields. Uncommonness means that some dimension and some fractional two-colouring give a monochromatic solution average strictly below $2^{1-k}$. The coefficient conventions, dependence of the field-size threshold, and possible missing hypotheses remain unresolved.
