The sharp Laptev-Safronov threshold and a Lorentz-stable tube obstruction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
For a closed operator, $\sigma_d$ denotes isolated eigenvalues of finite algebraic multiplicity and the resolvent is $(P-z)^{-1}$ where defined. Schatten norm $\|K\|_p$ is the $\ell^p$ norm of singular values; $p=1$ is trace class. Weyl quantization is the symmetric pseudodifferential quantization of a symbol with semiclassical parameter $h$. A pseudomode has unit norm and small residual, which need not identify a true eigenvalue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
The canonical catalogue record bundles source items 7.1--10.1.
  1. For $-\Delta+V$ on $\mathbb R^d$ with complex potential, find the largest applicable $p>d/2$ for a uniform bound placing all non-real eigenvalues in the disk of radius $D(\int|V|^p)^{1/(p-d/2)}$. Also determine behavior of embedded self-adjoint eigenvalues under non-self-adjoint perturbations. The source formulates the Laptev--Safronov proposal $|\lambda|^\gamma\le D_{\gamma,d}\int|V|^{\gamma+d/2}$, for every $d\in\mathbb N$ and $0<\gamma\le d/2$, and records then-known cases $d=1,\gamma=1/2$ and $d\ge2,0<\gamma\le1/2$.
  2. For bounded self-adjoint $A$ and trace-class $K$, assume that for each $\varepsilon>0$, $\sum_{\lambda\in\sigma_d(A+K)}\operatorname{dist}(\lambda,\sigma(A))^{1+\varepsilon}\le C(\varepsilon)\|K\|_{1+\varepsilon}^{1+\varepsilon}$. Does a uniform endpoint estimate $\sum_{\lambda\in\sigma_d(A+K)}\operatorname{dist}(\lambda,\sigma(A))\le C\|K\|_1$ follow?
  3. For appropriate semiclassical Weyl operators $P=p^w(x,hD)$ on $\mathbb R^n$, with $\operatorname{Re}p\ge0$, is the upper resolvent growth $O(1)\exp(O(1)h^{-n})$ sharp, especially for $n\ge2$, for spectral parameters in a bounded complex neighborhood away from the spectrum? The source contrasts superpolynomial or analytic $\exp(c/h)$ pseudomode lower bounds.
  4. Adapt the semiclassical pseudomode construction for smooth Schrödinger potentials to discontinuous potentials, beyond the particular complex Heaviside model mentioned in the source.

Scaling of the eigenvalue-bound subproblem.
For a potential $V$ and dilation factor $a>0$, let $V_a(x)=a^2V(ax)$. Unitary dilation transforms $-\Delta+V_a$ into $a^2(-\Delta+V)$, so an eigenvalue $\lambda$ becomes $a^2\lambda$, while $\int|V_a|^p=a^{2p-d}\int|V|^p$. A homogeneous bound $|\lambda|\le C(\int|V|^p)^\alpha$ can therefore be dilation invariant only if $2=\alpha(2p-d)$, namely $\alpha=(p-d/2)^{-1}$. This checks the proposed exponent but supplies no admissible upper range for $p$.
An endpoint inference that cannot use sequence bounds alone.
The numerical sequence $a_j=1/j$ satisfies $\sum_ja_j^{1+\varepsilon}<\infty$ for every $\varepsilon>0$, whereas $\sum_ja_j=\infty$. Integral comparison proves both assertions. Thus finiteness of all higher-power distance sums does not alone imply the trace-class endpoint. The constants as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$, and the relation to an actual operator perturbation, are indispensable. This sequence is not claimed to be realizable by the operators in the question and is therefore not a counterexample to that endpoint conjecture.
