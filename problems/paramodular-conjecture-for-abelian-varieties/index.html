Math-1064

Paramodular Conjecture for Abelian Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N\geq1$ be odd and squarefree, let $K$ be a totally real number field of degree $d=[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]$, and let $\mathcal O=\mathcal O_K$. Write $K(N)$ for the genus-two paramodular group of level $N$, as in the source; this group notation is distinct from the coefficient field $K$. The proposed paramodular correspondence relates weight-two, degree-two paramodular Hecke newforms $f$, whose Hecke eigenvalues generate $K$, to isogeny classes of simple abelian varieties $A/{\mathbb{Q}}$ satisfying \[ \dim A=2d,\qquad \operatorname{cond}(A)=N^d, \qquad A\text{ has endomorphisms by }\mathcal O. \] The correspondence is required to satisfy \[ L(A,s)=\prod_{\sigma:K\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}}L_{\mathrm{spin}}(f^\sigma,s), \] where $f^\sigma$ denotes the Hecke-eigenvalue conjugate and $L(A,s)$ is the Hasse--Weil $L$-function. The source asks for a bijection, not just existence of an abelian variety for each form.

The raw record additionally states the prime-level specialization: rational nonlift Hecke eigenforms in $S_2^2(K(p))$ should correspond bijectively to rational abelian surfaces of conductor $p$, with \[ L_{\mathrm{spin}}(f,s)=L(A,s). \] Here rational means rational Hecke eigenvalues on the form side and definition over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ on the abelian-variety side. Odd prime $p$ is compatible with the general hypotheses; the raw specialization itself says only "prime."

Several choices affecting a precise bijection are not supplied. The general formulation does not explicitly impose the nonlift restriction that appears in the prime specialization. It also does not say whether forms are normalized eigenforms, eigenlines, or Galois orbits; the product over conjugates naturally loses the distinction between conjugate forms, so no orbit convention is silently chosen. Likewise, "simple," the field of definition of the $\mathcal O$-endomorphisms, whether this action is part of the isogeny data, and the exact local $L$-factor normalization require source review.

Short English statement

The proposed paramodular bijection pairs suitable weight-two genus-two newforms with simple abelian varieties over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ of dimension $2d$ and conductor $N^d$, matching the product of conjugate spinor $L$-functions with the Hasse--Weil function. The raw source also requires nonlift forms in its rational prime-level specialization.
