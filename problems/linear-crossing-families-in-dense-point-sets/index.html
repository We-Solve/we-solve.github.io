Math-7046

Linear Crossing Families in Dense Point Sets

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\ge 2$ be an integer and let $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ have cardinality $n$. With $\|\cdot\|_2$ denoting Euclidean distance, define \[ \delta(P)=\min_{p\ne q\in P}\|p-q\|_2, \qquad \operatorname{diam}(P)=\max_{p,q\in P}\|p-q\|_2. \] A segment spanned by $P$ is a closed straight segment $[a,b]$ with distinct endpoints $a,b\in P$. For this draft, two such segments cross properly if their supporting lines are distinct and their relative interiors intersect. A crossing family is a set of spanned segments any two distinct members of which cross properly. Thus shared endpoints and collinear overlaps do not count as crossings; concurrent proper crossings are permitted.

Question, with the asymptotic constants made explicit. Is it true that for every fixed real $C>0$ there exist a real $c=c(C)>0$ and an integer $n_0=n_0(C)\ge 2$ such that, for every integer $n\ge n_0$ and every set $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ satisfying \[ |P|=n,\qquad \delta(P)=1,\qquad \operatorname{diam}(P)\le C\sqrt n, \] there is a crossing family $\mathcal F$ of segments spanned by $P$ with \[ |\mathcal F|\ge cn? \] The dependence of $c$ and $n_0$ on the fixed diameter constant $C$ is the asymptotic interpretation used here; the source does not explicitly specify this dependence. The source also does not define crossing family or impose general position. The proper-crossing convention above therefore requires confirmation against the source, and no general-position hypothesis has been added. In particular, configurations with collinear points remain within the stated domain.

Short English statement

Must every sufficiently large planar point set with minimum separation exactly $1$ and diameter at most a fixed constant times $\sqrt n$ contain linearly many spanned segments that pairwise cross properly? The proposed linear constant may depend on the diameter constant. The crossing convention and any intended general-position assumptions require review.
