Removing Logarithmic Losses in Multiplicative Approximation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a conventional interpretation of the displayed expressions, let $\alpha,\beta\in{\mathbb{R}}$, let $p$ be a fixed prime, and let $q$ tend to infinity through positive integers. These domain conventions and the intended quantification over $p$ are not explicit in the supplied statement and require confirmation. Define \[ \|x\|=\inf_{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}}|x-n|,\qquad v_p(q)=\max\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}:p^k\mid q\},\qquad |q|_p=p^{-v_p(q)}. \] Use natural logarithms and restrict the expressions below to $q\ge3$; this restriction does not affect their limiting behavior. Put \[ L(q)=q\log q\log\log q,\qquad M(q)=q\log q. \] For $w\in\{L,M\}$, define \[ S_w=\left\{(\alpha,\beta)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2: \liminf_{\substack{q\to\infty\\q\in{\mathbb{N}}}}w(q)\|q\alpha\|\|q\beta\|>0\right\}, \] \[ T_{w,p}=\left\{\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}: \liminf_{\substack{q\to\infty\\q\in{\mathbb{N}}}}w(q)|q|_p\|q\alpha\|>0\right\}. \] Here $\dim_H$ denotes Hausdorff dimension in the usual Euclidean metric. The supplied record presents \[ \dim_H S_L=2,\qquad \dim_H T_{L,p}=1 \] as background results; these assertions are reproduced as supplied and have not been independently verified.

The logarithmic-loss question is whether the corresponding full-dimension conclusions hold after removing $\log\log q$, namely whether \[ \dim_H S_M=2,\qquad \dim_H T_{M,p}=1. \] The record also asks whether "these sets" are winning and what happens when they are intersected with a planar curve. The winning question requires specification of the game, its parameters, and whether it concerns the original sets, the reduced-factor sets, or both. For a planar curve $C\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^2$, the planar set has the well-defined intersection $S_L\cap C$, and likewise $S_M\cap C$ for the reduced-factor version. However, the record specifies neither the class of curves nor a particular intersection conclusion, such as a Hausdorff-dimension formula. Since $T_{w,p}\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$, its intersection with a planar curve also requires an embedding, parametrization, or other interpretation that is not supplied. These portions therefore remain requests for a precise formulation rather than fully specified conjectures.

Short English statement

The supplied record states that two multiplicative Diophantine approximation sets have full Hausdorff dimension when the weight is $q\log q\log\log q$. It asks whether full dimension persists with weight $q\log q$, whether the relevant sets are winning, and how they meet planar curves. The prime quantifier, winning game, curve hypotheses, and intended intersection conclusions need clarification. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
