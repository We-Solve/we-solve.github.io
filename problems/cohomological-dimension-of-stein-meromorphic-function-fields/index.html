Math-3781

Cohomological Dimension of Stein Meromorphic Function Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $K$, let $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ be a separable closure and let $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K)$ be its absolute Galois group. Using the usual Galois-cohomological convention, define \[ \operatorname{cd}(K)=\inf\left\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:H^q_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_K,A)=0\text{ for every discrete torsion }G_K\text{-module }A\text{ and every integer }q>d\right\}, \] with value $\infty$ if the set is empty.

In the usual neighborhood-based terminology, a Stein compact set is a compact subset $S$ of a complex analytic space $X$ that has a basis of Stein open neighborhoods in $X$. A possible meaning of meromorphic functions along $S$ is the ring of meromorphic germs \[ \mathcal M(S)=\varinjlim_{U\supseteq S}\Gamma(U,\mathcal M_X), \] where $U$ ranges over open neighborhoods and $\mathcal M_X$ is the sheaf of meromorphic functions. The supplied record does not specify this construction, the ambient space, or hypotheses ensuring that this ring is a field. It also does not specify how the complex dimension of the compact subset is defined. These conventions therefore require confirmation from the source.

Intended question, subject to unresolved hypotheses. For every Stein compact set $S$ of complex dimension $n$, with $\mathcal M(S)$ understood as the meromorphic function field intended in the source, is it true that \[ \operatorname{cd}\left(\mathcal M(S)\right)=n? \] Here $n$ is the nonnegative integer described as the complex dimension in the supplied statement. No restriction excluding dimension zero is supplied. The record provides no connectedness, normality, irreducibility, reducedness, purity-of-dimension, or ambient hypotheses for this question. Consequently, the displayed question records the intended target, but does not determine a fully specified universal assertion until the meaning of dimension and the field construction are resolved. The question form preserves the original excerpt; the reconstructed statement's instruction to prove the equality is not evidence that the equality is established.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Galois cohomological dimension of the meromorphic function field associated with a Stein compact set equals its complex dimension. The supplied record leaves essential geometric hypotheses and the field construction unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
