Perverse Point Sheaves and the Category $D(X,B)$

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Two requested definitions. Give a correct definition of the category $D(X,B)$ and an intrinsic definition of perverse point sheaves. The source does not define $D(X,B)$; that missing definition is part of the problem, not notation to be replaced automatically by $D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)$. The attached literature assessment places the request in the context of Kawamata log terminal (KLT) pairs, but does not specify the intended field, dimension, boundary coefficients, or axioms for the category.

In the usual characteristic-zero birational setting, a KLT pair consists of a normal variety $X$ and a boundary divisor $B$, with $K_X+B$ real Cartier and every log discrepancy positive. For a log resolution $f:Y\to X$, one writes $K_Y=f^*(K_X+B)+\sum_E a(E,X,B)E$, including the relevant strict transforms in this discrepancy notation, and requires $a(E,X,B)>-1$ for all divisors over $X$. A rational-boundary version uses rational coefficients and ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier $K_X+B$. These are explanatory conventions; the record does not determine which coefficient setting the desired category must handle.

The first request is to attach the intended category to such a pair with a precise construction and appropriate independence of any auxiliary presentation. The required relationship with ordinary derived categories, stacks, or birational maps is not specified, so no particular candidate or equivalence criterion is asserted as the answer. The second request asks for a definition of perverse point sheaves in terms of the intended intrinsic geometric or categorical data. A perverse coherent sheaf is an object in a specified perverse coherent heart of a derived category, and the point objects require further defining conditions. In relative constructions that heart may depend on a morphism $X\to Y$; such a morphism, its hypotheses, the perversity, and what intrinsic permits as input must be specified before this becomes a determined definition problem. A relative construction must not silently be declared intrinsic to $(X,B)$ alone.

The supplied literature triage, checked 2026-08-21, labels the problem partially solved. It reports relative perverse coherent and perverse point sheaf constructions in important threefold-flop settings, including canonical covering stacks, while cautioning that these results do not establish a general $D(X,B)$ theory for arbitrary KLT pairs. This is a dated assessment requiring independent verification, not a claim of a completed general definition.

Short English statement

Define a category attached to the pair $(X,B)$ and characterize perverse point sheaves intrinsically. The supplied triage reports relative constructions for certain threefold flops, while leaving the general KLT-pair theory unresolved. The category's required properties and the allowed intrinsic input are not specified.
