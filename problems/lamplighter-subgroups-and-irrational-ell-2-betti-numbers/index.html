Lamplighter Subgroups and Irrational $\ell^2$-Betti Numbers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be an ambient group. The supplied record does not specify the intended class of groups $G$, and the report title alone does not establish any additional hypotheses on $G$.

For a prime $p$, write ${\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$ for the cyclic group of order $p$. Under the standard restricted regular wreath-product interpretation, for a group $K$ define \[ ({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})\wr K = \left(\bigoplus_{k\in K}{\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}\right)\rtimes K, \] where the direct sum consists of finitely supported functions $f:K\to{\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$, and $K$ acts by left translation: \[ (h\cdot f)(k)=f(h^{-1}k)\qquad(h,k\in K). \] Here containment in $G$ is interpreted as the existence of an injective group homomorphism into $G$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the wreath-product convention; the displayed definition makes this conventional reading explicit and requires confirmation against the source.

The symbol $C(G)$ is not defined in the supplied record. Its title and literature assessment associate it with $\ell^2$-Betti values, but do not determine which values are included, the coefficient ring, or the operators or spaces used to define them. Retaining $C(G)$ as the unspecified set intended by the source, the first question is whether \[ C(G)\not\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists\,p\text{ prime}\;\exists\text{ an injective homomorphism } ({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})\wr{\mathbb{Z}}\longrightarrow G. \] The source then asks, more generally, whether \[ C(G)\not\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists\,p\text{ prime}\;\exists\,K\text{ an infinite finitely generated group}\; \exists\text{ an injective homomorphism } ({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})\wr K\longrightarrow G. \] In each question, $C(G)\not\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}}$ means that some element of $C(G)$ is not rational. These are two proposed equivalences, not asserted results. A fully specified mathematical statement requires the missing definition of $C(G)$ and the intended hypotheses on $G$.

Short English statement

The source asks whether having a non-rational value in the unspecified invariant $C(G)$ is equivalent to containing a lamplighter subgroup $({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})\wr{\mathbb{Z}}$, and asks a second version allowing any infinite finitely generated group $K$ in place of ${\mathbb{Z}}$. The definition of $C(G)$, the ambient group hypotheses, and the wreath-product convention require source review.
