Asymptotic Counting Near Curved Submanifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M\subset{\mathbb{R}}^m$ be a bounded immersed submanifold with boundary, write $n=\dim M$, and put $R=m-n$. The displayed exponent below requires $R>0$; the supplied statement does not explicitly impose this restriction. The source asks for unspecified "proper" curvature conditions on $M$.

The proposed asymptotic has the form \[ N(M;Q,\delta)\sim c_M\delta^R Q^{n+1},\qquad c_M>0, \] as $Q\to\infty$, subject to \[ \delta\ge Q^{-1/R+\epsilon}. \] Here $c_M$ is to depend only on $M$. The original excerpt says "for some $\epsilon>0$." The supplied record does not determine the full quantification over $\epsilon$, the upper range of $\delta$, or the uniformity in $\delta$. Once those choices and the counting function are specified, the symbol $\sim$ means that \[ \frac{N(M;Q,\delta)}{c_M\delta^R Q^{n+1}}\longrightarrow 1 \] along the intended admissible choices of $Q$ and $\delta$.

The supplied excerpt also introduces a local weighted counting problem. For $x_0\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$, $\varepsilon_0>0$, and $f_r\in C^\ell({\mathbb{R}}^n)$, $1\le r\le R$, with $\ell\ge2$, a local graph is \[ M_{\mathrm{loc}}=\{(x,f_1(x),\ldots,f_R(x)):x\in B_{\varepsilon_0}(x_0)\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{n+R}, \] where $B_{\varepsilon_0}(x_0)$ is the open Euclidean ball. Let $w\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n)$ be nonnegative, with support contained in a sufficiently small open neighbourhood of $x_0$, the required smallness depending on the manifold. For $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$, define \[ \|t\|_{{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}}=\min_{b\in{\mathbb{Z}}}|t-b|. \] Reading the damaged summation layout in the supplied excerpt in its natural order gives, for positive integers $Q$ and $0\le\delta\le\tfrac12$, \[ N_w(Q,\delta)=\sum_{q=1}^{Q}\ \sum_{\substack{a\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n\\ \|qf_r(a/q)\|_{{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}}\le\delta\ \text{for every }1\le r\le R}}w(a/q), \qquad N_0=\sum_{q=1}^{Q}\sum_{a\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n}w(a/q). \] Thus these local sums count integer numerator--denominator pairs with weights; their displayed conditions impose no coprimality restriction. The notation $N_0$ suppresses its dependence on $Q$ and $w$.

The excerpt does not define the global quantity $N(M;Q,\delta)$ or identify it with $N_w(Q,\delta)$. In particular, its distance convention, denominator convention, treatment of repeated representations, and treatment of an immersion's multiplicities are unspecified. The local upper bound $\delta\le\tfrac12$ is not explicitly attached to the global conjecture. Nor does the excerpt state the higher-codimension curvature hypothesis or a conclusion for the local weighted count. Consequently, the global display is a qualified conjectural template, not a fully specified mathematical assertion; these missing definitions and hypotheses must be recovered before a precise theorem can be formulated.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture predicts a count of order $\delta^R Q^{\dim M+1}$ near a bounded immersed manifold with boundary, above the stated scale $Q^{-1/R+\epsilon}$. Its global counting function and curvature assumptions are missing. The excerpt supplies a local weighted count, but does not establish how it determines the global conjecture. This statement-only draft requires human mathematical review.
