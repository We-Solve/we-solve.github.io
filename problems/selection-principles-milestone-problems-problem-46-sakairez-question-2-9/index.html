Definitions and mathematical statement

For a topological space $X$, use the customary real-valued interpretation \[ C_p(X)=\{f:X\to{\mathbb{R}}:f\text{ is continuous}\}, \] with the topology inherited from the product ${\mathbb{R}}^X$. Thus a basic neighborhood of $f\in C_p(X)$ is specified by a finite set $F\subseteq X$ and $\varepsilon>0$, and consists of all $g\in C_p(X)$ such that $|g(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon$ for every $x\in F$.

A space $Y$ has the Pytkeev property if, whenever $A\subseteq Y$ and $y\in\overline{A\setminus\{y\}}$, there is a countable family $\mathcal P$ of infinite subsets of $A\setminus\{y\}$ such that every neighborhood $V$ of $y$ contains some $P\in\mathcal P$.

Use the following standard countable-cover convention to express the selection property. An open cover of a space $Y$ is a family of open subsets whose union is $Y$, with $Y$ itself excluded as a member. An open $\omega$-cover is such a cover in which every finite subset of $Y$ is contained in one member. An open $\gamma$-cover is a countably infinite such cover $\mathcal U$ for which \[ \left|\{U\in\mathcal U:y\notin U\}\right|<\infty \qquad(y\in Y). \] Write $\Gamma(Y)$ for the family of these $\gamma$-covers. The property $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Gamma)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_k)_{k\geq1}$ in $\Gamma(Y)$, with no $\mathcal U_k$ having a finite subcover, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_k\subseteq\mathcal U_k$ such that \[ \left\{\bigcup\mathcal F_k:k\geq1\right\}\in\Gamma(Y). \] Finite powers $X^n$, for positive integers $n$, carry the product topology.

The question attributed in the supplied record to $[\mathrm{SakaiRez},\ \text{Question }2.9]$, and reproduced as Problem 46 in Tsaban's list, is whether \[ C_p(X)\text{ has the Pytkeev property} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad (\forall n\geq1)\;X^n\models\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Gamma). \] Here $X$ must range over the class intended by the source. The supplied excerpt does not specify that class or its separation assumptions, so the scope of this quantifier remains unresolved. The real-valued interpretation of $C_p(X)$ and the countable-cover conventions above are explicit standard interpretations requiring confirmation against the complete source; they are not additional hypotheses supplied by the record.

The excerpt also supplies the contextual implication that, if every finite power of $X$ satisfies $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Gamma)$, then every open $\omega$-cover of $X$ is $\omega$-shrinkable, attributed to $[\mathrm{SakaiRez}]$. It says that a positive answer to the displayed question would therefore suffice for an antecedent objective. Neither the definition of $\omega$-shrinkability nor that antecedent objective is supplied, and both remain unresolved here.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether the Pytkeev property of the pointwise topology on the continuous real-valued functions on $X$ forces every positive finite power of $X$ to satisfy $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Gamma)$. The intended class of spaces and the source's cover conventions require confirmation.
