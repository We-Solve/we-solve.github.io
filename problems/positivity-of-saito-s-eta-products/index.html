Math-7458

Positivity of Saito's Eta Products

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $h\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, let $\varphi(h)=|\{1\leq a\leq h:\gcd(a,h)=1\}|$, and let $\mu$ be the Möbius function: $\mu(1)=1$, $\mu(d)=0$ if a prime square divides $d$, and $\mu(d)=(-1)^k$ if $d$ is a product of $k$ distinct primes. For $\tau$ in the complex upper half-plane set $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$ and \[ \eta(\tau)=e^{2\pi i\tau/24}\prod_{m\geq1}(1-q^m),\qquad F_h(\tau)=\eta(h\tau)^{\varphi(h)}\prod_{d\mid h}\eta(d\tau)^{-\mu(d)}. \] The divisors $d$ are positive divisors. Write \[ \alpha_h=\frac{h\varphi(h)-\sum_{d\mid h}d\mu(d)}{24},\qquad F_h(\tau)=e^{2\pi i\alpha_h\tau}\sum_{n\geq0}a_h(n)q^n. \] Equivalently the coefficients are defined by the formal power series \[ \sum_{n\geq0}a_h(n)q^n =\prod_{m\geq1}(1-q^{hm})^{\varphi(h)} \prod_{d\mid h}\prod_{m\geq1}(1-q^{dm})^{-\mu(d)}. \] All exponents of the factors are integers and each coefficient depends on only finitely many factors, so this defines $a_h(n)\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. Factoring out the initial rational power of $q$ makes the Fourier-coefficient convention explicit without requiring $\alpha_h$ to be an integer.

The cleaned conjecture, attributed in the original record to K. Saito, asks whether \[ \forall h\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\qquad a_h(n)\geq0. \] The specifically emphasized case is to prove or disprove this assertion for every $h$ divisible by at least two distinct primes. Nonnegative means zero is allowed; it is not a strict-positivity assertion. Natural numbers are interpreted here as positive because the displayed eta product is not defined at $h=0$.

The original wording singles out that multiple-prime case. The verification field reports that the official report was checked and that Saito's conjecture and the remaining composite case were then stated explicitly. The dated literature assessment, checked 2026-08-21, instead describes an extraction containing unnamed conjectures with missing statements. That description appears to concern an earlier or different extraction and does not assess the explicit eta-product formulation on its own. No proof, classification of already established cases, or independently verified current open status is supplied by this draft.

Short English statement

Saito's conjecture predicts that every coefficient of the displayed eta product, after its initial power of $q$ is separated, is nonnegative. The source particularly asks for the case where $h$ has at least two distinct prime divisors. The explicit repaired statement is retained together with the older, apparently incompatible literature note.
