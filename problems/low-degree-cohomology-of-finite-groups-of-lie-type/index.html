Math-6542

Low-Degree Cohomology of Finite Groups of Lie Type

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Gamma$ be a finite group of Lie type, let $k$ be a coefficient field, and let $M$ be a simple $k\Gamma$-module. Its group cohomology is \[ H^i(\Gamma,M)=\operatorname{Ext}^i_{k\Gamma}(k,M), \] where $k$ denotes the trivial $k\Gamma$-module.

For a specified root datum with chosen simple coroots $\alpha_1^\vee,\ldots,\alpha_\ell^\vee$, the fundamental weights $\omega_j$ satisfy \[ \langle\omega_j,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=\delta_{ij} \qquad(1\leq i,j\leq\ell). \] Here a fundamental dominant highest weight means one of these $\omega_j$, provided it belongs to the character lattice of the chosen algebraic group. A highest-weight description of a finite-group module requires an appropriate algebraic-group realization and a specified relationship between its highest-weight modules and the finite-group modules.

Requested computation. Determine \[ H^1(\Gamma,M)\qquad\text{and}\qquad H^2(\Gamma,M) \] for finite groups of Lie type $\Gamma$ and simple coefficient modules $M$ whose highest weight, in the intended highest-weight realization, is fundamental dominant, throughout the family intended by the source.

Unresolved scope. Short English statement

Compute first and second group cohomology for finite groups of Lie type with simple coefficients labelled by fundamental dominant highest weights. The source leaves the coefficient field, highest-weight realization, group forms, and parameter ranges unspecified. The supplied registry labels the problem partially solved; that label and the accompanying dated literature assessment have not been independently verified.
