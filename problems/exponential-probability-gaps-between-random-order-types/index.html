Exponential Probability Gaps Between Random Order Types

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on ${\mathbb{R}}^2$ such that $\mu(\ell)=0$ for every line $\ell\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$. For a positive integer $n$, let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be independent random points with common distribution $\mu$, and write $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$. These points are almost surely distinct, with no three collinear.

For a labeled configuration $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ in general position, define its orientation data by \[ \chi_x(i,j,k)=\operatorname{sgn}\det(x_j-x_i,x_k-x_i) \] for distinct indices $i,j,k$. A labeled oriented order type records these signs. An unlabeled oriented order type records them up to a permutation of the indices. A convention that also identifies a configuration with its mirror image additionally identifies $\chi_x$ with $-\chi_x$. The supplied record does not specify which convention is intended.

Once an order-type convention is fixed, let $\mathcal T_n$ be the corresponding collection of realizable planar order types on $n$ points in general position, and define \[ p_{\mu,n}(\omega)=\Pr[X\text{ realizes }\omega],\qquad \omega\in\mathcal T_n. \] The cleaned source asks for an absolute constant $c>1$ and order types $\omega_1,\omega_2\in\mathcal T_n$ satisfying \[ p_{\mu,n}(\omega_1)>c^n p_{\mu,n}(\omega_2). \] Here an absolute constant is understood to be independent of both $\mu$ and $n$; the selected order types may depend on $\mu$ and $n$.

Unresolved quantification. The source does not quantify $n$ or give a range or lower threshold. If the intended range were a specified set $I\subseteq{\mathbb{N}}$, the uniform formulation would be \[ \exists c>1\;\forall\mu\text{ charging no lines}\;\forall n\in I\;\exists\omega_1,\omega_2\in\mathcal T_n: \quad p_{\mu,n}(\omega_1)>c^n p_{\mu,n}(\omega_2). \] This is a formulation schema, not an assertion that a particular range $I$ was supplied. An eventual-in-$n$ formulation would also require specifying whether its threshold may depend on $\mu$.

Unresolved source qualifications. The original statement requires only $c>0$, whereas the cleaned statement requires an absolute $c>1$; these conditions are not equivalent. No positivity condition on $p_{\mu,n}(\omega_2)$ is supplied. Consequently, the displayed inequality permits a zero-probability second type and must not silently be replaced by a ratio of positive probabilities. The order-type convention, the quantification of $n$, and the discrepancy concerning $c$ require review before a unique precise conjecture can be stated.

Short English statement

For independent points drawn from a planar probability measure assigning zero mass to every line, the cleaned record asks whether two order types can have probabilities separated by a factor exceeding $c^n$, with a universal $c>1$. The range of $n$, the order-type convention, and whether both probabilities must be positive are unspecified. The original record instead says $c>0$. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
