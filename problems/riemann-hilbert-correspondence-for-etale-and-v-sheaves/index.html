Riemann–Hilbert Correspondence for Étale and v-Sheaves

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ denote the prime occurring in the intended correspondence, and let $\mathbb F_p=\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$. An étale $\mathbb F_p$-sheaf means a sheaf of $\mathbb F_p$-vector spaces on an étale site; the underlying geometric object and site must be specified to determine its category. Such a sheaf is annihilated by $p$. The supplied record does not define the overconvergence condition or specify the intended meaning of $v$-sheaves in the proposed extension.

The extracted question is the following: in the geometric setting of the Riemann--Hilbert correspondence referred to in the source passage, extend that correspondence from overconvergent étale $\mathbb F_p$-sheaves to all étale $\mathbb F_p$-sheaves in that setting. The source further conjectures an extension to $v$-sheaves. Each extension is required to recover the preceding correspondence on its original domain, up to the appropriate natural identification.

This is an unresolved formulation qualification: the supplied passage refers to "the above Riemann--Hilbert correspondence," but does not supply that correspondence. In particular, its ambient spaces, target category, functor, definition of overconvergence, and equivalence or other correspondence properties are absent. The passage also does not determine whether the final extension concerns sheaves of $\mathbb F_p$-vector spaces for the $v$-topology, geometric objects called $v$-sheaves, or another construction specified in the omitted context. Consequently, the requested extension cannot be stated as a fully specified functorial conjecture from this record alone. No constructibility, finiteness, boundedness, or geometric hypotheses are added here.

The registry labels the problem partially solved. Its supplied dated assessment says that a $p$-torsion Riemann--Hilbert correspondence is established for overconvergent sheaves and small $v$-stacks, while the conjectural extension to all étale sheaves and $v$-sheaves is incomplete. Short English statement

Extend the source's $p$-torsion Riemann--Hilbert correspondence from overconvergent étale $\mathbb F_p$-sheaves to all étale $\mathbb F_p$-sheaves, and conjecturally to $v$-sheaves, compatibly with the original correspondence. The supplied excerpt omits the ambient setting, target category, and meaning of the final extension, so a fully precise conjecture requires the preceding source context. The partially solved status is a supplied dated assessment.
