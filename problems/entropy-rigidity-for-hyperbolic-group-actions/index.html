Entropy Rigidity for Hyperbolic Group Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual terminology, a metric space $(X,d)$ is proper if every closed bounded subset is compact, geodesic if every two points are joined by a distance-realizing geodesic segment, and Gromov hyperbolic if its geodesic triangles are $\delta$-thin for some $\delta\geq 0$. A geometric action of a discrete group $\Gamma$ means a proper, cocompact action by isometries. Write $\partial_\infty X$ for the Gromov boundary. The supplied hypothesis is \[ \partial_\infty X\cong S^n \] as topological spaces. Here $n$ is conventionally a nonnegative integer; the source record does not explicitly specify its range.

For a basepoint $o\in X$, use the usual orbit-counting definition of the critical exponent: \[ e(\Gamma;X,d)=\limsup_{R\to\infty}\frac{1}{R}\log\#\{\gamma\in\Gamma:d(o,\gamma o)\leq R\}. \] This quantity is independent of the basepoint and is denoted by $e(\Gamma)$ in the supplied statement. This explicit definition uses the usual discrete-group interpretation of a geometric action, which the record does not separately specify.

Under the additive-error convention, a rough isometry from $X$ to a metric space $Y$ is a map $f:X\to Y$ for which there exist constants $A,B\geq0$ such that \[ \left|d_Y(f(x),f(x'))-d(x,x')\right|\leq A\qquad(x,x'\in X), \] and every point of $Y$ lies within distance $B$ of $f(X)$. Write $\mathbb H^{n+1}$ for real hyperbolic space, conventionally normalized to sectional curvature $-1$.

Question as supplied. For every proper geodesic Gromov hyperbolic space $X$ with a geometric action by $\Gamma$ and $\partial_\infty X\cong S^n$, is it true that \[ e(\Gamma)\geq n, \qquad e(\Gamma)=n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X\text{ is rough-isometric to }\mathbb H^{n+1}? \] Unresolved qualifications. In particular, the record gives no metric-scale normalization or additional curvature hypothesis. The numerical critical exponent depends on metric scale, so the bound and equality target require clarification. Short English statement

The extracted question asks whether a geometric group action on a proper geodesic hyperbolic space with boundary $S^n$ has critical exponent at least $n$, with equality precisely when the space is rough-isometric to $\mathbb H^{n+1}$.
