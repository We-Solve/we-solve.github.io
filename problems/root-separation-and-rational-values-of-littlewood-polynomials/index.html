Root Separation and Rational Values of Littlewood Polynomials

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $d\geq 1$, let \[ \mathcal P_d=\left\{\sum_{j=0}^{d}a_jx^j:a_j\in\{-1,0,1\}\right\}, \qquad \mathcal P_d^*=\mathcal P_d\setminus\{0\}. \] Thus degrees are at most $d$, and the displayed source family includes the zero polynomial. The source asks about minimum root distances and minimum rational evaluations, and its reconstructed statement requests the following exponential lower bounds. Necessary qualifications and unresolved interpretations are made explicit below.

For $K\in\{{\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}}\}$, define \[ \Delta_d(K)=\min\left\{|\alpha-\beta|:\ P,Q\in\mathcal P_d^*,\ \alpha,\beta\in K,\ P(\alpha)=Q(\beta)=0,\ \alpha\ne\beta\right\}. \] Here $P$ and $Q$ are independently chosen; they are not required to be distinct. Excluding the zero polynomial is necessary to obtain the intended finite collection of polynomial roots. The source does not explicitly specify whether the roots are real or complex, so $K$ remains an unresolved parameter. For the intended choice of $K$, the root-separation question is whether \[ \exists C_1>0\ \forall d\geq1:\qquad \Delta_d(K)\geq \exp(-C_1d), \] with $C_1$ an absolute constant.

For a rational number $r=p/q\in[9/10,19/20]$, represented with $p,q\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, $q>0$, and $\gcd(p,q)=1$, define the minimum positive evaluation \[ m_d^+(p/q)=\min\left\{|P(p/q)|:\ P\in\mathcal P_d,\ P(p/q)\ne0\right\}. \] The source instead writes $\min_{P\in\mathcal P_d}|P(p/q)|$. That literal minimum is zero because $0\in\mathcal P_d$; consequently it cannot satisfy the proposed positive lower bound. The definition of $m_d^+$ is an explicit candidate repair requiring review, not an unqualified transcription.

Under this repair, the recorded baseline is $m_d^+(p/q)\geq q^{-d}$. A precise version of the requested improvement with an absolute exponential rate is \[ \begin{gathered} \exists C_2>0\ \forall r=p/q\in{\mathbb{Q}}\cap[9/10,19/20]\ \exists c_{p,q}>0\ \forall d\geq1:\\ m_d^+(p/q)\geq c_{p,q}\exp(-C_2d). \end{gathered} \] Here $c_{p,q}$ may depend on the fixed reduced rational but not on $d$. The source explicitly fixes $p/q$, but does not fully specify whether the second exponential rate must be uniform over these rationals. The displayed quantifier order expresses the absolute-rate interpretation and requires confirmation. If $C_2$ may depend arbitrarily on $p/q$, the stated baseline already has that exponential form, so the intended meaning of improvement would need further specification.

Short English statement

The record asks whether roots of polynomials with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$ have an absolute exponential separation bound, and whether their nonzero values at fixed rationals in $[9/10,19/20]$ admit a stronger exponential lower bound. The root domain, exclusion of zero evaluations, and uniformity of the second exponential rate require clarification. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
