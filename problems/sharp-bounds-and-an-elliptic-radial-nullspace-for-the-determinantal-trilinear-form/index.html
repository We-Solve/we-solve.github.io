Math-1292

Sharp bounds and an elliptic-radial nullspace for the determinantal trilinear form

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $x,y\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$, put $\det(x,y)=x_1y_2-x_2y_1$. On Schwartz inputs define the determinant-symmetric truncation \[T_\varepsilon(f,g,h)=\int_{|\det(x,y)|>\varepsilon}\frac{f(x)g(y)h(x+y)}{\det(x,y)}{\,\mathrm{d}} x{\,\mathrm{d}} y,\qquad\varepsilon>0,\] and, when it exists, $T=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}T_\varepsilon$. The denominator is signed. The original displayed integral does not specify a principal value; this is the aid's intended reconstruction.

Original question (Street). Determine exponents for which \[|T(f,g,h)|\le C_{p,q,r}\|f\|_{L^p({\mathbb{R}}^2)}\|g\|_{L^q({\mathbb{R}}^2)}\|h\|_{L^r({\mathbb{R}}^2)}.\] The source already says a quite satisfactory answer is given by Gressman et al. [7], without supplying that bibliography.

Attached unreviewed sharp-range account. The aid identifies $T$ with three times the Gressman--He--Kovač--Street--Thiele--Yung form after reflecting the third input, and reports the sharp strong-type Banach range \[2<p,q,r<\infty,\qquad\frac1p+\frac1q+\frac1r=1.\] It reports failure outside that range for the corresponding strong Lebesgue estimate, without claiming an exclusion of every weak-type or Lorentz endpoint variant. The exact normalization of the cited form and publication details are absent; the factor three is retained as the aid's normalization statement.

The aid additionally claims an elliptic-radial nullspace. For any positive-definite quadratic form $Q$ on ${\mathbb{R}}^2$, call $f$ $Q$-radial if it depends only on $Q[x]$. For every $\varepsilon>0$, $T_\varepsilon(f,g,h)=0$ whenever any two inputs are $Q$-radial, initially with inputs for which the truncated integral is defined, such as Schwartz functions. The two radial inputs must use the same $Q$.

Let $P_Q$ be averaging over the compact determinant-one $Q$-orthogonal rotation group and $d_Qf=f-P_Qf$. The aid's angular-defect consequence in the sharp range can be expressed, for the first two chosen inputs, as \[|T(f,g,h)|\le C_{p,q,r}\left(\|d_Qf\|_p\|g\|_q+\|f\|_p\|d_Qg\|_q\right)\|h\|_r,\] with analogous inequalities for either other selected pair. The averaging is measure preserving and contractive in the indicated norms. This records the aid's cancellation/refinement claim; it does not supply a new proof of the sharp theorem or an unspecified endpoint estimate. All statements require independent review.

Short English statement

Street asks for bounds on a signed determinant trilinear principal value. The aid reports the sharp $p,q,r>2$ Banach range and an additional cancellation when any two inputs are radial for the same ellipse geometry, yielding bounds by their angular defects. The principal-value and normalization conventions are explicit.
