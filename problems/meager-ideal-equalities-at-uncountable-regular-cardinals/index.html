Meager Ideal Equalities at Uncountable Regular Cardinals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\kappa$ be an uncountable regular cardinal, meaning $\kappa>\aleph_0$ and $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\kappa$, and assume \[ 2^{<\kappa}:=\sup_{\lambda<\kappa}2^\lambda=\kappa. \] For $f,g\in\kappa^\kappa$, define eventual domination by \[ f\leq^* g\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists\alpha<\kappa\ \forall\beta\in[\alpha,\kappa)\; f(\beta)\leq g(\beta). \] Define the bounding and dominating numbers by \[ \mathfrak b_\kappa= \min\left\{|B|:B\subseteq\kappa^\kappa\ \text{and}\ \neg\exists g\in\kappa^\kappa\ \forall f\in B\;(f\leq^*g)\right\}, \] \[ \mathfrak d_\kappa= \min\left\{|D|:D\subseteq\kappa^\kappa\ \text{and}\ \forall f\in\kappa^\kappa\ \exists g\in D\;(f\leq^*g)\right\}. \] The supplied record does not define the underlying space or the precise convention for $\mathcal M_\kappa$. The following definitions therefore provide a conditional formulation. For a specified choice $A=\{0,1\}$ or $A=\kappa$, put $X=A^\kappa$ and give $X$ the topology with basic open sets \[ [s]=\{x\in A^\kappa:x\restriction\alpha=s\}, \qquad \alpha<\kappa,\quad s\in A^\alpha. \] A set $N\subseteq X$ is nowhere dense if $\operatorname{int}_X(\overline N^{\,X})=\varnothing$. Under the convention that a $\kappa$-meager set is covered by at most $\kappa$ nowhere dense sets, define \[ \mathcal M_\kappa^X= \left\{B\subseteq X:\exists\langle N_\xi:\xi<\kappa\rangle\; \left[\forall\xi<\kappa\;(N_\xi\text{ is nowhere dense in }X) \ \text{and}\ B\subseteq\bigcup_{\xi<\kappa}N_\xi\right]\right\}. \] For an ideal $\mathcal I$ on $X$, its additivity and cofinality are \[ \operatorname{add}(\mathcal I)= \min\left\{|\mathcal F|:\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal I, \ \bigcup\mathcal F\notin\mathcal I\right\}, \] \[ \operatorname{cof}(\mathcal I)= \min\left\{|\mathcal F|:\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal I, \ \forall B\in\mathcal I\ \exists C\in\mathcal F\;(B\subseteq C)\right\}. \] Question. For every uncountable regular cardinal $\kappa$ satisfying $2^{<\kappa}=\kappa$, must both equalities \[ \operatorname{add}(\mathcal M_\kappa)=\mathfrak b_\kappa \qquad\text{and}\qquad \mathfrak d_\kappa=\operatorname{cof}(\mathcal M_\kappa) \] hold? Here identifying $\mathcal M_\kappa$ with the explicitly defined $\mathcal M_\kappa^X$ requires confirmation of the intended space and covering convention. The conditional definitions above do not assert that the two choices of $X$ give equivalent questions.

Short English statement

For every uncountable regular cardinal $\kappa$ with $2^{<\kappa}=\kappa$, does the additivity of the generalized meager ideal equal the bounding number, and does its cofinality equal the dominating number? The source leaves the underlying space and meagerness convention unspecified, so the explicit formulation requires confirmation of those conventions.
