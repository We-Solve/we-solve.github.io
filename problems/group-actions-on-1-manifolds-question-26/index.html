Group Actions on 1-Manifolds — Question 26

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $\operatorname{Diff}^t_+(S^1)$ denote the orientation-preserving circle diffeomorphisms of class $C^t$. For $f\in\operatorname{Diff}^t_+(S^1)$, choose a lift $F\colon{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfying $F(x+1)=F(x)+1$. Its rotation number is \[ \rho(f)=\left[\lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{F^k(x)-x}{k}\right]\in{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}. \] The class is independent of the lift and of $x$. Irrational rotation number means $\rho(f)\notin{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$. Write $f^k$ for the $k$-fold iterate and $\operatorname{id}_{S^1}$ for the identity map.

The source asks to determine the regularity values $r>1$ for which there exists $s\ge r$, chosen independently of $f$ and its irrational rotation number, such that every $C^s$ circle diffeomorphism with irrational rotation number satisfies \[ f^{q_n}\longrightarrow\operatorname{id}_{S^1} \qquad\text{in the }C^r\text{ topology as }n\to\infty, \] where $(q_n)$ is described in the source as the sequence of denominators of the rational approximations of the rotation number.

Unresolved conventions. The supplied record does not specify which rational approximations define $(q_n)$. If the intended approximations are the regular continued-fraction convergents $p_n/q_n$ of the representative $\alpha(f)\in(0,1)$ of $\rho(f)$, with each fraction reduced and $q_n>0$, then the requested set has the following conditional formulation: \[ \left\{r\in{\mathbb{R}}:r>1,\ \exists s\in{\mathbb{R}},\ s\ge r,\ \forall f\in\operatorname{Diff}^s_+(S^1),\quad \rho(f)\notin{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}}\ \Longrightarrow\ f^{q_n(\alpha(f))}\xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{C^r}\operatorname{id}_{S^1} \right\}. \] The continued-fraction interpretation requires confirmation. The displayed real-exponent formulation also requires a specified meaning of $C^t$ and its topology for nonintegral $t$; the record does not state whether only integer regularities are intended. For an integer $r=m$, convergence in $C^m$ means uniform convergence of the maps and their derivatives through order $m$ in smooth local coordinates. Orientation preservation is made explicit because it is the setting of the rotation number used here; it is implicit in the source wording.

Short English statement

Determine which regularities $r>1$ admit a single higher regularity $s\ge r$ such that every $C^s$ circle diffeomorphism with irrational rotation number has iterates approaching the identity in $C^r$ along the specified approximation denominators. The intended approximation sequence and the convention for noninteger regularity require clarification. The supplied registry labels this problem open; that label is not independently verified.
