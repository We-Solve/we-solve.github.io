Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.92

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ denote the field with $p$ elements. A pre-Lie algebra over ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ is an ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector space $A$ equipped with an ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-bilinear product satisfying \[ (xy)z-x(yz)=(yx)z-y(xz)\qquad(x,y,z\in A). \] Define $L_1(A)=A$ and \[ L_{r+1}(A)=\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}\{xy:x\in A,\ y\in L_r(A)\}\qquad(r\geq 1). \] The algebra is left nilpotent if $L_n(A)=\{0\}$ for some integer $n\geq 1$; thus the products in this definition are nested to the right, as in the source.

A left brace is a set $A$ with operations $+$ and $\circ$ such that $(A,+)$ is an abelian group, $(A,\circ)$ is a group, and \[ a\circ(b+c)+a=a\circ b+a\circ c\qquad(a,b,c\in A). \] Write \[ a*b=a\circ b-a-b. \] An ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-brace is a left brace whose additive group is equipped with an ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector-space structure satisfying \[ a*(\alpha b)=\alpha(a*b)\qquad(a,b\in A,\ \alpha\in{\mathbb{F}}_p). \]

  1. (a) Does there exist a function $B:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}$, with $B(k)\geq 2$, such that for every $k\in{\mathbb{N}}$, every prime $p>B(k)$, and every ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-brace $A$ of cardinality $p^k$, the operation \[ a\cdot b=-\sum_{i=0}^{p-2}(2^i)^{-1}\left((2^i a)*b\right)\qquad(a,b\in A) \] makes the same vector space $A$ a pre-Lie algebra? Here $2^i a$ denotes scalar multiplication and $(2^i)^{-1}$ is the inverse in ${\mathbb{F}}_p$. The threshold formulation expresses the source phrase "$p$ sufficiently large relative to $k$"; no numerical bound is supplied. Taking $B(k)\geq 2$ ensures that the displayed inverses exist.
  2. (b) For every $k\in{\mathbb{N}}$ and every prime $p>2^k$, is there a bijective correspondence between ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-braces of cardinality $p^k$ and left nilpotent pre-Lie algebras over ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ of cardinality $p^k$? The source does not specify whether this correspondence concerns structures on a fixed underlying vector space, isomorphism classes, or a correspondence with additional compatibility properties. It also does not explicitly identify the correspondence with the operation in part (a). These aspects of the intended target remain unresolved.

The source includes a comment dated 2025: if \[ 2\equiv\xi^{p^{n-1}}\pmod p, \] where $\xi$ is a primitive root modulo $p$, and if $A$ is strongly nilpotent of nilpotency index less than $p$, then the conclusion of part (a) follows from the cited 2022 work of A. Smoktunowicz. The parameter $n$ and the meaning and indexing convention of strong nilpotency are not defined in the supplied record, so this comment cannot be made into a fully specified auxiliary assertion without further source clarification. The comment also states that the result was not known without strong nilpotency; this is retained as a dated source statement.

The supplied later discussion labels part (a) an affirmative but unverified AI solution claim and labels part (b) solved affirmatively. These are supplied source assessments, not mathematical conclusions verified in this draft. The record describes the questions as seeking an analogue of the Lazard correspondence and states that an affirmative answer to either would have consequences for set-theoretic solutions of the Yang--Baxter equation and for Hopf--Galois extensions; no precise consequences are supplied.

Short English statement

The questions ask whether a weighted sum of brace products always produces a pre-Lie algebra when $p$ is sufficiently large relative to $k$, and whether braces and left nilpotent pre-Lie algebras of size $p^k$ correspond bijectively when $p>2^k$. The intended meaning of correspondence and parts of the 2025 comment need clarification. The supplied record labels part (b) solved and reports an unverified affirmative AI claim for part (a); neither assessment is independently verified here.
