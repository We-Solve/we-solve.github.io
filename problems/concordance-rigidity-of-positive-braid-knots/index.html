Math-4996

Concordance Rigidity of Positive Braid Knots

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 1$, let $B_n$ be the braid group with standard generators $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$. A positive braid is a braid represented by a word using only these generators with positive exponents. A positive braid knot is a knot in $S^3$ isotopic to the closure of such a braid, where the closure has one component. Allowing the empty braid includes the unknot; the supplied record states no nontriviality restriction.

Here is a precise smooth, unoriented formulation of the supplied question. Let $K_0,K_1\subset S^3$ be arbitrary smooth positive braid knots, with no restriction on their braid indices. They are smoothly concordant if there is a smooth proper embedding \[ F:S^1\times[0,1]\longrightarrow S^3\times[0,1] \] with \[ F(S^1\times\{i\})=K_i\times\{i\}\qquad(i=0,1), \] whose interior lies in $S^3\times(0,1)$. They are ambiently isotopic if there is a smooth ambient isotopy $H_t:S^3\to S^3$, $t\in[0,1]$, satisfying $H_0=\operatorname{id}_{S^3}$ and $H_1(K_0)=K_1$.

The question is whether, for every such pair, \[ K_0\text{ and }K_1\text{ are smoothly concordant} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad K_0\text{ and }K_1\text{ are ambiently isotopic}. \]

The supplied record does not specify the concordance category or orientation conventions. The displayed formulation explicitly uses smooth concordance and unoriented ambient isotopy; it is a candidate interpretation requiring review, not a verified identification of the source's intended conventions. A topological concordance version would instead require a locally flat properly embedded annulus. If knots are regarded as oriented, the concordance must respect the prescribed boundary orientations, with boundary $(-K_0)\sqcup K_1$, and the isotopy must preserve knot orientations. These choices remain unresolved in the supplied evidence.

Short English statement

Must any two concordant positive braid knots in $S^3$ be isotopic? The question concerns all positive braid knots, with unrestricted braid index. A precise smooth formulation is given above, but the source does not specify the smooth versus topological concordance category or orientation conventions. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified.
