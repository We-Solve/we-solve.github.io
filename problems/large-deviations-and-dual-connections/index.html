Math-13021

Large Deviations and Dual Connections

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A sequence of random variables $Z_n$ satisfies a large-deviation principle with speed $a_n\to\infty$ and lower-semicontinuous rate $I$ if the lower bound on open sets and upper bound on closed sets have the form \[ -\inf_{x\in O}I(x)\leq\liminf_n\frac1{a_n}\log\Pr(Z_n\in O), \qquad \limsup_n\frac1{a_n}\log\Pr(Z_n\in C)\leq-\inf_{x\in C}I(x). \] Relative entropy of probabilities is \[ D(P\|Q)=\int\log\left(\frac{dP}{dQ}\right)\,dP \quad(P\ll Q),\qquad D(P\|Q)=+\infty\text{ otherwise}. \] The source concerns large-deviation settings with relative-entropy rate functions; not every large-deviation rate is asserted to have this form.

On an information manifold with metric $g$, two affine connections $\nabla,\nabla^*$ are dual when \[ Xg(Y,Z)=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_X^*Z) \] for vector fields $X,Y,Z$. Torsion, flatness, choice of statistical model, and any $\alpha$-connection parameter require additional hypotheses.

Research theme (Shun-ichi Amari). Investigate how relative-entropy large-deviation principles relate to dual-connection structures in information geometry. In particular, seek precise links between their probabilistic rate functions/divergences and the geometric metric/connection data. The excerpt does not prescribe a class of probability models, a rate-function construction, a specific dual pair, or a theorem to establish, so the theme is not a uniquely quantified universal assertion.

The supplied summary says the relative-entropy/canonical-divergence connection is well developed, refers to dual or $\alpha$-connection structures, and leaves a fully general exhaustive theory open/in flux. It gives no precise theorem or regularity hypotheses. This does not assert that arbitrary dual-connection structures are realized by entropy-rate LDPs, nor that one relative-entropy divergence realizes every parameter choice. The record's open label is a research-theme status rather than a declaration that the basic connection is unproved.

Short English statement

Study how large-deviation principles with relative-entropy rate functions encode dual affine connections in information geometry. The basic link is reported as established, but the source asks a broad research theme without specifying models, connection classes, or a target theorem.
