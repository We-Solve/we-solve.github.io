Kourovka Notebook Problem 7.58

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be an absolutely free group with a chosen free basis $X=\{x_i:i\in I\}$, let $R\trianglelefteq F$, and let $\mathfrak V$ be any variety of groups. No finiteness assumption on $I$ or $F/R$ is imposed. For a group $H$, let $\mathfrak V(H)$ denote the verbal subgroup generated by all values in $H$ of words that are identities in $\mathfrak V$. Put $Q=F/R$ and $q_i=x_iR$.

A relatively free group in $\mathfrak V$ on a specified set $S$ means the quotient of the absolutely free group on $S$ by its $\mathfrak V$-verbal subgroup. Let $A$ be relatively free in $\mathfrak V$ on generators indexed by $I$. Use the following basis-dependent realization of its $\mathfrak V$-verbal wreath product with $Q$. Let $B$ be relatively free in $\mathfrak V$ on the symbols \[ \{a_{i,t}:i\in I,\ t\in Q\}. \] Let $Q$ act on $B$ by $q\cdot a_{i,t}=a_{i,qt}$, and set \[ W=B\rtimes Q,\qquad (b,q)(c,r)=(b(q\cdot c),qr). \] The embedding recalled in the source is represented, with these conventions, by the induced map \[ \iota:F/\mathfrak V(R)\longrightarrow W, \qquad \iota\left(x_i\mathfrak V(R)\right)=(a_{i,1_Q},q_i). \] The source supplies the assertion that this induced map is an isomorphic embedding.

Problem. For arbitrary $F$, $R$, and $\mathfrak V$ as above, find a criterion characterizing precisely the elements of $W$ in the image of $\iota$; that is, give necessary and sufficient conditions on $(b,q)\in B\rtimes Q$ for \[ (b,q)\in\iota\left(F/\mathfrak V(R)\right). \] The source does not explicitly specify the embedding or wreath-product conventions; the realization above fixes these choices for this draft and requires comparison with the cited construction during mathematical review. The source states that a criterion is already known when $\mathfrak V$ is the variety of all abelian groups. It also records an explicitly unverified AI claim of a general criterion; that claim is not adopted here as an established solution.

Short English statement

Given a free group $F$, a normal subgroup $R$, and a variety of groups $\mathfrak V$, characterize the image of the source's embedding of $F/\mathfrak V(R)$ into the corresponding verbal wreath product. The source reports a known criterion for the abelian variety and an unverified AI claim for the general problem.
