OpenAI's claim

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
There is a polynomial-time uniform quantum circuit family with $\mathbb P(\text{output is the prime factorization of }N)=1$ for every $N\ge2$. Resources grow polynomially in $\lceil\log_2N\rceil$, with separate bounds for gates and qubits. One fixed finite gate set suffices: \[ \mathrm{NOT},\ \mathrm{CNOT},\ \mathrm{Toffoli},\ H,\ \operatorname{diag}(1,i), \] together with inverses and singly controlled versions. This is exact success, rather than merely success approaching one.

Outline of the argument

Order trials are arranged to have prescribed success probabilities while preserving exact classical records. Circuit phase functions and fraction-label recovery replace arbitrary-angle approximations, and exact amplification plus recursive arithmetic gives a worst-case polynomial factorization family.
