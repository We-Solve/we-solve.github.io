Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 15

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A foliation by planes of ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ is a foliation $\mathcal F$ whose leaves are each homeomorphic to ${\mathbb{R}}^2$. Its leaf space is the quotient \[ L(\mathcal F)={\mathbb{R}}^3/{\sim_{\mathcal F}},\qquad x\sim_{\mathcal F}y\iff x\text{ and }y\text{ belong to the same leaf}, \] equipped with the quotient topology. Two such foliations $\mathcal F$ and $\mathcal G$ are topologically equivalent if there exists a homeomorphism $H:{\mathbb{R}}^3\to{\mathbb{R}}^3$ carrying every leaf of $\mathcal F$ onto a leaf of $\mathcal G$.

Requested argument. Supply an alternative argument for the assertion attributed in the source to Palmeira's theorem that the leaf space determines a foliation by planes of ${\mathbb{R}}^3$, avoiding the disputed step asserting that an infinite braid extends to an isotopy of the plane. Under a topological interpretation of "determines," the intended conclusion would have the form \[ L(\mathcal F)\cong L(\mathcal G) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal F\text{ and }\mathcal G\text{ are topologically equivalent}, \] for every pair $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$ satisfying the hypotheses of the relevant theorem.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not state those hypotheses, the regularity of the foliations, or the intended category of equivalence. Consequently, the displayed implication records a possible topological meaning of the requested conclusion; it is not an unconditional assertion for all foliations by planes. It asks only for the existence of an equivalence when the leaf spaces are homeomorphic, without requiring a lift of each specified leaf-space homeomorphism. Whether the original theorem requires that stronger conclusion is unspecified. The source reports counterexamples to the infinite-braid extension proposition, but supplies neither its precise formulation nor the counterexamples. That report is retained as a source qualification and is not independently verified here.

Short English statement

Find an alternative argument for the claim attributed to Palmeira that the leaf space determines a foliation of ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ by planes, avoiding the infinite-braid extension step disputed by the source. The theorem's precise hypotheses and intended notion of equivalence must be recovered before the target can be stated without qualification.
