Math-1640

Chern and slope obstructions for pseudoholomorphic plane foliations on the four-torus

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M^{2n},J)$ be an almost complex manifold, $J^2=-I$. A pseudoholomorphic line in the source is a map $f:{\mathbb{R}}^2\to M$ satisfying \[ f_y=J(f)f_x. \] Problem 30 (Victor Bangert). Develop a global, nonperturbative theory of foliations or laminations by such curves. The source attributes to Moser a perturbative theorem on $\mathbb T^{2n}$: when $J$ is close to a standard complex structure and $v\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$ is sufficiently irrational, there is a pseudoholomorphic foliation conjugate to a linear foliation by real two-planes containing $v$. The topology/smallness and arithmetic hypotheses, regularity of conjugacy and leaf parametrizations are not supplied. The source emphasizes $\mathbb T^4$, comparing positivity of pseudoholomorphic intersection with Hedlund's intersection-based theory of minimal geodesics on $\mathbb T^2$. It does not state a universal existence theorem for arbitrary $J$.

Reported Chern obstruction in dimension four. Let $P\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^4$ be a constant real two-plane and let a $C^1$ diffeomorphism $F:\mathbb T^4\to\mathbb T^4$ send the linear plane foliation to a foliation whose tangent planes are $J$-invariant. The aid reports the necessary condition \[ c_1(T\mathbb T^4,J)=0\in H^2(\mathbb T^4;{\mathbb{Z}}). \] This uses the global transported tangent and normal framings of a linearly conjugate foliation; no weaker merely topological conjugacy is substituted.

For a reported family violating this condition, trivialize $T\mathbb T^4\cong\mathbb T^4\times{\mathbb{R}}^4$ and identify ${\mathbb{R}}^4$ with the quaternions. The orthogonal complex structures given by multiplication by unit imaginary quaternions form a twistor sphere $S^2$. Pull this family back by \[ \mathbb T^4\xrightarrow{\pi}\mathbb T^2\xrightarrow{g}S^2, \qquad\deg(g)\ne0. \] The aid reports that the resulting smooth almost complex structures have nonzero first Chern class, and therefore admit no foliation of the specified $C^1$-conjugate linear type. Nonzero degrees give an infinite obstruction family. This does not exclude every possible pseudoholomorphic foliation or lamination lacking such a conjugacy.

Reported slope condition under taming. If a closed two-form $\omega$ tames $J$, meaning $\omega(v,Jv)>0$ for every nonzero tangent vector, orient the foliation by $J$. The linear foliation's translation-invariant transverse measure transports through $F$, defining a Ruelle--Sullivan homology class $h\in H_2(\mathbb T^4;{\mathbb{R}})$. The aid reports \[ \langle[\omega],h\rangle>0. \] The choice of orientation and transported transverse measure is part of this slope statement; no positivity is asserted without the taming hypothesis.

Reported local graph equations. In a transverse coordinate chart $(x_1,x_2,y_1,y_2)$, write \[ J=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad X_i=\partial_{x_i}+\sum_{a=1}^2P_{ai}(x,y)\partial_{y_a}. \] For a $C^1$ graph-plane field, $J$-invariance is the algebraic Riccati equation \[ C+DP-PA-PBP=0. \] Its integrability is the commuting-frame condition \[ \partial_{x_i}P_{aj}+\sum_bP_{bi}\partial_{y_b}P_{aj} -\partial_{x_j}P_{ai}-\sum_bP_{bj}\partial_{y_b}P_{ai}=0 \quad(a=1,2;\ i,j=1,2). \] These equations concern graph charts where transversality holds. They do not supply global chart patching, irrationality control or a nonperturbative existence/classification theorem. All supplementary obstructions and equations are reported aid results requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

Bangert asks for a global theory beyond Moser's perturbative foliations. The aid supplies necessary Chern and tame-slope conditions for a specific $C^1$-conjugate linear class on $\mathbb T^4$, plus local graph equations. These restrict that class without ruling out all other foliations or solving the global program.
