Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Let $\Gamma $ be a countably infinite ICC group commensurable with a property-(T) lattice in a finite nonempty product $H_i(k_i)^+$, where $k_i$ is R, C or a finite extension of $Q_p$ (with primes allowed to differ), each $H_i$ is a connected adjoint absolutely simple algebraic group, $H_i(k_i)^+$ is generated by the $k_i-points$ of unipotent radicals of proper parabolic subgroups, and each such topological group is noncompact and has property (T), and $\Lambda $ any countably infinite ICC group. For arbitrary normalized circle-valued cocycles $\mu $ and $\omega $, is every bifinite $L_\mu (\Gamma )–L_\omega (\Lambda )$ correspondence a closed summand of finitely many models obtained by induction from actual isomorphisms between finite-index subgroups and finite-dimensional projective representations with multiplier $\mu /\delta *\omega $?

Arithmetic classification of twisted lattice correspondences

OpenAI's claim

Finite correspondences involving arbitrarily twisted ICC lattice factors over characteristic-zero local fields are claimed to arise from finite-index group identifications and finite-dimensional projective fibers, including mixed-place and eligible rank-one lattices. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $\mathscr K$ consist of ICC groups that are countably infinite and abstractly commensurable with lattices in products, with a positive finite number of factors, of $\mathbf H_i(k_i)^+$, where $k_i$ is real, complex or a finite extension of a $p$-adic field, $\mathbf H_i$ is connected adjoint absolutely simple, and each factor is noncompact with property $(T)$. The plus group is generated by rational points of unipotent radicals of proper parabolics; ICC means all nonidentity conjugacy classes are infinite, and abstract commensurability means isomorphic finite-index subgroups. No irreducibility, cocompactness or torsion premise is imposed. For $\Gamma\in\mathscr K$, any countably infinite ICC $\Lambda$ and normalized scalar cocycles $\mu,\omega$, OpenAI claims every separable bifinite $L_\mu(\Gamma)$--$L_\omega(\Lambda)$ correspondence is a closed bimodule summand of a finite sum of induced models \[ E=L^2(L_\mu(\Gamma))\ \overline\otimes_{L_{\mu|_A}(A)}\ (V_\sigma\otimes L^2(L_\omega(\Lambda))), \] for finite-index $A\le\Gamma$, $B\le\Lambda$, actual isomorphism $\delta:A\to B$ and finite-dimensional projective unitary $\sigma$ with \[ \sigma(a)\sigma(b)=\frac{\mu(a,b)}{\omega(\delta(a),\delta(b))}\sigma(ab). \] Conversely these models and summands are bifinite. For $n=\dim V_\sigma$ it claims left dimension $n[\Lambda:B]$ and right dimension $n[\Gamma:A]$. Twisted factors have canonical unitaries multiplying by the displayed cocycles; bifinite means finite module dimension on both sides, not mutual-commutant actions.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 286, 2026-09-23): The argument removes compact projective pieces and constructs boundary observations within the remaining weakly mixing correspondence. Positivity is upgraded to commuting projection-valued measurements, allowing their joint distribution to recover finite-dimensional pieces permuted by both groups. Stabilizers of these pieces supply actual finite-index subgroup isomorphisms, with cocycle ratios recorded by the projective fibers.
