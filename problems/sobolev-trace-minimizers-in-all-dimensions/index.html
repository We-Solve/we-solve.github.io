Sobolev–Trace Minimizers in All Dimensions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$, $n\geq 2$, and let $p\in(1,n)$. Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be a bounded domain with $C^2$ boundary. The supplied record denotes by $\Phi_\Omega(T)$ the optimal value of a Sobolev--trace variational problem with prescribed boundary norm $T>0$.

Unresolved definition. The record does not supply the functional, admissible function space, interior normalization, boundary norm or its exponent, or the meaning of a classical versus generalized minimizer. Thus $\Phi_\Omega(T)$ cannot be defined self-containedly from the supplied evidence. In the intended question, a minimizer means an admissible function attaining the infimum in the source's variational problem; its admissibility conditions remain unspecified.

Intended attainment question. For every integer $n\geq2$, every real $p\in(1,n)$, every bounded domain $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ with $C^2$ boundary, and every $T>0$, is the infimum defining $\Phi_\Omega(T)$ attained by a minimizer in the original admissible class, without imposing $n>2p$? A fully specified mathematical statement requires the missing definition of the variational problem.

Supplied context and qualifications. The supplied dated assessment reports classical minimizer existence when $n>2p$, generalized minimizers in every dimension, and rigidity in several restricted regimes. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified here; generalized attainment must not be substituted for the requested attainment in the original class.

The original excerpt asks whether "Theorem 1" holds in all dimensions, but does not include that theorem. It also states a contextual rigidity criterion: if $\Omega$ is connected, $\Phi_\Omega(T)=\Phi_B(T)$ for a parameter in a specified range, and a minimizer of problem (2) exists for that parameter, then $\Omega$ is a ball. The record does not define $B$, $I(B)$, problem (2), or formula (4). Its parameter range is corrupted: it contains an unattached sharp symbol and the text I(B)1/p. Consequently, neither that range nor its relation to the attainment question can be repaired reliably from this record.

Short English statement

The intended question asks whether every prescribed positive boundary norm admits a Sobolev--trace minimizer on every bounded $C^2$ domain for $1<p<n$, including dimensions with $n\leq2p$. The variational functional and admissibility conditions are missing, so the statement remains conditional on recovering the source's definitions. The supplied status is partially solved; this has not been independently verified.
