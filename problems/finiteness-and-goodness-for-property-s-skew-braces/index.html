Finiteness and Goodness for Property-S Skew Braces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A (left) skew brace is a set $B$ with group operations $+$ and $\circ$, having the same identity $0$, such that \[ a\circ(b+c)=(a\circ b)-a+(a\circ c)\qquad(a,b,c\in B). \] Neither group is assumed abelian. Put \[ \lambda_a(b)=-a+(a\circ b),\qquad a*b=-a+(a\circ b)-b. \] An ideal of $B$ means a subset normal in both groups and invariant under every $\lambda_a$, $a\in B$. For $X\subseteq B$, let $\langle X\rangle_{\mathrm{ideal},B}$ denote the least ideal of $B$ containing $X$. In the formulations below, "finitely generated ideal" is interpreted as $\langle X\rangle_{\mathrm{ideal},B}$ for some finite $X$. Generation as a skew brace means generation under both group operations and their inverses; it is a distinct requirement.

For $x\in B$, define \[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{Fix}^{r}(x)&=\{b\in B:x*b=0\},& \operatorname{Fix}^{l}(x)&=\{b\in B:b*x=0\},\\ C_x^+&=\{b\in B:b+x=x+b\},& C_x^\circ&=\{b\in B:b\circ x=x\circ b\}. \end{aligned} \] The brace has property $(S)$ if, for every $x\in B$, \[ [(B,+):\operatorname{Fix}^{r}(x)\cap C_x^+]<\infty, \qquad [(B,\circ):\operatorname{Fix}^{l}(x)\cap C_x^\circ]<\infty. \] Under the ambient-ideal interpretation above, call an ideal $I$ good if every ideal $J$ of $B$ contained in $I$ that is finitely generated as an ideal of $B$ is finitely generated as a skew brace. In particular, goodness of $B$ asks whether \[ \forall X\subseteq B\text{ finite},\quad \langle X\rangle_{\mathrm{ideal},B} \text{ is finitely generated as a skew brace}. \]

The supplied cleaned record asks the following two questions for every skew brace $B$ with property $(S)$:

  1. Is $\langle X\rangle_{\mathrm{ideal},B}$ finite for every finite subset $X\subseteq B$?
  2. Is $B$ good?
These are retained as separate source formulations; their equivalence is not asserted in this draft.

Unresolved source qualifications. The original passage also asks whether every periodic skew brace with property $(S)$ is good, and attributes an equivalence with the unrestricted goodness question to Theorems 3.14, 3.25 and 3.27 of its reference [1]. The supplied record neither identifies [1] nor reproduces those theorems. It also does not specify which underlying group is required to be periodic, where a periodic group is one in which every element has finite order. The first finiteness question in the original passage refers to a previously specified $B$, whose complete preceding hypotheses are not supplied. Whether periodicity or another hypothesis belongs to that finiteness question therefore requires review. The ambient interpretation of ideal generation used above must likewise be checked against the source, particularly for goodness of a proper ideal $I$.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether ideals generated by finitely many elements in a skew brace with property $(S)$ must be finite, and whether such ideals must be finitely generated as skew braces. The original passage also includes a periodicity restriction and a claimed equivalence whose hypotheses and cited justification are not fully supplied. These formulations require human mathematical review.
