Math-1349

A finite certificate for three-stripe triangle avoidance

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T=\{t_1,t_2,t_3\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ consist of three distinct points, allowing collinearity. A copy means a congruent image under a Euclidean isometry, including reflections, without rescaling. The original conjecture is \[ \forall T\ \exists c:{\mathbb{R}}^2\to\{0,1,2\}\quad \forall\text{ Euclidean isometries }g:\quad c\text{ is not constant on }g(T). \] No measurability restriction on arbitrary colorings is specified. The supplied partial result studies only the cyclic half-open stripe colorings \[ c_w(x,y)=\lfloor y/w\rfloor\pmod3,\qquad w>0. \]

Write $D=\operatorname{diam}(T)$, $u_\theta=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$, and \[ p_i(\theta)=(t_i-t_1)\cdot u_\theta,\quad K=\left\lceil\frac{D+w}{3w}\right\rceil. \] For integers $k_1=0$, $k_2,k_3\in[-K,K]$, put $r_i(\theta)=p_i(\theta)-3wk_i$ and $R_{k_2,k_3}(\theta)=\max_i r_i(\theta)-\min_i r_i(\theta)$. The exact certificate reported by the aid is \[ c_w\text{ admits a monochromatic congruent copy of }T \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists k_2,k_3,\theta:\ R_{k_2,k_3}(\theta)<w. \] The offsets belong to one coherent triple, rather than independent pairwise modular choices. A common vertical translation places all residuals in one half-open interval of width $w$ exactly when the range is strictly smaller than $w$; equality is excluded. Varying the projection direction accounts for rotations and reflections, while the common translation is eliminated by the range test.

For each offset pair, the global minimum of $R$ is found among angles where either $r_i=r_j$ or $(r_i-r_j)'=0$ for some distinct pair. Each of the three pairs gives at most two angles of each type, so at most twelve critical angles suffice. Therefore at most \[ 12(2K+1)^2 \] range evaluations decide whether this particular stripe coloring avoids $T$, in an exact-real comparison convention. This is a finite certificate for a prescribed $w$, not a general decision algorithm for arbitrary colorings or unspecified noncomputable real input.

The aid also reports a boundary-safe stripe avoidance theorem for every nondegenerate triangle whose smallest angle is at least $30^\circ$: some suitable stripe width avoids all congruent copies. Its width choice and full special-case proof are not supplied in the summary and require review; no unprovided width formula is inserted.

Finally, a coloring constant on lines in one fixed direction cannot avoid a collinear three-point set, which can be placed on one such line. A strategy requiring every segment of one prescribed positive length to have differently colored endpoints would be a proper three-coloring of that distance graph, which the aid reports impossible. These are obstructions to particular approaches, not refutations of the original three-point conjecture. The report describes the full conjecture as open and supplies no unrestricted coloring construction.

Short English statement

The full conjecture asks to avoid each prescribed three-point configuration with three colors. The supplied result gives an exact finite test for one cyclic stripe family, including coherent offsets and half-open boundaries, and reports a triangle special case. It does not decide the general coloring question or cover collinear sets with line-constant stripes.
