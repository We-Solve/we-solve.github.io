Math-4027

Random Moduli of General Brownian Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A conformal modulus records the conformal equivalence class of a surface. For a fixed topological type $\tau$, let $\mathcal M_\tau$ denote the space of conformal equivalence classes of surfaces of that type, with boundary and marking conventions to be specified. Two surfaces represent the same class when there is a conformal isomorphism between them respecting those conventions.

Suppose that a Brownian surface $B_\tau$ of type $\tau$ has a specified probability law $\mathbb P_\tau$ and a specified associated conformal structure. Write $\operatorname{mod}_\tau(B_\tau)\in\mathcal M_\tau$ for its conformal class. Once the requisite measurable structures and measurability are established, its random conformal moduli have distribution \[ \mu_\tau=(\operatorname{mod}_\tau)_*\mathbb P_\tau, \qquad \mu_\tau(A)=\mathbb P_\tau\!\left(\operatorname{mod}_\tau(B_\tau)\in A\right) \] for each measurable $A\subseteq\mathcal M_\tau$.

Problem, conditional on specifying the model. For every topological type $\tau$ covered by the intended Brownian-surface model, derive the distribution $\mu_\tau$ of its conformal moduli, extending the Brownian-annulus case described as rigorously derived in the supplied record.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the Brownian-surface probability law, its normalization or conditioning, the construction of its conformal structure, or the admissible topological types. In particular, compactness, orientability, boundary components, punctures, markings, and any prescribed area or boundary lengths are unspecified. Thus the displayed formulation identifies the requested distribution only conditionally; it does not select a particular model or assert the existence of the conformal structure or moduli law. The retained original passage states the annulus result but does not explicitly state the general-topology extension; that extension appears in the supplied curated statement.

Short English statement

Determine the probability distribution of the conformal equivalence class of a Brownian surface for each intended topological type, extending the annulus case. A precise model and its boundary, marking, and normalization conventions remain unspecified. The supplied record labels this direction open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
