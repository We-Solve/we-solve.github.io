Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
At SK critical $\beta =1$, do Gaussian and Rademacher couplings yield the same joint limiting continuous law for the precisely rescaled equilibrium autocorrelation $A_n(t)$ and quench autocorrelation $B_n(s,t)$, on positive-time compact sets? Ask for positive-time entrance densities $h_s$ selected by Gaussian dynamics, $h_{s+t}=S_t$ $h_s$, $h_s$ $Pi\to \delta _0$ as $s\downarrow 0$, $h_s\to 1$ in L1 as s$\to$infinity, and $B(s,t)\to A(t)$ locally uniformly in probability as waiting time s$\to$infinity.

A universal joint critical SK equilibrium and quench autocorrelation law

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: Critical stationary SK autocorrelation is claimed to have a common random functional limit for Gaussian and sign couplings after time scaling by $n^{2/3}$ and amplitude by $n^{1/3}$; The critical SK quench from independent fair spins is claimed to share a functional scaling law for Gaussian and sign couplings and to relax to the stationary limit at long waiting times. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For independent standard Gaussian or fair Rademacher $J_{ij}$, $i<j$, with $J_{ji}=J_{ij},J_{ii}=0$, use the zero-field SK measure \[ \pi_{n,\beta}^J(x)=Z^{-1}\exp\left(\frac\beta{\sqrt n}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j\right),\qquad x\in\{-1,1\}^n. \] Continuous heat-bath dynamics refreshes every site at rate one; a discrete step refreshes one uniformly selected site, including attempts that retain its spin. At $\beta=1$, write $P_t^J$ for the rate-one-per-site semigroup, and let \[ C_{n,J}(t)=n^{-1}\sum_i\langle x_i,P_t^Jx_i\rangle_\pi,\qquad A_{n,J}(s)=n^{1/3}C_{n,J}(sn^{2/3}). \] There exists a common probability law $Q_{\mathrm{crit}}$ on $C_{\mathrm{loc}}((0,\infty))$ such that $\operatorname{Law}(A_{n,J})\Longrightarrow Q_{\mathrm{crit}}$ for both interaction distributions. This law is nondegenerate and supported on nonzero, nonnegative, nonincreasing continuous functions tending to zero at infinity. The topology is uniform convergence on compact positive-time intervals.
Result 2.
For independent standard Gaussian or fair Rademacher $J_{ij}$, $i<j$, with $J_{ji}=J_{ij},J_{ii}=0$, use the zero-field SK measure \[ \pi_{n,\beta}^J(x)=Z^{-1}\exp\left(\frac\beta{\sqrt n}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}x_ix_j\right),\qquad x\in\{-1,1\}^n. \] Continuous heat-bath dynamics refreshes every site at rate one; a discrete step refreshes one uniformly selected site, including attempts that retain its spin. At $\beta=1$, start the quench from uniform independent spins $\nu_n$, independent of $J$. With $T_n=n^{2/3}$ and heat-bath semigroup $P_t^J$, define \[ A_{n,J}(t)=n^{-2/3}\sum_i\langle x_i,P_{tT_n}^Jx_i\rangle_\pi,\quad B_{n,J}(s,t)=n^{-2/3}\sum_i\mathbb E_{\nu_n}^J[x_i(sT_n)x_i((s+t)T_n)]. \] To specify the common limit, let $(g_a)_{a\ge1}$ be the increasing GOE edge process, normalized by $n^{2/3}(2-\lambda_a)\Longrightarrow g_a$. For $b+g_1>0$, set $\ell_a=(g_a+b)^{-1}$ and \[ D_g(b)=\lim_{L\to\infty}\left[\sqrt L-\sum_a\left((g_a+b)^{-1}-(g_a+L)^{-1}\right)\right]. \] The defining limit for $D_g(b)$ is in probability over the edge process; an almost-sure version is fixed along a deterministic sequence of $L$. For independent $Z_a\sim N(0,\ell_a)$, let $\Pi_g$ be their law conditioned, by continuous densities, on $\sum_a(Z_a^2-\ell_a)=D_g(b)$; it is independent of the admissible $b$. Let $H_g$ generate the closed cylinder-gradient form $\int\sum_a|\partial_aF|^2\,d\Pi_g$, and set $S_t^g=e^{-tc_*H_g}$ with the common positive microscopic coefficient $c_*$. There are measurable densities $h_s^g\in L^2(\Pi_g)$ with \[ h_s^g\ge0,\quad\Pi_g[h_s^g]=1,\quad h_{s+t}^g=S_t^gh_s^g,\quad h_s^g\Pi_g\Longrightarrow\delta_0\ (s\downarrow0),\quad \|h_s^g-1\|_{L^1(\Pi_g)}\to0\ (s\to\infty). \] Here the initial convergence uses the product topology. The locally uniformly convergent series \[ A_g(t)=\sum_a\int x_aS_t^gx_a\,d\Pi_g,\qquad B_g(s,t)=\sum_a\int h_s^gx_aS_t^gx_a\,d\Pi_g \] define continuous limits, and for either interaction law \[ (A_{n,J},B_{n,J})\Longrightarrow(A_g,B_g) \quad\text{in }C_{\mathrm{loc}}((0,\infty))\times C_{\mathrm{loc}}((0,\infty)^2). \] For every $0<a<b<\infty$, $\sup_{a\le t\le b}|B_g(s,t)-A_g(t)|\to0$ in probability as $s\to\infty$.

Outline of the argument

A conditioned spectral-edge measure and quantitative observation estimates control variance at the cap scale. Microscopic relaxation identifies the effective coefficient, resolvent-trace convergence yields the Gaussian process limit, and coupling comparison transports it to sign disorder.

Bottom-edge Gaussian estimates control warming on the critical clock. Positive-time limits select an entrance law from the uniform initial state, while smooth truncation of dynamical histories permits disorder replacement; comparison with the stationary edge input identifies the eventual relaxation.
