Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.1

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a group with identity element $1_G$. For $S\subseteq G$, let $\langle S\rangle$ be the subgroup generated by $S$, and write $\langle g,S\rangle=\langle\{g\}\cup S\rangle$. An element $g\in G$ is a non-near generator if \[ \forall S\subseteq G,\qquad [G:\langle g,S\rangle]<\infty\ \Longrightarrow\ [G:\langle S\rangle]<\infty. \] Define the lower near Frattini subgroup by \[ \lambda(G)=\{g\in G:g\text{ is a non-near generator}\}. \] A subgroup $M\leq G$ is nearly maximal if \[ [G:M]=\infty\quad\text{and}\quad \forall H\leq G,\qquad M\subsetneq H\ \Longrightarrow\ [G:H]<\infty. \] The upper near Frattini subgroup is \[ \mu(G)=\bigcap_{\substack{M\leq G\\M\text{ nearly maximal}}}M. \] Here an intersection over an empty family of subgroups is taken to be $G$; the supplied record does not explicitly state this convention. The source states that $\lambda(G)$ and $\mu(G)$ are characteristic subgroups and that $\lambda(G)\leq\mu(G)$. When they coincide, their common value is called the near Frattini subgroup and is denoted by $\psi(G)$.

The two questions proposed by M. K. Azarian are:

  1. (a) For every group $G$ that is the knot group of a product of knots, is \[ \lambda(G)=\mu(G)=\{1_G\}? \]
  2. (b) For every cable knot group $G$, is \[ \lambda(G)=\mu(G)=\{1_G\}? \]
These equalities express the supplied questions $\psi(G)=1$ while explicitly including the equality needed for $\psi(G)$ to be defined.

The knot terminology requires qualification. Under the customary interpretation of knots in $S^3$, the knot group of $K$ is $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$, considered up to isomorphism. The supplied record does not specify the ambient manifold or knot category, define a product of knots, or state the permitted cabling parameters. Interpreting a product as a finite connected sum is a possible reading, but is not imposed here. Thus the exact knot classes quantified over in (a) and (b) remain subject to these unresolved source qualifications.

Short English statement

Azarian asks whether the lower and upper near Frattini subgroups are both trivial for every knot group arising from a product of knots, and for every cable knot group. This formulation includes the equality required to define $\psi(G)$. The precise knot classes, product operation, and cabling conventions require clarification from the source.
