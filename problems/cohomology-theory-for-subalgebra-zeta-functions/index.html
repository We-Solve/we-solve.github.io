Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns subalgebra zeta functions and their proposed relationship with flag Hilbert--Poincaré series. For the combinatorial context, let $K$ be a number field and let $\mathcal A$ be a finite set of hyperplanes in affine space $\mathbb A^n_K$. The excerpt denotes by $L(\mathcal A)$ the set of intersections of these hyperplanes, excluding $\mathbb A^n_K$. It does not specify how empty intersections are treated. Its extracted formula for the flag Hilbert--Poincaré series contains notation $\pi_F(Y)$ and factors involving $T_X/(1-T_X)$, but the summation range, the meaning of $F$, and the definition of $\pi_F(Y)$ are not supplied reliably enough to state the formula precisely.

Requested construction. Construct a cohomology theory for subalgebra zeta functions that unifies their arithmetic zeta-function theory with the combinatorial theory of flag Hilbert--Poincaré series.

Unresolved scope. This is a research-program formulation rather than a fully specified existence statement. The record does not define the class of algebras or subalgebras to be counted, the arithmetic base, the relevant local or global zeta functions, the objects and coefficients of the proposed cohomology theory, or the identities and compatibility properties constituting unification. Moreover, the original question refers to "these two theories" without supplying its preceding context; the identification of those theories above follows the supplied cleaned statement. No precise cohomological axioms or recovery formula can therefore be asserted from this record.

Short English statement

Seek a cohomology theory connecting the arithmetic of subalgebra zeta functions with the combinatorics of flag Hilbert--Poincaré series. The supplied record leaves the scope and required meaning of this connection unspecified, so this remains a statement-only research question requiring mathematical review.
