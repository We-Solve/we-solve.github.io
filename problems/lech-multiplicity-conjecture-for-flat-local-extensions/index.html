Lech Multiplicity Conjecture for Flat Local Extensions

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For a flat local map of nonzero Noetherian rings, the proposed inequality is $e(R)\leq e(S)$ without a dimension or characteristic restriction.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For any flat local homomorphism $(R,\mathfrak m)\to(S,\mathfrak n)$ between nonzero Noetherian local rings, the claimed Hilbert--Samuel multiplicities satisfy \[ e_{\mathfrak m}(R)\le e_{\mathfrak n}(S). \] The theorem imposes no extra condition on residue fields, dimensions, or characteristic.

Outline of the argument

The argument replaces unavailable Ulrich modules by short free complexes and a uniform estimate for their Euler lengths. Cohen factorization connects those estimates to multiplicity, while normalized-length algebras provide the parameter calculations in positive and mixed characteristic.

Formal verification
