Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every log-concave probability density f on $R^{m}$, must its differential entropy $h(f)=-\int f$ logf satisfy $h(f)\geq m+\tfrac12 \log $ det Cov(f)? Is equality equivalent, up to almost-everywhere change, to f being the density of $M(E_{1},\dots ,E_m)+b$ with M invertible and independent standard exponential $E_i$ supported on $[0,\infty )$? No symmetry or smoothness is assumed.

Sharp entropy lower bound for log-concave densities

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 101; 2026-10-05), the asserted result is that simplices uniquely maximize the isotropic constant, and log-concave densities satisfy the stated sharp entropy--covariance bound with affine exponential-product equality.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For every $n\ge1$ and every full-dimensional convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$, use $V=|K|$, its centroid $b$, covariance $\Sigma_K=V^{-1}\int_K(x-b)(x-b)^{\mathsf T}\,dx$, and $L_K=(\det\Sigma_K/V^2)^{1/(2n)}$. The claimed sharp bound, with equality exactly for simplices, is \[ L_K\le\frac{(n!)^{1/n}}{(n+1)^{(n+1)/(2n)}\sqrt{n+2}}. \] The associated entropy assertion concerns every log-concave probability density $f$ on $\mathbb R^m$, $m\ge1$: \[ h(f)=-\int f\log f\ge m+\tfrac12\log\det\operatorname{Cov}(f). \] Equality is claimed precisely for affine images $M(E_1,\ldots,E_m)^{\mathsf T}+b$, with $M$ invertible and independent standard exponential variables $E_i$; densities are identified almost everywhere.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 101; 2026-10-05), gaussian decomposition expands the entropy deficit into weighted chaos terms and commutator squares. Certified scalar inequalities control the expansion, and vanishing slack forces an exponential-product law; applying the bound to uniform bodies yields simplex rigidity.
