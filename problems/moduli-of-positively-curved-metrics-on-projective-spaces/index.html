Moduli of Positively Curved Metrics on Projective Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, let $\mathbb{CP}^{n}$ denote complex projective $n$-space, regarded as a smooth compact manifold without boundary of real dimension $2n$. Under the interpretation that "positively curved" means positive sectional curvature, define \[ \mathcal R_{\sec>0}(\mathbb{CP}^{n}) =\left\{g\in\Gamma^{\infty}(\operatorname{Sym}^{2}T^{*}\mathbb{CP}^{n}):\begin{array}{l}g\text{ is positive definite, and}\\ \sec_g(\sigma)>0\text{ for every }x\in\mathbb{CP}^{n}\\ \text{and every real two-dimensional subspace }\sigma\subseteq T_x\mathbb{CP}^{n}\end{array}\right\}. \] One conventional moduli space is \[ \mathcal M_{\sec>0}(\mathbb{CP}^{n}) =\mathcal R_{\sec>0}(\mathbb{CP}^{n})/\operatorname{Diff}(\mathbb{CP}^{n}), \] where the full smooth diffeomorphism group acts by pullback, $\phi\cdot g=\phi^{*}g$. For this convention, the metric space carries the $C^{\infty}$ topology and the moduli space carries the quotient topology; no additional identification by constant rescaling is imposed.

Source question, with unresolved conventions. What can be said about the moduli space of positively curved metrics when the sphere $S^{d}$ is replaced by complex projective space? With the conventions just displayed, this asks for information about $\mathcal M_{\sec>0}(\mathbb{CP}^{n})$.

Short English statement

The question asks what can be learned about moduli spaces of positively curved Riemannian metrics on complex projective spaces, in comparison with the sphere setting. Positive sectional curvature and quotienting by all diffeomorphisms give one conventional formulation, but the supplied record leaves these conventions, the dimensions, and the precise desired conclusion unresolved.
