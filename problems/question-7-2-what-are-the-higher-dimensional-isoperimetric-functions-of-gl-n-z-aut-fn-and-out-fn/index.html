Question 7.2 — What are the higher-dimensional isoperimetric functions of GL(n, Z), Aut(Fn)and Out(Fn)?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each rank $n$ in the source's intended range, consider \[ \mathrm{GL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}}),\qquad\operatorname{Aut}(F_n),\qquad \operatorname{Out}(F_n), \] where $F_n$ is free of rank $n$ and $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ is the quotient by inner automorphisms. Question 7.2 asks for their higher-dimensional isoperimetric functions.

One common homotopical convention, for a suitable finite-type classifying-space model with contractible universal cover $X$, considers the least $(k+1)$-volume filling of an admissible $k$-sphere and takes the worst such volume for sphere volume at most $v$: \[ \delta_G^{(k)}(v)= \sup_{\operatorname{Vol}_k(f)\le v} \inf_{\partial F=f}\operatorname{Vol}_{k+1}(F). \] This is explanatory rather than a selected source definition. Homological filling functions instead fill cycles by chains and can differ, as can coefficient, norm and admissibility conventions. Appropriate finiteness assumptions on the model are needed.

The record does not specify the filling dimensions meant by higher-dimensional, the allowed ranks, sphere versus cycle fillings, volume model, coefficients, or whether functions are to be determined exactly or up to the usual coarse asymptotic equivalence. Those choices must be recovered before giving a precise rank/dimension family of targets. No ordinary one-dimensional Dehn-function result or bounded-cohomology statement is substituted for the higher-dimensional request.

Short English statement

Determine higher-dimensional filling/isoperimetric growth for the integer linear group and free-group automorphism and outer automorphism groups. The rank/dimension range and homotopical versus homological filling conventions are missing, so the requested function families remain to be specified.
