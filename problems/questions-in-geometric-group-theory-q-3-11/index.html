Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 3.11

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be a free group of finite rank $n$, write $[a,b]=aba^{-1}b^{-1}$, and define \[ \operatorname{genus}(x)=\min\{g\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}:x=[a_1,b_1]\cdots[a_g,b_g],\ a_i,b_i\in F_n\} \quad(x\in[F_n,F_n]). \] For $\operatorname{genus}(x)=g$, let \[ \mathcal E_g(x)=\{(a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g)\in F_n^{2g}:\prod_{i=1}^g[a_i,b_i]=x\}. \] The requested bound is an explicit finite function $B(g)$, independent of $x$, such that \[ \#\left(\mathcal E_g(x)/{\sim_{\mathrm N}}\right)\le B(g) \quad\text{whenever }\operatorname{genus}(x)=g, \] where $\sim_{\mathrm N}$ is the source's Nielsen equivalence of expressions. The excerpt does not define this equivalence. In particular, it does not specify whether one uses arbitrary Nielsen changes of the $2g$ generators or automorphisms preserving the boundary word $\prod_{i=1}^g[u_i,v_i]$, nor whether boundary conjugation is allowed. The numerical target cannot be fixed independently of that convention. The statement explicitly requests independence from $x$; the supplied research summary additionally requests independence from $n$.

For $g\ge1$, the supplied partial-progress summary proposes \[ f(g)\le W_{\mathrm{tot}}(g)\le\sum_{e=2g}^{6g-3}(2e-1)!! \le(4g-2)(12g-7)!!, \qquad f(g)\le\frac{(12g)!}{(6g)!}, \] where $(2e-1)!!=\prod_{j=1}^{e}(2j-1)$, and describes $W_{\mathrm{tot}}(g)$ as counting all Wicks forms. It reports a literature lower bound $2^g\le f(g)$, describes the displayed upper estimate as roughly $(12g)^{6g}$ up to exponential factors, and distinguishes Bestvina--Feighn's equivalence-class lower bound from Duncan--Vdovina's $g!$ growth of raw presentations. These are supplied, unverified claims, not conclusions established here. The summary explicitly says its upper bound does not appear in the literature and that its surjectivity input was not checked in Culler's full paper. Its last caveat is truncated. For $g=0$, the expression set has the unique empty expression for $x=1$; the displayed positive-genus formula is not applied.

Short English statement

Bound, in terms of the minimal number of commutators alone, the number of Nielsen-equivalence classes of minimal commutator expressions for an element of a free group. The precise equivalence convention and the provenance of the supplied proposed bound require review.
