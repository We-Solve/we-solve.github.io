Kourovka Notebook Problem 4.44

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, let $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ denote the group, under composition, of all bijective homomorphisms from $G$ to itself.

Describe all groups $G$ for which $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is abelian; equivalently, those groups satisfying \[ \forall\alpha,\beta\in\operatorname{Aut}(G)\;\forall g\in G,\qquad \alpha(\beta(g))=\beta(\alpha(g)). \] The supplied statement imposes no finiteness assumption or other restriction on $G$, so the requested description concerns groups in general. The record calls this a well-known problem; it does not specify a preferred form of classification.

Short English statement

Describe all groups whose automorphisms commute with one another. The supplied question includes both finite and infinite groups.
