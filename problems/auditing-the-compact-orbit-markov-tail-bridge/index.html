Auditing the compact-orbit/Markov-tail bridge

Let $d\geq2$, let $\Lambda\subset{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be unimodular, and let $A$ be the positive diagonal determinant-one group. Define \[ \operatorname{sys}(\Lambda)=\min_{v\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}}\|v\|_2, \qquad\nu(\Lambda)=\inf_{v\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}}|v_1\cdots v_d|. \] The supplied exact identity is \[ \inf_{a\in A}\operatorname{sys}(a\Lambda) =\sqrt d\,\nu(\Lambda)^{1/d}. \] The infimum may be zero or unattained. The minimum $\nu$ is constant on the $A$-orbit.

In the unimodular lattice space $X_d=\operatorname{SL}_d({\mathbb{R}})/\operatorname{SL}_d({\mathbb{Z}})$, Mahler's criterion makes \[ K_\epsilon=\{\Lambda:\operatorname{sys}(\Lambda)\geq\sqrt d\,\epsilon^{1/d}\} \] compact for every $\epsilon>0$. The identity says that an orbit is contained in $K_\epsilon$ exactly when its $\nu$-value is at least $\epsilon$.

For any specified family $\mathscr O$ of compact closed $A$-orbit objects, the aid's geometric equivalence can therefore be formulated precisely as \[ \forall K\subset X_d\text{ compact},\quad \#\{O\in\mathscr O:O\subseteq K\}<\infty \] if and only if \[ \forall\epsilon>0,\quad \#\{O\in\mathscr O:\nu(O)\geq\epsilon\}<\infty. \] Here confinement means containment of the entire orbit, not merely meeting a compact set. Compactness of a given $K$ supplies a uniform positive systole bound for the reverse direction. The missing source statements must be checked before identifying this precise confinement formulation with their wording.

Passing from orbit objects to a numerical positive spectrum $V\subset{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}$ requires two additional gates. If $\nu(\mathscr O)\subseteq V$, finiteness of orbit tails implies finiteness of $V\cap[\epsilon,\infty)$ only after a coverage hypothesis that every relevant value of $V$ is realized by an orbit in $\mathscr O$. Conversely, finite numerical tails imply finite orbit tails only if every value has finitely many orbit objects in its fiber. Neither coverage nor finite fibers follows merely from the compactness of $K_\epsilon$.

The aid reports abstract countermodels for these omissions: unrepresented numerical values defeat the coverage direction, while infinitely many orbit objects assigned one positive value defeat the finite-fiber direction. These logical countermodels are not claimed to be actual lattice-orbit constructions. The source's alleged numerical reformulation is not reproduced, so its precise spectrum, normalization, and missing arithmetic gates remain unresolved rather than silently supplied.
