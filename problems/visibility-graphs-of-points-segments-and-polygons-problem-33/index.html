Visibility Graphs of Points, Segments and Polygons — Problem 33

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied source asks: "Draw a simple polygon whose visibility graph is the given graph $G$."

A precise candidate interpretation is the following vertex-visibility realization problem. Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite simple undirected graph with $|V|=n\geq 3$. A simple polygon $P$ is a closed polygonal chain with distinct vertices $p_1,\ldots,p_n$, whose nonadjacent edges are disjoint and whose adjacent edges meet only at their common endpoint. Write $\overline{\Omega}_P$ for the closed region bounded by this chain, including its boundary. Under the convention that boundary visibility counts, its vertex visibility graph has vertex set $\{p_1,\ldots,p_n\}$, with distinct vertices $p_i,p_j$ adjacent exactly when \[ [p_i,p_j]\subseteq\overline{\Omega}_P, \] where $[p_i,p_j]$ denotes the closed straight line segment joining them.

Under this interpretation, the task is to construct such a polygon $P$ and a bijection $f:V\to\{p_1,\ldots,p_n\}$ satisfying \[ \forall u,v\in V,\quad u\ne v\implies \left(\{u,v\}\in E\iff[f(u),f(v)]\subseteq\overline{\Omega}_P\right). \] The cyclic order of the polygon vertices is part of the desired output unless separately supplied.

This formulation is conditional on conventions absent from the supplied record: the record does not explicitly define visibility, identify the graph vertices with polygon vertices, specify degeneracy restrictions, or state whether a polygon boundary order is supplied. It also does not say whether $G$ is promised to admit a realization. Consequently, no requirement to recognize nonrealizable inputs, report nonexistence, or achieve a particular computational complexity is asserted here. These qualifications require human review against the source before this candidate formulation can be treated as definitive.

Short English statement

Given a graph $G$, construct a simple polygon whose vertex visibility graph is isomorphic to $G$, under the explicitly stated convention that a segment contained in the closed polygonal region gives visibility. The supplied record leaves visibility conventions, input promises, and algorithmic requirements unresolved.
