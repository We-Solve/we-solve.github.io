Math-6594

Frame Property of the Gaussian Gabor System on $\Lambda_3$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Identify ${\mathbb{C}}^2$ with time--frequency space ${\mathbb{R}}^2\times{\mathbb{R}}^2$ by $z=x+i\omega$. Define \[ A_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&2/5\\0&\sqrt{21}/5\end{pmatrix},\qquad \Lambda_3=A_3({\mathbb{Z}}+i{\mathbb{Z}})^2 =\{A_3m+iA_3n:m,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2\}. \] To make the operator conventions explicit, consider the customary normalized Gaussian and time--frequency shifts \[ g(t)=\sqrt{2}\,e^{-\pi\lVert t\rVert^2},\qquad (\pi(x,\omega)g)(t)=e^{2\pi i\omega\cdot t}g(t-x), \qquad t,x,\omega\in{\mathbb{R}}^2. \] Here $\lVert g\rVert_{L^2({\mathbb{R}}^2)}=1$, and $L^2({\mathbb{R}}^2)$ is taken over ${\mathbb{C}}$. Under these conventions, the associated Gabor system is \[ G(g,\Lambda_3)=\{\pi(A_3m,A_3n)g:m,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2\}. \] The problem is to prove or disprove that there exist constants $0<a\le b<\infty$ such that, for every $f\in L^2({\mathbb{R}}^2)$, \[ a\lVert f\rVert_2^2 \le \sum_{m,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2} \left|\left\langle f,\pi(A_3m,A_3n)g\right\rangle\right|^2 \le b\lVert f\rVert_2^2. \] The supplied record specifies a normalized Gaussian and an associated Gabor system but does not explicitly state the Gaussian exponent, modulation normalization, or complex-to-phase-space convention. The displayed choices give a precise conventional interpretation; their agreement with the report requires human verification. In particular, unit norm alone does not determine Gaussian width.

Short English statement

Determine whether the normalized Gaussian Gabor system on the specified lattice $\Lambda_3$ is a frame for $L^2({\mathbb{R}}^2)$, meaning that its coefficients satisfy uniform positive lower and finite upper frame bounds. The explicit Gaussian and time--frequency conventions in this draft require confirmation against the cited report.
