Defining Higher-Dimensional Elliptic Fibrations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f\colon X\to B$ be a morphism of complex algebraic varieties. A morphism is proper if it is separated, of finite type, and universally closed. For $b\in B({\mathbb{C}})$, write $X_b=X\times_B\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}$ for its fiber. A smooth, projective, connected complex curve $C$ has genus one if $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}H^1(C,\mathcal O_C)=1$. In pointed terminology, an elliptic curve additionally has a distinguished point $O\in C({\mathbb{C}})$.

The supplied record describes the workshop's common convention as follows: an elliptic fibration is a proper morphism of complex algebraic varieties whose general fiber is an elliptic curve. If "general fiber" means fibers over a nonempty dense Zariski-open subset, and "elliptic curve" here refers to an underlying genus-one curve without a specified origin, this convention has the formulation \[ f\colon X\to B\text{ is proper},\qquad \exists\,U\subseteq B\text{ nonempty dense Zariski-open}\quad \forall b\in U({\mathbb{C}}),\quad X_b\text{ is a smooth, projective, connected curve of genus }1. \] This is an explicit interpretation of a terminology fragment, not a recovered conjecture. The record does not specify its conventions for varieties, the precise meaning of general fiber, or whether fiberwise origins are part of the data. In particular, it does not require a section $s\colon B\to X$ satisfying $f\circ s=\operatorname{id}_B$. Smoothness, existence of a section, flatness, and prescribed singular fibers are listed as possible refinements, without selecting any of them; the intended smoothness requirement and any restrictions on fibers outside $U$ remain unspecified. No determinate open mathematical assertion is supplied.

Short English statement

The record discusses the meaning of an elliptic fibration: a proper morphism of complex algebraic varieties with general fiber an elliptic curve. Additional requirements vary with the convention. The supplied text is a definition discussion, so it does not determine an open problem to formalize.
