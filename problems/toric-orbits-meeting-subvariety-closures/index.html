Toric Orbits Meeting Subvariety Closures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T=({\mathbb{C}}^*)^n$ be the given complex algebraic torus, let $Y\subseteq T$ be a subvariety, and let $X_\Sigma$ be a toric variety whose dense torus is identified with $T$. Write \[ \overline{Y}^{\,X_\Sigma} \] for the Zariski closure of $Y$ in $X_\Sigma$. For a point $x\in X_\Sigma$, its $T$-orbit is \[ T\cdot x=\{t\cdot x:t\in T\}. \] Question. For every such pair $(Y,X_\Sigma)$, characterize the set \[ \mathcal{I}(Y,X_\Sigma) = \left\{O\subseteq X_\Sigma: O\text{ is a }T\text{-orbit and }O\cap\overline{Y}^{\,X_\Sigma}\ne\varnothing\right\}. \] Thus the requested characterization should determine, for each $T$-orbit of the given toric variety, whether it meets the closure of $Y$. No completeness or projectivity hypothesis on $X_\Sigma$ is supplied.

The supplied curation describes this as a question extracted from a tropicalization passage, but states that the passage does not preserve a distinct conjecture or theorem to verify. Accordingly, this draft records the orbit-intersection question without asserting a proposed criterion or an independently verified open-problem status.

Short English statement

Given a subvariety of a complex algebraic torus and a toric variety containing that torus as its dense orbit, determine exactly which torus orbits meet the subvariety's Zariski closure. The supplied record labels the question open, while its dated assessment says that no distinct conjecture or theorem was preserved.
