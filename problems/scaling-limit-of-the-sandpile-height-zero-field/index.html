Scaling Limit of the Sandpile Height-Zero Field

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $D\subset{\mathbb{C}}$ be a bounded smooth domain and, for $\varepsilon>0$, put $D_\varepsilon=D\cap\varepsilon{\mathbb{Z}}^2$. Let $\eta$ have the Abelian sandpile measure $\nu_\varepsilon$ on $D_\varepsilon$, as named in the supplied record. Define the centered height-zero observable by \[ h_\varepsilon(z)=\mathbf{1}_{\{\eta(z)=0\}}-\nu_\varepsilon\left(\eta(z)=0\right),\qquad z\in D_\varepsilon. \] The record does not specify the boundary or sink convention defining $\nu_\varepsilon$, or the convention for labeling sandpile heights.

The proposed scaling-limit question is whether a suitably rescaled realization of $h_\varepsilon$ converges, as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$, in a suitable function or distribution space, to the proposed generalized field \[ :\!|\nabla h|^2\!:\,. \] Here the continuum field $h$, its law, normalization and boundary conditions, and the meaning and existence of the indicated renormalized quadratic-gradient field are not defined in the supplied record.

For example, a distributional formulation could seek deterministic factors $a_\varepsilon$, a specified space $\mathcal X$ of distributions on $D$, and a construction of $Y=:\!|\nabla h|^2\!:$, such that the random distributions defined by \[ \langle X_\varepsilon,\varphi\rangle =a_\varepsilon\varepsilon^2\sum_{z\in D_\varepsilon}h_\varepsilon(z)\varphi(z), \qquad \varphi\in C_c^\infty(D), \] satisfy \[ X_\varepsilon\xrightarrow[\varepsilon\downarrow0]{\mathrm{law}}Y \quad\text{in }\mathcal X. \] This pairing and convergence in law are an explicitly conditional way to formulate the question; the source does not select this embedding or mode of convergence. The normalization, topology, target construction and sandpile boundary conventions remain unresolved specifications. Thus the supplied record determines an intended scaling-limit question, but not a fully specified convergence proposition.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the centered indicator of sandpile height zero on a fine square lattice in a bounded smooth planar domain has a rescaled continuum limit described by $:\!|\nabla h|^2\!:$. The scaling, boundary conventions, continuum field, renormalization and convergence space require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
