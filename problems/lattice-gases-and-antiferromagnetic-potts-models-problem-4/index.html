Lattice Gases and Antiferromagnetic Potts Models — Problem 4

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G=(V,E)$ be either the infinite hexagonal lattice (the vertices and edges of the regular hexagonal tiling) or the square lattice ($V={\mathbb{Z}}^2$, with nearest-neighbor edges). For an integer $q\ge 2$, a spin configuration is an element of $\Omega_q=\{1,\ldots,q\}^{V}$. A conventional dimensionless parameter for the antiferromagnetic Potts model is $K\ge 0$, with equal neighboring spins penalized by the factor $e^{-K}$. For finite $\Lambda\subset V$ and exterior configuration $\tau$, its finite-volume conditional distribution is \[ \gamma_{\Lambda,K}^{\tau}(\sigma_\Lambda) =\frac{1}{Z_{\Lambda,K}^{\tau}} \exp\!\left[-K\!\sum_{\substack{\{x,y\}\in E\\\{x,y\}\cap\Lambda\ne\varnothing}} \mathbf{1}_{\{\eta_x=\eta_y\}}\right], \qquad \eta|_\Lambda=\sigma_\Lambda,\quad \eta|_{V\setminus\Lambda}=\tau. \] Here $Z_{\Lambda,K}^{\tau}$ normalizes the distribution. An infinite-volume Gibbs measure is a probability measure on $\Omega_q$ whose conditional distributions in every finite $\Lambda$ agree almost surely with these kernels. If zero temperature is included, the corresponding hard-constraint model is the uniform proper-coloring specification: conditionally on the exterior, all proper extensions to $\Lambda$ have equal probability, for admissible exterior configurations.

The supplied problem asks for uniqueness of the infinite-volume Gibbs measure and exponential decay of correlations for \[ (G,q)\in \left\{(G_{\mathrm{hex}},3), (G_{\mathrm{sq}},4), (G_{\mathrm{sq}},5), (G_{\mathrm{sq}},6)\right\}. \] The source also says "etc.," without identifying further lattice--$q$ pairs.

One precise interpretation of exponential decay is the following local-observable clustering property. For each parameter value under consideration, there exists $m>0$ such that, for every pair of finite sets $A,B\subset V$ and bounded observables $f$ and $g$ depending respectively on spins in $A$ and $B$, there is a finite constant $C_{f,g}$, independent of the lattice translation $t$, for which \[ \left|\operatorname{Cov}_{\mu}(f,g_t)\right| \le C_{f,g}\exp\!\left[-m\,d_G(A,tB)\right]. \] Here $g_t$ is the translated observable and $d_G$ is graph distance between supports. This is an explicit possible interpretation, requiring review: the supplied excerpt does not specify the class of correlations or the uniformity of the decay bounds.

Unresolved qualification. The excerpt supplies no temperature or coupling range and does not say whether zero temperature is included. Short English statement

Sokal asks for uniqueness of the infinite-volume antiferromagnetic Potts Gibbs measure and exponential decay of correlations for three colors on the hexagonal lattice and four, five, or six colors on the square lattice. The supplied excerpt leaves the temperature range, the precise correlation bound, and the additional cases indicated by "etc." unspecified.
