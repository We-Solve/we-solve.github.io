Math-966

Poisson Eigenvalue Statistics for Random Band Matrices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the random band-matrix family $X_{N,W}$ of the source, establish Poisson local eigenvalue statistics throughout the full regime \[ N\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad\frac{W(N)^2}{N}\longrightarrow0, \] rather than only under the stronger source hypothesis $W(N)^\nu/N\to0$. The exponent $\nu$ is not specified, and no numerical value is invented. The requested conclusion must apply to every admissible bandwidth sequence in the intended full regime, not merely one power-law subclass.

The matrix ensemble is not defined in the record. Its entry distributions, symmetry class, independence assumptions, variance normalization, geometry and boundary convention for the band, and any requirement $W\to\infty$ must be recovered. In particular, "random band matrix" alone does not determine a universal ensemble for which the claim can be asserted.

Poisson statistics normally refer to an unfolded local eigenvalue point process. For illustration, if $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_N$ are eigenvalues in a normalization with positive density $\varrho(E)$ at a fixed energy $E$, one writes \[ \Xi_{N,E}=\sum_{j=1}^N \delta_{N\varrho(E)(\lambda_j-E)}. \] The usual target is convergence in distribution, in the local/vague point-process topology, to the unit-intensity Poisson process on ${\mathbb{R}}$: counts in disjoint bounded test sets have independent Poisson laws with means equal to their lengths. This expression is explanatory, not a supplied normalization. The record does not state the energy range, unfolding density, convergence topology, or whether the assertion is pointwise or uniform in energy.

The full-bandwidth target and the comparison regime are retained exactly. The claim is not replaced by localization alone, empirical-density convergence, or a statement under a larger unspecified exponent. The supplied assessment reports Poisson results in narrower bandwidth regimes; it supplies no complete theorem hypotheses or admissible range of $W$ beyond the displayed ratios.

Short English statement

The question asks for Poisson local eigenvalue statistics for the source's band matrices whenever $W^2/N$ tends to zero. The ensemble, eigenvalue normalization, energy range, and bandwidth side conditions are missing, so the standard unfolded point-process description is only explanatory.
