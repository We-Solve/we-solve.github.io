Geometry of Continued Fractions — Further open questions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the classical normalization, let $Q(x,y)=ax^2+bxy+cy^2$ be a real indefinite binary quadratic form with discriminant $b^2-4ac=1$, and define \[ m(Q)=\inf\{|Q(u,v)|:(u,v)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}\}. \] The finite Markov spectrum is \[ M=\{m(Q)^{-1}:b^2-4ac=1,\ m(Q)>0\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}. \] Forms with $m(Q)=0$ can instead be represented by an infinite spectral value if an extended spectrum is intended. The source does not specify its normalization or whether that value is included.

For the supplied comparison with the Lagrange spectrum, a classical finite-value convention is \[ L=\left\{\limsup_{q\to\infty}\frac{1}{q\|q\alpha\|}:\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}},\ \limsup_{q\to\infty}\frac{1}{q\|q\alpha\|}<\infty\right\}, \] where $q$ runs through positive integers and $\|t\|=\inf_{z\in{\mathbb{Z}}}|t-z|$.

Research direction. Study the geometric properties of the Markov spectrum. With the above convention, this concerns the geometry of the subset $M\subset{\mathbb{R}}$, but the source supplies no selected property, conjectural formula, parameter range, or criterion for a complete answer. It is therefore an open-ended program rather than a single quantified assertion. The research summary identifies fractal/Hausdorff-dimension structure and the structure of $M\setminus L$ as areas with extensive partial progress, and reports that Cusick's conjecture has been resolved, without stating that conjecture or citing a result. These references to progress are retained as supplied claims; they do not resolve the entire stated research direction.

The registry status is open and its research classification is OPEN-TRIAGE, whereas the summary describes a literature-survey program with many solved subquestions and no single complete resolution. No particular new subquestion is invented to replace the original direction.

Short English statement

Investigate the geometry of the Markov spectrum, viewed in its classical normalization as a subset of the real line. This is a broad research program, with reported progress on fractal dimensions and the difference from the Lagrange spectrum, rather than one conjecture with a specified completion criterion.
