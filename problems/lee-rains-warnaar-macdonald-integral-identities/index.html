Lee–Rains–Warnaar Macdonald Integral Identities

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let a partition be a finite weakly decreasing sequence $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_\ell)$ of positive integers, with the empty partition also allowed. Its Young diagram is $D_\lambda=\{(i,j):1\le i\le\ell,\ 1\le j\le\lambda_i\}$. A domino consists of two cells sharing an edge. A partition has empty $2$-core precisely when its Young diagram can be tiled by dominoes. The supplied excerpt gives $(6,4,3,1)$ as an example. Write $P_\lambda(x;q,t)$ for the Macdonald symmetric polynomials, indexed by partitions, with parameters $q,t$; their Schur specialization is $q=t$.

Requested conjectural assertion, specified by reference. Establish the pair of vanishing-integral identities for Macdonald polynomials attributed to Lee, Rains and Warnaar in reference [3, Conjecture 9.2] of the supplied Oberwolfach excerpt, throughout their intended parameter ranges, including the general case beyond the Schur specialization $q=t$.

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not reproduce either integral or its proposed evaluation. It also omits the integration variables, measure, domain or contour, auxiliary parameters, admissibility and convergence hypotheses, partition restrictions, and exact vanishing conditions. Thus the assertion above identifies the intended target by reference but is not a self-contained, fully quantified mathematical conjecture. The excerpt associates the surrounding conjectures with partitions having empty $2$-core; it does not specify how that condition enters either of the two integral identities. No vanishing criterion can therefore be inferred from that association alone. The exact identities and their hypotheses must be supplied before a precise standalone statement can be completed.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the particular pair of Lee--Rains--Warnaar vanishing-integral conjectures holds for general Macdonald parameters beyond $q=t$. The supplied curation credits Albion with proving the Schur case. The actual integral formulas and hypotheses are missing, so this draft identifies the target but requires source recovery and human mathematical review.
