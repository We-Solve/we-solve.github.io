Math-15207

Polynomial Degree of IA-Group Homology Functors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathrm{Ab}$ denote the category of abelian groups, and let $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$. The supplied record names a category $S({\mathbb{Z}})$, a family of groups $\operatorname{IA}_{\bullet}$, and a homology functor \[ \mathcal H_k:=H_k(\operatorname{IA}_{\bullet})\colon S({\mathbb{Z}})\longrightarrow\mathrm{Ab}. \] Here $H_k$ denotes group homology in degree $k$, but the coefficient module and its action are not specified in the record.

Conjecture attributed to Djament in the supplied curation. For every nonnegative integer $k$, the functor $\mathcal H_k$ is weakly polynomial of degree at most $3k$: \[ \forall k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\qquad \deg_{\mathrm{weak}}\!\left(H_k(\operatorname{IA}_{\bullet})\right)\leq 3k. \] The notation $\deg_{\mathrm{weak}}(F)\leq d$ here abbreviates the source's assertion that $F$ is weakly polynomial of degree at most $d$.

Unresolved definitions. The supplied record does not define the objects or morphisms of $S({\mathbb{Z}})$, the groups $\operatorname{IA}_{\bullet}$, the maps making their homology a functor on this category, or the intended definition of weak polynomiality. A customary possible interpretation of $\operatorname{IA}_n$ is the kernel of the action of the automorphism group of a free group of rank $n$ on its abelianization, but that interpretation is not specified by the supplied text and is not imposed here. Likewise, integral homology with trivial coefficients is not silently assumed. The displayed conjecture therefore preserves the source formulation, but requires these definitions and conventions to be confirmed before it becomes a self-contained mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture, attributed to Djament, asks whether the weak polynomial degree of the IA-group homology functor in every degree $k\geq 0$ is at most $3k$. The category, IA groups, homology coefficients, functorial maps, and weak polynomiality convention require confirmation from the cited sources.
