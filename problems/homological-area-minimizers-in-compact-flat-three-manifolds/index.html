Homological Area Minimizers in Compact Flat Three-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{F}}_2={\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. For a compact flat three-manifold $M$, let $H_2(M;{\mathbb{F}}_2)$ denote its second homology group with mod-two coefficients. Given a specified class $\alpha\in H_2(M;{\mathbb{F}}_2)$ and a specified collection $\mathcal C(M,\alpha)$ of admissible surface representatives of $\alpha$, define \[ m(M,\alpha)=\inf\{\operatorname{Area}(T):T\in\mathcal C(M,\alpha)\}. \] An admissible representative $S$ is an area minimizer in $\alpha$ if \[ [S]=\alpha,\qquad \operatorname{Area}(S)=m(M,\alpha). \] The supplied record does not specify the admissible category, for example whether competitors are smooth embedded surfaces or mod-two integral cycles. Thus $\mathcal C(M,\alpha)$ remains to be fixed from the source context.

Write $M_X(h)$ for the compact flat three-manifold called the $X$ Cosm in the supplied record, where \[ X\in\{\mathrm{Tri},\mathrm{Tetra},\mathrm{Hexa}\},\qquad h\in I_X, \] and $h$ is its height parameter. The parameter domains $I_X$, the quotient constructions, and the metric normalizations are not supplied. Let $P_X(h)$ denote the horizontal planar surface referred to in the record, and let $S_X(h)$ denote the corresponding catenoid-like candidate, with names assigned by \[ S_{\mathrm{Tri}}=\mathrm{rPD},\qquad S_{\mathrm{Tetra}}=\mathrm{tP},\qquad S_{\mathrm{Hexa}}=\mathrm{H}. \] These names identify source candidates; the record does not provide their constructions or their dependence on $h$.

Question, with unresolved source parameters. For each of these three families, is there a division of the admissible height range into a planar range $I_X^{\mathrm{plan}}$ and a complementary catenoid-like range $I_X^{\mathrm{cat}}$ such that, in the intended homology class $\alpha_X(h)$, \[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{Area}(P_X(h))&=m(M_X(h),\alpha_X(h)) &&\text{for every }h\in I_X^{\mathrm{plan}},\\ \operatorname{Area}(S_X(h))&=m(M_X(h),\alpha_X(h)) &&\text{for every }h\in I_X^{\mathrm{cat}}? \end{aligned} \] The intended representatives must belong to the indicated class wherever they are compared. Their homology classes, the height ranges, and the treatment of any transition value are not specified in the supplied record; in particular, no assertion of uniqueness or of strict inequalities between competing areas is included here.

For the Tetra Cosm, the record additionally refers to two remaining nonzero homology classes. Denote the class intended for $\mathrm{CLP}$ by $\beta(h)$, and that intended for $\mathrm{tD}$ by $\gamma(h)$. Are the named minimal surfaces area minimizers in those respective classes, that is, \[ \operatorname{Area}(\mathrm{CLP}(h))=m(M_{\mathrm{Tetra}}(h),\beta(h)), \qquad \operatorname{Area}(\mathrm{tD}(h))=m(M_{\mathrm{Tetra}}(h),\gamma(h))? \] Here the notation records the intended dependence on the ambient metric without asserting a supplied construction. The parameter values for which these assertions are intended, the identification of the two classes, and the realizations of the surfaces remain unspecified. Consequently, these formulas give the variational content of the supplied questions, but a fully determined formulation requires the missing geometric and parameter data.

Short English statement

The question asks whether varying the height of the Tri, Tetra, and Hexa Cosms changes the mod-two area minimizer from a horizontal planar surface to, respectively, $\mathrm{rPD}$, $\mathrm{tP}$, and $\mathrm{H}$, and whether $\mathrm{CLP}$ and $\mathrm{tD}$ minimize area in the two other indicated nonzero classes of the Tetra Cosm. The supplied record leaves the metrics, parameter ranges, transition behavior, surface constructions, and competitor category unresolved.
