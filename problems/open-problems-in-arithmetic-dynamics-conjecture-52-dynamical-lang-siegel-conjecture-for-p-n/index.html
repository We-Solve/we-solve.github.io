Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Conjecture 52 (Dynamical Lang--Siegel Conjecture for $\mathbb{P}^N$)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N\geq 1$ denote the projective dimension, as implicitly intended in the supplied statement, and let \[ f:\mathbb{P}^N_{\mathbb{Q}}\longrightarrow\mathbb{P}^N_{\mathbb{Q}} \] be a morphism of degree $d\geq 2$. Write $f^0=\mathrm{id}$, and let $f^n$ denote the $n$-fold iterate. For each $n\geq 1$, choose a homogeneous presentation \[ f^n=[F_0^{(n)}:\cdots:F_N^{(n)}], \qquad F_i^{(n)}\in\mathbb{Q}[X_0,\ldots,X_N], \] whose coordinate forms have degree $d^n$ and no common projective zero. Assume that \[ F_0^{(n)}\neq cX_0^{d^n} \quad\text{for every }c\in\mathbb{Q} \quad\text{and every }1\leq n\leq N+1. \] This condition is independent of the common nonzero scalar used in the homogeneous presentation.

Set $H=\{X_0=0\}$. A rational point $Q\notin H$ is integral relative to $H$ in the standard coordinates if \[ Q=[1:b_1:\cdots:b_N],\qquad b_1,\ldots,b_N\in\mathbb{Z}. \] Equivalently, if $Q=[a_0:\cdots:a_N]$ is represented by relatively prime integer coordinates, then $Q$ is integral relative to $H$ exactly when $|a_0|=1$. Define \[ \mathcal{O}_f(P)=\{f^n(P):n\geq 0\}. \] A point $P$ is wandering if this orbit is infinite.

Supplied conjectural assertion (Conjecture 52). For every $P\in\mathbb{P}^N(\mathbb{Q})$, \[ \#\left\{Q\in\mathcal{O}_f(P):Q\text{ is integral relative to }H\right\}<\infty. \] The source further gives the following assertion as a more precise formulation. If $P$ is wandering, choose, for every $n\geq 1$, coordinates \[ f^n(P)=[A_0(n):\cdots:A_N(n)],\qquad A_i(n)\in\mathbb{Z},\qquad \gcd(A_0(n),\ldots,A_N(n))=1. \] Then the asserted limit is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{\log|A_0(n)|} {\log\max_{0\leq i\leq N}|A_i(n)|}=1. \] The absolute values make this expression independent of the simultaneous sign choice for the primitive coordinates.

Unresolved qualification. The displayed quotient requires $A_0(n)\neq 0$ and $\max_i|A_i(n)|>1$. A real-valued limit therefore requires these conditions on an eventual tail. The supplied statement does not specify eventual avoidance of $H$, a convention for $\log 0$, or a restriction to selected indices. It also states no Zariski-density assumption on the orbit and no exclusion of orbits in proper invariant subvarieties. Whether an additional orbit hypothesis is intended requires source and mathematical review; none has been inserted into the assertions above.

Short English statement

The supplied Dynamical Lang--Siegel conjecture predicts that a rational orbit contains only finitely many points with integral affine coordinates relative to $X_0=0$, under the stated restriction on the first $N+1$ iterates. For a wandering point it further asserts that the logarithm of the first primitive coordinate is asymptotic to the logarithmic projective height. Possible missing orbit hypotheses and the meaning of this limit when the first coordinate vanishes remain unresolved.
