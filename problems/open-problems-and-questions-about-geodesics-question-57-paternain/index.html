Open Problems and Questions about Geodesics — Question 57 (Paternain)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be a closed surface, meaning a compact connected surface without boundary, and let $g$ be a Riemannian metric on $S$. Under the usual smooth-metric interpretation, define its unit tangent bundle by \[ T^1_gS=\{(x,v)\in TS:g_x(v,v)=1\}. \] The geodesic flow $\varphi_g^t:T^1_gS\to T^1_gS$ sends an initial unit tangent vector to the velocity at time $t$ of its geodesic. Let $\mu_g$ be the invariant Liouville measure normalized to have total mass one. More explicitly, if $\pi:T^1_gS\to S$ is the bundle projection and $\alpha_g$ is the canonical contact form \[ (\alpha_g)_{(x,v)}(\xi)=g_x\left(v,{\,\mathrm{d}}\pi_{(x,v)}\xi\right), \] then $\mu_g$ is the probability measure obtained by normalizing the volume density $\lvert\alpha_g\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha_g\rvert$. Define the Liouville entropy of the flow to be its measure-theoretic entropy per unit time, \[ h_{\mathrm L}(g):=h_{\mu_g}(\varphi_g^1). \] The question attributed to Paternain is whether \[ \exists\,(S,g)\quad \text{such that}\quad S\text{ is closed},\qquad \operatorname{genus}(S)\ge 2,\qquad h_{\mathrm L}(g)=0. \] The source does not explicitly specify orientability or the regularity class of the metric. The displayed formulation retains its genus condition; the intended genus convention and the usual smooth-metric interpretation require confirmation.

As motivation, the supplied excerpt attributes to Mañé, under the reference label [Mane1996], a generic alternative for Hamiltonian flows on surfaces: being Anosov or having "zero Liouville exponents." The hypotheses, meaning of genericity, and precise exponent terminology are not supplied, so this contextual assertion is not formulated here as a theorem. The excerpt describes the present question as an easier version of a preceding question that is absent from the record.

Short English statement

Does some closed surface of genus at least two admit a Riemannian metric whose geodesic flow has zero measure-theoretic entropy with respect to normalized Liouville measure? This is the supplied question attributed to Paternain; its orientability and metric-regularity conventions need confirmation.
