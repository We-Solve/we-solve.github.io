Banach Spaces and Ramsey Theory — Problem 5

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a Banach space over its scalar field $\mathbb K$. The supplied record does not specify whether $\mathbb K={\mathbb{R}}$ or $\mathbb K={\mathbb{C}}$. Recall that $X$ is reflexive if its canonical embedding into $X^{**}$ is surjective. A sequence $(x_n)_{n\geq1}$ in $X$ is normalized and weakly null if $\|x_n\|=1$ for every $n$ and $f(x_n)\to0$ for every $f\in X^*$.

For this draft, interpret the spreading-model notation in the following standard sense; the supplied excerpt does not itself define it. A normalized basic sequence $(e_j)_{j\geq1}$ in a Banach space is a spreading model generated by $(x_n)$ if, for every $k\geq1$, every $(a_1,\ldots,a_k)\in\mathbb K^k$, and every $\varepsilon>0$, there is $N\geq1$ such that \[ \left|\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_jx_{n_j}\right\|_X-\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_je_j\right\|\right|<\varepsilon \quad\text{whenever }N\leq n_1<\cdots<n_k. \] Write $\mathrm{SP}_w(X)$ for the spreading models generated by normalized weakly null sequences in $X$. Two basic sequences $(e_j)$ and $(v_j)$ are equivalent, written $(e_j)\sim(v_j)$, if there are constants $0<A\leq B<\infty$ such that, for every $k\geq1$ and every $(a_1,\ldots,a_k)\in\mathbb K^k$, \[ A\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_jv_j\right\| \leq\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_je_j\right\| \leq B\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k a_jv_j\right\|. \] Thus $\mathrm{SP}_w(X)/{\sim}$ denotes equivalence classes of these basic sequences.

Let $c_0(\mathbb K)$ be the space of scalar sequences tending to zero, with the supremum norm, and, for $1\leq p<\infty$, let $\ell_p(\mathbb K)$ be the space of scalar sequences $(a_j)$ with $\sum_j|a_j|^p<\infty$, with norm $(\sum_j|a_j|^p)^{1/p}$. Their standard unit vectors are denoted by $(u_j)$.

The countable spreading models problem, attributed in the supplied record to S. J. Dilworth, E. Odell and B. Sari, asks: if $X$ is reflexive and \[ \left|\mathrm{SP}_w(X)/{\sim}\right|\leq\aleph_0, \] must there exist a normalized weakly null sequence in $X$ generating a spreading model equivalent to $(u_j)$ in $c_0(\mathbb K)$, or equivalent to $(u_j)$ in $\ell_p(\mathbb K)$ for some real $p$ with $1\leq p<\infty$?

The supplied statement imposes neither separability nor an explicit infinite-dimensionality assumption. In particular, its cardinality condition allows $\mathrm{SP}_w(X)$ to be empty. Whether an ambient convention excludes finite-dimensional spaces, or whether an infinite-dimensionality hypothesis is missing, requires source review. No such hypothesis has been silently added here.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether a reflexive Banach space with at most countably many equivalence classes of weakly null spreading models must have one equivalent to the standard basis of $c_0$ or of some $\ell_p$, $1\leq p<\infty$. The scalar-field convention and a possible implicit infinite-dimensionality assumption require review. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied metadata, not an independently verified status assessment.
