Dimension and Quadraticity of Transposition Nichols Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record contains incompatible formulations. The following makes the corrected statement about transpositions precise conditional on a choice of ground field; the record does not specify that field or its characteristic.

Let $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ satisfy $n\geq6$, and let \[ \mathcal O_2^n=\{(i\,j)\in S_n:1\leq i<j\leq n\} \] be the conjugacy class of transpositions, with rack operation $x\triangleright y=xyx^{-1}$. For a ground field $k$, let $V_n$ have basis $\{e_x:x\in\mathcal O_2^n\}$ and braiding \[ c(e_x\otimes e_y)=-e_{x\triangleright y}\otimes e_x. \] This is the braiding associated with the constant cocycle $-1\in k^\times$. Give the tensor algebra $T(V_n)$ its braided Hopf algebra structure with $V_n$ primitive. Let $I_n$ be the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{d\geq2}V_n^{\otimes d}$. The associated Nichols algebra is \[ \mathfrak B_k(\mathcal O_2^n,-1)=T(V_n)/I_n. \] Set $I_{n,2}=I_n\cap V_n^{\otimes2}$. The algebra is quadratic if its defining ideal is generated by its degree-two part, that is, \[ I_n=\langle I_{n,2}\rangle. \] Equivalently, the canonical surjection from its quadratic cover \[ T(V_n)/\langle I_{n,2}\rangle\longrightarrow\mathfrak B_k(\mathcal O_2^n,-1) \] is an isomorphism.

Conditional problem. For each integer $n\geq6$, determine $\dim_k\mathfrak B_k(\mathcal O_2^n,-1)$, including whether it is finite, and determine whether this Nichols algebra is quadratic. The intended ground field and characteristic must be recovered from the authoritative source before this becomes a fully specified statement; no universal quantification over ground fields is asserted here.

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied dated literature assessment instead concerns a fixed-point-free involution rack denoted $O_{2n}$, with cocycle $-1$ and parameter $n\geq6$. It reports unresolved finite dimensionality and quadraticity, and states that infinite dimensionality of the quadratic cover does not establish equality with the Nichols algebra. This assessment cannot be transferred to the transposition rack without clarification. The original statement instead asks about quadraticity for the Nichols algebra of the Jordanian quantum plane, or more generally a Nichols algebra of triangular type in a stated finite-Gelfand--Kirillov-dimension setting. The requisite setting and hypotheses are absent, so that alternative cannot be formulated precisely from this record.

Short English statement

For every integer $n\geq6$, the corrected statement asks for the dimension of the Nichols algebra attached to transpositions in $S_n$ with constant cocycle $-1$, and whether its relations are generated in degree two. The ground field is unspecified. Conflicting passages concern fixed-point-free involutions and triangular Nichols algebras, so the intended formulation and applicability of the supplied open-status assessment require review.
