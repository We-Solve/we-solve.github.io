Lyapunov points versus uniform dichotomy intervals

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks broadly about spectral properties and Lyapunov spectra for sequential or nonstationary systems. It does not specify the cocycle, state space, spectrum, time axis, or stationarity assumptions. The aid distinguishes from-origin growth exponents from a uniform exponential-dichotomy spectrum in a deterministic finite-dimensional setting.

For a scalar equation $x_{n+1}=e^{s_n}x_n$, $n\ge0$, assume $s_n$ is a bounded real sequence. Define uniform long-window endpoints \[ \underline\beta=\lim_{L\to\infty}\inf_{m\ge0} \frac1L\sum_{j=m}^{m+L-1}s_j,\qquad \overline\beta=\lim_{L\to\infty}\sup_{m\ge0} \frac1L\sum_{j=m}^{m+L-1}s_j. \] The aid reports that its logarithmic uniform dichotomy spectrum is exactly $[\underline\beta,\overline\beta]$. Here a parameter $\lambda$ lies outside that spectrum when the reweighted evolution $e^{-\lambda(n-m)}\Phi(n,m)$ has a uniform exponential dichotomy: invariant projections, and constants $C,\alpha>0$, bound stable forward evolution and unstable backward evolution by $Ce^{-\alpha|n-m|}$, for all valid time pairs. These are uniform-in-start-time estimates, not only estimates from time zero. Bounded logarithmic increments, rather than merely bounded multipliers possibly tending to zero, are explicit.

An explicit balanced-run realization of the reported example uses \[ A_+=\operatorname{diag}(e,e^{-2}),\qquad A_-=\operatorname{diag}(e^{-1},e^{-2}), \] in successive blocks $+^1,-^1,+^2,-^2,+^3,-^3,\ldots$. Let $s_j\in\{1,-1\}$ be this sign sequence and \[ \Phi(n,m)=\operatorname{diag} \left(\exp\left(\sum_{j=m}^{n-1}s_j\right),e^{-2(n-m)}\right). \] The aid reports convergence of the full from-origin singular-value exponents to $0,-2$, with the top finite-time error $O(n^{-1/2})$. Every product is positive diagonal and normal, so eigenvalue moduli agree with its singular values. The invariant coordinate splitting is uniformly dominated, with \[ \frac{\|\Phi(n,m)|_{{\mathbb{R}} e_2}\|} {\|\Phi(n,m)|_{{\mathbb{R}} e_1}\|}\le e^{-(n-m)}\qquad(n\ge m). \] Nevertheless the unbounded positive and negative runs give first-coordinate uniform endpoints $-1,1$, and the second coordinate is constant $-2$. Its reported full dichotomy spectrum is therefore \[ \{-2\}\cup[-1,1]. \] Thus normality, equality of product eigenvalue/singular-value moduli, domination, and convergent origin exponents do not collapse this uniform spectrum to the two origin points. The example separates notions of spectrum; it does not classify general sequential transfer operators, infinite-dimensional cocycles, or all nonstationary systems asked about by the source.

Short English statement

The source asks about spectra in nonstationary dynamics. The supplied aid separates ordinary growth from one starting time from uniform growth over all time windows, with a dominated positive diagonal example having origin exponents $0,-2$ but dichotomy spectrum $\{-2\}\cup[-1,1]$.
