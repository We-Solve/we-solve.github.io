Math-4435

Conlon–Lee Conjecture for Graph Subdivisions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite undirected simple graph $F$, define \[ \operatorname{ex}(n,F)=\max\{e(G): |V(G)|=n,\ G\text{ is a finite undirected simple graph containing no subgraph isomorphic to }F\}, \] whenever the family over which the maximum is taken is nonempty. Containment here means ordinary, not necessarily induced, subgraph containment. These are the conventional graph and extremal-number interpretations used in this draft; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify them.

For an even integer $k\geq2$, construct $H^{k-1}$ by replacing each edge $uv$ of $H$ with a path from $u$ to $v$ having exactly $k$ edges and $k-1$ new internal vertices. All new internal vertices are distinct across replacement paths and lie outside $V(H)$; isolated vertices of $H$ are retained.

The supplied Conlon--Lee subdivision conjecture asks whether, for every even integer $k\geq2$ and every graph $H$, there exists $\delta=\delta(k,H)>0$ such that \[ \operatorname{ex}(n,H^{k-1})=O\!\left(n^{1+1/k-\delta}\right) \qquad(n\to\infty). \] Explicitly, for each fixed pair $(k,H)$, the requested conclusion is that there exist real constants $\delta>0$, $C>0$, and an integer $n_0\geq1$ such that, for every integer $n\geq n_0$, \[ \operatorname{ex}(n,H^{k-1})\leq Cn^{1+1/k-\delta}. \] The constants may depend on $k$ and $H$, but not on $n$.

Unresolved boundary qualification. The source quantifies over every graph $H$, without excluding edgeless graphs. Under ordinary subgraph containment, if $H$ is edgeless and $n\geq |V(H)|$, every $n$-vertex graph contains $H^{k-1}=H$. The defining family for the maximum is then empty. Thus the universal wording requires either an explicit convention for this case or a hypothesis that $H$ has an edge; neither is supplied. For finite simple $H$ with at least one edge, the displayed formulation is well-defined.

The supplied dated curation labels the record partially solved and reports the bipartite case as proved and the general conjecture as open. It also describes a separate $L$-subdivision problem as completely resolved by Corollary 13. That separate problem is not formulated in the supplied record. These are supplied source assessments, not independently verified mathematical or literature conclusions.

Short English statement

For each fixed even integer $k\geq2$ and graph $H$, the conjecture asks whether forbidding the subdivision obtained by replacing each edge of $H$ with a path of $k$ edges forces an extremal bound with exponent strictly below $1+1/k$. The improvement may depend on $k$ and $H$. The graph conventions and edgeless boundary case require confirmation; the supplied partial-resolution assessment remains unverified.
