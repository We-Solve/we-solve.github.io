Positive Self-Stress Functionals on Planar $K_{4}$ Embeddings

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K_4$ be the complete graph on vertices $1,2,3,4$. A planar realization is a tuple $P=(p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4)$ of distinct points of ${\mathbb{R}}^2$, with an edge represented by the segment joining its endpoints. The supplied record requires general position but does not define it. The usual condition that no three points are collinear is a possible interpretation, requiring confirmation. It is also unspecified whether crossings are allowed or whether "embedding" requires a crossing-free drawing.

A self-stress of $P$ is a collection $\omega=(\omega_{ij})_{1\leq i<j\leq4}$, extended by $\omega_{ji}=\omega_{ij}$, satisfying \[ \sum_{j\ne i}\omega_{ij}(p_j-p_i)=0 \qquad (i=1,2,3,4). \] For an affinely spanning realization of $K_4$, the self-stress space is one-dimensional. Consequently, specifying an associated nonzero self-stress requires a choice of sign; a strict positivity condition cannot be interpreted independently of that choice.

Characterize all admissible functions $f$ satisfying \[ \forall P\in\mathcal G,\qquad L_P(f)>0. \] The record does not specify the admissible function class, any regularity assumptions, or whether this global segment-function interpretation is intended instead of a configuration-dependent assignment.

For precision about the proposed equivalence, one conventional rigidity map is \[ R_P:({\mathbb{R}}^2)^4\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^6,\qquad (R_Pu)_{ij}=(p_i-p_j)\mathbin{\cdot}(u_i-u_j). \] Its range consists of edge assignments arising from vertex velocities, with this normalization of the map. The source does not specify how additions from these configuration-dependent ranges are to define a single admissible function on segments.

Under the conventional interpretation of brackets as signed doubled triangle area, define \[ [xyz]=\det(y-x,z-x),\qquad F_{a,b}(\overline{xy})=[axy][byx], \] where $a,b\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$. The product is unchanged when $x$ and $y$ are interchanged. The record asks whether every function with the required positivity is obtained from this construction by positive scaling and addition of a function in the rigidity-map range. At an individual realization, the displayed candidate form would read \[ \left(f_{ij}(P)\right)_{i<j} =c\left([ap_ip_j][bp_jp_i]\right)_{i<j}+R_Pu, \qquad c>0,\quad u\in({\mathbb{R}}^2)^4. \] This expression records the proposed form, not a settled universal formulation: the record supplies neither restrictions on $a,b$ nor the quantifiers determining whether $a,b,c,u$ may depend on $P$. Short English statement

For permanent UnsolvedMath ID $30001136$, the question asks which assignments to planar segments have a strictly positive pairing with the selected self-stress of every admissible realization of $K_4$, and whether the displayed area-product construction generates all such assignments under the stated operations. The stress sign, realization class, function domain, auxiliary-point restrictions, and equivalence quantifiers remain unresolved. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
