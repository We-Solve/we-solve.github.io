Practice-Based Histories of Applied Mathematics

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Nature and scope of the task. The supplied record presents a methodological question in the history of mathematics, rather than a mathematical proposition. It supplies no formal definitions of applied mathematics, mathematicians' practices, knowledge transmission, canonical texts, or disciplines. These expressions therefore retain their historical meanings, whose precise scope must be established through source-based research.

Source-stated research task. Develop a historical analysis of applied mathematics based on what mathematicians do, including the transmission of knowledge and the formation of canonical texts and disciplines.

The intended methodological direction is to move from a present-day impression toward an evidence-based history of mathematicians' practices. The record does not specify a historical period, geographical scope, population of mathematicians, documentary corpus, criteria for identifying canonical texts or disciplines, or criteria for completing the analysis. Consequently, it does not determine a mathematical statement with specified domains, hypotheses, boundary conditions, or quantifiers. Adding these would introduce content absent from the supplied evidence. This draft preserves the research task and leaves its historical scope and methodology unresolved.

Short English statement

Develop an evidence-based history of applied mathematics through mathematicians' activities, including how knowledge circulates and how canonical texts and disciplines emerge. This is a historical research agenda with unspecified scope and methods; the supplied record does not formulate a mathematical conjecture.
