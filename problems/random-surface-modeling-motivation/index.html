Short English statement

The source asks how to model unspecified differences between shapes and efficiently sample many similar shapes, possibly evolving in time. It mentions Gaussian perturbations of modes and stochastic surface models, but the excerpt lacks the definitions and hypotheses needed for a precise mathematical problem.
