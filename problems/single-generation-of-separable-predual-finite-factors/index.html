Single generation of separable-predual finite factors

OpenAI's claim

All type-II_1 factors with separable predual are claimed to have a single operator generator; the established direct-integral reduction then gives single generation for general separable-predual von Neumann algebras. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every type-$\mathrm{II}_1$ factor $M$ with separable predual, OpenAI claims self-adjoint $a,b\in M$ with \[ M=W^*(a,b)=W^*(a+ib). \] Here $W^*$ means generated von Neumann algebra. Its relative generation input says that for every irreducible inclusion $P\subset M$ of such finite factors, the unitaries $u$ with $W^*(P,u)=M$ form a dense $G_\delta$ in the trace $2$-norm topology. The headline conclusion is single non-self-adjoint generation.
