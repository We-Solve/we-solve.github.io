Complexity in Enumerative Combinatorics — Conjecture 13 (cf. [Ges-sem])

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $i\in{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfy $i^2=-1$. For every integer $n\geq 0$, define the Gessel sequence by \[ b_n=2\cdot 5^n-(3+4i)^n-(3-4i)^n. \] The index range is supported by the source's displayed generating series, which starts at $n=0$. The source states that $b_n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ for every $n\geq 0$, and that the sequence is C-recursive, meaning that it satisfies a linear recurrence with constant coefficients. It presents this sequence as an example without an easy combinatorial interpretation.

Unresolved conjectural statement. The supplied extraction of Conjecture 13 ends with a broken sentence referring to a sequence and to type $\mathrm{(C1)}$. It supplies neither the definition of this type nor a complete assertion about membership in it. Consequently, the sequence is precisely specified above, but the intended conjecture cannot be reconstructed as a definite mathematical statement from the supplied record. The displayed integrality expansion and generating-function formula are also truncated and are not reproduced as valid identities.

Short English statement

The record defines the Gessel sequence $b_n=2\cdot5^n-(3+4i)^n-(3-4i)^n$ for $n\geq0$. Its intended conjecture concerns type $\mathrm{(C1)}$, but the extraction omits both the type's definition and the complete conjectural assertion. This statement-only draft therefore requires source recovery and human mathematical review.
