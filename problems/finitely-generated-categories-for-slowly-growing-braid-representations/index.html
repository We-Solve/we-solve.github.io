Math-15210

Finitely Generated Categories for Slowly Growing Braid Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 2$, the braid group $B_n$ has presentation \[ B_n=\left\langle \sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\ \middle|\ \sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\ (1\leq i\leq n-2),\quad \sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i\ (|i-j|\geq 2)\right\rangle. \] Set $B_1=\{1\}$ as a convention for the formulation below. The symmetric group $S_n$ is the group of permutations of an $n$-element set. The category $\mathrm{FI}$ consists of finite sets and injective maps; the automorphism group of an $n$-element object is $S_n$.

For a field $k$, a representation of $B_n$ is a vector space $V_n$ over $k$ with a homomorphism $\rho_n:B_n\to\mathrm{GL}_k(V_n)$. For an essentially small category $\mathcal C$, a $\mathcal C$-module over $k$ means a covariant functor $M:\mathcal C\to\mathrm{Vect}_k$. It is finitely generated if there are finitely many objects $d_1,\ldots,d_r$ and vectors $x_i\in M(d_i)$ such that, for every object $d$, \[ M(d)=\operatorname{span}_k\{M(f)(x_i):1\leq i\leq r,\ f\in\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(d_i,d)\}. \] These definitions describe a possible field-valued interpretation; the source does not specify a coefficient field or ring.

Source question. Is there a category playing for the braid groups $B_n$ the role that $\mathrm{FI}$ plays for the symmetric groups $S_n$, such that every slowly growing sequence of braid-group representations arises from a finitely generated module over that category?

A conditional formal framework for this question is the following. After fixing coefficients and defining the predicate $\mathsf{Slow}$ on representation sequences, seek a single essentially small category $\mathcal C$, objects $c_n$, and homomorphisms \[ \iota_n:B_n\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathcal C}(c_n) \] such that \[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every sequence }(V_n,\rho_n)_{n\geq1}\text{ satisfying }\mathsf{Slow},\\ \text{there exist a finitely generated }M:\mathcal C\to\mathrm{Vect}_k \text{ and isomorphisms }\phi_n:V_n\xrightarrow{\sim}M(c_n),\\ \phi_n\rho_n(b)=M(\iota_n(b))\phi_n \qquad(n\geq1,\ b\in B_n). \end{gathered} \] Here the category and its braid-group actions are chosen before the universally quantified representation sequence. The source does not specify whether the homomorphisms $\iota_n$ should be isomorphisms, what additional structure the category must possess, or whether realization is required in every rank or only eventually. Consequently this framework explains one meaning of realization, but is not asserted to be a uniquely determined formalization of the source.

Unresolved qualifications. The source gives no definition or numerical bound for "slowly growing," no coefficient domain or characteristic, no explicit finite-dimensionality hypothesis, and no compatibility conditions between successive representations. It also does not identify the precise $\mathrm{FI}$-module theorem being used as the model. These missing data prevent a fully specified conjecture. In particular, polynomial growth, subexponential growth, and linear growth must not be substituted for the undefined predicate without further evidence.

Short English statement

The question asks for a common categorical framework in which every slowly growing sequence of braid-group representations comes from a finitely generated functor. The source leaves the growth condition, coefficients, compatibility requirements, and required categorical structure unspecified, so the displayed formulation is conditional on resolving those choices. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
