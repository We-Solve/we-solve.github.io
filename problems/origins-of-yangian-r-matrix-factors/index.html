Origins of Yangian R-Matrix Factors

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $s\in{\mathbb{C}}$ denote the complex spectral parameter. The supplied record uses $R_{V,W}(s)$ for a Yangian $R$-matrix associated with representations $V,W$, and refers to its diagonal factor $R_0(s)$ and lower-triangular factor $R_-(s)$. These are source-dependent symbols: the record does not specify the underlying Yangian, the allowed representations, the tensor-product convention, the decomposition defining triangularity, the factorization order, or the normalization of the factors. Thus no particular factorization identity or universally quantified class of representations is asserted here. The domain on which the factors are defined, including any excluded singularities, also remains unspecified.

The extracted problem asks for the following interpretations of the factors in the intended source setting:

  1. Obtain the diagonal factor $R_0(s)$ from the four-dimensional Chern--Simons theory of Costello--Witten--Yamazaki.
  2. Identify the interpretation of the lower-triangular factor $R_-(s)$ in the geometric setting of Maulik--Okounkov.
The supplied record does not specify the Chern--Simons setup, the relevant geometric objects, or the precise correspondence by which either factor is to be recovered. Consequently, these are research questions with unresolved formulation data, rather than a fully specified theorem or existence conjecture.

The original passage also asks how to interpret $R_{V,W}(s)$ as a function of one complex variable and how to construct it explicitly. According to the supplied literature assessment dated 2026-08-22, the report gives explicit meromorphic constructions answering those first two questions, while the two interpretation questions above remain open. This is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion.

Short English statement

Explain how the diagonal factor of the source's Yangian $R$-matrix arises from four-dimensional Chern--Simons theory, and what its lower-triangular factor means in Maulik--Okounkov geometry. The algebra, representations, factor conventions, and intended correspondences require clarification from the cited report. The supplied partial-solution label concerns the broader original passage and is not independently verified.
