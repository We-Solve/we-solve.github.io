Special Lagrangian Fibrations in Calabi-Yau Degenerations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ denote the complex dimension of the Calabi--Yau manifolds under consideration, and write $X_t$ for a smooth fibre of the degeneration. On such a fibre, let $g_t$ denote the Ricci-flat Kähler metric under consideration, $\omega_t$ its Kähler form, and $\Omega_t$ a chosen nowhere-vanishing holomorphic $n$-form. The source does not specify the class of Calabi--Yau manifolds, the degeneration data, the Kähler classes determining the metrics, or the normalization of these forms.

Write $T^n={\mathbb{R}}^n/{\mathbb{Z}}^n$. An oriented real $n$-dimensional submanifold $L\subset X_t$ is special Lagrangian of phase $\theta\in{\mathbb{R}}/(2\pi{\mathbb{Z}})$ if \[ \omega_t|_L=0,\qquad \operatorname{Im}(e^{-i\theta}\Omega_t)|_L=0, \] and $\operatorname{Re}(e^{-i\theta}\Omega_t)|_L$ is a positive volume form for the chosen orientation.

On a region consisting of regular fibres, the requested fibration would be described by an open subset $U_t\subset X_t$, a smooth real $n$-manifold $B_t$, and a smooth surjective submersion \[ f_t:U_t\longrightarrow B_t \] whose fibres $L_{t,b}=f_t^{-1}(b)$, for every $b\in B_t$, are diffeomorphic to $T^n$ and special Lagrangian for $(\omega_t,\Omega_t)$. This describes the regular part of the requested geometric structure; it does not prescribe an extension across singular fibres or the omitted region.

Source question. In a large-complex-structure degeneration of Calabi--Yau $n$-folds, does the Ricci-flat metric admit such a special Lagrangian $T^n$-fibration on a generic region, as predicted by the Strominger--Yau--Zaslow conjecture?

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define "large-complex-structure degeneration" or "generic region." In particular, it does not specify a monodromy condition, the permitted degeneration families, whether the region must be dense or satisfy an asymptotic measure condition, or the required behaviour near its boundary. It also does not specify whether the assertion concerns every sufficiently nearby smooth fibre, a sequence of fibres approaching the limit, or an asymptotic or approximate fibration. No requirement concerning a common special Lagrangian phase for different fibres is stated. Thus the source identifies a geometric existence question but does not determine a unique fully quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether Calabi--Yau manifolds approaching a large-complex-structure limit carry a fibration by special Lagrangian $n$-tori on a generic region, as predicted by Strominger--Yau--Zaslow. The degeneration hypotheses, metric choices, meaning of generic region, and quantifiers near the limit remain unspecified in the supplied record.
