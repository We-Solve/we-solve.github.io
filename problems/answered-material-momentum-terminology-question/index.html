Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source item asks: "Is there confusion in the literature between conservation of material momentum and an equation governing microstructure?" Its supplied primary-source verification states that the report immediately answers "Yes." Accordingly this permanent record is an answered rhetorical or terminology question, not an unresolved quantified mathematical conjecture.

The two expressions refer to different proposed roles in continuum mechanics: a material-momentum conservation or balance law and an equation for microstructural degrees of freedom. The report excerpt does not supply the constitutive model, material-momentum definition, internal variables, variational setting, or either equation. No general identity, equivalence, or independence theorem between these objects can therefore be reconstructed from the question. A substantive follow-up would require the actual model and citations being compared; none is reproduced here.

The registry still labels the item open, and its literature triage checked 2026-08-21 says that the report records but does not resolve the issue. That assessment conflicts directly with the more specific supplied statement-verification field reporting the immediate answer. Both metadata claims are recorded, while the attributed source answer is preserved. The designation unrecoverable concerns the absence of an independent unresolved mathematical problem, not missing text of the rhetorical question.

Short English statement

The report's terminology question is answered "Yes" according to the supplied source verification. It should be retained as an answered source item, with the conflicting open-status metadata flagged, rather than turned into a new mathematical conjecture.
