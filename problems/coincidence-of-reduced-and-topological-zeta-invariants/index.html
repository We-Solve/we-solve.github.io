Math-14905

Coincidence of Reduced and Topological Zeta Invariants

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a $d$-dimensional algebra in the class intended by the source. The supplied record does not specify the base field or ring, the permitted algebra structures, or any restriction on $d$. It refers to a topological zeta function $Z_A^{\mathrm{top}}(s)$ and a reduced zeta function $Z_A^{\mathrm{red}}(T)$, but does not define these functions or specify what they count.

The symbols $m_{\mathrm{top}}(A)$ and $m_{\mathrm{red}}(A)$ denote invariants extracted, respectively, from the leading behavior of $Z_A^{\mathrm{top}}(s)$ at $s=0$ and of $Z_A^{\mathrm{red}}(T)$ at $T=1$. Their extraction formulas, including the powers, signs, and normalizations involved, are absent from the supplied record. Thus these descriptions do not determine the invariants precisely.

Coincidence conjecture, with unresolved definitions. For every algebra $A$ in the source's intended class, the conjectured assertion is \[ m_{\mathrm{red}}(A)=m_{\mathrm{top}}(A)\in{\mathbb{Q}}_{>0}. \] This is the supplied target, pending specification of the algebra class, the zeta functions, and the invariant normalizations; it is not a fully specified mathematical statement from the available evidence.

The excerpt additionally states that the topological degree conjecture is equivalent to $m_{\mathrm{top}}(A)$ being nonzero and finite. It also states that, if the Hilbert series conjecture holds, then $Z_A^{\mathrm{red}}(T)$ has a pole of order $d$ at $T=1$, whence $m_{\mathrm{red}}(A)$ is nonzero and finite. Neither auxiliary conjecture is formulated in the supplied record. These are source-reported relationships, not additional assumptions silently imposed on the coincidence conjecture.

Short English statement

The conjecture asserts that the invariants obtained from the topological and reduced zeta functions agree for every algebra covered by the source, and that their common value is a positive rational number. The supplied record omits the algebra hypotheses and the formulas defining these functions and invariants, so a fully precise formulation requires further source clarification.
