Trace-Cone Classification after Razak–Jacelon Stabilization

OpenAI's claim

The full topological cone of extended lower-semicontinuous traces is claimed to classify separable nuclear algebras after Razak--Jacelon and compact stabilization, realizing prescribed cone maps despite arbitrary ideals and nondense finite domains. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For separable nuclear complex $C^*$-algebras $A,B$, let $T(A),T(B)$ be their cones of lower-semicontinuous tracial weights, including zero and infinite values. Use the topology $\tau_i\to\tau$ when $\limsup_i\tau_i((a-\varepsilon)_+)\le\tau(a)\le\liminf_i\tau_i(a)$ for every positive $a$ and $\varepsilon>0$. OpenAI claims \[ T(A)\simeq T(B)\text{ as topological additive cones}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A\otimes\mathcal W\otimes\mathcal K\simeq B\otimes\mathcal W\otimes\mathcal K. \] Cone isomorphisms preserve addition, zero and positive scalar multiplication; $\mathcal W$ denotes the Razak--Jacelon algebra; $\mathcal K$ denotes compact operators. Tensor products are spatial. The final intertwining theorem also claims an isomorphism implementing a prescribed compatible trace-cone map and Cuntz-semigroup map on these stabilizations.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 301, 2026-09-25): Local rank controllers define error ideals and small stabilizing homomorphisms, allowing countably many approximation errors to be absorbed. Completely positive cone maps match trace moments while lower ideal comparisons preserve exactly where weights become infinite. Separate existence and norm-uniqueness steps then support approximate intertwining, producing an isomorphism with the prescribed trace-cone action.
