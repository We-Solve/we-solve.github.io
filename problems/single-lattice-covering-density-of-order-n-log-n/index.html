Math-10040

Single-lattice covering density of order n log n

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaiunknowngpt-6 astraunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Does an absolute $C>0$ exist such that, for every $n\geq 2$ and convex body $K\subset R^{n}$, some full-rank lattice $\Lambda $ satisfies $K+\Lambda =R^{n}$ and $vol_n(K)/det\Lambda \leq Cn$ log n? No symmetry, smoothness or normalization of K is assumed.

Short English statement

Does an absolute $C>0$ exist such that, for every $n\geq 2$ and convex body $K\subset R^{n}$, some full-rank lattice $\Lambda $ satisfies $K+\Lambda =R^{n}$ and $vol_n(K)/det\Lambda \leq Cn$ log n? No symmetry, smoothness or normalization of K is assumed.
