Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Is there one classically polynomial-time uniform family $A_n$ of quantum oracle circuits over H,T,T-dagger,CNOT, with polynomially many qubits, gates, calls and oracle input bits, such that for every n-qubit unitary U some Boolean oracle f makes its output channel within diamond distance 1/2 of conjugation by U? The oracle may depend on U but the circuit template may only depend on n.

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. A target-dependent Boolean oracle is claimed to let one uniformly generated polynomial quantum circuit approximate any $n$-qubit unitary channel to diamond error at most $1/2$.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
The source claims a polynomial $p$ and a deterministic classical circuit generator running in time $p(n)$ on $1^n$. Its circuit $A_n$ uses $H,T,T^\dagger,\mathrm{CNOT}$ and one Boolean oracle on $m(n)\le p(n)$ bits, with qubit, gate, and query counts each bounded by $p(n)$. For every $U\in\mathcal U(2^n)$, there exists a Boolean function $f$ such that \[ \|\Phi_{n,f}-\mathcal U\|_\diamond\le1/2, \qquad\mathcal U(\rho)=U\rho U^*. \] The oracle depends on the desired unitary, while the circuit generator is uniform and independent of it. The oracle acts by $O_f|x,b\rangle=|x,b\oplus f(x)\rangle$. To define $\Phi_{n,f}$, initialize all work qubits to zero, run $A_n$ using $O_f$, retain the designated $n$ output qubits, and take the partial trace over the other registers.

Outline of the argument

Controlled tasks and programmable slots build a contracting block. Fourier fields encode a smaller-unitary representation in Boolean queries, and the reduction assembles the approximation with the fixed elementary gate set; finding the oracle efficiently is outside the theorem.
