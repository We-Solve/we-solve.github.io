Algebraicity of Rigid Cocycle Values at Real Multiplication Points

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source uses the terms rigid meromorphic cocycle $J$, real-multiplication point $\tau$, and its value $J[\tau]$, but does not specify their underlying arithmetic and analytic framework. Write $H_\tau$ for the narrow ring class field attached to $\tau$, as designated by the source. The associated real quadratic field, order, and field embeddings are not specified. For subfields $K,L$ of a common ambient field, their compositum $KL$ is the smallest subfield containing both.

The supplied conjectural assertion is: for every rigid meromorphic cocycle $J$, there exists a finite extension $H_J/{\mathbb{Q}}$ such that \[ J[\tau]\in H_JH_\tau \qquad\text{for every real-multiplication point }\tau. \] The extension $H_J$ may depend on $J$, but must work simultaneously for every $\tau$. The assertion requires a common ambient field in which the values and the two number fields can be compared.

This is a qualified transcription rather than a complete intrinsic formulation: the source does not define the cocycle space, the domain of real-multiplication points, the evaluation and its normalization, or the order determining $H_\tau$. In particular, it does not explain whether evaluations are defined and finite at every point quantified over, or how poles and other undefined evaluations are handled. These omissions require mathematical review; no restriction of the stated universal quantifier is imposed here.

Short English statement

The conjecture asserts that each rigid meromorphic cocycle has a single finite number field extension which, when composed with the narrow ring class field of any real-multiplication point, contains its value there. The supplied record leaves the precise cocycle and evaluation framework unspecified.
