Concordance Rigidity of Braid-Positive Knots

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the formulation below, use the conventional smooth oriented knot setting: a knot is a smooth oriented embedded circle in the standard sphere $S^3$. The supplied source does not explicitly specify ambient-space or orientation conventions; these conventions require confirmation for the intended statement.

For $n\geq 1$, let $B_n$ denote the Artin braid group with standard generators $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$. A positive braid is a braid admitting a word using only these generators, with no inverse generators. A knot is braid-positive if it is isotopic to the closure of such a braid for some $n$; the closure must have exactly one component. Allowing the empty word includes the identity braid in $B_1$, whose closure is the unknot. The source does not explicitly address the empty-word convention.

Two oriented knots $K_0,K_1\subset S^3$ are smoothly concordant if there is a smooth properly embedded oriented annulus $A\subset S^3\times[0,1]$ with oriented boundary \[ \partial A=(-K_0)\times\{0\}\;\sqcup\;K_1\times\{1\}, \] where $-K_0$ denotes reversal of the knot orientation. They are isotopic if there is a smooth ambient isotopy $H_t:S^3\to S^3$, $0\leq t\leq1$, with $H_0=\operatorname{id}_{S^3}$ and $H_1(K_0)=K_1$, respecting knot orientations under the stated convention.

Question. For every pair of braid-positive knots $K_0,K_1\subset S^3$, does smooth concordance imply isotopy? Equivalently, under the conventions above, is \[ \forall K_0,K_1\text{ braid-positive},\qquad K_0\sim_{\mathrm{smooth\ concordance}}K_1 \ \Longrightarrow\ K_0\sim_{\mathrm{ambient\ isotopy}}K_1 \] true?

Supplied context and qualifications. The excerpt states that braid-positive knots are fibered and strongly quasipositive, and presents this question as a special case of Baker's conjecture. Those properties are supplied background, not additional restrictions on the quantified class. The excerpt singles out closures of positive braids in $B_3$ as a potentially accessible special case, citing classification of conjugacy classes of three-strand braids and earlier work. The target question nevertheless concerns positive braids on any number of strands. The excerpt also introduces $\upsilon(K)=\Upsilon_K(1)$, described there as a smooth concordance invariant from knot Floer homology defined by Ozsváth--Stipsicz--Szabó, and $g(K)$, the Seifert genus. Neither invariant appears in the target assertion. The excerpt announces a corollary about positive three-braid knots but does not supply its statement; no such corollary is incorporated here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether two knots that each arise as the closure of a positive braid must represent the same knot type whenever they cobound a smooth concordance annulus. It concerns all braid-positive knots, with positive three-braid knots highlighted only as a special case. The ambient-space, orientation, and empty-word conventions above are explicit provisional conventions requiring review. The supplied registry labels the question open; that status is not independently verified here.
