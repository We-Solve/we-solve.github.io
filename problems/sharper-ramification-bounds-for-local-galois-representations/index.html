Sharper Ramification Bounds for Local Galois Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Unresolved scope. The supplied record omits the hypotheses on the base field, the normalization of the valuation, the meaning of the different notation, the lattice used for reduction, and some parameter conventions. The following is a conditional reconstruction under explicit conventional choices; these choices require comparison with the cited report before this can be treated as its precise formulation.

Assume that $p>2$ is prime, that $K/{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ is a finite extension, and that $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K/K)$. Normalize $v_K$ by $v_K(K^\times)={\mathbb{Z}}$, and put $e=v_K(p)$. Assume that $n,r\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $n\geq1$ and $r\geq1$. Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional crystalline ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$-representation of $G_K$ whose Hodge--Tate weights lie in $[0,r]$, with the sign convention for these weights still to be checked against the source. Choose a $G_K$-stable ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-lattice $T\subset V$, and set \[ T_n=T/p^nT,\qquad \rho_n:G_K\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathbb{Z}}/p^n{\mathbb{Z}}}(T_n),\qquad L_n=\overline K^{\ker(\rho_n)}. \] Thus $L_n/K$ is a finite Galois extension. Interpret $\mathcal D_{L_n/K}$ as its different ideal. If $v_{L_n}$ is the integer-normalized valuation on $L_n$, use the convention \[ v_K(\mathcal D_{L_n/K}) =\frac{v_{L_n}(\mathcal D_{L_n/K})}{e(L_n/K)}, \] where $e(L_n/K)$ is the relative ramification index.

For this conditional reconstruction, interpret the source's $\mathbb N$ as ${\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$, and define $\alpha'$ and $\beta'$ by \[ \frac{r}{p-1}=p^{\alpha'}\beta',\qquad \alpha'\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\qquad p^{-1}<\beta'\leq1. \] The supplied proposed assertion is that, for every choice of the data satisfying these hypotheses, \[ v_K(\mathcal D_{L_n/K}) \leq 1+e\left(n+\alpha'+\beta'\right)-p^{-(n+\alpha')}. \] The original wording asks to replace $\alpha,\beta$ in the ramification bound of Theorem 2 by $\alpha',\beta'$ defined above and prove that the resulting bound remains valid. Theorem 2 is not reproduced in the supplied record, so its complete scope and its agreement with this reconstruction remain unresolved. In particular, positivity and integrality of $r$, the inclusion of zero in $\mathbb N$, and the treatment of weight bound $r=0$ are not supplied; the displayed decomposition cannot hold when $r=0$.

Short English statement

The proposed bound controls the different of the finite extension detected by a crystalline representation modulo $p^n$, using parameters determined by $r/(p-1)$. The displayed formulation uses explicit provisional local-field and lattice conventions because the supplied record omits them. The record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status or establish the inequality.
