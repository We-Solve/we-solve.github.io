Uniform gap transfer and an elementary irrational diagonal family

Definitions and mathematical statement

Conjecture 34 is attributed to Davenport--Lewis. The extracted sentence only assumes that $Q$ is not a multiple of a rational form; the supplied research aid recovers its intended setting as a nondegenerate positive-definite real quadratic form in $d\geq3$ variables. Under that explicit contextual qualification, write \[ Q(x)=x^{\mathsf T}Ax,\quad A=A^{\mathsf T}>0,\qquad A\ne cB\quad(c\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus\{0\},\ B\in\operatorname{Mat}_d({\mathbb{Q}})). \] Positive definiteness makes $V_Q=\{Q(x):x\in{\mathbb{Z}}^d\}$ locally finite and unbounded. Enumerate its distinct values as $0=\lambda_0<\lambda_1<\cdots$. The conjecture asserts \[ \lim_{j\to\infty}(\lambda_{j+1}-\lambda_j)=0. \] Equivalently, for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $T$ such that every interval $(t,t+\varepsilon]$, $t\geq T$, contains a represented value. Multiplicities of representations do not create additional consecutive values.

The aid reports a counting-function reduction, rather than a solution in the unresolved dimensions. If $N(t)$ is a locally finite positive weighted count whose jumps are supported exactly at the represented values, $A_0>0$, and $\beta\geq1$, then either of the hypotheses \[ N(t)=A_0t^\beta+o(t^{\beta-1}),\qquad\hbox{or}\qquad N(t)=A_0t^\beta+O(t^{\beta-1-\delta})\quad(\delta>0) \] forces vanishing tail gaps; the second gives a uniform bound of order $T^{-\delta}$ on gaps starting beyond $T$. More precisely, for a suitable constant $L>0$ and sufficiently large $T$, every $(x,x+LT^{-\delta}]$ with $x\geq T$ contains a represented value. This applies conditionally to the distinct-value count $\#(V_Q\cap[0,t])$, or to the multiplicity-weighted ellipsoid count $\#\{x\in{\mathbb{Z}}^d:Q(x)\leq t\}$, with the respective asymptotic constants and exponents; the needed error bounds are hypotheses, not established here for all $Q$.

The aid additionally reports an elementary shrinking-gap argument for \[ Q(x)=a x_1^2+b(x_2^2+x_3^2+x_4^2+x_5^2) +R(x_6,\ldots,x_d), \quad a,b>0,\quad a/b\notin{\mathbb{Q}}, \] where $d\geq5$ and $R$ is a positive-definite extra block, omitted for $d=5$. It describes a Weyl-equidistribution and four-squares proof but supplies no full proof in this record. Its literature summary says the general conjecture is known for $d\geq5$ and apparently open for $d=3,4$. These are supplied, unverified literature qualifications, not an independent current-status assessment.

Short English statement

For an irrational positive-definite quadratic form in at least three variables, do successive distinct values on the integer lattice become arbitrarily close uniformly in the tail? The supplied aid reports the general result in dimensions at least five, unresolved cases in dimensions three and four, and conditional counting-error criteria that force shrinking gaps.
