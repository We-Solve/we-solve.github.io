10 Lectures and 42 Open Problems — Deterministic Restricted Isometry Property matrices

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $A\in\mathbb C^{M\times N}$ and an integer $s\ge1$, define the restricted isometry constant \[ \delta_s(A)=\sup_{\substack{x\ne0\\|\operatorname{supp}(x)|\le s}} \left|\frac{\|Ax\|_2^2}{\|x\|_2^2}-1\right|. \] Here $\operatorname{supp}(x)=\{j:x_j\ne0\}$; the same definition applies to real matrices and vectors. Thus $(s,1/3)$-RIP means $(2/3)\|x\|_2^2\le\|Ax\|_2^2\le(4/3)\|x\|_2^2$ simultaneously for every vector supported on at most $s$ coordinates.

Construct explicit deterministic rectangular matrices, with $M<N$, that achieve the source's target scale \[ s\ \gtrsim\ \frac{M^{3/5}}{(\log N)^C} \] for an absolute constant $C$, in nonvacuous growing parameter ranges. The hidden positive constant is independent of $M,N$. The requested construction should produce the matrix in polynomial time in its output dimensions, with a specified exact or adequately controlled finite-precision representation. Its guarantee must hold for its actual output, without an unverified random choice.

The interesting ranges have the displayed target substantially larger than $\sqrt M$; otherwise this does not break the usual coherence barrier. An identity matrix with $M=N$ therefore does not answer the question. Likewise, a construction good for one specified sparse vector is insufficient: the same matrix must preserve all sparse vectors of the required order. State explicitly which pairs $(M,N)$ the construction supports and any padding needed for other dimensions.

Short English statement

Construct explicit deterministic restricted-isometry matrices with sparsity of order M to the three-fifths power, up to logarithmic factors.
