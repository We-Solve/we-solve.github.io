Question 5.18 — For which k≥ 1 is it true that Aut(Ig(k)) = Mod ± g?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Mod}_g^{\pm}$ be the extended mapping class group, including orientation-reversing classes, and let $H_k=\mathcal I_g(k)$ be the source's filtration subgroup for $k\ge1$. Its exact definition and genus hypotheses are not provided. The summary calls these Johnson-filtration terms, while the closing sentence refers to a lower central series; those filtrations are not silently identified.

The automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(H_k)$ consists of all abstract automorphisms of $H_k$. Its abstract commensurator $\operatorname{Comm}(H_k)$ consists of isomorphisms between finite-index subgroups, identified when they agree on a further finite-index subgroup. For a filtration invariant under $\operatorname{Mod}_g^{\pm}$, conjugation gives natural maps \[ \operatorname{Mod}_g^{\pm}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(H_k) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{Mod}_g^{\pm}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Comm}(H_k). \] Question 5.18 asks for which $k\ge1$ the source's equalities \[ \operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal I_g(k))=\operatorname{Mod}_g^{\pm}, \qquad \operatorname{Comm}(\mathcal I_g(k))=\operatorname{Mod}_g^{\pm} \] hold. A usual rigidity interpretation is that the respective natural conjugation maps are isomorphisms; literal set equality is not intended. The source does not explicitly distinguish that interpretation from abstract group isomorphism.

The source says Theorem 5.17 answers the questions for $k=1,2$, but the theorem's statement and hypotheses are missing, so they are not reconstructed here. Its motivation is recovery of the ambient mapping class group from deeper subgroups. The summary reports known Torelli/filtration automorphism results, mentioning genus at least $3$, but no all-$k$ result or complete references. The two requested rigidity properties are retained separately.

Short English statement

Determine which filtration terms have full automorphism group and abstract commensurator equal, in the intended natural sense, to the extended mapping class group. The source cites answers at the first two terms, but the exact filtration and genus hypotheses are missing.
