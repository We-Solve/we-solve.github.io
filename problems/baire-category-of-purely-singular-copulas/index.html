Math-6736

Baire Category of Purely Singular Copulas

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $d$, let $\lambda_d$ denote Lebesgue measure on $[0,1]^d$. A $d$-dimensional copula is a function $C:[0,1]^d\to[0,1]$ of the form \[ C(u_1,\ldots,u_d)=\mu_C\!\left(\prod_{j=1}^d[0,u_j]\right), \] where $\mu_C$ is a Borel probability measure on $[0,1]^d$ whose every one-dimensional marginal is uniform on $[0,1]$. In particular, $C(u)=0$ whenever a coordinate of $u$ is zero, and $C(1,\ldots,1,t,1,\ldots,1)=t$ for every coordinate position and $t\in[0,1]$. Write $\mathcal C_d$ for the set of these copulas. A copula is purely singular if $\mu_C\perp\lambda_d$, meaning that there is a Borel set $N\subseteq[0,1]^d$ with $\lambda_d(N)=0$ and $\mu_C(N)=1$. Set \[ \mathcal S_d=\{C\in\mathcal C_d:\mu_C\perp\lambda_d\}. \] The supplied expanded statement defines \[ D_1(C_1,C_2)=\int_{[0,1]^d}|C_1(u)-C_2(u)|\,\mathrm du. \] A subset of $\mathcal C_d$ is meager in $(\mathcal C_d,D_1)$ if it is a countable union of sets whose closures have empty interior in that ambient metric space. A nonmeager subset is said to be of second category.

Question as expanded in the supplied record. Is $\mathcal S_d$ nonmeager in $(\mathcal C_d,D_1)$? Equivalently, is it impossible to write $\mathcal S_d$ as a countable union of nowhere dense subsets of this ambient space?

Unresolved qualifications. The original quotation asks whether purely singular copulas are of second category in $(\mathcal C_d,D_1)$, but does not itself define $D_1$. The integral formula above comes from the supplied expanded statement; its agreement with the metric intended in the cited report requires verification. The accompanying literature assessment distinguishes a uniform metric from $D_1$, so the intended metric and that assessment must be reconciled before this draft is treated as a faithful source formalization. The record also does not specify the intended range or quantification of $d$; the question is therefore displayed with $d$ as a parameter, without asserting that the original problem concerns every positive integer or that it excludes $d=1$.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether purely singular copulas form a nonmeager subset of the space of copulas under the displayed integral distance. Pure singularity concerns the associated probability measure. The intended metric and dimension range require source verification. The record labels the problem open; that label and its dated literature assessment are supplied curation, not independently verified conclusions.
