Math-3793

Numerical Criterion for Deformed Hermitian–Yang–Mills Metrics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension $n$, let $\omega$ be its Kähler form, and let $L\to X$ be a holomorphic line bundle. Its Chern character is $\operatorname{ch}(L)=\exp(c_1(L))$. For an irreducible holomorphic subvariety $V\subseteq X$ of complex dimension $d$, define \[ Z_V(L,\omega)=\int_{[V]}e^{-i[\omega]}\operatorname{ch}(L) =\frac{1}{d!}\int_{[V]}\left(c_1(L)-i[\omega]\right)^d. \] Here integration means pairing the degree-$2d$ component with the fundamental cycle of $V$, including when $V$ is singular.

For clarity about the named differential equation, one conventional normalization associates to a smooth Hermitian metric $h$ on $L$ the real curvature form $\alpha_h=\frac{i}{2\pi}F_h$, representing $c_1(L)$. With a constant phase $\widehat\theta\in{\mathbb{R}}$, the deformed Hermitian--Yang--Mills equation in this normalization is \[ \operatorname{Im}\!\left(e^{-i\widehat\theta}(\omega+i\alpha_h)^n\right)=0, \qquad \operatorname{Re}\!\left(e^{-i\widehat\theta}(\omega+i\alpha_h)^n\right)>0, \] where positivity means positivity as a real top-degree form. The supplied record does not specify its curvature normalization, prescribed phase or lift of the phase, or any additional admissibility condition; this displayed convention does not resolve those omissions.

The supplied question asks whether \[ \text{the deformed Hermitian--Yang--Mills equation on }L\text{ admits a solution} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Arg}Z_V(L,\omega)>-\operatorname{Arg}Z_X(L,\omega) \quad\text{for every irreducible holomorphic subvariety }V\subset X. \] This is the recorded target, subject to unresolved qualifications: a real-valued branch or compatible lifts of the arguments must be specified, and each argument requires its central charge to be nonzero. The source also leaves unclear whether $V$ must be proper, whether zero-dimensional subvarieties are included, and whether restrictions on dimension, phase, or positivity are intended. An ordered inequality between arguments is not defined solely by their values modulo $2\pi$. Consequently, the supplied formulation does not determine a unique precise conjecture without these choices; no missing hypothesis or branch is imposed here.

Short English statement

The recorded question asks whether existence of a deformed Hermitian--Yang--Mills metric on a holomorphic line bundle over a compact Kähler manifold is equivalent to the stated argument inequality for every irreducible holomorphic subvariety. The phase convention, argument branches, nonvanishing requirements, and range of subvarieties need clarification before this becomes an unambiguous mathematical assertion.
