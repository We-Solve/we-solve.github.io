Math-3874

Convergence of Multiplicative Polynomial Averages

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. A function $f:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is bounded if there exists $B\in[0,\infty)$ such that $|f(k)|\le B$ for every $k\in{\mathbb{N}}$, and completely multiplicative if \[ f(ab)=f(a)f(b)\qquad\text{for all }a,b\in{\mathbb{N}}. \] No additional normalization of $f(1)$ is specified in the supplied statement.

Let $P\in{\mathbb{Z}}[x,y]$ be homogeneous: there is an integer $d\ge0$ such that every monomial of $P$ with nonzero coefficient has total degree $d$. Assume \[ P(m,n)>0\qquad\text{for every }(m,n)\in{\mathbb{N}}^2. \] Thus every value $P(m,n)$ appearing below belongs to the domain of $f$. Positivity is required on positive integer pairs; no condition on the coordinate axes is stated. The source specifies no restriction on the degree beyond homogeneity.

Question. For every bounded completely multiplicative function $f:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{R}}$ and every homogeneous polynomial $P\in{\mathbb{Z}}[x,y]$ satisfying the stated positivity condition, does there exist $L=L(f,P)\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that \[ \lim_{\substack{N\to\infty\\N\in{\mathbb{N}}}} \frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{m=1}^{N}\sum_{n=1}^{N}f\left(P(m,n)\right)=L? \]

Short English statement

For every bounded real completely multiplicative function $f$ and every homogeneous integer polynomial $P$ that is positive on positive integer pairs, must the average of $f(P(m,n))$ over $1\le m,n\le N$ converge as $N\to\infty$? The supplied record labels this question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
