Math-3631

Realizability and Consequences of Graph Profiles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not define the graph category, the sampling convention for the profile, or whether realizability is exact or asymptotic. The following is therefore a conditional formulation under the finite, simple, undirected graph interpretation with uniformly sampled induced subgraphs; these conventions require confirmation from the source.

Let $k\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $\mathcal H_k$ denote the set of isomorphism types of finite simple undirected graphs on $k$ vertices. For a finite simple undirected graph $G$ with $n=|V(G)|\geq k$, define its induced $k$-profile by \[ \pi_k(G)(H)=\frac{\left|\{S\subseteq V(G): |S|=k,\ G[S]\cong H\}\right|}{\binom{n}{k}},\qquad H\in\mathcal H_k, \] where $G[S]$ is the subgraph induced by $S$, and $G[S]\cong H$ means that it belongs to the isomorphism type $H$. Thus $\pi_k(G)$ is a probability distribution on $\mathcal H_k$.

Under these conventions, the first question is to characterize, for each $k\geq 1$, the set \[ \mathcal R_k=\left\{p:\mathcal H_k\to[0,1]:\ \sum_{H\in\mathcal H_k}p(H)=1,\ \exists\,G\text{ finite simple undirected},\ |V(G)|\geq k,\ \pi_k(G)=p\right\}. \] The second question asks which global properties of $G$ can be deduced from $\pi_k(G)$. For a specified graph property $\mathcal P$ and a realizable profile $p$, a conclusion that $G$ has $\mathcal P$ would require \[ \forall\,G\text{ finite simple undirected with }|V(G)|\geq k,\qquad \pi_k(G)=p\ \Longrightarrow\ G\in\mathcal P. \] The source does not specify the global properties to be considered, whether the order of $G$ is known, or any intended quantitative conclusions. It also does not distinguish the exact finite realizability question above from realizability by limits of profiles along graph sequences. Those alternatives are unresolved qualifications, and this draft does not identify them with the displayed formulation.

Short English statement

Conditionally interpreting the profile as the distribution of induced subgraphs on a uniformly chosen $k$-vertex subset, characterize the distributions realized by finite graphs and the global graph properties they force. The graph conventions, exact versus limiting interpretation, and intended global conclusions need source confirmation.
