Bogomolov-Pop pro-ell reconstruction

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 009; 2026-09-23), the asserted result is that for function fields of dimension at least two with algebraically closed constant fields, if $\ell$ is unequal to the characteristic, its pro-$\ell$ abelian-by-central quotient determines both the constant field and the function field's perfect closure.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For a fixed prime $\ell$ and fields of characteristic different from $\ell$, let $G_F^{(\ell)}$ be the maximal pro-$\ell$ absolute Galois quotient, $\Pi_F^a=G_F^{(\ell)}/[G_F^{(\ell)},G_F^{(\ell)}]$ and $\Pi_F^c=G_F^{(\ell)}/[[G_F^{(\ell)},G_F^{(\ell)}],G_F^{(\ell)}]$, using closed commutator subgroups. A continuous $\mathbb Z_{\ell}$-linear isomorphism of abelian quotients is compatible when it carries the induced alternating commutator pairing to the other pairing through an isomorphism of central kernels. For function fields $K/k,L/l$ over algebraically closed constants with transcendence degrees at least two, the claimed bijection is \[ \operatorname{Isom}_{\rm F}^{i}(K,L)\xrightarrow{\sim} \operatorname{Isom}^{c}(\Pi_L^a,\Pi_K^a)/\mathbb Z_{\ell}^{\times}. \] The left uses perfect-closure field isomorphisms carrying constants onto constants, modulo integral Frobenius powers in positive characteristic; the right identifies compatible maps up to unit scaling. This includes transcendence degree two and $\ell=2$.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 009; 2026-09-23), commutator relations detect valuations through alternating characters. Kummer tests and uniformly bounded pencils identify curve subfields and rational quotients, from which incidence reconstruction yields the field with exactly the scalar and Frobenius ambiguities.
