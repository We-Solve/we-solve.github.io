Spherical Hecke Modules on Homogeneous Schemes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field and $\mathcal O_F$ its ring of integers. The source considers a homogeneous spherical $G$-scheme $X$ over $\mathcal O_F$. Write $G_F$ and $X_F$ for their generic fibres. In the usual reductive setting, geometric homogeneity means that $G_{\overline F}$ acts transitively on $X_{\overline F}$, and sphericity means that a Borel subgroup of the connected reductive group $G_{\overline F}$ has an open orbit on $X_{\overline F}$. These generic-fibre conditions explain the terminology; the source does not specify the required integral-model conditions or the precise hypotheses on $G$.

To make the requested algebra and module explicit, the following is a conditional formulation under the conventional interpretation using $F$-points and complex-valued functions. Set $L=G_F(F)$ and $Y=X_F(F)$, and let $K\subseteq L$ be a compact open subgroup. Normalize a left Haar measure ${\,\mathrm{d}} g$ on $L$ by $\operatorname{vol}(K)=1$. Define \[ \mathcal H(L,K)=\{h\in C_c^\infty(L,{\mathbb{C}}):h(k_1gk_2)=h(g)\text{ for all }g\in L,\ k_1,k_2\in K\}, \] where $C_c^\infty$ denotes locally constant, compactly supported functions. Its multiplication is convolution: \[ (h_1*h_2)(g)=\int_L h_1(a)h_2(a^{-1}g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} a. \] For a left action of $L$ on $Y$, define \[ M(Y,K)=\{f\in C_c^\infty(Y,{\mathbb{C}}):f(k^{-1}y)=f(y)\text{ for all }k\in K,\ y\in Y\}. \] The associated left Hecke action is \[ (h\cdot f)(y)=\int_L h(g)f(g^{-1}y)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} g. \] These formulas specify conventions for the objects to be described; they do not supply the requested structural description.

Problem. For every homogeneous spherical $G$-scheme $X$ over $\mathcal O_F$ within the source's intended class, describe the spherical Hecke algebra $\mathcal H(G,K)$ and the module structure of $C_c^\infty(X)^K$ over that algebra. Under the conditional conventions above, this asks for a structural description of $\mathcal H(L,K)$ together with its action on $M(Y,K)$.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not identify $K$, the coefficient field, the point space denoted by $X$ in the function notation, or the exact reductive and integral hypotheses. In particular, it does not state whether $K=G(\mathcal O_F)$, whether this subgroup is hyperspecial, or whether splitness or unramifiedness is required. The conditional formulation therefore requires confirmation before it can be treated as an exact formulation of the source problem. The supplied literature assessment mentions a uniform basis, whereas the problem statement itself requests a module description without prescribing a basis.

Short English statement

For a homogeneous spherical scheme over the ring of integers of a nonarchimedean local field, describe the spherical Hecke algebra and its action on the $K$-invariant, locally constant, compactly supported functions. The intended subgroup $K$, function space, coefficients, and integral hypotheses need confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this is a dated curation claim, not an independently verified conclusion.
