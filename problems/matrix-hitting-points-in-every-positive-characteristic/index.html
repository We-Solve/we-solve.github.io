Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given a promised prime p in binary and n,s in unary, can one construct in polynomial bit time an n-tuple of d×d matrices over $F_p$, $d=O(n^{3}s^{6})$, on which every nonzero free noncommutative polynomial formula of size $\leq s$ over every field of characteristic p evaluates nonzero?

Matrix hitting points in every positive characteristic

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 116; 2026-10-04), the asserted result is that every positive characteristic has one polynomial-size prime-field matrix tuple detecting all nonzero bounded-size division-free noncommutative formulas, including arbitrary extension coefficients.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
A prime $p$ is supplied in binary, with primality promised, and positive integers $n,s$ in unary. The claim is a uniform deterministic algorithm producing $d=O(n^3s^6)$ and matrices $T_1,\ldots,T_n\in\operatorname{Mat}_d(\mathbb F_p)$ such that \[ \forall F\text{ with }\operatorname{char}F=p\ \forall 0\ne f\in F\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle: \quad\operatorname{formula\ size}(f)\le s\ \Longrightarrow\ f(T_1,\ldots,T_n)\ne0. \] The matrices are viewed over $F$ through its prime subfield, so one tuple hits formulas with arbitrary extension-field coefficients. With $L=\lceil\log_2p\rceil$, its total bit time and dense binary output length are at most \[ C(n+s+L+1)^k \] for absolute positive integers $C,k$; entries are ordinary residues from $0$ to $p-1$.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 116; 2026-10-04), a multiplicative-shift system faithfully encodes word series. Derivative-row and minor arguments bound the first nonzero scalar coefficient, so truncating the word operators yields a uniform finite matrix construction without differentiation's characteristic obstruction.
