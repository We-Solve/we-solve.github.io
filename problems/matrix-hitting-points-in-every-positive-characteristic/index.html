Matrix hitting points in every positive characteristic

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given a promised prime p in binary and n,s in unary, can one construct in polynomial bit time an n-tuple of d×d matrices over $F_p$, $d=O(n^{3}s^{6})$, on which every nonzero free noncommutative polynomial formula of size $\leq s$ over every field of characteristic p evaluates nonzero?

Short English statement

Given a promised prime p in binary and n,s in unary, can one construct in polynomial bit time an n-tuple of d×d matrices over $F_p$, $d=O(n^{3}s^{6})$, on which every nonzero free noncommutative polynomial formula of size $\leq s$ over every field of characteristic p evaluates nonzero?
