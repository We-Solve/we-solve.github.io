Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2025) — Conjecture 2

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each prime $p\equiv 1\pmod 4$, let ${\mathbb{F}}_p={\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$ and let $Q_p=\{a^2:a\in{\mathbb{F}}_p\setminus\{0\}\}$. The Paley graph $G_p$ is the simple undirected graph with vertex set ${\mathbb{F}}_p$, in which distinct vertices $x,y$ are adjacent exactly when $x-y\in Q_p$. Its 1-localization at $0$ is the induced graph \[ G_{p,1}=G_p[Q_p]. \] For a finite simple graph $H$, write $\overline H$ for its complement and $\omega(H)$ for its clique number. Use the Lovász theta convention \[ \vartheta(H)=\max\left\{\sum_{u,v\in V(H)}X_{uv}: X\in{\mathbb{R}}^{V(H)\times V(H)},\ X=X^{\mathsf T},\ X\succeq0,\ \operatorname{tr}(X)=1,\ X_{uv}=0\ \text{whenever }\{u,v\}\in E(H)\right\}. \] Conjecture 2 (Bandeira et al., as attributed in the supplied record). As $p$ tends to infinity through primes congruent to $1$ modulo $4$, \[ \vartheta\!\left(\overline{G_{p,1}}\right)\sim\sqrt{\frac p2}, \qquad\text{that is,}\qquad \lim_{\substack{p\to\infty\\p\text{ prime},\ p\equiv1\pmod4}} \frac{\vartheta(\overline{G_{p,1}})}{\sqrt{p/2}}=1. \] The supplied background states that localization at any other vertex gives an isomorphic graph, cites this fact to [Kunisky-2023-SpectralPseudorandomnessPaleyClique], and displays \[ \omega(G_p)\leq\omega(G_{p,1})+1\leq\vartheta(\overline{G_p})+1. \] The last theta term in that displayed background concerns the full graph $G_p$; the conjectured asymptotic concerns the localized graph $G_{p,1}$. These are retained as distinct expressions. The background attributes empirical evidence for the conjectured asymptotic to [MMP-2019-LPCliquesPaley], says that it would recover the Hanson--Petridis bound, and states that $G_{p,1}$ is circulant, so the corresponding theta semidefinite program reduces to a linear program. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified assertions here; the precise Hanson--Petridis bound and the linear-program formulation are not supplied.

Short English statement

For primes $p\equiv1\pmod4$, restrict the Paley graph to the neighbors of $0$. The conjecture predicts that the Lovász theta number of the complement of this induced graph, divided by $\sqrt{p/2}$, tends to $1$. The registry labels the problem open; this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
