Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.82

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite group $G$, let $F(G)$ be its largest nilpotent normal subgroup. A component of $G$ is a subnormal quasisimple subgroup, where a group $L$ is quasisimple if $L=[L,L]$ and $L/Z(L)$ is nonabelian simple. Here subnormal means connected to $G$ by a finite chain of normal inclusions. Let $E(G)$ be the subgroup generated by all components of $G$. The generalized Fitting subgroup is \[ F^*(G)=F(G)E(G). \] Define $F^*_0(G)=1$, and, for each integer $i\geq 0$, let $\pi_i:G\to G/F^*_i(G)$ be the quotient map and set \[ F^*_{i+1}(G)=\pi_i^{-1}\left(F^*(G/F^*_i(G))\right). \] The generalized Fitting height is \[ h^*(G)=\min\{k\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}:F^*_k(G)=G\}. \] A group $K$ is soluble if its derived series, defined by $K^{(0)}=K$ and $K^{(j+1)}=[K^{(j)},K^{(j)}]$, reaches the identity subgroup. For a finite soluble group $K$, define $F_0(K)=1$ and let $F_{i+1}(K)$ be the inverse image of $F(K/F_i(K))$ under the quotient map. Its Fitting height is \[ h(K)=\min\{k\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}:F_k(K)=K\}. \] For soluble $G$, these heights satisfy $h^*(G)=h(G)$.

The question proposed by P. Shumyatsky is whether \[ \forall\,G\text{ finite},\qquad \exists\,K\leq G\quad\text{such that }K\text{ is soluble and }h(K)=h^*(G). \] The supplied source starts its generalized Fitting series at $F^*_1(G)=F^*(G)$ and does not explicitly specify the height of the trivial group. The definitions above explicitly adopt the convention $h^*(1)=h(1)=0$; with this convention the trivial-group instance is satisfied by $K=1$.

Short English statement

Does every finite group have a soluble subgroup whose Fitting height equals the generalized Fitting height of the whole group? The supplied record labels this question open; that label is not independently verified here.
