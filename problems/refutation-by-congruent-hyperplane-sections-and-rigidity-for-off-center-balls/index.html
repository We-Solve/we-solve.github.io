Math-1393

Refutation by congruent hyperplane sections and rigidity for off-center balls

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix integers $n\geq3$ and $2\leq k\leq n-1$, and let $G(n,k)$ denote the Grassmannian of $k$-dimensional linear subspaces of ${\mathbb{R}}^n$. A star body about the origin is a compact set $K=\{ru:u\in S^{n-1},\ 0\leq r\leq\rho_K(u)\}$ with continuous positive radial function $\rho_K$. The source asks whether, for all such $K,L$, \[ \left[\forall S\in G(n,k)\ \exists U_S\in O(S),\ t_S\in S: U_S(K\cap S)+t_S=L\cap S\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ K=L\text{ or }K=-L. \] The motions can depend independently on $S$; congruence permits both reflections and translations. The source separately reports an affirmative result for $k=2$ when congruence is replaced by origin-fixing plane rotations, citing [R].

The attached aid attributes to Ning Zhang (2018) convex-body counterexamples in every $n\geq3$ for the hyperplane case $k=n-1$, even with $t_S=0$. It reports that direct origin-fixing rotations suffice for these hyperplane counterexamples when $n\geq4$. This refutes the unrestricted general assertion, not every fixed-$k$ variant; in particular it does not contradict the reported plane-rotation result.

The aid's positive special case is precise for Euclidean balls. Let $K=B(c,R)$ and $L=B(d,Q)$, where $R,Q>0$, $\|c\|<R$, and $\|d\|<Q$. For every allowed $n,k$, congruence of their central $k$-sections under arbitrary independent rigid motions implies $R=Q$ and $d=\pm c$, hence $K=\pm L$. For orthogonal projection $P_S$, the squared section radii are \[ R^2-\|c\|^2+\|P_Sc\|^2,\qquad Q^2-\|d\|^2+\|P_Sd\|^2. \] The supplied algebraic reduction says their equality for all $S$ forces $cc^{\mathsf T}-dd^{\mathsf T}$ to be scalar; its rank at most two and $n\geq3$ force that scalar to vanish. This restricted rigidity statement does not establish rigidity for general star bodies. The counterexample attribution and ball claim require independent verification.

Short English statement

Do congruent central sections determine star bodies up to reflection through the origin? The supplied aid reports hyperplane-section counterexamples, distinguishes the positive plane-rotation result, and gives rigidity for balls even when the section motions include translations.
