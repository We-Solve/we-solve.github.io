Math-7364

Law-of-Iterated-Logarithm Bounds for Dyadic Martingales

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $I_k(x)$ denote the nested dyadic intervals containing $x$, with $|I_k(x)|=2^{-k}$. For example, the standard half-open dyadic grid gives \[ I_k(x)=\left[2^{-k}\lfloor 2^kx\rfloor,\,2^{-k}(\lfloor 2^kx\rfloor+1)\right). \] This specifies a conventional interpretation of the interval notation; the supplied record does not specify a grid or endpoint convention.

For a function $f$ integrable on an interval $I$, its interval mean is \[ f_I=\frac{1}{|I|}\int_I f(t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] For real-valued $f$ with these means defined, set \[ d_k(x)=f_{I_{k+1}(x)}-f_{I_k(x)},\qquad Q_n(x)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}d_k(x)^2\in[0,+\infty]. \] Real-valuedness makes this series nonnegative. The source does not specify the scalar field or the admissible function class; real-valued local integrability is an interpretation sufficient to define these means, not a confirmed complete hypothesis of the intended problem.

The supplied question asks whether, for every function in the intended but unspecified admissible class, \[ \limsup_{n\to\infty} \frac{\left|f_{I_n(x)}-f(x)\right|} {\sqrt{Q_n(x)\,\left|\log\left|\log Q_n(x)\right|\right|}} <+\infty \qquad\text{for Lebesgue-almost every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}. \] Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm. Finiteness is pointwise almost everywhere; the displayed assertion does not require a common bound independent of $x$ or $f$.

Unresolved qualifications. The record supplies neither the function class nor conventions making the quotient meaningful at all relevant indices. In particular, the expression is undefined at $Q_n(x)=0$, $Q_n(x)=1$, or $Q_n(x)=+\infty$, and its denominator vanishes at $Q_n(x)=e^{-1}$ or $Q_n(x)=e$. A condition such as $0<Q_n(x)<e^{-1}$ eventually would make the displayed quotient well-defined, but is not supplied and is not imposed here. The case of eventually vanishing square-function tails requires an explicit convention or a separate formulation. Consequently, this is a statement template preserving the supplied question, pending recovery of its missing hypotheses and boundary conventions.

Short English statement

The question attributed in the supplied curation to Nicolau asks whether the error between a function and its dyadic interval means has a finite almost-everywhere limsup after normalization by its remaining dyadic square function and an iterated logarithm. The function class and conventions for degenerate square-function tails are missing, so the intended assertion requires clarification.
