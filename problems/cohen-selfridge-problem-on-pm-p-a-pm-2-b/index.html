Cohen-Selfridge Problem on $\pm p^a \pm 2^b$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The requested object is the least positive odd integer not expressible as $\pm p^a\pm2^b$, with $p$ an odd prime. The record does not state the allowed exponent sets. To expose this missing datum, let $E_a$ and $E_b$ denote chosen sets of allowed exponents. Under the usual independent-sign reading, define \[\mathcal R(E_a,E_b)=\{\epsilon_1p^a+\epsilon_2 2^b:p\text{ is an odd prime},\ a\in E_a,\ b\in E_b,\ \epsilon_1,\epsilon_2\in\{-1,1\}\}.\] The target, once conventions have been fixed, is \[\min\left(\{1,3,5,\ldots\}\setminus\mathcal R(E_a,E_b)\right),\] provided this set is nonempty. In particular, allowing exponent zero, requiring strictly positive exponents, or tying the two signs together can change the representable set. Neither an exponent convention nor a different sign convention is silently selected here. The question requires excluding every allowed representation of a candidate and verifying that each smaller positive odd integer has an allowed representation.

The supplied background attributes to Cohen and Selfridge the observation that the requested number exceeds $2^{18}$, but does not supply its exact exponent/sign conventions. The literature triage checked 2026-08-17 reports infinitely many nonrepresentable odd integers in related forms, including an arithmetic progression that is neither a sum nor a difference of a power of two and a prime, and later covering-congruence constructions. Such related forms must be distinguished from the full displayed expression allowing $p^a$: the review explicitly says the least number for this exact record was not verified and requests a dedicated formulation and computational/status audit. The registry labels the problem open; the dated review labels it partially solved / PARTIAL-PROGRESS. No exact least value or exact-form existence result is inferred from the related constructions.

Short English statement

Find the first positive odd integer that cannot be written as a signed sum of an odd-prime power and a power of two. The exponent ranges and sign conventions must be fixed before identifying the first exception; the supplied results concern related nonrepresentation problems.
