Math-4351

Superexpanders and Separation of L-p from Hilbert Embeddability

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a metric space $(X,d)$, write \[ B_X(x,R)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)\le R\}. \] The cardinality convention for bounded geometry is \[ \forall R>0\quad \sup_{x\in X}|B_X(x,R)|<\infty. \] Some conventions additionally require uniform discreteness, meaning that there exists $\delta>0$ such that $d(x,y)\ge\delta$ whenever $x\ne y$. The supplied record does not specify whether this additional condition is intended.

For $2<p<\infty$, let $\ell_p$ be the space of scalar sequences $a=(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ satisfying $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|^p<\infty$, equipped with the metric induced by \[ \|a\|_p=\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|^p\right)^{1/p}. \] The scalar field may be ${\mathbb{R}}$ or ${\mathbb{C}}$; the source does not specify one. A coarse embedding of a metric space $(X,d_X)$ into a metric space $(Y,d_Y)$ is a map $f:X\to Y$ for which there exist nondecreasing functions $\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ such that \[ \lim_{t\to\infty}\rho_-(t)=\infty, \qquad \rho_-(d_X(x,y))\le d_Y(f(x),f(y))\le\rho_+(d_X(x,y)) \quad(x,y\in X). \]

Question under the coarse-embedding interpretation. Do there exist a real number $p$ with $2<p<\infty$ and a metric space $X$ of bounded geometry such that $X$ admits a coarse embedding into $\ell_p$, while for every Hilbert space $H$, equipped with its norm metric, there is no coarse embedding $X\to H$?

The word "embeds" is unqualified in the supplied statement. The coarse-embedding interpretation is suggested by the report topic and supplied literature links, but is not explicitly confirmed by the record. Consequently, the displayed question is a conditional formalization, pending confirmation of both the embedding notion and the bounded-geometry convention. The corrected source statement retains only this separation question; the original additional question about whether all expander families are super-expanders was removed as a duplicate of OWR-16160-001.

Short English statement

Under the suggested coarse-embedding interpretation, the question asks whether some bounded-geometry metric space coarsely embeds into $\ell_p$ for at least one $2<p<\infty$, yet coarsely embeds into no Hilbert space. The embedding notion and any uniform-discreteness requirement need confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
