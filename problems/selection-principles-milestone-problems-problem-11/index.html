Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. For $f,g\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}$, write $f\leq^{*}g$ if $f(n)\leq g(n)$ for all but finitely many $n$. The bounding and dominating numbers are \[ \mathfrak b=\min\left\{|B|:B\subseteq{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}},\ (\forall g\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}})(\exists f\in B)\ f\not\leq^{*}g\right\}, \] \[ \mathfrak d=\min\left\{|D|:D\subseteq{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}},\ (\forall f\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}})(\exists g\in D)\ f\leq^{*}g\right\}. \] For an infinite cardinal $\kappa$, $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ denotes its cofinality.

The supplied excerpt does not specify the underlying class of spaces, the ambient space for unions, or the conventions for covers. The following is a conventional countable-open-cover expansion of its notation, conditional on those conventions being the intended ones. For a topological space $X$, let $\mathcal O(X)$ consist of countable families $\mathcal U$ of open proper subsets of $X$ such that $\bigcup\mathcal U=X$. Let $\Gamma(X)\subseteq\mathcal O(X)$ consist of the infinite covers $\mathcal U$ satisfying \[ (\forall x\in X)\quad \left|\{U\in\mathcal U:x\notin U\}\right|<\aleph_0. \] The property $\mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\Gamma)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ with $\mathcal U_n\in\Gamma(X)$, there are choices $U_n\in\mathcal U_n$ such that the family $\{U_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\}$ belongs to $\Gamma(X)$. In particular, the resulting family must be infinite.

For the background assertion, $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\mathcal O)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ of members of $\Gamma(X)$, each having no finite subcover, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that \[ \left\{\bigcup\mathcal F_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\right\}\in\mathcal O(X). \]

For a property $P$ of admissible subspaces of a common ambient topological space, its additivity is conventionally \[ \mathsf{add}(P)=\min\left\{|\mathcal F|:(\forall X\in\mathcal F)\ X\text{ has }P,\quad \bigcup\mathcal F\text{ does not have }P\right\}, \] where the members and their union carry the relevant subspace topologies, and the minimum is taken over the intended admissible families. This expression presupposes that a witnessing family exists. The supplied record does not identify the ambient space or admissible class, so it does not determine that part of this definition.

The question attributed to Tsaban, Problem 11 (2003), is whether \[ \mathsf{add}\left(\mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\Gamma)\right)=\mathfrak b. \] The source excerpt also reports the background bounds \[ \mathfrak b\leq\mathsf{add}\left(\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\mathcal O)\right)\leq\operatorname{cf}(\mathfrak d), \] and reports that the additivity of a corresponding combinatorial notion of smallness equals $\mathfrak b$, citing $[\mathrm{AddQuad}]$. That combinatorial notion and the bibliographic expansion of this label are not supplied. These are source-reported background assertions; neither their current literature status nor their hypotheses have been independently verified here. The registry labels the question open.

Short English statement

Tsaban's Problem 11 asks whether the least number of spaces satisfying $\mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\Gamma)$ whose union fails that property is the bounding number $\mathfrak b$. The supplied excerpt leaves the intended class of spaces and cover conventions unspecified, so the expanded formulation is conditional on the stated conventions.
