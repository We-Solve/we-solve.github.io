Math-1745

Exact rank-three targets beyond torus reduction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks which vector bundles over $S^2\times S^2$ or the spaces extracted as ${\mathbb{C}} P^2\mathbin{\pm}{\mathbb{C}} P^2$, with structure group not reducible to a torus, admit $\sec\geq0$. The aid interprets the latter bases as ${\mathbb{C}} P^2\#\overline{{\mathbb{C}} P}^{,2}$ and ${\mathbb{C}} P^2\#{\mathbb{C}} P^2$, and the metric question as existence of a complete nonnegatively sectionally curved metric on the bundle's total space. Rank, real versus complex category and completeness are not explicit in the original sentence. The trailing "4 COMPILED BY M. KERIN" is a page/footer, not an additional condition.

The reported partial result concerns oriented real rank-three bundles $E\to B$, with structure group $SO(3)$, over these closed simply connected oriented four-manifolds. Reduction to a maximal torus $SO(2)$ is equivalent to splitting as ${\mathbb{R}}\oplus\eta$ with $\eta$ an oriented real plane bundle. The aid gives the exact characteristic-class criterion \[ \exists e\in H^2(B;{\mathbb{Z}}):\quad e\bmod2=w_2(E),\qquad e\smile e=p_1(E). \] Here $e$ is the Euler class of $\eta$. Sufficiency uses the Dold--Whitney classification, whose admissibility condition is $p_1\bmod4=\mathcal P(w_2)$, with $\mathcal P$ the Pontryagin square. An arbitrary class pair not satisfying this condition is not declared a bundle.

The criterion can be written without hiding parity data. Choose generators $x,y$ and the positive generator $u\in H^4(B;{\mathbb{Z}})$, write $w_2=\alpha\bar x+\beta\bar y$ with $\alpha,\beta\in\{0,1\}$ and $p_1=nu$, and require integers $a\equiv\alpha$, $b\equiv\beta\pmod2$. For the respective intersection forms it is \[ \begin{array}{c|c} B&\text{torus-reduction equation}\\\hline S^2\times S^2&n=2ab\\ {\mathbb{C}} P^2\#\overline{{\mathbb{C}} P}^{,2}&n=a^2-b^2\\ {\mathbb{C}} P^2\#{\mathbb{C}} P^2&n=a^2+b^2. \end{array} \] These equations encode the reported exceptional congruences on the first two bases. On the positive-definite base, the aid reports that after Dold--Whitney admissibility and sum-of-two-squares representability, the required parities can be chosen automatically. For $n>0$, ordinary representability means every prime congruent to $3$ modulo $4$ occurs to even exponent; $n=0$ and negative $n$ require their usual separate treatment. No signature orientation is silently changed.

The summary gives genuine non-torus targets with \[ (w_2,p_1)=(0,4u),\quad(0,8u),\quad(0,12u) \] on $S^2\times S^2$, ${\mathbb{C}} P^2\#\overline{{\mathbb{C}} P}^{,2}$ and ${\mathbb{C}} P^2\#{\mathbb{C}} P^2$, respectively. It reports that Dold--Whitney realizes these data but the displayed reduction equations have no solutions. This classifies their failure of torus reduction, not their total-space curvature. The aid explicitly does not decide whether those total spaces admit complete $\sec\geq0$ metrics, and does not answer the original problem in other ranks or bundle categories.

Short English statement

The aid classifies torus reduction for oriented rank-three bundles by characteristic classes and gives three valid non-torus targets. It leaves the requested nonnegative-curvature metrics on their total spaces undecided. The rank-three reduction calculation is therefore not a solution of the full metric problem.
