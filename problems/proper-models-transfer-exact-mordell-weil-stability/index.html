Math-1583

Proper models transfer exact Mordell-Weil stability

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Poonen's Question 32 asks whether \[ \forall K/{\mathbb{Q}}\text{ a number field}\ \exists X/{\mathbb{Z}}\text{ of finite type}:\quad |X({\mathbb{Z}})|=\infty,\qquad X(\mathcal O_K)=X({\mathbb{Z}}). \] Here $\mathcal O_K$ is the ring of integers, and the equality means that the natural base-extension map on sections is onto, with its usual injective identification. The source allows a scheme of finite type, not only a smooth, affine, or proper variety; $X$ may depend on $K$.

The aid supplies a conditional elliptic-curve route. Fix $K$, and suppose there is an elliptic curve $E/{\mathbb{Q}}$ of positive rational Mordell--Weil rank satisfying the exact point equality \[ E(K)=E({\mathbb{Q}}). \] Let $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{Z}}$ be any proper finite-presentation model with generic fiber identified with $E$. The reported natural restriction bijections are \[ \mathcal X({\mathbb{Z}})\cong E({\mathbb{Q}}),\qquad \mathcal X(\mathcal O_K)\cong E(K). \] Properness over the Dedekind bases supplies extension of generic points, and separatedness supplies uniqueness. Smoothness of the bad fibers is not required. Thus exact point stability and positive rank make $\mathcal X$ a solution for this $K$.

After these identifications the additional section classes are measured by \[ E(K)/E({\mathbb{Q}}). \] This is a quotient of Mordell--Weil groups, not an assertion that an arbitrary proper model has a smooth global group-scheme structure. If only $\operatorname{rank}E(K)=\operatorname{rank}E({\mathbb{Q}})$, the quotient is finite but may be nonzero. Every nonzero coset still gives unwanted $\mathcal O_K$-sections of every such proper model.

Under equal ranks, exact equality is equivalent to saturation of $E({\mathbb{Q}})$ in $E(K)$: for every $P\in E(K)$ and integer $m\ge1$, $mP\in E({\mathbb{Q}})$ implies $P\in E({\mathbb{Q}})$. Extra torsion and finite division phenomena therefore cannot be ignored. Changing singular bad fibers while staying in the proper-model category cannot eliminate those extra generic points. This does not assert an obstruction for every nonproper finite-type construction permitted by the original question.

The aid says the rank-stability results it checked from 2025/2026 do not establish the exact equality hypothesis. Their identities and precise hypotheses are not supplied. No positive-rank exact-stability curve for every $K$, and hence no unconditional answer to the universal question, is claimed.

Short English statement

The source seeks an infinite integral point set gaining no points over a chosen number field's integers. The aid reduces this to exact point stability of a positive-rank elliptic curve and shows why equal ranks alone are insufficient. Proper models preserve every extra generic point, regardless of singular bad fibers.
