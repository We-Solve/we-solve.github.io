Math-3573

Reducibility of Trisections with Nonseparating Three Spheres

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual closed-manifold convention, let $X$ be a closed, connected, oriented smooth $4$-manifold. A smooth embedded $3$-sphere is an embedding $e:S^3\hookrightarrow X$; it is nonseparating if $X\setminus e(S^3)$ is connected.

A $(g;k_0,k_1,k_2)$-trisection is a decomposition $\mathcal T=(X_0,X_1,X_2)$ with $X=X_0\cup X_1\cup X_2$, where each $X_i$ is a $4$-dimensional $1$-handlebody diffeomorphic to the boundary connected sum of $k_i$ copies of $S^1\times B^3$, with $k_i=0$ meaning $B^4$. Each pairwise intersection $H_{ij}=X_i\cap X_j$ is a $3$-dimensional handlebody of genus $g$, and the triple intersection $\Sigma=X_0\cap X_1\cap X_2$ is their common boundary, a closed connected surface of genus $g$. The parameters satisfy $g\geq 0$ and $0\leq k_i\leq g$; equality of the three $k_i$ is not required.

Such a trisection is reducible if there is a simple closed curve $c\subset\Sigma$, essential in $\Sigma$, which bounds a properly embedded disk in each of $H_{01},H_{12},H_{20}$. Here essential means that $c$ does not bound a disk in $\Sigma$.

Conjecture 5, under this conventional interpretation. For every closed, connected, oriented smooth $4$-manifold $X$, \[ \left(\exists\, e:S^3\hookrightarrow X\text{ a smooth embedding with }X\setminus e(S^3)\text{ connected}\right) \Longrightarrow \left(\forall\,\mathcal T\text{ a trisection of }X,\ \mathcal T\text{ is reducible}\right). \] The reducing curve may depend on the trisection. The supplied statement says only "smooth $4$-manifold" and does not explicitly impose closedness, connectedness, or orientability, or define trisection or reducibility. Thus the closed, connected, oriented convention above is an explicit interpretive qualification requiring review; the intended scope for manifolds with boundary, noncompact manifolds, or other trisection conventions is unresolved.

The original-statement field also appends a distinct Problem 4. It assumes a nonseparating $3$-manifold $Y\subset X$ and a trisection central surface $\Sigma$, and asks for an upper bound using the topology of $Y$ and an induced loop. The ambient expression is printed as $\text{D0 Y D1 Y D2}$, and the quantity to be bounded as $\mathrm{dp}\Sigma\mathrm{q}$. These objects and the loop construction are not defined in the supplied record, so this additional problem cannot be given a precise formulation from the supplied evidence.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the presence of a nonseparating embedded $3$-sphere forces every trisection of a smooth $4$-manifold to admit an essential central-surface curve bounding disks in all three pairwise handlebodies. The standard closed, connected, oriented interpretation is stated explicitly; the source's broader scope and its appended Problem 4 require clarification.
