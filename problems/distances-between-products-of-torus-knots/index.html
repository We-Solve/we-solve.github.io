Math-15664

Distances Between Products of Torus Knots

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for exact values of a rectangle transformation cost and a torus-knot cobordism distance, written respectively as \[ E(a\times b,c\times d) \qquad\text{and}\qquad d_{\chi}\left(T(a,b),T(c,d)\right). \] Here $a\times b$ and $c\times d$ denote the rectangles referred to in the supplied background, and $T(a,b)$, $T(c,d)$ denote the torus objects called knots in that record. The parameter domain and admissibility conditions are not supplied. Under the usual positive-integer convention, $T(p,q)$ is a torus knot when $\gcd(p,q)=1$, and otherwise is a torus link; this convention and any coprimality restrictions are not specified by the record and are not imposed here.

Requested determination. Determine the exact values of \[ E(a\times b,c\times d) \quad\text{and}\quad d_{\chi}\left(T(a,b),T(c,d)\right) \] for every admissible parameter quadruple $(a,b,c,d)$, once the intended domain and the definitions of both quantities have been recovered from an authoritative source.

Unresolved formulation. The record does not define the permitted rectangle transformations, their costs, or the conditions for a transformation to exist. It also does not define $d_{\chi}$: the ambient cobordism setting, regularity category, orientation conventions, allowed surface components, and the precise Euler-characteristic objective and normalization are unspecified. Thus the displayed request preserves the supplied target but does not determine a unique mathematical optimization problem.

Short English statement

Determine the exact rectangle transformation cost $E(a\times b,c\times d)$ and cobordism distance $d_{\chi}(T(a,b),T(c,d))$. The supplied record omits the parameter restrictions and definitions needed to make either quantity precise. Its open status is a supplied curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
