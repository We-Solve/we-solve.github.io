Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt does not specify the ambient class of spaces or whether covers must be countable. The following gives a precise conditional formulation using countable covers; these choices require confirmation against the source context.

Let $X$ be a topological space. Write $\mathcal{O}(X)$ for the collection of countable families $\mathcal{U}$ of open proper subsets of $X$ such that $\bigcup\mathcal{U}=X$. Write $\Gamma(X)$ for those $\mathcal{U}\in\mathcal{O}(X)$ which are infinite and satisfy \[ \forall x\in X,\qquad \{U\in\mathcal{U}:x\notin U\}\text{ is finite}. \] The property $\mathsf{S}_1(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{O})$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal{U}_n)_{n\geq 1}$ from $\mathcal{O}(X)$, there are choices $U_n\in\mathcal{U}_n$ such that $\{U_n:n\geq 1\}\in\mathcal{O}(X)$.

The property $\mathsf{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal{O},\Gamma)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal{U}_n)_{n\geq 1}$ from $\mathcal{O}(X)$, each having no finite subcover of $X$, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal{V}_n\subseteq\mathcal{U}_n$ such that \[ \left\{\bigcup\mathcal{V}_n:n\geq 1\right\}\in\Gamma(X). \] Set \[ P(X)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X\text{ satisfies both }\mathsf{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal{O},\Gamma) \text{ and }\mathsf{S}_1(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{O}). \] For the intended ambient class $\mathscr{C}$, which is unspecified in the supplied excerpt, the finite-product question is whether \[ \forall m\geq 1\;\forall X_1,\ldots,X_m\in\mathscr{C},\qquad \left(\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}P(X_i)\right) \Longrightarrow P\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^{m}X_i\right), \] where the product has the product topology. Thus the intersection in the source denotes simultaneous satisfaction of the two properties.

The source reports that this conjunction is equivalent to the Gerlits--Nagy $(*)$ property and also to $\mathsf{S}_1(\Omega,\mathcal{O}^{\gamma\text{-}*\mathrm{gp}*})$, citing $[\mathrm{coc7}]$. Here an $\omega$-cover is a cover by proper subsets such that every finite subset of the space is contained in some member; $\Omega$ denotes the corresponding open covers, subject to the unresolved countability convention. The excerpt does not define the decorated target cover class or identify $[\mathrm{coc7}]$. These equivalences are retained as supplied contextual assertions, not independently verified formulations.

Short English statement

Must every finite product of spaces satisfying both $\mathsf{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal{O},\Gamma)$ and $\mathsf{S}_1(\mathcal{O},\mathcal{O})$ again satisfy both properties? The source labels this problem open. Its intended ambient class and cover conventions require verification; the displayed formulation explicitly uses countable covers.
