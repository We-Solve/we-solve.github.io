Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 25

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime $p>2$ and a power $q$ of $p$. Let $\psi$ denote the additive character occurring in the source, and let $\mathcal L(u,f,\psi)$ denote the associated Artin--Schreier $L$-function. The source does not specify the domain or nontriviality of $\psi$, or the normalization of $\mathcal L$.

For positive integers $d$, define the following families, interpreting the misplaced braces in the supplied transcription as restrictions on $d$: \[ \mathcal{AS}^{0}_d=\{f\in{\mathbb{F}}_q[x]:\deg f=d\},\qquad \gcd(d,p)=1, \] \[ \mathcal{AS}^{0,\mathrm{odd}}_d =\{f\in{\mathbb{F}}_q[x]:\deg f=d,\ f(x)=-f(-x)\}, \qquad \gcd(d,2p)=1, \] and \[ \begin{split} \mathcal{AS}^{\mathrm{ord}}_d =\bigl\{f\in{\mathbb{F}}_q(x):\;&f=h/g,\quad h,g\in{\mathbb{F}}_q[x],\quad g\ne0,\\ &\gcd(h,g)=1,\quad g\text{ squarefree},\\ &\max(\deg h,\deg g)=d,\quad \deg g\in\{d,d-1\}\bigr\}. \end{split} \] Here the condition called $\deg f=d$ in the source is explicitly $\max(\deg h,\deg g)=d$ for a reduced representation.

For each family $\mathcal F_d$, retain the source's moment notation in the family-indexed form \[ M_{k,\mathcal F}(d) =\left\langle\mathcal L(q^{1/2},f,\psi)^k\right\rangle_{f\in\mathcal F_d}. \] The brackets denote the source's family average; its measure and normalization are not specified. The argument $q^{1/2}$ is reproduced literally. Whether this is the intended evaluation point depends on the unspecified convention for $\mathcal L$ and requires clarification.

Moment targets. With $q$ fixed and $d\to\infty$, subject to the displayed restrictions on $d$, compute the moments for the following orders: \[ \begin{array}{c|c} \mathcal F_d&k\\ \hline \mathcal{AS}^{\mathrm{ord}}_d&1,2,3,4,5,6\\ \mathcal{AS}^{0}_d&1,2,3,4\\ \mathcal{AS}^{0,\mathrm{odd}}_d&1,2. \end{array} \] The source does not specify whether "compute" requires an exact formula, a leading asymptotic, further terms, or a particular error bound.

One-level density targets. In the same fixed-$q$, growing-$d$ regime, compute the family-averaged one-level density of zeros against a Schwartz test function $\phi$, with the following Fourier-support extensions:

  1. For $\mathcal{AS}^{\mathrm{ord}}_d$, the source lists \[ \operatorname{supp}\widehat\phi\subset(-1-\delta,1+\delta) \quad\text{for some }\delta>0, \] or \[ \operatorname{supp}\widehat\phi\subset(-2-\delta,2+\delta) \quad\text{for some }\delta>0. \] The source does not clarify whether these are alternative milestones or separately required targets.
  2. For $\mathcal{AS}^{0}_d$, the listed support condition is \[ \operatorname{supp}\widehat\phi \subset\left(-(2-2/p)-\delta,\ 2-2/p+\delta\right). \]
  3. For $\mathcal{AS}^{0,\mathrm{odd}}_d$, the listed support condition is \[ \operatorname{supp}\widehat\phi \subset\left(-(1-1/p)-\delta,\ 1-1/p+\delta\right). \]
In the last two items, the supplied text does not explicitly quantify $\delta$; interpreting it as a positive support extension requires confirmation. The source also leaves the zero scaling, treatment of periodic zero ordinates, Fourier-transform convention, averaging measure, and precise density asymptotic unspecified. Thus these are faithfully recorded research targets, rather than fully normalized density assertions.

Short English statement

For fixed $q$ of odd characteristic, determine the listed moments and extend the Fourier-support range for one-level zero-density results in the ordinary, polynomial, and odd-polynomial Artin--Schreier families as $d\to\infty$. The supplied formulation leaves essential normalization and quantifier choices unresolved, including the literal moment evaluation point $q^{1/2}$. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied status information, not an independently verified assessment.
