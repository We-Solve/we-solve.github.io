Problem 4.7 — Compute the Spg(Z)-moduleHk c (T c,red g ) in some range k≥ko.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the standard Torelli-space interpretation, $\mathcal T_g$ parametrizes smooth genus-$g$ curves with a symplectic homology marking, $\mathcal T_g^c$ allows stable curves of compact type, and $\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}}$ is its reducible locus. Compact type means that the dual graph is a tree, so the generalized Jacobian is compact. These conventions and the coefficient ring must be confirmed from the chapter; they are not defined in the extracted problem. The source's $\operatorname{Sp}_g({\mathbb{Z}})$ acts by changing markings (customarily $\operatorname{Sp}(2g,{\mathbb{Z}})$).

The principal task is to compute the module \[H_c^k(\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}})\] under this action in a range $k\geq k_0$, including both the compactly supported cohomology groups and their representation structure. The range threshold $k_0$, genus range and coefficients are not supplied.

The following reported information is preserved with extraction caveats. The text writes \[H_c^{6g-7}(\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}})=H_0(B_g^c),\qquad H_c^{6g-8}(\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}})\twoheadrightarrow H_1(B_g^c),\] where $B_g^c$ is called the normalization, or disjoint union of irreducible components. It describes those components through symplectic translates of the gluing products \[\mathcal T_{h,1}^c\times\mathcal T_{g-h,1}^c, \qquad1\leq h\leq\lfloor g/2\rfloor.\] The extracted orbit/coset indexing is garbled, including possible interchange stabilizers when $h=g-h$, so it is not replaced by an invented quotient formula. The two displayed degrees also require checking: they appear shifted relative to the later diagram and the usual real dimension $6g-8$ of this reducible locus. They are source reports, not validated identities or silently corrected statements.

The background folk conjecture says $H_k(\mathcal T_g)$ is finitely generated for $k<g-1$, imposing finiteness constraints on $\mathcal T_g^c$ and its reducible locus if true. The coefficient and degree conventions are not repeated. For $g\geq3$ the source gives a Gysin sequence whose arrows/line breaks are damaged. Legible fragments include \[H_c^{6g-10}(\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}})\to H_3(\mathcal T_g)\to H_3(\mathcal T_g^c),\] \[H_c^{6g-9}(\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}})\to H_2(\mathcal T_g)\to H_2(\mathcal T_g^c),\] \[H_c^{6g-8}(\mathcal T_g^{c,\mathrm{red}})\to H_1(\mathcal T_g) \xrightarrow{\tau}H_1(\mathcal T_g^c)\to0.\] The diagram additionally displays $H_c^{6g-10}(\mathcal T_g^c)$, $H_c^{6g-9}(\mathcal T_g^c)$, $H_1(B_g^c)$, $H_0(B_g^c)$ and $(\bigwedge^3 H)/H$, but their full connecting/identification arrows cannot be reconstructed reliably. It identifies $\tau$ as the Johnson homomorphism induced by $\mathcal T_g\hookrightarrow\mathcal T_g^c$, with $H$ the usual first-homology module. No complete exact sequence is fabricated from the fragments.

A further suggested topic is the topology of the branching locus of the period map $\mathcal T_g^c\to\mathcal J_g^c$, described as the compact-type hyperelliptic locus $\mathcal H_g^c$. The source invokes A'Campo [1]: the image of the hyperelliptic mapping class group $\Delta_g$ contains the level-two symplectic subgroup, customarily \[\operatorname{Sp}_g({\mathbb{Z}})[2]=\{A\in\operatorname{Sp}_g({\mathbb{Z}}):A\equiv I\pmod2\}.\] Its extracted definition repeats the level subgroup inside its own defining set and needs checking. It defines $\Delta_g$ as the centralizer of a hyperelliptic involution in $\Gamma_g$, and as the orbifold fundamental group of smooth hyperelliptic moduli. The mod-two image is printed as $S_{2g-2}$, described as permuting Weierstrass points, while the component formula uses $S_{2g+2}$: \[\#\pi_0(\mathcal H_g^c)=\frac{|\operatorname{Sp}_g({\mathbb{F}}_2)|}{|S_{2g+2}|} =\frac{2^{g^2}\prod_{j=1}^{g}(2^{2j}-1)}{(2g+2)!}.\] The two symmetric-group indices are incompatible as written and require source confirmation; the formula is retained as reported. The source further states that each component is smooth and immerses in Siegel space $\mathfrak h_g$ by the period map, that the irreducible components of $\mathcal H_g$ are disjoint in $\mathcal J_g$, and that in genus three there are $36$ compact-type components whose images are cut out by the $36$ even theta nulls $\vartheta_\alpha:\mathfrak h_3\to{\mathbb{C}}$. Definitions and genus hypotheses for these reports are missing; they are contextual results, not an independent verification or the principal module computation.

Short English statement

Compute high-degree compactly supported cohomology of the reducible compact-type Torelli locus as a symplectic module. The record also includes finiteness and hyperelliptic-locus context, but its degree shifts, coset indexing and displayed Gysin diagram are damaged.
