Math-3672

Economical Toroidal Resolutions of Log Canonical Pairs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,\Delta)$ denote a log canonical pair of dimension $n$. In the usual discrepancy notation, if $f:W\to X$ is a birational model on which a prime divisor $F$ appears, its log discrepancy is \[ a(F;X,\Delta)=1+\operatorname{coeff}_{F}\left(K_W-f^*(K_X+\Delta)\right). \] This notation requires the usual hypotheses permitting the pullback of $K_X+\Delta$. Log canonicity means that all such log discrepancies are nonnegative. A non-klt centre of a log canonical pair is the centre of a divisor with log discrepancy zero.

For a projective birational morphism $\pi:Y\to X$, write $\widetilde\Delta$ for the strict transform of $\Delta$, and write \[ \Gamma=\widetilde\Delta+E. \] The conventional interpretation of $E$ is the reduced sum of the $\pi$-exceptional prime divisors on $Y$; the supplied record does not explicitly define $E$, so this interpretation requires confirmation.

Numerical existence question, with an unresolved local condition. For every positive integer $n$, does there exist a nonnegative integer $s=s(n)$ such that every $n$-dimensional log canonical pair $(X,\Delta)$ admits a projective birational morphism $\pi:Y\to X$ satisfying \[ \#\{F\subset Y:F\text{ is a }\pi\text{-exceptional prime divisor}\}\leq s(n), \qquad a(F;X,\Delta)=0 \quad\text{for every such }F, \] and the prescribed local condition on $(Y,\Gamma)$ near the generic point of every non-klt centre?

The supplied formulations prescribe different local conditions:

  • The original statement requires $(Y,\Gamma)$ to be toroidal near these generic points.
  • The clean statement and current statement require $(Y,\Gamma)$ to be quotient-dlt near these generic points.
These are retained as distinct candidate formulations; their equivalence is not asserted. The record does not explicitly identify whether the non-klt centres belong to $(X,\Delta)$ or $(Y,\Gamma)$, nor specify how a neighbourhood on $Y$ is to be understood if the centres are on $X$. It also leaves the ground field, characteristic, coefficient domain of $\Delta$, and precise local meanings of toroidal and quotient-dlt unspecified. Thus the supplied evidence does not determine a single fully specified conjecture.

The supplied literature assessment further describes the extracted assertion as only a numerical interpretation of an economical-resolution conjecture whose full statement is omitted. The question above records the supplied numerical assertion and does not reconstruct that missing conjecture.

Short English statement

The proposed bound depends only on dimension: every log canonical pair should admit a projective birational modification extracting at most that many divisors, all with log discrepancy zero, and satisfying a local condition near non-klt centres. The original record asks for toroidal structure, whereas the cleaned version asks for quotient-dlt structure. This difference, the ambient pair of the centres, and missing foundational hypotheses require review. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified status.
