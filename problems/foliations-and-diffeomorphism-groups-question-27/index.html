Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 27

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold with first Betti number $b_1(M)=\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}H^1(M;{\mathbb{R}})\geq 1$. An integral real cohomology class means an element of \[ \operatorname{im}\left(H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})\longrightarrow H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})\right). \] Write $x_M$ for the Thurston seminorm on $H_2(M;{\mathbb{R}})$. In the conventional orientable setting, for an integral homology class $z$, it is given by \[ x_M(z)=\min_{[S]=z}\sum_i\max\{0,-\chi(S_i)\}, \] where $S=\bigsqcup_i S_i\subset M$ ranges over embedded closed oriented surfaces representing $z$; it extends homogeneously and continuously to real homology. Define its dual, allowing the value $+\infty$, by \[ x_M^*(a)=\inf\left\{C\geq 0:\ |\langle a,z\rangle|\leq Cx_M(z)\text{ for every }z\in H_2(M;{\mathbb{R}})\right\}, \] with $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$.

Let $\mathrm{Homeo}_+(S^1)$ be the group of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of the circle. For a representation $\rho:\pi_1(M)\to\mathrm{Homeo}_+(S^1)$, let $e_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(\rho)\in H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})$ denote the Euler class of its associated oriented flat circle bundle $\widetilde M\times_{\rho}S^1\to M$, and let $e_{{\mathbb{R}}}(\rho)$ be its image in real cohomology.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such $M$, \[ \forall a\in\operatorname{im}\left(H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})\to H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})\right),\qquad x_M^*(a)=1\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\rho:\pi_1(M)\to\mathrm{Homeo}_+(S^1) \quad e_{{\mathbb{R}}}(\rho)=a. \] The source does not explicitly assume that $M$ is orientable. The surface description above specifies the usual orientable interpretation; whether nonorientable manifolds are intended, and which Thurston-norm convention applies to them, requires clarification. Since the source places $a$ in real cohomology, the displayed conclusion concerns the real image of the Euler class. It does not prescribe an integral lift of $a$, including its torsion component.

Short English statement

For a closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold with positive first Betti number, must every integral real second cohomology class of dual Thurston norm exactly one be the real Euler class of an orientation-preserving circle action of its fundamental group? The intended orientability assumption and any requirement concerning a particular integral lift remain unspecified.
