Math-15483

Conjugacy Classes and Characters of Finite p-Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and $P$ a finite $p$-group. The conjugacy class of $x\in P$ is $x^P=\{g xg^{-1}:g\in P\}$, and the class number is \[ h_P=\#\{x^P:x\in P\}. \] In the customary ordinary-character interpretation, $\operatorname{Irr}(P)$ is the set of irreducible complex characters of $P$; $\chi(1)$ is the degree of $\chi$. For every integer $k\geq0$, put \[ a_k(P)=\#\{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(P):\chi(1)=p^k\}. \] The four requested tasks are to develop general methods that parametrize the $P$-conjugacy classes, compute $h_P$, describe $\operatorname{Irr}(P)$, and compute each $a_k(P)$.

This is a structural and computational research program, not one stated numerical formula or a claim that finite-group data cannot be obtained by finite enumeration. The source supplies no input presentation, complexity target, preferred parametrizing objects, or distinguished family of $p$-groups. Its literature assessment refers to a generalized Cayley approach but gives neither the construction nor the classes to which it applies. These details are needed to formulate a specific uniform method or theorem.

Short English statement

Develop general structural methods for conjugacy classes, their number, irreducible characters, and the number of characters of each degree $p^k$ in finite $p$-groups.
