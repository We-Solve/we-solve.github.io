Mutable Laurent Polynomials and Toric Smoothing Components

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A lattice polytope is the convex hull of finitely many points of a lattice. For a lattice $N$ with dual $M=\operatorname{Hom}(N,{\mathbb{Z}})$ and a full-dimensional lattice polytope $F\subset N_{{\mathbb{R}}}$, its cone at height one is \[ \sigma_F=\operatorname{cone}\{(v,1):v\in F\}\subset N_{{\mathbb{R}}}\oplus{\mathbb{R}}. \] Once a ground field $k$ is specified, the standard associated affine toric variety is \[ U_F=\operatorname{Spec}k[\sigma_F^{\vee}\cap(M\oplus{\mathbb{Z}})]. \] Under this construction, $\dim U_F=\dim F+1$. Thus the supplied simultaneous requirements that $F$ be three-dimensional and that $U_F$ be a threefold are incompatible: a three-dimensional $F$ yields a fourfold, whereas a threefold requires a two-dimensional $F$. The intended dimension cannot be determined from the record.

For a nonzero Laurent polynomial $f=\sum_{u\in N}a_u x^u\in k[N]$, with finitely many nonzero coefficients, define \[ \operatorname{Newt}(f)=\operatorname{conv}\{u\in N:a_u\ne0\}. \] In a specified deformation setting, a smoothing component means an irreducible component of the base of a miniversal deformation whose general fiber is smooth. The record does not specify the ground field, the deformation setting, or a definition of $0$-mutability. It also does not specify coefficient normalizations or any equivalence relation on Laurent polynomials.

Supplied conjecture, pending resolution of these qualifications. For every lattice polytope $F$ satisfying the intended dimensional and geometric hypotheses, there is a bijection \[ \left\{\text{smoothing components of the miniversal deformation of }U_F\right\} \;\longleftrightarrow\; \left\{f:\ f\text{ is a }0\text{-mutable Laurent polynomial and }\operatorname{Newt}(f)=F\right\}. \] This displays the proposed correspondence only; its right-hand side remains undefined without the missing definition and conventions, and the dimensional conflict prevents a single unqualified formulation. The supplied background describes the proposal as a generalization of Altmann's deformation construction for relevant toric Gorenstein threefold singularities, but does not specify what restrictions "relevant" imposes.

Short English statement

The proposed conjecture pairs smoothing components of a toric singularity with $0$-mutable Laurent polynomials having a prescribed Newton polytope. The supplied formulation has a dimension conflict and omits the definition of $0$-mutability and essential conventions. It therefore remains a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
