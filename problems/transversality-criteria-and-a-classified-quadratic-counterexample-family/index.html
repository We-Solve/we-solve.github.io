Math-1704

Transversality criteria and a classified quadratic counterexample family

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Peter Ebenfelt's Problem 12 asks for hypersurface hypotheses forcing transversality of every noncollapsed holomorphic map. Let $0\in M\subset{\mathbb{C}}^{n+1}$, $0\in M'\subset{\mathbb{C}}^{N+1}$ be $C^\infty$ or real-analytic real hypersurfaces, $n\le N$, and let $H:({\mathbb{C}}^{n+1},0)\to({\mathbb{C}}^{N+1},0)$ be holomorphic with $H(M)\subset M'$. Assume no full neighborhood is mapped into $M'$. Determine conditions on $M,M'$ under which every such $H$ is transverse at 0, meaning \[ d_0H(T_0{\mathbb{C}}^{n+1})+T_0M'=T_0{\mathbb{C}}^{N+1} \] as real tangent spaces. More generally the target tangent spaces are based at $H(x)$. With real defining functions $\rho,\rho'$, write $\rho'\circ H=a\rho$; on $M$, transversality is equivalent to $a(x)\ne0$.

The source records equal-dimensional finite-map transversality when either hypersurface is of finite type, and transversality when both are strictly pseudoconvex, under its noncollapse assumptions. A finite holomorphic germ here has isolated fiber over 0, with the usual equal-dimensional interpretation in that statement. These are reported background cases, not a complete criterion.

The supplied verification restores the fractions and signs in the source's example: \[ H(z,w)=(z+z^2+\tfrac i2w,\ z-z^2-\tfrac i2w,\ -2zw), \] \[ M=\{\operatorname{Im}w=|z|^2\},\qquad M'=\{\operatorname{Im}W=-|Z_1|^2+|Z_2|^2\}. \] For $\rho=\operatorname{Im}w-|z|^2$ and $\rho'=\operatorname{Im}W+|Z_1|^2-|Z_2|^2$, the aid reports the exact identity \[ \rho'\circ H=-2(z+\overline z)\rho. \] Thus the map is transverse precisely on $M\setminus\{\operatorname{Re}z=0\}$, and fails at 0 despite not collapsing a neighborhood into the target. The target is indefinite, so this does not contradict the strictly pseudoconvex case.

Quadratic ansatz classified in the aid. For $a,b,c\in{\mathbb{C}}$, set \[ H_{a,b,c}(z,w)=(z+az^2+bw,\ z-az^2-bw,\ czw). \] The aid reports \[ H_{a,b,c}(M)\subset M' \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a=2i\overline b,\quad c=-4i\overline b. \] Under these conditions, \[ \rho'\circ H_{a,b,c}=(cz+\overline{cz})\rho. \] If $b=0$, the map is $(z,z,0)$ and is collapsed into $M'$. If $b\ne0$, it is an immersion at 0 and a finite germ of multiplicity one onto its image, with exact nontransversality locus $\operatorname{Re}(cz)=0$ on $M$. Finite here is in the positive-codimension, finite-onto-image sense and does not invoke the source's equal-dimensional result. The original example is $(a,b,c)=(1,i/2,-2)$.

The aid also reports post-workshop criteria involving equal-dimensional generic-full-rank maps, Levi-rank/dimension/Jacobian-rank-stratum inequalities in positive codimension, and a 2025 low-codimension theorem for maps into same-signature hyperquadrics. Their exact inequalities, scope and references are absent; no quantitative criterion is invented from those summaries. The classification of the displayed ansatz is not a classification of all holomorphic maps or a solution of the general hypersurface-condition problem.

Short English statement

The source seeks hypotheses making every noncollapsed hypersurface map transverse. The aid verifies an indefinite-target counterexample and classifies a quadratic family containing it, with an exact real multiplier detecting transversality. Positive-codimension finite immersions can still fail transversality; broader reported criteria need their missing hypotheses and references.
