Math-15307

Average Normal-Cone Width and Random Polytope Diameters

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation, let $m,n$ be positive integers and let $A=(a_{ij})\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m\times n}$. Define the polyhedron \[ P(A)=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:Ax\leq\mathbf 1_m\}, \] where the inequalities are coordinatewise and $\mathbf 1_m$ is the all-ones vector. In the Gaussian model specified by the source, the entries $a_{ij}$ are mutually independent random variables with distribution $N(0,1)$. The source specifies no relationship between $m$ and $n$.

For a nonempty polyhedron $P\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$ and $v\in P$, its outer normal cone at $v$ is \[ N_P(v)=\{u\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\langle u,x-v\rangle\leq 0\text{ for every }x\in P\}. \] If $P$ is a bounded polytope, its combinatorial diameter is the diameter of its vertex-edge graph: the maximum, over pairs of vertices, of the minimum number of edges in a path joining them. This is a possible interpretation of the source's term "diameter," but the supplied record does not explicitly specify the diameter convention.

Source question, with unresolved specifications. Can one define a quantitative notion of normal cones being "wide on average" and derive useful polyhedral-diameter bounds from it, including a diameter bound for the Gaussian model $P(A)$ above?

The source does not define cone width, the cones over which to average, their weighting, or the desired bound. It also does not specify whether a Gaussian-model bound should hold in expectation, with a stated probability, or under conditioning. Although $0$ lies in the interior of $P(A)$, this polyhedron need not be bounded; the source supplies neither a boundedness hypothesis nor a convention for unbounded realizations. Consequently, the question is a research objective requiring these specifications, rather than a fully quantified conjectural inequality.

Short English statement

The question asks whether an average measure of normal-cone width can control polyhedral diameter, particularly for $P(A)=\{x:Ax\leq\mathbf 1_m\}$ with independent standard Gaussian matrix entries. The averaging rule, diameter convention, dimensional regime, boundedness treatment, and probabilistic form of the bound remain unspecified.
