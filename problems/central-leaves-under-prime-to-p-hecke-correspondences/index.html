Math-3585

Central Leaves Under Prime-to-p Hecke Correspondences

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(G,X)$ be a Shimura datum of Hodge type with reflex field $E$. Retain the introductory assumption that the adjoint group $G^{\mathrm{ad}}$ is ${\mathbb{Q}}$-simple. Let $p$ be a prime such that $G_{{\mathbb{Q}}_p}$ is quasi-split and becomes split over an unramified extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$. Choose a hyperspecial subgroup $U_p\subseteq G({\mathbb{Q}}_p)$, a sufficiently small compact open subgroup $U^p\subseteq G(\mathbb A_f^p)$, and a place $v\mid p$ of $E$. Here $\mathbb A_f^p$ denotes the finite adeles away from $p$, and $U=U^pU_p$.

Let $k=\overline{\kappa(v)}$, and write \[ S=\operatorname{Sh}_{G,U} \] for the geometric special fibre over $k$ of the canonical integral model over $\mathcal O_{E,v}$ of the Shimura variety of level $U$. The supplied excerpt attributes existence of this integral model to its reference [9], whose bibliographic entry is not supplied.

Let $C\subseteq S$ be a central leaf in the sense of references [5] and [8] of the excerpt. In the usual Hodge-type interpretation, a central leaf fixes the isomorphism class of the associated $p$-divisible group together with its relevant additional tensor structure. The excerpt does not specify this structure or its precise isomorphism convention, so this description does not replace the missing referenced definition. Let $h(G)$ denote the Coxeter number used in the excerpt; its convention for the reductive group $G$ is not specified there. Stability under prime-to-$p$ Hecke operators means preservation by all the corresponding prime-to-$p$ Hecke correspondences, with the usual interpretation through changes of level.

Statement explicitly supplied as a proved theorem (Theorem 1). For every central leaf $C\subseteq S$ and every nonempty reduced closed subvariety $Z\subseteq C$ stable under the prime-to-$p$ Hecke operators, \[ p\geq h(G)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z=C. \] Closedness here is relative to $C$. The assertion is recorded as a theorem in the supplied excerpt, not as an independently verified result.

Unresolved intended problem. The supplied dated curation describes the general Hodge-type Hecke orbit conjecture without the size restriction as remaining open. Short English statement

The supplied theorem says that, when $p\geq h(G)$, every nonempty reduced closed subset of a central leaf that is stable under all prime-to-$p$ Hecke correspondences equals the whole leaf. The curation suggests removing this bound in the Hodge-type setting, but the exact open conjecture is missing. The record labels the topic partially solved and the extracted problem statement unrecoverable; this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
