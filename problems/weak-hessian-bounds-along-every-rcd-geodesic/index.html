Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
On a full-support unreduced $\operatorname{RCD}(K,N)$ space, $K\in\mathbb R$, $1<N<\infty$, let $F$ be bounded and globally Lipschitz, and $G$ bounded and continuous. For compactly supported test $g\in D(\Delta)$, bounded and globally Lipschitz with $\Delta g\in W^{1,2}$, put $Y=\nabla g$ and \[H_F(Y,Y)(h)=-\int\operatorname{div}(hY)\langle\nabla F,Y\rangle\,dm-\int h\langle\nabla F,\nabla_YY\rangle\,dm.\] Suppose $H_F(\nabla g,\nabla g)(h)\le\int hG|\nabla g|^2\,dm$ for every such $g$ and nonnegative compactly supported Lipschitz $h$. Then, for any minimizing geodesic $\sigma:[0,1]\to M$ parameterized at constant speed $\ell$, the claim says it is claimed to satisfy \[(F\circ\sigma)^{\prime\prime}\le\ell^2G\circ\sigma\] in distributions on $(0,1)$. The conclusion is for every geodesic, not only almost every geodesic of a test plan.

Outline of the argument

Weak Hessians on every geodesic: The measure is multiplied by an exponential of the function, giving a weighted Bochner inequality with shifted curvature. Resolvent regularity and a common graph core place this inequality on the actual weighted generator domain. Curvature-dimension comparison for the weighted measures and a large-parameter limit extract the desired inequality on any prescribed geodesic.
