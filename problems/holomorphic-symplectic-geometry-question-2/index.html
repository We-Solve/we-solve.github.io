Holomorphic Symplectic Geometry — Question 2

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ and $X'$ be hyperkähler manifolds with $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X'$. The supplied excerpt does not define the class of hyperkähler manifolds intended. Under the customary compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic interpretation, such a manifold is a simply connected compact Kähler manifold whose space of holomorphic two-forms is generated by an everywhere nondegenerate form. The formulation below uses this interpretation conditionally; its applicability to the intended source convention requires review.

Write $q_X$ and $q_{X'}$ for the Beauville--Bogomolov integral quadratic forms on second cohomology. A Hodge isometry is an isomorphism of abelian groups \[ \lambda:H^2(X,{\mathbb{Z}})\xrightarrow{\sim}H^2(X',{\mathbb{Z}}) \] that satisfies \[ q_{X'}(\lambda(\alpha))=q_X(\alpha) \qquad\text{for every }\alpha\in H^2(X,{\mathbb{Z}}), \] and whose complexification satisfies \[ \lambda_{{\mathbb{C}}}\left(H^{p,q}(X)\right)=H^{p,q}(X') \qquad(p,q\geq 0,\ p+q=2). \] The identification of the quadratic forms with the Beauville--Bogomolov forms is the standard interpretation of the source's phrase "Hodge isometry"; the supplied excerpt does not state it explicitly.

Question (Beauville, 2010, Question 2). If such a Hodge isometry exists, what geometric relationship between $X$ and $X'$ follows? What additional structure, required to be preserved by $\lambda$, would suffice to conclude that $X$ and $X'$ are biholomorphic?

The additional structure is deliberately unspecified in the supplied question, so this is a request for suitable hypotheses rather than a single stated implication with fixed extra assumptions. The source asks for the existence of an isomorphism between the manifolds; it does not explicitly require that the given $\lambda$ be induced by that isomorphism.

Short English statement

Beauville asks what can be deduced about two hyperkähler manifolds of equal dimension when their integral second cohomology groups are Hodge isometric, and which additional preserved structure would guarantee that the manifolds are biholomorphic. The intended hyperkähler convention and the extra structure require clarification. The supplied registry labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
