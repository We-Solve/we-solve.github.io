Power improvement for planar halving lines

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Do absolute constants $\epsilon >0$ and C exist such that every even n and every n-point planar set in general position determine at most C $n^{4/3-\epsilon }$ lines that leave exactly (n-2)/2 other points on each side?

Power improvement for planar halving lines

OpenAI's claim

Planar halving-line saving: The claimed number of halving pairs in a sufficiently large even planar configuration with no three collinear is $O(n^{4/3-\varepsilon})$ for one absolute $\varepsilon>0$. The constants are existential.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For an even-sized planar point set $P$, with no three points collinear, let $h(P)$ count pairs whose line leaves $(|P|-2)/2$ points in each open halfplane. The claimed constants $\epsilon>0,C<\infty,n_0$ are absolute and satisfy \[h(P)\le C|P|^{4/3-\epsilon}\quad(|P|\ge n_0).\] The all-ranks theorem also bounds by $CN^{4/3-\epsilon}$ the number $S_k(P)$ of switches of the $k$ points minimizing $y-sx$ as $s$ ranges from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$, for every rank $0\le k\le N$ of a generic $N$-point configuration. This power improvement does not give matching sharp bounds at every rank. Here the all-ranks genericity conditions prohibit three collinear points, repeated first coordinates, equal slopes from different point pairs, and any proper intersection of two point-pair segments lying on a third such segment. Its parameters range over $N\ge1$ and $0\le k\le N$.

Outline of the argument

Planar halving-line saving: Rotating rank sweeps track membership changes in the lower point set, including points that later return. Complete bipartite exchange graphs and convex-chain decompositions control crossings of intervals with high activity. Near saturation yields limiting rank-coordinate measures with an impossible concentration pattern; a little-oh packing improvement at one fixed scale then contracts the recurrence.
