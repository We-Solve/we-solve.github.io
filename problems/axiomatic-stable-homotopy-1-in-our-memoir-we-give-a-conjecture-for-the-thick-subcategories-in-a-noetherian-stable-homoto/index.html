Axiomatic stable homotopy 1 — In our memoir, we give a conjecture for the thick subcategories in a Noetherian stable homotopy catego…

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a Noetherian stable homotopy category in the precise sense of the source's memoir, with sphere/unit object $S$, and let $R=\pi_*S$ be its graded coefficient ring. The conjecture asks for a bijection between the intended thick subcategories and specialization-closed subsets of $\operatorname{Spec}R$.

A thick subcategory is full, triangulated and closed under retracts. For the standard graded interpretation, $\operatorname{Spec}^hR$ consists of homogeneous prime ideals, and specialization-closed means $\mathfrak p\in Z$, $\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak q$ imply $\mathfrak q\in Z$. The usual support formulation would use \[ \operatorname{supp}_R(X)=\{\mathfrak p:(\pi_*X)_{\mathfrak p}\ne0\}, \qquad \mathcal T\longmapsto\bigcup_{X\in\mathcal T}\operatorname{supp}_R(X), \] with inverse $Z\mapsto\{X:\operatorname{supp}_R(X)\subseteq Z\}$ inside the intended object class.

The memoir's axioms and exact correspondence are not supplied. In particular one must specify compact/small objects versus all objects, thick subcategories versus tensor ideals, whether $S$ generates, the Noetherian condition and ordinary versus homogeneous spectrum. These choices determine whether the displayed usual support maps are the intended ones and whether they classify all requested subcategories. They are not silently imposed as stronger hypotheses. The summary mentions Balmer-spectrum/stratification cases as substantial progress but supplies no theorem covering the full memoir setting.

Short English statement

Prove the intended thick-subcategory classification by specialization-closed coefficient-spectrum subsets, with the memoir's object, ideal and support conventions recovered.
