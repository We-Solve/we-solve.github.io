Math-4493

Equivariant Topological Modular Forms Duality for Compact Lie Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R=\operatorname{TMF}$ denote the spectrum of periodic topological modular forms. For a compact Lie group $G$, let $L=\operatorname{Lie}(G)$ be its real adjoint representation, with action induced by conjugation. Write $E_G$ for the $G$-equivariant topological modular forms spectrum intended by the source. Representation desuspension means $\Sigma^{-L}E_G=S^{-L}\wedge E_G$, where $S^{-L}$ is the inverse of the representation sphere $S^L$ in the relevant equivariant stable category.

Using the source's fixed-point notation and intended $R$-module structures, set \[ M_G=E_G^G=\operatorname{TMF}^G, \qquad N_G=(\Sigma^{-L}E_G)^G. \] An $R$-module $M$ is dualizable with dual $N$ if there are $R$-linear evaluation and coevaluation maps \[ \operatorname{ev}:N\otimes_R M\longrightarrow R, \qquad \operatorname{coev}:R\longrightarrow M\otimes_R N \] whose two triangle composites are the identity maps of $M$ and $N$, up to coherent homotopy. Here $\otimes_R$ is the derived tensor product. In that case $N$ is equivalent to the internal $R$-linear function spectrum $F_R(M,R)$.

Question. For every compact Lie group $G$, is $M_G$ dualizable as an $R$-module, with dual equivalent to $N_G$? Equivalently, does the proposed module $N_G$ admit evaluation and coevaluation maps satisfying the duality identities for $M_G$? The proposed identification of the dual is \[ F_R(\operatorname{TMF}^G,\operatorname{TMF}) \simeq (\Sigma^{-L}\operatorname{TMF})^G, \] together with the assertion that $\operatorname{TMF}^G$ is dualizable.

Unresolved qualification. The supplied record does not specify the equivariant construction of $\operatorname{TMF}$, the fixed-point functor denoted by $(-)^G$, or the construction of the indicated $R$-module structures. Representation suspension requires an appropriate equivariant setting; categorical fixed points and homotopy fixed points cannot silently be interchanged. The formulation above retains the source's intended objects conditionally on those choices and does not quantify over every possible equivariant refinement.

Short English statement

For every compact Lie group $G$, the question asks whether its equivariant topological modular forms fixed points form a dualizable $\operatorname{TMF}$-module, with dual obtained by desuspending equivariant $\operatorname{TMF}$ by the adjoint representation before taking fixed points. The equivariant model and fixed-point convention require clarification. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved; that assessment is not independently verified here.
