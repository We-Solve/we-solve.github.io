Math-4110

Hilbert-Space Coarse Embeddings of Coarse Median Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the following standard finite approximation convention for a coarse median space. A median algebra is a set $M$ with a symmetric ternary operation $m\colon M^3\to M$ satisfying, for all $a,b,c,d,e\in M$, \[ m(a,a,b)=a,\qquad m(m(a,b,c),d,e)=m(m(a,d,e),m(b,d,e),c). \] A metric space $(X,d)$ is coarse median if it admits an operation $\mu\colon X^3\to X$, a constant $K\geq 1$, and a function $h\colon{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\to[0,\infty)$ with the following properties. First, for all $x_1,x_2,x_3,y_1,y_2,y_3\in X$, \[ d\left(\mu(x_1,x_2,x_3),\mu(y_1,y_2,y_3)\right) \leq K\sum_{i=1}^3d(x_i,y_i)+h(0). \] Second, for every integer $p\geq1$ and every nonempty finite subset $A\subseteq X$ with $|A|\leq p$, there exist a finite median algebra $(M,m)$ and maps $\pi\colon A\to M$ and $\lambda\colon M\to X$ such that \[ d(a,\lambda\pi(a))\leq h(p)\quad(a\in A) \] and \[ d\left(\lambda m(u,v,w),\mu(\lambda u,\lambda v,\lambda w)\right)\leq h(p) \quad(u,v,w\in M). \] A coarse embedding into a Hilbert space is a map $f\colon X\to\mathcal H$ for which there exist nondecreasing functions $\rho_-,\rho_+\colon[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ satisfying \[ \lim_{t\to\infty}\rho_-(t)=\infty,\qquad \rho_-(d(x,y))\leq\lVert f(x)-f(y)\rVert_{\mathcal H}\leq\rho_+(d(x,y)) \quad(x,y\in X). \] Question. Does every coarse median metric space $(X,d)$ admit a coarse embedding into some Hilbert space $\mathcal H$? The Hilbert space, map, and control functions may depend on $X$.

Short English statement

Does every metric space with a coarse median operation admit a map into a Hilbert space whose distances have an upper bound depending only on the original distance and a lower bound tending to infinity with that distance? The source supplies the label "open"; its precise convention for coarse median spaces requires confirmation.
