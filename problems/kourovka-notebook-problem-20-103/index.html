Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.103

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, define its derived series by \[ G^{(0)}=G,\qquad G^{(k+1)}=[G^{(k)},G^{(k)}]\quad(k\geq 0), \] where $[H,H]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$ with $x,y\in H$. The group $G$ is soluble of derived length exactly $n$ if \[ G^{(n)}=\{1\}\quad\text{and}\quad G^{(n-1)}\neq\{1\}. \] It is torsion-free if \[ \forall g\in G\;\forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1},\qquad g^m=1\Longrightarrow g=1. \] Question (source formulation). For every integer $n\geq 3$, let $G_\Gamma$ be a partially commutative soluble group of derived length $n$, with defining graph $\Gamma$, in the sense intended in Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.103. Must \[ \forall g\in G_\Gamma\;\forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1},\qquad g^m=1\Longrightarrow g=1 \] hold?

Unresolved qualification. The supplied statement says that the definition of $G_\Gamma$ is similar to the derived-length-two case in Problem 17.104, but neither that definition nor the text of Problem 17.104 is supplied. Consequently, the class of groups quantified over is not fully specified by this record. In particular, no defining presentation or restriction on the defining graph, including finiteness, is imposed here without source support. The phrase "derived length $n$" is retained as exact derived length; identifying the intended construction requires consultation of the referenced definition.

Short English statement

Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.103, proposed by E. I. Timoshenko, asks whether every partially commutative soluble group of derived length at least three is torsion-free. The intended construction of these groups is referred to Problem 17.104 and is missing from the supplied record, so the precise class of groups requires clarification.
