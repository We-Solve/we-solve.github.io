Malle-Type Asymptotics for Refined Cover Counts

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual interpretation of $S_n$, let $n$ be a positive integer and let $S_n$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. A subgroup $G\leq S_n$ is transitive if, for every $i,j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, some $g\in G$ satisfies $g(i)=j$. The source calls $G$ countable; with this interpretation of $S_n$, $G$ is necessarily finite. Let $c\subseteq G$ be conjugation-invariant, meaning that $gcg^{-1}=c$ for every $g\in G$.

The source denotes by $N^c_{G,q}(X)$ a refined count of geometrically connected $G$-covers whose ramification monodromy lies in $c$. For a scheme over a field $k$, geometrically connected means that its base change to an algebraic closure $\overline{k}$ is connected. However, the supplied record does not specify the base field or base curve, the meaning of $G$-cover, the equivalence relation or weights used in counting, or the quantity bounded by $X$. Consequently, this description does not yet define a counting function. In particular, interpreting $q$ as the cardinality of a finite field would require an additional hypothesis.

Proposed asymptotic, conditional on specifying the counting problem. For each admissible pair $(G,c)$, the source asks whether there are constants $a,b,C$, depending on $G$ and $c$, such that \[ N^c_{G,q}(X)\sim C X^a(\log X)^{b-1} \qquad (X\longrightarrow\infty). \] Here $f(X)\sim h(X)$ means that $h(X)$ is nonzero for all sufficiently large admissible $X$ and that $f(X)/h(X)\to1$. The source does not specify the domains or allowable values of $a,b,C$, the admissible values of $q$ and $X$, or whether $q$ is fixed during this limit. Its stated dependence of the constants on $G,c$ leaves their possible dependence on $q$, the permutation action, and other counting data unresolved. No uniformity in $q$ is specified.

The condition on ramification also requires clarification: the record does not specify which branch points are constrained, how monodromy elements are chosen, or hypotheses concerning characteristic, tame ramification, and wild ramification. It imposes no generating or nonemptiness condition on $c$, nor other conditions ensuring that the proposed asymptotic is applicable. These omissions prevent a fully specified mathematical conjecture; the displayed formula preserves the supplied target without resolving them.

Short English statement

The proposed refined function-field Malle-type question asks whether counts of geometrically connected covers, with ramification restricted by $c$, have a power-of-$X$ asymptotic with a logarithmic factor. The supplied record leaves the counting function and several hypotheses undefined, so this is a conditional statement template requiring mathematical review. The supplied partially_solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
