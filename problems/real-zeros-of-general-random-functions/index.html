Real Zeros of General Random Functions

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns the number of real zeros of random functions, with random polynomials mentioned in its background. It does not specify a family of functions, their probability laws, or their domains. Consequently, it supplies a research programme rather than a fully determined mathematical problem.

To express the target conditionally, let $f_n$ be a specified family of random functions on specified sets $D_n\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$. If distinct zeros are intended, define \[ N_n=\#\{x\in D_n:f_n(x)=0\}. \] This is a possible counting convention, not a convention established by the supplied record. Counting zeros with multiplicity would require a different definition and sufficient regularity to define multiplicity. Measurability and almost-sure finiteness of the count must also be established for the chosen model.

Conditional mathematical target. For each intended model and counting convention, determine the expectation ${\mathbb{E}}[N_n]$, the variance \[ \operatorname{Var}(N_n)={\mathbb{E}}\left[(N_n-{\mathbb{E}}[N_n])^2\right], \] when these quantities are defined, and their asymptotic behaviour in a specified limiting regime. Determine suitable deterministic centering constants $a_n\in{\mathbb{R}}$, scales $b_n>0$, and a probability law $L$, if such choices exist, for which \[ \frac{N_n-a_n}{b_n}\xrightarrow{\mathrm d}L \qquad(n\to\infty). \] The sequential indexing, limiting regime, and affine normalization above are an explanatory framework; the record does not specify them or assert that such a limit exists. In particular, no Gaussian limit is stipulated.

Unresolved source qualifications. The original excerpt refers to $N_{n,\xi}$ without defining either the random function or $\xi$. It contains an OCR-corrupted logarithmic bound and an expectation formula attributed to Kac, involving integrals and $p(t,0,y)$. The bound cannot be recovered reliably from the supplied text, and the meaning of $p$ and the hypotheses of the formula are absent. Neither is asserted here as a mathematical result. The intended classes of functions, coefficient assumptions, zero-counting convention, observation domain, and asymptotic parameter remain unspecified.

Short English statement

For specified random-function models, determine the mean, variance, and suitably normalized limiting distribution of their real-zero counts. The supplied record leaves the models, counting convention, and limiting regime unspecified, so this is a conditional formulation requiring source clarification and human mathematical review.
