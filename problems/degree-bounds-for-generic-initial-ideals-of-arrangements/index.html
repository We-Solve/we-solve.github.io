Math-7399

Degree Bounds for Generic Initial Ideals of Arrangements

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A central hyperplane arrangement $\mathcal A$ in $K^\ell$ is a finite collection of hyperplanes through the origin, where $K$ is a field. Put $S=K[x_1,\ldots,x_\ell]$, with total-degree grading. If $\alpha_H$ is a nonzero linear form defining $H\in\mathcal A$, its defining polynomial is \[ Q(\mathcal A)=\prod_{H\in\mathcal A}\alpha_H. \] The supplied record uses $J(\mathcal A)$ without defining it. Two conventions requiring clarification are the ideal generated by the partial derivatives of $Q(\mathcal A)$, and the ideal generated by $Q(\mathcal A)$ together with those derivatives: \[ J_{\partial}=\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x_1},\ldots,\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x_\ell}\right),\qquad J_{Q,\partial}=(Q)+J_{\partial}. \] These conventions agree when $\deg Q$ is invertible in $K$, by Euler's identity; the record supplies no characteristic hypothesis ensuring this.

The notation $\operatorname{rgin}$ conventionally denotes the generic initial ideal for graded reverse lexicographic order, ordinarily with $x_1>\cdots>x_\ell$: it is the monomial initial ideal obtained after a sufficiently general invertible linear change of coordinates. The record does not explicitly specify this order, a field hypothesis supporting the generic-coordinate construction over $K$, or an extension-field convention. These choices must be confirmed rather than treated as additional source hypotheses.

With the intended meanings of $J(\mathcal A)$ and $\operatorname{rgin}$ fixed, write \[ I=\operatorname{rgin}(J(\mathcal A)),\qquad D=\{d\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}:x_1^d\in I\}. \] A minimal monomial generator of $I$ is a monomial in its unique minimal monomial generating set, denoted $G(I)$. A monomial $t=x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_\ell^{a_\ell}$ involves $x_3$ precisely when $a_3>0$, equivalently $x_3\mid t$; its degree is $\deg t=\sum_i a_i$.

Source conjecture, subject to the unresolved conventions above. For every central arrangement in the intended class, set $d_0=\min D$. Must \[ \forall t\in G(I),\qquad x_3\mid t\ \Longrightarrow\ \deg t\geq d_0 \] hold?

This formulation requires $\ell\geq 3$ for $x_3$ to exist and $D\neq\varnothing$ for $d_0$ to be defined. Neither requirement is explicitly stated in the supplied record. No value is assigned to $\min\varnothing$, and no claim is made here that the missing conditions follow from the supplied hypotheses. The source does not specify whether empty arrangements or other degenerate cases are excluded.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every minimal monomial generator involving the third variable has degree at least the least exponent of a pure power of the first variable in the generic initial ideal of the arrangement's Jacobian ideal. The field hypotheses, ideal and generic-initial-ideal conventions, and existence of that least exponent require clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
