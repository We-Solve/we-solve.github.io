Math-3577

Slice-Genus Bounds for Homogeneous Braid Quasimorphisms

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be a fixed integer, and let $B_n$ denote the braid group on $n$ strands. The supplied statement leaves the range and quantification of $n$ implicit; the formulation below uses the usual interpretation that the question is asked separately for each positive integer $n$. For $\beta\in B_n$, let $\widehat\beta\subset S^3$ be its oriented braid closure.

A quasimorphism on a group $G$ is a function $f:G\to{\mathbb{R}}$ with finite defect \[ D_f:=\sup_{a,b\in G}|f(ab)-f(a)-f(b)|<\infty. \] It is homogeneous if $f(g^k)=k f(g)$ for every $g\in G$ and every $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. The supplied transcription truncates the homogeneity definition; this is its standard interpretation.

A link is a nonempty oriented smooth closed one-dimensional submanifold $L\subset S^3$. Define \[ \chi_4(L):=\max\left\{\chi(F): F\subset B^4\text{ is a compact smooth properly embedded oriented surface,} \ \partial F=L\subset\partial B^4=S^3,\text{ and }F\text{ has no closed components}\right\}. \] The surfaces may be disconnected, and their boundary orientations must agree with the orientation of $L$. For a knot $K$, its smooth slice genus $g_4(K)$ is the minimum genus of a compact connected smooth properly embedded oriented surface in $B^4$ with oriented boundary $K$; thus $2g_4(K)=1-\chi_4(K)$.

Question. Is the following assertion true for each fixed positive integer $n$? \[ \forall f:B_n\to{\mathbb{R}}\text{ homogeneous quasimorphism},\quad \exists A,C\in{\mathbb{R}}\quad\forall\beta\in B_n,\qquad |f(\beta)|\leq A\chi_4(\widehat\beta)+C. \] The constants may depend on $f$ and the fixed $n$, but not on $\beta$. No sign restriction on $A$ or $C$ is stated in the source.

Supplied background and special case. Let $\operatorname{wr}:B_n\to{\mathbb{Z}}$ be the writhe homomorphism, assigning $1$ to each standard positive braid generator. The record attributes the slice-Bennequin inequality \[ |\operatorname{wr}(\beta)|\leq n-\chi_4(\widehat\beta) \qquad(\beta\in B_n) \] to references labelled [Rud93, KM94]. It also describes the fractional Dehn twist coefficient $\omega:B_n\to{\mathbb{R}}$ as a homogeneous quasimorphism and attributes to [Fel22, Theorem 3] the special-case bound \[ |\omega(\beta)|\leq n-\chi_4(\widehat\beta) \qquad(\beta\in B_n). \] These are supplied contextual assertions, not results verified in this draft. The record does not provide a definition or normalization of $\omega$, or full bibliographic entries for the bracketed references. It cites [GO89, Mal04, HKM07, HKM08, FH19] for relations of this coefficient to knots, three-manifolds, and contact topology.

Short English statement

For each fixed number of braid strands, ask whether every homogeneous quasimorphism is bounded in absolute value by an affine function of the maximal Euler characteristic of a smooth oriented surface in the four-ball bounded by the braid closure. The constants may depend on the quasimorphism and strand number. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved, citing a bound for the fractional Dehn twist coefficient while describing the universal question as unresolved; this assessment has not been independently verified.
