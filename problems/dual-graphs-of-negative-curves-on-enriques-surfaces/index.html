Dual Graphs of Negative Curves on Enriques Surfaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The Enriques lattice is conventionally written \[ L_{\mathrm{Enr}}=U\oplus E_8(-1), \] where $U$ is the rank-two lattice with Gram matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, and $E_8(-1)$ is the negative-definite $E_8$ root lattice. A root is an element $r\in L_{\mathrm{Enr}}$ satisfying $(r,r)=-2$.

In the usual geometric interpretation, a $(-2)$-curve on a smooth projective surface is a smooth rational curve $C$ with self-intersection $C^2=-2$. For a finite collection of distinct such curves $C_1,\ldots,C_n$, its intersection data are \[ C_i^2=-2,\qquad m_{ij}=C_i\cdot C_j\quad(i\ne j). \] A dual graph may encode these data by vertices corresponding to the curves and intersection labels $m_{ij}$. Correspondingly, roots $r_1,\ldots,r_n$ in $L_{\mathrm{Enr}}$ encode these data when $(r_i,r_j)=m_{ij}$. This explains the customary intersection interpretation; the supplied record does not specify the intended graph conventions or the precise lattice data retained by an abstract graph.

Classification and realization problem. Classify the abstract good dual graphs in the Enriques lattice, and find Enriques surfaces realizing the corresponding $(-2)$-curve configurations.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define the predicate "good." Consequently, it does not determine the class of graphs to be classified. It also leaves unspecified the ground field and characteristic, the intended convention for Enriques surfaces, whether only finite configurations are considered, and whether classification is up to graph isomorphism or up to lattice isometry of embedded configurations. For a fixed geometric setting and a finite graph carrying intersection labels $m_{ij}$, realization in the intersection sense means the existence of an Enriques surface $X$ and distinct $(-2)$-curves $C_1,\ldots,C_n\subset X$ satisfying $C_i\cdot C_j=m_{ij}$ for every $i\ne j$. The record does not state whether a prescribed lattice embedding must also be realized, or whether every abstract good graph is expected to be realizable. These missing qualifications prevent a fully specified quantified classification statement.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a classification of abstract good dual graphs in the Enriques lattice and for Enriques surfaces containing the corresponding configurations of $(-2)$-curves. The meaning of "good," the geometric setting, and the equivalence and realization requirements must be recovered from the source before the target can be made fully precise.
