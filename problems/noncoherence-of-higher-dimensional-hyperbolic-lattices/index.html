Math-864

Noncoherence of Higher-Dimensional Hyperbolic Lattices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq4$, let $\mathbb H^n$ be real hyperbolic $n$-space and $\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$ its full isometry group. A lattice $\Gamma$ is a discrete subgroup of finite Haar covolume. Uniform means cocompact; nonuniform means finite covolume with noncompact quotient. The question imposes neither choice and does not assume arithmeticity, torsion-freeness or orientation preservation.

A group $\Gamma$ is coherent if every finitely generated subgroup $H\leq\Gamma$ is finitely presented. It is noncoherent if some subgroup $H\leq\Gamma$ is generated by finitely many elements but admits no presentation with finitely many generators and relations. Thus the conjectural assertion is \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq4}\ \forall\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n) \text{ a lattice}\ \exists H\leq\Gamma, \quad H\text{ finitely generated but not finitely presented}. \] The subgroup $H$ may depend on the lattice and dimension. No bound on its number of generators and no universal isomorphism type for the obstruction subgroup is requested. Noncoherence is not the assertion that $\Gamma$ itself fails to be finitely presented, nor is the existence of a merely infinitely generated subgroup sufficient.

The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports noncoherence for substantial families, mentioning uniform arithmetic lattices in every dimension at least four and all nonuniform arithmetic lattices in dimensions at least six. It says the assertion for every lattice is not known. The wording does not list the exact uniform arithmetic families, so it is not strengthened to a theorem for every uniform arithmetic lattice. The reported all-nonuniform-arithmetic statement is retained with its dimension threshold $n\geq6$, not extended to dimensions four or five. These are attributed partial-progress claims and do not restrict the universal question to arithmetic cases.

The partially-solved label and editorial verification note are supplied, dated metadata. No noncoherent subgroup is constructed and no current theorem status is independently checked here.

Short English statement

Does every finite-covolume real-hyperbolic lattice in dimension at least four contain a finitely generated subgroup that is not finitely presented? The question includes both compact and noncompact quotients and arbitrary lattices. The source's reported arithmetic families remain partial progress rather than added hypotheses.
