Math-4876

Elementary Equivalence of Universal Sofic Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks whether all universal sofic groups $S_U$ are elementarily equivalent. The record does not define $S_U$ or specify the logical language. The following gives a conventional construction and a precise conditional formulation; its identification with the intended source formulation requires review.

For each integer $n\geq 1$, let $\operatorname{Sym}(n)$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, with normalized Hamming distance \[ d_n(\sigma,\tau)=\frac{1}{n}\left|\{i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}:\sigma(i)\ne\tau(i)\}\right|. \] Let $U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on the positive integers, meaning an ultrafilter containing no finite set. Define the normal subgroup \[ N_U=\left\{(\sigma_n)_{n\geq1}\in\prod_{n\geq1}\operatorname{Sym}(n):\lim_{n\to U}d_n(\sigma_n,1)=0\right\} \] and the metric ultraproduct \[ S_U=\left(\prod_{n\geq1}\operatorname{Sym}(n)\right)/N_U, \qquad d_U([\sigma_n],[\tau_n])=\lim_{n\to U}d_n(\sigma_n,\tau_n). \] Here $\lim_{n\to U}$ denotes the ultrafilter limit of a bounded real sequence.

In the ordinary first-order language of groups $L_{\mathrm{grp}}=\{\cdot,{}^{-1},1\}$, two groups are elementarily equivalent if they satisfy exactly the same first-order sentences without parameters. Under the construction above and this language convention, the question is whether \[ \text{for every pair of nonprincipal ultrafilters }U,V\text{ on the positive integers,} \quad S_U\equiv_{L_{\mathrm{grp}}}S_V. \] Equivalently, for every such $U,V$ and every $L_{\mathrm{grp}}$-sentence $\varphi$, does \[ S_U\models\varphi\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S_V\models\varphi \] hold?

If elementary equivalence is instead intended in continuous logic for groups equipped with their normalized Hamming metric, the corresponding question is whether every continuous sentence in that metric group language has the same value in $(S_U,d_U)$ and $(S_V,d_V)$, for every such $U,V$. These are distinct interpretations of the question; the supplied record does not resolve the choice. It also does not specify whether other sequences of permutation degrees are included in the quantifier over universal sofic groups.

Short English statement

Do all universal sofic groups satisfy the same logical sentences? Using the conventional ultraproduct of finite symmetric groups, this asks whether changing the nonprincipal ultrafilter changes the elementary theory. The source record leaves the construction conventions and the choice between ordinary group logic and continuous metric logic unspecified.
