Open Problems in Pluripotential Theory — Question 32

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A quasi-plurisubharmonic function is a function locally expressible as the sum of a plurisubharmonic function and a smooth real-valued function, with the usual exclusion of functions identically $-\infty$ on a connected component. Write $dd^c$ using a fixed consistent normalization, and let $\nu(R,x)$ denote the Lelong number of a positive closed $(1,1)$-current $R$ at $x$.

The supplied excerpt uses an ambient complex manifold $X$, a form $\omega$, and a quasi-plurisubharmonic function $\varphi$, but does not state their full hypotheses. To express its proposed conclusion, suppose the datum admits the positive closed $(1,1)$-current \[ T=\omega+dd^c\varphi. \] For such a datum, the requested desingularization property is the following: \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every }\varepsilon>0\text{ there exist a finite composition of blow-ups}\quad \pi_\varepsilon:X_\varepsilon\longrightarrow X,\\ D_\varepsilon=\sum_{j=1}^{m_\varepsilon}a_{\varepsilon,j}D_{\varepsilon,j}, \qquad a_{\varepsilon,j}>0, \qquad\text{and a positive closed }(1,1)\text{-current }R_\varepsilon,\\ \pi_\varepsilon^*T=[D_\varepsilon]+R_\varepsilon, \qquad \nu(R_\varepsilon,x)<\varepsilon\quad\text{for every }x\in X_\varepsilon. \end{gathered} \] Here $X_\varepsilon$ is smooth, the $D_{\varepsilon,j}$ are distinct irreducible hypersurfaces, and their union has simple normal crossings: locally it is a union of coordinate hyperplanes. The sum may be empty. The current $[D_\varepsilon]$ means $\sum_j a_{\varepsilon,j}[D_{\varepsilon,j}]$, where $[D_{\varepsilon,j}]$ is the current of integration over that hypersurface.

Question 32, attributed in the source to Favre--Guedj--Jonsson. Does this property hold in every complex dimension $n=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X\geq3$, for all data in the intended ambient setting of the source?

The supplied excerpt reports that the property holds when $n\leq2$, citing Blel--Mimouni [BM05], Guedj [G05b], and Favre--Jonsson [FJ05]. This is a supplied background assertion. The excerpt does not specify the ambient class of $X$, the assumptions on $\omega$, or the permitted blow-up centers. Consequently, the quantified desingularization property above is explicit, but the class of initial data over which the question is intended to range remains unresolved; in particular, no assertion for all complex manifolds is being substituted for the source question.

Short English statement

The question asks whether, in complex dimension at least three, finitely many blow-ups can separate the singularities of the given current into a divisor with simple normal crossings and a positive remainder whose Lelong number is strictly less than any prescribed $\varepsilon>0$ at every point. The supplied excerpt reports this in dimensions at most two, but omits ambient hypotheses needed to fix the exact higher-dimensional statement.
