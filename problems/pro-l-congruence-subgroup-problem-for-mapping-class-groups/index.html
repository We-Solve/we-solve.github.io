Math-14659

Pro-l Congruence Subgroup Problem for Mapping Class Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a prime $\ell$, a pro-$\ell$ group is an inverse limit of finite groups whose orders are powers of $\ell$. The supplied record asks about a representation denoted by $\rho^{\ell\text{-csp}}_{g,r}$, described as the pro-$\ell$ congruence-subgroup representation associated with the congruence subgroup problem for mapping class groups. However, the record does not define this representation, its domain or codomain, or the admissible values and hypotheses for $g,r,\ell$. Thus the following records the requested property without supplying a missing construction.

Write $D=\operatorname{dom}(\rho^{\ell\text{-csp}}_{g,r})$ solely as placeholder notation for the unspecified domain. The question is whether \[ \forall x,y\in D,\qquad \rho^{\ell\text{-csp}}_{g,r}(x)=\rho^{\ell\text{-csp}}_{g,r}(y) \ \Longrightarrow\ x=y. \] A fully specified mathematical problem requires the definition of $\rho^{\ell\text{-csp}}_{g,r}$ and the intended parameter range; neither can be recovered from the supplied statement alone.

The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports injectivity in several cases, including genus zero and genus at most two for $\ell=2$, and non-injectivity in genus one for $\ell>7$. It also reports that no general answer is known. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified assertions here. Their full hypotheses, including any restrictions involving $r$, are absent from the record.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the representation $\rho^{\ell\text{-csp}}_{g,r}$ distinguishes every pair of elements in its domain. The representation and its parameter restrictions are missing, so its precise formulation remains unresolved. The supplied curation reports both positive and negative cases.
