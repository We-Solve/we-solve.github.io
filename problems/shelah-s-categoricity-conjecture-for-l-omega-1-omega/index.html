OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 240; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that in ZFC, each bound on Löwenheim--Skolem number has a uniform threshold above which categoricity in one cardinal implies categoricity throughout the same tail.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For every infinite cardinal $\lambda$, the claim gives a uniform cardinal threshold $\mu(\lambda)\ge\lambda$ for all abstract elementary classes $K$ of Löwenheim--Skolem number at most $\lambda$: \[ (\exists\kappa\ge\mu(\lambda):I(K,\kappa)=1) \Longrightarrow(\forall\kappa'\ge\mu(\lambda):I(K,\kappa')=1). \] Here $I(K,\kappa)$ is the number of model isomorphism classes of cardinality $\kappa$. The same threshold occurs in the premise and conclusion. A second formulation asserts that categoricity in unboundedly many cardinals forces categoricity on a tail, without adding amalgamation or tameness assumptions.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 240; 2026-09-24), fixed order-indexed presentations encode short model orbits. A direct reversal theorem for strong maps preserves bounded tuple records; elimination and stabilization of strong-map classes then create the stable diagrams and closed-level amalgamation needed for tail transfer.
