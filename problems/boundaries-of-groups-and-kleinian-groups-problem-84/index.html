Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 84

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a group and let $\rho_1,\rho_2:G\to\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$ be discrete faithful representations, with $\mathbb H^n$ read as real hyperbolic space. Define their translation lengths by \[ \ell_{\rho_i}(g)=\inf_{x\in\mathbb H^n}d(\rho_i(g)x,x). \] The source's comparison formula is damaged in extraction. If its intended meaning is two-sided multiplicative comparison, a division-free formulation is \[ C^{-1}\ell_{\rho_2}(g)\le\ell_{\rho_1}(g) \le C\ell_{\rho_2}(g)\qquad(g\in G), \] for a constant $C>0$. This also specifies agreement of the zero-length sets. A ratio formulation instead needs an explicit restriction to positive lengths, since at least the identity has length zero and parabolic or elliptic elements can as well. The all-element convention must be confirmed; it is not silently inferred from the malformed display.

Let $\Lambda_i=\Lambda(\rho_i(G))\subseteq\partial_\infty\mathbb H^n$ be the limit sets, namely accumulation points at infinity of group orbits. Under the intended length-comparison hypothesis, the question asks whether there exists an appropriate quasiconformal map $f:\Lambda_1\to\Lambda_2$ satisfying \[ f(\rho_1(g)\xi)=\rho_2(g)f(\xi) \qquad(g\in G,\ \xi\in\Lambda_1), \] so that it is equivariant for $\rho_2\circ\rho_1^{-1}$. It further asks whether $f$ can be chosen with distortion $K=K(C)$.

The record does not define quasiconformality on these possibly non-open, fractal limit sets. Possible meanings include an intrinsic metric condition or restriction of a quasiconformal map of the ambient boundary sphere; these are not identified here. The source does not explicitly specify that $f$ is a homeomorphism, although its conjugacy language suggests that intention. The requested uniform dependence on $C$, and any allowed dimension dependence, require confirmation. No finite generation, non-elementarity, geometric finiteness, or convex cocompactness assumption is added.

Short English statement

Does comparable marked translation length force an equivariant quasiconformal identification of the limit sets, with distortion controlled only by the comparison constant? The corrupted inequality, zero translation lengths, and meaning of quasiconformality on a limit set are essential formulation issues.

The supplied summary calls this an open rigidity question and mentions Markovic, Cannon--Thurston/quasiconformal rigidity, and real-tree length spectra without a precise theorem or reference. The OPEN-TRIAGE label and historical 2005 problem are retained with current status marked for review.
