Problem 3.2 — Compute the (outer) automorphism group of Mod(N ).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Mod}(N)$ be the mapping class group of the surface $N$ in the source's conventions. The extract does not define $N$; the research summary discusses nonorientable surfaces, but supplies no genus, punctures, boundary data or mapping-class conventions.

For a group $G$, $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is its group of abstract automorphisms, $\operatorname{Inn}(G)$ consists of conjugations $x\mapsto gxg^{-1}$, and \[ \operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G), \qquad\operatorname{Inn}(G)\cong G/Z(G). \] Problem 3.2 on PDF page 98 asks to compute the "(outer) automorphism group" of $\operatorname{Mod}(N)$. Thus the relevant targets are $\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{Mod}(N))$ and/or $\operatorname{Out}(\operatorname{Mod}(N))$, according to the parenthetical convention. An exact answer must specify the intended one and all allowed $N$, including small or exceptional topological types, rather than replace the question by a large-genus rigidity assertion.

The summary reports Ivanov-type results for nonorientable mapping class groups and says the full computation for all $N$ is not settled, but gives no precise range or primary reference. These are supplied status descriptions, not an independently verified all-surface classification.

Short English statement

Compute the automorphism or outer automorphism group of the source's mapping class group $\operatorname{Mod}(N)$. The surface class and parenthetical choice of target are missing; the summary suggests nonorientable surfaces but does not give precise theorem ranges.
