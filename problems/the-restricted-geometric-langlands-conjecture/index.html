The Restricted Geometric Langlands Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 014; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that restricted geometric Langlands has full spectral support in positive characteristic in the two stated characteristic regimes, with additional very-good and Weyl-order conditions over arbitrary algebraically closed fields.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $p>0$, $\ell\ne p$, $E=\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}$, $X/k$ smooth projective connected and $G/k$ connected reductive. Require the four common conditions: an invariant nondegenerate Lie-algebra form nondegenerate on every Levi center, Chevalley restriction for every Levi, semisimple Lie-algebra centralizers being Levi, and nilpotent elements over every extension lying in parabolic unipotent-radical Lie algebras. Regime A additionally requires $k=\overline{\mathbb F}_q$ and descent of $X$ and split $G$ to $\mathbb F_q$. Regime B instead permits arbitrary algebraically closed $k$, but requires $p$ very good for $G$ and $p\nmid|W_G|$. Very good excludes primes dividing $n+1$ for $A_n$, $2$ for $B,C,D$, $2,3$ for $G_2,F_4,E_6,E_7$, and $2,3,5$ for $E_8$.

Let $Y=\operatorname{LS}_{\widehat G}^{\rm restr}(X)$ be the restricted spectral prestack, parametrizing right-t-exact symmetric monoidal functors from dual-group representations to $\operatorname{QCoh}(T)\otimes_E\operatorname{QLisse}_E(X)$ on derived affine $T$. The claim says the previously occurring open-and-closed union $Y'$ equals all of $Y$, giving a $\operatorname{QCoh}(Y)$-linear equivalence \[ \mathbb L_G^{\rm restr}:\operatorname{Shv}_{\rm Nilp}(\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)) \xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{IndCoh}_{\rm Nilp}(Y). \] The automorphic and spectral categories each retain nilpotent singular support. This positive-characteristic result is restricted to the two stated regimes.
