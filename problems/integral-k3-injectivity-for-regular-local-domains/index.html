Integral K3 injectivity for regular local domains

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. A different two-dimensional ramified ring in mixed characteristic $(0,5)$ is claimed to violate integral Gersten injectivity in degree three.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $\zeta$ be a primitive fifth root of unity, $V=\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q_5(\zeta)}$, and $\pi=1-\zeta$. In $\mathbb P^2_V$, take the hypersurface $(X-Z)^5-XY^4+Y^5+\zeta Z^5=0$, and localize at its special-fiber point $[0:0:1]$, obtaining $A$. The source claims that $A$ is a regular Noetherian local domain of dimension two and mixed characteristic $(0,5)$, with $\pi\in\mathfrak m_A^2$, and some integral class $c\in K_3(A)$ satisfies \[ c|_{\operatorname{Frac}A}=0,\qquad c|_{A[1/\pi]}\bmod5\ne0\text{ in }G_3(A[1/\pi];\mathbb Z/5). \] Thus the integral restriction $K_3(A)\to K_3(\operatorname{Frac}A)$ has nonzero kernel.

Outline of the argument

A divisor on a cyclotomic quintic supplies a Bott-symbol class whose coefficient boundary vanishes by a Steinberg relation. The resulting integral lift is generically zero by support, and a norm-valuation test detects it using the different residue-degree behavior of closed points.
