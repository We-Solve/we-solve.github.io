A finite-group Cartan-Leray spectral sequence for Morel's A1-homology

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The reconstructed question, attributed to Kirsten Wickelgren, is whether there is a Serre spectral sequence for Morel's $H_*^{{\mathbb{A}}^1}(-,{\mathbb{Z}})$. The original extraction loses letters and the homology subscript/argument. For a field $k$, let $\operatorname{Ab}_{\mathrm{Nis}}(\operatorname{Sm}_k)$ denote abelian Nisnevich sheaves on smooth $k$-schemes. For a simplicial Nisnevich sheaf $X$, the transfer-free ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-homology sheaves are \[ H_q^{{\mathbb{A}}^1}(X,{\mathbb{Z}})=\mathcal H_q\left(L_{{\mathbb{A}}^1}C_*({\mathbb{Z}}[X])\right), \] with Nisnevich sheafification and ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-localization of the chain complex. These are not homology with transfers silently substituted. A general Serre construction would have to specify the class of ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-fiber sequences, twisting or monodromy, its $E^2$ page and convergence; none is specified in the original question.

The supplied report gives a homotopy-quotient special case. Over a perfect field $k$, let a finite constant group $\Gamma$ act strictly on a simplicial Nisnevich sheaf $X$. Let $E\Gamma$ be a free contractible simplicial $\Gamma$-object and put $X_{h\Gamma}=E\Gamma\times_\Gamma X$. It claims a natural strongly convergent first-quadrant homological spectral sequence \[ E^2_{p,q}=H_p\left(\Gamma;H_q^{{\mathbb{A}}^1}(X,{\mathbb{Z}})\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad H_{p+q}^{{\mathbb{A}}^1}(X_{h\Gamma},{\mathbb{Z}}),\qquad p,q\geq0. \] Here $H_p(\Gamma;M)$ means derived coinvariants in the category of abelian Nisnevich sheaves, namely the homology sheaf of ${\mathbb{Z}}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{{\mathbb{Z}}[\Gamma]}M$. It is not merely group homology of field-valued sections.

If $X$ is ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-connected and its basepoint is fixed by $\Gamma$, the report claims the degree-one edge extension splits and gives \[ H_1^{{\mathbb{A}}^1}(X_{h\Gamma},{\mathbb{Z}})\cong \left(H_1^{{\mathbb{A}}^1}(X,{\mathbb{Z}})\right)_\Gamma\oplus \underline{\Gamma_{\mathrm{ab}}}, \] where $\underline{\Gamma_{\mathrm{ab}}}$ is the constant abelian Nisnevich sheaf. The fixed basepoint is part of the claimed splitting, and ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-connectedness supplies the constant degree-zero coefficient. No order-invertibility assumption is added to the report's finite-constant-group statement. The report expressly does not construct a spectral sequence for arbitrary ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-fiber sequences; proofs and exact model/localization conventions remain to be checked.

Short English statement

Seek a Serre spectral sequence for Morel's transfer-free ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-homology. The supplied partial result is a finite-constant-group homotopy-quotient spectral sequence over a perfect field, using sheaf-derived coinvariants, with a split degree-one formula for equivariantly pointed ${\mathbb{A}}^1$-connected spaces. It leaves the general fiber-sequence question open.
