Math-14510

Minimax Estimation of Queue Service-Time Distributions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual meaning of an $M/G/\infty$ queue, customers arrive according to a homogeneous Poisson process of rate $\lambda>0$. Their service times are independent and identically distributed nonnegative random variables, independent of the arrival process, with distribution function \[ G(t)=\mathbb{P}(S\leq t),\qquad t\geq0. \] There are infinitely many servers, so each customer begins service immediately. Let $X(t)$ denote the number of customers in service at time $t$. The observations concern this queue-length process; the record does not specify continuous observation, discrete sampling, the observation horizon, or the initial state.

A conditional mathematical formulation of the requested minimax problem is as follows. Once an observation scheme indexed by $r$, a parameter class $\Theta$, and a nonnegative loss function $L$ have been specified, let $Y_r$ denote the observed queue-length data and let $\mathbb{P}_{\theta,r}$ be their law for $\theta\in\Theta$. Write $G_\theta$ for the service-time distribution function associated with $\theta$; the parameter $\theta$ also includes any unknown arrival-rate or initialization parameters. Define \[ R_r^*(\Theta,L) = \inf_{\widehat G_r}\sup_{\theta\in\Theta} \mathbb{E}_{\theta,r}\!\left[ L\left(\widehat G_r(Y_r),G_\theta\right) \right], \] where the infimum is over measurable estimators of the service-time distribution based on $Y_r$.

Problem. Determine the optimal achievable minimax accuracy for estimating $G$ from queue-length observations of the $M/G/\infty$ queue, expressed through $R_r^*(\Theta,L)$ for a specified statistical experiment. The supplied record does not determine that experiment, the parameter class, the loss, or an asymptotic regime. Consequently, this formula describes the requested family of problems, but the record does not specify a single minimax risk or rate to determine. No stationarity, finite-mean, smoothness, or tail assumption is imposed here on behalf of the source.

Short English statement

Estimate the service-time distribution of an $M/G/\infty$ queue using observations of how many customers are in service, and determine the best possible worst-case estimation accuracy. A precise accuracy statement requires an observation scheme, an admissible distribution class, and a loss function, which the supplied record leaves unspecified.
