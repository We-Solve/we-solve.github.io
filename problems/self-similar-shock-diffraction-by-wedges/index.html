Math-4667

Self-Similar Shock Diffraction by Wedges

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix arbitrary parameters $\gamma>1$ and $\rho_0>0$. Let $\Lambda\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ be the domain described in the supplied record as the upper half-plane outside a wedge. The wedge angle, orientation, vertex, and precise domain are not specified. Write $\xi=(\xi_1,\xi_2)$ for the self-similar spatial variables, and let $D$ and $\operatorname{div}$ denote differentiation with respect to $\xi$. Let $\Gamma_{\mathrm w}$ denote the wedge portion of $\partial\Lambda$, and let $\nu$ be a unit normal there wherever defined.

For a real-valued potential $\phi$, set \[ H_\phi=\rho_0-(\gamma-1)\left(\phi+\frac12|D\phi|^2\right), \qquad \rho_\phi=\rho(|D\phi|^2,\phi)=H_\phi^{1/(\gamma-1)}. \] The physical, real, nonnegative-density interpretation requires $H_\phi\geq0$ wherever this expression is used. Whether the intended problem requires strictly positive density or permits vacuum is not specified.

Requested existence problem. Seek a self-similar weak solution $\phi$ on $\Lambda$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{div}(\rho_\phi D\phi)+2\rho_\phi=0 \quad\text{in }\Lambda, \qquad D\phi\cdot\nu=0 \quad\text{on }\Gamma_{\mathrm w}, \] and approaching at infinity the two incident constant-state potentials separated by the incident shock. Those potentials, their parameters, the incident shock, and the meaning of this far-field convergence are not specified in the supplied record.

At the level of the interior equation alone, its distributional interpretation is \[ \int_\Lambda\left(\rho_\phi D\phi\cdot D\eta-2\rho_\phi\eta\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\xi=0 \qquad\text{for every }\eta\in C_c^\infty(\Lambda), \] provided $\rho_\phi$ and $\rho_\phi D\phi$ are locally integrable. This identity does not specify the intended solution regularity, boundary traces, or shock admissibility. The record also does not state whether a boundary condition is required on any remaining portion of the boundary of the upper half-plane. Consequently, the supplied formulation describes an existence problem but does not determine a fully specified boundary-value problem or its admissible parameter range.

Short English statement

For given $\gamma>1$ and $\rho_0>0$, seek a self-similar weak potential-flow solution outside a wedge in the upper half-plane. It must satisfy the stated density law and equation, obey slip on the wedge, and approach two incident constant states separated by a shock at infinity. The wedge geometry, incident data, admissibility conditions, and precise far-field requirement remain unspecified and require mathematical review.
