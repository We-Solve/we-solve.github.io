Math-15275

Symplectic Models for Relative Class Group Distributions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a number field $K$, let $\operatorname{Cl}(K)$ denote its ideal class group and let $\mathcal O_K^{\times}$ denote its unit group. For a prime $\ell$ and a finite abelian group $A$, written additively, its $\ell$-primary part is \[ A[\ell^{\infty}]=\{a\in A:\ell^n a=0\text{ for some integer }n\geq 0\}. \] The supplied record does not specify which primes $\ell$ are admissible or what definition of relative class group is intended.

The source proposes the following conjectural comparison: after incorporating the contribution of unit groups, the joint distribution over number fields of the relative class-group $\ell$-part and two additional class-group invariants agrees with the distribution predicted by a random symplectic-similitude model. Its intended mathematical form can therefore be recorded only schematically as \[ \mu_{\mathrm{arithmetic}}=\mu_{\mathrm{symplectic\text{-}similitude}}, \] where both sides are intended to describe the same triple of invariants, with the unit-group contribution incorporated as required by the source.

This is not yet a fully specified equality of probability measures: the record supplies neither the family of number fields and underlying extensions, nor their ordering or probability law, nor the relative class-group convention, nor the two accompanying invariants. It also does not define the unit-group contribution, the parameters and probability law of the random symplectic-similitude model, or the procedure extracting the triple from that model. These omissions prevent a precise quantified conjecture from being recovered from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

The proposed conjecture compares the joint distribution of three class-group invariants over number fields with a random symplectic-similitude prediction, taking unit groups into account. The supplied record leaves the field family, two of the invariants, and the model unspecified, so it determines only a schematic statement requiring mathematical review.
