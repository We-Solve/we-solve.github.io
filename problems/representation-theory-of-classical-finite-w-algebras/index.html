Representation Theory of Classical Finite W-Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $U(\mathfrak g,e)$ for the finite $W$-algebra associated with a Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ and a nilpotent element $e\in\mathfrak g$, once the ground field, admissible hypotheses, and construction have been specified. This notation identifies the usual input data; the supplied record does not specify those choices.

Source problem. Determine the structure and representation theory of finite $W$-algebras associated with classical Lie algebras.

Unresolved scope. The record does not specify the ground field or its characteristic, the precise class of classical Lie algebras, the nilpotent elements under consideration, or the intended categories of representations. It also does not specify what constitutes determining the structure or representation theory, such as presentations, classification of modules, or character formulas. Consequently, this is a research programme rather than a uniquely specified proposition or classification problem. No additional hypotheses, quantifiers, or completion criterion are imposed in this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks for an understanding of the algebras and representations of finite $W$-algebras attached to classical Lie algebras. Its intended scope and required outputs remain unspecified, so a precise classification target requires human clarification.
