Enumeration of Matchings — Problem 13

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite square-grid region encoded by a finite set $R\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}^2$ of unit cells, a domino tiling is a partition of $R$ into two-element sets $\{(i,j),(k,\ell)\}$ satisfying $|i-k|+|j-\ell|=1$. Define \[ T(R)=\#\left\{\mathcal D:\mathcal D\text{ is a domino tiling of }R\right\}. \] The supplied problem asks for a general formula for the number of domino tilings of even pillows. If these objects are finite square-grid regions, and $\mathcal E$ denotes their intended family, the requested enumeration is a formula for $T(R)$ for every $R\in\mathcal E$.

This is only a conditional formulation: the supplied record does not define a pillow, its boundary, its size parameters, or the meaning of even. Consequently, it does not specify $\mathcal E$, and the intended enumeration problem cannot be stated completely from the supplied material.

Short English statement

Propp's Problem 13 asks for a general formula counting domino tilings of even pillows. The supplied record omits the definition of these regions and the meaning of even, so the family to be counted remains unspecified.
