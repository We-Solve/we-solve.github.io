Math-4210

Non-Liftable Monodromy of Surface Bundles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the conventional interpretation of the surface notation, let $\Sigma_k$ denote a closed, connected, oriented smooth surface of genus $k$. The supplied background specifies closed fibers; closedness and orientability of the base are understood here from the notation and require confirmation. The record gives no explicit permitted ranges for the genera $g$ and $h$.

Let $\operatorname{Diff}^{+}(\Sigma_g)$ be the group of orientation-preserving smooth diffeomorphisms, and let \[ \operatorname{Mod}_g=\pi_0\left(\operatorname{Diff}^{+}(\Sigma_g)\right) \] be its mapping class group. Write $q:\operatorname{Diff}^{+}(\Sigma_g)\to\operatorname{Mod}_g$ for the homomorphism taking a diffeomorphism to its isotopy class. An oriented smooth surface bundle $p:E\to\Sigma_h$ with fiber $\Sigma_g$ determines, after choosing a base point and identifying its fiber with $\Sigma_g$, a monodromy homomorphism \[ \rho:\pi_1(\Sigma_h)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}_g. \] A lift in the category of groups is a group homomorphism $\widetilde\rho:\pi_1(\Sigma_h)\to\operatorname{Diff}^{+}(\Sigma_g)$ satisfying $q\circ\widetilde\rho=\rho$. Here the lifting question concerns abstract groups, with no additional continuity requirement.

A smooth bundle is called flat here if it admits local trivializations whose transition diffeomorphisms are locally constant on overlaps, equivalently a flat smooth bundle connection. It is nonflat if no such structure exists on that bundle.

Existence question. Do there exist genera $g,h$ and an oriented smooth surface bundle \[ \Sigma_g\longrightarrow E\xrightarrow{\ p\ }\Sigma_h \] which is nonflat and whose monodromy satisfies \[ \nexists\,\widetilde\rho:\pi_1(\Sigma_h)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^{+}(\Sigma_g) \quad\text{a group homomorphism with}\quad q\circ\widetilde\rho=\rho\,? \] The intended genus restrictions remain unspecified. Both requirements from the supplied statement are retained; no equivalence between them is asserted here. Nonflatness concerns the bundle itself, without imposing preservation of a distinguished section.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a surface bundle over a surface can have no flat structure and have monodromy that cannot be lifted from mapping classes to actual orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms by a group homomorphism. The intended genus ranges and the conventional interpretation of the base surface require confirmation. Failure to find a flat connection preserving a chosen section does not by itself meet the stated target.
