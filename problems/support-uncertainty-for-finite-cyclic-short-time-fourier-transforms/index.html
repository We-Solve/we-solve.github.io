Math-6680

Support Uncertainty for Finite Cyclic Short-Time Fourier Transforms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group, written additively, and put $n=|G|$. Let ${\mathbb{C}}^G$ be the space of complex-valued functions on $G$, and let $\widehat G$ be its character group. For a function $f$ on a finite set $S$, define \[ \operatorname{supp}(f)=\{s\in S:f(s)\ne0\},\qquad \|f\|_0=|\operatorname{supp}(f)|. \] Using standard unnormalized conventions, define the Fourier transform and the short-time Fourier transform with window $\varphi\in{\mathbb{C}}^G$ by \[ \widehat x(\chi)=\sum_{t\in G}x(t)\overline{\chi(t)},\qquad \chi\in\widehat G, \] \[ V_\varphi x(a,\chi)=\sum_{t\in G}x(t)\overline{\varphi(t-a)}\,\overline{\chi(t)},\qquad (a,\chi)\in G\times\widehat G. \] Thus $\|V_\varphi x\|_0$ counts nonzero coefficients among the $n^2$ time-frequency positions. These conventions supply definitions omitted from the record; nonzero normalization factors do not change support sizes.

The supplied question asks whether, for every finite cyclic group $G$, there exists $\varphi\in{\mathbb{C}}^G\setminus\{0\}$ such that \[ \left\{\left(\|x\|_0,\|V_\varphi x\|_0\right):x\in{\mathbb{C}}^G\right\} = \left\{\left(\|y\|_0,n^2-n+\|\widehat y\|_0\right):y\in{\mathbb{C}}^G\right\}. \] This is equality of attainable sets of pairs; it does not require the same signal to realize corresponding pairs on both sides.

Unresolved boundary qualification. The supplied quantifiers include the zero signal. The unique pair with first coordinate zero on the left is $(0,0)$, whereas the unique such pair on the right is $(0,n^2-n)$. These differ when $n>1$. Consequently, the literal formulation has a zero-signal incompatibility with its presentation as an open uncertainty question. Whether the intended statement excludes zero signals on both sides, treats the zero pair separately, or uses another qualification requires source review. No such repair is adopted here.

Short English statement

For each finite cyclic group, the question seeks a nonzero window whose short-time Fourier transform realizes exactly the support-size pairs obtained by shifting Fourier support sizes by $|G|^2-|G|$. The supplied formulation includes zero signals, creating an unresolved boundary inconsistency for $|G|>1$. Its supplied open status and publication-verification label are not independent mathematical verification.
