Math-4472

Projectivity of the Stein-Factorized Period-Map Completion

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Phi:B\to\mathcal P$ denote the period map referred to in the supplied record, and write its stated Stein factorization as \[ B\xrightarrow{\widehat\Phi}\widehat{\mathcal P}\xrightarrow{\nu}\mathcal P, \qquad \Phi=\nu\circ\widehat\Phi. \] Here the Stein factorization terminology indicates that $\widehat\Phi$ has connected fibers and $\nu$ is finite. Let $X=\widehat{\mathcal P}^{T}$ be the specified compact complex analytic variety containing $\widehat{\mathcal P}$ as an open subspace. A compact complex analytic variety is projective if it admits a closed holomorphic embedding into complex projective space.

Question. Is this specified completion $X$ projective; that is, does there exist an integer $N\geq 0$ and a closed holomorphic embedding \[ \iota:\widehat{\mathcal P}^{T}\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}^{N}({\mathbb{C}}), \] so that $\widehat{\mathcal P}^{T}$ is a projective completion of $\widehat{\mathcal P}$?

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify the base $B$, the underlying variation of Hodge structure, the hypotheses on the period map, or the construction denoted by the superscript $T$. The question therefore concerns the particular completion intended by the source, whose full definition is missing here, and does not quantify over arbitrary compact analytic spaces containing $\widehat{\mathcal P}$. A completion ordinarily requires a dense open inclusion; the displayed source statement explicitly asserts only openness. It also does not specify whether this inclusion is analytically open or is intended to be Zariski open in a compatible algebraic structure. The displayed projectivity question expresses the stated central target, while these additional completion qualifications require clarification.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the particular compact analytic completion associated with the Stein factorization of a period map is projective. The supplied record leaves the period-map hypotheses and the construction of this completion unspecified. Its dated assessment reports partial progress, including projective fibers, while retaining projectivity of the completion as a conjecture; this assessment is not independently verified here.
