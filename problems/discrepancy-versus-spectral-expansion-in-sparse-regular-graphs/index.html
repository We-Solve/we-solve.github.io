Discrepancy versus Spectral Expansion in Sparse Regular Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns an asymptotic family of regular graphs. For a precise provisional formulation, use the convention that graphs are finite, simple, and undirected; the source record does not explicitly specify this convention.

Let $(G_k)_{k\geq 1}$ be a sequence of such graphs, where $G_k=(V_k,E_k)$ is $d_k$-regular, $n_k=|V_k|\to\infty$, $d_k>0$, and $d_k/n_k\to 0$. No assumption that $d_k\to\infty$ is supplied. Write $A_k$ for the adjacency matrix and $\mathbf{1}_k$ for the all-ones vector. For arbitrary, possibly overlapping sets $S,T\subseteq V_k$, define \[ e_k(S,T)=\sum_{u\in S}\sum_{v\in T}(A_k)_{uv}. \] Thus an edge with both endpoints in $S\cap T$ contributes twice. Interpret the discrepancy hypothesis uniformly over vertex sets as \[ \Delta_k:=\max_{S,T\subseteq V_k} \left|e_k(S,T)-\frac{d_k|S||T|}{n_k}\right|, \qquad \frac{\Delta_k}{n_kd_k}\longrightarrow 0. \] Since $A_k\mathbf{1}_k=d_k\mathbf{1}_k$, the subspace $\mathbf{1}_k^\perp$ is invariant under $A_k$. Define \[ \lambda_*(G_k)=\max\left\{|\lambda|:\lambda\text{ is an eigenvalue of }A_k|_{\mathbf{1}_k^\perp}\right\}. \] Here only the constant-vector eigenvalue is excluded: additional occurrences of $d_k$, and the eigenvalue $-d_k$ if present, are included.

Question under these conventions. Must every sequence satisfying the preceding hypotheses satisfy \[ \frac{\lambda_*(G_k)}{d_k}\longrightarrow 0? \] The uniform interpretation of the error, the edge-counting convention for overlapping sets, and the meaning of "nontrivial" eigenvalues require confirmation against the original source. The supplied record does not impose connectedness, nonbipartiteness, vertex transitivity, or a Cayley graph structure. Its open-problem label and its claim of prior statement verification are supplied editorial assessments, not mathematical verification of this draft.

Short English statement

For sparse regular graphs, does uniformly small absolute discrepancy between all pairs of vertex sets force every adjacency eigenvalue orthogonal to the constant vector to be small compared with the degree? The draft makes the asymptotic quantifiers explicit, but the graph, edge-counting, and eigenvalue conventions require source confirmation.
