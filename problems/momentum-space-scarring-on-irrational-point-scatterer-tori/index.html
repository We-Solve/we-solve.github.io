An irrational rectangular flat torus has a nonrational squared aspect ratio. A point scatterer is a selfadjoint point-interaction extension of the Laplacian at a fixed torus point, with a coupling convention. Eigenfunctions have Fourier coefficients indexed by the dual lattice. A conditional Fourier-shell criterion.
Consider the usual Green-function coefficient ansatz for a new point-interaction eigenfunction, $c_\xi=C/(|\xi|^2-\lambda)$ on the dual lattice, with $\lambda$ off the unperturbed spectrum and $C$ chosen for unit norm. Let a shell $S_E=\{\xi:|\xi|^2=E\}$ have multiplicity $r_E$ and put $\delta=|E-\lambda|$. Its exact normalized momentum mass is \[ P_E=\frac{r_E/\delta^2}{r_E/\delta^2+\sum_{\xi\notin S_E}(|\xi|^2-\lambda)^{-2}}. \] If along a sequence $\delta^2\sum_{\xi\notin S_E}(|\xi|^2-\lambda)^{-2}\leq C_0$, then $P_E\geq r_E/(r_E+C_0)$. If the left side tends to zero, $P_E\to1$.
Directional consequence and caveat.
On an irrational rectangular torus, equality of $m^2/a^2+n^2/b^2$ for two integer pairs forces equality of both squares when $a^2/b^2$ is irrational. Thus a nonzero shell has at most four sign-related directions. Extracting a subsequence of these finitely many directions gives limiting atoms whenever their shell mass tends to one. This is a precise sufficient scarring criterion under the coefficient ansatz and tail estimate.
