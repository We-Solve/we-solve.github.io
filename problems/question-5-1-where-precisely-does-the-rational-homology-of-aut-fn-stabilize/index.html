Question 5.1 — Where precisely does the rational homology of Aut(Fn) stabilize?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be the rank-$n$ free group. Extending automorphisms by fixing a new generator gives $s_n:\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Aut}(F_{n+1})$. The problem asks for the exact rational homological stability threshold as a function of degree $i$: the least rank after which the corresponding homology is stable, both for automorphism and outer automorphism groups.

For automorphisms this can be expressed as the least $N$ such that every $(s_n)_*$ on $H_i(-;{\mathbb{Q}})$ is an isomorphism for $n\geq N$. The supplied summary reports Galatius's stable rational homology as zero in positive degrees. In that interpretation, a map-independent version for either group family $G_n$ is \[ N_G(i)=\min\{N:H_i(G_n;{\mathbb{Q}})=0\text{ for every }n\geq N\}, \qquad i\geq1. \] Degree zero is ${\mathbb{Q}}$, not zero. The threshold is not the first isolated vanishing rank. For $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$, the source's comparison/stabilization model is missing: basis extension on automorphisms does not simply descend to a canonical homomorphism on outer automorphisms, so such a map is not invented.

The source reports only two nontrivial unstable outer-group classes then known [49, 26]. It describes Morita's infinite series of cycles [69], using Kontsevich's connection with cohomology of an infinite-dimensional Lie algebra. The first generates $H_4(\operatorname{Out}(F_4);{\mathbb{Q}})\cong{\mathbb{Q}}$, and Conant--Vogtmann [26] prove nontriviality of the second in $H_8(\operatorname{Out}(F_6);{\mathbb{Q}})$. Morita and Conant--Vogtmann also construct cycles indexed by odd-valent graphs. Cycle construction alone is not asserted to make every member a nonzero class. The exact Lie-algebra/weight correspondence is not supplied.

Short English statement

Determine the optimal rational stability or positive-degree vanishing rank for each homological degree of free-group automorphism and outer automorphism groups, retaining the reported unstable Morita classes.
