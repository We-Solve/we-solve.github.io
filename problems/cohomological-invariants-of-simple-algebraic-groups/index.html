Cohomological Invariants of Simple Algebraic Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a general computation, but does not specify a base field, a range of degrees, or all coefficient conventions. The following defines the requested object under the ordinary Galois-cohomology interpretation; these conventions require confirmation against the source.

Let $k$ be a field, let $k_s$ be a separable closure, and write $\Gamma_k=\operatorname{Gal}(k_s/k)$. Let $G$ be a simple algebraic group over $k$, and let $M$ be a discrete abelian group with continuous $\Gamma_k$-action. For each field extension $K/k$, let $M_K$ denote the corresponding restricted Galois module and let $H^n(K,M_K)$ denote continuous Galois cohomology. Here $n\geq 0$ is a fixed cohomological degree; the source does not specify which degrees are intended. Write $H^1(K,G)$ for the pointed set of isomorphism classes of $G_K$-torsors, and write $1_K$ for the class of the trivial torsor.

A degree-$n$ cohomological invariant with coefficients in $M$ is a family of maps \[ a_K:H^1(K,G)\longrightarrow H^n(K,M_K),\qquad K/k\text{ a field extension}, \] natural under embeddings of fields over $k$. Thus, for every such embedding $K\hookrightarrow L$ and every $\xi\in H^1(K,G)$, \[ a_L(\xi_L)=\operatorname{res}_{L/K}\left(a_K(\xi)\right). \] The invariant is normalized if $a_K(1_K)=0$ for every extension $K/k$. Pointwise addition makes the normalized invariants an abelian group, denoted \[ \operatorname{Inv}^{n}(G,M)^{\mathrm{norm}}. \]

Requested computation. Determine $\operatorname{Inv}^{n}(G,M)^{\mathrm{norm}}$ for simple algebraic groups $G$ and Galois modules $M$, including the basic coefficient case $M={\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. This retains the generality requested in the supplied record; it does not assert a computation or a uniform classification.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify restrictions on $k$, whether simplicity is over $k$ or absolute, or whether particular isogeny types are intended. It also does not specify coefficient twists or whether a cohomology theory other than ordinary Galois cohomology is intended in positive characteristic. In particular, the meaning and intended scope of the coefficient case ${\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$ in characteristic $2$ require confirmation. The definitions above describe one conventional reading without resolving these omissions.

Short English statement

Compute the abelian group of natural rules assigning a degree-$n$ cohomology class to each torsor under a simple algebraic group, with value zero on every trivial torsor. The request includes coefficients ${\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. The base field, intended notion of simplicity, degrees, and coefficient conventions remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
