Math-4511

Unified Constructions of Supercuspidal Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology only, let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field of residue characteristic $p$, and let $G$ be a connected reductive algebraic group over $F$. Under the usual complex-coefficient convention, a representation $(\pi,V)$ of $G(F)$ is smooth if every vector has an open stabilizer. An irreducible smooth representation $\pi$ is supercuspidal if it is not a subquotient of any normalized parabolically induced representation $\operatorname{Ind}_{P(F)}^{G(F)}\sigma$, where $P$ is a proper $F$-parabolic subgroup with Levi factor $M$, and $\sigma$ is an irreducible smooth complex representation of $M(F)$.

Requested construction, with unresolved scope. Does there exist a common construction of supercuspidal representations of reductive $p$-adic groups that simultaneously generalizes the Bushnell--Kutzko and Yu constructions?

The supplied record does not specify the class of fields or groups, the coefficient field, the permitted residue characteristics, any tameness or other hypotheses, the input data of the proposed construction, or the precise sense in which it must recover either existing construction. The conventions above explain standard terminology; they do not supply missing hypotheses for the requested construction. In particular, the record does not determine whether all supercuspidal representations must be obtained or only the representations covered by the two named constructions. Consequently, this is a construction problem with unresolved quantifiers and compatibility requirements, rather than a fully specified mathematical assertion.

Short English statement

Find a common framework that recovers both the Bushnell--Kutzko and Yu constructions of supercuspidal representations. The supplied record leaves the scope and meaning of this generalization unspecified, so a precise existence assertion requires further source clarification.
