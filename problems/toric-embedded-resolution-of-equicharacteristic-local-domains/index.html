Math-62

Toric Embedded Resolution of Equicharacteristic Local Domains

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a Noetherian excellent local domain, let $k=R/\mathfrak m$ be algebraically closed, and assume that $R$ is equicharacteristic, meaning that $\operatorname{char}(R)=\operatorname{char}(k)$. Write $X=\operatorname{Spec}R$ and let $x\in X$ be its closed point.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such $R$, there exist a finite-dimensional affine space $\mathbb A_k^N$ and a toric birational map \[ \pi:Z\longrightarrow\mathbb A_k^N \] such that the strict transform of $X$ is nonsingular and transverse to every nondense toric orbit over $x$. For a toric variety $Z$ with dense torus $T_Z$, a nondense toric orbit means an orbit $O\ne T_Z$.

The source does not specify an embedding or other ambient realization of $X$ in $\mathbb A_k^N$. Consequently its strict transform under $\pi$ is not defined by the supplied data. In a setting where an embedding $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb A_k^N$ and a birational morphism $\pi$ have been specified, and where $U\subseteq\mathbb A_k^N$ is an open subset over which $\pi$ is an isomorphism with $X\cap U$ dense in $X$, the strict transform would be the scheme-theoretic closure \[ Y=\overline{\pi^{-1}(X\cap U)}^{\,Z}. \] This explains the terminology conditionally; it does not supply the missing embedding. The stated hypotheses do not assert that $R$ is a finitely generated $k$-algebra, a localization of one, or equipped with a coefficient field. A finite-dimensional algebraic embedding must therefore not be assumed.

There are further unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify whether $\pi$ must be a proper birational toric morphism, whether nonsingularity of $Y$ is required everywhere or only above $x$, or the precise meaning of transversality when the ambient toric variety is singular. In a smooth ambient setting, the usual tangent-space criterion for transversality at a point $y\in Y\cap O$ is \[ T_yY+T_yO=T_yZ, \] with $Y$ and $O$ smooth at $y$. The phrase "over the closed point" suggests a condition at points mapping to $x$, but the source does not state the exact quantifier. Thus the displayed question preserves the supplied target, while its ambient realization and these geometric conditions require clarification before it becomes a fully specified mathematical proposition.

Short English statement

For every excellent equicharacteristic Noetherian local domain with algebraically closed residue field, the source asks for a toric modification making its strict transform nonsingular and transverse to the toric boundary above the closed point. The ambient embedding and several geometric qualifications are unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
