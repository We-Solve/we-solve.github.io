A Laughlin gap stable under every small bounded scalar perturbation

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The expanding-sphere Laughlin gap and unique ground state are claimed stable under small bounded projected scalar disorder, uniformly over profiles and particle number.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $q=3(N-1)$, $U_q$ be the spin-$q/2$ lowest Landau level on a sphere of area $2\pi q$, and \[ H_{N,q}=\sum_{i<j}P_{ij}^{(1)}\quad\text{on }\bigwedge^NU_q, \] where each projector selects pair total spin $q-1$. Perturb by $\lambda\sum_iT_q(\varphi)^{(i)}$, with $T_q(\varphi)=\Pi_qM_\varphi\Pi_q|_{U_q}$. The source claims constants $\lambda_*,\Delta_*>0$, $N_*<\infty$, such that \[ E_1(N,\lambda,\varphi)-E_0(N,\lambda,\varphi)\ge\Delta_* \] for every $N\ge N_*$, bounded real measurable $\varphi$ with $\|\varphi\|_\infty\le1$, and real $|\lambda|\le\lambda_*$. Eigenvalues count multiplicity, so the ground state is unique.

Outline of the argument

A local lowest-Landau-level calculus tracks pinned zeros and weighted observables. Orthogonal branch readout, charge bounds, and rotation averaging control local removal of interaction energy; the unperturbed Fock-space gap then absorbs the bounded perturbation.
