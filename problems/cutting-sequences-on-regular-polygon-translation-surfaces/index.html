Cutting Sequences on Regular Polygon Translation Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A=\{a_1,\ldots,a_k\}$, and let $A^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ denote the set of bi-infinite words $w=(w_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}}$ with letters in $A$. The source asks for a characterization of cutting sequences of geodesics on translation surfaces obtained from regular $2k$-gons, together with a reconstruction of a geodesic from its sequence.

The following makes one conventional interpretation precise, conditionally on choices not specified in the record. For an integer $k\geq 2$, let $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ be a nondegenerate regular $2k$-gon. Identify each side with its opposite side by translation to obtain a translation surface $S$, and label the $k$ pairs of identified sides bijectively by $a_1,\ldots,a_k$. Retain the polygonal presentation, so that crossings of the identified sides can be recorded. A translation surface is a surface with Euclidean coordinate charts away from finitely many distinguished points, whose transition maps are translations; a geodesic is locally straight in these charts.

For this conditional interpretation, let $\mathcal G(S)$ consist of oriented unit-speed geodesics $\gamma:{\mathbb{R}}\to S$ which avoid all images of polygon vertices, cross the identified sides transversely, and have their entire set of crossing times indexed as \[ \cdots<t_{-1}<t_0=0<t_1<\cdots, \qquad \lim_{n\to\pm\infty}t_n=\pm\infty. \] Thus a crossing is distinguished as index zero. Define the cutting sequence map \[ C_S:\mathcal G(S)\longrightarrow A^{{\mathbb{Z}}},\qquad C_S(\gamma)_n=a_i \] when the crossing at time $t_n$ is through the identified side pair labelled $a_i$.

Conditional mathematical formulation. For every such $k$ and labelled surface $S$, give necessary and sufficient conditions on $w\in A^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ for \[ w\in C_S(\mathcal G(S)). \] For every realizable $w$, determine the fiber \[ C_S^{-1}(w)=\{\gamma\in\mathcal G(S):C_S(\gamma)=w\}, \] and determine in what sense the geodesic can be recovered from $w$, including whether it is unique under the intended equivalence of geodesics.

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not explicitly specify opposite-side identification, the permitted values of $k$, the labelling convention, or the treatment of vertex encounters, singular geodesics, and trajectories along sides. The class $\mathcal G(S)$ above is therefore a conditional regular-trajectory formulation, not a verified exhaustive interpretation of the source. The meaning of recovery is also unspecified: it could concern direction, the geometric trajectory, or a parametrized geodesic, possibly modulo time translation or surface symmetries. If the distinguished crossing is forgotten, coding is naturally considered modulo the index shift $(\sigma w)_n=w_{n+1}$. These choices require mathematical review before a definitive formulation can be accepted.

Short English statement

Characterize exactly which bi-infinite words encode geodesics on translation surfaces obtained from regular $2k$-gons, and determine what geometric information the word recovers. The displayed formulation conditionally uses opposite-side gluing and geodesics avoiding polygon vertices with transverse crossings indexed by ${\mathbb{Z}}$; the source leaves these conventions and the meaning of unique recovery unresolved.
