Symmetric Function Bases for Bipartite Determinant Inequalities

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer and write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$; the source does not explicitly state the range of $n$. Let $G$ be a bipartite graph with parts $X=[n]$ and $Y$. For every nonempty $S\subseteq[n]$, define \[ X_S=\left|\{y\in Y:N_G(y)=S\}\right|, \] where $N_G(y)$ is the neighborhood of $y$. These counts must be finite for their numerical use below; the source does not explicitly impose this condition. Vertices with empty neighborhood do not enter the displayed definitions.

For the polynomial formulation, regard the symbols $X_S$ as independent indeterminates and work in \[ R_n={\mathbb{Q}}[X_S:\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[n]]. \] This rational coefficient ring accommodates the displayed coefficients; the source does not specify the coefficient ring for its requested basis. Define $M\in\operatorname{Mat}_{n\times n}(R_n)$ by the formulas exactly as supplied: \[ M_{ii}=\sum_{\substack{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[n]\\i\in S}}|S|, \qquad M_{ij}=\sum_{\substack{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[n]\\i\in S,\ j\notin S}}\frac{X_S}{|S|} \quad(i\ne j). \] In particular, the supplied diagonal formula contains no factor $X_S$. Whether this omission is intentional is unresolved; no such factor has been inserted here. Let \[ D=\operatorname{diag}(M_{11},\ldots,M_{nn}),\qquad P_n=\det M,\qquad Q_n=\det D. \] The relabeling action is the action of $\mathfrak S_n$ on $R_n$ defined by $\sigma\cdot X_S=X_{\sigma(S)}$. The source identifies $P_n$ and $Q_n$ as invariant under this action. This action permutes subset labels through permutations of $[n]$; it does not assert symmetry under arbitrary permutations of all the indeterminates.

Requested mathematical target. Find a natural basis from symmetric-function theory in which to express $P_n$ and $Q_n$. The source does not specify the ambient space having this basis, the meaning of naturality, or whether a compatible construction for all $n$ is required. Thus this is a basis-construction question whose intended precise formulation remains to be clarified.

The original statement also asks whether a proposed sum-of-squares refinement of the difference of these determinants is valid. No formula for that refinement, order of subtraction, domain of validity, or allowed square terms is supplied. The background describes a stronger nonnegative-square decomposition, but supplies no additional defining expression. This additional target cannot be stated as a precise conjecture from the supplied record.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a natural symmetric-function basis for two determinant polynomials invariant under relabeling one part of a bipartite graph. The diagonal formula is retained exactly as supplied, although its lack of an $X_S$ factor requires review. An additional sum-of-squares question is mentioned without its proposed formula. The supplied dated curation labels the remaining questions open and reports the determinant inequality proved; these assessments are not independently verified here.
