Stable Low-Rank Matrix Completion from Random Samples

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n_1,n_2$ be positive integers and let $M\in\mathbb K^{n_1\times n_2}$ have rank $r$, where $\mathbb K$ denotes either ${\mathbb{R}}$ or ${\mathbb{C}}$. The source does not specify the field or the quantitative range intended by low rank. For a matrix $X$, define its nuclear norm by \[ \|X\|_*:=\sum_{j=1}^{\min(n_1,n_2)}\sigma_j(X), \] where $\sigma_j(X)$ are its singular values. Let $\Omega\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n_1\}\times\{1,\ldots,n_2\}$ be a random set of observed positions, and let $P_\Omega X=(X_{ij})_{(i,j)\in\Omega}$, with any fixed ordering of the coordinates.

The intended question concerns matrices that are sufficiently incoherent. The record supplies no definition or numerical threshold for this condition, no sampling law for $\Omega$, and no required sample size. These are unresolved hypotheses, not conditions that may be omitted.

For noiseless observations, nuclear-norm completion is the optimization problem \[ \operatorname*{minimize}_{X\in\mathbb K^{n_1\times n_2}}\ \|X\|_* \qquad\text{subject to}\qquad P_\Omega X=P_\Omega M. \] Its exact-recovery target is that $M$ be the unique minimizer, so that all entries outside $\Omega$ are recovered exactly. The source asks whether this target holds for sufficiently incoherent low-rank matrices under random sampling, but does not specify the success probability, parameter regime, or whether recovery is required for each fixed matrix or simultaneously for a class of matrices.

For noisy observations, write $y=P_\Omega M+e$, where $e$ is the observation error. A constrained formulation of the requested stability question is \[ \operatorname*{minimize}_{X\in\mathbb K^{n_1\times n_2}}\ \|X\|_* \qquad\text{subject to}\qquad \|P_\Omega X-y\|_{\mathrm{obs}}\leq\delta, \qquad \|e\|_{\mathrm{obs}}\leq\delta, \] where $\delta\geq0$ and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{obs}}$ denotes a chosen observation-error norm. The intended stability target is a controlled bound on $\|\widehat M-M\|_{\mathrm{err}}$ for a recovered minimizer $\widehat M$, with the bound tending to zero as the noise bound tends to zero in a fixed admissible setting. Neither norm, the quantitative bound, its parameter dependence, nor the required uniformity over errors and minimizers is supplied. The displayed noisy program is therefore an explicit possible interpretation of the request, not a source-verified choice of noise model or algorithmic variant.

Thus the supplied problem asks for sampling, rank, and incoherence hypotheses ensuring exact recovery and stability by nuclear-norm minimization. It does not determine a single fully quantified theorem. The supplied literature assessment labels the problem partially solved and reports established exact-recovery results under incoherence and random-sampling hypotheses; that dated assessment does not itself specify or establish the requested noisy guarantee.

Short English statement

Can minimizing the sum of singular values reconstruct a sufficiently incoherent low-rank matrix exactly from randomly observed entries, and give controlled reconstruction error when those observations contain bounded noise? The source leaves the sampling law, sample size, incoherence condition, probability guarantee, and quantitative meaning of stability unspecified. Its partially solved label is supplied dated curation.
