Vanishing Lie Brackets for Non-Quasitriangular Hopf Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the cohomology theory, coefficients, ground field, or construction of the graded Lie bracket. The following is therefore a conditional formulation under the usual Hopf-cohomology interpretation; these choices require confirmation from the cited report.

Let $k$ denote the unspecified ground field, and let $H$ be a Hopf algebra over $k$, with coproduct $\Delta$ and counit $\varepsilon$. Give $k$ the trivial left $H$-module structure $h\cdot a=\varepsilon(h)a$, and interpret cohomology as \[ \mathrm{H}^{n}(H,k)=\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{H}(k,k), \qquad \mathrm{H}^{>0}(H,k)=\bigoplus_{n\geq 1}\mathrm{H}^{n}(H,k). \] Interpret the bracket as the canonical Gerstenhaber bracket on this cohomology, with degree convention \[ [-,-]\colon \mathrm{H}^{p}(H,k)\times\mathrm{H}^{q}(H,k) \longrightarrow \mathrm{H}^{p+q-1}(H,k). \] Thus the bracket has degree $-1$, and becomes a degree-zero graded Lie bracket when a class of cohomological degree $n$ is assigned shifted degree $n-1$.

Let $H\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}$ denote the category of left $H$-modules, with tensor product over $k$, unit $k$, and action \[ h\cdot(m\otimes n)=\sum h_{(1)}m\otimes h_{(2)}n, \qquad \Delta(h)=\sum h_{(1)}\otimes h_{(2)}. \] A braiding means a natural family of invertible $H$-linear maps $c_{M,N}\colon M\otimes_k N\to N\otimes_k M$ satisfying the two hexagon identities for this monoidal structure.

Under these interpretations, the question is whether every such $H$ for which $H\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}$ admits no braiding satisfies \[ \forall p,q\geq 1\quad \forall \alpha\in\mathrm{H}^{p}(H,k)\quad \forall \beta\in\mathrm{H}^{q}(H,k), \qquad [\alpha,\beta]=0. \] The range of permitted ground fields and any additional hypotheses on $H$ remain unspecified. The record's title and literature assessment use "non-quasitriangular," whereas its question uses absence of a braiding on the module category. Their intended relationship, including the precise module category considered, requires source confirmation. If the intended cohomology is Hochschild cohomology or uses other coefficients, the displayed formulation must be revised.

Short English statement

Under the provisional interpretation $\mathrm{H}^{*}(H,k)=\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{H}(k,k)$, ask whether the Gerstenhaber bracket of every pair of positive-degree cohomology classes vanishes whenever the usual tensor category of $H$-modules admits no braiding. The cohomology, bracket, ground-field restrictions, and intended non-quasitriangular hypothesis require confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
