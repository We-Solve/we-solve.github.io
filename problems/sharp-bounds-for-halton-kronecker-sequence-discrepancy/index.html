Math-3610

Sharp Bounds for Halton–Kronecker Sequence Discrepancy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 0$, write its finite binary expansion as $n=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_j(n)2^j$, where $a_j(n)\in\{0,1\}$ and all but finitely many digits vanish. Define the base-$2$ radical inverse by \[ \phi_2(n)=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_j(n)2^{-j-1}, \] so that $\phi_2(0)=0$. For $x\in{\mathbb{R}}$, let $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$. The sequence in question is \[ z_n=\left(\phi_2(n),\{n\sqrt{2}\}\right)\in[0,1)^2,\qquad n\geq 0. \] For a positive integer $N$, consider its first $N$ points, indexed by $0\leq n<N$. One standard normalized discrepancy convention is \[ D_N=\sup_{\substack{0\leq u_i\leq v_i\leq 1\\i=1,2}} \left|\frac{1}{N}\#\left\{0\leq n<N:z_n\in[u_1,v_1)\times[u_2,v_2)\right\}-(v_1-u_1)(v_2-u_2)\right|. \] The corresponding star discrepancy is \[ D_N^*=\sup_{0\leq t_1,t_2\leq 1} \left|\frac{1}{N}\#\left\{0\leq n<N:z_n\in[0,t_1)\times[0,t_2)\right\}-t_1t_2\right|. \] The supplied record does not specify which discrepancy convention is intended. These two conventions satisfy $D_N^*\leq D_N\leq 4D_N^*$, so they give equivalent versions of the requested asymptotic bound. The displayed definition of $D_N$ makes a standard interpretation explicit; its agreement with the report's notation remains to be checked.

The question is whether, for every real $\varepsilon>0$, there exist constants $C_\varepsilon>0$ and an integer $N_\varepsilon\geq 2$ such that, for every integer $N\geq N_\varepsilon$, \[ D_N\leq C_\varepsilon\frac{(\log N)^{2+\varepsilon}}{N}. \] Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm, and the constants may depend on $\varepsilon$. This is the question supplied by the record, not an assertion that the bound holds.

Short English statement

Does the discrepancy of the first $N$ points $(\phi_2(n),\{n\sqrt{2}\})$, starting at $n=0$, have order at most $(\log N)^{2+\varepsilon}/N$ for every $\varepsilon>0$? The supplied discrepancy convention is unspecified; normalized box discrepancy and star discrepancy yield equivalent bounds here. The supplied open label is dated curation, not independently verified status.
