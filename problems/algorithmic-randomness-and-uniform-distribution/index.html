Algorithmic Randomness and Uniform Distribution

Definitions and mathematical statement

For reference, a standard classical notion is uniform distribution modulo $1$. Let $(x_n)_{n\geq 1}$ be a sequence in ${\mathbb{R}}$, and write $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$. The sequence is uniformly distributed modulo $1$ if \[ \forall\,0\leq a<b\leq 1,\qquad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{1}{N}\#\left\{1\leq n\leq N:a\leq\{x_n\}<b\right\}=b-a. \] This definition supplies a conventional reference point; the source does not specify whether distribution modulo $1$ is the intended setting.
