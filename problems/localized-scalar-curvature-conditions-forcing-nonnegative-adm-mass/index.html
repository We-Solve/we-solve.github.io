Localized Scalar Curvature Conditions Forcing Nonnegative ADM Mass

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,g)$ be an asymptotically flat Riemannian manifold with compact boundary $\partial X$, and let $\operatorname{Scal}_g$ denote its scalar curvature. Assume \[ \operatorname{Scal}_g(x)\geq 0\qquad\text{for every }x\in X. \] For a designated asymptotically flat end $E$, write $m_{\mathrm{ADM}}(E,g)$ for its ADM mass, provided the dimensional, decay, and integrability hypotheses needed to define that mass hold. The supplied record does not specify these hypotheses or designate an end when there is more than one.

A positive scalar-curvature lower bound on a nonempty region $\Omega\subseteq X$ means that, for some constant $\kappa>0$, \[ \operatorname{Scal}_g(x)\geq\kappa\qquad\text{for every }x\in\Omega. \] When $\partial X\neq\varnothing$, define the distance of this region from the boundary by \[ d_g(\Omega,\partial X) =\inf\{\operatorname{dist}_g(x,y):x\in\Omega,\ y\in\partial X\}, \] where $\operatorname{dist}_g$ is the Riemannian distance.

Problem. Find sufficient metric conditions involving such a positive lower bound $\kappa$ on a region $\Omega$ and its distance $d_g(\Omega,\partial X)$ from the compact boundary that force \[ m_{\mathrm{ADM}}(E,g)\geq 0. \] This is a request to determine sufficient conditions, rather than a specified implication with a supplied numerical threshold. The record gives no dimension, asymptotic decay convention, completeness assumption, boundary mean-curvature condition, admissible class of regions, or quantitative relation between curvature and distance. It also leaves unclear whether the conclusion concerns a single designated end or every end. Compactness of the boundary does not itself require the boundary to be nonempty; the intended treatment of an empty boundary is unspecified. No spin assumption appears in the question, and none is imposed here. These unresolved qualifications prevent the record from determining a single fully quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

For an asymptotically flat manifold with compact boundary and everywhere nonnegative scalar curvature, seek sufficient geometric conditions using a positive scalar-curvature bound on a region and that region's distance from the boundary to guarantee nonnegative ADM mass. The dimension, asymptotic hypotheses, region geometry, boundary conditions, and quantitative criterion remain unspecified. The supplied dated curation reports partial results in the spin setting and an unresolved general nonspin question; this assessment requires independent verification.
