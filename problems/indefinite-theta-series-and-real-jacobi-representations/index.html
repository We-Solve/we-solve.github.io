Indefinite Theta Series and Real Jacobi Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A lattice $L$ is a discrete subgroup spanning a finite-dimensional real vector space $V$. A quadratic form $Q\colon V\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is indefinite if it takes both positive and negative values. These definitions explain the terminology; the supplied record does not specify a particular lattice, quadratic form, or signature.

Write $\Theta$ symbolically for an indefinite theta series formed from a lattice sum over cones, and $\widehat\Theta$ for a modular completion associated with it. Let $\mathcal C_J$ denote the real-Jacobi Casimir operator intended by the source. The stated condition on the completion is \[ \mathcal C_J\widehat\Theta=0. \] These symbols do not specify a summation formula, a completion procedure, or a normalization of the operator.

Question. Can indefinite theta series of this cone-sum type, together with their modular completions satisfying the displayed equation, be realized through principal-series representations of the real Jacobi group?

Unresolved formulation. The original wording asks whether series "of the described type" can be "connected to" principal-series representations; the supplied rewritten statement uses "realized through." The record does not supply the description needed to identify that class or define either relationship. It specifies neither the dimension and convention for the real Jacobi group nor the inducing data for its principal series. It also omits the cones, summation kernels, variables, convergence or regularization conditions, modular transformation laws, completion data, and Casimir formula. The quantifier is unresolved: the question could concern every series in an intended class, a subclass characterized by additional hypotheses, or particular examples. Thus the displayed condition and the question preserve the supplied target, but the record does not determine a fully quantified representation-theoretic assertion.

Short English statement

The question asks whether cone-sum indefinite theta series and their modular completions annihilated by a real-Jacobi Casimir operator admit a principal-series representation interpretation. The intended class, quantifiers, and meaning of realization remain unspecified.
