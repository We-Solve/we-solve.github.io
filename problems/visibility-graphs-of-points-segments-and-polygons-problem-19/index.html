Visibility Graphs of Points, Segments and Polygons — Problem 19

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a graph in the intended graph domain. A representation by horizontal bars of a common positive length has bars \[ B_v=[a_v,a_v+\ell]\times\{h_v\},\qquad a_v,h_v\in{\mathbb{R}},\quad v\in V, \] where the same $\ell>0$ is used for every vertex. A uniform scaling permits the normalization $\ell=1$.

Fix the admissibility and visibility conventions for the underlying bar visibility representation. Write $\operatorname{Vis}(B_u,B_v;\mathcal B)$ for visibility between two bars in the complete family $\mathcal B=(B_v)_{v\in V}$, according to those conventions. In the undirected interpretation with adjacency represented exactly by visibility, the representation must satisfy \[ \{u,v\}\in E \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Vis}(B_u,B_v;\mathcal B) \qquad(u,v\in V,\ u\ne v). \] A graph admitting an admissible representation of this kind with all bars of equal positive length is a unit bar visibility graph.

Problem (Ghosh--Goswami, 2010, Problem 19). Find necessary and sufficient graph-theoretic conditions characterizing the unit bar visibility graphs.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied excerpt does not define the underlying bar visibility convention. In particular, it does not specify the admissible intersections or placements of bars, whether visibility requires an unobstructed vertical corridor of positive width or merely a vertical line segment, or the treatment of endpoint contacts. It also does not explicitly specify the graph domain or whether every visibility must correspond to an edge. The displayed equivalence records the exact-adjacency, undirected interpretation conditionally; these conventions require confirmation before this becomes a fully specified characterization problem.

Short English statement

Characterize the graphs that admit a bar visibility representation in which every horizontal bar has the same positive length. The supplied excerpt leaves the underlying visibility conventions and graph domain unspecified, so those qualifications require review.
