Math-831

Rational Points on Cartan Modular Curves

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N$ be a positive integer and set $R_N={\mathbb{Z}}/N{\mathbb{Z}}$. A subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{GL}_2(R_N)$ has full determinant if \[ \det(G)=R_N^{\times}. \] For the purposes of this draft, use the usual compactified coarse modular curve convention: $X_G/{\mathbb{Q}}$ is the smooth projective modular curve obtained by compactifying the coarse moduli curve associated with $G$. Over $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, its noncuspidal moduli points represent elliptic curves equipped with a $G$-orbit of ordered bases of their $N$-torsion, up to isomorphism. The model over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ is the standard modular model associated with $G$. At $N=1$, use the usual level-one curve. Write \[ X_G({\mathbb{Q}})=\operatorname{Hom}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{Q}},X_G). \] This denotes rational points of the coarse curve; no identification with individual level structures defined over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ is asserted. The source does not specify compactification or the treatment of $-I$. Under the convention used here, cusps are included, and replacing $G$ by $\langle G,-I\rangle$ leaves the coarse modular curve unchanged. These conventions require confirmation against the intended source formulation.

For a prime $p$, identify $R_p$ with ${\mathbb{F}}_p$. A standard Borel subgroup and a standard split Cartan subgroup are, respectively, \[ B_p=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&d\end{pmatrix}:a,d\in{\mathbb{F}}_p^{\times},\ b\in{\mathbb{F}}_p\right\}, \qquad C_{\mathrm{s}}(p)=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&d\end{pmatrix}:a,d\in{\mathbb{F}}_p^{\times}\right\}. \] Choose an ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-basis of ${\mathbb{F}}_{p^2}$. Multiplication by nonzero elements of ${\mathbb{F}}_{p^2}$, expressed in this basis, gives a nonsplit Cartan subgroup \[ C_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)=\{m_\alpha:\alpha\in{\mathbb{F}}_{p^2}^{\times}\}\leq\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p), \qquad m_\alpha(x)=\alpha x. \] The basis choice changes this subgroup only by conjugacy. For any subgroup $C\leq\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p)$, define its normalizer by \[ \operatorname{Norm}(C)=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p):gCg^{-1}=C\}. \] Use the notation $X_{\mathrm{s}}^+(p)=X_{\operatorname{Norm}(C_{\mathrm{s}}(p))}$ and $X_{\mathrm{ns}}^+(p)=X_{\operatorname{Norm}(C_{\mathrm{ns}}(p))}$.

Problem. For every positive integer $N$ and every full-determinant subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{Z}}/N{\mathbb{Z}})$, describe the complete set $X_G({\mathbb{Q}})$. In particular, carry out this determination for every prime $p$ when $G$ is a Borel subgroup, the normalizer of a split Cartan subgroup, or the normalizer of a nonsplit Cartan subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p)$, including their conjugates. The source states no restriction on the prime. It does not prescribe the form of a description, such as a parametrization, an explicit list, or a uniform classification theorem; that intended output remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Determine all rational points on modular curves associated with full-determinant matrix subgroups, with particular emphasis on prime-level Borel and Cartan-normalizer curves. This draft explicitly uses compactified coarse modular curves, so rational cusps are included; the source does not specify these conventions. The supplied label is partially solved, and the accompanying literature assessment requires independent review.
