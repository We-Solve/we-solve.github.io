Split-branch degenerations as a stable-surface boundary application

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The original asks for applications of stable-surface moduli to surface theory, analogous to using curve degenerations for Brill--Noether statements. It specifies no single theorem or class of applications. A stable surface conventionally has semi-log-canonical singularities and ample ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier canonical divisor; the precise moduli and field setup are omitted.

The supplied report proposes a complex double-plane example, so that the topological signature and characteristic-zero double-cover interpretation are meaningful. For each integer $d\geq4$, take a smooth plane curve $C=\{f_d=0\}$ and an appropriate general degree-$2d$ polynomial $g_{2d}$. It describes the family germ \[ z^2=f_d^2+t g_{2d}, \] with $z$ of weight $d$, as flat, Gorenstein, and stable, with reduced nonnormal central fiber $X_d={\mathbb{P}}^2\cup_C{\mathbb{P}}^2$ and smooth double-plane general fibers. The precise coefficient and parameter-open hypotheses are not supplied; this is not asserted for every fiber over an unrestricted parameter line.

For the smooth fibers it reports \[ \begin{gathered} K^2=2(d-3)^2,\qquad q=0,\qquad p_g=(d-1)(d-2)/2=g(C),\\ \chi(\mathcal O)=(d^2-3d+4)/2,\qquad c_2=4d^2-6d+6,\qquad\operatorname{signature}=2-2d^2, \end{gathered} \] with Noether defect $K^2-2p_g+4=(d-4)(d-5)$. Here $q=h^1(\mathcal O)$ and $p_g=h^0(\omega)$.

Put $V_j=H^0({\mathbb{P}}^2,\mathcal O(j))$ and let $T^1_{X_d}$ denote the local smoothing sheaf, conventionally $\mathcal E xt^1(\Omega^1_{X_d},\mathcal O_{X_d})$. The report identifies the normal quotient to the square-branch locus at $[f_d^2]$ with \[ V_{2d}/f_dV_d\cong H^0(C,T^1_{X_d}),\qquad \dim H^0(C,T^1_{X_d})=3d(d+1)/2. \] Its assertion of canonicity needs the chosen equation or the usual projective tangent-line factor made explicit. It claims every global local-smoothing section lifts within the branch chart, and a generic normal direction has $2d^2$ simple zeros producing exactly $2d^2$ ordinary double points of the total threefold. Positive degree of $T^1$ is reported to obstruct a smooth total space while this reduced central model is fixed; it does not obstruct the claimed smoothing with singular total space or variants changing the central model. These form an unreviewed boundary-to-surface example rather than a complete solution of the broad application program.

Short English statement

Use stable-surface boundary degenerations to obtain surface-theoretic information. The report proposes split double planes, computes their smooth-fiber invariants, and relates branch directions to local smoothing sections and total-space nodes. It distinguishes smoothing the fibers from having a smooth total threefold.
