Math-6878

Graph Distances in Associahedra

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the standard type $A$ interpretation of an associahedron, let $n\geq 3$ be an integer and let $P_n$ be a convex polygon with $n$ cyclically labelled vertices. A triangulation of $P_n$ is a maximal collection of pairwise noncrossing internal diagonals. Let $\mathcal T_n$ be the set of these triangulations. A flip replaces one diagonal of a triangulation by the other diagonal of the quadrilateral formed by its two incident triangles.

Define the associahedron graph $G_n$ to have vertex set $\mathcal T_n$, with two vertices adjacent exactly when they differ by one flip. For $T,U\in\mathcal T_n$, define \[ d_n(T,U)=\min\left\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:\exists\,T_0,\ldots,T_k\in\mathcal T_n,\ T_0=T,\ T_k=U,\ T_{i-1}\text{ and }T_i\text{ differ by one flip for }1\leq i\leq k\right\}. \] The graph is connected, so this minimum exists; in particular, $d_n(T,T)=0$. After fixing a boundary edge as a root, triangulations correspond to rooted ordered full binary trees with $n-1$ leaves. A full binary tree has exactly two children at each internal vertex, and a rotation is the local replacement $((A,B),C)\leftrightarrow(A,(B,C))$. Under this correspondence, flip distance equals minimum rotation distance.

Problem. Uniformly for every integer $n\geq 3$ and every pair $T,U\in\mathcal T_n$, determine $d_n(T,U)$. The supplied curated formulation requests an exact structural characterization or an efficient exact algorithm beyond the supplied complexity classification. Neither the required form of a structural characterization nor the meaning of efficiency is specified, so that additional objective remains unresolved as a precise performance requirement.

For context, the original excerpt also discusses graph distance in general polytopes and graph diameter, which in this model is \[ \operatorname{diam}(G_n)=\max_{T,U\in\mathcal T_n}d_n(T,U). \] The curated target concerns individual pair distances. Short English statement

Given any two triangulations of the same convex polygon, determine the minimum number of diagonal flips transforming one into the other, equivalently the minimum number of rotations between their corresponding rooted ordered binary trees. The source requests further exact structural or algorithmic understanding, but leaves the desired efficiency and characterization criteria unspecified. Its partially solved status and literature claims are supplied dated assessments.
