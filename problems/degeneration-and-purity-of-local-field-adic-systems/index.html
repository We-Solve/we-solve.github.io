Degeneration and Purity of Local-Field Adic Systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ and $\ell$ be distinct primes, let $K$ be a $p$-adic local field, and let $X$ be a smooth variety over $K$. Here a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-adic local system means a lisse sheaf of finite-dimensional $\mathbb Q_\ell$-vector spaces on the étale site of $X$. The supplied formulation concerns local systems $\mathcal L$ that are pure, but the supplied excerpt does not define purity over $K$, specify a weight, or give the additional context implicit in the original phrase "such local systems."

The two questions attributed to Moritz Kerz, in joint work with Hélène Esnault, are the following, intended for every local system in that source-specific class:

  1. Does $\mathcal L$ degenerate to a limiting mixed structure at each ramification point?
  2. Is the cohomology of $\mathcal L$ pure?

These questions retain unresolved qualifications. For the first, the record specifies neither a compactification, model, or degeneration of $X$, nor the meaning of a ramification point or the category and properties of the proposed limiting mixed structure. Thus the points being quantified over and the required limiting object are not determined. For the second, the record does not specify the cohomology theory, the support condition, the degrees, the arithmetic action used to measure purity, or the expected weights. In particular, it does not distinguish cohomology over $K$ from geometric cohomology over an algebraic closure of $K$. No properness hypothesis is supplied. Such hypotheses and choices cannot be inserted silently. Consequently these are source-faithful problem statements, but the supplied record does not determine fully specified mathematical assertions.

Short English statement

For pure $\mathbb Q_\ell$-adic local systems on a smooth variety over a $p$-adic local field, with $\ell\ne p$, the source asks whether ramification admits limiting mixed structures and whether cohomology is pure. The meanings of purity, the limiting structure, and the intended cohomology require further source context.
