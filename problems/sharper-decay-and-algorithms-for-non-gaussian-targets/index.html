Sharper Decay and Algorithms for Non-Gaussian Targets

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\pi$ denote the non-Gaussian log-concave target in the supplied record, and let $\lambda>0$ denote its stated convexity parameter. Write $G$ for the source's admissible parameter space, $E\colon G\to{\mathbb{R}}$ for its energy, and $(\Sigma(t),m(t))$ for its projected gradient trajectory with initial state $(\Sigma_0,m_0)$. These symbols identify the objects mentioned in the excerpt; the target's underlying space, the precise convexity hypothesis, the definitions of $G$ and $E$, and the evolution equation are not supplied.

As background, the excerpt reports Theorem 5 (Unique minimizer): $E$ attains a unique minimizer $(\Sigma_{\mathrm{opt}},m_{\mathrm{opt}})\in G$. Set \[ E_*:=\min_{(\Lambda,n)\in G}E(\Lambda,n) =E(\Sigma_{\mathrm{opt}},m_{\mathrm{opt}}). \] The excerpt also reports Theorem 6 (Convergence), whose displayed estimate can be written equivalently as \[ E(\Sigma(t),m(t))-E_* \leq e^{-\gamma t}\left(E(\Sigma_0,m_0)-E_*\right), \qquad \gamma\propto\lambda. \] This is a reported background result, subject to hypotheses absent from the supplied excerpt. In particular, the admissible initial states, time interval, and dependencies of the proportionality constants are unspecified.

The requested problem has two parts:

  1. For the source's projected gradient dynamics with a non-Gaussian log-concave target, establish a sharper decay estimate whose convergence guarantee does not deteriorate with $\lambda$. The excerpt motivates this request by comparison with the Gaussian-target case. It does not specify a replacement rate or the quantities to be held fixed when varying $\lambda$, so a precise uniform inequality cannot be determined from this record alone.
  2. Construct a numerical scheme for these dynamics and prove its convergence for non-Gaussian targets under explicitly stated assumptions. The source does not specify the discretization, convergence topology, step-size conditions, or whether convergence is intended toward the continuous trajectory, the unique energy minimizer, or both.
Thus this is a statement-only formulation of the supplied research objectives; the missing model definitions and theorem hypotheses must be recovered before a fully quantified mathematical statement can be given.

Short English statement

For the projected gradient model described by the source, improve the energy decay guarantee for non-Gaussian log-concave targets so that it does not deteriorate with the convexity parameter $\lambda>0$, and construct a numerical method with a convergence proof. The supplied excerpt omits essential model definitions and convergence specifications, so these objectives require further source clarification.
