Diamond-Complete Hypercube Formulas for Kazhdan–Lusztig Polynomials

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a conjectural hypercube-cluster formula, identified by its title as relating to Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials. It does not supply the formula itself or complete definitions of its hypotheses. Consequently, the following is a schematic statement of the recorded target, requiring reconstruction from the cited report before it can be a self-contained mathematical conjecture.

For an ambient partially ordered set $(P,\leq)$, interval notation means \[ [x,y]=\{z\in P:x\leq z\leq y\}. \] The intended ambient ordered set, graph structure, and restrictions on the endpoints $x\leq y$ are not specified in the supplied statement. The dated assessment describes the unresolved assertion as being in type $A$; this does not by itself specify these missing data.

With the intended ambient data and endpoints fixed, the recorded conjecture asks whether, for every $c'\in[x,y]$, the proposed formula holds whenever both of the following conditions are satisfied:

  1. The subinterval $[x,c']$ is diamond complete, in the sense attributed in the excerpt to Patimo, reference [2].
  2. For every vertex $v\in[x,c']$, the edges leaving $[x,c']$ from $v$ span a hypercube cluster.
Equivalently, its quantifier structure is schematically \[ \forall c'\in[x,y],\quad \left( \begin{gathered} [x,c']\text{ is diamond complete},\\ \text{and for every }v\in[x,c'],\\ \text{the edges leaving }[x,c']\text{ from }v\\ \text{span a hypercube cluster} \end{gathered} \right) \Longrightarrow \left(\text{the proposed hypercube-cluster formula holds for }c'\right). \] Here the technical predicates and the conclusion retain their source-dependent meanings; they have not been defined by this display. The excerpt informally connects the hypercube-cluster condition with existence of least common multiples, but gives neither the relevant order nor the collections for which these must exist. It also omits the graph and its edge convention, and whether diamond completeness is relative to the interval or a larger graph.

The extracted geometric background mentions a quotient of a punctured object by an attractive torus action, and an action fixing $\mathrm{GL}_n/B\subset\mathrm{GL}_{n+1}/B$ while acting nontrivially on the remainder, producing an open/closed decomposition. The notation for the punctured object and its quotient is corrupted, and the decomposition and its relationship to the conjectural formula are not supplied. These fragments do not determine additional precise hypotheses.

Short English statement

The recorded conjecture asks whether the proposed hypercube-cluster formula holds for every eligible $c'\in[x,y]$: the interval $[x,c']$ must be diamond complete, and the edges leaving it from each of its vertices must span a hypercube cluster. The formula and essential technical definitions are missing, so this remains a schematic, statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
