Relative Bogomolov Conjecture for Abelian Schemes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns an abelian scheme $\pi:A\to S$, where the original excerpt specifies that $S$ is an irreducible ${\mathbb{Q}}$-variety, and an irreducible closed subvariety $X\subseteq A$ satisfying $\pi(X)=S$. The excerpt does not define the canonical height or a horizontal torsion coset. Accordingly, the following explicit formulation is conditional on the conventions below, which require confirmation against the complete mathematical source.

Choose a symmetric relatively ample line bundle $\mathcal L$ on $A$. For an algebraic point $x\in A(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})$, put $s=\pi(x)$, let $A_s$ be the fiber over $s$, and define \[ \widehat h_{\mathcal L}(x)=\widehat h_{\mathcal L|_{A_s}}(x), \] where the right side is the Néron--Tate canonical height on the abelian variety $A_s$. For each real $\varepsilon>0$, set \[ X_{\leq\varepsilon}=\{x\in X(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}):\widehat h_{\mathcal L}(x)\leq\varepsilon\}. \] Having a Zariski-dense set of points of arbitrarily small height means that $X_{\leq\varepsilon}$ is Zariski dense in $X_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}}$ for every $\varepsilon>0$.

For a provisional geometric interpretation of the other alternative, call an irreducible closed subvariety $T\subseteq A$ a horizontal torsion coset if $\pi(T)=S$ and every irreducible component of its geometric generic fiber is a translate $B+t$, where $B$ is an abelian subvariety of $A_{\overline\eta}$, $t$ is a torsion point of $A_{\overline\eta}$, and $\overline\eta$ is a geometric generic point of $S$. This is an explicit candidate convention, not a definition recovered from the supplied excerpt.

Under these conventions, the proposed relative Bogomolov statement is that, for every such $\pi$ and $X$, \[ \left(\exists\varepsilon>0:\ \overline{X_{\leq\varepsilon}}^{\,\mathrm{Zar}} \subsetneq X_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}}\right) \quad\text{or}\quad \left(\exists T\subseteq A:\ T\text{ is a horizontal torsion coset},\ X\subseteq T,\ \dim T-\dim X\leq\dim S\right). \] Here $\dim T-\dim X$ is the codimension of $X$ in the containing coset $T$. The alternatives are not asserted to be mutually exclusive. In particular, the second alternative does not require $X$ itself to be a horizontal torsion coset. The polarization convention, geometric density convention, and precise intended definition of horizontal torsion coset remain unresolved source qualifications.

Short English statement

For an irreducible subvariety dominating the base of an abelian scheme, the proposed dichotomy says that some positive height threshold gives a non-dense set of points, or the subvariety lies in a horizontal torsion coset with codimension at most the dimension of the base. The explicit formulation is conditional on height and torsion-coset conventions omitted from the supplied excerpt.
