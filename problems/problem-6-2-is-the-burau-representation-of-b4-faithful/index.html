Problem 6.2 — Is the Burau representation of B4 faithful?

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[B_4=\langle\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3\mid \sigma_1\sigma_3=\sigma_3\sigma_1,\ \sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\ (i=1,2)\rangle\] be the braid group on four strands. With $t$ an indeterminate and $R={\mathbb{Z}}[t,t^{-1}]$, one standard unreduced Burau convention is \[\rho_4:B_4\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_4(R),\qquad \rho_4(\sigma_i)=I_{i-1}\oplus\begin{pmatrix}1-t&t\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\oplus I_{3-i}.\] A representation is faithful if it is injective. The source question is whether \[\ker\rho_4=\{1\},\quad\text{equivalently}\quad \forall\beta\in B_4,\ \rho_4(\beta)=I_4\Longrightarrow\beta=1.\] This is the generic Burau representation, not a representation obtained by assigning a numerical value to $t$. The customary reduced representation is the action after removing the invariant trivial direction; its generic faithfulness question is equivalent. The source does not choose a matrix convention, so the display fixes a standard representative of that question rather than a specialized parameter.

The supplied research summary reports an affirmative resolution in July 2026 by V. Bharathram, J. S. Birman and T. E. Brendle, with a point-pushing argument and an asserted corollary on faithfulness of the Jones representation of $B_4$. It also reports faithfulness for $n\leq3$ attributed to Magnus--Peluso and nonfaithfulness for $n\geq5$ attributed to Moody, Long--Paton and Bigelow. These are supplied literature assertions; neither the cited 2026 result nor its Jones-representation corollary is independently verified by this statement draft.

Short English statement

Does the generic Burau representation distinguish every nonidentity four-strand braid? The registry marks the question solved and supplies a July 2026 affirmative preprint claim, which remains unverified here.
