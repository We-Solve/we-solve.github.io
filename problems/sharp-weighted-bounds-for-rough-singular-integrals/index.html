Math-4285

Sharp Weighted Bounds for Rough Singular Integrals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ denote the fixed Euclidean dimension, and let $S^{n-1}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ carry surface measure $\sigma$. The supplied record does not specify the intended range of dimensions. Let $\Omega\in L^\infty(S^{n-1})$ satisfy \[ \int_{S^{n-1}}\Omega(\theta)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\sigma(\theta)=0. \] Using the usual radial truncation convention, define \[ T_{\Omega,\varepsilon}f(x) =\int_{|y|>\varepsilon}\frac{\Omega(y/|y|)}{|y|^n}f(x-y)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} y, \qquad \varepsilon>0, \] and \[ T_\Omega f(x)=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}T_{\Omega,\varepsilon}f(x), \qquad T_\Omega^\star f(x)=\sup_{\varepsilon>0}|T_{\Omega,\varepsilon}f(x)|. \] The principal-value formula is initially interpreted on smooth compactly supported functions wherever the limit exists; the weighted operator norm refers to the corresponding bounded extension. The record names these operators without specifying their truncation convention.

A weight $w$ is a measurable function positive and finite almost everywhere, with $w,w^{-1}\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}({\mathbb{R}}^n)$. Define \[ [w]_{A_2}=\sup_Q \left(\frac{1}{|Q|}\int_Qw(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right) \left(\frac{1}{|Q|}\int_Qw(x)^{-1}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right), \] where the supremum is over cubes with sides parallel to the coordinate axes. Write $w\in A_2$ when this quantity is finite, and set \[ \|f\|_{L^2(w)}=\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|f(x)|^2w(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right)^{1/2}. \]

  1. For each admissible fixed $n$ and $\Omega$, does there exist a finite constant $C_{n,\Omega}$, independent of $w$, such that for every $w\in A_2$, \[ \|T_\Omega\|_{L^2(w)\to L^2(w)}\le C_{n,\Omega}[w]_{A_2}? \] The supplied statement asks to replace the quadratic dependence $[w]_{A_2}^2$ by linear dependence. It does not specify the dependence of the implicit constant on $\Omega$. In particular, the stronger uniform formulation $C_{n,\Omega}=C_n\|\Omega\|_{L^\infty(S^{n-1})}$ should be distinguished from the fixed-kernel formulation displayed here.
  2. Is $T_\Omega^\star$ of weak type $(1,1)$ with respect to Lebesgue measure? Explicitly, for each admissible fixed $n$ and $\Omega$, does there exist a finite constant $A_{n,\Omega}$ such that for every $f\in L^1({\mathbb{R}}^n)$ and every $\lambda>0$, \[ \left|\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:T_\Omega^\star f(x)>\lambda\}\right| \le \frac{A_{n,\Omega}}{\lambda}\|f\|_{L^1({\mathbb{R}}^n)}? \]
The supplied dated curation labels the combined record partially solved, attributes resolution of the second question to Lai, and describes the first question as remaining open with quadratic bounds as the general benchmark. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

For a bounded mean-zero angular kernel, ask whether its rough homogeneous singular integral has a weighted $L^2$ bound linear in the $A_2$ characteristic, and whether its maximal truncation satisfies an unweighted weak $(1,1)$ estimate. The supplied curation attributes a solution of the endpoint question to Lai and leaves the linear weighted bound open; that assessment requires independent verification.
