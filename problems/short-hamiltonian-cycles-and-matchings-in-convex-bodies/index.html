Short Hamiltonian Cycles and Matchings in Convex Bodies

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Work in the Euclidean plane ${\mathbb{R}}^2$, with Euclidean norm $\|\cdot\|_2$. Write \[ B_{\mathrm{cl}}=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:\|x\|_2\leq 1\},\qquad B_{\mathrm{op}}=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:\|x\|_2<1\}. \] The supplied source calls $B$ the unit disk without explicitly specifying whether its boundary is included. The questions below are therefore stated with $B$ denoting the intended one of these two disks; that boundary convention remains to be confirmed.

For a finite set $P=\{p_1,\ldots,p_n\}\subseteq B$ with $n\geq 3$, a Hamiltonian cycle means a cyclic ordering $p_{\sigma(1)},\ldots,p_{\sigma(n)}$ of all its points, with straight edges between consecutive points and from the last point to the first. Its squared-edge cost is \[ H(P,\sigma)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|p_{\sigma(i+1)}-p_{\sigma(i)}\|_2^2, \qquad \sigma(n+1)=\sigma(1), \] where $\sigma$ is a permutation of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. No restriction on edge crossings is imposed in this graph-theoretic formulation; the supplied record does not explicitly discuss crossings.

Hamiltonian-cycle question. Is it true that for every integer $n\geq 3$ and every $n$-point set $P\subseteq B$, there exists such a permutation $\sigma$ satisfying \[ H(P,\sigma)\leq 8? \] The source says only that $P$ is finite. Its intended cycle convention for $|P|\in\{0,1,2\}$ is not supplied, so those cases are not included in the displayed formulation.

For a finite set $P$ of even cardinality, a perfect matching is a collection $M$ of pairwise disjoint two-element subsets of $P$ whose union is $P$. Define its squared-edge cost by \[ W(M)=\sum_{\{p,q\}\in M}\|p-q\|_2^2. \] Perfect-matching question. Is it true that for every integer $m\geq 0$ and every $2m$-point set $P\subseteq B$, there exists a perfect matching $M$ satisfying \[ W(M)\leq 4? \] Here the empty set has the empty perfect matching, with cost zero.

Requested extension. Determine analogous bounds for finite point sets in other convex figures $C$. The cleaned statement describes these as convex bodies; a conventional planar convex body is a compact convex subset of ${\mathbb{R}}^2$ with nonempty interior. The original statement asks for analogues with $C$ in place of the disk "or the unit square." The supplied record gives no unit-square bound, precise class of convex figures, normalization of $C$, or proposed dependence of the bounds on $C$. Thus this extension is an open-ended request, not a uniquely specified additional inequality.

Short English statement

For every finite set of points in the Euclidean unit disk, ask whether a Hamiltonian cycle can have total squared edge length at most $8$, and, when the number of points is even, whether a perfect matching can have total squared edge length at most $4$. The cycle statement above covers at least three points. Boundary conventions, smaller cycle cases, and the intended bounds for other convex figures require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
