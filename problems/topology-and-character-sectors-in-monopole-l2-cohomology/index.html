Topology and character sectors in monopole L2-cohomology

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
For a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$, $L^2$ norms use the metric volume and pointwise metric on forms. Reduced $L^2$ cohomology is $\ker d/\overline{\operatorname{im}d}$ for the specified closed $L^2$ complex. On a complete manifold its harmonic representatives satisfy $d\alpha=d^*\alpha=0$ and $\int_M|\alpha|^2<\infty$. Higher-dimensional monopole moduli are complete noncompact spaces obtained from gauge equations with specified charges and behavior at infinity. Special holonomy constrains parallel tensors and curvature but does not itself characterize the ends. A topological description of $L^2$ cohomology must account for the metric growth and end conditions. L-modules in Saper’s framework are a proposed tool, not assumed to apply automatically. Extended $L^2$ harmonic forms in the final related question need not themselves be $L^2$; the approximation and residual condition are part of their definition.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Find a topological interpretation of $L^2$ cohomology for higher-dimensional monopole moduli spaces. Identify suitable tools and decide whether Saper’s L-module techniques can be used. For the associated complete special-holonomy manifolds, understand the asymptotic geometry and $L^2$ cohomology, and find general vanishing theorems, potentially before a complete asymptotic description is available. In the $SU(2)$ monopole case over $\mathbb R^3$, prove that the harmonic forms obtained by the source's cited Segal--Selby construction exhaust all $L^2$ harmonic forms, rather than merely constructing some of them. The following related question in the source asks to study extended $L^2$ harmonic forms on noncompact spaces: forms $\omega$ with $(d+d^*)\omega=0$, admitting locally convergent approximants $\omega_j\in C_0^\infty$ such that $(d+d^*)\omega_j\to0$ in $L^2$.
Scope and source qualification.
The title names character sectors, but the full source asks about the entire moduli-space cohomology and geometric programme. Source remarks about known SU(2) constructions are historical context and do not establish surjectivity here. The surjectivity question in the source remarks and the following anonymous extended-form question are recorded explicitly; the latter is not attributed to Hunsicker. The catalogue title/status was paired with a machine-generated research attempt; that attempt is not peer-reviewed evidence of settlement. The statement covers the original source questions rather than only the title’s subcase.

Short English statement

Give topological descriptions, asymptotics and vanishing results for L² cohomology of monopole and special-holonomy moduli spaces.
