Math-3401

Jump Processes from Families of Lévy Measures

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Four requests. Given a family of Lévy measures $\mu(x,dh)$ on ${\mathbb{R}}^d$, determine when it defines corresponding jump processes; obtain their heat-kernel properties; determine regularity of their harmonic functions; and determine whether the generator extends to that of a Feller semigroup. These are four linked tasks, not a claim that every pointwise family automatically has all the stated properties.

A Lévy measure at $x$ is a positive Borel measure on ${\mathbb{R}}^d\setminus\{0\}$ with $\int(1\wedge|h|^2)\mu(x,dh)<\infty$. To be a jump kernel the family also needs appropriate measurability in $x$, not merely this pointwise integrability. One standard compensated candidate, with zero drift relative to the displayed truncation, is\[Lf(x)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d\setminus\{0\}}\left(f(x+h)-f(x)-\mathbf1_{\{|h|\le1\}}\nabla f(x)\cdot h\right)\,\mu(x,dh),\qquad f\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^d).\] The source does not supply its generator convention, drift, symmetry, or spatial regularity, so this formula is explicit explanatory notation rather than an asserted model for every intended case. A process associated with $L$ can be specified through the martingale problem for $f(X_t)-f(X_0)-\int_0^tLf(X_s)\,ds$, with any lifetime and localization conditions stated. Existence, uniqueness in law, conservativeness, and the strong Markov property are distinct requirements needing hypotheses.

If a transition semigroup has a density $P_t(x,dy)=p(t,x,y)\,dy$, its heat-kernel task includes existence and the intended bounds or regularity of $p$. Such a Lebesgue density is not automatic for arbitrary jump kernels. In an open set $D$, a probabilistically harmonic function obeys the appropriate exit-time mean-value property $h(x)=\mathbb E^x h(X_{\tau_U})$ for suitable $U\Subset D$, whenever the values and expectations are defined; determining its continuity, Hölder regularity, or Harnack properties requires specifying the desired class. A pointwise equation $Lh=0$ is not imposed on a function outside the operator's domain.

A Feller semigroup is a strongly continuous positive contraction semigroup on $C_0({\mathbb{R}}^d)$. The extension question asks whether there is such a generator $G$ with the intended test-function domain included in $\operatorname{Dom}(G)$ and $Gf=Lf$ there; it is not automatically a claim that the unclosed operator $L$ itself is a generator or that its closure is the only extension. The family class, uniform integrability, behavior at infinity, desired estimates, and domain assumptions are not supplied.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the record partially solved. It reports constructions, heat-kernel estimates, Harnack and harmonic regularity results, and Feller properties for important variable-order classes while saying the full arbitrary-measure program is not settled. Exact theorem hypotheses are absent, and this dated assessment requires independent verification.

Short English statement

Find hypotheses giving a jump process from the measure family, heat-kernel information, harmonic-function regularity, and a Feller-generator extension. Pointwise Lévy integrability alone does not specify the required spatial measurability, operator convention, or analytic properties.
