Informal Question about a Rescaled Einstein Limit

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\overline M$ be a smooth manifold with boundary and let $M=\operatorname{int}(\overline M)$. A boundary defining function is a smooth function $\rho:\overline M\to[0,\infty)$ such that \[ \rho^{-1}(0)=\partial\overline M,\qquad \rho>0\text{ on }M,\qquad \mathrm d\rho\ne0\text{ on }\partial\overline M. \] The supplied passage calls a metric $g$ Poincaré--Einstein when it is Einstein on the interior and $\rho^2g$ extends smoothly across the boundary. In Riemannian notation the Einstein condition is \[ \operatorname{Ric}(g)=\lambda g \] for a constant $\lambda$. The passage does not specify the dimension, a normalization or sign for $\lambda$, or completeness hypotheses.

To obtain the usual Riemannian conformal infinity, the extension $\overline g=\rho^2g$ must additionally be a nondegenerate Riemannian metric up to the boundary. This requirement is not explicit in the supplied wording. Under this requirement, write $\iota:\partial\overline M\hookrightarrow\overline M$ for the inclusion and set \[ h=\iota^*\overline g,\qquad [h]=\{e^{2f}h:f\in C^\infty(\partial\overline M,{\mathbb{R}})\}. \] If $\rho'=e^\omega\rho$ is another defining function near the boundary, then \[ \iota^*((\rho')^2g)=e^{2\omega|_{\partial\overline M}}h. \] Thus $[h]$ is independent of the defining function. It is called the conformal infinity, and $g$ is a filling of the boundary conformal manifold $(\partial\overline M,[h])$. The source instead says a filling of $(M,g)$; its intended boundary data require clarification.

Context retained from the passage. The source identifies the Poincaré model of the hyperbolic metric as Poincaré--Einstein. Its damaged typography appears to describe $g_m^+$ as a filling of $S^3/{\mathbb{Z}}_m$ and $g_m^-$ as its "cap." These symbols are retained only as a tentative transcription: the metrics, allowed values of $m$, quotient action, boundary conformal class, and meaning of "cap" are unspecified. The passage attributes fillings of this quotient to Page--Pope and asserts that their apparently different fillings must be isometric to the author's fillings. The hypotheses and identification needed to formulate that assertion are absent. It also mentions the role of such fillings in Witten's AdS/CFT correspondence and expresses hope that the described fillings could help determine renormalised volumes in the sense of reference [An]. These are supplied contextual claims and motivations, not additional problem statements or verified conclusions.

Short English statement

The record asks whether an unspecified rescaled Einstein limit is "new or interesting." It recalls conformal infinity and Poincaré--Einstein fillings, but does not define the metric or give a testable target.
