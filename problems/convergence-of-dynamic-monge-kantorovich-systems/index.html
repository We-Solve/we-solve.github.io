Convergence of Dynamic Monge–Kantorovich Systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record denotes by $\mu(t)$ the density variable and by $u(t)$ the potential variable of a dynamic Monge--Kantorovich system. It denotes by $(\mu^*,u^*)$ an optimal-transport density and potential satisfying the associated stationary Monge--Kantorovich equations. Neither the evolution system nor the stationary equations are supplied explicitly.

Conjectural convergence target. For a solution trajectory $t\mapsto(\mu(t),u(t))$ defined for all $t\geq 0$, the proposed assertion is \[ \left(\mu(t),u(t)\right)\longrightarrow\left(\mu^*,u^*\right) \qquad\text{as }t\to\infty. \] This formula records the supplied target only: a fully quantified convergence conjecture cannot be specified from this record. The spatial domain, transport data and cost, evolution equations, initial and boundary conditions, admissible solution class, and convergence topology are absent. The record also does not specify how the limiting potential is selected or normalized, whether convergence of potentials is understood modulo additive constants, or whether the assertion concerns every admissible initial state and every corresponding global solution. No uniqueness of the limiting pair or global existence is asserted here.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the dynamic Monge--Kantorovich density and potential approach a stationary optimal-transport density and potential as time tends to infinity. The supplied record leaves the equations, admissible data, solution quantifiers, potential normalization, and meaning of convergence unspecified, so this remains a schematic statement requiring mathematical review.
