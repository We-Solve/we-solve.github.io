Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.88

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathfrak A$ be the variety of abelian groups and $\mathfrak N_c$ the variety of nilpotent groups of class at most $c$. Under the standard convention for products of group varieties, $G\in\mathfrak A\mathfrak N_c$ means that there exists an abelian normal subgroup $A\trianglelefteq G$ such that $G/A\in\mathfrak N_c$.

Let $F_r=F_r(\mathfrak A\mathfrak N_c)$ be the relatively free group of rank $r$ in this variety. Thus a free generating set $X$ of cardinality $r$ has the property that every map from $X$ to a group in $\mathfrak A\mathfrak N_c$ extends uniquely to a homomorphism from $F_r$. An element $g\in F_r$ is primitive if it belongs to some free basis of $F_r$.

Question. Does there exist an algorithm recognizing primitive elements of $F_r$? Under the usual interpretation with fixed positive integers $c$ and $r$, fix a free basis $X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_r\}$. The requested algorithm must halt on every finite word $w$ in $X^{\pm1}$ and decide whether its value $g=w(X)\in F_r$ satisfies \[ \exists\,(b_1,\ldots,b_r)\text{ a free basis of }F_r\quad b_1=g. \] The supplied source does not explicitly specify the ranges of $c$ and $r$, whether infinite rank or the boundary case $c=0$ is included, or whether a single algorithm uniform in $c$ and $r$ is required. The finite-rank, fixed-parameter formulation above is therefore an explicit interpretation requiring review.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether one can decide, from a word in a relatively free abelian-by-nilpotent group, whether the represented element belongs to a free basis. The intended parameter ranges and any requirement of uniformity remain unspecified.
