Uniform interior gradient Hölder estimates for infinity-harmonic functions

OpenAI's claim

Interior infinity-harmonic regularity: In each dimension $d\ge3$, bounded infinity-harmonic functions are claimed to satisfy a uniform local gradient estimate of class $C^{\alpha_d}$. The exponent is positive and dimension-dependent, with no explicit endpoint or boundary claim.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every integer $d\ge3$ there are $0<\alpha_d\le1/3$ and $C_d<\infty$ such that each bounded continuous viscosity solution of \[\Delta_\infty u=\sum_{i,j=1}^d u_iu_ju_{ij}=0\quad\text{in }B_1\subset\mathbb R^d\] is claimed to lie in $C^{1,\alpha_d}(B_{1/2})$ and satisfy \[\|\nabla u\|_{L^\infty(B_{1/2})}+[\nabla u]_{C^{0,\alpha_d}(B_{1/2})}\le C_d(\sup_{B_1}u-\inf_{B_1}u).\] The equation is the unnormalized infinity Laplacian, with upper and lower $C^2$ tests including those of zero gradient.

Outline of the argument

Interior infinity-harmonic regularity: If uniform affine approximation were too slow, a rescaling would produce a global limiting solution that remains nonaffine. Intersecting-cylinder estimates retain affine fits at every center and scale. A Liouville argument uses a variance-penalized horizontal envelope and localized contacts to force that limit to be affine, contradicting the failure and recovering a Hölder gradient estimate.
