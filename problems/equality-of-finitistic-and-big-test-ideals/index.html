Equality of Finitistic and Big Test Ideals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian domain with identity and characteristic $p>0$. The ring is $F$-finite if its Frobenius map $F:R\to R$, $r\mapsto r^p$, is finite; equivalently, $R$, with scalar multiplication $r\cdot x=r^p x$, is a finitely generated $R$-module. Let $0\ne\mathfrak a\subseteq R$ be an ideal, allowing $\mathfrak a=R$.

For a precise formulation, use the customary interpretation $t\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the number domain of $t$ or define its test-ideal conventions; the following definitions state the interpretation used in this draft.

For $e\geq0$, put $q=p^e$. Let $R^{(e)}$ be the $R$-bimodule whose underlying additive group is $R$, with ordinary left multiplication and right action $x\cdot r=xr^q$. For an $R$-module $M$, set $F_R^e(M)=R^{(e)}\otimes_R M$. Define the $\mathfrak a^t$-tight closure of zero by \[ 0_M^{*\mathfrak a^t} = \left\{z\in M:\begin{array}{l} \text{there exist }c\in R\setminus\{0\}\text{ and }e_0\geq0\text{ such that}\\ \text{for every integer }e\geq e_0\text{ and every } b\in\mathfrak a^{\lceil tp^e\rceil},\\ cb(1\otimes z)=0\text{ in }F_R^e(M) \end{array}\right\}. \] Here $\mathfrak a^t$ denotes the ideal--exponent pair used in tight closure, rather than an ordinary ideal power with a nonintegral exponent. For a subset $N\subseteq M$, write $\operatorname{Ann}_R(N)=\{r\in R:rn=0\text{ for every }n\in N\}$. Under the stated convention, define \[ \tau_{\mathrm{fg}}(\mathfrak a^t) =\bigcap_{\substack{M\text{ a finitely generated}\\R\text{-module}}} \operatorname{Ann}_R\left(0_M^{*\mathfrak a^t}\right), \qquad \tau_b(\mathfrak a^t) =\bigcap_{M\text{ an }R\text{-module}} \operatorname{Ann}_R\left(0_M^{*\mathfrak a^t}\right). \] The question is whether, for every $F$-finite Noetherian domain $R$ of positive characteristic, every nonzero ideal $\mathfrak a\subseteq R$, and every $t\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}$, one has \[ \tau_{\mathrm{fg}}(\mathfrak a^t)=\tau_b(\mathfrak a^t). \] No locality, normality, or further hypothesis is stated in the supplied record. The real-exponent interpretation and the explicit tight-closure conventions above require comparison with the cited source before acceptance.

Short English statement

The question asks whether testing the tight closure of zero in finitely generated modules gives the same ideal as testing it in all modules, for every $F$-finite Noetherian domain of positive characteristic, every nonzero ideal, and every positive exponent. This draft uses positive real exponents and explicitly stated standard conventions, which the supplied record does not itself define.
