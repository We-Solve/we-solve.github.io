Math-3461

Collapse Preservation under Subdivision

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A simplicial complex is a collection of simplices closed under taking faces; a subcomplex is a subcollection that is itself a simplicial complex. An elementary simplicial collapse removes a pair of simplices $\tau,\sigma$, where $\tau$ is a nonempty codimension-one face of $\sigma$ and $\sigma$ is the unique maximal simplex containing $\tau$. Write $K\searrow L$ if $L$ is obtained from $K$ by a finite sequence of elementary simplicial collapses, allowing a sequence of length zero.

A subdivision $D$ of $C$ triangulates each simplex of $C$ compatibly with its faces. Under the subdivision identification $|D|=|C|$, its restriction to a subcomplex $C'\subseteq C$ is \[ D'=D|_{C'}:=\{\delta\in D:|\delta|\subseteq |C'|\}, \] which is a subdivision of $C'$.

Subdivision preservation question. For every simplicial complex $C$, every subcomplex $C'\subseteq C$, and every subdivision $D$ of $C$ restricting to a subdivision $D'$ of $C'$, is the implication \[ C\searrow C'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad D\searrow D' \] valid?

Unresolved scope qualification. The supplied statement does not specify whether the complexes and subdivisions must be finite or locally finite, or whether infinite collapse sequences are permitted. The definition above gives the finite-sequence interpretation of collapse; it does not settle the intended scope for infinite complexes or subdivisions. These conventions require confirmation from the source before a definitive formulation is accepted.

Supplied literature qualification. The supplied curation labels the problem partially solved and reports that the stated subdivision question remains open, while the barycentric subdivision $\operatorname{sd}D$ collapses to $\operatorname{sd}D'$, with additional positive cases in low dimensions. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified assertions here. The hypotheses of the reported barycentric result and the dimensions and hypotheses of the further positive cases are not specified in the record.

Short English statement

If a simplicial complex collapses onto a subcomplex, must every subdivision collapse onto the induced subdivision of that subcomplex? The source leaves finiteness and collapse-sequence conventions unspecified. Its supplied dated assessment reports partial progress after barycentric subdivision and in unspecified low-dimensional cases.
