Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.86

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group and $p$ a prime. Write $\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$ for the set of Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$, and suppose that \[ s_p(G):=|\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)|=1+kp>1, \qquad k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}. \] Choose $P\in\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$. Define \[ G_p:=\{g\in G:\operatorname{ord}(g)=p^a\text{ for some }a\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}\}, \] so that the identity is included. The source attributes to Frobenius the assertion that \[ f_p(G):=\frac{|G_p|}{|P|}\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}. \] It further uses the integral parametrization \[ f_p(G)=1+\ell(p-1), \qquad \ell=\frac{f_p(G)-1}{p-1}. \] The intended question, attributed to P. Schmid, is whether every finite group $G$ and prime $p$ satisfying $s_p(G)>1$ obey \[ \ell^p\ge k^{p-1}. \] There is an unresolved inconsistency in the accompanying source assertions. The record states \[ f_p(G)=1+\ell(p-1)\le k-\frac{k-1}{p-1}, \] and says that usually $\ell<k$, but that $\ell=k$ if $k<p$. In the latter case, the asserted equality gives $f_p(G)=1+k(p-1)>k$, whereas the displayed upper bound is at most $k$. These auxiliary claims are retained here as supplied, require source verification, and are not imposed as additional hypotheses restricting the universal question. The word "usually" has no specified mathematical quantifier.

For context, a finite group is called $p$-solvable if it has a subnormal series whose factors are $p$-groups or groups of order coprime to $p$. The record reports that P. Gheri proved \[ f_p(G)^p\ge s_p(G)^{p-1} \] when $G$ is $p$-solvable. This is the supplied contextual result, with its original quantities and hypothesis; it is not identified with the requested inequality in $\ell$ and $k$.

Short English statement

For a finite group with more than one Sylow $p$-subgroup, write their number as $1+kp$, and write the number of $p$-elements divided by the order of a Sylow $p$-subgroup as $1+\ell(p-1)$. P. Schmid asks whether $\ell^p\ge k^{p-1}$ always holds. An accompanying bound in the supplied record conflicts with its stated case $\ell=k$ for $k<p$, so that part of the formulation requires review.
