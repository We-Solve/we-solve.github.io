Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.109

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $r\geq 1$, let $F=F(x_1,\ldots,x_r)$ be the free group on the variables of a word $w$, and assume $w\neq 1$ in $F$. For a group $G$, define \[ V_w(G)=\{w(g_1,\ldots,g_r):g_1,\ldots,g_r\in G\}, \qquad w(G)=\langle V_w(G)\rangle. \] The width of $w$ in $G$ is at most $n$ if every element of $w(G)$ is a product of at most $n$ elements of $V_w(G)\cup V_w(G)^{-1}$. Since $w(1,\ldots,1)=1$, this also permits a product of exactly $n$ such factors by padding with identity values.

A word $w$ is uniformly elliptic in a class $\mathcal C$ if there exists a function $f:{\mathbb{N}}_{\geq 1}\to{\mathbb{N}}_{\geq 1}$ such that, for every $d\geq 1$ and every group $G\in\mathcal C$ generated by at most $d$ elements, the width of $w$ in $G$ is at most $f(d)$. The function may depend on $w$ and $\mathcal C$, but not on $G$.

Write $F'=[F,F]$, $F''=[F',F']$, and, for each prime $p$, \[ (F')^p=\langle u^p:u\in F'\rangle. \] Thus $F''(F')^p$ denotes the product of these normal subgroups of $F$.

Let $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sol}}$ be the class of all finite soluble groups and let $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{fin}}$ be the class of all finite groups. Here a group is soluble if its derived series reaches the trivial subgroup after finitely many steps.

Problem (D. Segal). Determine whether each of the following two assertions holds: for every nonidentity word $w\in F$, \[ \begin{aligned} \text{(a)}\quad &w\text{ is uniformly elliptic in }\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sol}} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (\forall p\text{ prime})\;w\notin F''(F')^p;\\ \text{(b)}\quad &w\text{ is uniformly elliptic in }\mathcal C_{\mathrm{fin}} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (\forall p\text{ prime})\;w\notin F''(F')^p. \end{aligned} \]

For background, the supplied record attributes to A. Jaikin-Zapirain the result that, for each fixed prime $p$, a nonidentity word $w$ is uniformly elliptic in the class of finite $p$-groups if and only if $w\notin F''(F')^p$.

The record also states that, for a class $\mathcal C$ of finite groups, uniform ellipticity is equivalent to the algebraic verbal subgroup $w(G)$ being closed in every finitely generated pro-$\mathcal C$ group $G$. A pro-$\mathcal C$ group means an inverse limit of finite groups in $\mathcal C$, equipped with its inverse-limit topology; finite generation here means topological finite generation. The record supplies no closure hypotheses on a general class $\mathcal C$, so its unrestricted formulation is retained as a source qualification requiring review. The two classes in parts (a) and (b) are explicitly specified above.

Short English statement

For each nonidentity group word, ask whether its width admits a bound depending only on the number of generators throughout all finite soluble groups, and separately throughout all finite groups, exactly when the word lies outside $F''(F')^p$ for every prime $p$. The supplied record attributes the corresponding fixed-prime result for finite $p$-groups to A. Jaikin-Zapirain.
