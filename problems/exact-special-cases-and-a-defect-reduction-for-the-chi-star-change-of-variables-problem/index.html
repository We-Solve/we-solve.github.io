Math-1648

Exact special cases and a defect reduction for the chi-star change-of-variables problem

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ be a self-adjoint tuple in a tracial von Neumann algebra $(M,\tau)$. Write $\chi$ for microstates free entropy and $\chi^*$ for non-microstates free entropy, with consistent normalization. For an admissible analytic self-adjoint change of variables $F$, let $JF(X)=(\partial_jF_i(X))_{i,j}$ be its free difference-quotient Jacobian; $\partial_jX_k=\delta_{jk}1\otimes1$. Put \[ \mathcal J_F(X)=(\operatorname{Tr}_n\otimes\tau\otimes\tau^{\mathrm{op}}) \left(\log|JF(X)|\right), \] where the matrix trace is unnormalized. Analytic domains, invertibility, and determinant-integrability assumptions must be those needed for Voiculescu's microstates change-of-variables formula. The source asks whether the corresponding formula \[ \chi^*(F(X))=\chi^*(X)+\mathcal J_F(X) \] holds as well. The extract supplies none of those hypotheses or relative-entropy conventions.

The aid reports the following exact special cases. For conditional non-microstates entropy relative to an unchanged algebra $B$, conformal affine changes satisfy \[ \chi^*(aUX+b:B)=\chi^*(X:B)+n\log|a|, \] where $a\ne0$ is real, $U\in O(n)$, and $b\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$. For one variable and a bi-Lipschitz $C^1$ real functional-calculus map on the spectral interval, it reports \[ \chi^*(f(X))=\chi^*(X)+ \iint\log\left|\frac{f(s)-f(t)}{s-t}\right|\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_X(s){\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_X(t), \] with diagonal value $\log|f'(s)|$ and the relevant finiteness conventions. Separate coordinate transformations on a free family obey the corresponding sum of these terms; freeness is not silently dropped. For a standard semicircular family $S$ and arbitrary $A\in\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{R}})$, it states $\chi^*(AS+b)=\chi^*(S)+\log|\det A|$. This input-specific linear case does not assert the formula for arbitrary interacting tuples.

For a tuple and image with both entropies finite, define the gap $D(X)=\chi^*(X)-\chi(X)$ and the proposed-formula residual \[ r_F(X)=\chi^*(F(X))-\chi^*(X)-\mathcal J_F(X). \] Whenever the microstates formula applies, the aid's exact reduction is \[ r_F(X)=D(F(X))-D(X). \] Thus the proposed formula is precisely invariance of the entropy gap under that change of variables. On an admissible analytic orbit of a semicircular tuple, where $D(S)=0$, it is equivalent to $\chi(F(S))=\chi^*(F(S))$. For composable admissible maps satisfying the Jacobian chain rule it also gives \[ r_{G\circ F}(X)=r_F(X)+r_G(F(X)). \] Endpoint finiteness is essential to these subtractions; no $\infty-\infty$ expression is used. The aid describes the general interacting nonlinear case as unresolved in its checked literature. The reduction does not establish gap invariance or equality of the two entropies in general.

Short English statement

The question asks whether non-microstates free entropy has the same Jacobian transformation law as microstates entropy. The supplied result records exact restricted cases and rewrites the general residual as the change in the gap between the two entropies, without proving that the gap is invariant.
