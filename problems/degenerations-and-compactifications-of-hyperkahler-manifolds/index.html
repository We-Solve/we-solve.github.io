Degenerations and Compactifications of Hyperkähler Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Scope and conventions. The supplied record poses a research programme about hyperkähler manifolds of dimension greater than two; it does not specify enough hypotheses to determine a single classification problem or theorem.

In one common complex-geometric convention, an irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold is a simply connected compact Kähler manifold $X$ such that \[ H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)={\mathbb{C}}\sigma, \] where $\sigma$ is an everywhere nondegenerate holomorphic two-form. Under this convention, $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X=2n$, and the dimension restriction becomes $n\geq 2$. The source does not specify whether its term "hyperkähler manifold" means precisely this class, a broader class, or whether dimension is complex or real. This definition therefore explains a possible convention without adding it to the supplied hypotheses.

A common framework for a one-parameter degeneration is a proper flat family \[ \pi:\mathcal X\longrightarrow\Delta, \qquad \Delta^*=\Delta\setminus\{0\}, \] over a complex disc, smooth over $\Delta^*$, whose fibres over $\Delta^*$ are manifolds in the class under consideration. The fibre $\mathcal X_0$ is the limiting fibre. The source does not specify this framework, permitted singularities, projectivity, or the equivalence relation used to classify such families or limiting fibres.

For a specified object $Y$ and deformation category, a versal deformation is a deformation over a pointed base germ from which every sufficiently small deformation of $Y$ in that category is induced by base change; the inducing map need not be unique. A partial compactification of a moduli space $M$ generally means a space $\overline M$ containing $M$ as a dense open subspace and adding boundary objects, without necessarily requiring $\overline M$ to be proper. The intended deformation category, moduli problem, boundary objects, and additional properties of $\overline M$ are unspecified here.

Source question. For hyperkähler manifolds of dimension greater than two, with the preceding conventions still to be fixed:

  1. Classify their degenerations, after specifying the families or limiting objects to be classified and the equivalence relation.
  2. Describe the known examples of such degenerations.
  3. Describe what is known about their versal deformation spaces, after specifying which objects are being deformed.
  4. Determine when their moduli spaces admit partial compactifications, after specifying the moduli problem and the required boundary and compactification properties.
These four requests retain the source's scope. The missing specifications prevent their replacement by a uniquely determined quantified mathematical assertion.

Short English statement

The source asks for a classification and examples of degenerations of hyperkähler manifolds of dimension greater than two, information about versal deformation spaces, and conditions for partial compactifications of their moduli spaces. Its geometric conventions and moduli conditions are not fixed, so this remains a research programme requiring clarification and human mathematical review.
