Elementary expansion for Grothendieck infinity-groupoids

OpenAI's claim

For every coherator under the Ara--Henry convention, weak globular infinity-groupoids are claimed to model spaces; elementary cellular expansions preserve components and all homotopy groups, resolving Henry's pushout question. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Fix any Grothendieck coherator $\mathcal C$ in the convention whose generating filler of dimension $j$ has pasting-scheme arity of dimension at most $j$. Let $D_n$ be the free model on an $n$-globe, with source inclusion $s_n:D_n\to D_{n+1}$. For any cellular $\mathcal C$-infinity-groupoid $X$ and any cell $a:D_n\to X$, $n\ge0$, OpenAI claims \[ X\longrightarrow X\amalg_{D_n}D_{n+1} \] is a weak equivalence, inducing a bijection of components and isomorphisms of all based homotopy groups. Cellular means built from the initial model by possibly transfinite boundary attachments. The statement is an elementary-expansion theorem for every coherator, used in the homotopy-hypothesis argument.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 312, 2026-09-24): Reflection preserves low-dimensional cells while making higher fillers unique. Descending induction on expansion dimension, combined with partial cylinders fixed on lower boundaries, controls newly generated composites and coherence cells. Stagewise interpretation of free operations gives endpoint lifts and a retract argument proving expansion invariance; the resulting semi-model structure then invokes the comparison with spaces.
