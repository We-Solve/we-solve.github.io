Higher-Codimension Weyl Laws and Equidistributed Min–Max Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p\in{\mathbb{N}}$ tend to infinity through positive integers. The source denotes higher-codimension $p$-widths of $M$ by $\omega_p^k(M)$ and its Riemannian volume by $\operatorname{vol}(M)$. The displayed exponents suggest that $M$ has ambient dimension $n+1$ and that the objects measured by $\omega_p^k$ have dimension $k$. These dimensional conventions are not explicitly defined in the supplied excerpt. Neither the admissible values of $n,k$ nor the precise class of manifolds, boundary conditions, coefficient groups, cycle spaces, or sweepout classes defining these widths are specified. Consequently, the following records the mathematical targets with those qualifications unresolved.
  1. Determine the constants denoted by $a(n)$ in the source. The underlying Weyl-law normalization defining these constants is not supplied, and their relationship to the constants $a(n,k)$ below is not stated explicitly.
  2. Decide whether there are constants $a(n,k)$, depending only on the indicated dimensions, such that, for every compact Riemannian manifold $M$ in the intended but unspecified class and every admissible pair $(n,k)$ in the intended higher-codimension range, \[ \lim_{p\to\infty} \omega_p^k(M)\,p^{-\frac{n+1-k}{n+1}} = a(n,k)\,\operatorname{vol}(M)^{\frac{k}{n+1}}. \] The formula is preserved exactly as supplied. The compact Riemannian setting is identified in the supplied literature assessment; the excerpt does not specify whether manifolds are closed or may have boundary, or how boundary conditions enter the widths.
  3. Determine analogous asymptotic limits for the quantities called Steenrod-power widths in the source. The relevant cohomology operations, indexing, width construction, and asymptotic normalization are not supplied, so no specific limit formula for this part can be extracted.
  4. Determine whether $p$-parameter min--max families yield minimal surfaces that become equidistributed as $p\to\infty$. The source does not specify the dimension meant by "surfaces," the quantifier over families and choices of min--max outputs, multiplicities, averaging procedure, or the measures and convergence defining equidistribution. This part therefore remains a qualitative question rather than a fully quantified convergence assertion.

These are statement-only targets. The supplied open-status labels and literature assessment are dated source curation, not mathematical conclusions verified in this draft.

Short English statement

The source asks for Weyl-law constants, a higher-codimension width asymptotic on compact Riemannian manifolds, analogous Steenrod-power asymptotics, and equidistribution of min--max minimal surfaces. It supplies the displayed higher-codimension limit but omits several definitions, hypotheses, and convergence conventions needed for a complete precise formulation.
