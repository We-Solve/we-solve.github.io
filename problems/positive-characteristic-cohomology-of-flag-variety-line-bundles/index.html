Math-823

Positive Characteristic Cohomology of Flag Variety Line Bundles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$. Let $X$ be a flag variety over $k$, and let $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ denote the group of isomorphism classes of line bundles on $X$. A line bundle is a locally free $\mathcal O_X$-module of rank one. For a line bundle $\mathcal L$ and an integer $i\geq 0$, write $H^i(X,\mathcal L)$ for its sheaf cohomology, regarded as a $k$-vector space.

Problem (statement scheme with unresolved scope). For every positive characteristic and every field and flag variety within the intended scope of the source, determine \[ H^i(X,\mathcal L) \qquad \text{for every }[\mathcal L]\in\operatorname{Pic}(X) \text{ and every integer }i\geq 0. \] In particular, give necessary and sufficient conditions on the parameters $k,X,\mathcal L,i$ for \[ H^i(X,\mathcal L)=0, \qquad\text{equivalently, characterize when }H^i(X,\mathcal L)\neq 0. \]

Short English statement

Determine the cohomology of every line bundle on the flag varieties intended by the source in characteristic $p>0$, including exact criteria for vanishing in each degree. The allowed fields, flag varieties, and desired representation structure remain unspecified.
