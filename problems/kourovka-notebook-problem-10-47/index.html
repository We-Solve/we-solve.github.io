Kourovka Notebook Problem 10.47

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ be an associative commutative ring with identity such that $2\cdot 1_R$ and $3\cdot 1_R$ are units. The identity is implicit in the source's requirement that $2$ and $3$ have inverses.

The supplied record does not define $PE_2(R)$. Under the conventional interpretation as the projective elementary group, define \[ E_2(R)=\left\langle \begin{pmatrix}1&r\\0&1\end{pmatrix}, \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\r&1\end{pmatrix} \;\middle|\;r\in R \right\rangle\leq \operatorname{GL}_2(R), \] and \[ PE_2(R)=E_2(R)\big/\left(E_2(R)\cap\{uI_2:u\in R^\times\}\right), \] where $R^\times$ is the group of units of $R$ and $I_2$ is the identity matrix. This interpretation of the notation requires confirmation against the original source.

Problem (V. M. Petechuk). For every such ring $R$, describe all abstract group automorphisms of $PE_2(R)$; equivalently, classify all bijections \[ \varphi:PE_2(R)\longrightarrow PE_2(R) \quad\text{such that}\quad \varphi(xy)=\varphi(x)\varphi(y) \quad\text{for all }x,y\in PE_2(R). \] The source requests a description of these automorphisms but supplies no proposed classification or required form of that description. The displayed formulation is conditional on the interpretation of $PE_2(R)$ above.

Short English statement

For every associative commutative ring in which $2$ and $3$ are invertible, describe all automorphisms of its projective elementary group of degree $2$. The meaning of $PE_2(R)$ must be confirmed because the supplied record does not define it. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied metadata, not an independently verified assessment.
