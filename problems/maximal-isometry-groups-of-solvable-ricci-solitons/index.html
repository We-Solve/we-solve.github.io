Maximal Isometry Groups of Solvable Ricci Solitons

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a real Lie group $S$ with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{s}$, define its derived series by \[ \mathfrak{s}^{(0)}=\mathfrak{s},\qquad \mathfrak{s}^{(j+1)}=[\mathfrak{s}^{(j)},\mathfrak{s}^{(j)}]\quad(j\geq 0). \] The group is called solvable when $\mathfrak{s}^{(m)}=0$ for some integer $m\geq 0$. For a Riemannian metric $g$ on $S$, its full isometry group is \[ \operatorname{Isom}(S,g)=\{\phi:S\to S\mid \phi\text{ is a smooth diffeomorphism and }\phi^*g=g\}. \] The metric is Einstein if $\operatorname{Ric}_g=\kappa g$ for some constant $\kappa\in{\mathbb{R}}$. The Ricci soliton equation is \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g+\tfrac12\mathcal{L}_Xg=\lambda g, \] where $X$ is a smooth vector field on $S$, $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$ is constant, and $\mathcal{L}_X$ denotes Lie differentiation. In this sign convention, expanding solitons have $\lambda<0$. Completeness requirements for the metric and the soliton vector field are not specified in the supplied record.

Source question. To what extent do maximal-isometry-group results for Einstein metrics extend to Ricci soliton metrics on general solvable Lie groups?

Unresolved formulation. The record does not identify the Einstein theorem or the precise maximality property to be extended. It also does not specify the comparison class of metrics, whether one compares full isometry groups or their identity components, or whether maximality means group inclusion after a change of coordinates, maximal dimension, or another property. Consequently, the supplied question is a research program rather than a uniquely determined quantified conjecture.

No restriction to left-invariant metrics is imposed by the wording supplied. If such a restriction is intended, it means $L_s^*g=g$ for every $s\in S$, where $L_s(t)=st$. Whether this restriction is intended, whether $S$ must be connected or simply connected, and whether the target concerns all solitons or only expanding solitons require clarification. These hypotheses must be specified before a precise maximal-isometry assertion can be stated.

Short English statement

Determine which maximal-isometry results for Einstein metrics remain valid for Ricci soliton metrics on solvable Lie groups. The supplied record does not identify the comparison theorem, the meaning of maximality, or all required hypotheses, so it does not determine a single precise conjecture.
