Math-9859

Artin Groups in Helly and Injective Geometry

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Which two-dimensional Artin groups of hyperbolic type are virtually Helly; which Artin groups act properly on CAT(0) cube complexes; are parabolic subgroups stable under intersection; is the complex of proper parabolic two-dimensional Artin subgroups NPC; is the three-generator all-label-3 Artin group Helly; are all two-dimensional Artin groups systolic; is reducibility equivalent to direct-product decomposition; are Artin groups and dihedral Artin groups hierarchically hyperbolic; do non-Helly groups act geometrically on coarse Helly graphs; are uniform lattices in $SL_n(K)$ injective or in $GL_n(K)$ Helly; and what are the injective hulls of $GL_n(R)/O(n)$ and the hyperbolic plane, and the Coxeter groups realizable as reflection groups in injective metric spaces?

Formulation references: [S1], [S2].
