Artin Groups in Helly and Injective Geometry

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Every intersection, including an infinite intersection, of parabolic subgroups in a finite-rank Artin group is claimed parabolic.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $A_M$ be the Artin group of a finite Coxeter matrix on $S$; $A_X$ denotes its standard parabolic subgroup for $X\subseteq S$. For arbitrary $X,Y\subseteq S$ and $g,h\in A_M$, the claim is \[ \exists Z\subseteq S,\ k\in A_M:\qquad gA_Xg^{-1}\cap hA_Yh^{-1}=kA_Zk^{-1}. \] Every family of parabolics has the same intersection as some subfamily of size at most $|S|$, so arbitrary intersections are parabolic and every subset has a least parabolic closure. The algorithmic corollary takes $M,X$ and words $u,v$ and decides \[ v\in uA_Xu^{-1}; \] choosing $X=\varnothing$ yields a uniform word-problem algorithm. If the diagram is irreducible and has an infinite label, the additional corollary gives acylindrical hyperbolicity and, for every proper parabolic $P$, a $g$ with $P\cap gPg^{-1}=\{1\}$.

Outline of the argument

Relative categorical layers track positive divisors and caps. Two-point uniqueness and a relative frame control null layers, while harmonic heights produce connected minima and bounded positive-height representatives; these give the parabolic carrier of the intersection.
