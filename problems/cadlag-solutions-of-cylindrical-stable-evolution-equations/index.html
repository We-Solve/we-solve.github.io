Càdlàg Solutions of Cylindrical Stable Evolution Equations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H$ denote the Hilbert space in the supplied record, and consider the linear stochastic evolution equation \[ \mathrm{d}X_t=AX_t\,\mathrm{d}t+\mathrm{d}Z_t, \] where $Z$ is described as a cylindrical stable process with coefficients $(\beta_k)_k$ and stable index $\alpha$. The supplied summability assumption is \[ \sum_k\frac{\beta_k^{\alpha}}{\gamma_k}<\infty. \] The record does not define $(\gamma_k)_k$, specify its relation to $A$, give the range of $\alpha$, or specify the signs and normalization of the coefficients. These omissions must be resolved before the displayed assumption determines a precise class of equations.

An $H$-valued path $x$ on a time interval $I$ is càdlàg in the norm topology if it is right-continuous at every point where $I$ extends to the right and has a left limit in $H$ at every point where $I$ extends to the left. Thus, at the applicable points, \[ \lim_{\substack{s\downarrow t\\s\in I}}\|x(s)-x(t)\|_H=0, \qquad \lim_{\substack{s\uparrow t\\s\in I}}x(s)\ \text{exists in }H. \] For a stochastic process, having càdlàg trajectories means that this path property holds almost surely. The source does not specify the time interval or whether it asks for the given process itself to have this property or for the existence of a càdlàg modification.

Source question, with unresolved qualifications. Under the displayed summability assumption, can conditions substantially weaker than \[ \sum_k\beta_k^{\alpha}<\infty \] ensure that the $H$-valued solution has càdlàg trajectories? The expression "substantially weaker" is not quantified in the record. Neither a proposed replacement condition nor the full hypotheses on the equation, initial data, and solution concept are supplied. Consequently, this is a research question requiring further specification, rather than a fully quantified conjecture; in particular, the record does not explicitly assert that the first summability condition alone suffices.

Short English statement

The question asks whether an $H$-valued solution of a linear evolution equation driven by cylindrical stable noise can have almost surely càdlàg paths under assumptions substantially weaker than $\sum_k\beta_k^{\alpha}<\infty$, while retaining $\sum_k\beta_k^{\alpha}/\gamma_k<\infty$. The replacement assumptions and several features of the equation are unspecified, so a precise theorem cannot yet be extracted.
