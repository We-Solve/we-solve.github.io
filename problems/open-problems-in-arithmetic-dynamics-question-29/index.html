Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Question 29

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f:{\mathbb{P}}^N\dashrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}^N$ be a dominant rational map defined over $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$. Write $I_f$ for its indeterminacy locus and define \[ {\mathbb{P}}^N(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})_f=\{P\in{\mathbb{P}}^N(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}): f^n(P)\text{ is defined for every }n\geq 0\}, \qquad \mathcal O_f(P)=\{f^n(P):n\geq0\}. \] In positive dimension, the usual first dynamical degree is \[ \delta_f=\lim_{m\to\infty}\left(\deg(f^m)\right)^{1/m}, \] where the degree of a rational map is the common degree of homogeneous coordinate polynomials after cancellation of common factors. The excerpt does not explicitly specify the range of $N$; this definition uses the usual setting $N\geq1$.

The supplied statement uses a height $h$ without defining it. A conventional interpretation is the absolute logarithmic Weil height on ${\mathbb{P}}^N(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})$: for homogeneous coordinates in a number field $K$, \[ h([x_0:\cdots:x_N])= \frac{1}{[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]}\sum_{v\in M_K}[K_v:{\mathbb{Q}}_v]\log\max_{0\leq i\leq N}|x_i|_v, \] with the usual normalized absolute values extending those of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. This interpretation requires confirmation from the source; the excerpt does not specify whether a modified height such as $\max\{1,h\}$ is intended.

Question (supplied source, Question 29). Suppose $P\in{\mathbb{P}}^N(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})_f$ satisfies \[ \overline{\mathcal O_f(P)}^{\mathrm{Zar}}={\mathbb{P}}^N. \] Do there exist integers $k_f\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ and $\ell_f\in\{0,1,\ldots,N\}$ such that \[ h(f^n(P))\asymp \delta_f^n n^{\ell_f}(\log n)^{k_f} \qquad(n\to\infty)? \] Explicitly, the requested comparison means that there are constants $c,C>0$ and an integer $n_0\geq2$, independent of $n$, such that for every integer $n\geq n_0$, \[ c\,\delta_f^n n^{\ell_f}(\log n)^{k_f} \leq h(f^n(P)) \leq C\,\delta_f^n n^{\ell_f}(\log n)^{k_f}. \] The source expressly allows the comparison constants to depend on $f$ and $P$. Further, when $\delta_f>1$, can the comparison always be obtained with $k_f=0$?

The displayed subscripts suggest exponents attached to $f$, but the supplied wording first fixes both $f$ and $P$. It does not explicitly resolve whether one pair of exponents must work for every Zariski-dense orbit of a fixed map, or whether the exponents may also depend on $P$. This distinction remains unresolved here.

Short English statement

For a dominant rational self-map of projective space over $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, ask whether the height along every everywhere-defined, Zariski-dense orbit is comparable to exponential growth given by the dynamical degree, multiplied by an integer power of $n$ between $0$ and $N$ and an integer power of $\log n$. If the dynamical degree exceeds $1$, ask whether the logarithmic factor can always be omitted. The height convention and whether the exponents must be uniform over dense orbits of a fixed map require clarification.
