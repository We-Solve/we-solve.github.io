Amenable Actions on Unital Simple Stably Finite Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A nonzero unital $C^*$-algebra $A$ is simple if its only norm-closed two-sided ideals are $\{0\}$ and $A$. It is stably finite if, for every integer $n\geq 1$ and every $v\in M_n(A)$, \[ v^*v=1_{M_n(A)}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad vv^*=1_{M_n(A)}. \] An action of a group $G$ on $A$ is a homomorphism $\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)$, where $\operatorname{Aut}(A)$ denotes the group of $*$-automorphisms of $A$.

The supplied question asks whether there exist a nonamenable group $G$, a nonzero unital simple stably finite $C^*$-algebra $A$, and an amenable action $\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)$. This is an existential question about at least one nonamenable group; it does not assert existence for every nonamenable group.

The supplied excerpt does not specify a topology or cardinality restriction on $G$, or define amenability of the action. For an explicit conditional formulation, suppose that $G$ is discrete and that action amenability means amenability in the following bidual sense. A discrete group $G$ is amenable if there is a positive unital linear functional $m:\ell^\infty(G)\to{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfying $m(L_gf)=m(f)$ for all $g\in G$, where $(L_gf)(h)=f(g^{-1}h)$. Write $Z=Z(A^{**})$ for the center of the bidual von Neumann algebra. The action extends to $A^{**}$ and restricts to an action on $Z$. On the algebra $\ell^\infty(G,Z)$ of bounded $Z$-valued functions, define \[ (g\cdot F)(h)=\alpha_g^{**}\left(F(g^{-1}h)\right). \] Under this convention, $\alpha$ is amenable if there exists a conditional expectation \[ P:\ell^\infty(G,Z)\longrightarrow Z \] onto the constant functions such that $P(g\cdot F)=\alpha_g^{**}(P(F))$ for all $g\in G$ and $F\in\ell^\infty(G,Z)$. Here a conditional expectation is a positive unital $Z$-bimodule map fixing the constant functions; normality is not required.

With these explicitly conditional conventions, the question becomes \[ \exists\,G,A,\alpha\quad \begin{cases} G\text{ is a nonamenable discrete group},\\ A\text{ is a nonzero unital simple stably finite }C^*\text{-algebra},\\ \alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)\text{ is amenable in the sense above}. \end{cases} \] Whether this discrete-group convention and this definition of action amenability are exactly those intended by the report requires source review. No countability, separability, or nuclearity hypothesis is added. The adjacent talk title mentions tracial amenability, but the question itself asks for amenability; these terms must not be identified without further evidence.

Short English statement

Does there exist a nonamenable group acting amenably on a nonzero unital simple stably finite $C^*$-algebra? The question is existential. The supplied excerpt leaves the group category and the definition of action amenability unstated, so the explicit discrete formulation above is conditional and requires human review. The supplied record labels the problem open.
