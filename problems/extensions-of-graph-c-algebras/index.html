Math-3513

Extensions of Graph $C^*$-Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a directed graph $E=(E^0,E^1,s,r)$, write $C^*(E)$ for its graph $C^*$-algebra: the universal $C^*$-algebra generated by mutually orthogonal vertex projections $p_v$ and edge partial isometries $t_e$ with mutually orthogonal range projections, satisfying \[ t_e^*t_e=p_{r(e)},\qquad t_et_e^*\leq p_{s(e)},\qquad p_v=\sum_{s(e)=v}t_et_e^*\quad\text{if }0<|s^{-1}(v)|<\infty. \] The last relation imposes no equality at sinks or infinite emitters. The supplied record does not specify the permitted class of directed graphs. Denote that intended class by $\mathcal G$; countability, finiteness, and row-finiteness restrictions remain unresolved.

Extension problem. For every $C^*$-algebra $A$ and closed two-sided ideal $I\subseteq A$, consider the canonical short exact sequence \[ 0\longrightarrow I\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow A/I\longrightarrow0. \] Assume there exist $E,F\in\mathcal G$ with \[ I\cong C^*(E),\qquad A/I\cong C^*(F), \] where $\cong$ denotes $*$-isomorphism. Determine necessary and sufficient conditions on this extension for \[ \exists G\in\mathcal G\quad A\cong C^*(G). \] Determine whether the obstructions to this conclusion can be characterized using $K$-theoretic data of the extension. The record does not specify which groups, maps, orders, scales, or other additional structures constitute the intended $K$-theoretic data, so it supplies no precise candidate completeness assertion. The formulation above uses actual $*$-isomorphism, following the literal wording; no stabilization is stated in the source.

Supplied dated qualification. The record labels the problem partially solved. Its literature assessment dated 2026-08-21 reports that the broad extension question is not fully settled, that a complete $K$-theoretical criterion is known for essential extensions of two simple graph $C^*$-algebras, and that earlier results cover further restricted classes. Here essential means that $I\cap J\neq\{0\}$ for every nonzero closed two-sided ideal $J$ of $A$; simplicity means being nonzero with no nonzero proper closed two-sided ideals. These are qualifications of the reported restricted results, not additional hypotheses on the broad problem. Their complete theorem hypotheses are not supplied. This assessment is supplied dated curation and has not been independently verified.

Short English statement

Given a $C^*$-algebra whose ideal and quotient are graph $C^*$-algebras, characterize when the whole algebra is a graph $C^*$-algebra and whether $K$-theory captures the obstructions. The allowed graph class and the precise $K$-theoretic data remain unspecified. The supplied 2026-08-21 assessment labels the problem partially solved and reports a criterion for essential extensions with simple graph ideal and quotient.
