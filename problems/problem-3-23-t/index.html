Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a surface $\Sigma$, consider links in $\Sigma\times[0,1]$ and finite-type invariants defined by alternating resolution of transverse double points. Let $\mathcal A_\Sigma$ be the surface-diagram algebra used in the source; its precise diagram, relation, coefficient and completion conventions are not supplied.

T. Kohno asks for an explicit universal finite-type invariant of these links with values in $\mathcal A_\Sigma$. In the standard graded formulation, its degree-$d$ truncation should recover every invariant of order at most $d$ by linear evaluation, compatibly with the symbol/diagram correspondence. If the invariant is a formal series, its target is the corresponding degree completion; whether the source's notation $\mathcal A_\Sigma$ already incorporates completion must be checked. The request is for an explicit construction, not just an abstract existence or a family of nonuniversal scalar invariants.

The source reports genus zero solved by the Kontsevich integral, a higher-genus construction suggestion by Deligne at Oberwolfach in 1995, and the punctured-surface case solved by Andersen, Mattes and Reshetikhin. These are supplied historical progress statements; they do not specify a completed construction for the remaining surface classes.

The appended context fixes a simple Lie group $G$ and the moduli space $M_G(\Sigma)$ of flat $G$-connections. It states that its smooth-function algebra $\mathcal C(M_G(\Sigma))$ is Poisson, as is $\mathcal A_\Sigma$, and that coloring each diagram component by a representation of $G$ yields a natural Poisson algebra homomorphism \[ \tau:\mathcal A_\Sigma\longrightarrow\mathcal C(M_G(\Sigma)),\qquad \tau(\{a,b\})=\{\tau(a),\tau(b)\}. \] This is retained as context for the next problem announced but not supplied. Smooth-locus or singular-moduli conventions and the symplectic/Poisson hypotheses are not stated; a globally smooth symplectic moduli space is not assumed silently. The excerpt also leaves the topology/orientation of $\Sigma$ and link framing conventions unresolved.

Short English statement

Explicitly construct a universal finite-type invariant for links in a thickened surface, valued in the source's surface-diagram algebra. Preserve the reported genus-zero and punctured-surface progress and its Poisson/flat-connection context, with missing conventions marked.
