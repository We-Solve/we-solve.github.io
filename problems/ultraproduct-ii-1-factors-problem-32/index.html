Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 32

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied statement concerns embeddings into an ultrapower in the setting of $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors. It does not explicitly specify the hypotheses on $N$, the ultrafilter, the meaning of separability, or the meaning of conjugation by a completely positive map. The formulation below uses the conventional tracial reading and records these qualifications rather than treating them as supplied hypotheses.

Let $N$ be a $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with normalized faithful normal trace $\tau$, and let $\mathcal U$ be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on ${\mathbb{N}}$. Write $\|x\|_2=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}$ and define the tracial ultrapower by \[ N^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},N)/\mathcal I_{\mathcal U},\qquad \mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\left\{(x_n)\in\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},N):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_2=0\right\}. \] Interpret the supplied phrase "separable subfactor" as a $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor $M$ with separable predual; the source record specifies no ambient inclusion. Let $\pi_1,\pi_2:M\to N^{\mathcal U}$ be embeddings, understood here as unital injective $*$-homomorphisms.

For each $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$, let $\varphi_n:N\to N$ be unital and completely positive, meaning that $\varphi_n(1)=1$ and $\operatorname{id}_{M_k({\mathbb{C}})}\otimes\varphi_n$ is positive for every $k\geq1$. The notation $\Phi=(\varphi_n)_{\mathcal U}$ is interpreted as the well-defined map \[ \Phi:N^{\mathcal U}\longrightarrow N^{\mathcal U},\qquad \Phi([(x_n)]_{\mathcal U})=[(\varphi_n(x_n))]_{\mathcal U}. \] This interpretation requires preservation of the ideal $\mathcal I_{\mathcal U}$: for every norm-bounded sequence $(x_n)$, \[ \lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_2=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|\varphi_n(x_n)\|_2=0. \] The record does not explicitly supply this requirement or hypotheses ensuring it; unital complete positivity alone does not ensure it.

Interpret "$\Phi$ conjugates $\pi_1$ to $\pi_2$" as \[ \Phi\circ\pi_1=\pi_2. \] Under these interpretations, the question is whether, for every such choice of data, there exists $u\in N^{\mathcal U}$ such that \[ u^*u=uu^*=1 \qquad\text{and}\qquad u\pi_1(x)u^*=\pi_2(x)\quad\text{for every }x\in M. \] The supplied wording does not establish whether additional assumptions on the embeddings or a stronger meaning of conjugation by $\Phi$ are intended.

Short English statement

Under the conventional tracial interpretation, must two embeddings of a separable factor into $N^{\mathcal U}$ be unitarily conjugate whenever a well-defined map induced by a sequence of unital completely positive maps carries the first embedding to the second? The supplied record labels this question open. Its ultrapower conventions and the meaning of conjugation by the induced map require clarification.
