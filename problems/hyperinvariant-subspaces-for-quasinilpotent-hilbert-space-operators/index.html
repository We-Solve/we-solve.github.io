Hyperinvariant subspaces for quasinilpotent Hilbert-space operators

OpenAI's claim

Each separable infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space supports, according to the paper, a nonzero norm-quasinilpotent operator with no proper nonzero closed hyperinvariant subspace and a transitive commutant. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $H$ be any separable complex Hilbert space of infinite dimension. OpenAI claims a nonzero bounded operator $S$ with \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\|S^n\|^{1/n}=0 \] such that any closed subspace invariant under all operators commuting with $S$ is either zero or the entire space. Equivalently the commutant $\{S\}'$ is a transitive complex operator algebra that is unital, proper and closed in the strong topology. The claim concerns hyperinvariant subspaces; ordinary invariant subspaces of $S$ may exist.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 293, 2026-09-27): Quadratically weighted bilateral shifts have explicitly classified invariant tails, and the construction organizes them over a 2-adic probability base. A bounded commuting transport moves these tails backward, forcing an impossible strict descent for any hyperinvariant projection field. Uniform estimates on powers establish quasinilpotence of the entire operator, not just of individual fibers.
