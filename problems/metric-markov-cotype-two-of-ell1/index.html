Metric Markov cotype two of ell1

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 332; 2026-10-05), the asserted result is that real $\ell_1$ has metric Markov cotype two, giving uniformly controlled Lipschitz extension from Hilbert-space subsets to this target.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For every finite reversible stochastic matrix $A=(a_{ij})$ with stationary distribution $\pi$, every integer $t\ge1$ and every $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\ell_1(\mathbb R)$, the claim supplies $y_1,\ldots,y_n$ satisfying \[ \sum_i\pi_i\|x_i-y_i\|_1^2+t\sum_{i,j}\pi_i a_{ij}\|y_i-y_j\|_1^2 \le3024\sum_{i,j}\pi_i\left(\frac1t\sum_{s=1}^tA^s\right)_{ij}\|x_i-x_j\|_1^2. \] Thus the metric Markov cotype-two constant is at most $12\sqrt{21}$. The asserted extension consequence is one universal $K<\infty$ such that every Lipschitz $f:S\to\ell_1$ from a subset of any real Hilbert space $\mathcal H$ extends to $F:\mathcal H\to\ell_1$ with $\operatorname{Lip}(F)\le K\operatorname{Lip}(f)$.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 332; 2026-10-05), finite cut representations reconstruct an expected coordinatewise median of three stopped-walk endpoints. A flat-map martingale and quartic potential control the geometric endpoint costs, which a metric comparison converts to the Cesàro energy needed for cotype.
