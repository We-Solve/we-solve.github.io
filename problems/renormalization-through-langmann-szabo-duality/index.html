Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
The Moyal deformation replaces ordinary multiplication of fields by a noncommutative star product with coordinate commutators $[x^\mu,x^\nu]=i\theta^{\mu\nu}$. Langmann--Szabo duality relates position and momentum descriptions of certain such scalar models, exchanging the kinetic and harmonic-oscillator terms. Perturbative renormalizability means that ultraviolet divergences at all perturbative orders can be absorbed into a fixed finite set of parameters and field normalizations.
Source scope and qualifications.
The original report already describes the successful harmonic scalar construction. Its broad construction question is historical and model dependent; it must not be converted into a claim that no example is known, or into an unsupported theorem for all noncommutative field theories.

Quadratic duality test.
Fix $\Omega>0$. For a Schwartz function $f$ on $\mathbb R^d$, use the unitary Fourier transform $\widehat f(p)=(2\pi)^{-d/2}\int e^{-ipx}f(x)dx$. Integration by parts gives $\widehat{\partial_jf}=ip_j\widehat f$ and $\widehat{x_jf}=i\partial_{p_j}\widehat f$. Consequently \[ Q_\Omega(f)=\int(|\nabla f|^2+\Omega^2|x f|^2+m^2|f|^2), \quad Q_\Omega(\widehat f)=\Omega^2Q_{1/\Omega}(f) \] provided the mass coefficient on the right is replaced by $m^2/\Omega^2$; for $\Omega=1$ no replacement is needed. This is the oscillator-level mechanism behind exchanging position and momentum, in units where the exchange has unit scale.
Attempt to infer renormalizability.
The calculation selects a self-dual quadratic form, but self-duality does not restrict an action to finitely many interactions. For each integer $k\geq2$, the functional \[ I_k(f)=\int |f(x)|^{2k}dx+\int|\widehat f(p)|^{2k}dp \] is Fourier invariant, because two Fourier transforms reflect the argument. These functionals are independent: replacing $f$ by $tf$ gives distinct powers $t^{2k}$. Thus symmetry alone allows arbitrarily many couplings even on this simple scalar function space.
Boundary and source scope.
These $I_k$ are illustrative nonlocal functionals, not asserted to be admissible Moyal-local interactions. Their role is to invalidate the inference from duality alone to a finite counterterm space. The missing step is a model-specific all-order power-counting and subtraction argument respecting the star product. The report already contains successful harmonic scalar constructions; this exercise neither rediscovers their proof nor settles gauge extensions or all noncommutative theories.
