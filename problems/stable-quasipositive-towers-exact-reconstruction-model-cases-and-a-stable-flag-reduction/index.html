Math-1748

Stable quasipositive towers: exact reconstruction, model cases, and a stable-flag reduction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the usual meaning of quasi-positive sectional curvature: $\sec\geq0$ everywhere and all two-planes at some point have strictly positive curvature. For a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$ put $\operatorname{cohom}(M,g)=\dim(M/\operatorname{Iso}(M,g))$, using the full isometry group and its quotient metric. The source asks, for each positive integer $k$, whether there is an integer $n_0(k)$ such that every intended quasi-positively curved $(M^n,g)$ with $n\geq n_0(k)$ and cohomogeneity at most $k$ admits a tower \[ M_0=M^n\subset M_1^{n+k}\subset M_2^{n+2k}\subset\cdots \] with isometric totally geodesic inclusions, quasi-positive metrics $g_i$, a direct union modeling $BG$ for some Lie group $G$, and \[ M_i/\operatorname{Iso}(M_i,g_i)\cong M_0/\operatorname{Iso}(M_0,g_0) \] isometrically for every $i$. Compactness, completeness, the topology on the union, and whether modeling $BG$ means homotopy equivalence are not explicit in the source. The model statements below use closed connected manifolds and the standard weak/direct-limit topology; a metric completion is a different construction. The codimension is exactly $k$ at every step, not merely bounded by $k$.

The aid reports full model towers with standard metrics: round spheres $S^{n+ik}$ and real projective spaces ${\mathbb{R}}\mathrm P^{n+ik}$ for any positive $k$; complex projective spaces ${\mathbb{C}}\mathrm P^{a+i(k/2)}$ when $k$ is even and $n=2a$; and quaternionic projective spaces $\mathbb H\mathrm P^{a+i(k/4)}$ when $4\mid k$ and $n=4a$. Their full isometry groups are transitive, so all quotient spaces are points. Their stable unions have respectively the homotopy types of $B\{1\}$, $B({\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}})$, $BU(1)$, and $BSp(1)$. The sphere union is contractible. These individual families do not establish the universal threshold $n_0(k)$.

The aid also invokes Wilking's real, complex, and quaternionic projectivized tangent-bundle families as near-solutions, with fixed steps $k=2,4,8$ respectively. Its stable flag description uses the unit scalar groups $O(1)={\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$, $U(1)$, and $Sp(1)$, so the corresponding limits are \[ B(({\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}})^2),\qquad B(U(1)^2),\qquad B(Sp(1)^2). \] For those chosen families and metrics it reports fixed dimension steps, quasi-positive curvature, totally geodesic inclusions, the cohomogeneity bounds, and the stable classifying-space clauses. The finite-stage metrics and embedding formulas are not supplied here. Crucially, stage-independence of the quotient metric by the full isometry group remains uncertified; constancy for a chosen subgroup action would not prove that clause.

Finally the aid reports a conditional connectedness argument requiring a normal-geodesic visibility hypothesis to transfer Wilking's positive-curvature stabilization method. The precise hypothesis and proposition are absent, so it supplies no general stabilization theorem for quasi-positive curvature. The full quantified source question remains unresolved by the reported model and near-model towers.

Short English statement

The source asks for a fixed-codimension stabilization tower for every sufficiently large low-cohomogeneity quasi-positively curved manifold, preserving the full-isometry quotient metric and ending in a classifying space. Standard sphere and projective towers satisfy all clauses in their own families. The supplied Wilking near-models retain a specific gap: their full-isometry quotient metrics are not certified constant. Neither family proves the universal threshold statement.
