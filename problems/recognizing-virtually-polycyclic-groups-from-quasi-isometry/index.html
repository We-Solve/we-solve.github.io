Recognizing virtually polycyclic groups from quasi-isometry

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Virtual polycyclicity is claimed determined by the coarse geometry among groups with finite generators.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $P$ be finitely generated and virtually polycyclic, and let $H$ be any finitely generated group. The source claims \[ H\text{ quasi-isometric to }P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H\text{ virtually polycyclic}. \] Quasi-isometry refers to word metrics from finite generating sets.

Outline of the argument

Cartan models and weighted boxes define canonical coarse height data. Path packing and recurrent switches force stationary slopes, and neighboring affine chart limits agree; the resulting solvable-lattice structure identifies virtual polycyclicity without fixing the original ambient Lie group.

Formal verification

For family 255, the documented theorem recognizes virtual polycyclicity by quasi-isometry and places a finite-index subgroup in a possibly different solvable Lie lattice; a height-control supplement is also listed.
