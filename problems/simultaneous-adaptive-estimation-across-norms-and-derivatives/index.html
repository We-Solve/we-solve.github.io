Simultaneous Adaptive Estimation Across Norms and Derivatives

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(D,\mathcal A,\mu)$ denote the domain and reference measure of the estimation problem. These are not specified in the supplied record. For a measurable function $g$, write \[ \|g\|_{L^p(\mu)}=\left(\int_D |g|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu\right)^{1/p}\quad(1\le p<\infty),\qquad \|g\|_{L^\infty(\mu)}=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{x\in D}|g(x)|. \] Write \[ \mathcal H^\beta(Q;D)=\{f:\|f\|_{C^\beta(D)}\le Q\} \] for a Hölder ball of smoothness $\beta>0$ and radius $Q>0$. This notation is provisional: the domain, boundary conditions, Hölder norm, and convention at integer smoothness are not supplied. Neither the admissible ranges of $\beta,Q$ nor the observation model and sample-size parameter are specified.

An adaptive estimator here means an estimator constructed from the observations without knowing $\beta$ or $Q$. Simultaneous adaptation asks for one estimator sequence that attains the required performance for all the losses and parameter classes under consideration, rather than selecting a different estimator using the unknown parameters or the requested loss.

Research questions (permanent catalogue entry). In the intended statistical experiment, determine whether such an estimator can satisfy the following requirements:

  1. Attain the appropriate adaptive estimation rates over $\mathcal H^\beta(Q;D)$, simultaneously for every intended $L^p$ loss and every admissible unknown pair $(\beta,Q)$.
  2. Simultaneously attain the requested sharp $L^2$ and $L^\infty$ rates for $f$ and its derivatives. If the domain is Euclidean and ordinary partial derivatives are intended, write $D^\alpha f$ for the derivative indexed by a multi-index $\alpha$, with $D^0f=f$; the admissible derivative orders and their relation to $\beta$ must be specified before this requirement is well defined.
  3. Extend the intended estimation method to a setting that does not assume coefficientwise bounds on wavelet coefficients.
  4. Characterize the settings in which simultaneous adaptation is possible and construct an estimator attaining the corresponding performance guarantees.
The record does not specify the statistical experiment, risk functional, benchmark rates, meaning of sharpness, admissible derivative orders, wavelet assumptions, or the method to be generalized. Its original wording asks for "clean" rates whereas its clean statement asks for "sharp" rates; these need not express the same requirement. Consequently, these are parameterized research questions, not a fully specified theorem or conjecture. No particular rate, risk criterion, or meaning of sharpness is asserted here.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether one estimator, without knowing the Hölder smoothness or radius, can adapt simultaneously across $L^p$ losses and derivatives, achieve the requested $L^2$ and $L^\infty$ performance, and extend beyond coefficientwise wavelet restrictions. It also asks for a characterization of when this is possible and a construction. Essential model assumptions and performance benchmarks are missing, so a unique precise statistical assertion cannot yet be extracted.
