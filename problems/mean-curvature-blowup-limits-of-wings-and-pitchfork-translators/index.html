Math-4485

Mean-Curvature Blowup Limits of Wings and Pitchfork Translators

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a smooth hypersurface flow $M_t\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{n+1}$, mean curvature flow means that its normal velocity equals its mean curvature vector: $(\partial_t X)^\perp=\mathbf H$. A translating soliton is a hypersurface $\Sigma$ with a nonzero constant vector $V$ such that $\Sigma_t=\Sigma+tV$ is a mean curvature flow; equivalently, $\mathbf H_\Sigma=V^\perp$. For a smooth hypersurface, its Gaussian entropy is \[ \lambda(\Sigma)=\sup_{x_0\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n+1},\,r>0}(4\pi r^2)^{-n/2}\int_\Sigma\exp\!\left(-\frac{|x-x_0|^2}{4r^2}\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mathcal H^n(x). \] Finite entropy means $\lambda(\Sigma)<\infty$. These definitions use a general dimension $n$; the supplied record does not explicitly specify the dimension of the requested flows.

Source question. Do the finite-entropy $\Delta$-wings and pitchfork translators of mean curvature flow arise as blow-up limits of mean curvature flows? The supplied dated assessment specifically identifies occurrence of pitchfork translators as blow-up limits of closed mean curvature flows as unresolved. Here closed means compact without boundary. This restriction is absent from the supplied question itself and must be retained as an unresolved qualification.

For an individual translator $\Sigma$, a possible precise realization condition would be the existence of a mean curvature flow $(M_t)$, points $x_j$, times $t_j$, and scales $a_j\to\infty$ such that the parabolically rescaled flows \[ \widetilde M^{(j)}_s=a_j\left(M_{t_j+a_j^{-2}s}-x_j\right) \] converge to $\Sigma+sV$ on a specified limiting time interval. The original flows must be defined at the displayed times. This is a formulation template, not a choice of the intended convergence: the source does not specify smooth or weak convergence, multiplicity, allowed centers and times, or the required relation to a singularity.

The supplied record names $\Delta$-wings and pitchfork translators without giving their geometric definitions or parameter ranges. Consequently, the intended families must be identified from the cited sources before this question becomes a fully specified existence statement. It is also unspecified whether every member of each family must occur, whether a separate originating flow may be used for each member, and whether finite entropy qualifies both families. The broader question about both families and the narrower dated assessment about pitchfork translators are preserved without identifying their scopes.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the specified $\Delta$-wing and pitchfork translating solutions can occur as limits obtained by magnifying mean curvature flows. The supplied assessment emphasizes flows of closed hypersurfaces for pitchfork translators. The intended families, quantifiers, and notion of blow-up convergence require clarification before a fully precise statement can be fixed.
