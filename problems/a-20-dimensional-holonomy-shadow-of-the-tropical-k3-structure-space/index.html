Math-1502

A 20-dimensional holonomy shadow of the tropical K3 structure space

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Margaret Symington's A.17 describes a tropical Calabi--Yau sphere as a singular integral-affine structure $\mathcal A$ on $B=S^2\setminus\{p_1,\ldots,p_{24}\}$. Around each singular point it requires (A) monodromy conjugate to the positive shear \[ T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}, \] and (B) an injective local affine isomorphism $\Phi:(U\setminus R,\mathcal A)\to({\mathbb{R}}^2,\mathcal A_0)$, where $R$ is a ray from the point. The matrix is the contextual reading of damaged extracted typography. The source asks for the geometry or topology of the set of such structures on $S^2$, without specifying a topology on that set, markings, or an equivalence relation.

It reports the closed-surface possibilities under its local conditions: a torus or Klein bottle with no singularities, a sphere with 24, or ${\mathbb{R}}{\mathbb{P}}^2$ with 12. It says each occurs as the base of a singular Lagrangian fibration, whose singular fibers have genus-one Lefschetz nodal topology, described as spheres with one positive self-intersection. This wording concerns a positive node, not an asserted homological self-intersection number $+1$. It attributes injectivity in (B) to a three-dimensional contact-geometric argument without giving its proof or citation.

The aid's reduction fixes substantially more data: label the 24 punctures, choose peripheral generators \[ \pi_1(B)=\langle\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_{24}\mid\gamma_1\cdots\gamma_{24}=1\rangle, \] and fix a positive factorization $M_1\cdots M_{24}=I$ in $SL(2,{\mathbb{Z}})$, with each $M_i$ conjugate to $T$. Let $\rho(\gamma_i)=M_i$ and $V={\mathbb{R}}^2$ with this action. An affine lift has the form \[ \widehat\rho(\gamma)(x)=\rho(\gamma)x+c(\gamma),\qquad c(\gamma\delta)=c(\gamma)+\rho(\gamma)c(\delta). \] The peripheral transformation has a fixed point exactly when $c(\gamma_i)\in\operatorname{im}(I-M_i)$. Modulo global translations, these lifts are the parabolic cohomology space \[ H^1_{\mathrm{par}}(B;V_\rho)= \ker\left(H^1(\pi_1(B);V_\rho)\longrightarrow \bigoplus_{i=1}^{24}H^1(\langle\gamma_i\rangle;V_\rho)\right). \] In coordinates $b_i=c(\gamma_i)$, this is \[ \frac{\{(b_i): b_i\in\operatorname{im}(I-M_i),\quad \sum_{i=1}^{24}(M_1\cdots M_{i-1})b_i=0\}} {\{((I-M_i)v)_{i=1}^{24}:v\in{\mathbb{R}}^2\}}. \] The aid reports real dimension $24-2-2=20$ for the fixed positive 24-node factorization. This is a translation-holonomy quotient with the linear representation fixed, not a quotient by all changes of marking, braid actions, or arbitrary linear affine conjugacy.

It further reports that this is only a holonomy shadow. If a node in affine coordinates has fixed-point parameter $p$, its translational term is $(I-M)p$. Moving it to $p+tv$ along an eigenline with $Mv=v$ leaves this term unchanged. The aid interprets such nodal slides as geometric/developing-map data invisible to affine holonomy. Local fixed-point conditions alone do not establish global realizability or the injective cut-chart condition (B), so dimension 20 is not the dimension of the full unspecified structure space.

The original remark says that removing (B) but keeping monodromy condition (A) allows $12k$ singularities on $S^2$ for every integer $k\ge2$. It also proposes more general tropical manifolds built from tropical Calabi--Yau strata with boundary and suitable compatibility, giving an affine cylinder with affine boundary as an example. It records Zharkov's question about tropical Gromov--Witten calculations on a Calabi--Yau and asks whether they extend to Lagrangian fibrations over these more general bases, specifically $S^2\times T^2$ over the cylinder. No invariant, boundary conditions, fibration data, or compatibility axioms for that additional request are supplied. The aid reports modern polarized, triangulated, metric, and almost-toric moduli constructions, without claiming one unqualified moduli space answers all interpretations.

Short English statement

The question concerns the space of singular affine structures on a sphere with 24 positive nodes and injective cut charts. The aid identifies a 20-dimensional space of translation holonomies after fixing a labeled linear monodromy factorization, but node slides show that holonomy omits geometric data. The broader moduli and tropical Gromov--Witten requests remain underspecified.
