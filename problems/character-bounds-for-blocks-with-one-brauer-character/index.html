Math-3563

Character Bounds for Blocks with One Brauer Character

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group, let $p$ be a prime, and let $B$ be a $p$-block, using a splitting $p$-modular system so that ordinary and Brauer characters can be associated to the same block. Let $D$ be a defect group of $B$. Define \[ k(B)=|\operatorname{Irr}(B)|,\qquad l(B)=|\operatorname{IBr}(B)|, \] where $\operatorname{Irr}(B)$ consists of the ordinary irreducible characters in $B$ and $\operatorname{IBr}(B)$ of its irreducible Brauer characters, equivalently the isomorphism classes of simple modules in the block over a splitting field of characteristic $p$. Finally, \[ k(D)=|\operatorname{Irr}(D)| =\#\{\text{conjugacy classes of }D\}. \]

The question is whether \[ \forall G\ \forall p\ \forall B\text{ a }p\text{-block of }G, \qquad l(B)=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k(B)\le k(D). \] Thus a block with exactly one irreducible Brauer character should have no more ordinary irreducible characters than its defect group has conjugacy classes. No abelianness or other restriction on $D$ is stated. The right-hand side is $k(D)$, not $|D|$; replacing it by the defect-group order would change the requested bound. The standard splitting-system terminology is explanatory and should be checked against the report's conventions.

Short English statement

Does a block with one irreducible Brauer character have at most as many ordinary irreducible characters as its defect group has conjugacy classes? The target is the exact $k(D)$ bound, including nonabelian defect groups.
