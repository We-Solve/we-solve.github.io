Geometry of Continued Fractions — Combinatorial structure of sails

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A combinatorial type of a convex polytope is its face lattice, up to an inclusion-preserving isomorphism; this fixes incidence relations rather than a particular geometric realization. In a conventional lattice-sail construction, for a pointed convex cone $C\subset\mathbb R^d$ with apex $0$ and a lattice $\Lambda$, one first forms \[ K(C,\Lambda)=\operatorname{conv}\big((C\cap\Lambda)\setminus\{0\}\big) \] and obtains the sail from the boundary of this lattice convex hull, with the cone-boundary and irrationality conventions fixed by the chosen sail definition. This describes the standard framework only: the supplied record does not specify those conventions or which cones are assigned to a polytope.

Multidimensional IKEA problem. For every prescribed polytope combinatorial type $\tau$, describe all collections of sails of cones that arise from polytopes $P$ whose face lattice is isomorphic to $\tau$. In schematic notation, if $\mathcal S(P)$ denotes the source's intended collection, determine \[ \{\mathcal S(P):P\text{ is a polytope of combinatorial type }\tau\}. \] A precise classification requires recovering the cone assignment, lattice, admissible realizations, dimension scope, and equivalence relation on collections; they are absent from the supplied statement. In particular, tangent cones and normal cones are not silently identified. The supplied research summary reports restricted constructive results and no verified full resolution, with moderate-confidence OPEN-TRIAGE status.

Short English statement

For a fixed polytope incidence pattern, classify the collections of lattice sails realizable by its geometric realizations. The source omits the cone and sail conventions needed to turn this request into a complete classification statement.
