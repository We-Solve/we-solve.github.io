Kourovka Notebook Problem 10.31

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be an algebraic group, let $H$ be a closed normal subgroup of $G$, and let \[ Q=G/H,\qquad f:G\longrightarrow Q \] be the quotient and its canonical homomorphism. The supplied source does not specify the ground field or the precise category of algebraic groups and quotients; these choices remain unresolved.

For a fixed ground field $k$ and an interpretation in which $G$, $H$, $Q$, and $f$ are defined over $k$, a rational section of $f$ means a rational map $s:Q\dashrightarrow G$, defined over $k$, represented by a morphism $s_U:U\to G$ on a dense open subset $U\subseteq Q$, such that \[ f\circ s_U=\iota_U, \] where $\iota_U:U\hookrightarrow Q$ is the inclusion. Equivalently, $f\circ s=\operatorname{id}_Q$ as rational maps. This definition does not require $s$ to be a group homomorphism or to be defined everywhere.

Problem. Determine necessary and sufficient conditions on $G$, $H$, and the relevant field of definition for the canonical homomorphism $f:G\to G/H$ to admit a rational section.

Unresolved source qualifications. The source writes the section identity as $sf=1$. The identity above uses the usual geometric meaning of a section; the source's order of composition is not explained. The ground field, whether the section must be defined over that field or after extending it, and any conventions concerning affineness, connectedness, smoothness, or reducedness are not supplied. No such extra hypotheses are imposed here; a definitive formulation requires these conventions to be fixed.

Short English statement

For an algebraic group $G$ and a closed normal subgroup $H$, determine when the quotient map $G\to G/H$ has a rational section, meaning a map defined on a dense open subset that chooses a representative of each quotient point there. The ground field, field of definition, and composition convention require clarification.
