Math-4424

Admissibility and Finite Length on the Drinfeld Tower

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $F$ be a $p$-adic field, understood in the usual sense of a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$, and put $G=\operatorname{GL}_2(F)$. For each integer $n\geq 0$, let $\Sigma_n$ denote the level-$n$ space of the Drinfeld tower referred to in the supplied record. Write $\mathcal O(\Sigma_n)$ for its space of global analytic functions and retain the source notation \[ V_n=\mathcal O(\Sigma_n)^*. \] The intended action on a dual of a function space is the contragredient action: if the action on functions is $(g\cdot f)(x)=f(g^{-1}x)$, then $(g\cdot\lambda)(f)=\lambda(g^{-1}\cdot f)$.

Question. For every $p$-adic field $F$ and every integer $n\geq 0$, is $V_n$ an admissible locally analytic representation of $\operatorname{GL}_2(F)$, and does $V_n$ have finite length in the intended category of locally analytic representations? Finite length means the existence of a finite composition series \[ 0=V_{n,0}\subset V_{n,1}\subset\cdots\subset V_{n,r}=V_n \] in that category, with each successive quotient nonzero and simple.

Unresolved conventions. The supplied record does not specify the geometric base field or component convention for $\Sigma_n$, the coefficient field of the representations, the topology on $\mathcal O(\Sigma_n)$, or whether ${}^*$ denotes a continuous dual with the strong topology or another dual. It also does not specify whether local analyticity is over $F$ or over ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$, the corresponding admissibility convention, or the category and exactness convention used for finite length. These choices must be recovered from the cited source before the question determines a fully specified representation-theoretic statement; no particular choice is asserted here.

Short English statement

For every $p$-adic field $F$ and every level $n\geq 0$ of the Drinfeld tower, ask whether the dual of its analytic functions is an admissible locally analytic $\operatorname{GL}_2(F)$-representation of finite length. The supplied record leaves several geometric, coefficient, topology, and category conventions unresolved.
