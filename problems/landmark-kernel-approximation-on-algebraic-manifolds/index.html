Landmark Kernel Approximation on Algebraic Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal M\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be an algebraic manifold of dimension $D$, and let $k$ be a degree-$d$ polynomial kernel, as specified in the supplied record. The record does not specify the defining algebraic or smoothness conventions for $\mathcal M$, the precise polynomial kernel, its normalization, or whether degree refers to total degree or degree in each argument. Fix $x,y\in\mathcal M$.

Let $Z=(z_1,\ldots,z_m)$ consist of independent, identically distributed standard-normal landmark points. If this means ambient standard-normal sampling, then $z_i\sim\mathcal N(0,I_n)$, where $I_n$ is the $n\times n$ identity matrix; this interpretation requires $k$ to be defined at the sampled ambient points, which need not lie on $\mathcal M$. A distribution supported on $\mathcal M$ is not specified.

Whenever the kernel evaluations exist, define \[ K(x,Z)=\begin{pmatrix}k(x,z_1)&\cdots&k(x,z_m)\end{pmatrix},\qquad K(Z,y)=\begin{pmatrix}k(z_1,y)\\\vdots\\k(z_m,y)\end{pmatrix}, \] \[ K(Z,Z)=\left(k(z_i,z_j)\right)_{i,j=1}^{m}. \] On outcomes for which $K(Z,Z)$ is invertible, define the landmark approximation error \[ E_Z(x,y)=k(x,y)-K(x,Z)K(Z,Z)^{-1}K(Z,y). \] The inverse and hence this error are undefined on singular outcomes. The record supplies neither an almost-sure invertibility hypothesis nor a convention for singular outcomes. In particular, a polynomial kernel can have finite feature rank, so invertibility cannot be inferred from a lower bound on the number of landmarks.

The source requests an asymptotic estimate of compressed-sensing type under the sampling-size condition \[ m\geq\lambda Dd\log n, \] with the following displayed inequality offered as an example of the desired form: \[ \Pr\!\left(\lvert E_Z(x,y)\rvert\geq\varepsilon\right) \leq C\varepsilon^{-c\lambda}. \] Here probability is intended to concern the random landmarks, with $x,y$ fixed. This is a proposed bound shape, not a fully specified quantified assertion: the source gives no ranges for $\lambda$ and $\varepsilon$, no quantifiers or permitted dependencies for $C,c$, no normalization or geometric bounds, and no asymptotic parameter or limiting regime. The meaning of the probability also requires resolution of singular outcomes. No pseudoinverse, regularization, conditioning event, or additional hypothesis is supplied, and none is substituted here. A precise theorem or conjecture therefore requires further source clarification.

Short English statement

For fixed points on an algebraic manifold, the source asks for a probabilistic bound on the error of a polynomial-kernel landmark approximation using independent standard-normal landmarks. Its sample-size condition and illustrative tail bound are preserved, but the kernel specification, treatment of singular matrices, constant dependencies, parameter ranges, and asymptotic regime remain unresolved. The supplied literature assessment also refers to a Gaussian kernel, whereas the mathematical statement specifies a polynomial kernel. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
