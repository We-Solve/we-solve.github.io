Math-81

Categoricity of the Complex Exponential Field

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb C_{\exp}=({\mathbb{C}};+ ,\cdot,0,1,\exp)$, where $\exp(z)=e^z$ is the usual complex exponential. Let $\mathbb B_{\exp}=(B;+ ,\cdot,0,1,\exp_B)$ denote the intended Zilber pseudo-exponential field. The requested assertion is \[ \mathbb C_{\exp}\cong\mathbb B_{\exp}. \] Explicitly, this asks for a field isomorphism $f:{\mathbb{C}}\to B$ such that \[ \forall z\in{\mathbb{C}},\qquad f(e^z)=\exp_B(f(z)). \] For this assertion, the intended model $B$ must have cardinality $|{\mathbb{C}}|=2^{\aleph_0}$. The supplied excerpt does not explicitly specify the cardinality of $\mathbb B_{\exp}$; the continuum-sized model is the intended interpretation requiring confirmation.

The source describes the target through the following exponential-field axioms and asserts that, for $\mathbb C_{\exp}$, the unresolved requirements are Schanuel's conjecture and strong exponential-algebraic closedness.

  1. The underlying field $F$ is algebraically closed of characteristic zero, and $\exp_F:(F,+)\to(F^\times,\cdot)$ is a surjective group homomorphism with \[ \ker(\exp_F)=\tau{\mathbb{Z}} \] for some $\tau\in F$ transcendental over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. For the usual complex exponential, $\tau=2\pi i$.
  2. Schanuel's conjecture for $\mathbb C_{\exp}$ is the assertion that, for every integer $n\geq1$ and every tuple $\bar z=(z_1,\ldots,z_n)\in{\mathbb{C}}^n$, \[ \operatorname{trdeg}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}{\mathbb{Q}}(z_1,\ldots,z_n,e^{z_1},\ldots,e^{z_n}) \geq \dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\{z_1,\ldots,z_n\}. \]
  3. To describe strong exponential-algebraic closedness, put \[ G_n=\mathbb G_a^n\times\mathbb G_m^n, \qquad G_n({\mathbb{C}})={\mathbb{C}}^n\times({\mathbb{C}}^\times)^n. \] Write coordinates as $(\bar x,\bar y)$. For an integer matrix $M=(m_{ij})\in\operatorname{Mat}_{r\times n}({\mathbb{Z}})$, define \[ [M](\bar x,\bar y)=\left(M\bar x,\left(\prod_{j=1}^n y_j^{m_{ij}}\right)_{i=1}^r\right). \] An irreducible algebraic subvariety $V\subseteq G_n$ is rotund if, for every $r\geq1$ and every such $M$, \[ \dim\overline{[M](V)}^{\mathrm{Zar}}\geq\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}M. \] It is additively free if no nonzero $\bar m\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n$ makes $\sum_jm_jx_j$ constant on $V$, and multiplicatively free if no nonzero $\bar m\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n$ makes $\prod_jy_j^{m_j}$ constant on $V$. Here "free" is interpreted as satisfying both conditions.

    With these conventions, the supplied SEAC clause asks that, for every $n\geq1$, every subfield $A\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ finitely generated over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and every free, rotund irreducible algebraic subvariety $V\subseteq G_n$ defined over $A$, there exist $\bar z\in{\mathbb{C}}^n$ such that \[ (\bar z,e^{\bar z})\in V, \qquad \operatorname{trdeg}_{A}A(\bar z,e^{\bar z})=\dim V, \] where $e^{\bar z}=(e^{z_1},\ldots,e^{z_n})$. The transcendence-degree equality expresses genericity in $V$ over $A$. Dropping this equality gives the source's exponential-algebraic closedness condition, EAC.

    Unresolved qualification: the excerpt does not define "free" or specify irreducibility. The clause above uses explicit irreducible-variety and additive-and-multiplicative-freeness conventions. It retains the excerpt's absence of a dimension restriction. Whether the intended SEAC axiom instead explicitly restricts to $\dim V=n$, and how that formulation relates to the displayed quantification, requires source review; no equivalence between those formulations is asserted here.

  4. The source also requires the countable closure property for exponential-algebraic closure: \[ \forall S\subseteq F\text{ finite},\qquad |\operatorname{ecl}_{F}(S)|\leq\aleph_0. \] Here exponential-algebraic closure is the closure obtained from coordinates of nonsingular solutions of finite square systems of exponential-polynomial equations with parameters from $S$; nonsingularity means that the corresponding Jacobian determinant is nonzero, with formal differentiation satisfying $\partial\exp(t)=\exp(t)\partial t$. The supplied excerpt names this closure but does not give its full construction. It states that the countable closure property holds in $\mathbb C_{\exp}$, attributing this to the Ax--Schanuel property. This attribution is retained as a supplied claim.
Thus the supplied conjecture is the exponential-field isomorphism above, described in the record as equivalent to Schanuel's conjecture together with SEAC for the usual complex exponential. This is a statement-only draft; the model specification, SEAC conventions, and claimed equivalence require human mathematical review.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether the usual complex exponential field is isomorphic to the intended continuum-sized Zilber pseudo-exponential field. The supplied record describes this as equivalent to Schanuel's conjecture and strong exponential-algebraic closedness for complex exponentiation. The excerpt leaves some model and SEAC conventions unstated; these qualifications remain unresolved.
