Bounded coordinates for negatively pinched Kähler manifolds

OpenAI's claim

Pinched Kähler uniformization obstruction: A contractible complex threefold is claimed to have a complete Kähler metric with sectional curvature between two negative constants but no bounded holomorphic coordinate system. In particular, it is not biholomorphic to a bounded domain.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
The claimed example is a contractible domain $M\subset\mathbb C^3$ with a smooth complete Kähler metric and finite $0<A\le B$ satisfying \[-B\le K_g(\sigma)\le-A<0\] for all real tangent two-planes. Every bounded holomorphic $F:M\to\mathbb C^3$ has a Jacobian determinant vanishing somewhere. In particular $M$ cannot be biholomorphic to any bounded domain in $\mathbb C^3$. Both negative sectional-curvature pinching bounds are present.

Outline of the argument

Pinched Kähler uniformization obstruction: Disk fibers over the complex two-ball have radii prescribed by a smooth base function. The Hessian of a logarithmic fiber potential gives the Kähler metric, with explicit control of completeness and two-sided real sectional curvature. The chosen radius behavior imposes a holomorphic obstruction preventing a bounded map with nonvanishing Jacobian.
