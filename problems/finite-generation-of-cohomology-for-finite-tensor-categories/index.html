Finite Generation of Cohomology for Finite Tensor Categories

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a field $k$. For the conditional formulation below, a finite tensor category over $k$ means a $k$-linear abelian category $\mathcal C$, equivalent as a $k$-linear abelian category to the category of finite-dimensional modules over a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra, equipped with a rigid monoidal structure whose tensor product is $k$-bilinear and exact in each variable, and whose unit object $\mathbf 1$ is simple with $\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal C}(\mathbf 1)=k$. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, its characteristic, or whether it must be algebraically closed; this convention therefore makes a conditional statement precise without resolving the intended field hypotheses.

Define the graded cohomology algebra, with Yoneda multiplication, by \[ H^\bullet(\mathcal C)=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathcal C}^{n}(\mathbf 1,\mathbf 1). \] For an object $X\in\mathcal C$, define \[ H^\bullet(\mathcal C,X)=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathcal C}^{n}(\mathbf 1,X), \] with its graded right $H^\bullet(\mathcal C)$-module structure given by Yoneda composition. Finite generation of the algebra means generation by finitely many homogeneous elements over $k$; finite generation of a coefficient module means generation by finitely many homogeneous elements over this algebra.

The explicit algebra finite-generation question in the supplied record is whether, for every finite tensor category $\mathcal C$ over the intended ground field, \[ H^\bullet(\mathcal C)\text{ is a finitely generated }k\text{-algebra}. \] The record identifies this question with the Etingof--Ostrik finite-generation conjecture. To distinguish the coefficient condition that this terminology can entail, the fuller formulation asks in addition whether \[ \text{for every }X\in\mathcal C,\qquad H^\bullet(\mathcal C,X)\text{ is finitely generated over }H^\bullet(\mathcal C). \] The supplied statement does not explicitly include this additional condition or establish that algebra finite generation alone is equivalent to the fuller formulation. Their intended relationship requires source review.

For a finite-dimensional Hopf algebra $A$ over $k$, take $\mathcal C=A\text{-}\mathrm{mod}_{\mathrm{fd}}$, the category of finite-dimensional left $A$-modules, with unit the trivial module $k$ defined by the counit. The corresponding questions concern finite generation of $\operatorname{Ext}_{A}^{\bullet}(k,k)$ as a $k$-algebra and, in the fuller formulation, of $\operatorname{Ext}_{A}^{\bullet}(k,M)$ over that algebra for every finite-dimensional left $A$-module $M$. The source proposes further Hopf-algebra classes as intermediate targets; it specifies no particular additional class. These are special cases of the tensor-category question, and the record supplies no reduction of all finite tensor categories to these cases.

The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports positive results for complex pointed Hopf algebras with abelian group-likes and for quasi-split finite-dimensional Hopf algebras, while reporting the general conjecture open. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified conclusions; the precise hypotheses and coverage of those results require review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the cohomology algebra of every finite tensor category is finitely generated. A fuller interpretation of the named Etingof--Ostrik conjecture also requires finite generation of cohomology with coefficients in every object. Finite-dimensional Hopf algebras provide the suggested special cases. The intended ground-field hypotheses and the inclusion of the coefficient condition remain unresolved in the supplied record.
