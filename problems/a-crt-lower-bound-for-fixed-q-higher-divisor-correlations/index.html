Math-1254

A CRT lower bound for fixed-q higher divisor correlations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime power $q$, a polynomial $h\in{\mathbb{F}}_q[x]$ and an integer $k\geq3$. The AIM question asks for asymptotics, with $q,h,k$ fixed and $n\to\infty$, for the shifted divisor correlation $\sum_{\deg f=n}d_k(f)d_k(f+h)$. The original does not specify monicity or unit conventions for $d_k$ and allows $h=0$ in its wording.

In the supplied report's monic normalization, $d_k(f)$ counts ordered factorizations of a monic $f$ into $k$ monic factors, including factors $1$. For fixed nonzero $h$ and $n>\deg h$, set \[ S_k(n;h)=\sum_{\substack{f\text{ monic}\\\deg f=n}}d_k(f)d_k(f+h),\qquad r=k-1. \] For a monic polynomial $L$, put $|L|=q^{\deg L}$. The exactly solvable small-lcm sector is \[ T_{r,h}(n)=\sum_{\substack{A,B\text{ monic}\\\deg\operatorname{lcm}(A,B)\leq n\\\gcd(A,B)\mid h}} \frac{d_r(A)d_r(B)}{|\operatorname{lcm}(A,B)|}. \] The compatibility condition is that the congruences $f\equiv0\pmod A$ and $f\equiv-h\pmod B$ be simultaneously soluble. The report claims \[ S_k(n;h)\geq q^n T_{r,h}(n),\qquad T_{r,h}(n)=\frac{C_{q,r}(h)}{(2r)!}n^{2r}+O_{q,k,h}(n^{2r-1}), \] with a convergent positive shift-sensitive Euler product. In this CRT normalization its local factors can be written using monic irreducibles $P$, $Q_P=q^{\deg P}$ and $v_P(h)$ as \[ C_{q,r}(h)=\prod_P\left[(1-Q_P^{-1})^{2r} \sum_{\substack{a,b\geq0\\\min(a,b)\leq v_P(h)}} \binom{a+r-1}{r-1}\binom{b+r-1}{r-1}Q_P^{-\max(a,b)}\right]. \] This is the product associated to the displayed sector; the report's detailed convergence and coefficient analysis is not supplied.

Its consequence is the positive lower bound \[ \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{S_k(n;h)}{q^n n^{2k-2}} \geq\frac{C_{q,k-1}(h)}{(2k-2)!}>0. \] This only bounds part of the sum from below. Contributions with lcm degree exceeding $n$ are not controlled here, so no matching upper bound or full asymptotic is asserted. The zero-shift moment and a sum over all leading coefficients require separate conventions and analysis.

Short English statement

Find fixed-$q$ asymptotics for higher divisor correlations at a fixed shift. The report handles monic polynomials and nonzero shifts, computing the complete small-lcm CRT sector and a positive lower bound on the expected scale. It does not determine the entire correlation or cover the zero shift.
