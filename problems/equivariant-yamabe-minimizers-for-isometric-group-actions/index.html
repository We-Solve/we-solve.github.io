Equivariant Yamabe Minimizers for Isometric Group Actions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the dimension, compactness, connectedness, or boundary conditions of $(M,g)$. Consequently, its unrestricted intended scope cannot be determined. The formulation below makes precise the customary closed-manifold interpretation, conditional on confirming the additional hypotheses that $M$ is a nonempty smooth compact manifold without boundary of dimension $n\geq 3$, and that $g$ is a smooth Riemannian metric. These hypotheses are an explicit interpretive completion, not hypotheses stated in the supplied record; connectedness is left unrestricted.

Under this interpretation, let $G\leq\operatorname{Isom}(M,g)$ be any subgroup. No closedness or finiteness assumption on $G$ is imposed. A metric $h$ is $G$-invariant if $\gamma^*h=h$ for every $\gamma\in G$. Define the $G$-invariant conformal class by \[ [g]_G=\left\{u^{4/(n-2)}g:\ u\in C^\infty(M,{\mathbb{R}}),\ u>0,\ u\circ\gamma=u\text{ for every }\gamma\in G\right\}. \] For a smooth metric $h$, let $R_h$ denote its scalar curvature, let ${\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_h$ denote its Riemannian volume measure, and write $\operatorname{Vol}_h(M)=\int_M{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_h$. Its normalized Yamabe functional is \[ \mathcal{Y}(h)=\frac{\displaystyle\int_M R_h\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_h}{\displaystyle\operatorname{Vol}_h(M)^{(n-2)/n}}. \] Set \[ Y_G(M,[g])=\inf_{h\in[g]_G}\mathcal{Y}(h). \] The question, under the stated conditional interpretation, is whether for every such $(M,g)$ and every subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{Isom}(M,g)$, there exists $h_*\in[g]_G$ satisfying \[ \mathcal{Y}(h_*)=Y_G(M,[g]), \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad \mathcal{Y}(h_*)\leq\mathcal{Y}(h)\quad\text{for every }h\in[g]_G. \] The supplied boundary-related reference does not specify a boundary version of this question. If manifolds with boundary, noncompact manifolds, or other dimensions are intended, the admissible class and functional require further specification.

Short English statement

Does every isometric group action admit a conformal metric that is invariant under the group and minimizes the normalized total scalar curvature among all invariant conformal metrics? The displayed formulation uses an explicitly conditional closed-manifold interpretation in dimension $n\geq3$, because the source record omits the necessary geometric scope. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
