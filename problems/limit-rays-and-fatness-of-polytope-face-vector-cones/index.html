Math-4502

Limit Rays and Fatness of Polytope Face Vector Cones

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks two questions about polytope face vectors. Its notation is not fully defined, so the following separates the recorded questions from conditional interpretations.

Under the usual interpretation, $\mathcal P^d$ denotes the class of convex $d$-dimensional polytopes, $\sigma^d$ denotes a $d$-simplex, and $f_i(P)$ counts the $i$-dimensional faces of $P$. The record does not explicitly specify this class or the coordinates included in $f(P)$.

Limit-ray question. For every integer $d>4$, is every ray of \[ \operatorname{Cone}_{f(\sigma^d)}\left(f(\mathcal P^d)\right) \] a limit ray? Here $f(\mathcal P^d)=\{f(P):P\in\mathcal P^d\}$. The supplied record does not define the cone construction, whether closure is taken, the placement of its apex, or what qualifies as a limit ray. In particular, it does not specify the sequences, normalization, topology, or any unboundedness requirement used in defining a limit ray. These conventions must be recovered before this question has a precise mathematical formulation.

Four-dimensional ratio question, as recorded. Does there exist a real constant $C>0$, independent of $P$, such that for every $P\in\mathcal P^4$, \[ \frac{f_1(P)+f_4(P)}{f_0(P)+f_3(P)}\le C? \] The index $4$ in the numerator is retained exactly from the source record. With ordinary face-dimension indexing, a four-dimensional polytope has $f_4(P)=1$ if the whole polytope is counted as a face; if its face vector contains only proper faces, $f_4(P)$ is not a coordinate. Under the former convention and the convex-polytope interpretation, the denominator is positive and the displayed question becomes \[ \exists C>0\ \forall P\in\mathcal P^4:\qquad f_1(P)+1\le C\left(f_0(P)+f_3(P)\right). \] Whether this literal expression is the intended fatness parameter is unresolved. No replacement of $f_4$ or alternative indexing convention is assumed.

Short English statement

The record asks whether every ray of a simplex-based face-vector cone is a limit ray in each dimension $d>4$, and whether the displayed four-dimensional ratio admits a uniform upper bound. The cone and limit-ray conventions are missing, and the numerator's $f_4$ requires verification. Both questions are supplied as open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
