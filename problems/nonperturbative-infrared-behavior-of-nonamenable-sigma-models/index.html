Nonperturbative Infrared Behavior of Nonamenable Sigma Models

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A nonlinear sigma model has fields taking values in a target manifold and an action determined by its target metric. Noncompact hyperbolic targets can have nonamenable symmetry groups, meaning that they lack an invariant mean on bounded functions. Infrared behavior concerns long spatial scales; an infrared-free perturbative RG prediction says that the effective coupling tends to zero at those scales. Nonperturbative analysis seeks conclusions about the full model rather than a formal finite-order series.

For two-dimensional nonlinear sigma models with noncompact target spaces and nonamenable symmetry groups, does a rigorous nonperturbative analysis confirm the perturbative renormalization-group prediction of infrared asymptotic freedom, or is that prediction an artifact of perturbation theory?

Short English statement

Determine rigorously whether noncompact sigma models really have the long-distance behavior predicted by perturbation theory.
