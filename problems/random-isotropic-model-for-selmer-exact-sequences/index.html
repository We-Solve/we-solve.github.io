Random Isotropic Model for Selmer Exact Sequences

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and $n\geq 1$. Write $\mathbb Z_p$ for the ring of $p$-adic integers and $\mathbb Q_p$ for its fraction field, and set \[ V_n=\mathbb Z_p^{2n},\qquad W_n=\mathbb Z_p^n\times\{0\},\qquad D_p=\mathbb Q_p/\mathbb Z_p. \] The supplied corrected statement uses $\operatorname{OGr}_n(\mathbb Z_p)$ as a parameter space of rank-$n$ maximal isotropic direct summands of $V_n$. A quadratic form on $V_n$, with respect to which isotropy is defined and $W_n$ is maximal isotropic, is needed to specify this space; the supplied record does not give that form. For a quadratic form $q$, isotropy of a submodule means that $q$ vanishes on that submodule. The probability measure intended by a random element of this parameter space is also unspecified.

Conditional on these choices, let $Z_n$ be such a random maximal isotropic direct summand and define \[ \begin{aligned} R_n&=(Z_n\cap W_n)\otimes_{\mathbb Z_p}D_p,\\ S_n&=(Z_n\otimes_{\mathbb Z_p}D_p)\cap (W_n\otimes_{\mathbb Z_p}D_p),\\ T_n&=S_n/R_n. \end{aligned} \] The intersection defining $S_n$ is taken inside $V_n\otimes_{\mathbb Z_p}D_p$, using the maps induced by inclusion. The quotient uses the natural inclusion of $R_n$ into $S_n$. Denote the resulting short exact sequence by \[ \mathcal X_n=\left(0\longrightarrow R_n\longrightarrow S_n \longrightarrow T_n\longrightarrow 0\right). \] Question, subject to unresolved specifications. Does the distribution of $\mathcal X_n$ have a limit as $n\to\infty$, and is that limit the distribution of the corresponding sequences for elliptic curves over $k$? Equivalently, if $\nu_k$ denotes the intended elliptic-curve distribution, the proposed comparison is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\operatorname{Law}(\mathcal X_n)=\nu_k. \] This equation is a conditional formulation: the record does not specify $k$, the elliptic-curve sequences, the family and ordering of curves, the probability measure defining $\nu_k$, or the space of sequence classes and notion of convergence. Its background describes the intended comparison as one with elliptic-curve Selmer data over number fields, but does not supply these missing definitions.

The formulas above follow the supplied corrected statement, using $D_p=\mathbb Q_p/\mathbb Z_p$. The field labeled original statement instead tensors with $\mathbb Q_p$, which gives a materially different construction. The record attributes this discrepancy to extraction corruption; that attribution has not been independently verified.

Short English statement

The proposed model forms an exact sequence from the intersection of a fixed maximal isotropic lattice with a random one, using tensor products with $\mathbb Q_p/\mathbb Z_p$. It asks whether the limiting distribution agrees with the corresponding elliptic-curve Selmer sequences. The quadratic form, random sampling law, field $k$, and elliptic-curve distribution require specification before this becomes a fully precise conjecture. The supplied record labels the problem open; this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
