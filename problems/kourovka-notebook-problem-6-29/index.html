Kourovka Notebook Problem 6.29

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a topological group $G$, let \[ \operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{top}}(G)=\{\alpha:G\to G:\alpha\text{ is a group isomorphism and both }\alpha,\alpha^{-1}\text{ are continuous}\}, \] with composition as the group operation. For an abstract group $H$, let $\operatorname{Aut}(H)$ denote its group of all group automorphisms. When $H$ carries the discrete topology, every automorphism is a topological automorphism.

The question proposed by O. V. Mel'nikov is whether, for every finite group $A$, \[ \left[\exists\text{ a locally compact topological group }G\quad \operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{top}}(G)\cong A\right] \Longrightarrow \left[\exists\text{ a group }H\quad \operatorname{Aut}(H)\cong A\right]. \] All displayed isomorphisms are isomorphisms of abstract groups; the record specifies no topology on an automorphism group. The group $H$ is to be given the discrete topology, and no restriction on its cardinality is stated.

The phrase "locally compact group" retains the convention of the cited source: the supplied record does not explicitly specify whether Hausdorffness is included, so that separation convention requires confirmation before the scope is fixed.

The supplied statement reports that the implication holds when $A$ is cyclic and that local compactness of $G$ is essential, citing R. J. Wille. These are supplied background assertions, not independently verified conclusions of this draft; no counterexample supporting the latter assertion is included in the record.

Short English statement

If a finite group $A$ occurs as the full group of topological automorphisms of some locally compact group, must it also occur as the full automorphism group of some discrete group? The discrete group need not be finite. The source reports an affirmative answer for cyclic $A$ and says that local compactness is essential; its separation convention needs confirmation.
