Math-1127

Polynomial Bounds for Polytope Diameters

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret a $d$-polytope in the usual sense of a bounded convex polytope of affine dimension $d$ over ${\mathbb{R}}$. This domain convention is not explicitly stated in the supplied record and requires source confirmation. For $d\geq 1$, let $n(P)$ be the number of facets of such a polytope $P$, so $n(P)\geq d+1$. Let $G(P)$ be its graph, whose vertices and edges are the vertices and edges of $P$, and define \[ \delta(P)=\max_{u,v\in V(G(P))}\operatorname{dist}_{G(P)}(u,v), \] where distance counts edges in a shortest path. For every pair $(d,n)$ for which such polytopes exist, write \[ \Delta(d,n)=\max\{\delta(P):\dim P=d,\ n(P)=n\}. \] The supplied problem asks the following three questions.
  1. Do there exist constants $C>0$ and an integer $k\geq 0$, independent of both dimension and facet count, such that every polytope $P$ of positive dimension satisfies \[ \delta(P)\leq C\,n(P)^k? \] The original statement additionally describes this question as equivalent to its restriction to polytopes with $n=2d$ facets. That restricted question is whether there exist constants $C'>0$ and an integer $k'\geq 0$ such that \[ \Delta(d,2d)\leq C'(2d)^{k'}\qquad\text{for every integer }d\geq 1. \] The equivalence is retained as a qualification supplied by the source record; no proof or independent verification is supplied here.
  2. Does there exist a sequence of polytopes $(P_j)_{j\geq 1}$, with dimensions allowed to vary, such that \[ n(P_j)\longrightarrow\infty, \qquad \frac{\delta(P_j)}{n(P_j)}\longrightarrow\infty? \] This expresses "growing superlinearly" as an unbounded diameter-to-facet ratio along a family. The supplied wording does not specify a quantitative growth rate, such as a fixed power greater than one.
  3. Is it true that, for every integer $d\geq 1$ and every $d$-polytope $P$ with $n$ facets, \[ \delta(P)\leq d(n-d)? \]
These are questions about actual polytopes. The supplied record labels the problem open; this label and its literature assessment are supplied curation, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether polytope graph diameters have a polynomial bound in the number of facets that is uniform over all dimensions, whether some family has an unbounded diameter-to-facet ratio, and whether the specific bound $d(n-d)$ always holds. The original record also states an equivalence between the polynomial-bound question and its restriction to $2d$ facets. Domain conventions, the meaning of superlinear growth, and the supplied literature status require human review.
