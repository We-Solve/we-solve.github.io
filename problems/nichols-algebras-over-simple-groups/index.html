Math-6925

Nichols Algebras over Simple Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite simple group, where simplicity means that $G\neq\{e\}$ and its only normal subgroups are $\{e\}$ and $G$. This convention includes cyclic groups of prime order; the supplied statement does not specify that $G$ is nonabelian.

Using the left--left convention, a Yetter--Drinfeld module over ${\mathbb{C}} G$ is a complex vector space $V$ with a linear $G$-action and a grading \[ V=\bigoplus_{h\in G}V_h, \qquad g\cdot V_h=V_{ghg^{-1}}\quad(g,h\in G). \] Its braiding is determined by \[ c(v\otimes w)=h\cdot w\otimes v \qquad(v\in V_h,\ w\in V). \] Let $T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}V^{\otimes n}$ be the tensor algebra with its braided Hopf algebra structure in which every element of $V$ is primitive. The Nichols algebra of $V$ is \[ \mathcal B(V)=T(V)/I(V), \] where $I(V)$ is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{n\geq2}V^{\otimes n}$.

Question as supplied. Is it true that, for every finite simple group $G$ and every nonzero Yetter--Drinfeld module $V$ over ${\mathbb{C}} G$, \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\mathcal B(V)=\infty? \] No finite-dimensionality assumption on $V$ is supplied.

Unresolved formulation qualification. The record does not establish whether "finite simple group" was intended to mean "finite nonabelian simple group." These give different quantified questions. The displayed question retains the literal supplied wording; its identification with the intended source conjecture requires review. The supplied original excerpt names Conjecture 1 but omits its mathematical content and the identity of its reference [1].

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every nonzero Yetter--Drinfeld module over the complex group algebra of a finite simple group has an infinite-dimensional Nichols algebra. Whether the group must be nonabelian remains unresolved in this draft. The record labels the problem open; this is supplied curation, not an independently verified status.
