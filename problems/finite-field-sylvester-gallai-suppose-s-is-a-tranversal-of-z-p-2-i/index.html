Math-1880

Finite field Sylvester-Gallai: Suppose S is a tranversal of Z_(p)^(2), i

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret $p$ as a prime and let ${\mathbb{F}}_p={\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$. A transversal of the two coordinate parallel classes in ${\mathbb{F}}_p^2$ is a set $S$ meeting each horizontal and each vertical line in exactly one point. Equivalently, \[ S=\{(x,f(x)):x\in{\mathbb{F}}_p\} \] for a permutation $f:{\mathbb{F}}_p\to{\mathbb{F}}_p$. An affine line is a set \[ \ell_{a,b,c}=\{(x,y)\in{\mathbb{F}}_p^2:ax+by=c\},\qquad (a,b)\ne(0,0), \] and it is ordinary for $S$ if $|S\cap\ell_{a,b,c}|=2$. The literal source question, attributed to Solymosi, is whether every such transversal has an ordinary line. The description allows any two distinct parallel classes, which may be put into coordinate form by an invertible affine change of coordinates.

The literal statement lacks a non-collinearity hypothesis: for an odd prime, a transversal that is itself an affine line has no ordinary line. The supplied research summary refers to a "corrected statement" without stating it explicitly. Its natural nontrivial candidate is \[ \forall p\text{ prime}\ \forall S\subseteq{\mathbb{F}}_p^2,\quad \left(S\text{ is a transversal and is not contained in a line}\right) \Longrightarrow\exists\ell:\ |S\cap\ell|=2. \] This candidate records the apparent intended correction rather than silently changing the supplied question. The case $p=2$ also needs care in the summary's counting claim: a full affine line then has exactly two points and is ordinary.

Short English statement

Must a non-collinear permutation graph over a prime field have a line through exactly two of its points? The source omits non-collinearity, while its research summary discusses a corrected conjecture.

  • The supplied research summary claims a new self-contained analytic argument for the corrected conjecture for $p\leq5$, and exhaustive verification through $p\leq13$, with the no-ordinary-line transversals described as the $p(p-1)$ affine lines. No argument, computation certificate, or publication reference is supplied, and the last description requires excluding $p=2$. The summary calls the problem open for $p\geq17$.
  • The summary names Rédei's direction bound without a precise citation. For a hypothetical non-collinear no-ordinary-line transversal it reports at least $(p+3)/2$ determined slopes, at most $(p-1)/2$ determined slopes through each point, and the inequality $3|D|\leq\sum_{x\in S}r(x)\leq p(p-1)/2$. Here $D$ denotes determined directions and $r(x)$ is apparently their number through $x$; the source does not supply the promised preceding argument or detailed definitions. These are recorded claims, not verified inputs to the formulation.
