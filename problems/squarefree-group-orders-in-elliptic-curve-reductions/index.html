Math-4202

Squarefree Group Orders in Elliptic-Curve Reductions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E/{\mathbb{Q}}$ be an elliptic curve satisfying $\operatorname{End}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(E)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}$, where $\operatorname{End}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(E)$ is the ring of endomorphisms defined over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. For a rational prime $p$ of good reduction, let $E_p/{\mathbb{F}}_p$ denote its reduction and put $N_p=\#E_p({\mathbb{F}}_p)$. A positive integer $n$ is squarefree if $\ell^2\nmid n$ for every prime $\ell$. Write $\pi(x)=\#\{p\le x:p\text{ is prime}\}$ and \[ S_E(x)=\#\{p\le x:p\text{ is a prime of good reduction for }E,\ N_p\text{ is squarefree}\}. \] The supplied question asks whether, for every such $E$, there exists an explicit constant $\delta(E)$, depending on $E$, such that \[ S_E(x)\sim\delta(E)\pi(x)\qquad(x\to\infty). \] Here $f(x)\sim g(x)$ means that $f(x)/g(x)\to1$. Restriction to primes of good reduction makes the reduction notation well defined; the source does not explicitly state this restriction.

Two qualifications remain unresolved. First, the supplied literature assessment calls the stated hypothesis the non-CM case, whereas the condition $\operatorname{End}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(E)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}$ does not itself impose the geometric non-CM condition $\operatorname{End}_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}}(E)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}$. The displayed hypothesis is retained literally. Second, the source gives neither a positivity hypothesis nor a convention for $\delta(E)=0$. With the usual meaning of $\sim$, the displayed assertion requires $\delta(E)>0$; a possibly zero density statement $S_E(x)=\delta(E)\pi(x)+o(\pi(x))$ would be a different formulation. No choice between these intended meanings, or definition of what counts as an explicit constant, is supplied.

Short English statement

For an elliptic curve over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with the stated rational endomorphism ring, the question asks for an explicit asymptotic proportion of good primes whose reductions have squarefree group order. The intended non-CM hypothesis and treatment of a possible zero proportion require clarification.
