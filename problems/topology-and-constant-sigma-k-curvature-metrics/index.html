Math-742

Topology and Constant $\sigma_k$-Curvature Metrics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Three-part problem. For integers $2\le k<n/2$, characterize compact $n$-manifolds admitting a $k$-positive metric, identify topological obstructions, and determine when a conformal class containing such a metric also contains a metric of constant $\sigma_k$-curvature. The strict upper bound on $k$ is retained, so the stated range implies $n\ge5$.

In the standard Schouten-tensor convention, for a smooth Riemannian metric $g$ in dimension $n\ge3$, define\[A_g=\frac1{n-2}\left(\operatorname{Ric}_g-\frac{R_g}{2(n-1)}g\right),\qquad \sigma_k(g)=\sigma_k\left(\lambda(g^{-1}A_g)\right),\] where $\sigma_j(\lambda)=\sum_{i_1<\cdots<i_j}\lambda_{i_1}\cdots\lambda_{i_j}$ is the $j$th elementary symmetric polynomial. The positive Gårding cone is $\Gamma_k=\{\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\sigma_j(\lambda)>0\text{ for }1\le j\le k\}$, equivalently the component of $\{\sigma_k>0\}$ containing the positive orthant. A $k$-positive, or $k$-admissible, metric has $\lambda(g^{-1}A_g)\in\Gamma_k$ at every point. This standard interpretation must be checked because the source does not itself define $k$-positive; positivity of $\sigma_k$ alone does not specify the elliptic branch.

Under the usual closed-manifold interpretation, the conformal existence question asks: if $g$ is $k$-admissible, is there a smooth $u:M\to{\mathbb{R}}$ and a constant $c>0$ for which $\widehat g=e^{2u}g$ remains $k$-admissible and $\sigma_k(\widehat g)\equiv c$? More generally, determine the precise conditions on the conformal class ensuring such a solution. No volume normalization, prescribed value of $c$, or local-conformal-flatness assumption is supplied. The source says compact but does not explicitly exclude boundary; boundary problems would need additional boundary data. The separate metric-existence and topological-obstruction tasks are not reduced to this conformal PDE.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the problem partially solved. It reports broad existence and compactness results in locally conformally flat and low-$k$ cases, with general non-locally-conformally-flat cases described as unresolved. The exact theorem scopes and any restrictions within the stated $2\le k<n/2$ range are not reproduced, so no blanket assertion for all such cases is inferred. This dated assessment requires independent verification.

Short English statement

Classify manifolds with Schouten $k$-positive metrics, find their topological obstructions, and solve the constant-$\sigma_k$ problem within admissible conformal classes. The specified range is strictly $2\le k<n/2$, and the positive elliptic cone must be retained.
