Math-1225

Rational ideal compression and the non-Q-Gorenstein sign defect

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Alexeev's question asks for definitions of log canonical, log terminal and related singularities for a pair $(X,Z)$, where $X$ is normal and $K_X$ need not be ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier, and $Z$ is a formal sum of subschemes. The original does not specify the field or allowed coefficients. The attached aid uses characteristic zero and an effective rational sum $Z=\sum_jt_jZ_j$, with ideals $\mathfrak a_j\ne0$ and $t_j\in{\mathbb{Q}}_{\geq0}$.

For a proper birational morphism $f:Y\to X$ with smooth $Y$ and compatible canonical divisors, define the natural pullback of a Weil divisor $D$ by \[ \mathcal O_Y(-f^{\natural}D)= (\mathcal O_X(-D)\cdot\mathcal O_Y)^{\vee\vee}. \] The asymptotic pullback $f^*D$ is obtained coefficientwise from $m^{-1}f^{\natural}(mD)$ along sufficiently divisible indices. For a prime divisor $E$ over $X$, put $\operatorname{ord}_E Z=\sum_jt_j\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak a_j$. The limiting and plus discrepancy expressions are \[ a^-_{m,E}(X,Z)=\operatorname{ord}_E\!\left(K_Y-\frac1m f^{\natural}(mK_X)\right)-\operatorname{ord}_E Z, \] \[ a^+_E(X,Z)=\operatorname{ord}_E\left(K_Y+f^*(-K_X)\right)-\operatorname{ord}_E Z. \] Their log-canonical and log-terminal tests use thresholds $\geq-1$ and $>-1$, respectively, for the relevant divisors, with the limiting definition also requiring a specified sufficiently-divisible-$m$ convention. These competing extensions must be distinguished; in the ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Gorenstein case and divisible index, they recover the usual discrepancy expressions. The aid reports de Fernex--Hacon's 2009 limiting/plus theory as a published answer to the historical request, without supplying all definitional quantifiers for every "etc." class.

Its rational compression result chooses $q\geq1$ with $qt_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ and sets \[ \mathfrak b=\prod_j\mathfrak a_j^{qt_j},\qquad \operatorname{ord}_E Z=\frac1q\operatorname{ord}_E\mathfrak b. \] It reports that the limiting/plus and Mather--Jacobian subscheme contributions depend only on this weighted ideal up to integral closure. This claim has the aid's rational effective-coefficient scope, not arbitrary real or signed sums.

The aid further reports the exact subscheme-independent gap \[ a^+_E(X,Z)-a^-_{m,E}(X,Z) =\operatorname{ord}_E\!\left(f^*(-K_X)+\frac1m f^{\natural}(mK_X)\right)\geq0. \] It says this gap decreases along divisible indices and, along factorial indices, converges to \[ \operatorname{ord}_E\left(f^*(-K_X)+f^*K_X\right), \] the asymptotic sign-linearity defect. No equivalence between vanishing of this defect and every possible numerical or ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier condition is asserted here.

Short English statement

Define singularity classes for normal pairs without a ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier canonical divisor. The attached aid reports the existing limiting/plus discrepancy approach, compresses rational subscheme sums into one weighted product ideal, and compares the two discrepancies by a nonnegative sign-linearity defect.
