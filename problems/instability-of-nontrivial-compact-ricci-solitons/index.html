Instability of Nontrivial Compact Ricci Solitons

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Ricci soliton is a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$ equipped with a vector field $X$ and a constant $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g+\tfrac12\mathcal L_Xg=\lambda g. \] It is a gradient soliton when $X=\nabla^g f_0$, so that $\operatorname{Ric}_g+\nabla_g^2f_0=\lambda g$, and it is shrinking when $\lambda>0$. An Einstein metric satisfies $\operatorname{Ric}_g=cg$ for a constant $c$. The supplied record does not explicitly define "nontrivial" or specify the manifold and operator conventions underlying its entropy statement.

Here is a precise conditional formulation in the usual closed, connected, gradient shrinking setting. Let $M$ be a smooth, closed, connected manifold of dimension $n$, and let $g$ be a smooth Riemannian metric with \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g+\nabla_g^2f_0=\frac{1}{2\tau_0}g, \qquad \tau_0>0. \] For this formulation, call the soliton nontrivial when $g$ is not Einstein. These conventions require confirmation against the original source; they are not additional hypotheses explicitly supplied by the record.

For $\tau>0$ and $f\in C^\infty(M)$ satisfying \[ \int_M(4\pi\tau)^{-n/2}e^{-f}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g=1, \] define Perelman's entropy functionals by \[ \mathcal W(g,f,\tau)=\int_M\left[\tau(R_g+|\nabla^g f|_g^2)+f-n\right](4\pi\tau)^{-n/2}e^{-f}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g, \] \[ \mu(g,\tau)=\inf_{f\text{ satisfying the normalization}}\mathcal W(g,f,\tau), \qquad \nu(g)=\inf_{\tau>0}\mu(g,\tau). \] Here $R_g$ is scalar curvature and ${\,\mathrm{d}} V_g$ is Riemannian volume. At a soliton where the second variation is defined, write \[ Q_g(h,k)=\left.\frac{\partial^2}{\partial s\,\partial t}\right|_{s=t=0}\nu(g+sh+tk), \qquad h,k\in C^\infty(M,\operatorname{Sym}^2T^*M). \] Normalize $f_0$ so that ${\,\mathrm{d}}\rho_0=(4\pi\tau_0)^{-n/2}e^{-f_0}{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g$ has total mass one, and represent the Hessian by $H_g$ through \[ Q_g(h,k)=\int_M\langle H_gh,k\rangle_g\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\rho_0. \] Question. For every nontrivial compact Ricci soliton in the intended source setting, does its entropy Hessian have a positive eigenvalue? Under the conventions above, this asks whether every such $(M,g,f_0,\tau_0)$ admits $\eta>0$ and a nonzero smooth symmetric two-tensor $h$ such that \[ H_gh=\eta h? \] The positive sign is the instability convention in the supplied statement. The source record does not identify the precise operator domain, gauge restriction, or treatment of the null directions arising from diffeomorphisms and scaling. Thus the displayed realization makes the intended question explicit but remains subject to source and mathematical review.

Short English statement

Must every nontrivial compact Ricci soliton have a positive eigenvalue in the second variation of Perelman's $\nu$-entropy? The supplied record labels this question open. The precise meaning of nontriviality, the manifold conventions, and the Hessian realization require confirmation; the displayed formulation uses a closed, connected, gradient shrinking soliton with a non-Einstein metric.
