Hyperfiniteness of Minimal-Subshift Conjugacy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the conventional interpretation of symbolic subshifts as two-sided shifts over finite alphabets, formulate the question as follows. These conventions are not explicitly specified in the supplied record and require source confirmation.

For each integer $k\geq 1$, let $A_k=\{0,\ldots,k-1\}$, give $A_k^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ the product topology, and define the shift by \[ (\sigma_k x)(n)=x(n+1)\qquad(n\in{\mathbb{Z}}). \] A subshift is a nonempty closed subset $X\subseteq A_k^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ satisfying $\sigma_k[X]=X$. It is minimal if \[ \overline{\{\sigma_k^m x:m\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}}=X \qquad\text{for every }x\in X. \] Let $\mathcal M_k$ be the space of minimal subshifts over $A_k$, with the Borel structure inherited from the Vietoris topology on the hyperspace of nonempty compact subsets of $A_k^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$. Use the tagged disjoint union \[ \mathcal M=\bigsqcup_{k\geq1}\mathcal M_k \] as a coding space for minimal symbolic subshifts over finite alphabets.

For $X\in\mathcal M_k$ and $Y\in\mathcal M_\ell$, define topological conjugacy by \[ X\mathrel{E_{\mathrm{conj}}}Y \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{there exists a homeomorphism }h:X\longrightarrow Y \text{ with }h\circ(\sigma_k|_X)=(\sigma_\ell|_Y)\circ h. \] An equivalence relation $E$ on a standard Borel space $S$ is hyperfinite if there exist Borel equivalence relations $F_j\subseteq S\times S$, indexed by $j\in{\mathbb{N}}$, such that every $F_j$-class is finite and \[ F_j\subseteq F_{j+1}\quad\text{for every }j, \qquad E=\bigcup_{j\in{\mathbb{N}}}F_j. \] No uniform bound on the sizes of the finite classes is required.

A point $x\in A_k^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ is a Toeplitz point if \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\ \exists p\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\ \forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}, \qquad x(n+mp)=x(n). \] Let $\mathcal T\subseteq\mathcal M$ consist of those minimal subshifts that are orbit closures of Toeplitz points: \[ X=\overline{\{\sigma_k^m x:m\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}} \quad\text{for some Toeplitz point }x\in A_k^{{\mathbb{Z}}}. \] This definition includes periodic Toeplitz points; the supplied record does not state whether periodic systems are excluded by its terminology.

Question. Is $E_{\mathrm{conj}}$ on $\mathcal M$ hyperfinite? In particular, is the restricted relation \[ E_{\mathrm{conj}}\!\upharpoonright\mathcal T =E_{\mathrm{conj}}\cap(\mathcal T\times\mathcal T) \] hyperfinite, using the inherited Borel structure?

The supplied original passage mentions the minimal-subshift question and a connection with a conjecture of Thomas concerning isomorphism of complete groups. No precise formulation of that conjecture or of the connection is supplied, so neither is added as a mathematical assertion here. The Toeplitz subquestion occurs in the supplied reconstructed statement, rather than in the supplied original passage. The record labels the problem open; this is a supplied status, not an independently verified conclusion.

Short English statement

Does topological conjugacy of minimal symbolic subshifts admit an increasing exhaustion by Borel equivalence relations with finite classes? The supplied statement also asks this for Toeplitz subshifts. The formulation uses two-sided shifts over finite alphabets, subject to confirmation of the source conventions. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
