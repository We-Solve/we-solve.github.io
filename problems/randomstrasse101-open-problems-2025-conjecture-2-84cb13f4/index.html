Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2025) — Conjecture 2

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M\geq 2$ be an integer and set $N=2M-1$. Write $[N]=\{1,\ldots,N\}$. For $A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N\times M}$, let $A_k$ denote its $k$-th row, and let $A_S$ be the submatrix consisting of the rows indexed by $S\subseteq[N]$. Complements are taken in $[N]$. Assume that every set of $M$ rows of $A$ spans ${\mathbb{R}}^M$.

The supplied source defines \[ \omega(A)=\min_{\substack{S\subseteq[N]\\ \operatorname{rank}(A_{S^c})<n}}\sigma_M(A_S), \] but does not define $n$. Thus its literal definition does not determine a quantity from $A$ alone. The following formulation is conditional on interpreting $n=M$, and on interpreting the row norm as the Euclidean norm.

Under these interpretations, define \[ \omega(A):=\min_{\substack{S\subseteq[N]\\ \operatorname{rank}(A_{S^c})<M}}\sigma_M(A_S), \qquad \|A_k\|_2:=\left(\sum_{j=1}^{M}A_{kj}^{\,2}\right)^{1/2}. \] Here $\sigma_M(A_S)$ is the $M$-th singular value in nonincreasing order. The spanning assumption implies that the admissible sets are exactly those with $|S|\geq M$. Consequently, every matrix occurring in this minimum has at least $M$ rows and has full column rank, so its $M$-th singular value is well defined and positive.

Conjecture 2, conditional formulation. There exist constants $C>0$ and $\beta\in(0,1)$, independent of $M$ and $A$, such that for every integer $M\geq2$ and every $A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{(2M-1)\times M}$ for which every set of $M$ rows spans ${\mathbb{R}}^M$, \[ \omega(A)\leq C\,\beta^M\max_{k\in[2M-1]}\|A_k\|_2. \]

The source also states a complement-property characterization of injectivity of a map $\mathcal A$ by $\omega(A)>0$, and describes stability as closely connected to the inverse of $\omega(A)$. The supplied record does not define $\mathcal A$, its domain, or the stability notion, so these contextual assertions cannot be formulated precisely from this record.

Short English statement

Conditionally interpreting the undefined source symbol $n$ as $M$ and the row norm as Euclidean, the conjecture asks for universal constants giving an exponentially small upper bound on $\omega(A)$, relative to the largest row norm, for every real $(2M-1)\times M$ matrix whose every $M$ rows span ${\mathbb{R}}^M$. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied curation and has not been independently verified.
