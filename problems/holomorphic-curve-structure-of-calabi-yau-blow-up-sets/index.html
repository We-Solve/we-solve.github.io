Holomorphic-Curve Structure of Calabi–Yau Blow-Up Sets

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An almost-complex structure on a smooth manifold $X$ is a smooth bundle endomorphism $J:TX\to TX$ satisfying $J^2=-\mathrm{id}_{TX}$. In the usual parametrized terminology, a $J$-holomorphic curve is represented by a nonconstant map $u:(\Sigma,j)\to(X,J)$ from a Riemann surface satisfying \[ \mathrm{d}u\circ j=J\circ\mathrm{d}u. \] Its image is a subset of $X$. The supplied record does not specify which class of such curves is intended.

The proposed conditional assertion concerns Donaldson's almost-complex Calabi--Yau equation: assume the intended $\varepsilon$-regularity estimate holds. If the a priori estimates in Donaldson's conjecture fail, must the resulting blow-up set $B$ admit a representation \[ B=\bigcup_{\alpha\in I}C_\alpha, \] where each $C_\alpha$ is a $J$-holomorphic curve?

This is a schematic formulation of the supplied speculative assertion. The record does not state the equation, the ambient geometric hypotheses, the regularity estimate, the a priori estimates, the degenerating sequence, or the definition of $B$. Consequently, the relevant domains, boundary conditions, convergence conditions, and quantification over blow-up sequences cannot be made precise from this record. It also does not specify whether the union is finite or locally finite, whether curves are closed or compact, or whether singularities or multiplicities are intended. These qualifications remain unresolved. The proposed curve structure is described as potentially leading toward Donaldson's conjecture; no implication establishing that conjecture is supplied.

Short English statement

Assuming an unspecified $\varepsilon$-regularity estimate for Donaldson's almost-complex Calabi--Yau equation, the question asks whether failure of the conjectured a priori estimates produces a blow-up set made of $J$-holomorphic curves. The supplied formulation is speculative and lacks the geometric and analytic hypotheses needed for a fully quantified statement.
