Chemical Distance in Critical Percolation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology only, let $G=(V,E)$ be a graph equipped with a percolation configuration $\omega$, with openness of paths interpreted according to the chosen percolation model. For vertices $x,y$ admitted by that configuration, the chemical distance is \[ d_\omega(x,y)=\inf\left\{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:\text{there is an open path of }n\text{ edges from }x\text{ to }y\right\}, \] with $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$ and the length-zero path allowed at an admitted vertex. This definition explains the terminology; the supplied record does not specify the intended graph, percolation model, critical parameter, or conditioning on connectivity.

Source research request. Obtain stronger results for chemical distance in critical percolation and its relation to metrics associated with nonsimple conformal loop ensembles (CLE).

Short English statement

The record asks for stronger results about shortest open-path distances in critical percolation and their relation to nonsimple CLE metrics.
