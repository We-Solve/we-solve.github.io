Definitions and mathematical statement

For each positive integer $p$, let $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_p)\in[0,\infty)^p$. The supplied observation model consists of independent random variables satisfying \[ Y_i\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda_i),\qquad i=1,\ldots,p. \] The recorded construction goal is to find an empirical-Bayes estimator that approximates an unknown-prior Bayes estimator with uniformly small excess quadratic risk as $p\to\infty$. The prior model, uniformity class, risk normalization, and quantitative meaning of "small" are not specified.

To explain the missing specifications, consider the following possible hierarchical interpretation, which is not asserted to be the uniquely intended formulation. Let $G$ be a probability measure on $[0,\infty)$, let $\Lambda_1,\ldots,\Lambda_p$ be independent with common distribution $G$, and, conditionally on these variables, let the observations be independent with $Y_i\mid\Lambda_i\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Lambda_i)$. Write ${\mathbb{N}}_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and define the marginal mass function \[ f_G(y)=\int_{[0,\infty)}e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^y}{y!}\,G({\,\mathrm{d}}\lambda),\qquad y\in{\mathbb{N}}_0, \] where $\lambda^0=1$, including at $\lambda=0$. Wherever $f_G(y)>0$, the posterior mean is \[ \delta_G(y)=\frac{\int_{[0,\infty)}\lambda e^{-\lambda}\lambda^y/y!\,G({\,\mathrm{d}}\lambda)}{f_G(y)} =(y+1)\frac{f_G(y+1)}{f_G(y)}. \] Its value at observations with zero marginal probability may be assigned arbitrarily. When the relevant quadratic risks are finite, the oracle Bayes estimator under this hierarchical interpretation is $\delta_G^{(p)}(Y)=(\delta_G(Y_1),\ldots,\delta_G(Y_p))$.

For illustration, choose average quadratic risk and define, for an estimator $\widehat\lambda^{(p)}:{\mathbb{N}}_0^p\to[0,\infty)^p$, \[ R_p(\widehat\lambda^{(p)},G)=\frac1p\mathbb E_G\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^p\left(\widehat\lambda_i^{(p)}(Y)-\Lambda_i\right)^2\right]. \] Here the expectation includes both the latent intensities and the observations. A possible precise target would require specified classes $\mathcal G_p$ of priors and specified numbers $\varepsilon_p\downarrow0$, and would ask for estimators that do not depend on the unknown $G$ such that \[ \sup_{G\in\mathcal G_p}\left\{R_p(\widehat\lambda^{(p)},G)-R_p(\delta_G^{(p)},G)\right\}\leq\varepsilon_p, \] with assumptions ensuring that the displayed differences are well defined. This is only a formulation template: neither these classes nor this rate or normalization is supplied. The record also does not resolve whether the intended risk instead conditions on a deterministic intensity vector or uses total quadratic loss.

Short English statement

Construct an estimator of independent Poisson intensities that approaches the quadratic risk of an oracle knowing the prior as the number of observations grows. The supplied record leaves the prior model, uniformity class, loss normalization, and error rate unspecified. Its verification note says the source itself answers the extracted construction goal, so a separate precise open problem cannot be recovered from this record.
