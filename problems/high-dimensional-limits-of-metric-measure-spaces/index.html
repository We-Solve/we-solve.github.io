High-Dimensional Limits of Metric-Measure Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the Riemannian part of the question, write a sequence as $(M_j,g_j,\mu_j)_{j\in{\mathbb{N}}}$, where $(M_j,g_j)$ is a finite-dimensional Riemannian manifold and $\mu_j$ is a measure to be specified. The condition that dimensions tend to infinity means \[ n_j:=\dim M_j,\qquad \lim_{j\to\infty}n_j=+\infty. \] The record does not prescribe whether $\mu_j$ is Riemannian volume, normalized volume, or another measure. For the metric-measure part, write $(X_j,d_j,\mu_j)$, where $d_j$ is a metric and $\mu_j$ is a Borel measure. Neither the additional regularity of these spaces nor the notion of dimension used in the condition $\dim X_j\to\infty$ is specified.

Research question. How should sequences of Riemannian or metric-measure spaces whose dimensions tend to infinity be studied, particularly with respect to concentration convergence, curvature-dimension conditions, and Laplacian eigenvalues?

The supplied question asks for a framework relating these three subjects, but does not specify a mathematical conclusion. In particular, it does not select a definition of concentration convergence, a limiting object, a curvature-dimension condition or its parameters, or a spectral convergence assertion. Consequently, it cannot yet be expressed as a uniquely determined quantified conjecture.

For clarity about the spectral terminology, if a nonnegative self-adjoint Laplacian realization $L_j$ on $L^2(X_j,\mu_j)$ has been specified, then an eigenvalue is a number $\lambda$ for which \[ L_j u=\lambda u \quad\text{for some }u\in\operatorname{Dom}(L_j)\setminus\{0\}. \] This conditional definition does not assert that such a realization or any eigenvalues exist for the spaces in the record. Compactness, completeness, boundary conditions, discreteness of spectrum, metric and measure normalizations, and any scaling of eigenvalues remain unspecified. These choices are required before a precise convergence or stability statement can be formulated.

Short English statement

The question concerns spaces of increasing dimension and asks how concentration, curvature-dimension conditions, and Laplacian eigenvalues should be studied together. The supplied record presents a broad research program rather than a single precise conjecture; the class of spaces, convergence notions, curvature assumptions, and spectral target require specification.
