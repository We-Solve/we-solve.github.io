Math-293

A collision-stable global-residue reduction for the Higgs-moduli Bethe formula

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The main source is Conjecture $6.16$ on the rank-$n$, degree-$d$, fixed-determinant trace-free Higgs moduli space $M=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Dol}}^d(\operatorname{SL}(n,{\mathbb{C}}))$ over a compact Riemann surface. Precise determinant, stability, coprimality and genus hypotheses are not given in the conjecture excerpt. With $F$ denoting fixed components for the relevant Higgs-field scaling action, it writes the localized equivariant volume \[ \int_Me^\alpha=\sum_F\int_F\frac{e^{\alpha|_F}}{E(N_F)}. \] Here an equivariant Euler denominator and localized noncompact integration convention are intended; neither the equivariant class $\alpha$ nor that convention is supplied. The conjecture asks for this and all equivariant intersection numbers as iterated residues of an expression $BA(y_1,\ldots,y_n)$. Poles indexed by ordered partitions $n=\lambda_1+\cdots+\lambda_k$ should reproduce fixed-locus contributions of type $(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_k)$; all remaining poles should solve Bethe Ansatz equations, with \[ \int_Me^\alpha=-\sum_{\text{Bethe poles}}\operatorname{Res}(BA). \] The expression, equations, independent variables, residue order, orientations, Weyl factors and compactification are absent, so this is the source's schematic target, not a fully specified residue identity.

The aid reports a rigorous rank-two formula for a universal invariant sector, but no all-rank proof of this exact conjecture. Its all-rank conditional reduction requires an applicable compact several-variable global-residue theorem; a partition-pole/fixed-locus match with consistent residue order and Weyl normalization; exhaustion of finite poles by partition and Bethe poles; and vanishing total boundary residue. Under those checks the global zero-sum identity gives the displayed negative Bethe sum. The checks must also hold for each insertion to cover all intersection numbers. They are not proved for the unspecified $BA$ here.

For collision stability the aid uses the following complete-intersection residue formulation. Let $f_1(y,t),\ldots,f_r(y,t)$ and $a(y,t)$ be holomorphic on a parameterized neighborhood of a relatively compact domain in ${\mathbb{C}}^r$. Assume the common-zero scheme is finite, isolated inside the domain, with no zeros escaping or meeting its boundary and with the local complete-intersection residue setup preserved. For simple zeros at generic $t$, the total residue is \[ \sum_{f(y,t)=0}\frac{a(y,t)}{\det(\partial f_i/\partial y_j)(y,t)}. \] Its limit when roots collide is the sum of the local Grothendieck residues of $a\,{\,\mathrm{d}} y_1\wedge\cdots\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}} y_r/(f_1\cdots f_r)$ on the entire zero-dimensional scheme at the limiting parameter, including multiplicities. This defines a collision-stable total Bethe contribution under the stated no-escape and residue hypotheses. An individual Jacobian denominator is not used at a multiple root; positive-dimensional limits or boundary escape are outside this statement.

The canonical extraction also contains Section $6.5$, "Arithmetic approach." Its data and normalization issues are retained separately. For a compact Riemann surface $\Sigma$ of genus $g$, integers $n\geq1,d$ with $\gcd(n,d)=1$, and primitive central twist $\xi_n$ corresponding to $d$, it describes \[ \mathcal M_B^d(\operatorname{GL}(n,{\mathbb{C}}))= \{(A_i,B_i)\in\operatorname{GL}(n,{\mathbb{C}})^{2g}: \prod_{i=1}^g A_i^{-1}B_i^{-1}A_iB_i=\xi_n I\}/\operatorname{GL}(n,{\mathbb{C}}) \] and Simpson's nonabelian-Hodge diffeomorphism with the rank-$n$, degree-$d$ Dolbeault moduli. Over ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ the twist must exist, in particular with characteristic prime to $n$ and a suitable $n$-th root of unity in the field. Quotient means a moduli/GIT construction; orbit, coarse-point and stack-weighted counting must be distinguished.

The source's Frobenius--Schur character expression is \[ S_{n,g}(q)=\sum_{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(\operatorname{GL}(n,{\mathbb{F}}_q))} \frac{|\operatorname{GL}(n,{\mathbb{F}}_q)|^{2g-2}}{\chi(1)^{2g-1}} \chi(\xi_n I). \] It defines the ordinary mixed-Hodge polynomial $\mathcal H_n(x,y,t)=\sum_{p,q,k}h^{p,q;k}x^py^qt^k$ from Deligne's filtrations and labels as Theorem $6.17$ the equality of $\mathcal H_n(\sqrt q,\sqrt q,-1)$, the finite-field quotient count, and $S_{n,g}(q)$. This equality needs a counting audit: already for $n=1$, the coarse quotient count is $(q-1)^{2g}$, while the printed character sum is $(q-1)^{2g-1}$. The residual scalar-stabilizer factor separates coarse and weighted counts. Point-count/Hodge comparison also requires the appropriate arithmetic spread and support/normalization conventions; the printed equality is therefore recorded as a source theorem claim with an unresolved normalization issue, not silently corrected.

The same arithmetic passage introduces formal two-variable functions, distinct from $\mathcal H_n(x,y,t)$: \[ V_n(q,t)=\frac{H_n(q,t)(qt^2)^{(1-g)n(n-1)}}{(qt^2-1)(q-1)},\qquad Z_n(q,t,T)=\exp\!\left(\sum_{r\geq1} \frac{V_n(q^r,-(-t)^r)T^r}{r}\right), \] \[ H_\lambda^g(q,t)=\prod_{z\in d(\lambda)} \frac{(qt^2)^{(2-2g)\ell(z)} (1+q^{h(z)}t^{2\ell(z)+1})^{2g}} {(1-q^{h(z)}t^{2\ell(z)+2})(1-q^{h(z)}t^{2\ell(z)})}, \] \[ \prod_{n\geq1}Z_n(q,t,T^n) =\sum_{\lambda\in\mathcal P}H_\lambda^g(q,t)T^{|\lambda|}. \] Here $\mathcal P$ is the set of partitions and $d(\lambda)$ its Young diagram. These formulas retain the intended fraction layout of the damaged extraction. It calls $a(z),\ell(z),h(z)$ arm, leg and hook lengths and prints $h=a+\ell-1$. Under the usual convention excluding the box from arm and leg, the hook is instead $a+\ell+1$; inclusive conventions and the diagram must be checked before using these formulas. The excerpt ends before stating its promised subsequent conjectures, so no further Hodge-polynomial identity is invented.

Short English statement

The Bethe-residue conjecture is supplied only schematically. The aid reduces it to explicit global-residue and localization checks and treats colliding Bethe roots by total scheme residues. The merged arithmetic passage is retained with its character-count normalization discrepancy, hook-length ambiguity and missing later conjectures.
