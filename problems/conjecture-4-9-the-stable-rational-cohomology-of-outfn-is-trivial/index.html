Math-509

Conjecture 4.9 — The stable rational cohomology of OutFn is trivial.

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be the rank-$n$ free group and $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$. The conjecture is vanishing of stable reduced rational cohomology: \[\lim_{n\to\infty}\widetilde H^*(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);{\mathbb{Q}})=0.\] In the degreewise stable interpretation this means \[\forall k\geq1\ \exists N_k\ \forall n\geq N_k,\qquad H^k(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);{\mathbb{Q}})=0.\] It does not demand one rank threshold working simultaneously in every degree. Ordinary degree-zero cohomology remains ${\mathbb{Q}}$; reduced degree zero vanishes. There is no positive-degree exception, in particular none at degree one. Stable maps/ranges are those of the intended homological-stability framework, not an unstated natural inclusion $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Out}(F_{n+1})$.

The source then proposes studying cohomology with twisted coefficients. A precise version must specify coefficient modules $V_n$, the base ring and, if a stable question is intended, compatibility as $n$ varies, and ask for $H^k(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);V_n)$. The trivial-coefficient conjecture does not assert vanishing for arbitrary such modules or supply a particular family to compute.

The supplied summary attributes the stable rational result to Galatius's stable-homology theorem and marks it solved. Its tentative phrases "mostly," a possible degree-one exception and its informal sphere-space notation do not alter the exact all-positive-degree source assertion. The precise integral infinite-loop-space model is not reconstructed from that imprecise summary.

Short English statement

Stable rational cohomology should vanish in every positive degree, with only ordinary degree zero remaining. The record reports this solved by Galatius, while the separate twisted-coefficient programme needs specified modules.
