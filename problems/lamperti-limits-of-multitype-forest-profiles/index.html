Lamperti Limits of Multitype-Forest Profiles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A conventional generation profile can be defined as follows. Let $d\geq 1$, and let $F$ be a finite rooted forest with vertex types $\tau:V(F)\to\{1,\ldots,d\}$. For $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$, put \[ Z_F^{(i)}(k)=\#\{v\in V(F):\tau(v)=i,\ \operatorname{dist}(v,\text{root of its component})=k\},\qquad 1\leq i\leq d. \] The vector $Z_F(k)=(Z_F^{(1)}(k),\ldots,Z_F^{(d)}(k))$ is the type-resolved generation profile under this convention; in particular, generation zero consists of the roots. This definition supplies terminology, but the record does not establish that this is the precise profile convention intended by the source.

Supplied conjectural target. For uniform multitype forests with prescribed degree sequences, establish convergence of their profiles and characterize the limiting profile as a solution of a Lamperti transform.

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not specify a sequence of forest models, the meaning of a multitype degree sequence, the treatment of labels or child ordering, the root data, the number of types along the sequence, the asymptotic hypotheses, the profile normalization, or the mode and topology of convergence. It also supplies no defining Lamperti equation, driving processes, initial conditions, or boundary conventions. Consequently, the target above is a research objective rather than a complete quantified convergence conjecture. No particular scaling, limiting law, or Lamperti equation is asserted here.

The supplied original excerpt reads: "Finally, using our results, we conjecture the following. Population Dynamics and Statistical Physics in Synergy 631". It contains no mathematical formulation of the conjecture, so its omitted hypotheses and conclusion cannot be recovered from that excerpt.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether suitably rescaled generation profiles of uniform multitype forests with prescribed degrees converge to a limit described by a Lamperti transform. The necessary model, scaling, convergence hypotheses, and limiting equation are missing, so a precise theorem statement requires further source evidence and human mathematical review.
