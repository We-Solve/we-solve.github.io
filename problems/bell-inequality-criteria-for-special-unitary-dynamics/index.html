Bell Inequality Criteria for Special Unitary Dynamics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The specified dynamical group is \[ G=\mathrm{SU}(2)=\{U\in M_2({\mathbb{C}}):U^*U=I_2,\ \det U=1\}, \] where $U^*$ is the conjugate transpose. For a complex Hilbert space $\mathcal H$, a pure state is represented by a ray $[\psi]\in{\mathbb{P}}(\mathcal H)$, where $\psi\ne0$ and vectors differing by a nonzero complex scalar represent the same ray. The supplied record does not specify $\mathcal H$, a representation of $G$ on $\mathcal H$, or which rays are to be called classical or entangled.

Question as supplied. Does the Bell-inequality criterion for distinguishing classical from entangled pure states admit an analogue when the dynamical group is $G=\mathrm{SU}(2)$?

Unresolved formulation. The record does not identify the original Bell-inequality criterion, the permitted observables or measurement arrangements, the inequalities sought, or whether the desired analogue must be necessary, sufficient, or both. It also does not specify whether the question concerns a particular representation or a quantified family of representations of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$. Thus the displayed group does not by itself determine a precise criterion or its quantifiers. The supplied literature assessment additionally describes an unresolved geometric question about linear dependence of classical states, but gives neither that formulation nor its connection to the stated Bell-inequality question. These missing specifications prevent a unique precise mathematical reformulation.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks for an analogue, for $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ dynamics, of a Bell-inequality test distinguishing classical and entangled pure states. The state space, representation, meaning of classicality, and required test are unspecified. The record labels the problem open; this is supplied curation rather than an independently verified status.
