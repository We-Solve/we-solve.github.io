Iwasawa Main Conjecture for Dihedral Ring Class Towers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be an imaginary quadratic field. The supplied formulation takes $K/k$ to be a finite extension contained in a cyclotomic extension of $k$, and takes $F$ to be the maximal totally real subfield of $K$. The intended cyclotomic extension and any additional conditions on $K$ are not specified.

Let $E$ denote the elliptic curve appearing in the source record, and let $p\geq 5$ be a prime. Write $E[p]$ for its group of geometric $p$-torsion points, a two-dimensional ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector space with its Galois action. The record does not specify the field of definition of $E$ or the Galois group whose representation is intended. Let $N$, $\phi_F$, and the minimal modular degree denote the conductor or level, automorphic form, and modular degree intended by the source; their precise definitions and required modularity hypotheses are absent from the supplied extraction.

The stated hypotheses are:

  1. the root number of $L(E/k,s)$ is $+1$;
  2. the representation on $E[p]$ has image $\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p)$;
  3. $p$ does not divide the minimal modular degree;
  4. for every finite prime $v$ of $F$ satisfying \[ v^2\mid N\mathcal O_F \qquad\text{and}\qquad p\mid\left(\operatorname{N}(v)+1\right), \] the restriction of $E[p]$ to $I_v$ is irreducible. Here $\operatorname{N}(v)=\#(\mathcal O_F/v)$, and $I_v$ denotes an inertia group at $v$; the restriction presupposes a compatible Galois representation over $F$, which the extraction does not specify.

Write $K[p^\infty]$ for the source's dihedral ring-class tower and $J_K(\infty)$ for the group used in its Iwasawa algebra. Neither the tower nor this group is defined in the supplied record. For a profinite group $H$, the conventional ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-coefficient Iwasawa algebra is \[ {\mathbb{Z}}_p[[H]]:=\varprojlim_U{\mathbb{Z}}_p[H/U], \] where $U$ ranges over the open normal subgroups of $H$. The notation $\Lambda(J_K(\infty))$ is retained from the record, whose coefficient ring and treatment of finite torsion factors are unspecified.

The notation $X(E/L)$ conventionally denotes the Pontryagin dual of the relevant $p$-primary Selmer group: \[ X(E/L)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{cont}}\!\left(\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/L),{\mathbb{Q}}_p/{\mathbb{Z}}_p\right). \] This describes the intended type of object; the local conditions defining the Selmer group in this problem are not supplied. Likewise, $L_p(\phi_F,K[p^\infty])$ denotes the intended $p$-adic $L$-function, whose construction, normalization, and coefficient ring are not supplied. For an element $f$ of a commutative ring, $(f)$ denotes its principal ideal. The characteristic ideal below is understood only after the relevant Iwasawa algebra, module, and hypotheses ensuring that it is defined have been fixed.

Source-qualified target. With all these objects interpreted as in the cited report, the extracted main-conjecture question is whether \[ \left(L_p(\phi_F,K[p^\infty])\right) = \operatorname{char}_{\Lambda(J_K(\infty))} X(E/K[p^\infty]). \] The supplied correction reports that torsion of $X(E/K[p^\infty])$ over the intended Iwasawa algebra and the ideal inclusion \[ \left(L_p(\phi_F,K[p^\infty])\right) \subseteq \operatorname{char}_{\Lambda(J_K(\infty))} X(E/K[p^\infty]) \] are already known under the source's hypotheses. Thus the remaining target in that corrected formulation is the reverse ideal inclusion \[ \operatorname{char}_{\Lambda(J_K(\infty))} X(E/K[p^\infty]) \subseteq \left(L_p(\phi_F,K[p^\infty])\right). \] These reports of known results are supplied source qualifications, not independently verified assertions here. The earlier formulation instead asks for torsion of $X(E/k_\infty)$ over $\Lambda(G)$ and equality of its characteristic ideal with a $p$-adic $L$-function ideal. The record does not identify $k_\infty$ with $K[p^\infty]$, or $G$ with $J_K(\infty)$. Consequently, the displayed target preserves the corrected extraction but is not a fully specified standalone conjecture until the missing definitions and hypotheses are recovered.

Short English statement

Under the listed hypotheses, the corrected source asks for equality between a $p$-adic $L$-function ideal and the characteristic ideal of a Selmer dual over a dihedral ring-class tower. It reports torsion and one ideal inclusion as known, leaving the reverse inclusion as the target. Several essential definitions and hypotheses are missing, so this is a source-qualified statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
