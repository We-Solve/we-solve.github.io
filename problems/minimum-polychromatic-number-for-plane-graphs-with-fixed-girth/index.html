Math-5564

Minimum polychromatic number for plane graphs with fixed girth

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite plane graph with its embedding fixed, and let $V(F)$ be the set of vertices incident with a face $F$, including the outer face. A map $c:V(G)\to\{1,\ldots,k\}$ is polychromatic if \[ \{c(v):v\in V(F)\}=\{1,\ldots,k\}\quad\text{for every face }F. \] Adjacent vertices may have the same color. Define $p(G)$ as the largest positive integer $k$ for which such a coloring exists. Let $g(G)$ be the length of a shortest face, in the source's terminology, and set \[ p(g)=\min\{p(G):g(G)=g\}. \] This parameter concerns face length, not necessarily the length of the shortest graph cycle. The graph class and the treatment of repeated vertices or edges in face boundaries must be fixed: boundary-walk length with multiplicity and the cardinality $|V(F)|$ differ when a face boundary repeats vertices.

Problem. Determine $p(g)$ exactly for every positive integer $g$. The source's original first-open-case bound is $2\leq p(5)\leq4$. The supplied research summary reports the later refinement \[ 2\leq p(5)\leq3, \] so it leaves $p(5)\in\{2,3\}$ unresolved. It also reports \[ p(1)=p(2)=1,\qquad p(3)=p(4)=2, \] and, for $g\geq5$, \[ \left\lfloor\frac{3g-5}{4}\right\rfloor \leq p(g)\leq \left\lfloor\frac{3g+1}{4}\right\rfloor. \] The quantifier $g(G)=g$ is exact, not $g(G)\geq g$. The small cases and stated results must be interpreted in the source's graph and face-length conventions rather than silently imposed on a different restricted class.

Short English statement

Find the minimum possible number of colors that can appear on every face of a plane graph with prescribed shortest-face length. The supplied updated first case is $p(5)=2$ or $3$, and face length must be distinguished from graph girth.
