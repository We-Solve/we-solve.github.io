Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Conjecture 17 (Dynamical Andr{\'e}--Oort Conjecture)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a map $f$, let $f^0=\mathrm{id}$ and let $f^n$ denote its $n$-fold iterate for $n\geq 1$. The forward orbit of a point $c$ is \[ \mathcal O_f(c)=\{f^n(c):n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\}. \] A map is postcritically finite (PCF) if every critical point has finite forward orbit; equivalently, each critical point $c$ satisfies $f^m(c)=f^n(c)$ for some integers $0\leq m<n$.

The source uses $\mathcal M_d^{\mathrm{crit}}$ for a critically marked moduli space. A critical marking specifies critical points $c_i$ of the represented map. Equalities of the form \[ f^m(c_i)=f^n(c_j),\qquad m,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}, \] express relations between marked critical orbits. These equalities explain the terminology, but the excerpt does not define the full class of relations intended by its conclusion.

Supplied conjecture (Baker--DeMarco, approximately 2011; source Conjecture 17). Let $X\subseteq\mathcal M_d^{\mathrm{crit}}$ be an algebraic subvariety, and put \[ S_X=\{x\in X: x\text{ represents a PCF map}\}. \] The proposed implication is \[ \overline{S_X}^{\,\mathrm{Zar}}=X \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\text{ is cut out by ``critical orbit relations.''} \] This is asserted for every algebraic subvariety $X$ in the source's intended moduli space.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied excerpt does not specify the base field, the allowed values of $d$, the underlying class of maps, or the precise marking and equivalence conventions defining $\mathcal M_d^{\mathrm{crit}}$. It also does not define which critical orbit relations are allowed or whether "cut out" means a set-theoretic, scheme-theoretic, or componentwise characterization. Consequently, the displayed implication preserves the source formulation, but its conclusion cannot be made fully precise from this excerpt alone.

Short English statement

The supplied Dynamical Andr{é}--Oort conjecture predicts that an algebraic subvariety containing a Zariski dense set of postcritically finite maps is defined by relations among critical orbits. The intended moduli space and the exact meaning of those defining relations require clarification.
