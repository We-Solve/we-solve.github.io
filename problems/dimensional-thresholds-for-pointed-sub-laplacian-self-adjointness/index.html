Dimensional Thresholds for Pointed Sub-Laplacian Self-Adjointness

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns a three-dimensional equiregular sub-Riemannian structure locally modeled on the Heisenberg group and an operator obtained by deleting a point. Under the usual step-two interpretation, write this structure as $(M,H,g_H)$, where $M$ is a smooth manifold, $H\subset TM$ is a smooth distribution, and $g_H$ is an inner product on $H$, with \[ \dim M=3,\qquad \operatorname{rank}H=2,\qquad H+[H,H]=TM. \] Here $[H,H]$ denotes the directions generated by brackets of local horizontal vector fields. The corresponding homogeneous dimension is \[ Q=1\cdot\dim H+2\cdot\dim(TM/H)=2+2=4. \] The source does not specify whether being locally modeled on the Heisenberg group means local equivalence to its standard structure or only having its nilpotent approximation.

To specify the analytic question, one must additionally fix a positive smooth density $\mu$ on $M$ and a point $p\in M$. Given these data, define the horizontal gradient by \[ g_H(\nabla_H f,X)={\,\mathrm{d}} f(X)\qquad(X\in H), \] and define divergence by $\mathcal L_X\mu=(\operatorname{div}_{\mu}X)\mu$. A standard interpretation of the pointed sub-Laplacian is the densely defined symmetric operator \[ A_{p,\mu}f=-\operatorname{div}_{\mu}(\nabla_H f),\qquad \mathcal D(A_{p,\mu})=C_c^\infty(M\setminus\{p\}),\qquad A_{p,\mu}:\mathcal D(A_{p,\mu})\subset L^2(M,\mu)\longrightarrow L^2(M,\mu). \] This choice of density and initial domain is an explicit interpretation, not information supplied by the record. Essential self-adjointness means that the operator closure is self-adjoint, equivalently \[ \overline{A_{p,\mu}}=A_{p,\mu}^{*}, \] or that both deficiency spaces $\ker(A_{p,\mu}^{*}-iI)$ and $\ker(A_{p,\mu}^{*}+iI)$ vanish.

Requested mathematical target. Determine whether the topological dimension $3$, horizontal dimension $2$, or homogeneous dimension $4$ governs essential self-adjointness of the pointed sub-Laplacian in the stated Heisenberg-type setting. The source supplies neither a definition of "governs" nor a quantified threshold criterion. Since all three dimensions are fixed in this setting, a self-adjointness determination alone does not specify a general dimensional criterion. Moreover, no density, completeness assumption, boundary conditions, or control of other ends or singularities is stated. Consequently, the record does not determine a unique global operator problem or a precise universal assertion without further qualifications.

The supplied literature assessment reports essential self-adjointness after deletion of a point in the three-dimensional genuine sub-Riemannian case, including the Heisenberg example, while reporting that no general criterion identifies the relevant dimension for all pointed sub-Laplacians. These are supplied dated curation claims; their theorem hypotheses and present validity have not been independently verified here.

Short English statement

The problem asks which notion of dimension controls uniqueness of the self-adjoint realization of a sub-Laplacian after removing a point from a three-dimensional Heisenberg-type space. The candidate dimensions are $3$, $2$, and $4$. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved, but leaves the operator data and the meaning of a dimensional criterion unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
