Math-3370

Extending Character-Field Bounds Beyond p-Solvable Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $G$ be a finite group, and let $P$ be a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $G$. Write $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ for the irreducible complex characters of $G$. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, put \[ {\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(g):g\in G\right),\qquad \chi_P=\operatorname{Res}^{G}_{P}\chi. \] Under the cyclotomic interpretation of the supplied notation, let ${\mathbb{Q}}_n={\mathbb{Q}}(\zeta_n)$, where $\zeta_n$ is a primitive $n$-th root of unity, and let the conductor of $\chi$ mean the least positive integer $n$ such that ${\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}}_n$. Write this conductor as $p^a m$, with $a\geq0$, $m\geq1$, and $p\nmid m$. Define \[ {\mathbb{Q}}_p(\chi_P)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\zeta_p,\chi(x):x\in P\right), \qquad \chi(1)_p=p^{v_p(\chi(1))}, \] where $v_p(d)$ is the largest nonnegative integer $b$ such that $p^b\mid d$.

Question, with an unresolved boundary qualification. For every such $p,G,P,\chi$ with $a\geq1$, does the following inequality hold, without any assumption that $G$ is $p$-solvable? \[ \left[{\mathbb{Q}}_{p^a}:{\mathbb{Q}}_p(\chi_P)\right]\leq\chi(1)_p. \] Here the bracket denotes the degree of a field extension. With the cyclotomic interpretation above and $a\geq1$, the fields have the inclusion needed to form this degree. A finite group is $p$-solvable if it has a finite normal series whose factors each have either $p$-power order or order relatively prime to $p$.

The supplied statement does not explicitly require $a\geq1$. When $a=0$, its upper field is ${\mathbb{Q}}_1={\mathbb{Q}}$, whereas its lower field contains ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$; for odd $p$, the displayed expression therefore does not define an extension degree. The intended treatment of this boundary case is missing. The positive-exponent formulation above must consequently be reviewed against the intended full question. The cyclotomic notation and conductor convention are also interpretations requiring confirmation from the report.

The original excerpt separately asks whether "the theorem above" extends to non-$p$-solvable groups, but does not supply that theorem. Its full extension question cannot be stated precisely from this record. The excerpt also contains an incomplete discussion of the linear constituents of an unspecified character $\psi$, grouped by their orders; it does not provide enough context to turn that discussion into an additional assertion or hypothesis.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the degree of a cyclotomic field over the field generated by the restricted character values and a primitive $p$-th root of unity is at most the $p$-part of the character degree, for every finite group. The supplied formulation requires clarification when the conductor is prime to $p$, and its separate reference to an earlier theorem is incomplete. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
