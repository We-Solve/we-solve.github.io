Math-911

Carlsson’s Homology Bound for Free Group Actions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $G$ be an elementary abelian $p$-group of rank $d$, meaning that $d$ is a nonnegative integer and \[ G\cong ({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})^d. \] Let $X$ be a finite CW complex equipped with a continuous action of $G$. The action is free if, for every $x\in X$ and $g\in G$, the equality $g\cdot x=x$ implies that $g$ is the identity element. For a coefficient field $k$, write $H_n(X;k)$ for ordinary singular homology and define the total Betti number by \[ b_k(X):=\sum_{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}}\dim_k H_n(X;k). \] This sum is finite because $X$ is a finite CW complex.

The supplied formulation of the conjecture attributed to Gunnar Carlsson asks whether, for every prime $p$, every rank $d$, every such group $G$, and every finite CW complex $X$ described by the source as "non-trivial," \[ G\text{ acts freely on }X \quad\Longrightarrow\quad b_k(X)\geq 2^d. \] The coefficient-field hypothesis and the meaning of "non-trivial" remain unresolved in the supplied record. In particular, the record does not specify the characteristic of $k$ or its relationship to $p$. The displayed implication therefore records the intended statement template, rather than asserting a conjecture quantified over all coefficient fields. No connectedness or cellularity hypothesis is supplied, and none is added here.

Short English statement

The question attributed to Gunnar Carlsson asks whether a free action of an elementary abelian $p$-group of rank $d$ on a nontrivial finite CW complex forces its total homology dimension over $k$ to be at least $2^d$. The supplied record leaves the coefficient-field condition and the meaning of "nontrivial" unspecified, so these require clarification before the statement is mathematically definitive.
