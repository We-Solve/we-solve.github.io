Math-14639

Uniform Long-Time Sobolev Bounds for Nonlinear Beam Equations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/(2\pi{\mathbb{Z}})$ be the spatial circle, and let $m\in[1,2]$. The supplied record displays the nonlinear beam equation \[ \partial_t^2\psi(x,t)+\partial_x^4\psi(x,t)+m\psi(x,t)+f(\psi(x,t))=0, \qquad x\in\mathbb T, \] where $\psi=\psi(x,t)$, the potential $F$ is real analytic in a neighborhood of the origin, $F(0)=0$, and \[ f(\psi):=\partial_\psi F(\psi). \] The torus specifies periodicity in the spatial variable. The record does not specify a time interval, initial data, a solution class, or the scalar field of the unknown. No further vanishing conditions on derivatives of $F$ are supplied.

The recorded question is literally \[ \text{Can one prove } f(t)=c\varepsilon \text{ for some absolute constant }c\,? \] Here the time-dependent quantity denoted by $f(t)$ is undefined and must not be identified without justification with the nonlinearity $f(\psi)$. The parameter $\varepsilon$ is also undefined. The title and supplied curation describe an intended question about uniform long-time Sobolev bounds, but neither the Sobolev norm nor its order, the initial smallness hypothesis, the time scale, the permitted dependence of $c$, or the relevant quantifiers over $m$, $F$, and solutions are recoverable from the statement. In particular, the displayed equality cannot silently be replaced by an inequality.

The record ends with an incomplete fragment mentioning the beam equation after a convenient linear symplectic change of variables. Neither that transformation nor the transformed variables are supplied. Thus the equation and the literal question above preserve the recoverable content; they do not constitute a fully specified Sobolev stability conjecture.

The supplied registry labels the statement unrecoverable and the literature status partially_solved. Its dated assessment attributes to Feola and Massetti a uniform $O(\varepsilon)$ Sobolev bound with constant $8$, on a long sub-exponential time interval and for almost every mass parameter, while saying that the unrestricted absolute-constant bound requested in the record is not established. A separate supplied verification note says that the question is immediately followed by a stability theorem providing a constant multiple of the initial norm on its stated time scale. These are supplied descriptions with missing theorem hypotheses and time-scale formulas, not independently verified mathematical assertions or a complete formulation of the target.

Short English statement

The source asks for a uniform small Sobolev bound for a nonlinear beam equation on the circle, with mass $m\in[1,2]$ and an analytic potential. Its literal formula $f(t)=c\varepsilon$ leaves the measured quantity, smallness parameter, time interval, and quantifiers undefined. The supplied dated curation reports partial progress, but the intended precise statement remains unrecoverable and requires human mathematical review.
