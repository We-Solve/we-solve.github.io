Polynomial Bounds for Dominant Rational-Curve Components

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source leaves the ground field unspecified. The following formulation is conditional on specifying an algebraically closed field $k$; the intended field and any characteristic restriction remain unresolved.

Let $X$ be a smooth projective integral variety over $k$ whose anticanonical divisor $-K_X$ is ample, so that $X$ is Fano. Fix an ample Cartier divisor $L$ on $X$. Let $\operatorname{Mor}_k({\mathbb{P}}^1_k,X)$ denote the scheme parametrizing morphisms $f\colon{\mathbb{P}}^1_k\to X$. Define the $L$-degree of such a morphism by \[ \deg_L(f)=\deg_{{\mathbb{P}}^1_k}\left(f^*\mathcal O_X(L)\right). \] This degree is constant on each irreducible component $M$ of the morphism scheme; write it as $\deg_L(M)$. Interpret a dominant component as an irreducible component $M$ for which the evaluation morphism \[ \operatorname{ev}_M\colon{\mathbb{P}}^1_k\times M\longrightarrow X, \qquad (t,f)\longmapsto f(t), \] has Zariski dense image. This interpretation concerns the family of morphisms parametrized by $M$, and requires confirmation against the source's intended terminology.

For each integer $d\geq 0$, set \[ N_{X,L}(d)=\#\left\{M\subseteq\operatorname{Mor}_k({\mathbb{P}}^1_k,X):\ M\text{ is an irreducible component},\ \operatorname{ev}_M\text{ is dominant},\ \deg_L(M)\leq d\right\}. \] The question is whether, for every fixed pair $(X,L)$ over the intended ground field, there exist a real constant $C>0$ and an integer $r\geq 0$, independent of $d$, such that \[ N_{X,L}(d)\leq C(1+d)^r \qquad\text{for every integer }d\geq 0. \] The constants may depend on $X$, $L$, and the ground field; the supplied statement does not request uniformity across varieties or polarizations. The full morphism scheme is counted, including multiple covers and degree-zero morphisms, since the supplied statement makes no exclusion. The Cartier interpretation of the divisor and the meaning of dominant component are explicit conventions for this draft, subject to review.

Short English statement

For a fixed smooth projective Fano variety $X$ and ample divisor $L$, count the irreducible components of the scheme of parametrized rational curves whose families cover a dense subset of $X$ and whose $L$-degree is at most $d$. Must this count grow at most polynomially in $d$? The ground field and the precise meaning of dominant component need confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; that label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
