Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 48

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor is a von Neumann algebra with center ${\mathbb{C}} 1$, no nonzero minimal projections, and a faithful normal tracial state $\tau_A$ normalized by $\tau_A(1)=1$. Interpret separability as separability of the predual. Write $\|x\|_{2,A}=\tau_A(x^*x)^{1/2}$, and let $\overline{\otimes}$ denote the spatial von Neumann algebra tensor product.

The source uses a common ultrafilter $\mathcal U$ without specifying its index set or whether it is nonprincipal. For a fixed index set $I$ and ultrafilter $\mathcal U$ on $I$, the tracial ultrapower notation means \[ A^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(I,A)\big/\{(a_j)_{j\in I}:\lim_{j\to\mathcal U}\|a_j\|_{2,A}=0\}, \] where $\ell^\infty(I,A)$ consists of operator-norm bounded families. Write $A\equiv B$ if $A$ and $B$ have the same values for every sentence in the continuous first-order language of tracial von Neumann algebras.

For the common $\mathcal U$ intended by the source, is the following implication true for every four separable $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors $N_1,N_2,M_1,M_2$? \[ N_1^{\mathcal U}\overline{\otimes}N_2^{\mathcal U} \cong M_1^{\mathcal U}\overline{\otimes}M_2^{\mathcal U} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists\sigma\in S_2\ \forall i\in\{1,2\},\quad N_i\equiv M_{\sigma(i)}. \] Here $\cong$ denotes an isomorphism of von Neumann algebras and $S_2$ is the permutation group of $\{1,2\}$. The ultrapowers are taken separately before forming the tensor products. The intended restrictions on $\mathcal U$, and the scope of its quantification, remain unspecified in the supplied record.

Short English statement

If tensor products of the respective tracial ultrapowers of two pairs of separable $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors are isomorphic, must the original factors have matching elementary theories after possibly interchanging one pair? The source labels this question open; its intended ultrafilter assumptions require clarification.
