Asymptotics of Expanding Vacuum Spacetimes Near Singularities

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A globally hyperbolic spacetime has Cauchy hypersurfaces crossed once by every inextendible causal curve. Vacuum means zero matter stress tensor. A constant-mean-curvature (CMC) foliation uses spatial slices on which the trace of their second fundamental form is spatially constant; its sign convention and monotone time parameter describe expansion. Compact Cauchy slices describe a spatially closed cosmological model. Past-singularity asymptotics and future expanding behavior are distinct temporal limits.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For a globally hyperbolic expanding vacuum spacetime with compact Cauchy hypersurfaces and a CMC foliation, what is the asymptotic behavior as the past singularity is approached, and what is the future behavior of the spacetime?

The report discusses the $3+1$-dimensional setting, with some results extending further. No particular rescaled limit, topology or genericity condition is fixed by the extraction; both temporal directions remain in the research target.

Short English statement

An exact compact Kasner benchmark.
On $(0,\infty)\times\mathbb T^3$, take $g=-dt^2+\sum_{i=1}^3t^{2p_i}dx_i^2$ with $\sum p_i=\sum p_i^2=1$. Direct Christoffel differentiation gives $R_{00}=(\sum p_i-\sum p_i^2)/t^2=0$ and $R_{ii}=p_i(\sum p_j-1)t^{2p_i-2}=0$, with off-diagonal Ricci entries zero. Thus the metric is vacuum. In the convention $k=-\frac12\partial_tg_{\rm spatial}$, the mean curvature is $-1/t$, and the compact slice volume is its unit-coordinate volume times $t$. Its orthonormal curvature components give \[ |\operatorname{Riem}|_{\rm Lorentz}^2 =\frac4{t^4}\left[\sum_i p_i^2(p_i-1)^2+\sum_{i<j}p_i^2p_j^2\right], \] where the left side denotes the full curvature contraction in this diagonal metric. The bracket is positive except for permutations of $(1,0,0)$.
Two temporal limits.
Generic members of this family therefore have curvature blowup as $t\downarrow0$ and curvature decay as $t\to\infty$. The exceptional flat triple also shows that the CMC parameter alone does not certify curvature blowup.
