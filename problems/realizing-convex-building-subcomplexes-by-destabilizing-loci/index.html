Realizing Convex Building Subcomplexes by Destabilizing Loci

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be the building in the supplied question, and let $Y\subseteq X$ be a convex contractible subcomplex. Contractibility means that the geometric realization $|Y|$ admits a homotopy from its identity map to a constant map. The supplied record does not specify the class of buildings, the geometry used for convexity, or the group $G$ and its relationship to $X$.

Read stabilization as setwise stabilization. For the intended automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(X)$, define \[ \operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{Aut}(X)}(Y) =\{h\in\operatorname{Aut}(X):h(Y)=Y\}. \] The question is whether, for every such $Y$, there exist a variety $V$, an action $\alpha:G\times V\to V$, and a point $v\in V$ such that \[ C_0(v)\subseteq Y \quad\text{and}\quad h\left(C_0(v)\right)=C_0(v) \quad\text{for every }h\in\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{Aut}(X)}(Y). \] Here $C_0(v)$ is retained as an undefined source symbol: the record supplies no construction of this subset of $X$, no definition of the admissible actions, and no base field or hypotheses on $G$ or $V$. It also does not state whether $C_0(v)$ must be nonempty. These omissions prevent a fully specified mathematical formulation; no definition of a destabilizing locus is being inferred.

The requested relation is containment $C_0(v)\subseteq Y$, together with invariance under the stabilizer of $Y$. The supplied literature assessment instead discusses realizing every such $Y$ as $C_0(v)$, which suggests equality. These formulations are not interchangeable, and the containment formulation above follows the supplied original and cleaned statements.

Short English statement

For a convex contractible subcomplex $Y$ of a building $X$, ask whether an action of the intended group $G$ on some variety and a point $v$ can produce a subset $C_0(v)\subseteq Y$ preserved by every automorphism preserving $Y$. The record leaves $G$, $C_0(v)$, and the admissible geometric and algebraic settings undefined, so this is a statement-only draft requiring clarification and human mathematical review.
