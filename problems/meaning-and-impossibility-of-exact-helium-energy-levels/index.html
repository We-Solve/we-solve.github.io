Meaning and Impossibility of Exact Helium Energy Levels

Definitions and notation.
Energy levels are spectral values of the quantum Hamiltonian. For a nonrelativistic helium atom with fixed nucleus, the Hamiltonian contains kinetic energies for two electrons, two electron--nucleus Coulomb attractions and the electron--electron repulsion. Hydrogen’s one-electron Coulomb model supplies the comparison. The source asks first for a precise meaning of "exact determination"; it does not prescribe a function class or algorithmic model.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Give a mathematically precise meaning to determining the energy levels of the helium atom exactly, in the sense that hydrogen energy levels are exact, and prove or disprove that such exact determination is impossible.
Scope and source qualification.
The fixed-nucleus nonrelativistic model explains the terminology without silently resolving the source’s intended model or admissible exact expressions. The definition and the proposed impossibility theorem are both part of the target.

Short English statement

Make exact determination of helium energy levels precise, then prove or refute that such determination is impossible.

A variational benchmark, not an exact-expression theorem.
In atomic units choose the fixed-nucleus Hamiltonian \[H_Z=-\tfrac12(\Delta_{x_1}+\Delta_{x_2})-Z/|x_1|-Z/|x_2|+1/|x_1-x_2|\] with $Z=2$. Take the spatially symmetric product $\Psi_a(x_1,x_2)=\phi_a(x_1)\phi_a(x_2)$, $\phi_a(x)=(a^3/\pi)^{1/2}e^{-a|x|}$, $a>0$; combine it with an antisymmetric spin singlet. Radial integration gives $\langle-\Delta/2\rangle=a^2/2$ and $\langle1/r\rangle=a$. The electron repulsion is \[16a^6\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^\infty\frac{r^2s^2e^{-2a(r+s)}}{\max(r,s)}\,dr\,ds=5a/8. \] Here the angular average of $|x-y|^{-1}$ is $1/\max(r,s)$; it follows by integrating $(r^2+s^2-2rsu)^{-1/2}$ over $-1<u<1$. Splitting the radial integral at $r=s$, and using $\int_0^\infty r^ne^{-cr}dr=n!/c^{n+1}$, evaluates it as stated. Therefore \[\langle H_Z\rangle=a^2-(2Z-5/8)a,\qquad E_0(Z)\le-(Z-5/16)^2\] when $Z>5/16$, by choosing $a=Z-5/16$. For helium this upper bound is $-729/256$.
An exact expression for a bound is not an exact level.
The rational expression above is only a Rayleigh quotient minimum within a one-parameter family. Equality with the true energy would require this product to solve the full eigenvalue equation. Away from coincidence and the nucleus, dividing $H_Z\Psi_a$ by $\Psi_a$ leaves the nonconstant term $1/|x_1-x_2|$, which cannot be canceled by functions of the individual radii alone. Choosing equal individual radii and varying their mutual angle proves nonconstancy directly. Thus this trial family cannot produce an exact helium eigenfunction.
