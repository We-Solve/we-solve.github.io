Math-1320

Tangent spaces and prescribed-jet smoothings on the monogenic chart

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $n,d\ge1$, and $R=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$. Let $H=\operatorname{Hilb}^d(\mathbb A_k^n)$ and let $H_{\mathrm{sm}}$ be the reduced closure of its locus of $d$ distinct points, the smoothable component. At a length-$d$ ideal $I$, the whole-Hilbert tangent space is $\operatorname{Hom}_R(I,R/I)$. The original asks to describe $T_{[I]}H_{\mathrm{sm}}$ for smoothable $I$, rather than assuming it equals this ambient tangent space at every point. The original does not specify the base field; the attached aid works in arbitrary characteristic over algebraically closed fields.

The aid treats the monogenic chart on which $1,x_1,\ldots,x_1^{d-1}$ is a basis of $R/I$. Its ideals have the form \[ I=(f(x_1),x_2-p_2(x_1),\ldots,x_n-p_n(x_1)), \] where $f$ is monic of degree $d$ and $\deg p_i<d$. Their coefficient vectors form $\mathbb A_k^{nd}$. The chart lies in $H_{\mathrm{sm}}$, and at every point on it the aid gives \[ T_{[I]}H_{\mathrm{sm}}=T_{[I]}H=\operatorname{Hom}_R(I,R/I)\simeq(R/I)^n, \qquad\dim_k T_{[I]}H_{\mathrm{sm}}=nd. \] The displayed isomorphism uses the chosen generators or coefficient coordinates.

More precisely, for a coefficient vector $q$ and any tangent vector $v\in k^{nd}$, choose a vector $q_*$ with squarefree first polynomial and set \[ \gamma(s)=q+s v+s^2(q_*-q-v). \] The aid states that this is a polynomial flat smoothing family in the chart, with $\gamma(0)=q$, $\gamma'(0)=v$, and generic fiber $d$ geometrically distinct points; indeed $\gamma(1)=q_*$ ensures the discriminant is not identically zero. Thus every chart tangent vector is realized by a smoothing with its exact prescribed first jet, in every characteristic. After a coordinate change the chart contains every curvilinear length-$d$ scheme, meaning each local component has embedding dimension at most one. The source supplies no corresponding description for all noncurvilinear points of the smoothable component.

Short English statement

Describe tangents to the smoothable Hilbert component. The supplied aid gives the full answer on the monogenic chart, including curvilinear schemes, and realizes every tangent there by an exact-first-jet smoothing. It does not identify all tangents at arbitrary smoothable points.
