Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 13.5

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a commutative ring $R$ with identity, let \[ \operatorname{SL}_2(R)=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}:a,b,c,d\in R,\ ad-bc=1\right\}. \] A group is finitely generated if some finite subset generates it as an abstract group, allowing products and inverses. Write \[ R_1={\mathbb{Z}}[X,X^{-1}],\qquad R_{2,F}=F[X,X^{-1},Y,Y^{-1}], \] where $X,Y$ are commuting indeterminates and $F$ is a field. These Laurent polynomial rings consist of finite sums with integral, respectively $F$-valued, coefficients and arbitrary integer exponents.

The source asks:

  1. Is $\operatorname{SL}_2(R_1)$ finitely generated?
  2. For a field $F$, is $\operatorname{SL}_2(R_{2,F})$ finitely generated? The family question must retain its dependence on $F$, rather than silently restrict the field.
Equivalently, each individual finite-generation question asks whether there exist $r\ge0$ and matrices $A_1,\ldots,A_r$ in the indicated group such that every group element is a finite product of the $A_i^{\pm1}$.

The supplied August 2026 research summary reports that the first question, and the second question for $F={\mathbb{F}}_q$ with $q$ a prime power, remain open. It separately claims a negative answer for every infinite field $F$, by a finitely-generated-ring argument referred to as "Work done", Section 1, which is not included. Accordingly the finite-field case is the remaining open family in that supplied assessment, while the original statement itself includes all fields. No verification or new proof of the infinite-field claim is supplied here.

The summary also mentions reductions associated with Cohn, Suslin, Bachmuth--Mochizuki, and Chu, as well as non-finite-presentation and homological obstructions. These are different from non-finite-generation and do not by themselves answer the questions. Their exact groups, coefficient systems, and hypotheses are missing. Its elementary-generation notation $\operatorname{GE}_2$ is undefined in the record; the displayed implication "$\operatorname{GE}_2\Rightarrow$ finitely generated" is retained only as a reported proposed connection in these settings, not as a supplied equivalence or a criterion proved here.

Short English statement

Are the determinant-one two-by-two matrix groups over the one-variable integral and two-variable field Laurent polynomial rings finitely generated? The supplied August 2026 summary leaves the integral ring and all finite-field cases open, while claiming infinite fields give negative answers. The cited obstruction and elementary-generation results are incomplete and do not replace a finite-generation answer.
