Math-3875

Settled Frobenius Elements in Arboreal Representations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a number field, let $\overline K$ be an algebraic closure, let $f\in K[x]$ have degree $d\geq 2$, and let $\alpha\in K$. Write $f^{\circ n}$ for the $n$-fold iterate, with $f^{\circ 0}(x)=x$. The preimage tree has level \[ V_n=\{(n,\beta):\beta\in\overline K,\ f^{\circ n}(\beta)=\alpha\}, \] with an edge from $(n+1,\beta)$ to $(n,f(\beta))$. Level labels distinguish repeated algebraic values occurring at different depths.

To identify this tree with the regular rooted $d$-ary tree $T_d$, one needs every polynomial $f^{\circ n}(x)-\alpha$, $n\geq1$, to have $d^n$ distinct roots. In characteristic zero this is equivalent to requiring \[ \alpha\neq f^{\circ m}(c) \quad\text{for every }c\in\overline K\text{ with }f'(c)=0 \text{ and every }m\geq1. \] This regularity hypothesis is absent from the supplied statement; it must be confirmed or an alternative tree construction specified.

Assuming regularity, the action on preimages gives the arboreal representation \[ \rho_{f,\alpha}:\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K/K)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(T_d), \qquad G_{f,\alpha}=\operatorname{im}(\rho_{f,\alpha}). \] Equip this group with the topology induced by its actions on finite levels. Equivalently, if $K_n$ is the splitting field of $f^{\circ n}(x)-\alpha$ and $L=\bigcup_{n\geq1}K_n$, then $G_{f,\alpha}\cong\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$.

For $g\in\operatorname{Aut}(T_d)$, a cycle $C$ of its permutation of $V_n$ is called stable if, for every $m>n$, all descendants of vertices in $C$ at level $m$ form one cycle of length $|C|d^{m-n}$. Set \[ s_n(g)=\frac{\#\{v\in V_n:v\text{ belongs to a stable cycle of }g\}}{d^n}. \] The automorphism $g$ is settled if $\lim_{n\to\infty}s_n(g)=1$. Write $S(G)=\{g\in G:g\text{ is settled}\}$.

At a finite prime $\mathfrak p$ of $K$ unramified in $L/K$, arithmetic Frobenius is defined, up to conjugacy in $G_{f,\alpha}$, by its compatible finite-level actions inducing $x\mapsto x^{\mathrm N\mathfrak p}$ on residue fields. Here $\mathrm N\mathfrak p=\#(\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p)$. Settledness is invariant under conjugation. At a ramified prime, a Frobenius element in a decomposition group modulo inertia does not specify a unique element of $G_{f,\alpha}$.

Supplied conjectural target, with unresolved qualifications. The record requests settledness of all intended Frobenius images and density of settled elements. If $\mathcal F_{f,\alpha}\subseteq G_{f,\alpha}$ denotes the intended collection of Frobenius images, these conclusions are \[ \mathcal F_{f,\alpha}\subseteq S(G_{f,\alpha}), \qquad \overline{S(G_{f,\alpha})}=G_{f,\alpha}. \] The second conclusion means explicitly that \[ \forall n\geq0\ \forall h\in G_{f,\alpha}\ \exists g\in S(G_{f,\alpha}) \quad g|_{V_n}=h|_{V_n}. \] The supplied wording quantifies over all number fields $K$, all polynomials $f\in K[x]$ of degree at least two, and all $\alpha\in K$, but does not supply the regularity restriction or specify the primes and ramification conventions defining $\mathcal F_{f,\alpha}$. Thus it does not determine a complete quantified Frobenius conjecture. The asserted implication introduced by "hence" also requires justification that the intended Frobenius images are dense; that premise is not supplied. These qualifications remain unresolved, and the displayed target is a statement-only reconstruction requiring mathematical review.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether Frobenius images in polynomial arboreal Galois groups are settled: at increasingly deep levels, almost every vertex should lie in a cycle that continues as one cycle through every deeper level. It also asks whether settled elements are dense. The regularity of the preimage tree and the intended primes and Frobenius conventions require clarification. The supplied status is partially solved, according to dated curation rather than independent verification.
