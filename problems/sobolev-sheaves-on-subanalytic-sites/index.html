Sobolev Sheaves on Subanalytic Sites

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for a complex of Sobolev sheaves on the subanalytic site of a manifold $M$. In the usual interpretation, $M$ is a real analytic manifold and $M_{\mathrm{sa}}$ has relatively compact subanalytic open subsets as objects, with finite open coverings. The supplied record does not specify the analytic structure, boundary conventions for $M$, or precise site convention; these must be fixed before the question is fully determined.

Write $H^s_{\mathrm{Sob}}(U)$ for the ordinary Sobolev space of regularity $s$ on $U$, separating this notation from the sought sheaf complex $\mathcal S^s$. A relatively compact open subset satisfies $\overline U\subset M$ compact. A Lipschitz open subset is understood to have boundary locally representable as a Lipschitz graph in suitable coordinates. The source does not specify the Sobolev convention, scalar field, auxiliary geometric data, or whether any boundary conditions are intended; none are imposed here.

Existence question. For a manifold $M$ in the intended class and a real regularity parameter $s$, does there exist a complex of sheaves $\mathcal S^s$ on $M_{\mathrm{sa}}$ whose sections on every relatively compact Lipschitz open subset $U\subset M$ are concentrated in degree zero and recover $H^s_{\mathrm{Sob}}(U)$? In particular, the source asks whether such complexes exist throughout the range $s\geq\tfrac12$.

If sections are intended as derived sections, the requested property is \[ R\Gamma(U;\mathcal S^s)\simeq H^s_{\mathrm{Sob}}(U)[0], \] where $[0]$ places the Sobolev space in degree zero. Equivalently, \[ H^j\!\left(R\Gamma(U;\mathcal S^s)\right)\cong \begin{cases} H^s_{\mathrm{Sob}}(U),&j=0,\\ 0,&j\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}. \end{cases} \] This is a conditional interpretation: the supplied wording does not distinguish derived sections from the complex obtained by taking sections term by term, nor does it specify whether the identifications must respect restriction maps or Sobolev topologies.

Unresolved scope. A relatively compact Lipschitz open subset need not be subanalytic. Consequently, the source's quantifier over every such $U$ requires either a specified extension of evaluation beyond the objects of $M_{\mathrm{sa}}$, or a correction restricting $U$ to subanalytic Lipschitz opens. Neither choice is made here. The record also leaves the class and dimension of $M$, the exact quantification over $s$, and the category and boundedness of the complex unspecified. These qualifications prevent an unconditional precise formulation from being extracted from the record alone.

Short English statement

The question asks whether ordinary Sobolev spaces can be recovered, with no cohomology in other degrees, from a complex of sheaves on a subanalytic site, especially for every $s\geq\tfrac12$. The intended manifold class, Sobolev convention, meaning of sections, and evaluation on Lipschitz opens that are not subanalytic remain unresolved. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
