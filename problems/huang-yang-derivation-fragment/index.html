The model description concerns spin-$\tfrac12$ fermions with spin populations $N_{\uparrow}$ and $N_{\downarrow}$, total particle number \[ N=N_{\uparrow}+N_{\downarrow}, \] and spatial box \[ \Lambda=[-L/2,L/2]^3\subset{\mathbb{R}}^3 \] with periodic boundary conditions.
