Math-871

Bestvina–Davis Candidates for the Eilenberg–Ganea Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, define its integral cohomological dimension by \[ \operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(G)=\operatorname{pd}_{{\mathbb{Z}} G}{\mathbb{Z}}, \] where ${\mathbb{Z}}$ is the trivial ${\mathbb{Z}} G$-module. Its geometric dimension $\operatorname{gd}(G)$ is the least dimension of a CW complex $K(G,1)$, meaning a connected CW complex with fundamental group $G$ and contractible universal cover. The Eilenberg--Ganea conjecture in the case at issue asserts that $\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(G)=2$ implies $\operatorname{gd}(G)=2$.

A simplicial complex is flag if every finite set of pairwise adjacent vertices spans a simplex. For a finite flag complex $L$, let $V(L)$ and $E(L)$ denote its vertex and edge sets, and define the associated right-angled Coxeter group by \[ W_L=\left\langle s_v\ (v\in V(L))\ \middle|\ s_v^2=1\ (v\in V(L)),\quad s_vs_w=s_ws_v\ (\{v,w\}\in E(L))\right\rangle. \] A subgroup $H\leq W_L$ has finite index if $[W_L:H]<\infty$, and is torsion-free if its identity is its only element of finite order.

For a specified coefficient ring $A$, a nonempty complex $L$ is acyclic over $A$ if $\widetilde H_k(L;A)=0$ for every integer $k\geq 0$. The supplied record does not specify the coefficients intended by "acyclic." A unitary representation of $\pi_1(L)$ is a homomorphism into the unitary group of a complex Hilbert space; it is nontrivial if some group element acts other than as the identity. The record does not specify whether the representation must be finite-dimensional.

Supplied question, subject to these unresolved conventions. Let $L$ be a finite acyclic flag complex of dimension exactly two whose fundamental group admits a nontrivial unitary representation. Let $H\leq W_L$ be any torsion-free subgroup of finite index. Taking \[ \operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(H)=2 \] as the premise asserted in the supplied record, must every such $L$ and $H$ satisfy \[ \operatorname{gd}(H)=3? \] An affirmative answer would make these groups counterexamples to the stated Eilenberg--Ganea conjecture. The original wording restricts attention to groups arising from the Bestvina--Davis construction; the supplied clean formulation describes them using the hypotheses above. Whether that description includes all necessary construction hypotheses requires source review. No coefficient convention, representation dimension, or additional construction hypothesis is resolved by this draft.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every torsion-free finite-index subgroup in the stated Bestvina--Davis family has geometric dimension three despite having integral cohomological dimension two. The meaning of acyclicity, the allowed dimension of the unitary representation, and the completeness of the construction hypotheses require review.
