Math-3512

Metric Mean Dimension for Amenable Group Actions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a countable amenable group, understood with the discrete topology, acting continuously on a compact metrizable space $X$. Write $gx$ for the action and $\mathcal M(X)$ for the set of metrics inducing the topology of $X$. The following definitions apply to nonempty $X$; the supplied record does not specify conventions for the empty space.

Choose a Følner sequence $(F_n)_{n\geq 1}$ of nonempty finite subsets of $G$, so that \[ \frac{|gF_n\mathbin{\triangle}F_n|}{|F_n|}\longrightarrow 0 \qquad\text{for every }g\in G. \] The source does not require $G$ to be infinite, and no such restriction is imposed here.

For a finite open cover $\mathcal U$ of $X$, put \[ \operatorname{ord}(\mathcal U) =\max_{x\in X}\left(\sum_{U\in\mathcal U}\mathbf 1_U(x)-1\right), \qquad D(\mathcal U)=\min_{\mathcal V\succcurlyeq\mathcal U}\operatorname{ord}(\mathcal V), \] where the minimum ranges over finite open covers $\mathcal V$ refining $\mathcal U$. Let $\bigvee$ denote the common refinement formed by intersections. The mean topological dimension is given by the Følner formula \[ \operatorname{mdim}(X,G) =\sup_{\mathcal U}\limsup_{n\to\infty} \frac{D\!\left(\bigvee_{g\in F_n}g^{-1}\mathcal U\right)}{|F_n|}, \] where $\mathcal U$ ranges over finite open covers and $g^{-1}\mathcal U=\{\{x:gx\in U\}:U\in\mathcal U\}$.

For $d\in\mathcal M(X)$, a nonempty finite set $F\subseteq G$, and $\varepsilon>0$, define \[ d_F(x,y)=\max_{g\in F}d(gx,gy). \] Let $N(X,d_F,\varepsilon)$ be the least cardinality of a finite open cover of $X$ whose members have $d_F$-diameter strictly less than $\varepsilon$, and put \[ h_d(\varepsilon)=\limsup_{n\to\infty} \frac{\log N(X,d_{F_n},\varepsilon)}{|F_n|}. \] The lower and upper metric mean dimensions are \[ \underline{\operatorname{mdim}}_{\mathrm M}(X,G,d) =\liminf_{\varepsilon\downarrow0} \frac{h_d(\varepsilon)}{\log(1/\varepsilon)}, \qquad \overline{\operatorname{mdim}}_{\mathrm M}(X,G,d) =\limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0} \frac{h_d(\varepsilon)}{\log(1/\varepsilon)}. \] These quantities may take the value $+\infty$.

Source question and unresolved convention. For every such action, is \[ \operatorname{mdim}(X,G) =\min_{d\in\mathcal M(X)}\operatorname{mdim}_{\mathrm M}(X,G,d)? \] The source does not specify whether $\operatorname{mdim}_{\mathrm M}$ means the lower or the upper metric mean dimension. Thus, for either chosen convention $\star\in\{\mathrm{lower},\mathrm{upper}\}$, the corresponding precise candidate statement is that every such action admits $d_*\in\mathcal M(X)$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{mdim}(X,G) =\operatorname{mdim}_{\mathrm M}^{\star}(X,G,d_*) \leq\operatorname{mdim}_{\mathrm M}^{\star}(X,G,d) \quad\text{for every }d\in\mathcal M(X). \] No choice between these conventions is asserted here. The word "minimum" includes attainment by a compatible metric; replacing it by an infimum would change the requested statement. Empty-space conventions also remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every continuous action of a countable amenable group on a compact metrizable space has a compatible metric whose metric mean dimension attains the mean topological dimension and is minimal among all compatible metrics. The source leaves the lower versus upper metric mean dimension convention unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved; that label and the accompanying literature assessment are not independently verified here.
