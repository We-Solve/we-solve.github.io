Math-15041

Asymptotic Lengths of Local Cohomology Modules

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a local ring of finite Krull dimension $d$, let $I\subseteq R$ be an ideal, and let $(I_n)_{n\geq 1}$ be a sequence of ideals satisfying $I_{n+1}\subseteq I_n$. Interpret cofinality with the powers of $I$ as the mutual containment condition \[ (\forall k\geq 1)(\exists n\geq 1)\ I_n\subseteq I^k, \qquad (\forall n\geq 1)(\exists k\geq 1)\ I^k\subseteq I_n. \] Write $H^i_{\mathfrak m}(M)$ for the local cohomology of an $R$-module $M$ with support in $\mathfrak m$, and $\operatorname{length}_R(M)$ for its composition length. For a fixed nonnegative local-cohomology index $i$, suppose that \[ (\exists N\geq 1)(\forall n\geq N)\qquad \ell_n^i:=\operatorname{length}_R H^i_{\mathfrak m}(R/I_n)<\infty. \] The supplied question asks: what asymptotic behavior, as $n\to\infty$, can be established for $\ell_n^i$ under these hypotheses?

The formulation remains qualified: the record does not explicitly specify commutativity or Noetherianity of $R$, the intended range of $i$, or a convention for local cohomology if rings outside the usual commutative Noetherian setting are intended. These conventions require confirmation. The finiteness hypothesis above concerns the chosen index $i$; simultaneous finiteness for all indices is not stated. No multiplicative compatibility or quantitative comparison between $I_n$ and powers of $I$ is supplied. The record specifies neither a normalization nor a particular asymptotic assertion, such as a growth bound or existence of a limit. Consequently, this is a broad asymptotic question rather than a specified conjectural formula.

Short English statement

For a decreasing sequence of ideals cofinal with the powers of an ideal $I$, study the eventual growth of the finite lengths $\operatorname{length}_R H^i_{\mathfrak m}(R/I_n)$ at a fixed index $i$. The ring conventions, index range, and intended asymptotic target require clarification. The supplied status is partially_solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
