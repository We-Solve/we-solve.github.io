Invariant Involutions for Quantum Affine Evaluation Modules

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathfrak g$ be an affine Lie algebra in the scope intended by the source, and let $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ denote its quantum enveloping algebra. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, restrictions on $q$, the allowed affine types, or the precise evaluation-module construction. Let $V(z)$ be a finite-dimensional evaluation module in that construction, with underlying module $V$ and spectral parameter $z$.

Write $\operatorname{Aut}^{\mathrm{inv}}_{\mathrm{II}}(\mathfrak g)$ for the source's class of involutive automorphisms of the second kind. In particular, its elements satisfy $\zeta^2=\mathrm{id}_{\mathfrak g}$. The exact definition of this class and the construction of the associated quantum automorphism $\zeta_q$ are not supplied. Whenever $\zeta_q$ is defined, the pullback $\zeta_q^*V$ of a representation $\rho:U_q(\mathfrak g)\to\operatorname{End}(V)$ has action \[ x\cdot_{\zeta_q^*V}v=\rho(\zeta_q(x))v. \]

Conjecture 3, as extracted from the supplied report. For every evaluation module $V(z)$ in the intended class, there exists \[ \zeta\in\operatorname{Aut}^{\mathrm{inv}}_{\mathrm{II}}(\mathfrak g) \quad\text{such that}\quad \zeta_q^*V\cong V. \] Here the displayed isomorphism interprets the source notation $\zeta_q^*V=V$ as equivalence of modules. Under this interpretation, the conclusion means that there is an invertible linear map $T:V\to V$ satisfying \[ T\rho(\zeta_q(x))=\rho(x)T \qquad\text{for every }x\in U_q(\mathfrak g). \] The automorphism $\zeta$ may depend on $V(z)$. Whether the source requires literal equality of actions instead of an isomorphism remains unresolved. The clean statement's word "invariant" is also unresolved: the original passage uses notation for involutions and discusses involutions of the second kind.

Qualifications and motivation supplied by the source. A pair of involutions of the second kind yields a solution of the universal reflection equation, labeled (2) in the report, in a completion of $U_q(\mathfrak g)^{\otimes 2}$. Its evaluation on integrable category-$\mathcal O$ modules is described in terms of a type-$B$, or cylindrical, ribbon Artin braid groupoid. For finite-dimensional $\mathfrak g$ and $\zeta=\mathrm{id}$, the passage says this recovers the construction labeled [BK19]. In affine type, evaluation on finite-dimensional evaluation modules produces matrix-valued formal Laurent series. Under an unspecified "mild assumption" on $\zeta$, the source asserts \[ \zeta_q^*(V(z))=(\zeta_q^*V)(z^{-1}), \] leading to the generalized parameter-dependent reflection equation labeled [Ch92]. The conjecture is intended to recover the original untwisted or transpose-twisted parameter-dependent reflection equation, with boundary quantum-integrability references [Ch84, Sk88]. The record does not state whether the unspecified mild assumption is part of the conjecture's required choice of $\zeta$.

The supplied passage reports verification for $U_q(\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2)$. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and says that existence for arbitrary evaluation modules and affine types remains conjectural. These are supplied source assessments, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every finite-dimensional quantum affine evaluation module admits an involutive automorphism of the second kind whose quantum pullback preserves the underlying module up to isomorphism. The exact evaluation-module class, quantum lift, parameter assumptions, and meaning of preservation require clarification. The supplied source reports the affine $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ case as verified.
