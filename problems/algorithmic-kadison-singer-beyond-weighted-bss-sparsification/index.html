Math-1303

Algorithmic Kadison-Singer beyond weighted BSS sparsification

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Original request (Leonid Gurvits). Revisit a Batson--S--S/BSS-type approach to an algorithmic Kadison--Singer theorem, despite the note's report that its proponents had thought the approach would not work. The source gives no precise algorithmic conjecture, quantitative target or input model; this is a proposal to revisit a technique, not a proved impossibility statement.

Attached unreviewed status and algorithm claim. The aid reports the broad polynomial-time Weaver/KS construction as open, while saying BSS-inspired determinant barriers cover an equal-norm dense regime $m\ge49d^2$. The full hypotheses, output guarantee and reference for that regime are not supplied.

Its separate rounded-state dynamic program has the following specified input: rational real symmetric positive-semidefinite $d$-by-$d$ matrices $A_1,\ldots,A_m$ with $\sum_iA_i=I_d$. Define \[\operatorname{OPT}=\min_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^m}\left\|\sum_i\sigma_iA_i\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}.\] For a positive rational tolerance, explicitly restricting to $0<\tau\le1$ for the nontrivial approximation range, the aid claims a deterministic output signing with \[\left\|\sum_i\sigma_iA_i\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\operatorname{OPT}+\tau\] and at most \[\left(\frac{6dm}{\tau}\right)^{d(d+1)/2}\] rounded states per layer. The tolerance restriction avoids reading a state bound below one for arbitrarily large $\tau$; larger tolerances are trivial since every signing has discrepancy at most one. The real-symmetric state dimension is essential and is not silently replaced by complex Hermitian dimension.

The aid says an explicit rational bit-complexity analysis accompanies the algorithm, but that analysis is not in the summary. The stated count is exponential in dimension and depends polynomially on $1/\tau$ only when $d$ is fixed; it is not a dimension-uniform polynomial-time KS solution. An existential bound on $\operatorname{OPT}$, including an applicable MSS bound for rank-one inputs, would translate to that bound plus $\tau$ in fixed dimension, subject to the claimed implementation details.

The aid also reports a one-dimensional counterexample to "support-only deweighting" of a BSS certificate: dropping positive sparsification weights and retaining just their support need not yield the desired unweighted partition. The actual counterexample is not supplied. It separately claims that for commuting rank-one summands with $\sum_iA_i=I$ and $\|A_i\|\le\alpha$, greedy signing achieves operator discrepancy at most $\alpha$ in the sign convention above. These special results do not settle whether other BSS-inspired constructions work. All algorithmic, status and novelty claims require independent review.

Short English statement

Gurvits proposes revisiting BSS methods for constructive Kadison--Singer. The aid offers an additive-optimal signing algorithm for fixed dimension, with explicit state count but omitted bit-complexity details, plus special commuting results and a deweighting obstruction. It does not claim a polynomial-time solution uniform in dimension.
