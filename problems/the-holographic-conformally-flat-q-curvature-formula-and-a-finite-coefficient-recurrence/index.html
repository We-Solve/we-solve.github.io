The holographic conformally flat Q-curvature formula and a finite coefficient recurrence

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Problem 19 asks for a formula for Q-curvature in the conformally flat case and for a proof that the procedure of Eastwood--Slovák produces it. The supplied background attributes the structural Pfaffian-plus-divergence statement to Branson, Gilkey and Pohjanpelto. The aid supplies the Graham--Juhl formula and a finite harmonic-extension recurrence; the original Eastwood--Slovák procedure itself is not quoted, so exact identification of that procedure needs source review.

Fix $n=2N\geq4$. To match the recurrence signs, use the nonnegative Laplacian $\Delta_g=-\operatorname{div}_g\nabla$. Write \[ J=R/[2(n-1)],\qquad P=(\operatorname{Ric}-Jg)/(n-2),\qquad c_N=\frac{(-1)^N}{2^{2N}N!(N-1)!}. \] For a locally conformally flat metric use the formal normal-form Poincaré family \[ g_+=x^{-2}({\,\mathrm{d}} x^2+g_x),\qquad g_x=g((I-x^2P/2)\cdot,(I-x^2P/2)\cdot), \] with volume ratio \[ v(x)=\sqrt{\det g_x/\det g}=\sum_{j=0}^Nv^{(2j)}x^{2j}, \qquad v^{(2j)}=(-2)^{-j}\sigma_j(P),\quad v^{(0)}=1. \] Expand the operator with the stated order of multiplication as \[ v(x)\Delta_{g_x}=\sum_{a\geq0}x^{2a}L_{2a}. \] Let $p_0=I$ and let $p_{2r}$, $1\leq r<N$, be the coefficients of the formal harmonic extension $u=f+\sum_{r=1}^{N-1}x^{2r}p_{2r}f+\text{higher terms}$. The aid's explicit triangular recurrence is \[ p_{2r}=-\frac{ \displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}L_{2(r-1-j)}p_{2j} +2(n-2r)\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{r-1} j\,v^{(2r-2j)}p_{2j}} {2r(n-2r)}. \] Here $L_{2a}p_{2j}$ is operator composition and $v^{(2r-2j)}$ multiplies on the left; empty sums are zero. In particular \[ p_2=-\frac{\Delta_g}{2(n-2)}. \] This computes the required subcritical coefficients from lower orders, and gives $p_{2r}1=0$ inductively. At $r=N$ the factor $n-2r$ vanishes: the critical obstruction, rather than this division formula, governs harmonic extension. The Q formula needs only $r<N$ and avoids that undefined step.

With $p_{2j}^*$ the adjoint for ${\,\mathrm{d}}\mathrm{vol}_g$, the reported holographic identity is \[ 2n c_NQ_n=n v^{(n)}+ \sum_{j=1}^{N-1}(n-2j)p_{2j}^*v^{(n-2j)}. \] Normalize the conformally flat scalar Pfaffian as $\operatorname{Pf}=N!\sigma_N(P)$. Then \[ Q_n=2^{N-1}(N-1)!\operatorname{Pf} +\frac1{2n c_N}\sum_{j=1}^{N-1} (n-2j)p_{2j}^*v^{(n-2j)}. \] The second term is the divergence of a natural one-form, because each positive-order $p_{2j}$ annihilates constants. It integrates to zero on a closed manifold, with boundary terms retained in a boundary setting. This gives a finite local algorithm rather than only the existence of an unspecified divergence.

In dimension two the Schouten formula above is not used: $Q_2=\operatorname{Pf}=K$, $v^{(2)}=-K/2$, and the sum is empty. In dimension four the sign convention gives \[ Q_4=4\sigma_2(P)+\Delta_gJ, \] which equals $4\sigma_2(P)-\operatorname{div}\nabla J$. On a unit round $S^{2N}$, the normalization gives $Q_{2N}=(2N-1)!$, including $Q_2=1$, $Q_4=6$, $Q_6=120$. The aid reports additional dimension-six coefficient checks but does not supply their expanded expression. No silent replacement of the full original procedure by a merely similar operator construction is intended.

Short English statement

The aid supplies a holographic Q-curvature formula and a triangular recurrence for every subcritical harmonic-extension coefficient. In the conformally flat case it gives the Pfaffian term plus an explicit divergence, with the critical denominator excluded. Matching this construction to the unquoted Eastwood--Slovák procedure still requires source verification.
