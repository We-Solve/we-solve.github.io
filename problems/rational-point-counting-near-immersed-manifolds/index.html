Rational-Point Counting Near Immersed Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact immersed manifold. The supplied record uses $N(M;Q,\delta)$ for a counting function concerning rational points near or on $M$, but does not supply its definition.

Source question. Determine the asymptotic behavior of \[ N(M;Q,\delta)\qquad\text{as }Q\to\infty\text{ and }\delta\to 0. \] The original excerpt asks, "What can we say about the counting function" in this limiting regime.

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not specify the ambient space, the dimension or regularity of $M$, whether manifolds with boundary are allowed, or whether the counting function counts points in the image of an immersion or counts preimages with multiplicity. It also does not define the rational points being counted, their height or denominator restrictions, any primitivity convention, the distance or norm used for proximity, or how $\delta$ enters that proximity condition. In particular, a fixed distance threshold and a denominator-dependent threshold cannot be distinguished from the supplied text. The parameter domains, the permitted dependence of $\delta$ on $Q$, any required uniformity, and the intended main term or error estimate are unspecified. Consequently, the displayed question records the supplied research target but does not yet define a precise counting problem. No particular counting convention or additional geometric hypothesis is imposed here.

Short English statement

The problem asks how many rational points lie near or on a compact immersed manifold as $Q$ grows and $\delta$ tends to zero. The supplied record omits the definition of the count and the admissible joint limiting regime, so a precise asymptotic statement requires clarification. Its partially solved label is supplied dated curation.
