Images of Infinite Galois Representations

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ be an algebraic closure of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}/{\mathbb{Q}})$ be the absolute Galois group, equipped with its Krull topology. The source uses a coefficient ring $A$ and the group $\operatorname{GL}_2(A)$ of invertible two-by-two matrices over $A$, but does not specify the class of coefficient rings or the topology on the target.

Subject to these unresolved coefficient and topology conventions, consider a continuous representation \[ \rho:G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_2(A) \] which is irreducible, is not induced from a character of any index-two subgroup of $G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, and has infinite image. Define \[ \operatorname{Im}\rho=\{\rho(g):g\in G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\}\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_2(A), \qquad |\operatorname{Im}\rho|=\infty. \] The requested problem is to determine the structure of $\operatorname{Im}\rho$ for representations satisfying these hypotheses.

The intended meaning of structure---for example, abstract group structure, topological group structure, or classification as a subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}_2(A)$---is likewise unspecified. Short English statement

Describe the image of an infinite, irreducible two-dimensional representation of the absolute Galois group of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, assuming it is not induced from a character of an index-two subgroup. The coefficient ring, topology, irreducibility convention, and intended classification must be clarified.
