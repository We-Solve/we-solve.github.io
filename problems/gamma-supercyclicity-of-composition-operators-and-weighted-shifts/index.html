$\Gamma$-Supercyclicity of Composition Operators and Weighted Shifts

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a linear operator $A:E\to E$ on a complex topological vector space and a set $\Gamma\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$, define the $\Gamma$-orbit of $x\in E$ by \[ \operatorname{Orb}_{\Gamma}(A,x)=\{\gamma A^n x:\gamma\in\Gamma,\ n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\}. \] Using the usual convention, $A$ is $\Gamma$-supercyclic if there exists $x\in E\setminus\{0\}$ such that \[ \overline{\operatorname{Orb}_{\Gamma}(A,x)}^{\,E}=E. \] The closure uses the topology of the space on which $A$ acts. This convention makes explicit the orbit and density terminology; the supplied record does not specify these conventions or the underlying function and sequence spaces.

The intended setting is a dissipative composition dynamical system $(X,\mathcal B,\mu,f,T_f)$ of bounded distortion generated by a set $W$, together with its associated weighted shift $B_w$. Here the composition operator acts formally by $T_f\varphi=\varphi\circ f$. The supplied record does not give the precise definition of this class of systems, the function space on which $T_f$ acts, or the formula labelled $(\mathrm{WS})$ defining $B_w$. These are unresolved hypotheses, rather than definitions supplied by this draft.

Intended question. Characterize the subsets $\Gamma\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ for which the equivalence \[ T_f\text{ is }\Gamma\text{-supercyclic} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad B_w\text{ is }\Gamma\text{-supercyclic} \] holds for every system and associated shift in the intended class. In particular, is this equivalence valid for every $\Gamma\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ and every such system? No restrictions on $\Gamma$, such as nonemptiness, boundedness, or closure under multiplication, are supplied. The universal quantification over systems is the natural class-wide reading of the question; the record does not explicitly distinguish it from a characterization for a fixed pair $(T_f,B_w)$.

Source-reported special cases and qualification. The extracted passage attributes the equivalence for $\Gamma={\mathbb{R}}$ to reference [7, Theorem 4.5] and for $\Gamma={\mathbb{C}}$ to [7, Corollary 4.6], under the dissipative bounded-distortion hypotheses generated by $W$. It also attributes to [7, Theorem 4.8] a characterization of ${\mathbb{C}}$-supercyclicity, whose extracted condition reads \[ \text{for every }q\in{\mathbb{N}},\qquad \liminf_{n\to+\infty} \mu\left(f^{q-n}(W)\right)\, \mu\left(f^{q+n}(W)\right)=0. \] This formula records the apparent reading of the supplied extraction only. Its typography, hypotheses, the meaning of negative iterates, and the convention for ${\mathbb{N}}$ require checking against the cited report. It is not asserted here as a verified theorem or used to fill the missing definitions.

Short English statement

Determine which scalar sets $\Gamma\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ preserve $\Gamma$-supercyclicity between a dissipative composition operator of bounded distortion and its associated weighted shift. The supplied source reports the equivalence for ${\mathbb{R}}$ and ${\mathbb{C}}$ and asks about arbitrary $\Gamma$. The precise operator spaces, system hypotheses, and shift construction are missing, so this remains a qualified statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
