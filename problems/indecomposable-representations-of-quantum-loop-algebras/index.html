Indecomposable Representations of Quantum Loop Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $k$ and a $k$-algebra $A$, a finite-dimensional representation means a left $A$-module $M$ with $\dim_k M<\infty$. Such a module is indecomposable if $M\ne 0$ and every decomposition $M\cong M_1\oplus M_2$ into $A$-modules has $M_1=0$ or $M_2=0$. These definitions explain the terminology; the supplied record does not specify the field or the quantum loop algebras intended.

The supplied statement is: "Classify the finite-dimensional indecomposable representations of quantum loop algebras; equivalently, split their Grothendieck groups." Under the conventional interpretation that classification is up to module isomorphism, its recoverable classification target, for each intended algebra $A$ over its intended field $k$, is to describe \[ \left\{[M]_{\cong}: M\text{ is an indecomposable left }A\text{-module},\ \dim_k M<\infty\right\}. \] The field, underlying Lie-theoretic data, deformation parameter and its restrictions, version of the quantum loop algebra, and permitted representation category are unspecified. Thus this is a conditional description of the target, not a fully determined problem with supplied hypotheses.

For an additive category $\mathcal C$ of finite-dimensional modules, its split Grothendieck group is the abelian group generated by isomorphism classes $[M]$, subject to $[M\oplus N]=[M]+[N]$. The ordinary Grothendieck group instead imposes $[M]=[L]+[N]$ for short exact sequences $0\to L\to M\to N\to0$. The phrase "split their Grothendieck groups" does not specify which group is meant or what splitting is requested. Its asserted equivalence with classifying indecomposables cannot be adopted without clarification.
