Tail-Dependence Estimators Under Long-Range Dependence

Stationarity means that finite-dimensional distributions are invariant under admissible common time shifts; max-stability means that its finite-dimensional distributions are max-stable. Short English statement

Determine the limiting distribution, with any necessary centering and scaling, of the stated empirical tail-dependence estimator for a stationary max-stable time series with long-range dependence, when thresholds increase and the expected number of exceedances tends to infinity. A fully specified statement requires the source's estimator formula, parameter domains, and definition of long-range dependence.
