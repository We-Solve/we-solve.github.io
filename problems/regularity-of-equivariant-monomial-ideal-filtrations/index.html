Math-4997

Regularity of Equivariant Monomial-Ideal Filtrations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write ${\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. The monoid $\operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}})$ consists of strictly increasing maps $\pi:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}$, with composition as its operation. For a standard graded polynomial ring $S$ over a field $K$ and a nonzero finitely generated graded $S$-module $M$, its Castelnuovo--Mumford regularity is \[ \operatorname{reg}_S(M)=\max\{j-i:\operatorname{Tor}_i^S(M,K)_j\ne0\}. \] In the question below, regularity is taken for the ideal as a graded module, as indicated by the notation $\operatorname{reg}(I_n)$.

The supplied record does not specify the ambient graded rings $R_n$, the coefficient field, the action of $\operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}})$, the precise filtration and stability conventions, or the definition of the weight $w(I_r)$. These are unresolved parts of the formulation; the notation below retains their source-dependent meanings and does not supply missing hypotheses.

The recorded question is the following. Let $I=(I_n)_{n\ge1}$ be an $\operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}})$-invariant filtration with stability index $r$, where each $I_n\subset R_n$ is a nonzero proper homogeneous ideal. Must there exist integers $a,b$ and $N\ge r$, depending on the filtration, such that \[ \operatorname{reg}_{R_n}(I_n)=an+b \qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge N? \] For every such filtration consisting of monomial ideals, must there exist a constant $D$, independent of $n$, and an integer $N\ge r$ such that \[ \operatorname{reg}_{R_n}(I_n)=\left(w(I_r)-1\right)n+D \qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge N? \] The prescribed slope in the second question cannot be interpreted numerically until $w$ is defined. The supplied record specifically reports that eventual linearity remains open even for nonsaturated filtrations of squarefree monomial ideals. Here a squarefree monomial ideal means an ideal generated by monomials whose variable exponents belong to $\{0,1\}$; the meaning of saturation of the filtration is not supplied. This reported open status is a source qualification, not an independently verified conclusion.

Short English statement

The question asks whether regularity is eventually an affine function of the index for every specified equivariant filtration, and whether monomial filtrations have the particular slope $w(I_r)-1$. The supplied record includes nonsaturated squarefree monomial filtrations among the reported open cases. Missing ambient-ring, action, stability, saturation, and weight definitions require human review before this can be a fully specified standalone statement.
