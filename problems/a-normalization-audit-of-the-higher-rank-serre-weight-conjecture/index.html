A normalization audit of the higher-rank Serre weight conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Approximate Conjecture 6, with all appended discussion retained. Fix a residual representation $\bar\rho:G_{\mathbb{Q}}\to\operatorname{GL}_n(k)$, $k$ a splitting field of characteristic $p$, satisfying the intended global hypotheses, which are not fully reproduced. For a finite-dimensional coefficient module $\sigma$ of $\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{F}}_p)$, the source predicts an attached Hecke eigenclass in some degree $d$ of $H^d(\Gamma_1(N),\sigma)$, with $p\nmid N$, exactly when \[ N(\bar\rho)\mid N,\qquad \operatorname{JH}(\sigma)\cap\operatorname{Wt}(\bar\rho|_{I_p})\ne\varnothing. \] Here $N(\bar\rho)$ is the intended prime-to-$p$ conductor, $\operatorname{JH}$ denotes simple constituents, and $I_p$ is inertia. The arithmetic subgroup, global parity/irreducibility conditions, weight recipe and Hecke normalization are not specified. The source explicitly labels the conjecture approximate and does not choose a single cohomological degree; it suggests a middle range without defining it.

Reported attachment and filtration repairs. In rank at least three, the aid requires attachment through the full unramified Hecke polynomial, with normalized eigenvalues giving every coefficient of \[ \det(T-\bar\rho(\operatorname{Frob}_\ell)),\qquad\ell\nmid Np, \] rather than only the trace coefficient $T_\ell$. Even trace and determinant together do not determine a rank-three characteristic polynomial: over an algebraic closure, the invertible diagonal matrices with characteristic polynomials $(T-1)^3$ and $T^3-3T^2+cT-1$, $c\ne3$, have the same trace and determinant but different middle coefficient. This is a pointwise matrix diagnostic, not an exhibited pair of global representations with identical data at all primes. Exact powers of $\ell$ in the Hecke normalization remain unstated.

For a coefficient short exact sequence, the Hecke-equivariant long exact cohomology sequence implies one direction: eigensupport occurring in the whole module occurs in a Jordan--Hölder factor. The reverse is not formal, because connecting maps may kill a factor's class in the extension. Thus the displayed arbitrary-$\sigma$ equivalence needs additional input beyond the irreducible-weight statement. It is not repaired merely by listing the factors.

Preserved Remark 3 and characteristic-zero comparison. The source hopes that \[ \operatorname{Wt}(\bar\rho|_{I_p}) \subseteq\operatorname{Wt}(\bar\rho|_{I_p}^{\mathrm{ss}}), \] mentions an Ash et al. rank-three recipe, and says Florian generally considers the semisimple case; no complete references/definitions are supplied. It asks for analogous assertions over an imaginary quadratic $E$ using a unitary group that becomes an inner form of $\operatorname{GL}_n$ over $E$ and has $G({\mathbb{Q}}_p)\cong\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{Q}}_p)$. The Galois representation is then $\bar\rho:G_E\to\operatorname{GL}_n(k)$, with polarization $\bar\rho^c\cong\bar\rho^\vee\otimes\nu$ for an unspecified character $\nu$. The source reports constructing Galois representations in that setting and asks whether either direction of the conjecture can be proved; its exact geometric/global hypotheses are absent.

For dominant integral $a_1\geq\cdots\geq a_n$, let $W_a$ be the characteristic-zero highest-weight representation. The source expects a corresponding characteristic-zero eigenclass to have a continuous de Rham Galois representation, with matching Hecke data and Hodge--Tate numbers \[ a_1+n-1,\ a_2+n-2,\ldots,\ a_n \] in its sign convention. They are distinct. It writes ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$ coefficients, but an eigenvalue field may require an extension. For $n=2$, its example is $W_a=\operatorname{Sym}^{a_1-a_2}(\mathrm{Std})\otimes\det^{a_2}$. Reduction of a chosen integral lattice need not remain irreducible. Its statement of a unique simple submodule uses an unstated Weyl-versus-dual-Weyl convention: a Weyl module ordinarily has a unique simple head, while the dual convention has a simple socle. No inconsistent lattice identification is silently adopted.

Finite-field labels and the reported central-twist audit. For $q=p^f$, simple algebraic highest-weight modules $F_a$ restrict to the usual finite-group labels with $0\leq a_i-a_{i+1}<q$, modulo simultaneous central shifts by $(q-1)(1,\ldots,1)$. The source's regular subset additionally requires all base-$p$ digits of each difference to be less than $p-1$. Put $C_f=(q-1)/(p-1)$. The printed coordinate formula is \[ R(a)_i=a_{n+1-i}-(n-i)C_f. \] The aid reports the exact calculation \[ R^2(a)=a-(n-1)C_f(1,\ldots,1). \] Thus it is generally a square equal to a central determinant twist, not a literal involution. The twist is trivial modulo $q-1$ only when the corresponding divisibility holds. Selecting dominant regular representatives after the source's coordinate congruences requires extra conventions; the source warns that a regular weight is not always assigned. For $q=p$, the formula is $(a_n-(n-1),\ldots,a_1)$. The corrupted rank-three diagram labels top $(p-1,p-1)$, bottom $(0,0)$, irregular boundary nodes and an inner "reducible" region, and describes a reflection. Its missing edges/dashed line and the precise module meant by reducible are not reconstructed from OCR art; the central-twist formula qualifies the reflection language.

Preserved semisimple inertia recipe. The notes describe a tame semisimple decomposition \[ \bar\rho|_{I_p}^{\mathrm{ss}}= \bigoplus_j(\chi_j\oplus\chi_j^p\oplus\cdots\oplus\chi_j^{p^{s_j-1}}), \qquad\sum_js_j=n, \] where $\chi_j$ factors through ${\mathbb{F}}_{p^{s_j}}^*$ and $s_j$ is its minimal Frobenius orbit length. They form the torus $T\cong\prod_j{\mathbb{F}}_{p^{s_j}}^*$ in $\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{F}}_p)$, lift characters to characteristic zero, and attach $V(T,\widetilde\chi)$. Regularity, signs and degeneracies needed for this Deligne--Lusztig/cuspidal-induction description are not fully given, so "usually irreducible" is not an unconditional assertion. In the rank-three split case the indicated principal series is induction from the torus product character $\widetilde\chi_1\otimes\widetilde\chi_2\otimes\widetilde\chi_3$ inflated to the Borel, not a direct sum of Borel characters. In the niveau-three case the source gives, on $\alpha\in{\mathbb{F}}_{p^3}^*\setminus{\mathbb{F}}_p^*$, the character value \[ \widetilde\chi(\alpha)+\widetilde\chi(\alpha)^p+ \widetilde\chi(\alpha)^{p^2}, \] under its chosen cuspidal/character convention. This is part of an incomplete recipe, not a full definition of $\operatorname{Wt}$.

The aid proves only these normalization/filtration audits and leaves the global modularity-and-weight equivalence unresolved. The long appended remarks, historical assertions and reconstructed formulas require independent mathematical and source verification.

Short English statement

The aid audits an approximate higher-rank Serre-weight conjecture: use the full Hecke polynomial, keep the Jordan--Hölder implication one-way without extra input, and account for the central twist in the printed weight map. All lengthy unitary, highest-weight and inertia-recipe remarks are retained with their missing conventions, without claiming the global conjecture.
