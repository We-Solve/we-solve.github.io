Group Actions on 1-Manifolds — Question 18

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $m$ be normalized Lebesgue measure on $S^1$. Write $\operatorname{Diff}^{2}_{+}(S^1)$ for the group of orientation-preserving $C^2$ circle diffeomorphisms. A probability distribution on a finitely generated subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{Diff}^{2}_{+}(S^1)$ is a function $\mu:G\to[0,1]$ satisfying $\sum_{g\in G}\mu(g)=1$. A Borel probability measure $\nu$ on $S^1$ is $\mu$-stationary if \[ \nu=\sum_{g\in G}\mu(g)\,g_*\nu, \qquad (g_*\nu)(A)=\nu(g^{-1}(A)) \] for every Borel set $A\subseteq S^1$. Absolute continuity $\nu\ll m$ means that $m(A)=0$ implies $\nu(A)=0$ for every Borel set $A$.

The supplied question asks whether, for every finitely generated $G\leq\operatorname{Diff}^{2}_{+}(S^1)$, there exist a non-degenerate probability distribution $\mu$ on $G$ and a Borel probability measure $\nu$ on $S^1$ such that \[ \nu=\sum_{g\in G}\mu(g)\,g_*\nu \quad\text{and}\quad \nu\ll m. \] The source record does not define "non-degenerate." In particular, it does not specify whether the support must generate $G$ as a group, generate it as a semigroup, or equal $G$. This qualification remains unresolved. The displayed formulation interprets "the stationary measure" existentially; the record supplies neither a uniqueness assertion nor hypotheses guaranteeing uniqueness. No further restrictions on the action are supplied.

Short English statement

Navas asks whether every finitely generated group of orientation-preserving $C^2$ circle diffeomorphisms admits a non-degenerate random walk having an absolutely continuous stationary probability measure. The meaning of "non-degenerate" and the intended uniqueness qualification require clarification from the source.
