Elementary Fourth-Moment Bounds for Cubic Exponential Sums

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Set $f(X)=X^3+X$, so that $d=\deg f=3$. The supplied record does not define $W_f(a;p)$, including its exponential phase, summation domain, or normalization. Thus a complete mathematical statement cannot be recovered from this record alone.

The following expresses the recorded target conditionally on supplying that definition. Suppose that, for each relevant prime $p$, it assigns complex values $W_f(a;p)$ indexed by $a\in{\mathbb{F}}_p^\times$. Interpret the extracted fourth moment as \[ M_f(p)=\frac1p\sum_{a\in{\mathbb{F}}_p^\times}|W_f(a;p)|^4. \] Here the factor $1/p$ is retained from the original extract; interpreting the sum as running over nonzero residue classes is a reconstruction because its range is incomplete in the supplied original text.

The requested task is to prove, by more elementary methods described in the source as going no further than the Chebotarev Density Theorem, the following alternative: \[ \lim_{\substack{p\to\infty\\p\ \mathrm{prime}}}M_f(p)=2, \] or there exist $\delta=\delta(d)>0$, a set $\mathcal P$ of primes, and constants $C\geq0$ and $p_0$ such that \[ \lim_{x\to\infty} \frac{\#\{p\in\mathcal P:p\leq x\}}{\pi(x)} \quad\text{exists and is at least }\delta, \] \[ M_f(p)\geq3-Cp^{-1/2} \qquad(p\in\mathcal P,\ p\geq p_0), \] where $\pi(x)$ counts the primes at most $x$. The last inequality is the usual lower-bound interpretation of the supplied notation $M_f(p)\geq3+O(p^{-1/2})$; its implied constant must be independent of $p$, but the record does not specify its permitted dependencies.

This is a request for an elementary proof of a special case of the source's Theorem 2. That theorem, its hypotheses, and the definition of the permitted proof methods are not supplied. The first alternative is supplied by the reconstructed statement but is absent from the fragmentary original quotation. The formulation above therefore remains conditional and requires comparison with the full source before mathematical acceptance.

Short English statement

For $f(X)=X^3+X$, seek a proof using methods no deeper than Chebotarev density that the recorded fourth moment tends to $2$, or has lower bound $3-O(p^{-1/2})$ on a set of primes of positive natural density. The exponential sum $W_f(a;p)$ and the full theorem hypotheses are missing, so this is a conditional statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
