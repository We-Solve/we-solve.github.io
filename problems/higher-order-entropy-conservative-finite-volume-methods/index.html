Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite-volume discretization evolves approximate cell averages by numerical fluxes across cell boundaries, with additional source contributions when the governing equation is a balance law. Entropy conservation refers to satisfying a discrete entropy balance without artificial entropy production; physical entropy-source contributions and boundary entropy fluxes must be accounted for when present. The governing equation, entropy, numerical entropy flux, and treatment of sources and boundaries are needed to specify this requirement mathematically.
