Two Erdős Problems on Partial Block Designs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, let $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. A partial block design on $[n]$ is a finite family of subsets $A_1,\ldots,A_m\subseteq[n]$ satisfying $|A_i\cap A_j|\leq1$ whenever $i<j$. A pairwise balanced block design on $[n]$ is understood here in the usual sense: every block has at least two elements, and every unordered pair of distinct elements of $[n]$ lies in exactly one block. The supplied record uses the latter term without explicitly defining it; this interpretation requires source review.

The supplied statement attributes the following two questions to Erdős.

  1. Is it true that \[ \begin{gathered} \forall c\in(0,1)\ \exists C=C(c)\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}\ \forall n,m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\ \forall A_1,\ldots,A_m\subseteq[n],\\ \left[\left(\forall i,\ |A_i|>c\sqrt n\right)\ \land\ \left(\forall i<j,\ |A_i\cap A_j|\leq1\right)\right]\\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists B\subseteq[n]\ \forall i\in\{1,\ldots,m\},\quad 0<|B\cap A_i|\leq C\,? \end{gathered} \] Thus $C$ may depend on $c$, but must be independent of $n$, $m$, and the family of blocks. No requirement that every pair of points be covered is imposed in this question.
  2. For $n\geq2$, a finite sequence of integers \[ n\geq x_1\geq\cdots\geq x_m\geq2,\qquad m\geq1, \] is called block-compatible for $n$ if there exists a pairwise balanced block design $A_1,\ldots,A_m$ on $[n]$ with $|A_i|=x_i$ for every $i$. Let $\mathcal S_n$ be the set of these sequences, counting each sequence once regardless of the number of designs realizing it. Is it true that \[ \exists c_0>0\ \exists n_0\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq2}\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq n_0},\qquad |\mathcal S_n|\geq\exp\!\left(c_0\sqrt n\log n\right)? \] Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm, and $c_0$ is an absolute constant independent of $n$, unrelated to the parameter $c$ in the first question.

These are the two questions in the supplied cleaned statement. The supplied dated literature assessment says that the separate counting lower bound has been settled, while a necessary-and-sufficient characterization of block-compatible sequences remains unresolved. That characterization is not explicitly posed in the supplied mathematical statement. Consequently, the second question is retained as a source question, without asserting that it remains open; no additional characterization problem is invented here. The registry's overall open label and its statement-verification label are supplied metadata, not independent mathematical verification.

Short English statement

The first Erdős question asks whether every partial block design whose blocks have size greater than $c\sqrt n$ admits a set meeting every block in between one and $C(c)$ points. The second asks for at least $\exp(c_0\sqrt n\log n)$ distinct realizable nonincreasing block-size sequences for every sufficiently large $n$. Supplied dated curation reports the counting bound as settled and a structural characterization as unresolved; the intended current open target therefore requires review.
