Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.85

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, and let $F$ be a free pro-$p$ group with free pro-$p$ basis $X$. No finiteness assumption on $X$ is supplied. For $t\in F$, write \[ t^F=\overline{\langle f^{-1}tf\mid f\in F\rangle} \] for its closed normal closure. Fix $r\in F$, put $R=r^F$, and let $q:F\to F/R$ be the quotient homomorphism. An element $s\in F$ is associated to $r$ if $s^F=R$.

(a) Suppose that \[ \forall s\in F,\qquad s^F=R\ \Longrightarrow\ \neg\exists u\in F\;(s=u^p). \] Must $F/R$ be torsion-free? Explicitly, must \[ \forall g\in F/R\;\forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1},\qquad g^n=1\ \Longrightarrow\ g=1 \] hold?

(b) For $Y\subseteq X$, let $F_Y=\overline{\langle Y\rangle}\leq F$. The natural interpretation of an element depending on $Y$ is that it belongs to $F_Y$. Under this interpretation, define \[ \mathcal S_r=\{Y\subseteq X\mid \exists s\in F_Y\text{ with }s^F=R\}. \] The source states that an element associated to $r$ depends on a minimal subset $X'$ of $X$. For such an $X'$, and each $x\in X'$, must the images $q(y)$, indexed by $y\in X\setminus\{x\}$, freely generate a free pro-$p$ group? Equivalently, is the restriction \[ q\big|_{F_{X\setminus\{x\}}}:F_{X\setminus\{x\}}\longrightarrow F/R \] injective, or, equivalently, does \[ R\cap F_{X\setminus\{x\}}=\{1\} \] hold?

The source does not define dependence or specify the meaning of minimality. In particular, minimality under inclusion in $\mathcal S_r$, minimum cardinality among members of $\mathcal S_r$, and minimal support for a particular associated element are not distinguished. Thus part (b) retains an unresolved qualification: the displayed question expresses its requested conclusion, but a fully quantified formulation requires clarification of the choice of $X'$. The existence of such a minimal subset is stated in the source and is not established here. Part (b) is presented separately; the source does not explicitly say that the hypothesis of part (a) carries over.

Short English statement

For a free pro-$p$ group, part (a) asks whether the quotient by one closed normal relation is torsion-free when no element generating the same closed normal subgroup is a $p$th power. Part (b) asks whether deleting a basis element occurring in a suitably minimal associated relation leaves freely generating images in the quotient. The meaning of minimality in part (b) requires clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
