Universal-Cover Decomposition for Nef Anticanonical Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
If $X$ is a compact Kähler manifold with nef anticanonical class, does its universal cover decompose as

$$\widetilde X\simeq\mathbb C^q\times\prod_jY_j\times\prod_kS_k\times Z,$$

where each $Y_j$ is an irreducible Calabi–Yau manifold, each $S_k$ is an irreducible hyperkähler manifold, and $Z$ is rationally connected?

Formulation references: [S1], [S2].

Universal-Cover Decomposition for Nef Anticanonical Manifolds

OpenAI's claim

Among smooth complex Fano varieties in dimension six, those with nef tangent are claimed to be rational homogeneous, with a universal-cover consequence when dimension minus maximal étale irregularity is at most six. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
OpenAI claims rational homogeneity for each smooth connected complex projective Fano manifold of dimension six whose tangent bundle is nef (a quotient of a complex semisimple group by a parabolic subgroup). Its Kähler uniformization corollary considers a compact connected Kähler $X$ with analytically nef tangent bundle and \[ 0\le d=\dim X-\widetilde q(X)\le6,\qquad \widetilde q(X)=\max_{X'\to X\text{ finite étale}}h^1(X',\mathcal O_{X'}). \] It claims a connected finite étale cover whose Albanese morphism is a holomorphically locally trivial bundle with rational homogeneous fiber $F$ of dimension $d$, and \[ \widetilde X\simeq F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}. \] Thus the universal cover is quasi-projective and holomorphically convex, with Remmert reduction to the affine factor. In particular the corollary applies in complex dimension at most six.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 067, 2026-09-25): Known contractions reduce the new case to Picard number one and pseudoindices four or five. The universal minimal-curve family has two smooth fibrations whose projection identities give linear intersection constraints; characteristic-number and integrality comparisons rule out the remaining models and identify the homogeneous possibilities.
