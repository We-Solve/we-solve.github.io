Group Actions on 1-Manifolds — Question 12

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be an interval or a circle, and let $\operatorname{Homeo}(M)$ denote its group of homeomorphisms. In real projective coordinates, a projective transformation has the form \[ x\longmapsto \frac{ax+b}{cx+d},\qquad a,b,c,d\in{\mathbb{R}},\quad ad-bc\ne0, \] interpreted on its domain in the real projective line. A piecewise-projective homeomorphism agrees with projective transformations on its pieces. The supplied record does not specify the permitted partitions, the projective transformation subgroup, or the interval and endpoint conventions; these must be fixed before the class of admissible actions is fully determined.

For a specified smooth structure on $M$, write $\operatorname{Diff}^{1}(M)$ for the group of bijections whose maps and inverses are continuously differentiable, with the applicable boundary convention if $M$ has endpoints. An action $G\leq\operatorname{Homeo}(M)$ is topologically conjugate to a group of $C^{1}$ diffeomorphisms if \[ \exists h\in\operatorname{Homeo}(M)\quad \forall g\in G,\qquad h\circ g\circ h^{-1}\in\operatorname{Diff}^{1}(M). \] The same homeomorphism $h$ must conjugate every element of $G$.

Classification question (Navas, 2017). Determine which groups $G\leq\operatorname{Homeo}(M)$ consisting of piecewise-projective homeomorphisms satisfy this simultaneous conjugacy condition, for interval and circle actions. The supplied statement imposes no finite-generation hypothesis or explicit orientation restriction. This formulation concerns the given action; merely admitting some other abstractly isomorphic $C^{1}$ action does not express the stated conjugacy requirement. The unresolved conventions above are part of the formulation requiring review.

Short English statement

Classify the piecewise-projective actions on an interval or circle that can be changed into $C^{1}$ diffeomorphism actions by one common homeomorphic change of coordinates. The source leaves the interval, endpoint, partition, and projective-transformation conventions unspecified.
