Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 48

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a hyperbolic group and $Z=\partial_\infty G$. Boundary analysis requires a specified metric and, for modulus or measurable structures, a reference measure. These data and regularity assumptions are not supplied in the excerpt.

Program (Jeremy Tyson). Study relationships between different notions of conformal structure on $Z$, including the following incomplete list.

  1. Metric $1$-quasiconformality, written $H(f)=1$. A usual pointwise dilatation compares the largest and smallest image distances at radius $r$ about $x$, taking their ratio as $r\downarrow0$. The source does not say whether $H$ is a supremum or essential supremum of pointwise dilatations, or what exceptional sets are allowed.
  2. Preservation of modulus for curves joining two compacta. Given a measure $\nu$ and exponent $p$, one standard modulus is \[ \operatorname{Mod}_p\Gamma=\inf_\rho\int_Z\rho^p\,d\nu, \qquad\int_\gamma\rho\,ds\geq1\quad(\gamma\in\Gamma), \] where $\Gamma$ is the rectifiable-curve family connecting the chosen compact sets. The measure, exponent, and curve conventions are absent.
  3. Quasisymmetry with distortion as close to linear as desired. The standard inequality is \[ \frac{d(f(x),f(a))}{d(f(x),f(b))} \leq\eta_x\left(\frac{d(x,a)}{d(x,b)}\right). \] The source expressly permits the control functions to depend on the point $x$ where conformality is tested. It gives no neighborhood/scale rule, topology of closeness to linear, or uniformity in $x$; this is not silently replaced by a single global control function.
  4. When the appropriate Poincaré/differentiability hypotheses hold, use Cheeger's cotangent bundle $T^*Z$ to define a measurable bounded conformal structure $\mu$ for which $\operatorname{Conf}(Z,\mu)$ is a convergence group. A typical conformal structure is a measurable field of inner-product classes modulo positive scaling, with a specified bounded-distortion reference. The excerpt does not define those bounds, the conformal automorphisms, or the topology/discreteness convention for convergence-group action.
The request is to identify implications, equivalences, hypotheses, or constructions among these notions, not to assume that all four are equivalent on every hyperbolic-group boundary. Doubling, Ahlfors regularity, Loewner conditions, or a Cheeger differentiable structure must be supplied where needed rather than asserted universally.

The summary describes a survey-style program with partial equivalences on Loewner boundaries, naming Heinonen--Koskela, Bourdon--Pajot, Keith, and others without exact citations. It leaves general aspects open and does not give a complete classification theorem.

Short English statement

Compare metric $1$-quasiconformality, curve-modulus preservation, point-dependent nearly linear quasisymmetry, and measurable conformal structures on hyperbolic-group boundaries. The metric-measure hypotheses and precise distortion/convergence conventions are missing; no universal equivalence is assumed.
