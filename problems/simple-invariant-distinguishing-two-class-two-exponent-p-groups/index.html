Math-6903

Simple Invariant Distinguishing Two Class-Two Exponent-$p$ Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be an odd prime, and let $\omega$ be a generator of the multiplicative group ${\mathbb{F}}_p^\times$. Use the commutator convention $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$. The presentations below are understood relative to groups of exponent dividing $p$ and nilpotency class at most two: in addition to the displayed relations, impose $x^p=1$ and $[[x,y],z]=1$ for all group elements $x,y,z$. Thus they specify quotients of the free group on the five displayed generators by these ambient identities and the displayed relators. The supplied source describes the resulting groups as having exponent $p$ and nilpotency class two.

Define \[ \begin{aligned} G_1=\langle a,b,c,d,e\mid{}&[c,b]=[d,b]=[e,c]=[e,d]=1,\\ &[d,c]=[b,a],\quad [e,b]=[c,a],\\ &[e,a]=[d,a]^\omega\rangle, \end{aligned} \] and \[ \begin{aligned} G_2=\langle a,b,c,d,e\mid{}&[d,a]=[e,a]=[c,b]=[d,b]=[e,c]=1,\\ &[d,c]=[b,a],\quad [e,b]=[c,a]\rangle. \end{aligned} \] Here $[d,a]^\omega$ means $[d,a]^m$, where $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ represents $\omega\in{\mathbb{F}}_p$; the exponent identity makes this independent of the representative.

A group invariant is an assignment $I$ to groups, with values in a specified set, such that $H\cong K$ implies $I(H)=I(K)$. For arbitrary odd $p$ and a chosen generator $\omega$, find such an invariant that is simpler than the automorphism-group order and establish \[ I(G_1)\ne I(G_2), \] thereby proving $G_1\not\cong G_2$. The source does not specify a mathematical criterion for "simpler," so this comparison remains a qualitative requirement requiring explanation in any proposed answer.

The supplied dated curation states that automorphism-group order already distinguishes these groups and that no simpler invariant answering the extracted question had been established. These are supplied source assessments, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

For each odd prime $p$ and chosen generator $\omega\in{\mathbb{F}}_p^\times$, seek an isomorphism invariant that distinguishes the two presented groups of exponent $p$ and nilpotency class two and is simpler than computing their automorphism-group orders. The meaning of "simpler" is unspecified. The supplied open status and literature assessment require independent review.
