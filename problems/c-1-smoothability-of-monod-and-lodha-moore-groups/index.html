$C^1$ Smoothability of Monod and Lodha–Moore Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record names the Monod groups $H(A)$ and the Lodha--Moore group $G_0$, but provides neither their definitions nor the admissible parameters $A$. Consequently, the following describes the mathematical targets conditionally on identifying those groups and any intended actions; it is not a complete specification of a single problem.

For $X={\mathbb{R}}$ or $X=[0,1]$, let $\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$ denote the group of homeomorphisms of $X$, and let $\operatorname{Diff}^{1}(X)$ denote the group of bijections of $X$ whose maps and inverses are continuously differentiable. For the interval, use the convention that derivatives extend continuously to the endpoints as one-sided derivatives. The source does not specify its endpoint convention or whether only orientation-preserving maps are intended; these choices require confirmation. The interval $[0,1]$ represents a nondegenerate compact interval by affine change of coordinates, assuming this is what the source means by a closed interval.

A faithful $C^1$ action of a group $K$ on $X$ is an injective homomorphism \[ \rho:K\longrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^{1}(X). \] For a specified action $\alpha:K\to\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$, say that $\alpha$ is $C^1$-smoothable if there exists $h\in\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$ such that \[ h\alpha(g)h^{-1}\in\operatorname{Diff}^{1}(X) \qquad\text{for every }g\in K. \] This definition concerns a specified action, whereas the existence of a faithful $C^1$ action concerns the abstract group. The supplied record does not identify the actions to be smoothed and does not justify identifying these two questions.

The recorded questions are: for the intended Monod groups $H(A)$ and the intended Lodha--Moore group $G_0$, are their intended actions $C^1$-smoothable, and do these groups admit faithful $C^1$ actions on ${\mathbb{R}}$ or on a nondegenerate closed interval? For each intended group $K$, the latter questions take the forms \[ \exists\,\rho_{{\mathbb{R}}}:K\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}), \qquad \exists\,\rho_I:K\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^{1}([0,1]). \] They are displayed separately because the source does not specify whether its word "or" requests either realization, both realization questions, or a comparison with smoothability. The domain and quantifier for $A$, the group definitions, and the designated actions remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The record asks about $C^1$ smoothability and faithful differentiable actions of Monod groups $H(A)$ and the Lodha--Moore group $G_0$ on the real line or a closed interval. A precise statement requires the missing group definitions, the allowed parameters $A$, and the actions intended for smoothing. Smoothability of a specified action and existence of some faithful action are distinct targets. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
