Math-3841

Finite Generation of Birational-Sequence Monoids

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a monoid $M$ with operation $\circ$ and identity $e$, a subset $F\subseteq M$ generates $M$ if every element of $M$ is a finite product of elements of $F$, allowing the empty product $e$. Thus $M$ is finitely generated if \[ \exists F\subseteq M\quad |F|<\infty,\qquad M=\left\{f_1\circ\cdots\circ f_k : k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\ f_1,\ldots,f_k\in F\right\}. \] Here $\circ$ is neutral notation for the monoid operation, not an additional assertion about the construction.

The supplied question is the following: for every birational sequence $S$ and every total order $\prec$ that is proper in the intended PBW/Newton--Okounkov construction, is the associated monoid $\Gamma(S,\prec)$ finitely generated? Equivalently, does every such pair $(S,\prec)$ admit a finite subset $F\subseteq\Gamma(S,\prec)$ generating that monoid?

Unresolved scope. The supplied record does not define a birational sequence, the condition that an order is proper, or the construction of $\Gamma$. It also does not specify the ambient algebraic group or Lie algebra, the representation-theoretic hypotheses, or the domains over which these data vary. Consequently, the universal quantifiers above retain the source's intended scope; the record alone does not determine a fully self-contained mathematical formulation.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every monoid obtained from a birational sequence and a proper total order in the intended PBW/Newton--Okounkov construction has a finite generating set. The construction and its admissibility conditions are unspecified in the supplied record and require source clarification.
