The essential singularity of the planar XY correlation length

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The XY correlation length is claimed to satisfy $\sqrt{b_c-b}\log(1/m(b))\to A$ with $0<A<\infty$ on approach from the massive side.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For angles $\theta_x\in\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)$ on $\Lambda_R=[-R,R]^2\cap\mathbb Z^2$, use the free-boundary cosine law proportional to $\exp[b\sum_{\{x,y\}\subset\Lambda_R,\,|x-y|=1}\cos(\theta_x-\theta_y)]$, relative to product uniform angles. Count each edge once and omit exterior edges. Define \[ C_b(r)=\lim_{R\to\infty}\mathbb E_{R,b}^{\mathrm{free}}\cos(\theta_0-\theta_{re_1}),\quad m(b)=\lim_{r\to\infty}-r^{-1}\log C_b(r),\quad b_c=\inf\{b>0:m(b)=0\}. \] The thermodynamic limit precedes the separation limit. For $0<b<b_c$, the axis correlation length is $\xi(b)=m(b)^{-1}$. There exists $A_{\mathrm{XY}}\in(0,\infty)$ such that \[ \lim_{b\uparrow b_c}\sqrt{b_c-b}\,\log\xi(b)=A_{\mathrm{XY}}. \] This concerns the nearest-neighbour cosine model and approach from its positive-mass phase.

Outline of the argument

The analytic activity map from the critical companions is followed on scales that grow with the inverse-temperature distance. Dual-height fluctuation estimates identify the scale at which the marginal flow leaves the critical regime, and the spin--height mass relation converts that scale into the essential singularity.
