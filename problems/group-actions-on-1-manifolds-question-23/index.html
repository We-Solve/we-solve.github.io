Group Actions on 1-Manifolds — Question 23

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $\operatorname{Homeo}(S^1)$ denote the group of circle homeomorphisms. Let $G\leq\operatorname{Homeo}(S^1)$ be finitely generated, and write $\rho_0:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Homeo}(S^1)$ for its given action. An additional circle action of the abstract group $G$ is a homomorphism $\rho:G\to\operatorname{Homeo}(S^1)$. For a finite generating set $S\subseteq G$, the usual $C^0$ topology on the space of actions is the topology of uniform convergence of the homeomorphisms assigned to the generators and their inverses.

The supplied question asks whether the following implication holds: if $\rho_0$ is $C^0$ structurally stable and $G$ admits infinitely many pairwise non-semiconjugate circle actions, must there exist $a,b\in G$ such that \[ \langle a,b\rangle\cong F_2, \] where $F_2$ is the free group on two generators? Equivalently, the conclusion requires that every nonempty reduced word in $a^{\pm1}$ and $b^{\pm1}$ represent a nonidentity element of $G$.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define $C^0$ structural stability or semiconjugacy. In particular, it does not specify the equivalence required between nearby actions, the permitted regularity or monotonicity of a semiconjugating map, or whether semiconjugacy is intended as a symmetric equivalence relation. It also does not specify whether only orientation-preserving actions are allowed or whether the additional actions must be faithful. The implication above retains these source terms pending clarification; it is not a fully specified assertion until those conventions are fixed.

Short English statement

For a finitely generated group with a $C^0$ structurally stable circle action, does the existence of infinitely many pairwise non-semiconjugate circle actions force a free subgroup of rank two? The source conventions for stability, semiconjugacy, orientation, and faithfulness require clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that label is not independently verified.
