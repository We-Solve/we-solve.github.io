Math-7351

Trace Fields of Hyperbolic 3-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A number field is a finite extension $K/{\mathbb{Q}}$. An embedding $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$ is nonreal if $\sigma(K)\not\subset{\mathbb{R}}$. A real place means an embedding into ${\mathbb{R}}$; a field may have both real and nonreal embeddings, but a chosen real embedding cannot satisfy the displayed nonreal condition.

For a subgroup $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$, its trace field is the embedded field \[ k_{\mathrm{tr}}(\Gamma)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\{\operatorname{tr}(\gamma):\gamma\in\Gamma\}\right)\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}. \] If the holonomy of an orientable hyperbolic $3$-manifold $M$ is represented by a specified lift $\widetilde\rho:\pi_1(M)\to\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$, this convention gives $k_{\mathrm{tr}}(M,\widetilde\rho)=k_{\mathrm{tr}}(\widetilde\rho(\pi_1(M)))$. The source does not specify this convention or distinguish the trace field from the invariant trace field, whose standard lifted-group expression is ${\mathbb{Q}}(\{\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^2):\gamma\in\Gamma\})$.

Question as supplied. Is every pair $(K,\sigma)$ with $\sigma(K)\not\subset{\mathbb{R}}$ the trace field of some hyperbolic $3$-manifold?

Under the interpretation that $K$ is a number field, $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$ is the specified embedding, and the ordinary trace-field convention above is intended, the realization sought is \[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every number field }K\text{ and embedding }\sigma:K\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}} \text{ with }\sigma(K)\not\subset{\mathbb{R}},\\ \text{there exist a hyperbolic }3\text{-manifold }M \text{ and a lifted holonomy }\widetilde\rho \text{ such that }k_{\mathrm{tr}}(M,\widetilde\rho)=\sigma(K). \end{gathered} \] This is a conditional interpretation, not a resolved formulation: the supplied question does not specify completeness, finite volume, orientability, connectedness, or treatment of boundary, nor does it specify the precise holonomy and trace-field conventions. Equality here is equality of subfields of ${\mathbb{C}}$, recording the chosen embedding rather than only the abstract isomorphism class of $K$.

Unresolved source conflict. The supplied literature assessment instead describes a number field with a real place and a chosen real embedding, and calls the pair admissible without defining admissibility. A chosen real embedding contradicts the explicit condition $\sigma(K)\not\subset{\mathbb{R}}$. The two descriptions cannot be adopted simultaneously; the intended domain and manifold conventions require human source review.

Short English statement

The displayed question asks whether every number field with a specified nonreal complex embedding can occur as an embedded trace field of a hyperbolic $3$-manifold. The accompanying assessment instead specifies a real embedding, so the intended formulation remains unresolved. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified status.
