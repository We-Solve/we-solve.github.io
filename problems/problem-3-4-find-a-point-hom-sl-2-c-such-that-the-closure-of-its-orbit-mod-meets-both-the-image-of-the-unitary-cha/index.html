Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\pi=\pi_1(\Sigma)$ and let \[ X(\Sigma)=\operatorname{Hom}(\pi,\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}}))//\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}}) \] be the character space, with $[\rho]$ the character of a representation. The mapping class action is by precomposition, well-defined on characters. Let $U(\Sigma)$ be the image of $\operatorname{Hom}(\pi,\mathrm{SU}(2))$ in $X(\Sigma)$, and let $Q(\Sigma)$ be the quasi-Fuchsian character locus. In the usual closed genus-at-least-two setting, the latter consists of characters whose projectivizations are discrete faithful quasi-Fuchsian representations. Punctured/boundary versions need peripheral conventions that the excerpt does not provide.

Problem 3.4. Find $\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(\pi,\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}}))$ such that, for the same orbit closure \[ C_\rho=\overline{\operatorname{Mod}_{\Sigma}\cdot[\rho]}, \] both \[ C_\rho\cap U(\Sigma)\ne\varnothing, \qquad C_\rho\cap\overline{Q(\Sigma)}\ne\varnothing \] hold. The unitary and quasi-Fuchsian limiting characters need not coincide. The natural reading uses the ordinary complex-analytic topology, but the closure topology and precise class of surfaces are unstated and must be confirmed; Zariski closure would be a different problem. The representation itself is not required by the source to be unitary, quasi-Fuchsian, or faithful.

The appended discussion describes homological representations arising from character spaces. The chapter author and Neumann [51] are reported to obtain finite-image mapping-class actions for relative $\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$ character varieties of the one-holed torus and four-holed sphere. Atiyah--Bott [3] are cited for compact-group topology via infinite-dimensional Morse theory; the source says the action on rational cohomology of nonsingular components factors through the symplectic representation on $H^*(\Sigma)$. Biswas [9] is cited for the resulting Torelli triviality in that nonsingular cohomological setting, not a pointwise triviality of the representation space. In contrast, Cappell--Lee--Miller [17,18] are reported to detect nontrivial Torelli action on homology of the full $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ character variety for closed $\Sigma$. These distinct homology/cohomology and smooth/full-space scopes are not collapsed into one assertion.

Short English statement

Find one complex surface-group character whose mapping-class orbit closure meets both the unitary-character locus and the closure of the quasi-Fuchsian locus. Specify the surface and closure topology; the source's homological-action discussion is separate context.
