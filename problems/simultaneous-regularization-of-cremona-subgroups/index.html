Simultaneous Regularization of Cremona Subgroups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\operatorname{Cr}_2(k)=\operatorname{Bir}_k({\mathbb{P}}^2_k)$ for the plane Cremona group over a field $k$, namely the group of birational self-maps of the projective plane defined over $k$. The supplied record does not specify the allowed base fields or the category of surfaces on which regularization is required. To expose these unresolved parameters, let $\mathcal S$ denote the intended class of surfaces over $k$ birational to ${\mathbb{P}}^2_k$; this is a placeholder for missing source qualifications, not an additional hypothesis attributed to the source.

For a subgroup $H\leq\operatorname{Cr}_2(k)$, define regularizability relative to this class by \[ \operatorname{Reg}_{\mathcal S}(H) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists S\in\mathcal S\;\exists\text{ a }k\text{-birational map }\phi:{\mathbb{P}}^2_k\dashrightarrow S \quad \phi H\phi^{-1}\subseteq\operatorname{Aut}_k(S). \] An element $g$ is regularizable when $\operatorname{Reg}_{\mathcal S}(\langle g\rangle)$ holds. The surface and conjugating map in this definition may depend on $g$.

Question, with unresolved source parameters displayed. For the intended choices of $k$ and $\mathcal S$, does every finitely generated subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{Cr}_2(k)$ satisfy \[ \left[\forall g\in G,\ \operatorname{Reg}_{\mathcal S}(\langle g\rangle)\right] \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Reg}_{\mathcal S}(G)? \] Thus the conclusion requires one surface and one birational conjugacy that work simultaneously for every element of $G$. The record does not state whether the surfaces must be smooth, projective, or complete, or whether any extension of the base field is permitted; these choices must be checked before this schema can be regarded as a fully specified formulation.

The record also describes the question as asking whether the locally elliptic action of $G$ on the blow-up complex must be globally elliptic. If these terms mean, respectively, that each element has a fixed point and that the whole group has a common fixed point, the corresponding schema for the stated complex $X$ is \[ \left[\forall g\in G,\ \operatorname{Fix}_X(g)\ne\varnothing\right] \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Fix}_X(G)\ne\varnothing, \qquad \operatorname{Fix}_X(G)=\bigcap_{g\in G}\operatorname{Fix}_X(g). \] The construction of the blow-up complex, the precise convention for local ellipticity, and whether fixed vertices or arbitrary fixed points are intended are not supplied. Consequently, this equivalence is retained as a source assertion requiring verification, rather than asserted here as an independently established fact.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a finitely generated group of plane birational transformations can be conjugated simultaneously into the automorphism group of one surface whenever each individual transformation can be regularized on a possibly different surface. The base-field assumptions and the permitted surfaces remain unspecified. The supplied curation labels the problem open and reports a positive result over finite fields; these are dated source assessments requiring independent verification.
