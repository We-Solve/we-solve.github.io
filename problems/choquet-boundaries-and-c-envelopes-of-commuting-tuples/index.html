Choquet Boundaries and $C^*$-Envelopes of Commuting Tuples

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks to compute the noncommutative Choquet boundary and the $C^*$-envelope associated with a commuting operator tuple $A=(A_1,\ldots,A_d)$. These objects require a specified operator system or operator algebra; the record does not identify that choice or the precise class of admissible tuples.

A conditional formulation in the usual bounded-operator setting is as follows. Let $d\geq 1$ be a finite integer, let $H\neq\{0\}$ be a complex Hilbert space, and let $A_1,\ldots,A_d\in B(H)$ satisfy \[ A_iA_j=A_jA_i\qquad(1\leq i,j\leq d). \] Put $C_A=C^*(I_H,A_1,\ldots,A_d)$. An operator system $\mathcal S\subseteq C_A$ is a norm-closed complex linear subspace containing $I_H$ and closed under adjoints. Suppose that $C^*(\mathcal S)=C_A$. One possible choice is \[ \mathcal S_A=\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{C}}}\{I_H,A_1,\ldots,A_d,A_1^*,\ldots,A_d^*\}, \] but this choice is not specified by the source. Another possible intended object is the norm-closed unital operator algebra generated by the tuple.

For a specified $\mathcal S$, a boundary representation is an irreducible unital $*$-representation $\pi:C_A\to B(K)$, with $K\neq\{0\}$, having the following unique extension property: every unital completely positive map $\Phi:C_A\to B(K)$ satisfying $\Phi|_{\mathcal S}=\pi|_{\mathcal S}$ equals $\pi$. The noncommutative Choquet boundary $\partial_{\mathrm{nc}}\mathcal S$ consists of these representations up to unitary equivalence. Here complete positivity means that every matrix amplification of $\Phi$ preserves positive elements.

The Shilov ideal $J_{\mathcal S}$ is the largest closed two-sided ideal of $C_A$ for which the quotient map is completely isometric on $\mathcal S$: for every integer $n\geq1$ and every $[s_{ij}]\in M_n(\mathcal S)$, \[ \|[s_{ij}+J_{\mathcal S}]\|_{M_n(C_A/J_{\mathcal S})} =\|[s_{ij}]\|_{M_n(C_A)}. \] The corresponding $C^*$-envelope is $C^*_{\mathrm e}(\mathcal S)=C_A/J_{\mathcal S}$, with its induced copy of $\mathcal S$.

Conditional problem. After specifying the intended operator system or operator algebra and the admissible class of commuting tuples, determine its noncommutative Choquet boundary and thereby identify its $C^*$-envelope. The boundedness, finite dimension of the tuple, unital convention, and operator-system choice above are explicit conventions for this conditional formulation, not additional hypotheses established by the supplied record. The source also leaves the required meaning of "compute" unspecified.

Short English statement

Determine the boundary representations and the resulting $C^*$-envelope associated with a commuting operator tuple. A precise target requires the intended operator system or operator algebra, the class of tuples, and the meaning of "compute" to be specified. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified here.
