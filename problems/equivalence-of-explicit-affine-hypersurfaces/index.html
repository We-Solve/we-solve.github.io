Math-14981

Equivalence of Explicit Affine Hypersurfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Retain the source's intended polynomials and recurrence over ${\mathbb{C}}$: \[ H_0=Y,\qquad H_1=X,\qquad r=X^3+XYZ-Y^3,\qquad a_n=\deg H_n, \] \[ H_{n+1}=\frac{r^{a_n}+H_n^3}{H_{n-1}}\qquad(n\geq1). \] For a polynomial $P$, write $V(P)=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^3:P(z)=0\}$. The proposed hypersurfaces are \[ U_n=V(H_n-1),\qquad V_n=V(XY-Z^{a_n}-1). \] For every $n\geq3$, the source asks whether there exists a holomorphic automorphism $\Phi_n:{\mathbb{C}}^3\to{\mathbb{C}}^3$ with $\Phi_n(U_n)=V_n$, and separately whether such ambient equivalence holds by a real diffeomorphism or by a homeomorphism. In the usual underlying-real-manifold interpretation these latter maps act on ${\mathbb{C}}^3\cong{\mathbb{R}}^6$; equivalence only of real loci in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ would be a different question.

The recurrence as printed does not define a polynomial sequence. Since $a_1=1$, its first step gives \[ H_2=\frac{r+X^3}{Y}=\frac{2X^3+XYZ-Y^3}{Y}. \] The numerator restricts to $2X^3$ at $Y=0$, so it is not divisible by $Y$ over ${\mathbb{C}}$. Consequently $H_2$ is not a polynomial, and the intended polynomial degree $a_2$ and subsequent hypersurfaces are not determined in the stated sense. A rational-function degree or a numerator-clearing convention would change the construction and is not supplied. No sign, exponent or definition is silently corrected; the three ambient-equivalence questions are retained pending recovery of the intended recurrence.

Short English statement

Compare the specified hypersurfaces by holomorphic, smooth real and topological ambient changes of coordinates. The printed recurrence fails to produce a polynomial already at $H_2$, so the hypersurface family must be corrected from the source before those questions are fully defined.
