Finiteness and Rigidity of Fast Bump Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $G$ is of type $F_\infty$ if there exists a connected CW complex $X$ with $\pi_1(X)\cong G$, contractible universal cover, and finite $k$-skeleton for every integer $k\geq 0$.

For each integer $n>1$, let $\mathcal C_n$ provisionally denote the class intended by the source: groups generated by $n$ fast one-bump functions, excluding nontrivial direct sums and wreath products. This notation records an unresolved source qualification: the supplied record does not define the ambient homeomorphism group, "one-bump," or "fast," and does not specify precisely which direct-sum and wreath-product decompositions are excluded. Consequently, $\mathcal C_n$ cannot be defined unambiguously from the supplied record alone. Generation by $n$ functions is not stated to mean that $n$ is the minimum number of generators.

The finiteness question is, conditional on specifying this class, \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\quad n>1\ \Longrightarrow\ \forall G\in\mathcal C_n,\quad G\text{ is of type }F_\infty. \] The source also asks whether all such groups are isomorphic. Under the reading that comparisons are made for each fixed $n$, this is \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\quad n>1\ \Longrightarrow\ \forall G,H\in\mathcal C_n,\quad G\cong H. \] The supplied wording does not explicitly settle whether the isomorphism question instead compares groups obtained for different values of $n$. These are questions, not asserted conclusions, and the displayed formulations remain conditional on resolving the missing definitions and exclusions.

Short English statement

For each integer $n>1$, the source asks whether groups generated by $n$ fast one-bump functions, subject to its direct-sum and wreath-product exclusions, have type $F_\infty$, and whether all such groups are isomorphic. The technical hypotheses, exclusions, and scope of the isomorphism comparison require clarification before this becomes a fully specified mathematical statement.
