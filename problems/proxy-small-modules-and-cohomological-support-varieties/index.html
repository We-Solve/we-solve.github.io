Math-3591

Proxy-Small Modules and Cohomological Support Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the module question, use the standard commutative local-algebra convention: $R$ is a commutative Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$. Let $D(R)$ be the derived category of complexes of $R$-modules. For $X\in D(R)$, let $\operatorname{thick}_R(X)$ denote the smallest full triangulated subcategory containing $X$ and closed under direct summands. Interpret the source notation $\langle X\rangle$ in this sense. A complex is perfect if it is isomorphic in $D(R)$ to a bounded complex of finitely generated projective $R$-modules; these are precisely the objects of $\operatorname{thick}_R(R)$.

Define ordinary support by \[ \operatorname{Supp}_R X =\{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}R: H^i(X)_{\mathfrak p}\ne 0\text{ for some }i\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}. \] Regard a finitely generated module $M$ as a complex concentrated in degree zero. It is proxy small if there exists a perfect complex $P$ such that \[ P\in\operatorname{thick}_R(M) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{Supp}_R P=\operatorname{Supp}_R M. \] Equivalently, $P\in\operatorname{thick}_R(M)\cap\operatorname{thick}_R(R)$ with the stated support equality. The interpretation of the angle brackets is a standard convention made explicit here; it is not defined in the supplied excerpt.

Call $R$ a complete intersection if its $\mathfrak m$-adic completion has a presentation \[ \widehat R\cong Q/(f_1,\ldots,f_c), \] where $Q$ is a regular local ring and $f_1,\ldots,f_c$ is a $Q$-regular sequence, with the empty sequence allowed.

Main question. Is the following implication valid for every commutative Noetherian local ring $R$? \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every finitely generated }R\text{-module }M, \text{ there exists }P\in \operatorname{thick}_R(M)\cap\operatorname{thick}_R(R)\\ \text{with }\operatorname{Supp}_R P=\operatorname{Supp}_R M \end{gathered} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad R\text{ is a complete intersection}. \] The supplied report excerpt attributes a characterization involving all $M\in D^b(R)$ to Pollitz and asks whether it can be restricted to finitely generated modules. This attribution is retained without asserting an independently verified theorem.

Further support-variety questions. The original excerpt also asks when \[ V_R(R)={\mathbb{A}}^n \] and, more generally, how small $V_R(M)$ can be. The registry formulation additionally asks which conical subvarieties occur as $V_R(M)$ for $M\in D^b(R)$. These questions are retained as qualified source targets: the supplied record does not define the cohomological support construction $V_R$, its base field or ambient space, the integer $n$, the intended measure of smallness, or whether $D^b(R)$ requires finitely generated cohomology. Consequently, these further questions cannot be given a unique precise formulation from the supplied evidence alone.

Short English statement

Must a commutative Noetherian local ring be a complete intersection if every finitely generated module can build a perfect complex with the same ordinary support through finitely many triangulated operations and direct summands? The record also asks about the size and realizability of cohomological support varieties, but leaves essential conventions for those questions unspecified.
