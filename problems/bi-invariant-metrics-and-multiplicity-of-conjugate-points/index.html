Math-2014

Bi-invariant metrics and multiplicity of conjugate points

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a compact connected Lie group and $g$ a left-invariant Riemannian metric, so $L_a^*g=g$ for every $a\in G$, where $L_a(x)=ax$. The metric is bi-invariant if also $R_a^*g=g$ for every $a$, where $R_a(x)=xa$.

For a geodesic segment $\gamma:[0,T]\to G$, interpret its index as the fixed-endpoint Morse index of the energy: the maximal dimension of a subspace on which the index form \[ I_\gamma(V,V)=\int_0^T\left( |D_tV|^2-\langle R(V,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma,V\rangle \right)\,\mathrm dt \] is negative definite, for $H^1$ vector fields along $\gamma$ vanishing at both endpoints, with the usual compatible curvature sign convention. Zero directions, including endpoint degeneracy, contribute nullity rather than index.

Gorodski's question asks whether \[ \left(\forall\gamma\text{ geodesic segment},\quad \operatorname{ind}(\gamma)\in2{\mathbb{Z}}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad g\text{ is bi-invariant}. \] The parity condition concerns every segment and every segment length, not only closed geodesics or one chosen endpoint. No simple-connectivity or semisimplicity hypothesis is added. The fixed-endpoint interpretation is standard but is not explicitly defined in the short source statement.

Short English statement

Does even Morse index for every geodesic segment characterize bi-invariant metrics among left-invariant metrics on compact connected Lie groups? The source asks for the implication to bi-invariance, without additional group restrictions.

The supplied summary reports no verified direct resolution and cites related rigidity and spectral-isolation results as context only. It explicitly says those do not settle the even-index characterization. The OPEN-TRIAGE label and 2018 question are retained with current status marked for review.
