Vanishing Conjectures for Birational Cohomology of Projective Space

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $F$ be a finite field of characteristic $p$, choose an algebraic closure $\overline F$, and put $G_F=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline F/F)$. Let $\ell\ne p$ be a prime. Write $\mathbb Q_\ell(r)$ for the usual $r$-th Tate twist of the rational $\ell$-adic coefficient sheaf. For a $G_F$-module $V$, write $V^{G_F}=\{v\in V:gv=v\text{ for every }g\in G_F\}$.

The supplied record does not define $F_0$ or specify the cohomology construction. Under the conventional interpretation as cohomology at the generic point, set $X=\mathbb P^n_{\overline F}$ and interpret the notation by \[ H^m(F_0X,\mathbb Q_\ell(r)) :=\varinjlim_{\substack{U\subseteq X\\ U\text{ nonempty Zariski open}}} H^m_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(U,\mathbb Q_\ell(r)), \] where the transition maps are restriction maps and each term is rational $\ell$-adic étale cohomology. The descent of projective space to $F$, together with the Tate twist, induces the $G_F$-action on this direct limit. This interpretation is an explicit formulation convention for this draft; its agreement with the source's intended cohomology theory requires review.

With this qualification, the requested vanishing conjectures are: for every prime $p$, every finite field $F$ of characteristic $p$, every prime $\ell\ne p$, and every pair of integers $i,n\ge 2$, \[ H^{2i}\!\left(F_0\mathbb P^n_{\overline F},\mathbb Q_\ell(i+1)\right)^{G_F}=0 \qquad\text{and}\qquad H^{2i-1}\!\left(F_0\mathbb P^n_{\overline F},\mathbb Q_\ell(i)\right)^{G_F}=0. \] The two integer parameters are independently quantified; the supplied statement imposes no additional relation between $i$ and $n$.

The supplied excerpt provides context rather than a complete formulation of its numbered conjectures. It reports equivalences, for fixed characteristic $p$, between Conjectures 1, 2, and 3 for all smooth projective varieties over finite fields of characteristic $p$ and Conjecture 4, and between Conjectures 1 and 3 over the same class and Conjecture 5; it describes Conjecture 5 as weaker than Conjecture 4. Their definitions are absent, so these numbered statements cannot be reconstructed here. More specifically, the excerpt reports that vanishing of \[ H^4\!\left(F_0\mathbb P^4_{\overline F},\mathbb Q_\ell(3)\right)^{G_F} \] for all finite fields $F$ of characteristic $p$ implies the Tate and 1-semisimplicity conjectures for surfaces and the Beilinson conjecture for threefolds over finite fields of characteristic $p$. Its treatment of the prime $\ell$ in that implication is not explicit in the supplied excerpt. The excerpt calls these the first open cases and states that the Beilinson conjecture is known for surfaces. These are supplied source claims, not independently verified assertions of this draft.

Short English statement

For every finite field, every prime $\ell$ different from its characteristic, and all integers $i,n\ge2$, the conjectures ask for the indicated two groups of Galois-invariant birational cohomology classes of geometric projective space to vanish. The meaning of $F_0$-cohomology requires source confirmation; this draft states an explicit generic-point interpretation. The registry labels the problem open and the statement corrected and verified, but those labels are supplied curation rather than independent verification.
