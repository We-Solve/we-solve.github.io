Kirby Problem 3.57

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for an equivariant homotopy refinement of Némethi's combinatorial lattice theory for plumbed three-manifolds. A plumbing presentation $\Gamma$ specifies a four-manifold built from plumbed disk bundles and its oriented boundary $Y_\Gamma$. The precise class of graphs/bundles, definiteness and homological assumptions are not fixed in the question. In the usual stable-homotopy formulation, the target is a combinatorially constructed lattice spectrum $\mathcal X_\Gamma$ with an $S^1$ or $\operatorname{Pin}(2)$ action and a comparison \[ \mathcal X_\Gamma\simeq_G\operatorname{SWF}(Y_\Gamma) \qquad(G=S^1\text{ or }\operatorname{Pin}(2)), \] with the required spin or spin$^c$ data, representation suspensions and grading normalization specified. Here $S^1$ is the unit complex numbers and $\operatorname{Pin}(2)=S^1\cup jS^1$ in the unit quaternions. The right side denotes the corresponding Seiberg--Witten Floer stable homotopy invariant. Pin(2) symmetry requires appropriate spin/self-conjugacy data and is not asserted for every unspecified spin$^c$ structure.

The comparison should refine the chosen lattice homology theory and compute the equivariant Floer type, rather than merely an isomorphism of homology modules. The source does not specify which lattice homology/cohomology version is recovered, the precise equivariant category or grading shifts, allowed plumbing moves or a fully stated functoriality requirement. These must be fixed before the request becomes one definite construction theorem. Its "or" permits an $S^1$ refinement or a Pin(2) refinement, rather than silently requiring both at once.

The background credits Némethi [Ném05, Ném08], building on Ozsváth--Szabó [OS03b], with lattice invariants for plumbed manifolds. It describes Zemke [Zem25]'s identification of lattice and Heegaard Floer homology for boundaries of plumbing trees as an announcement, while also supplying a Duke Mathematical Journal 2025 publication citation. Both the wording and published bibliographic item are retained; homology agreement is not a full equivariant homotopy comparison.

The notes cite computational applications [CK14, DM19, AKS20], recovery of algebraic-link Floer complexes from multivariable Alexander polynomials [BLZ24], knot lattice homology [OSS14a], and surgery exact sequences [Ném11, Gre13d]. They say cobordism maps for lattice homology had not been defined in the literature under review. They specifically report Dai--Sasahira--Stoffregen [DSS23]'s modified lattice spectrum agreeing with Manolescu's Seiberg--Witten Floer spectrum in a restricted range, but do not supply that range's exact hypotheses and do not establish agreement in all cases. The triage's lack of a verified general model does not erase this supplied restricted-case report.

The source suggests computation of Steenrod squares and equivariant operations as routes to finer numerical and homology-cobordism information for graph manifolds, with [LS14]'s Khovanov refinement as an analogy and Problem 3.56 as a cross-reference. These are proposed applications rather than already computed consequences. The literature triage, checked 2026-08-17, labels the general refinement partially solved and open, and calls for the equivariant lattice construction and SWF comparison. The primary registry status is "open". No new construction or source verification is claimed.

Short English statement

Build a combinatorial $S^1$- or Pin(2)-equivariant lattice spectrum that recovers the Seiberg--Witten Floer stable homotopy type of the intended plumbed manifolds. The source records homology equivalences and restricted spectral comparisons but leaves a general refinement, its scope and conventions open.
