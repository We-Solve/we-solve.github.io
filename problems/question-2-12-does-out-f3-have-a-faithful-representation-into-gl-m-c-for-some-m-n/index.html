Question 2.12 — Does Out(F3) have a faithful representation into GL(m, C) for some m∈ N?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_3$ be the free group of rank $3$, and let \[ \operatorname{Out}(F_3)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_3)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_3). \] A complex linear representation is a group homomorphism into $\operatorname{GL}_m({\mathbb{C}})$, and is faithful when its kernel is trivial.

Question 2.12. Does there exist a finite positive integer $m$ and an injective homomorphism \[ \rho:\operatorname{Out}(F_3)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_m({\mathbb{C}})? \] The rank is exactly $3$, and the coefficient field is exactly ${\mathbb{C}}$. The source imposes no dimension bound, discreteness of the image, or additional geometric condition.

The source notes braid-group linearity [8] and says closed-surface mapping-class-group linearity was unknown in its context. It credits Brendle and Hamidi--Tehrani [13] with showing that the Formanek--Procesi approach cannot be directly adapted there: the particular "poison subgroup" described in earlier, unprovided text does not arise. This does not prove that all obstructions to linearity are absent or that those mapping class groups are linear.

It describes the persistence of the $\operatorname{Out}(F_3)$ question as evidence of behavior different from larger ranks and mentions the existence of finite-index subgroups mapping onto ${\mathbb{Z}}$ as another such instance. The exact comparison ranks and hypotheses are not supplied. Its promised automatic-structure and isoperimetric discussion is absent. All these are source-era comparisons, not new claims of current resolution.

Short English statement

Is the rank-three outer automorphism group of a free group linear over ${\mathbb{C}}$ in some finite dimension? The source's braid, mapping-class, and larger-rank comparisons are retained as context without changing the fixed-rank question.
