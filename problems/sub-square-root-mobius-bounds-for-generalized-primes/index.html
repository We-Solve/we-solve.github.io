Sub-Square-Root Möbius Bounds for Generalized Primes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual discrete Beurling convention, a generalized prime system is a nondecreasing sequence $\mathcal P=(p_j)_{j\geq 1}$ of real numbers with $p_j>1$ and $p_j\to\infty$. Regard its generalized integers as formal products $n=\prod_j p_j^{\nu_j}$, where $\nu_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ and only finitely many exponents are nonzero. Write $|n|=\prod_j p_j^{\nu_j}$ for the numerical value. Distinct formal products are counted separately even when their numerical values coincide. Define \[ \mu_{\mathcal P}(n)= \begin{cases} (-1)^{\sum_j\nu_j},&\nu_j\in\{0,1\}\text{ for every }j,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \] and, for real $x\geq1$, \[ N_{\mathcal P}(x)=\sum_{|n|\leq x}1, \qquad M_{\mathcal P}(x)=\sum_{|n|\leq x}\mu_{\mathcal P}(n). \] The empty product has value $1$ and Möbius value $1$.

First question, under this convention. Does there exist a generalized prime system $\mathcal P$ and a real number $\alpha<\tfrac12$ such that \[ \exists C>0\ \exists x_0\geq1\ \forall x\in{\mathbb{R}},\quad x\geq x_0\ \Longrightarrow\ |M_{\mathcal P}(x)|\leq Cx^\alpha? \] The supplied record does not specify a density hypothesis, an asymptotic normalization for $N_{\mathcal P}$, or further admissibility conditions. In particular, the reference to periodic errors $N(x)-x$ does not state whether a condition such as $N_{\mathcal P}(x)\sim x$ is required in this existence question. The definitions above make the standard discrete interpretation explicit; they do not establish that it captures every hypothesis of the intended report question.

Second question, requiring clarification. Can the construction method involving periodic errors $N(x)-x$ be adapted to better nonperiodic approximations of $\lfloor x\rfloor$? The original statement further requests approximations whose zero real parts have supremum greater than $\tfrac12$. If $Z$ denotes the intended zero set, that wording asks for \[ \sup\{\operatorname{Re}\rho:\rho\in Z\}>\tfrac12. \] The cleaned statement instead describes associated Mellin transforms with zeros whose real parts approach $\tfrac12$. It specifies neither the side of approach nor the supremum of those real parts. These descriptions do not specify an equivalent zero condition. Moreover, the record gives neither the approximation class and meaning of "better", nor the construction method, Mellin-transform formula, analytic continuation, or intended zero set. Consequently this second question cannot be made into a unique quantified mathematical statement from the supplied evidence.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether a Beurling generalized prime system can have summatory Möbius function $O(x^\alpha)$ for some real $\alpha<1/2$. Its intended additional hypotheses are unspecified. A related question concerns adapting periodic constructions to nonperiodic approximations of $\lfloor x\rfloor$, but the approximation criterion, transform, and required zero condition need clarification. The supplied open label is a dated curation claim.
