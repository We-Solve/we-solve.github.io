Math-796

Hyperbolic Orbifold Realizations of Compact PL Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ denote the manifold dimension; the supplied record does not specify its allowed range. A PL manifold is a piecewise-linear manifold, and a PL isomorphism is a homeomorphism that is piecewise linear, with piecewise-linear inverse, with respect to the given PL structures.

Write \[ q(x)=x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2-x_{n+1}^2, \qquad O(n,1)=\{A\in\mathrm{GL}_{n+1}({\mathbb{R}}):q(Ax)=q(x)\text{ for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n+1}\}. \] Use the negative-line model \[ \mathbb H^n=\{[x]\in\mathbb{RP}^{n}:q(x)<0\}, \] with its hyperbolic metric and the induced projective action of $O(n,1)$. A cocompact lattice $\Gamma\subset O(n,1)$ is a discrete subgroup for which $O(n,1)/\Gamma$ is compact. No torsion-free hypothesis is imposed. Accordingly, $\mathbb H^n/\Gamma$ in the question is interpreted as the underlying orbit space of the hyperbolic orbifold, equipped with its quotient PL structure; the requested isomorphism concerns that PL space.

Source question. For every compact PL $n$-manifold $M$ without boundary, does there exist a cocompact lattice $\Gamma\subset O(n,1)$ such that \[ \mathbb H^n/\Gamma\cong_{\mathrm{PL}}M? \] The dimension of the hyperbolic space must equal the dimension of $M$. The source does not impose orientability.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not explicitly require $M$ to be connected or nonempty. Since $\mathbb H^n/\Gamma$ is connected and nonempty, the literal formulation cannot realize disconnected or empty manifolds if those are admitted by the convention for manifolds. Whether connectedness and nonemptiness were intended is therefore unresolved. The range of dimensions is also unspecified. The underlying-space interpretation above follows the supplied orbifold-realization context, but the record does not explicitly define the quotient PL structure.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every compact PL manifold without boundary can be realized, in the same dimension and up to PL isomorphism, as the underlying space of a compact hyperbolic orbifold obtained from a cocompact lattice in $O(n,1)$. Torsion is allowed. The dimension range and the intended connectedness and nonemptiness conventions remain unspecified.
