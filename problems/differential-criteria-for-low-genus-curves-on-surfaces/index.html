Differential Criteria for Low-Genus Curves on Surfaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a smooth projective surface over ${\mathbb{C}}$, let $\pi:X\to{\mathbb{P}}^2_{{\mathbb{C}}}$ be a finite morphism with branch divisor $B$, and fix $g\geq0$. For the following formulation, a curve means a reduced irreducible curve. Its geometric genus is the genus of its smooth projective normalization. Set \[ \mathcal C_g(X)=\{C\subset X:C\text{ is a curve of geometric genus at most }g\}. \] The displayed cohomology group in the source requires $L$ to be an invertible sheaf on ${\mathbb{P}}^2_{{\mathbb{C}}}$. Under this interpretation, write $L\simeq\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^2}(d)$ and define its degree to be $d$. Define \[ \mathcal A_g=\left\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}:\begin{array}{l}\text{there exists }0\ne s\in H^0({\mathbb{P}}^2,\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^2}(d))\\\text{such that }s|_{\pi(C)_{\mathrm{red}}}=0\text{ for every }C\in\mathcal C_g(X)\end{array}\right\}. \] The minimum-degree hypothesis presupposes $\mathcal A_g\ne\varnothing$. When this holds, let $d_{\min}=\min\mathcal A_g$ and let $L\simeq\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^2}(d_{\min})$.

For a positive integer $r$, $S^r\Omega^1_{{\mathbb{P}}^2/{\mathbb{C}}}$ denotes the $r$-th symmetric power of the cotangent sheaf. Given \[ \omega\in H^0\!\left({\mathbb{P}}^2,L\otimes S^r\Omega^1_{{\mathbb{P}}^2/{\mathbb{C}}}\right), \] and a reduced irreducible curve $D\subset{\mathbb{P}}^2$, let $\nu_D:\widetilde D\to D\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}^2$ be its normalization followed by inclusion. Using the usual differential interpretation, call $D$ $\omega$-integral when the image of $\nu_D^*\omega$ under the natural map to \[ H^0\!\left(\widetilde D,\nu_D^*L\otimes(\Omega^1_{\widetilde D/{\mathbb{C}}})^{\otimes r}\right) \] is zero.

The supplied clean statement asks whether the minimum-degree hypothesis guarantees a section $\omega$ in the displayed cohomology group satisfying \[ \begin{aligned} & D\text{ is }\omega\text{-integral} &&\text{for every irreducible component }D\text{ of }B,\\ & \pi(C)_{\mathrm{red}}\text{ is }\omega\text{-integral} &&\text{for every }C\in\mathcal C_g(X). \end{aligned} \] This remains a qualified formulation: the source does not specify the value, range, or quantifier for $r$, and does not explicitly require $\omega\ne0$. The positive-order definition above does not resolve those omissions. The original statement instead asks which hypotheses involving $X$, $B$, and $L$ guarantee an appropriate differential and make a low-genus curve method work; it does not specify that method's required conclusion. The supplied background also mentions completeness of an integral-curve list and a higher-dimensional extension without formulating either target. These broader requests cannot be identified precisely with the clean existence question from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

For a smooth projective complex surface admitting a finite map to ${\mathbb{P}}^2$, consider a plane line bundle of smallest degree having a nonzero section vanishing on every image of a curve of geometric genus at most $g$. The supplied question asks for a twisted symmetric differential whose integral curves include these images and all branch components. The order of the differential, its required nonvanishing, and the broader method and extension targets remain unspecified.
