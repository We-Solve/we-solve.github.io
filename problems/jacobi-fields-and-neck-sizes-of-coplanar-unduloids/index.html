Math-4944

Jacobi Fields and Neck Sizes of Coplanar Unduloids

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the usual Euclidean constant-mean-curvature (CMC) surface setting. A coplanar $k$-unduloid is understood in the standard complete Alexandrov-embedded genus-zero, $k$-ended convention, with unduloid-type ends and a common reflection plane; the exact source class needs verification. The neck parameters $n_i$ are not defined in the excerpt, so their scale must be fixed consistently with its displayed equalities.

For a two-sided CMC immersion $F:M\to{\mathbb{R}}^3$, let $A$ be the second fundamental form and use Jacobi operator \[ J_M=\Delta_M+|A|^2. \] A scalar normal Jacobi field satisfies $J_Mu=0$. Define \[ \mathcal J_{L^2}(M)=\{u\in C^\infty(M):J_Mu=0,\ \int_M|u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A_M<\infty\}. \] The sign convention for the Laplacian is the usual geometric one, with the equivalent kernel unaffected by a simultaneous operator sign change.

The source has four distinct questions:

  1. Are the stated upper bounds on $\dim\mathcal J_{L^2}(M)$ sharp for coplanar $k$-unduloids? The bound's formula, dependence on neck data, and whether sharpness is pointwise or over a family are not supplied.
  2. For a triunduloid, does a nonzero $L^2$ Jacobi field exist in either exceptional neck configuration \[ n_1+n_2+n_3=2\pi, \qquad n_i+n_j=n_\ell\quad\text{with }\{i,j,\ell\}=\{1,2,3\}? \] The source's normalization and the existence/admissibility of surfaces at these equalities must be specified; no positive kernel dimension is inferred merely from equality.
  3. Can the methods extend to other CMC surfaces to bound neck sizes or the dimensions of Jacobi-field spaces? The methods, target surface class, and field growth conditions are not given.
  4. Independently, is every tempered Jacobi field on a CMC surface integrable? Here integrability would mean realization as the normal derivative of a differentiable family of CMC immersions, with the fixed-mean-curvature and admissible surface/deformation class declared. "Tempered" requires an end-growth or weighted-space definition; it is not silently identified with $L^2$, bounded, or one fixed polynomial growth bound.
The last question is not reduced to the first three or restricted to a neck equality by the source. Modding out ambient motions, fixing topology/end data, and varying the mean curvature can change the formulation and require explicit conventions.

The supplied literature triage dated 21 August 2026 reports broad later nondegeneracy results for coplanar CMC surfaces, but says the report's combined sharpness, exceptional-case, extension, and general integrability questions are not all resolved. It does not specify exactly which hypotheses and subquestions the cited theorem settles. No current existence or integrability claim for an exceptional case is inferred from that broad assessment.

Short English statement

The draft preserves four separate targets: sharpness of missing dimension bounds, $L^2$-field existence at the two stated triunduloid equalities, extensions to other CMC surfaces, and integrability of tempered fields. Neck normalization, growth conditions, and deformation classes remain necessary source details.
