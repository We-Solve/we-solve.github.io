Math-4879

Borel–Serre-Type Compactifications for General Lattices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a semisimple Lie group and let $\Gamma\leq G$ be a lattice, meaning a discrete subgroup for which $G/\Gamma$ has finite $G$-invariant volume. Regard $\Gamma$ as a discrete group. A model for the classifying space for proper $\Gamma$-actions, denoted $\underline{E}\Gamma$, is a $\Gamma$-CW complex $Y$ such that, for every subgroup $H\leq\Gamma$, \[ Y^H=\{y\in Y:hy=y\text{ for every }h\in H\} \quad\text{satisfies}\quad \begin{cases} Y^H\text{ is contractible},&H\text{ is finite},\\ Y^H=\varnothing,&H\text{ is infinite}. \end{cases} \] Use cofinite here to mean that $Y$ has finitely many $\Gamma$-orbits of cells. This convention requires confirmation against the source's intended use of the term.

Question. For every lattice $\Gamma$ in a semisimple Lie group $G$, does there exist an analogue of the Borel--Serre compactification that provides a cofinite model $Y$ for $\underline{E}\Gamma$? In particular, the requested construction must satisfy the fixed-point conditions above and have finitely many $\Gamma$-orbits of cells.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not specify what additional geometric, boundary, functoriality, or compactification properties constitute an "analogue of the Borel--Serre compactification." Consequently, the existence of a cofinite model alone does not fully specify the requested target. Nor does the record state conventions concerning connectedness, the centre, or compact factors of $G$, or impose irreducibility, arithmeticity, torsion-freeness, or nonuniformity on $\Gamma$. No such restrictions are added here. The intended scope of the universal quantifier over semisimple Lie groups and the intended meaning of the compactification analogue require mathematical review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every lattice in a semisimple Lie group admits a Borel--Serre-type construction giving a classifying space for proper actions with finitely many cell orbits. Such a space must have contractible fixed-point sets for finite subgroups and no fixed points for infinite subgroups. The required extra properties of the compactification analogue and the precise scope of the Lie groups are unspecified. The supplied partially_solved label is a dated curation assessment, not an independently verified conclusion.
