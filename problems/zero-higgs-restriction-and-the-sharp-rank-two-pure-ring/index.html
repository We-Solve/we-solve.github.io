Math-1485

Zero-Higgs restriction and the sharp rank-two pure ring

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C$ be a smooth projective complex curve of genus $g\geq2$, and take $n\geq2$ and $d\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $\gcd(n,d)=1$. Let $N^d$ be the moduli space of stable rank-$n$, degree-$d$ bundles, and $M^d_{\mathrm{Dol}}$ the corresponding stable Higgs-bundle moduli space, with unrestricted determinant. Choose compatible normalized tautological classes $a_i\in H^{2i}(-;{\mathbb{Q}})$, $2\leq i\leq n$, as intended by the source. Their precise universal-class normalization is not included in this extract. Define the pure rings here to mean the subalgebras $P_{\mathrm{bun}}^d={\mathbb{Q}}[a_2,\ldots,a_n]\subset H^*(N^d;{\mathbb{Q}})$ and $P_{\mathrm{Dol}}^d={\mathbb{Q}}[a_2,\ldots,a_n]\subset H^*(M^d_{\mathrm{Dol}};{\mathbb{Q}})$, not an unqualified identification with an entire Hodge-theoretic pure part.

Hausel's Conjecture 6.20 asks that these graded rings be isomorphic and independent of degree, and proposes a generating-series formula for $P_n(t)=\sum_j\dim P^jt^j$. The supplied aid gives the canonical zero-Higgs restriction $j^*$, where $j(E)=(E,0)$. For \[ S={\mathbb{Q}}[A_2,\ldots,A_n],\quad |A_i|=2i,\qquad I_{\mathrm{Dol}}=\ker(S\to P_{\mathrm{Dol}}^d),\quad I_{\mathrm{bun}}=\ker(S\to P_{\mathrm{bun}}^d), \] it reports $I_{\mathrm{Dol}}\subseteq I_{\mathrm{bun}}$ and the surjection \[ j^*:S/I_{\mathrm{Dol}}\twoheadrightarrow S/I_{\mathrm{bun}}. \] Thus the generator-compatible comparison is equivalent to equality of relation ideals; equality of the graded Hilbert series suffices. This comparison is not proved in arbitrary rank by the reduction alone.

For $n=2$ and every odd $d$, the aid reports \[ P_{\mathrm{Dol}}^d\cong P_{\mathrm{bun}}^d\cong{\mathbb{Q}}[\beta]/(\beta^g),\qquad \beta=a_2,\quad |\beta|=4,\quad\beta^{g-1}\ne0. \] The basis is $1,\beta,\ldots,\beta^{g-1}$, with multiplication truncated at exponent $g$, and $P_2(t)=\sum_{r=0}^{g-1}t^{4r}$. The historical extract cites the degree-one Newstead vanishing and higher-rank vanishing results. Separately, the aid attributes independence of the complex Higgs pure ring in all coprime degrees, preserving normalized tautological generators, to de Cataldo--Maulik--Shen--Zhang (2025). It does not identify that result with the still-required higher-rank bundle/Higgs comparison.

The extraction damages the proposed generating series. Its recoverable architecture uses auxiliary $V_n,Z_n$ with \[ Z_n(t,T)=\exp\left(\sum_{r\geq1}\frac{V_n(t^r)T^r}{r}\right),\qquad \prod_{n\geq1}Z_n(t,T^n)=\sum_{\lambda\in\mathcal P}H^g_\lambda(t)T^{|\lambda|}. \] It prints $H^g_\lambda(t)=t^{4(1-g)n(\lambda')}\prod_{x\in d(\lambda),\,a(x)=0}(1-t^{2h(x)})^{-1}$, with $n(\lambda')$ described using a sum of leg symbols. Arm, leg, hook and transposed-partition conventions, and the placement of the $(t^2-1)$ factor relating $V_n$ to $P_n$, require verification. The printed ingredients also include $t^{2(1-g)n(n-1)}$. The displayed rank-three rational expression is likewise layout damaged. These incomplete normalizations are not promoted to a fully verified coefficient formula.

The appended section 7 is separate. For a linear Hamiltonian action and central regular parameters $\alpha$, it introduces $I_\alpha=\ker\kappa_\alpha$ and an intended common locally-free-locus restriction. Whenever a well-defined invariant locus $U$ contains the relevant level sets and gives a factorization $H_G^*({\mathbb{C}}^N)\to H_G^*(U)\to H_G^*(\mu^{-1}(\alpha))$, one has $\ker(H_G^*({\mathbb{C}}^N)\to H_G^*(U))\subseteq I_\alpha$. The raw $X/G$, already-quotiented $X$, and missing equivariant superscripts are inconsistent; they are not silently repaired into a theorem about a possibly undefined double quotient.

Short English statement

The source conjectures equality and degree independence of tautological pure rings for bundle and Higgs-bundle moduli. The supplied aid gives a zero-Higgs quotient reduction and the exact rank-two ring in odd degree. Its damaged generating-series and appended Kirwan-locus notation remain explicitly unresolved.
