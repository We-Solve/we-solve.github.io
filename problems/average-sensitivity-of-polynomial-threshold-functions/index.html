Average sensitivity of polynomial threshold functions

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 127; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that degree-$d$ Boolean polynomial threshold functions have total influence at most $8d\sqrt n$, uniformly for $1\le d\le n$, assigning positive sign at zero.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For integers $n\ge1$ and $1\le d\le n$, take a multilinear polynomial $p$ with real coefficients and $\deg p\le d$. Set $f(x)=\operatorname{sgn}(p(x))$ on $\{-1,1\}^n$, taking $\operatorname{sgn}(0)=1$. With uniform input and $x^{\oplus i}$ obtained by reversing coordinate $i$, the asserted average-sensitivity estimate is \[ I(f)=\sum_{i=1}^n\Pr[f(x)\ne f(x^{\oplus i})]\le8d\sqrt n. \]
