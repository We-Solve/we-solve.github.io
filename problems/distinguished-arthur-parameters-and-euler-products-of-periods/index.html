Short English statement

The problem asks whether suitable multiplicity-one assumptions make a global invariant functional factor as an Euler product of the local functionals occurring in Plancherel formulas, and whether the local Plancherel spectrum has only $X$-distinguished Arthur parameters. The supplied record leaves the admissible setting, normalizations, parameter condition, and meaning of spectral support unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
