Math-5693

Distribution of elliptic-curve group structures

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $q$ be a prime power and let $E/{\mathbb{F}}_q$ be an elliptic curve. Its rational-point group has the form \[ E({\mathbb{F}}_q)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/m{\mathbb{Z}}\times{\mathbb{Z}}/n{\mathbb{Z}},\qquad m\mid n, \] with $m\mid q-1$ and $|mn-(q+1)|\leq2\sqrt q$. These are necessary structural constraints, not an assertion that every pair satisfying them is realized.

For example, under uniform sampling of ${\mathbb{F}}_q$-isomorphism classes one can define \[ C_q(m,n)=\#\{[E]_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}:E({\mathbb{F}}_q)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/m{\mathbb{Z}}\times{\mathbb{Z}}/n{\mathbb{Z}}\},\qquad P_q(m,n)=\frac{C_q(m,n)}{\sum_{a,b}C_q(a,b)}. \] The source asks to study this type of distribution and identify its correct nonuniform law. It does not prescribe uniform isomorphism-class sampling: weighting each curve by $1/|\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}E|$, sampling equation coefficients, or conditioning on ordinary/supersingular curves gives different measures. The display is one explicit candidate model.

A limiting law also needs an asymptotic regime and a normalization of the changing groups: $q$ through primes, all prime powers, or fixed-characteristic extensions are different questions. Fixed-field exact counts, asymptotic frequencies, and pointwise uniform estimates are distinct targets. No such regime, error term or conjectural density is supplied, so the request is a distribution programme rather than one specified limiting theorem.

Short English statement

Determine how elliptic-curve rational-point group types are distributed, using a specified sampling measure and finite-field asymptotic regime. Exact counts, cyclic frequencies and a limiting nonuniform law are separate questions.
