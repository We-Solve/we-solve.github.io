Math-3557

Existence of Solutions to the Inverse $\sigma_k$ Equation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension $n$, and identify the Kähler metrics $\omega$ and $\chi$ with their smooth, real, closed, positive $(1,1)$-forms. Write $[\chi]$ for the real de Rham cohomology class of $\chi$, and write $\alpha^j$ for the $j$-fold wedge product of a form $\alpha$, with $\alpha^0=1$.

The supplied record does not specify the range of the integer $k$. The displayed equation requires $0\leq k\leq n$; the intended range within these bounds remains to be confirmed. For any such fixed $k$, integration of the proposed equation forces the normalization \[ c_k=\binom{n}{k}\frac{\displaystyle\int_M\chi^{n-k}\wedge\omega^k}{\displaystyle\int_M\chi^n}. \] This expression depends only on the cohomology classes of $\chi$ and $\omega$. It gives the normalization required by the displayed equation; the supplied record calls $c_k$ the associated topological constant without explicitly defining it.

Existence question. Given $(M,\omega)$, $\chi$, and an intended index $k$ as above, does there exist a smooth Kähler form $\widetilde\chi$ with $[\widetilde\chi]=[\chi]$ such that the following equality of differential forms holds everywhere on $M$? \[ c_k\widetilde\chi^n =\binom{n}{k}\widetilde\chi^{n-k}\wedge\omega^k. \] No connectedness hypothesis is explicitly supplied. If disconnected manifolds are included, the same constant must satisfy the equation on every component, and integration on each component imposes the corresponding componentwise compatibility condition.

The supplied dated curation attributes solvability under a cone condition to Fang, Lai, and Ma, and says that the report leaves the case where that condition fails open. The cone condition itself is not supplied, so that residual case cannot be stated precisely from this record. These are supplied literature qualifications, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a given Kähler class contains a Kähler metric satisfying the displayed inverse $\sigma_k$ equation. The intended range of $k$ and the cone condition mentioned in the supplied partial-solvability assessment require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
