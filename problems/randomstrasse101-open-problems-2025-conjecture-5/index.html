Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2025) — Conjecture 5

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal P$ denote the intended unbounded set of admissible parameters $p$, and let $\Phi_p=[\varphi_{p,1}\ \cdots\ \varphi_{p,N_p}]\in\mathbb K_p^{d_p\times N_p}$ denote the intended Paley equiangular tight frame, where $\mathbb K_p\in\{{\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}}\}$. The supplied record does not specify $\mathcal P$, the construction, its dimensions, or its scalar field. An equiangular tight frame, after common rescaling to unit column norms, satisfies \[ \|\varphi_{p,j}\|_2=1,\qquad \Phi_p\Phi_p^*=\frac{N_p}{d_p}I_{d_p},\qquad |\langle\varphi_{p,i},\varphi_{p,j}\rangle|=\mu_p\quad(i\ne j) \] for some $\mu_p\ge0$. Common rescaling does not change the condition numbers below.

For $S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N_p\}$, let $\Phi_{p,S}$ be the matrix consisting of the distinct columns indexed by $S$. Define \[ \sigma_{\max}(B)=\sup_{\|x\|_2=1}\|Bx\|_2,\qquad \sigma_{\min}(B)=\inf_{\|x\|_2=1}\|Bx\|_2, \] with $x$ ranging over the column-coordinate space of $B$, and define $\kappa_2(B)=\sigma_{\max}(B)/\sigma_{\min}(B)$, taking $\kappa_2(B)=+\infty$ when $\sigma_{\min}(B)=0$.

Conditional formulation of the supplied conjecture. Once the intended Paley family is specified, and if the source notation $m\sim p^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ means asymptotic equivalence, the condition-number assertion can be written as follows: there exist $\varepsilon>0$, $K\ge1$, $p_0>0$, and positive integers $m_p\le N_p$, indexed by $p\in\mathcal P$, such that \[ \lim_{\substack{p\to\infty\\p\in\mathcal P}} \frac{m_p}{p^{1/2+\varepsilon}}=1 \] and \[ \forall p\in\mathcal P,\ p\ge p_0,\qquad \forall S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N_p\},\quad |S|=m_p\ \Longrightarrow\ \kappa_2(\Phi_{p,S})\le K. \] Thus the constant must be uniform over both the parameter and every selection of the prescribed number of distinct columns. This bound in particular requires every such selected matrix to be injective.

The record calls this the restricted isometry property beyond the square-root bottleneck. For comparison, the usual unit-column restricted isometry condition of order $m_p$, with a uniform constant $0\le\delta<1$, is \[ (1-\delta)\|x\|_2^2\le\|\Phi_p x\|_2^2 \le(1+\delta)\|x\|_2^2 \quad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb K_p^{N_p} \text{ having at most }m_p\text{ nonzero coordinates}. \] The supplied record gives no distortion threshold or normalization convention for this terminology. Its claim that this is an equivalent restatement of uniformly bounded condition numbers therefore needs clarification. The displayed conjecture formalizes its explicit condition-number wording under the stated interpretation of $\sim$; it does not resolve the missing construction data or assert that the two formulations are equivalent.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture predicts that every selection of about $p^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ distinct columns from the intended Paley equiangular tight frame has Euclidean condition number bounded uniformly in $p$, for some fixed $\varepsilon>0$. The admissible parameters, exact frame construction, meaning of the size asymptotic, and intended restricted isometry convention require clarification. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied status information, not an independently verified assessment.
