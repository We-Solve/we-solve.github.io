Optimal Character-Ratio Bounds for Almost Quasisimple Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group. Write \[ Z(G)=\{z\in G:zx=xz\text{ for every }x\in G\} \] for its center, and let $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ denote the irreducible complex characters of $G$. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, its degree is $\chi(1)$; the character is nonlinear when $\chi(1)>1$. A finite group $H$ is quasisimple if $H=[H,H]$ and $H/Z(H)$ is nonabelian simple. The supplied record does not define its convention for almost quasisimple; the intended class of groups must therefore be confirmed before this becomes a fully specified statement.

For every finite almost quasisimple group $G$, in the source's intended sense, and every $g\in G\setminus Z(G)$, the requested bounds are as follows.

  1. (A) Find an explicit real number $\gamma(g)$, as small as possible, such that \[ 0<\gamma(g)<1, \qquad \frac{|\chi(g)|}{\chi(1)}\leq\gamma(g) \quad\text{for every }\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G) \text{ with }\chi(1)>1. \]
  2. (B) Find an explicit real number $\alpha(g)$, as small as possible, such that \[ 0<\alpha(g)<1, \qquad |\chi(g)|\leq\chi(1)^{\alpha(g)} \quad\text{for every }\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G). \]
Here $G$ is fixed when writing $\gamma(g)$ and $\alpha(g)$. Each bound must hold simultaneously for all characters in its stated range; the record does not specify which group or element invariants an explicit expression may use.

Unresolved qualifications. The original excerpt asks for bounds that are "as small as possible," whereas the cleaned statement asks for the "smallest explicit" bounds. No definition of explicitness, admissible family of expressions, or guarantee that a minimum exists within the open interval $(0,1)$ is supplied. The optimization request is retained without asserting existence of such minima. The precise convention for almost quasisimple groups, and whether the stated noncentrality condition ensures the requested strict bounds for every character in the indicated ranges, require mathematical review. No faithfulness hypothesis or restriction on character kernels is supplied.

Source context, not independently verified. The excerpt reports that a combination of results attributed to Gluck, Gluck--Magaard, and Guralnick with the unnamed speaker permits $\gamma=79/80$, unless $E(G)=A_n$. Here $E(G)$ denotes the layer, generated by the subnormal quasisimple subgroups of $G$, and $A_n$ denotes an alternating group. The precise hypotheses and meaning of the displayed exceptional case must be checked against the report; the supplied record does not give the referenced bibliography entries.

The excerpt then focuses on finite classical groups $G=\operatorname{Cl}(V)$, with $V={\mathbb{F}}_q^n$, and discusses support using codimensions of kernels of $g-\lambda I$ after scalar extension. The scalar-field notation and the numerical support-dependent character bound are damaged in the supplied transcription, so no precise auxiliary formula is asserted here. That auxiliary passage ranges over nontrivial irreducible characters and all elements of the classical group, a scope distinct from the two requested bounds above.

Short English statement

For each finite almost quasisimple group and each noncentral element, seek explicit optimal bounds on all nonlinear irreducible character ratios and on all irreducible character values as powers of their degrees. The definition of almost quasisimple, feasibility of strict bounds, and meaning and attainment of optimality require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
