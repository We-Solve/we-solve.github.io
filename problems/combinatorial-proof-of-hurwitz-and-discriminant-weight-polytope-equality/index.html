Math-6898

Combinatorial Proof of Hurwitz and Discriminant Weight-Polytope Equality

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a specified split algebraic torus $T$ over a field $k$, let $M=\operatorname{Hom}(T,\mathbb G_m)$ be its character lattice. If a nonzero polynomial $F$ belongs to a specified finite-dimensional $T$-representation and has weight decomposition \[ F=\sum_{\chi\in M}F_\chi,\qquad t\cdot F_\chi=\chi(t)F_\chi, \] its weight polytope is \[ W_T(F)=\operatorname{conv}\{\chi\in M:F_\chi\ne0\}\subset M\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}. \] This definition requires the representation, including the action on polynomial coordinates, to be fixed.

The supplied problem asks for a purely combinatorial proof of the coincidence \[ W\!\left(\Delta_{X\times\mathbb P^{n-1}}\right) = W\!\left(\operatorname{Hu}_A\right), \] where the source designates $\Delta_{X\times\mathbb P^{n-1}}$ as a discriminant and $\operatorname{Hu}_A$ as a Hurwitz form. The requested object is a combinatorial proof of this equality, rather than a new assertion that the equality holds.

Unresolved scope. The supplied record does not define $A$, its relation to $X$, the base field, the dimension and embedding of $X$, the meaning or range of $n$, the embedding of $X\times\mathbb P^{n-1}$, or the hypotheses ensuring that the designated discriminant and Hurwitz form exist as nonzero defining polynomials. The projective-space notation requires $n$ to be a positive integer, but the intended range is not supplied. The torus actions and the identification of the weight spaces in which the displayed equality is asserted are also missing. Accordingly, the equation records the intended target; it does not specify an unconditional theorem for all varieties or all configurations. A fully quantified formulation requires recovery of these data from the source context. No definition of "purely combinatorial proof" or permitted proof ingredients is supplied.

Short English statement

The problem requests a purely combinatorial proof that the discriminant of $X\times\mathbb P^{n-1}$ and the Hurwitz form associated with $A$ have the same weight polytope. The supplied curation says that the equality is established and labels the requested combinatorial reproof open. The geometric hypotheses and the common weight-space identification are missing, so the precise scope requires human review.
