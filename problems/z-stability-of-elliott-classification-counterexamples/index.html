Z-Stability of Elliott Classification Counterexamples

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal Z$ denote the Jiang--Su algebra. A complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ is $\mathcal Z$-stable, or absorbs $\mathcal Z$, if \[ A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z \] as $C^*$-algebras. Under the usual convention, an elementary $C^*$-algebra is one isomorphic to $\mathcal K(H)$, the algebra of compact operators on a nonzero complex Hilbert space $H$; nonelementary means not of this form. The supplied record does not explicitly specify this convention.

The phrase "counterexample to the Elliott classification conjecture" does not specify a unique class of algebras in the supplied record. Write $\mathscr C$ provisionally for the intended class of counterexample algebras. With that unresolved parameter, the proposed assertion is \[ \forall A\in\mathscr C,\qquad \left(A\text{ is nonelementary}\right) \Longrightarrow A\not\cong A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z. \] For example, if counterexample means membership in a pair violating classification by a specified invariant $\operatorname{Ell}$ on a specified class $\mathscr D$, one would first need to define \[ \mathscr C= \left\{A\in\mathscr D: \exists B\in\mathscr D,\quad \operatorname{Ell}(A)\cong\operatorname{Ell}(B) \ \text{and}\ A\not\cong B \right\}. \] This is a possible specification of the missing parameter, not a choice supplied by the source. The record does not identify $\mathscr D$, the precise invariant, the meaning of its isomorphisms, or whether this pair-based meaning of counterexample is intended.

To express the second formulation, fix a free ultrafilter $\omega$ on ${\mathbb{N}}$ and, for a unital $C^*$-algebra $A$, use the norm ultrapower convention \[ \ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},A)=\{(a_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}:\sup_n\|a_n\|<\infty\}, \qquad I_\omega=\{(a_n)\in\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},A):\lim_{n\to\omega}\|a_n\|=0\}, \] \[ A_\omega=\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},A)/I_\omega, \qquad A_\omega\cap A' =\{x\in A_\omega:xa=ax\text{ for every }a\in A\}, \] where $A$ is identified with its constant-sequence image. A unital embedding means an injective unital $*$-homomorphism. The source's second proposed assertion is \[ \forall A\in\mathscr C,\qquad \left(A\text{ is unital and nonelementary}\right) \Longrightarrow \nexists\text{ a unital embedding } \mathcal Z\longrightarrow A_\omega\cap A'. \] The ultrapower convention and the choice and quantification of $\omega$ are not specified in the record. The source calls this formulation equivalent to nonabsorption, but does not supply the hypotheses needed to justify that equivalence. In particular, separability is not stated, and the unital formulation does not itself address nonunital algebras. Both displayed assertions therefore remain provisional formulations of the supplied question, pending specification of the intended classification setting and applicable equivalence hypotheses. The supplied dated assessment describes the underlying report assertion as contextual information about known counterexamples and does not establish the universal claim.

Short English statement

The proposed question asks whether every nonelementary counterexample to the intended Elliott classification conjecture fails to absorb the Jiang--Su algebra $\mathcal Z$. For unital algebras, the source also asks whether $\mathcal Z$ fails to embed unitally into $A_\omega\cap A'$. The class of counterexamples and the hypotheses supporting the claimed equivalence are unspecified. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
