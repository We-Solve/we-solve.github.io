Kirby Problem 4.63

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual setting of Seiberg--Witten invariants, let $X$ be a closed connected oriented smooth $4$-manifold with $\pi_1(X)=1$, with the additional invariant/chamber choices required below. Let $Q_X$ be the integral intersection form on $H_2(X;{\mathbb{Z}})$, and let $b_2(X)$ and $b_2^\pm(X)$ be its rank and positive/negative indices. The form is indefinite when both indices are positive.

For the chosen Seiberg--Witten invariant, let $\mathcal B(X)=\{c_1(\mathfrak s):\operatorname{SW}_X(\mathfrak s)\ne0\}\subseteq H^2(X;{\mathbb{Z}})$, where $\mathfrak s$ is a spin-$c$ structure. Define \[\operatorname{br}(X)=\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\operatorname{Span}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\{b\otimes1:b\in\mathcal B(X)\}\subseteq H^2(X;{\mathbb{Q}}),\] with value zero if there are no basic classes. This is the rank-of-span notion in the source, not the number of basic classes. Closedness, orientation, invariant version, and homology-orientation conventions are not explicit in the stored statement. In particular $b_2^+=1$ is included in its odd-$b_2^+$ range and requires chamber choices; none is silently supplied or removed by restricting to $b_2^+>1$.

The three questions are:

  • (a) Find an upper bound for $\operatorname{br}(X)$ in terms of topological invariants of $X$. The tautological bound $0\leq\operatorname{br}(X)\leq b_2(X)$ follows from its definition; to be a nontrivial open request this must seek additional strength, but the source gives no exact required improvement beyond part (b).
  • (b) Is $\operatorname{br}(X)\leq b_2^+(X)$ for every simply connected $X$ in the intended scope with $b_2^+(X)$ odd?
  • (c) Does there exist an indefinite simply connected smooth $X$ with $b_2(X)\geq3$ such that the induced diffeomorphism action on $Q_X$ has finite image, or even trivial image?
For oriented $X$, an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism acts on $H_2(X;{\mathbb{Z}})$ by an integral isometry, giving $ev_*:\operatorname{Diff}^+(X)\to\operatorname{Aut}(Q_X)$. The source writes $\operatorname{Diff}(X)$ without a superscript; whether it means this group must be checked, since an orientation-reversing map generally reverses the intersection pairing. Trivial image means every allowed diffeomorphism acts as the identity, a stronger requirement than finite image.

The source describes Fintushel--Stern knot surgery on independent disjoint tori under nonvanishing Seiberg--Witten hypotheses as producing independent basic-class directions, and cites Gompf--Mrowka examples with $\operatorname{br}(X)=b_2^+(X)$. Those examples show the proposed bound's possible sharpness rather than prove it universally. The notes explain that diffeomorphisms permute basic classes up to sign; a basic-class span of full rank $b_2(X)$ would force the isometry image to be finite. In the indefinite setting $b_2^+(X)<b_2(X)$, so the cited rank-$b_2^+$ examples alone do not establish that full-rank mechanism or part (c). The indefinite/rank-at-least-three restriction is intended to avoid a trivially finite ambient form automorphism group.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-17 labels the programme partially solved / PARTIAL-PROGRESS and reports neither the intended stronger general bound nor the required finite/trivial-image example verified. The registry is open. The literal topological scope, chamber, action-group, and nontrivial-strength conventions remain explicit formulation issues.

Short English statement

How large can the span of Seiberg--Witten basic classes be, especially compared with $b_2^+$, and can their constraints make the diffeomorphism action on an indefinite intersection form finite or trivial? The source reports sharp-rank examples but no universal inequality or required action example, with several scope and chamber conventions missing.
