Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 9

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt does not define $I_k(T)$ or $a_3$. The following formulation is conditional on their customary normalization for moments of the Riemann zeta function; this normalization requires confirmation against the complete source.

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the meromorphic continuation of $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}$, initially defined for $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$. For a positive integer $k$ and a real number $T>0$, set \[ I_k(T)=\int_0^T\left|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)\right|^{2k}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] Under the customary arithmetic-factor normalization, set \[ a_3=\prod_{p\ \mathrm{prime}}\left(1-\frac1p\right)^9 \sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\binom{r+2}{2}^{\!2}p^{-r}. \] The excerpt attributes to Soundararajan, reference [S], the lower bound \[ I_3(T)\geq \frac{20.26\,a_3}{9!}(1+o(1))T(\log T)^9 \qquad(T\to\infty). \] The principal problem is to exhibit a real constant $C>20.26$ and prove that, for every real $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $T_0>1$ such that every real $T\geq T_0$ satisfies \[ I_3(T)\geq \frac{(C-\varepsilon)a_3}{9!}\,T(\log T)^9. \] Equivalently, prove \[ \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{9!\,I_3(T)}{a_3T(\log T)^9}\geq C>20.26. \] The source describes $42$ as the best possible coefficient. This is retained as the source's stated target, without asserting an independently verified upper bound or asymptotic formula.

The source also asks for improvements to lower bounds for $I_k(T)$ for $k>3$. Under the interpretation above, $k$ ranges over integers at least $4$; the supplied excerpt gives neither the relevant baseline bounds nor their constants, so this additional request remains unspecified quantitatively.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly provisional standard normalization, improve the coefficient $20.26$ in the lower bound for the sixth moment of the Riemann zeta function. The source gives $42$ as the optimal target and also requests improvements for higher moments, without specifying their baseline bounds.
