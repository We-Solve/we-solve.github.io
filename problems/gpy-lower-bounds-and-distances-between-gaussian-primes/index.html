Math-9667

GPY lower bounds and distances between Gaussian primes

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Can the Goldston--Pintz--Yıldırım method produce an asymptotic count of primes with specified spacing, or at least a lower bound of the correct order? The latter asks for a positive proportion of the admissible tuples under consideration to contain at least two primes. A second question asks whether the method can be adapted to obtain small distances between Gaussian primes.

Formulation references: [S1], [S2].

Short English statement

Can the Goldston--Pintz--Yıldırım method produce an asymptotic count of primes with specified spacing, or at least a lower bound of the correct order? The latter asks for a positive proportion of the admissible tuples under consideration to contain at least two primes. A second question asks whether the method can be adapted to obtain small distances between Gaussian primes.
