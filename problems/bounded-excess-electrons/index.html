Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For the $N$-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian with nuclear charge $Z$, let $N_0(Z)$ be the least $N$ after which adding electrons no longer lowers the ground-state energy. Prove that $N_0(Z)-Z$ remains bounded as $Z\to\infty$.

For the $N$-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian with nuclear charge $Z$, let $N_0(Z)$ be the least $N$ after which adding electrons no longer lowers the ground-state energy. Prove that $N_0(Z)-Z$ remains bounded as $Z\to\infty$.

Bounded Excess Electrons

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Coulomb molecules are claimed to bind at most $Z+CM$ electrons, and neutral atoms to have universally bounded first ionization energy and half-electron exterior radius.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For $M$ fixed nuclei at distinct positions $R_j\in\mathbb R^3$, charges $z_j\ge1$, and total $Z=\sum_jz_j$, use the antisymmetric spin-two electron Hamiltonian \[ H_n=\sum_{i=1}^n\left(-\tfrac12\Delta_i-\sum_j\frac{z_j}{|x_i-R_j|}\right) +\sum_{i<j}\frac1{|x_i-x_j|},\qquad E_n=\inf\sigma(H_n),\quad E_0=0. \] The claimed constant $C<\infty$ is universal over charges and positions, and obeys \[ E_n<E_{n-1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n\le Z+CM, \qquad n>Z+CM\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_n=E_{n-1}. \] For a single nucleus the strict-binding bound becomes $n\le Z+C$, including real $Z\ge1$.

Outline of the argument

Ground states are compared across particle sectors using common localization and screening fields. Local count bounds, changes of observation law, and estimates in nuclear voids control excess particles; deterministic comparison fields give both sides of the neutral-atom energy and radius bounds.
