Kourovka Notebook Problem 8.45

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathfrak X$ and $\mathfrak Y$ be classes of groups, and let $\mathfrak X\cap\mathfrak Y$ denote the class of groups belonging to both. Using the quotient convention, a group $G$ is residually a $\mathfrak C$-group if \[ \forall g\in G\setminus\{1\}\;\exists N\trianglelefteq G \quad\left(g\notin N\ \text{and}\ G/N\in\mathfrak C\right). \] Thus each nonidentity element must survive in some quotient belonging to $\mathfrak C$; the quotient may depend on the element.

The supplied problem asks under what conditions the implication \[ \left\{ \begin{aligned} &G\text{ is residually a }\mathfrak X\text{-group},\\ &G\text{ is residually a }\mathfrak Y\text{-group} \end{aligned} \right. \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ is residually a }(\mathfrak X\cap\mathfrak Y)\text{-group} \] holds. Explicitly, its conclusion requires \[ \forall g\in G\setminus\{1\}\;\exists N\trianglelefteq G \quad\left(g\notin N\ \text{and}\ G/N\in\mathfrak X\cap\mathfrak Y\right), \] whereas the hypotheses allow separate quotients in $\mathfrak X$ and in $\mathfrak Y$.

A class-level interpretation is to characterize the pairs $(\mathfrak X,\mathfrak Y)$ for which this implication holds for every group $G$. The source does not specify whether the requested conditions concern only these classes, the group $G$, or both. It supplies no closure assumptions on the classes and no explicit convention for residuality. Consequently, the displayed formulation uses a stated convention and preserves the request for conditions; it does not assert that the implication holds for arbitrary classes and groups.

Short English statement

Determine when being residually in each of two classes of groups guarantees being residually in their intersection. Under the quotient convention, every nonidentity element must survive in a quotient belonging to both classes. The source leaves the permitted classes and the scope of the requested conditions unspecified.
