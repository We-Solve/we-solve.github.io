Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 37

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth $3$-manifold and let $r$ denote the regularity parameter intended by the source. Write $\mathscr F^r(M)$ for the space of codimension-one $C^r$ foliations on $M$ that are oriented and co-oriented: the tangent planes to the leaves have a continuous orientation, and the normal line bundle has a continuous orientation. The admissible values of $r$, the hypotheses on $M$, boundary conventions, and the precise $C^r$ topology on this space are not specified in the supplied record.

A leaf $L$ is a connected maximal integral surface of a foliation. For an orientable surface $L$, interpret its genus as \[ g(L)=\sup\{g(K):K\subseteq L\text{ is a compact connected subsurface}\}\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}. \] This convention includes noncompact leaves; its intended use requires confirmation from the source.

Under the usual interpretation of genericity, a subset is residual if its complement is a countable union of nowhere dense sets. With the scope and topology above still to be specified, the requested assertion is that there exists a residual subset $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathscr F^r(M)$ such that \[ \forall\mathcal F\in\mathcal G\quad \forall L\text{ a leaf of }\mathcal F,\qquad g(L)<2. \] Equivalently, every leaf of every foliation in this residual subset has genus $0$ or $1$. The residual subset concerns entire foliations, and the conclusion concerns every leaf of each such foliation. This is a formulation scheme: the supplied record does not determine the range of manifolds or regularities over which it is asserted.

Short English statement

Question 37 asks whether a $C^r$-generic oriented and co-oriented codimension-one foliation of a $3$-manifold has only leaves of genus $0$ or $1$. The regularity range, manifold hypotheses, topology for genericity, and genus convention for noncompact leaves require clarification.
