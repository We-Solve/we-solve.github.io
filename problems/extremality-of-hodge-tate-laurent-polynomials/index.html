Extremality of Hodge–Tate Laurent Polynomials

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Laurent polynomial in finitely many variables has the form \[ f(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\sum_{m\in S}c_m x_1^{m_1}\cdots x_n^{m_n},\qquad S\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}^n\text{ finite}. \] The supplied record does not specify the coefficient domain, the allowed values of $n$, or additional admissibility hypotheses.

Conjecture, with unresolved source-dependent terminology. Every Laurent polynomial that is Hodge--Tate at infinity is extremal.
