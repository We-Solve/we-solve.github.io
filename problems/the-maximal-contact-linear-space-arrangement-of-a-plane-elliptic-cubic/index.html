Definitions and mathematical statement

The entire original item is the setup sentence: "Problem 3 (Suggested by Michel) Let $X\subset{\mathbb{P}}^2$ be an elliptic curve." It contains no question, conclusion to prove, or requested quantity. An elliptic curve is a smooth projective geometrically connected genus-one curve with the appropriate field/point convention; the supplied embedding is the only additional datum. The field and chosen origin are not specified. No problem is invented from the later registry title.

The supplied research aid reports that the original PDF confirms this is setup for a separately numbered next problem. It nevertheless gives an auxiliary structural proposition, distinct from the original item. In that proposition take a smooth plane cubic $E$ with a flex origin $o$ and a positive degree $d$. It claims that degree-$d$ plane curves not containing $E$ and supported on a single intersection point $p$ exist precisely when $[3d](p-o)=0$, equivalently when the contact divisor is $3d[p]$ and $\mathcal O_E(d)\cong\mathcal O_E(3d[p])$ under the flex normalization. "$3d$-torsion" means killed by $3d$, not necessarily of exact order $3d$.

Writing $g_d=(d-1)(d-2)/2$, it reports that the projective closure of each fixed-$p$ parameter locus is a linear ${\mathbb{P}}^{g_d}$ and the non-containing locus is ${\mathbb{A}}^{g_d}$. For distinct eligible points, every pair of closures is claimed to intersect scheme-theoretically in the same core of forms divisible by the equation of $E$, denoted ${\mathbb{P}}^{g_d-1}$. When $d=1,2$, this notation requires the empty-core convention ${\mathbb{P}}^{-1}=\varnothing$, which the summary does not spell out.

Over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, the report counts $(3d)^2$ eligible points and hence a disjoint union of that many affine loci after deleting the core. It also claims an affine-bundle version over a smooth family of cubics, without specifying flex/torsion markings or base-change assumptions. It explicitly treats rationality as a separate delta-invariant condition and claims no enumerative count for the next record. All this is unreviewed auxiliary content, not a reconstructed missing question or a solution of the separately numbered problem.
