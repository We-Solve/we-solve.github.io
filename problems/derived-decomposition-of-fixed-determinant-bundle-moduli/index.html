Derived Decomposition of Fixed-Determinant Bundle Moduli

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a scheme $X$, write $D^b(X)=D^b(\operatorname{Coh}(X))$ for the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves, and write $D^b(k)$ for the bounded derived category of finite-dimensional vector spaces over the ground field $k$. For $i\geq 0$, let $\operatorname{Sym}^i C=C^i/\mathfrak S_i$, with $\operatorname{Sym}^0 C=\operatorname{Spec}k$.

Conditional formulation. Fix the ground field $k$ intended by the source. Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve of genus $g$, and let $L$ be a line bundle on $C$ with $\deg L=1$. To interpret the displayed indexing, assume $g\geq 2$; to specify the usual moduli-space interpretation, let $M=M_C(2,L)$ be the coarse moduli space of stable rank-two vector bundles $E$ on $C$ satisfying $\det E\simeq L$. Here stability means that every line subbundle $F\subset E$ satisfies $\deg F<\deg(E)/2=1/2$. The genus restriction and stable coarse-moduli interpretation are supplementary qualifications requiring confirmation, since the supplied statement does not explicitly give them. The ground field is also unresolved; this formulation does not assert the conjecture over every field.

Does $D^b(M)$ admit a full semiorthogonal decomposition \[ D^b(M)=\left\langle \mathcal A_0^{(1)},\mathcal A_0^{(2)}, \mathcal A_1^{(1)},\mathcal A_1^{(2)},\ldots, \mathcal A_{g-2}^{(1)},\mathcal A_{g-2}^{(2)}, \mathcal B \right\rangle, \] where \[ \mathcal A_i^{(a)}\simeq D^b(\operatorname{Sym}^i C) \quad(0\leq i\leq g-2,\ a\in\{1,2\}), \qquad \mathcal B\simeq D^b(\operatorname{Sym}^{g-1}C)? \] Each component is to be realized as a full admissible triangulated subcategory through a fully faithful exact functor. If the ordered components are $\mathcal T_1,\ldots,\mathcal T_{2g-1}$, semiorthogonality means \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{D^b(M)}(V,U[n])=0 \quad\text{for all }j>i,\ U\in\mathcal T_i,\ V\in\mathcal T_j,\ n\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] Fullness means that these components generate all of $D^b(M)$ as a triangulated category closed under direct summands, with no additional complementary component. For $g=2$, the list consists precisely of the two $i=0$ components followed by $\mathcal B$.

The two $i=0$ components are exactly the two displayed copies of $D^b(k)$. The supplied original wording additionally says "together with the indicated exceptional" $D^b({\mathbb{C}})$ components, which could suggest further components, whereas the cleaned display contains no extra components beyond those already counted. The displayed question above follows the cleaned display; the intended component count requires source review. Cases $g=0,1$ are not specified by that display.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the derived category of the intended rank-two fixed-determinant bundle moduli space is generated semiorthogonally by two copies of the derived category of each symmetric power $\operatorname{Sym}^i C$, for $0\leq i\leq g-2$, and one copy for $i=g-1$. The ground field, genus range, stability and moduli conventions, and potentially ambiguous exceptional-component count require confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified here.
