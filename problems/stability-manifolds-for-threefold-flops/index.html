Stability Manifolds for Threefold Flops

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f\colon X\to Y$ be a threefold flopping contraction in the intended setup of the supplied source. Write $\mathcal D$ for its associated triangulated category and \[ B=\widehat E\setminus\widehat{\mathcal H} \] for the associated complexified hyperplane complement, where $\widehat{\mathcal H}$ denotes the removed hyperplane locus. The supplied record does not specify the construction of $\mathcal D$, $\widehat E$, or $\widehat{\mathcal H}$, or the full hypotheses on $f$. Thus the questions below are relative to the intended source setup, rather than a fully specified class of contractions.

Let $\operatorname{Stab}(\mathcal D)$ denote the space of Bridgeland stability conditions on $\mathcal D$, and let \[ S=\operatorname{Stab}^{\circ}(\mathcal D) \] be the distinguished connected component referred to in the source. The precise convention for stability conditions and any restriction on their central charges are not supplied. For a base point $b\in B$, the supplied excerpt attributes to Theorem 2 a mutation homomorphism \[ \phi\colon\pi_1(B,b)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal D) \] and a regular covering map \[ p\colon S\longrightarrow B, \qquad \operatorname{Deck}(p)\cong\operatorname{im}(\phi). \] Here $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal D)$ denotes the intended group of categorical autoequivalences; its precise conventions are not specified. The excerpt states that monodromy around a root hyperplane corresponds to the mutation autoequivalences of its reference [13]. It further states that, for a threefold flopping contraction, these mutations have a geometric interpretation through Bridgeland--Chen flop functors. These assertions are supplied background, not independently verified here.

Questions. For the intended threefold-flop setup, determine the answers to the following questions:

  1. Is $p\colon S\to B$ the universal covering map?
  2. Is the mutation action faithful, that is, \[ \ker(\phi)=\{1\}? \] The source presents this as equivalent to the preceding question. That equivalence depends on the intended relationship between $\phi$ and covering monodromy; an abstract identification of the deck group with $\operatorname{im}(\phi)$ alone does not specify that relationship.
  3. Is the entire stability manifold connected, equivalently, \[ \operatorname{Stab}(\mathcal D)=S? \]
  4. Is $S$ contractible, meaning homotopy equivalent to a point?

The reconstructed statement also asks whether $B$ is a $K(\pi,1)$ space, with $\pi=\pi_1(B,b)$. In the usual setting of connected spaces admitting universal covers and having CW homotopy type, this means that the universal cover is contractible, equivalently that $\pi_n(B,b)=0$ for every integer $n\geq2$. If $p$ is the universal cover, this is equivalent to contractibility of $S$. Without that qualification, the record's wording "equivalently" does not justify identifying the $K(\pi,1)$ question with contractibility of this particular cover, or with the entire list of questions, especially global connectedness of $\operatorname{Stab}(\mathcal D)$.

The original excerpt also gives a parallel formulation for a category $\mathcal C$, with complement $E\setminus\mathcal H$, a homomorphism to $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal C)$, and a regularly covering component $\operatorname{Stab}^{\circ}(\mathcal C)$. Its Remark 3 mentions spherical twist functors, citing [9], when $f$ is the minimal resolution of a Kleinian singularity. The constructions of these parallel objects are likewise absent. The reconstructed target supplied for this record is the threefold-flop category $\mathcal D$. The original third question is truncated, so its full wording cannot be recovered from that excerpt alone.

Short English statement

For the category associated with a threefold flop, ask whether the distinguished component of its Bridgeland stability space is the universal cover of the associated hyperplane complement, whether mutations act faithfully, whether all stability conditions lie in that component, and whether the component is contractible. The supplied record omits essential definitions and geometric hypotheses. Its $K(\pi,1)$ reformulation requires qualifications, including identifying the distinguished cover as universal.
