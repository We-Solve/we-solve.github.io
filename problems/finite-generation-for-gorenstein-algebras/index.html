Math-3642

Finite Generation for Gorenstein Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $k$ for the ground field and $A$ for an associative unital finite-dimensional $k$-algebra. The supplied source does not specify the ground field or any restrictions on it; the formulation below is relative to a fixed ground field, whose intended hypotheses require confirmation. Use the convention that $A$ is Gorenstein when its regular left and right modules both have finite injective dimension: \[ \operatorname{injdim}_A({}_A A)<\infty, \qquad \operatorname{injdim}_{A^{\mathrm{op}}}(A_A)<\infty. \] Let $S_1,\ldots,S_r$ represent the isomorphism classes of simple left $A$-modules, and put $S=\bigoplus_{i=1}^r S_i$. Set \[ A^e=A\otimes_k A^{\mathrm{op}},\qquad \operatorname{HH}^{*}(A)=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{A^e}(A,A), \qquad E_A(S)=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_A(S,S). \] Here $\operatorname{HH}^{*}(A)$ is the Hochschild cohomology ring with its cup product, and $E_A(S)$ is the Yoneda algebra with its Yoneda product. Equip $E_A(S)$ with the usual graded $\operatorname{HH}^{*}(A)$-module structure induced by the Hochschild cohomology action on extensions. Condition $\mathrm{Fg}$ means that $\operatorname{HH}^{*}(A)$ is a noetherian ring and that $E_A(S)$ is finitely generated as a graded module over this ring.

Problem. Characterize the finite-dimensional Gorenstein algebras $A$ for which $\mathrm{Fg}$ holds; equivalently, determine necessary and sufficient conditions, within this class, for \[ \operatorname{HH}^{*}(A)\text{ to be noetherian} \quad\text{and}\quad E_A(S)\text{ to be finitely generated over }\operatorname{HH}^{*}(A). \] The source asks for a characterization, not an assertion that every Gorenstein algebra satisfies $\mathrm{Fg}$. The choice of left modules and the two-sided injective-dimension convention make the terminology explicit; the source does not itself specify these conventions or the intended ground-field hypotheses.

Short English statement

Determine which finite-dimensional Gorenstein algebras have noetherian Hochschild cohomology and a Yoneda algebra of the direct sum of representatives of the simple modules that is finitely generated over Hochschild cohomology. The intended assumptions on the ground field remain unspecified. The supplied registry labels the problem partially solved; that label and its dated literature assessment are not independently verified here.
