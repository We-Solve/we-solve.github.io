Math-751

Torsion in the Integral Homology-Cobordism Group

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual smooth interpretation, an integral homology three-sphere is a closed, connected, oriented smooth three-manifold $Y$ satisfying \[ H_k(Y;{\mathbb{Z}})\cong H_k(S^3;{\mathbb{Z}})\qquad\text{for every }k\geq 0. \] Two such manifolds $Y_0,Y_1$ are integrally homology cobordant if there is a compact, oriented smooth four-manifold $W$ with \[ \partial W=(-Y_0)\sqcup Y_1 \] such that both boundary inclusions induce isomorphisms on homology with coefficients in ${\mathbb{Z}}$ in every degree. Here $-Y_0$ denotes reversal of orientation. The group $\Theta^3_{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ consists of these homology-cobordism classes, with addition induced by oriented connected sum, identity $[S^3]$, and inverse $-[Y]=[-Y]$.

The question is whether this group has a nonzero element of finite order: \[ \exists [Y]\in\Theta^3_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\;\exists n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\quad n\geq 2,\qquad [Y]\neq [S^3],\qquad n[Y]=[S^3]. \] Here $n[Y]$ is the class of the connected sum of $n$ copies of $Y$.

The supplied record does not explicitly specify the smooth or topological category. The formulation above uses the usual smooth interpretation of this notation; confirmation of that convention from the cited report is required. The supplied label open and the supplied assertion of source verification are curation metadata, not independent verification in this draft.

Short English statement

Does the integral homology-cobordism group of oriented integral homology three-spheres contain a nonzero element of finite order? Under the smooth interpretation, this asks whether some sphere has a nonzero class whose connected sum with itself finitely many times represents $[S^3]$. The category requires source confirmation.
