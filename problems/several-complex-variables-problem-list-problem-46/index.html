Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer. Interpret a complete-circular domain in the standard balanced sense: $D\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ is a nonempty connected open set such that $\lambda z\in D$ whenever $z\in D$ and $|\lambda|\leq 1$. Assume that $D$ is bounded and pseudoconvex. Its Minkowski function is \[ h(z)=\inf\{t>0:z/t\in D\},\qquad z\in{\mathbb{C}}^n, \] so that $D=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^n:h(z)<1\}$. No continuity of $h$ is assumed in the main question.

Let $\Delta=\{\zeta\in{\mathbb{C}}:|\zeta|<1\}$, with Poincaré distance \[ \rho(\zeta,\eta)=\operatorname{artanh}\left|\frac{\zeta-\eta}{1-\overline\eta\zeta}\right|. \] For definiteness, use the Carathéodory distance \[ c_D(z,w)=\sup_{f\in\mathcal O(D,\Delta)}\rho(f(z),f(w)). \] Let $k_D$ denote the Kobayashi distance, namely the largest pseudodistance on $D$ for which every holomorphic map from $\Delta$ to $D$ decreases distance from $\rho$.

To define the Bergman distance, put \[ A^2(D)=\left\{f\in\mathcal O(D):\int_D|f|^2\,\mathrm dV<\infty\right\},\qquad K_D(z,z)=\sup_{\substack{f\in A^2(D)\\ \|f\|_{L^2(D)}\leq 1}}|f(z)|^2. \] The Bergman metric and its induced path distance are \[ \beta_D(z;v)^2=\sum_{j,\ell=1}^n \frac{\partial^2\log K_D(z,z)}{\partial z_j\,\partial\overline z_\ell} v_j\overline v_\ell, \qquad b_D(z,w)=\inf_\gamma\int_0^1\beta_D(\gamma(t);\gamma'(t))\,\mathrm dt, \] where the infimum is over piecewise continuously differentiable curves in $D$ joining $z$ to $w$. For $d_D\in\{c_D,b_D,k_D\}$, completeness means that every $d_D$-Cauchy sequence converges in $d_D$ to a point of $D$.

Problem (P. Pflug; source Problem 46). Characterize, by properties of the Minkowski function $h$, when $D$ is Carathéodory complete, when it is Bergman complete, and when it is Kobayashi complete. A characterization is sought separately for each of these three notions, with necessary and sufficient conditions valid for the stated class of domains.

Supplied historical qualifications. The source reports the following as known: (a) continuity of $h$ implies Bergman completeness of $D$; (b) for $n\geq 3$, continuity of $h$ does not imply Kobayashi completeness of $D$. These are assertions supplied with the 1995 problem, not independently verified results in this draft. The source does not specify whether Carathéodory completeness uses $c_D$ as defined above or the path distance induced by the infinitesimal Carathéodory metric; that convention remains to be checked.

Short English statement

For a bounded pseudoconvex balanced domain, find necessary and sufficient conditions on its Minkowski function for completeness in each of the Carathéodory, Bergman, and Kobayashi distances. The source reports that continuity suffices for Bergman completeness but does not suffice for Kobayashi completeness in dimensions $n\geq 3$. The precise Carathéodory convention requires review.
