Math-1020

Universality of Superexpansion Among Expander Sequences

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For this draft, use the standard unweighted graph convention described below. Let $(G_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$, where $G_n=(V_n,E_n)$, be a sequence of finite connected simple undirected graphs with $|V_n|\ge 2$ and $|V_n|\to\infty$. Each edge in $E_n$ is an unordered pair of vertices. Call this sequence an expander sequence if there exist constants $D<\infty$ and $h>0$ such that, for every $n$, every vertex of $G_n$ has degree at most $D$, and \[ |\partial_{E_n}S|\ge h|S| \qquad\text{whenever }S\subseteq V_n,\quad 0<|S|\le |V_n|/2, \] where \[ \partial_{E_n}S=\{\{u,v\}\in E_n:u\in S,\ v\in V_n\setminus S\}. \] A Banach space $X$ is superreflexive if it admits an equivalent uniformly convex norm. A norm $\lVert\cdot\rVert_*$ is uniformly convex if, for each $\varepsilon\in(0,2]$, there is $\delta>0$ such that \[ \lVert x\rVert_*=\lVert y\rVert_*=1, \quad \lVert x-y\rVert_*\ge\varepsilon \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left\lVert\frac{x+y}{2}\right\rVert_*\le 1-\delta. \] Using the quadratic Poincaré convention, call $(G_n)$ a superexpander sequence if, for every superreflexive Banach space $(X,\lVert\cdot\rVert_X)$, there is a constant $C_X\in(0,\infty)$ such that, for every $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$ and every map $f:V_n\to X$, \[ \frac{1}{|V_n|^2}\sum_{u,v\in V_n} \lVert f(u)-f(v)\rVert_X^2 \le \frac{C_X}{|E_n|}\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E_n} \lVert f(u)-f(v)\rVert_X^2. \] Here $C_X$ may depend on $X$ and on the graph sequence, but must be independent of $n$ and $f$.

Question. Is every expander sequence a superexpander sequence?

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied statement does not specify graph regularity, graph weighting, the normalization of the Poincaré inequality, or its exponent. The definitions above make explicit a standard bounded-degree, unweighted, quadratic formulation; their agreement with the intended workshop formulation requires human mathematical review.

Short English statement

Does every bounded-degree expander sequence satisfy a uniform quadratic Poincaré inequality for maps into each superreflexive Banach space? The constant may depend on the space and the sequence, but not on the graph index or the map. The precise graph and inequality conventions remain subject to source review.
