Research prompt and limits of formalization. The supplied record concerns historical personae of women in science and categories grounded in scientific practices, in circumstances where women lacked access to scientific diplomas and scientific positions. The record does not define "personae," specify the scientific practices or categories, or give criteria for what constitutes a successful characterization.

The retained research questions are: What can be learned from historical personae of women in science? Can categories based on scientific practices characterize women when they lacked access to scientific diplomas and positions? The supplied background describes the relevant restrictions on access as systematic.

Short English statement

The prompt asks what historical personae reveal about women in science and whether scientific practices can provide categories for characterizing women systematically excluded from scientific diplomas and positions. The supplied record identifies this as a historical or sociological inquiry; it does not supply a precise mathematical problem.
