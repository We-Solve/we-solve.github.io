Weighted Fundamental Lemma for Classical Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied target is to prove the weighted fundamental lemma for nonsplit classical groups, with the stated aim of removing the corresponding conditional inputs to the endoscopic classification of their representations. The record does not specify the version of the weighted fundamental lemma, its hypotheses, or its orbital-integral identity. Consequently, it does not determine a complete quantified conjecture. In particular, the assertion that this would make the classification unconditional requires clarification of the other assumptions mentioned in the source.

The following describes the supplied classification context, without asserting an unconditional theorem or supplying the missing lemma. Let $G$ be a quasi-split symplectic or orthogonal group; the source also discusses quasi-split unitary groups, attributing their treatment to Mok. Its displayed formulas use $G({\mathbb{Q}}_v)$ and $G({\mathbb{A}})$, where $v$ is a place of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, ${\mathbb{Q}}_v$ is the corresponding completion, and ${\mathbb{A}}$ is the adele ring of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. Whether this base field is intended for the requested lemma is unspecified. Write $\widehat G$ for the dual group. The source describes $\Psi(G)$ as a set of formal global parameters constructed using cuspidal automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_N$, with $N$ associated with the standard representation of $\widehat G$. It identifies a subset \[ \Phi(G)=\{\phi\in\Psi(G):\phi|_{\mathrm{SL}_2}=1\}, \] whose elements it calls generic, or tempered. The parameter domain and the indicated $\mathrm{SL}_2$ factor are not defined in the record.

The supplied excerpt attributes to Arthur, under unspecified assumptions, a classification with the following features. There is a decomposition of the discrete automorphic spectrum, written schematically as \[ L^2_{\mathrm{disc}}(G)=\bigoplus_{\psi\in\Psi_2(G)}L^2_\psi(G). \] Here $L^2_{\mathrm{disc}}(G)$ denotes the source's discrete automorphic spectrum, and $\Psi_2(G)$ its indexing set of discrete parameters; their precise definitions and the quotient defining the spectrum are not supplied. At each place $v$, local packets $\Pi_\phi(G({\mathbb{Q}}_v))$ and $\Pi_\psi(G({\mathbb{Q}}_v))$ are described as covering, respectively, the irreducible tempered and irreducible admissible representations. The record does not define the local parameter sets $\Phi(G_v)$ and $\Psi(G_v)$, and the damaged transcription does not reliably specify the union or disjoint-union assertions. In particular, the claimed coverage of all irreducible admissible representations by the displayed $\Psi$-packets is retained only as a source assertion requiring clarification.

The excerpt further gives a packet-labeling map \[ \Pi_\psi(G({\mathbb{Q}}_v))\longrightarrow\operatorname{Irr}(S_\psi), \qquad \pi_v\longmapsto\rho_{\pi_v}, \qquad S_\psi=\operatorname{Cent}(\psi,\widehat G). \] This is the supplied notation: it does not clarify whether a component group or a quotient of this centralizer is intended. It also gives localization maps $\operatorname{loc}_v:\Psi(G)\to\Psi(G_v)$ and a schematic multiplicity formula \[ L^2_\psi(G)=\bigoplus_{\pi}\pi^{\oplus m(\psi,\pi)}, \qquad m(\psi,\pi)=\operatorname{mult}\!\left(\epsilon_\psi, \bigotimes_v\left.\rho_{\pi_v}\right|_{S_\psi}\right). \] The sum is described as running over irreducible admissible restricted tensor products $\pi=\bigotimes'_v\pi_v$ of $G({\mathbb{A}})$ satisfying the source's condition $\operatorname{loc}_v(\psi)=\psi_{\pi_v}$ at every place. The notation $\psi_{\pi_v}$, the maps needed for the restrictions, and the tensor-product conventions are unspecified. The source calls $\epsilon_\psi$ an explicit sign character and says it is trivial for $\psi\in\Phi(G)$. These formulas record the intended context, not a repaired precise theorem.

The source describes the proof as an induction on the dimension of the standard representation of $\widehat G$, using comparisons of trace formulas that require local information. It also states an expectation for general groups that cuspidal automorphic representations admitting a Whittaker model have tempered local components and should correspond to $\Phi(G)$. This is presented as an expectation. Finally, it says that describing all discrete automorphic representations, including those that are not cuspidal, requires the $\mathrm{SL}_2$ factor even for $\mathrm{GL}_N$, attributing the conjecture to Arthur and its proof to Mœglin--Waldspurger. None of this supplies the missing weighted fundamental lemma identity or its precise range of validity.

Short English statement

The requested problem is to establish a weighted fundamental lemma for nonsplit classical groups and thereby remove the corresponding conditional input to endoscopic classification. The supplied record gives conditional classification context, but omits the lemma's identity, hypotheses, and exact scope. A precise theorem statement therefore requires source clarification and human mathematical review. The supplied status is partially solved; it is not an independently verified status assessment.
