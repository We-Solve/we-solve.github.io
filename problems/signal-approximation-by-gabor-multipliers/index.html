Signal Approximation by Gabor Multipliers

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H=L^2({\mathbb{R}})$, the space of square-integrable signals modulo equality almost everywhere, with norm \[ \|x\|_2=\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}}|x(t)|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t\right)^{1/2}. \] The supplied record does not specify whether signals are real-valued or complex-valued.

A Gabor multiplier requires a choice of Gabor analysis and synthesis systems, consisting of time-frequency translates of specified windows, together with an admissible class of masks. To describe the missing data schematically, let $(g_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ and $(h_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ be such analysis and synthesis systems. For a mask $m=(m_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$, the corresponding multiplier has the form \[ M_m x=\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}m_\lambda\langle x,g_\lambda\rangle h_\lambda, \] whenever the chosen synthesis procedure makes this expression well-defined in $H$. This is an explanatory schema, not a specification of the systems or mask class intended by the source. The record supplies neither these choices nor hypotheses ensuring convergence or boundedness.

Source question, with unresolved qualifications. Given $x_0,x_1\in L^2({\mathbb{R}})$, determine assumptions under which there exists a mask $m$ such that $M_mx_0$ approximates $x_1$. The meaning and required accuracy of approximation are not specified, so this wording does not determine a unique quantified mathematical problem.

For example, after fixing the missing systems and a nonempty admissible mask class $\mathcal M$, and requiring each $M_mx_0$ to be defined, set \[ \mathcal R(x_0)=\{M_mx_0:m\in\mathcal M\}\subseteq H. \] If approximation is intended in the $L^2$ norm, approximation at a prescribed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$ would mean \[ \exists m\in\mathcal M\qquad \|M_mx_0-x_1\|_2<\varepsilon, \] whereas arbitrary accuracy would mean \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists m\in\mathcal M\qquad \|M_mx_0-x_1\|_2<\varepsilon, \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad x_1\in\overline{\mathcal R(x_0)}^{\,\|\cdot\|_2}. \] Exact recovery would instead mean $x_1\in\mathcal R(x_0)$. These are distinct possible targets; the record does not select one. Its reference to an optimal-mask formula also does not specify a variational objective here.

The supplied statement-verification note describes the report as presenting an approximation setup followed by variational solutions and inverse algorithms, rather than a standalone unresolved problem. It marks the statement as unrecoverable and supplies no clean replacement. Accordingly, the conditional formulations above identify missing specifications and are not asserted to be the report's intended open problem.

Short English statement

The record asks when a Gabor multiplier can transform a given square-integrable signal into an approximation of another. A precise problem requires the Gabor systems, admissible masks, approximation criterion, and accuracy quantifiers. These are missing. The supplied curation labels the topic partially solved, while its verification note says that no standalone unresolved statement was recovered.
