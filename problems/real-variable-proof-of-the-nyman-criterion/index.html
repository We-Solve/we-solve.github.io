Real-Variable Proof of the Nyman Criterion

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For real $x\geq 2$, let $\pi(x)=\#\{p\leq x:p\text{ is prime}\}$, and let $\operatorname{li}(x)=\operatorname{PV}\int_0^x du/\log u$ be the logarithmic integral. For $u\in{\mathbb{R}}$, write $\{u\}=u-\lfloor u\rfloor$. For $t>0$ and real $\alpha>1$, define \[ \chi(t)=\begin{cases}1,&t>1,\\0,&0<t\leq 1,\end{cases} \qquad e_\alpha(t)=\{t/\alpha\}. \] The first assertion in the supplied statement is \[ \tag{A} \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists C_\varepsilon>0\ \exists X_\varepsilon\geq2\ \forall x\in{\mathbb{R}},\ x\geq X_\varepsilon: \quad |\pi(x)-\operatorname{li}(x)|\leq C_\varepsilon x^{1/2+\varepsilon}. \] The source writes the second assertion using a sum $\sum_\alpha c_\alpha e_\alpha$, without explicitly specifying its support or coefficient field. Under the finite-linear-combination interpretation, its precise formulation, for a specified field $K\in\{{\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}}\}$, is \[ \tag{B$_K$} \begin{gathered} \forall\delta>0\ \exists n\in{\mathbb{N}},\ n\geq1,\ \exists\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n\in(1,\infty)\ \exists c_1,\ldots,c_n\in K:\\ \int_0^\infty\left|\chi(t)-\sum_{j=1}^n c_j\{t/\alpha_j\}\right|^2\frac{dt}{t^2}<\delta. \end{gathered} \] The parameters and coefficients may depend on $\delta$. The integral is with respect to the weighted Lebesgue measure $t^{-2}\,dt$. No additional coefficient constraint is stated in the supplied record.

The requested task is to give a real-variable proof of the equivalence of (A) and the intended second assertion. With the explicitly qualified finite-sum interpretation above, this is the equivalence of (A) and $\mathrm{(B}_K\mathrm{)}$, after the coefficient field has been specified. The finite-support interpretation and coefficient field require confirmation from the cited report; an infinite-sum interpretation would additionally require a convergence convention. The supplied record also does not define which methods qualify as a real-variable proof. Its original wording refers to assertions (2) and (3) of the report; the expanded assertions above come from the supplied clean statement, without independent verification of that correspondence. The target is a proof with the requested methodological restriction, rather than a request to establish (A) unconditionally.

Short English statement

Find a real-variable proof that the stated prime-counting error estimate is equivalent to arbitrarily accurate approximation of $\chi$ by combinations of the fractional-part functions $e_\alpha$ in $L^2((0,\infty),t^{-2}\,dt)$. The displayed finite-sum formulation is explicitly conditional on that interpretation of the source. The coefficient field and the permitted proof methods require clarification. The supplied open label concerns the requested proof method.
