Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Fix $n\ge4$, $k=n-1$, $\omega=|\mathbb S^k|$. Take a smooth connected oriented $n$-dimensional exterior, complete with its nonempty compact boundary included and one Euclidean end outside a compact set. Let $g,K,E$ be smooth and purely electric, with $\tau=\operatorname{tr}_gK$ and \[ 2\mu_m=R_g+\tau^2-|K|_g^2-k(k-1)|E|_g^2,\quad J_m=\operatorname{div}_g(K-\tau g),\quad \mu_m\ge|J_m|_g,\quad\operatorname{div}_gE=0. \] Assume $\mu_m,|J_m|\in L^1$, $g-\delta=O_2(r^{-q})$, $K=O_1(r^{-1-q})$ for $q>(n-2)/2$, and $E=O_1(r^{-k})$. Let ADM energy $\mathcal E$ and momentum $P$ be the finite fluxes with factors $(2k\omega)^{-1}$ and $(k\omega)^{-1}$, and $Q=\omega^{-1}\lim_R\int_{r=R}g(E,\nu_R)dA_g$. Each boundary component satisfies $H+\epsilon_a\operatorname{tr}_{S_a}K\le0$ for a chosen sign $\epsilon_a\in\{-1,1\}$. Choose a connected smooth end-side region $D\subset\Omega$, closed in $\Omega$, containing the distant end and having its manifold interior in $\operatorname{int}\Omega$. A full cut consists of all of its intrinsic boundary $\Gamma=\partial D$; require this boundary to be compact, smoothly embedded and two-sided. All components and portions coinciding with $S=\partial\Omega$ count. Put $a_g(S)=\inf_\Gamma\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)$. Put $r_A=(a_g(S)/\omega)^{1/k}$ and $X_A=r_A^{k-1}$. The stated premise is the neutral numerical theorem for all $d\ge3$ with $O_6(r^{-q_0})$ metric decay, $O_5(r^{-1-q_0})$ tensor decay, $(d-2)/2<q_0<d-2$, positive full-cut area, future-timelike ADM vector and uniform future trapping. It supplies the neutral bound $\sqrt{\mathcal E^2-|P|^2}\ge\frac12(A_*/\omega_{d-1})^{(d-2)/(d-1)}$. Under that premise the charged extension proves \[ 0<a_g(S)\le|S|_g,\quad\mathcal E>|P|,\quad m=\sqrt{\mathcal E^2-|P|^2}\ge|Q|,\quad X_A\le m+\sqrt{m^2-Q^2}. \] For $X_A>|Q|$, this equals $m\ge\frac12(X_A+Q^2/X_A)$; for $0<X_A\le|Q|$, it imposes only $m\ge|Q|$. At $Q=0$, $m\ge X_A/2$. The electric field normalization in spacetime is $E^\flat=[k(k-1)/2]^{-1/2}\iota^*(i_N\mathcal F)$, with vanishing pullback magnetic two-form.

Outline of the argument

A coupled scalar system converts electric flux into a controlled energy decrease while retaining enclosure bounds; equality of that conversion reconstructs the original spacelike exterior with vanishing induced magnetic form.
