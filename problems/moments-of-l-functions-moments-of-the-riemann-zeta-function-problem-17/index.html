Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 17

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a Hecke--Maass cusp form $f$, write $L(s,f)$ for its standard $L$-function and $L(s,\operatorname{ad}f)$ for its adjoint $L$-function. The spectral parameter $t_f$ is related to the Laplace eigenvalue by $\lambda_f=\tfrac14+t_f^2$. The source does not specify how exceptional imaginary spectral parameters are handled in its cutoff.

The supplied record attributes to Jutila the level-one twelfth-moment estimate \[ \sum_{t_f\leq T}\frac{L\!\left(\tfrac12,f\right)^{12}}{L(1,\operatorname{ad}f)} \ll_{\varepsilon}T^{4+\varepsilon}, \] where the sum is over Hecke--Maass cusp forms of level $1$, and the estimate is understood for every $\varepsilon>0$ as $T\to\infty$. The notation $\ll_{\varepsilon}$ permits the implied constant to depend on $\varepsilon$.

Problem (Peter Humphries, Virginia, as attributed in the source). Prove a level-aspect analogue of this Weyl-strength twelfth-moment estimate by summing over Hecke--Maass cusp forms of level $q$, with spectral parameter bounded independently of the positive integer level $q$, as $q\to\infty$.

Unresolved quantitative and family specifications. The supplied question does not state the desired exponent of $q$, restrictions on the levels, or the precise family at level $q$. In particular, it does not specify the congruence subgroup, character, inclusion of oldforms, or multiplicity convention. It also does not explicitly prescribe the level-aspect weight or its normalization. Thus the source supplies a research question, but not a uniquely specified level-aspect inequality. These choices require clarification before a fully quantified numerical conjecture can be stated.

Short English statement

The question asks for an analogue, as the level grows and the spectral parameter stays bounded, of Jutila's stated level-one twelfth-moment bound. The desired power of the level and the precise family and weighting are unspecified. The registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
