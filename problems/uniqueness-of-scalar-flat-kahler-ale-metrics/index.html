Math-4452

Uniqueness of Scalar-Flat Kähler ALE Metrics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,J)$ be a complex manifold of complex dimension $n$. A Kähler metric $g$ has an associated real, closed, positive $(1,1)$-form $\omega$, given by $\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)$. It is scalar-flat if its scalar curvature satisfies $\operatorname{Scal}(g)\equiv 0$. Write $[\omega]_{\mathrm{dR}}\in H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(X;{\mathbb{R}})$ for its ordinary de Rham cohomology class.

The abbreviation ALE means asymptotically locally Euclidean: outside a compact set, the metric is asymptotic to a Euclidean quotient by a finite group acting freely on the sphere at infinity. The supplied record does not specify the dimension, completeness requirement, end conditions, decay rate, derivative estimates, asymptotic coordinates, or normalization at infinity. These conditions must be fixed before the admissible class of metrics is fully determined.

Uniqueness question, with unresolved qualifications. For a fixed ALE Kähler complex manifold $(X,J)$ admitting scalar-flat Kähler metrics, and a Kähler class $\alpha$ containing such a metric, is the scalar-flat Kähler metric in $\alpha$ unique? Under the literal interpretation of uniqueness as equality of metrics on the fixed complex manifold, and of Kähler class as ordinary de Rham class, the proposed implication is \[ \left. \begin{gathered} g_0,g_1\text{ are admissible scalar-flat ALE Kähler metrics on }(X,J),\\ [\omega_0]_{\mathrm{dR}}=[\omega_1]_{\mathrm{dR}}=\alpha \end{gathered} \right\} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad g_0=g_1\;? \] Here admissibility refers to the still unspecified asymptotic and completeness conditions. The source does not determine whether uniqueness instead means uniqueness modulo a specified group of holomorphic automorphisms, or whether the class includes additional asymptotic data. Thus this implication records a literal reading of the question, rather than a fully resolved formulation. No existence assertion is made for classes that do not contain a scalar-flat Kähler metric.

Short English statement

On a fixed ALE Kähler manifold, must two scalar-flat Kähler metrics in the same Kähler class coincide? The supplied record leaves the asymptotic conditions, the meaning of Kähler class on this noncompact manifold, and possible equivalence under automorphisms unspecified. Its open label is supplied curation, not an independently verified status.
