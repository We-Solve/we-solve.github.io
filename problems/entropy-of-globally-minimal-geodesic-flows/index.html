Math-1083

Entropy of Globally Minimal Geodesic Flows

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact manifold of hyperbolic type, as meant in the report, and let $g$ be any Riemannian metric on it. Write $SM=\{v\in TM:g(v,v)=1\}$ for its unit tangent bundle and $\varphi_t$ for the geodesic flow. For a vector $v$, lift its unit-speed geodesic to the universal Riemannian cover $(\widetilde M,\widetilde g)$. Define \[ SM_{\min}=\{v\in SM: d_{\widetilde g}(\widetilde\gamma_v(s),\widetilde\gamma_v(t))=|t-s| \text{ for all }s,t\in{\mathbb{R}}\}. \] Thus the lifted entire geodesic minimizes distance on every finite segment. In the customary closed-manifold setting, volume entropy is \[ h_{\mathrm{Vol}}(g)=\lim_{R\to\infty}\frac1R \log\operatorname{Vol}_{\widetilde g}(B_{\widetilde g}(\widetilde x,R)). \] The first question is whether \[ h_{\mathrm{top}}(\varphi_1|_{SM_{\min}})=h_{\mathrm{Vol}}(g) \] for every metric in the intended manifold class. Here flow entropy is measured per unit time, using the time-one map.

Separately, if $M$ is a higher-genus surface, let $\mu_L$ be the normalized invariant Liouville measure on $SM$. Does every metric $g$ satisfy \[ h_{\mu_L}(\varphi_1)>0, \] where the left side is measure-theoretic entropy? In the customary orientable closed-surface reading, higher genus means genus at least two; the source does not explicitly state orientation or boundary conventions. The second question concerns this specific invariant measure, not merely positive topological entropy.

The phrase "hyperbolic type" is not defined in the extraction. It must not be replaced without source evidence by a curvature assumption on the arbitrary metric $g$. Compactness versus closedness and the regularity of allowed metrics likewise need confirmation for the complete-flow and entropy definitions above.

Short English statement

Does the geodesic flow restricted to geodesics minimizing globally in the universal cover have entropy equal to volume growth entropy? Separately, must every higher-genus surface metric have positive entropy for Liouville measure?
