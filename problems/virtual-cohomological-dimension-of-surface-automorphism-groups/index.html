Virtual Cohomological Dimension of Surface Automorphism Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be a surface belonging to one of the three classes named in the source: Coble surfaces, Enriques surfaces, or elliptic K3 surfaces. Write $k$ for its ground field and $\operatorname{Aut}(S)$ for its automorphism group. The source does not specify $k$, its characteristic, whether automorphisms are taken over $k$ or a geometric extension, or the precise conventions for these surface classes. Consequently, the universal quantification below remains subject to unresolved hypotheses.

For a discrete group $G$ admitting a torsion-free subgroup $H$ of finite index, its virtual cohomological dimension is \[ \operatorname{vcd}(G)=\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(H), \] where $\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(H)$ is the projective dimension of the trivial ${\mathbb{Z}}[H]$-module ${\mathbb{Z}}$. This value is independent of the choice of such $H$. The source does not state the hypotheses ensuring that this definition applies to $\operatorname{Aut}(S)$.

Under the usual smooth-generic-fibre convention, a genus-one fibration is a surjective morphism $f:S\to{\mathbb{P}}^1_k$ with connected fibres whose generic fibre $C_f$, over $K=k({\mathbb{P}}^1_k)$, is a smooth geometrically integral curve of genus one. It need not have a section. Its Jacobian has generic fibre $J_f=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C_f)$, an elliptic curve over $K$. Under the rational-section interpretation of Mordell--Weil rank, one writes \[ \operatorname{MW\text{-}rk}(f) =\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}J_f(K) =\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\left(J_f(K)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}\right). \] The source does not settle whether this rank is taken over the ground field or geometrically, whether a constant-trace quotient is intended, or whether quasi-elliptic fibrations in positive characteristic are included. The displayed definitions describe a conventional interpretation rather than resolve those omissions.

Question supplied by the source. For every Coble surface, Enriques surface, or elliptic K3 surface $S$ in the intended setting, is \[ \operatorname{vcd}\left(\operatorname{Aut}(S)\right) =\max_{\substack{f:S\to{\mathbb{P}}^1\\ f\text{ a genus-one fibration}}} \operatorname{MW\text{-}rk}(f)? \] The maximum is over all genus-one fibrations on $S$, not merely a chosen elliptic fibration. No convention for an empty indexing set is supplied, and no hypotheses guaranteeing existence and attainment of this maximum are stated. The ground-field, surface-class, fibration, automorphism, and rank conventions must be fixed before this becomes a fully specified universal assertion.

Short English statement

The question asks whether, for every Coble surface, Enriques surface, or elliptic K3 surface in the intended setting, the virtual cohomological dimension of its automorphism group equals the largest Mordell--Weil rank of the Jacobians of its genus-one fibrations. The source leaves several hypotheses and conventions unspecified. Its partially solved label is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
