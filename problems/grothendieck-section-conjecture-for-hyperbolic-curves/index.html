Grothendieck Section Conjecture for Hyperbolic Curves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a number field, let $\bar k$ be an algebraic closure, and put $G_k=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar k/k)$. Let $X/k$ be a smooth, geometrically connected curve, and choose a geometric base point $\bar x$ on $X_{\bar k}$. Throughout, fundamental groups are profinite étale fundamental groups. Assume that $\pi_1(X_{\bar k},\bar x)$ is nonabelian. There is an exact sequence \[ 1\longrightarrow \pi_1(X_{\bar k},\bar x)\longrightarrow \pi_1(X,\bar x)\xrightarrow{p}G_k\longrightarrow 1. \] A section is a continuous group homomorphism $s:G_k\to\pi_1(X,\bar x)$ satisfying $p\circ s=\operatorname{id}_{G_k}$. Two sections are equivalent if they are conjugate by an element of $\pi_1(X_{\bar k},\bar x)$. Write $\operatorname{Sec}(X/k)$ for the set of equivalence classes.

To make the word noncuspidal precise, use the following standard boundary interpretation, which is not explicitly defined in the supplied record. Let $\overline X$ be the smooth projective compactification of $X$, and let $B=\overline X\setminus X$ be its reduced boundary. For $c\in B(k)$, choose a decomposition subgroup $D_c\subseteq\pi_1(X,\bar x)$ associated with a prolongation of the valuation at $c$ to the maximal extension of $k(X)$ unramified over $X$. Call a section class $[s]$ cuspidal if, for some $c\in B(k)$ and some representative $s'$ of $[s]$, \[ s'(G_k)\subseteq D_c. \] The existence condition is independent of the choices of decomposition subgroups. Let $\operatorname{Sec}_{\mathrm{nc}}(X/k)$ denote the classes that are not cuspidal. If $X$ is projective, its boundary is empty and every section class is noncuspidal.

Each $a\in X(k)$, viewed as a morphism $\operatorname{Spec}k\to X$, induces a section up to geometric conjugacy. Thus there is a point-to-section map \[ \kappa_X:X(k)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sec}(X/k),\qquad a\longmapsto[s_a]. \] Statement requested by the supplied record (Grothendieck's section conjecture). For every number field $k$ and every smooth, geometrically connected curve $X/k$ with nonabelian geometric étale fundamental group, is $\kappa_X$ injective with image exactly $\operatorname{Sec}_{\mathrm{nc}}(X/k)$? Equivalently, does it induce a bijection \[ X(k)\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Sec}_{\mathrm{nc}}(X/k)? \] The supplied record labels this problem open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified status assessment. The boundary interpretation above requires comparison with the cited report during mathematical review.

Short English statement

For every number field and every smooth, geometrically connected curve with nonabelian geometric étale fundamental group, the conjecture asks whether rational points correspond bijectively to continuous sections of the arithmetic fundamental-group sequence, up to geometric conjugacy, after excluding sections arising at boundary points. The supplied source does not explicitly define the boundary exclusion; the draft states the standard interpretation for review.
