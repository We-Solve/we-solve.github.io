Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns Borel reducibility of isomorphism relations for countable models. The excerpt does not define its descriptive-set-theoretic notation; the following statement uses the conventional interpretation specified here, subject to confirmation against the full source.

Let $X$ be an uncountable standard Borel space. Write $=_X$ for equality on $X$, and define the equivalence relation $=_X^{+}$ on $X^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ by \[ (x_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}\ =_X^{+}\ (y_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \{x_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\}=\{y_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\}. \] Thus repetitions and order are disregarded. For equivalence relations $E$ on a standard Borel space $U$ and $F$ on a standard Borel space $V$, write $E\leq_B F$ if there exists a Borel function $f:U\to V$ such that \[ \forall u,u'\in U\qquad u\mathrel{E}u'\ \Longleftrightarrow\ f(u)\mathrel{F}f(u'). \] Write $E\sim_B F$ when both reductions exist, and $E<_B F$ when $E\leq_B F$ and $F\not\leq_B E$.

For a countable first-order language $L$ and a complete consistent $L$-theory $T$, interpret $\mathrm{Mod}_{\omega}(T)$ as the standard Borel space of $L$-structures with underlying set $\omega$ satisfying $T$. Let $\cong_T$ be isomorphism on this space: $M\cong_T N$ if a bijection of $\omega$ induces an $L$-isomorphism from $M$ to $N$.

Question (the catalogue statement; source Problem 21). Does there exist a countable first-order language $L$ and a complete consistent $L$-theory $T$ such that \[ =_X\ <_B\ \cong_T\ <_B\ =_X^{+}\,? \] Equivalently, the required conditions are \[ =_X\leq_B\cong_T,\qquad \cong_T\not\leq_B=_X,\qquad \cong_T\leq_B=_X^{+},\qquad =_X^{+}\not\leq_B\cong_T. \] Under these conventions, the Borel reducibility question is independent of the choice of uncountable standard Borel space $X$, since Borel bijections transfer the two equality relations.

Supplied motivating assertions. The excerpt asserts that there are complete theories $T_0,T_1$ with \[ =_X\sim_B\cong_{T_0},\qquad =_X^{+}\sim_B\cong_{T_1}. \] For $T_0$, it proposes the theory of an equivalence relation on $\omega$ having infinitely many classes of size $n$ for every positive integer $n$. For $T_1$, it proposes the theory of infinitely many independent equivalence relations on $\omega$, each having infinitely many classes of size $n$ for every positive integer $n$. These assertions are supplied background, not independently verified here. The excerpt does not define independence, specify whether infinite equivalence classes are present in these examples, or explicitly define $=$, $=^{+}$, and $\mathrm{Mod}(T)$. The displayed question is therefore a statement under the stated conventional interpretations, requiring source confirmation.

Short English statement

Does some complete first-order theory in a countable language have an isomorphism relation on its models with domain $\omega$ that is strictly above equality and strictly below equality of countable sets of reals in Borel reducibility? This uses an explicitly stated conventional interpretation of the source notation. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that status and the motivating examples have not been independently verified.
