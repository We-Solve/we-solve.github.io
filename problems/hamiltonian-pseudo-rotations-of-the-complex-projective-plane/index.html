Hamiltonian Pseudo-Rotations of the Complex Projective Plane

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M=\mathbb{CP}^{2}$, the space of complex one-dimensional linear subspaces of ${\mathbb{C}}^{3}$, and let $\omega$ denote the intended symplectic form. The supplied record does not specify this form; the conventional choice is the Fubini--Study form, with normalization still to be specified. For a fixed $\omega$, a smooth Hamiltonian diffeomorphism is a map $\phi=\phi_H^1$, where $\phi_H^t$ is the flow of a smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian $H\colon[0,1]\times M\to{\mathbb{R}}$, with vector field determined by $\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-{\,\mathrm{d}} H_t$. Write $\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)$ for the set of these diffeomorphisms and define \[ \operatorname{Per}(\phi)=\bigcup_{k\geq1}\operatorname{Fix}(\phi^k),\qquad \operatorname{Fix}(\phi^k)=\{x\in M:\phi^k(x)=x\}. \] Thus "exactly three periodic points" means \[ \#\operatorname{Per}(\phi)=3, \] counting points over all positive integer periods, rather than counting periodic orbits or only fixed points. The source calls the desired maps Hamiltonian pseudo-rotations; it does not specify additional nondegeneracy conditions.

The construction question is whether the Anosov--Katok conjugation method can produce a map \[ \phi\in\operatorname{Ham}(\mathbb{CP}^{2},\omega),\qquad \#\operatorname{Per}(\phi)=3, \] that is a genuine pseudo-rotation beyond true rotations. For reference, with the Fubini--Study form, the usual projective unitary rotations have the form \[ R_U([z])=[Uz],\qquad U\in\operatorname{U}(3). \] The record does not specify whether "beyond true rotations" excludes only maps of this form, or also maps conjugate to them, nor does it specify the permitted conjugacies. Consequently, the displayed periodic-point condition is precise, but the required exclusion of rotations and the exact admissible Anosov--Katok construction remain unresolved parts of the formulation. The smooth setting above is the usual Hamiltonian interpretation; the source does not explicitly state the regularity or convergence topology required of the construction.

Short English statement

Can the Anosov--Katok conjugation method construct a Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of $\mathbb{CP}^{2}$ having exactly three periodic points in total and satisfying the source's requirement of being beyond true rotations? The symplectic convention, construction regularity, and meaning of exclusion of rotations require clarification.
