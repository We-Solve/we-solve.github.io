Kourovka Notebook Problem 5.36

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A profinite group is a topological group isomorphic, as a topological group, to an inverse limit of finite groups endowed with the discrete topology. Say that a profinite group $G$ satisfies the maximum condition for closed subgroups if every ascending sequence of closed subgroups \[ H_1\leq H_2\leq H_3\leq\cdots\leq G \] stabilizes: there exists $n_0\in{\mathbb{N}}$, with $n_0\geq 1$, such that $H_n=H_{n_0}$ for every $n\geq n_0$. Here closedness refers to the profinite topology, and the subgroups are not required to be normal. Equivalently, every nonempty family of closed subgroups of $G$ has a member maximal under inclusion within that family.

Problem (Yu. N. Mukhin, 1976; catalogue entry). Characterize the profinite groups that satisfy the maximum condition for closed subgroups. No finiteness, countability, or metrizability hypothesis is imposed on $G$.

Short English statement

Which profinite groups have the property that every increasing sequence of closed subgroups eventually becomes constant? The task is to characterize all such groups.
