Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 7.36

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|<1\}$, let $m$ be plane Lebesgue measure, and let $E\subseteq\mathbb D$ be measurable. The source defines the two-dimensional Hilbert transform of $\mathbf1_E$ by \[ f_E(z)=-\frac1\pi\iint_E\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}} m(w)}{(w-z)^2},\qquad z\in{\mathbb{C}}\setminus E. \] For arbitrary measurable $E$, points outside $E$ can still be arbitrarily close to its mass, so the literal integral needs an interpretation. The standard singular-integral reading is the almost-everywhere principal value \[ f_E(z)=\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}-\frac1\pi \iint_{E\setminus B(z,\epsilon)}\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}} m(w)}{(w-z)^2}. \] Away from the closure of $E$ the ordinary integral suffices. Source review should confirm the intended almost-everywhere version; the estimate concerns its integral over $\mathbb D\setminus E$.

The source reports constants $a,b$ such that \[ \iint_{\mathbb D\setminus E}|f_E|\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m \leq a\,m(E)\log\frac\pi{m(E)}+b\,m(E) \] for every measurable $E\subseteq\mathbb D$, by Calderón--Zygmund theory. Determine the smallest admissible leading coefficient $a$, where the additive constant $b$ may depend on $a$ but must be independent of $E$. The conjectured sharp endpoint is \[ \exists b\in{\mathbb{R}}\ \forall E\subseteq\mathbb D\text{ measurable},\quad \iint_{\mathbb D\setminus E}|f_E|\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m \leq m(E)\log\frac\pi{m(E)}+b\,m(E). \] At $m(E)=0$, use the continuous convention $m(E)\log(\pi/m(E))=0$. The source says small centered disks show that a coefficient below one is impossible. Proving only inequalities with every $a>1$ would not by itself establish the displayed endpoint with $a=1$.

The source calls this equivalent to Gehring--Reich's area-distribution Problem 7.9, whose statement is not supplied. It also reports a sharp one-dimensional analogue for $E\subset[-1,1]$ and the transform $-\pi^{-1}\int_E{\,\mathrm{d}} t/(x-t)$, by subordination or a Stein--Weiss theorem; no exact analogue inequality is included.

Short English statement

Find the sharp leading logarithmic constant in the integral estimate for the planar Hilbert transform of an indicator set. The conjectured coefficient is one with a single additive constant working for every measurable subset of the disk. The registry labels this solved, while its research summary says OPEN-TRIAGE and no definitive resolution located after Hayman's 2018 update. No resolving citation is supplied, so that status conflict is retained rather than silently resolved.
