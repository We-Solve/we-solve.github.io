Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 12.5

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the surface domain in the supplied research summary: $S=S_{g,p}$ is a connected orientable surface of finite type, genus $g$, with $p$ punctures and no boundary, and $\xi(S)=3g-3+p\ge1$. Let $\mathcal T(S)$ be its Teichmüller space, with Weil--Petersson distance $d_{\mathrm{WP}}$, and let $\operatorname{Mod}(S)$ be its mapping class group with a word metric from a finite generating set. For a metric space $X$, define its quasi-flat rank as the supremum of integers $k\ge0$ for which there is a map $q:{\mathbb{R}}^k\to X$ and constants $L\ge1,A\ge0$ satisfying \[ L^{-1}\|u-v\|-A\le d(q(u),q(v))\le L\|u-v\|+A\qquad(u,v\in{\mathbb{R}}^k). \] Brock asks for these ranks for $(\mathcal T(S),d_{\mathrm{WP}})$ and $\operatorname{Mod}(S)$. Under the supplied domain and meaning of rank, the research summary records the answers \[ \operatorname{rank}\operatorname{Mod}(S)=3g-3+p, \qquad \operatorname{rank}(\mathcal T(S),d_{\mathrm{WP}}) =\left\lfloor\frac{3g+p-2}{2}\right\rfloor =\left\lfloor\frac{\xi(S)+1}{2}\right\rfloor. \] It identifies the first value with maximal free-abelian subgroup rank and the second with the analogous pants-graph rank, using Brock's quasi-isometry theorem. It describes the second value in terms of disjoint nonannular essential subsurfaces; the relevant subsurfaces must have positive complexity for that interpretation, rather than including pairs of pants. The literal source question does not specify the surface or rank convention; the formulas here retain precisely the $\xi(S)\ge1$ domain of the summary, without extending them to excluded low-complexity surfaces or surfaces with boundary.

The summary also asserts that maximal quasi-flats are Hausdorff-close to unions of "Dehn twist flats"/standard orthants. Its intended class of standard pieces and hypotheses are not specified, so that ancillary claim is not promoted to a precise additional theorem here. The summary ends in a truncated consistency-check sentence.

Short English statement

Determine the largest dimension of a Euclidean space that quasi-isometrically embeds into the mapping class group or into Teichmüller space with the Weil--Petersson metric. The supplied summary gives the displayed ranks for connected punctured orientable surfaces of complexity at least one.
