Axiomatic stable homotopy 6 — My general feeling about stable homotopy categories is that they are like commutative rings.

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source proposes an analogy between stable homotopy categories and commutative rings, and asks to develop it, in particular by defining an object $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal C$ for a stable homotopy category $\mathcal C$. It does not specify axioms on $\mathcal C$, a tensor product, the desired universal property, or whether the result should be a space, a ringed space, or another structure. These choices are unresolved parts of the request.

To make the construction named in the supplied research summary precise, suppose additionally that $\mathcal T$ is an essentially small tensor triangulated category. A thick tensor ideal $\mathcal I$ is a full triangulated subcategory closed under direct summands and under $x\otimes y$ for $x\in\mathcal I$ and $y\in\mathcal T$. A prime thick tensor ideal is a proper such ideal $\mathcal P$ satisfying \[ x\otimes y\in\mathcal P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x\in\mathcal P\ \text{or}\ y\in\mathcal P. \] The Balmer spectrum is the set $\operatorname{Spc}(\mathcal T)$ of these prime ideals with the topology whose closed sets are \[ Z(\mathcal E)=\{\mathcal P:\mathcal P\cap\mathcal E=\varnothing\},\qquad \mathcal E\subseteq\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal T). \] In particular $\operatorname{supp}(x)=\{\mathcal P:x\notin\mathcal P\}$ is closed. For a large compactly generated tensor triangulated category $\mathcal C$, the usual small-category candidate is $\operatorname{Spc}(\mathcal C^c)$ when its compact objects form a tensor triangulated category; applying this requires the corresponding hypotheses. This conditional description records the construction invoked by the summary, without declaring it to settle every unspecified aspect of the original program.

Short English statement

Develop the idea that a stable homotopy category behaves like a commutative ring, including a suitable prime spectrum. The supplied summary reports the program substantially resolved through Balmer's tensor triangular geometry. The original source gives no axioms or success criterion, so that broad assessment cannot be read as a single fully specified theorem about every stable homotopy category.
