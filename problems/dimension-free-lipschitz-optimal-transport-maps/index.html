Math-3429

Dimension-Free Lipschitz Optimal Transport Maps

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a dimension-independent Lipschitz bound for a quadratic optimal-transport map, but it omits the definitions of $\gamma$, $\mu$, and $f$, and the relation between these objects. Consequently, it does not determine a complete conjecture with specified hypotheses.

The following describes the intended bound under a conditional Euclidean interpretation, which requires confirmation from the surrounding source text. For Borel probability measures $\gamma,\mu$ on ${\mathbb{R}}^n$ with finite second moments, let $\Pi(\gamma,\mu)$ denote the Borel probability measures on ${\mathbb{R}}^n\times{\mathbb{R}}^n$ having marginals $\gamma$ and $\mu$. A measurable map $T\colon{\mathbb{R}}^n\to{\mathbb{R}}^n$ is a quadratic optimal-transport map if $T_{\#}\gamma=\mu$, meaning $\gamma(T^{-1}(B))=\mu(B)$ for every Borel set $B$, and \[ \int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}|x-T(x)|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\gamma(x) =\inf_{\pi\in\Pi(\gamma,\mu)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n\times{\mathbb{R}}^n}|x-y|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\pi(x,y). \] These are conditional definitions, not hypotheses recovered from the supplied record; existence and uniqueness of such a map are not specified there.

Write $L=\|f\|_{\mathrm{Lip}}$, retaining the source notation because its precise convention is unspecified. The intended question is whether there is a finite-valued function \[ C\colon[0,\infty)\longrightarrow[0,\infty) \] such that, for every positive integer $n$ and every admissible choice of the source data in dimension $n$, the quadratic optimal-transport map from $\gamma$ to $\mu$ has a globally defined representative $\widetilde T$ satisfying \[ |\widetilde T(x)-\widetilde T(y)|\le C(L)|x-y| \qquad\text{for all }x,y\in{\mathbb{R}}^n. \] Here $C$ must be independent of $n$ and of all other data except $L$. The admissible class, the measures, the role and domain of $f$, and the meaning of the definite phrase "the quadratic optimal-transport map" remain unresolved. Thus this is a quantified template for the intended bound, not a complete formulation of the source conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the quadratic optimal-transport map from $\gamma$ to $\mu$ has a global Lipschitz bound controlled solely by $\|f\|_{\mathrm{Lip}}$, uniformly in dimension. The supplied excerpt omits the definitions and hypotheses needed to specify the measures and their dependence on $f$. The record labels the problem open; this is supplied curation, not an independently verified status.
