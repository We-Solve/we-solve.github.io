Zaremba Conjecture for Bounded Continued Fraction Digits

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}_{>0}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. For $k\geq 1$ and positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_k$, define the finite continued fraction recursively by \[ [0;a_1]=\frac{1}{a_1},\qquad [0;a_1,\ldots,a_k]=\frac{1}{a_1+[0;a_2,\ldots,a_k]}\quad(k\geq2). \] For a finite nonempty alphabet $\mathcal A\subset{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}$, let \[ D_{\mathcal A}=\left\{d\in{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}:\begin{array}{l} \text{there exist }b,k\in{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\text{ and }a_1,\ldots,a_k\in\mathcal A,\\ 1\leq b\leq d,\quad \gcd(b,d)=1,\quad b/d=[0;a_1,\ldots,a_k] \end{array}\right\}. \] Thus membership requires the existence of a finite expansion with digits in $\mathcal A$. No condition $a_k\geq2$ is imposed; in particular, $1=[0;1]$ supplies the denominator $d=1$.

Zaremba's conjecture (source Conjecture 13). There exists $A\in{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}$ such that \[ D_{\{1,2,\ldots,A\}}={\mathbb{N}}_{>0}. \] Equivalently, there is a single bound $A$ such that every positive integer $d$ admits a reduced fraction $b/d$ with a finite continued-fraction expansion whose positive digits are all at most $A$.

Qualifications from the supplied source. The report attributes the conjecture to Zaremba and motivates it by applications to random numbers and numerical integration. Its Theorem 14, attributed to Bourgain--Kontorovich, states the weaker density-one conclusion \[ \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{\lvert D_{\{1,\ldots,50\}}\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}\rvert}{N}=1, \] where $N$ tends through positive integers; equivalently, the numerator is $N+o(N)$. The excerpt attributes to Huang the sufficiency of the bound $5$ for this density-one conclusion, and to Magee--Oh--Winter a power-saving error $O(N^{1-\varepsilon})$ for some $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$. The exact alphabet attached to the latter assertion is not explicit in the supplied excerpt. The earlier quantitative error formula is damaged by extraction and is not reconstructed here. These contextual assertions are supplied attributions, not independently checked results, and none is asserted here to establish the every-denominator conjecture.

Short English statement

Zaremba's conjecture asks whether one fixed positive integer $A$ bounds the continued-fraction digits of a suitable reduced fraction for every positive denominator. The numerator and expansion may depend on the denominator. The denominator $1$ is included using $[0;1]=1$. The supplied record labels the conjecture open; its dated literature assessment and contextual results require independent review.
