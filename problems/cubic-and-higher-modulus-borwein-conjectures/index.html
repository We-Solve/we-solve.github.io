Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record names several coefficient-sign conjectures but does not supply their defining expressions, coefficient sign patterns, parameter domains, or quantifiers. Consequently, their precise mathematical statements cannot be reconstructed from this record alone.

The named targets must be distinguished as follows. Wang's Cubic Borwein Conjecture is identified as Conjecture 3 in the report's modulus-$3$ family, alongside the First and Second Borwein Conjectures. The Third Borwein Conjecture is a separate conjecture pertaining to modulus $5$. The excerpt also mentions further conjectures of Borwein type for moduli $4$ and $7$, based on computer experiments.

The requested problem, at the level supported by the supplied evidence, is:

Establish the full coefficient-sign assertions of Wang's Cubic Borwein Conjecture and the Third Borwein Conjecture pertaining to modulus $5$, and establish the additional Borwein-type conjectures for moduli $4$ and $7$ mentioned in the report.
This is an unresolved formulation of the intended targets, rather than a complete quantified mathematical statement: the expressions whose coefficients are considered, the asserted signs, all parameter ranges, and any exceptional or boundary cases are missing.

The report claims a uniform proof of the First and Second Borwein Conjectures and a proof of two thirds of Wang's Cubic Borwein Conjecture. It expresses confidence that its approach could prove three fifths of the modulus-$5$ Third Borwein Conjecture, while stating that further refinements would be needed for full proofs of the latter and of Wang's cubic conjecture. The excerpt does not identify the coefficient classes represented by these fractions. Its prospective modulus-$5$ claim must not be treated as an established partial proof.

Short English statement

The intended task is to prove the remaining cubic Borwein sign assertions and the related conjectures for moduli $4$, $5$, and $7$. The supplied excerpt omits the formulas, sign patterns, and parameter ranges needed for a precise mathematical statement. Its partial-proof claims and dated status assessment are supplied source claims requiring review.
