Positive consistency of Naimark's problem and a local hereditary reduction

OpenAI's claim

Within ZFC, the paper claims an infinite-dimensional simple unital C*-algebra carrying a faithful trace but only one unitary-equivalence class of nonzero irreducible representations, providing another counterexample to Naimark's problem. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
In ZFC, OpenAI claims a simple complex $C^*$-algebra $A$, infinite-dimensional and unital, admitting a faithful tracial state for which every nonzero irreducible representation lies in one unitary-equivalence class, while \[ A\not\simeq K(H)\quad\text{for every complex Hilbert space }H. \] Here $K(H)$ denotes compact operators. No separability assumption appears in the theorem, so the claim must not be read as a separable counterexample.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 297, 2026-09-24): Reduced HNN adjunctions join selected pure-state classes using matching peaking projections while preserving unique extensions of all old pure states. Reduced-word estimates prevent unintended identifications. Dependency-closed subiterations ensure countable determination, so a recursion of length continuum-successor eventually catches every pure state without a diamond prediction principle.
