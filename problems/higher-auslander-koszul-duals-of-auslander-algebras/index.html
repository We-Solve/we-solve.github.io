Higher-Auslander Koszul Duals of Auslander Algebras

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns representation-finite algebras in the context of finite-dimensional algebras. Its ground field, grading conventions, and precise meaning of Koszul dual are not specified. The following definitions describe a conditional formulation; these missing qualifications require review.

Let $k$ be a field and let $B$ be a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra. Write $\operatorname{mod}B$ for the category of finite-dimensional right $B$-modules. The algebra $B$ is representation-finite if this category has only finitely many isomorphism classes of indecomposable objects. Choose representatives $M_1,\ldots,M_t$ of these classes and put \[ M=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{t}M_i,\qquad A=\operatorname{End}_B(M). \] With this module-side convention, $A$ is the Auslander algebra of $B$.

For a finite-dimensional algebra $C$, its right global dimension $\operatorname{gldim}C$ is the supremum of the projective dimensions of right $C$-modules. Its right dominant dimension $\operatorname{domdim}C$ is the supremum of the integers $r\geq 0$ such that $I^0,\ldots,I^{r-1}$ are projective in a minimal injective resolution \[ 0\longrightarrow C_C\longrightarrow I^0\longrightarrow I^1\longrightarrow\cdots. \] The supremum may be infinite. Under the convention used here, $C$ is higher Auslander if there is an integer $n\geq 1$ such that \[ \operatorname{gldim}C\leq n+1\leq\operatorname{domdim}C. \] The intended indexing convention and treatment of semisimple algebras should be confirmed from the source.

To specify one usual meaning of Koszul dual, suppose additionally that $A$ has a nonnegative grading $A=\bigoplus_{j\geq0}A_j$, with $A_0$ semisimple, and is Koszul: the graded right module $A_0=A/A_{>0}$ has a graded projective resolution whose term in homological degree $i$ is generated in internal degree $i$. One convention then defines \[ A^{!}=\bigoplus_{i\geq0}\operatorname{Ext}^{i}_A(A_0,A_0) \] with Yoneda multiplication, using Ext over the underlying algebra and grading the result by homological degree. Opposite-algebra conventions also occur. The supplied record neither gives such a grading nor asserts that every Auslander algebra under consideration is Koszul. It also does not say whether an Ext algebra without a Koszul hypothesis is intended.

Classification question. Determine which representation-finite algebras $B$ have the property that the intended Koszul dual $A^{!}$ of their Auslander algebra is a finite-dimensional higher Auslander algebra; with the convention above, this means \[ \exists n\in\mathbb Z_{\geq1}\quad \operatorname{gldim}(A^{!})\leq n+1\leq\operatorname{domdim}(A^{!}). \] This formulation is conditional on resolving the field, grading, duality, and higher Auslander conventions. It does not impose a Koszul hypothesis on the original question without source confirmation. The supplied registry labels the question open; this is a supplied status, not an independently verified mathematical assessment.

Short English statement

Classify the representation-finite algebras whose Auslander algebras have higher Auslander Koszul duals. The supplied record leaves the ground field and the meaning and hypotheses of Koszul duality unspecified, so the displayed formulation remains conditional and requires human mathematical review.
