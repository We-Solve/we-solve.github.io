Math-7335

Doubly Singular Irreducible Partitions in Type A

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record requests a classification of doubly singular $(2,0)$-irreducible partitions. The formulation below uses the usual interpretation of $(2,0)$: Hecke parameter $q=-1$ over a field of characteristic zero, with irreducibility referring to a Specht module. These conventions and the intended base field require confirmation from the full source; they are not defined in the supplied fragment.

Fix a field $K$ of characteristic zero. A partition of an integer $n\geq 1$ is a finite sequence $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)$ with $\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_r>0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{r}\lambda_i=n$. Its conjugate partition is defined by $\lambda'_j=\#\{i:\lambda_i\geq j\}$. A partition is $2$-singular if some positive part occurs at least twice. It is doubly singular if both $\lambda$ and $\lambda'$ are $2$-singular.

Let $H_n(K,-1)$ be the type-A Iwahori--Hecke algebra with generators $T_1,\ldots,T_{n-1}$, relations \[ (T_i+1)^2=0,\qquad T_iT_{i+1}T_i=T_{i+1}T_iT_{i+1},\qquad T_iT_j=T_jT_i\quad (|i-j|>1), \] for all indices for which the displayed expressions are defined. Let $S_K^\lambda$ denote its Specht module indexed by $\lambda$. Under the interpretation above, $\lambda$ is $(2,0)$-irreducible if $S_K^\lambda$ is a nonzero module with no nonzero proper submodule.

The requested classification, under these conventions and for the intended choice of $K$, is to give an explicit necessary and sufficient combinatorial condition on $\lambda$, valid for every $n\geq1$, describing precisely the set \[ \mathcal C_K= \bigcup_{n\geq1} \left\{\lambda\vdash n: \lambda\text{ and }\lambda'\text{ are }2\text{-singular, and } S_K^\lambda\text{ is irreducible}\right\}. \] The source verification describes this as the remaining characteristic-zero case; that description is supplied attribution, not an independently verified claim.

The original statement additionally reports a conjectural construction from rectangular partitions, meaning partitions $(a^b)$ with $a,b\geq1$, by unspecified modifications. No modifications or exact criterion are supplied, so no equivalence with such a construction can be stated precisely here. The corrected statement deliberately omits that conjectural clause. Consequently, this draft records the classification target but does not reconstruct the missing conjecture.

Short English statement

Classify partitions whose rows and conjugate rows both contain repeated positive parts and whose associated type-A Hecke Specht modules are irreducible at parameter $q=-1$ in characteristic zero. This uses an explicitly stated conventional interpretation of $(2,0)$; the intended field and the omitted conjectural modifications of rectangles require source confirmation. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical assessment.
