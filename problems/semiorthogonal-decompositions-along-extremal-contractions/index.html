Semiorthogonal Decompositions Along Extremal Contractions

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a smooth projective variety $W$, write $D^b(W)=D^b(\operatorname{Coh}(W))$ for the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves. A Fano variety is a smooth projective variety whose anticanonical line bundle is ample. The source does not specify the ground field or its characteristic; these must be fixed before a fully specified conjecture is obtained. An extremal contraction is understood in the usual Mori-theoretic sense of a contraction associated with an extremal ray, subject to the unresolved qualifications concerning the omitted diagram below.

A semiorthogonal decomposition \[ D^b(W)=\langle\mathcal C_1,\ldots,\mathcal C_m\rangle \] consists of full triangulated subcategories that generate $D^b(W)$ and satisfy \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{D^b(W)}(C_j,C_i[n])=0 \quad\text{for all }j>i,\ n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ C_j\in\mathcal C_j,\ C_i\in\mathcal C_i. \] A subcategory is admissible if its inclusion has both left and right adjoints. Mutations of admissible semiorthogonal blocks use these adjoints to replace adjacent blocks by the corresponding left or right mutated blocks. The admissibility hypotheses and the precise mutation requirement intended in the source are not explicitly supplied.

Conjectural statement, with unresolved source qualifications. Let $X,Y,Z$ be smooth Fano varieties, and let \[ f\colon X\longrightarrow Y, \qquad h\colon X\longrightarrow Z \] be extremal contractions. The supplied question asks whether, for every such configuration in the intended class, there exist finite semiorthogonal decompositions \[ D^b(Y)=\langle\mathcal A_1,\ldots,\mathcal A_s\rangle, \qquad D^b(Z)=\langle\mathcal B_1,\ldots,\mathcal B_t\rangle \] and additional blocks $\mathcal P_1,\ldots,\mathcal P_r$ and $\mathcal Q_1,\ldots,\mathcal Q_u$ in $D^b(X)$ such that \[ \begin{aligned} D^b(X) &=\langle\mathbf Lf^*\mathcal A_1,\ldots,\mathbf Lf^*\mathcal A_s, \mathcal P_1,\ldots,\mathcal P_r\rangle,\\ D^b(X) &=\langle\mathcal Q_1,\ldots,\mathcal Q_u, \mathbf Lh^*\mathcal B_1,\ldots,\mathbf Lh^*\mathcal B_t\rangle, \end{aligned} \] with the two decompositions of $D^b(X)$ related by mutation. The question is existential in the decompositions; the supplied wording does not assert compatibility for every preassigned decomposition of either target.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether two extremal contractions from a smooth Fano variety to smooth Fano targets admit semiorthogonal decompositions whose pullback blocks extend to decompositions of the source variety, with the resulting decompositions related by mutation. The ground field, possible restrictions in the omitted diagram, the exact meaning of compatibility, and the required mutation relation remain unresolved. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
