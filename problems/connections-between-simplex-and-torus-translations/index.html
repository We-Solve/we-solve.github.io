Math-15589

Connections Between Simplex and Torus Translations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Terminology and missing construction. The supplied record names a "simplex map" and an "invariant fundamental set" but does not define either object. The following gives standard terminology only; it does not identify the map or impose hypotheses absent from the source.

A piecewise translation on a set $X\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$ is a map $T:X\to X$ with a partition $X=\bigsqcup_{i\in I}X_i$ and vectors $v_i\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$ such that \[ T(x)=x+v_i\qquad(x\in X_i). \] The supplied record does not specify the dimension, the set $X$, the partition, the translation vectors, or the rule assigning boundary points to pieces.

For a full-rank lattice $\Lambda\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$, the quotient ${\mathbb{R}}^n/\Lambda$ is a torus. A torus translation has the form \[ \tau_a(x+\Lambda)=x+a+\Lambda \] for some $a\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$. An exact fundamental set for $\Lambda$ contains exactly one representative of each coset in ${\mathbb{R}}^n/\Lambda$. Fundamental sets may instead be considered up to sets of measure zero or with boundary identifications; the supplied record does not specify the intended convention. It also does not specify whether invariance means $T(F)=F$, forward invariance $T(F)\subseteq F$, or another convention.

A subset is connected if it cannot be expressed as the union of two disjoint, nonempty subsets that are open in its relative topology. Under the usual convex-geometric convention, a polytope is the convex hull of finitely many points and is therefore convex. The source's phrase "possibly non-convex polytope" consequently requires a different, unspecified convention; a finite union of convex polytopes is one possible interpretation, but it is not selected here.

Source question, pending specification. Determine the relationship between the piecewise-translation and torus-translation roles of the intended simplex map. Determine whether its intended invariant fundamental set is connected and whether it has a possibly non-convex polytope shape in the source's intended sense.

The source supplies no construction, parameter range, lattice, translation, or quantifier specifying which simplex maps or fundamental sets are intended. It also does not specify whether the relationship sought is a factor relation, a conjugacy, or another correspondence. Thus the question above preserves the supplied target, but a uniquely quantified mathematical statement cannot be recovered from this record alone.

Short English statement

The question asks how an unspecified simplex map relates piecewise translations to translations of a torus, and whether its invariant fundamental set is connected and has a possibly non-convex polytope shape. The map, admissible parameters, fundamental-set convention, and intended meaning of shape must be recovered before a precise conjecture can be stated. The registry labels the problem open; this label is supplied curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
