Math-981

Return Probabilities and Stability of the Liouville Property

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$, and let $\mu$ be a discrete probability distribution on $G$. Symmetry means that $\mu(g)=\mu(g^{-1})$ for every $g\in G$. For independent increments $Y_1,Y_2,\ldots$ with distribution $\mu$, define $X_0=e$ and $X_n=Y_1\cdots Y_n$. Write $\mu^{(n)}$ for the distribution of $X_n$, so that $\mu^{(2n)}(e)=\mathbb P(X_{2n}=e)$.

A bounded function $f:G\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is $\mu$-harmonic if \[ f(x)=\sum_{g\in G}\mu(g)f(xg)\qquad(x\in G). \] The pair $(G,\mu)$ has the Liouville property if every bounded $\mu$-harmonic function on $G$ is constant.

Return-probability question. The supplied record asks whether a symmetric random walk satisfying a lower bound of the form \[ \mu^{(2n)}(e)\geq \exp(-c\sqrt n) \] necessarily has the Liouville property. Reading the bound as requiring a fixed constant $c>0$, independent of $n$, and validity for every positive integer $n$, the proposed implication is \[ \left[\exists c>0\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{N}},\ n\geq1: \mu^{(2n)}(e)\geq e^{-c\sqrt n}\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ \left[\text{every bounded $\mu$-harmonic function on $G$ is constant}\right]. \] The source does not explicitly supply the quantifiers on $c$ and $n$. It also does not specify whether $\operatorname{supp}\mu$ must generate $G$, whether $G$ must be countable or finitely generated, or whether $\mu$ must satisfy finite-support, moment, or entropy assumptions. These are unresolved qualifications of the intended question; the displayed implication records a conventional reading of the bound, not a resolution of the missing hypotheses.

Changing-generators question. A conventional precise interpretation uses simple random walks on finite symmetric generating sets. For a nonempty finite set $S\subseteq G$ with $S=S^{-1}$ and $\langle S\rangle=G$, put \[ \mu_S(g)=\begin{cases}|S|^{-1},&g\in S,\\0,&g\notin S.\end{cases} \] Under this interpretation, the question is whether, for every finitely generated group $G$ and every two such sets $S,T$, \[ (G,\mu_S)\text{ has the Liouville property} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (G,\mu_T)\text{ has the Liouville property}. \] The supplied record does not specify symmetry of the generating sets, the rule assigning a step distribution to a generating set, or whether arbitrary symmetric weights are intended. Consequently this simple-random-walk formulation remains an explicitly qualified interpretation requiring source review.

Short English statement

The record asks whether a return probability bounded below by $e^{-c\sqrt n}$ forces all bounded harmonic functions to be constant, and whether the Liouville property is invariant under changing a finite generating set. Essential assumptions on the first walk and the probability measures associated with generating sets remain unspecified. The supplied open status is dated curation, not independently verified here.
