Lifting Hypersimplicial Subdivisions to Zonotopal Tilings

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a classification of triples $(n,d,k)$, but does not define the role or range of $k$, the admissible subdivisions, or the extension relation. The following standard geometric model makes a possible interpretation explicit; its identification with the source question requires mathematical review.

Let $d\geq 1$, $n\geq d+1$, and $t_1<\cdots<t_n$ be real numbers. Set \[ a_i=(t_i,t_i^2,\ldots,t_i^d),\qquad C_{n,d}=\operatorname{conv}\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}. \] Write $\widehat a_i=(a_i,1)$, and define the linear map \[ \pi:{\mathbb{R}}^n\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^{d+1},\qquad \pi(x)=\sum_{i=1}^n x_i\widehat a_i. \] In the homogenized convention, the associated cyclic zonotope is \[ Z=\pi([0,1]^n)=\sum_{i=1}^n[0,\widehat a_i]. \] For an integer $0\leq k\leq n$, define the hypersimplex and its image by \[ \Delta(n,k)=\left\{x\in[0,1]^n:\sum_{i=1}^n x_i=k\right\}, \qquad P_k=\pi(\Delta(n,k)). \] Thus $P_k$ is the section of $Z$ by the hyperplane $H_k=\{(y,s):s=k\}$, and $P_1=C_{n,d}\times\{1\}$. In particular, for general $k$, the object being subdivided in this model is $P_k$, rather than literally $C_{n,d}$.

For this possible interpretation, call a subdivision hypersimplicial if it is a finite polytopal subdivision of $P_k$ whose cells are images under $\pi$ of faces of $\Delta(n,k)$. Here a polytopal subdivision covers its underlying polytope, contains every face of each cell, and has intersections that are common faces. Model a zonotopal tiling as a polytopal subdivision of $Z$ whose cells are images under $\pi$ of faces of $[0,1]^n$. Its restriction to $H_k$ consists of the nonempty intersections of its cells with $H_k$, with repeated cells identified.

Under precisely these conventions, the classification question would be to determine \[ \left\{(n,d,k): \begin{array}{l} \text{every hypersimplicial subdivision }\mathcal S\text{ of }P_k\\ \text{is the restriction to }H_k\text{ of some zonotopal tiling }\mathcal T\text{ of }Z \end{array}\right\}. \] The displayed parameter ranges and definitions specify this possible reading only. The supplied record does not establish that it intends these ranges, allows arbitrary rather than fine subdivisions or tilings, uses equality of the restricted subdivision rather than refinement, or imposes no additional conditions on projected face labels. It also does not specify whether the property is required for a fixed realization $(t_i)$ or for every realization. Consequently, this is a conditional formulation, not a fully recovered definitive statement of the source problem.

Short English statement

Classify the parameters for which every hypersimplicial subdivision can be obtained as a level section of a tiling of the associated cyclic zonotope. The supplied statement leaves the meaning of $k$ and several subdivision and lifting conventions unspecified; the mathematical formulation above is explicitly conditional. The record labels the problem partially solved, but its dated literature assessment has not been independently verified.
