Facet Connectivity in the Fan of $D_h$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record refers to an object $D_h$, its defining fan, and quantities $d_i$, but does not define these objects, the parameter $h$, the index range for $i$, or the ambient space. Consequently, the following is a conditional transcription of the intended question, rather than a fully specified mathematical conjecture.

Write $\Sigma_h$ for the fan referred to in the source and $I$ for the intended index set of the quantities $d_i$. For cones $F$ and $\tau$, write $F\prec_1\tau$ when $F$ is a face of $\tau$ with $\dim F=\dim\tau-1$; such a face is called a facet. Interpret two cones meeting in a common facet to mean that their intersection is a facet of each. These conventions explicate the terminology; the missing source context must confirm their applicability.

Question as supplied. For every cone $\sigma\in\Sigma_h$, do there exist a positive integer $r$, cones $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_r\in\Sigma_h$, an index $i\in I$, and a facet $F\prec_1\sigma_r$ such that \[ \sigma_1=\sigma, \qquad \sigma_j\cap\sigma_{j+1}\prec_1\sigma_j \quad\text{and}\quad \sigma_j\cap\sigma_{j+1}\prec_1\sigma_{j+1} \quad(1\leq j<r), \] and $F$ is defined by $d_i=0$? Under the usual interpretation of the last phrase as a defining hyperplane section, its intended expression is \[ F=\sigma_r\cap\{x:d_i(x)=0\}, \] where the meaning and domain of $d_i(x)$ must be supplied by the original context. For $r=1$, there are no consecutive-cone conditions.

Unresolved qualifications. The record says every cone, without restricting to maximal cones or cones of a specified dimension. Under the facet convention above, consecutive cones necessarily have equal dimension. Moreover, a fan ordinarily contains the zero cone, which has no facet under this convention. The intended range of cones and any exceptional conventions therefore require clarification; no restriction to maximal cones has been silently imposed. The permitted values and quantification of $h$, the construction of $D_h$ and $\Sigma_h$, and the meaning of the equations $d_i=0$ remain unspecified.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every cone in the fan defining $D_h$ can reach a cone with a facet defined by some $d_i=0$, through a finite sequence in which consecutive cones share a facet. The supplied record omits the definitions and parameter ranges needed for a complete statement, and its quantifier over every cone requires clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
