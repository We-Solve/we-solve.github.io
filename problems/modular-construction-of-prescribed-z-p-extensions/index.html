Modular Construction of Prescribed $\mathbb Z_p$-Extensions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be the prime implicit in the supplied record, and let ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ denote the additive topological group of $p$-adic integers. The record specifies a cyclotomic ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-extension $F_\infty/F$; write \[ \Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(F_\infty/F)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}_p. \] The permitted base fields $F$, restrictions on $p$, and admissible auxiliary prime sets $Q$ are not supplied. Thus the quantification over these data cannot be recovered from this record.

The requested construction, for the intended admissible data, is a field extension $L_Q/F_\infty$ such that $L_Q/F$ is Galois and \[ H=\operatorname{Gal}(L_Q/F_\infty)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}_p \] as topological groups. Writing $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L_Q/F)$, these requirements give an exact sequence of profinite groups \[ 1\longrightarrow H\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow\Gamma\longrightarrow1. \] Since $H$ is abelian, conjugation by a lift $\widetilde\gamma\in G$ of $\gamma\in\Gamma$ defines an action of $\Gamma$ on $H$ independent of the lift. If $\chi:\Gamma\to{\mathbb{Z}}_p^\times$ denotes the intended cyclotomic character, the prescribed cyclotomic action means that there is a topological group isomorphism $\iota:H\to({\mathbb{Z}}_p,+)$ satisfying \[ \iota(\widetilde\gamma h\widetilde\gamma^{-1}) =\chi(\gamma)\,\iota(h) \qquad(\gamma\in\Gamma,\ h\in H). \] The record does not specify the character or hypotheses making this interpretation available. In particular, descent of the full $p$-adic cyclotomic character to $\Gamma$ must not be assumed without suitable hypotheses.

For a prime $w$ of $F_\infty$, let $I_w\subseteq H$ denote its inertia subgroup in $L_Q/F_\infty$; this is independent of the choice of a prime above $w$, since $H$ is abelian. Unramifiedness at $w$ means $I_w=\{1\}$, and ramification means $I_w\ne\{1\}$. The requested extension must be unramified outside the primes prescribed by $Q$, and ramified at $Q$. However, the record does not identify the field whose primes constitute $Q$, their admissibility conditions, or whether ramification is required at every prescribed prime or merely at some prescribed prime. These alternatives are not interchangeable and remain unresolved.

The question asks whether such an extension can be constructed by modular methods. No precise meaning of the required modular construction is supplied. Consequently, the conditions above describe the recoverable part of the requested construction, not a fully specified universally quantified existence conjecture.

The supplied background also asserts an equivalence with a Leopoldt-type finiteness condition and says that a reciprocity input is proved. Neither the finiteness condition nor the reciprocity statement and their hypotheses are supplied, so neither can be incorporated as a precise assertion here.

Short English statement

For the intended cyclotomic extension $F_\infty/F$ and auxiliary primes $Q$, construct by modular methods a further ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-extension that is Galois over $F$, has the prescribed cyclotomic conjugation action, and has exactly the required ramification behavior. The admissible data, cyclotomic character, ramification quantifiers, and meaning of modular construction remain insufficiently specified.
