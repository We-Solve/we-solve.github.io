Recoverable notation. Whenever a group $G$, a coefficient ring $R$, a subgroup $K$, and a category $\operatorname{Mod}_R(G)$ of smooth representations have been specified, write \[ V^K=\{v\in V:kv=v\text{ for every }k\in K\}. \] The condition that $V$ is generated by its $K$-invariant vectors means \[ V=\operatorname{span}_R\{gv:g\in G,\ v\in V^K\}. \] Let $\mathcal C_K$ denote the full subcategory of such representations, and let $\operatorname{Irr}_R(G)$ denote the isomorphism classes of irreducible objects of $\operatorname{Mod}_R(G)$. These definitions explain the extracted notation; they do not determine the missing hypotheses on $G$, $R$, or $K$.

Extracted assertions requiring source recovery. The fragment denotes an induced representation by $P=\operatorname{ind}_K^G(1_R)$, with $1_R$ apparently the trivial $K$-representation on $R$. It reports that, if $P$ is projective, the invariants assignment $V\mapsto V^K$ gives a bijection of the schematic form \[ \{[V]\in\operatorname{Irr}_R(G):V^K\ne0\} \longleftrightarrow \{[M]:M\text{ is a simple }H_R(G,K)\text{-module}\}, \] and an equivalence \[ \mathcal C_K\simeq\operatorname{Mod}(H_R(G,K)). \] The fragment also mentions stability under subrepresentations, an extension from projectivity to "almost projectivity," and an Iwahori subgroup as an example. These are reported source claims, not assertions validated by this draft: the induction convention, coefficient hypotheses, definition of almost projectivity, Hecke algebra definition, module handedness, and precise role of stability under subrepresentations are absent or unclear. The opening claim about a convolution algebra being isomorphic to an opposite algebra has lost an essential referent.

A detached footnote appears to qualify some assertion as true for mod $p$ representations of $\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Q_p)$ but false in general. Both the attachment of the footnote and the restoration of its damaged group notation require verification; no assertion above is assigned that qualification without evidence.

Unresolved survey target. The recoverable question is only what is known and unknown about smooth modular representations and their Hecke algebras. The supplied material does not identify a particular classification problem, universal assertion, or conjectural equivalence to formalize as an open problem.
