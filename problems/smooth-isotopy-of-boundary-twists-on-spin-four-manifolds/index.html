Math-3575

Smooth Isotopy of Boundary Twists on Spin Four Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a closed, simply connected, smooth $4$-manifold with a spin structure; in particular, $X$ is oriented and $w_2(TX)=0$. Here closed means compact without boundary, and simply connected includes connectedness.

The supplied question asks which such $X$ have boundary twist $t_X$ smoothly isotopic to the identity. However, the supplied excerpt does not define $t_X$. Since $X$ itself has no boundary, the manifold on which this twist acts and the boundary conditions for the isotopy must be specified.

A standard candidate interpretation, stated here explicitly as a reconstruction requiring source confirmation, is the following. Choose a smoothly embedded closed ball $D^4\subset X$ and set \[ X^{\circ}=X\setminus\operatorname{int}(D^4),\qquad \partial X^{\circ}\cong S^3. \] Choose a collar $S^3\times[0,1]\subset X^{\circ}$, with $S^3\times\{0\}=\partial X^{\circ}$. Let $\gamma:[0,1]\to\operatorname{SO}(4)$ be a smooth based loop, constant at the identity near both endpoints, representing the nonzero element of $\pi_1(\operatorname{SO}(4))\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. Using the standard action on $S^3\subset{\mathbb{R}}^4$, define a diffeomorphism by \[ t_X(u,s)=(\gamma(s)u,s) \] on the collar and by the identity elsewhere. This diffeomorphism fixes the boundary pointwise.

Under this reconstructed interpretation, the classification question is: for which closed, spin, simply connected, smooth $4$-manifolds $X$ does there exist a smooth isotopy $F:X^{\circ}\times[0,1]\to X^{\circ}$ such that, writing $F_r=F(\cdot,r)$, \[ F_0=t_X,\qquad F_1=\operatorname{id}_{X^{\circ}},\qquad F_r\text{ is a diffeomorphism},\qquad F_r|_{\partial X^{\circ}}=\operatorname{id}_{\partial X^{\circ}} \quad\text{for every }r\in[0,1]? \] The source must confirm both this construction and the requirement that the isotopy fix the boundary pointwise. It must also clarify the intended dependence on the choices used in constructing the twist. This conditional formulation does not resolve those omissions. The adjacent discussion of homeomorphisms with unrestricted boundary behavior is not sufficient to determine the intended smooth isotopy convention.

Short English statement

The question asks which closed, spin, simply connected, smooth four-manifolds have a smoothly trivial boundary twist. A conventional interpretation uses a twist on the manifold with a ball removed and an isotopy fixing its boundary pointwise. The supplied excerpt does not define the twist or specify that isotopy convention, so this interpretation requires confirmation. The supplied record labels the general problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
