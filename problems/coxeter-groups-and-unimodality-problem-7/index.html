Coxeter Groups and Unimodality — Problem 7

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system, with length function $\ell:W\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ and Bruhat order $\leq$. Thus $x\leq w$ means that a reduced expression for $w$ contains a subword that is a reduced expression for $x$.

Fix an indeterminate $q^{1/2}$. The Hecke algebra $\mathcal H(W)$ over ${\mathbb{Z}}[q^{1/2},q^{-1/2}]$ has basis $\{T_w:w\in W\}$, identity $T_e$, and multiplication determined, for $s\in S$, by \[ T_sT_w=\begin{cases} T_{sw},&\ell(sw)=\ell(w)+1,\\ qT_{sw}+(q-1)T_w,&\ell(sw)=\ell(w)-1. \end{cases} \] Its bar involution is determined by $\overline{q^{1/2}}=q^{-1/2}$ and $\overline{T_w}=(T_{w^{-1}})^{-1}$. For $x\leq w$, the Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials $P_{x,w}(q)\in{\mathbb{Z}}[q]$ are characterized by bar invariance of \[ C'_w=q^{-\ell(w)/2}\sum_{x\leq w}P_{x,w}(q)T_x, \qquad \overline{C'_w}=C'_w, \] together with $P_{w,w}(q)=1$ and \[ \deg P_{x,w}\leq\frac{\ell(w)-\ell(x)-1}{2}\qquad(x<w). \] Set $P_{x,w}(q)=0$ when $x\not\leq w$.

Problem (Brenti, 2024, Problem 7). Find a combinatorial interpretation for Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials. In the usual general formulation, this asks for a uniform, explicitly specified combinatorial construction, from $(W,S)$ and $x\leq w$, of a finite set $\mathcal A_{x,w}$ and a statistic $\operatorname{stat}:\mathcal A_{x,w}\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ such that \[ P_{x,w}(q)=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_{x,w}}q^{\operatorname{stat}(a)}. \] Equivalently, the construction would interpret each coefficient $[q^k]P_{x,w}(q)$ as the number of objects of statistic $k$. The combinatorial objects and their statistic should be specified independently of the numerical coefficients being interpreted.

Unresolved scope and interpretation. Short English statement

Brenti asks for a combinatorial explanation of the coefficients of Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials, ideally by constructing objects whose statistic records the exponent of $q$. The supplied wording leaves the precise scope and admissible kind of interpretation unspecified. Its open label and its reference to a proof of nonnegativity are supplied source assessments, not independently verified conclusions.
