Kourovka Notebook Problem 10.45

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ be an associative ring with identity, and let $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ satisfy $n\geq 3$. Write $GL_n(R)$ for the group of invertible $n\times n$ matrices over $R$. For distinct indices $i,j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $e_{ij}$ be the corresponding matrix unit, and define the elementary subgroup by \[ E_n(R)=\left\langle I_n+r e_{ij}:r\in R,\ i\ne j\right\rangle. \] For subgroups $A,B\leq GL_n(R)$, set \[ [A,B]=\left\langle a^{-1}b^{-1}ab:a\in A,\ b\in B\right\rangle. \] A subgroup $N\leq GL_n(R)$ is normalized by $E_n(R)$ if $gNg^{-1}=N$ for every $g\in E_n(R)$.

The supplied problem, proposed by V. M. Petechuk, asks whether, for every such $R,n,N$, \[ E_n(R)\leq N \quad\text{or}\quad \exists\, I\lneq R\text{ an ideal such that } \Lambda_I[N,E_n(R)]=1. \] The supplied record does not define $\Lambda_I$, specify its action on a subgroup, or explicitly specify whether ideals are two-sided. Consequently, the second alternative is not fully determined by the supplied evidence.

If $\Lambda_I$ denotes the homomorphism induced by reduction modulo a two-sided ideal $I$, define \[ \pi_I:GL_n(R)\longrightarrow GL_n(R/I),\qquad (a_{ij})\longmapsto(a_{ij}+I). \] Under that interpretation, the requested assertion is precisely \[ \forall R\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq3}\ \forall N\leq GL_n(R),\qquad \left(\forall g\in E_n(R),\ gNg^{-1}=N\right) \Longrightarrow \left( E_n(R)\leq N\ \lor\ \exists\, I\lneq R\text{ a two-sided ideal},\ \pi_I\left([N,E_n(R)]\right)=\{I_n\} \right). \] Here $R$ ranges over associative rings with identity, and the identity matrix in the last expression is over $R/I$. This reduction-map formulation is conditional on confirmation of the undefined source notation; it is not an established identification of $\Lambda_I$.

Short English statement

Petechuk asks whether every subgroup of $GL_n(R)$, for an associative unital ring $R$ and $n\geq3$, that is normalized by the elementary subgroup either contains that subgroup or satisfies the stated commutator condition for some proper ideal. The source leaves $\Lambda_I$ undefined; a precise formulation using reduction modulo a two-sided ideal is given conditionally and requires source confirmation.
