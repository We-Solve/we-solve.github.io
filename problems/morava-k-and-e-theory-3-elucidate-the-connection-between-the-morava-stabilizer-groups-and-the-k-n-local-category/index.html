Morava K- and E-theory 3 — Elucidate the connection between the Morava stabilizer groups and the K(n)-local category.

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime $p$ and height $n$. Let $K(n)$ be Morava $K$-theory and $L_{K(n)}$ Bousfield localization at it. Choose a precise Morava $E$-theory $E$, its profinite stabilizer group $S$ and continuous action on $E$. The source expressly permits either $L_{K(n)}E(n)$ or a Lubin--Tate theory, and ideally asks for every reasonable choice; these choices must not be identified without comparison. For a Lubin--Tate theory the coefficient topology comes from its complete deformation ring; an extended stabilizer may include Galois automorphisms, depending on the chosen residue field and theory.

Distinguish the stable operation ring $E^*E=[E,\Sigma^*E]$, ordinary cooperations $E_*E=\pi_*(E\wedge E)$, and completed cooperations \[ E_*^\vee E=\pi_*L_{K(n)}(E\wedge E). \] The concrete task is to define these structures and prove the source's expected identifications, with grading/topology appropriate to each choice: \[ E^*E\cong E^*[[S]]_{\mathrm{tw}},\qquad E_*^\vee E\cong C_{\mathrm{cts}}(S,E_*). \] The twisted multiplication is specified on elementary symbols by $(a[s])(b[t])=a\,s(b)[st]$, reflecting the stabilizer action. Completion affects both coefficients and the profinite group: an inverse-limit description uses compatible coefficient quotients and open finite-index subgroups, or cofinal open normal subgroups with actions descending to the quotients. The function object must use the specified coefficient topology degreewise. Which stabilizer, ideal-adic completion, left/right structures and comparison maps apply are part of the problem, not extra established hypotheses.

More broadly, clarify how these stabilizer structures relate to the $K(n)$-local category. The source calls the formulas folklore, credits Hopkins and others with likely knowledge, and discusses Hovey--Strickland's memoir without claiming a published proof there. The summary labels the formulas standard/solved, but its first formula uses cooperation notation for the operation-group-ring assertion. This mismatch is not adopted.

Short English statement

Make precise and prove the stabilizer descriptions of Morava $E$-theory operations and completed cooperations, distinguishing the theory choices, coefficient/group completions and continuous functions.
