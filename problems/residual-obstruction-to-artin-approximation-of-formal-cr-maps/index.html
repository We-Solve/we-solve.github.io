Residual obstruction to Artin approximation of formal CR maps

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,0)\subset({\mathbb{C}}^n,0)$ and $(M',0)\subset({\mathbb{C}}^N,0)$ be germs of real-analytic manifolds, both assumed in the source to have Bloom--Graham--Kohn finite type at the distinguished points. Let $\rho=(\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_d)$ and $\rho'=(\rho'_1,\ldots,\rho'_{d'})$ be real-analytic defining functions. The intended CR or generic-submanifold hypotheses needed for this finite-type terminology are not stated separately in the record. Let \[ f(z)\in{\mathbb{C}}[[z_1,\ldots,z_n]]^N,\qquad f(0)=0. \] Write $\overline f(\zeta)$ for coefficientwise conjugation with an independent formal variable $\zeta$. Formal containment means that each component of $\rho'(f(z),\overline f(\zeta))$ belongs to the formal ideal generated by $\rho(z,\zeta)$; equivalently, \[ \rho'(f(z),\overline f(\zeta))=A(z,\zeta)\rho(z,\zeta) \] for a $d'\times d$ matrix of formal power series $A$. For hypersurfaces this reduces to the scalar multiplier displayed in the source.

The question proposed by Nordine Mir is whether, for every such $f$ and every integer $\ell\geq1$, there is a convergent holomorphic germ \[ f_\ell:({\mathbb{C}}^n,0)\longrightarrow({\mathbb{C}}^N,0),\qquad f_\ell(M)\subset M',\qquad j_0^\ell f_\ell=j_0^\ell f. \] Thus all coefficients of total degree at most $\ell$ must agree, and the approximating germ may depend on $\ell$. The target dimension $N$ and the containment for $f_\ell$ correct evident inconsistencies in the extracted question, which instead prints target ${\mathbb{C}}^n$ and containment for $f$. This is an approximation assertion, not an assertion that every $f$ converges. The source reports an affirmative answer for real-algebraic $M'$ and cites [MMZ].

The appended complexity sentence posits an unspecified germ $A_0\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n\times{\mathbb{C}}^N$ formally containing the graph and writes $c(f)=\dim A_0-N$, with $c(f)=0$ if and only if $f$ converges. This cannot be adopted as a definition without further conditions: the graph has dimension $n$, and an arbitrary containing germ does not determine an invariant. Even for a convergent map, choosing $A_0$ to be its graph gives $n-N$. A dimension excess over the source dimension and a suitable minimal analytic carrier would require an explicitly corrected definition; neither is silently imposed here.

The supplied research aid reports approximation in three settings: convergence results of Lamel--Mir when the formal image is not contained in the target's infinite D'Angelo-type locus; factorization through a fixed complex-analytic germ contained in $M'$, followed by complex Artin approximation; and targets locally biholomorphically equivalent to real-algebraic germs, by transfer of [MMZ]. Its proposed residual obstruction consists of divergent maps formally valued in the infinite-type locus without such a fixed positive-dimensional complex-analytic carrier. The precise hypotheses and the meaning of formal containment in that locus are not supplied, so these are reported reductions requiring verification, rather than additional theorems asserted here. The aid also reports a finite-type-target example with a divergent formal map, but supplies no equation for it. Such divergence alone would not negate jet approximation. The general real-analytic-target question is not declared settled by this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether every finite jet of a formal map between the specified finite-type real-analytic germs can be matched by an actual holomorphic map preserving the target. The algebraic-target case and the research aid's proposed reductions are retained with their source qualifications. The complexity formula is recorded as unresolved rather than used as an invariant.
