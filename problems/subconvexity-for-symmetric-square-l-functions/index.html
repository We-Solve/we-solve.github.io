Math-4479

Subconvexity for Symmetric-Square L-Functions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for subconvexity at the central point $s=\tfrac12$ for $L(\operatorname{sym}^{2}u_j,s)$ as the spectral parameter $t_j$ grows. Here $\operatorname{sym}^{2}$ denotes the symmetric-square lift. The record does not specify the family $(u_j)_j$, its underlying group, level, character, or the normalization relating $t_j$ to its spectral eigenvalue. Consequently, a fully specified unconditional formulation cannot be recovered from this record alone.

Under the conventional interpretation that $(u_j)_j$ is an intended family of Hecke--Maass cusp forms with Laplace eigenvalues $\tfrac14+t_j^2$, with all auxiliary arithmetic data fixed, let $L(\operatorname{sym}^{2}u_j,s)$ denote the standard finite symmetric-square $L$-function in the normalization with central point $s=\tfrac12$. Let $Q_j$ be its analytic conductor at that point. A precise conditional formulation of the requested subconvexity bound is: there exists $\delta>0$ such that, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a constant $C_{\varepsilon}>0$, depending on the fixed family data but independent of $j$, for which \[ \left|L\!\left(\operatorname{sym}^{2}u_j,\tfrac12\right)\right| \le C_{\varepsilon}Q_j^{\,1/4-\delta+\varepsilon} \qquad\text{for every }j. \] If the intended fixed-level spectral family has $Q_j\asymp(1+|t_j|)^2$, this is equivalently a request for some $\eta>0$ such that, for every $\varepsilon>0$, \[ \left|L\!\left(\operatorname{sym}^{2}u_j,\tfrac12\right)\right| \ll_{\varepsilon,\,\mathrm{fixed\ data}} (1+|t_j|)^{1/2-\eta+\varepsilon} \qquad\text{for every }j. \] These family assumptions and conductor specialization are explicitly conditional interpretations, not hypotheses supplied by the record. The target concerns growth in the form's spectral parameter $t_j$, with the evaluation point held at $s=\tfrac12$.

Short English statement

Obtain a uniform power saving over the convexity bound for central values of symmetric-square $L$-functions as the forms' spectral parameters grow. The intended family and its arithmetic data are missing, so the displayed formulation is conditional on specifying them. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved, reporting off-central or mixed-range results and strong moment estimates while identifying the central spectral-aspect bound as outstanding; this assessment has not been independently verified.
