Invariant Valuations for Noncompact Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the explicitly named example, let $p,q\geq 1$ be integers, put $V={\mathbb{R}}^{p+q}$, and define \[ Q_{p,q}(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{p}x_i^2-\sum_{i=p+1}^{p+q}x_i^2, \qquad O(p,q)=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}(V):Q_{p,q}(gx)=Q_{p,q}(x)\text{ for all }x\in V\}. \] This gives the indefinite orthogonal example mentioned in the source; the source does not fix $p,q$, a signature range, or a dimension.

For terminology in this linear model, let $\mathcal K(V)$ be the collection of compact convex subsets of $V$, including the empty set. For an abelian group $A$, an ordinary $A$-valued valuation is a map $\phi:\mathcal K(V)\to A$ satisfying $\phi(\varnothing)=0$ and \[ \phi(K\cup L)+\phi(K\cap L)=\phi(K)+\phi(L) \] whenever $K,L\in\mathcal K(V)$ and $K\cup L$ is convex. If a group $G$ acts on $\mathcal K(V)$, such a valuation is $G$-invariant when \[ \phi(gK)=\phi(K)\qquad(g\in G,\ K\in\mathcal K(V)). \] These definitions supply ordinary valuation terminology only. The source does not specify the value space, continuity, translation invariance, homogeneity, or a meaning of generalized valuation; generalized valuations need not be pointwise functions on all convex bodies.

Research question as supplied. For noncompact transformation groups $G$, with $O(p,q)$ as an explicit example, develop an appropriate generalized invariant valuation framework and corresponding kinematic formulas extending or replacing those of compact-group invariant valuation theory.

The supplied record does not determine a single quantified conjecture or a particular kinematic identity. It leaves unspecified the admissible groups and actions, ambient spaces, valuation spaces, regularity assumptions, averaging measures, and convergence or distributional interpretation of the proposed formulas. In particular, it does not state whether translations are included in the acting group, or whether the question is intended for every noncompact group or for selected classes. These choices remain unresolved and must be supplied before a precise existence or classification assertion can be formulated.

The supplied dated curation labels the question partially solved and reports classifications of continuous and generalized invariant valuations for indefinite orthogonal groups, while emphasizing that the extracted question is broader than that class. Short English statement

The question asks how invariant valuations and kinematic formulas should be formulated for noncompact groups, especially indefinite orthogonal groups $O(p,q)$. It is a research-program question: the source does not specify enough hypotheses to state one precise conjecture. The supplied curation reports partial progress for indefinite orthogonal groups, subject to independent verification and human mathematical review.
