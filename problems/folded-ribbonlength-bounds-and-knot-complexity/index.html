Folded Ribbonlength Bounds and Knot Complexity

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ denote a knot or link type, and write $\operatorname{Cr}(K)$ for its crossing number, meaning the minimum number of crossings among regular diagrams representing $K$. The supplied record concerns folded ribbon realizations $K_w$, with width parameter $w>0$, and their folded ribbonlength $\operatorname{Rib}(K_w)$. It does not define admissible folded ribbons, their length normalization, allowed overlaps or degeneracies, ribbon equivalence, or framing conventions. Thus a complete geometric definition cannot be recovered from this record alone.

For a fixed choice of those conventions, let $\mathcal A(K)$ denote the admissible folded ribbon realizations of $K$, and, whenever this class is nonempty, put \[ R_{\mathcal A}(K):=\inf_{K_w\in\mathcal A(K)}\operatorname{Rib}(K_w). \] This notation makes the dependence on the unspecified admissibility conventions explicit; it does not supply those conventions.

  1. Determine lower bounds for folded ribbonlength of knots and links. An invariant lower bound would take the form \[ \operatorname{Rib}(K_w)\geq B(K) \qquad\text{for every }K_w\in\mathcal A(K), \] equivalently $R_{\mathcal A}(K)\geq B(K)$. The source does not specify the desired function $B$, its dependence on crossing number or other invariants, or an intended sharpness criterion.

  2. Investigate whether folded ribbon realizations can be constructed with a crossing-number upper bound of exponent $p$ satisfying $1\leq p<3/2$. If the intended bound is universal over the knot and link types under consideration, its explicit quantifiers are \[ \begin{gathered} \exists p\in[1,3/2)\ \exists C>0\ \exists N_0\geq1\ \forall K:\\ \operatorname{Cr}(K)\geq N_0 \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists K_w\in\mathcal A(K)\quad \operatorname{Rib}(K_w)\leq C\,\operatorname{Cr}(K)^p. \end{gathered} \] Here $p,C,N_0$ are independent of $K$. The source does not settle whether this uniform scope is intended for all knots and links, for knots alone, or for particular families. This asymptotic formulation leaves types with crossing number below $N_0$ outside the stated bound.

  3. Investigate families with superlinear folded ribbonlength in crossing number. If this refers to the infimum over all admissible realizations, a precise formulation is to seek types $(K_n)_{n\geq1}$ with nonempty admissible classes such that \[ \operatorname{Cr}(K_n)\longrightarrow\infty, \qquad \frac{R_{\mathcal A}(K_n)}{\operatorname{Cr}(K_n)}\longrightarrow\infty. \] The source does not specify whether this limiting formulation, a particular superlinear rate, or merely growth of selected realizations is intended. These are different targets. The upper-bound and superlinear-family questions are retained as separate questions; the source gives no precise logical alternative between them.

  4. Determine how folded ribbonlength depends on ribbon equivalence and framing, including which admissible classes and infima are to be compared when either is fixed or varied. The equivalence relation, framing data, and requested comparison are not supplied, so this part remains an explicitly unresolved formulation task.

Short English statement

The problem asks for lower bounds and improved crossing-number upper bounds for folded ribbonlength, for families whose optimal folded ribbonlength grows superlinearly, and for the effects of ribbon equivalence and framing. The displayed formulations are conditional on geometric conventions absent from the supplied record. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
