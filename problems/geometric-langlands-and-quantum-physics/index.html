Geometric Langlands and Quantum Physics

Definitions and notation.
The geometric Langlands programme relates geometric objects associated with a reductive group and its Langlands dual, such as local systems and sheaf-theoretic data on moduli spaces of bundles over a curve. In quantum field theory a duality relates descriptions of physical observables or state spaces. The challenge is to make connections between such mathematical correspondences and fundamental physical symmetries precise.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Connect the Langlands program to fundamental physics symmetries.
Scope and source qualification.
This broad research objective fixes neither a group, space-time theory nor single proposed equivalence. The historical DARPA mirror was unavailable during this check.

Short English statement

Explain rigorous connections between Langlands correspondences and symmetries or dualities in fundamental physics.
