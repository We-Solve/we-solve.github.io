Consistency bounds and a width obstruction for two merged inner-model questions

Definitions and mathematical statement

This corrupted extraction merges printed Questions 8 and 9. Question 8 asks the consistency strength of $\mathrm{MM}(\mathfrak c)$, with $\mathfrak c=2^{\aleph_0}$. Question 9 is reconstructed by the aid as the consistency strength of \[ T_9=\mathrm{ZFC}+\neg\square_{\omega_2} +\neg\square(\omega_2)+2^{\omega_1}=\omega_2. \] The control character replacing the square symbols and the missing exponents make this an explicitly unverified reconstruction. Consistency strength asks for appropriate relative-consistency lower and upper bounds, ideally an equiconsistency characterization, not a proof of consistency in ZFC.

For the square conventions used by the aid, $\square_{\kappa,\lambda}$ is a coherent sequence on limit ordinals below $\kappa^+$, with a nonempty family of at most $\lambda$ clubs at each level, every club of order type at most $\kappa$, coherence under taking initial segments at limit points, and no common club thread. Write $\square_\kappa=\square_{\kappa,1}$; Jensen's weak square is $\square_{\kappa,\kappa}$. By contrast, $\square(\theta)$ is a one-club coherent threadless sequence of length $\theta$, without the $\kappa$-order-type bound. In particular $\square_{\omega_2}$ concerns length $\omega_3$, whereas $\square(\omega_2)$ concerns length $\omega_2$. Parentheses and subscripts must not be interchanged.

The aid uses $\mathrm{MM}(\mathfrak c)$ and $\mathrm{MM}^{++}(\mathfrak c)$ for size-restricted Martin's Maximum variants. The exact poset-size, stationary-set-preservation, dense-set, and stationary-name conventions are not reproduced; their intended full definitions require checking. With those conventions, it reports three normalizations: \[ \mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{MM}^{++}(\mathfrak c)\ \vdash\ \left(T_9\text{ holds}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \neg\square_{\omega_2}\right), \] where saying $T_9$ holds means its three added assertions hold in the model; \[ T_9\ \vdash\ \neg\square_{\omega_2,1} \ \wedge\ \square_{\omega_2,\omega_2}; \] and \[ \mathrm{ZFC}+2^{\omega_1}=\omega_2 +\neg\square(\omega_2)+\neg\square(\omega_3) \ \vdash\ T_9. \] Thus the target simultaneously forbids width one and permits Jensen's positive weak-square principle; it does not negate every square-width variant. These implications simplify the target but do not determine either consistency strength.

The original Question 8 gives the upper bound $\mathrm{AD}_{{\mathbb{R}}}+\text{``}\Theta\text{ is regular''}$, attributed to Woodin's $\mathbb P_{\max}$ book, and a safe lower bound $L({\mathbb{R}})\models\mathrm{AD}$, attributed to Steel--Zoble, adding that more may be known. Here $\Theta$ denotes the supremum of ordinals that are surjective images of ${\mathbb{R}}$, $\mathrm{AD}_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ concerns length-$\omega$ games with real moves, and $\mathrm{AD}$ is ordinary determinacy. The ambient choice/dependent-choice theory for these bounds is not supplied.

For Question 9 the source says its upper bound is weaker than $\mathrm{AD}_{{\mathbb{R}}}+\text{``}\Theta\text{ is Mahlo''}$, without giving a precise weaker theory. It states PD as a lower bound, with $L({\mathbb{R}})\models\mathrm{AD}$ only tentatively in parentheses. PD means projective determinacy. These are reported bounds and uncertainties, not a current independent literature assessment.

A further damaged fragment mentions an index set with $\operatorname{cf}(\theta_\alpha)\ge\aleph_2$ and $\theta_\alpha$ regular in HOD. Its sequence, index range, ambient model, and asserted property of that set are missing, so it is not made into an additional consistency hypothesis. The trailing footnote about alternating cofinalities, nonprojection in $L$, and an elementary embedding $L\to L$ has no complete hypotheses or attachment; no nontrivial-embedding or large-cardinal conclusion is inferred from it. The aid states that the exact consistency strengths remain open in its dated assessment.

Short English statement

The record merges two consistency-strength questions and corrupts their square notation. The aid separates them and gives three implications for the second target, including coexistence of failed width-one square with positive weak square. Its historical determinacy bounds, tentative stronger lower bound, and damaged fragments remain explicitly qualified rather than turned into an equiconsistency theorem.
