For $M/N\to \alpha >0$, bi-orthogonally invariant M-by-N disorder whose singular empirical law tends in probability to a compactly supported nu and whose singular values have no outliers from supp(nu), does the bounded-continuous activation pressure converge in probability and mean to a deterministic function of (alpha,nu,f)? Spherical perceptron pressure with orthogonally invariant disorder

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. A spherical perceptron with bi-orthogonally invariant disorder is claimed to have an explicit pressure formula for bounded continuous activation and compact singular-value limits without outliers.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $M/N\to\alpha>0$, $r_N=\min(M,N)$, and let the law of a real $M\times N$ matrix $A_N$ be invariant under left and right orthogonal multiplication. Suppose its singular values $s_{N,r}$ satisfy, in probability, \[ r_N^{-1}\sum_{r=1}^{r_N}\delta_{s_{N,r}}\Longrightarrow\nu,\qquad \max_{r\le r_N}\operatorname{dist}(s_{N,r},\operatorname{supp}\nu)\to0, \] for a deterministic compactly supported law $\nu$ on $[0,\infty)$. For bounded continuous $f$, with $\sigma_N$ the uniform probability on $\|x\|^2=N$, put \[ F_N=N^{-1}\log\int e^{\sum_{r=1}^Mf((A_Nx)_r)}\,d\sigma_N(x). \] Then $F_N$ converges in probability and mean to a deterministic $F_{\alpha,\nu}(f)$. Its finite spectral-block formula is \[ \sup_{\rho_i>0,\ \sum_i\rho_i=1} \left\{\frac12\sum_iv_i\log(\rho_i/v_i)+ \alpha P_p^f\left((s_j^2\rho_j/\alpha)_{j=1}^h\right)\right\}. \] Here positive singular-value blocks have $n_j/M\to p_j>0$, $v_j=\alpha p_j$, and $\sum_jp_j=\min(1,1/\alpha)$; when $\alpha<1$ an input null block has $v_0=1-\alpha$. The angular functional $P_p^f(a)$ is the deterministic limit, as $M\to\infty$, of \[ M^{-1}\log\int\exp\left(\sum_{r=1}^Mf\left(\sum_jy_{j,r}\right)\right) \prod_jd\sigma_{E_j,Ma_j}(y_j), \] where mutually orthogonal Haar subspaces $E_j\subset\mathbb R^M$ have dimension ratios $p_j$, and $\sigma_{E_j,Ma_j}$ is uniform probability on their spheres of squared radius $Ma_j$ (a point mass for $a_j=0$). Taking increasingly fine operator-norm spectral approximations gives $F_{\alpha,\nu}(f)$. Blocks of vanishing proportion are omitted.

Outline of the argument

Marked probability cascades encode the overlap hierarchy while orthogonal invariance controls coordinate cavity increments. Conditional change-of-measure calculations and spectral data determine the disorder contribution; smooth compactly supported activations are handled first and then approximation removes that regularity.
