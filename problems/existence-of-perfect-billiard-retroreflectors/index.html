Math-7348

Existence of Perfect Billiard Retroreflectors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A billiard trajectory travels along straight lines between reflections. At a regular reflecting boundary point with unit normal $n$, specular reflection changes an incoming unit velocity $v$ to \[ v-2\langle v,n\rangle n. \] When a trajectory has incoming and outgoing asymptotic unit velocities $v_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $v_{\mathrm{out}}$, retroreflection means \[ v_{\mathrm{out}}=-v_{\mathrm{in}}. \] This condition concerns reversal of direction; it does not require the outgoing ray to retrace the incoming line.

The supplied question asks whether a perfect billiard retroreflector exists: a body that reverses the direction of every incident particle or ray. Schematically, if $\mathcal B$ denotes the intended class of bodies and $\mathcal I(B)$ denotes all incident ray data for $B$, the requested property is \[ \exists B\in\mathcal B\quad \forall z\in\mathcal I(B),\qquad v_{\mathrm{out}}(B,z)\text{ exists and } v_{\mathrm{out}}(B,z)=-v_{\mathrm{in}}(z). \] Here the existence of an outgoing velocity expresses the requirement that each incident ray actually emerge with reversed direction.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the ambient dimension, the admissible class $\mathcal B$, or a complete definition of $\mathcal I(B)$. In particular, it does not state boundedness, boundary regularity, allowable cavities or reflecting components, or conventions for tangential impacts, singular boundary points, trapped trajectories, and infinitely many reflections. Thus the displayed formula records the intended universal property but is not a fully specified existence problem until those choices are supplied. The source says every incident particle or ray; replacing this by almost every incident datum would change the stated quantifier and is not justified by this record.

Short English statement

Does there exist a billiard body that sends every incident particle or ray back with exactly the opposite velocity direction? The source leaves the dimension, allowable bodies, and exceptional-trajectory conventions unspecified, so these require clarification before the question has a fully precise formulation.
