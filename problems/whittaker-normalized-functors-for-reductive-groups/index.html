Whittaker-Normalized Functors for Reductive Groups

Definitions and mathematical statement

Formulation and unresolved data. The supplied record uses a reductive triple $(G,B,T)$, a Whittaker datum $\psi$, a category $\operatorname{Rep}_{[T,1]}G$, an algebra $Z_G$, a dual group $\check G$, and a space $\mathcal X_{\check G}$. It does not specify the ground field, the representation coefficients, the precise class of groups or representations, the definition of the subscript $[T,1]$, or the construction of $\mathcal X_{\check G}$. Thus the following is a conditional statement of the supplied conjectural target, rather than a completely specified conjecture.

Write \[ \mathcal C_G=D^+(\operatorname{Rep}_{[T,1]}G), \qquad \mathcal D_G=D^+\operatorname{QCoh}([\mathcal X_{\check G}/\check G]). \] Here $D^+$ denotes bounded-below derived-category notation, $\operatorname{QCoh}$ denotes quasi-coherent sheaves, and $[\mathcal X_{\check G}/\check G]$ denotes the indicated quotient stack, once the space and group action have been specified. The precise derived-category conventions remain unspecified.

Conjectural target as supplied. For every reductive triple $(G,B,T)$ with Whittaker datum $\psi$ in the intended, unspecified setting, does there exist a fully faithful $Z_G$-linear functor \[ R_G^\psi:\mathcal C_G\longrightarrow\mathcal D_G \] with all of the following properties?

  1. For every parabolic subgroup $P$ with $B\subseteq P\subseteq G$, the functor is compatible with parabolic induction.
  2. When $G$ is a torus, the functor agrees with the canonical equivalence referred to in the source record.
  3. The compactly induced Whittaker object is sent to the structure sheaf: \[ R_G^\psi(W_G^\psi)\simeq \mathcal O_{[\mathcal X_{\check G}/\check G]}, \] where $W_G^\psi$ is only a label here for the object mentioned in the record; its construction and placement in $\mathcal C_G$ are not supplied.
Full faithfulness means that, for every $A,B\in\mathcal C_G$, the induced map \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C_G}(A,B) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D_G}(R_G^\psi A,R_G^\psi B) \] is a bijection. The requirement of $Z_G$-linearity presupposes specified compatible actions of $Z_G$ on the two categories, which the record does not provide. Likewise, the parabolic compatibility cannot be made into a precise comparison isomorphism without the relevant functors, their normalizations, and any coherence requirements. The torus equivalence and the compact-induction normalization also require source definitions absent from the supplied record. These omissions must be resolved before this schematic target can be treated as a precise mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks for fully faithful $Z_G$-linear functors from the indicated representation categories to quasi-coherent sheaves on dual-group quotient stacks, compatible with every parabolic induction and normalized by the torus equivalence and the Whittaker object. Essential definitions and comparison functors are missing, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
