Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 5.1

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H^n$ be real hyperbolic space and $G\le\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$. Under the standard axis convention set \[ \operatorname{Ax}(G)=\{\operatorname{axis}(g):g\in G\text{ is loxodromic}\}, \] where an axis is the unoriented geodesic line on which $g$ acts by nonzero translation, possibly with a rotational component in higher dimensions. Parabolic and elliptic elements contribute no such axes. Two subgroups in this fixed ambient group are commensurable if their intersection has finite index in each.

Anderson's literal source question is whether, for every pair of finitely generated discrete groups $G_1,G_2\le\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$, \[ \operatorname{Ax}(G_1)=\operatorname{Ax}(G_2) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad [G_1:G_1\cap G_2]<\infty\ \text{ and }\ [G_2:G_1\cap G_2]<\infty. \] The source gives no range of $n$; the usual setting is $n\ge2$. The supplied later research summary warns that the literal assertion is false for elementary parabolic groups, which have empty axis sets, and identifies the intended open question as the same implication with both groups non-elementary. In the usual convention non-elementary means that the limit set in $\partial\mathbb H^n$ has more than two points. The non-elementary restriction is an explicit source-supplied correction, not a hypothesis present in the original displayed question.

For context, the summary proposes studying the axis-set stabilizer and commensurator, \[ \Sigma(G)=\{h\in\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n):h\operatorname{Ax}(G)=\operatorname{Ax}(G)\},\qquad \operatorname{Comm}(G)=\{h:hGh^{-1}\text{ is commensurable with }G\}, \] and the equality $\Sigma(G)=\operatorname{Comm}(G)$. No precise reduction theorem or hypotheses establishing equivalence with the question are supplied, so this is recorded as a suggested framework rather than an asserted equivalent formulation.

The supplied summary reports affirmative results when both groups are arithmetic Fuchsian, or both are arithmetic Kleinian, and partial results for other Kleinian classes. It describes the general non-arithmetic non-elementary problem as open, including the case of Fuchsian groups isoaxial with $\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{Z}})$, where isoaxial means equality of the axis sets. These are supplied status claims, not independently verified results.

Short English statement

If two finitely generated discrete hyperbolic isometry groups have exactly the same loxodromic axes, must they share a subgroup of finite index in each? The later source summary says a non-elementary hypothesis is needed and reports arithmetic Fuchsian and Kleinian cases, while leaving the general non-arithmetic question open. The original statement and this explicitly qualified intended version are kept distinct.
