Math-3739

Maximal Real Betti Numbers of Tridegree Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $Y=(\mathbb{P}^{1}({\mathbb{C}}))^{3}$, equipped with coordinatewise complex conjugation $\sigma$. Under the standard interpretation of a real algebraic surface in this ambient space, $X\subset Y$ is a nonsingular complex algebraic hypersurface invariant under $\sigma$, and its real part is \[ \mathbb{R}X=X^{\sigma}=X\cap(\mathbb{P}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}))^{3}. \] For a prescribed multidegree $\mathbf d=(d_1,d_2,d_3)$, let $\mathcal S_{\mathbf d}$ denote the class of such hypersurfaces of multidegree $\mathbf d$. Here multidegree means that a defining multihomogeneous equation has degree $d_i$ in the homogeneous coordinates of the $i$-th projective-line factor; equivalently, its divisor line bundle is \[ \mathcal O_Y(d_1,d_2,d_3)=\bigotimes_{i=1}^{3}\pi_i^{*}\mathcal O_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}(d_i), \] where $\pi_i$ is the projection onto the $i$-th factor.

For a specified coefficient field $K$, define \[ b_1(\mathbb{R}X;K)=\dim_K H_1(\mathbb{R}X;K). \] The extremal question is to determine, for every prescribed multidegree for which $\mathcal S_{\mathbf d}$ is nonempty, the largest attainable value \[ M_K(\mathbf d)=\max_{X\in\mathcal S_{\mathbf d}} b_1(\mathbb{R}X;K). \] The supplied statement writes $b_1(\mathbb{R}X)$ without specifying coefficients. Thus this formula makes the coefficient choice explicit but does not resolve the intended choice: rational coefficients and $\mathbb F_2$ coefficients can give different first Betti numbers for nonorientable real surfaces. The source also does not specify the allowed range of multidegrees or whether irreducibility or connectedness is required. No such additional restrictions are imposed here; their intended role requires review. Coordinatewise conjugation is the standard real structure used in this formulation, but the supplied statement does not explicitly identify the real structure.

Short English statement

For each prescribed multidegree, determine the greatest first Betti number attainable by the real part of a nonsingular real algebraic surface in the product of three complex projective lines. The intended homology coefficients, degree range, and any irreducibility or connectedness requirements need clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
