A wall-crossing formula describes how specified mathematical data change when a parameter crosses a specified wall in a parameter space. In this problem, the formulas to be compared are those arising from family Floer homology and the Kontsevich--Soibelman wall-crossing formulas. Refined tropical counts are counts of tropical objects with additional weighting or refinement data; the source does not specify the objects, weights, or refinement parameter intended here.
