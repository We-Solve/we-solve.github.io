Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 87

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H^n_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ be real hyperbolic $n$-space. A lattice $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n_{{\mathbb{R}}})$ is discrete with finite-covolume quotient. Arithmetic is understood in the usual algebraic-group/integral-points sense, up to commensurability; the source does not restrict to lattices of simplest quadratic-form type. Both cocompact and noncocompact lattices and possible torsion remain in scope.

A group is coherent if every finitely generated subgroup is finitely presented. Noncoherence means that at least one finitely generated subgroup fails to be finitely presented.

Conjecture (M. Kapovich, L. Potyagailo, E. B. Vinberg). For every integer $n\geq4$ and every arithmetic lattice $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n_{{\mathbb{R}}})$, there exists \[ H\leq\Gamma\quad\text{with $H$ finitely generated but not finitely presented}. \] No normality, finite-index, orientation-preserving, or torsion-free requirement on this subgroup or lattice is added.

The source refers to [38] for partial results, but its bibliography entry is absent from the record. It also says every lattice in $\operatorname{Isom}({\rm HH}^n)$, $n\geq2$, has Property T. The extracted "HH" appears to denote quaternionic hyperbolic space, a different ambient geometry from the real hyperbolic space of the conjecture, and requires notation checking. Property T means that a unitary representation with almost invariant unit vectors has a nonzero invariant vector. This contextual remark must not be read as assigning Property T to the real-hyperbolic lattices in the conjecture.

The summary reports the all-arithmetic-lattice noncoherence assertion still open and makes a qualitative remark about Property T and high-rank linear groups. It supplies no implication proving noncoherence from Property T and no precise additional results. These are unverified supplied status/context claims.

Short English statement

Show that every arithmetic real-hyperbolic lattice in dimension at least four contains a finitely generated subgroup that is not finitely presented. The separate Property T comment concerns a differently notated, apparently quaternionic space and does not supply the desired noncoherence theorem.
