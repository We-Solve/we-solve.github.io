Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 1$, let $S^n$ denote the standard $n$-sphere and $B^n$ the closed $n$-ball. Set $X=S^1\times B^3$, so that $\partial X=S^1\times S^2$.

The supplied fragment is headed "Problem 3. (Slice missing slice disc problem)." A tentative normalization of its damaged mathematical typography places a knot \[ K\subset S^1\times S^2=\partial(S^1\times B^3). \] It describes $K$ by reference to an unavailable Figure 1 and asserts that $K$ bounds two "obvious ribbon discs" $D_1$ and $D_2$. On the natural reading that these discs lie in $X$, the boundary relations would be \[ \partial D_1=\partial D_2=K. \] This ambient interpretation is provisional: the supplied extraction does not provide a complete specification of the discs, their embedding category, or the convention for "ribbon." No additional ribbon condition is imposed here.
