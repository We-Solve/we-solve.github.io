Open Problems in Pluripotential Theory — Question 9

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt leaves the ambient geometric hypotheses and the meaning of its bracket inequality unstated. The following is a conditional formulation in the conventional compact Kähler setting; this setting and the interpretation as a total-mass comparison require confirmation against the source context.

Let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension $n\geq 1$, and let $T,S$ be positive closed real $(1,1)$-currents on $X$ representing the same cohomology class. Choose a smooth closed real $(1,1)$-form $\theta$ in this class and $\theta$-plurisubharmonic potentials $u,v$, so that \[ T=\theta+\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial u, \qquad S=\theta+\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial v. \] Here a $\theta$-plurisubharmonic potential is a quasi-plurisubharmonic function whose displayed associated current is positive. Say that $T$ is more singular than $S$ if there exists $C\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that $u\leq v+C$ on $X$.

Write $\langle T^n\rangle$ for the non-pluripolar Monge--Ampère measure of $T$. Locally, if $T=\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial w$ with $w$ plurisubharmonic, this measure is defined by the increasing limit \[ \lim_{j\to\infty} \mathbf{1}_{\{w>-j\}} \left(\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial\max\{w,-j\}\right)^n, \] where the powers of the locally bounded truncations are understood in the Bedford--Taylor sense. Define its total mass by \[ M(T):=\int_X\langle T^n\rangle. \]

The total-mass interpretation of the supplied question is: for every such $X,T,S$, does \[ [T]=[S]\quad\text{and}\quad u\leq v+C\text{ for some }C\in{\mathbb{R}} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad M(T)\leq M(S) \] hold without assuming that either current has small unbounded locus?

The source defines small unbounded locus to mean that local potentials are locally bounded outside a closed complete pluripolar set. It attributes the comparison under this additional assumption for both currents to [BEGZ10, Theorem 1.16], and describes that assumption as apparently technical. The question is attributed there to Boucksom--Eyssidieux--Guedj--Zeriahi.

The excerpt writes $\langle T^n\rangle\leq\langle S^n\rangle$ without integrals. If those brackets denote measures rather than their total masses or top-degree cohomology classes, the displayed inequality instead means that $\langle S^n\rangle-\langle T^n\rangle$ is a positive measure. That is a different formulation, and the supplied excerpt alone does not resolve the notation.

Short English statement

Under an explicitly provisional compact Kähler and total-mass interpretation, ask whether making a positive closed $(1,1)$-current more singular, while preserving its cohomology class, can only decrease its non-pluripolar Monge--Ampère mass. The source asks to remove the small-unbounded-locus hypothesis. Its ambient hypotheses and whether its inequality compares masses or measures require confirmation.
