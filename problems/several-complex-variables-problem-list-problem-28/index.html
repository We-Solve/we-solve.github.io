Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$, and let $D\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ be a pseudoconvex domain with real-analytic boundary. Here real-analytic boundary means that each boundary point has a neighborhood $U$ with a real-valued real-analytic defining function $\rho$ such that $D\cap U=\{\rho<0\}$ and ${\,\mathrm{d}}\rho\neq 0$ on $\partial D\cap U$. The source describes $D$ as "real analytically bounded"; whether this also requires $D$ to be bounded is not explicit in the supplied record.

For a fixed degree $1\leq q\leq n$, let $L^2_{(0,q)}(D)$ denote the space of square-integrable $(0,q)$-forms with respect to Euclidean volume. Let $\bar\partial$ be the maximal distributional Cauchy--Riemann operator, and let $\bar\partial^{\ast}$ be its Hilbert-space adjoint. The $\bar\partial$-Neumann Laplacian is \[ \square_q=\bar\partial\bar\partial^{\ast}+\bar\partial^{\ast}\bar\partial, \] with domain \[ \operatorname{Dom}(\square_q)=\left\{u\in\operatorname{Dom}(\bar\partial)\cap\operatorname{Dom}(\bar\partial^{\ast}):\ \bar\partial u\in\operatorname{Dom}(\bar\partial^{\ast}),\ \bar\partial^{\ast}u\in\operatorname{Dom}(\bar\partial)\right\}. \] These adjoint-domain requirements encode the $\bar\partial$-Neumann boundary conditions.

Write $\mathcal A^\omega_{(0,q)}(\overline D)$ for forms whose coefficients, near every point of $\overline D$, are restrictions of functions real analytic in the $2n$ real coordinates on an open neighborhood of that point.

The source asks whether the $\bar\partial$-Neumann problem on every such $D$ is globally real-analytic hypoelliptic up to the boundary. A standard precise operator interpretation is whether \[ \forall u\in\operatorname{Dom}(\square_q),\qquad \square_q u\in\mathcal A^\omega_{(0,q)}(\overline D) \ \Longrightarrow\ u\in\mathcal A^\omega_{(0,q)}(\overline D). \] The supplied statement does not specify the form degree, its quantifier, or the intended solution normalization. Consequently, the displayed implication is an explicit candidate interpretation, not a resolution of those omissions. In particular, the record does not determine whether the intended assertion concerns this implication for all solutions or analytic regularity of a distinguished $\bar\partial$-Neumann solution operator.

Short English statement

Does a pseudoconvex domain with real-analytic boundary always have global real-analytic regularity up to the boundary for the $\bar\partial$-Neumann problem? The source leaves the form degree, the precise solution formulation, and any additional boundedness requirement unspecified. It attributes the question to D. S. Tartakoff.
