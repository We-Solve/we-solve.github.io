Math-1370

Minimum difference sets in fixed dimension

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For integers $d\geq1$ and $n\geq d+1$, define \[ g_d(n)=\min\{|A-A|:A\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}^d,\ |A|=n, \operatorname{aff}_{{\mathbb{R}}}(A)={\mathbb{R}}^d\}. \] Y. Stanchescu's question is to determine this pointwise minimum. Full-dimensional means not contained in any proper affine hyperplane, not merely that the linear span contains the origin.

The source remarks quote Freiman--Heppes--Uhrin's bound $|A-A|\geq(d+1)|A|-d(d+1)/2$, sharp in the stated sense for $d=1,2$, and Stanchescu's best-possible three-dimensional bound $|A-A|\geq(9/2)|A|-9$. For $d\geq4$ they conjecture $|A-A|\geq(2d-2+1/(d-1))|A|-C_d$. Sharpness of a general inequality does not by itself specify equality for every cardinality.

The attached aid attributes to Conlon--Lim the stronger large-set inequality, for each fixed $d\geq2$ and sufficiently large full-dimensional finite $A\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$, \[ |A-A|\geq\left(2d-2+\frac1{d-1}\right)|A|-(2d^2-4d+3). \] It consequently applies to the lattice sets here. This is a supplied literature conclusion resolving the large-set sharp inequality, not automatically the exact pointwise minimum at every residue or every small $n$. In dimension one the ordinary value is $g_1(n)=2n-1$.

The aid's two-height simplex-cylinder formula can be made explicit in standard lattice coordinates as follows. For $d\geq2$, put $q=d-1$, $U=\{0,e_1,\ldots,e_{d-2}\}$, and $P_a=\{je_{d-1}:0\leq j<a\}$. For positive integers $a,b$ define \[ B_{a,b}=(U+P_a)\cup(e_d-U+P_b). \] It has $q(a+b)$ points and is full-dimensional when $\max(a,b)\geq2$. The recorded exact count is \[ |B_{a,b}-B_{a,b}|=(q^2-q+1)(2\max(a,b)-1)+q(q+1)(a+b-1). \] For balanced heights $a=b$, it matches the displayed large-set lower bound when $n$ is a sufficiently large multiple of $2(d-1)$. The aid further reports an $O_d(1)$-width all-residue window for $g_d(n)$ using balanced constructions, odd-cardinality rounding of $|A-A|$, and dimension-preserving deletions. Its explicit window endpoints and finite thresholds are not supplied. The remaining exact pointwise values are not claimed determined by that statement.

Short English statement

Determine the smallest difference set of an $n$-point full-dimensional lattice set. The supplied aid reports the sharp large-set inequality and constructions matching it for sufficiently large multiples of $2(d-1)$, while retaining a bounded-width uncertainty for other cardinalities.
