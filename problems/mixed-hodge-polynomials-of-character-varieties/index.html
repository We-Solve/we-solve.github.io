Mixed Hodge Polynomials of Character Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let a partition be a finite weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers, with size equal to the sum of its parts. Write $\mu=(\mu^1,\ldots,\mu^k)$ for a $k$-tuple of partitions. For a box $s$ in the Young diagram of a partition $\lambda$, let $a(s)$ and $l(s)$ denote its arm length and leg length, respectively: the numbers of boxes strictly to its right and strictly below it.

The supplied record associates to $\mu$ a generic $\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{C}})$ character variety $\mathcal M_\mu$. It does not define this variety, the genericity conditions, or the relation between $n$ and the sizes of the partitions. In particular, it does not explicitly state the usual common-size requirement $|\mu^1|=\cdots=|\mu^k|=n$. The parameter $g$ appears in the following formula without an explicit definition or range in the record.

With $z,w$ formal variables, the supplied generating-function prescription is \[ \mathcal H_\lambda(z,w) =\prod_{s\in\lambda} \frac{\left(z^{2a(s)+1}-w^{2l(s)+1}\right)^{2g}} {\left(z^{2a(s)+2}-w^{2l(s)}\right) \left(z^{2a(s)}-w^{2l(s)+2}\right)}, \] \[ \begin{aligned} &(z^2-1)(1-w^2)\operatorname{Log}\!\left( \sum_\lambda \mathcal H_\lambda(z,w) \prod_{i=1}^k\widetilde H_\lambda(x_i;z^2,w^2) \right)\\ &\hspace{3em}=\sum_\mu\mathbb H_\mu(z,w)m_\mu(x). \end{aligned} \] Here the record calls $\widetilde H_\lambda$ Macdonald polynomials. Under the conventional interpretation of the symmetric-function notation, $x_1,\ldots,x_k$ are separate alphabets and \[ m_\mu(x)=\prod_{i=1}^k m_{\mu^i}(x_i), \] where $m_\nu$ is the monomial symmetric function indexed by $\nu$. This interpretation requires confirmation, as do the normalization of $\widetilde H_\lambda$, the meaning of $\operatorname{Log}$, and the formal completion and indexing used in the expansion. In particular, the record does not explicitly specify whether $\operatorname{Log}$ is the plethystic logarithm.

Conjectural target as supplied. For every tuple $\mu$ and every choice of parameters satisfying the intended, but incompletely specified, character-variety hypotheses, are the functions $\mathbb H_\mu(z,w)$ polynomials in $z,w$ that encode the mixed Hodge numbers of $\mathcal M_\mu$?

The polynomiality clause is an explicit target. The mixed-Hodge clause is not a complete mathematical identity in the supplied record: no substitution, grading convention, dimension shift, or choice between ordinary and compactly supported cohomology is given. Consequently, the displayed prescription and the stated conjectural target are preserved here, but a precise universal mixed-Hodge formula cannot be supplied without additional source information. These unresolved qualifications are part of this statement-only draft.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether coefficients $\mathbb H_\mu(z,w)$, obtained from a Macdonald-polynomial generating function, are polynomials describing the mixed Hodge numbers of generic $\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{C}})$ character varieties. The record omits the exact character-variety hypotheses and the identity relating these coefficients to mixed Hodge numbers, so the full conjecture requires source clarification and mathematical review.
