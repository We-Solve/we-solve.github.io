Geometric Fixed Points for Poincaré Infinity-Categories

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C_2={\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. For a genuine $C_2$-equivariant spectrum $X$, write $\Phi^{C_2}X$ for its geometric fixed-point spectrum. Write $\mathrm{THR}$ for real topological Hochschild homology. The supplied passage concerns a ring spectrum $R$ with additional equivariant or duality structure, but does not specify that structure or the hypotheses on $R$.

The passage reports the equivalence \[ \Phi^{C_2}\mathrm{THR}(R) \simeq \Phi^{C_2}R\otimes_R\Phi^{C_2}R. \] Here $\otimes_R$ denotes a derived relative tensor product, provided the required right and left $R$-module structures have been specified. Those structures, the referenced $C_2$-action, and the hypotheses under which the displayed equivalence holds are absent from the supplied excerpt. Accordingly, this formula is retained as a reported comparison, not asserted for arbitrary ring spectra.

Problem. Determine how the geometric fixed-point construction and the displayed comparison should extend from the ring-spectrum setting to Poincaré $\infty$-categories. In particular, formulate the appropriate categorical inputs, the geometric fixed-point object associated with their real topological Hochschild homology, and a categorical counterpart of the right-hand side that specializes to the reported formula under the appropriate ring-spectrum comparison.

A Poincaré $\infty$-category here means an $\infty$-category equipped with Poincaré structure in the sense intended by the cited report. The supplied record does not specify its axioms, admissible size conditions, or the allowed class of Poincaré structures. It also does not specify the categorical tensor construction or a ring-to-category comparison. Thus the requested extension is a formulation and construction problem; the evidence does not determine a fully quantified conjectural equivalence.

Short English statement

Extend the reported geometric fixed-point formula for real topological Hochschild homology of ring spectra to Poincaré $\infty$-categories. The source leaves the required structures, hypotheses, and categorical analogue of the tensor product unspecified, so these remain part of the problem.
