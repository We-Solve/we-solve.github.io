Math-15658

Noncoherence of Higher-Rank Semisimple Lattices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $\Gamma$ is coherent if every finitely generated subgroup of $\Gamma$ is finitely presented. Thus $\Gamma$ is noncoherent if there exists a subgroup $H\leq\Gamma$ that is finitely generated but not finitely presented. A lattice in a Lie group $G$ is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma<G$ for which $G/\Gamma$ has finite invariant volume. Two Lie groups are locally isomorphic when their Lie algebras are isomorphic.

Original source formulation, as supplied. The record gives the following conjecture: if $G$ is a semisimple Lie group not locally isomorphic to $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}})$ or $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$, then every lattice in $G$ is noncoherent. Equivalently, the asserted conclusion for each such lattice $\Gamma<G$ is \[ \exists H\leq\Gamma\quad H\text{ is finitely generated and is not finitely presented}. \] The original formulation does not explicitly specify connectedness, whether the Lie groups are real, or restrictions concerning compact groups, compact factors, or the center. These qualifications require source review; none is added here.

Conflicting normalized formulation. The fields statement and clean_statement instead assume that $G$ is a connected semisimple real Lie group, with the same two local-isomorphism exclusions, and ask whether every infinite-index subgroup of a lattice $\Gamma<G$ has infinite covolume. If covolume is interpreted in the ambient group $G$, this asks \[ \forall H\leq\Gamma,\qquad [\Gamma:H]=\infty \ \Longrightarrow\ \operatorname{vol}(G/H)=\infty. \] Here $[\Gamma:H]$ is the number of cosets of $H$ in $\Gamma$, and volume means invariant quotient volume induced by a fixed Haar measure on $G$. The choice of ambient group for covolume is not explicit in the supplied question. This formulation concerns volume and index, whereas the original concerns finite presentation of finitely generated subgroups. The record supplies no equivalence between them, so they are retained as distinct formulations pending review. Neither the intended target nor the missing hypotheses should be inferred from the title alone.

Short English statement

The supplied original conjecture says that every lattice in the specified semisimple Lie groups contains a finitely generated subgroup that is not finitely presented. The normalized question instead concerns infinite index and covolume. The intended statement and its full hypotheses require human source review. The supplied open label is a curation claim, not an independently verified status.
