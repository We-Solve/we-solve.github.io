Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.26

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A right order on a group $G$ is a strict total order $<_G$ satisfying \[ g<_G h\ \Longrightarrow\ gt<_G ht \qquad(g,h,t\in G). \] A group is right-orderable if it admits a right order. A right-ordered group is a pair $(G,<_G)$ with a specified right order. An order-preserving embedding $\iota:(G,<_G)\hookrightarrow(H,<_H)$ is an injective group homomorphism satisfying $g<_G h\Longrightarrow\iota(g)<_H\iota(h)$.

An equation over $G$ has the displayed form \[ w(a_1,\ldots,a_k,x_1,\ldots,x_n)=1, \] where the $a_i$ are specified elements of $G$, the $x_j$ are unknown group elements, and $w$ is a group word. A solution in an overgroup consists of a group embedding $\iota:G\hookrightarrow H$ and elements $b_1,\ldots,b_n\in H$ such that \[ w\left(\iota(a_1),\ldots,\iota(a_k),b_1,\ldots,b_n\right)=1_H. \]

Unresolved scope of equations. The supplied record does not specify which equations are eligible for the proposed closure property. In particular, it supplies no hypothesis excluding equations already incompatible with injectivity on the coefficient group, and it does not say whether solvability in some group overgroup is assumed. An unrestricted quantifier over all group words would include such incompatible equations. Thus a precise unconditional formulation cannot be recovered from the supplied text alone.

To display the requested closure conditions without silently adding a hypothesis, write $\mathcal E(G)$ for the unspecified family of equations over $G$ intended by the source. This is a placeholder for missing source information. The two questions are then:

  1. For every right-orderable group $G$ and every equation $w=1$ in $\mathcal E(G)$, do there exist a right-orderable group $H$, a group embedding $\iota:G\hookrightarrow H$, and a solution of $w=1$ in $H$ under $\iota$?
  2. For every right-ordered group $(G,<_G)$ and every equation $w=1$ in $\mathcal E(G)$, do there exist a right-ordered group $(H,<_H)$, an order-preserving group embedding $\iota:(G,<_G)\hookrightarrow(H,<_H)$, and a solution of $w=1$ in $H$ under $\iota$?
The second question quantifies over the specified right order on $G$, rather than merely over the existence of some right order.

The source reports an affirmative result for equations with a single constant, $k=1$, citing J. Group Theory 11 (2008), 623--633. This supplied assertion is retained with its source qualification; its precise hypotheses and its coverage of the two closure questions require checking the cited article.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether equations over a right-orderable group can be solved in a right-orderable overgroup, and whether a specified right order can also be preserved by the embedding. The supplied record reports an affirmative single-constant case, but does not specify the admissible equations, so the general formulation remains conditional on clarifying that scope.
