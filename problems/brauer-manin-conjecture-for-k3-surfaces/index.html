Math-1102

Brauer–Manin Conjecture for K3 Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a number field, let $\bar k$ be an algebraic closure, and let $\Omega_k$ denote the set of all places of $k$. A K3 surface over $k$ is a smooth, projective, geometrically integral surface $X$ such that \[ \omega_{X_{\bar k}}\cong\mathcal O_{X_{\bar k}},\qquad H^1(X_{\bar k},\mathcal O_{X_{\bar k}})=0. \] For $v\in\Omega_k$, write $k_v$ for the completion of $k$. Since $X$ is proper, its adelic points may be identified with \[ X(\mathbb A_k)=\prod_{v\in\Omega_k}X(k_v), \] with the usual adelic topology. Write \[ \operatorname{Br}(X)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(X,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}} \] for its cohomological Brauer group. For $\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(X)$ and $P_v\in X(k_v)$, let $\alpha(P_v)\in\operatorname{Br}(k_v)$ be its evaluation, and let $\operatorname{inv}_v:\operatorname{Br}(k_v)\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ be the local invariant map. The Brauer--Manin set is \[ X(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}= \left\{(P_v)_v\in X(\mathbb A_k): \sum_{v\in\Omega_k}\operatorname{inv}_v(\alpha(P_v))=0 \text{ for every }\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(X)\right\}. \] Each such sum has only finitely many nonzero terms. Global reciprocity places the diagonal image of $X(k)$ in this set.

Conjecture (as supplied). For every number field $k$ and every K3 surface $X/k$, the diagonal image of $X(k)$ is dense in $X(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}$. Explicitly, for every $(P_v)_v\in X(\mathbb A_k)^{\operatorname{Br}}$, every finite subset $S\subset\Omega_k$, and every choice of open neighborhoods $U_v\subset X(k_v)$ of $P_v$ for $v\in S$, there exists $P\in X(k)$ whose image lies in $U_v$ for every $v\in S$.

In particular, nonemptiness of the Brauer--Manin set would imply $X(k)\ne\varnothing$, and every finite local approximation arising from a Brauer--Manin adelic point would be realizable by a rational point. These are the supplied meanings of the Brauer--Manin obstruction being the only obstruction to the Hasse principle and weak approximation.

The formulation above uses the full adelic topology, including the usual topology at archimedean places. The supplied record does not specify whether any modification at archimedean places was intended; that source convention requires review.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every K3 surface over every number field, rational points can approximate every adelic point orthogonal to the entire Brauer group, at any finitely many places simultaneously. Its supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
