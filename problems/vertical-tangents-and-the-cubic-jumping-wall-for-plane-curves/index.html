Math-1204

Vertical tangents and the cubic jumping wall for plane curves

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Eric Larson asks for possible fiber dimensions and vertical tangent-space dimensions of the evaluation map \[ \pi:\mathcal M_{g,n}({\mathbb{P}}^r,\beta)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Conf}({\mathbb{P}}^r,n), \qquad [f:C\to{\mathbb{P}}^r,p_1,\ldots,p_n]\longmapsto(f(p_1),\ldots,f(p_n)). \] Here $g$ is the source genus, $\beta$ has projective degree $d$, and the target is the ordered configuration space of pairwise distinct image points; restrict to the locus where the images are distinct. The exact stable-map/open-locus and component conventions, as well as the ground field, are not fixed. The verification specifies ${\mathbb{P}}^r$, resolving the extracted prose's ${\mathbb{P}}^n$. The question especially concerns dominant evaluation maps. The source reports an explicit dominance characterization when $d\ge g+r$, without giving that characterization, and cites Stevens for canonical curves in that range. It asks for more than dominance.

On the smooth embedded locus, let $N_f$ be the normal bundle of $f$ and $D=\sum_i p_i$. The attached unreviewed aid reports \[ T_{\mathrm{vert}}=H^0(C,N_f(-D)),\quad \operatorname{coker}(d\pi)=\ker\left(H^1(C,N_f(-D))\to H^1(C,N_f)\right), \] \[ \dim T_{\mathrm{vert}}=(r+1)d-(r-3)(g-1)-(r-1)n+h^1(C,N_f(-D)). \] For a smooth plane curve of degree $e\ge3$, let $H$ be its hyperplane divisor, $N_e=e(e+3)/2$ and $g_e=(e-1)(e-2)/2$. The aid gives $\dim T_{\mathrm{vert}}=N_e-n+h^0(C,\mathcal O_C(D-3H))$. It claims no jumps for $n<3e$; at $n=3e$ the jump occurs exactly for $D\in|3H|$, equivalently when the marked divisor is a scheme-theoretic cubic intersection. Then it reports differential corank one and fiber dimension $g_e$, one above expected dimension $g_e-1$.

If $F_C$ defines $C$ and $Q$ is the cubic cutting out $D$, the reported linear system is \[ {\mathbb{P}}\left(kF_C+QH^0({\mathbb{P}}^2,\mathcal O(e-3))\right). \] To represent the smooth embedded fixed-configuration fiber, only its smooth members belong to the locus under discussion; the full projective system can contain singular curves. The aid calls it the whole smooth embedded fiber without explicitly making this open-locus restriction. Its plane result does not classify all stable-map fibers or tangent dimensions in every component.

Short English statement

Determine fiber and tangent dimensions when curves through marked projective points are evaluated at those points, especially in the dominant range. The aid gives normal-bundle formulas and a first plane-curve jumping locus at cubic intersections.
