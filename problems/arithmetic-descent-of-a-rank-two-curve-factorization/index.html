Math-1268

Arithmetic descent of a rank-two curve factorization

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field with separable closure $\bar k$, and for a geometrically connected variety $X/k$ use the arithmetic and geometric étale fundamental groups in \[ 1\longrightarrow\pi_1(X_{\bar k})\longrightarrow\pi_1(X) \longrightarrow G_k\longrightarrow1, \qquad G_k=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar k/k). \] Basepoints are understood; changing them changes representations and induced maps by the usual conjugacies. For a prime $\ell$, the algebraic envelope of a representation into $\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ is the Zariski closure of its image.

The workshop source attributes to C. Simpson a geometric factorization result for a representation $\rho_X:\pi_1(X_{\bar k})\to\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ whose algebraic envelope contains $\operatorname{SL}_2$: it factors through a representation attached to a curve or Shimura variety $Y$, via a map from $X$ to a cover of $Y$. J. Getz asks for a version with the full $\pi_1(X)$ in place of the geometric group. The record does not state the base field, hypotheses on $X$, precise cover, topology/comparison conventions of the cited theorem, or allowed field extensions. This paraphrase is therefore retained as source context, not asserted as a complete theorem of Simpson in the displayed generality. A condition on the full arithmetic envelope and a condition on the envelope of its geometric restriction must not silently be identified.

The supplied partial result is conditional and concerns an honest smooth curve target, not a general Shimura target or orbifold. Suppose a continuous full representation $\rho:\pi_1(X)\to\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ has a geometric restriction factoring through a dominant algebraic map $f_{\bar k}:X_{\bar k}\to C_{\bar k}$ to a smooth curve, with $(f_{\bar k})_*$ surjective on geometric étale fundamental groups. For every finite extension $k'/k$ over which this map and target are defined as $f:X_{k'}\to C$, the report asserts a unique factorization \[ \rho|_{\pi_1(X_{k'})}=\rho_C\circ f_*,\qquad \rho_C:\pi_1(C)\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_\ell), \] with uniqueness interpreted after compatible basepoint choices. This assumes the geometric factorization and surjectivity; it does not establish their existence for every full representation in the original question.

For a projective curve target, the report additionally claims that a normalized geometric factorization descends over its field of moduli, that the required extension degree equals the size of the Galois orbit of its factorization class, and that uniqueness of the normalized geometric factorization gives descent and arithmetic factorization over $k$. The normalization, equivalence relation on factorizations, field hypotheses, and finiteness/descent assumptions are not defined in the record. These field-of-moduli claims require separate source review; they cannot be inferred here from an arbitrary unnormalized curve or map. This remains a conditional curve-factorization/descent result rather than a resolution of the full curve-or-Shimura question.

Short English statement

Getz asks whether a geometric rank-two factorization result has an arithmetic fundamental-group version. The supplied report lifts an already existing, surjective smooth-curve factorization once its map is defined over a finite extension, and makes additional conditional projective descent claims. It does not provide the general factorization or settle Shimura targets.
