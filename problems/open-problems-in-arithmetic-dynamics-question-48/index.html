Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Question 48

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\ge 2$ be a fixed integer, and let $q$ range over prime powers. Write ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ for the field with $q$ elements. For a field $K$, define \[ \operatorname{Poly}_d^1(K)=\{f\in K[x]:\deg f=d\}, \] and, as in the supplied record, \[ \operatorname{End}_d^1(K)=\left\{\frac{A}{B}\in K(x):\gcd(A,B)=1,\ \max\{\deg A,\deg B\}=d\right\}. \] The fractions in this definition represent rational functions, so different numerator--denominator representations of the same function do not give different elements.

The supplied excerpt uses the predicate "$(f,0)$ is stable over ${\mathbb{F}}_q$" without defining it. Retain this predicate as $\mathsf{Stable}_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}(f,0)$; its mathematical meaning must be recovered from the source before this becomes a complete standalone statement. With that qualification, set \[ \operatorname{Poly}_d^1({\mathbb{F}}_q)^{\mathrm{stab}} =\{f\in\operatorname{Poly}_d^1({\mathbb{F}}_q):\mathsf{Stable}_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}(f,0)\}. \] The source also writes \[ \operatorname{Rat}_d^1({\mathbb{F}}_q)^{\mathrm{stab}} =\{f\in\operatorname{Rat}_d^1({\mathbb{F}}_q):\mathsf{Stable}_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}(f,0)\}, \] but does not define $\operatorname{Rat}_d^1$, despite defining $\operatorname{End}_d^1$. If $\operatorname{Rat}_d^1(K)$ is intended to mean $\operatorname{End}_d^1(K)$, the rational-function counting problem concerns all degree-$d$ rational functions satisfying the same stability predicate. This identification is conditional and requires source verification.

Question. For each fixed $d\ge2$, determine the asymptotic growth, as $q\to\infty$ through prime powers, of \[ \#\operatorname{Poly}_d^1({\mathbb{F}}_q)^{\mathrm{stab}} \quad\text{and}\quad \#\operatorname{Rat}_d^1({\mathbb{F}}_q)^{\mathrm{stab}}, \] subject to resolving the definitions above. These are counts of maps themselves; no quotient by conjugacy is specified.

The source additionally asks analogous counting questions with stability replaced by eventual stability, and for maps of ${\mathbb{A}}^N$ or ${\mathbb{P}}^N$ with $N\ge2$. The excerpt does not define eventual stability or specify the higher-dimensional families, degree restrictions, marked data, or corresponding stability predicates. These extensions therefore remain requests for analogous formulations, rather than fully specified counting problems. No particular asymptotic formula or growth rate is conjectured in the supplied excerpt.

Short English statement

For a fixed degree $d\ge2$, count the polynomials and rational functions over ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ for which the pair $(f,0)$ is stable, and determine how those counts grow as $q$ increases. The supplied excerpt leaves stability undefined and uses an undefined rational-map notation. It also requests eventual-stability and higher-dimensional analogues without enough details to formulate them uniquely.
