Math-6470

Induced Four-Cycle Profiles Above Half Density

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Adopt the conventional interpretation that graphs are finite, simple, and undirected; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify this convention. For a graph $G$ on $n\geq 4$ vertices, define its edge density and its induced four-cycle density by \[ p(G)=\frac{|E(G)|}{\binom{n}{2}},\qquad t_{\mathrm{ind}}(C_4,G)=\frac{N_{\mathrm{ind}}(C_4,G)}{\binom{n}{4}}, \] where $C_4$ is the cycle on four vertices and \[ N_{\mathrm{ind}}(C_4,G) =\left|\{S\subseteq V(G): |S|=4,\ G[S]\cong C_4\}\right|. \] Thus each qualifying four-element vertex set is counted once.

For each fixed $\xi\in[0,1]$, define the asymptotic maximum by \[ \Phi_{C_4}(\xi)= \sup\left\{ \limsup_{j\to\infty}t_{\mathrm{ind}}(C_4,G_j): |V(G_j)|\to\infty,\quad p(G_j)\to\xi \right\}. \] Using convergent edge densities makes the asymptotic question meaningful even when $\xi\binom{n}{2}$ is not an integer.

Problem. Determine $\Phi_{C_4}(\xi)$, equivalently the sharp asymptotic maximum number of induced copies of $C_4$ at prescribed limiting edge density $\xi$. In particular, for every fixed $\xi\in(1/2,1]$, is this maximum asymptotically attained by the construction referred to in the source as the extremal construction for the triangle-density problem? If $(H_j)$ denotes the intended construction at density $\xi$, the proposed attainment means \[ |V(H_j)|\to\infty,\qquad p(H_j)\to\xi, \qquad N_{\mathrm{ind}}(C_4,H_j) =\left(\Phi_{C_4}(\xi)+o(1)\right) \binom{|V(H_j)|}{4}. \] The supplied record does not describe $(H_j)$, state the triangle-density optimization precisely, or provide the claimed formula. Consequently, the construction-specific conjecture remains a source-dependent target rather than an explicit formula in this draft. The report context attributes the conjecture to Liu, Mubayi, and Reiher.

Short English statement

For each fixed edge density, determine the largest asymptotic proportion of four-vertex sets that induce a cycle. Above density $1/2$, the source asks whether the construction for the triangle-density problem attains this maximum. That construction and its proposed formula are not specified in the supplied record.
