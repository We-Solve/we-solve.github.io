Open Problems and Questions about Geodesics — Conjecture 12 (Klingenberg)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed manifold means a compact smooth manifold without boundary. For a Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$, let \[ S_gM=\{v\in TM:g(v,v)=1\} \] be its unit tangent bundle, and let $\varphi_t)_{t\in{\mathbb{R}}}$ denote its geodesic flow. Write $X$ for the vector field generating this flow. The Anosov condition is the existence of a continuous, $D\varphi_t$-invariant splitting \[ T(S_gM)=E^s\oplus {\mathbb{R}} X\oplus E^u \] and constants $C\geq 1$, $\lambda>0$ such that, with respect to an auxiliary Riemannian norm, for every $v\in S_gM$ and $t\geq0$, \[ \begin{aligned} \|D\varphi_t(v)\xi\|&\leq C e^{-\lambda t}\|\xi\| &&(\xi\in E^s_v),\\ \|D\varphi_{-t}(v)\eta\|&\leq C e^{-\lambda t}\|\eta\| &&(\eta\in E^u_v). \end{aligned} \] For a Riemannian metric $h$, write $K_h(p,\sigma)$ for the sectional curvature of a two-dimensional linear subspace $\sigma\subset T_pM$.

Conjecture 12 (attributed to Klingenberg). For every closed smooth manifold $M$, \[ \begin{gathered} \text{there exists a Riemannian metric }g\text{ on }M \text{ whose geodesic flow is Anosov} \\[2pt] \Longrightarrow \\[2pt] \text{there exists a Riemannian metric }h\text{ on }M \text{ such that } K_h(p,\sigma)<0 \quad\text{for every }p\in M\text{ and every two-plane }\sigma\subset T_pM. \end{gathered} \] The metric $h$ need not equal $g$, and its sectional curvature need not be constant. The source states no dimension restriction and does not specify rank conventions for the stable and unstable bundles in degenerate low-dimensional cases; this qualification remains unresolved.

The supplied background distinguishes manifolds admitting strictly negative sectional curvature from those admitting constant negative sectional curvature. It credits early examples admitting the former but not the latter to Mostow--Siu [Mos-Siu1980] and Gromov--Thurston [Gro-Thu1987], and credits Ontaneda [Ontaneda2011, Ontaneda2014] with substantially enlarging the supply of examples. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

Klingenberg's conjecture asks whether every closed manifold carrying a Riemannian metric with Anosov geodesic flow also carries a possibly different Riemannian metric with strictly negative sectional curvature. Constant curvature is not required. The source labels the conjecture open; that status has not been independently verified.
