Li–Szegedy Smooth-Retract Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a graph $G$, write $V(G)$ and $E(G)$ for its vertex and edge sets. A graph is bipartite if its vertex set admits a partition into two classes such that every edge joins different classes. An induced subtree $T$ of $H$ is a connected, acyclic induced subgraph of $H$: two vertices of $T$ are adjacent in $T$ exactly when they are adjacent in $H$.

A graph homomorphism $r:H\to T$ is a map $r:V(H)\to V(T)$ preserving adjacency. It is a retraction onto $T$ if \[ r(v)=v\qquad\text{for every }v\in V(T). \] Thus $H$ retracts onto $T$ precisely when such a homomorphism exists.

The supplied record attributes the following conjecture to Li and Szegedy: for every bipartite graph $H$ and every induced subtree $T$ of $H$, \[ \left(\exists\,r:H\to T\text{ a graph homomorphism with }r|_{V(T)}=\mathrm{id}_{V(T)}\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ T\text{ is smooth in }H\text{ in the sense of Li and Szegedy}. \] The supplied record does not define Li--Szegedy smoothness. The conclusion therefore retains the source terminology and is not a self-contained mathematical predicate in this draft. Its exact definition, including any analytic data and quantifiers, must be recovered from the cited sources before a complete formal statement can be given. The record also leaves the graph conventions, including finiteness and whether graphs are simple, unspecified; no additional restrictions are imposed here.

Short English statement

The supplied Li--Szegedy conjecture says that every induced subtree of a bipartite graph onto which the graph retracts is smooth in that graph. The meaning of smoothness and the intended graph conventions require source verification. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
