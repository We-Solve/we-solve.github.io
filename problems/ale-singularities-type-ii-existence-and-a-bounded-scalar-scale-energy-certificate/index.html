ALE singularities: Type-II existence and a bounded-scalar scale-energy certificate

OpenAI's claim

Four-dimensional Ricci-flow extension: The paper claims smooth continuation of a Ricci flow on a closed four-manifold through any finite time at which scalar curvature stays uniformly bounded. The continuation occurs on the same manifold.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
On a closed connected smooth four-manifold consider a Ricci flow $g(t)$ whose initial metric and time evolution are smooth for $0\le t<T<\infty$. The claimed implication is \[\sup_{M\times[0,T)}|\operatorname{Scal}_{g(t)}|<\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\exists\epsilon>0:\ g(t)\text{ extends smoothly to }[0,T+\epsilon).\] In particular at a finite maximal four-dimensional existence time, $\limsup_{t\uparrow T}\max_M\operatorname{Scal}_{g(t)}=+\infty$. No corresponding extension conclusion in arbitrary dimension is asserted.

Outline of the argument

Four-dimensional Ricci-flow extension: Outermost concentration scales produce buffered time patches and finite Ricci-flat bubble trees. Parabolic annular estimates and a time maximal function convert spacetime Ricci energy into the static weighted error of a companion elliptic theorem. Comparing positive functional variation with static smallness forces the root energy to vanish, contradicting the selected change of concentration scale.
