Stability of Peaked Reduced Ostrovsky Waves

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A traveling wave has the form $u(x,t)=U_*(z)$, where $z=x-ct$ and $c$ is its propagation speed. The supplied profile formula is \[ U_*(z)=\frac{3z^2-\pi^2}{18}, \] with periodic extension understood. To specify that extension, one must prescribe a fundamental interval $I$ of length $L>0$, use the displayed formula on $I$, and impose $U_*(z+L)=U_*(z)$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify $I$, $L$, or $c$; thus this description does not select a unique periodic profile.

Source question. For the reduced Ostrovsky equation, determine whether the indicated peaked periodic traveling waves are nonlinearly stable or nonlinearly unstable.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not give the equation's normalization, the spatial domain, the solution concept, the admissible perturbations, the function space or topology, or the time interval over which stability is required. It also does not specify whether stability is measured relative to a fixed wave or modulo spatial translation. These choices determine the hypotheses and quantifiers of a nonlinear stability statement, so a unique precise stability assertion cannot be extracted from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

Determine the nonlinear stability or instability of the periodically extended peaked traveling wave with profile $U_*(z)=(3z^2-\pi^2)/18$ for the reduced Ostrovsky equation. The periodic extension, equation normalization, and meaning of nonlinear stability require clarification before the question has a fully specified mathematical formulation.
