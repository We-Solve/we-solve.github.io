Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.10

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime. A pro-$p$ group is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. Let $F$ be a free pro-$p$ group of finite rank $d\geq 2$, where freeness is understood in the category of pro-$p$ groups and continuous homomorphisms. Thus $F$ is finitely generated as a topological group and is non-abelian.

A totally disconnected locally compact group is a Hausdorff topological group that is locally compact and whose connected components are singletons. A topological embedding $\iota:F\to G$ is a continuous injective homomorphism that is a homeomorphism onto its image. The embedding has open image when $\iota(F)$ is an open subgroup of $G$. A topological group $G$ is compactly generated if there is a compact subset $K\subseteq G$ such that $G=\langle K\rangle$, with generation here understood algebraically.

  1. (a) Does there exist a simple totally disconnected locally compact group $G$ and a topological embedding $\iota:F\to G$ with open image?
  2. (b) Can such a group $G$ additionally be required to be compactly generated? Equivalently, can one require a compact subset $K\subseteq G$ with $G=\langle K\rangle$ in addition to the conditions in part (a)?

The source leaves two qualifications unresolved. First, it does not explicitly specify whether the questions are existential in $p$ and $d$, or are to hold for every prime $p$ and every finite rank $d\geq2$, or for another range of these parameters. The notation above exposes these parameters without imposing a quantifier absent from the supplied wording. Second, the source does not specify whether "simple" means abstractly simple, with no nontrivial proper normal subgroup, or topologically simple, with no nontrivial proper closed normal subgroup. These give different possible formulations, and no choice between them is made here.

The supplied source labels part (a) affirmatively solved and the overall problem partially solved. Its comment of 2025 reports partial progress on part (b). These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether a finitely generated non-abelian free pro-$p$ group can occur as an open subgroup of a simple totally disconnected locally compact group, and whether the containing group can also be compactly generated. The supplied record reports an affirmative solution to part (a) and partial progress on part (b). The intended meaning of simplicity and the quantifiers over the prime and free rank require clarification.
