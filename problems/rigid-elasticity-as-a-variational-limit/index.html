Rigid Elasticity as a Variational Limit

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks whether rigid-elasticity models arise as variational limits of models whose elastic constants tend to infinity, with $\Gamma$-convergence suggested as one possible notion of limit. Neither the class of models nor its mathematical data are specified.

A conditional formulation of the suggested $\Gamma$-convergence target is as follows. Once a state space $X$, a topology $\tau$, finite-elasticity energy functionals $E_n\colon X\to\overline{{\mathbb{R}}}$, and a proposed rigid-elasticity energy $E_{\mathrm{rig}}\colon X\to\overline{{\mathbb{R}}}$ have been specified, ask whether \[ E_n\xrightarrow{\Gamma(\tau)}E_{\mathrm{rig}} \] as the elastic constants associated with $E_n$ tend to infinity in the intended sense. Here $\overline{{\mathbb{R}}}={\mathbb{R}}\cup\{-\infty,+\infty\}$. In a setting where sequential characterization of $\Gamma$-convergence is appropriate, this requires both \[ \forall x\in X\;\forall (x_n)\subset X,\qquad x_n\xrightarrow{\tau}x\ \Longrightarrow\ E_{\mathrm{rig}}(x)\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}E_n(x_n), \] and \[ \forall x\in X\;\exists (x_n)\subset X,\qquad x_n\xrightarrow{\tau}x, \qquad \limsup_{n\to\infty}E_n(x_n)\leq E_{\mathrm{rig}}(x). \] This is a formulation template, not a specified conjecture for a particular model. The record leaves unresolved the spatial dimension and domain, state variables, constitutive assumptions, boundary conditions, energy scaling, topology, meaning of divergence of the elastic constants, and definition of the proposed rigid-elasticity limit. It also does not say whether the target concerns a particular family or every family in some class. Consequently, no precise model-specific assertion or universal quantifier over models can be recovered from the supplied text alone.

Short English statement

Can models with increasingly large elastic constants converge variationally to rigid-elasticity models, for example through $\Gamma$-convergence of their energies? The model class, energies, topology, and boundary conditions must be specified before this becomes a precise mathematical problem. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; that label is dated curation, not independent verification.
