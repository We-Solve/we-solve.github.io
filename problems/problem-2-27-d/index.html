Problem 2.27 — (D.

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix the source's class $\mathcal L$ of links and a coefficient field $k$. Let $V$ be the unital commutative $k$-algebra of finite-type invariants on $\mathcal L$, with pointwise multiplication. Finite type of order at most $d$ means that the invariant, extended to singular links by the crossing-difference rule, vanishes on links with more than $d$ transverse double points. The source does not fix $k$, orientation/framing, or component-number conventions.

For any ideal $I\lhd V$, define \[ Z(I)=\{L\in\mathcal L:\forall v\in I,\ v(L)=0\}, \qquad J(I)=\{v\in V:\forall L\in Z(I),\ v(L)=0\}, \] \[ \sqrt I=\{v\in V:\exists m\ge1,\ v^m\in I\}. \] Evaluation $\operatorname{ev}_L:V\to k$ is an algebra homomorphism. Because $k$ is a field, each evaluation kernel is prime, and \[ J(I)=\bigcap_{L\in Z(I)}\ker\operatorname{ev}_L \supseteq\sqrt I. \] If $Z(I)=\varnothing$, the intersection convention gives $J(I)=V$.

Bar-Natan asks whether equality always holds: \[ \forall I\lhd V,\qquad J(I)=\sqrt I. \] Equivalently, whenever $v\notin\sqrt I$, must there be an actual link $L$ with all invariants in $I$ zero and $v(L)\ne0$? The ideal is not assumed finitely generated. This evaluates on links, not automatically on every algebra homomorphism $V\to k$ or a completed/formal link space.

The reference to a similar statement has a missing antecedent. The exact coefficient field and admissible evaluation points are especially important in this Nullstellensatz-type problem; changing them can change the answer, and density of some evaluations is not by itself a proof of the stated zero-locus equality. No hypotheses from the preceding statement are invented. The supplied research summary only calls the question not obviously resolved and provides no precise theorem.

Short English statement

Is the ideal of all finite-type link invariants vanishing on the common link-zero set of $I$ exactly $\sqrt I$? The coefficient field and link/evaluation category must be fixed, and actual link evaluations must not be replaced silently by arbitrary algebraic characters.
