Math-1492

Codimension-one connectivity and robust facet chains

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed nonarchimedean valued field and \[ R=k[x_1^{\pm1},\ldots,x_d^{\pm1}],\qquad \mathfrak p\subset R\text{ a proper prime ideal},\qquad r=\dim(R/\mathfrak p). \] Write $\operatorname{Trop}(\mathfrak p)\subset\mathbb R^d$ for its nonarchimedean amoeba, equivalently tropicalization, using the coefficient valuation in initial ideals. Fix a finite pure $r$-dimensional polyhedral structure on it. Its simple facet graph $G$ has one vertex per $r$-face, with an edge when two facets share an $(r-1)$-face. Item A.6 asks whether $G$ is always connected, a property called connectedness through codimension one. It does not ask for higher homotopy connectedness of the support.

The source background attributes ordinary connectedness to a then-forthcoming paper of Einsiedler, Lind and Kapranov. It also calls the amoeba homogeneous. For a general nontrivial coefficient valuation, a polyhedral complex need not be a homogeneous fan; that wording needs the appropriate constant-coefficient or valuation convention and is not imposed on the general statement here.

The aid reports an affirmative solution by Cartwright--Payne: the tropicalization of the irreducible variety defined by $\mathfrak p$ is connected through codimension one, in every characteristic under the algebraically closed-field hypothesis. The zero-dimensional case is the single-point, single-facet case and is connected without any ridge adjacency. A single higher-dimensional facet is likewise connected as a one-vertex graph.

In characteristic zero the aid additionally invokes Maclagan--Yu. Let $\ell$ be the lineality dimension in the chosen pure polyhedral structure, and put $q=r-\ell$. The reported stronger graph consequence is that deletion of at most $q-1$ facet vertices preserves connectedness, with the standard size qualifications. In particular, when \[ q>0,\qquad |V(G)|\geq q+1, \] the candidate corollary is \[ \kappa(G)\geq q=r-\ell, \] where $\kappa$ is vertex connectivity. Menger's theorem then supplies $q$ pairwise internally vertex-disjoint facet chains between any two distinct facets. The report obtains this simple graph as the 2-section of the facet-ridge hypergraph: facets incident to a common ridge form a clique. Without the size and positivity conditions, this numerical connectivity claim is not asserted, although the original connectedness conclusion remains. The higher-connectivity statement retains its characteristic-zero restriction and exact polyhedral-hypothesis review needs.

Short English statement

The source asks whether facets of a prime tropicalization are linked through shared ridges. The supplied report attributes an affirmative all-characteristic result to Cartwright--Payne. In characteristic zero it derives a stronger vertex-connectivity and disjoint-facet-chain bound from Maclagan--Yu, subject to lineality and graph-size qualifications.
