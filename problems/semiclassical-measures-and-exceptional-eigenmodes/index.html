Semiclassical Measures and Exceptional Eigenmodes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For normalized eigenfunctions $u_h$ in a high-energy or semiclassical regime, a semiclassical measure $\nu$ is a weak limit satisfying $\langle u_h,\operatorname{Op}_h(a)u_h\rangle\to\int a\,d\nu$ for smooth phase-space observables $a$. Such limits are invariant under the corresponding classical Hamiltonian or geodesic flow. Liouville measure is the normalized natural volume measure on the relevant energy surface. Quantum ergodicity gives this limit on a density-one subsequence under classical ergodicity; quantum unique ergodicity asks for it on every eigenfunction sequence.
Source scope and qualifications.
The report ranges over several quantized systems and highlights Laplace eigenfunctions on compact negatively curved manifolds. It does not claim that every invariant measure is realizable for every system; arithmetic joint eigenbases and unqualified Laplace eigenbases are different cases. Classification, rates, and exceptional modes are separate retained requests.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
What are the possible semiclassical measures, and do they include all classically invariant measures? What is the rate of convergence to Liouville measure, and can exceptional eigenmodes exhibit the behavior of exceptional invariant measures?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The question asks whether exceptional eigenmodes occur; arithmetic cases are proved, while the general negative-curvature case remains open. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Classify possible eigenfunction limits, quantify approach to equidistribution, and determine whether exceptional eigenmodes can follow exceptional classical measures.
