Infinite Transverse Actions on Codimension-One Foliations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual complex algebraic setting, a possibly singular codimension-one foliation on a smooth projective variety $X$ of dimension $n$ is described by a saturated coherent subsheaf $T_{\mathcal F}\subseteq T_X$ of rank $n-1$ that is closed under the Lie bracket. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the ground field or the permitted singularities; this conventional description requires confirmation against the source.

The supplied conjecture is the universally quantified implication \[ \left. \begin{gathered} X\text{ is a projective manifold},\\ \mathcal F\text{ is a codimension-one foliation on }X,\\ f\in\operatorname{End}(X,\mathcal F),\\ f\text{ has infinite transverse action} \end{gathered} \right\} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal F\text{ is virtually transversely additive}. \] This is recorded as a conjecture, not as a proved implication.

The record does not define $\operatorname{End}(X,\mathcal F)$, "infinite transverse action," or "virtually transversely additive." Consequently, these expressions retain their source terminology in the displayed statement. In particular, it remains to determine whether the allowed maps are regular or dominant rational self-maps, what equivalence or induced action defines transverse infinitude, and what kind of covering and additive structure are required by the conclusion. These unresolved definitions prevent a fully specified formulation from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether a self-map preserving a codimension-one foliation on a projective manifold must force the foliation to be virtually transversely additive whenever its transverse action is infinite. The precise map class, transverse action, and meaning of the virtual additive structure require source confirmation.
