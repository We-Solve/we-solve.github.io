Virtual Nonorderability of Cocompact Arithmetic Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a semisimple Lie group, meaning that its Lie algebra is semisimple. A subgroup $\Gamma\leq G$ is cocompact and discrete if it is discrete in the topology of $G$ and the quotient $G/\Gamma$ is compact. A strict total order $<$ on a group $H$ is left invariant if \[ \forall a,b,c\in H,\qquad a<b\Longrightarrow ca<cb. \] The group $H$ is left-orderable if it admits such an order. A group $\Gamma$ is virtually left-orderable if some subgroup $H\leq\Gamma$ with $[\Gamma:H]<\infty$ is left-orderable.

The supplied question is whether there exist a semisimple Lie group $G$ and a cocompact arithmetic subgroup $\Gamma\leq G$ such that \[ \forall H\leq\Gamma,\qquad [\Gamma:H]<\infty\Longrightarrow H\text{ admits no left-invariant strict total order}. \] Here an arithmetic subgroup is understood to be discrete, with the arithmetic condition interpreted according to the cited report. The supplied record does not specify the algebraic-group data defining arithmeticity, the permitted compact factors, or conventions concerning connectedness and the centre of $G$. These details require source review; no additional restrictions on $G$ are imposed in this draft.

The formulation is provisional because the supplied curation describes the extracted passage as truncated immediately before an explicitly numbered Open problem 1, while separate verification metadata asserts that the displayed question was checked against the official report. These supplied claims do not resolve the provenance issue within the available evidence.

Short English statement

Does there exist a cocompact arithmetic subgroup of a semisimple Lie group that is not virtually left-orderable? Every subgroup of finite index must fail to admit a left-invariant total order.
