Math-7039

Frame Bundles as Stack Fiber Products

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a rank-$n$ vector bundle $E$ on $S$ and its classifying map $f_E:S\to BGL_n$. It does not specify the base, the category containing $S$, the topology used for the classifying stack, or the map $\mathrm{pt}\to BGL_n$. Consequently, the displayed fiber product requires additional conventions.

One precise conditional formulation is the following. Fix a base scheme $B$, a $B$-scheme $S$, and a finite locally free $\mathcal O_S$-module $E$ of constant rank $n$. Interpret $BGL_{n,B}$ as the fppf classifying stack of rank-$n$ vector bundles, and interpret $\mathrm{pt}\to BGL_n$ as the morphism $B\to BGL_{n,B}$ classifying $\mathcal O_B^{\oplus n}$. These are explicit supplementary conventions, not hypotheses specified in the source record.

Define the frame functor of $E$ on $S$-schemes by \[ \operatorname{Fr}_S(E)(T\xrightarrow{g}S) = \operatorname{Isom}_{\mathcal O_T} \left(\mathcal O_T^{\oplus n},g^*E\right). \] The recoverable clarification asks for the geometric interpretation of the stack fiber product, with the requested identification \[ S\mathbin{\times^{(2)}_{BGL_{n,B}}}B \simeq \operatorname{Fr}_S(E), \] where $\times^{(2)}$ denotes the $2$-fiber product and the frame functor is viewed as its associated fppf sheaf or stack over $S$. This states the identification requested by the supplied record; no proof is provided.

The source qualifications prevent promoting this clarification to a verified open problem. The registry labels the item open, but its supplied statement-verification note says that the official report immediately identifies the fiber product with the frame bundle and treats the question as an answered clarification. Its statement status is unrecoverable, with no clean statement supplied. The dated literature assessment instead describes the extracted text as the meta-question "How to make this geometric?" in a discussion of stacks, says that it is not a complete mathematical claim, and assigns no definite resolution. These supplied descriptions are retained without independently adjudicating them.

Short English statement

Under explicitly stated scheme and stack conventions, the question asks how pulling back the trivial-bundle point along the classifying map of $E$ describes the bundle of frames of $E$. The supplied record contains conflicting status descriptions: an open label, an assessment of an incomplete meta-question, and a verification note describing an already answered clarification. This draft therefore records a conditional formulation requiring human review.
