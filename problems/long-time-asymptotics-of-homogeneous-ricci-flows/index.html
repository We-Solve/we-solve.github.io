Math-4158

Long-Time Asymptotics of Homogeneous Ricci Flows

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and let $g(t)$, $t\in[0,\infty)$, be a smooth family of Riemannian metrics satisfying the Ricci flow equation \[ \frac{\partial g(t)}{\partial t}=-2\operatorname{Ric}(g(t)). \] The flow is called immortal because it exists for every nonnegative time. Homogeneity means that, for each $t$, the isometry group of $(M,g(t))$ acts transitively on $M$.

Problem. For every such immortal homogeneous Ricci flow, determine the asymptotic behavior of $g(t)$ as $t\to\infty$.

Unresolved qualifications. This is an asymptotic classification question rather than a specified convergence conjecture. The supplied statement does not specify a normalization, a topology of convergence, whether to identify metrics by diffeomorphisms, or whether limits are to be pointed, subsequential, or taken along the entire flow. It also states no explicit dimension, compactness, connectedness, completeness, or curvature hypotheses. These omissions prevent formulation of a unique, more specific limit assertion from the supplied evidence alone.

The supplied dated assessment reports convergence of parabolic blow-down subsequences to expanding solitons and says that a unique full asymptotic description is not known in general. This assessment does not specify the hypotheses or convergence framework of the cited results; it is retained as supplied context and is not asserted here as an independently verified theorem.

Short English statement

Determine the long-time geometry of every homogeneous Ricci flow that exists for all $t\geq0$. The source leaves the normalization and meaning of asymptotic convergence unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved, subject to the dated, unverified literature assessment.
