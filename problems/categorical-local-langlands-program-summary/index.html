Categorical Local Langlands Program Summary

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $E/{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ be a finite extension, let $G/E$ be a connected reductive quasisplit group, and let $\ell\ne p$ be a prime. The supplied excerpt describes the following setup for the categorical local Langlands program. Its assertions are retained as source exposition; the record does not supply a separate conjectural statement.

On the automorphic side, write $\operatorname{Bun}_G$ for the stack of $G$-bundles on the Fargues--Fontaine curve. The excerpt says that this stack behaves like a smooth Artin stack of dimension zero and has strata $\operatorname{Bun}_G^b$ indexed by the Kottwitz set $B(G)$. For each $b\in B(G)$, it associates a group $G_b$, an inner form of a Levi subgroup of $G$, and describes the corresponding stratum as essentially the classifying stack of the locally profinite group $G_b(E)$. The word "essentially" is a material qualification: the excerpt does not specify an exact identification of stacks.

Using the coefficient notation appearing in the supplied transcription, set \[ D(\operatorname{Bun}_G):=D_{\mathrm{lis}}(\operatorname{Bun}_G,{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell). \] The excerpt attributes this category of constructible $\ell$-adic sheaves to Fargues--Scholze and reports equivalences \[ D(\operatorname{Bun}_G^b)\simeq D(G_b(E),{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) \qquad (b\in B(G)), \] where the right side denotes the derived category of smooth ${\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-representations of $G_b(E)$. Writing $i_b$ for the inclusion of the stratum, the excerpt supplies functors \[ i_{b!}:D(G_b(E),{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)\longrightarrow D(\operatorname{Bun}_G), \qquad i_b^*:D(\operatorname{Bun}_G)\longrightarrow D(G_b(E),{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell). \] No adjunction between these two displayed functors is asserted here. The excerpt further reports a semiorthogonal decomposition into the categories $D(G_b(E),{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$, without specifying its ordering or precise categorical conventions. For the neutral class $b=1$, it states that $G_1=G$ and that $i_{1!}$ embeds smooth $G(E)$-representations fully faithfully into sheaves on $\operatorname{Bun}_G$.

On the spectral side, let $W_E$ denote the Weil group of $E$, let $\widehat G$ denote the dual group, and retain the source notation ${}^{L}G$ for the $L$-group. The excerpt describes $\operatorname{Par}_G$ as a stack of $\ell$-adically continuous $L$-parameters \[ \phi:W_E\longrightarrow {}^{L}G({\mathbb{Q}}_\ell). \] It explicitly acknowledges that making this definition precise is subtle, but does not supply that definition. Attributing the results to independent works of Fargues--Scholze, Zhu, Hellmann, and Dat--Helm--Kurinczuk--Moss, it reports that $\operatorname{Par}_G$ is a reduced Artin stack, is globally a local complete intersection of pure dimension zero over $\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, and has each connected component expressible as a quotient of an affine variety by a reductive group action. It also reports a canonical map \[ \operatorname{Par}_G\longrightarrow B\widehat G, \] where $B\widehat G$ denotes the classifying stack of $\widehat G$, distinct from the Kottwitz set $B(G)$.

Unresolved mathematical target. David Hansen describes joint work in progress with Lucas Mann whose goal is to formulate a program proving the categorical local Langlands conjecture of Fargues--Scholze, cited in the excerpt as reference [3], for many groups. Consequently, the setup above cannot be promoted to a precise conjecture, a proposed equivalence of categories, or a universally quantified assertion for all such $G$. Short English statement

This record preserves David Hansen's introductory account of joint work with Lucas Mann on the categorical local Langlands program. It describes automorphic and spectral objects for a connected reductive quasisplit group over a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$, with $\ell\ne p$. The supplied excerpt does not state the conjecture or specify the groups targeted by the program, so a separate precise problem cannot be recovered.
