Stable Minimal Hypersurfaces and Entire Minimal Graphs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record combines two questions with different levels of specificity.

For a smooth Riemannian manifold $(M^n,g)$, a smooth immersion $\iota:\Sigma^{n-1}\to M$ is minimal if its mean curvature vector vanishes. It is complete if the induced metric $\iota^*g$ is complete. It is stable if the second variation of area is nonnegative for every smooth compactly supported normal variation. If the immersion is two-sided, with global unit normal $\nu$, stability is equivalently \[ \int_\Sigma\left(|\nabla\varphi|^2-\left(|A|^2+\operatorname{Ric}_g(\nu,\nu)\right)\varphi^2\right)\,\mathrm d\mu\geq0 \qquad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Sigma), \] where $A$ is the second fundamental form and all intrinsic quantities use the induced metric.

For a fixed ambient manifold, under a provisional smooth, boundaryless immersion interpretation, the existence question is \[ \exists\,\iota:\Sigma^{n-1}\to M^n\quad\text{such that }\iota\text{ is complete, stable, and minimal}. \] The source asks about asymptotically flat ambient manifolds but supplies no definition of asymptotic flatness, decay rate, end conditions, or dimension restrictions. It also does not specify whether this existence assertion concerns some such manifold, every such manifold, or a subclass satisfying additional hypotheses. Consequently, the ambient class and its quantification remain unresolved. Embeddedness, properness, two-sidedness, and boundary conditions are likewise unspecified; the provisional convention above requires review. The background additionally mentions rigidity, but supplies no precise rigidity conclusion.

The second question has the following classical dimensionwise formulation. For each integer $n\geq2$, let $u\in C^2({\mathbb{R}}^{n-1};{\mathbb{R}})$ and define its entire graph by \[ \Gamma_u=\{(x,u(x)):x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n-1}\}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n. \] Its minimality in the Euclidean metric is expressed by \[ \operatorname{div}\left(\frac{\nabla u}{\sqrt{1+|\nabla u|^2}}\right)=0 \qquad\text{on }{\mathbb{R}}^{n-1}. \] For which ambient dimensions $n$ does the following assertion hold? \[ \forall u\in C^2({\mathbb{R}}^{n-1};{\mathbb{R}}),\qquad \operatorname{div}\left(\frac{\nabla u}{\sqrt{1+|\nabla u|^2}}\right)=0 \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists a\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n-1}\ \exists b\in{\mathbb{R}}\ \forall x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n-1},\quad u(x)=a\cdot x+b. \] Here the graph is defined on all of ${\mathbb{R}}^{n-1}$, with no finite boundary or prescribed condition at infinity. The $C^2$ interpretation and the dimensionwise presentation make explicit conventional choices that the source does not state.

Short English statement

The record asks about the existence of complete stable minimal hypersurfaces in asymptotically flat manifolds and whether every entire Euclidean minimal graph is affine. The graph question admits a classical dimensionwise statement. The ambient existence question still requires precise geometric hypotheses and quantifiers; the mentioned rigidity question has no supplied conclusion.
