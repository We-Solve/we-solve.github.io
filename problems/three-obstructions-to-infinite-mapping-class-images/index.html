Math-1435

Three obstructions to infinite mapping-class images

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be the rank-$n$ free group. The source asks to understand $\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Out}(F_n),\operatorname{MCG}(S))$, particularly whether an infinite image is possible when $n\ge4$. It does not fix the surface's genus, punctures, boundary or mapping-class convention. The attached aid's quantitative conclusions concern $\operatorname{Mod}(S_g)$, the orientation-preserving mapping class group of a closed orientable surface, with $g\ge2$ for the stated Hurwitz bound.

Write \[ \mathcal I_n=\ker\left(\operatorname{Out}(F_n)\to\operatorname{GL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}})\right) \] for the outer Torelli subgroup. The aid reports that if $n\ge5$ and $\rho:\operatorname{Out}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Mod}(S_g)$ has infinite image, then simultaneously \[ (n+1)!\le84(g-1),\qquad \rho(\operatorname{Out}(F_n))\text{ is nonamenable},\qquad |\rho(\mathcal I_n)|=\infty. \] For $n=4$, it reports the factorial and Torelli conclusions unconditionally, but makes the nonamenability conclusion conditional on the computer-assisted Property $(T)$ theorem stated in Nitsche's arXiv:2009.05134. Its rank-five and higher Property $(T)$ inputs are described as peer reviewed. These conditions are necessary obstructions, not a sufficient condition for an infinite-image homomorphism.

The stronger Torelli statement reported in the aid is: for $n\ge4$, every finite-index $\Gamma\le\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ and homomorphism $\rho:\Gamma\to\operatorname{Mod}(S_g)$ satisfy \[ |\rho(\Gamma\cap\mathcal I_n)|<\infty \quad\Longrightarrow\quad |\rho(\Gamma)|<\infty. \] The factorial statement pertains to the full source group, not automatically to every finite-index subgroup.

The original remark, attributed only to "Juan", warns that the surface and rank need bounds because virtual infinite abelianization can produce an infinite virtually abelian mapping-class image. The aid repairs the quotient construction: if a finite-index subgroup of a finitely generated group $G$ surjects onto ${\mathbb{Z}}$, take its normal finite-index core $K$. Then $K$ still has infinite abelianization, and \[ G/[K,K] \] is an infinite finitely generated virtually abelian quotient, with finite-index abelian subgroup $K/[K,K]$. The aid reports its embedding into the mapping class group of a sufficiently large, explicitly bounded surface; the actual genus formula is absent from the supplied summary. This is a conditional construction, not a claim that every infinite mapping-class image is virtually abelian. Peer-reviewed Property $(T)$ excludes this mechanism for $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$, $n\ge5$, and the aid excludes it conditionally on the stated preprint for $n=4$. The three gates and this repair do not classify the full Hom set or settle all surfaces in the original question.

Short English statement

Investigate infinite mapping-class images of $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$. For closed targets, the supplied aid gives a genus bound, a Torelli-image obstruction and a nonamenability obstruction, with a separate rank-four preprint caveat, and clarifies the original virtual-abelianization construction without claiming a full classification.
