Math-3608

Prime Affine Hopf Algebras of GK Dimension One

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be the intended ground field. A Hopf $k$-algebra $H$ is a unital associative $k$-algebra with a coassociative coproduct $\Delta:H\to H\otimes_k H$, counit $\epsilon:H\to k$ and antipode $S:H\to H$, with the bialgebra compatibilities and antipode identities \[ m(S\otimes\operatorname{id})\Delta =m(\operatorname{id}\otimes S)\Delta=\eta\epsilon. \] It is affine when finitely generated as a $k$-algebra, and prime when the product of two nonzero two-sided ideals is nonzero. Primeness is not replaced by the stronger domain condition.

For a finite-dimensional generating subspace $V\subseteq H$ containing $1$, the Gelfand--Kirillov dimension is \[ \operatorname{GKdim}H= \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\dim_k(V^n)}{\log n}, \] independent of the choice of such $V$. The question is to describe all Hopf algebras satisfying \[ H\text{ prime and affine},\qquad\operatorname{GKdim}H=1. \] In the usual classification interpretation, give explicit models up to Hopf-algebra isomorphism, prove that every object in the class occurs, and determine when model parameters give isomorphic Hopf algebras. A Hopf isomorphism must respect the coproduct, counit and antipode, not merely the underlying algebra. The source does not explicitly specify that equivalence relation, so an algebra-only classification cannot automatically substitute for it.

The record omits the ground-field restrictions, including characteristic and algebraic closedness. These affect the class to be described and are not filled in by assuming a familiar characteristic-zero theorem. No regularity, finite-global-dimension, domain, commutativity, cocommutativity or additional finiteness condition is appended. In particular GK dimension one is not the same requirement as global dimension one.

The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports completion of the prime affine regular GK-dimension-one classification, while saying the unrestricted prime affine classification remains broader and only partially resolved. The exact meaning of regular and field hypotheses in those results are not reproduced. This special-case report is kept separate from the full question rather than used to insert regularity. The partially-solved label and reported official-source verification remain supplied metadata, not independently checked current conclusions.

Short English statement

Classify all prime finitely generated Hopf algebras whose Gelfand--Kirillov dimension is one, with the ground field and isomorphism convention to be confirmed. The source's completed regular case is a narrower result and does not justify adding regularity to the requested classification.
