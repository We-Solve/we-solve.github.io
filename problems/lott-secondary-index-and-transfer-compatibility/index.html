Write ${\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$ for the additive quotient group of real numbers by integers. Retain $KR^0$ and $KR_{{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}}^{-1}$ exactly as the source's notation for the proposed source and target functors. Lott's secondary-index conjecture, as supplied. There exists a natural transformation \[ \eta\colon KR^0\longrightarrow KR_{{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}}^{-1} \] that identifies analytic push-forward with the Becker--Gottlieb--Dold transfer.

The intended compatibility can be expressed schematically as follows. Write $\mathcal F(X)=KR^0(X)$ and $\mathcal G(X)=KR_{{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}}^{-1}(X)$. For every map $p\colon E\to B$ admitted by the intended theory, if the relevant operators have types \[ p_*^{\mathrm{an}}\colon\mathcal F(E)\to\mathcal F(B), \qquad \operatorname{tr}_p\colon\mathcal G(E)\to\mathcal G(B), \] then the required identity is \[ \eta_B\left(p_*^{\mathrm{an}}(x)\right) = \operatorname{tr}_p\left(\eta_E(x)\right) \qquad\text{for every }x\in\mathcal F(E). \] Here $p_*^{\mathrm{an}}$ denotes the intended analytic push-forward and $\operatorname{tr}_p$ the intended Becker--Gottlieb--Dold transfer. This formula records the requested correspondence; its displayed operator types are conditional and are not additional hypotheses supplied by the record. Naturality means that the components $\eta_X$ commute with the functorial maps in the intended category.
