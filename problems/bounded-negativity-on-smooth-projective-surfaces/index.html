Math-851

Bounded Negativity on Smooth Projective Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a smooth projective surface over ${\mathbb{C}}$. A reduced effective curve on $X$ means an effective divisor \[ C=C_1+\cdots+C_r,\qquad r\geq 1, \] where the $C_i$ are distinct integral curves, each with multiplicity one. Write $C^2=\deg(C\cdot C)$ for its self-intersection number on $X$; explicitly, \[ C^2=\sum_{i=1}^r C_i^2+2\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq r}C_i\cdot C_j. \] The question is whether \[ \forall X\text{ smooth projective over }{\mathbb{C}}\text{ of dimension }2,\quad \exists b_X\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\quad \forall C\text{ reduced effective on }X,\qquad C^2\geq -b_X. \] The constant may depend on $X$, but must be independent of $C$. Requiring $b_X\geq 0$ does not change the existence question. The supplied formulation includes reducible curves and imposes no connectedness requirement on $C$. Its precise correspondence with the cited workshop discussion requires source verification: the record supplies a report-wide page range but no specific problem location.

Short English statement

For each smooth projective complex surface, is there a uniform lower bound for the self-intersection numbers of all its reduced effective curves, including reducible curves? The supplied record labels this question open; that status is supplied dated curation and has not been independently verified here.
