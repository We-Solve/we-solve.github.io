Derived Geometric Satake for the Basic Object

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ and $G^\vee$ denote the group and its Langlands dual intended in the supplied source. Let $X$ denote the geometric datum defining the basic object $\delta_X$, and let $M^\vee$ denote its proposed dual geometric object, equipped with the relevant $G^\vee$-action and grading. The source does not specify the admissible class of these data, the base field, or the sheaf coefficients.

Write $\mathcal C_X$ for the intended category containing $\delta_X$, and $\Phi$ for the applicable derived geometric Satake equivalence. Here $\mathcal C_X$ is a placeholder for a category whose precise identification is unresolved below. Write $(-)^{\mathrm{sh}}$ for the source's shearing operation, which reinterprets grading in cohomological terms; its degree conventions are not supplied. The notation $\mathcal O(M^\vee)$ denotes the structure sheaf or function algebra regarded as an object of the intended equivariant quasi-coherent category, whose precise realization also requires specification.

The intended conjectural assertion is that, for every datum in the source's unspecified range of applicability, \[ \Phi(\delta_X)\simeq \mathcal O(M^\vee)^{\mathrm{sh}}, \] and that the resulting identification of endomorphism algebras has the form \[ \operatorname{End}_{\mathcal C_X}(\delta_X) \simeq \left(\mathcal O(M^\vee)^{G^\vee}\right)^{\mathrm{sh}}. \] These formulas retain the source's notation: whether endomorphisms mean a derived endomorphism object, a graded algebra, or degree-zero endomorphisms, and whether invariants are ordinary or derived, is not determined by the record.

The extracted passage places $\delta_X$ in \[ \operatorname{Shv}_{G(D)}\left(G(D^\circ)/G(D)\right), \] where $D$ denotes a formal disk and $D^\circ$ its punctured disk, and places the proposed image in an equivariant quasi-coherent category over $\check{\mathfrak g}^{*}$, with $\check{\mathfrak g}=\operatorname{Lie}(G^\vee)$ and an additional $\mathbb G_m$-grading. Elsewhere the same extraction expresses the endomorphisms using $\operatorname{Shv}_{G_{\mathcal O}}(X_F)$ and $\operatorname{Shv}_{G_{\mathcal O}}(X_{\mathcal O})$, with objects $\delta_X$ and $\omega_X$, and describes the answer as equivariant Borel--Moore homology of $X_{\mathcal O}$, after shearing. The definitions of these spaces and objects, and the identifications connecting these categories, are absent. Consequently, the displayed conjecture is a conditional reconstruction of the intended target, not a fully specified universally quantified statement.

Short English statement

The proposed compatibility says that derived geometric Satake sends the basic object $\delta_X$ to the sheared structure sheaf of the proposed dual space $M^\vee$, with its endomorphisms corresponding to sheared invariant functions. The admissible geometric data, categories, shearing conventions, and meanings of endomorphisms and invariants require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
