Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.92

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Identify ${\mathbb{R}}^2$ with ${\mathbb{C}}$, and put \[ \mathbb H=\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0\},\qquad B^2=\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0,\ |z|<1\}. \] The target is the upper half of the unit disk, exactly as printed. Let $\mathcal F$ be the source's class of analytic conformal maps $f:\mathbb H\to B^2$; whether conformal explicitly requires global injectivity should be confirmed. An angular limit $\alpha$ at $0$ means that $f(z)\to\alpha$ as $z\to0$ in each nontangential region $|\operatorname{Re}z|\le C\operatorname{Im}z$, $C>0$.

The source first reports a sufficient condition on an approach set $E\subset\mathbb H$: convergence $f(z)\to\alpha$ along $E$ forces the same angular limit if $E$ is a curve terminating at zero, or under the weaker radial-density condition \[ \liminf_{r\downarrow0}\frac{m(A\cap(0,r))}{r}>0, \qquad A=\{|z|:z\in E\}, \] where $m$ is one-dimensional Lebesgue measure, subject to the missing measurability convention for arbitrary $E$.

The converse paragraph introduces a set $K$ but repeats convergence along $E$, leaving $K$ absent from its stated transfer property. A natural intended correction would require, for every $f\in\mathcal F$ and every candidate limit $\alpha$, that convergence along $K$ imply angular convergence to $\alpha$. The relation between $E$ and $K$ is not given, so this correction is explicit and conditional. An approach-set formulation also needs $0\in\overline K$ to avoid vacuous limits.

For a fixed positive hyperbolic width $\ell$, let \[ K_1=\{z\in\mathbb H:\rho(z,K)<\ell\},\qquad \rho(z,K)=\inf_{w\in K}\rho(z,w),\qquad B'=\{|z|:z\in K_1\}, \] where $\rho$ is the Poincaré distance on $\mathbb H$. The requested thickness conclusion is \[ \limsup_{r\downarrow0}\frac{m(B'\cap(0,r))}{r}>0. \] This is positive upper radial density, not the stronger lower-density condition used in the sufficient result. The question concerns whether it is forced by the intended universal transfer property of $K$. The source does not specify whether the assertion must hold for every $\ell>0$ or a particular width.

The source additionally attributes to conformality the extension of a given limit along $K$ to the thickened set $K_1$, but the printed hypothesis instead uses $E$; the same identification issue must be resolved there. It reports the desired density conclusion when $K$ lies in an angle with vertex zero whose closure stays inside $\mathbb H$; since the vertex itself is on $\partial\mathbb H$, this must exclude the vertex when describing the closure, or use a clarified angular convention. These are attributed background claims. The supplied triage labels the problem open as of 2018 and reports no definitive later resolution; no independent verification is claimed.

Short English statement

Must an approach set that forces angular limits for every conformal map have a hyperbolic thickening with positive upper radial density at zero? The printed converse switches from $E$ to $K$ without relating them, and leaves the thickening width and approach-set conventions unclear. The target half-disk and upper-versus-lower density distinction are retained exactly.
