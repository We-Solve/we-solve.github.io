Math-1699

A divisor-trace criterion for removability of meromorphic sphere maps

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,p)\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ be a generic real-analytic submanifold germ, meaning $T_aM+iT_aM=T_a{\mathbb{C}}^n$, and let $n\leq N$. Write $B^N=\{w\in{\mathbb{C}}^N:\sum_j|w_j|^2<1\}$. Suppose $f:M\to\partial B^N$ is continuous and CR and is the restriction of a meromorphic mapping on a neighborhood of $p$ wherever that mapping is defined. Francine Meylan asks when $f$ extends holomorphically to a full neighborhood of $p$, in particular whether minimality of $M$ suffices. Here minimality means that the local CR orbit has full dimension in $M$. The source comments that the assertion holds when $M$ has real codimension $1$, citing [Ch].

The supplied partial reduction can be stated using a reduced common-denominator representation \[ F=(P_1,\ldots,P_N)/q, \] where $P_j,q$ are holomorphic near $p$, $q\not\equiv0$, and no irreducible holomorphic factor of $q$ divides all the $P_j$. Sphere-valuedness gives the real-analytic identity \[ \sum_{j=1}^N|P_j|^2=|q|^2\quad\text{on }M. \] Let $h$ be an irreducible denominator factor and $H=\{h=0\}$. If the trace $M\cap H$ contains a real-analytic submanifold $S$ in the smooth locus of the relevant irreducible hypersurface, generic within $H$ and a holomorphic uniqueness set there, then the aid asserts that every $P_j$ vanishes on $H$. Thus $h$ divides all numerators, contradicting reducedness. Such a factor cannot remain as a nonremovable pole. The uniqueness-set requirement is relative to the complex hypersurface, not just genericity in ambient ${\mathbb{C}}^n$.

Under minimality, the aid states that the required uniqueness condition follows whenever the trace has an ordinary rank-two zero: a nearby point $a\in M\cap H$ with \[ \operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{R}}}{\,\mathrm{d}}(h|_M)_a=2, \] where $h|_M$ is viewed as a map to ${\mathbb{C}}\simeq{\mathbb{R}}^2$. Consequently a reduced counterexample to holomorphic extension with minimal source would necessarily satisfy \[ \operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{R}}}{\,\mathrm{d}}(h|_M)_a<2 \quad\text{at every nearby }a\in M\cap\{h=0\} \] for every irreducible factor $h$ still occurring in its denominator, with irreducible representatives and minimality taken on a sufficiently small neighborhood. This is a necessary restriction on a hypothetical counterexample. It does not remove the remaining factors whose entire zero trace is critical, and therefore does not settle the minimal-source question.

Short English statement

Ask when a continuous sphere-valued CR map with a meromorphic extension is actually holomorphic near the source point. The supplied aid removes pole factors whose trace is a uniqueness set, and under minimality restricts any surviving factor to a wholly critical trace.
