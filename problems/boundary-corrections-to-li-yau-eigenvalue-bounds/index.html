Boundary Corrections to Li–Yau Eigenvalue Bounds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation, let $d\in{\mathbb{N}}$ and let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be a nonempty bounded open set. Write \[ V=|\Omega|,\qquad S=\mathcal H^{d-1}(\partial\Omega),\qquad \omega_d=|\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:|x|<1\}|. \] Here $\mathcal H^{d-1}$ denotes Hausdorff measure. Boundary-measure expressions below require $S<\infty$. This is a framework for explaining the question; the supplied record does not specify the dimension, admissible domain class, or boundary regularity required for the proposed improvement.

Let \[ 0<\lambda_1^D(\Omega)\le\lambda_2^D(\Omega)\le\cdots \] be the eigenvalues of the nonnegative Dirichlet Laplacian $-\Delta$, counted with multiplicity. The Li--Yau lower bound for their partial sums is \[ \sum_{j=1}^{k}\lambda_j^D(\Omega) \ge \frac{d}{d+2}\frac{4\pi^2}{(\omega_d V)^{2/d}}\,k^{1+2/d}, \qquad k\in{\mathbb{N}},\quad k\ge1. \] The supplied problem asks whether this bound admits a strengthening by a nonnegative, nontrivial correction involving the boundary surface measure $S$. Schematically, the requested form is \[ \sum_{j=1}^{k}\lambda_j^D(\Omega) \ge \frac{d}{d+2}\frac{4\pi^2}{(\omega_d V)^{2/d}}\,k^{1+2/d} +\mathcal B_D(\Omega,k), \] where $\mathcal B_D$ is to involve $S$. This display records the direction of the requested improvement; it does not specify a proposed formula or assert that such an inequality holds. The source record leaves unresolved the allowable additional geometric dependence, the size and strict positivity of the correction, its uniformity over domains, and whether it should hold for every $k$ or only in a specified regime.

Where the Neumann Laplacian has discrete spectrum, write its eigenvalues, including all zero eigenvalues and counting multiplicity, as \[ 0=\mu_1^N(\Omega)\le\mu_2^N(\Omega)\le\cdots. \] The problem also requests analogous boundary-measure corrections for sums of Neumann eigenvalues and for Berezin-type bounds, where appropriate. For reference, the usual order-one Dirichlet Riesz mean is \[ R_D(z)=\sum_{j\ge1}(z-\lambda_j^D(\Omega))_+, \qquad (t)_+=\max\{t,0\},\qquad z\ge0, \] and its standard Berezin upper bound is \[ R_D(z)\le \frac{2}{d+2}\frac{\omega_d}{(2\pi)^d}\,V z^{1+d/2}. \] A strengthening of this particular upper bound would subtract a nonnegative boundary correction. However, the supplied record does not identify the intended Berezin-type functional or its order, nor the precise Neumann baseline, inequality direction, or parameter ranges. Consequently, these extensions remain requests for a precise formulation, rather than specified universally quantified inequalities. No connectedness assumption, boundary regularity hypothesis, or particular correction term is supplied.

Short English statement

Can the Dirichlet Li--Yau lower bound for sums of Laplacian eigenvalues be improved by a term involving boundary surface area? The record also asks for analogous Neumann and Berezin-type improvements where appropriate. It does not specify the correction, admissible domains, or all parameter ranges, so the displayed target is schematic and requires human mathematical review.
