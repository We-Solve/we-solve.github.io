Exact Thresholds for Rainbow Matchings in Hypergraphs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a hypergraph $H=(V,E)$, a matching is a set $M\subseteq E$ of pairwise disjoint edges. If the edges carry colours given by a map $c:E\to C$, then $M$ is rainbow when its edges have pairwise distinct colours. Thus a rainbow matching of size $t$ satisfies \[ |M|=t,\qquad e\cap e'=\varnothing\quad\text{and}\quad c(e)\ne c(e')\qquad(e,e'\in M,\ e\ne e'). \] A hypergraph is $r$-uniform if every edge has exactly $r$ vertices. These definitions explain the terminology; the supplied record does not specify the full class of coloured hypergraphs intended in the question.

The requested problem is to determine how large $N$ must be, in terms of $t$ and $r$, to guarantee a rainbow matching of size $t$ in every admissible configuration. However, the supplied excerpt does not define $N$, the admissible configurations, or their required colouring and matching hypotheses. Consequently, a fully quantified threshold statement cannot be recovered from this record alone.

The supplied dated curation reports that, for each fixed uniformity $r\ge 3$, the functions denoted by $f(r,t)$ and $F(r,t)$ have order $t^r$, with positive multiplicative constants depending on $r$. It also reports that the conjecture attributed to Glebov--Sudakov--Szabó is false and describes that conjecture as linear. The record does not define these functions or state the conjecture's precise formulation. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified results in this draft. The curation labels the problem partially solved and says that the exact threshold remains undetermined.

Short English statement

Determine the threshold $N$, as a function of $t$ and $r$, that forces $t$ pairwise disjoint edges with distinct colours. The source excerpt omits what $N$ counts and the hypotheses on the coloured hypergraph, so the intended threshold cannot yet be stated precisely. The supplied curation reports order $t^r$ for fixed $r\ge 3$, while describing the exact threshold as unresolved.
