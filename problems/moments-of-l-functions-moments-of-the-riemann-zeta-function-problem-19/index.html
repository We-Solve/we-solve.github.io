Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 19

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual interpretation of primitive forms here as holomorphic cuspidal Hecke eigenforms, let $\mathcal H_k$ denote the set of primitive forms of level $1$ and even integral weight $k\geq 2$, with each form normalized to have first Fourier coefficient $1$. For $f\in\mathcal H_k$ and $g\in\mathcal H_\ell$, let $L(s,f\otimes g)$ denote their Rankin--Selberg $L$-function in the normalization with central point $s=\tfrac12$.

Fix a constant $C\geq0$, independent of $K$. A natural unweighted interpretation of the sixth moment in the supplied question is \[ M_6(K;C)= \sum_{\substack{k,\ell\in 2{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\\ K<k+\ell\leq2K\\ |k-\ell|\leq C}} \ \sum_{f\in\mathcal H_k}\ \sum_{g\in\mathcal H_\ell} \left|L\!\left(\tfrac12,f\otimes g\right)\right|^6. \] The fixed constant $C$ makes explicit the source condition $|k-\ell|=O(1)$.

Requested task. Use the recent work of Humphries--Khan, cited as [HK] in the supplied source, to obtain upper bounds in the weight aspect for the sixth moment of these central values as $K\to\infty$, subject to the displayed restrictions on $k$ and $\ell$.

Unresolved qualifications. The displayed sum is an explicit possible interpretation, not a uniquely specified moment in the source. The record does not state whether the moment is unweighted or harmonically weighted, whether it is a sum or a normalized average, or whether the bound is intended for each admissible weight pair or after summation over weights. It gives no target exponent or precise dependence on the bounded weight difference. The holomorphic cuspidal interpretation and the normalization above make the notation explicit but require confirmation against the original source. The bibliographic identity and applicable hypotheses of [HK] are not supplied.

Short English statement

Alia Hamieh asks for upper bounds for sixth moments of central Rankin--Selberg values attached to level $1$ primitive forms whose weights differ by a bounded amount and satisfy $K<k+\ell\leq2K$, using work of Humphries--Khan. The averaging convention and the precise reference [HK] remain unresolved. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this is not an independently verified status assessment.
