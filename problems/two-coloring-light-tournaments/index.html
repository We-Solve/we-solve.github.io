Recovered definition and attempted 2-SAT encoding

For an arc $u\to v$, the primary report defines $N(u,v)=\{w:v\to w\to u\}$ and calls the arc light when the induced tournament on $N(u,v)$ is acyclic. A tournament is light when every arc is light. We use this supplied primary definition, leaving the qualified catalogue excerpt intact. A tournament is acyclic exactly when it has no directed triangle. To see this, a shortest directed cycle of length at least four has a chord between its first and third vertices; either orientation produces a shorter directed cycle (a triangle in one direction). Thus a two-coloring is exactly a binary assignment in which every directed triangle is nonmonochromatic. A tempting route is to encode these constraints as 2-SAT, perhaps with auxiliary Boolean variables. Even the single directed triangle obstructs a direct exact encoding preserving its three color variables.
Proposition: The ternary nonmonochromatic relation cannot be the projection of the satisfying assignments of any 2-CNF formula.
Proof: Satisfying assignments of a 2-CNF formula are closed under coordinatewise majority of three assignments. If their majority falsified a two-literal clause, at least two assignments would falsify its first literal and at least two would falsify its second literal. Two subsets of size two of a three-element set intersect, so some assignment would falsify both, a contradiction. Unit clauses obey the same property. Projections retain majority closure: choose witnesses for three projected assignments and take the majority including the auxiliary coordinates. But $001,010,100$ are all nonmonochromatic and their majority $000$ is monochromatic. This contradicts the necessary closure property. ∎
The directed triangle is light because each arc neighborhood is a singleton. So lightness does not make every individual triangle constraint directly expressible by existential 2-CNF. This does not rule out a more sophisticated polynomial algorithm or a decision reduction that does not preserve all assignments.

Verification and remaining gaps

All eight assignments were checked, giving six legal colorings and the displayed majority violation. The necessary global structure of light tournaments has not been exploited to obtain two colors, and no hardness reduction is proved.
