A dimension-free boundary-regularity threshold for proper ball maps

Definitions and mathematical statement

Problem 3 (X. Huang). Is there one integer $t\geq0$, independent of both dimensions, such that every proper holomorphic map $F:B^n\to B^N$, $1<n<N$, of class $C^t$ on the closed ball is rational? Here $B^j$ is the unit ball in ${\mathbb{C}}^j$, proper means inverse images of compact sets are compact, and rational means its components are rational functions holomorphic on the ball. If nonintegral $t=k+\alpha$ is intended, specify the distinct Hölder convention $C^{k,\alpha}$, $0<\alpha<1$, rather than silently rounding the threshold.

Let $\tau(n,N)$ be the least integer regularity that forces rationality for all such maps. The aid reports \[ 1\leq\tau(n,N)\leq N-n+1, \qquad \tau(n,N)\text{ is nondecreasing in }N. \] It reports $C^2$ sufficiency in low codimension and $C^3$ sufficiency for $N\leq n(n+1)/2$, but supplies no full citations or the precise range of the former statement. These are qualified literature reports, and no dimension-independent bound is supplied as proved.

Reported Veronese-tensor propagation. For an integer $d\geq1$, let $M_d=\binom{n+d-1}{d}$, and set \[ V_d(z)=\left(\sqrt{\frac{d!}{\alpha!}}z^\alpha\right)_{|\alpha|=d}, \qquad T_dF=V_d\otimes F:B^n\to B^{NM_d}. \] The multinomial normalization gives \[ \|V_d(z)\|^2=\|z\|^{2d},\qquad \|T_dF(z)\|^2=\|z\|^{2d}\|F(z)\|^2. \] The aid reports that $T_dF$ is proper whenever $F$ is proper, and for every integer $k\geq0$ and $0<\alpha<1$, \[ F\in C^k(\overline{B^n})\ \Longleftrightarrow\ T_dF\in C^k(\overline{B^n}), \] \[ F\in C^{k,\alpha}(\overline{B^n})\ \Longleftrightarrow\ T_dF\in C^{k,\alpha}(\overline{B^n}). \] Thus the exact boundary regularity is retained. Every boundary point has some $z_j\ne0$, and the $z_j^dF$ component permits local recovery by division there; no division at the interior origin is needed. The corresponding rationality equivalence is \[ T_dF\text{ rational}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F\text{ rational}. \] The aid also reports equivalence of holomorphic extendability across each boundary point/patch. Hence failure of extension on every boundary piece is preserved, not inferred merely from nonrationality.

Any future counterexample at one target dimension therefore propagates to arbitrarily large targets without losing its integer or Hölder regularity, since $n>1$ and $M_d\to\infty$. This is a propagation lemma, not a counterexample or an upper/lower bound resolving the universal-threshold problem. The report says that problem remains open in its checked literature, with no date or complete bibliography supplied.

Short English statement

The source asks for a rationality threshold independent of both ball dimensions. The aid reports dimension-dependent bounds and a Veronese-tensor lemma preserving properness, rationality, exact boundary regularity and extendability. It propagates a hypothetical counterexample but provides none.
