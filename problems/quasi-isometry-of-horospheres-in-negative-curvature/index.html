Math-3493

Quasi-Isometry of Horospheres in Negative Curvature

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X^{n+1},g)$ be a closed Riemannian manifold, meaning compact and without boundary, with strictly negative sectional curvature. Let $\pi:(\widetilde X,\widetilde g)\to(X,g)$ be its universal Riemannian covering, with $\widetilde g=\pi^*g$, and write $d_{\widetilde g}$ for its distance. The use of a universal cover is understood componentwise if connectedness is not implicit.

For a unit-speed geodesic ray $\gamma:[0,\infty)\to\widetilde X$, define its Busemann function by \[ b_\gamma(x)=\lim_{s\to\infty}\left(d_{\widetilde g}(x,\gamma(s))-s\right). \] A horosphere is a level set $H_{\gamma,t}=b_\gamma^{-1}(t)$, where $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$. Equip each horosphere $H$ with the restricted Riemannian metric $\widetilde g|_{TH}$ and its intrinsic length distance \[ d_H(p,q)=\inf_{\alpha}\operatorname{Length}_{\widetilde g}(\alpha), \] where the infimum is over piecewise smooth curves $\alpha$ in $H$ joining $p$ to $q$. This interprets the source notation $(H,\widetilde g|_H)$ as the induced Riemannian metric.

A map $f:(H,d_H)\to(K,d_K)$ is a $(\lambda,C)$-quasi-isometry if $\lambda\ge1$, $C\ge0$, \[ \lambda^{-1}d_H(p,q)-C\le d_K(f(p),f(q))\le\lambda d_H(p,q)+C \qquad(p,q\in H), \] and every point of $K$ has distance at most $C$ from $f(H)$.

Question. For every closed negatively curved $(X^{n+1},g)$, are all horospheres in $(\widetilde X,\widetilde g)$ pairwise quasi-isometric? Explicitly, does \[ \forall H,K\quad\exists\lambda_{H,K}\ge1\quad\exists C_{H,K}\ge0\quad\exists f_{H,K}:H\to K \] hold with $f_{H,K}$ a $(\lambda_{H,K},C_{H,K})$-quasi-isometry?

If this holds, can the constants be chosen uniformly over all pairs of horospheres in the fixed universal cover, namely, \[ \exists\lambda_X\ge1\quad\exists C_X\ge0\quad\forall H,K\quad\exists f_{H,K}:H\to K \] with every $f_{H,K}$ a $(\lambda_X,C_X)$-quasi-isometry? Here the constants may depend on $(X,g)$. The source does not explicitly specify the dependence intended by "uniformly"; this is the natural within-manifold interpretation, and any intended uniformity across different manifolds remains unresolved. The source also gives no explicit range for $n$.

Short English statement

For a closed negatively curved manifold, must every two horospheres in its universal cover be quasi-isometric when equipped with their intrinsic induced Riemannian distances? If so, can one choose the quasi-isometry constants independently of the two horospheres, for that fixed manifold? The scope of the word "uniformly" requires source review.
