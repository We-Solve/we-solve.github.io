Even-Codimension Stratifications of Codimension-Two Embeddings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A piecewise-linear (PL) manifold is a manifold equipped with a PL structure. Here simply connected means path connected with trivial fundamental group. A homotopy equivalence $M\simeq N$ means that there are continuous maps $f:M\to N$ and $g:N\to M$ such that $g\circ f\simeq\operatorname{id}_M$ and $f\circ g\simeq\operatorname{id}_N$. The notation $D^2$ denotes a two-dimensional disk; the source does not specify its boundary convention. An embedding is a map that is a homeomorphism onto its image; whether a PL embedding is required here is not specified.

Source question. Let $M$ be a simply connected PL manifold with $M\simeq N$. The supplied statement asserts that there is consequently an embedding \[ i:M\hookrightarrow N\times D^2. \] Can this embedding be stratified using only strata of even codimension?

Unresolved qualifications. The asserted implication from homotopy equivalence to the existence of this embedding is retained as a source assertion, not established here. The record does not specify the nature or dimension of $N$, the dimension of $M$, compactness or boundary hypotheses, or further conditions needed for that assertion. It also does not specify which embedding is intended, whether the question concerns every such embedding or the existence of a suitable one, or whether the embedding must represent the given homotopy equivalence under projection to $N$.

A stratum is a manifold piece of a stratification, but the admissible stratifications and the compatibility required with $i$ are not defined in the record. For a stratum $S$ in a manifold $X$ of dimension $d$, its ambient codimension is $d-\dim S$; even codimension means that this integer belongs to $2{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$. The record does not identify whether codimension is measured in $M$, in $N\times D^2$, or in another stratified space, nor whether the image must be a union of ambient strata. Thus the question above preserves the supplied target, but a fully specified quantified formulation requires these choices to be resolved.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the asserted embedding of a simply connected PL manifold $M$, homotopy equivalent to $N$, into $N\times D^2$ admits a stratification with only even-codimensional strata. The hypotheses guaranteeing the embedding, the meaning of a compatible stratification, and the reference space for codimension remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
