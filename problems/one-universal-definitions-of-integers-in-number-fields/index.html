One-universal definitions of integers in number fields

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a number field and $\mathcal O_K$ its ring of integers. The source's bound/free-variable collision and final $\theta/\phi$ mismatch require explicit notation repair. With $a$ the free variable, the three questions are: \[ \begin{array}{ll} \text{(a)}&\exists\theta_K(a)\text{ with prefix }\forall t\exists\mathbf y \text{ defining }\mathcal O_K\text{ in }K;\\ \text{(b)}&\text{the same one-scalar-universal prefix defining }\mathbb Z\text{ in }K;\\ \text{(c)}&\exists\phi_K(a)\text{ with a purely universal prefix defining }\mathbb Z\text{ in }K. \end{array} \] The universal block in (c) is not stated to contain only one scalar variable. The language, parameters and uniformity across $K$ are not fully specified; use the intended ring language and retain those choices for review.

The aid reports affirmative answers to all three for $K=\mathbb Q$, attributed to Koenigsmann. It reports purely universal ten-variable definitions of $\mathcal O_K$, attributed to Daans, but emphasizes that ten universal variables do not answer the one-universal requirement in (a). Its checked literature did not settle the exact general-number-field questions as stated. Full definitions, references and parameter conventions are absent.

The aid gives a conditional prenex transfer lemma. Suppose $R\subseteq F$ is defined by \[ R(z)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \forall\mathbf s\in F^q\;G(z,\mathbf s)\ne0, \] and $A\subseteq R$ is defined inside $R$ by $\forall t\exists\mathbf y\,\Phi(a,t,\mathbf y)$, with $m$ existential variables and quantifier-free matrix. Standard relativization gives a definition in $F$ with prefix \[ \forall^{q+1}\exists^{q+m}\forall^{qm}. \] For clarity an explicit matrix, with $q$-tuples $\mathbf s_0,\mathbf r,\mathbf s_1,\ldots,\mathbf s_m$, is \[ \begin{split} \forall\mathbf s_0\forall t\exists\mathbf r\exists\mathbf y \forall\mathbf s_1\cdots\forall\mathbf s_m\quad G(a,\mathbf s_0)\ne0\ \wedge\ \bigl(&G(t,\mathbf r)=0\ \vee\\ &(\Phi(a,t,\mathbf y)\wedge \bigwedge_{j=1}^mG(y_j,\mathbf s_j)\ne0)\bigr). \end{split} \] The first conjunct retains membership of the free variable in $R$; the disjunction handles universal $t$ outside $R$. For the reported ten-variable integrality realization this becomes $\forall^{11}\exists^{10+m}\forall^{10m}$, provided its matrix has the assumed form. It is not a one-universal formula and therefore does not solve (a) or (b).

Finally the aid reports that polynomial or rational one-parameter maps cannot surject onto $F^q$, $q\geq2$, for these infinite fields: their coordinates are algebraically dependent, so their images lie in a proper algebraic locus. This excludes that particular algebraic sweep as a way of compressing universal variables. It says nothing decisive about arbitrary definable arithmetic coding or every logical quantifier reduction.

Short English statement

The source distinguishes one universal variable followed by existentials from an arbitrary universal block. The aid reports the rational-field case, ten-variable integrality progress and an exact relativization bound, while leaving the precise general-number-field requests unresolved. Failure of a one-parameter algebraic sweep does not rule out all definable coding.
