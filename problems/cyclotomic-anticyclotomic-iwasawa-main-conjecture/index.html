Cyclotomic–Anticyclotomic Iwasawa Main Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E/{\mathbb{Q}}$ be an elliptic curve, let $p$ be a prime at which $E$ has good ordinary or multiplicative reduction, and let $k$ be an imaginary quadratic field. Let $k^{\mathrm{cyc}}/k$ and $k^{\mathrm{ac}}/k$ denote its cyclotomic and anticyclotomic ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-extensions, respectively, and put \[ k_\infty=k^{\mathrm{cyc}}k^{\mathrm{ac}},\qquad G=\operatorname{Gal}(k_\infty/k),\qquad \Lambda(G)={\mathbb{Z}}_p[[G]]=\varprojlim_U{\mathbb{Z}}_p[G/U], \] where $U$ ranges over the open subgroups of $G$.

For an explicit conventional interpretation of the dual Selmer group, let $E[p^\infty]=\bigcup_{n\geq1}E[p^n]$. For each finite extension $F/k$ contained in $k_\infty$, define the usual $p$-primary Selmer group by \[ \operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/F)=\ker\!\left(H^1(F,E[p^\infty])\longrightarrow\prod_v H^1(F_v,E)[p^\infty]\right), \] where $v$ ranges over all places of $F$, the maps are induced by localization and the Kummer sequence, and $H^1$ denotes continuous Galois cohomology. Set \[ \operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/k_\infty)=\varinjlim_F\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/F),\qquad X(E/k_\infty)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{cont}}\!\left(\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/k_\infty),{\mathbb{Q}}_p/{\mathbb{Z}}_p\right), \] using restriction maps in the direct limit and the discrete topology on the Selmer group. The dual has the induced $\Lambda(G)$-module structure. This is a stated conventional interpretation: the supplied excerpt does not specify its Selmer local conditions or whether any local conditions are omitted or modified.

Write $f$ for the weight-two modular form associated with $E$, as suggested by the notation in the source, and retain $L_p(f,k_\infty)$ as the source's symbol for the intended two-variable $p$-adic $L$-function. Its construction, normalization, coefficient ring, and Euler-factor conventions are not supplied. For a finitely generated torsion $\Lambda(G)$-module $M$, write $\operatorname{char}_{\Lambda(G)}(M)$ for its characteristic ideal; for an element $a\in\Lambda(G)$, write $(a)$ for the principal ideal it generates.

Source conjectural target. For every triple $(E,p,k)$ satisfying the stated hypotheses, is $X(E/k_\infty)$ torsion over $\Lambda(G)$, and does \[ \left(L_p(f,k_\infty)\right)=\operatorname{char}_{\Lambda(G)}\!\left(X(E/k_\infty)\right) \] hold as an equality of ideals of $\Lambda(G)$? The characteristic-ideal assertion presupposes finite generation of $X(E/k_\infty)$, and the left-hand side presupposes a specified integral realization of $L_p(f,k_\infty)$ in that ring. Neither prerequisite is explained in the supplied excerpt. Consequently, this records the intended conjectural assertion, with the Selmer and analytic conventions still requiring confirmation. No restriction to odd $p$, splitting hypothesis on $p$ in $k$, distinction between split and nonsplit multiplicative reduction, or additional conductor hypothesis is supplied; none is imposed here.

Short English statement

For an elliptic curve over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with good ordinary or multiplicative reduction at $p$, the source asks whether its dual Selmer group over the combined cyclotomic and anticyclotomic extension of an imaginary quadratic field is torsion, with characteristic ideal generated by the intended two-variable $p$-adic $L$-function. The exact Selmer and $L$-function conventions remain unresolved. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
