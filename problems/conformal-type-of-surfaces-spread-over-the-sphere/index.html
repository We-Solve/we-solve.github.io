Conformal Type of Surfaces Spread over the Sphere

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,f)$ be a surface spread over the Riemann sphere, with associated conformal surface $X_f$. In a usual topological formulation, $X$ is a connected oriented surface and $f:X\to\widehat{{\mathbb{C}}}$ is a nonconstant open discrete orientation-preserving map, so the local branched-cover charts give the conformal structure in which $f$ is meromorphic. The exact allowed singularities and surface class are not supplied; no global surjectivity is assumed merely from "spread over."

The source asks how the topological and metric properties of $f$ determine both:

  1. the conformal type of $X_f$, stated as parabolic versus hyperbolic;
  2. the value-distribution properties of the meromorphic function $f$.
A standard metric associated to such a map measures path length by the spherical length of its image. The source does not explicitly choose this metric or specify which data about it, branching, or inverse singularities should serve as criteria.

For a simply connected noncompact Riemann surface, the usual uniformization dichotomy is conformal equivalence to ${\mathbb{C}}$ (parabolic) or to the disk (hyperbolic). The record does not state simple connectedness or noncompactness. For more general surfaces, terminology based on universal-cover type and terminology based on potential theory need not be interchangeable, and a compact elliptic case may require separate treatment. Thus no silent simple-connectedness assumption or chosen extension of the dichotomy is imposed.

The requested value-distribution properties are not specified by a particular growth function, deficiency, omitted-value bound, or asymptotic claim. The task remains to find criteria from the intended topological/metric data for the two kinds of analytic conclusions. The supplied assessment mentions strong results under finite or uniformly separated singularity hypotheses but gives no precise theorems; these hypotheses are contextual and are not imposed on the general question.

Short English statement

The question asks for criteria linking the geometry and topology of a meromorphic covering to its surface's conformal type and to its value distribution. The allowed surfaces, metric data, type convention, and desired value-distribution invariants are missing.
