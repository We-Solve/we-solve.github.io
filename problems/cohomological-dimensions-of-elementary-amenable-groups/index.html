Math-4778

Cohomological Dimensions of Elementary Amenable Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a group and let $kG$ denote its group ring over the coefficient ring $k$. Regard $k$ as the trivial $kG$-module via the augmentation. Write \[ \operatorname{cd}_k(G)=\operatorname{pd}_{kG}(k),\qquad \operatorname{hd}_k(G)=\operatorname{fd}_{kG}(k), \] where projective and flat dimensions may be infinite. For a finite subgroup $F\leq G$, invertibility of its order means $|F|1_k\in k^\times$.

An elementary amenable group belongs to the smallest class containing finite groups and abelian groups and closed under extensions and directed unions. Write $h(G)$ for its extended Hirsch length: this agrees with $\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}({\mathbb{Q}}\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}A)$ for an abelian group $A$, is additive under extensions, and is the supremum of the Hirsch lengths in a directed union. A group is locally finite if each of its finitely generated subgroups is finite. Write $\aleph_\omega=\sup_{n<\omega}\aleph_n$. A filtered colimit below is a colimit in the category of groups of a diagram indexed by a small filtered category; the transition homomorphisms are not assumed injective.

Coefficient qualification. The supplied original statement specifies a field $k$, whereas the supplied statement and clean statement specify a commutative ring $k$. The following records the proposed formulas with this discrepancy unresolved; the commutative-ring version must not be identified silently with the field version. The ring conventions concerning an identity and exclusion of the zero ring are not supplied.

First proposed equivalence. For every elementary amenable group $G$, is \[ \operatorname{cd}_k(G)<\infty \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left[ h(G)<\infty,\quad |G|<\aleph_\omega,\quad |F|1_k\in k^\times\text{ for every finite }F\leq G \right]? \]

Second proposed equivalence. For every group $G$, without the elementary amenability assumption, is \[ \operatorname{hd}_k(G)\leq 1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left[ G\cong\varinjlim_{i\in I}G_i \text{ for a small filtered diagram with } \operatorname{cd}_k(G_i)\leq 1\text{ for every }i \right]? \]

Third proposed equivalence. For an elementary amenable group $G$, let $\mathcal A_k(G)$ denote the supplied action condition: $G$ admits an action on a contractible complex of dimension $2$, with finite stabilizers whose orders are invertible in $k$. The category of complexes, regularity of the action, and meaning of stabilizer are not specified in the record.

Define the three listed structural conditions as follows:

  1. $C_1(G)$: there exist a subgroup $H\leq G$ with $[G:H]<\infty$ and an integer $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}$ such that \[ H\cong\langle x,y\mid y^{-1}xy=x^n\rangle. \]
  2. $C_2(G)$: there exists a countably infinite locally finite normal subgroup $T\trianglelefteq G$ such that $G/T\cong{\mathbb{Z}}$ or $G/T\cong D_\infty$, where $D_\infty={\mathbb{Z}}\rtimes({\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}})$ and the nonidentity element of ${\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$ acts on ${\mathbb{Z}}$ by inversion.
  3. $C_3(G)$: $G$ is locally finite and $|G|=\aleph_1$.
The clean statement asks whether \[ \operatorname{cd}_k(G)=2 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\mathcal A_k(G) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad C_1(G)\lor C_2(G)\lor C_3(G). \] The original statement instead says that exactly one of $C_1(G),C_2(G),C_3(G)$ holds. This exclusivity qualification is retained as an unresolved difference between the supplied formulations.

Further unresolved qualifications. No minimality condition on the dimension of the contractible complex is supplied, although the asserted cohomological dimension is exactly $2$. Also, the structural alternatives do not explicitly impose invertibility in $k$ of every finite-subgroup order, while that restriction occurs elsewhere in the record. It is unresolved whether a global restriction of this kind, or another omitted hypothesis, was intended for the third equivalence. These are proposed source formulations requiring review, not asserted theorems.

Short English statement

The record asks for three characterizations: finite cohomological dimension for elementary amenable groups, homological dimension at most one for arbitrary groups, and cohomological dimension exactly two for elementary amenable groups. The last characterization uses both a contractible complex action and three structural alternatives. The coefficient domain, exclusivity of those alternatives, and qualifications on the action and torsion remain unresolved. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
