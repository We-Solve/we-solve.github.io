Math-6734

Reduction of Torsion Endotrivial Modules to Trivial p-Prime Core

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the definitions below, let $G$ be a finite group, let $p$ be a prime dividing $|G|$, and let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p$. This supplies the usual modular setting for interpreting the question; the record does not specify the coefficient field, whether it must be algebraically closed, or explicitly state the divisibility assumption.

A finite-dimensional $kG$-module $M$ is endotrivial if \[ \operatorname{End}_k(M)\cong k\oplus P \] as $kG$-modules, where $k$ is the trivial module, $P$ is projective, and $G$ acts on endomorphisms by $(g\cdot f)(m)=g f(g^{-1}m)$. Two modules are stably isomorphic if they become isomorphic after adding finite-dimensional projective modules. The endotrivial-module group $T_k(G)$, written $T(G)$ in the source, consists of stable isomorphism classes of endotrivial modules, with multiplication induced by tensor product over $k$ with diagonal $G$-action, identity $[k]$, and inverse induced by the dual module. Its torsion subgroup is \[ TT_k(G)=T_T(G)=\left\{[M]\in T_k(G):\text{ there exists }n\geq 1\text{ with }[M^{\otimes_k n}]=[k]\right\}. \] Here $T_T(G)$ denotes the source's alternative notation for this subgroup, with dependence on $k$ suppressed. Define \[ O_{p'}(G)=\text{the largest normal subgroup of }G\text{ whose order is relatively prime to }p. \]

Source question. Can the determination of $TT(G)$ for finite groups be reduced essentially to its determination for groups $H$ satisfying $O_{p'}(H)=1$?

The word "essentially" is not defined in the supplied record. No reduction procedure, permitted auxiliary data, reconstruction statement, or comparison map is specified. Consequently, the question above preserves the supplied target but does not specify a fully quantified reduction theorem. In particular, the role of $G/O_{p'}(G)$, the coefficient-field hypotheses, and the intended scope of uniformity in $G$ and $p$ require clarification.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether understanding the finite-order classes of endotrivial modules can be reduced to studying finite groups with no nontrivial normal subgroup of order prime to $p$. The intended meaning of the reduction and the coefficient-field assumptions are unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
