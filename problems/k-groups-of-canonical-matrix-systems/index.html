Math-1916

K-groups of canonical matrix systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A subshift in the usual two-sided finite-alphabet setting is a closed shift-invariant subset $X\subseteq\mathcal A^{{\mathbb{Z}}}$, where $\mathcal A$ is finite and $(\sigma x)_j=x_{j+1}$. Write $(M_X,I_X)$ for its canonical matrix system in the intended source convention. The record does not define that construction or the associated groups $K_0(M_X,I_X)$ and $K_1(M_X,I_X)$.

The requested range classification is to characterize exactly the ordered pairs of abelian-group isomorphism classes \[ \mathcal R=\{([K_0(M_X,I_X)],[K_1(M_X,I_X)]): X\text{ is an admissible subshift}\}. \] Equivalently, give necessary and sufficient conditions on abelian groups $G_0,G_1$ for \[ \exists X\quad K_0(M_X,I_X)\cong G_0 \quad\text{and}\quad K_1(M_X,I_X)\cong G_1. \] The two groups must be realized by the same canonical system. The question concerns their underlying abelian groups; no order cone, distinguished unit, or additional invariant is included in the supplied statement. No restriction to finite-type shifts or finitely generated groups is stated. Realization by an arbitrary noncanonical matrix system would not alone answer the question.

The canonical-system and K-group definitions, and the precise admissible subshift class, must be supplied before this range problem becomes fully determined. In particular, a finite-matrix kernel/cokernel formula or a chosen associated operator algebra is not silently substituted for these source-specific groups. The supplied research summary reports the full range problem open through 2026 and relates it to dimension-group and operator-algebraic classification, without supplying a characterization.

Short English statement

Which pairs of abelian groups can be realized simultaneously as the two K-groups of one subshift's canonical matrix system? The canonical construction and admissible class must be fixed to specify the range.
