Axiomatic stable homotopy 9 — Suppose G is a self-equivalence of the stable homotopy category.

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathrm{SH}=\operatorname{Ho}(\mathrm{Sp})$ be the stable homotopy category of spectra, with suspension autoequivalence $\Sigma$ and sphere spectrum $S^0$. For $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, write $S^m=\Sigma^m S^0$, using inverse suspension when $m<0$. The principal question in the source is: \[ \text{For every category equivalence }G:\mathrm{SH}\longrightarrow\mathrm{SH}, \quad\text{does there exist }m\in{\mathbb{Z}}\text{ with }G\cong\Sigma^m\text{ naturally?} \] The word "self-equivalence" in the source carries no explicit requirement of exactness, suspension compatibility, enrichment, or symmetric monoidal compatibility. If any such structure is intended, it must be stated as an additional hypothesis rather than silently imposed. The source reports the following partial observation under the additional hypothesis $G\Sigma\cong\Sigma G$: there is an integer $m$ such that $G(S^0)\cong S^m$. This is an assertion about the image of the sphere, not by itself a natural identification on every spectrum.

The source also asks the analogous question for $\operatorname{Ho}(\mathrm{Sp}_{K(h)})$ and $\operatorname{Ho}(\mathrm{Sp}_{E(h)})$, the categories of local spectra for Morava $K$-theory $K(h)$ and Johnson--Wilson theory $E(h)$ at a fixed prime. Namely, must every self-equivalence of either category be naturally isomorphic to an integer power of its suspension? The prime, height range, and structural requirements are not specified in the record.

The later summary introduces grading and Picard twists and structure-preserving equivalences. Tensoring with an invertible object gives a different classification question from classifying strong symmetric monoidal self-equivalences, which must preserve the tensor unit. These distinctions cannot be suppressed when interpreting its claimed progress.

Short English statement

Must every self-equivalence of the stable homotopy category be an integer suspension, and do analogous statements hold after $K(h)$- or $E(h)$-localization? The original author reports identifying the image of the sphere when the functor commutes with suspension. The supplied summary reports positive partial progress under additional structural qualifications, but does not specify theorems that settle the original unrestricted question.
