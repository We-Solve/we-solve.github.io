Symmetry Structure of Mutually Unbiased Bases

Definitions and mathematical statement

For context, let $d,k\geq 1$ be integers. An orthonormal basis $B_a=(b_{a,1},\ldots,b_{a,d})$ of ${\mathbb{C}}^d$ satisfies $\langle b_{a,i},b_{a,j}\rangle=\delta_{ij}$. A family $(B_1,\ldots,B_k)$ is a family of mutually unbiased bases (MUBs) if \[ |\langle b_{a,i},b_{b,j}\rangle|^2=\frac{1}{d} \qquad (1\leq a,b\leq k,\ a\neq b,\ 1\leq i,j\leq d). \] These are contextual definitions; the supplied record does not specify values or intended quantifiers for $d$ and $k$, or a precise symmetry question about such families.

Short English statement

Mutually unbiased bases have squared overlap $1/d$ between vectors from different bases in ${\mathbb{C}}^d$. This record asks about their symmetry, but its supplied verification describes the question as an expository heading answered by a wreath-product action in the report.
