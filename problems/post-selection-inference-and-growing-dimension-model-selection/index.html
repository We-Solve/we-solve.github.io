Math-3350

Post-Selection Inference and Growing-Dimension Model Selection

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $D_n$ denote data of sample size $n$, let $\{\mathcal M_j\}$ be the intended candidate models, and let $\widehat j(D_n)$ be a model-selection rule. In the familiar maximum-likelihood convention, $\mathrm{AIC}_j=-2\ell_j(\widehat\theta_j)+2d_j$ and $\mathrm{BIC}_j=-2\ell_j(\widehat\theta_j)+d_j\log n$, where $d_j$ is model dimension. The record gives neither a sampling model nor a definition of FIC; a focused information criterion additionally needs the chosen focus and its risk setup. The following preserves all four requests without choosing those missing data.
  1. After selection by AIC or FIC, determine the actual coverage of a nominal $95$-percent interval. For a fixed target $\psi(\theta)$, a possible unconditional quantity is \[ \Pr_\theta\{\psi(\theta)\in I_{\widehat j(D_n)}(D_n)\}. \] This differs from coverage conditional on $\widehat j=j$, and from coverage for a model-dependent target $\psi_{\widehat j}(\theta)$. The source does not select one, and nominal $0.95$ is not assumed to equal actual coverage. The interval construction and how the selection data are reused are unspecified.
  2. Determine how to repair the associated over-optimism in confidence intervals and $p$-values. A calibration target might require interval coverage at least $1-\alpha$ or $\Pr_\theta\{p_{\mathrm{adj}}\leq t\}\leq t$ for $0\leq t\leq1$ under the relevant null. Conditional versus unconditional, pointwise versus uniform, and finite-sample versus asymptotic versions are different requirements; these are illustrative criteria, not a selected repair or a claim that every post-selection procedure has the same bias.
  3. Determine limit distributions in Bayesian model averaging. A conventional posterior for a common estimand is the mixture $\sum_j\Pr(\mathcal M_j\mid D_n)\Pi_j(\,\cdot\mid D_n)$. The source does not specify whether the requested limit concerns this random posterior, posterior model weights, a posterior mean or another model-averaged statistic. Priors, compatibility of estimands across models, centering and scaling, data-generating law and misspecification regime are absent.
  4. Develop the behavior of AIC/BIC theory when parameter dimension increases with sample size, say $d_n\to\infty$. The rate relative to $n$, number and structure of candidates, regularity and sparsity assumptions, and the performance target are missing. Prediction/risk properties and probability of selecting a designated true model are distinct targets; no dimension threshold or theorem is inferred from the criterion names alone.
The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports later asymptotic theory for model selection and averaging but says the broad list remains unresolved. This does not give an individual theorem status for every formulation above. The verification field reports retaining all four questions byte-for-byte; the partially-solved label is supplied metadata, not an independently checked claim about any chosen statistical model.

Short English statement

The source asks for actual post-selection coverage, repairs to confidence intervals and $p$-values, Bayesian model-averaging limits, and AIC/BIC theory with growing dimension. A precise answer in each case needs the data model, target, construction and asymptotic regime. In particular, conditional and unconditional guarantees must be distinguished.
