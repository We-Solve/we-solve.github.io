Boundary Conditions Preserving Optimal Hardy–Sobolev Constants

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\Omega$ for the domain and $W^{m,p}(\Omega)$ for the Sobolev space appearing in the supplied record. In its usual integer-order interpretation, this is the space of functions whose weak derivatives through order $m$ belong to $L^p(\Omega)$. The record does not specify the admissible values of $m,p,n$, the hypotheses on $\Omega$, or the precise Hardy--Sobolev inequalities under consideration.

For a boundary condition $B$, write formally \[ X_B=\{u\in W^{m,p}(\Omega):u\text{ satisfies }B\}. \] This notation requires an appropriate interpretation of boundary traces, which is not supplied. Let $H_B(p,m,n;\Omega)$ and $S_B(p,m,n;\Omega)$ denote placeholders for the optimal constants in the intended inequalities on $X_B$. These symbols are not definitions of particular variational quotients: the inequalities, weights, exponents, differential operators, normalizations, and optimization conventions are absent from the record.

Source-level classification problem. Determine which boundary conditions $B$, other than the Dirichlet and Navier conditions referred to in the source, preserve the reference optimal constants denoted there by $H(p,m,n)$ and $S(p,m,n)$; formally, the requested preservation is \[ H_B(p,m,n;\Omega)=H(p,m,n),\qquad S_B(p,m,n;\Omega)=S(p,m,n). \] These equalities express the supplied target only after the intended inequalities and reference constants have been specified. The record does not define the higher-order Dirichlet or Navier conditions, establish whether their reference constants coincide in every intended setting, or specify whether preservation is required simultaneously for both constants. It also leaves unspecified the class of competing boundary conditions and whether the assertion is to hold for a fixed domain and parameter tuple or uniformly over an admissible family. Thus a fully quantified mathematical classification problem cannot be recovered from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks which boundary conditions besides Dirichlet and Navier preserve the optimal constants in the intended Hardy--Sobolev embeddings. The supplied record omits the defining inequalities, parameter ranges, domain hypotheses, and boundary trace conventions, so the displayed preservation target remains conditional on those specifications.
