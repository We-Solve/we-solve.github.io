Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finitely generated one-relator group is a group with a presentation \[ G=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_m\mid r=1\rangle =F_m/\langle\!\langle r\rangle\!\rangle, \] where $m\ge1$, $F_m$ is the free group on the displayed generators, and $\langle\!\langle r\rangle\!\rangle$ is the smallest normal subgroup containing the word $r$. The relator may be a proper power; no torsion-free assumption is made. A redundant trivial relator causes no change to the question.

A discrete group has the Haagerup property if it admits a metrically proper action by affine isometries on a real Hilbert space $\mathcal H$, possibly of infinite dimension. In detail, there are orthogonal operators $\pi(g)$ and vectors $b(g)$ with \[ \pi(gh)=\pi(g)\pi(h),\qquad b(gh)=b(g)+\pi(g)b(h), \] so that $g\cdot v=\pi(g)v+b(g)$, and for every $R\ge0$ the set $\{g\in G:\|b(g)\|\le R\}$ is finite. This is properness of the orbit map of the origin; choosing another basepoint gives the same condition.

Does every one-relator group have this property? The Hilbert space and action may depend on the group, and no uniform dimension or bound on displacement is requested. Properness controls group elements, not merely the set of orbit points. The affine formulation makes explicit the source's isometric-action convention; group actions by bijective Hilbert space isometries are affine in the real setting.

Short English statement

Does every group with a finite presentation containing one relator admit a proper affine isometric action on a Hilbert space?

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For a finite set $X$ and relator $r\in F(X)$, suppose \[ G=F(X)/\langle\!\langle r\rangle\!\rangle \] is word-hyperbolic. The claim supplies a finite-index subgroup $H\le G$, a finite nonpositively curved cube complex $C$ with $\pi_1C\cong H$, and a cubical local isometry from $C$ to a right-angled Artin Salvetti complex. This is virtual compact specialness. The second theorem applies to any injective endomorphism $\phi$ of a finitely generated free group $F$: \[ F*_{\phi}=\langle F,t\mid t^{-1}ft=\phi(f),\ f\in F\rangle \text{ word-hyperbolic}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad F*_{\phi}\text{ virtually compact special}. \] Word-hyperbolicity is assumed in both assertions.

Outline of the argument

Primitive-extension reduction and a filling criterion organize progressive characters and walls. The resulting cubulation and hierarchy meet the virtual-specialness criterion; the free-by-cyclic consequence is then imported from the established special-group result.
