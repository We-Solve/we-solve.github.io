Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.110

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime. A finite group is $p$-soluble if it has a normal series whose factors are either $p$-groups or groups of order coprime to $p$. A group variety $\mathfrak V$ is a class of groups defined by group identities; it is proper if it is not the variety of all groups.

For a finite group $G$, consider normal series \[ 1=G_0\trianglelefteq G_1\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq G_m=G, \qquad G_i\trianglelefteq G, \] whose nontrivial factors are either $p$-soluble or finite direct products of nonabelian simple groups of order divisible by $p$. The supplied source defines the non-$p$-soluble length $\lambda_p(G)$ as the number of non-$p$-soluble factors in a shortest such series. We retain that wording: the supplied record does not explicitly distinguish minimizing the total number of factors from minimizing the number of non-$p$-soluble factors, and this convention requires confirmation against the original source.

Problem (Khukhro--Shumyatsky, Kourovka Notebook 18.110). Is it true that for every prime $p$ and every proper group variety $\mathfrak V$, there exists a nonnegative integer $B=B(p,\mathfrak V)$ such that every finite group $G$ satisfies \[ \left(P\in\mathfrak V\text{ for a Sylow }p\text{-subgroup }P\text{ of }G\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lambda_p(G)\le B? \] Membership in $\mathfrak V$ for one Sylow $p$-subgroup is equivalent to membership for every Sylow $p$-subgroup, since they are conjugate. The bound may depend on $p$ and $\mathfrak V$, but not on $G$.

Supplied qualification. The record labels the problem open, but its Comment of 2021 reports that the existence of such a bound for $p=2$ was proved by F. Fumagalli, F. Leinen, and O. Puglisi, citing a publication dated 2022. This reported special case and the overall status label are supplied source information, not independently verified literature assessments.

Short English statement

For each fixed prime $p$ and proper variety $\mathfrak V$, must finite groups whose Sylow $p$-subgroups lie in $\mathfrak V$ have uniformly bounded non-$p$-soluble length? The source reports an affirmative result for $p=2$. Its wording about a shortest normal series is retained with the convention issue stated explicitly.
