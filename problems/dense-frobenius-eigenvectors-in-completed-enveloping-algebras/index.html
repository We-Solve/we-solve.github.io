Dense Frobenius Eigenvectors in Completed Enveloping Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a finite field, let $\overline{k}$ be an algebraic closure, let $X$ be a variety over $k$, and let $\overline{x}$ be a geometric base point of $X_{\overline{k}}$. For a prime $\ell$, put \[ G_\ell=\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute et},\ell}(X_{\overline{k}},\overline{x}), \] where the superscript denotes the maximal pro-$\ell$ quotient of the geometric étale fundamental group. Write \[ \Lambda_\ell=\mathbb Z_\ell[[G_\ell]] =\varprojlim_{m,U}(\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z)[G_\ell/U], \qquad I=\ker\left(\Lambda_\ell\xrightarrow{\mathrm{aug}}\mathbb Z_\ell\right), \] where $m\geq 1$, $U$ ranges over open normal subgroups of $G_\ell$, and augmentation sends every group element to $1$. Assume that every augmentation quotient $I^n/I^{n+1}$ is torsion-free as a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module, for $n\geq 0$.

The supplied question asks whether there exists a real number $r>0$ such that the convergent group algebra denoted in the source record by \[ B_r=\mathbb Q_\ell[[G_\ell]]^{\leq\ell^{-r}} \] admits a set $E$ of Frobenius eigenvectors whose linear span is dense in $B_r$. With the intended scalar field and topology understood, the requested conclusion is \[ \overline{\operatorname{span}(E)}^{\,B_r}=B_r. \] The question is posed for arbitrary $X$, $k$, and prime $\ell$ subject to the stated augmentation hypothesis; no uniform choice of $r$ is requested.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define the convergence condition represented by ${}^{\leq\ell^{-r}}$, its topology, or the precise rational completed algebra used to construct $B_r$. Accordingly, $B_r$ and the displayed closure retain their source notation rather than specifying a complete analytic construction. The record also does not specify a Frobenius operator on this based algebra, the convention for Frobenius, or whether eigenvectors are taken over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ or after scalar extension. A Frobenius eigenvector would mean a nonzero vector $v$ satisfying $F(v)=\lambda v$ for the intended operator $F$ and an eigenvalue in the intended field; these choices remain unresolved. No restriction $\ell\neq\operatorname{char}(k)$, geometric connectedness assumption, or base-point compatibility with Frobenius is supplied, so none is imposed here. The original extraction refers to a completed enveloping algebra, whereas the corrected statement specifies the convergent group algebra above; their intended relationship is not supplied. This is therefore a statement-only reconstruction with unresolved formulation details.

Short English statement

For a variety over a finite field and a prime $\ell$, assume that all augmentation quotients of the completed integral group algebra of its geometric pro-$\ell$ fundamental group are torsion-free. The question asks whether some positive convergence parameter gives a rational convergent group algebra with a dense linear span of Frobenius eigenvectors. The convergence topology, Frobenius action, and eigenvalue field require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified here.
