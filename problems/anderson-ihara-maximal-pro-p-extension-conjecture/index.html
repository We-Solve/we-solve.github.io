Anderson-Ihara Maximal Pro-p Extension Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. For a prime $p$, let $\mu_p\subset\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}^{\times}$ be the group of $p$-th roots of unity, and put $K_p={\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_p)$. Define \[ M_p=\bigcup_{L}L, \] where the union ranges over finite Galois extensions $L/K_p$ inside $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ whose Galois groups have $p$-power order and which are unramified at every finite prime of $K_p$ not lying above $p$. Thus $M_p$ is the maximal pro-$p$ extension with this finite-prime ramification restriction. The source does not specify its convention concerning archimedean ramification, which may matter when $p=2$, or the intended range of primes.

Historical question, incompletely specified in the supplied record. With $\Omega^{*}$ denoting the field intended by the source, is \[ \Omega^{*}=M_p? \] The supplied excerpt does not define $\Omega^{*}$, its ambient field, or its dependence on $p$. Consequently this display records the historical target but is not a complete standalone conjecture. In particular, no definition of $\Omega^{*}$ as a fixed field of a representation kernel can be inferred from the excerpt alone. The base field in the question is explicitly ${\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_p)$.

Associated structure reported by the source. Write $G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}/{\mathbb{Q}})$. The excerpt refers to an outer representation $\rho_{0,3}:G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\to\operatorname{Out}(\Pi_{0,3})$ and a descending central series $\Pi_{0,3}(m)$, and gives \[ F^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\ker\!\left(G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\xrightarrow{\rho_{0,3}}\operatorname{Out}(\Pi_{0,3})\longrightarrow\operatorname{Out}\left(\Pi_{0,3}/\Pi_{0,3}(m+1)\right)\right),\qquad m\geq1, \] \[ \operatorname{gr}^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=F^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}/F^{m+1}G_{{\mathbb{Q}}},\qquad \mathfrak g=\bigoplus_{m\geq1}\operatorname{gr}^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}. \] Here $\operatorname{Out}$ denotes the automorphism group modulo inner automorphisms. The excerpt does not supply the construction of $\Pi_{0,3}$, the representation, or their conventions. It reports that each graded piece is isomorphic to a finite direct sum of copies of the Tate twist ${\mathbb{Z}}_p(m)$, and that commutators induce a graded Lie algebra structure on $\mathfrak g$. Here ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ denotes the ring of $p$-adic integers, and ${\mathbb{Z}}_p(m)$ its $m$-th cyclotomic Tate twist.

Put $\mu_{p^{\infty}}=\bigcup_{n\geq1}\mu_{p^n}$ and $G_{{\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}/{\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_{p^{\infty}}))$. For each odd integer $m\geq3$, the excerpt reports a Soulé character \[ \kappa_m:G_{{\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_{p^{\infty}})}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}_p(m) \] constructed as a Kummer character from cyclotomic $p$-units. It states that its restriction to $F^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ is nontrivial and factors through $\operatorname{gr}^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, and chooses $\sigma_m\in\operatorname{gr}^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ such that $\kappa_m(\sigma_m)$ generates $\kappa_m(F^mG_{{\mathbb{Q}}})$; the excerpt calls this image an $m$-th Soulé element. These are reported background assertions, not independently verified statements in this draft.

Unresolved source qualifications. The excerpt says that the historical question has an affirmative answer under the Deligne--Ihara conjecture, described there as now a theorem of Hain--Matsumoto and Brown concerning an associated graded Lie algebra over ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$. The supplied verification field repeats this implication. In contrast, the supplied dated literature assessment labels the problem partially solved and says that the general equality remains open, while citing Sharifi's theorem for odd irregular primes and the Deligne--Ihara implication in the relevant setting. The missing hypotheses and definition of $\Omega^{*}$ prevent reconciliation of these statements. The excerpt also mentions a Deligne--Ihara analogue and an analogue of Sharifi's result for once-punctured CM elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields when $p$ is ordinary; it gives no precise formulation of these analogues.

Short English statement

The historical Anderson--Ihara question asks whether the source's field $\Omega^{*}$ equals the maximal pro-$p$ extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}(\mu_p)$ unramified outside $p$. The supplied record omits the definition of $\Omega^{*}$ and the admissible primes, and contains unresolved status qualifications. This is an incomplete statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
