Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 25 (Freedman)

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p\colon S^7\to S^4$ be a given "generalized" Hopf fibration with fibre $S^3$, as in the supplied source. The source does not define "generalized", specify the manifold category, or identify the structure group of this fibration; these qualifications remain unresolved.

Under the usual integral-homology interpretation, a homology $4$-sphere is a closed, connected, oriented $4$-manifold $H$ satisfying \[ H_i(H;{\mathbb{Z}})\cong H_i(S^4;{\mathbb{Z}})\qquad\text{for every }i\geq 0. \] For chosen orientations, a continuous map $f\colon H\to S^4$ has degree one if \[ f_*[H]=[S^4]\quad\text{in }H_4(S^4;{\mathbb{Z}}). \] The integral coefficients and manifold conventions in this interpretation are not explicitly specified in the source; any additional regularity requirement on $H$ or $f$ must also be clarified.

The pullback of $p$ along $f$ is the projection \[ f^*S^7=\{(h,x)\in H\times S^7\mid f(h)=p(x)\}\longrightarrow H, \qquad (h,x)\longmapsto h. \] In the usual bundle-theoretic sense, this $S^3$-bundle is flat if it admits local trivializations whose transition functions are locally constant maps into the intended group of automorphisms of $S^3$. The source does not specify that group or whether these automorphisms must be homeomorphisms, diffeomorphisms, or satisfy additional conditions.

Question (Freedman, as attributed in the supplied record). For every given generalized Hopf fibration \[ S^3\longrightarrow S^7\xrightarrow{\,p\,}S^4, \] does there exist a homology $4$-sphere $H$ and a degree-one map $f\colon H\to S^4$ such that $f^*S^7\to H$ is a flat $S^3$-bundle? This is a conditional formulation: a fully specified mathematical statement requires clarification of "generalized", the category and structure group, and the homology convention.

Short English statement

Given a generalized Hopf fibration $S^3\to S^7\to S^4$, can one find a homology $4$-sphere mapping to $S^4$ with degree one so that the pulled-back $S^3$-bundle is flat? The source leaves the meaning of "generalized" and the relevant category and structure group unspecified. Integral homology is used above as an explicitly qualified conventional interpretation.
