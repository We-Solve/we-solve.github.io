Scalar Curvature Question [?28]: Besides, similar results are expected for allsingular Alexandrov spaces with lower curvature boundsbut the Alm

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An Alexandrov space with curvature bounded below satisfies the corresponding lower triangle-comparison condition. A conical singularity is locally modeled on, or asymptotic to, a metric cone over a link; the source does not distinguish these versions or specify the links. Develop the regularity theory needed for minimal/area-minimizing subvarieties in such singular ambient spaces, including conical singularities beyond isolated singular points, so as to extend the "similar results" referred to in the source. Those preceding results are not included in the record.

The extract then gives a separate smooth comparison statement: for an $m$-dimensional minimal subvariety $Y_{\min}^m\subset X$, a point $x\in Y_{\min}^m$, injectivity radius at least $R$, and an upper sectional-curvature bound $\kappa_0$ on $B_x(R)$, it reports \[ \operatorname{Vol}_m(Y_{\min}^m\cap B_x(R)) \geq\operatorname{Vol}_m(B^m(R,\kappa_0)), \] where $B^m(R,\kappa_0)$ is the radius-$R$ ball in the constant-curvature model. This is supplied background, not an asserted Alexandrov-lower-curvature theorem. Boundary, multiplicity, and admissible-radius conditions for its monotonicity argument are not provided.

Finally, the text discusses expected waist values for constant-curvature balls using $O(n)$-equivariant maps $B^n(R,\kappa_0)\to S^n$, but omits the relevant waist bounds and ends at an incomplete "section 3 in [?" reference. Neither those expected values nor a precise extension theorem can be reconstructed. A complete target must identify the minimizer category, regularity conclusion, singularity hypotheses, and the preceding results to be extended.

Short English statement

Develop regularity and comparison theory for minimal objects in singular Alexandrov spaces, particularly with conical singularities. The extract mixes this request with smooth upper-curvature comparison and a truncated waist discussion, whose missing hypotheses and values must remain explicit.
