Math-15078

Strong Surjectivity of Countryman Derived Orders Under PFA

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a linear order $L=(X,\leq_L)$, use the standard convention that $L$ is strongly surjective if, for every nonempty subset $A\subseteq X$ equipped with the induced order, there exists a surjection $f:X\to A$ such that \[ x\leq_L y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f(x)\leq_L f(y) \qquad(x,y\in X). \] The restriction to nonempty suborders is explicit here; the supplied record does not state its convention about empty orders.

In the usual terminology, a Countryman line is a linear order $C$ of cardinality $\aleph_1$ whose square $C\times C$, ordered coordinatewise, is a union of countably many chains. Thus there are chains $K_n\subseteq C\times C$, for $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$, with \[ C\times C=\bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}K_n, \qquad (c_0,c_1)\leq(d_0,d_1) \iff c_0\leq_C d_0\ \text{and}\ c_1\leq_C d_1. \] The Proper Forcing Axiom (PFA) asserts that, for every proper forcing partial order $\mathbb P$ and every family $(D_\xi)_{\xi<\omega_1}$ of dense subsets of $\mathbb P$, there is a filter on $\mathbb P$ meeting every $D_\xi$.

The supplied question asks whether, assuming PFA, the order $\eta_C$ constructed from a Countryman line $C$ is strongly surjective. Writing $X_C$ for its underlying set, the intended conclusion has the form \[ \forall A\subseteq X_C\; \left(A\ne\varnothing\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists f:X_C\twoheadrightarrow A\; \forall x,y\in X_C\, \left(x\leq_{\eta_C}y\Longrightarrow f(x)\leq_{\eta_C}f(y)\right)\right). \] This is a schematic formulation only: the supplied record does not define the construction of $\eta_C$, specify any additional requirements on $C$, or determine whether the assertion concerns every Countryman line, a selected line, or a particular construction up to isomorphism. Consequently, it does not determine a complete quantified assertion about a defined order. These omissions require source consultation and human mathematical review.

Short English statement

Assuming the Proper Forcing Axiom, must every nonempty suborder of the intended Countryman-derived order $\eta_C$ be the image of an order-preserving surjection from $\eta_C$? The supplied record omits the construction of $\eta_C$ and the scope of the choice of $C$, so the target remains incompletely specified.
