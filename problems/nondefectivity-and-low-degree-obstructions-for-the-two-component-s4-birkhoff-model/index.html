Research attempt

Literature and scope

Problem 58 concerns mixtures of the $S_4$ first-order ranking model, without fixing the number of components. I tested the simplest proposed shortcut: reuse the equations of one unmixed component. A positive two-component mixture already violates one of its quadratic binomials. This is a standard secant-versus-toric distinction, not a computation of the full secant ideal.
Proposition: Let $p_\sigma=Z^{-1}\prod_{i=1}^4\theta_{i,\sigma(i)}$ for $\sigma\in S_4$ and positive $\theta$. With $e$ the identity, $\pi=(12)$ and $\tau=(34)$, every such distribution satisfies $p_ep_{\pi\tau}=p_\pi p_\tau$. A strictly positive mixture of two such distributions can violate it.
Proof: The multisets of matrix entries used by the products $w_ew_{\pi\tau}$ and $w_\pi w_\tau$, where $w_\sigma=\prod_i\theta_{i,\sigma(i)}$, are identical: the two swaps concern disjoint row and column pairs. Their monomials agree; dividing by $Z^2$ proves the equality.

Put every $\theta$ entry equal to one except $\theta_{12}=a$ and $\theta_{34}=b$. The selected four unnormalized weights are $(1,a,b,ab)$. There are 24 permutations, six using each specified entry and two using both, so \[ Z(a,b)=24+6(a-1)+6(b-1)+2(a-1)(b-1). \] Use $(a,b)=(1,1)$ and $(2,2)$, whose normalizers are 24 and 38. Mix their normalized distributions with weights $24/62$ and $38/62$. The selected probabilities become $(2,3,3,5)/62$, so \[ p_ep_{\pi\tau}-p_\pi p_\tau=\frac{2\cdot5-3\cdot3}{62^2}=\frac1{3844}\ne0. \] All original weights and both mixture weights are positive; all 24 mixture probabilities are therefore positive. ∎

Verification and remaining gaps

Enumeration of all 24 permutations checked both normalizers and the displayed mixture minor exactly. The failed step is assuming a polynomial identity survives averaging points, which is valid for linear identities but not for this quadratic. The example does not determine any complete two-component secant ideal, its scheme structure, or the likelihood's multimodality. The mixture size remains an original-scope qualification, and no new target progress is claimed.
