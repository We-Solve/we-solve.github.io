Problem 8.8 — Find a geometric construction of a TQFT using H 0(MΣ,L⊗k).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write \[ V_k(\Sigma)=H^0\!\left(\mathcal M_\Sigma,L^{\otimes k}\right) \] for the space in the source. In the holomorphic interpretation, $H^0$ is the vector space of global holomorphic sections of the line bundle $L^{\otimes k}$ on $\mathcal M_\Sigma$. The record does not define the space $\mathcal M_\Sigma$, the line bundle $L$, their dependence on $\Sigma$, or the allowed levels $k$; this interpretation is conditional on that omitted geometric setup.

Problem. Find a geometric construction of a topological quantum field theory with state spaces $V_k(\Sigma)$. In particular, give a geometric rule assigning to a $3$-manifold $M$ with boundary identified with $\Sigma$ a vector \[ Z_k(M)\in H^0\!\left(\mathcal M_\Sigma,L^{\otimes k}\right), \qquad \partial M=\Sigma, \] as part of a TQFT assignment compatible with the relevant equivalences, disjoint union, and gluing of bordisms. The problem asks for a geometric construction, not merely for the choice of some vector in each state space. Any additional boundary, orientation, complex-structure, framing, or anomaly data needed to define these spaces and maps must be specified by a proposed construction; they are not fixed by this excerpt.

Short English statement

Construct the TQFT geometrically by assigning a section of $L^{\otimes k}$ over $\mathcal M_\Sigma$ to a $3$-manifold bounded by $\Sigma$, in a way compatible with the TQFT gluing rules. The underlying moduli space, line bundle, and level conventions are missing from the supplied excerpt.
