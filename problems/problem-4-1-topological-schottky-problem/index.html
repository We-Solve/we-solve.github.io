Problem 4.1 — ( Topological Schottky Problem).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Problem 4.1, "Topological Schottky Problem," asks to determine the homotopy type of the source's space $\mathcal J_g^c$, and use it to compute its ordinary and compactly supported cohomology, \[ H^\bullet(\mathcal J_g^c),\qquad H_c^\bullet(\mathcal J_g^c). \] It additionally asks to compute its intersection homology. The source gives no definitions of $\mathcal J_g^c$, its superscript $c$, coefficient system, or intersection-homology perversity/support convention. In particular the superscript must not be silently taken to mean a chosen closure or compactification.

The source identifies $g=4$ as the first interesting case and states that $\mathcal J_4^c$ is a singular divisor in $\mathfrak h_4$. Conventionally $\mathfrak h_g$ is Siegel upper half-space, \[ \mathfrak h_g=\{Z\in M_g({\mathbb{C}}):Z^T=Z,\ \operatorname{Im}Z>0\}, \] but the precise Jacobian-locus model, marking and topology require source confirmation.

The following context says that knowledge of $\mathcal J_g$, $\mathcal J_g^c$ or $\mathcal Q_g^c$ should help compute the $\sigma$-invariant homology \[ H_\bullet(\mathcal T_g)^{\langle\sigma\rangle}. \] The spaces $\mathcal Q_g^c$ and $\mathcal T_g$, and the involution $\sigma$, are not defined in the extract. It must not be assumed that $\mathcal T_g$ is contractible Teichmüller space simply from the letter used. For a coefficient ring $R$ in which $2$ is invertible, the source gives the eigenspace decomposition \[ H_\bullet(\mathcal T_g;R) =H_\bullet(\mathcal T_g;R)^+\oplus H_\bullet(\mathcal T_g;R)^-, \] where $\sigma$ acts by $+1$ and $-1$, respectively. This decomposition uses the projections $(1\pm\sigma)/2$ and is not asserted in characteristic $2$ or over arbitrary integral coefficients. The proposed connection is a motivation, not an already specified isomorphism from Jacobian-locus cohomology to these eigenspaces.

Short English statement

Determine the topology, ordinary and compactly supported cohomology, and intersection homology of the specified Jacobian-type locus, especially its singular genus-four case. The source also seeks consequences for invariant homology of another space under an involution. The relevant spaces and comparison maps are not defined in this extract.
