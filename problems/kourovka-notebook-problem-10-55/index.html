Kourovka Notebook Problem 10.55

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime number $p$. An abelian $p$-group is an abelian group $G$ such that every element is annihilated by some power of $p$. No cardinality bound on $G$ is specified. Put $R=\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(G)$, with multiplication given by composition, and regard $G$ as a left $R$-module by evaluation: $r\cdot g=r(g)$.

For a unital ring $R$, a left $R$-module $M$ is called slender if every $R$-linear map \[ f:\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}R\longrightarrow M \] satisfies $f(e_n)=0$ for all but finitely many $n$, where $e_n$ has $1_R$ in coordinate $n$ and zero elsewhere. Thus the quantifiers are \[ \forall f\in\operatorname{Hom}_R\!\left(\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}R,M\right)\quad \exists F\subseteq\{1,2,\ldots\}\text{ finite}\quad \forall n\notin F,\quad f(e_n)=0. \]

Problem. Characterize all abelian $p$-groups $G$ that are slender as modules over $\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(G)$ under the evaluation action. The characterization is sought for each prime $p$.

The supplied source states that the standard group \[ B=\bigoplus_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z \] is slender over its own endomorphism ring, attributing this fact to A. Mader. This is retained as supplied background, without independent verification. The source does not explicitly define module slenderness; the definition above states the convention used in this draft. The record also contains differing attribution signals: its statement begins with A. Mader, whereas its proposer metadata names S. V. Rychkov.

Short English statement

For each prime $p$, characterize the abelian $p$-groups that are slender under the natural action of their endomorphism rings, using the explicit definition above. The source gives $\bigoplus_{n\geq1}\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z$ as an example. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that label has not been independently verified.
