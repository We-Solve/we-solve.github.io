Triangle-Cone Characterization of Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For this draft, use the convention that $G=(V,E)$ is a finite simple undirected graph. The supplied statement does not explicitly specify these graph conventions, so their intended scope requires confirmation. Let \[ \mathcal T(G)=\{T\subseteq V: |T|=3,\ \tbinom{T}{2}\subseteq E\} \] be its set of triangles. For $T\in\mathcal T(G)$, define its edge incidence vector $\chi^T\in{\mathbb{R}}^E$ by \[ \chi^T_e=\begin{cases}1,&e\in\binom{T}{2},\\0,&e\notin\binom{T}{2}.\end{cases} \] The triangle cone is \[ C_\triangle(G)=\left\{\sum_{T\in\mathcal T(G)}\lambda_T\chi^T:\lambda_T\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}\text{ for every }T\in\mathcal T(G)\right\}. \] Write $\mathbf1_E\in{\mathbb{R}}^E$ for the vector whose every coordinate equals $1$.

Graph characterization problem. Characterize the graphs $G$ for which $\mathbf1_E\in C_\triangle(G)$. Explicitly, characterize when there exist nonnegative real triangle weights satisfying \[ \exists (\lambda_T)_{T\in\mathcal T(G)}\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}^{\mathcal T(G)}\quad\forall e\in E,\qquad \sum_{\substack{T\in\mathcal T(G)\\e\in\binom{T}{2}}}\lambda_T=1. \] Such weights are called a fractional triangle decomposition. Empty sums are zero; thus the displayed condition is vacuous when $E=\varnothing$, whereas an edge belonging to no triangle cannot satisfy its equation.

Related cone characterization problem. For each $G$, characterize linear inequalities defining $C_\triangle(G)$: give a graph-dependent description of a family $\mathcal A(G)\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^E$ such that \[ C_\triangle(G)=\left\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^E:\sum_{e\in E}a_ex_e\geq0\text{ for every }a\in\mathcal A(G)\right\}. \] The source calls these two characterization tasks equivalent. A description of the entire cone provides a criterion for membership of $\mathbf1_E$, but a characterization restricted to that vector does not, by itself, specify the entire cone. The intended sense of equivalence, and the desired form of the characterization, remain unresolved. The supplied record describes a broad research program, not a single proposed necessary-and-sufficient theorem.

Short English statement

Characterize graphs whose triangles can be assigned nonnegative real weights so that every edge receives total weight exactly $1$. The source also asks for defining inequalities for the full cone of triangle incidence vectors. The graph conventions and the asserted equivalence between these targets require review. The supplied open label is a dated curation assessment.
