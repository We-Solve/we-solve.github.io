Acting Groups and Identity-Component Automorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied formulation presupposes a base field $k$, but does not specify that field, its characteristic, or any algebraic-closure or perfectness hypothesis. Relative to the intended base, let $G$ be a simple adjoint algebraic group and let $P\subseteq G$ be a nonreduced parabolic subgroup scheme in the sense intended by the source. Here nonreduced means that the structure sheaf has nonzero nilpotent sections. The precise convention for a nonreduced parabolic subgroup scheme, and whether simplicity is meant over $k$ or geometrically, remain unspecified. Write \[ X=G/P \] for the intended homogeneous quotient, with its left $G$-action.

When the automorphism functor is represented by a group scheme $\operatorname{Aut}_{X/k}$, its value on a $k$-scheme $T$ is the group of $T$-automorphisms of $X\times_k T$. Let $\operatorname{Aut}_{X/k}^{0}$ denote its identity component, retaining its scheme structure. The supplied statement describes the homomorphism induced by the left action as a natural inclusion \[ \iota:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{X/k}^{0}. \] The missing setup must justify representability, factorization through the identity component, and the asserted inclusion; faithfulness as a group-scheme action cannot be inferred here merely from the word adjoint.

Problem, subject to the unresolved setup. For the intended class of pairs $(G,P)$, determine necessary and sufficient conditions for $\iota$ to be an isomorphism of group schemes. In the cases where the asserted inclusion is strict, determine the structure of $\operatorname{Aut}_{X/k}^{0}$. This second request occurs in the supplied cleaned statement but is not explicit in the supplied original fragment.

The original fragment also asserts that any parabolic subgroup is an intersection of parabolics with maximal reduced part. Its ambient hypotheses and the meaning of maximal reduced part are not supplied; this assertion is retained as unresolved contextual information and is not used as a theorem or an additional hypothesis.

Short English statement

For the intended homogeneous spaces $X=G/P$, with $G$ simple adjoint and $P$ a nonreduced parabolic subgroup scheme, characterize when the acting group equals the identity component of the automorphism group scheme, and describe that component when the supplied inclusion is strict. The base field, subgroup-scheme conventions, and faithfulness hypotheses require clarification before this becomes a fully specified problem.
