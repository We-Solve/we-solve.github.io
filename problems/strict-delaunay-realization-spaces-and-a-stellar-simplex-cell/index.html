Math-1444

Strict Delaunay realization spaces and a stellar simplex cell

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Theran asks for the configuration space of a fixed combinatorial type of Delaunay triangulation, noting "$d=2$ it is a ball" and asking whether $d=3$ is universal. A Delaunay simplex has an empty open circumball; the source's planar wording uses circumcircles. The meaning of universality, treatment of cospherical degeneracies, labels and quotient symmetries are not specified.

One precise strict model, used by the supplied aid, is as follows. Let $T$ be a labelled triangulation on $n\geq d+1$ vertices, and let $\mathcal C_d^{\mathrm{str}}(T)$ consist of ordered, pairwise distinct, affinely spanning configurations $p=(p_1,\ldots,p_n)\in({\mathbb{R}}^d)^n$ for which the straight simplices of $T$ triangulate $\operatorname{conv}\{p_i\}$, every maximal simplex is nondegenerate, and every vertex not in a maximal simplex lies strictly outside its circumsphere. Put \[ \mathcal R_d^{\mathrm{str}}(T)=\mathcal C_d^{\mathrm{str}}(T)/\operatorname{Sim}(d), \] where $\operatorname{Sim}(d)$ includes translations, positive uniform scalings and all orthogonal transformations, including reflections; labels are retained. Strict inequalities differ from the original empty-open-ball condition, which permits extra cospherical vertices.

For this strict convention the aid reports that every nonempty $\mathcal R_d^{\mathrm{str}}(T)$ is a smooth semialgebraic manifold without boundary of dimension \[ dn-\frac{d(d+1)}2-1. \] It also gives an explicit family. Let $T_\star$ be the stellar triangulation of a labelled $d$-simplex by one labelled interior point. The aid reports a semialgebraic homeomorphism \[ \mathcal R_d^{\mathrm{str}}(T_\star)\cong \{G\in\operatorname{Sym}_d({\mathbb{R}}):G>0,\ \operatorname{tr}G=1\} \times\operatorname{int}\Delta_d, \] where $G$ is the Gram matrix of the $d$ edge vectors from one labelled outer vertex after scale normalization, and $\operatorname{int}\Delta_d=\{(a_0,\ldots,a_d):a_i>0,\sum_i a_i=1\}$ records the interior point's barycentric coordinates. This product is an open ball of dimension $d(d+3)/2-1$; for $d=3$, it is an open $8$-ball.

The central question remains whether arbitrary realization-space complexity can occur in dimension three. A homotopy-type version would ask whether every nonempty primary basic semialgebraic set, defined by finitely many polynomial equations and strict inequalities with integer coefficients, has the homotopy type of some such realization space. Stable equivalence is a different, stronger formulation and is not identified with this version. The source supplies no exact universality convention.

The aid attributes a growing-dimension universality result to Adiprasito--Padrol--Theran and says they explicitly conjecture the fixed-three-dimensional case. It gives no detailed theorem citation or equivalence relation. The stellar open-ball family and the manifold dimension statement do not resolve that fixed-dimensional universality question, and the original planar ball remark needs its own conventions and hypotheses.

Short English statement

What topological spaces arise from point configurations with one fixed Delaunay triangulation? The source asks about universality in dimension three. The supplied aid studies a strict labelled similarity quotient and gives a stellar-simplex open-ball family, while distinguishing fixed dimension from universality obtained by allowing dimension to grow.
