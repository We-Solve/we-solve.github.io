Math-14057

Vinogradov-Type Bounds for Large-Order Dirichlet Characters

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Dirichlet character modulo a prime $q$ is a group homomorphism $\chi:({\mathbb{Z}}/q{\mathbb{Z}})^\times\to{\mathbb{C}}^\times$, extended periodically to integers by setting $\chi(n)=0$ when $q\mid n$. It is primitive if its conductor is $q$. Its order $\operatorname{ord}(\chi)$ is the least positive integer $d$ such that $\chi(a)^d=1$ for every $a\in({\mathbb{Z}}/q{\mathbb{Z}})^\times$. For a nonprincipal character, define \[ \ell(\chi)=\min\{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}:\chi(n)\notin\{0,1\}\}. \] This minimum exists because the character is nonprincipal.

The supplied problem asks for general conditions on primitive characters modulo primes, with orders tending to infinity, that ensure \[ \ell(\chi)=O_{\varepsilon}(q^{\varepsilon}) \qquad\text{for every }\varepsilon>0, \] and also ensure that $\chi$ takes "varied values" below $q^{\varepsilon}$.

To make the limiting regime explicit, one may consider a family $(q_j,\chi_j)_{j\geq1}$, where each $q_j$ is prime, each $\chi_j$ is primitive modulo $q_j$, and $\operatorname{ord}(\chi_j)\to\infty$ as $j\to\infty$. The requested small-value bound then has the schematic quantifier form \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists C_{\varepsilon}>0\ \exists j_0\ \forall j\geq j_0:\quad \ell(\chi_j)\leq C_{\varepsilon}q_j^{\varepsilon}. \] The problem is to identify hypotheses under which such a bound holds. The supplied record does not specify those hypotheses, the required growth of the character orders relative to the moduli, or whether the constant and threshold must be uniform across all admissible families. The display therefore describes the requested bound within a fixed family and does not resolve the intended uniformity.

For clarity, an auxiliary set recording distinct nonzero values is \[ V_{\chi}(X)=\{\chi(n):n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1},\ n<X,\ q\nmid n\}. \] The supplied phrase "varied values" does not specify a lower bound for $\lvert V_{\chi}(q^{\varepsilon})\rvert$, an equidistribution condition, or another quantitative property. It also does not say whether zero values are relevant or explicitly settle the endpoint convention. Thus the second requested conclusion cannot be stated as a definite mathematical predicate from the supplied evidence. No particular interpretation of that conclusion is asserted here.

Short English statement

Find conditions on primitive Dirichlet characters of prime modulus, with orders tending to infinity, that force the least positive integer with character value outside $\{0,1\}$ to be bounded by every positive power of the modulus, and force diversity of small character values. The required uniformity and the meaning of value diversity remain unspecified.
