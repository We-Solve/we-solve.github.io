Math-3504

Homological Hilbert-Scheme Conjecture for Algebraic Links

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for an identity relating nested punctual Hilbert schemes of a planar curve singularity $C$ at $(0,0)$ to the triply graded HOMFLY homology of its algebraic link. Write $\mu$ for its Milnor number and $a,q,t$ for formal grading variables.

The base field, reducedness, and isolatedness hypotheses are not explicitly specified. Under the usual interpretation as a reduced complex plane curve germ $C=\{f=0\}\subset({\mathbb{C}}^2,0)$ with an isolated singularity, its algebraic link is \[ L_{C,(0,0)}=C\cap S^3_{\varepsilon}, \] where $S^3_{\varepsilon}$ is a sufficiently small sphere centered at the origin, and its Milnor number is \[ \mu=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\frac{{\mathbb{C}}\{x,y\}}{(\partial f/\partial x,\partial f/\partial y)}. \] These hypotheses describe a conventional interpretation; the supplied record does not explicitly confirm them or specify the orientation convention for the link.

The notation $C_{(0,0)}^{[\ell,\ell+m]}$ refers to a nested punctual Hilbert scheme. For ordinary nesting, its points would be pairs $Z\subseteq Z'$ of finite subschemes of $C$, both supported at the origin, with respective lengths $\ell$ and $\ell+m$. Equivalently, their ideals satisfy $I_{Z'}\subseteq I_Z$ and \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}(\mathcal O_{C,0}/I_Z)=\ell, \qquad \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}(\mathcal O_{C,0}/I_{Z'})=\ell+m. \] The record does not define the intended incidence conditions, so this ordinary nesting description does not establish that it is the scheme intended by the formula. Any additional condition involving the maximal ideal must be specified from the source. The notation $P_t^{\mathrm{vir}}$ denotes the source's virtual Poincaré polynomial, and $P(L_{C,(0,0)})$ denotes the source's triply graded HOMFLY homology series; their precise conventions are not supplied.

Proposed identity in the supplied statement and clean statement. For every planar curve singularity in the intended, but incompletely specified, class, the proposed identity is \[ \left(\frac aq\right)^{\mu-1} \sum_{\ell,m}q^{2\ell}a^{2m}t^{m^2} P_t^{\mathrm{vir}}\!\left(C_{(0,0)}^{[\ell,\ell+m]}\right) =P(L_{C,(0,0)}). \] The summation ranges are not stated. Nonnegative integers $\ell,m$ would be consistent with the length interpretation above, but that range requires confirmation.

Conflicting supplied formulation. The original-statement field instead gives \[ \sum_{\ell,m}\left(\frac aq\right)^{\mu-1} q^{2\ell}a^{2m}t^m P^{\mathrm{vir}}\!\left(C_{(0,0)}^{[\ell,\ell+m]}\right) =P(L_{C,(0,0)}). \] In particular, the explicit factor is $t^m$ here and $t^{m^2}$ in the other formulation; the polynomial notation also differs. The supplied evidence does not explain a change of convention that reconciles these expressions. Thus the intended identity cannot be specified uniquely without resolving this discrepancy and fixing the incidence, polynomial, homology, and grading conventions. No equality or mathematical resolution is asserted by this draft.

Short English statement

The proposed conjecture identifies a generating series of virtual Poincaré polynomials of nested punctual Hilbert schemes with the triply graded HOMFLY homology series of the singularity's algebraic link. The supplied formulas disagree between $t^{m^2}$ and $t^m$, and several hypotheses and normalization conventions remain unspecified. The supplied status is partially solved; this is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
