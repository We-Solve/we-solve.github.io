Positive Probability of Zero Total Superfluid Momentum

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied conjecture is: for superfluids, the total momentum is zero with positive asymptotic probability. The record does not specify a mathematical definition of a superfluid, the probability law of total momentum, or the asymptotic regime, so it does not determine a unique fully quantified statement.

A conditional formulation can be given once these data are supplied. Let $n$ index a specified sequence of systems in a specified superfluid regime. Let $(\Omega_n,\mathcal F_n,\mathbb P_n)$ describe the total-momentum measurement distribution, and let $\mathbf P_n:\Omega_n\to{\mathbb{R}}^d$ denote total momentum, where the spatial dimension $d\geq 1$ must also be specified. Set \[ p_n:=\mathbb P_n\left(\mathbf P_n=\mathbf 0\right). \] If "positive asymptotic probability" means convergence of the probabilities of exactly zero momentum, the proposed assertion is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}p_n=p_0\qquad\text{for some }p_0>0. \] This is a conditional statement template, not a determination of the source's intended model or limit. The supplied record does not establish whether it instead intends $\liminf_{n\to\infty}p_n>0$, or positive mass at zero in a limiting momentum distribution. In particular, if the momentum laws converge weakly to a probability measure $\mu$, the assertion $\mu(\{\mathbf 0\})>0$ is generally different from the displayed assertion. The class of systems over which the conjecture is quantified, the equilibrium state or ensemble, interactions, boundary conditions, reference frame, temperature and density conditions, and limiting procedure remain unspecified.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether a superfluid has strictly positive asymptotic probability of exactly zero total momentum. A precise statement requires a specified superfluid model, state, reference frame, and limiting regime, together with clarification of whether positivity concerns exact finite-system probabilities or an atom of a limiting distribution. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
