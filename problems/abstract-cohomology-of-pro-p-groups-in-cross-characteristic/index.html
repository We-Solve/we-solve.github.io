Math-4121

Abstract Cohomology of Pro-$p$ Groups in Cross Characteristic

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct primes. A pro-$p$ group is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. For such a group $G$, let $G^{\delta}$ denote its underlying abstract group, with the topology forgotten. Let ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ be the field with $q$ elements.

The supplied statement does not explicitly specify a $G$-action on the coefficients. The formulation below uses the usual interpretation of unadorned coefficients: ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ is a trivial $G$-module. Confirmation of this interpretation from the source context is required. With this convention, define \[ H^n_{\mathrm{abs}}(G;{\mathbb{F}}_q):=H^n(G^{\delta};{\mathbb{F}}_q), \] where the right-hand side is ordinary group cohomology, computed using all group cochains, without a continuity requirement. Write $H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G;{\mathbb{F}}_q)$ for continuous group cohomology, using continuous cochains and giving ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ the discrete topology.

Question, under the trivial-action interpretation. Is it true that for every pair of distinct primes $p,q$, every pro-$p$ group $G$, and every integer $n\geq 1$, \[ H^n_{\mathrm{abs}}(G;{\mathbb{F}}_q)=0? \] The supplied record compares this proposed vanishing with the continuous-cohomology vanishing \[ H^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G;{\mathbb{F}}_q)=0\qquad(n\geq 1) \] under the same coefficient convention. No finite-generation or other additional restriction on $G$ appears in the supplied reconstructed statement. The verbatim source fragment is incomplete, so its missing context cannot independently confirm these hypotheses.

Short English statement

For every pro-$p$ group and every prime $q\neq p$, must ordinary group cohomology with trivial ${\mathbb{F}}_q$-coefficients vanish in every positive degree when continuity is not imposed on cochains? The trivial coefficient action is implicit in the supplied notation and requires confirmation. The record labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
