NLS Approximation on Periodic Nonlinear Wave Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns nonlinear wave systems on periodic graphs, with nonlinearities containing quadratic terms, and a nonlinear Schrödinger (NLS) approximation. The background refers to quantum graphs, suggesting a metric graph interpretation: functions are defined on edges parametrized by intervals, and differential equations hold along those edges. The particular graph, its periodicity, and the wave system are not specified.

In the usual unweighted scalar interpretation, Kirchhoff vertex conditions mean continuity at each vertex together with conservation of outward derivatives: \[ u_e(v,t)=u_{e'}(v,t)\quad\text{for all incident edge ends }e,e', \qquad \sum_{e\sim v}\partial_{\nu_e}u_e(v,t)=0. \] Here $\partial_{\nu_e}$ denotes differentiation from the vertex into the incident edge, and the traces and sum must be well defined. The supplied record does not specify whether these conditions apply componentwise to a system, whether weights occur, or how boundary vertices are treated.

Problem as supplied. Can the nonlinear Schrödinger approximation be justified for the original nonlinear wave systems on periodic graphs with quadratic nonlinearities and Kirchhoff vertex conditions?

A justification would require a theorem comparing solutions of a specified original wave system with an approximation constructed from solutions of a specified NLS envelope equation. The record supplies neither equation, the admissible initial data, the carrier modes, the small parameter and scaling, the approximation formula, the error norm, nor the time interval and error bound. Consequently, it does not determine a fully quantified mathematical proposition. These missing choices must be recovered from the intended source before a precise approximation theorem can be stated; no particular choices are imposed here.

The supplied dated curation describes the problem as open and attributes the difficulty in the quadratic case to vertex regularity obstructing the required normal-form analysis. This is a reported source assessment, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether NLS envelope dynamics rigorously approximate nonlinear wave systems with quadratic nonlinearities on periodic graphs satisfying Kirchhoff vertex conditions. The original system and the required approximation estimate are unspecified, so the supplied record supports a research question but not a fully quantified theorem statement. Its open status and explanation of the obstruction are supplied dated curation.
