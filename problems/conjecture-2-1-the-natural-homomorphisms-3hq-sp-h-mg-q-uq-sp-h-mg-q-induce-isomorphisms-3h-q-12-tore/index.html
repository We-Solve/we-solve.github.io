Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=H_1(\Sigma_g;{\mathbb{Q}})$ with its symplectic intersection pairing and let $U_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\Lambda^3H_{{\mathbb{Q}}}/H_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, where the usual embedding of $H_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ is given by wedging with the symplectic class. Let $\mathcal M_g$ and $\mathcal M_{g,*}$ be the closed-surface and one-marked-point mapping class groups. Write $R^*$ for their rational cohomological tautological rings, in the customary convention generated by Miller--Morita--Mumford classes and, in the pointed case, the marked-point Euler/psi class. The source genus and class conventions need confirmation.

Let $\mathrm{Sp}$ denote the symplectic invariance in the source. Use the pairing to identify $H_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ with its dual when needed; the extraction alternates between dual and primal notation. Define \[ A_*={\left(\Lambda^*((\Lambda^3H_{{\mathbb{Q}}})^*)/J_*\right)}^{\mathrm{Sp}},\qquad A={\left(\Lambda^*U_{{\mathbb{Q}}}^*/J\right)}^{\mathrm{Sp}}, \] where $J_*$ is the homogeneous ideal generated by the source-designated quadratic modules $[1^2]_{\mathrm{torelli}}\oplus[2^2]$, and $J$ is the ideal generated by its designated $[2^2]$. The labels describe symplectic representations, but their precise copies/embeddings in the exterior squares are not given here. The subscript "torelli" must not be discarded or replaced by every copy with the same abstract representation type.

Conjecture 2.1. The stated natural cohomology homomorphisms induce isomorphisms \[ A_*\xrightarrow{\cong}R^*(\mathcal M_{g,*}),\qquad A\xrightarrow{\cong}R^*(\mathcal M_g). \] Moreover, $A_*$ and $A$ are Poincaré duality algebras of formal dimensions $2g-2$ and $2g-4$, respectively. Here formal dimension $D$ means top nonzero graded degree $D$, with top piece ${\mathbb{Q}}$ and perfect product pairings in complementary degrees; it is not the total vector-space dimension. Both the isomorphisms and duality conclusions are part of the conjecture. The source does not reproduce the construction of the natural maps or the relation-module embeddings, so these remain dependencies of a fully explicit presentation.

For comparison, the source recalls that for one Riemann surface $X$, $H^*(\operatorname{Jac}(X);{\mathbb{Q}})$ has formal duality dimension $2g$ and \[ H^*(\operatorname{Jac}(X);{\mathbb{Q}})/([1^2])\cong H^*(X;{\mathbb{Q}}), \] where $[1^2]=\ker(\Lambda^2H_{{\mathbb{Q}}}^*\to{\mathbb{Q}})$ is the primitive part under contraction with the pairing, and the parentheses denote its generated ideal. It views the conjecture as a family analogue. It also writes $\Lambda^*U_{{\mathbb{Q}}}^*$ as the cohomology associated with the primitive third cohomology $PH^3(\operatorname{Jac})$, of exterior-algebra formal dimension $\binom{2g}{3}-2g$, and rewrites the unpointed quotient with the $[2^2]$ ideal. This is an exterior-algebra/associated-torus interpretation: the ordinary singular cohomology of a vector space $PH^3$ itself would not have that form. The omitted geometric meaning of that shorthand must be specified before treating it as a literal space.

Short English statement

The proposed symplectic-invariant quadratic quotients of exterior algebras should present the pointed and unpointed tautological cohomology rings, with perfect duality in top degrees $2g-2$ and $2g-4$. The precise relation modules and natural maps are missing from the excerpt.
