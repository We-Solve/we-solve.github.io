Definitions and mathematical statement

The source uses $I_k(T)$ for a moment of the Riemann zeta function. It does not supply the defining integral, its normalization, the allowed values of $k$, or the range and limiting regime of $T$. Retain this notation without choosing those missing specifications. The phrase "lower-order terms" refers to the conjectural contributions beyond the leading term in the moment asymptotics attributed in the source to CFKRS [CFKRS] and Diaconu--Goldfeld--Hoffstein [DGH]; their formulas and required error estimates are not supplied.

Problem (Anurag Sahay, Rochester; source Problem 14). Can the splitting conjecture in the Gonek--Hughes--Keating [GHK] hybrid Euler--Hadamard product be refined so as to recover the conjectural lower-order terms for $I_k(T)$ attributed to CFKRS [CFKRS] and Diaconu--Goldfeld--Hoffstein [DGH]?

Here "recover" retains the source's intended requirement of deriving the conjectured lower-order contributions from a refinement of the splitting conjecture. The supplied record does not state that conjecture, the proposed refinement, the target expansions, or whether the two cited approaches specify identical targets. Consequently, a fully quantified formulation, including the applicable moment parameters and the precision of recovery, cannot be extracted from this record alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether a refined splitting conjecture for the Gonek--Hughes--Keating hybrid Euler--Hadamard product can reproduce the conjectured lower-order terms of Riemann zeta moments. The moment definition, parameter ranges, target formulas, and required accuracy are absent from the supplied record and require mathematical review.
