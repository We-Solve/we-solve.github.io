Donkin’s Good Filtration Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret $G$ as a connected simple simply connected algebraic group over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p>0$. Choose a maximal torus $T$ and positive roots, and let $B$ be the Borel subgroup corresponding to the negative roots. Write $X(T)$ for the weight lattice, $X^+$ for its dominant weights, and $\rho$ for half the sum of the positive roots. For $\lambda\in X^+$, let $L(\lambda)$ denote the simple rational $G$-module of highest weight $\lambda$, and let $\nabla(\lambda)=\operatorname{ind}_B^G k_\lambda$ denote the induced module of highest weight $\lambda$, where $k_\lambda$ is the one-dimensional $B$-module defined by $\lambda$.

For an integer $r\geq 1$, define the set of $p^r$-restricted dominant weights by \[ X_r=\{\lambda\in X^+:0\leq\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle<p^r\text{ for every simple coroot }\alpha^\vee\}. \] Every $\lambda\in X^+$ has a unique expression $\lambda=\lambda_0+p^r\lambda_1$, with $\lambda_0\in X_r$ and $\lambda_1\in X^+$. Set \[ \nabla^{(p,r)}(\lambda)=L(\lambda_0)\otimes\nabla(\lambda_1)^{(r)}, \qquad \mathrm{St}_r=L((p^r-1)\rho). \] Here $V^{(r)}$ denotes the $r$-th Frobenius twist, using a standard split Frobenius morphism of $G$.

A finite-dimensional rational $G$-module $M$ has a good filtration if it admits a finite filtration by $G$-submodules \[ 0=M_0\subset M_1\subset\cdots\subset M_n=M \] whose successive quotients are modules $\nabla(\mu_i)$ with $\mu_i\in X^+$. It has a good $(p,r)$-filtration if it admits such a filtration with successive quotients $\nabla^{(p,r)}(\mu_i)$. The empty filtration is allowed when $M=0$.

The supplied formulation of Donkin's Good Filtration Conjecture asks whether, for every such $k$ and $G$, every integer $r\geq1$, and every finite-dimensional rational $G$-module $M$, \[ M\text{ has a good }(p,r)\text{-filtration} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mathrm{St}_r\otimes_k M\text{ has a good filtration}. \] The algebraic-group interpretation, the positive-integer range of $r$, and the filtration definitions above make explicit standard conventions omitted from the supplied statement; their agreement with the intended source requires review. The supplied statement imposes no further restriction on $p$. Its accompanying literature assessment concerns composition-factor multiplicities of induced modules, which is a different stated target and does not by itself establish the status of this equivalence.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asserts that a finite-dimensional rational module has a good $(p,r)$-filtration exactly when tensoring it with the $r$-th Steinberg module produces a module with a good filtration. The source labels it open, but its accompanying literature assessment addresses a different problem; the formulation and status require human review.
