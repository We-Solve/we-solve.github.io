Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

The report specifies the modified iteratively reweighted least-squares method of Kummerle and Maly (2023). It recovers $X_*$ from noiseless linear measurements $y=\mathcal A(X_*)$. Row sparsity means that only a small number of rows of $X_*$ are nonzero; low rank bounds its column-space dimension. The iteration uses joint low-rank and row-sparsity weights and updates smoothing parameters from singular values and row norms. Global convergence here means convergence to the ground-truth matrix $X_*$ from the source's allowed initializations.

A matrix $\Phi$ has restricted-isometry constant at most $\delta_s$ if $(1-\delta_s)\lVert x\rVert_2^2\leq\lVert\Phi x\rVert_2^2\leq(1+\delta_s)\lVert x\rVert_2^2$ for every vector with at most $s$ nonzero coordinates. Compressed sensing recovers structured vectors from measurements $\Phi x$.

The rank of a matrix is the dimension of its column space. Matrix completion reconstructs a low-rank matrix from a subset of its entries; matrix sensing reconstructs it from specified linear measurements. Tensor decomposition represents a multiway array as a sum of simple outer products.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Under which restricted-isometry or related assumptions on the measurement operator does the iteratively reweighted least-squares algorithm for simultaneously sparse and low-rank matrix recovery converge globally? [S2]
Scope qualification.
The algorithm is identified in the original report as Kummerle-Maly (2023), arXiv:2306.04961. The separately supplied arXiv:1801.06240 concerns an alternating Tikhonov/Lasso method and is not used to define this IRLS target. Exact restricted-isometry assumptions and initialization guarantees remain those sought by the question.
