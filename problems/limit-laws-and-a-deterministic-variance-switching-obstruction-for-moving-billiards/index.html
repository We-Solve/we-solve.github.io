Limit laws and a deterministic variance-switching obstruction for moving billiards

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks what limit theorems hold for random billiards with moving or deforming scatterers. The original spelling "billards" is reported by the supplied verification to occur in the PDF itself; "billiards" is a conservative terminology repair. Geometry, collision law, environmental randomness, time scale, observable, and limiting regime are not specified.

The aid reports literature results for perturbative finite-horizon random or sequential billiards: uniform memory loss; vector almost-sure invariance principles; annealed cell-index central limit theorems and mixing local limit theorems; and self-normalized sequential central limit theorems under variance growth. Their precise model, regularity, stationarity, nondegeneracy, and lattice hypotheses, rates, and references are absent. These are supplied scoped literature claims, not universal assertions for arbitrary moving walls.

Its separate deterministic obstruction is the following. Let $T_0,T_1$ preserve the same probability $\mu$ on a common phase space. Let $\varphi_i$ be bounded centered ${\mathbb{R}}^r$-valued observables with fixed-map CLTs \[ \frac1{\sqrt L}\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}\varphi_i\circ T_i^j \ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal N(0,\Sigma_i) \quad(L\to\infty),\qquad\Sigma_0\ne\Sigma_1, \] under initial law $\mu$. Choose alternating blocks of the two maps with lengths $L_m$, and put $N_m=\sum_{j=1}^mL_j$. Assume \[ N_{m-1}/\sqrt{L_m}\longrightarrow0. \] Within block $m$, use $T_{m\bmod2}$ and its corresponding observable. If $S_n$ is the concatenated additive sum along the resulting nonautonomous orbit, the aid reports \[ S_{N_m}/\sqrt{N_m}\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal N(0,\Sigma_i) \quad\text{along blocks with }m\bmod2=i. \] The common invariant measure gives each block's starting state law $\mu$; boundedness and the stated length condition make previous blocks negligible. No independence between blocks is assumed. Distinct covariance matrices therefore prevent one fixed-covariance $\sqrt n$-CLT along the full sequence. This does not exclude self-normalization, subsequential limits, or CLTs under additional stationary-environment assumptions.

Application to billiards is expressly conditional: one needs a rigorously verified close pair of finite-horizon dispersing billiard systems, a common phase-space/measure identification such as the aid's equal-arclength setup, bounded centered cell-displacement observables, distinct diffusion matrices, and admissibility of their concatenation. No such pair or proof is supplied in this record. A sequence of frozen collision maps also needs justification before being identified with a physical moving-wall flow. The theorem is a fixed-environment obstruction to an unrestricted claim, not a refutation of the reported annealed or otherwise qualified random-billiard theorems, and not a classification of all limit laws requested by the source.

Short English statement

The source asks broadly for limit laws in moving or deforming random billiards. The supplied aid reports qualified existing results and a general alternating-block obstruction: distinct fixed-system diffusion covariances can force different Gaussian subsequences, with a billiard realization left explicitly conditional.
