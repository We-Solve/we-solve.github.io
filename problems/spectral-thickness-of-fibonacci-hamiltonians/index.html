Spectral Thickness of Fibonacci Hamiltonians

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns the thickness of spectra of Fibonacci Hamiltonians. It does not specify the operator, its Hilbert space, potential normalization, phase convention, or coupling range. Consequently, the following is a conditional formulation of the intended question, requiring those choices to be supplied before it defines a particular spectral problem.

For an intended family of bounded self-adjoint operators $H_\lambda$, write $\Sigma_\lambda=\sigma(H_\lambda)$, where $\sigma(H)=\{E\in{\mathbb{R}}:H-E\operatorname{Id}\text{ has no bounded inverse}\}$. The standard interpretation of thickness is Newhouse thickness, defined as follows. Let $K\subset{\mathbb{R}}$ be a nonempty compact perfect nowhere dense set, and let $J$ be its convex hull. A presentation $\mathcal P=(U_n)_{n\geq1}$ enumerates the bounded connected components of $J\setminus K$. For an endpoint $u$ of $U_n$, let $B_{\mathcal P}(u)$ be the connected component of $J\setminus\bigcup_{j=1}^{n}U_j$ containing $u$. With $|\cdot|$ denoting interval length, define \[ \tau(K)=\sup_{\mathcal P}\inf_{n\geq1}\inf_{u\in\partial U_n}\frac{|B_{\mathcal P}(u)|}{|U_n|}. \] This interpretation requires confirmation that the intended spectra are Cantor sets and that this is the intended notion of thickness.

For a specified positive coupling range $I\subset(0,\infty)$ accumulating at zero, the monotonicity question can be expressed as \[ \forall\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in I,\qquad \lambda_1<\lambda_2\ \Longrightarrow\ \tau(\Sigma_{\lambda_1})\geq\tau(\Sigma_{\lambda_2}). \] Here positive coupling and nonincreasing monotonicity are explicit conditional interpretations: the source does not state the coupling range or whether strict decrease is intended.

The weak-coupling phrase "behaves like $1/\lambda$" also requires qualification. Comparability means \[ \exists c,C,\delta>0\ \forall\lambda\in I\cap(0,\delta),\qquad \frac{c}{\lambda}\leq\tau(\Sigma_\lambda)\leq\frac{C}{\lambda}. \] Asymptotic equivalence would instead mean \[ \lim_{\substack{\lambda\downarrow0\\\lambda\in I}}\lambda\tau(\Sigma_\lambda)=1, \] which is a stronger assertion. The supplied dated assessment reports comparability as established by weak-coupling work, while reporting that monotone decrease was conjectured and was not established by the located papers. These are supplied curation claims, not independently verified conclusions. The broader request for careful computations of spectral structure specifies no additional mathematical target.

Short English statement

For the intended Fibonacci Hamiltonian family, determine whether spectral thickness decreases as the coupling increases, and clarify its growth near zero coupling. The supplied assessment reports bounds comparable to $1/\lambda$, while leaving monotonicity unestablished by the located papers. The operator conventions, coupling range, strictness of monotonicity, and meaning of "behaves like" require clarification.
