Uniform Low-Regularity Schemes for Coupled Klein–Gordon Systems

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied research problem asks for an extension of uniformly accurate, low-regularity numerical approximations for the Klein--Gordon equation to more general Klein--Gordon-type systems, including coupled Klein--Gordon equations and Klein--Gordon--Schrödinger systems.

Uniform accuracy means that an approximation error bound remains valid with constants independent of the parameter or parameters over the intended parameter range. Schematically, if $\lambda\in\Lambda$ denotes such parameters, $\delta>0$ a discretization scale, and $E_\lambda(\delta)$ an error in a specified norm over a specified time interval, an algebraic uniform accuracy requirement would have the form \[ \sup_{\lambda\in\Lambda} E_\lambda(\delta)\leq C\delta^p, \qquad 0<\delta\leq\delta_0, \] where $p>0$, $C$, and $\delta_0$ are independent of $\lambda$. This is an explanatory template, not a bound specified by the source: the record supplies neither the parameters and their range nor the error norm, discretization, order, or admissible step restrictions. Low regularity refers to obtaining convergence estimates under weak regularity assumptions on the initial data and solutions; no function spaces or regularity thresholds are supplied here.

Statement of the research task. Extend the scalar Klein--Gordon approximation approach described by the source to more general Klein--Gordon-type systems, with coupled Klein--Gordon and Klein--Gordon--Schrödinger systems as examples, and establish approximation error estimates that combine low solution regularity with uniform accuracy in the relevant parameters.

It also does not specify whether temporal, spatial, or fully discrete approximations are intended. Consequently, it determines a research direction but does not determine a unique, fully quantified mathematical conjecture. Short English statement

Develop numerical approximations for coupled Klein--Gordon-type systems that converge for solutions of low regularity and retain their accuracy uniformly over the relevant parameter range. The supplied record leaves the equations, parameters, regularity assumptions, and convergence requirements unspecified. Its dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports progress for Klein--Gordon--Schrödinger systems; these claims require independent review.
