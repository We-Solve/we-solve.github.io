Math-6589

Roth Recurrence in Groups of Intermediate Growth

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finitely generated group $G$, choose a finite symmetric generating set $S$ and write \[ \beta_S(r)=\left|\{g\in G:|g|_S\le r\}\right|,\qquad r\in{\mathbb{N}},\ r\ge1, \] where $|g|_S$ is word length. Under the usual convention, $G$ has intermediate growth if \[ \lim_{r\to\infty}\frac{\log\beta_S(r)}{r}=0 \quad\text{and}\quad \nexists C>0,\ d\ge0\ \text{such that}\ \beta_S(r)\le Cr^d\ \text{for all }r\ge1. \] This property is independent of the finite generating set.

A measure-preserving action of $G$ on a measure space $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$ consists of invertible measurable maps $T_g:X\to X$ satisfying $T_e=\operatorname{id}_X$, $T_{gh}=T_g\circ T_h$, and $\mu(T_g^{-1}A)=\mu(A)$ for every $g\in G$ and $A\in\mathcal A$. Write $g^nB=T_g^n(B)$ for $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$.

On the existential reading of the unspecified elements $g_1,g_2$, the supplied question asks whether there exist a group $G$ of intermediate growth, a measure-preserving action of $G$ on $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$, elements $g_1,g_2\in G$, and $B\in\mathcal A$ such that \[ \mu(B)>0 \quad\text{and}\quad \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\},\qquad \mu\left(T_{g_1}^{n}(B)\cap T_{g_2}^{n}(B)\right)=0. \] Unresolved qualifications. The supplied statement does not specify whether $\mu(X)=1$, whether $\mu(X)<\infty$, or whether $\mu(B)<\infty$. The displayed formulation preserves that omission; it cannot be identified with an intended probability-space recurrence problem without clarification. The source also gives no explicit quantifiers or additional hypotheses for $g_1,g_2$, such as commutativity or generation of $G$. Its displayed intersection contains exactly the two translated sets and no additional factor $B$. These details must be checked against the intended source formulation before mathematical acceptance.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether a group of intermediate growth can act by measure-preserving transformations with a positive-measure set whose two specified translates have zero-measure intersection at every common nonzero integer power. The measure-space assumptions and the intended hypotheses on the two group elements remain unspecified. The supplied label is open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
