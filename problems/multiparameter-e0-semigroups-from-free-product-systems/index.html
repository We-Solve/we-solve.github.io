Phys-241 · Math-12179

Multiparameter E0-Semigroups from Free Product Systems

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a unital coefficient algebra $B$, a Hilbert $B$-bimodule is a right Hilbert $B$-module with a compatible left action. $\mathcal B^a(E)$ denotes its adjointable operators. A pointed free product system $(E_t,\omega_t)$ has central reference vectors and associative identifications $E_s*E_t\simeq E_{s+t}$, using the source's reduced free product over $B$. A tensor product subsystem instead has $E_s\odot E_t\simeq E_{s+t}$ with $\odot$ the internal tensor product over $B$. An $E_0$-semigroup is a continuous semigroup of unital $*$-endomorphisms. A unit is a section whose values multiply according to the product-system structure. The parameter is $\mathbf t=(t_1,\ldots,t_n)\in\mathbb R_+^n$, with addition componentwise. Each $E^i$ has a reference unit, and the proposed pointed free product at $\mathbf t$ uses these reference vectors. The inductive-limit dilation should give commuting endomorphisms indexed by $\mathbb R_+^n$. A multiparameter unit must satisfy the product law for all pairs of nonnegative parameter vectors.
Source scope and qualifications.
The report describes the free product construction and a full-Fock example. Its stated open program concerns the analysis of units, whether only the reference unit survives, and whether the induced Markov semigroups factor into strongly commuting one-parameter semigroups; those questions are not removed.
Units and factorization requested in the source.
Determine all unital units, including whether a nontrivial unit exists beyond the reference-vector unit. Decide whether the resulting Markov semigroups are products of strongly commuting one-parameter semigroups.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Given $n$ one-parameter free product systems $E^1,\ldots,E^n$, carry out the proposed construction

$$E(t_1,\ldots,t_n)=E^1_{t_1}*\cdots*E^n_{t_n}$$

and its inductive limit over the reference vector to obtain candidate $n$-parameter $E_0$-semigroups, and analyze the resulting systems and their units.

Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Free-product constructions exist for one-parameter systems and related cases, but the proposed n-parameter program and the associated type II Arveson-system question for group-valued Levy processes are not fully resolved. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Carry out and analyze the proposed multiparameter free-flow construction, especially its units and resulting Markov semigroups.
