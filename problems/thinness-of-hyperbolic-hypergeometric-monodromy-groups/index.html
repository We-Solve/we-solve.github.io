Math-5011

Thinness of Hyperbolic Hypergeometric Monodromy Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For purposes of interpreting arithmeticity and thinness, suppose an integral realization of a finitely generated group $\Gamma\leq \mathrm{GL}_n({\mathbb{Z}})$ has been specified. Let $G=\overline{\Gamma}^{\,\mathrm{Zar},{\mathbb{Q}}}$ be its algebraic Zariski closure over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and put \[ G({\mathbb{Z}})=G({\mathbb{Q}})\cap\mathrm{GL}_n({\mathbb{Z}}). \] In this realization, $\Gamma$ is called arithmetic if $[G({\mathbb{Z}}):\Gamma]<\infty$, and thin if it is Zariski dense in $G$ and $[G({\mathbb{Z}}):\Gamma]=\infty$. The supplied excerpt does not specify the integral realization or the admissible hypergeometric parameters $\alpha,\beta$; these definitions therefore explain the terminology conditionally and do not supply the missing parameter restrictions.

The source calls a hypergeometric monodromy group $\Gamma_{\alpha,\beta}$ a hyperbolic hypergeometric monodromy group (HHMG) when its associated algebraic group $G_{\alpha,\beta}$ is of special orthogonal type $\mathrm{SO}_n$ and \[ G_{\alpha,\beta}({\mathbb{R}})\cong\mathrm{SO}(n-1,1). \] This is the source's stated real-group condition; the relevant rational quadratic form and the construction of $G_{\alpha,\beta}$ are not supplied.

Classification question. Among the HHMGs in the source's intended hypergeometric family, determine which $\Gamma_{\alpha,\beta}$ are arithmetic and which are thin.

Conjecture attributed to Fuchs--Meiri--Sarnak (Conjecture 9). All but finitely many HHMGs are thin. Formally, if $\mathcal H$ denotes the intended collection of HHMGs with the source's intended convention for identifying groups, the assertion is \[ \#\{\Gamma\in\mathcal H:\Gamma\text{ is not thin}\}<\infty. \] Neither the complete definition of $\mathcal H$ nor the convention for counting distinct groups is included in the supplied record. Consequently, this quantified formulation remains conditional on those missing specifications.

The surrounding Question 8 asks the arithmeticity-versus-thinness question for the broader family of hypergeometric monodromy groups. The excerpt reports arithmeticity for $n\in\{2,3\}$, further work in the symplectic case $G_{\alpha,\beta}=\mathrm{Sp}_n$, and seven families together with sporadic examples in the hyperbolic case. It attributes to Fuchs--Meiri--Sarnak a certificate for thinness and proofs of thinness for some of those families and many sporadic examples. These are supplied contextual claims, subject to the omitted hypotheses of the underlying family, rather than independently verified assertions here.

Short English statement

Classify the arithmetic and thin hyperbolic hypergeometric monodromy groups, and formulate the Fuchs--Meiri--Sarnak conjecture that only finitely many are not thin. The supplied record omits the admissible parameters, integral realizations, and convention for counting distinct groups, so a fully specified quantification requires further source review. The supplied open status is dated curation.
