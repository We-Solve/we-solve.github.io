Math-3309

Kirby Problem 5.5

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Consider topological manifold pairs $(M_i^{n+k},N_i^n)$, $i=1,2$, with locally flat embeddings $N_i\subset M_i$. At an interior point, local flatness means that a neighborhood pair is homeomorphic to $({\mathbb{R}}^{n+k},{\mathbb{R}}^n)$ with the standard linear inclusion. In the usual positive-dimensional connected-sum construction, choose embedded standard ball pairs $(D^{n+k},D^n)$ around points of $N_i$, remove their interiors, and glue the boundary pairs \[ (S^{n+k-1},S^{n-1}) \] by a compatible homeomorphism. This produces a candidate pair denoted $(M_1,N_1)\#(M_2,N_2)$. If orientations are imposed, the charts and gluing must respect the selected connected-sum orientation convention.

Imported choice-independence question. Do different compatible choices of points, local ball-pair charts and gluing data always produce equivalent topological pairs? In a homeomorphism-of-pairs version, equivalence means a homeomorphism between the ambient manifolds carrying one embedded submanifold onto the other, preserving orientations when those are part of the data. The source does not say whether it instead asks for ambient isotopy, a relative/marked equivalence, or un-oriented homeomorphism. It also omits connectedness, orientations, local-orientation conventions, dimension range and possible boundary behavior. These choices are necessary to specify exactly which universal choice-independence assertion is intended.

The literature triage dated 2026-08-17 reports an affirmative codimension-one case from Brown's local-flatness results. It reports Livingston's positive result in codimension two in every dimension for connected, oriented, locally flat pairs, with independence up to orientation-preserving homeomorphism. Those hypotheses and that equivalence notion are retained, rather than extending the result to all pairs left unspecified in the short statement.

The source explains that normal-bundle uniqueness is essential in codimension two and fails in higher codimensions. It leaves codimension $k>2$ with $n+k>4$ unresolved: prove the appropriately specified connected sum independent of its data, or find pairs where it is not. It suggests that a pairwise torus trick could yield a solution if a corresponding pairwise version of Wall's non-simply-connected surgery were known and in print; this is a conditional proposed route, not a theorem supplied here.

The registry label is open and the triage label partially solved, reflecting the restricted low-codimension results. No source, PDF or topological proof was independently checked, and no present-day status assessment is claimed.

Short English statement

Remove standard ball pairs from two locally flat manifold pairs and glue their boundary pairs. Does the resulting topological pair depend on the choices? The supplied review reports codimension-one results and a connected oriented codimension-two theorem, while leaving higher codimension in ambient dimension above four open. The exact equivalence and orientation conventions still need specification.
