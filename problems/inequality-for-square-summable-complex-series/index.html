Math-3293

Inequality for square summable complex series

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let $\ell^2(\mathbb C)=\{\alpha=(\alpha_n)_{n\geq1}:\sum_{n\geq1}|\alpha_n|^2<\infty\}$. For $k\geq0$ define \[T_k(\alpha)=\sum_{\ell\geq0}\frac{\alpha_{2^k(2\ell+1)}}{\ell+1}.\] Each inner series converges absolutely by Cauchy--Schwarz, since $\sum_{\ell\geq0}(\ell+1)^{-2}=\pi^2/6$ and its selected coefficients are square summable.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every $\alpha\in\ell^2(\mathbb C)$, is \[\sum_{n\geq1}|\alpha_n|^2\geq\frac{6}{\pi^2}\sum_{k\geq0}\left|\sum_{\ell\geq0}\frac{\alpha_{2^k(2\ell+1)}}{\ell+1}\right|^2\] valid? [S2]

Short English statement

Does the stated weighted sum along the odd-part dyadic partition define a squared-norm bound with constant six over pi squared?
