Math-3708

Compactness of Positively Curved Shrinking Ricci Solitons

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a smooth Riemannian manifold $(M^n,g)$, a gradient shrinking Ricci soliton consists of a smooth function $f\colon M\to{\mathbb{R}}$ and a constant $\lambda>0$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g+\nabla_g^2 f=\lambda g. \] Here $\operatorname{Ric}_g$ is the Ricci tensor and $\nabla_g^2 f$ is the Hessian of $f$. Let $\mathcal R_x\colon\Lambda^2T_xM\to\Lambda^2T_xM$ be the self-adjoint curvature operator, with the sign convention that the round sphere has positive curvature operator. Pointwise positive curvature operator means \[ \langle\mathcal R_x\omega,\omega\rangle_g>0 \quad\text{for every }x\in M\text{ and every }0\ne\omega\in\Lambda^2T_xM. \] One interpretation of bounded positive curvature operator is that there exists a constant $C\in(0,\infty)$, independent of $x$, such that \[ 0<\langle\mathcal R_x\omega,\omega\rangle_g\le C\lVert\omega\rVert_g^2 \quad\text{for every }x\in M\text{ and }0\ne\omega\in\Lambda^2T_xM. \] This condition does not impose a uniform positive lower bound on the curvature operator.

The supplied assertion is that every gradient shrinking Ricci soliton with bounded positive curvature operator is compact. Its wording does not explicitly specify completeness, connectedness, boundary conventions, the dimension range, or whether positivity is uniform. Consequently, the following precise formulation is conditional on interpreting the intended objects as complete, connected manifolds without boundary of dimension at least two, and interpreting bounded positivity as above:

For every integer $n\ge2$, every complete, connected smooth Riemannian $n$-manifold $(M,g)$ without boundary, every $f\in C^\infty(M)$, and every $\lambda>0$, if \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g+\nabla_g^2 f=\lambda g \] and there exists $C\in(0,\infty)$ such that \[ 0<\langle\mathcal R_x\omega,\omega\rangle_g\le C\lVert\omega\rVert_g^2 \quad\text{for all }x\in M,\quad 0\ne\omega\in\Lambda^2T_xM, \] must $M$ be compact?

The added conventions in this conditional formulation require source confirmation; they are not asserted to have been explicitly stated in the supplied report. In particular, completeness cannot be silently inferred from the displayed soliton equation.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether bounded positive curvature operator forces a gradient shrinking Ricci soliton to be compact in every intended dimension. The displayed formulation makes customary completeness and manifold conventions explicit, but these conventions and the meaning of bounded positivity require confirmation. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
