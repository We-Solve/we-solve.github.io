Math-1086

Bimonster Quotients of Complex Hyperbolic Reflection Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be the particular complex-hyperbolic reflection group in the source's construction on the complex $13$-ball $\mathbb B^{13}$, let $\mathcal H$ be its mirror arrangement, and put $Y=\mathbb B^{13}\setminus\mathcal H$ and $X_0=Y/G$. The extract does not give the lattice or generators specifying $G$, so this is not a statement about an arbitrary ball reflection group. Let $\Pi$ denote the fundamental group intended by the source's notation $\pi_1(X_0)$. Ordinary versus orbifold fundamental group must be specified: residual stabilizers of the action can distinguish them. For the indicated meridian loops $m$ about discriminant components, the intended order-two quotient is schematically \[Q=\Pi/\langle\!\langle m^2\text{ for the indicated meridians }m\rangle\!\rangle.\] This is a quotient of the appropriate discriminant-complement group, not a silent simultaneous imposition of $m^2=1$ and an already-existing $m^3=1$ relation, which would kill $m$. The source's phrase order two rather than order three needs the precise construction to determine the group and relations being replaced.

Let $\mathbb M$ denote the Monster simple group. The bimonster is $(\mathbb M\times\mathbb M)\rtimes C_2$, where the nonidentity element of $C_2$ exchanges the two factors. The first question is whether \[Q\simeq(\mathbb M\times\mathbb M)\rtimes C_2.\] The report says the proposed quotient satisfies its known bimonster relations except possibly $w^{10}=1$, but does not provide the word $w$ or the relation list in this extract. The additional questions are whether the source's sixteen corresponding based loops generate $\Pi$, and what further relations $\Pi$ has. Generation of the quotient $Q$ is not substituted for generation of $\Pi$ itself. The sixteen loops, their base paths, and their configuration are not specified. The supplied 2026-08-21 triage labels the record partially solved and reports that a 2025 primary paper reduces the quotient identification to two possibilities, the bimonster or a group of order two, without settling which. That progress report is retained without treating the final identification or the separate sixteen-loop presentation questions as resolved.

Short English statement

Does the order-two meridian quotient of the source's ball-arrangement group equal the bimonster, and do its sixteen specified loops generate the full complement group? The reflection group, meridians, fundamental-group convention, and exceptional word are missing from the extract.
