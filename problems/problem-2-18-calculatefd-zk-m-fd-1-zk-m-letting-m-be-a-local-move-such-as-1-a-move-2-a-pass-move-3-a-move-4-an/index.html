Problem 2.18 — CalculateFd(ZK, m)/Fd+1(ZK, m), letting m be a local move such as (1) a # move, (2) a pass move, (3) a ∆ move, (4) an…

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal K$ denote the free abelian group on the intended isotopy classes of knots. For a fixed local move $m$, the standard local-move filtration $F_d({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal K,m)$ is generated by alternating sums \[ \sum_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,d\}}(-1)^{|S|}K_S, \] where $d$ applications of $m$ have disjoint supports and $K_S$ is obtained from the original knot by performing exactly the moves indexed by $S$. In this convention $F_0={\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal K$ and $F_{d+1}\subseteq F_d$. The excerpt does not include its filtration definition, so this standard convention needs source confirmation.

For every degree $d$, calculate the abelian group \[ F_d({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal K,m)/F_{d+1}({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal K,m) \] for local moves such as:

  1. the $\#$ move;
  2. the pass move;
  3. the $\Delta$ move;
  4. the $n$-gon move, with $n$ a move parameter distinct from the filtration degree $d$.
The $\#$ symbol names a local move and is not replaced by the connected-sum operation. The diagrams and orientation/framing conventions for these moves, the allowed range of $n$, and the knot category are not supplied. The coefficient group is ${\mathbb{Z}}$: computing rational dimensions alone would not determine possible torsion in the requested quotient. The phrase "such as" leaves the programme open to other local moves, without changing the four explicit cases.

Short English statement

Compute the integer associated graded groups of the knot filtration generated by local moves, particularly the $\#$, pass, $\Delta$, and $n$-gon moves. The exact move diagrams and filtration convention must be recovered from the source.
