E0-Semigroups from Free Product Systems

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a unital coefficient algebra $B$, a Hilbert $B$-bimodule is a right Hilbert $B$-module with a compatible left action. $\mathcal B^a(E)$ denotes its adjointable operators. A pointed free product system $(E_t,\omega_t)$ has central reference vectors and associative identifications $E_s*E_t\simeq E_{s+t}$, using the source's reduced free product over $B$. A tensor product subsystem instead has $E_s\odot E_t\simeq E_{s+t}$ with $\odot$ the internal tensor product over $B$. An $E_0$-semigroup is a continuous semigroup of unital $*$-endomorphisms. A unit is a section whose values multiply according to the product-system structure. The reference vectors define an inductive limit $E_\infty$ and a free left dilation $v_t:E_\infty*E_t\to E_\infty$. The source uses this dilation and the induced unital embeddings to construct an $E_0$-semigroup on $\mathcal B^a(E_\infty)$. A noise here has the reference-vector/vacuum structure specified in that construction.
Source scope and qualifications.
The free left dilation is already constructed in the report. The unresolved target is which noises arise by this construction, not existence of the construction alone.

An increment-moment obstruction.
In the scalar-state case, suppose candidate past and future increment algebras $A_1,A_2$ are freely independent in a state $\phi$. By the defining centered alternating-word condition, self-adjoint $x\in A_1,y\in A_2$ with $\phi(x)=\phi(y)=0$ satisfy \[ \phi(xyxy)=0. \] By contrast, if these same increment algebras commute and their state is a tensor product, then $xyxy=x^2y^2$ and $\phi(xyxy)=\phi(x^2)\phi(y^2)$. With both variances positive the two requirements are incompatible. A concrete tensor example is $x=Z\otimes I,y=I\otimes Z$ in the normalized trace on $M_2\otimes M_2$: the alternating moment is one.
A degenerate overlap.
If one centered increment algebra is zero in its state representation, the obstruction disappears. This includes the one-dimensional pointed free system, whose dilation is the identity semigroup. Thus the moment test has the correct trivial endpoint and cannot be used to exclude all noises with a tensor presentation.
What classification still needs.
The calculation only tests whether a specified pair of increments can simultaneously be tensor independent and free under the same state. An endomorphism semigroup might admit another increment realization, another reference vector, or an equivalence which changes these choices. Therefore it is not an intrinsic classification invariant of the bare semigroup. The first gap is a representation-independent reconstruction criterion for the source's free dilation. The already constructed free left dilation is not reproved or presented as the unresolved result.
