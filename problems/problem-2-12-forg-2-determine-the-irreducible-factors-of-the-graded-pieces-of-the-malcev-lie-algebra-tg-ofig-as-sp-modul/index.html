Problem 2.12 — Forg≥ 2, determine the irreducible factors of the graded pieces of the Malcev Lie algebra tg ofIg as Sp-modules.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each $g\geq2$, let $\mathcal I_g$ be the Torelli group in the source's surface convention. In the usual closed oriented case, \[ \mathcal I_g=\ker\left(\operatorname{Mod}_g\to \operatorname{Sp}(H_1(S_g;{\mathbb{Z}}))\right). \] Let $\mathfrak t_g$ denote its Malcev Lie algebra, conventionally over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. This is a pronilpotent Lie algebra associated to the group's rational unipotent completion. Retain the source's grading and $\operatorname{Sp}$-action; the excerpt does not specify the coefficient convention, filtration, or category of representations.

Problem 2.12. Determine, for every $g\geq2$, the irreducible factors of every graded piece of $\mathfrak t_g$ as $\operatorname{Sp}$-modules. If the intended grading is the lower-central grading, with $L^1\mathfrak t_g=\mathfrak t_g$ and $L^{d+1}\mathfrak t_g=[\mathfrak t_g,L^d\mathfrak t_g]$ in the appropriate completed sense, these pieces are \[ \operatorname{gr}^L_d\mathfrak t_g =L^d\mathfrak t_g/L^{d+1}\mathfrak t_g,\qquad d\geq1. \] In a finite-dimensional semisimple rational-representation setting, the requested data can be written as the irreducible types and multiplicities in \[ \operatorname{gr}_d\mathfrak t_g\cong\bigoplus_\lambda V_\lambda^{\oplus m_\lambda(g,d)}, \qquad\operatorname{Sp}=\operatorname{Sp}(H_1(S_g;{\mathbb{Q}})). \] This notation is conditional: the source does not establish finite-dimensionality or complete reducibility in every genus, especially the included genus $2$, or identify lower-central and any weight grading. Those conditions must not be assumed merely to force a finite decomposition.

The source says Hain [Ha3] gives an explicit, reasonably simple presentation of $\mathfrak t_g$ when $g>6$, while the representation problem still appears involved. The strict range $g>6$ is retained. Its trailing discussion credits Chris Leininger with noting natural virtual versions of preceding questions, including classification of normal subgroups of finite-index subgroups of $\operatorname{Mod}_g$, and classification of virtually normal $H\leq\operatorname{Mod}_g$, defined by \[ [\operatorname{Mod}_g:N_{\operatorname{Mod}_g}(H)]<\infty. \] These are contextual variations, not a restriction of the main representation problem to finite-index subgroups or to large genus.

Short English statement

For every genus $g\geq2$ and every graded degree, determine the irreducible symplectic constituents of the Torelli group's Malcev Lie algebra. Hain's reported presentation for $g>6$ does not itself supply those decompositions; low-genus and representation-category conventions need checking.
