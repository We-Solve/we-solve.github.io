Scalar Curvature Question [?82]: C0-ContinuousGuth-GerochLowerVolume Bound for Balls in the Coverings of Essential Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual closed oriented convention, an $n$-manifold $X$ is ${\mathbb{Q}}$-essential if its classifying map $c:X\to B\pi_1(X)$ satisfies $c_*[X]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\ne0$. The source does not spell out closedness, orientation, or alternatives with local coefficients. Let $g$ be a continuous positive-definite Riemannian metric, lift it to $\widetilde X$, and use its induced length distance and Riemannian volume density. Write $\omega_n$ for the Euclidean unit-ball volume.

The proposed $C^0$-continuous Guth--Geroch lower bound is \[ \forall R>0\quad\exists\widetilde x_R\in\widetilde X, \qquad \operatorname{vol}_g B_{\widetilde X}(\widetilde x_R,R) \ge\omega_n R^n. \] The center may depend on $R$. The claim is not a bound for every ball or a single center working at every radius. No scalar-curvature assumption is stated, and continuous metrics are explicitly included.

The passage reports the following rough corollary of Guth's mesoscopic filling-radius theorem: for some dimension-dependent universal $\varepsilon_n>0$, the same quantifiers hold with lower bound $\varepsilon_n\omega_nR^n$. The sharp target is coefficient $1$, rather than merely a positive dimensional constant.

It then states a Burago--Ivanov asymptotic-volume rigidity assertion: if a universal cover of a ${\mathbb{Q}}$-essential $X$ has balls $\widetilde B(R_i)$ with $R_i\to\infty$ and \[ \operatorname{vol}(\widetilde B(R_i))\le\omega_nR_i^n, \] then $X$ is flat, with the radii allowed to depend on the metric. The source footnote says the cited paper concerns $X$ homeomorphic to $T^n$ and claims a reduction of the general case via polynomial-growth group classification. Those broader hypotheses and the reduction are not supplied and are retained as unverified source context, not a checked theorem covering every essential manifold.

Finally the passage claims extensions of the Burago--Ivanov argument, and of Guth's theorem, to non-Riemannian manifolds and pseudomanifolds with arbitrary metrics and suitably defined Hilbert volumes. It explicitly warns that these volumes are not a priori additive. Their definition and the generalized essentialness and volume hypotheses are absent. These extension claims are not used to strengthen the precisely displayed Riemannian conjecture.

Short English statement

Must every universal cover of a rationally essential manifold contain, at each radius, at least one ball with Euclidean-or-greater volume, even for a continuous metric? The source gives a rough positive-constant bound as motivation and discusses asymptotic rigidity and generalized volumes.

The supplied summary claims torus and low-dimensional progress and mentions Guth and "Abiero/Guth," without exact results. It calls the general conjecture open. Those claims do not verify the all-radii sharp bound or turn the source's asymptotic discussion into a complete proof. The registry partial-progress label and 2017 passage are retained with current status marked for review.
