Math-1071

Combinatorial Interpretation of Stable Kronecker Coefficients

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A partition is a finite weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers; the empty partition is allowed. For a partition $\lambda$, write $|\lambda|$ for the sum of its parts and take $\lambda_1=0$ when $\lambda$ is empty. For an integer $n\geq |\lambda|+\lambda_1$, define the partition of $n$ \[ \lambda[n]=(n-|\lambda|,\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\ldots), \] with zero parts omitted. For partitions $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ of the same integer $n$, let $V^\alpha,V^\beta,V^\gamma$ be the corresponding irreducible complex representations of the symmetric group $S_n$. Their Kronecker coefficient is \[ g(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\operatorname{Hom}_{\S_n}\!\left(V^\gamma,V^\alpha\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}}V^\beta\right), \] where the tensor product has the diagonal $S_n$-action. For arbitrary partitions $\lambda,\mu,\nu$, the reduced, or stable, Kronecker coefficient is the eventual constant value \[ \overline g(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=g\left(\lambda[n],\mu[n],\nu[n]\right)\qquad\text{for all sufficiently large integers }n. \] Here $n$ must be large enough that all three displayed partitions are defined; the three original partitions need not have equal sizes.

Problem. Find a combinatorial interpretation of $\overline g(\lambda,\mu,\nu)$ for every triple of partitions $\lambda,\mu,\nu$, analogous to the Littlewood--Richardson rule. In counting-rule form, seek an explicitly described family of finite combinatorial objects $\mathcal C(\lambda,\mu,\nu)$, specified uniformly from the three partitions, such that \[ \#\mathcal C(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=\overline g(\lambda,\mu,\nu) \qquad\text{for every triple }(\lambda,\mu,\nu). \] The source does not specify a class of admissible objects, a computational complexity requirement, or a precise criterion for being analogous to the Littlewood--Richardson rule; these aspects of the requested interpretation remain unspecified.

Short English statement

Find a general combinatorial counting rule for reduced Kronecker coefficients, which are the eventual tensor-product multiplicities obtained by adding sufficiently long first rows to three arbitrary partitions. The desired rule should be analogous to the Littlewood--Richardson rule, although the source gives no precise requirements for that analogy.
