Debarre’s Minimal Cohomology-Class Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record calls this Debarre's minimal cohomology-class conjecture. It does not specify a ground field or cohomology theory. The following complex formulation makes these choices explicit and requires confirmation against the source.

Let $g,d\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ satisfy $1\leq d\leq g-2$. Let $(A,\Theta)$ be a complex principally polarized abelian variety of dimension $g$, indecomposable in the sense that it is not isomorphic, as a polarized abelian variety, to a product of two positive-dimensional principally polarized abelian varieties. Write $\theta=c_1(\mathcal O_A(\Theta))$. Let $X\subset A$ be a reduced closed subvariety all of whose irreducible components have dimension $d$. Its cohomology class $[X]$ is the Poincaré dual of its fundamental cycle. Assume the minimal-class equality \[ [X]=\frac{\theta^{g-d}}{(g-d)!}\qquad\text{in }H^{2(g-d)}(A,{\mathbb{Q}}). \] For a smooth connected projective complex curve $C$ of genus $g$, let $J(C)=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$, with its canonical principal polarization $\Theta_C$. After choosing $p_0\in C$, define \[ W_d(C)=\left\{\mathcal O_C(D-dp_0):D\text{ is an effective divisor of degree }d\right\}\subset J(C). \] Changing $p_0$ changes this subvariety by translation. For a smooth cubic threefold $Y\subset{\mathbb{P}}^4_{{\mathbb{C}}}$, let $F(Y)$ be the surface parametrizing lines on $Y$, let $J(Y)$ be its intermediate Jacobian with canonical principal polarization $\Theta_Y$, and let $\iota_Y:F(Y)\to J(Y)$ denote an Abel--Jacobi embedding, whose choice must be specified when interpreting the conclusion.

Question as stated in the corrected registry formulation. Must at least one of the following hold?

  1. There exist such a curve $C$, a polarized isomorphism $\varphi:(A,\Theta)\to(J(C),\Theta_C)$, and $a\in J(C)$ such that $\varphi(X)=a+W_d(C)$.
  2. One has $g=5$ and $d=2$, and there exist a smooth cubic threefold $Y$, a polarized isomorphism $\varphi:(A,\Theta)\to(J(Y),\Theta_Y)$, and $a\in J(Y)$ such that $\varphi(X)=a+\iota_Y(F(Y))$.
The supplied wording does not specify whether inversion of these embedded subvarieties is allowed or absorbed into the polarized isomorphisms and embedding choices. This convention requires review. The original statement uses an equivalence and calls $W_d(C)$ a curve; the corrected statement instead asks the displayed classification implication and allows arbitrary $d$ in the stated range. The question above follows that corrected statement, without asserting the converse.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every reduced, pure-dimensional subvariety of minimal cohomology class in an indecomposable principally polarized abelian variety must arise from effective divisors on a curve, or from the surface of lines on a smooth cubic threefold. The field, cohomology conventions, embedding signs, and difference between the original and corrected formulations require human review.
