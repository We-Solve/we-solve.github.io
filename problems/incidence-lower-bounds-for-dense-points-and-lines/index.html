Math-15122

Incidence Lower Bounds for Dense Points and Lines

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer and let $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ satisfy \[ |P|=n,\qquad \min_{\substack{p,q\in P\\p\neq q}}\|p-q\|_2=1. \] Write $K=\operatorname{conv}(P)$ and $\operatorname{diam}(P)=\max_{p,q\in P}\|p-q\|_2$. The supplied density hypothesis is $\operatorname{diam}(P)=O(\sqrt n)$. Let $L$ be a set of $n$ affine lines in ${\mathbb{R}}^2$. For a point $p$ and a line $\ell$, put \[ \operatorname{dist}(p,\ell)=\inf_{x\in\ell}\|p-x\|_2, \qquad I_n(P,L)=\left|\left\{(p,\ell)\in P\times L:\operatorname{dist}(p,\ell)\leq n^{-1/2}\right\}\right|. \] The source requires the lines to be pairwise essentially different, described as angular separation $1/n$ or disjoint $1/\sqrt n$-neighborhoods inside $K$. Denote this intended pairwise relation by $E_n(\ell,\ell';K)$. This notation is a placeholder: the supplied record does not give an unambiguous complete definition of that relation. In particular, it does not specify the angular convention, the precise threshold inequality, whether the neighborhoods are open or closed, or how lines whose neighborhoods miss $K$ are treated.

Source question. Under these hypotheses, must \[ I_n(P,L)=\Omega(n^{4/3}) \] hold?

A possible uniform interpretation of the asymptotic quantifiers is the following: for every fixed $C>0$, do there exist $c(C)>0$ and an integer $n_0(C)\geq2$ such that, for every integer $n\geq n_0(C)$, every such $P$ with $\operatorname{diam}(P)\leq C\sqrt n$, and every such $L$ with $E_n(\ell,\ell';K)$ for all distinct $\ell,\ell'\in L$, one has \[ I_n(P,L)\geq c(C)n^{4/3}? \] This quantifier convention is explicit but provisional; the record does not specify the dependence of the asymptotic constants. No condition requiring a line or its neighborhood to meet $K$ is supplied. The exact intended question therefore remains unresolved until the essential-difference relation and any missing line-location hypotheses are recovered. The supplied literature assessment concerns a different nerve question and does not substantiate this incidence question.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether $n$ separated planar points of diameter $O(\sqrt n)$, together with $n$ suitably distinct lines, must determine $\Omega(n^{4/3})$ point--line pairs at distance at most $n^{-1/2}$. The precise distinction condition for lines and possible line-location assumptions are incomplete. The supplied open-status assessment discusses a different problem.
