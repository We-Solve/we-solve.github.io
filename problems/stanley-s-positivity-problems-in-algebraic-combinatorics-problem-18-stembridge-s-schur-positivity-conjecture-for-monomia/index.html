Stanley's Positivity Problems in Algebraic Combinatorics — Problem 18: Stembridge's Schur-positivity conjecture for monomial immanants of Jacobi-Trudi matrices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer. A partition $\alpha$ is a weakly decreasing finite sequence of positive integers; write $|\alpha|$ for its size and $\ell(\alpha)$ for its length, and append zero parts when needed. For partitions $\nu,\mu$, write $\nu\subseteq\mu$ when $\nu_i\leq\mu_i$ for every $i$.

Work in the ring of symmetric functions over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ in the variables $x_1,x_2,\ldots$. Let $h_r$ be the complete homogeneous symmetric function of degree $r$, with $h_0=1$ and $h_r=0$ for $r<0$. Let $m_\alpha$, $s_\alpha$, and $p_\alpha$ denote the monomial, Schur, and power-sum symmetric functions, respectively.

To specify the monomial immanant, use the Frobenius characteristic convention \[ \operatorname{ch}(f)=\sum_{\rho\vdash n}\frac{f(\rho)}{z_\rho}p_\rho, \qquad z_\rho=\prod_{r\geq1}r^{a_r}a_r!, \] where $f$ is a class function on the symmetric group $S_n$, $f(\rho)$ is its value on permutations of cycle type $\rho$, and $a_r$ is the multiplicity of $r$ in $\rho$. Define $\phi^\lambda=\operatorname{ch}^{-1}(m_\lambda)$ for $\lambda\vdash n$. For an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ over a commutative ring, define \[ \operatorname{Imm}_{\phi^\lambda}(A) =\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\phi^\lambda(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^{n}A_{i,\sigma(i)}. \] The supplied record does not explicitly define $\phi^\lambda$; this is the standard Frobenius-characteristic interpretation of its monomial-immanant notation and requires confirmation against the cited sources.

For every pair of partitions $\nu\subseteq\mu$ with $\ell(\mu)\leq n$, form the Jacobi--Trudi matrix \[ H_{\mu/\nu}=\left[h_{\mu_i-\nu_j+j-i}\right]_{i,j=1}^{n}. \] The conjecture asks whether, for every such $n,\mu,\nu$ and every $\lambda\vdash n$, there exist coefficients $c_\theta\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ such that \[ \operatorname{Imm}_{\phi^\lambda}(H_{\mu/\nu}) =\sum_{\theta\vdash |\mu|-|\nu|}c_\theta s_\theta. \] Here the empty partition is allowed in degree zero, with $s_{\varnothing}=1$. This nonnegative Schur expansion is the meaning of Schur-positivity. The supplied registry labels the conjecture open; that label is not an independently verified assessment.

Short English statement

Stembridge's conjecture, listed as Stanley's Problem 18, asks whether every monomial immanant of every skew Jacobi--Trudi matrix has nonnegative integer coefficients in its Schur expansion. The draft makes the usual Frobenius-characteristic convention explicit; the supplied record does not specify that convention.
