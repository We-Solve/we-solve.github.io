Open Problems in Pluripotential Theory — Question 31 (Tosatti-Weinkove)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a Hermitian manifold of complex dimension $n$, with Hermitian form $\omega$. No closedness assumption on $\omega$ is supplied. To make the normalization issue explicit, write \[ dd^c=a\,\mathrm{i}\partial\bar\partial,\qquad a>0. \] For a fixed such convention, an $\omega$-plurisubharmonic function is an upper semicontinuous, locally integrable function $\phi:X\to[-\infty,\infty)$ satisfying \[ \omega+dd^c\phi\geq 0 \] in the sense of currents. The supplied record does not specify $a$; the question below is to be interpreted with the intended source normalization, whose identification remains unresolved, and is not asserted for every $a$.

Let $k\geq1$, let $z_1,\ldots,z_k\in X$, and let $\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_k>0$ satisfy \[ \sum_{j=1}^{k}\tau_j^n<\int_X\omega^n. \] The question is whether, for every such collection, there exists an $\omega$-plurisubharmonic function $\phi$ with \[ \phi(z)\leq\tau_j\log\lVert z-z_j\rVert+O(1) \qquad\text{near }z_j,\quad 1\leq j\leq k. \] Precisely, the local condition means that for each $j$ there are holomorphic coordinates $w_j$ centered at $z_j$, a radius $r_j>0$, and a constant $C_j\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that \[ \phi(x)\leq\tau_j\log\lVert w_j(x)\rVert+C_j \qquad\text{whenever }0<\lVert w_j(x)\rVert<r_j. \] Here the norm is the Euclidean norm in the coordinate space; changing centered holomorphic coordinates changes the logarithm by a bounded term.

The supplied statement does not specify compactness, connectedness, boundary conditions, finiteness of $\int_X\omega^n$, or pairwise distinctness of the marked points. These qualifications remain unresolved; no compactness, distinctness, or additional geometric hypothesis has been inserted.

Short English statement

Given marked points on a Hermitian manifold and positive weights whose $n$th powers sum to less than $\int_X\omega^n$, construct an $\omega$-plurisubharmonic function with at least the prescribed logarithmic singularities. The source labels this question open. The intended geometric hypotheses and normalization require human review.
