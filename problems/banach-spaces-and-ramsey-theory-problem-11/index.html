Banach Spaces and Ramsey Theory — Problem 11

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a scalar field $\mathbb K\in\{{\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}}\}$. The supplied record does not specify which scalar field is intended. Let $(X,\|\cdot\|)$ be an infinite-dimensional Banach space over $\mathbb K$, and let $\mathcal S_\infty(X)$ denote its infinite-dimensional closed linear subspaces. A norm $p$ on $X$ is equivalent to $\|\cdot\|$, written $p\sim\|\cdot\|$, if there exist constants $0<m\le M<\infty$ such that \[ m\|x\|\le p(x)\le M\|x\|\qquad(x\in X). \] For such a norm and a subspace $Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X)$, define \[ \Delta_Y(p)=\sup\left\{\frac{p(y)}{p(z)}:y,z\in Y,\ \|y\|=\|z\|=1\right\}. \] Use the following distortion terminology: $X$ is distortable if \[ \exists a>1\ \exists p\sim\|\cdot\|\ \forall Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(p)>a, \] and arbitrarily distortable if \[ \forall a>1\ \exists p\sim\|\cdot\|\ \forall Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(p)>a. \] Interpret boundedly distortable as distortable but not arbitrarily distortable. Thus the proposed statement of the question is: does there exist an infinite-dimensional Banach space $(X,\|\cdot\|)$ satisfying both \[ \exists a>1\ \exists p\sim\|\cdot\|\ \forall Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(p)>a \] and \[ \exists C>1\ \forall q\sim\|\cdot\|\ \exists Y\in\mathcal S_\infty(X),\qquad \Delta_Y(q)\le C? \] The bound $C$ is independent of the equivalent norm $q$; the subspace $Y$ may depend on $q$.

The supplied excerpt does not define bounded distortion and refers to the unidentified reference [OS-new]. Consequently, the interpretation above requires comparison with that reference. Strict distortion thresholds are used here; replacing them throughout by non-strict thresholds does not change the existential distinction between distortability and arbitrary distortability. The intended scalar-field scope also remains unresolved.

The source introduction describes it as a surprising phenomenon observed so far that "most" distortable Banach spaces are arbitrarily distortable. This is supplied qualitative motivation, with no specified mathematical meaning for "most", and is not an additional hypothesis.

Short English statement

Under the stated interpretation, the question asks whether a Banach space can admit an equivalent norm whose distortion persists on every infinite-dimensional subspace, while having a fixed finite bound such that every equivalent norm has some infinite-dimensional subspace with distortion at most that bound. The intended scalar field and the definition referenced as [OS-new] require source review.
