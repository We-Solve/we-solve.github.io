Left Exactness of n-Motivic Sheaf Inclusions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathcal H=HI(k)$, $\mathcal H_n=HI^{\leq n}(k)$, and let $I_n:\mathcal H_n\hookrightarrow\mathcal H$ denote the inclusion appearing in the supplied record. The record associates $\mathcal H_n$ with $n$-motivic sheaves, but does not define either category, specify the hypotheses on $k$, give the range of $n$, or state the topology, coefficient ring, or transfer conventions. These are unresolved prerequisites to a fully specified statement; the notation here retains the source's unspecified choices.

The principal question is whether $I_n$ is left exact. Under the categorical convention that left exactness means preservation of finite limits, this asks whether, for every finite diagram $D:J\to\mathcal H_n$, the comparison morphism \[ I_n\!\left(\varprojlim_J D\right)\longrightarrow\varprojlim_J(I_n\circ D) \] is an isomorphism, assuming the relevant finite limits exist. The existence of these limits and the intended exactness convention must be checked against the source definitions.

The original statement additionally asks whether the inclusion admits a left adjoint \[ L_n=(-)^{\leq n}:\mathcal H\longrightarrow\mathcal H_n. \] Precisely, this asks for a functor $L_n$ and bijections \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal H_n}(L_nF,G)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal H}(F,I_nG) \] natural in every $F\in\mathcal H$ and $G\in\mathcal H_n$. The supplied original statement presents this as a stronger question. That source-specific characterization is retained, but its relationship to the first question requires the missing categorical hypotheses and conventions.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether the stated inclusion of $n$-motivic sheaves preserves finite limits, and additionally whether it has a left adjoint. The definitions of the categories, the hypotheses on $k$, and the range of $n$ are missing from the supplied record, so this is a conditional formulation requiring source clarification and human mathematical review.
