Extremal Copositive Rays and Completely Positive Factorizations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each positive integer $n$, let $\mathbb S^n=\{A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n\times n}:A^{\mathsf T}=A\}$, with inner product $\langle M,A\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(MA)$, and put ${\mathbb{R}}_+^n=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:x_j\geq0\text{ for }1\leq j\leq n\}$. Define the copositive cone and the completely positive cone by \[ \operatorname{COP}_n=\{M\in\mathbb S^n:x^{\mathsf T}Mx\geq0\text{ for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}_+^n\}, \] \[ \operatorname{CP}_n=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{r}b_i b_i^{\mathsf T}:r\in{\mathbb{N}},\ r\geq1,\ b_i\in{\mathbb{R}}_+^n\text{ for every }i\right\}. \] Zero vectors are allowed among the $b_i$, so this definition includes the zero matrix.

For a convex cone $K\subseteq\mathbb S^n$ and a nonzero $M\in K$, the ray ${\mathbb{R}}_+M=\{tM:t\geq0\}$ is an extreme ray if, whenever $M=U+V$ with $U,V\in K$, both $U$ and $V$ belong to ${\mathbb{R}}_+M$. For $Y\in\mathbb S^n\setminus\{0\}$ and $\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}$, write \[ H(Y,\alpha)=\{A\in\mathbb S^n:\langle Y,A\rangle=\alpha\}. \] This is a supporting hyperplane of $K$ if $K\cap H(Y,\alpha)\neq\varnothing$ and, after possibly replacing $(Y,\alpha)$ by $(-Y,-\alpha)$, one has $\langle Y,A\rangle\geq\alpha$ for every $A\in K$.

The supplied problem asks for the following descriptions and construction in general dimension:

  1. Give a complete characterization of the nonzero matrices $M\in\operatorname{COP}_n$ whose rays ${\mathbb{R}}_+M$ are extreme, identifying matrices that differ by a positive scalar.
  2. Give a complete characterization of the supporting hyperplanes of $\operatorname{CP}_n$.
  3. Given an arbitrary $A\in\operatorname{CP}_n$, explicitly construct a finite integer $r\geq1$ and vectors $b_1,\ldots,b_r\in{\mathbb{R}}_+^n$ such that \[ A=\sum_{i=1}^{r}b_i b_i^{\mathsf T}. \]
The third request concerns explicit construction of a representation whose existence is part of the definition of complete positivity. The source does not specify an input encoding, an exact or approximate computational model, a complexity requirement, a prescribed value of $r$, or a minimality requirement. No rationality condition on $A$ or the factors is stated.

The phrase "in general dimension" is interpreted here as requesting a description valid for every positive integer $n$; the source gives no dimension restriction. The required form of a "complete characterization" is unspecified. In particular, the source does not explain whether the supporting-hyperplane request concerns all supporting hyperplanes in the definition above, distinguished supporting hyperplanes, or an explicit structural description of their normal matrices. These qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

For arbitrary dimension, describe all extreme rays of the copositive cone and all supporting hyperplanes of the completely positive cone, and construct a nonnegative vector factorization of any given completely positive matrix. The source leaves the required form of the descriptions and the meaning of explicit construction unspecified. Its open status is a supplied dated assessment, not an independently verified conclusion.
