Resolvent Degree of Polynomial Root Covers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify a base field or its characteristic. The following formulation makes that missing parameter explicit; it does not assert that every base field is intended.

Fix a field $k$ and a positive integer $n$. Let \[ \mathcal P_{n,k}=\mathbb A_k^n, \qquad p_a(T)=T^n+a_1T^{n-1}+\cdots+a_n, \] and define the incidence variety \[ \widetilde{\mathcal P}_{n,k} =\{(a,t)\in\mathbb A_k^n\times\mathbb A_k^1:p_a(t)=0\}. \] The projection $\pi_{n,k}(a,t)=a$ is the finite morphism recording a monic polynomial and forgetting its chosen root. It includes polynomials with repeated roots; its restriction to the locus of nonzero discriminant is finite étale. The resolvent degree below concerns the generic polynomial, so it imposes no requirement that a root-selection construction extend over the discriminant locus.

Here is an algebraic field-tower definition of the invariant, conditional on the choice of $k$. Put \[ K=k(a_1,\ldots,a_n),\qquad L=K(\alpha),\qquad p_a(\alpha)=0, \] where the coefficients are algebraically independent over $k$ and $\alpha$ is a root of the generic polynomial. Define $\operatorname{RD}_k(L/K)$ to be the least nonnegative integer $d$ for which there is a tower of finite field extensions \[ K=K_0\subset K_1\subset\cdots\subset K_r \] and a $K$-embedding $L\hookrightarrow K_r$, with the following property: for every $i$, there are finitely generated field extensions $F_i/k$ and $E_i/k$, with $E_i/F_i$ finite and $\operatorname{trdeg}_k F_i\le d$, and compatible $k$-embeddings $F_i\hookrightarrow K_{i-1}$ and $E_i\hookrightarrow K_i$, such that $K_i=K_{i-1}E_i$. Set \[ \operatorname{RD}_k(\widetilde{\mathcal P}_{n,k}/\mathcal P_{n,k}) =\operatorname{RD}_k(L/K). \]

The requested problem is to determine this resolvent degree as a function of $n$, once the intended base field and conventions have been fixed. The record additionally asks to decide the conjectures it calls Hilbert's sextic, thirteenth, and octic conjectures. It supplies neither their numerical target values nor their precise formulations, so no particular equalities or inequalities for those named conjectures can be stated from this record alone. The base-field qualification and the missing conjecture formulations remain unresolved.

Short English statement

Determine how many independent parameters are needed in successive algebraic auxiliary problems to obtain a root of a generic monic polynomial of degree $n$. The source also requests decisions on Hilbert's sextic, thirteenth, and octic conjectures, but leaves the base field and the precise conjectural assertions unspecified.
