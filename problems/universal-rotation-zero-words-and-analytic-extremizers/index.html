Math-3491

Universal Rotation-Zero Words and Analytic Extremizers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Identify the oriented circle with $S^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$. Model the universal covering group of $\operatorname{Homeo}^+(S^1)$ by \[ G=\{f:{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{R}}\mid f\text{ is an increasing homeomorphism and }f(x+1)=f(x)+1\text{ for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}\}, \] with composition as the group operation. For $f\in G$, its lifted rotation number, also called its translation number, is \[ \operatorname{rot}_{\mathrm{lift}}(f)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f^n(x)-x}{n}\in{\mathbb{R}}, \] where $f^n$ denotes the $n$th iterate; the limit exists and is independent of $x$. This is a real number, rather than its class modulo ${\mathbb{Z}}$. Let $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ be the free group on two generators, with identity $e$. A representation into $G$ means a group homomorphism $\rho:F_2\to G$.

The first question is whether \[ \exists w\in F_2\setminus\{e\}\quad\forall\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(F_2,G),\qquad \operatorname{rot}_{\mathrm{lift}}\left(\rho(w)\right)=0. \]

The second question is reported as follows: for arbitrary $w\in F_2$, does there exist a real-analytic representation $\rho$ such that \[ \operatorname{rot}\left(\rho(w)\right)=R(w,r,s)? \] The supplied record does not define $R(w,r,s)$, give the domains or quantifiers for $r,s$, or specify any constraints relating them to the images of $a,b$. It also does not specify the target and precise regularity requirements of a real-analytic representation, or explicitly identify the second question's $\operatorname{rot}$ with the lifted rotation number defined above. Thus this second displayed question preserves the supplied formulation but is not a fully specified mathematical assertion. In particular, interpreting $R$ as an extremal value under prescribed generator rotation numbers would require additional source information.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether a nonidentity word in the free group on two generators has lifted rotation number zero under every representation into the universal covering group of orientation-preserving circle homeomorphisms. The second asks for a real-analytic realization of the value $R(w,r,s)$ for arbitrary $w$, but the supplied record leaves that value, its parameters, and the analytic representation requirements undefined.
