Math-3975

Index Divisibility in p-Power Pullbacks of Curves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $k/{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ be a finite extension, and fix an algebraic closure $\bar k$. Let $A/k$ be an abelian variety and let $i:X\hookrightarrow A$ be a closed embedding over $k$, where $X/k$ is a smooth projective curve of genus at least $2$.

Define the prime-to-$p$ geometric torsion subgroup by \[ A(\bar k)_{\mathrm{tors},p'} =\{a\in A(\bar k):[m]a=0\text{ for some integer }m\geq 1\text{ with }\gcd(m,p)=1\}. \] Read the source hypothesis that $X$ contains no torsion points of order prime to $p$ as \[ i\left(X(\bar k)\right)\cap A(\bar k)_{\mathrm{tors},p'}=\varnothing. \] This uses the geometric interpretation of the torsion hypothesis; the supplied excerpt does not explicitly distinguish geometric torsion points from $k$-rational torsion points.

For every integer $n\geq 0$, let $[p^n]:A\to A$ denote multiplication by $p^n$, and define the scheme-theoretic inverse image \[ X_n=A\mathop{\times}_{A}X, \] where the maps to the common base $A$ are $[p^n]$ on the first factor and $i$ on the second factor. For a nonempty proper scheme $Y/k$, define its index by \[ \operatorname{ind}_k(Y) =\gcd\{[\kappa(y):k]:y\in Y\text{ is a closed point}\}, \] where $\kappa(y)$ is the residue field of $y$. This definition does not require $X_n$ to be connected.

Conjecture 1 (supplied source). Under the preceding hypotheses, does there exist an integer $N\geq 0$, allowed to depend on the given data, such that \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\qquad n\geq N\ \Longrightarrow\ p\mid\operatorname{ind}_k(X_n)? \] Equivalently, for every $n\geq N$, every closed point $x\in X_n$ must satisfy $p\mid[\kappa(x):k]$.

Source-reported partial result and qualifications. The excerpt reports that for every $r>0$, there exists a sufficiently large integer $n_r$ such that every closed point of $X_{n_r}$ having ramification index at most $r$ has degree divisible by $p$. Under the convention that ramification index is measured relative to $k$, this reads \[ \forall r>0\ \exists n_r\geq 0\ \forall x\in X_{n_r}\text{ closed},\qquad e(\kappa(x)/k)\leq r\ \Longrightarrow\ p\mid[\kappa(x):k]. \] The excerpt does not specify the reference field for the ramification index; this displayed formulation records the relative convention provisionally. The reported bound concerns points with bounded ramification and is not asserted here to establish the conjecture.

The excerpt also states consequences conditional on Conjecture 1 concerning toric Galois sections over finite extensions of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$, Selmer Galois sections over number fields, and standard Galois sections. Their notation is corrupted in the supplied transcription, and the statement labelled Theorem 3 is truncated after the introduction of a finite $\acute{e}$tale cover $Y\to X$. These consequences are retained as conditional source context; their full mathematical statements cannot be reconstructed reliably from the supplied text.

Short English statement

For a curve of genus at least $2$ embedded in an abelian variety over a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$, assume that the curve contains no prime-to-$p$ torsion point, interpreted geometrically. The conjecture asks whether every closed point of every sufficiently deep inverse image under multiplication by $p^n$ has degree divisible by $p$. The supplied source reports a partial result for points of bounded ramification and makes its subsequent section results conditional on this conjecture. This is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
