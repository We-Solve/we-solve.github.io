Math-5710

Random lattice embeddings with exponential intersection tails

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Give ${\mathbb{Z}}^k$ its nearest-neighbor graph structure. Benjamini asks whether, for some finite integer $d\geq3$, there is a probability measure $\mu$ on embeddings of ${\mathbb{Z}}^2$ into ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$ with an analogue of exponential intersection tails.

For two independent samples $\phi,\psi\sim\mu$, the ordinary counting form of an exponential intersection-tail condition would be \[ \Pr(I(\phi,\psi)\geq n)\leq C e^{-cn}\qquad(n\geq1) \] for constants $C<\infty$, $c>0$, and a specified intersection statistic $I$. For example, one could count common image vertices or common image edges, but these are different conditions and neither is identified by the excerpt as the intended two-dimensional analogue. The display explains the usual tail terminology rather than selecting an unsupplied definition.

The embedding notion is likewise unspecified: an injective nearest-neighbor graph map, a topological embedding using edge paths, and a metric embedding are different possibilities. Root normalization and how the two embeddings are placed relative to each other also matter. The source does not give these conventions or any invariance requirement on $\mu$. It therefore asks for an appropriate analogue and a measure realizing it, not an already defined claim for every dimension or an ordinary one-dimensional path measure.

Short English statement

In at least one finite dimension $d\geq3$, can random embeddings of the whole square lattice have exponentially decaying intersection tails? The embedding convention and the intersection statistic for the requested analogue must be specified.
