Algebraic Stories — Exterior algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let $E=\bigwedge_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C^n$ be the exterior algebra on $n$ degree-one generators, with graded pieces $E_i=\bigwedge^i\mathbb C^n$. For a homogeneous element $f\in E_d$ define \[\operatorname{Ann}_E(f)=\{a\in E:af=0\},\qquad (f)=Ef. \] If $d$ is odd then $f^2=0$, hence $(f)\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_E(f)$. A property holds for a generic form in $E_d$ if it holds on a nonempty Zariski-open subset of $E_d$.

Question in the published source.
For every $n\geq1$ and every odd integer $d$ with $1\leq d\leq n$, does a generic form $f\in E_d$ satisfy \[(\operatorname{Ann}_E(f))_i=(f)_i\qquad\text{for every integer }0\leq i<(n-d)/2?\] [S2]

Short English statement

Does a generic odd exterior form have only the annihilators generated by itself below the stated degree threshold?
