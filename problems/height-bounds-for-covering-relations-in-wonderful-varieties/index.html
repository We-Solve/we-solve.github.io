Height Bounds for Covering Relations in Wonderful Varieties

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied background identifies the intended scope as all wonderful varieties, but does not specify their ground field, characteristic, acting group, or precise class. Let $X$ range over that intended class. Write $\Sigma_X$ for the unspecified set denoted by $\Sigma$ in the source. Its formal additive monoid is \[ \mathbb N[\Sigma_X]=\left\{\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_X}n_\sigma\sigma:\ n_\sigma\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0},\quad n_\sigma=0\text{ for all but finitely many }\sigma\right\}. \] Here the use of nonnegative integer coefficients is an explicit convention for the formal monoid notation. Let $\mathcal C_X\subseteq\mathbb N[\Sigma_X]$ denote the elements called covering relations in the source, and retain its notation $\operatorname{ht}^{+}$ for positive height. These are placeholders: the supplied record does not define the covering relations, their underlying ordered set, or the positive-height function.

The requested bound, with the universal scope indicated by the supplied background, is \[ \forall X\ \forall\gamma\in\mathcal C_X,\qquad \operatorname{ht}^{+}(\gamma)\leq 2. \] This is a statement schema requiring completion from the original mathematical context. In particular, it cannot be interpreted as a fully specified conjecture until the class of $X$, the meaning of $\Sigma_X$, the covering relation, and the definition and normalization of positive height are supplied. No identification of positive height with the coefficient sum in $\mathbb N[\Sigma_X]$ is justified by this record.

Short English statement

The proposed universal bound says that every covering relation associated with a wonderful variety has positive height at most $2$. The supplied record leaves the relevant structures and height function undefined, so the precise conjecture requires clarification from its source context.
