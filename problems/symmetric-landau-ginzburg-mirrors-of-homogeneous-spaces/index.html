Symmetric Landau–Ginzburg Mirrors of Homogeneous Spaces

The supplied record concerns a homogeneous space $X$ and an associated Landau--Ginzburg mirror. A homogeneous space is a space equipped with a transitive group action; the record does not specify the group, stabilizer, ground field, geometric category, or class of homogeneous spaces intended. Write $(Y,W)$ schematically for the mirror space and its potential. This notation does not supply a construction of the mirror or specify whether its data are algebraic or analytic.

Source question. For a homogeneous space $X$ and its Landau--Ginzburg mirror in the intended, unspecified class, determine whether:

  1. their relationship admits a more symmetric formulation;
  2. the mirror admits coordinates naturally related to the cohomology of $X$;
  3. the indicated cohomological map extends to an isomorphism of bundles with connection.
The first two requests have no supplied mathematical criterion for symmetry, naturality, or the required relation between coordinates and cohomology.

For the third request, the meaning of an isomorphism of bundles with connection can be stated conditionally. Once a common parameter base $S$, bundles $\mathcal E_A,\mathcal E_B$ on $S$, and connections $\nabla_A,\nabla_B$ have been specified, such an isomorphism is an invertible $\mathcal O_S$-linear map \[ \Phi\colon\mathcal E_A\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal E_B \quad\text{such that}\quad \nabla_B\circ\Phi=(\Phi\otimes\mathrm{id})\circ\nabla_A. \] Here a connection satisfies the Leibniz rule $\nabla(fs)=f\nabla s+s\otimes{\,\mathrm{d}} f$, with any permitted poles requiring separate specification. Extending $\phi$ would additionally require specified identifications with fibers or restrictions of these bundles and agreement of $\Phi$ with $\phi$ under those identifications. None of these data, nor any boundary or singularity conditions, are supplied. This is therefore a conditional description of the requested compatibility, not a fully specified universal conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a homogeneous space and its Landau--Ginzburg mirror admit a more symmetric description, coordinates linked naturally to cohomology, and a cohomological correspondence extending to an isomorphism that preserves connections. The intended spaces, cohomology theories, map, parameter base, and connections are unspecified, so the record does not determine a complete precise conjecture. Its partially solved label and claims about selected examples are supplied curation requiring independent review.
