Perturbation Stability of Jiang–Su Absorption for $C^*$-Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal Z$ denote the Jiang--Su $C^*$-algebra. A $C^*$-algebra $A$ is separable if it has a countable norm-dense subset. Tensorial absorption of $\mathcal Z$, also called $\mathcal Z$-stability, means that there is a $C^*$-algebra isomorphism $A\cong A\otimes\mathcal Z$.

The supplied question does not define $d(A,B)$, specify a common ambient algebra, or specify a tensor-product convention. A customary interpretation is the Kadison--Kastler distance for concrete $C^*$-subalgebras $A,B$ of a common $C^*$-algebra $C$. Writing $A_1=\{a\in A:\|a\|\leq 1\}$ and similarly for $B_1$, this distance is \[ d_{\mathrm{KK}}^C(A,B)=\max\left\{ \sup_{a\in A_1}\inf_{b\in B_1}\|a-b\|, \sup_{b\in B_1}\inf_{a\in A_1}\|b-a\| \right\}. \] Under this interpretation, and taking $\otimes$ to mean the spatial $C^*$-tensor product, the question is whether \[ \begin{gathered} \exists\delta>0\ \ \forall\ C\text{ a }C^*\text{-algebra}\ \ \forall\ A,B\subseteq C\text{ }C^*\text{-subalgebras},\\ \left[A\text{ is separable},\quad A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z,\quad d_{\mathrm{KK}}^C(A,B)<\delta\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ B\cong B\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z. \end{gathered} \] Here $\delta$ is uniform over the algebras and their common realizations. No separate separability assumption on $B$, and no unitality or nuclearity assumption on $A$ or $B$, is stated in the supplied question. The displayed interpretation of the distance and ambient quantifiers requires confirmation against the original source; it is not a verified transcription of conventions from that report.

Short English statement

The question asks whether one universal positive perturbation bound guarantees that every sufficiently close $C^*$-algebra to a separable $\mathcal Z$-stable algebra is also $\mathcal Z$-stable. The distance and ambient conventions are missing from the supplied statement; the formulation above explicitly uses the customary Kadison--Kastler interpretation, subject to source confirmation.
