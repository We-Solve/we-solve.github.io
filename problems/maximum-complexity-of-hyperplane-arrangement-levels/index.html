Math-4480

Maximum Complexity of Hyperplane Arrangement Levels

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An affine hyperplane in ${\mathbb{R}}^d$ is a set $H=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:a\cdot x=b\}$, where $a\ne0$. An arrangement $\mathcal A$ of $n$ hyperplanes is the subdivision induced by those hyperplanes.

The supplied problem asks for the correct asymptotic order of the maximum size $f_k^{(d)}(n)$ of the $k$-level of such an arrangement. The record does not specify the convention for a level, the meaning of its size, or the admissible arrangements. Consequently, the following describes a possible level convention explicitly, without asserting that it is the source's intended convention.

Choose a vertical direction $v\ne0$, and suppose that every hyperplane is transverse to $v$. Write $H_i=\{x:a_i\cdot x=b_i\}$, choosing the sign of each equation so that $a_i\cdot v>0$. At a point $x$, the number of hyperplanes strictly below $x$, measured along $v$, is \[ \ell_{\mathcal A,v}(x)=\left|\{i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}:a_i\cdot x>b_i\}\right|. \] One possible zero-based convention, for $k\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, is \[ L_k(\mathcal A;v)=\overline{\left\{x\in\bigcup_{i=1}^nH_i: x\text{ lies on exactly one }H_i,\quad \ell_{\mathcal A,v}(x)=k\right\}}. \] This definition includes boundary points by closure. Other indexing and boundary conventions must be reconciled with the original source before this convention is adopted.

Once an admissible class $\mathfrak A_{n,d}$, a level convention, and a combinatorial size statistic $s$ have been fixed, the intended extremal quantity has the form \[ f_k^{(d)}(n)=\max_{\mathcal A\in\mathfrak A_{n,d}}s\left(L_k(\mathcal A)\right). \] The requested problem is to determine its sharp asymptotic order in the intended parameter range. The source record does not determine whether $s$ counts vertices, top-dimensional faces, or faces of all dimensions; it also leaves general-position assumptions and the asymptotic roles of $d$ and $k$ unspecified. The original statement refers to lower and upper bounds, but neither bound is supplied. Thus a unique, fully quantified extremal statement cannot yet be recovered.

Short English statement

Determine the sharp asymptotic maximum complexity of the $k$-level of an arrangement of $n$ hyperplanes in ${\mathbb{R}}^d$. The supplied record leaves the complexity statistic, arrangement hypotheses, indexing convention, parameter range, and referenced bounds unresolved. The explicit level definition above is conditional and requires source confirmation.
