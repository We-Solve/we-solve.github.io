Math-298

Global conformal invariants and the four-dimensional Q-Pfaffian bridge

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Definitions and mathematical statement

Conjecture 3 in its supplied Deser--Schwimmer interpretation. Let $n$ be even and let $p(g)$ be a universal scalar polynomial Riemannian invariant, formed from complete contractions of covariant derivatives of curvature, of weight $-n$: $p(c^2g)=c^{-n}p(g)$. Assume that for every closed $n$-dimensional Riemannian manifold and every smooth $\omega$, \[ \int_Mp(e^{2\omega}g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_{e^{2\omega}g} =\int_Mp(g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g. \] The conjecture asks for a universal constant $c$, a local conformal invariant $\ell$ of weight $-n$, and a natural vector field $T$ such that \[ p(g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g=c\,\operatorname{Pff}(g) +\ell(g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g +(\operatorname{div}_gT(g))\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g. \] Normalize the Euler form by $\int_M\operatorname{Pff}=\chi(M)$ on oriented closed manifolds; other normalizations change $c$. Pointwise conformal invariance means $\ell(e^{2\omega}g)=e^{-n\omega}\ell(g)$. The last term is an exact divergence and integrates to zero. The original phrase "$S$ as above" has its antecedent outside this extract; the universal complete-contraction and closed even-dimensional scope comes from the research aid. That aid attributes a proof in this scope to Alexakis. No extension to arbitrary nonpolynomial invariants, boundary terms or other signatures is inferred.

Source filtrations and Exercise 6. For curvature monomials of weight $-n$, the source counts $k_\nabla+2k_R=n$ and filters invariant densities by at most $p$ derivatives, denoted $\mathcal P^p$. Thus $\mathcal P^0\subset\mathcal P^2\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal P^{n-2}$ in the intended class, with the Pfaffian in $\mathcal P^0$. The aid interprets "$\mathcal P^{\mathrm{odd}}=0$" as vanishing of odd homogeneous derivative-degree pieces; it cannot mean that odd-index filtered spaces are zero. The source also says exact divergences inject into $\mathcal P^2/\mathcal P^0$; the relevant divergence subspace/quotient conventions are not specified enough here to adopt this as a general assertion for all derivative orders.

For the following curvature formulas let $n\ge4$, $J=R/(2(n-1))$ and $P_{ij}=(\operatorname{Ric}_{ij}-Jg_{ij})/(n-2)$. Write $\Delta_0=\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad}$ and $D=-\Delta_0$, so the sign is explicit. Exercise 6 asks for nontriviality of the top derivative class of $Q_n\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g$ in $\mathcal P^{n-2}/\mathcal P^{n-4}$ and agreement with $D^{(n-2)/2}J\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g$ in the compatible positive-Laplacian convention. The second filtration, $\mathcal L^p$, counts polynomial degree in the conformal factor derivatives in the transformation of a density: local conformal invariant densities lie in $\mathcal L^0$ and $Q$-curvature density in $\mathcal L^1$. The density formulation is essential to the stated linear law.

Supplied four-dimensional normalization dictionary. Put $E=\operatorname{Ric}-(R/4)g$ and let $W$ be the Weyl tensor, with full tensor contraction norms. The aid gives \[ \mathcal E_4=|\operatorname{Riem}|^2-4|\operatorname{Ric}|^2+R^2 =|W|^2-2|E|^2+\frac{R^2}{6}, \] \[ Q_4=-\frac16\Delta_0R-\frac12|E|^2+\frac{R^2}{24},\qquad \operatorname{Pff}_4=\frac{\mathcal E_4}{32\pi^2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g, \qquad \mathcal E_{\mathrm{AIM}}=4\mathcal E_4. \] In particular \[ \mathcal E_4=|W|^2+4Q_4+\frac23\Delta_0R, \] \[ Q_4\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g=8\pi^2\operatorname{Pff}_4 -\frac14|W|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g-\frac16\Delta_0R\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g, \] \[ \operatorname{Pff}_4=\frac1{8\pi^2}Q_4\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g +\frac1{32\pi^2}|W|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g +\frac1{48\pi^2}\Delta_0R\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g. \] These identities convert in both directions between a $Q_4$ decomposition and a normalized Pfaffian decomposition, with the displayed Weyl density locally conformally invariant and $\Delta_0R$ an exact divergence. They do not remove the universal hypotheses of Conjecture 3. The aid's informal phrase "positive Laplacian" is replaced by the explicit $\Delta_0,D$ definitions rather than mixed sign conventions.

Tractor construction and additional formula question in the extract. The standard conformal tractor bundle has a metric-dependent splitting \[ \mathcal T\cong\mathcal E[1]\oplus(T^*M\otimes\mathcal E[1]) \oplus\mathcal E[-1], \] with normal tractor connection, in suppressed density notation, \[ \nabla_a^{\mathcal T}\begin{pmatrix}\sigma\\\mu_b\\\tau\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix} \nabla_a\sigma-\mu_a\\ \nabla_a\mu_b+g_{ab}\tau+P_{ab}\sigma\\ \nabla_a\tau-P_a{}^b\mu_b \end{pmatrix}. \] The source uses $I_g=(2-n,0,J)$ as a section of $\mathcal T[-1]$, and operators $\mathcal B_{n-2}$, written with a section-sign glyph in the extraction, of leading form $D^{n/2-1}$ plus lower-order terms. Its identities are \[ \mathcal B_{n-2}I_g=(0,0,Q_n),\qquad I_{e^{2\omega}g}=I_g+\mathcal D\omega,\qquad \mathcal B_{n-2}\mathcal D\omega=(0,0,P_n\omega), \] where $\mathcal D$ is the tractor $D$ operator and $P_n$ the critical GJMS operator. In dimension four $\mathcal B_2$ is the coupled conformal Laplacian $-\nabla^a\nabla_a+(n-2)R/(4(n-1))$ in the source's convention. The scalar version of the transformation law is \[ e^{n\omega}Q_n(e^{2\omega}g)=Q_n(g)+P_n(g)\omega; \] the source's unweighted-looking $\widehat Q_n=Q_n+P_n\omega$ suppresses density identifications. It further expresses $I_g$ using a nonvanishing conformal scale, the tractor $D$ and fundamental $D$ operators as a logarithmic derivative; the indexed OCR formula is not unambiguously reconstructed. The source explains that these constructions arise from Fefferman--Graham ambient Laplacian powers on an $(n+2)$-dimensional ambient manifold and that tractor expansions give an effective small-dimension algorithm.

For completeness its displayed sixth-order expression, with vertical bars interpreted as covariant derivatives and its four-index $C$ interpreted as Weyl curvature, reads \[ \begin{aligned} Q_6={}&8|\nabla P|^2+16P^{ij}\Delta_0P_{ij} -32P^{ij}P_i{}^kP_{jk}-16|P|^2J+8J^3\\ &-8J\Delta_0J+\Delta_0^2J+16P^{ij}P^{kl}W_{ikjl}. \end{aligned} \] This translation requires checking the source's curvature and derivative conventions. The extract ends by asking for a general or simple inductive formula for $Q_n$, or equivalently in its approach for $\mathcal B_{n-2}$. That additional formula request is distinct from the decomposition theorem; the registry's solved label does not by itself settle every question in this long extract. References to equations (28) and (41) have missing or inconsistent antecedents in the supplied passage.

Short English statement

The main conjecture decomposes a universal global conformal invariant into an Euler term, a pointwise conformal invariant and a divergence; the aid attributes this theorem to Alexakis. The record also discusses derivative filtrations, a normalization-sensitive four-dimensional $Q$--Pfaffian identity, and a separate request for general tractor/$Q$-curvature formulas. Missing antecedents and suppressed density/sign conventions are explicit.
