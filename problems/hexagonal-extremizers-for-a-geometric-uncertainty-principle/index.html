Hexagonal Extremizers for a Geometric Uncertainty Principle

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $Q=[0,1]^2$, let $|E|$ denote planar Lebesgue measure, and write $E\mathbin\triangle F=(E\setminus F)\cup(F\setminus E)$. For $N\in{\mathbb{N}}$, consider a measurable partition $\mathcal P=(\Omega_1,\ldots,\Omega_N)$ of $Q$, with cells disjoint up to sets of measure zero. Require $|\Omega_i|>0$ for every $i$; this restriction is necessary for the displayed ratios, although it is not explicit in the supplied statement. Define \[ \mathcal A(\Omega_i)=\inf_{\substack{B\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2\text{ a disk}\\|B|=|\Omega_i|}}\frac{|\Omega_i\mathbin\triangle B|}{|\Omega_i|},\qquad \mathcal D(\Omega_i)=\frac{|\Omega_i|-\min_{1\le j\le N}|\Omega_j|}{|\Omega_i|}, \] and \[ F(\mathcal P)=\sum_{i=1}^{N}|\Omega_i|\mathcal A(\Omega_i)+\sum_{i=1}^{N}|\Omega_i|\mathcal D(\Omega_i). \] Here the comparison disks are interpreted as arbitrary planar disks; the supplied record imposes no containment requirement. Let $\mathfrak P_N$ be this class of positive-area partitions and put \[ m_N=\inf_{\mathcal P\in\mathfrak P_N}F(\mathcal P). \] Using an infimum does not assert existence of a minimizing partition.

Source question. Is the extremal partition for the stated geometric uncertainty principle a decomposition of $Q$ into hexagons, with boundary modifications? The supplied expanded formulation asks whether the minimum of $F$ is asymptotically attained by such partitions.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the asymptotic parameter, the geometry and sizes of the hexagons, or the admissible boundary modifications. If the intended regime is $N\to\infty$ and asymptotic attainment means vanishing absolute error, a possible precise target is the existence of partitions $\mathcal H_N\in\mathfrak P_N$ of the prescribed hexagonal type such that \[ \lim_{N\to\infty}\left(F(\mathcal H_N)-m_N\right)=0. \] This is a conditional interpretation requiring review, not a uniquely determined formulation supplied by the record. A definition of the hexagonal type and its boundary modifications is still needed. The original wording about an extremal partition also leaves unresolved whether exact finite-partition optimality was intended.

Short English statement

The question asks whether hexagonal decompositions of the unit square, suitably adjusted at its boundary, minimize asymptotically a weighted sum of distance from disks and inequality of cell areas. Positive cell areas are needed to define the functional. The limiting regime and the admissible hexagonal constructions remain unspecified.
