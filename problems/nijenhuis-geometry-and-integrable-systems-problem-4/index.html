Nijenhuis Geometry and Integrable Systems — Problem 4

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks: given a Nijenhuis operator $L$, describe all of its local conservation laws. The excerpt does not define "conservation law" or specify the manifold category and regularity, so a fully determined classification problem requires these qualifications.

For a conditional smooth real formulation, let $M$ be a smooth real manifold and let $L\colon TM\to TM$ be a smooth bundle endomorphism. Its Nijenhuis torsion is \[ N_L(X,Y)=[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]+L^2[X,Y], \] for smooth vector fields $X,Y$. The endomorphism $L$ is a Nijenhuis operator if $N_L(X,Y)=0$ for all such $X,Y$. Its action on a one-form $\alpha$ is defined by \[ (L^*\alpha)(X)=\alpha(LX). \] If a scalar conservation law is intended to mean a function $f$ satisfying $\mathrm d(L^*\mathrm df)=0$, then the requested problem becomes: for every point $p\in M$, determine all local solutions, on sufficiently small open neighborhoods $U$ of $p$, of \[ \mathcal C_L(U)=\left\{f\in C^\infty(U,{\mathbb{R}}):\mathrm d(L^*\mathrm df)=0\right\}. \] If conservation laws are instead represented by one-forms, the corresponding possible target is \[ \left\{\alpha\in\Omega^1(U):\mathrm d\alpha=0,\quad \mathrm d(L^*\alpha)=0\right\}. \] These are explicitly conditional interpretations: the supplied excerpt does not select either definition, specify an equivalence relation, or state whether constants are to be identified. It also does not specify boundary conditions or a smooth, analytic, or holomorphic category.

The source additionally asks the analogous question for arbitrary operators, without the Nijenhuis assumption, and refers to a discussion of superintegrable potentials that is not included in the supplied record.

Short English statement

Describe every local conservation law associated with a given Nijenhuis operator $L$. The excerpt leaves the meaning of conservation law and the geometric category unspecified; the displayed differential equations are conditional formulations requiring review. The source also mentions the analogous question for operators that are not Nijenhuis.
