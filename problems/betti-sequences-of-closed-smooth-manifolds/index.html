Math-4289

Betti Sequences of Closed Smooth Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a closed smooth manifold $M$, meaning a compact smooth manifold without boundary, define its rational Betti numbers by \[ b_i(M;{\mathbb{Q}})=\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H_i(M;{\mathbb{Q}}). \] The coefficient field is not specified in the source; the following is an explicit rational-coefficient interpretation requiring confirmation.

For each fixed positive integer $k$, characterize all tuples \[ (b_0,\ldots,b_{4k})\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}^{\,4k+1}, \qquad b_0=b_{4k}=1,\qquad b_{2k}\equiv 1\pmod 2, \] for which there exists a closed smooth manifold $M$ of dimension $4k$ satisfying \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H_i(M;{\mathbb{Q}})=b_i \qquad\text{for every }i\in\{0,\ldots,4k\}. \] The task is to give necessary and sufficient conditions for this existence. Connectedness and orientability need not be imposed separately in this rational interpretation: they follow from the prescribed zeroth and top Betti numbers. The source does not specify the range of $k$; the formulation above treats positive dimensions, and whether $k=0$ is intended remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Determine which finite sequences of nonnegative integers, with first and last entries equal to one and odd middle entry, are realized as the Betti numbers of a closed smooth manifold of dimension $4k$. Rational coefficients and positive $k$ are explicit provisional interpretations because the source leaves these conventions unstated.
