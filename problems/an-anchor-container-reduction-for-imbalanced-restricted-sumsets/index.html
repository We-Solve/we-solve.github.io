Math-1369

An anchor-container reduction for imbalanced restricted sumsets

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For finite nonempty $A,B\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}$ and $G\subseteq A\times B$, define \[ A+_G B=\{a+b:(a,b)\in G\},\qquad A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\}. \] Tao's question is whether, for every $0<\delta\leq1$ and $C>0$, there exist $c=c(\delta,C)>0$ and $K=K(\delta,C)>0$ with the following property. Whenever $|A|=m>n=|B|\geq1$, $|G|\geq\delta mn$, and \[ |A+_G B|<Cm, \] there are $A'\subseteq A$, $B'\subseteq B$ such that \[ |A'|\geq cm,\qquad |B'|\geq cn,\qquad |A'+B'|\leq Km. \] The constants must be independent of $m,n$, their ratio, the particular integer sets and $G$. The source also asks more generally what structure these hypotheses force, and notes that the balanced case $m=n$ is the Balog--Szemerédi theorem.

The supplied aid reports an anchor-container reduction. With $S=A+_G B$, it gives subsets satisfying \[ |A'|\geq\delta^2m,\qquad |B'|\geq\frac{\delta^2}{2-\delta^2}n,\qquad A'+B'\subseteq S+S-S, \] where $S+S-S=\{s_1+s_2-s_3:s_i\in S\}$. Hence, under the additional growth hypothesis $|S+S-S|\leq L|S|$, it reports the desired conclusion with $c=\delta^2/(2-\delta^2)$ and $K=LC$. To answer the original ratio-free question in this way, $L$ must itself be bounded in terms of $\delta,C$, or be a separately fixed structural parameter.

The aid singles out containment in a bounded-rank generalized arithmetic progression of size $O(m)$. An explicit version avoiding the ambiguity of "size" is \[ S\subseteq P=\{x_0+\textstyle\sum_{j=1}^r t_jv_j:0\leq t_j<L_j,\ t_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}, \quad x_0,v_j\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ L_j\in{\mathbb{N}},\quad \prod_jL_j\leq Dm. \] For fixed $r,D$, the reported container conclusion yields $|A'+B'|\leq3^rDm$. Here the product is parameter-box volume; for a proper progression it equals $|P|$. If the aid intends cardinality for an arbitrary nonproper progression, the precise extra size control needs checking.

Independently the aid reports the mixed energy bound. Define \[ E(A,B)=|\{(a,b,\widetilde a,\widetilde b)\in A\times B\times A\times B: a+b=\widetilde a+\widetilde b\}|. \] The hypotheses give \[ E(A,B)\geq\frac{|G|^2}{|S|}\geq\frac{\delta^2}{C}mn^2. \] The aid says that Tao--Vu's asymmetric Balog--Szemerédi--Gowers theorem supplies a known near-answer with arbitrarily small powers of $m/n$ lost. It does not specify the exact theorem, the locations of those losses, or their constants, so no more precise asymmetric theorem is asserted here. This reported near-answer and the conditional container result do not establish the full ratio-free conclusion.

Short English statement

Tao asks whether a dense graph with a small restricted sumset has large subsets with a small full sumset, using constants independent of the size ratio. The aid gives a threefold-sumset container and an energy reduction, with a ratio-free conclusion under extra growth control.
