Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 8

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold with $b_1(M):=\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}H^1(M;{\mathbb{R}})\geq 1$. The formulation below uses the usual oriented-manifold and cooriented-contact-structure interpretation. The supplied record does not explicitly impose these conventions, so their applicability requires review.

Under this interpretation, fix an orientation of $M$. For an integral homology class $z\in H_2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})$, the Thurston norm is \[ x_M(z)=\min_{[S]=z}\sum_i\max\{0,-\chi(S_i)\}, \] where $S=\bigsqcup_i S_i\subset M$ ranges over embedded closed oriented surfaces representing $z$. Extend this norm to $H_2(M;{\mathbb{R}})$ by homogeneity and continuity. For $a\in H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})$, define the dual Thurston norm by \[ x_M^*(a)=\sup\left\{|\langle a,z\rangle|:z\in H_2(M;{\mathbb{R}}),\ x_M(z)\leq 1\right\}. \] Let $r:H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})\to H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})$ be change of coefficients. Interpret an even integral real cohomology class as a class in $2r(H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}}))$. This interpretation does not select an integral lift or its torsion component.

A cooriented contact structure is a plane distribution $\xi=\ker\alpha\subset TM$, where $\alpha$ is a global smooth $1$-form satisfying $\alpha\wedge\mathrm d\alpha\neq 0$ everywhere. The orientation of $M$ and the coorientation of $\xi$ orient $\xi$, giving its Euler class $e(\xi)\in H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}})$. A contact structure is tight if it contains no overtwisted disk.

The supplied question, under these conventions, asks whether for every such $M$ and every $a\in H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})$, \[ \left(a\in 2r(H^2(M;{\mathbb{Z}}))\ \text{and}\ x_M^*(a)=1\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists\,\xi\ \text{tight and cooriented on }M \quad r(e(\xi))=a. \] The equality here is in real cohomology. The source does not specify whether a particular integral lift must be realized, whether contact structures must be positive relative to a chosen orientation, or whether orientation and coorientation are implicit hypotheses. These qualifications remain unresolved; the display records a conventional reading rather than a verified clarification of the source.

Short English statement

For every closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold with positive first Betti number, must every even integral real cohomology class on the boundary of the dual Thurston norm unit ball be the real Euler class of a tight contact structure? Orientation, coorientation, positivity, and the meaning of integral realization require clarification from the source.
