Math-7307

Rank-One-Isotropy Actions of $S_{5}$ on Spheres

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S_5$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$. For an integer $n\geq 0$, write $S^n=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n+1}:\|x\|=1\}$, with its standard smooth structure. A smooth action is a homomorphism $\rho:S_5\to\operatorname{Diff}(S^n)$. Its isotropy subgroup at $x\in S^n$ is \[ (S_5)_x=\{g\in S_5:\rho(g)(x)=x\}. \] Supplied question. Does $S_5$ admit a smooth action on a sphere with rank-one isotropy?

The supplied record does not define "rank-one isotropy." One possible rank convention for a finite group $H$ is its elementary abelian rank: \[ r_p(H)=\max\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})^k\text{ embeds in }H\},\qquad r(H)=\max_{p\text{ prime}}r_p(H). \] Under this convention, the question would ask for a dimension $n$ and a smooth action satisfying either \[ \forall x\in S^n,\quad r((S_5)_x)=1, \] or, if "rank-one isotropy" means isotropy of rank at most one, \[ \forall x\in S^n,\quad r((S_5)_x)\leq1. \] These are alternative conditional formulations: the second permits trivial stabilizers, whereas the first excludes them. The supplied evidence does not determine the rank convention or which condition is intended. It also does not specify a dimension restriction, whether the action must be effective, or any convention concerning the smooth structure of the sphere. The standard-sphere notation above makes the conditional formulations explicit; those unresolved qualifications require checking against the report before selecting a definitive statement. No existence or nonexistence assertion is made.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the permutation group $S_5$ can act smoothly on some sphere with rank-one stabilizers. The meaning of rank-one isotropy, including whether trivial stabilizers are allowed, is not specified in the supplied record and requires source clarification.
