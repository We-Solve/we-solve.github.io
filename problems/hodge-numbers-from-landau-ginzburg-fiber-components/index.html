Math-4382

Hodge Numbers from Landau–Ginzburg Fiber Components

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns a smooth Fano variety $X$ of dimension $n$, equipped with a toric Landau--Ginzburg model $f_X$. Under the usual complex-geometric interpretation, a smooth Fano variety is a smooth projective variety over ${\mathbb{C}}$ whose anticanonical line bundle is ample. For $n\geq 1$, its relevant Hodge number is \[ h^{1,n-1}(X)=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}H^{n-1}(X,\Omega_X^1), \] where $\Omega_X^1$ is the sheaf of algebraic differential one-forms. The base field and the dimension range are not explicitly specified in the supplied record; this complex-geometric interpretation requires confirmation.

To express the proposed fiber count, suppose a chosen compactification of $f_X$ is a morphism $\overline f_X\colon Y\to B$. Let $R\subseteq B$ be the set of parameters of the reducible fibers included in the count. If $R$ is finite and $r_t$ denotes the number of irreducible components of the fiber over $t$, counted without multiplicity, the supplied description becomes \[ k_{f_X}=\sum_{t\in R}r_t-\#R =\sum_{t\in R}(r_t-1). \] This formula interprets "components" as irreducible components. The record does not specify the compactification, which fibers are included, whether a fiber at infinity is included, or whether this count is independent of those choices. Finiteness and the component-counting convention also require confirmation.

The proposed mathematical assertion is that, for every smooth Fano variety $X$ of dimension $n$ equipped with a toric Landau--Ginzburg model $f_X$, \[ h^{1,n-1}(X)=k_{f_X}. \] Here the meaning of "toric Landau--Ginzburg model" and the allowed compactification choices must be supplied before this becomes a fully specified universal assertion. In particular, the record does not determine whether the equality is required for every admissible compactification or for a distinguished choice. These unresolved qualifications are part of this statement-only draft.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a particular Hodge number of a smooth Fano variety equals the total number of extra components in the reducible fibers of a compactified toric Landau--Ginzburg model: a fiber with $r$ components contributes $r-1$. The precise model and compactification conventions remain unspecified. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved; that status has not been independently verified.
