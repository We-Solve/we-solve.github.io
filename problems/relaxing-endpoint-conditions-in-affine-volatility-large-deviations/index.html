Statement of the requested problem. Determine whether the hypothesis $w(u_+)=w(u_-)$ can be weakened or omitted in the intended large-deviation theorem for affine stochastic-volatility models, and identify replacement hypotheses under which that theorem's large-deviation conclusion remains valid, with its other hypotheses retained.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether an endpoint matching assumption in an affine stochastic-volatility large-deviation theorem can be relaxed, and what assumptions could replace it. The supplied record omits the theorem and the definitions of its endpoint notation, so a precise theorem-level formulation requires further source clarification.
