CS-168 · Math-3010

Uniform quasipolynomial worst-case trace reconstruction

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For binary strings of known length $n\ge2$ and known rational retention probability $p\in(0,1]$, seek one randomized multitape Turing machine that reconstructs every input with probability at least $2/3$. At each fixed $p$, its independent ordinary deletion-trace budget should be \[ M(n,p)=\exp\left(O_p((\log n)^3(1+\log\log(2n))^6)\right). \] Its bit time must be polynomial in $n$, the binary encoding length of $p$, and the prescribed trace budget, on every internal random-bit sequence and every trace stream consisting of words of length at most $n$. Preprocessing, finite-precision computation, received bits and output are charged. Only external generation of independent traces is an oracle operation.

Short English statement

Can one uniform binary trace-reconstruction algorithm achieve the specified quasipolynomial trace and bit budgets at every fixed rational retention probability?

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For a binary word $x\in\{0,1\}^n$, $n\ge2$, an ordinary deletion trace independently retains each symbol with rational probability $p=a/b\in(0,1]$ and preserves order. Put $q=1-p$, let $L$ be the bit length of the reduced numerator and denominator, and define \[ X(n,p)=\begin{cases}1+\dfrac{\log n}{1+\log(1/q)},&q>0,\\1,&q=0,\end{cases} \qquad B(n,p)=p^{-1}(\log n)X(n,p)^2(1+\log X(n,p))^6. \] The claim gives absolute $C,c\ge1$ and one randomized multitape Turing machine, given $n,p$ in binary, that requests at most \[ M_C(n,p)=\lceil\exp(CB(n,p))\rceil \] independent traces. For each input word $x$, reconstruction succeeds with probability at least $2/3$. Regardless of the internal random tape and of the received stream, provided all its strings have length bounded by $n$, the running time is at most \[ (n+L+M_C(n,p))^c \] bit operations, including preprocessing, finite-precision calculations, received bits and output. Only external generation of independent traces is treated as an oracle operation.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 122; 2026-10-05), low-order trace moments propagate a distinguishing signal through scales using damped products and prescribed frequencies.
