Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given finite $\Phi \subset [0,1]\cap Q$, does some positive $m(\Phi )$ make $|\lfloor m(K_X+\Delta )\rfloor |$ nonempty and make its ratios generate the full Iitaka field for every normal integral projective complex lc fourfold $(X,\Delta )$ with effective rational boundary coefficients in $\Phi $ and rational Cartier pseudo-effective $K_X+\Delta $? The same m must work for every chosen canonical divisor.

Uniform effective log-Iitaka systems for fourfolds

OpenAI's claim

For log-canonical fourfolds with coefficients from a fixed finite rational set, a uniform rounded adjoint system is claimed to generate the full Iitaka field; a uniform klt Calabi--Yau canonical index is also claimed. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Fix a finite rational set $\Phi\subset[0,1]$. OpenAI claims an integer $m=m(\Phi)>0$ working for every normal integral projective complex fourfold $X$ with effective rational $\Delta$ whose nonzero coefficients lie in $\Phi$, log canonical pair $(X,\Delta)$, and pseudo-effective rational Cartier $D=K_X+\Delta$. Namely, \[ |\lfloor mD\rfloor|\ne\varnothing,\qquad \mathbb C\left(s/t:\ 0\ne s,t\in H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(\lfloor mD\rfloor))\right)=K(D)\subset\mathbb C(X). \] Here $K(D)$ is the field generated by ratios from all positive reflexive systems $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(\lfloor jD\rfloor))$. Equality of fields asserts the Iitaka fibration itself, rather than only the dimension of its image. The integer is independent of the chosen canonical divisor.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 034, 2026-09-26): Marked curve chains control degrees of rational quotient leaves with compatibility under projections and finite quotients. Scalar bounds and Frobenius estimates handle the index step; a semiample contraction and the relative big-base theorem then bring uniform floor systems back to the original pair.
