Math-10009

Degree-uniform power saving for intersective polynomial differences

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For each $k>=2$, is there $c_k>0$ such that for every intersective h in Z[x] of degree k with positive leading coefficient, all sufficiently large N and all A subset [N] avoiding nonzero h(t) differences for positive integers t satisfy $|A|<=C_h$ $N^{1-c_k}$, with $C_h$ allowed to depend on h?

Short English statement

For each $k>=2$, is there $c_k>0$ such that for every intersective h in Z[x] of degree k with positive leading coefficient, all sufficiently large N and all A subset [N] avoiding nonzero h(t) differences for positive integers t satisfy $|A|<=C_h$ $N^{1-c_k}$, with $C_h$ allowed to depend on h?
