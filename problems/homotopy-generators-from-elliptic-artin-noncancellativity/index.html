Homotopy Generators from Elliptic Artin Noncancellativity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $M=A(R,G)^+$ for the elliptic Artin monoid named in the supplied record, and write $X=S_{\mathrm{ell}}\setminus D_{\mathrm{ell}}$ for its elliptic discriminant complement. Fix the source's basepoint $*\in X$. The group $\pi_2(X,*)$ is the abelian group of based homotopy classes of based continuous maps $(S^2,s_0)\to(X,*)$.

The supplied record does not specify the admissible parameters $(R,G)$, a presentation of $M$, the construction of $S_{\mathrm{ell}}$ and $D_{\mathrm{ell}}$, or the meaning of the relation-class and division notation below. Consequently, the following records the intended conjecture conditionally on those source constructions, rather than supplying a complete intrinsic definition of its proposed generators.

Let $I$ denote the source-intended collection of pairs of noncancellative quadruples \[ ((a,b,c,d),(a',b,c,d')), \] with the displayed common entries $b,c$. For each such pair, the source proposes a difference class \[ \Delta_{a,a';b,c;d,d'}= a\backslash R(abd,acd)/d -a'\backslash R(a'bd',a'cd')/d' \quad\text{in }\pi_2(X,*). \] The intended conjecture is that these classes generate the second homotopy group: \[ \pi_2(X,*)= \left\langle \Delta_{a,a';b,c;d,d'}: ((a,b,c,d),(a',b,c,d'))\in I \right\rangle. \] Interpreting generation as generation of the underlying abelian group, this says that every $\alpha\in\pi_2(X,*)$ is a finite integer linear combination of the displayed difference classes. The supplied record does not resolve whether an additional $\pi_1(X,*)$-module interpretation is intended. It also does not define noncancellative quadruples or establish how the displayed expressions yield well-defined based homotopy classes; those definitions and qualifications must be recovered from the cited source before this can be a fully specified mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The proposed conjecture asks whether differences associated with pairs of noncancellative quadruples sharing $b,c$ generate $\pi_2$ of the elliptic discriminant complement. The supplied record labels the problem open, but omits definitions needed to identify the proposed classes and their precise range.
