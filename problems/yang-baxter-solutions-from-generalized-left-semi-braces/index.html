Yang–Baxter Solutions from Generalized Left Semi-Braces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be a generalized left semi-brace, with operations denoted by $+$ and $\circ$, and with the notation $a^{-}$ used in the supplied source. The record does not supply the defining axioms of a generalized left semi-brace or define this inverse notation; these must be fixed from the cited source before this formulation is fully self-contained.

Define the map $r\colon S\times S\to S\times S$ by \[ r(a,b)=\left(a\circ(a^{-}+b),(a^{-}+b)^{-}\circ b\right) \qquad(a,b\in S). \] Write $\operatorname{id}_S$ for the identity map on $S$, and define maps on $S^3$ by \[ r_{12}=r\times\operatorname{id}_S, \qquad r_{23}=\operatorname{id}_S\times r. \] The problem is to characterize precisely those generalized left semi-braces $S$ for which $r$ is a set-theoretic solution of the Yang--Baxter equation, expressed in braid form as \[ r_{12}\circ r_{23}\circ r_{12} = r_{23}\circ r_{12}\circ r_{23} \quad\text{as maps }S^3\longrightarrow S^3. \] Here composition between maps denotes ordinary function composition, whereas $\circ$ inside the formula for $r$ denotes the operation on $S$. The displayed map identity is required on every triple $(a,b,c)\in S^3$. The supplied record does not specify whether its term "set-theoretic solution" additionally requires bijectivity or nondegeneracy; any such requirement must be retained if imposed by the cited source. No finiteness hypothesis or additional restriction on $S$ is supplied.

Short English statement

Characterize the generalized left semi-braces for which the displayed map $r$ satisfies the Yang--Baxter equation. A fully self-contained statement requires confirmation of the semi-brace axioms, the meaning of $a^{-}$, and any additional requirements included in the source's definition of a solution.
