Math-15252

Cutoff Parameters for Quantum-Group Random Walks

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $N$, the quantum permutation group $S_N^+$ has coordinate $C^*$-algebra generated by projections $u_{ij}$, $1\leq i,j\leq N$, with mutually orthogonal entries in each row and column and with every row and column summing to $1$. Its coproduct satisfies \[ \Delta(u_{ij})=\sum_{k=1}^N u_{ik}\otimes u_{kj}. \] The supplied statement also uses coefficients $u_{ij}^{n}$. Their representation labels and index ranges are not defined in the record; in particular, the superscript must not silently be interpreted as an ordinary power.

Define polynomials $T_k$ by \[ T_0(X)=1,\qquad T_1(X)=X,\qquad XT_k(X)=T_{k+1}(X)+T_{k-1}(X)\quad(k\geq1). \] The source prescribes a random-walk state $\varphi$ on $S_N^+$ through \[ \varphi(u_{ij}^{n})=\delta_{ij}\left( \frac{N-1}{N}\frac{T_{2n}(\sqrt{N-2})}{T_{2n}(\sqrt N)}+\frac1N \right), \] where $\delta_{ij}$ is the Kronecker delta. The admissible values of $N$, the range of $n$, and the coefficient index ranges are not supplied. The assertion that these prescriptions define a state is part of the supplied formulation; no additional existence or positivity claim is established here.

The transition operator is \[ P_\varphi=(\operatorname{id}\otimes\varphi)\circ\Delta. \] For a linear map $P:A\to A$ on an operator space $A$, its completely bounded norm is \[ \|P\|_{\mathrm{cb}} =\sup_{r\geq1}\|\operatorname{id}_{M_r}\otimes P: M_r(A)\longrightarrow M_r(A)\|. \] The operator space or algebra completion on which this norm is intended is not specified in the supplied record.

Source conjecture, with unresolved formulation. Prove or disprove that the completely bounded norm of $P_\varphi$ gives the correct cutoff parameter for the prescribed quantum random walks on $S_N^+$. The original supplied wording refers more broadly to the relevant quantum-group random walks; the corrected supplied wording singles out the displayed family on $S_N^+$.

The record supplies no equation relating the norm to a proposed cutoff time, no asymptotic range of $N$, no distance to equilibrium, no initial condition, and no definition of cutoff in this setting. It also does not specify whether the intended norm involves the displayed operator itself, its iterates, or an operator measuring deviation from equilibrium. Consequently, the displayed prescription and the qualitative conjecture can be retained, but a uniquely quantified cutoff assertion cannot be recovered from this record alone.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether the completely bounded transition norm determines cutoff for a specified family of random walks on $S_N^+$. The state formula is retained exactly. The admissible parameters, representation indexing, norm setting, and meaning of the proposed cutoff parameter require clarification before the conjecture has a precise testable formulation.
