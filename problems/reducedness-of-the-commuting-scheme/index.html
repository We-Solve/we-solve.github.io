Reducedness of the Commuting Scheme

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the ground field or the class of Lie algebras denoted by $\mathfrak g$. The following construction gives a precise conditional interpretation without resolving those missing hypotheses.

Suppose that $k$ is a field and that $\mathfrak g$ is a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over $k$. Let \[ A=\operatorname{Sym}_k(\mathfrak g^*\oplus\mathfrak g^*) \] be the coordinate ring of $\mathfrak g\times\mathfrak g$. For each $\lambda\in\mathfrak g^*$, let $f_\lambda\in A$ be the polynomial defined by \[ f_\lambda(x,y)=\lambda([x,y]). \] Define the commutator ideal and commuting scheme by \[ I_{\mathrm{comm}}=(f_\lambda\mid\lambda\in\mathfrak g^*), \qquad \mathcal C_k(\mathfrak g)=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I_{\mathrm{comm}}). \] Thus the defining equations are the coordinates of $[x,y]=0$, with their scheme structure retained. A scheme is reduced if its structure sheaf has no nonzero nilpotent sections; for this affine scheme, this means that $A/I_{\mathrm{comm}}$ has no nonzero nilpotent elements.

Problem, subject to unresolved hypotheses. For the ground field $k$ and Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ intended by the source, determine whether $\mathcal C_k(\mathfrak g)$ is reduced. Equivalently, determine whether \[ \sqrt{I_{\mathrm{comm}}}=I_{\mathrm{comm}}, \] or, equivalently, whether for every $a\in A$ and every integer $m\geq 1$, \[ a^m\in I_{\mathrm{comm}}\ \Longrightarrow\ a\in I_{\mathrm{comm}}. \] The record does not determine the characteristic or algebraic closure assumptions on $k$, the structural hypotheses on $\mathfrak g$, or the intended range of quantification over these parameters. In particular, this draft does not assert a conjecture for every finite-dimensional Lie algebra over every field.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the equations saying that two elements of a Lie algebra commute define a scheme with no nonzero nilpotents in its coordinate ring. The field, the hypotheses on the Lie algebra, and the intended scope of the question are missing from the supplied record and require source clarification.
