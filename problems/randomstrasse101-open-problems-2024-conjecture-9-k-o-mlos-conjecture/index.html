Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2024) — Conjecture 9 (K\'{o}mlos Conjecture)

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the scalar field or define $\mathrm{disc}(A)$. Under the conventional interpretation using real matrices and sign vectors, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer and let $A=(a_{ij})\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n\times n}$. Define its signing discrepancy by \[ \mathrm{disc}(A):=\min_{x\in\{-1,1\}^{n}}\|Ax\|_{\infty} =\min_{x\in\{-1,1\}^{n}}\max_{1\leq i\leq n}\left|\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{ij}x_j\right|. \] With this explicitly stated interpretation, the supplied conjecture is that there exists a finite universal constant $K\geq 0$ such that, for every integer $n\geq 1$ and every $A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{n\times n}$ satisfying \[ \sum_{i=1}^{n}a_{ij}^{2}=1\qquad(1\leq j\leq n), \] there exists $x\in\{-1,1\}^{n}$ for which \[ \left|\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{ij}x_j\right|\leq K\qquad(1\leq i\leq n). \] The constant $K$ must be independent of both $n$ and $A$. The source explicitly states square matrices and columns of norm exactly one. The real scalar field and the signing definition of discrepancy are interpretive specifications requiring confirmation against the cited source.

Short English statement

The supplied record identifies this as the Kómlos Conjecture: replacing a requirement of $\pm1$ entries by unit Euclidean column norms should still permit signs for the columns that bound every coordinate of their signed sum by one universal constant. This draft uses an explicit real matrix interpretation of the otherwise undefined discrepancy notation.
