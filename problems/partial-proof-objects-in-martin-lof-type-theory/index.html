Partial Proof Objects in Martin-Löf Type Theory

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In Martin--Löf type theory, a typing judgment $\Gamma\vdash a:A$ states that, in context $\Gamma$, the term $a$ inhabits the type $A$. Under the propositions-as-types interpretation, such a term represents a proof of the proposition expressed by $A$.

The supplied problem asks: what mathematical objects or formal mechanisms can represent partial proofs in Martin--Löf type theory, analogously to the partial functions used in the predicative construction of Mahlo universes?

Here "partial proof" is the notion to be developed, not a notion defined by the supplied record. The record does not specify the presentation of Martin--Löf type theory, the particular predicative construction of Mahlo universes, the partial functions involved, or the properties required of the proposed analogy. Consequently, the supplied question is a request for a mathematical framework; it does not determine a precise existence assertion or theorem with specified hypotheses and quantifiers. These qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The question asks for a mathematical account of partial proofs in Martin--Löf type theory, inspired by partial functions in a predicative construction of Mahlo universes. The supplied record leaves both the intended analogy and the requirements for partial proofs unspecified.
