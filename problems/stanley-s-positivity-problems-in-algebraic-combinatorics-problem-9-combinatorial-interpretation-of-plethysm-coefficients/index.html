Stanley's Positivity Problems in Algebraic Combinatorics — Problem 9: Combinatorial interpretation of plethysm coefficients

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Lambda_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ be the graded ring of symmetric functions over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. A partition $\lambda\vdash d$ is a finite weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers whose sum is $d$; the empty partition has size zero. Write $s_\lambda$ for the corresponding Schur function, and equip $\Lambda_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ with the Hall inner product, normalized by \[ \langle s_\lambda,s_\nu\rangle=\delta_{\lambda,\nu}. \] For $r\geq1$, let $p_r=\sum_{i\geq1}x_i^r$. For $f,g\in\Lambda_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, plethysm $f[g]$ is obtained by expressing $f$ in the power sums and replacing each $p_r$ by the symmetric function obtained from $g$ under the substitution $p_k\mapsto p_{rk}$, with rational coefficients fixed. For a positive integer $m$, write $s_m=s_{(m)}$.

The explicit target in the supplied record is the following problem, attributed to Stanley (1999), Problem 9. For every pair of positive integers $m,n$ and every partition $\lambda\vdash mn$, give a positive combinatorial interpretation of \[ a_{m,n}^{\lambda}:=\langle s_m[s_n],s_\lambda\rangle, \qquad s_m[s_n]=\sum_{\lambda\vdash mn}a_{m,n}^{\lambda}s_\lambda. \] More precisely, describe finite sets $\mathcal C_{m,n}^{\lambda}$ by explicit combinatorial conditions and establish \[ a_{m,n}^{\lambda}=\#\mathcal C_{m,n}^{\lambda}, \] so that the interpretation itself supplies a combinatorial proof of the stated nonnegativity. Homogeneity gives coefficient zero for partitions whose size differs from $mn$.

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify the range of $m,n$; the formulation above makes explicit the usual positive-integer reading. Whether zero indices are intended remains unspecified. The source also mentions general plethysms $s_\alpha[s_\beta]$, whose coefficients are $\langle s_\alpha[s_\beta],s_\nu\rangle$ for $\nu\vdash |\alpha||\beta|$, but its explicit request concerns the single-row case $s_m[s_n]$. It is unclear whether a general positive rule is additionally required. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.

Short English statement

For positive integers $m,n$, find explicitly described finite combinatorial objects counted by each Schur coefficient of $s_m[s_n]$. Such a counting rule should itself explain the supplied assertion that these coefficients are nonnegative. The source leaves zero-index conventions and the intended scope of its mention of general Schur plethysms unresolved.
