Problem 11.5 — Construct the LMO invariant with coeﬃcients in a finite field.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}$ denote the source's Le--Murakami--Ohtsuki invariant of three-manifolds. The task is to construct its analogue with coefficients in a finite field ${\mathbb{F}}_q$, where $q$ is a prime power. The intended field or quantifier over fields is not stated.

For the usual closed Jacobi-diagram target, one possible coefficient framework is \[\widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing;{\mathbb{F}}_q)=\prod_{d\geq0}\mathcal A_d(\varnothing;{\mathbb{F}}_q),\] where $\mathcal A_d$ is spanned by vertex-oriented trivalent diagrams of degree $d$ (half the number of vertices), modulo the specified antisymmetry and IHX relations. A finite-field construction in this framework would give $Z_{\mathrm{LMO},{\mathbb{F}}_q}(M)$ in this completion, invariant under the appropriate equivalences of admissible three-manifolds and with a specified relation to the original LMO theory. This is a conditional target description; the source does not reproduce its diagram or normalization conventions or say whether an integral, truncated or modified target is intended.

The phrase "with coefficients in a finite field" cannot mean reducing arbitrary rational coefficients without further work. If $\operatorname{char}{\mathbb{F}}_q=p$, there is no unital ring map ${\mathbb{Q}}\to{\mathbb{F}}_q$, since the invertible rational integer $p$ would map to zero. One therefore needs a stated integral or localized coefficient model with valid reduction, a degree/characteristic restriction, or a different construction. The source does not select among these possibilities or restrict the degree. It likewise does not specify whether all closed three-manifolds, rational homology spheres or integral homology spheres are intended. An arbitrary finite-field-valued topological invariant alone would not identify the requested LMO analogue.

Short English statement

Construct a finite-field version of the LMO invariant, specifying its diagram target and compatibility with the original theory. Rational coefficients cannot simply be reduced modulo a prime without a valid coefficient model.
