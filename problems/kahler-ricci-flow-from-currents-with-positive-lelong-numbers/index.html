Kähler–Ricci Flow from Currents with Positive Lelong Numbers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks a qualitative question and does not supply enough hypotheses to determine a unique initial-value problem. The following definitions describe the conventional setting, with the missing qualifications made explicit.

Let $X$ be a complex manifold. In the usual interpretation of Kähler--Ricci flow from currents, the initial datum $T_0$ is a closed positive current of bidegree $(1,1)$. Positivity means that $T_0$ evaluates nonnegatively on compactly supported smooth strongly positive test forms of complementary bidegree. Closedness and bidegree are conventional here; the supplied statement says only "positive current."

Locally write $T_0=(i/\pi)\partial\bar\partial u$, where $u$ is a plurisubharmonic potential. Using this normalization, its Lelong number at $x\in X$ is \[ \nu(T_0,x)=\liminf_{z\to x}\frac{u(z)}{\log\lVert z-x\rVert}, \] computed in local holomorphic coordinates centered at $x$. This number is independent of the coordinates and the choice of local potential. Write \[ E_+(T_0)=\{x\in X:\nu(T_0,x)>0\}. \] The phrase "has positive Lelong numbers" is interpreted here as $E_+(T_0)\ne\varnothing$; the source gives no more specific condition on this locus.

For a smooth family of Kähler forms $\omega(t)$, the unnormalized Kähler--Ricci flow equation is \[ \frac{\partial\omega(t)}{\partial t}=-\operatorname{Ric}(\omega(t)), \qquad \operatorname{Ric}(\omega)=-i\partial\bar\partial\log\det(g_{j\bar k}), \] where $(g_{j\bar k})$ is the associated Hermitian metric matrix. A current-valued evolution starting from $T_0$ would ordinarily have the initial trace \[ T(t)\rightharpoonup T_0\quad\text{as }t\downarrow0, \] meaning convergence against every compactly supported smooth test form of complementary degree. Neither this initial-trace convention nor a definition of weak solution is supplied in the record; the smooth equation alone does not define the Ricci form of an arbitrary singular current.

Source question. What is the behavior of Kähler--Ricci flow with initial positive current $T_0$ having positive Lelong numbers? In the conventional interpretation above, the question concerns weak evolutions with $E_+(T_0)\ne\varnothing$. The source does not specify the ambient manifold, compactness, initial cohomology class, normalized versus unnormalized flow, time interval, weak-solution concept, or a particular behavior to prove. Thus it supplies a research question rather than a uniquely quantified conjecture; the displayed conventions do not resolve those omissions.

Short English statement

The problem asks how Kähler--Ricci flow behaves when its initial current has positive Lelong numbers, which measure local singularity concentration. The source leaves the geometric hypotheses, weak-solution framework, and intended conclusion unspecified, so a unique precise conjecture cannot be extracted without further source clarification.
