Bondal-Thomsen Polytopes within Lattice-Polytope Theory

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a lattice, meaning a free abelian group of finite rank, and let $M_{{\mathbb{R}}}=M\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}$. A lattice polytope in $M_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ is the convex hull of a finite nonempty subset of $M$. A rational polytope is the convex hull of a finite nonempty subset of $M\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}$. For a rational polytope $P$, its denominator relative to $M$ is \[ \operatorname{den}_{M}(P)=\min\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}:dP\text{ is a lattice polytope relative to }M\}. \] A zonotope is a Minkowski sum of finitely many line segments: \[ Z=[a_1,b_1]+\cdots+[a_r,b_r],\qquad [a,b]=\{(1-t)a+tb:0\leq t\leq1\}. \] It is a lattice zonotope when such a representation exists with all endpoints in $M$.
