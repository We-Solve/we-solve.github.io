Math-3835

Complete Graph Immersions from Minimum Degree

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the conventional interpretation using nonempty finite simple undirected graphs, let $d_G(v)$ be the number of neighbors of $v$, and let $\delta(G)=\min_{v\in V(G)}d_G(v)$. Let $K_n$ denote the complete simple graph on $n$ vertices. A weak immersion of $K_n$ in $G$ consists of an injective map $\phi:V(K_n)\to V(G)$ and, for each edge $uv\in E(K_n)$, a path $P_{uv}$ in $G$ joining $\phi(u)$ to $\phi(v)$, such that these paths are pairwise edge-disjoint. Their vertices may overlap. A strong immersion additionally requires that no internal vertex of any $P_{uv}$ belongs to $\phi(V(K_n))$.

Question under these conventions. Is it true that, for every integer $n\geq 1$ and every nonempty finite simple undirected graph $G$, \[ \delta(G)\geq n \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ contains a weak immersion of }K_n? \] For $n=1$, the immersion data consist of one chosen vertex and no paths; this boundary value is retained from the source.

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied statement says only "graph" and "immersion." It does not explicitly specify finiteness, simplicity, or whether weak or strong immersion is intended. Consequently, the displayed formulation is an explicit conventional interpretation requiring confirmation against the source, rather than a verified determination of those missing conventions. The background's reference to strong immersions does not by itself specify the immersion notion in the question. The degree threshold in the question is exactly $n$, not $n-1$.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every graph with minimum degree at least $n$ contains $n$ distinct branch vertices joined pairwise by edge-disjoint paths representing $K_n$, for every integer $n\geq1$. The intended graph class and whether these paths must also satisfy the strong-immersion condition require confirmation. The supplied registry labels the problem open.
