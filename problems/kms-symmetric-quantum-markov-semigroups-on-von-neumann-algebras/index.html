KMS-Symmetric Quantum Markov Semigroups on von Neumann Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a von Neumann algebra. In the usual unital convention, a quantum Markov semigroup is a family $(T_t)_{t\geq 0}$ of normal, unital, completely positive maps $T_t:M\to M$ such that \[ T_0=\operatorname{id}_M,\qquad T_{s+t}=T_sT_t\quad(s,t\geq0), \] with the appropriate continuity in $t$. The supplied excerpt does not specify that continuity convention. If the semigroup is continuous in operator norm, its bounded generator on $M$, with one common sign convention, is \[ A(x)=\lim_{t\downarrow0}\frac{T_t(x)-x}{t}. \] The supplied record does not establish whether its phrase "bounded generators" refers to generators on $M$ or to their implementations on an associated $L^2$-space, nor does it specify the sign convention.

KMS symmetry requires reference data, ordinarily a faithful normal state or a suitable faithful normal semifinite weight. For example, when a faithful normal state $\varphi$ is specified, write $j_\varphi(x)=\varphi^{1/4}x\varphi^{1/4}$ for the symmetric embedding into the associated noncommutative $L^2$-space. In this convention, KMS symmetry means \[ \langle j_\varphi(T_t(x)),j_\varphi(y)\rangle = \langle j_\varphi(x),j_\varphi(T_t(y))\rangle \quad(x,y\in M,\ t\geq0). \] This state-based description is conditional notation, not an additional hypothesis attributed to the source: an arbitrary von Neumann algebra need not admit a faithful normal state, and the intended state or weight framework is absent from the supplied excerpt.

An $M$-correspondence is a Hilbert space $\mathcal H$ with commuting normal unital representations of $M$ and its opposite algebra, giving left and right actions. Given a suitable algebra $\mathcal A\subseteq M$, a derivation into $\mathcal H$ is a linear map $\delta:\mathcal A\to\mathcal H$ satisfying \[ \delta(xy)=x\cdot\delta(y)+\delta(x)\cdot y \quad(x,y\in\mathcal A). \] These definitions alone do not specify the derivation characterization mentioned in the record.

Requested extension, with unresolved qualifications. Determine whether the necessary-and-sufficient derivation/correspondence characterization of KMS-symmetric quantum Markov semigroups asserted in the source report for matrix algebras extends to arbitrary von Neumann algebras, with the reconstructed registry statement restricting attention to bounded generators. The original excerpt conjectures that the matrix-algebra characterization continues to hold in this generality. However, it omits the characterization itself, including the relation between the generator and the derivation, the admissible correspondences, the derivation domain, and any modular or reality conditions. Consequently, the supplied evidence does not determine a complete quantified equivalence. An explicit characterization theorem cannot be stated precisely without that missing material.
