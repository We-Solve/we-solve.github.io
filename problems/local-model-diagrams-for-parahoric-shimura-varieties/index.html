Math-3480

Local-Model Diagrams for Parahoric Shimura Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A parahoric level refers to a level condition at a prime $p$ specified by a parahoric subgroup of the relevant reductive group over ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$. The supplied record does not specify the prime, Shimura datum, remaining level structure, or hypotheses on the reductive group.

Source question. For every Shimura variety with parahoric level, does there exist an associated local-model diagram \[ S_K \xleftarrow{\ \pi\ } \widetilde S_K \xrightarrow{\ q\ } M^{\mathrm{loc}} \] such that $\pi$ is a $K$-torsor and $q$ is $K$-equivariant and smooth?

Here $S_K$, $M^{\mathrm{loc}}$, and the acting object denoted by $K$ retain the source's notation. The record does not specify whether $S_K$ denotes an integral model, the base over which the diagram is defined, the construction of the associated local model, or the interpretation of $K$ as an object acting on schemes. In particular, the associated local model must be specified by the intended theory; allowing an arbitrary target scheme would not express the intended question.

To describe the torsor and equivariance requirements precisely once the missing data are supplied, suppose that a base scheme $B$, a group scheme $\mathcal G/B$, a $B$-scheme $S_K$, and the intended $\mathcal G$-scheme $M^{\mathrm{loc}}$ have been fixed. Under a left-action, fppf-torsor interpretation, the requirements would be that $\pi$ is faithfully flat and locally of finite presentation and that \[ \mathcal G\times_B\widetilde S_K \longrightarrow \widetilde S_K\times_{S_K}\widetilde S_K, \qquad (g,t)\longmapsto(g\cdot t,t), \] is an isomorphism, while $q$ is smooth and satisfies \[ q(g\cdot t)=g\cdot q(t) \] functorially on points over every $B$-scheme. This explains one possible precise meaning of the displayed conditions; the supplied record does not establish that this group scheme, topology, or base is the intended one. A parahoric subgroup of a $p$-adic group and a group scheme used in a torsor condition are distinct kinds of objects. Thus the source question is preserved, but a fully specified conjecture requires these unresolved choices.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every Shimura variety with parahoric level has an associated local model reached by a smooth equivariant map from a torsor over its intended model. The source leaves the base, integral-model interpretation, acting group scheme, torsor topology, and local model unspecified, so the formulation requires human mathematical review.
