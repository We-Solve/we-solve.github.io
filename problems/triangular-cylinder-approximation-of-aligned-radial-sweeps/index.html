Math-2628

Triangular-cylinder approximation of aligned radial sweeps

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $P\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a two-dimensional vector plane with unit normal $e$, and take linearly independent $a,b\in P$. Let $J=[\alpha,\beta]$ with $\alpha<\beta$, let $R:J\to[0,\infty)$ be continuous, let $w:J\to P$ be continuously differentiable, and fix $p_0\in P$ and $L>0$. Suppose $w'(t)=\lambda(t)(a+tb)$ for a continuous real function $\lambda$. Define \[ S=\{p_0+\rho(a+tb)+s(e+w(t)):t\in J,\ 0\le\rho\le R(t),\ |s|\le L\}. \] For every finite tagged partition of $J$, can one cover the part of $S$ over each partition interval by a compact cylinder having a nondegenerate triangular base and axis $e+w(\text{tag})$, with the sum of the base areas perpendicular to their respective axes converging to \[ |\det_P(a,b)|\int_\alpha^\beta \frac{R(t)^2}{2\sqrt{1+\lVert w(t)\rVert^2}}\,dt \] uniformly over all partitions and tags as the mesh tends to zero?

Short English statement

Do aligned radial sweeps admit these finite triangular-cylinder covers, with the prescribed uniform limit of total perpendicular base area?

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Choose a Euclidean plane $P$ with unit normal $e$, independent $a,b\in P$, $J=[\alpha,\beta]$ with $\alpha<\beta$, continuous $R\ge0$, a $C^1$ map $w:J\to P$, $p_0\in P$ and $L>0$. Set \[ \mathcal S=\{p_0+\rho(a+tb)+s(e+w(t)):t\in J,0\le\rho\le R(t),|s|\le L\}, \quad\mathcal A=|\det_P(a,b)|\int_{\alpha}^{\beta}\frac{R(t)^2}{2\sqrt{1+\|w(t)\|^2}}\,dt. \] If $w'(t)=\lambda(t)(a+tb)$ for continuous real $\lambda$, every tagged finite partition gives one covering cylinder per interval, with frozen axis $e+w(c_i)$ and nondegenerate compact triangular perpendicular base, whose total base area tends uniformly to $\mathcal A$ as the mesh tends to zero. Frozen projections of the actual interval sweeps give the same limit. The tetrahedral consequence claims, for each regular tetrahedron $K$ of positive edge length, a finite triangular-base cover with \[ \sum_i|B_i|<\tfrac12 A_{\min}(K),\qquad \sum_i\frac{|B_i|}{|\pi_{u_i^{\perp}}K|}<\tfrac12. \]

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 100; 2026-09-27), one triangular enclosure is assigned to each interval of a tagged partition. Complementary shortened beams leave a strip covered by horizontal cylinders; exact overlap and strip calculations cancel the first-order cost and leave a strict second-order saving.
