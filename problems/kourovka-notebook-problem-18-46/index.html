Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.46

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite group $G$, define \[ m(G)=\min\left\{|H|:\ H\text{ is a finite group and there exists an injective homomorphism }\iota:G\to H\text{ such that }(\forall g\in G)(\exists h\in H)\ h^2=\iota(g)\right\}. \] The square root may depend on $g$. The supplied statement asserts that such an overgroup always exists and that \[ (\forall\text{ finite groups }G)\qquad m(G)\le 2|G|^2. \] The problem asks whether this estimate is sharp.

The intended meaning of sharpness is not explicitly specified. If it means optimality of the constant $2$ among uniform quadratic bounds, the question is whether \[ (\forall c\in{\mathbb{R}},\ 0<c<2)(\exists\text{ a finite group }G)\qquad m(G)>c|G|^2. \] If it means attainment of the stated bound, the question instead asks whether some finite group satisfies $m(G)=2|G|^2$. These interpretations are not asserted to be equivalent.

The source additionally reports, attributing the result to D. V. Baranov and Ant. A. Klyachko, that the best possible estimate cannot be better than $|H|\le |G|^2$. The supplied record does not state the precise lower-bound quantifiers or the examples supporting this qualification; it is therefore retained as a reported restriction on possible improvements, without imposing an additional quantified theorem.

Short English statement

For each finite group, consider the smallest finite overgroup in which every original element has a square root. The supplied bound is twice the square of the original group order. Is this bound sharp? The source leaves the precise sense of sharpness and the quantifiers of its reported lower-bound obstruction unspecified.
