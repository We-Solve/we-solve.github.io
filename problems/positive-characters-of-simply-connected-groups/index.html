Math-4915

Positive Characters of Simply Connected Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a connected simply connected compact Lie group, and let $m_G$ be normalized Haar probability. Let $R(G)$ be the integral representation ring: its elements are virtual characters \[ f=\sum_{\pi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)}n_\pi\chi_\pi, \qquad n_\pi\in{\mathbb{Z}}, \] with finitely many nonzero coefficients. Define the character inner product by $\langle f,g\rangle=\int_G f(x)\overline{g(x)}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m_G(x)$, and let $1$ denote the trivial character.

The question is whether, for every such $G$ and every $f\in R(G)$, the hypotheses \[ f(x)\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}\quad\text{for all }x\in G, \qquad\langle f,1\rangle=1 \] imply the existence of an irreducible finite-dimensional complex character $\chi$ with \[ f(x)=\chi(x)\overline{\chi(x)}=|\chi(x)|^2 \qquad\text{for every }x\in G. \] Pointwise nonnegativity is the hypothesis; it does not mean that the virtual coefficients $n_\pi$ are assumed nonnegative. The conclusion would identify $f$ with the character of $V\otimes V^*$ for an irreducible $V$. Such characters do satisfy the two displayed hypotheses by character orthogonality; the converse is the requested assertion.

The supplied literature triage dated 21 August 2026 reports Serre's rank-one result and says its checked primary sources located no general-rank proof or counterexample. That is a limited dated literature assessment, not an independent claim that the general statement has been resolved or that every relevant source was checked. The assumptions include connectedness, simple connectivity, compactness, and the integral virtual-character condition throughout.

Short English statement

Must every pointwise nonnegative virtual character of Haar average one be the squared absolute value of an irreducible character? The virtual coefficients are allowed to be negative; only the function values are assumed nonnegative. The supplied triage reports a rank-one result and leaves the general case open.
