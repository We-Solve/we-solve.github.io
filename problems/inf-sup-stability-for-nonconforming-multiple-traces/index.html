Inf–Sup Stability for Nonconforming Multiple Traces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a spectral nonconforming Petrov--Galerkin multiple-traces discretization. Its corrected formulation uses the Cartesian product spaces \[ X_L:=X_L^0\times\cdots\times X_L^M, \qquad Y_L:=Y_L^0\times\cdots\times Y_L^M. \] Here the component spaces, the discretization index $L$, the number of components $M+1$, and their admissible values are not specified. The original extracted formulation instead describes tensor products; the supplied evidence does not resolve this discrepancy.

Writing \[ b_s(\lambda,\phi):=\langle F(s)\lambda,\phi\rangle_\times, \qquad \ell_g(\phi):=\langle g,\phi\rangle_\times, \] the stated discrete problem is to find $\lambda\in X_L$ such that \[ b_s(\lambda,\phi)=\ell_g(\phi) \qquad\text{for every }\phi\in Y_L. \] The operator $F(s)$, its parameter domain, the admissible data $g$, and the pairing $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_\times$ are not defined in the record.

Requested stability statement, conditional on missing specifications. Once the intended nontrivial normed trial and test spaces, operator, and pairing have been specified, the requested trial-side inf--sup estimate would have the form \[ \inf_{0\ne\lambda\in X_L} \sup_{0\ne\phi\in Y_L} \frac{\left|\langle F(s)\lambda,\phi\rangle_\times\right|} {\|\lambda\|_{X_L}\,\|\phi\|_{Y_L}} \geq\beta_L(s)>0. \] The source does not specify the norms, whether the spaces are real or complex, whether the pairing is bilinear or sesquilinear, the admissible parameter values, or whether the lower bound must be uniform in $L$, $s$, or other parameters. Consequently this display records the shape of the requested estimate, not a fully specified conjecture.

The supplied target also asks that the stability condition establish existence and uniqueness of the discrete solution. A positive trial-side inf--sup bound yields at most one solution, but existence for every admissible load requires additional hypotheses ensuring surjectivity of the induced discrete operator. Neither the dimensions of the trial and test spaces nor an appropriate complementary condition is supplied. The intended complete well-posedness assertion therefore remains unresolved.

Short English statement

The problem asks for inf--sup stability and consequent existence and uniqueness for a spectral nonconforming Petrov--Galerkin multiple-traces discretization. Essential spaces, norms, operator definitions, parameter ranges, and solvability hypotheses are missing. The original and corrected records also disagree about tensor versus Cartesian products, so this is a conditional statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
