Math-757

Piercing Large Convex Sets in the Unit Square

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $Q=[0,1]^2\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$, and let $\lambda_2$ denote planar Lebesgue measure. A finite point set $P\subseteq Q$ meets a set $K$ if $P\cap K\ne\varnothing$.

Conditional formulation. The following formulation assumes that the compact convex sets in the supplied statement are required to be contained in the unit square. This containment hypothesis is not explicit in the supplied wording. For $0<\varepsilon\le1$, put \[ \mathcal C_\varepsilon=\{K\subseteq Q:K\text{ is compact and convex, and }\lambda_2(K)\ge\varepsilon\}, \] and define, whenever the minimum exists, \[ f(\varepsilon)=\min\left\{|P|:P\subseteq Q\text{ is finite and }P\cap K\ne\varnothing\text{ for every }K\in\mathcal C_\varepsilon\right\}. \] The question is whether $f(\varepsilon)=O(\varepsilon^{-1})$ as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. Explicitly, does there exist $C>0$ and $\varepsilon_0\in(0,1]$ such that for every $\varepsilon\in(0,\varepsilon_0]$ there exists a finite $P_\varepsilon\subseteq Q$ satisfying \[ |P_\varepsilon|\le\frac{C}{\varepsilon} \quad\text{and}\quad \forall K\in\mathcal C_\varepsilon,\qquad P_\varepsilon\cap K\ne\varnothing? \] Here $P_\varepsilon$ may depend on $\varepsilon$, but must meet every member of $\mathcal C_\varepsilon$ simultaneously.

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied statement concerns all compact convex sets, whereas the background and literature assessment describe an original rectangle piercing question. These are different target families, and the supplied evidence does not resolve which is intended. No rectangle formulation is substituted here. The closed-square boundary convention and the containment hypothesis above require confirmation against the source. Without containment or another restriction relating the sets to the square, the wording specifies a different family.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly conditional interpretation that the target sets are compact convex subsets of the closed unit square, ask whether at most $C/\varepsilon$ points can simultaneously meet every target of area at least $\varepsilon$, with $C$ independent of sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$. The source leaves containment implicit and conflicts with its rectangle-specific literature discussion, so the intended formulation requires review.
