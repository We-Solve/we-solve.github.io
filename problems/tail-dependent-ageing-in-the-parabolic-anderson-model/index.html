Tail-Dependent Ageing in the Parabolic Anderson Model

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d$ denote a positive integer and let ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$ be the integer lattice. Consider the parabolic Anderson model on this lattice with random spatial potential, denoted here by $\xi$. The supplied record denotes its remaining-lifetime process by $R(t)$, but gives no mathematical definition of that process.

Source question. At what tail threshold does the ageing behaviour described by $R(t)$ change qualitatively when the distribution of the random potential has a tail lighter than the polynomial tails treated in the cited Oberwolfach report?

Unresolved formulation. The record does not specify the potential's distribution, dependence assumptions or admissible tail classes; the evolution equation, its normalization or initial condition; or the definition of $R(t)$. It also does not define the comparison meant by "lighter than polynomial", the qualitative change to be detected, or a limiting distribution or time normalization that would characterize ageing. Thus the supplied question identifies a research target, but does not determine a unique quantified mathematical conjecture or establish that a single tail threshold exists. These missing specifications must be recovered from the cited source before a more precise statement can be given.

Short English statement

Determine where ageing in the parabolic Anderson model on ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$, as measured by the remaining-lifetime process $R(t)$, changes as potential tails become lighter than the polynomial tails considered in the source report. The supplied record leaves the process, tail comparison and criterion for change undefined, so this is an unresolved research-question formulation requiring human mathematical review.
