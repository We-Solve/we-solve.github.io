Quantum Zeta Functions for Mirror Calabi–Yau Threefolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a smooth Calabi--Yau threefold $X$ over $\mathbb Q$ and a good prime $p$, its local zeta function encodes point counts, $Z_p(X,T)=\exp(\sum_{m\ge1}|X(\mathbb F_{p^m})|T^m/m)$; its cohomological factors are characteristic polynomials of Frobenius. An $L$-series assembles corresponding local factors over primes. A mirror pair exchanges the appropriate Hodge data. The weight motive in the report's explicit orbifold families contributes a common essential factor to the third-cohomology Frobenius polynomials of the pair.
Source scope and qualifications.
Quantum zeta function and quantum L-series are requested notions, not uniquely defined inputs. The report's ratio candidate has the desired duality but discards the weight-motive factor; its explicit orbifold results are context, not a universal construction for all Calabi--Yau mirror pairs.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For mirror pairs of Calabi–Yau threefolds over $\mathbb Q$, find a satisfactory quantum zeta function and quantum $L$-series that retain the essential weight-motive factor and satisfy the expected $T$-duality.
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The report proves restricted motive and slope-zeta statements, while later work still treats the general arithmetic and quantum zeta-function correspondence as conjectural. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Construct arithmetic mirror invariants with the expected duality while retaining the important motive information lost by simple zeta-function ratios.
