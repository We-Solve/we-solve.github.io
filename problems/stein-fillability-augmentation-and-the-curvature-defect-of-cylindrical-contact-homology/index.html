Stein fillability, augmentation, and the curvature defect of cylindrical contact homology

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Question 1.9 conjectures that cylindrical contact homology is defined for every Stein-fillable contact manifold. In the closed cooriented setting, write $\xi=\ker\alpha$; a Stein filling is a compatible Stein domain with this contact boundary. The original text describes an older sufficient condition excluding contractible Reeb orbits of certain Maslov indices, without listing those indices, and asserts invariance among forms satisfying that condition. It hopes problematic orbits might instead "algebraically cancel," and proposes pair-of-pants and related operations analogous to symplectomorphism Floer theory.

The paired aid reports modern foundations for the full contact-homology DGA and filling-induced augmentations, but distinguishes these from the original untwisted cylinder-only construction. To state its formal diagnostic, let $(\mathcal A,\partial)$ be a free graded unital DGA over a coefficient field, with differential of degree $-1$. On the generator space $V$, decompose by output word length: \[ \partial|_V=\partial_0+\partial_1+\partial_2+\cdots, \qquad \partial_k:V\to V^{\otimes k}. \] The graded-commutative version uses the corresponding symmetric words. Put $c=\partial_0$. Let $\iota_c$ replace one letter of a length-two word by $c$, using the graded Leibniz signs. The word-length-one part of $\partial^2=0$ is \[ \partial_1^2+\iota_c\partial_2=0. \] In the contact interpretation, constant/plane terms, cylinder terms and two-negative-end terms occupy these respective lengths. Thus the full DGA identity does not by itself say $\partial_1^2=0$.

An augmentation $\varepsilon:\mathcal A\to\mathbb k$ satisfies $\varepsilon\partial=0$. Translating generators by $T_\varepsilon(v)=v+\varepsilon(v)$ and conjugating gives \[ \partial^\varepsilon=T_\varepsilon\partial T_\varepsilon^{-1}, \qquad \partial_0^\varepsilon=0, \qquad (\partial_1^\varepsilon)^2=0. \] This is the augmentation-linearized differential, which may incorporate higher-word terms and is not necessarily the original $\partial_1$. The report gives an augmented five-generator algebraic countermodel with $\partial_1^2\ne0$, but does not reproduce its generator/differential table in this record. It demonstrates failure of a formal algebraic implication; no geometric realization as the DGA of a Stein-fillable contact manifold is supplied.

The aid consequently treats a filling-induced augmentation and linearized contact homology as available alternatives under their analytic and coefficient conventions, not as a proof of the literal cylinder-only conjecture or of all proposed operations. Different filling/augmentation choices also cannot be silently identified. The original index restrictions, continuation data, virtual/transversality foundations and exact meaning of "defined" must be fixed to formulate the geometric conjecture fully.

Short English statement

The supplied aid distinguishes full contact homology and augmentation-linearized homology from untwisted cylindrical homology. Its word-length identity shows why the cylinder part need not square to zero merely because an augmentation exists. Twisting by that augmentation removes the constant defect, but does not by itself settle the original Stein-fillable conjecture or its operations.
