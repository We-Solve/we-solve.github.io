Nonsmooth Homogeneous Complex Monge–Ampère Solutions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb{P}^{1}$ denote the complex projective line, let $D=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|<1\}$, and set $X=\mathbb{P}^{1}\times D$.

The requested construction is a solution $u$ of the homogeneous complex Monge--Ampère equation on $X$ for which there exists an interior point $p\in X$ such that $u$ is not of class $C^{2}$ on any open neighborhood of $p$. Here $C^{2}$ means twice continuously differentiable in local real coordinates. Thus the requested failure of regularity must occur in the interior, rather than solely at the boundary $\mathbb{P}^{1}\times\partial D$.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The supplied record does not specify the precise Monge--Ampère operator, any background differential form, the admissible class of solutions, or the weak interpretation of the equation needed for a solution that is not $C^{2}$. It also supplies no boundary data or regularity requirements for such data. Consequently, this is the stated construction target, but a fully specified equation and boundary-value problem cannot be recovered from the supplied evidence. In particular, the record does not determine whether smooth boundary data are required.

Short English statement

Construct a solution of the intended homogeneous complex Monge--Ampère problem on the complex projective line times the unit disc that fails to be twice continuously differentiable near at least one interior point. The equation, solution class, and boundary requirements need clarification before this becomes a fully precise construction problem.
