Monge Minimizers for Multimarginal Coulomb Transport

OpenAI's claim

Three-marginal Monge nonattainment: The manuscript claims smooth compactly supported identical marginals in three dimensions for which no preserving pair of maps attains the Coulomb transport minimum. The Monge and relaxed infima nevertheless agree, and inverse-power analogues in other dimensions are also announced.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For the three-marginal Coulomb cost $c(x_1,x_2,x_3)=\sum_{i<j}|x_i-x_j|^{-1}$, the claimed density $\rho\ge0$ has total mass one and $\rho,\sqrt\rho\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^3)$. Write $\mu=\rho\,dx$ and $E(\mu)=\inf_{\pi\in\Pi(\mu,\mu,\mu)}\int c\,d\pi$. This finite infimum is attained, but \[\int c(x,T_2x,T_3x)\,d\mu(x)>E(\mu)\] for every pair of Borel maps preserving $\mu$. Infinite cost on the left is allowed. Hence no Monge-type coupling attains the Kantorovich minimum even for this smooth compactly supported density.

Outline of the argument

Three-marginal Monge nonattainment: Two local families of triples share a supporting potential near a central component, while strict cost gaps exclude all other center patterns. Marginal mass imbalance then prevents a deterministic preserving map from selecting the required split between the two families. Approximate preserving maps approach the optimum, proving nonattainment without a gap between the infima.
