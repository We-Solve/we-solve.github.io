Computable Coherent Sets in Stochastic Dynamics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation only, if a time-dependent stochastic dynamical system has measurable state spaces $(E_t,\mathcal{E}_t)$ indexed by a time set $I$, a family of measurable sets is a collection \[ (A_t)_{t\in I},\qquad A_t\in\mathcal{E}_t\quad(t\in I). \] This notation does not specify the stochastic dynamics or define coherence; neither is specified in the supplied record.

Research question. Can coherent families of sets for a time-dependent stochastic dynamical system be characterized in a way that permits numerical computation, and can the resulting problem be solved efficiently in higher dimensions?

It also leaves unspecified the computational input model, the meaning of dimension, and the criterion for efficiency. Short English statement

The question asks for a characterization of coherent families in time-dependent stochastic dynamics that enables numerical computation, together with efficient methods in higher dimensions. The meanings of coherence, the admissible systems, and computational efficiency remain unspecified and require human mathematical review.
