Math-4921

Multiphase Models with Multiple Conserved Quantities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\varepsilon>0$ denote the diffuse-interface thickness in the phase-field models intended by the report. A diffuse-interface description uses order parameters that vary across layers of thickness tending to zero; a sharp-interface description instead uses phase regions and their separating interfaces, coupled to the relevant bulk fields and conservation laws.

The source asks to extend its second-order sharp-interface approximations to systems with multiple phases and an arbitrary finite number of conserved quantities. Thus the desired construction is not restricted to a fixed phase count or to one conserved field: for each admissible number of phases and conserved quantities, it should provide the corresponding sharp-interface equations, interfacial conditions and second-order approximation for the source's diffuse model. A conserved quantity has a balance law; when represented by a density $c_j$ in a closed or no-flux domain, this means $\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t}\int_\Omega c_j\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x=0$. This illustration does not impose unprovided boundary conditions on the requested models.

The extract supplies neither the diffuse equations, energy and mobility couplings nor the sharp-interface system being extended. It also does not specify whether "second order" means an error of order $\varepsilon^2$, an expansion including second-order correction terms, or another convention, and gives no norm, time interval or interface-regularity assumptions for a rigorous error statement. These details must be matched to the report. The target is therefore retained as the complete model-extension question, without inventing a universal phase-field system or claiming a numerical error theorem.

The literature assessment checked on 21 August 2026 says the extension from the one-conserved-quantity setting to the stated multiphase scope was identified as open, and that later phase-field existence theory does not give a general resolution of this approximation problem. Existence of diffuse solutions and a second-order sharp-interface approximation are distinct conclusions. The open label is dated triage requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

Extend the report's second-order diffuse-to-sharp interface approximation to multiple phases and any finite number of conserved quantities. The exact equations and meaning of second-order accuracy must come from the source; general phase-field existence results alone do not answer it.
