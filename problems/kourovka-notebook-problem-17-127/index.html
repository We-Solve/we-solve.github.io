Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.127

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime. A finite group $G$ is soluble of derived length $d$ if its derived series, defined by $G^{(0)}=G$ and $G^{(i+1)}=[G^{(i)},G^{(i)}]$, first reaches the trivial group at $G^{(d)}$. An elementary abelian $p$-group of order $p^n$ is a group isomorphic to $({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})^n$. For a subgroup $A\leq\operatorname{Aut}(G)$, define its common fixed-point subgroup by \[ C_G(A)=\{g\in G: a(g)=g\text{ for every }a\in A\}. \] For a group $H$, put $\gamma_1(H)=H$ and $\gamma_{j+1}(H)=[\gamma_j(H),H]$. Saying that $H$ is nilpotent of class at most $c$ means that $\gamma_{c+1}(H)=1$; the trivial group has class zero.

The question is whether there exist functions \[ f:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\qquad h:\{\text{primes}\}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0} \] such that, for every prime $p$, every finite soluble group $G$ of derived length $d$, and every elementary abelian subgroup $A\leq\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ of order $p^n$ satisfying $C_G(A)=1$, there is a normal series \[ 1=G_0\trianglelefteq G_1\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq G_k=G, \qquad k\leq f(n), \] whose factors satisfy \[ \gamma_{h(p,n,d)+1}(G_i/G_{i-1})=1 \qquad(1\leq i\leq k). \] Thus the length bound must depend only on $n$, and the bound on the nilpotency classes must depend only on $p,n,d$, uniformly over $G$ and $A$. No assumption that $p$ is coprime to $|G|$ is supplied. The cases $n=0$ or $d=0$ force $G=1$, for which the series of length zero is allowed.

There is a convention requiring clarification: the displayed chain expresses normality of each term in the next. If the source uses "normal series" to require every term to be normal in $G$, the additional requirement is $G_i\trianglelefteq G$ for all $i$. The supplied record does not explicitly distinguish these conventions.

The supplied source states that the answer is affirmative for $p=2$, and that an affirmative answer would follow from an affirmative answer to Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.125. These are retained as source assertions; the formulation of Problem 11.125 and the supporting arguments are not supplied.

Short English statement

Does a finite soluble group with a fixed-point-free elementary abelian group of automorphisms of order $p^n$ have a normal series whose length is bounded solely by $n$, with nilpotent factors of class bounded solely by $p,n,d$, where $d$ is its derived length? Here fixed-point-free means that the common fixed-point subgroup is trivial. The supplied source asserts the result for $p=2$; the convention for normal series requires clarification.
