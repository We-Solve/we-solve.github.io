Miscellaneous problems 1 — Build MU from the moduli stack of formal groups.

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $MU$ denote the complex bordism spectrum, equivalently the Thom spectrum of the universal stable complex bundle. Let $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{fg}}$ denote the moduli stack of one-dimensional commutative formal groups, customarily over $\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{Z}}$: it records formal groups and their isomorphisms, rather than only formal group laws with a chosen coordinate. Base and stack-topology conventions are not stated in the excerpt.

Miscellaneous problem 1 (Hovey). Construct $MU$ from $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{fg}}$. The goal is a topological or homotopical construction from the stack that recovers the spectrum, not merely the algebraic description of complex-bordism coefficients or the familiar cooperation Hopf algebroid. In particular, a presentation that already uses $MU$ as input does not by itself give the requested construction. The source does not specify the allowable construction, functoriality, equivalence notion, or whether ring-spectrum and complex-orientation structure must also be recovered.

Hovey suggests treating the stack as a space in Voevodsky's homotopy-theoretic setting and asks whether it might be an infinite loop space there. This is a tentative proposed route, not an assertion that the stack already has compatible deloopings. A precise version needs an ambient motivic category, base, descent topology, localization, meaning of delooping, and a realization/comparison to topological spectra. Recovering $MU$ rather than only its formal-group algebra is the intended endpoint.

The source calls this an old problem heard in Ravenel's "green book." The summary invokes a theorem of Lurie explaining constraints from the formal-group stack but provides no precise theorem or construction matching this request. Neither that report nor the speculative motivic route is asserted here as a completed recovery of $MU$.

Short English statement

Recover the complex bordism spectrum from the moduli stack of formal groups. The source proposes motivic infinite-loop-space ideas, but leaves the construction and realization procedure unspecified.
