Definitions and notation.
Let $a$ be the analytic symbol on a two-dimensional phase space specified in the source, $h>0$ the semiclassical parameter and $a^w(x,hD)$ its Weyl quantization. For a self-adjoint one-degree-of-freedom Hamiltonian, the classical action around an energy curve underlies Bohr--Sommerfeld quantization, with a Maslov correction. For a complex symbol the corresponding cycles and actions may be complex.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Bohr-Sommerfeld

Is there a Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization rule for $a^w(x,hD)$? Is there a link to the usual Bohr-Sommerfeld rule for Hermitian operators?

Scope and source qualification.
The source leaves the analytic symbol’s global assumptions and admissible cycles unspecified. The Hermitian rule is explanatory context, not an assertion that an unchanged real-action formula holds for arbitrary non-Hermitian operators. Short English statement

Find a complex Bohr--Sommerfeld eigenvalue rule and relate it to the Hermitian rule.
