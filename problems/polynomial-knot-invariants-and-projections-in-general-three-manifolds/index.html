Polynomial Knot Invariants and Projections in General Three-Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses a research programme rather than a single fully specified conjecture. A knot in a three-manifold $M$ is an embedded copy of $S^1$. The source does not specify the category of embeddings, the equivalence relation on knots, whether knots or manifolds are oriented, or whether $M$ is connected, compact, or allowed to have boundary. These choices must be fixed before a precise universal statement can be made.

For a specified class of three-manifolds and a specified knot equivalence relation $\sim_M$, write \[ \mathcal K(M)=\{K\subset M:K\text{ is an admissible embedded circle}\}/\!\sim_M. \] The source asks the following related questions for knots in general three-manifolds:

  1. Can one construct Jones/HOMFLYPT-type polynomial invariants? A polynomial-valued invariant would be a map \[ I_M:\mathcal K(M)\longrightarrow R_M, \] where $R_M$ is a specified polynomial or Laurent polynomial ring. Neither this ring nor the properties that make an invariant Jones/HOMFLYPT-type are specified. In particular, merely exhibiting an arbitrary invariant would not resolve the intended question; its required normalization, skein relations, relationship with the classical polynomials, and dependence on auxiliary choices remain to be stated.
  2. What projections of knots in $M$, together with what diagrammatic information, have useful computational and invariant-theoretic properties? The projection target, admissible singularities, auxiliary structures, and meaning of usefulness are unspecified.
  3. Is there a Reidemeister theorem for such projections? Once a diagram system and its allowed moves have been specified, the intended completeness criterion is that two diagrams represent equivalent knots if and only if they are related by the allowed diagram equivalences and moves. The source does not specify those moves or require a finite list, so no particular move theorem can be extracted from the record.
These questions retain their unresolved qualifications; they do not assert the existence of a particular invariant or diagram calculus. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports established Jones-type generalizations through Kauffman-bracket and related skein modules, while reporting that a single canonical Jones/HOMFLYPT polynomial with the full classical role for arbitrary three-manifolds is not established. This is a supplied literature assessment, not an independently verified theorem or status determination.

Short English statement

The problem asks how to extend Jones/HOMFLYPT-type invariants and knot diagrams to general three-manifolds, and whether suitable diagram moves characterize knot equivalence. The manifold class, knot conventions, invariant requirements, projections, and moves remain unspecified. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation requiring independent review.
