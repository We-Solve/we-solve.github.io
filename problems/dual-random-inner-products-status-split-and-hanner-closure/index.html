Math-1399

Dual random inner products: status split and Hanner closure

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$, $n\geq1$, be an origin-symmetric convex body, and define its polar \[ K^*=\{y:\langle x,y\rangle\leq1\text{ for all }x\in K\}. \] Interpret the random vectors as independent and uniform: $X\sim\operatorname{Unif}(K)$, $Y\sim\operatorname{Unif}(K^*)$. This probability convention is necessary and is not spelled out in the extracted phrase "distributed in". With $Z=\langle X,Y\rangle$, symmetry gives mean zero and $|Z|\leq1$. For $p>0$, put \[ S(K)=|K||K^*|,\quad J_p(K)=\int_K\int_{K^*}|\langle x,y\rangle|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} y\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x, \quad M_p(K)={\mathbb{E}}|Z|^p=\frac{J_p(K)}{S(K)}. \] Thus $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=M_2(K)$. All these quantities are unchanged by invertible linear transformations of $K$. For the Euclidean unit ball $B_2^n$, writing $\kappa_n=|B_2^n|$, \[ M_2(B_2^n)=\frac{n}{(n+2)^2},\qquad J_2(B_2^n)=\kappa_n^2\frac{n}{(n+2)^2}. \] The source asks whether ellipsoids maximize $M_2$, and separately whether they maximize $J_2$; Paouris asks the analogous moment questions $M_p(K)\leq M_p(B_2^n)$ and $J_p(K)\leq J_p(B_2^n)$, for $p>0$. Normalization by $S(K)$ is material.

For $0<c<1$, the source literally writes the event $Z=c$ and the volume of its region. Under the continuous uniform convention both have value zero for every body, so the literal level-set assertion is vacuous. The aid interprets the substantive variation as a tail comparison. One explicit symmetric convention is \[ T_c(K)=\Pr(|Z|\geq c),\qquad U_c(K)=\mathcal L^{2n}\{(x,y)\in K\times K^*:|\langle x,y\rangle|\geq c\}=S(K)T_c(K). \] The conjectural inequalities would be $T_c(K)\leq T_c(B_2^n)$ or, separately, $U_c(K)\leq U_c(B_2^n)$. Signed positive tails are half these quantities by symmetry, so they give equivalent comparisons; the source's equality sign is not silently rewritten.

The aid reports a mixed status. Klartag's 2018 construction refutes the normalized variance bound in sufficiently high dimension. It also reports failure of the normalized tail bound at some threshold, using \[ M_2(K)=2\int_0^1c\,T_c(K)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} c. \] By contrast, a May 2026 preprint by Böröczky, Patsalos and Saroglou is reported to prove \[ J_2(K)\leq J_2(B_2^n),\qquad \text{with equality exactly for ellipsoids}. \] The report leaves the general $p$-moment questions and pointwise unnormalized tail-volume comparisons unresolved in general. This means no full parameter classification is supplied; the normalized moment conjecture already fails at $p=2$.

The aid further records exact closure formulas. Let $A\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^a$, $B\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^b$ be origin-symmetric convex bodies, $a,b\geq1$, $n=a+b$, and define \[ A\oplus_1B=\operatorname{conv}\left((A\times\{0\})\cup(\{0\}\times B)\right),\quad \alpha_a=\frac{(a+1)(a+2)}{(n+1)(n+2)},\quad \alpha_b=\frac{(b+1)(b+2)}{(n+1)(n+2)}. \] For $\Sigma_K=|K|^{-1}\int_Kxx^{\mathsf T}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x$, the reported sum/product recursions have the explicit block form \[ \Sigma_{A\times B}=\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma_A,\Sigma_B),\qquad \Sigma_{A\oplus_1B}=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_a\Sigma_A,\alpha_b\Sigma_B), \] with polar identities $(A\times B)^*=A^*\oplus_1B^*$ and $(A\oplus_1B)^*=A^*\times B^*$. For either $H=A\times B$ or $H=A\oplus_1B$, the aid reports \[ M_2(H)=\alpha_aM_2(A)+\alpha_bM_2(B),\qquad S(H)=\frac{a!b!}{n!}S(A)S(B). \] Starting with symmetric intervals and iterating these two operations defines Hanner polytopes. Every $n$-dimensional Hanner polytope, independently of its decomposition, is reported to satisfy \[ S(H)=\frac{4^n}{n!},\qquad M_2(H)=\frac{2n}{3(n+1)(n+2)},\qquad J_2(H)=\frac{4^n}{n!}\frac{2n}{3(n+1)(n+2)}. \] These explicit-family identities are separate from the unresolved general moment and tail questions.

Short English statement

Normalized moments and unnormalized integrals have different reported outcomes. The aid attributes a high-dimensional variance counterexample to Klartag, a positive quadratic integral result to a May 2026 preprint, and exact sum/product formulas to its Hanner-polytope analysis. Literal equality-level events have zero probability and volume.
