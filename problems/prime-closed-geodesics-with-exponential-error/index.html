Prime closed geodesics with exponential error

Definitions and mathematical statement

The extracted source formula is fragmented. Its coherent working reconstruction is \[ N(T)=\left(1+O(e^{-\varepsilon T})\right) \operatorname{Li}(e^{hT}),\qquad \operatorname{Li}(x)=\int_2^x\frac{du}{\log u}, \] for some $\varepsilon>0$, where $h>0$ is topological entropy and $N(T)$ counts primitive oriented periodic geodesic-flow orbits of period at most $T$. The source omits the preceding dynamical/metric assumptions and the counting convention. The aid's transfer statement concerns a compact hyperbolic/Anosov geodesic setting with finite bounded-period counts and a positive shortest period, not every metric on every surface. The error constant and exponent need not be uniform over metrics.

For a reversible Riemannian geodesic flow, each unoriented geometric prime geodesic gives two oriented flow orbits. Hence the exact convention change is \[ N_{or}(T)=2N_{un}(T), \] and the unoriented main coefficient is $1/2$, not one. This factor is not automatically applicable to nonreversible Finsler flows. Counting proper iterates is a separate change: with $I(T)=\sum_{k\ge1}N(T/k)$, the aid reports \[ I(T)-N(T)=O(e^{hT/2}/T), \] generally sharp because double iterates already contribute $N(T/2)$ of that order under the usual prime asymptotic. Thus ignoring repeats can impose an $h/2$ relative-error barrier even when primitive counting has a better estimate.

A precise weighted normalization for the reported Chebyshev transfer is \[ \Psi(T)=\sum_{p\text{ primitive}}\ \sum_{k\ell(p)\le T}\ell(p). \] Assume for some $\delta>0$, \[ \Psi(T)=e^{hT}/h+O(e^{(h-\delta)T}). \] The aid reports that removing proper iterates and partial summation yield the reconstructed AIM formula with \[ \varepsilon=\min\{\delta,h/2\}. \] The weight is the primitive length $\ell(p)$, not the iterated length $k\ell(p)$. The estimate is a conditional input; the convention-transfer lemma does not prove the spectral gap required to obtain it.

The source says earlier results cover the intended metrics with $C^1$ stable/unstable bundles, mentioning surfaces and quarter-pinched metrics. Exact curvature, pinching strictness, regularity, and mixing hypotheses are not supplied, so this is retained as a source assessment rather than broadened to all surface metrics. The aid also gives a dated literature warning: the GLP erratum invalidates the original broader contact-bunching spectral-gap proof and restores it only under a stronger homogeneity hypothesis. It says arXiv:2501.02537v10 claims the full contact-Anosov case but is a substantially corrected preprint. No unconditional full-case solution is inferred from that claim, and no exact erratum hypotheses or complete bibliography are supplied. The partial result here is the conditional counting/convention conversion, not a resolution of the whole conjecture.

Short English statement

The aid reconstructs the fragmented conjecture as a primitive oriented prime-orbit count with relative exponential error. Its transfer lemma fixes the orientation factor, proper-iterate contribution, and Chebyshev normalization, while the broad spectral-gap input remains explicitly qualified by the erratum and preprint caveats.
