Math-10781

Rank vs. Genus

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaicodex (gpt-6) Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

For a compact connected orientable $3$-manifold $M$, let $r(M)$ be the minimum size of a generating set of $\pi_1(M)$. A Heegaard splitting writes $M$ as two compression bodies meeting along their positive boundary surface $\Sigma$; a compression body is obtained from $F\times[0,1]$ by adding $1$-handles on $F\times\{1\}$, with handlebodies allowed when the negative boundary is empty. Let $g(M)$ be the least genus of such a $\Sigma$. For closed $M$, both pieces are handlebodies. A hyperbolic manifold has a complete finite-volume metric of sectional curvature $-1$ on its interior; closed hyperbolic manifolds are included.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Does there exist a hyperbolic $3$-manifold $M$ with $r(M)<g(M)$? How large can the additive difference $g(M)-r(M)$ be, and can it be arbitrarily large? [S2]

Short English statement

Compare the least number of fundamental-group generators with the least Heegaard genus, asking whether strict inequality and unbounded gaps occur in hyperbolic three-manifolds. Li proves both for closed orientable examples. [S3]
