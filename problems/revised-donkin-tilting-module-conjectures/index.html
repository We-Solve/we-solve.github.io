Revised Donkin Tilting-Module Conjectures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of prime characteristic $p>0$, and let $G$ be a simple simply connected algebraic group over $k$, with root system $\Phi$ and Coxeter number $h$. The supplied statement asks for two assertions about Donkin's Tilting Module Conjecture, but does not state that conjecture or specify its Frobenius-kernel scope. The following gives an explicit conventional interpretation; its agreement with the intended source formulation requires human review.

Choose a maximal torus and a positive system of roots. Write $X^+$ for the dominant integral weights, $\rho$ for half the sum of the positive roots, and $w_0$ for the longest Weyl-group element. For an integer $r\geq 1$, let $G_r=\ker(F^r:G\longrightarrow G^{(r)})$ be the $r$-th Frobenius kernel, and put \[ X_r=\{\lambda\in X^+:0\leq\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle<p^r\text{ for every simple root }\alpha\}. \] For $\lambda\in X_r$, let $L_G(\lambda)$ be the simple rational $G$-module of highest weight $\lambda$, and let $Q_r(\lambda)$ be the projective cover of $L_G(\lambda)|_{G_r}$ in the category of finite-dimensional $G_r$-modules. For a dominant weight $\mu$, let $T_G(\mu)$ denote the indecomposable rational tilting $G$-module of highest weight $\mu$; a tilting module admits both a Weyl filtration and a dual Weyl filtration. Define the assertion \[ \mathcal D_r(G):\quad T_G\left(2(p^r-1)\rho+w_0\lambda\right)|_{G_r} \cong Q_r(\lambda) \qquad\text{for every }\lambda\in X_r. \] For a fixed root-system type $\Phi$ and a prime $p$, write $\mathcal D(\Phi,p)$ for the assertion that $\mathcal D_r(G)$ holds for every integer $r\geq1$, every algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p$, and every simple simply connected $G/k$ of type $\Phi$.

Requested assertions, conditional on this interpretation. For every irreducible root-system type $\Phi$, prove \[ \left(\forall\text{ primes }p,\ \mathcal D(\Phi,p)\right) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left(\Phi\text{ has type }A_n\text{ for some }n\geq1\text{ or type }B_2\right), \] and prove \[ \forall\text{ primes }p,\qquad p>h\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal D(\Phi,p). \] The first assertion concerns validity for all prime characteristics at a fixed type; it does not assert failure at every prime for other types. The second assertion uses the strict inequality $p>h$ and makes no assertion at $p=h$.

Unresolved source qualification. The supplied original excerpt describes a broad problem of lifting modular representations from Lie algebras to algebraic groups and names a contribution on Donkin's conjectures; it contains neither the two requested assertions nor the displayed module isomorphism. The reconstructed statement names Donkin's Tilting Module Conjecture without specifying whether it means $r=1$, all $r\geq1$, or a formulation retaining a torus action. The definition of $\mathcal D(\Phi,p)$ above is therefore an explicit candidate interpretation, not a source-verified identification. Both requested assertions remain conjectural targets in this draft.

Short English statement

The supplied target says that Donkin's Tilting Module Conjecture should hold in every prime characteristic exactly for types $A_n$ and $B_2$, and should hold in every type when $p>h$. An explicit formulation using projective modules for Frobenius kernels is given conditionally, because the supplied excerpt does not specify the precise version of the conjecture. The supplied open status and reconstruction claim require independent review.
