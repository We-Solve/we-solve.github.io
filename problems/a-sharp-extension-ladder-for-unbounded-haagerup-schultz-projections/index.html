A sharp extension ladder for unbounded Haagerup--Schultz projections

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied verification assigns Question 19 to the unbounded Haagerup--Schultz extension problem. The extraction also contains an independent Ozawa conjecture and the opening of a random-matrix discussion. All are retained separately; the string 40.7 is reported to combine page number 4 and section 0.7.

Primary question. In a finite tracial von Neumann algebra $(M,\tau)$, write $\widetilde M$ for the algebra of affiliated measurable operators and define \[ L^p(M,\tau)=\{T:\tau(|T|^p)<\infty\}\quad(p>0),\qquad M^\Delta=L_{\log}(M,\tau) =\{T:\tau(\log(1+|T|))<\infty\}. \] Does the main Haagerup--Schultz result extend from bounded $T\in M$ to $T\in L^p(M)$, for some or all $p$, to all $M^\Delta$, or to all $\widetilde M$? The source does not specify which part of the original result is meant. A strong interpretation asks for original-algebra, unique, hyperinvariant Brown-splitting projections: for Borel $E\subset{\mathbb{C}}$, a projection $p\in M$ invariant under $T$, with $\tau(p)=\mu_T(E)$, and Brown measures of the two diagonal compressions supported on $E$ and its complement, respectively, with the appropriate normalized restrictions when their traces are nonzero. Hyperinvariance refers to invariance under the bounded commutant of $T$, with the necessary domain conventions for unbounded operators. Brown measure $\mu_T$ in its usual logarithmic-integrability framework is available for $T\in M^\Delta$; no unrestricted Brown measure is specified for all affiliated operators.

The aid reports \[ L^p(M)\subset M^\Delta\quad(p>0), \] and attributes to Dykema, Sukochev and Zanin a Brown-splitting theorem in an enlarged finite algebra containing $M$, applicable to all $M^\Delta$ and hence every positive $p$. An enlarged-algebra splitting projection is not thereby a projection in $M$, and does not establish the full original-algebra uniqueness/hyperinvariance package. The latter general extension remains unverified in the supplied attempt.

The reported class boundaries are strict in every diffuse finite algebra: \[ \bigcup_{p>0}L^p(M)\ \subsetneq\ M^\Delta\ \subsetneq\ \widetilde M. \] In a diffuse abelian copy of $L^\infty(0,1)$, illustrative affiliated multiplication functions are $T_1(s)=e^{1/\sqrt s}$, which is log integrable but belongs to no positive $L^p$, and $T_2(s)=e^{1/s}$, which is measurable but not log integrable. For normal affiliated $T$, the aid reports original-algebra hyperinvariant spectral projections; when $T\in M^\Delta$, these give Brown-splitting projections. The general nonnormal case cannot be transferred by assuming modulus cutoffs are invariant. For example, \[ T=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad |T|=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\quad p=\mathbf1_{[0,3/2]}(|T|)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix} \] has $(1-p)Tp\ne0$, so even this bounded cutoff subspace is not $T$-invariant.

Appended Ozawa conjecture, a separate question. Let $M=L(\mathbb F_r)$ be a free group factor, and let $H$ be a nonzero $M$-$M$ Hilbert correspondence weakly contained in the coarse correspondence $C=L^2(M)\otimes L^2(M)$. The appended conjecture asks whether \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{M\text{-}M} (H\otimes_MH\otimes_MH,\ C)\ne\{0\}. \] Here the tensor products are Connes relative tensor products and $\operatorname{Hom}$ means bounded bimodule intertwiners. Nonzero $H$ is necessary and is not explicit in the extraction. The weak-containment condition is expressed there by continuity of \[ M\odot M^{\mathrm{op}}\longrightarrow B(H),\qquad x\otimes y^{\mathrm{op}}\longmapsto(\xi\mapsto x\xi y) \] for the minimal tensor norm; the opposite algebra makes the right action precise. The source gives examples from the basic construction over a hyperfinite $A\subset M$, interpreted as the completed correspondence $L^2(M)\otimes_A L^2(M)$. This conjecture is not addressed by the aid's affiliated-operator results. The source does not specify the free-group rank range or all correspondence conventions.

Appended Itzykson--Zuber context. The next section introduces random matrix laws $\mu_{A_N},\mu_{B_N}$ and prints \[ \operatorname{IZ}(A_N,B_N)=\iint e^{-N\operatorname{Tr}(AU^*BU)}\, {\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_{A_N}(A)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_{B_N}(B). \] It attributes to Guionnet and Zeitouni an asymptotic $\operatorname{IZ}\sim e^{-N\psi}$ under uniform operator-norm bounds. The displayed integral leaves $U$ and its averaging unspecified, and omits spectral convergence hypotheses and trace/exponential normalizations. It is retained as incomplete reported context, not silently changed into a standard Haar integral or an asserted asymptotic theorem. No further question follows within the supplied record.

Short English statement

The assigned question asks how far Haagerup--Schultz projections extend to unbounded operators. The aid separates log-integrable enlarged-algebra splitting from the stronger original-algebra package and identifies sharp integrability boundaries. The extraction also contains an independent free-group-factor conjecture and incomplete Itzykson--Zuber context, which are preserved without claiming they are resolved.
