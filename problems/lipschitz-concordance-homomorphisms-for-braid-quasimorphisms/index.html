Lipschitz Concordance Homomorphisms for Braid Quasimorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A real-valued quasimorphism on a group $G$ is a function $q:G\to{\mathbb{R}}$ for which there exists $D\geq0$ such that \[ |q(xy)-q(x)-q(y)|\leq D\qquad(x,y\in G). \] It is homogeneous if $q(g^n)=nq(g)$ for every $g\in G$ and $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. For an additive group $C$ equipped with a metric $d$, a real-valued homomorphism $I:C\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is $1$-Lipschitz if \[ I(x+y)=I(x)+I(y),\qquad |I(x)-I(y)|\leq d(x,y)\qquad(x,y\in C). \] Here $C$ and $d$ are placeholders: the supplied record does not specify the concordance group, its category, or its metric.

The supplied question asks whether, for the braid quasimorphism $f$ under discussion, there exist a $1$-Lipschitz concordance homomorphism $I$ and a scalar $r\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that $f=rI$. This equality requires an unspecified comparison between a concordance homomorphism and a function on braids. The background instead describes obtaining braid quasimorphisms by homogenizing knot invariants. If $B$ denotes the intended braid group and $\mathcal H(I):B\to{\mathbb{R}}$ denotes the intended construction from $I$, that reading would give the schematic question \[ \exists I\;\exists r\in{\mathbb{R}}\;\forall\beta\in B, \qquad f(\beta)=r\,\mathcal H(I)(\beta), \] where $I$ must satisfy the homomorphism and Lipschitz conditions above. Neither $B$, the admissible class of $f$, nor $\mathcal H$ is defined in the supplied excerpt, so this is a conditional formulation, not a fully specified mathematical statement.

There is also an unresolved quantifier discrepancy. The question text refers to a particular $f$, whereas the supplied literature assessment describes a single $I$ whose homogenization generates every such braid quasimorphism. If $\mathcal F$ were the intended class, the latter would have the different schematic form \[ \exists I\;\forall f\in\mathcal F\;\exists r_f\in{\mathbb{R}}\;\forall\beta\in B, \qquad f(\beta)=r_f\,\mathcal H(I)(\beta). \] The record does not determine which quantifier order is intended.

The adjacent source passage says that an unnamed theorem answers $[\mathrm{HKK}^{+}20,\ \text{Question }1.6]$, and that this theorem and the slice-Bennequin inequality can be proved by realizing the involved homogeneous quasimorphisms, denoted $\mathrm{wr}$ and $\omega$, respectively, as homogenizations of a certain type of knot invariant. The theorem, the invariants, and the required constructions are not supplied; these contextual claims do not specify or resolve the question above.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a braid quasimorphism arises, up to a real scalar, from a $1$-Lipschitz concordance homomorphism through an intended homogenization construction. The supplied record leaves the domains, metric, construction, admissible quasimorphisms, and quantifier order unresolved. It labels the problem open, but that label and the claimed source verification are supplied curation requiring human review.
