Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.99

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite solvable group and let $A\leq\operatorname{Aut}(G)$. Thus $A$ is finite. Define \[ C_G(A)=\{g\in G: a(g)=g\text{ for every }a\in A\}. \] Write $h(G)$ for the Fitting height of $G$: the least integer $n\geq0$ for which there is a series \[ 1=G_0\leq G_1\leq\cdots\leq G_n=G, \] with every $G_i$ normal in $G$ and every factor $G_i/G_{i-1}$ nilpotent. In particular, $h(1)=0$. Interpret the length of a subgroup chain as its number of strict inclusions, and set \[ l(A)=\max\{r\geq0:1=A_0<A_1<\cdots<A_r=A\}. \] The supplied record does not explicitly specify this chain-length convention; the formulation uses the usual convention above.

The question is whether, for every such $G$ and $A$, \[ C_G(A)=1\quad\text{and}\quad\gcd(|G|,|A|)=1 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad h(G)\leq l(A). \] No solvability assumption is imposed on $A$.

The supplied background says that when $A$ is solvable this question coincides with Kourovka Notebook Problem 5.30. It also reports two results for every finite group $A$, understood in the same setting of coprime automorphisms with trivial common fixed-point subgroup: there exists a finite solvable group $G$ admitting such an action of $A$ with $h(G)=l(A)$; and there exists a finite set of primes $\pi=\pi(A)$ such that every $G$ satisfying the hypotheses above and \[ p\nmid |G|\quad\text{for every }p\in\pi \] satisfies $h(G)\leq l(A)$. These are supplied background assertions attributed to Turull, not independently verified results in this draft. The source marks the main question as open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified literature assessment.

Short English statement

Does every coprime action of a finite group $A$ on a finite solvable group $G$, with $C_G(A)=1$, force the Fitting height of $G$ to be at most the maximum number of strict inclusions in a subgroup chain of $A$? The record attributes the question to A. Turull and supplies partial results and an open-status label that require source review.
