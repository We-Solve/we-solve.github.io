The requested invariants are denoted by \[ \langle\Lambda\tau_k(0)\rangle^{\pi,d}_{g,1}. \] Here $g$ is the genus, the subscript $1$ indicates one marked point, and $d$ is the degree parameter. Problem. With those data fixed as intended in the source, determine the invariants $\langle\Lambda\tau_k(0)\rangle^{\pi,d}_{g,1}$ in general, including higher genus, for all admissible choices of the parameters and insertion. The source explicitly states that degeneration of the target elliptic curve to a rational curve is not allowed in this setting.
