Ends of Critical Long-Range Percolation Clusters

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns one-dimensional long-range percolation at decay exponent $s=2$. In the usual lattice interpretation, let $G_\beta$ be a random undirected graph on ${\mathbb{Z}}$, with edge probabilities depending on displacement and satisfying \[ p_\beta(k):=\mathbb P_\beta\left(\{0,k\}\in E(G_\beta)\right),\qquad \lim_{|k|\to\infty}\frac{p_\beta(k)}{\beta |k|^{-2}}=1, \qquad \beta>0. \] The record does not specify the complete edge law, independence of distinct edges, probabilities at finite displacements, or the parameter family used to define criticality. Thus this notation describes the conventional interpretation of the stated tail, rather than a fully specified probability model.

For a connected, locally finite, infinite graph $C$, a ray is a one-way infinite simple path. Two rays determine the same end if, after deletion of any finite vertex set, tails of both rays lie in the same connected component. Write $e(C)$ for the number of ends. Equivalently, for the distinctions requested here, \[ e(C)=2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \sup_{\substack{F\subseteq V(C)\\F\text{ finite}}}N_\infty(C\setminus F)=2, \] where $N_\infty$ counts infinite connected components; $C$ has infinitely many ends precisely when this supremum is unbounded. No particular infinite cardinality of ends is asserted.

If $\beta$ is the intended parameter of a specified monotone family, write \[ \beta_c:=\inf\left\{\beta>0: \mathbb P_\beta\left(G_\beta\text{ has an infinite connected component}\right)>0\right\}. \] The source asks whether both of the following assertions hold at $s=2$:

  1. At criticality, the infinite cluster has exactly two ends.
  2. In the supercritical regime, the infinite cluster has infinitely many ends.
For a specified model having an almost surely unique infinite cluster $C_\infty$ at the relevant parameters, the intended almost-sure assertions would read \[ \mathbb P_{\beta_c}\left(e(C_\infty)=2\right)=1, \qquad \mathbb P_\beta\left(C_\infty\text{ has infinitely many ends}\right)=1 \quad\text{for every supercritical }\beta. \] These displays require existence and uniqueness of $C_\infty$, including at criticality. The supplied record does not establish those hypotheses, specify whether the critical assertion is conditional on existence, or explicitly give the almost-sure quantifier. If more than one infinite cluster can occur, it also does not specify which cluster, or whether every infinite cluster, is intended. These qualifications remain unresolved; the tail asymptotic alone does not supply the missing model specification.

Short English statement

For one-dimensional long-range percolation with edge probabilities asymptotic to $\beta |k|^{-2}$, the supplied question asks whether the critical infinite cluster has exactly two ends and a supercritical infinite cluster has infinitely many ends. The full model, the meaning of criticality, and the existence, uniqueness, and probabilistic quantifiers for the infinite cluster require clarification.
