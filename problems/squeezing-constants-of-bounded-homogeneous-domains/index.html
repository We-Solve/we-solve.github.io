Squeezing Constants of Bounded Homogeneous Domains

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual finite-dimensional formulation, let $n\geq 1$ and let $D\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ be a bounded domain, meaning a nonempty connected open set. Write $\mathbb B^n=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^n:\|z\|_2<1\}$. For $p\in D$, define the squeezing function by \[ s_D(p)=\sup\left\{r\in(0,1]:\text{there exists an injective holomorphic map }f:D\to\mathbb B^n,\ f(p)=0,\ r\mathbb B^n\subseteq f(D)\right\}. \] The domain $D$ is homogeneous if its group $\operatorname{Aut}(D)$ of biholomorphic self-maps acts transitively: for every $p,q\in D$, there exists $\phi\in\operatorname{Aut}(D)$ with $\phi(p)=q$. Biholomorphic invariance of the squeezing function then makes $s_D$ constant; denote its value by $s(D)$.

Problem. Determine $s(D)$ for every bounded homogeneous domain $D\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$, with $n\geq1$ arbitrary.

Unresolved scope qualifications. The supplied question does not explicitly restrict the ambient dimension. The definition above states its conventional finite-dimensional interpretation. The supplied background also mentions infinite-dimensional finite-rank bounded symmetric factors, but specifies neither an ambient space nor a squeezing-function definition for a general infinite-dimensional formulation. Thus an intended extension to infinite-dimensional bounded homogeneous domains remains unspecified. The word "compute" is also not accompanied by a required form of formula or effective procedure.

Short English statement

For each bounded homogeneous complex domain, determine the common value of its squeezing function, which measures how large a centered Euclidean ball can fit inside an injective holomorphic image of the domain in the unit ball. The displayed formulation uses finite-dimensional domains; any intended infinite-dimensional scope requires clarification.
