Critical Self-Similar Profiles for Focusing Cubic Wave Blowup

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\in{\mathbb{N}}$ with $d\geq5$. In the usual real-valued normalization, the focusing cubic wave equation is \[ \partial_t^2u-\Delta_xu=u^3. \] Here $\Delta_x=\sum_{j=1}^d\partial_{x_j}^2$. This normalization explains the conventional equation name; the supplied record does not specify the spatial domain, boundary conditions, solution regularity, initial-data space, or whether radial symmetry is assumed.

For terminology, the usual backward self-similar ansatz about a time $T$ and a spatial point $x_0$ is \[ u(t,x)=\frac{1}{T-t}\,f\!\left(\frac{x-x_0}{T-t}\right),\qquad t<T, \] on a region where this expression is defined. The symbol $f$ denotes a time-independent profile. In a radial formulation, one instead writes the profile as a function of $\lvert x-x_0\rvert/(T-t)$. The record does not specify which formulation is intended, the profile domain, or a formula or characterizing conditions for its distinguished profile $f^*$.

Source question, with unresolved qualifications. For the focusing cubic wave equation in dimensions $d\geq5$, does the source-designated profile $f^*$ describe solutions at the threshold between stable blowup and global forward existence, and also describe solutions near that threshold during at least some intermediate times?

Here global forward existence means existence for all future times in the intended solution class. A precise meaning of stable blowup requires a topology on initial data and a specified blowup behavior that persists under sufficiently small perturbations. A precise threshold likewise requires the intended initial-data space and a definition of the separating set. The source does not supply these choices. It also does not define the rescaling, norm, convergence or approximation criterion, time regime, or quantifiers intended by "describe" and "at least some intermediate times." Consequently, the question above preserves the supplied target but cannot be promoted to a fully quantified conjecture without additional source information.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the distinguished self-similar profile $f^*$ governs solutions at the boundary between stable blowup and global existence, and near that boundary during at least some intermediate times, for $d\geq5$. The supplied record leaves $f^*$, the solution setting, and the required approximation behavior undefined. Its open status is supplied dated curation.
