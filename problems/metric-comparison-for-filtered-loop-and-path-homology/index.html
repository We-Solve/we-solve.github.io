Metric comparison for filtered loop and path homology

Definitions and mathematical statement

Paternain's question asks how the sums of Betti numbers of length-filtered free-loop and fixed-endpoint path spaces grow, and whether this growth depends on the metric. The supplied text has damaged filtration notation. Following the aid, let $M$ be closed, fix a coefficient field $\mathbb k$, and use either the Sobolev free-loop space $X=\Lambda M$ or path space $X=\Omega(p,q)$. For a smooth Riemannian metric $g$, set \[ L_g(\gamma)=\int_0^1|\dot\gamma|_g\,du,\qquad X_g(T)=\{\gamma\in X:L_g(\gamma)\leq T\},\qquad B_g(T)=\sum_{i\geq0}\dim_{\mathbb k}H_i(X_g(T);\mathbb k). \] Sums may be infinite unless finiteness hypotheses are supplied. This is a length filtration, not a silently substituted energy filtration. The source asks about the raw $B_g$, which need not be monotone because sublevel classes can disappear under inclusion.

The source also attributes to [Gromov2001] a lower bound, for simply connected $M$, of the intended form \[ B_g(CN)\geq\sum_{i=0}^N b_i(\Lambda M), \] for a suitable constant $C$. The original superscripts and quantifiers are damaged; a complete citation and the precise constant, coefficient and filtration conventions are not supplied.

The paired aid treats persistent ranks instead. For $m\geq0$, define \[ R_g^{\leq m}(s,t)=\sum_{i=0}^m \operatorname{rank}\left(H_i(X_g(s))\to H_i(X_g(t))\right), \] \[ P_g^{\leq m}(T)=\sum_{i=0}^m \operatorname{rank}\left(H_i(X_g(T))\to H_i(X)\right). \] All homology uses $\mathbb k$. These image ranks differ from raw sublevel Betti sums.

If $A\geq1$ satisfies $A^{-2}g\leq h\leq A^2g$ as quadratic forms, then $A^{-1}L_g\leq L_h\leq AL_g$. Thus the two filtrations are multiplicatively $A$-interleaved. The reported two-scale consequence, for $t\geq A^2s$, is \[ R_g^{\leq m}(s,t)\leq R_h^{\leq m}(As,t/A), \] with the same statement after exchanging $g,h$. The image-to-total ranks obey \[ P_g^{\leq m}(T/A)\leq P_h^{\leq m}(T)\leq P_g^{\leq m}(AT). \] The analogous all-degree statements require meaningful finite sums. For a fixed total homology class, its minimax length, defined as the infimum of levels realizing that class in total homology, is also comparable by factors $A^{-1},A$.

The aid concludes that the polynomial growth exponent and qualitative exponential-growth type of these image-rank functions are independent of the smooth metric, whenever the chosen growth quantities are defined. Numerical exponential rates need only be comparable after rescaling length; equality is not asserted. These comparisons do not determine the growth or metric dependence of the nonmonotone raw Betti sum asked about in the original question.

Short English statement

Uniform metric comparison rescales lengths and interleaves the loop and path filtrations. The supplied aid deduces metric-independent growth type for persistent-to-total image ranks, plus a two-scale persistent-rank inequality. It explicitly leaves the original raw sublevel Betti-sum question unresolved.
