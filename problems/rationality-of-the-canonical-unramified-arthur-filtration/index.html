Rationality of the canonical unramified Arthur filtration

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 014; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that the canonical unramified Arthur filtration on all finitely supported automorphic functions is rational under the restricted theory's characteristic hypotheses for split connected semisimple groups.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $X/\mathbb F_q$ be smooth projective geometrically connected and $G$ split connected semisimple. On $H=G_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q}$ assume an invariant nondegenerate Lie-algebra form nondegenerate on all Levi centers, Chevalley restriction for every Levi, semisimple Lie-algebra centralizers being Levi, and every nilpotent element over any extension lying in a parabolic unipotent-radical Lie algebra. For $E=\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}$, $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q$, let $S$ be bundle isomorphism classes over $\mathbb F_q$ and $\mathcal A_E=E^{(S)}$. For a closed invariant $Y$ in the pinned dual-group nilpotent cone, use the derived-Satake support category $\mathcal C_Y$ and Frobenius $\Phi$ to define \[ \mathcal F_Y\mathcal A_E=\operatorname{im}(H^0\operatorname{Tr}(\Phi,\mathcal C_Y)\to\mathcal A_E). \] The claim says this entire canonical filtration is rational: \[ E\otimes_{\mathbb Q}(\mathcal A_{\mathbb Q}\cap\mathcal F_Y\mathcal A_E) \xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal F_Y\mathcal A_E \] for every such $Y$. The intersection is inside $\mathcal A_E$. Noncuspidal functions are included, and supported traces are defined by their image without assuming concentration in degree zero.

Outline of the argument
