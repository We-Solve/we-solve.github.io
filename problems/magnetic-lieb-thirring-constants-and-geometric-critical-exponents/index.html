Magnetic Lieb–Thirring Constants and Geometric Critical Exponents

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns magnetic Lieb--Thirring inequalities, but does not specify the operator class, regularity assumptions, or the precise comparison constant. The following is an explicit conventional scalar formulation on smooth Euclidean test data; extending it to the full intended class requires clarification.

Fix an integer $n\geq 1$ and $1\leq\gamma<3/2$. Let $A\in C^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}}^n)$ be a real magnetic vector potential, with magnetic field represented by $B_{jk}=\partial_jA_k-\partial_kA_j$. For a real potential $V\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n)$, let \[ H_{A,V}=(-i\nabla-A)^2+V \] be the self-adjoint operator obtained from the Friedrichs extension of its quadratic form initially defined on $C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n)$. Write $V_-(x)=\max\{-V(x),0\}$ and \[ S_\gamma(A,V)=\operatorname{Tr}(H_{A,V})_-^\gamma. \] Here the negative part is defined by spectral calculus; when the negative spectrum is discrete, this trace is the sum of $|\lambda|^\gamma$ over negative eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity. The trace is allowed to be infinite.

For this test class define \[ L_{\gamma,n}^{\mathrm{mag}} =\sup_{\substack{A\in C^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}}^n),\ V\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})\\ \int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}V_-^{\gamma+n/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x>0}} \frac{S_\gamma(A,V)}{\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}V_-^{\gamma+n/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}, \qquad L_{\gamma,n}^{0} =\sup_{\substack{V\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{R}})\\ \int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}V_-^{\gamma+n/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x>0}} \frac{S_\gamma(0,V)}{\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}V_-^{\gamma+n/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}. \] The Euclidean questions are whether a finite constant independent of $A$ and $V$ gives \[ S_\gamma(A,V)\leq L_{\gamma,n}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}V_-^{\gamma+n/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x, \] and, more specifically, whether magnetic fields alter the optimal constant, expressed in this formulation as the question \[ L_{\gamma,n}^{\mathrm{mag}}\stackrel{?}{=}L_{\gamma,n}^{0}. \] The original statement particularly asks whether, for $n=3$ and $\gamma=1$, there are magnetic and electric potentials violating the intended classical comparison bound.

The phrase "classical optimal constant" is unresolved in the supplied record. If it means the optimal nonmagnetic constant, the proposed counterexample would satisfy \[ S_1(A,V)>L_{1,3}^{0}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^3}V_-^{5/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] If it instead means the semiclassical phase-space constant \[ L_{\gamma,n}^{\mathrm{cl}}=(2\pi)^{-n}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}(1-|p|^2)_+^\gamma\,{\,\mathrm{d}} p, \qquad t_+=\max\{t,0\}, \] then the proposed counterexample would use $L_{1,3}^{\mathrm{cl}}$ in that inequality. These two comparison targets must not be identified without further source clarification. The record also does not specify whether "Li--Yau/Lieb--Thirring bound" refers to this negative-eigenvalue moment inequality, an eigenvalue-sum formulation, or a particular sharp constant.

The geometric question asks for Lieb--Thirring inequalities on Riemannian manifolds and for the dependence of their critical exponents on geometry. A conventional candidate, after fixing an $n$-dimensional Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$, a self-adjoint realization, and admissible potentials, is \[ \operatorname{Tr}(-\Delta_g+V)_-^\gamma \leq C_{\gamma,M,g}\int_M V_-^{\gamma+n/2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\operatorname{vol}_g, \] with $C_{\gamma,M,g}<\infty$ independent of $V$. A magnetic version would replace $-\Delta_g$ by a specified magnetic Laplacian and would require an explicit decision about uniformity in the magnetic field. One can then ask which $\gamma$ admit the chosen inequality, and how that set depends on geometry. The supplied record does not determine the manifold class, boundary conditions, admissible operator and potential classes, allowable geometric dependence of constants, or whether additional terms are permitted. Consequently it does not determine a unique manifold inequality or a unique meaning of its critical exponent.

The supplied dated assessment reports universal magnetic comparison bounds and identifies sharp magnetic constants, including the $n=3,\gamma=1$ counterexample question, as unresolved. This is a supplied qualification, not an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether arbitrary magnetic fields preserve the intended Lieb--Thirring bound and its optimal constant for $1\leq\gamma<3/2$, especially when $n=3$ and $\gamma=1$. It also asks which analogous inequalities hold on manifolds and how their critical exponents depend on geometry. The comparison constant and several analytic and geometric hypotheses remain unspecified; the displayed smooth scalar formulation is conditional and requires human review.
