Nonreduced Special Linear Systems on Plane Blowups

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\pi:X\to\mathbb{P}^{2}_{k}$ be the blowup at $r$ distinct very general points $p_1,\ldots,p_r$. Write $H$ for the pullback of the class of a line and $E_i$ for the exceptional divisor above $p_i$. The source does not specify the base field $k$ or its characteristic. The usual geometric meaning of "very general" is outside a countable union of proper closed subsets of the parameter space of ordered distinct point configurations; the field hypotheses needed for this interpretation remain unspecified.

Let \[ D=dH-\sum_{i=1}^{r}m_iE_i. \] For an integral divisor $D$, its coefficients $d,m_i$ are integers. The source does not explicitly impose nonnegativity or give their intended ranges. Let $|D|$ denote the complete linear system of effective divisors linearly equivalent to $D$, with \[ \dim|D|=\dim_k H^0(X,\mathcal{O}_X(D))-1, \] where $\dim\varnothing=-1$. Define the displayed expected dimension by \[ e(D)=\max\left\{\binom{d+2}{2}-\sum_{i=1}^{r}\binom{m_i+1}{2}-1,-1\right\}. \] The binomial terms have their ordinary combinatorial meaning when $d,m_i\geq0$; their intended interpretation outside that range is not specified by the source. Call $|D|$ special, in the sense of this record, when $\dim|D|>e(D)$.

For an effective divisor $A=\sum_j a_jC_j$, with distinct prime divisors $C_j$ and positive integer coefficients $a_j$, nonreduced means that some $a_j\geq2$. The supplied question is whether, for every choice of data in the intended scope, \[ \dim|D|>e(D) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \forall A\in|D|\;\exists j\;(a_j\geq2). \] Here the prime components and coefficients refer to each individual divisor $A$ on $X$. The extracted statement gives no restriction on $r$, whereas the background describes the SHGH conjecture for at least ten points. Thus whether the intended question assumes $r\geq10$, together with the ground-field and coefficient hypotheses, requires clarification before this becomes a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every effective divisor in a special complete linear system on a plane blowup at very general points must contain a repeated irreducible component. The source leaves the ground field and numerical scope incomplete, and its background mentions $r\geq10$ although the statement does not.
