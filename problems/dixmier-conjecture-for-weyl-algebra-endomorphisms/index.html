Math-956

Dixmier Conjecture for Weyl-Algebra Endomorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, the complex Weyl algebra is the unital associative algebra \[ A_n({\mathbb{C}})={\mathbb{C}}\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n,D_1,\ldots,D_n\rangle \big/\left([X_i,X_j],\ [D_i,D_j],\ [D_i,X_j]-\delta_{ij}1\right)_{1\leq i,j\leq n}, \] where $[a,b]=ab-ba$ and $\delta_{ij}$ is the Kronecker symbol. The angle brackets denote the free associative algebra. This makes explicit that the source's polynomial-style notation is subject to noncommutative Weyl relations: the $X_i$ commute among themselves and the $D_i$ commute among themselves, but the two families do not all commute.

The Dixmier conjecture, named in the original record, asks whether \[ \forall n\geq1\ \forall\phi\in\operatorname{End}_{{\mathbb{C}}\text{-}\mathrm{alg}}(A_n({\mathbb{C}})), \qquad \phi\in\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathbb{C}}\text{-}\mathrm{alg}}(A_n({\mathbb{C}})). \] Endomorphisms are unital ${\mathbb{C}}$-algebra maps and fix the scalar field pointwise. The conclusion asks for a ${\mathbb{C}}$-algebra map $\psi$ with $\psi\phi=\phi\psi=\operatorname{id}$. No assumption of degree preservation, continuity, filtration compatibility or prior surjectivity is made.

Equivalently, images $Q_i=\phi(X_i)$, $P_i=\phi(D_i)$ must satisfy \[ [Q_i,Q_j]=[P_i,P_j]=0,\qquad [P_i,Q_j]=\delta_{ij}1, \] and the question is whether every resulting unital endomorphism has an inverse. These are elements of the Weyl algebra, not merely commuting polynomial functions with a prescribed Jacobian. The field and the number of generator pairs remain those in the source; no passage to another characteristic, completion or additional generators is substituted.

The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports no authoritative complete resolution of the extracted conjecture located in its search. The open label and verification note reporting removal of the label and notation cleanup remain dated metadata, not independently verified current status.

Short English statement

Does every unital complex-algebra endomorphism of each Weyl algebra have an inverse? The noncommutative relations, fixed scalar field and unrestricted endomorphism class are explicit. No filtration-preserving or commutative-polynomial version is substituted.
