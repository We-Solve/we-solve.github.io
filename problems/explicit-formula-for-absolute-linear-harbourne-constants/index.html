Explicit Formula for Absolute Linear Harbourne Constants

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $d\geq2$. A prime power means an integer $p^r$, where $p$ is prime and $r\geq1$ is an integer. Define \[ q=q(d)=\min\{p^r:d\leq p^{2r}+p^r+1,\ p\text{ prime},\ r\geq1\},\qquad i=q^2+q+1-d. \] For this value of $q$, set \[ m_1(i)=q+1-i,\qquad m_2(i)=2q+1-i, \] \[ \varepsilon_1(i)=\begin{cases}1,&0\leq i\leq q-1,\\0,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases} \qquad \varepsilon_2(i)=\begin{cases}1,&i>q+1,\\0,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases} \] and \[ \begin{aligned} t_{q-1}(i)&=\begin{cases}qi-q^2-q,&i>q+1,\\0,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}\\ t_q(i)&=\begin{cases}qi,&i\leq q+1,\\2q^2-(i-2)q-1,&i>q+1,\end{cases}\\ t_{q+1}(i)&=\begin{cases}q^2+q-iq,&i\leq q+1,\\0,&i>q+1.\end{cases} \end{aligned} \] Define \[ h(d)=\frac{\begin{aligned}&q^2+q+1-i-\varepsilon_1(i)m_1(i)-\varepsilon_2(i)m_2(i)\\&\quad-t_{q-1}(i)(q-1)-t_q(i)q-t_{q+1}(i)(q+1)\end{aligned}}{\varepsilon_1(i)+\varepsilon_2(i)+t_{q-1}(i)+t_q(i)+t_{q+1}(i)}. \] The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every integer $d\geq2$, the quantity called the absolute linear Harbourne constant $H(d)$ satisfies \[ \begin{aligned} i\leq2q-2&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H(d)=h(d),\\ i=2q-1&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H(d)=-\frac{q^3-q^2+2q-2}{q^2+q-1}. \end{aligned} \] No assertion for $i>2q-1$ is supplied. The record does not define $H(d)$: in particular, it does not specify the ambient geometry, admissible line configurations, base fields or characteristics, or the extremum and normalization defining this constant. Thus these are the reported conjectural identities, with the meaning of $H(d)$ requiring clarification from the cited source before the statement is fully self-contained.

Short English statement

The conjecture predicts explicit values of the absolute linear Harbourne constant for the two stated ranges of $i=q^2+q+1-d$, where $q$ is the least eligible prime power. The definition and geometric scope of $H(d)$ are missing from the supplied record. Its partially solved status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
