Vogan Conjecture for Quasisplit Classical Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a $p$-adic field, and let $G$ be a quasisplit group of one of the forms $\operatorname{GL}_n$, $\operatorname{SO}_{2n+1}$, or $\operatorname{Sp}_{2n}$. The supplied formulation concerns the trivial twisting datum $\delta$. The record does not specify further restrictions on $F$, its residue characteristic, or the rank range; the scope of the cited formulation therefore requires confirmation.

Write $W_F$ for the Weil group of $F$, $\widehat G$ for the complex dual group, and ${}^{L}G$ for the $L$-group. Let \[ \psi:W_F\times\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})\times\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})\longrightarrow{}^{L}G \] be a local Arthur parameter. Denote its associated Langlands parameter by $\phi_\psi$, and its associated infinitesimal parameter by $\lambda$. The supplied transcription of the formula constructing $\phi_\psi$ is corrupted, so that formula is not reconstructed here. The precise admissibility conditions and parameter conventions must be taken from the cited source.

Let $\Pi_\psi(G)$ denote the local Arthur packet of smooth irreducible representations of $G(F)$, defined through Arthur's endoscopic classification in the classical-group cases. Let $\Pi_\lambda(G)$ denote the corresponding collection with infinitesimal parameter $\lambda$, and let $K\Pi_\lambda(G)$ denote its group of virtual representations, with generators $[\pi]$. For $\pi\in\Pi_\lambda(G)$, write $\mathcal P(\pi)$ for the corresponding equivariant perverse sheaf in the geometric construction used by the source. Write $\operatorname{Evs}_\psi$ for its vanishing-cycle functor and $\operatorname{NEvs}_\psi$ for the normalization used there. The supplied packet definition is \[ \Pi^{\mathrm{ABV}}_{\phi_\psi}(G) = \left\{\pi\in\Pi_\lambda(G): \operatorname{Evs}_\psi\left(\mathcal P(\pi)\right)\ne0\right\}. \] Here ABV denotes the Adams--Barbasch--Vogan construction. The geometric spaces, equivariance conventions, representation--sheaf correspondence, and normalization are not defined in the supplied record.

Let $\eta_\psi,\eta_{\psi,s}\in K\Pi_\lambda(G)$ be the virtual representations serving as analogues of Arthur's stable distributions and their endoscopic transfers, respectively. The source refers to Section 3.9 of its reference [1] for these objects. Let $\eta^{\operatorname{Evs}}_{\phi_\psi}$ and $\eta^{\operatorname{NEvs}}_{\phi_\psi,s}$ denote the virtual representations whose coefficients are prescribed by the respective functors; the source refers to Section 8.2 of [1]. For a parameter $\psi$, put \[ Z_{\widehat G}(\psi) =\left\{g\in\widehat G: g\psi(u)g^{-1}=\psi(u)\text{ for every }u \in W_F\times\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})\times\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}}) \right\}. \]

The requested quasisplit, trivial-$\delta$ version of Vogan's conjecture is the following collection of assertions, for every local Arthur parameter $\psi$ in the scope of the cited construction: \begin{align*} \Pi_\psi(G)&=\Pi^{\mathrm{ABV}}_{\phi_\psi}(G),\\ \eta_\psi&=\eta^{\operatorname{Evs}}_{\phi_\psi},\\ \eta_{\psi,s}&=\eta^{\operatorname{NEvs}}_{\phi_\psi,s} \qquad\text{for every semisimple }s\in Z_{\widehat G}(\psi). \end{align*} The last two equalities are equalities of the specified virtual representations. In particular, the original formulation uses $\operatorname{Evs}$ for the stable-distribution assertion and its normalization $\operatorname{NEvs}$ for the endoscopic assertion.

For $G=\operatorname{GL}_n$, the record states that \[ \Pi_\psi(G)=\Pi_{\phi_\psi}(G)=\{\pi_\psi\}, \qquad \eta_\psi=[\pi_\psi], \] where $\Pi_{\phi_\psi}(G)$ is the ordinary Langlands packet. It presents the remaining geometric comparison as \[ \Pi^{\mathrm{ABV}}_{\phi_\psi}(G)=\{\pi_\psi\}, \qquad \eta^{\operatorname{Evs}}_{\phi_\psi}=[\pi_\psi]. \] Its displayed coefficient expression is schematic: \[ \eta^{\operatorname{Evs}}_{\phi_\psi} = \sum_{\pi\in\Pi^{\mathrm{ABV}}_{\phi_\psi}(G)} \pm\dim\!\left(\operatorname{Evs}_\psi(\mathcal P(\pi))\right)[\pi]. \] The signs and dimension conventions are not specified in the supplied excerpt and must not be selected arbitrarily. The excerpt also describes a reduction using a nondegenerate pairing: equality of these virtual representations is equivalent to \[ \left\langle\eta^{\operatorname{Evs}}_{\phi_\psi},\mathcal F\right\rangle_\lambda = \left\langle[\pi_\psi],\mathcal F\right\rangle_\lambda \quad\text{for every }\mathcal F\in K\operatorname{Per}_{H_\lambda}(V_\lambda). \] Here the geometric group, space, category, and pairing retain the source's notation and require its definitions. The source says that stability and pure-inner-form issues do not arise in its general-linear discussion. Its final sentence begins a reduction through an irreducible parameter of a Levi subgroup but is truncated; no additional assertion is extracted from it.

These are reference-dependent statements: the missing geometric definitions and normalization conventions prevent this draft from being a fully self-contained formulation. The record reports the general-linear case as a theorem and labels the overall problem partially solved; these are supplied source claims, not independent mathematical verification.

Short English statement

For the quasisplit groups $\operatorname{GL}_n$, $\operatorname{SO}_{2n+1}$, and $\operatorname{Sp}_{2n}$, with trivial $\delta$, the conjecture identifies every local Arthur packet with its associated ABV packet. It also identifies Arthur's stable virtual representations with those computed by $\operatorname{Evs}$, and their endoscopic transfers with those computed by $\operatorname{NEvs}$, for every semisimple element of the parameter centralizer. Exact geometric conventions require the cited source. The supplied dated assessment reports the general-linear case and tempered classical-group cases as established, while the full classical-group assertions remain incomplete; this assessment is not independently verified.
