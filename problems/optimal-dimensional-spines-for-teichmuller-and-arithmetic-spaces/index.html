Math-843

Optimal-Dimensional Spines for Teichmüller and Arithmetic Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A spine of a topological space $Y$ is understood here as a subspace $S\subseteq Y$ admitting a strong deformation retraction: a continuous map $H:Y\times[0,1]\to Y$ such that \[ H(y,0)=y,\qquad H(y,1)\in S,\qquad H(s,t)=s \] for every $y\in Y$, $s\in S$, and $t\in[0,1]$. If a group $\Gamma$ acts on $Y$, a $\Gamma$-equivariant spine additionally requires $S$ to be $\Gamma$-invariant and \[ H(\gamma y,t)=\gamma H(y,t) \quad(\gamma\in\Gamma,\ y\in Y,\ t\in[0,1]). \] This is an explicit interpretation of the word "spine"; the supplied record does not specify its precise deformation-retraction, dimensional, or cocompactness requirements.

For a closed, connected, oriented surface $\Sigma_g$ of genus $g$, its Teichmüller space $\mathcal T_g$ is the space of marked conformal structures: pairs $(R,f)$, where $R$ is a Riemann surface and $f:\Sigma_g\to R$ is an orientation-preserving marking, modulo conformal isomorphisms compatible with the markings up to isotopy. Under the customary closed-surface interpretation with integer $g\geq2$, the first requested construction is a spine $S_g\subseteq\mathcal T_g$ satisfying \[ \dim S_g=4g-5. \] The genus range and absence of punctures or boundary are conventional interpretations, not explicit hypotheses of the supplied statement. The record also does not explicitly require equivariance under the mapping class group $\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g)$, the group of isotopy classes of orientation-preserving self-homeomorphisms of $\Sigma_g$. Whether such equivariance and a compact quotient are intended must be clarified; these conditions materially affect the target.

The arithmetic request in the supplied record is: for every nonuniform arithmetic subgroup $\Gamma<G$ acting on the associated symmetric space $X$, construct a $\Gamma$-equivariant spine $S\subseteq X$ with \[ \dim S=\operatorname{vcd}(\Gamma) =\dim X-\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(\Gamma). \] Here "nonuniform" conventionally means that $\Gamma$ is a lattice with noncompact quotient, and $\operatorname{vcd}(\Gamma)$ denotes the cohomological dimension over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ of a torsion-free subgroup of finite index, when such a subgroup exists. The notation $\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(\Gamma)$ requires a specified ambient algebraic group defined over ${\mathbb{Q}}$; in that setting the rational rank is the dimension of a maximal ${\mathbb{Q}}$-split torus of that algebraic group. The record supplies neither this algebraic group nor the structural hypotheses on $G$ and $X$ needed to interpret the displayed dimension identity. That identity is therefore retained as part of the source formulation, with its hypotheses unresolved. The required notion of dimension, any cellular structure, and any compact-quotient requirement also remain unspecified.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks for a spine of Teichmüller space of dimension $4g-5$, and, in an arithmetic setting, an equivariant spine whose dimension equals the virtual cohomological dimension. The intended genus range, mapping class group equivariance, quotient compactness, dimension convention, and arithmetic hypotheses require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
