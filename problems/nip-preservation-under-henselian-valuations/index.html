Math-3833

NIP Preservation under Henselian Valuations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the standard pure field language $L_{\mathrm{ring}}=\{0,1,+,-,\cdot\}$. A complete first-order theory $T$ has the independence property if there is a formula $\varphi(\bar x;\bar y)$ such that, for every integer $n\geq 1$, some model $M\models T$ contains tuples $\bar a_1,\ldots,\bar a_n$ and $\bar b_S$, one for each $S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$, satisfying \[ M\models\varphi(\bar a_i;\bar b_S)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad i\in S. \] The theory is NIP if it does not have the independence property. A structure is NIP when its complete theory is NIP.

A valuation on a field $K$ is a map $v:K\to\Gamma\cup\{\infty\}$, where $\Gamma$ is an ordered abelian group, such that $v(0)=\infty$, the restriction $K^\times\to\Gamma$ is surjective, and \[ v(ab)=v(a)+v(b),\qquad v(a+b)\geq\min\{v(a),v(b)\}. \] Here $\infty$ is larger than every element of $\Gamma$, with $\infty+\gamma=\infty$. Write \[ \mathcal O_v=\{a\in K:v(a)\geq0\},\qquad \mathfrak m_v=\{a\in K:v(a)>0\},\qquad Kv=\mathcal O_v/\mathfrak m_v. \] The valuation is henselian if, for every monic polynomial $f\in\mathcal O_v[X]$ and every $\alpha\in Kv$ with $\overline f(\alpha)=0$ and $\overline f'(\alpha)\neq0$, there is $a\in\mathcal O_v$ reducing to $\alpha$ such that $f(a)=0$. Interpret the valued field as the structure $(K,\mathcal O_v)$ in $L_{\mathrm{val}}=L_{\mathrm{ring}}\cup\{\mathcal O\}$, with a unary predicate for the valuation ring.

Question. For every field $K$ and every henselian valuation $v$ on $K$, does \[ \operatorname{Th}_{L_{\mathrm{ring}}}(K)\text{ is NIP} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Th}_{L_{\mathrm{val}}}(K,\mathcal O_v)\text{ is NIP} \] hold? The supplied statement imposes no characteristic restriction and does not exclude the trivial valuation.

Unresolved qualifications. The source record does not explicitly specify the languages; the formulation above uses the standard pure field language and valuation-ring predicate convention. Its supplied literature assessment instead discusses henselian expansions with NIP residue field in unspecified remaining imperfect or separably closed cases. That wording does not state the same hypothesis as NIP of $K$, and the record supplies no equivalence or precise description of those remaining cases. It is retained as a qualification of the supplied literature assessment, not as a replacement or restriction of the question.

Short English statement

Must adjoining a predicate for any henselian valuation ring to an NIP field preserve NIP? The question uses the standard pure field and valuation-ring languages. The supplied residue-field-based literature assessment has a different hypothesis and requires clarification.
