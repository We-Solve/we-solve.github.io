Scattering for the Quintic Resonant System

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $x\in{\mathbb{R}}$, $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$, and $j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2$. Write $|j|$ for the Euclidean norm and $\langle j\rangle=(1+|j|^2)^{1/2}$. For each $j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2$, define the resonance set \[ \mathcal R(j)=\left\{(j_1,\ldots,j_5)\in({\mathbb{Z}}^2)^5:\begin{array}{l} j_1-j_2+j_3-j_4+j_5=j,\\ |j_1|^2-|j_2|^2+|j_3|^2-|j_4|^2+|j_5|^2=|j|^2 \end{array}\right\}. \] The quintic resonant system for complex-valued functions $\vec u=(u_j)_{j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}$ is \[ i\partial_tu_j+\partial_{xx}u_j =\sum_{(j_1,\ldots,j_5)\in\mathcal R(j)} u_{j_1}\overline{u_{j_2}}u_{j_3}\overline{u_{j_4}}u_{j_5}, \qquad j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2. \] Define the weighted space \[ \mathcal H=\left\{\vec f=(f_j)_{j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}: f_j\in L^2({\mathbb{R}};{\mathbb{C}}),\quad \sum_{j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}\langle j\rangle^2\|f_j\|_{L^2({\mathbb{R}})}^2<\infty\right\}, \qquad \|\vec f\|_{\mathcal H}^2=\sum_{j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}\langle j\rangle^2\|f_j\|_{L^2({\mathbb{R}})}^2, \] and \[ E_{\mathrm{ls}}(\vec f)=\frac12\|\vec f\|_{\mathcal H}^2. \] Source question (Conjecture 2, as identified by the supplied registry). For every $E>0$ and every smooth initial datum $\vec u_0\in\mathcal H$ satisfying $E_{\mathrm{ls}}(\vec u_0)\le E$, does there exist a solution of the displayed system, defined for every $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$, such that \[ \vec u(0)=\vec u_0,\qquad E_{\mathrm{ls}}(\vec u(t))=E_{\mathrm{ls}}(\vec u_0)\quad\text{for every }t\in{\mathbb{R}}, \] and such that $\vec u$ scatters as $t\to+\infty$ and as $t\to-\infty$?

Unresolved formulation details. The supplied record does not specify the precise class of smooth sequence-valued initial data, the solution concept, the convergence interpretation of the resonant sums, or the topology of scattering. The energy defines $\mathcal H$, but does not by itself specify these choices. If scattering is intended in $\mathcal H$, its explicit meaning would be the existence of $\vec u_+,\vec u_-\in\mathcal H$ such that \[ \lim_{t\to\pm\infty}\sum_{j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}\langle j\rangle^2 \|u_j(t)-e^{it\partial_{xx}}(u_\pm)_j\|_{L^2({\mathbb{R}})}^2=0, \] where $e^{it\partial_{xx}}$ is the free Schrödinger evolution on ${\mathbb{R}}$. This is an explicitly identified possible interpretation, not a scattering topology verified from the supplied source. These choices require mathematical review before the question is fully specified.

Short English statement

For the quintic resonant Schrödinger system indexed by ${\mathbb{Z}}^2$, the question asks whether every smooth initial datum with bounded weighted $L^2$ energy admits a global solution conserving that energy and scattering in both time directions. The precise smoothness class, solution concept, and scattering topology are unspecified. Supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports a later result for a special case described as having two discrete components; that description and the literature claims require independent verification.
