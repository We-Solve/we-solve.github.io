Attempt: allocating a limited task-evaluation sample

The source merges participation in mathematics-education research with evaluation and improvement of tasks. I address only a defined evaluation-resource subproblem. Suppose task $i$ has a mean outcome $\mu_i$ and known variance $\sigma_i^2>0$, estimated from $n_i$ independent observations; samples for different tasks are independent. The target is $\sum_iw_i\mu_i$ for fixed positive weights summing to one, and the total sample budget is $N$.
Proposition: For positive real allocations $n_i$ summing to $N$, the variance of the weighted sample-mean estimator is minimized at \[ n_i=\frac{Nw_i\sigma_i}{\sum_jw_j\sigma_j}, \qquad \operatorname{Var}_{\min}=\frac{(\sum_iw_i\sigma_i)^2}{N}. \] This is a continuous allocation benchmark; integer allocations need not attain it.
Proof: Independence makes the variance of a sample mean $\sigma_i^2/n_i$ and removes cross-task covariances. Thus total variance is $\sum_iw_i^2\sigma_i^2/n_i$. By the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality, \[ \left(\sum_iw_i\sigma_i\right)^2 =\left(\sum_i\frac{w_i\sigma_i}{\sqrt{n_i}}\sqrt{n_i}\right)^2 \le\left(\sum_i\frac{w_i^2\sigma_i^2}{n_i}\right)N. \] Equality holds exactly when $w_i\sigma_i/\sqrt{n_i}$ is a common multiple of $\sqrt{n_i}$, so $n_i$ is proportional to $w_i\sigma_i$. Normalization gives the formula and attains the lower bound. The same estimator is unbiased because each sample mean has expectation $\mu_i$. ∎

Verification and remaining gaps

For two equally weighted tasks with standard deviations $1/2,1/10$ and budget 60, the allocation is $(50,10)$ with variance $3/2000=0.0015$. Equal allocation $(30,30)$ has variance $13/6000\approx0.002167$. Exact arithmetic checks both. Allocating equally is therefore not generally variance optimal, but unknown variances, unequal observation costs, selection and task interactions can change the problem. This known allocation calculation does not recruit contributors, validate task outcomes, identify causal impact or address every part of the merged agenda.
