Math-2851

Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.71

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write ${\mathbb{N}}_{>0}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. For subgroups $U\leq H\trianglelefteq A$ of a finite group $A$, put \[ U^a=a^{-1}Ua\qquad(a\in A). \] The subgroup $U$ is an $A$-covering subgroup of $H$ if \[ \bigcup_{a\in A}U^a=H. \] For finite groups, $[H:U]$ denotes the number of cosets of $U$ in $H$.

Does there exist a function $f:{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\to{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}$ such that, for every finite group $A$, every normal subgroup $H\trianglelefteq A$ with $H<A$, and every proper subgroup $U<H$, \[ \bigcup_{a\in A}U^a=H \quad\Longrightarrow\quad [H:U]\leq f([A:H])? \] Thus the proposed bound depends only on $n=[A:H]$, which satisfies $n\geq2$, and is uniform over all such triples $(A,H,U)$.

The proposers report an affirmative answer when $U$ is a maximal subgroup of $H$.

The supplied Comments of 2025 report that the conjecture is proved when $H$ acts innately transitively on the coset space $[H:U]$, citing M. Fusari, A. Previtali and P. Spiga; "for $n=3$ with $[H:U]\leq10$", citing L. Gogniat and P. Spiga; and when $H=UL$, where $L$ is a minimal $A$-invariant subgroup of $H$, citing M. Fusari, S. Harper and P. Spiga. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified conclusions. The wording of the $n=3$ claim does not specify whether $[H:U]\leq10$ is a proved bound or an additional restriction. The final claim does not explicitly specify whether minimality excludes the trivial subgroup. These qualifications affect the reported special cases and do not alter the main question above.

Short English statement

Is the index of a proper $A$-covering subgroup $U$ in a proper normal subgroup $H$ of a finite group $A$ bounded solely in terms of $[A:H]$? The supplied registry labels the problem open and reports several affirmative special cases, with unresolved wording in two of those reports.
