A CUE critical-point sum rule and the small-exponent Jensen bridge

Definitions and mathematical statement

Ashkan Nikeghbali's source question asks about the distribution of zeros of the derivative of a random unitary characteristic polynomial, especially near the unit circle, and about ${\mathbb{E}}[|f'|^s]$ as $s\to0$. The source does not specify the unitary ensemble, evaluation point, matrix-size limit, or normalization of $f$. Its extraction is marked reconstructed and unverified. The following precise formulation is the accompanying aid's CUE interpretation, not an implicit completion of all missing source data.

Let $N\geq2$, let $U_N$ be Haar distributed on the unitary group $U(N)$, and define \[ \Lambda_N(z)=\det(I-zU_N^*). \] Let $c_{1,N},\ldots,c_{N-1,N}$ be zeros of $\Lambda_N'$, counted with multiplicity. The aid reports the exact identity \[ \prod_{j=1}^{N-1}|c_{j,N}|=\frac{|\operatorname{Tr}U_N|}{N}. \] Almost surely these critical points lie in the open unit disk and none equals zero. Their total logarithmic radial depth is therefore \[ D_N=\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}\log\frac1{|c_{j,N}|} =\log N-\log|\operatorname{Tr}U_N|. \] The reported limiting law is \[ D_N-\log N\ \Longrightarrow\ W,\qquad \Pr(W\leq x)=\exp(-e^{-2x})\quad(x\in{\mathbb{R}}),\quad N\to\infty. \] Writing $K_N(r)=\#\{j:|c_{j,N}|\leq r\}$, the deterministic bound supplied by the aid is \[ K_N(r)\leq\frac{D_N}{\log(1/r)},\qquad 0<r<1. \] This controls critical points deep inside the disk but is not a complete near-boundary point-process law.

For the small-exponent bridge, fix $N$ and $0\leq r<1$, put $X_{N,r}=\log|\Lambda_N'(r)|$, and impose two-sided exponential integrability, for example ${\mathbb{E}}\exp(\delta|X_{N,r}|)<\infty$ and ${\mathbb{E}}\exp(\delta|X_{N,0}|)<\infty$ for some $\delta>0$. Under these explicit hypotheses the aid's Jensen interpretation takes the form \[ \left.\frac{d}{dq}\right|_{q=0} \left(\log{\mathbb{E}}|\Lambda_N'(r)|^q-\log{\mathbb{E}}|\Lambda_N'(0)|^q\right) ={\mathbb{E}} X_{N,r}-{\mathbb{E}} X_{N,0} =\int_0^r\frac{{\mathbb{E}} K_N(t)}{t}\,dt. \] The integral is zero at $r=0$. Rotational invariance supplies the connection between a fixed radial point and the angular Jensen mean. This formulation makes integrability an assumption; the supplied record does not establish it uniformly in $N$ or as $r\uparrow1$. The summary also reports an expansion of a Simm--Wei fixed-interior limiting formula giving two log-cumulants, but includes neither that formula, its normalization and limiting hypotheses, nor the cumulant values. These cannot be reconstructed as a precise additional theorem from this record.

Short English statement

For Haar unitary matrices, the supplied aid relates the product of critical-point radii exactly to the trace, yielding a limiting law for total radial depth and a bound on deep critical points. A conditional Jensen formula links the derivative of small-exponent moments at zero to the expected radial count. These statements do not specify the full boundary distribution requested in the original question.
