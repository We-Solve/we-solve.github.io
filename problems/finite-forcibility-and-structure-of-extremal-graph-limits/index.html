Finite Forcibility and Structure of Extremal Graph Limits

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A standard dense, simple, undirected graph interpretation is described below. The supplied record does not explicitly specify this setting, the meaning of subgraph density, or the permitted objective functions; consequently, the displayed formulation is a conditional interpretation requiring review.

A graphon is a symmetric measurable function $W:[0,1]^2\to[0,1]$, where $[0,1]$ carries Lebesgue measure. For a finite simple graph $F$, its homomorphism density in $W$ is \[ t(F,W)=\int_{[0,1]^{V(F)}}\prod_{\{u,v\}\in E(F)}W(x_u,x_v)\prod_{v\in V(F)}{\,\mathrm{d}} x_v. \] Two graphons $U,W$ are weakly isomorphic if $t(F,U)=t(F,W)$ for every finite simple graph $F$. A graphon $W$ is finitely forcible if there exist finitely many finite simple graphs $H_1,\ldots,H_m$ such that, for every graphon $U$, \[ \left(t(H_j,U)=t(H_j,W)\text{ for every }1\leq j\leq m\right) \Longrightarrow \left(t(F,U)=t(F,W)\text{ for every finite simple graph }F\right). \] The forcing graphs need not coincide with the graphs specifying an optimization problem.

For a finite simple graph $G$, put $t(F,G)=\operatorname{hom}(F,G)/|V(G)|^{|V(F)|}$, where $\operatorname{hom}(F,G)$ counts adjacency-preserving vertex maps. A sequence $(G_n)$ has graphon limit $W$ if $|V(G_n)|\to\infty$ and $t(F,G_n)\to t(F,W)$ for every finite simple graph $F$.

Under the additional interpretation that there are finitely many equality constraints and that a single homomorphism density is optimized, a precise candidate question is the following. For every integer $k\geq0$, every collection of finite simple graphs $F_0,F_1,\ldots,F_k$, and every $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_k)\in[0,1]^k$ for which \[ \mathcal S(a)=\{W:W\text{ is a graphon and }t(F_i,W)=a_i\text{ for }1\leq i\leq k\}\ne\varnothing, \] is there a finitely forcible graphon $W_*\in\mathcal S(a)$ satisfying \[ t(F_0,W_*)=\sup_{W\in\mathcal S(a)}t(F_0,W)? \] The corresponding minimization question replaces the supremum by the infimum. Interpreting an extremal family asymptotically asks for a sequence $(G_n)$ with graphon limit $W_*$, so that its constraint densities approach $a_i$ and its objective density approaches the extremal value. Exact satisfaction of the constraints by each finite graph is a different requirement and is not specified in the record.

The supplied question also asks for the possible structure and a complete characterization of extremal graphs or graph limits. It does not specify the scope or required form of such a characterization. Nor does it establish whether its universal question permits more general functions of densities, infinitely many constraints, induced densities, or other graph categories. The candidate formulation above therefore does not resolve the full source-level ambiguity. This is a statement-only draft, not an assertion that the proposed universal property holds.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether every density optimization problem has at least one extremal family converging to a graphon uniquely determined, up to weak isomorphism, by finitely many subgraph densities. It also seeks a structural characterization of extremal graphs. A conventional graphon formulation is provided, but the source leaves the allowed objectives, constraints, density convention, and meaning of complete characterization unspecified.
