Math-14904

Arithmetic Meaning of Euler-Kronecker Constants

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F/{\mathbb{Q}}$ be an arbitrary finite extension, and let $\mathcal O_F$ denote its ring of integers. For a nonzero integral ideal $\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathcal O_F$, put $N\mathfrak a=\#(\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak a)$. The Dedekind zeta function is \[ \zeta_F(s)=\sum_{0\ne\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathcal O_F}(N\mathfrak a)^{-s},\qquad \operatorname{Re}(s)>1. \] Use the standard Euler--Kronecker normalization: writing the Laurent expansion at $s=1$ as \[ \zeta_F(s)=\frac{\kappa_F}{s-1}+c_F+O(s-1),\qquad \kappa_F>0, \] define \[ \gamma_F:=\frac{c_F}{\kappa_F} =\lim_{s\to1^+}\left(\frac{\zeta_F'(s)}{\zeta_F(s)}+\frac{1}{s-1}\right)\in{\mathbb{R}}. \] This normalization gives \[ \gamma_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\gamma =\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k-\log n\right). \] The supplied original extract stops before the definition of $\gamma_F$; the standard normalization above makes the intended invariant explicit and requires confirmation against the complete report.

Research question. For an arbitrary number field $F$, determine what arithmetic information about $F$ is encoded by $\gamma_F$, and characterize that information through prime ideals and their norms, zeros of $\zeta_F$, class-field data, or other intrinsic arithmetic invariants of $F$.

Short English statement

For each number field $F$, the Euler--Kronecker constant $\gamma_F$ is the ratio of the constant Laurent coefficient of its Dedekind zeta function at $1$ to the residue there. The question asks for an arithmetic interpretation of this real number. The intended characterization remains unspecified, and a fragment mentioning ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-extensions leaves a possible additional scope unresolved.
