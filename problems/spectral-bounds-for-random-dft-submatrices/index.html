Spectral Bounds for Random DFT Submatrices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$, and let $p,q\in[0,1]$, as required by the stated sampling probabilities. For each $n$, form random subsets $\Omega_n,T_n\subseteq\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$ by including each row index with probability $1-q$ and each column index with probability $1-p$, with all these inclusion decisions independent. Write $F_{\Omega_n,T_n}$ for the corresponding submatrix of the discrete Fourier transform matrix. Its dimensions are $|\Omega_n|\times|T_n|$. Interpret $\|\cdot\|$ as the operator norm for Euclidean norms, consistent with the supplied singular-value description.

The source record does not specify the normalization of the Fourier matrix. Under the explicitly conditional choice of the unitary normalization, \[ (F_n)_{jk}=n^{-1/2}\exp(-2\pi\mathrm{i}jk/n),\qquad 0\le j,k<n, \] define \[ a(p,q)=\sqrt{p(1-q)}+\sqrt{q(1-p)},\qquad b(p,q)=\left|\sqrt{p(1-q)}-\sqrt{q(1-p)}\right|. \] The following limit statements express the usual interpretation of "with high probability," taking $p,q$ and the tolerance to be fixed while $n\to\infty$. The source record does not explicitly supply these quantifier conventions.

The first proposed bound asks whether, for every fixed admissible pair $p,q$ satisfying $p+q>1$ and every fixed $\varepsilon>0$, \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb{P}\!\left( \|F_{\Omega_n,T_n}\|<a(p,q)+\varepsilon \right)=1. \] Here the norm of a matrix with no rows or no columns is taken to be zero, an explicit convention needed for the sampling model.

The second proposed bound is stated separately under $p\ne q$; the supplied wording does not impose $p+q>1$ on this second question. Its displayed target is \[ \left\|(F_{\Omega_n,T_n})^{-1}\right\| <\left(b(p,q)-\varepsilon\right)^{-1} \quad\text{with probability tending to one.} \] A positive reciprocal bound requires $0<\varepsilon<b(p,q)$; this restriction is absent from the supplied wording. More fundamentally, the independently sampled submatrix is generally rectangular, so its ordinary two-sided inverse is undefined. No generalized inverse or restriction to a suitable subspace is specified. Thus this second display records an unresolved source formulation, rather than a well-defined probability assertion. A precise completion must specify the inverse operation, its treatment of rank deficiency and empty matrices, and the associated probability event. The record also does not specify whether boundary values $p,q\in\{0,1\}$ are intended to be included. These qualifications require source clarification before either conditional interpretation is accepted as the intended formulation.

Short English statement

For independently sampled rows and columns of a Fourier matrix, the supplied problem asks for a high-probability upper bound on the largest singular value when $p+q>1$, and a separate inverse-norm bound when $p\ne q$. The Fourier normalization and asymptotic conventions require confirmation. The inverse bound remains undefined for the generally rectangular sampled matrix until the intended inverse and boundary conventions are specified.
