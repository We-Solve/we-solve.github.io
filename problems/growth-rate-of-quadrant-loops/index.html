Growth Rate of Quadrant Loops

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns square-lattice loops with steps \[ N=(0,1),\qquad E=(1,0),\qquad S=(0,-1),\qquad W=(-1,0). \] It does not explicitly specify the base point, rooting convention, or precise meaning of a northwest or east--south corner. The following is therefore a conditional formulation requiring confirmation of those conventions.

Provisionally, a loop of length $2n$ is a step sequence $s_1,\ldots,s_{2n}$ with vertices \[ v_0=(0,0),\qquad v_j=\sum_{i=1}^{j}s_i,\qquad v_{2n}=(0,0). \] Different step sequences are counted separately. Let $\mathcal L_n^{\mathrm{all}}$ be the set of these loops, and let \[ \mathcal L_n^{\mathrm{quad}} =\{\omega\in\mathcal L_n^{\mathrm{all}}:v_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{\,2}\text{ for every }0\leq j\leq2n\}. \] Provisionally interpret the weighted corners as the ordered consecutive step pairs $(N,W)$ and $(E,S)$, and set \[ c(\omega)=\#\{j:1\leq j<2n,\ (s_j,s_{j+1})\in\{(N,W),(E,S)\}\}. \] This convention does not count a corner across the closing junction between $s_{2n}$ and $s_1$. Neither this junction convention nor the ordered-pair interpretation is resolved by the supplied record.

For a real parameter $a$, define the weighted sums \[ Q_n(a)=\sum_{\omega\in\mathcal L_n^{\mathrm{quad}}}a^{c(\omega)},\qquad U_n(a)=\sum_{\omega\in\mathcal L_n^{\mathrm{all}}}a^{c(\omega)}, \] where a loop with no weighted corners has weight $1$, including when $a=0$, and the empty loop contributes $Q_0(a)=U_0(a)=1$. Write \[ Q(a,u)=\sum_{n\geq0}Q_n(a)u^n,\qquad U(a,u)=\sum_{n\geq0}U_n(a)u^n, \] and let $R_Q(a)$ and $R_U(a)$ denote their radii of convergence in the complex variable $u$.

Conjectural target, subject to the unresolved conventions above. For every real $a\geq-1$, \[ R_Q(a)=R_U(a). \] Equivalently, by the Cauchy--Hadamard formula, the radius formulation is \[ \limsup_{n\to\infty}|Q_n(a)|^{1/n} = \limsup_{n\to\infty}|U_n(a)|^{1/n}. \] The normalization here is per $n$, as in the supplied generating function, although the loops have length $2n$. For negative $a$, these are signed weighted sums; the record does not supply positivity or existence of ordinary growth limits. Consequently, its phrase "exponential growth rate" cannot automatically be interpreted as an ordinary limit of unsigned coefficient roots.

The supplied record labels this problem open and reports that the corresponding radius formula is proved for unconfined and half-plane loops. These are supplied literature assessments, not mathematical claims independently verified in this draft. No explicit radius formula is supplied.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether requiring a weighted square-lattice loop to stay in the nonnegative quadrant leaves its generating-function radius unchanged for every real $a\geq-1$. Precise rooting and corner-counting conventions require confirmation. Negative weights also require distinguishing radius-based growth from an ordinary coefficient-growth limit.
