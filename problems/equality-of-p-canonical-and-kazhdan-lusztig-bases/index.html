Math-3421

Equality of $p$-Canonical and Kazhdan–Lusztig Bases

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An affine Weyl group is a Coxeter group arising from an affine root system; write $(W,S)$ for such a group with its simple reflections, and write $\leq$ for its Bruhat order. Under the usual equal-parameter interpretation, its Hecke algebra $\mathcal H(W)$ is the algebra over ${\mathbb{Z}}[v,v^{-1}]$ with standard basis $\{T_x:x\in W\}$, the braid relations, and quadratic relations \[ (T_s+v)(T_s-v^{-1})=0\qquad(s\in S). \] The bar involution sends $v$ to $v^{-1}$ and $T_x$ to $T_{x^{-1}}^{-1}$. In this normalization, the Kazhdan--Lusztig basis element, denoted by $H_x$ in the supplied record, is characterized by \[ \overline{H_x}=H_x, \qquad H_x\in T_x+\sum_{y<x}v{\mathbb{Z}}[v]T_y. \] For a prime $p$, the $p$-canonical basis is the basis obtained from normalized characters of indecomposable parity complexes in characteristic $p$ in a compatible geometric realization of the Hecke algebra. Denote its element indexed by $x$ by ${}^{p}H_x$. Any root datum or realization data needed for this construction must be fixed; denote these collectively by $D$.

Classification problem. For fixed $(W,S)$ and compatible data $D$, determine the set \[ \mathcal C(W,D)=\left\{(p,x):p\text{ is prime},\ x\in W,\ {}^{p}H_x=H_x\text{ in }\mathcal H(W)\right\}. \] Thus the requested criterion should characterize exactly when the equality holds, for every prime $p$ and every element $x$ of the chosen affine Weyl group.

Unresolved scope. The supplied record does not specify an affine type, root datum, geometric realization, or restrictions on the characteristic. It also does not explicitly state the Hecke-algebra normalization. The definitions above give a conventional interpretation with compatible normalization of both bases; they do not identify a uniquely specified realization or settle whether the intended classification concerns one fixed affine Weyl group or all affine Weyl groups.

Short English statement

Determine exactly which primes $p$ and affine Weyl-group elements $x$ have the same $p$-canonical and Kazhdan--Lusztig basis element. The affine Weyl group and any required realization data must be specified before this becomes a fully determined classification problem.
