Recurrence of High-Dimensional Scale-Free Percolation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\in{\mathbb{N}}$ with $d\geq3$. The supplied statement concerns a scale-free percolation graph $G=({\mathbb{Z}}^d,E)$ in a regime having a unique infinite connected component $\mathcal C_\infty$, with parameters satisfying \[ \gamma>2,\qquad \alpha>2d. \] The inequalities are strict; their boundary cases are not included. The record does not specify the graph's probability law, the meanings of $\alpha$ and $\gamma$, or their relation to any weight distribution or connection probabilities. These inequalities therefore describe the supplied regime but do not define the model.

Provided that $\mathcal C_\infty$ is locally finite, write $\deg_{\mathcal C_\infty}(x)$ for the number of neighbors of $x$ in this component. For a fixed realization of the graph, simple random walk $(X_n)_{n\geq0}$ on $\mathcal C_\infty$ has transition probabilities \[ \mathbb P^G(X_{n+1}=y\mid X_n=x) = \begin{cases} \deg_{\mathcal C_\infty}(x)^{-1},&\{x,y\}\in E,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \] Here $x,y\in\mathcal C_\infty$, and $\mathbb P_x^G$ denotes the walk law started at $x$. Define \[ T_x^+=\inf\{n\geq1:X_n=x\},\qquad \inf\varnothing=\infty. \] The walk is recurrent if $\mathbb P_x^G(T_x^+<\infty)=1$ for every $x\in\mathcal C_\infty$, and transient if this probability is strictly less than $1$ for every such $x$. On a connected, locally finite graph these alternatives do not depend on the starting vertex. Local finiteness is needed for this transition rule and is not established by the supplied record.

Requested mathematical target. Determine whether simple random walk on the unique infinite cluster is recurrent or transient in the stated scale-free percolation regime, for dimensions $d\geq3$ and the admissible parameter choices. The record does not explicitly specify the probability quantifier over graph realizations; an almost-sure classification would require the missing model law and a specification of whether the infinite-cluster assumption holds almost surely or is imposed by conditioning.

The source also asks for the analogous classification for long-range percolation. Its original wording refers to "regime (ii) of the stated theorem," but neither that theorem nor the long-range percolation law and parameter regime is supplied. Consequently, a precise long-range percolation assertion, and a complete model-specific scale-free percolation assertion, cannot be recovered from this record alone.

Short English statement

For scale-free percolation on ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$, with $d\geq3$, a unique infinite cluster, $\gamma>2$, and $\alpha>2d$, determine whether simple random walk is recurrent or transient. The source also asks the analogous long-range percolation question. Missing model definitions and the omitted theorem prevent a fully specified formulation.
