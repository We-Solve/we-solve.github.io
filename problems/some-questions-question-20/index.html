Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ with its fixed free generators. Given a probability space $(A,\nu)$, its Bernoulli, or i.i.d., action is the probability-preserving action $T$ of $F_2$ on $(X,\mu)=(A^{F_2},\nu^{F_2})$ defined by \[ (T_gx)(h)=x(g^{-1}h)\qquad(g,h\in F_2). \] A finite action means a homomorphism $T^{(m)}:F_2\to\operatorname{Sym}(X_m)$ on a nonempty finite set, equipped with uniform probability measure. No transitivity assumption is added.

One standard action version of local-global convergence is the following. Let $B_r$ be the word ball of radius $r$ in $F_2$ and let $[k]=\{1,\ldots,k\}$. For a probability-preserving action $U$ on $(Y,\rho)$, define $C_{r,k}(U)$ to be the closure, in the finite-dimensional probability simplex on $[k]^{B_r}$, of the distributions \[ \operatorname{Law}_{y\sim\rho}\left((c(U_{g^{-1}}y))_{g\in B_r}\right), \qquad c:Y\to[k]\text{ measurable}. \] The local-global convergence condition is convergence of these compact sets in Hausdorff distance, using the $\ell^1$ metric on probability vectors: \[ d_H\left(C_{r,k}(T^{(m)}),C_{r,k}(T)\right)\longrightarrow0 \quad\text{for every }r\geq0\text{ and }k\geq1. \] This requires approximation of all finite-color local statistics in both directions; merely approximating the uncolored local neighborhoods is insufficient.

Question. Is the i.i.d. action of $F_2$ a local-global limit of finite $F_2$-actions? With the convention above, this asks whether there exist nonempty finite sets $X_m$ and actions $T^{(m)}$ satisfying the displayed convergence for the intended Bernoulli action $T$. The source does not specify the base probability space $(A,\nu)$ or a definition of local-global convergence. If a nonatomic standard base, such as $([0,1],\text{Lebesgue measure})$, is intended, the formulas give that particular precise version; a choice of base is not silently imposed on the source question.

The supplied summary labels the problem OPEN-TRIAGE and says that its author found no published resolution within a capped search. It connects the question with local-global limits of finite graphs, factors of i.i.d., factoring/profiniteness, approximation of free-group shifts, conjectures attributed to Abért--Csóka--Herrero--Lipnowitz--Vervloet, and Bowen's work on stable actions. These are reported connections and a bounded-search status assessment, not a verified present-day openness claim.

Short English statement

Can the Bernoulli action of the two-generator free group be approximated by finite permutation actions so that all finite-color local statistics converge in the local-global sense? The Bernoulli base and precise convergence convention need source review; the supplied open assessment comes from a capped literature search.
