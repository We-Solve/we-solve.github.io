Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
The spin--statistics relation assigns Bose symmetry to integer spin $s\in\mathbb N_0$ and Fermi antisymmetry to half-integer spin $s\in\tfrac12+\mathbb N_0$. In three spatial dimensions the configuration space of $n$ indistinguishable particles is $((\mathbb R^3)^n\setminus\Delta)/S_n$, where $\Delta$ is the collision locus and $S_n$ permutes particles. Wavefunctions can be sections of bundles over this space; for two particles the relative direction space is $\mathbb{RP}^2$. The geometric formulation studies the exchange action and the rotation-group action on these bundles.
Source scope and qualifications.
The nonrelativistic bundle framework is the context. Topology alone permits a choice of exchange sign and does not automatically prove the physical relation; the report explicitly requires an additional physically motivated input. The two source questions remain separate.

A geometric countertest to topology alone.
For two particles remove collisions and keep the relative direction $n\in S^2$. For either $\varepsilon=1$ or $-1$, define \[ \mathcal H_\varepsilon=\{f\in L^2(S^2):f(-n)=\varepsilon f(n)\}. \] These are sections of the two flat complex line bundles over $\mathbb{RP}^2$ associated with the characters $\pm1$ of the deck transformation. Rotations act by $(U(R)f)(n)=f(R^{-1}n)$ and preserve each subspace because $R(-n)=-Rn$. This is a genuine representation of $SO(3)$, so a $2\pi$ rotation acts as the identity in both cases. Exchange instead acts by the independent sign $\varepsilon$.
A nonzero wrong-statistics sector.
The odd space is nonzero: $f(n)=n_3$ lies in it. It therefore describes spinless orbital wavefunctions with antisymmetric exchange, while obeying rotation covariance and the collision-free configuration-space construction. Adding a radial compactly supported factor and a center-of-mass wavefunction gives square-integrable two-particle states in the original three-dimensional setting. The free Laplacian preserves the exchange condition, since permutation and rotations commute with its differential expression.
What has and has not been disproved.
This does not contradict the physical spin--statistics theorem: no relativistic locality, positive-energy field algebra, or source-specific extra physical condition was imposed. It proves that the configuration bundle and ordinary rotation covariance alone cannot force Bose statistics for spin zero. A geometric proof needs an additional hypothesis identifying the exchange holonomy with the relevant spin rotation. Establishing a physically justified such hypothesis, then proving sufficiency and testing minimality, remains the exact missing step in both bundled source questions.
