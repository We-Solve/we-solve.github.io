Equal-entropy factors conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ and $B$ be the source's irreducible integer square matrices, with equal spectral radius $\rho(A)=\rho(B)$. To define their usual edge shifts of finite type, their entries must be nonnegative. The supplied statement says "integer matrices" without explicitly stating nonnegativity; the symbolic-dynamical interpretation below is conditional on that intended hypothesis.

For a nonnegative integer matrix $A$, form a finite directed graph with $A_{ij}$ edges from vertex $i$ to vertex $j$. Let $S_A$ be its bi-infinite edge paths with the product topology and the left shift $\sigma_A$; define $S_B$ and $\sigma_B$ similarly. A factor map from $S_A$ onto $S_B$ is a continuous surjection $\pi:S_A\to S_B$ with \[ \pi\circ\sigma_A=\sigma_B\circ\pi. \] Irreducibility means the corresponding graph is strongly connected; no mixing hypothesis is added.

Write $D_A,D_B$ for the dimension modules referred to by the source. Their module structure, order data if any, topology, and meaning of closed submodule are not defined in the supplied record. With these dependencies retained, the conjectural implication is \[ \left. \begin{gathered} \rho(A)=\rho(B),\\ \forall n\ge1,\quad\operatorname{tr}(A^n)>0 \ \Longrightarrow\ \operatorname{tr}(B^n)>0,\\ D_B\text{ is a quotient of a closed submodule of }D_A \end{gathered} \right\} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists\pi:S_A\twoheadrightarrow S_B\text{ a factor map}. \] The trace condition preserves its supplied implication direction and asserts existence of periodic points of the relevant periods, rather than an inequality between their numbers. The quotient condition must be defined in the intended category before this becomes a self-contained theorem statement; no module or closedness convention is silently chosen.

Short English statement

Do equal spectral radius, the stated periodic-point compatibility, and the dimension-module quotient condition force a factor map between the two irreducible shifts of finite type? The source calls this an equal-entropy factors conjecture.

The supplied record is inconsistent about status: its registry status is open and its classification is OPEN-TRIAGE, while the research summary says the SFT case is effectively solved by Boyle's Equal Entropy Factor Theorem and general factor theorems, with a caveat about the refined closed-submodule quotient formulation. It supplies neither the exact theorem nor a verified match of its hypotheses. The summary also calls the conditions entropy-reducing despite the equal-spectral-radius hypothesis. Those claims are retained for review, not used to declare the displayed refined assertion proved. Provenance records no later authoritative resolution check.
