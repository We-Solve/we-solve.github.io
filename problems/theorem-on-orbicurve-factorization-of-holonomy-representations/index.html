Math-4291

Theorem on Orbicurve Factorization of Holonomy Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Diff}({\mathbb{C}},0)$ denote the group, under composition, of germs at $0$ of holomorphic local biholomorphisms fixing $0$. For a representation $\rho:\pi_1(X,x)\to\operatorname{Diff}({\mathbb{C}},0)$, write \[ \Gamma=\rho(\pi_1(X,x)),\qquad Z(\Gamma)=\{g\in\Gamma:gh=hg\text{ for every }h\in\Gamma\}, \] and let $q:\Gamma\to\Gamma/Z(\Gamma)$ be the quotient homomorphism. A group is virtually abelian if it has an abelian subgroup of finite index. An orbicurve is a complex curve equipped with orbifold data; its orbifold fundamental group is denoted by $\pi_1^{\mathrm{orb}}(C,c)$. The supplied record does not specify the permitted curves, orbifold data, or morphisms.

Recorded assertion and unresolved formulation. The source fragment asserts that, whenever $X$ is quasi-projective and $\Gamma$ is not virtually abelian, the representation factors through an orbicurve representation induced by a morphism $X\to C$. Because the stated target of that representation is $\Gamma/Z(\Gamma)$, the type-consistent interpretation is the existence of an orbicurve $C$, a morphism $f:X\to C$ inducing an orbifold fundamental-group homomorphism, and a homomorphism $\overline\rho$ such that \[ \begin{gathered} f_*:\pi_1(X,x)\longrightarrow\pi_1^{\mathrm{orb}}(C,f(x)),\\ \overline\rho:\pi_1^{\mathrm{orb}}(C,f(x))\longrightarrow\Gamma/Z(\Gamma),\\ q\circ\rho=\overline\rho\circ f_*. \end{gathered} \] Here $\rho$ is regarded as taking values in its image $\Gamma$. This interpretation expresses factorization of $q\circ\rho$; it does not supply a factorization of $\rho$ itself through a homomorphism into $\Gamma$. The fragment does not resolve that distinction. It also does not specify whether $X$ must be smooth, connected, or subject to additional hypotheses, or what conditions on $f$ ensure the indicated orbifold homomorphism. Consequently, this display records an interpretation requiring source review, rather than an unqualified theorem statement.

Status qualification. The supplied statement-verification field identifies the assertion as Theorem 2 of the report and says that it provides evidence for the distinct foliation conjecture recorded as OWR-15582-002. It explicitly says that this assertion is not a source-stated open problem. The present record has supplied status open, but statement status unrecoverable and no clean statement. The associated Cerveau Lins Neto conjectural classification is mentioned without its formulation; no standalone open problem or formulation of that conjecture can therefore be recovered from this record.

Short English statement

The retained fragment concerns representations of the fundamental group of a quasi-projective space into holomorphic diffeomorphism germs fixing the origin. Its natural quotient-level reading says that, when the image is not virtually abelian, the representation modulo the center factors through an orbicurve. Missing hypotheses and the distinction between the original representation and its central quotient require review. Supplied verification identifies this as a theorem fragment supporting a separate conjecture, despite the registry's open label.
