Math-3458

Highly Ramified Figure-Eight Covers of Hyperbolic 3-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^3$ be the three-dimensional sphere, and let $K\subset S^3$ be an embedded figure-eight knot. A closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold is a compact $3$-manifold without boundary equipped with a complete Riemannian metric of constant sectional curvature $-1$. A finite covering $q:\widetilde M\to M$ means an unbranched covering with finitely many sheets.

For a branched covering $p:\widetilde M\to S^3$ with branch locus exactly $K$, the restriction \[ p:\widetilde M\setminus p^{-1}(K)\longrightarrow S^3\setminus K \] is an ordinary finite-sheeted covering. Along each component $C$ of $p^{-1}(K)$, its ramification index $e_C\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}$ is the transverse local degree: in suitable local coordinates the map has the form \[ (z,t)\longmapsto (z^{e_C},t). \] The components of the full inverse image $p^{-1}(K)$ include any components with index $1$; the condition below applies to all of them.

Question. Is it true that for every closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold $M$ and every real number $L>0$, there exist a finite covering $q:\widetilde M\to M$ and a branched covering $p:\widetilde M\to S^3$ with branch locus exactly $K$, such that \[ e_C>L\qquad\text{for every connected component }C\text{ of }p^{-1}(K)? \] Both covering maps may depend on $M$ and $L$. The supplied statement does not explicitly specify an orientability convention or whether the finite covering space must be connected; these conventions require confirmation against the original source. The supplied report label and year metadata also require bibliographic reconciliation.

Short English statement

Does every closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold admit, for every prescribed positive bound, a finite cover that maps to $S^3$ branched exactly over the figure-eight knot, with ramification index strictly greater than that bound on every component above the knot? The supplied record labels this question open; that status is dated curation and has not been independently verified.
