Math-932

Geometric Realizability of Algebraic Two-Complexes over Group Rings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finitely presented group and let $R={\mathbb{Z}}[G]$ be its integral group ring. If $X$ is a finite connected two-dimensional CW-complex with a specified isomorphism $\pi_1(X)\cong G$, its universal cover $\widetilde X$ has an augmented cellular chain complex \[ C_2(\widetilde X;{\mathbb{Z}})\xrightarrow{d_2}C_1(\widetilde X;{\mathbb{Z}})\xrightarrow{d_1}C_0(\widetilde X;{\mathbb{Z}})\xrightarrow{\varepsilon}{\mathbb{Z}}\longrightarrow0. \] Here the deck transformations give a left $R$-module convention, each $C_i(\widetilde X;{\mathbb{Z}})$ is finitely generated free over $R$, and ${\mathbb{Z}}$ is the trivial $R$-module. The complex is exact at $C_1$, $C_0$, and ${\mathbb{Z}}$; injectivity of $d_2$ is not required. The cellular complex relevant to the group ring is that of $\widetilde X$.

For an augmented complex $A_2\to A_1\to A_0\to{\mathbb{Z}}\to0$, literal cellular realization means that there exist such an $X$, a marking $\pi_1(X)\cong G$, and an isomorphism of augmented $R$-chain complexes from this complex to $C_*(\widetilde X;{\mathbb{Z}})\to{\mathbb{Z}}$, inducing the identity on the augmentation target.

Problem, with unresolved source qualifications. For every finitely presented group $G$, characterize which algebraic two-complexes over ${\mathbb{Z}}[G]$ admit geometric realization by finite connected two-dimensional CW-complexes with fundamental group identified with $G$.

The supplied record does not define the admissible algebraic two-complexes: in particular, it does not specify their finiteness, freeness, stable freeness or projectivity requirements, or their augmentation and exactness conditions. It also does not specify whether realization requires an actual chain isomorphism, as in the literal interpretation defined above, or only a chain homotopy equivalence, possibly after stabilization. These distinctions are part of the unresolved formulation; the literal interpretation is not asserted to be the intended version. The original excerpt refers only to certain algebraic two-complexes, whereas the curated statement requests a characterization. The omitted restriction must be recovered before this can be treated as a fully specified realization problem.

Short English statement

For a finitely presented group $G$, determine which algebraic two-complexes over ${\mathbb{Z}}[G]$ come from universal covers of finite two-dimensional CW-complexes with fundamental group $G$. The supplied record leaves both the admissible algebraic complexes and the required notion of equivalence unresolved. Its open-status assessment is supplied dated curation, requiring independent verification.
