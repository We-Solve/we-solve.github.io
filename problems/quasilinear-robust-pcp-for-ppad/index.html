Quasilinear robust PCP for PPAD

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An End-of-Line input $I$ consists of explicit Boolean circuits $P,S:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}^n$, with $n\ge1$, $P(0)=0$, $S(0)\ne0$, and $P(S(0))=0$. Its length $N$ counts both full circuit encodings. A solution is nonzero $v$ with $P(S(v))\ne v$ or $S(P(v))\ne v$.

A generalized circuit has finitely many nodes and a nonempty equally weighted gate list, each node the output of at most one gate. Node values lie in $[0,1]$. The gate types are constants, scaling, copying, saturated sum and difference, comparison, and Boolean OR, AND and NOT; rational gate parameters lie in $[0,1]$. Arithmetic gates have additive tolerance $\varepsilon$. Comparison sends an input gap larger than $\varepsilon$ to the corresponding output interval $[0,\varepsilon]$ or $[1-\varepsilon,1]$. Boolean gates use low inputs $\le\varepsilon$ and high inputs $\ge1-\varepsilon$, with the usual Boolean implications; absent a required implication, any output in $[0,1]$ is allowed.

Seek fixed rational $0<\varepsilon<1/10$, $0<\delta<1$, integers $A>0,b\ge0$, a polynomial $p$, and deterministic construction and decoding algorithms $R,D$. If $M$ denotes the complete output encoding length, define an acceptable witness to be a rational node assignment of binary length at most $p(M)$ satisfying at least $(1-\delta)$ of the gates. The locations of violated gates are arbitrary and every node keeps one globally consistent value. Require existence of an acceptable witness for every circuit in this model and \[ |R(I)|\le AN\lceil\log_2(N+2)\rceil^b,\qquad D(I,x)\text{ solves }I\quad\text{for every acceptable }x\text{ of }R(I). \] Construction time is polynomial in $N$; decoding time is polynomial in $N$ and the supplied witness length. The constants and algorithms are uniform, without advice or randomized construction. The encoding bound is part of acceptability, not a claim about all real assignments.

Short English statement

Can End-of-Line be reduced to robust approximate generalized circuits with quasilinear complete encoding, guaranteed polynomial-size rational witnesses, and efficient decoding of every acceptable witness?
