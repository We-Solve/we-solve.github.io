$R\infty$ Property for Relatively Hyperbolic Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Extracted $R_\infty$ question. Does every relatively hyperbolic group, and hence every Kleinian group, have property $R_\infty$? For $\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(G)$, elements $x,y\in G$ are $\varphi$-twisted conjugate if $y=gx\varphi(g)^{-1}$ for some $g\in G$. The Reidemeister number $R(\varphi)$ is the number of these classes. Property $R_\infty$ means $R(\varphi)=\infty$ for every automorphism, not merely for one automorphism or for ordinary conjugacy.

Essential scope gap. The literal all-groups wording includes elementary examples that do not have this property. For $G={\mathbb{Z}}$ and $\varphi(n)=-n$, twisted classes are the two residue classes modulo $2$, so $R(\varphi)=2$. The group ${\mathbb{Z}}$ is hyperbolic and occurs as an infinite cyclic Kleinian group generated by a loxodromic isometry. Thus an omitted non-elementarity or other qualifying restriction is essential to the intended research question; the unrestricted wording cannot simply be advertised as an unresolved universal assertion. This is a formulation diagnostic, not a claim to settle the intended qualified problem.

The source does not specify the definition and peripheral structure of relative hyperbolicity, the required finiteness hypotheses, or whether automorphisms must preserve that structure. A Kleinian group conventionally means a discrete subgroup of $\mathrm{PSL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$; the word alone does not supply the non-elementarity or finite-generation/geometric hypotheses needed for the stated and hence passage to a selected relatively hyperbolic class. These restrictions are not guessed. Once the intended class $\mathcal C$ is recovered, the actual target is $\forall G\in\mathcal C\,\forall\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(G),\ R(\varphi)=\infty$, with its precise automorphism scope.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the item partially solved and reports $R_\infty$ for non-elementary hyperbolic groups and broad relatively hyperbolic subclasses, while calling an unrestricted relatively hyperbolic assertion open. The elementary obstruction above shows that this last phrase needs reconciliation with the intended missing hypotheses. The cited later theorem's exact scope is not reproduced and no independent current-status conclusion is drawn.

Short English statement

Property $R_\infty$ requires infinitely many twisted conjugacy classes for every automorphism. The literal universal claim needs a missing restriction: ${\mathbb{Z}}$, also an elementary Kleinian group, has only two classes for inversion. The intended non-elementary or peripheral hypotheses must be recovered.
