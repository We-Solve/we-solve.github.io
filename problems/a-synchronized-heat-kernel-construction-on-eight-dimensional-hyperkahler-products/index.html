A synchronized heat-kernel construction on eight-dimensional hyperkähler products

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
A hyper-Kähler metric has three parallel complex structures satisfying the quaternionic relations. In four dimensions a gravitational instanton is a complete noncompact Ricci-flat geometry in the source’s self-dual setting. ALE and ALF describe different asymptotic geometries; ALF has an asymptotic circle direction over a three-dimensional base. For a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$, $L^2$ norms use the metric volume and pointwise metric on forms. Reduced $L^2$ cohomology is $\ker d/\overline{\operatorname{im}d}$ for the specified closed $L^2$ complex. On a complete manifold its harmonic representatives satisfy $d\alpha=d^*\alpha=0$ and $\int_M|\alpha|^2<\infty$. A Green function is a distributional inverse kernel for a specified elliptic operator and boundary behavior. The twistor space of a hyper-Kähler manifold encodes its sphere of complex structures. Quaternionic-Kähler geometry in this context has Einstein metric with nonzero scalar-curvature parameter. Noncommutative instanton moduli in the source use commutators $[x_\mu,x_\nu]=B_{\mu\nu}$ and moment-map equations deformed by $B$. Centered means the center-of-mass parameter is removed.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Generalize Atiyah’s Serre-class Green-function formalism from self-dual four-manifolds to eight-dimensional hyper-Kähler manifolds, and formulate its analogue for quaternionic manifolds. Determine whether every four-dimensional hyper-Kähler manifold with suitable noncompact decay is a limit as $\lambda\to0$ of quaternionic Einstein metrics $\operatorname{Ric}=\lambda g$. Determine asymptotic geometry of multi-monopole moduli spaces, motivated by N-body behavior. For centered $k$-instanton moduli $M_B^{k,n}$ on noncommutative $\mathbb R_B^4$ with gauge group $U(n)$ and deformed moment-map equations, define instanton number and determine whether there is a unique harmonic form in middle dimension. The source records affirmative cases for one instanton and for two instantons with $U(1)$. Prove or disprove that centered one-instanton moduli on $\mathbb R^3\times S_B^1$ with specified noncentral holonomy at infinity have exactly $n$ $L^2$-harmonic middle-dimensional forms.

A synchronized product Green kernel.
For the flat product $\mathbb R^4_x\times\mathbb R^4_y$, the heat kernel is the product at the same time parameter, \[H_t(x,y)=(4\pi t)^{-4}\exp[-(|x|^2+|y|^2)/(4t)].\] Integrating from zero to infinity, with $r^2=|x|^2+|y|^2>0$, gives \[G_8(x,y)=\int_0^\infty H_t(x,y)dt=\frac1{2\pi^4r^6}. \] Indeed substitution $s=r^2/(4t)$ reduces the integral to $(4\pi)^{-4}(4/r^2)^3\int_0^\infty s^2e^{-s}ds$, and the last integral is two by repeated integration by parts. Integrating the heat equation in time shows $-\Delta G_8=\delta_0$ distributionally; the boundary at infinity vanishes and the small-time heat kernel converges to the delta distribution.
Why multiplying Green functions fails.
In four dimensions $G_4(x)=1/(4\pi^2|x|^2)$. A product of two such Green functions satisfies \[-\Delta_{x,y}[G_4(x)G_4(y)]=\delta_0(x)G_4(y)+G_4(x)\delta_0(y),\] which has sources along the coordinate factors, rather than only at the product origin. Thus the product construction requires a synchronized heat integral, not the product of inverses.
Remaining full scope.
This is a flat eight-dimensional scalar-kernel benchmark. It does not extend Atiyah's Serre-class/twistor formalism or define its quaternionic analogue. Hyper-Kahler limits of quaternionic Einstein metrics, multi-monopole asymptotics, noncommutative instanton number, uniqueness of middle forms and the claimed n forms with circle holonomy all remain unproved. The first gap for the Green-function route is identifying a geometric cohomology construction compatible with the synchronized inverse and noncompact boundary conditions; the calculation alone provides no such correspondence.
