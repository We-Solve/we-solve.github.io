Energy Transfer Among Modes in Resonant Evolution

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. The supplied formulation concerns complex modal amplitudes $\alpha_n(\tau)$, indexed by $n\in{\mathbb{N}}_0$, satisfying the cubic resonant system \[ i\frac{\mathrm d\alpha_n}{\mathrm d\tau} =\sum_{\substack{j,k,l\in{\mathbb{N}}_0\\ n+j=k+l}} S_{njkl}\overline{\alpha_j}\alpha_k\alpha_l, \qquad n\in{\mathbb{N}}_0. \] Here $S_{njkl}$ denotes the interaction coefficients of the intended resonant system. For the modal weights $E_n$ appearing in the source, write \[ e_n(\tau)=E_n|\alpha_n(\tau)|^2, \qquad E(\tau)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}e_n(\tau). \] The source describes $E$ as conserved, so that its intended solutions satisfy $E(\tau)=E(0)$ on their interval of existence, with $0$ the initial time. The coefficients, weights, admissible initial data, solution class, convergence hypotheses, and interval of existence are not specified in the supplied record; conservation is retained as a source qualification rather than established here.

Problem. For the particular resonant system intended by the source, determine how the initial energy is distributed among the modes as the solution evolves, and characterize the conditions under which energy transfers from lower to higher mode indices.

Unresolved formulation. The record does not specify the intended coefficients or a quantitative meaning of transfer to high modes. In particular, it does not select a finite-time redistribution criterion, a long-time asymptotic criterion, or a universal cascade assertion. No definite quantification over systems or initial data can therefore be supplied without additional information. Positivity of the weights and the relation between mode index and physical frequency also require clarification.

Short English statement

For the intended cubic resonant system, describe the evolution of the modal contributions to the energy described by the source as conserved, and identify conditions for transfer from low to high modes. The interaction coefficients, energy weights, admissible data, and precise transfer criterion remain unspecified, so this is a qualified statement-only draft.
