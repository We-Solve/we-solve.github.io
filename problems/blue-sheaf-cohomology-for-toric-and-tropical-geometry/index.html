Blue Sheaf Cohomology for Toric and Tropical Geometry

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record presents a research programme rather than a fully specified conjecture. Its questions depend on constructions and hypotheses that the record does not define.

Let $X$ denote a toric geometric object and $\mathcal F$ a sheaf in the intended setting. Write $H^i(X,\mathcal F)$, for $i\geq 0$, for its intended sheaf cohomology, and write $(g_j)_{j\in J}$ for the family called the distinguished generators. This notation is conditional: the record does not specify the category of $X$, the allowed sheaves, the coefficient objects, the cohomology construction, or the construction and indexing of the distinguished generators.

The questions are:

  1. Explain the mathematical meaning of the distinguished generators and determine the hypotheses under which they form a basis. If the intended cohomology is an ordinary module over a specified commutative ring $R$, and $g_j\in H^i(X,\mathcal F)$, the basis question asks when the map \[ \bigoplus_{j\in J} R\longrightarrow H^i(X,\mathcal F),\qquad (a_j)_{j\in J}\longmapsto\sum_{j\in J}a_jg_j \] is an isomorphism. The sum is finite because its argument belongs to a direct sum. This module interpretation is conditional and does not specify a meaning of basis for blue coefficient objects.
  2. Determine what additional structure these generators induce on the Cox ring of $X$, whenever that ring is defined in the intended setting. The record does not specify the hypotheses for its construction, its grading conventions, or the proposed additional structure.
  3. Determine whether, and how, the distinguished generators can be used for explicit sheaf cohomology computations. The admissible inputs, desired outputs, and scope of such computations remain unspecified.
  4. Investigate the usefulness of blue sheaf cohomology for toroidal or logarithmic schemes, and formulate an extension to tropical schemes. The intended definitions of blue sheaf cohomology and tropical schemes, the applicable classes of objects, and the required properties of an extension are not supplied.
The original statement asks about usefulness for toroidal or logarithmic schemes; the cleaned statement instead asks for an extension to all three settings. These are different demands, and the supplied record does not resolve their relationship. Consequently, no unconditional assertion with fixed domains and quantifiers can be extracted without further source clarification.

Short English statement

Explain the distinguished generators in toric sheaf cohomology, characterize when they form a basis, identify their effect on the Cox ring, and investigate explicit computations. Explore blue sheaf cohomology for toroidal and logarithmic schemes and its extension to tropical schemes. The relevant constructions and hypotheses require clarification; this is a statement-only research draft requiring human mathematical review.
