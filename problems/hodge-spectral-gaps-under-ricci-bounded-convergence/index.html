Hodge Spectral Gaps Under Ricci Bounded Convergence

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X_i,g_i)$ be the sequence in the supplied record, with Gromov--Hausdorff limit $X$. The stated hypotheses are that the convergence is noncollapsed and that there exists a constant $K\geq 0$, independent of $i$, such that \[ \lvert\operatorname{Ric}_{g_i}\rvert\leq K\qquad\text{for every }i. \] The record does not specify the dimension, compactness, completeness, boundary conditions, meaning of noncollapse, or norm used for the Ricci bound. These omissions must be resolved before this becomes a fully specified mathematical problem.

For a space $Y$, the conventional real ordinary first Betti number is \[ b_1(Y;{\mathbb{R}}):=\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}H^1_{\mathrm{sing}}(Y;{\mathbb{R}}). \] The source writes $b_1(Y)$ without specifying coefficients or finiteness; the real interpretation is explicit here and requires confirmation. On a smooth Riemannian manifold, the Hodge Laplacian on differential $1$-forms has differential expression \[ \Delta_{H,Y}^{(1)}=\mathrm d\delta+\delta\mathrm d, \] where $\delta$ is the formal adjoint of $\mathrm d$. Its operator domain and any boundary conditions must also be specified. On the possibly singular limit $X$, a definition of differential forms and a realization of the Hodge Laplacian must be chosen. If such a realization is supplied, define its harmonic first Betti number by \[ h_1(X):=\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}\ker\Delta_{H,X}^{(1)}. \] This is an analytic definition and does not assume equality with ordinary cohomology.

Question recorded by the source. For every sequence satisfying the stated hypotheses, is there a uniform spectral gap above zero for the Hodge Laplacians on $1$-forms? Under the interpretation of a nonnegative self-adjoint realization on each $X_i$, a gap uniform along the sequence means \[ \exists c>0\ \forall i,\qquad \operatorname{Spec}\left(\Delta_{H,X_i}^{(1)}\right) \subseteq\{0\}\cup[c,\infty). \] The source does not specify whether $c$ may depend on the entire sequence or must depend only on fixed geometric parameters.

The source also asks whether the ordinary first Betti numbers converge to that of the limit and whether the harmonic first Betti number of the limit agrees with its ordinary first Betti number. With the explicit real-coefficient interpretation above, these targets are \[ \lim_{i\to\infty}b_1(X_i;{\mathbb{R}})=b_1(X;{\mathbb{R}}), \qquad h_1(X)=b_1(X;{\mathbb{R}}). \] The record describes the spectral-gap and Betti-number formulations as equivalent. That equivalence is retained as a source qualification, not asserted here: its precise meaning requires the missing geometric and analytic hypotheses, an appropriate spectral-convergence statement, and clarification of the relationship between harmonic forms and ordinary cohomology.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether noncollapsed Gromov--Hausdorff convergence with a uniform two-sided Ricci bound prevents positive Hodge eigenvalues on $1$-forms from approaching zero, preserves ordinary first Betti numbers, and identifies harmonic and ordinary first Betti numbers on the limit. The geometric setting, limit operator, coefficient convention, and claimed equivalence require clarification.
