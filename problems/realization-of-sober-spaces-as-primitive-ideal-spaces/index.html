Math-4769

Realization of Sober Spaces as Primitive Ideal Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a topological space. It is second-countable if its topology has a countable base, and it is $T_0$ if any two distinct points are distinguished by an open set. A nonempty closed subset is irreducible if it is not the union of two proper closed subsets. The space is sober if every nonempty irreducible closed subset is the closure of exactly one point.

For an explicit interpretation of the non-Hausdorff terminology, call a subset compact if every open cover has a finite subcover, without imposing a Hausdorff condition, and saturated if it is an intersection of open subsets. Interpret coherent as requiring the intersection of any two compact saturated subsets to be compact. Interpret locally compact as requiring that for every $x\in X$ and every open neighborhood $U$ of $x$, there exist an open set $V$ and a compact subset $K$ with \[ x\in V\subseteq K\subseteq U. \] The supplied record does not define coherence or non-Hausdorff local compactness; these conventions require confirmation against the cited report.

For a complex $C^*$-algebra $A$, let $\operatorname{Prim}(A)$ be the set of kernels of nonzero irreducible $*$-representations of $A$, equipped with the hull--kernel topology. Its closed sets are \[ \operatorname{hull}(I)=\{P\in\operatorname{Prim}(A):I\subseteq P\}, \] where $I$ ranges over the closed two-sided ideals of $A$. Interpret amenability in the Banach-algebra sense: for every Banach $A$-bimodule $M$, every bounded derivation $D:A\to M^*$ is inner. Thus a bounded linear map satisfying $D(ab)=a\cdot D(b)+D(a)\cdot b$ must have the form $D(a)=a\cdot\varphi-\varphi\cdot a$ for some $\varphi\in M^*$, using the induced dual module actions.

Question (attributed to Kirchberg by the supplied curation). Is it true that for every second-countable, coherent, locally compact, sober $T_0$ space $X$, there exist an amenable $C^*$-algebra $A$ and a homeomorphism \[ h:X\longrightarrow\operatorname{Prim}(A)? \] No separability or unitality condition on $A$, and no Hausdorff or global compactness condition on $X$, is imposed by the supplied statement. The empty space is not explicitly excluded. The question remains subject to confirmation of the terminology specified above.

Short English statement

Does every second-countable, coherent, locally compact, sober $T_0$ space occur as the primitive ideal space of an amenable $C^*$-algebra? The supplied curation attributes the question to Kirchberg and labels it open. The non-Hausdorff compactness terminology needs confirmation against the report.
