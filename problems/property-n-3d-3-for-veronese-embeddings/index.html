Math-775

Property $N_{3d-3}$ for Veronese Embeddings

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

To define the notation, let $k$ be a field, let $n\geq 0$ and $d\geq 1$ be integers, and put $V=H^0({\mathbb{P}}^n_k,\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^n_k}(d))$. The complete linear system defines the $d$-uple Veronese embedding \[ v_d\colon {\mathbb{P}}^n_k\longrightarrow {\mathbb{P}}(V^*)\cong{\mathbb{P}}^{\binom{n+d}{d}-1}_k. \] Let \[ S=\operatorname{Sym}_k(V),\qquad R=\bigoplus_{m\geq 0}H^0({\mathbb{P}}^n_k,\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^n_k}(md)), \] where $V$ has degree one in $S$, and the summand indexed by $m$ has degree $m$ in $R$. Multiplication of sections gives the natural graded map $S\to R$. For this embedding, property $N_p$, with $p\geq 0$, means that this map is surjective and that the minimal graded free resolution of $R$ over $S$ has the form \[ \cdots\longrightarrow F_2\longrightarrow F_1\longrightarrow S\longrightarrow R\longrightarrow 0, \qquad F_i\cong S(-i-1)^{\beta_i}\quad(1\leq i\leq p), \] for nonnegative integers $\beta_i$. Here $S(-a)$ denotes the graded free module whose generator has degree $a$; zero modules are allowed. Thus $N_0$ requires projective normality, while positive $p$ additionally requires linearity of the indicated syzygies.

The supplied corrected question asks whether, throughout its intended parameter range, \[ v_d({\mathbb{P}}^n_k)\text{ satisfies property }N_{3d-3}. \] The source record does not specify the ground field, its characteristic, or precise bounds on $n$ and $d$. The ranges used above make the notation meaningful; they do not establish that every such choice is intended by the source. In particular, no characteristic-free universal assertion is attributed to the report here.

The original extracted statement also asked how to determine efficiently an implicit equation from approximation complexes and multigraded syzygies, given a divisor $D$ on $X$ and a basepoint-free subspace $U\subset H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(D))$ with $\dim U=\dim X+2$ and hypersurface image in the source's notation ${\mathbb{P}}(U)$. Short English statement

The corrected question asks whether the degree-$d$ Veronese embedding has property $N_{3d-3}$: projective normality together with linear syzygies through homological degree $3d-3$. The intended field, characteristic, and parameter range require clarification. The implicitization question in the original extraction is assigned to the next record by the supplied correction.
