Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 31

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a field, and let $A$ and $B$ be finite-dimensional central division algebras over $F$, using the central simple algebra context of the supplied source. For a central simple $F$-algebra $C$, write $C\cong M_r(D)$, where $D$ is a central division $F$-algebra, and define its index by $\operatorname{ind}(C)=\sqrt{\dim_F D}$. In particular, put $a=\operatorname{ind}(A)$ and $b=\operatorname{ind}(B)$.

A field extension $E/F$ is a common splitting field of $A$ and $B$ if \[ A\otimes_F E\cong M_a(E) \qquad\text{and}\qquad B\otimes_F E\cong M_b(E) \] as $E$-algebras.

Problem 31. Suppose that \[ \operatorname{ind}(A\otimes_F B) <\operatorname{ind}(A)\operatorname{ind}(B). \] Describe the common splitting fields of $A$ and $B$.

Unresolved scope. The supplied formulation asks what these common splitting fields are, but specifies neither the desired form of a characterization nor any degree bound. It does not say whether the intended extensions must be finite, separable, or Galois. The definition above applies to any field extension; no additional restriction on the requested splitting fields is inferred. The source introduces this question as an alternative to considering maximal subfields, but the preceding formulation is not supplied.

Short English statement

For two central division algebras over the same field, suppose the index of their tensor product is strictly smaller than the product of their indices. The problem asks for a description of fields that split both algebras. The intended restrictions on those fields and the precise kind of description sought remain unspecified.
