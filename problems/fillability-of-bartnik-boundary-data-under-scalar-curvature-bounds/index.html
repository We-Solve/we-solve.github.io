Math-4906

Fillability of Bartnik Boundary Data Under Scalar Curvature Bounds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(Y,h)$ be a closed Riemannian manifold of dimension $n-1$, where closed means compact without boundary, and let $\varphi\in C^\infty(Y,{\mathbb{R}})$ be prescribed boundary mean-curvature data. Let $\sigma$ denote the prescribed scalar-curvature lower bound. The source does not explicitly specify the admissible dimensions or the domain of $\sigma$; interpreting $\sigma$ as a real constant is a qualification requiring confirmation.

For a smooth Riemannian $n$-manifold $(X,g)$ with boundary, let $g^\top$ denote the restriction of $g$ to tangent vectors along $\partial X$, let $R_g$ denote its scalar curvature, and let $H_g$ denote its boundary mean curvature. The source does not specify the normal direction, sign convention, or whether mean curvature is the trace or the normalized trace of the second fundamental form. Thus the following existence problem depends on fixing that convention.

Problem. Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $(Y,h,\varphi,\sigma)$ for the existence of a complete smooth Riemannian $n$-manifold $(X,g)$ and a diffeomorphism $\iota:Y\longrightarrow\partial X$ such that \[ R_g(x)\geq\sigma\quad\text{for every }x\in X, \qquad \iota^*(g^\top)=h, \qquad H_g\circ\iota=\varphi. \] The pair $(g^\top,H_g)$ is the Bartnik boundary data. The identification requires $Y$ to represent the entire boundary. No compactness assumption on $X$ is supplied. For a manifold with boundary, completeness can be interpreted as completeness of the intrinsic Riemannian length metric on $X$, including its boundary; the source does not explicitly confirm this interpretation. The dimension range, meaning of $\sigma$, completeness interpretation, and mean-curvature convention remain unresolved qualifications of this draft.

Short English statement

Characterize which prescribed boundary metrics and mean curvatures occur on the entire boundary of a complete Riemannian manifold with scalar curvature bounded below by $\sigma$. The source leaves the dimension range, the domain of $\sigma$, the completeness interpretation, and the mean-curvature convention unspecified.
