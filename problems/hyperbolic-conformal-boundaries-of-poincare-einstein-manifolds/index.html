Hyperbolic Conformal Boundaries of Poincaré–Einstein Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A standard interpretation of the supplied question is as follows. Let $n\geq 2$ be a boundary dimension, and let $\overline M$ be a compact smooth $(n+1)$-dimensional manifold with nonempty boundary $N=\partial\overline M$ and interior $M$. A boundary defining function is a function $\rho\in C^\infty(\overline M)$ satisfying \[ \rho>0\text{ on }M,\qquad \rho=0\text{ on }N,\qquad {\,\mathrm{d}}\rho\neq0\text{ on }N. \] A Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$ is conformally compactifiable if, for such a defining function, $\overline g=\rho^2g$ extends to a nondegenerate Riemannian metric on $\overline M$. The source does not specify the required regularity of this extension. In the usual normalization, the Poincaré--Einstein condition is \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g=-n g. \] The conformal boundary, or boundary at infinity, is $(N,[\gamma])$, where $\gamma=\overline g|_{TN}$ and $[\gamma]$ is its conformal class. Thus the standard interpretation of a boundary of constant sectional curvature $-1$ is that this conformal class contains a metric $h$ satisfying \[ K_h(\sigma)=-1 \quad\text{for every }x\in N\text{ and every two-dimensional subspace }\sigma\subset T_xN. \] For each fixed $n\geq2$, the resulting existence assertion is \[ \begin{gathered} \exists\,\overline M,g,\rho,h:\quad (M,g)\text{ is complete},\qquad \operatorname{Ric}_g=-ng,\\ \overline g=\rho^2g\text{ extends as above},\qquad N=\partial\overline M\text{ is compact and connected},\\ h\in[\overline g|_{TN}],\qquad K_h\equiv-1. \end{gathered} \] The supplied problem asks whether such a manifold exists, or whether no such manifold is possible. Its dimension is unspecified: the record does not determine whether a particular $n$ is intended or whether existence in some dimension is sufficient. The restriction $n\geq2$ above makes the sectional-curvature condition nonvacuous; it is an explicit interpretive convention, not a supplied dimension hypothesis. The conformal-representative interpretation and the compactification regularity also require confirmation from the source. No existence or nonexistence conclusion is asserted here.

Short English statement

Does a complete conformally compactifiable Poincaré--Einstein manifold have a compact connected conformal boundary admitting a metric of constant sectional curvature $-1$? The supplied record leaves the dimension and compactification regularity unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
