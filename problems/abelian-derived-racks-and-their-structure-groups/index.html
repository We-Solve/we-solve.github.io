Abelian Derived Racks and Their Structure Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write a set-theoretic Yang--Baxter map as $r\colon X\times X\to X\times X$, with $r(x,y)=(\lambda_x(y),\rho_y(x))$. The Yang--Baxter equation is \[ (r\times\operatorname{id}_X)(\operatorname{id}_X\times r)(r\times\operatorname{id}_X) = (\operatorname{id}_X\times r)(r\times\operatorname{id}_X)(\operatorname{id}_X\times r) \quad\text{on }X^3. \] Left nondegeneracy means that $\lambda_x\colon X\to X$ is bijective for every $x\in X$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify whether bijectivity of $r$ is included in its convention for a solution. No finiteness or involutivity hypothesis is stated.

For terminology, a left rack is a set $R$ with an operation $\triangleright$ such that each map $b\mapsto a\triangleright b$ is bijective and \[ a\triangleright(b\triangleright c) =(a\triangleright b)\triangleright(a\triangleright c) \qquad(a,b,c\in R). \] In this explicitly chosen notation, its structure group is \[ G(R)=\left\langle g_a\ (a\in R)\ \middle|\ g_a g_b=g_{a\triangleright b}g_a\quad(a,b\in R)\right\rangle. \] This fixes a notation for rack structure groups; the supplied record gives neither a left/right rack convention nor the construction of the derived rack from $r$. Denote the source's intended derived rack provisionally by $R_{\mathrm{der}}(X,r)$. The phrase "abelian rack" is also undefined in the supplied record and is retained as unresolved terminology.

Requested characterization problems. For left nondegenerate Yang--Baxter solutions in the source's intended class:

  1. Find an intrinsic property of $(X,r)$ equivalent to $G(R_{\mathrm{der}}(X,r))$ being abelian, that is, to $uv=vu$ for every $u,v$ in that group.
  2. Find an intrinsic property of $(X,r)$ equivalent to $R_{\mathrm{der}}(X,r)$ being an abelian rack, after the intended meaning of that term is specified.
  3. Determine whether splitting of the source's associated central extension, together with an additional condition, characterizes rack structure groups. Abstractly, a central extension is an exact sequence \[ 1\longrightarrow A\xrightarrow{\iota}E\xrightarrow{\pi}Q\longrightarrow1, \qquad \iota(A)\subseteq Z(E), \] and splitting means that there is a group homomorphism $s\colon Q\to E$ satisfying $\pi\circ s=\operatorname{id}_Q$. The record does not identify $A,E,Q$, their relation to the rack structure group, or the proposed additional condition; consequently, it does not determine a precise equivalence for this question.
  4. Give group-theoretic necessary and sufficient conditions for an abstract group $H$ to be isomorphic to $G(R)$ for a rack $R$ in the intended class. The record does not specify whether this class includes all racks or has additional restrictions.
These are characterization requests, not supplied conjectured equivalences. The missing definitions and hypotheses prevent a fully determined formulation of all four questions.

Short English statement

The problem asks how properties of a Yang--Baxter solution determine abelianness of its derived rack and of that rack's structure group, and how to recognize rack structure groups using group theory and central extensions. The derived-rack construction, the meaning of "abelian rack," and the relevant extension and additional condition are missing. This statement-only draft therefore retains those unresolved qualifications for human mathematical review.
