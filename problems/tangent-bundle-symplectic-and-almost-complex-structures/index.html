Math-5634

Tangent-Bundle Symplectic and Almost Complex Structures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X^{2n},\omega,J)$ be a symplectic manifold with compatible almost complex structure: $J^2=-1$, $\omega(JU,JV)=\omega(U,V)$, and $\omega(U,JU)>0$ for $U\ne0$. No integrability of $J$ is assumed.

For a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$, the metric identification $b_g:TM\to T^*M$, $v\mapsto g(v,\cdot)$, pulls the canonical cotangent symplectic form back to $\omega_g=b_g^*\omega_{\mathrm{can}}$. A torsion-free affine connection $\nabla$ splits $T(TM)$ into horizontal and vertical copies of $TM$. In this splitting its standard Dombrowski-type almost complex structure is \[ J_\nabla(U,V)=(-V,U). \] The source also requires the connection to be "compatible," but does not say whether this means metric compatibility, a statistical/Codazzi condition, or precisely compatibility of $J_\nabla$ with $\omega_g$. Those interpretations are not silently equated.

Characterization problem (Takashi Kurose). Give intrinsic necessary and sufficient conditions for $(X,\omega,J)$ to be locally of the above tangent-bundle form. More explicitly, near each relevant point seek a local diffeomorphism $\Phi$ into an open subset of $TM$, for suitable $M,g,\nabla$, satisfying \[ \Phi^*\omega_g=\omega,\qquad d\Phi\circ J=J_\nabla\circ d\Phi, \] with the intended compatibility condition on the torsion-free connection. The desired result is an intrinsic criterion/classification, not merely this restatement of local realization. Whether local charts must meet a distinguished zero section or preserve further tangent-bundle data is unspecified.

The supplied summary describes the construction and classical structure equations as understood, naming Dombrowski-type geometry, but says no fully general classification beyond those equations was verified. It gives no exact theorem or definition of compatible connection. This is attributed background, not certification that the characterization requested here is complete.

Short English statement

Characterize compatible symplectic/almost-complex structures locally modeled on a metric tangent bundle with the connection-induced Dombrowski structure. The meaning of connection compatibility and the required local tangent-bundle data are unspecified. Almost complex is not strengthened to complex or Kähler.
