Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Does an absolute C exist such that each centered log-concave probability measure $\mu $ with a Lebesgue density on $R^{n}$ and each $a>0$ satisfying $sup_{|\theta |=1}\int \exp (\langle x,\theta \rangle ^{2}/a^{2})d\mu \leq 2$ obeys $Ent_\mu (f^{2})\leq Ca^{2}\int |\nabla f|^{2}d\mu $ for every compactly supported smooth f? $Ent_\mu (g)=\int g$ $logg-(\int g)\log (\int g)$. Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Take any $n\ge1$, a centered log-concave probability measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb R^n$ with Lebesgue density, and $a>0$ such that $X\sim\mu$ obeys \[ \sup_{|\theta|=1}\mathbb E\exp(\langle X,\theta\rangle^2/a^2)\le2. \] The claimed absolute, dimension-independent constant $C$ gives, for all $f\in C_c^{\infty}(\mathbb R^n)$, \[ \operatorname{Ent}_{\mu}(f^2)\le Ca^2\int|\nabla f|^2\,d\mu, \qquad \operatorname{Ent}_{\mu}(g)=\int g\log g\,d\mu- (\int g\,d\mu)\log(\int g\,d\mu). \] Only linear-function subgaussianity is assumed, but a logarithmic Sobolev conclusion is claimed.
