Math-7205

Absolutely Convergent Multiplicative Expansions of the von Mangoldt Function

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}_{>0}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. The von Mangoldt function is \[ \Lambda(n)=\begin{cases} \log p,&n=p^k\text{ for a prime }p\text{ and an integer }k\geq 1,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \] where $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm; in particular, $\Lambda(1)=0$.

In the standard normalized convention, a function $h$ on ${\mathbb{N}}_{>0}$ is multiplicative if $h(1)=1$ and $h(ab)=h(a)h(b)$ whenever $a,b\in{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}$ satisfy $\gcd(a,b)=1$. This does not require complete multiplicativity.

The supplied question has the following formulation once a scalar field $K$ is specified. Do there exist functions \[ F:{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\longrightarrow K, \qquad G:{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\times{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\longrightarrow K \] such that $F$ is multiplicative, and, for every $n\in{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}$, \[ G(1,n)=1,\qquad G(ab,n)=G(a,n)G(b,n) \quad\text{whenever }\gcd(a,b)=1, \] \[ \sum_{q=1}^{\infty}|G(q,n)|<\infty, \qquad \Lambda(n)=F(n)\sum_{q=1}^{\infty}G(q,n)? \] Absolute convergence here is required separately for every positive integer $n$; no uniform convergence requirement is supplied.

The source does not specify the scalar codomains of $F$ and $G$. Thus the display is a formulation template, for example for $K={\mathbb{R}}$ or $K={\mathbb{C}}$, rather than a source-supported choice between them. The normalized convention for multiplicativity is made explicit above but is not defined in the record. No further restriction on the dependence of $G(q,n)$ on $n$, or any requirement that $G$ have a particular Ramanujan-expansion form, is supplied.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the von Mangoldt function can be expressed as a multiplicative prefactor times an absolutely convergent series whose terms are multiplicative in the summation variable for each fixed positive integer input. The scalar codomains and any intended additional restrictions on the coefficients remain unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
