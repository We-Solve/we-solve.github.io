Selection Principles in Mathematics — Problem 13 ([smzpow])

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Equip every subset of ${\mathbb{R}}$ with its subspace topology, and equip products with the product topology. For a topological space $T$, let $\mathcal O(T)$ be the collection of nonempty families of open subsets of $T$ whose union is $T$. Let $\Gamma(T)$ consist of the infinite open covers $\mathcal U$ of $T$ such that $T\notin\mathcal U$ and, for every $t\in T$, the set $\{U\in\mathcal U:t\notin U\}$ is finite.

The property $\mathsf S_1(\mathcal O,\mathcal O)$ holds for $T$ if, for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}$ in $\mathcal O(T)$, there exist choices $U_n\in\mathcal U_n$ for all $n\geq1$ such that \[ T=\bigcup_{n\geq1}U_n. \] The property $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma)$ holds for $T$ if, for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}$ of open covers of $T$, each having no finite subcover, there exist finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that \[ \left\{\bigcup\mathcal F_n:n\geq1\right\}\in\Gamma(T). \]

Question. For every pair $X,Y\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$, do the assumptions \[ X\models\mathsf S_1(\mathcal O,\mathcal O),\qquad Y\models\mathsf S_1(\mathcal O,\mathcal O),\qquad X\models\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma) \] imply \[ X\times Y\models\mathsf S_1(\mathcal O,\mathcal O)? \] Here $T\models P$ means that the space $T$ has property $P$. These are standard expansions of the selection principles; the supplied record does not reproduce the source's definitions. Its contextual reference label $[\mathrm{smzpow}]$ is unresolved in the supplied material.

Short English statement

Must the product of two Rothberger subsets of ${\mathbb{R}}$ be Rothberger when the first factor also has the Hurewicz property? Here Rothberger means $\mathsf S_1(\mathcal O,\mathcal O)$, and Hurewicz means $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma)$. The supplied registry labels this question open; that label has not been independently verified.
