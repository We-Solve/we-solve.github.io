Stochastic Regularization of Two-Dimensional Euler Flows

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb T^2$ denote the flat two-dimensional torus, with periodic spatial conditions. The deterministic incompressible Euler equations for velocity $u(t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$ and pressure $p(t,x)\in{\mathbb{R}}$ are \[ \partial_t u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=0, \qquad \nabla\cdot u=0, \qquad u(0,\cdot)=u_0. \] The initial velocity must satisfy the incompressibility constraint. The scalar vorticity is $\omega=\partial_1u_2-\partial_2u_1$; for sufficiently regular deterministic solutions it satisfies \[ \partial_t\omega+u\cdot\nabla\omega=0. \] For a spatial vector field $\sigma$, the transport operator on scalar vorticity is $L_\sigma\omega=\sigma\cdot\nabla\omega$. This defines the directional transport operation, but does not specify a stochastic Euler model.

Source question. Can a suitable transport-type stochastic perturbation of two-dimensional incompressible Euler flow on $\mathbb T^2$ restore uniqueness, prevent singular behavior, or otherwise improve well-posedness? The intended comparison is with the corresponding deterministic evolution for the relevant initial data.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record specifies neither an initial-data or vorticity class nor a solution concept, time interval, stochastic integration convention, covariance, spatial regularity, or admissible class of noise coefficients. It also leaves the meaning of uniqueness, singular behavior, and improved well-posedness unspecified. Consequently, the existential qualification "suitable" has no specified mathematical range, and the record does not determine a single fully quantified conjecture. The displayed deterministic equations provide context only; they do not select an unprovided stochastic equation or regularity hypothesis. A precise theorem-sized target requires these choices and a defined deterministic comparison.

Short English statement

The question asks whether suitable random transport can improve the behavior of incompressible Euler flow on the two-dimensional torus, especially uniqueness or avoidance of singular behavior. The supplied record leaves the noise, initial-data class, solution concept, and desired improvement unspecified, so this remains a qualified research question requiring human mathematical review.
