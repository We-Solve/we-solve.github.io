Math-7094

Continuous Choices of Points on Smooth Cubic Curves

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not explicitly specify the ground field or parameter space. The following gives a precise formulation under the conventional interpretation of smooth complex plane cubics parametrized by their equations; this interpretation requires confirmation against the source.

Let $V=\operatorname{Sym}^{3}(({\mathbb{C}}^{3})^{*})$, and give ${\mathbb{P}}(V)$ its complex analytic topology. Define \[ B=\{[f]\in{\mathbb{P}}(V): C_f=\{x\in{\mathbb{P}}^{2}({\mathbb{C}}):f(x)=0\}\text{ is smooth}\}. \] Thus equations differing by a nonzero scalar determine the same point of $B$. The universal family is \[ \mathcal C=\{([f],x)\in B\times{\mathbb{P}}^{2}({\mathbb{C}}):f(x)=0\}, \qquad \pi:\mathcal C\longrightarrow B,\quad ([f],x)\longmapsto[f]. \] For each integer $n\geq1$, let \[ \mathcal F_n=\{([f],x_1,\ldots,x_n)\in B\times({\mathbb{P}}^{2}({\mathbb{C}}))^n: f(x_i)=0\text{ for all }i,\ x_i\ne x_j\text{ for }i\ne j\}. \] Give $\mathcal F_n$ the subspace topology and let \[ \mathcal U_n=\mathcal F_n/\mathfrak S_n, \qquad q_n:\mathcal U_n\longrightarrow B, \] where the symmetric group permutes the points and $q_n$ remembers the cubic. A continuous multisection of degree $n$ is a continuous section $s:B\to\mathcal U_n$ of $q_n$. Its value assigns an unordered set of exactly $n$ distinct points of $C_f$ to each $[f]\in B$.

Two such sections $s_0,s_1$ are homotopic through multisections if there is a continuous map $H:B\times[0,1]\to\mathcal U_n$ satisfying \[ H(b,0)=s_0(b),\qquad H(b,1)=s_1(b),\qquad q_n(H(b,t))=b \quad(b\in B,\ t\in[0,1]). \] In particular, the points must remain distinct throughout the homotopy.

Classification problem. Determine all integers $n\geq1$ for which $q_n$ admits a continuous section, and for every such $n$, classify its sections up to homotopy through sections.

This formulation uses embedded complex cubics with fixed ambient projective plane. It imposes no additional equivariance under projective coordinate changes. The supplied record does not explicitly resolve whether this parameter space, a quotient moduli space, or a moduli stack is intended; these alternatives must not be identified without further source verification.

Short English statement

For each positive integer $n$, determine whether one can continuously assign an unordered set of $n$ distinct points to every smooth plane cubic, and classify all assignments up to continuous deformation that preserves distinctness. The displayed formulation assumes complex cubics parametrized by equations; the supplied record leaves that choice of domain implicit.
