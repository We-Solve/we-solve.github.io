Math-14227

Dini Spaces as Primitive Spectra of Amenable $C^*$-Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal D_{\mathrm{source}}$ denote the class of topological spaces called Dini spaces in the supplied source. The record does not define this term or specify its topological axioms. Consequently, the statement below preserves the source terminology; a self-contained characterization of its quantified domain remains unresolved.

A complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ is separable if it has a countable norm-dense subset. Interpret amenability in its standard $C^*$-algebra sense, equivalently nuclearity: for every finite subset $F\subseteq A$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there are an integer $n\geq 1$ and completely positive contractive linear maps \[ A\xrightarrow{\phi}M_n({\mathbb{C}})\xrightarrow{\psi}A \] such that $\|\psi(\phi(a))-a\|<\varepsilon$ for all $a\in F$. A linear map is completely positive if all its matrix amplifications preserve positivity, and contractive if its norm is at most one.

The primitive spectrum of $A$ is \[ \operatorname{Prim}(A)=\{\ker\pi:\pi\text{ is a nonzero irreducible }*\text{-representation of }A\}. \] Here irreducibility means that the representation has no proper nonzero closed invariant subspace. Equip $\operatorname{Prim}(A)$ with the hull--kernel topology, whose closed sets are \[ \operatorname{hull}(I)=\{P\in\operatorname{Prim}(A):I\subseteq P\}, \] where $I$ ranges over norm-closed two-sided ideals of $A$.

Question. Is it true that \[ \forall X\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{source}}\quad \exists\, A\text{ a separable amenable complex }C^*\text{-algebra}\quad X\cong\operatorname{Prim}(A), \] where $\cong$ denotes a homeomorphism? The record states no unitality requirement on $A$. Any boundary conditions or separation assumptions implicit in the term Dini space must be recovered from the source before this becomes a fully specified standalone statement.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every Dini space can occur, up to homeomorphism, as the primitive spectrum of a separable amenable $C^*$-algebra. The supplied record labels this question open, but does not define Dini space. Its exact topological domain therefore requires source review.
