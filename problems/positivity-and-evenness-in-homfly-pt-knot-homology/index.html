Math-4290

Positivity and Evenness in HOMFLY-PT Knot Homology

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for two classifications of knots. Its polynomial normalization, homology conventions, and intended category of knots are not specified, so the following formulation is conditional on fixing those conventions.

Let $\mathcal K$ denote the intended set of knot isotopy classes. For $K\in\mathcal K$, let $P_K(q,a)$ denote its HOMFLY--PT invariant in a fixed normalization. If this normalization gives an integral Laurent polynomial, write uniquely \[ P_K(q,a)=\sum_{(i,j)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}c_{i,j}(K)q^i(-a)^j, \qquad c_{i,j}(K)\in{\mathbb{Z}}, \] with finitely many nonzero coefficients. Interpret coefficient positivity provisionally as $c_{i,j}(K)\geq 0$ for every $i,j$, equivalently strict positivity for every nonzero coefficient. The first requested classification is to determine \[ \mathcal P=\{K\in\mathcal K: c_{i,j}(K)\geq 0\text{ for every }(i,j)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2\}. \] This definition requires clarification if the intended invariant is rational rather than Laurent polynomial, or if positivity refers to a particular expansion.

After fixing a reduced or unreduced version of triply graded Khovanov--Rozansky homology, its coefficient ring, and its absolute grading conventions, write \[ \mathcal H(K)=\bigoplus_{i,j,k\in{\mathbb{Z}}}\mathcal H^{i,j,k}(K), \] where $k$ denotes the homological degree. The second requested classification is to determine \[ \mathcal E=\{K\in\mathcal K:\mathcal H^{i,j,k}(K)=0 \text{ for all }i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}}\text{ and all }k\in 2{\mathbb{Z}}+1\}. \] Thus even concentration means vanishing in every odd homological degree. An odd shift of the homological grading changes this condition, so an absolute normalization is necessary.

The source asks to determine both classes; it does not explicitly assert that $\mathcal P=\mathcal E$. Its original wording says "even degrees," while the supplied cleaned statement specifies "even homological degrees." The choice of grading therefore remains a source qualification requiring review. No classification or equivalence is asserted here.

Short English statement

Classify knots whose HOMFLY--PT invariant has nonnegative coefficients in the monomials $q^i(-a)^j$, and separately classify knots whose triply graded Khovanov--Rozansky homology vanishes in odd homological degrees. Both questions require fixed invariant and grading conventions, which the supplied record does not provide.
