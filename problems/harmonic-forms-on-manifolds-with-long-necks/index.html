Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record refers to a family of stretched manifolds $X(r)$, with stretching parameter $r\to+\infty$, but does not specify its construction, Riemannian metrics, or boundary conditions. Consequently, the following notation is conditional on those data being supplied.

For each admissible $r$ and differential-form degree $k$, let $\Delta_r^k$ denote the Hodge Laplacian associated with the intended metric and let $\mathcal D(\Delta_r^k)$ denote its intended domain, including any boundary, integrability, or decay conditions. Its differential expression is \[ \Delta_r^k={\,\mathrm{d}}\,{\,\mathrm{d}}_r^*+{\,\mathrm{d}}_r^*{\,\mathrm{d}}, \] where ${\,\mathrm{d}}$ is the exterior derivative and ${\,\mathrm{d}}_r^*$ its metric-dependent formal adjoint. Write \[ \mathcal H_r^k =\{\omega\in\mathcal D(\Delta_r^k):\Delta_r^k\omega=0\} \] for the resulting space of harmonic $k$-forms. The coefficient field, relevant degrees, operator realization, and any normalization of forms remain unspecified by the record.

Source question. Describe the asymptotic behavior of the spaces of harmonic differential forms on $X(r)$ as $r\to+\infty$.

A precise asymptotic assertion requires the construction of $X(r)$, the geometric and analytic hypotheses, and a specified means of comparing forms or spaces for different values of $r$. The source does not specify a limiting space, comparison maps, topology of convergence, or desired asymptotic estimates. Thus the question is retained as an unresolved formulation request; the notation above does not select the missing hypotheses or assert a convergence theorem.
