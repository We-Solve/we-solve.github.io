Math-4214

Multiplicity Inequality for Quotients by Perfect Ideals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks about local rings without explicitly specifying commutativity, Noetherianity, or the meaning of multiplicity. The following formulation uses the customary setting of commutative Noetherian local rings and Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity; these conventions require confirmation against the cited report.

For a nonzero commutative Noetherian local ring $(A,\mathfrak m)$ of Krull dimension $d$, define its Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity by \[ e(A)=e_{\mathfrak m}(A)=\lim_{r\to\infty}\frac{d!\,\operatorname{length}_A(A/\mathfrak m^{r+1})}{r^d}. \] For a proper ideal $J$ of a commutative Noetherian local ring $T$, let $\operatorname{grade}_T(J)$ be the maximal length of a $T$-regular sequence contained in $J$. Here a sequence $x_1,\ldots,x_s$ is $T$-regular if multiplication by $x_i$ is injective on $T/(x_1,\ldots,x_{i-1})$ for every $i$, and the final quotient is nonzero. Call $J$ perfect if \[ \operatorname{pd}_T(T/J)=\operatorname{grade}_T(J)<\infty, \] where $\operatorname{pd}_T$ denotes projective dimension.

Question under these explicit conventions. For every nonzero commutative Noetherian local ring $(T,\mathfrak n)$ and every proper perfect ideal $J\subsetneq T$, must \[ e_{\mathfrak n}(T)\leq e_{\mathfrak n/J}(T/J) \] hold? The two multiplicities are computed in their respective Krull dimensions. The zero ideal is included whenever it satisfies the stated definition; no positive-height restriction is supplied. No Cohen--Macaulay, equidimensionality, characteristic, or residue-field hypothesis is stated in the record. Whether the report imposes any additional hypotheses remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Does quotienting a local ring by a perfect ideal always weakly increase its Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity? The formulation above makes conventional ring and multiplicity assumptions explicit, subject to source confirmation. The supplied curation labels the question open; that status has not been independently verified.
