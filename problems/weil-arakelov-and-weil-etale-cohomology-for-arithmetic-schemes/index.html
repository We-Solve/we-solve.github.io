Math-15003

Weil–Arakelov and Weil–Étale Cohomology for Arithmetic Schemes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An arithmetic scheme means a separated scheme of finite type over $\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{Z}}$. A complex of abelian groups is perfect if it is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex of finitely generated projective ${\mathbb{Z}}$-modules. A complex of real vector spaces is perfect if it is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex of finite-dimensional real vector spaces.

Conjectural existence problem, with unresolved source parameters. For every arithmetic scheme $X$, the source asks for compactly supported Weil--étale and Weil--Arakelov cohomology complexes \[ C_{W,c}(X,n):=R\Gamma_{W,c}(X,{\mathbb{Z}}(n)),\qquad C_{\mathrm{ar},c}(X,A,n):=R\Gamma_{\mathrm{ar},c}(X,A(n)). \] The supplied record does not specify the range or quantification of the twist $n$, the allowed coefficients $A$, or the full intended derived categories. These remain parameters requiring clarification; no additional restrictions on $X$ are supplied.

The requested complexes must satisfy at least the following conditions:

  1. $C_{W,c}(X,n)$ is a perfect complex of abelian groups.
  2. $C_{\mathrm{ar},c}(X,{\mathbb{R}},n)$ is a perfect complex of real vector spaces.
  3. Cup product with the source's fundamental class \[ \theta\in H^1(\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{Z}},{\mathbb{R}}(0)) \] makes the graded cohomology of $C_{\mathrm{ar},c}(X,{\mathbb{R}},n)$ into an acyclic complex. Explicitly, writing \[ V^i=H^i\left(C_{\mathrm{ar},c}(X,{\mathbb{R}},n)\right), \] the intended cup-product action must give maps $d_\theta^i:V^i\to V^{i+1}$ such that, for every $i\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, \[ d_\theta^{i+1}\circ d_\theta^i=0, \qquad \ker(d_\theta^i)=\operatorname{im}(d_\theta^{i-1}). \] This is acyclicity of the cohomology groups equipped with the cup-product differential; it does not assert that each $V^i$ vanishes.

Unresolved qualifications. The cohomology theory containing $\theta$, its normalization, and its action on compactly supported Weil--Arakelov cohomology are not defined in the supplied record. The displayed conditions alone do not characterize the intended cohomologies: zero complexes with zero action satisfy them. Additional structural requirements are therefore needed to distinguish the intended existence problem from a bare existence assertion about perfect complexes. The background mentions zeta-value properties, but provides no zeta-value formula or compatibility condition to state.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks for compactly supported Weil--étale and Weil--Arakelov cohomologies for every separated scheme of finite type over ${\mathbb{Z}}$. The integral Weil--étale complex and the real Weil--Arakelov complex should be perfect, and cup product with a fundamental class should give an exact complex on the latter's cohomology. The twist range, coefficient choices, and structural requirements identifying these cohomologies are missing. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review; the supplied open status is not independently verified.
