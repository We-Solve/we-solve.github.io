Accurate Answers to Adaptively Chosen Statistical Queries

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

A learning problem specifies a data distribution, a hypothesis class and a loss. Statistical consistency means that the estimator approaches its population target as the sample size increases; statistical and computational requirements are distinct.

A statistical query is an expectation $\mathbb E_{x\sim P}[q(x)]$ for a bounded function $q$. Adaptive queries may depend on earlier answers, so accuracy must account for the dependence between the sample and the selected queries.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given a population distribution P on X and an i.i.d. sample S of size n, can a possibly randomized procedure M(S) answer every adaptively chosen sequence of statistical queries accurately with high probability over S and M's randomness? [S2]
Scope qualification.
The query count, allowed query range, sample complexity, accuracy, and confidence are not quantified in the supplied question; unlimited-query accuracy is not silently asserted.

Short English statement

Answer adaptively selected population queries accurately despite their dependence on earlier sample-based answers.
