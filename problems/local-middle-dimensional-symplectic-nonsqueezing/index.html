Math-3489

Local Middle-Dimensional Symplectic Nonsqueezing

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the conventional standard symplectic coordinates $(q_1,p_1,\ldots,q_n,p_n)$ on ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, with Euclidean norm and symplectic form \[ \omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^{n}{\,\mathrm{d}} q_j\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}} p_j. \] For integers $1\leq k\leq n$, let $V_k=\{q_j=p_j=0\text{ for }j>k\}\cong{\mathbb{R}}^{2k}$, and let $P_k:{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}\to V_k$ be Euclidean orthogonal projection. This interprets the source's standard ${\mathbb{R}}^{2k}$ as a symplectic coordinate subspace. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the symplectic form, coordinate ordering, or admissible dimension range; these conventions and the intended range require confirmation. In particular, the strictly middle-dimensional range would be $1<k<n$.

Write \[ B_r(x)=\{y\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}:\|y-x\|<r\},\qquad B_1=B_1(0), \] and let $\operatorname{vol}_{2k}$ denote Euclidean Lebesgue measure on $V_k$, with \[ \omega_{2k}=\operatorname{vol}_{2k}\{v\in V_k:\|v\|<1\}. \] Here $\omega_{2k}$ is a volume constant, distinct from the symplectic form $\omega_0$. A smooth symplectomorphism is a smooth diffeomorphism $\phi:{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}\to{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$ satisfying $\phi^*\omega_0=\omega_0$.

For each admissible fixed pair $(n,k)$, the corrected supplied formulation asks the following two questions.

  1. For every smooth symplectomorphism $\phi:{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}\to{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$ and every $x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, does there exist $r_0>0$ such that \[ \operatorname{vol}_{2k}\left(P_k(\phi(B_r(x)))\right)\geq\omega_{2k}r^{2k} \qquad\text{for every }0<r<r_0? \] The radius $r_0$ may depend on $\phi,x,n,k$; no uniform radius is requested.
  2. For every smooth family $(\phi_t)_{t\in[0,1]}$ of symplectomorphisms of ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, meaning that $(t,z)\mapsto\phi_t(z)$ is smooth, with $\phi_0$ linear, does there exist $\delta\in(0,1]$ such that \[ \operatorname{vol}_{2k}\left(P_k(\phi_t(B_1))\right)\geq\omega_{2k} \qquad\text{for every }t\in[0,\delta)? \] The number $\delta$ may depend on the family and on $n,k$.

The original supplied formulation also asked for the corresponding global inequality for every $r>0$. The supplied correction and literature assessment report counterexamples to the global smooth nonlinear analogue and retain the two local questions above. The record labels these local questions open and labels its corrected statement corrected_verified; these are supplied curation claims, not independent verification. Its literature assessment also references local analytic non-squeezing, but supplies no precise analytic theorem or hypotheses to incorporate here.

Short English statement

For permanent UnsolvedMath ID $30002126$, the two questions ask whether projecting a smooth symplectic image onto a standard symplectic coordinate subspace preserves at least the corresponding ball volume, either for sufficiently small balls around each point or for sufficiently small nonnegative times along a smooth family starting at a linear map. The intended dimension range needs confirmation. The supplied dated curation labels the local questions open and reports counterexamples to the global analogue.
