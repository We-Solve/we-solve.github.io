Math-4358

Finiteness of Genera of Simply Connected Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a field finitely generated over its prime subfield, and let $G$ be an absolutely simple simply connected algebraic group over $K$. Here absolutely simple means that $G$ is connected and semisimple and, after extension to an algebraic closure of $K$, has no nontrivial proper connected normal algebraic subgroup. Simply connected is understood in the algebraic-group sense: every central isogeny $H\to G$ from a connected semisimple algebraic $K$-group is an isomorphism.

The supplied question is whether \[ \text{for every such pair }(K,G),\qquad K\text{ has good characteristic for }G \ \Longrightarrow\ \left|\operatorname{gen}_{K}(G)\right|<\infty. \] The source record does not define either $\operatorname{gen}_{K}(G)$ or the condition "good characteristic." Consequently, this displayed formulation preserves the supplied question but does not yet specify a complete mathematical proposition.

One conventional interpretation of genus uses maximal tori. A maximal $K$-torus of $G$ is a $K$-subgroup whose extension to an algebraic closure is a maximal torus. Under this interpretation, two groups have the same maximal $K$-tori if every maximal $K$-torus of either group is $K$-isomorphic to a maximal $K$-torus of the other. A possible definition is then \[ \operatorname{gen}_{K}(G)= \left\{ [G']_{K}: \begin{array}{l} G'\text{ is an inner }K\text{-form of }G,\\ G'\text{ and }G\text{ have the same maximal }K\text{-tori} \end{array} \right\}, \] where $[G']_{K}$ denotes a $K$-isomorphism class, and an inner form is a form whose twisting cocycle takes values in the inner automorphism group of $G$. This definition is an explicitly conditional interpretation, not a definition established by the supplied record. Both the intended genus convention and the exact characteristic restriction require source confirmation.

Short English statement

The recorded question asks whether every absolutely simple simply connected algebraic group over a finitely generated field of suitable characteristic has finite genus. The genus convention and the meaning of suitable characteristic are unspecified. The supplied registry treats this entry as a duplicate with an unrecoverable standalone statement; its open label is supplied curation, not an independently verified status.
