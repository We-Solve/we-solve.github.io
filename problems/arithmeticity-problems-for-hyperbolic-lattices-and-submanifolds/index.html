Arithmeticity Problems for Hyperbolic Lattices and Submanifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H^n$ denote real hyperbolic $n$-space of sectional curvature $-1$, and let $\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$ be its projective orthogonal isometry group. A lattice in this group is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma$ for which $\Gamma\backslash\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$ has finite Haar volume. A real hyperbolic $n$-manifold is a Riemannian $n$-manifold of constant sectional curvature $-1$. Completeness and finite volume are additional conditions that the supplied manifold question does not explicitly state. A submanifold is totally geodesic if its second fundamental form vanishes. A number field $k$ is totally real if every field embedding $\sigma:k\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$ has image in ${\mathbb{R}}$.

The corrected source record asks the following two questions.

  1. Does there exist an integer $n\geq4$ and a real hyperbolic $n$-manifold $M$ such that, for every integer $m$ satisfying $2\leq m\leq n-1$, $M$ contains no arithmetic totally geodesic $m$-submanifold?

    Here "arithmetic" is understood as referring to arithmeticity of the hyperbolic lattice defining the submanifold, rather than merely to arithmeticity of an abstract group. This interpretation requires clarification of the intended submanifolds: the record does not specify whether they must be complete, have finite volume, or be embedded rather than immersed. It also does not specify completeness or finite volume of $M$. Thus these conditions must be fixed before this question has a fully determined formulation in terms of hyperbolic lattices. The displayed existential quantifier follows the wording "a hyperbolic $n$-manifold, with $n\geq4$"; the record does not explicitly request an example for each such dimension.

  2. For every integer $n\geq4$ and every lattice $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$, is the trace field of $\Gamma$ totally real?

    The phrase "trace field" is not defined in the supplied record. Two possible precise conventions are the field of traces in the defining orthogonal representation and the adjoint trace field. To describe these without choosing between them, let $\pi:\operatorname{O}(n,1)\to\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$ be the projectivization map and set \[ k_{\mathrm{def}}(\Gamma)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\operatorname{tr}(A):A\in\pi^{-1}(\Gamma)\right), \qquad k_{\mathrm{Ad}}(\Gamma)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{Ad}(\gamma)):\gamma\in\Gamma\right), \] where $\operatorname{Ad}$ acts on the real Lie algebra $\mathfrak{so}(n,1)$. Using the full inverse image in the first expression avoids choosing representatives for projective elements. The intended trace convention must be confirmed; no equivalence of the resulting questions is asserted here. The source also leaves implicit the number-field setting needed for the stated definition of total reality.

The original statement additionally asked whether there are infinitely many commensurability classes of nonarithmetic lattices in $\operatorname{PU}(2,1)$, the projective unitary isometry group of complex hyperbolic two-space. The supplied correction explicitly removes that clause because it is subsumed by source problem OWR-16929-001. It is retained here as provenance, not as a third target for this entry. The supplied open and verification labels are registry metadata, not independent mathematical verification.

Short English statement

The corrected entry asks whether some real hyperbolic manifold of dimension at least four has no arithmetic totally geodesic submanifold of intermediate dimension, and whether every lattice in $\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$, for every $n\geq4$, has totally real trace field. The intended completeness, volume, embedding, and trace-field conventions require clarification. A former question about $\operatorname{PU}(2,1)$ was removed as a duplicate.
