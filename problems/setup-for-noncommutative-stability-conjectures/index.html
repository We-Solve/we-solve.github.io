Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record is introductory setup, not a recoverable standalone conjecture. It refers to conjectured generalizations of equations (3) and (4), whose statements are absent. Its supplied verification note identifies the intended later targets as Conjectures 10 and 12, preserved in subsequent records; neither conjecture is included here.

The recoverable mathematical content is an attributed lemma concerning a polynomial $p(x_1,\ldots,x_d)$ in noncommuting indeterminates, normalized by $p(0)=1$. For a matrix tuple $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_d)$, evaluation means substituting the matrices into each word of $p$, with the constant term evaluated as the identity matrix. The extraction does not explicitly specify the coefficient field, the matrix field, or the definition of $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{row}}$. A usual complex-matrix convention is \[ \|X\|_{\mathrm{row}}=\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{d}X_jX_j^*\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}^{1/2}, \] where $^*$ denotes conjugate transpose and $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ is the operator norm for the Euclidean norm. This convention is recorded for interpretation only; its applicability to the source must be checked.

With those unresolved conventions understood, the passage labeled Lemma 9 asserts the equivalence of the following conditions:

  1. For every positive integer $n$ and every tuple $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_d)$ of $n\times n$ matrices satisfying $\|X\|_{\mathrm{row}}\leq 1$, one has \[ \det p(X_1,\ldots,X_d)\ne 0. \]
  2. There exist a positive integer $m$ and a tuple $A=(A_1,\ldots,A_d)$ of $m\times m$ matrices satisfying $\|A\|_{\mathrm{row}}<1$ such that, for every positive integer $n$ and every tuple $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_d)$ of $n\times n$ matrices, without a norm restriction, \[ \det p(X_1,\ldots,X_d) = \det\!\left(I_m\otimes I_n-\sum_{j=1}^{d}A_j\otimes X_j\right). \] Here $I_k$ denotes the $k\times k$ identity matrix and $\otimes$ is the Kronecker product. The summation in this determinant is an explicit reconstruction of a damaged summation marker in the extraction.
This is a qualified transcription of an attributed lemma, not an independently verified theorem or a replacement for the missing conjectures.

Short English statement

This record introduces a proposed notion of noncommutative stability through an attributed equivalence between determinant nonvanishing on a closed row-norm ball and a determinantal representation with strictly contractive coefficient matrices. The actual conjectures are missing, and several conventions and interleaved formulas require source review. The supplied open label concerns an incomplete record; it does not establish that Lemma 9 is an open problem.
