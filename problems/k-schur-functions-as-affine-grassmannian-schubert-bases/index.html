Math-1710

k-Schur functions as affine Grassmannian Schubert bases

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix $k\ge1$, put $n=k+1$, and let $\Lambda$ be the integral graded ring of symmetric functions, with complete functions $h_i$, monomial functions $m_\lambda$, and the Hall pairing. Set \[ \Lambda_{(k)}={\mathbb{Z}}[h_1,\ldots,h_k],\qquad \Lambda^{(k)}=\Lambda/\langle m_\lambda:\lambda_1>k\rangle. \] These are graded dual under the Hall pairing. Index their standard $t=1$ $k$-Schur and dual $k$-Schur bases by $k$-bounded partitions $\lambda$, meaning $\lambda_1\le k$: write them $s_\lambda^{(k)}$ and $\mathfrak S_\lambda^{(k)}$, with \[ \langle s_\lambda^{(k)},\mathfrak S_\mu^{(k)}\rangle=\delta_{\lambda\mu}. \] Let $\operatorname{Gr}=SL_n({\mathbb{C}}((t)))/SL_n({\mathbb{C}}[[t]])$ be the affine Grassmannian. Its Schubert classes are indexed by affine Grassmannian elements of the affine symmetric group, identified with $k$-bounded partitions by the standard bijection. Denote the homology and cohomology Schubert classes by $\xi_\lambda$ and $\xi^\lambda$, in topological degrees $2|\lambda|$.

The source requests the type-$A$ identifications \[ \Lambda_{(k)}\xrightarrow{\sim}H_*(\operatorname{Gr};{\mathbb{Z}}), \quad s_\lambda^{(k)}\longmapsto\xi_\lambda, \qquad \Lambda^{(k)}\xrightarrow{\sim}H^*_{\rm gr}(\operatorname{Gr};{\mathbb{Z}}), \quad\mathfrak S_\lambda^{(k)}\longmapsto\xi^\lambda. \] The homology multiplication is the Pontryagin product and cohomology uses cup product; $H^*_{\rm gr}$ denotes the direct sum of the graded cohomology groups, dual degree by degree to homology. If a completed cohomology convention is used instead, the symmetric-function side must be completed as well. No extra deformation parameter $t$ is retained in the $k$-Schur functions here.

The phrase "symmetric component of affine Schubert polynomials" is interpreted by the specialization in the affine-flag representatives mentioned in the supplied aid: an affine Schubert representative indexed by $w$ specializes to its affine Stanley symmetric function $F_w$, and for the affine Grassmannian index $w_\lambda$, \[ F_{w_\lambda}=\mathfrak S_\lambda^{(k)} \] as a class in the dual symmetric-function model. A precise polynomial representative ring and specialization map require the conventions of the cited work, not a literal unspecified decomposition into a symmetric and nonsymmetric summand.

The supplied aid attributes the homology and cohomology statements to Lam and the affine-flag representative interpretation to Lee, and reports them as solved in the literature. It also reports the following low-degree consequence: in combinatorial degrees $d\le k$, both models have ordinary Schur representatives $s_\lambda$ (in the quotient where appropriate), and products of total degree at most $k$ have the usual Littlewood--Richardson coefficients. At degree $k+1$, each affine model has rank $p(k+1)-1$, where $p(d)$ counts partitions of $d$, whereas $\Lambda_{k+1}$ has rank $p(k+1)$. Hence the full uniform identification with unrestricted ordinary Schur calculus cannot extend to that degree; this does not preclude individual higher-degree coincidences.

Short English statement

Identify the type-$A$, $t=1$ $k$-Schur and dual bases with affine-Grassmannian homology and cohomology Schubert classes, and interpret the older symmetric-component phrase through affine Stanley specialization. The supplied report labels these literature results solved and records a sharp uniform low-degree comparison with ordinary Schur calculus.
