Math-3369

Sylow Restrictions and Character Fields of Values

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $G$ be a finite group, and let $P\leq G$ be a Sylow $p$-subgroup, so that $|P|$ is the largest power of $p$ dividing $|G|$. Write $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ for the irreducible complex characters of $G$. A character $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ has $p'$-degree if $p\nmid\chi(1)$.

For a positive integer $n$, put $\zeta_n=e^{2\pi i/n}$ and ${\mathbb{Q}}_n={\mathbb{Q}}(\zeta_n)$. Define \[ {\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(g):g\in G\right),\qquad \chi_P=\operatorname{Res}^{G}_{P}\chi,\qquad {\mathbb{Q}}(\chi_P)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(x):x\in P\right). \] Here $\chi_P$ need not be irreducible. Interpret the conductor of $\chi$ in the standard cyclotomic sense as \[ c(\chi)=\min\{n\geq1:{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}}_n\}. \] Write $c(\chi)=p^a m$, where $a\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$, $m\geq1$, and $p\nmid m$. With this convention, the restriction field lies in ${\mathbb{Q}}_{p^a}$, so the following field degree is defined; in particular, ${\mathbb{Q}}_{p^0}={\mathbb{Q}}_1={\mathbb{Q}}$.

Question from the supplied corrected statement. Is it true that, for every prime $p$, every finite group $G$, every Sylow $p$-subgroup $P$ of $G$, and every $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ of $p'$-degree with conductor $p^a m$ as above, \[ p\nmid[{\mathbb{Q}}_{p^a}:{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi_P)]\,? \]

Source qualifications. The conductor convention above makes explicit terminology left undefined in the supplied statement. The general-prime quantifier follows the supplied corrected statement. The supplied original excerpt is corrupted and ends with an instruction to fix $p=2$; it does not contain the displayed question in recoverable form. Thus the intended scope in the original report cannot be confirmed from this excerpt alone.

The surrounding excerpt motivates the question through characters of $p'$-degree and the McKay conjecture. It also states that character fields are abelian extensions of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and that, for ${\mathbb{Q}}\subseteq F\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}}_n$, the semidirect product $C_n\rtimes\operatorname{Gal}({\mathbb{Q}}_n/F)$, using the cyclotomic action on the cyclic group $C_n$, has an irreducible character with field of values $F$. These are supplied contextual claims, not additional hypotheses of the question.

Short English statement

For an irreducible character whose degree is prime to $p$, take the $p$-part of its cyclotomic conductor. Must the field generated by its values on a Sylow $p$-subgroup have index prime to $p$ in the corresponding cyclotomic field? This draft follows the supplied general-prime formulation; the corrupted original excerpt also mentions specializing to $p=2$.
