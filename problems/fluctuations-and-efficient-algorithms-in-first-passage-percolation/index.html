Math-437

Fluctuations and efficient algorithms in first-passage percolation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In a standard edge first-passage model on the nearest-neighbor graph of ${\mathbb{Z}}^2$, assign i.i.d. nonnegative passage times $t_e$ with common law $\nu$, and put \[ T(x,y)=\inf_{\pi:x\to y}\sum_{e\in\pi}t_e,\qquad T_n=T(0,(n,0)). \] The source asks whether boundary fluctuations have a Tracy--Widom limit and whether passage-time variance has order $n^{2/3}$. The latter, under a fixed law with finite relevant variances, means existence of constants $0<c_\nu\leq C_\nu<\infty$ such that \[ c_\nu n^{2/3}\leq\operatorname{Var}(T_n)\leq C_\nu n^{2/3} \] for all sufficiently large $n$.

The infected set at time $t$ is $B(t)=\{x:T(0,x)\leq t\}$. A boundary-fluctuation limit needs a specified boundary observable, direction, centering, scaling and Tracy--Widom variant. Boundary height, passage-time fluctuations and geodesic transverse displacement are different quantities; the variance exponent corresponds to an $n^{1/3}$ standard-deviation scale, not an asserted $n^{2/3}$ standard deviation. No particular limit distribution index or normalization is selected by the record.

The weight law and its hypotheses are absent. The claim cannot be universal over arbitrary i.i.d. laws: deterministic weights have zero variance. Atoms, zero-weight percolation and moment failures can also change the regime or finiteness of the quantities. The source must identify a fixed model or a nondegenerate universality class before the displayed bounds and limit are definite conjectures.

The final question asks for optimal computational bounds to find a shortest path or estimate its length. On the infinite graph, input/access to weights, a finite search region or exploration rule, exact versus approximate output, error probability and cost model must be specified. Reading a finite weighted box and adaptively querying an infinite random environment are distinct tasks. The source does not supply those conventions, and no algorithmic optimality assertion is inferred.

Short English statement

For a suitable planar i.i.d. first-passage model, determine the proposed Tracy--Widom boundary law and $n^{2/3}$ passage-time variance, and formulate optimal path/length computation. The weight law, normalization and computational model are essential missing data.
