Continuity of the Integrated Density of States

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
For finite-volume restrictions $H_\Lambda$ of a lattice or continuum Schrödinger operator, the integrated density of states is a thermodynamic limit of $|\Lambda|^{-1}$ times the number of eigenvalues at most $E$, with an appropriate volume normalization. Denote that distribution function by $k(E)$. Continuity means that the limiting spectral counting measure has no atom at any energy.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Prove continuity in energy of the integrated density of states $k(E)$ in the higher-dimensional continuum setting targeted by Simon. The source explicitly says that the one-dimensional and discrete cases were already known when this question was posed.
Scope and source qualification.
The original higher-dimensional continuum scope is restored; the bare catalogue sentence omitted it. The operator class and regularity hypotheses of the referenced integrated-density-of-states framework require specification, rather than an assertion for every arbitrary spectral model.

Short English statement

Show that the integrated density of states has no jumps as the energy varies.
