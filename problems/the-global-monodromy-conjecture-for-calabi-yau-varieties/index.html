The Global Monodromy Conjecture for Calabi–Yau Varieties

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record calls this the global monodromy conjecture for Calabi--Yau varieties. It does not specify the hypotheses on the field $K$, the convention for a Calabi--Yau variety, or the definition and coefficient ring of the motivic zeta function. The following is therefore a conditional transcription of the supplied claim, pending specification of those data.

For a Calabi--Yau variety $X/K$ in the intended setting, write $Z_X(T)$ for its motivic zeta function and $\mathbb L$ for the Lefschetz class, namely the class of the affine line in the intended motivic coefficient ring $R$. Let $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$ be a separable closure of $K$. Once an admissible prime $\ell$ and the intended tame monodromy operator $\sigma$ have been specified, put \[ H^i_\ell(X)=H^i_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(X\times_K K^{\mathrm{sep}},{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell),\qquad \chi_{i,\sigma}(U)=\det\left(U\,\mathrm{id}-\sigma\mid H^i_\ell(X)\right). \] The existence and meaning of this operator require hypotheses absent from the supplied record.

Using the conventional exponent ranges $a\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ and $b\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, define \[ m(a,b)=\operatorname{ord}_{{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}}}(a/b+{\mathbb{Z}}) =\frac{b}{\gcd(|a|,b)}. \] Thus $m(0,b)=1$. Let $\Phi_m(U)\in{\mathbb{Z}}[U]$ be the $m$-th cyclotomic polynomial.

The supplied conjectural assertion is that, for every such $X/K$, there exist a finite set $S\subset{\mathbb{Z}}\times{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, positive integers $e_{a,b}$ for $(a,b)\in S$, and a polynomial $P(T)\in R[T]$ such that \[ Z_X(T)=\frac{P(T)}{\displaystyle\prod_{(a,b)\in S}(1-\mathbb L^aT^b)^{e_{a,b}}}, \] and \[ \forall(a,b)\in S\quad\exists i\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\quad \Phi_{m(a,b)}(U)\mid\chi_{i,\sigma}(U) \quad\text{in }{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell[U]. \] The cohomological degree may depend on $(a,b)$. This asks for the existence of a rational expression with the stated property. The ring $R$, the interpretation of the equality, the availability of negative powers of $\mathbb L$, and the exponent ranges must be confirmed from the intended source context; the ranges displayed above are conventional and are not explicitly given in the supplied record. No precise definition of $Z_X(T)$, or additional hypothesis on $K$ or $X$, is supplied here.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every Calabi--Yau variety in the intended setting has a motivic zeta function admitting a finite rational denominator whose exponent ratios predict cyclotomic factors in some tame monodromy characteristic polynomial. Essential field, zeta-function, and monodromy conventions are missing, so this is a conditional statement requiring human mathematical review.
