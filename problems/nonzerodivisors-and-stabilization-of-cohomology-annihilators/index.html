Math-3641

Nonzerodivisors and Stabilization of Cohomology Annihilators

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Lambda$ be a noetherian algebra that is finitely generated as a module over its center \[ Z=Z(\Lambda)=\{z\in\Lambda: z\lambda=\lambda z\text{ for every }\lambda\in\Lambda\}. \] The supplied record does not define its cohomology-annihilator ideal or its degree approximations. A conventional candidate formulation is given below; its module-side convention, degree range, and indexing require confirmation from the cited source.

For this candidate formulation, let $\operatorname{mod}\Lambda$ denote the category of finitely generated right $\Lambda$-modules. Each $\operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(M,N)$ is a $Z$-module through the central action. For a $Z$-module $X$, write \[ \operatorname{ann}_Z(X)=\{z\in Z:zx=0\text{ for every }x\in X\}. \] For $s\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$, define the candidate ideals \[ \operatorname{ca}_s(\Lambda) = \bigcap_{\substack{M,N\in\operatorname{mod}\Lambda\\ i\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\ i\geq s}} \operatorname{ann}_Z\!\left(\operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(M,N)\right), \qquad \operatorname{ca}(\Lambda)=\bigcup_{s\geq 0}\operatorname{ca}_s(\Lambda). \] These definitions describe annihilation in every degree at least $s$, rather than only in degree $s$. The supplied phrase "degree-$s$ approximation" does not itself settle this distinction.

Under these conventions, the intended questions are the following, for every algebra satisfying the stated hypotheses:

  1. Does there exist $c\in\operatorname{ca}(\Lambda)$ that is a nonzerodivisor? If regularity is intended on $\Lambda$, this means \[ \text{for every }x\in\Lambda,\qquad cx=0\Longrightarrow x=0. \] Since $c$ is central, left and right multiplication give the same condition. If regularity is intended only in $Z$, the quantified element $x$ instead ranges over $Z$. The supplied record does not specify which requirement is intended.
  2. Does the set \[ \{s\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:\operatorname{ca}_s(\Lambda)=\operatorname{ca}(\Lambda)\} \] have a least element, and, when it does, what is its value for $\Lambda$? The record supplies neither an indexing convention nor a proposed formula or bound for this stabilization index.
The record also leaves unspecified whether "noetherian" means left noetherian, right noetherian, or both. Thus the candidate definitions above make the intended questions explicit conditionally; they do not constitute a verified reconstruction of the source formulation.

Short English statement

For a noetherian algebra finite over its center, ask whether its cohomology-annihilator ideal contains a nonzerodivisor and determine the first degree approximation equal to the full ideal. The supplied record leaves the annihilator convention, module side, indexing, and meaning of nonzerodivisor unresolved. Its supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that status.
