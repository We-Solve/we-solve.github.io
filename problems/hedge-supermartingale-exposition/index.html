A correct weighted moment-generating-function route

The preserved record is a fragment about Hedge and supermartingales. The complete workshop passage specifies bounded losses and defensive forecasting. We prove a pathwise potential statement, then state the extra probabilistic conditions needed for supermartingale terminology. This is a known exponential-weights calculation, not a newly solved research question. Let $K\ge2$, $\eta>0$, and $\ell_{t,k}\in[0,1]$. Write $R_{0,k}=0$, choose \[ w_{t,k}=\frac{e^{\eta R_{t-1,k}}}{\sum_j e^{\eta R_{t-1,j}}},\qquad R_{t,k}=R_{t-1,k}+\sum_jw_{t,j}\ell_{t,j}-\ell_{t,k}. \]
Proposition: The potential $\Phi_t=K^{-1}\sum_k\exp(\eta R_{t,k}-t\eta^2/8)$ is nonincreasing, starting at one. For each $k$ and $T\ge1$, \[ R_{T,k}\le\frac{\log K}{\eta}+\frac{T\eta}{8}. \] With $\eta=\sqrt{8\log K/T}$ this becomes $R_{T,k}\le\sqrt{T\log K/2}$.
Proof: For any finitely supported $X\in[0,1]$, set $h(u)=\log\mathbb E e^{u(X-\mathbb EX)}$. Differentiating this finite sum gives $h(0)=h'(0)=0$ and $h''(u)=\operatorname{Var}_u(X)$ under the exponentially tilted weights. That variance is at most $1/4$, since it is no larger than $\mathbb E_u(X-1/2)^2\le1/4$. Integrating twice on $[-\eta,0]$ yields \[ h(-\eta)=\int_{-\eta}^0(s+\eta)h''(s)\,ds\le\eta^2/8. \] At time $t$, let $X$ take the values $\ell_{t,k}$ with probabilities $w_{t,k}$. Direct cancellation of the potential normalization gives \[ \frac{\Phi_t}{\Phi_{t-1}} =e^{-\eta^2/8}\sum_kw_{t,k}e^{\eta(\mathbb EX-\ell_{t,k})} \le1. \] Induction proves $\Phi_T\le1$. Each nonnegative summand divided by $K$ is at most one, giving $\eta R_{T,k}-T\eta^2/8\le\log K$. Substitution of the stated $\eta$ proves the last bound. ∎
If losses are random on a probability space and the displayed process is adapted to a filtration, then each fixed-time potential is bounded and integrable, and pathwise monotonicity gives $\mathbb E[\Phi_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]\le\Phi_{t-1}$. Under these explicit conditions it is also a supermartingale. No unspecified stochastic law is assumed. The proof uses a weighted moment bound; it does not require an unqualified pointwise lower bound for $1+\eta r$ on the entire regret interval $[-1,1]$.
