Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every generalized amplitude-damping qubit channel $A_{\gamma ,\nu }$, $\gamma ,\nu \in [0,1]$, is its n-copy Holevo information exactly n times its one-copy value for every $n\geq 1$? Require that the one-copy optimizer is a phase-opposite equiprobable pair $\operatorname{sqrt}(1-p)|0>\pm \operatorname{sqrt}(p)|1>$, so its optimized output-entropy difference gives the unassisted classical capacity.

Single-letter classical capacity of generalized amplitude damping

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every generalized amplitude-damping qubit channel is claimed to have an explicit one-variable unassisted capacity formula achieved by binary pure states, with the listed universal partner-channel additivities.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For $\gamma,\nu\in[0,1]$, define the qubit channel \[ \mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}\begin{pmatrix}1-q&z\\\overline z&q\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}1-t&\sqrt{1-\gamma}z\\\sqrt{1-\gamma}\overline z&t\end{pmatrix}, \quad t=(1-\gamma)q+\gamma\nu. \] Put $h(t)=-t\log t-(1-t)\log(1-t)$, $g(u)=h((1+\sqrt{1-4u})/2)$, and $v_{\gamma,\nu}(p)=\gamma\nu(1-\nu)+\gamma(1-\gamma)(p-\nu)^2$. The claimed Holevo-information formula in bits is \[ \chi(\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}^{\otimes n}) =n\chi(\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}) =\frac n{\log2}\max_{0\le p\le1} [h((1-\gamma)p+\gamma\nu)-g(v_{\gamma,\nu}(p))] \] for every $n\ge1$, so its unassisted classical capacity is single-letter. Independent products of the two equiprobable states $\sqrt{1-p}|0\rangle\pm\sqrt p|1\rangle$, at a maximizing $p$, attain this information.

Outline of the argument

A block-entropy inequality is reduced to log-determinant comparisons and contraction convexity. This controls correlated channel inputs, proves the one-use binary optimum, and extends the entropy and Holevo bounds to arbitrary finite-dimensional tensor partners.
