Math-1272

An explicit CM projector and Kummer correspondence

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E/{\mathbb{Q}}$ be an elliptic curve with complex multiplication by an imaginary quadratic field $K$, and let $\chi$ be its associated Hecke character. The source asks for an explicit algebraic correspondence from $A=E\times E$ to an elliptic modular surface $\mathcal E\to X_{\mathrm{mod}}$, realizing their common rank-two $\chi^2$, $\overline\chi^{,2}$ summand in degree-two cohomology. It associates $\chi^2$ with a weight-three modular form, and gives its level as the norm of the conductor of $\chi^2$ times the absolute discriminant of $K$. A correspondence between these surfaces means a rational codimension-two cycle on $A\times\mathcal E$, not necessarily a rational map. The target surface, level convention, field and required action on the summand must be specified.

The attached aid reports an explicit source projector. Choose a nonzero trace-zero CM endomorphism $\delta$, put $n=\deg\delta$, let $a=\delta\times\delta$ act on $A$, and let $\pi_2$ be its degree-two Chow--Künneth projector. With $\Gamma_a$ the graph, it reports the rational Chow idempotent \[ p_T=\frac12\left(\pi_2-\frac1n\pi_2\circ{}^t\Gamma_a\circ\pi_2\right). \] Its realizations are reported to be exactly the two-dimensional $\chi^2$, $\overline\chi^{,2}$ summand. Although $\delta$ may require a CM field extension, the aid reports descent of the projected correspondence $p_T$ to ${\mathbb{Q}}$; this is not an assertion that $a$ itself is a ${\mathbb{Q}}$-endomorphism.

Let $S=\operatorname{Km}(A)$ be the resolved Kummer quotient, and let $\gamma$ be the resolved degree-two quotient graph regarded as a Chow correspondence. The aid reports \[ Z=\gamma\circ p_T,\qquad W=\frac12p_T\circ{}^t\gamma, \qquad W\circ Z=p_T,\qquad Z\circ W=p_S, \] where $p_S$ is the corresponding transcendental Chow projector on $S$. These are claimed identities in rational Chow motives, not only cohomology. The source identifies Shioda's level-four example as a K3 Kummer surface associated with $E\times E$; the aid says this supplies the requested correspondence in that known Kummer case. The level-four surface convention should not be silently identified with every newform-level convention.

For a general elliptic modular surface, the aid only reduces the task to finding a projected cycle \[ e_f\circ\zeta\circ p_T, \qquad \zeta\in\mathrm{CH}^2(A\times\mathcal E)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}, \] that is nonzero in the intended common-motive realization, where $e_f$ selects the modular-form summand. Its precise projector and nonvanishing criterion are not supplied. Merely being a nonzero Chow class would not by itself specify a nonzero cohomological action. The original C. Schoen comment relates this to extra cycles after supersingular reduction of $E$; it is retained as source context, not a construction of the arbitrary modular-surface correspondence.

Short English statement

Construct explicitly the shared CM weight-three correspondence between an elliptic self-product and an elliptic modular surface. The attached aid reports a rational CM projector and inverse Kummer correspondences for the known Kummer case, while leaving the general target to a projected-cycle construction.
