Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 3.2

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H^n$ have its standard metric of curvature $-1$. For a Kleinian group $H\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$, let $\Lambda H\subseteq\partial\mathbb H^n$ be its limit set and, when the convex-hull construction is defined, write \[ \operatorname{Core}(H)=\operatorname{Hull}(\Lambda H)/H, \qquad v_n(H)=\operatorname{Vol}_n(\operatorname{Core}(H)). \] Here the hull is taken inside $\mathbb H^n$, and the volume is the ambient $n$-dimensional hyperbolic volume, not an intrinsic lower-dimensional volume. Elementary groups and zero-volume cores require compatible conventions if included.

In dimension $2$, the motivating formula for a nontrivial finitely generated free Fuchsian group $A$ in the source's setting is \[ v_2(A)=2\pi\left(\operatorname{rk}(A)-1\right). \] Consequently its stated Hanna Neumann inequality is \[ 2\pi\,v_2(A\cap B)\leq v_2(A)v_2(B) \] for nontrivial free subgroups $A,B$ of a Fuchsian group, with zero core area for trivial intersections understood if necessary. The source's opening definition says "area$(G)$" but then uses $\mathbb H^2/A$; the notation $v_2(A)$ above consistently uses the group whose core is measured.

Swarup asks for analogous inequalities for torsion-free, not necessarily free, quasiconvex subgroups $A,B$ of a quasiconvex Kleinian group $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^n)$, replacing area by $v_n$. A proposed inequality has the form \[ c\,v_n(A\cap B)\leq v_n(A)v_n(B),\qquad c>0, \] possibly with a constant worse than $2\pi$. The source does not specify the permitted dependence of $c$. A universal dimension-dependent version would require $\forall n\geq3\ \exists c_n>0\ \forall\Gamma,A,B$ as above; a version allowing $c=c(\Gamma)$ is different. Neither quantifier choice is silently imposed here. Nor does the record define whether "quasiconvex Kleinian group" means a group with a quasiconvex orbit in $\mathbb H^n$ (the usual convex-cocompact setting), or another convention; admissibility and finiteness of the core volumes must be fixed accordingly.

The supplied research summary reports the free Fuchsian case as the Friedman--Mineyev theorem. It further claims a sharp-$2\pi$ extension to arbitrary torsion-free finitely generated Fuchsian subgroups by index/Euler-characteristic arguments in an omitted "Work done" section; that argument is not supplied here. It explicitly leaves the $n\geq3$ problem unresolved and says that its attempted $\ell^2$-Betti-number and rank-based reductions fail: the first available submultiplicativity result concerns graphs and does not measure core volume, and core volume is topologically unbounded in dimension $3$. These are limitations reported by the summary, not new theorems established in this draft.

Short English statement

The question seeks a higher-dimensional convex-core-volume analogue of the Hanna Neumann intersection inequality, allowing a weaker positive constant and nonfree torsion-free quasiconvex subgroups. The constant's allowed dependence and the precise Kleinian-group conventions are unspecified. The supplied summary leaves dimensions at least $3$ open and gives no accessible proof for its additional claimed Fuchsian extension.
