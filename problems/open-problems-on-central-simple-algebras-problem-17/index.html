Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 17

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a base field and let $K/F$ range over field extensions. A central simple $K$-algebra is a finite-dimensional associative unital $K$-algebra whose center is $K$ and which has no nonzero proper two-sided ideals. Its degree is $\deg(A)=\sqrt{\dim_K A}$. Write $\mathrm{CSA}_n(K)$ for the set of isomorphism classes of central simple $K$-algebras of degree $n$. This is naturally identified with $H^1(K,\operatorname{PGL}_n)$.

For a functor $\mathcal T$ from field extensions of $F$ to sets and an element $x\in\mathcal T(K)$, define \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(x)=\inf\left\{\operatorname{trdeg}_F K_0: F\subseteq K_0\subseteq K,\ x\in\operatorname{im}\left(\mathcal T(K_0)\longrightarrow\mathcal T(K)\right)\right\}, \qquad \operatorname{ed}_F(\mathcal T)=\sup_{K/F}\sup_{x\in\mathcal T(K)}\operatorname{ed}_F(x). \] For a prime $p$, essential $p$-dimension permits a finite extension of degree prime to $p$ before descent: \[ \operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(x)=\inf_{\substack{K'/K\text{ finite}\\p\nmid[K':K]}}\operatorname{ed}_F(x_{K'}), \qquad \operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(\mathcal T)=\sup_{K/F}\sup_{x\in\mathcal T(K)}\operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(x). \] For a finite group $G$, interpreted as a constant group scheme over $F$, write $\operatorname{ed}_F(G)=\operatorname{ed}_F(K\mapsto H^1(K,G))$.

Qualification concerning crossed products. The supplied record does not define $\operatorname{CPAlg}_n$, $\mathrm{Alg}_G$, or the hypotheses on $F$ and $G$. One base-change-stable interpretation is the following. A $G$-Galois $K$-algebra is a finite étale $K$-algebra $L$ of dimension $|G|$, equipped with a $G$-action such that the induced action on its geometric points is simply transitive. Under an unmarked convention, $\mathrm{Alg}_G(K)$ consists of isomorphism classes of central simple algebras of degree $|G|$ containing such an $L$ as a maximal étale subalgebra; the choice of $L$, its embedding, and its action are not retained. Then $\operatorname{CPAlg}_n(K)$ consists of the classes belonging to $\mathrm{Alg}_G(K)$ for some finite group $G$ of order $n$.

These conventions are explicit provisional interpretations, not definitions verified from the cited source. A definition retaining the embedded Galois algebra or its group action gives a different moduli problem. Requiring a Galois field extension instead of a Galois étale algebra also changes the formulation and requires care under scalar extension.

Problem, conditional on confirming the intended conventions and hypotheses. For positive integers $n$ and finite groups $G$, determine bounds for \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{CPAlg}_n) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{ed}_F(\mathrm{Alg}_G), \] and determine \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(\mathrm{Alg}_G)-\operatorname{ed}_F(G) \] whenever this difference is defined. Determine whether the implication \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{CPAlg}_n) =\operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{PGL}_n) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(\forall K/F\right)\left(\forall[A]\in\mathrm{CSA}_n(K)\right)\, [A]\in\operatorname{CPAlg}_n(K) \] holds. Thus the conclusion concerns every degree-$n$ central simple algebra over every extension of the same base field $F$.

The introductory reference to essential $p$-dimension supplies context but specifies neither a prime nor a precise additional $p$-dimension question. No such additional assertion is inferred here.

Short English statement

The problem asks for essential-dimension bounds for crossed-product algebras, their relation to the essential dimension of a finite group, and whether equality with the essential dimension of $\operatorname{PGL}_n$ forces every degree-$n$ algebra over every extension of $F$ to be a crossed product. The precise crossed-product conventions and base-field hypotheses require confirmation; the displayed formulation is conditional on them.
