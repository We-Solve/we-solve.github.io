Math-155

Schinzel's Hypothesis H

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra progpt 6 astra ultra Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f_1,\ldots,f_r\in{\mathbb{Z}}[x]$ be nonconstant irreducible polynomials with positive leading coefficients. The local admissibility condition is \[ \forall p\text{ prime}\ \exists n\in{\mathbb{Z}}:\quad p\nmid\prod_{i=1}^r f_i(n). \] The proposed conclusion is that $\{n\ge1:f_i(n)\text{ is prime for every }i\}$ is infinite. Irreducibility is over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ for primitive integer polynomials; local admissibility excludes fixed prime divisors. No asymptotic frequency is included in this qualitative assertion.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

If irreducible integer polynomials have no fixed local obstruction, must they simultaneously take prime values infinitely often?
