Spectral Bifurcations and Entropy Changes

Definitions and mathematical statement

Formulation and unresolved qualifications. The supplied record asks whether an observed correspondence between bifurcations of the Perron--Frobenius eigenspectrum and changes in topological entropy extends beyond the studied lid-driven-cavity flows. It does not specify the family of systems, the operator space, the spectral events, or the meaning of the correspondence. Consequently, it does not determine a precise universally quantified conjecture.

For clarity, a possible framework for defining the terms is the following; these are conditional definitions, not additional hypotheses supplied by the source. Let $X$ be a compact metric space with metric $d$, let $m$ be a Borel probability measure on $X$, and let $(T_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ be a family of continuous maps $X\to X$, indexed by a parameter space $\Lambda$. Suppose each map is nonsingular with respect to $m$, meaning that $m(T_\lambda^{-1}A)=0$ whenever $m(A)=0$. Its Perron--Frobenius operator $\mathcal P_\lambda$ on $L^1(m)$ is characterized by \[ \int_X (\mathcal P_\lambda f)g\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m =\int_X f(g\circ T_\lambda)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m, \qquad f\in L^1(m),\quad g\in L^\infty(m). \] Any use of another function space, an operator approximation, or a time map of a flow must be specified separately. In particular, the relevant eigenvalues and their bifurcations cannot be identified without this choice and a definition of a bifurcation event.

In this conditional compact continuous setting, define \[ d_{\lambda,n}(x,y)=\max_{0\leq j<n}d(T_\lambda^j x,T_\lambda^j y). \] Let $s_\lambda(n,\varepsilon)$ be the largest cardinality of a set whose distinct points have $d_{\lambda,n}$-distance greater than $\varepsilon$. The topological entropy is \[ h_{\mathrm{top}}(T_\lambda) =\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\limsup_{n\to\infty} \frac{1}{n}\log s_\lambda(n,\varepsilon). \] The supplied record does not establish that this framework is the intended setting. For a flow, the time map and time normalization would also need to be fixed.

Source question. Under what hypotheses, and for what classes of dynamical systems beyond the studied lid-driven-cavity flows, does the reported correspondence between Perron--Frobenius spectral bifurcations and changes in topological entropy persist? A precise assertion requires specifying whether the correspondence is an implication, an equivalence, a quantitative bound, or another relation, and whether an entropy change means a change in value, a transition from zero to positive entropy, a discontinuity, or another event. The original wording refers only to "system entropy"; the supplied cleaned statement specifies topological entropy. No particular interpretation of these unresolved features is asserted here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the reported connection between Perron--Frobenius spectral bifurcations and topological entropy changes extends beyond the studied lid-driven-cavity flows. The source record leaves the class of systems and the exact connection unspecified, so this remains a qualified research question requiring human mathematical review.
