Math-4162

Existence of a four-dimensional Euler brick

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

A four-dimensional rectangular box has positive edge lengths $a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4$. The diagonal in the coordinate face determined by edges $i,j$ has length $\sqrt{a_i^2+a_j^2}$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Do there exist $a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4\in\mathbb N$ and $d_{ij}\in\mathbb N$ for $1\le i<j\le4$ such that \[ a_i^2+a_j^2=d_{ij}^2\qquad(1\le i<j\le4)? \] Construct such a box or prove its nonexistence. [S2]

Short English statement

Find four positive integer edge lengths such that each of the six face diagonals also has integer length, or prove that no such choice exists.
