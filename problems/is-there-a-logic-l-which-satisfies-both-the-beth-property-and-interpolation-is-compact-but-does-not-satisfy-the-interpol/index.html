Math-13100

Is there a logic L which satisfies both the Beth property and Δ-interpolation, is compact but does not satisfy the interpolation property

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $L$ be an abstract model-theoretic logic with sentences over vocabularies and an isomorphism-invariant satisfaction relation. The source does not fix its closure properties, whether it extends first-order logic, or the allowed vocabulary sizes.

Under full compactness, any set $T$ of $L$-sentences has a model whenever every finite subset has a model. This is distinct from a restriction to countable $T$ or another cardinal-specific compactness property.

A standard theory-level Beth property says: whenever an $L$-theory in vocabulary $\tau\cup\{R\}$ implicitly defines a relation $R$ over $\tau$ (two models with the same $\tau$-reduct must agree on $R$), there is an $L$-formula over $\tau$ explicitly defining $R$ in all models of the theory. The source does not specify theory-versus-sentence or relation-versus-function variants.

Under the Craig-interpolation interpretation identified by the research summary, interpolation requires that whenever $\alpha\in L(\tau_1)$ and $\beta\in L(\tau_2)$ satisfy $\alpha\models\beta$, there be $\gamma\in L(\tau_1\cap\tau_2)$ with \[ \alpha\models\gamma\models\beta. \] Failure means there are such $\alpha,\beta$ with no interpolant in the common vocabulary.

The existence question is whether some $L$ simultaneously has compactness, the Beth property, and the source's $\Delta$-interpolation property, while failing this interpolation property. The specialized $\Delta$-interpolation condition is not defined in the record. It cannot be silently replaced by ordinary Craig interpolation or a guessed definability closure. Its exact definition, the logic framework, and the preceding property variants must be fixed to obtain a determinate existence problem.

Short English statement

Can a compact abstract logic have both Beth definability and $\Delta$-interpolation while failing Craig interpolation? The requested combination is preserved, but the specialized $\Delta$ property and abstract-logic conventions are missing.

The supplied summary places the question in the Makowsky--Shelah abstract-model-theory program, mentions fixed-point-logical results satisfying subsets of the properties, and reports no verified construction or impossibility theorem for this exact combination. This is an OPEN-TRIAGE assessment. The pinned Wikipedia primary-source audit remains required; unpublished background conflicts with published registry metadata.
