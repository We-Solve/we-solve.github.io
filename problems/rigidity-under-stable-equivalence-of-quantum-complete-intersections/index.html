Math-4710

Rigidity Under Stable Equivalence of Quantum Complete Intersections

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field. All algebras and algebra isomorphisms below are over $k$, and algebras are understood to be associative with identity. Under the usual presentation convention, a quantum complete intersection is an algebra \[ X\cong k\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle\Big/\left(x_i^{a_i}\ (1\le i\le n),\ x_ix_j-q_{ij}x_jx_i\ (1\le i<j\le n)\right), \] where $n\ge1$, the integers $a_i\ge2$, and $q_{ij}\in k^{\times}$. Its dimension is $\prod_{i=1}^n a_i$. The supplied record does not specify a presentation convention or whether the zero-generator case $X=k$ is included; the intended convention requires review.

For a $k$-algebra $A$, its center is \[ Z(A)=\{z\in A:za=az\text{ for every }a\in A\}. \] A finite-dimensional algebra $A$ is local if $A/J(A)$ is a division algebra, where $J(A)$ denotes its Jacobson radical. In particular, this definition does not require $A/J(A)\cong k$. It is symmetric if $A\cong\operatorname{Hom}_k(A,k)$ as $A$-$A$-bimodules, with the usual dual bimodule structure.

Here a stable equivalence of Morita type between $X$ and $Y$ means that there exist finite-dimensional bimodules ${}_X M_Y$ and ${}_Y N_X$, each projective as a left module and as a right module, such that \[ M\otimes_Y N\cong X\oplus P, \qquad N\otimes_X M\cong Y\oplus Q, \] as $X$-$X$-bimodules and $Y$-$Y$-bimodules, respectively, where $P$ is projective over $X\otimes_kX^{\mathrm{op}}$ and $Q$ is projective over $Y\otimes_kY^{\mathrm{op}}$.

Question. For every field $k$, every quantum complete intersection $X$ over $k$, and every finite-dimensional symmetric local $k$-algebra $Y$, suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are stably equivalent of Morita type and that \[ \dim_kY=\dim_kX, \qquad Z(X)\cong Z(Y)\quad\text{as }k\text{-algebras}. \] Must $X\cong Y$ as $k$-algebras?

No restriction on the characteristic or algebraic closure of $k$, and no additional symmetry hypothesis on $X$, is stated in the supplied formulation. The supplied dated curation reports a result of Kessar for a nine-dimensional case, but gives insufficient hypotheses to formulate that result precisely here; it is not used to restrict the question.

Short English statement

Must a quantum complete intersection be isomorphic to every finite-dimensional symmetric local algebra of the same dimension and with an isomorphic center to which it is stably equivalent of Morita type? The field is unrestricted in the supplied statement. The reported partial solution and the precise scope of its nine-dimensional case require independent review.
