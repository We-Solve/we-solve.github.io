Sharp Shape-Dependent Bounds for Dirichlet Eigenvalues

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ be a nonempty bounded open set. In the standard star-shaped interpretation of "starlike", there exists $x_0\in\Omega$ such that \[ (1-t)x_0+tx\in\Omega\qquad\text{for every }x\in\Omega\text{ and }t\in[0,1]. \] The supplied record does not specify additional boundary regularity or a stronger meaning of starlikeness.

Let $H_0^1(\Omega)$ be the closure of $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ in the Sobolev space $H^1(\Omega)$. Define the Dirichlet Laplacian through the quadratic form \[ q_\Omega[u]=\int_\Omega |\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x, \qquad u\in H_0^1(\Omega). \] Write its eigenvalues, in nondecreasing order and repeated according to multiplicity, as \[ 0<\lambda_1(\Omega)\leq\lambda_2(\Omega)\leq\cdots. \] Equivalently, an eigenvalue $\lambda$ admits a nonzero $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying \[ \int_\Omega\nabla u\cdot\overline{\nabla v}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x =\lambda\int_\Omega u\overline v\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x \qquad\text{for every }v\in H_0^1(\Omega). \] This variational definition specifies the Dirichlet boundary condition without assuming a smooth boundary.

Source question. How does the shape of a bounded planar starlike domain $\Omega$ affect its Dirichlet eigenvalues, and what geometrically sharp upper bounds depending on its shape hold?

Short English statement

The question asks how the geometry of a bounded planar starlike domain controls the eigenvalues of its Dirichlet Laplacian, especially through sharp upper bounds. The supplied fragment leaves the geometric data, spectral target, domain constraints, and sharpness criterion unspecified, so it does not determine a single precise conjecture.
