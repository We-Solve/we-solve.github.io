Holomorphic Symplectic Geometry — Conjecture 6

Definitions and mathematical statement

The excerpt does not specify the ambient hypotheses on $X$. The following is a conditional transcription using the customary interpretation of a hyperkähler manifold as a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold: a simply connected compact Kähler manifold $X$ for which $H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)={\mathbb{C}}\sigma$, where $\sigma$ is everywhere nondegenerate. This convention, and whether projectivity is additionally required, must be checked against the complete source context. Write $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X=2r$, with $r\geq 1$.

Under this interpretation, let $q_X$ denote the Beauville--Bogomolov quadratic form on $H^2(X,{\mathbb{R}})$, and write $q(L)=q_X(c_1(L))$ for a holomorphic line bundle $L$. A line bundle is nontrivial if it is not isomorphic to $\mathcal O_X$, and is nef if its first Chern class lies in the closure of the Kähler cone. A Lagrangian fibration $f:X\to{\mathbb{P}}^r$ means a surjective holomorphic map with connected fibers whose general smooth fiber has complex dimension $r$ and on which $\sigma$ restricts to zero. Here ${\mathbb{P}}^r$ is complex projective space and $\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^r}(k)$ is its degree-$k$ twisting line bundle.

  1. $\mathrm{a)}$ For every nontrivial nef holomorphic line bundle $L$ on $X$ satisfying $q(L)=0$, there exist a Lagrangian fibration $f:X\to{\mathbb{P}}^r$ and an integer $k\geq 1$ such that \[ L\cong f^*\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^r}(k). \]
  2. $\mathrm{b)}$ There exist a hyperkähler manifold $X'$ bimeromorphic to $X$ and a Lagrangian fibration $X'\to{\mathbb{P}}^r$ if and only if there exists a holomorphic line bundle $L$ on $X$ such that \[ L\not\cong\mathcal O_X\qquad\text{and}\qquad q(L)=0. \] No nefness or effectivity assumption on $L$ is stated in this part.

There is an unresolved notation issue in part $\mathrm{b)}$: the supplied text writes $L\neq\mathcal O_S$, but does not define $S$. The displayed formulation uses $L\not\cong\mathcal O_X$ only as a provisional interpretation of that expression. Together with the missing ambient hypotheses, this requires source verification before the transcription can be regarded as definitive.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly provisional ambient conventions, Beauville's conjecture asserts that every nontrivial nef line bundle of square zero comes from a Lagrangian fibration over projective space. It also asserts that such a fibration exists on a bimeromorphic hyperkähler model exactly when $X$ has a nontrivial line bundle of square zero. The hypotheses on $X$ and the undefined $\mathcal O_S$ in part $\mathrm{b)}$ require verification.
