Math-4154

Three-Event Correlation Inequalities in Product Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation, let $n\geq 1$, let $\Omega_1,\ldots,\Omega_n$ be nonempty finite sets, and let $\mu_i$ be a probability measure on $\Omega_i$. Write \[ \Omega=\prod_{i=1}^n\Omega_i, \qquad \mathbb P=\bigotimes_{i=1}^n\mu_i. \] To interpret increasing events, coordinate orders must also be specified. Given partial orders $\leq_i$ on the factors, use the coordinatewise order \[ x\leq y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x_i\leq_i y_i\text{ for every }i. \] An event $D\subseteq\Omega$ is increasing, or an up-set, if $x\in D$ and $x\leq y$ imply $y\in D$.

For increasing events $A,B,C\subseteq\Omega$, define their seven nonempty intersection probabilities by \[ \begin{aligned} a&=\mathbb P(A),& b&=\mathbb P(B),& c&=\mathbb P(C),\\ ab_*&=\mathbb P(A\cap B),& ac_*&=\mathbb P(A\cap C),& bc_*&=\mathbb P(B\cap C),\\ t&=\mathbb P(A\cap B\cap C). \end{aligned} \] Here $ab_*$, $ac_*$, and $bc_*$ denote intersection probabilities, not products of marginal probabilities. Equivalently, to avoid this notation, define the realizable set for a specified ordered product space by \[ \mathcal R(\Omega,\mathbb P)= \left\{ \left(\mathbb P(A),\mathbb P(B),\mathbb P(C), \mathbb P(A\cap B),\mathbb P(A\cap C),\mathbb P(B\cap C), \mathbb P(A\cap B\cap C)\right): A,B,C\subseteq\Omega\text{ are increasing} \right\}. \] Problem. Characterize the realizable joint probabilities of three increasing events in a finite product probability space. In particular, determine whether, for every such space and every three increasing events $A,B,C$, \[ \begin{aligned} &2\mathbb P(A\cap B\cap C) -\mathbb P(A\cap B)\mathbb P(C) -\mathbb P(A\cap C)\mathbb P(B)\\ &\qquad -\mathbb P(B\cap C)\mathbb P(A) +\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(B)\mathbb P(C) \geq 0. \end{aligned} \] Unresolved scope. The supplied record does not specify the coordinate orders or the permitted class of ordered factors. The order-relative definitions above explain the terminology; they do not establish that arbitrary partially ordered factors are intended. It is also unspecified whether the requested characterization concerns $\mathcal R(\Omega,\mathbb P)$ for each fixed space or the union of these sets as the permitted spaces vary. The universal quantifier in the inequality must therefore be read relative to the intended, presently unspecified class of ordered product spaces. The record imposes no strict-positivity condition on the factor probabilities and no exclusion of empty or full events.

Short English statement

Describe exactly which seven intersection probabilities can arise from three increasing events in a finite product probability space, and determine whether the displayed three-event inequality always holds. The permitted coordinate orders and the scope of the realizability characterization require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
