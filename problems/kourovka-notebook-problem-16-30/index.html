Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.30

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be an arbitrary group and let $A,B\leq G$. Define $AB=\{ab:a\in A,\ b\in B\}$; the hypothesis is $G=AB$. Neither finiteness of $G$ nor normality of $A$ or $B$ is assumed.

For a group $H$, a finite composition series means a chain \[ 1=H_0\triangleleft H_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft H_n=H \] with strict inclusions and nontrivial simple factors $H_i/H_{i-1}$ for $1\leq i\leq n$. A nontrivial group is simple if its only normal subgroups are the identity subgroup and itself. Here $n$ is a nonnegative integer, and $n=0$ is permitted precisely when $H=1$.

For this draft, interpret a principal series as a finite chief series: a finite chain \[ 1=H_0<H_1<\cdots<H_m=H \] in which every $H_i$ is normal in $H$, and for each $1\leq i\leq m$ there is no normal subgroup $N\trianglelefteq H$ satisfying $H_{i-1}<N<H_i$. Equivalently, $H_i/H_{i-1}$ is a minimal nontrivial normal subgroup of $H/H_{i-1}$. The empty chain is permitted for the trivial group. The supplied source does not define "principal series" or explicitly specify that the series are finite; these conventions require confirmation against the original source.

Questions (V. A. Vedernikov, Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.30). Under these conventions, are the following two universal implications valid?

  1. For every group $G$ and subgroups $A,B\leq G$ with $G=AB$, if each of $A$ and $B$ admits a finite composition series, then $G$ admits a finite composition series.
  2. For every group $G$ and subgroups $A,B\leq G$ with $G=AB$, if each of $A$ and $B$ admits a finite principal series, then $G$ admits a finite principal series.
In the second question, the principal series of $A$, $B$, and $G$ are taken relative to their respective ambient groups.

The supplied registry labels the problem open. Its discussion also reports an explicitly unverified AI claim that both implications fail. That report is a source qualification, not an established answer in this statement-only draft.

Short English statement

If a group is the setwise product of two subgroups, must it have a composition series when both subgroups do? Ask the same question for principal series, interpreted here as finite chief series. The source does not explicitly define its series conventions. It records an open status alongside an unverified claim of negative answers to both questions.
