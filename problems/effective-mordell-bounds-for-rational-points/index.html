Math-177

Effective Mordell Bounds for Rational Points

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra ultra Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A logarithmic height on a projective curve can be fixed by an explicit embedding into projective space. For an explicitly given smooth projective curve $X/K$ of genus at least two, seek an effectively computable constant $B_X$ with \[ h(P)\le B_X\qquad(P\in X(K)), \] or a terminating algorithm returning the entire finite set $X(K)$. Inputs include effective equations and number-field data. A finiteness proof without computable stopping information is not an effective solution; nor is a search that keeps finding points without certifying that none remain.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Can all rational points on every number-field curve of genus at least two be computed with a guaranteed stopping certificate?
