Math-5989

Visibility Graphs of Points, Segments and Polygons — Problem 40

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 3$, let a simple polygon with $n$ vertices mean a closed polygonal region $P\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ whose boundary is a simple closed polygonal curve with distinct designated vertices $p_1,\ldots,p_n$ in cyclic order. Adopt the closed-region visibility convention: two distinct vertices $p_i,p_j$ are visible precisely when their closed line segment satisfies $[p_i,p_j]\subseteq P$. The vertex visibility graph $G(P)$ is the finite simple undirected graph with vertex set $\{p_1,\ldots,p_n\}$ and an edge for each visible pair.

Two finite simple graphs $G_1=(V_1,E_1)$ and $G_2=(V_2,E_2)$ are isomorphic if there is a bijection $f:V_1\to V_2$ such that, for all distinct $v,w\in V_1$, \[ \{v,w\}\in E_1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\{f(v),f(w)\}\in E_2. \] Let \[ N(n)=\left|\left\{[G(P)]_{\cong}:P\text{ is a simple polygon with }n\text{ vertices}\right\}\right|. \] Thus graphs are counted up to abstract graph isomorphism; neither geometric coordinates nor a chosen cyclic boundary order are retained as additional graph data.

The supplied source reports the lower bound $N(n)\geq 2^{n-4}$, attributed to Ghosh [g-vap-07], and the upper bound $N(n)\leq 2^{O(n\log n)}$, attributed to an application of Warren's theorem [w-lbanm-68], with reference [s-grep-03]. The upper-bound notation means that there exist constants $C>0$ and $n_0\geq 3$ such that \[ N(n)\leq 2^{C n\log_2 n}\qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq n_0. \] The extract supplies no explicit range of validity for the reported lower bound.

Problem (Ghosh--Goswami survey, Problem 40). Improve the reported lower and upper bounds on $N(n)$ as a function of $n$. No specific target asymptotic rate or required size of improvement is supplied.

Unresolved conventions. The extract does not define polygon visibility or state whether consecutive collinear vertices or other geometric degeneracies are permitted. The closed-region visibility convention above is an explicit working interpretation requiring confirmation against the source. Likewise, whether a straight boundary point may count as a designated polygon vertex remains unspecified. These conventions must be settled before this draft determines a unique counting function.

Short English statement

Count, up to graph isomorphism, the vertex visibility graphs realizable by simple polygons with $n$ vertices. Improve the supplied lower bound $2^{n-4}$ and upper bound $2^{O(n\log n)}$. The extract leaves visibility and degeneracy conventions unspecified; the definitions above therefore require source confirmation. The registry labels the problem open, but that status is supplied metadata rather than an independently verified assessment.
