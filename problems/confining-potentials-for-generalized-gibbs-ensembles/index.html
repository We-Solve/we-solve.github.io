Math-13947

Confining Potentials for Generalized Gibbs Ensembles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N$ denote the particle number and $\ell>0$ the confining box length. The source considers the thermodynamic scaling \[ N\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad \ell=\nu N,\qquad \nu>0, \] so that the macroscopic particle density is $\nu^{-1}$.

The supplied excerpt describes conserved quantities using a matrix $L$ and the formal expression \[ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mu_n\operatorname{tr}(L^n) =\operatorname{tr}V(L). \] Here $V$ is the potential entering the generalized Gibbs ensemble (GGE), and $\mu_n$ are its formal coefficients. The excerpt does not define $L$, its spectral domain, or the admissible coefficients, and gives no convergence or functional-calculus hypotheses for this expression. The term indexed by $n=0$ is retained: although the constant function gives a trivial conservation law in the Hamiltonian description, the source emphasizes its relevance to particle density in hydrodynamics.

A separate box potential $V_{\mathrm{box},\ell}$ is introduced to keep particle positions essentially within a box of length $\ell$. The source notes that this auxiliary confinement generally breaks integrability and is used as a mathematical device for constructing the infinite-volume GGE. It does not specify the box potential or its boundary conditions.

Write $Z_{\mathrm{ca},N}(\ell,V)$ for the finite-volume partition function named in the source. The displayed thermodynamic quantity is the generalized free energy per unit length, \[ F_{\mathrm{ca}}(\nu,V) =\lim_{N\to\infty} \left[-\frac{1}{\nu N}\log Z_{\mathrm{ca},N}(\nu N,V)\right]. \] This formula defines $F_{\mathrm{ca}}$ only when the partition functions and the indicated limit are well defined; their existence is not asserted here.

Source question, with unresolved qualifications. Characterize precisely the confining potentials $V$ for which the intended Calogero-type GGE has the desired infinite-volume behavior under this scaling. The excerpt identifies construction of an infinite-volume GGE, spacetime stationarity, and the displayed free energy as relevant objectives. It does not specify which of these properties constitutes the characterization, whether the free energy must be finite, or whether uniqueness or independence of the auxiliary confinement is required. The underlying Hamiltonian, matrix $L$, phase space, reference measure, finite-volume Gibbs weight, dynamics, and mode of convergence are also absent. Consequently, the supplied record supports this qualified formulation but does not determine a fully specified necessary-and-sufficient mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The problem asks which confining potentials permit the intended Calogero-type generalized Gibbs ensemble to have a suitable infinite-volume limit at fixed particle density. The source also discusses stationarity and free energy per unit length. Its missing model definitions and unspecified limiting properties prevent a complete precise characterization from being stated.
