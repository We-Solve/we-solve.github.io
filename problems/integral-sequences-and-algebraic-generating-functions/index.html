Integral Sequences and Algebraic Generating Functions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Characterization problem. Characterize the integer sequences $(u_n)_{n\ge0}$ for which the ordinary generating function $U(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}u_nz^n$ is algebraic. Here $u_n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, and under the natural characteristic-zero formal interpretation, algebraic means algebraic over ${\mathbb{Q}}(z)$: there is a nonzero polynomial $P(z,y)\in{\mathbb{Q}}[z,y]$, equivalently one in ${\mathbb{Z}}[z,y]$ after clearing denominators, with\[P(z,U(z))=0\quad\text{in }{\mathbb{Q}}[[z]].\] The variable $z$ is formal; no convergence-radius assumption, positivity, recurrence, or special formula for the coefficients is supplied. This is an ordinary, not exponential, generating function.

The original statement asks to characterize the relation between integrality of all $u_n$ and algebraicity of a generating function $u$. The curated statement fixes integer coefficients first and asks which such series are algebraic. The record does not give an underlying family of sequences, a desired arithmetic or structural criterion, effective input data, or a required algorithm. The polynomial relation above defines algebraicity but by itself is not an additional characterization of integer sequences. No restriction to a guessed family of special-function, factorial-ratio, or recurrence sequences is inserted. The intended generality and meaning of a satisfactory characterization remain formulation questions.

Short English statement

Describe which integer-coefficient ordinary generating series satisfy a polynomial equation over ${\mathbb{Q}}(z)$.
