Newton Strata Meeting Weakly Admissible Loci

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathcal F=\mathcal F(G,b,\mu)$ for the period-domain flag space appearing in the supplied record, and let $\mathcal F^{\mathrm{wa}}\subseteq\mathcal F$ denote its weakly admissible locus. For each $[b']\in B(G)$, write $\mathcal F^{[b']}=\mathcal F(G,b,\mu)^{[b']}$ for the Newton stratum with that index.

In the usual local-field interpretation, if $F$ is the underlying nonarchimedean local field, $\breve F$ is the completion of its maximal unramified extension, and $\sigma$ is its Frobenius automorphism, then \[ B(G)=G(\breve F)/{\sim_\sigma},\qquad b_1\sim_\sigma b_2\ \Longleftrightarrow\ b_2=g^{-1}b_1\sigma(g)\text{ for some }g\in G(\breve F). \] This explains the customary notation conditionally; the supplied record does not specify the underlying field or the hypotheses on $G$, $b$, and $\mu$.

Classification problem. For fixed period-domain data $(G,b,\mu)$ in the intended source setting, determine the subset \[ \mathcal S(G,b,\mu) :=\left\{[b']\in B(G): \mathcal F(G,b,\mu)^{[b']}\cap \mathcal F(G,b,\mu)^{\mathrm{wa}} \neq\varnothing\right\}. \] Thus the requested characterization must give necessary and sufficient conditions on $[b']$ for this intersection to be nonempty.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The record does not provide the precise class of permitted period-domain data, the construction and indexing convention for its Newton strata, the definition of weak admissibility in this setting, or the field or geometric category in which nonemptiness is intended. These must be recovered from the source before a fully specified mathematical formulation can be accepted; no restriction to basic or Hodge--Newton-decomposable cases is imposed here.

Short English statement

For the intended fixed data $(G,b,\mu)$, characterize exactly which Newton strata contain a weakly admissible point. The supplied record omits hypotheses and conventions needed to make the period-domain setting fully precise. Its dated curation labels the problem partially solved and describes results in important basic and Hodge--Newton-decomposable cases; these claims require independent review.
