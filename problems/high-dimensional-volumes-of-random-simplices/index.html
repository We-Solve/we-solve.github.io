High-Dimensional Volumes of Random Simplices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for high-dimensional asymptotics of expected random-simplex volumes. Its sampling law, dimension convention, body scaling, and normalization are not specified, so it does not determine a unique sequence of expectations.

A parameterized formulation makes these missing data explicit. For each positive integer $d$, let $m_d\geq 1$ be an ambient dimension, let $K_d\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{m_d}$ be a compact convex set with nonempty interior, and let $\mu_d$ be a Borel probability measure on $K_d^{m_d+1}$. Let $(X_{d,0},\ldots,X_{d,m_d})$ have joint law $\mu_d$, and define \[ S_d=\operatorname{conv}\{X_{d,0},\ldots,X_{d,m_d}\},\qquad V_d=\operatorname{vol}_{m_d}(S_d), \] where $\operatorname{vol}_{m_d}$ is $m_d$-dimensional Lebesgue measure; this volume is zero when the vertices are affinely dependent. The expected volume is \[ A_d(K_d,\mu_d)={\mathbb{E}}[V_d] =\int_{K_d^{m_d+1}} \operatorname{vol}_{m_d}\!\left(\operatorname{conv}\{x_0,\ldots,x_{m_d}\}\right) \,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_d(x_0,\ldots,x_{m_d}). \] This framework does not assume that the vertices are independent or uniformly distributed: neither assumption is supplied by the record.

The requested task is to determine the asymptotic behavior of these expected volumes as $d\to\infty$ for the intended sequences of regular simplices, cubes, and other standard convex bodies, together with the intended normalized asymptotic quantities. The supplied literature assessment also explicitly mentions cross-polytopes. This is a formulation template requiring specification of $m_d$, $K_d$, $\mu_d$, and the normalization before a precise asymptotic target can be stated.

In particular, the source calls the random object a "$(d+1)$-simplex." In the usual dimensional terminology this has dimension $d+1$ and has $d+2$ vertices. A convex hull of $d+1$ points is instead at most $d$-dimensional. The record does not specify the ambient dimension or resolve whether its wording refers to dimension or to the number of sampled vertices. No identification of $m_d$ with $d$ or $d+1$ is therefore imposed. The scale of each containing body and the meaning of "normalized quantities" also remain unresolved.

Short English statement

Determine the high-dimensional asymptotics of the expected volume of a random simplex in a regular simplex, a cube, and other intended standard convex bodies, including the requested normalized quantities. The record leaves the dimension convention, vertex distribution, body scaling, and normalization unspecified; these must be resolved for a unique mathematical problem. Its supplied status is partially solved, which is a dated curation label rather than an independently verified conclusion.
