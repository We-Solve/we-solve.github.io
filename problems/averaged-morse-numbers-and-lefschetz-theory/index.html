Averaged Morse Numbers and Lefschetz Theory

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks three questions concerning Bagchi's lower-bound conjecture for the $g$-vector, the $g$-conjecture, and invariants denoted by $\mu_i$, described as averaged discrete Morse invariants. It does not supply the definitions of these invariants, their normalization or coefficient field, the admissible class of spaces or simplicial complexes, or the precise versions of the two conjectures. These omissions remain unresolved; consequently, the questions below cannot yet be expressed as a fully specified universally quantified conjecture.

For a finitely generated group $G$, define its minimum number of generators by \[ d(G)=\min\left\{|S|: S\subseteq G,\ S\text{ is finite},\ \langle S\rangle=G\right\}. \] For a path-connected space $X$ with basepoint $x_0$ and finitely generated fundamental group, $d(\pi_1(X,x_0))$ is independent of the choice of basepoint.

  1. Determine the relationship between Bagchi's lower-bound conjecture for the $g$-vector and the $g$-conjecture. The source does not specify the conjectural inequalities, hypotheses, or intended meaning of "relationship," so no particular implication or equivalence is asserted here.
  2. Determine the relationship between the invariants $\mu_i$ and discrete Morse theory. The intended indices, admissible discrete Morse functions, and type of comparison are not specified in the supplied record.
  3. Determine whether $\mu_1$ is a lower bound for the minimum number of generators of the fundamental group. For an admissible path-connected object $X$ with finitely generated fundamental group, the proposed lower-bound direction is \[ \mu_1(X)\leq d\left(\pi_1(X,x_0)\right). \] If $\mathcal C$ denotes the intended class of such objects, this asks whether the displayed inequality holds for every $X\in\mathcal C$. Both $\mathcal C$ and the definition of $\mu_1$ remain unspecified. This conditional transcription does not claim that the inequality is intended for all path-connected spaces or all finite simplicial complexes. The treatment of disconnected objects, if any, also remains unspecified.

Short English statement

The problem asks how Bagchi's lower-bound conjecture relates to the $g$-conjecture, how the averaged invariants $\mu_i$ relate to discrete Morse theory, and whether $\mu_1$ is at most the minimum number of generators of the fundamental group. The supplied record leaves the invariant definitions, conjecture versions, and domain unresolved. Its open status is a supplied dated assessment, not an independently verified conclusion.
