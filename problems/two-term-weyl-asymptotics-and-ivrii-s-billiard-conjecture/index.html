Two-Term Weyl Asymptotics and Ivrii's Billiard Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses two conjectural questions for every smooth planar domain $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$. Here a domain means a nonempty connected open set. Write $|\Omega|$ for its area and $\ell(\partial\Omega)$ for its boundary length. The record does not specify boundedness, the precise boundary regularity meant by smoothness, the spectral operator, its boundary conditions, or the coefficient $C$. Consequently, the spectral question below remains a formulation with unresolved parameters.

For a specified nonnegative self-adjoint spectral problem on $\Omega$ with discrete eigenvalues $\lambda_j$, listed with multiplicity, define \[ N(\lambda)=\#\{j:\lambda_j\leq\lambda\}. \] This definition requires a spectral realization for which the count is finite for each finite $\lambda$; such a realization is not supplied. In particular, the record does not identify whether the intended problem is the Dirichlet or Neumann Laplacian.

  1. For every smooth planar domain $\Omega$, is the intended eigenvalue counting function asymptotic to \[ N(\lambda)=\frac{|\Omega|}{4\pi}\lambda +C\,\ell(\partial\Omega)\lambda^{1/2} +o(\lambda^{1/2}) \qquad(\lambda\to+\infty)? \] For each fixed admissible domain and spectral realization, the remainder notation means that the difference between $N(\lambda)$ and the two displayed terms, divided by $\lambda^{1/2}$, tends to zero. The source leaves the value and permitted dependence of $C$ unspecified. The expression also requires finite geometric coefficients and an appropriate discrete spectrum, neither of which follows from the explicitly stated smoothness assumption alone.

  2. For every smooth planar billiard domain $\Omega$, does the set of periodic billiard trajectories have measure zero?

    To describe a conventional precise interpretation, let \[ S^1=\{v\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:|v|=1\},\qquad X_\Omega=\Omega\times S^1. \] A regular unit-speed billiard trajectory moves along straight lines in the interior and reflects at a transverse boundary collision according to \[ v_{\mathrm{out}}=v_{\mathrm{in}}-2\langle v_{\mathrm{in}},n(x)\rangle n(x), \] where $n(x)$ is the outward unit normal. On initial states for which the billiard flow $\Phi^t$ is defined through the required collisions, set \[ P_\Omega=\{z\in X_\Omega:\text{there exists }T>0\text{ with }\Phi^T(z)=z\}. \] With the phase-space measure $\mathrm d\mu_\Omega=\mathrm dx_1\,\mathrm dx_2\,\mathrm d\theta$, where $v=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$, the conventional question is \[ \mu_\Omega(P_\Omega)=0. \] The source does not specify this phase space or measure, or its convention for grazing and other singular trajectories. Thus this is an explicitly conditional interpretation of its measure-zero question, requiring confirmation against the report.

The displayed source statements quantify over all smooth planar domains and do not impose convexity. The supplied literature assessment instead singles out the conjecture for every smooth convex table as remaining open; this narrower scope does not replace the quantifiers in the statements. The two questions are presented separately, without asserting their equivalence.

Short English statement

The record asks whether every smooth planar domain has the displayed two-term eigenvalue asymptotic and whether its periodic billiard trajectories have measure zero. The spectral operator, boundary conditions, coefficient $C$, admissible domain class, and trajectory measure require clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
