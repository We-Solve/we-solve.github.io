Kourovka Notebook Problem 10.50

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group, let $\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(G)$ denote its irreducible complex characters, and let $1_G$ denote its principal character, so that $1_G(g)=1$ for every $g\in G$. A linear character of a cyclic subgroup $C\leq G$ is a homomorphism $\lambda:C\to{\mathbb{C}}^{\times}$. Its induced character is \[ \left(\operatorname{Ind}_{C}^{G}\lambda\right)(g) =\frac{1}{|C|}\sum_{\substack{x\in G\\x^{-1}gx\in C}}\lambda(x^{-1}gx), \qquad g\in G. \] These are the induced cyclic characters of $G$. The cyclic subgroups and their linear characters are unrestricted; in particular, the trivial subgroup and principal linear characters are included.

Describe all finite groups $G$ with the following property: for every $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(G)$, there exist integers $a_\chi$ and $b_{\chi,C,\lambda}$ such that, as class functions on $G$, \[ \chi=a_\chi 1_G+ \sum_{\substack{C\leq G\\C\text{ cyclic}}} \ \sum_{\lambda\in\operatorname{Hom}(C,{\mathbb{C}}^{\times})} b_{\chi,C,\lambda}\operatorname{Ind}_{C}^{G}\lambda. \] The coefficients may depend on $\chi$ and may be negative; both sums are finite.

The supplied source reports that computer-aided computations of A. V. Rukolaine establish this property for $\mathbb{S}_5$, $\mathrm{SL}_2(13)$, and $M_{11}$, respectively the symmetric group on five letters, the determinant-one matrix group over ${\mathbb{F}}_{13}$, and the Mathieu group $M_{11}$. This computational assertion is retained as source-reported background and has not been independently verified here.

Short English statement

Classify the finite groups whose every irreducible complex character is an integer linear combination of the principal character and characters induced from linear characters of cyclic subgroups. Negative coefficients are allowed. The supplied registry labels this problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
