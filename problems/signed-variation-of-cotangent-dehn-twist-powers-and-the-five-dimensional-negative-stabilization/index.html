Signed variation of cotangent Dehn-twist powers and the five-dimensional negative stabilization

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

This record is Giroux's Question 1.33, whose number is split across lines in the extraction. It has three parts about open-book monodromy. (a) asks whether boundary-trivial symplectomorphisms of Stein pages are generated by Dehn twists along parametrized Lagrangian spheres; asks for a formulation modulo the problem for higher-dimensional balls; and asks whether the fibrewise boundary twist on a Donaldson-hyperplane complement $F_k=W\setminus H_k$, for integral $[\omega]$ and $k\gg0$, factors into sphere twists or is smoothly isotopic to the identity in subcritical cases. The source cites Biran--Cieliebak for the absence of Lagrangian spheres in the subcritical situation. The complement/page truncation and exactness hypotheses are not specified. "The group is trivial" for the six-ball must mean a mapping-class statement, not literal absence of nonidentity compactly supported Hamiltonian maps.

(b) proposes replacing positive twists by odd powers, relating this to Ustilovsky contact spheres, and asks for Floer distinctions between $\varphi$ and $\varphi^3$, a stabilization-compatible Floer invariant, and comparison with contact homology. (c) proposes attaching a handle along the boundary of a Lagrangian disc and composing with a negative twist, asking whether the contact structure changes or becomes nonfillable; it suggests the $S^5$ cotangent-$S^2$ example. Boundary-triviality is understood in the usual open-book convention as identity near the boundary. Full symplectic mapping-class, Floer and filling conventions are not supplied.

The aid computes one concrete family. For $n\geq2$, let $W=D^*S^n$, let $\tau$ be its positive symplectic Dehn twist about the zero-section, and for an integer $k\ne0$ define the smooth open-book manifold \[ M(n,k)=\operatorname{OB}(D^*S^n,\tau^k),\qquad\dim M(n,k)=2n+1. \] The relevant variation map is \[ \operatorname{Var}(\tau^k):H_n(W,\partial W;{\mathbb{Z}})\simeq{\mathbb{Z}} \longrightarrow H_n(W;{\mathbb{Z}})\simeq{\mathbb{Z}}. \] Choose generators so the single positive twist has variation coefficient one. Its induced action on $H_n(W)$ is $(-1)^{n+1}$. The signed coefficient may then be written \[ v_k=\begin{cases} k,&n\text{ odd},\\ (1-(-1)^k)/2,&n\text{ even}. \end{cases} \] For positive powers this is the sum of the iterated monodromy actions; inverse powers obey the inverse-variation identity, giving the same displayed formula for negative $k$. In particular its absolute value is $|k|$ for odd $n$, one for even $n$ and odd $k$, and zero for even $n,k$.

The aid gives \[ H_n(M(n,k);{\mathbb{Z}})=\operatorname{coker}({\mathbb{Z}}\xrightarrow{v_k}{\mathbb{Z}}), \qquad H_{n+1}(M(n,k);{\mathbb{Z}})=\ker({\mathbb{Z}}\xrightarrow{v_k}{\mathbb{Z}}). \] Also $H_0=H_{2n+1}={\mathbb{Z}}$, and all other reduced homology vanishes outside these middle and top degrees. Explicitly:

  • For odd $n$, $H_n={\mathbb{Z}}/|k|{\mathbb{Z}}$ and $H_{n+1}=0$.
  • For even $n$ and odd $k$, both middle groups vanish.
  • For even $n,k$, both middle groups are ${\mathbb{Z}}$.
The total space is simply connected. Hence even-$n$, odd-power examples are homotopy spheres. This does not make them all standard smooth spheres in every dimension. For $n=2$, however, every odd positive or negative power is smoothly $S^5$, as reported by the aid.

At $n=2$, the supported contact structures for $k=1$ and $k=-1$ are not contactomorphic: the former is the standard positively stabilized fillable contact sphere, while the latter is a negatively stabilized overtwisted structure, reported not weakly fillable. This verifies the suggested five-dimensional contrast without requiring a Floer computation to distinguish the signs. Ordinary homology cannot distinguish any odd powers in the even-$n$ parity $2n+1=4m+1$ relevant to Ustilovsky, so it does not answer the requested $\tau$ versus $\tau^3$ contact/Floer distinction. It is also insensitive to reversing $k$'s sign. General Dehn-twist generation, Donaldson boundary-twist factorization and a stabilized Floer/contact-homology comparison are not solved by this calculation.

The source's six-ball uncertainty, higher-dimensional-ball pessimism and statements about the reach of the methods are historical workshop remarks, not present literature assessments.

Short English statement

The aid computes the topology of cotangent Dehn-twist open books, including negative powers. In the even-page-sphere parity all odd powers are homotopy spheres; for $S^2$ pages they are smoothly $S^5$. Positive and negative single twists give different contact structures there, while homology does not distinguish the odd-power Floer questions.
