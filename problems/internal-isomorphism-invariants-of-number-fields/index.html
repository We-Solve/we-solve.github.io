Internal Isomorphism Invariants of Number Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A number field is a field $K$ that is a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. Two number fields $K$ and $L$ are isomorphic if there exists a field isomorphism $K\to L$; such an isomorphism necessarily fixes ${\mathbb{Q}}$.

The supplied question asks whether an invariant internal to a number field can classify number fields up to isomorphism and provide an internal analogue of the external reconstruction results mentioned in the source record. Schematically, after specifying a class of invariant values $\mathcal X$, an equivalence relation $\sim$ on those values, and the admissible meaning of "internal," the intended classification requirement would be an assignment $K\mapsto I(K)\in\mathcal X$ satisfying \[ \forall\,\text{number fields }K,L,\qquad I(K)\sim I(L)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad K\cong L. \] The assignment would additionally have to satisfy the intended internality condition and the intended relationship to the external reconstruction results.

Unresolved formulation. The record does not define internality, the permitted invariant values or their equivalence, or the external reconstruction results to be matched. These are unresolved parts of the question, not freely chosen hypotheses. The displayed condition therefore expresses only the classification requirement, not a complete formalization of the intended problem. An unrestricted complete invariant would not by itself resolve the unspecified internality requirement.

Short English statement

The question asks whether data internal to each number field can determine that field up to isomorphism, in a way analogous to unspecified external reconstruction results. The supplied fragment does not define the permitted internal data or the required analogy, so the intended existence question remains incompletely specified.
