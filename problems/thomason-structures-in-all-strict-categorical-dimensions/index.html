Thomason structures in all strict categorical dimensions

OpenAI's claim

Strict globular n-categories are claimed to admit proper combinatorial Thomason model structures for every positive finite n and n=infinity, Quillen equivalent to spaces with fibrations detected by $\operatorname{Ex}^2$ of the Street nerve. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every positive integer $n$ and $n=\infty$, let $n\mathrm{Cat}$ be strict higher categories and $N_nX$ the Street nerve with simplices $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal O_m,X)$ from orientals. Let $c_n$ be its left adjoint and set $L_n=c_n\operatorname{Sd}^2$, $R_n=\operatorname{Ex}^2N_n$. OpenAI claims a proper combinatorial cofibrantly generated model structure with \[ W_n=R_n^{-1}(\text{weak homotopy equivalences}),\quad F_n=R_n^{-1}(\text{Kan fibrations}),\quad C_n={}^{\perp}(F_n\cap W_n). \] The generators are $L_n(\partial\Delta[m]\hookrightarrow\Delta[m])$ and $L_n(\Lambda^k[m]\hookrightarrow\Delta[m])$, and $L_n\dashv R_n$ is a Quillen equivalence with Kan--Quillen simplicial sets. Here left orthogonality is the lifting property, and subdivision and extension are the usual simplicial functors.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 317, 2026-09-25): Positive-chain prisms construct strict Gray cylinders that remain stationary on old cells during arbitrary attachments. Relative cover and sieve calculations compare categorical pushouts with nerve pushouts, supplying the transfer condition without assuming a free target. Finite orientals control the infinite-dimensional case, while reflection and smallness arguments give the model axioms and properness in each finite dimension.
