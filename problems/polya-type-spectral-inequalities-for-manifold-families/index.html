Pólya-Type Spectral Inequalities for Manifold Families

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The intended research question is to develop Pólya-type lower eigenvalue inequalities, with suitable correction terms, uniformly over specified families of Riemannian manifolds. The supplied record does not specify those families, the spectral operator, boundary conditions, metric normalization, correction terms, or allowed dependence of constants. Consequently, it does not determine a fully quantified manifold conjecture.

The record also supplies the following Euclidean-domain conjectural bounds. Let $n$ denote the ambient dimension, let $|\Omega|$ denote Euclidean volume for $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$, and let $k\in{\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. The notation $\rho_k(\Omega)$ denotes a spectral quantity whose definition is missing from the supplied record. Subject to resolving that omission and specifying the admissible domains, the stated quantifiers are: for every $\alpha>0$ and $M>0$, there exist $a=a(\alpha,M,n)>0$ and $b=b(\alpha,M,n)>0$ such that, for every admissible $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ with $|\Omega|=M$ and every $k\in{\mathbb{N}}$, \[ \begin{aligned} \rho_k(\Omega)&\geq a\,k^{2/(2n-1)} &&\text{if $\Omega$ is convex},\\ \rho_k(\Omega)&\geq b\,k^{1/n} &&\text{for general admissible $\Omega$}. \end{aligned} \] These are transcribed conjectural bounds, not a complete mathematical formulation: the role of $\alpha$, including any implicit occurrence in $\rho_k$, and the hypotheses on $\Omega$ are unspecified.

The source motivates these bounds by results for rectangles and unions of rectangles attributed to its reference [5]. It also states that an underlying conjecture fails for manifolds in general, while results for round $n$-hemispheres $H_+^n$, attributed to its reference [6], allow an inequality of a similar type after adding a correction term. The antecedent of the claimed failure is unclear. The hemisphere formula is corrupted in the supplied extraction: it contains $\lambda_k$, $C_n$, a power $2/n$, and the apparent subtractive term $(n-1)(n-2)/6$, but their complete arrangement and normalization cannot be recovered reliably. No hemisphere inequality or universal manifold assertion is therefore imposed here.

Short English statement

Seek Pólya-type lower eigenvalue bounds with correction terms that hold uniformly over suitable families of Riemannian manifolds. The supplied Euclidean conjectures predict growth rates $k^{2/(2n-1)}$ for convex domains and $k^{1/n}$ for general domains of fixed volume, but the spectral quantity and admissibility conditions are missing. A precise final formulation requires these definitions and recovery of the corrupted hemisphere formula.
