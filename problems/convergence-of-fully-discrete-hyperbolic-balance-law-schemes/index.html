Convergence of Fully Discrete Hyperbolic Balance-Law Schemes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A hyperbolic balance law is an evolution equation or system involving a hyperbolic flux and a source term. A fully discrete approximation discretizes both space and time. Let $U_{h,k}$ denote a numerical approximation with spatial mesh parameter $h>0$ and time step $k>0$, and let $u_{h,k}$ denote a corresponding reconstruction as a function of space and time. The reconstruction, the underlying balance law, and its data are not specified in the supplied record.

Requested convergence problem. Prove convergence of fully discrete approximations of hyperbolic balance laws, including the Lax--Friedrichs and Godunov schemes. Schematically, the intended conclusion is \[ u_{h,k}\longrightarrow u\qquad\text{as }h,k\to0, \] where $u$ is an appropriate solution of the prescribed balance law, and the meshes and time steps satisfy the conditions required by the intended numerical scheme.
