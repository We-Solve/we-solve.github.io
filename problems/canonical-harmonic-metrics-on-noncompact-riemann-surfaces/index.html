Canonical Harmonic Metrics on Noncompact Riemann Surfaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a noncompact Riemann surface, and write $K_X$ for its holomorphic cotangent bundle. The conventional interpretation of the tuple in the source is \[ \vec q=(q_2,\ldots,q_n),\qquad q_i\in H^0(X,K_X^{\otimes i}), \] with an integer $n\geq2$. The excerpt does not explicitly specify this range or the degrees of the differentials, so this interpretation requires confirmation.

Write \[ (E,\theta)=(K_{X,n},\theta(\vec q)) \] for the source-designated associated Higgs bundle, and retain $C_{K,X,n}$ for its designated compatibility structure. The supplied excerpt does not define these objects or the meaning of compatibility with $C_{K,X,n}$; these are unresolved parts of the formulation, not additional freely chosen data.

For a holomorphic vector bundle $E$ with Higgs field $\theta\in H^0(X,\operatorname{End}(E)\otimes K_X)$, a smooth positive-definite Hermitian metric $h$ is called harmonic if its Chern connection $D_h$ satisfies \[ F_{D_h}+[\theta,\theta^{*h}]=0. \] Here $\theta^{*h}$ is the Hermitian adjoint Higgs field and the bracket is the graded commutator of endomorphism-valued forms. Define, provisionally, \[ \mathcal H_C(X,\vec q)=\left\{h:\ h\text{ is a harmonic metric on }(K_{X,n},\theta(\vec q)),\quad h\text{ is compatible with }C_{K,X,n}\right\}. \] The compatibility condition in this display remains a source-dependent predicate whose definition must be recovered before the problem is fully specified.

Questions. For every noncompact Riemann surface $X$ and every differential tuple $\vec q$ admitted by the source's construction:

  1. Is $\mathcal H_C(X,\vec q)\neq\varnothing$?
  2. Can one specify a canonical notion of a best member of $\mathcal H_C(X,\vec q)$, such that exactly one best member exists for every such $(X,\vec q)$?
The second question asks for a selection criterion as well as existence and uniqueness relative to that criterion. No criterion, normalization, boundary behavior, or equivalence relation for uniqueness is supplied. It therefore does not assert that all compatible harmonic metrics are equal.

Qualification from the appended background. The extracted main-results passage additionally assumes that $X$ is hyperbolic, described there as equivalent to $X$ being biholomorphic to neither ${\mathbb{C}}$ nor ${\mathbb{C}}^*$. It denotes by $g_X$ the unique complete hyperbolic Kähler metric, with curvature normalization unspecified. It describes a reference metric $h_X$ assembled from powers of $g_X$ and fixed constants $a_k$, chosen to make $h_X$ harmonic for $(K_{X,n},\theta(0))$, followed by an increasing filtration $0\subset F_1\subset\cdots\subset F_n$. The powers, bundle construction, and continuation of the filtration passage are damaged or truncated in the supplied extraction. They do not supply a reliable definition of the best metric. The hyperbolicity assumption belongs to that appended passage and the supplied existence assessment; the original questions themselves are stated for noncompact Riemann surfaces generally.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether every admitted tuple of holomorphic differentials on a noncompact Riemann surface gives an associated Higgs bundle with a compatible harmonic metric, and whether a canonical criterion can select exactly one such metric. The bundle construction, compatibility condition, and meaning of best are not defined in the supplied excerpt. Supplied dated curation reports existence on noncompact hyperbolic surfaces and uniqueness under boundedness or completeness conditions, while describing unrestricted uniqueness as unsettled; these claims require independent review.
