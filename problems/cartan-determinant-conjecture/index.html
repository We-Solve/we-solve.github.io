Math-219

Cartan determinant conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be an Artin algebra: an associative unital algebra, finite as a module over a commutative Artinian ring acting centrally. Choose representatives $S_1,\ldots,S_n$ of the simple left $A$-modules and their projective covers $P_j\twoheadrightarrow S_j$. Its integral Cartan matrix is \[ C(A)_{ij}=[P_j:S_i], \] where the right-hand side counts composition factors. Use the same ordering for the simple modules and their corresponding projective covers. The global dimension of $A$ is the supremum of the projective dimensions of left $A$-modules.

Does finite global dimension imply $\det C(A)=1$ for every Artin algebra $A$? No algebraically closed coefficient-field hypothesis is imposed.

Short English statement

Must an Artin algebra of finite global dimension have Cartan determinant one?

Sources

Y. Han and Y. Qin, Reducing homological conjectures by n-recollements, arXiv:1410.3223, discussion of the Cartan determinant conjecture, https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3223. For recent progress on the representation-finite case see L. Chen and Q. Guo, The Cartan determinant conjecture for representation-finite Artin algebras, arXiv:2610.14638v2 (18 October 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14638v2. This is an independently written formulation of the general conjecture.
