Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For positive-integer Subset Sum with n items and bit length $b\leq n^c$ for any fixed c, can one uniform randomized classical word algorithm use $n^{O_c(1)}2^{n/2}$ operations and $O_c((n+b+2)^{15}2^{0.199n})$ writable words on every path, never falsely answering YES and answering YES instances correctly with probability $\geq 2/3$, without being given c?

Low-space worst-case Subset Sum at half-exponential time

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 138; 2026-09-26), the asserted result is that worst-case Subset Sum has a one-sided randomized solver in $\operatorname{poly}(n)2^{n/2}$ time and $O(2^{n/5})$ ordinary writable words on every execution.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
The input comprises positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ and a nonnegative integer $t$, with $n\ge2$; the question is whether $\sum_{i\in I}a_i=t$ for some subset $I$. Define \[ b=\max\{1,\lceil\log_2(1+\max(t,a_1,\ldots,a_n))\rceil\},\qquad w=\lceil4(n+b+\log_2(n+2))\rceil. \] The model has read-only random-access input and counted writable $w$-bit words, unit-cost word arithmetic and addressing, and unit-cost fresh random words; all stored randomness and preprocessing are charged. A single uniform algorithm has no false positive and accepts each positive instance with probability at least $2/3$. For every fixed integer $c\ge1$ there exist $A_c,k_c,N_c$ such that $n\ge N_c$ and $b\le n^c$ imply, on every random execution, \[ T\le A_c n^{k_c}2^{n/2},\qquad S\le A_c(n+b+2)^{15}2^{0.199n} \] in operations and writable words, respectively. The algorithm itself does not depend on $c$.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 138; 2026-09-26), distinct-sum counts and entropy estimates select between two trials. Compact block dictionaries generate larger representation lists through small-state streams, while bounded random-prime sampling and exact word operations preserve the time, storage, and one-sided guarantees.
