Math-841

Maeda Conjecture for Level-One Cusp Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard interpretation of the weight, let $k\geq 2$ be an even integer, and let \[ S_k(1)=S_k(\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{Z}})) \] be the complex vector space of holomorphic cusp forms of weight $k$ for the full modular group. Write $m_k=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}S_k(1)$. A normalized Hecke eigenform is a nonzero simultaneous eigenvector for all Hecke operators $T_n$, with Fourier expansion \[ f(z)=\sum_{n\geq 1}a_n(f)q^n,\qquad q=e^{2\pi i z},\qquad a_1(f)=1. \] Its coefficient field is the number field \[ {\mathbb{Q}}_f={\mathbb{Q}}\left(a_n(f):n\geq 1\right)\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}. \] Let $L_f\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ be the normal closure of ${\mathbb{Q}}_f/{\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $\mathfrak S_m$ denote the symmetric group on $m$ letters.

Maeda's conjecture, as formulated in the supplied record. For every such weight $k$ and every normalized Hecke eigenform $f\in S_k(1)$, does one have \[ [{\mathbb{Q}}_f:{\mathbb{Q}}]=m_k \qquad\text{and}\qquad \operatorname{Gal}(L_f/{\mathbb{Q}})\cong\mathfrak S_{m_k}? \] When $S_k(1)=\{0\}$, there is no normalized eigenform, so the assertion at that weight is vacuous. The supplied background also describes the prediction that all normalized Hecke eigenforms of a fixed weight form one Galois orbit, where Galois automorphisms act on Fourier coefficients.

Formulation qualifications. The source does not explicitly specify the range of $k$; positive even integral weights are the standard convention used here and require confirmation against the cited report. The original wording quantifies over every $f\in S_k(1)$, whereas the supplied cleaned statement restricts to normalized Hecke eigenforms. The formulation above follows that explicit restriction; it does not assert the claim for arbitrary cusp forms.

Short English statement

For each level-one weight, Maeda's conjecture predicts that every normalized cuspidal Hecke eigenform has coefficient-field degree equal to the dimension of the cusp-form space, and that its normal closure has the full symmetric Galois group. The supplied background also predicts a single Galois orbit. The weight convention and the restriction from arbitrary cusp forms to normalized eigenforms are explicit interpretation qualifications.
