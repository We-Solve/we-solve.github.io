Math-830

Optimal Order of Multidimensional Point-Set Discrepancy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $s\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $s\geq3$, and let $N\geq2$ be an integer. For a set $P\subset[0,1)^s$ of $N$ distinct points, define its normalized anchored discrepancy function by \[ \Delta_P(x)=\frac{1}{N}\#\left(P\cap[0,x)\right)-\prod_{j=1}^s x_j, \qquad x\in[0,1]^s, \] where $[0,x)=\prod_{j=1}^s[0,x_j)$. Define the star discrepancy and its optimal value by \[ D_N^*(P)=\sup_{x\in[0,1]^s}|\Delta_P(x)|, \qquad d_s^*(N)=\inf_{\substack{P\subset[0,1)^s\\ \#P=N}}D_N^*(P). \] The infimum expresses the source's optimization over point sets without assuming attainment. Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm.

Question under the anchored interpretation. For every fixed integer $s\geq3$, do there exist constants $c_s,C_s>0$ and an integer $N_s\geq2$ such that, for every integer $N\geq N_s$, \[ c_s\frac{(\log N)^{s-1}}{N} \leq d_s^*(N) \leq C_s\frac{(\log N)^{s-1}}{N}? \] In addition, do the Chen--Skriganov and digital-net constructions identified by the supplied curation attain the corresponding upper order, with constants independent of $N$ for fixed $s$?

Unresolved qualifications. The record calls the quantity $L^\infty$ discrepancy without specifying its test boxes; its literature assessment refers to star discrepancy. Thus the displayed formulation explicitly adopts anchored boxes and normalized discrepancy as an interpretation requiring confirmation against the report. The original statement does not specify a dimension restriction, whereas the supplied corrected statement imposes $s\geq3$, which is retained here. The original construction question refers to references [4] and [6]; their bibliographic identities, construction parameters, and admissible cardinalities are not supplied. Consequently, the construction question cannot yet be quantified precisely over particular families or over all versus only admissible values of $N$. An upper bound along a restricted sequence of cardinalities alone would not establish the displayed upper bound for every sufficiently large $N$.

Short English statement

For each fixed dimension $s\geq3$, is the smallest normalized star discrepancy of an $N$-point set comparable to $(\log N)^{s-1}/N$ as $N\to\infty$, and do the constructions named in the supplied curation achieve this order? The anchored interpretation and the precise construction families require source confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
