The Nekovář–Scholl Plectic Cohomology Conjecture

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record attributes the following conjecture to Nekovář--Scholl. Write \[ C=R\Gamma(\operatorname{Sh}_\infty,L_\xi) \in D^b(\Gamma_E,{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell). \] Here $R\Gamma$ denotes derived global sections, and $D^b$ denotes a bounded derived category. The source uses $\operatorname{Sh}_\infty$ for a Shimura-variety tower and $L_\xi$ for its coefficient system. It does not supply the underlying Shimura datum, the definition of the tower or coefficient system, the fields $E,F$, the precise plectic group $\Gamma_{F/{\mathbb{Q}}}$, or the representation-category conventions defining these derived categories. These data must be supplied before the conjecture becomes a fully specified mathematical assertion.

The requested lifting statement can be expressed conditionally as follows. Let \[ \operatorname{Res}_{[\mu]}: D^b(\Gamma_{F/{\mathbb{Q}}},{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) \longrightarrow D^b(\Gamma_E,{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) \] stand for the restriction functor intended by the source's phrase "via $[\mu]$." Its defining group homomorphism and dependence on $[\mu]$ are not provided in the record. For every instance covered by the intended, but unspecified, hypotheses, the conjecture asks for a canonical object \[ \widetilde C\in D^b(\Gamma_{F/{\mathbb{Q}}},{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) \] and a canonical isomorphism \[ \alpha:\operatorname{Res}_{[\mu]}\widetilde C \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } C, \] compatible with the Hecke actions. In particular, for each intended Hecke operator $T$, its lifted action $\widetilde T$ should satisfy \[ \alpha\circ\operatorname{Res}_{[\mu]}(\widetilde T) =T\circ\alpha. \] The Hecke algebra, its allowed operators, and the precise meaning of canonicity are not specified. This conditional expression records the requested lift; it does not supply the missing hypotheses or a uniqueness criterion.

The source partly motivates the conjecture by applications to semisimplicity of Shimura-variety cohomology as Galois representations, citing its references [4] and [5]. It also describes an in-progress result [12]: for distinct primes $p\ne\ell$ and Shimura data of abelian type, a canonical action of a local plectic reflex Galois group, more precisely a Weil-group version, is claimed on the corresponding cohomology. This is a qualified local claim, not a supplied proof of the displayed global conjecture. For its explanatory formalism, the excerpt fixes a torsion ${\mathbb{Z}}_\ell$-algebra $\Lambda$, uses constant coefficients, and says that ${\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$-coefficients can be obtained by an inverse limit and base change. It discusses the $v$-stack $\operatorname{Bun}_G$ over $\mathbf F_p$ of bundles on the Fargues--Fontaine curve and its derived category of $\Lambda$-valued $\acute{e}$tale sheaves. It attributes Hecke endofunctors with internal $W_{{\mathbb{Q}}_p}$-actions, or actions of a subgroup, to Fargues--Scholze [2], and a cohomology formula using abelian-type Igusa stacks to [3]. The excerpt ends during another in-progress theorem, so its full statement is unavailable.

Short English statement

The Nekovář--Scholl conjecture asks whether the derived cohomology of a Shimura-variety tower has a canonical plectic Galois action extending its given Galois action via $[\mu]$, compatibly with Hecke operators. The supplied excerpt omits the setup needed to specify this extension precisely. Its local abelian-type result is explicitly described as work in progress.
