Math-3507

Hilbert-Series Factorization for Finite-Dimensional Nichols Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathfrak B=\mathfrak B(V)=\bigoplus_{d\geq 0}\mathfrak B_d$ be a finite-dimensional Nichols algebra with its natural grading, where $V=\mathfrak B_1$. In its defining braided setting, a Nichols algebra is the quotient of the tensor algebra $T(V)$ by the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal having zero intersection with degrees zero and one. If $k$ is its ground field, define its Hilbert series by \[ H_{\mathfrak B}(t)=\sum_{d\geq 0}\dim_k(\mathfrak B_d)t^d\in\mathbb Z[t]. \] For a positive integer $r$ and a formal variable $u$, put \[ (r)_u=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}u^j. \] The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every finite-dimensional Nichols algebra in the intended class, there exist nonnegative integers $n,m$ and positive integers $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n,\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_m$, depending on $\mathfrak B$, such that \[ H_{\mathfrak B}(t) =\prod_{i=1}^{n}(\alpha_i)_t \prod_{j=1}^{m}(\beta_j)_{t^2}. \] Empty products are interpreted as $1$. The positive-integer parameter interpretation and empty-product convention make the displayed formula precise, but are not explicitly specified in the record. The ground field, its characteristic, and the permitted braided settings are also unspecified; consequently the intended domain of the universal quantifier remains unresolved. The record presents this as a conjecture, not as an established factorization theorem. Its background additionally describes the extraction as truncated, whereas its statement-verification field claims that the official report states the displayed conjecture; this discrepancy requires source review.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether the naturally graded Hilbert series of every finite-dimensional Nichols algebra factors into finite geometric sums in $t$ and $t^2$. The intended ground field and braided setting are unspecified, and the record contains conflicting qualifications about the completeness of the extraction.
