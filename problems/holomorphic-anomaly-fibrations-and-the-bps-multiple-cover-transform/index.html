Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
A fibered Calabi--Yau threefold is a Calabi--Yau space with a specified fibration, for example elliptic-curve or K3 generic fiber. Holomorphic anomaly equations govern anti-holomorphic dependence of topological-string amplitudes. BPS counting organizes curve-counting information into proposed integer invariants, with conventions for genus and multiple covers. Comparing equations before and after fiber integration requires specifying these conventions.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Investigate holomorphic anomaly equations in fibered Calabi--Yau threefolds and BPS counting. In particular, determine whether the equations integrate over elliptic fibrations or K3 fibers.
Scope and source qualification.
The final elliptic/K3 question is restored from source Problem 1.3. Canonical record 20002503 is its elliptic-fibration subquestion. This is a research programme, not a theorem about an unspecified pushforward.
