The solved smooth Hsiang-Kleiner classification and the limits of a direct Ricci-flow proof

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks whether Ricci flow can upgrade the Hsiang--Kleiner homeomorphism classification of positively curved four-manifolds with symmetry to a diffeomorphism classification. The trailing heading "8. Miscellaneous Problems" is not part of the question. In the original oriented setting, let $(M^4,g_0)$ be closed, connected, oriented, with strictly positive sectional curvature and an effective isometric $S^1$-action. The intended standard smooth types are $S^4$ and ${\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^2$, with the orientation of ${\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^2$ reversed when required.

The supplied aid says the diffeomorphism conclusion is already known by Grove--Wilking, Geometry & Topology 18 (2014), Theorem A: a closed positively curved four-manifold with effective isometric circle symmetry is equivariantly diffeomorphic to a linear action on $S^4$, ${\mathbb{R}}{\mathbb{P}}^4$, or ${\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^2$. In the oriented Hsiang--Kleiner setting the ${\mathbb{R}}{\mathbb{P}}^4$ case is excluded. This supplied theorem resolves the smooth classification, but its proof is not a four-dimensional curvature-preserving Ricci-flow deformation. The method-specific request remains distinct from the classification result.

The aid reports a persistence lemma. For a smooth Ricci flow on the closed fixed manifold, either \[ \partial_tg=-2\operatorname{Ric}(g) \quad\text{or}\quad \partial_tg=-2\operatorname{Ric}(g)+c(t)g \] with a spatially constant normalization, the original $S^1$-action remains effective and isometric throughout its smooth existence interval. Its fixed sets, isotropy groups, slice representations/weights and legally weighted orbit data remain those of the same underlying smooth action. Thus such a flow does not simplify a weighted exceptional circle merely by changing the metric. This assertion does not include passage through singularities, surgery or a change of underlying action.

In the original oriented hypotheses, the aid also reports that a smooth extension of the circle action to an effective $T^2$-action, together with the Hsiang--Kleiner bound $\chi(M)\le3$, forces the standard smooth structure $S^4$ or oriented $\pm{\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^2$. Producing that extension is an additional step, not a consequence of symmetry persistence alone. A flow-based strategy would need suitable canonical convergence/surgery or control of the knotting of weighted exceptional orbit data in the three-dimensional quotient. The source does not provide such a strategy as a theorem.

Finally the aid cites Bettiol--Krishnan examples showing that positive sectional curvature need not be preserved by four-dimensional Ricci flow. Their full symmetry and initial-metric hypotheses are not supplied here, so no stronger counterstatement for every Hsiang--Kleiner flow is invented. The examples rule out assuming general sectional-curvature preservation as an automatic step.

Short English statement

The supplied literature already gives the smooth classification, while the requested Ricci-flow proof is a separate method question. Smooth flow preserves the original circle action and its orbit data, and positive sectional curvature cannot be assumed to persist without further hypotheses.
