Heisenberg Actions on Hochschild–Serre Cohomology

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an object $S$ for which the relevant products and quotient stacks are defined, the symmetric group $\mathfrak S_n$ acts on $S^n$ by permuting the factors. The corresponding symmetric quotient stack is \[ \mathcal X_n=[S^n/\mathfrak S_n]. \] This is a conventional interpretation of "symmetric quotient stacks"; the supplied record does not specify the permitted objects $S$, the base field, or the range of $n$.

An ordinary Heisenberg Lie algebra associated with a vector space $V$ over a field $K$ and an alternating bilinear form $\omega$ is the central extension \[ \mathfrak h(V,\omega)=V\oplus Kc,\qquad [v,w]=\omega(v,w)c,\qquad [c,v]=0. \] An action on a vector space $M$ means a Lie algebra homomorphism $\mathfrak h(V,\omega)\to\operatorname{End}_K(M)$, where the target has the commutator bracket. These definitions explain ordinary Heisenberg terminology only: the source does not identify $K$, $V$, $\omega$, the central action, or whether a graded or super version is intended.
