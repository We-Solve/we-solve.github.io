Math-1351

Uniform color bounds for avoiding three-point collinear configurations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The isolated source asks only, "What is the minimum number of colors needed above?" Its preceding companion record, permanent ID 20000960, ends with the assertion that every three-point collinear set $T$ can be avoided monochromatically by a $16$-coloring of every Euclidean space ${\mathbb{R}}^N$. This supplies the antecedent used by the attached aid; it is distinct from the neighboring two-color plane conjecture.

Let $T$ be any set of three distinct collinear points, with their Euclidean distances fixed. For $n\geq1$, let $\chi_n(T)$ be the least integer $q\geq1$ for which there is a coloring $c:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to\{1,\ldots,q\}$ having no monochromatic isometric image of $T$. No measurability or regularity restriction is imposed. Define the dimension- and triple-uniform number \[ q_* =\min\{q\geq1:\ \forall T\ \forall n\geq1\ \exists c:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to\{1,\ldots,q\} \text{ avoiding a monochromatic congruent }T\}. \] The question under this recovered antecedent is to determine $q_*$. The coloring may depend on both $T$ and $n$; it is not one coloring avoiding all triples simultaneously.

The supplied aid records $3\leq q_*\leq16$, attributing the upper bound to Straus's classical theorem and the updated lower bound to Currier--Moore--Yip's 2024 plane theorem for equally spaced triples. It also records $\chi_1(T)=2$ for each $T$ and $\chi_n(T)\leq\chi_{n+1}(T)$.

For a triple whose consecutive gaps are $ps,qs$, where $p,q$ are coprime positive integers and $s>0$, the aid gives the explicit coloring, in every finite dimension, \[ c(x)=\left\lfloor\frac{\|x\|_2^2}{s^2}\right\rfloor \pmod{(p+q)(pq+1)}. \] It claims this avoids every monochromatic congruent copy of that triple, yielding four colors for equal gaps and nine for ratio $1:2$. This construction applies to rational gap ratios and is not asserted for all triples. The literature bounds and coloring lemma are supplied claims requiring independent verification, not a determination of $q_*$.

Short English statement

Determine the least universal number of colors that can avoid any specified three-point collinear configuration in every finite Euclidean dimension. The preceding record supplies the missing antecedent. The attached aid reports bounds $3$ to $16$ and explicit dimension-uniform colorings for rational gap ratios.
