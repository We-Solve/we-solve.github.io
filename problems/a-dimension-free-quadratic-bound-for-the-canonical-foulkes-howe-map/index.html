A dimension-free quadratic bound for the canonical Foulkes–Howe map

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The canonical Foulkes--Howe map is claimed onto whenever $a\geq2$ and $b\geq a(a-1)$, uniformly in the underlying dimension.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $V$ be any finite-dimensional complex vector space. Using averaging over permutations to identify symmetric powers with invariant tensors, multiplication in $(\operatorname{Sym}V)^{\otimes a}$ defines the canonical Foulkes--Howe map \[ \mu_{a,b,V}:\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^aV) \longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^a(\operatorname{Sym}^bV). \] The asserted dimension-independent sufficient bound is \[ a\ge2,\quad b\ge a(a-1)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mu_{a,b,V}\text{ surjective}. \]

Outline of the argument

An annihilator formulation reduces surjectivity to vanishing of symmetric multilinear forms. Two successive differential procedures first free a slot from common factors and then eliminate the remaining common power; injectivity of directional differentiation supplies the quadratic degree condition.

Formal verification

For family 210, the formal scope is canonical surjectivity in the quadratic range, its $a=1$ case, and reverse embeddings. For sixth powers this only covers $b\geq30$, rather than every $b\geq6$. The listed Comparator files are FoulkesHowe.lean. Named declarations include OAI.Problem346.canonical_foulkes_howe_surjective. This site has not executed the Lean code.
