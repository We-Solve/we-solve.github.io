Conference-matrix reduction and a strict coherence-gap certificate

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The record concatenates two questions. Problem 18 is attributed to Boumediene Et-Taoui. Let $v(n,r,\mathbb K)$ be the largest size of a family of distinct equi-isoclinic $n$-planes in $\mathbb K^r$, $\mathbb K={\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}},\mathbb H$. A standard projection formulation is $P_iP_jP_i=\lambda P_i$ for every $i\ne j$, with one common $0\leq\lambda<1$. It asks:
  1. Find $v(2,2r,{\mathbb{R}})$ for every $r\geq4$.
  2. Find $v(2,7,{\mathbb{R}})$.
  3. Find $v(3,3r,{\mathbb{R}})$ for every $r\geq3$.
  4. List all regular $v$-tuples in the source's $G(4,4v)$ whose symmetry groups are isomorphic to $S_v$. The Grassmannian, regularity and effective-symmetry conventions are not supplied.
  5. Find $v(3,3r,\mathbb K)$ for $\mathbb K={\mathbb{C}},\mathbb H$; the range of $r$ is not repeated explicitly.
  6. Find all complex $(2M,M)$ equiangular tight frames.
Problem 19, attributed to Emily King, asks to minimize coherence among complex unit-norm tight frames when no equiangular tight frame exists. The aid addresses only 18(f) and 19, not the remaining isoclinic classification questions.

For $N\geq M\geq1$, a complex UNTF is a matrix $F=[f_1\cdots f_N]\in{\mathbb{C}}^{M\times N}$ with $\|f_i\|=1$ and $FF^*=(N/M)I$. It is an ETF if all $|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|$, $i\ne j$, are equal. Let $G=F^*F$. The reported exact conference reduction for $N=2M$ is \[ Q=\sqrt{2M-1}(G-I). \] Then $F$ is an ETF if and only if $Q$ is a Hermitian complex conference matrix: zero diagonal, unit-modulus off-diagonal entries and $Q^2=(2M-1)I$. Conversely $G=I+Q/\sqrt{2M-1}$ is a positive semidefinite Gram matrix of rank $M$. Unitary frame equivalence, column phases and permutations correspond to the associated Gram/conference monomial-conjugacy equivalences. This translates the classification, rather than enumerating all conference matrices.

For Problem 19 define $\mu(F)=\max_{i<j}|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|$. The UNTF space is compact, so $\mu_*$ is attained. In the nontrivial range $N>M$, $N\geq3$, set \[ \beta=\frac{N-M}{M(N-1)},\qquad K=\binom N2,\qquad V(F)=\frac1K\sum_{i<j}(|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|^2-\beta)^2. \] The unordered squared correlations have mean $\beta$. The aid reports \[ \mu(F)^2-\beta\geq \max\left\{\frac{V(F)}\beta, \sqrt{\frac{V(F)}{K-1}}\right\}. \] If no ETF exists, $\nu=\min_{F\ \mathrm{UNTF}}V(F)>0$, hence \[ \mu_*^2\geq\beta+ \max\left\{\frac\nu\beta,\sqrt{\frac\nu{K-1}}\right\}>\beta. \] This is a strict gap above Welch, but a numerical optimum still requires evaluation of $\nu$ or stronger information.

The aid's row identity is $\sum_{j\ne i}|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|^2=(N-1)\beta$. For $0\leq\tau<\beta$, it yields the active-correlation count \[ \#\{j\ne i:|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|^2>\tau\} \geq\left\lceil\frac{(N-1)(\beta-\tau)}{\mu(F)^2-\tau}\right\rceil. \] These constraints do not determine the best coherence for every pair $(N,M)$, and the displayed denominators require the stated range rather than degenerate orthonormal cases.

Short English statement

The merged record asks several isoclinic classification questions and a non-ETF coherence optimization. The aid reduces the $(2M,M)$ complex ETF case to Hermitian conference matrices and proves a strict variance-based Welch gap plus rowwise correlation counts for UNTFs. It does not resolve all the concatenated questions.
