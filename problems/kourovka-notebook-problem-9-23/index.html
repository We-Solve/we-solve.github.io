Kourovka Notebook Problem 9.23

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $G$ be a finite group, and let $B$ be an ordinary $p$-block of $G$, with defect group $D=D(B)$. Here an ordinary $p$-block means a block in the usual modular representation theoretic partition of the irreducible complex characters of $G$. The source leaves the choice of prime and this interpretation of "block" implicit; the statements below use the standard interpretation that they range over every prime $p$, every finite group $G$, and every ordinary $p$-block $B$ of $G$.

Write $\operatorname{Irr}(B)$ for the set of irreducible complex characters belonging to $B$, and put $k(B)=|\operatorname{Irr}(B)|$. For a positive integer $n$, let $n_p$ denote the largest power of $p$ dividing $n$. Write $|G|_p=p^a$ and $|D|=p^d$. The height $h_B(\chi)$ of $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B)$ is the nonnegative integer determined by \[ \chi(1)_p=p^{a-d+h_B(\chi)}. \] Define \[ k_0(B)=\left|\{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B):h_B(\chi)=0\}\right|, \qquad D'=\langle x^{-1}y^{-1}xy:x,y\in D\rangle. \] A finite group is $p$-soluble if it has a finite normal series whose factors are either $p$-groups or groups of order coprime to $p$.

The source presents the following three assertions, universally quantified as above:

  1. (a) (R. Brauer) \[ k(B)\leq |D(B)|. \] The source reports that this inequality has been proved for $p$-soluble groups, citing D. Gluck, K. Magaard, U. Riese, and P. Schmid (2004).
  2. (b) (J. B. Olsson) \[ k_0(B)\leq |D(B):D(B)'|. \]
  3. (c) (R. Brauer) \[ D(B)\text{ is abelian}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad k_0(B)=k(B). \] Equivalently, the defect group is abelian if and only if every irreducible complex character in the block has height zero. The supplied discussion labels this assertion solved and attributes its proof to G. Malle, G. Navarro, A. A. Schaeffer Fry, and P. H. Tiep (2024).
The supplied registry labels the combined problem partially_solved. This label and the reported results are supplied source assessments, not independent mathematical or literature verification.

Short English statement

For every prime $p$ and every block of irreducible complex characters of a finite group, the problem asks for two bounds on character counts in terms of the block's defect group, and for the equivalence between an abelian defect group and all characters in the block having height zero. The source reports (a) proved for $p$-soluble groups and (c) proved in full; it labels the combined record partially solved.
