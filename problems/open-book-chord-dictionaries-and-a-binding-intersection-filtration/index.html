Math-1532

Open-book chord dictionaries and a binding-intersection filtration

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The workshop proposal is to compute a suitable relative/Legendrian contact-homology invariant from a contact open book, first in the complement of the binding and then after restoring it, possibly through an $A_\infty$ deformation. Auroux suggests generators coming from intersections of a page Lagrangian $L$ with its monodromy iterates $\varphi^k(L)$. A page Lagrangian is not automatically Legendrian for an arbitrary contact form: a zero-primitive condition or a specified Legendrian lift is needed.

The supplied aid gives a strict model. Let $(P^{2m},{\,\mathrm{d}}\lambda)$ be an exact symplectic page, $\varphi^*\lambda=\lambda$, and \[ Y^\circ=(P\times{\mathbb{R}})/((x,t+1)\sim(\varphi(x),t)),\qquad\alpha={\,\mathrm{d}} t+\lambda. \] Let $L\subset P$ be Lagrangian with $\lambda|_L=0$, so $\Lambda=L\times\{0\}$ is Legendrian. The Reeb vector field is $\partial_t$. A positive winding-$k$ chord, $k\geq1$, starts at $x\in L$ with $\varphi^k(x)\in L$; its endpoint identifies it with $L\cap\varphi^k(L)$, and its action is $k$. Nondegeneracy matches transversality of that intersection. These are exact statements only for the specified suspension and normalization.

For a chord-only holomorphic disk in the unperturbed complement, with one positive chord $c_+$, negative chords $c_1,\ldots,c_s$, boundary on ${\mathbb{R}}\times\Lambda$ and no interior orbit punctures, the aid reports \[ w(c_+)=\sum_iw(c_i),\qquad E_{{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha}(u)=\mathcal A(c_+)-\sum_i\mathcal A(c_i)=0. \] Thus the strict model has no nontrivial page contribution of this type, apart from trivial Reeb-trajectory configurations. The generator correspondence alone is not a chain identification with a Lagrangian Floer complex; perturbation and analytic choices cannot be omitted.

After restoring a binding $B$, assume the symplectized binding ${\mathbb{R}}\times B$ is a holomorphic hypersurface and that the disk has a well-defined finite relative intersection count and is not contained in it. The aid's defect formula is \[ w(c_+)-\sum_iw(c_i)=u\cdot({\mathbb{R}}\times B)\geq0. \] The integer on the right measures the order of a binding correction. Subject to a well-defined contact-homology differential, compactness and transversality/virtual hypotheses, total winding gives a filtration; zero-intersection terms recover the complement contribution in the associated graded, while positive-intersection terms lower winding. This does not by itself construct a gluing theorem or the proposed $A_\infty$ deformation.

The aid mentions rigorous but distinct settings: mapping-torus contact homology, Legendrian/Fukaya Koszul duality, Petr's circular-contactization mapping-torus theorem, and Colin--Ghiggini--Honda's binding-complement theorem for embedded contact homology. It says no unrestricted relative-contact-homology gluing formula was verified. These invariants and hypotheses are not identified with one another. The extract's final transition to Eliashberg's next questions is not a further question here.

Short English statement

Can contact open books turn relative contact homology into a Floer-theoretic computation, with binding restoration as a deformation? The supplied aid gives a precise chord dictionary and winding/intersection filtration in special models, while showing that the dictionary alone does not identify differentials.
