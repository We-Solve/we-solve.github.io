Selection Principles in Mathematics — Problem 4 ([SFH])

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Give each set $X\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$ its subspace topology. An open $\omega$-cover of $X$ is a family $\mathcal U$ of open subsets of $X$ such that $X\notin\mathcal U$ and, for every finite $F\subseteq X$, some $U\in\mathcal U$ satisfies $F\subseteq U$. Write $\Omega(X)$ for the family of countable open $\omega$-covers of $X$. This uses the standard countable-open-cover convention; the supplied record does not explicitly define $\Omega$.

The property $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Omega,\Omega)$ means that, for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ with $\mathcal U_n\in\Omega(X)$, there exist finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that \[ \bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}\mathcal F_n\in\Omega(X). \] Here the union is the family of all selected cover members, rather than the family of their finite unions within individual covers.

Let ${\mathbb{N}}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. For $f,g\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}$, write $f\leq^{*}g$ if $f(n)\leq g(n)$ for all sufficiently large $n$. The dominating number is \[ \mathfrak d=\min\left\{|D|:D\subseteq{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}\ \text{and}\ (\forall f\in{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}})(\exists g\in D)\ f\leq^{*}g\right\}. \]

The supplied existence question asks whether ZFC proves \[ \exists X\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}\quad\left(|X|=\mathfrak d\ \text{and}\ X\text{ satisfies }\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Omega,\Omega)\right). \] The phrase "in ZFC" is interpreted here as asking for a ZFC theorem of existence.

The record also contains an inserted update reporting a negative answer, with Laver's model as a counterexample and reference label [BBC]. It further reports an affirmative answer if choosing one element from each cover is replaced by choosing a union of two elements from each cover, with reference label [MHP]. These reports are preserved as supplied qualifications, not as independently verified conclusions. The one-element/two-element language does not specify a modification of the displayed finite-selection property, so the update's relationship to this existence question remains unresolved. The registry separately labels the record "open"; no resolution of that status conflict is asserted.

Short English statement

Does ZFC prove that there is a set of reals of cardinality $\mathfrak d$ satisfying $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Omega,\Omega)$? This property requires finite selections from every sequence of open $\omega$-covers whose combined family is again an $\omega$-cover. The supplied negative-answer update and its one-element/two-element qualification have an unclear relationship to this question and require source review.
