Math-3319

Size Ramsey Numbers of Ramanujan Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For finite simple undirected graphs $H$ and $G$, write $H\to(G)_2$ if every coloring of $E(H)$ with two colors contains a monochromatic subgraph isomorphic to $G$; the copy need not be induced. Define the two-color size Ramsey number by \[ \widehat r(G)=\min\left\{|E(H)|:H\text{ is a finite simple undirected graph and }H\to(G)_2\right\}. \] Here edge coloring, simple undirected graphs, and non-induced copies are the standard conventions used to interpret the supplied statement; the record does not explicitly specify these conventions.

For a connected $\Delta$-regular graph, the usual Ramanujan condition is that every adjacency eigenvalue other than the trivial eigenvalues $\Delta$, and $-\Delta$ when the graph is bipartite, has absolute value at most $2\sqrt{\Delta-1}$. The supplied record does not define Ramanujan graphs or specify connectedness, whether bipartite graphs are included, or the treatment of trivial eigenvalues for disconnected graphs. The term Ramanujan below retains this unresolved qualification.

Let $G$ be an $n$-vertex, $\Delta$-regular Ramanujan graph, where $\Delta\geq3$, and put \[ N=10^{10^{10\Delta n}}. \] Let $\mathcal G(N,p)$ denote the random graph on $N$ labeled vertices in which each pair of distinct vertices is an edge independently with probability $p$. Choose $p=p(G)\in[0,1]$ such that \[ \Pr\left(\mathcal G(N,p)\to(G)_2\right)=\frac12. \] The supplied question asks whether \[ \widehat r(G)=\Theta\!\left(p(G)N^2\right). \] The supplied literature assessment refers to every fixed degree. Under that reading, the precise asymptotic question is: for every fixed integer $\Delta\geq3$, do there exist constants $c_\Delta,C_\Delta>0$ and $n_0(\Delta)$ such that every $n$-vertex, $\Delta$-regular Ramanujan graph $G$ with $n\geq n_0(\Delta)$ satisfies \[ c_\Delta p(G)N^2\leq\widehat r(G)\leq C_\Delta p(G)N^2? \] The constants in this reading are uniform over the eligible graphs of each fixed degree. The displayed source statement itself does not specify the asymptotic regime or the permitted dependence of the implicit constants, so this quantifier interpretation requires confirmation.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the minimum number of edges in a graph forcing a monochromatic copy of a regular Ramanujan graph is comparable to $pN^2$, where the random graph on the specified enormous number $N$ of vertices has the forcing property with probability $1/2$. The supplied assessment suggests a bound uniform over graphs of each fixed degree, but the constant dependence and precise Ramanujan convention require review. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
