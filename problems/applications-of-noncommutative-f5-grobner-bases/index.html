Research attempt: Finite quotient certificates and a noncommutative overlap test

A complete small quotient calculation.
Let $F$ be a field of characteristic different from two and work in the free associative algebra $F\langle x,y\rangle$ with a two-sided ideal \[ I=(x^2,y^2,yx+xy). \] Use reductions $xx\mapsto0$, $yy\mapsto0$, $yx\mapsto-xy$. They terminate: the first two lower word length, while the last preserves length and lowers the number of pairs in which a $y$ precedes an $x$. The only overlapping length-three ambiguities are $xxx,yyy,yyx,yxx$. The first two reduce to zero on either path; for example $yyx\mapsto-yxy\mapsto xyy\mapsto0$, and $yxx\mapsto-xyx\mapsto xxy\mapsto0$. Disjoint reductions commute, including their scalar signs.

These local checks imply a unique result: if two terminating reduction paths first choose different steps, disjoint steps or a checked overlap can be joined; induction on the maximum remaining reduction length then joins the rest. Every normal word is one of $1,x,y,xy$. Consequently every polynomial has a unique normal form \[ a+bx+cy+dxy, \] which vanishes if and only if it lies in $I$. One direction follows because every replacement subtracts a two-sided multiple of a defining relation. For the other direction, multiplication of a relation on either side reduces to zero by the just-proved uniqueness, so every element of the generated ideal has zero normal form. In particular the four normal words are linearly independent in the quotient.

This supplies explicit symbolic certificates. For $r=bx+cy+dxy$ the multiplication table gives $r^2=0$. Therefore $a+r$ is invertible exactly when $a\ne0$, with inverse $a^{-1}-a^{-2}r$. Necessity follows by mapping the quotient to $F$ via $x,y\mapsto0$. Also \[ [a+bx+cy+dxy,x]=-2cxy,\qquad [a+bx+cy+dxy,y]=2bxy. \] Thus the centralizer of $x$ is the span of $1,x,xy$, and the center is the span of $1,xy$. These are checkable applications to units and commutation constraints in a finitely presented associative algebra, provided the engine computes a terminating basis with this sidedness and order.

A concrete failed shortcut.
It would be invalid to discard noncommutative overlaps merely because distinct leading words are not divisible by one another. For the relations $xy-x$ and $yx-y$, the word $xyx$ reduces by the left pair to $xx$ and by the right pair to $xy\mapsto x$. Thus the additional relation $x^2-x$ is forced; similarly $yxy$ forces $y^2-y$. With the four reductions \[ xx\mapsto x,\quad xy\mapsto x,\quad yx\mapsto y,\quad yy\mapsto y, \] any nonempty word reduces to its first letter. Every triple overlap has that same result and termination follows by length, so the repaired basis has normal words $1,x,y$. This exhibits an actual missing critical-pair calculation, not merely a warning that word order matters. A signature criterion may reject a pair only with a proof that its labelled consequence is already accounted for; the commutative "no divisibility" shortcut above is insufficient.
