Beauville–Voisin Conjecture for Hyperkähler Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is the Beauville--Voisin conjecture for projective hyperkähler manifolds. Its coefficient convention and the precise meaning of "hyperkähler" are not explicitly specified. The following is a precise formulation under the conventional interpretation of complex irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifolds and rational Chow groups; these conventions require confirmation against the original source.

Let $X$ be a smooth connected projective complex variety of dimension $d=2n$, with $n\geq 1$, which is simply connected and satisfies \[ H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)={\mathbb{C}}\omega \] for an everywhere nondegenerate holomorphic two-form $\omega$. Write \[ \mathrm{CH}^k(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\mathrm{CH}^k(X)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}} \] for codimension-$k$ algebraic cycles modulo rational equivalence with rational coefficients, and let $\mathrm{CH}^*(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ carry its intersection product. Define the graded subalgebra \[ R^*(X)={\mathbb{Q}}\left[c_1(L),\ c_i(T_X)\mid L\in\operatorname{Pic}(X),\ 1\leq i\leq d\right] \subseteq \mathrm{CH}^*(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}, \] where $T_X$ is the tangent bundle and $c_i$ denotes its $i$-th Chern class. Thus each element is a polynomial involving finitely many of these generators.

A class $z\in\mathrm{CH}^k(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ is numerically trivial if \[ \deg(z\cdot\alpha)=0 \qquad\text{for every }\alpha\in\mathrm{CH}^{d-k}(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}. \] The question, under the stated conventions, is whether for every such $X$, every integer $0\leq k\leq d$, and every $z\in R^k(X)$, \[ \left(\forall\alpha\in\mathrm{CH}^{d-k}(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}},\quad \deg(z\cdot\alpha)=0\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad z=0\text{ in }\mathrm{CH}^k(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}. \] For a polynomial with components in several codimensions, numerical triviality and the conclusion are interpreted componentwise. Vanishing here means rational equivalence to zero with rational coefficients. The source does not resolve whether an integral-coefficient assertion, or a broader convention for hyperkähler manifolds, was intended.

Short English statement

For every projective hyperkähler manifold, must a numerically trivial polynomial in divisor classes and tangent-bundle Chern classes be rationally equivalent to zero? The displayed formulation uses complex irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifolds and rational coefficients, subject to confirmation of these unstated source conventions.
