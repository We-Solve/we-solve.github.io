Math-6474

CMC Min–Max Width Under Nonnegative Scalar Curvature

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M^3,g)$ be a complete, asymptotically flat, three-dimensional Riemannian manifold without boundary. Assume that its scalar curvature satisfies $\operatorname{Scal}_g\geq 0$ everywhere and that $(M,g)$ is not isometric to Euclidean $({\mathbb{R}}^3,g_{\mathrm{Eucl}})$. For each real number $c>0$, the supplied record considers the functional \[ \mathcal A_c(\Omega)=\operatorname{Area}_g(\partial\Omega)-c\operatorname{Vol}_g(\Omega), \] where $\Omega\subset M$ is an admissible region. The record does not specify the class of admissible regions or the interpretation of boundary area for nonsmooth regions. For a smooth region with finite volume and finite boundary area, these terms mean its Riemannian volume and the Riemannian area of its boundary.

Write $\omega_c$ for the one-parameter min--max value of $\mathcal A_c$ referred to in the supplied statement. The proposed assertion is that, for every manifold satisfying the stated assumptions and every $c>0$, \[ \omega_c<\frac{16\pi}{3c^2}. \] This assertion remains conditional on the intended definition of $\omega_c$: the supplied record gives no sweepout class, parameter-space endpoint conditions, topology of continuity, or admissibility and finiteness conditions defining that min--max value. It also gives no precise definition or decay requirements for asymptotic flatness. These choices must be recovered from the source before the assertion is fully specified.

The retained original passage conjectures that an unidentified condition "(1)" holds whenever $M$ has nonnegative scalar curvature and is not isometric to Euclidean ${\mathbb{R}}^3$. The displayed strict inequality above is the supplied registry's interpretation of that condition; the equation and its surrounding definitions are absent from this record. The supplied background describes a conjecture about a suitable minimal hypersurface, whereas the title concerns constant mean curvature and the displayed functional has a positive volume coefficient. That terminology is preserved as an unresolved source qualification, without identifying these formulations.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every complete, boundaryless, asymptotically flat Riemannian three-manifold with nonnegative scalar curvature that is not Euclidean has $\omega_c<16\pi/(3c^2)$ for every $c>0$, where $\omega_c$ is the intended one-parameter min--max value of area minus $c$ times enclosed volume. The precise min--max construction and asymptotic assumptions are missing, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
