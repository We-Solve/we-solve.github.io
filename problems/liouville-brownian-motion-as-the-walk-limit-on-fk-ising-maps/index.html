Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For FK--Ising spherical maps sampled with weight $2^{\ell/2}$, use all-edge distance $d_n$, stationary degree measure $\mu_n(v)=\deg(v)/(2n)$, and any deterministic spatial scale $a_n\to0$ yielding the metric-measure limit $(S,D_h,\mu_h)$ of the ordinary unit-area $\sqrt3$-LQG sphere. Start any fixed number $r$ of conditionally independent simple walks from $\mu_n$, interpolated along unit cable edges. The source claims \[ (\overline M_n,a_nd_n,\mu_n;\overline X^{n,j}_{nt},\ 1\le j\le r) \Longrightarrow(S,D_h,\mu_h;B_t^{h,j},\ 1\le j\le r). \] Conditioned on $h$, the limit walks are independent Liouville Brownian motions started from $\mu_h$, with Dirichlet energy $\tfrac12\int|\nabla f|^2$. Paths converge uniformly on compact time intervals in the common metric embeddings; time acceleration is exactly $n$.
