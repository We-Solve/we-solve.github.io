Math-2060

Percolation thresholds along expander limits

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G_n=(V_n,E_n)$ be finite graphs with $|V_n|\to\infty$, maximum degree at most $D$, and uniform edge expansion: for some $h>0$, \[ |\partial_E A|\ge h|A| \quad\text{whenever }0<|A|\le|V_n|/2. \] The source assumes local convergence to an infinite graph $G$, without specifying roots. In a usual local-weak model a root $o_n$ is uniform in $V_n$, and the rooted neighborhoods converge in distribution to those of $(G,o)$; an almost-sure fixed rooted limit is a stronger specification than an arbitrary random rooted limit.

For independent bond percolation on a connected infinite graph, a standard critical value is \[ p_c(G)=\inf\{p\in[0,1]:\mathbb P_p(o\leftrightarrow\infty)>0\}. \] The intended assertion is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}p_c(G_n)=p_c(G). \] But the infinite-cluster definition does not define a finite-graph threshold. A finite giant-component threshold needs extra conventions. For example, \[ p_{n,\alpha}=\inf\{p:\mathbb P_p(|C_{\max}(G_n)|\ge\alpha|V_n|)\ge1/2\} \] depends on $\alpha>0$, and holding a positive fraction fixed is not automatically the onset threshold corresponding to $p_c(G)$. The source must specify whether a vanishing fraction, a double limit, another sharp-threshold convention, or a different finite observable defines its $p_c(G_n)$. Site versus bond percolation is also not stated explicitly; bond percolation is displayed as the usual model.

No transitivity, regularity, or deterministic-limit assumption is silently added. The supplied research summary reports major locality progress but expressly says its latest-status audit was incomplete. Results for vertex-transitive graphs or specially controlled local limits do not by themselves establish the stated arbitrary-expander version. The record provides no precise modern theorem resolving this exact finite-threshold formulation.

Short English statement

Do percolation thresholds converge along bounded-degree expanders with an infinite local limit? Specify the finite giant-component threshold, local convergence/root law, and percolation model before asserting the limit.
