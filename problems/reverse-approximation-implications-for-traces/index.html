Reverse Approximation Implications for Traces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for two reverse implications between approximation properties of traces. It does not specify the ambient class of algebras, separability, or unitality. The following is a precise conditional formulation for separable unital complex $C^*$-algebras; this scope is a drafting convention requiring source verification, not an assumption recovered from the supplied excerpt.

Let $A$ be a separable unital complex $C^*$-algebra. A tracial state on $A$ is a positive linear functional $\tau:A\to{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfying $\tau(1_A)=1$ and $\tau(ab)=\tau(ba)$ for all $a,b\in A$. For $x\in M_n({\mathbb{C}})$, put \[ \operatorname{tr}_n(x)=\frac{1}{n}\operatorname{Tr}(x),\qquad \|x\|_{2,n}=\operatorname{tr}_n(x^*x)^{1/2}. \] Choose positive integers $n_k$ and a nonprincipal ultrafilter $\omega$ on ${\mathbb{N}}$. Let \[ B=\left\{(x_k)\in\prod_{k\in{\mathbb{N}}}M_{n_k}({\mathbb{C}}):\sup_k\|x_k\|<\infty\right\}, \] \[ I_{\|\cdot\|,\omega}=\{(x_k)\in B:\lim_{k\to\omega}\|x_k\|=0\},\qquad I_{2,\omega}=\{(x_k)\in B:\lim_{k\to\omega}\|x_k\|_{2,n_k}=0\}. \] Each quotient $B/I_{\|\cdot\|,\omega}$ and $B/I_{2,\omega}$ carries the trace \[ \operatorname{tr}_{\omega}([(x_k)])=\lim_{k\to\omega}\operatorname{tr}_{n_k}(x_k). \] Under the conventions used here, $\tau$ is an MF trace if, for some such choices, there is a unital $*$-homomorphism \[ \pi:A\longrightarrow B/I_{\|\cdot\|,\omega},\qquad \tau=\operatorname{tr}_{\omega}\circ\pi. \] It is hyperlinear if the corresponding condition holds with target $B/I_{2,\omega}$. These homomorphisms are not required to be injective; faithfulness of $\tau$ is not assumed.

The trace $\tau$ is amenable if, for every finite subset $F\subseteq A$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there exist $n\geq1$ and a unital completely positive linear map $\phi:A\to M_n({\mathbb{C}})$ such that \[ |\operatorname{tr}_n(\phi(a))-\tau(a)|<\varepsilon\quad(a\in F), \] \[ \|\phi(ab)-\phi(a)\phi(b)\|_{2,n}<\varepsilon\quad(a,b\in F). \] Here complete positivity means that $\operatorname{id}_{M_m}\otimes\phi$ is positive for every $m\geq1$. The trace is quasidiagonal if the same finite-set condition holds with the operator-norm estimate \[ \|\phi(ab)-\phi(a)\phi(b)\|<\varepsilon\quad(a,b\in F) \] in place of the normalized $2$-norm estimate.

With these conventions, the two questions are whether, for every separable unital complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ and every tracial state $\tau$ on $A$, \[ \tau\text{ hyperlinear}\ \Longrightarrow\ \tau\text{ an MF trace}, \] and, separately, \[ \tau\text{ amenable}\ \Longrightarrow\ \tau\text{ quasidiagonal}. \] The supplied curation describes the forward implications from MF to hyperlinear and from quasidiagonal to amenable, and labels the reverse implications open. The intended algebra class and the report's precise terminology must be checked before this conditional formulation can be treated as the source's fully quantified statement.

Short English statement

The questions ask whether every hyperlinear trace is MF and whether every amenable trace is quasidiagonal. A conditional formulation for separable unital complex $C^*$-algebras is given above; the supplied excerpt does not establish that scope. The open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified assessment.
