$K_1$ Groups and Theta Images of Iwasawa Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a unital ring $A$, write $A^\times$ for its group of units and define its algebraic $K_1$-group by \[ K_1(A)=\mathrm{GL}_\infty(A)^{\mathrm{ab}}, \qquad \mathrm{GL}_\infty(A)=\varinjlim_n\mathrm{GL}_n(A), \] where stabilization sends $M$ to $\operatorname{diag}(M,1)$. For a profinite group $H$ and a unital coefficient ring $O$, the usual inverse-limit construction is \[ \Lambda_O(H)=\varprojlim_{U\trianglelefteq_{\mathrm{open}}H}O[H/U], \] with transition maps induced by quotient homomorphisms. The supplied record does not specify the coefficient ring, its topology, or the hypotheses on the groups needed for the intended Iwasawa-theoretic setting. For a profinite group $H$, its topological abelianization is $H^{\mathrm{ab}}=H/\overline{[H,H]}$; whether this is the intended convention must be confirmed from the source.

The supplied computational request is to determine the relevant $K_1$-groups and characterize the image of a map displayed as \[ \theta:K_1\left(\Lambda_O(G)\right) \longrightarrow \prod_{i\geq 0}\Lambda_O\left(G_i^{\mathrm{ab}}\right)^\times, \] and to give the corresponding characterization for a localized map denoted $\theta_S$. Here the index range $i\geq 0$ is retained exactly as supplied. The record does not define $O$, $G$, the family $(G_i)_{i\geq 0}$, the construction of $\theta$, the localization set $S$, or the domain and codomain of $\theta_S$. It also does not identify the full collection of "relevant Iwasawa algebras." Consequently, the displayed request is a schematic research-program description, and no definite image conditions, quantified conjecture, or complete standalone mathematical problem can be recovered from this record.

The supplied verification note states that the report presents the computation and theta-image characterization as Theorem 1.1 and its localized consequence, rather than as an open problem. This is a supplied source-verification claim, not an independently checked theorem statement. The registry nevertheless retains the status open, marks the statement unrecoverable, and supplies no clean replacement statement. These qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The record asks for computations of $K_1$-groups of Iwasawa algebras and descriptions of the images of $\theta$ and its localized version $\theta_S$. Essential objects, hypotheses, and maps are unspecified. Moreover, the supplied verification note identifies the material as a theorem and a localized consequence, while the registry retains an open label. A precise standalone problem therefore cannot be stated from the supplied evidence.
