Extreme Normalized Gaps between Riemann-Zeta Zeros

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the meromorphic continuation of $\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}m^{-s}$, initially defined for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$. Its nontrivial zeros are the zeros $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $0<\beta<1$. Consider all such zeros with $\gamma>0$, and list their ordinates in nondecreasing order: \[ 0<\gamma_1\leq\gamma_2\leq\cdots. \] For this draft, zeros are counted with their analytic multiplicities, so repeated ordinates are retained. This is a provisional interpretation of the source's non-strict ordering: the supplied record does not explicitly specify the multiplicity convention. No Riemann hypothesis or simplicity hypothesis is imposed in the supplied clean statement.

For $n\geq1$, define the normalized consecutive gap by \[ g_n=\frac{(\gamma_{n+1}-\gamma_n)\log\gamma_n}{2\pi}, \] where $\log$ is the natural logarithm. The conjecture asks whether both assertions hold: \[ \liminf_{n\to\infty}g_n=0, \qquad \limsup_{n\to\infty}g_n=+\infty. \] Equivalently, for every $\varepsilon>0$ and every integer $N\geq1$, there exists $n\geq N$ with $g_n<\varepsilon$; and for every $M>0$ and every integer $N\geq1$, there exists $n\geq N$ with $g_n>M$.

The supplied excerpt motivates the normalization using the zero-counting asymptotic \[ N(T)=\frac{T}{2\pi}\log\frac{T}{2\pi}-\frac{T}{2\pi}+O(\log T) \qquad(T\to\infty), \] where $N(T)$ counts nontrivial zeros with $0<\operatorname{Im}\rho<T$, under the same multiplicity convention. The scale $2\pi/\log T$ is an asymptotic mean-spacing scale. The first conjectured limit concerns gaps arbitrarily small relative to this scale, not gaps approaching this scale.

The original excerpt also contrasts these conjectures with a stated regular-spacing theorem for functions denoted $\Xi_\lambda(t)$ when $\lambda>0$. Those functions and the threshold $T_\lambda$ are not defined in the supplied record, and their counting formula is typographically damaged. Consequently that comparison cannot be made into a precise auxiliary statement from the supplied evidence alone; it supplies no additional hypothesis for the conjecture above.

Short English statement

List the positive ordinates of all nontrivial Riemann-zeta zeros in order, provisionally retaining multiplicities. The conjecture asks whether consecutive gaps, divided by the asymptotic mean-spacing scale, become arbitrarily small and arbitrarily large along subsequences tending to infinity. The supplied formulation assumes neither the Riemann hypothesis nor simplicity of the zeros. Its multiplicity convention requires confirmation.
