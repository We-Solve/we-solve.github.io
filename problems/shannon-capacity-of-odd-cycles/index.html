Math-1153

Shannon Capacity of Odd Cycles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For every integer $k\geq3$, let $C_{2k+1}$ be the undirected simple cycle with vertex set ${\mathbb{Z}}/(2k+1){\mathbb{Z}}$ and edges between consecutive residues. For a finite graph $G$, let $\alpha(G)$ be its maximum independent-set size. In the strong power $G^{\boxtimes m}$, two distinct words are adjacent exactly when in every coordinate their entries are equal or adjacent in $G$. Define the nonlogarithmic Shannon capacity by \[ \Theta(G)=\sup_{m\geq1}\alpha(G^{\boxtimes m})^{1/m} =\lim_{m\to\infty}\alpha(G^{\boxtimes m})^{1/m}. \] The limit exists by supermultiplicativity of the independence numbers. A logarithmic information-rate convention would instead report $\log_2\Theta(G)$.

The stated problem is to determine the exact values \[ \Theta(C_{2k+1})\qquad\text{for every }k\geq3. \] This asks for all odd lengths at least seven, rather than only a large-length asymptotic or a capacity of directed graphs or permutations.

The supplied literature assessment dated 21 August 2026 calls the material "background rather than a single conjecture" and says no unique problem is stated, while the original and cleaned statements explicitly give the odd-cycle determination problem above. That mismatch is preserved as a curation issue; it does not justify replacing the target by general digraph or permutation capacity. The assessment reports known bounds and small-digraph computations and lists the status as open, but supplies no exact odd-cycle formula or theorem to incorporate. The relevance of those capacity-variant references to this precise target requires verification.

Short English statement

Determine the strong-product Shannon capacity of every odd cycle of length at least seven. The statement is precise, but its supplied literature assessment discusses broader capacity background and conflicts with that precision; the mismatch is flagged rather than changing the target.
