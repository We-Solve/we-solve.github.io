Sheaf Models for Modular Category O

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A full subcategory $\mathcal S$ of an abelian category $\mathcal A$ is a Serre subcategory if it is closed under subobjects, quotients, and extensions. Its Serre quotient $\mathcal A/\mathcal S$ is an abelian category equipped with an exact functor $Q:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A/\mathcal S$ that annihilates $\mathcal S$ and is universal among exact functors from $\mathcal A$ that annihilate $\mathcal S$.

The supplied question asks whether the modular representation category $\mathcal O$, described as a Serre quotient, admits a description as an appropriate category of sheaves. Schematically, the requested description would be an equivalence \[ \mathcal O=\mathcal A/\mathcal S\simeq\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sh}}, \] where $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sh}}$ denotes the intended sheaf category. This notation only displays the requested form: the record does not specify $\mathcal A$, $\mathcal S$, the modular representation data, or $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sh}}$. In particular, it supplies neither the underlying space and coefficients for the sheaves nor the precise categorical sense of the proposed equivalence.

Unresolved source qualification. The record labels the problem open, but also marks its statement as unrecoverable. Its supplied statement-verification note says that the report immediately answers the question by presenting a motivic-sheaf model and a categorical equivalence, and that the question is rhetorical. Consequently, the supplied evidence does not determine a precise unresolved mathematical assertion. The displayed equivalence is a schematic transcription of the extracted question, not a newly formulated conjecture or a verified theorem.
