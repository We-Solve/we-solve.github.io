Induced ell-p paving endpoints and simultaneous all-p conference paving

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $1\leq p\leq\infty$, let \[ \|A\|_{p\to p}=\sup_{x\neq0}\frac{\|Ax\|_{\ell_p^n}}{\|x\|_{\ell_p^n}} \] be the induced operator norm, not a Schatten norm. For $\sigma\subset\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $P_\sigma$ be coordinate projection. If $\mathcal P=(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_k)$ is a partition, with empty blocks allowed, put $A_{\mathcal P}=\sum_jP_{\sigma_j}AP_{\sigma_j}$. Then $\|A_{\mathcal P}\|_{p\to p}=\max_j\|P_{\sigma_j}AP_{\sigma_j}\|_{p\to p}$.

Bill Johnson's source question asks, for every fixed $1\leq p<\infty$, whether \[ \forall\epsilon>0\ \exists k=k(\epsilon,p)<\infty\ \forall n\geq1\ \forall A\in M_n({\mathbb{C}}),\quad \left[ A_{ii}=0,\ \|A\|_{p\to p}=1\right] \Longrightarrow\exists\mathcal P\text{ with at most }k\text{ blocks}:\ \|A_{\mathcal P}\|_{p\to p}\leq\epsilon. \] The source does not specify real versus complex scalars; the displayed convention uses complex matrices. Let $R_p(\epsilon)$ be the least such number, or $\infty$ if none exists, extending the definition to $p=\infty$ for the supplied duality statement. For $0<\epsilon<1$, the aid reports \[ R_1(\epsilon)\leq\lceil2/\epsilon\rceil,\qquad R_2(\epsilon)=\Theta(\epsilon^{-2})\quad(\epsilon\downarrow0),\qquad R_p(\epsilon)=R_{p'}(\epsilon),\quad \frac1p+\frac1{p'}=1, \] with $1'=\infty$, $\infty'=1$. Its literature search located no general settlement for $1<p<\infty$, $p\neq2$; this is a supplied search qualification rather than a verified current nonexistence of a theorem.

The separate simultaneous-paving theorem concerns a real symmetric conference matrix $C$ of order $n\geq2$: $C_{ii}=0$, $C_{ij}\in\{-1,1\}$ for $i\neq j$, and $C^2=(n-1)I_n$. The aid states that, for every $0<\epsilon<1$, there is a single partition $\mathcal P$ into at most $\lceil54\epsilon^{-2}\rceil$ blocks such that \[ \|C_{\mathcal P}\|_{p\to p}\leq\epsilon\|C\|_{p\to p} \qquad\text{simultaneously for every }1\leq p\leq\infty. \] It obtains this from one Ravichandran--Srivastava multi-paving applied to the three Hermitian contractions \[ C/\sqrt{n-1},\qquad -C/\sqrt{n-1},\qquad (J-I_n)/(n-1), \] where $J$ is the all-ones matrix. The statement does not assert such simultaneous partitions for arbitrary matrices. The aid further reports that the infinite Paley conference family forces every fixed-$p$ uniform paving number to satisfy \[ R_p(\epsilon)\geq\epsilon^{-\min(p,p')}\qquad(0<\epsilon<1), \] including the endpoint convention above. This is a necessary bound for a uniform number valid over the family, not a claim that every individual finite matrix requires that many blocks.

Finally the aid reports an explicit rank-one direct-sum family which is exactly two-pavable while naive endpoint interpolation has unbounded dimension-dependent defect. Its matrices and the precise defect quantity are not supplied here, so no more detailed interpolation theorem is inferred. The original comments report $\mathrm{Pav}_1$ as long known, tentatively attributed to Bourgain, compare it informally with nonnegative-entry $\mathrm{Pav}_2$, and recall Bourgain--Tzafriri restricted invertibility with optimal estimates only at $p=2$ in their original work.

Short English statement

The question asks for a dimension-independent coordinate paving bound in induced $\ell_p$ operator norm. The supplied report gives the endpoint results and duality, while describing the general intermediate case as unlocated in its search. A separate theorem gives one partition that paves each real symmetric conference matrix in every $\ell_p$ norm, with a Paley-family lower bound.
