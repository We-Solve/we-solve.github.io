Math-359

Birch–Tate Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a totally real number field, meaning a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$ all of whose embeddings into ${\mathbb{C}}$ have image in ${\mathbb{R}}$, and let $\mathcal O_F$ be its ring of integers. For a commutative ring $A$, let $E(A)$ be the stable elementary matrix group, generated by the matrices $I+ae_{ij}$, with $a\in A$ and $i\ne j$, inside the direct limit of the general linear groups. The stable Steinberg group $\operatorname{St}(A)$ has generators $x_{ij}(a)$ and Steinberg relations \[ x_{ij}(a)x_{ij}(b)=x_{ij}(a+b), \] \[ [x_{ij}(a),x_{kl}(b)]=1\quad(i\ne l,\ j\ne k),\qquad [x_{ij}(a),x_{jk}(b)]=x_{ik}(ab)\quad(i,j,k\text{ distinct}), \] where $[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$. Its map to $E(A)$ sends $x_{ij}(a)$ to $I+ae_{ij}$, and the stable group $K_2(A)$ is its kernel. This kernel is the appropriate $K$-theoretic object; an unspecified finite-rank Steinberg-group centre is not used in its place.

Let $\zeta_F(s)$ be the Dedekind zeta function, initially \[ \zeta_F(s)=\sum_{0\ne\mathfrak a\subset\mathcal O_F}N(\mathfrak a)^{-s}\qquad(\operatorname{Re}(s)>1), \] where the sum ranges over nonzero integral ideals and $N(\mathfrak a)=|\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak a|$; its value at $s=-1$ is defined by analytic continuation. Define $w_2(F)$ as the greatest positive integer $m$ for which every $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(\overline F/F)$ satisfies \[ \chi_m(\sigma)^2\equiv1\pmod m, \] where $\chi_m$ is the cyclotomic character describing the action of $\sigma$ on the $m$th roots of unity. Equivalently, $w_2(F)$ is the order of the Galois invariants of the second Tate twist ${\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}}(2)$; the cyclotomic description specifies the normalization.

The intended standard Birch--Tate formula recovered by the supplied triage is \[ \forall\text{ totally real number fields }F,\qquad |K_2(\mathcal O_F)|=w_2(F)\,|\zeta_F(-1)|. \] The source's literal question omits the totally real hypothesis, the evaluation point, and this normalization. Its background mentions $s=-1$ but gives an unnormalized centre equality; the supplied triage warns that the unnormalized equality is false even for ${\mathbb{Q}}$ and that identifying a Steinberg-group centre requires stability hypotheses. The displayed formula is thus an explicitly qualified restoration, not the literal background assertion.

The registry labels the record open. Its literature triage dated 2026-08-17 classifies it as partially solved, attributes the odd-primary part for every totally real field to Wiles, and reports the full formula for totally real abelian extensions of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. It says that the $2$-primary part follows from the $2$-adic Iwasawa main conjecture and remains the outstanding part for arbitrary nonabelian totally real fields. In valuation terms, the reported odd-primary progress concerns every odd prime $\ell$ in \[ v_\ell(|K_2(\mathcal O_F)|)=v_\ell(w_2(F)|\zeta_F(-1)|), \] where $v_\ell$ is extended to nonzero rational numbers in the usual way. These are attributed dated assessments, not a new verification of the literature.

Short English statement

For a totally real number field, the Birch--Tate formula predicts that the order of $K_2$ of its integers equals $w_2(F)$ times the absolute Dedekind zeta value at $-1$. The stored centre question omits essential hypotheses and normalization. The supplied review reports the remaining general issue as the nonabelian $2$-primary case.
