Math-4367

Ideals in the Positive Witt Algebra

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The ground field and its characteristic are not specified in the supplied record. The indexing of the basis of the positive Witt algebra is also not stated explicitly. Consequently, the following is a formulation schema requiring confirmation of these conventions, not a conjecture asserted uniformly over all fields.

Let $k$ denote the intended ground field. Under the conventional positive-index interpretation, let \[ W_+=\bigoplus_{i\geq 1}ke_i, \qquad [e_i,e_j]=(j-i)e_{i+j}\quad(i,j\geq 1), \] where integer coefficients are interpreted in $k$. Let $A=U_k(W_+)$ be its universal enveloping algebra, namely the associative unital algebra generated by $W_+$ subject to $xy-yx=[x,y]$.

For a nonzero unital $k$-algebra $B$, define its Gelfand--Kirillov dimension by \[ \operatorname{GKdim}_k B =\sup_{\substack{V\subseteq B\text{ a finite-dimensional }k\text{-subspace}\\1_B\in V}} \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\dim_k(V^n)}{\log n}, \] where $V^n$ is the linear span of products of $n$ elements of $V$.

Subject to the unresolved ground-field and indexing conventions, prove or disprove each of the following assertions:

  1. For every nonzero two-sided ideal $I$ of $A$, the quotient $A/I$ has finite Gelfand--Kirillov growth. Explicitly, \[ \forall I\trianglelefteq A,\qquad I\neq\{0\}\ \Longrightarrow\ \left(A/I=0\ \text{or}\ \operatorname{GKdim}_k(A/I)<\infty\right). \] The zero quotient is included as having bounded growth, so no properness hypothesis on $I$ is added.
  2. The two-sided ideals of $A$ satisfy the ascending chain condition: for every sequence of two-sided ideals \[ I_1\subseteq I_2\subseteq I_3\subseteq\cdots, \] there exists $N\geq 1$ such that $I_n=I_N$ for all $n\geq N$.
The main supplied statement quantifies over nonzero ideals. Its literature assessment instead says "every nonzero quotient," which is a different condition: a nonzero quotient can have zero kernel, whereas a quotient by a nonzero ideal can be zero. The first assertion above follows the main statement; the intended literature wording requires review.

Short English statement

For the intended positive Witt algebra, ask whether every quotient by a nonzero two-sided ideal has finite Gelfand--Kirillov growth, and whether every increasing sequence of two-sided ideals eventually stabilizes. The ground field and basis indexing need confirmation. The supplied literature summary also uses a different quotient qualification.
