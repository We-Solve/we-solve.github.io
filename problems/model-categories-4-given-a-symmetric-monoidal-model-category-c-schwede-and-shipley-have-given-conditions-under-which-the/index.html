Model categories 4 — Given a symmetric monoidal model category C, Schwede and Shipley have given conditions under which the…

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a symmetric monoidal model category with tensor unit $\mathbf1$. For a symmetric operad $P$ internal to $\mathcal C$, its algebra category is $\operatorname{Alg}_P(\mathcal C)$. A transferred model structure of the kind contemplated in the source creates fibrations and weak equivalences in the underlying category $\mathcal C$.

Model categories problem 4 (Hovey). Generalize the replacement of strict commutative differential graded algebras over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ by $E_\infty$ differential graded algebras to a suitable general symmetric monoidal model setting. Determine hypotheses under which the relevant operad and operadic-algebra model structures exist, rather than assuming them for every $\mathcal C$.

The explicit first step in the source is to construct a model structure on the category of operads in $\mathcal C$, with cofibrant generation of $\mathcal C$ suggested as a likely assumption. A natural candidate uses aritywise weak equivalences and fibrations, but the source does not specify its complete hypotheses or operad conventions. Let $\operatorname{Com}(r)=\mathbf1$ with trivial symmetric-group actions denote the commutative operad in the intended convention. A homotopy-coherent replacement is an appropriate cofibrant resolution $E\to\operatorname{Com}$, and the generalization should explain when $E$-algebras admit the desired model theory. Cofibrancy of an operad, cofibrancy of its underlying symmetric collection, and admissibility of its algebra category must be distinguished; the excerpt does not prove any blanket implication between them.

The source credits Schwede--Shipley with conditions giving a model structure for monoids and contrasts the strict commutative integral-DGA case, where it says the desired underlying-fibration/quasi-isomorphism structure fails. The proposed $E_\infty$ replacement is not a claim that all strict commutative algebras can be rectified over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ or that their categories are automatically Quillen equivalent. Chain-complex boundedness, fibration conventions, operadic units, and other ambient hypotheses remain necessary specifications.

Short English statement

Find conditions giving model structures on operads and homotopy-commutative algebras in a symmetric monoidal model category, generalizing the use of $E_\infty$ DGAs in place of strict commutative DGAs over ${\mathbb{Z}}$.
