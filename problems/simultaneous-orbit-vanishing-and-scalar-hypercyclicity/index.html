Math-6702

Simultaneous Orbit Vanishing and Scalar Hypercyclicity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T$ be an operator on a Hilbert space $H$, using the usual bounded linear-operator interpretation. The scalar field, separability, and dimension assumptions are not supplied. With iterates indexed by ${\mathbb{N}}_0$, define the single-vector condition \[ \forall x\in H:\qquad\inf_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}_0}\|T^nx\|=0. \tag{1} \] This means that for every $x$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, some iterate makes $\|T^nx\|<\varepsilon$; the iterate may depend on $x$ and $\varepsilon$.

The source contains three questions:

  1. Does (1) imply \[ \forall u,v\in H:\quad \inf_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}_0}\left(\|T^nu\|+\|T^nv\|\right)=0\ ? \] The same iterate must make both norms small; separate infima do not express the requested conclusion.
  2. If both $T$ and $3T$ are hypercyclic, must $2T$ be hypercyclic? Hypercyclicity means existence of a vector with dense forward orbit in $H$. The vectors witnessing hypercyclicity of $T$ and $3T$ need not be the same. The scalar factors are exactly $1,3,2$ as supplied; no full interval of factors is substituted.
  3. Does "the first condition" imply that $T$ is hypercyclic? The natural antecedent is (1), but the text does not explicitly disambiguate this phrase from the preceding simultaneous-smallness question. Under the literal reading (1), no extra dense-orbit, transitivity, nontriviality, or growth assumption is present.

The third clause does not exclude zero or contracting operators, which signals a possible omitted hypothesis if a nontrivial hypercyclicity problem was intended. This defect is recorded rather than repaired. The source also does not specify whether the scalar-multiple question is independent of (1) or inherits the opening setup beyond being an operator on a Hilbert space. No answers to the bundled questions or additional hypotheses are asserted in this statement draft.

Short English statement

The entry bundles simultaneous smallness of two orbits, hypercyclicity of the intermediate scalar multiple $2T$, and a claimed connection from orbit smallness to hypercyclicity. The first question requires one shared iterate; the last appears to lack hypotheses, and the scope of the bundled clauses needs checking.
