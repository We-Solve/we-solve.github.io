Math-10299

Interface and field scaling limits of triangular real Lipschitz surfaces

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For uniformly sampled real-valued triangular-lattice Lipschitz surfaces with the specified two-arc boundary values, do the distinguished interface and centered height field converge to chordal $SLE_4$ and sigma times the domain GFF? Require constants independent of the marked domain, $v=2/\operatorname{sqrt}(3)$, $\sigma ^2=1/(vA)$, $\lambda ^2=\pi /(8vA)$, and limiting mean lambda times the difference of the two boundary-arc harmonic measures.
