Interface and field scaling limits of triangular real Lipschitz surfaces

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Uniform real triangular-lattice Lipschitz heights are claimed to give a Gaussian free field, and at a tuned two-arc amplitude their zero contour approaches chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_4$.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $D$ be bounded, smooth and simply connected, with two distinct marked boundary points, and let triangular-lattice polygons $D_\delta$ have uniformly converging marked boundaries. Sample real vertex heights with normalized Lebesgue measure on the polytope \[ h_\delta=\lambda\text{ on }A_{+,\delta},\quad h_\delta=-\lambda\text{ on }A_{-,\delta},\quad |h_\delta(x)-h_\delta(y)|\le1\ (x\sim y). \] Affine interpolation produces a zero-height chord $\gamma_\delta$ between the marks. There exist universal $A,\sigma>0$ and $\lambda\in(0,1/2]$ such that \[ \gamma_\delta\Longrightarrow\mathrm{SLE}_4(D;a,b),\qquad (h_\delta-\mathbb Eh_\delta)/\sigma\Longrightarrow\Phi_D. \] Curves use uniform distance modulo increasing reparametrization, and fields use distributional convergence with $\operatorname{Cov}\Phi_D=(-\Delta_D)^{-1}$. Writing $v=2/\sqrt3$, the constants satisfy \[ \sigma^2=(vA)^{-1},\qquad \lambda^2=\pi/(8vA),\qquad m_D(z)=\lambda[\omega_D(z,A_+)-\omega_D(z,A_-)], \] where $m_D$ is the limiting mean and $\omega_D$ denotes harmonic measure. The coefficient $A$ is determined implicitly by stationary cell flux, rather than by a claimed elementary value.

Outline of the argument

Local slack estimates and reflected stationary comparisons establish a tangent-flux cell problem. Its coefficient determines the bulk variance and tuned boundary amplitude; varying-continuum trace and conditional Dirichlet covariance estimates then identify both field and interface laws.
