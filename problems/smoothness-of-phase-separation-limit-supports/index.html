Math-3336

Smoothness of Phase-Separation Limit Supports

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Consider the phase-separation energy and its admissible configurations as defined in the source report. A stable critical point $u$ is understood variationally: for every admissible smooth one-parameter variation $u_t$ with $u_0=u$, \[ \left.\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t}E[u_t]\right|_{t=0}=0, \qquad \left.\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}^2}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t^2}E[u_t]\right|_{t=0}\ge0. \] For a parameter-dependent energy $E_\varepsilon$, apply these conditions at the relevant $\varepsilon$ and with the report's allowed variations. Any mass constraints or boundary restrictions on variations must be retained, but are not supplied in the extracted record.

Let $\mu$ be a limiting measure arising from a family of such stable critical points by the limiting procedure intended in the report. For a nonnegative Radon measure on a metric domain $\Omega$, the standard support is \[ \operatorname{supp}\mu =\{x\in\Omega:\mu(B_r(x)\cap\Omega)>0\text{ for every }r>0\}. \] The conjectural request is: for every such limiting measure, the support is smooth when the source's dimension parameter satisfies $n\le7$.

The exact energy, small parameter, associated measures, energy bounds, convergence procedure, and ambient geometry are missing. The record also does not state whether $n$ is the ambient dimension or another dimensional parameter, what dimension the smooth support should have, or whether boundary regularity is included. Thus smoothness is retained as the source's global support-regularity target rather than replaced by almost-everywhere regularity, rectifiability, or a bound on the size of a singular set. A precise theorem requires recovery of the source's meaning of smooth support, including its differentiability class and interior or boundary scope. No particular double-well energy or hypersurface model is inserted.

Short English statement

The question asks for full smoothness of the support of every limiting measure obtained from stable critical points of the report's phase-separation energy in dimensions at most seven. The energy, limiting procedure, and geometric meaning of smooth support still need to be specified.
