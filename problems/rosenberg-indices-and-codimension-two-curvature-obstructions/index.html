Math-3792

Rosenberg Indices and Codimension-Two Curvature Obstructions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Index-comparison problem. For a closed smooth spin manifold $M$, what is the relation between its Rosenberg indices $\alpha_{\mathrm{red}}(M)$, $\alpha_{\mathrm{max}}(M)$, and the codimension-two positive-scalar-curvature obstructions of Gromov and Lawson? The source asks for a relation, without specifying one equivalence or implication as the conjectured target.

In the usual connected real-spin convention, let $n=\dim M$, $\pi=\pi_1(M)$, and let $C^*_{\mathrm{max},{\mathbb{R}}}\pi$ and $C^*_{\mathrm r,{\mathbb{R}}}\pi$ denote the maximal and reduced real group $C^*$-algebras. The Rosenberg classes are the higher indices of the spin Dirac operator twisted by the corresponding flat Mishchenko bundles $\widetilde M\times_\pi C^*\pi$:\[\alpha_{\mathrm{max}}(M)\in KO_n(C^*_{\mathrm{max},{\mathbb{R}}}\pi),\qquad \alpha_{\mathrm{red}}(M)\in KO_n(C^*_{\mathrm r,{\mathbb{R}}}\pi).\] The canonical quotient of group $C^*$-algebras sends the maximal class to the reduced class. In particular reduced nonvanishing entails maximal nonvanishing, while the converse is not asserted merely from this map. A positive-scalar-curvature metric forces both classes to vanish by the spin Dirac obstruction. The source does not specify real versus complex index conventions, so the displayed real formulation is explicit rather than silently interchangeable with complex $K$-theory.

The codimension-two obstructions concern suitable embedded submanifolds $N\subset M$ of codimension two, with additional normal-bundle, fundamental-group, topology, and nonvanishing or geometric hypotheses. The source does not quote the precise Gromov--Lawson criterion or fix those data. The problem includes determining whether and under which hypotheses such an obstruction is detected by a nonzero ambient Rosenberg class, and what comparison mechanism relates the codimension-two data to the ambient indices. Any degree-lowering transfer map would require its own construction and assumptions; it is not supplied automatically by an inclusion of fundamental groups. No general equivalence between nonexistence of positive scalar curvature and nonvanishing of either index is asserted.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the item partially solved. It reports substantial development of codimension-two index obstructions and their relation to Rosenberg classes, but says the general relation is not completely settled. The cited theorems and their exact hypotheses are not reproduced. This dated assessment requires independent verification.

Short English statement

Determine which codimension-two curvature obstructions are detected by the ambient maximal or reduced higher Dirac index, and under what comparison hypotheses. The precise submanifold criterion is missing. The standard maximal-to-reduced index map alone does not supply every requested relation.
