Homeomorphism Classification of Compact Oriented Four-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ and $N$ be compact, connected, oriented topological manifolds of dimension $4$, as assumed in the supplied source. A homeomorphism $h\colon M\to N$ is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse. Thus the ordinary homeomorphism relation is \[ M\cong_{\mathrm{Top}}N \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists h\colon M\to N\text{ such that }h\text{ is a homeomorphism}. \] The extracted source asks how one can try to determine whether this relation holds for two such manifolds. It does not specify whether the homeomorphism must preserve the given orientations. If preservation is intended, the existential condition must additionally require that $h$ preserve orientation. The source also does not specify whether manifolds with boundary are allowed or whether any boundary identifications must be respected.
