Compactly Supported Representing Measures for Linear Functionals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a unital commutative ${\mathbb{R}}$-algebra. Its character space is \[ X(A)=\{\alpha:A\to{\mathbb{R}}:\alpha\text{ is a unital }{\mathbb{R}}\text{-algebra homomorphism}\}. \] Assume $X(A)\ne\varnothing$. For $a\in A$, define $\widehat a(\alpha)=\alpha(a)$. Equip $X(A)$ with the weakest topology making every evaluation map $\widehat a:X(A)\to{\mathbb{R}}$ continuous; this topology is Hausdorff.

Here a Radon measure means a nonnegative measure on the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of this topology that is locally finite and inner regular with respect to compact subsets. Its support $\operatorname{supp}\nu$ is the smallest closed subset $C\subseteq X(A)$ such that $\nu(X(A)\setminus C)=0$.

Characterization problem. For every such algebra $A$, characterize the ${\mathbb{R}}$-linear maps $L:A\to{\mathbb{R}}$ with $L(1)=1$ for which there exists a Radon measure $\nu$ on $X(A)$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{supp}\nu\text{ is compact},\qquad L(a)=\int_{X(A)}\widehat a(\alpha)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\nu(\alpha) \quad\text{for every }a\in A. \] Such a measure is called a representing Radon measure for $L$, and a functional admitting a representing Radon measure is called a moment functional. The normalization forces $\nu(X(A))=1$.

No positivity or growth hypothesis on $L$ is imposed in the question: the requested characterization must determine which normalized linear functionals admit this representation. This is not an assertion that every normalized linear functional does so. The supplied report excerpt states that a compactly supported representing measure is unique and that an intrinsic growth characterization also identifies its support. However, the excerpt omits the growth condition and truncates the theorem stating the characterization; those conditions cannot be reconstructed from the supplied text. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports that the cited work provides necessary and sufficient intrinsic conditions. It does not specify a remaining unresolved subproblem.

Short English statement

Determine exactly which normalized linear functionals on a unital commutative real algebra can be represented by integration against a nonnegative Radon probability measure with compact support on its character space. The supplied source reports an existing characterization, but its theorem is truncated and no precise remaining open question is supplied.
