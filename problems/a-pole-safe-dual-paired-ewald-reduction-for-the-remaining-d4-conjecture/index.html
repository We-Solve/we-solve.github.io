A pole-safe dual-paired Ewald reduction for the remaining D4 conjecture

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source conjectures global minimization of $E(L,s)$, for every fixed $s>0$, by "these lattices" in dimensions $4,8,24$. The paired aid identifies them as $D_4,E_8$ and the Leech lattice, respectively, with covolume fixed to one. The original record does not itself define $E$ or its normalization. The aid's Ewald formula uses the Epstein convention \[ Z_L(s)=\sum_{v\in L\setminus\{0\}}\|v\|^{-2s} \quad(\operatorname{Re}s>n/2), \] continued meromorphically for lower parameters. At $s=n/2$, comparison means the finite part after subtracting the common pole, not a finite value of the divergent series. The precise relation of the original $E$ to this convention requires source verification.

The source also states that the corresponding FCC assertion in dimension three is false for every $0<s<3/4$. The aid reports the $E_8$ and Leech components uniquely solved by CKMRV, including the finite-part comparison at the pole, while the $D_4$ component remains globally open apart from a sufficiently large-parameter range. These are supplied literature claims, not an independent status assessment.

For the reported remaining reduction, let $D$ be covolume-one $D_4$, which is isometric to its dual, and let $L$ be any covolume-one lattice in $\mathbb R^4$. Define \[ L^*=\{y:\langle y,v\rangle\in\mathbb Z\ \forall v\in L\},\qquad \Theta_L(t)=\sum_{v\in L}e^{-\pi t\|v\|^2}. \] The aid's pole-safe difference integral is \[ I_L(s)=\int_1^\infty\left[ (\Theta_L(t)-\Theta_D(t))t^{s-1} +(\Theta_{L^*}(t)-\Theta_D(t))t^{1-s}\right]dt. \] In this normalization the reported Ewald identity is \[ Z_L(s)-Z_D(s)=\frac{\pi^s}{\Gamma(s)}I_L(s),\qquad s>0, \] where at $s=2$ the left side denotes the finite-part difference. Its integral is convergent at every fixed real parameter; it subtracts the common singular contribution before comparison.

The remaining conjecture is therefore equivalent, in this interpretation, to \[ I_L(s)\geq0\qquad \text{for every covolume-one }L\subset\mathbb R^4 \text{ and every }s\geq1. \] Competitor duality gives $I_L(s)=I_{L^*}(2-s)$ and covers $0<s<1$ from the displayed range. The aid invokes Ryshkov's final-zeta theorem for the sufficiently large-$s$ tail. Once an actual valid threshold $S\geq2$ is fixed, only the finite parameter interval $[1,S]$ remains to be proved, still uniformly over every competitor. No numerical threshold or proof of the remaining integral inequality is supplied. This weighted dual-paired condition is not replaced by an unproved pointwise theta inequality.

Short English statement

The supplied aid reports the eight- and twenty-four-dimensional components solved and reduces the remaining normalized $D_4$ problem to a dual-paired theta integral. Pole subtraction is essential at $s=2$. Duality and a cited large-parameter theorem reduce the parameter range, but they do not prove the remaining finite-interval inequality for all lattices.
