Math-15351

Weak Compactness in HOD from Iterated Embeddings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An elementary embedding $j:V\to V$ preserves every first-order formula in the language of set theory: for every such formula $\varphi$ and every finite tuple $\vec a$ from $V$, \[ V\models\varphi(\vec a)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad V\models\varphi(j(\vec a)). \] For a nontrivial elementary embedding $e$ that moves an ordinal, its critical point is \[ \operatorname{crit}(e)=\min\{\xi\in\operatorname{Ord}:e(\xi)\ne\xi\}. \] The supplied hypothesis is $(V,j)\models\mathrm{ZF}$, with $j:V\to V$ elementary. The precise axiomatic interpretation of this expanded structure is not specified.

The source prescribes an iteration, indexed by ordinals, by \[ M_0=(V,j),\qquad M_\alpha=(N_\alpha,j_\alpha),\qquad M_{\alpha+1}=(N_\alpha,j_\alpha(j_\alpha)), \] with direct limits at limit ordinals, and writes \[ \lambda=\sup_{n<\omega}\sup\left(\operatorname{crit}(j^n)\right). \] These expressions are retained as supplied. They do not specify the operation $j_\alpha(j_\alpha)$, the transition maps for the direct limits, or the relationship between $j^n$ and the embeddings $j_\alpha$. Consequently, they do not yet determine a self-contained iteration or a unique intended $\lambda$. In particular, interpreting $j^n$ as ordinary composition would make $j^0$ the identity, whose critical point is undefined, and is not adopted here. Nontriviality and the existence of the displayed critical points are required for the expression to make sense but are not explicitly assumed in the record.

Let $\mathrm{HOD}^{V}$ denote the class of hereditarily ordinal-definable sets of the underlying universe $V$: every set in the transitive closure of $\{x\}$ must be definable in $V$ from ordinal parameters. The intended question, conditional on recovering the report's precise iteration and its definition of $\lambda$, is whether every instance satisfying those hypotheses satisfies \[ \mathrm{HOD}^{V}\models\text{``}\lambda\text{ is a weakly compact cardinal.''} \] Here weak compactness means that, internally to $\mathrm{HOD}^{V}$, $\lambda$ is an uncountable strongly inaccessible cardinal and \[ \lambda\longrightarrow(\lambda)^2_2. \] Explicitly, every coloring $c:[\lambda]^2\to\{0,1\}$ belonging to $\mathrm{HOD}^{V}$ has a homogeneous set $H\in\mathrm{HOD}^{V}$ with $H\subseteq\lambda$ and $|H|^{\mathrm{HOD}^{V}}=\lambda$. Strong inaccessibility here means regularity and the strong limit property, with cardinalities and power sets computed internally to $\mathrm{HOD}^{V}$. This explains the proposed conclusion without supplying the missing iteration conventions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the cardinal arising from the report's iterated elementary-embedding construction must be weakly compact in $\mathrm{HOD}$. The supplied formulas leave essential iteration conventions and the definition of $\lambda$ unresolved, so the intended assertion requires clarification from the cited report. The record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
