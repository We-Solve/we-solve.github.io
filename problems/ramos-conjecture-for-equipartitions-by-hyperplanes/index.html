Math-873

Ramos Conjecture for Equipartitions by Hyperplanes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $j,k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}$. The supplied record calls a simultaneous equipartition of $j$ masses by $k$ affine hyperplanes a division into $2^k$ regions, each containing the fraction $2^{-k}$ of every mass.

The following makes this condition precise under an explicitly conditional measure interpretation. Suppose that, for each positive integer $d$, $\mathcal M_d$ is a specified class of finite nonnegative Borel measures on ${\mathbb{R}}^d$, each assigning measure zero to every affine hyperplane. For affine hyperplanes \[ H_i=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:\langle a_i,x\rangle=b_i\},\qquad a_i\in{\mathbb{R}}^d\setminus\{0\},\quad b_i\in{\mathbb{R}},\quad 1\leq i\leq k, \] define their sign regions by \[ R_{\sigma}=\bigcap_{i=1}^k\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:\sigma_i(\langle a_i,x\rangle-b_i)>0\},\qquad \sigma\in\{-1,1\}^k. \] They simultaneously equipartition $\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_j\in\mathcal M_d$ if \[ \mu_r(R_{\sigma})=2^{-k}\mu_r({\mathbb{R}}^d) \quad\text{for every }r\in\{1,\ldots,j\}\text{ and every }\sigma\in\{-1,1\}^k. \] The boundary condition on the measures ensures that excluding the hyperplanes does not discard any mass. Here the source word "orthants" is interpreted as these sign regions; perpendicularity of the hyperplanes is not asserted.

For such a specified family $\mathcal M=(\mathcal M_d)_{d\geq1}$, define, whenever the minimum exists, \[ \Delta_{\mathcal M}(j,k)=\min\left\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}:\ \forall(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_j)\in\mathcal M_d^{\,j},\ \exists H_1,\ldots,H_k\text{ simultaneously equipartitioning them}\right\}. \] The record denotes its intended quantity by $\Delta(j,k)$, but does not specify the admissible class of masses. Thus the measure hypotheses above describe a conditional formulation, not hypotheses verified from the supplied source.

Ramos conjecture, as supplied. The stated known lower bound for $\Delta(j,k)$ is attained for every pair of positive integers $j,k$. If $L(j,k)$ denotes that intended lower bound, this assertion has the schematic form \[ \forall j,k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1},\qquad \Delta(j,k)=L(j,k). \] The supplied record gives no formula for $L(j,k)$. Consequently, neither the numerical target nor the admissible mass class can be fully specified from the supplied evidence alone.

Short English statement

The Ramos conjecture says that the least dimension allowing every collection of $j$ admissible masses to be divided equally among all $2^k$ sign regions of $k$ affine hyperplanes equals the stated known lower bound, for all positive integers $j,k$. The supplied record omits both the formula for that bound and the precise meaning of an admissible mass; the displayed measure formulation is therefore conditional.
