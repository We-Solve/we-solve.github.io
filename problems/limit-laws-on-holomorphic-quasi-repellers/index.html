Math-1931

Limit laws on holomorphic quasi-repellers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f$ act on the holomorphic quasi-repeller $X$ of the source, and let $m$ be an invariant probability measure with measure-theoretic entropy $h_m(f)>0$. The record does not define holomorphic quasi-repeller or impose ergodicity. Let $\mathcal F$ be the intended class of "reasonable" real-valued observables; its regularity and integrability are also unspecified.

For an integrable observable $\varphi$, put $\bar\varphi=\varphi-\int\varphi\,\mathrm dm$ and define the centered Birkhoff sums \[ S_n\varphi(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\bar\varphi(f^jx). \] Under an asymptotic-variance convention, positive variance means that \[ \sigma_\varphi^2=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\int_X(S_n\varphi)^2\,\mathrm dm \] exists and lies in $(0,\infty)$. The source does not specify whether this, rather than ordinary one-step variance, is intended.

The task is to characterize precisely those positive-entropy invariant measures $m$ for which every $\varphi\in\mathcal F$ with the required positive variance satisfies all three requested laws. For the above variance convention, the central limit theorem means \[ \frac{S_n\varphi}{\sigma_\varphi\sqrt n} \ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal N(0,1) \quad\text{under }m, \] and the law of the iterated logarithm means, $m$-almost surely, \[ \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{S_n\varphi}{\sqrt{2n\log\log n}}=\sigma_\varphi, \qquad \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{S_n\varphi}{\sqrt{2n\log\log n}}=-\sigma_\varphi. \] The almost-sure invariance principle asks for a coupling, possibly on an extension of the probability space, of the sums with a Brownian motion of variance $\sigma_\varphi^2$ and a suitably small almost-sure approximation error. The source does not prescribe that error rate or the precise ASIP version. The quasi-repeller, observable, variance, and ASIP conventions are therefore necessary inputs to the requested characterization, not freely chosen extra hypotheses.

Short English statement

Which positive-entropy invariant measures on a holomorphic quasi-repeller make every admissible positive-variance observable satisfy the ASIP, LIL, and CLT? The source asks for a characterization, not merely a sufficient criterion for one measure or observable.

The supplied summary reports the laws in expanding and positive-entropy equilibrium settings, mentioning Hölder observables, Bowen/Ruelle-type results, and Przytycki--Urbański. It says a general characterization beyond expanding settings is unresolved. Its discussion of arbitrary invariant measures is broader than the extracted question's positive-entropy domain and is not substituted for it. Provenance records no comprehensive modern resolution check of the 1992 source.
