Math-4381

Mirror Families from Log Calabi–Yau Pairs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $D_Y$ for the boundary divisor and set \[ U=Y\setminus\operatorname{Supp}(D_Y). \] A simple normal-crossings boundary is a reduced divisor whose components are smooth and meet locally as distinct coordinate hyperplanes. The usual linear-triviality condition for a log Calabi--Yau pair is $K_Y+D_Y\sim 0$; the supplied record does not specify its convention for this condition. For an $n$-dimensional pair, a common meaning of maximal boundary is the existence of a zero-dimensional boundary stratum, where $n$ components meet. This convention is not explicitly supplied either. The hypothesis that $D_Y$ supports an ample divisor asserts the existence of an ample divisor supported on the boundary; the allowed divisor coefficients and whether its support must equal the entire boundary are unspecified.

Intended question, with unresolved qualifications. For every simple normal-crossings Calabi--Yau pair $(Y,D_Y)$ with maximal boundary and with $D_Y$ supporting an ample divisor, does the Gross--Hacking--Keel curve-counting algebra construction produce a flat family of affine log Calabi--Yau varieties such that its torus quotient depends only on $U$ and is a mirror family of $U$? Here dependence only on $U$ expresses the proposed independence from the choice of compactifying pair $(Y,D_Y)$.

This is a statement of the supplied intended target, not a fully specified conjecture. The record supplies neither the ground field and dimension restrictions nor the curve-counting algebra, its coefficient ring, the family base, the acting torus, the quotient construction, or a mathematical criterion for being a mirror family. It also does not specify the equivalence under which the quotient is to depend only on $U$. Consequently these data cannot be replaced by a precise quantified construction from the supplied evidence alone.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Gross--Hacking--Keel curve-counting construction gives a flat affine log Calabi--Yau family whose torus quotient is an intrinsic mirror of the complement $U$. Essential construction data and conventions remain unspecified. The supplied review also reports an incomplete source extraction, despite the record's separate claim that the statement was verified.
