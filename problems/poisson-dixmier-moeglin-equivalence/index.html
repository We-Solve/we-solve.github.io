Math-4705

Poisson Dixmier–Moeglin Equivalence

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field and let $A$ be a commutative, unital, finitely generated $k$-algebra equipped with a Poisson bracket. Thus $\{-,-\}:A\times A\to A$ is a $k$-bilinear Lie bracket satisfying $\{a,bc\}=\{a,b\}c+b\{a,c\}$. These are conventions for interpreting the terminology; the supplied record does not specify the base field or additional hypotheses on $A$.

An ideal $I\subseteq A$ is a Poisson ideal if $\{A,I\}\subseteq I$. Write \[ \operatorname{PSpec}(A)=\{P\subsetneq A:P\text{ is a prime ideal and a Poisson ideal}\}, \] with the topology induced from $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$. The Poisson core of an ideal $J$ is the largest Poisson ideal contained in $J$, namely \[ \operatorname{Pcore}(J)=\sum_{\substack{I\subseteq J\\ I\text{ a Poisson ideal}}}I. \] For $P\in\operatorname{PSpec}(A)$, use the following conventions:

  • $P$ is Poisson-primitive if $P=\operatorname{Pcore}(\mathfrak m)$ for some maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$.
  • The bracket on $A/P$ extends to its fraction field $K_P=\operatorname{Frac}(A/P)$. Its Poisson center is \[ Z_{\mathrm{Pois}}(K_P)=\{z\in K_P:\{z,u\}=0\text{ for every }u\in K_P\}. \] The ideal $P$ is Poisson-rational if $Z_{\mathrm{Pois}}(K_P)$ is algebraic over $k$.
  • $P$ is Poisson-locally-closed if $\{P\}$ is locally closed in $\operatorname{PSpec}(A)$, meaning it is the intersection of an open subset and a closed subset of that space.
The Poisson Dixmier--Moeglin equivalence for $A$, with these conventions, is the assertion \[ \forall P\in\operatorname{PSpec}(A),\qquad P\text{ is Poisson-primitive} \ \Longleftrightarrow\ P\text{ is Poisson-rational} \ \Longleftrightarrow\ P\text{ is Poisson-locally-closed}. \] Question represented by the record. Does this equivalence hold for affine Poisson algebras in the intended setting, and under what hypotheses on $k$ and $A$ do these three classes coincide? The intended setting is unresolved: the record specifies neither characteristic nor algebraic closedness of $k$, nor further restrictions on $A$. Consequently, this draft defines the equivalence precisely for specified $(k,A)$, but does not silently turn the extraction into an unrestricted assertion over all fields and all affine Poisson algebras. The universal validity question and the request to determine sufficient or necessary conditions are distinct targets, although the source presents them together.

Short English statement

For each Poisson prime ideal, compare being the Poisson core of a maximal ideal, having fraction-field Poisson center algebraic over the base field, and being a locally closed point of the Poisson prime spectrum. The problem asks when these conditions are equivalent. The intended field hypotheses and algebra class are missing, so the scope requires human mathematical review.
