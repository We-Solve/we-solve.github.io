Normalized Ext Limits and Epsilon Multiplicity

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a particular ring $S$ and ideal $I\subseteq S$, whose definitions and hypotheses are absent from the supplied excerpt. They must be recovered from the cited report; the question must not be interpreted as quantified over all rings and ideals.

For a positive integer $d$, let $I^d$ denote the ordinary ideal power. Write $\operatorname{Ext}^3_S(M,S)$ for the third right derived functor of $\operatorname{Hom}_S(M,S)$, and $\ell_S(M)$ for the composition length of an $S$-module $M$, when finite. Provided the indicated lengths are finite for all sufficiently large positive integers $d$, put \[ a_d=\frac{\ell_S\!\left(\operatorname{Ext}^3_S(S/I^d,S)\right)}{d^6}. \] The source question is whether \[ \lim_{\substack{d\to\infty\\ d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}}}a_d \] exists for the intended $S$ and $I$. The supplied excerpt does not establish the required finiteness hypotheses or specify whether existence means a finite real limit or allows an extended real limit. Consequently this is an incomplete formulation pending recovery of the original setup.

The excerpt identifies this question as an instance of higher $\varepsilon$-multiplicities, citing its reference [5]. It separately reports that, when $\operatorname{char}(k)=2$, calculations in its reference [7, Theorem 1.9] imply that \[ \lim_{\substack{d\to\infty\\ d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}}} \frac{\ell_S\!\left(\operatorname{Ext}^3_S(I^d/I^{d+1},S)\right)}{d^5} \] does not exist. This is supplied contextual information about a different sequence, not a conclusion about $a_d$. The field $k$, its relationship to $S$, and the characteristic restrictions on the target question are not specified. The passage also describes explicit intermediate cohomology calculations for line bundles on $X$, or sheaves rendered as $D_dR(e)$ on $P$, as open in general and related to determinantal ideals; these objects are undefined in the supplied excerpt.

Short English statement

For the specific ring and ideal intended in the report, ask whether the lengths of $\operatorname{Ext}^3_S(S/I^d,S)$, divided by $d^6$, have a limit as positive integers $d$ tend to infinity. The setup and length finiteness assumptions are missing. The supplied record labels the question open and relates it to higher $\varepsilon$-multiplicities; these are source qualifications requiring review.
