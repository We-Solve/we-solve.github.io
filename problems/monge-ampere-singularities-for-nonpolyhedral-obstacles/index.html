Definitions and mathematical statement

For an obstacle represented by a function $\psi$, its graph is $\operatorname{graph}(\psi)=\{(x,\psi(x)):x\in\operatorname{dom}\psi\}$. A convex polytope is the convex hull of finitely many points in a finite-dimensional real affine space. These definitions explain the terminology without specifying an obstacle class absent from the record.

Research problem. Investigate singularity questions for Monge--Ampère obstacle problems when the graphs of the obstacles are not necessarily convex polytopes. The supplied original formulation asks to investigate these singularity questions; the supplied clean formulation asks to develop singular solutions in this setting. The intended scope therefore includes obstacles beyond those whose graphs are convex polytopes, but the record does not specify a precise existence, construction, or singularity-classification assertion.
