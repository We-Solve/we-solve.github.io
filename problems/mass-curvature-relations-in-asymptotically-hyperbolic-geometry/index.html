Mass Curvature Relations in Asymptotically Hyperbolic Geometry

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns asymptotically hyperbolic manifolds and two objects called the conformal mass covariant and the Weyl mass covariant. It supplies neither definitions of these covariants nor precise hypotheses defining their existing domains or the intended broader setting.

Statement of the supplied research problem. Extend the conformal and Weyl mass covariants to broader classes of asymptotically hyperbolic manifolds, retaining their existing values wherever they are already defined, and establish a tight relation between a given mass covariant and an associated curvature operator. The edited statement describes the desired relation as sharp and asks for such a relation for each mass.

Unresolved mathematical specifications. A fully quantified formulation requires the dimension, geometric category, asymptotic model, decay and regularity assumptions, and boundary conditions; the definitions, normalizations, transformation laws, and original domains of the two mass covariants; and the associated curvature operators. None of these is specified in the supplied record. The meaning of a tight or sharp relation is also unspecified: no identity, inequality, equivalence, optimal constant, or equality condition is given. Consequently, this is a statement of the research target with unresolved qualifications, rather than a uniquely determined theorem or conjecture.

Short English statement

The problem asks for extensions of conformal and Weyl mass covariants in asymptotically hyperbolic geometry, together with precise relations to curvature operators. The supplied record leaves the covariants, admissible geometric hypotheses, operators, and meaning of sharpness undefined, so a complete mathematical formulation requires source clarification.
