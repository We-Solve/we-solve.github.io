Math-3378

Lyapunov Positivity for Smooth Quasiperiodic Schrödinger Operators

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, let $v:\mathbb T\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be the smooth potential, and let $\omega$ satisfy the source's weak Diophantine condition. In the standard discrete one-frequency convention, the Schrödinger operator is \[ (H_{\lambda,\omega,\theta}u)_n=u_{n+1}+u_{n-1}+\lambda v(\theta+n\omega)u_n \quad(n\in{\mathbb{Z}}), \] acting on $\ell^2({\mathbb{Z}})$, with $\theta\in\mathbb T$ and real coupling $\lambda$. For $E\in{\mathbb{R}}$, define \[ A_{E,\lambda}(x)=\begin{pmatrix}E-\lambda v(x)&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}, \quad A_N(x)=A_{E,\lambda}(x+(N-1)\omega)\cdots A_{E,\lambda}(x), \] and the integrated Lyapunov exponent \[ L(E,\lambda,\omega)=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\int_{\mathbb T}\log\|A_N(x)\|\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] This makes the standard operator and averaged-exponent conventions explicit; it does not assert uniform pointwise phase convergence.

The source conjecture is that for every admissible smooth $v$ satisfying its transversality condition and every admissible $\omega$, there exists a finite threshold $\lambda_0$, which may be enlarged to at least one, such that \[ \forall\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}\ (|\lambda|>\lambda_0)\quad\forall E\in{\mathbb{R}},\qquad L(E,\lambda,\omega)\geq\tfrac14\log|\lambda|>0. \] The same threshold works for all energies, including energies not restricted to the spectrum, and both coupling signs are retained. The coefficient $1/4$ is part of the stated target.

Neither transversality nor weak Diophantine condition is defined in the record; one must not replace them by a Morse condition, a guessed derivative lower bound, or a standard stronger Diophantine inequality. Smooth ordinarily means $C^\infty$, but any finite differentiability threshold must be recovered from the source. The literal quantifier order allows $\lambda_0$ to depend on $v$ and $\omega$. The assessment calls the target nonperturbative, which might intend frequency-independent threshold control; that stronger dependence requirement is not explicitly supplied and must be checked.

The supplied literature assessment checked 2026-08-21 reports no theorem in the full stated smooth/weak-frequency generality, noting stronger analyticity, regularity, or frequency hypotheses in known results. It does not reproduce those result hypotheses.

Short English statement

For the intended transverse smooth potential and weak Diophantine frequency, ask whether sufficiently large coupling gives an energy-uniform Lyapunov lower bound $\frac14\log|\lambda|$. The two central hypotheses and any frequency-independent threshold requirement are undefined.
