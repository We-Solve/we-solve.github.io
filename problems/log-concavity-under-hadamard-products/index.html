Log-Concavity under Hadamard Products

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not determine an unambiguous reconstruction of the cited open question. The following makes its literal coefficientwise interpretation precise under an explicitly stated nonnegative real coefficient convention; that convention is not specified in the source excerpt.

Let $I=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. A sequence $a=(a_n)_{n\in I}$ of nonnegative real numbers is log-concave if \[ a_n^2\geq a_{n-1}a_{n+1}\qquad(n\geq1). \] Some conventions additionally require no internal zeros: whenever $i<j<k$ and $a_i a_k>0$, one requires $a_j>0$. The supplied record does not specify whether this additional condition is intended.

For formal power series \[ A(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nt^n,\qquad B(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}b_nt^n, \] define their coefficientwise Hadamard product by \[ (A\odot B)(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nb_nt^n. \] No analytic convergence is needed for this definition. Polynomials can be treated as such series by extending their coefficient sequences by zeros.

Literal question in the supplied cleaned statement. For every pair of nonnegative real log-concave sequences $a,b$, is the sequence $c_n=a_nb_n$ log-concave, that is, does \[ (a_nb_n)^2\geq(a_{n-1}b_{n-1})(a_{n+1}b_{n+1})\qquad(n\geq1) \] hold? The record also requests a characterization of the hypotheses under which preservation holds, but does not specify the larger class of coefficients, sequences, or operations within which that characterization is sought.

Unresolved source qualification. The original excerpt introduces sequences satisfying $a_n=p(n)$ for every $n\geq0$, where $p$ is a polynomial, and contains a corrupted generating-function display involving $W(p)$ and a denominator of exponent $\deg p+1$. This raises a materially different possible target: log-concavity of a generating-function numerator associated with polynomial evaluations. The excerpt does not reliably define $W$, its coefficient domain, or the relevant Hadamard operation on such numerators. In particular, coefficientwise multiplication of two numerators must not be identified without justification with the numerator associated with multiplying their evaluation sequences. The literal formulation above therefore cannot be certified as the intended statement of $[1,\text{ Question }6.1]$. The supplied open label is retained as source metadata, not asserted here as a verified mathematical fact.

Short English statement

The cleaned record asks whether multiplying corresponding terms of two log-concave coefficient sequences preserves log-concavity, and seeks the relevant hypotheses. Its original excerpt also discusses polynomial evaluation sequences and generating-function numerators, leaving the intended object of log-concavity unclear. This statement-only draft records a precise literal interpretation and preserves that unresolved distinction for human review.
