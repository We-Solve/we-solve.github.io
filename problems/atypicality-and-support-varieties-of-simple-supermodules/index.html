Atypicality and Support Varieties of Simple Supermodules

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns a Lie superalgebra $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak g_{\bar 0}\oplus\mathfrak g_{\bar 1}$, a simple highest-weight $\mathfrak g$-module $L(\lambda)$, and an associated Lie subsuperalgebra $\mathfrak e\subseteq\mathfrak g$, whose even part is $\mathfrak e_{\bar 0}$. The record does not specify the ground field, the precise class of Lie superalgebras, the module category, or the construction of $\mathfrak e$. Consequently, the following definitions describe a conventional interpretation conditional on those choices, rather than supplying missing source hypotheses.

In a root-theoretic setting where the usual definition of atypicality applies, fix positive even and odd roots $\Delta_{\bar 0}^{+}$ and $\Delta_{\bar 1}^{+}$, together with the bilinear form $(\cdot,\cdot)$ on weights used in that theory, and put \[ \rho=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Delta_{\bar 0}^{+}}\alpha-\frac12\sum_{\beta\in\Delta_{\bar 1}^{+}}\beta. \] The atypicality $\operatorname{atyp}(\lambda)$ is the largest integer $r\geq 0$ for which there are linearly independent odd roots $\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_r$ satisfying \[ (\beta_i,\beta_j)=0\quad(1\leq i,j\leq r), \qquad (\lambda+\rho,\beta_i)=0\quad(1\leq i\leq r). \] In particular, each $\beta_i$ is isotropic. The root data and hypotheses needed for this interpretation must be confirmed from the cited source.

For a relative cohomological interpretation, write \[ R_{\mathfrak e}=H^{\bullet}(\mathfrak e,\mathfrak e_{\bar 0};k), \qquad I_M=\operatorname{Ann}_{R_{\mathfrak e}} \operatorname{Ext}^{\bullet}_{(\mathfrak e,\mathfrak e_{\bar 0})}(M,M), \] where $k$ is the ground field and the action is the usual relative cohomology action. Whenever the intended theory supplies this commutative, finitely generated coordinate ring, its cohomological support variety is \[ V_{(\mathfrak e,\mathfrak e_{\bar 0})}(M) =\operatorname{MaxSpec}(R_{\mathfrak e}/I_M), \] with dimension understood as Krull dimension. For $M=L(\lambda)$, this construction uses restriction to $\mathfrak e$. The supplied wording also calls the variety a rank variety; identifying these constructions requires hypotheses and an identification not given in the record.

The source question is whether, for every simple highest-weight module in the intended scope, \[ \operatorname{atyp}(\lambda) =\dim V_{(\mathfrak e,\mathfrak e_{\bar 0})}\left(L(\lambda)\right). \] The universal quantifier is over the source's intended Lie superalgebras and modules, whose precise scope remains unresolved. The supplied literature assessment mentions both $\mathfrak{gl}(m\mid n)$ and simple basic classical Lie superalgebras, but does not provide the hypotheses needed to turn these mentions into a fully specified common formulation.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the atypicality of each intended simple highest-weight supermodule equals the dimension of its associated support or rank variety. The ground field, module category, class of Lie superalgebras, and choice of $\mathfrak e$ require clarification. Supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved, reporting the equality for $\mathfrak{gl}(m\mid n)$ and a conjectural or case-dependent situation for all simple basic classical Lie superalgebras; these claims are not independently verified here.
