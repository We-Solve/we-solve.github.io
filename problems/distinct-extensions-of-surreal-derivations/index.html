Math-15045

Distinct Extensions of Surreal Derivations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbf{No}$ denote the proper class of surreal numbers with its usual field operations, and let ${\mathbb{R}}$ denote its embedded real subfield. A field derivation on $\mathbf{No}$ is a class function $D:\mathbf{No}\to\mathbf{No}$ satisfying \[ D(x+y)=D(x)+D(y),\qquad D(xy)=xD(y)+yD(x) \quad(x,y\in\mathbf{No}). \] The source asks about surreal derivations, but the supplied record does not specify the additional axioms intended by that term. The field-derivation identities above are necessary background and do not constitute a replacement definition of the intended surreal derivations.

Write $K={\mathbb{R}}\langle L\rangle$ for the subfield designated in the source. Neither $L$ nor the closure operation represented by $\langle L\rangle$ is defined in the supplied record. Subject to recovering those definitions and the intended axioms for surreal derivations, the question is whether there exist surreal derivations $D_1,D_2:\mathbf{No}\to\mathbf{No}$ such that \[ \left(\forall a\in K,\ D_1(a)=D_2(a)\right) \quad\text{and}\quad \left(\exists x\in\mathbf{No},\ D_1(x)\ne D_2(x)\right). \] Quantification over these derivations is understood as quantification over class functions. Thus the question concerns distinct global derivations having a common restriction to the designated subfield. The original wording also asks more generally about distinct extensions of a surreal derivation to all of $\mathbf{No}$, without specifying the initial domain or the initial derivation. No universally quantified assertion about all initial derivations is supplied. The missing definitions prevent a fully self-contained formulation.

Short English statement

Can two distinct surreal derivations on all surreal numbers agree at every element of the designated subfield ${\mathbb{R}}\langle L\rangle$? The supplied record leaves $L$, the subfield-construction notation, and the precise axioms for a surreal derivation undefined, so these must be recovered before mathematical review.
