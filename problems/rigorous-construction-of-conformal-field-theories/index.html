A cutoff Gaussian construction test.
Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ be a real centered Gaussian vector with a positive semidefinite covariance matrix $C$. Define normalized imaginary vertex factors $V_j=\exp(i\alpha_jX_j+\alpha_j^2 C_{jj}/2)$, for real $\alpha_j$. Completing the square in the Gaussian characteristic function gives \[ \mathbb E\prod_jV_j =\exp\left(-\sum_{i<j}\alpha_i\alpha_j C_{ij}\right). \] Singular covariance matrices follow by adding $\epsilon I$ and taking the explicit formula's limit. This defines every finite-cutoff correlation without a formal path integral.
A covariance and scaling check.
If a proposed two-dimensional cutoff has off-diagonal limit $C_{ij}=-\log|x_i-x_j|$ up to controlled background terms, its displayed factor becomes $\prod_{i<j}|x_i-x_j|^{\alpha_i\alpha_j}$. For neutral charges $\sum_i\alpha_i=0$, dilation by $\lambda>0$ gives the factor \[ \lambda^{\sum_{i<j}\alpha_i\alpha_j} =\lambda^{-\frac12\sum_i\alpha_i^2}. \] The algebra thus passes a scaling test with individual dimensions $\alpha_i^2/2$. It is also invariant under simultaneous permutations of the insertions and their charges.
Where bootstrap is still missing.
The off-diagonal logarithmic formula is a conditional covariance ansatz here, not a proved measure construction on every domain. Permutation symmetry of finitely many correlations does not establish convergence of operator-product expansions, completeness of intermediate states or crossing in a conformal-block decomposition. The first gap is a compatible ultraviolet and zero-mode limit producing the required positive Hilbert-space or probabilistic structure and convergent expansions. The broad source construction and bootstrap programme is not settled by this elementary Gaussian consistency calculation.
