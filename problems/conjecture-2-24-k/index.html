Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An $m$-strand string link is a proper embedding of $m$ oriented intervals in $D^2\times[0,1]$ joining fixed corresponding endpoints on the bottom and top disks, considered up to ambient isotopy fixing the boundary. Let $d\geq1$. Standard $C_d$-equivalence is generated by isotopy and degree-$d$ tree-clasper surgeries; in the usual tree convention a degree-$d$ tree has $d+1$ leaves and $d-1$ trivalent nodes.

For an abelian group $A$, an invariant $v$ is extended to singular string links by the crossing-difference rule $v(L_\times)=v(L_+)-v(L_-)$. It has degree $<d$ if the iterated extension vanishes on string links with $d$ transverse double points.

The conjecture is that, for every $m,d\geq1$ and every pair of $m$-strand string links $L,L'$, \[ L\sim_{C_d}L' \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \forall\text{ abelian groups }A\ \forall A\text{-valued finite-type }v \text{ of degree }<d,\quad v(L)=v(L'). \] The coefficient quantifier is genuinely over all abelian groups; it is not restricted to rational-valued invariants. The source does not separately state a framing convention, which should be matched to its string-link and clasper definitions.

The source labels this Conjecture 2.24, attributed to Habiro with references [165] and [153, "Theorem 5"]. The supplied research summary calls the full assertion a theorem and cites Habiro (2000), Theorem 5.1. Its scope must be checked against the complete all-string-links, all-abelian-coefficients biconditional rather than inferred from a theorem number or from a knot-only/single-direction result. This draft retains the source conjecture and the reported resolution attribution separately.

Short English statement

Are two string links $C_d$-equivalent exactly when every finite-type invariant of degree below $d$, with values in any abelian group, agrees on them? Preserve the full biconditional and coefficient quantifier; the supplied theorem attribution needs scope checking.
