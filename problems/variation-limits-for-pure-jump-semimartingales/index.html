Math-6593

Variation Limits for Pure-Jump Semimartingales

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for an extension of a compound-Poisson variation limit to a suitably general pure-jump semimartingale. It does not specify the class of semimartingales or the additional hypotheses, so the following records the proposed limit and its unresolved qualifications rather than a fully quantified conjecture.

The starting moving-average representation is \[ X_t=X_0+\int_{-\infty}^{t}g(t-s)\,\mathrm dL_s, \qquad g(x)=x^\alpha f(x),\qquad 0<\alpha<\tfrac12, \] where initially $L$ is a pure-jump Lévy process and $f$ is smooth, has exponential decay, and satisfies $f(0)\ne0$. The kernel is used for nonnegative arguments. The proposed extension replaces $L$ by a pure-jump semimartingale. The stochastic integral, its existence, and the required negative-time extension of the driver remain to be specified.

For positive mesh sizes $\Delta_n$, define the realized quadratic variation statistic \[ V_t^n(X)=\sum_{i=1}^{\lfloor t/\Delta_n\rfloor} \left|X_{i\Delta_n}-X_{(i-1)\Delta_n}\right|^2, \qquad t\ge0, \] with an empty sum equal to zero. The intended asymptotic regime appears to be $\Delta_n\to0$; this is not explicitly stated in the supplied formula.

Write $x_+=\max\{x,0\}$, let $T_k$ denote jump times of the driver, and write $\Delta L_{T_k}=L_{T_k}-L_{T_k-}$. Set \[ H_\alpha(u)=\sum_{l=1}^{\infty} \left|(l-u)_+^\alpha-(l-1-u)_+^\alpha\right|^2. \] The source displays auxiliary variables $U_k$ without defining their law or independence properties. Under the conventional interpretation, which requires confirmation, these are independent $\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$ marks, independent of the original probability space, constructed on an extension of that space. With this interpretation, the displayed target is \[ W_t=f(0)^2\sum_{k:T_k\in[0,t]} |\Delta L_{T_k}|^2H_\alpha(U_k). \] The source asserts that, in the compound-Poisson case, \[ \Delta_n^{-2\alpha}V_t^n(X) \xrightarrow{d_{\mathrm{st}}}W_t. \] For a fixed $t$, stable convergence relative to an original sigma-field $\mathcal F$ means that \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}{\mathbb{E}}\!\left[Z\,\varphi\!\left(\Delta_n^{-2\alpha}V_t^n(X)\right)\right] =\widetilde{{\mathbb{E}}}\!\left[Z\,\varphi(W_t)\right] \] for every bounded $\mathcal F$-measurable random variable $Z$ and every bounded continuous $\varphi:{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{R}}$, where $\widetilde{{\mathbb{E}}}$ denotes expectation on the extension. The source does not identify $\mathcal F$ or specify whether a stronger process-level assertion is intended.

Requested extension. Determine and state suitable hypotheses on a sufficiently general pure-jump semimartingale driver and on the moving-average construction under which the same displayed stable limit holds. Neither the intended breadth of this class nor its required hypotheses are supplied; no particular replacement theorem is asserted here.

There are additional unresolved qualifications. The scalar domains of $L$, $X$, and $f$, and the exact meaning of smoothness and exponential decay, are unspecified. In particular, the factor $f(0)^2$ is retained exactly as supplied; a complex-valued formulation would require clarification. Also, if $X_0$ in the moving-average formula literally denotes the value of $X$ at zero, substituting $t=0$ forces the integral there to vanish, a condition absent from the record. The notation may instead intend an additive offset. Finally, the target includes jumps at time zero, while the statistic uses increments after zero; this endpoint convention requires confirmation.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks when a stated compound-Poisson limit for rescaled squared increments of a moving average extends to general pure-jump semimartingales. The intended driver class and hypotheses are missing, and the auxiliary marks, convergence mode, initial-value notation, and time-zero convention need clarification. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
