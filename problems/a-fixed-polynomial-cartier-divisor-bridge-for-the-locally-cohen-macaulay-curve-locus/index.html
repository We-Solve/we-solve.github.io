Math-1325

A fixed-polynomial Cartier-divisor bridge for the locally Cohen-Macaulay curve locus

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks whether the Hilbert scheme of locally Cohen--Macaulay curves in ${\mathbb{P}}^3$ is connected. A meaningful version fixes a Hilbert polynomial: over an algebraically closed field $k$, let \[ H_{d,g}^{\mathrm{LCM}}\subset\operatorname{Hilb}^{dt+1-g}({\mathbb{P}}^3_k) \] be the locus of pure one-dimensional locally Cohen--Macaulay subschemes, with no embedded or isolated zero-dimensional components. The question is whether each nonempty such locus is connected. The source does not explicitly state the field or fixed polynomial; the disjoint union over all polynomials cannot be the intended nontrivial assertion.

The aid's sufficient bridge uses a smooth geometrically integral surface $S\subset{\mathbb{P}}^3_k$, its hyperplane class $H$, and a nontrivial effective divisor class $D$. The complete linear system $|D|={\mathbb{P}} H^0(S,\mathcal O_S(D))$ parametrizes effective Cartier divisors and supplies an irreducible flat family wholly inside one locus $H_{d,g}^{\mathrm{LCM}}$, with \[ d=D\cdot H,\qquad g=1+\frac{D^2+D\cdot K_S}{2}. \] Every member remains locally Cohen--Macaulay, including reducible and nonreduced divisors. Such a family does not introduce embedded or isolated points and does not change the Hilbert polynomial.

For fixed $(d,g)$, form a graph whose vertices are images in $H_{d,g}^{\mathrm{LCM}}$ of such complete linear systems. Put an edge between two vertices when a specified curve lies in both images, hence is an effective Cartier divisor on both supporting surfaces. The aid reports that the union of images belonging to any connected subgraph is connected. Thus an explicit covering by a connected overlap graph would certify connectedness of the covered locus. Existence of some connected subgraph alone does not establish connectedness of the entire Hilbert locus.

The summary gives verified examples from smooth quadrics and complete intersections. On a smooth quadric $S\cong{\mathbb{P}}^1\times{\mathbb{P}}^1$, an effective nonzero class $(a,b)$ has \[ d=a+b,\qquad g=(a-1)(b-1),\qquad a,b\geq0,\ a+b>0, \] and its complete linear system gives such a connected family. On a smooth surface of degree $a$, the class $bH$, $b\geq1$, gives complete intersections of type $(a,b)$, with \[ d=ab,\qquad g=1+\frac{ab(a+b-4)}2. \] These are family-level connectedness certificates. The record supplies no covering or connecting graph for all locally Cohen--Macaulay curves of a fixed polynomial, and the global question remains unresolved in the aid's assessment.

Short English statement

The question concerns connectedness while keeping the degree and genus fixed and avoiding embedded points. Complete linear systems of Cartier divisors provide connected families that stay inside that locus. Shared curves can join these families in an overlap graph, but no graph covering the full Hilbert locus is supplied.
