Math-4824

Functional Inequality on the Boolean Cube

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $Q_n=\{0,1\}^n$ be the Boolean cube. Its dimension must be a nonnegative integer; the supplied record does not explicitly specify the range of $n$, including whether dimension zero is intended. For $x,y\in Q_n$, interpret $x\sim y$ as meaning that $x$ and $y$ differ in exactly one coordinate. Let \[ \mathcal E_n=\left\{\{x,y\}:x,y\in Q_n,\ x\sim y\right\}. \] For $f:Q_n\to{\mathbb{R}}$, the two possible edge-counting conventions are \[ E_{\mathrm{unord}}(f,f)=\sum_{\{x,y\}\in\mathcal E_n}(f(x)-f(y))^2, \qquad E_{\mathrm{ord}}(f,f)=\sum_{x\in Q_n}\sum_{\substack{y\in Q_n\\y\sim x}}(f(x)-f(y))^2 =2E_{\mathrm{unord}}(f,f). \] The supplied record writes $E(f,f)=\sum_{x\sim y}(f(x)-f(y))^2$ without specifying which convention is intended. This ambiguity affects the proposed inequality and remains unresolved.

Conjecture as supplied. For every intended dimension $n$, every nonempty subset $A\subseteq Q_n$, and every function $f:Q_n\to{\mathbb{R}}$, \[ E(f,f)+4\sum_{x\in Q_n}f(x)^2 \geq 2\left( \frac{\log_2(2^n/|A|)}{|A|} \sum_{x\in A}|f(x)| \right)^2. \] Here $|A|$ is the cardinality of $A$, $\log_2$ is the base-two logarithm, and the displayed vertex sums are unnormalized sums. The supplied formulation imposes no support condition on $f$, no normalization of $f$, and no further restriction on the nonempty set $A$. No such hypothesis has been added. A definitive formulation requires resolution of the edge-counting convention and confirmation of the intended dimension range.

The registry labels this a conjecture with status open. Its supplied literature assessment says that known logarithmic-Sobolev and Talagrand inequalities establish only weaker or supported cases, without specifying those cases. These are supplied source qualifications, not independently verified mathematical or literature conclusions.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture gives a lower bound for a Boolean-cube energy plus four times the squared $\ell^2$ norm, using the average of $|f|$ on an arbitrary nonempty subset and its base-two logarithmic size deficit. The edge-counting convention and the dimension range require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
