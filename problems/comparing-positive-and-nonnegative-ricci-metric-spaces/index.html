Math-15060

Comparing Positive and Nonnegative Ricci Metric Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a fixed smooth manifold $M$, let $\mathcal R(M)$ denote the set of smooth Riemannian metrics on $M$. Define \[ \begin{aligned} R^{>0}(M)&=\{g\in\mathcal R(M):\operatorname{Ric}_g(v,v)>0\text{ for every }x\in M\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xM\},\\ R^{\geq0}(M)&=\{g\in\mathcal R(M):\operatorname{Ric}_g(v,v)\geq0\text{ for every }x\in M\text{ and }v\in T_xM\}. \end{aligned} \] Here $\operatorname{Ric}_g$ is the Ricci curvature tensor of $g$. These definitions concern Ricci curvature, as specified in the supplied statement.

The source does not specify the class of manifolds, dimension, compactness, boundary conditions, or topology on the metric spaces. To interpret the following questions, fix a topology on $\mathcal R(M)$ and give both subsets the induced topology. No particular topology or class of manifolds is asserted to be the intended one.

The supplied problem asks how the homotopy types of these spaces compare. In particular, for the inclusion \[ i:R^{>0}(M)\hookrightarrow R^{\geq0}(M), \] is $i$ a homotopy equivalence? Explicitly, does there exist a continuous map $r:R^{\geq0}(M)\to R^{>0}(M)$ such that \[ r\circ i\simeq\operatorname{id}_{R^{>0}(M)},\qquad i\circ r\simeq\operatorname{id}_{R^{\geq0}(M)}? \] The source also asks whether $i$ is a "surjection on homotopy." One possible precise interpretation is that, for every $g\in R^{>0}(M)$ and every integer $k\geq1$, the induced map \[ i_*:\pi_k(R^{>0}(M),g)\longrightarrow\pi_k(R^{\geq0}(M),i(g)) \] is surjective. Whether this is required for every basepoint and degree, and whether it also includes surjectivity of \[ \pi_0(R^{>0}(M))\longrightarrow\pi_0(R^{\geq0}(M)), \] where $\pi_0$ denotes path components, is unresolved in the supplied record. Nonemptiness assumptions are also unspecified. Thus these are conditional formulations of the supplied questions; the record does not determine a fully quantified conjecture over manifolds.

Short English statement

The problem asks how the space of metrics with positive Ricci curvature compares with the space of metrics with nonnegative Ricci curvature, especially whether their inclusion is a homotopy equivalence or induces surjections on homotopy. The intended manifolds, topology, nonemptiness assumptions, and meaning of homotopy surjectivity require clarification. The supplied literature assessment discusses scalar curvature and a higher-index map, whose connection to this Ricci-curvature question is unspecified.
