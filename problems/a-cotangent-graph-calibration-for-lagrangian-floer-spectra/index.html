Math-1545

A cotangent-graph calibration for Lagrangian Floer spectra

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Question 1.42 records Peter Ozsvath's request for a spectrum theory, in the spirit of C. Manolescu, for Lagrangian intersections. Here a spectrum means a stable homotopy refinement of Floer theory, not an eigenvalue spectrum. The source does not specify the symplectic/Lagrangian class, brane and tangential data, grading, coefficients, analytic hypotheses or invariance requirements. The aid reports partial exact, tangential, parameterized and monotone-truncated constructions, not one universal theorem covering every Lagrangian pair.

Its concrete calibration is the following cotangent graph case. Let $Q$ be a closed smooth manifold, let $f:Q\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be Morse with a Morse--Smale auxiliary metric, and put \[ L_0=Q\subset T^*Q,\qquad L_\epsilon=\operatorname{graph}(\epsilon\,{\,\mathrm{d}} f),\qquad\epsilon>0. \] These are compact exact Lagrangians and intersect transversely at the critical points of $f$. For sufficiently small $\epsilon$ and compatible Floer data, the Morse--Floer comparison identifies the Floer complex with the Morse complex. Use ${\mathbb{F}}_2$ coefficients to avoid omitted orientation/branes assumptions and the cohomological grading agreeing with Morse degree.

The aid constructs a graph spectrum by the Morse-CJS realization, using the compactified gradient-flow data and coherent realization data that recover the Morse cell attachments of $Q$. In this convention its stable type is \[ \mathcal X_{\mathrm{graph}}(L_0,L_\epsilon) \simeq\Sigma^\infty Q_+, \] where $Q_+=Q\sqcup\{*\}$. Consequently \[ \widetilde H^k(\mathcal X_{\mathrm{graph}};{\mathbb{F}}_2) \simeq H^k(Q;{\mathbb{F}}_2) \simeq HF^k(L_0,L_\epsilon;{\mathbb{F}}_2). \] This specifies a Morse-calibrated special-case refinement and its normalization. It does not assert that every Floer moduli problem already carries the necessary compactification, framing, twisting and continuation data, nor that conventions using duals or suspension shifts can be compared without adjustment.

For $Q={\mathbb{C}}\mathrm P^2$, a perfect Morse function has generators in degrees $0,2,4$ and zero differential. Thus the naive wedge of sphere spectra \[ \mathbb S^0\vee\mathbb S^2\vee\mathbb S^4 \] has the same additive ${\mathbb{F}}_2$ cochain data as the graph Floer complex. It is nevertheless not stably equivalent to $\Sigma^\infty({\mathbb{C}}\mathrm P^2)_+$. Indeed for the degree-two generator $x$, \[ H^*({\mathbb{C}}\mathrm P^2;{\mathbb{F}}_2)={\mathbb{F}}_2[x]/(x^3),\quad|x|=2, \qquad\operatorname{Sq}^2(x)=x^2\ne0, \] whereas the corresponding operation from degree two to four on the wedge is zero. Stable equivalences preserve Steenrod operations. Therefore matching Floer generators, gradings and differential is insufficient: a proposal compatible with this Morse-CJS calibration must also retain the nontrivial stable attaching information detected by $\operatorname{Sq}^2$.

The aid gives this as a special-case construction and falsification test. It does not supply a complete universal spectrum construction, a comparison among all existing approaches, or full hypotheses for the exact/tangential/parameterized/monotone-truncated results it mentions.

Short English statement

The small cotangent graph pair has a Morse-calibrated spectrum $\Sigma^\infty Q_+$ refining its Floer cohomology. For $Q={\mathbb{C}}\mathrm P^2$, a nonzero Steenrod square rules out the naive wedge of generator spheres, even though the additive complex agrees. The general spectrum request retains additional geometric and analytic conditions.
