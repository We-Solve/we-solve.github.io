Math-2070

Geodesics in Gaussian-free-field random metrics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For definiteness of notation, let $V_n=\{1,\ldots,n\}^2$, $n\ge2$, with nearest-neighbor edges. Write $a_n=(1,n)$, $b_n=(n,n)$, $c_n=(1,1)$, and $d_n=(n,1)$ for its top and bottom corners. Given a real field $h_n$ on $V_n$, assign vertex weight $w_n(v)=\exp(h_n(v))$. With the usual vertex-sum convention, a path $P=(v_0,\ldots,v_m)$ has length $L_n(P)=\sum_{j=0}^{m}w_n(v_j)$, and \[ D_n(x,y)=\min_{P:x\to y}L_n(P)\qquad(x\ne y),\qquad D_n(x,x)=0. \] Choose shortest paths $\gamma_1(n)$ from $a_n$ to $b_n$ and $\gamma_2(n)$ from $c_n$ to $d_n$, with a specified tie rule if necessary. The first requested assertion is \[ \exists c>0\ \forall n\ge2:\quad {\mathbb{P}}\left(V(\gamma_1(n))\cap V(\gamma_2(n))\ne\varnothing\right)>c. \] Here the field is described in the source as a Gaussian free field "with no boundary conditions." On a finite connected graph the quadratic energy $\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}(h(u)-h(v))^2$ vanishes on constant fields, so a probability law with this free-boundary energy requires a choice such as a mean-zero representative, pinning, or a quotient by additive constants, as well as an energy normalization. Adding a common constant multiplies all weights and distances by the same positive factor and preserves the minimizing paths, but the record still does not specify the field law or normalized metric.

The remaining requests are to construct metric and geodesic scaling limits, determine geodesic dimension and height concentration, and identify any SLE parameter of limiting paths. The spatial and distance rescalings, convergence topology, definition of height, dimension convention, curve parametrization and boundary data are not supplied. These requests are retained as an unresolved program rather than assigned a particular Liouville metric or SLE law.

Short English statement

For the exponential of a Gaussian free field on a square grid, prove a positive lower bound, independent of grid size, for intersection of the top-to-top and bottom-to-bottom geodesics. The record also asks for scaling limits and their geometric properties. Its supplied partial-progress assessment discusses related GFF-weighted metrics without specifying enough data to identify these limits or an SLE parameter.
