Math-14928

Reconstructing Structures from Topological Symmetry Monoids

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be an infinite first-order structure with underlying set $|A|$. Define \[ \operatorname{Aut}(A)=\{g:|A|\to |A|:g\text{ is an automorphism of }A\} \] and \[ \operatorname{Emb}_{\mathrm{el}}(A)=\{e:|A|\to |A|:e\text{ is an elementary embedding}\}. \] Here an elementary embedding is an injective map such that, for every first-order formula $\varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ in the language of $A$ and every tuple $\bar a\in |A|^n$, \[ A\models\varphi(\bar a)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\models\varphi(e(\bar a)). \] Composition makes these objects a group and a monoid, respectively. For a conventional precise interpretation of their topology, give $|A|$ the discrete topology and give both objects the subspace topology inherited from $|A|^{|A|}$. Thus basic neighborhoods of a map $f$ specify its values on a finite subset $F\subseteq |A|$: \[ U(f,F)=\{h:h\!\upharpoonright_F=f\!\upharpoonright_F\}, \] where $h$ ranges over the relevant group or monoid. The supplied question does not explicitly specify this topology, so its use here is a convention requiring confirmation.

Reconstruction question. Determine what information about $A$ is determined by the topological group $\operatorname{Aut}(A)$, and separately by the topological monoid $\operatorname{Emb}_{\mathrm{el}}(A)$. More precisely, for a specified class $\mathcal K$ of infinite structures, determine the consequences for $A,B\in\mathcal K$ of each of \[ \operatorname{Aut}(A)\cong_{\mathrm{top.grp}}\operatorname{Aut}(B), \qquad \operatorname{Emb}_{\mathrm{el}}(A)\cong_{\mathrm{top.mon}}\operatorname{Emb}_{\mathrm{el}}(B). \] These denote algebraic isomorphisms that are homeomorphisms. Equivalently, determine which properties $P$ satisfy, for all $A,B\in\mathcal K$, \[ \operatorname{Aut}(A)\cong_{\mathrm{top.grp}}\operatorname{Aut}(B) \ \Longrightarrow\ (P(A)\Longleftrightarrow P(B)), \] and which satisfy the analogous implication for elementary-embedding monoids.

The accompanying talk title specifically concerns $\omega$-categorical structures: a structure is $\omega$-categorical when its complete first-order theory has exactly one countably infinite model up to isomorphism. The broader supplied statement concerns infinite structures without this restriction. The intended class $\mathcal K$, language restrictions, cardinalities, and precise reconstruction target are unresolved. In particular, the record does not specify a single conjectured implication to isomorphism or to bi-interpretability. Bi-interpretability means mutual first-order interpretability with the composite interpretations definably isomorphic to the respective original structures; the permitted use of parameters is also unspecified.

The supplied dated curation reports reconstruction up to bi-interpretability in the $\omega$-categorical setting and says that stronger exact reconstruction is not general. This is recorded as a qualified source assessment, not asserted here as a verified theorem or as a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

Ask which features of an infinite structure are determined by its automorphism group or its monoid of elementary self-embeddings, including their topology. The talk context singles out $\omega$-categorical structures, but the supplied question leaves the comparison class and the desired notion of reconstruction unspecified. This draft therefore states a reconstruction programme requiring human mathematical review.
