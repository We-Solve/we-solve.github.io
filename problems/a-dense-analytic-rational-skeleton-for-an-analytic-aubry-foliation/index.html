Math-1628

A dense analytic rational skeleton for an analytic Aubry foliation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$. Mather's question asks: if a real-analytic twist map $f:\mathbb T\times{\mathbb{R}}\to\mathbb T\times{\mathbb{R}}$ has a foliation by invariant curves $\Gamma_c$ which are Aubry--Mather sets, must every $\Gamma_c$ be real analytic? The question concerns regularity of individual leaves, not necessarily analytic dependence of the entire foliation on $c$.

The aid uses the following standard but stronger explicit interpretation. Assume $f$ is an exact symplectic positive twist diffeomorphism, isotopic to the identity, with lift $F=(X,Y)$ satisfying $\partial_yX>0$ and \[ f^*(y\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x)-y\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x={\,\mathrm{d}} S \] for a globally defined function $S$ on the cylinder. Suppose the leaves are whole graphs \[ \Gamma_c=\{(x,g_c(x)):x\in\mathbb T\},\qquad \int_{\mathbb T}g_c(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x=c, \] forming a continuous foliation, and each graph is the entire $c$-Aubry set of the associated variational problem. Here the $c$-modified discrete action subtracts $c$ times the lifted angular displacement; the Aubry condition includes the appropriate chain-recurrence condition, rather than merely an arbitrary invariant graph of minimizing orbits. The original wording does not explicitly fix exactness, twist sign, foliation topology, or which Aubry/Mather convention is intended.

Let $\rho(c)$ be the rotation number of the circle homeomorphism induced by $f$ on $\Gamma_c$, computed using the chosen lift. The supplied aid reports:

  1. If $\rho(c)=p/q\in{\mathbb{Q}}$ with $q>0$, every point of the whole Aubry leaf is periodic and \[ F^q(x,g_c(x))=(x+p,g_c(x)). \] The minimizing no-conjugate-points argument makes the fixed-displacement equation suitable for the analytic implicit-function theorem, so $g_c$ is real analytic.
  2. The map $c\mapsto\rho(c)$ is continuous and nondecreasing, with every nontrivial plateau having rational value. Consequently parameters with rational rotation number are dense, and the corresponding analytic leaves form a dense rational skeleton of the foliation.
These are reported partial results under the whole-Aubry-foliation interpretation. They do not prove analyticity of an irrational leaf: uniform real convergence of analytic graphs alone is insufficient. One sufficient additional estimate would be a common complex strip $|\operatorname{Im}z|<a$, $a>0$, on which a sequence of rational approximating graph functions has uniformly bounded holomorphic extensions. Together with convergence to $g_c$ on the real circle, normal-family compactness would then yield analyticity of that limit. Neither such uniform complex control for all irrational leaves nor a counterexample with degenerating control is supplied. No nonlinear foliation-conjugacy or global analytic-integrability conclusion is asserted.

Short English statement

Ask whether every invariant Aubry--Mather leaf of an analytic twist-map foliation is analytic. Under the aid's exact positive-twist, whole-Aubry interpretation, rational leaves give a dense analytic skeleton; the missing issue is analytic control at irrational limits.
