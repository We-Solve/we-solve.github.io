Problem 2: — For a fixed number R > 0, is there is compact subset K(R) of moduli space containing the projection of every Teichm¨…

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal T(S)=\mathcal T_{g,m}$ be the intended Teichmüller space, $\pi:\mathcal T(S)\to\mathcal M(S)$ the moduli projection, and $\Psi:\mathcal T(S)\to\mathcal C(S)$ the curve-complex map defined in the source's preceding discussion. Write $d$ for the target metric. The excerpt does not supply the choice or coarse conventions of $\Psi$, the surface complexity or the parameterization of geodesics. In the customary unit-Teichmüller-speed interpretation, let $\gamma:{\mathbb{R}}\to\mathcal T(S)$ be a complete geodesic and define the exact source condition \[d\left(\Psi(\gamma(s)),\Psi(\gamma(t))\right)\geq\frac{|s-t|}{R}-R\qquad(s,t\in{\mathbb{R}}).\tag{*}\] Only a lower bound is stated; no additional upper inequality is inserted.

The compound question asks:

  1. For every fixed $R>0$, is there a compact $K(R)\subset\mathcal M(S)$, independent of $\gamma$, such that every geodesic satisfying () has $\pi(\gamma({\mathbb{R}}))\subseteq K(R)$?
  2. Conversely, for every compact $K\subset\mathcal M(S)$, is there $R(K)>0$, independent of the geodesic, such that every complete geodesic with $\pi(\gamma({\mathbb{R}}))\subseteq K$ satisfies () for all real $s,t$? The source's notation "Mod($S$)" in this clause is interpreted as moduli space in light of its compact-set wording, not as a new sampling measure on the mapping class group.
  3. Analyze the $\mathcal C(S)$-images of Teichmüller geodesics associated with minimal filling geodesic laminations that are not uniquely ergodic. Minimal means every leaf is dense in the support; filling means the support meets every essential curve; nonunique ergodicity permits more than one nonproportional transverse measure on that support. The measure, transverse data and basepoint needed to determine an actual geodesic or ray must be specified: an unmeasured support alone does not single out one parameterized geodesic. No precise dynamical classification of these images is supplied.
The two compactness/coarse-progress implications have different uniformity quantifiers, both retained. The source does not say that a compact set may vary with each geodesic in the first clause, or that $R$ may vary with each geodesic in the second.

Short English statement

Ask whether uniform linear progress in the curve complex is equivalent, with the stated uniform constants, to staying in a compact part of moduli space. Also study the curve-complex images associated with filling but nonuniquely ergodic laminations.
