Math-15458

Mirror Symmetry for Maximally Degenerate Positive Calabi–Yau Pairs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record refers to pairs $(M,D)$ covered by a general construction of maximally degenerate positive Calabi--Yau pairs. It does not supply that construction or definitions of its hypotheses. Write $\mathcal G$ provisionally for the class of pairs covered by that construction; this notation records the intended scope but does not give an independently usable definition of the class.

Source problem. Formulate a single precise mirror-symmetry conjecture applicable to every pair $(M,D)\in\mathcal G$, and prove that conjecture in this full generality.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the ambient category or base field, the dimension or regularity of $M$, the nature of $D$, or the meanings of Calabi--Yau, positive, and maximally degenerate in the referenced construction. It also does not specify the mirror object, the structures to be compared, or the asserted correspondence. Consequently, the supplied evidence determines a research objective with universal scope, but does not determine a complete mathematical conjecture. The original statement restricts the scope to pairs covered by the referenced general construction; the shorter statement omits that restriction, and their equivalence cannot be established from the record.

Short English statement

Seek one precise mirror-symmetry conjecture and a proof covering every maximally degenerate positive Calabi--Yau pair admitted by the source's general construction. The construction, its hypotheses, and the intended mirror correspondence are missing, so a complete mathematical statement requires source clarification.
