Math-14517

Ax–Schanuel and Zilber–Pink for Abelian-Differential Strata

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An abelian differential on a smooth compact connected complex curve $X$ is a nonzero holomorphic one-form $\omega$ on $X$. In the usual unprojectivized convention, a stratum of abelian differentials parametrizes isomorphism classes of pairs $(X,\omega)$ with prescribed multiplicities of the zeros of $\omega$. The supplied record denotes its intended stratum by $S_\alpha$, but does not specify the genus, the admissible zero data $\alpha$, whether zeros are labelled, whether a connected component is intended, or whether differentials are considered up to scaling. This description therefore does not fix a convention for the source's $S_\alpha$.

The source poses two conjectural targets:

  1. Establish the Ax--Schanuel conjecture for $S_\alpha$. The supplied record does not state that conjecture's maps, ambient spaces, exceptional loci, hypotheses, or dimension inequality, so a precise Ax--Schanuel assertion cannot be recovered from this record alone.
  2. Establish the stated Zilber--Pink finiteness assertion. Write \[ \mathcal A_{\geq 3}(S_\alpha) :=\{s\in S_\alpha : s\text{ is an arithmetic point of rank at least }3\text{ in the source's sense}\}. \] The requested assertion is \[ \#\mathcal A_{\geq 3}(S_\alpha)<\infty. \] Here "arithmetic point" and its "rank" are unresolved source terms: the supplied record gives neither definition. This displayed formulation records the target but is not a complete intrinsic mathematical statement until those definitions and the convention for $S_\alpha$ are supplied. The record also leaves the range of strata to which the assertion is intended to apply implicit.

For context, the original excerpt attributes to the works labelled [Ler21] and [KL22] the first steps of a bi-algebraic heuristic for $S_\alpha$, motivated by its usefulness for diophantine questions on Shimura varieties. It reports a complete geometric characterization of arithmetic points; density of rank-one arithmetic points in the analytification $S_\alpha^{\mathrm{an}}$; and finiteness of arithmetic points on each algebraically primitive Teichmüller curve when the genus satisfies $g\geq 3$. It further reports linearity of bi-algebraic subvarieties in many cases, without specifying those cases. Finally, contrary to a hope attributed to [KL22], it attributes to [DM] examples of bi-algebraic subvarieties of $S_\alpha$ that are not linear. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified results or additional hypotheses that resolve the missing definitions.

Short English statement

The source asks for an Ax--Schanuel theorem for a stratum of abelian differentials and conjectures that the stratum has only finitely many arithmetic points of rank at least $3$. The relevant Ax--Schanuel formulation, the meanings of arithmetic point and rank, and the precise stratum convention are missing. Both targets therefore require clarification before a complete mathematical statement can be reviewed.
