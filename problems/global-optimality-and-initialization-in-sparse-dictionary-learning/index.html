Math-14562

Global Optimality and Initialization in Sparse Dictionary Learning

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation, a finite dictionary may be written as $D_\star=(d_1,\ldots,d_K)\in\mathbb{K}^{d\times K}$, with unit-norm atoms $\|d_j\|_2=1$. Here $d$ and $K$ are positive integers, and the source does not specify whether the scalar field $\mathbb{K}$ is ${\mathbb{R}}$ or ${\mathbb{C}}$. For $K\geq2$, its mutual coherence is \[ \mu(D_\star)=\max_{i\ne j}|\langle d_i,d_j\rangle|. \] A coefficient vector $c\in\mathbb{K}^K$ is $S$-sparse if $\|c\|_0:=|\{j:c_j\ne0\}|\leq S$, where $1\leq S\leq K$. The expression $x=D_\star c$ describes an exact sparse representation; it is notation for the setting, not an assertion that the intended problem excludes noise or approximately sparse coefficients. The source leaves the actual data-generating model unspecified.

The supplied problem asks the following three questions about sparse dictionary learning:

  1. Under the intended data-generating assumptions, is the generating dictionary $D_\star$ a global optimum of the criteria referred to as K-SVD and ITKsM?
  2. Are there efficient initialization strategies in the sparsity regime $S=O(\mu(D_\star)^{-2})$?
  3. Can the convergence radii of the algorithms referred to as ITKsM and ITKrM be extended to $O(1)$?
These questions retain unresolved qualifications from the source. The objective functions, their optimization directions, their admissible dictionary classes, and whether they are population or finite-sample criteria are not supplied. Neither the algorithm variants nor the initialization success criterion, computational model, sample requirements, or probability guarantees are specified. The regime $S=O(\mu^{-2})$ requires a positive coherence and an asymptotic family with specified uniform constants; neither is stated, and the zero-coherence case is unaddressed. The convergence metric, target accuracy, treatment of column permutations and signs or phases, and parameters governing the radius are also unspecified. In particular, an upper bound $O(1)$ alone does not express a radius bounded below by a positive constant independent of problem parameters. The intended meaning of this requested extension must therefore be clarified. The record supports these three qualified questions, but does not determine a unique fully quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether a generating sparse dictionary is globally optimal for the K-SVD and ITKsM criteria, whether efficient initialization is possible at sparsity $S=O(\mu^{-2})$, and whether ITKsM and ITKrM can converge from initializations at a constant-scale distance. The source leaves essential models, criteria, guarantees, and the meaning of constant-scale convergence unspecified; this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
