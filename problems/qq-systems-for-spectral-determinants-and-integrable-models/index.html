QQ Systems for Spectral Determinants and Integrable Models

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
An oper is a differential-geometric connection with additional flag and transversality structure; affine opers in the source generalize spectral ordinary differential equations. Spectral determinants encode their spectral or connection data. The $Q\widetilde Q$ system is the source's functional relation among two families of such functions, indexed by the relevant Lie-algebra data. On the integrable-model side, $Q$-operators or transfer data play an analogous spectral role.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
What structural mechanism produces the QQ-tilde system for spectral determinants of affine opers, and is there an analogous system on the integrable-model side?
Source scope and qualification.
The extraction does not include the functional relation, Lie type, shifts or normalizations. These are source-defined and must be supplied before using this record as a standalone equation; the two structural questions are retained without inventing a new QQ relation.

Short English statement

Explain the origin of the oper spectral-determinant functional relations and find their integrable-model counterpart.
