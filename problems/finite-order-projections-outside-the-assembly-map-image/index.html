Finite-Order Projections outside the Assembly-Map Image

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the topology of the group, the completion denoted by $C^*(G)$, or the assembly map. The following expresses its target under the discrete-group interpretation, while retaining the completion and assembly map as unresolved parameters.

Let $G$ be a discrete group and let $A=C^*(G)$ denote the intended group $C^*$-algebra. For a nonidentity element $g\in G$ of finite order $d\geq 2$, define \[ p_g=\frac{1}{d}\sum_{k=1}^{d}g^k\in{\mathbb{Q}} G. \] Its image in $A$ is a projection, and $[p_g]\in K_0(A)$ denotes its projection class. For this formulation, interpret the finite part as \[ K_0^{\mathrm{fin}}(A)=\big\langle [p_g]:g\in G\setminus\{e\},\ \operatorname{ord}(g)<\infty\big\rangle_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\subseteq K_0(A). \] This generating convention is not explicitly defined in the supplied record. Let $\mu:D\longrightarrow K_0(A)$ denote the intended assembly homomorphism; its domain $D$ and construction remain unspecified.

The proposed assertion is that, for every group $G$ in the intended scope, every integer $n\geq 1$, and every choice of nonidentity finite-order elements $g_1,\ldots,g_n\in G$ whose orders are pairwise distinct, the homomorphism \[ \Phi:{\mathbb{Z}}^n\longrightarrow K_0^{\mathrm{fin}}(A),\qquad (m_1,\ldots,m_n)\longmapsto\sum_{i=1}^{n}m_i[p_{g_i}] \] is injective and its image $H$ satisfies \[ H\cap\operatorname{im}(\mu)=\{0\}. \] Equivalently, these classes generate a free abelian subgroup of rank $n$, and every nonzero element of that subgroup lies outside the assembly-map image. This is an unresolved statement-only formulation: identifying the completion, assembly map, and group hypotheses is necessary to determine a unique conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether projections obtained by averaging over cyclic subgroups of distinct finite orders always give independent integral $K$-theory classes, with every nonzero integral combination outside the intended assembly-map image. The supplied record labels the problem open, but the group $C^*$-completion and assembly map require clarification.
