Optimal Nanostructures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source attributes "Optimal Nanostructures" to DARPA's Mathematical Challenge Eleven (2007). Its objective is to develop mathematics for creating optimal symmetric structures by nanoscale self-assembly. The supplied review describes the original challenge as a globally symmetric local-rule formulation; the background places it at the intersection of crystallography, optimization, and molecular dynamics.

In this context, local rules describe how constituent building blocks interact or attach, while global symmetry concerns the assembled structure as a whole. For a specified configuration space $X$ and a specified group $G$ acting on $X$, invariance of a configuration $x$ under that group means \[ g\cdot x=x\qquad\text{for every }g\in G. \] This is only a definition of a possible symmetry requirement: the source does not specify $X$, $G$, whether the target has all or only some of the proposed symmetries, or even a particular geometric or combinatorial model of an assembled structure.

Source-faithful research objective. Develop mathematical principles and design methods for local self-assembly rules that create globally symmetric nanoscale structures and achieve an appropriate optimality criterion. The source gives no objective function or ordering by which an assembly is optimal. It also supplies no building-block model, admissible rule class, assembly dynamics, constraints, or treatment of errors. Thus no optimization problem, existence theorem, or guarantee of successful assembly can be uniquely quantified from this record.

A precise version requires the self-assembly formalism, target symmetry, admissible designs and structures, optimization objective, and error or success criterion to be fixed. These are unresolved inputs, not hypotheses silently imposed in this draft. In particular, the record does not select deterministic versus stochastic assembly or require an almost-sure, high-probability, or worst-case success guarantee.

The literature triage dated 2026-08-17 calls this a collection of optimization and physical-design problems, states that nanoscale self-assembly and symmetric-structure design remain active research areas, and says a full solution or refutation is inapplicable without a model of local rules, objective, and assembly errors. Its partially solved classification and the registry's open label are supplied descriptions of a research agenda, not a verified status for a single proposition.

Short English statement

Find mathematical principles for designing local interactions that assemble nanoscale building blocks into globally symmetric structures, optimally according to a specified criterion. The source leaves the assembly model, symmetry, objective, and handling of errors unspecified, so it defines a research agenda rather than one theorem.
