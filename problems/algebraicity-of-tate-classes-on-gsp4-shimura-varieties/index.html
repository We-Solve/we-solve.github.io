Algebraicity of Tate Classes on GSp4 Shimura Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{A}}_f$ denote the finite adeles of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $K\subset\operatorname{GSp}_4({\mathbb{A}}_f)$ be an open compact subgroup. The supplied source describes $S_K(\operatorname{GSp}_4)$ as a three-dimensional Shimura variety parametrizing principally polarized abelian surfaces with level structure determined by $K$. No additional conditions on $K$ are supplied. For a prime $\ell$, write \[ V_K^3=H^3_{\mathrm{\acute et}}\!\left(S_K(\operatorname{GSp}_4)_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}},{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\right) \] for the geometric degree-three cohomology suggested by the record; the original excerpt does not unambiguously preserve the geometric base notation.

The motivating context is a classical cuspidal eigenform $g$ of weight two for $\operatorname{GL}_2$. The source attributes to Deligne a Galois representation $\rho_g$, described as a quotient of the degree-one étale cohomology of a modular curve $X_1(N)$ for sufficiently large $N$. It states that $\rho_g$ also occurs in the cohomology of other Shimura varieties, including the indicated $\operatorname{GSp}_4$ setting. The coefficient field, any required extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, and the precise occurrence are not specified. The excerpt begins a decomposition involving $\Pi_f^K\otimes\rho_{\Pi_f}$, but does not supply a complete formula, its indexing set, or the definitions and hypotheses for these factors.

For comparison, in the usual cycle-class formulation, if $X$ is a smooth variety over a number field $k$ and $r\geq 0$ is an integer, codimension-$r$ algebraic cycles have classes under \[ \operatorname{cl}_\ell:\operatorname{CH}^r(X_{\overline{k}})\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell \longrightarrow H^{2r}_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(X_{\overline{k}},{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell(r)). \] Here $\operatorname{CH}^r$ denotes cycles modulo rational equivalence, and $(r)$ denotes the Tate twist. A Tate class in this target is a class fixed by $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{k}/L)$ for some finite extension $L/k$.

Source question, with unresolved target. Determine when the classes referred to in the source, extracted from the relevant degree-three cohomology of $S_K(\operatorname{GSp}_4)$, arise from algebraic cycles in the intended Tate-conjecture construction. The record does not identify these extracted classes or the cycle-class map intended to produce them. Since ordinary codimension-$r$ cycle classes lie in degree $2r$, they do not directly lie in $V_K^3$. A product with another variety, a correspondence, or another cohomological construction might be intended, but none is specified. Consequently, a precise algebraicity or surjectivity assertion cannot be recovered without further source information; no such construction is assumed here.

Short English statement

The source asks when classes associated with the degree-three cohomology of the $\operatorname{GSp}_4$ Shimura threefold come from algebraic cycles. Its excerpt ends before identifying the relevant classes and construction. Ordinary cycle classes have even cohomological degree, so the missing construction is essential to a precise statement. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
