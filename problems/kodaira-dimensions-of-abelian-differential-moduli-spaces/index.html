Kodaira Dimensions of Abelian-Differential Moduli Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard interpretation in terms of holomorphic Abelian differentials with all zeros marked, let $g\geq 2$, let $n\geq 1$, and let $\mu=(m_1,\ldots,m_n)$ be a tuple of positive integers satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^n m_i=2g-2$. Define the stratum \[ \mathcal H_g(\mu)=\left\{(C,p_1,\ldots,p_n,\omega):\begin{array}{l}C\text{ is a smooth connected projective complex curve of genus }g,\\ p_1,\ldots,p_n\text{ are distinct ordered points},\\0\neq\omega\in H^0(C,K_C),\quad \operatorname{div}(\omega)=\sum_{i=1}^n m_i p_i\end{array}\right\}/\cong. \] Here $K_C$ is the canonical line bundle, and isomorphisms preserve the marked points and the differential. Its projectivization $\mathbb P\mathcal H_g(\mu)$ identifies $\omega$ with $a\omega$ for every $a\in{\mathbb{C}}^*$. These definitions make explicit a conventional interpretation; the supplied record does not specify the ground field, whether every marking must be a zero, or whether markings are ordered.

For an irreducible complex algebraic variety $X$, its ordinary birational Kodaira dimension means $\kappa(\widetilde X,K_{\widetilde X})$, where $\widetilde X$ is a smooth projective model of its function field. This invariant is $-\infty$ if all positive pluricanonical systems are empty, and otherwise is the maximum dimension of the images of the pluricanonical rational maps. General type means that this dimension equals $\dim X$. For a nonproper variety, logarithmic Kodaira dimension instead uses $K_{\overline X}+D$ on a smooth projective compactification with simple normal crossings boundary $D$; it need not equal the ordinary invariant.

Problem as supplied, with unresolved choices retained. Determine the behavior, as $g$ becomes large, of the Kodaira dimensions of moduli spaces of Abelian differentials, especially strata having few marked points. Once the intended moduli space and Kodaira-dimension convention are fixed, this asks for the relevant dimensions on its irreducible components as functions of $g$ and the stratum data $\mu$. The record does not specify whether the target is $\mathcal H_g(\mu)$, its projectivization, a locus of pointed curves, or a particular compactification, nor does it specify a quantitative meaning of "few" or an asymptotic quantifier. Consequently, no assertion of eventual general type for every such stratum is supplied.

The explicitly distinguished case is $n=1$ and $\mu=(2g-2)$. In the interpretation above, it consists of differentials with one zero of order $2g-2$. After identifying scalar multiples of the differential, its points correspond to subcanonical pointed curves \[ \mathcal S_g=\{(C,p): C\text{ is a smooth connected projective complex curve of genus }g,\ K_C\cong\mathcal O_C((2g-2)p)\}/\cong. \] The question includes the large-genus Kodaira-dimension behavior in this case. The intended components, treatment of hyperelliptic loci, and precise target invariant remain unspecified.

Short English statement

Determine how the Kodaira dimensions of strata of Abelian differentials behave in large genus, particularly when there are few marked zeros, including the one-zero stratum $\mu=(2g-2)$. A precise componentwise question requires specifying the moduli space, the Kodaira-dimension convention, and what "few" means. The supplied partially solved label and literature assessment are dated curation requiring independent review.
