Complete Curves and Positivity in Differential Strata

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns strata of holomorphic differentials, but does not specify the genus, zero multiplicities, base field, connected component, or whether differentials are projectivized. The following gives standard notation over ${\mathbb{C}}$ to describe the questions; this choice of setting requires confirmation against the source.

For an integer $g\geq 1$, let $\mu=(m_1,\ldots,m_n)$ be a list of positive integers satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^n m_i=2g-2$, allowing the empty list when $g=1$. Write $\mathcal H(\mu)$ for the moduli space of tuples $(X,\omega,p_1,\ldots,p_n)$, up to isomorphism, where $X$ is a smooth connected projective complex curve of genus $g$, $\omega$ is a nonzero holomorphic differential, the $p_i$ are distinct, and \[ \operatorname{div}(\omega)=\sum_{i=1}^n m_i p_i. \] This notation labels the zeros; the supplied record does not specify whether zeros are labeled or whether a moduli stack or a coarse moduli space is intended. Let \[ \mathbb P\mathcal H(\mu)=\mathcal H(\mu)/{\mathbb{C}}^{\times}, \qquad c\cdot(X,\omega,p_1,\ldots,p_n)=(X,c\omega,p_1,\ldots,p_n). \] On the projectivized stratum, the tautological line bundle $L$ has fiber ${\mathbb{C}}\omega$ at $(X,[\omega],p_1,\ldots,p_n)$. Use the convention $\eta=c_1(L)$; the source does not specify its sign convention or the intersection or cohomology theory in which this class is considered.

  1. Determine whether a stratum contains a complete curve, meaning a closed integral algebraic subvariety of dimension one that is proper over the base field. The supplied wording leaves unresolved whether the ambient space is $\mathcal H(\mu)$, $\mathbb P\mathcal H(\mu)$, or a chosen connected component. It also does not specify whether the question asks for existence in some stratum or a classification across strata.
  2. Determine the positive integers $k$ for which \[ \eta^k=0 \] on the intended stratum. A precise vanishing question requires specification of the projectivization, the ambient component, the ring containing $\eta^k$, and its coefficient system. If an unprojectivized stratum is intended, the meaning of its tautological class must also be specified.
  3. For an intended compactification $\overline S$ of a stratum $S$, determine positivity properties of the other divisor classes under consideration. The compactification, divisor classes, and meaning of positivity are not supplied; possible distinct notions include ampleness, nefness, bigness, and pseudoeffectivity. Thus this part remains a research question requiring these choices, rather than a specified proposition.

The supplied dated curation reports that complete curves are ruled out in unprojectivized holomorphic strata, while the projectivized complete-curve question and the requested vanishing and positivity questions remain open. This is a supplied literature assessment, not an independently verified theorem or status determination.

Short English statement

The problem asks about complete curves in strata of holomorphic differentials, vanishing powers of their tautological class $\eta$, and positivity of divisor classes on compactifications. Its precise scope requires clarification of projectivization, strata and components, the ring used for vanishing, and the compactification and positivity notion. The supplied open-status assessment is dated and requires independent review.
