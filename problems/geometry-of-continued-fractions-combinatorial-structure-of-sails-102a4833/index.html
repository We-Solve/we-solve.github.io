Geometry of Continued Fractions — Combinatorial structure of sails

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A standard Klein-sail construction starts with a pointed cone $C\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$, with vertex $0$, and takes the polyhedral boundary facing $0$ of \[ \operatorname{conv}\left((C\cap{\mathbb{Z}}^d)\setminus\{0\}\right). \] Write this sail as $S(C)$, with its face decomposition. When a specified group $U_C$ of integer-linear symmetries preserves the sail and acts freely and properly with compact quotient homeomorphic to a torus, the quotient faces give a torus decomposition $D(C)=S(C)/U_C$. Periodicity, the dimension, and the choice of the quotient symmetry group are required to make this construction definite; none is specified in the excerpt.

Arnold's supplied conjecture is that integer-noncongruent Klein sails have distinct torus decompositions. Once the intended admissible class, integer congruence relation $\cong_{{\mathbb{Z}}}$, and equivalence relation $\cong_D$ on torus decompositions are fixed, its precise injectivity form is \[ \forall S,S'\text{ in that class},\qquad D(S)\cong_D D(S')\ \Longrightarrow\ S\cong_{{\mathbb{Z}}}S'. \] For example, a possible integer-linear convention is $S'=A(S)$ for $A\in\operatorname{GL}_d({\mathbb{Z}})$, but the source excerpt does not distinguish this from orientation-preserving or integer-affine congruence. Likewise, "distinct decompositions" could compare unlabelled cell structures or cell structures carrying lattice-geometric labels and markings; those alternatives are not interchangeable. The displayed implication is consequently the exact logical target conditional on those missing definitions, rather than a selection of one interpretation.

The supplied moderate-confidence OPEN-TRIAGE summary treats the general conjecture as open, says no proof or counterexample was found in its retrieved literature, and mentions discussions in Arnold's problems and continued-fraction work involving Markov spectra and cubic fields. That search assessment is not an independently verified literature conclusion.

Short English statement

Does the torus decomposition determine a periodic Klein sail up to integer congruence? The allowed sails, quotient group, congruence convention, and information retained in the decomposition must be specified.
