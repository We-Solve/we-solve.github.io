Classification of every bounded stable Allen–Cahn solution in dimension seven

OpenAI's claim

De Giorgi rigidity in dimension eight: The paper claims that every strictly direction-monotone entire solution of the scalar Allen--Cahn equation in $\mathbb R^8$ is a planar heteroclinic. It also classifies stable solutions in $\mathbb R^7$ without an energy-growth assumption.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
The monotone theorem claims that if $u\in C^2(\mathbb R^8,(-1,1))$ solves $\Delta u=u^3-u$ and $\partial_8u>0$, then \[u(x)=\tanh((e\cdot x-c)/\sqrt2),\qquad e\in S^7,\quad e_8>0.\] No limits at infinity are assumed. Its stable seven-dimensional theorem states that every $v\in C^2(\mathbb R^7,[-1,1])$ solving the same equation and satisfying $\int(|\nabla\phi|^2+(3v^2-1)\phi^2)\ge0$ for compactly supported smooth $\phi$ is either $1$, $-1$ or a one-dimensional transition $\tanh((a\cdot x-b)/\sqrt2)$ with $a\in S^6$. No energy-growth assumption is used; lower-dimensional monotone cases follow by adding unused coordinates.

Outline of the argument

De Giorgi rigidity in dimension eight: Finite-density stability first forces a planar profile. To remove that density assumption in dimension seven, penalized reach controls both interface bending and neighboring sheets, and stability bounds a fourth curvature moment. An ambient radial test and matched sheet amplitudes contradict unbounded interface density; the stable directional limits of the eight-dimensional monotone solution then provide the well limits needed for the planar conclusion.
