Math-1283

A fixed-real-subfield criterion for a complex archimedean ball

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A subset $D\subseteq k$ is Diophantine over a number field $k$ if there are finitely many polynomials $P_j\in k[X,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m]$ such that \[ x\in D\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists y\in k^m\quad P_j(x,y)=0\text{ for every }j. \] Equivalently it is a coordinate projection of the $k$-points of an affine algebraic set. For a fixed archimedean place $v$ put $B_v=\{x\in k:|x|_v\le1\}$. Shlapentokh asks, for the source's nonreal number-field setting, to construct such a set $S\subseteq k^n$ whose projection is exactly $B_v$ for some chosen archimedean absolute value. The place is fixed for the whole ball, rather than chosen separately for each element. The extracted condition "$k\ 6\ \subset\ {\mathbb{R}}$" is read as a damaged non-containment symbol; whether this refers to a specified nonreal embedding or to an intrinsic condition on $k$ is not spelled out. Likewise "$|k|\le1$" is understood as the bound on the element being tested, not on the field itself.

The supplied partial result fixes an embedding $\sigma:k\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$ with nonreal image stable under complex conjugation. Let $c$ be its induced involution and $F=k^c$, embedded in ${\mathbb{R}}$ by $\tau=\sigma|_F$. It asserts \[ \{x\in k:|\sigma(x)|\le1\}\text{ is Diophantine over }k \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F\text{ is Diophantine over }k. \] For a nonzero $\theta\in k$ with $c(\theta)=-\theta$, the reported forward construction recovers equality of complex moduli from the ball, with a separate treatment of the zero-denominator case, and then uses \[ F=\{x\in k:|\sigma(x+\theta)|=|\sigma(x-\theta)|\}. \] For the reverse direction, write $x=a+b\theta$, $a,b\in F$, and set $d=-\theta^2\in F$, so $\tau(d)>0$. For the ordinary complex modulus the norm identity is \[ |\sigma(a+b\theta)|^2=\tau(a^2+d b^2). \] The report invokes Diophantine definability of the ordering at this chosen real embedding of $F$ to reconstruct the ball when $F$ is Diophantine over $k$. Its summary writes a variant $w(a^2+d b^2)$ without defining $w$; the displayed standard-modulus identity here specifies the embedding rather than assigning an unsupported meaning to $w$. The full definability constructions and their primary references are absent.

The report explicitly leaves the general intended complex-place construction open, in particular without conjugation stability or a Diophantine fixed real subfield. The source remarks that the problem would have consequences for Hilbert's tenth problem over rings of integers and is probably hard because of a near-equivalence, but does not state a precise reduction or equivalence theorem.

Short English statement

Define an exact archimedean unit ball over a nonreal number field by polynomial equations and existential projection. The supplied report reduces the conjugation-stable complex-embedding case to Diophantine definability of its fixed real subfield, while retaining the broader construction as open.
