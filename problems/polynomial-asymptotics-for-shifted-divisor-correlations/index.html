Math-833

Polynomial Asymptotics for Shifted Divisor Correlations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the conventional interpretation of the notation, let $r\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $r\geq3$, and let $h\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}$ be fixed independently of $X$. Write ${\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. For $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, define the $r$-fold divisor function by \[ d_r(m)=\#\left\{(a_1,\ldots,a_r)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}^{\,r}:a_1\cdots a_r=m\right\}. \] For real $X\geq1$, interpret the shifted correlation as \[ D_r(X;h)=\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\\ n\leq X,\ n+h\geq1}}d_r(n)d_r(n+h). \] The supplied record does not explicitly define $d_r$, specify the integer domains of $r,h,n$, or state a convention when $n+h\leq0$. The definitions above make these conventional choices explicit; their agreement with the intended source formulation requires review.

Conjecture as supplied, with these conventions. For every integer $r\geq3$ and every fixed nonzero integer $h$, there exists a polynomial $P_{r,h}\in{\mathbb{R}}[u]$, denoted $P_{2(r-1)}(u;h)$ in the supplied record, of degree exactly $2(r-1)$, such that \[ D_r(X;h)\sim X P_{r,h}(\log X)\qquad(X\to+\infty). \] Here $\log$ is the natural logarithm, the polynomial may depend on both $r$ and $h$ but not on $X$, and $\sim$ means \[ \lim_{X\to+\infty}\frac{D_r(X;h)}{X P_{r,h}(\log X)}=1. \] No uniformity as $r$ or $h$ varies is specified. This ratio asymptotic does not determine the lower-degree coefficients of $P_{r,h}$. If a full polynomial expansion with an error smaller than $X$ is intended, that stronger assertion is not specified by the displayed source statement and must be clarified.

Short English statement

For each fixed integer $r\geq3$ and fixed nonzero integer shift $h$, the supplied conjecture asks whether the correlation of the $r$-fold divisor function with its shift is asymptotic to $X$ times a polynomial in $\log X$ of degree $2(r-1)$. Integer domains and the treatment of nonpositive shifted arguments are explicit conventional interpretations requiring review. The displayed ratio asymptotic leaves lower polynomial coefficients undetermined.
