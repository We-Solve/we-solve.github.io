Math-1122

Congruence Kernels of Arithmetic Lattices in $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb C)$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Gamma\leq \operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$ be an arithmetic lattice. Here a lattice is a discrete subgroup for which $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})/\Gamma$ has finite invariant volume; arithmeticity means that $\Gamma$ is commensurable with an arithmetic subgroup arising from a defining number field and algebraic group. The supplied statement does not restrict $\Gamma$ to the nonuniform case or to Bianchi groups.

Fix a defining arithmetic realization and its congruence topology on $\Gamma$. A principal congruence subgroup is the kernel of reduction modulo a nonzero ideal in a compatible integral model; a congruence subgroup is a subgroup containing such a kernel. Let $\mathcal C_\Gamma$ be the family of finite-index normal congruence subgroups. Define the profinite completion, the congruence completion, and the congruence kernel by \[ \widehat\Gamma=\varprojlim_{\substack{N\trianglelefteq\Gamma\\[\Gamma:N]<\infty}}\Gamma/N, \qquad \Gamma^{\mathrm{cong}}=\varprojlim_{U\in\mathcal C_\Gamma}\Gamma/U, \qquad C(\Gamma)=\ker\left(\widehat\Gamma\longrightarrow\Gamma^{\mathrm{cong}}\right). \] The map is induced by the identity on $\Gamma$, and these groups carry their inverse-limit topologies. The source does not specify the arithmetic realization or integral model, so this definition makes explicit data implicit in its use of the congruence kernel.

Write $F_\omega$ for the source's free profinite group of countably infinite rank. Under the usual convergent-basis convention, this is a profinite group with a free generating sequence $(x_n)_{n\geq1}$ tending to the identity: for every profinite group $H$ and every sequence $(h_n)_{n\geq1}$ tending to the identity in $H$, there is a unique continuous homomorphism $F_\omega\to H$ sending $x_n$ to $h_n$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify this convention, which requires confirmation against the source context.

The question, read as a general question about arithmetic lattices, is whether \[ C(\Gamma)\cong F_\omega \] as topological groups for every arithmetic lattice $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$, with its defining congruence topology. More generally, determine the structure of $C(\Gamma)$. The source does not specify which structural invariants are sought in this broader request, nor does it explicitly resolve whether a narrower class of arithmetic lattices was intended.

Short English statement

For an arithmetic lattice in $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$, compare its completion using all finite quotients with its completion using congruence quotients. Is the kernel a free profinite group of countably infinite rank, and more generally what is its structure? The arithmetic data, intended scope, and infinite-rank convention require source confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
