Math-5585

Rectangular-lattice Landau extremal

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $a\in(0,1)$ put $b=\sqrt{1-a^2}>0$ and let \[ \Lambda_a=\{an+ibm:n,m\in\mathbb Z\},\qquad \mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}. \] Let $f_a:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C\setminus\Lambda_a$ be the holomorphic universal covering map normalized by \[ f_a(0)=\frac{a+ib}{2},\qquad f_a'(0)\in(0,\infty). \] Here $a,b$ are the positive side lengths of the rectangular lattice and $a^2+b^2=1$ fixes its scale. A universal covering map is a surjective holomorphic covering whose domain is simply connected; the stated normalization fixes the covering map.

Determine the extremal value \[ M=\sup_{0<a<1}f_a'(0) \] and the parameters $a$ at which it is attained. In particular, establish attainment if the requested maximum is to be realized inside $(0,1)$ and compute its exact value. The supplied summary describes degenerate/asymptotic cases as understood but does not supply their formulas; it leaves this interior optimization unresolved.

Short English statement

Among rectangular lattices whose squared side lengths sum to one, maximize the derivative at the center-normalized universal covering of the lattice complement. Determine the exact value and the maximizing lattice shapes.
