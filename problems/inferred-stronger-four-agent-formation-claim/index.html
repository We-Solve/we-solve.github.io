Inferred Stronger Four-Agent Formation Claim

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p_i(t)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$, $i\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, denote the positions of four labelled agents, and write $p=(p_1^\top,p_2^\top,p_3^\top,p_4^\top)^\top\in{\mathbb{R}}^8$. For prescribed target distances $\bar d_{ij}=\bar d_{ji}\geq0$, define \[ e_{ij}(p)=\lVert p_i-p_j\rVert^2-\bar d_{ij}^{\,2},\qquad \dot p_i=\sum_{\substack{j=1\\j\ne i}}^4 e_{ij}(p)(p_j-p_i). \] The sum includes all other agents, so the interaction graph is the complete graph on four vertices. Define the desired distance-realization set by \[ \mathcal D=\left\{p\in{\mathbb{R}}^8:\ \lVert p_i-p_j\rVert^2=\bar d_{ij}^{\,2}\text{ for every }1\leq i<j\leq4\right\}. \] Interpreting the target as a planar formation requires $\mathcal D\ne\varnothing$. A target realization $q\in\mathcal D$ is described in the record as either a nonrectangular quadrilateral or a triangle with an interior agent. The latter conventionally means that three target points are noncollinear and the fourth lies in the strict interior of their convex hull. The record does not specify quadrilateral vertex order, convexity, exclusions of degeneracies, or additional hypotheses on the target distances. These conventions therefore require review. Distance constraints alone also allow translations, rotations, and reflected realizations.

The retained stronger assertion, under an all-initial-conditions interpretation of "global convergence," would be: for every target covered by the intended geometric hypotheses and every $p^0\in{\mathbb{R}}^8$, the solution with $p(0)=p^0$ exists for all $t\geq0$ and satisfies \[ \lim_{t\to\infty}\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq4}e_{ij}(p(t))^2=0. \] This specifies convergence of distance errors; the record does not specify whether convergence to a single configuration is additionally intended.

No exceptional initial set is supplied. The background instead discusses almost-global convergence, whose exceptional set and hypotheses are not provided. Consequently, a precise, source-supported convergence conjecture cannot be recovered here without changing or supplementing the record.

Short English statement

Four planar agents adjust their positions using errors in all six prescribed pairwise distances. The retained record asks for convergence to nonrectangular quadrilateral targets or to a triangle with an interior agent. Its own verification notes reject this as an independently supported problem statement: unrestricted global convergence has stationary counterconfigurations, while the background refers to almost-global convergence under unspecified conditions. The report's source-backed saddle-point question is recorded separately as OWR-13497-006. This statement-only draft preserves permanent ID $30002807$ and requires human mathematical and source review.
