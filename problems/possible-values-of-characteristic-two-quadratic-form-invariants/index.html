Math-3546

Possible Values of Characteristic-Two Quadratic-Form Invariants

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ range over fields of characteristic $2$. Interpret a quadratic form over $F$ as a map $q\colon V\to F$ on a finite-dimensional $F$-vector space satisfying $q(av)=a^2q(v)$, with bilinear polar form \[ b_q(v,w)=q(v+w)-q(v)-q(w). \] The form is anisotropic if $q(v)=0$ implies $v=0$, and totally singular if $b_q=0$. Let $Q'(F)$ denote the anisotropic quadratic forms over $F$ that are not totally singular.

To make the supplied terminology explicit, use its standard interpretation: put \[ R_q=\operatorname{rad}(b_q)=\{v\in V:b_q(v,w)=0\text{ for every }w\in V\}, \qquad \operatorname{ql}(q)=q|_{R_q}, \] and define \[ \operatorname{rdim}(q)=\operatorname{rank}(b_q)=\dim V-\dim R_q. \] Thus $\operatorname{ql}(q)$ is the quasilinear part, of dimension $\dim R_q$, and the regular dimension is even because $b_q$ is alternating. These definitions explicate terms that the supplied record names without defining internally; their agreement with the cited report requires review.

For every positive integer $r$ and every positive integer $s$, the supplied invariants are \[ \begin{aligned} \widetilde u(F)&=\sup\{\dim q:q\in Q'(F)\},\\ u_r(F)&=\sup\{\dim q:q\in Q'(F),\ \operatorname{rdim}(q)=2r\},\\ \widetilde u_s(F)&=\sup\{\dim q:q\in Q'(F),\ \dim\operatorname{ql}(q)=s\}. \end{aligned} \] For a nonempty defining set, a bounded supremum is an integer and an unbounded supremum is interpreted as $+\infty$. The record does not specify a convention for the supremum of an empty defining set; values in those cases remain unresolved in this draft.

Problem. Determine the possible values of $\widetilde u(F)$ as $F$ varies over fields of characteristic $2$, and, for each fixed $r\geq1$ and each fixed $s\geq1$, determine the possible values of $u_r(F)$ and $\widetilde u_s(F)$, respectively, subject to clarification of the empty-set convention. No further restriction on $F$ is supplied. The broader original summary says only "fields"; this draft follows the explicit characteristic-$2$ hypothesis in the detailed statement.

Short English statement

Over fields of characteristic $2$, determine which supremal dimensions can occur for anisotropic quadratic forms that are not totally singular, either without further restriction, with regular dimension $2r$, or with quasilinear part of dimension $s$, where $r,s\geq1$. The empty-set convention and the expanded terminology require review. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
