Anabelian Reconstruction of Genus-Zero Configuration Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology, a hyperbolic curve over a field $k$ conventionally means a smooth geometrically connected curve $C$ whose smooth projective compactification has genus $g$ and whose geometric complement consists of $r$ points, with $2g-2+r>0$. Thus, in genus zero, $r\geq 3$. A hyperbolic polycurve conventionally admits a tower \[ X=X_m\longrightarrow X_{m-1}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow X_0=\operatorname{Spec}k \] of smooth morphisms whose geometric fibers are hyperbolic curves. These conventions explain the terminology; the supplied record does not specify the field or the precise permitted class of towers.

For an integer $n\geq 1$, the ordered configuration space of $C$ is \[ \operatorname{Conf}_n(C)=C^n\setminus\bigcup_{1\leq i<j\leq n}\Delta_{ij}, \] where products are over $k$ and $\Delta_{ij}$ is the locus where the $i$-th and $j$-th coordinates coincide. The unordered configuration space is the quotient by the symmetric group $S_n$. The source does not specify which convention is intended or the allowed values of $n$.

For a connected scheme $Z$ with geometric base point $\bar z$, write $\pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Z,\bar z)$ for its profinite étale fundamental group. The supplied reconstruction question has the schematic form \[ \pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute et}}(X,\bar x)\cong \pi_1^{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Y,\bar y) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\cong Y, \] where $X$ is a hyperbolic polycurve and $Y$ is a configuration space of a hyperbolic curve, subject to the source's qualification "in genus zero."

Unresolved scope. This is a schematic question, not a fully quantified conjecture. The record does not determine the base field, whether genus zero refers to the curve defining $Y$, the fibers in a polycurve tower, or both, or whether the groups are arithmetic or geometric. It also does not specify whether the group isomorphism must be continuous and compatible with additional arithmetic structure, or whether the resulting variety isomorphism is required over $k$ or over an algebraic closure. These choices must be recovered from the source before a precise universal statement can be asserted. The stated conclusion asks for existence of a variety isomorphism; the record does not explicitly require it to induce the given group isomorphism.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether a hyperbolic polycurve and a configuration space of a hyperbolic curve must be isomorphic when their étale fundamental groups are isomorphic, under an unspecified genus-zero restriction. The field, configuration convention, arithmetic compatibility, and meaning of genus zero require clarification. The supplied status is partially_solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
