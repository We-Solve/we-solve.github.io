Math-5602

Sharp Lichnerowicz theorem

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The target is a method for recovering a known sharp theorem. In a standard formulation, let $(M,g)$ be a connected closed Riemannian manifold of dimension $N\geq2$, and suppose \[ \operatorname{Ric}_x(v,v)\geq\rho\,g_x(v,v)\quad(x\in M,\ v\in T_xM),\qquad \rho>0. \] Let $\lambda_1(-\Delta_g)$ be the first positive eigenvalue of the nonnegative Laplace--Beltrami operator. The sharp Lichnerowicz estimate is \[ \lambda_1(-\Delta_g)\geq\frac{N}{N-1}\rho, \] whereas the source says the coarse-curvature argument recovers only the scale $\lambda_1\geq\rho$. Here $\inf\operatorname{Ric}$ in the source is understood as the infimum over unit tangent vectors, not an infimum of an uncontracted tensor.

For the source's $\varepsilon$-step random walk, denote its transition law from $x$ by $P_x^\varepsilon$. Coarse curvature uses \[ \kappa_\varepsilon(x,y)=1-\frac{W_1(P_x^\varepsilon,P_y^\varepsilon)}{d_g(x,y)},\qquad x\ne y, \] where $W_1$ minimizes expected geodesic distance over couplings of the two laws. The problem asks whether one can retain the directional information lost by this scalar contraction argument, for example using reflection couplings, to obtain the full factor $N/(N-1)$ in the Lichnerowicz conclusion.

Closedness, connectedness, positivity of $\rho$, and $N\geq2$ make the conventional displayed spectral theorem precise; these hypotheses are not all supplied in the source. The step distribution (such as a geodesic sphere or ball), scaling of the discrete spectral gap to the Laplacian, and admissible curvature/coupling refinement are also not specified. The question concerns deriving the sharp estimate by the indicated method, not whether the classical estimate is true.

Short English statement

Can a refinement of coarse Ricci curvature, perhaps through reflection couplings that retain direction, recover the sharp $N/(N-1)$ factor in the Lichnerowicz spectral-gap theorem? The theorem is the known target; the requested advance is the coupling or curvature argument.
