Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

For a knot $K$, colored HOMFLY--PT invariants are assembled into a generating series $P_K(x,a,q)$. Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Does the knots–quivers correspondence hold for every knot, relating its colored HOMFLY–PT generating series $P(x,a,q)$ to the motivic generating series $P_C(x,q)$ of a corresponding quiver?
Scope and source qualification.
The substitutions and framing conventions are part of the correspondence. Equality of one uncolored polynomial would not establish the stated all-color generating-series claim.

Short English statement

Determine whether every knot has a quiver whose motivic series reproduces all its colored HOMFLY--PT invariants.

The coefficient of $x_i$ is $(-q)^{C_{ii}}/(1-q^2)$, whereas for $i\ne j$ the coefficient of $x_ix_j$ is \[ \frac{(-q)^{C_{ii}+C_{jj}+2C_{ij}}}{(1-q^2)^2}. \] Thus the linear data see the diagonal entries but not off-diagonal arrows. With two vertices and both diagonal entries zero, matrices having $C_{12}=0$ or $C_{12}=1$ agree in every linear coefficient and disagree in the mixed quadratic coefficient by a factor $q^2$.
