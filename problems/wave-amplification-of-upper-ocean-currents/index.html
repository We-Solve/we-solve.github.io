Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses the question: Can waves amplify upper-ocean currents?

Here "waves," "upper-ocean currents," and "amplify" are physical terms for which the supplied record gives no mathematical definitions. It specifies no governing equations, spatial domain, initial or boundary conditions, admissible wave and current fields, coupling mechanism, or parameter regime. It also specifies no observable measuring current strength, comparison state, or time interval over which amplification should be assessed.

Consequently, the supplied evidence supports a motivating physical question but does not determine a precise mathematical proposition. In particular, it does not specify whether the intended target is existence of an amplifying configuration, amplification for a prescribed configuration, or a statement uniform over a class of configurations. These choices remain unresolved; no model or quantifiers are imposed in this draft.

Short English statement

The question asks whether waves can strengthen upper-ocean currents. A precise mathematical statement requires a specified wave--current model and a definition of strengthening, together with assumptions, comparison conditions, and quantifiers. The supplied record does not provide these.
