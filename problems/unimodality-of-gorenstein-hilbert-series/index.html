Unimodality of Gorenstein Hilbert Series

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field. A standard graded $k$-algebra is a commutative finitely generated algebra $R=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}R_n$ with $R_0=k$, generated over $k$ by $R_1$. Require $R$ to be an integral domain. Put $\mathfrak m=\bigoplus_{n>0}R_n$ and $d=\dim R$. The Cohen--Macaulay condition means $\operatorname{depth}R_{\mathfrak m}=d$; the Gorenstein condition means that $R_{\mathfrak m}$ has finite injective dimension as a module over itself. The source specifies no restrictions on the field, so the questions below are formulated over a fixed but otherwise unspecified $k$; the intended quantification over fields requires confirmation.

Define the Hilbert series and its $h$-polynomial by \[ H_R(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}(\dim_k R_n)t^n =\frac{h_R(t)}{(1-t)^d}, \qquad h_R(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{s}h_i t^i,\quad h_s\ne0. \] For the Cohen--Macaulay rings under consideration, the coefficients $h_i$ are nonnegative integers. The polynomial is called unimodal if there exists $j\in\{0,\ldots,s\}$ such that \[ h_0\leq h_1\leq\cdots\leq h_j \quad\text{and}\quad h_j\geq h_{j+1}\geq\cdots\geq h_s. \] It is called log-concave if \[ h_i^2\geq h_{i-1}h_{i+1} \qquad(1\leq i\leq s-1). \] Thus endpoint inequalities are not required, and log-concavity is vacuous when $s\leq1$.

Principal question. Is $h_R(t)$ unimodal for every standard graded Gorenstein domain $R$?

Related questions. Is $h_R(t)$ unimodal for every standard graded Cohen--Macaulay domain $R$? What happens when unimodality is replaced by log-concavity? Applying that replacement to both ring classes asks whether every standard graded Gorenstein domain, respectively every standard graded Cohen--Macaulay domain, has a log-concave $h$-polynomial. The wording of the source does not explicitly distinguish these two log-concavity variants, so this expansion requires source review.

Supplied status qualification. The record labels this compound problem partially solved. Its dated assessment attributes a disproof of the general Cohen--Macaulay log-concavity assertion to a Niesi--Robbiano example, while describing Gorenstein-domain unimodality as open in general. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified mathematical or current-status conclusions. They do not explicitly settle the other variants.

Short English statement

The principal question asks whether the numerator coefficients of the Hilbert series of every standard graded Gorenstein domain increase and then decrease. The record also asks about Cohen--Macaulay domains and about log-concavity. Its supplied dated assessment reports a counterexample to general Cohen--Macaulay log-concavity and leaves Gorenstein-domain unimodality open. Field restrictions and the precise scope of the log-concavity variants require review.
