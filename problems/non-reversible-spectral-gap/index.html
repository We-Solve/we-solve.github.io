Math-1893

Non-reversible spectral gap

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A customary setting for the question is a metric space $(X,d)$ equipped with a Markov kernel $P$. Write $P_x=P(x,\cdot)$, assume the relevant measures have finite first moments, and let \[ W_1(\mu,\nu)=\inf_{\gamma\in\Pi(\mu,\nu)}\int_{X\times X}d(u,v)\,\gamma({\,\mathrm{d}} u,{\,\mathrm{d}} v), \qquad \kappa(x,y)=1-\frac{W_1(P_x,P_y)}{d(x,y)}\quad(x\ne y), \] where $\Pi(\mu,\nu)$ is the set of couplings with the indicated marginals. A uniform positive coarse Ricci lower bound means $\kappa(x,y)\ge\kappa_0>0$ for every distinct pair in the specified space.

If $\pi$ is an invariant probability measure, $\pi P=\pi$, let $P$ also denote the operator $Pf(x)=\int f(y)P(x,{\,\mathrm{d}} y)$ on $L^2(\pi)$, and put \[ L^2_0(\pi)=\{f\in L^2(\pi):\textstyle\int f\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\pi=0\}, \qquad P_0=P|_{L^2_0(\pi)}. \] Reversibility means that $\pi({\,\mathrm{d}} x)P(x,{\,\mathrm{d}} y)$ is symmetric in $x,y$. The question asks what quantitative bounds positive coarse Ricci curvature yields when reversibility is absent, including bounds for the spectral radius of $P_0$, its operator norm $\|P_0\|_{2\to2}$, or a Poincaré inequality. One standard Poincaré formulation is \[ \operatorname{Var}_{\pi}(f)\le C\,\mathcal E(f,f),\qquad \mathcal E(f,f)=\frac12\int_{X\times X}|f(y)-f(x)|^2\,\pi({\,\mathrm{d}} x)P(x,{\,\mathrm{d}} y), \] where $\operatorname{Var}_{\pi}(f)=\int|f-\int f\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\pi|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\pi$. In a non-reversible setting these spectral-radius, one-step norm, and quadratic-form bounds are different targets and must not be identified automatically. The source does not select a normed function space or a specific Poincaré form; $L^2(\pi)$ and the displayed form are explicit conventional candidates.

A further question is whether finite-state approximation can transfer useful bounds to the general setting. Such a scheme must specify approximating metrics and kernels, preservation of a curvature bound, invariant measures, the mode of convergence, and the passage of the desired estimate to the limit. None is supplied in the excerpt. Likewise the existence of $\pi$, required regularity, and permissible dependence of constants on parameters other than curvature are not stated there.

The supplied research summary reports partial progress through non-local-gradient Poincaré and modified logarithmic Sobolev bounds for non-reversible chains under curvature assumptions, while saying sharp spectral-radius/operator-norm bounds and a general finite-approximation scheme are not fully closed. It supplies no exact inequalities or citations for those results. The 2008 source's open status has no recorded later authoritative resolution check; the later partial-progress label is retained as an attribution.

Short English statement

Determine what positive coarse Ricci curvature controls for non-reversible Markov chains: spectral radius, operator norm, or suitable Poincaré inequalities, and whether finite-state approximation transfers such estimates. The precise function space, hypotheses, constants, and approximation scheme remain to be specified from the original source.
