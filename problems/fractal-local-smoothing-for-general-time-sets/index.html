Short English statement

The problem asks whether the fractal local smoothing estimate holds for every time set $E\subset[1,2]$, every exponent $p>2$, and every regularity parameter $s>p^{-1}\nu_E^{\#}(p s_p)$. The supplied record omits the estimate and essential definitions, so a complete precise formulation requires human review of the cited source.
