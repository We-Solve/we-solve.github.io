Math-3725

Curves with Minimal Symbolic Initial-Degree Gap

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field and let $S=k[x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3]$ with its standard grading. The supplied record does not specify the intended base field, its characteristic, or whether it must be algebraically closed; the formulation below is conditional on these unresolved choices.

For a nonempty reduced projective curve $C\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}^3_k$, let $I=I(C)\subset S$ denote its saturated homogeneous vanishing ideal. Here the conventional interpretation of a curve as a scheme of pure dimension one is used; the supplied record does not explicitly settle this convention. Neither irreducibility nor reducibility is required. Define the initial degree of a nonzero homogeneous ideal $J\subset S$ by \[ \alpha(J)=\min\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:J_d\neq0\}. \] For the radical ideal $I$, define its second symbolic power by \[ I^{(2)}=\bigcap_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}(I)}\left(I^2S_{\mathfrak p}\cap S\right), \] where $\operatorname{Min}(I)$ is the set of minimal prime ideals over $I$, and each intersection with $S$ denotes contraction from the localization.

Classification problem. Determine all reduced curves $C\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}^3_k$, allowing irreducible curves, such that \[ \alpha\left(I(C)^{(2)}\right)-\alpha\left(I(C)\right)=1. \] No restriction to line arrangements or arithmetically Cohen--Macaulay curves is stated in the supplied problem. A definitive formulation requires clarification of the base-field hypotheses and the convention for curves.

Short English statement

Classify the reduced projective curves in three-dimensional projective space whose second symbolic power has initial degree exactly one greater than that of the curve's ideal. Irreducible curves are allowed. The intended base field and the precise convention for a curve remain unspecified.
