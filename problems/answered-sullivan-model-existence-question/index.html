Answered Sullivan-Model Existence Question

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: Does every connected manifold admit a free commutative differential graded algebra model with a morphism to its de Rham algebra inducing an isomorphism in cohomology?

A conventional interpretation uses a connected smooth manifold $M$ and real differential forms. This interpretation supplies smoothness and coefficients that the extracted question does not explicitly specify; its boundary convention is also unspecified. Write $(\Omega^*(M;{\mathbb{R}}),d_{\mathrm{dR}})$ for the algebra of smooth real differential forms with exterior derivative.

For a graded real vector space $V$, let $\wedge V$ denote the free unital graded-commutative algebra generated by $V$. Thus homogeneous elements satisfy $ab=(-1)^{|a||b|}ba$; the notation does not mean an ordinary exterior algebra on generators of every degree. A differential on this algebra is a linear map $d$ of degree $+1$ such that $d^2=0$ and \[ d(ab)=d(a)b+(-1)^{|a|}a\,d(b). \] A morphism $\phi$ of differential graded algebras preserves degrees, multiplication and the unit, and satisfies $\phi\circ d=d_{\mathrm{dR}}\circ\phi$. It is a quasi-isomorphism if its induced maps on cohomology are isomorphisms in every degree, where \[ H^k(A,d)=\frac{\ker(d:A^k\to A^{k+1})}{\operatorname{im}(d:A^{k-1}\to A^k)}. \]

Under the real smooth interpretation, the extracted question asks whether, for every connected $M$, there exist $V$, $d$, and a morphism \[ \phi:(\wedge V,d)\longrightarrow(\Omega^*(M;{\mathbb{R}}),d_{\mathrm{dR}}) \] such that \[ H^k(\phi):H^k(\wedge V,d)\xrightarrow{\;\cong\;}H^k(\Omega^*(M;{\mathbb{R}}),d_{\mathrm{dR}}) \quad\text{for every }k. \] The source does not specify the permitted generator degrees or additional conditions on the model. In particular, no minimality condition, Sullivan filtration, simple connectivity assumption, or finite-type hypothesis is imposed here.

Short English statement

The retained question asks whether every connected manifold has a free graded-commutative differential algebra mapping to its de Rham algebra and computing the same cohomology. A real smooth interpretation is described explicitly, but some conventions remain unspecified. The supplied verification says the report answers the question affirmatively, conflicting with the registry's open label and dated assessment.
