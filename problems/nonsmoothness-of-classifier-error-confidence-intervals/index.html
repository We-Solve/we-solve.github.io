Math-15602

Nonsmoothness of Classifier Error Confidence Intervals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a conventional interpretation of classification test error, let $P$ be the distribution of a test observation $(X,Y)$, and let $h$ be a classifier taking feature values to labels. Whenever the misclassification event is measurable, define \[ R_P(h)=P\{h(X)\ne Y\}. \] Let $D$ denote training data and let $\widehat h_D=A(D)$ be the classifier produced by a learning rule $A$. Under this interpretation, the test error of the learned classifier is the random quantity $R_P(\widehat h_D)$, evaluated on a fresh test observation with law $P$. For a resampling-based interval $C_{1-\alpha}(D)$, with nominal confidence level $1-\alpha$, where $0<\alpha<1$, its coverage for this target is \[ \Pr\!\left\{R_P(\widehat h_D)\in C_{1-\alpha}(D)\right\}, \] where probability includes the randomness of training and any randomized interval construction. This notation supplies a conventional interpretation, not hypotheses specified by the source.

Source conjecture. The poor coverage performance of resampling-based confidence intervals for a learned classifier's test error is partly caused by test error being a nonsmooth functional of the learned classifier.

The conjecture asks whether nonsmooth dependence in $h\mapsto R_P(h)$ contributes to the failure of such intervals to achieve their nominal coverage. The supplied record does not specify the learning rule, data-generating assumptions, resampling procedure, topology or notion of smoothness, finite-sample or asymptotic regime, or a quantitative meaning of "partly caused." It also does not explicitly distinguish the conditional error $R_P(\widehat h_D)$ from an error averaged over training data. Consequently, the source supports this qualitative conjecture, but does not determine a uniquely quantified theorem or a universal assertion about all classifiers.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether nonsmooth changes in a learned classifier's test error help explain poor coverage of resampling-based confidence intervals. The source leaves the statistical setting and the proposed causal mechanism insufficiently specified for a single precise theorem. Its supplied status is partially solved; this is a dated curation assessment requiring independent review.
