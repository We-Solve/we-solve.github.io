Distribution of Elliptic-Curve Selmer Exact Sequences

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a distribution of isomorphism classes of short exact sequences. It does not specify the domain of the field $k$, the allowed primes $p$, or the family, ordering, and counting convention used to define density. Consequently, the following gives a conditional formulation in the standard number-field setting; that setting is an explicit interpretive assumption, not a hypothesis supplied by the record.

Fix a number field $k$ and a rational prime $p$. Let ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ and ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$ denote the ring of $p$-adic integers and its fraction field. For an elliptic curve $E/k$, let $E(k)$ be its group of $k$-rational points, and put \[ E[p^\infty]=\bigcup_{n\geq 1}E[p^n](\overline{k}). \] Writing $H^1$ for Galois cohomology and letting $v$ range over all places of $k$, define the Tate--Shafarevich group by \[ \operatorname{Sha}(E/k)=\ker\!\left(H^1(k,E)\longrightarrow\prod_v H^1(k_v,E)\right). \] For an abelian group $G$, write \[ G[p^\infty]=\{g\in G: p^n g=0\text{ for some }n\geq 1\}. \] The $p$-primary Selmer group is \[ \operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/k)=\ker\!\left(H^1(k,E[p^\infty])\longrightarrow\prod_v H^1(k_v,E)[p^\infty]\right), \] where the maps are induced by localization and the Kummer sequence. Its natural short exact sequence of ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-modules is \[ \mathcal S_p(E/k):\qquad 0\longrightarrow E(k)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}({\mathbb{Q}}_p/{\mathbb{Z}}_p) \longrightarrow\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/k) \longrightarrow\operatorname{Sha}(E/k)[p^\infty] \longrightarrow 0. \] No finiteness assumption on $\operatorname{Sha}(E/k)$ is added here.

Let \[ S:\qquad 0\longrightarrow A\xrightarrow{i}B\xrightarrow{q}C\longrightarrow 0 \] be a fixed abstract short exact sequence of ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-modules. The assertion $\mathcal S_p(E/k)\cong S$ means that there are ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-linear isomorphisms between the corresponding three terms that commute with both arrows; it concerns the entire exact sequence, not merely the middle module.

Distribution question. Given $S$, determine the density of elliptic curves $E/k$ satisfying $\mathcal S_p(E/k)\cong S$. More explicitly, if a counting convention consists of increasing finite nonempty sets $\mathcal F_T$ of $k$-isomorphism classes of elliptic curves, exhausting a specified family as $T\to\infty$, the corresponding unweighted question is to establish existence and determine the value of \[ \delta_{k,p,\mathcal F}(S) =\lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{\#\{[E]\in\mathcal F_T:\mathcal S_p(E/k)\cong S\}} {\#\mathcal F_T}. \] This formula makes one possible meaning of density explicit; the supplied record does not select these sets, require unweighted counting of isomorphism classes, or specify an alternative convention. A definitive formulation therefore requires the missing field and counting hypotheses. The supplied open-status label and distribution-model assessment are dated curation, not independently established conclusions in this draft.

Short English statement

For a fixed prime $p$, the question asks how frequently the full Mordell--Weil--Selmer--Tate--Shafarevich exact sequence of an elliptic curve over $k$ is isomorphic to a prescribed abstract exact sequence of ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-modules. The field assumptions and the meaning of density are missing from the supplied statement, so the number-field formulation and counting limit above are conditional. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
