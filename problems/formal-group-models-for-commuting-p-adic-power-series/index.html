Formal Group Models for Commuting p-Adic Power Series

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $K$ be the $p$-adic field appearing in the supplied record. Write $\mathcal O_K$ for its valuation ring, $\mathfrak m_K$ for the maximal ideal, and $k_K=\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak m_K$ for the residue field. The record does not explicitly specify whether $K$ must be a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$.

Under the customary integral, origin-fixing interpretation of a Lubin pair, the power series belong to $T\mathcal O_K[[T]]$. Such a series $g$ is called stable when $g'(0)\ne0$ and $g'(0)$ is not a root of unity. Invertibility here means invertibility for composition in $T\mathcal O_K[[T]]$, equivalently $g'(0)\in\mathcal O_K^\times$. These conventions are needed to interpret the statement; the supplied record does not itself define "stable" or "Lubin pair." With these conventions, the hypotheses are \[ f,u\in T\mathcal O_K[[T]],\qquad f\circ u=u\circ f, \] \[ 0\ne f'(0)\in\mathfrak m_K,\qquad u'(0)\in\mathcal O_K^\times\text{ is not a root of unity},\qquad \overline f\ne0\text{ in }k_K[[T]]. \] The last condition is coefficientwise reduction modulo $\mathfrak m_K$.

For clarity, a one-dimensional commutative formal group law over a commutative ring $R$ is a series $S(X,Y)\in R[[X,Y]]$ satisfying \[ S(X,0)=X,\quad S(0,Y)=Y,\quad S(X,Y)=S(Y,X), \] \[ S(S(X,Y),Z)=S(X,S(Y,Z)), \] and admitting a formal inverse. An endomorphism of $S$ is a series $a\in TR[[T]]$ satisfying \[ a(S(X,Y))=S(a(X),a(Y)). \] This is the usual formal-group setting suggested by the question; the record does not expressly specify dimension, commutativity, or the coefficient ring of $S$.

Question, subject to the unresolved qualifications below. For every pair $(f,u)$ satisfying the supplied Lubin-pair hypotheses, does there exist a formal group $S$ and endomorphisms $a,b$ of $S$ to which $f,u$ are simultaneously semiconjugate?

To make this conclusion precise, a common coefficient ring and an admissible nonzero series $h$, with $h(0)=0$, must be specified. One possible direction of simultaneous semiconjugacy is \[ h\circ f=a\circ h,\qquad h\circ u=b\circ h; \] the reverse direction is \[ f\circ h=h\circ a,\qquad u\circ h=h\circ b. \] The supplied record does not determine the direction, whether the same $h$ is required for both series, the permitted coefficient extensions, or additional conditions on $h$. These alternatives are not asserted to be equivalent. Consequently, this is a qualified statement-only formulation, and a fully specified conjecture requires source clarification.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether every commuting stable Lubin pair, consisting of a noninvertible series $f$ with nonzero reduction and an invertible series $u$, admits a simultaneous formal-group model by semiconjugacy. The integral coefficient convention, the formal group's base ring, and the precise semiconjugacy requirements need clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; that literature assessment has not been independently verified.
