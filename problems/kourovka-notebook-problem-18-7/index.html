Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.7

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $m>2$, let $F_m$ be the free group on $m$ generators. For a positive integer $n$, define the free Burnside group of rank $m$ and exponent dividing $n$ by \[ B(m,n)=F_m/\langle\!\langle w^n\mid w\in F_m\rangle\!\rangle, \] where the denominator is the normal closure in $F_m$ of all $n$-th powers. For a group $G$, let $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ be its automorphism group, let \[ \operatorname{Inn}(G)=\{c_g\mid g\in G\},\qquad c_g(x)=gxg^{-1}, \] and define $\operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G)$. A group $H$ is complete if its center $Z(H)=\{h\in H\mid hx=xh\text{ for every }x\in H\}$ is trivial and every automorphism of $H$ is inner.

The question proposed by V. S. Atabekyan is: for every odd integer $n\geq 665$ and every integer $m>2$, is the group \[ H=\operatorname{Out}(B(m,n)) \] complete? Explicitly, does one always have \[ Z(H)=\{1\}\qquad\text{and}\qquad \forall\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(H)\ \exists h\in H\ \forall x\in H, \quad\alpha(x)=hxh^{-1}? \] Here $m$ is interpreted as a finite integer rank, as customary in this notation; the supplied statement does not explicitly discuss infinite ranks. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this label is not an independently verified mathematical or literature assessment.

Short English statement

For every odd integer $n\geq665$ and every finite integer rank $m>2$, must the outer automorphism group of the free Burnside group $B(m,n)$ have trivial center and only inner automorphisms? The question is attributed to V. S. Atabekyan (2014).
