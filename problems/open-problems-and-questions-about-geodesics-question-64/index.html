Open Problems and Questions about Geodesics — Question 64

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth closed manifold, meaning compact and without boundary. The supplied statement does not specify the signature of $g$. To interpret its use of light-like vectors and parallel tensors, take $g$ to be a smooth pseudo-Riemannian metric, with Levi-Civita connection $\nabla$; any intended restriction on its signature remains unresolved.

A smooth $(1,1)$-tensor field is a smooth bundle endomorphism $A:TM\to TM$. It is self-adjoint with respect to $g$, parallel, and nilpotent if, respectively, \[ g(AX,Y)=g(X,AY)\quad\text{for all smooth vector fields }X,Y, \qquad \nabla A=0, \qquad A^k=0\quad\text{for some integer }k\geq 1. \] Assume that such an $A$ exists with $A\not\equiv 0$.

Question (Burns, Matveev, Question 64). Must there exist either $N=M$, with $h=g$, or a smooth two-sheeted covering $p:N\to M$, with $h=p^*g$, for which there is a smooth vector field $V$ satisfying \[ V\not\equiv 0,\qquad \nabla^{h}V=0,\qquad h(V,V)=0\quad\text{everywhere on }N? \] Here $\nabla^h$ denotes the Levi-Civita connection of $h$. The displayed conditions express the requested nonzero light-like parallel vector field using nonzero to mean not identically zero. On a connected manifold a parallel vector field that is not identically zero is nowhere zero. The supplied record does not specify connectedness of the manifold or its double cover, or whether light-like is intended to require a nowhere-zero field on every component; these qualifications require review.

Short English statement

If a closed manifold has a nonzero parallel nilpotent endomorphism of its tangent bundle that is self-adjoint for its metric, must the manifold or a double cover carry a nonzero light-like parallel vector field? The metric signature and connectedness conventions are not specified in the supplied record.
