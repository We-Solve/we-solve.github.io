Math-1092

Nonunimodal Codimension-Four Gorenstein h-Vectors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field. In the usual commutative graded interpretation, a standard graded Artinian $k$-algebra is a finite-dimensional commutative algebra \[ A=\bigoplus_{i\geq 0}A_i,\qquad A_0=k, \] generated as a $k$-algebra by $A_1$. Put $\mathfrak m=\bigoplus_{i>0}A_i$. Such an algebra is Gorenstein if its socle \[ \operatorname{Soc}(A)=\{a\in A: \mathfrak m a=0\} \] has dimension one over $k$. Its codimension is $\dim_k A_1$. If $s=\max\{i:A_i\neq 0\}$, its $h$-vector is \[ h(A)=(h_0,\ldots,h_s),\qquad h_i=\dim_k A_i. \] This vector is unimodal if there exists $t\in\{0,\ldots,s\}$ such that \[ h_0\leq\cdots\leq h_t\geq h_{t+1}\geq\cdots\geq h_s, \] with empty chains at the endpoints interpreted vacuously; it is non-unimodal otherwise.

For a specified field $k$, the existence question is: does there exist a standard graded Artinian Gorenstein $k$-algebra $A$ satisfying $\dim_k A_1=4$ whose $h$-vector is non-unimodal?

The supplied source does not specify the base field, its characteristic, or whether the field is fixed or may be chosen as part of the example. Consequently, this formulation specifies the question relative to $k$, while the intended quantifier and restrictions on $k$ remain unresolved.

Short English statement

Can a standard graded Artinian Gorenstein algebra of codimension four have a Hilbert function that fails to increase weakly to a peak and then decrease weakly? The supplied statement leaves the base field and its characteristic unspecified.
