Math-14956

Weighted Centers on Bounded-Dimensional Median Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite connected undirected graph $G=(V,E)$, let $d_G(u,v)$ be the minimum number of edges in a path from $u$ to $v$, and define the interval \[ I_G(u,v)=\{x\in V:d_G(u,v)=d_G(u,x)+d_G(x,v)\}. \] The graph is a median graph if, for every $u,v,w\in V$, the intersection \[ I_G(u,v)\cap I_G(v,w)\cap I_G(w,u) \] contains exactly one vertex. Let $Q_k$ be the graph on $\{0,1\}^k$ whose adjacent vertices differ in exactly one coordinate. The cube dimension of a median graph is the largest integer $k\geq 0$ for which it contains an isometric copy of $Q_k$. Bounded cube dimension means that this dimension is at most a fixed constant $D$, independent of the number of vertices.

For the ordinary vertex Center problem, the eccentricity and radius are \[ e_G(v)=\max_{u\in V}d_G(v,u),\qquad \operatorname{rad}(G)=\min_{v\in V}e_G(v), \] and a center is a vertex attaining this minimum. The supplied source says that the ordinary Center problem is the all-one-profile case of its Weighted Center problem. However, it does not supply the definition of a profile or the formula for the weighted objective. Thus the ordinary objective above does not determine the intended weighted objective.

Source question, with unresolved specifications. Is there an almost-linear-time algorithm for the Weighted Center problem on median graphs of bounded cube dimension? Under the usual fixed-dimension reading, this asks for an algorithm for every fixed dimension bound $D$, valid for every admissible input graph with cube dimension at most $D$ and every admissible weight profile. The supplied record does not specify the admissible weights, the weighted objective, the permitted locations of a center, or the required output. Consequently, this is a statement of the source's algorithmic target rather than a fully specified computational problem. The finite graph and vertex-center conventions above describe the ordinary graph setting; their applicability to every part of the intended weighted problem requires confirmation. The meaning of almost-linear time, its input-size parameter, and its dependence on $D$ and weight encoding also remain unspecified.

Short English statement

The question asks whether Weighted Center can be computed in almost-linear time on median graphs when cube dimension is bounded by a fixed constant. The supplied record omits the definition of the weighted objective and several computational conventions, so a complete precise formulation requires further source clarification. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
