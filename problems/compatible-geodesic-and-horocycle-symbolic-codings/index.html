Compatible Geodesic and Horocycle Symbolic Codings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H=\{x+iy\in{\mathbb{C}}:y>0\}$ carry the hyperbolic metric ${\,\mathrm{d}} s^2=({\,\mathrm{d}} x^2+{\,\mathrm{d}} y^2)/y^2$, and let $\Gamma=\operatorname{PSL}_2({\mathbb{Z}})$. The modular surface $M=\Gamma\backslash\mathbb H$ is understood as a hyperbolic orbifold. Its geodesic flow acts on its oriented unit tangent bundle, identified with $X=\Gamma\backslash\operatorname{PSL}_2({\mathbb{R}})$, rather than on $M$ alone. In the standard right-action convention, write \[ g_t(\Gamma u)=\Gamma u\begin{pmatrix}e^{t/2}&0\\0&e^{-t/2}\end{pmatrix},\qquad t\in{\mathbb{R}}. \] For comparison, one convention for the horocycle flow on the same space is \[ h_s(\Gamma u)=\Gamma u\begin{pmatrix}1&s\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad s\in{\mathbb{R}}. \] These formulas fix notation; the supplied record does not specify the horocycle parametrization used in the intended comparison.

A usual framework for a symbolic coding of $g_t$ is a shift $\sigma$ on a space of symbolic sequences $\Sigma$, a positive roof function $r$, and a coding map from the suspension \[ \Sigma^r=\{(\omega,v):\omega\in\Sigma,\ 0\leq v\leq r(\omega)\}/\left((\omega,r(\omega))\sim(\sigma\omega,0)\right) \] to the relevant part of $X$, intertwining suspension time translation with $g_t$. The source does not prescribe the alphabet, admissibility conditions, regularity, coverage, multiplicity, or measure-theoretic properties of the coding; this framework explains the terminology without imposing those unspecified requirements.

Requested construction, as formulated in the supplied registry. Construct a symbolic coding of the geodesic flow on the modular surface that recovers the gap statistics obtained from the corresponding horocycle-flow coding.
