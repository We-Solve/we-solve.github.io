Math-1625

Quadratic-chain characterization of the Aubry set

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact connected Riemannian manifold without boundary and $f$ a smooth vector field with flow $\phi^t$. Connectedness and absence of boundary state the standard framework of the aid; the original says only compact manifold. Set \[ L_f(x,v)=\tfrac12\|v-f(x)\|_x^2. \] Source questions (Albert Fathi and the appended Hamilton--Jacobi item). Characterize the zero-cohomology Aubry set dynamically, and in particular determine whether it contains the flow's chain recurrent set. Also determine when zero is the unique viscosity solution of the displayed stationary Hamilton--Jacobi equation.

Two source defects require explicit separation. The invariant zero-action graph in $TM$ is $\{(x,f(x)):x\in M\}$, not generally the tangent zero section. The Legendre dual of $L_f$ is \[ H_f^+(x,p)=\tfrac12\|p\|_x^2+p(f(x)), \] whereas the source prints $H_f^-(x,p)=\tfrac12\|p\|_x^2-p(f(x))$. The latter is dual to $L_{-f}$. The cotangent zero section is invariant for these Hamiltonians. Furthermore every constant solves either equation at level zero, so uniqueness must mean modulo constants, or after normalization such as $u(x_*)=0$.

To distinguish projected and lifted sets, define the time-$t$ action \[ A_t(x,y)=\inf_{\gamma(0)=x,\,\gamma(t)=y} \int_0^t L_f(\gamma(s),\dot\gamma(s))\,\mathrm ds, \] with absolutely continuous curves. At the zero critical value, let \[ h_f(x,y)=\liminf_{t\to\infty}A_t(x,y),\qquad \mathcal A_f=\{x\in M:h_f(x,x)=0\}. \] The lifted Aubry set is on the zero-action graph; the dynamical comparison is between its projection $\mathcal A_f\subseteq M$ and recurrent subsets of $M$.

For $T>0$, a returning chain at $x$ consists of $x_0=x,x_1,\ldots,x_m=x$, $m\geq1$, and times $t_i\geq T$, $0\leq i<m$. Its jumps are \[ e_i=d(\phi^{t_i}(x_i),x_{i+1}). \] Define $\mathcal R_2(f)$ by requiring, for every $T>0$ and $\varepsilon>0$, such a chain with $\sum_i e_i^2<\varepsilon$. Define strong chain recurrence $\operatorname{SCR}(f)$ using $\sum_i e_i<\varepsilon$, and ordinary chain recurrence $\operatorname{CR}(f)$ using $\max_i e_i<\varepsilon$, with the same quantifiers and the fixed Riemannian distance.

Reported characterization and scope. The aid reports \[ \operatorname{SCR}(f)\subseteq\mathcal R_2(f) =\mathcal A_f\subseteq\operatorname{CR}(f). \] It also reports that all viscosity solutions of $H_f^+(x,Du)=0$ are constant, equivalently normalized uniqueness, exactly when $\mathcal R_2(f)=M$. For the literally printed minus Hamiltonian, the corresponding statement must be applied to $-f$; its sign is not silently identified with the dual of $L_f$.

Under the published Fathi--Figalli--Rifford Mather-disconnectedness hypotheses, including every smooth $f$ in dimension at most three according to the aid, it reports the stronger equality \[ \mathcal R_2(f)=\operatorname{CR}(f). \] Thus the requested reverse containment is reported in that stated scope. The exact general Mather-disconnectedness hypotheses and full primary references are absent; no unrestricted higher-dimensional reverse inclusion is claimed. Disconnected manifolds require componentwise constants/normalizations, and boundary manifolds require additional flow and variational boundary assumptions.

Short English statement

The aid characterizes the projected Aubry set by returning flow chains with arbitrarily small total squared jumps. It preserves the source's tangent/cotangent and Legendre-sign defects, normalizes the uniqueness question, and restricts equality with ordinary chain recurrence to the reported Mather-disconnectedness scope, including smooth dimensions at most three.
