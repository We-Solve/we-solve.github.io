Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.124

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, let $G'=[G,G]$ be its commutator subgroup and let $G''=[G',G']$. The group $G$ is metabelian if $G''=\{1\}$, equivalently if $G'$ is abelian. A finite presentation is an expression \[ P=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\mid r_1,\ldots,r_m\rangle, \] where $n,m$ are finite nonnegative integers and each $r_j$ is a word in the generators and their inverses. It defines the group $G_P=F_n/\langle\!\langle r_1,\ldots,r_m\rangle\!\rangle$, where $F_n$ is the free group on the indicated generators and the denominator is the normal closure of the relators.

Fix a standard effective encoding of finite presentations by natural numbers. A set of codes is recursively enumerable if an algorithm lists exactly its members, with repetitions allowed. The question, under the usual interpretation through finite presentations, is whether \[ \mathcal M=\{\operatorname{code}(P): P\text{ is a finite presentation and }G_P''=\{1\}\} \] is recursively enumerable. Equivalently, can an algorithm enumerate finite presentations of metabelian groups so that every finitely presented metabelian group occurs up to isomorphism and no group outside this class occurs?

The source does not explicitly specify an encoding or an enumeration convention. This formulation uses the conventional effective presentation interpretation and does not require distinct isomorphism types to occur only once. The supplied registry labels the problem open while recording an explicitly unverified AI claim of an affirmative answer; that claim is not treated here as an established result.

Short English statement

Can an algorithm list finite presentations for all finitely presented metabelian groups, covering every such group up to isomorphism and producing only metabelian groups? Repetitions are allowed. The supplied open label and unverified affirmative solution claim are source qualifications requiring review.
