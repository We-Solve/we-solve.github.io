Math-3553

The Tits Alternative for Analytic Surface Symplectomorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ denote a compact surface with the real-analytic structure implicit in the source, and let $\mu$ denote its prescribed area datum. The source does not specify whether boundary is allowed, whether connectedness or orientability is required, or the regularity and precise meaning of $\mu$. These conventions remain unresolved. When $\mu$ is an area form, the intended group is \[ \operatorname{Symp}^{\omega}_{\mu}(M) =\{f:M\to M\mid f\text{ is a real-analytic diffeomorphism and }f^{*}\mu=\mu\}, \] with composition as its group operation. If $\mu$ instead denotes an area measure, the preservation condition is $\mu(f^{-1}(A))=\mu(A)$ for every measurable set $A$. These interpretations must be distinguished, particularly concerning orientability and orientation-reversing maps; the supplied record does not settle the convention.

A group $G$ is finitely generated if $G=\langle S\rangle$ for some finite subset $S\subseteq G$. For a group $H$, set $H^{(0)}=H$ and \[ H^{(n+1)}=\left\langle xyx^{-1}y^{-1}:x,y\in H^{(n)}\right\rangle. \] The group $H$ is solvable if $H^{(r)}=\{e\}$ for some integer $r\geq 0$. A group $G$ is virtually solvable if it has a solvable subgroup $H\leq G$ of finite index. Let $F_2=\langle a,b\mid\ \rangle$ be the free group on two generators; containing a nonabelian free group is equivalent to admitting an injective homomorphism $F_2\hookrightarrow G$.

Conjecture (supplied surface formulation). For every compact surface $M$, with the analytic structure and preserved area datum $\mu$ understood as above, and every finitely generated subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{Symp}^{\omega}_{\mu}(M)$, \[ \left(\exists H\leq G:\ [G:H]<\infty\ \text{and}\ \exists r\geq 0,\ H^{(r)}=\{e\}\right) \quad\text{or}\quad \left(\exists\text{ an injective homomorphism }F_2\to G\right). \] The source leaves the admissible choices and quantification of $\mu$, as well as the surface conventions, implicit. Thus this is a statement-only transcription requiring those qualifications to be resolved before a fully specified formulation is accepted. The accompanying literature assessment concerns circle diffeomorphisms, whereas this conjecture concerns area-preserving surface diffeomorphisms; that assessment does not establish the literature status of this surface formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asserts that every finitely generated group of real-analytic area-preserving diffeomorphisms of a compact surface has a solvable subgroup of finite index or contains a free group on two generators. Surface and area-preservation conventions remain unspecified. The supplied open label and verification claims are curation metadata; the accompanying circle-diffeomorphism literature assessment does not verify this surface conjecture.
