Math-3469

Pure Resolutions of Paracanonical Level Curves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The usual interpretation of a level-$\ell$ curve $[C,\eta]$ is a smooth, connected projective curve $C$ together with a nontrivial line bundle $\eta\in\operatorname{Pic}^{0}(C)$ of exact finite order $\ell\geq2$: thus $\eta^{\otimes\ell}\cong\mathcal O_C$ and $\eta^{\otimes m}\not\cong\mathcal O_C$ for $1\leq m<\ell$. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, its characteristic, the permitted levels, or the precise moduli convention; this interpretation therefore requires confirmation.

Let $g$ be the genus of $C$, let $K_C$ denote its canonical line bundle, and put $L=K_C\otimes\eta$ and $V=H^0(C,L)$. Under the usual interpretation, $\dim V=g-1$. When the complete linear series of $L$ defines an embedding, its paracanonical embedding is \[ \varphi_L:C\hookrightarrow\mathbb P(V^{\vee})\cong\mathbb P^{g-2}. \] Let $S=\operatorname{Sym}V$ with its standard grading, and let $A=S/I_{\varphi_L(C)}$ be the homogeneous coordinate ring. Its minimal graded free resolution is called pure if each nonzero free module in homological degree $i$ has the form $S(-d_i)^{\beta_i}$ for a single degree $d_i$.

For $p\geq1$, define $K_{p,1}(C,L)$ as the middle cohomology of the Koszul complex \[ \bigwedge^{p+1}V\otimes H^0(C,\mathcal O_C) \xrightarrow{d} \bigwedge^pV\otimes H^0(C,L) \xrightarrow{d} \bigwedge^{p-1}V\otimes H^0(C,L^{\otimes2}), \] where $d$ is the alternating differential induced by multiplication of sections.

The supplied question is: for a general level-$\ell$ curve $[C,\eta]$ of every even genus $g\geq6$, does the paracanonically embedded curve have a pure minimal graded resolution of its homogeneous coordinate ring? The record presents this as equivalent to \[ K_{g/2-2,1}(C,K_C\otimes\eta)=0. \] Here "general" refers to a dense Zariski-open subset of the intended moduli space of level curves. That moduli space, the admissible levels and ground fields, and any exceptional cases are not specified in the record. The embedding and the asserted equivalence are retained as source-level qualifications; their hypotheses require mathematical review.

Short English statement

For a general curve of even genus $g\geq6$ equipped with a torsion line bundle $\eta$ of level $\ell$, ask whether its paracanonical homogeneous coordinate ring has a pure resolution. The supplied record states the equivalent target $K_{g/2-2,1}(C,K_C\otimes\eta)=0$. The ground field, level restrictions, and possible exceptions remain unresolved.
