Polynomial Identities and Symbolic Calculus for Noncommutative Algebras

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for the polynomial identities of a given noncommutative algebra $A$, particularly a matrix algebra, and for the symbolic calculus resulting from those identities. It does not specify the coefficient domain, associativity or unit conventions, the algebra $A$, or what constitutes the requested symbolic calculus. Consequently, it does not determine a unique mathematical problem.

A conditional formulation of the polynomial-identity part is as follows. Suppose that $K$ is a field and $A$ is an associative $K$-algebra. These are additional conventions for this formulation, not hypotheses established by the source. For each integer $m\geq 1$, let $K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_m\rangle_+$ denote the noncommutative polynomials over $K$ with zero constant term. Define \[ \operatorname{Id}_{K,m}(A)=\left\{f\in K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_m\rangle_+ : f(a_1,\ldots,a_m)=0\ \text{for every }(a_1,\ldots,a_m)\in A^m\right\}. \] Here evaluation replaces each variable by its assigned element of $A$, preserving the order of multiplication in every monomial. The zero-constant-term convention permits evaluation without requiring a multiplicative identity; the record does not prescribe this convention.

Requested task, conditional on these conventions. For the given algebra $A$, characterize $\operatorname{Id}_{K,m}(A)$ for every $m\geq 1$. The matrix-algebra specialization would take $A=M_n(K)$, the algebra of $n\times n$ matrices over $K$, for a specified integer $n\geq 1$, restricting to noncommutative instances when required. Neither $K$ nor $n$ is specified in the record. Construct the symbolic calculus intended by the source from these identities; however, its objects, operations, equivalence relation, and required properties are unspecified, so this second request cannot yet be stated precisely. The conditional formulation above does not resolve that missing target.

Short English statement

The record asks which noncommutative polynomial expressions vanish under every substitution into a given algebra, especially a matrix algebra, and what symbolic calculus follows. A conventional associative formulation is given conditionally, but the coefficient domain, particular algebra, and meaning of symbolic calculus remain unspecified.
