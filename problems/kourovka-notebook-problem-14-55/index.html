Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.55

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let ${\mathbb{F}}_p={\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$. Under the standard interpretation of the Nottingham group, put \[ J=N({\mathbb{F}}_p)=\left\{f(t)=t+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nt^n : a_n\in{\mathbb{F}}_p\right\}, \] with group operation given by composition of formal power series. Equip $J$ with the topology whose neighbourhood basis at the identity consists of \[ J_m=\{f\in J:f(t)\equiv t\pmod{t^{m+1}}\},\qquad m\geq1. \] This is the usual pro-$p$ topology on the Nottingham group.

A pro-$p$ group $G$ is finitely presented as a pro-$p$ group if there are integers $d,r\geq0$ and elements $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_r$ of the free pro-$p$ group $F_d$ on $d$ generators such that \[ G\cong F_d/\overline{\langle\!\langle\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_r\rangle\!\rangle} \] as topological groups, where the denominator is the smallest closed normal subgroup of $F_d$ containing all the $\rho_i$.

The remaining assertion, part (b) of the supplied source, is that $N({\mathbb{F}}_2)$ is finitely presented. With finite presentation understood in the pro-$2$ sense just defined, the requested assertion is therefore \[ \exists\,d,r\geq0\;\exists\,\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_r\in F_d: \quad N({\mathbb{F}}_2)\cong F_d/\overline{\langle\!\langle\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_r\rangle\!\rangle}. \] Here $F_d$ is free pro-$2$, and the isomorphism is continuous with continuous inverse.

This is a provisional explicit interpretation: the supplied excerpt refers to Archive problem 12.24 for the definition of $N({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})$, but that definition is absent. It also does not explicitly specify whether finite presentation is meant in the pro-$p$ or abstract group category. The definitions and category used above require confirmation against the referenced source; abstract finite presentation would be a different assertion.

For context, archived part (a) asks for finite presentation of $N({\mathbb{F}}_p)$ for every prime $p>2$. The supplied archive reports this part as proved by M. V. Ershov. That report is preserved as supplied attribution, not as an independently verified theorem or literature assessment.

Short English statement

Kourovka problem 14.55(b), proposed by C. R. Leedham-Green, asks whether the Nottingham group over ${\mathbb{F}}_2$ is finitely presented. The displayed formulation uses finite presentation in the pro-$2$ category, subject to checking the omitted source definition and intended category. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved and reports the cases $p>2$ as solved by M. V. Ershov.
