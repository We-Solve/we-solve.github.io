Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For fixed $k\geq 4$, let $t=k+1$ under joint WL refinement or $t=k$ under separate refinement. Given finite domains $D_i$ and pairwise label maps $\lambda ^i_ij:D_i\to L_ij$, can one explicitly produce two equal-order uncolored simple graphs that are k-WL-equivalent exactly when no choice $d_i\in D_i$ satisfies $\lambda ^i_ij(d_i)=\lambda ^j_ij(d_j)$ for all $i<j$, with common graph order at most $A[t\cdot 2^{t-2+3binom(t-1,2)}+12binom(t,3)]$ when all domain and label cardinalities are $\leq A$?

Parity witnesses for Weisfeiler–Leman equivalence

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 133; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that compatible-choice systems can be encoded by graph pairs whose fixed-dimensional WL equivalence is exactly absence of a successful choice, with specified early-round detection and conditional ETH consequences.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Fix $k\ge4$ and put $t=k+1$ for joint correlated-replacement $k$-WL, or $t=k$ for separate-coordinate refinement. A choice system has exactly $t$ finite domains $D_1,\ldots,D_t$ and pairwise label maps $\lambda_{ij}^i:D_i\to L_{ij}$, $\lambda_{ij}^j:D_j\to L_{ij}$. A successful choice satisfies $\lambda_{ij}^i(d_i)=\lambda_{ij}^j(d_j)$ for every pair. The claimed explicit uncolored simple graphs of equal order satisfy \[ X_0\equiv_kX_{\star}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{there is no successful choice}. \] Both common-color separate comparison and pure-tuple comparison within a disjoint union are allowed. If all domain and label sets have size at most $A\ge1$, the common graph order is bounded by \[ A\left(t2^{t-2+3\binom{t-1}{2}}+12\binom t3\right). \] If a successful choice exists, the histograms disagree by round two in the joint convention and by round one in the separate convention.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 133; 2026-09-25), homogeneous and affine parity lifts share a typed base graph. A dual parity certificate compares their lift counts, and bounded-treewidth homomorphism witnesses recover a compatible choice, making the refinement dimension track the number of domains.
