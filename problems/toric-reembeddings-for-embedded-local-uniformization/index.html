Toric Reembeddings for Embedded Local Uniformization

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote the field appearing in the source, and let $X\subset\mathbb{A}^{n}(k)$ be the given affine variety. No assumptions on the characteristic or algebraic closedness of $k$, or on the dimension, irreducibility, or reducedness conventions for $X$, are supplied. A re-embedding means a closed immersion \[ j\colon X\hookrightarrow\mathbb{A}^{N}_{k} \] for some finite integer $N$, identifying the same variety $X$ with its image. Here $\mathbb{A}^{N}_{k}$ expresses the algebraic ambient space; the source writes $\mathbb{A}^{N}(k)$ without distinguishing the variety from its set of rational points. A toric morphism, in the usual terminology, is a morphism between toric varieties whose restriction to their dense tori is a homomorphism and which is equivariant for that homomorphism. The source does not specify the toric structures or the class of toric maps allowed.

Construction problem as supplied. Given the affine embedding $X\subset\mathbb{A}^{n}(k)$, without a prescribed embedded resolution, construct suitable re-embeddings $j\colon X\hookrightarrow\mathbb{A}^{N}_{k}$ and toric maps that yield embedded local uniformizations of $X$.

Unresolved qualifications. This is a construction request with an incompletely specified success condition. In the usual integral setting, local uniformization is formulated relative to a valuation ring $V$ of the function field $k(X)$: one seeks a suitable birational model with a center whose local ring is regular and is dominated by $V$. Domination means that the local ring is contained in $V$ and its maximal ideal is the contraction of the maximal ideal of $V$. This explanation is conditional terminology, not an added integrality hypothesis or a selected interpretation of the source. The source specifies neither valuations nor centers, nor whether the construction is to work separately for each valuation or simultaneously for a collection. It also does not define the additional ambient and boundary conditions meant by embedded local uniformization, or the domains, targets, and modification properties of the toric maps. Consequently, a fully quantified existence assertion cannot be extracted without further source clarification. The phrase "without a prescribed embedded resolution" states that such a resolution is not given as input; it does not assert that an embedded resolution does not exist.

Short English statement

Construct new affine embeddings of the given variety and suitable toric maps that provide embedded local uniformizations, without starting from a prescribed embedded resolution. The field hypotheses, valuation quantifiers, permitted toric maps, and precise embedded regularity and boundary conditions remain unspecified in the supplied record.
