Normality of Varieties of Minimal Rational Tangents

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a variety $Y$, normality means that every local ring $\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ is an integrally closed domain. For an irreducible variety $X$, a property holds at a general point if it holds throughout some nonempty Zariski open subset of $X$.

To define a variety of minimal rational tangents, one must specify the ambient variety $X$ and the relevant family $\mathcal K$ of minimal rational curves. In a setting where these data and tangent directions are defined, its usual reduced underlying variety at a smooth point $x$ is \[ \mathcal C_x=\overline{\{[T_xC]\in\mathbb P(T_xX): C\text{ belongs to }\mathcal K,\ x\in C,\ C\text{ is smooth at }x\}}_{\mathrm{red}}. \] Here $[T_xC]$ denotes the line in $T_xX$ tangent to $C$. This description does not specify which family is intended or what minimality means in the source's setting.

Normality conjecture, as supplied. The variety of minimal rational tangents $\mathcal C_x$ is normal at a general point $x\in X$. Once the intended hypotheses and family have been fixed, the general-point assertion has the form \[ \exists\,U\subseteq X\text{ nonempty Zariski open}\quad \forall\,x\in U,\qquad \mathcal C_x\text{ is normal}. \] Normality here concerns the whole variety $\mathcal C_x$; the word "general" qualifies the base point $x$.

The supplied record does not state the hypotheses on $X$, its ground field, the choice or quantification of $\mathcal K$, or the intended scheme structure of $\mathcal C_x$. Consequently, the displayed assertion records the intended question but cannot be promoted to a fully specified universal conjecture from this record alone. The reduced construction above is explanatory and requires confirmation against the source context.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether the entire variety of minimal rational tangent directions is normal when the base point $x$ is general in $X$. The ambient hypotheses and the family of minimal rational curves are missing, so the precise scope requires source review. The record labels the problem open; that label is supplied dated curation.
