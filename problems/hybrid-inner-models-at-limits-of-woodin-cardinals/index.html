Hybrid Inner Models at Limits of Woodin Cardinals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $V$ denote the ambient set-theoretic universe. For an infinite cardinal $\theta$, write \[ H_\theta^V=\{x\in V:|\operatorname{tc}(\{x\})|^V<\theta\}, \] where $\operatorname{tc}$ denotes transitive closure. Write $\operatorname{Ord}$ for the ordinals and $\mathrm{AD}$ for the axiom of determinacy: every length-$\omega$, perfect-information game in which the players alternately choose natural numbers is determined. The terms measurable, Woodin, and strong refer to their standard large-cardinal meanings. Interpret "$\kappa$ is a limit of Woodin and strong cardinals" as requiring both classes separately to be unbounded below $\kappa$: \[ \forall\alpha<\kappa\ \exists\delta,\eta<\kappa\, \left(\alpha<\delta\land\alpha<\eta\land \delta\text{ is Woodin}\land\eta\text{ is strong}\right). \] Here $\operatorname{Coll}(\omega,{<}\kappa)$ denotes the usual Lévy collapse of the ordinals below $\kappa$ to countable size.

The supplied conjecture assumes that there is no inner model with a Woodin cardinal which is a limit of Woodin cardinals. It asks whether every measurable $\kappa$ which is a limit of Woodin and strong cardinals admits hybrid structures \[ M_0\mathrel{\triangleleft}M_1\subseteq H_{\kappa^+} \] such that, for every generic $g\subseteq\operatorname{Coll}(\omega,{<}\kappa)$, all four requirements hold:

  1. $H^-\mathrel{\triangleleft}M_0$.
  2. $L(M_0^\omega,\Gamma,\mathbb R)\models\mathrm{AD}$.
  3. $\operatorname{Ord}\cap M_0$ is the largest cardinal of $M_1$.
  4. $M_1$ is close to $V$ at $\kappa$.
The existential choice of $M_0,M_1$ precedes the universal quantifier over generic filters, as in the supplied cleaned statement.

Short English statement

Assuming there is no inner model with a Woodin cardinal that is itself a limit of Woodin cardinals, the conjecture asks for two nested hybrid structures below $H_{\kappa^+}$ whenever $\kappa$ is measurable and a limit of both Woodin and strong cardinals. The same structures must satisfy the four displayed requirements for every indicated collapse generic. Several essential structures, parameters, and the meaning of closeness are undefined in the supplied record, so a fully precise formulation requires further source context.
