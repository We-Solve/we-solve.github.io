Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.113

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $x\in{\mathbb{C}}$ and $\rho\in[0,1]$ satisfy $|x|\leq\rho$, and set \[ g_x(z)=\frac{z}{1-xz},\qquad \gamma=(1+\rho)^{-2}. \] The rational function $g_x$ is holomorphic in $\mathbb D$. Retaining the notation imported by the source from Problems 6.111 and 6.112, the known assertion is \[ N_\gamma(g_x)\subseteq S^*. \] Sheil-Small and Silvia ask whether the corresponding inclusion \[ \Sigma_\gamma(g_x)\subseteq S^* \] holds for every such $x,\rho$ with this exact value of $\gamma$. Inclusion is meant, without an additional claim that it is proper.

The supplied record does not define the neighborhood or transform constructions $N_\gamma$ and $\Sigma_\gamma$, their ambient function class, or $S^*$. Consequently the displayed source-specific inclusion is the precise available target, but is not a self-contained function-theoretic statement until the preceding definitions are supplied.

The source also says that not all convex functions belong to $S^*$ and concludes that the known $N_\gamma(g_x)$ inclusion cannot be replaced by $N_\gamma(g)$ for arbitrary convex $g$. This qualification must be retained, but it conflicts with reading $S^*$ as the usual normalized starlike class, since normalized convex functions are starlike about their image of $0$. Thus the meaning of $S^*$, the normalization, or the extracted assertion needs checking; a familiar notation must not silently be substituted for the undefined source class. The supplied summary reports openness as of 2018 and no definitive resolution located.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a known inclusion for $N_\gamma$-neighborhoods of $z/(1-xz)$ also holds for the source's $\Sigma_\gamma$ construction, at the same radius $(1+\rho)^{-2}$. Both constructions and the target class are missing from the extraction, and a convexity remark prevents assuming the usual meaning of $S^*$.
