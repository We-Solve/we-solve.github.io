Math-7594

Parity conjecture for elliptic curves

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

By the Mordell--Weil rank of $E/K$ mean the integer $r$ in $E(K)\cong E(K)_{\mathrm{tors}}\oplus{\mathbb{Z}}^r$. The global root number is $w(E/K)=\prod_v w(E/K_v)\in\{\pm1\}$, the product of normalized local epsilon-factor signs; almost every finite-place factor is one and each archimedean factor is $-1$. The target is \[ w(E/K)=(-1)^r \] for every elliptic curve over every number field. Root numbers are defined locally without presuming a global analytic continuation theorem. The claim concerns the algebraic rank, not merely parity of a conjectural analytic order of vanishing or a Selmer-group rank.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Does the product of an elliptic curve's local root-number signs always determine whether its rational-point rank over the number field is even or odd?
