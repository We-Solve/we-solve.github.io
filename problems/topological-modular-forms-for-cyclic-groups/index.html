Math-7184

Topological Modular Forms for Cyclic Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}$, and let $C_n={\mathbb{Z}}/n{\mathbb{Z}}$ be the cyclic group of order $n$. The source uses $\operatorname{TMF}$ for topological modular forms and asks about the spectrum denoted by $\operatorname{TMF}^{C_n}$.

Requested calculation. For every positive integer $n$, explicitly determine the equivalence type of the spectrum $\operatorname{TMF}^{C_n}$, or at least determine all its integer-graded homotopy groups \[ \pi_k\left(\operatorname{TMF}^{C_n}\right),\qquad k\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] For an ordinary spectrum $X$, the notation $\pi_k(X)=[\Sigma^k\mathbb S,X]$ denotes stable homotopy classes of maps from the $k$-fold suspension of the sphere spectrum.

Unresolved qualification. The supplied record does not specify the equivariant construction underlying $\operatorname{TMF}^{C_n}$. In particular, it does not identify a $C_n$-action or a genuine $C_n$-equivariant refinement, or state whether the superscript means categorical fixed points, homotopy fixed points, or another construction. It also does not specify a localization or completion. Consequently, the displayed request preserves the source notation but defines a unique calculation only after these conventions have been supplied. The integer grading above applies if the intended result is an ordinary fixed-point spectrum; if the intended target is an equivariant spectrum itself, the grading and homotopy invariant must also be specified.

Short English statement

For every positive integer $n$, calculate the spectrum written $\operatorname{TMF}^{C_n}$, or at least its homotopy groups in every degree. The supplied record does not define the equivariant construction or fixed-point convention, so the intended spectrum requires clarification. Its partially solved status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
