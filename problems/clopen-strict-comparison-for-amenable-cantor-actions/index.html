Math-4179

Clopen Strict Comparison for Amenable Cantor Actions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a countably infinite discrete amenable group. Here amenability means that for every finite $K\subseteq G$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a nonempty finite $F\subseteq G$ such that $|kF\mathbin{\triangle}F|<\varepsilon|F|$ for every $k\in K$. Let $X$ be a Cantor space, meaning a nonempty compact metrizable totally disconnected space without isolated points. Consider an action $G\curvearrowright X$ by homeomorphisms, written $(g,x)\mapsto gx$. The action is minimal if $\overline{Gx}=X$ for every $x\in X$. No freeness assumption is included in the supplied statement.

Write $M_G(X)$ for the set of Borel probability measures $\mu$ on $X$ satisfying $\mu(gE)=\mu(E)$ for every $g\in G$ and every Borel set $E\subseteq X$. A subset of $X$ is clopen if it is both open and closed. The action has clopen strict comparison if, for all clopen $A,B\subseteq X$, \[ \left(\forall\mu\in M_G(X),\ \mu(A)<\mu(B)\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ \begin{gathered} \exists n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\ \exists A_1,\ldots,A_n\subseteq X\text{ clopen},\ \exists g_1,\ldots,g_n\in G:\\ A=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{n}A_i,\qquad g_iA_i\subseteq B\quad(1\leq i\leq n),\\ g_iA_i\cap g_jA_j=\varnothing\quad(1\leq i<j\leq n). \end{gathered} \] The empty partition is allowed when $A=\varnothing$. The measure inequality is required separately for every invariant probability measure; no numerical gap is specified in the hypothesis.

Question. Does every minimal action by homeomorphisms of every countably infinite discrete amenable group on a Cantor space have clopen strict comparison?

The topological meaning of action used here is the conventional interpretation of the supplied Cantor action formulation. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved, but its literature assessment does not specify the precise hypotheses or full scope of the established subcases. Those claims require separate review and do not restrict the universal question above.

Short English statement

For every minimal action of a countably infinite amenable group on a Cantor space, must a clopen set that is smaller than another for every invariant probability measure split into finitely many clopen pieces whose group translates fit disjointly inside the other set? The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
