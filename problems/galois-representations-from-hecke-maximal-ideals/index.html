Galois Representations from Hecke Maximal Ideals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathbb T=\mathbb T_S(K)$ for the Hecke algebra named in the supplied record. Here $S$ and $K$ are source parameters whose meanings and permitted values are not specified. Let $\operatorname{Max}(\mathbb T)$ denote its set of maximal ideals, and let $\mathbb T_{\mathfrak m}$ denote localization at $\mathfrak m$, under the intended commutative Hecke-algebra convention.

Conjectural statement, with unresolved specifications. For every \[ \mathfrak m\in\operatorname{Max}(\mathbb T_S(K)), \] there exists a continuous semisimple residual Galois representation $\bar\rho_{\mathfrak m}$ satisfying all three requirements named in the source:

  1. its characteristic polynomials agree with the prescribed Hecke characteristic polynomials;
  2. its complex conjugations are maximally odd;
  3. it satisfies the prescribed Fontaine--Laffaille condition, described in the record as having Fontaine--Laffaille Hodge--Tate weights.
If $\mathfrak m$ is non-Eisenstein, the requested representation further admits a lift $\rho_{\mathfrak m}$ with analogous properties, and the associated Taylor--Wiles deformation map is an isomorphism: \[ R_S\longrightarrow\mathbb T_S(K)_{\mathfrak m}. \] The supplied statement places both the lifting assertion and the isomorphism assertion under the non-Eisenstein hypothesis.

Limits of the formulation. This is a schematic transcription of the requested assertions, not a fully specified conjecture. The record does not identify the underlying number field, Galois group, representation dimension, residual characteristic, coefficient fields or rings, or the topology defining continuity. It does not define the Hecke algebra, its cohomological realization, the Hecke characteristic polynomials, or the places where their comparison is required. It also does not define maximal oddness, the non-Eisenstein condition, the deformation problem represented by $R_S$, or the displayed map. Hodge--Tate weights ordinarily concern characteristic-zero local representations; the residual Fontaine--Laffaille requirement therefore needs an explicit interpretation, such as an appropriate lift condition, which is not supplied here. No such interpretation is selected in this draft. The coefficient ring of the lift, its reduction to $\bar\rho_{\mathfrak m}$, and the meaning of its analogous properties likewise remain unspecified.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every maximal ideal of the indicated Hecke algebra gives a residual Galois representation with prescribed Hecke, oddness, and Fontaine--Laffaille properties. For non-Eisenstein ideals it additionally asks for a lift and a Taylor--Wiles deformation-ring isomorphism. Essential arithmetic data and local conditions are missing, so this remains a schematic statement requiring mathematical review.
