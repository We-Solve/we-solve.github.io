Lex-plus-powers Betti bounds for Artinian quotients over C

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. For characteristic-zero regular sequences, the proposed lex-plus-powers ideal simultaneously preserves the Hilbert function and bounds every graded Betti number above.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For a characteristic-zero field $F$, a homogeneous ideal $I\subseteq S_F=F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ containing a regular sequence of degrees $2\le a_1\le\cdots\le a_c$, $1\le c\le n$, put $P_F=(x_1^{a_1},\ldots,x_c^{a_c})$. In degree $d$, define $J_d$ by adjoining to $(P_F)_d$ the lexicographically greatest $q_d=\dim I_d-\dim(P_F)_d$ monomials outside $P_F$. The source claims $J=\bigoplus_dJ_d$ is an ideal with \[ \dim_F(S_F/I)_d=\dim_F(S_F/J)_d\quad(d\ge0),\qquad \beta^{S_F}_{u,j}(S_F/I)\le\beta^{S_F}_{u,j}(S_F/J) \quad(u\ge0,\ j\in\mathbb Z). \] Here $\beta_{u,j}$ are graded Betti numbers. The additional generators of $I$ have no degree or number restriction. The main complex-field theorem is the full-length Artinian case $c=n$.

Outline of the argument

The division-coefficient commuting forms retain enough filtration information to compare minimal free resolutions, beyond merely counting graded dimensions. The Artinian Betti comparison is then transported through the established regular-sequence reduction and descent to arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficient fields.
