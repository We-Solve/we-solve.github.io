Math-6795

Third Neumann Eigenvalue Maximization Under Curvature Bounds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus\{0\}$. For a Riemannian surface $(M,g)$ with boundary and sufficient regularity for a discrete Neumann spectrum, write \[ 0=\mu_0(M,g)\leq\mu_1(M,g)\leq\mu_2(M,g)\leq\cdots, \] counting multiplicities, for the eigenvalues of \[ -\Delta_g u=\mu u\quad\text{in }M,\qquad \partial_\nu u=0\quad\text{on }\partial M, \] where $\nu$ is the outward unit normal. Under this indexing convention, the third eigenvalue is $\mu_2$. Write $\kappa_g$ for Gaussian curvature. Whenever such a disk exists, let $D_K(a)$ denote a geodesic disk of area $a$ in the complete simply connected surface of constant Gaussian curvature $K$.

The supplied source asks whether, among simply connected surfaces satisfying $\kappa_g\leq K$, the third Neumann eigenvalue is maximized by a disjoint union of two constant-curvature-$K$ disks. Essential specifications are missing: the source does not state an area constraint or another normalization, the relative sizes of the disks, the boundary regularity, or the indexing convention. Moreover, the proposed disconnected maximizer is not itself a simply connected surface under the usual convention that simply connected spaces are connected.

Under the additional, unconfirmed interpretation of fixed total area $A>0$, two equal-area comparison disks, and the indexing above, a candidate precise inequality is \[ \mu_2(M,g)\leq \mu_2\!\left(D_K(A/2)\sqcup D_K(A/2)\right) \] for every admissible simply connected $(M,g)$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{Area}_g(M)=A,\qquad \kappa_g(x)\leq K\quad(x\in M). \] This conditional formulation applies only when the comparison disks exist; the intended admissible area range for $K>0$ is not supplied. It is also unresolved whether the source intends attainment in an enlarged disconnected class or a sharp supremum over connected surfaces. These additional interpretations require confirmation before this can be treated as a fully specified conjecture.

The original passage states that the curvature-bound conjecture is known for $K=0$ by Girouard and Polterovich [10], and open for $K<0$ and $K>0$. It separately attributes constant-curvature results to Bucur and Henrot [3] in the Euclidean setting, Freitas and Laugesen [9] in the hyperbolic setting, and Bucur, Martinet and Nahon [4] for spherical domains. These are supplied source claims, not independently verified mathematical or literature conclusions.

Short English statement

The question concerns whether two constant-curvature-$K$ disks give the sharp upper bound for the third Neumann eigenvalue of simply connected surfaces with Gaussian curvature at most $K$, for $K\neq0$. A precise conjecture still requires a normalization, disk sizes, admissible surface and area conditions, and clarification of how a disconnected comparison realizes the maximum. The displayed fixed-area, equal-disk formulation is explicitly conditional. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved.
