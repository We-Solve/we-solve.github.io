Asymptotic Symmetry of Geometric PDE Singularities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns parabolic geometric partial differential equations and finite-time singularities. Here a finite-time singularity is understood only informally as a loss of regularity at a finite evolution time $T$. The record specifies neither an equation nor its evolving geometric objects, admissible solutions, initial data, boundary conditions, or regularity criterion for singularity formation.

Formulation problem. Formulate and interpret the following supplied folklore conjecture: finite-time singularities of parabolic geometric partial differential equations become asymptotically as symmetric as their topology permits.

A precise formulation requires specifying the class of equations and solutions being quantified over; the geometric object whose topology is relevant; the symmetry group and its action; the meaning of the symmetries permitted by that topology; and the limiting procedure and convergence notion defining asymptotic symmetry as time approaches the singular time. The record does not specify whether rescaling, changes of coordinates, or subsequences are intended, or whether the assertion concerns every singularity or a restricted class. Consequently, it supplies a question about formulating a conjecture, rather than a definite quantified proposition. No particular interpretation of these missing choices is asserted here.

Short English statement

The question asks how to make precise the folklore expectation that finite-time singularities in parabolic geometric evolution become as symmetric as their topology allows. The equations, topology, symmetry notion, limiting procedure, and quantifiers remain unspecified, so this is a formulation problem requiring human mathematical review.
