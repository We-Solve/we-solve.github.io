Math-3500

Positivity of Hyperbolic and Seifert Representation Volumes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed oriented $3$-manifold. The supplied record associates to $M$ its hyperbolic representation volume $HV(M)$ and its Seifert representation volume $SV(M)$, defined as maximal representation volumes for the respective groups \[ G_H=\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{C}}),\qquad G_S=\operatorname{Isom}\left(\widetilde{\operatorname{SL}}_2({\mathbb{R}})\right). \] For connected $M$, write $\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(M),G)$ for the set of group homomorphisms from its fundamental group to $G$. With $\operatorname{Vol}_G(M,\rho)$ denoting the representation-volume invariant intended by the source, the supplied definitions take the form \[ HV(M)=\max_{\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(M),G_H)}\operatorname{Vol}_{G_H}(M,\rho), \qquad SV(M)=\max_{\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(M),G_S)}\operatorname{Vol}_{G_S}(M,\rho). \] Here the use of a maximum follows the supplied record; its existence is not established in that record.

Classification problem. Determine necessary and sufficient conditions on a closed oriented $3$-manifold $M$ for each of the following properties, separately: \[ HV(M)>0,\qquad SV(M)>0. \] These questions concern the volumes of $M$ itself. Positivity on a finite covering manifold is a separate property and does not replace either requested condition.

Unresolved conventions. The supplied record does not construct $\operatorname{Vol}_G(M,\rho)$, specify its normalization or signed-versus-absolute convention, or state whether the Seifert isometry group means the full group, its orientation-preserving subgroup, or its identity component. The notation $\widetilde{\operatorname{SL}}_2({\mathbb{R}})$ is retained as the source's Seifert geometry notation; the geometric structure underlying its isometry group is not specified. Accordingly, the displayed formulas express the intended classification questions subject to these unresolved definitions. The source also does not explicitly require connectedness or define the invariants for disconnected manifolds.

Short English statement

Classify the closed oriented $3$-manifolds with positive hyperbolic representation volume, and separately those with positive Seifert representation volume. The source leaves some representation-volume and isometry-group conventions unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that status or replace positivity on the manifold by positivity on a finite cover.
