Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.67

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An involution in a group $G$ is an element of order exactly $2$. Define the property under consideration by \[ \exists a,b,c\in G:\quad a^2=b^2=c^2=1_G,\qquad a,b,c\ne 1_G,\qquad ab=ba,\qquad G=\langle a,b,c\rangle. \] Thus the three involutions generate $G$ as an abstract group, and two of them commute.

The problem, proposed by Ya. N. Nuzhin in 2002, asks: Which adjoint Chevalley groups of normal type over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ have this property?

The supplied record does not define "normal type" or specify the construction of the integral adjoint Chevalley groups intended. These conventions must be fixed before the requested classification has a fully precise domain; in particular, an identification with all integral points of an adjoint group scheme is not asserted here.

The supplied source reports the following background results, which are retained as attributed claims rather than independently verified assertions:

  • $\operatorname{SL}_n({\mathbb{Z}})$ has the property for $n>13$, attributed to M. C. Tamburini and P. Zucca (1997). This is background about special linear groups and does not itself specify the adjoint family in the question.
  • $\operatorname{PSL}_n({\mathbb{Z}})$ has the property if and only if $n>4$, attributed to N. Ya. Nuzhin (2008). The source does not explicitly give the initial range of $n$ for this assertion.
  • $\operatorname{PSp}_4({\mathbb{Z}})$ does not have the property. The source says this follows from the corresponding fact for $\operatorname{PSp}_4(3)$, referring to Archive problem 7.30.
  • The group denoted $G_2$ has the property, attributed to I. A. Timofeenko (2015).
  • The group denoted $E_l$ has the property, attributed to I. A. Timofeenko (2017).
For the last two claims, the notation is copied from the source: the integral base and group form are implicit in context, and the permitted values of $l$ in $E_l$ are not supplied. These qualifications remain unresolved; the background claims do not provide a precise list of the cases still being asked about.

Short English statement

Classify the adjoint Chevalley groups of normal type over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ that can be generated by three elements of order exactly two, with two of those generators commuting. The source supplies several attributed positive and negative cases, but leaves the integral-group convention, the meaning of normal type, and the ranks covered by $E_l$ unspecified. The registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status or the cited results.
