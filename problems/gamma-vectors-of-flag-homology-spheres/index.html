Math-1130

Gamma Vectors of Flag Homology Spheres

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Delta$ be a finite abstract simplicial complex of dimension $d-1$, where $d\geq 1$. Write $f_{i-1}(\Delta)$ for the number of faces with $i$ vertices, with $f_{-1}(\Delta)=1$ counting the empty face. The complex is flag if every set of vertices that are pairwise joined by edges is a face. A simplicial sphere means a simplicial complex whose geometric realization is homeomorphic to $S^{d-1}$.

To specify the homological terminology, fix a field $k$. A homology sphere over $k$ is a pure $(d-1)$-dimensional simplicial complex such that, for every face $\sigma$, including the empty face, its link \[ \operatorname{lk}_{\Delta}(\sigma)=\{\tau\in\Delta:\tau\cap\sigma=\varnothing,\ \tau\cup\sigma\in\Delta\} \] satisfies \[ \widetilde H_j(\operatorname{lk}_{\Delta}(\sigma);k)\cong \begin{cases} k,&j=d-|\sigma|-1,\\ 0,&j\ne d-|\sigma|-1. \end{cases} \] Here reduced homology uses the augmented convention, so the complex consisting only of the empty face has the homology of $S^{-1}$. The source does not specify homology coefficients; the statements below are parameterized by $k$, and the intended coefficient scope remains unresolved.

Define the $h$-polynomial by \[ h_{\Delta}(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{d}f_{i-1}(\Delta)t^i(1-t)^{d-i}. \] For a homology sphere, the Dehn--Sommerville symmetry gives $h_{\Delta}(t)=t^d h_{\Delta}(t^{-1})$. Consequently there are unique integers $\gamma_i(\Delta)$, for $0\leq i\leq m:=\lfloor d/2\rfloor$, such that \[ h_{\Delta}(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{m}\gamma_i(\Delta)t^i(1+t)^{d-2i}. \] Their ordered tuple is the $\gamma$-vector; in particular, $\gamma_0(\Delta)=1$.

A finite simplicial complex $B$ of dimension $r-1$ is balanced if its vertices admit a coloring with $r$ colors that is injective on each face. Permit $B=\{\varnothing\}$, with $r=0$. Face vectors here include the empty-face entry $f_{-1}(B)=1$, and are padded by zeros beyond the dimension of $B$.

The broad problem is to characterize the possible face vectors \[ \left(f_{-1}(\Delta),f_0(\Delta),\ldots,f_{d-1}(\Delta)\right) \] of flag simplicial spheres, in every dimension. The source additionally asks the following two questions for flag homology spheres: for every flag homology sphere $\Delta$ over the chosen field $k$, is \[ \gamma_i(\Delta)\geq 0\qquad(0\leq i\leq m)? \] More strongly, does there exist a finite balanced simplicial complex $B$ of dimension at most $m-1$ such that \[ f_{i-1}(B)=\gamma_i(\Delta)\qquad(0\leq i\leq m)? \] The latter assertion implies the former. The source attributes the nonnegativity conjecture to Gal, but supplies no individual attribution for the stronger assertion. It does not specify a more particular target for the broad face-vector characterization question. If the formal $(-1)$-sphere is included, the convention $\Delta=\{\varnothing\}$, $h_{\Delta}(t)=1$, and $\gamma(\Delta)=(1)$ gives the corresponding boundary case, realized by $B=\{\varnothing\}$.

Short English statement

Determine which face vectors occur for flag simplicial spheres. In particular, ask whether every flag homology sphere has a nonnegative $\gamma$-vector and whether that vector, including its empty-face entry, is the face vector of a balanced simplicial complex. The coefficient field for homology is unspecified in the source. The supplied open-status assessment is dated curation and has not been independently verified.
