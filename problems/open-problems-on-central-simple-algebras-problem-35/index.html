Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 35

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a field satisfying at least one of the following conditions: $F$ is finitely generated as a field extension of its prime subfield; or there exists an algebraically closed subfield $k\subseteq F$ such that $F/k$ is finitely generated as a field extension. A quaternion division algebra over $F$ means a central division $F$-algebra of dimension $4$. A finite field extension $E/F$ splits such an algebra $D$ if \[ D\otimes_F E\cong M_2(E) \] as $E$-algebras. A maximal subfield of $D$ is a commutative subfield containing $F$ that is maximal under inclusion; for a quaternion division algebra it has degree $2$ over $F$. Two algebras have the same maximal subfields if their maximal subfields have the same $F$-isomorphism classes, equivalently if every quadratic field extension $L/F$ admits an $F$-algebra embedding into one precisely when it admits such an embedding into the other.

Requested assertion (Problem 35). For every such field $F$ and every pair of quaternion division $F$-algebras $D_1,D_2$, prove that \[ \left[\text{for every finite field extension }E/F,\quad D_1\otimes_F E\cong M_2(E) \ \Longleftrightarrow\ D_2\otimes_F E\cong M_2(E)\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ D_1\cong_F D_2. \] The source also requests the stronger formulation in which the hypothesis is replaced by equality of maximal subfields: \[ \left[\text{for every quadratic field extension }L/F,\quad L\hookrightarrow_F D_1\ \Longleftrightarrow\ L\hookrightarrow_F D_2\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ D_1\cong_F D_2. \] Here $\hookrightarrow_F$ denotes the existence of an $F$-algebra embedding. The supplied statement imposes no characteristic restriction and does not specify separability. The displayed formulations therefore read finite extensions and maximal subfields literally, including inseparable ones when they occur. Whether the original source intended a separability restriction, especially in characteristic $2$, remains unresolved from the supplied record.

Short English statement

For a field finitely generated over its prime field or over an algebraically closed subfield, the problem asks whether a quaternion division algebra is determined up to isomorphism over that field by its finite splitting fields, or even by its maximal subfields. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status. No characteristic or separability restriction is supplied.
