Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 29

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer and let $G$ be an amenable group, regarded as discrete. Here amenability means the existence of a left-invariant mean on the bounded real-valued functions on $G$. Consider an action \[ \rho\colon G\longrightarrow \operatorname{Diff}^{1}({\mathbb{R}}^{2n}), \] where every group element acts by a $C^1$ diffeomorphism; actions of higher regularity are also included.

One construction of its Euler class uses the associated bundle \[ \pi\colon E_\rho=EG\times_G{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}\longrightarrow BG, \] where $EG$ is a contractible free $G$-space and $BG=EG/G$. The contractibility of the fibre permits a section $s\colon BG\to E_\rho$. Pulling back the vertical tangent bundle gives a real vector bundle $V_\rho=s^*T_\pi E_\rho$ of rank $2n$; its isomorphism class is independent of the section. Let $w_\rho\colon G\to\{\pm1\}$ record whether each diffeomorphism preserves or reverses orientation. For $A={\mathbb{Z}}$ or $A={\mathbb{R}}$, let $A_{w_\rho}$ denote the local coefficient system on $BG$ in which $g$ acts by multiplication by $w_\rho(g)$. The corresponding Euler class is \[ e_A(\rho)=e_A(V_\rho)\in H^{2n}(BG;A_{w_\rho}). \] For an orientation-preserving action these coefficients are untwisted, so the class lies in $H^{2n}(BG;A)$.

Source question, with unresolved conventions made explicit. Must the Euler class of every amenable group action on ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$ of regularity at least $C^1$ vanish? Once the coefficient and orientation conventions are fixed, the proposed vanishing takes the form \[ e_A(\rho)=0. \] The supplied question does not specify integral versus real coefficients, and does not explicitly require orientation preservation or specify twisted coefficients. Thus the displayed formula describes the possible precise formulations; it does not assert that the source asks for both coefficient choices or for the orientation-reversing extension. Treating $G$ as discrete and taking $n\geq1$ are explicit conventions for this draft; the source does not separately state these conventions.

Supplied context. The source reports that recent joint work with Fournier and Monod, cited there as fournier2024bounded, establishes bounded acyclicity of $\operatorname{Homeo}_0({\mathbb{R}}^d)$ for all $d$, and states as a consequence that the Euler class for actions on ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$ is unbounded. It then asks whether an analogue of Hirsch--Thurston holds for the actions above. These are supplied contextual assertions; the cited results and their coefficient conventions have not been independently verified here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether an amenable group acting on even-dimensional Euclidean space by diffeomorphisms of regularity at least $C^1$ must have vanishing Euler class. The source leaves the coefficient ring and orientation convention unspecified, so a definitive formulation requires those choices. The registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
