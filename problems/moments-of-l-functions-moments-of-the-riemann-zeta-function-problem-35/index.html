Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 35

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Using the standard ternary divisor-function convention, define, for each positive integer $n$, \[ d_3(n):=\#\{(a,b,c)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}^{3}:abc=n\}, \] where triples are ordered. For real $x\geq 1$, let \[ S(x):=\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\\n\leq x}}d_3(n)d_3(n+1). \] Determine an explicit leading asymptotic formula for $S(x)$ as $x\to+\infty$. The supplied statement does not define $d_3$; the standard convention above is an explicit interpretive assumption. It also does not specify a proposed main term, whether lower-order terms are required, or a desired error bound.

Short English statement

Determine the asymptotic growth of the sum of products of the ternary divisor function at consecutive positive integers. The source labels the problem folklore and records its status as open; that status is supplied metadata, not an independently verified assessment.
