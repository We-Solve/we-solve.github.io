Math-6588

Sharp Syzygy Bounds for Torus Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret the torus rank as the dimension of an acting torus $T\cong(S^1)^r$. Its rational equivariant coefficient ring is \[ R_r=H^*(BT;{\mathbb{Q}})\cong{\mathbb{Q}}[t_1,\ldots,t_r],\qquad |t_i|=2. \] For a $T$-space $X$, its rational Borel equivariant cohomology is the graded $R_r$-module \[ M_X=H_T^*(X;{\mathbb{Q}}):=H^*(ET\times_T X;{\mathbb{Q}}), \] where $ET$ is a contractible space with a free $T$-action and $BT=ET/T$.

Under the usual connected convention, a rational Poincaré duality space of formal dimension $n\geq0$ has finite-dimensional total rational cohomology, $H^0(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\cong H^n(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\cong{\mathbb{Q}}$, no cohomology above degree $n$, and perfect cup-product pairings \[ H^i(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\otimes H^{n-i}(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\longrightarrow H^n(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\cong{\mathbb{Q}} \qquad(0\leq i\leq n). \] The supplied record does not specify whether this connected convention or a broader convention is intended.

For a finitely generated graded $R_r$-module $M$, call $M$ a $0$-th syzygy automatically. For $k\geq1$, call it a $k$-th syzygy if there is an exact sequence of graded modules \[ 0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow F_{k-1}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow F_0\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow0, \] where the $F_i$ are finite free graded $R_r$-modules, allowing grading shifts, and $N$ is finitely generated. Set \[ \operatorname{syzord}_{R_r}(M)=\sup\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:M\text{ is a }k\text{-th syzygy}\}. \]

The supplied record asserts that, for the rational Poincaré duality spaces and torus actions intended by its source, \[ M_X\text{ not free over }R_r \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{syzord}_{R_r}(M_X)\leq \left\lfloor\frac{r-1}{2}\right\rfloor. \] The natural rank-by-rank interpretation of its sharpness question is: for every integer $r\geq5$, does there exist an admissible rational Poincaré duality space $X$ with an action of $T\cong(S^1)^r$ such that $M_X$ is finitely generated, is not free over $R_r$, and satisfies \[ \operatorname{syzord}_{R_r}(M_X)=\left\lfloor\frac{r-1}{2}\right\rfloor? \] This is a conditional formulation: the record does not specify the admissible category of spaces and actions, the relevant finiteness hypotheses, or whether effectiveness is required. It also does not disambiguate acting-torus dimension from other meanings of torus rank. These source hypotheses must be checked before the displayed question is treated as an exact transcription.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether the stated upper bound on the syzygy order of nonfree rational equivariant cohomology can be attained for every torus rank $r\geq5$. The formulation uses the acting-torus and finitely generated module conventions described above; the precise admissible spaces and actions remain unspecified.
