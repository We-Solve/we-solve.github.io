Double Jordan Stratifications of Artinian Gorenstein Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual commutative convention, an algebra $A$ is Artinian if its ideals satisfy the descending chain condition. It is Gorenstein if, for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, the socle \[ \operatorname{Soc}(A_{\mathfrak m})=\{z\in A_{\mathfrak m}:\mathfrak m A_{\mathfrak m}z=0\} \] is one-dimensional over the residue field $A_{\mathfrak m}/\mathfrak m A_{\mathfrak m}$. An ideal is principal if it has the form $(u)=Au$. The source does not specify a base field, its characteristic, whether the algebra is local, or the grading assumptions for all parts of the question.

Requested constructions. In the intended class of Artinian Gorenstein algebras, the supplied record asks the following questions.

  1. Given principal ideals $J=(u)$ and $K=(v)$, can the double $Q_{J,K}$-stratification be redefined so that its pieces have the expected dimensions and it retains its relation to the single-ideal stratifications $Q_J(a)$ and $Q_K(a)$?
  2. For nongraded Artinian Gorenstein algebras, can the $Q_{\ell,m}$-decomposition be used to define a Jordan order type?
  3. For a binomial, can the $Q_{x,y}$-decomposition be determined by combinatorial invariants of its associated poset? Can such a description also cover variables of different degrees and the case of three variables?

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not define any of these $Q$-stratifications or decompositions, the index $a$, the expected dimensions, or the relations that a redefinition must preserve. It also does not define Jordan order type, specify the roles or admissible choices of $\ell,m,x,y$, or construct an algebra and an associated poset from a binomial. Thus these are source-attributed construction questions; the supplied information does not determine a precise existence assertion or a combinatorial determination theorem. In particular, no choice of grading, binomial construction, poset, or dimension formula is imposed here.

Short English statement

The source asks for compatible double stratifications for two principal ideals, a Jordan order type for nongraded Artinian Gorenstein algebras, and a combinatorial description of the binomial case, including unequal variable degrees and three variables. The defining constructions and target conditions are missing, so a fully precise mathematical statement requires further source information.
