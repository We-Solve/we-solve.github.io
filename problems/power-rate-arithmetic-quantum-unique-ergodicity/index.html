Math-1003

Power-Rate Arithmetic Quantum Unique Ergodicity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the conventional interpretation of the modular surface, let \[ X=\operatorname{PSL}_2({\mathbb{Z}})\backslash\mathbb H, \qquad \mathbb H=\{x+iy:y>0\}, \qquad d\mu=\frac{3}{\pi}\frac{dx\,dy}{y^2}, \] so that $\mu(X)=1$. Use the nonnegative Laplacian \[ \Delta=-y^2(\partial_x^2+\partial_y^2). \] A weight-zero Hecke--Maass cusp form $\phi$ is a smooth automorphic function on $\mathbb H$, square integrable on $X$, that is a simultaneous eigenfunction of $\Delta$ and the usual Hecke operators and has vanishing constant Fourier coefficient at the cusp. Thus \[ \Delta\phi=\lambda_\phi\phi, \qquad \int_0^1\phi(x+iy)\,dx=0\quad(y>0). \] Normalize $\int_X|\phi|^2\,d\mu=1$, and define the probability measure \[ d\mu_\phi=|\phi|^2\,d\mu. \] These specify standard conventions for the surface, Laplacian, and normalization; the supplied record does not explicitly give them.

Requested assertion (strong arithmetic quantum unique ergodicity). Establish equidistribution of $\mu_\phi$ toward $\mu$ with a power saving as $\lambda_\phi\to\infty$ through Hecke--Maass cusp forms. A precise formulation using fixed smooth compactly supported test functions would be \[ \exists\delta>0\ \ \forall a\in C_c^\infty(X)\ \ \exists C_a>0\ \ \exists L_a>0\ \ \forall\phi: \quad \lambda_\phi\ge L_a \ \Longrightarrow\ \left|\int_X a\,d\mu_\phi-\int_X a\,d\mu\right| \le C_a\lambda_\phi^{-\delta}, \] where $\phi$ ranges over all forms satisfying the conditions above, and $\delta$ is independent of both $a$ and $\phi$.

Unresolved scope. The source specifies a rate $O(\lambda^{-\delta})$ but does not specify a topology or testing class for comparing measures, the permitted dependence of the implied constant, or whether a common exponent is required for all test functions. Consequently, the displayed quantified inequality is an explicitly proposed interpretation of the requested rate, not a uniquely determined transcription. In particular, it supplies no uniform estimate for varying test functions or sets and no separate quantitative estimate in the cusp. The intended scope requires human mathematical review.

Short English statement

For normalized Hecke--Maass cusp forms on the modular surface, the problem asks for their probability densities to approach normalized hyperbolic area at a power rate in the Laplace eigenvalue. A proposed precise interpretation tests against each fixed smooth compactly supported function, with one positive exponent valid for all forms and tests. The supplied record leaves the testing class and uniformity requirements unspecified.
