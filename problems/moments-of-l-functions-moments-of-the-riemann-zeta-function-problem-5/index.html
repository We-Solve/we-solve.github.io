Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 5

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt does not define $I_2(T)$ or give the coefficients of $P_4$. The following formulation is conditional on the conventional fourth-moment interpretation of this notation.

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the Riemann zeta function, defined for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$ by $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}$ and elsewhere by meromorphic continuation. Under the interpretation \[ I_2(T)=\int_0^T\left|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)\right|^4\,\mathrm{d}t,\qquad T>0, \] let $P_4$ denote the particular degree-four polynomial referred to in the source, and define \[ E_2(T)=I_2(T)-T P_4(\log T). \] The polynomial is the specified main-term polynomial, not an arbitrary polynomial of degree four; its coefficients are not supplied.

The record attributes to Zavorotnyi (1989) the estimate \[ E_2(T)=O_{\varepsilon}\!\left(T^{2/3+\varepsilon}\right) \qquad(T\to\infty) \] for every $\varepsilon>0$. Explicitly, for each $\varepsilon>0$, there are constants $C_{\varepsilon}>0$ and $T_{\varepsilon}>0$ such that \[ |E_2(T)|\le C_{\varepsilon}T^{2/3+\varepsilon} \qquad\text{for every real }T\ge T_{\varepsilon}. \] Problem. Improve this error-term bound. A power-saving improvement would, for example, establish the existence of a fixed real $\theta<2/3$ such that, for every $\varepsilon>0$, \[ E_2(T)=O_{\varepsilon}\!\left(T^{\theta+\varepsilon}\right) \qquad(T\to\infty). \] The source does not specify a target exponent or restrict the requested improvement to this form. The definition and integration limits of $I_2$, and the exact main-term polynomial, require confirmation from the cited source.

Short English statement

Under the conventional interpretation of $I_2(T)$ as the fourth moment of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line, improve the error bound $O_{\varepsilon}(T^{2/3+\varepsilon})$ attributed in the record to Zavorotnyi (1989). The excerpt omits the definition of $I_2$ and the coefficients of its degree-four main-term polynomial, so this formulation requires confirmation.
