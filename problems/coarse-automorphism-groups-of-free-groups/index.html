Coarse Automorphism Groups of Free Groups

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer, and let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$. If $S\subseteq F_n$ is a generating set, its word metric is \[ d_S(x,y)=\min\left\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:x^{-1}y=s_1\cdots s_k,\quad s_i\in S\cup S^{-1}\right\}, \] where the empty product is the identity. The supplied record does not specify the intended generating set $S$, restrictions on $S$, or the category denoted by $\mathrm{Crs}$.

For clarity, one possible standard metric interpretation can be defined precisely as follows. A map $f:X\to Y$ between metric spaces is bornologous if \[ \forall R\geq 0\ \exists T\geq 0\ \forall x,x'\in X,\qquad d_X(x,x')\leq R\ \Longrightarrow\ d_Y(f(x),f(x'))\leq T. \] Two maps $u,v:X\to Y$ are close if $\sup_{x\in X}d_Y(u(x),v(x))<\infty$. A coarse self-equivalence of $X$ is a bornologous map $f:X\to X$ admitting a bornologous map $g:X\to X$ such that $g\circ f$ and $f\circ g$ are close to the identity. Their closeness classes form a group under composition. If $\mathrm{Crs}$ means this coarse category, then $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{Crs}}(F_n,d_S)$ denotes this group for $X=(F_n,d_S)$. This interpretation is conditional: the supplied excerpt does not establish that it is the intended definition, particularly whether additional group compatibility is required.

Requested structural problem. For $n\geq 2$, describe the structure of \[ \operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{Crs}}(F_n,d_S) \] and of relevant subgroups. The supplied original excerpt motivates this investigation by asking whether this group, or some subgroup, can be used to study \[ \operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n), \] where $\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$ is the subgroup of conjugations by elements of $F_n$. The motivating analogy is the study of $\mathrm{GL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}})$ through the lattice relationship involving $\mathrm{SL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}})$ and $\mathrm{SL}(n,{\mathbb{R}})$; no corresponding lattice relationship or embedding for $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ is asserted here.

Short English statement

For free groups $F_n$ with $n\geq 2$, investigate the coarse automorphism group and relevant subgroups, motivated by possible applications to $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$. The generating set, precise notion of coarse automorphism, and desired structural conclusions require clarification.
