Must every infinite-dimensional real reflexive Banach space that admits an asymptotically midpoint uniformly convex norm also admit an equivalent asymptotically uniformly convex norm? Reflexive asymptotic midpoint renormability versus AUC

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 331; 2026-09-27), the asserted result is that a separable reflexive tree-norm space is asymptotically midpoint uniformly convex with modulus at least $\sqrt{1+t^2/12}-1$ but has no AUC renorming, and depth-$k$ diamonds incur distortion at least $\sqrt{1+k/12}$; In manuscript 2 (family 331; 2026-09-27), the asserted result is that four bounded-tree-potential norms have averaged midpoint lower bound $\sqrt{1+t^2/4}-1$ for $0<t<1$ but no AUC renorming, with the stated reflexive Hilbert-sum variants; In manuscript 3 (family 331; 2026-09-27), the asserted result is that recursive two-field tree norms have cubic or sixth-power midpoint bounds, including reflexive examples with no AUC equivalent norm; In manuscript 4 (family 331; 2026-09-27), the asserted result is that segment-forest duals and the infinite-height coordinate predual have small symmetric-lens tails and midpoint convexity, despite failing every AUC renorming, with reflexivity for the stated finite-height forest dual.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $\mathcal F=\coprod_{h\ge1}\{h\}\times\mathbb N^{\le h}$ be a forest of countably branching finite-height trees. Complete finitely supported real functions under \[ \rho(u)=\sup_{S_1,\ldots,S_m}\left(\sum_{i=1}^m(\sum_{v\in S_i}u(v))^2\right)^{1/2}, \] where the supremum ranges over finite families of pairwise disjoint ancestral segments, and call the completion $J$. The claim states that $X=J^*$ is separable, infinite dimensional and reflexive, admits no equivalent asymptotically uniformly convex norm, and its dual norm satisfies \[ \widehat\delta_X(t)\ge\sqrt{1+t^2/12}-1\quad(t>0). \] Here $\widehat\delta_X(t)=\inf_{\|x\|=1}\sup_F\inf_{y\in F,\|y\|=1}((\|x+ty\|+\|x-ty\|)/2-1)$, with $F$ closed and finite-codimensional. Every embedding of the $k$th countably branching diamond graph, with shortest-path metric, has distortion at least $\sqrt{1+k/12}$ for every $k\ge0$.
Manuscript 2.
Let $T=\mathbb N^{<\omega}$, root $o$, and $P_f(s)=\sum_{u\in I,\ u\preceq s}f_u$. Four symmetric test sets all require $|P_f(s)|\le1$. The root-free sibling set uses $I=T\setminus\{o\}$ and $\sum_{u\in\operatorname{ch}(r)}f_u^2\le1$; the root-inclusive sibling set uses $I=T$ with that same constraint. The antichain set uses finitely supported $f$ on $T$ and $\sum_{u\in A}f_u^2\le1$ for every finite antichain. The global set uses $f\in\ell_2(T)$ with $\sum_Tf_u^2\le1$. Complete finite vectors in $\|x\|_K=\sup_{f\in K}\sum_s f_sx_s$ to obtain $X_K$. Each is claimed separable infinite dimensional, has no equivalent AUC norm, and has averaged midpoint modulus \[ \widehat\delta_{X_K}(t)\ge\sqrt{1+t^2/4}-1\quad(0<t<1). \] For the segment construction, use disjoint component trees $\mathcal T_n=\mathbb N^{\le n}$, $n\ge1$. On nonnegative arrays recursively define \[ q_t(m)=m_t+(\sum_{u\in\operatorname{ch}(t)}q_u(m)^2)^{1/2},\qquad M_n(m)=q_{\varnothing}(m). \] A segment array $(a_{tv})_{t\preceq v}$ has start masses $m_t(a)=\sum_{v\succeq t}|a_{tv}|$ and output $(Pa)_s=\sum_{t\preceq s}\sum_{v\succeq s}a_{tv}$. Set \[ \|x\|_{S,n}=\inf_{Pa=x}M_n(m(a)),\qquad X_S=\left(\bigoplus_{n\ge1}(\ell_2(\mathcal T_n),\|\cdot\|_{S,n})\right)_{\ell_2}. \] The claimed complete component norms satisfy $\|x\|_2/(n+1)\le\|x\|_{S,n}\le\sqrt{n+1}\|x\|_2$, and supply a reflexive $X_S$ with contractive finite ancestral coordinate projections, unit coordinate and root-to-node path vectors, and weakly null sibling sequences; its maximum midpoint modulus satisfies \[ \delta^{\max}_{X_S}(t)\ge\sqrt{1+t^2/64}-1\quad(t>0). \] Both midpoint moduli take the infimum over unit centers, supremum over closed finite-codimensional subspaces and infimum over their large directions; the first uses the average of the two signed norms, the second their maximum.
Manuscript 3.
For finitely supported real vectors on a rooted countably branching tree, compute upward from zero fields below the support: \[ A_s=(\sum_{c\in\operatorname{ch}(s)}P_c^2)^{1/2},\quad B_s=(\sum_{c\in\operatorname{ch}(s)}Q_c^2)^{1/2},\quad P_s=\max\{A_s,v_s+B_s\},\quad Q_s=\max\{B_s,-v_s+A_s\}. \] On $\mathbb N^{<\omega}$ let $N_{\Sigma}=P_o+Q_o$. Join disjoint trees $\mathbb N^{\le n}$, $n\ge1$, under a new root $\rho$; with $v_{\rho}=0$ let $N_0=P_{\rho}=Q_{\rho}$, and with that coordinate free let $N_J=P_{\rho}+Q_{\rho}$. Complete to $X_{\Sigma},X_0,X_J$. All are claimed Banach spaces with no equivalent AUC norm, and $X_0,X_J$ are reflexive. The averaged midpoint estimates are \[ \widehat\delta_{N_{\Sigma}}(t),\widehat\delta_{N_J}(t)\ge t^3/128\quad(0<t<1). \] If $H$ is finite ancestral, $x$ supported in $H$, and $0\ne z$ vanishes on $H$, the further estimate is \[ \frac{N_0(x+z)+N_0(x-z)}2\ge N_0(x)+\frac{N_0(z)^3}{8(2N_0(x)+N_0(z))^2}. \] Coordinate restrictions to finite ancestral sets are contractive, their kernels finite-codimensional, and finite-support vectors dense there.
Manuscript 4.
Complete finitely supported real arrays on the forest $\coprod_{h\ge1}\mathbb N^{\le h}$ in the norm $\rho(a)=\sup(\sum_j|\sum_{v\in S_j}a_v|^2)^{1/2}$ over finite disjoint ancestral segments, and set $X=J^*$. Define the same segment completion $J_{\infty}$ on $\mathbb N^{<\omega}$ and let $B_{\infty}$ be the closed span of coordinate evaluations in $J_{\infty}^*$. For either $Y=X$ or $B_{\infty}$, finite ancestral $H$, $P_H$ coordinate restriction, $Q_H=I-P_H$, $x=P_Hx$ and $\|x\pm y\|\le R$, the asserted lens estimate is \[ \|Q_Hy\|\le2\sqrt{R^2-\|x\|^2}. \] Its averaged midpoint consequence is $\widehat\delta_Y(t)\ge(\sqrt{1+t^2/4}-1)/2$ for every $t>0$. The finite-height dual is claimed separable reflexive, with dense coordinate span and contractive ancestral projections. For any unbounded sequence of finite component heights and equivalent norm $N$ satisfying $a\|x\|\le N(x)\le b\|x\|$, it claims \[ \overline\delta_N(a/b)=\overline\delta_N(a/(2b))=0, \] so there is no equivalent AUC norm. Here the one-sided modulus is $\inf_{N(x)=1}\sup_F\inf_{y\in F,N(y)=1}(N(x+ty)-1)$.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 331; 2026-09-27), paired segment tests bound approximate midpoint tails in the coordinate completion. Fresh layers along a branching diamond accumulate an energy loss at each level, while uniformly bounded branches persist under equivalent norms and obstruct one-sided asymptotic convexity. For manuscript 2 (family 331; 2026-09-27), two inward tests split the tail of an arbitrary dual potential relative to a head norming functional. Clipping and subtree quotas bound path coefficients, while newly exposed coordinate energy gives midpoint growth; bounded branches survive all equivalent norms.

For manuscript 3 (family 331; 2026-09-27), least positive fields represent the vector through their difference. Root defects propagate until a stopping boundary, where accumulated Euclidean energy yields the midpoint estimate; finite-height Hilbert equivalence proves reflexivity, while bounded paths prevent AUC renorming. For manuscript 4 (family 331; 2026-09-27), finite convex laws are recovered by clipping cumulative segment sums and matching opposite masses at boundary crossings. Endpoint comparisons bound the tail by the squared-radius deficit, and completed atomic laws transfer the estimate to the specified spaces.
