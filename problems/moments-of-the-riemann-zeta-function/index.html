Math-941

Moments of the Riemann Zeta Function

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the Riemann zeta function, defined for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$ by $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}$ and elsewhere by meromorphic continuation. For an integer $k\geq1$ and a real number $T>0$, define \[ M_k(T)=\frac{1}{T}\int_0^T\left|\zeta\!\left(\frac12+it\right)\right|^{2k}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] The supplied cleaned statement asks for the following conjecture: for every integer $k\geq1$, there exists an explicitly specified polynomial \[ P_k(X)=\sum_{j=0}^{k^2}a_{k,j}X^j\in{\mathbb{R}}[X],\qquad a_{k,k^2}\ne0, \] whose coefficients depend only on $k$, such that \[ \lim_{T\to\infty}\left(M_k(T)-P_k(\log T)\right)=0. \] Here $\log$ is the natural logarithm, and the limit is taken with $k$ fixed; no uniformity in $k$ is asserted. The error is an additive $o(1)$, so the statement concerns the complete polynomial, including its constant term.

Unresolved source qualifications. The record attributes the surrounding general moment recipe to Conrey--Farmer--Keating--Rubinstein--Snaith, but supplies neither its coefficient formula nor a definition of what constitutes an explicit polynomial. The original excerpt is truncated and duplicated, discusses moments in families of $L$-functions, and ends by introducing a quadratic family without stating its conjecture. Thus the formulation above follows the supplied cleaned zeta-moment statement; the excerpt alone does not establish whether that statement captures the intended source problem in full.

Short English statement

For each fixed positive integer $k$, the conjecture asks for an explicit polynomial of degree $k^2$ in $\log T$ that approximates the normalized $2k$-th moment of the Riemann zeta function with error tending to zero. The coefficient prescription is absent, and the fragmentary original excerpt leaves its relationship to the broader family conjecture unresolved.
