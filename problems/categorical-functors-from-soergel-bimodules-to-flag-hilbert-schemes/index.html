Categorical Functors from Soergel Bimodules to Flag Hilbert Schemes

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write \[ \mathcal A_n=K_b(\operatorname{SBim}_n),\qquad \mathcal B_n=D_b\left(\operatorname{Coh}^T(\operatorname{FHilb}_n({\mathbb{C}}))\right). \] Here $K_b$ denotes the bounded homotopy category, and $D_b$ denotes the bounded derived category. The notation $\operatorname{SBim}_n$ refers to the intended category of Soergel bimodules, while $\operatorname{Coh}^T(X)$ denotes the category of $T$-equivariant coherent sheaves on $X$. The source does not specify the realization, coefficient ring, or grading conventions for the Soergel bimodules; the scheme denoted by $\operatorname{FHilb}_n({\mathbb{C}})$; or the group $T$ and its action. The displayed scheme notation is retained exactly without substituting a different ambient space.

The supplied question asks whether there exist adjoint functors between $\mathcal A_n$ and $\mathcal B_n$ such that the functor designated as forward is monoidal and satisfies \[ F\left(L_1\otimes\cdots\otimes L_{k-1}\right) \cong C(FT_k). \] The symbols $L_i$, $FT_k$, and $C$ are retained from the source: their definitions, category membership, and conventions are not supplied. In particular, interpreting the $L_i$ as objects of $\mathcal B_n$ and $C(FT_k)$ as an object of $\mathcal A_n$ would require a functor \[ F:\mathcal B_n\longrightarrow\mathcal A_n, \qquad G:\mathcal A_n\longrightarrow\mathcal B_n. \] This is a conditional typing of the displayed requirement, not a verified identification of the intended direction. The record does not determine whether the intended adjunction is $F\dashv G$ or $G\dashv F$. Its title suggests functors from Soergel bimodules to flag Hilbert schemes, but does not resolve which functor is designated as forward in the displayed condition.

The monoidal structures, the meaning of monoidal functor, and the relevant equivariant and derived tensor conventions must also be specified. No ranges or quantifiers for $n$ and $k$, relation between these indices, or boundary convention for the tensor product are supplied. Thus the displayed existence question records the mathematical target, but these unresolved qualifications prevent it from being a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

The problem asks for adjoint functors relating Soergel bimodules and equivariant coherent sheaves on a flag Hilbert scheme, with one functor preserving the intended monoidal structure and carrying a specified tensor product to $C(FT_k)$. The supplied record leaves essential objects, functor directions, and index ranges undefined, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
