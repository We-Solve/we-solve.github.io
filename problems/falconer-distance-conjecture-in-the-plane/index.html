Falconer Distance Conjecture in the Plane

OpenAI's claim

Falconer distances: For every $d\ge2$, the manuscript claims positive one-dimensional measure for the distance set of a compact subset of $\mathbb R^d$ with dimension exceeding $d/2$. Both the ambient dimension and the set itself are unrestricted within these hypotheses.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every integer $d\ge2$ and compact $E\subset\mathbb R^d$, \[\dim_H E>d/2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mathcal L^1\left(\{|x-y|:x,y\in E\}\right)>0.\] Here $\dim_H$ is Hausdorff dimension and $\mathcal L^1$ is Lebesgue measure on the distance axis.

Outline of the argument

Falconer distances: Auxiliary projections first provide angular nonconcentration and filters on pairs of points. A fractal Fourier estimate and a graph estimate with separate angular variables control shell approximations after small additional deletions. The filters are brought back to distances of the original pairs, and monotone shell summation produces a nonzero absolutely continuous distance measure.
