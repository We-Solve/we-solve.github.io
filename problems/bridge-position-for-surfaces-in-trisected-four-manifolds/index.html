Math-7482

Bridge Position for Surfaces in Trisected Four-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X=X_1\cup X_2\cup X_3$ denote the supplied trisection of a smooth four-manifold, and put $\Sigma=X_1\cap X_2\cap X_3$ and $H_{ij}=X_i\cap X_j$ for distinct $i,j\in\{1,2,3\}$. Here $\Sigma$ is the central surface. A boundary-parallel arc tangle in a three-manifold is a finite collection of disjoint properly embedded arcs admitting mutually disjoint parallelism disks: each arc and an arc in the boundary cobound one such disk. A boundary-parallel disk tangle in a four-manifold is a finite collection of disjoint properly embedded disks admitting mutually disjoint parallelism three-balls: each disk and a disk in the boundary cobound one such three-ball, with corners allowed along their common boundary.

Extracted bridge-position assertion. The supplied record asserts that every smoothly embedded surface $S\subset X$ can be isotoped to a surface $S'$ such that, for some $b\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}$, \[ S'\pitchfork\Sigma,\qquad \#(S'\cap\Sigma)=2b, \] and, for every pair of distinct indices $i,j$, the intersection $S'\cap H_{ij}$ is a boundary-parallel arc tangle, while, for every $i\in\{1,2,3\}$, the intersection $S'\cap X_i$ is a boundary-parallel disk tangle. These conditions describe bridge position relative to the given trisection.

Unresolved scope and intended target. This is a transcription of the extracted assertion, not a recovered open conjecture. The supplied verification states that the report labels this assertion Theorem 5, and that the neighboring open conjecture was not recovered. The record does not specify compactness, closedness, orientability, or boundary conditions for $X$ and $S$, nor whether a closed or relative trisection is intended. Consequently, the universal assertion above is schematic: the hypotheses needed for a precise theorem cannot be certified from the supplied extraction. No extension to surfaces with boundary, noncompact surfaces, or relative trisections is asserted here. The intended open problem remains unrecoverable.

Short English statement

The extracted assertion says that an embedded surface can be isotoped into bridge position for a given trisection: its intersections with the sectors are boundary-parallel disks, its intersections with pairwise overlaps are boundary-parallel arcs, and it meets the central surface transversely in an even number of points. The supplied verification identifies this assertion as Theorem 5, while the intended open conjecture and complete hypotheses are missing. The registry's open label therefore does not establish that this bridge-position assertion is an open problem.
