Universal Integral Relations for Closed Polygons

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a polygon size $n$ in the range intended by the source; that range is not specified in the supplied record. Let $\mathcal C_n$ denote the source's space of closed $n$-gons, and write $I_j(P),J_j(P)$ for its monodromy integrals at $P\in\mathcal C_n$. Denote their unspecified index set by $\mathcal J_n$. The ambient geometry, equivalence relation defining this space, admissibility conditions, and definitions and normalizations of these integrals are not supplied. Thus these symbols retain their source-dependent meanings.

For $j\in\mathcal J_n$, define the supplied weight by \[ w_n(j)= \begin{cases} 3j-k,&n=2k,\\ 3j-k+1,&n=2k+1. \end{cases} \] The record presents the following as identities holding for every $P\in\mathcal C_n$, with every sum taken over $j\in\mathcal J_n$: \[ \begin{aligned} \sum_j I_j(P)&=3,&\sum_j J_j(P)&=3,\\ \sum_j w_n(j)I_j(P)&=0,&\sum_j w_n(j)J_j(P)&=0,\\ \sum_j w_n(j)^2\left(I_j(P)-J_j(P)\right)&=0. \end{aligned} \] These identities are reproduced as supplied premises, not independently verified assertions.

The source poses three questions:

  1. Are the displayed identities the only universal relations among the $I_j,J_j$ on $\mathcal C_n$? Here a universal relation is an equality holding for every polygon in the intended space. The allowed class of relations and the meaning of being a consequence of the displayed identities are not specified; consequently, no precise algebraic or functional completeness assertion can be extracted without additional definitions.
  2. Does every inscribed polygon in the intended domain satisfy \[ I_j(P)=J_j(P)\qquad\text{for every }j\in\mathcal J_n? \] The record does not define the inscribing curve, the ambient geometry, or any genericity or nondegeneracy requirements. Its wording does not resolve whether this question is restricted to $\mathcal C_n$ or concerns a larger polygon space.
  3. Is the induced Poisson map on twisted $n$-gons in $\mathbb{RP}^{1}$ completely integrable? The map, its inducing construction, the definition of a twisted polygon, the relevant configuration space or quotient, and the Poisson structure are not supplied. Neither its rank nor the intended criterion of complete integrability is specified, so this question remains a source-dependent formulation.
A fully specified mathematical statement requires these missing definitions and hypotheses; they are not inferred here.

Short English statement

The source asks whether five displayed identities exhaust the universal relations among polygon monodromy integrals, whether inscribed polygons have matching integrals $I_j=J_j$, and whether an associated Poisson map on twisted polygons in $\mathbb{RP}^{1}$ is completely integrable. Essential definitions and domains are missing. These are supplied questions labeled open by the record, and this statement-only draft requires human mathematical review.
