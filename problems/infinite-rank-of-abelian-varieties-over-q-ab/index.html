Math-4686

Infinite Rank of Abelian Varieties over $\mathbb Q^{\mathrm{ab}}$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The maximal abelian extension ${\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}}\subseteq\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ is the compositum of all finite Galois extensions of ${\mathbb{Q}}$ in $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ with abelian Galois group. A nonzero abelian variety over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ means an abelian variety $A/{\mathbb{Q}}$ with $\dim A>0$. For an extension $K/{\mathbb{Q}}$, let $A(K)$ denote its abelian group of $K$-rational points, and define \[ \operatorname{rank} A(K)=\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\left(A(K)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}\right). \] This definition does not require $A(K)$ to be finitely generated.

The supplied question is whether \[ \text{for every abelian variety }A/{\mathbb{Q}}\text{ with }\dim A>0,\qquad \operatorname{rank} A({\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}})=\infty. \] Equivalently, for every such $A$ and every integer $n\geq 1$, there exist $P_1,\ldots,P_n\in A({\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}})$ whose images in $A({\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}})\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}$ are linearly independent over ${\mathbb{Q}}$.

The supplied record labels this question open. Its background and literature assessment instead discuss whether ${\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is ample. The record does not establish an equivalence between that field property and the infinite-rank assertion above; consequently, that assessment does not by itself verify the status of the stated question.

Short English statement

Does every positive-dimensional abelian variety defined over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ have infinitely many independent rational points over ${\mathbb{Q}}^{\mathrm{ab}}$, with independence measured after tensoring its group of points with ${\mathbb{Q}}$? The supplied open-status assessment concerns ampleness and requires review for its relevance to this precise question.
