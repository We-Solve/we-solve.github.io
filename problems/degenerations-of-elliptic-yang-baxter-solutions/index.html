Math-7244

Degenerations of Elliptic Yang–Baxter Solutions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathfrak g$ be the Lie algebra intended by the source. In a spectral-parameter convention, a classical $r$-matrix is a meromorphic tensor $r(x,y)\in\mathfrak g\otimes\mathfrak g$ satisfying \[ [r^{12}(x_1,x_2),r^{13}(x_1,x_3)] +[r^{12}(x_1,x_2),r^{23}(x_2,x_3)] +[r^{13}(x_1,x_3),r^{23}(x_2,x_3)]=0. \] Superscripts indicate the two tensor factors in which the tensor is placed. The usual nondegeneracy condition says that its contraction map $\mathfrak g^*\to\mathfrak g$ is generically invertible. The source's precise spectral, Lie-algebra and normalization conventions are not specified.

The task is to construct and understand degenerating families of nondegenerate classical solutions along the sequence \[ \text{elliptic}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{trigonometric}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{rational}, \] and to track the associated vector-bundle constructions as the smooth elliptic cubic degenerates to nodal and then cuspidal cubic models. The geometric interpretation connects the nodal smooth locus with multiplicative parameters and the cuspidal smooth locus with additive parameters; it does not assert an unnormalized limit formula for every $r$-matrix.

A precise degeneration must specify the family of cubics and bundle data, identify the resulting spectral-parameter tensors, and state the coordinate changes, rescalings or gauge transformations allowed before taking a limit. One must also determine which solutions occur in these families and how the bundle construction behaves at singular fibers. The extract provides no Lie algebra, bundle rank/degree, stability or limit-sheaf category, equivalence relation, parameter family or convergence criterion. In particular it does not justify asserting that every trigonometric solution for every Lie algebra is an elliptic degeneration, or that every bundle limit stays locally free. The classical Yang--Baxter equation is retained and not replaced by an associative variant.

Short English statement

Construct elliptic-to-trigonometric-to-rational degenerations of classical Yang--Baxter solutions and the corresponding bundle constructions on degenerating cubic curves. The permitted geometric data, transformations and limit notion are missing, so no universal classification or limit formula is asserted.
