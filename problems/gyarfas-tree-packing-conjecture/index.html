Math-737

Gyárfás Tree-Packing Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $K_n$ be the complete simple undirected graph with vertex set $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. A tree means a finite simple undirected connected graph with no cycles; in particular, the one-vertex graph is a tree. An edge decomposition into trees permits their embedded vertex sets to overlap, but requires their edge sets to partition $E(K_n)$.

The conjecture attributed in the supplied report to Gyárfás is the following: for every integer $n\geq 1$ and every sequence of trees $T_1,\ldots,T_n$ satisfying $|V(T_i)|=i$ for each $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, there exist injective maps \[ \varphi_i:V(T_i)\longrightarrow[n]\qquad(1\leq i\leq n) \] such that, on defining \[ F_i=\left\{\{\varphi_i(u),\varphi_i(v)\}:\{u,v\}\in E(T_i)\right\}, \] one has \[ E(K_n)=\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}F_i, \qquad F_i\cap F_j=\varnothing\quad\text{whenever }i\ne j. \] The copy of $T_1$ contributes no edges, and for $n=1$ the required edge partition is empty. Copies are subgraphs, not necessarily induced subgraphs.

The mathematical target is specified by the supplied passage, but its original reference labeled [6] is not bibliographically identified in the record. The supplied open status and verification labels are curation claims, not independent verification in this draft.

Short English statement

For every positive integer $n$, can every choice of trees having respectively $1,2,\ldots,n$ vertices be embedded in $K_n$ so that each edge belongs to exactly one embedded tree? The trees may share vertices. The supplied record attributes this conjecture to Gyárfás and labels it open.
