Math-3675

Subadditivity of Symplectic Capacities Under Convex Covers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Equip ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, for a positive integer $n$, with the standard symplectic form $\omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^{n}{\,\mathrm{d}} x_j\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}} y_j$. A convex body means a compact convex subset with nonempty interior. A finite convex cover of a convex body $X$ consists of convex bodies $X_1,\ldots,X_m\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, where $m\geq1$, satisfying \[ X\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}X_i. \] The covering bodies are not required to lie inside $X$.

In the usual normalized terminology, a symplectic capacity is a function $c$ on a specified class of symplectic sets, with values in $[0,\infty]$, which is monotone under symplectic embeddings, satisfies $c(\lambda A)=\lambda^2c(A)$ for $\lambda>0$, and obeys \[ c(B^{2n}(1))=c(Z^{2n}(1))=\pi, \] where \[ B^{2n}(1)=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{n}(x_j^2+y_j^2)<1\right\}, \qquad Z^{2n}(1)=\{x_1^2+y_1^2<1\}. \] Here a symplectic embedding $\phi$ satisfies $\phi^*\omega_0=\omega_0$. These are standard conventions for explaining the term; the supplied record does not specify the capacity's domain, normalization, or how its values on closed convex bodies are defined from any domain of open sets. In particular, using $c(X)$ directly or interpreting it as $c(\operatorname{int}X)$ requires a convention not supplied in the record.

Subadditivity question, with the literal quantifier order of the supplied statement. For every positive integer $n$, every convex body $X\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, and every finite convex cover $X_1,\ldots,X_m$ of $X$, does there exist a symplectic capacity $c$, defined on the sets involved, such that \[ c(X)\leq\sum_{i=1}^{m}c(X_i)? \] With this quantifier order, $c$ may depend on the cover. The record does not clarify whether the intended conjecture instead asks for one capacity satisfying this inequality for every finite convex cover in a fixed dimension, or for a compatible capacity across dimensions. Those are stronger uniformity requirements and are not silently imposed here. The unspecified capacity category and boundary convention remain qualifications requiring review.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether each finite convex cover of a convex body admits a symplectic capacity for which the sum of the capacities of the covering bodies is at least the capacity of the covered body. It remains unclear whether one capacity must work for all covers and how capacities are evaluated on closed bodies. The record labels the question open and describes it as a conjectural ingredient for Bang's problem; these are supplied assessments.
