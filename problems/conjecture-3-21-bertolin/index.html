Conjecture 3.21 — Bertolin

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal E_1,\ldots,\mathcal E_n$ be pairwise nonisogenous elliptic curves over ${\mathbb{C}}$. For each $h$, choose fundamental periods $\omega_{1h},\omega_{2h}$ for the associated Weierstrass function $\wp_h$, and associated quasiperiods $\eta_{1h},\eta_{2h}$. For example, in the usual normalization the Weierstrass zeta function satisfies $\zeta'_h=-\wp_h$ and $\zeta_h(z+\omega_{jh})-\zeta_h(z)=\eta_{jh}$. Let $P_{ih}\in\mathcal E_h({\mathbb{C}})$, $1\leq i\leq r_h$, and let $p_{ih}$ and $d_{ih}$ be their associated elliptic integrals of the first and second kinds, respectively, as specified by the source's missing normalization conventions.

The statement uses a field $k_h$ without defining it. The conventional intended choice is $k_h=\operatorname{End}(\mathcal E_h)\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}{\mathbb{Q}}$, embedded in ${\mathbb{C}}$ by its action on the analytic uniformization; this interpretation requires confirmation. Put $\kappa_h=[k_h:{\mathbb{Q}}]$, and let \[ d_h=\dim_{k_h}\operatorname{span}_{k_h} \{\overline p_{1h},\ldots,\overline p_{r_h,h}\} \quad\text{in }{\mathbb{C}}/(k_h\omega_{1h}+k_h\omega_{2h}). \] Here the bars denote classes in that quotient. The integer $d_h$ is distinct from the second-kind integrals $d_{ih}$.

Bertolin's conjecture as recorded here asserts \[ \operatorname{trdeg}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}{\mathbb{Q}}\left( \{j(\mathcal E_h),\omega_{1h},\omega_{2h},\eta_{1h},\eta_{2h}\}_{h=1}^n, \{P_{ih},p_{ih},d_{ih}\}_{1\leq h\leq n,\ 1\leq i\leq r_h} \right) \ \geq\ 2\sum_{h=1}^n d_h+4\sum_{h=1}^n\kappa_h^{-1}-n+1. \] The notation adjoining $P_{ih}$ must mean adjoining coordinates in specified models, since a point itself is not a complex field element; the models and treatment of the identity point are not supplied. No algebraicity of the curves, their invariants, or their chosen points is imposed by the excerpt. Nor is a normalization of the second-kind integrals given. These omissions must be resolved without reducing the conjecture to a statement only about periods.

Short English statement

For pairwise nonisogenous complex elliptic curves and chosen points, Bertolin predicts a specific transcendence-degree lower bound involving their invariants, periods, quasiperiods, point coordinates, and first- and second-kind elliptic integrals. The endomorphism-field and coordinate conventions need confirmation.
