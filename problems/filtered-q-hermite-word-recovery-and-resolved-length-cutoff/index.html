Math-345

Filtered q-Hermite word recovery and resolved length cutoff

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H_{\mathbb{R}}$ have orthonormal basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$, and $-1<q<1$. The $q$-Fock space is the vacuum space ${\mathbb{C}}\Omega$ plus tensor powers, completed using the permutation-weighted inner product with weights $q^{\operatorname{inv}(\sigma)}$. Write $l_i$ for creation by $e_i$, $s_i=l_i+l_i^*$, and \[ \Gamma_{q,n}=W^*(s_1,\ldots,s_n),\qquad l_i^*l_j=q l_jl_i^*+\delta_{ij}I. \] The vacuum state is the usual tracial state. Question 10 contains four related tasks: determine dependence of the von Neumann algebra isomorphism type on $q$, with combinatorial word recovery as a proposed approach; determine when generalized creation relations give a Cuntz extension; determine when generalized field algebras are type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors; and bound the completely bounded norms of length cutoffs. These tasks are not one solved assertion.

For word recovery, decompose a tensor word into maximal constant runs, \[ w=e_{i_1}^{\otimes\alpha_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{i_k}^{\otimes\alpha_k}, \quad\alpha_j\ge1,\quad i_j\ne i_{j+1},\quad N=\sum_j\alpha_j. \] Only adjacent run colors must differ; colors may repeat later. Define monic $q$-Hermite polynomials by \[ H_0^{(q)}=1,\quad H_1^{(q)}(x)=x,\quad H_{a+1}^{(q)}(x)=xH_a^{(q)}(x)-[a]_qH_{a-1}^{(q)}(x), \qquad[a]_q=\sum_{j=0}^{a-1}q^j. \] At $q=0$ these are the monic scaled second-kind Chebyshev polynomials; naming them merely "Chebytchev" leaves a normalization ambiguity. Put $v=H_{\alpha_1}^{(q)}(s_{i_1})\cdots H_{\alpha_k}^{(q)}(s_{i_k})\Omega$. If $w=e_{j_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{j_N}$ and $\Pi_d$ is orthogonal projection onto tensor length $d$, the aid's filtered formula is \[ \Pi_Nv=w,\qquad \Pi_{N-2}v= \sum_{\substack{1\le r<t\le N\\j_r=j_t\\r,t\text{ in distinct maximal runs}}} q^{t-r-1}\, e_{j_1}\otimes\cdots\widehat{e_{j_r}}\cdots\widehat{e_{j_t}}\cdots\otimes e_{j_N}. \] Hats denote deletion. Within-run contractions cancel. This identifies the first lower component, not all components of length $N-4,N-6,\ldots$, nor an orthonormal tensor-word basis for $q\ne0$.

The generalized relations in the source are \[ l_i^*l_j=\sum_{s,t}t_{i,j}^{s,t}l_sl_t^*+\delta_{ij}I. \] One must first specify coefficient conditions yielding a positive Fock representation and bounded operators. For such a representation the source asks when its creation $C^*$-algebra fits an exact sequence with compact ideal and Cuntz quotient, naturally $0\to\mathcal K\to C^*(l_i)\to\mathcal O_n\to0$ in a finite $n$-generator interpretation, and when $W^*(l_i+l_i^*)$ is a $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor. No general admissibility or necessary-and-sufficient coefficient criterion is supplied. The aid reports factoriality for scalar $q$ with finite $n\ge2$; $n=1$ is abelian and is excluded.

For the cutoff, let $Q_d$ keep exact-length-$d$ Wick words and kill other lengths, and $P_{\le k}=\sum_{d=0}^kQ_d$. The source's $P_k$ means length at most $k$, not exact length. The aid reports published bounds $\|Q_d\|_{\mathrm{cb}}\le C_q(d+1)$, implying \[ \|P_{\le k}\|_{\mathrm{cb}}\le\frac{C_q(k+1)(k+2)}2. \] The constant may depend on $q$. It reports the usual radial approximation consequence of weak-star complete metric approximation; the source additionally names CBAP of the noncommutative $L^p$ spaces, $1<p<\infty$, and exactness of the field $C^*$-algebra as intended consequences. Their precise approximation arguments and references are absent.

Finally the aid reports small-$|q|$ isomorphism results for finite generators, unresolved dependence outside that regime, and known dependence in an infinite-generator analogue. Neither the smallness threshold nor the precise infinite-generator statement or citations are supplied. These status reports remain qualified; they do not follow from the word formula or cutoff estimate.

Short English statement

The record combines isomorphism, generalized creation/factoriality, word recovery, and length-cutoff questions. The aid reports a quadratic cutoff bound from exact-length estimates and gives a maximal-run $q$-Hermite formula for the leading and first lower tensor components. Other general questions remain separately qualified.
