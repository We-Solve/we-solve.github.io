Finite Length of Permutation Modules over Arbitrary Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A first-order structure $M$ is $\omega$-categorical if its complete theory has, up to isomorphism, a unique countable model. Put $G=\operatorname{Aut}(M)$. A $G$-set $W$ is oligomorphic if, for every integer $n\geq 1$, the diagonal action of $G$ on $W^n$ has finitely many orbits.

For a field $F$, let $FG$ be the group algebra of the abstract group $G$, and define the permutation module \[ F[W]=\bigoplus_{w\in W}F e_w,\qquad g\cdot e_w=e_{g\cdot w}. \] Thus its elements are finite linear combinations of basis vectors indexed by $W$. An $FG$-module $V$ has finite composition length if there are an integer $\ell\geq 0$ and a chain of $FG$-submodules \[ 0=V_0\subsetneq V_1\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq V_\ell=V \] such that every quotient $V_i/V_{i-1}$, for $1\leq i\leq\ell$, is nonzero and has no proper nonzero $FG$-submodule. The case $\ell=0$ means $V=0$.

Question as supplied. For every $\omega$-categorical structure $M$, every oligomorphic $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$-set $W$, and every field $F$, does the module $F[W]$ have finite composition length over $F\operatorname{Aut}(M)$? There is no restriction on the characteristic of $F$ in the supplied statement.

Unresolved scope qualifications. The supplied statement does not specify the cardinality of $M$, the size of its language, or whether the action on $W$ must be continuous when $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ carries its usual permutation topology and $W$ is discrete. The question above retains the supplied quantifiers; it does not silently add these restrictions. Oligomorphicity alone is not being used as a substitute for an explicit continuity assumption.

The supplied literature assessment discusses the narrower class of homogeneous structures in finite relational languages. That restriction is absent from the supplied mathematical statement, and the incomplete original excerpt does not establish whether it was an intended hypothesis. The excerpt subsequently discusses ordered Ramsey structures, extreme amenability, and a dual module of definable functions; these are presented as surrounding exposition, not as additional hypotheses of the question above. The notation $F[W]$ here follows the supplied cleaned statement and denotes the finite-support permutation module, not that separate function module.

Short English statement

The recorded question asks whether every oligomorphic action of the automorphism group of an $\omega$-categorical structure yields a permutation module with a finite composition series over every field. The intended restrictions on the structure and action require review. The supplied status is "partially solved," based on dated curation rather than independent verification here.
