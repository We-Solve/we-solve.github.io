When holomorphic anomaly equations survive fiber integration

Definitions and notation.
For a Calabi--Yau threefold, the B-model genus-$g$ amplitude $F_g$ is a function or section over complex-structure moduli. A holomorphic anomaly equation describes its anti-holomorphic derivatives in terms of covariant derivatives of lower-genus amplitudes and Yukawa couplings. An elliptic fibration has generic fiber an elliptic curve. To integrate such an equation over a fibration requires specifying the relevant amplitudes, pushforward and boundary contributions.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Does the holomorphic anomaly equation "integrate" over elliptic fibrations?
