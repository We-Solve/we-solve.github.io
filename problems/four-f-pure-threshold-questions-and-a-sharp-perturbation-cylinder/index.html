Math-1229

Four F-pure-threshold questions and a sharp perturbation cylinder

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ have characteristic $p>0$, let $R=k[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]$, $\mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$, and take $0\ne f\in\mathfrak m$. Set \[ \nu_f(p^e)=\max\{a\geq0:f^a\notin\mathfrak m^{[p^e]}\},\qquad \operatorname{fpt}(f)=\lim_{e\to\infty}\frac{\nu_f(p^e)}{p^e}. \] Write $\tau(f^t)$ for the intended test ideal at exponent $t$. The merged record contains four questions, with $c=\operatorname{fpt}(f)$:
  1. Is $c$ rational, for arbitrary coefficient field $k$?
  2. Is $\tau(f^c)$ radical?
  3. If $V(\tau(f^c))$ is zero dimensional, does some integer $N>1$ satisfy $\operatorname{fpt}(f+g)=\operatorname{fpt}(f)$ for every $g\in\mathfrak m^N$?
  4. For fixed characteristic $p$, dimension $n$, and the intended coefficient-field scope, does the set of these thresholds satisfy ACC, meaning that it has no infinite strictly increasing sequence?
The original includes arbitrary $f$; zero and unit conventions must be specified if they are included. Its test-ideal definition over non-$F$-finite fields and whether zero dimensional means a nonempty support are not stated.

The attached aid reports rationality affirmative by Katzman--Lyubeznik--Zhang even for non-$F$-finite coefficient fields, radicality false by Mustata--Yoshida, and fixed-characteristic fixed-dimension ACC affirmative by Sato. It says it did not locate the exact perturbation statement with the displayed test-ideal hypothesis as a settled theorem. This is a limitation of its literature search, not a new assertion that no such theorem exists.

Its proved special-case claim uses a perfect field, $d\geq2$, $R=k[[x_0,\ldots,x_{d-1}]]$, and integers $\ell_i\geq1$: \[ f=x_0^p+\sum_{i=1}^{d-1}x_i^{\ell_i p+1}. \] For every $g\in\mathfrak m^{d(p-1)+1}$ and every $e\geq1$, the aid reports \[ \nu_{f+g}(p^e)=p^{e-1}-1, \qquad\operatorname{fpt}(f+g)=\frac1p. \] More generally, it reports the same Frobenius-level formula throughout the cylinder \[ x_0^p+\left(\mathfrak m^{[p]}\cap\mathfrak m^{p+1}\right). \] The boundary perturbation $g=\prod_{i=0}^{d-1}x_i^{p-1}$, of order $d(p-1)$, is reported to raise the threshold. Hence it calls $d(p-1)+1$ the sharp uniform order radius for this family. This family result does not settle every instance of the third question.

Short English statement

The merged record asks rationality, radicality of the threshold test ideal, finite-order perturbation invariance, and ACC for formal-power-series $F$-pure thresholds. The attached aid reports answers to the first, second and fourth, leaves the exact third formulation unlocated, and supplies a sharp perfect-field perturbation family.
