Definitions and mathematical statement

For a map $f\colon X\to X$, a periodic cycle of least period $n\geq 1$ is an orbit $C=\{x,f(x),\ldots,f^{n-1}(x)\}$ such that $f^n(x)=x$ and $f^k(x)\neq x$ for every integer $1\leq k<n$.

The supplied source, Question 15 of Berger and Bedford's Henon Maps: A List of Open Problems (2023), asks:

Does $H_2$ have both small saddle cycles and small elliptic cycles?
The supplied record does not define $H_2$, its phase space, or the terms "small," "saddle," and "elliptic" in this question. In particular, it does not specify whether smallness concerns orbit diameter, location near a distinguished point, or another quantity, nor whether the question requires arbitrarily small cycles or cycles satisfying a fixed bound. The domain and multiplier conditions governing the saddle and elliptic classifications are also absent.
