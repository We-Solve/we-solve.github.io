Intrinsic-Volume Deficits of Random Spherical Polytopes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a spherically convex body contained in an open hemisphere of its ambient sphere. Here spherical convexity means that the shorter great-circle arc joining any two points of $K$ lies in $K$. The source does not explicitly specify the ambient sphere or the dimension denoted by $d$.

Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be independent random points with the uniform distribution on $K$, and put \[ P_n=\operatorname{conv}_{\mathrm{sph}}\{X_1,\ldots,X_n\}, \] where $\operatorname{conv}_{\mathrm{sph}}$ denotes the smallest spherically convex set containing the indicated points. The usual interpretation of uniform sampling is normalized spherical volume: if $\mu$ is spherical volume on the ambient sphere and $0<\mu(K)<\infty$, then \[ \mathbb P(X_i\in A)=\frac{\mu(A\cap K)}{\mu(K)} \] for each measurable set $A$. This measure convention and the full-dimensionality needed for it require confirmation from the source.

The record refers to intrinsic-volume functionals $U_j$, but supplies neither their definitions nor their indexing and normalization conventions. Retaining these symbols without assigning them an unsupported definition, the proposed question is whether, for every body $K$ satisfying the intended hypotheses and every $j\in\{1,\ldots,d\}$, \[ \mathbb E\left[U_j(K)-U_j(P_n)\right] \asymp n^{-(d+1-j)}\qquad(n\to\infty). \] Here the two-sided interpretation of "of order" is that there exist constants $0<c_{K,j}\le C_{K,j}<\infty$ and an integer $n_0$, possibly depending on $K$, $j$, and the fixed ambient dimension, such that for every integer $n\ge n_0$, \[ c_{K,j}n^{-(d+1-j)} \le \mathbb E\left[U_j(K)-U_j(P_n)\right] \le C_{K,j}n^{-(d+1-j)}. \] The source does not explicitly define "of order"; the displayed interpretation requires confirmation. The exponent is retained exactly as supplied. A fully specified conjecture requires the ambient dimension, the definitions of $U_j$, the sampling convention, and any necessary regularity hypotheses to be checked. No smoothness or rolling-ball assumption has been added to the general question.

Short English statement

Sample $n$ independent uniform points from a spherically convex body contained in an open hemisphere, and take their spherical convex hull. The question asks whether the expected deficit in each $U_j$ has two-sided order $n^{-(d+1-j)}$. The dimension convention, the functionals $U_j$, the sampling measure, and any necessary additional hypotheses remain unresolved in the supplied record.
