Prorepresentable Obstructions for Local Action Deformations

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\rho$ be the local $G$-action on $k[[t]]$ in the supplied record, and let $N\leq G$. Write \[ B=k[[t]]^N=\{b\in k[[t]]:\rho(n)(b)=b\text{ for every }n\in N\}, \qquad \Theta=\operatorname{Der}_k(k[[t]]),\qquad \Theta^\sharp=\operatorname{Der}_k(B). \] Here $\operatorname{Der}_k(A)$ denotes the module of $k$-linear derivations of a $k$-algebra $A$. The action on derivations is by conjugation, \[ g\cdot\delta=\rho(g)\circ\delta\circ\rho(g)^{-1}, \qquad \Theta^N=\{\delta\in\Theta:n\cdot\delta=\delta\text{ for every }n\in N\}. \] Restriction to $B$ gives a map $\Theta^N\to\Theta^\sharp$. The source's quotient $\Theta^\sharp/\Theta^N$ presupposes that this map identifies $\Theta^N$ with a submodule of $\Theta^\sharp$. Its use of the quotient group $G/N$ also requires $N$ to be normal in $G$, although the supplied statement calls $N$ only a subgroup. Subject to these requirements and the induced $G/N$-module structure, put \[ Q=G/N,\qquad M=\Theta^\sharp/\Theta^N. \] The notation $H^1(Q,M)$ denotes first group cohomology.

Write $D_G$, $D_N^{G/N}$, and $D_{G/N}$ for the deformation functors denoted by these symbols in the source, and write \[ \psi=(\psi_N,\psi_Q):D_G\longrightarrow D_N^{G/N}\times D_{G/N} \] for its stated natural map. The supplied record does not define these functors, their coefficient category, their equivalence relation on deformations, or the superscript in $D_N^{G/N}$. These data must be recovered before this becomes a fully specified deformation problem.

Question, conditional on the missing setup. For every local action and subgroup satisfying the intended source hypotheses, does there exist a pro-representable functor $F$, together with an isomorphism of tangent spaces \[ T_F\cong H^1(Q,M) \] and natural transformations \[ a:D_N^{G/N}\longrightarrow F, \qquad b:D_{G/N}\longrightarrow F, \] such that $a\circ\psi_N=b\circ\psi_Q$ and the resulting natural transformation \[ \widetilde\psi:D_G\longrightarrow D_N^{G/N}\times_F D_{G/N} \] is smooth? The fiber product is understood objectwise: for every coefficient object $A$, \[ \left(D_N^{G/N}\times_F D_{G/N}\right)(A) =\{(x,y):x\in D_N^{G/N}(A),\ y\in D_{G/N}(A),\ a_A(x)=b_A(y)\}. \]

In the usual setting of deformation functors on local Artinian coefficient rings with residue field $k$, pro-representability means representation by continuous local homomorphisms from a complete local coefficient ring with residue field $k$, and $T_F=F(k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2))$ with its deformation-theoretic vector-space structure. Smoothness has its infinitesimal lifting meaning: writing $P=D_N^{G/N}\times_F D_{G/N}$, for every small extension $A'\twoheadrightarrow A$ in the intended coefficient category, the map \[ D_G(A')\longrightarrow D_G(A)\times_{P(A)}P(A') \] is surjective. A small extension here means a surjection whose kernel is annihilated by the maximal ideal of $A'$. These explanations are conditional on that conventional setting; the record does not specify the coefficient category, the characteristic or other hypotheses on $k$, finiteness conditions on $G$, or continuity and faithfulness conditions on $\rho$. No such additional hypotheses are asserted as supplied facts.

Short English statement

The question asks whether deformations of a local group action map smoothly to pairs of subgroup and quotient deformations that agree in a pro-representable functor $F$, whose tangent space is $H^1(G/N,\Theta^\sharp/\Theta^N)$. The source leaves essential hypotheses and deformation-functor definitions unspecified, so this is a conditional statement requiring mathematical review. Its open status is supplied dated curation.
