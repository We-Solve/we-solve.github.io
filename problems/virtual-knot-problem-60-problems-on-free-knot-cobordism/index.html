Virtual-knot problem 60 — Problems on Free Knot Cobordism

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A free knot is represented by a framed $4$-valent graph with one unicursal component, modulo the intended free-knot Reidemeister moves; framing specifies opposite half-edges. For the source's spanning-cobordism category, define the slice genus $g_s(K)$ as the minimum genus of an admissible spanning surface with boundary $K$, or $+\infty$ if none exists. The problem is to extend sliceness-obstruction methods to lower bounds on $g_s(K)$ for arbitrary-genus cobordisms, and investigate the stronger parity/group invariants described below.

The source gives two obstacles. First, the path joining two preimages of a generic double-line point need not be homotopically unique on a positive-genus spanning surface, so parity and justified parity need not be well defined. It proposes vanishing of the ${\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$-class dual to the double-line graph $\Psi$, noting a connection with atoms and even-valent graphs. Second, the Morse/Reeb structure can allow splitting and recombination that cancels signed invariant values: its schematic example starts with $L(K)=8$, splits into two components with $L(K_i)=4$, and then allows cancellation $4+(-4)=0$. This is presented as an obstruction to the proposed method, not a proved construction of a specific movie. The phrase that a Reeb graph is not necessarily a disc requires clarification, since a graph and a two-dimensional disc are different objects.

For a spanning surface $\mathcal D_g$ with boundary $S^1$, the source assigns the double-line collection a class \[ \kappa\in H_1(\mathcal D_g,S^1;{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}). \] It reports that this is the sole obstruction to the path-independent even/odd double-line parity and to checkerboard colouring, and defines the cobordism to be checkerboard or atomic when $\kappa=0$. Within this class, to distinguish the source's odd labels $b,b'$, one joins the two preimages of an odd-stratum point and counts intersections with even double lines. The remaining obstruction is the class of the even double lines, \[ \kappa'\in H_1(\mathcal D_g,S^1;{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}). \] An atomic cobordism is $2$-atomic when also $\kappa'=0$. The source states the theorem that if a one-component framed $4$-graph $K$ satisfies $L(K)\ne0$, then no $2$-atomic cobordism of any genus spans $K$. This is a reported theorem only for the restricted class, not an unrestricted slice-genus bound.

The continuation describes a strengthening $G_m$ of the group $G=G_1$, with invariants valued in conjugacy classes of $G_m$, obtained by refining even chords into types and related to iterated parity and deletion of odd crossings. It proposes that all these invariants obstruct sliceness, and asks to investigate their relation to cobordisms. It also reports virtual-knot variants retaining over/under information in addition to parity. The definitions of $L,G,G_m$, the generators/relations, admissible surface singularities, parity counts and cited proofs are missing; neither their full sliceness property nor any unrestricted genus estimate is asserted here.

Short English statement

Seek slice-genus bounds for free knots beyond genus-zero arguments, accounting for double-line homology obstructions and cancellation under Morse moves, and determine the cobordism strength of the refined group-valued parity invariants.
