Homotopy Types of Four-Manifold Homeomorphism and Diffeomorphism Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be the fixed manifold of dimension $4$ appearing in the source. Under the interpretation that boundary-fixing means fixing every boundary point, define \[ \operatorname{Homeo}_{\partial}(M) =\{h\colon M\to M\mid h\text{ is a homeomorphism and }h|_{\partial M}=\mathrm{id}_{\partial M}\}. \] If a smooth structure on $M$ is specified, define \[ \operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(M) =\{f\colon M\to M\mid f\text{ is a smooth diffeomorphism and }f|_{\partial M}=\mathrm{id}_{\partial M}\}. \] Both groups have composition as their operation. If $\partial M=\varnothing$, these boundary restrictions are vacuous. A homotopy type means an equivalence class of spaces under homotopy equivalence.

Problem. For the fixed $4$-manifold $M$, determine the homotopy types of the topological groups \[ \operatorname{Homeo}_{\partial}(M) \qquad\text{and}\qquad \operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(M). \] This is a request to determine the spaces up to homotopy equivalence, rather than merely selected homotopy groups.

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify which $4$-manifolds are intended, whether $M$ is compact, or which smooth structure is used for the diffeomorphism group. It also does not specify the group topologies. The compact-open topology for homeomorphisms and a $C^{\infty}$ topology for diffeomorphisms are possible conventions, but the intended conventions, especially for noncompact $M$, require confirmation. The definitions above use pointwise boundary fixing; requiring maps to equal the identity on a neighborhood of the boundary is another possible convention that the supplied record does not resolve. Thus the displayed problem retains the source's scope, but a fully specified homotopy-type question requires these choices.

Short English statement

For a fixed four-dimensional manifold, determine the homotopy types of its groups of boundary-fixing homeomorphisms and diffeomorphisms. The intended manifold hypotheses, smooth structure, group topologies, and boundary-fixing convention need clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
