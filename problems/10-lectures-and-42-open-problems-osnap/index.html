10 Lectures and 42 Open Problems — OSNAP

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d,m$ be positive integers and $0<s\leq d\leq m$. Let $z_1,\ldots,z_m\in{\mathbb{R}}^d$ have mutually independent entries, with \[ \mathbb P((z_k)_j=-s^{-1/2})=\mathbb P((z_k)_j=s^{-1/2})=\frac{s}{2m}, \qquad\mathbb P((z_k)_j=0)=1-\frac sm. \] Thus ${\mathbb{E}}(z_kz_k^T)=m^{-1}I_d$. If $A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m\times d}$ has rows $z_k^T$, then $\sum_kz_kz_k^T=A^TA$ and $\sum_k{\mathbb{E}}(z_kz_k^T)=I_d$. The entries are independently sparse; each column has expected, rather than fixed, support size $s$.

Part (3) of the source problem conjectures positive universal constants $c_1,c_2$ such that, for all admissible parameters, \[ m\geq c_1\frac{d+\log(1/\delta)}{\epsilon^2},\qquad s\geq c_2\frac{\log(d/\delta)}{\epsilon^2} \] imply \[ \mathbb P\!\left(\left\|\sum_{k=1}^{m} (z_kz_k^T-{\mathbb{E}}(z_kz_k^T))\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\geq\epsilon\right) =\mathbb P(\|A^TA-I_d\|_{\mathrm{op}}\geq\epsilon)<\delta. \] The norm is the Euclidean operator norm, and the constants must be independent of $d,m,s,\epsilon,\delta$. The conventional concentration range is $0<\epsilon<1$, $0<\delta<1$; the source does not explicitly state these ranges or whether $s$ is restricted to integers. They require confirmation, and no claim about a larger parameter range is inferred. The probability event retains $\geq\epsilon$ and the bound retains the strict $<\delta$.

The supplied assessment reports OSNAP-type bounds, up to logarithmic factors, of the forms $m=\widetilde O(d/\epsilon^2)$ and $s=\widetilde O(\log(d/\delta)/\epsilon)$, attributed to Nelson--Nguyên. These are not the exact displayed $s$-hypothesis and do not by themselves establish this independent-entry model or its universal-constant statement; full theorem/model details are absent.

Short English statement

Show that the covariance of independent sparse-sign rows concentrates near the identity using the stated sample and sparsity scales. The source model permits random column support sizes, so a result for a different sparse embedding model cannot be silently substituted. The supplied assessment records OSNAP-related partial progress but says the exact constants/formulation are not pinned down as a theorem. No independent resolution is asserted.
