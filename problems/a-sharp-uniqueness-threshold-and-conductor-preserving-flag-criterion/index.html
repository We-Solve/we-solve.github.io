Math-5146

A sharp uniqueness threshold and conductor-preserving flag criterion

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source fixes an integral surface $S\subset{\mathbb{P}}^3$ of degree $d$, with a double curve $D$ of degree $e$ and singularities described only as "triple points, pinch point, $\ldots$." Its conjecture asks for an integer $n_0$ such that every smooth curve $C\subset S$ of degree $n\ge n_0$ has the following property: a general curve $C'$ in its Hilbert-scheme component $H_C$ is contained in a degree-$d$ surface $S'$ having a double curve $D'$ of degree $e$. Here general means on a dense open subset of the component, and $H_C$ is a component of the Hilbert scheme with the Hilbert polynomial of $C$. The source does not specify a ground field, the full allowed singularity class, the dependence of $n_0$, or which component to choose if $[C]$ lies on several. The double curve is interpreted as belonging to $S'$, rather than requiring $C'\subset D'$.

The supplied partial result reports that $n>d^2$ makes $S$ the unique degree-$d$ surface containing $C$, and excludes containing surfaces of smaller degree. It calls $d^2$ the optimal uniform threshold for this uniqueness assertion; this does not supply the conjectural threshold $n_0$ for preservation of the double curve.

For the aid's local deformation criterion, work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, as a setting in which dominance and tangent criteria can be interpreted without inseparability issues; this field choice is not explicit in the original source. Specify a smooth locally closed stratum $B$ of degree-$d$ surfaces with simultaneous normalization and flat conductor data, preserving the prescribed ordinary singularity type and double-curve degree $e$. The existence and precise construction of such a stratum are additional data, not supplied by the original ellipsis. Let $F$ define $S$, and define \[ \rho:H^0(C,N_{C/{\mathbb{P}}^3})\longrightarrow H^0(C,\mathcal O_C(d)), \qquad \xi\longmapsto\xi(F), \] \[ r:T_{[S]}B\longrightarrow H^0(C,\mathcal O_C(d)), \qquad \eta\longmapsto\text{restriction to }C\text{ of the equation deformation }\eta. \] For the restricted flag space of pairs $(C',S')$ with $S'\in B$, the aid reports that the image of its projection differential to the curve Hilbert scheme is exactly \[ \{\xi\in H^0(C,N_{C/{\mathbb{P}}^3}):\rho(\xi)\in\operatorname{im}r\}. \] Assuming $n>d^2$, smoothness of the curve Hilbert point and flag point, and the containment \[ \rho\left(H^0(C,N_{C/{\mathbb{P}}^3})\right)\subseteq\operatorname{im}r, \] the aid reports that this flag projection is locally an open immersion. Thus the general curve on the corresponding Hilbert component lies on a unique surface in $B$, giving the desired double-curve degree for this specified local setting. Its tangent obstruction is \[ Q=\frac{\rho(H^0(C,N_{C/{\mathbb{P}}^3}))} {\rho(H^0(C,N_{C/{\mathbb{P}}^3}))\cap\operatorname{im}r}. \] It reports that nonvanishing of $Q$ at a general flag obstructs dominance, subject to the same smoothness and characteristic qualifications. The original conjecture remains broader: the aid does not prove the containment uniformly for all sufficiently large-degree curves or construct an appropriate stratum for every source surface.

Short English statement

Must sufficiently high-degree smooth curves on a surface with a double curve deform only to curves on surfaces with the same degree and double-curve degree? The supplied aid gives uniqueness of the containing surface above a degree threshold and a conditional tangent-space test for preserving its conductor data.
