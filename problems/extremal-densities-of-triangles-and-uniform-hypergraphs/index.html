Math-1008

Extremal Densities of Triangles and Uniform Hypergraphs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite simple graph $G$ on $N$ vertices, define \[ p(G)=\frac{|E(G)|}{\binom N2},\qquad t_r(G)=\frac{\#\{S\subseteq V(G):|S|=r,\ G[S]\cong K_r\}}{\binom Nr}. \] Thus each unordered vertex set is counted once. With the usual asymptotic interpretation, the minimum triangle-density problem asks to determine \[ f_3(p)=\inf\left\{\liminf_{j\to\infty}t_3(G_j): |V(G_j)|\to\infty,\ p(G_j)\to p\right\} \qquad\left(\frac12<p\leq1\right). \] Here $1/2$ is the ordinary graph triangle-free critical edge density. The record does not explicitly choose an asymptotic versus finite-size formulation or a density normalization; the displayed convention makes the standard asymptotic request precise.

For a finite simple three-uniform hypergraph $H$, edges are three-element subsets of its vertex set. Let $K_4^{(3)}$ have all four triples on four vertices and put \[ \operatorname{ex}(N,K_4^{(3)}) =\max\{|E(H)|:|V(H)|=N,\ H\text{ contains no }K_4^{(3)}\}, \] \[ \pi(K_4^{(3)}) =\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{ex}(N,K_4^{(3)})}{\binom N3}. \] Containment here is as a subhypergraph, not an additional induced-forbidden condition. The second explicit request is to determine this Turán density.

The source also requests analogous $K_r$ questions for $r>3$. In the ordinary graph setting the natural analogue is the corresponding function $f_r(p)$, obtained by replacing $t_3$ with $t_r$, above the graph $K_r$-free threshold $1-1/(r-1)$. In a three-uniform setting, analogous forbidden complete hypergraphs would be denoted $K_r^{(3)}$. The supplied shorthand $K_r$ does not identify which of these scopes, or which varying-uniformity variant, is intended, so these interpretations are recorded rather than conflated.

The supplied assessment dated 2026-08-21 calls the ordinary triangle question classical, reports important partial flag-algebra results for the three-uniform question, and says the general $K_4^{(3)}$ Turán-density problem remains open. It does not supply an exact value or a complete theorem for the higher-clique request.

Short English statement

The record combines a graph clique-density minimization problem at a prescribed edge density with a hypergraph problem maximizing the density while avoiding a complete four-vertex three-uniform hypergraph. It also asks for higher-clique analogues, whose graph or hypergraph scope needs clarification.
