Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A subnet $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A$ assigns $\mathcal B(I)\subseteq\mathcal A(I)$ compatibly with interval inclusion, covariance and the subnet axioms. The category $\operatorname{Rep}(\mathcal A)$ consists of the net's specified representations or sectors with fusion structure. A necessary-and-sufficient characterization must identify exactly which data determine a subnet and recover all such inclusions; additional data can distinguish nets or inclusions with the same representation category.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Given a conformal net $\mathcal A$, characterize all subnets $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A$ by necessary and sufficient conditions that include information not determined by $\operatorname{Rep}(\mathcal A)$ alone.
Source scope and qualification.
The target is classification for a given net, not reconstruction from $\operatorname{Rep}(\mathcal A)$ alone. Source assumptions such as irreducibility, index and covariance must be stated in an actual characterization.

Short English statement

Characterize every conformal subnet using enough information to go beyond the parent net's representation category.

A concrete source of compatible inclusions.
Suppose a finite group $G$ acts on a given conformal net by automorphisms $\alpha_g$ preserving each interval algebra and commuting with its covariance. Define \[ \mathcal B(I)=\{x\in\mathcal A(I):\alpha_g(x)=x\text{ for all }g\},\qquad E_I(x)=|G|^{-1}\sum_g\alpha_g(x). \] The fixed set is a star subalgebra: sums, products and adjoints of fixed elements remain fixed. Isotony follows from that of $\mathcal A$; locality follows because its disjoint-interval subalgebras already commute. Covariance follows from the commuting action. Also $E_I^2=E_I$, since each group product appears exactly $|G|$ times in the double sum. Its range is precisely $\mathcal B(I)$, and the expectations agree on nested interval algebras.
What the construction retains.
The actual action $g\mapsto\alpha_g$, including its embedding into the parent net, is extra information beyond a bare abstract representation category. Thus fixed-point constructions illustrate a non-tautological supply of candidate subnets using local inclusion data.
