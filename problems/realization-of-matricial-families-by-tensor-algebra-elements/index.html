Realization of Matricial Families by Tensor-Algebra Elements

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a $W^*$-algebra and let $E$ be a $W^*$-correspondence over $M$, that is, a self-dual right Hilbert $M$-module with a normal left action of $M$ by adjointable operators. Let $Z$ be an admissible sequence of weights defining the weighted tensor algebra $\mathcal T_+(E,Z)$, as intended in the supplied record. The record does not specify the admissibility conditions or the construction of this algebra.

Write $\Sigma$ for the intended index class of representations $\sigma:M\to B(H_\sigma)$, and write $D_{X,\sigma}$ for their associated weighted intertwiner domains, retaining the notation of the original statement. The representation class, the domains, and the relation between the parameter $X$ and the weights $Z$ are not defined in the supplied record. For $F\in\mathcal T_+(E,Z)$, denote its asserted Berezin transform by \[ \widehat F=(\widehat F_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Sigma},\qquad \widehat F_\sigma:D_{X,\sigma}\longrightarrow B(H_\sigma). \] Let $\mathfrak M(D)$ denote the intended class of matricial families $f=(f_\sigma)_{\sigma\in\Sigma}$ on these domains. This notation is a placeholder for the source's unspecified compatibility and regularity requirements, not a definition of those requirements.

The supplied premise is that $\widehat F\in\mathfrak M(D)$ for every $F\in\mathcal T_+(E,Z)$. The requested converse is the following realization question: for every choice of $M,E,Z$ satisfying the intended hypotheses, is it true that \[ \forall f\in\mathfrak M(D)\quad \exists F\in\mathcal T_+(E,Z)\quad \forall\sigma\in\Sigma\quad \forall\mathfrak z\in D_{X,\sigma},\qquad f_\sigma(\mathfrak z)=\widehat F_\sigma(\mathfrak z)? \] Equivalently, is the asserted Berezin-transform map $\mathcal T_+(E,Z)\to\mathfrak M(D)$ surjective? No uniqueness is requested. A fully specified mathematical assertion requires the missing definitions of admissibility, the representation class, the weighted domains, and the intended matricial-family conditions. In particular, the record does not state whether boundedness, continuity, analyticity, or boundary-extension conditions are imposed on the families.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every intended matricial family on the weighted intertwiner domains is the Berezin transform of a single element of $\mathcal T_+(E,Z)$. The supplied record leaves essential domain and family conditions unspecified, so the displayed realization question remains conditional on those definitions. Its supplied partially_solved label and unweighted comparison are dated curation requiring independent review.
