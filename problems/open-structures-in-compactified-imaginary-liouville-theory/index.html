Open Structures in Compactified Imaginary Liouville Theory

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

Imaginary Liouville theory uses an imaginary exponential coupling of a scalar field, with parameter $\beta$. Compactification identifies field values periodically. A logarithmic conformal theory may have nonsemisimple dilatation action and logarithmic correlations. CILT refers to the compactified imaginary Liouville theory defined in the report. BPZ minimal models are conformal field theories with central charge $c=1-6(p-q)^2/(pq)$ for coprime integers $p>q$. Felder's BRST reduction extracts the minimal-model state spaces from Coulomb-gas representations.

Questions recovered from the primary report.
The report's five questions ask: (i) how BPZ minimal models are related to CILT; (ii) whether Felder's BRST reduction has a path-integral formulation; (iii) whether the non-self-adjoint CILT Hamiltonian and its Jordan cells yield a logarithmic conformal field theory; (iv) how to construct CILT for $\beta\in[\sqrt2,2)$, where Gaussian multiplicative chaos renormalization is insufficient; and (v) whether there is a probabilistic theory when $\beta^2\notin\mathbb Q$.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Are compactified imaginary Liouville models related to CILT, do they admit a path-integral formulation, are they logarithmic CFTs, and is there a probabilistic theory when $\beta^2\notin\mathbb{Q}$?
Scope and source qualification.
The catalogue condenses five primary-source questions to four and omits the $\beta\in[\sqrt2,2)$ construction question. Its first relationship is with BPZ minimal models, and its path-integral question concerns Felder's BRST reduction. The report already constructs CILT in a smaller rational-coupling regime. The recovered questions above correct these ambiguities; the recorded catalogue wording below is retained for traceability. No positivity of the complex formal path-integral weight is assumed.

Short English statement

Relate compactified imaginary Liouville theory to minimal models, formulate Felder's reduction by a path integral, characterize its logarithmic structure and construct the remaining coupling regimes.
