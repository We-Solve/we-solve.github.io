Math-3397

Conical Kähler–Einstein Metrics and Ricci Lower Bounds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a Fano manifold, so that its anticanonical line bundle $-K_X$ is ample, and let $D\in |-K_X|$ be a smooth divisor. Write $c_1(X)=c_1(-K_X)$. Define the greatest Ricci lower bound by \[ R(X)=\sup\left\{t\in{\mathbb{R}}:\text{ there exists a smooth Kähler form }\omega\in c_1(X)\text{ with }\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)-t\omega>0\right\}, \] where the inequality means positivity as a real $(1,1)$-form. Use compatible normalizations in which $\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)$ represents $c_1(X)$, and let $\{D\}$ denote the current of integration along $D$, normalized to represent $c_1(\mathcal O_X(D))=c_1(X)$.

For $0<\beta<1$, the usual interpretation of a cone-singularity solution is a Kähler metric $\omega_\beta$ on $X\setminus D$, extending to a closed positive $(1,1)$-current in $c_1(X)$, with cone angle $2\pi\beta$ along $D$, satisfying \[ \operatorname{Ric}(\omega_\beta) =\beta\omega_\beta+(1-\beta)\{D\} \] as an equality of currents on $X$. Locally, in holomorphic coordinates with $D=\{z_1=0\}$, the cone-angle condition is ordinarily expressed by uniform comparability on the complement of $D$ with \[ \sqrt{-1}\left(|z_1|^{2\beta-2}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} z_1\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\overline z_1+ \sum_{j=2}^{\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}X}{\,\mathrm{d}} z_j\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\overline z_j\right). \] The supplied record does not specify the precise regularity class, the definition of the singular Ricci current, or normalization factors. The preceding conventions explain the standard intended interpretation; the exact admissible solution class requires confirmation from the source.

Source conjecture. For every such pair $(X,D)$, does a cone-singularity solution exist for every real $\beta$ satisfying $0<\beta<R(X)$? Moreover, if $R(X)<1$, is there no such solution for any real $\beta$ satisfying $R(X)<\beta<1$? The supplied formulation makes no assertion at $\beta=R(X)$, $\beta=0$, or $\beta=1$. These are conjectural assertions as supplied, rather than results established by this draft.

Short English statement

For a Fano manifold $X$ and a fixed smooth anticanonical divisor $D$, the conjecture asks whether conical Kähler--Einstein metrics exist at every angle $2\pi\beta$ below the threshold $R(X)$, and fail to exist above that threshold when $R(X)<1$. The threshold itself is excluded. The precise analytic meaning of a cone-singularity solution needs source confirmation.
