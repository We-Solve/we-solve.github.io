Math-5775

Virtual-knot problem 57 — Is it true that two equivalent realizable graph-links are equivalent in the class of realizable graph-links?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal G$ be the graph-link diagrams with the source's labels and graph Reidemeister moves, and let $\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal G$ be the realizable diagrams. In the usual graph-link interpretation, realizability means representability by the appropriate chord diagram: graph vertices correspond to chords and adjacency to linked, alternating endpoints, with the required labels also respected.

Write $G\sim H$ if a finite sequence of all allowed graph-link moves joins $G$ to $H$, and $G\sim_{\mathcal R}H$ if such a sequence can be chosen with every intermediate diagram in $\mathcal R$. The question is whether \[ \forall G,H\in\mathcal R,\qquad G\sim H\ \Longrightarrow\ G\sim_{\mathcal R}H. \] Equivalently, does inclusion induce an injection $\mathcal R/\!\sim_{\mathcal R}\ \to\mathcal G/\!\sim$? If not, construct realizable endpoints joined by an unrestricted move sequence but by no sequence staying realizable. The requirement is existence of an entirely realizable path, not that every unrestricted path be realizable. The precise labelled graph and move conventions are not supplied by the excerpt.

Short English statement

Can any two equivalent realizable graph-link diagrams be connected through realizable diagrams alone? If not, exhibit a pair for which every connecting sequence must leave that class.
