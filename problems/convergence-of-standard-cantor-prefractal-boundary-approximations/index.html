Convergence of Standard Cantor Prefractal Boundary Approximations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C$ denote the Cantor set referred to in the supplied source, and let $(C_j)$ denote its stated standard prefractal sequence. The record does not specify the construction or ambient space of $C$ and $C_j$. Let $\phi_j^h$ denote the source's boundary-element approximations and $\phi$ the intended limiting solution; their defining equations and function spaces are not supplied. Impose the stated parameter choices \[ i(j)=j,\qquad \delta=0. \] The requested convergence assertion is \[ \phi_j^h\longrightarrow\phi\qquad\text{as }j\to\infty. \] This is an unresolved formulation of the source's requested assertion: the record specifies neither the topology of convergence nor whether $h$ is fixed, depends on $j$, or tends to zero under an additional condition. It also supplies no governing boundary-value or integral equation, boundary conditions, admissible data, or discrete approximation spaces. Consequently, the displayed assertion cannot be made into a fully quantified mathematical statement from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether the boundary-element approximations associated with the standard Cantor prefractals converge to the intended limiting solution when $i(j)=j$ and $\delta=0$. The defining problem, approximation scheme, mesh limit, and meaning of convergence require clarification before a precise theorem can be stated.
