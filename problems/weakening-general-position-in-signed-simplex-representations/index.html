Weakening General Position in Signed Simplex Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $d$, let $P\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be a compact polyhedron with vertex set $V$. Write $\operatorname{aff}(S)$ for the affine hull of a set $S$. The hypothesis under discussion is \[ H_d(V):\qquad \operatorname{aff}(S)={\mathbb{R}}^d\quad\text{for every }S\subseteq V\text{ with }|S|=d+2. \] This condition is vacuous when $|V|<d+2$. Define the collection of nondegenerate simplices supported on vertices of $P$ by \[ \mathcal S_d(V)=\left\{\operatorname{conv}(T):T\subseteq V,\ |T|=d+1,\ \operatorname{aff}(T)={\mathbb{R}}^d\right\}. \] Here $\operatorname{conv}(T)$ denotes the convex hull of $T$.

To make the measure identity explicit in the full-dimensional interpretation, let $\lambda_d$ be Lebesgue measure on ${\mathbb{R}}^d$, suppose $\lambda_d(P)>0$, and define the uniform probability measure on any measurable set $Q$ of finite positive volume by \[ \mu_Q(A)=\frac{\lambda_d(A\cap Q)}{\lambda_d(Q)}\qquad(A\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d\text{ Borel}). \] A signed simplex representation means that there exist an integer $m\geq1$, simplices $\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_m\in\mathcal S_d(V)$, and coefficients $c_1,\ldots,c_m\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that \[ \mu_P=\sum_{j=1}^{m}c_j\mu_{\Delta_j} \] as finite signed Borel measures. The coefficients may be negative; this normalization forces $\sum_{j=1}^{m}c_j=1$. If uniform measure instead means the unnormalized restriction $\lambda_d\!\restriction_Q$, the corresponding representation uses that restriction on each set. For sets of positive $d$-dimensional volume, the existence of a representation is equivalent under these two conventions by rescaling coefficients.

Problem (supplied source formulation). Can the hypothesis $H_d(V)$ in the signed simplex representation theorem be omitted while retaining the stated representation for every polyhedron in the theorem's intended class? If it cannot be omitted, can it be replaced by a strictly weaker sufficient hypothesis on $V$? No particular replacement hypothesis is specified.

Unresolved scope qualifications. The supplied record does not reproduce the complete theorem. In particular, it does not specify whether "compact polyhedron" means a convex polytope or a broader polyhedral set, whether full dimensionality is required, or whether simplices must satisfy additional restrictions such as containment in $P$. The measure identity above is an explicit full-dimensional interpretation of the supplied conclusion, not a reconstruction of missing hypotheses. If lower-dimensional polyhedra or degenerate simplices are intended, the reference measure and admissible simplex dimensions must be specified. The original theorem must therefore be consulted before this interpretation can serve as a definitive statement.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the condition that every $d+2$ vertices affinely span ${\mathbb{R}}^d$ can be removed or weakened while still expressing a compact polyhedron's uniform measure as a finite real linear combination of uniform measures on simplices with $d+1$ vertices from its vertex set. The exact class of polyhedra and the complete theorem hypotheses remain unspecified in the supplied record.
