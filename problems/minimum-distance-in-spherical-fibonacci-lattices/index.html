Minimum Distance in Spherical Fibonacci Lattices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ denote the Fibonacci number intended in the supplied record, and write \[ Z_{F_n}=\{z_0,z_1,\ldots,z_{F_n-1}\} \] for its intended spherical Fibonacci point set. The record does not supply the construction, the sphere's radius, the indexing convention for Fibonacci numbers, or the admissible range of $n$. In particular, the labels $z_0,z_1$ cannot be identified from the supplied data alone.

For a finite set of distinct points in a Euclidean ambient space, define its least pairwise Euclidean distance by \[ \delta(Z_{F_n})=\min_{0\leq i<j<F_n}\|z_i-z_j\|_2, \qquad F_n\geq 2. \] Under the interpretation of the source's absolute-value notation as Euclidean chordal distance, the proposed identity is \[ \delta(Z_{F_n})=\|z_0-z_1\|_2=\frac{2}{\sqrt{F_n}}. \] Thus the question asks whether the indicated pair realizes the minimum and whether its distance has exactly the stated value, for the indices intended by the source. Equivalently, for those indices, it asks whether \[ \|z_0-z_1\|_2=\frac{2}{\sqrt{F_n}} \quad\text{and}\quad \|z_i-z_j\|_2\geq\frac{2}{\sqrt{F_n}} \quad\text{for every }0\leq i<j<F_n. \] The Euclidean interpretation, sphere normalization, coordinate construction, and index range require confirmation from the cited source before this becomes a fully specified conjecture. No geodesic-distance interpretation or particular sphere normalization is asserted here.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether two designated points in a spherical Fibonacci configuration attain its least pairwise distance, with exact value $2/\sqrt{F_n}$. The configuration, distance convention, normalization, and index range are insufficiently specified in the supplied record. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
