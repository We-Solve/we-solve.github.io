Math-4580

Kernels of Nonfaithful Type-Preserving Punctured-Torus Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T$ be a once-punctured torus and let $G=\pi_1(T)$. Write $\widehat{{\mathbb{Q}}}={\mathbb{Q}}\cup\{\infty\}$ and $\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{C}})=\operatorname{SL}(2,{\mathbb{C}})/\{\pm I\}$. A representation $\rho:G\to\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{C}})$ is discrete if its image is discrete in the usual topology, and nonfaithful if $\ker\rho\ne\{1\}$. For a subset $S\subseteq G$, its normal closure is \[ \langle\!\langle S\rangle\!\rangle_G =\bigcap_{\substack{N\trianglelefteq G\\S\subseteq N}}N. \] Under the usual slope interpretation, a marking of $T$ indexes the unoriented isotopy classes of essential nonperipheral simple closed curves by $r\in\widehat{{\mathbb{Q}}}$; $\beta_r\in G$ denotes a representative of the corresponding conjugacy class, up to inversion. Changing the representative by conjugation or inversion does not affect the normal closures below. The supplied record does not explicitly define $\beta_r$, so this interpretation requires confirmation against the cited report.

The record also does not define "type-preserving." A common peripheral condition is that a loop around the puncture has nontrivial parabolic image. Whether the source requires only this condition or also restricts which other elements may have parabolic image is unresolved here. The conjecture below retains the source's type-preserving hypothesis without choosing between these interpretations.

Conjectural statement, subject to these terminology qualifications. For every nonfaithful, discrete, type-preserving representation \[ \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{C}}), \] one of the following holds: \[ \exists r\in\widehat{{\mathbb{Q}}}\ \exists m\in{\mathbb{Z}},\quad m\ge2\quad\text{and}\quad \ker\rho=\langle\!\langle\beta_r^m\rangle\!\rangle_G; \] or \[ \begin{gathered} \exists r_1,r_2\in\widehat{{\mathbb{Q}}}\ \exists m_1,m_2\in{\mathbb{Z}},\qquad r_1\ne r_2,\quad m_1,m_2\ge2,\\ \ker\rho= \langle\!\langle\beta_{r_1}^{m_1},\beta_{r_2}^{m_2}\rangle\!\rangle_G. \end{gathered} \] The alternatives are not asserted to be mutually exclusive. No uniqueness of slopes or exponents, or assertion that the exponents are exact orders of the corresponding images, is included in the supplied statement.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every discrete, noninjective, type-preserving representation of a once-punctured torus group into $\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{C}})$ has kernel normally generated by a power of one simple loop, or by powers of two simple loops of distinct slopes, with all exponents at least $2$. The meanings of the slope notation and type-preserving condition require source confirmation. The supplied open status is a dated curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
