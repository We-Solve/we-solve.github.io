Math-3522

Edge-Disjoint Connected Spanning Subgraphs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an undirected graph $H=(V(H),E(H))$ and a set $S\subseteq V(H)$, define the edge cut \[ \delta_H(S)=\{e\in E(H):e\text{ has one endpoint in }S\text{ and the other in }V(H)\setminus S\}. \] For a positive integer $k$, the cut formulation of $k$-edge-connectivity is \[ |\delta_H(S)|\geq k\qquad\text{for every }\varnothing\neq S\subsetneq V(H). \] A spanning subgraph of $G$ has vertex set $V(G)$. Two subgraphs are edge-disjoint if their edge sets have empty intersection.

Under the positive-integer interpretation of the parameters and the undirected cut interpretation above, the supplied question is the following. For every $a_1,a_2\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}$ and every graph $G$ in the intended graph class, does \[ |\delta_G(S)|\geq a_1+a_2+2 \qquad\text{for every }\varnothing\neq S\subsetneq V(G) \] imply the existence of sets $E_1,E_2\subseteq E(G)$ such that \[ E_1\cap E_2=\varnothing, \qquad G_j=(V(G),E_j), \] and \[ |\delta_{G_j}(S)|\geq a_j \qquad\text{for }j\in\{1,2\}\text{ and every }\varnothing\neq S\subsetneq V(G)? \] The source requires edge-disjoint spanning subgraphs; it does not explicitly require $E_1\cup E_2=E(G)$.

Unresolved scope qualifications. The supplied record does not explicitly specify that the parameters are integers, that graphs are undirected or finite, whether parallel edges or loops are permitted, or whether graphs with fewer than two vertices are excluded. The formulation above makes the usual integer and undirected interpretations explicit without resolving the intended graph class. For graphs with fewer than two vertices the displayed cut conditions are vacuous; the intended boundary convention requires review.

Short English statement

Can every $(a_1+a_2+2)$-edge-connected graph contain two edge-disjoint spanning subgraphs whose edge-connectivities are at least $a_1$ and $a_2$, respectively, for all positive integer parameters? The intended graph class and boundary conventions remain unspecified in the supplied record.
