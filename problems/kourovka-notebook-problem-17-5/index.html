Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.5

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, use the commutator convention $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$ and left-normed iterated commutators $[x,y,z]=[[x,y],z]$. An element $a\in G$ is called a left $3$-Engel element if \[ [x,a,a,a]=1\qquad\text{for every }x\in G. \] Define the lower central series by $\gamma_1(G)=G$ and \[ \gamma_{n+1}(G)=\langle [u,v]:u\in\gamma_n(G),\ v\in G\rangle. \] The group $G$ is nilpotent of class at most $c$ if $\gamma_{c+1}(G)=\{1\}$.

The question is whether there exists a function \[ f:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0} \] such that, for every positive integer $d$, every nilpotent group $G$, and every generating tuple $a_1,\ldots,a_d$ satisfying \[ G=\langle a_1,\ldots,a_d\rangle, \qquad [x,a_i,a_i,a_i]=1 \quad(x\in G,\ 1\leq i\leq d), \] one has $\gamma_{f(d)+1}(G)=\{1\}$. No finiteness hypothesis on $G$ is stated in the source, and the generating tuple is not required to be minimal.

As background, the supplied record states that every group generated by two left $3$-Engel elements is nilpotent of class at most $4$, citing J. Pure Appl. Algebra 188 (2004), 1--6. This background assertion and its incomplete bibliographic reference are reproduced as supplied; they have not been independently verified.

Short English statement

Does the nilpotency class of a nilpotent group generated by $d$ left $3$-Engel elements admit a bound depending only on $d$? The supplied record attributes the problem to A. Abdollahi and reports a class bound of $4$ for groups generated by two such elements.
