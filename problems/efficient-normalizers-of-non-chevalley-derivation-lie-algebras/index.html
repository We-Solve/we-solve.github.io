Efficient Normalizers of Non-Chevalley Derivation Lie Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $k$ and a $k$-algebra $A$ with bilinear multiplication, its derivation Lie algebra is \[ \operatorname{Der}_k(A)=\{D\in\operatorname{End}_k(A):D(ab)=D(a)b+aD(b)\text{ for all }a,b\in A\}, \] with bracket $[D,E]=D\circ E-E\circ D$. This definition explains the usual algebra interpretation of derivations; the supplied record does not specify the objects whose derivations are intended, so it does not establish that this interpretation exhausts the problem's scope. A Lie algebra is called simple here if it is nonzero, nonabelian, and has no nonzero proper ideals. The intended meaning of "non-Chevalley" is not defined in the supplied record and must be fixed before selecting the input class.

For a represented Lie algebra $L\subseteq\mathfrak{gl}_k(V)=\operatorname{End}_k(V)$ and a specified subgroup $G\leq\operatorname{GL}_k(V)$, the group normalizer is \[ N_G(L)=\{g\in G:gLg^{-1}=L\}. \] A different possible target is the Lie algebra normalizer \[ N_{\mathfrak{gl}_k(V)}(L)=\{X\in\mathfrak{gl}_k(V):[X,L]\subseteq L\}. \] These definitions describe distinct possible readings; the source record does not select an ambient group, an ambient Lie algebra, or a representation.

Source question. Can normalizers of the non-Chevalley simple Lie algebras that arise as derivation algebras be constructed efficiently?

Schematically, the requested construction should apply to every member of the intended class of non-Chevalley simple derivation Lie algebras. However, that class, its ground fields and characteristics, its representations, the ambient normalizer, and the computational input and output conventions remain unspecified. In particular, "efficiently" cannot yet be replaced by a precise running-time bound. The question is therefore retained as an unresolved algorithmic formulation, rather than strengthened to a polynomial-time assertion or restricted to an invented domain.

Short English statement

The problem asks for an efficient method to construct normalizers of non-Chevalley simple Lie algebras occurring as derivation algebras. A precise algorithmic statement requires the intended fields, derivation objects, representations, ambient normalizers, and meaning of efficiency. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
