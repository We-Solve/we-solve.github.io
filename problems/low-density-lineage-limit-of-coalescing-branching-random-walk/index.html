Math-13158

Low-density lineage limit of coalescing branching random walk

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Consider each of the two stationary branching-coalescing particle systems on ${\mathbb{Z}}^3$ referred to in Aldous's source. Let $\rho$ denote its particle intensity and let $L^{(\rho)}$ be the intended lineage process. The generator, stationary law, parameterization, lineage sampling rule, and direction of genealogical time are not included in the record.

Write $\overline L^{(\rho)}$ for the source's operation of forgetting spatial positions and suppressing short parent-daughter jitter, and let $a(\rho)$ be the prescribed time-rescaling factor. Neither that operation nor the factor is given. The requested conclusion is finite-dimensional-distribution convergence to the specified Poisson split-and-merge lineage process $L$: \[ \left(\overline L^{(\rho)}_{a(\rho)t_1},\ldots, \overline L^{(\rho)}_{a(\rho)t_k}\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ (L_{t_1},\ldots,L_{t_k}) \qquad(\rho\downarrow0) \] for every finite set of times $0\le t_1<\cdots<t_k$ and for both source models, with the intended state space and initialization.

The limiting lineage state, splitting and merging mechanisms, rates, Poisson independence rules, and initial law are also absent. Thus the display records the exact type of convergence asked for but does not supply a guessed split-merge generator or a particular inverse-density time scale. The source asks only for finite-dimensional distributions, not an unannounced stronger path-space convergence theorem.

Short English statement

Prove the low-intensity lineage limit for each of Aldous's two stationary models on the three-dimensional integer lattice, after the specified time change and removal of spatial and short-jitter information. The models, rescaling, and limit generator must be recovered from the original source.

The supplied summary calls this open and lists related lineage work, but is uncertain about the underlying paper's authorship and date and ends mid-comparison. Those other models do not establish this exact limit. The OPEN-TRIAGE label is retained with current primary-literature audit required.
