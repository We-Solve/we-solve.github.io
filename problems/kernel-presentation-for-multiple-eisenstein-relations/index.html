Math-4101

Kernel Presentation for Multiple Eisenstein Relations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ H^{\geq 2}={\mathbb{Q}}\langle z_2,z_3,\ldots\rangle \] be the free associative algebra over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ on the indicated generators. The source considers a map $G$ sending words to multiple Eisenstein series. To interpret its kernel, take $G$ to mean the intended ${\mathbb{Q}}$-linear extension, with target the ${\mathbb{Q}}$-vector space $\mathcal E$ spanned by its values.

The supplied record uses a product $*$ on $H^{\geq 2}$, a relation map $R\colon H^{\geq 2}\times H^{\geq 2}\to H^{\geq 2}$, and an operator $\theta\colon H^{\geq 2}\to H^{\geq 2}$, but does not give their definitions. With these symbols interpreted as the specific operations intended in the cited report, set \[ \operatorname{DR}^{*} :=\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{Q}}} \left\{\theta^n\left(R(u,v)\right)*w: n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\quad u,v,w\in H^{\geq 2}\right\}, \qquad \theta^0=\operatorname{id}. \] Here the span consists of finite rational linear combinations. The conjecture is \[ \operatorname{DR}^{*}=\ker G =\{h\in H^{\geq 2}:G(h)=0\}. \] Equivalently, for every $h\in H^{\geq 2}$, the condition $G(h)=0$ holds if and only if there exist $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$, coefficients $a_i\in{\mathbb{Q}}$, integers $n_i\geq 0$, and elements $u_i,v_i,w_i\in H^{\geq 2}$ such that \[ h=\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_i\,\theta^{n_i}\left(R(u_i,v_i)\right)*w_i. \]

Unresolved specifications. This is a statement relative to the source's intended operations, not a self-contained definition of them. The supplied record does not specify the formulas for $*$, $R$, or $\theta$, the normalization and analytic domain of the multiple Eisenstein series, or the treatment of the empty word under $G$. These data must be recovered from the cited source before the conjecture can be made fully explicit.

For context, the excerpt introduces the intended formal quotient $\mathcal E^{\mathrm{ff}}=H^{\geq 2}_{*}/\operatorname{DR}^{*}$ and says that the conjecture would identify it with $\mathcal E$. It also attributes a conjectural $\mathfrak{sl}_2$-algebra structure on $\mathcal E$ to its reference [1], and reports such a structure for the formal quotient, with a proof comparing Fourier expansions. The operations defining these structures and the identities of the excerpt's references [1] and [2] are not supplied.

Short English statement

The conjecture says that every rational relation among the intended multiple Eisenstein series is a finite rational combination of derivatives of the basic relations $R(u,v)$, multiplied using $*$ by arbitrary elements of $H^{\geq 2}$, and that every such combination is a relation. The supplied record leaves the defining operations and analytic conventions unspecified, so this statement requires those source definitions and human mathematical review.
