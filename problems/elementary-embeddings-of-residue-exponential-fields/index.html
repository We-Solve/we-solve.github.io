Math-4222

Elementary Embeddings of Residue Exponential Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An ordered exponential field is conventionally a structure $\mathcal K=(K,0,1,+,-,\cdot,<,\exp_K)$ in which $K$ is an ordered field and $\exp_K:(K,+)\to(K_{>0},\cdot)$ is an order-preserving group isomorphism. Work in the language $L_{\exp}=\{0,1,+,-,\cdot,<,\exp\}$. The structure is o-minimal if every subset of $K$ definable with parameters in this language is a finite union of points and intervals.

The supplied record additionally requires an axiom or condition called $\mathrm{EXP}$, but does not state that condition. Accordingly, $\mathrm{EXP}(\mathcal K)$ below denotes the unspecified condition intended by the source; this draft does not supply a replacement definition.

To describe residue exponentiation, let $\mathcal O\subseteq K$ be a convex valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $k=\mathcal O/\mathfrak m$, equipped with its induced ordering. An induced residue exponential is given by \[ \exp_k(a+\mathfrak m)=\exp_K(a)+\mathfrak m\qquad(a\in\mathcal O), \] provided this formula is well-defined and makes $k$ an ordered exponential field. In particular, its use requires that exponentials of elements of $\mathcal O$ reduce to positive nonzero residue classes and that congruent inputs have congruent exponentials. The record does not specify the valuation ring or explain these compatibility requirements. If the natural valuation is intended, its valuation ring and maximal ideal are \[ \mathcal O_{\mathrm{nat}}=\{a\in K: |a|\le n\text{ for some positive integer }n\}, \qquad \mathfrak m_{\mathrm{nat}}=\{a\in K: |a|<1/n\text{ for every positive integer }n\}. \] This possible interpretation is recorded explicitly rather than imposed as an additional source hypothesis. Write $\mathcal K_{\mathrm{res}}$ for the residue exponential structure intended in the supplied record.

The requested assertion is the following formulation schema, pending clarification of $\mathrm{EXP}$ and the residue construction: \[ \forall\mathcal K\;\left[ \left(\mathcal K\text{ is an o-minimal ordered exponential field}\ \land\ \mathrm{EXP}(\mathcal K)\right) \Longrightarrow \exists j:\mathcal K_{\mathrm{res}}\hookrightarrow\mathcal K\text{ elementary in }L_{\exp} \right]. \] Here elementary means that for every nonnegative integer $n$, every $L_{\exp}$-formula $\varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$, and every $\bar a\in K_{\mathrm{res}}^n$, \[ \mathcal K_{\mathrm{res}}\models\varphi(\bar a) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mathcal K\models\varphi(j(\bar a)). \] The question asks for such an embedding into $\mathcal K$ itself. The source describes this as required by the strongest residue-field conjecture, but does not supply that conjecture's full formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether the residue exponential field of every o-minimal ordered exponential field satisfying $\mathrm{EXP}$ admits an elementary embedding into the original field, preserving all first-order formulas in the ordered exponential language. The meaning of $\mathrm{EXP}$ and the intended residue construction require clarification before this becomes a fully specified assertion.
