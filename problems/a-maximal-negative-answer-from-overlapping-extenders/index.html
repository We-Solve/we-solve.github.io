A maximal negative answer from overlapping extenders

Definitions and mathematical statement

The extracted problem has damaged numbering and superscripts. Its mathematical target is: assume there is no inner model with a Woodin cardinal, let $\kappa$ be a singular strong-limit cardinal with uncountable cofinality, and suppose \[ 2^\kappa=\lambda,\qquad \lambda\text{ regular},\qquad\lambda>\kappa^+. \] Must $o(\kappa)^K\geq\lambda$, where $K$ is the relevant core model? Strong limit means $2^\mu<\kappa$ for every cardinal $\mu<\kappa$. The source says a negative answer may have pcf applications and attributes a known lower-background case to Gitik--Mitchell, using a corrupted zero/paragraph-mark notation that the aid interprets as zero hand-grenade. The exact convention for $o(\kappa)^K$ is essential.

The supplied report gives a conditional relative-consistency answer, not an unconditional theorem from ZFC alone. Its background is an iterable anti-Woodin core-model setting below zero hand-grenade carrying the coherent uncountable increasing sequence of sufficiently long overlapping extenders required by a cited 2019 forcing construction of Gitik. Those detailed hypotheses are not reproduced. Relative to that background, the report asserts a cardinal-preserving extension in which \[ \kappa\text{ is singular strong limit},\quad \operatorname{cf}(\kappa)>\omega,\quad 2^\kappa=\lambda=\kappa^{++},\quad\lambda\text{ regular}, \] with the original anti-Woodin requirement retained and $\kappa$ still singular in the generically invariant core model $K$. The range of prescribed uncountable cofinalities and the exact preservation/invariance theorem must be checked in the cited construction.

Under the Gitik--Mitchell full-extender convention specified by the aid, a full $K$-extender with critical point $\kappa$ would make $\kappa$ measurable and therefore regular inside $K$. Consequently the reported $K$-singularity gives \[ o(\kappa)^K=0<\lambda. \] This concerns full extenders whose critical point is $\kappa$, not the assertion that no extender with smaller critical point can overlap $\kappa$. It yields the claimed negative relative-consistency instance only if the background, forcing and generic core-model invariance claims all hold with this order convention. It is not an absolute claim that every model satisfying the question's initial assumptions has $o(\kappa)^K=0$, and no pcf consequence is supplied.

The aid reports a negative relative-consistency answer from a specific overlapping-extender forcing background. In its stated core-model convention, the constructed cardinal stays singular in $K$, so no full extender can have that critical point and its order is zero. The conditional background and missing exact forcing hypotheses remain explicit.
