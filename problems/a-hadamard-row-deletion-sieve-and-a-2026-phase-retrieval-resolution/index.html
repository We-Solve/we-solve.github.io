Math-273

A Hadamard row-deletion sieve and a 2026 phase-retrieval resolution

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

This canonical record merges source problems $1$--$17$, rather than one problem. Items $18$--$21$ in the handout are outside this record. Let $F=(f_j)_{j=1}^N$ be a frame spanning ${\mathbb{C}}^M$ or ${\mathbb{R}}^M$. A unit-norm tight frame (UNTF) satisfies $\|f_j\|=1$ and $\sum_j f_jf_j^*=(N/M)I$. An equiangular tight frame (ETF) also has constant $|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|$ for $i\ne j$; its squared value is $(N-M)/(M(N-1))$ in the nondegenerate range. A real flat ETF has coordinates $\pm M^{-1/2}$. Write $\mathbb T=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|=1\}$, and define the phase-invariant intensity map \[ A_F:{\mathbb{C}}^M/\mathbb T\to{\mathbb{R}}^N,\qquad [x]\mapsto(|\langle x,f_j\rangle|^2)_{j=1}^N. \] Its injectivity means phase retrieval for all signals. The source questions are preserved individually:
  1. Zauner's conjecture: do complex ETFs with $N=M^2$ exist for every positive integer $M$?
  2. Hadamard conjecture: for every positive integer $k$, does a real $4k$-by-$4k$ matrix $H$ with entries $\pm1$ satisfy $HH^T=4kI$?
  3. Paulsen problem: quantify distance from a frame approximately tight and approximately equal-norm to a frame with both properties. The corrupted tolerance glyph is interpreted as $\varepsilon$; the source does not define the approximation norms, frame normalization or distance.
  4. Dustin Mixon: does $N<4M-4$ force noninjectivity of $A_F$?
  5. Dan Edidin: near $N=4M-4$, can the injective-frame locus be open while its complement has positive measure?
  6. Dan Edidin: what does injectivity of the displayed restricted map $B:[x]\mapsto(|\langle x,f_k\rangle|^2)_k$ imply for phase retrieval? Its extracted domain $\mathbb P^{M-1}/\mathbb T$ and missing codomain are unresolved; projective vectors without a norm convention do not define these intensities. The extraction labels this item "16" before item $7$.
  7. Bernhard Bodmann: find measurement numbers $N$ allowing a quantitative stability measure; the metric, signal class and constants are not specified.
  8. Dustin Mixon: find $N$ allowing a quantitative computational-efficiency measure; no algorithm or complexity model is specified.
  9. Ferenc Szollosi: determine the maximum number of equiangular lines in ${\mathbb{R}}^{14}$. The source's historical stated possibilities are $28,29,30$, without a current-status verification.
  10. Ferenc Szollosi: give a full algebraic classification of complex ETFs for small $(M,N)$; the parameter range and equivalence are unspecified.
  11. Matt Fickus: do real equimodular ETFs, represented by sign matrices after normalization, exist with redundancy $N/M$ not approximately $2$? "Approximately" is not quantified.
  12. Ferenc Szollosi: can ETFs be constructed from nonabelian difference sets? The group, difference-set convention and construction are not supplied.
  13. Matt Fickus: what integrality conditions on dimensions constrain complex equiangular frames? The source does not consistently require tightness in this item.
  14. Matt Fickus: seek an elementary proof of the proposed assertion that any $2(M-2)$ two-planes in ${\mathbb{R}}^M$ admit another two-plane having a principal angle $\pi/2$ with each. The intended principal-angle convention, dimensional restrictions and hypotheses must be checked; this is a requested assertion, not adopted as a proved theorem.
  15. Dustin Mixon: for fixed $N$, does "worst case coherence" strictly decrease as $M$ increases? For unit vectors coherence is $\max_{i\ne j}|\langle f_i,f_j\rangle|$; whether the question optimizes its minimum over frames or means another worst-case quantity is unspecified.
  16. Matt Fickus: find a threshold certifying that optimization over UNTFs for $U(F)=\max_{m\ne n}|\langle\varphi_n,\varphi_m\rangle|^2$ is in the well of a global minimizer. Neither the optimization dynamics nor a precise basin criterion is supplied.
  17. Zhiqiang Xu: for $x_0\in{\mathbb{C}}^M$ with $\|x_0\|_0\leq k$ and $b_j=|\langle f_j,x_0\rangle|$, determine minimal $N$ for (a) unique recovery up to phase and (b) recovery by \[ \min_{x\in{\mathbb{C}}^M}\|x\|_1\quad\text{subject to}\quad |\langle f_j,x\rangle|=b_j\quad(1\leq j\leq N). \] The source leaves uniform versus pointwise recovery and generic versus designed frames unspecified.

The aid's Hadamard partial result uses an order-$h$ Hadamard matrix split into $r$ rows and $h-r$ complementary rows, with $1\leq r<h$. After normalizing columns in their respective dimensions, one family is a flat ETF exactly when the other is. If $\lambda$ is the common absolute raw cross-inner-product of the $r$-row columns, the necessary conditions are \[ \lambda^2=\frac{r(h-r)}{h-1},\qquad \lambda\equiv r\pmod2,\qquad h-1\mid r(r-1). \] Thus fixed $r\geq2$ cannot produce such ETFs for arbitrarily large $h$; $r=1$ yields the standard complementary flat simplex family. This is a restriction on that deletion mechanism, not on all real flat ETFs or on the Hadamard conjecture itself.

The aid separately reports that the phase-retrieval-injective frame locus is open, and combines Vinzant's injective $11$-frame in ${\mathbb{C}}^4$ with Li's June 2026 nonempty open noninjective locus. Under those reported results, $(M,N)=(4,11)$ gives a nonempty open injective locus and a positive-Lebesgue-measure complement, answering item $5$ affirmatively and refuting the universal formulation in item $4$. Li's result is explicitly a recent preprint, and no precise citation is supplied. No resolution of the remaining merged questions is asserted.

Short English statement

This record contains seventeen frame and phase-retrieval questions. The supplied partial progress restricts ETFs made by deleting a fixed number of Hadamard rows and reports a $(4,11)$ phase-retrieval example with both injective and open noninjective regions. All other questions, ambiguities, historical bounds and the recent-preprint caveat remain separate.
