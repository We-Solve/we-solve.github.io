Math-2355

Model categories 3 — Every stable homotopy category I know of comes from a model category.

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(A,\Gamma)$ be a flat Hopf algebroid in the source's convention, let $\operatorname{Comod}_\Gamma$ be its category of comodules, and let $\operatorname{Ch}(\operatorname{Comod}_\Gamma)$ be the intended category of chain complexes of comodules. The unit-map flatness convention, graded/ungraded setting, and boundedness of complexes are not specified.

The source reports that Hovey and Strickland constructed a stable homotopy category $\mathcal S_{A,\Gamma}$ of comodules but had not yet produced its proposed model. Its definition, morphisms, and equivalences are not included.

Model-categories problem 3. Construct a model structure $\mathcal M_{A,\Gamma}$ on $\operatorname{Ch}(\operatorname{Comod}_\Gamma)$ whose homotopy category recovers that particular stable category: \[ \operatorname{Ho}(\mathcal M_{A,\Gamma})\simeq\mathcal S_{A,\Gamma}, \] with the intended stable/triangulated and any specified additional structure. A precise solution must identify weak equivalences, cofibrations and fibrations, verify the model axioms, and establish the required comparison. The source assumes flatness and does not explicitly restrict to an Adams-type Hopf algebroid or impose further generation hypotheses; such restrictions cannot be silently added.

The supplied summary marks the problem solved and cites general model structures for complexes and derived categories of comodules. However, the ordinary derived category obtained by inverting quasi-isomorphisms is not automatically the Hovey--Strickland stable homotopy category. A model for $D(\operatorname{Comod}_\Gamma)$ alone does not answer the stated comparison without identifying the exact weak equivalences and target. The broad solved claim therefore requires a precise theorem matching the flatness and category hypotheses.

Hovey's note that work was in progress and details could be obtained from him is historical source context, not an instruction to contact anyone.

Short English statement

Realize the specified Hovey--Strickland stable comodule category as the homotopy category of a model structure on chain complexes. The registry says solved, but a generic derived-category model is not enough without matching this particular stable category and its hypotheses.
