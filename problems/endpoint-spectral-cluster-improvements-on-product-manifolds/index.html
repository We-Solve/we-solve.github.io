Endpoint Spectral-Cluster Improvements on Product Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns an arbitrary product manifold $M=X\times Y$ of total dimension $d$. For integer $d>1$, define the critical exponent \[ q_d=\frac{2(d+1)}{d-1}, \] and, for real $\lambda>1$, define the proposed spectral-window width \[ \varepsilon(\lambda)=\frac{1}{\log\lambda}. \] The restriction $d>1$ makes the displayed critical exponent meaningful; the supplied record does not explicitly state its intended dimension restrictions.

Question as supplied. For every product manifold $M=X\times Y$ in the intended geometric class, do the logarithmically improved spectral-cluster bounds with window width $\varepsilon(\lambda)$, reported in the supplied source for $q>q_d$, extend to $q=q_d$ and to every $q$ with $2<q<q_d$?

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not specify the geometric class, metric, compactness or boundary assumptions, spectral operator, spectral-window convention, operator norms, or the numerical inequality and logarithmic gain whose extension is requested. Consequently, the question above preserves the supplied target but does not determine a precise quantified spectral-cluster inequality. These missing specifications must be recovered from the cited report before a complete mathematical statement can be formulated. In particular, no uniformity over manifolds or factors, and no particular dependence of constants on $M$ or $q$, is asserted here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether logarithmic improvements for spectral clusters on arbitrary product manifolds also hold at the critical exponent $q_d$ and throughout $2<q<q_d$. The supplied record omits the exact estimate and geometric hypotheses, so this remains an incomplete formulation requiring mathematical review.
