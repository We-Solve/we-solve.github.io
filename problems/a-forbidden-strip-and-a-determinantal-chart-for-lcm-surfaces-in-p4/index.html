A forbidden strip and a determinantal chart for lCM surfaces in P4

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks which numerical invariants occur for locally Cohen--Macaulay surfaces in ${\mathbb{P}}^4$, naming degree and sectional genus. Work geometrically over an algebraically closed field, since the source specifies no field. Let $S\subset{\mathbb{P}}^4$ be pure two-dimensional and locally Cohen--Macaulay, let $d=\deg S$, let $\pi=p_a(S\cap H)$ for a general regular hyperplane, and let $\chi=\chi(\mathcal O_S)$. Its Hilbert polynomial is \[ P_S(m)=\frac d2m^2+\left(\frac d2+1-\pi\right)m+\chi. \] The source does not require smoothness, integrality, nondegeneracy or arithmetic Cohen--Macaulayness.

The aid reports a forbidden strip, obtained from the regular hyperplane curve and Hartshorne's locally Cohen--Macaulay space-curve theorem: for $d\geq2$, \[ \pi=\binom{d-1}{2}\quad\hbox{or}\quad \pi\leq\binom{d-2}{2}. \] Degree one gives the plane case with sectional genus zero. This necessary restriction does not classify all possible triples.

For the arithmetically Cohen--Macaulay (ACM), codimension-two subgeography, write a Hilbert--Burch resolution \[ 0\longrightarrow\bigoplus_j\mathcal O(-b_j) \longrightarrow\bigoplus_i\mathcal O(-a_i) \longrightarrow\mathcal I_S\longrightarrow0. \] With $M_r=\sum_j b_j^r-\sum_i a_i^r$, and the rank/first-moment cancellations of this resolution, the aid's moment formulas are \[ d=\frac{M_2}{2},\qquad \pi=1+\frac{M_3-6M_2}{6},\qquad \chi=\frac{M_4-10M_3+35M_2}{24}. \] These formulas assume an actual codimension-two ACM resolution; arbitrary degree lists need not define a surface.

For every pair of positive integers $(t,e)$, the report gives a realizable family from maximal minors of a general $t\times(t+1)$ matrix of degree-$e$ forms, of expected codimension two. The exact invariants are \[ d=\frac{t(t+1)e^2}{2},\qquad \pi=1+\frac{t(t+1)e^2((2t+1)e-6)}6, \] \[ \chi=\frac{t(t+1)e^2\left((3t^2+3t+1)e^2-10(2t+1)e+35\right)}{24}. \] These are ACM, hence locally Cohen--Macaulay, examples; no assertion that every locally Cohen--Macaulay surface is ACM is made.

The aid further reports generation of new triples by sufficiently positive proper complete-intersection linkage. In the standard residual-link convention, let $S,S'$ be properly linked in a complete intersection $Y$ of degrees $(a,b)$, set $c=a+b-5$, and use the required local Cohen--Macaulay hypotheses. The transformation can be expressed by \[ P_{S'}(m)=P_Y(m)-P_S(c-m), \] hence \[ d'=ab-d,\qquad \pi'=\pi+\frac{(ab-2d)(a+b-4)}2, \] \[ \chi'=\chi(\mathcal O_Y)-\chi-\frac d2c^2-c\left(\frac d2+1-\pi\right), \] where \[ \chi(\mathcal O_Y)=\frac{ab\left(2a^2+3ab+2b^2-15a-15b+35\right)}{12}. \] A proper residual link must actually exist; these identities are not an unrestricted recipe for declaring arbitrary numerical triples realizable. The forbidden strip, determinantal chart and linkage operations leave the full geography problem unresolved in the supplied assessment.

Short English statement

The aid gives a necessary gap in possible sectional genera and an explicit two-parameter family of realizable degree, genus and Euler-characteristic triples. Hilbert--Burch resolutions and proper linkage provide further computations. These cover parts of the geography, rather than all locally Cohen--Macaulay surfaces in projective four-space.
