Distal Actions with Negative Infinite Sofic Entropy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks whether a distal action can have sofic topological entropy equal to $-\infty$. Its ambient group, space, and quantification over sofic approximations are not specified. The following is a precise formulation under the usual convention of countable discrete sofic groups acting on nonempty compact metrizable spaces; these conventions require confirmation against the source.

Let $G$ be a countable discrete group. A sofic approximation is a sequence $\Sigma=(\sigma_i\colon G\to\operatorname{Sym}(d_i))_{i\geq1}$, with $d_i\to\infty$, such that, for every $s,t\in G$, \[ \frac{1}{d_i}\left|\{v:\sigma_i(st)v=\sigma_i(s)\sigma_i(t)v\}\right|\longrightarrow1, \] and, for every distinct $s,t\in G$, \[ \frac{1}{d_i}\left|\{v:\sigma_i(s)v\ne\sigma_i(t)v\}\right|\longrightarrow1. \] The existence of such a sequence means that $G$ is sofic.

Let $G\curvearrowright X$ be an action by homeomorphisms on a nonempty compact metrizable space, and let $\rho$ be a compatible metric. The action is distal if \[ \forall x,y\in X,\quad x\ne y\ \Longrightarrow\ \inf_{g\in G}\rho(gx,gy)>0. \] For a nonempty finite set $F\subseteq G$ and $\delta>0$, define \[ \operatorname{Map}(\rho,F,\delta,\sigma_i) =\left\{\phi\colon\{1,\ldots,d_i\}\to X: \max_{s\in F}\left(\frac1{d_i}\sum_{v=1}^{d_i} \rho(s\phi(v),\phi(\sigma_i(s)v))^2\right)^{1/2}<\delta\right\}. \] For maps $\phi,\psi$, put $\rho_\infty(\phi,\psi)=\max_v\rho(\phi(v),\psi(v))$. Write $N_\varepsilon(Y,\rho_\infty)$ for the largest cardinality of a subset of $Y$ whose distinct points have distance at least $\varepsilon$, with $N_\varepsilon(\varnothing,\rho_\infty)=0$. In this convention, the sofic topological entropy relative to $\Sigma$ is \[ h_\Sigma(G\curvearrowright X) =\sup_{\varepsilon>0}\ \inf_{\substack{F\subseteq G\text{ finite}\\F\ne\varnothing}}\ \inf_{\delta>0} \limsup_{i\to\infty}\frac1{d_i}\log N_\varepsilon\left(\operatorname{Map}(\rho,F,\delta,\sigma_i),\rho_\infty\right), \qquad \log0=-\infty. \] Question under these conventions. Do there exist $G$, a sofic approximation $\Sigma$ of $G$, and a distal action $G\curvearrowright X$ as above such that \[ h_\Sigma(G\curvearrowright X)=-\infty? \] The existential quantifier over $\Sigma$ is an explicit possible interpretation, not a source-verified qualification. The supplied record does not determine whether a particular approximation is understood, whether the conclusion is required for every approximation, or whether additional ambient hypotheses apply.

Short English statement

Can a distal action have sofic topological entropy $-\infty$? A precise version in the standard compact metrizable setting is given above, but the intended group, space assumptions, and quantification over sofic approximations require source confirmation. The supplied record labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
