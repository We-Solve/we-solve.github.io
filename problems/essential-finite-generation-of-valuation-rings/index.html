Math-3710

Essential Finite Generation of Valuation Rings

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $L\subseteq F$ be fields with $[F:L]<\infty$, and let $v:F\to\Gamma\cup\{\infty\}$ be a valuation, where $\Gamma$ is an ordered abelian group. The valuation on $L$ is the restriction of $v$. For $K\in\{L,F\}$, define the valuation ring and its maximal ideal by \[ O_K=\{x\in K:v(x)\geq 0\},\qquad \mathfrak m_K=\{x\in K:v(x)>0\}. \] For finitely many elements $a_1,\ldots,a_r\in O_F$, put \[ A=O_L[a_1,\ldots,a_r],\qquad \mathfrak p=A\cap\mathfrak m_F. \] Then $\mathfrak p$ is a prime ideal of $A$, and its localization, viewed inside $F$, is \[ A_{\mathfrak p}=\left\{\frac{b}{s}:b\in A,\ s\in A\setminus\mathfrak p\right\}. \] The supplied question asks whether, for every such finite valued-field extension $(F\mid L,v)$, there exist an integer $r\geq 1$ and elements $a_1,\ldots,a_r\in O_F$ such that \[ O_F=\left(O_L[a_1,\ldots,a_r]\right)_{O_L[a_1,\ldots,a_r]\cap\mathfrak m_F}. \] Equivalently, is $O_F$ always essentially of finite type over $O_L$ in this specified sense? The supplied statement imposes no additional restriction on characteristic, valuation rank, separability, henselianity, or normality.

The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports a known necessary condition, a theorem characterizing finite generation of the integral closure, and sufficiency for henselian or normal extensions. It does not state those conditions, identify the precise integral closure in that theorem, or specify the hypotheses and meaning of those sufficiency claims. These contextual claims therefore cannot be formulated precisely from this record and are not additional hypotheses of the displayed question.

Short English statement

For every finite extension of valued fields, can the larger valuation ring be obtained by adjoining finitely many of its elements to the smaller valuation ring and then localizing at the contraction of its maximal ideal? The supplied partially solved label and special-case claims are dated curation requiring independent review.
