Partial Regularity of Multivalued Quasiconvex Minimizers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

For positive integers $Q,n$, let \[ \mathcal A_Q({\mathbb{R}}^n)=({\mathbb{R}}^n)^Q/\mathfrak S_Q, \] where $\mathfrak S_Q$ acts by permuting coordinates. Thus an element is an unordered collection of $Q$ points, counted with multiplicity. Its standard matching metric is \[ \mathcal G([a_1,\ldots,a_Q],[b_1,\ldots,b_Q]) =\min_{\sigma\in\mathfrak S_Q} \left(\sum_{i=1}^Q |a_i-b_{\sigma(i)}|^2\right)^{1/2}. \] A $Q$-valued map on a domain $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^m$ is a map $u:\Omega\to\mathcal A_Q({\mathbb{R}}^n)$. These definitions provide terminology; the source does not specify $m,n,Q$, the domain, or the admissible function space.

For a functional $\mathcal F$ and a specified competitor class $\mathcal C(u)$, the minimizing property means \[ \mathcal F(u)\leq\mathcal F(v) \qquad\text{for every }v\in\mathcal C(u). \] The competitor class encodes the boundary conditions or the permitted local variations, neither of which is supplied here.

Source question. Do minimizers of quasiconvex functionals on $Q$-valued functions have partial regularity, and for which subclasses of functionals do regularity results hold?

The requested partial regularity concerns regularity away from an exceptional set. The source does not specify the regularity of the local sheets, the treatment of branching, or the required size of that exceptional set. It also supplies no definition of quasiconvexity for the multivalued setting, no integrand class, and no growth, coercivity, ellipticity, or differentiability hypotheses. Consequently, the supplied question determines a research direction but does not determine a single quantified regularity assertion. A precise theorem or conjecture requires these choices; none is imposed in this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether minimizing maps taking unordered $Q$-tuples of values are regular away from a suitably small exceptional set, and which subclasses of quasiconvex functionals admit such results. The functional class, admissible variations, boundary conditions, and intended regularity conclusion remain unspecified.
