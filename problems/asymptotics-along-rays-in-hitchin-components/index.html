Asymptotics Along Rays in Hitchin Components

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be a closed oriented surface of genus $g\geq 2$. The supplied statement concerns a stable $\operatorname{SL}(n,{\mathbb{C}})$-Higgs bundle $(E,\varphi)$ on $S$. To interpret this terminology, one needs a complex structure on $S$; write $X$ for the resulting compact Riemann surface and $K_X$ for its holomorphic cotangent bundle. This complex structure is not specified in the record. Here $E$ is a holomorphic vector bundle of rank $n$ with trivialized determinant, and \[ \varphi\in H^0\left(X,\operatorname{End}(E)\otimes K_X\right),\qquad \operatorname{tr}(\varphi)=0. \] Stability means that every nonzero proper $\varphi$-invariant holomorphic subbundle $F\subset E$ satisfies \[ \frac{\deg F}{\operatorname{rank}F}<\frac{\deg E}{n}=0. \] The record does not state a range for the rank $n$.

For a positive real parameter $t$, consider the scaled Higgs bundle $(E,t\varphi)$. In the usual harmonic-metric interpretation, if $h_t$ is its determinant-normalized harmonic metric and $D_{h_t}$ is the corresponding Chern connection, the associated flat connection is \[ \nabla_t=D_{h_t}+t\varphi+t\varphi^{*_{h_t}}. \] This explains the standard construction implicit in the record; the record itself does not specify metric or gauge conventions. After choosing a base point $x_0\in S$ and a determinant-one frame at $x_0$, its holonomy gives a representation \[ \rho_t:\pi_1(S,x_0)\longrightarrow\operatorname{SL}(n,{\mathbb{C}}). \] Without that frame, the intrinsic object is the conjugacy class $[\rho_t]$.

For points $P,Q\in S$ and a chosen path $\gamma$ from $P$ to $Q$, parallel transport defines \[ \operatorname{PT}^{\nabla_t}_{\gamma}:E_P\longrightarrow E_Q. \] Frames at the endpoints turn this map into a matrix. The notation $T_{P,Q}(t)$ in the record suppresses the path and frame choices. Flatness makes transport invariant under homotopies fixing the endpoints, but does not generally make it independent of the path's homotopy class. The record does not specify whether $P,Q$ instead denote points on a universal cover.

Requested asymptotic problem. Determine the asymptotic behavior, as $t\to+\infty$, of the representations $\rho_t$ and the parallel-transport matrices $T_{P,Q}(t)$ associated with $(E,t\varphi)$, particularly for rays whose associated representations lie in a Hitchin component.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The record does not specify a topology, normalization, asymptotic scale, error estimate, or conjugacy-invariant observable in which the requested behavior is to be determined. It also does not quantify the paths, endpoints, or loops for which asymptotics are requested. The Hitchin-component specialization requires identifying an appropriate split real character variety and its embedding in the complex setting; the real group, lift conventions, and conditions selecting the intended Higgs-bundle rays are not supplied. Consequently, the displayed request is an asymptotic research question with unresolved specifications, rather than a uniquely determined convergence or expansion assertion.

Short English statement

For a stable Higgs bundle on a closed oriented surface of genus at least $2$, scale its Higgs field by $t>0$ and study the resulting holonomy and parallel transport as $t\to+\infty$, especially along Hitchin-component rays. The source leaves the precise asymptotic target and several geometric choices unspecified. Its partially solved classification is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
