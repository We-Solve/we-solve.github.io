Mutations of Higher Grassmannian Degenerations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For integers $k,n$ with $2<k\leq n$, let $\operatorname{Gr}_K(k,n)$ denote the Grassmannian of $k$-dimensional linear subspaces of $K^n$, where the base field $K$ remains to be specified. Its Plücker ideal is \[ I_{k,n}\subseteq K[p_J: J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\},\ |J|=k]. \] The supplied record refers to the tropicalization of this Plücker embedding. It does not specify the field, valuation, or fan structure used to interpret its maximal cones.

In a formulation using a trivially valued field and a fan on which initial ideals are constant on relative interiors, a maximal cone $C$ determines an initial ideal \[ J_C=\operatorname{in}_{w}(I_{k,n}),\qquad w\in\operatorname{relint}(C). \] When the associated initial degeneration is toric, denote it by $X_C$. A maximal tropical cone does not, merely by being maximal, guarantee a toric initial degeneration. Thus the phrase "toric degenerations arising from distinct maximal cones" requires either restricting to cones that give toric degenerations or specifying an additional construction for other cones.

Problem, with unresolved qualifications. For higher Grassmannians, meaning $k>2$, characterize the combinatorial mutations relating toric degenerations $X_C$ and $X_{C'}$ arising from distinct maximal cones $C\neq C'$ in the tropical Grassmannian. A precise characterization should specify the lattice polytopes or other combinatorial objects attached to these degenerations, the permitted mutation operations, and the conditions under which those objects are related by the permitted operations.

The supplied record does not define "such mutations": its quoted original passage refers to preceding context that is not supplied. In particular, it does not determine whether the intended relation is a single combinatorial mutation or a finite sequence of mutations, which polytopes and lattice identifications are intended, or which maximal cones are eligible. The statement therefore remains conditional on these choices. No assertion that every pair of toric degenerations is mutation-related is supplied or made here. The intended range of $n$, including whether the boundary cases $n=k$ and $n=k+1$ are excluded, is also unspecified.

Short English statement

Describe the combinatorial mutations connecting toric degenerations of Grassmannians with $k>2$ obtained from different maximal tropical cones. The supplied record leaves the mutation convention, associated combinatorial objects, and eligible cones unspecified, so this is a qualified statement requiring mathematical review.
