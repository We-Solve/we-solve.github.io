Stanley's Positivity Problems in Algebraic Combinatorics — Problem 8: Adin's cubical h-vector nonnegativity and GLBT for cubical complexes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $L$ be a finite pure cubical complex of rank $d$, so every maximal cell has dimension $d-1$, with $d\geq 1$. Finiteness and positive rank are explicit working qualifications for this draft; the supplied statement does not state them separately. Write $V(L)$ for its vertices and $f_j(L)$ for its number of $j$-dimensional cells. Define \[ \widetilde\chi(L)=-1+\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}(-1)^j f_j(L). \] For each $s\in V(L)$, let $\Delta_s=\operatorname{link}_L(s)$ be its simplicial vertex link, of dimension $d-2$. Use the increasing-power convention for its simplicial $h$-polynomial: \[ h(\Delta_s,x)=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1} f_{j-1}(\Delta_s)x^j(1-x)^{d-1-j},\qquad f_{-1}(\Delta_s)=1. \] For $d=1$, the vertex link is the complex consisting only of the empty face and its $h$-polynomial is $1$. The source record does not explicitly expand its simplicial $h$-polynomial notation; this convention requires confirmation against the cited source.

Define Adin's cubical $h$-vector $h(L)=(h_0(L),\ldots,h_d(L))$ by the supplied identity \[ \sum_{i=0}^{d}h_i(L)x^i =\frac{1}{1+x}\left( 2^{d-1}+x\sum_{s\in V(L)}h(\Delta_s,x) +(-2)^{d-1}\widetilde\chi(L)x^{d+1} \right). \]

To make the homological terminology explicit, fix a coefficient field $k$. Let $P(L)$ be the poset of nonempty faces of $L$, ordered by inclusion, and let $K$ be its order complex, whose simplices are chains in $P(L)$. Any artificially adjoined minimum or maximum is omitted. Interpret the Cohen--Macaulay condition over $k$ as \[ \widetilde H_j(\operatorname{link}_K\sigma;k)=0 \quad\text{for every face }\sigma\in K\text{, including }\varnothing, \text{ and every }j<\dim\operatorname{link}_K\sigma. \] Interpret the Gorenstein* condition over $k$ as requiring, for every such $\sigma$, \[ \widetilde H_j(\operatorname{link}_K\sigma;k)\cong \begin{cases} k,&j=\dim\operatorname{link}_K\sigma,\\ 0,&j\neq\dim\operatorname{link}_K\sigma. \end{cases} \] Here reduced homology uses the usual augmented convention in degree $-1$. The supplied record does not identify a coefficient field or specify the intended quantification over fields; the following questions are therefore stated with $k$ as an unresolved parameter.

  1. (a) For every such $L$ whose face poset is Cohen--Macaulay over $k$, is \[ h_i(L)\geq 0\qquad\text{for every }i\in\{0,\ldots,d\}? \]
  2. (b) For every such $L$ whose face poset is additionally Gorenstein* over $k$, is \[ h_0(L)\leq h_1(L)\leq\cdots\leq h_{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}(L)? \]
The supplied background attributes these questions to Ron M. Adin and reports that he proved part (a) when $L$ is shellable. The registry labels the record open; neither that status nor the reported partial result is independently verified here.

Short English statement

The questions ask whether Adin's cubical $h$-vector is entrywise nonnegative for every Cohen--Macaulay cubical complex and whether its entries increase through the middle under the additional Gorenstein* hypothesis. The supplied background reports a proof of nonnegativity for shellable complexes. The coefficient field, precise face-poset conventions, and simplicial $h$-polynomial convention require source review.
