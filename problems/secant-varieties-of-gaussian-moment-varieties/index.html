Secant Varieties of Gaussian Moment Varieties

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for the dimensions, degrees, and defining equations of Gaussian moment secant varieties for every triple of integers $k,n,d\geq 1$. It does not define $G_{n,d}$, its ground field, its ambient space, or its secant-indexing convention. The following gives a precise conditional formulation using complex projective moment varieties with unrestricted symmetric covariance matrices; these conventions require confirmation against the cited report.

Let $\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, let \[ A_{n,d}=\{\alpha\in\mathbb N_0^n:|\alpha|\leq d\},\qquad N=\binom{n+d}{d}-1, \] and give $\mathbb P^N_{\mathbb C}$ homogeneous coordinates $m_\alpha$, indexed by $A_{n,d}$. For $\mu\in\mathbb C^n$ and a symmetric matrix $\Sigma\in\operatorname{Sym}_n(\mathbb C)$, define the moment polynomials \[ M_\alpha(\mu,\Sigma)=\alpha!\,[t^\alpha]\exp\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^n\mu_i t_i+\frac12\sum_{i,j=1}^n\Sigma_{ij}t_it_j\right), \] where $|\alpha|=\sum_i\alpha_i$, $\alpha!=\prod_i\alpha_i!$, and coefficient extraction is in a formal power series. In particular, $M_0=1$. Under the stated convention, the Gaussian moment variety is the reduced projective Zariski closure \[ G_{n,d}=\overline{\left\{[M_\alpha(\mu,\Sigma)]_{\alpha\in A_{n,d}}:\mu\in\mathbb C^n,\ \Sigma\in\operatorname{Sym}_n(\mathbb C)\right\}}^{\mathrm{Zar}}\subseteq\mathbb P^N_{\mathbb C}. \] Here complex symmetric matrices are algebraic parameters; no positivity condition is imposed. Define the secant variety using spans of $k$ points: \[ \operatorname{Sec}_k(G_{n,d})=\overline{\bigcup_{x_1,\ldots,x_k\in G_{n,d}}\langle x_1,\ldots,x_k\rangle}^{\mathrm{Zar}}, \] with its reduced structure, so that $\operatorname{Sec}_1(G_{n,d})=G_{n,d}$.

Conditional problem. For every $k,n,d\in\mathbb Z_{\geq1}$, determine \[ \dim\operatorname{Sec}_k(G_{n,d}),\qquad \deg\operatorname{Sec}_k(G_{n,d}), \] and give generators for its homogeneous vanishing ideal \[ I\left(\operatorname{Sec}_k(G_{n,d})\right)\subseteq\mathbb C[m_\alpha:\alpha\in A_{n,d}]. \] Dimension and degree are projective dimension and degree in the displayed embedding. No restriction on the relative sizes of $k,n,d$ is supplied.

This conditional formulation uses moments of all total degrees at most $d$, full covariance parameters, and $k$-point secants. The supplied record does not establish these choices or whether defining equations are intended as ideal generators or merely set-theoretic equations. The homoscedastic reference does not itself establish a common-covariance restriction on the target problem. These qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

For every positive number of components $k$, number of variables $n$, and moment cutoff $d$, determine the dimension, degree, and defining equations of the corresponding Gaussian moment secant variety. A precise formulation is given conditionally using complex projective closures, unrestricted covariance matrices, and spans of $k$ points; the source record does not specify these conventions. Its partially solved label is supplied curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
