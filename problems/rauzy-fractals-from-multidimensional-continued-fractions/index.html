Rauzy Fractals from Multidimensional Continued Fractions

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a construction and conditional geometric properties, but does not specify enough data to formulate a unique quantified conjecture.

Let $b$ denote a Brun expansion in the sense intended by the source. The requested construction is an association \[ b\longmapsto\mathcal R(b), \] where $\mathcal R(b)$ is a Rauzy fractal with associated subtiles $\mathcal R_i(b)$, indexed by a set $I$. The dimension, version of the Brun algorithm, admissible expansions, construction of the fractal, indexing of subtiles, and ambient space are not specified in the supplied record. This notation records the requested objects; it does not supply their missing definitions or assert their existence.

Once an ambient measured space $(E,\mu)$ and measurable subtiles have been specified, disjointness in measure means \[ \mu\left(\mathcal R_i(b)\cap\mathcal R_j(b)\right)=0 \qquad(i,j\in I,\ i\ne j). \] The problem asks whether this property follows under the stated assumption of "almost-everywhere exponential convergence to that fractal." The record does not identify the converging objects, their metric, the parameter space and measure underlying "almost everywhere," or the dependence of exponential rates and constants on parameters. Consequently, that assumption cannot be made into a precise inequality from the supplied evidence.

The further task is to determine when the associated subtiles form a tiling. If the intended tiles are translates in a real vector space $E$, one would need translation sets $\Lambda_i\subseteq E$ and the indexed family \[ T_{i,t}=\mathcal R_i(b)+t, \qquad i\in I,\quad t\in\Lambda_i. \] For such a specified measurable family, coverage and disjointness up to measure zero would be expressed by \[ \mu\left(E\setminus\bigcup_{i\in I}\bigcup_{t\in\Lambda_i}T_{i,t}\right)=0, \qquad \mu(T_{i,t}\cap T_{j,s})=0 \quad\text{for }(i,t)\ne(j,s). \] These formulas only explain one possible measure-theoretic meaning of tiling. The source does not specify translations, a lattice, the space to be tiled, or whether exact coverage, local finiteness, or additional boundary conditions are required. Exact coverage would instead require the displayed union to equal $E$. Neither convention is selected here.

Thus the source-level task is to construct a Rauzy fractal from a Brun expansion, investigate measure-disjointness of its subtiles under the stated convergence assumption, and characterize its tiling properties. The missing definitions, hypotheses, and quantifiers require source clarification before this becomes a fully precise mathematical assertion.

Short English statement

Associate a Rauzy fractal and subtiles to a Brun expansion, then investigate whether the supplied almost-everywhere exponential convergence assumption implies measure-disjointness and when a tiling results. The algorithm, convergence hypothesis, measures, and tiling convention remain unspecified, so this is a qualified statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
