Uniqueness and Fundamental Solutions for Fractional Diffusion

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a complete, connected, noncompact Riemannian manifold of dimension $N$, with metric $g$ and Riemannian volume measure $\mu$. Write $\mu(\Omega)$ for the quantity denoted by $m(\Omega)$ in the source, distinguishing volume from the diffusion exponent $m$. The supplied geometric hypotheses are \[ \operatorname{Ric}_M\geq -(N-1)k\,g, \qquad \lambda_1(\Omega)\geq c\,\mu(\Omega)^{-2/N} \] for every nonempty relatively compact open set $\Omega\subset M$. Under the usual Dirichlet interpretation, \[ \lambda_1(\Omega)=\inf_{0\ne\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega)} \frac{\int_\Omega |\nabla\varphi|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu} {\int_\Omega |\varphi|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu}. \] The record does not state the permitted ranges of $k$ and $c$, whether a boundary is excluded, or any boundary conditions; these require confirmation.

For $s\in(0,1)$ and $m>1$, consider the evolution equation \[ \partial_t u=-(-\Delta_M)^s(u^m) \qquad\text{on }(0,\infty)\times M. \] Here $\Delta_M$ denotes the Laplace--Beltrami operator. The realization and definition of its fractional power are not supplied. The record also does not specify the admissible initial data, the sign convention for $u^m$ when $u<0$, or the definition of a weak dual solution.

Requested assertions, subject to these unresolved specifications.

  1. Establish uniqueness of weak dual solutions: two solutions in the intended admissible class with the same initial datum must agree. The class, initial trace, and sense of agreement must be specified before this becomes a fully quantified assertion.
  2. For each pole $y\in M$, construct a fundamental solution with initial datum $\delta_y$, the unit Dirac measure at $y$. The distributional meaning of this initial datum is \[ \lim_{t\downarrow0}\int_M U_y(t,x)\varphi(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(x)=\varphi(y) \qquad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty(M). \] The solution class, additional trace or integrability requirements, and any mass conservation requirement are unspecified.
  3. Establish pointwise bounds for general solutions in the intended class. No definite bound, dependence of constants, spatial regime, or meaning of pointwise evaluation is provided, so this target cannot be made into a specific inequality from the supplied record alone.
The original excerpt also mentions existence of a weak dual solution for data in a space apparently written $L^1_{G^s}(M)$, and smoothing estimates controlling $\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty(M)}$ in terms of a norm of the initial datum. That space is undefined in the record. Its example estimate is corrupted: it contains $\vartheta_1$, powers involving $2s\vartheta_1$ and $N\vartheta_1$, a maximum symbol, and the quantifier $t>0$, but their complete arrangement and parameter definitions cannot be recovered reliably. The excerpt does not clearly distinguish whether these additional bullets describe established results or further requested assertions.

Short English statement

For fractional porous medium diffusion on a complete noncompact manifold satisfying the supplied curvature and eigenvalue inequalities, the requested goals are uniqueness of weak dual solutions, existence of solutions starting from a Dirac mass, and pointwise estimates for general solutions. Essential solution definitions, data classes, operator conventions, and the desired estimates are missing. The supplied open status and verification labels are curation claims; this statement-only draft requires human mathematical review.
