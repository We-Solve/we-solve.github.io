Weak-Order Growth of Grothendieck Schubert Supports

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$, and let $S_n$ be the symmetric group on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. For $1\leq k<n$, let $s_k=(k\;k+1)$. For $v\in S_n$, define its Coxeter length by \[ \ell(v)=\#\{(i,j):1\leq i<j\leq n,\ v(i)>v(j)\}. \] The left weak order $\leq_L$ is the transitive closure of the relations $v<_L s_kv$ with $\ell(s_kv)=\ell(v)+1$. Thus left multiplication here interchanges the values $k$ and $k+1$.

Under the conventional interpretation using ordinary single Grothendieck polynomials, let $G_w$ denote the Grothendieck polynomial associated with $w$, and let $\mathfrak S_v$ denote the Schubert polynomial associated with $v$. Write its Schubert expansion as \[ G_w=\sum_{v\in S_n}a_{w,v}\mathfrak S_v, \qquad \operatorname{Supp}_S(G_w)=\{v\in S_n:a_{w,v}\neq0\}. \] Here $\deg(G_w)$ means total polynomial degree.

Conjecture 2, as supplied, under this finite-group interpretation. For every integer $n\geq1$, every $w\in S_n$, and every $u\in\operatorname{Supp}_S(G_w)$, \[ \ell(u)<\deg(G_w) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists k\in\{1,\ldots,n-1\}\quad \left(u<_L s_ku\ \text{ and }\ s_ku\in\operatorname{Supp}_S(G_w)\right). \] Equivalently, the required step satisfies $\ell(s_ku)=\ell(u)+1$. When $n=1$, the displayed index set is empty and the quantified implication retains its usual logical meaning.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every permutation in the Schubert support of $G_w$ whose length is below $\deg(G_w)$ admits a length-increasing left multiplication by an adjacent transposition that remains in the support. The finite-group formulation uses conventional polynomial and support definitions that require confirmation from the full report. The supplied status is partially solved; its dated assessment says that structured families are known while the arbitrary-permutation case remains unresolved.
