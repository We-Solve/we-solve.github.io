Algebraic and Geometric Overtwistedness

Definitions and mathematical statement

A contact structure on a smooth manifold $M$ of dimension $2n+1$ is a hyperplane distribution $\xi\subset TM$ locally expressible as $\ker\alpha$, where $\alpha\wedge(\mathrm d\alpha)^n$ is nowhere zero. This local definition does not impose coorientability. The supplied record does not specify the allowed dimensions, compactness, boundary conditions, or coorientability assumptions.

The supplied question asks whether algebraic overtwistedness and overtwistedness are equivalent for contact manifolds, emphasizing the implication from algebraic overtwistedness to overtwistedness. However, neither property is defined in the supplied record. In particular, no algebraic invariant, coefficient system, or criterion for algebraic overtwistedness is specified, and the intended geometric definition of overtwistedness is not stated.

To display the logical target without selecting missing definitions, let $\mathcal C$ denote the intended, unspecified class of contact manifolds, and let $A(M,\xi)$ and $O(M,\xi)$ be placeholders for the intended algebraic and geometric overtwistedness properties. The question has the formal shape \[ \forall (M,\xi)\in\mathcal C,\qquad A(M,\xi)\Longleftrightarrow O(M,\xi), \] with particular emphasis on \[ \forall (M,\xi)\in\mathcal C,\qquad A(M,\xi)\Longrightarrow O(M,\xi). \] These are formulation templates, not fully specified conjectures: $\mathcal C$, $A$, and $O$ require clarification from the source. The supplied literature assessment instead describes a truncated conditional conjecture about contact surgery, but no surgery construction, condition, or conclusion appears in the supplied statement. That discrepancy remains unresolved.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether algebraic overtwistedness is equivalent to geometric overtwistedness, especially whether the algebraic property forces the geometric one. The intended definitions and class of contact manifolds are missing, and the supplied contact-surgery assessment conflicts with the displayed question. The record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that label.
