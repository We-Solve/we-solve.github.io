Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
For a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$, $L^2$ norms use the metric volume and pointwise metric on forms. Reduced $L^2$ cohomology is $\ker d/\overline{\operatorname{im}d}$ for the specified closed $L^2$ complex. On a complete manifold its harmonic representatives satisfy $d\alpha=d^*\alpha=0$ and $\int_M|\alpha|^2<\infty$. For a triangulated universal cover, square-summable chains have coefficients with $\sum|a_i|^2<\infty$, together with the domain condition needed for their boundary operator. Write $D=d+d^*$. In the source’s nonparabolic-at-infinity setting, a compact core $K\subset\widetilde K$ gives the norm $N_{\widetilde K}(\alpha)=\|\alpha\|_{L^2(\widetilde K)}+\|D\alpha\|_{L^2(M\setminus\widetilde K)}$; $W$ is the completion of compactly supported smooth forms in that norm.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Define $L^2$ homology in a manner compatible with the square-summable-chain motivation; determine its dependence on the metric and when it is dual to $L^2$ cohomology. Determine the relation with group cohomology, for which the source refers to Lück. In the specified nonparabolic-at-infinity setting, describe $W$ alternatively; realize reduced $L^2$ cohomology as the cohomology of an appropriate complex or complexes; and put $W$ into a complex where possible. Determine when a Mayer--Vietoris sequence exists for reduced $L^2$ cohomology. The source records that nonparabolicity alone is insufficient, and asks whether additional closed-range control of $d$ on noncompact overlaps is sufficient.
Scope and source qualification.
The first sentence of the excerpt is Melrose’s answer to the prior Sobolev question; it is not substituted for this record’s actual L² questions. Closed range on overlaps remains a proposed sufficient condition rather than an established theorem. The catalogue title/status was paired with a machine-generated research attempt; that attempt is not peer-reviewed evidence of settlement. Short English statement

Define metric-sensitive L² homology, clarify the Fredholm completion and find conditions for reduced L² excision and Mayer–Vietoris sequences.
