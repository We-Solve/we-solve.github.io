Math-3611

The Optimal Constant in One-Dimensional Sequence Discrepancy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $I=[0,1)\subset{\mathbb{R}}$, and let $x=(x_n)_{n\geq1}\in I^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ be an arbitrary infinite sequence; no distinctness or equidistribution assumption is imposed. The supplied record uses $D_N$ without defining its discrepancy convention or normalization. For example, two possible normalized conventions are star discrepancy \[ D_N^*(x)=\sup_{0\leq t\leq1}\left|\frac1N\#\{1\leq n\leq N:x_n<t\}-t\right| \] and interval discrepancy \[ D_N^{\mathrm{int}}(x)=\sup_{0\leq a<b\leq1}\left|\frac1N\#\{1\leq n\leq N:a\leq x_n<b\}-(b-a)\right|. \] These are illustrative alternatives; the supplied record does not identify either as the intended $D_N$.

Conditional on fixing the intended discrepancy functional $D_N(x)$ and logarithm convention, define \[ c^*=\sup\left\{c\in{\mathbb{R}}:\ \forall x\in I^{{\mathbb{N}}}\ \forall M\in{\mathbb{N}}\ \exists N\in{\mathbb{N}},\quad N\geq\max\{M,2\}\ \text{and}\ D_N(x)\geq c\frac{\log N}{N}\right\}. \] The problem is to determine $c^*$. Thus the constant is universal over sequences, while the infinitely many indices may depend on the sequence and on $c$. The logarithm base is also unspecified; its choice affects the numerical constant. Consequently, the supplied formulation does not uniquely specify a numerical target until these conventions are resolved.

The supplied background additionally describes the report's request as improving a known lower estimate. It supplies neither that estimate nor its numerical value; this narrower description should be checked against the stated request to determine the optimal constant.

Short English statement

Determine the largest universal discrepancy constant, expressed as a supremum, for which every sequence in $[0,1)$ satisfies the stated lower bound infinitely often. The discrepancy definition, normalization, and logarithm base require clarification before a numerical target is fixed. The supplied open label is a dated curation claim.
