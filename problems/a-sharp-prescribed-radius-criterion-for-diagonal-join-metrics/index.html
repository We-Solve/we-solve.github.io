Math-1741

A sharp prescribed-radius criterion for diagonal join metrics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A smooth manifold has cohomogeneity one for a specified compact Lie group action if its orbit space is one-dimensional. The source asks to study existence and nonexistence of metrics with nonnegative sectional curvature on such manifolds. One must distinguish an arbitrary metric from a metric invariant under the given action: averaging a metric does not preserve sectional curvature. The supplied aid treats a fixed invariant diagonal family, not the unrestricted existence problem.

Let $p,q\ge1$, and take the effective standard join action of $SO(p+1)\times SO(q+1)$ on $S^{p+q+1}\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{p+1}\oplus{\mathbb{R}}^{q+1}$. Its regular orbits are $S^p\times S^q$, and its singular orbits are $S^p$ and $S^q$. Fix positive radii $A,B$ for their unit-round metric multiples and a desired orbit distance $L>0$. Consider \[ g={\,\mathrm{d}} t^2+f(t)^2g_{S^p(1)}+h(t)^2g_{S^q(1)},\qquad 0<t<L, \] with $f,h>0$ in the interior and smooth collapse conditions \[ f(0)=A,\quad f'(0)=0,\quad h(0)=0,\quad h'(0)=1, \qquad f(L)=0,\quad f'(L)=-1,\quad h(L)=B,\quad h'(L)=0, \] including the corresponding even/odd expansions at each singular orbit, not merely these first-order conditions.

The aid reports the exact feasibility criterion in this class: \[ \exists\text{ such a smooth invariant }g\text{ with }\sec_g\ge0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad L>\max\{A,B\}. \] The strictness is essential. Radial nonnegativity makes $f,h$ concave, with $-1\le f'\le0$, $0\le h'\le1$; the endpoint slope changes force $A<L$ and $B<L$. Sufficiency is reported by a smooth prescribed-mean construction with exact product collars, for instance constant $f=A$ and $h=t$ near zero, and $f=L-t$, $h=B$ near $L$.

The full curvature check in the diagonal class uses the radial values \[ -\frac{f''}{f},\qquad-\frac{h''}{h}, \] the mixed value $-f'h'/(fh)$, and the factor-plane values \[ \frac{1-(f')^2}{f^2},\qquad\frac{1-(h')^2}{h^2}, \] with a factor-plane block absent when its sphere is one-dimensional. The aid says all blocks and smooth endpoint extensions are checked. The criterion depends on the unit-round factor normalization, specified singular radii and diagonal ansatz; it is not asserted for all invariant metrics on this diagram, all group diagrams or noninvariant metrics. The original broad program remains only partially addressed.

Short English statement

Study nonnegative curvature on cohomogeneity-one manifolds with invariant and arbitrary metrics separated. The supplied worked family gives a sharp strict distance-versus-radius criterion for diagonal metrics on the standard sphere join, rather than a general classification.
