Optimal Systolic Ratio of the Two-Sphere

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^2=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^3:\|x\|=1\}$. Under the conventional interpretation using smooth Riemannian metrics, let $\mathcal G$ be the set of all smooth Riemannian metrics on $S^2$. For $g\in\mathcal G$, define \[ \operatorname{sys}(S^2,g)=\inf\{L_g(\gamma):\gamma\text{ is a nonconstant closed }g\text{-geodesic}\}, \qquad \rho(S^2,g)=\frac{\operatorname{sys}(S^2,g)^2}{\operatorname{Area}_g(S^2)}. \] Here $L_g(\gamma)$ denotes geodesic length and $\operatorname{Area}_g(S^2)$ denotes total Riemannian area. The systole in this formulation concerns closed geodesics without a noncontractibility requirement, since every loop on $S^2$ is contractible.

With these conventions, the question is whether \[ \sup_{g\in\mathcal G}\rho(S^2,g)=2\sqrt{3}. \] The supplied record does not explicitly define $\rho$, specify metric regularity, or state whether singular metrics are admissible. Thus the displayed formulation is conditional on the stated conventions and requires confirmation against the cited source. The background also mentions contact forms and local optimality of Zoll forms; it supplies no contact-geometric definitions or normalization, so that broader discussion does not specify an additional precise problem here.

Short English statement

Using squared shortest closed-geodesic length divided by total area as the systolic ratio, is its supremum over all smooth Riemannian metrics on the two-sphere equal to $2\sqrt{3}$? The ratio convention and admissible metric class require source confirmation.
