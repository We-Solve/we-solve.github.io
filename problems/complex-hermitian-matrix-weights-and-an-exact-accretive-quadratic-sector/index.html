Math-1657

Complex Hermitian matrix weights and an exact accretive quadratic sector

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks to extend the Guionnet--Maurel-Segala model \[ \exp\!\left[-n\operatorname{Tr}\left(P(A_1,\ldots,A_m)+\tfrac12\sum_iA_i^2\right)\right] \,{\,\mathrm{d}} A_1\cdots{\,\mathrm{d}} A_m \] to non-self-adjoint $P$, described in the source as polynomials with complex coefficients. The aid retains the original Hermitian integration cycle $A_i\in\operatorname{Herm}_n$; it does not replace it by a new complex contour. Formal self-adjointness, complex coefficients, and reality of a traced polynomial are distinct issues.

Let $V=P+\tfrac12\sum_iA_i^2$ and $V_H=(V+V^*)/2$ on that cycle. The unnormalized complex measure has total-variation density exactly $e^{-n\operatorname{Tr}V_H}$. Thus finite total variation is equivalent to integrability of the real-part Gibbs weight. Even then its complex partition function can vanish, preventing division by $Z_n$; a normalized complex expectation need not be positive.

The aid solves a quadratic sector. For a complex symmetric $m\times m$ matrix $B$, vectors $h\in{\mathbb{C}}^m$, and $c\in{\mathbb{C}}$, define \[ Z_n(B,h,c)=\int_{\operatorname{Herm}_n^m} e^{-n\operatorname{Tr}[\frac12\sum_{i,j}B_{ij}A_iA_j-\sum_ih_iA_i+cI]} \,{\,\mathrm{d}} A. \] It reports finite total variation if and only if $\operatorname{Re}B>0$, with real part understood as the real symmetric quadratic form on the color variables. In this accretive sector, \[ \frac{Z_n(B,h,c)}{Z_n(I,0,0)}= \exp\left[-\frac{n^2}{2}\operatorname{Logdet}B-n^2c +\frac{n^2}{2}h^TB^{-1}h\right]\ne0. \] The transpose has no complex conjugation. Here $\operatorname{Logdet}$ is the holomorphic logarithmic determinant continued from $B=I$ within the accretive symmetric sector, not an arbitrary scalar-log branch of $\det B$.

Writing $a=B^{-1}h$ and $C=B^{-1}$, the aid reports Gaussian Wick formulas at finite $n$ and the planar centered moments \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\langle\operatorname{Tr} (A_{i_1}-a_{i_1}I)\cdots(A_{i_k}-a_{i_k}I)\rangle =\sum_{\pi\in\mathrm{NC}_2(k)}\prod_{\{r,s\}\in\pi}C_{i_ri_s}. \] Here $\mathrm{NC}_2(k)$ denotes noncrossing pairings; the sum is zero for odd $k$. Brackets are normalized complex integration, not necessarily a probability expectation or positive noncommutative state.

For $P=i\lambda A_1A_2$, $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$, the relevant block is $B=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&i\lambda\\i\lambda&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, with $h=c=0$. The reported exact ratio is $(1+\lambda^2)^{-n^2/2}$, and the limiting mixed second moment is $-i\lambda/(1+\lambda^2)$. This works at every real coupling, but remains a quadratic special case rather than an extension theorem for arbitrary non-self-adjoint polynomial potentials.

Short English statement

On the original Hermitian contour, complex weights are integrable exactly when their real-part weight is integrable, but normalization can still fail at a partition-function zero. The aid gives a zero-free accretive quadratic sector with explicit partition functions and complex Wick limits, not a general nonlinear complex-potential theory.
