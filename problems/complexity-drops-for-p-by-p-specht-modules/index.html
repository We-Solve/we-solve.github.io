Math-4698

Complexity Drops for p-by-p Specht Modules

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The following formulates the question using the usual modular representation conventions. These conventions, including the meaning of a $p\times p$ partition and the ground field, are not explicitly defined in the supplied record and require confirmation against the report.

Let $p$ be a prime, let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$, and let $n\geq 1$. Write a partition of $n$ as \[ \lambda=(a_1^{m_1},\ldots,a_t^{m_t}),\qquad a_1>\cdots>a_t>0, \] where $m_j$ is the multiplicity of the part $a_j$. Under the standard convention used here, $\lambda$ is a $p\times p$ partition if \[ p\mid a_j\quad\text{and}\quad p\mid m_j\qquad(1\leq j\leq t). \] Thus its Young diagram consists of $p\times p$ squares arranged according to a partition; the term does not mean only the single square partition $(p^p)$.

Let $\operatorname{core}_p(\lambda)$ be the $p$-core obtained by successively removing rim hooks of length $p$. The block weight of $\lambda$ is \[ w=\frac{n-\lvert\operatorname{core}_p(\lambda)\rvert}{p}. \] Let $S_k^\lambda$ denote the Specht module for the symmetric group $\mathfrak S_n$, constructed over $k$ from the partition $\lambda$. No $p$-regularity assumption on $\lambda$ is imposed.

For a finite-dimensional $k\mathfrak S_n$-module $M$, let \[ \cdots\longrightarrow P_2\longrightarrow P_1\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow0 \] be a minimal projective resolution. Its complexity $c_{k\mathfrak S_n}(M)$ is zero when $M$ is projective; otherwise it is the least positive integer $c$ for which some constant $C>0$ satisfies \[ \dim_k P_r\leq C r^{c-1} \qquad\text{for all sufficiently large integers }r. \]

Question under these conventions. For every prime $p$, every such field $k$, and every nonempty $p\times p$ partition $\lambda$ of positive block weight $w$, must \[ c_{k\mathfrak S_n}(S_k^\lambda)=w-1 \] hold, or do there exist such partitions for which \[ c_{k\mathfrak S_n}(S_k^\lambda)<w-1? \] Describe these cases further, in particular by characterizing any partitions exhibiting a strict drop and determining their complexities.

The nonempty, positive-weight scope is an explicit interpretation: the supplied question does not discuss the empty partition or weight zero. The source asks about equality versus a smaller value; this draft does not independently establish the upper bound implicit in that framing. The requested description of cases is not given a more specific classification target in the record.

Short English statement

For a $p\times p$ partition $\lambda$ of block weight $w$, ask whether its Specht module always has complexity $w-1$, or whether the complexity can be smaller. Describe any such cases more precisely. The modular setting and partition convention stated above require source confirmation; this is a statement-only draft.
