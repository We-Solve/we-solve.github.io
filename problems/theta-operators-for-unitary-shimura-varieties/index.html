Theta Operators for Unitary Shimura Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ denote a prime. A unitary Shimura variety means a Shimura variety attached to a Shimura datum whose group is of unitary type. To discuss its modular forms in characteristic $p$, one must specify an integral model, a characteristic-$p$ fiber $S$, and the relevant automorphic bundles. For a specified automorphic bundle $\mathcal E$ on $S$, its global sections $H^0(S,\mathcal E)$ are the corresponding automorphic forms; any conditions at a compactification boundary must also be specified.

The classical structures named in the source are the Hasse invariant, the theta derivation, and theta cycles. In the classical modular-curve setting, the Hasse invariant is a modular form of weight $p-1$ whose nonvanishing detects the ordinary locus. The classical theta operator is characterized on Fourier expansions by \[ \theta\!\left(\sum_{n\geq 0}a_nq^n\right) =q\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} q}\left(\sum_{n\geq 0}a_nq^n\right) =\sum_{n\geq 0}n a_nq^n. \] Theta cycles describe the behavior of successive theta iterates, including their weights or filtrations. This description does not assert periodicity or a particular cycle length in the unitary setting.

Requested research program. Develop, for unitary Shimura varieties, characteristic-$p$ analogues of the Hasse invariant, the theta derivation, and theta cycles, including the relationships among these structures.

Unresolved scope. The supplied record does not specify the Shimura datum, signature, level, prime hypotheses, integral model, automorphic weights, boundary conditions, or precise properties required of the analogues. It also does not specify whether a Hasse invariant should detect the ordinary locus, the $\mu$-ordinary locus, or another stratum, or whether theta operators should be defined globally or on an open locus. Consequently, the source specifies a research program rather than a uniquely quantified existence theorem. No assertion of existence for every unitary Shimura variety or every prime, and no particular weight shift or cycle law, is imposed by this draft.

Short English statement

Develop characteristic-$p$ versions of Hasse invariants, theta operators, and theta cycles for unitary Shimura varieties. The source leaves the geometric setting and required identities unspecified, so a precise theorem requires further hypotheses. Its supplied partially_solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
