Math-4768

Cocycle Superrigidity for Bernoulli Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Gamma$ be a countable discrete group, and let $\beta^{(2)}_1(\Gamma)$ denote its first $\ell^2$-Betti number, defined as the von Neumann dimension over the group von Neumann algebra $L\Gamma$ of its reduced first $\ell^2$-homology.

For a standard probability space $(K,\kappa)$, the associated Bernoulli action is the probability-measure-preserving action $\Gamma\curvearrowright(X,\mu)=(K^\Gamma,\kappa^\Gamma)$ given by \[ (g\cdot x)(h)=x(g^{-1}h)\qquad(g,h\in\Gamma). \] The source does not specify the base probability space, whether it must be nontrivial, or whether the question quantifies over all such bases. Accordingly, the formulation below retains the base as a parameter; it does not resolve this missing quantifier.

Under the standard interpretation, $\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}$ is the class of Polish groups isomorphic, as topological groups, to closed subgroups of $\mathcal U(M)$, where $M$ is a finite von Neumann algebra with separable predual and a faithful normal tracial state $\tau$. The unitary group carries the topology induced by $\|u-v\|_{2,\tau}=\tau((u-v)^*(u-v))^{1/2}$.

For a probability-measure-preserving action $\Gamma\curvearrowright(X,\mu)$ and $L\in\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}$, a measurable cocycle is a measurable map $c:\Gamma\times X\to L$ satisfying \[ c(gh,x)=c(g,h\cdot x)c(h,x) \] for every $g,h\in\Gamma$ and $\mu$-almost every $x$. The action is $\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}$-cocycle superrigid if, for every such $L$ and $c$, there exist a group homomorphism $\rho:\Gamma\to L$ and a measurable map $b:X\to L$ such that \[ c(g,x)=b(g\cdot x)\rho(g)b(x)^{-1} \] for every $g\in\Gamma$ and $\mu$-almost every $x$.

Source question, with the base qualification unresolved. Does \[ \beta^{(2)}_1(\Gamma)=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Gamma\curvearrowright(K^\Gamma,\kappa^\Gamma) \text{ is }\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}\text{-cocycle superrigid} \] hold for countable groups $\Gamma$, with the intended choice or quantification of $(K,\kappa)$ to be clarified? The supplied wording imposes no infinitude, nonamenability, or other additional group hypothesis; none is silently added here. Whether an additional restriction was intended requires source review.

The source singles out $\Gamma=\mathbb Z\wr F_2$. Here $F_2$ is the free group on two generators, and the usual restricted wreath product is \[ \mathbb Z\wr F_2 =\left(\bigoplus_{h\in F_2}\mathbb Z\right)\rtimes F_2, \qquad (g\cdot f)(h)=f(g^{-1}h). \] Thus the singled-out question asks whether this group's Bernoulli action has the cocycle-superrigidity property defined above, subject to the same unresolved base qualification. The restricted-wreath-product and target-class definitions are standard interpretations of notation that the supplied record does not itself define.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether vanishing first $\ell^2$-Betti number forces every measurable cocycle of a Bernoulli action into a group in $\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{fin}}$ to be cohomologous to a homomorphism, especially for $\mathbb Z\wr F_2$. The intended Bernoulli bases and any omitted group restrictions remain unresolved. The record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
