Math-3622

Unramified Pro-$p$ Galois Groups over Infinite Extensions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Structure problem. Let $p$ be a prime, $F$ a number field, and $L/F$ a pro-$p$ Galois extension unramified outside a finite set $S$ containing the primes above $p$. Determine the Galois group $G_L$ of the maximal unramified pro-$p$ extension of $L$, and its maximal abelian quotient $X_L$. A pro-$p$ group is a profinite inverse limit of finite $p$-groups, so the hypothesis permits finite as well as infinite $L/F$; the title's reference to infinite extensions does not add an explicit infinitude assumption.

Fixing a compatible separable closure and the intended notion of unramifiedness, write $L^{\mathrm{ur},p}$ for the compositum of the unramified finite Galois $p$-extensions of $L$. Then\[G_L=\operatorname{Gal}(L^{\mathrm{ur},p}/L),\qquad X_L=G_L/\overline{[G_L,G_L]}.\] The closure of the commutator subgroup is essential: this is the maximal continuous abelian pro-$p$ quotient. Determining $X_L$ alone does not determine the full nonabelian $G_L$. The condition on ramification outside $S$ applies to the given extension $L/F$, while $L^{\mathrm{ur},p}/L$ is requested to be unramified in the chosen sense; these conditions are not interchanged.

The source does not specify whether archimedean ramification is allowed, a distinction particularly relevant when $p=2$, or the valuation-theoretic convention over an infinite algebraic base $L$. It also does not fix a family of towers, provide arithmetic data determining one example, or specify the desired form of a general answer, such as presentations, finiteness criteria, or invariants. Thus this is a structural research program over the stated class, not one numerical group computation. No ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-extension, cyclotomic, or finite-generation hypothesis is added.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the problem partially solved. It says that for finite base fields, class field theory identifies the maximal abelian quotient with the $p$-part of the class group, while nonabelian groups are known in special families only. In the number-field context this must be checked as a statement about finite-degree number-field bases, not silently read as a statement about finite fields of positive characteristic. For finite number-field $L$, ordinary versus narrow class groups also depends on the infinite-place convention. The triage supplies no precise infinite-base theorem or special-family hypotheses. Its dated general-status claim requires independent verification.

Short English statement

Describe the maximal unramified pro-$p$ Galois group over the given possibly infinite number-field extension and its continuous abelianization. Ramification conventions, especially at infinity and over an infinite base, matter. The class-field-theory abelian case does not supply the nonabelian structure.
