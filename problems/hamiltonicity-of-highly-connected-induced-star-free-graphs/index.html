Math-4091

Hamiltonicity of Highly Connected Induced-Star-Free Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a conventional interpretation, let graphs be finite, simple, and undirected, and let the parameter range over integers $k\geq 3$. These conventions are not explicitly specified in the supplied statement and require confirmation. For an integer $t\geq 1$, a graph $G$ is $t$-vertex-connected if $|V(G)|\geq t+1$ and deleting any set of fewer than $t$ vertices leaves a connected graph. The star $K_{1,k}$ has one centre adjacent to $k$ leaves and no edges between distinct leaves. A graph is induced-$K_{1,k}$-free if no subset of its vertices induces a graph isomorphic to $K_{1,k}$. A Hamiltonian cycle is a cycle of length at least three containing every vertex of the graph; a graph is Hamiltonian if it contains such a cycle.

Under these conventions, the question attributed to Alexey Pokrovskiy in the supplied report passage, labelled Conjecture 4, is whether \[ \exists f:\{3,4,5,\ldots\}\longrightarrow\mathbb Z_{\geq 1}\quad \forall k\geq 3\quad\forall G,\qquad \left(G\text{ is }f(k)\text{-vertex-connected and induced-}K_{1,k}\text{-free}\right) \Longrightarrow G\text{ is Hamiltonian}. \] The source does not explicitly give the domain or codomain of $f$, or the graph conventions; the displayed formulation is therefore a provisional conventional interpretation rather than a resolution of these omissions.

For context, a graph is claw-free if it is induced-$K_{1,3}$-free. The supplied passage states the Matthews--Sumner conjecture as: every $4$-vertex-connected claw-free graph is Hamiltonian. Its wording that this asks whether $f(3)\leq 4$ is understood as asking whether an admissible connectivity threshold at $k=3$ can be chosen at most $4$; no minimality of the function $f$ is specified. The passage further attributes to Ryjáček the assertion that an admissible threshold at $k=3$ exists and is at most $7$. These are retained as supplied contextual claims, without independent verification.

Short English statement

The question asks whether, for each intended star size $k$, sufficiently high vertex connectivity forces every graph with no induced $K_{1,k}$ to have a cycle through all its vertices. The displayed interpretation uses finite simple undirected graphs and $k\geq 3$, conventions requiring confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
