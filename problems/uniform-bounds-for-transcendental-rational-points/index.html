Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied cleaned formulation concerns a set $X\subseteq\mathbb{C}_p^n$ definable in a Hensel-minimal structure, where $\mathbb{C}_p$ conventionally denotes the completion of an algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}_p$. The record does not specify the prime $p$, the dimension restrictions on $n$, the structure, the permitted parameters, or the precise Hensel-minimality hypothesis.

The notation $X^{\mathrm{trans}}(\mathbb{Q},H)$ is not defined in the supplied excerpt. To display the requested quantifiers without inventing those definitions, let $T_X\subseteq X$ denote the intended transcendental part and let $h$ denote the intended height on rational points, both still to be specified from the source. Write, schematically, \[ N_{X,T_X,h}(H)=\#\{x\in T_X\cap\mathbb{Q}^n:h(x)\leq H\},\qquad H\in\mathbb{R},\ H\geq1, \] using the usual inclusion $\mathbb{Q}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{C}_p$ in each coordinate. This notation is a placeholder for the missing source definitions, not a choice of transcendental part or height.

Question as supplied, with unresolved qualifications. Can the number $\#X^{\mathrm{trans}}(\mathbb{Q},H)$ be bounded uniformly for all $H\geq1$, with upper bounds as in the source's Theorem 2? On the literal interpretation of uniform boundedness in $H$, the schematic assertion for each admissible $X$ is \[ \exists B<\infty\quad\forall H\in\mathbb{R}_{\geq1},\qquad N_{X,T_X,h}(H)\leq B. \] Here independence from $H$ is the only uniformity explicitly requested; the allowed dependence of $B$ on $X$, $p$, the structure, parameters, or complexity data is unspecified. The source's Theorem 2 and reference [15], said to treat the one-dimensional case, are absent from the supplied record. Consequently, this schematic assertion cannot be identified confidently with the full intended theorem-level question: neither the required form of the bounds nor their dependence on auxiliary data is available. The supplied cleaned statement paraphrases the requested comparison as bounds analogous to those known in dimension one. A complete precise formulation requires the omitted definitions and theorem.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether the transcendental rational points of a Hensel-minimally definable set admit a bound uniform in the height cutoff $H\geq1$, with the quantitative features of an omitted Theorem 2. The transcendental part, height convention, hypotheses, and allowed dependence of the bound remain unspecified.
