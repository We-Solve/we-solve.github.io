Math-2274

Problem 6 — Find special features of the monodromy of algebraic surfaces.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The problem is to identify special restrictions or structural features of monodromy arising from algebraic surfaces. In the usual complex-projective Lefschetz-pencil framework, a suitable pencil on a smooth projective surface $X$, after blowing up its basepoints, gives a fibration over a curve away from finitely many critical values and a monodromy homomorphism \[\rho:\pi_1(B\setminus\{b_1,\ldots,b_r\},b_0)\to\operatorname{Mod}(F),\] with corresponding local vanishing-cycle data. This is a conditional model of the monodromy intended by the source; it does not specify its omitted surface, pencil/fibration, boundary/marking or TLP conventions. The task asks for meaningful algebraic constraints on the resulting data, not a theorem asserting a stated necessary-and-sufficient classification.

The source lists three motivations, all retained:

  1. The programme includes, in principle, the problem of describing possible fundamental groups of algebraic surfaces; any claimed translation must specify how the monodromy and vanishing cycles determine that group.
  2. Hard Lefschetz imposes a constraint with a translation into the action of monodromy on homology. For a smooth compact Kähler surface and Kähler class $\omega$, its familiar surface-degree condition includes the isomorphism $\omega\smile-:H^1(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\to H^3(X;{\mathbb{Q}})$. The specific fiber-homology/monodromy condition intended here is not given and cannot be replaced merely by symplectic preservation.
  3. Seiberg--Witten theory imposes strong restrictions on the basic classes of algebraic surfaces, which the source proposes translating into the TLP viewpoint along [6,13]. Basic-class and chamber/orientation hypotheses and the meaning of TLP must be fixed; no unrestricted formula for basic classes is inserted.
The algebraic-surface category is not specified as smooth/projective/compact in the short problem. The hard-Lefschetz and Seiberg--Witten motivations require their respective hypotheses, so the conditional framework above is not silently extended to every singular or noncompact algebraic surface. The source supplies no desired list of monodromy features or completeness criterion.

Short English statement

Find constraints on algebraic-surface monodromy, motivated by fundamental groups, hard Lefschetz and Seiberg--Witten basic classes. The geometric category, monodromy data and TLP translation are not defined in the excerpt.
