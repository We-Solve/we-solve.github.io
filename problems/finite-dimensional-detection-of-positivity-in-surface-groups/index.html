Finite-Dimensional Detection of Positivity in Surface Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for an equivalence concerning Hermitian elements of a surface-group algebra, but does not specify the coefficient field or the meaning of positivity. The following makes the question precise conditionally on nonnegative operator positivity and a coefficient field $k\in\{{\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}}\}$. The choice of $k$ remains unresolved; this parameterized formulation does not assert that both choices were intended.

Let $S$ be a closed connected surface, meaning a compact connected topological $2$-manifold without boundary, choose $s_0\in S$, and put $G=\pi_1(S,s_0)$. No orientability or genus restriction is supplied. Let $k[G]$ be the group algebra consisting of finite sums $a=\sum_{g\in G}a_g g$, with multiplication induced by the group law and involution \[ \left(\sum_{g\in G}a_g g\right)^*=\sum_{g\in G}\overline{a_g}\,g^{-1}. \] Here conjugation is trivial when $k={\mathbb{R}}$. An element $a$ is Hermitian if $a=a^*$. Define the cone of finite sums of Hermitian squares by \[ \Sigma^2 k[G]=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_j^*b_j:\ m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\ b_j\in k[G]\right\}, \] where the empty sum is zero.

A unitary representation is a group homomorphism $\pi:G\to U(H)$, where $H$ is a complex Hilbert space. Its extension to $k[G]$ is $\pi(a)=\sum_g a_g\pi(g)$. For Hermitian $a$, write $\pi(a)\geq0$ if \[ \langle\pi(a)\xi,\xi\rangle\geq0\qquad\text{for every }\xi\in H. \] For the chosen coefficient field, the supplied question can then be written: for every such surface $S$ and every $a=a^*\in k[\pi_1(S,s_0)]$, are the following three conditions equivalent? \begin{align*} \text{(A)}\quad&\pi(a)\geq0\quad\text{for every unitary representation }\pi:G\to U(H)\text{ on every complex Hilbert space }H;\\ \text{(B)}\quad&\rho(a)\geq0\quad\text{for every integer }d\geq1\text{ and every }\rho:G\to U({\mathbb{C}}^d);\\ \text{(C)}\quad&a\in\Sigma^2 k[G]. \end{align*} Condition $\text{(C)}$ means exact algebraic membership, with no closure or perturbation by $\varepsilon 1$. The source does not clarify whether strict positivity, a closure, or an approximation statement was intended instead. Its word "hence" is retained here as part of the proposed equivalence, not as an established deduction.

The supplied dated curation reports that the finite-dimensional versus arbitrary-unitary-representation implication for surface groups is settled by Lubotzky--Shalom, while the equivalence with sums of Hermitian squares remains unresolved. It also reports a negative answer to a related Connes-embedding formulation for $F_2\times F_2$, where $F_2$ denotes the free group on two generators. That related formulation is not supplied, so no identification with the present statement is made. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

For each closed surface group and each Hermitian group-algebra element, the source asks whether nonnegativity in every unitary representation, nonnegativity in every finite-dimensional unitary representation, and exact expression as a finite sum of Hermitian squares are equivalent. The coefficient field and positivity convention need confirmation. Supplied curation distinguishes a reportedly settled representation-theoretic implication from an unresolved sums-of-squares equivalence.
