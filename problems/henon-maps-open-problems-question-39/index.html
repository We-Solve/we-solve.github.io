Henon Maps: Open Problems — Question 39

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d$ be a positive integer and let $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_d):{\mathbb{C}}^d\to{\mathbb{C}}^d$ be a polynomial automorphism, meaning that both $f$ and its inverse are polynomial maps. Write $f^n$ for its $n$-fold iterate and define $\deg(f)=\max_{1\le i\le d}\deg(f_i)$, using total polynomial degree. Under the usual interpretation of the symbol in the source, the first dynamical degree is \[ \lambda(f)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\deg(f^n)\right)^{1/n}. \] The supplied record does not itself define $\lambda$, so this interpretation requires confirmation against the source.

For a nonnegative integer $k$, the notation $\deg(f^n)\asymp n^k$ means that there exist constants $c,C>0$ and an integer $n_0\ge1$, allowed to depend on $f$, such that \[ c n^k\le\deg(f^n)\le C n^k\qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge n_0. \] With the convention ${\mathbb{N}}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, the first question is whether \[ \forall d\ge1\;\forall f\in\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{poly}}({\mathbb{C}}^d),\qquad \lambda(f)=1\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}\;\left(\deg(f^n)\asymp n^k\right). \] Here $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{poly}}({\mathbb{C}}^d)$ denotes the set of polynomial automorphisms just defined. The source writes $k\in{\mathbb{N}}$ without specifying whether $0$ is included; if it means positive integers, the quantified exponent set must instead be ${\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}$. This convention remains unresolved.

The further question is: whenever $\lambda(f)=1$ and $\deg(f^n)\asymp n^k$ for an integer $k\ge1$, must $f$ preserve a rational fibration? The supplied record does not define this last phrase. A possible interpretation of preservation is the existence of an algebraic variety $B$, a dominant rational map $\pi:{\mathbb{C}}^d\dashrightarrow B$ with $0<\dim B<d$, and a birational self-map $g:B\dashrightarrow B$ satisfying \[ \pi\circ f=g\circ\pi \] as rational maps. This is only a candidate interpretation: the record does not specify the dimension or rationality of $B$, connectedness or rationality conditions on the general fiber, or whether preservation instead requires $\pi\circ f=\pi$. Consequently, the fibration clause cannot be made unambiguous from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

Question 39 asks whether every polynomial automorphism of ${\mathbb{C}}^d$ with dynamical degree one has degree growth comparable to an integer power of the iteration number, and whether positive-power growth forces preservation of a rational fibration. The meanings of $\lambda$, ${\mathbb{N}}$, and rational-fibration preservation require source confirmation. The registry labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
