Definitions and mathematical statement

For every pure type system with arbitrary sorts, axioms and rules, if every legal expression in every valid context has some terminating $\beta$-reduction sequence, must every $\beta$-reduction sequence of each such expression terminate? Global weak normalization implies strong normalization in pure type systems

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 245; 2026-09-25), the asserted result is that weak beta normalization of an arbitrary pure type system implies strong beta normalization across all legal expressions and contexts, including reduction in annotations.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
A pure type system is specified by sorts $\mathcal S$, typing axioms $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal S^2$ and product rules $\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal S^3$, with the usual dependent-product formation and $\beta$ reduction. The manuscript claims, for every such triple, \[ [\text{every legal expression in every valid context has a finite }\beta\text{ reduction to normal form}] \ \Longrightarrow\ [\text{every }\beta\text{ reduction sequence from every such expression is finite}]. \] Thus global weak $\beta$ normalization implies global strong $\beta$ normalization. Neither the axioms nor product rules are assumed functional.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 245; 2026-09-25), a finite counterexample reduces to finitely many sorts and rules. Sets of possible typing sorts replace unique types in nonfunctional systems; substitution-stable observation candidates and typed data channels turn an infinite reduction into an obstruction to any normal form.
