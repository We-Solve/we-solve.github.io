Φ(G) and Φ'(G) Coincidence

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The literal imported question is \[\Phi(G)\stackrel{?}{=}\Phi'(G).\] The supplied statement does not define either side, and therefore this expression alone is not a well-posed mathematical assertion. The supplied literature triage identifies the intended source as Problem 57 of B. Green's 100 Open Problems: there $G$ is an abelian group, $\Phi(G)$ is a convex hull of generalized convolutions on $G$, and $\Phi'(G)$ imposes the condition that one factor depend only on $x_1+x_2$. The exact generalized-convolution formula, admissible factors, underlying function classes, averaging or measure conventions, and convex-hull conventions are not supplied here. Consequently the question can be retained only as the source-defined function-space equality, pending those definitions; no guessed definition of either space is introduced.

The original background's interpretation as equality of Frattini-like subgroups is explicitly identified as erroneous by the supplied review, which also calls the imported algebra category substantively wrong. It is not used as a definition. The review, checked 2026-08-17, links the intended problem to generalized convolution algebras and $\mathrm{UAP}^{2}$ functions, reports that Green conjecturally expects the spaces not to coincide, and reports no resolution. It says the intended source problem remains open, while no status can be assigned to the literal undefined statement. Its PARTIAL-PROGRESS label concerns this source diagnosis, not a theorem about Frattini subgroups.

Short English statement

Ask whether Green's two source-defined spaces of functions on an abelian group are equal. The local record omits their exact definitions and contains an erroneous Frattini-subgroup explanation, so the intended function-space question needs source definitions before a precise formulation is possible.
