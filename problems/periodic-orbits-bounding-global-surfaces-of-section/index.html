Periodic Orbits Bounding Global Surfaces of Section

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed smooth $3$-manifold, where closed means compact and without boundary, and let $\varphi:{\mathbb{R}}\times M\to M$ be a smooth flow. Write $\varphi_t(p)=\varphi(t,p)$ and let $X(p)=\left.\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t}\right|_{t=0}\varphi_t(p)$ be its generating vector field. Interpret a periodic orbit as the image of a nonconstant trajectory through a point $p$ such that $\varphi_T(p)=p$ for some $T>0$. For the supplied finite family $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_N$, put \[ L=\bigcup_{i=1}^{N}\gamma_i. \] For an explicit embedded-surface interpretation, a global surface of section with boundary $L$ is a compact smooth embedded surface $S\subset M$ such that, writing $S^\circ=S\setminus\partial S$, \[ \partial S=L,\qquad X(q)\notin T_qS\quad\text{for every }q\in S^\circ, \] and \[ \text{for every }p\in M\setminus L\text{ there exist }t_-<0<t_+\text{ such that } \varphi_{t_-}(p),\varphi_{t_+}(p)\in S^\circ. \] No connectedness or orientability requirement is imposed in this explicit interpretation. The supplied statement does not specify whether such requirements, or more general notions of surface embedding, are intended; these conventions require mathematical review.

Problem. Given $(M,\varphi_t,\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_N)$ as above, determine when there exists a global surface of section $S$ whose entire boundary is exactly $L$. The clean statement asks for conditions for existence, while the original wording asks whether the given collection bounds such a surface. No additional dynamical hypotheses or particular form of an existence criterion are supplied.

Short English statement

Determine when a specified finite union of periodic orbits of a smooth flow on a closed smooth $3$-manifold is exactly the boundary of a compact surface transverse to the flow in its interior and met by every trajectory outside the boundary in both forward and backward time. Surface conventions remain to be confirmed from the source.
