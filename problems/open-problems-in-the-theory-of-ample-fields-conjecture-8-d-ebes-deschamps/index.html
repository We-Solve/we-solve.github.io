Open Problems in the Theory of Ample Fields — Conjecture 8 (D\`ebes-Deschamps)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be an arbitrary field and let $t$ be an indeterminate over $K$. A finite split embedding problem over $K$ consists of a finite Galois extension $L/K$, a finite group $G$, and a surjective homomorphism \[ \alpha:G\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{Gal}(L/K) \] that admits a homomorphic section $s:\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\to G$, so that $\alpha\circ s=\mathrm{id}$. Identify $\operatorname{Gal}(L(t)/K(t))$ with $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ by restriction to $L$.

Using the standard proper-solution interpretation, a regular solution consists of a finite Galois extension $F/K(t)$ containing $L(t)$ and an isomorphism \[ \gamma:\operatorname{Gal}(F/K(t))\xrightarrow{\sim}G \] such that \[ \alpha\circ\gamma =\operatorname{res}_{L(t)/K(t)}, \] where the restriction map on the right is viewed as taking values in $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, and such that $F/L$ is regular. Here regular means that $F/L$ is separably generated and every element of $F$ algebraic over $L$ belongs to $L$.

Conjecture (Dèbes--Deschamps, as attributed in the supplied source). For every field $K$, every finite Galois extension $L/K$, every finite group $G$, and every split surjective homomorphism $\alpha:G\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, there exists a regular solution as above.

The supplied excerpt calls $\gamma$ a solution without explicitly specifying its surjectivity or bijectivity. The formulation above uses the conventional proper-solution meaning; this interpretation requires source review. The excerpt also says that this conjecture combines two preceding conjectures, whose statements are not included in the supplied record.

Short English statement

The conjecture asserts that every finite split embedding problem over every field can be realized by a Galois extension of a rational function field, with the prescribed finite Galois extension as its field of constants. The statement uses the proper-solution interpretation described above. Its open status is supplied registry information.
