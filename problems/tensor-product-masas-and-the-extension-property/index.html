Math-1082

Tensor-Product Masas and the Extension Property

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H$ be the Hilbert space intended in the report and let $B(H)$ be its bounded operators. A MASA is a maximal abelian self-adjoint subalgebra. Put \[ M=B(H)\otimes_{\max}B(H),\qquad D^c=\{x\in M:xd=dx\text{ for all }d\in D\} \] for the relative commutant of $D\subseteq M$. Tensor subalgebras below are understood as the norm closures of their algebraic tensors in this ambient maximal $C^*$-tensor product, not as weak-operator closures.

The source poses these questions:

  1. For every MASA $C\subseteq B(H)$, is \[ (C\otimes1)^c=C\otimes B(H)\quad\text{inside }M? \]
  2. For arbitrary MASAs $C_1,C_2\subseteq B(H)$, is $C_1\otimes C_2$ maximal abelian in $M$?
  3. In the special case $C\cong\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}})$, does $C$ have the extension property, and is $C\otimes C$ a MASA in $M$?
In the usual unital-inclusion convention, the extension property means that every pure state of $C$ has a unique pure-state extension to $B(H)$ (equivalently, a unique state extension). The source does not define this property explicitly. It also does not specify separability of $H$ or the concrete realization of the inclusion $C\cong\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}})$; the standard diagonal inclusion in $B(\ell^2({\mathbb{N}}))$ should not be silently identified with every unspecified representation.

The supplied assessment reports the Kadison--Singer extension component solved by Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava, while describing the arbitrary-MASA maximal-tensor commutant and MASA questions as unresolved in its cited literature. The three questions are therefore not assigned a single uniform open status, and no inference from the reported extension result to the remaining tensor questions is added without its hypotheses.

Short English statement

Determine relative commutants and maximal abelianness of tensor products of MASAs in $B(H)\otimes_{\max}B(H)$, including the $\ell^\infty$ case and its pure-state extension property. The source reports the Kadison--Singer component solved, separately from the general tensor questions.
