Hexagonal Asymptotics for Spectral Minimal Partitions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret a bounded planar domain as a nonempty bounded connected open set $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$, and write $A(\Omega)=|\Omega|$ for its Lebesgue area. The record does not specify the boundary conditions or the admissible partition class. The following gives a precise, provisional reading using Dirichlet eigenvalues and the maximum partition energy; these conventions require mathematical review against the source.

For a nonempty bounded open set $D\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$, define its first Dirichlet eigenvalue by \[ \lambda_1(D)=\inf_{0\ne u\in H_0^1(D)}\frac{\int_D |\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}{\int_D |u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}, \] where $H_0^1(D)$ is the closure of the smooth compactly supported functions on $D$ in the Sobolev $H^1$ norm. For each integer $k\ge1$, provisionally let \[ \mathcal P_k(\Omega)=\left\{(D_1,\ldots,D_k):\ D_i\subset\Omega\text{ are nonempty connected open sets, and }D_i\cap D_j=\varnothing\text{ for }i\ne j\right\}, \] and define \[ L_k(\Omega)=\inf_{(D_1,\ldots,D_k)\in\mathcal P_k(\Omega)}\max_{1\le i\le k}\lambda_1(D_i). \] This provisional admissible class imposes no additional covering or boundary regularity condition. The supplied record does not determine whether such conditions were intended.

Let $\mathrm{Hexa}_1$ denote the interior of a regular hexagon of area one, and interpret the source notation $\lambda(\mathrm{Hexa}_1)$ as $\lambda_1(\mathrm{Hexa}_1)$. With these conventions, the supplied conjecture asks whether, for every bounded planar domain $\Omega$, \[ \lim_{k\to\infty}\frac{L_k(\Omega)}{k}=\frac{\lambda_1(\mathrm{Hexa}_1)}{A(\Omega)}. \] No boundary regularity hypothesis on $\Omega$ is supplied. The intended scope of this quantifier and the provisional spectral and partition conventions remain to be checked against the cited report.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the least possible largest first Dirichlet eigenvalue among $k$ planar partition cells grows like $k$ times the first eigenvalue of a unit-area regular hexagon, divided by the area of the domain. The Dirichlet and partition conventions used here are provisional because the supplied statement does not define them.
