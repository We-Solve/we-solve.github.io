Math-258

Literal normalization obstruction and the solved polynomial Freiman-Ruzsa theorem

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Brought by T. Tao and originally stated by I. Ruzsa, the source asks whether $A\subseteq{\mathbb{F}}_2^n$ with $|A+A|\leq K|A|$ has a linear subspace $V$ satisfying \[ |V|<K^c|A|,\qquad |A\cap V|\geq K^{-c}|A|, \] for a fixed exponent. The strict sign and pure powers of $K$ are retained. The aid distinguishes this literal normalization from the solved polynomial Freiman--Ruzsa (PFR) statement.

At $K=1$, a non-linear affine coset $A=a+H$, $a\notin H$, is an immediate obstruction: the intersection requirement forces $A\subseteq V$, hence $H+\langle a\rangle\subseteq V$ and $|V|\geq2|A|$. Even a linear $A$ shows the strict size inequality alone cannot hold at $K=1$ together with full intersection.

The aid gives a robust family with $K>1$ tending to one. Let $|H|=q\geq4$ be a power of two, choose $a\notin H$ and set \[ A=a+(H\setminus\{0\}),\qquad |A|=q-1,\qquad A+A=H, \qquad K=\frac q{q-1}. \] For every fixed $c>0$, sufficiently large $q$ makes \[ K^{-c}(q-1)>q/2,\qquad K^c(q-1)<2q. \] A linear subspace meeting $A$ in more than $q/2$ points must contain both $H$ and $a$, so its size is at least $2q$, violating the other bound. Hence no fixed exponent repairs the literal two inequalities uniformly down to $K=1$.

The intended PFR problem is nevertheless reported solved. The summary invokes Liao's 2024 cover by at most $2K^9$ cosets of a subspace $H$ with $|H|\leq|A|$. For $K\geq2$, a coset meeting $A$ in at least $|A|/(2K^9)$ points is contained in its linear span $V=H+\langle a\rangle$, and the aid obtains the exact source inequalities with the single exponent $c=10$: \[ |V|<K^{10}|A|,\qquad |A\cap V|\geq K^{-10}|A|. \] The restriction $K\geq2$ absorbs the fixed factors and is essential to this stated corollary.

The original remark calls the following an equivalent formulation. For $f:{\mathbb{F}}_2^m\to{\mathbb{F}}_2^\infty$ let \[ S=\{f(x+y)-f(x)-f(y):x,y\in{\mathbb{F}}_2^m\}. \] Can one write $f=g+h$, with $g$ linear and $|\operatorname{im}h|$ polynomial in $|S|$? The aid says Gowers--Green--Manners--Tao, Corollary 1.4, proves this approximate-homomorphism statement. No explicit exponent is supplied. Equivalence here is with the properly normalized intended PFR theorem, not with the refuted all-$K$ strict inequalities word for word. The target ${\mathbb{F}}_2^\infty$ is understood as the direct-sum vector space; the finite domain gives finite image and defect set.

Short English statement

The literal linear-subspace bounds fail near doubling one because affine shifts require a fixed size factor. The aid separates that defect from the solved PFR theorem: a named covering bound gives the exact requested inequalities for $K\geq2$, and a named corollary proves the approximate-homomorphism formulation.
