Rational Certificates for Truncated Moment Nonrepresentability

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write ${\mathbb{R}}[x]={\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, and let $\Sigma_{{\mathbb{R}}}[x]$ be the set of finite sums of squares of polynomials in ${\mathbb{R}}[x]$. For real polynomials $g_1,\ldots,g_r$, define \[ K=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:g_i(x)\geq0\text{ for }1\leq i\leq r\},\qquad Q_{{\mathbb{R}}}(g)=\left\{\sigma_0+\sum_{i=1}^r\sigma_i g_i:\sigma_i\in\Sigma_{{\mathbb{R}}}[x]\right\}. \] Thus $p\in1+Q_{{\mathbb{R}}}(g)$ means that \[ p-1=\sum_j h_{0j}^2+\sum_{i=1}^r g_i\sum_j h_{ij}^2 \] for finitely many real polynomials $h_{ij}$. Such an identity is called a rational sum-of-squares certificate here if every $h_{ij}$ belongs to ${\mathbb{Q}}[x]$. The assertion that every certificate requires irrational coefficients means that a real certificate exists but no certificate of this rational form exists; it does not merely mean that some coefficients of the expanded multipliers are irrational.

For an integer $d\geq0$, let $A_d=\{\alpha\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^n:|\alpha|\leq d\}$. A rational truncated moment vector is $y=(y_\alpha)_{\alpha\in A_d}\in{\mathbb{Q}}^{A_d}$, with associated functional \[ L_y\!\left(\sum_{\alpha\in A_d}p_\alpha x^\alpha\right)=\sum_{\alpha\in A_d}p_\alpha y_\alpha. \] A representing measure on $K$ is a finite positive Borel measure supported on $K$, with the indicated monomials integrable, such that $y_\alpha=\int_K x^\alpha\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(x)$ for every $\alpha\in A_d$. No normalization $y_0=1$ is supplied.

Under the usual strict interpretation of a separating polynomial, the extracted question asks whether there exist data as above and $p\in{\mathbb{Q}}[x]$ of degree at most $d$ such that $y$ has no representing measure on $K$, \[ L_y(p)<0,\qquad p\in1+Q_{{\mathbb{R}}}(g), \] but there are no finite families $q_{ij}\in{\mathbb{Q}}[x]$ satisfying \[ p-1=\sum_j q_{0j}^2+\sum_{i=1}^r g_i\sum_j q_{ij}^2. \] Here membership in $1+Q_{{\mathbb{R}}}(g)$ gives $p\geq1$ on $K$, so the displayed negative evaluation expresses separation from moment vectors of positive measures.

Short English statement

Can a rational truncated moment vector with no representing positive measure on $K$ have a rational separating polynomial $p\in1+Q(g)$ whose sum-of-squares membership certificates all require irrational coefficients? The displayed formulation uses strict separation and total-degree truncation explicitly; the source leaves these conventions, the coefficient field of the generators, and certificate degree restrictions unresolved. The supplied record labels the problem open; this status is not independently verified here.
