Math-7325

Comparison of Majorization Relations for Probability Vectors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether a closure characterization for catalytic majorization remains valid for multiple-copy majorization. It does not explicitly specify the dimensions or whether probability vectors must be finite. The following gives the finite, fixed-dimension interpretation; this domain qualification requires verification against the full statement of Theorem 1.

For an integer $d\geq1$, define \[ \Delta_d=\left\{u\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:u_i\geq0,\ \sum_{i=1}^d u_i=1\right\},\qquad \Delta_d^{\circ}=\{u\in\Delta_d:u_i>0\text{ for every }i\}. \] For $u,v\in\Delta_d$, write $u\prec v$ if their coordinates, arranged in nonincreasing order, satisfy \[ \sum_{i=1}^r u_i^{\downarrow}\leq\sum_{i=1}^r v_i^{\downarrow} \quad(1\leq r<d),\qquad \sum_{i=1}^d u_i=\sum_{i=1}^d v_i. \] For finite vectors $u$ and $z$, their tensor product has coordinates $(u\otimes z)_{(i,j)}=u_i z_j$. Define \[ u\prec_C v \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists m\geq1\ \exists z\in\Delta_m:\ u\otimes z\prec v\otimes z, \] and \[ u\prec_M v \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ n\geq1:\ u^{\otimes n}\prec v^{\otimes n}. \] Here the tensor power has coordinates $u_{i_1}\cdots u_{i_n}$. For $u\in\Delta_d^{\circ}$ and real $p$, put \[ N_p(u)=\sum_{i=1}^d u_i^p. \] In particular, $N_0(u)=d$ and $N_1(u)=1$.

Fix $x,y\in\Delta_d^{\circ}$. The supplied characterization for $\prec_C$ asserts that the conditions \[ \begin{aligned} N_p(x)&\leq N_p(y)&&\text{for every real }p\geq1,\\ N_p(x)&\geq N_p(y)&&\text{for every real }0\leq p\leq1,\\ N_p(x)&\leq N_p(y)&&\text{for every real }p\leq0 \end{aligned} \tag{P} \] characterize the existence of probability vectors $x_k\prec_C y$ converging to $x$ in $\ell^1$.

The question is whether, under the finite, fixed-dimension interpretation above, for every $d\geq1$ and every $x,y\in\Delta_d^{\circ}$, condition $\mathrm{(P)}$ is equivalent to \[ \exists (x_k)_{k\geq1}\subseteq\Delta_d\ \exists(n_k)_{k\geq1}\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}:\quad x_k^{\otimes n_k}\prec y^{\otimes n_k}\ \text{for every }k, \qquad \lim_{k\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^d |(x_k)_i-x_i|=0. \] The integer $n_k$ may depend on $k$; no common tensor exponent is required. In this fixed-dimension interpretation, convergence to strictly positive $x$ makes all sufficiently late $x_k$ strictly positive, so requiring positive approximants gives the same limit condition. If varying dimensions or infinite probability vectors were intended, the ambient space, majorization convention, and treatment of zero coordinates and nonpositive powers would require additional specification. The stated catalytic characterization is supplied source content, not a theorem independently verified in this draft.

Short English statement

For strictly positive probability vectors, the supplied problem asks whether the same three families of power-sum inequalities that characterize limits under catalytic majorization also characterize limits under multiple-copy majorization. Each approximating vector may use its own number of tensor copies. The finite, fixed-dimension interpretation is explicit here but needs source verification. The record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
