Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

A hyperbolic-cross polynomial space selects multivariate monomials using a product-type degree bound. Sampling complexity is the number of function values needed to achieve the specified approximation error with the prescribed confidence.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Can better sampling measures lower the sample complexity of hyperbolic-cross polynomial approximation, and can sample-complexity estimates be proved for domains that are not unions of rectangles with nonvanishing volume, including triangles, simplices, and spheres? [S2]
