Math-4542

Uniform Doubling for Compact Connected Lie Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a compact connected Lie group, and let $\mathcal G_L(K)$ denote the set of all left-invariant Riemannian metrics on $K$: for each $g\in\mathcal G_L(K)$ and $a\in K$, left translation $L_a(x)=ax$ is an isometry. Write $d_g$ for the Riemannian distance and $\operatorname{vol}_g$ for the Riemannian volume measure. For $x\in K$ and $r>0$, define \[ B_g(x,r)=\{y\in K:d_g(x,y)<r\}, \qquad D(g)=\sup_{x\in K,\ r>0} \frac{\operatorname{vol}_g(B_g(x,2r))}{\operatorname{vol}_g(B_g(x,r))}. \] The group $K$ is called uniformly doubling if \[ \sup_{g\in\mathcal G_L(K)}D(g)<\infty. \] Question. Is every compact connected Lie group uniformly doubling? Equivalently, is it true that for every compact connected Lie group $K$ there exists a finite constant $C_K\geq 1$ such that \[ \operatorname{vol}_g(B_g(x,2r)) \leq C_K\operatorname{vol}_g(B_g(x,r)) \quad\text{for every }g\in\mathcal G_L(K),\ x\in K,\ r>0? \] The constant may depend on $K$, but must be independent of the metric, center, and radius. All positive radii are included, with no restriction relative to the diameter. The supplied record labels this question open. Its background also mentions proved cases and unspecified "related low-dimensional cases"; those are supplied literature qualifications, not additional hypotheses or independently verified claims.

Short English statement

For each compact connected Lie group $K$, does there exist a finite bound, depending only on $K$, for how much the volume of a ball can increase when its radius is doubled, simultaneously for every left-invariant Riemannian metric, every center, and every positive radius?
