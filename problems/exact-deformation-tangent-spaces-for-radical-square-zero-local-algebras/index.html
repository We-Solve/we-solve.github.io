Math-1335

Exact deformation tangent spaces for radical-square-zero local algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite commutative $k$-algebra $A$, let \[ T^1_{A/k}=\operatorname{Ext}^1_A(L_{A/k},A) \] be its first cotangent cohomology, parametrizing first-order algebra deformations. The recovered question asks, over an algebraically closed field $k$, whether there is a nontrivial finite local algebra $A\ne k$ with $T^1_{A/k}=0$. The source verification adopts characteristic zero for the corroborating modern formulation. The literal original baseline is $k^n$, not local for $n>1$; it is the finite-etale baseline if locality is dropped. This inconsistency is retained rather than silently treating $k^n$ as a local algebra.

The attached aid computes a special class in every characteristic. Let $V$ be a $k$-vector space of dimension $e\ge2$ and let $A=k\oplus V$ with $V^2=0$. Then it asserts the natural quotient \[ T^1_{A/k}\simeq\operatorname{Hom}_k(\operatorname{Sym}^2V,V)\big/\{\beta_c:c\in V^*\},\qquad \beta_c(u,v)=c(u)v+c(v)u. \] The map $c\mapsto\beta_c$ is injective for $e\ge2$, including characteristic $2$, and hence \[ \dim_kT^1_{A/k}=\frac{e^2(e+1)}2-e>0. \] Thus no algebra of this radical-square-zero class is a positive answer to the recovered question.

For a basis $x_1,\ldots,x_e$, the class $\psi(x_1,x_1)=x_2$ with all other basis-pair values zero is nonzero. The aid integrates it to the algebra over $k[t]$ that is free with basis $1,x_1,\ldots,x_e$, multiplication $x_1^2=t x_2$, and every other product $x_ix_j$ zero. Its special fiber is $A$ and its first jet is the indicated deformation class. For the minimal embedding $A=S/\mathfrak m^2$ with $S=k[x_1,\ldots,x_e]$, the embedded Hilbert tangent has dimension $e^2(e+1)/2$, while the ambient-derivation image has dimension $e$; quotienting gives the same $T^1$. These computations neither construct nor rule out rigid finite local algebras with higher nilpotence.

Short English statement

The repaired question seeks a nontrivial infinitesimally rigid finite local algebra. The supplied aid excludes all radical-square-zero algebras of embedding dimension at least two by an exact characteristic-free deformation calculation and an explicit integrated nontrivial class.
