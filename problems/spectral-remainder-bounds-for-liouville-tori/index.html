Math-1016

Spectral Remainder Bounds for Liouville Tori

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be the torus with a Liouville metric intended in the source. In the usual two-dimensional convention, such a metric is expressed in suitable periodic coordinates as $(a(s)+b(t))(ds^2+dt^2)$, with positive conformal factor. The source gives no coordinate periods, regularity, nondegeneracy or exclusion of flat cases, and does not explicitly state the dimension.

To make a standard spectral normalization visible, suppose $-\Delta\phi_j=\lambda_j^2\phi_j$, with $(\phi_j)$ an orthonormal eigenbasis and $\lambda_j\geq0$. In this frequency convention define \[ N(\lambda)=\#\{j:\lambda_j\leq\lambda\},\qquad R(\lambda)=N(\lambda)-\frac{\operatorname{Area}(M)}{4\pi}\lambda^2, \] \[ R_x(\lambda)=\sum_{\lambda_j\leq\lambda}|\phi_j(x)|^2-\frac{\lambda^2}{4\pi}, \qquad N_{x,y}(\lambda)=\sum_{\lambda_j\leq\lambda}\phi_j(x)\overline{\phi_j(y)}. \] These formulas specify the customary surface Laplacian version only. The record names the remainders but does not define the operator, cutoff or whether $\lambda$ is frequency or eigenvalue. An eigenvalue cutoff would change the main term and numerical exponent; no such change is silently made.

The printed question asks whether, for every $\varepsilon>0$, \[ R(\lambda)=O(\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon}),\qquad R_x(\lambda)=O(\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon})\quad\text{for every }x\in M, \] \[ N_{x,y}(\lambda)=O(\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon}) \quad\text{for every distinct }x,y\in M, \qquad\lambda\to\infty. \] Under the pointwise interpretation, constants may depend on $M,\varepsilon$, and for local/off-diagonal quantities on the fixed point or pair, but not on $\lambda$. The source does not request uniformity as $x$ approaches $y$, and no bound uniform over all Liouville metrics is added. All points and all distinct pairs remain in scope, not merely generic or almost-everywhere pairs. The diagonal is excluded only from the unrenormalized $N_{x,y}$ claim, not from the separately defined diagonal remainder.

The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports improved lattice-counting bounds for Liouville tori and says the conjectured square-root bounds include unresolved Hardy/Gauss circle questions. This assessment supports keeping track of the spectral scale but does not supply missing definitions or exact special hypotheses. The open label and notation-restoration verification remain dated metadata, not independently checked current results.

Short English statement

Ask for the same $\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ order in the global, diagonal-remainder and fixed off-diagonal spectral quantities. The source does not define its spectral parameter, so the usual surface-frequency normalization is explicit and conditional. Every point and distinct pair are retained, without uniformity near the diagonal.
