Math-7277

Existence of Gaussian Persistence Exponents

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f$ denote the Gaussian stationary process mentioned in the source, and let $P_f(N)\in[0,1]$ denote its persistence probability at horizon $N$. The source does not define the persistence event, the parameter set or value domain of $f$, or the admissible horizons $N$. Consequently, $P_f(N)$ remains a source-dependent quantity rather than a fully specified probability in this draft.

Requested statement. Determine conditions on $f$ that ensure the existence of the persistence exponent \[ \theta_f=-\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{1}{N}\log P_f(N), \] where the limit must be taken over the intended admissible horizons. The logarithm is the natural logarithm.

For the interpretation requiring a finite exponent and assuming $P_f(N)>0$ for all sufficiently large admissible $N$, the requested conclusion can be expressed precisely as \[ \exists\,\theta_f\in[0,\infty)\quad \forall\,\varepsilon>0\quad \exists\,N_0>0\quad \forall\text{ admissible }N\ge N_0,\qquad \left|-\frac{\log P_f(N)}{N}-\theta_f\right|<\varepsilon. \] Equivalently, under those qualifications, \[ P_f(N)=\exp\left(-\theta_f N+o(N)\right). \] These are conditional formulations: the source does not specify eventual positivity, whether $\theta_f=0$ is permitted, or whether an infinite exponent is allowed. If zero persistence probabilities are permitted, an extended-real interpretation would also require an explicit convention for $-\log 0$.

Unresolved scope. The persistence event must be supplied, including its threshold, strict or non-strict inequality, and observation times. Discrete observations and persistence throughout a continuous interval define different problems; the latter also requires an appropriate measurability framework. Centering, variance normalization, degeneracy exclusions, and spectral or covariance hypotheses are not stated. The source asks for conditions ensuring existence but does not specify whether it seeks sufficient conditions or a necessary-and-sufficient characterization. Its displayed limit describes an exponential logarithmic rate; it does not by itself request a multiplicative asymptotic equivalent or a prefactor.

Short English statement

Determine assumptions under which the persistence probability of a stationary Gaussian process has a well-defined exponential decay rate. The source leaves the persistence event and process framework unspecified, so the statement remains conditional on those definitions. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
