Math-1749

Dimension-dependent curvature-operator answer for PIC blow-up limits

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M^n,g(t))$, $n\geq4$, be a Ricci flow from a compact boundaryless Riemannian manifold with positive isotropic curvature (PIC). With a consistent curvature-tensor convention, PIC means \[ R_{1313}+R_{1414}+R_{2323}+R_{2424}-2R_{1234}>0 \] for every orthonormal four-frame. Nonnegative curvature operator (NCO) means $\langle\operatorname{Rm}\omega,\omega\rangle\geq0$ for every $\omega\in\Lambda^2TM$. The source asks whether every blow-up limit has NCO.

The supplied assessment concerns standard finite-time singularity-model blow-ups: for $t_i\to T<\infty$ and curvature scales $Q_i\to\infty$, take pointed smooth limits of \[ g_i(s)=Q_i g(t_i+s/Q_i), \] with completeness, bounded curvature on the required time intervals, and the ancient/noncollapsing hypotheses used by the model-classification results. It reports an affirmative answer in dimension $4$ and every $n\geq12$ from published pinching and ancient-$\kappa$-solution classification results. Assuming Zhengnan Chen's 2025 preprint theorem that complete ancient uniformly PIC flows in dimensions $n\geq9$ are weakly PIC2, it reports the same conclusion conditionally for $n=9,10,11$. The checked theory in the supplied aid leaves $n=5,6,7,8$ open. Full references and exact model hypotheses are not supplied.

Here weak PIC2 means that the curvature tensor extended by two flat directions has nonnegative isotropic curvature. A uniform PIC condition is a positive, scale-invariant quantitative margin, such as membership of $\operatorname{Rm}-\theta\operatorname{scal}I$ in the nonnegative PIC cone for one fixed $\theta>0$, in the theorem's convention. These conditions must not be identified with NCO: the aid explicitly requires a separate compact/noncompact ancient-model classification bridge from the pinched cone to NCO.

The attempt's independent general lemma is a cone-defect limit statement. Let $\mathcal C$ be a closed frame-invariant curvature cone invariant under multiplication by positive scalars. Suppose, on the region being rescaled, scalar curvature $r\geq0$ and \[ \operatorname{Rm}+f(r)I\in\mathcal C, \] where $I$ is the constant-curvature identity operator and $f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ is locally bounded with $f(r)/r\to0$ as $r\to\infty$. If a smooth parabolic blow-up has locally bounded rescaled scalar curvature, every limit curvature tensor lies in $\mathcal C$. Indeed for each $\varepsilon>0$, $f(r)\leq\varepsilon r+C_\varepsilon$, so on a rescaled compact region with scalar curvature bounded by $S$ the error is \[ Q_i^{-1}f(r_i)\leq\varepsilon S+C_\varepsilon/Q_i\longrightarrow0 \quad\text{after letting }\varepsilon\to0. \] Closedness then removes the defect. Local boundedness is needed to control bounded original scalar values. This lemma yields NCO directly only when $\mathcal C$ is the NCO cone; for weak PIC2 it gives that weaker condition and still needs the stated classification bridge. It does not by itself settle the unresolved dimensions or every possible interpretation of blow-up limit.

Short English statement

Must singularity blow-ups of a compact PIC Ricci flow have nonnegative curvature operator? The supplied assessment answers yes for standard models in dimension $4$ and at least $12$, gives a preprint-dependent route for $9$--$11$, and leaves $5$--$8$ unresolved. A cone-defect lemma explains why sublinear curvature errors disappear under smooth rescaling, while preserving the distinction between weak PIC2 and NCO.
