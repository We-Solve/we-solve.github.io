PDE-Inspired Graph Functionals and Their Continuum Limits

The supplied record describes a research programme rather than a single fully specified conjecture. Let $G$ denote a weighted graph, let $X_G$ denote its space of admissible states, and write $f_G$ for the PDE-inspired graph functional called $f$ in the source. Let $X$ denote a continuum space of admissible states and $F$ its proposed continuum counterpart. Here PDE means partial differential equation. The requested programme is to identify such data and establish correspondences addressing the following questions:

  1. Which structural properties of $F$ admit precisely formulated analogues for $f_G$, and under which hypotheses do those analogues hold?
  2. For which sequences of weighted graphs $(G_n)_{n\geq 1}$, choices of weights and scaling, and identifications of graph states with continuum states is there a convergence result of the schematic form \[ f_{G_n}\xrightarrow{\mathcal C}F \qquad (n\to\infty)? \] Here $\mathcal C$ denotes a notion of convergence to be specified, including the treatment of the varying spaces $X_{G_n}$. The display does not assert convergence or select a graph-limit regime, topology, or variational convergence notion.
  3. How can algorithms for minimizing $f_G$, with their admissible sets and constraints specified, be used in data analysis and image processing, and what mathematical conclusions support those uses?
  4. Which additional network problems admit PDE-inspired methods, and which additional PDE or variational systems admit meaningful and useful graph analogues?

Short English statement

The problem asks for graph counterparts of continuum variational properties, conditions for graph functionals to converge to continuum functionals, applications of graph minimization to data and images, and further PDE-inspired network models. It is an open-ended programme: the supplied record does not specify enough mathematical data for a single quantified conjecture. Its supplied status is partially solved, subject to the dated source qualifications below.
