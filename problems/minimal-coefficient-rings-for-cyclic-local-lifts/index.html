Math-3712

Minimal Coefficient Rings for Cyclic Local Lifts

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks whether the local lifting problem for cyclic groups $G$ outside an unspecified known construction can always be solved with \[ R=W(k)(\zeta_{|G|}), \] where $\zeta_{|G|}$ denotes a primitive root of unity of order $|G|$. The record does not specify the hypotheses on $k$, the groups covered by the known construction, or the precise meaning of this coefficient-ring notation. Consequently, it does not determine a fully specified mathematical assertion.

The following describes the usual local lifting formulation, conditionally on the missing conventions. Suppose that $k$ is a perfect field of characteristic $p>0$, so that $W(k)$ denotes its ring of $p$-typical Witt vectors. Write $k[[t]]$ for the formal power series ring. A faithful local action of a finite cyclic group $G$ is an injective homomorphism \[ \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}^{\mathrm{cont}}_k(k[[t]]), \] where continuity is for the $(t)$-adic topology. For a complete discrete valuation ring $R$ of characteristic zero with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_R$ and a specified identification $R/\mathfrak m_R\cong k$, a lift of $\rho$ is an injective homomorphism \[ \widetilde\rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}^{\mathrm{cont}}_R(R[[T]]) \] whose reduction modulo $\mathfrak m_R$, under $T\mapsto t$, equals $\rho$. Continuity here is for the $(\mathfrak m_R,T)$-adic topology.

Under this conventional interpretation, the intended question would ask whether, for every cyclic group outside the groups covered by the referenced construction and every faithful local action under consideration, such a lift exists over the prescribed ring $W(k)(\zeta_{|G|})$. This universal quantification is an interpretation of "the local lifting problem"; the supplied record does not explicitly state its scope or the allowed fields $k$.

The prescribed ring requires clarification: adjoining $\zeta_{|G|}$ integrally to $W(k)$, adjoining it to the fraction field, and taking a suitable ring of integers are distinct descriptions. A coefficient ring for the formulation above must also have residue field identified with $k$. No choice among these interpretations, no additional hypothesis on $k$, and no description of the excluded known construction is supplied here. These qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The question asks whether cyclic local actions outside an unspecified known construction lift to characteristic zero over the particular coefficient ring written $W(k)(\zeta_{|G|})$. The field hypotheses, excluded cases, and exact coefficient ring need clarification. The supplied dated assessment labels the stronger coefficient-ring question open in wild cyclic cases; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
