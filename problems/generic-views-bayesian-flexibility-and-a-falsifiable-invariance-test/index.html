The aid's identified mathematical issue is whether generic-view accounts are distinguishable from unconstrained Bayesian rationalizations. The following finite-model statements are introduced by that aid; they are not equations or posed theorems in the transcript.

Reported prior-absorption criterion. Let $\mathcal H,\mathcal I$ be finite hypothesis/image sets, with positive prior $\pi(h)$, positive specified likelihood $L(i\mid h)$ and positive genericity multiplier $G(h,i)$. The posterior proportional to $\pi(h)L(i\mid h)G(h,i)$ can use the same likelihood and absorb $G$ entirely into one image-independent object prior exactly when \[ G(h,i)=a(h)b(i) \] for positive functions $a,b$. Then the new prior is proportional to $\pi(h)a(h)$. Full positivity makes this equivalent to all cross-product identities $G(h,i)G(h',i')=G(h,i')G(h',i)$. With zeros, the criterion must be interpreted on the actual likelihood support; the supplied summary does not state those cases.

Reported arbitrary-table rationalization. Given any conditional table $Q(h\mid i)\geq0$ with $\sum_hQ(h\mid i)=1$, choose any strictly positive image evidence $e(i)$ summing to one. Define \[ P(h,i)=e(i)Q(h\mid i),\qquad \pi(h)=\sum_i e(i)Q(h\mid i),\qquad L(i\mid h)=\frac{e(i)Q(h\mid i)}{\pi(h)}\quad\text{if }\pi(h)>0. \] If $\pi(h)=0$, every $Q(h\mid i)=0$ and $L(\cdot\mid h)$ may be any probability vector. These choices give a normalized prior/likelihood model reproducing $Q$ on every image, since $e(i)>0$. The aid states that every joint law with full image support is recovered by choosing its own image marginal as $e$. For a general joint law, conditional probabilities are fixed only where the image marginal is positive; zero-evidence images remain version-arbitrary. Zero-prior hypotheses and zero-evidence images are therefore different phenomena.

Reported common-prior intervention test. Suppose environments $v$ implement specified viewpoint interventions and supply fixed positive generic-view kernels $K_v(i,h)$, incorporating any declared nuisance-view integration and genericity factors. Given positive observed posteriors $Q_v(h\mid i)$, the existence of a common positive prior with \[ Q_v(h\mid i)=\frac{\pi(h)K_v(i,h)}{\sum_{h'}\pi(h')K_v(i,h')} \] is equivalent to constancy across images and environments of every corrected odds ratio \[ \frac{Q_v(h\mid i)}{Q_v(h'\mid i)}\frac{K_v(i,h')}{K_v(i,h)} =\frac{\pi(h)}{\pi(h')}. \] The kernels and interventions must be specified independently; arbitrary refitting removes this restriction. The summary reports a worked two-view surface family with a prior-free ranking-reversal prediction, but gives no numerical kernels or reversal values, so that example is not reconstructed here.

The aid attributes to Nakayama--Shimojo a generic-sampling account as a degenerate Bayesian form neglecting object priors, and to Freeman viewpoint-prior integration yielding a marginal image likelihood.
