Adelic Harmonic Metrics on Line Bundles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ denote a line bundle on the source's space $X$. An adelic metrization $\overline M$ denotes $M$ equipped with metrics at the places of the source's field $K$. The supplied background describes the intended assertion as one over global fields, but the statement does not specify the hypotheses on $K$ or $X$.

Source assertion, with unresolved parameters. Does every line bundle $M$ on $X$ admit an admissible adelic metrization $\overline M$ whose metric at every place of $K$ has harmonic curvature and for which \[ \overline M\cdot\omega^n=\lambda(M)\,\omega^{n+1}, \] with this metrization unique up to multiplication by a degree-zero element of $\operatorname{Div}(K)$?

Unresolved qualifications. This is a transcription of the intended assertion, not a fully specified conjecture. The record does not define $X$, $n$, $\omega$, or $\lambda(M)$, nor does it specify the intersection product or the space in which the displayed equality holds. In particular, it does not state whether $n=\dim X$, what geometric or metric structure $\omega$ carries, or how $\lambda(M)$ is determined. The notions of admissibility and harmonic curvature, including their meaning at each type of place, are also unspecified. Finally, the definition of $\operatorname{Div}(K)$, its degree map, and its stated multiplicative action on metrizations must be supplied to make the uniqueness clause precise. These missing choices cannot be inferred from the displayed formula alone.

Short English statement

The source asks whether every line bundle admits an adelic metric with harmonic curvature at every place, satisfying the displayed intersection identity and unique modulo the stated degree-zero divisor action. Essential geometric hypotheses, definitions, and normalizations are missing, so a precise standalone formulation requires review of the cited report.
