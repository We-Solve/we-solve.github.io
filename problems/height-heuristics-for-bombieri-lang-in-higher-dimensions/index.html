Height Heuristics for Bombieri–Lang in Higher Dimensions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks for a higher-dimensional analogue of the curve condition $g>1$, together with a generalization of an unspecified crude height-counting heuristic that would predict the Bombieri--Lang conjecture for all varieties of general type. The following gives a conventional precise conjectural target; its field and geometric hypotheses are explicit interpretive choices because the supplied statement does not specify them.

Let $k$ be a number field and let $X/k$ be a smooth, projective, geometrically integral variety of dimension $n\geq 1$. Write $K_X$ for its canonical line bundle. Say that $X$ is of general type if $K_X$ is big, meaning \[ \limsup_{m\to\infty}\frac{\dim_k H^0(X,K_X^{\otimes m})}{m^n}>0. \] For a smooth projective geometrically integral curve $C/k$, this condition is equivalent to $g(C)>1$. Thus general type supplies the conventional higher-dimensional analogue of the stated curve condition.

Under this interpretation, the conjectural prediction to be explained is \[ \text{for every number field }k\text{ and every such }X/k\text{ of general type},\qquad \overline{X(k)}^{\mathrm{Zar}}\subsetneq X. \] Equivalently, for each such pair $(k,X)$, there exists a proper Zariski-closed subset $Z\subsetneq X$, defined over $k$, such that $X(k)\subseteq Z(k)$. The subset $Z$ may depend on $k$ and $X$. This predicts non-density, without asserting finiteness of $X(k)$ in higher dimensions.

To express the counting aspect, after fixing an ample line bundle $L$ on $X$ and an associated absolute multiplicative Weil height $H_L$, one may consider \[ N_{X,k,L}(B)=\#\{P\in X(k):H_L(P)\leq B\},\qquad B\geq 1. \] The source question is to formulate a higher-dimensional height-counting heuristic that predicts the displayed non-density statement for every variety of general type under the intended hypotheses. The supplied record gives neither the original crude heuristic nor a proposed counting law, height normalization, or treatment of exceptional subvarieties. Consequently, it does not determine a unique precise heuristic or asymptotic assertion. In particular, a prediction of slow growth for $N_{X,k,L}(B)$ alone is not a statement of Zariski non-density. These unresolved qualifications are part of this draft; no heuristic or proof is supplied.

Short English statement

General type is the conventional higher-dimensional analogue of $g>1$ for smooth projective curves. The question asks for a height-counting heuristic predicting that rational points on every general-type variety are contained in a proper closed subset. The displayed formulation uses explicit number-field and smooth projective hypotheses; the source does not specify these hypotheses or the heuristic to be generalized.
