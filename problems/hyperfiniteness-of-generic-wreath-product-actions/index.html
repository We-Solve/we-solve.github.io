Math-6876

Hyperfiniteness of Generic Wreath-Product Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X=2^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ be Cantor space with its product topology. With the usual restricted-wreath-product convention, put \[ G={\mathbb{Z}}\wr{\mathbb{Z}}=\left(\bigoplus_{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Z}}\right)\rtimes{\mathbb{Z}}. \] Here $\bigoplus_{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Z}}$ consists of the finitely supported functions $f:{\mathbb{Z}}\to{\mathbb{Z}}$, and $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ acts by $(\sigma_m f)(k)=f(k-m)$. Thus \[ (f,m)(h,n)=(f+\sigma_m h,m+n). \] A continuous action of $G$ on $X$ is a homomorphism $\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$. Its orbit equivalence relation is \[ x\mathrel{E_\alpha}y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (\exists g\in G)\;\alpha(g)(x)=y. \] The relation $E_\alpha$ is hyperfinite if there are Borel equivalence relations $F_n\subseteq X\times X$, indexed by $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$, such that every $F_n$-class is finite and \[ F_n\subseteq F_{n+1}\quad\text{for every }n\in{\mathbb{N}}, \qquad E_\alpha=\bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}F_n. \] This is a Borel condition on the entire space $X$; no measure or exceptional null set is specified.

Source question. Is the generic action of $G={\mathbb{Z}}\wr{\mathbb{Z}}$ on Cantor space hyperfinite?

Unresolved meaning of genericity. The supplied extract does not define "the generic action" or its ambient action space. A conventional Baire-category interpretation uses \[ \mathcal A=\operatorname{Hom}\left(G,\operatorname{Homeo}(X)\right), \] with the subspace topology inherited from $\operatorname{Homeo}(X)^G$, where $\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$ has the compact-open topology. Under this interpretation the precise question is whether there exists a comeager set $C\subseteq\mathcal A$ such that \[ \forall\alpha\in C\;\exists(F_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}\quad \left[ \begin{gathered} \text{each }F_n\text{ is a Borel equivalence relation on }X \text{ with finite classes},\\ F_n\subseteq F_{n+1}\text{ for every }n\in{\mathbb{N}}, \qquad E_\alpha=\bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}F_n \end{gathered} \right]. \] A set is comeager when its complement is a countable union of nowhere dense sets. This displayed formulation is conditional on the stated interpretation. If the source instead singles out an action whose conjugacy class is comeager, the question concerns that action's orbit relation; existence of such a conjugacy class is not established by the supplied extract. The extract also does not specify whether genericity is relative to all actions or to a restricted space such as the free actions. These qualifications require source clarification before the conditional formulation can be treated as definitive.

The surrounding source discussion places this example among unresolved questions about generic actions, including actions of amenable groups, after a result called Theorem 1. It attributes that theorem's proof to a connection between finite Borel asymptotic dimension and hyperfiniteness developed in its reference [1]. Neither Theorem 1 nor the bibliographic identification of that numbered reference is included in the extract; no additional hypothesis or conclusion from them is imposed here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the orbit equivalence relation of a generic continuous action of ${\mathbb{Z}}\wr{\mathbb{Z}}$ on Cantor space can be written as an increasing union of Borel equivalence relations with finite classes. The supplied extract leaves the meaning and ambient space of "generic" unspecified, so the detailed Baire-category formulation above remains conditional. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
