Math-6945

Polynomial Boundedness of Definably Complete Restricted Exponential Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal K=(K;0,1,+,-,\cdot,<,\ldots)$ be an expansion of an ordered field. In the definitions below, "definable" means definable in $\mathcal K$ with parameters from $K$. No Archimedean assumption is imposed.

The structure $\mathcal K$ is definably complete if every nonempty definable subset $A\subseteq K$ that is bounded above has a least upper bound in $K$. It is polynomially bounded if, for every definable function $f:K\to K$, there exist a standard positive integer $n\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 1}$ and an element $b\in K$, with $b>1$, such that \[ \forall x\in K\quad \left(x>b\ \Longrightarrow\ |f(x)|\leq x^n\right). \] The integer $n$ and threshold $b$ may depend on $f$.

Source terminology requiring clarification. The supplied record does not define a "restricted exponential field": it gives neither its precise language nor the domain, normalization, and axioms of its restricted exponential operation. Let $\mathscr C_{\mathrm{src}}$ denote the class intended by that term in the cited report. This notation retains the source's intended class without choosing additional axioms. The parameter convention above is the usual convention for the two model-theoretic properties; its agreement with the report's precise language and conventions requires review.

Question (as supplied). Is every definably complete restricted exponential field polynomially bounded? With the preceding terminology, the proposed assertion is \[ \forall\mathcal K\in\mathscr C_{\mathrm{src}}\quad \left( \mathcal K\text{ is definably complete} \ \Longrightarrow\ \mathcal K\text{ is polynomially bounded} \right). \] A fully axiomatized formulation requires the missing definition of $\mathscr C_{\mathrm{src}}$. The supplied dated assessment describes this unconditional question as open and reports a result under a Schanuel-type hypothesis. That hypothesis and the exact conditional result are not specified in the record and are not added as assumptions to this question.

Short English statement

The question asks whether definable completeness forces every definable unary function in a restricted exponential field to be eventually bounded in absolute value by an integer power of its argument. The precise restricted exponential structure must be recovered from the cited source. The supplied open-status assessment and conditional-result description require independent verification.
