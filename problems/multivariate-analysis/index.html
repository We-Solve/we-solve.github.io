Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let the $n$ columns $M_j\in\mathbb R^p$ be independent with law $N_p(\mu,\Sigma)$, where $\Sigma$ is positive definite with eigenvalues $\eta_1\ge\cdots\ge\eta_k>1$ and $\eta_{k+1}=\cdots=\eta_p=1$, with $k$ fixed as $p,n\to\infty$. Put $\bar M=n^{-1}\sum_j M_j$, $\widehat M_j=M_j-\bar M$, $S=n^{-1}\widehat M\widehat M^T$, and write $l_1\ge\cdots\ge l_p\ge0$ for the sample eigenvalues. Assume $p/n\to\gamma\in(0,\infty)$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Determine the asymptotic distributions of the leading sample eigenvalues $l_1,l_2,\ldots$ for these real spiked populations, identifying their appropriate centerings, scalings and joint laws throughout the different spike regimes, including the transition at $\eta=1+\sqrt\gamma$. [S2]

Short English statement

Find the limiting eigenvalue statistics for high-dimensional real Gaussian covariance matrices with finitely many population spikes, including phase transitions.
