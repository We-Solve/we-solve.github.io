Kirby Problem 4.90

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,\omega)$ be a closed symplectic 4-manifold with its symplectic orientation. For an $\omega$-compatible almost complex structure $J$, define \[ c_1^2(X)=\langle c_1(TX,J)^2,[X]\rangle,\qquad c_2(X)=\langle c_2(TX,J),[X]\rangle. \] The supplied identities are $c_1^2=2\chi(X)+3\sigma(X)$ and $c_2=\chi(X)$, where $\sigma$ is the signature with that orientation. The choice of compatible $J$ does not change these numbers.

For every such $X$ outside the intended ruled-surface class, the three source questions are:

  1. $c_1^2(X)\leq3c_2(X)$.
  2. $c_1^2(X)=3c_2(X)>0$ if and only if $X$ is $\mathbb{CP}^2$ or a complex ball quotient.
  3. $c_2(X)\geq0$.
A closed smooth complex ball quotient means $\mathbb B^2_{\mathbb{C}}/\Gamma$, where $\Gamma$ is a torsion-free cocompact discrete group of holomorphic isometries of the complex unit ball. Since $X$ initially has only a symplectic structure, the meaning of "is" in part (b) must be fixed, for example oriented diffeomorphism type rather than equality of chosen complex structures. The source does not state this equivalence explicitly. Connectedness is also implicit in the usual equality classification; if disconnected manifolds are allowed, a componentwise formulation is needed.

The meaning of ruled must likewise be fixed: an $S^2$-bundle over a closed surface, versus a class also including its blowups, yields different domains. The background uses Kähler ruled-surface language and a separate minimality discussion but does not give an explicit definition. No extra minimality or $b_2^+>1$ restriction is silently added to the main three questions. Minimal in that discussion means that there is no symplectically embedded sphere of self-intersection $-1$.

The source reports the three claims for Kähler surfaces, with part (a) the Bogomolov--Miyaoka--Yau inequality and the equality case linked to Yau's theorem. It attributes part (c)'s symplectic conjecture to Gompf and reports $c_1^2\geq0$ for minimal nonruled symplectic manifolds from Taubes and Li--Liu, so (a) would imply (c) in that case. It describes potential consequences for exotic symplectic manifolds homeomorphic to $\mathbb{CP}^2$ or to ruled surfaces over genus at least two, without claiming those consequences established. It also asks whether hypothetical non-Kähler equality examples might be aspherical, as a separate direction associated with Problem 4.91.

The background reports Auroux's branched-cover approach, expressing geography through a singular symplectic branch curve in $\mathbb{CP}^2$, with nodes, simple cusps and covering data; no missing numerical formula is invented. It proposes the broader Seiberg--Witten question for closed manifolds with $b_2^+>1$ and nontrivial invariants, and describes Feehan--Leness's announced program using non-Abelian monopoles and adaptations of Hitchin's Morse-theoretic analysis. These are approaches and proposed extensions, not completed proofs of (a).

The registry status is open. The supplied triage dated 2026-08-17 labels the row partially solved, records the topological identities and complex ball equality examples, and reports no full symplectic proof of the sharp inequalities or equality classification. No announced program, Kähler theorem or current status is independently verified here.

Short English statement

For closed nonruled symplectic 4-manifolds, ask for the BMY Chern-number inequality, its positive equality classification, and nonnegative Euler characteristic. The source records Kähler results and symplectic partial constraints, but the ruled and equality-equivalence conventions must be explicit, and announced proof programs are not full solutions.
