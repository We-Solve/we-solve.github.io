Math-7149

Fractional Coloring of Subcubic Triangle-Free Planar Graphs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is formalized below under the conventional interpretation of graphs as finite, undirected, and simple. The source does not explicitly specify these conventions; extension to other graph domains is unresolved in this draft.

Let $G=(V,E)$ be such a graph. The graph is planar if it admits a drawing in the plane in which edges meet only at common endpoints. It is triangle-free if it contains no cycle of length three. For $v\in V$, let $\deg_G(v)$ denote the number of vertices adjacent to $v$. Let \[ \mathcal I(G)=\{I\subseteq V: \text{no two distinct vertices of }I\text{ are adjacent}\} \] be the family of independent sets of $G$. A fractional coloring assigns a weight $w_I\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}$ to every $I\in\mathcal I(G)$, subject to \[ \sum_{\substack{I\in\mathcal I(G)\\v\in I}} w_I\geq 1 \qquad\text{for every }v\in V. \] The fractional chromatic number is \[ \chi_f(G)=\min\left\{\sum_{I\in\mathcal I(G)}w_I: w_I\geq 0\text{ for every }I\in\mathcal I(G),\quad \sum_{\substack{I\in\mathcal I(G)\\v\in I}}w_I\geq 1\text{ for every }v\in V\right\}. \] This definition gives $\chi_f(G)=0$ when $V=\varnothing$.

Question. Is it true that, for every finite simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$, \[ \left(G\text{ is planar and triangle-free, and } \deg_G(v)\leq 3\text{ for every }v\in V\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \chi_f(G)\leq\frac{8}{3}\,? \] No connectedness, regularity, or lower bound on the number of vertices is required in this formulation.

The supplied record labels the problem partially solved and describes improved fractional upper bounds while retaining the proposed $8/3$ bound as conjectural. These are supplied dated literature assessments, not independently verified mathematical or status claims.

Short English statement

Does every triangle-free planar graph in which each vertex has at most three neighbors have fractional chromatic number at most $8/3$? The displayed formulation uses finite simple undirected graphs, a convention not explicitly specified in the supplied source. The supplied partially solved label does not establish the proposed bound.
