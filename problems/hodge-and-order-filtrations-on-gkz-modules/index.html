Hodge and Order Filtrations on GKZ Modules

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write the intended integer column configuration as $A=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)$, with $a_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}^{d+1}$, solely to explain the semigroup notation in the record. Here $m$ denotes the number of columns; the record does not identify it with its symbol $n$. Define \[ {\mathbb{N}} A=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{m}u_j a_j : u_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\right\}\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}^{d+1}. \] For $c=(c_0,\ldots,c_d)\in{\mathbb{N}} A$, the integer $c_0$ is its first component. The notation $M_A^\beta$ denotes the GKZ module with configuration $A$ and parameter $\beta$, as named in the source. Its ambient space, defining convention, and the admissible configurations $A$ are not specified in the supplied record.

An increasing filtration on a module $M$ is a family $(F_pM)_{p\in{\mathbb{Z}}}$ with $F_pM\subseteq F_{p+1}M$. The source uses $F^H$ for a Hodge filtration and $F^{\mathrm{ord}}$ for an order filtration. In a specified cyclic differential-operator presentation $M=D/I$, the usual order filtration is the image of operators of order at most $p$ in $M$, with zero terms for $p<0$. The record supplies neither such a presentation and its normalization nor a Hodge-module realization determining $F^H$.

Recorded assertion, subject to unresolved hypotheses. For the intended configurations and parameters $c\in{\mathbb{N}} A$, the source asserts, for every $p\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, \[ F^H_p M_A^0=F^{\mathrm{ord}}_{p+d}M_A^0, \qquad F^H_p M_A^{-c}=F^{\mathrm{ord}}_{p-n-c_0}M_A^{-c}. \] These are equalities of filtration terms on the respective modules. The symbol $n$, the class of admissible configurations, and any additional restrictions on $c$ remain unspecified. In particular, rank, homogeneity, normality or saturation hypotheses cannot be inferred from the displayed assertion. Consequently, this is a faithful transcription of the proposed identities, not a fully specified universally quantified conjecture.

The supplied background instead discusses a GKZ weight-filtration conjecture and a full saturated-semigroup assertion. It gives no formulation of that assertion or explanation connecting it to these Hodge-filtration identities. That background qualification is retained as unresolved.

Short English statement

The recorded claim says that the Hodge filtrations on $M_A^0$ and $M_A^{-c}$ agree with their order filtrations after shifts by $d$ and $-n-c_0$, respectively, where $c\in{\mathbb{N}} A$. The allowed configurations, the meaning of $n$, and the Hodge structures are unspecified. The supplied partial-solution assessment concerns weight filtrations, whose connection to these identities requires review.
