Kourovka Notebook Problem 7.49

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $G$ be a finitely generated group. Let $N\trianglelefteq G$ be a minimal normal subgroup: $N\ne\{1\}$, and every subgroup $M\trianglelefteq G$ with $M\le N$ is either $\{1\}$ or $N$. Assume that $N$ is an elementary abelian $p$-group, meaning that it is abelian and every nonidentity element has order $p$; no finiteness assumption is imposed on $N$.

For a finite generating set $S$ of $G$, define the ambient word length and the relative ball-counting function by \[ \ell_S(g)=\min\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}:g=s_1\cdots s_k,\ s_i\in S\cup S^{-1}\}, \qquad \gamma_{N,S}(n)=\left|\{x\in N:\ell_S(x)\le n\}\right| \quad(n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}), \] where the empty product is the identity.

Is it true that, for every such $p,G,N$ and every finite generating set $S$ of $G$, either $N$ is finite or there exist constants $C>0$, $a>1$, and $n_0\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}$ such that \[ \gamma_{N,S}(n)\ge C a^n \qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge n_0? \] Here the source's unspecified "growth function for the elements of $N$" is interpreted as counting elements of $N$ in ambient word balls, and "bounded below by an exponential function" is interpreted as the eventual lower bound displayed above. The constants may depend on $G,N,S$. This exponential lower-bound property is independent of the choice of finite generating set, so the formulation also expresses the question for a chosen finite generating set.

Short English statement

For a finitely generated group $G$, must every infinite minimal normal subgroup that is an elementary abelian $p$-group have exponentially many elements in the word balls of $G$? This uses ambient word length and an eventual exponential lower bound. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified.
