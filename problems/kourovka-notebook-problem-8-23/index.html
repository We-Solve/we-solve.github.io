Math-6441

Kourovka Notebook Problem 8.23

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $G$ has a section isomorphic to a group $K$ if there exist a subgroup $H\leq G$ and a normal subgroup $N\trianglelefteq H$ such that $H/N\cong K$. Let \[ D=\langle r,s\mid r^9=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^{-1}\rangle \] be the dihedral group of order $18$.

Is the following implication true for every pair of groups $A$ and $B$? \[ \begin{aligned} &\exists H\leq A\times B\;\exists N\trianglelefteq H:\ H/N\cong D\\ &\quad\Longrightarrow \bigl(\exists H_A\leq A\;\exists N_A\trianglelefteq H_A:\ H_A/N_A\cong D\bigr)\\ &\qquad\qquad\lor \bigl(\exists H_B\leq B\;\exists N_B\trianglelefteq H_B:\ H_B/N_B\cong D\bigr)? \end{aligned} \] No finiteness assumption on $A$ or $B$ is stated in the supplied source record.

Short English statement

If the dihedral group of order $18$ is a quotient of a subgroup of $A\times B$, must it also be a quotient of a subgroup of at least one factor?
