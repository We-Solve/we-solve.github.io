Continuous-Spectrum Analysis of Nelson-Type Models

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
$H$ is the selfadjoint interacting Hamiltonian of the report's Nelson or nonrelativistic-QED model; $\sigma(H)$ is its spectrum and $\sigma_{\mathrm{pp}}(H)$ the set of its eigenvalues. $H_{\mathrm{el}}$ is the electronic Hamiltonian and $\Sigma_{\mathrm{el}}=\inf\sigma(H_{\mathrm{el}})$ is the threshold named in the report. A limiting-absorption principle establishes suitably weighted boundary values of $(H-E\mp i\varepsilon)^{-1}$ as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ on continuous spectral energies; local-decay estimates control the corresponding time evolution.
Source scope and qualifications.
The curated QED energy set reverses the inequality in primary Remark 6. The report specifies (sigma(H) minus sigma_pp(H)) intersected with energies below Sigma_el; the catalogue instead removes the interval below Sigma_el. Both are displayed explicitly and cannot be treated as equivalent.
Primary-source energy range.
Remark 6 requests $\sigma(H)\setminus\sigma_{\mathrm{pp}}(H)$ for the Nelson model and $\left(\sigma(H)\setminus\sigma_{\mathrm{pp}}(H)\right)\cap(-\infty,\Sigma_{\mathrm{el}})$ for the standard nonrelativistic-QED model. This is the source-faithful range, distinct from the preserved catalogue formula below.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Can the limiting-absorption and local-decay method be extended to $\sigma(H)\setminus\sigma_{\mathrm{pp}}(H)$ for the Nelson model and to $\sigma(H)\setminus(\sigma_{\mathrm{pp}}(H)\cup(-\infty,\Sigma_{\mathrm{el}}))$ for the standard model of nonrelativistic QED, where $\Sigma_{\mathrm{el}}=\inf\sigma(H_{\mathrm{el}})$?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Later spectral work proves limiting-absorption and continuous-spectrum results for important Nelson-model regimes, including below multi-boson thresholds, but the full extension to the general energy intervals stated in the conjecture is not established by the targeted search. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.
Scope and source qualification.
The curated QED energy set reverses the inequality in primary Remark 6. The report specifies (sigma(H) minus sigma_pp(H)) intersected with energies below Sigma_el; the catalogue instead removes the interval below Sigma_el. Both are displayed explicitly and cannot be treated as equivalent.

Short English statement

Extend the report's continuous-spectrum resolvent and decay method to its larger proposed energy ranges.
