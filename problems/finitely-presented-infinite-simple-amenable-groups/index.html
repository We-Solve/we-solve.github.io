OpenAI's claim

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
The existence claim is \[ \exists G:\quad |G|=\infty,\quad G\text{ finitely presented},\quad G\text{ simple},\quad G\text{ amenable}. \] Here simplicity excludes proper nontrivial normal subgroups. Both ordinary finite presentability and infinitude are part of the assertion.

Outline of the argument

Boolean polygons over a quadratic ring define a Stone space and piecewise translation tables. Track and slot comparisons give perfection and simplicity, while finite presentation is obtained from the controlled polygon moves and the translation geometry supplies amenability.
