Trimer Covers in the Triangular Grid — Problem 20

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T_n$ denote the region and let "stones" denote the allowed tiles intended in the supplied source. The supplied record does not define either object, the permitted tile orientations, or the equivalence convention for counting tilings. Consequently, the following formulation is conditional on recovering those definitions from the cited source; it does not specify a complete tiling model.

With those definitions fixed, let \[ a_n=\#\{\text{tilings of }T_n\text{ by stones}\}, \qquad S=\{n\in{\mathbb{N}}:n\equiv 0,2,9,11\pmod{12}\}. \] For a positive integer $a$, define \[ v_2(a)=\max\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:2^k\mid a\}, \] and set $v_2(0)=+\infty$. The source asks for the numbers $a_n$, and specifically asks whether \[ \lim_{\substack{n\to\infty\\n\in S}}a_n=0 \quad\text{in the }2\text{-adic topology}. \] Equivalently, is it true that \[ \forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\;\exists N\in{\mathbb{N}}\;\forall n\in S, \qquad n\geq N\Longrightarrow 2^m\mid a_n? \] This is also equivalent to $v_2(a_n)\to+\infty$ along $S$, with the convention above for zero counts.

As motivation, the supplied source associates the counts with OEIS A334875 and reports that the valuations of its nonzero terms begin \[ 0,0,1,3,2,3,4,3,4,3,5,8,6,8,\ldots. \] The record does not supply the indices corresponding to these values. Its comparison with the decreasing divisibility frequencies of randomly chosen positive integers is motivational, not a probabilistic hypothesis. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied source data, not an independently verified status assessment.

Short English statement

Count the tilings of the intended triangular-grid region $T_n$ by the intended stone tiles. Must these counts eventually be divisible by every fixed power of $2$, as $n$ tends to infinity through the residue classes $0,2,9,11\pmod{12}$? The supplied record omits the definitions needed to determine the tiling counts.
