Duplicate Bestvina-Complex Retraction Question

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: "Is the Bestvina complex an equivariant deformation retract of the Davis complex?" The record does not specify the acting group, the constructions of the two complexes, their hypotheses, or an inclusion between them. Consequently, the following gives the precise retraction condition once that missing context is supplied; it does not select or assert an intended class of examples.

Let $G$ be a group acting continuously on spaces $\Sigma$ and $B$, intended to denote the Davis complex and the Bestvina complex in the unspecified source setting. Suppose a $G$-equivariant topological embedding $i\colon B\hookrightarrow\Sigma$ has been specified. To say that $i(B)$ is a $G$-equivariant deformation retract of $\Sigma$ means that there exist continuous maps \[ r\colon\Sigma\longrightarrow B, \qquad H\colon\Sigma\times[0,1]\longrightarrow\Sigma \] such that \[ \begin{aligned} r(i(b))&=b &&(b\in B),\\ r(gx)&=g\,r(x) &&(g\in G,\ x\in\Sigma),\\ H(x,0)&=x &&(x\in\Sigma),\\ H(x,1)&=i(r(x)) &&(x\in\Sigma),\\ H(i(b),t)&=i(b) &&(b\in B,\ t\in[0,1]),\\ H(gx,t)&=g\,H(x,t) &&(g\in G,\ x\in\Sigma,\ t\in[0,1]). \end{aligned} \] Here the action on $[0,1]$ is trivial. Thus the conditional question is whether such $r$ and $H$ exist for the intended complexes and inclusion. This uses the convention that a deformation retraction fixes the retract throughout the homotopy; the supplied record does not establish the source's convention or specify whether an inclusion is prescribed or is to be constructed.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Davis complex can be continuously deformed onto the Bestvina complex, compatibly with the group action and while fixing the retract throughout. The acting group, constructions, hypotheses, and inclusion are unspecified.
