Math-74

Fargues-Scholze Categorical Local Langlands Equivalence

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix distinct primes $p$ and $\ell$, a finite extension $E/{\mathbb{Q}}_p$, and a quasi-split reductive group $G$ over $E$. Let $q$ be the cardinality of the residue field of $E$, and put $K=\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}_\ell$, following the cleaned statement. Fix a square root of $q$ in $K$ and a Whittaker datum $\mathfrak w$ for $G$; these are normalization data for the conjecture. A Whittaker datum is a $G(E)$-conjugacy class of a pair consisting of an $E$-rational Borel subgroup and a nondegenerate character of the $E$-points of its unipotent radical.

Write $W_E$ for the Weil group of $E$, $\widehat G$ for the Langlands dual group with the Weil action used in the parameter construction, and $\operatorname{Bun}_G$ for the stack of $G$-bundles on the Fargues--Fontaine curve in the Fargues--Scholze setting. Write \[ \mathcal P_G=\left[Z^1(W_E,\widehat G)_K/\widehat G\right] \] for the stack of $L$-parameters intended in the source. Here the notation $Z^1$ refers to Weil-group cocycles: formally, their cocycle relation and change by $h\in\widehat G$ are \[ c(ww')=c(w)\,w\left(c(w')\right),\qquad (h\cdot c)(w)=h\,c(w)\,w(h)^{-1}. \] These relations explain the quotient notation; they do not specify the continuity conditions or the derived moduli structure, which are not supplied in the record.

Let $D_{\mathrm{lis}}(\operatorname{Bun}_G,K)$ denote the derived category of lisse $\ell$-adic sheaves intended by the source, and let $\operatorname{IndCoh}(\mathcal P_G)$ denote its stated category of ind-coherent sheaves on the parameter stack. For $V\in\operatorname{Rep}_K(\widehat G)$, write $T_V$ for the Fargues--Scholze Hecke operator and $\mathcal V_V$ for the associated vector bundle on $\mathcal P_G$, using the source's conventions.

Conjecture attributed to Fargues--Scholze. With the normalization determined by $\mathfrak w$ and the chosen square root of $q$, construct an equivalence \[ \Phi_{\mathfrak w}:D_{\mathrm{lis}}(\operatorname{Bun}_G,K) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \operatorname{IndCoh}(\mathcal P_G) \] which, for every $V\in\operatorname{Rep}_K(\widehat G)$, intertwines the Hecke operator with tensoring by the corresponding vector bundle. In the displayed notation this asks for natural equivalences \[ \Phi_{\mathfrak w}\circ T_V \simeq \left(\mathcal V_V\otimes_K^{\mathbf L} -\right)\circ\Phi_{\mathfrak w}, \] where tensoring means the action of the vector bundle on ind-coherent sheaves.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The displays retain the target and quantifier stated in the supplied record. The extraction of the original formula is corrupted, and the record does not determine the precise categorical conventions, any singular-support restriction on $\operatorname{IndCoh}$, or the full definition of the parameter stack. Moreover, for a general quasi-split group, the Weil action matters: the record does not explain how every representation of $\widehat G$ supplies the asserted bundle and Hecke endofunctor, or whether additional Weil-equivariant or $L$-group data and a relative Hecke formulation are intended. Consequently these displays give the supplied conjectural shape, with those constructions still requiring clarification; no missing hypotheses or support conditions are asserted here. The cleaned statement uses $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}_\ell$, whereas the extracted original renders the coefficient field and square-root condition ambiguously.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks for a Whittaker-normalized equivalence between a category of sheaves on the stack of $G$-bundles on the Fargues--Fontaine curve and a category of ind-coherent sheaves on the stack of local Langlands parameters. It should carry every stated Hecke operator to tensoring by its corresponding vector bundle. The exact spectral category and the Hecke compatibility for general quasi-split groups remain insufficiently specified in the supplied extraction. This is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
