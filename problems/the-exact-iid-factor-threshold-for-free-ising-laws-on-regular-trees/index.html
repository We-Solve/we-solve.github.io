The exact IID-factor threshold for free Ising laws on regular trees

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The free Ising law on a $d$-regular tree is claimed to be a factor of iid exactly for $\tanh\beta\leq(d-1)^{-1/2}$, including equality.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
On the $d$-regular tree, $d\ge3$, the free zero-field Ising law has edge correlation $\theta=\tanh\beta$. A factor of iid is a measurable map of independent uniform vertex labels to spins commuting with automorphisms. The claimed threshold is \[ \mu_{d,\beta}\text{ factor of iid}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}\qquad(\beta\ge0). \] In that range the map can be Borel and equivariant on every label input. Finitary dependence or a coding-radius estimate is not asserted.

Outline of the argument

Posterior branch messages control how binary information combines and how intact side branches attenuate responses. Time-integrable response rows make an infinite Brownian innovation equation invertible; the resulting label-to-spin rule respects every tree automorphism even at criticality.

Formal verification
