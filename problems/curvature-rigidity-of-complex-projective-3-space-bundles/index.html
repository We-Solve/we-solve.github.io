Math-7014

Curvature Rigidity of Complex Projective 3-Space Bundles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb{CP}^{3}$ denote the space of complex lines in ${\mathbb{C}}^{4}$, and let $\pi\colon M\to S^{1}$ be a smooth fiber bundle with fiber $\mathbb{CP}^{3}$. For a diffeomorphism $\tau\colon\mathbb{CP}^{3}\to\mathbb{CP}^{3}$, define its mapping torus by \[ T_{\tau}=\left(\mathbb{CP}^{3}\times[0,1]\right)\big/\left((x,1)\sim(\tau(x),0)\right). \] This explains the notation $S^{1}\times_{\tau}\mathbb{CP}^{3}$ used in the supplied statement. However, the supplied record does not define its intended $\tau$. The questions below therefore retain an unspecified intended diffeomorphism $\tau$; they do not quantify universally or existentially over $\tau$.

A smoothly varying family of metrics on the fibers means a smooth positive definite symmetric bilinear form $h$ on the vertical tangent bundle $\ker({\,\mathrm{d}}\pi)$. It induces a Riemannian metric $h_b$ on each fiber $M_b=\pi^{-1}(b)$. Here the phrase "positively curved" is interpreted as positive sectional curvature on each fiber; this interpretation requires confirmation against the source.

For every such smooth bundle, the supplied record asks the following two separate questions:

  1. If there exists a Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$ such that $\sec_g(\sigma)\geq 0$ for every point $p\in M$ and every two-dimensional subspace $\sigma\subset T_pM$, must \[ M\cong S^{1}\times\mathbb{CP}^{3}\quad\text{or}\quad M\cong T_{\tau} \] hold, where $\cong$ denotes diffeomorphism of total spaces?
  2. If there exists a smooth vertical metric $h$ such that $\sec_{h_b}(\sigma)>0$ for every $b\in S^{1}$, every $p\in M_b$, and every two-dimensional subspace $\sigma\subset T_pM_b$, must the same diffeomorphism conclusion hold?
The second question is stated independently of the first curvature hypothesis. The conclusion as supplied concerns diffeomorphism, without requiring preservation of the bundle projection. Identification of the intended $\tau$ is necessary to make the proposed classification fully specified.

Short English statement

For a smooth $\mathbb{CP}^{3}$-bundle over the circle, ask separately whether nonnegative sectional curvature on its total space, or a smooth family of positively sectionally curved fiber metrics, forces its total space to be diffeomorphic to the product or the specified twisted mapping torus. The record leaves the twisting diffeomorphism $\tau$ undefined.
