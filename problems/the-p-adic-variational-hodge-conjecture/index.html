The p-Adic Variational Hodge Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p>0$ be a prime, let $k$ be a perfect field of characteristic $p$, and let $W=W(k)$ be its ring of Witt vectors. Let $X$ be a smooth projective $W$-scheme, with special fiber $X_1=X\times_{\operatorname{Spec}W}\operatorname{Spec}k$ and inclusion $i:X_1\hookrightarrow X$. For a scheme $Y$, write $CH^r(Y)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=CH^r(Y)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}$ for the group of codimension-$r$ algebraic cycles modulo rational equivalence with rational coefficients. Here $r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ is the conventional codimension range; the supplied statement gives no explicit range for $r$ and imposes no bound relating $r$ to $p$. The restriction $i^*:CH^r(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\to CH^r(X_1)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ is the refined Gysin pullback for the special-fiber inclusion.

Write $H^m_{\mathrm{dR}}(X/W)=\mathbb H^m(X,\Omega^\bullet_{X/W})$, and use a subscript ${\mathbb{Q}}$ to denote tensoring over ${\mathbb{Z}}$ with ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The Hodge filtration is \[ F^rH^{2r}_{\mathrm{dR}}(X/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}} =\operatorname{im}\!\left( \mathbb H^{2r}(X,\Omega^{\geq r}_{X/W})\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}} \longrightarrow H^{2r}_{\mathrm{dR}}(X/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}} \right). \] Let \[ \operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{cris}}:CH^r(X_1)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\longrightarrow H^{2r}_{\mathrm{cris}}(X_1/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}} \] be the crystalline cycle-class map, and let \[ c_X:H^{2r}_{\mathrm{cris}}(X_1/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\xrightarrow{\sim}H^{2r}_{\mathrm{dR}}(X/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}} \] be the crystalline--de Rham comparison associated with the smooth proper lift $X$.

Question (the supplied p-adic variational Hodge conjecture). For every such $p,k,X,r$ and every $\xi_1\in CH^r(X_1)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, does \[ c_X\left(\operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{cris}}(\xi_1)\right) \in F^rH^{2r}_{\mathrm{dR}}(X/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}} \] imply that there exists $\xi\in CH^r(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{cris}}(i^*\xi) =\operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{cris}}(\xi_1) \quad\text{in }H^{2r}_{\mathrm{cris}}(X_1/W)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}? \] The requested equality is equality of crystalline cycle classes; it does not require $i^*\xi=\xi_1$ in the Chow group. The supplied original statement phrases this implication declaratively, whereas the normalized statement asks whether it holds. It is retained here as a conjectural question. The supplied partial-solution and verification labels are curation claims, not mathematical conclusions established by this draft.

Short English statement

Given a smooth projective lift over a Witt ring, must every rational cycle on its special fiber whose crystalline class satisfies the indicated Hodge-filtration condition have the same crystalline class as the restriction of a rational cycle on the lift? Equality of the cycles in the Chow group is not requested. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this statement-only draft requires human mathematical review.
