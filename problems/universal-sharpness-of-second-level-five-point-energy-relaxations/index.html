Universal Sharpness of Second-Level Five-Point Energy Relaxations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ S^2=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^3:\|x\|_2=1\}. \] The supplied record concerns the minimum pair-interaction energy of five particles on this sphere. Let $\mathcal C_5\subseteq(S^2)^5$ denote the intended admissible configurations, let $\mathcal P$ denote the intended class of pair potentials, and let $\mathcal E_f(X)$ denote the intended total pair-interaction energy of a configuration $X\in\mathcal C_5$ for $f\in\mathcal P$. These are placeholders for definitions missing from the record. Write \[ E_{\min}(f)=\inf_{X\in\mathcal C_5}\mathcal E_f(X). \] Denote the value of the source's second-level relaxation by $E_2(f)$.

Conjecture as supplied, with unresolved parameters. Is this relaxation universally sharp, in the sense that \[ \forall f\in\mathcal P,\qquad E_2(f)=E_{\min}(f)? \] The quantifier permits the minimizing configurations, if they exist, to depend on $f$; it does not assert the existence of one configuration minimizing every potential.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define the relaxation $E_2$, its feasible objects or constraints, or its objective normalization. It also does not specify the potential class $\mathcal P$, the argument of a potential, or the formula and normalization of $\mathcal E_f$. In particular, it does not say whether potentials act on chordal distance, squared chordal distance, or another quantity. The accompanying literature assessment mentions completely monotone potentials, but does not supply the interval, endpoint conditions, or the precise relation of that class to the statement's "prescribed class." In standard terminology, complete monotonicity on an open interval $I$ means that $f\in C^\infty(I)$ and \[ (-1)^k f^{(k)}(t)\geq0\qquad(k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\ t\in I); \] this terminology alone does not determine the missing potential class here. Nor does the record specify whether coincident particles are excluded, how singular potentials are handled, or whether the energy infimum is attained. Finally, the assessment refers to a "four-point bound," without defining it or establishing its identification with $E_2$. Thus the displayed equality preserves the intended universal-sharpness question but is a schematic formulation requiring the missing source definitions before it becomes a fully specified mathematical conjecture.

Short English statement

For five particles on the unit sphere, the supplied conjecture asks whether the second-level relaxation gives exactly the least possible pair-interaction energy for every potential in the intended class. The relaxation, potential class, energy convention, and admissibility conditions are insufficiently specified in the supplied record. Its background mentions completely monotone potentials and a four-point bound, but their precise roles remain unresolved.
