Math-6555

Exceptional-Unit Bounds by Algebraic Degree

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\geq 7$ be an integer and let $\alpha$ be an algebraic integer with $[{\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha):{\mathbb{Q}}]=d$. Put $K={\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha)$, and let $\mathcal O_K$ denote its ring of integers. Interpret a unit as an element of $\mathcal O_K^{\times}$, that is, an element having a multiplicative inverse in $\mathcal O_K$.

The source defines $E_0(\alpha)=0$ if $\alpha-1$ is not a unit; otherwise it defines \[ E_0(\alpha)=\max\left\{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:\ \alpha^j-1\in\mathcal O_K^{\times}\text{ for every integer }1\leq j\leq n\right\}, \] provided this largest integer exists. It then writes \[ e(d)=\max\left\{E_0(\alpha):\ \alpha\text{ is an algebraic integer and }[{\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha):{\mathbb{Q}}]=d\right\}. \] The supplied conjecture is $e(d)<d$ for every integer $d\geq 7$.

A precise formulation of the intended bound, without presupposing existence of these maxima, is: \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every integer }d\geq 7\text{ and every algebraic integer }\alpha \text{ with }[{\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha):{\mathbb{Q}}]=d,\\ \text{there exists an integer }j\text{ with }1\leq j\leq d \text{ such that }\alpha^j-1\notin\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha)}^{\times}. \end{gathered} \] This formulation expresses the supplied inequality whenever the source's maxima are defined; if true, it also ensures that those maxima exist in the stated degree range. The supplied record does not explain their existence separately. The ring used for units is the standard number-field interpretation stated above; the source excerpt does not specify it explicitly.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every algebraic integer of degree $d\geq 7$ has at least one nonunit among $\alpha-1,\alpha^2-1,\ldots,\alpha^d-1$. The supplied record labels this problem open; that status and its source-verification claim have not been independently checked.
