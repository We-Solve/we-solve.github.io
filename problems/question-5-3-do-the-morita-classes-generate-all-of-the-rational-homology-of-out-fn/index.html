Question 5.3 — Do the Morita classes generate all of the rational homology of Out(Fn)?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$. For each degree $d>0$, let $M_{n,d}$ be the ${\mathbb{Q}}$-linear span in $H_d(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);{\mathbb{Q}})$ of the Morita classes meant by the source. The construction and exact scope of that family are not reproduced in the excerpt.

Question 5.3. Do the Morita classes generate all rational homology of $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$? In the natural vector-space reading, this asks \[ M_{n,d}=H_d(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);{\mathbb{Q}}) \quad\text{for every relevant rank }n\text{ and degree }d>0, \] with the canonical degree-zero class added when speaking of total homology. No product structure on fixed-rank group homology is supplied, so "generate" is not silently interpreted as generation of a homology ring. The genus/rank indexing and whether generalized Morita classes are included require the missing construction.

The source gives the following context. It describes the largest degree of a Morita class as approximately $4n/3$, not a precise formula. It says Morita cycles lift naturally to $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$, with the first two nontrivial in homology. Galatius's stable rational-homology result is invoked for their eventual disappearance, and Conant--Vogtmann [25] are credited with their disappearance after a single stabilization \[ s_n:\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Aut}(F_{n+1}). \] A universal generation result would, according to the source, improve the available stable-range bound. It also mentions conjectural connections with the Johnson homomorphism, homology-cobordism classes of homology cylinders, and the motivic Lie algebra associated with the algebraic mapping class group.

The statement that stable rational homology is trivial concerns positive degrees, not $H_0$. Likewise, the source's assertion that natural mapping-class/outer-automorphism/linear-group maps are zero in this discussion concerns their maps on positive-degree stable rational homology, not the underlying group homomorphisms. It notes that unstable maps may be nonzero, citing \[ H_8(\mathrm{GL}_6({\mathbb{Z}});{\mathbb{Q}})\cong{\mathbb{Q}} \] from [30], then introduces a following question absent here.

The registry summary claims later non-Morita unstable classes give a negative direction, but names no such class or primary citation. A verified class outside the Morita span would disprove generation without computing all rational homology. The missing complete homology computation therefore cannot by itself determine the status of this specific generation assertion.

Short English statement

Ask whether the specified Morita cycles span every positive-degree rational homology group of free-group outer automorphism groups. The source discusses stabilization and unstable maps; its summary's later counterexample claim lacks a verifiable witness or citation.
