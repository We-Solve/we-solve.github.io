Math-1569

Borel completeness after naming a constant

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Question 24 asks whether Borel completeness of $\cong_T$ implies Borel completeness of $\cong_{T'}$, but the excerpt omits the relation of $T'$ to $T$. The aid uses the one-named-constant interpretation: $T$ is complete in a countable language, $p\in S_1(T)$, and \[ T_p=\operatorname{Th}(M,a)\quad\text{for }M\models T,\ a\models p, \] with one new constant naming $a$. This complete expansion is determined by $p$; it is not the incomplete expansion allowing an arbitrary unnamed type for the constant.

Use standard spaces of countable models with domain $\omega$. For equivalence relations $E,F$, $E\le_B F$ means there is a Borel map reducing equivalence in both directions. An isomorphism relation is Borel complete when every isomorphism relation on countable structures in a countable language Borel reduces to it, equivalently when countable graph isomorphism does. The term does not assert that the isomorphism relation itself is a Borel subset of the square.

Let $\operatorname{Mod}(T)_p$ be the models realizing $p$, and let $E_p$ be pointed isomorphism for $T_p$. Its countable-set Friedman--Stanley jump $E_p^+$ identifies sequences exactly when they enumerate the same set of $E_p$-classes: \[ (x_i)\ E_p^+\ (y_j) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (\forall i\,\exists j\ x_iE_py_j)\ \wedge\ (\forall j\,\exists i\ x_iE_py_j). \] Multiplicity in the sequences is ignored. The aid reports \[ \cong_T|_{\operatorname{Mod}(T)_p}\ \le_B\ E_p^+. \] Its map lists all pointings $(M,a)$ for $a\in p(M)$, with repetitions if only finitely many realize $p$. The restriction to realizing models is essential; not every $T$-model need realize a nonisolated $p$.

A stronger special case removes the jump. Suppose $\mathcal C\subseteq\operatorname{Mod}(T)_p$ is an invariant Borel family on which $\cong_T$ is Borel complete and $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ acts transitively on $p(M)$ for every $M\in\mathcal C$. Then Borel choice of one realization in each countable presentation gives \[ \cong_T|_{\mathcal C}\le_B E_p, \] so $E_p$ is Borel complete. In particular, if a parameter-free formula defines a singleton in every $T$-model, naming that element gives Borel bireducible unpointed and pointed isomorphism relations. The aid does not claim that the transitivity/coding-family condition is automatic. Its general one-constant preservation question remains open in the supplied assessment, and Borel completeness of a jump is not used as a proof of Borel completeness of the underlying relation.

Short English statement

The aid interprets the question as naming one element of a fixed type. It reduces unpointed models realizing that type to sets of pointed isomorphism classes, and gets a direct reduction when the realizations form one automorphism orbit on a suitable coding family. Those conditions are special cases, not a resolution of unrestricted preservation.
