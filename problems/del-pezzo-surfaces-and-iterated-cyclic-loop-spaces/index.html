Del Pezzo Surfaces and Iterated Cyclic Loop Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A del Pezzo surface is a smooth projective surface whose anticanonical divisor is ample. The supplied record denotes a series of such surfaces by $B_k$, but does not specify the base field, the individual surfaces, or their additional geometric structures. Let $S^4=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^5:\sum_{i=1}^{5}x_i^2=1\}$.

A conventional interpretation of a cyclic loop space is the homotopy quotient \[ L_cX=\operatorname{Map}(S^1,X)\times_{S^1}ES^1, \] where $S^1$ acts on free loops by rotation of their parameter and $ES^1$ is a contractible space with a free $S^1$-action. Under this convention, iteration means $L_c^0X=X$ and $L_c^{k+1}X=L_c(L_c^kX)$. This definition is provided only to explain a conventional meaning of the notation: the supplied record does not specify its cyclic-loop construction or whether a rational homotopy model is intended.

Conjectural target as supplied. Find an explicit relation between the series \[ (B_k)_{k=0}^{8}\qquad\text{and}\qquad(L_c^kS^4)_{k=0}^{8} \] that, for every integer $0\leq k\leq8$, matches the $E_k$ symmetry patterns occurring on the two sides and relates further geometric data, such as volumes of curves on the del Pezzo surfaces.

The record does not define the intended relations, the symmetry structures denoted by $E_k$, their matching, or the loop-space quantities corresponding to curve volumes. Curve volumes also require additional geometric data, such as a specified Kähler form in a complex interpretation. Consequently, this is a statement of the supplied research target, not a fully specified existence assertion in a stated category; these qualifications require resolution before a precise theorem-level formulation is possible.

Short English statement

For each $k=0,\ldots,8$, seek an explicit connection between the del Pezzo surface $B_k$ and the iterated cyclic loop space $L_c^kS^4$, explaining their corresponding $E_k$ symmetries and relating geometric quantities such as curve volumes. The supplied record leaves the objects, correspondence, and volume data insufficiently specified for a fully precise mathematical assertion.
