Math-1042

Infinite Families of Nonarithmetic Complex-Hyperbolic Lattices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 2$, let $J=\operatorname{diag}(I_n,-1)$ and define \[ G_n=\operatorname{PU}(n,1)=\{A\in\operatorname{GL}_{n+1}({\mathbb{C}}):A^*JA=J\}/\{\lambda I_{n+1}:|\lambda|=1\}, \] where $A^*$ denotes conjugate transpose. A subgroup $\Gamma\leq G_n$ is a lattice if it is discrete and $\Gamma\backslash G_n$ has finite Haar volume. Both cocompact and noncocompact lattices are allowed.

Two lattices $\Gamma,\Lambda\leq G_n$ are commensurable up to ambient conjugacy if there exists $g\in G_n$ such that $\Gamma\cap g\Lambda g^{-1}$ has finite index in both $\Gamma$ and $g\Lambda g^{-1}$.

Use the standard algebraic-group meaning of arithmeticity: a lattice $\Gamma\leq G_n$ is arithmetic if there exist a connected semisimple algebraic group $\mathbf H$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, a faithful rational representation $\rho:\mathbf H\hookrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_m$, and a surjective continuous homomorphism \[ \phi:\mathbf H({\mathbb{R}})^\circ\longrightarrow G_n \] with compact kernel, such that $\Gamma$ is commensurable up to ambient conjugacy with $\phi(\Delta)$, where \[ \Delta=\{h\in\mathbf H({\mathbb{Q}})\cap\mathbf H({\mathbb{R}})^\circ:\rho(h)\in\operatorname{GL}_m({\mathbb{Z}})\}. \] Here $\mathbf H({\mathbb{R}})^\circ$ is the identity component. A lattice is nonarithmetic if it is not arithmetic in this sense.

Question. Is it true that for every integer $n\geq 2$ there exists a sequence $(\Gamma_{n,j})_{j\geq 1}$ of nonarithmetic lattices in $G_n$ such that, whenever $i\neq j$, the lattices $\Gamma_{n,i}$ and $\Gamma_{n,j}$ are not commensurable up to ambient conjugacy?

The supplied cleaned statement explicitly quantifies over every $n\geq 2$. The source record does not specify its convention for commensurability; the formulation above uses ambient conjugacy in $G_n$, and this convention requires confirmation against the report. The supplied citation labels the workshop $2020$, whereas the proposed year and DOI contain $2019$; this bibliographic discrepancy remains unresolved.

Short English statement

For each integer $n\geq 2$, ask whether $\operatorname{PU}(n,1)$ contains infinitely many pairwise incommensurable nonarithmetic lattices, allowing both compact and noncompact finite-volume quotients. The supplied record labels this question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
