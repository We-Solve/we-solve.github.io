Math-15328

Blockwise Chain Cancellation for Finite Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group and let $p$ be a prime. Under the usual $p$-adic interpretation of the source notation, ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ denotes the ring of $p$-adic integers. Let $b$ be a block idempotent of ${\mathbb{Z}}_pG$, that is, a primitive central idempotent, and assume that $b$ has positive defect, meaning that its defect groups are nontrivial.

For a strict nonempty chain of nontrivial $p$-subgroups \[ \sigma=(P_0<P_1<\cdots<P_r),\qquad 1\ne P_i\le G, \] conjugation by $G$ acts on its terms. Its stabilizer is \[ G_\sigma=\bigcap_{i=0}^{r}N_G(P_i),\qquad N_G(P)=\{g\in G:gPg^{-1}=P\}. \] These definitions describe the usual strict-chain interpretation; the supplied record does not specify its chain convention or whether the empty chain is included. Write $[\mathcal P(G)/G]$ formally for a choice of representatives of the conjugacy classes of chains intended by the source. For a ring $A$, $\operatorname{Mat}_n(A)$ denotes the ring of $n\times n$ matrices over $A$, and $|G:G_\sigma|$ is the subgroup index.

Recorded cancellation question. For every such $G,p,b$, the supplied record asks whether \[ \sum_{\sigma\in[\mathcal P(G)/G]} (-1)^{|\sigma|} \operatorname{Mat}_{|G:G_\sigma|} \left({\mathbb{Q}} G_\sigma b_\sigma\right)=0. \] Unresolved qualifications. This is a transcription of the intended target, not yet a well-defined algebraic equality. The record does not define $b_\sigma$, specify the additive group or other structure in which the matrix-algebra terms are summed, or define the zero on the right. It also does not specify whether $|\sigma|$ counts subgroup terms or strict inclusions, or whether an empty chain contributes. Finally, the formula explicitly uses ${\mathbb{Q}}$, whereas $b$ lies in a $p$-adic group ring and the supplied background describes a $p$-adic assertion. No construction placing $b_\sigma$ in ${\mathbb{Q}} G_\sigma$ is supplied. Replacing ${\mathbb{Q}}$ by ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$, choosing a Grothendieck group, or assigning local block idempotents would require additional source evidence. These choices remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The recorded question asks whether, for every finite group, prime, and positive-defect block, an alternating sum of matrix algebras attached to conjugacy classes of chains of nontrivial $p$-subgroups vanishes. The local block terms, coefficient field, chain conventions, and meaning of the sum require clarification before this becomes a precise conjecture. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
