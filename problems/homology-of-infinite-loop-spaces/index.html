Homology of Infinite Loop Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is \[ \text{How can one compute }H_*(\Omega^\infty X)\text{ from knowledge of }H_*(X)\text{?} \] In the conventional spectrum interpretation of this notation, $X$ is a spectrum and $\Omega^\infty X$ is its derived zeroth infinite loop space. The record does not explicitly specify this domain, a class of admissible spectra, or homology coefficients.

For clarity, one possible interpretation, conditional on choosing a coefficient ring $R$ and ordinary homology, uses \[ H_n(X;R)=\pi_n(HR\wedge X),\qquad n\in{\mathbb{Z}}, \] where $HR$ is the Eilenberg--Mac Lane spectrum of $R$ and the smash product is derived, and \[ H_n(\Omega^\infty X;R)=H_n^{\mathrm{sing}}(\Omega^\infty X;R),\qquad n\geq 0, \] where the right-hand side is singular homology. Under these explicitly conditional conventions, the question asks for a method to compute the latter graded homology from knowledge of the former.

The source does not specify whether the input consists only of graded homology groups or also includes operations or other structure. It also supplies no connectivity, component, localization, or finiteness hypotheses. Consequently, this is a computational research question with unresolved scope, rather than a fully specified assertion that such a method exists or that the output is determined by graded homology groups alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks how to recover the homology of the infinite loop space $\Omega^\infty X$ from the homology of $X$. A precise formulation requires specifying what kind of object $X$ is, the homology theory and coefficients, the permitted hypotheses, and what structure is included in the input. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is supplied dated curation.
