Positively Curved Projective Varieties — Conjecture 2

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold. A Kähler metric on $X$ is a Hermitian Riemannian metric $g$ whose associated real $2$-form $\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)$ is closed, where $J$ is the complex structure. Its holomorphic sectional curvature in a nonzero real tangent direction $v\in T_xX$ is \[ H_g(v)=K_g\left(\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{R}}}\{v,Jv\}\right), \] where $K_g$ denotes Riemannian sectional curvature. Positive holomorphic sectional curvature means \[ H_g(v)>0\qquad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and every }0\ne v\in T_xX. \] The hypothesis is the existence of such a Kähler metric; it does not quantify over all Kähler metrics on $X$.

A complex manifold is projective if it admits a holomorphic embedding into ${\mathbb{P}}^N_{{\mathbb{C}}}$ for some finite integer $N$. A positive-dimensional smooth projective variety is rationally connected if there is a nonempty Zariski-open subset $U\subseteq X$ such that every pair of distinct points of $U$ lies in the image of a morphism ${\mathbb{P}}^1_{{\mathbb{C}}}\to X$. A point is regarded as rationally connected.

Yau's conjecture, as reproduced in Matsumura's Conjecture 2. For every compact Kähler manifold $X$, if $X$ admits a Kähler metric with positive holomorphic sectional curvature, then $X$ is projective and rationally connected.

The supplied statement does not explicitly specify connectedness. The formulation above uses the customary connected interpretation of $X$; if disconnected manifolds are allowed, the asserted conclusion must instead be interpreted for each connected component. This qualification requires source review.

Short English statement

Yau's conjecture, reproduced by Matsumura in 2022, asserts that a compact connected Kähler manifold admitting an everywhere strictly positive holomorphic sectional curvature metric is projective and rationally connected. Connectedness is implicit in this formulation and is not explicit in the supplied source. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified status assessment.
