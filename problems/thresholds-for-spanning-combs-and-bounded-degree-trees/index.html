Math-3630

Thresholds for Spanning Combs and Bounded-Degree Trees

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 2$ and $p\in[0,1]$, let $G(n,p)$ be the random simple graph on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ in which each unordered pair of distinct vertices is an edge independently with probability $p$. A tree is a finite connected simple graph with no cycles. Write $\Delta(T)$ for its maximum vertex degree. An $n$-vertex graph contains a spanning copy of an $n$-vertex tree $T$ if there is a bijection from $V(T)$ to its vertex set that sends every edge of $T$ to an edge of the graph; the copy need not be induced.

The supplied conjecture asks whether the threshold for containing a spanning comb $\operatorname{Comb}_n$, and more generally for containing any prescribed bounded-degree spanning tree, has order \[ t_n=\frac{\log n}{n}. \] Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm; changing its base does not change the proposed order.

One precise fixed-sequence interpretation of the general conjecture is the following. Fix an integer $D\geq 2$ and a deterministic sequence $(T_n)_{n\geq 2}$ of trees satisfying $|V(T_n)|=n$ and $\Delta(T_n)\leq D$. Do there exist constants $0<c<C<\infty$, independent of $n$, such that \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{n\to\infty}\Pr\!\left(G\!\left(n,c\frac{\log n}{n}\right)\text{ contains a spanning copy of }T_n\right)&=0,\\ \lim_{n\to\infty}\Pr\!\left(G\!\left(n,C\frac{\log n}{n}\right)\text{ contains a spanning copy of }T_n\right)&=1? \end{aligned} \] The displayed probabilities are understood for sufficiently large $n$, when their edge probabilities belong to $[0,1]$.

This is an explicit interpretation, not a resolution of the source's missing quantifiers. The source does not specify whether the constants must depend only on $D$, whether they may depend on the prescribed tree sequence, or whether a single random graph is required to contain every tree of maximum degree at most $D$ simultaneously. The simultaneous assertion would be a different formulation and is not imposed here. The source also does not specify a formal definition of threshold order.

The comb clause cannot be made fully precise from this record: $\operatorname{Comb}_n$ is not defined, and its indexing, number of vertices, and any parity restrictions are not stated. Once that family and its admissible indices are specified, its threshold question can be expressed by the corresponding probability limits along those indices. No comb definition or parity convention is assumed in this draft.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether edge probability of order $(\log n)/n$ is the threshold for a random graph to contain a prescribed spanning comb, and more generally a prescribed spanning tree whose maximum degree is bounded independently of $n$. The comb definition and the intended uniformity of the tree quantifiers require clarification. The supplied open-status assessment concerns graph profiles and does not substantiate the status of this tree-embedding conjecture.
