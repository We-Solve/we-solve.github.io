Kourovka Notebook Problem 8.3

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $m,n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, let $p$ be a prime, and put $R_m={\mathbb{Z}}/p^m{\mathbb{Z}}$. Write $\rho:R_m\to{\mathbb{F}}_p$ for reduction modulo $p$, also applied entrywise to matrices. Define \[ U_n({\mathbb{F}}_p)=\{(u_{ij})\in\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{F}}_p):u_{ii}=1\text{ for all }i,\ u_{ij}=0\text{ for }i>j\}. \] The supplied statement describes $P_n(R_m)$ by requiring $a_{ii}\equiv1$ for every $i$, without specifying the modulus, and $a_{ij}\equiv0\pmod p$ for $i>j$. If the diagonal congruence is also modulo $p$, this gives the finite $p$-group \[ P_n(R_m)=\{A=(a_{ij})\in\operatorname{GL}_n(R_m):\rho(A)\in U_n({\mathbb{F}}_p)\}. \] There is no further restriction on entries above the diagonal. This interpretation of the diagonal condition requires confirmation: imposing $a_{ii}=1$ in $R_m$ instead does not in general give a group under matrix multiplication. The formulation below is conditional on the modulo-$p$ interpretation and does not silently resolve the source omission.

For a group $H$, write $H'=[H,H]$ for its commutator subgroup. A finite $p$-group $G$ is called regular if, for every $x,y\in G$, there exist an integer $r\geq0$ and elements $c_1,\ldots,c_r\in\langle x,y\rangle'$ such that \[ (xy)^p=x^py^p c_1^p\cdots c_r^p, \] where the product is the identity when $r=0$.

Problem (B. A. F. Wehrfritz, 1982). For every prime $p$, determine exactly which pairs $(m,n)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}^2$ make $P_n({\mathbb{Z}}/p^m{\mathbb{Z}})$ regular, subject to confirmation of the diagonal congruence above.

Supplied editorial qualifications. The editors' comment dated 2001 reports that regularity holds when $mn<p$, citing Yu. I. Merzlyakov [3]. It reports that A. V. Yagzhev [4] completely treated $m=1$, while explicitly warning that that paper is erroneous for $m>1$. It also reports that S. G. Kolesnikov [5] completed the case $m=2$. The editors' comment dated 2009 reports that Kolesnikov [6] proved regularity for every positive integer $m$ whenever $n^2<p$. These are supplied historical assessments; the complete classifications for $m=1$ and $m=2$ are not stated in the record. The local registry labels the problem open; that label is not an independently verified literature assessment.

Short English statement

For each prime $p$, classify the positive integers $m,n$ for which the indicated matrix group over ${\mathbb{Z}}/p^m{\mathbb{Z}}$ is a regular finite $p$-group. The source omits the modulus in its diagonal condition, so this draft conditionally reads that condition modulo $p$. Preserve the supplied results and warnings for special cases; the general open label has not been independently verified.
