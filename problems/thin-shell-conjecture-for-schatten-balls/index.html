Thin-Shell Conjecture for Schatten Balls

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a standard full-matrix interpretation, fix $\mathbb K\in\{{\mathbb{R}},{\mathbb{C}}\}$, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and regard $M_n(\mathbb K)$ as a real Euclidean space with inner product $\langle A,B\rangle=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(A^*B)$. Its real dimension is $d_n=n^2$ for $\mathbb K={\mathbb{R}}$ and $d_n=2n^2$ for $\mathbb K={\mathbb{C}}$. If $s_1(A),\ldots,s_n(A)$ are the singular values of $A$, define, under the usual normed-space convention $1\leq p\leq\infty$, \[ \|A\|_{S_p}=\left(\sum_{j=1}^n s_j(A)^p\right)^{1/p}\quad(1\leq p<\infty),\qquad \|A\|_{S_\infty}=\max_{1\leq j\leq n}s_j(A), \] and $B_p^n=\{A:\|A\|_{S_p}\leq1\}$. Let $Y_{n,p}$ be uniformly distributed on $B_p^n$ with respect to real Lebesgue measure. This distribution is centered by symmetry. Write $\Sigma_{n,p}$ for its covariance operator and set $X_{n,p}=\Sigma_{n,p}^{-1/2}Y_{n,p}$. Thus $X_{n,p}$ is isotropic and ${\mathbb{E}}\|X_{n,p}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=d_n$, where $\|A\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=\operatorname{tr}(A^*A)$.

A common squared-radius variance formulation of the requested thin-shell bound is the existence of an absolute constant $C<\infty$ such that \[ \operatorname{Var}\!\left(\|X_{n,p}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\right) ={\mathbb{E}}\left(\|X_{n,p}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2-d_n\right)^2 \leq C d_n \] for all dimensions and parameters in the intended family. The supplied question asks whether the thin-shell conjecture holds for the Schatten balls at the remaining values of $p$ outside the known large-$p$ results.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify those remaining values or dimension-dependent ranges, the scalar field, or whether full matrices or a restricted matrix space are intended. Consequently, the definitions above give a conditional standard interpretation, rather than a uniquely recovered source formulation. The record also does not state the exact thin-shell normalization or whether a bound uniform over all remaining $p$ is requested. A fixed-$p$ version would instead ask, for each remaining $p$, for a constant $C_p$ independent of $n$. These missing choices prevent a fully determined quantified statement.

Short English statement

Do the Schatten unit balls satisfy the thin-shell conjecture throughout the parameter ranges left outside the known large-$p$ results? In a standard isotropic variance formulation, this asks for a dimension-independent bound on the variance of squared Euclidean radius divided by dimension. The remaining parameter ranges and several matrix-space and uniformity conventions are unspecified in the supplied record.
