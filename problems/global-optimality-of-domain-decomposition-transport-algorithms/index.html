Shared notation.
Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.

A transport plan couples two probability measures; a transport map $T$ pushes one measure to another, meaning $T_\#\mu(B)=\mu(T^{-1}(B))$. Optimal transport minimizes the expected specified cost among admissible couplings.
