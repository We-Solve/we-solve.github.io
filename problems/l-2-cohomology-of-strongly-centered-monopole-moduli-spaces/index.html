$L^2$-Cohomology of Strongly Centered Monopole Moduli Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A finite-energy monopole on $\mathbb R^3$ consists of a connection $A$ and Higgs field $\Phi$ for the gauge group, in the Bogomolny/monopole setting of the source. Framed moduli divide by gauge transformations tending to the identity at infinity. Magnetic charge is determined by the asymptotic Higgs field: one positive integer $k$ for $SU(2)$, or a vector of $N-1$ charges for maximally broken $SU(N)$. The moduli metric is the $L^2$ inner product on infinitesimal field variations perpendicular to gauge directions. The strongly centered $SU(2)$ moduli space removes the center-of-mass translation and total-phase degrees of freedom, with the cover/discrete quotient fixed in the Sen-conjecture convention. Its reduced $L^2$ cohomology is represented by square-integrable harmonic differential forms for this metric. The charge and residual cyclic-group character sectors are relevant to the dimension question, not merely the dimension of ordinary topological cohomology.
Source scope and qualifications.
The report frames this as Sen's conjecture and seeks metric control at infinity. The freshly consulted Fritzsch--Kottke--Singer Part I abstract describes a partial result and explicitly says that a key compactification theorem is deferred to Part II; it is not taken as proof of the whole charge-general conjecture. The brief report does not state every character-sector formula, so none is invented.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Determine the dimension of the $L^2$-cohomology of the moduli space of strongly centered $\operatorname{SU}(2)$-monopoles on $\mathbb R^3$.
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The Sen conjecture for strongly centered SU(2) monopoles has substantial partial results: Fritzsch, Kottke, and Singer prove the coprime case and part of the predicted L2-cohomology statement, but the full charge-general claim is not established. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Determine the square-integrable cohomology dimensions of strongly centered SU(2) monopole moduli spaces.
