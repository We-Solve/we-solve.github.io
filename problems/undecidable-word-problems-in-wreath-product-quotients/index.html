Math-3386

Undecidable Word Problems in Wreath Product Quotients

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F=F(X)$ be the free group on a finite basis $X$, and let $S$ be a finite set. Write $S^0=\{\varepsilon\}$, where $\varepsilon$ is the empty word, and put $S^*=\bigsqcup_{n\geq 0}S^n$. Let $\operatorname{Sym}(S)$ be the group of permutations of $S$, and define the permutational wreath product by \[ F\wr_S\operatorname{Sym}(S)=F^S\rtimes\operatorname{Sym}(S), \qquad (\sigma\cdot h)_t=h_{\sigma^{-1}(t)}. \] Interpret the supplied wreath recursion as a group homomorphism \[ \phi:F\longrightarrow F^S\rtimes\operatorname{Sym}(S), \qquad \phi(g)=((g_t)_{t\in S},\sigma_g). \] Using the displayed coordinate convention, define actions $\rho_n:F\to\operatorname{Sym}(S^n)$ recursively by \[ \rho_0(g)(\varepsilon)=\varepsilon, \qquad \rho_{n+1}(g)(sw)=\sigma_g(s)\,\rho_n(g_{\sigma_g(s)})(w) \quad(s\in S,\ w\in S^n). \] Here juxtaposition on the right denotes concatenation of a letter and a word. Define \[ N=\bigcap_{n\geq0}\ker\rho_n =\{g\in F:\rho_n(g)(v)=v\text{ for every }n\geq0\text{ and every }v\in S^n\}. \] Thus $N$ is the kernel of the action on $S^*$.

The question is whether there exist a finite basis $X$, a finite set $S$, and a homomorphism $\phi$ as above for which $F/N$ has an undecidable word problem. Explicitly, can these data be chosen so that no algorithm halts on every finite word $u$ over $X\cup X^{-1}$ and correctly decides whether \[ [u]_F\in N, \quad\text{equivalently, whether }[u]_{F/N}=1? \] Undecidability here concerns the word problem of one fixed quotient, not merely a uniform decision problem as $\phi$ varies. No positive lower bound on $|X|$ or $|S|$, nor any additional restriction on $\phi$, is stated in the supplied record. The homomorphism interpretation is needed for the asserted group actions; the complete report passage is not supplied, so any additional hypotheses in its construction remain unverified.

Short English statement

Can a finitely generated free group, acting on all finite words over a finite alphabet through a wreath recursion, have an image with an undecidable word problem? The question asks for one fixed quotient by the kernel of this action. This is a statement-only draft; the supplied open status and report-verification claims require independent review.
