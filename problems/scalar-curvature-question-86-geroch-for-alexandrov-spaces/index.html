Scalar Curvature Question [?86]: Geroch for Alexandrov Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be an $n$-dimensional Alexandrov space with curvature bounded below by $-1$ in the lower triangle-comparison sense. This is not the CAT$(-1)$ upper-curvature condition. Assume the source's volumic scalar lower bound $\operatorname{Sc}^{\mathrm{vol}}_n(X)\ge0$. For a continuous map $\Phi:X\to T^n$, the source explicitly defines nonzero degree by nonvanishing of the induced top-homology map. With ordinary integral coefficients, this is \[ \Phi_*:H_n(X;{\mathbb{Z}})\longrightarrow H_n(T^n;{\mathbb{Z}})\cong{\mathbb{Z}}, \qquad\Phi_*\ne0. \] No Lipschitz restriction on $\Phi$ or distinguished fundamental class for the singular space is added.

The conjecture is \[ \operatorname{curv}_{\mathrm{Alex}}(X)\ge-1, \quad\operatorname{Sc}^{\mathrm{vol}}_n(X)\ge0, \quad\exists\Phi\text{ as above} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \widetilde X\cong{\mathbb{R}}^n\text{ isometrically}, \] where the universal cover carries the lifted length metric and ${\mathbb{R}}^n$ its Euclidean metric. The volumic predicate and homology coefficient convention are not defined in the extraction. Compactness and boundary assumptions are also not stated; they are not silently imposed merely because a nonzero top-homology map is required. The source itself says the volumic notion is provisional and intended to inspire a more satisfactory scalar-curvature definition. The supplied summary reports no located resolution of this singular-space extension of torus rigidity.

Adjacent section 27 begins Fredholm coarea and stable K-area. It allows complex Hilbert bundles with unitary connections described through parallel transport. Its proposed curvature control is that holonomy rotation angles around suitable surfaces satisfy \[ |\alpha|\le\int_S C(s)\,\mathrm dA(s). \] For a K-cohomology class $\kappa$ represented by a Fredholm bundle map $F:L_1\to L_2$, its coarea is described as the infimum of constants $c$ admitting such a representation and connections on both bundles with curvature norms at most $c$. In the compact-support version, $F$ must be a unitary isomorphism and preserve the connections outside a compact set. The source then proposes dual Fredholm K-area on K-homology, ordinary homology, and infinite-support homology, but ends before the formula. The admissible surfaces, infinite-dimensional curvature convention, and missing duality formula cannot be completed from the record. This adjacent framework is not an extra hypothesis of the Alexandrov conjecture.

Short English statement

Does a nonnegative-volumic-scalar-curvature Alexandrov space mapping nontrivially in top homology to the torus have Euclidean universal cover? Preserve the lower-curvature sign and the explicitly homological degree condition; the adjacent Fredholm framework is incomplete.
