Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.4

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group. For $x\in G$, let $\langle x\rangle$ denote the cyclic subgroup generated by $x$. The group $G$ is called cut, or inverse semi-rational, if \[ \forall x,y\in G,\qquad \langle x\rangle=\langle y\rangle \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists g\in G\;\left(g^{-1}xg=y\ \text{or}\ g^{-1}xg=y^{-1}\right). \] Let $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ be the set of ordinary irreducible complex characters of $G$, and define the field generated by its ordinary character-table entries by \[ {\mathbb{Q}}(G)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(g):\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G),\ g\in G\right)\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}. \] For a prime $p$, write $\operatorname{O}_p(G)$ for the largest normal subgroup of $G$ whose order is a power of $p$. For a finite group $H$, its exponent $\exp(H)$ is the least positive integer $n$ such that $h^n=1$ for every $h\in H$; in particular, $\exp(\{1\})=1$. A group is solvable if its derived series reaches the trivial subgroup.
  1. (a) Does there exist a real constant $c>0$, independent of $G$, such that \[ [{\mathbb{Q}}(G):{\mathbb{Q}}]\leq c \qquad\text{for every finite cut group }G? \] The supplied source reports that such a uniform bound exists when $G$ is additionally assumed solvable, attributing this result to J. F. Tent, J. Algebra 363 (2012), 73--82. This is retained as a source-reported qualification, without independent verification.

  2. (b) Is every Sylow $3$-subgroup of every finite cut group itself cut? Explicitly, if $P\leq G$ has order equal to the largest power of $3$ dividing $|G|$, must \[ \forall x,y\in P,\qquad \langle x\rangle=\langle y\rangle \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists u\in P\;\left(u^{-1}xu=y\ \text{or}\ u^{-1}xu=y^{-1}\right) \] hold?

  3. (c) For every finite solvable cut group $G$ and every $p\in\{5,7\}$, is it true that \[ \exp\left(\operatorname{O}_p(G)\right)\mid p? \]

Short English statement

For finite cut groups, ask whether the field generated by all ordinary character-table entries has uniformly bounded degree over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and whether every Sylow $3$-subgroup is cut. For finite solvable cut groups, also ask whether $\operatorname{O}_p(G)$ has exponent dividing $p$ for $p=5,7$. The supplied source reports a positive answer to the first question under the additional solvability assumption. Its open label and literature claims have not been independently verified.
