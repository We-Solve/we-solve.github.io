Definitions and mathematical statement

A coefficient of a polynomial is understood relative to a specified polynomial basis: schematically, if $p_1,\ldots,p_M$ are basis polynomials, then a polynomial kernel can be written as \[ K=\sum_{j=1}^{M}c_jp_j. \] Here this expression only explains the meaning of coefficients; the supplied record does not specify the variables, scalar field, basis, admissible kernels, or optimization problem defining the intended kernel.
