Conjecture 2.17 — [154] Every Vassiliev invariant of classical knots can be extended to a finite type invariant of long virtual knots.

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A classical oriented knot is an oriented embedded circle in $S^3$, up to isotopy. A long virtual knot is represented by a knot diagram of an oriented line, standard outside a compact set, with classical and virtual crossings, modulo the relevant Reidemeister and virtual detour moves. The precise virtual finite-type filtration is important and is not defined in the supplied statement.

The numbered claim [154], cross-referenced to Problem 3.9, is: every Vassiliev invariant $v$ of classical knots admits an extension $\widetilde v$ that is a finite-type invariant of long virtual knots. On a classical long knot $k$, extension means \[ \widetilde v(k)=v(\operatorname{cl}(k)), \] where $\operatorname{cl}(k)$ is its classical closure. The statement gives no coefficient group/ring, virtual finite-type convention, or bound comparing the two degrees. These are not silently supplied. In particular, classical-crossing and virtualization/semi-virtual formulations of virtual finite type must be distinguished when reviewing the claim.

The extraction then enters Section 2.8, "Finite type invariants derived from local moves." This is surrounding context. It takes a commutative ring $R$ with unit, the set $\mathcal K$ of isotopy classes of oriented knots, and a local move $m$ replacing a tangle inside a ball. If $K$ admits $m$ in $d$ disjoint balls and $K_S$ results from applying $m$ in balls indexed by $S\subset\{1,\ldots,d\}$, set \[ F_d(R\mathcal K,m)=\operatorname{span}_R \left\{\sum_{S\subset\{1,\ldots,d\}}(-1)^{|S|}K_S\right\}. \tag{13} \] The excerpt gives the descending filtration $R\mathcal K=F_0\supset F_1\supset F_2\supset\cdots$ and calls an $R$-linear invariant $v:R\mathcal K\to R$ finite type of $m$-degree $d$ when $v(F_{d+1})=0$; this condition bounds the degree and does not by itself assert exact degree. For the crossing-change move $\times$, it writes $F_d(R\mathcal K)=F_d(R\mathcal K,\times)$. It lists crossing change, double crossing change, $\#$ move, pass move, $\Delta$ move, doubled delta move, and $n$-gon move in Figure 10. The $\Delta$ and $n$-gon strands may have any orientations matching across the move, and an $n$-gon side has $n$ strands. The actual tangle pictures are missing. The contextual program asks to calculate the corresponding graded spaces; it does not provide a virtual-knot filtration for the numbered extension claim.

Short English statement

The main claim asks whether every classical Vassiliev invariant extends to a finite-type invariant of long virtual knots. The supplied registry calls this established by Goussarov--Polyak--Viro via Gauss-diagram formulas, while its title says conjecture and generic source evidence calls it open. These assessments and the unspecified virtual finite-type convention require source review. The following local-move discussion is contextual material.
