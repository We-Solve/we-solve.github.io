A spectral-gap criterion for the critical Paneitz kernel

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Problem 28. When does the critical conformal operator $P=P_n$ on a closed connected even-dimensional Riemannian manifold satisfy $\ker P_n={\mathbb{R}}$ on smooth functions? The source says this always holds for the two-dimensional Laplacian, while in dimension four there are both sufficient conditions and counterexamples, even among conformally flat metrics. Boundary conditions and connectedness must be explicit for the constants-only formulation.

Supplied four-dimensional spectral criterion. Use $\Delta=d^*d\ge0$ and the Paneitz convention \[ P_4=\Delta^2+\delta(T\,{\,\mathrm{d}}),\qquad T=\tfrac23Rg-2\operatorname{Ric},\quad\delta=d^*. \] Let $\lambda_1>0$ be the first positive eigenvalue of $\Delta$ and let \[ \beta=\max\left(0,\max_{x\in M}[-\lambda_{\min}(T_x)]\right) \] be the least nonnegative constant with $T\ge-\beta g$. The aid asserts that if $\beta<\lambda_1$, then for every mean-zero smooth $u$, \[ \int_MuP_4u\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g \ge\lambda_1(\lambda_1-\beta)\int_Mu^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g. \] Thus $P_4$ is nonnegative and has exactly the constant kernel. The strict curvature-spectral margin is stated to be open in the $C^2$ topology on smooth metrics; this is a sufficient condition rather than a classification of all constant-kernel metrics.

For an Einstein four-metric the aid gives $P_4=\Delta(\Delta+R/6)$. Its nonconstant kernel is exactly the Laplace eigenspace of eigenvalue $-R/6$, when that number is a positive Laplace eigenvalue. For $R\ge0$ there is no such nonconstant resonance.

Supplied conformally flat counterexample. Let $k>0$ and take the product of the round sphere $S^2(k)$ of curvature $k$ with a closed connected hyperbolic surface $\Sigma(-k)$ of curvature $-k$. This four-metric is locally conformally flat and has scalar curvature zero. If $a$ and $b$ are Laplace eigenvalues of its two factors, separation of variables gives the Paneitz eigenvalue \[ (a+b)^2-2ka+2kb. \] The aid asserts that the kernel consists of constants and the three first spherical harmonics pulled back from $S^2(k)$, hence has dimension four. Here $\beta=2k$ and $\lambda_1(M)=\min(2k,\lambda_1(\Sigma))$. Whenever $\lambda_1(\Sigma)\ge2k$, this gives $\beta=\lambda_1(M)$ while the kernel is nonconstant, so replacing the strict sufficient inequality by a non-strict one is invalid. The summary associates this example with Singer--Eastwood but supplies no full reference.

Adjacent determinant material, not part of the kernel question. Section III is extracted from T. Branson and R. Gover's "Origins, applications, and generalizations of the $Q$-curvature". It discusses a natural operator $A$ with positive definite leading symbol, a positive power of a conformally invariant operator, such as a GJMS operator or a Dirac square. In dimensions two, four and six, it reports the schematic anomaly formula \[ -\log\frac{\det_\zeta\widehat A}{\det_\zeta A} =\alpha\left\{\frac12\int_M\omega P\omega\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g +\int_M\omega Q\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g\right\} +\mathcal C_F[\widehat g,g]+H. \] Here $\widehat g=e^{2\omega}g$, $\mathcal C_F$ is a difference of integrated local scalar invariants, and $H$ is a nullspace-dependent correction, stated to vanish when $\ker A=0$. The hats/order in the extracted $F$ terms are damaged, so their sign is not guessed. Zeta determinant conventions with zero modes or negative eigenvalues require specification; positivity of the principal symbol alone does not supply positivity or invertibility of $A$. In particular $\ker P_4={\mathbb{R}}$ is not the same condition as $\ker A=0$.

Short English statement

The source asks when a critical conformal operator has only constant solutions. The supplied aid gives a strict curvature-versus-Laplace spectral bound for Paneitz, an Einstein resonance criterion and a conformally flat four-dimensional counterexample. The appended determinant discussion is separate and uses a different zero-nullspace condition.
