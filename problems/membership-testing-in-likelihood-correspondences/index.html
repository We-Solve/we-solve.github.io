Membership Testing in Likelihood Correspondences

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record names an algebraic statistical model $X$, data $u$, a candidate point $p$, and the likelihood correspondence $L_X$, but does not specify these objects or give a definition of $L_X$.

Conditionally on specifying the intended model and likelihood correspondence, let $P_X$ denote the ambient space for candidate points and let $U_X$ denote the data space, with $L_X\subseteq P_X\times U_X$. These spaces are placeholders for missing source specifications; no field, coordinate convention, positivity condition, or boundary convention is imposed here. For an admissible input $p\in P_X$ and $u\in U_X$, set $z=(p,u)$.

Requested membership task. Given the specified objects $X,L_X,p,u$, determine the truth value of \[ z=(p,u)\in L_X. \] Equivalently, determine the value of the membership function \[ \mathbf{1}_{L_X}\colon P_X\times U_X\longrightarrow\{0,1\},\qquad \mathbf{1}_{L_X}(p,u)= \begin{cases} 1,&(p,u)\in L_X,\\ 0,&(p,u)\notin L_X. \end{cases} \] This is a conditional formulation of the requested task, not a claim that an algorithm or a particular membership answer has been established.

Unresolved specifications. The record supplies no defining equations for $X$, no coordinates for $p$ or $u$, and no precise construction of $L_X$. In particular, the ground field, affine or projective setting, likelihood function, admissible data, critical-point conditions, and treatment of singular points, excluded loci, and boundary or closure points remain unspecified. Membership at such points must not be inferred from an unstated convention.

The supplied source-verification note describes the extraction as an expository worked membership task for which the report presents the trace test and witness-set construction as a method. It does not identify a separate unresolved conjecture. Consequently, this draft preserves the membership task without promoting it to an open mathematical assertion.

Short English statement

Given a fully specified algebraic statistical model, its likelihood correspondence, a candidate point, and data, decide whether their pair belongs to that correspondence. The supplied record omits the concrete inputs and necessary domain and boundary conventions. Its supplied verification note identifies an expository worked task, despite the registry's open label; human review is required to recover a complete formulation.
