Math-986

Anosov Geodesic Flows Without Negative Curvature Metrics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed smooth manifold is a compact smooth manifold without boundary. For a smooth Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$, let \[ S_gM=\{(x,v)\in TM:g_x(v,v)=1\} \] be its unit tangent bundle, and let $\varphi_g^t)_{t\in{\mathbb{R}}}$ be its geodesic flow. This flow is Anosov if there is a continuous, $D\varphi_g^t$-invariant splitting \[ T(S_gM)=E^s\oplus{\mathbb{R}} X_g\oplus E^u, \] where $X_g$ generates the flow, and constants $C\geq1$, $\lambda>0$ such that, for an auxiliary Riemannian norm, every $t\geq0$ satisfies \[ \|D\varphi_g^t(v)\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v\|\quad(v\in E^s),\qquad \|D\varphi_g^{-t}(w)\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|w\|\quad(w\in E^u). \] For the usual nondegenerate interpretation, both $E^s$ and $E^u$ have positive rank. A metric $h$ has strictly negative sectional curvature if \[ K_h(x,\sigma)<0 \quad\text{for every }x\in M\text{ and every two-dimensional subspace }\sigma\subset T_xM. \] Question. Does there exist a closed smooth manifold $M$ and a smooth Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$ whose geodesic flow is Anosov, such that \[ \nexists\text{ a smooth Riemannian metric }h\text{ on }M \quad\text{with}\quad K_h(x,\sigma)<0\text{ for every }x,\sigma? \] Here "negative curvature" is interpreted as strictly negative sectional curvature, in accordance with the Riemannian context. The obstruction concerns every metric $h$ on the same smooth manifold, not merely the metric $g$. The supplied question gives no dimension bound, connectedness assumption, or orientability assumption; none is added. Its background describes the unresolved target as higher-dimensional, without specifying a dimension range. Low-dimensional conventions, including the positive-rank requirement in the Anosov definition and the vacuity of sectional-curvature inequalities when there are no two-planes, require confirmation from the source.

Short English statement

Is there a closed smooth manifold carrying a Riemannian metric with Anosov geodesic flow, although no Riemannian metric on that manifold has strictly negative sectional curvature? The supplied background describes a higher-dimensional question but leaves the dimension range unspecified.
