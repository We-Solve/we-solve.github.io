Bose–Einstein Condensation in Quantum Boltzmann Dynamics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
In the isotropic Bose quantum Boltzmann model, $x\in[0,\infty)$ is kinetic energy. A measure solution $F_t$ is a positive finite mass-and-energy measure, with $\sup_{0\le t<T}\int(1+x)\,dF_t<\infty$, satisfying the source's weak collision equation against $C_b^2$ test functions. Its Lebesgue decomposition is $dF_t(x)=f(x,t)\sqrt{x}\,dx+d\mu_t(x)$. A condensate is the atom $\mu_t(\{0\})>0$ at zero energy. The source begins with no singular part, $dF_0=f_0(x)\sqrt{x}\,dx$, and seeks suitable low-temperature data with $f_0$ large near zero.
Source scope and qualifications.
Both the finite-time atom and the weaker concentration alternative are retained. The source writes the weaker quantity as lim_(epsilon->0) sup_(0<=t<=t_0) F_t([0,epsilon]); the catalogue limsup notation should be read with this temporal supremum. The source's existence question is not a claim of condensation from every initial datum, and its absolutely continuous initial-data condition cannot be dropped.
Source initial-data and concentration conventions.
The initial measure has no condensate or other singular part. The weaker alternative is $\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\sup_{0\le t\le t_0}F_t([0,\varepsilon])>0$, with $t_0<T$.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For the quantum Boltzmann equation for Bose–Einstein particles, does there exist a measure solution $F_t$ and $t_0\in(0,T)$ with $F_{t_0}(\{0\})=\mu_{t_0}(\{0\})>0$, or at least with $\limsup_{\varepsilon\to0^+,\ 0\leq t\leq t_0}F_t([0,\varepsilon])>0$?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Finite-time condensation is proved for broad classes of singular or low-temperature data, but not for every initial datum asked for in the extracted question. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Show that an initially noncondensed Bose gas can form a zero-energy condensate in finite time, or at least concentrate positive mass there.
