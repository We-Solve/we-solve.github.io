Math-1641

Quantizing Mather measures by equilibrium packets and smooth-graph WKB states

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Craig Evans asks whether Mather sets play a role in semiclassical eigenfunctions for $H(x,p)=\tfrac12|p|^2+V(x)$ with periodic potential, and conversely whether Mather sets can be quantized by quasimodes with good error bounds; he also asks whether a Diophantine condition would be useful. The aid identifies this as original Problem 31, whose leading digit was lost in the extraction. The source first gives the operator $-h^2\Delta/2+V$ but prints the opposite sign in its eigenvalue display. With $\Delta=\sum_j\partial_{x_j}^2$, the Hamiltonian-consistent choice used by the aid is \[ P_h=-\tfrac{h^2}{2}\Delta+V,\qquad h\downarrow0. \] The sign discrepancy is retained as a source qualification rather than silently resolved for both printed formulas. Use a smooth real periodic $V$ for the following supplied claims; the original does not specify its regularity.

The mechanical Lagrangian is $L(x,v)=\tfrac12|v|^2-V(x)$. At zero cohomology, put $V_{\max}=\max V$ and $K=\{x:V(x)=V_{\max}\}$. The aid asserts that all minimizing invariant probability measures are exactly \[ \mu({\,\mathrm{d}} x)\delta_0({\,\mathrm{d}} v),\qquad \mu\text{ any probability measure supported on }K, \] with momentum $p=v$. In particular the zero-class Mather set is $K\times\{0\}$. It reports that every such measure is realized by normalized smooth periodic quasimodes along a sequence $h_j\downarrow0$ with \[ \|(P_{h_j}-V_{\max})u_{h_j}\|_{L^2}\leq C h_j,\qquad \|u_{h_j}\|_{L^2}=1. \] Precisely, their semiclassical Wigner distributions satisfy \[ \langle\operatorname{Op}_{h_j}(a)u_{h_j},u_{h_j}\rangle \longrightarrow\int_K a(x,0)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(x) \] for standard smooth compact-momentum test symbols. The proposed construction is a diagonal sequence of superpositions of separated packets of width $\sqrt h$, with a residual constant uniform in the growing packet count. It includes singular and nonatomic measures on degenerate maximum sets. This is an $O(h)$ quasimode statement, not an exact-eigenfunction realization or an arbitrary-accuracy claim.

The second supplied construction assumes a smooth global Hamilton--Jacobi graph represented locally by $\eta={\,\mathrm{d}} S$, where $\eta$ is a closed real one-form on the torus and $H(x,\eta(x))=E$. Assume a positive smooth normalized invariant density $\rho$ with $\operatorname{div}(\rho\eta)=0$, and set $a=\sqrt\rho$. Whenever the global phase satisfies the Bohr--Sommerfeld condition \[ \frac1h\int_\gamma\eta\in2\pi{\mathbb{Z}}\quad\text{for every closed torus loop }\gamma, \] the phase $e^{iS/h}$ is globally periodic and \[ u_h=a e^{iS/h},\qquad (P_h-E)u_h=-\tfrac{h^2}{2}e^{iS/h}\Delta a. \] Thus the residual is $O(h^2)$ for this fixed smooth graph and amplitude. The condition applies to admissible values of $h$ and does not assert that an admissible sequence exists for every cohomology class. The packet result needs no Diophantine condition; the graph result uses the stated holonomy condition, which is not a general answer to the source's broader arithmetic question. Neither construction treats every nonzero-cohomology or nonsmooth Mather set.

Short English statement

Evans asks how Mather sets could be realized semiclassically. The aid realizes every zero-class minimizing measure over potential maxima by periodic $O(h)$ quasimodes, and gives $O(h^2)$ WKB states on smooth invariant graphs when the global phase is quantized. These partial constructions have distinct regularity, cohomology, and arithmetic restrictions.
