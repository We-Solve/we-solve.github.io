Functorial Chow Rings for Singular Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and let $X$ be a projective variety over $k$. The record specifies neither a restriction on the characteristic of $k$ nor a smoothness hypothesis on $X$; its title explicitly concerns singular varieties.

Requested construction. Define a theory assigning to projective varieties $X$ a graded ring denoted $CH^*(X)$, with the expected contravariant functoriality, cycle-class and Chern-class properties, and a rational Chern-character isomorphism.

Under the usual interpretation of contravariant functoriality, a morphism $f\colon X\to Y$ in the intended category would induce a graded ring homomorphism $f^*\colon CH^*(Y)\to CH^*(X)$, satisfying \[ (\operatorname{id}_X)^*=\operatorname{id}_{CH^*(X)},\qquad (g\circ f)^*=f^*\circ g^* \] for composable morphisms $f\colon X\to Y$ and $g\colon Y\to Z$. The source does not specify the intended class of morphisms, so these identities describe the usual meaning of functoriality without resolving its scope.

Unresolved specifications. The word "expected" is not accompanied by a complete list of axioms. In particular, the record does not specify whether the underlying groups must be the usual groups of algebraic cycles modulo rational equivalence, the target and compatibility requirements of a cycle-class map, the objects admitting Chern classes, or the source, grading conventions, and compatibility requirements of the rational Chern-character isomorphism. These choices cannot be supplied silently for singular varieties.

The supplied background assessment instead describes an incomplete prompt about Chern classes and cycles supported in a given codimension, and reports that surrounding text exhibits failures of a naive formula without stating a replacement theorem. Neither that formula nor its hypotheses are supplied. Consequently, this is an incomplete construction problem: the record does not determine a fully specified existence assertion or establish how the codimension formulation relates to the stated ring-construction question.

Short English statement

For projective varieties over an algebraically closed field, including singular varieties, the record asks for a Chow-ring theory with pullbacks, cycle classes, Chern classes, and a rational Chern-character isomorphism. The required axioms and several domains are unspecified, and the supplied background describes a potentially different codimension question. A precise complete conjecture therefore requires further source clarification.
