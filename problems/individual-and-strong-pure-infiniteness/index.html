Individual and strong pure infiniteness

OpenAI's claim

Proper infiniteness of all positive elements is claimed to imply strong pure infiniteness; under exactness a uniform finite-amplification version suffices, and separable nuclear algebras with that property absorb O_infinity. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For an exact complex $C^*$-algebra $C$, assume one fixed $n\ge1$ makes every amplified positive element $h^{\oplus n}$ properly infinite, meaning $h^{\oplus n}\oplus h^{\oplus n}\precsim h^{\oplus n}$ in Cuntz comparison. OpenAI claims every positive $h\in C$ is itself properly infinite and $C$ is strongly purely infinite. Its other theorem omits exactness but assumes every positive element already properly infinite. For all $a,b\ge0$, arbitrary $c\in C$ and $\varepsilon>0$, it claims $s,t\in C$ with \[ \|s^*as-a\|<\varepsilon,\qquad\|t^*bt-b\|<\varepsilon,\qquad\|s^*ct\|<\varepsilon. \] These are simultaneous diagonalization inequalities, not only individual positive-element comparison.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 303, 2026-09-25): Graph shrinking and polar deformation merge hereditary positive elements while retaining norm signals in all required quotients. Exactness rules out persistent amplification obstructions, while a separate nuclear argument uses matrix norms forbidden in exact targets. Controlled comparison coefficients then eliminate arbitrary mixed entries, establishing strong diagonalization and the central-sequence absorption conclusion.
