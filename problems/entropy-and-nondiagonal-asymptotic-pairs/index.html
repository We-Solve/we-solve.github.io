Entropy and Nondiagonal Asymptotic Pairs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks about an algebraic action on a compact metrizable space. Under the conventional interpretation, this means that $X$ is a compact metrizable topological group and that each element of $\Gamma$ acts by a continuous group automorphism. The record does not explicitly specify whether $X$ must be abelian. The formulation below uses this interpretation of algebraic action; its intended scope requires review.

Let $\Gamma$ be a countably infinite discrete amenable group, and let $\Gamma\curvearrowright X$ be such an algebraic action. Fix a metric $d$ compatible with the topology of $X$. The action is expansive if there exists $c>0$ such that \[ \forall x,y\in X,\qquad \left[d(gx,gy)\leq c\text{ for every }g\in\Gamma\right]\Longrightarrow x=y. \] A nondiagonal asymptotic pair is a pair $(x,y)\in X^2$ with $x\ne y$ such that \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists K\subseteq\Gamma\text{ finite}\ \forall g\in\Gamma\setminus K,\qquad d(gx,gy)<\varepsilon. \] Thus asymptotic convergence here means convergence outside finite subsets of the whole group.

Interpret the unspecified entropy $h$ in the record as topological entropy. Explicitly, choose a left Følner sequence $(F_n)_{n\geq1}$ of nonempty finite subsets of $\Gamma$, so that \[ \frac{|sF_n\mathbin{\triangle}F_n|}{|F_n|}\longrightarrow0\qquad(s\in\Gamma). \] For a finite open cover $\mathcal U$ of $X$, let $N(\mathcal U)$ be its minimum subcover cardinality, and put \[ h_{\mathrm{top}}(\Gamma\curvearrowright X) =\sup_{\mathcal U}\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{|F_n|} \log N\!\left(\bigvee_{g\in F_n}g^{-1}\mathcal U\right), \] where $g^{-1}\mathcal U=\{\{x\in X:gx\in U\}:U\in\mathcal U\}$, the join consists of intersections of cover members, and the supremum ranges over finite open covers.

Question under these conventions. For every $\Gamma$ and every expansive algebraic action as above, is \[ h_{\mathrm{top}}(\Gamma\curvearrowright X)>0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists x,y\in X\;\left[x\ne y\ \text{and}\ d(gx,gy)\longrightarrow0\text{ as }g\to\infty\right]? \] The interpretation of entropy, the algebraic structure required on $X$, and the intended asymptotic convention are not explicitly defined in the supplied excerpt. This is therefore a qualified reconstruction requiring mathematical review.

Short English statement

For an expansive algebraic action of a countably infinite discrete amenable group, ask whether positive topological entropy is equivalent to the existence of two distinct points whose translates approach one another outside every sufficiently large finite subset of the group. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; the precise algebraic scope and entropy convention require review.
