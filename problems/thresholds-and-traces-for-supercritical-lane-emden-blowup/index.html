Math-15012

Thresholds and Traces for Supercritical Lane–Emden Blowup

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be a smooth bounded domain. The source considers the supercritical Lane--Emden heat equation \[ \partial_t u-\Delta u=|u|^{p-2}u \quad\text{in }\Omega\times(0,T), \qquad p>2^*:=\frac{2n}{n-2}, \] and sets \[ \alpha:=\frac{2}{p-2},\qquad M_{\mathrm{crit}}:=\alpha(n-2-\alpha). \] The displayed Sobolev critical exponent presupposes the dimensional range $n\geq3$; this restriction is implicit rather than separately stated in the supplied record. No initial or boundary conditions, sign restrictions, or solution class are supplied.
  1. Can the threshold $M$ in the complete-blow-up result referred to by the source be chosen as \[ M=M_{\mathrm{crit}}=\alpha(n-2-\alpha) \] for supercritical exponents $p$ sufficiently close to $2^*$ from above? This interprets the source's phrase "for sufficiently small $p>2^*$" as a near-critical condition. The record does not specify the underlying complete-blow-up theorem, how $M$ enters its hypotheses, or the allowed dependence of the near-critical range on $n$, $\Omega$, or other data. Consequently this question cannot be given a complete quantified formulation from the supplied record alone.
  2. Does every finite-time blow-up solution with bounded energy admit a terminal trace satisfying \[ u(T)\in L^{p,\lambda}_0(\Omega)? \] Here $T<\infty$ denotes the blow-up time referred to in the source. The notation $L^{p,\lambda}_0(\Omega)$ is retained exactly as supplied. Neither $\lambda$, the function-space norm, the meaning of the subscript $0$, the energy functional and its boundedness condition, nor the meaning or topology of the terminal trace is specified. These omissions prevent this question from being a fully defined mathematical assertion.
The supplied literature assessment calls the target a Lorentz space, but provides no definition establishing that interpretation. It must not be silently replaced by a Lorentz-space or Morrey-space convention.

Short English statement

The source asks whether a complete-blow-up threshold equals $\alpha(n-2-\alpha)$ for exponents just above the Sobolev critical exponent, and whether every finite-time blow-up solution with bounded energy has a terminal trace in the stated space $L^{p,\lambda}_0(\Omega)$. The threshold theorem, boundary conditions, solution class, energy condition, and trace space require clarification before either question has a precise complete formulation.
