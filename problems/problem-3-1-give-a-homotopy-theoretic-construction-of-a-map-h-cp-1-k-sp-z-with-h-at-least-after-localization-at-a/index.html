Problem 3.1 — Give a homotopy theoretic construction of a map ρh: Ω ∞CP ∞ −1→K Sp(Z) with ρ≃ ρh◦α, at least after localization at a…

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb CP^\infty_{-1}$ denote the Thom spectrum of the negative universal complex line bundle in the source's convention, and let $\Omega^\infty\mathbb CP^\infty_{-1}$ be its infinite loop space. Let $X$ be the source's common domain of the stable mapping-class map $\alpha$ and symplectic map $\rho$: \[\alpha:X\to\Omega^\infty\mathbb CP^\infty_{-1},\qquad \rho:X\to K\operatorname{Sp}({\mathbb{Z}}).\] The exact model for $X$, components and $K\operatorname{Sp}({\mathbb{Z}})$ is omitted. The problem asks for a homotopy-theoretic construction \[\rho_h:\Omega^\infty\mathbb CP^\infty_{-1}\to K\operatorname{Sp}({\mathbb{Z}}),\qquad \rho\simeq\rho_h\circ\alpha,\] at least after localization at a regular prime. The source singles out the $2$-local case [12]. This is a homotopy-commutative factorization, not strict equality; a requirement that $\rho_h$ preserve infinite-loop structures would need specification. The localized version must specify the relevant components and compatible localization of the maps and spaces.

The source gives the following rational context on each component, indexed by $k$: \[H^*(\Omega_k^\infty\mathbb CP^\infty_{-1};{\mathbb{Q}})={\mathbb{Q}}[\kappa'_1,\kappa'_2,\ldots],\qquad |\kappa'_i|=2i,\] with the standard Miller--Morita--Mumford classes $\kappa_i=\alpha^*\kappa'_i$. It reports Borel's stable calculation \[H^*(B\operatorname{Sp}({\mathbb{Z}});{\mathbb{Q}})\cong H^*(\operatorname{Sp}/U;{\mathbb{Q}}) \cong{\mathbb{Q}}[c_1,c_3,\ldots],\] where $c_{2i+1}$ is the Chern-class image under the indicated map $\operatorname{Sp}/U\to BU$, of degree $4i+2$. It says the relevant pullback of $c_{2i+1}$ is a nonzero multiple of the odd $\kappa$-class, and consequently odd $\kappa_{2i+1}$ restrict to zero rationally on Torelli [17]. The exact stable-range/model conventions require the absent references.

There is a source notation issue: the extraction writes $\rho^*(c_{2i+1})$ as a multiple of $\kappa'_{2i+1}$, but the latter lives on the infinite-loop space, whereas $\rho$'s domain in the factorization is $X$. In the expected compatible interpretation these would be $\rho_h^*(c_{2i+1})=a_i\kappa'_{2i+1}$ and $\rho^*(c_{2i+1})=a_i\kappa_{2i+1}$, with $a_i\in{\mathbb{Q}}\setminus\{0\}$ and the necessary target cohomology identifications. This describes the degree/domain consistency to check, not a silently corrected theorem or a constructed map.

The supplied summary reports the underlying Mumford-conjecture equivalence proved by Madsen--Weiss. On components where $\alpha$ is a homotopy equivalence, a chosen homotopy inverse would yield an abstract factorization; the source's request is for the intended homotopy-theoretic construction, whose additional naturality/explicitness criterion is not stated. Rational polynomial cohomology alone does not specify that construction or its localized refinements.

Short English statement

Construct the specified symplectic $K$-theory map from the stable Thom-spectrum infinite-loop space, factoring $\rho$ through $\alpha$, at least locally at a regular prime and especially at two. Missing models and a pullback-prime notation mismatch need clarification.
