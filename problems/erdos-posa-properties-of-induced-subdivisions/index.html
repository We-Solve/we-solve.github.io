Math-4405

Erdős–Pósa Properties of Induced Subdivisions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the following conventional formulation, subject to the qualifications below. All graphs are finite, undirected, and simple. A subdivision of a graph $H$ is obtained by replacing each edge $uv\in E(H)$ by a $u$--$v$ path of positive length, with all replacement paths internally vertex-disjoint and with no internal vertex belonging to $V(H)$. An induced $H$-subdivision in a graph $G$ is an induced subgraph $G[X]$ isomorphic to a subdivision of $H$.

A class $\mathcal C$ of graphs has the vertex Erdős--Pósa property if there exists a function \[ f_{\mathcal C}\colon {\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\longrightarrow {\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0} \] such that, for every graph $G$ and every integer $k\geq 1$, at least one of the following holds:

  1. There exist pairwise disjoint sets $X_1,\ldots,X_k\subseteq V(G)$ such that $G[X_i]\in\mathcal C$ for every $i$.
  2. There exists $S\subseteq V(G)$ with $|S|\leq f_{\mathcal C}(k)$ such that $G-S$ contains no induced subgraph belonging to $\mathcal C$.
For a fixed graph $H$, let $\mathcal S_H$ be the class of all subdivisions of $H$. The main question is to characterize the graphs $H$ for which $\mathcal S_H$ has this property. The function may depend on $H$, but must be independent of $G$.

The supplied record singles out $H=K_4$, the complete graph on four vertices, and a fan whose path has length $3$. Under the usual fan and path-length conventions, the latter graph has vertices $a,v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3$ and exactly the edges \[ \{v_0v_1,v_1v_2,v_2v_3\}\cup\{av_i:0\leq i\leq 3\}. \] It asks whether each of these two classes $\mathcal S_H$ has the property.

A further singled-out question concerns induced even cycles. Interpreted as even holes, this asks whether the class \[ \mathcal E=\{C_{2r}:r\geq 2\} \] has the same property, where $C_m$ is the cycle on $m$ vertices and occurrences in $G$ must be induced. This parity-restricted question is distinct from the question for subdivisions of a fixed even cycle: unrestricted subdivision does not preserve even length.

The source does not explicitly specify finite simple graphs, the vertex rather than edge version of the property, or the fan convention. These conventions therefore require source review. The interpretation of the even-cycle subquestion as even holes also requires confirmation from the report. The supplied open status and verification label are registry annotations, not independent mathematical verification.

Short English statement

Characterize the graphs $H$ for which every graph either contains $k$ vertex-disjoint induced subdivisions of $H$, or has a vertex set of size bounded only by $H$ and $k$ meeting every such subdivision. The record highlights $K_4$, a fan over a path of three edges, and a separate question about induced even cycles. The stated conventions and interpretations require human mathematical review.
