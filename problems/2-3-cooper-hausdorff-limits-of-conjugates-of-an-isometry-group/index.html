Math-5781

2.3 (Cooper) — Hausdorff limits of conjugates of an isometry group

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real vector space and let $\beta:V\times V\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be a nondegenerate bilinear form, not assumed symmetric or skew-symmetric. Set \[ G=\operatorname{Isom}(\beta) =\{A\in\operatorname{GL}(V):\beta(Au,Av)=\beta(u,v)\ \forall u,v\in V\}. \] In a basis with form matrix $B$, this is $\{A:A^{\mathsf T}BA=B\}$.

Problem (Cooper). Classify the subgroups $H\subseteq\operatorname{GL}(V)$ for which there is a sequence $A_n\in\operatorname{GL}(V)$ such that \[ A_nGA_n^{-1}\longrightarrow H \] in the Hausdorff-limit sense intended by the source. In the usual closed-subgroup, locally Hausdorff (Chabauty) interpretation, the limit $H$ is closed and the convergence can be stated as follows:

  1. For every $h\in H$, there exist $h_n\in A_nGA_n^{-1}$ with $h_n\to h$.
  2. If $n_k\to\infty$ and $h_{n_k}\in A_{n_k}GA_{n_k}^{-1}$ converge in $\operatorname{GL}(V)$ to $h$, then $h\in H$.
These conditions specify a standard interpretation, not an unprovided choice of global Hausdorff metric on an unbounded space. The source says the classification is known when $\beta$ is nondegenerate symmetric or nondegenerate skew-symmetric; its question retains arbitrary nondegenerate real bilinear forms. The excerpt gives no reference for those known cases and does not explicitly define its Hausdorff topology.

Short English statement

For any nondegenerate real bilinear form, classify all limits of conjugates of its isometry group inside $\operatorname{GL}(V)$. The symmetric and skew-symmetric cases are reported known; the general form and the exact noncompact Hausdorff-limit convention must be retained.
