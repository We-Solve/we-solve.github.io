Lück Approximation for Kernels over Arbitrary Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a finitely generated free group and let $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ be a subfield. Let $(N_i)_{i\geq1}$ be a descending sequence of normal subgroups of $F$, and put \[ N=\bigcap_{i\geq1}N_i,\qquad G_i=F/N_i,\qquad G=F/N. \] No finite-index assumption on the $N_i$ is supplied.

The source does not explicitly define $\phi_H^A$ or $\dim_H$. Adopt the following standard Hilbert-space interpretation, subject to mathematical review. For a countable group $H$, let $\ell^2(H)$ be the Hilbert space of square-summable complex functions on $H$, and let $\delta_e$ be the unit vector at its identity. For $a=\sum_{h\in H}c_hh\in{\mathbb{C}}[H]$, define the bounded left-convolution operator \[ (\lambda_H(a)\xi)(x)=\sum_{h\in H}c_h\xi(h^{-1}x). \] For positive integers $n,m$, a matrix $A\in\operatorname{Mat}_{n\times m}(K[F])$, and $H\in\{G,G_1,G_2,\ldots\}$, let $A_H=(a_{rs}^{H})$ be its image under the quotient-induced homomorphism $K[F]\to K[H]$. Define \[ \phi_H^A:\ell^2(H)^m\longrightarrow\ell^2(H)^n, \qquad (\phi_H^A\xi)_r=\sum_{s=1}^{m}\lambda_H(a_{rs}^{H})\xi_s. \] If $P_H^A$ is the orthogonal projection onto $\ker\phi_H^A$, define its group von Neumann dimension by \[ \dim_H\ker\phi_H^A =\sum_{s=1}^{m}\left\langle P_H^A\delta_{e,s},\delta_{e,s}\right\rangle, \] where $\delta_{e,s}$ denotes $\delta_e$ in the $s$-th summand of $\ell^2(H)^m$.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such $F,K,(N_i)$, all positive integers $n,m$, and every $A\in\operatorname{Mat}_{n\times m}(K[F])$, the limit exists and satisfies \[ \lim_{i\to\infty}\dim_{G_i}\ker\phi_{G_i}^A =\dim_G\ker\phi_G^A. \] The cleaned statement permits equal consecutive subgroups, whereas the original statement uses $>$; whether strict descent was intended is unresolved. Also, $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ forces characteristic zero. The supplied title and literature assessment discuss arbitrary fields and positive characteristic, which are outside this stated domain; no positive-characteristic extension is formulated here.

Short English statement

For a descending chain of normal subgroups of a finitely generated free group, must the von Neumann dimensions of matrix kernels over the quotient groups converge to the corresponding dimension over the quotient by their intersection? The supplied statement assumes coefficients in a subfield of ${\mathbb{C}}$; the broader references to positive characteristic and the precise operator conventions require review.
