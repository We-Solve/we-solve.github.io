Kourovka Notebook Problem 5.33

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\rho,\tau\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $Q$ be the quaternion algebra over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with basis $1,i,j,ij$, where $i^2=\tau$, $j^2=\rho$, and $ij=-ji$. Its reduced norm is \[ f(x,y,z,u)=x^2-\tau y^2-\rho z^2+\rho\tau u^2. \] Assume that $f$ is indefinite over ${\mathbb{R}}$ and anisotropic over ${\mathbb{Q}}$; explicitly, it takes both positive and negative real values, and \[ \forall(x,y,z,u)\in{\mathbb{Q}}^4,\qquad f(x,y,z,u)=0\Longrightarrow x=y=z=u=0. \] These assumptions imply $\rho\tau\ne0$ and that $\tau$ is not a square in ${\mathbb{Q}}$. Put $K={\mathbb{Q}}(\sqrt{\tau})$, and use the representation \[ X(x,y,z,u)=\begin{pmatrix} x+\sqrt{\tau}\,y&\rho(z+\sqrt{\tau}\,u)\\ z-\sqrt{\tau}\,u&x-\sqrt{\tau}\,y \end{pmatrix}\in M_2(K), \qquad \det X=f(x,y,z,u). \] Let $p$ be any prime with $p\nmid\rho\tau$, and set \[ R={\mathbb{Z}}^{(p)}=\{m/p^t:m,t\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}={\mathbb{Z}}[1/p],\qquad \mathcal O=R\,1\oplus R\,i\oplus R\,j\oplus R\,ij. \] Define \[ G=\{X(x,y,z,u):x,y,z,u\in R,\ \det X(x,y,z,u)=1\}. \] Here $E$ denotes the identity matrix and $Z(G)$ the center of $G$.

The supplied conjecture is that, for every such choice of $\rho,\tau,p$, every normal subgroup $N\trianglelefteq G$ with $N\not\subseteq Z(G)$ contains a full congruence subgroup \[ N(\mathfrak A)=\{X\in G:X\equiv E\pmod{\mathfrak A}\} \] for some modulus $\mathfrak A$. The source does not specify the ambient ring of $\mathfrak A$, its admissible values, or the exact meaning of this matrix congruence, so this part of the formulation remains unresolved.

If the intended moduli are nonzero ideals $I$ of $R$, with congruence interpreted in the order $\mathcal O$, a precise conditional formulation is obtained by defining \[ \Gamma_{\mathcal O}(I)=\{X(x,y,z,u)\in G:x-1,y,z,u\in I\} =\ker\left(G\longrightarrow(\mathcal O/I\mathcal O)^\times\right). \] Under that interpretation the conjecture reads \[ \forall N\trianglelefteq G,\quad N\not\subseteq Z(G)\Longrightarrow \exists\,I\trianglelefteq R,\ I\ne(0),\quad \Gamma_{\mathcal O}(I)\subseteq N. \] This interpretation of the moduli requires confirmation against the original source. Allowing the zero modulus would make the containment assertion vacuous. The source calls its stated normal-subgroup condition the congruence subgroup property and additionally asserts that this property is independent of the matrix representation of $Q$; that assertion is retained as a source qualification.

Short English statement

For an indefinite quaternion norm that has no nonzero rational zero, form the group of norm-one elements whose four coordinates lie in ${\mathbb{Z}}[1/p]$, where $p\nmid\rho\tau$. The supplied conjecture asks whether every normal subgroup not contained in the center contains a full congruence subgroup. The source leaves the permitted congruence moduli unspecified; the displayed formulation using nonzero ideals of ${\mathbb{Z}}[1/p]$ is conditional on that interpretation.
