Uniform-time matrix-viscosity estimates at a fully damped equilibrium

Definitions and mathematical statement

Participant research objective (Cleopatra Christoforou). Construct solutions of dissipative hyperbolic balance laws as vanishing-viscosity limits, \[ u_t+\partial_xf(u)+g(u)=0,\qquad u_t^\epsilon+\partial_xf(u^\epsilon)+g(u^\epsilon) =\epsilon u_{xx}^\epsilon,\qquad\epsilon\downarrow0. \] The supplied verification identifies the extracted "$\leq$" symbols as corrupted epsilons. The participant statement reports extending Bianchini--Bressan's method using supplementary Lyapunov functionals and obtaining exponential decay of total variation. It proposes extending the techniques to other systems, especially physical viscosity, but gives no precise nonlinear hypotheses, initial-data class, domain or convergence topology. The aid interprets physical viscosity as $\epsilon\partial_x(B(u)u_x)$; this matrix-viscosity form is explicit interpretation rather than a displayed source equation.

Reported constant-coefficient periodic result. On $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/2\pi{\mathbb{Z}}$, consider \[ u_t+A u_x+D u=\epsilon B u_{xx},\qquad u(0)=u_0\in H^2(\mathbb T;{\mathbb{R}}^m). \] Let $A,D,B$ be constant real matrices, and suppose a symmetric positive-definite $S$ and $\gamma>0$ satisfy \[ SA=A^TS,\qquad SD+D^TS\geq2\gamma S,\qquad SB+B^TS\geq0. \] Use $\|u\|_{L^2_S}^2=\int_{\mathbb T}u^TSu\,\mathrm dx$, $ |u_0|_{H^2_S}=\|\partial_x^2u_0\|_{L^2_S}$, and \[ K_S(C)=\|S^{1/2}CS^{-1/2}\|_{\mathrm{op}}. \] The aid reports the uniform vanishing-viscosity estimate against the solution $u^0$ with $\epsilon=0$ and the same datum: \[ \|u^{\epsilon,B}(t)-u^0(t)\|_{L^2_S} \leq\epsilon K_S(B)t e^{-\gamma t}|u_0|_{H^2_S}, \qquad t\geq0, \] therefore its supremum over all time is at most $\epsilon K_S(B)|u_0|_{H^2_S}/(e\gamma)$.

More generally, if both $B_1,B_2$ satisfy the same entropy-positivity condition, then the reported comparison is \[ \|u^{\epsilon,B_1}(t)-u^{\epsilon,B_2}(t)\|_{L^2_S} \leq\epsilon K_S(B_1-B_2)t e^{-\gamma t}|u_0|_{H^2_S}, \] with the corresponding uniform bound obtained by replacing $te^{-\gamma t}$ by $1/(e\gamma)$. The viscosity matrices may be singular and need not commute with $A$; the common symmetrizer and full source damping remain hypotheses. This is a linear periodic estimate, not a nonlinear small-total-variation theorem for arbitrary physical viscosities.

Reported diagnostic limitations. The aid includes anti-diffusion and uncoupled-mode examples. Their mechanisms are made explicit by a scalar negative viscosity $B=-1$, whose Fourier modes can grow at rate $\epsilon k^2-\gamma$, and by an undamped component, for which the difference between $\cos x$ and $e^{-\epsilon t}\cos x$ does not remain $O(\epsilon)$ uniformly in time. These show why entropy positivity and a source of damping cannot simply be dropped from this estimate. Kawashima-type coupling may provide a different damping mechanism, but no general coupled replacement theorem or its hypotheses is supplied.

The original exponential total-variation claim and interest in mathematical biology/numerical methods are participant context. The explicit linear certificate does not verify the full nonlinear extension or supply a physical-system application. All reported claims require independent verification.

Short English statement

The participant asks to extend vanishing-viscosity methods to dissipative balance laws and physical viscosity. The aid supplies a periodic linear result: full source damping and a common entropy symmetrizer give uniform-time $O(\epsilon)$ comparison even for singular, noncommuting viscosity matrices. It does not establish the general nonlinear program.
