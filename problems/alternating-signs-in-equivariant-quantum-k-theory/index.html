Requested conjecture, with unresolved parameters. For every flag variety in the intended class, do all coefficients $c_{u,v}^{w,d}$, for all $u,v,w\in I$ and $d\in D$, satisfy the intended alternating-sign rule? A precise assertion of this kind would require a specified parity function $\varepsilon(u,v,w,d)\in{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$ and a specified positive cone $C_T$ in the chosen coefficient ring, so that the condition could be written \[ (-1)^{\varepsilon(u,v,w,d)}c_{u,v}^{w,d}\in C_T \qquad(u,v,w\in I,\ d\in D). \] Neither $\varepsilon$ nor $C_T$ is supplied. They are placeholders for missing data, not definitions of the intended sign rule. In particular, this display does not specify a complete mathematical conjecture.

The supplied statement also describes an equivalent alternating-sign assertion for a Pontryagin product in localized equivariant $K$-theory of an affine Grassmannian. Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether Schubert product coefficients in equivariant quantum $K$-theory of flag varieties obey an alternating-sign positivity rule, and asserts a corresponding Pontryagin-product formulation. The actual sign formula, positivity convention, geometric scope, and comparison map are missing, so this draft records a conjecture schema requiring mathematical review.
