Math-3416

Topological $\widehat A$-Classes on Witt Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A stratified pseudomanifold is a space decomposed into manifold strata, with local conical models whose cross-sections are called links. In the usual rational convention, such a space satisfies the Witt condition if every even-dimensional link $L^{2k}$ of a singular stratum satisfies \[ I^{\bar m}H_k(L;{\mathbb{Q}})=0, \] where $I^{\bar m}H_*$ denotes intersection homology with lower-middle perversity $\bar m(c)=\lfloor(c-2)/2\rfloor$. The supplied record does not specify the coefficient convention or the precise category of stratified spaces; this definition explains the usual terminology rather than supplying omitted hypotheses.

For a closed smooth oriented manifold $M$, the usual homology $\widehat A$-class is $\widehat A(TM)\cap[M]$, where $\widehat A(TM)$ is the rational characteristic class associated with the $\widehat A$ multiplicative sequence. This smooth construction does not itself define the requested class on a singular space.

Question. For a Witt space $X$, determine hypotheses under which a homology $\widehat A$-class can be defined topologically. When positive-scalar-curvature metrics on the links are used in its construction, determine its dependence on those metrics. Determine also whether a suitable spin Dirac operator defines a $K$-homology class whose Chern character is that homology $\widehat A$-class; symbolically, after making the required choices and constructions precise, ask whether \[ \widehat A_*(X;g_{\mathrm{links}}) =\operatorname{ch}([D_X]). \] Here $g_{\mathrm{links}}$ denotes the link metric data, and $[D_X]$ denotes the proposed class of a spin Dirac operator. These symbols express the requested constructions, not objects asserted to exist under the Witt condition alone.

The source leaves the necessary hypotheses as part of the question. In particular, it does not specify compactness, boundary conditions, orientation or spin data, regularity and compatibility of link metrics, the analytic domain of the Dirac operator, or the intended homology and $K$-homology theories. Consequently, the displayed identity is a conditional target requiring these specifications, rather than a fully determined assertion for every Witt space.

Short English statement

Determine when a Witt space admits a topologically defined homology $\widehat A$-class, how a construction using positive-scalar-curvature link metrics depends on those metrics, and whether the class is the Chern character of a spin Dirac $K$-homology class. The source does not supply the hypotheses needed to make all these constructions precise.
