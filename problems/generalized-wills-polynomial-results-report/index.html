Generalized Wills-Polynomial Results Report

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer. The source considers convex bodies $K,E\in\mathcal K^n$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^n$ and a nonnegative measure $\mu$ on $[0,\infty)$. It does not specify whether its convention for convex bodies requires nonempty interior.

Under the usual relative mixed-volume normalization, the coefficients $W_i(K;E)$ are characterized by \[ \operatorname{vol}_n(K+tE)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}W_i(K;E)t^i,\qquad t\geq0, \] where $K+tE=\{x+ty:x\in K,\ y\in E\}$. This normalization is an interpretive convention; the supplied record does not define $W_i$.

The displayed expression in the source is \[ g_{K;E}^{\mu}(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\binom{n}{i}W_i(K;E)m_i(\mu)z^i. \] The quantities $m_i(\mu)$ are not defined in the supplied record. If ordinary moments are intended, their definition would be \[ m_i(\mu)=\int_{[0,\infty)}t^i\,\mathrm d\mu(t),\qquad 0\leq i\leq n, \] with $t^0=1$, including at $t=0$. This interpretation requires confirmation. Under it, finiteness of all these moments is a sufficient additional hypothesis for the displayed coefficients to be finite. Nonnegativity of $\mu$ alone does not provide this hypothesis.

Whenever the coefficients are well-defined and finite and $g_{K;E}^{\mu}\not\equiv0$, let $\mathcal Z_{K,E}^{\mu}$ be the multiset of its complex roots, counted with algebraic multiplicity. Its degree may be smaller than $n$; a nonzero constant polynomial has an empty root multiset. Under the ordinary-moment interpretation, the allowed zero measure produces the zero polynomial, whose roots cannot be treated as a finite multiset counted with algebraic multiplicity.

Recorded research aim, with unresolved formulation. Determine structural properties shared by $\mathcal Z_{K,E}^{\mu}$ independently of the admissible choice of $\mu$. The source does not identify the properties to be characterized, the admissible measure class, or whether the intended uniformity fixes $n,K,E$ or also varies them. Consequently, this is a research aim rather than a specified proposition with complete quantifiers.
