Math-7065

Openness of Cones Generated by Ample Subvarieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a projective variety, and let $d$ denote the dimension of the subvarieties under consideration. Write \[ N_d(X)=\left(Z_d(X)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}\right)/\!\equiv_{\mathrm{num}}, \] where $Z_d(X)$ is the group of algebraic $d$-cycles and $\equiv_{\mathrm{num}}$ denotes numerical equivalence. Equip this finite-dimensional real vector space with its usual topology. The source does not specify the base field, the range of $d$, or additional hypotheses on $X$.

Let $\mathcal A_d(X)$ denote the collection of $d$-dimensional subvarieties of $X$ that are ample in the sense intended by the source. That sense is not defined in the supplied record and must be fixed before the question has a precise interpretation. Under the convention that a generated convex cone includes the origin, set \[ C_d^{\mathrm{amp}}(X)= \left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_i[Y_i]_{\mathrm{num}}: m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\quad a_i\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0},\quad Y_i\in\mathcal A_d(X)\right\}, \] where the sum for $m=0$ is $0$. No closure is taken.

Source question. For every projective variety $X$ and every dimension $d$ covered by the intended hypotheses, is the convex cone generated by the numerical classes of ample $d$-dimensional subvarieties open in $N_d(X)$?

Unresolved qualifications. With the displayed cone convention, the question asks whether $C_d^{\mathrm{amp}}(X)$ itself is open, including at the origin. The supplied wording does not establish whether the intended object instead excludes the origin, for example $C_d^{\mathrm{amp}}(X)\setminus\{0\}$. These are different formulations and must not be identified silently. Likewise, openness in the ambient space $N_d(X)$ is distinct from relative openness in the linear span of the cone. The wording names $N_d(X)$, but supplies no explicit topology convention beyond this. The definition of ample subvariety, base-field assumptions, admissible dimensions, and any boundary cases such as $d=0$ or $d=\dim X$ require clarification. This is a statement-only draft, with these qualifications unresolved.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether numerical classes of ample subvarieties of a fixed dimension generate an open cone in the real numerical cycle space of a projective variety. A precise formulation requires the intended definition of ample subvariety, the hypotheses on the variety and dimension, and a convention about inclusion of the origin. The record labels the problem open; that status is supplied curation and has not been independently verified.
