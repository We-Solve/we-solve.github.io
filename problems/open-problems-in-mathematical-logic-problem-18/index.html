Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a model and let $\mathbb P\in M$ be a forcing notion, as stipulated in the supplied record. The question is the incomplete implication schema \[ \text{Condition }(*)\text{ holds} \quad\stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}\quad M\models\text{``there exists a large cardinal''}. \] Here $M\models\varphi$ means that the sentence $\varphi$ holds in $M$. However, the supplied record does not state condition $(*)$, specify the theory or structural requirements for $M$, or identify the large-cardinal assertion intended in the conclusion. The quoted conclusion therefore does not specify a fixed sentence. This schema preserves the supplied question, but these missing qualifications prevent a complete mathematical formulation.

Short English statement

For a model $M$ containing a forcing notion $\mathbb P$, does condition $(*)$ imply that $M$ satisfies a large-cardinal existence assertion? The condition, requirements on the model, and intended large-cardinal assertion are missing from the supplied record.
