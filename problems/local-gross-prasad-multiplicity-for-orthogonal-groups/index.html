Local Gross–Prasad Multiplicity for Orthogonal Groups

The supplied record concerns the local Gross--Prasad conjecture for orthogonal groups. It uses parameter spaces $\Phi^{\mathrm{orth}}(d)$ and $\Phi^{\mathrm{orth}}(d-1,\delta)$, a genericity condition, corresponding $L$-packets, and a multiplicity $m(\pi,\sigma)$. The record does not define these objects sufficiently to specify a complete local representation-theoretic setting.

Extracted conjectural statement. For every generic parameter pair \[ \phi\in\Phi^{\mathrm{orth}}(d),\qquad \phi'\in\Phi^{\mathrm{orth}}(d-1,\delta), \] subject to the admissibility and compatibility conditions intended by the source, there exists a unique pair $(\pi_0,\sigma_0)\in\mathcal P(\phi,\phi')$ such that \[ m(\pi_0,\sigma_0)=1, \] and \[ m(\pi,\sigma)=0 \quad\text{for every }(\pi,\sigma)\in\mathcal P(\phi,\phi') \setminus\{(\pi_0,\sigma_0)\}. \] Here the admissible values of $d$ and $\delta$, the underlying local field, the meanings of the parameter spaces and genericity, the groups and packet conventions, and the definition of multiplicity remain unspecified. These missing data are qualifications on this transcription, not additional hypotheses supplied by the record. The source's genericity wording also requires confirmation as to whether it applies separately to both parameters or to their pair.

The supplied background calls this a multiplicity and epsilon-dichotomy conjecture, but supplies no epsilon-factor formula identifying the distinguished pair. Such a formula cannot be reconstructed from this record alone.

Short English statement

The extracted conjecture says that each generic compatible pair of orthogonal parameters has exactly one representation pair of multiplicity one in the intended packets; every other pair has multiplicity zero. The local setting, packet range, and multiplicity definition need clarification before this becomes a fully specified statement. The supplied label is partially_solved; this draft does not independently establish that status.
