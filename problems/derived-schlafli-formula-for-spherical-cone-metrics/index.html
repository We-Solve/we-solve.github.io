Math-6573

Derived Schläfli Formula for Spherical Cone Metrics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A spherical cone metric on the topological sphere $S^2$ is a metric of Gaussian curvature $+1$ away from finitely many marked points, with a conical singularity of total angle $\alpha_i>0$ at the $i$-th marked point. Let $k$ be the fixed number of such points, and set \[ A=2\pi(2-k)+\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_i. \] In this curvature normalization, $A$ is the area given by the cone version of Gauss--Bonnet.

Conditional formulation of the recorded relation. Consider any differentiable family of realizable spherical cone metrics with fixed $k$, parametrized locally by $t$ in an open interval $I\subset{\mathbb{R}}$, with differentiable angles $\alpha_i(t)>0$. Assume that the construction called its Hopf lift to $S^3$ in the supplied record is defined along this family and has volume \[ V(t)=\frac{A(t)^2}{8},\qquad A(t)=2\pi(2-k)+\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_i(t). \] The supplied relation, called the Schläfli formula in the record, is \[ 2\,\frac{\mathrm dV}{\mathrm dt}(t) =\frac{A(t)}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\frac{\mathrm d\alpha_i}{\mathrm dt}(t) \qquad(t\in I), \] or, along such admissible deformations, \[ 2\,\mathrm dV=\frac{A}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\mathrm d\alpha_i. \] The record does not specify the lifted metric, its normalization, the admissible angle configurations, or the deformation class. The formulation therefore assumes the stated volume formula and quantifies only over families for which the geometric construction exists; it does not assert that arbitrary independent angle variations are realizable. No limiting assertion at degenerations or vanishing cone angles is supplied.

Unresolved status of the intended problem. The supplied verification describes this relation as an already completed calculation from the stated volume formula, rather than an open problem. The registry nevertheless labels the entry open and its statement status unrecoverable. A separate supplied assessment says the extracted conjecture was truncated before its claim. These conflicting qualifications prevent identification of a distinct, precise open conjecture.

Short English statement

For a realizable differentiable family of spherical cone metrics with a fixed number of cone points, the record gives a volume formula for an unspecified Hopf lift and records the corresponding Schläfli relation. The supplied verification treats this as a completed calculation; the intended open problem and the precise lift construction remain unresolved.
