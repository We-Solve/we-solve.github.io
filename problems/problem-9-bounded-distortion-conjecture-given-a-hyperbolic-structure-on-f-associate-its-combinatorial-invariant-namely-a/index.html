Problem 9 — [Bounded Distortion Conjecture] Given a hyperbolic structure on F, associate its combinatorial invariant, namely, an…

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a surface in the source's hyperbolic ideal-cell-decomposition setting, with Teichmüller space $\mathcal T(F)$. For a marked hyperbolic structure $X$, the source associates an ideal cell decomposition $\Delta(X)$ and projective simplicial coordinates $[s_e(X)]$ on its edges. The dual fatgraph is the graph with one vertex per cell and one edge per shared side, with cyclic orders induced by the oriented surface. Use the projective simplicial coordinates as Strebel length coordinates on this fatgraph to construct a marked conformal structure $\Phi(X)$. This describes the intended map \[ X\longmapsto(\Delta(X),[s_e(X)])\longmapsto\Phi(X)\in\mathcal T(F). \] The excerpt does not specify the peripheral decorations needed for the hyperbolic construction, normalization of projective coordinates, or zero-edge/cell-boundary conventions. Those choices must be supplied before the map is completely defined.

Problem 9 (Bounded Distortion Conjecture). The resulting self-map of $\mathcal T(F)$ has bounded distortion in the Teichmüller metric. A natural comparison interpretation is uniformly bounded displacement: \[ \exists C(F)<\infty\quad\forall X\in\mathcal T(F),\qquad d_{\mathrm{Teich}}(X,\Phi(X))\le C(F). \] With $d_{\mathrm{Teich}}(X,Y)=\tfrac12\inf\log K(f)$ over marking-compatible quasiconformal maps, this would uniformly control the quasiconformal comparison of the hyperbolic-derived conformal structure and the Strebel-derived one. The source does not define "bounded distortion" explicitly; a Lipschitz/quasi-isometry bound comparing pairs of points would be a different assertion. The displayed displacement formulation is therefore an interpretation requiring source confirmation, not a certified replacement for the phrase.

The appended historical problem, attributed to Ed Witten in the early 1990s, asks for an orbifold compactification of $\mathcal M(F)$ with cells described by generalized fatgraphs, permitting matrix-model calculations such as [16,19,22]. The source allows the desired compactification to be Deligne--Mumford or another one. It says its preceding combinatorial compactification is instead a more general stratified space and announces a following solution guided by Dennis Sullivan. That solution is absent from this record; the compactification announcement must not be confused with a solution of the Bounded Distortion Conjecture.

Short English statement

Compare the conformal structure from a hyperbolic surface with the one obtained by using its simplicial coordinates as Strebel fatgraph lengths, and ask for uniform Teichmüller distortion control. The source separately announces a solution to an orbifold-compactification problem.
