Eigenvalue Counts for Low-Degree Random Regular Graphs

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Every fixed regular degree $d\geq3$ is claimed to give GOE eigenvalue statistics at fixed interior bulk energies for uniformly sampled simple regular graphs.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For fixed $d\ge3$, let $G_{n,d}$ be uniform among simple labeled $d$-regular graphs, at admissible sizes, and $\lambda_i$ its adjacency eigenvalues. Fix $|E|<2\sqrt{d-1}$ and define \[ \rho_d(E)=\frac{d\sqrt{4(d-1)-E^2}}{2\pi(d^2-E^2)},\qquad \Xi_{n,d,E}=\sum_i\delta_{n\rho_d(E)(\lambda_i-E)}. \] For GOE eigenvalues $\mu_i$, with off-diagonal variance $1/n$ and diagonal variance $2/n$, set $\Xi_n^{\mathrm{GOE}}=\sum_i\delta_{(n/\pi)\mu_i}$. The claim is that each nonnegative continuous compactly supported $h$ satisfies \[ \left|\mathbb E e^{-\int h\,d\Xi_{n,d,E}} -\mathbb E e^{-\int h\,d\Xi_n^{\mathrm{GOE}}}\right|\to0. \] The random graph has no further conditioning.

Outline of the argument

An entropy method gives quantitatively accurate Gaussian eigenvector marks and high moments at sampled edges. Reversible edge switches promote those marks to microscopic orthogonal invariance; a Gaussian perturbation and fixed-energy Dyson relaxation identify the point process, with compact-count control completing convergence.
