Math-4312

Irreducibility of Restricted Classical-Group Tensor Representations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $W$ be the natural module for the simple classical algebraic group $\mathcal G=\operatorname{Cl}(W)$ intended by the source, and let $G\le\mathcal G$ be Zariski closed. Consider, in the three separate cases, the $\mathcal G$-modules \[ W\otimes W^*,\qquad\operatorname{Sym}^k(W),\qquad\bigwedge^k(W), \] for the source's "small" values of $k$. Here $W^*$ is the dual module, and a composition factor is a simple subquotient in a composition series, not necessarily a direct summand.

Let $V$ be the source's largest composition factor of the chosen module. Determine when \[ V\mathbin{\downarrow}_G \] is irreducible, meaning that the same vector space with the restricted $G$-action has no nonzero proper invariant linear subspace. The classification must retain the selected simple factor; it is not replaced by irreducibility of the entire tensor, symmetric power, or exterior power.

The field, characteristic, classical-group type and isogeny convention, dimension of $W$, and numerical range of small $k$ are not supplied. Nor is "largest" defined as largest dimension, distinguished highest weight, or another choice, including what to do if several factors tie. These missing data matter especially when the ambient modules are not semisimple. No characteristic-zero or algebraically closed field is imposed as an explicit source assumption.

The subgroup may be finite or positive dimensional and connected or disconnected as written; no properness or connectedness restriction is added. The intended classification equivalence for subgroups also remains unspecified. The assessment reports a solution of the Kollár--Larsen symmetric-power problem for $k\ge4$ by Guralnick and Tiep, but says the broader Problem 2 and extracted small-$k$ cases remain partially classified. That is supplied literature context, not a theorem here about every selected composition factor.

Short English statement

The problem asks which Zariski-closed subgroups keep a specified large simple factor of a classical tensor, symmetric, or exterior power irreducible. The field, small-power range, and rule selecting the largest factor are missing; the whole module must not be substituted for that factor.
