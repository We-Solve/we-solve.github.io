Math-3424

Perfect Weyl-Group Complexes of Flag Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a complex reductive algebraic group, let $T\subseteq G$ be a maximal torus, and let $W=N_G(T)/T$ be its Weyl group. Let $k$ be an arbitrary field, with no restriction on its characteristic, and write $k[W]$ for the group algebra.

Under the usual topological interpretation, put $X=(G/T)({\mathbb{C}})$, with its complex analytic topology, and let $k_X$ denote the constant sheaf with value $k$. The right action of $W$ on $X$ is given by \[ (gT)\cdot(nT)=gnT,\qquad n\in N_G(T). \] Pullback along this right action induces a left $k[W]$-module structure on derived global sections. Thus the object to be described is \[ C_{G,k}:=R\Gamma(X,k_X)\in D(k[W]), \] where $D(k[W])$ denotes the derived category of left $k[W]$-modules. A complex is called perfect if it is quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex of finitely generated projective $k[W]$-modules.

Problem. For every such $G$ and $T$, and every field $k$, describe the perfect complex $C_{G,k}$ up to isomorphism in $D(k[W])$. The target is the derived object, including its $W$-action, rather than merely its cohomology groups.

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the cohomology theory or topology, the convention for the induced $W$-action, or whether "reductive group" requires connectedness. Short English statement

For a complex reductive group $G$, a maximal torus $T$, and any field $k$, describe the derived cohomology of $G/T$ together with its Weyl group action, as a perfect complex of $k[W]$-modules. The analytic cohomology convention and the connectedness convention require source confirmation.
