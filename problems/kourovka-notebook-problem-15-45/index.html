Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.45

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathfrak F$ denote the class of all finite groups. For a class $\mathfrak X$ of groups, define $\mathbf H_1\mathfrak X$ to consist of groups $G$ admitting an admissible action on a finite-dimensional contractible complex $X$ such that every cell stabilizer belongs to $\mathfrak X$. For precision in this draft, a complex is taken to be a CW complex, and an admissible action is a cellular action for which every element stabilizing a cell setwise fixes that cell pointwise. Thus, writing \[ G_\sigma=\{g\in G:g\sigma=\sigma\}, \] the stabilizer requirement is $G_\sigma\in\mathfrak X$ for every cell $\sigma$ of $X$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the category of complexes or define admissibility, so these conventions require confirmation. The complex need not have finitely many cells; its dimension is finite but may depend on $G$.

A class $\mathfrak C$ is $\mathbf H_1$-closed if $\mathbf H_1\mathfrak C\subseteq\mathfrak C$. Define $\mathbf H\mathfrak X$ to be the smallest $\mathbf H_1$-closed class containing $\mathfrak X$. Groups in $\mathbf H\mathfrak F$ are the hierarchically decomposable groups considered here. Set \[ \mathbf H_3\mathfrak F =\mathbf H_1\left(\mathbf H_1(\mathbf H_1\mathfrak F)\right). \]

Part (a), historical question, reported solved in the supplied record. Find a group $G$ such that \[ G\in\mathbf H\mathfrak F\setminus\mathbf H_3\mathfrak F. \]

Transfinite indexing qualification. The source specifies transfinite induction only as being performed "in the obvious way." One conventional completion, used for the conditional formulation below, is \[ \mathbf H_0\mathfrak F=\mathfrak F,\qquad \mathbf H_\alpha\mathfrak F =\mathbf H_1\left(\bigcup_{\beta<\alpha}\mathbf H_\beta\mathfrak F\right) \quad(\alpha>0). \] Here each cell stabilizer belongs to some earlier stage, and the earlier ordinal may depend on the cell; a single earlier stage containing all stabilizers is not required by this formula. In particular, this recursion applies $\mathbf H_1$ at limit ordinals as well. The supplied text does not explicitly distinguish this convention from a recursion that takes only the union of earlier stages at limit ordinals. The intended limit-stage definition must therefore be confirmed.

Part (b), conditional on the intended transfinite convention. Determine whether there exists an ordinal $\alpha$ such that \[ \mathbf H_\alpha\mathfrak F=\mathbf H\mathfrak F, \] meaning that, for every group $G$, \[ G\in\mathbf H_\alpha\mathfrak F \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G\in\mathbf H\mathfrak F. \] The quantifier over $\alpha$ is not restricted to countable ordinals, and no countability restriction on $G$ is supplied.

Supplied status and qualifications. The registry labels the overall problem partially solved. It reports part (a) solved and attributes to Januszkiewicz, Kropholler, and Leary the assertion that, for every countable ordinal $\alpha$, there is a group in $\mathbf H_{\alpha+1}\mathfrak F\setminus\mathbf H_\alpha\mathfrak F$, with the hierarchy understood in the source's intended sense. The editor consequently reports that an ordinal satisfying part (b) cannot be countable. The background also reports torsion-free examples with similar properties, attributed to Fournier-Facio and Sun, but does not specify their exact quantifiers or hierarchy properties. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks how many stages of actions on finite-dimensional contractible complexes are needed to obtain all hierarchically decomposable groups. Part (a), reported solved, asks for a group beyond the third stage. Part (b) asks whether any ordinal stage contains the entire class; the supplied source reports that no countable stage does. The precise limit-stage recursion and action conventions require confirmation.
