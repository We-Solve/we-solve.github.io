Math-6744

Nontrivial Actions on Invariant Prym Homology

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the notation of the supplied Prym-homology setting: $H_K$ denotes the Prym homology under consideration, $\mathcal G_K^\circ$ denotes the identity component of the group acting on it, and $\mathcal D_K\trianglelefteq\mathcal G_K^\circ$ is the connected normal subgroup generated by the one-parameter subgroups arising from lifted Dehn twists. Define the invariant part by \[ H_K^{\mathcal D_K}=\{v\in H_K: d\cdot v=v\text{ for every }d\in\mathcal D_K\}. \] Normality makes this invariant part stable under $\mathcal G_K^\circ$, and the action factors through $\mathcal G_K^\circ/\mathcal D_K$, with \[ (g\mathcal D_K)\cdot v=g\cdot v. \] Question. Does there exist an example in the intended Prym-homology setting such that \[ H_K^{\mathcal D_K}\neq\{0\} \quad\text{and}\quad \exists\,g\in\mathcal G_K^\circ\;\exists\,v\in H_K^{\mathcal D_K} \text{ with }g\cdot v\neq v? \] Thus the requested quotient action must be nontrivial, not merely defined on a nonzero invariant part.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define the admissible data $K$, the underlying surface or covering, the construction or coefficient domain of $H_K$, or the construction and category of $\mathcal G_K$. It also does not specify which lifted Dehn twists yield the one-parameter subgroups, or the sense of connectedness and subgroup generation. These definitions must be recovered from the cited report before the existential question has a fully specified domain. The display above records the intended question conditionally on that missing setting.

Short English statement

The question asks for a Prym-homology example whose subspace fixed by all the lifted-Dehn-twist one-parameter subgroups is nonzero and still carries a nontrivial action of $\mathcal G_K^\circ/\mathcal D_K$. The supplied record leaves the precise class of admissible examples unspecified.
