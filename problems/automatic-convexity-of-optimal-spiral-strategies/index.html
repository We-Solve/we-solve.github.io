Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether every optimal spiral-like fire-containment strategy necessarily satisfies both local convexity and assumption $(\mathrm{A1})$, described in the clean statement as an initial-angle condition.

To display the intended quantifiers, write $\mathcal S$ schematically for the class of admissible spiral-like strategies intended in the source report. Write $O(s)$ for optimality, $L(s)$ for the required local convexity, and $A_1(s)$ for satisfaction of the source's assumption $(\mathrm{A1})$. The extracted question is whether \[ \forall s\in\mathcal S,\qquad O(s)\Longrightarrow\left(L(s)\land A_1(s)\right). \] These symbols are placeholders for missing source definitions, not independently specified mathematical predicates. The supplied record does not define the fire-propagation model, admissibility constraints, spiral-like strategies, optimization criterion, relevant notion of local convexity, or the actual condition $(\mathrm{A1})$. Consequently, it determines the intended universal implication but does not supply enough information for a self-contained precise mathematical statement. Those definitions and their associated hypotheses must be recovered from the cited report before formal mathematical review.

The quoted report passage expresses a belief that local convexity and $(\mathrm{A1})$ hold automatically for optimal spiral-like strategies and calls this an open question; it does not assert the implication as a theorem.

Short English statement

Must every optimal spiral-like fire-containment strategy automatically be locally convex and satisfy the source's initial-angle assumption $(\mathrm{A1})$? The supplied record omits the model, optimality criterion, and definitions needed to make this question precise.
