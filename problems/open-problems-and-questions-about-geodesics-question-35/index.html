Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed manifold, meaning compact and without boundary. The source asks whether metrics with positive entropy are dense in $\mathcal G(M)$ for the $C^k$-topology. It does not explicitly define $\mathcal G(M)$, identify the entropy, or specify the value or range of $k$. Thus the following is a conditional formulation under the usual interpretation that $\mathcal G(M)$ denotes the smooth Riemannian metrics on a fixed smooth manifold $M$, and that entropy means topological entropy of the geodesic flow.

Fix a nonnegative integer $k$. The source works with the $C^k$-topology locally or on closed manifolds and assumes that an atlas can be taken finite with all derivatives of its transition functions bounded. In local coordinates, write a metric as $g=g_{ij}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x^i{\,\mathrm{d}} x^j$. Under the usual uniform-on-compact-subsets interpretation of coordinate convergence, \[ g^{(m)}\longrightarrow g\text{ in }C^k \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \sup_{x\in K}\left|\partial^\alpha g^{(m)}_{ij}(x)-\partial^\alpha g_{ij}(x)\right|\longrightarrow0 \] for every coordinate chart, every compact subset $K$ of its coordinate domain, all coordinate indices $i,j$, and every multi-index $\alpha$ with $|\alpha|\le k$. The source describes convergence of coordinate coefficients and their partial derivatives through order $k$, but does not explicitly state the mode of convergence; the uniform interpretation here is a stated qualification.

For $g\in\mathcal G(M)$, let \[ S_gM=\{(x,v)\in TM:g_x(v,v)=1\} \] be its unit tangent bundle, and let $\phi_g^t$ be its geodesic flow. Write $h_{\mathrm{top}}(\phi_g^1)$ for the topological entropy of the time-one map. Under these interpretations, the question is whether \[ \overline{\{g\in\mathcal G(M):h_{\mathrm{top}}(\phi_g^1)>0\}}^{\,C^k} =\mathcal G(M). \] Equivalently, does every $g\in\mathcal G(M)$ and every $C^k$-neighborhood $U$ of $g$ admit a metric $\widetilde g\in U$ whose geodesic flow has positive topological entropy?

The intended entropy, metric regularity, allowed values of $k$, and any dimension or connectedness restrictions require confirmation from the source context. If measure-theoretic entropy is intended, the relevant invariant measure must also be specified; the displayed topological-entropy formulation does not settle that interpretation.

Short English statement

On a closed manifold, can every metric be approximated in the $C^k$-topology by metrics with positive entropy? The displayed formulation interprets metrics as smooth Riemannian metrics and entropy as topological entropy of their geodesic flows. These interpretations and the intended range of $k$ remain unconfirmed. The supplied registry labels the problem open.
