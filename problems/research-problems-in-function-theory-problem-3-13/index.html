Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 3.13

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a subharmonic function $u$ on $\mathbb R^m$, its mass means its Riesz measure, a fixed positive normalization of the distributional Laplacian $\Delta u$. Saying that all mass lies on $S$ means $\operatorname{supp}(\Delta u)\subseteq S$; it does not say that $u$ itself vanishes off $S$. Let $N(r,u)$ and $T(r)$ be the higher-dimensional Nevanlinna counting and characteristic functions intended by the source, and put \[ \delta(u)=1-\limsup_{r\to\infty}\frac{N(r,u)}{T(r)},\qquad \rho(u)=\limsup_{r\to\infty}\frac{\log^+T(r)}{\log r} \] when the characteristic is nontrivial. The source refers to analogues of planar counting functions but gives no formulas or normalizations; these must be fixed for numerical sharp bounds.

Questions (D. Shea). For order $\rho>1$, determine the corresponding sharp upper bound on $\delta(u)$, analogous to the supplied result for $\rho<1$. In addition, if the Riesz mass is supported on a ray, can one find a uniform lower bound $A(\rho)$ on $\delta(u)$? More generally, for a fixed suitable lower-dimensional set $S\subset\mathbb R^m$, find a threshold $\rho_0(S)$ and a bound, written with suppressed dependencies as $A(\rho)$ in the source, such that \[ \delta(u)\geq A_{m,S}(\rho) \] for every eligible subharmonic $u$ of order $\rho>\rho_0(S)$ with Riesz measure supported on $S$. The conjectured high-order behavior is \[ \lim_{\rho\to\infty}A_{m,S}(\rho)=1 \] for fixed $m$ and $S$, in the range where the class is nonempty. This asks for a bound uniform over the functions of each order, not merely a statement about one fixed function.

The source reports a sharp upper bound for $\rho<1$, attained with mass on a ray, and attributes the planar high-order lower-bound result to Hellerstein and Shea. Its research summary describes the higher-dimensional $m>2$, $\rho>1$ problem as open as of 2018, with no recent resolution located.

Short English statement

Study deficiency bounds for high-order subharmonic functions, especially when their Riesz mass lies on a ray or another fixed lower-dimensional set. The proposed lower bound should approach one as the order tends to infinity.
