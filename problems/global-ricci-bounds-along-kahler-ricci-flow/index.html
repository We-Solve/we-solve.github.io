Global Ricci Bounds along Kähler–Ricci Flow

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ and $(\omega(t))_{t\in I}$ denote the complex manifold and Kähler--Ricci flow intended in the supplied source record, with $I$ its forward time interval. A Kähler form $\omega(t)$ determines a Hermitian metric $g(t)$. Its Ricci form is locally \[ \operatorname{Ric}(\omega(t))=-\sqrt{-1}\,\partial\bar\partial\log\det(g_{j\bar k}(t)), \] and $|\operatorname{Ric}(\omega(t))|_{\omega(t)}(x)$ denotes its pointwise norm with respect to the evolving metric at $x\in X$.

The recorded conjectural target is the existence of a finite constant $C$, independent of space and time, such that \[ \sup_{t\in I}\sup_{x\in X} |\operatorname{Ric}(\omega(t))|_{\omega(t)}(x)\le C. \] The constant may depend on the fixed geometric data and initial metric; the record requests no uniformity across different initial data. If the intended flow exists for every forward time, then $I=[0,\infty)$, and the displayed bound is required on that entire interval, including the initial time.

Unresolved hypotheses. The supplied record does not specify the geometric assumptions on $X$, its dimension, compactness, initial metric, any relevant fibration, the normalization of the flow, or hypotheses guaranteeing existence for all forward time. Consequently, the display states the requested bound for the intended flow but does not determine a complete conjecture quantified over a specified class of manifolds and flows. In particular, no exception near singular fibers is allowed in the requested global bound, should such fibers occur in the intended setting.

The original excerpt also describes a conditional limiting argument involving rescaled flows $\widetilde\omega_i$, points $x_i$, and the normalization \[ |\operatorname{Ric}(\widetilde\omega_i(0))|_{\widetilde\omega_i(0)}(x_i)=1. \] It proposes an eternal unnormalized limiting Kähler--Ricci flow, asserts conditionally that a cited Song--Tian result would make that limit scalar-flat and hence Ricci-flat, and expresses hope that the displayed normalization passes to the limit. The rescaling, convergence hypotheses, and cited result are not supplied, and the excerpt ends mid-sentence. These remarks are source motivation, not additional established conclusions or a completed argument.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Ricci curvature of the intended Kähler--Ricci flow has one finite bound valid at every point and every forward time, measured using the evolving metric. The supplied record omits the geometric hypotheses and flow normalization needed to specify the full conjecture. Its supplied partially solved label and literature assessment require independent review.
