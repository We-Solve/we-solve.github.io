Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for the proof-theoretic ordinals of second-order arithmetic, ZFC, and stronger theories. A standard recursive-well-order convention for an appropriately sound formal theory $T$ is \[ |T|_{\mathrm{WO}}=\sup\{\operatorname{ot}(\prec):\ \prec\text{ is a recursive well-order on }{\mathbb{N}}, \ T\vdash\operatorname{WO}(\prec)\}. \] Here $\operatorname{WO}(\prec)$ says that every nonempty subset of the domain has a $\prec$-least element, formalized in the language of the theory, and $\operatorname{ot}$ denotes actual ordinal order type. The recursive presentations and the formal proof system are part of the convention. Soundness assumptions are needed to interpret the intended calibration of strength, rather than treating arbitrary incorrect well-foundedness assertions as evidence.

The source's parenthetical "smallest ordinal a theory cannot prove well-founded" is a heuristic, not a definition independent of notation: theories prove statements about presentations/notations of orders, and different presentations of the same ordinal need not have the same provability behavior. A canonical supremum convention avoids choosing a least unprovable notation by code order.

The supplied summary identifies the second-order target as full second-order arithmetic $Z_2$, rather than a particular weak subsystem. This must mean a specified formal axiom-scheme/proof theory, not automatically full semantic consequence in second-order logic. ZFC can likewise be asked to prove well-foundedness of recursively presented orders; its quantification over set-theoretic ordinals does not by itself define a first unprovably well-founded ordinal.

The requested work is an explicit ordinal analysis or structural description of these invariants under a fixed convention. The source supplies no chosen notation system, base theory, proof calculus, soundness assumptions, or class of stronger theories. No ordinal value is asserted here, and the source's informal minimum formulation is not silently substituted for a precise supremum.

Short English statement

Determine an explicit ordinal analysis for full second-order arithmetic, ZFC, and specified stronger theories. First fix recursive-order presentations, provability and soundness conventions; the informal first-unprovable-ordinal phrase is representation-dependent.
