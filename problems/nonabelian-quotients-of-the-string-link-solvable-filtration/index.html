Nonabelian Quotients of the String Link Solvable Filtration

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $m\geq2$, and let $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$. Fix distinct marked points $p_1,\ldots,p_m$ in the interior of the disk $D^2$. An $m$-component string link consists of $m$ disjoint, ordered, oriented strands in $D^2\times[0,1]$, with strand $i$ running from $(p_i,0)$ to $(p_i,1)$, and with the usual fixed behavior near the boundary. String links modulo concordance relative to the boundary form a group $\mathcal C_m$ under stacking. The supplied record does not specify whether concordance is smooth or locally flat topological; the intended category must be checked in the cited source.

Write $\mathcal F_n^m$ and $\mathcal F_{n+\frac12}^m$ for the integer and subsequent half-integer terms of the $n$-solvable filtration of $\mathcal C_m$ referred to in the source. Thus the notation $\mathcal F_{n.5}^m$ in the record means $\mathcal F_{n+\frac12}^m$. These symbols retain the source's intended solvability convention: the supplied record does not give the defining four-dimensional solvability conditions, so a complete intrinsic definition of these subgroups cannot be recovered from the supplied text alone. The quotient notation presupposes $\mathcal F_{n+\frac12}^m\trianglelefteq\mathcal F_n^m$.

Question. With these source-dependent conventions fixed, is it true that \[ \forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq2}\;\forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\qquad \mathcal F_n^m/\mathcal F_{n+\frac12}^m \text{ is nonabelian}? \] Equivalently, writing $[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$, the question asks whether \[ \forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq2}\;\forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\; \exists x,y\in\mathcal F_n^m \quad [x,y]\notin\mathcal F_{n+\frac12}^m. \] The intended concordance category and precise solvability convention remain unresolved qualifications of this statement; the displayed question does not assert that the answer is affirmative.

Short English statement

For every integer number of components $m\geq2$ and every integer level $n\geq0$, must there be two $n$-solvable string-link concordance classes whose commutator does not lie in the next half-level of the solvable filtration? The record leaves the concordance category and technical definition of solvability unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved, subject to the dated source qualification below.
