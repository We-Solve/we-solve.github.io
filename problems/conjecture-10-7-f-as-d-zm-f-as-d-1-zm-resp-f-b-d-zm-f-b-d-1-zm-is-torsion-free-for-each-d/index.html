Conjecture 10.7 — F as d (ZM)/F as d+1(ZM) (resp.F b d (ZM)/F b d+1(ZM)) is torsion free for each d.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal M$ be the free abelian group on the source's intended 3-manifold classes, and let $F_d^{\mathrm{as}}$ and $F_d^{\mathrm b}$ be its two specified descending finite-type filtrations. Conjecture 10.7 asserts, for every degree $d$, that both \[ F_d^{\mathrm{as}}({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal M)/F_{d+1}^{\mathrm{as}}({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal M) \quad\text{and}\quad F_d^{\mathrm b}({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal M)/F_{d+1}^{\mathrm b}({\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal M) \] are torsion free.

Precisely, for either filtration $F^\star$, if $x\in F_d^\star$ and $m$ is a nonzero integer with $mx\in F_{d+1}^\star$, then $x\in F_{d+1}^\star$. This is the saturation of $F_{d+1}^\star$ inside $F_d^\star$. No assertion of finite generation or freeness is added.

In the usual finite-type 3-manifold context, the labels refer to algebraically split and boundary-link surgery filtrations, often defined by alternating sums over sublink surgeries. Their exact admissible links, framings, manifold class and degree indexing are not included here and must be recovered from the source. The integral coefficient group ${\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal M$ is explicit: rationalizing or inverting selected primes would change the torsion question. The conjecture is not replaced by the separate diagram-algebra torsion-freeness statement appearing in the next source item.

Short English statement

Are all associated graded groups of both specified integral 3-manifold finite-type filtrations torsion free? The exact surgery and indexing conventions are missing; the claim is integral and applies to each degree.
