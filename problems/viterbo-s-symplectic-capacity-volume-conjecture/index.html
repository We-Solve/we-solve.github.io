Viterbo's Symplectic Capacity–Volume Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Equip ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, for a positive integer $n$, with its standard symplectic form $\omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^n {\,\mathrm{d}} q_j\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}} p_j$. Let $\operatorname{vol}_{2n}$ denote Lebesgue volume, equivalently the volume associated with $\omega_0^n/n!$. Write $c_{\mathrm{EHZ}}$ for the Ekeland--Hofer--Zehnder symplectic capacity, with the usual normalization \[ c_{\mathrm{EHZ}}\left(B^{2n}(r)\right)=\pi r^2, \qquad B^{2n}(r)=\{z\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}:\lVert z\rVert<r\},\quad r>0. \] This normalization makes explicit the normalized-capacity interpretation mentioned in the supplied background.

Viterbo's capacity--volume conjecture, as supplied. For every positive integer $n$ and every convex domain $X\subset{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, is it true that \[ c_{\mathrm{EHZ}}(X)^n\leq n!\operatorname{vol}_{2n}(X)? \] The source does not specify whether "convex domain" is restricted to bounded open convex sets, whether closures are included, or which extension of the capacity is intended for nonsmooth or unbounded sets. It imposes no explicit boundary regularity. These conventions remain unresolved; the displayed question preserves the source's domain wording and does not silently add boundedness or smoothness. The quantification over positive integers makes explicit the implicit dimension parameter.

Short English statement

Viterbo's conjecture asks whether the $n$th power of the normalized Ekeland--Hofer--Zehnder capacity of every convex domain in ${\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$ is at most $n!$ times its Euclidean volume. The precise class of domains and associated capacity-extension conventions require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified here.
