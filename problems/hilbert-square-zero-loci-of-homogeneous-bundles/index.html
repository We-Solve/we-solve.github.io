Hilbert-Square Zero Loci of Homogeneous Bundles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not define the section space containing $\sigma$, the construction of $Y_\sigma$, or the class of surfaces containing $S$. Consequently, the following records the requested constructions with these parameters unresolved.

For a surface $S$ over a field $k$, write \[ S^{[2]}=\operatorname{Hilb}^{2}(S) \] for the Hilbert scheme of zero-dimensional subschemes of length two. The record does not specify $k$ or impose an explicit hypothesis on $S$; the K3 surfaces mentioned in reference titles do not by themselves supply such a hypothesis.

For a vector bundle $E$ on a variety $X$, global generation means that the evaluation morphism \[ H^0(X,E)\otimes\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow E \] is surjective. Given an algebraic group $G$ acting transitively on $X$, a homogeneous vector bundle means a vector bundle equipped with a compatible $G$-action linear on fibers. For $s\in H^0(X,E)$, let $Z(s)$ denote its scheme-theoretic zero locus. In this algebraic setting, a property holds for a general section if it holds for every section in some nonempty Zariski-open subset of $H^0(X,E)$.

Under the usual compact complex convention, an irreducible symplectic manifold is a connected, simply connected compact Kähler manifold $M$ such that \[ H^0(M,\Omega_M^2)={\mathbb{C}}\omega, \] where $\omega$ is an everywhere nondegenerate holomorphic two-form. The record does not explicitly specify this convention or the underlying field.

The source asks for the following.

  1. Find specific sections $\sigma$ for which there is an isomorphism \[ Y_\sigma\cong S^{[2]}. \] The intended section space, construction of $Y_\sigma$, allowed surfaces $S$, and category of isomorphism require source clarification. A birational equivalence alone does not satisfy the stated isomorphism requirement.
  2. Find other globally generated homogeneous vector bundles whose general zero locus is an irreducible symplectic manifold. For a specified admissible pair $(X,E)$, the general-section requirement has the form \[ \exists\,\varnothing\ne U\subseteq H^0(X,E)\text{ Zariski open}\quad \forall s\in U,\quad Z(s)\text{ is an irreducible symplectic manifold}. \] The admissible ambient varieties, groups, bundle ranks, dimensions of zero loci, and examples relative to which the bundles must be "other" are not supplied. The question requests examples; it does not explicitly require a complete classification.
The supplied dated assessment reports partial progress through Debarre--Voisin degenerations and special examples with isomorphic or birational Hilbert-square models, while describing an explicit $\sigma$ and a general bundle classification as remaining open. These are supplied literature qualifications, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

Find explicit sections whose associated varieties are isomorphic to Hilbert squares of the intended surfaces, and find further globally generated homogeneous bundles with irreducible symplectic general zero loci. The record omits the construction of the varieties, the section space, the surface hypotheses, and the permitted homogeneous spaces. Its supplied partial-solution assessment requires independent review.
