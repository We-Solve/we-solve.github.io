Math-6964

Representation Models for Toric Cohomology and Symmetric Functions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $B_n$ be the Boolean lattice of subsets of $[n]$ and let $C_n$ be an $n$-element chain. The product symbol in the source is interpreted as the Rees product of ranked posets: $P*Q$ consists of pairs $(p,q)$ with $\rho_P(p)\geq\rho_Q(q)$, ordered by \[ (p,q)\leq(p',q')\iff p\leq p',\ q\leq q',\ \rho_P(p')-\rho_P(p)\geq\rho_Q(q')-\rho_Q(q). \] Put $P_n=(B_n\setminus\{\widehat0\})*C_n$. The symmetric group $S_n$ acts on subsets and fixes the chain coordinate. With ranks $\rho(A)=|A|-1$ and chain coordinates $j=0,\ldots,n-1$, its maximal elements are $m_j=([n],j)$. After adjoining a minimum, the corresponding maximal open intervals are $I_{n,j}=(\widehat0,m_j)$; their order complexes carry induced $S_n$-representations on reduced homology.

Let $X_n$ be the smooth projective toric variety of the type-$A_{n-1}$ Coxeter fan, with its natural $S_n$-action. The first task is a direct, explicitly constructed equivariant isomorphism between its cohomology representation and the sign twist of the interval-homology representation whose Frobenius characteristics the report identifies. In standard indexing the component comparison takes the form \[ H^{2j}(X_n;{\mathbb{C}})\cong \operatorname{sgn}_{S_n}\otimes \widetilde H_{n-2}(\Delta(I_{n,j});{\mathbb{C}}), \qquad0\leq j\leq n-1. \] The component indexing and small-$n$ conventions must agree with the report; they are not specified in the extract. An abstract existence deduction from equality of characters is not the requested direct construction.

For a partition $\lambda\vdash n$ and an admissible index $j$, the second task asks for an actual finite-dimensional $S_n$-representation $V_{\lambda,j}$ with \[ \operatorname{ch}(V_{\lambda,j})=Q_{\lambda,j}. \] For a representation $V$, its Frobenius characteristic is $\operatorname{ch}(V)=\sum_{\mu\vdash n}\chi_V(\mu)p_\mu/z_\mu$, where $p_\mu$ is the power-sum symmetric function and $z_\mu$ the centralizer order of a permutation of cycle type $\mu$. Thus the existence question is equivalent to Schur expansion coefficients of $Q_{\lambda,j}$ being nonnegative integers. The source's partition-indexed Eulerian quasisymmetric function is retained; the extract gives no defining formula, index range or normalization for it. It is not replaced by the unrefined Eulerian function or by a virtual character.

Short English statement

Construct the direct equivariant correspondence between type-$A$ toric cohomology and sign-twisted Rees-product interval homology. Separately, realize every intended partition-indexed $Q_{\lambda,j}$ as the Frobenius characteristic of an actual symmetric-group representation.
