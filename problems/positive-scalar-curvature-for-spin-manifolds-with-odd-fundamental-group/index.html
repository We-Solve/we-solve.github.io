Math-7223

Positive Scalar Curvature for Spin Manifolds with Odd Fundamental Group

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed (compact without boundary), connected, smooth spin manifold of dimension $d\geq 5$, and let $G=\pi_1(M)$ be a finite abelian group of odd order. For a prime $p$, call $M$ $p$-atoral if, for every integer $\ell\geq 1$ and every $d$-tuple $c_1,\ldots,c_d\in H^1(M;{\mathbb{Z}}/p^\ell)$, \[ \langle c_1\smile\cdots\smile c_d,[M]_{p^\ell}\rangle=0\quad\text{in }{\mathbb{Z}}/p^\ell, \] where $[M]_{p^\ell}\in H_d(M;{\mathbb{Z}}/p^\ell)$ is the reduction of the fundamental class determined by the spin orientation. Assume this condition for every prime $p\mid |G|$. If $G$ is trivial, this collection of conditions is vacuous.

Using the standard real convention, write \[ \alpha(M)=\operatorname{Ind}_{C^*_{{\mathbb{R}}}G}\left(D_M\otimes\mathcal L_G\right)\in KO_d(C^*_{{\mathbb{R}}}G) \] for the Rosenberg invariant: the real higher index of the spin Dirac operator $D_M$, twisted by the flat bundle $\mathcal L_G=\widetilde M\times_G C^*_{{\mathbb{R}}}G$. Here $C^*_{{\mathbb{R}}}G$ is the real group $C^*$-algebra; its maximal and reduced completions agree for finite $G$.

The proposed statement is that, for every such spin manifold, \[ \alpha(M)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{there exists a smooth Riemannian metric }g\text{ on }M \text{ with }\operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0\text{ for every }x\in M. \] The supplied original formulation asks whether a non-spin positive-scalar-curvature existence theorem extends to this spin setting. The supplied cleaned statement identifies the invariant as the Rosenberg invariant; the real convention above makes that interpretation explicit, although the record does not specify its coefficient convention. This is a proposed implication requiring mathematical review, not an asserted theorem.

Short English statement

Does every closed connected smooth spin manifold of dimension at least five, with finite abelian fundamental group of odd order and the stated vanishing of all top-degree products of degree-one classes over ${\mathbb{Z}}/p^\ell$ for each relevant prime and every $\ell\geq1$, admit a metric of everywhere positive scalar curvature whenever its Rosenberg invariant vanishes? The supplied record labels this spin extension open.
