Math-4352

Dimension Constraints for Quadratic Forms over Quadric Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a field, with no restriction on its characteristic stated in the supplied record. A quadratic form on a finite-dimensional $F$-vector space $V$ is a map $r:V\to F$ such that $r(cv)=c^2r(v)$ and $r(u+v)-r(u)-r(v)$ is bilinear in $u,v$. Write $\dim(r)=\dim_F(V)$. The form is anisotropic if $r(v)=0$ implies $v=0$. For a field extension $L/F$, write $r_L$ for scalar extension and interpret its isotropy index as \[ i_0(r_L)=\max\{\dim_L U: U\subseteq V\otimes_F L\text{ is an }L\text{-linear subspace and }r_L|_U=0\}. \] This interpretation includes possibly singular forms in characteristic $2$; the source does not explicitly define $i_0$ or impose nonsingularity.

Let $p$ and $q$ be anisotropic quadratic forms over $F$, and let $Q_p\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}(V_p)$ be the projective quadric defined by $p=0$. Whenever $Q_p$ is nonempty and integral, write $F(p)=F(Q_p)$ for its function field. Let $s\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ be the unique integer satisfying \[ 2^s<\dim(p)\leq 2^{s+1}, \qquad k=\dim(q)-2i_0(q_{F(p)}). \] The supplied question asks whether, for every such pair over every field in the intended class, one has \[ k\geq0 \quad\text{and}\quad \exists a\in\mathbb N\ \exists\epsilon\in{\mathbb{Z}}:\qquad -k\leq\epsilon\leq k, \qquad \dim(q)=a\,2^{s+1}+\epsilon. \] The record does not specify whether $0\in\mathbb N$; that convention is left unresolved here because it changes the assertion. It also omits a dimension hypothesis on $p$ ensuring that $F(p)$ exists: a one-dimensional anisotropic form defines an empty projective quadric. Thus the displayed question preserves the supplied target wherever its function-field notation is defined; its unrestricted formulation requires clarification. No characteristic restriction or nonsingularity hypothesis has been added.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the isotropy acquired by $q$ over the function field of the quadric defined by $p$ forces $\dim(q)$ to lie within $k$ of a permitted multiple of $2^{s+1}$, and whether $k$ is always nonnegative. The convention for $\mathbb N$, the scope of quadratic forms, and the missing dimension hypothesis require review. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
