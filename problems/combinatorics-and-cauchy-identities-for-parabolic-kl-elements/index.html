Combinatorics and Cauchy Identities for Parabolic KL Elements

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In standard Coxeter terminology, for a Coxeter system $(W,S)$ and $J\subseteq S$, the standard parabolic subgroup is \[ W_J=\langle s:s\in J\rangle. \] A maximal proper standard parabolic corresponds to $J=S\setminus\{s\}$ for some $s\in S$; a non-maximal proper standard parabolic has $J\subsetneq S$ that is not maximal among proper subsets. These conventions explain possible terminology only: the supplied record does not specify the intended Coxeter system, parabolic modules, or meaning of "maximally parabolic." Kazhdan--Lusztig elements are the distinguished elements arising from the relevant Kazhdan--Lusztig construction; the construction and normalization intended here are not supplied.

The source poses two research questions:

  1. Find a combinatorial interpretation for evaluations of Kazhdan--Lusztig elements for non-maximal parabolic modules.
  2. Find a Cauchy summation formula for the Kazhdan--Lusztig elements of a maximally parabolic module.
The evaluations in the first question are unspecified. The summation indices, variables, coefficients, pairing of elements, and proposed right-hand side in the second question are also unspecified. Consequently, these are source-level research requests, not a uniquely determined quantified conjecture or identity. In particular, no assertion over all Coxeter systems or all parabolic modules is implied by this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks for combinatorial interpretations of evaluations of non-maximal parabolic Kazhdan--Lusztig elements and for a Cauchy summation identity in a maximally parabolic setting. The supplied record omits the algebraic setting and the evaluations and identity to be studied, so further source context is needed for a fully precise formulation.
