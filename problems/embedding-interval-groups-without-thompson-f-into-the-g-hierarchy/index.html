Embedding Interval Groups Without Thompson F into the G-Hierarchy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source does not define the family $(G_\xi)_{\xi<\varepsilon_0}$. Consequently, the following records its quantified assertion, but a complete mathematical formulation requires the intended definition of that family.

Under the conventional interpretation $I=[0,1]$, let $\mathrm{PL}_+(I)$ be the group, under composition, of increasing piecewise-linear homeomorphisms of $I$ with finitely many linear pieces. Such homeomorphisms fix both endpoints. The source does not explicitly specify the interval or the convention concerning the number of pieces; these conventions require confirmation.

Let Thompson's group $F$ be the subgroup of $\mathrm{PL}_+([0,1])$ consisting of maps whose breakpoints are dyadic rationals and whose slopes belong to $\{2^n:n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}$. A group embedding is an injective group homomorphism, and containing a copy of $F$ means having a subgroup isomorphic to $F$. Let $\varepsilon_0$ be the least nonzero ordinal $\alpha$ satisfying $\omega^\alpha=\alpha$. A group is elementary amenable if it belongs to the smallest class containing all finite groups and all abelian groups and closed under subgroups, quotients, group extensions, and directed unions.

The supplied conjecture asks whether \[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every finitely generated }G\leq\mathrm{PL}_+(I),\\ \left(\text{there is no injective homomorphism }F\to G\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ \left(\exists\xi<\varepsilon_0\;\exists\text{ injective homomorphism }G\to G_\xi\right). \end{gathered} \] The source also asks, as an asserted particular case, whether \[ \begin{gathered} \text{for every finitely generated elementary amenable }H\leq F,\\ \exists\xi<\varepsilon_0\;\exists\text{ injective homomorphism }H\to G_\xi. \end{gathered} \] Here $G_\xi$ must refer to the specific hierarchy intended by the source, rather than an arbitrary ordinal-indexed family. Its construction and indexing conventions are absent from the supplied record. The particular-case wording is preserved from the source; no proof of that implication is supplied here.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every finitely generated group of piecewise-linear interval homeomorphisms containing no subgroup isomorphic to Thompson's group $F$ embeds into a member of a specified hierarchy below $\varepsilon_0$. It also asks this for finitely generated elementary amenable subgroups of $F$. The hierarchy is undefined in the supplied record, so the statement requires source clarification and human mathematical review.
