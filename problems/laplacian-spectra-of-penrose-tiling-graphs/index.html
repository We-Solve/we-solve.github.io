For a specified graph and a specified self-adjoint realization $\Delta_G$ of its Laplacian on a Hilbert space $H_G$, define \[ \sigma(\Delta_G)=\{\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}:\Delta_G-\lambda I\text{ does not have an everywhere-defined bounded inverse on }H_G\}. \] The pure point, absolutely continuous, and singular continuous spectral subspaces are the subspaces determined by the corresponding types of spectral measures of $\Delta_G$. An eigenfunction at energy $\lambda$ is a nonzero $f\in\operatorname{Dom}(\Delta_G)$ satisfying $\Delta_G f=\lambda f$; it has finite support if $\{v\in V:f(v)\ne0\}$ is finite.
