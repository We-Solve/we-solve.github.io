Math-4350

Embedding Independence of Congruence Subgroups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N\geq 1$ be an integer, let $G$ be a finitely generated group, and let $\iota:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}})$ be an injective group homomorphism. For each integer $m\geq 1$, define the principal congruence subgroup \[ \Gamma_N(m)=\ker\!\left(\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}})\longrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}}/m{\mathbb{Z}})\right), \] where the map is reduction of matrix entries modulo $m$, and $\Gamma_N(1)=\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}})$. Use the standard convention that an ambient congruence subgroup is a subgroup $C\leq\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}})$ containing $\Gamma_N(m)$ for some $m\geq 1$. Transporting intersections back to the abstract group $G$, define \[ \mathcal C_{\iota}=\left\{\iota^{-1}(C): C\leq\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}}),\ \Gamma_N(m)\leq C\text{ for some }m\geq 1\right\}. \] Equivalently, with $K_{\iota}(m)=\iota^{-1}(\Gamma_N(m))$, this family consists of the subgroups $H\leq G$ containing $K_{\iota}(m)$ for at least one $m\geq 1$.

Question. For every integer $N\geq 1$, every finitely generated group $G$, and every pair of embeddings $\iota_1,\iota_2:G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}})$, must \[ \mathcal C_{\iota_1}=\mathcal C_{\iota_2} \] hold? Equivalently, can the family of congruence subgroups of the abstract group $G$ depend on its chosen embedding?

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied wording does not explicitly say whether the target dimension must remain fixed when comparing embeddings. The displayed question compares embeddings into the same $\operatorname{SL}_N({\mathbb{Z}})$; allowing embeddings into $\operatorname{SL}_{N_1}({\mathbb{Z}})$ and $\operatorname{SL}_{N_2}({\mathbb{Z}})$ gives the corresponding broader formulation, whose intended inclusion requires source review. The definition above uses all ambient congruence subgroups, rather than only principal congruence subgroups; the supplied record does not explicitly spell out this convention.

Short English statement

A chosen integral matrix embedding gives congruence subgroups of $G$ by pulling back ambient subgroups containing a principal congruence subgroup. The question asks whether changing the embedding changes this family. Whether comparison across different matrix dimensions is intended remains unspecified.
