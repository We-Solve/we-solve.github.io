Mechanisms Producing Equatorial Superrotation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
Equatorial superrotation is an eastward equatorial mean flow with axial angular momentum exceeding the local solid-body reference. Planetary-scale heating forces the large-scale atmospheric circulation; upscale momentum flux transfers momentum from finer-scale fluctuations to the mean flow. In the report's balanced first-baroclinic calculation, heating amplitude $S$ and momentum-flux amplitude $F$ produce a near-equatorial meridional momentum flux proportional to $-ySF e^{-y^2}$.
Source scope and qualifications.
The report gives a mechanism and sufficient conditions in a canonical balanced model. It does not provide universal necessary-and-sufficient conditions for all planets or all atmospheric regimes, and that broader claim is not substituted for the stated request.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
What features of planetary-scale heating and upscale momentum flux are necessary to create equatorial superrotation?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Planetary-scale heating and Rossby-wave momentum transport are established mechanisms for equatorial superrotation, but a complete necessary-and-sufficient characterization across planetary regimes remains open. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Identify which large-scale heating and smaller-scale momentum transfers produce eastward equatorial winds.
