Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.76

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A variety of groups is a class of groups defined by group identities. For a variety $\Theta$, let $\Theta^0$ be the category whose objects are the groups free in $\Theta$ on finitely many generators and whose morphisms are all group homomorphisms between these objects.

An automorphism $\varphi:\Theta^0\to\Theta^0$ of this category is called inner if it is naturally isomorphic to the identity functor. Explicitly, this means that there exists a family of group isomorphisms \[ s_A:A\longrightarrow\varphi(A) \qquad(A\in\operatorname{Ob}(\Theta^0)) \] such that, for every pair of objects $A,B$ and every homomorphism $\mu:A\to B$, \[ \varphi(\mu)=s_B\circ\mu\circ s_A^{-1}. \] Write $P(\Theta)$ for the assertion that every automorphism of $\Theta^0$ is inner.

For a group $G$, define its derived series by \[ G^{(0)}=G,\qquad G^{(j+1)}=[G^{(j)},G^{(j)}], \] where $[H,H]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$ with $x,y\in H$. For each integer $d\geq1$, let \[ \mathcal S_d=\{G:G^{(d)}=\{1\}\} \] be the variety of all groups of derived length at most $d$. The variety of all metabelian groups is $\mathcal S_2$; in particular, this includes groups whose derived length is less than two.

The remaining question in source part $b$ asks whether $P(\Theta)$ holds for the varieties of solvable groups, and whether it holds for the variety of all metabelian groups. The metabelian question has the precise formulation \[ P(\mathcal S_2)\;? \] A precise fixed-derived-length interpretation of the solvable question is \[ \text{For every integer }d\geq1,\quad P(\mathcal S_d)\;? \] This latter formulation is conditional on interpreting the source as referring to the varieties of all groups of bounded derived length. The source does not explicitly specify the derived-length parameter or whether more general varieties consisting of solvable groups are intended. The class of all solvable groups with no uniform bound on derived length is not itself a variety, so it cannot be substituted silently for $\Theta$.

For context, the supplied source reports that $P(\Theta)$ holds for the variety of all groups and for the variety of all abelian groups. Archived part $a$ originally asked whether it holds for the variety of all nilpotent groups of class at most two. The supplied archive reports an affirmative answer, even for nilpotent groups of any fixed class $n$, attributed to A. Tsurkov. The updated statement of part $b$ explicitly reports the result for the varieties of all nilpotent groups of class at most $d$, for $d\geq2$.

The supplied registry labels the combined record partially solved, with the nilpotent part archived as solved and the remaining questions retained in the main section. A separate supplied main-section comment claims an affirmative answer but expressly labels it an unverified AI claim and an unverified AI solution. That comment is not treated here as an established resolution.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every automorphism of the category of finitely generated free metabelian groups is naturally isomorphic to the identity, and asks the analogous question for varieties of solvable groups. The solvable formulation needs clarification about the intended varieties; one precise interpretation uses each fixed bound on derived length. The source reports the nilpotent part as solved and separately includes an expressly unverified AI claim about an affirmative answer.
