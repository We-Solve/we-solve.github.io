Deformation Spaces of Smooth Complete Toric Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a smooth complete toric variety. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, its characteristic, the dimension of $X$, or whether deformations are ordinary, equivariant, or subject to additional conditions. Completeness is part of the hypothesis; projectivity is not assumed.

For a possible formal interpretation, fix a field $k$ and a deformation problem for $X$ over $k$. Suppose that a miniversal formal deformation base is described by a complete Noetherian local $k$-algebra $(R_X,\mathfrak m_X)$ with residue field $k$. Its tangent cone at the distinguished point is then \[ C_X=\operatorname{Spec}\!\left(\operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m_X}R_X\right),\qquad \operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m_X}R_X =\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\mathfrak m_X^n/\mathfrak m_X^{n+1}. \] In this convention, the number of irreducible components of the base means the number of minimal prime ideals of $R_X$. These are conditional conventions for interpreting the question, not specifications supplied by the source. For ordinary infinitesimal deformations of a smooth variety over a field, first-order deformation classes are described by \[ T_X^1=\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathcal O_X}(\Omega^1_{X/k},\mathcal O_X) \cong H^1(X,\mathcal T_{X/k}),\qquad \mathcal T_{X/k}=\mathcal H om_{\mathcal O_X}(\Omega^1_{X/k},\mathcal O_X). \] This description does not specify the simplicial complexes intended by the source.

  1. Is the deformation space of every smooth complete toric variety determined by its tangent cone? Under the formal convention above, one possible precise interpretation asks whether, for any two such varieties $X$ and $Y$ over the same field and with the same deformation convention, \[ \operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m_X}R_X\cong \operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m_Y}R_Y \quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_X\cong R_Y, \] where the first isomorphism is of graded $k$-algebras and the second is of complete local $k$-algebras. The source does not specify this interpretation or distinguish it from asking whether each deformation germ is isomorphic to its tangent-cone germ. These are different questions, and neither is selected here as the definitive intended statement.
  2. Is there a relation between the number of irreducible components of the deformation space and the number of distinct simplicial complexes corresponding to first-order deformations of $X$? The supplied record defines neither the complexes, their correspondence with deformation classes, nor the equivalence relation meant by "distinct." It also proposes no particular equality, bound, or other relation. Consequently, this part remains a request for a relation rather than a fully specified mathematical proposition.

Both questions are retained as source questions. Their unresolved conventions require human mathematical review before a definitive formal statement can be fixed.

Short English statement

For a smooth complete toric variety, the source asks whether its deformation space can be recovered from its tangent cone, and whether its component count is related to a count of simplicial complexes describing first-order deformations. The deformation category, recovery criterion, complexes, and proposed numerical relation remain unspecified. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
