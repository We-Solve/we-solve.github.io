Triangle Equation Hyperplane Arrangements

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer, write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and let \[ V_n={\mathbb{R}}^{\binom n2}=\{(x_{ab})_{1\leq a<b\leq n}:x_{ab}\in{\mathbb{R}}\}. \] The source indexes its hyperplanes by unordered triples but does not specify how the indices in its equation are ordered. The following is therefore a conditional formulation using increasing representatives; this convention requires confirmation against the source: \[ H_{ijk}=\{x\in V_n:x_{ij}+x_{jk}-x_{ik}=0\},\qquad \mathcal H_n=\{H_{ijk}:1\leq i<j<k\leq n\}. \] Under this convention, $\mathcal H_n$ is empty when $n<3$. The source does not explicitly specify the range of $n$; positive integers are used here to state the family.

Define the intersection lattice by \[ L(\mathcal H_n)=\left\{\bigcap_{H\in S}H:S\subseteq\mathcal H_n\right\}, \qquad \bigcap_{H\in\varnothing}H=V_n, \] ordered by reverse inclusion. For $X\in L(\mathcal H_n)$, define the integers $\mu(V_n,X)$ by $\mu(V_n,V_n)=1$ and \[ \mu(V_n,X)=-\sum_{\substack{Y\in L(\mathcal H_n)\\ V_n\leq Y<X}}\mu(V_n,Y) \quad\text{when }X\ne V_n. \] Use the ambient-space convention for the characteristic polynomial: \[ \chi_{\mathcal H_n}(t)=\sum_{X\in L(\mathcal H_n)}\mu(V_n,X)t^{\dim X}. \] A region is a connected component of \[ V_n\setminus\bigcup_{H\in\mathcal H_n}H. \]

Requested determination. For each $n$, describe $L(\mathcal H_n)$, determine $\chi_{\mathcal H_n}(t)$, and describe and enumerate the regions. The supplied cleaned statement also requests other combinatorial invariants without specifying which ones. Its original wording asks more broadly what is known about the arrangement. Neither wording supplies a particular conjectural formula or theorem to prove. All these requests remain conditional on resolving the indexing convention above.

Short English statement

Study the real hyperplane arrangement defined by triangle equations among coordinates indexed by pairs of vertices. Describe its intersection lattice, characteristic polynomial, and regions as the number of vertices varies. The draft uses increasing vertex indices explicitly because the supplied unordered-triple notation leaves the equation's ordering unclear. The supplied open label and literature assessment are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
