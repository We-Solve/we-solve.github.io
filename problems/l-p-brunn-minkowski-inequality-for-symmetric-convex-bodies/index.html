$L_p$ Brunn–Minkowski inequality for symmetric convex bodies

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For origin-symmetric convex bodies $K,L\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ containing the origin in their interiors, let $h_K,h_L$ be their support functions. For $p>0$ and $0\leq t\leq1$, define the $L_p$ Wulff combination by \[tK+_p(1-t)L=\bigcap_{u\in S^{n-1}}\{x:\langle x,u\rangle\leq(th_K(u)^p+(1-t)h_L(u)^p)^{1/p}\}.\] At $p=0$, replace the right-hand side by $h_K(u)^th_L(u)^{1-t}$. Must $|tK+_p(1-t)L|\geq|K|^t|L|^{1-t}$ hold for every $p\geq0$?

Formulation references: [S1], [S2].

OpenAI claim summary for Math-2285

The Wulff combination on this page contains both support-function terms. The source claims the required geometric volume bound at $p=0$. For any $p>0$, the weighted power mean of the support functions is at least their weighted geometric mean. The resulting Wulff body therefore contains the $p=0$ body, and its volume obeys the displayed lower bound. This covers every $p\ge0$ in the recorded question; the additional power-volume inequality for $0<p<1$ is stronger than needed.

\$L_p\$ Brunn–Minkowski inequality for symmetric convex bodies

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 091; 2026-09-23), the asserted result is that symmetric convex bodies satisfy logarithmic Brunn--Minkowski in all dimensions, with the stated $L_p$, even-measure, and dilation consequences.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For a full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$, write $h_K(u)=\sup_{x\in K}x\cdot u$, and for a positive function $g$ on the unit sphere define $W[g]=\{x:x\cdot u\le g(u)\text{ for every unit }u\}$. For every integer $n\ge1$, every such pair $K,L$, and every $\lambda\in[0,1]$, the claim is \[ |W[h_K^{1-\lambda}h_L^{\lambda}]|\ge |K|^{1-\lambda}|L|^{\lambda}. \] Its stated consequence, for every $0<p<1$, is \[ \left|W[((1-\lambda)h_K^p+\lambda h_L^p)^{1/p}]\right|^{p/n} \ge(1-\lambda)|K|^{p/n}+\lambda|L|^{p/n}. \] Volumes are Lebesgue volumes, and central symmetry is an assumption in both assertions.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 091; 2026-09-23), symmetric polytopes are approximated by smooth even-power weights. Logarithmic concavity of their integrals follows from a variance estimate in moment coordinates, and limits first remove the power smoothing and then the Gaussian regularization.
