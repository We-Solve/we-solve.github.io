Math-15657

Asymptotic Counting in General Circle Packings

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $P$ be a circle packing in ${\mathbb{C}}$, described in the supplied statement as locally finite. Under the usual Euclidean convention, a circle $C$ has radius $r(C)>0$ and unsigned curvature $\operatorname{curv}(C)=1/r(C)$; a circle packing consists of circles whose bounded open disks have pairwise disjoint interiors. These conventions require confirmation against the source, which does not explicitly define them. For a bounded Borel set $E\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ and $T>0$, write \[ N_P(T,E)=\#\{C\in P:C\cap E\ne\varnothing,\ \operatorname{curv}(C)<T\}. \] Here intersection refers to the circle itself. Whether the intended source convention instead counts intersection with the enclosed disk remains unresolved.

The supplied question asks whether, for each such packing $P$, there exist a Borel measure $\omega_P$ on ${\mathbb{C}}$ and an exponent $\alpha$, both independent of $E$ and $T$, such that for every suitable bounded Borel set $E$, \[ N_P(T,E)\sim\omega_P(E)T^\alpha\qquad(T\to\infty). \] It also asks what analogous counting and distribution results hold for circle packings beyond Apollonian packings.

The supplied record does not define "suitable," specify the allowed exponents, impose nontriviality or local finiteness conditions on $\omega_P$, or explain how to interpret the displayed asymptotic when $\omega_P(E)=0$ or $\omega_P(E)=\infty$. For $0<\omega_P(E)<\infty$, the conventional meaning is \[ \lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_P(T,E)}{\omega_P(E)T^\alpha}=1. \] A potentially useful formulation covering zero measure sets would instead require $T^{-\alpha}N_P(T,E)\to\omega_P(E)$, but the record does not establish that this is the intended formulation. No boundary regularity condition, such as $\omega_P(\partial E)=0$, is supplied and none is imposed here.

The meaning of local finiteness is particularly consequential: finiteness of all circles meeting each compact set differs from finiteness only after a curvature cutoff. The record does not resolve this distinction. Its background also describes an unrestricted class of countable packings, whereas its displayed question assumes local finiteness. Thus this is a statement-only draft of the source question with unresolved hypotheses, rather than a fully specified universal assertion.

Short English statement

The question asks whether each locally finite planar circle packing admits a common power law for counting circles of curvature less than $T$ that meet a suitable bounded region, with a Borel measure describing spatial distribution. It also asks for extensions beyond Apollonian packings. The intended local finiteness condition, admissible regions, measure conditions, and treatment of zero measure regions need clarification. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
