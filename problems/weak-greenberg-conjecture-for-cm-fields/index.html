Math-3627

Weak Greenberg Conjecture for CM Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Weak Greenberg conjecture, curated form. For every odd prime $p$ and every CM field $K$ containing $\mu_p$, is\[\lambda^-(K)>\lambda^+(K)\quad\text{unless }\lambda^-(K)=\lambda^+(K)=0?\] A CM field is a totally imaginary quadratic extension of a totally real field $K^+$; its complex conjugation is denoted $c$. The condition $\mu_p\subset K$ means that all $p$th roots of unity belong to $K$. Neither the odd-prime hypothesis nor this root-of-unity hypothesis is removed.

Under the standard cyclotomic Iwasawa convention, let $K_\infty/K$ be the cyclotomic ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$-extension, with layers $[K_n:K]=p^n$. Set $A_n=\operatorname{Cl}(K_n)[p^\infty]$ and $X=\varprojlim_n A_n$, with norm transition maps. Complex conjugation acts on these groups; since $p\ne2$, the idempotents $(1+c)/2$ and $(1-c)/2$ define the plus and minus parts $X^+,X^-$. The invariants $\lambda^+,\lambda^-$ are the Iwasawa $\lambda$-invariants of these torsion modules over ${\mathbb{Z}}_p[[\operatorname{Gal}(K_\infty/K)]]$, equivalently the total degrees of their distinguished characteristic-polynomial factors with multiplicity. This is an explicit conventional interpretation: the excerpt does not itself define the tower, module, or invariant normalization. No vanishing of the corresponding $\mu$-invariants is assumed merely in order to ask the question.

The original prose instead states $\lambda^+=\lambda^-$ if and only if both vanish. The curated strict-inequality form also rules out $\lambda^-<\lambda^+$. Identifying these formulations therefore requires the relevant comparison inequality under the same hypotheses, which is not supplied as a theorem in this record. Both formulations are preserved rather than declared logically equivalent without that input. The weak conjecture does not by itself assert $\lambda^+=0$; strict inequality with a positive plus invariant remains allowed.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the problem partially solved. It reports substantial special hypotheses, described as abelian plus fields with total splitting and suitable circular-unit cohomology, while saying the general assertion remains open. The exact field, splitting, and cohomological conditions are not reproduced, so no unconditional theorem for all abelian CM fields is inferred. This dated assessment requires independent verification.

Short English statement

For the cyclotomic plus and minus Iwasawa invariants of a CM field containing $\mu_p$, the curated claim requires strict minus dominance except when both vanish. The original equality-only statement needs a comparison inequality to be equivalent. Neither formulation is silently strengthened to vanishing of the plus invariant.
