Serre weights and complete Hecke packets

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source is Conjecture 5 in the section "Taylor: Florian Herzig's Thesis" of the February 2006 workshop notes. The supplied verification restores $p>2$ and arithmetic Frobenius. Let $k=\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_p$, with any finite-field descent specified separately, and let $\rho:G_{\mathbb{Q}}\to\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ be a continuous absolutely irreducible odd representation. These hypotheses are needed background, not fully restated in the extracted conjecture. Let $N\ge1$, $p\nmid N$, and let \[ N(\rho)=\prod_{\ell\ne p}\ell^{a_\ell(\rho)} \] be its prime-to-$p$ Artin conductor. Write $\operatorname{JH}(\sigma)$ for the isomorphism classes of Jordan--Hölder constituents of a finite-dimensional modular $\mathrm{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p)$-representation $\sigma$, and $\operatorname{Wt}(\rho|_{I_p})$ for the intended set of Serre weights. The source does not give that local weight recipe.

Using the source's $\Gamma_1(N)$, $T_\ell$, and coefficient-action conventions, the printed assertion is \[ H^1(\Gamma_1(N),\sigma)\text{ contains a simultaneous Hecke eigenclass with } T_\ell\text{-eigenvalue }\operatorname{tr}\rho(\operatorname{Frob}_\ell) \text{ for every prime }\ell\nmid Np \] if and only if \[ N(\rho)\mid N,\qquad \operatorname{JH}(\sigma)\cap\operatorname{Wt}(\rho|_{I_p})\ne\varnothing. \] Determinant/nebentype, dual versus direct coefficient actions, irreducibility/localization hypotheses and the extension from irreducible weights to arbitrary $\sigma$ must be specified before claiming this literal formulation as a theorem. The aid reports the core $\mathrm{GL}_2/{\mathbb{Q}}$ Serre assertion as established by Khare--Wintenberger with Kisin's completion, subject to those conventions; it does not independently verify every compressed coefficient-system statement above.

The source asks how to generalize, records the possible inclusion $\operatorname{Wt}(\rho|_{I_p})\subset\operatorname{Wt}(\rho|_{I_p}^{\mathrm{ss}})$, and mentions an earlier explanation by "Matt", whose identity and explanation are absent. Full inertial extension data and semisimplification need not be interchangeable. For the Ash et al. higher-rank discussion, let $\rho:G_{\mathbb{Q}}\to\mathrm{GL}_n(k)$ be irreducible with complex conjugation $c$ satisfying \[ \left|\dim\ker(\rho(c)-1)-\dim\ker(\rho(c)+1)\right|\le1. \] The intended weight set consists of irreducible modular $\mathrm{GL}_n({\mathbb{F}}_p)$-representations, not a single irreducible representation. The block-matrix congruence in the source is naturally interpreted as \[ \Gamma_1^{(n)}(N)=\{g\in\mathrm{SL}_n({\mathbb{Z}}): \text{last row of }g\equiv(0,\ldots,0,1)\pmod N\}. \] The general weight recipe, cohomological degree, coefficient system and converse are not completely stated; the aid reports the broad higher-rank weight/level/degree problem unresolved.

Local Hecke-information correction in the aid. The source displays only the double coset of $\operatorname{diag}(\ell,1,\ldots,1)$, denoted $T(\ell,1)$. In the Ash--Doud--Pollack normalization, a full unramified packet $a(\ell,k)$ is encoded by \[ \det(1-\rho(\operatorname{Frob}_\ell)X) =\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k\ell^{k(k-1)/2}a(\ell,k)X^k, \qquad a(\ell,0)=1. \] The standard operators $T(\ell,k)$ use double cosets with $k$ diagonal entries equal to $\ell$ and the others 1, in the compatible convention. The scalar powers of $\ell$ are invertible modulo $p$. The first operator gives trace, and the last coefficient gives determinant.

For $n\ge3$, one trace and a fixed determinant do not determine an arbitrary local unramified characteristic polynomial. The aid's characteristic-independent matrices are the companions \[ C_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad C_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&-1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}. \] They both have trace zero and determinant one, but characteristic polynomials $Y^3-1$ and $Y^3+Y-1$. For larger $n$, adjoining $I_{n-3}$ preserves equality of traces and determinants and the difference of characteristic polynomials. Thus, within the standard elementary-symmetric Hecke family, local polynomial recovery requires all $T(\ell,k)$, $1\le k\le n$, or $1\le k\le n-1$ if determinant is already fixed. Omitting another independent coefficient leaves a local ambiguity.

This is a per-prime information test, not a counterexample to a global conjecture imposing trace identities at every Frobenius or additional global constraints. It neither constructs global Galois representations realizing the displayed local matrices nor settles the higher-rank Serre-weight problem.

Short English statement

The record combines a compressed $\mathrm{GL}_2$ Serre-weight statement with a higher-rank generalization. The aid reports the core rank-two theorem subject to missing conventions and identifies a local limitation of the displayed trace operator in higher rank. Recovering an arbitrary local characteristic polynomial needs the remaining elementary-symmetric Hecke data, while the global higher-rank weight and degree questions remain separate.
