Spectral-Gap Conjecture for Compact Simple Lie Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a compact connected simple Lie group, and let $m_G$ be its normalized Haar probability measure. Let $\mu$ be a Borel probability measure on $G$. Write $S=\operatorname{supp}(\mu)$ for its closed support. The measure is symmetric if $\mu(A)=\mu(A^{-1})$ for every Borel set $A\subseteq G$.

Define the convolution operator on $L^2(G,m_G)$ by \[ (\lambda_G(\mu)f)(x)=\int_G f(g^{-1}x)\,\mathrm{d}\mu(g), \] and define its invariant mean-zero subspace by \[ L^2_0(G)=\left\{f\in L^2(G,m_G):\int_G f\,\mathrm{d}m_G=0\right\}. \] As in Definition 3 of the supplied excerpt, $\mu$ has a spectral gap if \[ \left\|\left.\lambda_G(\mu)\right|_{L^2_0(G)}\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}<1. \] Here the operator norm is the supremum of $\|\lambda_G(\mu)f\|_2$ over all $f\in L^2_0(G)$ with $\|f\|_2=1$.

The excerpt does not explicitly define "non-degenerate." Its explanation identifies containment of the support in a proper closed subgroup as the obstruction. Using that explanation, interpret non-degeneracy as \[ \overline{\langle S\rangle}=G, \] where $\langle S\rangle$ is the subgroup generated by $S$. This interpretation is explicit but requires confirmation against the complete report: the accompanying lemma for general compact groups invokes products of two support elements, and the supplied literature assessment also uses the term "adapted."

Spectral Gap Conjecture (supplied Conjecture 4). For every compact connected simple Lie group $G$ and every symmetric Borel probability measure $\mu$ on $G$, is it true that \[ \left\|\left.\lambda_G(\mu)\right|_{L^2_0(G)}\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}<1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \overline{\langle\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\rangle}=G? \] No finite-support, absolute-continuity, or algebraicity hypothesis is supplied. The convention for "simple Lie group," including whether a nontrivial finite center is allowed, is not specified in the record.

The surrounding excerpt describes spectral gap as a uniform bound over nontrivial irreducible representations and states that it yields exponential equidistribution of convolution powers: for some $c>0$ and a Sobolev norm $S(f)$ of sufficiently high order, \[ \int_G f\,\mathrm{d}\mu^{*n} =\int_G f\,\mathrm{d}m_G+O\!\left(S(f)e^{-cn}\right) \qquad(f\in C^\infty(G)). \] The precise Sobolev order and implicit-constant dependencies are not supplied. These are contextual source claims, not additional hypotheses. The extracted exponential-mixing formula has an unclear limiting term and is not adopted as a mathematical assertion here.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every symmetric probability measure whose support generates a dense subgroup of a compact connected simple Lie group has a convolution operator with norm strictly below one on mean-zero square-integrable functions, and conversely. The interpretation of "non-degenerate" and the convention for simplicity require source confirmation. The record labels the conjecture open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
