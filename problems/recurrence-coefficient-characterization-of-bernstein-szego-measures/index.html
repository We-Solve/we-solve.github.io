Recurrence-Coefficient Characterization of Bernstein–Szegő Measures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The requested characterization concerns Bernstein--Szegő measures and the recurrence coefficients of their associated multivariate orthogonal polynomial spaces. The supplied record does not specify the underlying domain, the defining class of measures, the orthogonal spaces, or the recurrence convention. Consequently, the following expresses the intended characterization problem schematically; these missing specifications must be recovered before it becomes a fully determined mathematical statement.

Let $\mathcal B$ denote the class of Bernstein--Szegő measures intended in the cited report. Once the underlying domain, admissible measures, polynomial spaces, basis choices, and recurrence convention have been specified, write $R(\mu)$ for the resulting collection of recurrence coefficients associated with an admissible measure $\mu$.

Characterization problem. Find explicit necessary and sufficient conditions $\mathcal C$ on these recurrence coefficients such that, for every admissible measure $\mu$, \[ \mu\in\mathcal B \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R(\mu)\text{ satisfies }\mathcal C. \] The desired characterization is to be analogous to the spectral characterization of Bernstein--Szegő measures on \[ \mathbb T^2=\{(z,w)\in{\mathbb{C}}^2:|z|=|w|=1\} \] mentioned in the source as reference [3]. The supplied record does not identify that reference or specify which features of its characterization should extend to the requested setting.

Short English statement

Find explicit necessary and sufficient conditions on recurrence coefficients that characterize the Bernstein--Szegő measures intended in the source, analogous to a cited characterization on $\mathbb T^2$. The measure class, orthogonal spaces, and recurrence conventions are missing, so this is a schematic statement requiring recovery of the source definitions and human mathematical review.
