Some Questions — Question 30

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T_3=(V,E)$ be the infinite $3$-regular tree. Under the usual full-automorphism convention, a factor of i.i.d. process with values in a finite alphabet $A$ is a random labeling $Y\in A^V$ obtained as $Y=F(U)$, where $(U_v)_{v\in V}$ are independent uniform $[0,1]$ labels and $F:[0,1]^V\to A^V$ is measurable and $\operatorname{Aut}(T_3)$-equivariant, modulo null sets. Write $\mathcal F_A$ for the set of its laws. Weak convergence of laws on $A^V$ means convergence of all finite-coordinate distributions; equivalently, for every finite $W\subset V$ and $z\in A^W$, \[ \nu_m\{y:y|_W=z\}\longrightarrow\nu\{y:y|_W=z\}. \]

Closure question. Is $\mathcal F_A$ closed for this topology? More explicitly, must $\nu\in\mathcal F_A$ whenever $\nu_m\in\mathcal F_A$ and $\nu_m\Rightarrow\nu$? The source does not specify the output alphabet or acting group; the fixed-finite-alphabet and full-automorphism conventions above make one standard interpretation explicit.

Majority-on-balls question. Let $B_n(v)=\{w\in V:d(v,w)\leq n\}$. In the usual binary interpretation, start with independent unbiased labels $\xi_w\in\{-1,1\}$ and form a process whose value at $v$ is the majority sign of \[ \sum_{w\in B_n(v)}\xi_w. \] After specifying an equivariant rule for ties, denote its law by $\nu_n$. The source asks whether the weak limit of these majority processes is a factor of i.i.d., that is, whether the intended limit $\nu_n\Rightarrow\nu$ belongs to $\mathcal F_{\{-1,1\}}$. The record neither specifies the input distribution and tie rule nor establishes existence or uniqueness of this limit, and it does not specify a subsequence. These are unresolved qualifications rather than additional conclusions. In particular, $|B_n(v)|=3\cdot2^n-2$ is even for $n\geq1$, so ties require a convention at finite radii.

The supplied summary labels both questions OPEN-TRIAGE, connects them with closures of factor-of-i.i.d. laws and with rounding or belief-propagation limits, and reports no settled answer within a capped search. Its classification is expressly provisional and is not an independently verified openness claim.

Short English statement

Are weak limits of factor-of-i.i.d. processes on the $3$-regular tree still factors of i.i.d.? The specific test case is the putative limit of majority labels on larger balls. The source leaves the alphabet, symmetry convention, tie rule, and precise limit unspecified.
