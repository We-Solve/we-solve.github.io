Noetherian Enveloping Algebras of Lie Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Coefficient qualification. The supplied record does not specify the coefficient domain or characteristic. The following is a field-based formulation for a fixed field $k$; whether the intended questions concern every field or a restricted class of fields remains unresolved.

A ring is left Noetherian if every ascending chain of left ideals stabilizes, and right Noetherian is defined analogously. For the enveloping algebras and group algebras below, the standard maps induced by $x\mapsto -x$ and $g\mapsto g^{-1}$, respectively, identify each algebra with its opposite algebra, so left and right Noetherianity are equivalent. Here Noetherian means this equivalent condition.

For a Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ over $k$, its universal enveloping algebra is \[ U_k(\mathfrak g)=T_k(\mathfrak g)\big/\left\langle x\otimes y-y\otimes x-[x,y]:x,y\in\mathfrak g\right\rangle, \] where $T_k(\mathfrak g)$ is the tensor algebra and the brackets generate a two-sided ideal. The first question is whether \[ \text{for every Lie algebra }\mathfrak g\text{ over }k,\qquad U_k(\mathfrak g)\text{ Noetherian}\ \Longrightarrow\ \dim_k\mathfrak g<\infty. \]

For a group $G$, the group algebra $kG$ consists of finite formal sums $\sum_{g\in G}a_g g$, with coefficients in $k$, and multiplication extends the group operation bilinearly. A group $P$ is polycyclic if it has a finite subnormal series \[ \{1\}=P_0\triangleleft P_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft P_r=P \] whose factors $P_i/P_{i-1}$ are cyclic, either finite or infinite. A group $G$ is polycyclic-by-finite if it has a polycyclic normal subgroup of finite index. The second question is whether \[ \text{for every group }G,\qquad kG\text{ Noetherian}\ \Longrightarrow\ G\text{ polycyclic-by-finite}. \] No finite generation assumption on $\mathfrak g$ or $G$ is supplied. These are two separate questions in the extracted statement. The retained original passage supplies background about classification of Hopf algebras of finite Gelfand--Kirillov dimension and difficulties for Noetherian Hopf algebras, but stops at "Conjecture 1" without reproducing its mathematical content. That background does not explicitly impose a finite Gelfand--Kirillov dimension hypothesis on either question.

Short English statement

For a fixed coefficient field, ask whether a Noetherian universal enveloping algebra forces its Lie algebra to be finite-dimensional, and whether a Noetherian group algebra forces its group to have a polycyclic normal subgroup of finite index. The intended coefficient fields and characteristic restrictions require confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
