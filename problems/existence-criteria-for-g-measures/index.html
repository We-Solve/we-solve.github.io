Supplied question. What conditions on the size or shape of the state set $D$ ensure the existence of a $g$-measure?

Unresolved definitions and quantifiers. The supplied record calls $D$ a state set but specifies neither its ambient space nor its measurable, topological, or geometric structure. It does not define $g$, its domain, its normalization or regularity assumptions, or the meaning of a $g$-measure. Consequently, neither "size" nor "shape" has a specified mathematical meaning here. It is also unspecified whether $g$ is fixed, ranges over a prescribed class, or is to be chosen together with the measure. These omissions prevent a precise quantified existence statement. The faithful target is therefore the classification question above, with the definitions, hypotheses, and quantifiers still requiring recovery from the underlying source; no particular interpretation of $D$ or convention for $g$-measures is imposed.
