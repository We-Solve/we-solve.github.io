Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For axisymmetric initial data with ADM mass $m$ and total angular momentum $J_\infty$, prove $m\geq\sqrt{|J_\infty|}$ for manifolds with a trapped inner boundary. For multiple black holes satisfying $m\geq\mathcal M_{\mathrm{many}}$, determine the value and parameter dependence of $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{many}}$ and identify the geometric meaning of its minimizer.

Mass–Angular-Momentum Inequalities with Trapped and Multiple Horizons

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. For the designated axisymmetric electrovacuum class, the claimed bound is $m^2\geq A/(16\pi)+Q^2/2+\pi(Q^4+4J^2)/A$ with conserved total angular momentum.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let the smooth oriented connected exterior be diffeomorphic to $\mathbb R^3$ minus an open ball, with spherical smooth compact boundary $S$, complete metric including $S$, a single AF end, and smooth $g,K,\mathcal E,\mathcal B$. A smooth effective $U(1)$ action preserves all data and the boundary, with rotation generator of period $2\pi$ agreeing with ordinary axial rotation at infinity. Impose source-free electrovacuum constraints \[ 16\pi\mu=R_g+(\operatorname{tr}_gK)^2-|K|^2, \quad 8\pi J_{\rm con}=\operatorname{div}_g(K-(\operatorname{tr}_gK)g), \] \[ 8\pi\mu=|\mathcal E|^2+|\mathcal B|^2,\quad 4\pi J_{\rm con}=(\mathcal E\times\mathcal B)^\flat,\quad \operatorname{div}_g\mathcal E=\operatorname{div}_g\mathcal B=0. \] Use $g-\delta=O_4(r^{-1})$, $K=O_3(r^{-3})$, Coulomb leading fields $\mathcal E^i=Q_e x^i/r^3+O_3(r^{-3})$, $\mathcal B^i=Q_b x^i/r^3+O_3(r^{-3})$, integrable constraints and finite ADM energy, momentum, angular momentum and charge fluxes. Require $P_{\rm ADM}=0$, $m=E_{\rm ADM}>0$, $Q^2=Q_e^2+Q_b^2$, and let $J$ denote conserved angular momentum, with its electromagnetic boundary contribution included; it agrees with $J_{\rm ADM}$. The boundary is a future MOTS; no wholly exterior enclosing smooth surface is weakly outer trapped; $\operatorname{Area}_g(\Gamma)\ge A=|S|_g>0$ for every full cut $\Gamma$; and \[A\ge4\pi\sqrt{Q^4+4J^2}.\] \[ m^2\ge\frac{A}{16\pi}+\frac{Q^2}{2}+\frac{\pi(Q^4+4J^2)}{A}. \] Equality on the strict area branch $A>4\pi\sqrt{Q^4+4J^2}$ identifies the entire original data with a smooth spacelike slice of subextremal dyonic Kerr--Newman with matching mass, angular momentum and charges, including smooth attachment and future normal at the horizon and an end at spatial infinity. Conversely, admissible such slices attain equality. The numerical endpoint is included, but extremal equality is not classified. Here every full cut is the entire boundary of an end-side exterior region, including all boundary-coincident components, and $O_j$ includes coordinate derivatives through order $j$.

Outline of the argument

Axial variables are compared with a Kerr--Newman family at prescribed conformal capacity. Riemannian scale comparison and electromagnetic angular-momentum bookkeeping give the mass bound; saturation on the strict physical-area branch reconstructs an admissible dyonic spacetime slice.
