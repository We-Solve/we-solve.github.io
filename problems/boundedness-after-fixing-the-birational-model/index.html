Math-232

Boundedness after fixing the birational model

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Alexeev asks whether two classes are bounded, where bounded means that every member occurs, up to isomorphism, as a fiber of a family over a finite-type base: (1) semi-log-canonical varieties of general type with fixed $K^n$; (2) $\epsilon$-log-terminal Fano varieties. The supplied verification corrects the extraction to Problem 1.4, $K^n$ and $\epsilon$, and identifies "1.3. Positive characteristic issues" as the next section heading, not an assumption in this problem.

A precise intended characteristic-zero version fixes dimension $n$, positive rational volume $v$ in (1), and a constant $\epsilon>0$ in (2). In (1), take projective slc canonical models with $K_X$ ample and $K_X^n=v$. Semi-log-canonical means the usual $S_2$, nodal-in-codimension-one and ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier canonical conditions, with the normalization plus conductor log canonical. In (2), take normal projective Fano $n$-folds with $-K_X$ ample and ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier, and all log discrepancies exceeding $\epsilon$ under the $\epsilon$-log-terminal convention; the related $\epsilon$-log-canonical convention uses at least $\epsilon$. The dimension, field, positive fixed epsilon, canonical-model and precise discrepancy conventions are not spelled out in the damaged original.

The attached unreviewed aid reports affirmative boundedness for the canonical-model version by Hacon--McKernan--Xu and for fixed-dimensional $\epsilon$-log-terminal Fano varieties by Birkar's BAB theorem. It distinguishes a literal reading of (1) that only assumes $K_X$ big. For every integer $m\ge5$, it takes a smooth degree-$m$ surface $S_m\subset{\mathbb{P}}^3$ and blows up $m(m-4)^2-1$ distinct points. The resulting smooth general-type surface $X_m$ is reported to satisfy \[ K_{X_m}^2=1,\qquad \chi(\mathcal O_{X_m})=1+\binom{m-1}{3}. \] The aid uses this unbounded Euler characteristic to refute boundedness of the literal big-$K$ class. These surfaces have non-nef canonical divisor, so their numerical self-intersection $K^2=1$ is not a fixed canonical volume and does not contradict boundedness of canonical models. The exact boundedness theorem hypotheses and references are not reproduced; the source's broad "general type" wording cannot silently be replaced by ampleness without recording this distinction.

Short English statement

Ask for boundedness of fixed-volume slc canonical models and fixed-dimensional Fano varieties with a uniform singularity bound. The aid reports the characteristic-zero theorems and a blow-up family showing why mere general type with fixed canonical self-intersection is a different statement.
