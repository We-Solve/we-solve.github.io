Open Problems and Questions about Geodesics — Conjecture 30 (Spatzier)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth closed manifold, where closed means compact and without boundary, and let $g$ be a Riemannian metric on $M$. A Riemannian metric $h$ has no conjugate points if, for every $h$-geodesic $\gamma$ and every pair of distinct parameters $a,b$ in its domain, the only Jacobi field $J$ along $\gamma$ satisfying $J(a)=J(b)=0$ is the zero field. Here a Jacobi field satisfies \[ \frac{D^2J}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t^2}+R^h(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=0, \] where $D/{\,\mathrm{d}} t$ and $R^h$ are the covariant derivative and curvature tensor of $h$.

The source assumes that $(M,g)$ is a "closed symmetric space of non-compact type and of higher rank." Under the usual compact locally symmetric interpretation, this means that the universal Riemannian cover $(\widetilde M,\widetilde g)$ is a symmetric space of noncompact type, with no Euclidean or compact factors, and that its rank, the maximal dimension of a totally geodesic Euclidean flat, is at least $2$. This interpretation of the source wording requires confirmation: a globally symmetric space and a compact locally symmetric quotient are different notions. The supplied record does not impose irreducibility or specify a rank condition on individual factors.

Conjecture 30 (attributed in the supplied record to Spatzier). For every $(M,g)$ satisfying the source hypotheses, every Riemannian metric $h$ on $M$ with no conjugate points is a homothetic rescaling of $g$. Read as equality of metric tensors on the fixed manifold, the asserted conclusion is \[ \forall h\;\left[h\text{ has no conjugate points}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists c\in(0,\infty)\;h=cg\right]. \] The meaning of this conclusion is unresolved. If homothety is intended up to a diffeomorphism, the conclusion would instead be \[ \exists c\in(0,\infty)\ \exists\varphi\in\operatorname{Diff}(M) \quad h=c\,\varphi^*g, \] where $\operatorname{Diff}(M)$ is the set of smooth diffeomorphisms of $M$. These formulations are distinct. The supplied record does not specify which is intended, and no diffeomorphism allowance or irreducibility hypothesis is inserted into the conjecture here. These qualifications require resolution before this draft can be treated as an unambiguous mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture, attributed to Spatzier, asks whether every metric without conjugate points on the specified closed higher-rank symmetric manifold must agree with the given metric up to homothety. The meanings of "symmetric," the treatment of reducible spaces, and whether homothety allows a diffeomorphism require clarification. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied status information, not an independently verified assessment.
