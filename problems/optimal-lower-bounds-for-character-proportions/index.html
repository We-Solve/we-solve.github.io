Optimal Lower Bounds for Character Proportions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for optimal lower bounds for two character proportions denoted by $\theta(G)$ and $\theta'(G)$, associated with bounds attributed to Thompson and Gallagher. It does not define either proportion or specify the class of groups over which the infima are taken.

A conditional formulation of the requested optimization problem is as follows. Once a nonempty class $\mathcal G$ of admissible groups and functions $\theta,\theta'\colon\mathcal G\to[0,1]$ defining the intended proportions have been specified, determine \[ \alpha=\inf\{\theta(G):G\in\mathcal G\},\qquad \beta=\inf\{\theta'(G):G\in\mathcal G\}. \] Equivalently, determine $\alpha,\beta\in[0,1]$ such that \[ \begin{aligned} &\forall G\in\mathcal G,\quad \theta(G)\geq\alpha, &&\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists G\in\mathcal G,\quad \theta(G)<\alpha+\varepsilon,\\ &\forall G\in\mathcal G,\quad \theta'(G)\geq\beta, &&\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists G\in\mathcal G,\quad \theta'(G)<\beta+\varepsilon. \end{aligned} \] This is a formal template, not a completed formulation of the source problem: the admissible groups, the characters being counted, the defining conditions, and the denominators of both proportions remain unspecified. In particular, no domain restriction or treatment of degenerate cases can be recovered from the supplied text.

The supplied dated assessment describes universal lower bounds of $1/3$ for both proportions and evidence toward $1/2$, while reporting that the optimal bounds remain unproved in general. These are supplied curation claims, not independently verified assertions here. The excerpt refers to Questions 1 and 2 and a main conjecture in a reference labeled [6], but neither that reference's bibliographic entry nor the conjecture's statement is supplied; no conjectural equality is therefore asserted.

Short English statement

Determine the greatest universal lower bounds for the two character proportions attributed to Thompson and Gallagher. The supplied record omits the definitions of both proportions and the admissible class of groups, so only a conditional formulation is possible. Its open status and numerical background are supplied dated assessments requiring review.
