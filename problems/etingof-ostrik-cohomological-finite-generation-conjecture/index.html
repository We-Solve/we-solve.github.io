Etingof-Ostrik Cohomological Finite-Generation Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field. The supplied excerpt describes a finite tensor category over $k$ as a $k$-linear tensor category $\mathcal C$ satisfying the following conditions: $\mathcal C$ is abelian and rigid; $\dim_k\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,Y)<\infty$ for every pair of objects $X,Y$; every object has finite length; there are finitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects; and the tensor unit $\mathbf 1$ is simple. Rigidity means that every object has left and right duals. The excerpt states no restriction on the characteristic or algebraic closure of $k$.

For objects $M,N\in\mathcal C$, set \[ \operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\mathcal C}(M,N) =\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathcal C}(M,N), \qquad H^{*}(\mathcal C) =\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\mathcal C}(\mathbf 1,\mathbf 1), \] where higher extension groups may be understood as Yoneda extension groups, and the multiplication on $H^{*}(\mathcal C)$ is the Yoneda product. One conventional left action of $H^{*}(\mathcal C)$ on $\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\mathcal C}(M,N)$ is \[ h\cdot x=(h\otimes\operatorname{id}_N)\circ x, \qquad h\in H^p(\mathcal C),\quad x\in\operatorname{Ext}^{q}_{\mathcal C}(M,N), \] using $\mathbf 1\otimes N\cong N$ and Yoneda composition. Tensoring is exact in this abelian rigid setting. This action convention is made explicit here; the supplied excerpt does not specify a tensor side.

Conjecture 1.2 (attributed in the source to Etingof--Ostrik, 2004). For every finite tensor category $\mathcal C$,

  1. the cohomology algebra $H^{*}(\mathcal C)$ is Noetherian;
  2. for every pair of objects $M,N\in\mathcal C$, the graded module $\operatorname{Ext}^{*}_{\mathcal C}(M,N)$ is finitely generated over $H^{*}(\mathcal C)$.
Here finite generation means that there are finitely many homogeneous extension classes whose $H^{*}(\mathcal C)$-linear combinations exhaust the module. The source calls the conjunction of these properties cohomological finite generation, abbreviated CFG.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The displayed definition in the supplied excerpt omits an enough-projectives condition that is normally part of the finite abelian category hypothesis used for finite tensor categories. It also contains an unexplained symbol $A$ in the finite-simple-objects condition, interpreted above as referring to $\mathcal C$. Whether additional hypotheses were intended must be checked against the complete definition; they have not been silently added. The excerpt also does not specify whether Noetherianity refers to all ideals or only homogeneous ideals, or explicitly specify left/right conventions. These conventions and the module action require confirmation for a definitive formulation.

The source motivates CFG by its role in tensor triangular geometry for $\mathcal C$ and its ind-completion, and mentions a further conjecture concerning $\operatorname{stab}\mathcal C$. No statement of that further conjecture is supplied, so it is not included here.

Short English statement

The Etingof--Ostrik conjecture asserts that every finite tensor category has Noetherian cohomology and that the extensions between every pair of objects form a finitely generated module over that cohomology. The supplied definition and algebra-action conventions need confirmation. The record labels the problem partially solved; its dated assessment is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
