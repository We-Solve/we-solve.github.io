The solved discrete quadratic Carleson problem and a sharp-scale finite sampling reduction

Definitions and mathematical statement

For finitely supported $f:{\mathbb{Z}}\to{\mathbb{C}}$, define the discrete quadratic Carleson operator in the source's phase convention by \[ \mathcal C f(n)=\sup_{\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}} \left|\sum_{m\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}}\frac{f(n-m)}m e^{i\lambda m^2}\right|. \] The zero term must be omitted, although that omission is absent from the extracted summation. For general $\ell^2$ input, symmetric truncation and extension conventions are needed. Anderson and Pierce ask for $\|\mathcal C f\|_{\ell^2}\le C\|f\|_{\ell^2}$, their equation (0.9), as a discrete analogue of Stein--Wainger's polynomial Carleson theorem. The related (0.10) restricts the supremum to $\lambda\in\Lambda\subset[0,1]$. The source notes a sufficient condition of Krause and Lacey [9] for that restricted operator but does not state the condition.

The supplied assessment says the full question is solved in published literature by a stronger theorem bounding the supremum also over symmetric truncation radii on $\ell^p({\mathbb{Z}})$ for every $1<p<\infty$. The authors and precise reference of this stronger theorem are not supplied. This is a reported solution status, not a new proof from the finite-radius results below.

The candidate finite-dimensional reduction switches explicitly to the normalized phase $\theta=\lambda/(2\pi)\in{\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$. For integers $d,N\ge1$, set \[ T_{N,\theta}^{(d)}f(n)=\sum_{1\le|m|\le N}\frac{f(n-m)}m e^{2\pi i\theta m^d}, \qquad \mathcal M_{N,\Lambda}^{(d)}f=\sup_{\theta\in\Lambda}|T_{N,\theta}^{(d)}f|. \] The report asserts a pointwise sampling bound by $AN^d$ equally spaced rational phases, up to fixed multiplicative constants; equivalently a grid of size $\lceil AN^d\rceil$ controls the continuum supremum. The numerical sampling/control constants are not given.

For nonempty phase sets with torus Hausdorff distance at most $\delta$, its stated stability loss is \[ \|\mathcal M_{N,\Lambda}^{(d)}f-\mathcal M_{N,\Lambda'}^{(d)}f\|_{\ell^p} \le4\pi\delta\left(\sum_{m=1}^Nm^{d-1}\right)\|f\|_{\ell^p}, \] in the norm setting of the finite-radius theorem. The report does not specify its full $p$-range. It additionally claims that the operator-family parameter Lipschitz constant has sharp order $N^d$, with an exact odd-degree formula, but that formula and the relevant operator norm are not displayed in this record.

For a rational phase $\theta=a/q$, the asserted finite-Fourier model expands the periodic multiplier $e^{2\pi i a m^d/q}$ into its $q$ discrete Fourier modes. Consequently $T_{N,a/q}^{(d)}$ is exactly a finite linear combination of linearly modulated truncated discrete Hilbert transforms $\sum_{1\le|m|\le N}f(n-m)e^{2\pi i b m/q}/m$. The report supplies no coefficient bounds here. All of these candidate conclusions keep the radius $N$ finite; they alone do not establish the uniform infinite-radius Carleson theorem.

Short English statement

The source asks for a discrete quadratic Carleson bound and discusses restricted phase sets. The supplied report records a published stronger solution and separately studies finite-radius rational sampling, phase-set stability and sharp parameter sensitivity. These finite-radius reductions are not a replacement proof of the full theorem.
