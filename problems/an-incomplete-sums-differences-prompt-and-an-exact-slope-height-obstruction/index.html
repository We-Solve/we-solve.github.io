Let $\pi_r(x,y)=x+ry$ for real $r$, $\pi_\infty(x,y)=y$, and take $\pi_{-1}$ as the injective target projection. For coprime nonzero integers $a,b$, with $a\ne b$ and $q=\max(|a|,|b|)\ge2$, consider the scaled grid \[ \mathcal G=\{(bx,-ay):x,y\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}\}. \] Its target projection is $bx+ay$, injective on this grid, while the input slopes $0,-b/a,\infty$ have projection cardinalities $q,2q-1,q$. The aid reports that side length $q$ is maximal among consecutive square grids for that target injectivity. Thus its set-exponent certificate has \[ \alpha_0=\frac{2\log q}{\log(2q-1)},\qquad |\mathcal G|=q^2=(2q-1)^{\alpha_0}. \] For a literal unit-constant inequality $|\mathcal G|\le(\max_r|\pi_r\mathcal G|)^\alpha$, this obstructs $\alpha<\alpha_0$, whereas equality at $\alpha=\alpha_0$ is not a violation. The aid states failure at all $\alpha\le\alpha_0$; without its exact historical $SD$ definition, strictness and constants, that inclusive endpoint cannot be verified or promoted to a precise theorem here. In particular, allowing an unspecified multiplicative constant changes what a single finite grid can refute.

For the uniform distribution on the grid, the reported entropy ratio is \[ \frac{H(\mathcal G)}{\max_{r\in\{0,-b/a,\infty\}}H(\pi_r\mathcal G)} =\frac{2\log q}{h_q}, \] where $h_q$ is the entropy of the sum of two independent uniform variables on $\{0,\ldots,q-1\}$. Explicitly, \[ h_q=-\sum_{t=0}^{2q-2}\frac{c_t}{q^2}\log\frac{c_t}{q^2},\qquad c_t=\begin{cases}t+1,&0\le t<q,\\2q-1-t,&q\le t\le2q-2.\end{cases} \] All logarithms use a common base.
