The Tempered Numerical Period Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied fragment is headed "Tempered Numerical Conjecture" and labels the conjecture "Conjecture 2, [2, 14.2.1]." It chooses a coefficient field $k\simeq{\mathbb{C}}$. Its explicit input is an everywhere-unramified tempered automorphic representation $\pi$, together with a corresponding extended Langlands parameter, attributed to [6] and described by reference to [2, Chapter 2.6], of the displayed form \[ \phi\colon\Gamma_F\longrightarrow\check G\times\mathbb G_m. \] Here $\mathbb G_m$ denotes the multiplicative algebraic group. The fragment does not specify $F$, define $\Gamma_F$, identify the automorphic group or its dual $\check G$, or give the conditions imposed on this extended parameter. These omissions prevent a self-contained definition of the class of representations under consideration.

The registry's supplied formulation additionally requires a hyperspherical dual pair. Neither that pair nor the meaning of hyperspherical duality is defined in the supplied excerpt. The proposed assertion is that, for every such pair and every everywhere-unramified tempered automorphic representation satisfying the intended hypotheses, the normalized automorphic period equals the normalized $L$-value prescribed by the corresponding extended parameter. Schematically, this is \[ \underbrace{\text{normalized automorphic period of }\pi}_{\text{definition and normalization not supplied}} = \underbrace{\text{normalized }L\text{-value prescribed by }\phi}_{\text{definition and evaluation point not supplied}}. \] This display records the supplied intended target; it is not a recovered explicit formula. The excerpt neither defines the period nor specifies the $L$-function, its evaluation point, or the normalization of either side. Its disconnected final clause describes "the latter terms" as Artin $L$-functions of $\Gamma_F$, normalized according to [2, Chapter 11], but the terms to which this refers are absent. Consequently, the complete equality and its precise quantifiers cannot be reconstructed from the supplied evidence. The normalization qualification must be retained even though the exposition invites readers to omit the "norm" decoration on a first reading.

Short English statement

The supplied conjectural target equates a normalized automorphic period with a normalized $L$-value for an everywhere-unramified tempered representation and a hyperspherical dual pair. The excerpt supplies only part of the input and a disconnected normalization reference, so the exact formula and hypotheses remain unresolved.
