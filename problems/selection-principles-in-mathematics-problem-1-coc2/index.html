Selection Principles in Mathematics — Problem 1 ([coc2])

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a topological space. Under the usual nontrivial-cover convention, an open cover of $X$ is a family $\mathcal U$ of open subsets of $X$ such that $\bigcup\mathcal U=X$ and $X\notin\mathcal U$. Write $\mathcal O(X)$ for the class of such covers. An $\omega$-cover is an open cover $\mathcal U$ such that every finite subset of $X$ is contained in some member of $\mathcal U$; write $\Omega(X)$ for this class. A $\gamma$-cover is an infinite open cover $\mathcal U$ such that, for each $x\in X$, the set $\{U\in\mathcal U:x\notin U\}$ is finite; write $\Gamma(X)$ for this class.

For classes $\mathcal A(X)$ and $\mathcal B(X)$ of covers, the property $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq 1}$ with $\mathcal U_n\in\mathcal A(X)$, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that \[ \bigcup_{n\geq 1}\mathcal F_n\in\mathcal B(X). \] The property $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq 1}$ with $\mathcal U_n\in\mathcal A(X)$ and with no $\mathcal U_n$ admitting a finite subcover, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that \[ \left\{\bigcup\mathcal F_n:n\geq 1\right\}\in\mathcal B(X). \] Finite selections are allowed to be empty. The two displayed outputs are families of sets, so repeated members are identified.

Unresolved scope and conventions. The supplied record does not specify the ambient class of spaces, its separation or metrizability assumptions, or whether covers must be countable. The definitions above display the usual nontrivial-cover interpretation; if only countable covers are intended, all three cover classes must be restricted accordingly. These conventions are not confirmed by the supplied excerpt. Let $\mathscr C$ denote the intended, presently unspecified, class of spaces, with a fixed cover convention.

Problem. Is it true that \[ \forall X\in\mathscr C,\qquad X\models\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Omega) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X\models\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Omega)? \] Here the equality in the source is interpreted as equality of the classes of spaces satisfying the two properties. If this equivalence fails, is it nevertheless true that \[ \forall X\in\mathscr C,\qquad X\models\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\models\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Omega)? \] The second question retains the source's qualification "if not." A definitive formulation requires recovery of the intended ambient class and cover conventions.

Short English statement

For the intended class of spaces, ask whether $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Omega)$ and $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Omega)$ characterize the same spaces. If they do not, ask whether $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O,\Gamma)$ implies $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\Omega)$. The ambient class and precise cover conventions are absent from the supplied record.
