Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology only, let $A\in K^{n\times n}$, where $K$ is a field, and let $L$ be an extension field of $K$. An eigenvalue of $A$ in $L$ is a scalar $\lambda\in L$ for which there exists $v\in L^n\setminus\{0\}$ satisfying \[ Av=\lambda v. \] Such a vector $v$ is an eigenvector, and the corresponding eigenspace is $\ker(A-\lambda I_n)$. These definitions supply vocabulary only; they do not specify the matrix ensemble intended by the source.

The retained source question is: What do the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a typical large random matrix look like?
