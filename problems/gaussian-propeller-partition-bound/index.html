Gaussian propeller partition bound

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 096; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that every finite measurable Gaussian partition has squared first-moment sum at most $9/(8\pi)$, attained by three equal planar sectors with orthogonal extensions.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $\gamma_d$ be standard Gaussian probability on $\mathbb R^d$. For all positive integers $d,k$ and measurable partitions $(A_1,\ldots,A_k)$, allowing empty cells, the claimed bound is \[ \sum_{i=1}^k\left\|\int_{A_i}x\,d\gamma_d(x)\right\|_2^2\le\frac9{8\pi}. \] For every $d\ge2,k\ge3$, three sectors of angle $2\pi/3$ in a plane, multiplied by its orthogonal complement with remaining cells empty, are claimed to attain equality. The constant therefore does not increase with dimension or number of cells.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 096; 2026-09-24), optimization over partitions reduces to a finite family of Gaussian linear scores. Deleting a score and competing against pairs impose covariance constraints; certified scalar estimates eliminate five or more active cells and bound the remaining configurations sharply.
