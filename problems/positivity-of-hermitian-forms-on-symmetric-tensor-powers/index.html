Positivity of Hermitian Forms on Symmetric Tensor Powers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the formulation below, take $d$ and $k$ to be positive integers. This is an explicit conventional interpretation: the supplied record does not specify their ranges or whether tensor degree $k=0$ is intended.

Equip ${\mathbb{C}}^d$ with its standard Hermitian inner product, conjugate-linear in the first argument. Define the symmetric tensor power by \[ \operatorname{Sym}^k({\mathbb{C}}^d)=\{v\in({\mathbb{C}}^d)^{\otimes k}:U_\sigma v=v\text{ for every }\sigma\in S_k\}, \] where $S_k$ is the permutation group on $k$ letters and $U_\sigma$ permutes the tensor factors. Use the inner product inherited from the tensor product. For $y\in{\mathbb{C}}^d$, write $y^{\otimes k}=y\otimes\cdots\otimes y$, which belongs to $\operatorname{Sym}^k({\mathbb{C}}^d)$.

Let $W:\operatorname{Sym}^k({\mathbb{C}}^d)\to\operatorname{Sym}^k({\mathbb{C}}^d)$ be a complex-linear Hermitian operator, meaning $W=W^*$. Define the real-valued form \[ p_W(y)=\langle y^{\otimes k},W y^{\otimes k}\rangle. \] The problem is to characterize the operators $W$ for which \[ \forall y\in{\mathbb{C}}^d,\qquad p_W(y)\geq 0. \] The quantifier includes $y=0$, and the required inequality is non-strict. The source asks for a characterization without specifying its desired form, such as a finite criterion or a certificate.

Short English statement

Given a Hermitian operator on a symmetric tensor power of ${\mathbb{C}}^d$, characterize when its associated form is nonnegative on every vector $y\in{\mathbb{C}}^d$. The ranges of the dimension and tensor degree, and the intended type of characterization, are not explicitly specified in the source.
