Hermite-Projector Theorem Extraction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\geq 1$ and let $A={\mathbb{C}}[x_1,\ldots,x_d]$. An ideal projector is a ${\mathbb{C}}$-linear map $P:A\to A$ satisfying $P^2=P$ whose kernel is an ideal of $A$. The finite-dimensional setting mentioned in the supplied background is interpreted as $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\operatorname{ran}P<\infty$.

For clarity, adopt the following conventional interpretation of the terminology that the record leaves undefined. Put $V=\operatorname{ran}P$ and $n=\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}V$. A set $X=\{\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n\}\subset{\mathbb{C}}^d$ of distinct points is unisolvent for $V$ if the evaluation map $V\to{\mathbb{C}}^n$, $v\mapsto(v(\xi_1),\ldots,v(\xi_n))$, is an isomorphism. Its Lagrange projector $L_X:A\to V$ is defined by \[ (L_Xp)(\xi_j)=p(\xi_j)\qquad(p\in A,\ 1\leq j\leq n). \] In this interpretation, $P$ is Hermite if there exist sets $X_k$ unisolvent for $V$ such that \[ \lim_{k\to\infty}L_{X_k}p=Pp\qquad\text{for every }p\in A, \] where convergence takes place in the finite-dimensional space $V$. For $n=0$, the empty interpolation set gives the zero projector.

For polynomials $f_1,\ldots,f_d\in A$, write $f_i^{\mathrm{top}}$ for the highest-degree homogeneous part of a nonzero $f_i$, and set $f_i^{\mathrm{top}}=0$ if $f_i=0$. One usual meaning of the assertion that $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_d):{\mathbb{C}}^d\to{\mathbb{C}}^d$ has no roots at infinity is \[ \left\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^d\setminus\{0\}:f_i^{\mathrm{top}}(z)=0\text{ for every }1\leq i\leq d\right\}=\varnothing. \] This convention describes the common zeros at infinity of the separately homogenized defining equations.

Supplied theorem assertion, under these interpretations. For every finite-rank ideal projector $P:A\to A$ and every $f_1,\ldots,f_d\in A$, \[ \ker P=\langle f_1,\ldots,f_d\rangle \quad\text{and}\quad f\text{ has no roots at infinity} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad P\text{ is Hermite}. \] Here $\langle f_1,\ldots,f_d\rangle=\{\sum_{i=1}^d a_i f_i:a_i\in A\}$.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied sentence does not explicitly specify finite rank, the definition or topology of Hermite approximation, or the precise meaning of roots at infinity. The definitions above make a conventional interpretation explicit; their agreement with the cited passage requires mathematical review. In particular, the homogenized equations need not describe the projective closure of their affine zero set without further qualification. The record's verification field identifies the sentence as a proved sufficient condition, rather than an open problem, and supplies no separate recoverable conjecture. The broader Hermite-projector conjecture mentioned in the background is therefore not substituted for this assertion.

Short English statement

The supplied assertion says that an ideal projector whose kernel has $d$ polynomial generators defining a system with no roots at infinity is Hermite. A finite-rank interpretation and explicit conventions for the undefined terms are given above, subject to review. The record identifies this passage as a theorem extraction and does not provide a separate precise open problem.
