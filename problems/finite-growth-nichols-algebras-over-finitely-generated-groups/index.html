Math-1191

Finite-Growth Nichols Algebras over Finitely Generated Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finitely generated group and let ${\mathbb{C}} G$ be its group algebra. A left-left Yetter--Drinfeld module over ${\mathbb{C}} G$ is a complex vector space $V$ equipped with a left $G$-action and a grading \[ V=\bigoplus_{g\in G}V_g,\qquad h\cdot V_g=V_{hgh^{-1}}\quad(g,h\in G). \] Its support $\{g\in G:V_g\ne0\}$ is therefore a union of conjugacy classes. Write ${}^{{\mathbb{C}} G}_{{\mathbb{C}} G}\mathcal{YD}$ for this category. Its braiding is \[ c(v\otimes w)=g\cdot w\otimes v\qquad(v\in V_g,\ w\in V). \] No finite-dimensionality assumption on $V$ is stated in the supplied problem.

Give the tensor algebra $T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}V^{\otimes n}$ its braided graded Hopf algebra structure with $V$ primitive. The Nichols algebra is \[ \mathcal B(V)=T(V)/I(V), \] where $I(V)$ is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{n\ge2}V^{\otimes n}$. The bosonization $\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G$ has underlying vector space $\mathcal B(V)\otimes{\mathbb{C}} G$ and multiplication \[ (b\#g)(b'\#h)=b(g\cdot b')\#gh. \] For a unital complex algebra $A$, use the definition \[ \operatorname{GKdim}A= \sup_{\substack{W\subseteq A\text{ finite-dimensional}\\1\in W}} \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}(W^n)}{\log n}, \] where $W^n$ is the linear span of products of $n$ elements of $W$. This definition also applies when finite generation of $A$ has not been assumed.

Classification problem (source Question 2). For each finitely generated group $G$, classify the objects $V\in{}^{{\mathbb{C}} G}_{{\mathbb{C}} G}\mathcal{YD}$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{GKdim}\left(\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G\right)<\infty. \] The usual interpretation is classification up to linear isomorphisms preserving both the $G$-action and the $G$-grading, with $G$ fixed; the supplied source does not expressly specify the equivalence relation.

Scope and source qualifications. The surrounding report concerns pointed Hopf algebras over ${\mathbb{C}}$ of finite Gelfand--Kirillov dimension. It states, invoking Gromov, that in its finite-growth setting one may assume $G$ is nilpotent-by-finite, meaning that $G$ has a nilpotent subgroup of finite index. The supplied statement itself quantifies over all finitely generated groups, whereas the supplied literature assessment refers to a "nilpotent-group setting." These descriptions are retained as distinct source qualifications; no additional nilpotence hypothesis is imposed here.

The original excerpt presents Question 2 as the core of Question 1, which asks to classify, or at least characterize, coradically graded connected Hopf algebras $E=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}E_n$ in this category with $\operatorname{GKdim}(E\#{\mathbb{C}} G)<\infty$. It describes $E$, with $V=E_1$, as a post-Nichols algebra and identifies the subalgebra generated by $V$ with $\mathcal B(V)$. Dually, it calls a graded connected Hopf algebra $R$ generated by $R_1\simeq V$ a pre-Nichols algebra. The excerpt's parenthetical assertion about the "only pre- and post-Nichols algebra" is ambiguous and is not used as a hypothesis or theorem in this draft.

Short English statement

For each finitely generated group $G$, classify the complex Yetter--Drinfeld modules whose Nichols algebra, after bosonization with ${\mathbb{C}} G$, has finite Gelfand--Kirillov dimension. The source's contextual restriction to nilpotent-by-finite groups and its literature assessment's reference to nilpotent groups require reconciliation. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
