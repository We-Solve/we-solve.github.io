5.6 (Agol) — Cusp-preserving virtual domination

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Agol asks whether, for every pair of cusped hyperbolic $3$-manifolds $M_1,M_2$, there are a covering $p:M'_1\to M_1$ and a cusp-preserving map $f:M'_1\to M_2$ of nonzero degree.

For connected oriented manifolds without boundary, the usual degree of a proper map is specified by \[ f_*[M'_1]=d[M_2]\quad\text{in }H_3^{\mathrm{lf}}(M_2;{\mathbb{Z}}), \qquad d\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}, \] where locally finite homology supplies the fundamental classes. Properness is needed for this pushforward. In the standard finite-volume, finite-cover setting, truncating cusps gives compact cores $W'_1,W_2$ with torus boundaries, and a cusp-preserving representative is a map of pairs $(W'_1,\partial W'_1)\to(W_2,\partial W_2)$ of nonzero relative degree. No one-to-one or onto correspondence between individual cusp components is required by the printed question.

The source statement says only a cover; its research summary interprets this as a finite cover and calls the question virtual domination. Finiteness of the cover, finite-volume assumptions, orientation and exact cusp-end/properness conditions are not explicitly printed. The literal covering question and its finite-cover interpretation therefore remain distinguished. The requested degree is nonzero, not necessarily $1$.

Short English statement

Can a cover of any cusped hyperbolic $3$-manifold map with nonzero degree to any other while taking cusps to cusps? The summary intends a finite cover, a condition not explicit in the statement.
