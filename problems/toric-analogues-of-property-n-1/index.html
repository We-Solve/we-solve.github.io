Math-1163

Toric Analogues of Property $N_1$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for toric analogues of property $(N_1)$ and whether every ample line bundle on a smooth toric variety has that property. A precise formulation needs the base field, completeness/projectivity and the convention for $(N_1)$. In the standard projective syzygy setting, take a smooth projective toric variety $X$ over an algebraically closed field and an ample line bundle $L$ with its complete-linear-series embedding. Put \[ V=H^0(X,L),\qquad S=\operatorname{Sym}V,\qquad R(X,L)=\bigoplus_{m\geq0}H^0(X,L^{\otimes m}). \] Under the usual convention, $(N_0)$ requires the natural graded map $S\to R(X,L)$ to be surjective, and $(N_1)$ further requires its homogeneous kernel to be generated in degree two. Thus $(N_1)$ includes normal generation, not merely some quadratic equations in a possibly incomplete presentation. The precise source convention is not supplied.

For the toric polytope $P$ attached to $L$, with lattice $M$, the standard combinatorial formulation uses variables $z_u$ for $u\in P\cap M$ and \[ I_P=\ker\left(k[z_u:u\in P\cap M]\to k[M][t], \ z_u\mapsto\chi^u t\right). \] Normal generation means every lattice point of $mP$ is a sum of $m$ lattice points of $P$, for all $m\geq1$. The quadratic-generation condition asks for $I_P$ to be generated by relations $z_uz_v-z_{u'}z_{v'}$ with $u+v=u'+v'$. This describes the usual toric translation of the property; it does not prove it for every smooth ample polytope. A quadratic Gröbner basis is an additional stronger request and is not substituted for ordinary quadratic generation.

The universal question is whether this standard $(N_1)$ property holds for every such smooth toric $X$ and ample $L$, together with the broader request for the correct toric formulation. The original sentence omits projective and field hypotheses; other ambient conventions must be checked before claiming the same quantified statement.

The supplied literature assessment is mismatched: it discusses restricted graph ideals and a Scarf-type resolution question, without explaining a connection to the toric $(N_1)$ problem. Its statement that no later graph-specific theorem was found is therefore not treated as evidence that this line-bundle conjecture is unresolved. The record's open and corrected-verified labels, and the 21 August 2026 triage date, are retained as provenance rather than a verified status conclusion. No general proof or counterexample is supplied.

Short English statement

The usual toric reading combines integer decomposition of the polytope with quadratic generation of its toric ideal. The universal ample-line-bundle question remains the source’s target. Its literature assessment instead discusses Scarf resolutions of graph ideals, a mismatch that cannot certify this problem’s status.
