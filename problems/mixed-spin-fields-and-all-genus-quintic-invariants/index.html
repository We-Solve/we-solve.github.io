Mixed-Spin Fields and All-Genus Quintic Invariants

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ W(x_1,\ldots,x_5)=x_1^5+\cdots+x_5^5 \] be the Fermat quintic polynomial. FJRW refers to Fan--Jarvis--Ruan--Witten theory. Write $g\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ for the genus, and let $N_g(d)$ denote the genus-$g$, degree-$d$ Gromov--Witten invariant of the quintic threefold intended by the supplied source. The source does not specify the precise conventions defining these invariants or the allowed degree range.

Question. Do the moduli spaces of mixed-spin-$P$ fields yield an effective algorithm that determines both the FJRW invariants associated with $W$, with multiple insertions, in every genus, and the invariants $N_g(d)$ in every genus and every degree for which the intended invariants are defined?

Here determination by an effective algorithm means that the requested invariant can be computed by a terminating procedure from specified input data. The source does not specify those input data, the required initial invariants, or how such initial invariants would themselves be computed. Consequently, recurrences whose initial data remain undetermined do not by themselves specify the requested complete algorithm.

Unresolved scope. The FJRW symmetry group, state space, allowed insertions, number of markings, and inclusion or exclusion of descendants are not specified. The quintic threefold, its geometric hypotheses, the normalization of $N_g(d)$, and conventions for degree zero and unstable cases are also not specified. These choices remain unresolved; the question above preserves the supplied scope without selecting them. The supplied curation further reports that the extracted Conjecture 0.16 is truncated and identifies its recoverable claim as an all-genus FJRW algorithm. The inclusion and exact scope of the Gromov--Witten clause therefore require comparison with the complete original formulation.

Short English statement

The question asks whether mixed-spin-$P$ moduli spaces give a terminating method to compute all-genus Fermat-quintic FJRW invariants with multiple insertions and quintic-threefold Gromov--Witten invariants. The insertion conventions, degree boundaries, and required initial data remain unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
