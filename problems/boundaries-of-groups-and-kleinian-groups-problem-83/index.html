Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 83

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a Gromov-hyperbolic space with its intended Gromov boundary $\partial X$, and let $G\le\operatorname{Isom}(X)$ be finitely generated and discrete. For a base point $x_0\in X$, the limit set is the boundary accumulation set \[ \Lambda_X(G)=\{\xi\in\partial X:\exists(g_j)\subseteq G, \ g_jx_0\longrightarrow\xi\text{ in }X\cup\partial X\}. \] Use the subspace topology from the Gromov boundary. Local connectedness means that for every $\xi\in\Lambda_X(G)$ and every relative open neighborhood $U$ of $\xi$, there is a connected relative open set $V$ with $\xi\in V\subseteq U$.

Kapovich's question is the implication \[ \forall(X,G)\text{ as above},\qquad \Lambda_X(G)\text{ connected}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Lambda_X(G)\text{ locally connected}. \] No cocompactness, quasiconvexity, or Kleinian/arithmetic hypothesis is stated. In particular, the limit set is not silently identified with the abstract boundary of $G$, since the hypotheses do not say that $G$ is itself hyperbolic or that an orbit map is a quasi-isometric embedding.

The extraction does not specify properness or geodesicity of $X$, a compactification convention in the nonproper case, or whether discrete means a discrete subgroup in the isometry-group topology or properly discontinuous action. In the standard proper geodesic setting the boundary and orbit-limit definitions are unambiguous; restricting to that setting would be an additional hypothesis requiring confirmation. The supplied research summary reports no general proof and mentions Cannon--Thurston/local-connectivity work, but supplies no precise theorem for this full class. Its vague non-arithmetic/standard-hypothesis remark is not made an assumption.

Short English statement

Must a connected limit set of a finitely generated discrete isometry group of a Gromov-hyperbolic space be locally connected? Preserve the broad space class unless properness or other missing action conventions are confirmed.
