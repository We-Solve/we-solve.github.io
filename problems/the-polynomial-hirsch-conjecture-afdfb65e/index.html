The Polynomial Hirsch Conjecture

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.

The one-skeleton of a polyhedron has its vertices as nodes and its edges as graph edges. Diameter is the maximum shortest-path distance between pairs of vertices in this graph.

Is the diameter of the $1$-skeleton of every polyhedron bounded by a polynomial in its number $n$ of facets and its dimension $d$? [S2]
