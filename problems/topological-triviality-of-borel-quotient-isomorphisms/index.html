Topological Triviality of Borel Quotient Isomorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E$ and $F$ be equivalence relations on sets $M$ and $N$, respectively. Write $[x]_E=\{x'\in M:x'Ex\}$ and $M/E=\{[x]_E:x\in M\}$, and define $N/F$ similarly. Once structures in a common signature have been specified on these quotients, an isomorphism $\Phi:M/E\to N/F$ is a bijection preserving their specified operations, relations, and constants.

The supplied record calls these objects Borel quotient structures but does not specify their presentations, signature, admissible equivalence relations, or the meaning of topological triviality. These omissions prevent a definitive formal statement without additional source context.

A possible interpretation can be stated conditionally. Suppose that the intended presentations give $M$ and $N$ standard Borel structures, meaning measurable structures arising from Polish topologies. For an isomorphism $\Phi:M/E\to N/F$, define its relation on representatives by \[ \Gamma_{\Phi}=\{(x,y)\in M\times N:\Phi([x]_E)=[y]_F\}. \] Under the Borel-graph interpretation, $\Phi$ is called topologically trivial when $\Gamma_{\Phi}$ is Borel in the product standard Borel space $M\times N$. This interpretation is a provisional formulation, not a definition verified from the supplied report passage. In particular, no additional Borelness assumption on $E$ or $F$ is being imposed here.

The source asks which assumptions ensure that every isomorphism between the intended quotient structures is topologically trivial. Under the provisional interpretation above, the target is to identify hypotheses $H$, including the intended class of quotient presentations and any set-theoretic assumptions, such that for every admissible pair $(M,E),(N,F)$, \[ H\ \Longrightarrow\ \left(\forall\Phi\in\operatorname{Iso}(M/E,N/F)\right)\, \Gamma_{\Phi}\in\mathcal B(M\times N), \] where $\mathcal B(M\times N)$ denotes the product Borel $\sigma$-algebra. The admissible class, the scope of $H$, and the intended definition of topological triviality remain unresolved; this displayed template does not assert that any particular hypotheses suffice.

Short English statement

Determine assumptions ensuring that every isomorphism between the intended Borel quotient structures is topologically trivial. The source omits the precise class of quotient structures and the definition of topological triviality, so the Borel-graph formulation above is conditional and requires human mathematical review.
