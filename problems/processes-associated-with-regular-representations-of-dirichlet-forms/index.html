Processes Associated with Regular Representations of Dirichlet Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $m(\mathrm dx)=\rho(x)\,\mathrm dx$ for the measure appearing in the supplied record, and let $L^2({\mathbb{R}}^d,m)$ denote the space of square-integrable functions modulo equality $m$-almost everywhere. The record specifies a Dirichlet form $(\mathcal E,W)$ on this space, calls $(\mathcal E,H)$ its closed realization, and assumes that a regular representation of $(\mathcal E,W)$ is given. Denote that representation by $(\widetilde{\mathcal E},\widetilde W)$ on $L^2(S,\mu)$; here $S$, $\mu$, and the correspondence with the original form are placeholders for representation data that the record does not describe.

For clarity, association of a Markov process $X$ with a form means agreement of its transition semigroup with the semigroup $(T_t)_{t\geq0}$ determined by that form: for every bounded measurable $f$ belonging to the relevant $L^2$ space and every $t>0$, \[ P_t f(x):=\mathbb E_x[f(X_t)]=T_t f(x) \quad\text{for almost every }x \] with respect to the relevant reference measure. If the process has a cemetery state $\Delta$, use the convention $f(\Delta)=0$. This definition does not supply the hypotheses needed for a particular process realization or its uniqueness.

Problem. For the given regular representation, identify an associated stochastic process $\widetilde X$ on its representation state space and describe its relationship, through the representation data, to a process $X^H$ associated with $(\mathcal E,H)$ on the original space, whenever these process realizations exist.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define $W$ or $H$, explain the phrase "closed realization," specify the relationship between these domains, or give the regular representation and its correspondence maps. Under the usual definition, a Dirichlet form is already closed, so the phrase alone does not determine a second domain $H$. The record also omits conditions on $\rho$, regularity or other hypotheses ensuring process realizations, and any boundary or killing conditions. Consequently, this is a conditional formulation of the supplied identification problem; the record does not determine a unique concrete process or a precise comparison theorem.

Short English statement

Given a Dirichlet form and a chosen regular representation, identify the stochastic process represented by that form and explain its relationship to the process associated with the stated closed realization $(\mathcal E,H)$. The domains, representation maps, and process-existence hypotheses require clarification before a fully specified mathematical target can be stated.
