$p$-Adic Riemann–Hilbert and de Rham Compatibility

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $E$ be a number field, let $X$ be a smooth algebraic variety over $E$, and let $\sigma:E\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$ be a field embedding. Write \[ X_\sigma=X\times_{\operatorname{Spec}E,\sigma}\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}. \] Let $L$ be a geometric $p$-adic étale local system on $X$. Here a $p$-adic étale local system means a finite-rank lisse $p$-adic sheaf; the coefficient field and the additional condition denoted by "geometric" are not specified in the supplied record.

The source proposes a comparison of two tensor functors, one obtained through the complex Riemann--Hilbert correspondence and the other through algebraic de Rham theory. To express the intended target, provisionally denote their common source category by $\mathcal G_p(X)$, and denote by $\operatorname{Conn}_{\mathrm{rs}}(X_\sigma)$ the category of algebraic vector bundles on $X_\sigma$ equipped with integrable connections having regular singularities at infinity. Integrability means that the curvature vanishes. Regularity at infinity is part of the target even when $X$ is not proper. Write the proposed functors schematically as \[ \mathcal R_\sigma,\mathcal D_\sigma: \mathcal G_p(X)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Conn}_{\mathrm{rs}}(X_\sigma). \] These symbols name the intended constructions; their existence and precise definitions are not supplied by the record.

Source conjecture, with unresolved construction data. For every such $E,X,p,\sigma$, the two proposed functors are canonically isomorphic as tensor functors: \[ \mathcal R_\sigma\cong^{\otimes}\mathcal D_\sigma. \] Thus the proposed comparison must be natural in every geometric local system $L$, compatible with tensor products and tensor units, and an isomorphism of the resulting connections. Moreover, for every such $L$, the filtered de Rham realization is conjectured to be a complex variation of Hodge structures on $X_\sigma$.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define $\mathcal G_p(X)$, specify the coefficient field, or explain how $L$ produces the complex local system required by complex Riemann--Hilbert. The embedding $\sigma$ specifies the complex base variety but does not itself specify that coefficient comparison. Nor does the record specify the algebraic de Rham realization, its descent and comparison data, or its Hodge filtration. For a filtered connection $(\mathcal V,\nabla,F^\bullet)$, a variation of Hodge structures requires, in particular, Griffiths transversality \[ \nabla(F^a\mathcal V)\subseteq F^{a-1}\mathcal V\otimes\Omega^1_{X_\sigma} \qquad(a\in{\mathbb{Z}}), \] together with the appropriate Hodge-structure data and axioms. The weight, the convention for a complex variation, and any polarization requirement are unspecified. Consequently, the displayed assertions preserve the intended conjectural targets but do not constitute a fully determined formulation until these missing data are resolved.

Short English statement

For a smooth variety over a number field, the source conjectures that the complex Riemann--Hilbert and algebraic de Rham constructions attached to every geometric $p$-adic local system agree canonically as tensor functors. It also conjectures that the filtered de Rham realization is a complex variation of Hodge structures. The meaning of geometric, the coefficient comparisons, the realization functors, and the Hodge-theoretic conventions require clarification.
