Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.9

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 3$, let $F_n=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle$ be the free group with the specified free basis, and let $\mathbb S_n$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. Define the group of conjugating automorphisms by \[ C_n=\left\{\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_n):\ \exists\pi\in\mathbb S_n\ \exists(t_1,\ldots,t_n)\in F_n^n\ \forall i\in\{1,\ldots,n\},\quad \varphi(x_i)=t_i^{-1}x_{\pi(i)}t_i\right\}, \] with the operation inherited from $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$. Here the permutation and the conjugating elements may depend on $\varphi$.

A group $G$ is called linear if there exist a field $K$, a positive integer $d$, and an injective group homomorphism $G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_d(K)$.

The question is whether $C_n$ is linear for every integer $n\geq 3$; explicitly, whether \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ n\geq 3,\quad \exists\text{ a field }K_n\ \exists d_n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\ \exists\text{ an injective homomorphism }\rho_n:C_n\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{d_n}(K_n). \] The source does not prescribe a field or its characteristic. This formulation uses the convention above and permits both the field and the dimension to depend on $n$.

For context, the supplied source identifies the subgroup of conjugating automorphisms fixing $x_1x_2\cdots x_n$ with the braid group $B_n$, and states that $B_n$ is linear for every $n\geq 2$, whereas $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ is not linear for $n\geq 3$. These are supplied background assertions, not conclusions established by this draft.

Short English statement

For each rank $n\geq 3$, consider all automorphisms of the free group that send each specified free generator to a conjugate of a generator, allowing a permutation of the generators. Must this group admit a faithful representation by finite square matrices over some field?
