Math-3592

Birkhoff Classification over $mathbb Z/p^6mathbb Z$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and put $R_p={\mathbb{Z}}/p^6{\mathbb{Z}}$. Here $\operatorname{mod}R_p$ denotes the category of finitely generated $R_p$-modules; these are precisely finite abelian groups annihilated by $p^6$, equipped with their natural $R_p$-module structures. The exponent is required to divide $p^6$, not necessarily to equal $p^6$.

Define $\operatorname{mono}(1\to2,\operatorname{mod}R_p)$ to have as objects injective $R_p$-linear maps $f:A\hookrightarrow B$, where $A,B\in\operatorname{mod}R_p$. A morphism from $f:A\hookrightarrow B$ to $g:A'\hookrightarrow B'$ is a pair of $R_p$-linear maps $(\alpha:A\to A',\beta:B\to B')$ satisfying \[ \beta\circ f=g\circ\alpha. \] Under the usual interpretation of Birkhoff equivalence, $f$ and $g$ are equivalent exactly when such a pair exists with both $\alpha$ and $\beta$ isomorphisms. Equivalently, the problem concerns pairs $(B,U)$, with $B$ a finite abelian group annihilated by $p^6$ and $U\leq B$, where $(B,U)$ and $(B',U')$ are equivalent if there is a group isomorphism $\beta:B\to B'$ with $\beta(U)=U'$.

Classification problem. Describe the isomorphism classes of all objects of \[ \operatorname{mono}(1\to2,\operatorname{mod}({\mathbb{Z}}/p^6{\mathbb{Z}})), \] that is, classify all these embeddings up to the equivalence just specified, for arbitrary finite $A$ and $B$.

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied record does not explicitly quantify $p$; the formulation above treats it as an arbitrary prime, with the classification sought for each such $p$. Short English statement

For each prime $p$, classify embeddings between finite abelian groups annihilated by $p^6$, identifying two embeddings when isomorphisms of their domains and codomains make the resulting square commute. The interpretation of Birkhoff equivalence and the scope of the requested classification require human source review. The supplied curation labels the problem open.
