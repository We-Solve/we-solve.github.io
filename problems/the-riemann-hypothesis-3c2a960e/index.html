The Riemann Hypothesis

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $s\in{\mathbb{C}}$ with $\Re s>1$, define \[ \zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}=\prod_{p\text{ prime}}(1-p^{-s})^{-1}. \] Use its meromorphic continuation to ${\mathbb{C}}$, whose only pole is at $s=1$. The assertion is \[ \forall\rho\in{\mathbb{C}},\qquad \left(0<\Re\rho<1\ \text{and}\ \zeta(\rho)=0\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ \Re\rho=\tfrac12. \] The open critical strip excludes the negative even integer zeros. The assertion concerns locations, not multiplicities, of zeros.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Are all zeta zeros between the vertical lines zero and one located on the halfway line?
