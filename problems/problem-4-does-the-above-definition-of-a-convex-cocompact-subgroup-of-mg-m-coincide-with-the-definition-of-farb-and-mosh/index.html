Problem 4: — Does the above definition of a convex cocompact subgroup of Mg,m coincide with the definition of Farb and Mosher in […

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ and $M_{g,m}$ be the source's surface and mapping class group, and distinguish the missing preceding predicate $\mathrm{CC}_{\mathrm{src}}$ from Farb--Mosher convex cocompactness $\mathrm{CC}_{\mathrm{FM}}$. The compound problem asks:
  1. Do these two predicates agree for subgroups of $M_{g,m}$? The preceding source definition is absent and is not replaced by an assumed curve-graph or Teichmüller criterion.
  2. For each such convex cocompact subgroup $\Gamma$, is its natural surface-group extension \[ 1\longrightarrow\pi_1(S)\longrightarrow E_\Gamma \longrightarrow\Gamma\longrightarrow1 \] word hyperbolic, meaning a finitely generated group with a thin-triangle Cayley graph? The intended extension is induced by the mapping-class outer action, but its construction and closed versus punctured surface hypotheses must be supplied. Peripheral subgroups make that distinction material; no relative-hyperbolicity conclusion is substituted.
  3. Is there a convex cocompact subgroup $\Gamma\leq M_{g,m}$ isomorphic to $\pi_1(\Sigma_h)$ for a closed surface with $h\geq2$?

The accompanying discussion uses "Veech surfaces" for projections of complex Teichmüller geodesic disks whose stabilizers are lattices in $\operatorname{PSL}(2,{\mathbb{R}})$. In this usage they are curves/orbifold surfaces in moduli space, not the original flat surface. The disk is described as maximal embedded in a bounded-domain model in ${\mathbb{C}}^{3g-3+m}$. The source reports these quotients as finite type with isolated singularities and never closed [V], so their corresponding groups are virtually free, with distinguished conjugacy classes from cusps. It cites [McM1, McM2] for properties, [L] for elementary constructions and Leininger--Reid [LR] for explicit subgroup constructions. These contextual results are not asserted to produce the closed-surface subgroup in item (3).

Short English statement

Compare the two convex-cocompactness definitions, ask whether the associated surface-group extension is hyperbolic, and seek a convex cocompact closed-surface subgroup, retaining the Veech-curve context.
