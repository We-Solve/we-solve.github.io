Positive Maps and Multipartite Bound Entanglement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
$M_d(\mathbb C)$ is the matrix algebra, with Hilbert--Schmidt inner product $\langle F,G\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(F^\dagger G)$. A map is positive if it sends positive semidefinite matrices to positive semidefinite matrices, and completely positive if every tensor amplification by an identity map is positive. The source uses Hermitian orthonormal bases $F^{(i)}_\mu$ and real coefficients $\lambda^{(i)}_\mu$. For the four-qubit lattice states, let $\sigma_0=I_2$ and $\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3$ be Pauli matrices; set $|\psi_+\rangle=\tfrac12\sum_{a,b=1}^2|a,b\rangle_A|a,b\rangle_B$ and $|\psi_{\alpha\beta}\rangle=(I_4\otimes\sigma_\alpha\otimes\sigma_\beta)|\psi_+\rangle$. Here $\varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{0,1,2,3\}^2$ and $|I|$ is its cardinality. PPT means positivity after partial transposition across the $A|B$ partition.
Source scope and qualifications.
The supplied positivity preamble omits the coefficient hypotheses on Lambda_1 that are needed for the source's proposition. An arbitrary Lambda_1 with large negative coefficients does not satisfy the stated conclusion. The primary source requires all coefficients of Lambda_1 to be positive and sufficiently large relative to the single negative coefficient of Lambda_2. This missing premise is explicitly restored in the source clarification.
Primary-source hypothesis.
In the report's positivity proposition, all $\lambda^{(1)}_\mu$ are positive and are at least the absolute value of the single negative coefficient of $\Lambda_2$; every other coefficient of $\Lambda_2$ has the same lower bound. The request is to generalize this family, for example by relaxing the orthonormal-basis identity, and to study the entanglement-detection properties of the states $\rho_I$.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For $i=1,2$, let $$\Lambda_i[X]=\sum_{\mu=0}^{d_i^2-1}\lambda_\mu^{(i)}F_\mu^{(i)}XF_\mu^{(i)},$$ where the $F_\mu^{(i)}$ form Hermitian, Hilbert–Schmidt orthonormal bases of $M_{d_i}(\mathbb{C})$. If exactly one coefficient of $\Lambda_2$ is negative and every positive coefficient is at least its absolute value, then $\Lambda=\Lambda_1\otimes\operatorname{id}_{d_2}+\operatorname{id}_{d_1}\otimes\Lambda_2$ is positive. Can this family be generalized by relaxing the orthonormal-basis hypothesis? For $$\rho_I=\frac1{|I|}\sum_{(\alpha,\beta)\in I}|\psi_{\alpha\beta}\rangle\langle\psi_{\alpha\beta}|,$$ determine the properties of these states and, in particular, whether their entanglement can be detected by other criteria.

Short English statement

Extend the report's family of positive maps and determine how the associated Bell-diagonal states can exhibit detectable bound entanglement.
