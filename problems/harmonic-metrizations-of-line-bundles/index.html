Harmonic Metrizations of Line Bundles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ denote a line bundle on the space $X$, and let $\overline{M}$ denote $M$ equipped with a metrization. Write $c_1(\overline{M})$ for the curvature object associated with that metrization in the intended non-Archimedean theory. The supplied record does not specify that theory, the hypotheses on $X$, the background object $\omega$, or the dimension parameter $n$.

The source calls the curvature $\omega$-harmonic when it is $\omega$-bounded and satisfies \[ c_1(\overline{M})\,\omega^{n-1}=\lambda(M)\,\omega^n. \] Here $\omega$-boundedness, the products, their ambient space of equality, and the scalar $\lambda(M)$ are not defined in the supplied record.

Question, conditional on the missing setup. For every line bundle $M$ on $X$, does there exist a metrization $\overline{M}$ whose curvature is $\omega$-harmonic, and is this metrization unique up to scale? Under the usual norm interpretation, uniqueness up to scale means that any two admissible metrizations differ by multiplication by a single positive real constant on all of $X$; the intended metric convention remains to be specified.

Unresolved qualifications. A precise conjecture requires the underlying valued field, the class and hypotheses of $X$, the meaning and positivity assumptions of $\omega$, the relation of $n$ to $X$, the admissible class of metrics, the curvature and product constructions, the definition of $\omega$-boundedness, and the prescription of $\lambda(M)$. These cannot be recovered from the supplied statement alone.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every line bundle admits a metric, unique up to a constant scale, whose curvature is bounded relative to $\omega$ and satisfies the displayed proportionality equation. The geometric setting and several essential definitions are missing, so this is a conditional statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
