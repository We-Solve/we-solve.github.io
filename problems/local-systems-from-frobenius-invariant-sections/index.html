Math-15866

Local Systems from Frobenius-Invariant Sections

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Retain $F^a$ for the space and $M$ for the Frobenius object occurring in the source. The conjectural assertion is that the Frobenius invariants $M^{\varphi=1}$ form a local system of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$-vector spaces on $F^a$. Neither the space, the underlying coefficient sheaf, the construction of $M$, nor the prime-dependent hypotheses are defined in the record. The superscript $a$ is kept as part of the source notation and is not interpreted as an algebraic closure, an integer power or a named period-domain locus without evidence.

If $M$ is realized on an intended site of $F^a$ with a Frobenius operator compatible with restriction, the invariant sheaf would be described by \[ \mathcal L(U)=M^{\varphi=1}(U) =\{s\in M(U):\varphi(s)=s\} =\ker(\varphi-1)(U). \] This presumes the relevant identifications if Frobenius is semilinear or acts through a pullback; these cannot be read off from the notation alone. To obtain ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$-vector spaces, scalar multiplication by ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$ must preserve the invariant sections, for example because Frobenius fixes the coefficient copy of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$. No exactness or surjectivity of $\varphi-1$ is asserted.

In a locally constant finite-rank sheaf interpretation, the desired conclusion says that each point has a neighborhood in the chosen site on which $\mathcal L$ is isomorphic to a constant sheaf with fiber ${\mathbb{Q}}_p^r$, for some finite rank $r$, locally constant on the base. A $p$-adic local system can instead be expressed in an appropriate lisse or continuous-representation framework; the topology and coefficient conventions matter, and ordinary discrete locally constant sheaves on an unspecified site cannot be silently substituted. The record gives no rank, no integral lattice or tower of finite-level sheaves, and no site on which local triviality is meant.

The quantifier concerns the particular family $M$ constructed in the missing source context, not every sheaf equipped with an arbitrary endomorphism. A precise theorem requires that construction and its admissibility hypotheses. The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports related $p$-adic local-system constructions but no theorem found proving this exact assertion in the stated generality. Its open label and the verification field's report of source checking remain supplied metadata, not independently confirmed claims.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the sections fixed by Frobenius in the intended family $M$ form a ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$-local system on $F^a$. The construction of the family, its scalar structure, the base space and the topology defining a local system are absent, so they cannot be replaced by an arbitrary Frobenius module or an assumed site.
