Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a cluster algebra denoted by $A(B,x)$, with an initial tuple written $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_m)$. For nonnegative integer vectors $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)$ and $\mathbf d=(d_1,\ldots,d_m)$, write \[ x^a=\prod_{i=1}^m x_i^{a_i},\qquad a\cdot\mathbf d=\sum_{i=1}^m a_i d_i. \] Thus $a\cdot\mathbf d=0$ means that $a_i d_i=0$ for every $i$. Here nonnegative integer indexing is the interpretation used for the displayed exponents and dimension vectors.
