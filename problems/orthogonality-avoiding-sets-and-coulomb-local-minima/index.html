Orthogonality-Avoiding Sets and Coulomb Local Minima

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record contains two separate questions, which must not be interpreted as a single conjecture.

First, let \[ S^2=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^3:\|x\|_2=1\},\qquad \langle x,y\rangle=\sum_{i=1}^3x_i y_i. \] To interpret the numerical bound, provisionally let $\sigma$ be normalized surface area on $S^2$, so that $\sigma(S^2)=1$. This normalization is not explicitly specified in the supplied statement and requires confirmation. Define \[ \mathcal A=\{A\subseteq S^2:A\text{ is surface measurable and }\langle x,y\rangle\ne0\text{ for every }x,y\in A\}, \qquad \alpha=\sup_{A\in\mathcal A}\sigma(A). \] The orthogonality condition is pointwise for every pair, not merely for almost every pair. The first question asks for the exact value of $\alpha$ and whether the supplied upper bound $1/3$ can be improved, meaning whether \[ \exists\varepsilon>0\quad\forall A\in\mathcal A, \qquad \sigma(A)\le\frac13-\varepsilon. \] The record describes $1/3$ as a known upper bound; that description is supplied source information, not an independently verified assertion here. The use of a supremum does not assert that an extremizing measurable set exists.

Second, the record asks about the number $L_n$ of local minima of the Coulomb potential for $n$ points on $S^2$. A conventional candidate model, stated explicitly for review, is the collision-free configuration space \[ X_n=\{(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\in(S^2)^n:x_i\ne x_j\text{ whenever }i\ne j\},\qquad n\ge2, \] with energy \[ E_n(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}\frac1{\|x_i-x_j\|_2}. \] Here the distance is the ambient Euclidean distance. Under the non-strict convention, a configuration $x\in X_n$ is a local minimum if some neighborhood $U$ of $x$ in $X_n$ satisfies $E_n(x)\le E_n(y)$ for every $y\in U$. The supplied record does not explicitly fix this energy formula, the configuration-space convention, or the meaning of counting local minima.

In particular, it does not specify whether configurations related by rotations, reflections, or permutations are identified, nor whether strictness is required after any such identification. These choices matter because the conventional energy is invariant under simultaneous rotations. Thus $L_n$ cannot be assigned an unambiguous mathematical definition from the supplied record alone. Once the intended energy and counting convention are fixed, the requested eventual inequality is \[ \exists N\in{\mathbb{N}}\quad\forall n\in{\mathbb{N}},\qquad n\ge\max\{N,2\}\Longrightarrow L_n>1. \] This second formulation remains conditional on those unresolved conventions.

Short English statement

Two distinct questions are preserved: determine the largest possible normalized area of a measurable subset of $S^2$ containing no orthogonal pair, including whether the stated bound $1/3$ can be reduced; and determine whether every sufficiently large number of points admits more than one Coulomb local minimum under the intended counting convention. The area normalization and the definition of distinct local minima require confirmation. Both questions carry the supplied open label, without independent verification.
