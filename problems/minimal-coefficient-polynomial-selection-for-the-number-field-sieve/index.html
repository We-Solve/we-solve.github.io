Math-13898

Minimal-Coefficient Polynomial Selection for the Number Field Sieve

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Given a modulus $N$ and prescribed degrees $d_f,d_g$, construct irreducible coprime polynomials \[ f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{d_f}a_ix^i,\qquad g(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{d_g}b_jx^j\quad\text{in }{\mathbb{Z}}[x], \qquad a_{d_f}b_{d_g}\ne0, \] with a common root modulo $N$ and coefficients of minimal possible absolute size. In the usual nontrivial setting, $N$ is an integer greater than one and the degrees are positive integers; the source does not explicitly specify their admissible ranges or impose compositeness of $N$.

Having a common root means \[ \exists r\in{\mathbb{Z}}/N{\mathbb{Z}}:\qquad f(r)=g(r)=0\quad\text{in }{\mathbb{Z}}/N{\mathbb{Z}}. \] The source does not prescribe $r$, so it is a variable of the construction rather than fixed input. The standard polynomial-selection interpretation of coprime is $\gcd_{{\mathbb{Q}}[x]}(f,g)=1$. It cannot mean $(f,g)={\mathbb{Z}}[x]$ for $N>1$, since a polynomial identity $Af+Bg=1$ with $A,B\in{\mathbb{Z}}[x]$ would contradict the common-root condition upon evaluation modulo $N$. The intended irreducibility convention and any primitive or content normalization should be checked against the report.

The optimization objective is unresolved. For example, one possible scalar objective would minimize \[ H(f,g)=\max\left(\{|a_i|:0\le i\le d_f\}\cup\{|b_j|:0\le j\le d_g\}\right) \] over all admissible pairs, but the record does not select this objective rather than separate heights, weighted or skewed norms, or another comparison of coefficients. Nor does it supply a conjectural minimum, asymptotic rate in $N$, or computational complexity requirement. Thus the requested construction must retain the prescribed degrees, irreducibility, coprimality, common modular root, and a genuinely optimal coefficient criterion once that criterion is recovered; the example height above is not silently adopted as the source's definition.

Short English statement

The problem asks for optimally small integer polynomials of prescribed degrees that are irreducible, coprime over the rationals, and share a root modulo the input modulus. The exact meaning of optimal coefficient size and the admissible input conventions still need specification.
