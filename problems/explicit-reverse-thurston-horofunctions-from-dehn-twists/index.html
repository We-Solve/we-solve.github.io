Math-1731

Explicit reverse Thurston horofunctions from Dehn twists

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a finite-type hyperbolic-surface Teichmüller space on which the intended Thurston distance is \[ d_{\mathrm{Th}}(Y,X)=\log\sup_{\lambda\ne0}\frac{\ell_\lambda(X)}{\ell_\lambda(Y)}, \] with the appropriate measured-lamination convention. Put $d^{\mathrm{rev}}(X,Y)=d_{\mathrm{Th}}(Y,X)$ and fix a basepoint $b$. The reverse horofunction embedding is \[ Y\longmapsto h_Y^{\mathrm{rev}},\qquad h_Y^{\mathrm{rev}}(X)=d_{\mathrm{Th}}(Y,X)-d_{\mathrm{Th}}(Y,b). \] Its boundary consists of limits in the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets, excluding functions arising from interior points. Walsh's source question is to identify this reverse horofunction boundary, not the forward boundary. The extracted following paragraph defines reduced Teichmüller space for bordered surfaces, but does not clearly state whether it belongs to this problem; bordered/arc-metric conventions must be settled separately.

The aid's partial theorem concerns an unoriented essential nonperipheral simple closed curve $\alpha$, its Dehn twist $T_\alpha$, and any initial point $p$. It asserts, as $|n|\to\infty$, \[ \exp d_{\mathrm{Th}}(T_\alpha^n p,X) =|n|A_{p,\alpha}\ell_\alpha(X)+o(|n|),\qquad A_{p,\alpha}>0, \] locally uniformly in $X$. In the length-supremum convention its coefficient is the corresponding finite positive supremum of $i(\alpha,\lambda)/\ell_\lambda(p)$; positivity presupposes a lamination intersecting $\alpha$. Normalizing at $b$ cancels the coefficient and yields \[ h_{T_\alpha^n p}^{\mathrm{rev}}(X)\longrightarrow h_\alpha(X)=\log\frac{\ell_\alpha(X)}{\ell_\alpha(b)}. \] The limit is independent of $p$ and of the sign of the twisting direction. It is a boundary function: pinching $\alpha$ makes it unbounded below, whereas every fixed-point function $h_Y^{\mathrm{rev}}$ is bounded below by $-d_{\mathrm{Th}}(Y,b)$.

The aid further asserts that the assignment $\alpha\mapsto h_\alpha$ is injective on unoriented essential curve isotopy classes. Equivariance uses the basepoint-normalized action \[ (f\cdot h)(X)=h(f^{-1}X)-h(f^{-1}b),\qquad f\cdot h_\alpha=h_{f\alpha}. \] It is not plain precomposition when $f$ moves $b$. This identifies a family of explicit reverse boundary points, not every point of the boundary.

According to the aid the functional form matches Walsh's proposed Busemann formula for a one-component simple stump. It does not show that these points are Busemann points, meaning realizable by the appropriate almost-geodesic rays, nor that the twisting orbits are such rays. The full reverse boundary, topology, and Busemann classification remain beyond the supplied result. Its precise finite-type/boundary hypotheses and a complete source for the asymptotic need verification.

Short English statement

For the reversed metric, the supplied Dehn-twist asymptotic gives explicit normalized logarithmic length functions in the horofunction boundary. They distinguish unoriented curve classes and transform equivariantly after renormalizing at the basepoint. This constructs a subset of boundary points; it does not classify the whole boundary or establish Busemann realization, and the appended bordered-surface context needs clarification.
