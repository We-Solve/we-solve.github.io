Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime. A pro-$p$ group is a topological group that is an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. It is finitely generated if some finite subset generates a dense subgroup. A pro-$p$ group is $p$-adic analytic if it admits the structure of a finite-dimensional $p$-adic Lie group compatible with its topology.

All subgroups generated below are understood to be topologically closed. For closed subgroups $K,L\leq G$, write \[ K^{p^r}=\overline{\langle x^{p^r}:x\in K\rangle},\qquad [K,L]=\overline{\langle x^{-1}y^{-1}xy:x\in K,\ y\in L\rangle}. \] Use the following conventions for the five named filtrations:

  1. (a) The $p$-power series is $S_i=G^{p^i}$ for $i\geq0$.
  2. (b) The iterated $p$-power series is $S_0=G$ and $S_{i+1}=S_i^p$ for $i\geq0$.
  3. (c) The lower $p$-series is $P_1(G)=G$ and \[ P_{n+1}(G)=\overline{P_n(G)^p[P_n(G),G]},\qquad S_i=P_{i+1}(G). \]
  4. (d) The Frattini series is $S_0=G$ and $S_{i+1}=\Phi(S_i)$, where \[ \Phi(K)=\overline{K^p[K,K]} \] is the Frattini subgroup of a pro-$p$ group $K$.
  5. (e) The dimension subgroup series is taken to mean the Zassenhaus filtration. Define $\gamma_1(G)=G$ and $\gamma_{j+1}(G)=[\gamma_j(G),G]$, and put \[ D_n(G)=\overline{\left\langle \gamma_j(G)^{p^k}:j\geq1,\ k\geq0,\ jp^k\geq n\right\rangle},\qquad S_i=D_{i+1}(G). \]

For an infinite finitely generated pro-$p$ group $G$ and a filtration $\mathcal S=(S_i)_{i\geq0}$ as above, define the Hausdorff dimension of a closed subgroup $H\leq G$, relative to $\mathcal S$, by \[ \operatorname{hdim}^{\mathcal S}_G(H) =\liminf_{i\to\infty} \frac{\log_p[HS_i:S_i]}{\log_p[G:S_i]}, \] where the ratio is taken for sufficiently large $i$ with $[G:S_i]>1$. Its Hausdorff spectrum is \[ \operatorname{hspec}^{\mathcal S}(G) =\{\operatorname{hdim}^{\mathcal S}_G(H):H\leq G\text{ is closed}\}. \] Finite Hausdorff spectrum means that this set has finitely many elements.

Question. For each of the five filtrations $\mathcal S$ separately, is the following implication true for every prime $p$ and every infinite finitely generated pro-$p$ group $G$? \[ \left|\operatorname{hspec}^{\mathcal S}(G)\right|<\infty \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ is }p\text{-adic analytic}. \] The supplied question also allows finite $G$; such groups are already $p$-adic analytic of dimension zero, so restricting the displayed formulation to infinite $G$ avoids a finite-group convention for the dimension formula without removing a case requiring an answer. The five implications are separate questions, each assuming finiteness for its indicated filtration.

Unresolved source qualification. The supplied record names the filtrations and Hausdorff spectrum without defining them. This draft uses the conventions above, including the spectrum over all closed subgroups and the Zassenhaus interpretation of the dimension subgroup series. Agreement with the cited original problem requires human source review.

Short English statement

For each of five standard filtrations, ask whether a finitely generated pro-$p$ group must be $p$-adic analytic whenever its closed subgroups have only finitely many Hausdorff dimensions relative to that filtration. Each filtration gives a separate question. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that status and the filtration conventions have not been independently verified.
