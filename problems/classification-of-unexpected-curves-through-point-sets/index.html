Math-3843

Classification of Unexpected Curves Through Point Sets

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks which curves containing a finite point set $Z$ and vanishing to order $m_Z$ at a general point are unexpected. The record does not define $m_Z$, specify the ambient space or ground field, or give the allowed degrees. Thus it does not determine a unique mathematical classification problem.

A conditional formulation using the usual dimension comparison for plane curves is as follows. Suppose that the intended setting is ${\mathbb{P}}^2_k$ over an algebraically closed field $k$, with characteristic still to be specified. Let $Z\subset{\mathbb{P}}^2_k$ be a finite reduced point set, let $d,m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$, and let $p\in{\mathbb{P}}^2_k\setminus Z$. Write $\mathcal I_Z$ and $\mathcal I_p$ for the ideal sheaves of $Z$ and $p$, respectively, and define \[ V_d(Z)=H^0\!\left({\mathbb{P}}^2_k,\mathcal I_Z(d)\right),\qquad W_{d,m}(Z,p)=H^0\!\left({\mathbb{P}}^2_k,(\mathcal I_Z\cap\mathcal I_p^m)(d)\right). \] These are vector spaces of degree-$d$ forms; a nonzero member of $W_{d,m}(Z,p)$, modulo nonzero scalar multiplication, defines a curve containing $Z$ with multiplicity at least $m$ at $p$. The expected vector-space dimension after imposing the multiplicity condition is \[ e_{d,m}(Z)=\max\!\left\{0,\dim_k V_d(Z)-\binom{m+1}{2}\right\}. \] In this conditional formulation, the family is unexpected when \[ \dim_k W_{d,m}(Z,p)>e_{d,m}(Z) \] for $p$ in a nonempty Zariski-open subset of ${\mathbb{P}}^2_k\setminus Z$. Thus unexpectedness refers to an excess dimension of the family, rather than merely to the existence of one curve.

If this plane-curve convention is intended and $m_Z$ is a specified nonnegative integer associated with $Z$, the requested classification concerns the degrees $d$, point sets $Z$, and corresponding curves for which the displayed inequality holds with $m=m_Z$. However, neither this interpretation of unexpectedness nor the definition of $m_Z$ is supplied explicitly. No relation such as $d=m_Z+1$, no characteristic restriction, and no irreducibility or reducedness condition on the curves can be asserted from the record. These qualifications must be resolved before this conditional formulation can serve as a definitive statement of the source problem.

Short English statement

The question asks when curves through a finite point set, with prescribed multiplicity at a general point, form a larger family than the expected dimension predicts. The displayed plane-curve formulation is conditional: the source record leaves the ambient setting, the meaning of $m_Z$, the allowed degrees, and the precise classification target unspecified.
