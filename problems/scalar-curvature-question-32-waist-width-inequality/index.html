Scalar Curvature Question [?32]: Waist-Width Inequality

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The proposed waist--width inequality is \[ \operatorname{width}_{n-1}(X)\leq C_n\operatorname{waist}_{n-k+1}(X) \] for all complete Riemannian $n$-manifolds $X$, where $C_n$ depends only on dimension as printed. The source does not define these width/waist functionals, the range or quantifier of $k$, or their normalization. Urysohn widths, sweepout waists, volumes and length-normalized variants cannot be silently identified; the exact displayed indices are retained.

The appended discussion defines a contractibility radius. With $B_x(r)$ denoting a metric ball, write \[ \operatorname{contr}(X,r)=\inf\{R\geq r:\ \forall x\in X,\ B_x(r)\hookrightarrow B_x(R)\text{ is null-homotopic}\}, \] with value $+\infty$ if the class is empty. Thus contraction is permitted within the larger concentric ball, not required intrinsically in the smaller ball.

The supplied text then makes three additional claims. (A) Complete manifolds with cocompact isometry group have $\operatorname{contr}(X,r)=\infty$ for every $r>0$. (B) If $\operatorname{contr}(X,r_i)\leq r_{i+1}$ for $i=1,\ldots,n$ and $r_1\leq\cdots\leq r_{n+1}<\infty$, then \[ \operatorname{fil.rad}[X]\geq\frac{r_1}{(n+1)!}. \] (C) Universal covers $\widetilde X$ of compact aspherical $n$-manifolds have infinite filling radius and $\operatorname{waist}_m(\widetilde X)=\infty$ for $m=1,\ldots,n$.

Claim (A) is incompatible with the given definition: Euclidean space has cocompact isometry group and its convex balls contract within themselves, so its contractibility radius is finite. No replacement sign or inequality is guessed. The filling-radius/fundamental-class and waist conventions for noncompact spaces in (B)--(C), and their reference [41], are absent. These statements are therefore preserved as source claims requiring correction or contextual verification, not used as proved premises for the main conjecture.

Short English statement

Compare the source's waist and width quantities on every complete manifold. Their definitions and an index range are missing, and the appended contractibility-radius discussion contains an explicit inconsistency that must be resolved.
