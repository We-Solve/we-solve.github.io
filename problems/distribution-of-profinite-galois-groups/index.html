Distribution of Profinite Galois Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A pro-$p$ group, for a prime $p$, is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. For an integer $g\geq 0$, a pro-$p$ presentation with $g$ generators and $g$ relators has the form \[ G\cong F_g/\overline{\langle\!\langle r_1,\ldots,r_g\rangle\!\rangle}, \] where $F_g$ is the free pro-$p$ group on $g$ generators, each $r_i$ lies in $F_g$, and the denominator is the closed normal subgroup generated by the relators. The source does not specify whether the generator and relator counts must be minimal, which values of $g$ are intended, or whether $p$ and $g$ are fixed as $S$ varies. Isomorphisms of pro-$p$ groups are understood here as continuous isomorphisms, and $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ denotes continuous automorphisms; any intended restriction on these automorphisms remains unspecified.

The supplied question asks whether, for a given $g$-generator, $g$-relator pro-$p$ group $G$, varying a finite set $S$ of primes gives \[ \Pr\left(G_S\cong G\right)\stackrel{?}{=}\frac{A(G)}{|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|}. \] This is an incomplete distributional formulation: the supplied record does not define $G_S$, the function $A(G)$, the admissible sets $S$, or the probability measure or limiting-density procedure represented by $\Pr$. Although the title refers to Galois groups, no base field, extension, or ramification conditions defining $G_S$ are supplied. Furthermore, a pro-$p$ group need not have a finite automorphism group, so the numerical meaning of the denominator and of the proposed ratio also requires clarification. These missing data prevent a fully specified conjecture; the displayed equality preserves the source question without supplying additional arithmetic hypotheses or a random-group model.

Short English statement

The question asks whether varying a finite set of primes produces a specified pro-$p$ group $G$ with probability $A(G)/|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|$. The supplied record leaves the arithmetic group, weighting function, sampling procedure, and interpretation of the ratio undefined, so this remains a statement-only draft requiring clarification and human mathematical review.
