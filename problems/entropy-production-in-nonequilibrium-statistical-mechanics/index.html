Phys-101 · Math-12039

Entropy Production in Nonequilibrium Statistical Mechanics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra ultra 0% cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
A classical system has a phase-space evolution and a stationary probability measure, invariant under that evolution. A nonconservative external force performs work not represented by a scalar potential; thermostat forces remove energy so that stationary averages can exist. An entropy creation rate would be an observable or measurable functional of the nonequilibrium stationary state. The requested notion must apply away from equilibrium, including situations where temperature itself lacks an established definition.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Give a fundamental and experimentally accessible definition of the entropy creation rate for general classical systems in stationary nonequilibrium states under external nonconservative forces balanced on average by thermostat forces, without restricting to systems close to equilibrium.
Scope and source qualification.
The source also asks for general relations among stationary dynamical averages, analogous in role to equilibrium thermodynamic relations. It leaves the precise admissible model class and axioms for entropy production to the proposed theory.

Short English statement

Define an experimentally meaningful entropy production rate for general stationary nonequilibrium classical systems.
