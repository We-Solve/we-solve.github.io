Math-3976

Outer Automorphisms of p-Adic Absolute Galois Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $K$ be a $p$-adic field. Using the usual interpretation of this term, take $K$ to be a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$. Fix a separable closure $K^{\mathrm{sep}}$, and give the absolute Galois group \[ G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K) \] its Krull topology. Under the conventional continuous interpretation of automorphisms of a profinite group, define \[ \operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_K) =\{\alpha:G_K\longrightarrow G_K:\alpha\text{ is a continuous group isomorphism}\}, \qquad \operatorname{Inn}(G_K) =\{g\mapsto hgh^{-1}:h\in G_K\}, \] and \[ \operatorname{Out}_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_K) =\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_K)/\operatorname{Inn}(G_K). \] Problem. For each prime $p$ and each $p$-adic field $K$, determine the structure of $\operatorname{Out}_{\mathrm{cont}}(G_K)$, denoted $\operatorname{Out}(G_K)$ in the supplied source.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The source does not explicitly define a $p$-adic field or specify whether automorphisms must be continuous. The definitions above state the conventional interpretation used for this draft. It also does not specify what constitutes a determination of the structure, or whether the intended classification includes a topology on the outer automorphism group. Thus the request remains a structural classification problem rather than a specified conjectural isomorphism.

For context, write $G_K(p)$ for the maximal pro-$p$ quotient of $G_K$, namely the quotient by the intersection of all open normal subgroups of $p$-power index. The supplied excerpt reports a family of infinite-order elements in $\operatorname{Out}(G_K(p))$ generalizing surface Dehn twists. This is attributed background, not an independently verified assertion here, and the requested classification concerns $G_K$ itself. The excerpt motivates this work through arithmetic topology, attributed there to Mazur, and an analogy with surface mapping class groups; it specifies no identification with a mapping class group. Its subsequent discussion of surface-group splittings is truncated and supplies no additional precise hypothesis for the problem.

Short English statement

Describe the outer automorphism group of the absolute Galois group of each $p$-adic field, using continuous automorphisms under the stated convention. The supplied record labels the problem open. Its reported Dehn-twist constructions concern the maximal pro-$p$ quotient and do not supply a classification of the full group.
