Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.34

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite group $G$ is quasisimple if $G=[G,G]$ and $G/Z(G)$ is a nonabelian simple group, where $Z(G)$ is its centre. A complex representation is a homomorphism $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$, where $V$ is a nonzero finite-dimensional complex vector space. It is irreducible if its only $G$-invariant complex subspaces are $0$ and $V$.

To make reduction modulo a prime precise, adopt the following convention. Choose a number field $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ and a $K$-representation $V_K$ with ${\mathbb{C}}\otimes_K V_K\cong V$. Write $\mathcal O_K$ for the ring of integers of $K$. For each rational prime $q$, each prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset\mathcal O_K$ above $q$, and each $G$-invariant full lattice $L\subset V_K$ over the local ring $\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}$, form \[ \overline V_{\mathfrak p,L} =\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_q\otimes_{\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}} \left(L/\mathfrak p L\right), \] using an embedding of the residue field into $\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_q$. Here a full lattice is a free $\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}$-module of rank $\dim_K V_K$ whose $K$-span is $V_K$. Interpret "remains irreducible after reduction modulo any prime" as requiring $\overline V_{\mathfrak p,L}$ to be irreducible for every such choice of realization, prime, and invariant lattice. In particular, primes dividing $|G|$ are included. The source does not explicitly state the field or lattice convention; the interpretation above requires review.

Problem (catalogue entry; permanent ID 700727). Classify, up to isomorphism of groups and equivalence of representations, all pairs $(G,\rho)$ in which $G$ is a finite quasisimple group and $\rho$ is an irreducible complex representation that remains irreducible modulo every rational prime in the sense just specified. No faithfulness or nontriviality hypothesis is supplied.

The source singles out representations of degree \[ \frac{p^k-1}{2} \quad\text{of}\quad \operatorname{Sp}(2k,p), \] where $p$ is an odd prime and $k\in{\mathbb{N}}$, as an important example. Here $\operatorname{Sp}(2k,p)$ denotes the group preserving a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form on ${\mathbb{F}}_p^{2k}$. The source does not identify the representations within this degree or state parameter exceptions. Its convention for ${\mathbb{N}}$ is also unspecified; $k=0$ would give degree zero and hence no representation under the nonzero convention above. Any example within the requested classification must additionally satisfy the quasisimplicity hypothesis.

Short English statement

Classify the irreducible complex representations of finite quasisimple groups whose reductions remain irreducible in every prime characteristic. The draft makes the reduction convention explicit and retains the source's symplectic example with its unresolved parameter and representation qualifications. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this status has not been independently verified.
