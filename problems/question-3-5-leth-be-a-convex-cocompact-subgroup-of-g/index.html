Question 3.5 — LetH be a convex cocompact subgroup of Γg.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Gamma_g$ denote the source's mapping class group and let $H\leq\Gamma_g$ be convex cocompact. In the usual Teichmüller convention this means that an orbit of the finitely generated subgroup is quasiconvex for the Teichmüller metric. The exact surface, genus range, and convex-cocompact convention are not supplied.

A subgroup $H$ is separable in $\Gamma_g$ if for every $x\in\Gamma_g\setminus H$ there exist a finite group $F$ and homomorphism $q:\Gamma_g\to F$ with $q(x)\notin q(H)$. Equivalently, every such $x$ is excluded by some finite-index subgroup containing $H$, or $H$ is closed in the profinite topology.

Question 3.5. Is $\Gamma_g$ $H$-separable for every convex-cocompact subgroup $H$? Explicitly, \[ \forall x\notin H\ \exists F\text{ finite}\ \exists q:\Gamma_g\to F, \qquad q(x)\notin q(H). \] The finite quotient may depend on $x$ and $H$; a single quotient distinguishing all outside elements is not required. Nor is this a claim that all finitely generated subgroups are separable. The source then proposes focusing on surface subgroups, but the following question is not included. No assertion about arbitrary, possibly non-convex-cocompact surface subgroups is added.

Short English statement

Is every convex-cocompact mapping-class subgroup separable, so each element outside it can be distinguished in some finite quotient? The surface-subgroup specialization introduced next is omitted and is not silently broadened to all surface subgroups.
