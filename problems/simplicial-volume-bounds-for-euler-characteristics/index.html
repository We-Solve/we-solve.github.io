Math-1147

Simplicial Volume Bounds for Euler Characteristics

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks its questions for "every aspherical manifold," but does not specify compactness, boundary conditions, orientability, or connectedness. These omissions affect the definitions of simplicial volume and Euler characteristic. The following is a precise conditional formulation using closed, connected, oriented manifolds; these hypotheses are an explicit convention for this draft and are not confirmed by the supplied excerpt.

A connected manifold $M$ is aspherical if its universal covering space $\widetilde M$ is contractible. For a closed, connected, oriented $n$-manifold, let $[M]_{{\mathbb{R}}}\in H_n(M;{\mathbb{R}})$ be its real fundamental class. Define its simplicial volume by \[ \lVert M\rVert=\inf\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{k}|a_j|:\ z=\sum_{j=1}^{k}a_j\sigma_j\in C_n(M;{\mathbb{R}}),\ \partial z=0,\ [z]=[M]_{{\mathbb{R}}}\right\}, \] where the $\sigma_j$ are singular $n$-simplices and the chains are finite. Define \[ \chi(M)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}(-1)^i\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}H_i(M;{\mathbb{R}}). \] Let $\mathcal A_n$ denote the class of closed, connected, oriented, aspherical $n$-manifolds. Under this convention, the two supplied questions become:

  1. Is it true that, for every manifold dimension $n$ and every $M\in\mathcal A_n$, \[ \lVert M\rVert=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\chi(M)=0? \]
  2. More strongly, for every manifold dimension $n$, does there exist a finite constant $C(n)\in[0,\infty)$, independent of $M$, such that \[ \forall M\in\mathcal A_n,\qquad |\chi(M)|\leq C(n)\lVert M\rVert? \]
The original wording has the broader quantifier "every aspherical manifold." Its intended scope remains unresolved: the supplied record does not determine whether manifolds with boundary, noncompact manifolds, nonorientable manifolds, or disconnected manifolds are included, nor which versions of the invariants would then be intended. Consequently, the conditional formulation above must not be treated as a verified reconstruction of all source hypotheses.

Short English statement

The questions ask whether an aspherical manifold with zero simplicial volume must have zero Euler characteristic, and whether the absolute Euler characteristic admits a bound by simplicial volume with a constant depending only on dimension. The displayed formulation uses an explicitly provisional closed, connected, oriented setting because the supplied excerpt leaves the manifold class unspecified. The supplied curation labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
