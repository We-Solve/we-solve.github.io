p-Adic Period Maps and Hodge-Theoretic Variations

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a prime number $p$, write ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$ for the completion of ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with respect to the absolute value normalized by $|p|_p=p^{-1}$. The source does not specify a particular prime or a field over ${\mathbb{Q}}_p$.

To explain the classical terminology, a pure rational variation of Hodge structure of weight $w\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ on a complex manifold $S$ consists of a finite-rank rational local system $\mathbb V$ and a decreasing holomorphic filtration $F^\bullet$ by subbundles of $\mathcal V=\mathbb V\otimes_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\mathcal O_S$. At each $s\in S$, this filtration determines a decomposition \[ \mathbb V_s\otimes_{{\mathbb{Q}}}{\mathbb{C}}=\bigoplus_{a+b=w}H_s^{a,b},\qquad \overline{H_s^{a,b}}=H_s^{b,a},\qquad F_s^r=\bigoplus_{a\ge r}H_s^{a,w-a}. \] The flat connection $\nabla$ induced by $\mathbb V$ satisfies Griffiths transversality: \[ \nabla(F^r)\subseteq F^{r-1}\otimes_{\mathcal O_S}\Omega_S^1. \] After a local flat identification of the fibers, its period map sends $s$ to the filtration $F_s^\bullet$ in the corresponding space of Hodge filtrations. These definitions provide one classical reference setting; the source does not restrict its question to pure rational variations or specify polarization or additional structures.

Source question. What is the $p$-adic analogue of the classical theory of period maps and variations of Hodge structure?

This is a request for a theory, rather than a specified theorem or conjecture. The record does not prescribe a category of base spaces or families, coefficient fields, a notion of $p$-adic variation, a period target, or the classical properties that an analogue must reproduce. Short English statement

The problem asks for a $p$-adic counterpart of classical varying Hodge structures and their period maps. Its intended scope and success criteria are unspecified, so the supplied question remains a research program requiring clarification and human mathematical review.
