BNSR Invariants of Algebraic Bieri–Strebel Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finitely generated group $G$, a real character is a homomorphism $\chi:G\to({\mathbb{R}},+)$. Its character sphere is \[ S(G)=\left(\operatorname{Hom}(G,{\mathbb{R}})\setminus\{0\}\right)/{\sim}, \qquad \chi\sim\chi'\iff \chi'=a\chi\text{ for some }a>0. \] Write $[\chi]$ for the resulting positive ray.

One standard interpretation of the Bieri--Neumann--Strebel--Renz invariants is the homotopical family $\Sigma^n(G)\subseteq S(G)$. Here $n\geq1$, and $G$ must have type $F_n$: there must exist an Eilenberg--Mac Lane space $K(G,1)$ with finite $n$-skeleton. Choose such a space, let $X$ be the $n$-skeleton of its universal cover, and choose a continuous height function $h_\chi:X\to{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfying \[ h_\chi(gx)=h_\chi(x)+\chi(g). \] Put $X_{\chi,t}=h_\chi^{-1}([t,\infty))$. The standard controlled-connectivity definition is \[ [\chi]\in\Sigma^n(G) \iff \begin{gathered} \text{for every }t\in{\mathbb{R}}\text{ there exists }s\leq t\text{ such that, for every }0\leq j\leq n-1,\\ \text{every continuous }f:S^j\to X_{\chi,t}\text{ extends to a continuous }\\ \bar f:B^{j+1}\to X_{\chi,s}. \end{gathered} \] Here $S^j=\partial B^{j+1}$, and an extension agrees with $f$ on that boundary. The threshold $s$ must work for every map in the indicated dimensions. Homological invariants $\Sigma^n(G;R)$, with a specified coefficient ring $R$ and corresponding finiteness hypotheses, are another convention; the supplied record does not select between these conventions.

Source problem. Determine the Bieri--Neumann--Strebel--Renz invariant of every algebraic Bieri--Strebel group, extending the computation for the group denoted $F_\tau$ in the source. Under the homotopical, all-degree interpretation, this would mean giving an explicit description of \[ \Sigma^n(G)\subseteq S(G) \qquad\text{for every intended algebraic Bieri--Strebel group }G \text{ and every }n\geq1\text{ for which }G\text{ has type }F_n. \] This displayed interpretation is conditional: the supplied record does not define the class of algebraic Bieri--Strebel groups, its admissible parameters, or any interval, slope, breakpoint, or endpoint conditions. It also does not specify the degrees, a homotopical or homological convention, or homological coefficients. Consequently, the supplied evidence does not determine a fully specified quantified problem beyond the source target stated above. The notation $F_\tau$ is retained without assigning an unsupported definition to the group or a value to $\tau$.

The supplied account credits Molyneux, Nucinkis and Santos Rego with computing the invariant of $F_\tau$ using a method of Bieri, Geoghegan and Kochloukova. Its dated assessment also reports computations for further subclasses. These are supplied background claims, not computations or literature conclusions established by this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a general description of the BNSR invariants of algebraic Bieri--Strebel groups, extending the reported golden-mean example $F_\tau$. The source omits the definition of the group class and the precise invariant convention, so the homotopical formulation above is conditional. The supplied open status and reported special-case computations require independent review.
