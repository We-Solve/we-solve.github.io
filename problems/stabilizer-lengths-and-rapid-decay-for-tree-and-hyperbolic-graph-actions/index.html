Math-1440

Stabilizer lengths and rapid decay for tree and hyperbolic-graph actions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $\Gamma$ with length $L$, rapid decay (RD) means that some $C,s\ge0$ satisfy \[ \|\lambda(f)\|_{\mathcal B(\ell^2\Gamma)}\le C\left(\sum_{g\in\Gamma}|f(g)|^2(1+L(g))^{2s}\right)^{1/2} \] for every finitely supported $f$, where $\lambda(f)$ is left convolution. Length is symmetric, subadditive and zero at the identity; word length is the usual finite-generation case. The source field merges four questions from Section 5, RD for groups acting on special metric spaces.

Question 1 concerns a building of type $\widetilde A_2$, for example the Bruhat--Tits building of $\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{Q}}_p)$, and asks whether the square-root factor in the RRS shape-$(m,n)$ convolution estimate is necessary. The extraction preserves the polynomial factor $\tfrac12(m+1)(n+1)(m+n+2)$ and a square-root symbol but not its argument or full estimate. The corrupted $f?g$ is read as convolution $f*g$; the source specifies $g$ supported on elements of shape $(m,n)$. The acting-group, shape and missing-factor details require [RRS]; the full numerical bound is not guessed. The aid reports no published resolution of the sharp square-root question in its checked literature.

Question 2 asks whether D. Wise's non-Hopfian group has RD. The extracted presentation is $\langle a,b,s,t; a^s=(ab)^2,\ b^t=(ab)^2\rangle$, with superscripts interpreted as conjugation, but the record does not provide a fully verified presentation of the intended Wise example. The aid reports a positive answer by Barre--Pichot in 2015 for the intended named group. The original discussion calls it CAT(0), says it has "no distorted subgroup," says it does not act on "any cube complex," and says it is not relatively hyperbolic. The first unqualified implication is too broad: CAT(0) does not rule out all distorted finitely generated subgroups, though cyclic subgroups in a geometric CAT(0) setting are undistorted. The literal cube-action claim also cannot exclude trivial actions; an appropriate proper/geometric or other nontrivial-action condition is missing. These source qualifications are retained as formulation issues, not silently endorsed or repaired to an unverified theorem.

Question 3 asks for stabilizer conditions implying RD for an action on a simplicial tree, with a tentative possibly-unpublished Steger--Talbi assertion that RD is equivalent to RD of stabilizers for the induced length, and mentions HNN extensions. The aid proves the necessary direction: RD passes to every subgroup, hence every vertex and edge stabilizer, with length restricted from $\Gamma$. Intrinsic stabilizer word length is not interchangeable with that restriction.

The quantitative counterexample to that interchange is \[ \operatorname{BS}(1,q)=\langle a,t:tat^{-1}=a^q\rangle,\qquad q\ge2. \] For ambient word length $L$, a base-$q$ Horner construction gives \[ \left|\{a^k:L(a^k)\le(q+1)n\}\right|\ge q^n\quad(n\ge1). \] Thus the cyclic vertex subgroup has exponential ball growth for induced length, although its intrinsic word metric has linear growth and RD. Its Bass--Serre action has cyclic vertex and edge stabilizers, but the induced-length cyclic subgroup and the whole Baumslag--Solitar group fail RD. The amenable-subgroup obstruction to induced RD is therefore quantitatively visible. This refutes a converse phrased only with intrinsic stabilizer RD, not the source's correctly qualified induced-length possibility.

Question 4 asks whether trees can be replaced by bounded-degree $\delta$-hyperbolic graphs. The aid gives a positive restricted case: a cocompact action on a connected locally finite hyperbolic graph with finite vertex stabilizers yields RD for ambient word length. This is a proper cocompact setting, not an assertion for arbitrary actions on arbitrary bounded-degree hyperbolic graphs. Infinite stabilizers require further hypotheses, as the tree example already demonstrates.

The trailing "46 RD and geometry" is a merged page/next-section artifact. The verification reports PDF/RRS comparison but does not supply the complete recovered formulas in this record. Short English statement

The record combines four RD questions. The aid distinguishes intrinsic stabilizer lengths from ambiently induced lengths, gives an explicit Baumslag--Solitar obstruction to confusing them, and proves a proper cocompact hyperbolic-graph case. It reports Wise's RD question solved in 2015 but leaves the RRS sharp-factor problem unresolved, with damaged formulas and overbroad source claims flagged.
