Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A free-by-cyclic group here is a semidirect product $G=F\rtimes_\varphi\mathbb Z$, with $F$ a free group and $\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(F)$. A CAT(0) space is a geodesic metric space in which distances between points on any geodesic triangle are at most the corresponding distances in its Euclidean comparison triangle. A CAT(0) group acts by isometries on such a space properly discontinuously and cocompactly: compact subsets meet only finitely many of their translates, and the quotient is compact.

For a graph $\Gamma$, its right-angled Artin group is $A_\Gamma=\langle v\in V\Gamma\mid uv=vu\text{ for }\{u,v\}\in E\Gamma\rangle$. Its Salvetti cube complex has one vertex, one oriented loop for each generator, and the corresponding cubical torus for every finite clique, glued along common coordinate subtori.

A cube complex is built by isometrically gluing faces of unit Euclidean cubes. It is special in the Haglund--Wise sense if it admits a cubical local isometry to a Salvetti complex. A cubical local isometry maps the link of every vertex injectively onto a full subcomplex of the target link; full means containing every target simplex all of whose vertices it contains. A group is virtually special if a finite-index subgroup is the fundamental group of a special cube complex.

(a) Is every CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group virtually special? (b) Does every such group have a finite-index subgroup admitting an injective homomorphism into a right-angled Artin group?

Each question permits the subgroup, cube complex, or target graph to depend on $G$. Neither part prescribes a particular action or an embedding compatible with the semidirect-product coordinates.

Short English statement

Must a CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group be virtually special, or at least virtually embed into a right-angled Artin group?

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Every hyperbolic one-relator group is claimed virtually compact special and, by the cited theorem, virtually free-by-cyclic with possibly infinitely generated free kernel.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For a finite set $X$ and relator $r\in F(X)$, suppose \[ G=F(X)/\langle\!\langle r\rangle\!\rangle \] is word-hyperbolic. The claim supplies a finite-index subgroup $H\le G$, a finite nonpositively curved cube complex $C$ with $\pi_1C\cong H$, and a cubical local isometry from $C$ to a right-angled Artin Salvetti complex. This is virtual compact specialness. The second theorem applies to any injective endomorphism $\phi$ of a finitely generated free group $F$: \[ F*_{\phi}=\langle F,t\mid t^{-1}ft=\phi(f),\ f\in F\rangle \text{ word-hyperbolic}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad F*_{\phi}\text{ virtually compact special}. \] Word-hyperbolicity is assumed in both assertions.

Outline of the argument

Primitive-extension reduction and a filling criterion organize progressive characters and walls. The resulting cubulation and hierarchy meet the virtual-specialness criterion; the free-by-cyclic consequence is then imported from the established special-group result.
