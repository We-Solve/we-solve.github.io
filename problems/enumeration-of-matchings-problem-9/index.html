Enumeration of Matchings — Problem 9

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A domino is the union of two unit lattice squares sharing an edge. A domino tiling of a finite region of unit squares is a partition of its squares into dominoes. The source discusses Aztec rectangles whose dimensions differ by one, with the larger dimension odd, and deletion of the central square to restore equality between the numbers of black and white squares in a checkerboard coloring. This equality is a necessary condition for tileability.

The source does not explicitly define its Aztec-rectangle convention. A concrete coordinate interpretation consistent with its supplied illustration is the following. For a positive integer $n$, let \[ S_n=\left\{(i,j)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2: |i+j|\leq 2n,\quad |i-j|\leq 2n+1\right\}, \] and associate to $(i,j)$ the unit square \[ Q_{i,j}=[i-\tfrac12,i+\tfrac12]\times[j-\tfrac12,j+\tfrac12]. \] The central square is $Q_{0,0}$. Define the region after deletion by \[ R_n=\bigcup_{(i,j)\in S_n\setminus\{(0,0)\}}Q_{i,j}. \] Equivalently, let $V_n=S_n\setminus\{(0,0)\}$, and let \[ E_n=\left\{\{(i,j),(k,\ell)\}\subseteq V_n: |i-k|+|j-\ell|=1\right\}. \] A perfect matching is a subset $M\subseteq E_n$ in which every vertex of $V_n$ belongs to exactly one edge. Such matchings correspond to domino tilings of $R_n$. Write \[ T(n)=\#\left\{M\subseteq E_n:M\text{ is a perfect matching of }(V_n,E_n)\right\}. \] Enumeration problem (Propp, 1998, Problem 9). Find a formula, valid for every positive integer $n$, for the number of domino tilings of the $2n$-by-$(2n+1)$ Aztec rectangle with its central square removed; under the coordinate interpretation above, this is $T(n)$.

Unresolved qualifications. The coordinate realization is an explicit interpretation of the source terminology and illustration, rather than a definition quoted from the supplied record. Its identification with the intended Aztec rectangle requires review. The source also leaves the range of $n$ unstated; positive integers are used here as the intended nondegenerate range.

Short English statement

Propp asks for a formula counting domino tilings of a $2n$-by-$(2n+1)$ Aztec rectangle after deleting its central square.
