Pathwise Connected Choice in Dimension Two

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the underlying represented space or the information supplied about closed sets. The following is a conditional formulation using the usual compact-square interpretation; these representation choices require confirmation against the source.

Let $X=[0,1]^2$, with its Euclidean metric and the usual Cauchy representation of points. Represent a closed subset $A\subseteq X$ by negative information: an enumeration of rational open balls, intersected with $X$, whose union is $X\setminus A$. Define \[ \mathcal P_2=\{A\subseteq X:A\text{ is closed, nonempty, and pathwise connected}\}. \] Here pathwise connected means that for every $x,y\in A$ there exists a continuous map $\gamma:[0,1]\to A$ with $\gamma(0)=x$ and $\gamma(1)=y$. Pathwise connectedness is a promise on the input set; no paths or witnesses are supplied. Define the multivalued pathwise connected choice operation by \[ \operatorname{PCC}_2:\mathcal P_2\rightrightarrows X, \qquad \operatorname{PCC}_2(A)=A. \]

Let $2^{<{\mathbb{N}}}$ denote the finite binary strings and $2^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ the infinite binary sequences. A binary tree is a prefix-closed subset $T\subseteq 2^{<{\mathbb{N}}}$. Represent such trees by their characteristic functions, and restrict to trees having a node of every finite length. The multivalued operation associated with Weak König's Lemma is \[ \operatorname{WKL}(T)=[T] =\{p\in 2^{{\mathbb{N}}}:\forall n\in{\mathbb{N}},\ p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}n\in T\}, \] with the usual representation of binary sequences.

For multivalued operations $f,g$ on represented spaces, write $f\leq_{\mathrm{sW}}g$ if there exist computable partial transformations $K,H$ of names such that, for every realizer $G$ of $g$, the composition $H\circ G\circ K$ realizes $f$. A realizer maps each valid input name to a name of an allowed output. In particular, $K$ must produce a valid instance of $g$, and $H$ receives only the output name supplied by $G$. Write $f\equiv_{\mathrm{sW}}g$ when both strong reductions hold.

Under the conventions above, the question is whether \[ \operatorname{PCC}_2\equiv_{\mathrm{sW}}\operatorname{WKL}. \] The record compares this question with the supplied assertion that connected choice is strongly Weihrauch equivalent to $\operatorname{WKL}$ in every dimension at least $2$. Under analogous cube and negative-information conventions, connected choice selects a point from a nonempty closed connected subset of $[0,1]^n$. The supplied literature assessment further reports that the corresponding geometric reduction for pathwise connected choice is known in dimensions at least $3$, while dimension $2$ remains open. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified results in this draft.

Short English statement

Given negative information describing a nonempty closed pathwise connected subset of the unit square, select a point in that set. Is this operation strongly Weihrauch equivalent to finding an infinite branch through an infinite binary tree? The square and representation conventions require source confirmation; the supplied record labels the question open.
