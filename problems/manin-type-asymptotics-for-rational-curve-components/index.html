Manin-Type Asymptotics for Rational-Curve Components

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a smooth projective Fano variety, so that its anticanonical divisor $-K_X$ is ample. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, the range of $q$, or the meaning of a breaking map. The following therefore records the proposed asymptotic subject to unresolved specifications.

Write $\operatorname{Mor}(\mathbb P^1,X)$ for the scheme parametrizing morphisms from the projective line to $X$, over the unspecified ground field. For an irreducible component $W$, the usual interpretation of dominance is that the evaluation morphism \[ \operatorname{ev}_W:\mathbb P^1\times W\longrightarrow X, \qquad (t,f)\longmapsto f(t), \] is dominant. A morphism $f$ has anticanonical degree $d$ if \[ \deg f^*\mathcal O_X(-K_X)=d. \] The record does not specify whether components and dominance are to be interpreted over the ground field or after extending it to an algebraic closure.

Let $\mathcal W_d^{\circ}(X)$ denote the components of anticanonical degree $d$ that are dominant and whose family maps do not rationally factor through breaking maps, as required by the source. This last condition remains undefined in the supplied evidence. If the family map means $\operatorname{ev}_W$, a rational factorization through a map $b:Y\to X$ would mean a rational map $h:\mathbb P^1\times W\dashrightarrow Y$ satisfying $\operatorname{ev}_W=b\circ h$ as rational maps. The record does not identify the maps $b$ designated as breaking maps or specify which rational factorizations are to be excluded; this description alone does not define the filter.

Subject to these specifications, set \[ N^{\circ}(X,q,d)=\sum_{W\in\mathcal W_d^{\circ}(X)}q^{\dim W}. \] The question is whether, for every smooth projective Fano variety $X$ under the intended hypotheses and for $q$ in its intended range, there exists a constant $C>0$, independent of $d$, such that \[ N^{\circ}(X,q,d)\sim Cq^d d^{\rho(X)-1}\qquad(d\to\infty), \] that is, \[ \lim_{d\to\infty}\frac{N^{\circ}(X,q,d)}{Cq^d d^{\rho(X)-1}}=1. \] Here $\rho(X)$ conventionally denotes the Picard number, the rank of the Néron--Severi group; the record does not specify whether the arithmetic or geometric Picard number is intended. It also does not specify whether the limit runs through all positive integer degrees or an admissible subset, whether $q$ is fixed, or the permitted dependence of $C$ on other parameters. These qualifications must be resolved before this becomes a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

The proposed asymptotic counts dominant families of parametrized rational curves on a smooth projective Fano variety, weighting each component by $q$ to its dimension and excluding families that factor through breaking maps. It predicts growth $Cq^d d^{\rho(X)-1}$. The supplied record leaves the exclusion rule, ground field, parameter range, and degree convention unresolved.
