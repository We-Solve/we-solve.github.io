Admissibility and Prediction for Multidimensional Hyperbolic Balance Laws

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses a research programme rather than a fully specified mathematical conjecture. Its principal request is to find suitable admissibility criteria for physically reasonable solutions of multidimensional hyperbolic balance laws, especially the Euler system.

For notation only, a chosen deterministic balance law in conservation form may be written as \[ \partial_t u+\sum_{j=1}^{d}\partial_{x_j}F_j(u)=S(t,x,u),\qquad (t,x)\in I\times\Omega, \] where $d\geq2$, $\Omega\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$ is open, $I\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$ is an open time interval, $u:I\times\Omega\to U\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^m$ is the state, $F_j:U\to{\mathbb{R}}^m$ are fluxes, and $S:I\times\Omega\times U\to{\mathbb{R}}^m$ is a source term. These objects are placeholders, not model specifications supplied by the record. When $u$, $F_j(u)$, and $S(t,x,u)$ are locally integrable, the distributional balance law means \[ \int_{I\times\Omega}\left[u\cdot\partial_t\varphi+\sum_{j=1}^{d}F_j(u)\cdot\partial_{x_j}\varphi+S(t,x,u)\cdot\varphi\right]\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x=0 \] for every $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(I\times\Omega;{\mathbb{R}}^m)$. This interior formulation specifies neither initial nor boundary conditions. It also does not prescribe a stochastic interpretation for random systems.

An admissibility criterion would be an additional condition selecting physically reasonable solutions from the solutions of a specified balance law. The supplied research tasks are to identify suitable such conditions, especially for the Euler system; determine their implications for multiphase-flow models, random systems, and structure-preserving numerical schemes; and assess whether hyperbolic balance laws can predict pandemic evolution.

Unresolved formulation. The record specifies no particular fluxes, source terms, state space, hyperbolicity assumptions, Euler variant or constitutive closure, initial or boundary data, solution class, or definition of physical reasonableness. It does not identify the multiphase or random models, numerical structures to preserve, or a pandemic model, prediction target, time horizon, data, or accuracy criterion. Consequently, it does not determine a single quantified existence, uniqueness, stability, convergence, or prediction assertion. In particular, the request must not be strengthened to demand one universal admissibility criterion for all the mentioned models. The displayed equation is explanatory notation only; the unresolved choices require human mathematical review.

Short English statement

Find admissibility conditions selecting physically reasonable solutions of multidimensional hyperbolic balance laws, particularly Euler systems. Study their consequences for multiphase flow, random systems, and structure-preserving numerical methods, and assess their potential for predicting pandemic evolution. The supplied record leaves the models and success criteria unspecified, so this remains a research programme requiring further formulation.
