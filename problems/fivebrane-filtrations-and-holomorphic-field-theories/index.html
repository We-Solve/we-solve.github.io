Testing a naive rank filtration.
A tempting algebraic proxy for the proposed levels is the standard block inclusion $\mathfrak{sl}(N)\subset\mathfrak{sl}(N+1)$, $N>1$. It is a Lie subalgebra, but not an ideal. Let $E_{ij}$ denote the elementary matrix. Then $E_{21}$ belongs to the upper-left $N$-block, while \[ [E_{21},E_{1,N+1}]=E_{2,N+1} \] does not. Consequently the vector-space quotient $\mathfrak{sl}(N+1)/\mathfrak{sl}(N)$ has no bracket induced by arbitrary representatives. Its dimension $((N+1)^2-1)-(N^2-1)=2N+1$ is well defined but cannot by itself be interpreted as a quotient Lie algebra or field theory.
Why this matters for the attempted construction.
A nested family of spaces with plausible ranks does not establish the bracket-compatible filtration of $E(5|10)$. If a level is to be a quotient theory, the relevant ideals, differentials and higher operations have to descend; if it is a subtheory, closure and inclusion must instead be proved. Those interpretations cannot be silently interchanged.
