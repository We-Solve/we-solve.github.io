Multiple ergodic averages — Problem 25

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Hardy field is a field of germs at $+\infty$ of real-valued eventually differentiable functions that is closed under differentiation. Let $\ell\ge1$ and let $a_1,\ldots,a_\ell$ belong to one Hardy field, with polynomial growth: for each $i$, $|a_i(t)|\le C_i t^{k_i}$ for all sufficiently large $t$, for some $C_i>0$ and $k_i\ge0$. On the nonzero-function real-span interpretation, set \[ \operatorname{span}^*(\mathcal F)= \left\{\sum_{i=1}^\ell\lambda_i a_i:\lambda_i\in{\mathbb{R}}\right\}\setminus\{0\}, \qquad\mathcal F=\{a_1,\ldots,a_\ell\}. \] Assume the exact separation condition \[ \forall a\in\operatorname{span}^*(\mathcal F)\ \forall c\in{\mathbb{R}}\ \forall p\in{\mathbb{Z}}[t], \qquad \lim_{t\to\infty}|a(t)-cp(t)|=+\infty. \] This concerns every indicated linear combination and every real multiple of an integer polynomial, not merely each individual function or a failure of polynomial equality.

Writing $[s]=\lfloor s\rfloor$, the usual existence-of-recurrence target is: for every invertible probability-preserving system $(X,\mathcal B,\mu,T)$ and every $A\in\mathcal B$ with $\mu(A)>0$, there is a positive integer $n$, in the common domain of the representatives, such that \[ \mu\left(A\cap T^{-\lfloor a_1(n)\rfloor}A\cap\cdots \cap T^{-\lfloor a_\ell(n)\rfloor}A\right)>0. \] All powers belong to the same transformation. Invertibility accommodates negative integer parts. The source calls this being good for $\ell$-recurrence, but does not supply its full convention; whether arbitrarily large $n$, positive averaged recurrence, or further nontriviality conditions are included must be checked. Those stronger conclusions are not silently added. The coefficient domain and exclusion of zero in $\operatorname{span}^*$, and the integer-part convention, likewise require the source definitions.

The supplied research summary attributes an affirmative result to Bergelson--Moreira--Richter (2020). Its abbreviated phrase non-polynomial-growth hypothesis does not replace the displayed separation condition, since the functions here explicitly have polynomial growth.

Short English statement

Integer parts of Hardy-field functions should give simultaneous recurrence for one transformation when every nonzero linear combination diverges away from every real multiple of an integer polynomial. The supplied registry reports a literature solution, with exact recurrence conventions still to check.
