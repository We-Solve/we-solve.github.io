Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.63

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix an odd prime $p$ and a positive integer $s$. Write ${\mathbb{Z}}_p=\varprojlim_n{\mathbb{Z}}/p^n{\mathbb{Z}}$ for the ring of $p$-adic integers. For a finite $p$-group $P$, define its coclass by \[ \operatorname{cc}(P)=\log_p|P|-\operatorname{cl}(P), \] where $\operatorname{cl}(P)$ is its nilpotency class, with the trivial group assigned class zero.

The supplied record does not define a $p$-adic space group. For a conditional precise formulation, use the interpretation of a pro-$p$ group $G$ admitting an exact sequence of topological groups \[ 1\longrightarrow T\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow P\longrightarrow 1, \] where $T\cong({\mathbb{Z}}_p^d,+)$ for some finite integer $d\geq1$, $P$ is a finite $p$-group, and the induced conjugation action $P\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathbb{Z}}_p}(T)$ is faithful. Here $T$ is the translation subgroup and $P$ is the point group. No splitting of the extension is assumed. This interpretation requires confirmation against the cited source.

For a pro-$p$ group $G$, put $\gamma_1(G)=G$ and $\gamma_{n+1}(G)=\overline{[\gamma_n(G),G]}$. Use the convention that $G$ has finite coclass if, for all sufficiently large $n$, the quotient $G/\gamma_n(G)$ is finite and the integers $\operatorname{cc}(G/\gamma_n(G))$ are eventually constant at a finite value.

With these conventions, the proposed assertion is \[ \forall\,p\text{ odd prime}\quad\forall\,s\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1},\qquad \#\left\{[G]_{\mathrm{top}}:\begin{array}{l} G\text{ is a }p\text{-adic space group of finite coclass},\\ \operatorname{cc}(P)=s\text{ for its point group }P \end{array}\right\}<\infty, \] where $[G]_{\mathrm{top}}$ denotes the continuous group isomorphism class of $G$. The source does not explicitly specify this equivalence relation; it must also be confirmed. The finite coclass of $G$ is allowed to vary: the assertion imposes no fixed value or additional bound on it.

Short English statement

For every fixed odd prime $p$ and positive integer $s$, prove that only finitely many $p$-adic space groups of finite coclass have point group of coclass $s$, interpreted here up to continuous group isomorphism. The precise space-group convention and counting equivalence require source confirmation.
