Short English statement

The question asks whether every Diophantine pseudo-rotation admits a change of coordinates on its entire phase space that turns it into a rotation. The phase space, meaning of pseudo-rotation, Diophantine condition, and regularity of the conjugacy are unspecified, so the displayed statement is a conditional template requiring human mathematical review.
