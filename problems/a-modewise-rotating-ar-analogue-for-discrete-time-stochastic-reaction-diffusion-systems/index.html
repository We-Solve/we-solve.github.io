Math-5246

A modewise rotating-AR analogue for discrete-time stochastic reaction-diffusion systems

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Suppose the deterministic system $\dot x=f(x,y)$, $\dot y=g(x,y)$ has a stable fixed point, translated to $0$, and its real Jacobian $A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2\times2}$ has eigenvalues $-\lambda\pm i\omega$ with $\lambda>0$ and $\omega\ne0$. The source's noisy linearization is \[ dU_t=AU_t\,dt+\begin{pmatrix}\sigma_1&0\\0&\sigma_2\end{pmatrix}dW_t, \qquad U_t=\begin{pmatrix}x_t\\y_t\end{pmatrix}, \] with the usual interpretation of $W$ as a pair of independent standard Brownian motions. It states that this process can be approximated by a constant times a rotation times a pair of independent standard Ornstein--Uhlenbeck processes, and asks for an analogue for a discrete-time reaction--diffusion equation. The original gives no approximation parameter, error notion, spatial domain, coupling, discretization rule, boundary conditions, or noise covariance. Accordingly its continuous approximation is retained as a motivating assertion, not promoted to an exact theorem from complex eigenvalues alone.

The supplied machine aid addresses a specified periodic two-species coupled-map lattice with spatially white Gaussian innovations. It reports reduction, using nonredundant spatial Fourier modes (or a real mode basis), to independent two-coordinate autoregressions. A mode can be written \[ U_{j+1}=MU_j+Q^{1/2}\xi_{j+1},\qquad \xi_j\text{ independent }N(0,I_2),\quad Q>0. \] For a stable focus, $M$ has nonreal conjugate eigenvalues of modulus $\rho\in(0,1)$ and $\rho^2=\det M$. The aid's exact noise-adapted criterion is \[ MQM^T=\rho^2Q. \] Equivalently $C=Q^{-1/2}MQ^{1/2}=\rho R$ with $R\in\operatorname{SO}(2)$. Under stationary initialization, the co-rotating, rescaled coordinates \[ V_j=\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,R^{-j}Q^{-1/2}U_j \] then form a pair of independent standard stationary scalar AR(1) processes, namely coordinates with variance one and autoregressive multiplier $\rho$. Here independence concerns their full Gaussian processes. Without stationary initialization the corresponding recursion still needs an initial-law qualification before being called standard stationary. Complex eigenvalues alone do not imply the criterion.

For the approximate version, the aid defines the operator-norm defect \[ \delta=\|\rho^{-2}CC^T-I_2\|_2<1 \] and reports a proper orthogonal polar factor $R$ for which \[ \|C-\rho R\|_2\leq \frac{\rho\delta}{1+\sqrt{1-\delta}}. \] This is a one-step matrix approximation bound, not by itself a uniform-in-time pathwise or distributional error estimate. The summary also reports a discrete Lyapunov stationary covariance and temporal spectrum for every stable mode; their detailed formulas and the spatial lattice parameters are not supplied. These are a qualified modewise model of a discrete analogue, not a uniquely recovered discrete reaction--diffusion equation.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a discrete reaction--diffusion counterpart of a rotating Ornstein--Uhlenbeck approximation near a stable focus. The supplied partial model yields autoregressive spatial modes. Exact independent rotating AR coordinates require a compatibility condition between the mode matrix and noise covariance; complex eigenvalues alone are insufficient. The general spatial model and approximation regime remain unspecified.
