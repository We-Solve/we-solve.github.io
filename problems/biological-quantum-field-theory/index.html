A branching-process generating functional.
The supplied programme fixes no pathogen model. As a deliberately limited test, let a population count $N_t$ have transitions $n\to n+1$ at rate $\lambda n$ and $n\to n-1$ at rate $\mu n$, for constants $\lambda,\mu>0$. Start with one individual. Its probability generating function $G(z,t)=\mathbb E z^{N_t}$, $0\le z\le1$, satisfies \[\partial_tG=(z-1)(\lambda z-\mu)\partial_zG.\] Indeed the generator on $z^n$ is $\lambda n(z^{n+1}-z^n)+\mu n(z^{n-1}-z^n)$, which sums to the displayed expression. This is a concrete operator representation of the stochastic dynamics, though calling the auxiliary variable a field introduces no new biological law.
A control threshold and its derivation.
By conditioning on the first event and using independent descendant lineages, the extinction-by-time probability $q(t)=\mathbb P(N_t=0\mid N_0=1)$ solves \[q'=\lambda q^2-(\lambda+\mu)q+\mu=(1-q)(\mu-\lambda q),\quad q(0)=0.\] It increases and stays in $[0,1]$. Its limit must be a zero of the right-hand side: otherwise the derivative remains bounded below by a positive constant near the limit. The trajectory cannot cross a fixed point by uniqueness. Therefore $q(\infty)=1$ if $\mu\ge\lambda$, and $q(\infty)=\mu/\lambda$ if $\mu<\lambda$. From $k$ independent founders the extinction probability is its $k$th power. A constant intervention increasing removal to $\mu+c$, $c\ge0$, consequently yields certain eventual extinction exactly when $\mu+c\ge\lambda$.
What is missing.
This is an elementary branching model with neither genotype mutation nor spatial contacts, resource feedback or observed biological calibration. The critical case gives eventual extinction but not a finite uniform eradication time. The historical source mirror was unavailable in the supplied audit, and this derivation does not repair that source-access limitation. Connecting field-theoretic observables and controllable parameters to validated pathogen evolution is the first substantive modelling gap. No efficacy claim about actual interventions follows.
