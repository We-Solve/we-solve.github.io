Question 3.4 — If a subgroup G⊂Out(Fn) is not virtually abelian, then is H 2 b (G; R) infinite dimensional?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be a free group of rank $n$, and let $G\leq\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$. A group is virtually abelian if it contains an abelian subgroup of finite index. In the ordinary bounded-cohomology convention, $H_b^2(G;{\mathbb{R}})$ is the cohomology of bounded real group cochains with trivial coefficients, without silently replacing it by ordinary cohomology or a reduced quotient.

Question 3.4. If $G$ is not virtually abelian, must \[ \dim_{\mathbb{R}} H_b^2(G;{\mathbb{R}})=\infty? \] The source imposes no finite-generation, finite-index, or special geometric hypothesis on $G$, and specifies degree $2$ and real coefficients. The allowed free-group rank range is implicit.

The following context discusses embeddings as rank increases. It notes the evident embeddings $\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{Z}})\to\operatorname{GL}_m({\mathbb{Z}})$ and $\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\to\operatorname{Aut}(F_m)$ for $m\geq n$, while saying no comparable embedding of outer automorphism groups is obvious. It credits Bogopolski and Puga [10] with an embedding for a special rank relation, printed in the extraction as \[ m=1+(n-1)kn,\qquad \gcd(k,n-1)=1, \] with $k$ a natural number. The exponent placement is unresolved: the compressed $kn$ may mean the product $kn$ or $k^n$. The source also describes restriction to a suitable characteristic subgroup "of $F_m$," but does not explain how that restriction realizes the displayed direction. Both rank and characteristic-subgroup conventions need source verification; no corrected embedding theorem is silently asserted. This adjacent embedding discussion supplies no proof of the bounded-cohomology question.

Short English statement

Must every non-virtually-abelian subgroup of $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ have infinite-dimensional real bounded cohomology in degree two? The nearby rank-embedding claim is retained with unresolved exponent and subgroup-index notation.
