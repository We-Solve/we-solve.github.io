Kourovka Notebook Problem 3.3

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem does not specify the coefficient field or ring, its characteristic, whether the algebra has an identity, or whether automorphisms must fix the coefficients. Thus the following formulation is conditional on a standard interpretation, rather than a resolution of these omissions.

Fix a field $K$. For an integer $n\geq 2$, let \[ A_n(K)=K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle \] be the free unital associative $K$-algebra: its elements are finite $K$-linear combinations of words in the generators, including the empty word, and multiplication is induced by concatenation. Write $\operatorname{Aut}_{K}(A_n(K))$ for the group, under composition, of bijective unital $K$-algebra homomorphisms from $A_n(K)$ to itself.

Problem (L. A. Bokut', 1969; Kourovka Notebook 3.3). Under this interpretation, describe \[ \operatorname{Aut}_{K}\left(K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle\right) \qquad\text{for each integer }n\geq 2. \] The supplied source calls this a well-known problem. It does not specify the intended form of a description, nor whether the coefficient field is fixed or allowed to vary. These qualifications remain unresolved.

For interpreting the supplied background under the same convention, an affine automorphism sends \[ x_i\longmapsto \sum_{j=1}^{n}a_{ij}x_j+b_i, \qquad (a_{ij})\in\operatorname{GL}_n(K),\quad b_i\in K. \] An elementary automorphism fixes every generator other than some $x_i$ and sends \[ x_i\longmapsto a x_i+f, \qquad a\in K\setminus\{0\},\quad f\in K\langle x_1,\ldots,x_{i-1},x_{i+1},\ldots,x_n\rangle. \] The subgroup generated by affine and elementary automorphisms is called the tame subgroup; an automorphism outside it is called wild.

The supplied record reports that all automorphisms are tame when $n=2$, attributing this to A. J. Czerniakiewicz and L. G. Makar-Limanov. It also reports that the Anick automorphism is wild when $n=3$, attributing this to U. U. Umirbaev. The coefficient and characteristic hypotheses of these reports are not supplied, and no formula for the Anick automorphism is given. Finally, it reports an algorithm, attributed to A. V. Jagzhev, for recognizing which endomorphisms of free associative algebras of finite rank over constructive fields are automorphisms. The effective presentation meant by constructive fields is not specified. These are supplied background assertions, not independently verified results or additional requests to solve the problem.

Short English statement

Describe the automorphism group of a free associative algebra on $n\geq 2$ generators. A conditional formulation uses unital algebras over a fixed field $K$ and automorphisms fixing $K$; the source leaves these conventions and the intended form of the description unspecified. Its supplied background reports tameness in rank two, a wild Anick automorphism in rank three, and an automorphism-recognition algorithm over constructive fields.
