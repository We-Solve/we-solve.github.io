Valuation Bounds When the Center Meets the Negative Part

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a smooth Fano threefold and let $P\in S\subset X$. Here $P$ denotes a point, whereas $P(u)$ below denotes a divisor. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, the precise hypotheses on $S$, or the definition of $\tau$. For each real parameter $u\in[0,\tau]$, the record writes a Zariski decomposition \[ -K_X-uS=P(u)+N(u), \] with positive part $P(u)$ and effective negative part $N(u)$. The intended notion of Zariski decomposition on this threefold, and the hypotheses ensuring its existence throughout the interval, require clarification. Write $\operatorname{Supp}N(u)$ for the union of the prime divisors occurring with nonzero coefficient in $N(u)$, and write $(-K_X)^3$ for the anticanonical triple intersection number.

Reconstructed target in the supplied registry. Assuming \[ \exists u\in[0,\tau]\quad P\in\operatorname{Supp}N(u), \] the registry asks for a constant $K>0$, uniform in $F$, such that \[ \frac{3}{(-K_X)^3} \int_0^\tau \left(P(u)^2\cdot S\right) \operatorname{ord}_F\!\left(N(u)|_S\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} u \leq K A_S(F) \qquad \text{for every prime divisor }F\text{ over }X. \] This is a transcription of the reconstructed target, with unresolved qualifications, rather than an unambiguous mathematical conjecture. The permitted dependence of $K$ on $X,S,P$ and the decomposition data is not specified.

Unresolved valuation domain. A prime divisor over a variety is a prime divisor on a normal variety equipped with a proper birational morphism to that variety. The reconstructed statement quantifies over prime divisors over $X$, but the supplied original excerpt instead says "for every prime divisor $F$ over $S$." The terms $A_S(F)$ and $\operatorname{ord}_F(N(u)|_S)$ naturally require valuations over $S$; they are not intrinsically defined for an arbitrary prime divisor over $X$.

For clarity of notation only, if $S$ is normal and $\mathbb Q$-Gorenstein and $F$ is a prime divisor on a normal birational model $f:T\to S$, the usual log discrepancy is \[ A_S(F)=1+\operatorname{coeff}_F(K_T-f^*K_S). \] If $N(u)|_S$ is a defined $\mathbb R$-Cartier divisor, its order along such an $F$ is \[ \operatorname{ord}_F(N(u)|_S) =\operatorname{coeff}_F\!\left(f^*(N(u)|_S)\right). \] These conditional definitions do not supply missing hypotheses or resolve the conflict between the two quantifiers. In particular, the record must clarify the nature and singularities of $S$, whether the restriction $N(u)|_S$ exists, the meaning and integrability of the integrand, and whether the allowed divisors must satisfy a center condition involving $P$. No corrected choice of valuation domain or additional hypotheses is asserted here.

Short English statement

For permanent UnsolvedMath ID $30005795$, the supplied question seeks a bound, uniform in the allowed divisorial valuations, for an integral measuring the negative part of $-K_X-uS$ along $S$, in the case where the point $P$ lies in that negative part for some $u$. The reconstructed statement uses divisors over $X$, while the original excerpt uses divisors over $S$. This conflict and missing geometric hypotheses prevent a definitive formulation. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
