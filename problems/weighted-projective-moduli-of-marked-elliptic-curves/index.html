Weighted-Projective Moduli of Marked Elliptic Curves

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not define the objects $G$ and $M$, their admissible range, or the marking they specify on an elliptic curve. It also leaves the base field, the moduli functor, compactification, and the choice between a moduli stack and a coarse moduli space unspecified. Consequently, the classification question cannot yet be stated as an unconditional, fully specified mathematical problem.

For terminology only, given a field $k$ and positive integers $a_0,\ldots,a_n$, the weighted projective scheme is \[ \mathbb{P}_k(a_0,\ldots,a_n)=\operatorname{Proj} k[x_0,\ldots,x_n],\qquad \deg x_i=a_i. \] A weighted complete intersection over $k$, in the scheme interpretation, is a closed subscheme presented as \[ \operatorname{Proj}\left(k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_c)\right), \] where the $f_i$ are weighted homogeneous polynomials forming a regular sequence. These definitions explain standard scheme terminology; they do not resolve whether the source intends schemes or stacks, or requires additional smoothness or other conditions.

Conditional formulation of the supplied question. Once an admissible class $\mathcal{A}$ of pairs $(G,M)$ and the associated moduli objects $X_{G,M}$ of marked elliptic curves have been defined, classify all $(G,M)\in\mathcal{A}$ for which $X_{G,M}$ is isomorphic to a weighted projective space, or admits a realization as a complete intersection in a weighted projective space. Under a scheme interpretation over a fixed field $k$, the first property would mean that there exist $n\geq 0$ and positive integers $a_0,\ldots,a_n$ such that \[ X_{G,M}\cong\mathbb{P}_k(a_0,\ldots,a_n), \] and the second would mean that $X_{G,M}$ has a presentation of the weighted complete intersection form above. The symbols $\mathcal{A}$ and $X_{G,M}$ are placeholders for missing source definitions, not supplied constructions.

The source also asks how far this classification has progressed and what further applications these moduli spaces have. It specifies neither a criterion for measuring classification progress nor a particular application to establish. These parts remain research directions rather than determinate mathematical assertions.

Short English statement

Determine which pairs $(G,M)$ give moduli objects of marked elliptic curves that are weighted projective spaces or weighted complete intersections, and investigate their further applications. The supplied record does not define $(G,M)$, the markings, or the precise moduli objects, so the formulation remains conditional. Its open status and claim that the classification is incomplete are supplied dated assessments, not independently verified facts.
