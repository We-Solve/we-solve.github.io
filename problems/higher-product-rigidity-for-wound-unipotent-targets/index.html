Math-15788

Higher Product Rigidity for Wound Unipotent Targets

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $U$ be a wound unipotent group over $k$, as in the supplied record. No restriction on $\operatorname{char}(k)$ is supplied. Under the usual smooth connected convention, $k$-wound means that every $k$-morphism $\mathbb A^1_k\to U$ is constant; the precise conventions for the group hypotheses should be checked against the cited report.

For each $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $X_i\subseteq\mathbb P^1_k$ be a nonempty open subscheme and choose $x_i\in X_i(k)$. Write $D$ for the source's union of their complementary divisors, whose support is \[ \bigcup_{i=1}^{n}\left(\mathbb P^1_k\setminus X_i\right). \] The record does not specify divisor multiplicities or define the degree of primitivity of $D$. Denote that unspecified invariant provisionally by $\delta(D)$. This notation supplies no definition of the invariant, and its value when $D$ is empty is also unspecified.

Set $P=\prod_{j=1}^{n}X_j$, with all products taken over $k$. For each $i$, let \[ \iota_i:\prod_{j\ne i}X_j\longrightarrow P \] be the morphism inserting $x_i$ in the $i$-th coordinate. An empty product here means $\operatorname{Spec}(k)$. For a $k$-scheme $T$, write $1_T:T\to U$ for the composite of its structure morphism with the identity section of $U$.

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every such collection of data, \[ n>\delta(D) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left[ \forall f\in\operatorname{Hom}_k(P,U),\quad \left(\forall i\in\{1,\ldots,n\},\ f\circ\iota_i=1_{\prod_{j\ne i}X_j}\right) \Longrightarrow f=1_P \right]. \] The face restrictions and conclusion are equalities of morphisms of $k$-schemes, not merely equalities on $k$-rational points. This is a provisional formulation: the missing definition of $\delta(D)$ prevents a fully specified mathematical assertion.

Short English statement

The conjecture says that a morphism from a product of pointed open subsets of $\mathbb P^1_k$ to a wound unipotent group must be constantly the identity if it is the identity on every chosen coordinate face and the number of factors exceeds the degree of primitivity of the boundary. The supplied record leaves that boundary invariant undefined, so the numerical hypothesis requires clarification.
