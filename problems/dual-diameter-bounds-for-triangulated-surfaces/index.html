Math-6559

Dual-Diameter Bounds for Triangulated Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite triangulation $T$ of a surface, define its dual graph $G_T$ to have one vertex for each triangle of $T$, with two distinct vertices adjacent when their triangles share an edge. If $G_T$ is connected, its dual diameter is \[ \operatorname{diam}(G_T)=\max_{\sigma,\tau\in V(G_T)}d_{G_T}(\sigma,\tau), \] where $d_{G_T}$ is shortest-path distance.

Let $\mathcal S_n$ denote the intended class of triangulated surfaces with boundary having exactly $n$ vertices. The supplied record describes $\overline f(2,n)$ as the maximal dual diameter in this class. Thus, whenever the class and its maximum are well defined, \[ \overline f(2,n)=\max_{T\in\mathcal S_n}\operatorname{diam}(G_T). \] The proposed question is whether, for every admissible integer $n$, \[ \overline f(2,n)\leq n-3. \] Equivalently, once the intended class is specified, the proposed bound is $\operatorname{diam}(G_T)\leq |V(T)|-3$ for every $T$ in that class.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the precise class $\mathcal S_n$, including the triangulation convention, connectedness, allowed topology, whether the boundary must be nonempty, or whether interior vertices are allowed. It also does not specify the range of $n$. The dual-graph definition above supplies a conventional interpretation; its agreement with the cited source requires review. Consequently, this is a conditional formulation of the supplied question, pending clarification of its domain.

Short English statement

Does every triangulated surface in the intended class, with $n$ vertices, have dual diameter at most $n-3$? The allowed surfaces and triangulations require clarification. The supplied record labels the question open, but its dated literature assessment discusses a different topic and does not establish the status of this bound.
