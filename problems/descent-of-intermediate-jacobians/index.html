Descent of Intermediate Jacobians

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ be a subfield, let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over $K$, and put $X_{{\mathbb{C}}}=X\times_{\operatorname{Spec}K}\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}$. Write $2n+1$ for the odd cohomological degree in the source; the source does not explicitly specify the range of the integer $n$. Set \[ H_{{\mathbb{Z}}}=H^{2n+1}(X_{{\mathbb{C}}}({\mathbb{C}}),{\mathbb{Z}})/\operatorname{tors},\qquad H_{{\mathbb{C}}}=H^{2n+1}(X_{{\mathbb{C}}}({\mathbb{C}}),{\mathbb{C}}). \] Here $\operatorname{tors}$ denotes the torsion subgroup, and $H_{{\mathbb{Z}}}$ is identified with its image in $H_{{\mathbb{C}}}$. The Hodge decomposition is \[ H_{{\mathbb{C}}}=\bigoplus_{p+q=2n+1}H^{p,q}. \] The hypothesis that this cohomology has Hodge level $1$ means, with the literal convention for level, \[ \max\{\lvert p-q\rvert:H^{p,q}\ne0\}=1. \] Thus the only possible nonzero Hodge summands are $H^{n+1,n}$ and $H^{n,n+1}$. The Hodge-level condition is understood on the torsion-free cohomology; torsion carries no additional Hodge summands.

Let $F^{\bullet}$ be the Hodge filtration. Define the intermediate Jacobian by \[ J^{2n+1}(X_{{\mathbb{C}}})= \frac{H_{{\mathbb{C}}}}{F^{n+1}H_{{\mathbb{C}}}+H_{{\mathbb{Z}}}}, \qquad F^{n+1}H_{{\mathbb{C}}}=\bigoplus_{p\ge n+1}H^{p,2n+1-p}. \] Under the stated Hodge-level hypothesis and projectivity assumption, this complex torus is an abelian variety.

The question is whether, for every such $K$, $X$, and degree $2n+1$, there exist an abelian variety $A$ over $K$ and an isomorphism of complex abelian varieties \[ A\times_{\operatorname{Spec}K}\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}} \;\cong\;J^{2n+1}(X_{{\mathbb{C}}}). \] This is the meaning of admitting a model over $K$; the supplied question imposes no additional compatibility with a chosen polarization or Abel--Jacobi map.

The target is the whole intermediate Jacobian. The supplied literature assessment distinguishes it from the algebraic Abel--Jacobi image $J_a^{2n+1}(X_{{\mathbb{C}}})$, namely the image of algebraically trivial cycles of codimension $n+1$ under the Abel--Jacobi map. According to that supplied assessment, the cited distinguished-model result concerns this image and does not establish descent of the whole intermediate Jacobian in the stated generality. This assessment is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified literature conclusion.

Short English statement

For a smooth projective variety over a subfield $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$, assume its indicated odd-degree cohomology has Hodge level $1$. Must the entire associated intermediate-Jacobian abelian variety be the complex base change of an abelian variety over $K$? The supplied record labels this question open and distinguishes it from descent of the algebraic Abel--Jacobi image.
