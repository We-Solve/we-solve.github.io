Genus Three Hecke Trace Formula

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for a proof or disproof of a genus-three conjectural Hecke trace formula. Its parameters satisfy \[ a\geq b\geq c\geq 0. \] The source does not specify their domain, integrality or parity conditions, or the range of the parameter $p$. The displayed inequalities include equality cases and $c=0$; no further boundary restrictions are supplied.

The proposed formula, reproduced with the space on which the trace is taken made explicit, is \[ \operatorname{tr}\!\left(T(p)\mid S_{a-b,b-c,c+4}\right) = e_c(A_3\otimes\mathbb{F}_p,V_{a,b,c}) -e_{3,\mathrm{extra}}(a,b,c), \] where the supplied correction expression is \[ \begin{aligned} e_{3,\mathrm{extra}}(a,b,c) ={}&-e_c(A_2,V_{a+1,b+1})\\ &-e_{2,\mathrm{extra}}(a+1,b+1)\otimes S[c+2]\\ &+e_c(A_2,V_{a+1,c})\\ &+e_{2,\mathrm{extra}}(a+1,c)\otimes S[b+3]\\ &-e_c(A_2,V_{b,c})\\ &-e_{2,\mathrm{extra}}(b,c)\otimes S[a+4]. \end{aligned} \] Here $T(p)$ is described by the source as a Hecke operator on $S_{a-b,b-c,c+4}$. The source does not define this space or the normalization of the operator. It also supplies no definitions of $A_2$, $A_3$, the objects $V_{u,v}$ and $V_{a,b,c}$, the invariant $e_c$, the genus-two correction $e_{2,\mathrm{extra}}$, or the symbols $S[k]$ and $\otimes$. The subscript in $e_c$ is retained exactly as supplied.

In particular, the source does not specify the mathematical setting in which the correction expression takes values, the base for the $A_2$ terms, or how the right-hand side is to be compared with the numerical Hecke trace. If its terms are cohomological or motivic objects, an appropriate realization and trace operation would have to be specified; none is supplied. Thus the display records the proposed expression, but a fully defined, quantified conjecture cannot be recovered from this record alone. No additional hypotheses, missing operations, or definitions are imposed here.

Short English statement

Prove or disprove the displayed genus-three Hecke trace formula for parameters satisfying $a\geq b\geq c\geq0$, with the six supplied correction terms. The record leaves essential domains, definitions, normalizations, and the interpretation of the right-hand side unresolved, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
