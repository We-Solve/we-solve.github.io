$L^p$ Approximation for Higher-Rank Group Factors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $d\geq 2$, set \[ \Gamma_d=\operatorname{PSL}_{2d-1}({\mathbb{Z}}) =\operatorname{SL}_{2d-1}({\mathbb{Z}})/Z(\operatorname{SL}_{2d-1}({\mathbb{Z}})). \] Let $\lambda_d$ be the left regular representation of $\Gamma_d$ on $\ell^2(\Gamma_d)$, defined by $(\lambda_d(g)\xi)(h)=\xi(g^{-1}h)$. Its group von Neumann algebra is \[ M_d=L(\Gamma_d)=\{\lambda_d(g):g\in\Gamma_d\}'', \] with canonical tracial state $\tau_d(x)=\langle x\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle$, where $\delta_e$ is the unit vector at the identity. For $1\leq p<\infty$, let $L^p(M_d,\tau_d)$ be the completion of $M_d$ for \[ \|x\|_p=\left(\tau_d(|x|^p)\right)^{1/p}, \] equipped with its canonical noncommutative $L^p$ operator-space structure. If the exponent $p=\infty$ is admitted, use $L^\infty(M_d,\tau_d)=M_d$ with its usual operator-space structure.

An operator space $X$ has the completely bounded approximation property if there are a constant $C<\infty$ and a net of finite-rank linear maps $T_\alpha:X\to X$ such that \[ \sup_\alpha\|T_\alpha\|_{\mathrm{cb}}\leq C, \qquad \lim_\alpha\|T_\alpha x-x\|_X=0 \quad\text{for every }x\in X, \] where \[ \|T\|_{\mathrm{cb}} =\sup_{n\geq1}\|\operatorname{id}_{M_n}\otimes T:M_n(X)\to M_n(X)\|. \] In particular, when $X=M_d$, this definition uses pointwise convergence in operator norm.

Question, with unresolved exponent range. For each integer $d\geq2$, is there an admissible exponent $p\neq2$ such that $L^p(M_d,\tau_d)$ has the completely bounded approximation property? If $\mathcal P$ denotes the intended exponent range, the natural per-dimension reading is \[ \forall d\in{\mathbb{N}},\ d\geq2,\quad \exists p\in\mathcal P\setminus\{2\}:\quad L^p(M_d,\tau_d)\text{ has the completely bounded approximation property}. \] The supplied record does not specify $\mathcal P$, including whether either endpoint is allowed. The definitions above cover the conventional Banach range $1\leq p\leq\infty$; they do not determine the intended range. On the displayed reading, $p$, the approximation bound, and the approximating maps may depend on $d$. A requirement of one exponent working for every $d$ would be a different quantifier order and is not explicit in the source.

Short English statement

For each integer $d\geq2$, the question asks whether the noncommutative $L^p$-space of the group von Neumann algebra of $\operatorname{PSL}_{2d-1}({\mathbb{Z}})$ admits uniformly completely bounded finite-rank approximations to the identity for some $p\neq2$. The intended exponent range and omitted surrounding hypothesis require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified here.
