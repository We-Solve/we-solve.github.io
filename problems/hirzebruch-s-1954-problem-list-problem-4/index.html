Hirzebruch's 1954 Problem List — Problem 4

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Identify Euclidean space $\mathbb{E}^4$ with ${\mathbb{R}}^4$ equipped with its standard inner product. Under the usual smooth embedded interpretation of a closed surface, let $M$ be a nonempty compact smooth two-dimensional manifold without boundary and let $f\colon M\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^4$ be a smooth embedding. Define the induced Riemannian metric by \[ g_p(v,w)=\langle {\,\mathrm{d}} f_p(v),{\,\mathrm{d}} f_p(w)\rangle, \qquad p\in M,\quad v,w\in T_pM, \] and write $K_g\colon M\to{\mathbb{R}}$ for its Gaussian curvature.

General existence question. Does there exist such a pair $(M,f)$ satisfying \[ \forall p\in M,\qquad K_g(p)<0? \] The source does not explicitly impose orientability on $M$ in this general question.

Genus-two question. In particular, does there exist such a pair $(M,f)$ with $M$ connected, orientable, and of genus $2$, satisfying the same strict curvature inequality at every point?

Interpretive qualifications. Here "closed" means compact and without boundary, and genus $2$ is interpreted in the usual orientable-surface sense. The supplied wording does not explicitly specify a differentiability class or distinguish embeddings from immersions. The displayed formulation uses smooth embeddings; whether an immersed formulation was also intended remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Can a closed surface embedded in Euclidean four-dimensional space have strictly negative Gaussian curvature everywhere for its induced metric? The source asks specifically whether this is possible for a surface of genus $2$. This draft uses the smooth embedded interpretation and requires human mathematical review.
