Problem 8 — (Orbits of square-tiled surfaces).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A square-tiled translation surface $(X,\omega)$ is conventionally obtained from finitely many unit Euclidean squares by translation identifications of parallel edges, with $\omega$ the induced holomorphic $1$-form. Equivalently it gives the corresponding branched cover of the standard flat torus, with the permitted branch data fixed by convention. A stratum $\mathcal H(k_1,\ldots,k_r)$ records zero orders of $\omega$; in the usual unmarked genus-$g$ setting, $\sum_i k_i=2g-2$.

The $\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}})$ action postcomposes the translation charts with a determinant-one linear map. Problem 8 asks to classify \[ \mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}})\cdot(X,\omega) \] for square-tiled surfaces in every stratum, and to describe their Teichmüller discs. The full orbit usually contains surfaces not tiled by unit squares, so the task concerns orbits containing the square-tiled representatives, not a claimed action of all $\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}})$ on the discrete square-tiled set.

In the usual marked model a Teichmüller disc is generated by the affine deformations of one translation surface, with rotation factored out; its projection to moduli and its affine stabilizer encode further geometry. The source does not specify whether the requested description concerns the marked disc, its moduli image, stabilizer/Veech group, or all of these. It does not restrict to primitive torus covers or fix the number of squares. Area normalization matters because $\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}})$ preserves area, so arbitrary rescaling must not be silently included.

The source highlights $\mathcal H(1,1)$, the genus-two stratum with two simple zeros, and says the problem was solved only for $\mathcal H(2)$, the genus-two double-zero stratum, citing [HuLe1] and [McM4]. That "only" is a source-era assessment, not a new current-literature claim. The supplied summary reports continued orbit-classification study and an open general problem without further precise results.

Short English statement

Classify the determinant-one linear orbits of all square-tiled surfaces in each stratum and describe their Teichmüller discs, especially in $\mathcal H(1,1)$. The source's $\mathcal H(2)$ solved-case report is retained historically, with area, covering and disc-description conventions still to be fixed.
