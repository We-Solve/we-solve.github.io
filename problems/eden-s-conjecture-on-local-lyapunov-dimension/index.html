Eden's conjecture on local Lyapunov dimension

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Phi^t$ be the source's smooth dissipative dynamical system and let $\mathcal A$ be its global attractor. In the usual flow setting, $\mathcal A$ is compact and invariant and attracts the relevant bounded subsets of the phase space. The source does not specify the phase-space dimension, the precise dissipativity convention, or whether a discrete-time version is also intended.

Write $D_{\mathrm{loc}}(x)$ for the intended local Lyapunov dimension at $x\in\mathcal A$. The record supplies no formula or limit convention for this quantity. In particular a dimension computed from asymptotic Lyapunov exponents and one defined through finite-time singular values require different regularity and limiting conventions; neither is silently selected.

For a flow define \[ E=\{x\in\mathcal A:\Phi^t(x)=x\text{ for every }t\}, \] and let $P_{\mathrm{u}}$ be the union of the unstable periodic orbits contained in $\mathcal A$. A periodic orbit has a positive period $\tau$ with $\Phi^\tau(x)=x$; the exact instability convention, including treatment of neutral directions, is not given.

Eden's conjecture as supplied asks whether \[ \exists x_*\in E\cup P_{\mathrm{u}}\quad D_{\mathrm{loc}}(x_*)= \sup_{x\in\mathcal A}D_{\mathrm{loc}}(x) \] for every system in the intended class. Thus the claim is actual attainment at an equilibrium or an unstable periodic orbit in the attractor, not approximation by a sequence of such orbits or attainment at an arbitrary invariant measure. The local-dimension definition and system class must be fixed before this implication becomes fully determined.

Short English statement

Must the largest local Lyapunov dimension on a global attractor occur at an equilibrium or an unstable periodic orbit within that attractor? The supplied summary says local Lyapunov dimension gives rigorous Hausdorff-dimension bounds and that the proposed attainment is confirmed in special, mostly low-dimensional or structurally stable cases. It gives no precise hypotheses or references for those statements and reports no verified general proof.

The registry labels the question open, but its cited status source is a Wikipedia unsolved-problem list, explicitly described in provenance as non-authoritative for resolution status. The OPEN-TRIAGE assessment is retained without an independent status claim.
