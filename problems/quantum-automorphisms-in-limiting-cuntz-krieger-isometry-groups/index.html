Quantum Automorphisms in Limiting Cuntz-Krieger Isometry Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The intended objects are attached to a finite zero--one matrix $A$. For a square instance $A\in\{0,1\}^{n\times n}$, its classical automorphism group is \[ \operatorname{Aut}(A)=\{\sigma\in S_n:P_\sigma A=AP_\sigma\}, \] where $P_\sigma$ is the permutation matrix of $\sigma$. Regard this group as a classical compact quantum group. Write $\operatorname{QAut}(A)$ for the graph quantum automorphism group intended in the cited report, and $G_A^\infty$ for the report's limiting quantum isometry group associated with its Cuntz--Krieger construction and family $(G_A^\ell)_\ell$. The supplied excerpt does not give the defining relations of $\operatorname{QAut}(A)$, the construction of $G_A^\ell$, the meaning of the limiting operation, or the complete hypotheses on $A$. These source-dependent objects therefore cannot be reconstructed unambiguously from this record alone.

For compact quantum groups $H$ and $G$, say that $H$ is a quantum subgroup of $G$ if there is a surjective unital $*$-homomorphism between their universal compact quantum group $C^*$-algebras, \[ \pi:C^u(G)\longrightarrow C^u(H), \qquad (\pi\otimes\pi)\circ\Delta_G=\Delta_H\circ\pi, \] where $\Delta_G$ and $\Delta_H$ are the coproducts.

The intended question is: for every finite zero--one matrix $A$ in the domain of the report's constructions, does \[ \operatorname{QAut}(A)\ne\operatorname{Aut}(A) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{QAut}(A)\text{ is a quantum subgroup of }G_A^\infty \] hold? Here the inequality expresses the presence of quantum automorphisms beyond the classical automorphism group, using the report's identification of the classical group inside the quantum group. Equivalently, under that hypothesis, must there exist a surjective coproduct-preserving map \[ C^u(G_A^\infty)\longrightarrow C^u(\operatorname{QAut}(A))? \] The qualification concerning the domain is necessary because the supplied clean statement says only "finite zero--one matrix," whereas the omitted construction may require additional hypotheses. It is not a resolution of those missing hypotheses.

The excerpt supplies the first-level inclusion $\operatorname{QAut}(A)\leq G_A^1$ as background and asks whether the corresponding inclusion holds for $G_A^\infty$ in the nonclassical case. This draft records that question without asserting its answer.

Short English statement

Whenever the report's constructions are defined and $A$ has quantum automorphisms beyond its classical automorphisms, must its full quantum automorphism group occur as a quantum subgroup of the limiting Cuntz--Krieger quantum isometry group $G_A^\infty$? The matrix hypotheses and the source-specific construction of the limiting group are missing from the supplied excerpt.
