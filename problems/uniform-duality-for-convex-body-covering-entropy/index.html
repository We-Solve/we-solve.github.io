Math-2701

Uniform duality for convex-body covering entropy

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For convex bodies $K,L$ symmetric about zero, let $N(K,L)$ be the least number of translates of $L$ covering $K$, and let \[ K^\circ=\{y:\sup_{x\in K}x\cdot y\leq1\} \] be the polar of $K$, with $L^\circ$ defined similarly. Do constants $a,b\geq1$, independent of the dimension and the bodies, exist such that \[ \log N(K,L)\leq b\log N(L^\circ,a^{-1}K^\circ) \] always holds?

Short English statement

Are there constants $a,b\geq1$, independent of dimension and of the convex bodies $K,L$ symmetric about zero, such that $\log N(K,L)\leq b\log N(L^\circ,a^{-1}K^\circ)$ always holds, where $N(K,L)$ is the least number of translates of $L$ covering $K$ and $K^\circ=\{y:\sup_{x\in K}x\cdot y\leq1\}$ is the polar, defined similarly for $L$?

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Write $N(K,L)$ for the least number of translates of $L$ covering $K$, and $K^{\circ}$ for the Euclidean polar. The claimed counterexamples satisfy \[ \forall a,b\ge1\ \exists n\ge1\ \exists K=-K\subset\mathbb R^n:\quad \log N(K,[-1,1]^n)>b\log N(([-1,1]^n)^{\circ},a^{-1}K^{\circ}), \] where $K$ is a full-dimensional convex body. Thus no fixed pair of dilation and multiplicative constants supplies the proposed dual covering-number comparison in every dimension.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 329; 2026-09-24), partitions compress convex-hull columns uniformly across coordinates. Finite-field zero estimates supply many separated rows whose dual images remain compressible; choosing the matrix parameters makes the primal entropy arbitrarily larger than the proposed polar comparison.
