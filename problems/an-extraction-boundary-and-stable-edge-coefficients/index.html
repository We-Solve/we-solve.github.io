The research aid gives a separate Coxeter-theoretic coefficient result motivated by that cross-reference. Let $W$ be an irreducible finite Coxeter group of rank $\ell\ge1$, acting on the complexified reflection representation $V$, with basic invariant degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_\ell$ and Coxeter number $h$. For an integer $k\ge0$ define the standard $q$-Fuss--Catalan polynomial \[ C_{W,k}(q)=\prod_{i=1}^\ell \frac{1-q^{kh+d_i}}{1-q^{d_i}}, \qquad N=\deg C_{W,k}=\ell kh. \] Write \[ p_W(r)=\#\{(a_1,\ldots,a_\ell)\in{\mathbb{N}}^\ell: \sum_i d_i a_i=r\} =\dim{\mathbb{C}}[V]^W_r, \] with $p_W(r)=0$ for negative integers $r$. For $c_r=[q^r]C_{W,k}(q)$, the reported full inclusion--exclusion formula is \[ c_r=\sum_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,\ell\}}(-1)^{|S|} p_W\left(r-\sum_{i\in S}(kh+d_i)\right). \] The product is reciprocal, $c_{N-r}=c_r$ for $0\le r\le N$. Since the least invariant degree is $2$ and is unique for irreducible $W$, the low window is \[ c_r=p_W(r)\quad(0\le r\le kh+1), \] and the high window is \[ c_{N-r}=p_W(r)\quad(0\le r\le\min\{kh+1,N\}). \] The first low-edge defect is exactly \[ c_{kh+2}=p_W(kh+2)-1. \] Coefficients beyond polynomial degree are understood as zero, and the high-index formula is only used when its exponent is nonnegative. These identities describe edge coefficients, not equality of the polynomial with the whole invariant-ring Hilbert series. They are an auxiliary supplied result and do not recover or solve a missing independent Problem 2.1.
