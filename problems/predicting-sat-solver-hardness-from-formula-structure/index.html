Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.

A formula-space measure bounds the maximum number of clauses or formulas retained simultaneously during a derivation. It differs from proof length and depends on the chosen proof system.

Record scope and qualifications.
The catalogue title is retained as metadata. Named certificates, contracts, bounds or frameworks in that title are not asserted as results of the original source extract. Hardness may mean worst-case complexity or performance of one solver; the supplied question does not choose a solver, distribution, or quantitative hardness criterion.

Source-record limitation: The explanation of solver success and formula hardness is a broad agenda without a solver model, hardness criterion, or stated proposition.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
What explains the success of SAT solvers, how can one determine whether a particular propositional formula is hard or tractable, and can formula space complexity serve as a useful measure of hardness? [S2]
