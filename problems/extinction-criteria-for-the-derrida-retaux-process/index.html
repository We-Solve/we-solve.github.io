Math-7279

Extinction Criteria for the Derrida–Retaux Process

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mu_0$ be an initial probability distribution on the state space denoted by $\mathbb{R}_+$ in the supplied source, and let $(X_t)_{t\geq 0}$ denote the continuous-time Derrida--Retaux process with $\mathcal{L}(X_0)=\mu_0$. The supplied record does not specify the process dynamics, the convention for the endpoint of $\mathbb{R}_+$, or the definition and domain of the quantity $F_\infty(\mu_0)$. Consequently, these symbols retain their source-specific meanings; the following is a conditional formulation requiring those definitions before it becomes self-contained.

Characterization problem. Determine a condition $\mathcal{C}$ on the initial law such that, for every initial law $\mu_0$ admissible for the source model, \[ F_\infty(\mu_0)=0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mathcal{C}(\mu_0). \] The record describes the target as general initial laws and supplies no moment or integrability restrictions. Any restrictions required to define the process or $F_\infty$ remain to be recovered from the source.

Additional conjecture reported by the source. For every such admissible initial law, does \[ F_\infty(\mu_0)=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \forall\varepsilon>0,\qquad \lim_{t\to\infty}\mathbb{P}(|X_t|>\varepsilon)=0 \] hold? The conclusion is precisely convergence of $X_t$ to zero in probability. Neither this implication nor the requested characterization is asserted here as a theorem.

Short English statement

Find an exact criterion on the initial distribution for the continuous-time Derrida--Retaux quantity $F_\infty$ to vanish. The source also conjectures that vanishing forces the process to converge to zero in probability. The supplied record omits the dynamics and the definition of $F_\infty$, so a self-contained formulation requires further source clarification.
