Extremal Bounds Relating Word Period Parameters

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns finite words. For a finite word $w=a_1\cdots a_n$, its length is $|w|=n$. Let $\mathcal W$ denote the intended class of words. The record does not specify the alphabet, its allowed size, whether the empty word is admitted, or any further restrictions on $\mathcal W$. It also supplies no definitions of the parameters $\tau(w)$ and $\pi(w)$, or of Assous--Pouzet words. These omissions prevent a fully specified mathematical statement; the following preserves the requested questions as conditional formulations pending those definitions.
  1. Determine whether \[ \forall w\in\mathcal W,\qquad |w|>\frac{7}{3}\tau(w)-4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \tau(w)=\pi(w). \] The strict inequality and the constant $4$ are part of the supplied question.

  2. Determine the infimum of the constants $d$ for which there exists a counterexample satisfying \[ |w|\geq\frac{7}{3}\tau(w)-d. \] The meaning of "counterexample" and the domain of $d$ require clarification. In particular, the record does not say whether a counterexample means a word with unequal parameters, a word with $\tau(w)<\pi(w)$, or a counterexample to the entire implication in the first question. If the intended meaning is $\tau(w)<\pi(w)$ and $d$ ranges over ${\mathbb{R}}$, the requested quantity has the conditional formulation \[ \inf\left\{d\in{\mathbb{R}}: \exists w\in\mathcal W,\quad \tau(w)<\pi(w)\ \text{and}\ |w|\geq\frac{7}{3}\tau(w)-d \right\}. \] Neither this interpretation nor attainment of the infimum is established by the supplied record.

  3. Determine whether "such extremal words" must be Assous--Pouzet words. The record supplies neither a definition of that family nor a precise extremality condition. In particular, it does not specify whether extremality means attainment of the infimum in the preceding question.

  4. For each fixed admissible parameter $\delta$, describe all words in \[ \mathcal E_{\delta} =\left\{w\in\mathcal W: \tau(w)<\pi(w),\quad |w|\geq\frac{7}{3}\tau(w)-\delta \right\}. \] The domain and any sign restriction on $\delta$ are unspecified. The strict comparison of the two parameters and the non-strict length bound are preserved exactly.

The record presents these as bundled Ehrenfeucht--Silberger questions. Its supplied dated assessment labels the bundle partially solved and attributes an optimal general $7/3$ bound to Holub and Nowotka, while reporting that the requested extremal classifications and parameterized descriptions are not all settled. This assessment does not supply definitions or a precise theorem sufficient to resolve the formulation gaps above.

Short English statement

The questions ask how close words with $\tau(w)<\pi(w)$ can come to a length bound with coefficient $7/3$, which words are extremal, and how to classify words within a fixed additive allowance. A complete statement requires the missing definitions of the two word parameters, the admissible words and constants, and the Assous--Pouzet family. The supplied partially solved status is dated curation requiring independent review.
