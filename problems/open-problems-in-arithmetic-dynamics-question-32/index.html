Let $B$ denote the parameter space in the supplied source, and retain its notation $K_v$ for the local field at a place $v$ of an underlying field $K$. The source does not specify these objects or their hypotheses. Write $B(K_v)$ for the set of $K_v$-rational points of $B$. Question 32. Describe the parameter dependence of the function displayed in the source, \[ h_v\colon B(K_v)\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}\cup\{\infty\}, \qquad t\longmapsto\hat\lambda_{f_t,v}(P_t). \] In particular, to what extent is $h_v$ a Weil function associated to a divisor \[ D\in\operatorname{Div}(B)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}? \]

For clarity about the terminology, if the intended divisor is a real Cartier divisor $D=\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_iD_i$, with $a_i\in{\mathbb{R}}$ and Cartier divisors $D_i$, a local Weil function has, away from the supports of the $D_i$, the local form \[ \lambda_{D,v}(t)=-\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_i\log|g_i(t)|_v+\alpha(t), \] where $g_i$ is a local defining rational function for $D_i$, and $\alpha$ is a continuous local correction term in the relevant local topology. This explains the terminology conditionally; the supplied record does not specify whether $\operatorname{Div}(B)$ means Cartier or Weil divisors, or which convention for Weil functions is intended.

The question remains a request for a description, rather than a fully specified universal assertion. The source does not state the admissible families, the domain of valid specialization, the treatment of exceptional parameters, or whether the proposed relation with a Weil function means exact equality or equality up to a specified error. These qualifications must be resolved before extracting a sharper quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

For a varying dynamical map and a varying point, describe how the local canonical height depends on the parameter, and determine whether it behaves as a Weil function of a real divisor on the parameter space. The supplied formulation leaves the field, base space, admissible families, normalization, and exceptional parameters unspecified.
