Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 86

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $G$ is coherent if \[ \forall H\leq G,\qquad H\text{ finitely generated}\ \Longrightarrow\ H\text{ finitely presented}. \] This is the explicit algebraic property recalled in the source. It is a condition on every finitely generated subgroup, not merely on $G$ itself.

The actual problem is the broader request to find new restrictions on Kleinian groups. In a real-hyperbolic interpretation, these are discrete groups of isometries of $\mathbb H^n$; the classical three-dimensional usage concerns discrete subgroups of $\operatorname{PSL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$. The source does not specify the ambient dimension, torsion assumptions, finite generation, or geometric finiteness conditions.

No particular new restriction is proposed, and the recalled coherence definition is not itself a conjecture that all higher-dimensional Kleinian groups are coherent. The supplied summary suggests higher-dimensional questions after three-dimensional progress, but does not determine a single class or quantified assertion. A precise theorem target must first identify both the Kleinian-group class and the proposed algebraic or geometric restriction.

Short English statement

Find additional algebraic or geometric constraints on Kleinian groups. Coherence is supplied as context, without a conjecture asserting it for all dimensions.
