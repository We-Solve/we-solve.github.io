Bounded approximation in uniformly discrete Lipschitz-free spaces

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 330; 2026-09-26), the asserted result is that a countable uniformly discrete metric space has a real Lipschitz-free space with the approximation property but without any bounded approximation constant.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For a pointed metric space $(M,d,o)$, let $\mathcal F(M)$ be the closed span of evaluation functionals $\delta_x$ in the dual of real Lipschitz functions vanishing at $o$. The claim supplies a countable unbounded nonproper space with \[ d(x,y)\ge1\ (x\ne y),\qquad \mathcal F(M)\text{ has AP but fails }\lambda\text{-BAP for every finite }\lambda\ge1. \] AP means uniform approximation of the identity on compact sets by finite-rank operators; $\lambda$-BAP additionally bounds their norms by $\lambda$. Thus uniform discreteness does not force bounded approximation for the free space.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 330; 2026-09-26), colored-graph blocks carry signed measures and opposing local bump tests that force large finite-rank norms. Each block still admits coefficient truncation, but a metric wedge joins blocks with unbounded required constants, preserving AP and defeating BAP.
