Quasiconvexity of Multiple-Valued Integrands

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question uses a base set $B$, a target $M$ with tangent bundle $TM$, a domain dimension $m$, and a multiplicity $Q$. The notation implicitly requires positive integers $m,Q$ and a differentiable structure on $M$, but the record does not specify further hypotheses on these objects. Interpret the tangent-map bundle as \[ \operatorname{Hom}({\mathbb{R}}^m,TM) =\bigsqcup_{\nu\in M}\operatorname{Hom}({\mathbb{R}}^m,T_\nu M). \] Thus an argument of the integrand consists of $x\in B$, $\nu\in M$, and a linear map $N:{\mathbb{R}}^m\to T_\nu M$.

At the level of sets, the space of unordered $Q$-tuples in $M$, with repetitions allowed, is \[ \mathcal A_Q(M)=M^Q/S_Q, \] where $S_Q$ is the permutation group acting on the entries. A $Q$-valued map into $M$ takes values in $\mathcal A_Q(M)$. This set-theoretic definition does not specify a metric, a function space, or a notion of derivative for such maps.

Question, with unresolved source qualifications. Suppose that \[ f:B\times\operatorname{Hom}({\mathbb{R}}^m,TM)\longrightarrow[0,+\infty) \] is quasiconvex in the sense intended by the source. For $x\in B$, $\nu_i\in M$, and $N_i\in\operatorname{Hom}({\mathbb{R}}^m,T_{\nu_i}M)$, $1\leq i\leq Q$, define \[ e_M(x,\nu_1,\ldots,\nu_Q,N_1,\ldots,N_Q) :=\sum_{i=1}^Q f(x,\nu_i,N_i). \] The pairs $(\nu_i,N_i)$ are considered up to simultaneous permutation. Must $e_M$ be quasiconvex on the space of $Q$-valued maps into $M$?

The supplied record does not define either quasiconvexity predicate or the admissible variations, comparison domains, and boundary conditions. It also leaves the geometry of $M$, the role and domain properties of $B$, and any analytic assumptions on $f$ unspecified. Consequently, this is a faithful conditional restatement of the supplied question; a fully quantified variational inequality cannot be fixed from the supplied evidence alone.

Short English statement

Does summing a nonnegative quasiconvex integrand over the $Q$ entries of a multiple-valued map preserve quasiconvexity? The supplied record labels this question open, but its precise variational definitions and hypotheses are missing. This statement-only draft requires human mathematical review.
