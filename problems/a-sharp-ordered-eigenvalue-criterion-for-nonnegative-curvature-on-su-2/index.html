Math-1739

A sharp ordered-eigenvalue criterion for nonnegative curvature on SU(2)

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a compact Lie group $G$ and a closed subgroup $H$. A metric on the fixed homogeneous presentation $G/H$ is $G$-invariant and is determined by an $\operatorname{Ad}(H)$-invariant positive-definite inner product on $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak h$. Problem 26 asks to classify those inner products whose resulting sectional curvatures are all nonnegative. Classification up to automorphism, equivariant isometry, arbitrary isometry, or scale should be distinguished; the original sentence does not choose an equivalence relation. Its terminal "3" is unattached extraction text and gives no stated dimension hypothesis.

The supplied partial classification concerns $SU(2)/\{e\}$ only. Fix a Lie-algebra basis $E_1,E_2,E_3$ with $[E_1,E_2]=2E_3$ and cyclic analogues. Up to a Lie-group automorphism, every left-invariant metric is diagonal, \[ \langle E_i,E_j\rangle=x_i\delta_{ij},\qquad 0<x_1\leq x_2\leq x_3. \] In this bracket normalization the principal sectional curvature of $E_i\wedge E_j$, with remaining index $k$, has numerator \[ N_k=(x_i-x_j)^2+2x_k(x_i+x_j)-3x_k^2 \] and positive denominator $x_1x_2x_3$. The curvature operator is diagonal in the corresponding principal two-plane basis. In the ordered chamber $N_1,N_2$ are strictly positive, so the aid's complete criterion for this presentation is \[ \sec\geq0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (x_1-x_2)^2+2x_3(x_1+x_2)-3x_3^2\geq0, \] equivalently \[ x_3\leq\frac{x_1+x_2+2\sqrt{x_1^2-x_1x_2+x_2^2}}3. \] Strict inequality gives positive sectional curvature. Equality gives exactly one flat tangent two-plane at the identity, $\operatorname{span}(E_1,E_2)$, and its left translates; the other principal curvatures stay positive. The aid's sharp consequences are \[ x_3-x_2\leq x_1/3,\qquad x_3/x_2\leq4/3. \] The eigenvalue proportions $(1,1,4/3)$ attain both bounds. Rescaling all $x_i$ by a common positive factor does not affect the curvature-sign criterion.

This is a complete left-invariant $SU(2)$ answer up to automorphism, but does not classify nonnegatively curved homogeneous metrics on arbitrary compact presentations $G/H$. The record supplies no general classification beyond this case.

Short English statement

Classify the nonnegatively curved invariant metrics on a fixed compact homogeneous space. The supplied partial result treats left-invariant $SU(2)$ metrics: after ordering their three metric eigenvalues, one explicit inequality determines the full nonnegative-curvature region, its unique flat boundary plane and sharp spectral-gap bounds.
