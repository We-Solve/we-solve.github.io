An exact-flat Boolean model for Coxeter-Catalan numerology

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for structural explanations of Coxeter--Catalan coincidences among noncrossing partitions $NC_W$, nonnesting objects $NN_W$, and facets of the cluster complex $\Delta_W$. In a finite crystallographic Coxeter setting, $NC_W$ is the interval from $1$ to a Coxeter element in absolute/reflection order, $NN_W$ consists of antichains of the positive-root poset, and $\Delta_W$ is the cluster complex on almost-positive roots. The noncrystallographic meaning of the root-poset model is not specified in the extract. The source states common Catalan cardinalities and common Narayana rank/height/$h$-numbers, asks for statistic-preserving bijections and a general algebraic framework, asks how to formalize Bessis's "dual" Coxeter systems, and asks for an invariant-theoretic meaning of $\operatorname{Cat}(W)$, noting that exponents equal invariant degrees minus one. Its no-known-formula/no-theoretical-connection language is historical source wording, not a current assessment.

The remarks add two refinements. Chapoton's $M$, $F$, and $H$ triangles should satisfy explanatory structural relationships. Also, for the Coxeter intersection lattice $\Pi_W$ and maps \[ f(w)=\operatorname{Fix}(w),\qquad g(A)=\bigcap_{\alpha\in A}\alpha^\perp, \] the source reports equinumerous inverse images of every $W$-orbit in $\Pi_W$. It asks for a theoretical proof, an analogous cluster statistic, and compatibility with the triangle refinements. It describes an explicit Reading bijection from $NC_W$ to cluster facets with a then-case-by-case proof; that remark is truncated before its final reference context.

The aid supplies only the Boolean special case $W=A_1^n$. Take coordinate roots $\alpha_i$ and commuting coordinate reflections $s_i$. For each $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ define \[ w_I=\prod_{i\in I}s_i,\qquad A_I=\{\alpha_i:i\in I\},\qquad C_I=\{\alpha_i:i\in I\}\cup\{-\alpha_i:i\notin I\}. \] These index all noncrossing elements, root antichains, and cluster facets, respectively. The reported correspondence preserves reflection rank and antichain size $|I|$. Order the facets by any linear extension of subset inclusion. The shelling restriction face of $C_I$ is its positive-root part, so its restriction statistic is also $|I|$. Defining $q(C_I)$ as the intersection of the hyperplanes perpendicular to that positive part gives the exact-flat identity \[ \operatorname{Fix}(w_I)=g(A_I)=q(C_I) =\{x:x_i=0\text{ for }i\in I\}. \] The common multivariate flat enumerator is $\sum_I\prod_{i\in I}z_i=\prod_i(1+z_i)$; the common Narayana/$h$-polynomial is $(1+t)^n$, and the common total is $2^n$.

With $F(x,y)$ counting faces by their positive and negative roots, $H(x,y)=\sum_Ax^{|A|}y^{|A\cap\{\alpha_i\}|}$, and \[ M(x,y)=\sum_{I\subseteq J}(-1)^{|J\setminus I|}x^{|J|}y^{|I|}, \] the aid's convention-explicit Boolean factorizations are \[ F=(1+x+y)^n,\qquad H=(1+xy)^n,\qquad M=(1-x+xy)^n. \] They do not identify the triangles as equal functions. This exact-flat Boolean example does not give the requested general-type bijections, uniform refined proof, dual-Coxeter framework, or invariant-theoretic interpretation; the full general Chapoton relations are not stated in the supplied record.

Short English statement

The broad question seeks structural and refined explanations of Coxeter--Catalan numerology. The aid treats only products of rank-one systems, where subsets give exact-flat bijections and explicit triangle factorizations. The source's general algebraic, invariant-theoretic, and refinement questions remain separate.
