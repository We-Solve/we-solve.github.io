Problem 6.1 — ([188, Problem 3]) Is the representation of the braid group inside the Temperley-Lieb algebra faithful?

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The braid group $B_n$ has generators $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$ with braid relations and commuting generators at distance at least two. In a common generic bracket normalization, let $A$ be indeterminate, $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$, and let $TL_n$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}(A)$ have generators $e_i$ with \[ e_i^2=\delta e_i,\qquad e_ie_{i\pm1}e_i=e_i,\qquad e_ie_j=e_je_i\ (|i-j|\geq2). \] The braid representation takes values in its unit group: \[ \rho_n(\sigma_i)=A\,1+A^{-1}e_i,\qquad \rho_n(\sigma_i^{-1})=A^{-1}\,1+Ae_i. \] The question is whether $\rho_n$ is faithful, that is, $\ker\rho_n=\{1\}$, in the source's intended strand and parameter range. The displayed standard normalization must match that source before importing results. A generic indeterminate parameter and specialization at a number or root of unity are different problems; no specialization is silently chosen.

The supplied summary reports classical faithfulness for $n\leq3$, a simultaneous-faithfulness relation among the $B_4$ Temperley--Lieb/Jones/Burau representations from Bigelow, and a July 2026 preprint claiming faithfulness of $B_4$ Burau. Its stated conclusion of $B_4$ faithfulness is therefore recorded as dependent on that recent claim and the precise equivalence/normalization, not independently verified here. The summary leaves $n\geq5$ open and mentions a conjectural connection at five strands to Jones/HOMFLY unknot detection; no theorem-equivalence with that detection problem is asserted without its exact hypotheses.

Short English statement

Is the generic braid-group map into Temperley--Lieb units injective? Keep the strand range and parameter convention explicit, and treat the supplied recent four-strand solution claim with its actual source scope.
