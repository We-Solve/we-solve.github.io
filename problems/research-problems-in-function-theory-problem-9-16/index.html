Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 9.16

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A:{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfy $|A(x_1)-A(x_2)|\le M|x_1-x_2|$ for a finite Lipschitz constant $M$, and let \[ \Gamma=\{x+iA(x):x\in{\mathbb{R}}\},\qquad E\subseteq\Gamma\text{ compact},\qquad \Omega=\widehat{\mathbb{C}}\setminus E. \] The source denotes $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}={\mathbb{C}}\cup\{\infty\}$ by ${\mathbb{C}}^*$; this is the Riemann sphere, not the punctured plane. Let $H^\infty(\Omega)$ be the bounded holomorphic functions on $\Omega$, with holomorphy at $\infty$ interpreted in the coordinate $1/z$.

Garnett asks to prove the corona theorem for this domain. In its finite-generator form, the target is: for every integer $m\ge1$ and $f_1,\ldots,f_m\in H^\infty(\Omega)$ satisfying \[ \inf_{z\in\Omega}\sum_{j=1}^m|f_j(z)|>0, \] there exist $g_1,\ldots,g_m\in H^\infty(\Omega)$ such that \[ \sum_{j=1}^m f_j(z)g_j(z)=1\qquad(z\in\Omega). \] No quantitative uniform bounds on the solution norms beyond boundedness are requested in the excerpt.

The source additionally inserts the hypothesis $\Delta_1(t)>0$, but does not define $\Delta_1$, identify its dependence on $E$ or $\Gamma$, or state a range or quantifier for $t$. That clause is material and cannot simply be discarded; the corona target above is conditional on the intended full hypotheses, rather than a strengthened claim for every compact subset without that clause.

The supplied OPEN-TRIAGE summary describes many planar corona results, including Carleson's disk theorem and work on infinitely connected domains, but gives no precise resolution for the stated Lipschitz-graph class with its missing hypothesis. Its broad "largely settled" language is not treated as an exact theorem for this source item.

Short English statement

Prove the bounded-holomorphic Bezout form of the corona theorem for the sphere minus a compact subset of a Lipschitz graph. The extra condition $\Delta_1(t)>0$ is undefined and must be restored.
