Henon Maps: Open Problems — Question 28

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not define the family or the set $J^+_{a,c}$, or specify the parameter domain. The following formulation is therefore conditional on the complex quadratic Hénon convention \[ H_{a,c}(x,y)=(x^2+c-ay,x),\qquad (x,y)\in{\mathbb{C}}^2,\quad (a,c)\in{\mathbb{C}}^*\times{\mathbb{C}}, \] where ${\mathbb{C}}^*={\mathbb{C}}\setminus\{0\}$. These conventions require verification against the source. Under these conventions, define \[ K^+_{a,c}=\left\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^2:\sup_{n\geq0}\|H_{a,c}^{\,n}(z)\|<\infty\right\},\qquad J^+_{a,c}=\partial K^+_{a,c}, \] where the boundary is taken in ${\mathbb{C}}^2$, and let \[ d(a,c)=\dim_{\mathrm H}J^+_{a,c} \] be the Hausdorff dimension for the Euclidean metric on ${\mathbb{C}}^2\cong{\mathbb{R}}^4$.

The requested problem is to determine an asymptotic expansion of $d(a,c)$ as $(a,c)\to(0,0)$. The source does not specify the scale of the expansion, its remainder, or restrictions on the relative rates at which the two parameters approach zero.

In particular, the source asks whether there is a lower bound \[ d(a,c)\geq 3+h(a),\qquad h(a)>0, \] uniform for $c$ in a neighborhood of zero. A formulation with one common neighborhood would be: do there exist $\delta,\varepsilon>0$ and a function \[ h:\{a\in{\mathbb{C}}:0<|a|<\delta\}\longrightarrow(0,\infty) \] such that \[ \forall a\ (0<|a|<\delta)\quad\forall c\ (|c|<\varepsilon),\qquad d(a,c)\geq3+h(a)? \] A weaker interpretation permits the neighborhood of $c=0$ to depend on $a$, replacing this condition by \[ \forall a\ (0<|a|<\delta)\quad\exists\varepsilon_a>0\quad\forall c\ (|c|<\varepsilon_a),\qquad d(a,c)\geq3+h(a). \] The record does not determine which quantifier order is intended. Excluding $a=0$ in these conditional formulations reflects the invertible Hénon parameter domain assumed above; the source does not explicitly specify whether the degenerate parameter itself is included.

Short English statement

Determine how the Hausdorff dimension of the forward Julia set $J^+_{a,c}$ behaves as $(a,c)\to(0,0)$. In particular, ask whether it exceeds $3$ by a positive quantity depending only on $a$, uniformly for $c$ near zero. The family convention, parameter domain, treatment of $a=0$, and precise uniformity require source verification.
