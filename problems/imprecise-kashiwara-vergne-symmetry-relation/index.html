Short English statement

The record suggests a conjectural relationship between Kashiwara--Vergne symmetries and a prounipotent Grothendieck--Teichmüller group, but does not specify what that relationship means. It cannot presently be turned into a precise standalone conjecture. The supplied open label concerns this imprecise extracted relationship, while the supplied assessment describes the Kashiwara--Vergne problem itself as solved.
