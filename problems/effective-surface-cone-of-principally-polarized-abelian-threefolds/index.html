Effective Surface Cone of Principally Polarized Abelian Threefolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal A_3$ denote the moduli space of principally polarized abelian varieties of dimension three, and let $X$ denote the toroidal compactification intended by the source. The supplied record does not specify the base field, the toroidal decomposition, or whether a stack or its coarse moduli space is intended.

Under a numerical-cycle interpretation, let $N_2(X)_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ be the real vector space of algebraic cycles of dimension two modulo numerical equivalence, and define \[ C_2(X)=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_i[V_i]:\ r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\ a_i\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0},\ V_i\subseteq X\text{ an integral closed surface}\right\}. \] Here $[V_i]$ denotes the numerical class of $V_i$, and the empty sum is zero. The supplied record does not specify whether its notation $\operatorname{Eff}_2$ means $C_2(X)$, its closure $\overline{C_2(X)}$, or a cone in another cycle-class space.

Write $s_A,s_F,s_D,s_{K3+1},s_{C4}$ for the classes intended by the source symbols $S_A,S_F,S_D,\sigma_{K3+1},\sigma_{C4}$, respectively. Their geometric definitions are not included in the supplied record. With these unresolved choices retained, the surface-cone question in the supplied clean statement is \[ \operatorname{Eff}_2(X)\stackrel{?}{=} {\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}s_A+ {\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}s_F+ {\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}s_D+ {\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}s_{K3+1}+ {\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}s_{C4}. \] Equivalently, under the numerical-cycle interpretation and the source's assertion that the five classes are effective, the question asks whether every class $\alpha\in\operatorname{Eff}_2(X)$ admits coefficients $a_A,a_F,a_D,a_{K3+1},a_{C4}\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}$ such that \[ \alpha=a_As_A+a_Fs_F+a_Ds_D+a_{K3+1}s_{K3+1}+a_{C4}s_{C4}. \] The record writes $\operatorname{Eff}_2(\mathcal A_3)$ while describing cycles on a compactification; $X$ makes that intended ambient space explicit without selecting a compactification.

There is a material conflict in the supplied formulations: the original statement asks about a codimension-two effective cone, whereas the clean statement asks about surfaces. In the usual genus-three moduli setting, $\dim\mathcal A_3=6$; surfaces have codimension four, and codimension-two cycles have dimension four. Thus these formulations are not interchangeable. The displayed question records the supplied clean surface formulation, but the intended target, the five geometric classes, and the cone convention require confirmation before this becomes a fully specified mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The supplied clean formulation asks whether every effective surface class on the intended toroidal compactification of $\mathcal A_3$ is a nonnegative real combination of five named classes. The compactification, class definitions, and cone convention are missing, and the original codimension-two wording conflicts with the surface formulation. The supplied open status and extremality assessment are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
