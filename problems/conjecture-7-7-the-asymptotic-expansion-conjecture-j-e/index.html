Conjecture 7.7 — (The asymptotic expansion conjecture, J.E.

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed oriented three-manifold, and let $\{c_0=0,c_1,\ldots,c_m\}$ be the source's finite set of values of the Chern--Simons functional on flat $G$-connections on $M$. Let $Z_k^G(M)$ be the quantum invariant at level $k$, and put $r=k+h^\vee$, where $h^\vee$ is the indicated dual Coxeter number. The class of groups $G$, normalization of the functional and invariant, and auxiliary framing/anomaly data are not specified in the excerpt.

The asymptotic expansion conjecture asserts that there exist, for each $0\leq j\leq m$, numbers \[d_j\in{\mathbb{Q}},\quad\widetilde I_j\in{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}},\quad v_j\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0},\quad a_j^e\in{\mathbb{C}}\quad(e\geq1),\] independent of $r$, such that, with $d=\max_{0\leq j\leq m}d_j$, \[Z_k^G(M)\sim\sum_{j=0}^m e^{2\pi i r c_j}r^{d_j}e^{\pi i\widetilde I_j/4}v_j \left(1+\sum_{e=1}^{\infty}a_j^e r^{-e}\right)\qquad(r\to\infty).\] Precisely, for every integer $E\geq0$ there exists a constant $C_E\geq0$, independent of $r$, for which \[\left|Z_k^G(M)-\sum_{j=0}^m e^{2\pi i r c_j}r^{d_j}e^{\pi i\widetilde I_j/4}v_j \left(1+\sum_{e=1}^{E}a_j^e r^{-e}\right)\right| \leq C_E r^{d-E-1}.\] The source states this for all $r=2,3,4,\ldots$; since $r=k+h^\vee$, the meaning for levels not admissible for a chosen $G$ requires clarification. Empty sums at $E=0$ are zero. The constants and expansion data may depend on $M,G$ and the fixed normalization, but $C_E$ cannot depend on $r$. This is an asymptotic expansion with truncation bounds, not a claim that the infinite series converges.

There is a literal phase ambiguity: $e^{\pi i\widetilde I_j/4}$ is not defined by a class $\widetilde I_j\in{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, since changing a representative by $1$ changes the phase. The formula therefore retains the source's notation pending a specified lift or corrected quotient. If the $c_j$ are classes modulo ${\mathbb{Z}}$, their phases are well defined for integral $r$, but that convention must likewise be stated. No lift or corrected phase group is silently selected.

Short English statement

Andersen's conjecture expresses quantum invariants at large level as finitely many Chern--Simons phases times rational powers and inverse-level asymptotic series, with a uniform bound after every truncation. The extracted phase quotient and level range need clarification.
