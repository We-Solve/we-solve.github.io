Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the intended class of spaces, define the $\delta$-property, or state whether covers must be countable. The following is a conditional formalization using the usual open-cover and limit-inferior interpretation; its agreement with the cited source requires review.

Let $X$ be a topological space. An open cover is a family $\mathcal U$ of proper open subsets of $X$ whose union is $X$. An $\omega$-cover is an open cover such that every finite subset $F\subseteq X$ is contained in some $U\in\mathcal U$. A $\gamma$-cover is an infinite open cover $\mathcal V$ such that \[ \forall x\in X,\qquad \{V\in\mathcal V:x\notin V\}\text{ is finite}. \] Write $\Omega(X)$ and $\Gamma(X)$ for these respective classes. If the source restricts admissible covers, these classes must be restricted consistently; no such restriction is specified in the supplied record.

The property $\binom{\Omega}{\Gamma}$ means \[ \forall\mathcal U\in\Omega(X)\ \exists\mathcal V\subseteq\mathcal U, \qquad \mathcal V\in\Gamma(X). \] For a sequence $(A_n)_{n\geq1}$ of subsets of $X$, define \[ \liminf_{n\to\infty}A_n =\bigcup_{m\geq1}\bigcap_{n\geq m}A_n. \] Let $L(\mathcal U)$ be the smallest subfamily of $\mathcal P(X)$ containing $\mathcal U$ and closed under this operation on sequences of its members. In the interpretation adopted here, the $\delta$-property is \[ \forall\mathcal U\in\Omega(X),\qquad X\in L(\mathcal U). \] The sets in $L(\mathcal U)$ need not be open.

The source's question is whether, for every $X$ in its intended class of spaces, \[ X\text{ has the }\delta\text{-property} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X\text{ satisfies }\binom{\Omega}{\Gamma}. \] The source states the implication from right to left, so the requested implication is from left to right. The intended class of spaces and cover conventions remain unresolved.

For context, $\mathsf S_1(\Omega,\Gamma)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}$ of members of $\Omega(X)$, there exist choices $U_n\in\mathcal U_n$ such that $\{U_n:n\geq1\}\in\Gamma(X)$. The supplied statement attributes the equality of properties $\mathsf S_1(\Omega,\Gamma)=\binom{\Omega}{\Gamma}$ to reference [GN]. This is retained as supplied context, subject to the source's unresolved hypotheses and conventions.

Short English statement

Does every space with the $\delta$-property have a $\gamma$-subcover of each open $\omega$-cover? The source states the reverse implication. The formulation above uses a conventional interpretation of the undefined $\delta$-property; the intended spaces and cover restrictions require source review.
