Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Fix $J>0$ and a real potential $V(A)$ on finite nonempty subsets of $\mathbb Z^2$, vanishing for odd $|A|$ or diameter greater than a fixed finite range, and invariant under translations, reflections and quarter-turns. For spins in $D_a=D\cap a\mathbb Z^2$, use \[ H=-J\sum_{|u-v|=a}\sigma_u\sigma_v-\lambda\sum_AV(A)\prod_{x\in A}\sigma_{ax}, \] counting unordered terms once. Free boundary retains interactions contained in $D_a$; constant $+$ or $-$ boundary retains every interaction meeting $D_a$ with exterior spins fixed. Let $D$ be bounded and simply connected with $C^2$ Jordan boundary. There exist $\lambda_0>0$ and factors $\beta_c,Z_\sigma,Z_\epsilon$, continuous at zero, with $\beta_c(0)=\log(1+\sqrt2)/(2J)$ and $Z_\sigma(0)=Z_\epsilon(0)=1$, all factors nonzero for $|\lambda|<\lambda_0$. Define, at $\beta_c(\lambda)$, \[ S_a(x)=a^{-1/8}\sigma_{[x]_a},\qquad E_{a,j}(y)=a^{-1}[\sigma_{[y]_a}\sigma_{[y]_a+ae_j} -\langle\sigma_{[y]_a}\sigma_{[y]_a+ae_j}\rangle], \] where $[x]_a=a\lfloor x/a\rfloor$. For every $b\in\{\mathrm{free},+,-\}$, all $n,m\ge0$ with $n+m\ge1$, distinct interior insertions and $j_k\in\{1,2\}$, \[ \left\langle\prod_{i=1}^nS_a(x_i)\prod_{k=1}^mE_{a,j_k}(y_k)\right\rangle \longrightarrow Z_\sigma(\lambda)^nZ_\epsilon(\lambda)^m \mathcal C_D^b(\boldsymbol x;\boldsymbol y), \] where $\mathcal C_D^b$ is the corresponding pure nearest-neighbour critical limit with identical centering. The same limit holds in the plane after first taking periodic-box thermodynamic limits. For a conformal bijection $\varphi:D\to D'$, the covariance convention is \[ \mathcal C_D^b(\boldsymbol x;\boldsymbol y)= \left(\prod_i|\varphi'(x_i)|^{1/8}\prod_k|\varphi'(y_k)|\right) \mathcal C_{D'}^b(\varphi\boldsymbol x;\varphi\boldsymbol y). \] In particular the plane two-spin limit is a nonzero multiple of $|x-y|^{-1/4}$.
