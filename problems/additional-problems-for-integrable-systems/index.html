Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
A soliton is a localized persistent wave of an integrable PDE; a two-soliton initial profile produces two such waves in the unperturbed focusing NLS. In a Toda chain, particle accelerations are differences of adjacent exponential forces. A shock driver has $x_0(t)=2at$ and initially equally spaced particles at rest. For a trace-class positive contraction $K$, the Fredholm determinant $\det(I-K)=\prod_j(1-\lambda_j)$ is naturally interpretable through independent Bernoulli variables with probabilities $\lambda_j$. In the source's power perturbation, $\ell>0$ denotes the exponent and $\varepsilon>0$ is sufficiently small for the regime under consideration.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Analyze the long-time Cauchy problem for the perturbed focusing equation $i\widetilde u_t+\widetilde u_{xx}+2|\widetilde u|^2\widetilde u+\varepsilon|\widetilde u|^\ell\widetilde u=0$, with decaying initial data, especially data giving a two-soliton solution when $\varepsilon=0$. Prove rigorously by Riemann--Hilbert steepest descent the asymptotic binary periodic motion in the Toda shock problem with $x_k(0)=k$, $\dot x_k(0)=0$ for $k\ge1$, $x_0(t)=2at$, $a>1$. Analyze the same shock problem for suitable small perturbations $F$ of the force $e^x$, under the source’s condition that the corresponding doubly infinite chain has a temporally periodic, spatially two-periodic solution. Seek a useful intrinsic probabilistic definition of integrability, motivated by expressing key statistics through independent coin flips.
Scope and source qualification.
The printed focusing equation has an untilde u in its cubic term; the consistent unknown is explicitly used here as an editorial correction. The truncated integrable-probability question is restored. Suitability assumptions on perturbations are left as a required part of the programme, and the two-slit target has its separate canonical record 8100008. The source's defocusing theorem assumes $\ell>7/2$, while it expects extension to $\ell>2$; its later focusing comparison uses $\ell>2$ as the proposed regime. A proved defocusing threshold is not a proved focusing threshold.

Short English statement

Analyze perturbed focusing solitons and Toda shocks, and seek a definition of integrability intrinsic to probability.

Testing the independent-coin characterization.
Let $K$ be any positive trace-class contraction, with eigenvalues $0\le\lambda_j\le1$ and $\sum_j\lambda_j<\infty$. Construct independent Bernoulli variables $B_j$ with $\mathbb P(B_j=1)=\lambda_j$. Then $S=\sum_jB_j$ is finite almost surely: its expectation is the finite sum of the probabilities, and a nonnegative random variable with finite expectation cannot be infinite on a positive-probability event. Independence and finite products give \[\mathbb E z^S=\prod_j(1-\lambda_j+z\lambda_j),\quad0\le z\le1,\qquad \mathbb P(S=0)=\prod_j(1-\lambda_j)=\det(I-K). \] The infinite-product formula follows by bounded convergence from partial sums. This proves the desired coin interpretation at the spectral level for an arbitrary positive trace-class contraction.
A limitation of this definition.
The property therefore does not by itself distinguish an algebraically solvable model: the eigenvalues of $K$ may be inaccessible, and only a single count distribution is represented. Conversely any prescribed summable sequence in $[0,1]$ defines a diagonal positive trace-class contraction and hence such a coin model. A useful intrinsic definition needs constraints on how the statistic and its kernel are generated, and on accessible joint observables, not merely the existence of this product after diagonalization.
