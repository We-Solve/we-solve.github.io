Mirzakhani-typical recurrence and equidistribution of classical stretch lines

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks whether a stretch line is typically recurrent, dense, or equidistributed in moduli space. Its word "typically" does not choose a measure or parameterization. The supplied result specifies closed oriented surfaces of genus $g\geq2$, directed classical unit-speed Thurston stretch lines, and normalized Mirzakhani measure on the unit-length measured-lamination phase space \[ \mathcal P^1\mathcal M_g=\{(X,\lambda):X\in\mathcal T_g,\ \lambda\in\mathcal{ML}(S)\setminus\{0\},\ \ell_X(\lambda)=1\}/\operatorname{Mod}(S). \] Let $\mu_{\mathrm{Th}}$ be Thurston measure on measured laminations and define \[ B(X)=\mu_{\mathrm{Th}}\{\lambda:\ell_X(\lambda)\leq1\},\qquad b_g=\int_{\mathcal M_g}B(X)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_{\mathrm{WP}}(X). \] The phase probability $\widehat\mu_{\mathrm{Mir}}$ uses the radial pushforward $\lambda\mapsto\lambda/\ell_X(\lambda)$ of the length-at-most-one cone in each fiber, together with Weil--Petersson measure, normalized by $b_g$. Thus its moduli-space marginal is \[ {\,\mathrm{d}}\nu_g(X)=\frac{B(X)}{b_g}\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_{\mathrm{WP}}(X). \] In particular the asserted equilibrium law is not simply normalized Weil--Petersson measure.

The aid invokes Calderon--Farre's ergodicity and geodesicity results to identify a full-measure domain of a measure-preserving ergodic phase flow $\Phi_t$ whose projected orbits $X_t$ are these classical directed stretch lines. With exactly those theorem hypotheses and the unit-speed time parameter, it concludes that for $\widehat\mu_{\mathrm{Mir}}$-almost every initial phase point the phase orbit is recurrent, its moduli projection is dense, and \[ \frac1T\int_0^T\delta_{X_t}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t\ \Longrightarrow\ \nu_g\qquad(T\to\infty). \] Equivalently the averages of bounded continuous test functions converge to their $\nu_g$ integrals. The common almost-everywhere statement uses the specified phase measure; it is not a claim for every starting surface, every fixed lamination, or every stretch line.

The aid's stronger visit conclusion is that, simultaneously for every nonempty moduli-space open set $U$, \[ \liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac1T\operatorname{Leb}\{t\in[0,T]:X_t\in U\}>0. \] It follows from ergodicity on a countable base and full support of $\nu_g$, without requiring zero boundary measure for arbitrary $U$. Consequently $\liminf_{t\to\infty}\operatorname{sys}(X_t)=0$, while for some fixed $\epsilon_0>0$ the set of times with $\operatorname{sys}(X_t)\geq\epsilon_0$ has positive lower frequency. Arbitrarily thin excursions and repeated thick-part returns are compatible. Recurrence is a phase-flow conclusion from Poincaré recurrence; equidistribution follows from Birkhoff and the stated marginal formula. The precise phase-flow construction, full-measure domain, and theorem references are not included in the supplied record.

The trailing source sentence about Lenzhen--Masur concerns when Teichmüller geodesic rays remain bounded distance apart. It is retained as separate context and is not a recurrence theorem for Thurston stretch lines.

Short English statement

With a specified closed-surface phase measure and unit-speed stretch flow, the supplied aid reports almost-everywhere recurrence, density, and equidistribution. The projected probability has density $B/b_g$ relative to Weil--Petersson measure. Typical lines visit every open set with positive lower frequency, including thin regions and a fixed thick part. This is a measure-specific statement, not one about all stretch lines.
