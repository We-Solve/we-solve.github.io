Math-9647

The density hypothesis for zeroes of the Riemann zeta function

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Let $N(\sigma,T)$ count zeros $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ of the Riemann zeta function with $\beta\geq\sigma$ and $0<\gamma\leq T$, counting multiplicity. For every $1/2\leq\sigma\leq1$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, must \[N(\sigma,T)\ll_{\sigma,\varepsilon}T^{2(1-\sigma)+\varepsilon}\] hold as $T\to\infty$?

Formulation references: [S1], [S2].
