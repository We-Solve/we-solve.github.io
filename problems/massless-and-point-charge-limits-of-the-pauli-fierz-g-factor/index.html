Massless and Point-Charge Limits of the Pauli–Fierz $g$-Factor

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
The translation-invariant Pauli--Fierz Hamiltonian decomposes into total-momentum fibers $H(p)$ acting on electron-spin and photon Fock space. $m_{\mathrm{ph}}$ regularizes the photon infrared dispersion and $\widehat\varphi(k)$ is the charge form factor providing the ultraviolet cutoff. The $g$-factor measures the spin response relative to the effective orbital magnetic response and is defined by the report's equation (14). The point-charge limit sends $\varphi$ to a delta distribution, equivalently $\widehat\varphi(k)\to(2\pi)^{-3/2}$ in its Fourier convention. The fine-structure constant is $\alpha=e^2/(4\pi)$ in the report's units.
Source scope and qualifications.
The perturbative first correction is not a proof that the full cutoff limits exist. The two cutoff removals, possible order of limits, all-alpha dependence, and the second-order coefficient are separate questions. The g-factor definition is the source's equation (14), including effective-mass normalization.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For the Pauli–Fierz Hamiltonian $H(p)$ with photon dispersion $\omega(k)=\sqrt{|k|^2+m_{\mathrm{ph}}^2}$, does its $g$-factor have a finite limit as $m_{\mathrm{ph}}\to0$ and $\widehat\varphi(k)\to(2\pi)^{-3/2}$? If the point-charge limit exists, determine its dependence on the fine-structure constant $\alpha$, including the coefficient of $O(\alpha^2)$ in $$g_\infty=2\left(1+\frac{4\alpha}{3\pi}+O(\alpha^2)\right)$$.
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Perturbative coefficients and several ultraviolet or point-limit results are known, but the report's full infrared limit, alpha-dependence, and O(alpha squared) questions for the interacting Hamiltonian remain unresolved. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Determine whether the Pauli--Fierz g-factor survives removal of both photon-mass and charge-size cutoffs, and compute its dependence on coupling.
