Math-4564

Braid-Group Extensions of Zamolodchikov Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Delta$ and $\Delta'$ be simply laced Dynkin diagrams. Write $h(\Gamma)$ for the Coxeter number of a Dynkin diagram $\Gamma$, and put \[ k=h(\Delta),\qquad n=k+h(\Delta'),\qquad d=\gcd(k,n). \] Thus $h(\Delta')=n-k$. Denote the cluster algebra in the supplied statement by $\mathcal A=\mathcal A(\Delta\square\Delta')$, and denote its Zamolodchikov transformation by $\rho$. Let $C_\rho=\langle\rho\rangle$ be the cyclic group of transformations generated by $\rho$.

Conjecture as supplied. For every such pair $(\Delta,\Delta')$, does the cyclic action of $C_\rho$ on $\mathcal A$ extend to an action of the extended braid group $\widetilde{\operatorname{Br}}_d$ on $\mathcal A$?

Unresolved specifications. The record does not define the product construction $\Delta\square\Delta'$, its required quiver orientations, the coefficient convention for $\mathcal A$, the transformation $\rho$, or the class of transformations allowed in the proposed action. Its original wording uses $\Delta\otimes\Delta'$ in place of $\Delta\square\Delta'$; their intended relationship requires confirmation. The record also supplies no presentation of $\widetilde{\operatorname{Br}}_d$, no conventions for small values of $d$, and no identification specifying how the cyclic action is to be recovered from that group.

For precision, if extension is intended through a specified homomorphism $\iota:C_\rho\to\widetilde{\operatorname{Br}}_d$, and $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{allowed}}(\mathcal A)$ denotes the intended group of allowed transformations, the extension condition would be the existence of a homomorphism \[ \Phi:\widetilde{\operatorname{Br}}_d\longrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{allowed}}(\mathcal A) \quad\text{such that}\quad \Phi\circ\iota=\alpha, \] where $\alpha$ is the given cyclic action. Neither $\iota$ nor the allowed transformation group is specified in the record. This conditional explanation does not supply the missing conventions or assert that an embedding of the cyclic group is intended.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every pair of simply laced Dynkin diagrams, the cyclic Zamolodchikov action on their associated cluster algebra extends to the indicated extended braid group, with index $d=\gcd(h(\Delta),h(\Delta)+h(\Delta')))$. The product, group, and extension conventions require clarification before this becomes a fully specified mathematical assertion.
