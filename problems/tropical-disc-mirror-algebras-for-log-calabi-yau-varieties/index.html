Tropical-Disc Mirror Algebras for Log Calabi–Yau Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $U$ be a complex affine variety satisfying the source's hypothesis of being a log Calabi--Yau variety with maximal boundary. The supplied record does not specify the permitted singularities, the compactification or boundary conditions defining this hypothesis, or the construction of the integral tropical points $U^t({\mathbb{Z}})$. Retain these as source-dependent terms requiring clarification, and put \[ S_U:=U^t({\mathbb{Z}}),\qquad A_U:=\bigoplus_{p\in S_U}{\mathbb{C}}\vartheta_p. \] Thus the $\vartheta_p$ are formal basis vectors, and each element of $A_U$ is a finite complex linear combination of them.

The proposed assertion is that, for every such $U$, this vector space admits a natural finitely generated commutative associative ${\mathbb{C}}$-algebra structure with \[ \vartheta_p\vartheta_q=\sum_{r\in S_U}\alpha_U(p,q,r)\vartheta_r, \qquad \alpha_U(p,q,r)\in{\mathbb{Z}}, \] where, for every $p,q\in S_U$, only finitely many $r$ have $\alpha_U(p,q,r)\ne0$. The structure constants are required to count tropical discs. The source does not specify the tropical discs, their constraints, their multiplicities, or the finiteness and invariance conditions needed to define these counts. In particular, it does not determine whether the counts are ordinary nonnegative counts or signed or weighted counts.

The assertion further asks that \[ U^\vee:=\operatorname{Spec}(A_U) \] be an affine log Calabi--Yau mirror with maximal boundary and that applying the same construction twice recover $U$, expressed in the original record as $(U^\vee)^\vee=U$. For varieties this recovery would ordinarily be formulated as an isomorphism \[ (U^\vee)^\vee\cong U, \] but the record does not specify the required canonicity or compatibility with additional data. The usual spectrum construction presupposes a unital algebra; the source does not explicitly state the unit requirement or identify a unit among the basis elements. The meanings of naturality and of being a mirror also require specification.

Finally, the supplied clean statement asks that the induced canonical bases restrict to bases of regular functions on every partial minimal model. The source does not define these models or identify precisely which varieties and bases are involved. If the intended model is a variety $X$ containing a variety $W$ as a dense open subvariety, and $\mathcal B_W$ is the induced basis of $\mathcal O(W)$, one possible precise interpretation, using the restriction injection $\mathcal O(X)\hookrightarrow\mathcal O(W)$, is \[ \mathcal O(X)=\bigoplus_{b\in\mathcal B_W\cap\mathcal O(X)}{\mathbb{C}} b. \] This is a conditional interpretation of the restriction clause, not a definition supplied by the record. The intended class of partial minimal models and the applicable canonical bases must be fixed before that clause becomes a fully determined assertion.

Short English statement

For every affine log Calabi--Yau variety with maximal boundary, the question asks whether its integral tropical points index a natural algebra whose multiplication counts tropical discs, whose spectrum is an involutive mirror, and whose induced bases are compatible with all partial minimal models. The source leaves several geometric definitions and counting conventions unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
