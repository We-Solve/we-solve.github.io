Math-4337

Periodicity of Twisted Outer-Automorphism-Group Cohomology

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $i\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ and let $p$ be a prime. For $n\geq 1$, let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$, and write $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$. Put ${\mathbb{F}}_p={\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$. For a group $G$ and a specified $G$-module $M$, write $H^i(G;M)$ for group cohomology.

The supplied question asks whether \[ H^i\left(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);{\mathbb{F}}_p^{\,n}\right) \] is eventually periodic in $n$, for every fixed $i$ and $p$. The coefficient action is not explicitly specified. A natural interpretation identifies ${\mathbb{F}}_p^{\,n}$ with \[ V_n=(F_n/[F_n,F_n])\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{F}}_p, \] with the action induced by automorphisms of $F_n$; inner automorphisms act trivially on this quotient, so this gives an $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$-module. This interpretation requires confirmation from the source.

Under this coefficient interpretation, and interpreting eventual periodicity as periodicity of isomorphism classes of ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector spaces, the question is whether \[ \forall i\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\ \forall p\text{ prime}\ \exists N\geq1\ \exists T\geq1\ \forall n\geq N,\qquad H^i\left(\operatorname{Out}(F_{n+T});V_{n+T}\right) \cong_{{\mathbb{F}}_p} H^i\left(\operatorname{Out}(F_n);V_n\right). \] Here $N$ and $T$ may depend on $i$ and $p$. The supplied record does not specify whether periodicity concerns these isomorphism classes, numerical dimensions, or isomorphisms compatible with additional maps or structure; no such maps or structure are supplied.

If the initial question has an affirmative answer, the record asks the same question for coefficients given by a polynomial functor or a VIC-module. For a specified coefficient system $(M_n)_{n\geq1}$ with specified $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$-actions, the corresponding question would replace $V_n$ by $M_n$ in the displayed periodicity condition, allowing $N$ and $T$ also to depend on that system. The source record does not specify the polynomial functors, their source and target categories or degree conditions, or the base ring, morphism convention, variance, and coefficient construction for VIC-modules. Consequently, the intended class of coefficient systems and the precise quantifiers for this extension remain unresolved.

Short English statement

For each fixed cohomological degree and prime, ask whether the cohomology of $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ with the indicated rank-$n$ modular coefficients eventually repeats with a positive period as $n$ grows. If so, ask the analogous question for polynomial-functor or VIC-module coefficients. The coefficient action, meaning of repetition, and scope of the generalized coefficients require confirmation.
