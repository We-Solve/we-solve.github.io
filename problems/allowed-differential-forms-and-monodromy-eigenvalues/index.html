Allowed Differential Forms and Monodromy Eigenvalues

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether allowed differential forms can capture precisely the monodromy eigenvalues of a function $f$ through poles of associated topological zeta functions. It does not specify the class of functions, the ambient space, the local or global setting, or the meaning of "allowed." Consequently, it does not determine a complete mathematical conjecture.

The following is a conditional formulation of one possible intended target. Once the missing geometric setting and definitions have been fixed, let $M_f\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}^\times$ denote the relevant set of monodromy eigenvalues, let $\mathcal A(f)$ be a nonempty family of allowed differential forms, and let \[ P_{f,\omega}=\{s\in{\mathbb{C}}:s\text{ is an actual pole of }Z_{\mathrm{top}}(f,\omega;s)\} \] for each $\omega\in\mathcal A(f)$. Here $Z_{\mathrm{top}}(f,\omega;s)$ is a placeholder for the associated topological zeta function; its construction and localization are not supplied.

If the intended correspondence sends a pole $s$ to $\exp(2\pi i s)$, and if completeness is intended across the family of forms, the question becomes whether one can specify an allowed family satisfying \[ \bigcup_{\omega\in\mathcal A(f)} \{\exp(2\pi i s):s\in P_{f,\omega}\}=M_f. \] Equivalently, the two requirements are \[ \forall\omega\in\mathcal A(f)\ \forall s\in P_{f,\omega},\qquad \exp(2\pi i s)\in M_f, \] and \[ \forall\lambda\in M_f\ \exists\omega\in\mathcal A(f)\ \exists s\in P_{f,\omega},\qquad \lambda=\exp(2\pi i s). \] This expresses absence of extraneous poles under the chosen correspondence and recovery of every eigenvalue across the family. It asserts neither a bijection nor equality of multiplicities.

The exponential correspondence, its sign convention, and the family interpretation are explicit unresolved interpretations, not details established by the supplied excerpt. The record also does not determine whether the assertion should hold for every function in some class, whether a single form must recover all eigenvalues, or whether every form meeting an independently defined allowedness criterion must satisfy the first requirement. Its background mentions a monomial example but gives no example or hypotheses from which to specialize this template.

Short English statement

The question asks whether suitable differential forms make the poles of topological zeta functions detect every relevant monodromy eigenvalue without producing extraneous values. The supplied excerpt leaves the geometric setting, allowedness criterion, pole correspondence, and scope of the eigenvalues unspecified, so the displayed formulation is conditional and requires human mathematical review.
