Research question. Do the quasi-polynomial generalizations of symmetric Macdonald polynomials discussed in the cited source admit natural quantum-group interpretations?

Here an interpretation would require a mathematical construction relating the intended quasi-polynomial family to quantum-group representation theory. The record specifies neither the quantum groups nor the required relationship, and it supplies no criterion for natural. Short English statement

The question asks whether the particular quasi-polynomial extensions of symmetric Macdonald polynomials intended by the source have a natural explanation through quantum groups. The family, admissible parameters, and meaning of a natural interpretation require clarification before this becomes a precise mathematical proposition.
