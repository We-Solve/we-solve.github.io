The damaged extract and the established source claim

The retained catalogue fragment stops before the last displayed condition of Theorem1.3. Reading the primary report, printed page2974, recovers that condition as acceptance probability one. The source presents it as a corollary of $\mathrm{MIP}^*=\mathrm{RE}$: a two-query test separating its stated infinite-dimensional model from finite-dimensional strategies. That deep result is existing source context, not proved or independently validated in this manuscript. The catalogue fragment must not be turned into a newly solved dimension tradeoff. We instead attempt a finite algebraic witness and determine exactly what it can certify.

All moments leave dimension amplification invisible

In the finite positive-measurement model, replace a family on $\mathbb C^d$ by $M'_{a\mid x}=M_{a\mid x}\otimes I_k$ on $\mathbb C^{dk}$ for any integer $k\ge1$. Positivity and $\sum_a(M'_{a\mid x})^2=I$ are preserved. Every product has squared Frobenius norm multiplied by $k$, because $\operatorname{tr}((C\otimes I_k)(C^*\otimes I_k)) =k\operatorname{tr}(CC^*)$. Normalization by dimension also multiplies by $k$, so all transcript probabilities, including adaptive ones, agree exactly. No test in this access model can force an upper bound on the represented Hilbert-space dimension of every passing realization. A lower bound on the minimum possible dimension is compatible with this invariance; exact represented dimension is a different target.

An exact finite relation system with an exponential witness

Suppose Hermitian matrices $U_1,\ldots,U_{2m}$ on a nonzero finite-dimensional space satisfy \[ U_i^2=I,\qquad U_iU_j=-U_jU_i\quad(i\ne j),\qquad m\ge1. \] Then the dimension is divisible by $2^m$, in particular at least $2^m$. Define $Z_j=iU_{2j-1}U_{2j}$. Anticommutation shows $Z_j^*=Z_j$ and $Z_j^2=I$. For distinct $j,k$, moving one pair of $U$'s past the other requires four sign changes, so the $Z_j$ commute. Their commuting spectral projectors \[ P_\varepsilon=\prod_{j=1}^m(I+\varepsilon_jZ_j)/2, \qquad\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^m, \] are orthogonal and sum to $I$: this follows by distributing the product and using the two complementary projectors for each involution. At least one has nonzero range.

Conjugation by $U_{2j-1}$ changes the sign of $Z_j$ and preserves every $Z_k$ for $k\ne j$, using respectively one and two anticommutation sign changes. Thus this unitary is a bijection between the joint eigenspaces with sign vectors differing only at coordinate $j$. By successive flips all $2^m$ joint eigenspaces have the same positive dimension. Summing their dimensions proves the assertion.

The bound is attained. With the explicit Pauli matrices \[ X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\quad Z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}, \] put on $m$ qubits \[ U_{2j-1}=Z^{\otimes(j-1)}\otimes X\otimes I^{\otimes(m-j)}, \quad U_{2j}=Z^{\otimes(j-1)}\otimes Y\otimes I^{\otimes(m-j)}. \] Each is Hermitian and squares to $I$. The two at the same position anticommute because $XY=-YX$. For two at different positions, the earlier $X$ or $Y$ encounters one $Z$ at its position, contributing one minus sign; every other tensor factor commutes. Thus all distinct pairs anticommute. The ambient dimension is $2^m$, as required. The case $m=0$ is the empty relation system with arbitrary positive dimension.

The failed passage from relations to a query tradeoff

The relation system has $2m$ observables and quadratically many pair relations, but this does not make it a tester with that many queries. A sequential measurement oracle supplies probabilities of products of positive measurement operators. It does not grant coherent access to the signed observables or to arbitrary polynomial matrix identities. Although an involution can be written as the difference of its two spectral projectors, expanding a relation into signed words is not by itself an accept/reject experiment with perfect completeness and controlled soundness. No such reduction is supplied here. Nor does the exact common-eigenspace proof establish stability under approximate anticommutation: its exact projectors and exact unitary bijections would require quantitative replacements. Finally, the finite relation calculation says nothing about the infinite-dimensional tracial formulation needed for the source's stated deep corollary.
