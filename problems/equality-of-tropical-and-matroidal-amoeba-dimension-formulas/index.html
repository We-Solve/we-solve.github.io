Math-6641

Equality of Tropical and Matroidal Amoeba-Dimension Formulas

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$, write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and let $M$ be a loopless matroid on $[n]$. Its rank function is $\operatorname{rk}_M(A)=\max\{|I|:I\subseteq A\text{ is independent in }M\}$; looplessness means $\operatorname{rk}_M(\{j\})=1$ for every $j\in[n]$.

Interpret the matroid-fan support as the unprojectivized Bergman support \[ F=\left\{w\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\text{for every circuit }C\text{ of }M,\ \min_{j\in C}w_j\text{ is attained at least twice}\right\}. \] Here a circuit is a minimally dependent subset. This uses the minimum convention for the Bergman support; the supplied record does not explicitly specify its fan convention. In particular, $F$ is taken in ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, without quotienting by the all-ones direction.

A rationally spanned subspace is a real linear subspace $U\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$ having a basis in ${\mathbb{Q}}^n$. Define its Minkowski sum with $F$ by \[ U+F=\{u+f:u\in U,\ f\in F\}. \] For such a polyhedral set, $\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}(U+F)$ denotes its polyhedral dimension, namely the largest dimension of its polyhedral pieces, rather than the dimension of its linear span.

The proposed statement is that, for every such $M$, \[ \min_{\substack{U\leq{\mathbb{R}}^n\\U\text{ has a basis in }{\mathbb{Q}}^n}} \left(2\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}(U+F)-\dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}U\right) = \min_{\substack{1\leq k\leq n\\[n]=P_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup P_k\\P_i\neq\varnothing\ (1\leq i\leq k)}} \sum_{i=1}^{k}\left(2\operatorname{rk}_M(P_i)-1\right). \] Thus the right-hand minimum ranges over all partitions into nonempty blocks, with the number of blocks allowed to vary. The source states that the inequality from left to right follows by choosing $U=\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{R}}}\{\mathbf{1}_{P_1},\ldots,\mathbf{1}_{P_k}\}$, where $(\mathbf{1}_{P_i})_j=1$ for $j\in P_i$ and is zero otherwise; it asks for the converse.

The nonempty-ground-set assumption and the explicit interpretation of matroid-fan support above require confirmation against the intended source conventions. No empty-ground-set formulation is supplied.

Short English statement

For every loopless matroid on a nonempty finite ground set, ask whether minimizing a dimension expression over rationally spanned subspaces gives the same value as minimizing a rank expression over partitions into nonempty blocks. The supplied dated assessment reports the equality for complex-realizable matroids and describes the non-realizable case as conjectural; these are supplied curation claims requiring independent review.
