Algebraic Rigidity of Higher-Rank Anosov Actions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k\geq 2$ be an integer, let $G$ be either ${\mathbb{R}}^k$ or ${\mathbb{Z}}^k$, and let $M$ be a compact smooth manifold. A $C^\infty$ action is a smooth map $\alpha:G\times M\to M$ satisfying $\alpha(0,x)=x$ and $\alpha(g+h,x)=\alpha(g,\alpha(h,x))$. Write $\alpha_g(x)=\alpha(g,x)$.

The supplied report calls such an action Anosov if there exists $g\in G$ such that $\alpha_g$ is normally hyperbolic with respect to the orbit foliation $\mathcal O$ of $G$. Normal hyperbolicity here refers to invariant contracting and expanding directions transverse to the orbit directions, with their contraction and expansion dominating the dynamics tangent to the orbits. The supplied passage stops at the beginning of the tangent-splitting definition, so its complete technical conventions are unavailable.

For two actions $\alpha$ and $\beta$ of the same group, a $C^\infty$ conjugacy is a smooth diffeomorphism $h$ satisfying \[ h\circ\alpha_g=\beta_g\circ h\qquad\text{for every }g\in G. \] A factor relation is expressed by an equivariant surjection $\pi:M\to N$, satisfying $\pi\circ\alpha_g=\gamma_g\circ\pi$ for a factor action $\gamma$. This general description does not specify the additional regularity or nontriviality requirements intended for a $C^\infty$ rank-one factor.

Katok--Spatzier conjecture, as supplied. Every $C^\infty$ Anosov action of ${\mathbb{R}}^k$ or ${\mathbb{Z}}^k$, with $k\geq2$, on a compact manifold, having no $C^\infty$ rank-one factors, is $C^\infty$-conjugate to an algebraic action after passage to finite covers.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define precisely which factors count as rank-one factors, which algebraic actions are allowed, or what operations are included in passage to finite covers. In particular, it does not specify whether a finite-index restriction of a ${\mathbb{Z}}^k$ action is permitted when forming a lift. It also does not state local freeness or boundary conventions for $M$, despite using an orbit foliation. These conventions must be recovered from the cited source before this can be treated as a fully specified classification assertion; no additional hypotheses or choices are imposed here.

Short English statement

The supplied Katok--Spatzier conjecture asks whether every smooth higher-rank Anosov action on a compact manifold, without smooth rank-one factors, becomes smoothly conjugate to an algebraic action after passing to finite covers. The precise meanings of rank-one factor, algebraic action, and the permitted covering operations remain unspecified in the supplied excerpt.
