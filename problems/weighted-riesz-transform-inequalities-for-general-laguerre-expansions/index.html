Math-6514

Weighted Riesz Transform Inequalities for General Laguerre Expansions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 1$, write ${\mathbb{R}}_+^n=(0,\infty)^n$ and let $m=(m_1,\ldots,m_n)$ denote the type parameter of the intended Laguerre expansion. Set \[ d\mu_m(r)=\prod_{k=1}^n r_k^{2m_k+1}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} r_k, \qquad w_{m,p}(r)=\prod_{k=1}^n r_k^{m_k(p-2)}. \] The symbol $R_{j,m}$, for $1\leq j\leq n$, denotes the Riesz transform associated with that expansion in the supplied statement. The record does not give its operator definition, normalization, or initial domain, and does not specify the numerical range of the coordinates of an admissible arbitrary type $m$.

Conjecture as supplied, with unresolved scope. For every $p\in(1,\infty)$, does there exist a finite constant $C_p>0$, depending only on $p$, such that for every $n\geq1$, every admissible type $m$ in the intended arbitrary-type Laguerre family, every $j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and every function $f$ in the intended operator domain with finite right-hand side, \[ \int_{{\mathbb{R}}_+^n}|R_{j,m}f(r)|^p w_{m,p}(r)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_m(r) \leq C_p \int_{{\mathbb{R}}_+^n}|f(r)|^p w_{m,p}(r)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_m(r)? \] In particular, the constant is required to be independent of $n$ and $m$. The supplied record leaves the admissible type set and function domain unspecified; these must be recovered from the cited formulation before this becomes a fully specified operator inequality. No boundary conditions or operator realization at the coordinate hyperplanes are supplied.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether each Laguerre Riesz transform satisfies the displayed weighted $L^p$ inequality for arbitrary Laguerre type, with a constant depending only on $p$, uniformly in dimension and type. The type range and precise operator domain remain unresolved. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
