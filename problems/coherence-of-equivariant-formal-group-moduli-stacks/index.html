Coherence of Equivariant Formal-Group Moduli Stacks

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a compact Lie group, and let $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{FG}}^G$ denote the equivariant formal-group moduli stack intended by the supplied source record. The record does not supply its construction or a comodule presentation. For the intended presentation, define $\mathcal C_G$ to be the full category of sheaves corresponding to comodules whose underlying modules are finitely presented. Here finite presentation means having finitely many generators and finitely many relations, with any grading conventions taken from the intended presentation. The coefficient ring, comodule structure, and grading conventions are not specified in the supplied record.

Conjecture (supplied corrected formulation). For every compact Lie group $G$, the category $\mathcal C_G$ is abelian. This is the meaning of coherence asserted for $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{FG}}^G$ in the record. Explicitly, $\mathcal C_G$ is to be additive, every morphism $f\colon M\to N$ is to have a kernel and a cokernel in $\mathcal C_G$, and the canonical morphism \[ \operatorname{coker}(\ker f\longrightarrow M) \longrightarrow \ker(N\longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}f) \] is to be an isomorphism.

Unresolved source qualification. This formulation follows the supplied statement and clean statement. The supplied original excerpt instead states a conjecture about universal support and a Balmer-spectrum homeomorphism for finite $G$-spectra; it does not state the coherence conjecture. Thus the excerpt alone does not establish the intended stack, its comodule presentation, or the scope of the coherence assertion. In particular, the quantifier over all compact Lie groups is retained from the corrected formulation and requires source verification.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every compact Lie group $G$, the comodules representing finitely presented sheaves on the intended equivariant formal-group stack form an abelian category. The stack and presentation are not defined in the record, and the original excerpt concerns a different conjecture. This is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical and source review.
