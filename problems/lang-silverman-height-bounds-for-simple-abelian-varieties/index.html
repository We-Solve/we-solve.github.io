Math-962

Lang–Silverman Height Bounds for Simple Abelian Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a number field, that is, a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $g\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $g\geq 1$. For an abelian variety $A/K$, write $A(K)$ for its group of $K$-rational points. A point $P\in A(K)$ is nontorsion if $[n]P\neq 0$ for every integer $n\geq 1$. A line bundle $L$ on $A$ is symmetric if $[-1]^*L\simeq L$. For an ample symmetric line bundle $L$, its Néron--Tate height is \[ \widehat h_{A,L}(P)=\lim_{n\to\infty}4^{-n}h_L([2^n]P), \] where $h_L$ is a logarithmic Weil height associated with $L$, with a fixed height normalization. Write $h_F(A/K)$ for the Faltings height appearing in the supplied record.

An abelian variety is $K$-simple if it has no nonzero proper abelian subvariety defined over $K$, and is geometrically simple if this holds after extension to an algebraic closure of $K$. The supplied record uses "simple" without explicitly distinguishing these meanings. It also does not specify the stable versus nonstable convention for $h_F(A/K)$ or the height normalizations. These qualifications remain unresolved in the following source formulation.

The supplied Lang--Silverman conjecture asks: for every integer $g\geq 1$ and every number field $K$, does there exist a real constant $c_2(K,g)>0$, depending only on $K$ and $g$, such that for every simple abelian variety $A/K$ of dimension $g$, every ample symmetric line bundle $L$ on $A$, and every nontorsion point $P\in A(K)$, \[ \widehat h_{A,L}(P)\geq c_2(K,g)\max\{h_F(A/K),1\}? \] In particular, the asserted constant is uniform in $A$, $L$, and $P$. The unresolved conventions above require clarification before this becomes a fully unambiguous statement.

Short English statement

For each fixed number field $K$ and dimension $g\geq 1$, the supplied conjecture asks for a positive constant that uniformly bounds the canonical height of every nontorsion rational point on every simple $g$-dimensional abelian variety below by that constant times the larger of its Faltings height and $1$, for every ample symmetric line bundle. The meanings of simplicity and the Faltings-height convention require clarification.
