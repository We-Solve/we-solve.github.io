Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb C_v$ denote the ambient valued field appearing in the supplied source, with its $v$-topology. Let $K\subseteq\mathbb C_v$ be a subfield that is locally compact in the induced topology. Let $\overline K\subseteq\mathbb C_v$ be an algebraic closure of $K$, embedded over $K$, and assume that \[ \operatorname{cl}_{\mathbb C_v}(\overline K)=\mathbb C_v. \] Here $\operatorname{cl}_{\mathbb C_v}$ denotes topological closure. Let $K(z)$ be the field of rational functions in one variable over $K$. A rational function $f\in K(z)$ induces a self-map of $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb C_v)$; if $f=P/Q$ with coprime polynomials $P,Q\in K[z]$, its degree is $\max\{\deg P,\deg Q\}$.

The conclusion uses the source's notion of a wandering domain. Under the usual interpretation in terms of Fatou components, fix the intended analytic dynamical space and its Fatou set $\mathcal F_v(f)$. For a Fatou component $U$, let $U_n$ be the Fatou component containing $f^n(U)$, where $f^0$ is the identity and $f^n$ is the $n$-fold iterate for $n\geq1$. A wandering domain is a component whose forward component itinerary is not eventually periodic.

Conjecture 36 (as supplied). For every $K\subseteq\mathbb C_v$ satisfying the local compactness and density hypotheses above, and every $f\in K(z)$ with $\deg(f)\geq2$, the map $f$ has no wandering domains. In the Fatou-component interpretation, this says that for every Fatou component $U$, there exist integers $m\geq0$ and $p\geq1$ such that \[ U_{n+p}=U_n\qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq m. \] Unresolved qualification. The supplied excerpt does not define $\mathbb C_v$, specify its valuation or characteristic, or identify the analytic space and notion of domain used in the conclusion. In particular, it does not specify a classical or Berkovich interpretation, or whether another non-Archimedean domain convention is intended. The component formulation above is conditional on the intended convention; the excerpt alone does not determine a fully specified analytic formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asserts that a rational map of degree at least two over a locally compact subfield $K\subseteq\mathbb C_v$, whose embedded algebraic closure is dense in $\mathbb C_v$, has no wandering domains. The ambient field and the precise analytic meaning of a domain require clarification from the source context.
