Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $n$ be an integer with $n>1$. A pro-$p$ group is a topological group that is an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. Let $F$ be the free pro-$p$ group of rank $n$: it has distinguished topological generators $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ such that, for every pro-$p$ group $G$ and every $(g_1,\ldots,g_n)\in G^n$, there is a unique continuous homomorphism $F\to G$ sending $x_i$ to $g_i$ for each $i$.

Write $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(F)$ for the group of continuous automorphisms of $F$, equipped with its usual profinite topology. A neighborhood basis of the identity consists of the kernels of the maps \[ \operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(F)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(F/U), \] where $U$ ranges over open characteristic subgroups of $F$. This is the conventional topological interpretation of the source's notation $\mathrm{Aut}\,F$; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify the topology.

For a profinite group $H$ and a prime $\ell$, its continuous $\ell$-cohomological dimension $\operatorname{cd}_{\ell}(H)$ is the least nonnegative integer $d$, if one exists, such that \[ H^q_{\mathrm{cont}}(H,M)=0 \quad\text{for every integer }q>d \text{ and every discrete continuous }H\text{-module }M \text{ whose underlying abelian group is }\ell\text{-primary torsion}. \] If there is no such integer, set $\operatorname{cd}_{\ell}(H)=\infty$. The full continuous cohomological dimension is $\operatorname{cd}(H)=\sup_{\ell\text{ prime}}\operatorname{cd}_{\ell}(H)$.

Question (catalogue entry). For every prime $p$ and every free pro-$p$ group $F$ of finite rank $n>1$, does $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(F)$ possess an open subgroup of finite cohomological dimension?

Unresolved convention. The supplied record does not specify whether "cohomological dimension" means full continuous cohomological dimension or continuous $p$-cohomological dimension. With the definitions above, the respective precise readings ask for an open subgroup $H\leq\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{cont}}(F)$ satisfying $\operatorname{cd}(H)<\infty$ or $\operatorname{cd}_p(H)<\infty$. The intended convention requires source verification; neither reading is silently selected here.

Short English statement

For a free pro-$p$ group on finitely many generators, with at least two generators, ask whether its continuous automorphism group has an open subgroup of finite cohomological dimension. The supplied record labels the problem open. The intended cohomological-dimension convention remains to be verified.
