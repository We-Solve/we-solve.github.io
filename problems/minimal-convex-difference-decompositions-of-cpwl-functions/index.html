Minimal Convex Difference Decompositions of CPWL Functions

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

For continuous piecewise-linear $f$, each affine region carries a linear formula. A convex-difference representation is $f=g-h$ with convex piecewise-linear $g,h$; minimizing pieces requires counting the regions according to the source's convention.

A continuous piecewise-linear function is continuous and agrees with an affine function on each member of a finite polyhedral partition. A difference-of-convex decomposition writes it as $g-h$ with both $g$ and $h$ convex.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given a continuous piecewise-linear function $f$, decompose it as $f=g-h$ with $g$ and $h$ convex continuous piecewise-linear functions having as few linear pieces as possible. Construct a finite procedure that finds a globally minimal decomposition in arbitrary dimension. [S2]
Scope qualification.
The objective counting $g$ and $h$ pieces, encoding, and treatment of compatible subdivisions are not specified. The target is global across decompositions, not optimality on one fixed complex.

Short English statement

Find a finite globally optimal procedure for decomposing a piecewise-linear function as a difference of two convex ones.
