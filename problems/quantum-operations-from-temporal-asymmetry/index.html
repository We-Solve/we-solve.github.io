The supplied record does not specify mathematical definitions of an "arrow of time," the relevant "operations," their "quantum features," their descriptions in "common language (classical terms)," or information extracted from the past. Does the arrow of time enforce the quantum features of operations that are described by common language (classical terms), extracted from past information?

Short English statement

The question asks whether temporal asymmetry forces quantum characteristics on operations described in classical language using past information. The supplied record provides neither the definitions nor the logical relationship needed for a precise mathematical statement, and contains conflicting descriptions of whether this is an open problem.
