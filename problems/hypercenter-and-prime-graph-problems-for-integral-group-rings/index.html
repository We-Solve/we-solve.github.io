Math-1065

Hypercenter and Prime-Graph Problems for Integral Group Rings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, let ${\mathbb{Z}} G$ be its integral group ring and let $U=U({\mathbb{Z}} G)$ be its full unit group, containing the canonical copy of $G$. Define the finite-conjugacy center and normalizer by \[\Delta(U)=\{u\in U:[U:C_U(u)]<\infty\},\qquad N_U(G)=\{u\in U:uGu^{-1}=G\}.\] Thus finite conjugacy concerns the size of a conjugacy class, not finite order of $u$. Define the upper central series by $Z_0(U)=1$, $Z_{\gamma+1}(U)/Z_\gamma(U)=Z(U/Z_\gamma(U))$, and union at limit ordinals. Its stable terminal term is the hypercenter, denoted $Z_\infty(U)$ in the source. The first two questions are whether \[Z_\infty(U)\subseteq\Delta(U),\qquad\Delta(U)\subseteq N_U(G).\] The extract does not state whether this first pair assumes finite $G$; that assumption is explicit only in the next pair.

Now let $G$ be finite and define the normalized units by \[V({\mathbb{Z}} G)=\ker\left(\varepsilon:U({\mathbb{Z}} G)\to\{1,-1\}\right),\qquad\varepsilon\left(\sum_g a_gg\right)=\sum_g a_g.\] A section of a group is a quotient $B/N$ with $N\trianglelefteq B$ and $B$ a subgroup. Is every simple section of every finite subgroup $A\leq V({\mathbb{Z}} G)$ isomorphic to a section of $G$? Explicitly, if $N\trianglelefteq B\leq A$ and $B/N$ is simple, must there exist $N'\trianglelefteq B'\leq G$ with $B/N\simeq B'/N'$? The source does not restrict simple to nonabelian simple groups. Finally, for any group $K$, define its prime graph $\Pi(K)$ to have as vertices the primes $p$ for which $K$ contains an element of order $p$, with distinct $p,q$ adjacent when $K$ contains an element of order $pq$. Does \[\Pi(V({\mathbb{Z}} G))=\Pi(G)\] hold, including both vertex sets and edges? This definition uses finite-order elements even though the full unit group is infinite. The supplied 2026-08-21 literature triage labels the record open and reports many known families but no general answer to the prime-graph and subgroup-section problems.

Short English statement

Ask for two inclusions among the hypercenter, finite-conjugacy center, and normalizer in the full unit group. Separately, for finite $G$, ask whether simple sections of finite normalized-unit subgroups occur as sections of $G$ and whether their prime graphs coincide.
