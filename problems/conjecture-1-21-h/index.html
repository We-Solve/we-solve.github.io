Conjecture 1.21 — (H.

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $L\subset S^3$ be a hyperbolic link, meaning that its complement $X_L=S^3\setminus L$ carries the complete finite-volume hyperbolic structure intended by the source. Write $\operatorname{vol}(X_L)$ for its hyperbolic volume and $\operatorname{CS}(X_L)$ for its Chern--Simons invariant, with normalization and lift still to be specified. Let $J_N(L)$, $N\ge1$, denote exactly the colored-Jones-derived complex sequence used in the source; its normalization and evaluation parameter are not given in the record.

Conjecture 1.21, attributed to H. Murakami, J. Murakami, M. Okamoto, T. Takata and Y. Yokota [297], states that for every such hyperbolic link, with an appropriate choice of branch of the logarithm, \[ 2\pi i\,\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{\log J_N(L)}{N} =\operatorname{CS}(X_L)+i\operatorname{vol}(X_L). \] It states moreover that \[ \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{J_{N+1}(L)}{J_N(L)} =\exp\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{CS}(X_L)+i\operatorname{vol}(X_L)}{2\pi i}\right). \tag{7} \] These are two assertions, not an inference from the first limit to the ratio limit. Their meaning requires a specification of $J_N$, eventual nonvanishing where logs and ratios are used, a compatible logarithm-branch prescription, and the normalization and representative of the possibly period-valued Chern--Simons invariant. The displayed order $\operatorname{CS}+i\operatorname{vol}$, factor $2\pi i$, and exponential denominator are preserved exactly from the supplied statement; no alternative complex-volume convention has been substituted.

Short English statement

For every hyperbolic link, the specified colored-Jones sequence is conjectured to have both a logarithmic asymptotic and a consecutive-term ratio determined by Chern--Simons invariant plus $i$ times hyperbolic volume. The supplied partial-progress assessment says this is proved for some knots and open in general, without listing the knots or supplying those proofs.
