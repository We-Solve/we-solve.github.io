Problem 11.4 — Characterize those elements of ˆA(∅)conn which are of the form log Z L M O(M ) for integral/rational homology 3-spheres.

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal A_n(\varnothing;{\mathbb{Q}})$ be the rational space of closed vertex-oriented trivalent Jacobi diagrams with $2n$ vertices modulo antisymmetry and IHX, with multiplication $\sqcup$ given by disjoint union. Write \[ \widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing;{\mathbb{Q}}) =\prod_{n\geq0}\mathcal A_n(\varnothing;{\mathbb{Q}}),\qquad \widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing;{\mathbb{Q}})_{\mathrm{conn}} =\prod_{n\geq1}\mathcal A_n(\varnothing;{\mathbb{Q}})_{\mathrm{conn}}, \] where the connected subspace is spanned by diagrams with connected graph. For a group-like LMO invariant normalized to have degree-zero term $1$, its logarithm is the formal degree-completed series \[ \log Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M) =\sum_{r\geq1}\frac{(-1)^{r+1}}r \left(Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M)-1\right)^{\sqcup r}, \] which lies in the completed connected subspace.

An integral, respectively rational, homology $3$-sphere has the homology of $S^3$ over ${\mathbb{Z}}$, respectively ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The problem is to characterize the two images \[ \left\{\log Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M): M\text{ an integral homology }3\text{-sphere}\right\},\qquad \left\{\log Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}(M): M\text{ a rational homology }3\text{-sphere}\right\} \] inside $\widehat{\mathcal A}(\varnothing)_{\mathrm{conn}}$: give necessary and sufficient conditions for a connected diagram series to occur in each case. The excerpt does not explicitly fix coefficients, orientation/equivalence conventions, or the normalization of $Z_{\mathrm{LMO}}$; the rational, unit-normalized interpretation above specifies the usual formal logarithm and must be checked against the source convention, especially for rational homology spheres.

Short English statement

Determine exactly which completed series of connected Jacobi diagrams occur as logarithms of LMO invariants of integral homology spheres, and which occur for rational homology spheres.
