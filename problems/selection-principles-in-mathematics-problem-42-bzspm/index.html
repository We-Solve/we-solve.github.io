Selection Principles in Mathematics — Problem 42 ([BZSPM])

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record omits definitions of its selection principles and the precise ambient category of analytic groups. The following is a formulation under explicit standard conventions; agreement with the original source requires review.

Let ${\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. A Polish space is a separable completely metrizable space. Here, an analytic space means a continuous image of a Polish space. No additional separation or metrizability assumption on the image is imposed by this definition; whether the source intends such assumptions is unresolved.

For a topological space $X$, let $\mathcal O(X)$ be the family of open covers of $X$ that do not contain $X$ itself as a member. Let $\Omega(X)$ consist of those $\mathcal U\in\mathcal O(X)$ such that every finite subset of $X$ is contained in some member of $\mathcal U$. Let $\Gamma(X)$ consist of those infinite $\mathcal U\in\mathcal O(X)$ such that, for every $x\in X$, the set $\{U\in\mathcal U:x\notin U\}$ is finite.

For families $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ of covers of $X$, the property $\mathsf S_1(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ in $\mathcal A$, there are choices $U_n\in\mathcal U_n$ such that $\{U_n:n\in{\mathbb{N}}\}\in\mathcal B$. The property $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ means that for every such sequence there are finite, possibly empty, families $\mathcal V_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that $\bigcup_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}\mathcal V_n\in\mathcal B$.

Let $G$ be a topological group with identity $e$, and use left translates. For an open neighborhood $U$ of $e$, put \[ \mathcal O(U)=\{gU:g\in G\},\qquad \Omega(U)=\{FU:\varnothing\ne F\subseteq G\text{ finite}\}, \qquad FU=\bigcup_{f\in F}fU. \] Define \[ \mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}}(G) =\{\mathcal O(U):U\text{ is an open neighborhood of }e,\ U\ne G\}, \] \[ \Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}}(G) =\{\Omega(U):U\text{ is an open neighborhood of }e, \ FU\ne G\text{ for every finite }F\subseteq G\}. \] Thus the latter definition excludes neighborhoods whose finitely many left translates cover $G$. These cover conventions are explicit interpretive choices because the supplied excerpt does not define them.

The question associated with permanent UnsolvedMath ID $800614$, source code $\texttt{catalogue entry}$, is whether there exists an analytic topological group $G$ such that \[ G\models\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}\left(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}}(G),\mathcal O(G)\right) \quad\text{and}\quad G\not\models\mathsf S_1\left(\Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}}(G),\Gamma(G)\right). \] The intended separation and metrizability restrictions on $G$ remain to be confirmed.

As background attributed to $[\mathrm{BZSPM}]$, the supplied record asserts that, for analytic groups, \[ G\not\models\mathsf S_1(\Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}},\Gamma) \ \Longrightarrow\ G\times G\not\models\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}},\mathcal O), \] where $G\times G$ has its product topology and coordinatewise group operation. It also asserts that, within analytic groups, \[ \mathsf S_1(\Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}},\Gamma) \ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathsf S_1(\Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}},\Omega), \] and that, within analytic abelian groups, \[ \mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{nbd}},\mathcal O) \ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathsf S_1(\Omega_{\mathrm{nbd}},\Gamma). \] These are supplied background assertions, not independently verified results in this draft. The record labels the existence question for general analytic groups as open.

Short English statement

Does an analytic topological group satisfy the finite-selection property for neighborhood-translate covers while failing the single-selection property from neighborhood-generated $\omega$-covers to $\gamma$-covers? The supplied record attributes an equivalence for analytic abelian groups to $[\mathrm{BZSPM}]$ and labels the general question open. The precise ambient category and cover conventions require source review.
