Math-1528

Semi-local versus tame-at-infinity generating families

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Question 1.2. Which Legendrian submanifolds $L$ admit generating functions with prescribed behavior at infinity, typically quadratic at infinity, and what changes for other asymptotic conditions? The source also mentions a Giroux criterion without infinity restrictions, explained by Yasha Eliashberg, and a formal infinite-dimensional action-functional construction. These are distinct categories and are not identified with global quadratic-at-infinity existence.

For a precise standard model, assume an ambient jet space $J^1B=T^*B\times{\mathbb{R}}$ with contact form ${\,\mathrm{d}} z-p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} q$. A finite-dimensional generating family $F:B\times{\mathbb{R}}^N\to{\mathbb{R}}$ has $\partial_\eta F$ transverse to zero and generates \[ \Sigma_F=\{\partial_\eta F=0\},\qquad (q,\eta)\longmapsto(q,\partial_qF(q,\eta),F(q,\eta)). \] For an embedded generated $L$, require this map to identify $\Sigma_F$ with $L$. A generating-family germ is defined near its fiber-critical locus. It is different from a globally defined family satisfying an asymptotic model. For compact $B$, one common quadratic-at-infinity convention is $F(q,\eta)=Q(\eta)$ outside a compact subset, with $Q$ a fixed nondegenerate quadratic form; a linear model uses a nonzero linear form. Noncompact bases, linear-quadratic models, equivalences by fiber diffeomorphism and stabilization, and fiberwise tameness require separate conventions. The source does not fix them or its ambient jet-space hypotheses.

Supplied obstruction-level example. In standard contact ${\mathbb{R}}^3=J^1{\mathbb{R}}$, let $U$ be the maximal Legendrian unknot with Thurston--Bennequin number $\operatorname{tb}(U)=-1$ and rotation number $\operatorname{rot}(U)=0$. Let $S_+$ and $S_-$ be positive and negative stabilizations. The aid asserts \[ \operatorname{tb}(S_\pm U)=-2,\quad \operatorname{rot}(S_\pm U)=\pm1, \qquad \operatorname{tb}(S_+S_-U)=-3,\quad \operatorname{rot}(S_+S_-U)=0. \] Under the stated Giroux--Latour semi-local stable-Lagrangian-Gauss-map criterion, the single stabilizations have no generating-family germ, while the opposite double stabilization has such a germ. Nevertheless the latter has no global linear-at-infinity generating family in the aid's convention, because its unknot Thurston--Bennequin number is not maximal. Thus vanishing of the germ obstruction does not imply global asymptotically controlled existence. This does not identify linear-at-infinity with quadratic-at-infinity, nor make rotation the only obstruction in every ambient space or dimension.

The aid reports a May 2026 Courte--Shende preprint giving a homotopy-pullback classification of fiberwise tame generating functions. No exact space, diagram, hypotheses, title or preprint identifier is supplied, so this is retained as a dated literature report rather than a fully stated classification theorem. Likewise the source's infinite-dimensional action functional has no specified analytic domain or compactness data and does not settle finite-dimensional tame existence.

Theme 1.3, Lisa Traynor. Clarify the relation between polynomial invariants obtained by Morse theory of a suitable generating family and those from linearized contact homology. A generating-family difference function has the form $F(q,\eta_2)-F(q,\eta_1)$; its homology requires suitable infinity conditions and grading conventions. Linearized contact homology requires an augmentation and its own coefficient/grading choices. The source deliberately leaves "suitable", the contact-homology definition and even the precision of the comparison tentative. No equality for every generating family or every augmentation is inferred from its comment that the invariants encode similar information.

Short English statement

The existence question depends on whether generating families are germs, global quadratic/linear models or fiberwise tame objects. The supplied unknot examples separate the semi-local Gauss-map obstruction from global infinity restrictions. The accompanying Traynor theme asks for a precise comparison of generating-family and linearized-contact-homology invariants, with its choices still unspecified.
