Definite Determinantal Multiples of Hyperbolic Polynomials

Definitions and mathematical statement

The following is the conventional homogeneous real formulation of the supplied conjecture. The source record does not explicitly specify its normalization or boundary conventions; those choices are stated here and require confirmation against the cited report.

Let $n,d\geq 1$, and let $h\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ be homogeneous of degree $d$. A polynomial $h$ is hyperbolic with respect to $e\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$ if $h(e)>0$ and, for every $x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$, the polynomial $t\mapsto h(te-x)$ has only real roots. Here positivity at $e$ is a normalization convention. Define its closed hyperbolicity cone by \[ C_h(e)=\left\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\text{every root of }t\mapsto h(te-x)\text{ is nonnegative}\right\}. \] Thus this convention includes the boundary. Apply the same definition to any positive-degree homogeneous polynomial hyperbolic with respect to $e$. A positive constant polynomial is also allowed as a hyperbolic polynomial of degree zero, with cone ${\mathbb{R}}^n$.

A homogeneous polynomial $p$ of degree $m\geq1$ has a definite determinantal representation with respect to $e$ if there exist real symmetric matrices $A_1,\ldots,A_n\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m\times m}$ such that \[ p(x)=\det\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_iA_i\right) \quad\text{for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^n e_iA_i\succ0. \] Here $\succ0$ means positive definite.

Conjecture (supplied formulation of the generalized Lax conjecture). For every $n,d\geq1$, every homogeneous $h\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ of degree $d$, and every $e\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$ with respect to which $h$ is hyperbolic, there exist an integer $r\geq0$ and a homogeneous polynomial $q\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ of degree $r$, hyperbolic with respect to the same $e$, such that \[ C_h(e)\subseteq C_q(e) \] and $qh$ has a definite determinantal representation with respect to $e$. Explicitly, there must exist real symmetric matrices $A_1,\ldots,A_n\in{\mathbb{R}}^{(d+r)\times(d+r)}$ satisfying \[ q(x)h(x)=\det\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_iA_i\right) \quad\text{for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^n e_iA_i\succ0. \] The inclusion need not be strict. No bound on $r$ is supplied. Homogeneity, real coefficients, positive normalization, real symmetric matrices, definiteness at $e$, the closed-cone convention, and allowance of constant multipliers are standard conventions made explicit here; the supplied statement does not define them. Its treatment of constant input polynomials $h$ is unspecified.

Short English statement

Given a real homogeneous polynomial hyperbolic in a direction $e$, can one multiply it by another polynomial hyperbolic in the same direction, whose hyperbolicity cone contains the original cone, so that the product is the determinant of a real symmetric linear matrix pencil positive definite at $e$? This draft uses explicit standard conventions that require source confirmation. The supplied dated curation labels the problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
