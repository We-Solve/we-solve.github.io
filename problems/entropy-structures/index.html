Math-1915

Entropy structures

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a diffeomorphism $f:M\to M$ of a compact Riemannian manifold, let $\mathcal M_f(M)$ be the compact convex space of $f$-invariant Borel probability measures. Its entropy function is $\mu\mapsto h_\mu(f)$, the measure-theoretic entropy. The entropy structure is finer data: in the standard theory it is an equivalence class of specified sequences of entropy approximations $(h_k)$ on $\mathcal M_f(M)$, converging to this entropy function, with equivalence given by uniform domination in both directions. Namely, for two admissible sequences on the same base, domination of $(v_k)$ by $(u_j)$ means \[ \forall k\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists j\ \forall\mu: \quad v_k(\mu)\le u_j(\mu)+\varepsilon. \] Only the approximation sequences allowed by entropy-structure theory are intended; an arbitrary pointwise approximation to entropy is not a definition of an entropy structure. The supplied excerpt does not give their construction or axioms.

For each regularity $1\le r\le\infty$, characterize precisely the entropy structures realizable by $C^r$ diffeomorphisms of compact Riemannian manifolds. In abstract terms, determine the class \[ \{\mathcal H(f): f:M\to M\text{ is a }C^r\text{ diffeomorphism of a compact Riemannian manifold }M\}, \] where $\mathcal H(f)$ denotes its entropy structure. Comparisons of abstract structures ordinarily include their invariant-measure base, with a specified affine identification of bases when necessary. The source does not spell out that realization-equivalence convention, boundary or dimension restrictions, or the meaning of $C^r$ at noninteger exponents if those are included. For finite integer $r$, diffeomorphism means that both the map and inverse are $C^r$; $r=\infty$ denotes smoothness.

The supplied research summary reports a characterization in the general topological entropy-structure setting, attributes it to Boyle--Downarowicz, and reports surface-diffeomorphism and entropy-structure progress by Burguet (2011, 2015), including what it calls "universal two-resolution covers". It nevertheless describes the full smooth regularity-constrained classification as incomplete, especially for $r\ge2$ and higher dimensions. These are supplied attributions without precise theorem statements; a general topological realization result is not asserted here to solve smooth realization. The 2008 source open label has no recorded later authoritative resolution check.

Short English statement

For each differentiability class $C^r$, identify exactly which entropy structures can arise from diffeomorphisms of compact manifolds. The full structure of entropy approximation is intended, rather than just an entropy value or function; the source reports partial smooth results and leaves the precise realization conventions to the original theory.
