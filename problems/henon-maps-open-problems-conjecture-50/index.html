Math-604

Henon Maps: Open Problems — Conjecture 50

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a number field, fix an algebraic closure $\overline K$, let $B\geq 1$ be a degree bound, and let $d\geq 2$ be an integer. For $P=(x,y)\in(\overline K)^2$, set $K(P)=K(x,y)$. Write $\mathcal H_d(K)$ for the family referred to in the supplied record as generalized Hénon maps of degree $d$ over $K$. For $f\in\mathcal H_d(K)$, let $h(f)$ denote the height of its tuple of coefficients, and retain the notation $\hat h_f(P)$ used in the source.

The supplied excerpt does not define the precise family $\mathcal H_d(K)$, the function $\hat h_f$, or the coefficient-height conventions. Consequently, the following statement retains these source-dependent objects and requires their definitions to be checked against the cited source.

The conjecture asserts that there exist constants $\epsilon>0$, $C\in{\mathbb{R}}$, and $N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$, depending only on $K,B,d$, such that for every $f\in\mathcal H_d(K)$, \[ \#\left\{P\in(\overline K)^2: [K(P):K]\leq B\ \text{and}\ \hat h_f(P)<\epsilon h(f)+C \right\}\leq N. \] In particular, the same $\epsilon,C,N$ must work for every map in the indicated family. The height inequality is strict, whereas the field-degree bound is inclusive. The supplied record labels this assertion open; that label is not an independently verified mathematical assessment.

Short English statement

For fixed $K,B,d$, the conjecture asks for a uniform bound on the number of algebraic points of field degree at most $B$ whose height $\hat h_f$ is less than $\epsilon h(f)+C$, as the generalized Hénon map $f$ varies. The constants may depend on $K,B,d$, but not on $f$. The map family and height conventions remain subject to source clarification.
