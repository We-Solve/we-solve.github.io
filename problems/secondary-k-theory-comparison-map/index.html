Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns Lott's secondary K-group and uses the notation \[ G(M):=KR^0(M),\qquad D(M):=KR_d^0(M),\qquad T(M):=KR_{\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}}^{-1}(M). \] These are labels for the groups appearing in the source record, not independent definitions of them. The record does not specify the coefficient object $R$, the class of admissible spaces $M$, or the constructions defining these groups. It describes generators of $G(M)$ by triples $(V,g_V,\eta)$, but does not supply their admissibility conditions or the defining relations. The operations $\operatorname{cycl}$ and $a$ below likewise retain their source notation; their domains and definitions are not supplied.

The extracted assertion is that there is a well-defined group homomorphism \[ \Phi_M:G(M)\longrightarrow D(M), \qquad D(M)\cong T(M), \] whose value on every admissible generator is \[ \Phi_M\left([(V,g_V,\eta)]\right) =\operatorname{cycl}(V,g_V)-a(\eta). \] Here brackets denote the class of the indicated generator in the secondary K-group. Well-definedness requires that the displayed expression belong to $D(M)$ and respect every defining relation of $G(M)$. The supplied record does not provide the hypotheses or relations needed to state those requirements explicitly, nor does it explain the asserted identification $D(M)\cong T(M)$.

The supplied statement-verification field says that this extracted assertion is a construction and result proved in the cited report, rather than an unresolved question; it marks the statement as unrecoverable and supplies no clean replacement. Separately, the supplied dated literature assessment describes an existence part of Lott's conjecture as settled and a transfer-compatibility part as unproved. No transfer maps, compatibility equation, or hypotheses for that remaining part are supplied. Consequently, the displayed assertion preserves the extracted comparison-map claim, but the supplied evidence does not determine a precise remaining open problem.

Short English statement

The extracted claim sends each generator $(V,g_V,\eta)$ of Lott's secondary K-group to $\operatorname{cycl}(V,g_V)-a(\eta)$ in the indicated differential algebraic K-theory group. The supplied verification calls this a proved result. Its separate dated assessment mentions unresolved transfer compatibility, but gives insufficient definitions and hypotheses to formulate that question precisely.
