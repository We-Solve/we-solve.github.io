Linear Systems and Eigenvalue Problems — Problem 24

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ and $A\in{\mathbb{C}}^{n\times n}$. For a nonzero starting vector $b\in{\mathbb{C}}^n$ and an integer $1\leq m\leq n$, define the Krylov space \[ \mathcal K_m(A,b)=\operatorname{span}\{b,Ab,\ldots,A^{m-1}b\}. \] Write $d=\dim\mathcal K_m(A,b)$, and let $Q\in{\mathbb{C}}^{n\times d}$ satisfy $Q^*Q=I_d$ and $\operatorname{range}(Q)=\mathcal K_m(A,b)$. Set $H=Q^*AQ$. The source specifies a random starting vector but does not specify its probability law, the Krylov order $m$, or how to handle a zero starting vector. The actual dimension $d$ allows for linear dependence among the Krylov vectors.

Under the usual eigenvector condition-number interpretation, define \[ \kappa_V(H)=\inf\left\{\|V\|_2\|V^{-1}\|_2: V\in{\mathbb{C}}^{d\times d}\text{ is invertible and }V^{-1}HV\text{ is diagonal}\right\}, \] with $\kappa_V(H)=+\infty$ if $H$ is not diagonalizable. Here $\|\cdot\|_2$ is the operator norm induced by the Euclidean vector norm. This convention for $\kappa_V$ is a standard interpretation of notation that the supplied record does not define explicitly.

For $\varepsilon>0$, use the spectral-norm pseudospectrum \[ \Lambda_\varepsilon(H)=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:\sigma_{\min}(zI_d-H)\leq\varepsilon\}, \] where $\sigma_{\min}$ denotes the smallest singular value. Let $\operatorname{area}$ denote two-dimensional Lebesgue measure on ${\mathbb{C}}\cong{\mathbb{R}}^2$. The pseudospectral norm convention is also not specified in the source.

The source asks whether, for arbitrary $A$, random Krylov compression yields a polynomially bounded eigenvector condition number with high probability, or whether it yields an expected pseudospectral area bounded by a polynomial in $n$ times $\varepsilon^\beta$, for $\beta$ close to $2$. Schematically, these proposed bounds have the forms \[ \Pr_b\!\left\{\kappa_V(Q^*AQ)\leq p(n)\right\}\geq 1-\delta_n \qquad\text{or}\qquad {\mathbb{E}}_b\!\left[\operatorname{area}\Lambda_\varepsilon(Q^*AQ)\right]\leq p(n)\varepsilon^\beta, \] where $p$ is a polynomial. These displays are formulation templates: the source does not specify the law of $b$, the permitted Krylov orders, the failure probability $\delta_n$, the range of $\varepsilon$, the precise meaning of $\beta$ being close to $2$, or the permitted dependence of constants and exponents on these parameters or on $A$. No normalization of $A$ is supplied. Consequently, the record does not determine a fully quantified conjecture. The source additionally reports that the problem is open even when $A$ is a circulant shift matrix; this is a supplied status qualification, not an independently verified literature assessment.

Short English statement

For an arbitrary complex matrix, form its compression to a Krylov space generated by a random vector. The source asks whether the compressed matrix has polynomially controlled eigenvector conditioning with high probability, or an expected $\varepsilon$-pseudospectral area at most $\mathrm{poly}(n)\varepsilon^\beta$ with $\beta$ close to $2$. It reports openness even for a circulant shift matrix. The random-vector law, Krylov order, quantitative probability threshold, and other parameter qualifications remain unspecified.
