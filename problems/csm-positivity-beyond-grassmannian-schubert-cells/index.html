Math-3656

CSM Positivity Beyond Grassmannian Schubert Cells

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The following is the standard complex-algebraic interpretation of the recorded question; the source does not explicitly specify the base field, the class of groups, or the coefficient basis.

Let $G$ be a connected reductive algebraic group over ${\mathbb{C}}$, let $B\subseteq G$ be a Borel subgroup, and let $P\supseteq B$ be a parabolic subgroup. Set $X=G/P$. Choose a maximal torus $T\subseteq B$, write $W$ for the Weyl group of $(G,T)$, and write $W_P$ for the Weyl group of a Levi subgroup of $P$ containing $T$. Let $W^P$ denote the minimal-length representatives of $W/W_P$. For $w\in W^P$, define the Schubert cell and its closure by \[ X_w^{\circ}=BwP/P,\qquad X_w=\overline{X_w^{\circ}}\subseteq X. \] Write $u\leq w$ when $X_u\subseteq X_w$.

Let $c_*$ be the Chern--Schwartz--MacPherson transformation from integer-valued algebraically constructible functions on $X$ to its integral Chow group $A_*(X)$, normalized by $c_*(\mathbf{1}_X)=c(TX)\cap[X]$ for smooth $X$. Regard $\mathbf{1}_{X_w^{\circ}}$ as a constructible function on $X$, equal to zero outside the cell, and define the integers $a_{u,w}$ through the Schubert-basis expansion \[ c_{\mathrm{SM}}(X_w^{\circ}):=c_*(\mathbf{1}_{X_w^{\circ}}) =\sum_{\substack{u\in W^P\\u\leq w}}a_{u,w}[X_u] \quad\text{in }A_*(X). \] The generalized-flag formulation of the question is whether, for every such $G,B,P$ and every $u,w\in W^P$ with $u\leq w$, \[ a_{u,w}\geq 0. \] The recorded question asks specifically for the extension beyond Grassmannians. The formulation above includes Grassmannians as well; the supplied record does not delimit more precisely which other flag varieties are intended.

The supplied dated curation reports that this ordinary nonnegativity statement has been proved for all Schubert cells in generalized flag manifolds, while a stronger equivariant positivity conjecture is only partially established. This is a supplied literature assessment, not an independently verified conclusion. No precise equivariant conjecture is supplied, so that stronger assertion is not formulated here.

Short English statement

Expand the ordinary Chern--Schwartz--MacPherson class of each Schubert cell in the basis of Schubert-variety classes. The question asks whether every coefficient is nonnegative for flag varieties beyond Grassmannians. A standard complex-algebraic interpretation is stated explicitly above. The supplied dated assessment labels the problem partially solved and reports ordinary positivity as proved, while leaving a stronger, unspecified equivariant question partially established.
