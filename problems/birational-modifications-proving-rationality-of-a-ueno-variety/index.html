Birational Modifications Proving Rationality of a Ueno Variety

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote the base field intended in the source, let $t_1,t_2$ be algebraically independent over $k$, and put $K=k(t_1,t_2)$. The supplied record does not specify $k$, its characteristic, or the construction of the Ueno-type variety $X_{4,6}$. An integral $k$-variety $V$ of dimension $d$ is $k$-rational if it is birational over $k$ to ${\mathbb{P}}^d_k$, equivalently if \[ k(V)\cong_k k(u_1,\ldots,u_d), \] where $u_1,\ldots,u_d$ are algebraically independent over $k$. Birational modifications preserve the function field over the specified base field.

The supplied statement describes $X_{4,6}$ birationally by a diagonal cubic surface $S$ over $K$, but supplies neither its equation nor the precise birational presentation. On the interpretation that this description gives a function-field identification \[ k(X_{4,6})\cong_k K(S), \] the intended rationality target is \[ k(X_{4,6})\cong_k k(u_1,u_2,u_3,u_4), \] since a surface over $K$ has function field of transcendence degree $4$ over $k$.

Problem as supplied. Can the cubic surface in this birational description be modified birationally, analogously to the conic-bundle treatment of $X_{3,4}$, to prove that $X_{4,6}$ is rational over the intended base field? The construction of $X_{3,4}$, the referenced conic-bundle treatment, and the permitted modifications are not specified. Consequently, this is a conditional formulation of the target, pending recovery of the missing definitions and hypotheses from the cited report. Rationality of $S$ over $K$ would be sufficient under the displayed presentation, but the record does not require that stronger intermediate conclusion.

Short English statement

The question asks whether birational modifications of the diagonal cubic-surface description of $X_{4,6}$, inspired by the conic-bundle treatment of $X_{3,4}$, can prove rationality. The supplied record omits the base-field hypotheses, the definition of the varieties, and the cubic equation, so the formulation remains conditional on those source details.
