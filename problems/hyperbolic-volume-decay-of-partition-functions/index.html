Hyperbolic-Volume Decay of Partition Functions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ denote a triangulated hyperbolic $3$-manifold of the kind intended by the supplied quantum-Teichmüller topological quantum field theory (TQFT) model, allowing conical singularities along edges. Write $\operatorname{vol}(X)$ for the hyperbolic volume of its specified geometric structure, including its conical data, and $Z_b(X)$ for the partition function described in the record as a state integral. The record does not specify the admissible class of $X$, the construction or normalization of $Z_b(X)$, or the domain of the parameter $b$; these symbols therefore refer to the intended model rather than a fully supplied definition.

For each fixed admissible $X$, the cleaned source formulation asks whether \[ \lvert Z_b(X)\rvert\sim \exp\!\left(-\frac{\operatorname{vol}(X)}{2\pi b^2}\right) \qquad (b\to0). \] With the usual meaning of asymptotic equivalence, this asks whether \[ \lim_{b\to0} \frac{\lvert Z_b(X)\rvert} {\exp\!\left(-\operatorname{vol}(X)/(2\pi b^2)\right)}=1, \] where the limit must be taken within the intended parameter domain, which is not supplied.

The original wording asks only for exponential decay with rate given by hyperbolic volume. If the intended parameter regime is positive real $b\to0^+$, its natural logarithmic-rate formulation is \[ \lim_{b\to0^+}2\pi b^2\log\lvert Z_b(X)\rvert =-\operatorname{vol}(X), \] with $\log 0=-\infty$ if an extended-real interpretation is used. This rate statement is weaker than the displayed asymptotic equivalence: it does not require a multiplicative prefactor tending to $1$. The supplied evidence does not resolve which strength is intended, nor does it establish the positive-real parameter regime. Both qualifications require review before a definitive mathematical statement can be fixed.

Short English statement

For each fixed admissible triangulated hyperbolic $3$-manifold, possibly with conical singularities along edges, does its quantum-Teichmüller partition function decay at a rate determined by its hyperbolic volume as $b\to0$? The supplied asymptotic-equivalence formula is stronger than a logarithmic decay-rate claim; the intended strength, model normalization, admissibility conditions, and parameter domain remain unspecified.
