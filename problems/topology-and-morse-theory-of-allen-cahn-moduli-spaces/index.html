Math-4469

Topology and Morse Theory of Allen-Cahn Moduli Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns the planar Allen--Cahn equation \[ \Delta u+u-u^3=0\qquad\text{on }{\mathbb{R}}^2. \] Under the conventional classical, real-valued interpretation, a solution is a function $u\in C^2({\mathbb{R}}^2;{\mathbb{R}})$ satisfying this equation pointwise. The source does not explicitly specify regularity or the target space.

The notation $M_{2m}$ denotes the source's proposed moduli space of solutions having $2m$ ends. However, the supplied record does not define an end, prescribe asymptotic conditions, specify admissible values of $m$, or state whether solutions are identified under translations, rotations, reflections, or the transformation $u\mapsto -u$. It also does not specify a topology or differentiable structure on $M_{2m}$. These choices remain unresolved and are necessary for a precise moduli-space formulation.

With those qualifications, the requested problem is: for each admissible $m$, determine whether $M_{2m}$ is connected, determine its dimension, and classify the Morse indices of its solutions.

For clarity, one conventional meaning of the Morse index of an individual classical real-valued solution is obtained from the quadratic form \[ Q_u(\phi)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^2}\left(|\nabla\phi|^2+(3u^2-1)\phi^2\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x, \qquad \phi\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^2;{\mathbb{R}}). \] Under this convention, \[ \operatorname{ind}(u)=\sup\left\{\dim V:\ V\subset C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^2;{\mathbb{R}})\text{ is a finite-dimensional real vector subspace and }Q_u(\phi)<0\text{ for every }0\ne\phi\in V\right\}, \] with value $+\infty$ if these dimensions are unbounded. This convention is stated explicitly for interpretation; the supplied record does not select a variation space or an index convention. Nor does it assert that all solutions with the same number of ends have the same index. A complete formulation must specify whether dimension refers to a smooth manifold, local dimensions, or dimensions of strata.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether the space of planar Allen--Cahn solutions with $2m$ ends is connected, what its dimension is, and which Morse indices its solutions have. The supplied record leaves the meaning of ends, asymptotic conditions, symmetry identifications, topology, and dimension unspecified, so the full formulation requires clarification and human mathematical review.
