Multipartite Dense-Coding Capacity

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A shared multipartite state is a positive trace-one operator $\rho$ on $\bigotimes_i\mathcal H_{A_i}\otimes\bigotimes_{j=1}^r\mathcal H_{B_j}$. Sender $A_i$ encodes a classical message by a local unitary and sends its subsystem to its assigned receiver. Local operations and classical communication (LOCC) permit the receivers to exchange classical messages, but not to perform a joint quantum operation. The dense-coding capacity is the supremum asymptotic classical communication rate, in bits per use, achievable with vanishing decoding error in the chosen receiver protocol. The unassisted benchmark is $\sum_i\log_2\dim\mathcal H_{A_i}$. Bound entanglement means entanglement from which the relevant parties cannot distil singlets by LOCC.
Source scope and qualifications.
The capacity depends on whether receiver decoding is local, LOCC, or global and on the assignment of senders to receivers. The report distinguishes these protocols; the question must retain that distinction. It asks both for more-than-two-receiver capacities or bounds and for usefulness of multipartite bound entanglement.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For distributed dense coding with more than two receivers, determine the capacity, or at least a nontrivial upper bound. Can multipartite bound-entangled states be useful for distributed dense coding?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The single-receiver capacity is exact and the two-receiver case has nontrivial upper bounds, but the general more-than-two-receiver capacity and usefulness of multipartite bound entanglement remain unresolved in the literature checked. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Determine how much information several separated receivers can decode, and whether undistillable multipartite entanglement can help.
