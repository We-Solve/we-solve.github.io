Math-4319

Group Extensions Realized by Kodaira Fibrations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the conventional interpretation of the terminology, a Kodaira fibration is a surjective holomorphic submersion $f\colon S\to C$ from a smooth compact connected complex surface to a smooth compact connected complex curve, with connected fibres, such that $g(C)\geq 2$, every fibre has genus at least $2$, and the family is non-isotrivial, meaning that its fibres are not all mutually biholomorphic. The supplied record does not explicitly state these hypotheses; this definition makes the conventional interpretation explicit and requires confirmation against the source.

Under this interpretation, let $B$ and $F_b$ be smooth compact connected complex curves of genus at least $2$. Consider a short exact sequence of groups \[ \mathcal E\colon\quad 1\longrightarrow\pi_1(F_b)\xrightarrow{\iota}G\xrightarrow{p}\pi_1(B)\longrightarrow1, \] where $\iota$ is injective, $p$ is surjective, and $\operatorname{im}(\iota)=\ker(p)$. Fundamental groups are taken with compatible basepoints, suppressed from the notation.

Problem, following the corrected registry wording. Characterize the extensions $\mathcal E$ for which there exist a Kodaira fibration $f\colon S\to C$ and an abstract group isomorphism \[ G\cong\pi_1(S). \]

Unresolved scope of realization. The displayed condition concerns the abstract middle group. Realization of the given extension is a more specific requirement: there must also be a point $c\in C$ and isomorphisms making the following diagram commute, with the bottom row induced by $f$: \[ \begin{array}{ccccccccc} 1&\longrightarrow&\pi_1(F_b)&\xrightarrow{\iota}&G&\xrightarrow{p}&\pi_1(B)&\longrightarrow&1\\ &&\downarrow\scriptstyle\cong&&\downarrow\scriptstyle\cong&&\downarrow\scriptstyle\cong&&\\ 1&\longrightarrow&\pi_1(f^{-1}(c))&\longrightarrow&\pi_1(S)&\xrightarrow{f_*}&\pi_1(C)&\longrightarrow&1. \end{array} \] The supplied wording does not determine whether this compatibility is required, or whether $C$ and $f^{-1}(c)$ must be biholomorphic to the specified curves $B$ and $F_b$. These requirements must not be silently identified with abstract group realization.

The original wording asks for the fundamental group of a "Kodaira surface," whereas the corrected wording asks for that of a Kodaira fibration. Here the corrected wording supplies the target; the original terminology remains unresolved because "Kodaira surface" also has a distinct standard meaning in complex-surface classification.

Short English statement

Determine which extensions of a curve fundamental group by another curve fundamental group have a middle group isomorphic to the fundamental group of a Kodaira fibration. The source leaves unresolved whether the specified extension itself, and the specified complex curves, must also be realized.
