Math-1135

Paramodular Correspondence for Abelian Surfaces and Fourfolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N$ be a positive integer, as required for a conductor or paramodular level. An abelian surface has dimension $2$, and an abelian fourfold has dimension $4$. Write $[X]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ for the ${\mathbb{Q}}$-isogeny class of an abelian variety $X/{\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $\operatorname{cond}(X)$ denote its arithmetic conductor. Define \[ A_N=\left\{[A]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}: \dim A=2,\ \operatorname{cond}(A)=N,\ \operatorname{End}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(A)={\mathbb{Z}}\right\}. \] Here $\operatorname{End}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(A)$ is the ring of endomorphisms defined over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The source also specifies \[ B_N=\left\{[B]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}: \dim B=4,\ \operatorname{cond}(B)=N^2,\ B\text{ has quaternionic multiplication}\right\}, \] but does not specify the precise quaternionic-multiplication condition. In particular, it does not identify the quaternion algebra, the field of definition of its action, or additional endomorphism or simplicity conditions.

Let $P_N$ denote the source's cuspidal nonlift Siegel paramodular newforms of level $N$ with rational Hecke eigenvalues, modulo multiplication by a nonzero scalar. The original statement further qualifies these forms as "suitable." The weight, the meaning of this suitability qualification, and the precise lift and newform conventions are not supplied, so this description does not yet determine a precise set $P_N$.

Subject to resolving these qualifications, the proposed correspondence asserts that for every positive integer $N$ there exists a bijection \[ \Phi_N:A_N\cup B_N\longrightarrow P_N \] such that \[ \begin{aligned} L_{\mathrm{HW}}(A,s)&=L_{\mathrm{spin}}(\Phi_N([A]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}),s) &&\text{for every }[A]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\in A_N,\\ L_{\mathrm{HW}}(B,s)&=\left(L_{\mathrm{spin}}(\Phi_N([B]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}),s)\right)^2 &&\text{for every }[B]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\in B_N. \end{aligned} \] Here $L_{\mathrm{HW}}$ denotes the Hasse--Weil $L$-function of the abelian variety, and $L_{\mathrm{spin}}$ denotes the spin $L$-function of the form. The source does not specify their normalization, completion, or conventions at primes dividing $N$. These choices must be fixed compatibly before the displayed equalities are fully precise. This is the supplied conjectural target, with unresolved hypotheses explicitly retained.

Short English statement

For each positive level $N$, the proposed correspondence pairs the specified rational abelian surfaces and quaternionic-multiplication fourfolds with rational-eigenvalue paramodular forms. The associated Hasse--Weil $L$-function should equal the spin $L$-function for a surface and its square for a fourfold. The fourfold conditions and the class of eligible forms require clarification.
