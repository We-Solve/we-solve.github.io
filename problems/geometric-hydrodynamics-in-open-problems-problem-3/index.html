Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a geometric description, rather than asserting a particular curvature formula or existence theorem. Write $M$ for the underlying manifold and $\mu$ for its volume structure. The comparison object in the source is the group of volume-preserving diffeomorphisms, \[ \mathscr{D}_{\mu}(M)=\{\phi\in\operatorname{Diff}(M):\phi^*\mu=\mu\}, \] with the regularity and boundary conventions left unspecified by the supplied record.

Let $\mathcal G\rightrightarrows B$ denote the intended Lie groupoid of multiphase or generalized volume-preserving diffeomorphisms, with source and target maps $s,t$. This notation is conditional: the supplied record does not define $\mathcal G$, its objects, or its arrows. A source-wise metric is a family of metrics $G_b$ on the source fibers $s^{-1}(b)$, for $b\in B$. With the convention that $kh$ is defined when $s(k)=t(h)$, right multiplication by an arrow $h$ is \[ R_h:s^{-1}(t(h))\longrightarrow s^{-1}(s(h)),\qquad k\longmapsto kh. \] Right invariance means \[ R_h^*G_{s(h)}=G_{t(h)}\qquad\text{for every }h\in\mathcal G. \] The requested metric is the source's $L^2$ metric. Its integral formula cannot be specified from this record: the phase data, velocity representation, integration measures, and underlying pointwise inner products are not supplied.

Problem. Describe the differential geometry of this right-invariant, source-wise $L^2$ metric on the Lie groupoid of multiphase or generalized volume-preserving diffeomorphisms, analogously to Arnold's description for $\mathscr{D}_{\mu}(M)$. In particular, compute sectional curvatures and, wherever a mathematically defined Ricci contraction exists, Ricci curvatures, and describe conjugate points along geodesics in the source fibers.

For precision concerning these requested quantities, whenever a source fiber admits the requisite Levi--Civita connection $\nabla$, use the convention \[ R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z. \] For linearly independent tangent vectors $u,v$ at a point of that fiber, its sectional curvature is \[ K(u,v)=\frac{\langle R(u,v)v,u\rangle} {\langle u,u\rangle\langle v,v\rangle-\langle u,v\rangle^2}. \] The formal Ricci contraction is \[ \operatorname{Ric}(u,v)=\operatorname{tr}\left(w\longmapsto R(w,u)v\right), \] but an infinite-dimensional setting requires a justified notion and existence of this trace; neither is supplied. Along a geodesic $\gamma$, a time $T>0$ is conjugate to time $0$ in the Jacobi-field sense if there is a nonzero Jacobi field $J$ satisfying \[ \nabla_{\dot\gamma}^{\,2}J+R(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=0, \qquad J(0)=J(T)=0. \] These definitions specify the requested geometric quantities where they exist; they do not assert that the necessary analytic structures exist for the intended groupoid. A fully specified formulation still requires the source's definitions of multiphase and generalized diffeomorphisms, its $L^2$ metric, and its manifold, regularity, and boundary hypotheses.

Short English statement

Describe the geometry of the source-wise, right-invariant $L^2$ metric on the intended groupoid of multiphase or generalized volume-preserving diffeomorphisms, including sectional and Ricci curvature and conjugate points. The supplied record omits the groupoid and metric constructions and the analytic hypotheses needed to make every requested quantity precise.
