Lie-Algebraic Parametrization of TMF Classes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathrm{TMF}$ denote the spectrum named in the supplied record, and let $\pi_n(\mathrm{TMF})$ denote its degree-$n$ homotopy group. The precise version of this spectrum and the intended range of degrees are not specified. For a nonnegative integer $n$, let \[ \mathcal L_n=\{\mathfrak g:\mathfrak g\text{ is a real semisimple Lie algebra of compact type},\ \dim_{{\mathbb{R}}}\mathfrak g=n\}/\cong. \] Here compact type means that $\mathfrak g$ is the Lie algebra of a compact Lie group, and $\cong$ denotes Lie algebra isomorphism. Using isomorphism classes is an explicit interpretation of the proposed parameters; the source does not specify the parameter space or the meaning of naturality.

For an $n$-dimensional compact Lie group $G$, an ordered basis $(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ of $T_eG$ defines a left-invariant tangent framing by \[ \lambda_g=\left(({\,\mathrm{d}} L_g)_e v_1,\ldots,({\,\mathrm{d}} L_g)_e v_n\right),\qquad g\in G, \] where $L_g$ is left multiplication. Under the usual interpretation of a framed manifold as a stable homotopy class, and provided the intended $\mathrm{TMF}$ has a specified unit $\eta:\mathbb S\to\mathrm{TMF}$, write \[ \mathcal C_n=\left\{\eta_*[G,\lambda]\in\pi_n(\mathrm{TMF}):G\text{ is a compact Lie group of dimension }n,\ \lambda\text{ is a left-invariant framing}\right\}. \] Here $\mathbb S$ is the sphere spectrum and $[G,\lambda]\in\pi_n(\mathbb S)$ is the framed bordism class. This makes explicit a conventional interpretation of the phrase "classes represented by compact Lie groups with their left-invariant framings"; the record does not specify this map, restrict connectedness, or fix framing choices.

Question and conjectural target. Does the proposed Stolz--Teichner cocycle construction produce a natural family of elements \[ \Theta_n:\mathcal L_n\longrightarrow\pi_n(\mathrm{TMF})? \] The original wording asks for such a family and describes its realization by the cocycle construction as plausible. It does not explicitly require $\Theta_n$ to be injective, surjective, or a bijection onto all of $\pi_n(\mathrm{TMF})$.

The further supplied conjecture is that the relevant construction hits exactly $\mathcal C_n$. If its degree-$n$ output is denoted schematically by \[ F_n:\mathcal D_n\longrightarrow\pi_n(\mathrm{TMF}), \] this target is \[ \operatorname{im}(F_n)=\mathcal C_n. \] Neither $\mathcal D_n$ nor the precise cocycle construction is supplied. In particular, the record does not determine whether $F_n$ means only the semisimple Lie algebra family $\Theta_n$ or a broader construction. Thus the displayed equality is a conditional formulation of the stated target, not a fully specified conjecture or an asserted result.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether $n$-dimensional semisimple compact-type Lie algebras naturally give classes in $\pi_n(\mathrm{TMF})$ through a proposed Stolz--Teichner cocycle construction, and conjectures that the relevant construction produces exactly the classes represented by compact Lie groups with left-invariant framings. The construction, its domain, and the strength of the proposed parametrization remain unspecified. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
