Higher Extensions Between Additive and Multiplicative Algebraic Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $k$ for the unspecified base field intended by the source, and write $G_a$ and $G_m$ for the additive and multiplicative algebraic groups over that field. Thus, on a commutative $k$-algebra $R$, their groups of points are $G_a(R)=(R,+)$ and $G_m(R)=R^\times$. This notation does not impose a universal quantifier over base fields.

The supplied background describes $\mathcal C$ as the full category of commutative algebraic groups. It does not specify the base field, its characteristic, the conventions governing the objects of $\mathcal C$, or the exact structure used to define extensions. These choices remain unresolved. Under the Yoneda interpretation, $\operatorname{Ext}^i_{\mathcal C}(G_a,G_m)$, for $i\geq 1$, is the group of equivalence classes of exact extensions \[ 0\longrightarrow G_m\longrightarrow E_{i-1}\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow E_0\longrightarrow G_a\longrightarrow 0, \] with terms in the intended category and equivalence generated by morphisms of extensions inducing the identity on the endpoints; addition is the Baer sum. This interpretation is conditional on the intended category and extension theory supporting this construction.

With those source conventions fixed, the requested problems are:

  1. Describe $\operatorname{Ext}^2_{\mathcal C}(G_a,G_m)$ by explicit generators and defining relations, in the proposed analogue of the Milnor conjecture.
  2. Determine whether there exists an integer $N\geq 1$ such that \[ \forall i\in{\mathbb{Z}},\qquad i\geq N\ \Longrightarrow\ \operatorname{Ext}^i_{\mathcal C}(G_a,G_m)=0. \]
The proposed Milnor-conjecture analogue is not described in the supplied record, so its intended generators, relations, and comparison objects cannot be specified here. The scope of the base-field assumptions and any uniformity requirement on $N$ are likewise unspecified.

Short English statement

For the intended category of commutative algebraic groups, give generators and relations for the second extension group from the additive group to the multiplicative group, and ask whether all sufficiently high extension groups between these objects vanish. The base field, precise category, extension theory, and proposed Milnor-conjecture analogue require clarification.
