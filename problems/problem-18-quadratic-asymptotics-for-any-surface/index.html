Problem 18 — (Quadratic asymptotics for any surface).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be an individual translation surface or surface with the flat structure of a quadratic differential, in the source's admissible finite-area category. A saddle connection is a flat geodesic segment joining designated singular/marked endpoints with no singularity in its interior. Let $N_{\mathrm{sc}}(X,R)$ count such segments of length at most $R$, with an orientation/multiplicity convention to be fixed.

For regular closed geodesics, counting each geometric curve literally gives infinitely many parallel curves in a cylinder. The customary finite interpretation is to count maximal cylinders of periodic geodesics, or the corresponding primitive classes, by circumference at most $R$; write the selected count as $N_{\mathrm{cl}}(X,R)$. The source does not state which interpretation, treatment of iterates or any cylinder weights is intended, so they must be specified.

The question asks whether every such individual $X$ has exact quadratic asymptotics for both counts, meaning constants $c_{\mathrm{sc}}(X)$, $c_{\mathrm{cl}}(X)$ with \[\lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{N_{\mathrm{sc}}(X,R)}{R^2}=c_{\mathrm{sc}}(X),\qquad \lim_{R\to\infty}\frac{N_{\mathrm{cl}}(X,R)}{R^2}=c_{\mathrm{cl}}(X),\] or equivalently the respective counts equal $c(X)R^2+o(R^2)$. If "exact quadratic" requires strictly positive finite constants, the hypotheses excluding counts that vanish must be included; in particular unmarked nonsingular torus conventions affect saddle connections. The excerpt does not fix that positivity convention.

The assertion is pointwise in all surfaces/differentials, not only almost everywhere in a stratum, and it asks for limits rather than only upper and lower quadratic bounds. The differential class (holomorphic or allowed poles), compactness, marked singularities, area normalization and count conventions are absent. These affect which objects and coefficients occur, but no uniform constant independent of $X$ or uniform error rate is asserted.

Short English statement

Does each individual flat surface have a true quadratic leading term for saddle-connection and periodic-geodesic counts? The source requires every surface, while closed-geodesic counting and singularity conventions need clarification.
