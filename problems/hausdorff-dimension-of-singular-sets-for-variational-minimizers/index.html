Math-3334

Hausdorff Dimension of Singular Sets for Variational Minimizers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Strict singular-dimension problem. Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be bounded and open, and consider\[\mathcal F(v)=\int_\Omega F(x,v,Dv)\,dx.\] Assume the source's $p$-growth, uniform $p$-ellipticity in the gradient variable, and Hölder continuity in $(x,v)$. If $u\in W^{1,p}(\Omega,{\mathbb{R}}^N)$ is a local minimizer and $\Sigma_u=\Omega\setminus\Omega_u$ is its singular set, the requested conclusion is\[\dim_{\mathrm H}(\Sigma_u)<n.\] Here $Dv\in{\mathbb{R}}^{N\times n}$ is the weak derivative and $\dim_{\mathrm H}$ is Hausdorff dimension. Under the usual compact-variation convention, local minimality means $\mathcal F(u;U)\le\mathcal F(u+\varphi;U)$ for every $U\Subset\Omega$ and $\varphi\in W^{1,p}_0(U,{\mathbb{R}}^N)$ for which the energies are admissible, where $\mathcal F(v;U)=\int_UF(x,v,Dv)\,dx$. The precise minimizer convention must be checked if the source uses a smaller variation class.

A partial-regularity theorem supplies an open regular set $\Omega_u$ of full Lebesgue measure. For a dimension assertion its definition matters: it must be the intended intrinsic regularity locus, not an arbitrarily chosen smaller full-measure open set. For example, if the intended regularity is local $C^{1,\beta}$, one uses the maximal set of points having a neighborhood with that regularity in the specified exponent convention. The record does not specify the regularity level or the exact definition of $\Omega_u$. Removing extra exceptional points from an otherwise regular locus could change the dimension question. Mere measure zero of $\Sigma_u$ is weaker than the requested strict inequality, since a Lebesgue-null subset can still have Hausdorff dimension $n$.

The admissible range of $p$, differentiability of $F$ in the gradient variable, precise growth and ellipticity inequalities, coercivity and upper bounds, Hölder exponent and weighted modulus in $(x,v)$, and dependence of constants are absent. Uniformly nondegenerate and degenerate $p$-elliptic conventions must not be interchanged. The target asks for strict dimension loss; it does not itself specify a universal numerical deficit or a sharper codimension. Consequently no particular structural assumptions or deficit estimate are silently added.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the item open because it describes an extraction truncated after the singular-set definition. The later curated field and verification note state this strict-dimension problem and report checking its hypotheses in the primary report. The source layers need reconciliation; the fragment-based open label is not independent present-day verification of this more explicit assertion.

Short English statement

Improve almost-everywhere partial regularity to a strictly subambient Hausdorff dimension for the genuine singular locus of a minimizer. The exact integrand hypotheses and regularity locus are missing. A measure-zero exceptional set alone does not give the requested dimension loss.
