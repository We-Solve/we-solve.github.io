Absolutely Continuous Spectrum Under a Weighted L2 Condition

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
The operator $-\Delta+V$ is a continuum Schrödinger operator on $L^2(\mathbb R^\nu)$, realized self-adjointly for a real potential under the source’s standing operator-domain assumptions. The displayed integral is a weighted square-integrability condition, with weight $|x|^{1-\nu}$. The absolutely continuous part is characterized by spectral measures having Lebesgue densities; its multiplicity is the number of independent spectral channels almost everywhere.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Let $V$ be a function on $\mathbb R^\nu$, $\nu\geq2$, satisfying $\int |x|^{-\nu+1}|V(x)|^2\,d^\nu x<\infty$. Prove that $-\Delta+V$ has absolutely continuous spectrum of infinite multiplicity on $[0,\infty)$.
Scope and source qualification.
The brief catalogue statement does not specify local regularity or the self-adjoint realization. Those operator-domain hypotheses require consultation of the primary source, and are not invented here. Multiplicity on an interval is understood almost everywhere in spectral theory.

Short English statement

Determine whether the stated weighted square-integrability condition forces infinitely many absolutely continuous spectral channels at nonnegative energies.
