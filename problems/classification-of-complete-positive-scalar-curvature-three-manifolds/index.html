Math-1159

Classification of Complete Positive-Scalar-Curvature Three-Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a smooth three-dimensional manifold $M$, let $[M]_{\mathrm{diff}}$ denote its diffeomorphism class. A smooth Riemannian metric $g$ has pointwise positive scalar curvature if its scalar curvature function satisfies \[ \operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0\qquad\text{for every }x\in M. \] For a manifold without boundary, completeness means completeness of the Riemannian distance $d_g$, equivalently geodesic completeness. The metric satisfying the scalar curvature inequality must itself be complete. No uniform positive lower bound on scalar curvature, or prescribed rate of decay at infinity, is required in the supplied question.

The classification problem is to give necessary and sufficient conditions on the diffeomorphism class of $M$ for the existence of such a metric; equivalently, to describe \[ \left\{[M]_{\mathrm{diff}}:\ \dim M=3,\ \exists\text{ a smooth Riemannian metric }g\text{ on }M,\ (M,d_g)\text{ is complete},\ \forall x\in M,\ \operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0\right\}. \] This asks for a classification of the underlying manifolds, extending the classification of the closed orientable case referred to in the source. Here closed means compact and without boundary. The supplied record does not state that closed classification or specify precisely how the extension should be presented.

The admissible category of manifolds in the displayed classification remains a source qualification: the record does not explicitly specify orientability, connectedness, or whether boundary is excluded. The completeness convention above gives the usual interpretation for manifolds without boundary; if manifolds with boundary are intended, the meaning of completeness and any boundary conditions require clarification. These scope questions are unresolved rather than additional hypotheses imposed by this draft.

Short English statement

Classify, up to diffeomorphism, the smooth three-manifolds that admit a complete Riemannian metric whose scalar curvature is positive at every point, extending the closed orientable classification mentioned in the source. Positivity need not have a uniform positive lower bound. The intended orientability, connectedness, and boundary conventions are not specified.
