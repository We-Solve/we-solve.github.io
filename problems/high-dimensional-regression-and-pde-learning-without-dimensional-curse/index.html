Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.

For squared-loss regression, the usual target is the conditional mean $m(x)=\mathbb E[Y\mid X=x]$, when defined, and approximation error may be measured by $\mathbb E[(\widehat m(X)-m(X))^2]$. Other losses or norms define other targets. A PDE method approximates a solution $u$ of a specified differential equation, with initial or boundary data, in a chosen error norm. The curse of dimensionality refers to sample or computation requirements growing exponentially with dimension, for example $\epsilon^{-c d}$ to obtain error $\epsilon$ in dimension $d$. Training data fit the estimator; validation data estimate its performance under a specified sampling distribution.
