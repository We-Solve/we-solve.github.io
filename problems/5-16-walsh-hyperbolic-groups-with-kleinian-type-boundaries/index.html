5.16 (Walsh) — Hyperbolic groups with Kleinian-type boundaries

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. A hyperbolic group with two-sphere boundary is claimed to act geometrically on hyperbolic three-space with finite kernel.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $G$ be word-hyperbolic with Gromov boundary $\partial G\cong S^2$. The source claims a homomorphism \[ \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^3) \] whose action on hyperbolic three-space is proper and cocompact, and whose kernel is finite. With the additional assumption that $G$ is torsion-free, the corollaries identify $G\cong\pi_1(M)$, where $M$ is closed and has a hyperbolic metric in dimension three, and supply a finite-index subgroup \[ H\cong\pi_1(\Sigma)\rtimes\mathbb Z, \] where $\Sigma$ is a closed connected orientable surface. They also assert residual finiteness, virtual compact specialness, and positive virtual first Betti number in that torsion-free case.

Outline of the argument

The argument applies a boundary modulus criterion. Unbounded modulus produces scalar limits analyzed by exponential records or subexponential probability flows; comparing same-depth expansions forces the required measure control and the three-dimensional hyperbolic action.
