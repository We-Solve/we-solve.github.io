Universality Classes for Interacting Particle Fluctuations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Terminology. A one-dimensional interacting particle system describes particles whose stochastic evolution depends on their configuration in one spatial dimension. In an exclusion system, each site can contain at most one particle. Finite-range exclusion permits jumps only over a bounded spatial distance; nearest-neighbour exclusion restricts jumps to adjacent sites. The source does not specify the underlying spatial domain, jump rates, or allowed interactions.

Integrated particle-density fluctuations refer to a spatially accumulated, centered particle-density field, with a reference point and a time-dependent current term where required by the chosen height convention. The centering, reference convention, and initial distribution are not specified in the supplied record. KPZ denotes the Kardar--Parisi--Zhang universality class, and EW denotes the Edwards--Wilkinson universality class. The record does not specify a mathematical definition of membership in either class.

Source research problem. Determine explicit hypotheses on one-dimensional interacting particle systems extending beyond nearest-neighbour simple exclusion, including finite-range exclusion systems, under which their integrated particle-density fluctuations rigorously exhibit the predicted KPZ or EW scaling behaviour. The desired results should specify which hypotheses lead to each class and establish the corresponding fluctuation limits.

Unresolved formulation. For a precise convergence assertion, one must specify the stochastic dynamics, spatial domain and boundary conditions, initial laws or stationary measures, density regime, centering and observation frame, space--time and fluctuation rescalings, limiting random object, and mode of convergence. None of these is fixed by the supplied statement. In particular, it does not distinguish convergence of a single observable, convergence of finite-dimensional distributions, and convergence of a random field or process. It also does not say whether KPZ or EW behaviour is intended to mean scaling exponents, limiting distributions, or a particular stochastic evolution. Thus the supplied record states a research programme seeking sufficient hypotheses, rather than a fully quantified conjecture for a specified family of systems; a unique precise limit theorem cannot be extracted without additional source information.

Short English statement

Find conditions ensuring that integrated density fluctuations in one-dimensional interacting particle systems beyond nearest-neighbour exclusion, including finite-range exclusion, have KPZ or Edwards--Wilkinson scaling limits. The supplied record leaves the models, hypotheses, rescalings, and convergence targets unspecified, so a precise theorem remains to be formulated.
