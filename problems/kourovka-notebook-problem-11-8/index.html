Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.8

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ and $H$ be finite groups. For a finite group $X$, let $\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(X)$ denote the set of irreducible complex characters of $X$, and define the multiset \[ \chi_1(X)=\{\!\{\chi(1):\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(X)\}\!\}. \] Thus each irreducible character contributes one occurrence of its degree; distinct characters of the same degree contribute repeated occurrences. Explicitly, $\chi_1(G)=\chi_1(H)$ means that, for every integer $d\geq 1$, \[ \left|\{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(G):\chi(1)=d\}\right| = \left|\{\psi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(H):\psi(1)=d\}\right|. \] As noted in the source, this implies $|G|=|H|$, using \[ |X|=\sum_{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(X)}\chi(1)^2. \] Define $X^{(0)}=X$ and $X^{(n+1)}=[X^{(n)},X^{(n)}]$, where $[K,K]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$ with $a,b\in K$. The group $X$ is soluble if $X^{(n)}=\{1\}$ for some integer $n\geq 0$.

The remaining question, part $\mathrm{(b)}$ of Kourovka Notebook Problem 11.8, proposed by Ya. G. Berkovich, is whether \[ \forall\,G,H\text{ finite groups},\qquad \left(\chi_1(G)=\chi_1(H)\ \text{and}\ G\text{ is soluble}\right) \Longrightarrow H\text{ is soluble}. \] No nontriviality assumption on $G$ or $H$ is imposed.

The source additionally reports that, under the same multiset equality, $H$ is a Frobenius group whenever $G$ is a Frobenius group. Here a finite Frobenius group means a finite group admitting a faithful transitive permutation action with nontrivial point stabilizers in which every nonidentity element fixes at most one point. This auxiliary assertion is retained as supplied background; the record provides no specific reference for it.

The supplied archive also retains part $\mathrm{(a)}$, asking whether \[ \forall\,G,H\text{ finite groups},\qquad \left(\chi_1(G)=\chi_1(H)\ \text{and}\ G\text{ is simple}\right) \Longrightarrow H\text{ is simple}. \] Here a simple group is a nontrivial group whose only normal subgroups are $\{1\}$ and the whole group. The archive reports an affirmative solution of part $\mathrm{(a)}$, attributed to H. P. Tong-Viet. That solved designation and the designation of part $\mathrm{(b)}$ as remaining are supplied curation, not an independently verified literature assessment.

Short English statement

For finite groups $G$ and $H$, suppose their irreducible complex character degrees agree, including the number of occurrences of each degree. Part $\mathrm{(b)}$ asks whether solubility of $G$ forces solubility of $H$. The supplied record classifies the problem as partially solved and reports that the analogous simplicity question in part $\mathrm{(a)}$ has an affirmative solution.
