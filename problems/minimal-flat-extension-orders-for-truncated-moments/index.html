Minimal Flat Extension Orders for Truncated Moments

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks: given a truncated moment sequence $\beta^{(2d)}$, find a good estimate for the smallest integer $k\geq 0$ such that a moment matrix $M_{d+k}$ extending the given data admits a flat extension $M_{d+k+1}$. The ambient domain, support restrictions, and admissibility assumptions on the initial data are not specified in the record.

A precise conditional formulation under the real multivariate, positive semidefinite convention is as follows. Fix integers $n\geq 1$ and $d\geq 0$, and let \[ \beta^{(2d)}=(\beta_\alpha)_{\alpha\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}^{n},\ |\alpha|\leq 2d},\qquad \beta_\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}},\qquad |\alpha|=\alpha_1+\cdots+\alpha_n. \] For an array $\gamma=(\gamma_\alpha)_{|\alpha|\leq 2t}$, define its moment matrix by \[ M_t(\gamma)=\left(\gamma_{\alpha+\delta}\right)_{|\alpha|,|\delta|\leq t}. \] Under this convention, a flat extension of $M_t(\gamma)$ is a moment matrix $M_{t+1}(\widetilde\gamma)$ satisfying \[ \widetilde\gamma_\alpha=\gamma_\alpha\quad(|\alpha|\leq 2t),\qquad M_{t+1}(\widetilde\gamma)\succeq 0,\qquad \operatorname{rank}M_{t+1}(\widetilde\gamma)=\operatorname{rank}M_t(\gamma). \] Thus define the set of feasible extension orders by \[ \mathcal F(\beta)=\left\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:\begin{array}{l} \text{there exists a real array }\gamma=(\gamma_\alpha)_{|\alpha|\leq 2(d+k+1)}\text{ with}\\ \gamma_\alpha=\beta_\alpha\quad(|\alpha|\leq 2d),\\ M_{d+k+1}(\gamma)\succeq 0,\\ \operatorname{rank}M_{d+k+1}(\gamma)=\operatorname{rank}M_{d+k}(\gamma) \end{array}\right\}. \] When $\mathcal F(\beta)\neq\varnothing$, the target quantity is \[ k_{\min}(\beta)=\min\mathcal F(\beta). \] The problem is to obtain a good estimate for this least order from the given truncated data. The intermediate matrix $M_{d+k}$ must itself be chosen as an extension of the initial data; it is not determined by $\beta^{(2d)}$ when $k>0$.

This conditional formulation does not resolve the source's missing hypotheses: it does not specify whether the intended setting is real or complex, whether a representing measure is assumed to exist, whether its support is restricted to a prescribed set, or whether positivity is included in the intended meaning of flat extension. If support constraints are intended, the displayed feasibility condition requires corresponding additional conditions. The record also does not define what qualifies as a good estimate or specify the parameters on which it may depend. If $\mathcal F(\beta)=\varnothing$, no smallest finite order exists in this formulation; the source does not state how such inputs are to be treated.

Short English statement

Estimate how far a truncated moment sequence must be extended before one further moment-matrix extension can preserve rank. The displayed formulation makes the intermediate extensions existential choices under a stated real positive semidefinite convention. The intended domain, support conditions, existence assumptions, and meaning of a good estimate still require clarification.
