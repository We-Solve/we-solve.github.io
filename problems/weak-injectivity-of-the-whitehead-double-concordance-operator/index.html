Math-781

Weak Injectivity of the Whitehead-Double Concordance Operator

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{sm}}$ denote the smooth knot concordance group of oriented knots in $S^3$: two knots represent the same element if they cobound a smooth, properly embedded oriented annulus in $S^3\times[0,1]$. The group operation is connected sum. Let $U$ be the unknot, whose concordance class $[U]$ is the identity.

Write $\operatorname{Wh}\colon\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{sm}}\to\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{sm}}$ for the satellite map induced by the Whitehead-double pattern intended in the source. Here $\operatorname{Wh}([K])=[\operatorname{Wh}(K)]$. Calling this an operator does not assert that it is a group homomorphism.

The supplied weak injectivity question is whether \[ \forall [K]\in\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{sm}},\qquad \operatorname{Wh}([K])=\operatorname{Wh}([U]) \ \Longrightarrow\ [K]=[U]. \] Equivalently, does smooth concordance of $\operatorname{Wh}(K)$ to $\operatorname{Wh}(U)$ imply that $K$ is smoothly concordant to the unknot, or equivalently bounds a smooth, properly embedded disk in the standard $4$-ball $B^4$? All equalities in the question are equalities of concordance classes, rather than assertions of knot isotopy.

Unresolved qualification. The supplied record does not specify the clasp sign or twisting convention for the Whitehead-double pattern. Thus the displayed statement records the intended logical question for a fixed intended pattern, but the record alone does not uniquely determine that pattern. No quantification over all Whitehead-double variants is asserted.

Short English statement

For the intended Whitehead-double satellite operator, must a knot be smoothly concordant to the unknot whenever its Whitehead double is smoothly concordant to the Whitehead double of the unknot? The supplied record labels this question open. The precise Whitehead-double convention remains unspecified.
