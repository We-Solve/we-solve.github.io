Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.65

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ denote the set of irreducible complex characters of $G$. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, define its field of values by \[ {\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(g):g\in G\right). \] For a positive integer $k$, call $G$ $k$-rational if \[ [{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi):{\mathbb{Q}}]\mid k \qquad\text{for every }\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G). \] Here the positive-integer domain of $k$ is the conventional interpretation of the divisibility condition; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify that domain.

A composition series of $G$ is a chain \[ 1=G_0\triangleleft G_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft G_n=G \] whose quotients $G_i/G_{i-1}$ are nontrivial simple groups. A cyclic composition factor necessarily has prime order; write $C_p$ for a cyclic group of prime order $p$.

Does there exist a function \[ f:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}} \] such that, for every positive integer $k$, every finite $k$-rational group $G$, and every prime $p$, \[ \left(C_p\text{ is isomorphic to a composition factor of }G\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad p\leq f(k)? \] Thus the bound must depend only on $k$, uniformly over all such finite groups and their cyclic composition factors.

The supplied record states that, when $k=1$, one has $p\leq 11$, attributing this result to J. G. Thompson. This attribution and special-case assertion are retained as supplied background and have not been independently verified.

Short English statement

Is there a bound depending only on the positive integer $k$ for the prime orders of cyclic composition factors of finite groups whose irreducible complex character fields all have degrees dividing $k$? The supplied record labels this problem open and attributes the bound $11$ for $k=1$ to J. G. Thompson; these are supplied assessments, not independently verified conclusions.
