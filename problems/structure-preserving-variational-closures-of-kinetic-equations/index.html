Structure-Preserving Variational Closures of Kinetic Equations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $x$ for the spatial variable, $t$ for time, $c$ for the kinetic variable, and $W(x,t)$ for the reduced state. The prescribed form of the closure ansatz is \[ f(x,t,c)=f^{\star}(W(x,t),c). \] The kinetic equation, the closure map $f^{\star}$, the dimensions and domains of these variables, and the admissible reduced states are not specified in the supplied record. Let $\phi_{\alpha}(x,t,c)$ denote the test functions sought for the variational formulation; their index set and admissible function spaces are likewise unspecified.

Statement of the modeling problem. For a kinetic equation reduced by this closure ansatz, determine test functions $\phi_{\alpha}$ such that the reduced equations preserve the physical conservation laws of the kinetic model. Determine the resulting evolution equations for $W(x,t)$, and investigate whether they are symmetric hyperbolic balance laws admitting an entropy. The conserved quantities and the sense in which their conservation is to be retained must be specified for the particular kinetic model. The record does not distinguish local balance identities from global conservation under boundary conditions.

For terminology, a first-order system is symmetric hyperbolic on an admissible state region if, in suitable state coordinates $U$, it has the form \[ A^0(U)\partial_t U+\sum_{j=1}^{d}A^j(U)\partial_{x_j}U=B(U), \] where $A^0(U)$ is symmetric positive definite and every $A^j(U)$ is symmetric throughout that region. Here $d$, the coordinate change from $W$ to $U$, and all coefficients would have to be determined from the specified model and reduction; this display is a definition of the structural property under investigation, not an asserted reduced equation.

If a reduced system admits a conservative balance-law representation \[ \partial_t W+\sum_{j=1}^{d}\partial_{x_j}F^j(W)=S(W), \] a differentiable entropy pair $(\eta,q^1,\ldots,q^d)$ satisfies \[ Dq^j(W)=D\eta(W)\,DF^j(W),\qquad j=1,\ldots,d. \] For smooth solutions this compatibility yields the entropy balance \[ \partial_t\eta(W)+\sum_{j=1}^{d}\partial_{x_j}q^j(W) =D\eta(W)S(W). \] The supplied record does not specify whether the intended entropy must be convex or strictly convex, what sign condition its production should satisfy, or whether an entropy inequality for weak solutions is required. Neither the conservative representation nor any of these additional properties is assumed to hold.

Unresolved scope. This is an underspecified modeling question. The source does not state a universal assertion over all kinetic equations or all closures, and it does not supply the data needed to formulate one particular reduced initial or boundary value problem. A precise model-specific existence or characterization statement requires those missing data.

Short English statement

For the closure $f^{\star}(W(x,t),c)$, find variational test functions that preserve the kinetic model's physical conservation laws, derive the equations for $W$, and determine whether they form symmetric hyperbolic balance laws with an entropy. The kinetic model, closure, conserved quantities, domains, boundary conditions, and intended entropy requirements are unspecified, so the supplied question remains a modeling target requiring further hypotheses.
