Math-3998

Profinite Reconstruction of Complex Projective Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a connected smooth complex projective variety $X$, let $X^{\mathrm{an}}$ denote its underlying complex analytic space, choose a basepoint $x\in X^{\mathrm{an}}$, and write $\pi_1(X,x)=\pi_1(X^{\mathrm{an}},x)$ for its topological fundamental group. Its algebraic fundamental group is the profinite group classifying pointed finite étale covers; over ${\mathbb{C}}$, it is naturally identified with \[ \pi_1^{\mathrm{alg}}(X,x)\cong\widehat{\pi_1(X,x)} =\varprojlim_{\substack{N\trianglelefteq\pi_1(X,x)\\[\pi_1(X,x):N]<\infty}}\pi_1(X,x)/N. \] Here isomorphisms of algebraic fundamental groups mean continuous isomorphisms of profinite groups. The isomorphism types in the questions do not depend on the chosen basepoints.

On the connected-variety interpretation of the supplied source, the two reconstruction questions ask whether the following implications hold for every pair of connected smooth complex projective varieties $X,Y$ and choices of basepoints $x,y$:

  1. Does \[ \pi_1^{\mathrm{alg}}(X,x)\cong\pi_1^{\mathrm{alg}}(Y,y) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X^{\mathrm{an}}\cong_{\mathrm{homeo}}Y^{\mathrm{an}} \] hold, where the conclusion means that the underlying topological spaces are homeomorphic?
  2. Does \[ \pi_1^{\mathrm{alg}}(X,x)\cong\pi_1^{\mathrm{alg}}(Y,y) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \pi_1(X,x)\cong\pi_1(Y,y) \] hold, where the conclusion is an isomorphism of abstract groups?

Unresolved scope qualifications. The source does not explicitly state connectedness; the formulation above uses the connected interpretation conventionally needed for a single fundamental group to describe the variety. If disconnected varieties are intended, the treatment of their components must be specified. The source also gives no fixed dimension, asphericity hypothesis, or restricted comparison family. These implications transcribe the unrestricted reconstruction questions on the connected interpretation; whether the original discussion intended additional restrictions requires review.

Supplied literature qualification. The registry labels the problem partially solved. Its dated assessment reports profinite rigidity and homeomorphism recovery for important families, including products of hyperbolic Riemann surfaces, and describes both questions for arbitrary smooth complex projective varieties as remaining open. This is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion. The record does not supply precise statements or comparison classes for those reported results.

Short English statement

The source asks whether the algebraic fundamental group determines a smooth complex projective variety up to homeomorphism, and whether it determines the variety's topological fundamental group up to group isomorphism. The displayed formulation interprets determination by comparison with every other connected smooth complex projective variety. Connectedness and any intended restrictions on the comparison class require review. The supplied partially solved status and literature assessment are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
