Pleating-Ray Tilings of the Representation-Space Complement

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Put ${\mathbb{C}}^*={\mathbb{C}}\setminus\{0\}$. For $\omega\in{\mathbb{C}}^*$, consider the ordered pair of generators \[ A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad B_\omega=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\\omega&1\end{pmatrix}. \] Let $\mathcal R\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}^*$ denote the Riley slice associated with these marked generators, as designated in the supplied source, and set \[ X={\mathbb{C}}^*\setminus\overline{\mathcal R}. \] The closure may be taken in ${\mathbb{C}}$ or relatively in ${\mathbb{C}}^*$, since these give the same set $X$. The index set ${\mathbb{Q}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$ identifies rational numbers whose difference is an even integer.

Conjecture, as supplied. There exists a natural tiling of $X$ by quadrilaterals $\mathcal R_c(r)$, indexed by every $r\in{\mathbb{Q}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$, such that each $\mathcal R_c(r)$ contains the extended pleating ray of slope $r$ as a diagonal. Each tile arises from restrictions of holonomy representations of hyperbolic Dehn fillings of \[ S^3\setminus\left(K(r^*)\cup\alpha_r\right). \] Here the hyperbolic qualification is retained from the supplied original formulation.

Unresolved specifications. The supplied record does not give a membership definition of the Riley slice, including the precise representation and marking conventions. It also does not define the extended pleating rays, the operation $r\mapsto r^*$, the objects $K(r^*)$ and $\alpha_r$, or the subscript $c$. The permitted Dehn fillings, the groups to which holonomy representations are restricted, and the identification of those restrictions with parameters $\omega$ are unspecified. Finally, the meanings of natural, quadrilateral, diagonal, and tiling, including boundary membership and allowed intersections, are not specified. Consequently this is the source's conjectural assertion with its unresolved terminology preserved; the supplied record alone does not determine a fully specified tiling theorem.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether the complement of the closed Riley slice in ${\mathbb{C}}^*$ has quadrilateral tiles indexed by rational slopes modulo $2$, with extended pleating rays as diagonals and a construction using restricted hyperbolic Dehn-filling holonomy representations. Several objects and boundary conventions remain undefined in the supplied record, so a precise final formulation requires mathematical source review.
