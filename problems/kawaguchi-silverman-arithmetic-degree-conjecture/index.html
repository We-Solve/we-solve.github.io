Kawaguchi–Silverman Arithmetic Degree Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K\subset\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ be a number field, let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over $K$, and let $f$ be a dominant self-map defined over $K$. The supplied record does not specify whether a self-map must be a morphism $f\colon X\to X$ or may be a rational map $f\colon X\dashrightarrow X$. Both interpretations are described below; the intended scope requires confirmation.

For the rational-map interpretation, write $I_f$ for the indeterminacy locus and require that $P\in X(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})$ have a well-defined forward orbit: starting with $P_0=P$, each $P_n$ lies outside $I_f$, and $P_{n+1}=f(P_n)$ for every integer $n\geq 0$. Write \[ \mathcal O_f(P)=\{P_n:n\geq 0\}. \] For a morphism, every algebraic point has a well-defined forward orbit. Zariski density means \[ \overline{\mathcal O_f(P)}^{\mathrm{Zar}}=X_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}}. \]

Choose an ample Cartier divisor $H$ on $X$ and an associated logarithmic Weil height $h_H\colon X(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})\to{\mathbb{R}}$. Set $h_H^+(Q)=\max\{1,h_H(Q)\}$. The arithmetic degree, when its defining limit exists, is \[ \alpha(f,P)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(h_H^+(P_n)\right)^{1/n}. \] For $d=\dim X\geq 1$, interpret the dynamical degree in the record as the first dynamical degree \[ \delta(f)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\left((f^n)^*H\right)\cdot H^{d-1}\right)^{1/n}. \] Here the intersection number is taken on $X$; for rational maps, pullback is defined through the graph of the indicated iterate $f^n$. In particular, the formula uses $(f^n)^*$, without assuming it equals $(f^*)^n$. These are the usual height and intersection definitions; the resulting degrees, when defined as above, do not depend on the ample divisor or associated height chosen.

Conjectural statement. For every such $K,X,f$ and every algebraic point $P$ with a well-defined Zariski-dense forward orbit, does the arithmetic-degree limit exist and satisfy \[ \alpha(f,P)=\delta(f)? \] Under the morphism interpretation, the well-defined-orbit condition is automatic. Under the rational-map interpretation, it is a necessary explicit qualification absent from the supplied wording. The record also does not state whether existence of the arithmetic-degree limit is assumed or is part of the conjectural conclusion; the displayed formulation includes existence in the conclusion. The intersection formula above treats positive-dimensional varieties; the source does not specify a convention for dimension zero.

The supplied attribution identifies this as the Kawaguchi--Silverman conjecture. Its supplied dated assessment labels the problem partially solved, says that the abelian-variety case is proved, mentions further polarized, int-amplified, and other cases, and says that the general dominant-map claim remains open. Those are supplied curation claims, not mathematical or literature findings independently verified here; the hypotheses of the cited special cases are not supplied.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every algebraic point with a Zariski-dense forward orbit has height growth rate equal to the first dynamical degree of the map. The source leaves unresolved whether rational maps are included and whether existence of the height-growth limit is assumed. Rational maps require every iterate of the point to be defined. The supplied partially solved label and descriptions of known cases require independent review.
