Math-3419

Algebraic Stories — Polynomial generation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let $p$ be a prime and identify each element $a\in\mathbb F_p$ with its integer representative $\bar a\in\{0,\ldots,p-1\}$ when used as an exponent. Define \[Z(f)=\{a\in\mathbb F_p:f(a)=0\},\qquad\phi(f)=\sum_{a\in Z(f)}x^{\bar a},\quad f\in\mathbb F_p[x]. \] After one application the degree is at most $p-1$, so every orbit eventually enters a finite periodic cycle. A cycle of length $q\geq1$ consists of distinct polynomials $f_0,\ldots,f_{q-1}$ with $\phi(f_j)=f_{j+1}$, indices taken modulo $q$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For each prime $p$, determine all periodic cycles of $\phi$ in one variable and, in particular, all their possible lengths. [S2]

Short English statement

Classify the cycles and cycle lengths of the map that records a polynomial’s zeros as monomial exponents.
