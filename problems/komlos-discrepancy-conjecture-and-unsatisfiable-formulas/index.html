Komlos Discrepancy Conjecture and Unsatisfiable Formulas

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For positive integers $m,n$, let $A=(a_{ij})\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m\times n}$ have columns $v_1,\ldots,v_n\in{\mathbb{R}}^m$ satisfying \[ \|v_j\|_2=\left(\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}^2\right)^{1/2}\leq 1\qquad(1\leq j\leq n). \] Define its signed coordinate discrepancy by \[ \operatorname{disc}(A):=\min_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}\|A\varepsilon\|_\infty =\min_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}\max_{1\leq i\leq m}\left|\sum_{j=1}^n\varepsilon_j a_{ij}\right|. \] The broader target in the supplied curated statement is the Komlós conjecture, in its standard real finite-dimensional formulation: \[ \exists C\in[0,\infty)\ \ \forall m,n\geq1\ \ \forall A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m\times n},\qquad \left[\forall j,\ \sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}^2\leq1\right] \Longrightarrow \operatorname{disc}(A)\leq C. \] Thus $C$ must be independent of both dimensions and of the vectors. The real ambient field is an explicit conventional interpretation; the supplied excerpt does not specify it.

The narrower question attributed to Dmitriy (Tim) Kunisky asks whether discrepancy constructions from unsatisfiable Boolean formulas can exceed the lower bound $1+\sqrt{2}$. A Boolean formula is unsatisfiable if no assignment of truth values to its variables makes it true. Interpreting the construction as producing a matrix normalized as above, the requested strict lower bound would mean \[ \operatorname{disc}(A)>1+\sqrt{2}, \] or equivalently \[ \forall\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n\quad\exists i\in\{1,\ldots,m\}:\quad \left|\sum_{j=1}^n\varepsilon_j a_{ij}\right|>1+\sqrt{2}. \] However, the supplied record does not define the permitted formulas, the formula-to-matrix construction, or its normalization. Consequently, the intended restricted existence question cannot be specified completely from this record. The broader conjecture appears explicitly in the curated statement; the extracted original question explicitly asks about exceeding the construction lower bound.

Short English statement

Can the intended constructions from unsatisfiable Boolean formulas produce normalized discrepancy instances with discrepancy strictly greater than $1+\sqrt{2}$? The construction is unspecified in the supplied record. The broader curated target asks for a universal bound on signed coordinate discrepancy for every finite family of vectors of Euclidean norm at most one.
