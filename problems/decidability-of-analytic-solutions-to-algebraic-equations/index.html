Math-4193

Decidability of Analytic Solutions to Algebraic Equations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be part of the input, and interpret an algebraic equation with coefficients in ${\mathbb{Z}}[z]$ as a single polynomial equation \[ P(z,X_1,\ldots,X_n)=0, \qquad P\in{\mathbb{Z}}[z,X_1,\ldots,X_n], \] with no differentiation. The polynomial is specified by its finite list of integer coefficients and monomials. For an open set $U\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$, let $\mathcal O(U)$ denote the ring of single-valued holomorphic functions on $U$. A solution on $U$ is a tuple $(f_1,\ldots,f_n)\in\mathcal O(U)^n$ satisfying \[ P\left(z,f_1(z),\ldots,f_n(z)\right)=0 \quad\text{for every }z\in U. \] The source asks about deciding existence of such solutions under the following domain requirements:
  1. Analytic at $0$: there exists an open set $U\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ containing $0$ on which a solution exists. Equivalently, the unknown functions are holomorphic germs at $0$ satisfying the equation as an identity of germs.
  2. Analytic on an open superset of the unit disc: there exists an open set $U\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ containing the unit disc on which a solution exists. The supplied record does not specify whether "unit disc" means \[ \mathbb D=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|<1\} \quad\text{or}\quad \overline{\mathbb D}=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|\leq1\}. \] Thus, for either specified choice $K\in\{\mathbb D,\overline{\mathbb D}\}$, the corresponding precise predicate is \[ \exists\,U\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}\text{ open},\quad K\subseteq U,\quad \exists\,(f_1,\ldots,f_n)\in\mathcal O(U)^n, \quad P(z,f_1(z),\ldots,f_n(z))\equiv0\text{ on }U. \] The source also does not explicitly say whether "superset" requires strict containment. These alternatives are left unresolved rather than identified.
  3. Analytic on the whole complex plane: there exists a tuple $(f_1,\ldots,f_n)\in\mathcal O({\mathbb{C}})^n$ satisfying the equation identically on ${\mathbb{C}}$.
Decision question. For each intended domain requirement, does there exist an algorithm which, for every input $n\geq1$ and $P\in{\mathbb{Z}}[z,X_1,\ldots,X_n]$, terminates and answers yes exactly when a solution with that requirement exists, and answers no otherwise? The domain requirements are treated here as separate decision questions. The source's use of "or" does not explicitly distinguish separate questions from a combined disjunctive question; the intended logical organization and the unit-disc convention require confirmation from the source. No solution tuple is required as algorithmic output.

Short English statement

Given one polynomial equation in finitely many unknown functions of the complex variable $z$, with coefficients in ${\mathbb{Z}}[z]$, ask whether existence of a holomorphic solution can be decided near $0$, on an open set containing the unit disc, or on all of ${\mathbb{C}}$. The unit-disc boundary convention and the logical role of "or" remain unresolved. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
