Math-869

Action Dimension versus $L^{2}$ Cohomological Dimension

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. The supplied record asks about every group $G$, without stating countability, torsion, or finiteness hypotheses. The following makes a conventional interpretation explicit; the source record does not itself specify all the conventions below.

Regard $G$ as a discrete group. Define its action dimension by \[ \operatorname{actdim}(G)=\inf\left\{n\in{\mathbb{N}}_0:\ G\text{ acts properly by homeomorphisms on a nonempty contractible }n\text{-manifold}\right\}, \] with $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. Here manifolds are taken to be Hausdorff and second countable, without boundary, and properness means that for every compact subset $K$ of the manifold, the set $\{g\in G:gK\cap K\ne\varnothing\}$ is finite. This definition allows finite stabilizers.

For the interpretation of $L^2$-dimension through $L^2$-Betti numbers, let $\mathcal N(G)$ be the group von Neumann algebra and set \[ b_n^{(2)}(G)=\dim_{\mathcal N(G)}H_n(G;\mathcal N(G)),\qquad \dim_{(2)}(G)=\sup\left(\{0\}\cup\{n\in{\mathbb{N}}_0:b_n^{(2)}(G)>0\}\right). \] Thus this convention gives dimension zero when all $L^2$-Betti numbers vanish, and allows dimension $+\infty$.

Action Dimension Conjecture, under these conventions. Is it true that every discrete group $G$ satisfies \[ \operatorname{actdim}(G)\geq 2\dim_{(2)}(G), \] where the inequality is interpreted in the extended nonnegative real numbers?

Unresolved source qualifications. The original statement writes $\ell^2\operatorname{dim}(G)$, while the cleaned statement calls this the $L^2$-cohomological dimension. The supplied record does not establish that this means precisely the nonvanishing degree of $L^2$-Betti numbers defined above, or specify its value in the all-vanishing case. It also does not specify the allowable manifold category, boundary convention, or action convention. These choices require source confirmation; the displayed formulation is a qualified interpretation, not a verified expansion of the source.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the least dimension of a contractible manifold admitting a proper action of $G$ is at least twice its $L^2$-dimension. The draft spells out one conventional interpretation, but the exact dimension and action conventions require human review. The supplied record labels the problem open.
