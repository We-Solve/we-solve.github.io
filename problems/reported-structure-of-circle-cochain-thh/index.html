Reported Structure of Circle-Cochain THH

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb S$ denote the sphere spectrum. For a space $X$, let $X_+$ be $X$ with a disjoint basepoint and write \[ D_+(X)=F(\Sigma^\infty X_+,\mathbb S) \] for its spherical cochain ring spectrum. Here $F$ denotes the function spectrum; the supplied passage also uses the notation $F(X_+,\mathbb S)$ and $\mathbb S^X$. Let $B{\mathbb{Z}}$ be the classifying space of the discrete group ${\mathbb{Z}}$. The supplied setup is \[ A=D_+(S^1)\simeq D_+(B{\mathbb{Z}})\simeq\mathbb S^{h{\mathbb{Z}}}, \qquad R=\operatorname{THH}(A), \] where ${\mathbb{Z}}$ acts trivially on $\mathbb S$, the superscript $h{\mathbb{Z}}$ denotes homotopy fixed points, and $\operatorname{THH}$ denotes topological Hochschild homology. The equivalences use $S^1\simeq B{\mathbb{Z}}$.

The passage states that for any ${\mathbb{Z}}$-equivariant $A_\infty$ ring spectrum $B$, there is a natural map \[ R\longrightarrow\operatorname{THH}(B^{h{\mathbb{Z}}}). \] This is retained as a source assertion describing the setup. The precise conventions for equivariant $A_\infty$ ring spectra, including units and compatibility of the action with multiplication, are not specified in the extract. The passage also describes the usual circle action on $R$, calls this the outer circle, and denotes it by $T$. Its next sentence is truncated before explaining what this circle is to be distinguished from.

The passage attributes a role in the disproof of the Telescope Conjecture to $R$, or its $p$-adic completion. This contextual claim is supplied attribution, not an independently verified conclusion here; neither a prime $p$ nor a completion convention is specified.

Short English statement

The extract, attributed to Neil P. Strickland, describes the topological Hochschild homology of spherical cochains on the circle and its outer circle action. It supplies no complete mathematical question, so this draft preserves the setup and explicitly leaves the intended problem unresolved.
