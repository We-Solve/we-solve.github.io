Donaldson's Almost-Complex Calabi–Yau Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record attributes the following a priori estimate conjecture to Donaldson. We formulate its usual closed-manifold interpretation explicitly: the source says "compact" but does not specify whether a boundary is allowed or supply boundary conditions.

Let $M$ be a compact smooth real four-manifold without boundary, and let $\omega$ be a symplectic form, meaning a smooth closed nondegenerate real two-form. Orient $M$ by $\omega^2=\omega\wedge\omega$. Let $J:TM\to TM$ be a smooth almost-complex structure, so that $J^2=-\operatorname{id}_{TM}$, tamed by $\omega$, meaning \[ \omega(v,Jv)>0\qquad\text{for every nonzero }v\in TM. \] Let $\sigma$ be a smooth volume form, positive for this orientation, satisfying \[ \int_M\sigma=\int_M\omega\wedge\omega. \] Positivity is necessary for the solutions considered below; it is implicit in the intended volume-form convention.

A symplectic form $\widetilde\omega$ is compatible with $J$ if, at every point, \[ \widetilde\omega(Ju,Jv)=\widetilde\omega(u,v),\qquad \widetilde\omega(v,Jv)>0\quad(v\ne0). \] Fix a smooth auxiliary Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$, and define \[ \|\eta\|_{C^k(g)}=\sum_{j=0}^{k}\sup_{x\in M}|\nabla_g^{\,j}\eta(x)|_g \] for smooth two-forms $\eta$, where $\nabla_g$ is the Levi--Civita connection.

Conjectural a priori bounds. For every such fixed datum $(M,\omega,J,\sigma)$ and every integer $k\ge0$, is there a finite constant \[ C_k=C_k(M,\omega,J,\sigma;g) \] such that every smooth symplectic form $\widetilde\omega$ satisfying \[ [\widetilde\omega]=[\omega]\ \text{in }H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;{\mathbb{R}}),\qquad \widetilde\omega\text{ is compatible with }J,\qquad \widetilde\omega\wedge\widetilde\omega=\sigma \] obeys \[ \|\widetilde\omega\|_{C^k(g)}\le C_k? \] The constants must be independent of the particular solution $\widetilde\omega$. The auxiliary metric merely specifies the norms; it is fixed throughout. This is a conditional estimate for existing solutions, with no existence assertion. The original wording omits the integral normalization, whereas the supplied clean statement includes it; for a closed manifold it is necessary by cohomology.

Short English statement

Donaldson's conjecture asks whether all derivatives of every $J$-compatible symplectic solution of $\widetilde\omega^2=\sigma$ in the fixed cohomology class $[\omega]$ admit bounds depending only on the prescribed geometric data and derivative order. The boundaryless interpretation needs confirmation because the source specifies compactness without boundary conditions.
