Nonconstancy of the Multiplication-Table Limit Profile

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f_{\mathrm{Mult}}$ denote the limiting profile referred to in the supplied record for the multiplication-table problem. Its definition, domain, normalization, and governing asymptotic formula are not supplied. Write $D$ for its intended domain, without specifying that domain. The supplied nonconstancy conjecture is \[ \exists x,y\in D\quad f_{\mathrm{Mult}}(x)\ne f_{\mathrm{Mult}}(y). \] The record expresses the conjecture as \[ \frac{\max_{x\in D} f_{\mathrm{Mult}}(x)}{\min_{x\in D} f_{\mathrm{Mult}}(x)}>1. \] This ratio formulation is equivalent to nonconstancy if the maximum and minimum exist as finite real numbers and the minimum is strictly positive. These hypotheses are not established in the supplied excerpt. Thus the display records the intended conjecture, but a complete mathematical formulation requires the missing definition of the profile and the hypotheses justifying its extrema and positivity.

For context, for a positive integer $n$, put $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. The description of the multiplication table gives the intended counting function \[ M(n)=\left|\{ab:a,b\in[n]\}\right|. \] The extracted source instead restricts the counted products to $[n]$, while calling the result the number of distinct entries in the $n\times n$ table. These are incompatible descriptions; the displayed definition follows the verbal description and explicitly records this discrepancy. The source also describes $M(n)$ as roughly the number of integers in $[n^2]$ having a divisor in $[n/2,n]$; it supplies no precise meaning of "roughly."

With logarithms interpreted as natural logarithms, the source gives the Erdős--Tenenbaum--Ford constant \[ \delta=1-\frac{1+\log\log 2}{\log 2}\approx0.0860713. \] It attributes the exponent-scale estimate $M(n)=n^2(\log n)^{-\delta+o(1)}$ to Erdős and the sharper comparison \[ M(n)\asymp n^2(\log n)^{-\delta}(\log\log n)^{-3/2} \qquad(n\to\infty) \] to Ford. Here $A(n)\asymp B(n)$ means that positive constants $c,C$ satisfy $cB(n)\le A(n)\le CB(n)$ for all sufficiently large $n$. These are supplied background attributions, not independently checked results.

The extracted passage appears to report \[ \frac{\max_x f_{\mathrm{Mult}}(x)}{\min_x f_{\mathrm{Mult}}(x)} \le \frac{\max_x g(x)}{\min_x g(x)} <1+2\cdot10^{-7}, \] but its formula layout is damaged and the definition of $g$ is unreadable. This comparison cannot be certified from the supplied text. The supplied dated assessment attributes an asymptotic and a rigorous upper ratio bound to Green and Sawhney, while describing the strict lower bound above as unproved. The excerpt also says that an inequality of this type can be proved for divisors of smooth numbers in sufficiently short dyadic intervals; it supplies no quantitative hypotheses or precise theorem for that comparison. Its statement of "Theorem 1 (Green-S. '25+)" is truncated. None of these incomplete passages supplies the missing definition of $f_{\mathrm{Mult}}$.

Short English statement

The intended conjecture says that the limiting profile for the number of distinct products in a multiplication table takes at least two different values. The supplied record does not define that profile or its domain. Its maximum-to-minimum ratio formulation additionally requires attained finite extrema and a strictly positive minimum. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
