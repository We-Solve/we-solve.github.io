Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 37

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be an arbitrary field and $G$ a finite group. A central division algebra over $F$ is a finite-dimensional associative division algebra $A$ whose center is $F$; its degree is $\deg(A)=\sqrt{\dim_F A}$. A $G$-crossed product division algebra over $F$ means such an algebra $A$ containing a maximal commutative subfield $L$ for which $L/F$ is Galois and $\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\cong G$. In particular, $[L:F]=\deg(A)=|G|$.

The group $G$ is called $F$-admissible if such an algebra exists. Determine, for an arbitrary field $F$, precisely which finite groups $G$ are $F$-admissible; equivalently, characterize the pairs $(F,G)$ for which there exist a finite-dimensional central division $F$-algebra $A$ and a subfield $L\subseteq A$ satisfying \[ L/F\text{ is Galois},\qquad \operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\cong G,\qquad [L:F]=\deg(A)=|G|. \] The supplied formulation imposes no restriction on the characteristic or other properties of $F$.

Short English statement

For each field $F$, classify the finite groups that occur as the Galois group of a maximal subfield of a finite-dimensional central division algebra over $F$. The field extension and division algebra are both allowed to vary.
