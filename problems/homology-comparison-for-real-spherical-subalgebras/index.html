Homology Comparison for Real Spherical Subalgebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied formulation omits the ambient reductive group and the precise homology convention. The following describes the usual interpretation and makes these unresolved qualifications explicit.

Let $G$ be an ambient real reductive group, let $\mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}(G)$, and let $K$ be a maximal compact subgroup. The precise class of groups intended by the source is unspecified. The infinitesimal real-sphericity condition on a real Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak h\subseteq\mathfrak g$ is that there exists a minimal parabolic subalgebra $\mathfrak q\subseteq\mathfrak g$ such that \[ \mathfrak g=\mathfrak h+\mathfrak q \] as real vector spaces. Whether the source additionally requires $\mathfrak h$ to arise from a closed or algebraic subgroup is not stated.

Write $\mathfrak g_{{\mathbb{C}}}=\mathfrak g\otimes_{{\mathbb{R}}}{\mathbb{C}}$ and $\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}}=\mathfrak h\otimes_{{\mathbb{R}}}{\mathbb{C}}$. In the usual complex-coefficient interpretation, a Harish--Chandra module $V$ is an admissible, finitely generated $(\mathfrak g_{{\mathbb{C}}},K)$-module: its compatible $K$-action is locally finite, and every irreducible $K$-type has finite multiplicity. Let $i:V\hookrightarrow V^{\infty}$ be the inclusion into the smooth moderate-growth completion named in the source. Conventionally this means a smooth Fréchet globalization whose $K$-finite vectors identify with $V$; the exact globalization category is not specified in the record.

For clarity, under the ordinary algebraic Lie algebra homology convention, set \[ C_p(\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}},W)=\bigwedge^p\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}}\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}}W, \qquad H_p^{\mathrm{alg}}(\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}},W) =\frac{\ker(d_p)}{\operatorname{im}(d_{p+1})}, \] where $d_p$ is the Chevalley--Eilenberg differential for the restricted $\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}}$-action on $W$. This quotient uses the actual image, without replacing it by its closure. The inclusion $i$ induces the chain map $\operatorname{id}\otimes i$ and hence the comparison map.

Comparison question, conditional on these conventions. For every ambient datum satisfying the intended source hypotheses, every real spherical $\mathfrak h$, every Harish--Chandra module $V$, and every integer $p\geq 0$, is \[ \Phi_p=H_p^{\mathrm{alg}}(\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}},i): H_p^{\mathrm{alg}}(\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}},V) \longrightarrow H_p^{\mathrm{alg}}(\mathfrak h_{{\mathbb{C}}},V^{\infty}) \] an isomorphism of complex vector spaces?

The source writes this map as $H_p(\mathfrak h,V)\to H_p(\mathfrak h,V^{\infty})$, without specifying complexification, algebraic versus topological homology, reduced versus unreduced homology, or whether a topological isomorphism is intended. Consequently the displayed interpretation is a conditional statement-only draft, not a resolution of those missing conventions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether passing from a Harish--Chandra module to its smooth moderate-growth completion preserves every nonnegative-degree Lie algebra homology group for a real spherical subalgebra. The ambient group, globalization category, and homology conventions need clarification. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved; that assessment has not been independently verified.
