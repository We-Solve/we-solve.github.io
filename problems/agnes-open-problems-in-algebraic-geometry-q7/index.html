AGNES Open Problems in Algebraic Geometry — Q7

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, the supplied question concerns the moduli space denoted by $M^{K3}_{2n}$. Under the conventional interpretation as the moduli space of complex primitively polarized $K3$ surfaces, its points represent pairs $(S,H)$, where $S$ is a smooth projective complex $K3$ surface and $H$ is an ample line bundle with primitive first Chern class and intersection number $H^2=2n$. The supplied record does not specify the ground field, primitivity convention, stack versus coarse moduli space, or the quantification over $n$; this interpretation is therefore provisional.

Under this interpretation, a Noether--Lefschetz divisor is an irreducible divisorial component of a locus where $\operatorname{Pic}(S)$ contains a class $\beta$ independent of $H$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, with prescribed intersection numbers $H\cdot\beta$ and $\beta^2$. Let $\mathcal D_{\mathrm{NL}}$ denote the collection of these divisors. Here $\operatorname{Pic}(M^{K3}_{2n})$ is the group of line bundles on the chosen moduli object. Interpreting each divisor as a rational Picard class, the question is whether \[ \operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\left\{[D]:D\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{NL}}\right\} = \operatorname{Pic}(M^{K3}_{2n})\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}. \] Equivalently, must every rational Picard class be a finite rational linear combination of Noether--Lefschetz divisor classes? The choice of moduli object and the interpretation of divisor classes must be fixed for a definitive formulation.

The supplied motivation considers a compact Calabi--Yau threefold $X$, a $K3$ fibration $\pi:X\to{\mathbb{P}}^1$, and an ample line bundle $L$ on $X$. It states that this family determines a morphism from ${\mathbb{P}}^1$ to the moduli of polarized $K3$ surfaces. The record gives no hypotheses on singular fibers or treatment of degenerations, so the asserted morphism over the entire base needs qualification; a smooth polarized family gives the corresponding moduli map over its smooth locus.

As historical background, the supplied source attributes to [mpnl] a calculation of the Gromov--Witten theory of $X$ in $\pi$-fiber classes in terms of the Noether--Lefschetz numbers of $\pi$ and the Katz--Klemm--Vafa conjecture [kkv] concerning $\lambda_g$ integrals in the reduced Gromov--Witten theory of a fixed $K3$ surface. It reports that the conjecture is proved for all classes in genus zero in [kmps] and for primitive classes in all genera in [mpt]. These are supplied historical source statements, not independently verified literature assessments.

Short English statement

Do Noether--Lefschetz divisors generate every rational Picard class on the moduli space $M^{K3}_{2n}$ of polarized $K3$ surfaces? The precise moduli convention and the intended quantification over $n$ require clarification. The registry labels the problem open; that label has not been independently verified.
