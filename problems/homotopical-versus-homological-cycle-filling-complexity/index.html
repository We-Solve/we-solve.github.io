Homotopical versus Homological Cycle-Filling Complexity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite connected graph, regarded as a one-dimensional CW complex, with each edge assigned length $1$. For a closed edge path $c$, let $\ell(c)$ be its number of edge traversals, counted with multiplicity. The supplied record does not specify whether its attaching cycles must be simple cycles or may be arbitrary closed edge paths. Accordingly, fix a class $\mathcal C(G)$ of admissible attaching cycles; the two interpretations to be distinguished are all simple cycles and all nonconstant closed edge paths. The definitions and questions below are conditional on that choice.

For a finite list $c_1,\ldots,c_m\in\mathcal C(G)$, let \[ X(G;c_1,\ldots,c_m)=G\cup_{c_1}D^2\cup\cdots\cup_{c_m}D^2 \] be the space obtained by attaching one $2$-cell along each listed path. Define \[ \alpha_{\pi_1}^{\mathcal C}(G)=\min\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\ell(c_i):m\geq 0,\ c_i\in\mathcal C(G),\ \pi_1\left(X(G;c_1,\ldots,c_m)\right)=\{1\}\right\} \] and \[ \alpha_{\mathbb F_2}^{\mathcal C}(G)=\min\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\ell(c_i):m\geq 0,\ c_i\in\mathcal C(G),\ H_1\left(X(G;c_1,\ldots,c_m);\mathbb F_2\right)=0\right\}, \] where $\mathbb F_2$ is the field with two elements and the empty list has cost $0$. Equivalently, the homological condition says that the classes $[c_1],\ldots,[c_m]$ span $H_1(G;\mathbb F_2)$.

The supplied question asks whether, for every real $R>0$, there exists a finite connected graph $G$ with $H_1(G;\mathbb F_2)\neq 0$ such that \[ \frac{\alpha_{\pi_1}^{\mathcal C}(G)}{\alpha_{\mathbb F_2}^{\mathcal C}(G)}>R. \] The restriction to graphs with nonzero first homology makes the ratio defined: for a tree both costs are $0$.

The bounded-degree question asks whether there exists a fixed integer $D\geq 1$ such that, for every real $R>0$, there exists such a graph $G$ satisfying additionally \[ \Delta(G)\leq D, \qquad \frac{\alpha_{\pi_1}^{\mathcal C}(G)}{\alpha_{\mathbb F_2}^{\mathcal C}(G)}>R, \] where $\Delta(G)$ denotes maximum vertex degree. Thus the degree bound must be independent of $R$.

The notation $\alpha_{\pi_1}^{\mathcal C}$ and $\alpha_{\mathbb F_2}^{\mathcal C}$ makes the unresolved attaching-cycle convention explicit; identifying these quantities with the source's $\alpha_{\pi_1}$ and $\alpha_{\mathbb F_2}$ requires checking that convention. The record also does not specify whether graphs must be simple or may have loops and parallel edges. These choices remain unresolved in this statement-only draft.

Short English statement

Can the least total length of cycles whose attached disks make a finite connected graph simply connected exceed, by an arbitrarily large factor, the least total length needed to eliminate first homology over $\mathbb F_2$? Can this happen with a single uniform bound on vertex degrees? Trees are excluded from the ratio, and the precise graph and attaching-cycle conventions require confirmation.
