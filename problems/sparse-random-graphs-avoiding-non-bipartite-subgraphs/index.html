Sparse Random Graphs Avoiding Non-Bipartite Subgraphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks for density thresholds and typical structure of uniformly random $H$-free graphs with $m$ edges, where $H$ is nonbipartite. A graph is nonbipartite if its vertices cannot be partitioned into two sets such that every edge has one endpoint in each set.

The record does not specify the number of vertices, the graph conventions, the asymptotic regime, or the structural properties whose thresholds are sought. Consequently, it does not determine a unique precise threshold problem. The following describes a possible conventional framework, conditional on assumptions that require confirmation.

Assume that graphs are finite, simple, undirected, and labelled; that $H$ is fixed independently of the number $n$ of vertices; and that $H$-free means containing no subgraph isomorphic to $H$, without requiring that subgraph to be induced. For a positive integer $n$ and an integer $m$ satisfying $0\le m\le\binom{n}{2}$, put \[ \mathcal F_H(n,m)=\left\{G:V(G)=\{1,\ldots,n\},\ |E(G)|=m,\ G\text{ contains no subgraph isomorphic to }H\right\}. \] Whenever $\mathcal F_H(n,m)\ne\varnothing$, let $G_{n,m}^{(H)}$ have the uniform distribution on this family, so that \[ \Pr\left(G_{n,m}^{(H)}=G\right)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal F_H(n,m)|} \qquad\text{for every }G\in\mathcal F_H(n,m). \] In this framework, a proposed structural property $\mathcal P_n$ is typical along a sequence $m=m(n)$ if \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\Pr\left(G_{n,m(n)}^{(H)}\in\mathcal P_n\right)=1, \] with the sampling family nonempty for all sufficiently large $n$.

Requested determination, with unresolved scope. For a general nonbipartite graph $H$, determine the density regimes and the structural properties that hold typically for the uniformly sampled $H$-free graph with $m$ edges, together with the thresholds between the intended structural regimes. The source does not identify those properties or regimes, specify whether density means edge count, edge density $m/\binom{n}{2}$, or another normalization, or state whether threshold means an order of magnitude, a sharp transition, or an exact asymptotic constant. These choices, as well as the conditional graph conventions above, must be resolved before this request can be expressed as a specific quantified threshold assertion.

Short English statement

For a nonbipartite forbidden graph $H$, describe how the typical structure of a uniformly random $H$-free graph changes with its number $m$ of edges. The supplied record leaves the vertex count, sampling conventions, limiting regime, and target structural transitions unspecified; the displayed model is therefore conditional and requires human mathematical review.
