Positive Energy Conditions and Spaces of Initial Data

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses a research question rather than a fully quantified conjecture. The following notation describes a conventional framework; the manifold hypotheses and the intended classes remain to be specified.

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold. An initial data set on $M$ consists of a Riemannian metric $g$ and a smooth symmetric covariant $2$-tensor $k$. Under the conventional constraint normalization with zero cosmological constant, define the energy density $\rho$ and momentum density $J$ by \[ 2\rho(g,k)=\operatorname{Scal}_g+(\operatorname{tr}_g k)^2-|k|_g^2, \qquad J(g,k)=\operatorname{div}_g k-\mathrm d(\operatorname{tr}_g k). \] The strict dominant energy condition is the pointwise inequality \[ \rho(g,k)(x)>|J(g,k)(x)|_g\qquad\text{for every }x\in M. \] This normalization is an explicit interpretive convention, not an additional hypothesis stated in the source.

Write \[ \mathcal R^{>0}(M)=\{g:\operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0\text{ for every }x\in M\}, \] \[ \mathcal I^{\mathrm{strict}}(M) =\{(g,k):\rho(g,k)(x)>|J(g,k)(x)|_g\text{ for every }x\in M\}. \] Any restrictions on the metrics and tensors, including boundary or asymptotic conditions, must be specified before these sets define the intended spaces. With compatible restrictions and topologies, the time-symmetric assignment gives a map \[ \iota:\mathcal R^{>0}(M)\longrightarrow\mathcal I^{\mathrm{strict}}(M), \qquad \iota(g)=(g,0), \] since $\rho(g,0)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Scal}_g$ and $J(g,0)=0$.

Source question. Which known results concerning nonnegative or positive scalar curvature extend to initial data sets satisfying the strict dominant energy condition? In particular, do the nontrivial homotopy classes detected in the space of positive-scalar-curvature metrics give nontrivial classes in the corresponding space of initial data sets?

A possible precise interpretation of the homotopy question, after fixing the missing hypotheses, is the following: for a specified base metric $g_0$, degree $q\geq1$, and specified detected class \[ 0\ne\alpha\in\pi_q(\mathcal R^{>0}(M),g_0), \] is \[ \iota_*(\alpha)\ne0 \quad\text{in}\quad \pi_q(\mathcal I^{\mathrm{strict}}(M),(g_0,0))? \] The source does not identify the classes, detection methods, degrees, or hypotheses under which this implication is intended, nor does it explicitly specify $\iota$ as the comparison map. It therefore does not justify replacing its question by an unconditional injectivity conjecture. Whether path components are also intended is unspecified. Moreover, a metric with merely nonnegative scalar curvature yields only $\rho(g,0)\geq|J(g,0)|_g$, so the nonnegative-scalar-curvature portion requires a separate formulation when the target condition is strict.

Short English statement

The question asks whether results about nonnegative or positive scalar curvature, especially detected nontrivial homotopy classes of positive-scalar-curvature metrics, extend to spaces of initial data satisfying the strict dominant energy condition. The manifold hypotheses, topology, comparison map, and classes to be preserved are unspecified, so the displayed homotopy question is a conditional interpretation requiring mathematical review.
