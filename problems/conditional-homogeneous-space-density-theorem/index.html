In the usual number-field interpretation, let $k$ be a number field, let $\bar{k}$ be an algebraic closure, and let $G$ be a semisimple simply connected algebraic group over $k$. Let $X$ be a homogeneous $G$-space over $k$, meaning that $G_{\bar{k}}$ acts transitively on $X_{\bar{k}}$. The number-field hypothesis and this description of $X$ are contextual reconstructions, not explicitly supplied hypotheses of the extracted statement. For a geometric point $\bar{x}\in X(\bar{k})$, its geometric stabilizer is \[ H=\operatorname{Stab}_{G_{\bar{k}}}(\bar{x}). \] The supplied hypotheses require $H$ to be finite and solvable. Here solvability means that the derived series of its geometric-point group terminates in the trivial group.

Under this number-field interpretation, write ${\mathbb{A}}_k$ for the adele ring of $k$, and write \[ \operatorname{Br}(X)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}} \] for the cohomological Brauer group. The Brauer--Manin set is \[ X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}(X)} =\left\{(x_v)_v\in X({\mathbb{A}}_k): \sum_v\operatorname{inv}_v\left(b(x_v)\right)=0\text{ in }{\mathbb{Q}}/{\mathbb{Z}} \text{ for every }b\in\operatorname{Br}(X)\right\}, \] where $v$ ranges over the places of $k$ and $\operatorname{inv}_v$ is the local Brauer invariant.

Short English statement

The extracted assertion concerns density of rational points in a Brauer--Manin set for a homogeneous space with semisimple simply connected acting group and finite solvable geometric stabilizer, assuming an unidentified Conjecture 9.1. Its field, precise space, and density topology remain unresolved. Supplied verification describes the conditional assertion as a theorem in the source, despite the retained open label; this draft requires human mathematical and source review.
