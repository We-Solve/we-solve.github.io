Math-7272

Reflectionless Potentials and Global Camassa–Holm Solutions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks two distinct questions in the setting of its Sturm--Liouville hierarchy. Write $\mathcal S_{\mathrm{SW}}$ for the Segal--Wilson potentials in that setting and $\mathcal R$ for its reflectionless potentials. The first question is the universal inclusion \[ \mathcal S_{\mathrm{SW}}\subseteq\mathcal R. \] Here reflectionlessness is a spectral/scattering property of the associated Sturm--Liouville operator, rather than a regularity condition on the potential. In a setting with scattering reflection coefficients it means their vanishing on the relevant continuous spectrum; an equivalent Weyl-function formulation requires the operator, spectral set and sign conventions to be fixed. The record supplies neither a definition of its Segal--Wilson class nor those spectral conventions, so the two classes in this inclusion retain their report-dependent meanings.

The second question concerns the Camassa--Holm initial-value problem: for each admissible reflectionless potential of the special form \[ a=(1,1,y)\in\mathcal R, \] when the initial datum $u_0$ is derived from $a$ by the construction in the report, does there exist a global solution of the Camassa--Holm equation with initial datum $u_0$? The requested assertion has the quantifier structure \[ \forall a=(1,1,y)\in\mathcal R\text{ admissible for that construction},\quad \exists u\text{ global},\qquad u|_{t=0}=u_0(a). \] The supplied text does not give the construction $a\mapsto u_0(a)$, assumptions on $y$, equation normalization or spatial domain, boundary conditions, solution regularity, or whether global time means $t\geq0$ or $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$. No such choices, and no uniqueness assertion, are added here. In particular, the second question must not be replaced by global existence for arbitrary Camassa--Holm initial data.

Short English statement

Are all the report's Segal--Wilson Sturm--Liouville potentials reflectionless? Separately, does every eligible reflectionless triple $(1,1,y)$ yield global Camassa--Holm evolution under the report's initial-data construction? Both questions retain the report-dependent analytic conventions that the extracted record omits.
