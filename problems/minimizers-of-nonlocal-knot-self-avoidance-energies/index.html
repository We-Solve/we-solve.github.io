Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: "For nonlocal self-avoidance energies on embeddings, do minimizers or stationary points exist within prescribed isotopy classes?" The record does not specify an energy functional, the source and target of the embeddings, their regularity, the meaning of isotopy, or the admissible variations. Consequently, it determines an existence problem schema rather than a fully quantified conjecture.

To express this schema, let $M$ and $N$ denote the intended source and ambient spaces, and let $\mathcal A$ denote the intended admissible space of embeddings $f\colon M\hookrightarrow N$, with its regularity and topology still to be specified. An embedding is a map that is a homeomorphism onto its image, with any additional differential regularity required by the chosen setting. Once the intended notion of isotopy is fixed, write $[f]_{\mathrm{iso}}$ for the isotopy class of $f$. For a prescribed class $\kappa$, put \[ \mathcal A_\kappa=\{f\in\mathcal A:[f]_{\mathrm{iso}}=\kappa\}. \] Neither ambient isotopy nor isotopy through embeddings is selected here, because the record does not distinguish them. Let $E$ denote the intended nonlocal self-avoidance energy on $\mathcal A$; its formula and hypotheses remain unspecified.

The minimizer question asks whether there exists $f_*\in\mathcal A_\kappa$ such that \[ E(f_*)\leq E(f)\qquad\text{for every }f\in\mathcal A_\kappa. \] If the intended formulation requires finite energy, it must additionally require $E(f_*)\in{\mathbb{R}}$. The source does not explicitly state this requirement or specify the range of $E$.

For a differentiable variational formulation, the stationary-point question asks whether there exists $f_*\in\mathcal A_\kappa$ such that \[ \left.\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t}E(f_t)\right|_{t=0}=0 \] for every admissible differentiable variation $(f_t)_{|t|<\delta}$ through $f_0=f_*$, with $\delta>0$, preserving the intended constraints and isotopy class. This formula applies only after the variation class and the existence of the indicated first derivatives have been specified.

The unresolved target is existence of minimizers, stationary points, or both, for the intended energies and prescribed isotopy classes. The record does not determine whether this is asserted for every such energy and class, only for a particular family, or as a request to identify sufficient hypotheses. No universal existence assertion is made here. The title refers to knots, while the supplied statement concerns embeddings without specifying their dimensions; the knot setting is therefore not imposed.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether a nonlocal self-avoidance energy attains its minimum, or has a stationary embedding, within a prescribed isotopy class. A precise conjecture requires the energy, embedding spaces, constraints, and meaning of stationarity to be specified. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation requiring independent review.
