Kourovka Notebook Problem 14.89

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ range over all primes, and let $P$ be a finite group of order $p^m$, where $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$. Write uniquely \[ m=2n+e,\qquad n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\quad e\in\{0,1\}. \] Let $k(P)$ denote the number of conjugacy classes of $P$. The supplied source attributes to P. Hall the assertion that there is an integer $a(P)\geq 0$ such that \[ k(P)=n(p^2-1)+p^e+a(P)(p^2-1)(p-1). \] This integer $a(P)$ is called the abundance of $P$.

Define the lower central series by $\gamma_1(P)=P$ and $\gamma_{i+1}(P)=[\gamma_i(P),P]$. Let $c(P)$ be the least nonnegative integer $c$ such that $\gamma_{c+1}(P)=1$, and define the coclass by \[ r(P)=m-c(P),\qquad |P|=p^{c(P)+r(P)}. \] This convention assigns class $0$ to the trivial group; the source does not specify its convention for that boundary case. For $s\in P$, define \[ C_P(s)=\{x\in P:xs=sx\}. \]

  1. (a) Does there exist a function $g:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ such that, for every prime $p$ and every finite $p$-group $P$, \[ r(P)\leq g(a(P))? \] Thus the bound must be independent of both $p$ and $|P|$.
  2. (b) Does there exist a function $f:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ such that, for every prime $p$ and every finite $p$-group $P$, there exists $s\in P$ satisfying \[ |C_P(s)|\leq p^{f(a(P))}? \] Here $f$ must be independent of $p$ and $|P|$, although $s$ may depend on $P$.

The source states that abundance $0$ implies coclass $1$, and reports that all examples known to its authors with abundance $1$ have coclass at most $3$. The first assertion requires a boundary qualification: a group of order $p$ has abundance $0$ and coclass $0$. No restriction excluding this case is supplied. The statement about known abundance-$1$ examples is a source-specific observation, not a universal assertion.

For part (b), the source states that $f(0)=2$ is available and tentatively suggests $f(1)=3$. The latter value is not presented as established.

The source also attributes to A. Jaikin-Zapirain a result asserting the existence of a function $h$, depending only on the prime $p$ and the abundance $a$, such that \[ |P|\leq p^{h(p,a(P))} \] for every finite $p$-group $P$. The notation $h$ distinguishes this supplied background bound from the function sought in part (b).

Short English statement

For a finite $p$-group, define its abundance by the supplied formula for its number of conjugacy classes. Ask whether abundance alone bounds its coclass and whether some element has centralizer of order at most $p$ raised to a power depending only on abundance. Both bounds must be uniform over all primes. The source's small-abundance remarks retain their stated qualifications, including a missing boundary exception in its coclass claim.
