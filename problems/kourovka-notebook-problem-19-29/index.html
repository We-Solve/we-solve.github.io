Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.29

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a group. Write $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ for its automorphism group and $\operatorname{Inn}(G)=\{\widehat g:g\in G\}$, where $\widehat g(x)=gxg^{-1}$. Set \[ \operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G). \] Regard an element $\Phi\in\operatorname{Out}(G)$ as its coset of representatives in $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$. Two representatives $\varphi,\psi\in\Phi$ are called isogradient if \[ \varphi=\widehat g^{-1}\circ\psi\circ\widehat g \qquad\text{for some }g\in G. \] Let $S(\Phi)$ be the cardinality of the set of equivalence classes under this relation.

A group is residually finite if, for each $x\in G\setminus\{1\}$, there exist a finite group $F$ and a homomorphism $q:G\to F$ such that $q(x)\ne1$. A group $H$ is soluble if its derived series, defined by $H^{(0)}=H$ and $H^{(n+1)}=[H^{(n)},H^{(n)}]$, satisfies $H^{(d)}=\{1\}$ for some integer $d\ge0$.

Conjecture (A. L. Fel'shtyn and E. V. Troitsky; Kourovka Notebook, Problem 19.29). For every finitely generated residually finite group $G$, \[ \left(\exists\,\Phi\in\operatorname{Out}(G)\text{ with }S(\Phi)<\infty\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ \left(\exists\,H\le G\text{ with }[G:H]<\infty \text{ and }H\text{ soluble}\right). \] The supplied registry labels this conjecture open; that label is supplied source information and is not an independently verified assessment.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether a finitely generated residually finite group must contain a soluble subgroup of finite index whenever some outer automorphism class contains only finitely many classes of representatives under conjugation by inner automorphisms.
