Math-7077

Totik–Widom Bounds Beyond Parreau–Widom Sets

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E\subset{\mathbb{R}}$ be compact. For a Borel probability measure $\mu$ supported on $E$, define its logarithmic energy by \[ I(\mu)=\iint_{E\times E}\log\frac{1}{|x-y|}\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(y), \qquad \operatorname{cap}(E)=\exp\!\left(-\inf_{\mu}I(\mu)\right). \] The usual Green-function formulation below requires $\operatorname{cap}(E)>0$. For each integer $n\geq1$, define the monic Chebyshev norm and its capacity-normalized factor by \[ t_n(E)=\inf\left\{\max_{x\in E}|P(x)|:P\in{\mathbb{C}}[x],\ P\text{ monic},\ \deg P=n\right\}, \qquad W_n(E)=\frac{t_n(E)}{\operatorname{cap}(E)^n}. \] A Totik--Widom bound means that there exists a finite constant $C_E>0$, independent of $n$, such that \[ t_n(E)\leq C_E\operatorname{cap}(E)^n\quad\text{for every integer }n\geq1, \] or equivalently $\sup_{n\geq1}W_n(E)<\infty$.

Let $g_E$ be the Green function of $\widehat{{\mathbb{C}}}\setminus E$ with pole at infinity, normalized by \[ g_E(z)=\log|z|-\log\operatorname{cap}(E)+o(1)\qquad (z\to\infty). \] Call $E$ regular if $g_E$ extends continuously to zero at every point of $E$. For regular $E$, write the bounded components of $[\min E,\max E]\setminus E$ as $(a_j,b_j)$, with index set $J$, and let $c_j\in(a_j,b_j)$ be the unique point at which $g_E$ attains its maximum on that gap. The Parreau--Widom critical-value condition is \[ \operatorname{PW}(E):=\sum_{j\in J}g_E(c_j)<\infty, \] where an empty sum is zero and the sum is allowed to diverge to $+\infty$.

The supplied question asks whether there exists a compact real set that is not Parreau--Widom and nevertheless satisfies a Totik--Widom bound. Under the interpretation in which admissible sets are regular and have positive logarithmic capacity, its precise formulation is: \[ \begin{gathered} \text{Does there exist a regular compact }E\subset{\mathbb{R}}\text{ with }\operatorname{cap}(E)>0\text{ such that}\\ \sum_{j\in J}g_E(c_j)=+\infty \quad\text{and}\quad \exists C_E\in(0,\infty)\ \forall n\geq1:\ t_n(E)\leq C_E\operatorname{cap}(E)^n\ ? \end{gathered} \] Unresolved qualification. The supplied statement does not explicitly impose positive capacity or regularity, and it does not define its convention for Parreau--Widom sets. Positive capacity is needed for the displayed normalization and Green function. Regularity is an additional substantive qualification: if regularity is included in the definition of a Parreau--Widom set but is not imposed on admissible $E$, failure to be Parreau--Widom need not mean divergence of the critical-value sum. Thus the regular-set formulation above is conditional on the intended convention and must not be treated as an unqualified replacement of the supplied question.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a compact subset of the real line can fail the Parreau--Widom condition while its monic Chebyshev norms remain bounded by a fixed multiple of the corresponding powers of logarithmic capacity. The source leaves the capacity and regularity hypotheses implicit; the displayed formulation makes the usual regular, positive-capacity interpretation explicit, subject to review.
