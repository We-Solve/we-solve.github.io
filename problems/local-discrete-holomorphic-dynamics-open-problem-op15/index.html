Local Discrete Holomorphic Dynamics — Open Problem OP15

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $f\colon({\mathbb{C}}^n,0)\to({\mathbb{C}}^n,0)$ be a holomorphic germ tangent to the identity, meaning $f(0)=0$ and $Df(0)=I_n$. For a nonidentity such germ, write \[ f(z)=z+P_{\nu}(z)+\sum_{j>\nu}P_j(z),\qquad \nu\geq2, \] where each $P_j\colon{\mathbb{C}}^n\to{\mathbb{C}}^n$ is homogeneous of degree $j$ and $P_{\nu}\not\equiv0$. A characteristic direction is a projective class $[v]\in{\mathbb{P}}^{n-1}({\mathbb{C}})$, with $v\neq0$, for which \[ P_{\nu}(v)=\lambda v \] for some $\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}$. Such a direction is called degenerate if $P_{\nu}(v)=0$. Thus the condition that all characteristic directions are degenerate is \[ \forall v\in{\mathbb{C}}^n\setminus\{0\},\qquad P_{\nu}(v)\in{\mathbb{C}} v\ \Longrightarrow\ P_{\nu}(v)=0. \] A formal change of coordinates is a formal power series map $H\in{\mathbb{C}}[[z_1,\ldots,z_n]]^n$ with $H(0)=0$ and invertible linear part. Two germs are formally conjugate under such a change when \[ H\circ f=g\circ H. \] A formal invariant is a quantity constant under the chosen formal conjugacy relation; a formal normal form is a specified representative of a formal conjugacy class.

Problem (Abate, OP15, 2011). Find formal normal forms and formal invariants for holomorphic germs tangent to the identity having only degenerate characteristic directions.

The supplied formulation does not specify the allowed dimensions, whether the identity germ is included, or whether coordinate changes may have arbitrary invertible linear part or must themselves be tangent to the identity. The definitions above explain the nonidentity case in finite complex dimension and the usual unrestricted formal conjugacy relation; these unresolved scope and equivalence conventions require confirmation before a definitive classification target can be fixed. The source reports partial results under the reference labels [BM], [AT2], and [AR], without supplying their bibliographic entries.

Short English statement

Abate's OP15 asks for formal normal forms and invariants of holomorphic germs tangent to the identity whose characteristic directions are all degenerate. The dimension, treatment of the identity germ, and permitted formal coordinate changes need clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open and reports partial results in [BM], [AT2], and [AR]; these assessments have not been independently verified.
