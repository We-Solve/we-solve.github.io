Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Conjecture 41

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be an arbitrary field. Its Brauer group $\operatorname{Br}(F)$ is the abelian group of Brauer equivalence classes of finite-dimensional central simple $F$-algebras, with addition induced by tensor product over $F$. Here two such algebras $A$ and $B$ are Brauer equivalent if $M_r(A)\cong M_s(B)$ as $F$-algebras for some positive integers $r,s$. The identity is the class of $F$.

For a prime $p$, define the $p$-primary component by \[ (\operatorname{Br}(F))_p =\{\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(F):p^n\alpha=0\text{ for some integer }n\geq 1\}. \] A subgroup $D$ of an abelian group is divisible if, for every positive integer $m$ and every $d\in D$, there exists $e\in D$ with $me=d$. An elementary abelian $2$-group is an abelian group annihilated by $2$, equivalently a vector space over $\mathbb{F}_2$; no finiteness assumption is imposed.

Conjecture 41 (attributed in the supplied source to Brumer--Rosen). For every field $F$ and every prime $p$, at least one of the following holds:

  1. $(\operatorname{Br}(F))_p=0$.
  2. There exists a subgroup $D\leq(\operatorname{Br}(F))_p$ such that $D\neq 0$ and $D$ is divisible.
  3. $p=2$ and $(\operatorname{Br}(F))_2$ is an elementary abelian $2$-group.
The supplied statement imposes no restriction on the characteristic of $F$. The alternatives are stated inclusively: the zero group also satisfies the definition of an elementary abelian $2$-group. The source's reference label BrumerRosen is supplied without its bibliographic entry, so the original Brumer--Rosen reference remains unresolved.

Short English statement

The conjecture asserts that, for any field and prime $p$, the $p$-primary part of the Brauer group is zero or contains a nonzero divisible subgroup, with the additional possibility of an elementary abelian group when $p=2$. The registry labels this conjecture open; that label is supplied source data and has not been independently verified.
