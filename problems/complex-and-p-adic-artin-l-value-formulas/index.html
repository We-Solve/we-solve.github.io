Complex and $p$-Adic Artin $L$-Value Formulas

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq2$ and let $p$ be a prime. In the source's Artin-motivic setting, $G$ is the relevant finite Galois quotient, $E$ the coefficient field, $\pi$ the specified idempotent, $\rho$ the representation on $E[G]\pi$, and $M=M_\pi^E$ the associated motive. These data and their admissibility hypotheses are not reproduced. Work only in the source's totally-real/odd and CM/even parity cases. This wording appears to refer to the underlying field and parity of $n$, with the appropriate idempotent or complex-conjugation conditions, but those exact conditions must be recovered; arbitrary $\pi$ in either field case is not silently allowed.

Conjecture 3, as identified by the supplied verification, has all of the following clauses: \[ L(n,\rho\otimes\operatorname{id},{\mathbb{Q}})\,D(M)^{1/2,\infty} =e(n,M)R_{n,\infty}(M), \] \[ L_p(n,\rho\otimes\omega_p^{1-n},{\mathbb{Q}})\,D(M)^{1/2,p} =e_p(n,M)\operatorname{Eul}_p(n,\rho\otimes\operatorname{id},{\mathbb{Q}})R_{n,p}(M), \] \[ e_p(n,M)=e(n,M), \] and \[ L_p(n,\rho\otimes\omega_p^{1-n},{\mathbb{Q}})\ne0, \qquad R_{n,p}(M)\ne0. \] Here the symbols denote the source's complex and $p$-adic Artin $L$-values, discriminant or comparison factors, archimedean and $p$-adic regulators, Euler factor, and correction factors. The twist $\omega_p^{1-n}$ occurs in the $p$-adic $L$-value, while the displayed Euler factor uses $\rho\otimes\operatorname{id}$; those distinct arguments are preserved. The usual $\omega_p$ is a Teichmüller-type cyclotomic character, but its exact convention, including $p=2$, is not supplied.

The formulas require common coefficient embeddings, rational structures, regulator bases, and coherent choices for $D(M)^{1/2,\infty}$ and $D(M)^{1/2,p}$. Equality of $e_p$ and $e$ must be interpreted through those source identifications, not by equating arbitrary complex and $p$-adic numbers. Likewise, the allowed correction-factor field and their independence or normalization must be fixed; choosing unrelated factors as ratios would not establish the intended comparison. No normalization or extra power of $\pi$, period, or Euler factor is inserted.

The supplied assessment checked 2026-08-21 reports proofs in cyclotomic-idempotent cases and numerical checks, while saying the general nonvanishing assertion remains unresolved. The exact proven cases and their hypotheses are not reproduced; numerical checks do not supply a proof of any unrestricted clause.

Short English statement

In the specified parity cases, compare complex and $p$-adic Artin special values with their regulator formulas, require the same correction factor in both, and require both the $p$-adic value and regulator to be nonzero. The motive and comparison normalizations are missing.
