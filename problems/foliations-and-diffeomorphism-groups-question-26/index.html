Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 26

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $S^3=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^4:\|x\|_2=1\}$. The supplied question concerns an $S^3$-bundle $p:E\to M$ over a $4$-manifold, with nonzero Euler class and nonzero first Pontryagin class, denoted provisionally by $e(p)$ and $p_1(p)$. The bundle category and the construction of these characteristic classes are not specified. For example, if $p$ is the unit sphere bundle of an oriented real rank-four vector bundle $V\to M$, these conditions mean \[ e(V)\ne0,\qquad p_1(V)\ne0\quad\text{in }H^4(M;{\mathbb{Z}}). \] This example is a convention requiring confirmation, not an additional hypothesis supplied by the source.

A $C^0$-flat $S^3$-bundle is understood here to have locally constant transition functions valued in $\operatorname{Homeo}(S^3)$, or in its orientation-preserving subgroup if fiber orientations are required. In the closed oriented setting, a bordism of bundles from $p:E\to M$ to $p':E'\to M'$ consists of a compact oriented $5$-manifold $W$, an identification $\partial W\cong M\sqcup(-M')$, and an $S^3$-bundle over $W$ restricting to the two given bundles. The common bundle category remains to be specified.

For a closed oriented $4$-manifold $N$, its simplicial volume is \[ \|N\|=\inf\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{r}|a_j|:\ c=\sum_{j=1}^{r}a_j\sigma_j\text{ is a real singular cycle representing }[N]_{{\mathbb{R}}}\right\}. \] Under these closed oriented conventions, a fixed-base interpretation of the question is: for every such $M$, does there exist $C_M\in[0,\infty)$ such that every admissible bundle $p:E\to M$ satisfying $e(p)\ne0$ and $p_1(p)\ne0$ admits a bordism to a $C^0$-flat bundle $p':E'\to M'$ with \[ \|M'\|\le C_M? \] Here $M'$ and the bordism may depend on $p$, whereas $C_M$ may not. The supplied source attributes the existence of some flat bordant representative to Mather--Thurston theory; this attribution is retained without independently asserting its hypotheses or validity. The source does not specify whether the desired bound may depend on $M$, or instead must be a single constant for all bases. It also does not specify closedness, orientations, boundary conditions, or a noncompact version of simplicial volume. Consequently, the displayed formulation is conditional and does not resolve these omissions.

Short English statement

Question 26 asks whether an $S^3$-bundle over a $4$-manifold, with nonzero Euler and first Pontryagin classes, can be replaced within its bundle bordism class by a $C^0$-flat bundle whose base has simplicial volume bounded independently of the original bundle map. The allowed dependence on the base and several manifold and bundle conventions require clarification.
