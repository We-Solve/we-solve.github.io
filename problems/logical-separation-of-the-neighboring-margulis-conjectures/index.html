Math-5360

Logical separation of the neighboring Margulis conjectures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The assigned extract is transitional prose: it says that Conjecture 29 is a special case of a general Cartan-invariant closed-set conjecture [130], then introduces another conjecture of G. Margulis without stating it. Companion records identify the two different assertions. For \[ X_3=\mathrm{SL}_3({\mathbb{R}})/\mathrm{SL}_3({\mathbb{Z}}),\qquad A=\{\operatorname{diag}(a_1,a_2,a_3):a_i\ne0,\ a_1a_2a_3=1\}, \] Conjecture 29 says that every relatively compact $A$-orbit is closed. Conjecture 30 says that for every compact $K\subset X_3$, only finitely many closed $A$-orbits are contained in $K$. The latter concerns containment of whole orbits, not merely their meeting $K$. This context record does not prove either assertion or an implication between them.

The attached aid reports logical independence in abstract dynamics. For a continuous action of ${\mathbb{R}}^2$ on a metric space, consider properties \[ \text{every relatively compact orbit is closed},\qquad \text{each compact set contains only finitely many closed orbits}. \] Two faithful actions on compact metric spaces separate them. First, on $[0,1]^2$, let \[ \psi_s(x)=\frac{e^s x}{1-x+e^s x},\qquad (s,t)\cdot(x,y)=(\psi_s(x),\psi_t(y)). \] Exactly the four corner orbits are closed, so compact-set finiteness holds, while the interior orbit is relatively compact and not closed. Second, on $\mathbb T^2\times[1,2]$, let \[ (s,t)\cdot(x,y,r)=(x+rs,y+rt,r), \qquad(x,y)\in\mathbb T^2. \] Every orbit is a full closed torus fiber, but the whole compact space contains infinitely many such orbits. The varying scale $r$ makes this ${\mathbb{R}}^2$-action faithful. These examples show that neither abstract property alone implies the other; they are not counterexamples in $X_3$.

The aid additionally reports a root-unipotent escape lemma and a conditional orbit-closure amplification proposition isolating the extra homogeneous input behind an An--Weiss compact-minimal-set reduction. Its logical structure requires an amplification hypothesis forcing a suitable nonclosed compact invariant configuration to contain a complete root-unipotent orbit, together with an escape statement excluding that containment. No unconditional amplification or implication between the arithmetic conjectures is supplied, and full hypotheses and references for this auxiliary reduction are absent from the summary.

Short English statement

This transition links two distinct Margulis conjectures: closing bounded diagonal orbits and finiteness of closed orbits in compact sets. The supplied compact-space examples show those properties are independent in abstract dynamics; extra homogeneous hypotheses are needed for any arithmetic implication.
