Motivic Origins of Étale Height Pairings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be the function field in the supplied problem, let $B$ denote its base, and let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over $K$. The supplied record does not specify the hypotheses on $B$, the field of constants, or the dimension of $X$. Define the rational Picard group by \[ \operatorname{Pic}(B)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}:=\operatorname{Pic}(B)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}, \] where $\operatorname{Pic}(B)$ is the group of isomorphism classes of line bundles on $B$, with tensor product as its operation. A cycle is homologically trivial relative to a chosen cycle class map if its image under that map is zero; the relevant cycle class map and cycle groups are not specified in the supplied record.

Question. For homologically trivial cycles on $X$, does the constructed generalized Beilinson $\acute{\mathrm{e}}$tale height pairing arise from a motivic pairing with values in $\operatorname{Pic}(B)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, with compatibility under $\acute{\mathrm{e}}$tale realization recovering the given height pairing?

Here "arise from a motivic pairing" retains the source's intended motivic requirement. The record does not specify the motivic category, the two inputs of the pairing, their cycle codimensions or equivalence relations, the coefficient and realization conventions, the target of the constructed $\acute{\mathrm{e}}$tale pairing, or the comparison maps. Thus this is a statement of the supplied existence question; a fully specified quantified formulation requires those missing construction data. No particular choices of these data, or additional hypotheses on $B$, are asserted.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the generalized Beilinson $\acute{\mathrm{e}}$tale height pairing for homologically trivial cycles on a smooth projective variety over a function field comes from a motivic pairing valued in the rational Picard group of the base. The supplied record leaves the precise cycle groups, base hypotheses, and realization compatibility unspecified.
