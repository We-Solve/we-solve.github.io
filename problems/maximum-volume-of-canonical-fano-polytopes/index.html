Math-4300

Maximum Volume of Canonical Fano Polytopes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $d\geq4$, let $N$ be a lattice of rank $d$, and put $N_{{\mathbb{R}}}=N\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}$. A $d$-dimensional lattice polytope is the convex hull of finitely many points of $N$, with affine span $N_{{\mathbb{R}}}$. Such a polytope $P$ is called canonical Fano if \[ \operatorname{int}(P)\cap N=\{0\}. \] Use lattice-relative ordinary volume $\operatorname{vol}_N$, for which a fundamental parallelepiped of $N$ has volume $1$. The supplied record does not explicitly specify its volume normalization; this convention, consistent with the displayed denominator $d!$, requires confirmation against the source.

Define the Sylvester sequence by \[ s_1=2,\qquad s_{i+1}=\prod_{j=1}^{i}s_j+1\quad(i\geq1). \] The proposed assertion is that every such $P$ satisfies \[ \operatorname{vol}_N(P)\leq\frac{2(s_d-1)^2}{d!}, \] with equality only for the canonical Fano simplex denoted $R_{(d)}$ in the supplied statement (written $R(d)$ in its original-statement field).

The equality clause remains incompletely specified: the supplied record gives no definition or vertices of $R_{(d)}$ and does not specify the equivalence relation intended by "only for." Thus the inequality is formulated under the stated volume convention, while a precise identification of its equality cases requires additional source information. The source asks for a sharp bound and equality characterization; these are proposed assertions, not conclusions established here.

Short English statement

For every integer $d\geq4$, the problem asks whether a $d$-dimensional lattice polytope whose unique interior lattice point is the origin has volume at most $2(s_d-1)^2/d!$, with equality only for a designated simplex $R_{(d)}$. The record does not define that simplex or the intended equivalence of equality cases, and its volume convention needs confirmation.
