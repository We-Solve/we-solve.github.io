Math-4521

Reduced $C^*$-Algebras of Free Orthogonal Quantum Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record uses $\mathrm{FO}_n$ in its cleaned statement and $F O_n^+$ in its original statement, without defining either notation. The following formulation adopts the conventional free orthogonal quantum group interpretation suggested by the title; this interpretation requires human verification against the cited report.

For each positive integer $n$, let $A_n=C^u(O_n^+)$ be the universal unital complex $C^*$-algebra generated by elements $u_{ij}=u_{ij}^*$, $1\leq i,j\leq n$, satisfying \[ \sum_{k=1}^n u_{ik}u_{jk}=\delta_{ij}1, \qquad \sum_{k=1}^n u_{ki}u_{kj}=\delta_{ij}1 \quad (1\leq i,j\leq n). \] Equip this algebra with the coproduct \[ \Delta_n(u_{ij})=\sum_{k=1}^n u_{ik}\otimes u_{kj}. \] Let $h_n$ be its Haar state, characterized by \[ (h_n\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta_n(a) =(\mathrm{id}\otimes h_n)\Delta_n(a) =h_n(a)1 \quad(a\in A_n), \] and let $\pi_{h_n}$ be its GNS representation. Define the reduced algebra \[ B_n=\overline{\pi_{h_n}(A_n)}^{\lVert\cdot\rVert}. \] Under the stated interpretation, $\mathrm{FO}_n$ denotes the discrete quantum group dual to $O_n^+$, and the source expression $C_r^*(\mathrm{FO}_n)$ denotes $B_n$.

Question. Given $M,N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}$ with $M\neq N$, determine whether there exists a complex $C^*$-algebra isomorphism \[ \Phi:B_M\longrightarrow B_N. \] Here an isomorphism is a bijective complex-linear map preserving multiplication and the involution; no compatibility with the coproduct is imposed. The question concerns the underlying reduced $C^*$-algebras. All positive ranks, including $1$ and $2$, are retained as supplied. The record does not clarify whether an additional rank restriction was intended or reconcile its two quantum group notations.

Short English statement

For distinct positive integers $M$ and $N$, determine whether the reduced $C^*$-algebras associated with the free orthogonal quantum groups of those ranks are isomorphic. This formulation uses an explicitly stated interpretation of the source notation. The supplied open label is dated curation, not an independently verified assertion that every such pair remains unresolved.
