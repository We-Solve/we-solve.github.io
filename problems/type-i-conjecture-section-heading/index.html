Definitions and mathematical statement

The only recoverable mathematical setup is: \[ G\text{ is a second countable locally compact group.} \] Second countability means that the topology of $G$ has a countable base. Local compactness means that the identity has a compact neighbourhood, and hence every point does by translation.
