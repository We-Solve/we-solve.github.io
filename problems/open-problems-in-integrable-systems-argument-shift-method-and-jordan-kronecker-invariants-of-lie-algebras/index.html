Open Problems in Integrable Systems — Argument shift method and Jordan-Kronecker invariants of Lie algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite-dimensional Lie algebra $\mathfrak l$ over $\mathbb C$ and $x\in\mathfrak l^*$, define the skew-symmetric form \[ A_x(u,v)=\langle x,[u,v]\rangle\qquad(u,v\in\mathfrak l). \] The Jordan--Kronecker data of the pencil $A_x+\lambda A_y$, for a generic pair $(x,y)\in\mathfrak l^*\times\mathfrak l^*$, come from its simultaneous canonical decomposition under changes of basis: Jordan-block sizes at characteristic values (including a possible value at infinity) and Kronecker minimal indices, equivalently the odd-dimensional Kronecker block sizes. Generic means on a nonempty Zariski-open subset where the relevant block type is constant. Actual moving characteristic values are distinguished from the generic block-type data. The complex finite-dimensional setting is conventional here; the source does not explicitly specify its field.

Problem. Compute these invariants for interesting classes of Lie algebras, in particular all the following classes:

  • (a) Semidirect sums $\mathfrak g\ltimes_{\rho}V$, where $\mathfrak g$ is semisimple, $\rho:\mathfrak g\to\operatorname{End}(V)$ is a representation, and $V$ is abelian as a Lie algebra. The bracket is \[ [(u,v),(u',v')]=([u,u'],\rho(u)v'-\rho(u')v). \]
  • (b) Borel subalgebras of simple Lie algebras; over $\mathbb C$, a Borel subalgebra is a maximal solvable subalgebra.
  • (c) Parabolic subalgebras of simple Lie algebras; over $\mathbb C$, a parabolic subalgebra contains a Borel subalgebra.
  • (d) Centralizers $\mathfrak g_a=\{u\in\mathfrak g:[u,a]=0\}$ of singular elements $a\in\mathfrak g$, for $\mathfrak g$ simple. In characteristic zero, singular means $\dim\mathfrak g_a>\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak g$, rather than minimal centralizer dimension.
  • (e) Lie algebras of small dimensions; no dimension cutoff is given.
The computation concerns the generic pencils of each algebra in these classes, not just a chosen exceptional pair of covectors. The supplied summary reports substantial progress, including semisimple cases and the argument-shift method, but does not verify completion of all five classes.

Short English statement

Determine the generic Jordan--Kronecker pencil data for the listed semidirect products, Borel and parabolic subalgebras, singular-element centralizers, and small-dimensional Lie algebras. The field and the meaning of small dimension need specification.
