Intrinsic Gerstenhaber Brackets from Projective Resolutions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source leaves the ground ring and algebra hypotheses unspecified. For a conditional conventional formulation, let $k$ be a commutative ring and $A$ an associative unital $k$-algebra, and put $A^e=A\otimes_k A^{\mathrm{op}}$. These conventions require confirmation from the source; in particular, no field, flatness, or projectivity hypothesis over $k$ is supplied. Work in a setting in which the intended Hochschild cohomology admits the stated identification with derived morphisms.

Let \[ \cdots\longrightarrow P_2\longrightarrow P_1\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow0 \] be an arbitrary projective resolution of the left $A^e$-module $A$. Write $C_P^n=\operatorname{Hom}_{A^e}(P_n,A)$, with differential induced by the differential of $P$. Let $D(A^e)$ denote the derived category of $A^e$-modules, let $\Sigma$ denote cohomological suspension, and set \[ E^n=\operatorname{Hom}_{D(A^e)}(A,\Sigma^n A). \] The intended identifications are \[ H^n(C_P^\bullet)\cong\operatorname{Ext}_{A^e}^n(A,A) \cong E^n, \qquad \phi_n:\operatorname{HH}^n(A)\xrightarrow{\sim}E^n. \] The comparison with the intended definition of $\operatorname{HH}^n(A)$, including its necessary base hypotheses, remains a source qualification.

The Gerstenhaber bracket is the canonical degree $-1$ graded Lie bracket on Hochschild cohomology: \[ [-,-]_G:\operatorname{HH}^p(A)\times\operatorname{HH}^q(A) \longrightarrow\operatorname{HH}^{p+q-1}(A). \] Here the graded Lie convention uses shifted degrees $p-1$ and $q-1$. In the ordinary module setting, negative-degree groups are zero, so the bracket of two degree-zero classes is zero.

Requested construction. For every such $A$ and every such projective resolution $P$, describe how to compute $[x,y]_G$ using $P$ and representatives in $C_P^\bullet$. Also construct intrinsically a family of $k$-bilinear maps \[ \beta_{p,q}:E^p\times E^q\longrightarrow E^{p+q-1} \qquad(p,q\geq0) \] such that, for all $x\in\operatorname{HH}^p(A)$ and $y\in\operatorname{HH}^q(A)$, \[ \beta_{p,q}\left(\phi_p(x),\phi_q(y)\right) =\phi_{p+q-1}\left([x,y]_G\right). \] For the zero target in degree $-1$, this identity means the zero map. The requested intrinsic construction must describe the operation on derived morphisms; merely transporting the bracket through $\phi$ states the compatibility condition. The source does not specify what categorical structure or auxiliary choices an intrinsic construction may use, nor whether the resolution description must provide an operation on cochains themselves.

Short English statement

Describe the Gerstenhaber bracket using any projective bimodule resolution, and construct a corresponding degree $-1$ bilinear operation intrinsically on derived morphisms from $A$ to its shifts. The ground-ring hypotheses and the meaning of "intrinsically" require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
