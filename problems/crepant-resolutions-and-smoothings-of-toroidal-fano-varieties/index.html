Crepant Resolutions and Smoothings of Toroidal Fano Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A log scheme is a scheme $Y$ equipped with a sheaf of commutative monoids $\mathcal M_Y$ and a homomorphism $\alpha:\mathcal M_Y\to\mathcal O_Y$, where $\mathcal O_Y$ is viewed multiplicatively, such that $\alpha^{-1}(\mathcal O_Y^\times)\to\mathcal O_Y^\times$ is an isomorphism.

Let $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}$ denote the class of log schemes obtained by the construction in part (b) of the supplied proposition, within that proposition's intended setup. Its inputs include a polytope $P$, its dual $P^\vee$, and a compatible collection $(f_\Delta)_{\Delta}$ of zero-mutable Laurent polynomials, one for each facet $\Delta$ of $P^\vee$. The supplied proposition asserts that such a collection gives a global generically log smooth structure $\mathcal M_Y$ on $Y$. Part (a) asserts that an individual choice on $\Delta$ gives a singular generically log smooth structure at the one-dimensional stratum of $Y$ corresponding to $\Delta$.

Conjecture (attributed in the source to Corti-R.). For every $(Y,\mathcal M_Y)\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}$, there exists a log-crepant log resolution of $(Y,\mathcal M_Y)$, and there also exists a smoothing of $(Y,\mathcal M_Y)$, in the senses intended by the source. These are two existence assertions; the supplied wording does not impose a compatibility condition between the resolution and the smoothing.

Unresolved scope and terminology. The supplied extract does not specify the full construction of $Y$, the definition of zero-mutability, the compatibility condition on the facet polynomials, or the precise meanings of log-crepant log resolution and smoothing in this setting. It also omits the ground field, log base, ambient dimension for the conjecture, and hypotheses on the log structures. Thus $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{src}}$ is a source-dependent class, and the conjecture above is not yet an intrinsic, fully specified formulation. In particular, no smoothing base, condition on its fibers, or formula for log crepancy is imposed here without source evidence.

Supplied contextual theorem. The extract separately states that every one of the 105 smooth Fano threefolds can be obtained by smoothing a toroidal crossing Fano variety $Y$ arising from a central subdivision of a reflexive polytope $P$, using for its log structure only products of the facet polynomials denoted $f_{\mathrm{Tom}}$, $f_{\mathrm{Jerry}}$, and $f_{A_n}$ on facets of $P^\vee$. These polynomial names are retained from the source; their definitions and the range of $n$ are not supplied. This contextual assertion does not supply the missing general hypotheses of the conjecture or assert its universal resolution conclusion.

Short English statement

The conjecture attributed to Corti-R. asks whether every log scheme produced by the source's compatible facetwise zero-mutable Laurent polynomial construction admits both a log-crepant log resolution and a smoothing. The construction and several essential terms are incompletely specified in the supplied extract, so the statement remains conditional on the original setup. The supplied partially solved label and the contextual assertion about 105 smooth Fano threefolds are source reports requiring independent mathematical review.
