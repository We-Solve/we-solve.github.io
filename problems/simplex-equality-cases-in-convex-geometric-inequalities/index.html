Math-7321

Simplex Equality Cases in Convex Geometric Inequalities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$ with $n>2$, and let $K\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be a convex body, meaning a compact convex set with nonempty interior. Write $|K|$ for its $n$-dimensional Lebesgue measure and $\operatorname{ext}(K)$ for its extreme points: $x\in K$ is extreme if $x=ty+(1-t)z$, with $y,z\in K$ and $0<t<1$, implies $y=z=x$. Define \[ g_K=\frac{1}{|K|}\int_K x\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x, \qquad C_2(K)=\frac{1}{|K|}\int_K\|x\|_2^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] Let $r$ be the radius parameter appearing in the supplied statement, and assume \[ \|v\|_2\ge r\qquad\text{for every }v\in\operatorname{ext}(K). \] Unresolved parameter qualification. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the admissible range of $r$, in particular whether $r>0$ is required or $r=0$ is permitted. The conjecture below retains this unresolved qualification; no additional hypothesis on $r$ is silently imposed.

The supplied record describes the following bound as sharp: \[ C_2(K)\ge\frac{r^2+(n+1)\|g_K\|_2^2}{n+2}. \] Conjectured equality characterization. Under these hypotheses, for an arbitrary convex body $K$, \[ C_2(K)=\frac{r^2+(n+1)\|g_K\|_2^2}{n+2} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \begin{gathered} K=\operatorname{conv}\{v_0,\ldots,v_n\},\\ v_0,\ldots,v_n\text{ are affinely independent},\\ \|v_i\|_2=r\quad(0\le i\le n). \end{gathered} \] Thus the sphere is centered at the origin. The original statement asks for this equivalence; the supplied expanded statement emphasizes the implication from equality to the simplex description. The original also refers to the hypotheses of an unreproduced Theorem 1, so completeness of the expanded hypotheses requires source review.

Short English statement

For $n>2$, consider a full-dimensional convex body whose extreme points have distance at least $r$ from the origin. The conjecture says that equality in the supplied second-moment bound holds exactly for simplices with all vertices on the sphere of radius $r$ centered at the origin. The admissible range of $r$ remains unspecified. The supplied dated assessment reports the equality characterization as established for polytopes and conjectural for general convex bodies.
