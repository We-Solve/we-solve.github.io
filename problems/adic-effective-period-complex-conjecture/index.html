Adic Effective-Period Complex Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime. Let $K$ be either ${\mathbb{Q}}$, equipped with the $p$-adic valuation, or $k(\pi)$, equipped with the $\pi$-adic valuation, where $k$ is a field of characteristic zero and $\pi$ is an indeterminate. Write $\widehat K$ for the completion of this valued field.

The supplied statement refers to an infinite-dimensional Tate ball $B_\infty$ over $\widehat K$ and a boundary-vanishing complex \[ \mathcal C_\bullet=\widetilde{\Omega}_{\infty-\bullet}^{K\text{-}\mathrm{alg}}(B_\infty). \] Its coefficients are described as power series algebraic over $K(z_1,z_2,\ldots)$. Here algebraic means satisfying a nonzero polynomial in one indeterminate with coefficients in that rational function field. The supplied record does not specify the power-series algebra, its convergence conditions, the construction of $B_\infty$, the graded spaces and differential of $\mathcal C_\bullet$, or the meaning of boundary vanishing. These remain source-dependent definitions, so the following is a conditional formulation using the intended complex.

Conjecture 5 (as identified in the supplied source). For every valued field $K$ of either form above, the intended complex satisfies \[ H_j(\mathcal C_\bullet)=0\qquad\text{for every nonzero degree }j, \] and its formal integration map \[ I:H_0(\mathcal C_\bullet)\longrightarrow\widehat K \] is injective. Equivalently, for every $\alpha\in H_0(\mathcal C_\bullet)$, one has $I(\alpha)=0\Rightarrow\alpha=0$.

The damaged original excerpt associates this map with integration of a representative $f\,\mathrm dz$ over $[0,1]^\infty$. This is formal integration notation; the supplied record does not define its infinite-dimensional meaning, convergence, or descent to homology. No analytic interpretation of that expression is imposed here. A fully self-contained formulation requires the missing definitions from the report.

Short English statement

For the stated $p$-adic and $\pi$-adic fields, the conjecture asks whether the intended complex of algebraic power-series forms vanishing on the boundary has zero homology outside degree zero, and whether formal integration detects every degree-zero homology class. The supplied record omits definitions needed to specify the complex and integration map completely.
