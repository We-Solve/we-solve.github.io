Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record uses a class or parameter region $\mathcal E_0$, a parameter $b$, mild dissipativity, and infinite renormalizability without defining them. Their intended meanings must be recovered from the cited source before a complete formulation is possible. In particular, no specific Hénon family, parameter domain, or definition of mild dissipativity is fixed by this record.

If $X$ denotes the intended ambient topological space containing $\mathcal E_0$, its boundary is \[ \partial_X\mathcal E_0 =\overline{\mathcal E_0}^{\,X}\cap\overline{X\setminus\mathcal E_0}^{\,X}. \] The choice of $X$ and its topology is unresolved. The record poses the following two questions.

  1. Describe $\partial_X\mathcal E_0$. In particular, is it a piecewise smooth arc? The meaning of arc, including its endpoint convention, and the smooth structure needed for piecewise smoothness are not specified.
  2. For every infinitely renormalizable mildly dissipative map $f\in\mathcal E_0$, does its sequence of renormalizations converge?
For the second question, let $\mathcal R_n(f)$ denote schematically the $n$th renormalization of $f$. Once the renormalization construction, its normalizations, and its target topological space $Y$ have been specified, the requested conclusion has the form \[ \forall f\in\mathcal E_0,\qquad \left(f\text{ is infinitely renormalizable and mildly dissipative}\right) \Longrightarrow \exists g\in Y:\ \mathcal R_n(f)\longrightarrow g\quad(n\to\infty). \] Here $Y$ and the convergence topology are unresolved placeholders, not additional universally quantified choices. The record does not specify renormalization combinatorics, domains, or a candidate limit.

The source motivates this question by proposing to implement the strategy attributed to reference [dCLM] for $b$ close to $0$. It supplies neither a quantitative range for $b$ nor an explicit statement that this proximity condition restricts the universal question above; that qualification remains unresolved.

Short English statement

The source asks whether the boundary of $\mathcal E_0$ is a piecewise smooth arc and whether every infinitely renormalizable mildly dissipative map in $\mathcal E_0$ has convergent renormalizations. It mentions the strategy of [dCLM] for $b$ close to $0$. Definitions of the family, $\mathcal E_0$, renormalization, and convergence are missing, so this is a qualified statement-only draft.
