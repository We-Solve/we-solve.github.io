Math-1654

Brown measure for unbounded affiliated operators

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(\mathcal M,\tau)$ be a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal trace normalized by $\tau(1)=1$, and let $\widetilde{\mathcal M}$ be its closed densely defined measurable affiliated operators. For $T\in\widetilde{\mathcal M}$, use $\nu_{|T|}$ for the spectral distribution of $|T|$ under $\tau$, avoiding confusion with the Brown measure being sought. Define \[ \mathcal M^\Delta=\left\{T:\int_0^\infty\log^+t\,\nu_{|T|}({\,\mathrm{d}} t)<\infty\right\}, \qquad \log\Delta(T)=\int_0^\infty\log t\,\nu_{|T|}({\,\mathrm{d}} t), \] where $\log^+t=\max\{\log t,0\}$ and $\exp(-\infty)=0$. The positive logarithmic part, lost in the damaged extraction, is essential: the negative part may be infinite. On this class $\Delta(T)$ is finite and nonnegative, possibly zero.

The source asks whether Brown measure extends to such unbounded operators. The supplied assessment attributes the affirmative answer to Haagerup and Schultz: for every $T\in\mathcal M^\Delta$, \[ u_T(\lambda)=\log\Delta(T-\lambda1) \] is a subharmonic function on ${\mathbb{C}}$, not identically $-\infty$, and \[ \mu_T=\frac1{2\pi}\Delta_{{\mathbb{R}}^2}u_T \] in the distributional sense is the uniquely determined probability Brown measure. The Laplacian symbol here is the real planar Laplacian, distinct from the Fuglede--Kadison determinant. Values $u_T(\lambda)=-\infty$ at some points are allowed; local integrability, rather than pointwise finiteness everywhere, is the distributional requirement.

The aid adds a bounded-quotient regularization. Suppose $T=AB^{-1}$ with $A,B\in\mathcal M$, $B$ injective with dense range and $\Delta(B)>0$. Such a factorization can be chosen using \[ B=(1+|T|^2)^{-1/2},\qquad A=TB. \] The logarithmic condition makes $\log\Delta(B)>-\infty$. Multiplicativity gives \[ u_T(\lambda)=\log\Delta(A-\lambda B)-\log\Delta(B). \] For $\varepsilon>0$ set \[ u_\varepsilon(\lambda)=\tfrac12\tau\log\left((A-\lambda B)^*(A-\lambda B) +\varepsilon1\right) -\log\Delta(B), \qquad\mu_\varepsilon=\frac1{2\pi}\Delta_{{\mathbb{R}}^2}u_\varepsilon. \] The reported $u_\varepsilon$ are smooth subharmonic functions decreasing to $u_T$, with convergence also in $L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Their positive Riesz measures converge vaguely to $\mu_T$: \[ \int\varphi\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_\varepsilon\longrightarrow \int\varphi\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_T \qquad(\varphi\in C_c({\mathbb{C}})). \] The limiting measure is independent of the bounded quotient factorization. No assertion of total-variation convergence of these regularizations is made. The solved label concerns the existence/uniqueness theorem in the supplied literature assessment; the additional approximation lemma remains a research-aid claim requiring verification.

Short English statement

Brown measure extends to affiliated operators with finite positive logarithmic singular-value moment by taking the normalized Laplacian of the log determinant. The aid also gives smooth bounded-quotient potentials whose Riesz measures converge vaguely to that measure, independently of the factorization.
