Math-1547

Repair and resolution of a core-model covering problem

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Printed Problem $15$ assumes no inner model with a Woodin cardinal, lets $K$ be the intended core model, assumes $\kappa$ is singular in both $K$ and $V$, and asks whether it is measurable in $K$. The aid reports that "singular in $K$" is actually printed, not just an OCR substitution. In any ZFC model a measurable cardinal is regular there; consequently no $\kappa$ can satisfy both the printed singularity and the requested measurability inside $K$. This invalidates the proposed implication as an intended regular-to-singular covering question, without saying the singularity hypotheses by themselves are contradictory.

The aid's explicit editorial repair replaces the first "singular" by "regular," motivated by the source's Cox attribution rather than silently changing it. The repaired question is: if $\kappa$ is a cardinal regular in $K$ but singular in $V$, under the stipulated absence of a Woodin inner model, must $\kappa$ be measurable in $K$? Regular and singular here mean cofinalities computed in the indicated model, not a shared external cofinality.

The aid attributes a positive answer for the repaired problem to Mitchell--Schimmerling's 2024 covering theorem under its stated hypothesis of no transitive class model of ZFC with a Woodin cardinal. In the reported application, \[ \operatorname{cf}^K(\kappa)=\kappa,\qquad \operatorname{cf}^V(\kappa)<\kappa,\qquad \kappa\text{ a cardinal in }V \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \kappa\text{ is measurable in }K. \] When $\operatorname{cf}^V(\kappa)$ is uncountable, the aid also reports \[ o^K(\kappa)\geq\operatorname{cf}^V(\kappa), \] where $o^K(\kappa)$ is its Mitchell order in $K$. For countable cofinality only the stated measurability conclusion is retained. A singular $V$-cardinal is greater than $\omega_2^V$; the aid says this and the relevant cofinality checks ensure applicability. The exact covering-theorem statement and remaining fine-structural hypotheses are not supplied and must be checked in the primary source, rather than reconstructed from a title.

The historical line "For $K$ below $0^{\mathsf P}$ this is known (Cox)" is reported to use the zero-pistol symbol; it was extracted as $0$ followed by a paragraph glyph. The aid identifies Cox's 2009 theorem as evidence for the one-word repair. It does not define that notation or supply a full theorem citation. The appended footnote $4$, about a model $N$ of condensation having a uniform witness function and indiscernibles relative to it, belongs to printed Problem $14$, not to the hypotheses of Problem $15$. Thus the source-repair claim and the repaired literature theorem must remain separate from the literal defective sentence.

Short English statement

The literal question asks whether a cardinal singular in $K$ is measurable there, which conflicts with measurability's regularity requirement. The aid explicitly repairs the first word to regular, guided by Cox's cited result, and reports a 2024 covering-theorem solution of that repaired problem, with an additional Mitchell-order bound at uncountable cofinality.
