Math-3589

Higher Structure Map Criterion for LICCI Ideals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a commutative Noetherian local Gorenstein ring, using the standard convention for this terminology, and let $I\subsetneq R$ be a perfect ideal of codimension $3$. Thus \[ \operatorname{pd}_R(R/I)=\operatorname{grade}_R(I)=3. \] Write a minimal free resolution as \[ 0\longrightarrow F_3\xrightarrow{d_3}F_2\xrightarrow{d_2}F_1\xrightarrow{d_1}R\longrightarrow R/I\longrightarrow0. \] Its ranks give the resolution format, recorded here as $(1,\operatorname{rank}F_1,\operatorname{rank}F_2,\operatorname{rank}F_3)$. This notation does not specify what the source means by a smaller format.

For ideals $J,K\subsetneq R$ of codimension $3$, a direct complete-intersection link is given by an ideal $C=(a_1,a_2,a_3)\subseteq J\cap K$, generated by an $R$-regular sequence, such that \[ J=C:K,\qquad K=C:J, \qquad C:J=\{r\in R:rJ\subseteq C\}. \] The ideal $I$ is LICCI if there is a finite chain of such links \[ I=I_0\sim I_1\sim\cdots\sim I_s \] ending at a complete-intersection ideal; a chain of length zero is allowed.

Conjecture 2, as supplied. For every such $R$ and $I$, the proposed criterion is \[ I\text{ is LICCI} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{there exists a higher structure map in }W(d_1) \text{ having a unit entry}. \] A unit entry means a coefficient in $R^\times=R\setminus\mathfrak m$. Here $W(d_i)$ retains the source's notation for representations containing higher structure maps; it is not being identified with the ordinary differential $d_i$. The supplied record does not define these representations, the allowed higher structure maps, their construction from a resolution, or the choices involved. Consequently, the right-hand condition remains source-dependent and cannot be made self-contained from this record alone.

The accompanying conjectural assertion asks whether the existence of a unit in $W(d_2)$ and $W(d_3)$ is equivalent to the existence of an ideal $I'$ linked to $I$ whose quotient has a resolution of smaller format. The record does not specify whether the two unit conditions are separate criteria or a joint condition, whether linkage here means one direct link or a finite chain, or the comparison of formats. No particular interpretation of these unresolved points is imposed in this draft.

Supplied contextual qualifications. The excerpt also asserts that one can construct a uniform family of generic examples of perfect ideals whose quotients have resolutions of ADE formats, and says these examples are expected to provide generic models for the resolutions. It describes three viewpoints: bigradings of Lie algebras of type $T_{p,q,r}$, with uniform descriptions of the differentials, referring to its reference [10]; defining ideals of affine pieces of Schubert varieties in certain homogeneous spaces of groups of type $T_{p,q,r}$, referring to [12]; and partial Jacobian ideals of sporadic invariants of prehomogeneous representations in the Sato--Kimura list, also referring to [12]. The listed invariants are $\Delta_4$, of degree $4$ for $\operatorname{SL}(6)$, $\Delta_7$, of degree $7$ for $\operatorname{SL}(7)$, and $\Delta_{16}$, of degree $16$ for $\operatorname{SL}(8)$. Their ambient representation formulas are corrupted in the supplied extraction and are not reconstructed here. The excerpt says that type $D_n$ recovers the Buchsbaum--Eisenbud generic Gorenstein ideals of codimension $3$, citing [4], and Anne Brown's almost complete intersections of codimension $3$, citing [2]. Definitions of ADE formats and the bibliographic identities of [2], [4], [10], and [12] are not supplied.

Finally, the excerpt introduces a Theorem 3 concerning perfect codimension-three ideals over local Gorenstein rings whose quotient resolutions have ADE format, mentioning joint work with L. Guerrieri and X. Ni, but ends immediately after the word "Then." Its conclusion is missing, so this fragment supplies no complete theorem statement.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every perfect codimension-three ideal over a local Gorenstein ring belongs to the complete-intersection linkage class exactly when a higher structure map in $W(d_1)$ has a unit coefficient. Related unit conditions involving $W(d_2)$ and $W(d_3)$ are proposed to detect linkage to smaller resolution formats. The higher-map constructions and the meaning of the latter criterion are not fully specified in the supplied record. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
