Murphy-Element Description of Centralizer-Algebra Centers

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns the center of $kS_n^{S_\ell}$. Its coefficient domain, parameter range, and polynomial symmetry action are not specified sufficiently to give a uniquely determined conjecture.

To explain the notation conditionally, suppose that $k$ is a field, $n\geq 1$, and $0\leq\ell\leq n$, and embed $S_\ell$ in $S_n$ by permuting the first $\ell$ letters. These are explanatory conventions, not hypotheses explicitly supplied by the record. Writing $kS_n$ for the group algebra, the usual centralizer interpretation is \[ A_{n,\ell}=kS_n^{S_\ell} =\{a\in kS_n:ha=ah\text{ for every }h\in S_\ell\}. \] Its center is \[ Z(A_{n,\ell})=\{z\in A_{n,\ell}:za=az\text{ for every }a\in A_{n,\ell}\}. \] Under the standard additive Jucys--Murphy convention, the Murphy elements are the commuting elements \[ J_1=0,\qquad J_i=\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}(j\ i)\in kS_n\quad(2\leq i\leq n). \] The record does not explicitly specify this normalization or the indexing of the Murphy elements.

The source asks whether $Z(A_{n,\ell})$ is the algebra of polynomials in the Murphy elements that are symmetric under $S_\ell\times S_{n-1}$. More precisely, if an intended action $\rho$ of $H=S_\ell\times S_{n-1}$ on $k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ were supplied, the corresponding candidate equality would be \[ Z(A_{n,\ell})\stackrel{?}{=} \{f(J_1,\ldots,J_n):f\in k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]^{H,\rho}\}, \] where the superscript denotes polynomials fixed by every element of $H$ under $\rho$. The action $\rho$ is missing: the displayed product does not specify how its two factors permute the variables or act on polynomial expressions. In particular, no replacement of the supplied $S_{n-1}$ by another symmetric group is justified by this record. Thus this display describes the unresolved target conditionally; it is not a fully specified, universally quantified conjecture. The intended coefficient assumptions, parameter range, Murphy convention, and symmetry action require clarification.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether the center of a symmetric-group centralizer algebra consists exactly of suitably symmetric polynomial expressions in Murphy elements. The expression $S_\ell\times S_{n-1}$ is retained exactly as supplied, but its action and the coefficient hypotheses are unspecified. The record labels the problem open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
