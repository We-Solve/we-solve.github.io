Generators and Support Loci of Bernstein–Sato Ideals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a germ \[ F=(f_1,\ldots,f_r):({\mathbb{C}}^n,0)\longrightarrow({\mathbb{C}}^r,0) \] and an associated Bernstein--Sato ideal denoted by $B_F$. The record does not supply the defining functional equation for this ideal, the precise class of germs, or the definition of the cohomology-support locus $V_F$. These omissions prevent an unconditional, fully specified formulation.

Writing $R={\mathbb{C}}[s_1,\ldots,s_r]$ for the polynomial ring in the parameter variables, the first proposed assertion is that, for every tuple $F$ in the intended class, the ideal $B_F\subseteq R$ admits a generating family $(g_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ whose members are finite products of affine linear factors of the form \[ \ell(s_1,\ldots,s_r)=a_1s_1+\cdots+a_rs_r+b, \qquad a_i\in{\mathbb{Q}}_{\geq0},\quad b\in{\mathbb{Q}}_{>0}. \] Thus the proposed assertion is ideal generation, $B_F=(g_\lambda\mid\lambda\in\Lambda)$, and not merely a description of its zero set. The supplied coefficient conditions do not explicitly require any $a_i$ to be nonzero.

For an ideal $I\subseteq R$, define its complex zero set by \[ Z(I)=\{s\in{\mathbb{C}}^r:p(s)=0\text{ for every }p\in I\}. \] With the explicitly provisional convention \[ \operatorname{Exp}(s_1,\ldots,s_r) =\left(e^{2\pi\mathrm{i}s_1},\ldots,e^{2\pi\mathrm{i}s_r}\right) \in({\mathbb{C}}^\times)^r, \] the second proposed assertion is \[ \operatorname{Exp}\left(Z(B_F)\right)=V_F. \] The source extraction does not specify the exponential convention or define $V_F$, including the spaces, local systems, cohomological degrees, and points involved in its construction. Consequently this equality records the supplied target but remains unresolved as a precise mathematical statement. In particular, no choice between support at the origin and support obtained from nearby points is made here. The intended definition of $B_F$, including its parameter-shift convention, must also be recovered before either assertion can be assessed.

Short English statement

For every germ tuple $F$ in the intended class, the problem asks whether its Bernstein--Sato ideal has generators that are products of affine linear factors with nonnegative rational slopes and positive rational constant terms, and whether exponentiating its zero set gives the stated cohomology-support locus. The supplied extraction leaves essential definitions unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
