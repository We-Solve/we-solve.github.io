Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the conventional interpretation of the source notation, write $[{\mathbb{N}}]^{\aleph_0}$ for the family of infinite subsets of ${\mathbb{N}}$, and write $A\subseteq^*B$ when $A\setminus B$ is finite. A family $\mathcal F\subseteq[{\mathbb{N}}]^{\aleph_0}$ has the strong finite intersection property if every nonempty finite subfamily has infinite intersection. An infinite pseudointersection of $\mathcal F$ is a set $A\in[{\mathbb{N}}]^{\aleph_0}$ such that $A\subseteq^*F$ for every $F\in\mathcal F$. The pseudointersection number $\mathfrak p$ is the least cardinality of a family with the strong finite intersection property and no infinite pseudointersection. The tower number $\mathfrak t$ is the least cardinal $\kappa$ for which there is a family $(T_\alpha)_{\alpha<\kappa}$ of infinite subsets of ${\mathbb{N}}$ such that $T_\beta\subseteq^*T_\alpha$ whenever $\alpha<\beta<\kappa$, and the family has no infinite pseudointersection. These are conventional definitions; the supplied excerpt does not itself define these symbols.

The question in Problem 49 is whether \[ \mathfrak p_{\mathfrak p}=\mathfrak t. \] Here $\mathfrak p_{\mathfrak p}$ denotes the source's indexed invariant evaluated at the cardinal $\mathfrak p$.
