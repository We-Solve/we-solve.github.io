Several Complex Variables Problem List — Problem 80

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret a five-dimensional CR surface as an abstract CR manifold of hypersurface type and CR dimension two. Under the usual smooth interpretation, this consists of a smooth real manifold $M$ of dimension $5$ and a smooth complex subbundle $T^{0,1}M\subset {\mathbb{C}}\otimes_{{\mathbb{R}}}TM$ of complex rank $2$, satisfying \[ T^{0,1}M\cap\overline{T^{0,1}M}=\{0\},\qquad [\Gamma(T^{0,1}M),\Gamma(T^{0,1}M)]\subseteq\Gamma(T^{0,1}M). \] Write $T^{1,0}M=\overline{T^{0,1}M}$, and let $H\subset TM$ be the real rank-four distribution whose complexification is $T^{1,0}M\oplus T^{0,1}M$. Strong pseudoconvexity means that near every point there is a nonvanishing real one-form $\theta$ with $\ker\theta=H$ for which the Levi form \[ \mathcal L_{\theta}(Z,\overline W)=-i\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\theta(Z,\overline W),\qquad Z,W\in T^{1,0}M, \] is positive definite, after changing the sign of $\theta$ if necessary.

Under these conventions, the question is: for every such strongly pseudoconvex $M$ and every $p\in M$, does there exist an open neighborhood $U$ of $p$ and a smooth embedding \[ F=(f_1,f_2,f_3):U\longrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}^3 \] such that $L f_j=0$ for every smooth local section $L$ of $T^{0,1}M|_U$ and every $j\in\{1,2,3\}$? Here an embedding is a homeomorphism onto its image with injective real differential; its image is consequently a real hypersurface in ${\mathbb{C}}^3$.

The supplied statement does not specify differentiability, explicitly state hypersurface type, or name an ambient complex dimension. The formulation above makes the conventional smooth hypersurface interpretation explicit; these conventions require confirmation against the source. The term "surface" is retained from the source and is interpreted using its explicit real dimension $5$.

Short English statement

Does every strongly pseudoconvex abstract CR manifold of real dimension $5$ admit a local CR embedding near each point? Under the smooth hypersurface interpretation, the target is ${\mathbb{C}}^3$. The source leaves the regularity and embedding conventions unspecified.
