Eventual Linearity of Equivariant Projective Dimensions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write ${\mathbb{N}}_{>0}=\{1,2,\ldots\}$ and let \[ \operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}}_{>0})=\{\pi:{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}:\pi(i)<\pi(j)\text{ whenever }i<j\} \] be the monoid of strictly increasing maps, with composition as its operation. For an $R_n$-module $M$, its projective dimension $\operatorname{pd}_{R_n}(M)$ is the least length of a projective resolution, or $\infty$ if no finite projective resolution exists.

The supplied record does not specify the rings $R_n$, their coefficient field, their variables or grading, their transition maps, or the action of $\operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}}_{>0})$. Consequently, the conjecture below is relative to the intended ambient system, which must be recovered from the cited source before a complete formalization is possible. Under the usual compatible-map interpretation, an increasing map $\pi$ with $\pi(m)\leq n$ induces a ring homomorphism $\pi_{m,n}:R_m\to R_n$, and invariance means \[ \pi_{m,n}(I_m)\subseteq I_n \qquad(m,n\geq1,\ \pi\in\operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}}_{>0}),\ \pi(m)\leq n). \] This is an explicitly conditional interpretation; the record does not supply the action needed to instantiate it or the precise convention for "filtration."

Conjecture 3, attributed in the supplied excerpt to [3, Conjecture 1.3]. For the intended ring system and action, let $I=(I_n)_{n\geq1}$ be any $\operatorname{Inc}({\mathbb{N}}_{>0})$-invariant filtration such that $I_n$ is a homogeneous ideal and \[ 0\neq I_n\subsetneq R_n\qquad\text{for every }n\geq1. \] Is the sequence $\left(\operatorname{pd}_{R_n}(R_n/I_n)\right)_{n\geq1}$ eventually linear? Explicitly, the target is \[ \exists a\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\ \exists b\in{\mathbb{Z}}\ \exists N\in{\mathbb{N}}_{>0}\ \forall n\geq N:\qquad \operatorname{pd}_{R_n}(R_n/I_n)=an+b. \] Here eventual linearity means eventual equality with an affine function, with finite values; integrality and nonnegativity of the slope follow from the nonnegative integer values at consecutive sufficiently large indices.

The supplied excerpt reports that Conjecture 3 is proved for saturated chains of monomial ideals. Neither "saturated" nor the precise ambient monomial setting is defined in this record, so this reported special case cannot be formulated completely here. The excerpt separately reports an asymptotic result for Conjecture 2 for all chains of monomial ideals, saturated or otherwise; Conjecture 2 is not supplied, and that report must not be substituted for the present conjecture. These are supplied source claims, not independently verified results.

Short English statement

For every invariant filtration of nonzero proper homogeneous ideals in the intended ring system, the conjecture asks whether the projective dimensions of the quotients eventually equal $an+b$. The ambient rings and action are missing from the record. Supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports the saturated monomial case as proved; human review must verify the setting and these claims.
