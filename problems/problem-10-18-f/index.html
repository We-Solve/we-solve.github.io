Problem (F. Deloup). Classify the monoid of isomorphism classes of the quadratic forms $q_\sigma$, with operation induced by orthogonal sum: \[ \mathcal Q=\{[q_\sigma]\},\qquad [q_\sigma]+[q_\tau]=[q_\sigma\perp q_\tau]. \] Here an isomorphism is an isomorphism of the underlying algebraic objects that preserves the quadratic form and any required structure. When the forms have additive domains $A_\sigma,A_\tau$ and a common additive target, the usual orthogonal sum is \[ (q_\sigma\perp q_\tau)(x,y)=q_\sigma(x)+q_\tau(y), \qquad(x,y)\in A_\sigma\oplus A_\tau. \] This formula describes the operation conditionally; it does not identify the missing family. A classification should determine the isomorphism classes and their sum, for example through complete invariants or a presentation of the monoid, including its neutral element in the intended setup. The problem concerns actual isomorphism classes under orthogonal sum; it does not specify passing to a Witt group or a stable-equivalence quotient. Until $q_\sigma$ is defined, a particular monoid cannot be reconstructed from this record.

Short English statement

Deloup asks to classify the quadratic forms denoted $q_\sigma$, together with their orthogonal-sum operation. The family itself is not defined in the excerpt, so its underlying domains and quadratic-form conventions must be recovered before a concrete classification problem is fixed.
