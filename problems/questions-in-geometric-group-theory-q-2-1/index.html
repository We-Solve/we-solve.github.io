Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 2.1

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, write $\operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G)$. Say it is virtually generated by Dehn twists if a subgroup generated by the intended twist classes has finite index. In a standard splitting $G=A*_C B$, an element $z\in C_A(C)$ gives a twist fixing $A$ and conjugating $B$ by $z$, with analogous HNN twists. Infinite order must be measured in $\operatorname{Out}(G)$, not inferred merely from the order of $z$. The source does not specify every permitted edge-group or twist convention.

A CAT(0) group here has the usual geometric-action meaning: it acts properly and cocompactly by isometries on a proper CAT(0) geodesic metric space. No torsion-free hypothesis is imposed on the CAT(0) groups. The source attributes three related questions to Swarup:

  1. Find a proof of Johannson's virtual Dehn-twist generation theorem for $\operatorname{Out}(\pi_1M)$, for a Haken 3-manifold $M$, along the lines of Rips--Sela's theorem for torsion-free one-ended hyperbolic groups. This asks for a proof approach, not whether the already named Johannson theorem is true.
  2. Does the analogous virtual Dehn-twist generation assertion hold for CAT(0) groups?
  3. In particular, is \[ \forall G\text{ a CAT(0) group},\quad |\operatorname{Out}(G)|=\infty \Longrightarrow\exists\delta\in\operatorname{Out}(G)\text{ an intended Dehn twist of infinite order}? \]
The Haken/manifold boundary conventions are not reproduced; the usual orientable Haken setting includes irreducibility and a two-sided incompressible surface. No additional torsion-free or one-ended restriction is transferred from part (1) to parts (2)--(3).

The nested research summary labels the record SOLVED-IN-LITERATURE and reports a negative answer to (3), hence to general virtual twist generation: Fioravanti, arXiv:2601.22789, Example 8.3, is said to give CAT(0) groups with infinite outer automorphism group for every finite-index subgroup and with no nontrivial Dehn twists. The summary calls the construction published explicitly but supplies only an arXiv identifier, not a publication venue; no stronger bibliographic status is inferred. It attributes ingredients to Italiano--Martelli--Migliorini, Groves--Manning and Martelli (2023--2025), without complete references.

The same summary records qualified positive results for hyperbolic groups, toral relatively hyperbolic groups, isolated-flat CAT(0) groups with abelian flat stabilizers, and special cubulated groups up to characteristic finite index. It names Levitt, Theorem 1.4; Groves, Theorem 5.9; and Fioravanti, Theorems C and E, including an undefined poison-subgroup condition. Their precise statements and scope are not supplied, so they are not expanded into unqualified theorems. The summary's elementary ${\mathbb{Z}}^2*_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Z}}^2$ observation concerns only twists of a natural splitting and is not treated as a proof of absence of all twists. Its discussion of part (1) ends "effectively yes --- the Rips--Sela program now$\ldots$" and is truncated, so no complete methodological proof is reconstructed.

The source was manually transcribed from the 2004 list, accessed 2026-07-29, and its status and rights evidence remain NEEDS_REVIEW despite the later solved label. All recent resolution claims remain supplied research-summary reports, not an independently checked proof or current-status determination.

Short English statement

Swarup asks for a Rips--Sela-style proof of Johannson's outer-automorphism theorem, its extension to CAT(0) groups, and an infinite-order twist whenever the outer automorphism group is infinite. The supplied summary reports a general CAT(0) counterexample, but its bibliographic details and the first part's truncated proof-method claim require review.
