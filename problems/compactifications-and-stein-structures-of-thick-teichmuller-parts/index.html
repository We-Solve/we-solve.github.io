Compactifications and Stein Structures of Thick Teichmüller Parts

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses four related questions about compactifications of moduli spaces and thick parts of Teichmüller space. Several objects and comparison maps are unspecified, so the questions below retain those qualifications rather than assert a fully determined conjecture.

For the usual closed-surface interpretation, let $S_g$ be a closed connected oriented surface of genus $g\geq 2$, and let $T(S_g)$ denote its Teichmüller space of marked conformal structures. Using the hyperbolic metric of curvature $-1$ associated with such a structure $X$, define \[ \operatorname{sys}(X)=\inf_{\gamma}\ell_X(\gamma), \] where $\gamma$ ranges over essential simple closed curves and $\ell_X(\gamma)$ is the length of the geodesic representative. For $\varepsilon>0$, distinguish \[ T^{\geq\varepsilon}(S_g)=\{X\in T(S_g):\operatorname{sys}(X)\geq\varepsilon\},\qquad T^{>\varepsilon}(S_g)=\{X\in T(S_g):\operatorname{sys}(X)>\varepsilon\}. \] These definitions explain conventional possible meanings of the source notation $T^\varepsilon(S_g)$; the record does not specify the surface hypotheses, genus range, permitted values of $\varepsilon$, metric normalization, or boundary convention. In particular, the closed-surface interpretation above is conditional, not an additional supplied hypothesis.

  1. Which classes of the Deligne--Mumford compactification come from the Penner compactification? A precise version requires the moduli problem, the particular Penner compactification, the meaning of "classes," their coefficient system and degrees, and the comparison map or correspondence through which they come from that compactification. None of these choices is supplied sufficiently to define an image of a specified map.
  2. Is the Sullivan space homotopic to a suitably compactified moduli space? If "homotopic" means "homotopy equivalent," this asks whether there are continuous maps $u:\mathcal S\to\overline{\mathcal M}$ and $v:\overline{\mathcal M}\to\mathcal S$ satisfying \[ v\circ u\simeq\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal S},\qquad u\circ v\simeq\operatorname{id}_{\overline{\mathcal M}}. \] Here $\mathcal S$ and $\overline{\mathcal M}$ are placeholders: the record does not define the Sullivan space, its topology, the moduli problem, or the allowed compactification. The interpretation as homotopy equivalence also requires confirmation.
  3. Is $T^\varepsilon(S_g)$ a Stein manifold admitting a plurisubharmonic exhaustion? For a complex manifold $U$, a continuous function $\rho:U\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is plurisubharmonic if its pullback along every holomorphic disk is subharmonic; it is an exhaustion if $\{x\in U:\rho(x)\leq c\}$ is compact for every $c\in{\mathbb{R}}$. The source does not specify additional smoothness or strict plurisubharmonicity. The set $T^{>\varepsilon}(S_g)$ is an open subset of $T(S_g)$ and inherits a complex structure. The closed inequality does not by itself provide a complex manifold without boundary, so its interpretation in the Stein question must be specified. The record also does not state whether the question concerns every admissible $\varepsilon$, sufficiently small $\varepsilon$, or some $\varepsilon$.
  4. What is the Dolbeault cohomology of the intended thick part? For a specified complex manifold $U$, write $\mathcal A^{p,q}(U)$ for smooth complex differential forms of type $(p,q)$, and define \[ H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(U)= \frac{\ker\left(\bar\partial:\mathcal A^{p,q}(U)\to\mathcal A^{p,q+1}(U)\right)} {\operatorname{im}\left(\bar\partial:\mathcal A^{p,q-1}(U)\to\mathcal A^{p,q}(U)\right)}, \qquad 0\leq p,q\leq\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}U, \] with zero denominator when $q=0$. This gives the usual smooth-form interpretation if the intended thick part is an open complex manifold. For a thick part including its boundary, the function spaces, boundary regularity, and any boundary conditions must instead be specified. The record supplies none of these choices.

Short English statement

The source asks which Deligne--Mumford classes arise from a Penner compactification, whether a Sullivan space has the homotopy type of a suitably compactified moduli space, and whether a thick Teichmüller part is Stein with a plurisubharmonic exhaustion, together with a computation of its Dolbeault cohomology. The compactifications, comparison maps, parameter ranges, and treatment of the thick-part boundary require clarification. The supplied open-status label and literature assessment are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
