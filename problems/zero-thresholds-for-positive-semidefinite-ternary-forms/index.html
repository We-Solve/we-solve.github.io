Zero Thresholds for Positive Semidefinite Ternary Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the intended real interpretation, a ternary form of degree $2k$ is a homogeneous polynomial $p\in{\mathbb{R}}[x,y,z]_{2k}$, where ${\mathbb{R}}[x,y,z]_m$ denotes the space of homogeneous polynomials of degree $m$, including the zero polynomial. It is positive semidefinite if $p(v)\geq0$ for every $v\in{\mathbb{R}}^3$. Define its set of distinct real projective zeros by \[ Z_{{\mathbb{R}}}(p)=\{[v]\in\mathbb{RP}^{2}:p(v)=0\}, \] where $[v]$ is the equivalence class of a nonzero real vector under multiplication by nonzero real scalars. An indefinite real form $h$ means a homogeneous polynomial taking both positive and negative values on ${\mathbb{R}}^3$.

For each integer $k\geq3$, let $\alpha(k)$ be the least integer $a$, provided such a least integer exists, with the following property: for every $p\in{\mathbb{R}}[x,y,z]_{2k}$, \[ \left(p(v)\geq0\text{ for all }v\in{\mathbb{R}}^3\ \text{ and }\ \#Z_{{\mathbb{R}}}(p)>a\right) \Longrightarrow \left\{ \begin{gathered} \text{there exist an integer }d\text{ with }1\leq d\leq k,\\ h\in{\mathbb{R}}[x,y,z]_d\text{ indefinite, and }q\in{\mathbb{R}}[x,y,z]_{2k-2d},\\ p=h^2q. \end{gathered} \right. \] The zero polynomial is included because the supplied statement does not exclude it. An infinite projective zero set satisfies the strict inequality against every finite integer $a$.

The supplied conjecture is \[ \alpha(k)\geq k^2+1\qquad\text{for every integer }k\geq3. \] The source record does not explicitly specify the coefficient field, whether projective zeros are real and counted distinctly, or the precise convention for indefiniteness. The definitions above state the natural real, sign-changing interpretation; confirmation against the source is required. The existence of a finite least threshold is presupposed by the supplied formulation and is not established here.

Short English statement

For each integer $k\geq3$, consider the least zero-count threshold above which every nonnegative real ternary form of degree $2k$ has a square factor whose underlying form changes sign. The conjecture says that this threshold is at least $k^2+1$. The real-projective interpretation and the existence of the threshold require source confirmation.
