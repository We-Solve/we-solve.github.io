One possible meaning of monotonicity for a Markov process is the following. On a partially ordered measurable state space $(\Omega,\preceq)$, a transition semigroup $(P_t)_{t\geq0}$ is order preserving if, for every bounded measurable increasing function $f$, every $t\geq0$, and every $x,y\in\Omega$, \[ x\preceq y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (P_tf)(x)\leq(P_tf)(y). \] Here increasing means that $f(x)\leq f(y)$ whenever $x\preceq y$. This definition is explanatory only: the supplied fragment does not establish whether the intended assumptions are this dynamical property, stochastic monotonicity of equilibrium measures in parameters or boundary conditions, or another monotonicity property.
