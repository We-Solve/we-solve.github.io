Math-15693

Local Compactness and Nuclear-Space Extensions of Character Spectra

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a commutative complex $C^*$-algebra $\mathcal L$, let $X=\operatorname{Spec}(\mathcal L)$ be its character spectrum: the set of nonzero $*$-homomorphisms $\chi:\mathcal L\to{\mathbb{C}}$, with the topology of pointwise convergence on $\mathcal L$. Thus $X$ is locally compact Hausdorff and $\mathcal L\cong C_0(X)$, where $C_0(X)$ denotes the continuous complex-valued functions vanishing at infinity.

The supplied record singles out characters belonging to a class denoted by $\operatorname{Rep}_0(\mathcal L,{\mathbb{C}})$. Write \[ X_0=\{\chi\in X:\chi\in\operatorname{Rep}_0(\mathcal L,{\mathbb{C}})\}, \] and give $X_0$ the subspace topology inherited from $X$. The record does not define $\operatorname{Rep}_0$ or specify the report's construction of $\mathcal L$; these are unresolved qualifications essential to identifying the intended subset.

Local-compactness question. For the algebra and distinguished character class intended in the cited report, is $X_0$ locally compact? Explicitly, is it true that \[ \forall\chi\in X_0\quad\exists U\subseteq X_0:\quad \chi\in U,\qquad U\text{ is open in }X_0,\qquad \overline U^{\,X_0}\text{ is compact}? \] Here the closure and compactness are taken in $X_0$. The supplied record does not determine whether the report intends a particular algebra or a quantified class of algebras, so this question cannot be promoted to an assertion about every commutative $C^*$-algebra and every distinguished subset of its spectrum.

Extension question. Does the character-spectrum formalism referred to in the report for ${\mathbb{R}}^{({\mathbb{N}})}$ admit an analogous construction for separable nuclear spaces? Algebraically, \[ {\mathbb{R}}^{({\mathbb{N}})}=\{(x_n)_{n\geq1}:x_n\in{\mathbb{R}},\ x_n=0\text{ for all but finitely many }n\}. \] The source record does not specify its topology, the scalar field and precise category of the proposed nuclear spaces, the construction to be extended, or the properties that an extension must preserve. Consequently, the extension question is retained as a research target with unresolved specifications; the supplied evidence does not support a fully quantified existence statement.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether the report's distinguished subset of a commutative $C^*$-algebra's character spectrum is locally compact in its inherited topology. The second asks whether the associated formalism for ${\mathbb{R}}^{({\mathbb{N}})}$ extends to separable nuclear spaces. The distinguished character class and the requirements for the extension are not defined in the supplied record, so both questions retain explicit unresolved qualifications.
