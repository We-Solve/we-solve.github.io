Open Problems and Questions about Geodesics — Question 55 ([Sanchez2015])

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,g)$ be a compact Lorentzian manifold. A global conformal relation to a metric of constant curvature means that there exist a smooth Lorentzian metric $h$ on $M$, a smooth function $u\colon M\to{\mathbb{R}}$, and a constant $K\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that \[ g=e^{2u}h, \] and every nondegenerate tangent two-plane has sectional curvature $K$ with respect to $h$. A conformal diffeomorphism to another Lorentzian manifold of constant sectional curvature gives this formulation by pulling back its metric.

The question is whether these assumptions imply that $(M,g)$ is geodesically complete: does every maximal affinely parameterized $g$-geodesic $\gamma\colon I\to M$ have $I={\mathbb{R}}$? This asks for completeness of all geodesics, including timelike, null, and spacelike geodesics.

Here smooth manifolds without boundary and sectional curvature are the conventional interpretations of the source wording. The source does not explicitly specify a boundary convention, a dimension restriction, or regularity hypotheses; these interpretations require confirmation in mathematical review.

Short English statement

Must every compact Lorentzian manifold whose metric is globally conformal to a Lorentzian metric of constant sectional curvature be geodesically complete? Completeness concerns the original metric and affine parameters. The source supplies an open-problem label; the formulation and its conventions require human review.
