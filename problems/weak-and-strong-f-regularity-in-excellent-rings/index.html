Weak and Strong F-Regularity in Excellent Rings

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian ring with identity and prime characteristic $p>0$. Write $R^{\circ}=R\setminus\bigcup_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}(R)}\mathfrak p$. Excellence means that $R$ is universally catenary, every finitely generated $R$-algebra has open regular locus, and, for every prime $\mathfrak p$, the completion map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to\widehat{R_{\mathfrak p}}$ has geometrically regular fibers.

For $e\geq 0$, put $q=p^e$. Let $R^{(e)}$ denote the bimodule whose underlying left $R$-module is $R$, with right action $a\cdot r=ar^q$, and set $F_R^e(M)=R^{(e)}\otimes_R M$. For an inclusion $N\subseteq M$ of $R$-modules, let $N_M^{[q]}$ be the image of $F_R^e(N)\to F_R^e(M)$. Its tight closure is \[ N_M^*=\left\{x\in M:\ \exists c\in R^{\circ}\ \exists e_0\geq0\ \forall e\geq e_0,\quad c(1\otimes x)\in N_M^{[p^e]}\right\}. \] In particular, for an ideal $I\subseteq R$, writing $I^{[q]}=(a^q:a\in I)$, this gives \[ I^*=\left\{x\in R:\ \exists c\in R^{\circ}\ \exists e_0\geq0\ \forall e\geq e_0,\quad cx^{p^e}\in I^{[p^e]}\right\}. \] The finitistic tight closure is \[ N_M^{*\mathrm{fg}}= \bigcup_{\substack{L\subseteq M\\L\text{ finitely generated}}} \operatorname{im}\left((N\cap L)_L^*\longrightarrow M\right). \] The ring $R$ is weakly $F$-regular if every ideal is tightly closed; equivalently, $N_M^{*\mathrm{fg}}=N$ for every inclusion of modules. Following the module-theoretic convention indicated by the supplied excerpt, $R$ is strongly $F$-regular if $N_M^*=N$ for every inclusion $N\subseteq M$ of arbitrary $R$-modules. For comparison with the supplied background, $R$ is $F$-regular if $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is weakly $F$-regular for every $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(R)$.

Conjecture 1 (the weak implies strong conjecture). For every excellent ring $R$ of prime characteristic $p>0$, does \[ \left(\forall I\subseteq R\text{ an ideal},\ I^*=I\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(\forall R\text{-modules }N\subseteq M,\ N_M^*=N\right) \] hold?

The quantification over modules on the right includes modules that are not finitely generated. No locality, domain, finite-type, or $F$-finiteness hypothesis is imposed on the conjecture. The source extraction damages the notation distinguishing finitistic and unrestricted tight closure; the definitions above reconstruct that distinction and require mathematical review against the report. In particular, a definition restricted to finite Frobenius maps has not been substituted for the supplied general-ring formulation.

Short English statement

Must every excellent weakly $F$-regular ring of prime characteristic $p>0$ be strongly $F$-regular? In the module-theoretic formulation used here, the question asks whether tight closure being trivial for all ideals forces it to be trivial for every inclusion of arbitrary modules. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; that status and the reconstructed definitions require independent review.
