Principality of Artin–Schreier Deformation Ideals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p=3$, and let $\pi:C\to{\mathbb{P}}^1$ be the wildly ramified Artin--Schreier cover intended in the supplied source, with exactly one branch point, namely $\infty$. An Artin--Schreier cover in characteristic $3$ is a cyclic cover of degree $3$ described at the function-field level by an equation $y^3-y=f$. The ground field and the particular cover are not specified in the record.

Write $R_\pi$ for the ring called the universal deformation ring of $\pi$ in the source. The supplied record asserts a presentation \[ R_\pi=R_\infty\cong W[[t_1,t_2]]/I,\qquad I\ne(0), \] where $W[[t_1,t_2]]$ denotes the formal power series ring in two variables over the coefficient ring denoted by $W$. The record does not define $W$, the deformation functor, or the local deformation problem represented by $R_\infty$; the displayed presentation is therefore retained as a source-supplied premise for the intended example, rather than asserted for every Artin--Schreier cover with one branch point.

Question. For this intended deformation problem and presentation, is $I$ principal? Explicitly, setting $A=W[[t_1,t_2]]$, does there exist $F\in A\setminus\{0\}$ such that \[ I=(F)=\{FG:G\in A\}? \] The missing specification of the cover, coefficient ring, and deformation problem must be recovered before this can be treated as a fully determined mathematical question.

Short English statement

For the intended Artin--Schreier cover in characteristic $3$, branched only at infinity, the supplied source describes its deformation ring as $W[[t_1,t_2]]/I$ with $I\ne(0)$. The question asks whether this ideal can be generated by one element. The record leaves the particular cover, the coefficient ring $W$, and the deformation conditions unspecified.
