Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 37

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ denote the separable hyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with normalized trace $\tau_R$. Under the usual interpretation, $\mathcal U$ is a nonprincipal ultrafilter on ${\mathbb{N}}$, and the tracial ultrapower is \[ R^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},R)\big/\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\tau_R}=0\},\qquad \|x\|_{2,\tau_R}=\tau_R(x^*x)^{1/2}. \] Here separability of a von Neumann algebra means separability of its predual. A type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor is a von Neumann algebra with center ${\mathbb{C}}1$, a faithful normal normalized trace, and no nonzero minimal projections. A separable type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor is Connes embeddable if it admits a unital trace-preserving embedding into a tracial ultrapower of $R$. Embeddings here are injective unital trace-preserving $*$-homomorphisms.

Question attributed to S. Popa. For every Connes embeddable separable type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor $N$, does there exist an embedding \[ \pi:N\hookrightarrow R^{\mathcal U} \] such that its relative commutant \[ \pi(N)'\cap R^{\mathcal U} =\{x\in R^{\mathcal U}:x\pi(a)=\pi(a)x\text{ for every }a\in N\} \] is a factor, equivalently, \[ Z\left(\pi(N)'\cap R^{\mathcal U}\right)={\mathbb{C}}1? \] The source does not specify the index set or properties of $\mathcal U$, or whether it is fixed in advance or existentially quantified. The displayed formulation uses the usual nonprincipal-ultrafilter-on-${\mathbb{N}}$ convention; its intended ultrafilter quantification requires confirmation.

The supplied record labels this question open. It also reports a negative resolution of a reduced $C^*$-algebra version, citing the key elayavalli2025negativeresolutioncalgebraictarski. The record supplies neither that version's precise statement nor bibliographic details for the cited work, so this report does not specify a negative answer to the factor question above.

Short English statement

The question, attributed to S. Popa, asks whether every separable Connes embeddable type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor can be embedded into $R^{\mathcal U}$ so that its relative commutant has trivial center. The intended quantification of $\mathcal U$ is unspecified. The supplied open status and the reported negative result for a reduced $C^*$-algebra version have not been independently verified.
