Math-3431

Fractional Moments of Möbius Character and Archimedean Twists

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mu:{\mathbb{N}}\to\{-1,0,1\}$ be the Möbius function: $\mu(1)=1$, $\mu(n)=0$ if a prime square divides $n$, and $\mu(n)=(-1)^k$ if $n$ is a product of $k$ distinct primes. For an integer modulus $r\geq2$, let $\sum_{\chi\bmod r}$ denote the sum over all Dirichlet characters modulo $r$, including the principal character. All sums over $n\leq x$ are over positive integers. Write $n^{it}=\exp(it\log n)$, with natural logarithms.

The supplied conjecture asks that, for every fixed $A>0$ and every $q\in[0,1]$, \[ \frac{1}{r-1}\sum_{\chi\bmod r} \left|\sum_{n\leq x}\mu(n)\chi(n)\right|^{2q} \ll \left(\frac{x}{1+(1-q)\sqrt{\log\log x}}\right)^q, \qquad x\leq r^A, \] and \[ \frac{1}{2T}\int_{-T}^{T} \left|\sum_{n\leq x}\mu(n)n^{it}\right|^{2q}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t \ll \left(\frac{x}{1+(1-q)\sqrt{\log\log x}}\right)^q, \qquad x\leq T^A, \] where $T>0$. Here $\ll$ denotes an upper bound by a constant multiple of the right-hand side; the permitted dependence of that constant is not specified in the supplied record.

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify whether $r$ must be prime or may be any integer modulus. The denominator $r-1$ is retained exactly; for composite $r$, the number of characters is $\varphi(r)$, not generally $r-1$. The displayed right-hand sides are real-valued as written for $x\geq e$; this identifies a domain on which the formulas make sense, but the source supplies no lower bound on $x$ or convention for smaller $x$. The endpoint $q=0$ also requires a convention for $|0|^0$, which is not supplied. Consequently, the displays preserve the requested estimates but do not resolve the missing modulus restriction, lower ranges, endpoint convention, or constant uniformity.

Short English statement

The conjecture seeks upper bounds for fractional moments of Möbius sums twisted by Dirichlet characters and by $n^{it}$, throughout the respective ranges $x\leq r^A$ and $x\leq T^A$, for every fixed $A>0$ and $0\leq q\leq1$. The modulus assumptions, small-$x$ range, zero-moment convention, and uniformity of the bounds require clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft makes no independent status assessment.
