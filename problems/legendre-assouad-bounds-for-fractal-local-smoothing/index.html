Math-4046

Legendre–Assouad Bounds for Fractal Local Smoothing

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d$ denote the spatial dimension and let $\Delta=\sum_{k=1}^{d}\partial_{x_k}^{2}$ be the Laplacian on ${\mathbb{R}}^d$. The half-wave operator $e^{it\sqrt{-\Delta}}$ has Fourier multiplier $e^{it|\xi|}$. For a real exponent $p>2$, put \[ s_p=(d-1)\left(\frac12-\frac1p\right). \] The record refers to a time set $E$, discrete time sets $E_j$, frequency projections $P_j$, and a function $\nu_E^{\#}$ called the Legendre--Assouad function. Their precise definitions and admissibility conditions are not supplied.

Under the intended uniform-estimate interpretation, the proposed assertion is: for every real $p>2$ and every real $s$ satisfying \[ s>\frac1p\nu_E^{\#}(p s_p), \] there exists a finite constant $C$ such that \[ \left(\sum_{t\in E_j} \|e^{it\sqrt{-\Delta}}P_jf\|_{L^p({\mathbb{R}}^d)}^p\right)^{1/p} \le C\,2^{js}\|f\|_{L^p({\mathbb{R}}^d)} \] for every admissible frequency index $j$ and every admissible function $f$, with $C$ independent of $j$ and $f$. The admissible dimensions, time sets, index range, function class, and other permitted dependencies of $C$ require confirmation from the source. In particular, neither the construction nor the spacing or covering properties of $E_j$ may be inferred from the supplied record.

The original transcription contains an incomplete candidate definition of the form \[ \nu_E^{\#}(\alpha) =\lim_{\delta\to0} \frac{\log\!\left(\sup_{\delta\le |J|\le1} |J|^{-a}N(E\cap J,\delta)\right)} {\log(1/\delta)}. \] This is reproduced only as an unresolved transcription fragment, not as an established definition. The exponent $a$ is not identified with the argument $\alpha$; the covering number $N$, the class of $J$, the allowed time sets, and the existence of the displayed limit are unspecified. Whether a limit superior is intended also requires verification. Consequently, the supplied evidence does not determine a fully specified conjecture.

The supplied source account states that the estimate holds for radial functions and fails below the proposed threshold $s<p^{-1}\nu_E^{\#}(p s_p)$, and that setting $E=[1,2]$ recovers the classical local smoothing conjecture. These are supplied claims with unresolved underlying hypotheses, not independently verified results. No assertion at the boundary $s=p^{-1}\nu_E^{\#}(p s_p)$ is supplied.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether a frequency-localized half-wave evolution, sampled at times in $E_j$, satisfies the displayed $L^p$ estimate whenever $s$ exceeds the threshold determined by the Legendre--Assouad function of $E$. Essential definitions and hypotheses are missing or corrupted in the supplied transcription. The supplied dated assessment labels the general estimate open and reports radial validity and sharpness below the threshold; these claims require source verification and human mathematical review.
