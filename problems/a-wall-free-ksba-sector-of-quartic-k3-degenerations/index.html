A wall-free KSBA sector of quartic K3 degenerations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The AIM problem asks for geometric compactifications of polarized K3 surfaces, or Calabi--Yau manifolds more generally, in the Alexeev--Kollár--Shepherd-Barron stable-pair framework. Its concrete example is a K3 surface with very ample divisor $H$ satisfying $H^2=4$: classify stable pairs appearing as degenerations, compare their singularities with Shah's GIT-semistable quartics and with Martin Olsson's log-K3 moduli. A stable pair requires semi-log-canonical singularities and an ample ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Cartier log canonical divisor. A chosen divisor marking and its coefficient are extra data beyond an abstract polarization and are not fixed by the original item.

The report describes existing KSBA compactifications in every K3 degree and an explicit degree-four Type II nef-$\lambda$-family/Tyurin sector, while saying a complete marked-hyperplane/Type III classification was not located. It gives no references, definition of the $\lambda$-family, or assessment date. These are supplied scope-limited literature claims, not a complete classification.

Its new special case concerns a reduced simple-normal-crossing factorized quartic \[ X_0=V(F_{d_1}\cdots F_{d_s})\subset{\mathbb{P}}^3,\qquad \sum_i d_i=4, \] with smooth components meeting with simple normal crossings, and a general Cartier hyperplane section $H$. The report claims that $(X_0,\epsilon H)$ is Gorenstein KSBA stable and ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Gorenstein smoothable for every rational $0<\epsilon\leq1$. The four reducible degree partitions are $1+3$, $2+2$, $1+1+2$ and $1+1+1+1$. For a quartic hypersurface $K_{X_0}\sim0$, so the asserted ample log canonical divisor is $\epsilon H$. The full interval and the generality/transversality of $H$ are essential.

In the two-component case write $X_0=S_a\cup S_b$, $a+b=4$, and $C=S_a\cap S_b$. For a general smoothing $F_aF_b+tG_4=0$, the report's node set is $Z=C\cap V(G_4)$ and its stated identities are \[ |Z|=4ab=\deg N_{C/S_a}+\deg N_{C/S_b},\qquad \mathcal O_C(Z)\cong\mathcal O_C(4), \] \[ N_{C/S_a}\otimes N_{C/S_b}\otimes\mathcal O_C(-Z)\cong\mathcal O_C. \] It claims that a single global projective blowup resolves the ordinary threefold nodes, twelve for $(a,b)=(1,3)$ and sixteen for $(2,2)$; the displayed Picard-group identity is the resulting $d$-semistability correction. The relative log canonical model is reported to contract back to the raw reducible quartic family. The exact blowup center, family hypotheses and contraction construction are not supplied.

The field/characteristic for these assertions is not stated; the usual characteristic-zero K3 interpretation requires confirmation. The SNC sector does not classify arbitrary quartic degenerations, all marked-hyperplane Type III limits, or general Calabi--Yau compactifications.

Short English statement

Seek stable-pair compactifications of polarized K3 and Calabi--Yau varieties, explicitly classifying degree-four K3 limits and comparing GIT and log approaches. The report treats only a specified SNC quartic sector, stable for every rational coefficient $0<\epsilon\leq1$, with exact node and normal-bundle identities in the two-component cases. The wider classification remains outside that result.
