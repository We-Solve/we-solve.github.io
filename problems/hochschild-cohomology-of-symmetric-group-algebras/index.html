Hochschild Cohomology of Symmetric-Group Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ be the field with $p$ elements, and let $S_n$ be the symmetric group on $n$ letters. The group algebra $A={\mathbb{F}}_pS_n$ is the ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector space with basis $S_n$, with multiplication obtained by extending the group multiplication bilinearly.

For a unital associative algebra $A$ over a field $k$, let \[ \operatorname{Der}_k(A)=\{D\in\operatorname{End}_k(A):D(ab)=D(a)b+aD(b)\text{ for all }a,b\in A\}. \] Let $\operatorname{Inn}(A)$ be the subspace of derivations of the form $a\mapsto xa-ax$, for $x\in A$. The first Hochschild cohomology is \[ HH^1(A)=\operatorname{Der}_k(A)/\operatorname{Inn}(A), \] with Lie bracket induced by $[D,E]=D\circ E-E\circ D$. For a Lie algebra $L$ over $k$, define its derived series by \[ L^{(0)}=L,\qquad L^{(j+1)}=\operatorname{span}_k\{[x,y]:x,y\in L^{(j)}\}. \] The Lie algebra is solvable if $L^{(d)}=0$ for some integer $d\geq0$.

Solvability conjecture. For every prime $p$ and every integer $n\geq1$, the Lie algebra $HH^1({\mathbb{F}}_pS_n)$ is solvable. Equivalently, \[ \forall p\text{ prime}\;\forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\;\exists d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}, \qquad \left(HH^1({\mathbb{F}}_pS_n)\right)^{(d)}=0. \] The integer $d$ may depend on both $p$ and $n$; no uniform bound is asserted.

Associated generating-function question. For an algebra $A$ over $k$, higher Hochschild cohomology is $HH^r(A)=\operatorname{Ext}_{A\otimes_k A^{\mathrm{op}}}^{r}(A,A)$, where $r\geq0$. A block algebra of a finite-dimensional algebra is a direct summand $Ae$ associated with a primitive central idempotent $e$. The source asks whether the preceding results on generating functions for Hochschild cohomology can be extended to (blocks of) alternating groups, other finite Coxeter groups, Hecke algebras, and diagram algebras. For finite groups, the algebraic setting is that of their group algebras. This is an exploratory question, rather than a specified generating-function identity.

The supplied extraction includes a damaged formula apparently involving coefficients $\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}HH^r(kS_n)$, powers $t^n$, a sum beginning at $n=0$, and an expression $t^p\phi(t^p)P(t)$. It does not define $k$, the intended range or role of $r$, $\phi$, or $P$. The preceding generating-function results are also absent. Accordingly, no equality is asserted from this fragment. The algebra families, parameters, coefficient fields, block choices, and intended identities for the extension question require clarification from the source.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether, for every prime $p$ and every positive integer $n$, repeated derived Lie brackets eventually vanish in the space of derivations of ${\mathbb{F}}_pS_n$ modulo inner derivations. The source also asks for extensions of Hochschild-cohomology generating functions to further algebra families and blocks, but the supplied record does not specify enough data to formulate those extensions as precise identities.
