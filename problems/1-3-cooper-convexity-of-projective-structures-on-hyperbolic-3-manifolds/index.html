1.3 (Cooper) — Convexity of projective structures on hyperbolic 3-manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed hyperbolic three-manifold, meaning a compact manifold without boundary admitting a complete metric of constant sectional curvature $-1$. In the standard real-projective interpretation, a projective structure $\sigma$ on $M$ is an atlas into ${\mathbb{R}} P^3$ with transition maps locally restricted from $\operatorname{PGL}_4({\mathbb{R}})$. It has a developing map and holonomy \[\operatorname{dev}_\sigma:\widetilde M\to{\mathbb{R}} P^3,\qquad \rho_\sigma:\pi_1(M)\to\operatorname{PGL}_4({\mathbb{R}}),\] with $\operatorname{dev}_\sigma(\gamma x)=\rho_\sigma(\gamma)\operatorname{dev}_\sigma(x)$.

Cooper asks whether every such structure is convex. Under the developing-map convention, this means that $\operatorname{dev}_\sigma$ identifies $\widetilde M$ with a convex domain $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}} P^3$, so that the structure is the quotient of $\Omega$ by its holonomy action. In an affine-chart version, $\Omega$ lies in an affine chart and is convex in its affine coordinates. Proper convexity additionally requires the appropriate bounded-domain/projective-closure condition; the excerpt does not specify whether "convex" is intended to include that stronger condition.

The quantifiers are \[\forall M\text{ closed hyperbolic of dimension }3,\quad \forall\sigma\text{ projective on }M,\quad\sigma\text{ is convex}.\] The structures range over all projective structures, without a restriction to the hyperbolic structure's deformation component or a preselected holonomy representation. Existence of a hyperbolic metric on $M$ does not itself define $\sigma$. The source's real-versus-other projective convention and exact convexity convention require confirmation; no extra properness or holonomy hypothesis is silently added.

Short English statement

Must every projective structure on a closed hyperbolic three-manifold develop injectively onto a convex domain? The question concerns all structures, with the source's exact convexity convention still to be fixed.
