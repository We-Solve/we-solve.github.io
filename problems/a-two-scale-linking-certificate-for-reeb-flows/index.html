A two-scale linking certificate for Reeb flows

Definitions and mathematical statement

The complete numbered Question 1.40 says only that Mitsumatsu discussed linking numbers of vector-field orbits in relation to Reeb fields and foliations. It contains no determinate conjecture, hypotheses, or requested conclusion. The aid explicitly does not reconstruct a lost question. The following is its contextual three-dimensional model, not an asserted recovery of the original intended statement.

Let $M$ be a closed oriented three-manifold. For exact two-forms define the helicity pairing \[ \mathcal B({\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha,{\,\mathrm{d}}\beta)=\int_M\alpha\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\beta. \] The integral is independent of the primitive choices. If $\alpha\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha$ is a positive contact volume, the restriction to the real line generated by ${\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha$ is positive definite. For a foliation-defining one-form $\theta$ with $\theta\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\theta=0$, the line generated by ${\,\mathrm{d}}\theta$ is null for this quadratic pairing (or is the zero space if ${\,\mathrm{d}}\theta=0$). No positive-definiteness claim is made for the whole space of exact two-forms.

The Reeb field $R_\alpha$, defined by $\alpha(R_\alpha)=1$ and $\iota_{R_\alpha}{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha=0$, has exact flux with respect to $\mu=\alpha\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha$: \[ \iota_{R_\alpha}\mu={\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha,\qquad \mathcal H(R_\alpha;\mu)=\int_M\alpha\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha. \] The volume convention is not normalized to a probability measure. If an additional adapted open book is given, positive Reeb crossing of pages gives positive oriented intersection with a fixed page. Calling this a knot linking number requires that the page and orbit satisfy the requisite homological conditions; for example on an oriented integral homology three-sphere a page bounding the binding computes its linking with a disjoint orbit. No such open book or homological setting is part of the original one-sentence record.

For its explicit family, fix $a,b>0$ and use the normalization \[ E(a,b)=\{(z_1,z_2)\in{\mathbb{C}}^2:\pi|z_1|^2/a+\pi|z_2|^2/b=1\}, \qquad \alpha=\tfrac12\sum_{j=1}^2(x_j{\,\mathrm{d}} y_j-y_j{\,\mathrm{d}} x_j)|_{E(a,b)}. \] Let $B_1=\{z_2=0\}$ and $B_2=\{z_1=0\}$, oriented by the Reeb flow. In this convention their minimal periods are $T(B_1)=a$, $T(B_2)=b$, and contact helicity is $\mathcal H=ab$. The aid reports for every other periodic Reeb orbit $\delta$, disjoint from the chosen axis, \[ \mathcal H\,\frac{\operatorname{lk}(B_i,\delta)}{T(\delta)}=T(B_i). \] For a multiply covered orbit both the period and linking are counted with its covering multiplicity; for a knot use its minimal period.

The pages around $B_1$ are given by fixed $\arg z_2$, and around $B_2$ by fixed $\arg z_1$. For any invariant probability measure supported away from the corresponding axis, define $\rho_i$ as the mean positive page rate in turns per unit Reeb time. The aid's measure version is \[ \mathcal H\rho_i=T(B_i),\qquad \rho_1=1/b,\quad\rho_2=1/a. \] Thus the identities compare analytic helicity, periodic linking, and mean page crossing for this family. They neither supply a general linking theorem for every Reeb flow nor answer an unspecified foliation question.
