Clique-Sequence Rank and Generic Rigidity Matroids

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K_n$ denote the complete simple graph on $n$ vertices, with edge set $E=E(K_n)$. A copy of $K_t$ in $K_n$ is identified with its edge set. The supplied record does not specify the admissible range of the integer parameters $n,t$, define a proper $K_t$-sequence, or state whether the empty sequence is permitted. These omissions prevent an unconditional definition of the proposed rank function.

Write $\mathcal P_{n,t}$ for the intended family of finite proper $K_t$-sequences, pending clarification of that term. Conditional on this family being specified and the following minima existing, define, for every $F\subseteq E$, \[ r_t(F)=\min_{(C_1,\ldots,C_k)\in\mathcal P_{n,t}} \left(\left|F\cup\bigcup_{i=1}^{k}C_i\right|-k\right). \] If the empty sequence is permitted, its union is $\varnothing$ and its contribution is $|F|$; its admissibility is not established by the supplied record.

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every intended pair $n,t$, this function is submodular: \[ r_t(A)+r_t(B)\ge r_t(A\cup B)+r_t(A\cap B) \qquad\text{for all }A,B\subseteq E, \] and is the rank function of a matroid on $E$. Here a matroid rank function on a finite set $E$ is an integer-valued function $r:2^E\to{\mathbb{Z}}$ satisfying \[ 0\le r(A)\le |A|,\qquad A\subseteq B\Longrightarrow r(A)\le r(B), \] together with the displayed submodular inequality. Submodularity alone does not supply the other rank axioms; their applicability here depends on the missing definition and conventions.

The highlighted conjecture is that, when $t=5$, the resulting matroid equals the generic $3$-rigidity matroid on the same labelled edge set. Under the conventional real interpretation, choose points $p_1,\ldots,p_n\in{\mathbb{R}}^3$ whose $3n$ coordinates are algebraically independent over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. For $F\subseteq E$, let $R_3(F,p)$ be the matrix with one row for each edge $\{i,j\}\in F$, with blocks $p_i-p_j$ and $p_j-p_i$ in the three columns for vertices $i$ and $j$, respectively, and zeros elsewhere. The generic $3$-rigidity matroid is the matroid of linear independence of these rows. With this conventional interpretation, the highlighted assertion is \[ r_5(F)=\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{R}}}R_3(F,p) \qquad\text{for every }F\subseteq E(K_n). \] The record does not explicitly specify a ground field; the real interpretation above explains the standard terminology and requires confirmation against the intended source. No missing proper-sequence condition is supplied by this draft.

Short English statement

The conjecture proposes a matroid rank obtained by minimizing the size of an edge union minus the length of a proper clique sequence. For $t=5$, it further identifies this rank with generic rigidity rank in three dimensions. The supplied record leaves "proper sequence," the parameter range, and the empty-sequence convention undefined, so the formulation remains conditional and requires human mathematical review.
