Computing Hecke Operators for Arithmetic-Group Cohomology

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

Arithmetic-group cohomology is the cohomology of the specified arithmetic group or associated quotient. A Hecke operator is a correspondence-induced linear action on it; eigenvalues are its scalar actions on eigenvectors.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Can Hecke operators and their eigenvalues be computed for all arithmetic groups for which the cohomology can be calculated? [S2]
Scope qualification.
The group family, cohomology coefficients, degree, and input representation must be read from the source.
