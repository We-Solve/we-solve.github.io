Waldschmidt Constants of Plane Curve Singular Sets

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify a ground field or its characteristic. The following notation gives a conditional formulation over a fixed algebraically closed field $k$; it does not assert that every characteristic is intended.

Let $C\subset{\mathbb{P}}^2_k$ be a reduced plane curve, and let $Z=\operatorname{Sing}(C)_{\mathrm{red}}$ be its finite set of singular points, endowed with the reduced structure. For $Z\ne\varnothing$, put $S=k[x_0,x_1,x_2]$ and let \[ I_Z=\bigcap_{p\in Z}\mathfrak p_p\subset S, \qquad I_Z^{(m)}=\bigcap_{p\in Z}\mathfrak p_p^{\,m} \quad(m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}), \] where $\mathfrak p_p$ is the homogeneous ideal of the point $p$. Thus $I_Z^{(m)}$ consists of homogeneous forms vanishing to order at least $m$ at every point of $Z$. For a nonzero homogeneous ideal $J\subset S$, define its initial degree by \[ \alpha(J)=\min\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}:J_d\ne0\}. \] The Waldschmidt constant is \[ \widehat\alpha(I_Z) =\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{\alpha(I_Z^{(m)})}{m} =\inf_{m\ge1}\frac{\alpha(I_Z^{(m)})}{m}. \]

The source asks whether, over its intended ground field, every reduced plane curve with nonempty singular set satisfies \[ \widehat\alpha\left(I_{\operatorname{Sing}(C)_{\mathrm{red}}}\right)\in{\mathbb{Q}}, \] and whether this constant can be computed. It specifically asks for its computation when $C$ is the union of the $21$ lines in Klein's arrangement, with $Z$ the full singular set of that union.

The ground field, the meaning of computability, and a defining realization of the named arrangement are absent from the supplied record. Consequently, the computational request is retained as a request to determine the constant, without imposing an unspecified algorithmic model. The source also does not address smooth curves: if one adopts $I_{\varnothing}=S$, the same degree convention gives $\widehat\alpha(I_{\varnothing})=0$. This is an explicit convention for that boundary case, not an additional source hypothesis.

Short English statement

For the reduced set of singular points of a reduced plane curve, ask whether the asymptotic minimum degree per unit of imposed vanishing is always rational and whether it can be computed, particularly for Klein's arrangement of $21$ lines. The intended ground field and the precise computational requirement remain unspecified.
