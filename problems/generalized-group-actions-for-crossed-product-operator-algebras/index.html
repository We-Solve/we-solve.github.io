Generalized Group Actions for Crossed-Product Operator Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a $C^*$-algebra, and let $\operatorname{Aut}(A)$ denote its group of $*$-automorphisms. At the algebraic level, an action of a group $G$ on $A$ is a homomorphism $\alpha\colon G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)$. When a topology on $G$ is involved, additional continuity hypotheses must be specified; the supplied question does not specify them. A crossed product associated with an ordinary action is customarily denoted by $A\rtimes_\alpha G$, with the particular crossed-product construction requiring further conventions.

Source question. How can group actions be generalized so that a crossed-product $C^*$-algebra can still be defined?

The requested objective is to identify a notion of generalized action and an associated crossed-product construction extending an ordinary group-action construction. The record does not specify the admissible groups, generalized acting objects, coefficient algebras, continuity conditions, full or reduced crossed-product convention, or a universal property characterizing the extension. Consequently, it does not determine a single quantified mathematical assertion.
