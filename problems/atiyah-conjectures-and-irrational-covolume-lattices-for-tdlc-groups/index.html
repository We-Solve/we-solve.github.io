Atiyah Conjectures and Irrational-Covolume Lattices for TDLC Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A totally disconnected locally compact group (TDLC group) is a Hausdorff locally compact topological group whose connected components are singletons. Such a group is compactly generated if some compact subset generates it as a group. A lattice in a locally compact group $G$ is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma\leq G$ for which $G/\Gamma$ carries a nonzero finite $G$-invariant measure. Given a Haar measure $\mu$ on $G$, the covolume $\operatorname{covol}_{\mu}(\Gamma)$ is the total mass of the compatible quotient measure, using counting measure on $\Gamma$.

The supplied record asks the following two questions.

  1. Does a suitable version of the Atiyah conjecture hold for TDLC groups? The cleaned statement additionally asks whether TDLC groups can have irrational $L^2$-Betti numbers. Writing $b_n^{(2)}(G,\mu)$ for the intended $L^2$-Betti number in degree $n$, whenever it is defined, the irrationality question concerns the existence of an admissible group, measure, and degree with \[ b_n^{(2)}(G,\mu)\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}. \] Here the admissible class, the construction of these invariants, the Haar normalization, and the precise Atiyah assertion are not specified. This display therefore describes the requested phenomenon, rather than a fully determined conjecture. An Atiyah assertion might impose restrictions stronger than rationality, so the two phrasings cannot be treated as a specified logical dichotomy.
  2. Does there exist a compactly generated TDLC group $G$ with a lattice $\Gamma$ of irrational covolume? This question requires a Haar normalization: replacing $\mu$ by $c\mu$, for $c>0$, gives \[ \operatorname{covol}_{c\mu}(\Gamma) =c\,\operatorname{covol}_{\mu}(\Gamma). \] Consequently, unrestricted rescaling can change the rationality of any positive finite covolume. One possible convention is to specify a compact open subgroup $K\leq G$ and normalize Haar measure by $\mu_K(K)=1$. Under that convention, the question would be whether \[ \exists\,G,K,\Gamma:\quad \begin{gathered} G\text{ is a compactly generated TDLC group},\\ K\leq G\text{ is compact and open},\qquad \Gamma\leq G\text{ is a lattice},\\ \operatorname{covol}_{\mu_K}(\Gamma)\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}. \end{gathered} \] This is an explicitly conditional formulation: the supplied record does not state that normalization or otherwise resolve the scale dependence.
The first question does not explicitly assume compact generation; that hypothesis is supplied only for the lattice question. No second countability, unimodularity, finiteness condition for the intended $L^2$-invariants, or restriction to uniform lattices is explicitly stated. Any hypotheses required by the intended theory must be recovered from the source before a definitive formulation is accepted.

The original extract continues into a section headed Yuri Santos Rego, Questions 8 and 9, about virtually RFRS Coxeter groups and profinite completions. Those appear to be adjacent material rather than additional clauses of the two TDLC questions; their attachment and attribution remain unverified.

Short English statement

The record asks for an Atiyah-type statement for totally disconnected locally compact groups, including a question about irrational $L^2$-Betti numbers, and asks whether a compactly generated such group can have a lattice of irrational covolume. A precise formulation needs the intended $L^2$-theory, hypotheses, and Haar normalization. The supplied open status and verification claims are curation labels requiring human review.
