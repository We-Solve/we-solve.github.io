Math-126

Analytic Continuation and Functional Equations of Automorphic L-Functions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ range over number fields, let $\mathbb A_k$ denote its ring of adeles, and let $G$ be a split reductive algebraic group over $k$. Let $\widehat G$ denote the complex dual group of $G$. Let $\sigma$ be a cuspidal automorphic representation of $G(\mathbb A_k)$, and let $\rho\colon\widehat G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ be a finite-dimensional dual-group representation. Write $\widetilde\sigma$ for the contragredient representation of $\sigma$.

At an unramified finite place $v$, let $q_v$ be the residue-field cardinality and let $c_v(\sigma)$ denote the Satake conjugacy class, once a normalization has been fixed. The corresponding unramified local factor is \[ L_v(s,\sigma,\rho)=\det\!\left(I_V-q_v^{-s}\rho(c_v(\sigma))\right)^{-1}. \] For a finite set $S$ containing the archimedean places and all ramified places, these factors specify the partial Euler product \[ L^S(s,\sigma,\rho)=\prod_{v\notin S}L_v(s,\sigma,\rho) \] where it converges. The supplied record does not specify the normalization of these factors, the factors at the omitted places, or the epsilon factor.

Requested assertion, subject to the unresolved conventions below. For every tuple $(k,G,\sigma,\rho)$ as above, the associated automorphic $L$-function $L(s,\sigma,\rho)$, initially defined in its domain of convergence, admits meromorphic continuation to all $s\in{\mathbb{C}}$ and satisfies the identity of meromorphic functions \[ L(s,\sigma,\rho)=\epsilon(s,\sigma,\rho)\,L(1-s,\widetilde\sigma,\rho). \] The same representation $\rho$ on both sides is retained exactly as supplied.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not say whether $L$ is completed or has omitted local factors; the displayed functional equation therefore requires a compatible specification of all local factors and normalizations. Its epsilon factor must be specified independently as part of the intended automorphic construction, rather than defined as the quotient of the two displayed $L$-functions. The record also does not explicitly specify connectedness in the term "split reductive group," the representation category intended for $\rho$, or any conventions concerning central characters or unitarity of $\sigma$. These omissions prevent a fully convention-independent formulation; no additional hypotheses are imposed here.

Short English statement

For every number field, split reductive group, cuspidal automorphic representation, and finite-dimensional dual-group representation in the supplied scope, the conjecture asks for meromorphic continuation of the associated automorphic $L$-function to ${\mathbb{C}}$ and the stated functional equation. Local factors, completion, and normalization conventions require clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
