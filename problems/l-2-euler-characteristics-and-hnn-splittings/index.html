Math-7167

$L^2$ Euler Characteristics and HNN Splittings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be a free group of finite rank $n$, let $\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$, and let \[ G=F_n\rtimes_\alpha{\mathbb{Z}}. \] Thus, choosing a generator $s$ of the displayed cyclic factor, one may write \[ G=\langle F_n,s\mid sxs^{-1}=\alpha(x)\text{ for every }x\in F_n\rangle. \] A character here means a group homomorphism $\phi:G\to{\mathbb{Z}}$. The supplied statement does not require $\phi$ to be nonzero or surjective.

An HNN splitting $G=A*_B$ means an isomorphism with a presentation \[ \left\langle A,t\ \middle|\ t^{-1}\iota_0(b)t=\iota_1(b)\text{ for every }b\in B\right\rangle, \] where $\iota_0,\iota_1:B\hookrightarrow A$ are injective homomorphisms. Its standard associated character $\psi:G\to{\mathbb{Z}}$ satisfies $\psi(a)=0$ for every $a\in A$ and $\psi(t)=1$. In particular, $\psi$ is surjective. The source uses the phrase "inducing $\phi$" without specifying whether this means $\phi=\psi$, equality up to sign, or a different convention involving an integral multiple of $\psi$. This distinction is unresolved for the supplied domain of all characters.

For a group $H$, write \[ b_k^{(2)}(H)=\dim_{\mathcal N(H)}H_k^{(2)}(H), \qquad \chi^{(2)}(H)=\sum_{k\geq 0}(-1)^k b_k^{(2)}(H), \] where $\mathcal N(H)$ is its group von Neumann algebra and $H_k^{(2)}(H)$ denotes its reduced $L^2$-homology, whenever the alternating sum is defined under the chosen finiteness or convergence convention. Likewise, when the rational Betti numbers are finite and vanish in sufficiently high degrees, the ordinary group Euler characteristic is \[ \chi(H)=\sum_{k\geq 0}(-1)^k\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H_k(H;{\mathbb{Q}}). \] The supplied record specifies no convention for Euler characteristics beyond these finiteness conditions, and supplies no explicit hypotheses ensuring that every quantity in the proposed equality is defined.

Conjectural equality as supplied. For every free-by-cyclic group $G=F_n\rtimes_\alpha{\mathbb{Z}}$ and every character $\phi:G\to{\mathbb{Z}}$, is \[ -\chi^{(2)}(\ker\phi) = \min\left\{ -\chi(A)\ \middle|\ \begin{gathered} G=A*_B\text{ is an HNN splitting inducing }\phi,\\ A\text{ and }B\text{ are finitely generated} \end{gathered} \right\}? \] This is the extracted conjectural formula, with its unresolved qualifications retained. A precise assertion for the entire stated domain requires a convention for "inducing," treatment of zero and non-surjective characters, conditions or conventions making both Euler characteristics meaningful, and a rule for an empty admissible set or a set whose minimum is not attained. Under the standard associated-character convention above, only surjective characters can be induced; no restriction to such characters is silently imposed here.

Short English statement

The proposed equality compares the negative $L^2$-Euler characteristic of the kernel of an integer-valued character of a free-by-cyclic group with the smallest negative Euler characteristic of a finitely generated HNN base inducing that character. The meaning of "inducing," the allowed characters, and the existence of the Euler characteristics and minimum require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
