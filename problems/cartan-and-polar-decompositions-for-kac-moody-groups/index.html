Cartan and Polar Decompositions for Kac–Moody Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a Kac--Moody group and let $K$ denote its unitary form, as designated in the source. In the Laurent-polynomial example, the source specifies a Chevalley involution on \[ G=\operatorname{SL}_n(F[t,t^{-1}]), \qquad F[t,t^{-1}]=\left\{\sum_{j=r}^{s}a_jt^j:r,s\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ r\leq s,\ a_j\in F\right\}. \] Here $F$ denotes the coefficient field and $t$ is an indeterminate. The permitted fields, matrix sizes, precise involution, and construction of the associated unitary form are not specified in the supplied record.

For subsets $X,Y,Z\subseteq G$, write \[ XYZ=\{xyz:x\in X,\ y\in Y,\ z\in Z\}. \] Thus, for a specified Cartan factor $A\subseteq G$, \[ G=KAK \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \forall g\in G\ \exists k_1,k_2\in K\ \exists a\in A: \ g=k_1ak_2. \] The source does not define $A$; it must be prescribed by the intended Cartan-decomposition convention, rather than chosen arbitrarily. Let $B$ be a Borel subgroup and let $U$ be its unipotent radical, in the intended Kac--Moody group construction. Then \[ G=KUK \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \forall g\in G\ \exists k_1,k_2\in K\ \exists u\in U: \ g=k_1uk_2. \] Write $W$ for the Weyl group of the specified Kac--Moody root datum. Spherical means that $W$ is finite; nonspherical means that $W$ is infinite.

The supplied record asks the following related questions:

  1. Under what hypotheses on $G$, its coefficient field, and its unitary form do Cartan or polar decompositions exist?
  2. With $A$ prescribed by the intended Cartan-decomposition convention, does \[ G=KAK\ \Longrightarrow\ |W|<\infty \] hold throughout the intended class of Kac--Moody groups?
  3. For $G=\operatorname{SL}_n(F[t,t^{-1}])$, equipped with the Chevalley involution intended by the source, does a polar decomposition exist?
  4. In the nonspherical case, under what hypotheses does $G=KUK$ hold, particularly for $G=\operatorname{SL}_n(F[t,t^{-1}])$?
The supplied record does not define a polar decomposition or its factors. Consequently, the polar-decomposition questions and the implication involving $A$ remain dependent on missing conventions. No unique fully specified assertion for those parts can be extracted without additional source hypotheses. The choice of Kac--Moody group construction and Borel subgroup also remains to be fixed.

Short English statement

The problem asks when Kac--Moody groups admit Cartan or polar decompositions, whether a Cartan factorization forces finite Weyl group, and when every element is a product of a unitary element, a unipotent element, and another unitary element. Laurent-polynomial special linear groups are the principal example. Several necessary conventions and hypotheses are absent, so this is a qualified statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
