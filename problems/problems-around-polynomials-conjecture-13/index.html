Problems Around Polynomials — Conjecture 13

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p\in{\mathbb{R}}[x]$ be a nonzero polynomial of degree $k$. The source associates polynomials $P_i(x)$ to $p$ and conjectures \[ \#_r P_i\ \le\ \min\{\deg P_i,k\} \] for every index $i$ in the intended family. Its supplied summary calls these Jensen polynomials, but the record contains no formula defining $P_i$, no index range, and no normalization. The symbol cannot be identified with some other standard construction bearing the name Jensen polynomial without further source information.

The real-zero counting convention is also absent. For a nonzero polynomial $Q$, two possible meanings are \[ \#_r^{\mathrm{dist}}Q=\#\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}:Q(x)=0\},\qquad \#_r^{\mathrm{mult}}Q=\sum_{\substack{x\in{\mathbb{R}}\\Q(x)=0}}\operatorname{ord}_x(Q). \] The displayed conjecture retains the source symbol $\#_r$ pending a choice; it does not silently replace a multiplicity count by a distinct-root count. Both the degree and ordinary root counts require separate conventions for an identically zero $P_i$, and the source does not say which indices avoid this case. The admissible degrees $k$, including constant $p$, also need confirmation.

The supplied research summary labels the full $P_i$ assertion open. It reports that the $i=1$ inequality $\#_rP_1\le\#_{nr}p$, together with the stated bounds $\deg P_1\le2k-2$ and $\#_{nr}p\le k$, is a proved Hawaii-conjecture base case attributed to Tyaglov (2011). Here $\#_{nr}p$ denotes the intended number of nonreal zeros, again with an unspecified multiplicity convention. The exact definitions and hypotheses must be checked before this can be used as a theorem about the missing $P_1$.

The summary separately mentions a conjectural adjoining family \[ \#_rG_i\le\min\{\deg G_i,\#_{nr}p\}, \] called Corollary 1 in the source, and reports a 2024 counterexample to its $i=1$ member by Katkova--Tyaglov--Vishnyakova. The $G_i$ are also undefined here. That reported counterexample is not asserted to refute the distinct $P_i$ conjecture. All literature-status statements are supplied assessments, not independent verification.

Short English statement

For the source's family $P_i$ associated to a degree-$k$ real polynomial, bound the number of real zeros by both its degree and $k$. The definitions of that family, its indices, and root multiplicities are missing, so the assertion remains conditional. A reported counterexample for a neighboring $G_i$ family must be kept separate from this conjecture.
