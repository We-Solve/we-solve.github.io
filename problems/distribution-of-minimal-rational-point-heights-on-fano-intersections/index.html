Distribution of Minimal Rational-Point Heights on Fano Intersections

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns smooth Fano complete intersections $V/{\mathbb{Q}}$ in a family denoted by $\mathcal V_{d,n}$. Here Fano means that the anticanonical line bundle of the smooth projective variety is ample. The record does not specify the meanings or admissible values of $d,n$, the ambient projective space, the multidegree, or the parametrization and counting convention for $\mathcal V_{d,n}$.

Write $H(V)$ for the parameter height mentioned in the source. If $h_V$ denotes the intended height on $V({\mathbb{Q}})$, the intended least rational-point height is \[ M(V)=\min\{h_V(P):P\in V({\mathbb{Q}})\}, \] whenever this set is nonempty and its minimum exists. Neither $H$ nor $h_V$, including their normalizations, is defined in the supplied record. No value of $M(V)$ is specified when $V({\mathbb{Q}})=\varnothing$; in particular, the convention $M(V)=+\infty$ cannot be attributed to the source without further evidence.

Proposed statement, conditional on resolving these missing specifications. For fixed admissible family parameters $(d,n)$, put \[ N(A)=\#\{V\in\mathcal V_{d,n}:H(V)\leq A\}, \qquad N_t(A)=\#\{V\in\mathcal V_{d,n}:H(V)\leq A,\ M(V)\leq tH(V)\}. \] Provided these counts are well defined, finite, and $N(A)>0$ for all sufficiently large $A$, the supplied question asks whether \[ L(t):=\limsup_{A\to\infty}\frac{N_t(A)}{N(A)}=O(t) \qquad(t\downarrow0,\ 0<t<1). \] Explicitly, this asks whether there exist $C>0$ and $t_0\in(0,1)$ such that \[ \forall t\in(0,t_0),\qquad L(t)\leq Ct. \] The limit superior in $A$ is taken first, for each fixed $t$. This formulation makes $C$ independent of $t$; any intended uniformity in $d,n$, or in height choices, is unspecified. The record also leaves the scope of the quantification over admissible families unresolved. Thus this is an incomplete proposed statement, not a fully specified conjecture or a claim of mathematical verification.

Short English statement

The proposed question asks whether, among the specified Fano complete intersections of bounded parameter height, the limiting upper proportion whose least rational-point height is at most $t$ times their parameter height is bounded by a constant times $t$ as $t\downarrow0$. The family, heights, counting convention, and treatment of varieties without rational points require specification before this becomes a precise mathematical assertion.
