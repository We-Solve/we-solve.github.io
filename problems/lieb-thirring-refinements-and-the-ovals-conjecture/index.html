Lieb–Thirring Refinements and the Ovals Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record bundles several questions and does not specify enough hypotheses to make all of them fully quantified conjectures.
  1. Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $V\geq 0$ be a potential on ${\mathbb{R}}^n$. Write formally \[ H_V=-\Delta-V\quad\text{on }L^2({\mathbb{R}}^n). \] Whenever a self-adjoint realization and a nonempty discrete negative spectrum are justified, denote the negative eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, by $E_j$, with $E_1$ the lowest eigenvalue. The supplied ordering is $E_1<E_2\leq\cdots\leq0$; only strictly negative eigenvalues enter the sum. For the exponent $\gamma$, define \[ S_\gamma(V)=\sum_{E_j<0}|E_j|^\gamma. \] The question is whether suitable integrability assumptions on a power of $V$, strong enough to ensure $S_\gamma(V)<\infty$, yield \[ S_\gamma(V)\leq C\,|E_1|^\gamma, \] where $C$ depends only on $n$, $\gamma$, and the specified integrability data, uniformly over potentials satisfying those data. The supplied record does not specify the admissible range of $\gamma$, the power of $V$, its norm or bound, or the operator hypotheses. Thus the integrability data and the class over which uniformity is requested remain undefined; finiteness of the sum alone does not supply those missing hypotheses.

  2. In dimension $n=1$ and for $\gamma=1$, the record asks for a direct proof of the Eden--Foias result, or a sharpening by correction terms. It also asks for a direct estimate of the eigenvalue sum without the Birman--Schwinger transformation. No formula for the Eden--Foias inequality, its hypotheses, the proposed correction terms, or the intended direct estimate is supplied. These are therefore retained as research requests whose precise mathematical targets require source clarification.

  3. For the geometric question, let $\mathcal C$ denote the intended class of smooth closed curves of length $2\pi$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$. Under the usual regular-curve, arclength interpretation, an admissible curve has a smooth parametrization \[ r:{\mathbb{R}}/(2\pi{\mathbb{Z}})\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^3, \qquad |r'(s)|=1, \] and curvature $\kappa(s)=|r''(s)|$. With periodic boundary conditions, define \[ L_r=-\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}^2}{{\,\mathrm{d}} s^2}+\kappa(s)^2, \qquad \lambda_1(r)=\inf_{\substack{u\in H^1_{\mathrm{per}}(0,2\pi)\\u\ne0}} \frac{\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\left(|u'(s)|^2+\kappa(s)^2|u(s)|^2\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s} {\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}|u(s)|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s}. \] The supplied Ovals question asks whether the circle minimizes $\lambda_1$ in $\mathcal C$. Since the length-$2\pi$ circle has curvature $1$, this is the proposed assertion \[ \lambda_1(r)\geq1\qquad\text{for every }r\in\mathcal C. \] No uniqueness of a minimizer is asserted. The record does not specify whether $\mathcal C$ requires embeddedness or permits self-intersections or multiple coverings; that choice remains unresolved. Arclength parametrization and periodic boundary conditions make explicit the usual interpretation of the closed-curve operator and require confirmation against the source.

The registry labels this bundle partially_solved and its statement reconstructed_unverified. Its supplied verification note expressly withholds full source verification because the integrability exponent and norm are unspecified. These labels and the accompanying literature assessment are supplied curation, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

This record asks for a uniform comparison of a Schrödinger eigenvalue moment with the ground-state energy, direct or improved one-dimensional Eden--Foias estimates, and circle minimization for a curvature-dependent periodic operator. The spectral hypotheses and some geometric conventions remain unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical and source review.
