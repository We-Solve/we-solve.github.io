Math-958

Mordell–Lang for Semiabelian Varieties in Positive Characteristic

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $L$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $p>0$, and identify $\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_p$ with the subfield of elements of $L$ algebraic over ${\mathbb{F}}_p$. Let $G/L$ be semiabelian: a connected commutative algebraic group fitting into an exact sequence \[ 0\longrightarrow T\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow0, \] where $T$ is a torus and $A$ an abelian variety. Let $X\subseteq G$ be an irreducible subvariety and $\Lambda\leq G(L)$ a finite-rank subgroup. Under the ordinary abelian-group rank convention this means $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}}(\Lambda\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}{\mathbb{Q}})<\infty$, not finite generation; the source does not give a different rank convention or restrict denominators or torsion.

Assume $X(L)\cap\Lambda$ is Zariski dense in $X$. The full positive-characteristic Mordell--Lang question in the record asks whether there exist \[ g\in G(L),\quad G'\leq G\text{ an algebraic subgroup},\quad G_0/\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_p\text{ semiabelian},\quad X_0\subseteq G_0\text{ defined over }\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_p, \] and a surjective algebraic group homomorphism over $L$, \[ h:G'\longrightarrow (G_0)_L, \] such that \[ X=g+h^{-1}((X_0)_L). \] Here the subscript $L$ denotes base change, making the inverse image and translation in $G$ precise. This is the source's definition of special. The translation $g$, subgroup $G'$ and map $h$ are not required to descend to the constant field; the specified constant objects are $G_0,X_0$.

The formula is retained as equality with the full inverse image, not weakened to containment or to a selected irreducible component. For a variety interpretation in characteristic $p$, it is naturally an equality of reduced subvarieties/underlying closed sets; the source does not specify a nonreduced scheme-theoretic convention. No connectedness of $G'$, separability of $h$, or properness of $G'$ is appended. The conclusion is not replaced by an ordinary subgroup coset, which would discard its constant-variety component.

The hypotheses range over the full stated finite-rank class, rather than only finitely generated groups or prime-to-$p$ divisions. The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports no authoritative complete resolution of this extracted claim located in its search. Its open label and reported official-source verification are retained as dated metadata, not independently checked current status.

Short English statement

Does a Zariski-dense intersection with a finite-rank subgroup force the stated constant-variety pullback description of $X$? The characteristic-$p$ constant data and full inverse-image equality are preserved. Finite rank is not silently restricted to finite generation, and the map and translation need not be constant-defined.
