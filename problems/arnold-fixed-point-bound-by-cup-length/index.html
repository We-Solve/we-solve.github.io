Arnold Fixed Point Bound by Cup Length

OpenAI's claim

Degenerate Arnold fixed-point obstruction: A Hamiltonian map of the complex quadric threefold is claimed to have exactly three fixed points although its critical number and rational cup-length lower bound are four. At least one fixed point is degenerate.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For the complex quadric $Q^3=\{[z]\in\mathbb{CP}^4:\sum_{i=0}^4z_i^2=0\}$ with restricted Fubini--Study symplectic form, the claim constructs a smooth Hamiltonian diffeomorphism $\phi$ with \[\#\operatorname{Fix}(\phi)=3<4=\operatorname{Crit}(Q^3)=\operatorname{cuplength}(Q^3;\mathbb Q).\] At least one fixed point is degenerate. Here $\operatorname{Crit}$ minimizes over all smooth functions, including degenerate ones; the cup-length convention is the longest nonvanishing product of positive-degree classes, with one added to that count.

Outline of the argument

Degenerate Arnold fixed-point obstruction: A finite-order Hamiltonian map is perturbed by the flow of an invariant extension of a function on its fixed locus. A power identity excludes new fixed points elsewhere, and symmetric criticality identifies the remaining fixed points with critical points on that locus. A suitably chosen function on the quadric surface then supplies the three-point count.
