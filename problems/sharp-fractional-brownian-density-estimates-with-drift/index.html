Sharp Fractional Brownian Density Estimates with Drift

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation only, let $n,d$ be positive integers and let $B^H=(B^{H,1},\ldots,B^{H,d})$ denote a fractional Brownian motion with Hurst parameter $H\in(0,1)$. Each standard component is a centered Gaussian process with covariance \[ {\mathbb{E}}[B^{H,i}_tB^{H,i}_s]=\frac12\left(t^{2H}+s^{2H}-|t-s|^{2H}\right),\qquad s,t\geq0. \] The source does not specify the admissible Hurst parameters or the covariance between different components. A schematic equation expressing the requested extension is \[ {\,\mathrm{d}} X_t=b(X_t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t+\sum_{i=1}^{d}V_i(X_t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mathbf B^{H,i}_t,\qquad X_0=x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n, \] where $b$ denotes a drift vector field, $V_1,\ldots,V_d$ denote noise vector fields, and $\mathbf B^H$ denotes a suitable rough-path enhancement of the driver. This display is explanatory notation, not a specification of hypotheses supplied by the source. Its interpretation requires a choice of enhancement, coefficient assumptions, and a time interval on which the solution exists; none is specified in the record. The source also does not specify whether the intended drift may depend on time.

When the law of $X_t$ has a density with respect to Lebesgue measure, write \[ \mathbb P(X_t\in A)=\int_A p_t(x,y)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} y \] for Borel sets $A\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$. Lebesgue density is the interpretation used here to explain the question; the source does not explicitly identify the state space or reference measure.

The question asks whether sharp density estimates for fractional-Brownian rough differential equations extend to equations with drift, while accounting for the stated loss of fractional-Brownian scaling and the necessary sub-Riemannian accessibility conditions.
