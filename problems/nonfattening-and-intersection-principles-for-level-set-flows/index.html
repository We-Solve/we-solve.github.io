Nonfattening and Intersection Principles for Level-Set Flows

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M_t)_{t\geq0}$ denote the level-set mean-curvature flow in the supplied record. The ambient space, admissible initial data, and construction of the associated inner and outer flows are not specified. Consequently, the following records the proposed equivalence with its unresolved qualifications; it is not a fully specified conjecture.

In the hypersurface interpretation, let $X$ denote the ambient space and let $n$ denote the hypersurface dimension. These roles for $X$ and $n$ require confirmation from the source. Nonfattening means that the evolving hypersurface level set has empty ambient interior: \[ \operatorname{Int}_X(M_t)=\varnothing\qquad\text{for every }t\geq0. \] This definition treats $M_t$ as the hypersurface level set, rather than an evolving enclosed region. Write $M_t^{\mathrm{in}}$ and $M_t^{\mathrm{out}}$ for the associated inner and outer hypersurface flows, once their construction and initial-region conventions have been fixed. Nondiscrepancy is the condition \[ M_t^{\mathrm{in}}=M_t=M_t^{\mathrm{out}} \qquad\text{for every }t\geq0. \] The supplied record does not provide enough information to define these two associated flows independently.

The stated intersection principle quantifies over every smooth closed mean-curvature flow $(N_t)_{t\geq0}$ in the same ambient space satisfying \[ N_t\not\subseteq M_t\qquad\text{for every }t\geq0. \] Here a smooth closed flow is understood to consist of compact hypersurfaces without boundary evolving with normal velocity equal to their mean-curvature vector. The displayed noncontainment is an explicitly provisional reading of the corrupted source expression $N_t\,6\subseteq M_t$. For each such comparison flow, the principle requires \[ \dim(M_t\cap N_t)\leq\dim(M_s\cap N_s) \qquad(0\leq s\leq t), \] and also \[ \forall s\geq0,\qquad \dim(M_s\cap N_s)<n-1 \ \Longrightarrow\ \left(\forall t>s,\ M_t\cap N_t=\varnothing\right). \] The meaning of $\dim$, its value on the empty set, and the meaning of $n$ are not specified in the record. These dimension statements therefore require those conventions before they become precise. The all-time comparison-flow quantifier is retained as supplied; replacing it by a common smooth existence interval would require source confirmation.

The problem is to determine under which hypotheses, and in which directions, the following equivalences hold: \[ \text{nonfattening} \ \stackrel{?}{\Longleftrightarrow}\ \text{nondiscrepancy} \ \stackrel{?}{\Longleftrightarrow}\ \text{the stated intersection principle}. \] The original excerpt attributes a special-case result to Hershkovits--White for flows with "mean convex neighborhoods of", but the sentence ends at that point. The missing hypothesis and the exact scope of that attribution cannot be recovered from the supplied text.

Short English statement

Determine when nonfattening, agreement of the inner and outer flows, and the stated intersection principle characterize the same level-set mean-curvature flows. The supplied record labels the general question open, but missing geometric hypotheses, dimension conventions, and a truncated special-case attribution prevent a definitive formulation without source review.
