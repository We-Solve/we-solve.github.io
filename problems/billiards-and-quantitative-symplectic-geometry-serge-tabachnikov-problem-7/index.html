Billiards and Quantitative Symplectic Geometry — Serge Tabachnikov — Problem 7

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Sigma$ be a strictly convex closed hypersurface for which the intended multidimensional outer symplectic billiard construction is defined. Write $(P_\Sigma,\omega_\Sigma)$ for its symplectic phase space and $T_\Sigma:D_\Sigma\to P_\Sigma$, with $D_\Sigma\subseteq P_\Sigma$, for its billiard map. These are placeholders for the intended construction: the supplied record does not define the ambient space, the required regularity of $\Sigma$, the phase space, or the map.

If $\dim P_\Sigma=2m$, let $\mu_\Sigma$ denote the measure associated with the symplectic volume density $\lvert\omega_\Sigma^{\wedge m}\rvert/m!$. Define \[ D_\Sigma^{(3)}=\{x\in D_\Sigma:T_\Sigma x\in D_\Sigma,\ T_\Sigma^2x\in D_\Sigma\}, \] \[ F_3(\Sigma)=\{x\in D_\Sigma^{(3)}:T_\Sigma^3x=x\},\qquad E_3(\Sigma)=\{x\in F_3(\Sigma):T_\Sigma x\ne x\}. \] Thus $F_3(\Sigma)$ consists of phase points whose period divides $3$, and $E_3(\Sigma)$ consists of phase points of least period $3$.

The requested conjectural assertion is that, for every strictly convex closed hypersurface in the intended multidimensional setting, the set of $3$-periodic phase points has $\mu_\Sigma$-measure zero. Under the convention that $3$-periodic means $T_\Sigma^3x=x$, this is \[ \mu_\Sigma\left(F_3(\Sigma)\right)=0. \] Under the least-period convention, it is instead \[ \mu_\Sigma\left(E_3(\Sigma)\right)=0. \] The supplied record does not specify this convention or establish that fixed points are absent. It also does not specify the ambient dimension range meant by "multidimensional." Consequently, the displayed assertions express the target conditionally on the missing construction and conventions; they are not a complete geometric definition of the dynamics.

Short English statement

Serge Tabachnikov asks for the period-$3$ case of Ivrii's conjecture for multidimensional outer symplectic billiards associated with a strictly convex closed hypersurface: the corresponding periodic phase points should have zero symplectic volume. The supplied record leaves the precise dynamics, admissible dimensions, regularity assumptions, and period convention unspecified.
