Comparison of Algebraic and Analytic Modular Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ \mathfrak H=\{\tau\in{\mathbb{C}}:\operatorname{Im}\tau>0\} \] be the complex upper half-plane. For an algebraic modular curve $X$ and a line bundle $\omega$ on $X$, the notation $H^0(X,\omega^{\otimes k})$ denotes the space of global sections of the indicated tensor power. In the usual modular-curve interpretation, $\omega$ is the Hodge bundle, whose fiber at a point representing an elliptic curve $E$ is the space of invariant differentials on $E$. This interpretation requires a suitable moduli space or stack; the supplied record does not specify one. The symbol $k$ denotes the weight in the source, whose allowed values are not stated.

Source question. For a holomorphic modular form, explain how its description as an algebraic global section \[ s\in H^0(X,\omega^{\otimes k}) \] and its description as a holomorphic modular function $f:\mathfrak H\to{\mathbb{C}}$ of weight $k$ can be identified or related. Here "modular function" means the function representing the modular form, and does not impose weight zero.

Unresolved qualifications. The source specifies neither the level or modular group, the algebraic base field and complex realization, the moduli interpretation of $X$, nor whether $X$ is compactified. It also omits the transformation law, any character or multiplier, the conditions at cusps, the extension of $\omega$ over a compactification, and the normalization used to express a section as a function. Consequently, the displayed descriptions identify the objects to be compared but do not determine a precise comparison map or a proposition asserting that such a map is an isomorphism. The supplied question is therefore retained as a request for a comparison, with its missing hypotheses unresolved.

Short English statement

The question asks how the algebraic-section and upper-half-plane descriptions of a holomorphic modular form correspond. The supplied record leaves the level, weight conventions, geometric model, and cusp conditions unspecified, so it does not determine a single precise comparison assertion.
