Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record describes a family of consistency questions referring to the report's Theorems 1--4. Those theorem statements, the affected cardinal invariants, and the parameter hypotheses are not included, so a complete quantified consistency assertion cannot be recovered from this record alone.

A cardinal $\delta$ is supercompact if, for every cardinal $\lambda\geq\delta$, there is an elementary embedding $j:V\to M$ into a transitive class with critical point $\delta$, with $j(\delta)>\lambda$, and with $M$ closed under $\lambda$-indexed sequences from $V$. Here $V$ denotes the ground model. For the source's unspecified parameter $\kappa$, define, when the indicated set is nonempty, \[ \theta^V=\min\{\delta>\kappa:V\models\text{``}\delta\text{ is supercompact''}\}. \] The record does not specify the domain or hypotheses for $\kappa$.

Let $\mathcal P$ stand schematically for an intended pattern of cardinal invariants from the report's Theorems 1--4, and let $(\mathfrak x_i)_{i\in I}$ stand for the invariants affected by the corresponding Boolean-ultrapower construction. Neither $\mathcal P$ nor $I$ is supplied; these symbols are placeholders, not reconstructed theorem statements.

The questions reported are whether the intended consistency patterns can be established without assuming a supercompact cardinal, or whether the affected invariants can consistently receive smaller, more accessible values than those produced by the reported constructions. The corrected formulation describes the latter direction using values below the first ground-model supercompact above $\kappa$. If $W$ denotes a resulting model and that ground-model cardinal exists, the indicated upper-bound condition would read \[ \mathfrak x_i^W<\theta^V\qquad(i\in I), \] with the comparison made between ordinals. This display records only the stated bound; it does not specify the missing invariant pattern, the intended smaller values, or the required preservation properties. In particular, the record does not say which patterns or parameter choices are quantified over, or whether every affected invariant must satisfy the bound simultaneously.

The two directions must be distinguished: proving consistency without a supercompact-cardinal assumption does not itself mean requiring the resulting model to have no supercompact cardinals. Moreover, $\theta^V$ is undefined if there is no ground-model supercompact above $\kappa$. The original formulation asks generally for smaller values, whereas the corrected formulation additionally mentions weakening the large-cardinal assumption and the specific ground-model bound. Their exact relationship requires the missing theorem statements.

For the cardinal-invariant patterns in the report's Theorems 1--4, ask whether the supercompact-cardinal assumption can be avoided, or whether the invariants can consistently have smaller values, described in the corrected record as below the first ground-model supercompact above $\kappa$.
