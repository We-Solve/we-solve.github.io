Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.38

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb D=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|<1\}$ and let $S$ conventionally denote the normalized univalent functions on $\mathbb D$: $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$. The source uses $g\prec f$ without defining the symbol. Under the usual analytic-subordination convention it means $g=f\circ\omega$ for a holomorphic self-map $\omega$ of $\mathbb D$ fixing $0$.

Requested proof simplification (P. L. Duren). The record attributes to Tao-Shing Shah the following best-possible assertions under $g\prec f$, $f\in S$, and $g'(0)/f'(0)\in{\mathbb{R}}$: \[ |g(z)|\leq|f(z)|\quad\text{for }|z|\leq\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}, \] \[ |g'(z)|\leq|f'(z)|\quad\text{for }|z|\leq3-\sqrt8. \] Here best possible means the respective radius cannot be increased uniformly over the intended class. The task is to find simpler proofs of the reported sharp results, not to establish new larger radii. The source says Shah's proofs are technically involved, and reports that Goluzin gave simpler proofs with worse constants, whose methods appear incapable of obtaining the two displayed sharp bounds.

The supplied hypotheses require verification before these can be treated as precise theorems. Under standard subordination, a real derivative ratio permits $\omega(z)=-z$. For instance $f(z)=z/(1-z)^2\in S$ and $g(z)=f(-z)$ have $g'(0)/f'(0)=-1$, while $|g(-r)|>|f(-r)|$ for $0<r<1$. Thus the literal real-ratio hypothesis conflicts with the first displayed claim under that convention. The record may omit a sign/normalization restriction or use a different meaning of $\prec$; no repair is chosen here.

The research summary says the results are established and the item is open only as a simpler-proof request, with no simpler proof located. This accurately distinguishes the source's intended task from an unresolved truth conjecture, but it does not resolve the formulation mismatch.

Short English statement

Find simpler proofs of Shah's two reported sharp domination-radius inequalities for subordinate functions, with radii $(3-\sqrt5)/2$ and $3-\sqrt8$. The printed real derivative-ratio condition conflicts with standard subordination, so the intended hypotheses must first be checked.
