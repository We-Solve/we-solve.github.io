Math-2774

Overfull Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite undirected simple graph, put $n=|V|$, and let $\Delta(G)=\max_{v\in V}\deg_G(v)$ be its maximum degree. A proper edge coloring assigns colors to the edges so that edges sharing a vertex have different colors. The chromatic index $\chi'(G)$ is the least number of colors in such a coloring. Vizing's theorem, cited in the supplied background, places $\chi'(G)$ in $\{\Delta(G),\Delta(G)+1\}$; these cases are called class 1 and class 2, respectively.

A subgraph $H\subseteq G$ is overfull if \[ |E(H)|>\Delta(H)\left\lfloor\frac{|V(H)|}{2}\right\rfloor. \] The subgraph condition in the source additionally requires $\Delta(H)=\Delta(G)$. Thus the relevant condition can be written \[ \exists H\subseteq G:\quad \Delta(H)=\Delta(G) \quad\text{and}\quad |E(H)|>\Delta(G)\left\lfloor\frac{|V(H)|}{2}\right\rfloor. \] Here the number of vertices in the overfull inequality belongs to $H$, not to the ambient graph unless $H=G$.

Imported conjecture. For every such graph $G$ satisfying $\Delta(G)\geq n/3$, is the equivalence \[ \chi'(G)=\Delta(G)+1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists H\subseteq G\text{ with }\Delta(H)=\Delta(G) \text{ and }|E(H)|>\Delta(G)\left\lfloor\frac{|V(H)|}{2}\right\rfloor \] true? The non-strict threshold $\Delta(G)\geq n/3$ is exactly the threshold in the imported short statement and background.

Threshold qualification. The literature triage dated 2026-08-17 instead describes the Overfull Conjecture and the remaining range using $\Delta(G)>n/3$. The two domains differ when $n$ is divisible by $3$ and $\Delta(G)=n/3$. Accordingly the displayed imported formulation is not silently identified with the strict-threshold literature formulation; the intended treatment of equality requires review.

The supplied background notes that an overfull subgraph with the stated maximum degree forces class 2. The dated review describes the full strict-threshold conjecture as unresolved and reports progress for sufficiently large robust expanders in a high-degree regime, including a stated consequence for sufficiently large graphs with $\Delta(G)\geq(1+\varepsilon)n/2$. It does not reproduce the exact theorem hypotheses, robust-expansion definition, or dependence of the size threshold on $\varepsilon$. These partial-progress descriptions are retained as supplied context without upgrading them into a fully quantified theorem. The registry label open and triage label partially solved are dated classifications, not an independent literature verification.

Short English statement

For a simple graph of sufficiently large maximum degree relative to its order, the Overfull Conjecture asks whether needing one extra edge color is exactly explained by a subgraph with too many edges for that many matchings. The import uses $\Delta\geq n/3$, whereas its literature review uses $\Delta>n/3$; the equality case must be checked.
