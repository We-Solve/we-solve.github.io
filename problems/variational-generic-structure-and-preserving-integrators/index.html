Variational GENERIC Structure and Preserving Integrators

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.

Definitions and conventions.
GENERIC abbreviates General Equation for the Non-Equilibrium Reversible--Irreversible Coupling. With state $z$, energy $E$, entropy $S$, an antisymmetric Poisson operator $L$ and positive dissipative operator $M$, its schematic evolution is $\dot z=L(z)DE(z)+M(z)DS(z)$. Degeneracy conditions $LDS=0$ and $MDE=0$ separate reversible entropy conservation from irreversible energy conservation. A complete variational principle must recover both parts together. A structure-preserving integrator is a time discretization respecting the specified energy, entropy and Poisson/dissipation constraints, with the precise invariants stated rather than presumed from the name.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Does there exist a variational principle that embodies the complete GENERIC structure of nonequilibrium thermodynamics, including its reversible Poisson and irreversible dissipative parts? If so, can its discretization yield structure-preserving GENERIC integrators?
Source scope and qualification.
The whole coupled structure and the follow-on discretization question are required. A variational principle for the Hamiltonian part alone or for a dissipation-only gradient flow does not answer the original target.

Short English statement

Find a variational formulation for full reversible and irreversible GENERIC dynamics and derive integrators that preserve its structure.

A restricted discrete calculation.
Let the state lie in $\mathbb R^n$, let $E,S$ be continuously differentiable, and suppose constant matrices $L,M$ satisfy $L^T=-L$, $M^T=M\ge0$, and $L\nabla S(z)=M\nabla E(z)=0$ at every state on the relevant line segments. For old and new states $x,y$, define averaged gradients \[ e=\int_0^1\nabla E(x+s(y-x))\,ds,\qquad s_*=\int_0^1\nabla S(x+s(y-x))\,ds. \] Consider the implicit step $y-x=h(Le+Ms_*)$, with $h\ge0$. Conditional on a solution existing, the chain rule and the constant degeneracy conditions prove \[ E(y)-E(x)=e^T(y-x)=0,\qquad S(y)-S(x)=h\,s_*^TMs_*\ge0. \] The cross terms vanish because $Me=0$ and $Ls_*=0$, while skew symmetry gives $e^TLe=0$. At $h=0$ the identity step is recovered. For small $h$, local solvability follows directly by a contraction if the two averaged gradients are locally Lipschitz in $y$ and the iterate is confined to a ball; no global step-size assertion is made.
Failed extension and source scope.
When $L,M$ depend on state, inserting them at the midpoint need not annihilate the averaged gradients. Thus pointwise GENERIC degeneracy does not prove the two equalities above for that discretization. Nor does the calculation supply a variational principle for the full coupled Poisson/dissipative dynamics; minimizing a squared equation residual would merely encode a pre-existing equation. The source asks both for that principle and for its discretization. This elementary constant-operator integrator check addresses a diagnostic special setting only, and neither Jacobi preservation nor the full variable-operator target is claimed.
