Derivative Formula for Multiple Eisenstein Series

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A={\mathbb{Q}}\langle z_2,z_3,\ldots\rangle$ be the free associative unital ${\mathbb{Q}}$-algebra on the indicated generators. Its basis consists of finite words in these generators, including the empty word. The supplied record denotes the intended multiple Eisenstein series associated with $w$ by $G(w)$, with dependence on a variable $\tau$.

The record describes an operator $\theta$, called a derivation in its background assessment, by \[ \theta(w)=-D\left(\varphi(w)\right), \] and says that its construction uses the operator $\mathrm{Drop1}$. It supplies no definitions of $D$, $\varphi$, or $\mathrm{Drop1}$, nor the spaces on which they act. Thus this formula does not by itself define $\theta$ explicitly.

Conjectural target, subject to the unresolved definitions below. With the intended multiple Eisenstein evaluation and operators, establish \[ \forall w\in A,\qquad 2\pi i\,\frac{d}{d\tau}G(w)(\tau) =G\left(\theta(w)\right)(\tau) \] at every $\tau$ in the intended common domain. The source record does not specify that domain, the normalization or regularization of $G$, its evaluation on operator outputs, or its convention on the empty word. These conventions must be recovered before this is a fully specified mathematical assertion. The wording also calls $w$ a word while quantifying over the whole algebra; extending a wordwise identity to all algebra elements requires an appropriate linearity convention that is not explicitly supplied.

The original extracted text additionally reports that $R(z_2,z_2)$ yields the weight-six relation \[ 6G_{3,3}-3G_{4,2}-G_6=0. \] This is retained as a supplied illustrative relation, not as an independently verified assertion or a definition of the target. Neither $R$ nor the conventions for the indexed series are defined in the record. The original extraction is incomplete around the conjecture itself, so the displayed derivative identity above follows the supplied reconstructed statement.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that differentiation of a multiple Eisenstein series is represented by the operator $\theta=-D\circ\varphi$, with the factor $2\pi i$ shown above. Definitions of the series and operators are missing, so this remains a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
