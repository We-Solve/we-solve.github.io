An invariant transverse-Jacobi criterion.
Along a chosen null geodesic with affine parameter $s$, suppose the screen Jacobi equation is $A''(s)+\mathcal R(s)A(s)=0$, where $A$ is a real $2\times2$ matrix, $A(0)=0$, $A'(0)=I$, and $\mathcal R$ is a continuous symmetric optical tidal matrix in a parallel orthonormal screen frame. A nonzero initial screen vector $v$ produces Jacobi field $A(s)v$; consequently a conjugate point at $s>0$ is exactly $\det A(s)=0$. Orthogonal changes of endpoint screen frames multiply $A$ on the left and right by invertible matrices, preserving the zero set of the determinant.
Exact curvature tests.
For $\mathcal R=0$, $A=sI$, with no positive conjugate point. For $\mathcal R=\kappa I$, $\kappa>0$, $A=\sin(\sqrt\kappa s)I/\sqrt\kappa$, and the first is $\pi/\sqrt\kappa$. For negative $\kappa$, the hyperbolic-sine solution has none. Wherever $A$ is invertible, $B=A'A^{-1}$ satisfies $B'+B^2+\mathcal R=0$, by direct differentiation. Its trace is the logarithmic derivative $(\det A)'/\det A$, linking area expansion to the caustic criterion.
Gap.
This ODE certificate identifies what to compute, but no Kerr ray, optical tidal matrix or conjugate location is evaluated. The constant-curvature tests are not Kerr substitutes. Higher-order/near-observer/off-equatorial sheets, astroid formation, stability under a specified metric perturbation class, optical terminology and the lightlike character of stable sheets remain unresolved. The first missing step is a controlled Kerr Jacobi integration and endpoint-map analysis; no plotting or numerical verification is claimed.
