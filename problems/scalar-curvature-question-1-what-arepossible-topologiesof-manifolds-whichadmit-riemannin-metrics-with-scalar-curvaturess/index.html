Scalar Curvature Question [?1]: ○What arepossible topologiesof manifolds whichadmit Riemannin metrics with scalar curvaturesSc > 0

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a smooth manifold $M$ and a smooth Riemannian metric $g$, let $\operatorname{Scal}_g:M\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be the trace of the Ricci tensor with respect to $g$. Say that $g$ has positive scalar curvature when \[ \operatorname{Scal}_g(x)>0\quad\text{for every }x\in M. \] Gromov asks: which topological types of manifolds admit such a metric? For any specified class $\mathcal C$ of smooth manifolds, this asks to characterize \[ \{[M]_{\mathrm{homeomorphism}}: M\in\mathcal C,\ \exists g\text{ Riemannian on }M\text{ with }\operatorname{Scal}_g>0\}. \] The source does not specify $\mathcal C$: dimension, compactness, absence of boundary, orientability, and completeness of the metric are not stated. Nor does it specify whether "topologies" means homeomorphism types, diffeomorphism types, or another classification. The display gives one precise interpretation after choosing those conventions; it does not restrict the question to closed or simply connected manifolds. A topological manifold also needs a smooth structure before the metric condition can be imposed, and existence for some smooth structure must be distinguished from existence for a fixed one.

Short English statement

Classify the manifolds that support a metric with positive scalar curvature at every point. The intended class of manifolds and the meaning of topological classification need clarification.
