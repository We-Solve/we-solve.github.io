Math-3524

Local Clique-Weighted Chromatic Bounds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the provisional interpretation below, let $G=(V(G),E(G))$ be a nonempty finite simple undirected graph. A proper vertex coloring assigns different colors to adjacent vertices, and $\chi(G)$ is the minimum number of colors in such a coloring. For $v\in V(G)$, define \[ N_G(v)=\{u\in V(G):\{u,v\}\in E(G)\},\qquad d_G(v)=|N_G(v)|. \] Interpret $\widetilde N(v)$ provisionally as the closed neighborhood $N_G(v)\cup\{v\}$. For $S\subseteq V(G)$, let $G[S]$ denote the induced subgraph on $S$, and let $\omega(H)$ denote the maximum number of pairwise adjacent vertices in a graph $H$.

Under these explicitly provisional conventions, the supplied question is whether \[ \exists\,\varepsilon\in{\mathbb{R}},\ \varepsilon>0,\quad \forall\,G\text{ nonempty, finite, simple, and undirected},\qquad \chi(G)\leq \max_{v\in V(G)} \left((1-\varepsilon)(d_G(v)+1) +\varepsilon\,\omega\left(G[\widetilde N(v)]\right)\right). \] The constant $\varepsilon$ must be independent of $G$. The displayed bound is retained exactly as supplied, without a ceiling or other rounding operation and without an additional upper restriction on $\varepsilon$.

The supplied record says "every graph" but does not explicitly specify finiteness, simplicity, directedness, exclusion of the empty graph, or the meaning of $\widetilde N(v)$. Thus the conventions above give a conditional formalization, not a verified identification of the intended domain or notation. For the empty graph the displayed maximum has no supplied convention. The intended neighborhood convention, graph domain, and exact unrounded formulation require source review.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether one positive constant, independent of the graph, bounds the chromatic number by the largest vertexwise weighted combination of degree plus one and the clique number in the indicated neighborhood. The graph domain and neighborhood notation are unresolved; the finite simple graph and closed neighborhood interpretation is provisional. The bound is reproduced without rounding.
