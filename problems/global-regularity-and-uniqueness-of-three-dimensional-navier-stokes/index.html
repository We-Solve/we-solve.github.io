Global Regularity and Uniqueness of Three-Dimensional Navier–Stokes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks whether three-dimensional Leray--Hopf solutions on ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ are globally regular and unique. It does not specify the initial-data class, forcing, or the meaning of regularity at the initial time. The following makes explicit a conventional unforced interpretation; these choices require confirmation against the source.

Fix a viscosity $\nu>0$, and define \[ L^2_\sigma({\mathbb{R}}^3)=\overline{\{v\in C_c^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^3;{\mathbb{R}}^3):\nabla\cdot v=0\}}^{\,L^2({\mathbb{R}}^3)}. \] For initial velocity $u_0\in L^2_\sigma({\mathbb{R}}^3)$, consider the unforced incompressible Navier--Stokes equations \[ \partial_t u+(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nu\Delta u+\nabla p=0, \qquad \nabla\cdot u=0 \quad\text{on }(0,\infty)\times{\mathbb{R}}^3, \qquad u(0)=u_0. \] Here $u$ is the velocity and $p$ is the pressure. Under this interpretation, a Leray--Hopf solution is a distributional solution for which, for every $T>0$, \[ u\in L^\infty(0,T;L^2_\sigma({\mathbb{R}}^3))\cap L^2(0,T;H^1({\mathbb{R}}^3;{\mathbb{R}}^3)), \qquad u\in C_w([0,\infty);L^2_\sigma({\mathbb{R}}^3)), \] where $C_w$ denotes continuity for the weak $L^2$ topology, and \[ \lim_{t\downarrow0}\|u(t)-u_0\|_{L^2}=0. \] It also satisfies the energy inequality \[ \frac12\|u(t)\|_{L^2}^2+\nu\int_s^t\|\nabla u(\tau)\|_{L^2}^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\tau \leq\frac12\|u(s)\|_{L^2}^2 \] for $s=0$, and for almost every $s>0$, for every $t\geq s$. The spatial domain is all of ${\mathbb{R}}^3$; there is no spatial boundary, and the stated function spaces impose finite energy.

Question under these explicit conventions. For every $\nu>0$ and every $u_0\in L^2_\sigma({\mathbb{R}}^3)$, is every Leray--Hopf solution globally regular in the sense that \[ u\in C^\infty((0,\infty)\times{\mathbb{R}}^3;{\mathbb{R}}^3), \] and is its velocity unique among Leray--Hopf solutions with the same viscosity and initial velocity? Explicitly, must any two such solutions $u,v$ satisfy $u(t)=v(t)$ in $L^2({\mathbb{R}}^3)$ for every $t\geq0$? Pressure uniqueness is understood only up to addition of a function of time.

Unresolved qualifications. Zero forcing, arbitrary finite-energy divergence-free initial data, and smoothness only at positive times are explicit interpretive choices, not hypotheses supplied by the record. If smooth initial data or a nonzero force is intended, its admissible class must be specified. Smoothness through $t=0$ requires additional initial-data assumptions. The record does not determine which of these formulations is intended, and no equivalence between them is asserted here.

Short English statement

The question concerns whether every three-dimensional Leray--Hopf solution on ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ remains regular for all positive times and whether solutions with the same data coincide. The displayed formulation explicitly adopts zero forcing and finite-energy initial data; the source record leaves those choices and initial-time regularity unspecified. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified assessment.
