Math-4568

Periodicity of Symmetric Tame Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source does not specify the ground field or explicitly state the finite-dimensional algebra conventions. The following makes the question precise conditionally in the customary setting of finite-dimensional associative unital algebras over an algebraically closed field $k$. These conventions require source confirmation; no characteristic restriction is supplied.

Let $A$ be such an algebra. It is indecomposable if it is not isomorphic to a direct product of two nonzero algebras. It is symmetric if \[ A\cong D(A):=\operatorname{Hom}_k(A,k) \] as $A$-$A$-bimodules, where $(a\cdot f\cdot b)(x)=f(bxa)$.

For each integer $d\geq 1$, let $\mu_A(d)$ be the least number of $A$-$k[t]$-bimodules, each free of rank $d$ as a right $k[t]$-module, whose specializations \[ M\otimes_{k[t]} k[t]/(t-\lambda),\qquad \lambda\in k, \] include, up to isomorphism, all but finitely many indecomposable left $A$-modules of $k$-dimension $d$. Set $\mu_A(d)=\infty$ if no such finite collection exists. The algebra is tame if $\mu_A(d)<\infty$ for every $d$. It has polynomial growth if there exist $C>0$ and an integer $r\geq0$ such that $\mu_A(d)\leq C d^r$ for every $d\geq1$. Thus, within the tame class, non-polynomial growth means \[ \forall C>0\ \forall r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\ \exists d\geq1: \mu_A(d)>C d^r. \]

For a finite-dimensional left $A$-module $M$, let $\Omega_A^n(M)$ denote the $n$-th syzygy in a minimal projective resolution. The module is periodic if $\Omega_A^n(M)\cong M$ for some integer $n>0$. Put $A^e=A\otimes_k A^{\mathrm{op}}$, so that $A$-$A$-bimodules are left $A^e$-modules. The algebra $A$ is periodic if $\Omega_{A^e}^n(A)\cong A$ for some $n>0$; its period is conventionally the least such $n$.

Question, under the conditional conventions above. For every indecomposable symmetric tame algebra $A$ of non-polynomial growth, does \[ \left(\forall\text{ simple left }A\text{-modules }S\right) \left(\exists n_S\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\right)\quad \Omega_A^{n_S}(S)\cong S \] imply that $A$ is periodic with least bimodule period $4$? Explicitly, the latter conclusion is \[ \Omega_{A^e}^{4}(A)\cong A, \qquad \Omega_{A^e}^{j}(A)\not\cong A\quad(1\leq j<4). \] The source says "period $4$" without defining whether minimality is intended. If it means only $\Omega_{A^e}^{4}(A)\cong A$, the exclusion of smaller periods must be omitted; this distinction remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Must an indecomposable symmetric tame algebra of non-polynomial growth have bimodule period $4$ whenever every simple module is periodic? The ground-field hypotheses and whether $4$ must be the least period require confirmation from the source.
