Math-15006

Polynomial-Rate Surrogates for Noisy Manifold Estimation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ denote the smooth manifold to be estimated from noisy observations in an embedding space. The supplied record does not specify the embedding space, manifold dimension, smoothness order, boundary conditions, observation model, noise distribution, or admissible class of manifolds. It assumes that estimating $M$ has logarithmic minimax convergence rates, without specifying the loss or the precise rate.

Source question. What surrogate object associated with $M$ preserves its essential geometric or topological features and can be estimated at a polynomial rate of convergence?

The following notation makes the intended statistical question explicit while retaining its unresolved parameters. Let $\Theta$ be an as-yet unspecified class of statistical parameters, with a smooth embedded manifold $M_\theta$ and a law $P_\theta^{(n)}$ for the noisy observations at sample size $n$. This notation imposes no independence assumption. For a target $U:\Theta\to\mathcal U$ and a nonnegative loss $L$, define its minimax risk by \[ R_n(U;\Theta,L) :=\inf_{\widehat U_n}\sup_{\theta\in\Theta} {\mathbb{E}}_{P_\theta^{(n)}}\!\left[L\left(\widehat U_n,U(\theta)\right)\right], \] where the infimum is over measurable estimators based on the observations. The logarithmic-rate premise concerns $U(\theta)=M_\theta$, for an unspecified manifold loss.

After fixing the model and the features to be retained, a possible precise interpretation asks for a surrogate space $\mathcal S$, a map $T$ assigning $T(M)\in\mathcal S$ to each admissible manifold, a loss $L_{\mathcal S}$, estimators $\widehat T_n$, and constants $\alpha>0$, $C>0$, and $n_0\in{\mathbb{N}}$, such that \[ \sup_{\theta\in\Theta} {\mathbb{E}}_{P_\theta^{(n)}}\!\left[ L_{\mathcal S}\left(\widehat T_n,T(M_\theta)\right) \right] \le C n^{-\alpha} \qquad\text{for every }n\ge n_0. \] If preservation means exact recovery of a specified feature map $\Phi$, its requirement could be expressed by the existence of a map $g$ satisfying \[ g\left(T(M_\theta)\right)=\Phi(M_\theta) \qquad\text{for every }\theta\in\Theta. \] These formulas are an explicitly conditional formulation template, not additional hypotheses supplied by the source. The record does not specify $\Phi$, whether preservation is exact or approximate, or whether polynomial convergence is intended in uniform expected loss as above. Consequently, a unique fully quantified mathematical problem cannot be recovered from this record.

Short English statement

Find an estimable surrogate for a smooth manifold observed through noise that retains the relevant geometry or topology and admits polynomial convergence despite the assumed logarithmic minimax rates for estimating the manifold itself. The observation model, losses, retained features, and meaning of preservation remain unspecified, so the displayed formulation requires human mathematical review.
