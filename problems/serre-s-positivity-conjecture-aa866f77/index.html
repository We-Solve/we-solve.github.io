Serre's Positivity Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a mathematical result relevant to this question in the selected manuscript. Serre intersection numbers are claimed to be positive when two nonzero finite modules have complementary support dimensions and a finite-length tensor product over a regular local ring, even in ramified mixed characteristic.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Take nonzero finite modules $M,N$ over a local ring $(R,\mathfrak m)$ which is Noetherian and regular, with $\dim R=d$. Here finite means finitely generated. Define \[ \chi^R(M,N)=\sum_{i\ge0}(-1)^i\operatorname{length}_R\operatorname{Tor}^R_i(M,N). \] The source asserts \[ \operatorname{length}_R(M\otimes_RN)<\infty,\quad \dim_RM+\dim_RN=d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\chi^R(M,N)>0. \] A separate corollary, for primes $P,Q$ in a regular local ring $A$, assumes finite length of $A/(P+Q)$, complementary dimensions, and \[ T=\operatorname{gr}(A/P)\otimes_{\operatorname{gr}A}\operatorname{gr}(A/Q),\qquad\dim T\le1. \] It then gives $\chi^A(A/P,A/Q)\ge e_{\mathfrak m}(A/P)e_{\mathfrak m}(A/Q)$, with equality precisely when $\dim T=0$. Here the graded rings use maximal-ideal filtrations and $e_{\mathfrak m}$ denotes Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity.

Outline of the argument

The reductions pass to completed quotient domains and algebras carrying normalized length. Galois-averaged supported K-theory compares ordinary Tor Euler numbers with normalized ones; parameter Koszul exactness and truncated finite resolutions then force each relevant normalized contribution to be positive.
