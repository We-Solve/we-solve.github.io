Robinson's Defect Conjecture and Commuting Sylow Subgroups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concatenates two independent conjectural assertions. Its supplied verification field labels the extraction unrecoverable, and no clean statement is supplied. The formulations below therefore record explicit interpretations requiring mathematical review; they are not certified transcriptions of the cited report.

Let $G$ be a finite group and $p$ a prime. Write $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ for the ordinary irreducible complex characters of $G$. For a positive integer $n$, let $v_p(n)$ be its $p$-adic valuation and put $n_p=p^{v_p(n)}$. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, define the additive and multiplicative character defects by \[ d_p(\chi)=v_p(|G|)-v_p(\chi(1)),\qquad \Delta_p(\chi)=p^{d_p(\chi)}=\frac{|G|_p}{\chi(1)_p}. \] A $p$-block $B$ is a block in ordinary modular character theory at the prime $p$; $\operatorname{Irr}(B)$ denotes its ordinary irreducible characters. Let $D\leq G$ be a defect group of $B$, and let \[ Z(D)=\{z\in D:zd=dz\text{ for every }d\in D\} \] be its center.

First extracted assertion, attributed to Robinson. The source uses the undefined notation $\operatorname{pdef}(\chi)$. If this means the multiplicative defect $\Delta_p(\chi)$, its literal assertion is that, for every finite group $G$, every prime $p$, every $p$-block $B$ with defect group $D$, and every $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B)$, \[ \Delta_p(\chi)\geq |Z(D)|, \qquad \Delta_p(\chi)=|Z(D)| \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad D\text{ is abelian}. \] Equivalently, under this interpretation, the inequality is $d_p(\chi)\geq\log_p|Z(D)|$, with the corresponding equality clause. The normalization of $\operatorname{pdef}$, the intended character domain, and the scope of the equality clause require source verification. An additive interpretation cannot silently be substituted into the source's displayed comparison with $|Z(D)|$.

Second extracted assertion, concerning commuting Sylow subgroups. For a prime $r$, let $\operatorname{Syl}_r(G)$ denote the Sylow $r$-subgroups of $G$, and let $B_0^{(r)}(G)$ be the principal $r$-block, namely the block containing the trivial character. Interpret commuting subgroups elementwise: $[P,Q]=1$ means that $xy=yx$ for every $x\in P$ and $y\in Q$. Under these conventions, the literal extracted assertion is that, for every finite group $G$ and primes $p,q$, \[ \begin{aligned} &\exists P\in\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)\ \exists Q\in\operatorname{Syl}_q(G):[P,Q]=1\\ &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left(\forall\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B_0^{(p)}(G)),\ q\nmid\chi(1)\right) \ \land\ \left(\forall\psi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B_0^{(q)}(G)),\ p\nmid\psi(1)\right). \end{aligned} \] The source does not specify whether $p$ and $q$ must be distinct, whether they must divide $|G|$, or whether further hypotheses apply. The quantification above reproduces the absence of those restrictions; its intended scope, including the case $p=q$, remains unresolved. Neither assertion is promoted here to a verified theorem or a verified current open problem.

Short English statement

The record combines Robinson's character-defect assertion with a separate proposed characterization of commuting Sylow subgroups using degrees of characters in two principal blocks. Both are displayed under explicit character-theoretic conventions. The defect normalization, equality quantifiers, and prime restrictions need review. The supplied record labels the extraction unrecoverable and its mathematical status partially solved; these are supplied curation labels, not independently verified conclusions.
