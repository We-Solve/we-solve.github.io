Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
On the uniform cube $\Omega_n=\{-1,1\}^n$, $n\ge0$, take any soft binary channel $u:\Omega_n\to[-1,1]$. Define, using natural logarithms, \[ H(r)=-\tfrac{1+r}2\log\tfrac{1+r}2-\tfrac{1-r}2\log\tfrac{1-r}2, \quad\psi(r)=\log2-H(r),\quad I(u)=H(\mathbb Eu)-\mathbb EH(u). \] Under independent symmetric coordinate noise of mean $\rho\in[-1,1]$, $T_{\rho}$ is conditional averaging. The claimed sharp contraction is \[ I(T_{\rho} u)\le\psi(|\rho|\psi^{-1}(I(u))), \] where $\psi^{-1}$ is the increasing nonnegative branch from $[0,\log2]$ to $[0,1]$. For $n\ge1$, every $u(x)=ax_i$ with $|a|\le1$ attains equality. Output bias is unrestricted.
