Math-7412

Blocks of Symmetric-Group Centralizer Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record leaves the hypotheses on $k$, the ranges of $n,\ell$, and the subgroup embedding unspecified. The following gives a conditional formulation using the usual symmetric-group embedding; these conventions require source confirmation.

Let $k$ be a field, let $0\leq \ell\leq n$ be integers, and embed $S_\ell$ in $S_n$ by letting it permute the first $\ell$ letters and fix the others. Identify $kS_\ell$ with its image in $kS_n$, and define \[ A=kS_n^{S_\ell}:=\{a\in kS_n: hah^{-1}=a\text{ for every }h\in S_\ell\} =\{a\in kS_n: ax=xa\text{ for every }x\in kS_\ell\}. \] For a finite-dimensional unital $k$-algebra $B$, a block idempotent is a nonzero central idempotent that cannot be written as a sum of two nonzero orthogonal central idempotents. Its associated block is the ideal $Bb$.

Question (source conjecture, conditional formulation). For the intended choices of $k,n,\ell$, does every block idempotent $b$ of $A$ satisfy \[ b=ef \] for some block idempotent $e$ of $kS_n$ and some block idempotent $f$ of $kS_\ell$? Here the product is taken in $kS_n$. This interprets the source phrase "blocks represented by products" as equality of block idempotents. The supplied record does not determine whether arbitrary fields are intended, whether positive characteristic or splitting hypotheses are required, or whether the displayed parameter boundary cases are included.

Short English statement

Under the stated conditional interpretation, the question asks whether every block idempotent of the symmetric-group centralizer algebra is a product of block idempotents from the two group algebras. The field hypotheses, parameter ranges, subgroup embedding, and precise meaning of "represented" require confirmation. The supplied open-status label is dated curation.
