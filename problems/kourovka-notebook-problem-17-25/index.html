Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.25

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $I$ be the $2\times2$ identity matrix, and let $e_{ij}$ be the $2\times2$ matrix with entry $1$ in position $(i,j)$ and all other entries zero. For each $\alpha\in{\mathbb{C}}$, set \[ A_\alpha=I+\alpha e_{12}=\begin{pmatrix}1&\alpha\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=I+e_{21}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}, \] and let $G_\alpha=\langle A_\alpha,B\rangle\leq\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})$. Let $F(a,b)$ denote the free group on the two formal generators $a,b$, and let $\phi_\alpha:F(a,b)\to G_\alpha$ be the surjective homomorphism determined by $\phi_\alpha(a)=A_\alpha$ and $\phi_\alpha(b)=B$. Define \[ X=\{\alpha\in{\mathbb{C}}:\ker\phi_\alpha\neq\{1\}\}. \] Thus membership in $X$ means that these particular generators satisfy a nontrivial reduced word relation.
  1. (a) Is it true that \[ {\mathbb{Q}}\cap(-4,4)\subseteq X? \]
  2. (b) Is it true that \[ {\mathbb{Q}}\cap[27/7,4)\cap X\neq\varnothing? \]
Here rational numbers are regarded as complex numbers via the usual inclusion ${\mathbb{Q}}\subset{\mathbb{C}}$. The registry names G. M. Bergman as proposer, while the supplied statement begins with an attribution to S. P. Farbman; their respective roles are not explained in the supplied record.

Short English statement

For each complex parameter, consider the two specified unipotent matrices. Let $X$ contain the parameters for which these matrices satisfy a nontrivial group relation. The questions ask whether every rational parameter strictly between $-4$ and $4$ lies in $X$, and whether at least one rational parameter in $[27/7,4)$ lies in $X$.
