Limiting Distributions of Mixed Character Sums

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $e(u)=\exp(2\pi i u)$, using the standard analytic number theory convention, which the supplied record does not explicitly define. For each prime $q$, let $\chi_q$ denote the deterministic character referred to in the source. The surrounding excerpt concerns Dirichlet characters, apparently modulo $q$, but the prescription selecting $\chi_q$ is missing. The supplied literature assessment calls it a quadratic character; the main statement merely calls it a prescribed deterministic character. These descriptions require reconciliation before fixing the character family.

Let $\Theta$ have the uniform probability distribution on $[0,1]$. For a real cutoff $x$, interpret the displayed sum in the usual positive-integer sense as the complex-valued random variable \[ S(\chi_q,x,\Theta)=\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\\1\leq n\leq x}}\chi_q(n)e(n\Theta). \] For every Borel set $B\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$, its law is \[ \mu_{q,x}(B)=\int_0^1\mathbf{1}_{B}\!\left(\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\\1\leq n\leq x}}\chi_q(n)e(n\theta)\right)\,\mathrm d\theta. \] The requested problem is to determine the limiting distribution of this sum as $q\to\infty$ through primes, with randomness coming from $\Theta$. In particular, after specifying the cutoff regime, the unnormalized formulation would ask for a probability measure $\mu$ on ${\mathbb{C}}$ such that \[ \mu_{q,x(q)}\Longrightarrow\mu, \] where $x(q)$ is only a placeholder for the cutoff regime that must be supplied; it is not an additional hypothesis extracted from the record. A fixed cutoff would instead mean $\mu_{q,x}\Longrightarrow\mu$ for that fixed $x$. The record does not say whether $x$ is fixed, depends on $q$, or parametrizes a stochastic process. It also supplies no centering or normalization for the sum in the question. Thus it does not determine a unique precise limiting-distribution problem.

The damaged original excerpt also mentions functions $F_{k,\chi,\alpha,\beta}(t)$, integers $k\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}$, the strict summation boundaries $\alpha q<n<(\alpha+\beta)q$, and summands $\chi(n)e(n(k+t)/q)$. Its prefactor appears to involve $e(-\alpha k)$ and $q^{1/2}$, but the extracted formula is not reliably delimited. The domains of $\alpha,\beta,t$ and the connection of these functions to the requested limit are unspecified. This auxiliary excerpt therefore does not justify imposing its apparent scaling or a process interpretation on the stated question.

Short English statement

Determine the limiting probability law of a deterministic character sum when its phase is uniform on $[0,1]$ and the prime modulus tends to infinity. The supplied record leaves the character prescription, cutoff regime, and any intended normalization or process interpretation unresolved, so the target requires clarification before a unique precise formulation is possible.
