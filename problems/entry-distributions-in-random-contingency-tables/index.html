Math-7418

Entry Distributions in Random Contingency Tables

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For positive integers $m,n$, let $R=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{m}$ and $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{n}$ be prescribed margins with equal total sum. Define \[ \mathcal T(R,C)=\left\{(d_{ij})\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{m\times n}:\ \sum_{j=1}^{n}d_{ij}=r_i\ (1\leq i\leq m),\quad \sum_{i=1}^{m}d_{ij}=c_j\ (1\leq j\leq n)\right\}. \] Let $D$ be uniform on this finite, nonempty set; thus $\Pr(D=d)=|\mathcal T(R,C)|^{-1}$ for every $d\in\mathcal T(R,C)$. A geometric distribution on ${\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ with parameter $p\in(0,1)$ has probabilities $\Pr(G=k)=p(1-p)^k$, $k\geq0$; the boundary case $p=1$ denotes the point mass at zero.

The general source question asks whether an individual entry of $D$ has an asymptotically geometric distribution as the dimensions grow. To formulate this as a definite asymptotic assertion, one must specify a sequence $(m_t,n_t,R^{(t)},C^{(t)})$, the selected entries $(i_t,j_t)$, admissible growth conditions on the margins and dimensions, the geometric parameters, and the meaning of asymptotic approximation. These are not specified in the supplied record, so no universal convergence assertion is imposed here.

The distinguished special case is precise at each finite size: for every integer $n\geq2$, let \[ R^{(n)}=C^{(n)}=(3n,\underbrace{n,\ldots,n}_{n-1\text{ entries}}),\qquad D^{(n)}\sim\operatorname{Unif}\mathcal T(R^{(n)},C^{(n)}). \] The source asks whether ${\mathbb{E}}[D^{(n)}_{11}]$ grows linearly in $n$. Under the order-of-magnitude interpretation of this phrase, the question is whether there exist constants $a,b>0$ and an integer $n_0\geq2$ such that \[ a n\leq {\mathbb{E}}[D^{(n)}_{11}]\leq b n\qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq n_0. \] The source does not specify whether it instead intends the stronger assertion that ${\mathbb{E}}[D^{(n)}_{11}]/n$ converges to a finite positive constant. No constant or limiting formula is supplied.

Short English statement

Choose uniformly from all nonnegative integer matrices with fixed row and column sums. The general question asks when a selected entry is approximately geometric as the dimensions increase, but the asymptotic regime and approximation criterion are unspecified. For square tables with margins $(3n,n,\ldots,n)$, the concrete question concerns linear growth of the expected upper-left entry. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
