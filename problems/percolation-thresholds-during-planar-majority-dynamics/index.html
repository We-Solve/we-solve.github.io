Percolation Thresholds During Planar Majority Dynamics

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ denote the nearest-neighbour square lattice with vertex set ${\mathbb{Z}}^2$, or the hexagonal lattice mentioned in the source. For each $p\in[0,1]$, the initial opinions $(\eta_0^p(v))_{v\in V(G)}$ take values in $\{0,1\}$, independently, with $\mathbb P(\eta_0^p(v)=1)=p$. The source calls the subsequent evolution majority dynamics, but does not specify its update schedule, clock rates, independence of update randomness, or rule for resolving ties. Consequently, the questions below require a specified dynamics before they define a unique mathematical problem.

For a specified such evolution, let $T$ be its set of finite times and define the opinion-$1$ subgraph at time $t\in T$ to be the subgraph of $G$ induced by $\{v:\eta_t^p(v)=1\}$. Write \[ A_t^G=\{\text{the opinion-$1$ subgraph at time $t$ has an infinite connected component}\}. \] One precise interpretation of the critical probability is \[ p_c^G(t)=\inf\left\{p\in[0,1]:\mathbb P_p^G(A_t^G)>0\right\}, \] where $\mathbb P_p^G$ includes the initial opinions and any randomness of the evolution. This is an explicitly stated positive-probability interpretation: the supplied record does not specify a threshold convention or hypotheses guaranteeing equivalence with an almost-sure percolation threshold.

Subject to those unresolved specifications, the source asks the following questions.

  1. For the square lattice, is the threshold strictly decreasing, in the sense that \[ p_c^{{\mathbb{Z}}^2}(t_2)<p_c^{{\mathbb{Z}}^2}(t_1) \qquad\text{for every }t_1,t_2\in T\text{ with }t_1<t_2? \]
  2. Does continuity at infinity hold, meaning \[ \lim_{\substack{t\to\infty\\t\in T}}p_c^{{\mathbb{Z}}^2}(t) =p_c^{{\mathbb{Z}}^2}(\infty), \] and is \[ p_c^{{\mathbb{Z}}^2}(\infty)=\frac12? \] Here $p_c^{{\mathbb{Z}}^2}(\infty)$ must be defined independently of the displayed limit for continuity to be a substantive question. The source does not define an infinite-time configuration, a limiting distribution, or another infinite-time percolation event. If a limiting configuration is intended, its existence and the mode of convergence must be specified; they are not assumed here.
  3. For the hexagonal lattice $H$, is \[ p_c^H(t)=\frac12\qquad\text{for every }t\in T, \] and are there exceptional times? The supplied phrase "for every time" does not specify whether it includes $0$, $\infty$, or only positive finite times. If it includes $0$, it also asserts that the initial independent site-percolation threshold on the intended hexagonal graph equals $1/2$; this aspect requires source verification. The exceptional-time question does not specify the initial parameter $p$, the time interval, or whether exceptional times concern percolation or its absence. In particular, it does not determine whether the intended event is the existence of a random time at which percolation occurs despite having probability zero at every deterministic time, or the converse phenomenon.

The supplied record labels the problem partially solved. Its dated assessment reports finite-time continuity and monotone non-increase of the square-lattice threshold, while describing strict decrease, the infinite-time questions, and the hexagonal-lattice questions as open. These are supplied literature-status claims, not independently verified results in this draft.

Short English statement

Starting from independent Bernoulli-$p$ opinions, ask how the threshold for an infinite connected opinion-$1$ cluster changes under majority dynamics. The square-lattice questions concern strict decrease and an infinite-time threshold of $1/2$; the hexagonal-lattice questions concern a constant threshold of $1/2$ and exceptional times. The dynamics, infinite-time object, and exceptional-time event require clarification.
