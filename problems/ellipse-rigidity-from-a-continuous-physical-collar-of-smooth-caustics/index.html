Ellipse rigidity from a continuous physical collar of smooth caustics

OpenAI's claim

A smooth positively curved convex billiard table is claimed to be an ellipse whenever its near-boundary region has a continuous full foliation by smooth convex caustics, with no transverse differentiability required. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $\Omega$ be a bounded strictly convex planar domain with a smooth regular simple closed boundary of positive curvature. Assume a relatively open collar $A\subset\overline\Omega$ of the boundary and a homeomorphism $H_0:S^1\times[0,1)\to A$ whose level zero is $\partial\Omega$ and whose positive levels are smooth closed convex caustics. A caustic means a curve to which tangency of a billiard segment is preserved by reflection $v\mapsto v-2(v\cdot n)n$, with outward unit normal $n$. OpenAI claims \[ \Omega=\{x:(x-c)^TQ(x-c)<1\},\qquad Q=Q^T>0. \] The statement assumes a continuous collar of caustics and includes circles.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 147, 2026-09-24): Periodic rational caustics give a Fourier action system whose near-identity derivative supports analytic Newton inversion. Values and parameter jets produce a joint glancing conjugacy; finite reflected-chain jet determination then controls complex continuation and rules out a first meromorphic wall, yielding global ellipse rigidity.
