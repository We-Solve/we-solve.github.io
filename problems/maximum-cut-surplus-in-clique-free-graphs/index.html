Maximum-Cut Surplus in Clique-Free Graphs

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the conventional interpretation that graphs are finite, simple, and undirected; these conventions are not explicit in the supplied record. For an integer $r\geq 3$, let $K_r$ denote the complete graph on $r$ vertices. A graph is $K_r$-free if it contains no subgraph isomorphic to $K_r$.

For $G=(V,E)$, write $m=|E|$ and define \[ \operatorname{MaxCut}(G)=\max_{A\subseteq V}\left|\{\{u,v\}\in E:u\in A,\ v\in V\setminus A\}\right|, \qquad \operatorname{surp}(G)=\operatorname{MaxCut}(G)-\frac m2. \] For each integer $m\geq 0$, define the largest universally guaranteed surplus by \[ s_r(m)=\inf\{\operatorname{surp}(G):G\text{ is }K_r\text{-free and }|E(G)|=m\}. \] The extremal question is to determine $s_r(m)$, or its sharp asymptotic order as $m\to\infty$, for each fixed integer $r\geq 3$. The record does not specify whether an exact formula or sharp asymptotic order is intended. For $m=0$, the definition gives $s_r(0)=0$.

The explicitly stated subsidiary question is whether \[ \exists\varepsilon>0\ \forall r\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ r\geq 3\ \exists c_r>0\ \exists M_r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}\ \forall m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq M_r}, \qquad s_r(m)\geq c_r m^{1/2+\varepsilon}. \] Thus $\varepsilon$ must be independent of $r$, while the positive constant and threshold in $\Omega_r$ may depend on $r$. Equivalently, every $K_r$-free graph with sufficiently many edges must admit a cut with at least $m/2+c_r m^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ edges.

The supplied dated assessment labels the problem partially solved: it attributes an affirmative resolution of this absolute-$\varepsilon$ question to Zhang and a strengthening to Jin--Milojevic--Tomon, while reporting that a stronger exponent conjecture of Alon, Bollobás, Krivelevich, and Sudakov remains open. This is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical or literature assessment. The stronger conjecture's exponent and full statement are absent, so they cannot be reconstructed here. The original excerpt also asks about improving an unspecified preceding bound when $G$ is far from a disjoint union of cliques; neither that bound nor a meaning of distance is supplied, so this contextual question has no precise formulation in the record.

Short English statement

For each fixed $r\geq 3$, determine the greatest surplus above half the edges that every $K_r$-free graph must have in its maximum cut. The highlighted question asks for a bound $c_r m^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ with one positive $\varepsilon$ valid for all $r$. Supplied curation dated 2026-08-22 reports this subsidiary question solved and a stronger, unspecified exponent conjecture still open; these claims require independent review.
