Definitions and conventions.
Relaxation replaces a nonconvex material energy by an effective energy allowing fine-scale microstructure, commonly through a lower-semicontinuous envelope or an admissible microstructure minimization. Monolithic relaxation treats mechanical and magnetic variables together. Staggered relaxation alternates or successively relaxes subsets of those variables. Equivalence requires equality of the resulting effective energies or solution behavior under stated coupling and admissibility assumptions.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For coupled magnetomechanical systems, should relaxation be formulated monolithically or as a staggered sequence of partial relaxations, and under what conditions are the two procedures equivalent?
Source scope and qualification.
The ordering, topology and compatibility constraints of the partial relaxations matter. Equality of two numerical minimization runs does not establish equivalence of the variational relaxation procedures.

Short English statement

Decide when combined and successive relaxations give the same effective magnetomechanical model.

A separately convex countercheck.
Consider the scalar coupled energy $W(x,y)=(xy-1)^2$ on $\mathbb R^2$. For fixed $y$, its second derivative in $x$ is $2y^2\ge0$, and the symmetric statement holds for $y$. Thus convexifying in one variable at a time does nothing, even after arbitrarily many alternating steps. The joint convex envelope behaves differently at the origin. Every convex minorant $F\le W$ obeys \[ F(0,0)\le\tfrac12F(1,1)+\tfrac12F(-1,-1)\le0, \] whereas the zero function is a convex minorant because $W\ge0$. The joint convex envelope is therefore zero at the origin, while the original and all staggered separate convexifications equal one there.
Mechanism and limits.
The lowering uses a correlated mixture of the two variables: both change sign together. Convexification in a single variable with the other fixed cannot access those zero-energy states. This gives an exact finite-dimensional failure of a universal monolithic/staggered equivalence.

The example is not claimed to be a compatible magnetoelastic microstructure.
