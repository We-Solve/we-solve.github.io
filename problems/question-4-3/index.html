Question 4.3. Is there a geometric map from $B\mathcal C_2$ into $TC(\mathrm{pt})$? A precise construction must identify the target space or the suspension-spectrum convention, state how surface-category data produce the map, and specify any required nontriviality, normalization, or compatibility. The adjective "geometric" is essential: an arbitrary constant map does not express the intended request, yet no formal criterion distinguishing the desired map is given. No existence of an arbitrary continuous map is substituted for the problem.

The source suggests Kevin Costello's manuscript [3], relating conformal field theory to linear Hochschild homology, as a possible starting point. It says that manuscript announces a description of the Deligne--Mumford compactification from the open moduli space, and mentions preliminary work of Søren Galatius and Yasha Eliashberg giving a homotopical description of a partial compactification of stable moduli space. These are attributed historical directions rather than a supplied construction of the requested map.

The summary associates $B\mathcal C_2$ with ${\mathbb{Z}}\times B\Gamma_\infty^+$ through Madsen--Tillmann--Weiss/GMTW and mentions an $\Omega^\infty_{-1}\mathbb{CP}^{\infty}_{-1}$ factor of $TC(\mathrm{pt})^{\wedge}_p$. The category, loop shifts, Thom-spectrum notation, prime, and completion conventions are not supplied. In particular, an equivalence for a loop space must not be identified with one for the unlooped classifying space. The compressed factor notation and the stated identification are retained as unresolved summary data, not asserted as a verified equivalence determining the map.
