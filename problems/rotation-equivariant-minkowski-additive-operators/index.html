Math-1412

Rotation-equivariant Minkowski-additive operators

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal K^n$ be the nonempty compact convex subsets of ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, including singletons, with Hausdorff topology, and let $h_K(u)=\max_{x\in K}\langle x,u\rangle$. Rolf Schneider's question asks to classify continuous $T:\mathcal K^n\to\mathcal K^n$ satisfying \[ h_{T(K+L)}=h_{T(K)}+h_{T(L)},\qquad T(\theta K)=\theta T(K)\quad(\theta\in SO(n)). \] The first identity is Minkowski additivity. Translation invariance is not assumed. Rotations are taken to mean proper rotations, an essential convention in dimension two.

For $n\ge2$, the attached aid gives the unique decomposition \[ T(K)=\Phi(K)+\{A s(K)\}, \] where $s(K)=n\int_{S^{n-1}}u h_K(u)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\sigma(u)$ is the Steiner point for probability-normalized spherical measure, $A$ is linear and commutes with $SO(n)$, and $\Phi$ is a translation-invariant continuous rotation-equivariant Minkowski-additive operator. The map on singletons is $T(\{x\})=\{Ax\}$ and is exactly the translation defect. For $n\ge3$, $A=aI$; for $n=2$, $A=aI+bJ$ with $J$ rotation by $\pi/2$. Requiring reflection equivariance would remove the $bJ$ term, so it cannot be removed under the stated proper-rotation hypothesis alone.

For the normalized part the aid asserts a unique centered signed generating-measure representation \[ h_{\Phi(K)}=h_K*\mu, \] using the appropriate rotation convolution and stabilizer-zonal convention. In dimension two the stabilizer for the $SO(2)$ action is trivial, so no unmentioned reflection symmetry is imposed. The nontrivial admissibility requirement is that the convolution be a support function for every $K$. The summary does not give the precise representation theorem's regularity hypotheses or an intrinsic description of the higher-dimensional generator cone.

Its harmonic audit states that the first multiplier vanishes, $m_1=0$, the constant multiplier satisfies $m_0\ge0$, and $m_0=0$ forces $\Phi=0$. With probability-normalized convolution, $m_0=\mu(S^{n-1})$, and centering removes the first moment. The aid reports full classifications in the planar and weakly monotone settings, while leaving the intrinsic general higher-dimensional cone open, but does not reproduce those classification criteria. These are partial structural reductions of the original classification question, not a complete explicit cone description. The $n=1$ case is outside the supplied decomposition discussion.

Short English statement

Classify continuous rotation-equivariant Minkowski-additive operators without silently adding translation invariance. The supplied aid isolates the singleton translation defect, the planar rotation exception and the normalized convolution problem, whose full higher-dimensional generator cone is not specified.
