Efficient Simulation of Metastable Transitions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $N$ be a positive integer and let $\beta>0$, using the implicit dimension and inverse-temperature conventions of the supplied formulation. Let $V:{\mathbb{R}}^{3N}\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be a potential and let $(W_t)_{t\geq0}$ be standard Brownian motion in ${\mathbb{R}}^{3N}$. The dynamics under consideration are \[ {\,\mathrm{d}} X_t=-\nabla V(X_t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t+\sqrt{2\beta^{-1}}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} W_t. \] The source describes the invariant density as proportional to $e^{-\beta V}$. More explicitly, if \[ 0<Z_\beta:=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^{3N}}e^{-\beta V(x)}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x<\infty, \] its proposed equilibrium probability measure is \[ \pi_\beta({\,\mathrm{d}} x)=Z_\beta^{-1}e^{-\beta V(x)}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] The record does not specify the regularity, growth, or other hypotheses on $V$ needed for a well-defined nonexplosive diffusion and an invariant probability measure. These requirements must therefore remain explicit qualifications. When such a stationary diffusion exists, an equilibrium trajectory means a trajectory of this dynamics with $X_0$ distributed according to $\pi_\beta$.

Research problem. For a metastable potential $V$, develop efficient methods for generating equilibrium trajectories that leave one potential well and reach another. The supplied expanded statement also asks how available rare-event methods should be classified and improved.

This is a research-program formulation rather than a fully specified conjecture or algorithmic performance assertion. The source does not define the wells, metastability, the observation interval, the precise distribution of transition trajectories to be sampled, or a criterion of efficiency. In particular, sampling stationary trajectories until a transition occurs and sampling trajectories conditioned on a transition specify different tasks; the record does not select a precise target path distribution. No uniform quantifier over potentials, temperature regime, approximation tolerance, or computational complexity bound is supplied.

Short English statement

The problem asks for efficient simulation of transitions between wells of an overdamped Langevin diffusion in equilibrium, together with classification and improvement of rare-event methods. The admissible potentials, transition-path distribution, and meaning of efficiency are unspecified, so this remains a qualified research question requiring human mathematical review.
