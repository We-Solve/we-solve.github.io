Étale Homotopy Realization of Khovanov Homology

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\operatorname{Kh}(K)$ for the Khovanov homology of a link $K$. The supplied record does not specify the ambient space and orientation conventions for links, the coefficient ring, the reduced or unreduced version of Khovanov homology, or the grading conventions. These choices must be fixed before a precise realization statement can be formulated.

The source asks whether, for every link $K$ in the intended class, one can associate an appropriate étale homotopy type $E(K)$ that represents $\operatorname{Kh}(K)$ and is itself a link invariant. Schematically, the requirements are \[ K\sim K'\ \Longrightarrow\ E(K)\simeq E(K'), \qquad \operatorname{Rec}(E(K))\cong\operatorname{Kh}(K). \] Here $\sim$ denotes the intended equivalence of links, $\simeq$ denotes the intended equivalence of étale homotopy types, and $\operatorname{Rec}$ is a placeholder for the required recovery operation. None of these choices is specified sufficiently in the supplied record. In particular, the record does not identify the site, topos, or other object whose étale homotopy type is intended, nor whether representation means recovery by homology, cohomology, or another construction, with which coefficients and gradings. The displayed conditions therefore express the source question schematically; they do not supply a fully determined realization conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every link admits an étale homotopy type that is invariant under the intended equivalence of links and recovers its Khovanov homology. The supplied record leaves the homotopy construction, recovery operation, and several conventions unspecified, so a precise conjecture requires further source clarification.
