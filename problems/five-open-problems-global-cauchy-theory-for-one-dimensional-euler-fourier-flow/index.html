Five Open Problems — Global Cauchy theory for one-dimensional Euler–Fourier flow

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In conventional Eulerian notation, let $\rho$ be density, $u$ velocity, $\theta$ temperature, $p(\rho,\theta)$ pressure, $e(\rho,\theta)$ specific internal energy, and $\kappa(\rho,\theta)$ heat conductivity. Write $E=\rho(e+u^2/2)$. The one-dimensional Euler--Fourier system has the standard form \[ \partial_t\rho+\partial_x(\rho u)=0, \qquad \partial_t(\rho u)+\partial_x(\rho u^2+p)=0, \] \[ \partial_tE+\partial_x((E+p)u)=\partial_x(\kappa\partial_x\theta). \] These equations describe inviscid momentum balance with Fourier heat conduction; they are contextual notation, since the supplied excerpt does not specify its constitutive laws or coordinate convention.

Serre's problem is to develop a theory for solutions for all $t\ge0$ of the corresponding Cauchy problem, starting from data constrained only by finite energy and entropy. It permits consideration of additional local boundedness hypotheses on $\rho_0,\rho_0^{-1},u_0,\theta_0,\theta_0^{-1}$. These optional stronger hypotheses are not imposed on the finite-energy-and-entropy-only target; bounds on reciprocal density or temperature in particular restrict vacuum or zero-temperature behavior. The supplied research summary describes the unrestricted target as allowing vacuum in the data.

A precise theorem needs the pressure/internal-energy/entropy laws, conductivity, spatial domain, initial-trace and weak-solution notions, and exact energy/entropy integrability and inequalities. On an unbounded domain, absolute versus relative energy/entropy also matters. "Global theory" does not specify which of existence, uniqueness, regularity, stability, or admissibility is required. None of these missing choices is guessed, and no particular ideal-gas model or uniform positivity bound is silently added.

The supplied PARTIAL-PROGRESS summary reports global results under additional density and entropy hypotheses, while saying the general finite-energy-and-entropy-only Cauchy theory remains open. It provides no precise theorem references or hypotheses for those partial results.

Short English statement

Develop global Cauchy theory for one-dimensional compressible Euler flow with heat conduction using only finite energy and entropy, with optional local data bounds. The constitutive model and the intended solution and integrability framework are absent.
