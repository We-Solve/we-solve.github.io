Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.86

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F=F(x_1,x_2,x_3)$ be the free group on three generators. For $r_1,r_2,r_3\in F$, assume that \[ F/\langle\!\langle r_1,r_2,r_3\rangle\!\rangle_F=\{1\}, \] where $\langle\!\langle S\rangle\!\rangle_F$ denotes the smallest normal subgroup of $F$ containing $S$. Thus $\langle x_1,x_2,x_3\mid r_1,r_2,r_3\rangle$ presents the trivial group. The source describes the following as simple questions related to the Whitehead asphericity conjecture.
  1. (a) For every triple $(r_1,r_2,r_3)$ satisfying the assumption above, prove that \[ G=F/\langle\!\langle r_1,r_2\rangle\!\rangle_F \] is torsion-free. Explicitly, the assertion is \[ \forall g\in G\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1},\qquad g^n=1\Longrightarrow g=1. \]
  2. (b) Put $R_i=\langle\!\langle r_i\rangle\!\rangle_F$ for $i=1,2,3$. For subgroups $A,B\leq F$, write \[ [A,B]=\langle a^{-1}b^{-1}ab\mid a\in A,\ b\in B\rangle. \] Since $R_1$ and $R_2$ are normal in $F$, their commutator subgroup $[R_1,R_2]$ is normal in $F$. The archived question asks whether, for every triple satisfying the same assumption, \[ H=F/[R_1,R_2] \] is residually soluble. Here a group $S$ is soluble if its derived series, defined by $S^{(0)}=S$ and $S^{(k+1)}=[S^{(k)},S^{(k)}]$, reaches $\{1\}$ after finitely many steps. A group $H$ is residually soluble if, for every $h\in H\setminus\{1\}$, there exist a soluble group $S$ and a homomorphism $\varphi:H\to S$ with $\varphi(h)\ne 1$. No finiteness condition on $S$ is imposed.

The supplied record places part (a) in the remaining main-section material and part (b) in the Archive of Solved Problems. It reports a negative answer to part (b), attributed to V. A. Roman'kov. These are supplied source and status qualifications, not independently verified conclusions of this draft; no counterexample or proof is supplied here.

Short English statement

Starting with any three-generator, three-relator presentation of the trivial group, part (a) asks whether deleting the third relator always leaves a torsion-free group. Part (b) asks whether quotienting the free group by the commutator of the normal closures of the first two relators always gives a residually soluble group. The supplied record reports part (b) as negatively resolved and part (a) as remaining.
