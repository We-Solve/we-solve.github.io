Math-1722

Horofunction drift obstructs recurrence and finite invariant measure for the marked harmonic-stretch flow

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks to define a geodesic flow for the asymmetric Thurston metric on Teichmüller space and study ergodicity and mixing, inspired by Eskin--Mirzakhani's work for the Teichmüller metric. Nonuniqueness of Thurston geodesics means a flow requires a specified state space and geodesic selection. Ergodicity and measure-theoretic mixing additionally require an invariant measure; marked space and its mapping-class quotient are different dynamical settings. The extracted following sentence and page header are not additional hypotheses of Problem 2.7.

The supplied aid reports Pan--Wolf's 2026 continuous endpoint-labelled harmonic-stretch selection. Write $\mathcal P$ for its state space, $\psi_t$ for the selected flow, $X(p)\in\mathcal T(S)$ for the marked basepoint of a state, and $\eta(p)$ for its forward endpoint, unchanged by the flow. The complete definition, surface range and domain of this state space are not supplied here; this notation is conditional on that construction, not a claim that every tangent vector canonically determines a geodesic.

Fix $O\in\mathcal T(S)$. Use the forward Walsh horofunction normalization \[ h_\eta^O(X)=\lim_{Z\to\eta}\left(d_{\rm Th}(X,Z)-d_{\rm Th}(O,Z)\right),\qquad h_\eta^O(O)=0, \] where the limit is in the relevant horofunction compactification. The aid reports that on the selected endpoint flow, \[ B_O(p)=h_{\eta(p)}^O(X(p)),\qquad B_O(\psi_t p)=B_O(p)-t. \] Thus $B_O$ is an exact continuous time coordinate on marked state space. No state is forward recurrent: returns to a fixed neighborhood with bounded $B_O$-values are impossible for sufficiently large positive time. The same coordinate excludes topological mixing and any nonzero finite invariant Borel measure. Indeed such a measure would push forward to a nonzero finite translation-invariant measure on ${\mathbb{R}}$, which cannot exist. These statements concern the marked flow and do not address infinite invariant measures or assert quotient nonergodicity.

For a mapping class $g$, the normalized horofunction transforms as \[ B_O(gp)=B_O(p)-h_{\eta(p)}^O(g^{-1}O). \] The endpoint term is a basepoint-dependent cocycle, so $B_O$ need not descend to the quotient. If quotient recurrence is witnessed by $t_n\to+\infty$ and mapping classes $g_n$ with $g_n\psi_{t_n}p\to p$, the identity forces \[ h_{\eta(p)}^O(g_n^{-1}O)=-t_n+o(1). \] Thus quotient recurrence requires cocycle compensation of the marked drift; the marked obstruction alone cannot prohibit it. General quotient ergodicity, mixing, and the intended invariant-measure construction remain open in the supplied account. The problem is not resolved simply by selecting geodesics or by showing nonrecurrence before taking the quotient.

Short English statement

Choose a precise Thurston geodesic flow and a measure before asking about ergodicity or mixing. The supplied endpoint-selected flow has a horofunction drifting exactly by minus time on marked space; a mapping-class cocycle prevents this obstruction from automatically descending to the quotient.
