Math-3671

Positive Bases for Surface Skein Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Put $R={\mathbb{Z}}[t^{\pm1}]$ and $R_{\geq0}={\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}[t^{\pm1}]$, the set of finite Laurent polynomials whose coefficients are nonnegative integers. The source denotes by $S(\Sigma)$ the Kauffman bracket skein algebra of a surface $\Sigma$ over $R$. It does not specify the precise class of surfaces, the skein relations and parameter convention, or boundary and puncture conventions. Its background refers to a compact-surface formulation, but the statement itself only says "a surface." These qualifications remain unresolved.

Let $\mathcal L(\Sigma)$ denote the source's intended set of integer laminations. For a lamination written $\mu=\{(a_i,C_i)\}_{i=1}^{r}$, the proposed element is \[ b_T(\mu)=\prod_{i=1}^{r}T_{a_i}(C_i)\in S(\Sigma), \] where each $C_i$ is interpreted as a curve class in the skein algebra and polynomial evaluation uses its algebra multiplication. The source does not specify the admissible curves, their framing, the allowed weights, the treatment of peripheral or contractible curves, or the normalization of the Chebyshev polynomials $T_j$. Thus this notation retains the source's intended conventions without selecting missing ones. In particular, no choice between classical and rescaled Chebyshev polynomials is made here.

Question, conditional on the intended conventions. For every surface $\Sigma$ in the intended class, does the indexed family \[ \left(b_T(\mu)\right)_{\mu\in\mathcal L(\Sigma)} \] form an $R$-module basis of $S(\Sigma)$ such that, for every $\mu,\nu\in\mathcal L(\Sigma)$, its uniquely determined multiplication coefficients satisfy \[ b_T(\mu)b_T(\nu) =\sum_{\lambda\in\mathcal L(\Sigma)} c_{\mu,\nu}^{\lambda}(t)b_T(\lambda), \qquad c_{\mu,\nu}^{\lambda}(t)\in R_{\geq0}, \] with only finitely many nonzero coefficients for each pair $(\mu,\nu)$? Being an $R$-module basis means that every element of $S(\Sigma)$ has a unique finite $R$-linear expansion in this family. The missing conventions must be recovered before this conditional formulation becomes a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Chebyshev-decorated integer laminations form a basis of the surface skein algebra whose multiplication coefficients are Laurent polynomials in $t$ with nonnegative integer coefficients. The supplied record leaves the surface class, lamination conventions, skein parameter convention, and Chebyshev normalization unresolved. Its supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
