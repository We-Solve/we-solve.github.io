Math-1143

Decomposition Numbers of Symmetric Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, let $p$ be a prime, and let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p$. Write $S_n$ for the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. A partition $\lambda\vdash n$ is a finite nonincreasing sequence of positive integers with sum $n$. A partition is $p$-regular if no positive integer occurs as a part at least $p$ times. Let $\mathcal P(n)$ denote the partitions of $n$, and let $\mathcal P_p(n)$ denote the $p$-regular partitions of $n$.

For $\lambda\in\mathcal P(n)$, let $S_k^\lambda$ be the Specht module over the group algebra $kS_n$. For $\mu\in\mathcal P_p(n)$, let $D_k^\mu$ be the simple $kS_n$-module indexed by $\mu$ in the standard Specht-module parametrization. Define the decomposition number \[ d_{\lambda\mu}(k)=[S_k^\lambda:D_k^\mu]\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0} \] to be the number of composition factors isomorphic to $D_k^\mu$ in a composition series of $S_k^\lambda$; this multiplicity is independent of the chosen composition series.

Problem. Determine every entry of the decomposition matrix \[ \left(d_{\lambda\mu}(k)\right)_{\lambda\in\mathcal P(n),\ \mu\in\mathcal P_p(n)} \] for symmetric groups over fields of prime characteristic, uniformly for all positive integers $n$, all primes $p$, and all fields $k$ of characteristic $p$.

The source does not explicitly quantify over $n$ and $p$; the uniform quantification above expresses the general determination problem stated there. It imposes no algebraic-closure assumption on the field. The Specht-module indexing makes explicit the customary meaning of decomposition numbers for symmetric groups. The source does not specify a required form of the determination, such as a closed formula or a particular algorithm. Its supplied open status and literature assessment are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

Determine all multiplicities of simple modules in the composition factors of every Specht module for every symmetric group over a field of prime characteristic. The draft uses the standard partition indexing and interprets the request uniformly in the degree and characteristic; the supplied source does not prescribe a formula or computational method.
