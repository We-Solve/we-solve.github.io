Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for the spectral decomposition of $L^2(G({\mathbb{Z}})\backslash G({\mathbb{R}}))$ for a reductive group $G$, including discrete and continuous spectra and their multiplicities. Its formulation is incomplete: defining $G({\mathbb{Z}})$ requires an integral model or equivalent arithmetic data, and the record does not specify the field of definition, the treatment of the center, or a central character.

Conditionally, after specifying an algebraic reductive group over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, an integral model defining a discrete subgroup $\Gamma=G({\mathbb{Z}})\subseteq G({\mathbb{R}})$, and an invariant quotient measure, set \[ H=L^2(\Gamma\backslash G({\mathbb{R}})),\qquad (R(g)f)(\Gamma x)=f(\Gamma xg). \] Here $H$ consists of square-integrable complex-valued functions modulo equality almost everywhere. Let $H_{\mathrm{disc}}$ be the closed span of all irreducible closed $G({\mathbb{R}})$-invariant subspaces of $H$, and let $H_{\mathrm{cont}}=H_{\mathrm{disc}}^{\perp}$. The extracted request is to describe the equivariant decomposition \[ H=H_{\mathrm{disc}}\oplus H_{\mathrm{cont}}, \] identify the irreducible unitary representations occurring in the discrete part and their multiplicities, and specify the measure class and measurable multiplicity function in a direct-integral description of the continuous part. Such a description has the schematic form \[ H_{\mathrm{disc}}\simeq\widehat{\bigoplus}_{\pi\in I_{\mathrm{disc}}}(H_\pi\otimes M_\pi),\qquad H_{\mathrm{cont}}\simeq\int_{\widehat{G({\mathbb{R}})}}^{\oplus}(H_\pi\otimes M_{\mathrm{cont}}(\pi))\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(\pi), \] where $\widehat{G({\mathbb{R}})}$ denotes the unitary dual, $H_\pi$ carries $\pi$, and the multiplicity spaces carry the trivial action. The continuous spectral measure is non-atomic; its measure class and the dimensions of its multiplicity spaces are understood up to the usual almost-everywhere equivalence. This notation specifies the kind of spectral data requested, without asserting a particular decomposition or finite multiplicity under the incomplete original hypotheses.

Short English statement

The extracted question asks which irreducible representations occur in the automorphic $L^2$-space, with what discrete multiplicities and continuous spectral measure and multiplicities. Arithmetic and central hypotheses are missing.
