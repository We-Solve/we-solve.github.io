Math-4460

Virtual Simplicity of Exotic Affine-Building Lattices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An affine building of type $\widetilde A_2$ is a Euclidean building whose apartments have the triangular Coxeter tessellation of type $\widetilde A_2$. A Bruhat--Tits building means an affine building arising from the Bruhat--Tits construction for an algebraic group over a nonarchimedean valued field; the condition below concerns isomorphism as buildings.

A group $H$ is simple if $H\ne\{1\}$ and its only normal subgroups are $\{1\}$ and $H$. A group $\Gamma$ is virtually simple if it contains a simple subgroup of finite index, that is, \[ \exists H\leq\Gamma\quad [\Gamma:H]<\infty,\qquad H\ne\{1\},\qquad \forall N\trianglelefteq H\quad (N=\{1\}\ \lor\ N=H). \] The subgroup $H$ is not required to be normal in $\Gamma$.

The supplied record does not define an $\widetilde A_2$-lattice. Under the usual topological interpretation, when $X$ is locally finite, $\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ has the compact-open topology, and a lattice is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ for which $\operatorname{Aut}(X)/\Gamma$ carries a nonzero finite invariant measure. Cocompactness additionally means that this quotient is compact. The record does not specify whether this interpretation is intended, whether cocompactness is required, or whether a narrower class of actions is intended. These choices are therefore left unresolved.

Conjecture as supplied, with its unresolved domain made explicit. Let $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{source}}$ denote the intended class of pairs $(X,\Gamma)$ consisting of an associated building and an $\widetilde A_2$-lattice acting on it. Is it true that \[ \forall (X,\Gamma)\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{source}},\qquad X\text{ is not a Bruhat--Tits building} \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists H\leq\Gamma\; \left([\Gamma:H]<\infty\ \land\ H\text{ is simple}\right)? \] This records the universal conjecture without resolving the missing definition of $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{source}}$. In particular, the reference to cocompact lattices in the supplied literature assessment is not silently imposed on the conjecture.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every $\widetilde A_2$-lattice whose associated building is not Bruhat--Tits contains a simple subgroup of finite index. The intended class of lattices, including any cocompactness or local finiteness requirements, needs clarification. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
