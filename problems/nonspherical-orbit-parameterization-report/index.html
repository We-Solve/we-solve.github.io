Nonspherical Orbit-Parameterization Report

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$ and a subgroup $K\leq G$, let \[ K\backslash G=\{Kg:g\in G\} \] be the set of left cosets. Given a subgroup $B\leq G$, its right action on this set is \[ (Kg)\cdot b=Kgb\qquad(g\in G,\ b\in B). \] The corresponding orbit set is the double-coset set \[ (K\backslash G)/B=K\backslash G/B =\{KgB:g\in G\}, \] where $g,g'\in G$ represent the same double coset precisely when $g'=kgb$ for some $k\in K$ and $b\in B$.

Recorded research aim, with unresolved hypotheses. For a nonspherical Kac--Moody group $G$ with unitary form $K$, describe the orbit and double-coset structure associated with $K\backslash G$, especially by parametrizing the $B$-orbits, equivalently $K\backslash G/B$, in a manner replacing the spherical description referred to by the source. In the usual terminology, nonspherical means that the associated Weyl group $W$ is infinite. To interpret $B$ as a Borel subgroup requires choosing the intended Borel subgroup of $G$; the supplied statement does not define $B$.

The record does not specify the ground field, the realization or completion of the Kac--Moody group, the involution or other construction defining its unitary form, or the spherical description to be generalized. The formulation above therefore records an aim conditional on these missing choices, rather than a precise conjecture over an established class of groups.

Short English statement

The recorded aim asks how to describe the orbits of a Borel subgroup on the left-coset space of a nonspherical Kac--Moody group modulo its unitary form. These orbits are the double cosets $K\backslash G/B$. Essential hypotheses are missing, and the supplied verification says the originating contribution already states an orbit-parametrization theorem. A precise remaining open problem cannot be recovered from this record alone.
