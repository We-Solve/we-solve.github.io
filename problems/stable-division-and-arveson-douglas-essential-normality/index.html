Stable Division and Arveson–Douglas Essential Normality

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $P={\mathbb{C}}[z_1,\ldots,z_d]$ be the polynomial ring appearing in the record. An ideal $I\subseteq P$ is homogeneous if every homogeneous component of each polynomial in $I$ also belongs to $I$.

The supplied question asks whether essential normality of a compressed coordinate tuple associated with $I$ implies stable division for $I$. The record does not specify the ambient Hilbert space, its norm, the space onto which the coordinates are compressed, or the precise stable division property. These omissions prevent a unique fully specified operator-theoretic statement.

For clarity, one possible precise formulation can be described conditionally. Suppose $H$ is a Hilbert space completion of $P$ in which each coordinate multiplication operator $M_{z_j}$ is bounded. Write \[ \mathcal M_I=\overline{I}^{\,H},\qquad Q_I=H\ominus\mathcal M_I. \] If the intended compression is to the quotient module, its coordinate operators are \[ S_j=P_{Q_I}M_{z_j}|_{Q_I},\qquad 1\leq j\leq d. \] The tuple $(S_1,\ldots,S_d)$ is essentially normal if \[ [S_i^*,S_j]=S_i^*S_j-S_jS_i^*\in\mathcal K(Q_I) \qquad(1\leq i,j\leq d), \] where $\mathcal K(Q_I)$ denotes the compact operators on $Q_I$.

An exact polynomial stable division condition relative to $H$ would require a finite homogeneous generating family $f_1,\ldots,f_r$ for $I$ and a constant $C>0$ such that, for every $f\in I$, there exist $a_1,\ldots,a_r\in P$ satisfying \[ f=\sum_{k=1}^{r}a_kf_k, \qquad \sum_{k=1}^{r}\|a_kf_k\|_H^2\leq C\|f\|_H^2. \] Here the generators and $C$ are independent of $f$. This condition is presented as a candidate interpretation; the supplied record does not identify its intended stable division definition.

Question, subject to the unresolved specifications above. For every homogeneous ideal $I\subseteq P$ in the intended ambient module, does essential normality of its intended compressed coordinate tuple imply its intended stable division property? Under the conditional quotient-module and exact-division interpretations just displayed, this asks whether the compactness conditions imply the displayed uniform division estimate. The choice of $H$, the compression convention, and the division convention must be recovered from the cited source before this conditional formulation can be identified with the source problem.

Short English statement

The question asks whether essential normality of the coordinate operators associated with a homogeneous polynomial ideal forces uniformly controlled polynomial division by a fixed finite generating family. The supplied record leaves the Hilbert space, compression convention, and stable division definition unspecified, so the displayed formulation is conditional and requires human mathematical review.
