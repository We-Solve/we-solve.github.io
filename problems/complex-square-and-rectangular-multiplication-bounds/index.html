In manuscript 1 (family 107; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that characteristic-zero matrix multiplication has square exponent below $2.258$, dual exponent above $0.465$, and rectangular exponent at aspect $0.709$ below $2.092$, with the stated finite-characteristic exceptions.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
In exact complex arithmetic, let $\omega$ be the square matrix-multiplication exponent, $w(k)$ the exponent for multiplying an $n\times n^k$ matrix by an $n^k\times n$ matrix, and $\alpha=\sup\{k:w(k)=2\}$. The claimed bounds are \[ \omega<1129/500=2.258,\qquad\alpha>0.465,\qquad w(0.709)<2.092. \] These are arithmetic exponents, rather than bit-complexity or finite-precision bounds.
