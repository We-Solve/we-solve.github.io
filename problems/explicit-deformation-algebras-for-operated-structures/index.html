Explicit Deformation Algebras for Operated Structures

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An operated algebraic structure here means an algebraic structure equipped with prescribed distinguished operators and relations. The supplied record does not specify the operators, their arities, their relations, the ground ring, or the ambient category. Consequently, it specifies a research programme rather than a single fully determined deformation problem.

In the operadic setting described by the excerpt, let $\mathcal P$ denote the operad encoding the chosen structure and let $A$ be a $\mathcal P$-algebra. These are parameters whose admissible class must still be specified. Write $\operatorname{End}_A$ for the endomorphism operad; in a specified linear setting its arity-$n$ component is $\operatorname{Hom}(A^{\otimes n},A)$. The algebra structure is encoded by an operad morphism $\alpha\colon\mathcal P\to\operatorname{End}_A$.

A differential graded Lie algebra is a graded module $\mathfrak g$ with a degree-$1$ differential $d$, satisfying $d^2=0$, and a degree-$0$ graded Lie bracket for which $d$ is a graded derivation. In cohomological grading, an $L_\infty$-algebra has graded skew-symmetric operations \[ \ell_n\colon\mathfrak g^{\otimes n}\longrightarrow\mathfrak g, \qquad |\ell_n|=2-n,\qquad n\geq 1, \] satisfying the higher Jacobi identities. A differential graded Lie algebra is the special case with $\ell_n=0$ for $n\geq3$. These descriptions do not supply the missing hypotheses on the ground ring needed for a particular deformation-theoretic application.

Requested construction. For the intended operated structures $\mathcal P$ and their algebras $A$, construct explicitly a differential graded Lie algebra or an $L_\infty$-algebra $\mathfrak g_A$ governing deformations of the structure on $A$. An explicit construction should specify the underlying graded object, its differential and bracket or higher brackets, and explain how its deformation data and equivalences correspond to the intended deformations and equivalences of $A$. The record does not determine whether the underlying object is fixed, which operations are allowed to deform, or which notion of equivalence is intended; thus a precise controlling correspondence cannot yet be prescribed.

Also develop homotopy versions of these operated structures, analogous to $A_\infty$-algebras for associative algebras. In an ambient category of operads with a specified model structure, a cofibrant resolution is a weak equivalence \[ q\colon\mathcal Q\longrightarrow\mathcal P \] with $\mathcal Q$ cofibrant. Algebras over such a resolution provide the operadic interpretation of homotopy $\mathcal P$-algebras. The supplied excerpt states that, given a cofibrant resolution, in particular a minimal model when available, one can form the deformation complex and construct its $L_\infty$-structure. The requested work concerns explicit constructions and homotopy versions; neither a particular resolution nor a definition of minimality is supplied. Existence of a minimal model for every possible operated structure is not asserted here.

The supplied dated assessment additionally identifies a general explicit small model as a remaining research target, but does not define the intended class of structures or what counts as small. These qualifications remain unresolved.

Short English statement

Construct explicit differential graded Lie or $L_\infty$-algebras controlling deformations of the intended operated algebraic structures, and describe their homotopy versions using suitable operadic resolutions. The record leaves the operators, relations, base, deformation equivalences, and meaning of a small model unspecified, so this is a qualified research programme requiring further specification. Its partially solved label is supplied dated curation.
