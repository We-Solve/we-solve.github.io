Math-4877

Reducibility and Manifold Realization of Finite Poincaré Complexes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group is a virtual Poincaré-duality group if it has a torsion-free subgroup of finite index that is a Poincaré-duality group. The latter means a group satisfying group-cohomological Poincaré duality, with its orientation module allowed to be nontrivial. A finite Poincaré complex of dimension $d$ is a connected finite CW complex equipped with an orientation local system and a fundamental class inducing Poincaré duality with local coefficients. Write $\widetilde X$ for its universal cover and $\simeq$ for homotopy equivalence.

The Spivak normal fibration $\nu_X$ is the stable spherical fibration associated with the Poincaré duality of $X$. Reducibility requires specifying a bundle category: a vector-bundle reduction is a lift of its classifying map $X\to BG$ through $BO\to BG$, where $BG$ classifies stable spherical fibrations. Piecewise-linear and topological bundle reductions instead use $BPL\to BG$ and $BTOP\to BG$, respectively. The supplied record does not specify which reduction is intended.

Supplied formulation. Let $\Gamma$ be a virtual Poincaré-duality group described in the record as having cohomological dimension $k$, and suppose its Farrell cohomology is periodic of period $n+1$. Let $X$ be a finite Poincaré complex supplied by the referenced construction, satisfying \[ \dim X=n+k,\qquad \pi_1(X)\cong\Gamma,\qquad \widetilde X\simeq S^n. \] The two questions in the clean statement are whether $\nu_X$ is reducible in the intended bundle category, and whether there exists a closed manifold $M$ such that \[ X\simeq M. \] Here a closed manifold is compact and without boundary; its intended category is not specified.

Unresolved quantification and hypotheses. The original wording asks whether $X$ can be constructed with reducible Spivak normal fibre space, and whether such an $X$ can be homotopy equivalent to a closed manifold. This is an existence question about a choice of $X$ supplied by a theorem. The clean wording instead asks about an already chosen resulting $X$. These do not specify the same quantification over possible complexes, so the draft retains both readings. The construction theorem itself is absent, and its full hypotheses and permitted choices cannot be recovered from this record.

The phrase "cohomological dimension $k$" also requires clarification: ordinary integral cohomological dimension and virtual cohomological dimension need not agree for a virtual Poincaré-duality group. The record does not specify which is meant. Likewise, it does not state the coefficient convention for periodic Farrell cohomology, whether $n+1$ is required to be the least positive period, or the parameter restrictions imposed by the referenced theorem. No additional hypotheses or choices of these conventions are asserted here.

Short English statement

For a finite Poincaré complex $X$ arising from the stated group-theoretic construction, ask whether its Spivak normal fibration admits the intended bundle reduction and whether $X$ is homotopy equivalent to a closed manifold. The original source wording asks whether a suitable $X$ can be chosen; the clean statement asks about a resulting $X$. The missing construction theorem, dimension convention, and bundle and manifold categories require clarification.
