Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.42

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be the normalized schlicht class: the injective analytic functions on $\mathbb D$ with $f(0)=0$ and $f'(0)=1$. Since $f(z)/z$ is analytic and zero-free with value one at zero, its analytic logarithm normalized to vanish at zero defines \[ \log\frac{f(z)}z=2\sum_{k=1}^\infty\gamma_k z^k. \] Real coefficients means that every coefficient of $f(z)=z+\sum_{j\geq2}a_jz^j$ is real. A standard close-to-convex convention is existence of a convex univalent function $g$ and $\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that $\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\alpha}f'/g')>0$ in $\mathbb D$.

The displayed Milin conjecture in the source is \[ \forall N\geq1,\qquad \sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{k=1}^{n} k|\gamma_k|^2 \leq\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k. \] Equivalently, \[ \sum_{k=1}^{N}(N+1-k)\left(k|\gamma_k|^2-\frac1k\right)\leq0. \] P. L. Duren's question asks whether the source inequality labeled C6.4 holds for all $N$ if $f$ has real coefficients, or if $f$ is close-to-convex. The excerpt does not attach that label explicitly to a displayed formula; identifying it with the double-sum inequality above is strongly suggested by context but remains a source-label dependency.

The source also reports that starlike functions satisfy $|\gamma_k|\leq1/k$, whereas this fails for general $S$ even in order of magnitude. It attributes to Milin the distinct single-sum estimate \[ \sum_{k=1}^{n}k|\gamma_k|^2\leq\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k+\delta, \qquad\delta<0.312, \] and says its correction cannot be replaced by zero. This is not the weighted double-sum statement and must not be conflated with it. The excerpt reports C6.4 for $N=1,2,3$ and says the double-sum conjecture would imply Robertson's conjecture, referring to Problem 6.39.

The supplied assessment reports that de Branges proved the strong Milin/Lebedev--Milin conjecture as part of the Bieberbach resolution. If C6.4 is the displayed double sum, that full-$S$ result implies its affirmative answer for both subclasses. The assessment nevertheless describes the specific variants as only partly answered; the label attachment and intended remaining variant scope require review rather than silently reconciling those phrases.

Short English statement

The question concerns logarithmic coefficients of normalized univalent functions, especially real-coefficient and close-to-convex subclasses. The source distinguishes a single-sum bound requiring a correction from the stronger weighted Milin inequality. The supplied assessment labels the latter solved by de Branges; this settles both subclass versions if the source label C6.4 denotes that same inequality. The draft retains the unresolved label/scope qualification and attributes all literature claims.
