Math-4750

Meromorphic Ruelle Zeta Functions for Piecewise Hyperbolic Systems

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns piecewise-affine dynamical systems preserving complementary stable and unstable cone fields. Write $T\colon X\to X$ for such a system. Piecewise affine means that, on each element of a specified partition of the phase space, the map is affine in the relevant affine coordinates. The record does not specify the phase space, partition, treatment of partition boundaries, or the precise invariance, complementarity, and expansion or contraction conditions for the cone fields. Consequently, it does not determine the class of systems being quantified over.

For reference, under the unweighted discrete-time periodic-point convention, put \[ \operatorname{Fix}(T^n)=\{x\in X:T^n x=x\},\qquad N_n=\#\operatorname{Fix}(T^n), \] and define \[ \zeta_T(z)=\exp\!\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{N_n}{n}z^n\right). \] This formula defines a holomorphic germ at $0$ if all $N_n$ are finite and the displayed series has positive radius of convergence. These conditions and this particular zeta-function convention are not specified in the supplied record; they are conditional explanations of the notation, not additional source hypotheses.

Requested statement, subject to the unresolved specifications. For each piecewise-affine system preserving complementary stable and unstable cone fields in the class intended by the source report, construct a meromorphic continuation of its Ruelle zeta function and determine domains on which that continuation exists. More precisely, once its defining holomorphic germ on a neighborhood $V$ of $0$ is fixed, seek a connected open domain $U\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ containing $V$ and a meromorphic function $Z_T$ on $U$ satisfying \[ Z_T|_V=\zeta_T|_V. \] The intended continuation should extend beyond the original domain of definition. The supplied record does not specify a target domain, a quantitative lower bound for continuation, or whether maximal domains are sought. Nor does it identify the exact Ruelle zeta convention. A fully quantified formulation therefore requires the missing definitions from the report; the displayed periodic-point formula cannot be asserted to be its intended normalization.

Short English statement

For the piecewise-affine systems with stable and unstable cone fields intended by the report, construct meromorphic continuations of their Ruelle zeta functions and determine where they continue. The supplied record leaves the precise system class, zeta convention, and target domains unresolved. Its partially_solved label is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
