Tamagawa Formula for Connected Linear Algebraic Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A global field is a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$, or a finite extension of ${\mathbb{F}}_q(t)$, where ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ is a finite field. Let $G$ be a connected linear algebraic group over a global field $k$. Write $\mathbf G_m$ for the multiplicative group over $k$, and write $\#S$ for the cardinality of a set $S$. The notation $\tau(G)$ denotes the Tamagawa number, namely the volume of the adelic quotient $G(k)\backslash G(\mathbb A_k)$ with its Tamagawa measure, subject to the normalization and convergence conventions needed for the class of groups under consideration; $\mathbb A_k$ is the adele ring of $k$. Those conventions are not specified in the supplied record.

The supplied question asks whether, for every global field $k$ and every connected linear algebraic $k$-group $G$, one has \[ \tau(G)=\frac{\#\operatorname{Ext}^{1}(G,\mathbf G_m)}{\#X(G)}. \] The record does not define $X(G)$ or specify the category and meaning of $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$. Thus this displayed question preserves the source formulation, but does not yet determine a fully specified mathematical assertion. In particular, the cardinalities in the quotient must be finite, and the denominator nonzero, for the displayed right-hand side to have its usual numerical meaning.

One possible interpretation, requiring confirmation from the cited source, is that $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}(G,\mathbf G_m)$ is the group of equivalence classes of central extensions of algebraic $k$-groups \[ 1\longrightarrow\mathbf G_m\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow1, \] with equivalences inducing the identity on the kernel and quotient, and with Baer sum as group operation. Another possible intended convention is \[ X(G)=\left\{\xi\in H^1(k,G):\xi|_{k_v}\text{ is the distinguished trivial class for every place }v\text{ of }k\right\}, \] where $k_v$ is the completion at $v$, and $H^1$ denotes the pointed set of isomorphism classes of torsors for the faithfully flat, finitely presented topology. For noncommutative $G$, this is a pointed-set condition, not an assertion that $H^1(k,G)$ is a group. These are explicitly provisional interpretations, not definitions established by the supplied record. The meanings of these two invariants, their finiteness, and the Tamagawa-measure conventions require human mathematical review before the universal identity can be treated as a precise statement.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks whether a Tamagawa-number formula holds for every connected linear algebraic group over every global field. Its notation leaves $X(G)$, the extension invariant, and the measure conventions insufficiently specified. The supplied status is partially solved; its dated literature assessment is recorded below without independent verification.
