Unimodular Placing Triangulations after Lattice Dilation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a lattice, meaning a free abelian group of finite rank, and put $M_{{\mathbb{R}}}=M\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}$. A lattice polytope is the convex hull of a nonempty finite subset of $M$. For such a polytope $P$, write \[ r=\dim P,\qquad L_P=M\cap\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{R}}}(P-P),\qquad nP=\{nx:x\in P\},\qquad A_n=nP\cap M. \] A triangulation of $nP$ with vertices in $A_n$ is a finite collection of simplices with vertices in $A_n$, closed under taking faces, whose union is $nP$, and such that the intersection of any two simplices is a common face. It is unimodular if, for every maximal simplex $\operatorname{conv}(v_0,\ldots,v_r)$, the vectors $v_1-v_0,\ldots,v_r-v_0$ form a ${\mathbb{Z}}$-basis of $L_P$. For $r=0$, this uses the empty basis of the zero lattice.

Here a placing triangulation for an ordering $a_1\prec\cdots\prec a_N$ of $A_n$ means the ordinary incremental placing construction. Start with the point $a_1$. At each subsequent point $a_i$, let $Q$ be the convex hull of the preceding points. If $a_i\notin\operatorname{aff}(Q)$, take the cone from $a_i$ over the current triangulation. If $a_i\in\operatorname{aff}(Q)\setminus Q$, retain the current triangulation and adjoin cones from $a_i$ over its triangulated boundary facets strictly visible from $a_i$, together with all their faces. A boundary facet is strictly visible when $a_i$ lies strictly on the opposite side of its supporting hyperplane from the relative interior of $Q$. If $a_i\in Q$, leave the triangulation unchanged. Thus this convention does not require every ordered point to become a vertex.

Proposed statement (catalogue entry). For every lattice $M$ and every lattice polytope $P\subset M_{{\mathbb{R}}}$, there exist an integer $n\geq 1$ and a total ordering $\prec$ of $A_n$ such that the induced placing triangulation $T_{\prec}(A_n)$ is unimodular.

Short English statement

The proposed conjecture asks whether every lattice polytope has a positive integral dilation whose lattice points can be ordered so that incremental placing produces only unimodular maximal simplices. The placing convention and the treatment of lower-dimensional polytopes are explicit above but require source confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
