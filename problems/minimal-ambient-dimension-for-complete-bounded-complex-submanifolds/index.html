Math-6719

Minimal Ambient Dimension for Complete Bounded Complex Submanifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ with $k>1$, and let $N\geq k$ be an integer. A complete bounded embedded complex $k$-dimensional submanifold of ${\mathbb{C}}^N$ means a nonempty complex submanifold $M\subset{\mathbb{C}}^N$ of complex dimension $k$, whose inclusion is a holomorphic embedding, such that \[ \exists R>0\ \forall p\in M,\qquad \|p\|<R, \] and such that the Riemannian metric induced on $M$ by the standard Euclidean metric of ${\mathbb{C}}^N$ is complete on every connected component. Here completeness refers to the intrinsic Riemannian distance, rather than the restriction of the ambient distance. Submanifolds are understood to have no boundary, as in the usual convention; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify this convention or the metric.

Define \[ \mathcal D_k=\{N\in{\mathbb{Z}}:N\geq k\text{ and such a submanifold }M\subset{\mathbb{C}}^N\text{ exists}\}. \] The corrected supplied statement records the existence assertion $2k\in\mathcal D_k$ for every integer $k>1$. Taking that assertion as supplied background, the question is whether \[ \forall k\in{\mathbb{Z}},\quad k>1\ \Longrightarrow\ \min\mathcal D_k=2k. \] Equivalently, for each integer $k>1$, does there fail to exist a complete bounded embedded complex $k$-dimensional submanifold of ${\mathbb{C}}^N$ for every integer $N$ satisfying $k\leq N<2k$?

The original supplied wording makes existence in ${\mathbb{C}}^{2k}$ conditional and does not explicitly state $k>1$; the formulation above follows the corrected statement and retains the original qualification here. Existence and the reported open status are supplied source assertions, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

For each integer $k>1$, the supplied corrected record states that complete bounded embedded complex $k$-dimensional submanifolds exist in ${\mathbb{C}}^{2k}$. The question asks whether any such submanifold can exist in a smaller ambient complex dimension, with completeness understood for the induced Euclidean Riemannian metric.
