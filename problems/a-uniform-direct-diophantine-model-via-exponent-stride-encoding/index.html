A uniform direct Diophantine model via exponent-stride encoding

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime $p$ and put $R={\mathbb{F}}_p[Z]$. Demeyer's Question 4 asks for fixed Diophantine formulas, independent of $q=p^n$, $n\ge1$, defining a domain $S_q$ and its addition/multiplication graphs over $R$, with a bijection $S_q\cong{\mathbb{F}}_q[Z]$. The exact proposed parameter is $t=Z^q$, though another reasonable encoding of $q$ is permitted. Auxiliary primed variables in the source are existentially quantified. The prime is fixed; no uniformity across all primes is requested.

The aid attributes to Demeyer's 2007 theorem that every recursively enumerable finite-arity relation over $R$ is Diophantine, and uses it to report a positive answer even for the exact parameter. Choose computably for each $n$ an irreducible polynomial of degree $n$ over ${\mathbb{F}}_p$, giving a basis $1,u_n,\ldots,u_n^{n-1}$ of ${\mathbb{F}}_{p^n}$. Define the additive bijection \[ e_n:{\mathbb{F}}_{p^n}[Z]\longrightarrow R,\qquad e_n\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}u_n^i a_i(Z)\right) =\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}Z^i a_i(Z^n). \] The coefficient polynomials $a_i$ lie in $R$. Splitting exponents into residue classes modulo $n$ gives the inverse, so the domain can be $S_q=R$, with no quotient equivalence relation. Addition transports to ordinary addition; multiplication need not be ordinary multiplication in $R$.

Consider the single global relation \[ H=\{(Z^{p^n},a,b,c):n\ge1,\quad c=e_n(e_n^{-1}(a)e_n^{-1}(b))\}\subseteq R^4. \] The aid reports it recursive, since the parameter determines $n$ and the finite-field arithmetic and stride operations are effective. Demeyer's theorem yields one polynomial $h(t,a,b,c,\bar w)$ over $R$, with fixed witness length, such that \[ (t,a,b,c)\in H\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists\bar w\in R^{|\bar w|}:h(t,a,b,c,\bar w)=0. \] Together with the identically true domain equation and $a+b-c=0$, these provide fixed $f,g,h$ working at every $t=Z^q$. The summary supplies no explicit expanded polynomial $h$; its existence comes from the named representability theorem.

The aid also records a single-equation packing method valid in characteristic two as well: over $R$, $U^2+ZV^2=0$ forces $U=V=0$, by the opposite parities of the $Z$-adic valuations of nonzero terms. Iteration combines finite systems without relying on ordered-field sums of squares. The source's suggestion that the modeling result might imply r.e. equals Diophantine is historical speculation; this reported construction instead uses the subsequently available equality theorem and does not prove that suggested converse implication.

Short English statement

The aid reports a uniform direct model using all of ${\mathbb{F}}_p[Z]$ as the domain. Stride encoding makes addition ordinary, and the known r.e.-to-Diophantine theorem supplies one parameterized polynomial for all transported multiplication tables. Uniformity is in the finite extension degree for a fixed prime, and no quotient or explicit expanded multiplication polynomial is claimed.
