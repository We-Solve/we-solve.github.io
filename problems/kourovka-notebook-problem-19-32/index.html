Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.32

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p\geq 673$ be a prime and let $r\geq 2$ be an integer. Let $F_r$ be the free group with free generators $a_1,\ldots,a_r$, and put \[ B=B(r,p):=F_r/\langle\!\langle g^p:g\in F_r\rangle\!\rangle, \] where the denominator denotes normal closure. Write $a_1,\ldots,a_r$ also for their images in $B$. Thus $B$ is the free group of rank $r$ in the variety of groups satisfying $y^p=1$.

A one-variable equation over $B$ is an equation $w(a_1,\ldots,a_r,x)=1$, where $w$ is a group word with coefficients in $B$, represented by an element of the free product $B*\langle x\rangle$, with $\langle x\rangle\cong{\mathbb{Z}}$. For $b\in B$, evaluation fixes the coefficients and substitutes $b$ for $x$. Define \[ S_w:=\{b\in B:w(a_1,\ldots,a_r,b)=1\}. \] Interpreting the source's term "non-identical" as meaning that the equation does not hold for every substitution in $B$, the question is whether, for every such $p,r$ and every such word $w$, \[ \left(\exists b\in B\; w(a_1,\ldots,a_r,b)\neq 1\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad |S_w|<\infty. \] The source does not explicitly define "non-identical". The displayed formulation uses the interpretation just stated; whether the intended condition instead refers to nontriviality of a word in a relatively free Burnside group requires confirmation. Formal nontriviality in $B*\langle x\rangle$ alone is a different condition. No uniform bound on the finite number of solutions is requested.

Short English statement

For every prime $p\geq 673$ and integer $r\geq 2$, must every one-variable equation over the free Burnside group $B(r,p)$ that is not satisfied by all elements have only finitely many solutions? This formulation interprets the source's undefined term "non-identical" as failure under at least one substitution; the intended meaning requires review.
