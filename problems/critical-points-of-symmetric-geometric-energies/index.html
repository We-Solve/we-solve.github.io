Short English statement

The problem asks whether symmetry methods for polygonal minimization also describe critical points and polygons with more sides, and whether a suitable Wulff shape minimizes the energy at sufficiently small mass. The supplied excerpt omits the energy, admissible class, mass definition, and anisotropy, so a precise conjecture requires the missing context. The supplied status is partially solved; it is a dated curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
