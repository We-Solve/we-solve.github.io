Math-7157

Coordinate Realization of Characteristic-Polyhedron Multiplicity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(R,\mathfrak m,k)$ be an excellent regular local ring, with residue field $k=R/\mathfrak m$, and let $(u,y)=(u_1,\ldots,u_d,y_1,\ldots,y_r)$ be a regular system of parameters. Write $I=(u_1,\ldots,u_d)R$, and let $\widehat R$ be the $\mathfrak m$-adic completion. For a tuple $a$ in a ring $S$, write $(a)S$ for its generated ideal. Let $f\in R\setminus I$.

Unresolved definition. The record calls $m=m(f)$ a multiplicity defined from the characteristic polyhedron but does not supply that definition or explicitly specify the construction of $\operatorname{in}_0(f)$. The following precise reconstruction is conditional on interpreting these as the order and initial form of the image of $f$ modulo $I$. This interpretation requires source confirmation.

Under this interpretation, put $\overline R=R/I$, let $\overline{\mathfrak m}=\mathfrak m/I$, and denote reduction modulo $I$ by a bar. Define \[ m=\max\{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:\overline f\in\overline{\mathfrak m}^{\,n}\}. \] The classes of $\overline y_1,\ldots,\overline y_r$ identify \[ \operatorname{gr}_{\overline{\mathfrak m}}(\overline R) \cong k[Y_1,\ldots,Y_r]. \] Let $F=\operatorname{in}_0(f)$ be the nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree $m$ corresponding to the class of $\overline f$ in $\overline{\mathfrak m}^{\,m}/\overline{\mathfrak m}^{\,m+1}$. For a $k$-linear subspace $T$ of the degree-one component $k[Y_1,\ldots,Y_r]_1$, let $k[T]$ denote the $k$-subalgebra generated by $T$. Assume \[ \nexists\,T\subsetneq k[Y_1,\ldots,Y_r]_1 \quad\text{such that}\quad F\in k[T]. \] Question, conditional on the interpretation above. For every tuple $\varphi_1,\ldots,\varphi_r\in I\widehat R$, put $\widehat y_j=y_j+\varphi_j$. If \[ f\in\left((\widehat y_1,\ldots,\widehat y_r)\widehat R\right)^m, \] must there exist $z_1,\ldots,z_r\in R$ such that $(u_1,\ldots,u_d,z_1,\ldots,z_r)$ is a regular system of parameters and \[ (z_1,\ldots,z_r)\widehat R =(\widehat y_1,\ldots,\widehat y_r)\widehat R, \qquad f\in\left((z_1,\ldots,z_r)R\right)^m? \] Here ideal powers are ordinary ideal powers, with zeroth power equal to the ambient ring; no additional assumption that $f$ is a nonunit has been inserted.

The supplied original transcription places the shifts in $IR$ and writes the ideal equality over $R$; the supplied corrected statement instead places the shifts and ideal equality in $\widehat R$, as used above. The record attributes this change to restoration of completion notation. These differing formulations must remain distinguishable pending human source review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether formal coordinates that make $f$ belong to the specified power of their ideal can be realized by parameters in $R$ generating exactly the same ideal after completion. The initial form must require all the indicated linear directions. This draft uses an explicitly conditional interpretation of the source's undefined multiplicity and initial form, and requires human mathematical review.
