Math-4672

Eventual Linearity of Homological Invariants in Symmetric Ideal Chains

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks about a sequence of ideals $I_n\subseteq R_n$, where $R_n$ are polynomial rings and the sequence is described as a $\operatorname{Sym}$-invariant chain. The record does not specify the coefficient field, the variables of $R_n$, the transition maps between rings, the symmetric-group actions, or the precise compatibility conditions defining such a chain. Thus the class of chains quantified over below remains dependent on these missing definitions.

For a nonzero module $M$ over a ring $R$, its projective dimension $\operatorname{pd}_R(M)$ is the least length of a projective resolution, or $+\infty$ if no finite projective resolution exists. To explain the regularity notation conditionally, if $R_n$ is a standard graded polynomial ring over a field $k$ and $I_n$ is a nonzero homogeneous ideal, its Castelnuovo--Mumford regularity as an $R_n$-module is \[ \operatorname{reg}_{R_n}(I_n) =\max\{j-i:\operatorname{Tor}^{R_n}_i(I_n,k)_j\ne 0\}. \] Here the subscript $j$ denotes internal degree. The source writes this invariant as $\operatorname{reg}(I_n)$. The standard grading and homogeneity assumptions needed for this interpretation are not explicitly supplied; they require confirmation rather than silent addition to the problem.

Eventual-linearity question, subject to these unresolved specifications. For every $\operatorname{Sym}$-invariant chain $(I_n\subseteq R_n)$ in the intended class, do there exist integers $A,B,C,D$ and an index $N$ such that, for every index $n\ge N$, \[ \operatorname{pd}_{R_n}(R_n/I_n)=An+B, \qquad \operatorname{reg}_{R_n}(I_n)=Cn+D? \] The integers and the threshold may depend on the chain. Here "linear" means affine linear, as specified by the displayed formulas. The source concerns the projective dimension of the quotient and the regularity of the ideal. It supplies no exclusions or conventions for $I_n=0$ or $I_n=R_n$; these cases must be resolved before the question is a fully specified assertion with integer-valued invariants.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every intended symmetric chain of polynomial ideals eventually has affine-linear projective dimension for its quotients and affine-linear regularity for its ideals. The ambient rings, symmetry and chain conditions, grading assumptions, and treatment of zero modules need clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a dated curation label, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
