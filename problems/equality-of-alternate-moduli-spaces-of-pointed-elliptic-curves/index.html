Math-15482

Equality of Alternate Moduli Spaces of Pointed Elliptic Curves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an intended number of markings $n$, let $\mathcal M_{1,n}$ denote the moduli of smooth connected genus-one curves with $n$ distinct labelled marked points. The source names alternate birational models $M_{1,n}(m)$ and $\widetilde M_{1,n}(m)$, with the latter described as obtained from normalization of a reduced stack. It does not give the stability condition, the stack, its coarse-space construction, the parameter range, or a ground field.

Fix $n$, and write $I_n$ for the source's unstated admissible values of $m$. The requested equality is, geometrically, the canonical identification \[ \forall m\in I_n,\qquad M_{1,n}(m)\ \cong\ \widetilde M_{1,n}(m), \] extending the identification on the common smooth pointed-elliptic-curve locus. The record says every $m$, rather than only small values or an unspecified sufficiently large range. It does not ask merely for birational equivalence, which is already part of their description as models of the same moduli problem. Literal set equality would depend on a chosen construction and is not the invariant mathematical target.

For an object $\mathfrak X$, reduction removes nilpotents in its structure sheaf, and normalization, when defined in the relevant category, gives a morphism \[ \nu:(\mathfrak X_{\mathrm{red}})^\nu\longrightarrow\mathfrak X_{\mathrm{red}}. \] If the two named objects were precisely a reduced object and its normalization, the requested identification would amount to this normalization morphism being an isomorphism. The extraction does not identify them sufficiently to make that equivalence unconditional, especially because stacks and coarse moduli spaces may be involved.

The supplied assessment dated 2026-08-21 calls the equality one between an $m$-stable model and a $\operatorname{Proj}$ model, while the original and cleaned statements describe normalization of a reduced stack. No graded ring defining the $\operatorname{Proj}$ model, or theorem relating it to that normalization, is supplied. These descriptions must be reconciled; they are not silently assumed identical. The assessment reports the all-$m$ equality as unresolved, but supplies no complete formulation or independent later-source details.

Short English statement

The question asks whether two birational compactification models of pointed genus-one curves agree for every allowed stability parameter. The constructions and parameter range are absent, and the supplied normalization description must be reconciled with the assessment's description as a Proj model.
