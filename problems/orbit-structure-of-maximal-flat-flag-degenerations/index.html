Orbit Structure of Maximal Flat Flag Degenerations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record names a group $G=\operatorname{Aut}_{Q}(M)$, a space $X=\operatorname{Fl}^{mf}(V)$, and a natural action $G\curvearrowright X$. It does not define $Q$, $M$, $V$, the superscript $mf$, or the action. These symbols are therefore retained as source notation, without assigning unsupported hypotheses.

For a specified action, the orbit of $x\in X$ and the orbit set are \[ G\cdot x=\{g\cdot x:g\in G\},\qquad G\backslash X=\{G\cdot x:x\in X\}. \] The requested problem is to determine whether \[ \lvert G\backslash X\rvert<\infty, \] to give an explicit parametrization $\lambda\mapsto\mathcal O_\lambda$ that is a bijection from a described parameter set $\Lambda$ onto $G\backslash X$, and to determine exactly which pairs $\lambda,\mu\in\Lambda$ satisfy \[ \mathcal O_\lambda\subseteq\overline{\mathcal O_\mu}^{\,X}. \] Here the closure must be taken in the intended topology on $X$, which the supplied record does not specify. The underlying field, permitted objects, flag conditions, and scope of quantification over $Q,M,V$ are also absent. Consequently, this is a precise description of the requested orbit questions conditional on the missing setup, rather than a fully specified mathematical problem.

Short English statement

For the action denoted by $\operatorname{Aut}_{Q}(M)\curvearrowright\operatorname{Fl}^{mf}(V)$, determine whether there are finitely many orbits, describe parameters distinguishing them, and characterize inclusion of one orbit in the closure of another. The supplied record omits the definitions and hypotheses needed to specify the action, flags, and closure topology.
