Sharp Linear-Programming Bounds for Sphere Packing

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 1$, let $B_n=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\|x\|\leq 1\}$, where $\|\cdot\|$ is the Euclidean norm, and let $v_n=\operatorname{vol}(B_n)$. A packing of unit balls has a center set $C\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ satisfying $\|c-c'\|\geq 2$ for distinct $c,c'\in C$. Define its upper density by \[ \overline\delta(C)=\limsup_{R\to\infty}\frac{v_n\,\#(C\cap[-R,R]^n)}{(2R)^n}, \qquad \Delta_n=\sup_C\overline\delta(C). \] Here the supremum ranges over all such center sets.

To make the first question precise in a standard Cohn--Elkies formulation, use the Fourier transform convention \[ \widehat f(y)=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi i\langle x,y\rangle}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] Call a real, even Schwartz function $f:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to{\mathbb{R}}$ admissible if \[ \widehat f(0)>0,\qquad \widehat f(y)\geq 0\quad(y\in{\mathbb{R}}^n),\qquad f(x)\leq 0\quad(\|x\|\geq 2). \] Such a function gives the Cohn--Elkies upper bound $\Delta_n\leq v_n f(0)/\widehat f(0)$.

Question 1. Do there exist a real number $\lambda$ with $0.604<\lambda<0.605$, an integer $n_0$, a real sequence $(\varepsilon_n)_{n\geq n_0}$ tending to zero, and an admissible function $f_n$ in each dimension $n\geq n_0$, such that \[ \Delta_n\leq v_n\frac{f_n(0)}{\widehat f_n(0)}\leq 2^{-(\lambda+\varepsilon_n)n}\qquad(n\geq n_0)? \] This expresses the supplied target $2^{-(\lambda+o(1))n}$ as an upper bound obtainable by the method. It does not assert that $\lambda$ is the exact exponential rate of the optimal Cohn--Elkies bound. The source record does not specify whether that stronger interpretation is intended, nor does it supply a precise admissible function class; the Schwartz formulation above is an explicit conventional interpretation.

For a full-rank lattice $L\subset{\mathbb{R}}^4$, let $\operatorname{covol}(L)$ be the volume of a fundamental parallelepiped and let $m(L)=\min\{\|x\|:x\in L\setminus\{0\}\}$. Define \[ \Delta_4^{\mathrm{lat}}=\sup_{\substack{L\subset{\mathbb{R}}^4\text{ full-rank lattice}\\m(L)\geq 2}}\frac{v_4}{\operatorname{covol}(L)}. \] Question 2. Is the strengthened three-point lattice upper bound referred to in the supplied record sharp in dimension $4$? If $U_4^{\mathrm{str},3}$ denotes the optimal upper-bound value of that particular construction, the intended sharpness question is schematically \[ U_4^{\mathrm{str},3}=\Delta_4^{\mathrm{lat}}\,? \] The record supplies neither the optimization constraints defining this strengthened bound nor its value. Consequently, $U_4^{\mathrm{str},3}$ is only a placeholder for the source-specific construction, and this second question cannot be made fully explicit from the supplied record alone. The original excerpt refers to "it" and "this new bound" without including their antecedent definitions; their identification with the two methods above comes from the supplied clean statement. The supplied curation describes both questions as conjectural and supported by computations, which are not proofs.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether Cohn--Elkies sphere-packing bounds can achieve an asymptotic decay rate $2^{-(\lambda+o(1))n}$ for some $0.604<\lambda<0.605$. The second asks whether a particular strengthened three-point bound equals the optimal lattice packing density in dimension $4$; its defining constraints are missing. Both are labeled open in the supplied dated curation, without independent verification here.
