Lattice Gases and Antiferromagnetic Potts Models — Conjecture 1

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the usual simple, undirected graph convention. Let $G=(V,E)$ be a countably infinite graph with finite maximum degree $\Delta\ge 3$, and let $w=(w_x)_{x\in V}$ be nonnegative real vertex activities. A hard-core configuration is a function $\sigma\in\{0,1\}^{V}$ satisfying $\sigma_x\sigma_y=0$ whenever $\{x,y\}\in E$. Thus the occupied vertices form an independent set.

For a finite set $\Lambda\subset V$ and a hard-core configuration $\eta$ on $V\setminus\Lambda$, let $\mathcal A_\Lambda(\eta)$ consist of the configurations $\xi\in\{0,1\}^{\Lambda}$ whose union with $\eta$ is a hard-core configuration on $G$. Define \[ Z_\Lambda^\eta(w)=\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal A_\Lambda(\eta)}\prod_{\substack{x\in\Lambda\\\xi_x=1}}w_x, \qquad \gamma_\Lambda^\eta(\xi)=\frac{\mathbf 1_{\{\xi\in\mathcal A_\Lambda(\eta)\}}}{Z_\Lambda^\eta(w)}\prod_{\substack{x\in\Lambda\\\xi_x=1}}w_x. \] The empty product is $1$; in particular, $Z_\Lambda^\eta(w)>0$, including when some activities vanish. A Gibbs measure is a probability measure $\mu$ on hard-core configurations, with the sigma-algebra generated by the coordinate maps, such that for every finite $\Lambda\subset V$, its conditional distribution on $\Lambda$, given the configuration $\eta$ outside $\Lambda$, equals $\gamma_\Lambda^\eta$ for $\mu$-almost every outside configuration.

Conjecture 1 (Sokal, 2000; supplied formulation for $\Delta\ge3$). For every such graph $G$ and every activity assignment $w$, if there exists a single $\epsilon>0$ such that \[ 0\le w_x\le (1-\epsilon)\lambda_c(\Delta) \quad\text{for every }x\in V, \qquad \lambda_c(\Delta)=\frac{(\Delta-1)^{\Delta-1}}{(\Delta-2)^\Delta}, \] then the hard-core lattice gas on $G$ has exactly one Gibbs measure. The source also records the asymptotic relation $\lambda_c(\Delta)\sim e/\Delta$ as $\Delta\to\infty$.

The source tentatively asks whether the same uniqueness conclusion holds under the inclusive bound $0\le w_x\le\lambda_c(\Delta)$ for every vertex, corresponding to $\epsilon=0$. This is an additional tentative assertion, not a hypothesis of the main conjecture.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied statement says any countably infinite graph but does not specify graph conventions or restrict $\Delta$. The precise formulation above uses simple, undirected graphs and finite $\Delta\ge3$, where the displayed threshold is unambiguous. Its intended interpretation for degrees $0,1,2$, unbounded degree, or other graph conventions is not supplied.

Short English statement

Sokal's supplied conjecture predicts a unique hard-core Gibbs measure on every countably infinite graph when all vertex activities stay uniformly below the stated degree-dependent threshold. It tentatively includes equality at the threshold. The graph conventions and degree cases outside finite $\Delta\ge3$ require clarification.
