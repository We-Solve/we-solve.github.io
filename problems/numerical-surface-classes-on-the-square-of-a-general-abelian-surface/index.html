Math-12777

Numerical surface classes on the square of a general abelian surface

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

C. Schoen asks to describe the surfaces in $J\times J$ for a general abelian surface $J$, over ${\mathbb{C}}$ for example. A surface means a two-dimensional closed subvariety; possible targets include individual embedded surfaces, Chow classes, numerical classes, or the effective/pseudo-effective cone. The original does not choose among them or specify a polarization type and the meaning of "general." Its surrounding numerals "0," "3" and "1" are unexplained extraction artifacts; no different product or verified source numbering is inferred from them.

The attached unreviewed aid restricts to a very general principally polarized complex abelian surface $(J,\Theta)$. Let $p_i:J\times J\to J$, let $\theta_i=p_i^*[\Theta]$, and fix the Poincaré convention $\lambda=m^*[\Theta]-\theta_1-\theta_2$, where $m$ is addition and a symmetric polarization representative is used. In the real codimension-two numerical cycle space it reports the basis \[ \theta_1^2,\ \theta_1\theta_2,\ \theta_2^2,\ \theta_1\lambda,\ \theta_2\lambda,\ \lambda^2. \] For a class $\alpha$, set \[ (X_1,\ldots,X_6)= (\alpha\theta_1^2,\alpha\theta_1\theta_2,\alpha\theta_2^2, \alpha\theta_1\lambda,\alpha\theta_2\lambda,\alpha\lambda^2), \] where products mean intersection degrees. The aid attributes an exact pseudo-effective cone to Debarre--Ein--Lazarsfeld--Voisin and claims recovery of coefficients by inversion of the intersection matrix. Its proposed test requires \[ 6X_2+X_6\ge2|X_2+X_6| \] and positivity of a specified scaled $3\times3$ matrix. That matrix and the inversion coefficients are only referred to as equations (5.8) of an unavailable report.md; they are not supplied here. Thus the displayed inequality alone is not presented as a complete criterion. Pseudo-effective means closure of the cone generated by effective numerical surface classes.

In the same very-general setting, the aid claims every abelian subgroup surface is the image of $x\mapsto(mx,nx)$ for a primitive integer pair $(m,n)$, with class \[ \frac12(n^2\theta_1+m^2\theta_2-mn\lambda)^2. \] A primitive pair has $\gcd(m,n)=1$; simultaneous sign change gives the same subgroup. Translating such a subgroup gives further embedded surfaces with its numerical class. These numerical and subgroup descriptions do not classify all embedded surfaces or their Chow classes, and do not extend without qualification from very general principally polarized surfaces to the original unspecified family.

Short English statement

Clarify whether describing surfaces in an abelian-square means geometric classification or cycle-cone computation. The aid gives numerical data and subgroup classes only for a very general principally polarized surface; its complete positivity matrix is missing.
