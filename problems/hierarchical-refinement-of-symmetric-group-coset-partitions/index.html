Hierarchical Refinement of Symmetric-Group Coset Partitions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and let $S_n$ be the group of all permutations of $[n]$. Under the pointwise-stabilizer interpretation, for $A\subseteq[n]$ define \[ H_A=\{\sigma\in S_n:\sigma(a)=a\text{ for every }a\in A\}. \] For $0\leq k\leq n$, a $k$-coset is a left coset $gH_A$, where $g\in S_n$ and $|A|=k$; the set $A$ may vary between parts. Equivalently, it is a set \[ C(A,f)=\{\sigma\in S_n:\sigma|_A=f\}, \] where $|A|=k$ and $f:A\to[n]$ is injective. In particular, the unique $0$-coset is $S_n$.

A partition of $S_n$ is a family of pairwise disjoint nonempty subsets whose union is $S_n$. A partition $\mathcal P$ refines a partition $\mathcal Q$ if every member of $\mathcal P$ is contained in some member of $\mathcal Q$.

Question under this interpretation. For every integer $n\geq1$, every integer $t$ with $1\leq t\leq n$, and every partition $\mathcal P$ of $S_n$ into $t$-cosets, does there exist a partition $\mathcal Q$ of $S_n$ into $(t-1)$-cosets such that \[ \forall P\in\mathcal P\quad\exists Q\in\mathcal Q\quad P\subseteq Q? \] Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied statement does not explicitly specify the range of $n,t$, whether the stabilizer is pointwise or setwise, or whether the stabilized set may vary between parts. The formulation above makes the usual pointwise interpretation explicit, with varying stabilized sets and the natural range $1\leq t\leq n$; these choices require confirmation against the source. The case $t=1$ uses the convention for $0$-cosets stated above.

Short English statement

Under the pointwise-stabilizer interpretation, each part consists of all permutations with prescribed images on $t$ distinct points. The question asks whether every such partition can be grouped into parts that each prescribe images on $t-1$ points. The supplied record labels the problem open; its intended parameter range and stabilizer convention require review.
