Math-340

Exact flat-torus Mather quotient and current status

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact connected smooth manifold without boundary, and $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$. Let $L:TM\times\mathbb T\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be $C^\infty$, strictly convex with positive-definite fiber Hessian, uniformly superlinear in velocity, and with complete Euler--Lagrange flow. These make the source's "usual hypotheses of the Mather theory" explicit. Fix $c\in H^1(M;{\mathbb{R}})$, a smooth closed one-form $\eta_c$ representing it, and \[ \alpha(c)=-\inf_\mu\int(L-\eta_c)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu,\qquad L_c=L-\eta_c+\alpha(c), \] where $\mu$ ranges over invariant probability measures with finite action on the time-extended phase space. Thus $\inf_\mu\int L_c\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu=0$.

For each positive integer $N$, define \[ h_c^N(x,y)=\inf_{\substack{\gamma\in AC([0,N],M)\\\gamma(0)=x,\ \gamma(N)=y}} \int_0^N L_c(\gamma(t),\dot\gamma(t),t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t, \qquad h_c(x,y)=\liminf_{N\to\infty}h_c^N(x,y). \] All endpoints are at time zero modulo one. Accordingly take the projected Aubry set in that time slice, \[ \mathcal A_c=\{x\in M:h_c(x,x)=0\},\qquad \rho_c(x,y)=h_c(x,y)+h_c(y,x)\quad(x,y\in\mathcal A_c). \] On $\mathcal A_c$, $\rho_c$ is a pseudometric. Let $x\sim y$ mean $\rho_c(x,y)=0$, and give $\overline{\mathcal A}_c=\mathcal A_c/\!\sim$ the induced metric $\bar\rho_c([x],[y])=\rho_c(x,y)$. John Mather's two questions are whether, for every such $M,L,c$, this quotient is totally disconnected, and whether \[ \dim_H(\overline{\mathcal A}_c,\bar\rho_c)=0. \] Totally disconnected means that every connected component is a singleton. The Hausdorff dimension is for the quotient metric, not for the Aubry set in an ambient Riemannian metric.

The supplied explicit family is $M={\mathbb{R}}^d/{\mathbb{Z}}^d$, $L(x,v,t)=\tfrac12v^{\mathsf T}Av$, with a constant symmetric positive-definite $d\times d$ matrix $A$. Identify $c$ with a constant covector, and put $b=A^{-1}c$. Then \[ \alpha(c)=\tfrac12c^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}c,\qquad L_c=\tfrac12(v-b)^{\mathsf T}A(v-b). \] For lifts $X,Y\in{\mathbb{R}}^d$ of $x,y$, the aid's all-winding formula is \[ h_c^N(x,y)=\frac1{2N}\min_{\ell\in{\mathbb{Z}}^d} (Y-X+\ell-Nb)^{\mathsf T}A(Y-X+\ell-Nb), \qquad 0\le h_c^N(x,y)\le\frac{\lambda_{\max}(A)d}{8N}. \] The bound is uniform in $x,y,c$ and uses no rationality assumption on $b$. Hence $h_c\equiv0$, $\mathcal A_c=M$, and its quotient is a singleton, although the projected Aubry set is the whole torus. Both requested quotient properties hold in this family.

The aid distinguishes known autonomous and low-dimensional results from the still-open arbitrary-dimensional smooth time-periodic questions, but gives no precise theorem ranges or references. No blanket autonomous theorem or solution of the full questions is inferred from the flat kinetic family.

Short English statement

Mather asks whether every smooth time-periodic Aubry quotient is totally disconnected and has zero Hausdorff dimension. The aid's constant-kinetic flat-torus family has an exact $O(1/N)$ action formula: its Aubry set is the whole torus but its quotient is one point, for every cohomology class.
