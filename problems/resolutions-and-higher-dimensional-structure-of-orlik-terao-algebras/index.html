Resolutions and Higher-Dimensional Structure of Orlik–Terao Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for a description of the structure and minimal free resolutions of Orlik--Terao algebras. It does not specify a ground field or all arrangement hypotheses. The following standard central/projective convention makes the notation explicit without resolving those omissions.

Let $k$ be a field, let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $k$-vector space, and let $A=\{H_1,\ldots,H_n\}$ be a finite central hyperplane arrangement with defining nonzero linear forms $\alpha_i\in V^*$, so that $H_i=\ker\alpha_i$. Set $R=k[y_1,\ldots,y_n]$, with $\deg y_i=1$. For each nonzero linear relation $c=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)$ satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^n c_i\alpha_i=0$, write $S(c)=\{i:c_i\ne0\}$ and define \[ \partial(c)=\sum_{i\in S(c)}c_i\prod_{j\in S(c)\setminus\{i\}}y_j. \] The Orlik--Terao ideal and algebra are \[ I_A=(\partial(c):c\ne0,\ \textstyle\sum_i c_i\alpha_i=0)\subseteq R, \qquad \operatorname{OT}(A)=R/I_A. \] For a projective arrangement, this convention uses the corresponding central arrangement of defining linear forms. In particular, the source's notation $A\subseteq\mathbb P^2$ is understood as an arrangement of projective lines, represented by linear forms on $k^3$, rather than as a set of points.

A minimal graded free resolution of $\operatorname{OT}(A)$ over $R$ is an exact sequence \[ \cdots\longrightarrow F_2\xrightarrow{d_2}F_1\xrightarrow{d_1}F_0 \longrightarrow R/I_A\longrightarrow0, \qquad F_p=\bigoplus_{q\in{\mathbb{Z}}}R(-q)^{\beta_{p,q}}, \] whose differentials are homogeneous of degree zero and satisfy $d_p(F_p)\subseteq(y_1,\ldots,y_n)F_{p-1}$ for $p\ge1$. Here $R(-q)$ denotes the grading shift with its generator in degree $q$, and $\beta_{p,q}$ are the graded Betti numbers.

Problem. Describe the phenomena exhibited by $\operatorname{OT}(A)$ in higher dimensions and for reflection arrangements, and determine its minimal graded free resolution over $R$, including in the case of projective line arrangements in $\mathbb P^2$. A reflection arrangement is, in the usual finite linear-group setting, the collection of hyperplanes fixed pointwise by reflections, where a reflection is a nonidentity linear transformation whose fixed subspace has codimension one.

The source does not specify the field, characteristic, class of reflection groups, meaning of "higher dimensions," or particular structural invariants meant by "phenomena." Consequently, the structural part remains a research question rather than a uniquely specified proposition, and the displayed conventions do not assert that every field or arrangement class is intended.

Short English statement

Describe the structure of Orlik--Terao algebras in higher dimensions and for reflection arrangements, and determine their minimal graded free resolutions, even for arrangements of lines in $\mathbb P^2$. The source leaves the ground field, reflection-group class, and precise structural targets unspecified. Its supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
