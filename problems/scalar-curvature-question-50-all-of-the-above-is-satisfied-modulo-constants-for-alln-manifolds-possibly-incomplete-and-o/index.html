Scalar Curvature Question [?50]: All of the above is satisﬁed, modulo constants, for alln-manifolds, possibly incomplete and/or with boundaries

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X^n$ be a Riemannian manifold with $\operatorname{Sc}_X\ge\sigma>0$, allowing incompleteness and boundary and imposing no spin hypothesis in the proposed general result. The source's $X_{-r}$ denotes an $r$-interior. A natural interpretation is points at distance at least $r$ from the ordinary boundary and missing points in the metric completion; the exact boundary and strict/weak distance convention are not supplied.

With the source-specific width, waist, and K-area definitions fixed, the conjectured estimates are \[ \operatorname{width}_{n-1}(X)\le C_1(n)\sigma^{-1/2}, \] \[ \operatorname{width}_{n-2}(X_{-r})\le C_2(n)\sigma^{-1/2} \quad\text{for }r\ge c_2(n)\sigma^{-1/2}, \] \[ \operatorname{waist}_{2}(X_{-r})\le C_3(n)\sigma^{-1} \quad\text{for }r\ge c_3(n)\sigma^{-1/2}, \] \[ \operatorname{Karea}(X_{-r})\le C_4(n)\sigma^{-1} \quad\text{for }r\ge c_4(n)\sigma^{-1/2}. \] For each admissible dimension the positive constants depend only on $n$, not on $X,\sigma,r$, and need not agree with one another or with their optimal values. The original labels mbnd 1, mbnd 2, -bnd 2, and boundary estimate are kept as source labels rather than additional operators.

The source states that its K-area is zero by definition in odd dimensions. For a meaningful dimension-three version of the last estimate, it explicitly replaces that term by \[ \operatorname{Karea}((X^3)_{-r}\times{\mathbb{R}}), \] with the intended product metric. A general odd-dimensional stabilization convention is not otherwise given. Width indices also require an admissible dimension range, absent from the all-n wording.

Among the first three inequalities, the source reports a definite result only for the second in dimension three, via minimal surfaces and reference [54], referring back to section 16. It reports the K-area estimate in the complete spin case from a twisted Dirac index theorem. Crucially it calls the incomplete case problematic even if spin, starting in dimension three; this is not weakened to an issue only for nonspin spaces. The final suggestion to extend the metric to a complete manifold, perhaps on $X\times{\mathbb{R}}^N$, ends mid-question and supplies no completed reduction.

Short English statement

Prove the four stated dimension-only width, waist, and interior K-area bounds under $\operatorname{Sc}\ge\sigma>0$, including incomplete manifolds and boundary. Preserve distinct constants, the odd-dimensional K-area issue, and the limited proved cases.
