Math-4848

Arthur Parameters from Automorphic Descent Integrals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathbb A$ for the adelic ring appearing in the source; its underlying global field is not specified. Let $\sigma$ and $\pi$ be irreducible cuspidal automorphic representations of \[ \operatorname{SO}_{2k}(\mathbb A) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{SO}_{2ab+2k}(\mathbb A), \] respectively, as stated in the supplied record. Denote the source's proposed global Arthur parameter $\psi_{\operatorname{SO}_{2k}}$ by $\Psi$. The notation $\Psi\oplus(\tau,b)$ denotes the formal direct sum with the Arthur summand written $(\tau,b)$ in the source. The superscript $\psi$ in the descent integral is retained separately; the record does not identify it with $\Psi$.

Source question. Suppose that the specified automorphic descent integral satisfies \[ I_{a,k,l}^{\psi}(\tau,\sigma;\pi)\ne 0, \] and impose the parity conditions \[ \begin{cases} b\in 2\mathbb Z,&\text{if $\tau$ is of symplectic type},\\ b\in 2\mathbb Z+1,&\text{if $\tau$ is of orthogonal type}. \end{cases} \] Under the intended hypotheses of the source, is it true that \[ \left[\sigma\text{ has global Arthur parameter }\Psi\right] \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left[\pi\text{ has global Arthur parameter }\Psi\oplus(\tau,b)\right]? \]

Unresolved qualifications. This records the proposed equivalence, but the supplied information does not determine a fully specified conjecture. It omits the global field, the forms of the special orthogonal groups, the domain and hypotheses on $\tau$, the ranges and relations of $a,b,k,l$, the datum $\psi$, and the definition and nonvanishing convention of the integral. The symplectic and orthogonal types therefore cannot be specified from the supplied record alone. The quantification over $\Psi$ and the conventions for global Arthur parameters of even special orthogonal groups also require clarification.

There is a possible dimension discrepancy that must remain unresolved. Under the conventional interpretation that $\tau$ is a representation of $\operatorname{GL}_{d}(\mathbb A)$, the block $(\tau,b)$ has standard dimension $db$. Compatibility with the displayed target group would consequently require \[ 2k+db=2ab+2k. \] The record does not specify $d$. In particular, an interpretation with $d=a$ would generally conflict with the displayed group dimension. Neither the group subscript nor the Arthur summand has been altered here.

Short English statement

The source asks whether nonvanishing of a specified automorphic descent integral forces an exact equivalence between the Arthur parameter of $\sigma$ and the parameter obtained for $\pi$ by adjoining $(\tau,b)$, with the stated parity restriction on $b$. Essential descent data and parameter conventions are missing, and the displayed dimensions may be incompatible under some interpretations of $\tau$. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
