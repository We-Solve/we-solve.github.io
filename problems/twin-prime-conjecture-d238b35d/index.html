Twin Prime Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra ultra Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal P=\{p\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge2}:p\text{ has exactly two positive divisors}\}$. The conjecture is \[ \forall X>0\ \exists p>X:\qquad p\in\mathcal P,\quad p+2\in\mathcal P. \] Equivalently, $\#\{p\le x:p,p+2\in\mathcal P\}\to\infty$. No particular asymptotic formula for this counting function is required.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Are prime pairs separated by exactly two found arbitrarily far along the integers?
