Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.57

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ G=\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{Q}})=\{A\in M_2({\mathbb{Q}}):\det A=1\}. \] Equip $G$ with the Zariski topology induced by the affine algebraic group $\operatorname{SL}_2$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. Thus a subgroup $H\leq G$ is Zariski dense if every polynomial $f\in{\mathbb{Q}}[x_{11},x_{12},x_{21},x_{22}]$ that vanishes on $H$ also vanishes on all of $G$. Say that $H$ has no nontrivial finite quotients if, for every finite group $F$ and every group homomorphism $\varphi:H\to F$, one has $\varphi(H)=\{1_F\}$.

Is it true that, for every subgroup $H\leq G$, \[ \left(H\text{ is Zariski dense in }G\ \text{and }H\text{ has no nontrivial finite quotients}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad H=G? \]

Short English statement

Must every Zariski dense subgroup of $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{Q}})$ with no nontrivial finite quotients equal $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{Q}})$? The supplied registry labels this problem open; that status is not independently verified.
