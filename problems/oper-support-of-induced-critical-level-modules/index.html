Oper Support of Induced Critical-Level Modules

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\mathfrak g_n$, $(\mathfrak h^*/W)_n$, and $\check G$ for the objects denoted by these symbols in the supplied source. Interpret $Z(\mathfrak g_n)$ as the center of the universal enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak g_n)$. For a parameter $\Lambda\in(\mathfrak h^*/W)_n$, let $\chi_\Lambda$ denote the central character intended by the source's parameterization. Saying that the center acts through $\Lambda$ means \[ zv=\chi_\Lambda(z)v \qquad\text{for every }z\in Z(\mathfrak g_n)\text{ and }v\in V. \] Let $M_n(V)$ denote the induced critical-level module referred to in the source, and retain its notation \[ Y_{n,-\Lambda}:=\operatorname{Op}^{\operatorname{ord}_n}_{\check G}(D)_{-\Lambda} \] for the proposed oper subscheme.

Scheme-theoretic support on a specified closed subscheme means that its defining ideal acts by zero through the relevant central action. In an affine presentation with ambient scheme $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, closed subscheme $Y=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$, and an $A$-module $M$, this means $IM=0$, or equivalently that the action factors through $A/I$.

Requested assertion, conditional on the missing source conventions. For every index $n$ in the intended range, every $\Lambda\in(\mathfrak h^*/W)_n$, and every $\mathfrak g_n$-module $V$ whose center acts through $\chi_\Lambda$, the module $M_n(V)$ is scheme-theoretically supported on $Y_{n,-\Lambda}$. Thus, if $I_{n,-\Lambda}$ is the defining ideal of this subscheme in an affine oper-space presentation carrying the specified critical central action, the assertion is \[ I_{n,-\Lambda}\,M_n(V)=0. \] The sign $-\Lambda$ is retained exactly as supplied.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the base field, hypotheses on $\mathfrak g$, the definition or indexing convention for $\mathfrak g_n$, the range of $n$, the meanings of $\mathfrak h$, $W$, and the subscript $n$ on the quotient, or the central-character parameterization. It also omits the induction construction, the critical-level normalization, the relevant central action, the meanings of $D$ and $\operatorname{ord}_n$, and the scheme structure and parameter map defining the fiber labeled $-\Lambda$. Consequently this is a conditional transcription of the intended assertion; a fully specified mathematical formulation requires these conventions.

Short English statement

The source asks whether prescribing the central character $\Lambda$ of a $\mathfrak g_n$-module forces its induced critical-level module to be annihilated by the defining ideal of the oper subscheme labeled $-\Lambda$. Essential constructions and conventions are missing. Supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports the case $n=0$ as a theorem; these claims are not independently verified here.
