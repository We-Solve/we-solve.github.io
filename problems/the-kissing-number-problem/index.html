The Kissing Number Problem

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Write $S^{n-1}=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:\lVert x\rVert=1\}$, with the Euclidean inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$. The kissing number is \[ \tau_n=\max\{|C|:C\subset S^{n-1},\ \langle x,y\rangle\leq\tfrac12\text{ whenever }x\ne y\text{ in }C\}. \] The maximum is finite. Multiplying points of $C$ by $2$ gives centers of disjoint-interior unit balls tangent to the unit ball centered at the origin.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Determine $\tau_n$ for every integer $n\geq1$, that is, determine the maximum number of pairwise nonoverlapping unit balls in $\mathbb R^n$ that can simultaneously touch a central unit ball. [S2]

Short English statement

What is the largest possible number of equal spheres that can touch one central sphere without overlapping?
