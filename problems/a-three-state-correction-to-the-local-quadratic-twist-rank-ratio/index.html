A three-state correction to the local quadratic-twist rank ratio

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Brian Conrey's AIM question compares high-rank quadratic twists according to a local value. The supplied report explicitly reconstructs the setting as follows: fix odd $q$, an elliptic curve $E/{\mathbb{F}}_q(T)$, a finite rational good-reduction place $P=(T-a)$ with $a\in{\mathbb{F}}_q$, and monic squarefree polynomials $f$ of degree $n$. Let $E^f$ be the quadratic twist and let $h(f)$ denote the event $\operatorname{rank}E^f\geq2$. The original PDF does not state these field/place/family assumptions or define ${\mathbb{F}}_q[T]_n$; the rank convention also needs confirmation.

Partition the chosen family $\mathcal S_n$ into $\mathcal S_n^+$, $\mathcal S_n^-$ and $\mathcal S_n^0$ by $f(a)$ being a nonzero square, a nonsquare, or zero. The literally printed ratio is \[ R_n=\frac{\#\{f\in\mathcal S_n^+:h(f)\}} {\#\{f\in\mathcal S_n^-\cup\mathcal S_n^0:h(f)\}}. \] Indeed "not a nonzero square" includes zero, a ramified state. This differs from the cleaned unramified ratio with only $\mathcal S_n^-$ in the denominator. The extracted proposed local constant involves point counts on $E_P$ and its quadratic twist, but the radical/fraction formatting is damaged, so its exact value $C_P$ is not silently reconstructed. The source motivates the comparison by number-field numerical evidence, random matrix theory, moments of $L$-functions and BSD.

The report's unconditional local-counting theorem is \[ A_n:=\#\mathcal S_n^++\#\mathcal S_n^-= \frac{(q-1)(q^n-(-1)^n)}{q+1}, \] \[ \#\mathcal S_n^+-\#\mathcal S_n^-= \begin{cases}0&n\text{ odd},\\1-q&n>0\text{ even}. \end{cases} \] For fixed $q$ as $n\to\infty$, the resulting proportions in the full squarefree family are respectively \[ \frac{q}{2(q+1)},\qquad\frac{q}{2(q+1)},\qquad\frac1{q+1}. \] No high-rank asymptotic follows from these unconditional counts alone.

Put $r_\epsilon(n)=\#\{f\in\mathcal S_n^\epsilon:h(f)\}/\#\mathcal S_n^\epsilon$ for $\epsilon=+,-,0$. Conditionally, if the rates are defined, $r_-(n)>0$ eventually and \[ r_+(n)/r_-(n)\to C,\qquad r_0(n)/r_-(n)\to\lambda \] with finite nonnegative limits, the report gives \[ R_n\longrightarrow\frac{C}{1+2\lambda/q}, \] whereas the cleaned square/nonsquare ratio tends to $C$. If $C=C_P$ and $\lambda=1$, the literal prediction is $qC_P/(q+2)$ rather than $C_P$. The existence and values of these rank-conditioned limits are unproved in the supplied record, which explicitly labels this consequence conditional.

Short English statement

Compare high-rank quadratic twists with square and nonsquare local values. The literal denominator also counts zeros, a positive-density ramified state. The report proves three-state local proportions, but its corrected limiting high-rank ratio remains conditional on unproved relative rank frequencies and on the explicit reconstructed family assumptions.
