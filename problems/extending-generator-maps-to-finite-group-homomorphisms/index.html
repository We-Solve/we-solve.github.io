Math-4982

Extending Generator Maps to Finite Group Homomorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The explicit statement in the supplied registry concerns integral matrix groups. Let $n\geq 1$, let $g_1,\ldots,g_k$ be a finite list of matrices in \[ \operatorname{SL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}})=\{A\in M_n({\mathbb{Z}}):\det A=1\}, \] and put $G=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_k\rangle$. Let $H$ be a finite group with specified generators $h_1,\ldots,h_k$, and consider the prescribed assignment $g_i\mapsto h_i$.

The requested determination is whether there exists a group homomorphism \[ \varphi:G\longrightarrow H \quad\text{such that}\quad \varphi(g_i)=h_i\quad\text{for every }i\in\{1,\ldots,k\}. \] Any such extension is necessarily surjective because the prescribed images generate $H$.

For an integer $m\geq 2$, let \[ \rho_m:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{SL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}}/m{\mathbb{Z}}) \] be reduction of matrix entries modulo $m$. In the standard interpretation used here, an extension factors through a congruence homomorphism if, for some $m\geq2$, there is a homomorphism $\psi_m:\rho_m(G)\to H$ satisfying $\varphi=\psi_m\circ\rho_m$. The requested determination must include potential extensions for which no such factorization exists for any $m\geq2$; it is not restricted to congruence quotients.

There is an unresolved discrepancy in the supplied record: its literature assessment refers to a permutation group and a small finite target group, whereas its original, clean, and displayed statements specify $G\leq\operatorname{SL}(n,{\mathbb{Z}})$ and an arbitrary finite group $H$. The formulation above follows those explicit statements and does not resolve that discrepancy. The record also does not specify an encoding of $H$, a required complexity bound, or a precise algorithmic success criterion beyond determining whether the extension exists.

Short English statement

Given generators of an integral matrix group and prescribed images generating a finite group, determine whether those images extend to a group homomorphism, including extensions that do not factor through reduction modulo any integer. The supplied background describes a different input setting, so the intended computational scope requires review.
