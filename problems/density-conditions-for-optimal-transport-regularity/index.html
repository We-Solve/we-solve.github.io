Math-13969

Density Conditions for Optimal-Transport Regularity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for sufficient density hypotheses for regularity of an optimal transport map when usual geometric hypotheses fail. It does not specify a complete mathematical setting, so the following notation describes the intended question schematically.

Let $X$ and $Y$ denote the unspecified source and target spaces, with reference measures $m_X$ and $m_Y$. Write the source and target measures as \[ {\,\mathrm{d}}\mu=\rho\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m_X,\qquad {\,\mathrm{d}}\nu=\sigma\,{\,\mathrm{d}} m_Y, \] where $\rho$ and $\sigma$ are their nonnegative densities. A measurable transport map $T:X\to Y$ satisfies $T_{\#}\mu=\nu$, meaning \[ \mu(T^{-1}(B))=\nu(B) \] for every measurable target set $B$. Optimality refers to the transport cost and admissible class of the intended problem; neither is specified in the record. The existence or uniqueness of such an optimal map is not asserted here.

Source question. Can hypotheses on $\rho$ and $\sigma$ guarantee regularity of the optimal transport map when standard geometric conditions, such as the Ma--Trudinger--Wang (MTW) condition, fail?

Unresolved scope. The record does not specify the cost, governing equation, dimension, spaces or domains, geometric and boundary hypotheses, density class, or required regularity. It also does not identify the version of the MTW condition or quantify the permitted geometric failures. In particular, it does not distinguish regularity in the interior from regularity up to the boundary, or specify whether a regular representative is sought for an almost-everywhere-defined map. Thus the question is a request to identify sufficient hypotheses in an unspecified setting, rather than a determinate universally quantified conjecture. A precise implication cannot be extracted without additional source information.

Short English statement

Can suitable assumptions on the source and target densities ensure regularity of an optimal transport map when usual geometric assumptions, including an MTW condition, fail? The supplied record leaves both the transport setting and the desired regularity unspecified. Its open label is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical status.
