Math-6396

Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.31

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite group $G$, define its spectrum by \[ \omega(G)=\{\operatorname{ord}(g):g\in G\}, \] where $\operatorname{ord}(g)$ is the least positive integer $n$ such that $g^n=1$. Define \[ h(G)=\left|\left\{[H]_{\cong}: H\text{ is a finite group and }\omega(H)=\omega(G)\right\}\right|, \] where $[H]_{\cong}$ denotes the isomorphism class of $H$. Thus groups are counted up to isomorphism; finiteness of this cardinality is not assumed.

Determine $h(L)$ in each of the following cases:

  1. (a) $L=S_{10}$, the group of all permutations of a set with ten elements.
  2. (b) $L=\operatorname{Aut}(J_2)$, where $J_2$ is the simple sporadic Janko group and $\operatorname{Aut}(J_2)$ is its full automorphism group.
  3. (c) \[ \operatorname{PSL}(2,q)<L\leq\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{PSL}(2,q)). \] Here, for a prime power $q$, ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ denotes a field with $q$ elements and \[ \operatorname{PSL}(2,q)=\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_q)/Z(\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_q)), \] where $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_q)$ is the group of determinant-one matrices and $Z$ denotes the center. The subgroup inclusion uses the identification of $\operatorname{PSL}(2,q)$ with its group of inner automorphisms. The first inclusion is strict and the second permits equality. For every intended value of $q$, determine $h(L)$ for every such intermediate group $L$.
The supplied source does not specify the range of $q$ in part (c). The finite-field notation requires a prime power, but whether the intended range includes $q=2,3$, or is restricted to the simple cases $q\geq4$, remains unresolved. This draft does not choose between those ranges.

Short English statement

Count, up to isomorphism, all finite groups having exactly the same element orders as $S_{10}$, $\operatorname{Aut}(J_2)$, and each group strictly above $\operatorname{PSL}(2,q)$ inside its full automorphism group. The intended range of $q$ requires clarification. The registry labels the problem open; that label and the cited status reference are supplied source information, not independently verified assessments.
