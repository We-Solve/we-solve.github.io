Several Complex Variables Problem List — Problem 15

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $\Omega\Subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ be a pseudoconvex domain with smooth boundary. Here a domain is a connected open set, $\Omega\Subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ means that its closure is compact, and smooth boundary is understood to mean $C^\infty$ boundary. Write $H(\Omega)$ for the space of holomorphic functions on $\Omega$. For a positive integer $k$, let $C^k(\overline\Omega)$ consist of functions whose real partial derivatives of orders at most $k$ on $\Omega$ extend continuously to $\overline\Omega$, with norm \[ \|f\|_{C^k(\overline\Omega)}=\max_{|\alpha|\leq k}\sup_{z\in\overline\Omega}|D^\alpha f(z)|, \qquad \alpha\in{\mathbb{N}}_0^{2n}. \] Set \[ C^\infty(\overline\Omega)=\bigcap_{m\geq 0}C^m(\overline\Omega), \qquad A^k(\overline\Omega)=H(\Omega)\cap C^k(\overline\Omega), \quad k\in\{1,2,\ldots,\infty\}. \] Equip $A^k(\overline\Omega)$ with the induced $C^k$ topology; for $k=\infty$, use the topology defined by all finite-order $C^m$ seminorms.

Is it true that, for every such $n$ and $\Omega$, and every integer $k\geq 1$, \[ \forall f\in A^k(\overline\Omega)\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists g\in A^\infty(\overline\Omega):\quad \|f-g\|_{C^k(\overline\Omega)}<\varepsilon? \] The source also includes $k=\infty$; in that case the requested density is the automatic density of $A^\infty(\overline\Omega)$ in itself. No additional boundary hypothesis beyond smoothness and pseudoconvexity is supplied.

Short English statement

Can every holomorphic function on a smoothly bounded, relatively compact pseudoconvex domain in ${\mathbb{C}}^n$, with derivatives continuous through order $k$ on the closure, be approximated uniformly together with those derivatives by holomorphic functions smooth to every order on the closure? The substantive question concerns finite integers $k\geq 1$.
