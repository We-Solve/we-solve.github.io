Geometry of Singular Monge–Ampère Solution Loci

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns dimensions $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$, $n\geq5$, and the formal equation \[ \det D^2u=1+\sum_{i\in I}a_i\delta_{p_i}, \] where $u$ is convex, $p_i$ are mass locations, $a_i$ are mass sizes, and $\delta_p$ is the unit Dirac measure at $p$. The index set $I$, domain, admissible masses, boundary conditions, and normalization are not specified in the supplied record.

A standard measure interpretation can be described conditionally as follows. For a specified open convex domain $\Omega\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$ and a finite-valued convex function $u:\Omega\to{\mathbb{R}}$, define \[ \partial u(x)=\{q\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:u(y)\geq u(x)+q\cdot(y-x)\text{ for every }y\in\Omega\}, \qquad \partial u(E)=\bigcup_{x\in E}\partial u(x). \] The Aleksandrov Monge--Ampère measure is \[ \operatorname{MA}_u(E)=\mathcal L^n(\partial u(E)) \] for Borel sets $E\subseteq\Omega$, where $\mathcal L^n$ is Lebesgue measure. With this interpretation, the displayed equation means \[ \operatorname{MA}_u=\mathcal L^n\!\restriction_\Omega+\sum_{i\in I}a_i\delta_{p_i}, \] with $p_i\in\Omega$ and with the right-hand side required to define a nonnegative locally finite measure. Here the scalar $1$ in the formal equation represents Lebesgue density one. This interpretation is supplied to explain the notation; the record does not explicitly identify its solution notion or assert that this domain framework is the intended one. It does not specify whether $I$ is finite, whether the points are distinct, or what sign restrictions are imposed on individual masses.

Requested mathematical targets. For the intended class of convex solutions and its unspecified parameter space, the source asks:

  1. For dimensions $n\geq5$, determine whether singular solutions analogous to the lower-dimensional examples referenced in the report exist. The examples and the precise singular structures to be reproduced are not included in the supplied record.
  2. Determine whether the relevant singularities persist under small perturbations of the locations $p_i$ and masses $a_i$. The admissible perturbations, their topology, the choice or existence of corresponding solutions, and the meaning of persistence are unspecified.
  3. Determine the dimension, regularity, connectivity, and other geometric properties of the locus of singular solutions in the source's "solution orbifold," including algebro-geometric conditions that exclude singular structures. The solution orbifold, its equivalence relation, and its geometric structure are not defined in the supplied record.
A spatial singular set of one solution and a locus of singular solutions in a parameter or moduli space are different objects. The original statement explicitly requests the latter, whereas the supplied literature assessment refers to regularity and connectivity of "the singular set." The record does not resolve whether additional spatial singular-set questions are intended, nor does it specify a regularity criterion defining singularity. Consequently these targets cannot be converted into a uniquely quantified conjecture without additional source information.

The supplied status is partially solved. Its dated curation reports that higher-dimensional polyhedral singular structures and stability under perturbation have been proved, while the extracted regularity and connectivity questions for the singular set remain unsettled in general. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified results or a new assessment of which formulations remain open.

Short English statement

For convex Monge--Ampère solutions with Lebesgue density one and Dirac masses, the source asks about singular examples in dimensions $n\geq5$, persistence under perturbing the masses, and the geometry of the singular-solution locus in a purported solution orbifold. The domain, solution class, singularity criterion, perturbation conditions, and orbifold are unspecified. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports progress on polyhedral structures and stability; its precise scope requires review.
