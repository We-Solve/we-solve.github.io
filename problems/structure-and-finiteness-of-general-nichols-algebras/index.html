Math-853

Structure and Finiteness of General Nichols Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field, whose characteristic and other restrictions are not specified in the supplied record. Under the standard invertible-braiding convention, a braided vector space is a pair $(V,c)$, where $V$ is a $k$-vector space and $c:V\otimes_k V\to V\otimes_k V$ is an invertible linear map satisfying \[ (c\otimes\mathrm{id}_V)(\mathrm{id}_V\otimes c)(c\otimes\mathrm{id}_V) = (\mathrm{id}_V\otimes c)(c\otimes\mathrm{id}_V)(\mathrm{id}_V\otimes c) \] on $V^{\otimes 3}$. The record does not specify whether $V$ is finite dimensional.

Equip $T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}V^{\otimes n}$ with its induced braided Hopf algebra structure in which every element of $V$ is primitive. Its Nichols algebra is \[ \mathcal B(V,c)=T(V)/I(V,c), \] where $I(V,c)$ is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{n\geq 2}V^{\otimes n}$. Write $\mathcal B(V)$ when the braiding is understood.

For a unital $k$-algebra $A$, define \[ \operatorname{GKdim}_k A = \sup_{\substack{W\subseteq A,\ \dim_k W<\infty\\1\in W}} \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\dim_k(W^n)}{\log n}, \] where $W^n$ is the linear span of products of $n$ elements of $W$. This definition also applies when $A$ is not finitely generated. An algebra is left noetherian, respectively right noetherian, if every ascending chain of left ideals, respectively right ideals, stabilizes. The source's unqualified use of "noetherian" does not specify which convention is intended.

Problem. For braided vector spaces $(V,c)$ in the intended, but incompletely specified, class, describe the structure of $\mathcal B(V,c)$ and give necessary and sufficient conditions on the braided data for each of the following properties: \[ \dim_k\mathcal B(V,c)<\infty, \qquad \mathcal B(V,c)\text{ is noetherian}, \qquad \operatorname{GKdim}_k\mathcal B(V,c)<\infty. \] The three properties are separate requested characterizations. The source does not define a particular classification format for "describe the structure," fix hypotheses on the field or the dimension of $V$, or resolve the sidedness of noetherianity. Those qualifications remain unresolved; the definitions above do not supply missing source hypotheses.

Short English statement

Describe the Nichols algebra associated with a braided vector space, and characterize separately when it is finite dimensional, noetherian, or of finite Gelfand--Kirillov dimension. The intended field, dimension restrictions, noetherianity convention, and scope of the structural description need clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
