Expanders

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the standard simple-random-walk convention on a finite connected simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$. Write $d_G$ for graph distance, $\deg(x)$ for degree, and \[ m_x=\frac1{\deg(x)}\sum_{y\sim x}\delta_y,\qquad (Pf)(x)=\frac1{\deg(x)}\sum_{y\sim x}f(y). \] With $W_1$ computed from $d_G$, put $\kappa_G(x,y)=1-W_1(m_x,m_y)/d_G(x,y)$ for $x\ne y$. The spectral gap is $1-\lambda_2(P)$, where eigenvalues are ordered by their real value, equivalently the first positive eigenvalue of $I-P$ for the reversible walk.

Does there exist a sequence $(G_j)_{j\geq1}$ and constants $D<\infty$, $\varepsilon>0$ such that, for every $j$, \[ \max_{x\in V(G_j)}\deg(x)\leq D,\qquad 1-\lambda_2(P_j)\geq\varepsilon,\qquad \kappa_{G_j}(x,y)\geq0\quad(x\ne y), \] and $\operatorname{diam}(G_j)\to\infty$? This states bounded-degree expansion and nonnegative coarse Ricci curvature with a common graph-walk convention.

The supplied literature assessment reports a negative answer by Salez (2022). It additionally reports a stronger obstruction allowing vanishing proportions of large degrees, large eigenvalues, and negatively curved edges, and an analogous obstruction under Bakry--Émery $CD(0,\infty)$ curvature. Those refinements are source reports; their full hypotheses and quantifiers are not included and are not asserted here as a separate formal theorem. The original item does not explicitly fix walk laziness or spectral normalization, so these conventions must be matched when applying the cited result.

Short English statement

Can bounded-degree graphs form an expander family of growing diameter while having nonnegative transport-based Ricci curvature? The supplied Salez reference reports that no such family exists.
