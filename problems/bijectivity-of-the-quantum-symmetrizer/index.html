Bijectivity of the Quantum Symmetrizer

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a set and let $r:X\times X\to X\times X$ satisfy the braid form of the set-theoretic Yang--Baxter equation \[ (r\times\mathrm{id}_X)(\mathrm{id}_X\times r)(r\times\mathrm{id}_X) = (\mathrm{id}_X\times r)(r\times\mathrm{id}_X)(\mathrm{id}_X\times r) \quad\text{on }X^3. \] Here involutivity means $r^2=\mathrm{id}_{X\times X}$, and idempotence means $r^2=r$. Bijectivity of $r$ is not imposed in this preliminary definition, since the supplied record explicitly discusses idempotent solutions; the precise intended class of solutions remains unspecified.

The supplied question is: Is the quantum symmetrizer $\operatorname{QS}$ bijective for general set-theoretic Yang--Baxter solutions? The record does not define this map, its domain, its codomain, or any coefficient or grading conventions. Consequently, it does not determine a complete mathematical statement of bijectivity.

To express only the logical form of the intended question, write \[ \operatorname{QS}_{X,r}:D_{X,r}\longrightarrow C_{X,r}, \] where $D_{X,r}$ and $C_{X,r}$ are placeholders for the unspecified source and target of the intended construction. Once that construction and the admissible class of solutions are fixed, the proposed assertion would be \[ \text{for every admissible }(X,r),\qquad \forall c\in C_{X,r}\;\exists!\,d\in D_{X,r} \quad \operatorname{QS}_{X,r}(d)=c. \] This conditional formulation does not supply the missing construction or hypotheses. The supplied dated assessment reports that bijectivity is proved for involutive and idempotent solutions and remains open for general solutions. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether the quantum symmetrizer associated with every solution in the intended general class is bijective. A precise conjecture requires the definition of that map, its source and target, and the admissible hypotheses. The supplied record labels the general case open and reports positive results for involutive and idempotent solutions; these claims require independent review.
