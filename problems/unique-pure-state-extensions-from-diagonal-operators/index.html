Math-1131

Unique Pure State Extensions from Diagonal Operators

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard complex operator-algebra interpretation, let $H$ be an infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space with a fixed orthonormal basis $(e_i)_{i\in I}$. Let $B(H)$ be the unital algebra of all bounded complex-linear operators on $H$, and define its diagonal subalgebra by \[ D=\{T\in B(H): \text{there exists }\lambda\in\ell^\infty(I)\text{ such that }Te_i=\lambda_i e_i\text{ for every }i\in I\}, \] where $\ell^\infty(I)$ denotes the algebra of bounded complex-valued families indexed by $I$.

For a unital complex $C^*$-algebra $A$, a state is a complex-linear functional $\varphi:A\to{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfying $\varphi(a^*a)\geq 0$ for every $a\in A$ and $\varphi(1_A)=1$. A state is pure if it is an extreme point of the convex set of states: whenever $\varphi=t\varphi_1+(1-t)\varphi_2$, where $0<t<1$ and $\varphi_1,\varphi_2$ are states, one has $\varphi_1=\varphi_2=\varphi$. No normality assumption is imposed.

The supplied question, under this interpretation, asks whether for every such $H$, every fixed orthonormal basis, and every pure state $\varphi$ on $D$, there exists exactly one pure state $\psi$ on $B(H)$ such that \[ \psi|_D=\varphi. \] Equivalently, is \[ \left|\{\psi:\psi\text{ is a pure state on }B(H),\ \psi|_D=\varphi\}\right|=1 \] for every pure state $\varphi$ on $D$?

The source wording does not explicitly specify the scalar field, an orthonormal basis, or separability. The complex field and basis above make the conventional meaning of diagonal operators explicit. The unrestricted index set retains the wording's absence of a separability hypothesis; whether the intended question was restricted to separable Hilbert spaces requires source review. The supplied curation describes existence as clear and uniqueness as the open issue. This is a supplied assessment, not a proof or an independently verified current status.

Short English statement

After fixing an orthonormal basis of an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, ask whether every pure state on the diagonal operator algebra has exactly one pure-state extension to all bounded operators. The complex setting is made explicit, and the source leaves separability unspecified. Its supplied open label and claim that existence is clear require independent review.
