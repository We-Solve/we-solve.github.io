Wasserstein Velocities for Boltzmann Curves

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the supplied family $f_t:{\mathbb{R}}^d\to{\mathbb{R}}$, define, whenever these expressions are meaningful, \[ Z_t:=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d}e^{-f_t(x)}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x,\qquad \rho_t(x):=Z_t^{-1}e^{-f_t(x)},\qquad \mu_t:=\rho_t(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] The interpretation as probability densities requires measurability in $x$ and $0<Z_t<\infty$. The source does not specify the time interval, time regularity, moment assumptions, or the meaning of "globally integrable."

Problem as supplied. Find general conditions on the prescribed family $(f_t)$ ensuring the existence of a measurable velocity field $v(t,x)=v_t(x)\in{\mathbb{R}}^d$ satisfying \[ \partial_t\rho_t+\nabla\!\cdot(\rho_t v_t)=0 \] and the intended global integrability requirement. The supplied reconstruction also describes the target as absolute continuity of the associated Fisher--Rao curve in Wasserstein space. The exponent and regularity needed to make this equivalence precise are unresolved in the supplied record.

For clarity, the following gives a conditional precise interpretation, without selecting the missing parameters. Fix a finite interval $I=[a,b]$, $a<b$, and an exponent $1\le p<\infty$. Write \[ \mathcal P_p({\mathbb{R}}^d):=\left\{\mu:\mu\text{ is a Borel probability measure on }{\mathbb{R}}^d,\quad \int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d}|x|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(x)<\infty\right\}, \] and define \[ W_p(\mu,\nu):=\left(\inf_{\pi\in\Pi(\mu,\nu)}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d\times{\mathbb{R}}^d}|x-y|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\pi(x,y)\right)^{1/p}, \] where $\Pi(\mu,\nu)$ is the set of Borel probability measures with marginals $\mu$ and $\nu$. A curve $\mu:I\to\mathcal P_p({\mathbb{R}}^d)$ is absolutely continuous in $W_p$ if there is a nonnegative $g\in L^1(I)$ such that \[ W_p(\mu_s,\mu_t)\le\int_s^t g(r)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} r\qquad(a\le s\le t\le b). \] One possible velocity requirement is \[ \int_a^b\left(\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d}|v_t(x)|^p\rho_t(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\right)^{1/p}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t<\infty, \] together with the distributional continuity equation \[ \int_a^b\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d}\rho_t(x)\left(\partial_t\varphi(t,x)+v_t(x)\cdot\nabla_x\varphi(t,x)\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t=0 \quad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^\infty((a,b)\times{\mathbb{R}}^d). \] Under this interpretation, the question asks for hypotheses on $(f_t)$ ensuring such a field and absolute continuity of the prescribed probability curve, with suitable time representatives and endpoint compatibility. The displayed weak equation alone does not prescribe endpoint values. The source does not determine whether this mixed integrability condition, a finite space--time $p$-action, or another condition is intended. It also does not supply hypotheses making $(\rho_t)$ a Fisher--Rao absolutely continuous curve. Consequently, the claimed equivalence and the definitive formulation require human review; no particular exponent, time horizon, or additional regularity assumption is asserted to be the source's intended choice.

Short English statement

Determine general conditions under which a prescribed family of normalized Boltzmann densities can be transported by an integrable velocity field satisfying the continuity equation, or has the intended absolute continuity in Wasserstein geometry. The supplied formulation leaves the time domain, Wasserstein exponent, integrability requirement, and Fisher--Rao regularity unspecified, so its asserted equivalence requires clarification.
