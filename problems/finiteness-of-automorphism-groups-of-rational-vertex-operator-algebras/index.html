Finiteness of Automorphism Groups of Rational Vertex Operator Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source proposes a conjecture about vertex operator algebras (VOAs). It does not explicitly specify the ground field or the convention for rationality. The following formulation uses the usual complex-VOA and admissible-module conventions; these conventions require confirmation against the source.

Let $V=(V,Y,\mathbf{1},\omega)$ be a vertex operator algebra over ${\mathbb{C}}$, with vacuum $\mathbf{1}$, conformal vector $\omega$, and mode notation \[ Y(a,z)=\sum_{m\in{\mathbb{Z}}}a_m z^{-m-1},\qquad L(0)=\omega_1. \] Being of CFT type means that \[ V=\bigoplus_{n\geq 0}V_n,\qquad V_n=\{v\in V:L(0)v=nv\},\qquad \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}V_n<\infty,\qquad V_0={\mathbb{C}}\mathbf{1}. \] Define \[ C_2(V)=\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{C}}}\{a_{-2}b:a,b\in V\}. \] The algebra is $C_2$-cofinite if $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}(V/C_2(V))<\infty$. Under the convention used here, $V$ is rational if every admissible $V$-module is a direct sum of irreducible admissible modules. Here an admissible module is a weak $V$-module with a grading $M=\bigoplus_{r\geq0}M(r)$ satisfying \[ a_m M(r)\subseteq M(r+n-m-1) \quad(a\in V_n,\ m\in{\mathbb{Z}}), \] where $M(s)=0$ for $s<0$.

The VOA automorphism group is \[ \operatorname{Aut}(V)=\left\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(V): \begin{array}{l} g\mathbf{1}=\mathbf{1},\quad g\omega=\omega,\\ gY(a,z)b=Y(ga,z)gb\quad\text{for all }a,b\in V \end{array}\right\}. \] Conjecture, under the conventions above. For every rational, $C_2$-cofinite vertex operator algebra $V$ of CFT type, \[ V_1=\{0\}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |\operatorname{Aut}(V)|<\infty. \] The supplied statement imposes no separate simplicity or self-contragredience hypothesis; none is added here. Whether the original source implicitly requires either condition remains unresolved. The conjectural designation and the supplied open-status assessment are source labels, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that every rational, $C_2$-cofinite VOA of CFT type whose weight-one subspace is zero has only finitely many automorphisms preserving its vacuum, conformal vector, and vertex operations. The complex ground field and admissible-module meaning of rationality are explicit interpretive conventions requiring source confirmation.
