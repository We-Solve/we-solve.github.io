Math-7001

Complete Surfaces inside the Genus-Four Moduli Space

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal M_4$ denote the moduli space of smooth genus-$4$ complex curves, understood in the usual sense of smooth, connected, projective curves over ${\mathbb{C}}$. In the standard coarse algebraic moduli space interpretation, a closed complex algebraic subvariety $S\subseteq\mathcal M_4$ is called complete if its structure morphism $S\to\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}$ is proper.

Does there exist a closed complex algebraic subvariety $S\subseteq\mathcal M_4$ such that \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}S=2 \qquad\text{and}\qquad S\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}\ \text{is proper}? \] The source imposes no smoothness condition on $S$. Completeness is required inside the moduli space of smooth curves, so a surface lying only in a compactification does not satisfy the question. The supplied record does not explicitly distinguish the coarse moduli space from the moduli stack or specify whether "complex subvariety" is intended algebraically or analytically; the formulation above makes the standard algebraic interpretation explicit.

Short English statement

Does the moduli space of smooth genus-$4$ complex curves contain a complete complex surface? In the algebraic interpretation stated above, the surface must be proper over ${\mathbb{C}}$, must have complex dimension $2$, and need not be smooth.
