Math-4208

Coherence and Finiteness Properties of Surface-Bundle Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group $\Gamma$ is coherent if every finitely generated subgroup of $\Gamma$ is finitely presented. A finite $K(G,1)$ means a connected CW complex with finitely many cells, fundamental group isomorphic to $G$, and contractible universal cover.

For clarity, the following describes the usual integral interpretation of the homological terminology. A group $H$ has type $\mathrm{FP}_m$, for an integer $m\geq 0$, if the trivial ${\mathbb{Z}} H$-module ${\mathbb{Z}}$ admits a projective resolution whose modules in degrees $0,\ldots,m$ are finitely generated. Type $\mathrm{FP}$ means that it admits a finite-length resolution by finitely generated projective ${\mathbb{Z}} H$-modules. This is distinguished from type $\mathrm{FP}_{\infty}$, which requires finite generation in every degree without requiring finite length. An integral Poincaré duality group of dimension $n$, denoted $\mathrm{PD}^n$, is a group $P$ of type $\mathrm{FP}$ such that \[ H^i(P;{\mathbb{Z}} P)\cong \begin{cases} 0,&i\neq n,\\ {\mathbb{Z}},&i=n, \end{cases} \] as abelian groups; the induced action on the degree-$n$ dualizing module may be nontrivial. The source does not specify coefficients, orientability, or the precise meaning of its unindexed $\mathrm{FP}$; these conventions therefore require confirmation against the report.

Consider a surface bundle \[ F\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow B, \qquad \pi=\pi_1(E,e), \] where $B$ is a closed surface and $e\in E$ is a chosen basepoint. Here closed means compact without boundary. The supplied record concerns surface bundles, but its wording does not separately specify whether $F$ is closed, whether the surfaces are connected or orientable, or whether there are genus restrictions. The intended range of bundles must consequently be checked; none of these restrictions is silently imposed here.

The supplied three-part question is:

  1. For every bundle in the intended class, is $\pi$ coherent? Equivalently, does \[ \forall H\leq\pi,\qquad H\text{ finitely generated}\ \Longrightarrow\ H\text{ finitely presented} \] hold?
  2. For every such bundle and every finitely presented subgroup $G\leq\pi$, does $G$ admit a finite $K(G,1)$?
  3. More generally, for every dimension $n$, every $\mathrm{PD}^n$ group $P$, and every subgroup $H\leq P$, does \[ H\text{ of type }\mathrm{FP}_{\lfloor n/2\rfloor} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad H\text{ of type }\mathrm{FP} \] hold? The subscript notation here represents the source's notation $\mathrm{FP}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$. Under the integral interpretation above, $n$ is a nonnegative integer; the source states no narrower dimension range.
These are the recorded questions, subject to the unresolved scope and terminology qualifications above, and are not assertions that the implications hold.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether fundamental groups of the intended surface bundles are coherent and whether all their finitely presented subgroups have finite classifying spaces. It also asks whether a subgroup of a Poincaré duality group with the stated partial homological finiteness property must have type $\mathrm{FP}$. The permitted surfaces and the homological conventions need confirmation. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
