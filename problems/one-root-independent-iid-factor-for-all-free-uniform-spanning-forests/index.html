One root-independent IID factor for all free uniform spanning forests

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The free spanning forest on every infinite locally finite simple graph is claimed obtainable by one root-free Borel rule from iid labels; invariant strongly Rayleigh processes have an analogous group-equivariant construction.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
One Borel root-independent rule $\Phi(G,U,e)\in\{0,1\}$ is claimed to work for every infinite graph that is connected, has finite degrees, and is simple, undirected and unweighted. With independent uniform vertex labels, its selected edge set \[ \{e:\Phi(G,U,e)=1\} \] has the free uniform spanning-forest law and the rule commutes with graph isomorphisms. The second theorem takes any countable group $\Gamma$ and any translation-invariant strongly Rayleigh law $\nu$ on $\{0,1\}^\Gamma$. It supplies a Borel map $\Phi_\nu:[0,1]^\Gamma\to\{0,1\}^\Gamma$, equivariant at every input, whose iid-uniform input has output law $\nu$.

Outline of the argument

Finite determinant tilts define a canonical response drift for the free forest law. Brownian observations provide independent innovations, and a deterministic inverse reconstructs the sampler; vertex labels are converted to independent edge paths in a measurable rule that also applies to varying graphs.
