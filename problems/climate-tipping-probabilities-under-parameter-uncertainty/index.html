Climate Tipping Probabilities under Parameter Uncertainty

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a climate model with uncertain parameters, but specifies neither the model equations nor a class of admissible models. It also does not specify the parameter domain, the uncertainty description, or the meaning of tipping. Consequently, it supplies a research question rather than a fully determined mathematical problem.

For probability notation only, suppose that a complete model specification provides a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$, a measurable parameter space $(\Theta,\mathcal T)$, and a measurable uncertain parameter $\vartheta:\Omega\to\Theta$. Its parameter law is the measure $\mu$ defined by \[ \mu(B)=\mathbb P\left(\vartheta\in B\right),\qquad B\in\mathcal T. \] If the model, initial data, forcing, observation interval, and tipping criterion determine a measurable event $A\in\mathcal F$ meaning that the system tips, then its tipping probability is, by definition, \[ p_{\mathrm{tip}}=\mathbb P(A). \] These objects are a conditional framework for stating the probability question; their existence and particular choices are not supplied by the source. In particular, no distribution of parameter uncertainty is prescribed.

Source question. For the intended climate model with uncertain parameters:

  1. Determine how parameter uncertainty affects the system's bifurcation behaviour.
  2. Identify which parameters contribute most to the tipping dynamics.
  3. Determine the probability that the system tips.
A precise mathematical target requires specification of the dynamical model, admissible parameters and their uncertainty, the bifurcation notion and varied control parameters, the tipping event and time horizon, and a criterion for comparing parameter contributions. The supplied record does not determine these choices or universal quantifiers over a model class. No unique bifurcation object, parameter ranking, or tipping probability is therefore defined by the record alone.

Short English statement

The question asks how uncertain climate-model parameters affect bifurcations, which parameters most influence tipping, and how likely tipping is. The model, uncertainty law, tipping criterion, time horizon, and measure of parameter influence remain unspecified, so a human must supply these before the question has a definite mathematical target.
