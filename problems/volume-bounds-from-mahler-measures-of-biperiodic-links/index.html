Math-7063

Volume Bounds from Mahler Measures of Biperiodic Links

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\widetilde L\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2\times(-1,1)$ denote a biperiodic alternating link: its diagram is invariant under a rank-two lattice $\Gamma$ of planar translations, and overcrossings and undercrossings alternate along each strand. Put $T^2={\mathbb{R}}^2/\Gamma$, and denote the toroidal quotient link by \[ K=\widetilde L/\Gamma\subset T^2\times(-1,1). \] The source calls the biperiodic link $L$ and its toroidally alternating quotient $\Lambda$; thus $K$ denotes the source's $\Lambda$. The additional restriction "semi-regular" is retained as a source-specific qualification: the supplied record gives no definition of this class.

For a nonzero Laurent polynomial $p\in{\mathbb{C}}[z^{\pm1},w^{\pm1}]$, its logarithmic Mahler measure is \[ m(p)=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^2}\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} \log\left|p(e^{i\theta},e^{i\phi})\right|\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\theta\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\phi. \] Here $p(z,w)$ is intended to be the characteristic polynomial of the toroidal dimer model associated with the link diagram. The supplied record does not specify the construction of that model, its weights, or the normalization of its characteristic polynomial.

Conjectural statement, subject to the qualifications below. For every semi-regular biperiodic alternating link $\widetilde L$ and its toroidal quotient $K$, is \[ \operatorname{vol}\!\left((T^2\times(-1,1))\setminus K\right) \leq 2\pi\,m(p(z,w))? \] Here the volume is intended as the volume of a complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric on the quotient complement, when such a metric exists.

Unresolved formulation issues. The supplied formula instead writes \[ \operatorname{vol}\!\left((T^2\times(-1,1))\setminus L\right) \leq 2\pi\,m(p(z,w)), \] although it previously uses $L$ for the biperiodic link and $\Lambda$ for the quotient. The formulation with $K$ above explicitly records the natural quotient interpretation; the supplied evidence does not establish that this is the intended identification. It also does not state the precise semi-regularity conditions, the hypotheses guaranteeing the indicated hyperbolic volume, or the polynomial normalization. In particular, replacing $p$ by $cp$, for $c\ne0$, changes $m(p)$ by $\log|c|$, so an arbitrary scalar normalization cannot be ignored. These missing specifications prevent a fully self-contained unconditional formulation.

Short English statement

The proposed inequality bounds the hyperbolic volume of a toroidal quotient of a semi-regular biperiodic alternating link by $2\pi$ times the logarithmic Mahler measure of its associated dimer polynomial. The quotient notation, precise link class, volume hypotheses, and polynomial normalization require clarification. The record labels the conjecture open; this is a supplied status assessment.
