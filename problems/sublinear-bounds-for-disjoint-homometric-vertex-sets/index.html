Math-6599

Sublinear Bounds for Disjoint Homometric Vertex Sets

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite connected graph $G$, let $V(G)$ be its vertex set and let $d_G(u,v)$ denote the length of a shortest path between $u$ and $v$ in $G$. For $S\subseteq V(G)$, define its multiset of pairwise distances by \[ D_G(S)=\left\{\!\left\{d_G(u,v):\{u,v\}\subseteq S,\ u\ne v\right\}\!\right\}, \] where each unordered pair of distinct vertices contributes once, and equal distances retain their multiplicities. Two subsets $A,B\subseteq V(G)$ are homometric if $D_G(A)=D_G(B)$; they are disjoint if $A\cap B=\varnothing$.

The supplied statement calls $h(n)$ the largest integer with a universal existence property, but does not explicitly connect that integer to the cardinalities of the subsets. The following precise formulation is therefore conditional on the intended requirement being $|A|=|B|=h(n)$. It also explicitly adopts finite simple undirected unweighted graphs and distances measured in the ambient graph $G$; these conventions are not expressly specified in the supplied statement.

Under these conventions, for each integer $n\ge 2$, define \[ \begin{aligned} h(n)=\max\bigl\{k\in\{1,\ldots,\lfloor n/2\rfloor\}:{}&\text{ for every connected graph }G\text{ with }|V(G)|=n,\\ &\text{ there exist }A,B\subseteq V(G)\text{ with }A\cap B=\varnothing,\\ &|A|=|B|=k\text{ and }D_G(A)=D_G(B)\bigr\}. \end{aligned} \] The question is whether \[ h(n)=o(n)\qquad(n\to\infty), \] that is, whether \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\ge 2\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\qquad n\ge N\Longrightarrow h(n)<\varepsilon n. \] Restricting this formulation to $n\ge2$ avoids imposing an unspecified convention concerning empty subsets at $n=1$ and suffices for the asymptotic question. The missing cardinality requirement and the graph and distance conventions require confirmation against the cited source before this conditional formulation is accepted.

Short English statement

Does the largest size of two disjoint homometric vertex subsets guaranteed in every connected $n$-vertex graph grow sublinearly in $n$? Here homometric means having identical multisets of pairwise distances. This draft makes the intended subset size and graph conventions explicit, subject to source confirmation.
