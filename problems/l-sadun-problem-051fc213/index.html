Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Matching rules specify permitted local arrangements of finitely many labeled prototile types. For a type $t$, let $d(t)$ be its number density per unit volume in the resulting tilings. If $R$ is a finite union of tiles, write $N_t(R)$ for the number of its type-$t$ tiles and \[D_t(R)=|N_t(R)-d(t)\operatorname{vol}(R)|.\] Write $|\partial R|$ for $(d-1)$-dimensional boundary measure. Condition (A), low fluctuation, requires a constant $c_t$ for each tile type, uniform over finite regions $R$ in the tilings, such that $D_t(R)\leq c_t|\partial R|$. Condition (B) requires the same bound for every finite patch satisfying the local matching rules, including patches that need not extend to a complete admissible tiling. The densities and constants belong to the chosen rule system, rather than varying with $R$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Find matching rules in dimension $2$ and/or dimension $3$ satisfying both (A) and (B). [S2]

Short English statement

Find local tile rules whose discrepancy stays bounded by boundary size even for every locally admissible finite patch.
