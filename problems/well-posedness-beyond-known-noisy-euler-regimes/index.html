Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb T^2$ denote the two-dimensional torus, and let $\omega$ denote the scalar vorticity in the stochastic two-dimensional incompressible Euler model intended by the supplied source. Write $L^p(\mathbb T^2)$ for the Lebesgue spaces with respect to area measure and $L^\infty(\mathbb T^2)$ for the essentially bounded functions. The record does not specify the stochastic equation, the noise, or the range of exponents in the existence results it describes.

Source-qualified research question. For that intended stochastic Euler model, determine whether one can extend existence to initial vorticities beyond the reportedly treated $L^p$ classes, extend uniqueness from the reportedly treated $L^\infty$ class to appropriate finite $L^p$ classes, and prove a suitable noncollapse property of an associated stochastic flow. The original formulation also asks about further regularization results; this additional target must be retained, although it is absent from the cleaned formulation.

Consequently, no definite quantified existence, uniqueness, noncollapse, or regularization assertion can be extracted without additional source information. Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether results for noisy two-dimensional Euler vorticity on the torus extend to rougher initial data, whether uniqueness extends from $L^\infty$ to finite $L^p$, and whether the stochastic flow avoids collapse. The original wording also asks about regularization. The noise, solution classes, exponent ranges, and desired flow properties are unspecified, so a precise conjecture requires further source clarification.
