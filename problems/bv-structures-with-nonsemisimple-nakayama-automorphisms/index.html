Math-6808

BV Structures with Nonsemisimple Nakayama Automorphisms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{F}}$ be a field of characteristic $0$, and let $h(x)\in{\mathbb{F}}[x]$. Define the first Weyl algebra and the subalgebra appearing in the supplied extract by \[ A_1={\mathbb{F}}\langle x,y\rangle/(yx-xy-1),\qquad A_h={\mathbb{F}}\langle x,h(x)y\rangle\subseteq A_1. \] Here the second expression denotes the unital subalgebra generated by the indicated elements. The extract allows an arbitrary polynomial $h$; no restriction excluding $h=0$ is supplied.

For an associative ${\mathbb{F}}$-algebra $A$, put $A^e=A\otimes_{{\mathbb{F}}}A^{\mathrm{op}}$ and \[ HH^n(A)=\operatorname{Ext}_{A^e}^n(A,A),\qquad HH^\bullet(A)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}HH^n(A). \] Use the canonical cup product $\smile$ and Gerstenhaber bracket $[-,-]_G$, of degrees $0$ and $-1$, respectively. A Batalin--Vilkovisky structure compatible with these operations means an ${\mathbb{F}}$-linear operator \[ \Delta:HH^n(A)\longrightarrow HH^{n-1}(A),\qquad \Delta^2=0, \] where $HH^{-1}(A)=0$, such that, for all homogeneous $a\in HH^p(A)$ and $b\in HH^q(A)$, \[ [a,b]_G=(-1)^p\left(\Delta(a\smile b)-\Delta(a)\smile b-(-1)^p a\smile\Delta(b)\right). \] This fixes a sign convention for the bracket-generating operator.

The supplied reconstructed statement additionally assumes that $A_h$ is twisted Calabi--Yau. In the usual terminology, this means that $A_h$ has a bounded resolution by finitely generated projective $A_h^e$-modules and, for some integer $d\geq0$ and automorphism $\nu$, \[ \operatorname{Ext}_{A_h^e}^{i}(A_h,A_h^e)\cong \begin{cases} 0,&i\ne d,\\ {}_1(A_h)_{\nu},&i=d, \end{cases} \] as bimodules, using the convention that the right action on ${}_1(A_h)_{\nu}$ is $u\cdot a=u\nu(a)$. Such an automorphism is called a Nakayama automorphism.

Question, subject to the qualifications below. For $A_h$ as above, under the reconstructed twisted Calabi--Yau hypothesis and the source condition that its Nakayama automorphism is not semisimple, does $HH^\bullet(A_h)$ admit an operator $\Delta$ satisfying the displayed conditions?

Unresolved qualifications. The extract specifies the Weyl subalgebra family, whereas the reconstructed statement describes $A_h$ only as a twisted Calabi--Yau algebra. It is therefore unclear whether the intended scope is precisely this family or all twisted Calabi--Yau algebras. The extract does not supply the additional twisted Calabi--Yau hypothesis or its applicable range of $h$. It also does not specify the meaning of semisimplicity for the automorphism on this potentially infinite-dimensional algebra, the field over which it is to be tested, or whether the question seeks existence for every qualifying $h$, for some qualifying $h$, or a classification. The displayed question records the conditional existence problem for a qualifying member without resolving those scope issues. Compatibility with the canonical Gerstenhaber structure is the interpretation used here; the extract does not explicitly define its intended BV structure.

Short English statement

For the algebra generated by $x$ and $h(x)y$ inside the first Weyl algebra over a characteristic-zero field, ask whether its Hochschild cohomology has a square-zero operator of degree $-1$ generating the usual Gerstenhaber bracket when the Nakayama automorphism is nonsemisimple. The supplied reconstruction adds a twisted Calabi--Yau assumption. Its scope, the precise semisimplicity condition, and the quantification over $h$ require clarification.
