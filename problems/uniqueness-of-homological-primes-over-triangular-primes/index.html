Uniqueness of Homological Primes over Triangular Primes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks about uniqueness of homological primes over triangular primes, but does not specify size or rigidity hypotheses on the tensor-triangulated category. The following is a precise formulation in the conventional essentially small setting; its intended scope requires confirmation against the source.

Let $\mathcal K$ be an essentially small tensor-triangulated category: a triangulated category with a symmetric monoidal product $\otimes$, unit $\mathbf 1$, and tensor product exact in each variable. A triangular prime is a proper thick tensor ideal $P\subsetneq\mathcal K$ such that \[ x\otimes y\in P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x\in P\ \text{or}\ y\in P. \] Here thick means closed under isomorphisms, shifts, distinguished triangles, and direct summands, and being a tensor ideal means that $x\in P$ implies $x\otimes y\in P$ for every $y\in\mathcal K$.

Write $\operatorname{mod}\mathcal K$ for the category of finitely presented additive functors $\mathcal K^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{Ab}$. Thus an object $M$ admits a presentation \[ \mathcal K(-,a)\longrightarrow\mathcal K(-,b)\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow0 \] for objects $a,b\in\mathcal K$. Let \[ h\colon\mathcal K\longrightarrow\operatorname{mod}\mathcal K, \qquad h(x)=\mathcal K(-,x) \] be the Yoneda functor. Equip $\operatorname{mod}\mathcal K$ with the induced right-exact tensor product, characterized on representable functors by $h(x)\otimes h(y)\cong h(x\otimes y)$.

A Serre tensor ideal $\mathcal B$ is a full subcategory of $\operatorname{mod}\mathcal K$, closed under isomorphisms, subobjects, quotients, and extensions, and satisfying $M\otimes N\in\mathcal B$ whenever $M\in\mathcal B$ and $N\in\operatorname{mod}\mathcal K$. In this formulation, a homological prime means a proper Serre tensor ideal maximal among all proper Serre tensor ideals. Define \[ h^{-1}(\mathcal B)=\{x\in\mathcal K\mid h(x)\in\mathcal B\}. \]

For each triangular prime $P$, set \[ \mathscr H_P=\{\mathcal B\subsetneq\operatorname{mod}\mathcal K\mid \mathcal B\text{ is a maximal proper Serre tensor ideal and }h^{-1}(\mathcal B)=P\}. \] Uniqueness question. For every category $\mathcal K$ in the intended class and every triangular prime $P\subsetneq\mathcal K$, must \[ \mathcal B_1,\mathcal B_2\in\mathscr H_P \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal B_1=\mathcal B_2? \] The supplied wording "is there a unique" can also be read as asking $\lvert\mathscr H_P\rvert=1$, which additionally requires existence. The record does not explicitly distinguish that reading from uniqueness conditional on existence. It also does not specify whether every object of $\mathcal K$ must be rigid, meaning dualizable. These qualifications remain unresolved rather than being asserted as source hypotheses.

Short English statement

Given a triangular prime $P$, can two distinct maximal proper Serre tensor ideals have the same Yoneda preimage $P$? The record asks whether this is impossible. The intended category hypotheses and whether existence is included require clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
