Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Conjecture 32 (Amitsur's Conjecture)

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard interpretation of the central-simple-algebra notation in the supplied source, let $F$ be a field and let $A$ and $B$ be finite-dimensional central simple $F$-algebras. Here central simple means that the algebra has centre $F$ and has no nonzero proper two-sided ideals. Define \[ \deg(A)=\sqrt{\dim_F A},\qquad \deg(B)=\sqrt{\dim_F B}. \] Let $\operatorname{Br}(F)$ denote the Brauer group of finite-dimensional central simple $F$-algebras up to Brauer equivalence, with addition induced by tensor product, and write $[A]$ and $[B]$ for their classes. Thus $\langle[A]\rangle$ denotes the cyclic subgroup generated by $[A]$.

For a central simple $F$-algebra $C$ of degree $n$, let $\operatorname{SB}(C)$ be its Severi--Brauer variety: for every field extension $E/F$, its $E$-points parametrize right ideals of $C\otimes_F E$ having $E$-dimension $n$. Write \[ F(C)=F\left(\operatorname{SB}(C)\right) \] for its function field.

Amitsur's conjecture, as conventionally interpreted from the supplied Conjecture 32. For every field $F$ and every pair $A,B$ of finite-dimensional central simple $F$-algebras, \[ \deg(A)=\deg(B) \quad\text{and}\quad \langle[A]\rangle=\langle[B]\rangle\subseteq\operatorname{Br}(F) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad F(A)\cong_F F(B). \] Here $\cong_F$ means a field isomorphism restricting to the identity on $F$. Equivalently, the corresponding Severi--Brauer varieties are birational over $F$.

The supplied excerpt does not define $F(A)$, explicitly specify the class of algebras or the base-field conditions, or say whether the isomorphism fixes $F$. The statement above makes the standard interpretation explicit; these contextual choices require confirmation against the full source. No characteristic restriction or exclusion of degree $1$ appears in the supplied excerpt. With the definitions above, degree $1$ gives $\operatorname{SB}(F)=\operatorname{Spec}(F)$ and function field $F$. The excerpt calls this a converse of an omitted assertion labelled $(2)$; that assertion is not reconstructed here.

Short English statement

Amitsur's conjecture asks whether central simple algebras of the same degree whose Brauer classes generate the same subgroup have Severi--Brauer varieties birational over their common base field. This uses the standard interpretation of notation omitted from the supplied excerpt. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified assessment.
