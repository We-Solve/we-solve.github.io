Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.101

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source attributes to P. Hall the following definition: a group $G$ is homogeneous if, for every pair of finitely generated subgroups $H_1,H_2\leq G$, every isomorphism $H_1\to H_2$ extends to an automorphism of $G$. It also attributes to Higman--Neumann--Neumann the assertion that every group embeds into a homogeneous group. The question asks for an analogous assertion for representations of groups.

The coefficient system and the morphisms of the intended representation category are not specified in the supplied record. The following is an explicit formulation under the usual interpretation using a fixed coefficient ring and equivariant pairs of maps; these choices require confirmation.

Fix a unital coefficient ring $K$. A representation $(V,G)$ consists of a group $G$ and a unital left $K[G]$-module $V$. A morphism $(f,\varphi):(V,G)\to(W,L)$ consists of a $K$-linear map $f:V\to W$ and a group homomorphism $\varphi:G\to L$ satisfying \[ f(g\cdot v)=\varphi(g)\cdot f(v)\qquad(g\in G,\ v\in V). \] It is an embedding if both maps are injective, and an isomorphism if both are bijective. A subrepresentation $(U,H)$ of $(V,G)$ consists of a subgroup $H\leq G$ and a $K[H]$-submodule $U\subseteq V$. As specified in the source, $(U,H)$ is finitely generated precisely when $H$ is finitely generated as a group and $U$ is finitely generated as a $K[H]$-module.

Under this interpretation, call $(W,L)$ homogeneous if, for any finitely generated subrepresentations $(U_1,H_1)$ and $(U_2,H_2)$, every isomorphism \[ (f,\varphi):(U_1,H_1)\longrightarrow(U_2,H_2) \] extends to an automorphism $(F,\Phi)$ of $(W,L)$, meaning \[ F|_{U_1}=f,\qquad \Phi|_{H_1}=\varphi. \] The proposed representation analogue is the assertion \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every group }G\text{ and every left }K[G]\text{-module }V,\\ \text{there exist a homogeneous representation }(W,L) \text{ and an embedding }(V,G)\hookrightarrow(W,L). \end{gathered} \] No finite-generation assumption is imposed on the input representation. The source does not determine whether $K$ must be a field, what other coefficient rings are allowed, or how the question is quantified over coefficients. Consequently, the displayed assertion is a conditional formalization of the intended categorical analogue, pending resolution of those qualifications.

Short English statement

Does every representation of a group embed into a representation in which every isomorphism between finitely generated subrepresentations extends to an automorphism? Here finite generation requires both a finitely generated group and a finitely generated module over its group algebra. The coefficient system and intended category require clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open and records unverified AI solution claims.
