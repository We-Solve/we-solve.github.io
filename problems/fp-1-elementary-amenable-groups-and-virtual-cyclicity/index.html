Math-7309

$FP_{1}$ Elementary Amenable Groups and Virtual Cyclicity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A group is elementary amenable if it belongs to the smallest class containing all finite groups and all abelian groups and closed under taking subgroups, quotient groups, group extensions, and directed unions. A group $G$ is virtually cyclic if it contains a cyclic subgroup of finite index; finite groups are included in this definition.

Under the ordinary integral group-homological convention, $G$ has type $FP_1$ if the trivial left ${\mathbb{Z}} G$-module ${\mathbb{Z}}$ admits a projective resolution \[ \cdots \longrightarrow P_2\longrightarrow P_1\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow {\mathbb{Z}}\longrightarrow 0 \] in which $P_0$ and $P_1$ are finitely generated. Ordinary type $FP_\infty$ requires every $P_i$ to be finitely generated.

The supplied question, interpreted using this ordinary integral convention, asks whether \[ \forall G,\qquad \left(G\text{ is elementary amenable and has type }FP_1\right) \ \stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}\ \left(\exists C\leq G:\ C\text{ is cyclic and }[G:C]<\infty\right). \] Here $G$ ranges over groups, with no further hypotheses supplied.

Unresolved qualification. The record writes only $FP_1$, while its references and literature assessment raise a possible distinction between ordinary and Bredon homological finiteness. Bredon finiteness requires a specified family of subgroups and an associated orbit category; the record specifies neither. Consequently, the displayed ordinary interpretation is conditional and cannot be identified confidently with the intended Question 8. No Bredon family, coefficient convention for such a formulation, or additional group hypothesis is inferred. The supplied assertion concerning an $FP_\infty$ theorem has the same unresolved finiteness-convention issue.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every elementary amenable group satisfying the intended $FP_1$ finiteness condition contains a cyclic subgroup of finite index. The meaning of $FP_1$ requires review: ordinary integral finiteness is defined above conditionally, but the supplied Bredon reference may indicate a different intended condition. The supplied open-status label and claimed $FP_\infty$ result are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
