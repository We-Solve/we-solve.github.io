Persistence and Permanence of Weakly Reversible Reaction Networks

OpenAI's claim

Manuscript 1 (family 149, 2026-09-25): Every positive trajectory of a finite weakly reversible constant-rate mass-action system is claimed to exist forever and keep all concentrations between positive finite bounds, even in unbounded compatibility classes. Manuscript 2 (family 149, 2026-10-05): Each positive stoichiometric class of a weakly reversible constant-rate mass-action system is claimed to have one compact convex forward-invariant set eventually entered by every positive trajectory.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Take a finite reaction network $\mathcal C\subset\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^d$, $\mathcal R\subset\mathcal C\times\mathcal C$, with positive constant rates $\kappa_{y\to y'}$. Assume weak reversibility: every directed reaction lies on a directed cycle. Its mass-action system is \[ \dot x=\sum_{y\to y'}\kappa_{y\to y'}x^y(y'-y),\qquad x^y=\prod_i x_i^{y_i}. \] OpenAI claims every positive initial point has a global positive solution and a trajectory-dependent $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$ with $\varepsilon\le x_i(t)\le\varepsilon^{-1}$ for all $t\ge0$ and all species $i$. With the extra complex-balance hypothesis (at some positive equilibrium, total inflow equals total outflow at each complex), it records convergence within each positive stoichiometric class to its sole positive equilibrium $x^0+\operatorname{span}\{y'-y\}$ intersected with the positive orthant. The convergence conclusion is not asserted from weak reversibility alone.
Mathematical claim 2.
For a finite weakly reversible mass-action network with fixed positive rates, define its stoichiometric subspace $S=\operatorname{span}\{y'-y\}$ and positive class $P=(x^0+S)\cap\mathbb R_{>0}^d$. OpenAI claims a nonempty compact convex forward-invariant $K_P\subset P$ eventually containing every trajectory from $P$. Each such solution is global and positive, and \[ \exists\varepsilon_P\in(0,1)\ \forall x^0\in P\ \exists T(x^0)<\infty:\quad \varepsilon_P\le x_i(t)\le\varepsilon_P^{-1}\quad(t\ge T(x^0)). \] Weak reversibility means every directed reaction lies on a directed cycle, and the dynamics are $\dot x=\sum\kappa_{y\to y'}x^y(y'-y)$. The bounds depend on the network, rates and class, rather than the initial point; only the entry time may depend on that point.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 149, 2026-09-25): An affine approximation lemma controls exponent pairings separately rather than relying on a single large concentration product. It builds a compact convex polytope inside the positive orthant around each initial condition, with all boundary reaction directions pointing inward; invariance supplies boundedness, persistence, and global existence.

Manuscript 2 (family 149, 2026-10-05): The affine trapping construction is strengthened by strict increase outside a selected trapping set. Connecting such sets across scales removes their dependence on the initial point, giving a common absorbing region and hence concentration bounds uniform over the whole fixed class after trajectory-dependent entry times.
