The chromatic-to-divisibility implication hidden in a source fragment

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The original five-word fragment "Conjecture 3 which in turn implies" is not a standalone conjecture. The attached aid reports its source sentence as "Conjecture 4 implies Conjecture 3 which in turn implies Conjecture 2." The full numbered conjectures are not included here; the aid extracts the following chromatic-to-divisibility implication from that context.

For a finite graph $F$ with at least one edge, let $\omega(F)$ be maximum clique size, $\chi(F)$ chromatic number, and $d(F)$ the least number of parts in a vertex partition such that no part contains a maximum clique of $F$. The aid states the exact rounded bound \[ d(F)\le\left\lceil\frac{\chi(F)}{\omega(F)-1}\right\rceil. \] For triangle-free $F$ with an edge, $\omega(F)=2$ and avoiding a maximum clique means ordinary proper coloring, so equality holds: $d(F)=\chi(F)$.

A graph is $k$-divisible if every induced subgraph $H$ having an edge satisfies $d(H)\le k$. If a hereditary class satisfies $\chi(H)\le f(\omega(H))$, the reported transfer bound is \[ k\le\sup_{\substack{w\ge2\\w\ \mathrm{attained\ as\ a\ clique\ number}}} \left\lceil\frac{f(w)}{w-1}\right\rceil \] in the sense that a finite right side is a sufficient uniform divisibility constant. It need not be the smallest possible class constant. In particular $f(w)=2w-1$ gives $3$-divisibility. Maximum cliques here are distinguished from inclusionwise maximal cliques; the statement concerns ordinary divisibility, not strong clique-color divisibility.

The aid attributes to Chudnovsky--Seymour (2023) a bisimplicial-vertex theorem for even-hole-free graphs. Bisimplicial means the neighborhood is the union of two cliques; hereditary application yields the chromatic bound $\chi(H)\le2\omega(H)-1$ and therefore $3$-divisibility. Odd cycles of length at least five are even-hole-free and have $d=3$, so the constant three is sharp in that class. The aid reports that this makes the historical implication chain unconditional in its even-hole-free setting; exact source labels and bibliographic theorem details remain to be checked.

Short English statement

The record is a fragment of an implication chain. The supplied aid makes a rounded chromatic-to-divisibility transfer precise and applies a reported even-hole-free structural theorem, with odd cycles witnessing the sharp constant three.
