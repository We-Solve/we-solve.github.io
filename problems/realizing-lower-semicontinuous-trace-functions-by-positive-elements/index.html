Realizing Lower Semicontinuous Trace Functions by Positive Elements

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a separable, unital, simple, exact $C^*$-algebra. Let $T(A)$ denote its space of tracial states, equipped with the weak-* topology: these are positive linear functionals $\tau:A\to{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfying $\tau(1_A)=1$ and $\tau(xy)=\tau(yx)$ for all $x,y\in A$. Let $\mathcal K$ be the compact operators on $\ell^2({\mathbb{N}})$, and use the minimal tensor product $A\otimes\mathcal K$. Its positive cone is denoted by $(A\otimes\mathcal K)^+$.

For positive elements $a,b\in A\otimes\mathcal K$, write $a\precsim b$ if there is a sequence $(v_n)$ in $A\otimes\mathcal K$ such that $v_n b v_n^*\to a$ in norm. Cuntz equivalence means comparison in both directions, and $[a]$ denotes the Cuntz equivalence class of $a$. For $\tau\in T(A)$, let $\tau\otimes\operatorname{Tr}$ be the extended positive trace on $A\otimes\mathcal K$, where $\operatorname{Tr}$ is the usual unnormalized trace on $\mathcal K$. Define \[ d_\tau([0])=0,\qquad d_\tau([a])=\lim_{n\to\infty}(\tau\otimes\operatorname{Tr})\!\left((a/\lVert a\rVert)^{1/n}\right)\in[0,\infty] \quad(a\ne0). \]

The source uses $\operatorname{LAff}(T(A))_{++}$ without specifying whether functions must be finite-valued. To make this unresolved convention explicit, for $I=(0,\infty)$ or $I=(0,\infty]$, let \[ \mathcal L_I(T(A))=\{f:T(A)\to I:\ f\text{ is lower semicontinuous and affine}\}. \] Here lower semicontinuity means that $\{\tau:f(\tau)>r\}$ is open for every $r\in{\mathbb{R}}$, and affinity means \[ f(t\tau+(1-t)\sigma)=t f(\tau)+(1-t)f(\sigma) \quad(\tau,\sigma\in T(A),\ 0<t<1), \] using extended-real arithmetic when $I=(0,\infty]$. Thus strict positivity means positivity at every trace. The finite-valued and extended-valued interpretations are recorded separately; the supplied record does not determine which is intended.

Source question. For the intended choice of $I$, is it true that for every separable, unital, simple, exact $C^*$-algebra $A$ and every $f\in\mathcal L_I(T(A))$, there exists $a\in(A\otimes\mathcal K)^+$ such that \[ \forall\tau\in T(A),\qquad d_\tau([a])=f(\tau)? \] The source imposes no explicit infinite-dimensionality, stable-finiteness, or nonempty-trace-space hypothesis. These conditions have not been added. If $T(A)=\varnothing$, the equality quantified over traces is vacuous. Whether any additional restriction on $A$ was intended requires review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every strictly positive lower semicontinuous affine function on the tracial state space of a separable, unital, simple, exact $C^*$-algebra occurs as the trace-rank function of a positive element of its stabilization. The source leaves the allowance of infinite function values and possible additional hypotheses on the algebra unresolved. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not independently verified.
