Existence of Quantum Crystals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
A neutral quantum system has total nuclear charge equal to the number of electrons, in the corresponding charge units. A ground state minimizes the energy of its many-body Hamiltonian. A periodic thermodynamic limit would have particle-density or other specified observables invariant under a full-rank lattice of translations. A limit must specify normalization, boundary conditions and a convergence notion.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Prove that, as the number of nuclei tends to infinity, the ground state of some neutral system of nuclei and electrons approaches a periodic limit, establishing the existence of crystals from quantum principles.
Scope and source qualification.
The existential word some is preserved. The catalogue leaves the system, treatment of nuclei and topology of the periodic limit unspecified; a statement for every material or a prescribed lattice would be stronger.

Short English statement

Prove that a suitable neutral quantum system develops a periodic crystal structure as its size tends to infinity.
