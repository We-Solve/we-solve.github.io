Math-1635

Tensorization of white-noise hyperbolic minimizers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Put $\mathbb T^n={\mathbb{R}}^n/{\mathbb{Z}}^n$. The question attributed to Kostia Khanin concerns a smooth Tonelli Lagrangian $L_0(x,v)$ on $\mathbb T^n\times{\mathbb{R}}^n$, convex and superlinear in $v$ with positive-definite velocity Hessian, formally perturbed by \[ L(x,v,t)=L_0(x,v)+\sum_{i=1}^{N}F_i(x)\dot w_i(t), \] where the $w_i$ are independent Brownian motions. The natural forcing vector is $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_N):\mathbb T^n\to{\mathbb{R}}^N$, assumed to be an embedding. The extract instead writes $F_1,\ldots,F_n$ and an undefined target $M$ for the minimizer; the aid explicitly repairs these to $N$ components and $\gamma:{\mathbb{R}}\to\mathbb T^n$. Since $\dot w_i$ is distributional, the action and random flow require a pathwise stochastic interpretation, not an ordinary smooth time-dependent Lagrangian.

A global minimizer means a curve whose restriction to every finite interval minimizes the pathwise action among curves with the same endpoints. The source states almost-sure uniqueness and conjectures almost-sure hyperbolicity of this minimizer; it attributes the one-dimensional result to E--Khanin--Mazel--Sinai. It does not fully specify cohomology/tilt, action convention, flow regularity, or whether hyperbolicity means a nonuniform Lyapunov splitting or a uniform exponential dichotomy.

The aid's special case assumes a separable system \[ L_0(x,v)=\sum_{j=1}^n\ell_j(x_j,v_j),\qquad \sum_iF_i(x)\dot w_i(t)= \sum_{j=1}^n\sum_{k=1}^{N_j}f_{j,k}(x_j)\dot w_{j,k}(t), \quad N=\sum_jN_j. \] Assume every one-dimensional factor has, almost surely, a unique global minimizer $\gamma_j$ and a derivative cocycle with Lyapunov exponents $-\lambda_j,\lambda_j$, $\lambda_j>0$, under the requisite integrability hypotheses. Then the reported product result is that the global minimizer is uniquely $\gamma=(\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_n)$ and \[ D\Phi_t=\bigoplus_{j=1}^nD\Phi_{j,t},\qquad \operatorname{Lyap}(D\Phi)=\{-\lambda_1,\lambda_1,\ldots,-\lambda_n,\lambda_n\} \] with multiplicities. In particular its gap from zero is exactly $\min_j\lambda_j>0$. The assertions hold on the intersection of the finitely many factor probability-one events. If each map $(f_{j,1},\ldots,f_{j,N_j}):\mathbb T\to{\mathbb{R}}^{N_j}$ is an embedding, their coordinate-block product is an embedding of $\mathbb T^n$, preserving the stated forcing hypothesis.

The action splitting and block cocycle justify this separable higher-dimensional case. They do not address general coupled $L_0$ or forcing. The spectral-gap statement concerns Lyapunov hyperbolicity; a stronger uniform-in-time dichotomy would require corresponding factor assumptions and estimates.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the unique random action minimizer is almost surely hyperbolic in higher dimensions. The supplied result handles independent coordinate blocks: minimizers multiply, Lyapunov spectra combine, and the smallest positive factor exponent is the product gap. Coupled systems remain outside this argument.
