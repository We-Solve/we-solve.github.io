Inclusions and Approximability in Operator Ideals

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks two questions about operator ideals denoted by $L_q^{\mathrm{hilb}}$, $L_p^{\mathrm{app}}$, and $L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}$. It does not define these ideals, specify their underlying number sequences or membership conditions, give the admissible exponent range, or identify the intended class of spaces. These omissions prevent a fully determined mathematical statement.

For clarity, the standard Banach-space meaning of approximability is as follows. For Banach spaces $E,F$ over a fixed scalar field, let $\mathcal L(E,F)$ be the bounded linear operators and let $\mathcal F(E,F)$ be those with finite-dimensional range. Define \[ \mathcal A(E,F)=\overline{\mathcal F(E,F)}^{\|\cdot\|}. \] Thus $T$ is approximable precisely when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $S\in\mathcal F(E,F)$ with $\|T-S\|<\varepsilon$. This definition states a conventional interpretation; the supplied record does not confirm the ambient Banach-space setting, scalar field, or intended meaning of approximability.

To retain the source quantifiers without supplying missing hypotheses, let $D$ stand for the unspecified admissible exponent set and let $\mathscr C$ stand for the unspecified class of ordered pairs of spaces. These are placeholders requiring source clarification. With the three ideal symbols retaining their source meanings, the first question is whether \[ \forall p,q\in D,\qquad \frac1q\geq\frac1p+1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \forall(E,F)\in\mathscr C,\quad L_q^{\mathrm{hilb}}(E,F)\subseteq L_p^{\mathrm{app}}(E,F). \] The equality case $1/q=1/p+1$ is explicitly included. Any convention for infinite exponents must be specified before interpreting this formula at such endpoints.

The second question is to determine all admissible $p$ such that every operator in $L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}$ is approximable. Under the Banach-space interpretation above, its schematic target is the set \[ \left\{p\in D:\ \forall(E,F)\in\mathscr C\ \forall T\in L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}(E,F),\quad T\in\mathcal A(E,F)\right\}. \] Neither formula resolves the missing definitions or hypotheses. In particular, the superscripts alone do not specify the operator ideals or their sequence summability requirements.

Short English statement

The source asks whether $L_q^{\mathrm{hilb}}\subseteq L_p^{\mathrm{app}}$ whenever $1/q\geq1/p+1$, including equality, and which exponents $p$ make every operator in $L_p^{\mathrm{ent}}$ approximable. The ideal definitions, exponent ranges, and ambient spaces require clarification. The displayed statements preserve these questions schematically and require human mathematical review.
