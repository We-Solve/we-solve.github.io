Intrinsic-Flat Stability of the Positive Mass Theorem

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M_j,g_j)$, $j\in{\mathbb{N}}$, be a sequence of asymptotically flat Riemannian three-manifolds. Write $R_{g_j}$ for scalar curvature and $m_{\mathrm{ADM}}(M_j,g_j)$ for the ADM mass of the asymptotically flat end under consideration. The source does not specify the number of ends, the end used to compute mass, or the regularity and decay assumptions included in asymptotic flatness. A closed minimal surface means a compact surface without boundary whose mean curvature vanishes, lying in $\operatorname{Int}(M_j)$; the permitted class of such surfaces is not further specified.

A surface $\Sigma_j\subset M_j$ has constant mean curvature (CMC) if its mean curvature is constant on the surface. Its area is computed using the metric induced by $g_j$. The natural cross-sequence reading of "constant area" is that there is a fixed $A>0$ such that \[ \operatorname{Area}_{g_j}(\Sigma_j)=A\qquad\text{for every }j. \] The constant mean curvature itself is not asserted to be independent of $j$.

For oriented integral current spaces, intrinsic-flat distance is defined by taking the infimum of the flat distance between their currents after distance-preserving embeddings into a common complete metric space. Pointed intrinsic-flat convergence is the corresponding local convergence about specified base points. The source does not specify a precise pointed convergence convention or the orientation and local integral current hypotheses needed for this formulation.

Conjectural target. Suppose that \[ R_{g_j}\geq 0,\qquad \operatorname{Int}(M_j)\text{ contains no closed minimal surface},\qquad m_{\mathrm{ADM}}(M_j,g_j)\longrightarrow 0. \] Choose CMC surfaces $\Sigma_j\subset M_j$ with the same fixed area $A>0$, and base points $p_j\in\Sigma_j$. The proposed conclusion is \[ (M_j,d_{g_j},p_j)\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{pointed\ intrinsic\ flat}\ } ({\mathbb{R}}^3,d_{\mathrm{Eucl}},0), \] with the associated current structures understood when defined.

Unresolved qualifications. This displays the intended implication, but the supplied record does not determine whether it is asserted for every admissible choice of $\Sigma_j$ and $p_j$, or for a suitably selected sequence of such surfaces and points. It also does not specify whether the surfaces must be closed, embedded, connected, stable, or enclosing a distinguished region, or whether their existence is an additional hypothesis. Completeness, boundary conditions, orientation, connectedness, and the precise asymptotic hypotheses are unstated. These omissions prevent a uniquely specified, fully quantified formulation; no additional hypotheses or selection rule are imposed here.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether asymptotically flat three-manifolds with nonnegative scalar curvature, no interior closed minimal surfaces, and ADM masses tending to zero approach Euclidean three-space in pointed intrinsic-flat convergence, with base points on CMC surfaces of one fixed area. The admissible surfaces, selection of base points, and several geometric and convergence hypotheses remain unspecified in the supplied record.
