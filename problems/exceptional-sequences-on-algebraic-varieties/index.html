Exceptional Sequences on Algebraic Varieties

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source leaves the ground field and some categorical conventions unspecified. To express its questions, let $k$ denote a ground field, without imposing a characteristic or algebraic-closure assumption, and let $X$ be an algebraic variety over $k$. Write $D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)$ for the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves on $X$, and regard coherent sheaves as complexes concentrated in degree zero.

A coherent sheaf $E$ is exceptional over $k$ if \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)}(E,E)\cong k, \qquad \operatorname{Hom}_{D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)}(E,E[q])=0 \quad(q\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}). \] Here the first isomorphism is an isomorphism of $k$-algebras. A finite nonempty ordered sequence $(E_1,\ldots,E_m)$ of coherent sheaves is exceptional if every $E_i$ is exceptional and \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)}(E_j,E_i[q])=0 \quad\text{for every }1\leq i<j\leq m\text{ and every }q\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] It is strongly exceptional if, in addition, \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X)}(E_i,E_j[q])=0 \quad\text{for every }1\leq i,j\leq m\text{ and every }q\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\}. \] It is full if \[ \operatorname{thick}(E_1,\ldots,E_m)=D^b(\operatorname{Coh}X), \] where $\operatorname{thick}$ denotes the smallest full triangulated subcategory containing these objects and closed under direct summands. An invertible sheaf is a locally free coherent sheaf of rank one.

Source questions. Does every algebraic variety $X$ admit a full exceptional sequence of coherent sheaves? Does every such $X$ admit a strongly exceptional sequence of coherent sheaves? In particular, for every smooth complete rational surface $X$, do the corresponding sequences exist with every $E_i$ invertible?

Unresolved qualifications. The source phrase "a full exceptional sequence, or a strongly exceptional sequence" does not specify whether the strongly exceptional sequence must also be full. Thus the existence of a strongly exceptional sequence and the existence of a full strongly exceptional sequence must be distinguished; the latter requires both conditions defined above. No required length is supplied for a sequence that is not full. The questions above preserve these alternatives rather than selecting an intended one. The ground field, the precise convention for "variety," and the ambient derived category are not specified by the record; the displayed definitions give an explicit conventional interpretation for review, not verified source conventions. Over a specified field $k$, $k$-rationality of a surface means birationality over $k$ to ${\mathbb{P}}^2_k$, while geometric rationality means rationality after extension to an algebraic closure. The record does not distinguish these notions. Completeness is retained as stated, and no additional projectivity hypothesis is imposed.

Short English statement

The problem asks about full exceptional sequences and strongly exceptional sequences of coherent sheaves on algebraic varieties, with a special question about sequences of line bundles on smooth complete rational surfaces. It remains unclear whether fullness is required in the strongly exceptional case, and the ground field and rationality convention are unspecified. The supplied label is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
