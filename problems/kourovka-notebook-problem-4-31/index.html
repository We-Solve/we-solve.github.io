Kourovka Notebook Problem 4.31

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, write $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$. Let $\mathcal N_2$ denote the class of all groups satisfying \[ \forall x,y,z\in G,\qquad [[x,y],z]=1. \] Thus $\mathcal N_2$ consists of groups of nilpotency class at most $2$, including abelian groups and the trivial group; no finiteness restriction is imposed.

A group quasi-identity is a universally quantified implication \[ \forall x_1,\ldots,x_n,\qquad \left(\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}u_i(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=v_i(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\right) \Longrightarrow u(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=v(x_1,\ldots,x_n), \] where $m\geq 0$ is finite and all displayed terms are group terms. A quasivariety of groups is the class of all groups satisfying some set of group quasi-identities.

Consider the lattice \[ \mathsf L_q(\mathcal N_2) =\{\mathcal Q:\mathcal Q\text{ is a quasivariety of groups and }\mathcal Q\subseteq\mathcal N_2\}, \] ordered by inclusion. For any family $(\mathcal Q_i)_{i\in I}$ in this lattice, its meet is the intersection within $\mathcal N_2$, and its join is \[ \bigvee_{i\in I}\mathcal Q_i =\bigcap\left\{\mathcal R\in\mathsf L_q(\mathcal N_2): \bigcup_{i\in I}\mathcal Q_i\subseteq\mathcal R\right\}. \] The empty meet is $\mathcal N_2$.

Problem (M. I. Kargapolov, 1973). Describe the lattice $\mathsf L_q(\mathcal N_2)$, including its elements and their inclusion relations.

Unresolved qualification. The source says "nilpotency class 2" without explicitly distinguishing class exactly $2$ from class at most $2$. The formulation above uses class at most $2$, the interpretation compatible with an ambient variety of groups. If exact class $2$ was intended literally, clarification is necessary: every quasivariety of groups contains the trivial group, which does not have class exactly $2$. The source also does not prescribe a particular form of lattice description.

Short English statement

Describe all quasivarieties contained in the class of groups of nilpotency class at most $2$, and determine how they are ordered by inclusion. The phrase "class 2" requires the qualification explained above. The supplied registry labels this problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
