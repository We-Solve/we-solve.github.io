Math-1146

Beilinson–Soulé Vanishing Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $K_m(R)$ for the algebraic $K$-group of a ring $R$ in degree $m$, and write $F_n^\gamma K_m(R)$ for the subgroup denoted by the $n$-th step of the gamma filtration in the supplied record. The record does not specify the ring conventions, the coefficient convention, the construction of this filtration on higher $K$-groups, or the allowed filtration indices.

The supplied formulation of the Beilinson--Soulé vanishing conjecture asks whether, for every regular ring $R$ and every admissible filtration index $n$, \[ K_m(R)=F_n^\gamma K_m(R) \qquad\text{for every integer }m\geq\max\{2n-1,1\}. \] Here "admissible filtration index" retains an unresolved source qualification: the record does not give a domain for $n$. Likewise, "regular ring" is retained without adding unstated commutativity, Noetherianity, or dimension hypotheses.

The record also describes this as equivalent to vanishing of Adams eigenspaces $K_m(R)^{(n)}$ in an "excluded range," except for $K_0(R)^{(0)}$. Under the usual rational interpretation, when Adams operations $\psi^k$ are defined, the notation would mean \[ K_m(R)^{(n)} = \left\{x\in K_m(R)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}: \psi^k(x)=k^n x\text{ for every integer }k\geq1\right\}. \] This rational interpretation is explanatory and is not specified by the record. The record supplies neither explicit inequalities defining the excluded range nor the hypotheses and filtration conventions needed to establish the asserted equivalence. In particular, the boundary degrees of the eigenspace formulation remain unresolved. The exception $K_0(R)^{(0)}$ is preserved as supplied; it does not assert that this group vanishes.

The accompanying literature assessment says that the entry combines Beilinson--Soulé and Parshin vanishing statements, but no separate Parshin statement or its hypotheses is supplied. Consequently, no additional Parshin assertion is formulated here.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether sufficiently high algebraic $K$-groups of every regular ring lie entirely in a specified gamma-filtration step. Its claimed equivalent Adams-eigenspace formulation needs clarification of coefficients, index ranges, boundary degrees, and ring hypotheses. The supplied status is "partially solved"; this is a dated curation label, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
