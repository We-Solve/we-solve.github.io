Nijenhuis Geometry and Integrable Systems — Problem 5

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a conventional smooth formulation, let $M$ be a smooth manifold of dimension $n\geq 1$, and let $L\colon TM\to TM$ be a smooth field of endomorphisms. Its Nijenhuis torsion is \[ N_L(X,Y)=[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]+L^2[X,Y] \] for smooth vector fields $X,Y$. The operator $L$ is called Nijenhuis if $N_L=0$.

On an open set $U\subseteq M$, a pseudo-Riemannian metric $g$ is a smooth, symmetric, nondegenerate covariant tensor of order two. Write $\nabla^g$ for its Levi-Civita connection and $X^\flat=g(X,\cdot)$. The usual tensor-equation interpretation of geodesic compatibility requires \[ g(LX,Y)=g(X,LY) \] and \[ \nabla^g_X L =\frac12\left(X\otimes {\,\mathrm{d}}(\operatorname{tr}L) +\operatorname{grad}_g(\operatorname{tr}L)\otimes X^\flat\right) \qquad\text{for every smooth vector field }X\text{ on }U. \] Here $(X\otimes\alpha)(Y)=\alpha(Y)X$, and $\operatorname{grad}_g f$ is characterized by $g(\operatorname{grad}_g f,Y)={\,\mathrm{d}} f(Y)$. Let $\mathcal G_L(U)$ denote the metrics satisfying these equations.

Problem. Characterize the Nijenhuis operators $L$ that admit at least one geodesically compatible metric, and, for each prescribed $L$, describe all such metrics. Under the local interpretation, this asks for necessary and sufficient conditions on the germ of $L$ at an arbitrary point $p\in M$ for the existence of a neighborhood $U\ni p$ with $\mathcal G_L(U)\neq\varnothing$, and for a description of all compatible metric germs at $p$. The source particularly emphasizes neighborhoods of singular points of $L$.

Formulation qualifications. The supplied excerpt does not define geodesic compatibility, prescribe a regularity category, specify whether $L$ must be invertible, or settle whether a global classification is also requested. The smooth tensor-equation formulation above is therefore an explicit conventional interpretation requiring confirmation against the cited source. It imposes no invertibility condition on $L$. The excerpt also does not define its terms "singular points" and "regular points."

Supplied scope and prior-work qualifications. The source reports that the existence question is known near almost every point, by the local descriptions in references [splitting2] and [splitting1]. No measure or precise genericity convention for "almost every" is supplied. It reports a solution under the additional assumptions that $L$ has $n$ distinct eigenvalues at generic points and $g$ is Riemannian, citing [Matveev2006], and separately states that the Riemannian case is solved in [BMF]. It describes the case of non-Riemannian signatures as still open even in dimension two, while also citing [BMP] and [japan] for the description of two-dimensional compatible pairs of signature $(+,-)$ near regular points. These are supplied source assessments, not independently verified results; the precise remaining scope, particularly at singular points, requires review.

Short English statement

Determine which Nijenhuis operators admit a geodesically compatible metric and describe every compatible metric for a given operator. The source emphasizes singular points and unresolved questions for indefinite metrics, including dimension two, while reporting known Riemannian and regular-point results. The displayed compatibility equations give a conventional smooth interpretation; the exact definitions, regularity, invertibility requirements, and local or global scope need confirmation from the source.
