Math-243

A Brauer-free finite local sieve for Fano complete intersections

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X/k$ be smooth projective and geometrically rationally connected over a number field. Write $\operatorname{Br}_0X=\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{Br}k\to\operatorname{Br}X)$, and define \[ X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}X}= \{(P_v):\sum_v\operatorname{inv}_v\alpha(P_v)=0 \text{ for every }\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}X\}. \] The verbal Hasse-principle question asks whether nonemptiness of this set implies $X(k)\ne\varnothing$. The extracted display $X(k)=X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}X}$ is not literally the usual density formulation; the attached aid interprets it as \[ \overline{X(k)}=X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}X}, \] with closure in the intended adelic topology, a stronger weak-approximation assertion. Whether a closure bar was lost and the exact archimedean convention are not independently verified. Both the verbal Hasse target and this stronger interpretation are retained.

The source highlights Fano varieties of dimension at least three, including smooth complete intersections $X\subset{\mathbb{P}}^n_k$ of multidegree $(d_1,\ldots,d_c)$ with $\sum_i d_i\leq n$. Its remarks suggest a large diagonal-quartic-threefold search; discuss the old Hasse problem for nonsingular cubics in at least five variables and a conditional diagonal-cubic result with an unresolved extracted finiteness object "X"; and ask the Hasse principle for smooth intersections of two quadrics in ${\mathbb{P}}^5$, reporting weak approximation if a rational point exists and calling six variables critical. These are source-time remarks, not newly checked status conclusions.

For the stated smooth complete intersections with $\dim X=n-c\geq3$, the aid reports \[ \operatorname{Br}X=\operatorname{Br}_0X, \qquad X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}X}=X({\mathbb{A}}_k). \] It also reports local points at all nonarchimedean places of good complete-intersection reduction, using Chevalley--Warning and Hensel lifting. Thus only archimedean and finitely many bad-reduction places need local-solubility testing. The stronger recovered conjecture becomes ordinary weak approximation, and its Hasse consequence becomes the ordinary Hasse principle; neither is proved by this reduction.

For a diagonal quartic threefold over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, \[ X:\ \sum_{i=0}^4a_ix_i^4=0\subset{\mathbb{P}}^4, \qquad a_i\in{\mathbb{Z}}\setminus\{0\},\quad\gcd(a_0,\ldots,a_4)=1, \] the aid reports the exact finite local sieve: \[ X({\mathbb{A}}_{{\mathbb{Q}}})^{\operatorname{Br}X}\ne\varnothing \ \Longleftrightarrow\ \text{the }a_i\text{ have both signs and }X({\mathbb{Q}}_p)\ne\varnothing \text{ for every }p\mid2\prod_i a_i. \] It supplies no individual coefficient example or explicit bad-prime decision algorithm. The sieve certifies adelic/Brauer--Manin nonemptiness, not the existence of a rational point in the remaining cases.

Short English statement

Ask whether Brauer--Manin is the only obstruction for rationally connected varieties, distinguishing the Hasse statement from adelic density. The attached aid reduces the stated high-dimensional Fano complete intersections to ordinary weak approximation/Hasse questions and gives a finite local sieve for diagonal quartic threefolds, without proving the global conjecture.
