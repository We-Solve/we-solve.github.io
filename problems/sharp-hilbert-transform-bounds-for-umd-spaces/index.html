Sharp Hilbert-Transform Bounds for UMD Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns the comparison of a Hilbert-transform norm and a martingale unconditionality constant. The definitions below use the standard real-line normalization and the usual range $1<p<\infty$; these conventions are not explicitly specified in the supplied statement.

Let $X$ be a Banach space and $1<p<\infty$. Write $L^p({\mathbb{R}};X)$ for the Bochner space with respect to Lebesgue measure. On smooth, compactly supported functions taking values in a finite-dimensional subspace of $X$, define \[ Hf(t)=\frac{1}{\pi}\operatorname{p.v.}\int_{{\mathbb{R}}}\frac{f(s)}{t-s}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s, \qquad h_p(X)=\|H\|_{L^p({\mathbb{R}};X)\to L^p({\mathbb{R}};X)}, \] where the norm refers to the bounded extension when it exists.

Define $m_p(X)$ to be the least $M\geq 1$ such that \[ \left\|\sum_{k=1}^{n}\varepsilon_k d_k\right\|_{L^p(\Omega;X)} \leq M\left\|\sum_{k=1}^{n}d_k\right\|_{L^p(\Omega;X)} \] for every positive integer $n$, every filtered probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P,(\mathcal F_k)_{k=0}^{n})$, every sequence $d_k\in L^p(\Omega;X)$ with $d_k$ measurable with respect to $\mathcal F_k$ and $\mathbb E[d_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]=0$, and every deterministic choice $\varepsilon_k\in\{-1,1\}$. Set $m_p(X)=\infty$ if no finite $M$ exists. A Banach space has the unconditional martingale differences property, abbreviated UMD, if $m_p(X)<\infty$ for every $1<p<\infty$.

Requested comparison. For UMD Banach spaces $X$, determine the sharp dependence of $h_p(X)$ on $m_p(X)$. In particular, the supplied statement asks whether they are comparable by linear factors. If "universal" means independent of both $X$ and $p$, the precise proposed comparison is \[ \exists\,0<c\leq C<\infty\quad \forall p\in(1,\infty)\quad \forall\text{ UMD Banach spaces }X:\qquad c\,m_p(X)\leq h_p(X)\leq C\,m_p(X). \] If instead $p$ is fixed and the comparison constants may depend on $p$, the proposed comparison is \[ \forall p\in(1,\infty)\quad \exists\,0<c_p\leq C_p<\infty\quad \forall\text{ UMD Banach spaces }X:\qquad c_p m_p(X)\leq h_p(X)\leq C_p m_p(X). \] The supplied record does not explicitly resolve this distinction. It also does not specify the scalar field of $X$, or whether the Hilbert transform is realized on the line or the circle. Thus the displayed definitions give an explicit conventional interpretation requiring confirmation. The original excerpt asks whether "these inequalities" are sharp, but the inequalities themselves are absent; their individual sharpness questions cannot be reconstructed from the excerpt.

Short English statement

Compare the norm of the Hilbert transform on $X$-valued $L^p$ functions with the constant controlling sign changes of $X$-valued martingale differences. The supplied question asks for sharp bounds, especially linear comparison. Whether the comparison must be uniform in $p$, and the precise inequalities referenced by the original excerpt, require clarification. The source labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
