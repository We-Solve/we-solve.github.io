Math-14523

Nonnegativity of Fusion-System Multiplicities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $S$ be a finite $p$-group, and let $\mathcal F$ be a saturated fusion system on $S$, using the standard terminology of fusion systems: its objects are the subgroups of $S$, its morphisms are injective group homomorphisms, it contains the homomorphisms induced by conjugation in $S$, its morphisms factor as isomorphisms followed by inclusions, and it satisfies the Sylow and extension saturation axioms. Let $d\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}$.

The supplied conjecture is the universal nonnegativity assertion \[ m(\mathcal F,d)\geq 0 \qquad\text{for every saturated fusion system }\mathcal F \text{ and every }d\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}. \] The prime and underlying finite group are implicit in the source terminology; no restriction on the prime is stated.

Unresolved definition. The supplied record does not define $m(\mathcal F,d)$, specify its codomain, or give a formula identifying this invariant. The displayed inequality therefore preserves the source assertion but cannot yet be a self-contained mathematical conjecture. Its precise interpretation requires the intended definition of $m$ and any hypotheses or auxiliary data belonging to that definition. In particular, the title alone does not justify interpreting $m$ as the cardinality of a set or as an actual multiplicity in a representation.

Short English statement

The conjecture asserts that the source's quantity $m(\mathcal F,d)$ is nonnegative for every saturated fusion system and every nonnegative integer $d$, including $d=0$. The supplied record omits the definition of $m$, so the mathematical target remains incomplete and requires human review.
