Math-7021

Positivity of Kac-Type Polynomials for Concealed-Canonical Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C$ range over the almost concealed-canonical algebras intended in the cited source, and let $d$ range over all dimension vectors allowed for $C$ in that source. Write $P_{C,d}(T)$ for the associated Kac-type polynomial named in the supplied record. The supplied dated literature assessment reports polynomiality and integrality; taking that reported integrality as part of the intended setup, write \[ P_{C,d}(T)=\sum_{j\geq 0}a_{C,d,j}T^j,\qquad a_{C,d,j}\in{\mathbb{Z}}, \] where only finitely many coefficients are nonzero. The question is whether \[ \forall C\text{ almost concealed-canonical},\quad \forall d\text{ a dimension vector for }C,\quad \forall j\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\qquad a_{C,d,j}\geq 0. \] Equivalently, is $P_{C,d}(T)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}[T]$ for every such pair $(C,d)$, where ${\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}[T]$ denotes the polynomials with nonnegative integer coefficients?

The supplied record does not define an almost concealed-canonical algebra or the construction of $P_{C,d}(T)$, and it does not specify the base field, the indexing and admissibility conventions for dimension vectors, or any boundary restrictions. These conventions must be recovered from the cited source before this can be regarded as a fully specified mathematical statement; the displayed quantifiers retain the source's intended domains without selecting additional hypotheses.

Short English statement

For every almost concealed-canonical algebra $C$ and every dimension vector $d$ admitted by the source, must every coefficient of its polynomial $P_{C,d}(T)$ be nonnegative? The polynomial construction and algebraic conventions require clarification from the cited source. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
