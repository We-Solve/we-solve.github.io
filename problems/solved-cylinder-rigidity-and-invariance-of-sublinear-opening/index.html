Math-1455

Solved cylinder rigidity and invariance of sublinear opening

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $C={\mathbb{R}}\times S^1$ carry a complete smooth Riemannian metric $g$ without conjugate points. For $p\in C$, define the shortest noncontractible based-loop length by \[ \ell_C(p)=\inf\{L_g(\gamma):\gamma(0)=\gamma(1)=p,\ [\gamma]\ne0\in\pi_1(C,p)\}. \] Loops are piecewise smooth; a minimizing geodesic loop need not have matching tangent vectors at its base point. Excluding contractible and constant loops is essential and is an explicit reconstruction of the OCR-damaged source wording.

The Bangert--Burns--Knieper conjecture assumes, for a fixed $p_0\in C$, the full two-ended limit \[ \lim_{d_g(p,p_0)\to\infty}\frac{\ell_C(p)}{d_g(p,p_0)}=0, \] meaning that for every $\epsilon>0$ there is $R<\infty$ such that $\ell_C(p)\leq\epsilon d_g(p,p_0)$ whenever $d_g(p,p_0)\geq R$. Its conclusion is flatness: $K_g\equiv0$.

The supplied research aid reports that Bangert--Emmerich, Theorem $2'$, Journal of Differential Geometry 94 (2013), proves this under a weaker hypothesis: each of the two ends admits a sequence $p_i$ escaping through that end with \[ \frac{\ell_C(p_i)}{d_g(p_i,p_0)}\longrightarrow0. \] The sequences may differ between ends. This endwise existence condition is weaker than the source's full limit; it is not a requirement on every escaping sequence. The solved-in-literature assessment and cited theorem's full hypotheses still require independent source verification.

The aid additionally reports elementary invariance properties valid for complete Riemannian cylinders. The function $\ell_C$ is 2-Lipschitz: \[ |\ell_C(p)-\ell_C(q)|\leq2d_g(p,q). \] The full sublinear-opening condition is independent of $p_0$ and equivalent to a full limit on each end separately. For a connected $m$-fold cyclic cover $\pi:C_m\to C$, equipped with the lifted metric, write $p=\pi(\widetilde p)$ and fix $\widetilde p_0$ above $p_0$. The reported comparisons are \[ \ell_C(p)\leq\ell_{C_m}(\widetilde p)\leq m\ell_C(p), \qquad d_g(p,p_0)\leq d_{\pi^*g}(\widetilde p,\widetilde p_0) \leq d_g(p,p_0)+A, \] where one may take the finite constant $A=\max_{q\in\pi^{-1}(p_0)}d_{\pi^*g}(q,\widetilde p_0)$. Consequently full sublinear opening holds downstairs if and only if it holds upstairs. This is a finite connected cover statement; no comparable assertion for the infinite universal cover is being made.

The extraction then begins section 7.1 with two geodesics in a complete simply connected manifold without conjugate points, but includes no actual divergence question. That incomplete adjacent sentence is not an additional problem to reconstruct.

Short English statement

A complete cylinder without conjugate points is conjectured to be flat if its shortest essential based loops grow sublinearly in distance along both ends. The supplied aid cites a published theorem with a weaker, one-sequence-per-end hypothesis and records basepoint and finite-cover invariance of the full condition.
