Math-5110

Pure-state extensions across a split ideal

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The primary AIM question asks when pure states of a MASA $A$ extend uniquely to states of a $C^*$-algebra $B=I\oplus A$, with $I$ an ideal. The report interprets this as an internal linear splitting $B=I\dotplus A$, with $I\cap A=0$, rather than a $C^*$ direct product. In its unital formulation, $A$ contains the unit of $B$, is abelian and satisfies $A'\cap B=A$. A state is a positive linear functional of norm one; a pure state of $A$ is a character $\phi$. The original does not specify unitality or the splitting convention.

The report defines the positive eigenfunctional cone \[ \mathcal C_\phi=\{\tau\in I^*_+:\tau(ai)=\phi(a)\tau(i),\ \tau(ia)=\tau(i)\phi(a)\ \text{for all }a\in A,i\in I\}. \] It claims the states extending $\phi$ are exactly \[ \psi_\tau(i+a)=\tau(i)+\phi(a),\qquad \tau\in\mathcal C_\phi,\quad\|\tau\|\leq1. \] Thus uniqueness is equivalent to $\mathcal C_\phi=\{0\}$, the unique extension then factoring through $B/I\cong A$. If $I\cong\mathcal K(H)$ and $A$ is a MASA, the report claims this cone vanishes for every character.

In contrast it reports a separable unital example with \[ I\cong C_0((0,1],\mathcal K({\mathbb{C}}\oplus L^2[0,1])),\qquad A\cong C([0,1]) \] a MASA in an internal split $B$, such that evaluation at $1/2$ has two distinct pure extensions. Its stated mechanism is an eigenvector appearing only in the endpoint fiber. The embedding of $A$, field of representations and formula for $B$ are not supplied, so the claimed explicit example cannot be reconstructed without further data. It shows the report's asserted insufficiency of MASA plus linear splitting, without contradicting the compact-operator-ideal case.

Short English statement

Characterize unique pure-state extension across an internally split ideal and MASA. The report gives an exact positive-functional criterion, a compact-operator-ideal positive case and an incompletely specified continuous-field counterexample. The appended workshop agenda is preserved as attributed context, including its independent paving question and incomplete quantitative claims.
