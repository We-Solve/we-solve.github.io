Math-6986

Donaldson Symplectic Calabi–Yau Equation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A symplectic form on a smooth real four-dimensional manifold $M$ is a smooth real differential two-form $\omega$ satisfying ${\,\mathrm{d}}\omega=0$ and nondegeneracy at every point. It determines the orientation for which $\omega^2:=\omega\wedge\omega$ is positive. Write $[\omega]\in H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;{\mathbb{R}})$ for its de Rham cohomology class. For a closed oriented four-manifold, $b_2^+(M)$ denotes the positive index of the intersection form on $H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})$.

Question as supplied. For every compact symplectic four-manifold denoted $(M,J,\omega)$ with $b_2^+(M)=1$, and every smooth real-valued function $F\in C^\infty(M;{\mathbb{R}})$ satisfying \[ \int_M e^F\omega^2=\int_M\omega^2, \] does there exist a symplectic form $\widetilde{\omega}$ on $M$ such that \[ [\widetilde{\omega}]=[\omega]\quad\text{in }H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(M;{\mathbb{R}}), \qquad \widetilde{\omega}\wedge\widetilde{\omega} =e^F\omega\wedge\omega? \] Here $\widetilde{\omega}\in[\omega]$ in the source is interpreted as equality of de Rham cohomology classes.

Unresolved qualifications. The source includes $J$ without defining it or imposing any relation between $J$, $\omega$, and $\widetilde{\omega}$. If $J$ is intended to be an almost complex structure, its defining condition is $J^2=-\mathrm{id}_{TM}$. Compatibility of a form $\eta$ with such a $J$ would require $\eta(Ju,Jv)=\eta(u,v)$ and $\eta(v,Jv)>0$ for nonzero $v$; taming would require the latter positivity condition. Neither requirement is explicitly present in the supplied question, and neither is added here. Consequently the displayed question does not specify the intended role of $J$ in the problem named by the title. The source also does not explicitly say that $M$ is connected or has empty boundary. The definition of $b_2^+$ above uses the closed-manifold interpretation; a boundary interpretation and any boundary conditions remain unspecified. If disconnected manifolds are allowed, the source supplies only the displayed total-volume normalization, with no separate normalization on each component.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every normalized density $e^F\omega^2$ can be realized as the square of a symplectic form cohomologous to $\omega$ on a compact four-manifold with $b_2^+=1$. The role of $J$, any compatibility or taming requirements, and the intended connectedness and boundary assumptions require clarification before this can serve as a precise formulation of the named Donaldson problem.
