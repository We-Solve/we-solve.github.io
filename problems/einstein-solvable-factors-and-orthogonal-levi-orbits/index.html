Math-3705

Einstein Solvable Factors and Orthogonal Levi Orbits

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,g)=(G/K,g)$ be a noncompact, nonflat homogeneous Einstein space, with base point $o=eK$. In the Riemannian interpretation used here, $G$ acts transitively by isometries, $K$ is the stabilizer of $o$, and \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g=\lambda g \] for a constant $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$; nonflat means that the Riemann curvature tensor is not identically zero. No sign condition on $\lambda$ is supplied.

Assume the stated global Levi decomposition \[ G=G_1\ltimes G_2,\qquad G_2=AN, \] where $G_1$ is a semisimple Levi factor and $G_2$ is the solvable radical. The source does not define $A$ or $N$, or specify additional properties of their product; the notation $AN$ is therefore retained without imposing further hypotheses.

Define the solvable-factor orbit and its induced metric by \[ S=G_2\cdot o\cong G_2/(G_2\cap K),\qquad g_S=\iota_S^*g, \] where $\iota_S:S\longrightarrow M$ is the orbit immersion. This gives a precise induced geometry even when $G_2$ itself cannot be identified with a subspace of $M$. Such an identification via $s\mapsto sK$ requires $G_2\cap K=\{e\}$, a condition absent from the supplied statement.

For a product decomposition $G_1=H_1\cdots H_k$ into simple Lie subgroups, let $O_i=H_i\cdot o$. Write $\mathfrak h_i$ for the Lie algebra of $H_i$, and define \[ X_o^*=\left.\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}\right|_{t=0}\exp(tX)\cdot o, \qquad V_i=T_oO_i=\{X_o^*:X\in\mathfrak h_i\}. \]

The supplied problem asks whether the following assertions always hold for the stated data:

  1. The induced metric on the solvable-factor orbit is Einstein: there exists a constant $\mu\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that \[ \operatorname{Ric}_{g_S}=\mu g_S. \] No equality between $\mu$ and the ambient Einstein constant $\lambda$ is asserted.
  2. Whenever $G_1=H_1\cdots H_k$ is a product of simple subgroups, their orbits through $o$ have pairwise orthogonal tangent spaces at $o$: \[ \forall\,1\leq i<j\leq k\quad \forall\,X\in\mathfrak h_i\quad \forall\,Y\in\mathfrak h_j,\qquad g_o(X_o^*,Y_o^*)=0. \]

These are conditional formulations requiring source clarification. The source does not specify whether additional restrictions on $A,N,K$, or the choice of Levi factor are intended. The first assertion uses the orbit interpretation of the phrase "$G_2$ as a subspace." The second uses orthogonality at the common base point; no assertion about orbit distributions elsewhere is supplied. Riemannian signature is the conventional interpretation adopted here, but is not explicitly stated in the record.

Short English statement

For a noncompact, nonflat homogeneous Einstein space with the stated Levi decomposition, ask whether the orbit of its solvable radical, with the induced metric, is Einstein. Also ask whether the simple Levi-factor orbits through the base point have pairwise orthogonal tangent spaces there. The meaning of $G_2=AN$, the identification of $G_2$ with an orbit, and any additional intended hypotheses require review.
