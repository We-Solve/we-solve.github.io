Convexity of Noncompletable Nonnegative Partial Tensors

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite discrete interpretation can be formulated as follows; the supplied record does not specify that this is its complete intended scope. Fix a positive integer $d$, positive integers $n_1,\ldots,n_d$, and a set of observed positions \[ E\subseteq I:=\prod_{k=1}^{d}\{1,\ldots,n_k\}. \] A nonnegative partial tensor with observation pattern $E$ is a vector $x=(x_i)_{i\in E}\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}^{E}$. An independent probability completion of $x$ consists of vectors $p^{(k)}\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}^{n_k}$ satisfying \[ \sum_{j=1}^{n_k}p^{(k)}_j=1\quad(1\leq k\leq d), \qquad x_{(i_1,\ldots,i_d)}=\prod_{k=1}^{d}p^{(k)}_{i_k} \quad\left((i_1,\ldots,i_d)\in E\right). \] Thus the full tensor $T_{i_1,\ldots,i_d}=\prod_{k=1}^{d}p^{(k)}_{i_k}$ is the joint probability mass function of independent random variables. Define \[ \mathcal C_E=\left\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}^{E}:x\text{ has an independent probability completion}\right\}, \qquad \mathcal N_E={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}^{E}\setminus\mathcal C_E. \] Under this interpretation, the question is whether, for every choice of $d,n_1,\ldots,n_d,E$, the set $\mathcal N_E$ is convex; explicitly, whether \[ \forall x,y\in\mathcal N_E\ \forall\lambda\in[0,1], \qquad \lambda x+(1-\lambda)y\in\mathcal N_E. \] This is a conditional formalization requiring review. The record does not specify the allowed tensor orders, dimensions, observation patterns, or ambient restrictions on the observed entries. For example, if admissible partial tensors must satisfy $\sum_{i\in E}x_i\leq1$, the set whose convexity is asked about would instead be \[ \left\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}^{E}:\sum_{i\in E}x_i\leq1\right\}\setminus\mathcal C_E. \] The supplied evidence does not resolve which ambient set is intended. Convexity also requires a fixed observation pattern, or a specified common ambient space for comparing different patterns.

Short English statement

The question asks whether convex combinations of nonnegative partial tensors that admit no completion to an independent joint probability distribution must also admit no such completion. The displayed formulation uses finite discrete variables and a fixed observation pattern; the intended admissible entries and range of patterns remain unspecified.
