Removing Complementary-Cap Restrictions for Spherical Neumann Eigenvalues

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^2=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^3:|x|=1\}$ carry its standard round metric and area measure ${\,\mathrm{d}} A$, so that $|S^2|=4\pi$. For $0<a\leq 2\pi$, let $C_a$ be a geodesic spherical cap of area $a$; its geodesic radius is $\arccos(1-a/(2\pi))$.

For a connected open domain $\Omega\subset S^2$ whose Neumann Laplacian has discrete spectrum, write its eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, as \[ 0=\lambda_1^N(\Omega)<\lambda_2^N(\Omega)\leq\lambda_3^N(\Omega)\leq\cdots. \] Here the Neumann Laplacian is the operator associated with the quadratic form $u\mapsto\int_\Omega |\nabla_{S^2}u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A$ on $H^1(\Omega)$. In this setting, \[ \lambda_2^N(\Omega)=\inf_{\substack{u\in H^1(\Omega)\setminus\{0\}\\ \int_\Omega u\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A=0}} \frac{\int_\Omega |\nabla_{S^2}u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A}{\int_\Omega |u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A}. \] Connectedness makes the second eigenvalue equal to the first positive eigenvalue. The source does not specify boundary regularity or its precise admissible class of domains; the spectral setting above is an explicit working interpretation requiring review.

Proposed inequality recorded by the source. For every $a\in(0,2\pi]$ and every admissible domain $\Omega\subset S^2$ with $|\Omega|=a$, establish \[ \lambda_2^N(\Omega)\leq\lambda_2^N(C_a), \] without imposing simple connectedness or the source's additional condition that $\Omega$ lie outside a complementary cap. The source does not define that complementary-cap condition precisely.

Unresolved source qualification. The supplied original discussion reports the inequality for simply connected domains through $94\%$ of the sphere, with extension toward the full sphere posed separately, and for arbitrary domains of area at most half the sphere subject to the complementary-cap condition. The present target is removal of the latter condition. However, the supplied dated literature assessment also reports counterexamples for multiply connected domains. It does not specify their areas or other hypotheses, so their applicability to the displayed inequality cannot be determined from this record. Accordingly, the displayed assertion is a proposed target, not an established theorem or an independently verified open conjecture.

Short English statement

The proposed comparison says that, at every prescribed positive area up to half the round sphere, a spherical cap has the largest second Neumann eigenvalue, without a complementary-cap restriction. The source leaves the admissible domain class unclear, and its report of multiply connected counterexamples requires checking before this target can be treated as open or valid.
