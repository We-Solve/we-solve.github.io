Definitions and mathematical statement

Problem (T. Kerler, nonsemisimple versus semisimple TQFTs and the double conjecture). Retain the category-dependent theories denoted by $V_{\mathcal C}$, $W_{\mathcal B}$, and $V_{D(\mathcal B)}$ in the source. Here $D(\mathcal B)$ refers to the Drinfel'd double construction mentioned in the accompanying discussion. The supplied excerpt does not define the categories, the construction of these theories, or their admissibility hypotheses.
  1. Clarify the difference in content between the two theories whose names both flatten to $V_{\mathcal C}$ in the extracted record. Ask whether a homological TQFT $H$ makes one essentially equivalent to the tensor product of the other with $H$. The extracted comparison literally reads $V_{\mathcal C}$ versus $V_{\mathcal C}\otimes H$; missing typographical distinctions must be recovered before this can be an unambiguous equivalence assertion. In the usual meaning of homological, the mapping-class-group actions of $H$ factor through their actions on first homology; the exact convention and meaning of "essentially equivalent" are not supplied.
  2. Construct $W_{\mathcal B}$ extending the Turaev--Viro TQFT construction to nonsemisimple $\mathcal B$, analogously to the generalization of the Reshetikhin--Turaev construction in [216]. For quantum-group examples and closed $3$-manifolds, the construction should reproduce a version of the Kuperberg invariant. The version and normalization are not specified.
  3. Determine the relationship between $W_{\mathcal B}$ and $V_{D(\mathcal B)}$; in particular, ask whether they are isomorphic as TQFTs in an appropriate sense. Neither this sense nor the full hypotheses on $\mathcal B$ are provided.
The source records genus-one evidence for the $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ case suggesting that the $H$ in (1) is the Frohman--Nicas $U(1)$ theory. It anticipates technical requirements in (2), as suggested by [236]: "minimal" cell decompositions of cobordisms rather than general triangulations, and combings rather than framings. It identifies (3) with Question 5 of [209], motivated by work of and discussions with D. Kazhdan and S. Gelfand in 1994, while allowing that it may have been posed earlier. For categories arising from subfactors and for closed manifolds, it reports results answering the conjecture in [204]. It presents more general results as a route to understanding the two TQFT constructions and a topological picture of the Drinfel'd double; no unrestricted equivalence is asserted as established.

Short English statement

Kerler asks how semisimple and nonsemisimple TQFTs differ, whether a homological factor relates them, how to extend Turaev--Viro theory to nonsemisimple categories, and whether that extension agrees with the theory of the Drinfel'd double. Some notation distinguishing the two theories in the first question is missing from the extraction.
