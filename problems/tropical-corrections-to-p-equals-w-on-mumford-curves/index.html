Definitions and mathematical statement

The notation $P=W$ conventionally refers to a comparison between a perverse filtration $P$ and a weight filtration $W$ on suitable cohomological objects. Such a comparison requires specified spaces, cohomology theories, filtrations, an identification between the relevant objects, and indexing conventions. None of these data, nor a definition of a tropical correction, is provided in the supplied passage.

A Mumford curve ordinarily means a smooth proper curve over a non-Archimedean field with totally degenerate reduction. This terminology provides context only: the supplied record does not specify a base field, genus, reduction convention, or associated geometric construction on which the proposed filtrations would be defined.

Does a $P=W$ phenomenon with a tropical correction occur in other non-Archimedean settings, such as Mumford curves?

The original wording asks for a phenomenon similar to one in preceding report context, but that preceding mathematical construction is not included. Consequently, the question cannot yet be expressed as a definite equality of filtrations or a quantified existence statement for a specified correction.
