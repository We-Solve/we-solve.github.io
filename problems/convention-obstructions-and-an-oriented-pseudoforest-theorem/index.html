Math-5215

Convention obstructions and an oriented-pseudoforest theorem

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite digraph $D$, let $d^+(v),d^-(v)$ be degrees, counting arcs with multiplicity when parallel arcs are allowed, and let $d(u,v)$ be directed distance, with unreachable distance infinity. Define \[ S(D)=\sum_v|d^+(v)-d^-(v)|,\qquad p(D)=|\{v:d^+(v)>d^-(v)\}|, \] \[ R_{\le2}(D)=|\{(u,v):u\ne v,\ d(u,v)\le2\}|. \] The ordered-pair and distinct-terminal convention is forced by the source note claiming equality for transitive tournaments; counting trivial self-pairs would change that note. The source, Conjecture 6.35 attributed to Thomassé, asserts \[ S(D)+R_{\le2}(D)\ge2|E(D)|+p(D). \] The aid reports that the AIM source uses a general digraph convention permitting digons and parallel arcs, under which the assertion is false.

Its first obstruction is a two-vertex digon: $|E|=2$, $S=p=0$, and $R_{\le2}=2$, so the proposed inequality would be $2\ge4$. Its second is a directed triangle with every arc doubled: $|E|=6$, all indegrees and outdegrees equal two, and all six distinct ordered pairs have distance at most two, giving the false inequality $6\ge12$. Thus forbidding digons alone does not repair the assertion while parallel arcs remain allowed.

For a likely repaired class, let $D$ be finite, simple and oriented: no loops, parallel arcs, or antiparallel pairs. Put \[ q(D)=|\{(u,v):d(u,v)=2\}|,\qquad m=|E(D)|. \] Then $R_{\le2}=m+q(D)$, and the repaired inequality is exactly \[ q(D)+S(D)\ge m+p(D). \] This class restriction is a proposed repair, not silently substituted for the original general-digraph wording. The source's transitive-tournament equality is retained in this setting.

The supplied aid reports the repaired inequality for every oriented pseudoforest, meaning that the underlying undirected graph has at most one cycle per connected component. For its slack \[ \sigma(D)=q(D)+S(D)-m-p(D), \] it reports an exact nonnegative increment when a pendant vertex and its incident oriented edge are attached. Repeatedly deleting pendant vertices reduces each component to an isolated vertex or an oriented cycle core. The reported equality classification is that the cores have zero slack and every pendant attachment has zero increment. The actual piecewise increment formulas and explicit list of zero-slack cycle orientations are not supplied, so the classification cannot be expanded into a full orientation test from this record alone. No proof for arbitrary oriented graphs is claimed. Consequently the supplied refuted label applies to the original broad convention, while the repaired general assertion remains outside the established special-family result.

Short English statement

The printed inequality fails for a digon and also for a doubled directed triangle. The supplied aid separates these convention counterexamples from a proposed simple-oriented repair, which it reports proved only for pseudoforests with a recursive equality criterion whose full formulas are missing.
