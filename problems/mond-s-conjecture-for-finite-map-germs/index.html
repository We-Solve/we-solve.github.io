Mond's Conjecture for Finite Map Germs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the customary holomorphic interpretation of the source statement: let $n\geq 1$, let $S\subset{\mathbb{C}}^n$ be a finite set, and let \[ f:({\mathbb{C}}^n,S)\longrightarrow({\mathbb{C}}^{n+1},0) \] be a holomorphic multigerm. Two such multigerms are $\mathcal A$-equivalent if they differ by biholomorphic changes of source and target coordinates. The germ is $\mathcal A$-finite if its $\mathcal A$-equivalence class is determined by a finite jet.

Let $\theta(f)$ be the space of holomorphic vector fields along $f$, and let $\theta_{{\mathbb{C}}^n,S}$ and $\theta_{{\mathbb{C}}^{n+1},0}$ denote holomorphic source and target vector fields, respectively, without requiring them to vanish at the marked points. Define the extended normal space and extended codimension by \[ N\mathcal A_e(f)= \frac{\theta(f)} {\{df\circ\xi:\xi\in\theta_{{\mathbb{C}}^n,S}\} +\{\eta\circ f:\eta\in\theta_{{\mathbb{C}}^{n+1},0}\}}, \qquad \mathcal A_e\text{-}\operatorname{codim}(f) =\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}N\mathcal A_e(f). \] Here the quotient is a quotient of complex vector spaces. For a sufficiently small stable perturbation of a representative of $f$, let $X_u$ denote its local image, called a disentanglement. In the setting in which the image Milnor number is defined by this construction, put \[ \mu_I(f)=\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\widetilde H_n(X_u;{\mathbb{Z}}). \] When the disentanglement has the homotopy type of a bouquet of $n$-spheres, this is the number of those spheres.

Proposed statement (Mond's conjecture, as supplied). For every such $\mathcal A$-finite multigerm in the source's stipulated nice dimensions, \[ \mathcal A_e\text{-}\operatorname{codim}(f)\leq\mu_I(f). \] Moreover, if $f$ is weighted homogeneous, then \[ \mathcal A_e\text{-}\operatorname{codim}(f)=\mu_I(f). \] Weighted homogeneity customarily means homogeneity under positive weighted coordinate dilations in suitable coordinates. For a single source point, this means that there are positive integer weights $w_1,\ldots,w_n$ and degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_{n+1}$ such that \[ f_j(t^{w_1}x_1,\ldots,t^{w_n}x_n)=t^{d_j}f_j(x) \qquad(1\leq j\leq n+1). \] The supplied record does not specify the compatibility convention for weighted homogeneity across multiple source branches, nor the precise convention or numerical range for nice dimensions. These qualifications remain unresolved; no unrestricted assertion for all $n$ or all multigerm weight conventions is intended.

Short English statement

For an $\mathcal A$-finite holomorphic map multigerm from ${\mathbb{C}}^n$ to ${\mathbb{C}}^{n+1}$ in the stipulated nice dimensions, the proposed inequality bounds its extended $\mathcal A$-codimension by its image Milnor number. Equality is proposed when the germ is weighted homogeneous. The exact dimension range and the multigerm weight convention require source clarification.
