Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 13

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ be the free group on two generators. Its group von Neumann algebra $L(\mathbb F_2)$ is generated by the left regular representation on $\ell^2(\mathbb F_2)$, with canonical trace $\tau(x)=\langle x\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle$.

Under the usual interpretation in the continuous first-order language of tracial von Neumann algebras, a unital trace-preserving embedding $\iota:A\to B$ is existential if, for every finite parameter tuple $\bar a$ from $A$ and every quantifier-free formula $\varphi(\bar x,\bar y)$, \[ \inf_{\bar y\in A}\varphi^A(\bar a,\bar y) = \inf_{\bar y\in B}\varphi^B(\iota(\bar a),\bar y), \] where the variables range over their specified operator-norm balls in both structures.

Question 1. Does there exist an existential embedding \[ \iota:L(\mathbb F_2)\hookrightarrow\mathbb M^{\mathcal U}? \] Here $\mathbb M^{\mathcal U}$ retains the source notation. The supplied record does not define the matrix algebras, their traces, the index set, or the ultrafilter used to construct this target. Thus this question requires the source's missing convention for $\mathbb M^{\mathcal U}$; the interpretation of existentiality above is also subject to confirmation of the intended language.

Question 2. Is the source's random embedding of $L(\mathbb F_2)$ into $\mathbb M^{\mathcal U}$, described as arising from Haar-sampling, existential in this sense? Haar-sampling ordinarily refers to choosing unitaries according to normalized Haar probability measure on unitary groups. The record does not specify the matrix dimensions, joint distribution and independence assumptions, construction of the embedding, or intended probabilistic quantifier. In particular, it does not state whether existentiality is required almost surely. Consequently, the second question cannot be given a fully quantified probabilistic formulation from the supplied record alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether the free group factor $L(\mathbb F_2)$ embeds existentially into the target denoted $\mathbb M^{\mathcal U}$, and whether an embedding obtained by Haar-sampling is existential. Existentiality means that allowing witnesses in the larger algebra does not improve the infimum of any quantifier-free formula with parameters from the smaller algebra. The target construction and random sampling procedure require clarification.
