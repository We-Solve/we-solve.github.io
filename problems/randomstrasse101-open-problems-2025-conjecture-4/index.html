Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2025) — Conjecture 4

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an odd prime power $p\equiv 1\pmod 4$, the Paley graph $G_p$ has vertex set ${\mathbb{F}}_p$; distinct vertices $u,v$ are adjacent when $u-v$ is a nonzero square in ${\mathbb{F}}_p$. Its clique number $\omega(G_p)$ is the maximum cardinality of a set of pairwise adjacent vertices. The supplied record does not specify whether its parameter $p$ ranges over primes or over prime powers, or whether further restrictions apply.

One standard interpretation of the degree-$4$ Sum-of-Squares upper bound for the clique number of a finite graph $G=(V,E)$ is the following. Introduce real variables $(x_v)_{v\in V}$, and let ${\mathbb{R}}[x]_{\leq d}$ denote the real polynomials of total degree at most $d$. Define \[ S_4(G)=\sup_L\sum_{v\in V}L(x_v), \] where $L:{\mathbb{R}}[x]_{\leq4}\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is linear and satisfies \[ \begin{aligned} L(1)&=1,\\ L(f^2)&\geq0 &&\text{for every }f\in{\mathbb{R}}[x]_{\leq2},\\ L\left((x_v^2-x_v)r\right)&=0 &&\text{for every }v\in V,\ r\in{\mathbb{R}}[x]_{\leq2},\\ L(x_ux_vr)&=0 &&\text{for every distinct }u,v\text{ with }\{u,v\}\notin E, \ r\in{\mathbb{R}}[x]_{\leq2}. \end{aligned} \] This is an explicit candidate convention for the relaxation. The supplied record refers instead to the precise construction in reference KY-2022-PaleyDegree4SOS; its identification with the convention above has not been established from the supplied evidence.

Let $\mathcal P$ denote the intended unbounded set of admissible Paley orders. Subject to resolving both the domain $\mathcal P$ and the intended relaxation, the conjecture attributed to Bandeira et al., Conjecture 4, is \[ \exists\varepsilon>0\ \exists C>0\ \exists p_0>0\ \forall p\in\mathcal P:\qquad p\geq p_0\ \Longrightarrow\ S_4(G_p)\leq C p^{1/2-\varepsilon}. \] Here $\varepsilon,C,p_0$ are independent of $p$. Thus the requested improvement concerns the value of the degree-$4$ relaxation itself.

The supplied background describes numerical evidence suggesting a polynomial improvement over the $\sqrt p$ scale and reports that the authors of KY-2022-PaleyDegree4SOS prove that the relaxation cannot improve beyond an $\Omega(p^{1/3})$ scale. That reported lower bound, its parameter domain, and its precise hypotheses have not been independently verified. The registry labels the conjecture open; this is a supplied status label.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the degree-$4$ Sum-of-Squares upper bound for the clique number of the Paley graph is at most $C p^{1/2-\varepsilon}$, for some fixed $\varepsilon>0$ and all sufficiently large admissible $p$. The intended range of $p$ and the precise relaxation require confirmation. The supplied record reports numerical support and a lower-bound obstruction at the $p^{1/3}$ scale.
