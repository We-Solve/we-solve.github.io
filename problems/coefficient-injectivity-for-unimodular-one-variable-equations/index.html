Coefficient injectivity for unimodular one-variable equations

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For every nontrivial group $G$, the free product $G*\mathbb Z$ is claimed not normally generated by a single element.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
In the free product $H=A*\langle t\rangle$, a word $w$ is unimodular when its total exponent of $t$ equals $1$ or $-1$. For any coefficient group $A$ and such $w$, the source claims \[ A\longrightarrow H/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle_H\text{ injective}. \] The Kervaire corollary drops the exponent assumption for a different conclusion: \[ A\ne1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle_H\ne1\qquad(w\in H). \]

Outline of the argument

A traced spectral phase provides a unitary with a controlled large fixed space. Translating a putative coefficient relation into a planar surface yields one nontrivial boundary, which the phase calculation excludes; this prevents a normal generator of the required form.

Formal verification

For family 256, the selected statement is coefficient injectivity for a single relator of exponent sum $1$ or $-1$ in $A*\mathbb Z$. It underlies the Kervaire consequence but does not include the newer general nonsingular-system theorem. The listed Comparator files are Kervaire.lean.
