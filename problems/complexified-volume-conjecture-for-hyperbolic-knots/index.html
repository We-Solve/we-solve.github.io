Math-15155

Complexified Volume Conjecture for Hyperbolic Knots

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K\subset S^3$ be a hyperbolic knot, meaning that its complement $M_K=S^3\setminus K$ admits a complete hyperbolic metric of sectional curvature $-1$ and finite volume. Write $\operatorname{Vol}(M_K)$ for that hyperbolic volume. For a positive integer $N$, put $q_N=e^{2\pi i/N}$, and let $J_N(K;q_N)$ denote the root-of-unity specialization of the $N$-colored Jones invariant named in the supplied record. Its normalization, framing convention, and precise indexing convention are not specified there. Write $\operatorname{CS}(M_K)$ for the Chern--Simons quantity appearing in the record; its normalization, orientation convention, period, and choice of representative are likewise unspecified.

The supplied complexified volume conjecture asks whether, for every hyperbolic knot $K\subset S^3$, \[ \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log J_N\!\left(K;e^{2\pi i/N}\right) \stackrel{?}{=} \operatorname{Vol}(M_K)+i\operatorname{CS}(M_K). \] Here $N$ tends to infinity through positive integers. This display records the intended target, but the supplied data do not determine a well-defined equality of complex numbers. To interpret its left-hand side, one must specify logarithm values $L_N\in{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfying \[ e^{L_N}=J_N(K;q_N) \] for all sufficiently large $N$, which requires eventual nonvanishing, and specify how these logarithm values are selected across $N$. Changing $L_N$ by $2\pi i k_N$, with $k_N\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, changes the scaled term by $4\pi^2 i k_N/N$, so the limiting imaginary part is not determined without a selection rule. The record supplies neither such a rule nor an eventual nonvanishing qualification. It also does not specify whether the right-hand side uses a chosen lift of the Chern--Simons quantity or whether the proposed equality is intended modulo a period. These unresolved choices must be fixed before the displayed target becomes a precise conjecture; no choices are imposed in this draft.

Short English statement

For every hyperbolic knot, the supplied conjecture relates the asymptotic logarithm of its colored Jones invariant at $e^{2\pi i/N}$ to the hyperbolic volume and Chern--Simons quantity of its complement. The logarithm selection, invariant normalizations, and meaning of the Chern--Simons term remain unspecified, so the complex equality requires clarification.
