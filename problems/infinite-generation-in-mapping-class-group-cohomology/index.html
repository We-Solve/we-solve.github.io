Infinite Generation in Mapping-Class-Group Cohomology

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied corrected formulation concerns a torsion-free subgroup $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Mod}_g$ of finite index and the curve complex $\mathcal C_g$. The record does not specify the range of the genus $g$, the surface conventions, or the coefficient ring.

Under the usual closed-surface interpretation, let $S_g$ be a closed, connected, oriented surface of genus $g$, and let $\operatorname{Mod}_g$ be its group of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms modulo isotopy. For $g\geq 2$, the curve complex $\mathcal C_g$ has vertices given by isotopy classes of essential simple closed curves on $S_g$; a finite set of distinct vertices spans a simplex when its classes admit pairwise disjoint representatives. The mapping class group acts on this complex and hence on its homology. These are conventional interpretations, not additional hypotheses verified from the supplied report.

With integral coefficients as an explicit provisional convention, put \[ M_g=H_{2g-2}(\mathcal C_g;{\mathbb{Z}}), \] viewed as a ${\mathbb{Z}}\Gamma$-module by restricting the induced mapping class group action. The corrected question, under these conventions, is: \[ \text{For every torsion-free finite-index subgroup }\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Mod}_g, \quad H^{2g-1}(\Gamma;M_g) \text{ is not finitely generated as an abelian group?} \] Here $H^k(\Gamma;M)$ denotes group cohomology with coefficients in the $\Gamma$-module $M$. The intended genus range must be supplied before this can be read as a fully quantified conjecture; the coefficient and finite-generation conventions also require confirmation.

There is a material conflict within the record. Its original formulation calls $\mathcal C_g$ the universal curve and asks about \[ H^{2g-1}\left(\Gamma;H^{2g-2}(\mathcal C_g)\right), \] whereas its statement and clean statement call $\mathcal C_g$ the curve complex and use homology $H_{2g-2}$. These formulations must not be identified. The draft above follows the supplied corrected formulation provisionally; the original universal curve, its base, and its coefficient action are not specified sufficiently to formalize that alternative.

Short English statement

For a torsion-free finite-index subgroup of the genus-$g$ mapping class group, the supplied corrected question asks whether its degree-$2g-1$ group cohomology, with coefficients in the degree-$2g-2$ homology of the curve complex, is infinitely generated. The genus range and coefficient conventions are missing, and the original formulation uses a different space and cohomology instead of homology. The supplied open status requires independent review.
