Cyclic Labelings and Full-Twist Braid Words

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $B_n$ denote the braid group on $n$ strands, and let $a_{ij}$, for $1\leq i<j\leq n$, denote its Birman--Ko--Lee generators. The supplied passage does not specify the admissible values of $n$, the convention for these generators, or the convention for the full twist.

Conjecture as supplied. Cyclic labelings correspond exactly to reduced words representing the full twist in the squares $a_{ij}^{2}$ of the Birman--Ko--Lee generators.

This is an incomplete formulation: the supplied record does not define a cyclic labeling, its underlying combinatorial objects, the proposed correspondence, or the meaning of "reduced" for words in these squared generators. It also does not specify whether inverse letters are allowed or whether words or labelings are considered modulo equivalences. Consequently, a fully quantified mathematical statement cannot be recovered from this record alone.

Supplied local-move context. Write $(ij)$ for an edge with endpoints $i,j$ in the edge-list notation of the passage. The passage states that the following local replacements preserve the property of being a cyclic labeling, when the displayed edges have consecutive labels:

  1. Disjoint edges commute: \[ (ij)(kl)\;\longleftrightarrow\;(kl)(ij), \] provided that it is not the case that $i<k<j<l$ cyclically.
  2. Triangle edges can be rotated: \[ (ij)(jk)(ik)\;\longleftrightarrow\;(ik)(ij)(jk) \;\longleftrightarrow\;(jk)(ik)(ij), \] when $i<j<k$ cyclically.
  3. A "pierced triangle" has the replacement \[ (ij)(jl)(jk)(ik)\;\longleftrightarrow\;(ik)(ij)(jl)(jk), \] when $i<j<k<l$ cyclically. The consecutive-label condition is explicit for the first two moves in the supplied passage but is not separately stated for this third move; its precise applicability remains unresolved.
These are recorded as replacements of edge lists, as in the source passage. The record does not establish that they generate every equivalence relevant to the conjectured global correspondence, and the moves do not supply the missing definition of a cyclic labeling.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether cyclic labelings correspond exactly to reduced expressions for the full twist using squared Birman--Ko--Lee braid generators.
