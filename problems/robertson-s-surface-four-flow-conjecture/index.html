Robertson’s Surface Four-Flow Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record attributes both assertions below to Robertson (1994), as Conjectures 5 and 6. Its dated curation describes the projective-plane assertion as solved and the general assertion as open; these status claims have not been independently verified here.

For a provisional conventional formulation, let graphs be finite undirected graphs, allowing loops and parallel edges, and let surfaces be compact connected surfaces without boundary, including both orientable and nonorientable surfaces. These conventions are not specified in the supplied extract and require confirmation against the source. No cellularity assumption on embeddings is stated in the extract.

A graph is $2$-edge-connected if it is connected and deleting any one edge leaves it connected. For an embedding $\iota:G\hookrightarrow S$, define its face-width by \[ \operatorname{fw}(\iota)=\min_C\left|C\cap\iota(G)\right|, \] where $C$ ranges over noncontractible simple closed curves in $S$ whose intersection with the embedded graph is finite. Curves may pass through vertices; each intersection point is counted once. If there is no noncontractible simple closed curve, set $\operatorname{fw}(\iota)=+\infty$.

A nowhere-zero $4$-flow on $G$ consists of an orientation of its edges and a function \[ f:E(G)\longrightarrow\{-3,-2,-1,1,2,3\} \] satisfying conservation at every vertex: \[ \sum_{e\in\delta^+(v)}f(e)=\sum_{e\in\delta^-(v)}f(e) \qquad(v\in V(G)). \] Here $\delta^+(v)$ and $\delta^-(v)$ denote outgoing and incoming edges, respectively; an oriented loop contributes to both sums.

Projective-plane assertion (source Conjecture 5). For every $2$-edge-connected graph $G$ and every embedding $\iota:G\hookrightarrow\mathbb{RP}^{2}$, \[ \operatorname{fw}(\iota)\ge4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ has a nowhere-zero }4\text{-flow}. \]

General surface assertion (source Conjecture 6). For every surface $S$, there exists a positive integer $r(S)$ such that, for every $2$-edge-connected graph $G$ and every embedding $\iota:G\hookrightarrow S$, \[ \operatorname{fw}(\iota)\ge r(S) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ has a nowhere-zero }4\text{-flow}. \] The threshold depends only on $S$, not on $G$ or its embedding. Taking a positive integer threshold expresses the source's unspecified constant threshold equivalently because finite face-width is integer-valued. The graph and surface conventions above, and whether embeddings must be cellular, remain source-verification issues rather than settled hypotheses of the supplied extract.

Short English statement

Robertson's general assertion asks whether each surface has a face-width threshold forcing every $2$-edge-connected graph embedded in that surface to admit a nowhere-zero $4$-flow. The record also includes the projective-plane assertion with threshold $4$. Supplied curation dated 2026-08-22 labels that special case solved and the general assertion open; neither status is independently verified here. The intended graph, surface, and embedding conventions require confirmation.
