Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.1

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $K$, define its centre by \[ Z(K)=\{z\in K:zk=kz\text{ for every }k\in K\}. \] For a finite non-abelian group $K$, its non-commuting graph $\Gamma_K$ is the simple undirected graph with vertex set $K\setminus Z(K)$, in which distinct vertices $x,y$ are adjacent exactly when $xy\ne yx$. Thus $\Gamma_G\cong\Gamma_H$ means that there exists a bijection \[ f:G\setminus Z(G)\longrightarrow H\setminus Z(H) \] such that, for every pair of distinct vertices $x,y$, \[ xy\ne yx\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(x)f(y)\ne f(y)f(x). \] For a group $K$, put $Z_0(K)=\{1\}$ and define $Z_{i+1}(K)$ as the inverse image of $Z(K/Z_i(K))$ under the quotient map. The group $K$ is nilpotent if $Z_c(K)=K$ for some integer $c\geq0$. Put $K^{(0)}=K$ and \[ K^{(i+1)}=\langle a^{-1}b^{-1}ab:a,b\in K^{(i)}\rangle. \] The group $K$ is solvable if $K^{(d)}=\{1\}$ for some integer $d\geq0$.

The remaining questions of the supplied record are the following two universal assertions, each quantified over all finite non-abelian groups $G,H$:

  1. (b) Is it true that \[ \left(\Gamma_G\cong\Gamma_H\ \text{and }H\text{ is nilpotent}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ is nilpotent}? \]
  2. (c) Is it true that \[ \left(\Gamma_G\cong\Gamma_H\ \text{and }H\text{ is solvable}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\text{ is solvable}? \]
No equality of group orders or inequality between centre orders is assumed in these questions.

For part (b), the supplied comment of 2009 reports an affirmative answer under the additional hypothesis $|H|=|G|$. The supplied comments of 2023 report an affirmative answer under the additional hypothesis $|Z(H)|\geq |Z(G)|$, citing a 2024 publication and describing the use of an earlier result with the further assumption that $C_H(h)$ is abelian for every $h\in H\setminus Z(H)$, where \[ C_H(h)=\{u\in H:uh=hu\}. \] These are supplied descriptions of partial results, not independently verified assertions here.

The record also retains archived part (a): for every pair of finite non-abelian groups $G,H$, does \[ \left(\Gamma_G\cong\Gamma_H\ \text{and }H\text{ is simple}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad G\cong H \] hold? Here a simple group is a nontrivial group whose only normal subgroups are $\{1\}$ and the whole group. The supplied archive labels part (a) affirmatively solved modulo the classification of finite simple groups, with separate references for sporadic simple groups, alternating groups, and simple groups of Lie type. Its comment of 2009 also reports an affirmative result for "groups with disconnected prime graphs"; the supplied wording does not explicitly identify which group bears this additional condition. This historical qualification is retained without choosing an interpretation or adding it to parts (b) and (c).

Short English statement

For finite non-abelian groups, does an isomorphism of their non-commuting graphs transfer nilpotence, or solvability, from one group to the other? The supplied record labels the problem partially solved, reports additional hypotheses giving affirmative answers to part (b), and archives the simple-group recognition question (a) as solved modulo the classification of finite simple groups. These source assessments require human review.
