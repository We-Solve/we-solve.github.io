Requested NLS reduction. Find a direct mathematical link from a three-dimensional NLS equation to a one-dimensional NLS equation by reduction to singular solutions, without passing through the LIA procedure. A precise realization would need to specify both equations, the relevant class of singular solutions, and the reduction and the sense in which it produces the one-dimensional evolution. Analogous compressible Euler question. Find a reduction of the three-dimensional compressible Euler equations to singular solutions supported on one-dimensional submanifolds. In dimensional notation, such a supporting submanifold would be $\Gamma\subset M$, with $\dim M=3$ and $\dim\Gamma=1$. The ambient domain, the Euler system and its constitutive assumptions, the meaning of support for its unknowns, and the resulting one-dimensional equations are not specified. Short English statement

Find a direct reduction from three-dimensional NLS to one-dimensional NLS through singular solutions, bypassing LIA. Seek an analogous reduction of three-dimensional compressible Euler flow to singular solutions supported on curves. The supplied source leaves the equations, solution notions, and meaning of reduction unspecified.
