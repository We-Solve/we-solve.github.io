Math-3906

Point-to-Nonincident-Line Separation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 2$, consider indexed points $p_1,\ldots,p_n$ in the closed unit square $[0,1]^2\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ and full affine lines $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_n\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ satisfying $p_i\in\ell_i$ for every $i$. Interpret distance as Euclidean distance: \[ d(p,\ell)=\inf_{x\in\ell}\|p-x\|_2, \qquad \delta((p_i,\ell_i)_{i=1}^n)=\min_{\substack{1\leq i,j\leq n\\i\ne j}}d(p_i,\ell_j). \] The minimum is over ordered pairs of distinct indices. The supplied reconstruction imposes no explicit distinctness assumptions on the points or lines and no prohibition on additional incidences $p_i\in\ell_j$ with $i\ne j$.

Define the extremal quantity \[ D_n=\sup\left\{\delta((p_i,\ell_i)_{i=1}^n):p_i\in[0,1]^2,\ \ell_i\text{ is an affine line in }{\mathbb{R}}^2,\ p_i\in\ell_i\text{ for all }i\right\}. \] The reconstructed problem asks to determine $D_n$, and in particular to prove or disprove the assertion \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N_{\varepsilon}\in{\mathbb{N}},\ N_{\varepsilon}\geq2,\ \forall n\geq N_{\varepsilon},\qquad D_n\leq n^{-1+\varepsilon}. \] Equivalently, the displayed bound must hold for every admissible configuration once $n$ is sufficiently large depending only on $\varepsilon$. The supremum expresses the largest possible separation without making an additional assertion about attainment.

This formulation follows the supplied reconstructed statement. The supplied original excerpt asks for the smallest point-to-line distance with unequal indices but does not itself specify the unit-square normalization, the paired incidences, or the asymptotic bound. The record labels the reconstruction as checked against the official report, while its supplied literature assessment says that normalization and hypotheses are missing. This tension remains unresolved; the formulation above is conditional on the reconstruction accurately representing the intended problem.

Short English statement

Among $n$ points in the closed unit square, each paired with a line through it, maximize the minimum Euclidean distance from a point to a line with a different index. The reconstructed question asks whether this separation is at most $n^{-1+\varepsilon}$ for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$ and all sufficiently large $n$. Its supplied open status and reconstruction require independent review.
