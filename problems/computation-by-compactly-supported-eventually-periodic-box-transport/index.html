Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every machine and input and positive computable viscosity, do effective coefficients $f_0,f_1$ on $R^3$ yield $f=f_0+\nu $ $f_1$ and a zero-data smooth Navier–Stokes solution with pressure zero, both supported in one fixed compact spatial set and having globally bounded mixed derivatives, one-periodic after $t=1$, whose particle from (4,0,0) enters $(-1,2)^3$ exactly on halting? Require uniqueness in the stated C H2 intersect C1 L2 comparison class and, for a fixed machine, an input-independent repeated field.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For the incompressible system $\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta u+f$, $\nabla\cdot u=0$, $u(0)=0$, choose viscosity $\nu>0$, whose value is computable. On $\mathbb R^3$, the construction supplies effective coefficients $f_0,f_1$ with $f=f_0+\nu f_1$ and an explicit zero-data solution $(U,0)$. Both fields vanish outside the same fixed compact spatial region, every mixed derivative is bounded, and both are period one after $t=1$. For the fixed particle $x_*=(4,0,0)$, \[\exists t\ge0:X(t;x_*)\in(-1,2)^3\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad M(w)\text{ halts}.\] The repeated mechanism depends only on the machine; uniqueness holds among $u\in CH^2\cap C^1L^2$ with $u,\nabla u$ bounded on any finite interval, and a $CH^1$ pressure representative after choosing its additive constant.

Outline of the argument

Full-box transport and finite computation: Localized curls evacuate overlapping source boxes into storage, deform them there, and deliver them along obstacle-avoiding detours. A three-stack recorder keeps the finite instruction system reversible. The construction checks observations at every intermediate time and derives effective derivative bounds, with separate torus and slow-clock variants treated under their stated comparison classes.
