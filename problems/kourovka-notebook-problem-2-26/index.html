Kourovka Notebook Problem 2.26

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A skew field is understood here to mean a division ring $D$ with $1\ne 0$, with commutative division rings allowed. Write $D^{\times}=D\setminus\{0\}$ for its multiplicative group. Interpret orderable in the ordered-division-ring sense: there exists a total order $<$ on $D$ such that, for all $a,b,c\in D$, \[ a<b\implies a+c<b+c, \qquad 0<a\ \text{and}\ 0<b\implies 0<ab. \] The supplied record does not explicitly define this terminology.

Characterize, by necessary and sufficient conditions expressed in terms of the abstract group, all groups $G$ satisfying \[ \exists\text{ an orderable division ring }D\quad G\cong D^{\times}. \] There is no finiteness or cardinality restriction in the supplied statement. The requested characterization concerns the full multiplicative group of nonzero elements; an order is not part of the given structure on $G$.

Short English statement

Determine exactly which abstract groups occur as the multiplicative groups of nonzero elements of orderable division rings. Here orderability is interpreted as the existence of a total order compatible with addition and with multiplication of positive elements.
