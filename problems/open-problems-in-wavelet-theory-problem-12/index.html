Open Problems in Wavelet Theory — Problem 12

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$. In the standard terminology, the Schwartz class is \[ \mathcal S({\mathbb{R}}^n)=\left\{f\in C^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{C}}):\sup_{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n}|x^\alpha\partial^\beta f(x)|<\infty\text{ for all }\alpha,\beta\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^n\right\}. \] Here $x^\alpha=\prod_{i=1}^n x_i^{\alpha_i}$ and $\partial^\beta=\prod_{i=1}^n\partial_{x_i}^{\beta_i}$. An integer expansive dilation is a matrix $A\in M_n({\mathbb{Z}})$ whose eigenvalues all have modulus greater than $1$. The prescribed translation lattice is $\Gamma={\mathbb{Z}}^n$.

The supplied question asks whether Schwartz class wavelets exist for these dilations and this lattice. The record does not define "wavelet" or specify the number of generators. Under the finite orthonormal wavelet-family interpretation, a precise candidate formulation is: for every $n\geq1$ and every integer expansive matrix $A\in M_n({\mathbb{Z}})$, do there exist a positive integer $r$ and functions $\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_r\in\mathcal S({\mathbb{R}}^n)$ such that \[ \left\{x\longmapsto |\det A|^{j/2}\psi_\ell(A^j x-k):j\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ k\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n,\ 1\leq\ell\leq r\right\} \] is an orthonormal basis of $L^2({\mathbb{R}}^n;{\mathbb{C}})$, with respect to Lebesgue measure?

This is a conditional formulation requiring confirmation against the source's wavelet conventions. The supplied record does not establish whether a single generator, a prescribed number of generators, or another wavelet notion is intended. Its description of the Schwartz class as all "$\infty$-regular" functions omits the rapid-decay conditions in the standard definition above; smoothness alone is insufficient. No additional restriction on $A$ or $n$ is supplied.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every integer expansive dilation, with translations by ${\mathbb{Z}}^n$, admits wavelets in the Schwartz class. The displayed formulation uses a finite orthonormal family; the intended wavelet convention and number of generators require source confirmation. The supplied registry labels the problem open.
