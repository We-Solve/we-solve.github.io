Math-5300

Mixed-cell multiplicity and the real parity obstruction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ be Laurent polynomials on $({\mathbb{C}}^*)^n$, with Newton polytopes $P_i$. Set \[ \operatorname{Log}(z)=(\log|z_1|,\ldots,\log|z_n|),\qquad \mathcal A_i=\operatorname{Log}(\{f_i=0\}). \] Let $K$ be a connected compact component of $\bigcap_i\mathcal A_i$ and let $N_K$ count common zeros in $\operatorname{Log}^{-1}(K)$ when that zero set is finite, with a multiplicity convention to be specified. The Ronkin function is \[ R_i(x)=\frac1{(2\pi)^n}\int_{[0,2\pi]^n} \log|f_i(e^{x+i\theta})|\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\theta. \] Write $\operatorname{MA}(R_1,\ldots,R_n)$ for the mixed Monge--Ampère measure, with its normalization kept explicit when comparing it to mixed volume.

Question A.2, contributed by Frédéric Bihan. Does the mixed Monge--Ampère mass of $K$ equal the volume/multiplicity of a mixed cell corresponding to $K$ in tropical Bernstein theory? Is there a bijection between the system's solutions in $\operatorname{Log}^{-1}(K)$ and the solutions of the mixed cell's binomial system, taking real solutions to real solutions? The existence of a distinguished cell corresponding to an ordinary amoeba-intersection component, the chosen regular subdivision/degeneration, nondegeneracy and multiplicity hypotheses are not specified. The background says the mixed measure equals the fixed system's root count, whereas the aid warns that the relevant local formula is a phase-averaged root count; this distinction prevents that background assertion from being adopted unqualified.

Precise binomial special case from the aid. For a transverse fully mixed cell, choose integer edge endpoints $u_i,v_i\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n$ and nonzero coefficients $a_i,b_i$, and put \[ Q=[u_1,v_1]+\cdots+[u_n,v_n],\qquad A_{ij}=(v_i-u_i)_j,\quad\det A\ne0. \] The associated binomial equations $a_i z^{u_i}+b_i z^{v_i}=0$ are equivalent to $z^{v_i-u_i}=c_i$, where $c_i=-a_i/b_i$. Over ${\mathbb{C}}$, they have exactly $|\det A|$ simple torus roots. This determinant is the Bernstein cell multiplicity, or parallelepiped volume with a fundamental lattice parallelepiped of volume one; it must not be confused with an additional $n!$-rescaling of simplex-normalized volume. In an isolated transverse regular non-Archimedean degeneration in characteristic zero, with this binomial initial system and valid simple-root lifting hypotheses, the aid asserts that the corresponding valuation cluster has the same $|\det A|$ roots. A valuation cluster in a specified degeneration is not automatically $\operatorname{Log}^{-1}(K)$ for an arbitrary original complex system.

For real binomial coefficients, encode the signs by $\operatorname{sgn}(c_i)=(-1)^{\tau_i}$ and write $\overline A=A\bmod2$. Then the exact number of real torus roots of the binomial system is \[ \begin{cases} 0,&\tau\notin\operatorname{im}(\overline A:{\mathbb{F}}_2^n\to{\mathbb{F}}_2^n),\\ 2^{n-\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{F}}_2}\overline A}, &\tau\in\operatorname{im}\overline A. \end{cases} \] The absolute values are fixed by the invertible real linear system $A\log|z|=\log|c|$; the parity equation controls the possible signs. Thus cell volume alone contains insufficient real-root information. A bijection sending real roots of the original system to real binomial roots requires at least the corresponding real-count inequality; a conjugation-equivariant bijection would require equality. The source asks only the forward real-preservation condition, not explicitly equivariance or real preservation by the inverse. Applying the parity formula to an actual correspondence requires the missing degeneration and lifting data, not merely equality of complex multiplicities.

Short English statement

Bihan asks whether an amoeba-intersection component is counted by a corresponding tropical mixed cell and admits a real-preserving binomial correspondence. The supplied aid gives the determinant multiplicity and an exact signed parity test for a transverse binomial cell, while warning that a phase-averaged measure and a degeneration's valuation cluster do not automatically count the original fixed-phase solutions.
