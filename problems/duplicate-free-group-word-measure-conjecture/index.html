Math-4001

Duplicate Free-Group Word-Measure Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\in{\mathbb{N}}$ with $n\geq 2$, and let $F=F(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ be a free group with the indicated free basis. For any finite group $G$ and any word $w\in F$, define the word map \[ w_G:G^n\longrightarrow G,\qquad (g_1,\ldots,g_n)\longmapsto w(g_1,\ldots,g_n), \] where evaluation substitutes $g_i$ for $x_i$. The associated word measure is the pushforward of the uniform probability measure on $G^n$: for every subset $S\subseteq G$, \[ P_{w,G}(S)=\frac{\lvert w_G^{-1}(S)\rvert}{\lvert G\rvert^n}. \] Here $\operatorname{Aut}(F)$ denotes the group of automorphisms of $F$.

Conjecture 4, as reconstructed from the supplied extraction. For every finite rank $n\geq 2$, every free group $F$ of rank $n$, and every pair $w,w'\in F$, \[ \left[\text{for every finite group }G\text{ and every }S\subseteq G,\quad P_{w,G}(S)=P_{w',G}(S)\right] \ \Longrightarrow\ \left[\text{there exists }\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(F)\text{ such that }\varphi(w)=w'\right]. \] Both word measures use the same chosen basis of $F$. No exclusion of the identity word or restriction to a particular class of finite groups is supplied.

Short English statement

The extracted conjecture asks whether two words in the same non-abelian free group must be related by a free-group automorphism whenever their evaluations have identical probability distributions in every finite group. The supplied record is flagged as an unrecoverable duplicate, and its probability formula needs reconstruction. Its partially solved label and listed special cases are supplied dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
