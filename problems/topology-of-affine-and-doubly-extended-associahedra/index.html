Topology of Affine and Doubly Extended Associahedra

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $k\geq 0$, let $S^k=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{k+1}:\|x\|=1\}$. Write $X\cong Y$ for homeomorphism and $X\simeq Y$ for homotopy equivalence. Products of spheres carry the product topology. For a cluster algebra $\mathcal A$, its cluster complex $\Delta(\mathcal A)$ is the abstract simplicial complex whose vertices are cluster variables and whose faces are finite subsets contained in a cluster; topological assertions refer to its geometric realization $|\Delta(\mathcal A)|$. The rank is the number of mutable variables in a seed.

Let $\operatorname{Ass}_{\mathrm{aff}}(\mathcal A)$ and $\operatorname{Ass}_{\mathrm{dbl}}(\mathcal A)$ denote, respectively, the affine generalized associahedron and the doubly extended associahedron intended in the cited report. These are placeholders for the report's constructions: the supplied record does not define their cells, identifications, or topology. It also does not define the precise class of doubly extended cluster algebras or specify coefficient conventions.

The supplied passage attributes the following assertions to Conjecture 7:

  1. For every affine cluster algebra $\mathcal A$ of rank $n+1$, \[ \operatorname{Ass}_{\mathrm{aff}}(\mathcal A)\cong S^n. \]
  2. For every doubly extended cluster algebra $\mathcal A$ of rank $n+2$, \[ |\Delta(\mathcal A)|\simeq S^{n-1}. \]
  3. For every doubly extended cluster algebra $\mathcal A$, the associated doubly extended associahedron is asserted to have a product-of-spheres description. The apparent intended formulas are \[ \operatorname{Ass}_{\mathrm{dbl}}(\mathcal A)\cong \begin{cases} S^{n-1}\times S^2,&\text{outside the exceptional type},\\ S^7\times S^1\times S^1,&\text{in the exceptional type}. \end{cases} \] The exceptional type appears to be $E_8^{(1,1)}$, but its superscript is displaced in the supplied extraction. The exponents in the exceptional sphere product are likewise displaced. Thus this display is an explicitly tentative reconstruction, not a verified transcription. The use of $\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal A)=n+2$ in this third assertion appears to continue the convention in the second assertion, but is not restated in the extracted third assertion.
The assertions are universally quantified over the source's intended classes. Their precise admissible ranks are not supplied. The sphere notation defined above covers the first assertion when $n\geq0$ and the other assertions when $n\geq1$; the record does not resolve any intended lower-rank cases or convention for $S^{-1}$. These missing construction, scope, and transcription details must be resolved before this becomes a fully specified mathematical statement.

Short English statement

Conjecture 7 predicts that affine generalized associahedra are spheres, that doubly extended cluster complexes have the homotopy type of spheres, and that doubly extended associahedra are products of spheres with one exceptional type. The source constructions, rank bounds, and damaged exceptional formula require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
