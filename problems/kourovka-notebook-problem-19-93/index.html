Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.93

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{N}}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. For a prime $p$, let $C_p$ denote the cyclic group of order $p$, and let \[ W_p=C_p\wr C_p=(C_p)^p\rtimes C_p \] be the regular wreath product, in which the final $C_p$ acts by cyclically permuting the $p$ coordinates. An epimorphic image of a group means the image of a surjective group homomorphism; thus $G$ has no epimorphic image isomorphic to $W_p$ precisely when no normal subgroup $N\trianglelefteq G$ satisfies $G/N\cong W_p$.

For this draft, interpret the $p$-lower central series as the lower exponent-$p$ central series \[ P_1(G)=G,\qquad P_{n+1}(G)=P_n(G)^p[P_n(G),G]\quad(n\geq1). \] Here $H^p$ is the subgroup generated by the $p$-th powers of elements of $H$, and $[H,G]$ is generated by the commutators $[h,g]=h^{-1}g^{-1}hg$, with $h\in H$ and $g\in G$. The supplied record does not define its series convention; this interpretation requires confirmation against the original source.

For a set $S$ of primes, consider the assertion \[ \begin{gathered} \exists f:{\mathbb{N}}\times{\mathbb{N}}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{N}}\quad \forall p\in S\quad\forall d\in{\mathbb{N}}\quad \forall\text{ finite }p\text{-groups }G:\\ \left( G\text{ is generated by }d\text{ elements and } \nexists N\trianglelefteq G\text{ with }G/N\cong W_p\right) \Longrightarrow \left(\forall n\geq1,\quad |P_n(G)/P_{n+1}(G)|\leq f(p,d)\right). \end{gathered} \] Thus the bound may depend on $p$ and $d$, but must be independent of $G$ and the series index $n$. The source asks for this assertion in the two cases \[ \text{(a) }S=\{p:p\text{ is prime and }p\geq7\}, \qquad \text{(b) }S=\{2,3,5\}. \] The phrase "with $d$ generators" is interpreted here as admitting a generating set of size at most $d$; the record does not explicitly require $d$ to be the minimal number of generators.

Supplied status qualification. The registry labels the problem partially solved. Its supplied discussion reports that part (a) is refuted for $p\geq7$, attributing an AI solution to M. Kinyon and J. Urban and reporting confirmation by P. Spiga. This is a supplied source assessment, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion. Part (b) is retained as the remaining conjectural question in that record. The source also invites comparison with a characterization by Aner Shalev of finitely generated $p$-adic analytic pro-$p$ groups, without supplying the characterization or a precise citation.

Short English statement

P. Spiga asks whether excluding the quotient $C_p\wr C_p$ forces every factor of the $p$-lower central series of a finite $d$-generated $p$-group to have order bounded solely in terms of $p$ and $d$. The supplied record reports a refutation for primes $p\geq7$ and retains the cases $p=2,3,5$ as conjectural. The precise series convention requires source confirmation.
