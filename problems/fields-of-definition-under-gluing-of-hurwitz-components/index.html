Fields of Definition under Gluing of Hurwitz Components

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns fields of definition of components of Hurwitz spaces, but does not specify the Hurwitz spaces, the admissible fields, or the gluing operation.

For clarity, a conventional meaning of descent can be described conditionally. Let $K$ be a field, let $\overline K$ be an algebraic closure, and suppose that the relevant ambient space $H$ is defined over $K$. A specified geometric component $C\subseteq H_{\overline K}$ is defined over $K$, in the sense of embedded descent, if there is a closed subscheme $C_0\subseteq H$ whose base change to $\overline K$ is $C$, with its specified embedding. Whether the source intends this meaning, a field of moduli, or descent of additional covering data is not specified.

Question, in schematic form. For every field $K$ and every collection of Hurwitz-space components admitted by the intended construction, if all input components are defined over $K$, must each component obtained from them by the intended gluing construction also be defined over $K$?

This restates the supplied target, subject to unresolved qualifications: the permitted fields and input collections, the moduli problem, the meaning of component, the gluing construction, and the rule selecting its output components are absent. In particular, the universal quantifiers above range only over the unspecified admissible data; they do not assert that arbitrary fields or arbitrary Hurwitz components admit this construction. A fully precise mathematical formulation requires this missing context.

Short English statement

The question asks whether gluing Hurwitz-space components defined over a field $K$ preserves definition over $K$. The supplied record omits the construction and its hypotheses, so the statement remains schematic and requires human mathematical review.
