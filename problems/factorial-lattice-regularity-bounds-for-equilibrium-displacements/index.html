Factorial Lattice-Regularity Bounds for Equilibrium Displacements

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a lattice function $f$, the forward discrete gradient is $Df(\ell)=(f(\ell+e_i)-f(\ell))_{i=1}^d$, with $e_i$ the coordinate lattice vectors; $D^j$ is its iterated tensor-valued finite difference. The displacement $\bar u$ is measured relative to the reference lattice and satisfies the source's equilibrium equations and defect boundary conditions. The norm $|D^j\bar u(\ell)|$ is a fixed finite-dimensional tensor norm. The proposed constant $C$ is independent of differentiation order $j$ and far-field site $\ell$.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Let $\bar u:\mathbb Z^d\to\mathbb R^d$ be an equilibrium displacement field for a crystalline defect. Is there a constant $C>0$ such that, for every $j\geq1$ and lattice site $\ell$ away from the defect,

$$\lvert D^j\bar u(\ell)\rvert\leq Cj!\lvert\ell\rvert^{1-d-j}$$

hold?

Source scope and qualification.
The report already gives bounds with order-dependent constants $C_j$; the target is the stronger common-$C$ factorial estimate. The defect model, stability and meaning of away from the defect must remain the source hypotheses.

Short English statement

Can the far-field regularity constants for crystalline-defect equilibria be controlled by a single factorial bound in every derivative order?
