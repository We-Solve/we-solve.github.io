Chamber Structures of Schur-Positive Multiplicity Functions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology, a partition is a finite weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers, with the empty partition also allowed. Write $s_\lambda$ for the Schur function indexed by a partition $\lambda$. In a Schur expansion \[ F=\sum_{\lambda}a_\lambda s_\lambda, \] Schur positivity means that the coefficients $a_\lambda$ are nonnegative. If these coefficients are intended as representation multiplicities, they must be nonnegative integers. The supplied record does not specify the coefficient ring, the permitted class of symmetric functions or formal series, or whether $F$ denotes a single function or a parameterized family.

A conventional coefficient multiplicity function would be \[ m_F(\lambda)=[s_\lambda]F=a_\lambda. \] This is a possible interpretation of the notation, not a definition established by the supplied source record: the record does not identify the arguments of $m_F$, their domain, or their relation to Schur coefficients.

For clarity about the requested terminology, suppose an index domain $D$ lies in a finite-rank lattice $L$. A function $f:D\to{\mathbb{Q}}$ has a quasipolynomial description on a specified region $C\subseteq L\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{R}}$ if there are a finite-index sublattice $L'\subseteq L$ and polynomials $p_\gamma$, one for each $\gamma\in L/L'$, such that \[ f(x)=p_\gamma(x)\qquad \text{for }x\in D\cap C\cap(\gamma+L'). \] A piecewise-quasipolynomial chamber description supplies such descriptions on specified polyhedral regions covering the domain, with their boundaries accounted for. This explains the terminology conditionally; the record supplies neither such a lattice nor a chamber decomposition, and does not establish that such a description exists.

Research question as supplied. Characterize the multiplicity functions $m_F$ arising from Schur-positive symmetric functions, including their piecewise-quasipolynomial chamber structures and the behavior of \[ m_F(\omega_0+k\omega). \] The intended class of $F$, the definition and domain of $m_F$, the meanings of $\omega_0$ and $\omega$, and the range of $k$ are unresolved. Any specialization of this expression must specify these data and ensure that its argument belongs to the domain of $m_F$. The source also leaves unspecified whether the desired behavior concerns exact formulas, eventual quasipolynomiality, growth, or another property. Consequently, the record supports this research question but does not determine a fully quantified conjecture or theorem.

Short English statement

The question asks for a description of multiplicities associated with Schur-positive symmetric functions, their possible quasipolynomial formulas on chambers, and their behavior along expressions $\omega_0+k\omega$. The supplied record leaves the multiplicity function, index domain, parameters, and requested behavior unspecified, so a precise conjecture requires further source clarification.
