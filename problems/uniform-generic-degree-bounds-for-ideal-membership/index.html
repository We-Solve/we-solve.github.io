Math-4718

Uniform Generic Degree Bounds for Ideal Membership

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A standard graded $k$-algebra is a finitely generated commutative graded algebra $R=\bigoplus_{m\geq 0}R_m$ with $R_0=k$, generated over $k$ by $R_1$. Write $d=\dim R$ for its Krull dimension. For prescribed degrees $a_1,\ldots,a_n$, homogeneous elements $f_i\in R_{a_i}$ generate the homogeneous ideal \[ I=(f_1,\ldots,f_n),\qquad I_m=\sum_{i=1}^n f_iR_{m-a_i}, \] where $R_j=0$ for $j<0$. Thus $R_{m_0}\subseteq I$ means $I_{m_0}=R_{m_0}$.

The supplied question asks whether there is a nonnegative integer \[ m_0=M(d;a_1,\ldots,a_n) \] that works uniformly for every standard graded algebra of dimension $d$ under consideration and for generic choices of the $f_i$. The bound is to depend only on $d$ and the prescribed degree list, rather than on the individual algebra or chosen forms.

One precise geometric interpretation of generic choices can be expressed using the affine parameter space \[ \mathcal P_R=\prod_{i=1}^n R_{a_i}. \] Under this interpretation, the requested property is that there exists a nonempty Zariski open subset $U_R\subseteq\mathcal P_R$ such that, for every algebraically closed extension $K/k$ and every tuple $(f_1,\ldots,f_n)\in U_R(K)$, \[ (R\otimes_k K)_{M(d;a_1,\ldots,a_n)} \subseteq (f_1,\ldots,f_n)\subseteq R\otimes_k K. \] Here the open subset may depend on $R$, whereas the integer bound must be uniform. This geometric interpretation is a possible precise reading, not a verified additional source hypothesis.

The supplied record does not specify restrictions on the field, the relation between $n$ and $d$, whether the degrees must be positive, or any hypothesis that the generated ideal has finite colength. It also does not specify whether genericity is geometric or refers to $k$-rational choices. These omissions prevent an unqualified formulation of the intended universal assertion. The requested containment concerns ordinary ideal membership. The supplied background distinguishes it from a reported tight-closure bound; it does not provide the hypotheses or statement of that bound.

Short English statement

The question asks for a degree, depending only on the dimension and the degrees of the chosen forms, at which generic forms generate every homogeneous element of an arbitrary standard graded algebra. The field assumptions, admissible generators, and meaning of genericity require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
