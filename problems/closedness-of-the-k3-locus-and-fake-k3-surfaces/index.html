Closedness of the K3 Locus and Fake K3 Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses two questions. Its base field, topology, and meaning of "fake K3 surface" are not specified; the following makes the conventional interpretation of the first question explicit while retaining those limitations.

For an intended algebraically closed base field $k$, let $M_g$ denote the moduli space of smooth, projective, geometrically connected curves of genus $g$ over $k$. Under the projective interpretation, a K3 surface is a smooth, projective, geometrically connected surface $S$ with $\omega_S\cong\mathcal O_S$ and $H^1(S,\mathcal O_S)=0$. Define \[ (K3)_g=\left\{[C]\in M_g(k):\text{there exist a K3 surface }S/k\text{ and a closed immersion }C\hookrightarrow S\right\}. \] No primitivity, ampleness, or polarization-degree restriction is stated in the supplied problem.

  1. For each integer $g\geq12$, is $(K3)_g$ closed in $M_g$? If "closed" means Zariski closed, the question is whether this set is the set of $k$-points of a Zariski-closed subset of $M_g$, with closure taken inside the moduli space of smooth curves. The source does not specify the field or its characteristic, nor explicitly identify the topology; this formulation is conditional on these conventions and does not assert a question uniformly over all fields.
  2. Can Lazarsfeld's proof of the Gieseker--Petri theorem be generalized to curves on fake K3 surfaces? For reference, for a smooth projective curve $C$ and a line bundle $L$ on $C$, the Petri multiplication map is \[ \mu_{C,L}:H^0(C,L)\otimes_k H^0(C,\omega_C\otimes L^{-1})\longrightarrow H^0(C,\omega_C). \] The Petri condition is injectivity of $\mu_{C,L}$ for every line bundle $L$. The supplied record does not define fake K3 surfaces, identify the allowed curves or linear systems on them, or specify whether a general or every curve is intended. Consequently, this second question remains a request for a proof generalization with unresolved hypotheses; it cannot be replaced by a precise universal Petri assertion from the supplied information.

Short English statement

For each $g\geq12$, ask whether the smooth genus-$g$ curves that embed in K3 surfaces form a closed locus in $M_g$. Separately, ask whether Lazarsfeld's Gieseker--Petri argument extends to curves on fake K3 surfaces. The field, topology, meaning of fake K3, and hypotheses of the proposed extension require clarification.
