A fixed-point reconstruction ambiguity.
On $M_2(\mathbb C)$, let $U_m=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{2\pi i/m})$, $m\ge2$, and let the cyclic group act by $\alpha_j(x)=U_m^jxU_m^{-j}$. Averaging gives \[ E_m\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&d\end{pmatrix}, \] because $\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}e^{2\pi ij/m}=0$. The fixed algebra and its averaging expectation are therefore identical for every $m$, while the acting cyclic automorphism groups have different orders. Each action is faithful, since $\alpha_j$ fixes the off-diagonal matrix unit only when $j$ is divisible by $m$.
What fails in a naive bijection.
Recovering an arbitrary compact group merely from the range of an averaging projection cannot be injective in this diagnostic class. Groups are special hypergroups, so adding the word hypergroup cannot by itself repair that loss of action information. Additional canonicality and admissibility hypotheses must do the work.
