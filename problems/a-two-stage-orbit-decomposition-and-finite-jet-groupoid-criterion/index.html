Math-1694

A two-stage orbit decomposition and finite-jet groupoid criterion

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth CR manifold and $p\in M$. A local CR automorphism is a smooth CR diffeomorphism $h:U\to V$ between open subsets of $M$. Define its full local-automorphism orbit by \[ O(p)=\{q\in M:\text{there are }U\ni p,\ V\ni q,\ h:U\overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}V\text{ CR},\ h(p)=q\}. \] D. Zaitsev asks whether $O(p)$ is always a manifold and, if it is, whether \[ T_qO(p)=D_q,\qquad D_q=\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{R}}}\{X(q):X\text{ is a local infinitesimal CR automorphism near }q\} \quad(q\in O(p)). \] Here an infinitesimal CR automorphism is a real vector field whose local flow consists of CR diffeomorphisms. The local interpretation is natural for the local orbit; globally defined vector fields may give a different span. The original word "manifold" does not specify embedded versus immersed topology.

The source remark reports affirmative answers when $M$ is minimal, holomorphically nondegenerate and real analytic. In the usual terminology, minimality means the CR orbit is locally open, and holomorphic nondegeneracy excludes a nonzero germ of holomorphic vector field tangent to a real-analytic CR realization. Exact hypotheses and the reference for this special case are not supplied.

The aid's general reduction uses the family of all local infinitesimal CR automorphisms. Its Stefan--Sussmann flow leaves $L$ are connected immersed integral manifolds with \[ T_qL=D_q. \] They lie in $O(p)$ and the full orbit is a disjoint union of them. Local CR automorphisms preserve this family and its distribution, so all leaves occurring in a fixed $O(p)$ have the same dimension. These canonical leaves are asserted to have the initial immersed structure; the full pseudogroup orbit can still be a larger union of leaves.

Suppose $O(p)$ has a compatible initial manifold structure, meaning its inclusion in $M$ is an injective immersion with the corresponding initial smooth-map property. The aid states that \[ D_q=T_qO(p)\ \text{for all }q\in O(p) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{every infinitesimal leaf is open in }O(p). \] Equivalently, the quotient of $O(p)$ by these leaves is discrete in the quotient topology. This does not assume that an arbitrary full local-automorphism orbit already possesses such a manifold structure.

A further sufficient condition is a regular finite-jet Lie-groupoid realization of all local CR automorphism germs. To make the needed compatibility explicit, let $\mathcal G\rightrightarrows M$ have source and target maps $s,t$, have the same orbit relation as the full CR pseudogroup, and reconstruct smooth local CR transformations so that its Lie-algebroid anchor \[ a=\left.{\,\mathrm{d}} t\right|_{\ker{\,\mathrm{d}} s\text{ at units}} \] has image $D$. Under the reported regular finite-jet realization hypotheses, the groupoid orbit $t(s^{-1}(p))=O(p)$ is an initial immersed manifold with tangent space $D$. If the groupoid is additionally proper, meaning $(s,t):\mathcal G\to M\times M$ is proper, its orbits are closed embedded submanifolds. Properness is a sufficient upgrade here, not a claimed necessary condition. Existence of this groupoid, its regularity and smooth reconstruction are additional conditions not established for arbitrary smooth CR manifolds.

Short English statement

Ask whether the full local CR-automorphism orbit is a manifold and whether local infinitesimal automorphisms span its tangent space. The supplied aid separates that orbit from its infinitesimal flow leaves and gives a conditional finite-jet Lie-groupoid criterion, with properness yielding embedded orbits.
