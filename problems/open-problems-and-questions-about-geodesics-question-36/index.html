Open Problems and Questions about Geodesics — Question 36

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed manifold, meaning compact and without boundary, with $\dim M\geq 3$. The supplied record denotes its space of metrics by $\mathcal{G}(M)$, but does not specify the regularity of the metrics or the topology on this space. Under the intended Riemannian interpretation, with sufficient regularity to define a geodesic flow, set \[ S_gM=\{(x,v)\in TM:g_x(v,v)=1\}, \] and let $\varphi_g^t:S_gM\to S_gM$, $t\in{\mathbb{R}}$, be the geodesic flow. Call this flow transitive precisely when \[ \exists z\in S_gM\qquad \overline{\{\varphi_g^t(z):t\in{\mathbb{R}}\}}^{\,S_gM}=S_gM. \] Thus the source's notation $SM$ is interpreted as the unit tangent bundle for the metric under consideration.

Write \[ \mathcal{T}(M)=\{g\in\mathcal{G}(M):\varphi_g^t\text{ has a dense orbit in }S_gM\}. \] The question asks whether, for every closed $M$ of dimension at least three, \[ \overline{\mathcal{T}(M)}^{\,\tau}=\mathcal{G}(M), \] where $\tau$ must be the intended topology on $\mathcal{G}(M)$. Equivalently, does every nonempty $\tau$-open subset of $\mathcal{G}(M)$ contain a metric with transitive geodesic flow?

The supplied excerpt leaves $\mathcal{G}(M)$ and $\tau$ undefined, so this is a parameterized formulation rather than a fully specified density assertion. It also does not state a connectedness hypothesis on $M$; connectedness has not been added here. These qualifications require clarification from the source context.

Short English statement

For a closed manifold of dimension at least three, must metrics whose geodesic flow has a dense orbit be dense among all metrics? The metric class, its topology, and any intended connectedness hypothesis are not specified in the supplied excerpt.
