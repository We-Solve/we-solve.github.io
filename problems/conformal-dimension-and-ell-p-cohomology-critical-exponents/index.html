Math-3402

Conformal Dimension and $\ell^p$-Cohomology Critical Exponents

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,d)$ be a compact uniformly perfect metric space. One standard uniform-perfectness convention requires a constant $c>1$ such that, whenever $X\setminus B_d(x,r)\ne\varnothing$, the annulus $B_d(x,r)\setminus B_d(x,r/c)$ is nonempty. The source gives no further doubling, regularity or Poincaré hypothesis.

In the usual quasisymmetric convention, the conformal gauge $\mathcal J(X,d)$ consists of metrics $\rho$ on $X$ for which the identity from $(X,d)$ to $(X,\rho)$ is quasisymmetric: there exists an increasing homeomorphism $\eta:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ such that \[ \frac{\rho(x,a)}{\rho(x,b)}\leq \eta\left(\frac{d(x,a)}{d(x,b)}\right) \] for all distinct $x,a,b$. The ordinary conformal dimension is then \[ \operatorname{Cdim}(X,d)=\inf_{\rho\in\mathcal J(X,d)}\dim_H(X,\rho). \] These standard conventions make explicit one meaning of the displayed terms; the record does not itself define the gauge. Restricting the infimum to Ahlfors-regular metrics would instead give an Ahlfors-regular conformal-dimension variant and is not silently imposed.

Retain $p(\mathcal J(X,d))$ for the source's critical exponent of first $\ell^p$-cohomology over that gauge. The question is whether \[ \forall (X,d)\text{ compact and uniformly perfect},\qquad \operatorname{Cdim}(X,d)=p(\mathcal J(X,d)). \] The critical exponent is not defined in the record. To recover it one needs the graph, filling or other cochain model attached to the gauge, the class of $p$, coefficient and summability conventions, and whether cohomology is reduced. A threshold for nonzero cohomology, a threshold for boundary functions separating points, and a threshold involving a reduced cohomology group are different definitions. No formula of the form $\inf\{p:H^1_{\ell^p}\ne0\}$ is assigned to the source without that missing specification. How the invariant is taken "over" the gauge, including any infimum or invariance statement, is also absent.

The 2026-08-21 literature assessment reports the equality under Ahlfors-regularity and a Poincaré inequality, while leaving the unrestricted question unresolved. For context Ahlfors $Q$-regularity ordinarily means a measure satisfying $C^{-1}r^Q\leq\mu(B(x,r))\leq Cr^Q$ over the intended scale range. The cited Poincaré exponent and precise theorem hypotheses are not supplied. Those additional conditions are retained only as an attributed special-case report, not appended to the universal question. The open label and notation-only verification note are dated metadata, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

Does ordinary conformal dimension equal the source's first-$\ell^p$-cohomology critical exponent for every compact uniformly perfect metric space? The unrestricted scope is retained. The precise cohomology model and critical-exponent definition are missing, and the source's reported regular/Poincaré cases are kept separate from the general question.
