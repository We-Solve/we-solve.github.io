An ultrafilter-free local criterion for O2-ultrapower embeddability

OpenAI's claim

One explicit separable unital full group C*-algebra is claimed to embed into no norm ultrapower of a nonzero unital nuclear algebra, giving a negative instance for O_2 as well. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let \[ G=\mathbb Z[1/2]^3\rtimes(\operatorname{SL}_3(\mathbb Z)\times\mathbb Z),\qquad (M,k)v=2^kMv,\qquad A=C^*(G). \] OpenAI claims that for any nuclear $C^*$-algebra $B$ assumed nonzero and unital, and for any free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\mathbb N$, a unital embedding $A\hookrightarrow B^\omega$ is impossible. This also excludes $\mathcal O_2^\omega$ as a target. The algebra $A$ is the full group algebra and is separable and unital; $B^\omega=\ell^\infty(\mathbb N,B)/\{(b_n):\lim_\omega\|b_n\|=0\}$ is the norm ultrapower. This addresses nuclear norm-ultrapower embeddings.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 292, 2026-09-23): An ascending HNN construction supplies a property-T base with finite-index self-embeddings. A nuclear tensor-product estimate restricts the finite-dimensional representation types approximated by arbitrary unitary tuples, uniformly in dimension. Applying this to coordinate lifts of a proposed ultrapower embedding and then inducing through the self-embedding produces incompatible finite sets of representation types.

Formal verification

For family 292, the scope page documents the following selections. The selected full-group algebra has no unital embedding in a norm ultrapower of any nonzero unital nuclear target. In particular, the Cuntz-algebra ultrapower target is excluded. The documented statement covers the main claim of manuscript 1. Its selected declaration also covers the clause used for this problem.
