Math-539

Group Actions on 1-Manifolds — Question 33

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $\mathrm{Diff}^{\omega}_+(S^1)$ be the group, under composition, of orientation-preserving real-analytic diffeomorphisms of $S^1$. For a group $G$, write $G'=[G,G]$ for its commutator subgroup and $G''=[G',G']$. The group $G$ is metabelian if $G''=\{1\}$. Let $F_2$ denote the free group on two generators.

Under the interpretation that the source's phrase "free subgroup" means a nonabelian free subgroup, its explicit question is whether \[ \forall G\leq \mathrm{Diff}^{\omega}_+(S^1),\qquad G''\neq\{1\}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\text{ an injective group homomorphism }F_2\longrightarrow G. \] No finite-generation hypothesis is imposed in the supplied formulation.

The source calls this a question about the Tits alternative and then gives the displayed implication in words. The conventional Tits alternative uses a virtually solvable alternative, whereas this explicit formulation uses metabelianity itself. Their equivalence is not supplied and is not asserted here. Also, the source does not explicitly specify that the free subgroup must be nonabelian; the displayed statement records that interpretation as a qualification requiring review.

Short English statement

The explicit question asks whether every non-metabelian subgroup of the orientation-preserving real-analytic diffeomorphism group of the circle contains a copy of $F_2$, interpreting "free subgroup" as nonabelian. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that status is not independently verified.
