Math-7292

Perimeter-Normalized Neumann Eigenvalue Maximizers

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal C$ denote the class of nonempty bounded open convex sets $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$. Boundedness and nonempty interior are the conventional hypotheses used here to make the spectral optimization precise; they are implicit rather than explicit in the supplied statement. Write $L(\Omega)=\mathcal H^1(\partial\Omega)$ for the perimeter. Define the first positive Neumann eigenvalue of the Laplacian by \[ \mu_1(\Omega)=\inf_{\substack{u\in H^1(\Omega)\setminus\{0\}\\ \int_\Omega u\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x=0}} \frac{\int_\Omega |\nabla u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}{\int_\Omega |u|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}. \] Thus the Neumann boundary condition is understood in the weak sense. Set $F(\Omega)=\mu_1(\Omega)L(\Omega)^2$. Let $Q=(0,1)^2$, and let $T$ be the interior of the equilateral triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(1,0)$, and $(1/2,\sqrt3/2)$.

Determine \[ \sup_{\Omega\in\mathcal C}F(\Omega) \] and all domains attaining it. In particular, is it true that \[ F(\Omega)\leq F(Q)=F(T)\qquad\text{for every }\Omega\in\mathcal C, \] with equality if and only if $\Omega$ is similar to $Q$ or $T$? Here similarity allows translation, rotation, reflection, and positive uniform scaling.

The source additionally asks whether an underlying principle explains the equal values for the square and equilateral triangle. It does not specify a mathematical criterion for such an explanation, so this remains a qualitative part of the question.

Short English statement

Among bounded convex planar domains, determine the largest first positive Neumann eigenvalue multiplied by the square of the perimeter, and characterize every maximizing shape. The proposed maximizers are exactly squares and equilateral triangles, up to similarity. The source also asks for a conceptual explanation of their equal values; that request has no specified formal criterion.
