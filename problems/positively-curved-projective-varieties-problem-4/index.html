Positively Curved Projective Varieties — Problem 4

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,g)$ be a compact Kähler manifold whose holomorphic sectional curvature is nonnegative in every nonzero holomorphic tangent direction. Write $\pi_1(X,x)$ for its fundamental group at a base point $x$. The source poses the following three questions.
  1. Is $\pi_1(X,x)$ an "extension of a finite group by $\mathbb Z^{\oplus 2m}$" for some integer $m\geq 0$? Under the usual convention for an extension of a group by another group, this asks for a finite group $G$ and an exact sequence \[ 1\longrightarrow \mathbb Z^{\oplus 2m}\longrightarrow \pi_1(X,x)\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow 1. \] The source gives no exact sequence. If the quoted wording instead intends a finite kernel and free abelian quotient, the sequence would be \[ 1\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow \pi_1(X,x)\longrightarrow \mathbb Z^{\oplus 2m}\longrightarrow 1. \] These formulations must be distinguished; the intended ordering requires source review.
  2. For an appropriate finite étale cover $p:X^*\to X$, consider its Albanese map \[ \alpha:X^*\longrightarrow \operatorname{Alb}(X^*). \] Here a finite étale cover is a finite unramified holomorphic covering, and $\operatorname{Alb}(X^*)$ is the Albanese complex torus, with its map determined up to translation after the usual base-point choice. Does the fiber $F$ admit a Kähler metric $g_F$ such that \[ n_{\mathrm{tf}}(F,g_F)=\dim F? \] The invariant $n_{\mathrm{tf}}$ is not defined in the supplied record, so this equality is retained as a source condition whose precise meaning requires verification. The source does not explicitly require $g_F$ to have semi-positive holomorphic sectional curvature.
  3. For the fiber $F$ of the Albanese map of an appropriate finite étale cover as above, is $F$ projective and rationally connected? For a smooth projective variety, rational connectedness means that two general points can be joined by the image of a morphism from $\mathbb P^1$.
The expression "appropriate finite étale cover" has no stated selection criterion. The record also does not specify whether $F$ denotes every fiber, a general fiber, or a selected fiber; whether the relevant fibers are smooth and connected; or whether one cover must serve both fiber questions. These qualifications remain unresolved, and no smoothness, connectedness, surjectivity, or additional curvature assertion about $\alpha$ or $F$ is assumed here.

Short English statement

Matsumura asks about the fundamental group of a compact Kähler manifold with semi-positive holomorphic sectional curvature and about the fibers of the Albanese map after a suitable finite étale cover. The fiber questions concern a metric condition involving $n_{\mathrm{tf}}$, and projectivity and rational connectedness. The supplied record leaves the invariant and several cover and fiber qualifications unspecified.
