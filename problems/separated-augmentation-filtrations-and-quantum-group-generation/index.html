Math-5005

Separated Augmentation Filtrations and Quantum Group Generation

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote the ground field, which is not specified in the supplied record, and let $A$ be a Hopf algebra over $k$, with counit $\varepsilon:A\to k$. Define its augmentation ideal and augmentation residual by \[ I=\ker\varepsilon,\qquad I^n=\operatorname{span}_k\{a_1\cdots a_n:a_1,\ldots,a_n\in I\}\quad(n\geq1),\qquad I^\infty=\bigcap_{n\geq1}I^n. \] The source describes $I^\infty$ as a Hopf ideal and consequently intends $A/I^\infty$ as a Hopf algebra quotient.
  1. Characterize the Hopf algebras $A$ for which $I^\infty=\{0\}$. More generally, describe $A/I^\infty$ in terms of $A$. The ground field and any intended restrictions on the class of Hopf algebras are unresolved in the supplied record; the surrounding discussion concerns compact quantum groups.
  2. Determine $I^\infty$ when $A=\mathcal O(O_N^+)$, the Hopf $*$-algebra of polynomial representative functions on the free orthogonal compact quantum group. Explicitly, for a positive integer $N$, this is the universal unital complex $*$-algebra generated by self-adjoint elements $u_{ij}$, $1\leq i,j\leq N$, satisfying \[ \sum_{r=1}^N u_{ir}u_{jr}=\delta_{ij}1,\qquad \sum_{r=1}^N u_{ri}u_{rj}=\delta_{ij}1, \] with \[ \Delta(u_{ij})=\sum_{r=1}^N u_{ir}\otimes u_{rj},\qquad \varepsilon(u_{ij})=\delta_{ij},\qquad S(u_{ij})=u_{ji}. \] The intended range of $N$ is not specified in the supplied record.
  3. For the intended values of $N$, decide whether there exist a discrete group $\Gamma$ and a closed quantum subgroup inclusion $\iota:\widehat\Gamma\hookrightarrow O_N^+$ such that $\Gamma$ is residually torsion-free nilpotent and \[ O_N^+=\langle SO_N,\iota(\widehat\Gamma)\rangle_{\mathrm{top}}. \] Here $SO_N=\{g\in M_N({\mathbb{R}}):g^{\mathsf T}g=1,\ \det g=1\}$ is included through its standard fundamental representation. The compact quantum group $\widehat\Gamma$ has polynomial Hopf $*$-algebra ${\mathbb{C}}[\Gamma]$, with $\Delta(g)=g\otimes g$, $\varepsilon(g)=1$, and $g^*=g^{-1}$ for $g\in\Gamma$. Residual torsion-free nilpotence means that for every $g\in\Gamma\setminus\{e\}$, there is a homomorphism $\phi_g:\Gamma\to H_g$ to a torsion-free nilpotent group with $\phi_g(g)\ne e$. A group is torsion-free if $h^m=e$, for an integer $m\geq1$, implies $h=e$; it is nilpotent if its lower central series terminates at the trivial group. Topological generation means that no proper closed quantum subgroup of $O_N^+$ contains both indicated subgroups. The supplied record does not specify whether this existence assertion is intended for every positive integer $N$, a restricted range, or a fixed $N$.

Short English statement

The problem asks when the powers of a Hopf algebra's augmentation ideal have zero intersection, how to describe the resulting quotient in general, and what this intersection is for the free orthogonal quantum group. It also asks whether that quantum group can be topologically generated by its classical special orthogonal subgroup together with the dual of a residually torsion-free nilpotent discrete group. The general ground field, any additional Hopf algebra hypotheses, and the intended range and quantification of $N$ require clarification. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
