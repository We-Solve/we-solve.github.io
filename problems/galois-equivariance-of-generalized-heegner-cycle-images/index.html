For every field $H\subseteq K^{\mathrm{ab}}$ and every generalized Heegner cycle \[ \Delta_\varphi\in CH^{r+1}(X_r)_0(H), \] the source asks whether \[ \Phi_C(\Delta_\varphi)\in A(H)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}} \] and \[ \Phi_C(\Delta_\varphi^\sigma) =\Phi_C(\Delta_\varphi)^\sigma \qquad\text{for every }\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(H/K). \] For the displayed Galois group to have its stated meaning, $H$ must contain $K$; thus the intended field condition appears to be $K\subseteq H\subseteq K^{\mathrm{ab}}$. Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the image of every generalized Heegner cycle defined over an abelian extension $H$ of $K$ is an $H$-rational point of the intended group $A$, with rational coefficients, and whether taking this image commutes with every element of $\operatorname{Gal}(H/K)$. The source omits definitions and hypotheses needed to make this a fully specified mathematical statement.
