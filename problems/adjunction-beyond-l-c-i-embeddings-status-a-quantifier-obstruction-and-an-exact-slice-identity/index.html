Math-1228

Adjunction beyond l.c.i. embeddings: status, a quantifier obstruction, and an exact slice identity

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

This record accidentally merges Problems 8, 9, and 10. The source-verification report specifies varieties over ${\mathbb{C}}$. For a prime divisor $E$ over a smooth variety and an ideal pair $(X,\mathfrak a^c)$, write $a_E(X,\mathfrak a^c)=A_X(E)-c\operatorname{ord}_E(\mathfrak a)$, with $A_X(E)$ the log discrepancy. Log canonicity means these numbers are nonnegative. For normal ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Gorenstein varieties use the corresponding discrepancy definition.
  1. Problem 8 (Schwede). Let $Y$ be smooth projective and $X\subset Y$ irreducible, normal, ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Gorenstein, and of codimension $r$, with ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$. The source recalls $(Y,\mathcal I^r)$ log canonical if and only if $X$ is log canonical in the locally complete-intersection case, and says the ordinary-power equivalence fails in general. It asks whether \[ (Y,\mathcal I^{(r)})\text{ is log canonical} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X\text{ is log canonical}, \] where $\mathcal I^{(r)}$ is the symbolic power, obtained by taking the ordinary $r$th power at the generic point of $X$ and contracting. The ideal pair on the left has exponent one; symbolic power is not replaced by ordinary power.
  2. Problem 9 (Mustaţă). Find an adjunction formula for multiplier ideals under restriction to subvarieties not defined by a regular sequence. The relevant pair/ideal, restriction hypotheses, and desired correction terms are unspecified. The report says corrected formulas using adjoint and l.c.i.-defect ideals are available, without stating them.
  3. Problem 10 (de Fernex). Let $X$ be smooth and $B\subsetneq X$ closed. Assume a prime divisor $E$ over $X$ has center $P$ and $a_E(X,cB)\leq0$ for some $c>0$. For every integer $1\leq e<\dim X$, ask for a smooth codimension-$e$ subvariety $Y$ containing $P$ and a divisor $F$ over $Y$ with center exactly $P$ such that \[ a_F(Y,cB|_Y-eP)\leq0. \] The damaged source condition "$Y*B$" is read by the research report as $Y\not\subseteq B$; its promised repaired transcription is not included. A valuative interpretation of the negative term would add $e\operatorname{ord}_F(\mathcal I_{P/Y})$ to $A_Y(F)-c\operatorname{ord}_F(\mathcal I_B\mathcal O_Y)$; the precise pair convention needs confirmation, especially for positive-codimension centers.

The report says it did not find the exact symbolic-power equivalence settled, reports a corrected affirmative framework for Problem 9, and restricts known Problem 10 results to homogeneity/tangency hypotheses. Its candidate counterexample to a literal positive-dimensional-center reading is $X={\mathbb{A}}^3$, $P=B=\{x=y=0\}$, and $c=e=2$, with $E$ the ordinary blow-up divisor. Separately, for a smooth codimension-$q$ center and that ordinary blow-up divisor, it claims that every $1\leq e<q$ admits, locally near the generic point of $P$, an order-preserving smooth slice satisfying \[ a_F(Y,cB|_Y-eP)=a_E(X,cB). \] Here $F$ is exceptional over $Y$ if $q-e\geq2$, and $F=P$ as a divisor on $Y$ if $q-e=1$. This local special-divisor identity is not the unrestricted global assertion for arbitrary $E$. All status, counterexample, and slice claims remain unreviewed.

Short English statement

Keep the three merged questions separate: symbolic-power inversion of adjunction, multiplier-ideal adjunction beyond regular embeddings, and preservation of a discrepancy on smooth slices. The report proposes a quantifier obstruction and a restricted local slicing identity, while giving only qualified status assessments for the other questions.
