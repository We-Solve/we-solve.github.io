Cocircuit Graph Diameter Conjecture for Oriented Matroids

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a finite oriented matroid on a ground set $E$, with $|E|=n$ and underlying matroid rank $r$. Write $\mathcal L(M)\subseteq\{0,+,-\}^{E}$ for its covectors. For a sign vector $X$, define its zero set and support by \[ X^0=\{e\in E:X_e=0\},\qquad \operatorname{supp}(X)=E\setminus X^0. \] A cocircuit is a nonzero covector whose support is inclusion-minimal among supports of nonzero covectors. Denote the set of cocircuits by $\mathcal C^*(M)$. For sign vectors $X,Y$, define their separation set by \[ S(X,Y)=\{e\in E:X_e=-Y_e\ne0\}. \] For $r\ge2$, use the following cocircuit graph convention: $G^*(M)$ is the undirected graph with vertex set $\mathcal C^*(M)$, in which distinct vertices $X,Y$ are adjacent precisely when \[ S(X,Y)=\varnothing \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{rk}_M(X^0\cap Y^0)=r-2. \] Here $\operatorname{rk}_M$ denotes rank in the underlying matroid. For a nonempty connected graph, distance is the minimum number of edges in a path, and diameter is the maximum distance between two vertices. For a nonempty disconnected graph, take its diameter to be $+\infty$.

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every oriented matroid $M$ of rank $r$ on $n$ elements, \[ \operatorname{diam}\left(G^*(M)\right)\le n-r+2. \] No uniformity, simplicity, or realizability hypothesis is supplied. The record also gives no lower bound on $r$ and no conventions for cocircuit graphs in ranks zero and one or for an empty graph. Thus the displayed conjecture preserves the supplied quantification, but its intended low-rank scope requires clarification; the explicit graph definition above fixes the interpretation only for $r\ge2$.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether any two cocircuits of an oriented matroid on $n$ elements and of rank $r$ can be joined in its cocircuit graph using at most $n-r+2$ edges. The source supplies no additional structural hypotheses, and its intended treatment of ranks zero and one remains unclear. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
