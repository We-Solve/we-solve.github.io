The all-temperature spherical Gaussian perceptron pressure

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The spherical Gaussian perceptron is claimed to have an exact limiting-pressure formula at every positive density and temperature for bounded continuous pattern potentials.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Fix $\alpha,\beta>0$, bounded continuous real $\phi$, $M_N=\lfloor\alpha N\rfloor$, and independent standard Gaussian pattern vectors $g^a$. On $S_N=\{x:|x|^2=N\}$, with uniform probability measure, set $H_N(x)=\sum_{a\le M_N}\phi(g^a\cdot x/\sqrt N)$ and $p_N=N^{-1}\log\int e^{\beta H_N}d\sigma_N$. For nondecreasing measurable $m:[0,1]\to[0,1]$, define \[ D_m(t)=\int_t^1m(s)ds,\quad S(m)=\tfrac12\int_0^1\left(D_m(t)^{-1}-(1-t)^{-1}\right)dt, \] \[ V_f(m)=\sup_v\mathbb E\left[f(B_1+\int_0^1m(t)v_tdt)-\tfrac12\int_0^1m(t)v_t^2dt\right], \] where controls are progressively measurable with finite expected cost. The claim is \[ \mathbb E p_N\to\inf_m[\alpha V_{\beta\phi}(m)+S(m)]=\mathcal P, \qquad p_N\to\mathcal P\text{ in probability}. \] Trials of infinite entropy cost have value infinity; $m$ need not equal one near the endpoint.

Outline of the argument

Pattern-addition and coordinate-addition cavity increments are treated separately because the nonlinear Hamiltonian is not Gaussian. Perturbative overlap identities and ultrametricity reduce the array to finite cascades, whose row functional and spherical entropy give the matching variational bounds.
