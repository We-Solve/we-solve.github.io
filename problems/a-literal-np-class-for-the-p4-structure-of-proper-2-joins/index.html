A literal NP class for the P4-structure of proper 2-joins

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite simple graph $G$, define its $P_4$-structure $\mathcal P_4(G)$ as the $4$-uniform hypergraph on $V(G)$ whose hyperedges are exactly the four-vertex sets inducing a chordless four-vertex path. The source asks for a class $\mathcal C_1$ of $4$-uniform hypergraphs such that every graph with a $2$-join has its $P_4$-structure in $\mathcal C_1$, no odd ring belongs to $\mathcal C_1$, and membership in $\mathcal C_1$ is in NP. The excerpt does not define $2$-join or odd ring; the aid reports a connected non-path convention in the surrounding source.

An explicit working convention for this analysis is a partition $X_1,X_2$, with $|X_i|\ge3$, and disjoint nonempty attachment sets $A_i,B_i\subset X_i$. Cross edges are exactly the complete pairs $A_1\times A_2$ and $B_1\times B_2$. Require each component of $G[X_i]$ to meet both attachments, and exclude a side that is merely a path with singleton attachment endpoints. Exact compatibility of this convention with the source's proper $2$-join must be checked. Interpret an odd ring as $\mathcal P_4(C_{2t+1})$, $t\ge2$, up to hypergraph isomorphism. Complementing a graph preserves its $P_4$-structure.

The aid proposes the witness language \[ \mathcal C_{\mathrm{real}}= \{\mathcal H:\exists G\text{ on }V(\mathcal H),\ \mathcal P_4(G)=\mathcal H,\ G\text{ has a source-proper }2\text{-join}\}. \] A certificate consists of the graph adjacency matrix and its split/attachment data. The reported verifier checks the split, connectedness/non-path conditions, and every four-vertex set for exact equality of $P_4$-structures in $O(n^4)$ time for $n$ explicitly listed vertices. Thus membership is in NP, and the universal inclusion bullet follows directly under the same $2$-join convention.

To exclude odd rings, the aid invokes Chvátal's classification of their graph realizations as odd holes or odd antiholes, together with direct arguments that neither has a source-proper connected non-path $2$-join. The full classification hypotheses and proofs are not supplied, so this essential exclusion gate requires reference verification. No heredity or forbidden-induced-ring condition is requested. The aid says strengthening exact odd-ring exclusion to exclusion of every induced odd ring makes the first universal bullet inconsistent, because a graph with a proper $2$-join can still contain an odd hole.
