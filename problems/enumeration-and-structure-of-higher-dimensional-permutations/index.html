Math-4224

Enumeration and Structure of Higher-Dimensional Permutations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. For an integer $d\geq 3$, a $d$-dimensional permutation of order $n$ is a function \[ A:[n]^{d+1}\longrightarrow\{0,1\} \] such that, for every $j\in\{1,\ldots,d+1\}$ and every fixed choice of $i_k\in[n]$ for $k\ne j$, \[ \sum_{t=1}^{n}A(i_1,\ldots,i_{j-1},t,i_{j+1},\ldots,i_{d+1})=1. \] Thus every axis-parallel line contains exactly one entry equal to $1$. Let $P_d(n)$ denote the number of these functions, counted as arrays on the specified indexed domain $[n]^{d+1}$.

The supplied clean statement asks whether, for every fixed integer $d\geq3$, \[ P_d(n)=\left((1+o(1))\frac{n}{e^d}\right)^{n^d} \qquad(n\to\infty), \] where $n$ tends to infinity through positive integers, $e$ is the base of the natural logarithm, and the $o(1)$ term may depend on the fixed dimension $d$. Equivalently, is there, for each such $d$, a real sequence $\varepsilon_d(n)\to0$ such that \[ P_d(n)=\exp\!\left(n^d\left(\log n-d+\varepsilon_d(n)\right)\right)? \]

The original source-record wording asks more broadly for an appropriate higher-dimensional analogue of a permutation and its basic enumeration and structural properties. The displayed question formalizes the specific enumeration target in the supplied clean statement; the broader structural request supplies no precise additional assertion to formalize. The record labels the problem open and the clean statement corrected_verified, and reports verification against the official report. These are supplied curation claims, not independent verification by this draft.

Short English statement

Count the zero--one arrays on $[n]^{d+1}$ having exactly one $1$ on every axis-parallel line. For each fixed integer $d\geq3$, does their number equal $(n/e^d)^{n^d}\exp(o(n^d))$ as $n\to\infty$? The original broader request about structural properties remains unspecified.
