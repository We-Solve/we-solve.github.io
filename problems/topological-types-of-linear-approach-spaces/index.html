Math-15769

Topological Types of Linear-Approach Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a translation surface, let $d_X$ be its flat length metric, and let $\overline X$ be its metric completion. Fix $x\in\overline X$. A translation structure is given by Euclidean coordinate charts on the regular part of $X$ whose transition maps are translations.

Using an endpoint parametrization, a linear approach to $x$ is represented by a unit-speed geodesic trajectory \[ \gamma:(0,\varepsilon)\longrightarrow X,\qquad \varepsilon>0,\qquad \lim_{t\downarrow0}d_{\overline X}(\gamma(t),x)=0. \] Here the trajectory lies in the regular flat part and is locally a straight line in translation coordinates. Two representatives $\gamma_1:(0,\varepsilon_1)\to X$ and $\gamma_2:(0,\varepsilon_2)\to X$ define the same germ if there exists $0<\delta<\min\{\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2\}$ such that \[ \gamma_1(t)=\gamma_2(t)\qquad(0<t<\delta). \] Write $L(x)$ for the set of these germs, equipped with the topology intended in the source report.

Classification question. Characterize, up to homeomorphism, all topological spaces $T$ for which there exist a translation surface $X$ and a point $x\in\overline X$ such that \[ T\cong L(x). \]

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define the topology on $L(x)$; the germ equivalence relation above specifies only its underlying set. Thus the topological classification target remains conditional on recovering that topology from the cited report. The record also does not specify the permitted class of translation surfaces, including any restrictions on area, genus, compactness, boundary, or completion singularities. These restrictions must be recovered before the range of the existential quantifier over $X$ is fully determined. The endpoint parametrization and regular-trajectory convention above make the usual germ interpretation explicit, but require confirmation against the report.

Short English statement

Determine which topological spaces occur as spaces of germs of geodesic trajectories approaching a point in the metric completion of a translation surface. The topology on the germ space and the precise class of allowed translation surfaces are missing from the supplied record, so a fully specified classification problem requires checking the cited report.
