Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.16

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finitely generated group and $S$ a finite symmetric generating set. Define its word-growth function by \[ \gamma_{G,S}(n)=\left|\{g\in G:\lvert g\rvert_S\le n\}\right|,\qquad n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}, \] where $\lvert g\rvert_S$ is the minimum length of a word in $S$ representing $g$. For functions $f,g:{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}\to{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}$, write $f\preccurlyeq g$ if there exist positive integers $A,B$ and $n_0\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}$ such that $f(n)\le A g(Bn)$ for every $n\ge n_0$; write $f\asymp g$ if both $f\preccurlyeq g$ and $g\preccurlyeq f$. The equivalence class of $\gamma_{G,S}$ is independent of the finite generating set.

For the branch-group terminology, use the following standard convention. A rooted spherically homogeneous tree $T$ has a root and, for each $k\ge0$, every vertex at distance $k$ from the root has the same finite number $d_k\ge2$ of children. Let $L_k$ be its $k$-th level. For an action of $G$ by root-preserving tree automorphisms and a vertex $v$, the rigid stabilizer $\operatorname{rist}_G(v)$ consists of elements fixing every vertex outside the subtree rooted at $v$. Put \[ \operatorname{rist}_G(k)=\langle\operatorname{rist}_G(v):v\in L_k\rangle. \] A group is a branch group if it admits such a faithful action, transitive on every level, with $[G:\operatorname{rist}_G(k)]<\infty$ for every $k\ge0$.

Under the interpretation that "rate of growth is $e^{\sqrt n}$" means growth equivalence $\asymp$, the two questions are:

  1. (a) Does there exist a finitely generated branch group $G$ such that \[ \gamma_{G,S}(n)\asymp \exp(\sqrt n)? \]
  2. (b) Does there exist a finitely generated group $G$ such that \[ \gamma_{G,S}(n)\asymp \exp(\sqrt n)? \]
In each question, $S$ may be any finite symmetric generating set. The supplied record does not define its growth-comparison convention or its branch-group convention; the definitions above make explicit the interpretation used in this draft. In particular, the source does not specify literal equality, asymptotic ratio one, or two-sided constant multiples of $\exp(\sqrt n)$. It also states that the question is related to Kourovka Notebook Problem 9.9, whose statement is not supplied.

Short English statement

Does a finitely generated branch group have word growth equivalent to $\exp(\sqrt n)$? Does any finitely generated group have that growth? This draft uses the stated coarse growth equivalence; the source leaves that convention implicit. The supplied registry labels the problem open, without independent verification here.
