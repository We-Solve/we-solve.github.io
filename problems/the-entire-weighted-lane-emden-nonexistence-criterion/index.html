The entire weighted Lane–Emden nonexistence criterion

OpenAI's claim

Subcritical Hénon--Lane--Emden nonexistence: The paper claims nonexistence of positive entire weighted Lane--Emden solutions under the strict subcritical inequality. Solutions may be only continuous at the origin and no asymptotic restriction is imposed.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every integer $n\ge2$, $p,q>0$ and real weights $A,B$ satisfying \[\frac{n+A}{p+1}+\frac{n+B}{q+1}>n-2,\] the claim rules out functions $u,v\in C^2(\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\})\cap C(\mathbb R^n)$ positive everywhere and solving \[-\Delta u=|x|^Av^p,\qquad-\Delta v=|x|^Bu^q\quad(x\ne0).\] There is no symmetry, boundedness or growth-at-infinity assumption. Continuity and strict positivity at the origin are retained even for singular real weights.

Outline of the argument

Subcritical Hénon--Lane--Emden nonexistence: Potential estimates first handle the origin and exceptional low-exponent regimes. A localized virial identity compares the two interaction marginals. A geometric interval inequality and its weighted pressure consequence control the localization errors, and scaling makes the strict subcritical imbalance incompatible with a positive entire solution.
