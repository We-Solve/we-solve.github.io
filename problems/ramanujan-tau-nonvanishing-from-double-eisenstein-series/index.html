Ramanujan Tau Nonvanishing from Double Eisenstein Series

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}$ and $q=e^{2\pi i z}$ for $z\in\mathbb H$. Define the discriminant modular form and Ramanujan's tau function by \[ \Delta(z)=q\prod_{m=1}^{\infty}(1-q^m)^{24} =\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\tau(n)q^n. \] Thus $\tau(n)$, for each positive integer $n$, is the coefficient of $q^n$ in this expansion. Let $\mathcal P=\{p\in{\mathbb{Z}}:p\ge 2\text{ and }p\text{ is prime}\}$.

The requested problem is to use double-Eisenstein-series representations of $\Delta$ to establish \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge 1},\qquad \tau(n)\ne 0, \] or, as the explicitly weaker alternative, to establish \[ \#\{p\in\mathcal P:\tau(p)=0\}<\infty. \] The latter means that there exists an integer $B\ge 1$ such that $\tau(p)\ne 0$ for every prime $p>B$; no bound is specified in the supplied record.

The original supplied question asks whether "this formula" can be applied to the non-vanishing problem of $\tau(n)$. The record identifies double-Eisenstein-series representations as the intended method but does not supply the formula or define the relevant double Eisenstein series. Consequently, the non-vanishing targets are precise, while the particular representation and the intended argument remain unspecified. The supplied report assessment describes the proposed argument as heuristic; no proof is asserted here.

Short English statement

Use a double-Eisenstein-series representation of $\Delta$ to prove that every positive-index Ramanujan coefficient $\tau(n)$ is nonzero, or at least that only finitely many primes have $\tau(p)=0$. The specific representation is absent from the supplied record, and its proposed application is described there as heuristic.
