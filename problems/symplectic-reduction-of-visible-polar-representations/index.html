Symplectic Reduction of Visible Polar Representations

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns a visible polar representation $(G,V)$. The record does not specify the ground field, the precise hypotheses on $G$, or the convention for Hamiltonian reduction. The following customary complex reductive setting explains the notation conditionally; these conventions require confirmation against the original source.

Let $G$ be a complex reductive algebraic group acting linearly on a finite-dimensional complex vector space $V$, and put $\mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}(G)$. Write \[ V/\!/G=\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}[V]^G, \qquad \pi:V\longrightarrow V/\!/G. \] The representation is called visible if every fiber of $\pi$ contains only finitely many $G$-orbits. A vector is called semisimple if its $G$-orbit is closed. In the usual definition of a polar representation, there is a semisimple vector $v\in V$ such that \[ \mathfrak c_v=\{x\in V:\mathfrak g\cdot x\subseteq\mathfrak g\cdot v\}, \qquad \dim\mathfrak c_v=\dim(V/\!/G). \] Such a subspace $\mathfrak c=\mathfrak c_v$ is a Cartan subspace. Its associated finite group is \[ W=N_G(\mathfrak c)/Z_G(\mathfrak c), \] where the numerator preserves $\mathfrak c$ setwise and the denominator fixes it pointwise. Let $\mathfrak c^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(\mathfrak c,{\mathbb{C}})$, with the dual $W$-action. Interpret the left-hand quotient as the affine quotient \[ (\mathfrak c\oplus\mathfrak c^*)/W =\operatorname{Spec}{\mathbb{C}}[\mathfrak c\oplus\mathfrak c^*]^W, \] with its Poisson structure induced by the canonical symplectic structure on $\mathfrak c\oplus\mathfrak c^*$.

Identify $V\oplus V^*$ with $T^*V$. Its cotangent moment map is \[ \mu:V\oplus V^*\longrightarrow\mathfrak g^*, \qquad \mu(v,\lambda)(\xi)=\lambda(\xi\cdot v). \] If $I_\mu\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}[V\oplus V^*]$ is the ideal generated by the functions $(v,\lambda)\mapsto\lambda(\xi\cdot v)$, for $\xi\in\mathfrak g$, two possible zero-level reduction conventions are \[ X_{\mathrm{sch}}=\operatorname{Spec}\left(({\mathbb{C}}[V\oplus V^*]/I_\mu)^G\right), \qquad X_{\mathrm{red}}=\operatorname{Spec}\left(({\mathbb{C}}[V\oplus V^*]/\sqrt{I_\mu})^G\right). \] The supplied notation $(V\oplus V^*)\mathbin{///}G$ does not specify which convention is intended. No equality of these two constructions is assumed here.

Question (permanent catalogue entry). For every visible polar representation $(G,V)$ in the intended source setting, is the natural Poisson morphism named in the record \[ \phi:(\mathfrak c\oplus\mathfrak c^*)/W \longrightarrow (V\oplus V^*)\mathbin{///}G \] an isomorphism of Poisson spaces? The record does not provide the construction of $\phi$, including how the Cartan dual enters that construction. Its precise construction, the category of spaces, the reduction convention, and the ambient representation hypotheses remain qualifications requiring source review. Stability and local freeness are not assumptions in the supplied general question.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the natural Poisson map from the finite quotient of a Cartan subspace together with its dual to the symplectic reduction of $V\oplus V^*$ is an isomorphism for every visible polar representation. The ground field, group hypotheses, reduction convention, and construction of the map require confirmation from the source.
