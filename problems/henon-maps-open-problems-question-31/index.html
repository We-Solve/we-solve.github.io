Henon Maps: Open Problems — Question 31

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks about two generalized Hénon maps $f_1,f_2$. In the standard complex setting, an elementary generalized Hénon map has the form \[ (x,y)\longmapsto (y,p(y)-ax),\qquad p\in{\mathbb{C}}[y],\quad \deg p\geq 2,\quad a\in{\mathbb{C}}\setminus\{0\}. \] The supplied record does not specify whether its class of generalized Hénon maps includes finite compositions or other coordinate conventions; this example does not impose an additional restriction on the source question.

For a map $f_i\colon{\mathbb{C}}^2\to{\mathbb{C}}^2$ in the intended class, define its forward filled set by \[ K_i^+=\left\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^2:\sup_{n\geq 0}\|f_i^n(z)\|<\infty\right\}. \] Let $W_i$ be an unstable manifold for $f_i$, and let $U_i\subseteq W_i$ be relatively open. The associated local unstable slice is \[ S_i=K_i^+\cap U_i. \] The source does not specify the invariant points or other invariant objects whose unstable manifolds are allowed.

Question. Characterize the circumstances under which there exist choices of unstable manifolds $W_1,W_2$ and relatively open subsets $U_1\subseteq W_1$, $U_2\subseteq W_2$ such that the slices $S_1$ and $S_2$ are biholomorphically equivalent.

The meaning of this equivalence requires clarification because the slices need not themselves be open complex manifolds. If the intended meaning is equivalence induced by a biholomorphism of the chosen ambient open subsets, the condition is \[ \exists\,h\colon U_1\longrightarrow U_2 \quad\text{biholomorphic, with}\quad h(K_1^+\cap U_1)=K_2^+\cap U_2. \] This is a conditional interpretation, not a qualification explicitly supplied by the source. Equivalence on smaller neighborhoods or at specified germs could give a different formulation. The record also does not require the slices to be nonempty or specify a distinguished point that they must contain. These unresolved choices must be settled before a unique precise formulation can be asserted.

Short English statement

Berger and Bedford ask when a local unstable slice of one generalized Hénon map can be biholomorphically equivalent to a local unstable slice of another. A slice is the intersection of the forward bounded-orbit set with a relatively open part of an unstable manifold. The allowed unstable manifolds, nonemptiness requirements, and precise meaning of biholomorphic equivalence remain unspecified in the supplied record.
