Math-1211

Complementary tropical minors for relative Koszul complexes

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use degeneration techniques, tropical independence, limit linear series, or their combination to understand higher syzygies. The original notes that Green's conjectures describe generic canonically embedded curves by non-tropical techniques; it seeks a degeneration approach, not merely a restatement of known vanishing. Its field, genus, embeddings and specific syzygy groups are unspecified.

For a smooth projective curve $C$ and line bundle $L$, set $V=H^0(C,L)$. Koszul cohomology $K_{p,q}(C,L)$ is the middle homology of \[ \bigwedge^{p+1}V\otimes H^0(C,L^{q-1})\longrightarrow \bigwedge^pV\otimes H^0(C,L^q)\longrightarrow \bigwedge^{p-1}V\otimes H^0(C,L^{q+1}), \] with differentials induced by multiplication of sections. Canonical syzygies take $L=K_C$ when the canonical map is an embedding. A precise proposed degeneration should identify its family, corresponding complexes and the specialization or lifting step yielding information on these groups.

The attached unreviewed aid proposes a complementary-minor criterion. Let $R$ be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field $K$, residue field $k$, normalized valuation $\nu$, and a complex of finite free modules \[ A\xrightarrow{u}B\xrightarrow{v}C,\qquad vu=0. \] Partition a basis of the middle module $B$ into coordinates $I\sqcup J$. Choose a square $|I|$-minor of $u$ on rows $I$ and a square $|J|$-minor of $v$ on columns $J$, with noncancelling initial determinants of valuations $\alpha,\beta$. "Noncancelling" requires the initial sum of lowest-valuation determinant terms to be nonzero. The aid claims generic exactness at $B_K$, together with \[ \operatorname{length}_R(\ker v/\operatorname{im}u)\le\alpha,\quad \operatorname{length}_R(\operatorname{coker}v)_{\mathrm{tors}}\le\beta,\quad \dim_k H_{\mathrm{mid}}(A_k\to B_k\to C_k)\le\alpha+\beta. \] In particular two unit minors give special-fiber exactness. The aid reports a two-variable relative Koszul example with generic and integral $K_{1,1}=0$ but one-dimensional special $K_{1,1}$, labeled as generated by $y\otimes v$, illustrating the outgoing-cokernel contribution. Its family and those symbols are not supplied. Applying the criterion to geometric degeneration complexes with explicit tropical certificates remains additional work; it is not a full degeneration proof of general canonical syzygies.

Short English statement

Develop degeneration methods for higher curve syzygies. The aid offers a complementary-minor certificate for generic vanishing and a quantitative special-fiber defect bound, whose geometric application still requires a specified family.
