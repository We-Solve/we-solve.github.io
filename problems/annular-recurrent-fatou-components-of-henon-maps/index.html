Math-4870

Annular Recurrent Fatou Components of Hénon Maps

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F\colon{\mathbb{C}}^2\to{\mathbb{C}}^2$ be a polynomial Hénon map in the class intended by the cited source. A Fatou component $U$ is a connected component of the open set on which the forward iterates $\{F^n:n\geq0\}$ form a normal family. Recurrence means that there exist $z\in U$, integers $n_j\to\infty$, and $w\in U$ such that $F^{n_j}(z)\to w$.

For parameters $0\leq r<R\leq\infty$, write \[ A(r,R)=\{\zeta\in{\mathbb{C}}:r<|\zeta|<R\}. \] An irrational rotation on this domain is the map $\zeta\mapsto e^{2\pi i\alpha}\zeta$, where $\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}$. A finite-modulus annulus has $0<r<R<\infty$. The supplied record does not specify whether its use of "annulus" also permits punctured or infinite-modulus conformal types.

Existence question, subject to the source qualifications below. Does there exist a polynomial Hénon map $F$ having a recurrent Fatou component $U$ for which the associated one-dimensional rotation domain in case 2 of the cited classification is conformally equivalent to an annulus?

Writing $S$ for that associated one-dimensional domain, the annular rotation condition means that, for an appropriate return iterate $F^k$, there are a biholomorphism $\phi\colon S\to A(r,R)$ and $\alpha\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus{\mathbb{Q}}$ such that \[ \phi\circ(F^k|_S)\circ\phi^{-1}(\zeta) =e^{2\pi i\alpha}\zeta \qquad(\zeta\in A(r,R)). \] Here $k\geq1$, and the admissible annulus types and the precise relationship between $S$ and $U$ must be taken from the source classification.

Unresolved formulation qualification. A Fatou component of a map on ${\mathbb{C}}^2$ is open in ${\mathbb{C}}^2$, hence has complex dimension two. It therefore cannot itself be biholomorphic to a one-dimensional annulus. The original wording asks about the one-dimensional rotation domain associated with case 2, whereas the cleaned wording identifies the Fatou component with that domain. The associated-domain reading above preserves the original target, but the supplied record does not reproduce case 2, its hypotheses, or its definition of the associated domain. Thus the displayed conjugacy is an explanation of annular irrational rotation, not a complete replacement for the missing classification conditions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the one-dimensional rotation domain associated with a recurrent Fatou component of a polynomial Hénon map can be an annulus. The ambient Fatou component has complex dimension two. The precise classification hypotheses, the relationship between the component and its rotation domain, and the allowed annulus types require source review.
