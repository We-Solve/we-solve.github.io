All-Genus Gromov–Witten Invariants of Hilbert Schemes

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a fixed integer $d$ in the intended range, let $X_d=\operatorname{Hilb}^{d}({\mathbb{C}}^{2})$, the Hilbert scheme parametrizing zero-dimensional subschemes of ${\mathbb{C}}^{2}$ of length $d$. The supplied record uses a torus $T$, equivariant parameters $t_1,t_2$, curve classes $\beta_n$ indexed by $n\geq 0$, and equivariant cohomology insertions $a_{\mu}=|\mu\rangle$. It does not specify the torus action, the classes $\beta_n$, or the indexing and normalization of these insertions. These symbols therefore retain their intended source meanings; the formulation below is conditional on supplying those definitions.

Write $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,r}(X_d,\beta_n)$ for the Deligne--Mumford stack of stable maps from connected nodal curves of arithmetic genus $g$, with $r$ marked points, to $X_d$, representing $\beta_n$. Here the bar and connected-domain convention express the usual stable-map interpretation, which requires confirmation against the source. Let \[ \operatorname{ev}_i:\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,r}(X_d,\beta_n)\longrightarrow X_d, \qquad 1\leq i\leq r, \] be the evaluation maps. The supplied excerpt states that this stack is equivariantly proper and carries a $T$-equivariant virtual fundamental class. Subject to the intended equivariant integration convention, define the primary invariant \[ I_{g,r,n}(a_{\mu_1},\ldots,a_{\mu_r}) = \int_{[\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,r}(X_d,\beta_n)]^{\mathrm{vir}}_T} \prod_{i=1}^{r}\operatorname{ev}_i^{*}(a_{\mu_i}) \] and its generating series in the formal variable $q$, \[ F_{g,r}^{(d)}(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_r;q) = \sum_{n\geq0}q^n I_{g,r,n}(a_{\mu_1},\ldots,a_{\mu_r}). \] The source writes this generating series as $\langle\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_r\rangle_g^{X_d}$ and places it in ${\mathbb{Q}}(t_1,t_2,q)$. A formal generating series and a rational function are distinct objects: the rational-function assertion and any expansion convention are retained as source qualifications requiring verification.

Problem. Give a method to compute these primary equivariant Gromov--Witten invariants, or equivalently their generating series, for every integer $g\geq0$, for the intended values of $d$, every permitted number $r$ of markings, and all permitted insertion labels $\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_r$, including every indexed curve class $\beta_n$, $n\geq0$. The ranges of $d$, $r$, and the labels, the curve-class definitions, and the treatment of unstable degree-zero cases must be supplied before this becomes a fully specified computational problem. No descendant insertions occur in the displayed source formula.

For context, the excerpt attributes the genus-zero computation to R. Pandharipande and A. Okounkov, citing its reference [5], and says that the genus-zero theory is governed by quantum multiplication \[ M_D=D\star: H_T^*(X_d)[[q]]\longrightarrow H_T^*(X_d)[[q]]. \] Neither $D$ nor the bibliographic entry [5] is defined in the supplied record.

Short English statement

Find a method computing the displayed primary equivariant Gromov--Witten generating series of $\operatorname{Hilb}^{d}({\mathbb{C}}^2)$ in every genus and for all intended curve classes and insertions. The supplied record leaves essential conventions unspecified, including the torus action, the classes $\beta_n$, and the insertion basis. Its partially solved status and literature assessment are supplied curation, not independently verified conclusions.
