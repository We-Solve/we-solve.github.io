Coefficient Criterion for Irredundant String-Cone Inequalities

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a distinguished presentation of a string cone by tropicalized inequalities, but does not specify the string-cone data, coordinates, or normalized defining forms. The following states the proposed criterion conditionally on those choices being supplied.

Let $n,m\geq 1$, and let the designated defining linear forms, in the designated coordinates, be \[ \ell_j(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_{jk}x_k\qquad (1\leq j\leq m). \] Using the convention that the inequalities are written with nonnegative right-hand orientation, let the associated string cone be \[ C=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\ell_j(x)\geq 0\text{ for every }1\leq j\leq m\}. \] This indexed presentation is irredundant if removal of any one inequality strictly enlarges the feasible set; equivalently, \[ \forall j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\ \exists x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n: \quad \ell_j(x)<0 \quad\text{and}\quad \ell_i(x)\geq 0\ \text{for every }i\ne j. \] The proposed equivalence, for every presentation belonging to the intended source-specific class, is \[ \left[\text{the designated presentation of }C\text{ is irredundant}\right] \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left[\forall j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\ \forall k\in\{1,\ldots,n\},\ a_{jk}\in\{-1,0,1\}\right]. \] Unresolved qualifications. The class of string cones and the distinguished presentation to which this quantifier applies are absent from the supplied excerpt. In particular, the Lie-theoretic data, any reduced-word choices, coordinate conventions, normalization of forms, and treatment of repeated inequalities must be recovered from the cited sources. The coefficient condition depends on normalization, whereas irredundancy is invariant under positive rescaling of individual forms. Thus the statement cannot be interpreted as a criterion for arbitrary linear-inequality presentations. The original excerpt refers to an omitted "above theorem," so the displayed equivalence is a conditional reconstruction from the supplied clean statement, not a fully recovered source formulation.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the designated tropical inequalities for a string cone are irredundant exactly when all their coefficients lie in $\{-1,0,1\}$. The supplied record omits the precise family of cones and normalized inequalities, so those choices remain unresolved. Its open status and claim that the coefficient condition is sufficient are supplied dated assessments, not independently verified facts.
