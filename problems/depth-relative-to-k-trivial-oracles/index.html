Math-15792

Depth Relative to K-Trivial Oracles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Identify an oracle $A\subseteq{\mathbb{N}}$ with its characteristic sequence in $2^{{\mathbb{N}}}$, where ${\mathbb{N}}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. Fix an optimal prefix-free machine, and let $K(\sigma)$ denote the prefix-free Kolmogorov complexity of a finite binary string $\sigma$. Write $K(n)$ for the complexity of a fixed effective encoding of $n$. The oracle $A$ is $K$-trivial if \[ \exists c\in{\mathbb{N}}\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{N}}\qquad K(A\upharpoonright n)\leq K(n)+c, \] where $A\upharpoonright n$ is its initial segment of length $n$.

Let $\mathcal D\subseteq 2^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ denote the class of deep sequences intended by the source, and let $\mathcal D^{A}\subseteq 2^{{\mathbb{N}}}$ denote its intended class of sequences that are deep relative to $A$. These are provisional predicate names: the supplied record does not define depth or its relativization. In particular, it does not specify the underlying complexity convention, the time bounds, or the quantifier pattern in the depth condition. Those definitions must be recovered from the supplied references before this becomes a fully specified mathematical problem.

Source question. For each $K$-trivial oracle $A$, determine whether \[ \mathcal D^{A}\subsetneq\mathcal D \qquad\text{or}\qquad \mathcal D^{A}=\mathcal D. \] Here the reading of "$A$-depth is strictly stronger than depth" is \[ \left(\forall X\in 2^{{\mathbb{N}}}\;[X\in\mathcal D^{A}\Rightarrow X\in\mathcal D]\right) \ \land\ \left(\exists X\in 2^{{\mathbb{N}}}\;[X\in\mathcal D\land X\notin\mathcal D^{A}]\right), \] and coincidence means \[ \forall X\in 2^{{\mathbb{N}}}\qquad X\in\mathcal D^{A}\Longleftrightarrow X\in\mathcal D. \] More generally, characterize the $K$-trivial oracles satisfying each of these two comparisons. The alternatives preserve the source's formulation; this draft does not establish the inclusion or assert that the alternatives are exhaustive. The intended depth definitions and any hypotheses supporting that dichotomy remain unresolved.

Short English statement

For each $K$-trivial oracle $A$, determine whether depth relative to $A$ is a strictly stronger property than ordinary depth or is the same property, and characterize which oracles give each outcome. The supplied record leaves the precise depth definitions unspecified, so this is a conditional formulation requiring source verification and human mathematical review.
