Universal Lefschetz Parameters for Neighborly Spheres

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: "For neighborly spheres, does every linear system of parameters have the Lefschetz property?" Its precise interpretation requires a coefficient field, a class of spheres, and a choice of Lefschetz property, none of which is specified in the supplied statement.

The following gives a conditional algebraic formulation. Let $k$ be a field and let $\Delta$ be a finite simplicial complex of dimension $d-1$ on vertex set $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. Its Stanley--Reisner ring, with the standard grading, is \[ k[\Delta]=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I_\Delta, \qquad I_\Delta=\left(\prod_{j\in F}x_j : F\subseteq[n],\ F\notin\Delta\right). \] A linear system of parameters is a sequence $\Theta=(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_d)$ of degree-one elements of $k[\Delta]$ for which \[ A(\Delta,\Theta)=k[\Delta]/(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_d) \] is finite-dimensional over $k$. Write $A_i$ for its degree-$i$ component.

A standard convention calls a $(d-1)$-dimensional simplicial sphere neighborly if every subset of at most $\lfloor d/2\rfloor$ vertices is a face. This convention is a candidate interpretation here; the supplied record does not specify its convention or whether spheres are topological, piecewise-linear, or homology spheres.

The weak Lefschetz property for $A=A(\Delta,\Theta)$ means that there exists $\ell\in A_1$ such that, for every integer $i\geq0$, \[ \operatorname{rank}_k(\,\cdot\ell:A_i\longrightarrow A_{i+1}) =\min\{\dim_k A_i,\dim_k A_{i+1}\}. \] The strong Lefschetz property means that there exists $\ell\in A_1$ such that, for every pair of integers $i\geq0$ and $r\geq1$, \[ \operatorname{rank}_k(\,\cdot\ell^r:A_i\longrightarrow A_{i+r}) =\min\{\dim_k A_i,\dim_k A_{i+r}\}. \] After specifying the field, the sphere class, the neighborliness convention, and a property $\mathcal P$, the proposed universal assertion takes the form \[ \text{for every admissible neighborly sphere }\Delta \text{ and every linear system of parameters }\Theta, \quad A(\Delta,\Theta)\text{ has }\mathcal P. \] In either displayed Lefschetz interpretation, the Lefschetz element may depend on $\Delta$ and $\Theta$. The source explicitly quantifies over every linear system of parameters, rather than only generic choices. It does not identify $\mathcal P$ as weak or strong Lefschetz, specify characteristic restrictions, or determine whether the field is fixed or universally quantified. Consequently these candidate formulations must remain unresolved alternatives pending source clarification.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every choice of linear parameters for a neighborly sphere yields an Artinian quotient with the intended Lefschetz property. A precise statement still requires the coefficient field, the meaning of sphere and neighborliness, and whether weak or strong Lefschetz is intended. The supplied record labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
