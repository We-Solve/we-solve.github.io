Some Questions — Question 50

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be the class of measurable real-valued functions $f:{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{R}}$, with the precise measurable convention to be fixed. Define the shears \[ H_f(x,y)=(x,y+f(x)),\qquad V_f(x,y)=(x+f(y),y). \] They are bijections with $H_f^{-1}=H_{-f}$ and $V_f^{-1}=V_{-f}$. Put \[ S=\{H_f:f\in M\}\cup\{V_f:f\in M\},\qquad \mathcal G=\langle S\rangle. \] Here $\langle S\rangle$ means the subgroup consisting of finite compositions of the displayed maps; no topological closure is taken. Under the usual Lebesgue-measurable interpretation these are invertible Lebesgue-measure-preserving transformations of ${\mathbb{R}}^2$. Whether transformations are compared pointwise or modulo null sets is not specified in the source.

Questions. (1) Give an intrinsic description of the group $\mathcal G$ generated by the horizontal and vertical measurable shears. (2) Is this generation bounded: does there exist an integer $N\geq0$ such that \[ \forall T\in\mathcal G\quad \exists m\in\{0,\ldots,N\}\ \exists S_1,\ldots,S_m\in S: T=S_1\circ\cdots\circ S_m? \] The empty composition is the identity. Equivalently, if \[ \ell_S(T)=\min\{m\geq0:T=S_1\circ\cdots\circ S_m,\ S_i\in S\}, \] then the second question asks whether $\sup_{T\in\mathcal G}\ell_S(T)<\infty$. The bound must be uniform over all generated transformations, not merely finite for each transformation separately.

The supplied research summary labels both parts OPEN-TRIAGE and says that no published resolution was found within a capped search. It describes connections with measurable dynamics, follow-the-leader or unimodular shear maps, and full-group-type constructions, but supplies no precise identification theorem or citation. The search limitation and provisional status are retained; no answer to either question is asserted.

Short English statement

Describe the group obtained by composing arbitrary measurable horizontal and vertical shears of the plane. Then determine whether one fixed number of shears suffices for every element of that group. The precise measurability and equality conventions need source confirmation.
