Math-14050

Sharp Universal Gaussian Mean-Estimation Constant

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d,N\geq 1$ be integers, let $\mu^*\in{\mathbb{R}}^d$, and let $\Sigma$ be a symmetric positive semidefinite $d\times d$ matrix. Let $S\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be nonempty and symmetric, meaning $S=-S$, and set \[ p_S(x)=\sup_{v\in S}\langle v,x\rangle. \] This expression can take the value $+\infty$; when it is finite on all of ${\mathbb{R}}^d$, it is the pseudonorm appearing in the source. Symmetry alone does not ensure this finiteness.

Let $G_1,\ldots,G_N$ be independent random vectors with distribution $\mathcal N(\mu^*,\Sigma)$, and define \[ \overline G_N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N G_i. \] For $g\sim\mathcal N(0,I_d)$, define the Gaussian mean width and covariance radius by \[ w_\Sigma(S)={\mathbb{E}}\sup_{v\in S}\langle g,\Sigma^{1/2}v\rangle, \qquad r_\Sigma(S)=\sup_{v\in S}\|\Sigma^{1/2}v\|_2. \] The weak variance of the empirical-mean process is \[ \sigma_S^2 =\sup_{v\in S}{\mathbb{E}}\left[\langle v,\overline G_N-\mu^*\rangle^2\right] =\frac{r_\Sigma(S)^2}{N}. \] The supplied excerpt attributes to the Borell--TIS inequality the following bound, understood when the displayed width and radius are finite: for every $0<\delta<1$, \[ \Pr\!\left\{ p_S(\overline G_N-\mu^*) \leq \frac{w_\Sigma(S)}{\sqrt N} +r_\Sigma(S)\sqrt{\frac{2\log(1/\delta)}{N}} \right\}\geq 1-\delta. \] For $S=B_2^d=\{v\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:\|v\|_2\leq1\}$, the excerpt describes the corresponding Euclidean rate using \[ \sqrt{\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(\Sigma)}{N}} +\sqrt{\frac{\|\Sigma\|_{\mathrm{op}}\log(1/\delta)}{N}}, \] where $\|\Sigma\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ is the operator norm. This rate expression does not specify sharp numerical constants.

Requested target and unresolved formulation. The registry asks to determine the sharp constant in the universal deviation-minimax rate for Gaussian mean estimation over all pseudonorms. The supplied excerpt, however, explicitly says that sharp optimal constants are not considered and ends before completing its discussion of optimality. It does not define a minimax criterion or specify an inequality whose constant is to be optimized. Thus the displayed empirical-mean bound supplies context, but the supplied evidence does not determine a unique sharp-constant problem. The admissible estimators, treatment of the covariance, parameter class, confidence range for minimax optimality, and normalization of the target rate remain unspecified.

Short English statement

The record requests a sharp universal constant for Gaussian mean estimation measured by arbitrary pseudonorms. Its excerpt supplies an empirical-mean deviation bound but explicitly leaves sharp constants untreated. A precise minimax optimization problem cannot be recovered without additional specifications.
