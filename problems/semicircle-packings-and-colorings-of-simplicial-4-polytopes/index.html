Semicircle Packings and Colorings of Simplicial 4-Polytopes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The record contains two separate questions. Several conventions needed for a fully precise statement are not specified; the formulations below make those conventions explicit and remain conditional on their confirmation.

For a plane curve $\gamma\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ and $r>0$, put \[ B_r=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:\|x\|\le r\},\qquad K_{r,\gamma}=\gamma+B_r=\{x+y:x\in\gamma,\ y\in B_r\}. \] The source denotes by $f_c(r,\gamma)$ the maximum density of a packing by congruent copies of $K_{r,\gamma}$. It does not specify the density convention or establish that a maximum is attained. One conventional precise interpretation is \[ f_c(r,\gamma)=\sup_{\mathcal P}\limsup_{T\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{area}\!\left(B_T\cap\bigcup_{K\in\mathcal P}K\right)}{\operatorname{area}(B_T)}, \] where $\mathcal P$ ranges over locally finite families of congruent copies of $K_{r,\gamma}$ with pairwise disjoint interiors. Here congruence is provisionally taken to allow all Euclidean isometries; these packing and density conventions require confirmation against the source.

Semicircle packing question. Interpreting the semicircle as a fixed closed circular arc, write \[ \gamma_1=\{R(\cos t,\sin t):0\le t\le\pi\},\qquad R>0\text{ fixed}. \] The intended question appears to be whether \[ f_c(r,\gamma_1)=\Theta(\sqrt r)\qquad(r\downarrow0). \] Under this interpretation, the assertion asks whether there exist constants $c,C,r_0>0$, independent of $r$ but allowed to depend on the fixed semicircle, such that \[ \forall r\in(0,r_0),\qquad c\sqrt r\le f_c(r,\gamma_1)\le C\sqrt r. \] The supplied statement does not explicitly give the limiting regime, the radius normalization, or the endpoint convention. Thus $r\downarrow0$ and the displayed arc are explicit provisional interpretations, not additional verified source facts.

Polytope coloring question. A simplicial convex $4$-polytope is a convex polytope of affine dimension $4$ whose facets are tetrahedra; its $2$-faces are triangles. Under the intended but unstated interpretation that vertices are colored, the question is whether \[ \exists k\in{\mathbb{N}},\ k\ge1,\quad\forall P\text{ a simplicial convex }4\text{-polytope},\quad\exists\chi:V(P)\to\{1,\ldots,k\} \] such that, for every $2$-face $F$ of $P$, \[ \left|\{\chi(v):v\in V(F)\}\right|\ge2. \] Thus one constant $k$ must work for every such polytope, regardless of its number of vertices. No proper coloring of the edges is required by this condition. The vertex-coloring interpretation and the ordinary convex meaning of polytope require confirmation because the supplied wording does not expressly state them.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether the best packing density of a thin circular semicircle grows on the order of $\sqrt r$, provisionally as $r\downarrow0$. The second asks whether a universal finite number of vertex colors suffices for every simplicial convex $4$-polytope so that no triangular face has all vertices the same color. The limiting regime, packing conventions, and coloring domain need source confirmation.
