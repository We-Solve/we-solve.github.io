Open Problems in Pluripotential Theory — Question 15

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied extract leaves the ambient space $X$, the form $\omega$, and the admissible measures $\mu$ unspecified. Its proposed equivalence is \[ \begin{aligned} &\exists\,\varepsilon_\mu>0\quad\forall\,u\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega),\qquad \int_X e^{-\varepsilon_\mu u}\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu<\infty \\[2mm] &\hspace{35mm}\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists\,\varphi\in\mathcal{H}ol(X,\omega)\quad \mu=\operatorname{MA}(\varphi). \end{aligned} \] The same $\varepsilon_\mu$ must work for every $u$; the first condition does not assert a uniform bound on these integrals.

For interpretation, in the usual setting where $X$ is a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension $n$ and $\omega$ is a Kähler form, $\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)$ denotes the upper semicontinuous, locally integrable functions $u:X\to[-\infty,\infty)$ satisfying $\omega+dd^c u\geq0$ as currents. The notation $\mathcal{H}ol(X,\omega)$ is naturally read as the Hölder continuous $\omega$-plurisubharmonic potentials, namely \[ \bigcup_{0<\alpha\leq1}\left(\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)\cap C^{0,\alpha}(X)\right). \] These ambient hypotheses and this interpretation of $\mathcal{H}ol$ are not defined in the supplied extract and require confirmation.

For such a Hölder continuous potential, the usual complex Monge--Ampère measure is the Bedford--Taylor measure $(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n$, sometimes normalized by division by $\int_X\omega^n$. The extract does not specify the convention for $dd^c$, whether $\operatorname{MA}$ is normalized, or the corresponding mass requirement on $\mu$. Consequently, the displayed equivalence records the proposed question, but its complete hypotheses and operator conventions remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The question asks whether integrability of $e^{-\varepsilon_\mu u}$ for every $\omega$-plurisubharmonic function $u$, with one common positive $\varepsilon_\mu$, is equivalent to representing $\mu$ as the Monge--Ampère measure of a Hölder continuous $\omega$-plurisubharmonic potential. The ambient hypotheses, notation, and measure normalization require confirmation from the full source context.
