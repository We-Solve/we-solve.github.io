Math-2789

Ordinary binary affine Liouville correlations

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Omega(n)$ count prime factors with multiplicity and put $\lambda(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}$ for positive integers $n$. Fix integers $a_1,a_2\ge1$ and $b_1,b_2\ge0$ with $a_1b_2-a_2b_1\ne0$. Must \[ \sum_{1\le n\le X}\lambda(a_1n+b_1)\lambda(a_2n+b_2)=o(X) \qquad(X\to\infty) \] hold through all real cutoffs $X$?

Short English statement

Must the ordinary average of the Liouville product along every such pair of distinct integer affine forms tend to zero?

OpenAI claim summary for Math-2789

The source treats the same fixed positive slopes, nonnegative offsets and nonproportionality condition as this question, with no coprimality restriction. Its estimate holds through all real $X\ge3$ and saves a positive power of $\log X$. Dividing by $X$ therefore gives the requested ordinary-average limit zero, with additional quantitative decay. Higher-order Liouville correlations are a separate question.

Ordinary binary affine Liouville correlations

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 007; 2026-09-24), the asserted result is that ordinary two-point Liouville averages have a logarithmic power saving on fixed nonproportional affine forms, and bounded multiplicative functions satisfy the corrected binary Elliott assertion under uniform nonpretentiousness.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
For Liouville $\lambda(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}$, there is claimed an absolute $c>0$ such that every fixed $a_1,a_2\ge1$, $b_1,b_2\ge0$ with $a_1b_2-a_2b_1\ne0$ has a constant $C$ satisfying \[ |\sum_{n\le X}\lambda(a_1n+b_1)\lambda(a_2n+b_2)|\le C X/(\log X)^c\quad(X\ge3). \] The coefficients need not be coprime, but are fixed as $X$ grows. A second claim concerns multiplicative $f_1,f_2:\mathbb N\to\{z:|z|\le1\}$, at least one satisfying, for every fixed Dirichlet character $\chi$, \[ \inf_{|t|\le N}\left(\sum_{p\le N}\frac{1-\operatorname{Re}(f(p)\overline{\chi(p)p^{it}})}p\right)^{1/2}\longrightarrow\infty. \] For any distinct fixed nonnegative $h_1,h_2$ the claimed ordinary, rather than logarithmically weighted, conclusion is \[ N^{-1}\sum_{n=1}^Nf_1(n+h_1)f_2(n+h_2)\longrightarrow0. \]
