Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record is an incomplete theorem excerpt, marked unrecoverable by the supplied statement verification. It does not determine a separately posed open problem. The following records its displayed identity and qualifications without promoting it to a universal conjecture.

The excerpt considers a suspended polynomial \[ p(x,y,z,w)=x^2+y^2+g(z,w) \] and the threefold singularity $X=(p=0,0)$. It denotes the corresponding Milnor fibre by $F$ and the link by $L$. In the usual interpretation for an isolated complex hypersurface germ, these are respectively a sufficiently small smooth local fibre and the intersection of the singular hypersurface with a sufficiently small sphere. This interpretation is conditional: the excerpt does not explicitly specify the ground field or the hypotheses on $g$ ensuring that these objects have the intended properties.

A small ${\mathbb{Q}}$-factorialization is a proper birational morphism $\pi:Y\to X$ whose exceptional locus has codimension at least two in $Y$, with $Y$ normal and ${\mathbb{Q}}$-factorial; the latter means that every Weil divisor has a positive integer multiple that is Cartier. For a small ${\mathbb{Q}}$-factorialization as considered in the excerpt, let $F_1,\ldots,F_m$ denote the Milnor fibres of the resulting ${\mathbb{Q}}$-factorial singularities. Write $SH^j(M)$ for degree-$j$ symplectic cohomology of a Milnor fibre $M$, and $b_2(L)$ for the second Betti number of $L$. The coefficient field, grading conventions, and finiteness assumptions needed for the dimensions are not specified in the supplied fragment.

The displayed assertion, reported there as Theorem 3, is \[ \text{for every }k\in{\mathbb{Z}}\text{ with }k\leq 0,\qquad \dim SH^{2k}(F) = \sum_{i=1}^{m}\dim SH^{2k}(F_i)+b_2(L). \] The excerpt presents this identity for suspended polynomials, but its opening refers to a refined version of an unidentified Conjecture 5.3 and to the singularities considered in an unidentified reference [1]. The supplied dated assessment restricts the reported proof to suspended polynomials in the considered quasihomogeneous compound-$A$ families. Consequently, the complete hypotheses and scope of the displayed theorem cannot be recovered here, and the identity is not asserted for arbitrary suspended polynomials or all isolated compound Du Val singularities.

As additional context, the excerpt reports that the links of two quasihomogeneous compound-$A_n$ singularities are contactomorphic if and only if the singularities are deformation equivalent in the sense of [1, Definition 5.1]. That definition and its precise scope are absent. The next theorem begins with Fermat-type equations \[ p_i=x^2+y^2+z^{e_i}+w^{f_i}=0, \] but its conclusion and exponent hypotheses are missing. Neither this truncated theorem nor the contextual contactomorphism claim supplies a recoverable independent problem statement.

Short English statement

This record preserves a reported symplectic-cohomology identity relating a Milnor fibre to the Milnor fibres arising from a small ${\mathbb{Q}}$-factorialization and the second Betti number of its link. The supplied verification identifies the extraction as a proved-result fragment, with incomplete hypotheses, rather than a separately posed open problem. A precise universal conjecture cannot be recovered from the supplied evidence.
