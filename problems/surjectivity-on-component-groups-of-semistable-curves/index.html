Surjectivity on Component Groups of Semistable Curves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be the field appearing in the supplied record, and let \[ f\colon X'\longrightarrow X \] be a finite morphism of smooth, proper, geometrically connected curves over $K$. The record specifies an induced map \[ f_*\colon\Phi_{X'}\longrightarrow\Phi_X, \] but does not define the groups $\Phi_{X'}$ and $\Phi_X$ or the construction of this map.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such morphism satisfying the additional conditions below, $f_*$ is surjective:

  1. the minimal regular model of $X'$ "consists of two rational components meeting transversally";
  2. $X$ is a Mumford curve and $g(X)\geq 2$, where $g(X)$ denotes its genus.
Here surjectivity means \[ \forall\phi\in\Phi_X\quad\exists\phi'\in\Phi_{X'}\quad f_*(\phi')=\phi. \]

Unresolved qualifications. This is a schematic rendering of the supplied question, not a fully specified conjecture. The record gives no valuation, completeness, residue-field, or other hypotheses on $K$, and supplies no base for the regular model. It is unclear whether the condition on two components refers to the special fiber of that model; this interpretation has not been silently substituted for the supplied wording. The field over which the components are rational, their multiplicities, and their number of intersection points are unspecified. The meaning of Mumford curve over the intended field, including any splitting conventions, also requires clarification. A possible interpretation of $\Phi_Y$ is the component group of the special fiber of the Néron model of the Jacobian of $Y$, with $f_*$ induced by pushforward on Jacobians, but the supplied record does not establish that interpretation or whether surjectivity concerns group schemes, geometric component groups, or residue-field rational points.

The supplied dated assessment additionally says that the extracted record is only the lead-in to Question 21 and contains no independently stated mathematical claim. Its relationship to the explicit question reproduced here must therefore be checked before this draft is treated as an authoritative formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether a finite morphism of curves induces a surjection on the indicated component groups when the source has the stated two-component model and the target is a Mumford curve of genus at least $2$. The field, component groups, model condition, and source context need clarification. The supplied open status is a curation label, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
