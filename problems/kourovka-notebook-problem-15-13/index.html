Kourovka Notebook Problem 15.13

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite presentation of a group is an expression \[ G\cong\langle s_1,\ldots,s_d\mid r_1,\ldots,r_k\rangle, \] where $d,k$ are finite nonnegative integers and the relators $r_j$ are words in the generators and their inverses.

For the term branch group, use the following standard rooted-tree formulation. Let $T$ be an infinite rooted tree such that every vertex at distance $n$ from the root has exactly $m_n$ children, where $2\leq m_n<\infty$. Let $L_n$ denote its level of vertices at distance $n$ from the root. Suppose that $G$ acts faithfully on $T$ by root-preserving automorphisms. For a vertex $v$, let $T_v$ be the subtree consisting of $v$ and all its descendants, and define the rigid stabilizer \[ \operatorname{rist}_G(v)=\{g\in G: g(w)=w\text{ for every vertex }w\notin T_v\}. \] Define the rigid level stabilizer by \[ \operatorname{rist}_G(n)=\left\langle\operatorname{rist}_G(v):v\in L_n\right\rangle. \] The action is a branch action if it is transitive on every $L_n$ and \[ [G:\operatorname{rist}_G(n)]<\infty\qquad\text{for every }n\geq0. \] A group admitting such an action is called a branch group.

Problem. Does there exist a finitely presented group $G$ admitting a branch action as defined above?

Formulation qualification. The supplied record does not define "branch group." The rooted-tree definition above makes explicit the conventional interpretation used in this draft; its agreement with the intended source convention requires human review. The supplied registry labels the problem open; that label is not an independently verified assessment.

Short English statement

Does any branch group have a presentation with finitely many generators and finitely many defining relations? Here the branch condition is made explicit through a faithful, level-transitive action on a rooted tree whose rigid level stabilizers all have finite index. The supplied source does not state its definition of branch group.
