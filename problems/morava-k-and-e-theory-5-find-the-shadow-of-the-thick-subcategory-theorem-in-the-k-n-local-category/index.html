Morava K- and E-theory 5 — Find the shadow of the thick subcategory theorem in the K(n)-local category.

OpenAI's claim

At every prime and positive height n, stabilizer-invariant primes and radicals are claimed to follow height strata. The tensor-ideal count for dualizable K(n)-local spectra is n+2, with n+1 points forming the Balmer chain. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Fix a prime $p$ and $n\ge1$. Let $\mathcal D_{(n,p)}$ be the category of dualizable $K(n)$-local spectra. For each $0\le k\le n$, choose a finite type-$k$ spectrum $F(k)$ (its first nonzero Morava $K$-theory is height $k$) and let $\mathcal D_k$ be the thick tensor ideal generated by $L_{K(n)}F(k)$. OpenAI claims that all thick tensor ideals are exactly \[ \mathcal D_{(n,p)}=\mathcal D_0\supsetneq\mathcal D_1\supsetneq\cdots\supsetneq\mathcal D_n\supsetneq\mathcal D_{n+1}=0. \] This gives $n+2$ ideals; choosing a different finite type-$k$ generator produces the same ideal. Thick tensor ideals are closed under finite cofibers, retracts and tensoring with dualizable objects.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 311, 2026-09-24): In characteristic p, Frobenius separation converts any orbit-vanishing analytic equation into a Honda endomorphism relation. Fargues--Fontaine bundle geometry and a determinant-degree argument contradict that relation using independent vectors. The characteristic-zero period-map branch handles the other primes, after which minimal-prime analysis and the established support comparison transfer the height chain to the tensor category.
