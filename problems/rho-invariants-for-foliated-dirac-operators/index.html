Math-4517

Rho Invariants for Foliated Dirac Operators

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Dirac-type operator is a first-order differential operator whose principal symbol satisfies the Clifford relation for the relevant metric. In a foliated setting one considers a leafwise Dirac operator on a foliated manifold $(M,\mathcal F)$; in a covering setting one considers a $\Gamma$-equivariant lift $\widetilde D$ to a regular covering $\widetilde M\to M$. The supplied record does not fix the manifolds, parity, compactness, transverse structure, group, coefficients, or self-adjoint realization.

The problem has three parts: construct appropriate secondary invariants of $\rho$-type for Dirac operators in the foliated and group-covering settings discussed in the report; determine their mathematical usefulness, including suitable invariance or stability properties; and obtain purely geometric applications. It requests a theory and applications rather than stating a single universal existence theorem.

For orientation only, a classical odd-dimensional compact self-adjoint setting admits differences such as \[ \rho_\alpha(D)=\eta(D_\alpha)-m\eta(D), \] where $D_\alpha$ is a twist by a rank-$m$ flat unitary bundle. A covering analogue, when defined under the necessary analytic hypotheses, compares $\eta_\Gamma(\widetilde D)$ with $\eta(D)$. These formulas illustrate the secondary nature of a $\rho$-invariant; they do not specify the invariant requested by the extraction. Reduced eta conventions, kernel corrections, and real-valued versus quotient-valued targets can change the formulation.

In a trace-based construction, eta expressions involve a regularized version of \[ \frac1{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^\infty t^{-1/2}\,\tau(D e^{-tD^2})\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] For coverings the trace may be a group trace; for foliations a transverse measure or a groupoid/higher-index framework may be required. Small-time regularization and large-time convergence or replacement constructions must be justified, not presumed from this formal expression. The record does not specify whether it seeks measured numerical invariants, higher rho classes, or both, and does not define a precise meaning of "useful" or a particular geometric application.

The supplied assessment dated 2026-08-21 reports that foliated eta and rho invariants and stability/homotopy properties have been established under important hypotheses, but says the broad questions remain unresolved in full generality. Its omitted hypotheses prevent promotion of that summary to an unconditional construction or homotopy-invariance theorem.

Short English statement

The question seeks secondary spectral or index-theoretic invariants for foliated Dirac operators and covering spaces, together with meaningful properties and geometric uses. The extraction is broad: the invariant's target, trace framework, analytic conditions, and application are not fixed.
