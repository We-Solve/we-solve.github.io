Several Complex Variables Problem List — Problem 3

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ and $N$ be strongly pseudoconvex CR manifolds of real dimension $3$. Their CR structures consist of real rank-two distributions $H_M\subset TM$ and $H_N\subset TN$, with complex structures $J_M$ and $J_N$ on these distributions. Strong pseudoconvexity makes the horizontal distributions contact distributions. A smooth map $f:M\to N$ is CR if, for every $p\in M$, \[ {\,\mathrm{d}} f_p(H_{M,p})\subseteq H_{N,f(p)},\qquad {\,\mathrm{d}} f_p\circ J_{M,p}=J_{N,f(p)}\circ{\,\mathrm{d}} f_p\quad\text{on }H_{M,p}. \] Given a smooth positive definite inner product $g$ on a horizontal distribution $H$, its Carnot--Carathéodory distance is locally defined by \[ d_g(x,y)=\inf_{\gamma}\int_0^1\sqrt{g_{\gamma(t)}(\dot\gamma(t),\dot\gamma(t))}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t, \] where the infimum runs over absolutely continuous curves joining $x$ to $y$ with $\dot\gamma(t)\in H_{\gamma(t)}$ almost everywhere. Distances may be considered separately on connected components. For CR-compatible choices one requires $g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y)$.

Source question. Suppose $f:M\to N$ is a homeomorphism that maps infinitesimal spheres for Carnot--Carathéodory metrics to infinitesimal spheres, as required by the source's definition of conformality. Must $f$ be CR?

The phrase "maps infinitesimal spheres to infinitesimal spheres" is not given a limiting definition in the supplied record. One possible precise metric interpretation, after fixing distances $d_M,d_N$, is the following. For sufficiently small $r>0$, put \[ L_f(p,r)=\sup_{d_M(p,q)=r}d_N(f(p),f(q)),\qquad \ell_f(p,r)=\inf_{d_M(p,q)=r}d_N(f(p),f(q)). \] Metric conformality could then mean \[ \limsup_{r\downarrow0}\frac{L_f(p,r)}{\ell_f(p,r)}=1 \qquad\text{for every }p\in M. \] Under this interpretation, a precise version asks whether every homeomorphism satisfying this condition is smooth and satisfies the CR identities above. This is a conditional formalization: the supplied source does not explicitly select this distortion condition, specify the horizontal metrics or their quantification, or state the regularity intended by the conclusion. It also imposes no explicit orientation condition and does not discuss whether conjugate-CR maps are to be included. These qualifications must be resolved before treating this as a uniquely determined statement.

Short English statement

The question attributed to Puqi Tang asks whether a conformal homeomorphism between strongly pseudoconvex CR three-manifolds must be CR, where conformality means preserving infinitesimal Carnot--Carathéodory spheres. The exact metric definition, metric choices, orientation conventions, and intended regularity remain unspecified. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied source data, not an independently verified status assessment.
