New Rational Lyapunov Exponents on Hilbert Modular Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}$. A Hilbert modular surface is an arithmetic quotient of $\mathbb H\times\mathbb H$ associated with a real quadratic field, with the two factors corresponding to its two real embeddings. The source does not specify the field, arithmetic subgroup, level structure, or treatment of quotient singularities and compactification.

A Kobayashi geodesic curve is understood here as a complex curve whose lifted parametrization from its universal covering disk is a holomorphic isometric embedding for the Kobayashi distances. The precise class of curves intended by the source, including any algebraicity or finite-volume requirement, remains unspecified.

A Lyapunov exponent describes an asymptotic exponential growth rate of vectors under a linear cocycle over a dynamical flow. Its definition requires the cocycle, flow, invariant measure, and time normalization. These data, and the particular exponent attached to a geodesic curve, are not identified in the supplied record. Write $\lambda(C)$ provisionally for that intended exponent, without selecting these missing data.

Source question, with unresolved qualifications. Can one construct a Hilbert modular surface $X$ and a Kobayashi geodesic curve $C\subset X$ such that the intended Lyapunov exponent satisfies \[ \lambda(C)\in{\mathbb{Q}}\setminus \left\{1,\frac12,\frac13,\frac15,\frac17\right\}? \] This expresses the request for an exponent outside the displayed list; it does not assert realizability of every rational number outside that list. The source does not say whether the surface may vary freely, whether a particular surface is intended, or whether one distinguished exponent or any member of a Lyapunov spectrum is meant. Consequently, the displayed formulation is provisional until these qualifications and the defining dynamical data are recovered.

Short English statement

Construct a Kobayashi geodesic curve on a Hilbert modular surface with a rational Lyapunov exponent outside $\{1,1/2,1/3,1/5,1/7\}$. The supplied record leaves the relevant dynamical system, exponent normalization, and admissible class of curves and surfaces unspecified.
