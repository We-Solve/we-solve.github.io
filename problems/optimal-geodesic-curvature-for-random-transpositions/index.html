Optimal Geodesic Curvature for Random Transpositions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer, let $S_n$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and let $T_n=\{(i\,j):1\leq i<j\leq n\}$ be its set of transpositions. A random-transposition chain has transitions obtained by multiplying a permutation by a transposition. Its transition rates and time normalization must be specified to determine a numerical curvature constant; these are not supplied in the record.

The supplied background identifies the intended quantity as an entropic geodesic-convexity constant. The following makes that interpretation explicit, conditionally on the missing transport structure. Let $\pi_n(\sigma)=1/n!$ be the uniform probability measure on $S_n$, and define \[ \mathcal P_n=\left\{\rho:S_n\to[0,\infty):\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\rho(\sigma)\pi_n(\sigma)=1\right\},\qquad \operatorname{Ent}_{\pi_n}(\rho)=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\rho(\sigma)\log\rho(\sigma)\,\pi_n(\sigma), \] with $0\log 0=0$. If $\mathcal W_n$ is the intended discrete transport metric associated with the intended random-transposition generator, define, using the displayed convexity convention, \[ \kappa_n=\sup\left\{k\in{\mathbb{R}}:\ \operatorname{Ent}_{\pi_n}(\rho_t)\leq (1-t)\operatorname{Ent}_{\pi_n}(\rho_0)+t\operatorname{Ent}_{\pi_n}(\rho_1) -\frac{k}{2}t(1-t)\mathcal W_n(\rho_0,\rho_1)^2 \ \text{for every constant-speed $\mathcal W_n$-geodesic $(\rho_t)_{t\in[0,1]}$ and every $t\in[0,1]$}\right\}. \] This is a conditional interpretation: the record does not define $\mathcal W_n$, the generator normalization, the precise geodesic domain, or the convexity convention. Those choices must be recovered from the cited sources before identifying this definition with the source's $\kappa$.

Requested statement. For the random-transposition model and conventions intended by the source, determine the asymptotic order of its optimal constant $\kappa_n$ as $n\to\infty$. The supplied record reports the bounds \[ \frac{4}{n^2-n}\leq\kappa_n\leq\frac{2}{n-1}\qquad(n\geq2). \] The target is matching upper and lower bounds: identify an explicit positive scale $a_n$ and constants $0<c\leq C<\infty$, independent of $n$, such that $c a_n\leq\kappa_n\leq C a_n$ for all sufficiently large $n$. The reported numerical bounds and this asymptotic target refer to the source's normalization, which remains unspecified in the supplied record.

Short English statement

Determine the asymptotic size of the optimal entropic geodesic-convexity constant for random transpositions on $S_n$. The supplied bounds range from order $n^{-2}$ to order $n^{-1}$. A complete formulation requires the source's generator normalization and discrete transport metric. The supplied open-status assessment has not been independently verified.
