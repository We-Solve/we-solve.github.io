Math-942

Moments of Quadratic Dirichlet L-Functions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a nonzero integer $d$, say that $d$ is squarefree if no prime square divides $d$, and write \[ \mathcal S(N)=\{d\in{\mathbb{Z}}:0<|d|\leq N,\ d\text{ squarefree}\}. \] For a Dirichlet character $\chi$, let $L(s,\chi)$ denote the analytic continuation of the Dirichlet series \[ L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\chi(n)}{n^s},\qquad \operatorname{Re}(s)>1. \] The source intends $\chi_d$ to be a quadratic Dirichlet character associated with $d$, but does not specify this association. Consequently, the following is the supplied conjectural assertion with an unresolved character convention, rather than a fully determined formulation.

For each fixed integer $k\geq1$, there exists an explicit polynomial $Q_k$, independent of $N$, of degree exactly $k(k+1)/2$, such that \[ \frac{1}{N}\sum_{d\in\mathcal S(N)}L\!\left(\frac12,\chi_d\right)^k =Q_k(\log N)+o(1) \qquad(N\to\infty). \] Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm, and $o(1)$ means that, for each fixed $k$, the difference between the displayed average and $Q_k(\log N)$ tends to zero. No uniformity in $k$ is asserted. The denominator is $N$, as supplied, and both signs of $d$ occur.

The character convention requires clarification: squarefree integers and fundamental discriminants are different indexing sets. For example, one possible convention associates a squarefree $d$ with the fundamental discriminant \[ D(d)=\begin{cases}d,&d\equiv1\pmod4,\\4d,&d\not\equiv1\pmod4,\end{cases} \] and then uses the Kronecker character $\left(\frac{D(d)}{\cdot}\right)$. The supplied record does not establish that this is its intended convention. This draft therefore does not adopt that convention or replace the squarefree indexing condition by a fundamental-discriminant condition. The meaning of "explicit" is also left unspecified: neither coefficients of $Q_k$ nor a precise recipe for them is supplied.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture predicts a polynomial in $\log N$, of degree $k(k+1)/2$, for every fixed positive integral moment of central quadratic Dirichlet $L$-values, with an additive error tending to zero after division by $N$. A precise statement still requires the intended definition of $\chi_d$ for signed squarefree integers. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
