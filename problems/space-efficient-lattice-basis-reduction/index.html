A limited buffer-detection time--space calculation

The report asks for less space in a generalized-birthday procedure, but its entire baseline algorithm and acceptable time tradeoff are not supplied in the extract. Assume integers $M\ge1$ and $1\le S\le M$. We test one simple potential route: discard all but $S$ stored values while continuing to compare each fresh independent uniform value from a set of size $M$ with the current buffer. The buffer may be selected adaptively from the past, contains at most $S$ values, and a collision is detected only by equality between a fresh value and one of those stored values. No comparisons to discarded values, compressed certificates, recomputation or dependent walk are allowed in this restricted model.
Proposition: In $T$ fresh draws the probability of detecting a collision is at most $TS/M$. Hence success probability at least $1/2$ requires $T\ge M/(2S)$.
Proof: Conditional on any past history and buffer choice, the fresh value is uniform and independent. The buffer contains at most $S$ distinct values, so the conditional chance of a match is at most $S/M$. Averaging gives the same unconditional bound for each draw. A union bound over the $T$ draws proves the assertion, even if the process stops on the first collision, by imagining independent unused draws after stopping. ∎
Thus maintaining a success probability bounded away from zero in $O(\sqrt M)$ draws in this model requires $S=\Omega(\sqrt M)$. This is not a general collision-finding space lower bound: memory can be encoded differently, work can be repeated, and dependent iteration can change what information is retained. It also does not address the multi-list algebraic structure in generalized birthday algorithms.

Verification and remaining gaps

For $M=4$, store the latest value only, starting with one uniform sample and making two further fresh draws. Exhaustive enumeration of all $4^3$ triples gives detection probability $7/16$ for equality of adjacent samples, versus $5/8$ for any repeated value among all three samples. The exact missed case has first and third values equal but different from the discarded middle value. The proposed simple-discard route loses evidence in precisely this way. No improved implementation, acceptable time--space tradeoff for the source algorithm, or unrestricted lower bound was established.
