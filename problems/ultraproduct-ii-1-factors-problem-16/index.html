Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 16

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor is a diffuse von Neumann algebra $M$ with center ${\mathbb{C}} 1$ and a faithful normal tracial state $\tau_M$, normalized by $\tau_M(1)=1$. Write $\|x\|_{2,M}=\tau_M(x^*x)^{1/2}$.

In the finite-set formulation, $M$ has property $\Gamma$ if, for every finite subset $F\subseteq M$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a unitary $u\in M$ such that \[ \tau_M(u)=0,\qquad \|ux-xu\|_{2,M}<\varepsilon\quad(x\in F). \] A non-$\Gamma$ factor is a factor without this property.

Let $\mathcal R$ denote the separable hyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor. For a free ultrafilter $\mathcal U$ on ${\mathbb{N}}$, its tracial ultrapower is \[ \mathcal R^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty({\mathbb{N}},\mathcal R)\big/\{(x_n):\lim_{n\to\mathcal U}\|x_n\|_{2,\mathcal R}=0\}. \] Under the usual separable-predual convention, a factor $M$ is Connes embeddable if it admits a unital trace-preserving embedding into $\mathcal R^{\mathcal U}$ for some such $\mathcal U$. The source does not specify separability of the predual. Thus this definition specifies the standard separable interpretation, while the intended embeddability convention and property-$\Gamma$ convention for any nonseparable factors remain unresolved.

Write $M\equiv N$ when $M$ and $N$ are elementarily equivalent in the continuous first-order language of tracial von Neumann algebras: every sentence has the same value in $M$ and $N$.

The source asks the following two existence questions, with the separability qualification above unresolved.

  1. Do there exist Connes embeddable type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors $M,N$, each without property $\Gamma$, such that $M\not\equiv N$?
  2. Does there exist a family $(M_\alpha)_{\alpha<2^{\aleph_0}}$ of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors such that every $M_\alpha$ lacks property $\Gamma$ and \[ \alpha\ne\beta\ \Longrightarrow\ M_\alpha\not\equiv M_\beta? \]
The second question does not explicitly repeat Connes embeddability. The displayed formulation preserves that omission; whether embeddability is intended to carry over from the first question requires clarification.

The supplied statement includes a solved attribution to J. Peterson, whereas the registry status is open. These are conflicting supplied labels, not independently verified conclusions about either question.

Short English statement

The problem asks for two Connes embeddable type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors without property $\Gamma$ that have different first-order theories, and for continuum many pairwise elementarily inequivalent non-$\Gamma$ factors. Separability is unspecified, and Connes embeddability is not explicitly required in the second question. The supplied solved attribution conflicts with the registry's open label.
