Math-3565

Prime-Reduced Standard and Costandard Modules

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the report's algebraic-group highest-weight setting in characteristic $p$, with Coxeter number $h$, dominant weights $X^+$ and $p$-restricted dominant weights \[ X_1^+=\{\nu\in X^+:0\le\langle\nu,\alpha^\vee\rangle<p \text{ for every simple coroot }\alpha^\vee\}. \] Write $L(\nu)$ for the simple highest-weight module, $\Delta(\nu)$ for the Weyl module and $\nabla(\nu)$ for the dual Weyl module. The superscript $(1)$ denotes Frobenius twist. Regular means $p$-regular in the affine-dot-action convention: the shifted weight avoids the affine root hyperplanes. In standard notation this is $\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle\notin p{\mathbb{Z}}$ for every root, where $\rho$ is half the sum of positive roots.

Assume $p>h$. For each regular dominant $\lambda=\lambda_0+p\lambda_1$, with $\lambda_0\in X_1^+$ and $\lambda_1\in X^+$, define exactly the prime-marked modules \[ \Delta'_{\mathrm{red}}(\lambda) =L(\lambda_0)\otimes\Delta(\lambda_1)^{(1)},\qquad \nabla'_{\mathrm{red}}(\lambda) =L(\lambda_0)\otimes\nabla(\lambda_1)^{(1)}. \] Conjecture 3.6 asks whether, for every such weight, \[ \Delta'_{\mathrm{red}}(\lambda)[-l(\lambda)]\in E^L, \qquad \nabla'_{\mathrm{red}}(\lambda)[-l(\lambda)]\in E^R. \] Here $[-l(\lambda)]$ is the source's derived shift, $l(\lambda)$ its affine-length convention, and $E^L,E^R$ its specified left/right derived subcategories. Their generators or homological membership criteria are not given in the extract, and cannot be replaced by invented Ext-vanishing definitions. The full group/root-datum hypotheses and block/truncation conventions must likewise be matched to the report.

The original extraction also mentions the distinct Conjecture 3.7 filtration request, separately stated in companion permanent ID $30000452$: $\Delta(\lambda)$ should have a finite filtration with sections $\Delta_{\mathrm{red}}(\nu)$, and $\nabla(\lambda)$ one with sections $\nabla_{\mathrm{red}}(\nu)$, for regular dominant $\nu$. Those are unprimed reduced modules; they are not identified with the tensor-defined prime modules above. Companion ID $30000450$ likewise concerns unprimed-module membership in $E^L,E^R$. The verified primary target of this record is precisely Conjecture 3.6, while those bundled neighboring requests are retained as related context.

The literature assessment checked on 21 August 2026 says the surrounding conditional results do not prove the full regular-weight homological and filtration assertions. No restriction to the Jantzen region or additional character-formula hypothesis is inserted as though it solved the stated all-regular-weight question. The open label is dated triage requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

For $p>h$, do the tensor-defined prime-reduced standard and costandard modules, shifted by minus the prescribed affine length, lie in the report's left/right derived subcategories for every regular dominant weight? Prime and unprimed reduced modules, and the separate filtration conjecture, must remain distinct.
