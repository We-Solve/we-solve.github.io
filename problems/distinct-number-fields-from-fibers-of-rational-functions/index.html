Distinct Number Fields from Fibers of Rational Functions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns a curve $X/{\mathbb{Q}}$ of genus $g$ and a nonconstant rational function $t\in{\mathbb{Q}}(X)$ of degree $\nu\geq2$. For an integral curve, the function-field interpretation of this degree is $\nu=[{\mathbb{Q}}(X):{\mathbb{Q}}(t)]$. The source does not explicitly specify smoothness, projectivity, or geometric integrality, or its convention for genus when these hypotheses are absent.

Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$. For each positive integer $n$, consider an algebraic point $P_n\in X(\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}})$ at which $t$ is defined and such that \[ t(P_n)=n. \] Write ${\mathbb{Q}}(P_n)$ for its field of definition over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, viewed as a finite extension inside $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$. For a chosen sequence $(P_n)_{n\geq1}$, set \[ D_N=\#\{{\mathbb{Q}}(P_n):1\leq n\leq N\},\qquad N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}, \] where this displayed definition counts distinct subfields of the fixed algebraic closure.

The requested lower bound is \[ D_N\geq c(g,\nu)N \qquad\text{for every integer }N\geq N_0, \] with $c(g,\nu)>0$, depending only on $g$ and $\nu$, and some integer $N_0\geq1$.

The formulation is unresolved in several respects: the source does not say whether the bound must hold for every sequence of choices $P_n$, or whether suitable choices may be made; it does not specify the permitted dependence of $N_0$; and it does not explicitly distinguish equality of embedded fields from isomorphism over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. If fields are to be counted up to ${\mathbb{Q}}$-isomorphism, $D_N$ must instead denote the number of their isomorphism classes. The source also does not state hypotheses ensuring that every indicated fiber contains an eligible point on the chosen model of $X$. These qualifications require clarification before the displayed target becomes a fully quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether points above the integers under a rational function of degree at least two yield linearly many distinct number fields, with a positive constant depending only on the genus and degree. The curve hypotheses, choice of points, meaning of distinct fields, and dependence of the threshold require clarification.
