Zero-entropy probability system with no smooth positive-volume model

OpenAI's claim

A zero-entropy invertible ergodic nonatomic probability system is claimed to have no realization by any finite-dimensional smooth diffeomorphism preserving a strictly positive smooth density. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
OpenAI claims an ergodic invertible probability-preserving transformation $(X,\mu,T)$ on a standard nonatomic probability space with \[ h_\mu(T)=0, \] which is not conjugate measurably after deleting invariant sets of measure zero to any smooth diffeomorphism of a compact manifold of finite dimension whose preserved probability measure has smooth everywhere-positive density. The exclusion covers nonorientable models as well as manifolds with a smooth boundary. The measure class is positive smooth volume; arbitrary singular invariant measures are not covered.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 152, 2026-09-25): For smooth positive volume, a dyadic switching estimate joins the future of one nearby point to the past of another with controlled output measure and summable cell complexity. Derivative-log bounds and finite boundary-value calculations establish that estimate; the constructed system violates the resulting measure-theoretic switching constraint in every finite dimension.
