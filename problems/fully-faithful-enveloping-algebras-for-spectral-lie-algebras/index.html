Math-3873

Fully Faithful Enveloping Algebras for Spectral Lie Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T(n)$ denote the chromatic telescope intended by the source, and let $L_{T(n)}$ denote Bousfield localization of spectra with respect to $T(n)$. A spectrum $X$ is $T(n)$-local if its localization map $X\to L_{T(n)}X$ is an equivalence. The supplied record does not specify the prime, the permitted heights $n$, or the telescope data; these remain parameters requiring confirmation from the source.

Write $\mathcal L_{T(n)}$ for the intended $\infty$-category of $T(n)$-local spectral Lie algebras, $\mathcal H_{T(n)}$ for the intended $\infty$-category of $T(n)$-local spectral Hopf algebras, and \[ U\colon \mathcal L_{T(n)}\longrightarrow \mathcal H_{T(n)} \] for the universal enveloping algebra functor equipped with the Hopf structure intended by the source. Here $\operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal C}(A,B)$ denotes the mapping space between objects $A,B$ of an $\infty$-category $\mathcal C$.

Conjectural statement. For every pair $\mathfrak g,\mathfrak h\in\mathcal L_{T(n)}$, the map induced by $U$, \[ \operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal L_{T(n)}}(\mathfrak g,\mathfrak h) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal H_{T(n)}}(U\mathfrak g,U\mathfrak h), \] is an equivalence of spaces. Thus $U$ is fully faithful in the $\infty$-categorical sense, including all higher mapping information.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. This expresses the full-faithfulness assertion in the supplied statement, conditional on the intended categories and functor. The record does not identify the spectral Lie operad, specify the Hopf algebra structure and its morphisms, or state whether cocommutativity, an antipode, completion, or additional restrictions on objects are required. Consequently, these symbols do not yet determine a unique fully specified conjecture. The supplied original excerpt calls the intended assertion "Conjecture A" and a parallel of the Milnor--Moore theorem, but does not reproduce its mathematical content.

Supplied status qualification. The record labels the problem partially solved. Its dated assessment states that full faithfulness holds on abelian $T(n)$-local spectral Lie algebras, including infinite-loop cases, while the assertion for all $T(n)$-local spectral Lie algebras remains unproved. These are supplied curation claims, not independently verified results here; the meanings and hypotheses of the stated special cases require source confirmation.

Short English statement

The conjecture says that universal enveloping algebras, with their intended spectral Hopf structure, retain the entire mapping space between any two $T(n)$-local spectral Lie algebras. The source record leaves the precise categories and chromatic parameters unspecified. Its supplied partial-solution assessment covers abelian and infinite-loop cases, subject to independent verification.
