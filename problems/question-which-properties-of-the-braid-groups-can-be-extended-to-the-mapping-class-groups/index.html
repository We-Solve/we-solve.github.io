Question — Which properties of the braid groups can be extended to the mapping class groups?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Research question. Which properties of braid groups extend to mapping class groups? Here $B_n$ denotes the braid group on $n$ strands, and the mapping class group depends on the chosen surface and on orientation, boundary, and marked-point conventions.

The source poses a comparative program, not one universally quantified theorem about every formal property. A precise instance must identify a braid-group property, the relevant strand range, the target surface class, and whether an analogue, a literal transfer, or a specified strengthening is sought. The excerpt gives no chosen properties, transfer map, target genus range, or criterion for completion. Thus no fixed list of positive or negative examples, exhaustive classification, or decision algorithm is invented.

The supplied research summary mentions residual finiteness and linearity and asserts that many properties fail to extend, including a claim of mapping-class-group nonlinearity. It provides no hypotheses or supporting citations for those assertions. They are retained as unverified editorial suggestions, not mathematical facts or answers to the source question.

Short English statement

Investigate which specified braid-group properties have analogues for specified mapping class groups. The source gives a broad research program; the summary's unsupported examples do not supply a verified classification.
