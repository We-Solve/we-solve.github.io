Math-4671

Eventual Lefschetz Dichotomies for Symmetric Ideal Chains

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the coefficient field, ambient rings, grading, transition maps, or precise meaning of a $\operatorname{Sym}$-invariant chain. Consequently, it does not determine a complete class of chains over which the question is quantified. The following gives a conditional formulation of its Lefschetz assertion.

Suppose that a fixed field $K$ and homogeneous ideals $I_n$ in graded commutative $K$-algebras $R_n$ are given, and that each quotient \[ A_n=R_n/I_n=\bigoplus_{i\geq 0}(A_n)_i \] is a finite-dimensional standard graded $K$-algebra: $(A_n)_0=K$, and $A_n$ is generated by $(A_n)_1$. These are additional hypotheses for defining the usual graded Lefschetz properties; they are not fully specified in the source record. Saying that $I_n$ has a Lefschetz property is interpreted here as saying that $A_n$ has that property.

A finite-dimensional standard graded $K$-algebra $A$ has the weak Lefschetz property, denoted $\operatorname{WLP}(A)$, if there exists $\ell\in A_1$ such that, for every integer $i\geq0$, multiplication induces a map \[ \times\ell:A_i\longrightarrow A_{i+1} \] of rank $\min\{\dim_K A_i,\dim_K A_{i+1}\}$. It has the strong Lefschetz property, denoted $\operatorname{SLP}(A)$, if there exists $\ell\in A_1$ such that, for all integers $i,d\geq0$, the map \[ \times\ell^d:A_i\longrightarrow A_{i+d} \] has rank $\min\{\dim_K A_i,\dim_K A_{i+d}\}$. These definitions require the Lefschetz element to be defined over $K$; whether the intended question instead permits extending the field is unspecified.

Proposed eventual-constancy question. For every chain satisfying the intended, but incompletely specified, $\operatorname{Sym}$-invariance assumptions, is it true that, separately for each $P\in\{\operatorname{WLP},\operatorname{SLP}\}$, \[ \left(\exists N\geq1\;\forall n\geq N:\ P(A_n)\right) \quad\lor\quad \left(\exists N\geq1\;\forall n\geq N:\ \neg P(A_n)\right)? \] The threshold and the eventual truth value may depend on the chain and on the chosen property. This separate interpretation follows the supplied cleaned statement. A precise unconditional formulation requires the missing algebraic and symmetry hypotheses. Moreover, the supplied literature assessment says that the extracted report passage is only a general remark about possible limit behavior and identifies no definite theorem or conjecture; attribution of this precise question to that passage therefore remains unresolved.

Short English statement

The proposed question asks whether the weak and strong Lefschetz properties each eventually stabilize along every intended symmetric chain of Artinian quotients. Each property may eventually hold everywhere or eventually fail everywhere, with its own threshold. The source leaves the class of chains and essential algebraic hypotheses unspecified, and its dated assessment questions whether the report contains this precise conjecture.
