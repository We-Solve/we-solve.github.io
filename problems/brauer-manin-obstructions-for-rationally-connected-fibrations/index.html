Brauer–Manin Obstructions for Rationally Connected Fibrations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a number field, let $\Omega_k$ be its set of places, and write ${\mathbb{A}}_k$ for its ring of adeles. For a proper variety $Y$ over a number field $L$, put \[ \operatorname{Br}(Y)=H^2_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Y,\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}, \qquad Y({\mathbb{A}}_L)^{\operatorname{Br}(Y)} = \left\{(y_v)\in Y({\mathbb{A}}_L): \sum_{v\in\Omega_L}\operatorname{inv}_v\left(\alpha(y_v)\right)=0 \text{ for every }\alpha\in\operatorname{Br}(Y)\right\}. \] Here $\alpha(y_v)\in\operatorname{Br}(L_v)$ is obtained by evaluation, and $\operatorname{inv}_v$ is the local invariant map. Saying that the Brauer--Manin obstruction is the only obstruction to the Hasse principle for $Y/L$ means \[ Y({\mathbb{A}}_L)^{\operatorname{Br}(Y)}\ne\varnothing \quad\Longrightarrow\quad Y(L)\ne\varnothing. \tag{H} \] This is an existence assertion; it does not assert density or weak approximation.

Let \[ f:X\longrightarrow\mathbb P^1_k \] be a projective surjective morphism whose generic fibre $X_\eta$, over $K=k(\mathbb P^1_k)$, is geometrically rationally connected. This last condition means rational connectedness after extension to an algebraic closure $\overline K$; in the usual smooth projective setting, two general geometric points can be joined by the image of a morphism from $\mathbb P^1_{\overline K}$.

Question supplied by the record. If every smooth fibre of $f$ satisfies $(\mathrm H)$, must $X/k$ satisfy $(\mathrm H)$?

For the interpretation involving fibres over $k$-rational base points, define \[ T_k=\{t\in\mathbb P^1(k):X_t=f^{-1}(t)\text{ is smooth over }k\}. \] The question then has the explicit form \[ \left[ \forall t\in T_k,\quad X_t({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}(X_t)}\ne\varnothing \Longrightarrow X_t(k)\ne\varnothing \right] \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left[ X({\mathbb{A}}_k)^{\operatorname{Br}(X)}\ne\varnothing \Longrightarrow X(k)\ne\varnothing \right]. \] Unresolved qualifications. The source does not specify whether "every smooth fibre" means only fibres over $k$-rational points or all smooth fibres over closed points. In the latter interpretation, the hypothesis is instead $(\mathrm H)$ for $X_t/k(t)$ at every closed point $t$ with smooth fibre, where $k(t)$ is its residue number field. These quantifiers are different, and the intended one requires review. The source also does not explicitly assume that $X$ is smooth or geometrically integral, or specify its scheme-theoretic conventions. No such additional hypothesis is inserted here; the intended total-space hypotheses and the convention for rational connectedness must be confirmed before this becomes a fully qualified formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether the sufficiency of the Brauer--Manin obstruction for rational points on every smooth fibre passes to the total space of a projective fibration over $\mathbb P^1$ with geometrically rationally connected generic fibre. The scope of the fibre quantifier and the regularity and integrality hypotheses on the total space remain unresolved. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
