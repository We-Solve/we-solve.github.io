Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 11

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a field. A central simple $F$-algebra is a finite-dimensional associative $F$-algebra with identity, center $F$, and no nonzero proper two-sided ideals. Its degree is $\deg(A)=\sqrt{\dim_F A}$. The Brauer group $\operatorname{Br}(F)$ consists of equivalence classes of central simple $F$-algebras, where two algebras are equivalent if suitable matrix algebras over them are isomorphic; its operation is induced by tensor product over $F$. Write $[A]$ for the class of $A$, and let $\operatorname{per}(A)$ be the order of $[A]$ in $\operatorname{Br}(F)$. Thus the source's word "similar" means equality of Brauer classes.

A cyclic algebra of degree $m$ is a cyclic crossed product $(L/F,\sigma,a)$, where $L/F$ is a cyclic Galois field extension of degree $m$, $\sigma$ generates its Galois group, and $a\in F^\times$. It is generated by $L$ and an element $u$ satisfying $u^m=a$ and $ux=\sigma(x)u$ for $x\in L$.

Let $m,n$ be positive integers with the same prime divisors and with $m\mid n$. Interpreting the requested number as a uniform bound on the number of factors, define \[ \ell_F(m,n)=\min\left\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:\begin{array}{l} \text{for every central simple }F\text{-algebra }A\text{ with}\ \deg(A)=n\ \text{and}\ \operatorname{per}(A)\mid m,\\ \text{there exist }0\leq t\leq k\text{ cyclic }F\text{-algebras }C_1,\ldots,C_t\\ \text{of degree }m\text{ such that }[A]=[C_1\otimes_F\cdots\otimes_F C_t] \end{array}\right\}. \] The empty tensor product represents the zero Brauer class. Set $\ell_F(m,n)=\infty$ if no finite uniform bound exists. The source literally uses a tensor product of $\ell_F(m,n)$ factors; whether it requires exactly that many factors, and which split cyclic factors are permitted for padding, is not specified.

The source assumes that $\operatorname{Br}_p[m](F)$ is generated by cyclic algebras. The notation $\operatorname{Br}_p[m](F)$, including the role of its subscript, is not defined in the supplied record, so this hypothesis is retained without identifying it with a particular subgroup of $\operatorname{Br}(F)$.

Problem. Let $p$ be a prime and let $r\leq s$ be integer exponents for which $p^r,p^s$ are positive integers. Suppose that $\operatorname{Br}_p[p^r](F)$ is generated by cyclic algebras, in the source's unresolved notation. Determine \[ \ell_F(p^r,p^s). \] The source does not explicitly specify whether the boundary case $r=0$ is included.

As background, the supplied source states that if $F$ contains the $m$-th roots of unity, the generic division algebra and the Merkurjev--Suslin theorem show that $\ell_F(m,n)$ is finite. It further states that in this case one may reduce to $m$ a prime power by [Tig84, Th. 2.2], and calls the invariant the symbol length. These are supplied contextual assertions, not independently verified here. The roots-of-unity condition is not explicitly imposed in the final problem, and no characteristic restriction on $F$ is supplied.

Short English statement

For a field $F$, determine the least uniform number of cyclic algebras of degree $p^r$ needed to represent every Brauer class having a representative of degree $p^s$ and period dividing $p^r$, under the source's cyclic-generation hypothesis. The supplied notation for that hypothesis and some boundary conventions require clarification.
