Math-3966

Boundary entropy of an attracting basin

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let $U\subset\widehat{\mathbb C}$ be a simply connected domain, $f:U\to U$ a proper holomorphic map of degree $d\geq2$, and suppose its iterates converge to a constant in $U$. Suppose also that $f$ extends holomorphically to a neighbourhood of $\overline U$. A point $x\in\partial U$ is accessible from $U$ if there is a continuous curve $\eta:[0,1]\to\overline U$ with $\eta([0,1))\subset U$ and $\eta(1)=x$. A periodic point satisfies $f^p(x)=x$ for some $p\geq1$. For a continuous map $T$ on a compact metric space, its topological entropy is $h_{\rm top}(T)=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\log s_n(\varepsilon)$, where $s_n(\varepsilon)$ is the largest size of a set whose distinct points are separated by more than $\varepsilon$ in $\max_{0\leq j<n}d(T^jx,T^jy)$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Is $h_{\rm top}(f|_{\partial U})=\log d$? [S2]

Short English statement

Does the boundary dynamics of this attracting domain have entropy equal to the logarithm of the map degree inside the domain?
