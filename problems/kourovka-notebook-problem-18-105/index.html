Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.105

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, and let ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ be the field with $p$ elements. Write \[ M=\left\{(a_i)_{i\geq 1}:a_i\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\ a_i=0\text{ for all but finitely many }i\right\}, \qquad x^a=\prod_{i\geq 1}x_i^{a_i}. \] Following the explicit description in the supplied source, define the commutative formal power series algebra \[ R=\left\{\sum_{a\in M}c_a x^a:c_a\in{\mathbb{F}}_p\right\}, \] with no restriction on the number of nonzero coefficients. Addition is coefficientwise, and multiplication is given by \[ \left(\sum_{a\in M}c_a x^a\right)\left(\sum_{b\in M}d_b x^b\right) =\sum_{e\in M}\left(\sum_{a+b=e}c_a d_b\right)x^e. \] For each fixed $e$, the inner sum is finite. Let \[ I=\left\{\sum_{a\in M}c_a x^a\in R:c_0=0\right\} \] be the maximal ideal of series with zero constant coefficient. Define the abstract ideal generated by products of two elements of $I$ by \[ J=I^2=\left\{\sum_{k=1}^{m}f_k g_k:m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\ f_k,g_k\in I\right\}. \] Here the sum is finite, and the empty sum is zero; no closure operation is included in the definition of $J$.

Question (John S. Wilson, Kourovka Notebook 18.105). For a prime $p$ and the algebra $R$ above, does there exist an ideal $U$ of $R$ such that \[ U\subsetneq I \qquad\text{and}\qquad U+J=I? \] The prime is an arbitrary parameter; the ideal $U$, if it exists, may depend on $p$.

The source additionally states that an affirmative answer makes $\mathrm{SL}_3(R)$ and the kernel of the reduction homomorphism \[ \mathrm{SL}_3(R)\longrightarrow\mathrm{SL}_3(R/U) \] provide an answer to the preceding question. Here $\mathrm{SL}_3(A)$ denotes the group of $3\times3$ matrices of determinant one over a commutative ring $A$. The preceding question is not included in the supplied record, so the intended group-theoretic consequence cannot be stated completely from this evidence.

Short English statement

For the algebra of arbitrary formal series in countably many commuting variables over ${\mathbb{F}}_p$, let $I$ be the ideal of series with zero constant term. Wilson asks whether a proper ideal $U\subsetneq I$ can satisfy $U+I^2=I$, where $I^2$ consists of finite sums of products of elements of $I$. The source's further application refers to an unavailable preceding question.
