Math-4180

Thom’s Conjecture for the Hyperfinite Factor’s Unitary Group

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ denote the hyperfinite factor of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ referred to in the supplied conjecture, and let $\tau$ be its normalized faithful normal trace, so that $\tau(1)=1$. A factor is a von Neumann algebra whose center consists of scalar multiples of its identity. Type $\mathrm{II}_1$ means that the factor is finite and has no nonzero minimal projections. Hyperfiniteness means that the algebra is the weak operator closure of a directed union of finite-dimensional unital $*$-subalgebras.

Define \[ \mathcal U(R)=\{u\in R:u^*u=uu^*=1\},\qquad \|x\|_2=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2},\qquad d_2(u,v)=\|u-v\|_2. \] A subgroup $\Gamma\leq\mathcal U(R)$ is uniformly discrete for this metric if there is a real number $\delta>0$ such that \[ \|g-h\|_2\geq\delta \quad\text{for all distinct }g,h\in\Gamma. \] By unitary invariance of the trace norm, this is equivalent to $\|g-1\|_2\geq\delta$ for every $g\in\Gamma\setminus\{1\}$. A free nonabelian subgroup means a subgroup isomorphic to a free group on at least two generators.

Conjecture (A. Thom, attributed as Andreas Thom in the supplied verification note). The group $\mathcal U(R)$ has no uniformly discrete free nonabelian subgroup with respect to $d_2$.

Equivalently, let $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ be the abstract free group on two generators, with identity $e$. The assertion is \[ \forall\text{ injective homomorphisms }\rho:F_2\longrightarrow\mathcal U(R),\quad \forall\varepsilon>0,\quad \exists w\in F_2\setminus\{e\}:\quad 0<\|\rho(w)-1\|_2<\varepsilon. \] The rank-two formulation suffices because every free nonabelian group contains a subgroup isomorphic to $F_2$, and a subgroup of a uniformly discrete group is uniformly discrete.

Unresolved scope qualification. The source uses the phrase "the hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor" without explicitly stating separability. Its usual interpretation is the hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with separable predual. The supplied record does not specify whether any broader, nonseparable formulation is intended; no such extension is asserted here.

Short English statement

Thom's conjecture says that any algebraically free pair of unitaries in the specified hyperfinite $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor generates nonidentity elements arbitrarily close to the identity in the normalized trace $2$-norm. The record labels the conjecture open; this is supplied curation, not an independently verified status assessment. The separability convention requires review.
