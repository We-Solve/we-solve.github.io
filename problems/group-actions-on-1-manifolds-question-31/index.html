Group Actions on 1-Manifolds — Question 31

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathrm{S}^1={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $G=\mathrm{Diff}^{\omega}_+(\mathrm{S}^1)$ be the group, under composition, of orientation-preserving real-analytic diffeomorphisms of the circle. Write $H^\bullet_{\mathrm{cont}}(G)$ for the continuous cohomology intended by the source, with its cup product, and denote its named Euler and Godbillon--Vey classes by $e$ and $\mathrm{GV}$, respectively.

The source asks whether these two classes generate the continuous cohomology as a graded algebra. Symbolically, writing $k$ for the intended coefficient ring, the question is \[ H^\bullet_{\mathrm{cont}}(G)\stackrel{?}{=}k[e,\mathrm{GV}]. \] Here $k[e,\mathrm{GV}]$ means the unital subalgebra generated by the two classes: every cohomology class should be expressible as a finite sum \[ \sum_{(i,j)\in F} a_{ij}\,e^{\smile i}\smile\mathrm{GV}^{\smile j}, \qquad F\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}^2\text{ finite},\quad a_{ij}\in k, \] where a zeroth cup power is the unit. This notation does not assert algebraic independence.

Unresolved conventions. The supplied record does not specify a topology on $G$, the coefficients and their action, the precise continuous-cohomology construction, or the degrees and definitions of the two named classes in that construction. The notation above retains these unspecified conventions; $k$ is a placeholder, not a universally quantified coefficient ring. A fully specified mathematical statement requires these choices to be recovered from the source. The interpretation of "generated" as generation under cup product is also an interpretation of the supplied wording.

Short English statement

Navas asks whether every continuous cohomology class of the group of orientation-preserving real-analytic circle diffeomorphisms can be expressed using the Euler and Godbillon--Vey classes, coefficients, addition, and cup products. The supplied record leaves the topology and cohomological conventions unspecified and labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
