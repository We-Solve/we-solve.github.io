Math-1341

Solved by semi-random triangle-free graph packings

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A graph $G$ is $r$-Ramsey for $H$ if every $r$-edge-coloring has a monochromatic $H$, and is Ramsey-minimal if no proper subgraph is $r$-Ramsey for $H$. Put \[ s_r(H)=\min\{\delta(G):G\text{ is }r\text{-Ramsey-minimal for }H\}. \] The aid recovers the source's abbreviation $s_r(3)$ as $s_r(K_3)$.

Source conjecture, item 13. As $r\to\infty$, \[ s_r(K_3)=\Theta(r^2\log r), \] meaning upper and lower bounds with absolute positive constants for sufficiently large $r$. The original remark suggests this would follow from an extension of the triangle-free process to $r$ colors.

Supplied resolution. The aid attributes the matching lower order and identity $s_r(K_3)=P_r(2)$ to FGLPS, and the upper order to Guo--Warnke. Here $P_r(2)$ is the least $|V|$ admitting pairwise edge-disjoint triangle-free graphs $G_1,\ldots,G_r$ on the same $V$ such that every vertex coloring $c:V\to\{1,\ldots,r\}$ has an edge $uv\in E(G_i)$ with $c(u)=c(v)=i$ for some $i$. This is the strong-coloring certificate property, not ordinary Ramsey arrowing of each layer.

The report describes Guo--Warnke packings on $n$ vertices with $\Theta(\sqrt{n/\log n})$ edge-disjoint triangle-free layers, each of independence number $O(\sqrt{n\log n})$. Choosing $n=\Theta(r^2\log r)$ with a suitable constant gives $r$ layers with $\alpha(G_i)<n/r$, hence the matching $P_r(2)$ upper bound. Their method is reported to iterate a host-robust semi-random Rödl-nibble variant, rather than implement one simultaneous ordinary $r$-color triangle-free process. The source remark is thus a proposed route, not a literal description of the supplied proof.

Reported aggregate certificate and deletion reserve. More generally, the certificate follows whenever \[ \sum_{i=1}^r\alpha(G_i)<|V|. \] Indeed, avoiding a strong edge would make every color class independent in its corresponding layer. On deleting $X\subset V$, the same layers restricted to $V\setminus X$ retain the property whenever \[ |V\setminus X|>\sum_i\alpha(G_i). \] The aid deduces that for every fixed $\eta\in(0,1)$ there is an $O_\eta(r^2\log r)$-vertex certificate surviving deletion of every $X$ with $|X|<\eta|V|$. This resilience concerns the auxiliary layer certificate; a Ramsey-minimal host itself is not claimed to remain Ramsey after vertex deletion. The literature resolution and the additional certificate corollaries are supplied claims requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

The source conjectures order $r^2\log r$ for the smallest minimum degree of an $r$-Ramsey-minimal triangle host. The aid reports a complete literature resolution via triangle-free layer packings and gives an aggregate independence-number test with a vertex-deletion reserve for those auxiliary certificates.
