Math-1612

A cusp obstruction and periodic arithmetic tiling models

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $n\ge2$, put $X_n=\mathrm{SL}_n({\mathbb{R}})/\mathrm{SL}_n({\mathbb{Z}})$, the space of unimodular lattices, and let $A$ be the determinant-one diagonal group acting by left multiplication. Its positive component is parametrized by \[ H=\{h\in{\mathbb{R}}^n:\textstyle\sum_i h_i=0\},\qquad a_h=\operatorname{diag}(e^{h_1},\ldots,e^{h_n}). \] S. Katok's Question 55 asks for analogues of geometric and arithmetic symbolic codings of this Weyl chamber action. The source does not prescribe an alphabet, regularity or multiplicity of the coding map, the treatment of the cusp, or a precise equivalence to lower-rank codings. It particularly suggests fractal tilings, as proposed by F. Ledrappier and S. Mozes.

The supplied aid reports a limited global obstruction. A closed finite-alphabet ${\mathbb{Z}}^{n-1}$ subshift is a closed invariant subset of $\mathcal A^{{\mathbb{Z}}^{n-1}}$ for a finite discrete alphabet $\mathcal A$, and is compact. It cannot admit a continuous surjection onto the full $X_n$, even when only the corresponding discrete diagonal subaction is considered. The quotient is noncompact: the lattices \[ \operatorname{diag}(e^{-t},e^{t/(n-1)},\ldots,e^{t/(n-1)}){\mathbb{Z}}^n, \qquad t\to\infty, \] have shortest-vector length tending to zero and escape compact sets by Mahler's criterion. This rules out a closed finite-alphabet compact topological cover, not all symbolic, nonclosed, countable-alphabet or measurable codings.

The aid's arithmetic model concerns a compact positive-diagonal orbit. Let $K$ be a totally real degree-$n$ number field with embeddings $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n$, and $M\subset K$ a full rank-$n$ ${\mathbb{Z}}$-module. Normalize its Minkowski image to a unimodular lattice $L=c\sigma(M)$, $c>0$. Define its multiplier order and totally positive units by \[ \mathcal O_M=\{u\in K:uM\subset M\},\qquad \mathcal O_M^{\times,+}=\{u\in K:uM=M,\ \sigma_i(u)>0\text{ for all }i\}. \] The exact period lattice reported by the aid is \[ P_M=\{(\log\sigma_1(u),\ldots,\log\sigma_n(u)): u\in\mathcal O_M^{\times,+}\}\subset H. \] These positive units have norm one, and $P_M$ is a full lattice in $H$. The orbit map identifies $A^0L$ equivariantly with $H/P_M$. Choosing a fundamental parallelotope for $P_M$ gives a one-parallelotope periodic tiling of $H$; its translation hull is likewise $H/P_M$, with exact translation periods $P_M$. This is an explicit compact-orbit tiling realization, not a global coding of $X_n$. It describes $A^0$; the finitely many sign components of the full $A$ must be separately accounted for.

The source notes a symbolic construction [181] appearing in a wrong proof of Littlewood, with related [179, 180, 9]; it does not endorse that proof. It stresses the complexity of higher-dimensional Markov partitions, citing [29] for fractal sets in dimension three. It reports tiling-based successes for certain toral automorphisms and shifts [161, 13, 86, 87] and a Cartan action on $(\mathrm{GL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_p)\times\mathrm{GL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_q))/\Gamma$ with irreducible lattice $\Gamma$ [145, 147]. These precedents do not supply the requested full real-quotient coding.

Short English statement

Construct geometric or arithmetic symbolic codings for the diagonal action on $\mathrm{SL}_n({\mathbb{R}})/\mathrm{SL}_n({\mathbb{Z}})$. The supplied progress rules out compact finite-alphabet covers of the whole cusp-containing space and gives exact periodic tiling models for compact totally real arithmetic orbits.
