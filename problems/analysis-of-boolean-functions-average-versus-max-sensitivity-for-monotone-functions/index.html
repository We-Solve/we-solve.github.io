CS-41 · Math-5948

Analysis of Boolean Functions — Average versus Max Sensitivity for Monotone Functions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 1$, equip $\{-1,1\}^n$ with the coordinatewise order induced by $-1<1$. A Boolean function $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$ is monotone if $x\leq y$ implies $f(x)\leq f(y)$. For $x\in\{-1,1\}^n$ and $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, let $x^{\oplus i}$ denote the point obtained by reversing coordinate $i$. Define the sensitivity at $x$, maximum sensitivity, and total influence by \[ s_f(x)=\left|\{i:f(x)\neq f(x^{\oplus i})\}\right|,\qquad \operatorname{sens}[f]=\max_{x\in\{-1,1\}^n}s_f(x), \] \[ \mathbf I[f]=\sum_{i=1}^n\Pr_{x\sim\mathrm{Unif}(\{-1,1\}^n)}[f(x)\neq f(x^{\oplus i})] =2^{-n}\sum_{x\in\{-1,1\}^n}s_f(x). \] The supplied statement reads $\mathbf I[f]<o(\operatorname{sens}[f])$, without specifying an asymptotic parameter or uniformity. The strict inequality with a little-$o$ expression is not itself a precise asymptotic assertion. A natural precise interpretation, recorded here subject to confirmation of the intended formulation, is the following conjecture: \[ \begin{gathered} \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists S\geq 1\ \forall n\geq 1\ \forall\text{ monotone }f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\},\\ \operatorname{sens}[f]\geq S\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathbf I[f]\leq\varepsilon\operatorname{sens}[f]. \end{gathered} \] Thus the proposed interpretation is uniform over dimensions and monotone functions as maximum sensitivity tends to infinity. Constant functions have both quantities equal to zero and do not enter this asymptotic regime. No balance or essential-coordinate assumption is supplied. The source does not explicitly establish that this is its intended meaning.

As historical context, the supplied remarks report that the tightest example known in the source's context has $\mathbf I[f]\approx\operatorname{sens}[f]^{0.61}$, attributed to O'Donnell and Servedio, reference label [OS08b]. The meaning of the approximation, the exact exponent, and the example's asymptotic parameters are not specified in the supplied record; this remark is retained as a source report.

Short English statement

The proposed interpretation asks whether the average number of sensitive coordinates of a monotone Boolean function is uniformly negligible compared with its maximum number of sensitive coordinates, as that maximum grows. The source's asymptotic notation requires clarification. The registry labels the problem open; this label and the historical example are supplied information, not independently verified conclusions.
