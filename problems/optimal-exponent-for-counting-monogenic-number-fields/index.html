Optimal Exponent for Counting Monogenic Number Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ be a fixed positive integer in the degree range intended by the source; that range is not specified in the supplied record. A degree-$n$ number field is a finite extension $K/{\mathbb{Q}}$ with $[K:{\mathbb{Q}}]=n$. Write $\mathcal O_K$ for its ring of integers and $\operatorname{Disc}(K)$ for its signed field discriminant. The field is monogenic if there exists $\theta\in\mathcal O_K$ such that $\mathcal O_K={\mathbb{Z}}[\theta]$. Let $\widetilde K$ be its normal closure over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and let $S_n$ denote the symmetric group on $n$ letters.

Under the conventions of counting fields up to ${\mathbb{Q}}$-isomorphism and bounding the absolute field discriminant, define, for real $X>0$, \[ N_n(X)=\#\left\{[K]_{{\mathbb{Q}}}:\begin{array}{l} [K:{\mathbb{Q}}]=n,\quad \mathcal O_K={\mathbb{Z}}[\theta]\text{ for some }\theta\in\mathcal O_K,\\ \operatorname{Gal}(\widetilde K/{\mathbb{Q}})\cong S_n,\quad |\operatorname{Disc}(K)|<X \end{array}\right\}. \] These counting and discriminant conventions are explicit interpretive choices: the supplied statement does not specify them.

The supplied literature assessment attributes to Bhargava--Shankar--Wang the lower bound \[ N_n(X)\gg_n X^{1/2+1/n}. \] For a fixed admissible degree, this means that there exist constants $c_n>0$ and $X_n>0$ such that $N_n(X)\ge c_nX^{1/2+1/n}$ for every real $X\ge X_n$. The degree range and any hypotheses of the cited result are not supplied here.

A precise exponent-only interpretation of the proposed optimality is the following conditional formulation: for each fixed degree $n$ to which the source intends the conjecture to apply, and every real $\varepsilon>0$, there exist constants $C_{n,\varepsilon}>0$ and $X_{n,\varepsilon}>0$ such that \[ N_n(X)\le C_{n,\varepsilon}X^{1/2+1/n+\varepsilon} \qquad\text{for every real }X\ge X_{n,\varepsilon}. \] Together with the attributed lower bound, this expresses $N_n(X)=X^{1/2+1/n+o(1)}$ as $X\to\infty$, with $n$ fixed. The source does not distinguish this exponent-only assertion from the stronger bound $N_n(X)\ll_n X^{1/2+1/n}$, or from an asymptotic formula. Consequently, the intended degree range, counting conventions, discriminant convention, and strength of optimality remain unresolved; this is a conditional statement-only draft.

Short English statement

The proposed claim is that the supplied lower-bound exponent $1/2+1/n$ gives the growth exponent for the number of degree-$n$ monogenic fields whose normal closures have Galois group $S_n$. The displayed formulation uses absolute discriminants, counts isomorphism classes, and allows an arbitrarily small positive addition to the upper-bound exponent. These conventions, the intended degrees, and whether a stronger upper bound is sought require source review.
