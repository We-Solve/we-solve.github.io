Math-7176

Continuous Selection of Piecewise Linear Planar Extensions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T=ABC$ denote the fixed reference triangle, interpreted in the usual sense as the closed filled nondegenerate triangle, and let $\partial T$ be its boundary. A map on either of these polyhedra is piecewise linear (PL) if it is continuous and affine on every simplex of some finite triangulation of its domain. The triangulation may depend on the map; the supplied statement imposes no fixed triangulation or bound on its size. Define \[ \mathcal B=\{f:\partial T\to{\mathbb{R}}^2: f\text{ is PL and injective}\},\qquad \mathcal E=\{F:T\to{\mathbb{R}}^2: F\text{ is PL and injective}\}. \] Restriction defines $r:\mathcal E\to\mathcal B$ by $r(F)=F|_{\partial T}$.

The question is whether there exists a continuous selection of extensions, that is, a map \[ S:\mathcal B\longrightarrow\mathcal E, \qquad r\circ S=\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal B}. \] Equivalently, for every $f\in\mathcal B$, the selected map $S(f)$ must be a PL injective map on all of $T$ satisfying $S(f)|_{\partial T}=f$, and the selection must depend continuously on $f$.

Unresolved qualification. The supplied record does not specify topologies on $\mathcal B$ and $\mathcal E$, so continuity of $S$ is not fully defined. One possible precise interpretation equips these spaces with the uniform metrics \[ d_{\partial T}(f,g)=\sup_{x\in\partial T}\|f(x)-g(x)\|_2, \qquad d_T(F,G)=\sup_{x\in T}\|F(x)-G(x)\|_2. \] Under this additional interpretation, the question asks whether such a section $S$ exists with \[ \forall f\in\mathcal B\ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall g\in\mathcal B, \quad d_{\partial T}(f,g)<\delta \ \Longrightarrow\ d_T(S(f),S(g))<\varepsilon. \] This metric formulation is conditional: the supplied evidence does not establish that these are the intended topologies.

Short English statement

Can every injective piecewise linear map of a fixed triangle's boundary into the plane be assigned an injective piecewise linear extension over the whole triangle, with the assignment continuous as the boundary map varies? The source leaves the topologies on the map spaces unspecified; the uniform-topology formulation above therefore requires confirmation.
