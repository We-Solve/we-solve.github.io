Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.

A refutation is a derivation of contradiction from an unsatisfiable constraint system using specified inference rules. Proof size counts the encoded derivation, and degree in algebraic proof systems is the largest polynomial degree appearing.

Record scope and qualifications.
The catalogue title is retained as metadata. Named certificates, contracts, bounds or frameworks in that title are not asserted as results of the original source extract. Source-record limitation: The bundle depends on undefined variants of 3-PIGEON/2-PIGEON and a random-oracle easiness criterion, together with several different proof and graph-density questions; no single complete source formulation can be imported without replacing those definitions.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Is there a total NP search class strictly beyond $\mathrm{FP}$ that is easy in the random-oracle model? Can $3$-PIGEON be randomly black-box reduced to $2$-PIGEON? Are Stabbing Planes and Cutting Planes equivalent up to quasipolynomial simulation? Is nonnegativity of binomials of graph densities decidable? Does the two-sorted theory $\mathrm{VPV}$ prove the nondeterministic time-hierarchy theorem? [S2]
