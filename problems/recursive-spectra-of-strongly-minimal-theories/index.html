Math-3419

Recursive Spectra of Strongly Minimal Theories

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the standard convention that a strongly minimal theory is a complete first-order theory $T$ with infinite models such that, in every model $M\models T$, every subset of its universe definable by a formula in one free variable, with parameters from $M$, is finite or cofinite. Let $L$ be the finite language of $T$, with an effective coding of its symbols and formulas.

A countable model $M\models T$ is recursively presentable if it is isomorphic to an $L$-structure with domain ${\mathbb{N}}$ whose atomic diagram is decidable: there is an algorithm deciding every atomic or negated atomic sentence with named elements of the domain. Define the collections of isomorphism types \[ \operatorname{Mod}_{\aleph_0}(T)=\{[M]_{\cong}:M\models T,\ |M|=\aleph_0\}, \qquad \operatorname{Rec}(T)=\{[M]_{\cong}\in\operatorname{Mod}_{\aleph_0}(T):M\text{ is recursively presentable}\}. \] Let $P_T$ denote the prime model of $T$, meaning a model admitting an elementary embedding into every model of $T$.

The source asks whether, for every strongly minimal theory $T$ in a finite language satisfying the source's qualification "Zilber's conjecture," one has \[ \operatorname{Rec}(T)\in \left\{\varnothing,\ \operatorname{Mod}_{\aleph_0}(T),\ \{[P_T]_{\cong}\}\right\}. \] Thus the third alternative means that exactly the isomorphism type of the prime model is recursively presentable. The supplied record does not state which formulation of Zilber's conjecture is intended, or precisely what it means for $T$ to satisfy it. Accordingly, this is a conditional statement template retaining an unresolved source hypothesis, rather than a fully specified assertion about a determined class of theories.

Short English statement

For a strongly minimal theory in a finite language satisfying the unspecified Zilber hypothesis, must the recursively presentable countable models comprise no isomorphism types, every countable isomorphism type, or exactly the prime model's isomorphism type? The intended Zilber hypothesis requires clarification.
