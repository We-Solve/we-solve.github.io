Math-4296

Minimal surfaces — Question 4.1

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

A codimension-one foliation $\mathcal F$ decomposes a $3$-manifold $M$ into surface leaves, locally as the horizontal slices of a product chart. In the terminology of the source, it is taut if a closed curve transverse to $\mathcal F$ meets every leaf. A minimal foliation means that every leaf is dense; a minimal surface means a surface with vanishing mean curvature for the specified Riemannian metric. These two uses of minimal are distinct.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Given a taut foliation $\mathcal F$ on $M$, describe the set of Riemannian metrics $g$ for which some ambient isotopy $h_t$ with $h_0=\operatorname{id}_M$ makes every leaf of $(h_1)_*\mathcal F$ a minimal surface in $(M,g)$. The metric is fixed while the isotopy is chosen; the question asks for the full set of such metrics, not only existence of one. [S2]

Short English statement

Characterize all metrics for which a given taut foliation can be isotoped so that every leaf is minimal.
