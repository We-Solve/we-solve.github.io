Math-183

Sarnak's Möbius Disjointness Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra progpt 6 astra ultra Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The Möbius function is $\mu(n)=0$ when a prime square divides $n$, and $\mu(n)=(-1)^r$ when $n$ is a product of $r$ distinct primes. A compact metric system consists of a continuous map $T:X\to X$; zero topological entropy means its orbit complexity has zero exponential growth rate. The assertion is \[ \frac1N\sum_{n=1}^{N}\mu(n)f(T^nx)\longrightarrow0 \qquad(f\in C(X),\ x\in X). \] Every point is quantified, not just almost every point under a chosen invariant probability measure. The test functions may be complex-valued.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Is the Möbius function asymptotically uncorrelated with every continuous observable of every zero-entropy dynamical system?
