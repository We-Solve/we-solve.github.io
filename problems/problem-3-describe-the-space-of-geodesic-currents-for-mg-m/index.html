Problem 3: — Describe the space of geodesic currents for Mg,m.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G=M_{g,m}$ be the intended mapping class group, $X=\partial\mathcal C(S)$ the boundary of its curve complex, and $Y=(X\times X)\setminus\Delta$, where $\Delta=\{(x,x):x\in X\}$. The surface convention, boundary topology and metrics, and definition of geodesic current are not supplied. A conventional candidate is a positive $G$-invariant Borel measure on $Y$, with any local-finiteness, Radon, and flip-symmetry conditions explicitly fixed. These conditions are not silently assumed, particularly since the boundary need not have the compactness properties of a classical hyperbolic-surface boundary.

Problem 3.

  1. Describe the space of geodesic currents for $M_{g,m}$.
  2. Are weighted sums of Dirac masses at pairs of fixed points of pseudo-Anosov elements dense in that space? For a pseudo-Anosov $f$, write its endpoints as $(f^-,f^+)\in Y$. The relevant atomic currents must satisfy the chosen invariance and finiteness conditions; a single Dirac mass at one ordered pair is generally not a $G$-invariant measure. The excerpt does not specify orbit sums, weights, convergence, ordered versus unordered pairs, or the topology in which density is requested.
  3. Does there exist an absolutely continuous current $\mu$ in the source's precise null-set sense: is there a $G$-invariant measure class $[\mu_0]$ on $X$ such that for every Borel $A\subseteq X$, \[ \mu_0(A)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mu\left((A\times X)\setminus\Delta\right)=0? \] An invariant measure class means that the action preserves its null sets, not necessarily a selected measure. The displayed condition compares the first marginal's null sets with $[\mu_0]$; it is not replaced by absolute continuity with respect to $\mu_0\times\mu_0$.
  4. Is there a distinguished such current whose invariant boundary measure class is determined by Hausdorff measure for one of the source's boundary distance functions $\delta$? The choice of $\delta$, Hausdorff dimension or gauge, and meaning of "distinguished" are not specified. No uniqueness or normalization is added.
All questions concern this mapping-class/curve-boundary setting, not automatically the space of currents on a hyperbolic surface studied by Bonahon.

Short English statement

Describe currents on pairs of distinct curve-boundary points, test density of pseudo-Anosov atomic currents, and seek a current whose marginal null sets match an invariant boundary measure class, possibly a distinguished Hausdorff class. The measure, topology, and metric conventions are missing.
