Basis Selection for Lagrangian Decision-Rule Approximations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A multistage stochastic mixed-integer program (MSMIP) is a stochastic optimization model with decisions made at successive stages, some decision components constrained to be integer-valued, and information restrictions governing dependence on observed randomness. The supplied record does not specify the particular MSMIP, its probability model, its constraints, or the Lagrangian relaxation under consideration.

Here LDR denotes a linear decision rule: a function represented as a linear combination of prescribed basis functions. In the Lagrangian-dual setting, these rules restrict the dependence of dual multipliers on stochastic information. Schematically, for a stage $t$, one may write \[ \lambda_t=\sum_{j=1}^{m_t}\theta_{tj}\phi_{tj}, \] where $\phi_{tj}$ are selected basis functions of the information permitted for the multiplier at that stage, $m_t$ is the number of basis functions, and $\theta_{tj}$ are deterministic coefficients of compatible dimensions. This is explanatory notation for a finite-basis restriction, not a specification of the source's model. The multiplier information, dimensions, admissibility conditions, and meaning of equality of random functions remain unspecified.

Research question. For LDR restrictions in Lagrangian duals of the multistage stochastic mixed-integer program (MSMIP), which basis functions should be used in the LDR approximations, and how can the resulting restricted Lagrangian-dual problems be optimized efficiently?

More explicitly, the requested tasks are to select basis functions for the dual decision rules and to develop efficient methods for optimizing the corresponding restricted dual objective over admissible coefficients. The record supplies neither a particular dual objective nor a quantified criterion for basis quality or computational efficiency. Consequently, it does not determine a unique optimization problem, convergence assertion, or complexity claim. These qualifications must be resolved before this research question can be replaced by a fully quantified mathematical statement.

Short English statement

Choose basis functions for linear decision-rule restrictions of Lagrangian dual multipliers in multistage stochastic mixed-integer programming, and develop efficient methods for optimizing the restricted duals. The supplied record leaves the model class, admissibility requirements, approximation criteria, and meaning of efficiency unspecified.
