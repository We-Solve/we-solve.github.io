Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.8

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

All groups under consideration are finite. A formation $\mathfrak F$ is a nonempty class of finite groups closed under homomorphic images and finite subdirect products. A subdirect product of groups $G_1,\ldots,G_n$ is a subgroup of $\prod_{i=1}^n G_i$ whose projection onto each $G_i$ is surjective. For a finite group $H$, let $\Phi(H)$ denote its Frattini subgroup, the intersection of its maximal proper subgroups, with $\Phi(1)=1$.

Let $\mathcal X$ be a class of finite simple groups. Write $O_{\mathcal X}(G)$ for the largest normal subgroup of $G$ all of whose composition factors belong to $\mathcal X$. Here composition factors are the simple factors in a composition series, considered up to isomorphism. The trivial subgroup satisfies this condition vacuously.

The source imposes the hypothesis $\pi(\mathcal X)=\operatorname{char}(\mathcal X)$ without defining either symbol. Under their conventional interpretation in this setting, these are \[ \pi(\mathcal X)=\{p\text{ prime}:p\mid |S|\text{ for some }S\in\mathcal X\},\qquad \operatorname{char}(\mathcal X)=\{p\text{ prime}:C_p\in\mathcal X\}, \] where $C_p$ is the cyclic group of order $p$. This interpretation requires confirmation against the cited source.

For a class $\mathcal X$ satisfying the stated equality, a formation $\mathfrak F$ is $\mathcal X$-saturated if \[ \text{for every finite group }G,\qquad G/\Phi\left(O_{\mathcal X}(G)\right)\in\mathfrak F \ \Longrightarrow\ G\in\mathfrak F. \] The question is whether, for every such class $\mathcal X$ and every formation $\mathfrak F$, \[ \mathfrak F\text{ is }\mathcal X\text{-saturated} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathfrak F\text{ is }\mathcal X\text{-local in the sense of F\"orster}. \] The supplied record refers to Förster's 1985 publication for the definition of $\mathcal X$-locality but does not reproduce it. Consequently, the conclusion is retained as a source-dependent predicate; a self-contained definition of that predicate remains unresolved.

Short English statement

For every class $\mathcal X$ of finite simple groups satisfying the source's condition $\pi(\mathcal X)=\operatorname{char}(\mathcal X)$, must every $\mathcal X$-saturated formation be $\mathcal X$-local in Förster's sense? The locality definition is absent from the supplied record, and the interpretation of the prime-set notation requires confirmation.
