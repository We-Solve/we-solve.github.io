Question 2.4 — For which T∈ Map+ g,n is it possible to classify factorizations of T as a product of positive Dehn twists in Mapg,n?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}$ and its positive submonoid $\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}^+$ have the source's surface and curve conventions. Typically the latter is generated by right-handed Dehn twists in admissible simple closed curves, but the excerpt does not define whether $n$ counts boundary components or marked points, or which curves are allowed. Fix $T\in\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}^+$, and let \[\mathcal F(T)=\coprod_{r\geq0}\{(t_{c_1},\ldots,t_{c_r}):t_{c_1}\cdots t_{c_r}=T,\ \text{all twists positive and admissible}\}.\] The $r=0$ convention matters only if the identity is included; the source does not state it. No fixed factorization length is imposed.

A Hurwitz move replaces adjacent factors $(a,b)$ by $(b,b^{-1}ab)$, or its inverse, preserving their product. Conjugation preserves positivity of Dehn twists. Global conjugation sends every factor $a_i$ to $h^{-1}a_i h$, changing the product to $h^{-1}Th$. Thus on the fixed-product set $\mathcal F(T)$ it must use $h\in C_{\operatorname{Map}_{g,n}}(T)$, or else the classification must explicitly include all products conjugate to $T$. The source does not state this convention.

The broad task is to identify those $T$ for which positive factorizations can be classified. Its concrete subquestions ask for which $T$ the orbit set under the specified Hurwitz and global-conjugation equivalence has cardinality one, or is finite: \[|\mathcal F(T)/\!\sim|=1,\qquad\text{or}\qquad|\mathcal F(T)/\!\sim|<\infty.\] Since Hurwitz moves and conjugation preserve length, these are all-length assertions; no insertion/deletion, stabilization or other factorization relation is added. The source does not prescribe a classification algorithm, invariant list, genus range or particular family of $T$.

Short English statement

Identify mapping classes whose positive Dehn-twist factorizations admit a classification, especially those with one or finitely many Hurwitz/global-conjugacy classes. The surface, admissible curves and fixed-product conjugation convention must be specified.
