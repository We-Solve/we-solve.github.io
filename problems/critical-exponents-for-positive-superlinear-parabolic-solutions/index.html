Math-739

Critical Exponents for Positive Superlinear Parabolic Solutions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Eternal-solution threshold question. For the superlinear exponent $p>1$ and spatial dimension $N$, does there exist a positive classical solution\[u\in C^{2,1}({\mathbb{R}}^N\times{\mathbb{R}}),\qquad u(x,t)>0,\qquad \partial_tu-\Delta_xu=u^p\quad\text{on }{\mathbb{R}}^N\times{\mathbb{R}}\] if and only if $p(N-2)\ge N+2$? Here $C^{2,1}$ means twice continuously differentiable in space and once in time, with the derivatives required by the equation continuous. The original prose identifies a superlinear problem, supplying the intended $p>1$ convention; the curated statement supplies the PDE and full real time domain.

For $N\ge3$, the proposed threshold is $p\ge(N+2)/(N-2)$, including equality. If the dimension quantifier includes $N=1,2$, the inequality never holds for $p>1$, so the stated equivalence asks for nonexistence of such solutions in those dimensions. The intended dimension range is not explicit and needs source checking. No radial symmetry, uniform boundedness, spatial decay, initial condition, or prescribed data are added to the assertion. Existence means at least one solution for the given $N,p$; the opposite direction requires excluding every positive classical solution below threshold, including nonradial ones.

Although the source calls this an ancient-time equation, ${\mathbb{R}}^N\times{\mathbb{R}}$ specifies an eternal solution, defined for every real time. An ancient solution only on ${\mathbb{R}}^N\times(-\infty,T)$, possibly singular at a finite terminal time, is a different notion. The full real time domain is retained exactly rather than silently weakened to a half-line.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the item open. It reports a theorem for a radial case but not the unrestricted if-and-only-if assertion, and says no general resolution was found in that search. The exact cited theorem and its elliptic or parabolic hypotheses are not reproduced, so no unstated reduction from all solutions to radial or stationary ones is made. This dated assessment requires independent verification.

Short English statement

The stated criterion concerns positive solutions existing for all space and all real time, with no radial or boundedness assumption. It proposes the Sobolev-type threshold including equality. Despite the word ancient, the displayed time domain is eternal; a past half-line problem would be different.
