Branko Grunbaum in Many Dimensions — Conjecture 6 (Ramos's conjecture)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $j,k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge 1}$. The source describes $\Delta(j,k)$ as the minimum dimension $d$ such that every collection of $j$ objects in ${\mathbb{R}}^d$ can be simultaneously partitioned into $2^k$ equal pieces by $k$ hyperplanes, and states Ramos's conjecture as \[ \forall j,k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge 1},\qquad \Delta(j,k)=\left\lceil\frac{(2^k-1)j}{k}\right\rceil. \] The source does not define the admissible objects, the quantity to be equalized, or the treatment of points on the hyperplanes. Consequently, its definition of $\Delta$ is incomplete.

An explicit possible mass interpretation is the following; the measure hypotheses below are additional interpretive assumptions requiring source verification. For each positive integer $d$, consider finite Borel measures $\mu$ on ${\mathbb{R}}^d$ satisfying $0<\mu({\mathbb{R}}^d)<\infty$ and $\mu(H)=0$ for every affine hyperplane $H$. Given $k$ affine hyperplanes \[ H_r=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:\langle a_r,x\rangle=b_r\},\qquad a_r\in{\mathbb{R}}^d\setminus\{0\},\quad b_r\in{\mathbb{R}},\quad 1\le r\le k, \] define, for each $\sigma\in\{-1,1\}^k$, \[ C_\sigma=\bigcap_{r=1}^k \{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d:\sigma_r(\langle a_r,x\rangle-b_r)>0\}. \] These hyperplanes simultaneously equipartition $\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_j$ if \[ \mu_i(C_\sigma)=2^{-k}\mu_i({\mathbb{R}}^d) \quad\text{for every }i\in\{1,\ldots,j\} \text{ and every }\sigma\in\{-1,1\}^k. \] The hyperplane-null hypothesis makes the omitted boundaries null for every measure. Define $\Delta_{\mathrm{mass}}(j,k)$ to be the least positive integer $d$ for which every such ordered $j$-tuple of measures admits such hyperplanes, with value $+\infty$ if no such dimension exists. Under this explicitly conditional interpretation, the proposed statement is \[ \forall j,k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1},\qquad \Delta_{\mathrm{mass}}(j,k)= \left\lceil\frac{(2^k-1)j}{k}\right\rceil. \] Identifying this conditional formulation with the source's $\Delta$ requires confirmation of the intended object class and equality convention.

Short English statement

Ramos's conjecture, as recorded here, predicts the least dimension in which $k$ hyperplanes simultaneously divide each of $j$ objects into $2^k$ equal parts. The predicted dimension is $\lceil(2^k-1)j/k\rceil$. The source leaves the objects and equality notion undefined; the measure formulation above is conditional and requires human mathematical review.
