Kourovka Notebook Problem 4.56

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian ring with identity, and let $\Lambda$ be an $R$-algebra that is finitely generated as an $R$-module. Write $\operatorname{Max}(R)$ for the set of maximal ideals of $R$, and let $\Lambda^{(n)}$ denote the direct sum of $n$ copies of $\Lambda$. For an $R$-module $M$ and $\mathfrak m\in\operatorname{Max}(R)$, put \[ M_{\mathfrak m}=(R\setminus\mathfrak m)^{-1}M. \] The source uses $\operatorname{Mod}\Lambda$ without specifying whether its objects are left or right modules; a consistent choice of side is required throughout.

Let $T$ be the full subcategory of $\operatorname{Mod}\Lambda$ consisting of modules $U$ for which there exist a finite nonnegative integer $n$, a module $P$, and an exact sequence of $\Lambda$-modules \[ 0\longrightarrow P\longrightarrow\Lambda^{(n)}\longrightarrow U\longrightarrow0 \] such that \[ P_{\mathfrak m}\cong\Lambda_{\mathfrak m}^{(n)} \qquad\text{for every }\mathfrak m\in\operatorname{Max}(R). \] Here the displayed local isomorphisms are isomorphisms of $\Lambda_{\mathfrak m}$-modules. Define $\mathbb G(T)$ to be the abelian group generated by the isomorphism classes $[U]$ of objects of $T$, subject to the relations \[ [B]=[A]+[C] \] for every short exact sequence $0\to A\to B\to C\to0$ of $\Lambda$-modules whose three terms belong to $T$.

  1. (a) Describe $\mathbb G(T)$. In particular, characterize, for $U,V\in T$, when $[U]=[V]$ in $\mathbb G(T)$.
  2. (b) The source proposes the following conjecture. Suppose \[ d=\dim\left(\operatorname{Max}(R)\right)<\infty. \] For every $U,V\in T$, every finite integer $n>d$, and every pair of epimorphisms of $\Lambda$-modules \[ \varphi:\Lambda^{(n)}\twoheadrightarrow U, \qquad \psi:\Lambda^{(n)}\twoheadrightarrow V, \] is it true that \[ [U]=[V]\text{ in }\mathbb G(T) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ker\varphi=\ker\psi? \]
The conclusion in (b) reproduces the source's literal equality of submodules of the common domain $\Lambda^{(n)}$. The supplied record does not clarify whether equality, abstract isomorphism, or equivalence under an automorphism of the domain was intended; these conclusions must not be identified. The source also does not specify the dimension convention in $\dim(\operatorname{Max}(R))$, which is retained here without replacing it by $\dim R$. The epimorphisms in (b) are quantified as written; the source does not explicitly require them to be the epimorphisms in the presentations defining $T$.

Short English statement

For a module-finite algebra over a commutative Noetherian ring, consider modules admitting a finite free presentation whose kernel becomes free of the same rank at every maximal ideal. Describe their Grothendieck group and equality of its classes. The source further conjectures that equal classes force literal equality of kernels of any two epimorphisms from the same free module of rank greater than the stated dimension of the maximal spectrum. The intended kernel comparison, dimension convention, and module handedness require clarification.
