Quadratic Nil Algebras and Algebraic Division Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An associative algebra is nil if every element is nilpotent; in this question the generated associative algebra must be understood without adjoining an identity. A quadratic algebra has a presentation by generators of degree one and homogeneous relations of degree two, equivalently a quotient of the positive-degree tensor algebra by an ideal generated in degree two. The first question asks whether such a nil algebra can have infinite dimension over its ground field. The source does not specify the ground field, its quantifier, or whether the space of generators is required to be finite dimensional.

For the two concrete questions let $F$ have characteristic $p>0$, and set \[ R=F[t_0,t_1,\ldots]/(t_i^p:i\geq0),\qquad \partial_i=\frac{\partial}{\partial t_i}. \] The derivations descend to $R$ in characteristic $p$. With empty products equal to $1$, define \[ V_1=\sum_{i\geq1}\left(\prod_{j=0}^{i-2}t_j^{p-1}\right)\partial_i, \qquad V_2=\sum_{i\geq2}\left(\prod_{j=1}^{i-2}t_j^{p-1}\right)\partial_i. \] These sums act locally finitely on every polynomial of $R$. Let $A\subseteq\operatorname{End}_F(R)$ be the $F$-linear span of all nonempty words in $V_1,V_2$, with multiplication given by composition. The second question is \[ \forall a\in A\quad\exists n\geq1:\quad a^n=0\;? \] Let $L\subseteq\operatorname{Der}_F(R)$ be the Lie algebra generated by $V_1,V_2$, with commutator bracket, let $U=U(L)$ be its ordinary universal enveloping algebra, and let $Z=Z(U)$. The third question asks whether \[ D=(Z\setminus\{0\})^{-1}U \] is an algebraic division algebra. Here the assertion that $D$ is a division algebra means that every nonzero element is invertible. A natural possible meaning of algebraicity is that every $d\in D$ satisfies a nonzero polynomial over $K=\operatorname{Frac}(Z)$; algebraicity over $F$ is a different requirement. The supplied formulation does not identify the base field for algebraicity, so this distinction remains unresolved. The first existence question and the two questions about this construction are retained separately; no quadratic presentation of $A$ is supplied.

Short English statement

Ask whether an infinite-dimensional quadratic nil algebra exists, whether the specified two derivations generate a nil associative algebra, and whether the central localization of their Lie algebra's enveloping algebra is an algebraic division algebra. The algebraicity base field and some conventions for the first question require clarification.
