Math-6681

Answered Drury–Arveson Kernel-Norm Question

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer and let $\mathbb B_n=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}^n:\|z\|_2<1\}$. The Drury--Arveson space $H_n^2$ is the reproducing-kernel Hilbert space of holomorphic functions on $\mathbb B_n$ with kernel \[ K_z(w)=\frac{1}{1-\langle w,z\rangle},\qquad \langle w,z\rangle=\sum_{j=1}^n w_j\overline{z_j}. \] A holomorphic function $f$ is a multiplier of $H_n^2$ if multiplication defines a bounded operator $M_f:H_n^2\to H_n^2$. Equivalently, there exists $C<\infty$ such that, for every $h\in H_n^2$, one has $fh\in H_n^2$ and $\|fh\|_{H_n^2}\leq C\|h\|_{H_n^2}$.

The source retains the question whether, for every $f\in H_n^2$, \[ \sup_{z\in\mathbb B_n}\|f k_z\|_{H_n^2}<\infty \quad\Longrightarrow\quad f\in\operatorname{Mult}(H_n^2). \] Here finiteness includes the requirement that $f k_z\in H_n^2$ for every $z\in\mathbb B_n$. The supplied record does not define $k_z$, specify its normalization, or specify the intended dimension or range of dimensions. In particular, the unnormalized kernel $K_z$ and the normalized kernel \[ \widehat K_z(w)=\frac{K_z(w)}{\sqrt{K_z(z)}}=\frac{\sqrt{1-\|z\|_2^2}}{1-\langle w,z\rangle} \] are distinct conventions. If $k_z=\widehat K_z$ is intended, the displayed hypothesis becomes \[ \forall z\in\mathbb B_n:\ f\widehat K_z\in H_n^2, \qquad \sup_{z\in\mathbb B_n}\|f\widehat K_z\|_{H_n^2}<\infty. \] This is a conditional interpretation, not a verified recovery of the source notation.

Source qualification. The supplied statement-verification field says that the official report immediately answers the retained question negatively by exhibiting a function satisfying the displayed bound that is not a multiplier. The counterexample and its dimensional and normalization hypotheses are not supplied. The record therefore labels the statement unrecoverable and promotes no separate clean statement. Its supplied status is partially_solved. Separately, the supplied dated literature assessment concerns a simpler direct criterion for special derivative measures: it says that a full Carleson-measure characterization gives a solution in principle, that the requested simpler criterion has not been obtained, and that natural kernel tests are insufficient. No derivative measures or precise criterion are defined in this record, so that discussion does not determine an additional mathematical statement.

Short English statement

The retained question asks whether uniform bounds on products of a Drury--Arveson function with kernel functions force bounded multiplication on the whole space. The supplied verification says the report answers this negatively, but the kernel normalization, relevant dimensions, and counterexample are missing. The separate derivative-measure discussion has no supplied precise formulation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
