Math-1270

A semisimple-unipotent reduction for the torsion field of J(X(ell))

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Original question (C. Hall). Let $X(\ell)$ be a modular curve. What can be said about the splitting field of the $\ell$-torsion of $J(X(\ell))$? Hall's comment says this information is needed to discuss the $L$-function of $E\to X(\ell)$ modulo $\ell$, as a polynomial in ${\mathbb{F}}_\ell[T]$. The family $E$, ground field, level convention and desired information about the field are not specified. The OCR backticks are restored as $\ell$; the supplied verification identifies this as item 14, although the extraction and source-item metadata say 4.

Attached unreviewed principal-level interpretation. Let $\ell$ be prime, $K_\ell={\mathbb{Q}}(\zeta_\ell)$, and let $X(\ell)$ be the standard geometrically connected principal-level modular curve over this cyclotomic field, with a fixed Weil-pairing component. Put $J_\ell=J(X(\ell))$, $g=\dim J_\ell$, and \[\rho_\ell:G_{K_\ell}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathbb{F}}_\ell}(J_\ell[\ell]),\qquad L_\ell=\overline K_\ell^{\ker\rho_\ell}=K_\ell(J_\ell[\ell]).\] The aid reports that $L_\ell/K_\ell$ is unramified outside primes above $\ell$ and that the polarization, with $\mu_\ell\subset K_\ell$, places its image in $\mathrm{Sp}_{2g}({\mathbb{F}}_\ell)$.

Let $\rho_\ell^{\mathrm{ss}}$ be its semisimplification and $L_\ell^{\mathrm{ss}}$ the field fixed by its kernel. The kernel and field are independent of the choice of a model for the semisimplification. The reported canonical tower and bounds are \[K_\ell\subseteq L_\ell^{\mathrm{ss}}\subseteq L_\ell,\qquad\operatorname{Gal}(L_\ell/L_\ell^{\mathrm{ss}})\text{ an }\ell\text{-group},\qquad [L_\ell:L_\ell^{\mathrm{ss}}]\le\ell^{g^2},\] \[[L_\ell:K_\ell]\mid |\mathrm{Sp}_{2g}({\mathbb{F}}_\ell)|=\ell^{g^2}\prod_{i=1}^g(\ell^{2i}-1).\] The $\ell^{g^2}$ bound is a Sylow bound, not a claim that it is attained by this particular torsion image.

The aid asserts the following equivalent conditions: $L_\ell/K_\ell$ is an $\ell$-extension; $J_\ell[\ell]^{\mathrm{ss}}$ is a direct sum of trivial representations; and every good local Frobenius polynomial away from $\ell$ satisfies \[P_v(T)\equiv(1-T)^{2g}\pmod\ell.\] It also says a density-one set of such good places suffices, with density interpreted for primes of $K_\ell$. For $\ell=2,3,5$ it reports $g=0$, hence $L_\ell=K_\ell$.

The reduction separates semisimple data from the upper extension-class layer: local characteristic polynomials alone do not recover that layer. It does not determine the full torsion image or splitting field in general, nor identify the unspecified mod-$\ell$ $L$-function in Hall's motivation. All ramification, group-image and criterion claims are retained as research-aid assertions requiring review.

Short English statement

Hall asks about the torsion splitting field of a modular-curve Jacobian. The aid chooses principal level over the cyclotomic field, gives a semisimple tower and symplectic degree bounds, and characterizes an $\ell$-extension through Frobenius polynomials. These tests do not recover the extension-class layer or the full field.
