Math-1322

Generically nonreduced components of Hilbert schemes of points

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $k$, $\operatorname{Hilb}^d({\mathbb{A}}_k^n)$ parametrizes zero-dimensional subschemes of length $d$. "A nonreduced component" is read as generic nonreducedness of the Hilbert scheme along an irreducible component, not nonreducedness of its support given the reduced scheme structure.

Problem 5. Do there exist $d,n$ for which $\operatorname{Hilb}^d({\mathbb{A}}^n)$ has a nonreduced component? If so, exhibit one. The source does not fix the field or characteristic.

Supplied characteristic-zero answer. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, the aid attributes to Jelisiejew's 2024 theorem the following examples. For each $s\in\{6,7,8,9\}$, the very-compressed locus of local algebras with Hilbert function \[ (\dim A/\mathfrak m,\dim\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2, \dim\mathfrak m^2/\mathfrak m^3,\dim\mathfrak m^3/\mathfrak m^4) =(1,4,10,s),\qquad\mathfrak m^4=0, \] has closure equal to the reduced support of a generically nonreduced irreducible component of $\operatorname{Hilb}^{15+s}({\mathbb{A}}^4)$. Here the length is $1+4+10+s=15+s$. The exact theorem's definition of the very-compressed locus and its dense cubic-generated open part must be checked against the underlying reference.

On the dense cubic-generated parts the report gives the lower bounds \[ \begin{array}{c|rrrr} d&21&22&23&24\\\hline \dim T_{[Z]}\operatorname{Hilb}^d({\mathbb{A}}^4)-\dim C_{\mathrm{red}} &\geq10&\geq7&\geq12&\geq25 \end{array} \] for the corresponding component $C$, at the general points specified by the report. A tangent-excess test applies on a dense smooth locus of the reduced support away from other components: excess there detects generic nonreducedness. Excess at a single arbitrary singular or intersection point would not suffice.

Reported propagation. If $Z$ is one of the finite subschemes and $p_1,\ldots,p_m$ are distinct reduced points outside its support, then the aid proves the completed-local-ring statement \[ \widehat{\mathcal O}_{\operatorname{Hilb}^{d+m}({\mathbb{A}}^4),[Z\sqcup\{p_1,\ldots,p_m\}]} \simeq \widehat{\mathcal O}_{\operatorname{Hilb}^{d}({\mathbb{A}}^4),[Z]} [[t_1,\ldots,t_{4m}]]. \] This noncanonical smooth factor adds $4m$ to both tangent and reduced-component dimensions. Hence the stated lower-bound excess and generic nilpotents persist on the associated component. Starting with length $21$ supplies a generically nonreduced component of $\operatorname{Hilb}^d({\mathbb{A}}^4)$ for every $d\geq21$.

The aid therefore reports a solved existence problem in characteristic zero. It does not claim minimality of length $21$, classify all such components, or give the same statement in positive characteristic.

Short English statement

The source asks for a genuinely generically nonreduced component of a Hilbert scheme of points. The aid cites characteristic-zero examples in affine four-space of lengths $21$ through $24$, with explicit tangent-excess lower bounds. Adding disjoint reduced points preserves the completed local structure up to smooth variables and yields every larger length.
