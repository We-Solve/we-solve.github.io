Math-5053

Bounded Conjugation-Invariant Metrics on Higher-Rank Lattices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $H$ be a higher-rank simple Lie group with finite center, as specified in the supplied statement, and let $G\leq H$ be a lattice: a discrete subgroup such that $H/G$ has finite $H$-invariant volume. Let $S\subseteq G$ be a finite generating set. To make the word metric symmetric, put \[ T_S=\bigcup_{h\in G}h(S\cup S^{-1})h^{-1}. \] For $g\in G$, define \[ \ell_S(g)=\min\left\{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:g=t_1\cdots t_n,\quad t_i\in T_S\right\}, \] where the empty product is the identity, and define \[ d_S(x,y)=\ell_S(x^{-1}y)\qquad(x,y\in G). \] This is the conjugation-invariant word metric associated with $S$; adjoining inverses makes explicit the usual symmetric word-metric convention.

The question is whether, for every such $H$, every lattice $G\leq H$, and every finite generating set $S$ of $G$, there exists $N=N(G,S)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ such that \[ \forall x,y\in G,\qquad d_S(x,y)\leq N. \] Equivalently, must every element of $G$ be a product of at most $N$ conjugates in $G$ of elements of $S\cup S^{-1}$? No bound uniform over lattices or generating sets is requested. Boundedness is independent of the choice of finite generating set, although the bound may depend on that choice.

Short English statement

For a lattice in a higher-rank simple Lie group with finite center, must there be a finite bound on the number of conjugates of generators and their inverses needed to express any group element? The bound may depend on the lattice and generating set. The ambient rank and connectedness conventions remain to be confirmed. The supplied record labels the question open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
