Variational Eddy Currents with Degenerate Conductivity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ \mathcal S=\{\sigma\in L^\infty({\mathbb{R}}^3;{\mathbb{R}}):\sigma(x)\geq 0\text{ for almost every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}^3\}. \] Here $L^\infty$ denotes the space of essentially bounded measurable functions, identified up to equality almost everywhere. Conductivities in $\mathcal S$ may vanish; no strictly positive lower bound is assumed.

Source question. For every $\sigma\in\mathcal S$, can the mapping \[ A\longmapsto\phi_A \] be defined in the variational formulation of the eddy-current equation?

Consequently, the question above preserves the supplied target, but a precise existence or well-definedness statement requires this missing context from the cited report. Short English statement

The question asks whether the assignment $A\mapsto\phi_A$ in an eddy-current variational formulation extends to every essentially bounded, nonnegative conductivity on ${\mathbb{R}}^3$, including conductivities that vanish. The supplied record omits the formulation and spaces needed to specify this assignment precisely.
