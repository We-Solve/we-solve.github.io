Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 1

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prescribed prime number. A central division algebra over a field $F$ is a finite-dimensional associative unital $F$-algebra $D$ whose center is $F$ and in which every nonzero element is invertible. Its degree is $\deg(D)=\sqrt{\dim_F D}$. Such an algebra of degree $p$ is called cyclic if it contains a maximal subfield $L$ such that $L/F$ is Galois and $\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$.

The supplied construction problem asks: given $p$, construct a field $F$ and a central division algebra $D/F$ satisfying \[ \dim_F D=p^2, \qquad \nexists\, L\subseteq D\text{ a subfield containing }F \text{ with }[L:F]=p,\ L/F\text{ Galois, and } \operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}. \] The field may depend on $p$; the supplied record imposes no characteristic restriction on $F$. This is an existence and construction target, not an assertion that such algebras exist. Reading "given a prime" as an arbitrary prescribed prime gives a universal quantifier over primes. The source record supplies no exclusions or admissible range for $p$, so the intended scope and feasibility of that unrestricted reading require mathematical review.

Short English statement

For a given prime $p$, find a field and a central division algebra of degree $p$ having no cyclic Galois maximal subfield. The supplied formulation does not restrict the prime, and that scope requires review.
