Math-7286

Admissible Gelfand–Zetlin Faces Representing Smooth Schubert Cycles

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G=GL_n({\mathbb{C}})$, let $B\subseteq G$ be a Borel subgroup, and let $X=G/B$ be the complete flag variety. For $w\in S_n$, choose a permutation-matrix representative $\dot w$, and write \[ C_w=B\dot wB/B,\qquad Y_w=\overline{C_w}\subseteq X. \] Thus $C_w$ is a Schubert cell, $Y_w$ is its Schubert variety, and $[Y_w]$ denotes its fundamental cycle. The smoothness hypothesis concerns the entire closure $Y_w$.

For clarity of notation, the usual Gelfand--Zetlin array convention for a real tuple $\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_n$ describes $Q_\lambda$ by coordinates $x_{i,j}$, with $1\leq j\leq i\leq n-1$, fixed top row $x_{n,j}=\lambda_j$, and inequalities \[ x_{i+1,j}\geq x_{i,j}\geq x_{i+1,j+1} \qquad(1\leq j\leq i\leq n-1). \] This convention explains the polytope notation; it does not resolve the source's missing hypotheses on $\lambda$, including whether strict inequalities or integrality are required.

Source question, with unresolved terminology retained. For the intended parameters $n$ and $\lambda$, does every Schubert cell whose closure is smooth have its Schubert cycle represented by an admissible face of $Q_\lambda$? Schematically, the requested assertion is \[ \forall w\in S_n,\qquad Y_w\text{ smooth} \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\,\Gamma\text{ a face of }Q_\lambda \text{ such that }\Gamma\text{ is admissible and represents }[Y_w]. \] Here "admissible" and "represents" retain their intended source-specific meanings: the supplied record does not define admissibility or supply a map from faces to Schubert cycles. Consequently, this is a formulation schema requiring those definitions before it becomes a fully specified mathematical conjecture. The phrase that face combinatorics should "capture the cycle's geometry" supplies no additional precise condition. The record also does not specify the allowed parameters or whether the assertion must hold for every permitted $\lambda$.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every Schubert variety with smooth closure in the complete flag variety for $GL_n({\mathbb{C}})$ has its fundamental cycle represented by an admissible face of a Gelfand--Zetlin polytope. The source leaves admissibility, the face-to-cycle correspondence, and the hypotheses and quantifiers on $\lambda$ unspecified.
