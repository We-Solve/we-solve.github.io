Math-4079

Large-Genus Hyperbolic Surfaces Above the One-Quarter Spectral Threshold

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed hyperbolic surface is a compact, connected, smooth surface without boundary, equipped with a Riemannian metric of constant Gaussian curvature $-1$. Under the usual interpretation of the source notation $\mathcal M_g$, restrict to orientable surfaces of genus $g\geq 2$, and let $\mathcal M_g$ denote their isometry classes. Orientability is implicit in this notation rather than explicit in the supplied corrected statement; this interpretation requires review against the report.

Use the nonnegative Laplace--Beltrami operator $\Delta_X=-\operatorname{div}_X\nabla_X$. Write its eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, as \[ 0=\lambda_0(X)<\lambda_1(X)\leq\lambda_2(X)\leq\cdots. \] Thus $\lambda_1(X)$ is the first positive Laplace eigenvalue.

The supplied question is whether \[ \forall G\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ G\geq 2,\quad \exists g\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ g\geq G,\quad \exists X\in\mathcal M_g \quad\text{such that}\quad \lambda_1(X)>\frac14. \] The inequality is strict for each selected surface. No positive lower bound on $\lambda_1(X)-\tfrac14$ independent of genus is requested. The source calls the threshold asymptotically optimal, but supplies no precise formulation of that qualification; it is retained as source commentary, not an additional asserted theorem. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.

Short English statement

Can one find closed orientable hyperbolic surfaces with unbounded genus whose first positive Laplace eigenvalue exceeds $1/4$? The curvature normalization is $-1$, and the excess over $1/4$ may depend on the surface. Orientability follows the usual reading of the source notation $\mathcal M_g$ and remains subject to source review.
