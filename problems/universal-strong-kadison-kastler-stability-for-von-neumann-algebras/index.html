Math-10230

Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability for von Neumann algebras

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every $\varepsilon >0$, is there $\delta >0$ such that any two unital von Neumann algebras on the same complex Hilbert space, with the same identity and two-sided unit-ball Hausdorff distance below $\delta $, are conjugate by a unitary u satisfying $||u-1||<\varepsilon $? The bound must be independent of both algebras and of the Hilbert space.

Short English statement

For every $\varepsilon >0$, is there $\delta >0$ such that any two unital von Neumann algebras on the same complex Hilbert space, with the same identity and two-sided unit-ball Hausdorff distance below $\delta $, are conjugate by a unitary u satisfying $||u-1||<\varepsilon $? The bound must be independent of both algebras and of the Hilbert space.
