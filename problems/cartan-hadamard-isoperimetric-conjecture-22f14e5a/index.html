Cartan–Hadamard Isoperimetric Conjecture

OpenAI's claim

Model isoperimetry and equality rigidity: The manuscript claims sharp comparison with the constant-curvature model ball for every finite-volume finite-perimeter set in a simply connected complete manifold whose sectional curvatures do not exceed $\kappa\le0$; in the Euclidean comparison, bounded equality sets are precisely regions isometric to round Euclidean balls, up to null sets.

Sharp fillings in CAT$0$: The paper claims the Euclidean-optimal integral filling inequality for compactly supported integer cycles of dimension at least two in proper CAT$0$ spaces; arbitrary integer multiplicities and ambient dimensions are permitted.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $n\ge2$, $\kappa\le0$, and assume $(M^n,g)$ is a smooth Riemannian manifold, simply connected and complete, satisfying $\sec_g\le\kappa$. For a measurable set of finite volume and finite ambient perimeter, the claim is \[P_g(E)\ge\mathcal I_{\sqrt{-\kappa}}(\operatorname{vol}_g E).\] Here $s_b(r)=r$ for $b=0$ and $s_b(r)=\sinh(br)/b$ otherwise, $A_b(r)=n\omega_ns_b(r)^{n-1}$, $V_b(r)=n\omega_n\int_0^rs_b(t)^{n-1}\,dt$, and $\mathcal I_b=A_b\circ V_b^{-1}$; $\omega_n$ is unit-ball volume. When $\kappa=0$, equality $P_g(E)=n\omega_n^{1/n}\operatorname{vol}(E)^{(n-1)/n}$ for bounded positive-volume $E$ holds exactly when, up to a volume-zero set, $E$ is an open region isometric to a round Euclidean ball.
Mathematical claim 2.
For every proper CAT$(0)$ metric space $X$ and integer $n\ge2$, each compactly supported integral $n$-current $T$ with $\partial T=0$ is claimed to bound a compactly supported integral $(n+1)$-current $S$ with \[\partial S=T,\qquad\mathbf M(S)\le\frac{\mathbf M(T)^{(n+1)/n}}{(n+1)^{(n+1)/n}\omega_{n+1}^{1/n}}.\] No ambient-dimension or multiplicity restriction is imposed, and the constant is claimed optimal. Its manifold corollary gives $\operatorname{area}(\partial\Omega)\ge d\omega_d^{1/d}\operatorname{vol}(\Omega)^{(d-1)/d}$ for smooth bounded domains in a smooth Cartan--Hadamard $d$-manifold, $d\ge3$. For relatively compact measurable $E$ of finite perimeter the assertion instead uses the ambient variation $P_g(E)=|D\mathbf1_E|_g(M)$: \[P_g(E)\ge d\omega_d^{1/d}\operatorname{vol}_g(E)^{(d-1)/d}.\] For smooth $\Omega$, $P_g(\Omega)=\operatorname{area}_g(\partial\Omega)$.

Outline of the argument

Model isoperimetry and equality rigidity: A sharp critical Sobolev inequality is established on immersed hypersurfaces using radial sphere maps and positive weights compatible with Jacobi comparison. This controls the boundary geometry needed for the isoperimetric estimate. Approximation extends the bound to finite-perimeter sets, and the equality analysis forces the geometric comparison to be exact, identifying a Euclidean ball region.

Sharp fillings in CAT$0$: Induction reduces a failing inequality to a compact CAT$0$ space and an extremizing cycle of small normalized mass. Inward geodesic variations estimate its curvature-like behavior in current charts. The resulting sharp mass comparison contradicts extremality, and Euclidean examples show that the coefficient cannot be improved.
