Torsion Growth and Hyperbolic Volume in Finite Covers

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns an irreducible orientable $3$-manifold $X$ with empty boundary or boundary consisting of tori. Here irreducibility means that every embedded $2$-sphere bounds a $3$-ball. The source does not explicitly impose compactness or connectedness; the constructions below require clarification of these hypotheses.

For connected $X$, write $\pi=\pi_1(X,x_0)$, where $x_0$ is a basepoint, and let \[ \mathcal{N}(\pi)=\{G\triangleleft\pi:[\pi:G]<\infty\}. \] For $G\in\mathcal{N}(\pi)$, let $X_G\to X$ be the corresponding connected finite covering, of degree $[\pi:G]$. For an abelian group $A$, define \[ \operatorname{Tor}(A)=\{a\in A:na=0\text{ for some integer }n\geq1\}. \] Provided this torsion subgroup is finite, set \[ t(X,G)=\left|\operatorname{Tor}\left(H_1(X_G;{\mathbb{Z}})\right)\right|. \] The source defines $\operatorname{Vol}(X)$ as the sum of the volumes of the hyperbolic pieces of $X$. In the usual compact connected setting, these are the hyperbolic pieces of its JSJ decomposition, with the empty sum equal to zero. The source does not specify hypotheses ensuring that this decomposition and volume sum are available in its full stated domain.

With those qualifications, the supplied question asks whether, for every such $X$, \[ \limsup_{G\to\infty} t(X,G)^{1/[\pi:G]} =\exp\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(X)}{6\pi}\right). \] Here $\pi$ in the denominator $6\pi$ denotes the usual numerical constant. The notation $G\to\infty$ is retained from the source and is not defined there. In particular, the source does not specify a directed family of subgroups, an index-growth condition, or a quantifier over towers. Thus this display records the proposed equality, but a fully quantified conjecture requires an explicit limiting convention and clarification of the manifold hypotheses. The accompanying literature assessment discusses hyperbolic $3$-manifolds, whereas the supplied statement allows manifolds with several geometric pieces; this discrepancy is unresolved.

Short English statement

For finite normal covers of an irreducible orientable $3$-manifold with empty or torus boundary, the question compares the exponential growth of torsion in first integral homology with the total volume of its hyperbolic pieces. The intended limiting family of covers, compactness and connectedness assumptions, and the scope beyond hyperbolic manifolds require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
