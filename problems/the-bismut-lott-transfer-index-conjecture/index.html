Math-3394

The Bismut–Lott Transfer Index Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R$ be a number ring, and let $\pi\colon W\to B$ be a proper smooth submersion equipped with a Riemannian structure $g$. Let $V$ be a local system on $W$ of finitely generated projective $R$-modules, and let $h^V$ denote its metric data. These data are understood in the Bismut--Lott setting referenced by the supplied record.

Write $\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{top}}(V,h^V)$ for the differential Becker--Gottlieb transfer index, and write $\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{an}}(V,h^V)$ for the analytic index corrected by the Bismut--Lott torsion form. Define their difference, in the common differential index group of the referenced construction, by \[ \delta(V,h^V;\pi,g) :=\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{top}}(V,h^V) -\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{an}}(V,h^V). \] The transfer index conjecture asks whether, for every such choice of data for which these constructions are defined, \[ \delta(V,h^V;\pi,g)=0, \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad \operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{top}}(V,h^V) =\operatorname{index}^{\mathrm{an}}(V,h^V). \] The requested equality is integral equality of the index classes, retaining any torsion information, rather than merely equality after passing to real invariants.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The supplied record does not identify the precise differential index group, its degree or normalization, or the definitions of the two index constructions. It also does not specify the convention for a number ring, the components of the Riemannian structure, the metric requirements, or boundary and other admissibility hypotheses on $W$, $B$, and the fibers. Consequently, the universal quantifier above is restricted to the intended source setting; it does not assert an extension to arbitrary boundary conditions or additional geometric settings. A fully intrinsic formulation requires these missing specifications from the referenced construction.

Short English statement

For every admissible proper submersion and metrized projective local system in the referenced Bismut--Lott setting, do the differential topological transfer index and the torsion-corrected analytic index agree integrally? The supplied record leaves some construction details and hypotheses unspecified, so this is a conditional statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
