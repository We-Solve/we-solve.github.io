Yau–Tian–Donaldson Conjecture for Polarized Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A smooth polarized complex projective variety is a pair $(X,L)$, where $X$ is a smooth complex projective variety and $L$ is an ample holomorphic line bundle. Regard $c_1(L)$ as a real cohomology class in $H^2(X,{\mathbb{R}})$. A Kähler form $\omega$ represents this class if $[\omega]=c_1(L)$; it defines a constant-scalar-curvature Kähler metric if its scalar curvature $S_\omega$ is constant on $X$.

In the test-configuration formulation, K-stability is expressed through positivity of the Donaldson--Futaki invariant for every admissible nontrivial test configuration of $(X,L)$. Such a configuration is an equivariant flat projective degeneration over ${\mathbb{A}}^1_{{\mathbb{C}}}$, equipped with a relatively ample polarization whose nonzero fibers are isomorphic to $(X,L^{\otimes r})$ for some positive integer $r$. The supplied record does not specify the admissible class of configurations, the treatment of trivial or product configurations, or the precise convention for K-stability; these choices require confirmation from the source.

Supplied Yau--Tian--Donaldson conjecture. For every smooth polarized complex projective variety $(X,L)$ satisfying the source's finite-automorphism-group hypothesis, is the following equivalence true? \[ \left(\exists\,\omega\text{ K\"ahler on }X:\ [\omega]=c_1(L)\ \text{and}\ S_\omega\text{ is constant}\right) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (X,L)\text{ is K-stable}. \] The finite-automorphism-group hypothesis is retained as supplied: the record does not explicitly distinguish the automorphism group of $X$ from the group of automorphisms preserving the polarization. Consequently, the displayed equivalence records the supplied target but still requires these conventions to become a fully specified assertion. The supplied dated literature assessment also questions whether the extraction identifies an unresolved standalone claim, despite the separately supplied corrected statement and verification label.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether, for a smooth polarized complex projective variety with finite automorphism group, the polarization class contains a Kähler metric of constant scalar curvature exactly when the polarized variety is K-stable. The intended automorphism group and K-stability conventions require clarification. Its supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
