$q$-Whittaker Eigenfunctions of Lattice Quantum Toda Operators

Let $G$ be a semisimple simply connected group. The supplied record does not specify its base field. Let $W$ denote its Weyl group. Write $\Lambda$ for the weight lattice intended by the source and $\Lambda_+$ for its dominant cone. Source-level conjecture schema. For every semisimple simply connected group $G$, with the intended source conventions fixed, there exists exactly one family $\Psi\in\mathcal P^{\Lambda}$ such that \[ \begin{aligned} \Psi_{\check\lambda}(q,z)&=0 &&\text{for every }\check\lambda\in\Lambda\setminus\Lambda_+,\\ \Psi_0(q,z)&=1,\\ M_f^{\mathrm{lat}}\left(\Psi(q,z)\right)&=f(z)\Psi(q,z) &&\text{for every }f\in\mathcal A^W. \end{aligned} \] The last equality is an equality of lattice-indexed families, with multiplication by $f(z)$ on each component. The source does not specify the domain of $z$, the coefficient ring, whether $q$ is formal or specialized, or any restrictions on its values. Consequently, this schema preserves the supplied existence and uniqueness claim but is not a fully specified mathematical conjecture until those data and the operators are recovered. No replacement by a modified or related Toda formulation is intended.

Short English statement

For each semisimple simply connected group, the proposed family of $q$-Whittaker polynomials should be uniquely determined by vanishing at nondominant indices, normalization at zero, and simultaneous lattice quantum Toda eigenvalue equations for every Weyl-invariant function in the intended algebra. The supplied record leaves the lattice, function spaces, parameter domains, and operators insufficiently defined, so the formulation requires source verification and human mathematical review.
