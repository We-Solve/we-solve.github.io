Integral Shimura Models with Universal Shtukas

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record describes a conjecture about integral models of Shimura varieties with parahoric level structure. A parahoric group scheme $\mathcal G$ alone does not specify the associated Shimura varieties, their reflex field, or the integral base. Consequently, the following is a schematic statement of the recorded requirements; the missing data and qualifications must be supplied before it becomes a fully specified conjecture.

Write $B$ for the intended ring of reflex integers, $\eta$ for its generic point, $X_K$ for the intended Shimura variety at level $K$, and $\mathcal P_{\eta,K}$ for its asserted generic-fiber $\mathcal G$-shtuka. These symbols denote data presupposed by the source, not data determined by the supplied group scheme. An integral model here means a model $\mathcal S_K$ over $B$, together with an identification \[ \mathcal S_K\times_B\eta\simeq X_K. \] Normality means that its local rings are integrally closed domains; flatness is required for its structural morphism to $B$.

The recorded conjectural construction asks for a compatible inverse system $(\mathcal S_K)_K$ of normal, flat integral models satisfying the following conditions:

  1. The transition morphisms for the intended changes of level are finite and $'{e}$tale and extend the corresponding morphisms between the generic fibers.
  2. The models satisfy the valuative extension property intended in the source. The supplied record does not specify the test valuation rings, the maps to be extended, the relevant level of the inverse system, or whether uniqueness is part of this property.
  3. For each intended level $K$, the generic-fiber shtuka $\mathcal P_{\eta,K}$ extends to a $\mathcal G$-shtuka $\mathcal P_K$ over $\mathcal S_K$, compatibly with the system.
  4. Every formal completion specified by the intended local uniformization assertion is identified with the corresponding integral local Shimura variety, and the extended shtuka is identified with the tautological shtuka under that identification. The record does not specify the completion loci, local Shimura data, base changes, framing choices, or precise compatibility morphisms.

The permitted levels, the integral base, the definition of a $\mathcal G$-shtuka in use, and the precise valuative and formal-local assertions remain unresolved. In particular, the phrase "every formal completion" cannot be given a precise quantifier from this record alone. The requirements above preserve the supplied target without asserting a more specific extension theorem or uniformization theorem.

Short English statement

Construct compatible normal, flat integral models of the intended Shimura varieties with finite $'{e}$tale transition maps, an extension property, extended universal shtukas, and compatible descriptions of their formal completions by integral local Shimura varieties. The supplied record omits data needed to state these requirements precisely. Its partially solved label and verification claims are supplied curation, not independent mathematical verification.
