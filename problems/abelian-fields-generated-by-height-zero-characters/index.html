Math-3765

Abelian Fields Generated by Height-Zero Characters

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $F\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ be an abelian number field, meaning a finite Galois extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with abelian Galois group. For each positive integer $r$, put ${\mathbb{Q}}_r={\mathbb{Q}}(\zeta_r)$, where $\zeta_r=e^{2\pi i/r}$. The conductor of $F$ is the least positive integer $n$ such that $F\subseteq{\mathbb{Q}}_n$. Write \[ n=p^a m,\qquad a\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\quad m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1},\quad \gcd(m,p)=1. \] Let $F{\mathbb{Q}}_m=\langle F,{\mathbb{Q}}_m\rangle$ denote the compositum inside ${\mathbb{Q}}_n$.

For a finite group $G$, let $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ denote its ordinary irreducible complex characters. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, define its field of values by \[ {\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(g):g\in G\right). \] Let $B$ be a $p$-block of $G$, and let $D$ be a defect group of $B$. If $|G|_p=p^u$ and $|D|=p^d$, where $t_p$ denotes the largest power of $p$ dividing a positive integer $t$, the height $h_B(\chi)$ of $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B)$ is determined by \[ \chi(1)_p=p^{u-d+h_B(\chi)}. \] Thus height zero means $\chi(1)_p=p^{u-d}$.

Conjectural statement (supplied as Conjecture B). For every prime $p$ and every abelian number field $F$ with conductor decomposed as above, \[ \begin{aligned} &\exists\,\text{a finite group }G,\ \exists\,\text{a }p\text{-block }B\text{ of }G,\ \exists\,\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(B):\\[-2pt] &\hspace{30mm} h_B(\chi)=0\quad\text{and}\quad{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)=F \end{aligned} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \gcd\!\left([{\mathbb{Q}}_n:F{\mathbb{Q}}_m],p\right)=1. \] The group, block, and character may depend on $p$ and $F$.

Supplied qualifications. The original report excerpt begins mid-sentence; the complete hypotheses and quantifiers above follow the supplied reconstructed statement. The excerpt reports that the coprimality condition implies existence of the character, and that the reverse implication is reduced to height-zero characters in blocks of quasisimple groups. Here quasisimple means perfect with quotient by its center a nonabelian simple group. It also reports that the Alperin--McKay--Navarro conjecture implies Conjecture B. These are attributed source claims, not independently verified conclusions of this draft. The supplied dated assessment labels the problem partially solved and reports no general proof. This statement-only formulation requires human mathematical review.

Short English statement

The conjecture characterizes which abelian number fields occur as fields of values of height-zero irreducible characters for a fixed prime $p$. The proposed criterion is that adjoining the prime-to-$p$ cyclotomic field leaves an index coprime to $p$ in the cyclotomic field of the conductor. The supplied report says the existence direction is established and the reverse direction is reduced to quasisimple groups; these claims require independent verification.
