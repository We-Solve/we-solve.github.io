Ultraproduct II_1 Factors — Problem 22

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal R$ denote the separable hyperfinite factor of type $\mathrm{II}_1$. A factor of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ is an infinite-dimensional von Neumann algebra with center ${\mathbb{C}} 1$ and a faithful normal tracial state $\tau$ satisfying $\tau(1)=1$. Such a factor $P$ is called McDuff if \[ P\cong P\,\overline{\otimes}\,\mathcal R, \] where $\overline{\otimes}$ denotes the spatial tensor product of von Neumann algebras and $\cong$ denotes von Neumann algebra isomorphism.

Under the type $\mathrm{II}_1$-factor interpretation suggested by the source title, the question is: for factors $M,N_1,N_2$ of type $\mathrm{II}_1$, does \[ \left(M\cong N_1\,\overline{\otimes}\,N_2 \quad\text{and}\quad M\text{ is McDuff}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left(N_1\text{ is McDuff}\quad\text{or}\quad N_2\text{ is McDuff}\right) \] always hold?

The supplied excerpt does not explicitly state the type assumptions on the tensor factors or whether separable preduals are required. The displayed formulation therefore records the suggested type $\mathrm{II}_1$-factor interpretation; its intended scope, including separability hypotheses, requires source clarification. The introductory assertion that Jolissaint proved certain group von Neumann algebras McDuff refers to an unidentified family, so it cannot supply an additional precise hypothesis here.

Short English statement

If a McDuff factor of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ decomposes as a tensor product of two factors, must at least one tensor factor be McDuff?
