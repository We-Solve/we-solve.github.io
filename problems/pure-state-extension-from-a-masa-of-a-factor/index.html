Math-1296

Pure-state extension from a MASA of a factor

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a von Neumann factor, so its center is ${\mathbb{C}}1$, and let $A\subseteq M$ be a unital maximal abelian von Neumann subalgebra (MASA), equivalently $A'\cap M=A$. A state means a positive unital functional on the underlying $C^*$-algebra, not necessarily a normal state. A pure state is an extreme point of the state space; for abelian $A$, these are its characters $\chi\in\widehat A$. Put \[ \operatorname{Ext}_M(\chi)=\{\varphi\in M^*:\varphi\text{ a state},\ \varphi|_A=\chi\}. \] The source asks which inclusions have the all-pure-state unique extension property \[ \#\operatorname{Ext}_M(\chi)=1\quad\text{for every }\chi\in\widehat A. \] Failure means at least one pure state has distinct extensions, not that every pure state fails uniqueness.

The November 2014 compilation reports: MSS gives uniqueness for discrete MASAs of $B(H)$ on separable $H$; any continuous part of such a MASA prevents the all-state property, as in the original Kadison--Singer paper; every MASA of a type II factor acting on separable $H$ fails it; nonseparable examples can behave either way; and no type III results were known there. These are historical assessments, not present-day claims that no type III theorem exists.

The supplied later assessment states that for every MASA $A\subseteq M$ with $M$ having separable predual, the all-pure-state property holds exactly when $M$ is type I and $A$ is the range of a normal conditional expectation. It says, in particular, that every MASA in a separable-predual type II or III factor has a singular pure state with nonunique, necessarily singular, extensions, while the arbitrary-predual factor problem remains unclassified. These claims require their absent primary theorem reference. Separable predual is an intrinsic hypothesis; the historical discussion's chosen Hilbert-space representation is not silently substituted for it.

The candidate quantitative statement allows any unital abelian von Neumann subalgebra $A\subseteq M$, without requiring a factor or maximality. A conditional expectation onto $A$ is a positive unital $A$-bimodular projection $M\to A$; normal means ultraweakly continuous. In the report's singular convention, composing the singular expectation $F$ with normal positive functionals on $A$ yields singular functionals on $M$, whose normal component is zero. If both a normal expectation $E$ and a singular expectation $F$ exist, the asserted proposition is \[ \|E-F\|=2,\qquad \sup_{\chi\in\widehat A}\operatorname{diam}\operatorname{Ext}_M(\chi)=2. \] The first norm is the operator norm of a map $M\to A$; fiber diameter is $\sup_{\varphi,\psi\in\operatorname{Ext}_M(\chi)}\|\varphi-\psi\|_{M^*}$. This is a supremum over pure states and does not assert its attainment by a single specified character. The report supplies no construction of the expectations or full proof.

Short English statement

Determine when every pure state on a MASA has exactly one extension to its factor. The supplied later report gives a separable-predual classification and a norm-two obstruction from normal and singular expectations, while the original 2014 status statements and the arbitrary-predual problem remain separately qualified.
