Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 38

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite group $H$ is metacyclic if it has a cyclic normal subgroup $C\trianglelefteq H$ such that $H/C$ is cyclic. A finite group $G$ is Sylow-metacyclic if, for every prime $p\mid |G|$, every Sylow $p$-subgroup of $G$ is metacyclic.

For a field $F$, a finite group $G$ is $F$-admissible if there exist a finite-dimensional central division algebra $D$ over $F$ and a maximal commutative subfield $L\subseteq D$, containing $F$, such that $L/F$ is Galois and $\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)\cong G$. Here central means $Z(D)=F$, and maximal means that no larger commutative subfield of $D$ contains $L$. In this situation, \[ [L:F]=\deg(D)=|G|,\qquad \dim_F D=|G|^2. \]

Statement (the ${\mathbb{Q}}$-admissibility conjecture). For every finite Sylow-metacyclic group $G$, there exist a finite-dimensional central division algebra $D$ over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ and a maximal subfield $L\subseteq D$ such that $L/{\mathbb{Q}}$ is Galois and \[ \operatorname{Gal}(L/{\mathbb{Q}})\cong G. \]

The supplied extract describes the surrounding problem as an extension of the inverse Galois problem, especially interesting over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and mentions Problem 5 of the reference labeled [Am:surv]. Because this contextual passage precedes the words introducing the "next problem," its precise connection to the displayed conjecture is unresolved. The extract says that the conjecture was first recognized in the reference labeled [Schacher:th1]; neither reference label is bibliographically resolved in the supplied record.

Short English statement

Every finite group whose Sylow subgroups each have a cyclic normal subgroup with cyclic quotient should occur as the Galois group of a maximal subfield of a finite-dimensional central division algebra over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The supplied registry labels this conjecture open; that status has not been independently verified.
