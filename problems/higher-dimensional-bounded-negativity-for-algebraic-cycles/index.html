Higher-Dimensional Bounded Negativity for Algebraic Cycles

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety of dimension $n$ over a field $k$. This notation is conditional: the supplied record does not specify the field, its characteristic, or any further hypotheses on $X$. A codimension-$p$ algebraic cycle on $X$ is a finite formal sum $\alpha=\sum_i m_i[V_i]$, where $m_i\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ and each $V_i\subseteq X$ is an integral closed subvariety of codimension $p$. Such a cycle is effective if every $m_i\geq 0$. The record does not specify whether the intended objects are integral subvarieties, effective cycles, arbitrary cycles, or classes modulo an equivalence relation.

The supplied research question is: Which natural higher-dimensional generalizations of the Bounded Negativity Conjecture hold for cycles on smooth projective varieties? It asks for the formulation and investigation of suitable generalizations; it does not supply a particular quantified conjecture.

The intended higher-dimensional negativity invariant and the proposed bound are unresolved. For example, an intersection product of two codimension-$p$ cycle classes has codimension $2p$, so its degree gives a self-intersection number directly only when $2p=n$. Other dimensions require a different construction or additional intersection data, neither of which is specified. The record also leaves unspecified the permitted cycle dimensions, any positivity or normalization conditions, and the data on which a bound may depend. Consequently, this draft preserves the research question without selecting a particular generalization or asserting an inequality.

Short English statement

Determine suitable formulations of bounded negativity for algebraic cycles on smooth projective varieties of higher dimension, and determine which formulations hold. The supplied question leaves the field, cycle class, numerical invariant, and quantifiers for the proposed bounds unspecified, so it does not determine a single precise conjecture.
