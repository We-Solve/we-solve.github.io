Generic Vanishing of Localized Shimura-Variety Cohomology

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a space $X_K$ associated with a group $G$ and a level $K$, in the setting of Shimura-variety cohomology. The precise definitions and hypotheses on $G$ and $X_K$ are absent from the supplied excerpt. Let $\ell$ be a prime and let \[ R\in\{{\mathbb{F}}_\ell,{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell,{\mathbb{C}}\}. \] Write $H^i(X_K,R)$ and $H_c^i(X_K,R)$ for the cohomology and compactly supported cohomology intended by the source; the cohomology theories and coefficient conventions are not specified in the supplied record.

The excerpt describes a set $S$ of bad primes such that $G$ is unramified at primes outside $S$, together with a level decomposition \[ K=K^S\prod_{p\notin S}K_p, \] where $K_p\subseteq G({\mathbb{Q}}_p)$ is a hyperspecial maximal compact open subgroup. This displayed decomposition is a reconstruction of a typographically corrupted passage in the supplied excerpt; the notation $K^S$ is retained from that passage without an independently supplied definition. For a prime $p\notin S$, the local Hecke algebra is written \[ \mathbb T_p=C_c\left(K_p\backslash G({\mathbb{Q}}_p)/K_p,R\right). \] Here $C_c$ denotes compactly supported, locally constant, $R$-valued functions on the double-coset space, with the Hecke convolution product intended by the source. The record supplies Hecke actions on both cohomology groups. For a $\mathbb T_p$-module $M$ and a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_p\subset\mathbb T_p$, write \[ M_{\mathfrak m_p} =(\mathbb T_p\setminus\mathfrak m_p)^{-1}M \] for localization.

Conjectural statement as supplied. Let $\mathfrak m_p$ be a generic maximal ideal of $\mathbb T_p$. With $q_0$ and $l_0$ denoting the degree parameters intended by the source, for every cohomological degree $i$, \[ H^i(X_K,R)_{\mathfrak m_p}\ne 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad i\ge q_0, \] and \[ H_c^i(X_K,R)_{\mathfrak m_p}\ne 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad i\le q_0+l_0. \] The condition generic, the definitions of $q_0$ and $l_0$, the allowable geometric data, and any required relation between $p$ and $\ell$ are not provided. Consequently, these implications faithfully record the supplied target but do not constitute a fully specified standalone conjecture until those missing hypotheses and definitions are recovered.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture predicts that localization at a generic local Hecke maximal ideal forces ordinary cohomology to vanish below $q_0$, and compactly supported cohomology to vanish above $q_0+l_0$, for coefficients ${\mathbb{F}}_\ell$, ${\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, or ${\mathbb{C}}$. The meaning of genericity, the degree parameters, and the full geometric hypotheses require clarification from the source.
