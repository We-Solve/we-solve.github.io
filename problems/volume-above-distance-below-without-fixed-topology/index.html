Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M_j,g_j)$, $j\in{\mathbb{N}}$, and $(M_\infty,g_\infty)$ denote the compact oriented Riemannian manifolds in the supplied formulation. A common dimension $n$ is implicit, but the admissible dimensions and boundary conditions are not specified. Write $d_j$ and $d_\infty$ for the induced distances, and $T_j$ and $T_\infty$ for integration with the given orientations, whenever these data define integral current spaces. Denote these spaces by \[ \mathcal M_j=(M_j,d_j,T_j),\qquad \mathcal M_\infty=(M_\infty,d_\infty,T_\infty). \] Here $d_{\mathcal F}$ denotes intrinsic flat distance. Interpret the requested volume-preserving intrinsic flat convergence as \[ d_{\mathcal F}(\mathcal M_j,\mathcal M_\infty)\longrightarrow0, \qquad \operatorname{Vol}_{g_j}(M_j)\longrightarrow \operatorname{Vol}_{g_\infty}(M_\infty). \] The explicit numerical hypotheses in the supplied reconstruction are that there exists $D<\infty$ such that \[ \operatorname{diam}(M_j,d_j)\le D\quad\text{for every }j\in{\mathbb{N}}, \qquad \operatorname{Vol}_{g_j}(M_j)\longrightarrow \operatorname{Vol}_{g_\infty}(M_\infty). \] It also requires metric tensors to be bounded below by factors tending to one relative to the limit metric. On a common smooth manifold, a tensor comparison of this kind can be expressed as \[ g_j(v,v)\ge a_j g_\infty(v,v) \quad\text{for every tangent vector }v, \qquad a_j>0,\quad a_j\longrightarrow1. \] This expression has no defined meaning for tangent vectors on different manifolds without additional comparison data. The supplied record specifies neither such data nor their required properties.

Short English statement

The intended problem asks for an extension of a volume-above, distance-below convergence theorem to manifolds whose topology may differ from that of the limit. Uniform diameter control, volume convergence, and an appropriate lower metric comparison should imply volume-preserving intrinsic flat convergence. The supplied record does not define the comparison between different manifolds or provide Theorem 8, so essential hypotheses remain unresolved.
