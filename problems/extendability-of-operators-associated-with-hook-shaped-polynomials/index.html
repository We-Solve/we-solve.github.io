Extendability of Operators Associated with Hook-Shaped Polynomials

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record describes this assertion as a speculative conjecture, identified as Conjecture 3. For a polynomial $p$, write $T_p$ for the operator referred to in the record as its associated operator. This notation does not define that operator: its construction, domain, and codomain are absent from the supplied text.

The intended conjecture is the following conditional statement, universally quantified over the intended class of polynomials: \[ \begin{gathered} p is hook-shaped and symmetrically hyperbolic \ \Longrightarrow
\left(T_p is extendable\right)  \Longleftrightarrow  \left(p is weakly sum-of-squares hyperbolic\right). \end{gathered} Here the entire equivalence is the conclusion of the implication.

The intended polynomial class and the meanings of "hook-shaped," "symmetrically hyperbolic," "extendable," and "weakly sum-of-squares hyperbolic" are not defined in the supplied record. In particular, the coefficient field, number of variables, degree restrictions, any homogeneity or hyperbolicity-direction assumptions, and the permitted operator extensions are unspecified. Consequently, this display preserves the supplied assertion but is only a formulation schema; a precise, self-contained conjecture requires the missing definitions and hypotheses from the cited source.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that, for every hook-shaped, symmetrically hyperbolic polynomial in the intended class, its associated operator is extendable exactly when the polynomial is weakly sum-of-squares hyperbolic. The record omits the specialized definitions and operator construction needed to make this statement precise.
