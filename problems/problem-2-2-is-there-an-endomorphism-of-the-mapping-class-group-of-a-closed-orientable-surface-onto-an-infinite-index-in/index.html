Problem 2.2 — Is there an endomorphism of the mapping class group of a (closed) orientable surface onto an infinite index infinite…

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G=\operatorname{Mod}(S_g)$ for a closed orientable surface in the source's intended genus range. The first question is whether there exists an endomorphism $f:G\to G$ with \[|f(G)|=\infty,\qquad [G:f(G)]=\infty.\] The source conjectures that no such endomorphism exists. This concerns a homomorphism onto its own image, and neither surjectivity onto $G$ nor injectivity of $f$ is assumed.

Conditionally, if such $f$ exists, the second question asks whether for some $n\geq1$, \[f|_{f^n(G)}:f^n(G)\to f^{n+1}(G)\text{ is injective}.\] Equivalently, \[\ker f\cap f^n(G)=\{1\},\quad\text{or}\quad\ker f^{n+1}=\ker f^n.\] This is eventual injectivity on an iterated image, not a demand that the original map be injective. The genus range is not stated in this excerpt and matters for small-genus exceptional groups; no universal claim including every low genus is added.

The following context is about a different class of maps. Let $\operatorname{Mod}_{g,p}^*$ be the extended mapping class group of a genus-$g$ surface with $p$ punctures, including orientation-reversing diffeomorphisms, and let $\Gamma\leq\operatorname{Mod}_{g,p}^*$ have finite index. The source reports that an injective homomorphism $\Gamma\to\operatorname{Mod}_{g,p}^*$ is the restriction of an automorphism of the ambient group, attributing this to Irmak [18,19] for $g=2,p\geq2$ and for $g\geq3$, and to Bell--Margalit [1] for $g=0$. The genus-zero puncture/complexity restrictions and any exceptional cases are not reproduced, so this is a reported rigidity statement requiring exact hypotheses. It does not directly answer the noninjective endomorphism question above. The final sentence introduces another result that is absent.

Short English statement

Can an endomorphism of the closed-surface mapping class group have infinite image of infinite index? If so, must it become injective on some iterated image? The source conjectures nonexistence and separately cites finite-index embedding rigidity.
