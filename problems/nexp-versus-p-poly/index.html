CS-12 · Math-147

NEXP versus P/poly

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra pro Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Define $\mathsf{NEXP}=\bigcup_{k\ge1}\mathsf{NTIME}(2^{n^k})$, using nondeterministic machines with the indicated time bound on every branch. Membership in $\mathsf{P/poly}$ means that each input length has a polynomial-size Boolean circuit, with no uniform-generation condition. The question is \[ \mathsf{NEXP}\stackrel{?}{\subseteq}\mathsf{P/poly}. \] To refute containment, one needs one language in $\mathsf{NEXP}$ lacking every polynomial circuit-size bound, not merely a lower bound in a restricted circuit class.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Could every nondeterministic exponential-time language nevertheless have polynomial-size nonuniform Boolean circuits?
