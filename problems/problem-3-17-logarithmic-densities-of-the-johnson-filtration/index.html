Problem 3.17 — (Logarithmic densities of the Johnson filtration).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\pi=\pi_1(\Sigma_g)$, with lower central series $\Gamma_1\pi=\pi$, $\Gamma_{k+1}\pi=[\pi,\Gamma_k\pi]$. The Johnson filtration $\mathcal I_g(m)$ is conventionally defined using kernels of the mapping-class action on nilpotent quotients of $\pi$, with an outer action for an unpointed closed surface. For example, one common indexing uses the kernel of $\operatorname{Mod}_g\to\operatorname{Out}(\pi/\Gamma_{m+1}\pi)$. The source's precise indexing and surface conventions are not supplied.

Problem 3.17 asks for the asymptotics of \[ d_{\log}(\mathcal I_g(m)) \] both as $g\to\infty$ and as $m\to\infty$. It also highlights the analogous question for $\Gamma_k\pi$ inside $\pi$ as $k\to\infty$. The defining formula for $d_{\log}$ is missing. One common possible meaning, for $H\le G$ and finite generating set $S$, is \[ \lim_{r\to\infty} \frac{\log|H\cap B_S^G(r)|}{\log|B_S^G(r)|}, \] if the limit exists. This is explicitly conditional: limit versus upper/lower limit, ambient metric, and generating sets as $g$ varies must be recovered before using this formula. The source does not specify fixed-other-parameter versus joint/uniform asymptotics. Its "as far as I know" observation about the surface-group case is a source-era statement, not a current audit.

The subsequent "Entropy" section is contextual. For a finitely generated group $G$, let $B_r(G,S)$ denote the cardinality of the radius-$r$ word ball. It defines \[ w(G,S)=\lim_{r\to\infty}B_r(G,S)^{1/r},\qquad \operatorname{ent}(G)=\inf_{S\text{ finite generating}}\log w(G,S), \] with existence of the first limit attributed to submultiplicativity. It reports that this group entropy bounds, after multiplying by a diameter factor, volume-growth and geodesic-flow entropies of closed Riemannian manifolds, referring to [Harp1]. No exact normalization or inequality is supplied.

The context attributes positive entropy for "nonsolvable" finitely generated linear groups to Eskin--Mozes--Oh [EMO], uses the homological surjection $\operatorname{Mod}_g\to\mathrm{Sp}(2g,{\mathbb{Z}})$ to infer positivity for $\operatorname{Mod}_g$, and reports positivity for the Torelli group $\mathcal I_g$ via a non-virtually-solvable linear image from its action on homology of the universal abelian cover, described as essentially the Magnus representation. The exact theorem hypotheses must be checked: nonsolvable is not interchangeable with non-virtually-solvable, and finite nonsolvable linear groups have zero exponential growth entropy. Genus and finite-generation hypotheses are also needed for the claims about $\mathcal I_g$. These are retained as qualified contextual reports, not answers to the logarithmic-density problem. Footnote 4 refers to Barry Mazur [Maz] without its antecedent or bibliography entry.

Short English statement

Determine logarithmic-density asymptotics for the Johnson filtration in large genus and deep filtration, and for the lower central series of a surface group. The density convention and parameter regime are missing. The following entropy discussion is retained as context, with its theorem hypotheses and genus restrictions explicitly requiring review.
