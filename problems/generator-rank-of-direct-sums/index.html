Generator Rank of Direct Sums

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ and $B$ be arbitrary separable complex $C^*$-algebras; neither is assumed unital. Their direct sum $A\oplus B$ has coordinatewise algebraic operations and involution, with $\|(a,b)\|=\max\{\|a\|,\|b\|\}$.

The supplied record does not define generator rank. To make the question explicit, use the following unitization convention, which requires confirmation against the cited source. For a unital $C^*$-algebra $D$, write $D_{\mathrm{sa}}=\{d\in D:d=d^*\}$, and define \[ \operatorname{Gen}_{m}(D)_{\mathrm{sa}}=\{(d_1,\ldots,d_m)\in D_{\mathrm{sa}}^m:C^*(d_1,\ldots,d_m)=D\},\qquad m\geq 1. \] Here $C^*(d_1,\ldots,d_m)$ is the smallest norm-closed $*$-subalgebra containing the listed elements; the identity is not adjoined automatically. Set \[ \operatorname{gr}(D)=\min\left\{k\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}:\operatorname{Gen}_{k+1}(D)_{\mathrm{sa}}\text{ is dense in }D_{\mathrm{sa}}^{k+1}\right\}, \] with value $\infty$ if this set is empty, and with density taken in the maximum product norm. For nonunital $D$, set $\operatorname{gr}(D)=\operatorname{gr}(\widetilde D)$, where $\widetilde D$ is its minimal unitization.

Under this explicit convention, the question is whether, for every such pair $A,B$, \[ \operatorname{gr}(A\oplus B)=\max\{\operatorname{gr}(A),\operatorname{gr}(B)\}, \] where the maximum is taken in $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}$.

The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports the formula as proved for commutative, real-rank-zero, and subhomogeneous summands, while describing the arbitrary separable case as unclear. These are supplied literature qualifications, not independently verified conclusions; the record does not specify the precise hypotheses of those reported special cases.

Short English statement

For every pair of separable complex $C^*$-algebras, does taking their direct sum make the generator rank equal to the larger of the two individual ranks? The draft explicitly uses the unitization convention for nonunital algebras, pending source confirmation. The supplied partially solved label and special-case claims are dated curation requiring independent review.
