Math-1324

Irrational Hilbert-scheme components and a threshold-10 MRC strengthening

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic zero, let $\operatorname{Hilb}^{D}({\mathbb{A}}_k^n)$ denote the Hilbert scheme of length-$D$ zero-dimensional subschemes of affine $n$-space. As specified in the supplied source verification, "component" means an irreducible component. A component is called rational when its reduced underlying integral variety is birational over $k$ to affine space, equivalently its function field is purely transcendental over $k$. The original question is the existential assertion \[ \exists D,n\ge1\ \ \exists W\text{ an irreducible component of } \operatorname{Hilb}^{D}({\mathbb{A}}_k^n),\qquad W_{\mathrm{red}}\text{ is not rational}. \] The original extracted text does not itself specify a ground field; characteristic zero and algebraic closedness are the setting of the supplied answer. The trailing text "$>12$ RECORDED ON WHITEBOARD BY IZZET COSKUN, AND EDITED BY LI LI" is an OCR/footer artifact, not an inequality restricting $D$ or $n$.

The supplied aid reports a positive answer: Farkas--Pandharipande--Sammartano constructed components that are not rationally connected for ambient dimensions $n\ge12$, and Wu's June 2026 one-variable-frame preprint extends this to every $n\ge10$. No assertion of optimality of these thresholds is supplied.

Its proposed strengthening has the quantifier order \[ \forall g\ge24\ \ \exists D_g\ge1\ \ \forall n\ge10\ \ \exists W_{g,n} \subset\operatorname{Hilb}^{D_g}({\mathbb{A}}_k^n), \] where $W_{g,n}$ is an irreducible component whose reduced variety admits a dominant rational map to the moduli space $\mathcal M_g$ of smooth projective connected genus-$g$ curves, and whose maximal rationally connected quotient has dimension at least $3g-3$. Here MRC dimension means the dimension of the base of the maximal rationally connected fibration of a smooth projective model of the function field. The aid derives the MRC conclusion using functoriality and the properties of the genus-$g$ moduli target; domination alone without a suitable non-uniruled target would not establish that bound. It claims the length can be kept unchanged while extending ambient dimension, so the same $D_g$ works for every $n\ge10$.

The reported consequences are that these reduced components are neither rational, stably rational, nor unirational, and that MRC dimensions are unbounded already for $n=10$ and $n=11$. Stable rationality means becoming rational after taking a product with some affine space; unirationality means admitting a dominant rational map from a projective space. These stronger conclusions and the uniform-length construction are attributed to the unreviewed aid and require verification of the cited results and field hypotheses; no explicit value of $D_g$ is supplied.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a Hilbert scheme of points has an irreducible component that is not rational. The supplied aid reports such components in characteristic zero, with a June 2026 preprint lowering the ambient-dimension threshold to ten and a stronger uniform-length claim involving moduli of curves.
