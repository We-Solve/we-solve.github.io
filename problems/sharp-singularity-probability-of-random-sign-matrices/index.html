Math-1068

Sharp Singularity Probability of Random Sign Matrices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 1$, let $B_n=(b_{ij})_{1\leq i,j\leq n}$ be a random matrix whose entries are mutually independent and satisfy \[ \mathbb P(b_{ij}=1)=\mathbb P(b_{ij}=-1)=\frac12. \] Regard $B_n$ as a matrix over ${\mathbb{R}}$, and write \[ p_n:=\mathbb P(\det B_n=0). \] The supplied historical problem asks for the sharp asymptotic behavior of $p_n$ as $n\to\infty$, with the conjectural exponential scale \[ p_n=\left(\frac12+o_n(1)\right)^n =2^{-(1+o_n(1))n}. \] Here each occurrence of $o_n(1)$ denotes a real sequence tending to zero as $n\to\infty$; the sequences need not coincide. Equivalently, the displayed assertion is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}p_n^{1/n}=\frac12, \qquad\text{or}\qquad p_n=2^{-n}\exp(o(n)). \] These expressions specify exponential accuracy and do not specify a leading multiplicative factor or a relative-error asymptotic.

There is an unresolved mismatch in the supplied record: its statement concerns uniform random sign matrices, whereas its dated literature assessment concerns a sparse Bernoulli singularity conjecture and an unspecified range of parameter sequences. No sparse entry distribution, parameter range, or corresponding conjectural formula is supplied. Thus that assessment cannot be unambiguously attached to the displayed sign-matrix formulation. The displayed assertion is retained as a historical conjectural target; no present-day mathematical status is asserted here.

Short English statement

For an $n\times n$ matrix of independent uniform signs, determine the probability of singularity as $n\to\infty$. The supplied historical conjecture gives the exponential scale $2^{-n}\exp(o(n))$. Its relationship to the record's sparse Bernoulli literature assessment requires clarification.
