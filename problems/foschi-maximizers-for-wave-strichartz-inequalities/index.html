Foschi Maximizers for Wave Strichartz Inequalities

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, $d\geq2$, and put \[ \phi_d(x)=(1+|x|^2)^{-(d-1)/2},\qquad x\in{\mathbb{R}}^d. \] The wave evolution associated with the stated initial datum is \[ \begin{cases} \partial_t^2u-\Delta_xu=0 & \text{on }{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{R}}^d,\\ u(0,x)=\phi_d(x),\\ \partial_tu(0,x)=0. \end{cases} \] Equivalently, write $u_d(t)=\cos(t\sqrt{-\Delta})\phi_d$, where $\sqrt{-\Delta}$ is the spatial Fourier multiplier with symbol $|\xi|$.

Question attributed to the Foschi conjecture in the supplied record. For every integer $d\geq2$, does the datum $(\phi_d,0)$ attain equality in the sharp wave Strichartz inequality intended by the source?

Unresolved specification of the inequality. The supplied question does not state the spacetime exponent or norm, the initial-data space or norm, or the class of competing data. These choices are necessary to define a maximizer. More explicitly, if the intended inequality is \[ \|u\|_{Y_d}\leq C_d\|(u(0),\partial_tu(0))\|_{X_d}, \] where $X_d$ is the intended normed initial-data space, $Y_d$ is the intended spacetime normed space, and $C_d$ is the least valid constant, the requested assertion would be \[ \|u_d\|_{Y_d}=C_d\|(\phi_d,0)\|_{X_d} \qquad\text{for every integer }d\geq2. \] Here $X_d$ and $Y_d$ are placeholders for missing source specifications, not choices supplied by this draft. In particular, admissibility of the proposed datum in the intended space and the precise equality-case problem remain to be established from the source.

The original excerpt instead discusses an estimate for the positive cone \[ K_d^+=\{(\tau,\xi)\in{\mathbb{R}}\times{\mathbb{R}}^d:\tau=|\xi|\}, \] with measure $\mu_+$ defined by \[ \int_{K_d^+}F\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu_+ =\int_{{\mathbb{R}}^d}F(|\xi|,\xi)\,\frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}\xi}{2|\xi|}. \] Its displayed estimate has the form \[ \|\widehat{f\mu_+}\|_{L^q({\mathbb{R}}^{1+d})} \leq C_{d,p}\|f\|_{L^p(\mu_+)}. \] The excerpt says that this cone estimate generalizes an earlier inequality and that the Foschi conjecture is to be extended to that setting. However, the earlier inequality and the generalized maximizer conjecture are absent, and the displayed restrictions on $p,q$ are corrupted. Thus the excerpt does not determine the missing wave inequality or establish that its maximizer question is identical to the generalized cone question.

Short English statement

For every spatial dimension $d\geq2$, the supplied question asks whether the wave with initial displacement $(1+|x|^2)^{-(d-1)/2}$ and zero initial velocity attains the optimal Strichartz bound. The exact inequality and competing data class are missing, so a unique fully specified maximization problem cannot yet be stated. The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved.
