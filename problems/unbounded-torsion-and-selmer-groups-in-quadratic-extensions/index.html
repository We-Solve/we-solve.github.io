Math-815

Unbounded Torsion and Selmer Groups in Quadratic Extensions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime $\ell$, a number field $K$, and an abelian variety $A/K$. Let $L$ range over quadratic extensions of $K$, up to $K$-isomorphism; thus $[L:K]=2$. Write $\mathcal O_L$ for the ring of integers of $L$, and let $\operatorname{Pic}(\mathcal O_L)$ be its ideal class group. Its $\ell$-torsion subgroup is \[ \operatorname{Pic}(\mathcal O_L)[\ell] =\{c\in\operatorname{Pic}(\mathcal O_L):\ell c=0\}. \] Write $A_L=A\times_K L$ for the base change of $A$, and $A_L[\ell]$ for the kernel of multiplication by $\ell$. For each place $v$ of $L$, let $L_v$ be the completion and let \[ \delta_v:A_L(L_v)/\ell A_L(L_v)\longrightarrow H^1(L_v,A_L[\ell]) \] be the Kummer map. Here $H^1$ denotes continuous Galois cohomology. Define the $\ell$-Selmer group by \[ \operatorname{Sel}_{\ell}(A_L) =\{\xi\in H^1(L,A_L[\ell]): \operatorname{res}_v(\xi)\in\operatorname{im}(\delta_v) \text{ for every place }v\text{ of }L\}. \] Both groups whose cardinalities occur below are finite for each fixed $L$.

The supplied record proposes the following two unboundedness assertions, with $\ell$, $K$, and, in the second assertion, $A$ fixed while $L$ varies:

  1. For every integer $B\geq 1$, there exists a quadratic extension $L/K$ such that \[ \#\operatorname{Pic}(\mathcal O_L)[\ell]>B. \]
  2. For every integer $B\geq 1$, there exists a quadratic extension $L/K$ such that \[ \#\operatorname{Sel}_{\ell}(A_L)>B. \]
These are separate assertions: the extensions witnessing the two inequalities need not be the same. The source presents these assertions for arbitrary prime $\ell$, number field $K$, and abelian variety $A/K$. It does not explicitly require $\dim A>0$; whether its convention excludes the zero-dimensional abelian variety is an unresolved qualification of the intended second assertion. No such hypothesis has been silently added here.

Short English statement

For a fixed prime and number field, the first conjecture asks whether the prime-torsion subgroup of the ideal class group can be arbitrarily large over quadratic extensions. For a fixed abelian variety over that field, the second asks the same question for its Selmer groups after base change. These are separate unboundedness conjectures. The intended dimension restriction on the abelian variety requires clarification.
