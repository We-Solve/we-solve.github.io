Kourovka Notebook Problem 4.65

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For distinct prime numbers $p$ and $q$, define the positive integer \[ S(p,q)=\frac{p^q-1}{p-1}=\sum_{i=0}^{q-1}p^i. \] Here a prime is a positive integer greater than $1$ whose only positive divisors are $1$ and itself. The conjecture proposed by J. G. Thompson is \[ \forall p,q\text{ prime},\qquad p\ne q\ \Longrightarrow\ S(p,q)\nmid S(q,p). \] Thus the assertion concerns every ordered pair of distinct primes, including pairs containing $2$. Equivalently, there is no integer $k\geq 1$ such that \[ \frac{q^p-1}{q-1}=k\frac{p^q-1}{p-1}. \] The supplied source states that validity of this conjecture would simplify the proof of solvability of groups of odd order by making detailed use of generators and relations unnecessary. This is preserved as source-provided context, not as an independently verified implication. The registry labels the conjecture open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified assessment.

Short English statement

Thompson conjectures that, for every pair of distinct primes $p,q$, the integer $1+p+\cdots+p^{q-1}$ does not divide $1+q+\cdots+q^{p-1}$. The supplied record labels the conjecture open and describes a possible simplification of the proof of solvability of groups of odd order if it holds.
