Reeb Orbits Bounding Global Disk Sections

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S^3$ denote the smooth three-sphere. Under the usual contact-geometric interpretation of the supplied question, let $\alpha$ be a smooth contact form on $S^3$, so that $\alpha\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha$ is nowhere zero, and suppose that its contact structure $\xi=\ker\alpha$ is tight, meaning that it contains no overtwisted disk. Its Reeb vector field $R_\alpha$ and flow $\phi_t$ are defined by \[ \alpha(R_\alpha)=1,\qquad {\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha(R_\alpha,v)=0\quad\text{for every tangent vector }v,\qquad \frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t}\phi_t(x)=R_\alpha(\phi_t(x)). \] The flow is defined for all real times because $S^3$ is compact.

A closed Reeb orbit is the image $\gamma$ of a periodic trajectory: there are $p\in S^3$ and $\tau>0$ such that $\phi_\tau(p)=p$ and $\gamma=\{\phi_t(p):t\in{\mathbb{R}}\}$. Here $\gamma$ is treated as a geometric orbit; the supplied record does not specify how multiple covers are to be treated.

For this draft, a disk-like global surface of section bounded by $\gamma$ means the image $D=u(\overline{\mathbb D})$ of a smooth embedding of the closed disk \[ \overline{\mathbb D}=\{(a,b)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:a^2+b^2\leq 1\} \] into $S^3$, with $u(\partial\overline{\mathbb D})=\gamma$. Write $D^\circ=u(\mathbb D)$. Require \[ R_\alpha(q)\notin T_qD^\circ\qquad(q\in D^\circ) \] and \[ \begin{gathered} \forall x\in S^3\setminus\gamma\quad\forall T>0\quad \exists t_+,t_-\in{\mathbb{R}}:\\ t_+>T,\qquad t_-<-T,\qquad \phi_{t_+}(x)\in D^\circ,\qquad \phi_{t_-}(x)\in D^\circ. \end{gathered} \] Thus every trajectory other than the boundary orbit meets the disk interior transversely at arbitrarily large positive and negative times. The boundary orbit itself is tangent to the Reeb flow and is excluded from this intersection requirement.

Question represented by the displayed source statement. For a contact form $\alpha$ on the tight three-sphere, characterize the closed Reeb orbits $\gamma$ for which such a disk $D$ exists.

Short English statement

The displayed question asks which periodic Reeb trajectories on the tight three-sphere bound an embedded disk whose interior is transverse to the flow and is visited at arbitrarily large positive and negative times by every other trajectory.
