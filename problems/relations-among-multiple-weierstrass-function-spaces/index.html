Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record calls $\mathcal P_k$ spaces of multiple Weierstrass functions and $\mathcal E_k$ Eisenstein-series spaces. It does not define these spaces, their coefficient field, their ambient function space, or their grading conventions. It also does not define the operators $C_n$, the unindexed spaces $\mathcal E$ and $\mathcal P$, the product $\mathcal E\cdot\mathcal P$, or the symbols $\Delta$ and $\wp_2$. These symbols are therefore retained as source notation without assigning additional meanings.

The reconstructed statement supplied by the registry asks for \[ C_n(\mathcal P_{k+n})=\mathcal E_k \qquad\text{for all }k\geq0\text{ and }n\geq3, \] and \[ \frac{d}{dz}(\mathcal E\cdot\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal P. \] It further asks for \[ \mathcal E_k\cap\mathcal P_k=\Delta\mathcal E_{k-12}, \qquad \mathcal E_k\cap(\mathcal P_{k-2}\wp_2) =\Delta\mathcal E_{k-14}\wp_2. \] The admissible index sets for $k,n$ are not explicitly specified. No range of $k$ is separately supplied for the intersection identities, and no convention is supplied for spaces with negative indices. The domain of $z$, the meaning of differentiation on these spaces, and the common ambient space needed for the intersections are also unspecified. Consequently, these formulas preserve the supplied target but do not yet constitute a self-contained mathematical conjecture.

The original extracted passage describes a conjecture as an analogue of an unspecified Theorem 4 and refers to a further conjecture concerning $C_0(\mathcal P_k)$ and $C_2(\mathcal P_k)$. The actual conjectural formulas are absent from that passage. It states that dimension conjectures for $\mathcal E_k$ had been given by Okounkov [5] and Bachmann--Kühn [1], and that determining dimension conjectures for the indicated $C_0$ and $C_2$ spaces would yield a dimension conjecture for $\mathcal P_k$. Short English statement

The supplied reconstructed claim relates coefficient-operator images, differentiation, and intersections of spaces of multiple Weierstrass functions and Eisenstein series. Essential definitions and index conventions are missing, and the original extract omits the conjectural formulas. The registry labels the problem open; this draft preserves that supplied label without independently verifying it.
