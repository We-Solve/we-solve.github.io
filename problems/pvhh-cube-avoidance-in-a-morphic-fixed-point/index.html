Math-7359

PVHH-Cube Avoidance in a Morphic Fixed Point

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A=\{0,1,3,4\}\subset{\mathbb{Z}}$, with each letter interpreted as its integer value. Let $A^*$ denote the finite words over $A$, including the empty word, and define the morphism $\sigma:A^*\to A^*$, extended by concatenation, by \[ \sigma(0)=03,\qquad \sigma(1)=43,\qquad \sigma(3)=1,\qquad \sigma(4)=01. \] Let \[ w=\lim_{k\to\infty}\sigma^k(0)=w_0w_1w_2\cdots \] be the one-sided infinite fixed point beginning with $0$; the limit is understood through its nested finite prefixes. For a finite word $x=a_1\cdots a_m$, define its length and letter sum by \[ |x|=m,\qquad S(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{m}a_j. \] A factor is a contiguous finite block of $w$.

Conjecture (supplied attribution: Conjecture 48 of the cited workshop report). There are no three nonempty words $x_1,x_2,x_3\in A^*$ such that $x_1x_2x_3$ is a factor of $w$ and \[ |x_1|=|x_2|=|x_3|,\qquad S(x_1)=S(x_2)=S(x_3). \] Equivalently, for every integer $i\geq0$ and every integer $n\geq1$, the three integers \[ \sum_{j=0}^{n-1}w_{i+j},\qquad \sum_{j=0}^{n-1}w_{i+n+j},\qquad \sum_{j=0}^{n-1}w_{i+2n+j} \] are not all equal.

The nonempty-block condition uses the usual convention for cube avoidance; the supplied formulas do not explicitly state it. Only equal lengths and equal integer letter sums are required: the blocks need not be identical or have identical letter multiplicities. The source labels this claim open and its transcription corrected and verified; those are supplied curation labels, not independent verification here.

Short English statement

Starting with $0$, repeatedly apply the given substitution to obtain an infinite word. The conjecture says that no contiguous block splits into three nonempty consecutive pieces of equal length whose integer letter sums are all equal. The supplied open-status and source-verification assessments require independent review.
