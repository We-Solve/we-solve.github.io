Higher Central-Derivative Formulas for L-Functions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A global field is either a number field (a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$) or a global function field (a finite extension of ${\mathbb{F}}_q(t)$, where $q$ is a prime power). The supplied problem concerns automorphic $L$-functions over both kinds of global field.

For an automorphic datum $\pi$ with an associated $L$-function $L(s,\pi)$, let $s_c$ denote its central point after a normalization has been specified. If $L(s,\pi)$ is holomorphic near $s_c$, define its central derivatives by \[ L^{(r)}(s_c,\pi)=\left.\frac{\mathrm d^r}{\mathrm ds^r}L(s,\pi)\right|_{s=s_c},\qquad r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}. \] Holomorphy here is a condition needed for this notation to describe finite derivatives, not a hypothesis supplied for a specified family in the record. The record does not specify whether the intended object is an uncompleted, completed, or otherwise normalized $L$-function.

Research problem. Develop identities expressing higher-order central derivatives of automorphic $L$-functions over number fields and global function fields, extending the Waldspurger and Gross--Zagier formulas in the relevant settings.

Unresolved scope. This is a research program rather than a fully quantified conjecture. The source record does not specify the automorphic groups, representations, precise $L$-functions, central-point conventions, derivative orders, local or global hypotheses, or the geometric or arithmetic quantities and normalization constants that should occur in the identities. Consequently, it does not justify an assertion for every automorphic datum or every derivative order. These choices must be supplied before a precise family of identities can be stated.

Short English statement

Seek formulas for higher central derivatives of automorphic $L$-functions over number fields and function fields that extend the Waldspurger and Gross--Zagier formulas. The supplied record leaves the families, hypotheses, normalizations, and right-hand sides unspecified, so this draft preserves a research program requiring further mathematical formulation.
