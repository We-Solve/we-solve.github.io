Phys-21 · Math-7674

Bound Entanglement with Negative Partial Transpose

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra ultra Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite-dimensional bipartite state is a positive semidefinite trace-one operator $\rho$ on $H_A\otimes H_B$. It is entangled if it is not a convex combination of product states. Partial transpose $\rho^{T_B}$ transposes the second subsystem in an orthonormal basis; having a negative eigenvalue is basis independent. LOCC consists of local quantum operations with classical communication. The target asks for $\rho^{T_B}\not\succeq0$ but zero asymptotic rate of distilling maximally entangled pairs from $\rho^{\otimes n}$. Equivalently, in the standard finite-dimensional distillability criterion, seek an NPT state with \[ \langle\psi|(\rho^{T_B})^{\otimes n}|\psi\rangle\ge0 \] for every $n\ge1$ and every vector $\psi$ of Schmidt rank at most two across $A^n:B^n$. This is not merely failure of single-copy distillation.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Can a quantum state have a negative partial transpose yet yield no distillable entanglement, even when arbitrarily many copies and two-way classical communication are available?

Sources

  • S1 P. Horodecki, L. Rudnicki, and K. Zyczkowski, Five Open Problems in Quantum Information Theory, PRX Quantum 3 (2022). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.03233. Formulation source.
