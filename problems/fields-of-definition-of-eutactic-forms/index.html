Math-15031

Fields of Definition of Eutactic Forms

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For standard background over ${\mathbb{R}}$, let $n\geq 1$, let $A\in M_n({\mathbb{R}})$ be symmetric and positive definite, and put $q_A(x)=x^{\mathsf T}Ax$. Relative to the lattice ${\mathbb{Z}}^n$, define \[ m(A)=\min_{0\ne v\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n}q_A(v),\qquad S(A)=\{v\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n\setminus\{0\}:q_A(v)=m(A)\}. \] The form is eutactic if there exist real numbers $\lambda_v>0$, one for every $v\in S(A)$, such that \[ A^{-1}=\sum_{v\in S(A)}\lambda_v vv^{\mathsf T}. \] This supplies the usual real lattice meaning of eutaxy; it does not specify a number-field generalization absent from the record.

The supplied question is: can one predict the field of definition of a eutactic form over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, or over another number field $k$? Here a number field means a finite extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The record does not define the intended field of definition or the meaning of eutaxy over $k$. Consequently, a unique precise mathematical assertion cannot be recovered from the supplied wording.

In particular, for a real matrix $A=(a_{ij})$, the coefficient field ${\mathbb{Q}}(a_{ij}:1\leq i,j\leq n)$ and the field generated by the coefficients of the normalized matrix $A/m(A)$ are possible conventions, but the source selects neither. It also does not state whether the intended field concerns the form, its similarity class, or coefficients in a eutaxy identity. For a number-field formulation, the relevant embeddings, positivity conditions, lattice or module, and equivalence relation remain unspecified.

The supplied literature assessment additionally reports the question whether every number field admitting a real embedding can occur. A real embedding is a field embedding $k\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}$. This reported realizability question is retained as a source qualification, not identified with the original prediction question: what must have field of definition $k$, under which convention, and in which dimensions is not specified.

Short English statement

The problem asks how to determine the field of definition associated with a eutactic quadratic form over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ or another number field. The record also reports a realizability question for number fields with a real embedding. Precise formulations require definitions of the intended field, normalization, equivalence, and number-field notion of eutaxy; these are missing.
