Math-6756

Limit Theorems in the Condensation Regime

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\beta$ denote the mutation probability and let $\mu$ denote the mutant-fitness distribution in the stochastic house-of-cards model. Thus $\beta\in[0,1]$; the displayed source criterion treats $\mu$ as a probability measure on $[0,1]$. Let $N(t)$ denote the population size at time $t\geq0$. The supplied record does not specify the stochastic dynamics, initial population, or additional assumptions on $\mu$, so these data require clarification before a complete probabilistic statement can be given.

Assume the stated condensation condition \[ \beta\int_{[0,1]}\frac{1}{1-x}\,\mu(\mathrm{d}x)<1. \] The integral has a singularity at $x=1$. For $\beta>0$, this condition requires a finite integral and therefore $\mu(\{1\})=0$. The source does not specify whether the endpoint mutation probabilities are allowed or how to interpret the criterion when $\beta=0$ and the integral is infinite.

The supplied statement reports exponential growth rate \[ \lambda^*=1-\beta, \] while describing the growth as not strictly exponential. The problem is to determine the correction to $e^{\lambda^*t}$ that yields a nondegenerate limit theorem for $N(t)$.

For example, a possible normalization target would be to identify a deterministic function $a(t)>0$ and a finite random variable $W$, not almost surely constant, such that \[ \frac{N(t)}{e^{(1-\beta)t}a(t)} \xrightarrow[t\to\infty]{\mathrm{d}} W. \] This display is an explicitly provisional interpretation of the requested limit theorem, not an additional assertion from the source. The source does not prescribe deterministic normalization, convergence in distribution, the meaning of nondegeneracy, or the precise meaning of "not strictly exponential." Its supplied literature assessment refers to a conjectural logarithmic correction but gives no formula or fitness-tail hypotheses that would specify such a correction.

Short English statement

In the stated condensation regime, determine how to correct the reported exponential population growth $e^{(1-\beta)t}$ to obtain a nondegenerate limit theorem. A complete formulation requires the model dynamics, fitness assumptions, normalization class, and convergence mode, which the supplied record does not specify.
