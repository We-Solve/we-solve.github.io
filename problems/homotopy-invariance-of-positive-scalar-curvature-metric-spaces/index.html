Homotopy Invariance of Positive-Scalar-Curvature Metric Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed manifold is compact and has no boundary. To interpret Riemannian metrics, take the manifolds to be smooth; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify the smooth category. For a smooth manifold $X$, define \[ R^+(X)=\{g\in\Gamma^\infty(\operatorname{Sym}^2T^*X): g\text{ is positive definite and }\operatorname{scal}_g(x)>0\text{ for every }x\in X\}, \] with the $C^\infty$ topology. Here $\operatorname{scal}_g$ denotes scalar curvature. This is the space of metrics themselves, without taking a quotient by diffeomorphisms. The topology is a standard interpretive convention and is not specified in the supplied record.

Under the usual stable normal convention, let $\nu_X:X\to BO$ classify the stable normal bundle of $X$, where $BO$ is the stable classifying space for real vector bundles. A normal $2$-type of $X$ is represented by a fibration $p:B\to BO$ and a lift $\bar\nu_X:X\to B$, with $p\circ\bar\nu_X\simeq\nu_X$, such that $\bar\nu_X$ is $3$-connected and each homotopy fiber of $p$ has vanishing homotopy groups in degrees at least $3$. A $3$-connected map induces a bijection on path components, isomorphisms on $\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$, and a surjection on $\pi_3$, at every basepoint. Having the same normal $2$-type means that the corresponding fibrations over $BO$ are fiber homotopy equivalent; equivalently, a common such fibration can be used for both normal lifts. The source does not specify its convention for normal $2$-types, connectedness, or additional tangential structures; this definition records the usual unstructured stable normal interpretation, subject to source review.

The supplied conjecture is the following: for every integer $d\geq5$ and every pair of closed smooth $d$-manifolds $M,N$ with the same normal $2$-type, \[ R^+(M)\neq\varnothing\quad\text{and}\quad R^+(N)\neq\varnothing \quad\Longrightarrow\quad R^+(M)\simeq R^+(N). \] Explicitly, the conclusion asks for continuous maps $F:R^+(M)\to R^+(N)$ and $G:R^+(N)\to R^+(M)$ satisfying \[ G\circ F\simeq\operatorname{id}_{R^+(M)},\qquad F\circ G\simeq\operatorname{id}_{R^+(N)}. \] No connectedness, orientability, spin structure, or normal bordism relation between $M$ and $N$ is explicitly assumed in the supplied formulation. Whether the original discussion imposes any such qualifications remains unresolved. The source labels this assertion a conjecture and supplies an open-status assessment; neither constitutes an independently verified mathematical conclusion here.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asserts that, in every dimension $d\geq5$, two closed manifolds with the same normal $2$-type have homotopy equivalent spaces of positive scalar curvature metrics, provided both spaces are nonempty. Smoothness, the $C^\infty$ topology, and the precise normal $2$-type convention require confirmation against the original source.
