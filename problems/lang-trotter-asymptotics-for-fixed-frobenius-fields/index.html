Math-832

Lang–Trotter Asymptotics for Fixed Frobenius Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E/{\mathbb{Q}}$ be an elliptic curve without complex multiplication, meaning that $\operatorname{End}_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}}(E)\cong{\mathbb{Z}}$, and let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field, that is, a degree-two extension of ${\mathbb{Q}}$ with no real embedding. For each rational prime $p$ of good reduction for $E$, write $E_p/{\mathbb{F}}_p$ for its reduction and define \[ a_p(E)=p+1-\#E_p({\mathbb{F}}_p). \] Let $\pi_p(E)\in{\mathbb{C}}$ be either root of the Frobenius polynomial \[ T^2-a_p(E)T+p. \] The field ${\mathbb{Q}}(\pi_p(E))$ is independent of the choice of root, since the two roots sum to $a_p(E)$. The reduction is called ordinary when $p\nmid a_p(E)$. For real $x>1$, define \[ \Pi_E(K;x)=\#\left\{p\le x : p\text{ is a rational prime of good ordinary reduction for }E,\quad {\mathbb{Q}}(\pi_p(E))\cong_{{\mathbb{Q}}}K\right\}. \] The supplied Lang--Trotter conjecture for fixed Frobenius fields asks whether, for every such $E$ and every such $K$, there exists a real constant $c(E,K)>0$, independent of $x$, such that \[ \Pi_E(K;x)\sim c(E,K)\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\log x}\qquad(x\to\infty), \] where $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm. Equivalently, the ratio of the left-hand side to the displayed expression tends to $1$. Strict positivity of $c(E,K)$ is part of the supplied conjecture; no additional restrictions on $E$ or $K$ are supplied. The source record labels this problem open and its statement corrected_verified; these are supplied curation labels, not independent mathematical verification.

Short English statement

For every elliptic curve over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ without complex multiplication and every imaginary quadratic field $K$, the conjecture predicts that the number of good ordinary primes up to $x$ whose Frobenius field is isomorphic to $K$ is asymptotic to a positive constant times $\sqrt{x}/\log x$. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
