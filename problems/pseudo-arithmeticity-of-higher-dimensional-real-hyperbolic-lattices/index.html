Pseudo-Arithmeticity of Higher-Dimensional Real-Hyperbolic Lattices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 4$, let \[ q_n(x)=x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2-x_{n+1}^2, \qquad \operatorname{PO}(n,1)=\operatorname{O}(q_n;{\mathbb{R}})/\{\pm I\}. \] A lattice in this real Lie group is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma$ for which $\Gamma\backslash\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$ has finite invariant Haar volume. No torsion-free or cocompactness hypothesis is imposed in the supplied statement.

The question recorded in the source is: \[ \text{For every integer }n\geq4\text{ and every lattice } \Gamma\subset\operatorname{PO}(n,1), \quad\text{is }\Gamma\text{ pseudo-arithmetic?} \] The supplied record does not define pseudo-arithmetic. Its intended technical meaning must be recovered from the cited source before this question becomes a fully specified mathematical statement. The displayed formulation preserves the explicitly stated real-hyperbolic target; the accompanying literature assessment instead concerns complex-hyperbolic lattices. Those formulations cannot be identified from the supplied evidence. The record also does not specify an identity-component convention for the ambient group; the notation above uses the full projective orthogonal group.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every finite-covolume discrete subgroup of the real projective orthogonal group $\operatorname{PO}(n,1)$, in every integer dimension $n\geq4$, is pseudo-arithmetic. The technical definition of pseudo-arithmeticity is missing, and the accompanying complex-hyperbolic literature assessment does not match the stated real-hyperbolic setting.
