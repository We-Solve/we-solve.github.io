Math-1735

The dual Wilking bound and singular join tests

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a finite-dimensional positively curved Alexandrov space and $Y\subset X$ a totally geodesic subspace of codimension $k=\dim X-\dim Y\ge1$. In the usual compact, closed-subspace interpretation, the source asks whether \[ H_i(X\setminus Y;{\mathbb{Z}})=0\qquad\text{for all }i>2k-2. \] The source does not explicitly specify compactness, boundary, coefficients, connectedness or the precise intrinsic/extrinsic meaning of totally geodesic in the singular setting. Integral homology is an explicit formulation; other coefficients or reduced homology should be stated separately. In particular codimension one would allow nontrivial $H_0$ while requiring positive-degree vanishing.

The supplied aid separates two inputs behind the smooth result. For a closed oriented $n$-manifold $X$ and a suitable closed locally contractible subspace $Y$, Alexander--Lefschetz duality gives \[ H_i(X\setminus Y;R)\cong H^{n-i}(X,Y;R). \] If the relative groups on the right vanish in degrees $j\le n-2k+1$, the desired cutoff follows. Wilking's $(n-2k+1)$-connected inclusion supplies such relative vanishing in its manifold setting. For nonorientable manifolds the integral statement uses the orientation local system; ${\mathbb{Z}}/2$ avoids that issue. In singular Alexandrov spaces neither the inclusion-connectivity assertion nor this manifold duality can simply be assumed. A valid extension must supply the relevant relative-cohomology vanishing and an appropriate ambient duality, or another argument.

The aid proves a singular join special case. For a spherical join $X=A*B$ with $Y=A$ the indicated factor, the complement strongly deformation-retracts onto $B$, by increasing the join parameter to the $B$-end. Since \[ k=\dim B+1, \qquad H_i(X\setminus A;R)\cong H_i(B;R)=0\quad(i>k-1), \] this gives a stronger cutoff whenever the factors satisfy the Alexandrov/join hypotheses and homological dimension properties used in the construction. Those full factor hypotheses are not reproduced here.

The aid emphasizes the example $X=\operatorname{Susp}({\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^m)={\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^m*S^0$, $Y={\mathbb{C}}{\mathbb{P}}^m$, with the appropriately scaled positively curved metric, $m\ge1$. This equator can obstruct a naive extension of Wilking's inclusion-connectivity conclusion, but its complement consists of two contractible components. It therefore satisfies the codimension-one complement bound and is not a counterexample to the source question. The general Alexandrov complement bound remains open in the supplied account.

Short English statement

Ask whether removing a codimension-$k$ totally geodesic subspace leaves homology only through degree $2k-2$. The supplied reduction separates connectivity from duality, proves a stronger join-factor case, and explains why a projective-suspension connectivity counterexample does not refute the complement bound.
