Full dimension for the lattice solutions of Conjecture 33

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The record is the end of a contextual sentence saying that a solution set for Conjecture 33 has full Hausdorff dimension. The actual conjecture and its preceding definitions are absent. The following Section 5.5 sentence introduces a nondegenerate positive-definite quadratic form in dimension $d\geq3$ and is unrelated spillover, not a second complete problem.

The aid's lattice interpretation uses \[ X=\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{R}})/\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{Z}}),\qquad\dim X=8, \] and the positive determinant-one diagonal group $A$. For a fixed finite or countable family of nontrivial diagonal rays, let $F_j=\{\exp(tv_j):t\in{\mathbb{R}}\}\subset A$ be their underlying one-parameter subgroups and set \[ E=\bigcap_j\{x\in X:\overline{F_jx}\text{ is compact}\},\qquad E_A=\{x\in X:\overline{Ax}\text{ is compact}\}. \] Full-subgroup boundedness implies boundedness of each prescribed ray. "Bounded" here means relatively compact in the quotient, not eventual divergence or a particular coordinate norm.

The aid attributes to AGK the thickness assertion \[ \dim_H(E\cap U)=8\quad\text{for every nonempty open }U\subset X, \] and to EKL the dimension statement $\dim_HE_A=2$. Hausdorff dimension is computed in smooth local coordinates, equivalently using a compatible smooth Riemannian metric. These are substantive literature inputs with hypotheses requiring full reference verification.

It follows in the reported setting that $E\setminus E_A$ is thick and every point in it has bounded orbits under all prescribed $F_j$, but an unbounded full $A$-orbit. More robustly, if an obstruction set $Z$ satisfies $\dim_H(Z\cap U)<8$ on every nonempty open set under consideration, then \[ \dim_H\left((E\setminus(E_A\cup Z))\cap U\right)=8. \] Removing the lower-dimensional union cannot remove a full-dimensional part of $E\cap U$. This is a Hausdorff-dimension conclusion, not a positive-volume statement.

The family is prescribed and countable. No simultaneous boundedness for every ray in an uncountable direction family is inferred, even from a chosen dense countable family, without additional uniformity. Nor is an unbounded $A$-orbit asserted to diverge along every ray. The result is a robust-avoidance corollary for the supplied lattice model, not a reconstruction or solution of the missing full Conjecture 33.

Short English statement

The supplied aid combines a thick countable-ray boundedness set with a lower-dimensional full-diagonal bounded set. Their difference remains thick, even after another dimension-subcritical deletion, but this does not impose all uncountably many ray conditions.
