Functional equations and an Euler-product obstruction for mixed mock L-series

Definitions and mathematical statement

Definitions and notation.
A mixed mock modular form combines mock-modular and modular factors under a specified convention; its nonholomorphic completion encodes the shadow. For a Fourier expansion $f(\tau)=\sum a_nq^n$, an L-series is a Dirichlet series built from its coefficients, typically $\sum_{n\ge1}a_n n^{-s}$ in a region of convergence. Growth, cusps and completion may affect analytic continuation and functional equations.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Do the L-series of mixed mock modular forms have interesting properties?
Scope and source qualification.
The source asks about interesting properties without prescribing a single theorem. No Euler product, universal functional equation or convergence condition is silently imposed. Identify significant analytic or arithmetic properties of L-series attached to mixed mock modular forms.
