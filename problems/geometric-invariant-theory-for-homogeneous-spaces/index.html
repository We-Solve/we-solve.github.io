Geometric Invariant Theory for Homogeneous Spaces

Definitions and mathematical statement

A smooth homogeneous space is a smooth manifold $M$ equipped with a transitive smooth action of a Lie group $G$; choosing a point identifies $M$ with $G/H$, where $H$ is its closed stabilizer subgroup. A homogeneous Riemannian space additionally carries a $G$-invariant Riemannian metric. These definitions describe the differential-geometric setting suggested by the workshop context; the supplied question does not explicitly restrict its homogeneous spaces to this setting.

Geometric invariant theory studies algebraic group actions, their invariants, orbit structure, and quotient constructions. The source specifies no acting algebraic group, representation or variety, base field, or stability notion.

Research question. What additional tools from geometric invariant theory can be applied to obtain new information about the geometry of homogeneous spaces?
