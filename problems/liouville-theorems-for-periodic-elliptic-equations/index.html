Math-4945

Liouville Theorems for Periodic Elliptic Equations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A standard periodic elliptic framework takes an operator \[ L=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq2m}a_\alpha(x)\partial^\alpha \] on ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, with coefficients periodic under a full-rank lattice and, for a system, matrix-valued coefficients acting on ${\mathbb{C}}^r$. The exact coefficient regularity, ellipticity convention, reality/self-adjointness, and lower-order assumptions are not supplied by the source. Second-order scalar divergence form is one starting subclass, not an assumption on all requested extensions.

One common Liouville property in this setting is finite dimensionality of every polynomial-growth solution space \[ \mathcal V_N(L)=\left\{u:Lu=0,\ \sup_x(1+|x|)^{-N}\|u(x)\|<\infty\right\}, \qquad\dim\mathcal V_N(L)<\infty\quad(N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}), \] with the appropriate classical or weak solution class. The record does not explicitly define "the Liouville theorem," so this convention must be checked against the original context. It is not the same assertion as saying every bounded solution is constant; constants need not even solve an operator with arbitrary lower-order terms.

The corrected source asks the following linked questions:

  1. Which Liouville results extend beyond second-order scalar divergence-form equations to other periodic scalar equations, higher-order operators, and matrix-valued systems?
  2. What criteria decide whether a given periodic elliptic equation has the intended property?
  3. Can the decision be effective? An algorithmic version must specify how the coefficients and hypotheses are encoded, with a finite or computable input model; a qualitative criterion alone does not supply an algorithm.
  4. Can analogous criteria be obtained for elliptic operators on abelian coverings? A standard model is a regular covering of a compact base with deck group ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$, an invariant operator, and polynomial growth measured in a deck word metric or a compatible lifted metric. The exact cover and growth conventions remain to be fixed.
The question does not demand a single unsupported theorem covering every operator in these classes, nor a particular formula for the dimensions.

The supplied literature triage dated 21 August 2026 reports necessary-and-sufficient Liouville criteria by Kuchment--Pinchover for broad periodic elliptic classes and systems, but says the extracted request extends beyond those stated results. The actual theorem hypotheses and criterion are not reproduced, so no universal higher-order/matrix criterion or effective procedure is asserted here. The record retains its supplied partial-resolution assessment.

Short English statement

The source seeks Liouville criteria beyond scalar divergence-form equations, including higher-order systems and abelian covers, and asks for effective decisions. The growth-based property, operator hypotheses, and algorithmic input model need to be specified before a single theorem can be stated.
