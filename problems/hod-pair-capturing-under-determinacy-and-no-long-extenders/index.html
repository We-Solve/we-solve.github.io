HOD-Pair Capturing Under Determinacy and No Long Extenders

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record uses $\mathrm{AD}^{+}$ for a strengthened axiom of determinacy, $\mathrm{NLE}$ for the no-long-extenders hypothesis described below, and $\mathrm{HPC}$ for HOD-pair capturing. Here HOD denotes the class of hereditarily ordinal-definable sets. The record does not supply the axiom package defining $\mathrm{AD}^{+}$ or the quantified definition of HOD-pair capturing.

As stated in the source record, $\mathrm{NLE}$ asserts that there is no countable pure extender mouse containing a long extender that admits an $\omega_1$-iteration strategy. Its logical form is \[ \neg\exists M\,\exists\Sigma\;\left[ M\text{ is a countable pure extender mouse} \ \land\ M\text{ contains a long extender} \ \land\ \Sigma\text{ is an }\omega_1\text{-iteration strategy for }M \right]. \] The technical conventions for mice, long extenders, and iteration strategies, including the interpretation of iteration length $\omega_1$, are not specified in the supplied record.

Conjecture as recorded. Does the implication \[ \left(\mathrm{AD}^{+}\land\mathrm{NLE}\right) \ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{HPC} \] hold?

This preserves the recorded conjecture, but is not a self-contained formal statement: an underlying set-theoretic theory, the exact definition of $\mathrm{AD}^{+}$, the definition and quantifiers of HOD-pair capturing, and the relevant inner-model-theoretic conventions must be supplied before the implication can be formulated precisely. No additional hypotheses or conventions are imposed here.

Short English statement

The recorded conjecture asks whether strengthened determinacy together with the absence of an $\omega_1$-iterable countable pure extender mouse containing a long extender implies HOD-pair capturing. Essential technical definitions and background assumptions are missing from the supplied record, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
