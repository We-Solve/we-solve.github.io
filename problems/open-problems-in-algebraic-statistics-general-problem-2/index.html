Open Problems in Algebraic Statistics — General Problem 2

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal M$ be a graphical statistical model with a specified collection of observed random variables and a specified collection of hidden random variables. Its observed model $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{obs}}$ consists of the distributions of the observed variables obtained by marginalizing the hidden variables in distributions belonging to $\mathcal M$. If a chosen algebraic realization places this observed model in an affine space $k^N$, define its model variety by \[ V_{\mathcal M}=\overline{\mathcal M_{\mathrm{obs}}}^{\mathrm{Zar}}\subseteq k^N, \] and its coordinate ring by $k[V_{\mathcal M}]=k[x_1,\ldots,x_N]/I(V_{\mathcal M})$. The field, ambient space, and algebraic realization are additional choices; the supplied record does not specify them or guarantee such a realization for every interpretation of a graphical model.

For terminology, if $W\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}^n_k$ is a projective variety over an algebraically closed field and $r\geq1$ is an integer, its $r$-th secant variety is \[ \sigma_r(W)=\overline{\bigcup_{w_1,\ldots,w_r\in W}\langle w_1,\ldots,w_r\rangle}^{\mathrm{Zar}}. \] This definition does not select the secant varieties intended by the source.

Research direction attributed to Sturmfels (2007), General Problem 2. Study the geometry and commutative algebra of graphical models with hidden random variables. Construct their associated varieties by gluing familiar secant varieties and by applying representation theory.

This is a research program rather than a fully quantified conjecture. The supplied record does not specify the class of graphs, probability distributions, state spaces, algebraic realizations, gluing operations, or representations. It therefore supplies no definite universal construction or equality of varieties to assert. Its introductory sentence refers to a particular open problem in $[\mathrm{ASCB},\text{ Conjecture }3.24]$ as a placeholder for this broader direction, but does not include that particular problem or the conjecture's statement.

Short English statement

Sturmfels asks for a geometric and commutative algebraic study of graphical models with hidden variables, using constructions from secant varieties and representation theory. The supplied passage gives a broad research direction; it does not specify enough data for a single precise conjecture. The referenced particular problem in $[\mathrm{ASCB},\text{ Conjecture }3.24]$ is absent.
