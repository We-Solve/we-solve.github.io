Theta Invariants and New Families of MRD Codes

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

For an $\mathbb F_{q^m}$-linear code $C\subseteq\mathbb F_{q^m}^{n}$ and a Galois automorphism $\theta$ fixing $\mathbb F_q$, put $S_i^\theta(C)=\sum_{j=0}^{i}\theta^j(C)$, $s_i^\theta(C)=\dim_{\mathbb F_{q^m}}S_i^\theta(C)$, and $\Delta_i^\theta(C)=s_{i+1}^\theta(C)-s_i^\theta(C)$. Rank distance is defined by expanding coordinates over $\mathbb F_q$. For $n\leq m$, a $k$-dimensional maximum-rank-distance (MRD) code has minimum rank distance $n-k+1$. Gabidulin codes are obtained by evaluating linearized polynomials at $\mathbb F_q$-linearly independent field elements.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Can the theta-dimension and theta-increment invariants of rank-metric codes characterize families of MRD codes other than Gabidulin codes, or yield new code constructions? [S2]

Use the dimensions of successive Galois sums to recognize or construct maximum-rank-distance codes beyond the Gabidulin family.
