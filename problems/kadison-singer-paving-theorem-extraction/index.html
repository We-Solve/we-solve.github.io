Math-878

Kadison–Singer Paving Theorem Extraction

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $m$ denote the number of vectors and $d$ their finite complex dimension in the supplied record. For $u\in{\mathbb{C}}^d$, let $u^*$ be its conjugate transpose, so that $uu^*$ is the operator $x\mapsto u(u^*x)$. Write $I_d$ for the identity operator, use the Euclidean norm for vectors, and use the induced operator norm for matrices.

The supplied assertion is as follows. For every positive integer $r$, every real number $\delta>0$, and every family $u_1,\ldots,u_m\in{\mathbb{C}}^d$ satisfying \[ \sum_{i=1}^{m}u_i u_i^*=I_d, \qquad \|u_i\|^2\leq\delta\quad(1\leq i\leq m), \] there exist subsets $S_1,\ldots,S_r\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}$ such that \[ \bigcup_{j=1}^{r}S_j=\{1,\ldots,m\}, \qquad S_j\cap S_k=\varnothing\quad(j\neq k), \] and \[ \left\|\sum_{i\in S_j}u_i u_i^*\right\| \leq \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt r}+\sqrt\delta\right)^2 \quad\text{for every }j\in\{1,\ldots,r\}. \] Here the subsets are interpreted as a labeled partition in which empty parts are permitted, and an empty sum is the zero operator. This convention is required to accommodate unrestricted $r$, including $r>m$, but is not explicit in the source. Positivity of $r$ is required by the displayed bound; the record does not specify whether zero values of $m$ or $d$ are permitted.

The displayed assertion is transcribed from the supplied statement, not presented as a verified open problem. The supplied verification describes it as a proved paving theorem and marks the extraction unrecoverable, with no separate clean statement promoted. In contrast, the registry retains an open label, and its dated literature assessment says that the extracted corollary is truncated before its conclusion, although the supplied statement contains a conclusion. These incompatible qualifications leave the intended open-problem target unresolved.

Short English statement

The supplied assertion asks for a partition of a finite family of complex vectors whose rank-one operators sum to the identity, with each part satisfying the stated operator-norm bound. Empty parts are permitted in this formulation. The record conflicts about completeness and status: it retains an open label while separately identifying the assertion as a proved theorem. No distinct open question is recoverable from the supplied evidence.
