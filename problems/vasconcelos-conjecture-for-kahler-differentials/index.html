Vasconcelos Conjecture for Kähler Differentials

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $S$ be a commutative Noetherian $k$-algebra essentially of finite type, meaning that $S\cong U^{-1}A$ for a finitely generated commutative $k$-algebra $A$ and a multiplicatively closed subset $U\subseteq A$. The module of Kähler differentials $\Omega^1_{S/k}$ is the $S$-module equipped with the universal $k$-linear derivation ${\,\mathrm{d}}:S\to\Omega^1_{S/k}$. Finite projective dimension means that, for some integer $d\geq 0$, there is an exact sequence \[ 0\longrightarrow P_d\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow\Omega^1_{S/k}\longrightarrow 0 \] with each $P_i$ a projective $S$-module. The ring $S$ is reduced if it has no nonzero nilpotent elements.

The supplied cleaned statement asks whether finite projective dimension of $\Omega^1_{S/k}$ forces $S$ to be a reduced complete intersection. The meaning of complete intersection is not specified. A precise candidate using the local meaning is the following question: \[ \operatorname{pd}_S\Omega^1_{S/k}<\infty \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left\{\begin{array}{l} S\text{ is reduced},\\ \text{for every }\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}S,\quad S_{\mathfrak p}\cong Q/(g_1,\ldots,g_c)\text{ for some regular local ring }Q\\ \text{and some }Q\text{-regular sequence }g_1,\ldots,g_c. \end{array}\right. \] Here the choices of $Q$, $c\geq0$, and the sequence may depend on $\mathfrak p$. A sequence is regular if its generated ideal is proper and each $g_i$ is a nonzerodivisor on $Q/(g_1,\ldots,g_{i-1})$; the empty sequence is allowed.

This local formulation is conditional on that interpretation of complete intersection. The supplied evidence does not establish whether the intended conclusion instead requires a single global complete intersection presentation. Moreover, the original extracted passage is a truncated conjecture fragment followed by a symmetric-group coinvariant-algebra example and a general setup for an essentially finite type homomorphism $\varphi:R\to S$ of commutative Noetherian rings. It does not itself state the displayed implication. Its reference to a conjecture known to hold more generally has no identifiable antecedent. The record separately labels the cleaned statement as corrected and verified, while its supplied dated literature assessment says that no definite claim can be identified from the extraction. These unresolved source qualifications prevent treating this candidate as a securely recovered original statement.

Short English statement

The supplied cleaned question asks whether a characteristic-zero algebra essentially of finite type must be reduced and a complete intersection whenever its module of Kähler differentials has finite projective dimension. The precise local interpretation is displayed above, but the intended meaning of complete intersection and the recovery of the conjecture from the truncated source remain unresolved.
