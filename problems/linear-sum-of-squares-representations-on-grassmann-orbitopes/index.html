Math-14491

Linear Sum-of-Squares Representations on Grassmann Orbitopes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether every calibration of every Grassmann orbitope admits a sum-of-squares certificate with linear summands. It does not specify the admissible integer pairs $(k,n)$, the ambient coordinates, or the precise vanishing ideal. Accordingly, the following is a conditional formulation of the requested certificate, pending those specifications.

Under a real-coordinate interpretation, let $V_{k,n}$ be the intended finite-dimensional real ambient vector space, let $G_{k,n}\subseteq V_{k,n}$ be the intended Grassmann orbitope, and let ${\mathbb{R}}[V_{k,n}]$ denote its ambient polynomial ring. Let $I_{k,n}\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}[V_{k,n}]$ be the intended vanishing ideal. For a linear functional $\ell\in V_{k,n}^{*}$, a supporting value $\delta\in{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfies \[ \ell(p)\leq\delta\quad\text{for every }p\in G_{k,n}, \qquad \ell(p_0)=\delta\quad\text{for some }p_0\in G_{k,n}. \] Thus $f=\delta-\ell$ is nonnegative on $G_{k,n}$ and vanishes at a supporting point.

Certificate question. For every admissible $(k,n)$ and every supporting pair $(\delta,\ell)$ corresponding to a calibration in the source's convention, do there exist a finite integer $m\geq0$ and permitted linear polynomials $h_1,\ldots,h_m$ such that \[ \delta-\ell-\sum_{i=1}^{m}h_i^2\in I_{k,n}? \] Equivalently, writing brackets for classes in the quotient ring, the requested identity is \[ [\delta-\ell]=\sum_{i=1}^{m}[h_i]^2 \quad\text{in }{\mathbb{R}}[V_{k,n}]/I_{k,n}. \] If "linear" means affine linear, the permitted representatives are $h_i(p)=a_i+\lambda_i(p)$, with $a_i\in{\mathbb{R}}$ and $\lambda_i\in V_{k,n}^{*}$. If it means homogeneous linear, one must instead require $a_i=0$. The supplied record does not resolve this distinction.

Unresolved qualifications. The source also writes the certificate as a pointwise identity $\delta-\ell(p)=\sum_i h_i(p)^2$ on $G_{k,n}$. A congruence modulo a vanishing ideal gives an identity on the set defining that ideal; its identification with an identity on the entire orbitope requires clarification. In particular, the ideal of the convex orbitope and the ideal of a Grassmannian or generating orbit must not be identified without justification. The source does not define "calibration," specify its normalization, or establish here which supporting pairs it includes. Its assertion that this question is equivalent to Harvey--Lawson Question 6.5 is retained as a supplied attribution, not as an independently verified equivalence.

Short English statement

For every Grassmann orbitope, the supplied question asks whether each supporting nonnegative function associated with a calibration is a sum of squares of linear polynomial classes modulo the intended vanishing ideal. A precise unconditional statement requires the parameter range, coordinate model, calibration convention, meaning of "linear," and defining set of the ideal. The record labels the problem open; this draft preserves that supplied label without independently verifying it.
