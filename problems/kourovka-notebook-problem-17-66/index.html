Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.66

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group and $k$ a field. A $kG$-module $V$ is faithful if its action homomorphism $G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V)$ is injective, and absolutely irreducible if $\overline{k}\otimes_k V$ is irreducible over $\overline{k}G$, where $\overline{k}$ is an algebraic closure of $k$. Define \[ Z^1(G,V)=\{f:G\to V: f(gh)=f(g)+g\cdot f(h)\text{ for all }g,h\in G\}, \] \[ B^1(G,V)=\{f_v:G\to V: f_v(g)=g\cdot v-v\text{ for some }v\in V\}, \qquad H^1(G,V)=Z^1(G,V)/B^1(G,V). \] Dimensions are taken over the coefficient field $k$.

The source does not specify the coefficient field, its characteristic, or whether it is fixed or varies. To expose this unresolved qualification, let $\mathcal K$ denote the intended, unspecified class of coefficient fields. For a specified choice of $\mathcal K$, the question is whether \[ \exists d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\ \forall k\in\mathcal K\ \forall\text{ finite groups }G\ \forall\text{ faithful absolutely irreducible }kG\text{-modules }V, \qquad \dim_k H^1(G,V)\le d. \] Thus the bound must be independent of $G$ and $V$, and also independent of $k$ if coefficient fields vary. The choice of $\mathcal K$ remains unresolved; the supplied record does not determine a fully specified coefficient-field quantifier. The source also directs the reader to Archive Problem 16.55, whose statement is not supplied.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a single positive integer bounds the dimension of the first cohomology of every finite group with coefficients in any faithful absolutely irreducible module. The coefficient-field scope is unspecified and requires clarification. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
