Math-3926

Uniform Diameter Bounds for Finite Simple Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group with identity $1_G$. For a generating subset $A\subseteq G$, put $S=A\cup A^{-1}$. Under the undirected Cayley graph convention, $\Gamma(G,S)$ has vertex set $G$, with an edge between $g$ and $gs$ for $s\in S$. Its diameter is \[ \operatorname{diam}\Gamma(G,S) =\max_{g\in G}\min\{\ell\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:g=s_1\cdots s_\ell,\ s_i\in S\}, \] where the product of length zero is $1_G$. Define the worst generating-set diameter by \[ D(G)=\max_{\substack{A\subseteq G\\\langle A\rangle=G}} \operatorname{diam}\Gamma(G,A\cup A^{-1}). \] The source does not specify whether its Cayley graphs are directed or whether generating sets are assumed symmetric; the convention above makes the undirected interpretation explicit.

For a positive integer $n$, let $\operatorname{Sym}_n$ be the group of permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, and let $\operatorname{Alt}_n$ be its subgroup of even permutations. For a field $K$, write \[ \operatorname{SL}_n(K)=\{M\in\operatorname{Mat}_{n\times n}(K):\det M=1\}. \] The source writes $\operatorname{SL}_n$ without specifying a field, its size, or a quotient. The permutation and special linear groups mentioned as models are not all finite nonabelian simple groups; in particular, $\operatorname{Alt}_n$ is nonabelian simple for $n\geq5$, whereas no blanket simplicity assertion applies to the other named families.

Source problem. For finite nonabelian simple groups $G$, develop a common framework for bounds on the diameter of every generating Cayley graph. Determine which growth arguments and proof notions for $\operatorname{Sym}_n$ and $\operatorname{Alt}_n$ can serve as models for $\operatorname{SL}_n$ and, more broadly, linear algebraic groups, and which notions from the two settings can be formulated together. The supplied workshop question is a methodological research problem: it specifies neither a numerical bound nor a criterion that would uniquely determine a successful common framework. Its reference to ideas from "the proof" does not identify that proof.

Qualified quantitative interpretation. The supplied dated assessment describes the problem as a uniform Babai diameter and framework problem. A possible precise quantitative target under the undirected convention is \[ \exists C>0\ \exists d>0\ \forall G\text{ finite nonabelian simple},\qquad D(G)\leq C(\log |G|)^d, \] with constants independent of the group and the generating set. This formula is an interpretation of the assessment, not an explicit formula in the supplied workshop statement; it does not replace the separate request for transferable ideas and a common framework. The assessment also reports polylogarithmic bounds in bounded-rank Lie-type cases and unresolved alternating-group and unbounded-rank classical-group cases. These are supplied dated literature qualifications, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a common approach to bounding Cayley graph diameters of finite nonabelian simple groups, transferring ideas between permutation groups and linear algebraic groups. The source leaves the desired bound, graph convention, underlying field for $\operatorname{SL}_n$, and meaning of a successful framework unspecified. A uniform polylogarithmic bound is recorded only as a qualified interpretation of the supplied literature assessment.
