Compactifiable Gravitational Instantons

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $M=X\setminus D$ for the complex surface appearing in the source record. The intended objects are compactifiable gravitational instantons, but the record does not specify the hypotheses on $X$ and $D$, the permitted compactifications, or a definition of a gravitational-instanton metric. These choices must remain unresolved; in particular, completeness, curvature integrability, asymptotic conditions, and compatibility with the given complex structure cannot be silently supplied.

For clarity, a hyperkähler structure on a smooth real four-manifold consists of a Riemannian metric $g$ and parallel complex structures $I,J,K$, each orthogonal for $g$, satisfying \[ I^2=J^2=K^2=IJK=-\operatorname{id}. \] For such a structure, a hyperkähler rotation replaces the complex structure by \[ I_{a,b,c}=aI+bJ+cK, \qquad (a,b,c)\in{\mathbb{R}}^3,\qquad a^2+b^2+c^2=1. \] A biholomorphism is a holomorphic bijection with holomorphic inverse.

Classification problem. Classify the complex surfaces $X\setminus D$ carrying gravitational-instanton metrics, up to biholomorphism and hyperkähler rotation. The original formulation asks whether every such surface becomes biholomorphic, after hyperkähler rotation, to one of the known examples.

Schematically, if $\mathcal C$ denotes the intended class of admissible complements carrying such metrics and $\mathcal K$ denotes the intended collection of known examples, the proposed exhaustiveness assertion is \[ \forall M\in\mathcal C\;\exists N\in\mathcal K:\quad M\text{ is biholomorphic to }N\text{ after hyperkähler rotation}. \] This is a schematic statement requiring further source clarification: neither $\mathcal C$ nor $\mathcal K$ is specified sufficiently in the record to make it a fully determined conjecture. The record also does not settle whether the rotation assertion concerns every compatible gravitational-instanton metric on $M$, or the existence of a suitable such metric and hyperkähler structure.

Short English statement

Classify compactifiable complex surfaces carrying gravitational-instanton metrics, allowing biholomorphisms and changes of complex structure by hyperkähler rotation. The source asks whether known examples exhaust this class, but does not specify the admissible compactifications, metric hypotheses, or comparison examples. The supplied open label and literature assessment require independent review.
