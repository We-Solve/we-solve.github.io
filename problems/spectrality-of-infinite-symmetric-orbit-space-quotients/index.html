Spectrality of Infinite Symmetric Orbit-Space Quotients

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a topological space $X$, define $x\sim y$ if, for every open subset $U\subseteq X$, one has $x\in U$ if and only if $y\in U$. Its Kolmogorov quotient is $\operatorname{KQ}(X)=X/{\sim}$, endowed with the quotient topology.

A topological space $Y$ is spectral if it is $T_0$, is quasi-compact, has a basis of quasi-compact open subsets closed under finite intersections, and every nonempty irreducible closed subset has a unique generic point. Here quasi-compact means that every open cover has a finite subcover; a nonempty closed subset is irreducible if it is not the union of two proper closed subsets; and a generic point of a closed subset $C$ is a point $y$ with $\overline{\{y\}}=C$.

The supplied question is \[ \text{Is }\operatorname{KQ}\!\left(A_{\mathbb K,\infty}^{n}/G\right)\text{ a spectral space?} \] This question is retained with the intended objects and topology of the cited source, which are not defined in the supplied record. In particular, the record does not specify the allowed $\mathbb K$, the range of $n$, the meaning of $A_{\mathbb K,\infty}^{n}$, the group $G$ or its action, or the topology on $A_{\mathbb K,\infty}^{n}/G$. Consequently, the displayed question is a source-dependent formulation: its parameter quantifiers and hypotheses cannot be made explicit from the supplied evidence.
