Multiple ergodic averages — Problem 20

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the commuting-transformation setting identified by the research summary, let $(X,\mathcal B,\mu)$ be a probability space, let $T_1,\ldots,T_\ell$ be commuting measure-preserving transformations, and let $f_0,\ldots,f_\ell$ be bounded measurable complex-valued functions. Put $T_i^nf_i=f_i\circ T_i^n$ and \[ a(n)=\int_X f_0\prod_{i=1}^{\ell}T_i^nf_i\,\mathrm d\mu, \qquad n\ge1. \] These are the usual system and function hypotheses needed to read the extracted formula; the formula itself does not explicitly list them. Invertibility and any further source-wide assumptions require confirmation; no ergodicity hypothesis is added.

Under the standard uniform-limit convention, an $\ell$-step nilsequence is a uniform limit of basic sequences $n\mapsto F(g^nx_0)$, where $G/\Gamma$ is a compact nilmanifold with $G$ nilpotent of step at most $\ell$, $g\in G$, $x_0\in G/\Gamma$, and $F$ continuous. No connectedness restriction is added. The record does not spell out whether this or a basic-nilsequence convention is intended.

The question is whether for every such $\ell$, system, and choice of functions there exist an $\ell$-step nilsequence $\psi$ and a sequence $e$ such that, for every $n\ge1$, \[ a(n)=\psi(n)+e(n),\qquad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|e(n)|=0. \] The error condition is absolute Cesàro nullity along initial intervals. It is stronger than merely having a signed mean zero and does not assert uniform nullity over all translated intervals.

Short English statement

Does every commuting-transformation multiple-correlation sequence split into an $\ell$-step nilsequence and an error vanishing in absolute Cesàro mean? The exact initial-interval error condition and the nilpotency-step bound are retained.

The research summary reports a 2025 solution by J. Leng, citing Structured extensions and multi-correlation sequences. It says this was checked through the author's November 2025 progress page, while the preprint itself could not be fetched under network restrictions. The background instead says that same progress page does not record a resolution of Problem 20. These conflicting provenance statements are unresolved. The registry's solved label and SOLVED-IN-LITERATURE classification are reported without claiming an independent theorem check.
