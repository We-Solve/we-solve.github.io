Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns an acoustic-scattering obstacle with Lipschitz boundary $\Gamma$, a wavenumber $k$, a coupling parameter $\eta$, and the boundary integral operator \[ A_{k,\eta}=I+K'-i\eta S, \qquad i^2=-1. \] Here $I$ is the identity. The symbols $S$ and $K'$ conventionally denote the Helmholtz single-layer boundary operator and the adjoint double-layer boundary operator, respectively; their kernels, normalizations, normal orientation, and precise realizations are not specified in the supplied record.

To express a possible precise coercivity target, suppose a complex Hilbert space $H$ of boundary densities has been specified, with inner product linear in its first argument, and suppose the displayed expression defines a bounded operator $A_{k,\eta}:H\to H$. Real-part coercivity on this realization means \[ \exists c>0\quad\forall\varphi\in H:\qquad \operatorname{Re}\langle A_{k,\eta}\varphi,\varphi\rangle_H \ge c\|\varphi\|_H^2. \] Under this explicitly conditional interpretation, the problem is to determine conditions on $k$, $\eta$, the obstacle geometry, and the Lipschitz boundary $\Gamma$ for which this inequality holds. For a fixed configuration, $c$ must be independent of $\varphi$; the record does not specify whether uniformity in frequency, coupling, or a family of obstacles is required.

The source record does not identify the ambient dimension, admissible parameter domains, obstacle topology or boundedness, acoustic boundary condition, fundamental solution and radiation convention, density space, or pairing. It also does not specify whether "coercive" means the real-part inequality above, an inequality after a fixed complex rotation, or a modulus inequality. These choices must be recovered from the source before a definitive statement can be fixed. The supplied background discusses moving and deforming bodies, whereas the displayed question supplies no time dependence or deformation variables; their intended relationship is unresolved.

Short English statement

Determine when the acoustic boundary integral operator $I+K'-i\eta S$ is coercive, in terms of the wavenumber, coupling parameter, and obstacle geometry with Lipschitz boundary.
