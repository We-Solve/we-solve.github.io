Math-1138

Integrality of Rigid Irreducible Local Systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a connected smooth quasi-projective variety over ${\mathbb{C}}$, let $x\in X({\mathbb{C}})$, and let $X\hookrightarrow\overline X$ be a normal-crossing compactification, with boundary $D=\overline X\setminus X$. Write $\pi_1^{\mathrm{top}}(X,x)$ for the fundamental group of the associated complex analytic space. For an integer $n\geq 1$, a representation \[ \rho:\pi_1^{\mathrm{top}}(X,x)\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{C}}) \] is irreducible if the only $\rho$-invariant complex linear subspaces of ${\mathbb{C}}^n$ are $0$ and ${\mathbb{C}}^n$. For each irreducible component $D_i$ of the boundary, let $T_i$ denote the monodromy of $\rho$ around a small loop encircling a general smooth point of $D_i$, transported to the base point. Its conjugacy class is independent of the choices relevant here. Quasi-unipotent local monodromy means that for each $i$ there exists an integer $m_i\geq 1$ such that \[ (T_i^{m_i}-I_n)^n=0. \] For a number field $K$, its ring of integers $\mathcal O_K$ consists of the elements of $K$ integral over ${\mathbb{Z}}$.

Source-attributed conjectural statement (Simpson's integrality conjecture). For every such $X,x,\overline X$, every integer $n\geq 1$, and every rigid irreducible representation $\rho$ with quasi-unipotent local monodromies, do there exist a number field $K$, an embedding $\iota:K\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{C}}$, and a matrix $g\in\operatorname{GL}_n({\mathbb{C}})$ such that \[ g\rho(\gamma)g^{-1}\in\operatorname{GL}_n\left(\iota(\mathcal O_K)\right) \qquad\text{for every }\gamma\in\pi_1^{\mathrm{top}}(X,x)? \] Here the field, its embedding, and the conjugating matrix may depend on $\rho$, but must work simultaneously for every $\gamma$.

Short English statement

The supplied statement, attributed to Simpson, asks whether every rigid irreducible complex representation with quasi-unipotent boundary monodromy can be conjugated so that all its matrices lie in the ring of integers of one number field. The meaning of rigidity and any hypotheses hidden in the original phrase "as above" require clarification.
