Consensus Times on Erdős–Rényi Random Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 1$, let $p_n$ satisfy $0\leq p_n/n\leq 1$, and let $G_n\sim G(n,p_n/n)$ be the Erdős--Rényi graph on $V_n=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, with each unordered pair of distinct vertices independently joined with probability $p_n/n$. The source does not specify a further regime for $p_n$.

Let $X_t(v)$ denote the opinion at vertex $v$. Following the supplied reconstructed statement, take the initial opinions to be pairwise distinct, represented by $X_0(v)=v$. A voter update replaces the opinion at a vertex by the opinion at a neighboring vertex. The rule for selecting updates, the clock rates, and the treatment of isolated vertices must be specified to determine the stochastic process; these are absent from the supplied extract.

For a specified discrete-time voter dynamics $X^{\mathrm d}$, define \[ T_n^{\mathrm d}=\inf\left\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:\exists i\in V_n\ \forall v\in V_n,\ X_k^{\mathrm d}(v)=i\right\}. \] For a specified continuous-time voter dynamics $X^{\mathrm c}$, define \[ T_n^{\mathrm c}=\inf\left\{t\in[0,\infty):\exists i\in V_n\ \forall v\in V_n,\ X_t^{\mathrm c}(v)=i\right\}. \] In both definitions, $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. Equivalently, if $V_i(t)=\{v\in V_n:X_t(v)=i\}$, consensus means that $V_i(t)=V_n$ for some $i$. This gives a precise reading of the consensus event in the original extract.

Requested asymptotic problem. Determine, as $n\to\infty$, the asymptotic consensus times $T_n^{\mathrm d}$ and $T_n^{\mathrm c}$ as functions of the sequence $p_n$, with pairwise distinct initial opinions.

Unresolved qualifications. This request is a family of incompletely specified asymptotic questions. The source does not identify the update conventions, time normalization, density regimes, or whether the desired result concerns expectations, typical orders, limiting distributions, or another asymptotic criterion. It also does not specify whether graph randomness is averaged over or conditioned upon. With neighbor-copying dynamics and distinct initial opinions, disconnected components cannot exchange opinions, so consensus on all of $V_n$ is impossible on a disconnected graph. The source does not state whether to retain this infinite consensus time, condition on connectivity, or study a specified component. No such choice is made here.

Short English statement

Starting with a different opinion at every vertex of $G(n,p_n/n)$, determine how the time until every vertex has the same opinion scales with $n$ and $p_n$, in discrete and continuous time. A precise asymptotic target requires additional choices about update rules, clock normalization, disconnected graphs, and the probabilistic meaning of the asymptotics. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
