Math-7577

Arnold Conjecture on Hamiltonian Fixed Points

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A symplectic manifold $(M,\omega)$ carries a closed nondegenerate two-form. A Hamiltonian diffeomorphism is the time-one map of a time-dependent vector field $X_{H_t}$ satisfying $\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-{\,\mathrm{d}} H_t$. For a closed connected $M$, put \[ \operatorname{Crit}(M)=\min_{f\in C^\infty(M,{\mathbb{R}})}\#\{x:{\,\mathrm{d}} f(x)=0\}. \] The selected assertion is $\#\operatorname{Fix}(\phi)\ge\operatorname{Crit}(M)$ for every Hamiltonian diffeomorphism $\phi$. This minimum permits degenerate critical points; it is not the minimum restricted to Morse functions. Likewise the fixed points need not be nondegenerate. An infinite fixed-point set satisfies the cardinal lower bound automatically.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Does every Hamiltonian motion on a closed symplectic manifold leave at least as many points fixed as any smooth real function on that manifold must have critical points?
