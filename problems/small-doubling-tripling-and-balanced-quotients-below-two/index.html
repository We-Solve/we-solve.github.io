Math-1372

Small doubling, tripling, and balanced quotients below two

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a finite nonempty subset of an arbitrary group $G$, with no commutativity, symmetry, or identity-inclusion assumption. Write $A^j=\{a_1\cdots a_j:a_i\in A\}$. T. Tao's original question asks whether small doubling forces small tripling, quantitatively whether a bound $|A^2|\leq K|A|$ controls $|A^3|/|A|$ by a function of $K$ alone. The source records that Ben Green and Mei-Chu Chang pointed out this is false, using $A=H\cup\{x\}$ with $H$ a finite subgroup and $x$ outside its normalizer. Here $|A^2|\leq3|H|+1$, whereas $A^3$ contains $HxH$, whose cardinality is $|H|[H:H\cap xHx^{-1}]$. A quantitative unbounded-growth family needs large conjugate index; non-normality alone is not a numerical growth estimate.

The source instead asks for a large subset with small tripling, and more ambitiously for inverse structure, especially under $|A^2|<2|A|$. The supplied aid reports the explicit corrected subset theorem: \[ \forall K\geq1:\quad |A^2|\leq K|A| \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists A'\subseteq A:\quad |A'|\geq |A|/K,\qquad |(A')^3|\leq K^3|A'|. \] The conclusion concerns tripling of the selected subset, not tripling of the whole original set.

Its strict-threshold partial structural statement sets $d=2|A|-|A^2|>0$ and defines ordered quotient multiplicities \[ r_{A^{-1}A}(g)=\#\{(a,b)\in A^2:a^{-1}b=g\},\qquad r_{AA^{-1}}(g)=\#\{(a,b)\in A^2:ab^{-1}=g\}. \] The aid claims \[ A^{-1}A=AA^{-1}=:Q,\qquad r_{A^{-1}A}(g)\geq d,\quad r_{AA^{-1}}(g)\geq d\quad(g\in Q),\qquad |Q|\leq |A|^2/d. \] It describes the strict threshold as sharp and points to later weak-Kneser and critical-pair theorems for broader inverse structure, without supplying their precise hypotheses or classification. Thus these explicit conclusions are recorded separately from the open-ended request for an inverse theorem.

Short English statement

Small doubling need not control tripling of the whole set in a nonabelian group. The supplied aid gives a large-subset replacement and a balanced-quotient statement below the strict doubling threshold two, while its broader inverse-theorem references remain unspecified.
