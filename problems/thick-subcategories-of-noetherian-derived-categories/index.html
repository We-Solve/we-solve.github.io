Thick Subcategories of Noetherian Derived Categories

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, as in the supplied source. The source does not specify whether $A$ is commutative or whether modules are left or right modules; the constructions below are relative to a fixed module side, with the corresponding noetherian hypothesis. These choices require clarification before fixing the scope of the problem.

Write $D(A)$ for the unbounded derived category of the chosen category of $A$-modules. Two possible interpretations of the source notation are \[ D_{\mathrm{fg}}(A)=\{X\in D(A):H^n(X)\text{ is finitely generated for every }n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\} \] and \[ D^b_{\mathrm{fg}}(A)=\{X\in D_{\mathrm{fg}}(A):H^n(X)=0\text{ for all but finitely many }n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}. \] The source calls its category $D_f(A)$, describing finitely generated cohomology without explicitly requiring boundedness. Its reference to compact objects makes this omission material; neither interpretation is silently selected here.

Let $K(\operatorname{Inj}A)$ denote the homotopy category of unbounded cochain complexes of injective $A$-modules, whose morphisms are chain maps modulo chain homotopy. A thick subcategory of a triangulated category is a full subcategory closed under isomorphisms, shifts, distinguished triangles, and direct summands. A localizing subcategory is a full triangulated subcategory closed under isomorphisms and arbitrary set-indexed coproducts. An object $C$ in a triangulated category $\mathcal T$ with coproducts is compact if, for every set-indexed family $(X_i)_{i\in I}$, the canonical map \[ \bigoplus_{i\in I}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal T}(C,X_i) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal T}\!\left(C,\bigoplus_{i\in I}X_i\right) \] is an isomorphism. Write $\mathcal T^c$ for the full subcategory of compact objects. A localizing subcategory generated by compact objects means the smallest localizing subcategory containing some set of objects of $\mathcal T^c$.

Classification problem, with unresolved source qualifications. For every noetherian ring $A$ in the intended scope, classify the thick subcategories of the intended category $D_f(A)$. The supplied record also asks to classify all localizing subcategories of $K(\operatorname{Inj}A)$.

The record presents these requests as equivalent via compact objects. This equivalence requires qualification: an identification with compact objects does not by itself identify a classification of thick subcategories with a classification of all localizing subcategories. Localizing subcategories generated by ambient compact objects must be distinguished from arbitrary localizing subcategories. The record supplies neither a restriction to the former nor hypotheses ensuring that every localizing subcategory has that form. Thus the two classification requests are retained separately, and their asserted equivalence remains unresolved.

Short English statement

For a noetherian ring $A$, classify the thick subcategories of the source's derived category $D_f(A)$, and classify the localizing subcategories of the homotopy category of injective complexes. The module side, commutativity assumptions, cohomological boundedness, and claimed equivalence of these requests need clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
