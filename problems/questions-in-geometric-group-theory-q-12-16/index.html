Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 12.16

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedSolvedSolved

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_n$ be the free group of finite rank $n$, and let $h\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$. For a finite-index subgroup $K\leq F_n$ satisfying $h(K)=K$, write $K'=[K,K]$ for its commutator subgroup and \[ h_{K,\mathrm{ab}}:K/K'\longrightarrow K/K',\qquad xK'\longmapsto h(x)K'. \] Eigenvalues mean eigenvalues over ${\mathbb{C}}$ of the induced linear automorphism of $(K/K')\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{C}}$. A complex number $\zeta$ is a root of unity if $\zeta^m=1$ for some integer $m\geq1$.

Question. For every automorphism $h$ of $F_n$, does there exist $K\leq F_n$ such that \[ [F_n:K]<\infty,\qquad h(K)=K,\qquad \exists m\geq1\ \exists v\in(K/K')\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{C}}\setminus\{0\}: (h_{K,\mathrm{ab}}\otimes1)^m v=v? \] Thus the requested eigenvalue may become $1$ after passing to a positive power of the induced automorphism. The source does not state the rank range. The usual positive-rank interpretation is $n\geq1$; a universal statement allowing $n=0$ has no nonzero abelianization vector and needs separate clarification.

For the supplied progress discussion, define the mapping-torus group and virtual first Betti number by \[ G_h=F_n\rtimes_h{\mathbb{Z}},\qquad \operatorname{vb}_1(G_h)=\sup_{[G_h:L]<\infty}\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H_1(L;{\mathbb{Q}}). \] The registry labels the question solved and claims a positive answer for every $h$ and every $F_n$, using an asserted equivalence with $\operatorname{vb}_1(G_h)\geq2$. It further claims $\operatorname{vb}_1(G_h)=\infty$ for $n\geq2$, citing Brinkmann's hyperbolicity criterion, Button's largeness theorem in the ${\mathbb{Z}}^2$ case, and Hagen--Wise together with Agol in the hyperbolic case. This is retained as a supplied synthesis: its author explicitly reports finding no publication drawing this precise corollary, and says that the combination was checked by that author. This draft does not independently verify the equivalence, the case split, or that combination.

Short English statement

Does every free-group automorphism preserve a finite-index subgroup on whose abelianization it has a root-of-unity eigenvalue? The registry reports a positive answer assembled from published ingredients, while expressly stating that no publication of this precise corollary was found.
