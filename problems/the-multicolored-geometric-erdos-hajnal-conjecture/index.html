The Multicolored Geometric Erdős–Hajnal Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $q$ be a prime power, and let ${\mathbb{F}}_q$ be the field with $q$ elements. For a positive integer $m$, let $P_m=\operatorname{PG}(m-1,q)$ be the set of one-dimensional linear subspaces of ${\mathbb{F}}_q^m$. Write $[s]=\{1,\ldots,s\}$. An $s$-colouring is taken here to mean a map $c:P_m\to[s]$, without requiring every colour to occur.

To make the undefined term "copy" explicit, provisionally use colour-preserving linear embeddings: a copy of $c_0:P_k\to[s]$ in $c:P_n\to[s]$ is an injective ${\mathbb{F}}_q$-linear map $T:{\mathbb{F}}_q^k\to{\mathbb{F}}_q^n$ such that \[ c(T(L))=c_0(L)\qquad\text{for every }L\in P_k. \] Call $c$ $c_0$-free when no such map exists. The supplied record does not specify whether this is the intended notion of copy, whether semilinear embeddings are allowed, or whether colour labels may be permuted.

For a nonzero linear subspace $W\le{\mathbb{F}}_q^n$, put \[ \operatorname{PG}(W)=\{L\le W:\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}L=1\},\qquad d_{\mathrm{rank}}(W)=\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}W,\qquad d_{\mathrm{proj}}(W)=\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}W-1. \] The source does not specify which of these two dimensions it intends. For a fixed intended convention $D$, the supplied multicoloured geometric Erdős--Hajnal conjecture asks whether \[ \begin{gathered} \forall q\text{ a prime power}\quad \forall k,s\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}\quad \forall c_0:P_k\to[s]\quad \exists\delta,C\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}\quad \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}\quad \forall c:P_n\to[s],\\ c\text{ is }c_0\text{-free} \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\,0\ne W\le{\mathbb{F}}_q^n: \quad D(W)\ge Cn^\delta \quad\text{and}\quad \left|\{c(L):L\in\operatorname{PG}(W)\}\right|\le s-1. \end{gathered} \] Thus $C$ and $\delta$ may depend on $q,k,s,c_0$, but not on $n$ or $c$. This display records a conditional formalization, pending confirmation of the copy and dimension conventions; it does not quantify over both dimension conventions.

Unresolved boundary qualifications. The source states all natural-number parameters and all $n$, without an eventual-size restriction. Positive integers are used above because the displayed projective spaces require positive ambient vector-space dimensions. No restriction $s\ge2$ has been supplied: at $s=1$, the conclusion allows zero colours, although every nonempty projective subspace receives a colour. Also, projective dimension is zero for a point, so the projective-dimension interpretation requires clarification at $n=1$. These boundary issues must be resolved from the source rather than silently repaired.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether forbidding any fixed finite colouring of a projective geometry forces a subspace whose dimension is at least a positive power of the ambient parameter and on which some colour is absent. The constants may depend on the forbidden colouring and its parameters. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; the notions of copy, dimension, and the small-parameter boundary cases still require clarification.
