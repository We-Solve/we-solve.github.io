Problem 5.9 — (Cohomological Dimension).

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $G$, its integral cohomological dimension is \[\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(G)=\inf\{n:{\mathbb{Z}}\text{ has a length-}n\text{ projective resolution as a }{\mathbb{Z}} G\text{-module}\},\] with value $+\infty$ if no finite resolution exists. Equivalently it is the least bound on nonzero group cohomology degrees with arbitrary ${\mathbb{Z}} G$-module coefficients, not only trivial rational coefficients.

Let $\mathcal I_g$ be the Torelli group, the kernel of the action of $\operatorname{Mod}_g$ on $H_1(\Sigma_g;{\mathbb{Z}})$, and let $\mathcal K_g$ be its Johnson kernel in the source's convention. The problem asks to compute \[\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(\mathcal I_g),\qquad \operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(\mathcal K_g),\qquad \operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(\mathcal I_g(k))\quad\text{for every }k\geq1.\] The source omits the definition and indexing of $\mathcal I_g(k)$. The customary Johnson-filtration interpretation is the kernel of \[\operatorname{Mod}_g\to\operatorname{Out}\left(\pi_1\Sigma_g/\gamma_{k+1}(\pi_1\Sigma_g)\right),\] where $\gamma_1\pi=\pi$, $\gamma_{j+1}\pi=[\pi,\gamma_j\pi]$; this convention must be checked rather than assumed from the notation alone. The genus range also depends on context; the displayed ambient bound is the usual $g\geq2$ one.

The source supplies an upper-bound context: \[\operatorname{cd}_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(\mathcal I_g)\leq \operatorname{vcd}(\operatorname{Mod}_g)=4g-5.\] It then introduces lower bounds, but those subsequent bounds are absent. Computing the dimension of the ambient group is not a computation of the subgroup dimensions.

The research summary labels $\operatorname{vcd}(\operatorname{Mod}_g)=4g-5$ solved, attributes it to Harer with later Ivanov corrections, and reports nonvanishing with Steinberg coefficients, vanishing above that degree, and vanishing of top ordinary rational cohomology. Those coefficient-sensitive reports concern the ambient mapping class group and are not substituted for any of the requested dimensions. In particular the summary's SOLVED-IN-LITERATURE label does not match the subgroup question.

Short English statement

Compute the integral cohomological dimensions of Torelli, Johnson kernel and every specified filtration subgroup. The supplied ambient virtual-dimension theorem is only context and does not answer those subgroup questions.
