Math-4790

Orbit Closures of Full Cartan Torus Actions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $d\geq 1$, let $\mathbb T^d=\mathbb R^d/\mathbb Z^d$, with its quotient topology. An element $A\in\operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbb Z)$ induces the toral automorphism $x+\mathbb Z^d\mapsto Ax+\mathbb Z^d$. Call this automorphism totally irreducible if, for every integer $n\geq 1$, the linear map $A^n$ preserves no nonzero proper $\mathbb Q$-linear subspace of $\mathbb Q^d$. For an abelian group $H$, write $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}H=\dim_{\mathbb Q}(H\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q)$, regarding $H$ as a $\mathbb Z$-module.

The supplied normalized formulation considers abelian subgroups $G\leq\operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbb Z)$ with $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}G\geq 2$, containing a totally irreducible element, and maximal in rank among abelian subgroups containing them. Reading the last condition as maximality among supergroups of $G$, it means \[ \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}H\leq\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}G \quad\text{for every abelian }H\text{ with }G\leq H\leq\operatorname{SL}_d(\mathbb Z). \] The diagonal action is \[ g\cdot(x,y)=(gx,gy),\qquad g\in G,\quad (x,y)\in\mathbb T^d\times\mathbb T^d. \] Classification problem. For every such $d$ and $G$, classify all closed subsets of the form \[ \overline{G\cdot(x,y)} =\overline{\{(gx,gy):g\in G\}}, \qquad (x,y)\in\mathbb T^d\times\mathbb T^d, \] where closure is taken in the product topology.

Unresolved source qualification. The original wording asks this question for a "full Cartan action" by a group satisfying "Condition 1." Neither Condition 1 nor the source definition of full Cartan action is supplied. Consequently, equivalence with the normalized hypotheses above cannot be established from this record. The phrase "containing it" in the normalized statement also leaves the referent of the maximal-rank condition uncertain: the display above uses supergroups of $G$, whereas the wording could refer to abelian groups containing the totally irreducible element. This draft makes that reading explicit without resolving the source ambiguity.

Short English statement

Classify the closures of all diagonal orbits on the product of two identical tori under the stated abelian matrix actions. The original reference to Condition 1 and the precise maximal-rank hypothesis require source review. The supplied partially solved label and literature assessment are dated curation, not independently verified conclusions.
