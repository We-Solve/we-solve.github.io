Math-6735

Uniform Polynomial Bases for Stabilizer Modules

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)\vdash n$ be a partition, so that $\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_r>0$ and $\sum_i\lambda_i=n$. Its Young diagram consists of the boxes $(i,j)$ with $1\leq i\leq r$ and $1\leq j\leq\lambda_i$. A $\lambda$-tableau fills these boxes bijectively with $1,\ldots,n$. It is row-standard if its entries increase along each row, and standard if they also increase down each column. Write $\operatorname{RSt}(\lambda)$ and $\operatorname{St}(\lambda)$ for these two sets.

For a prime power $q$, let $\mathbb F_q$ be the field with $q$ elements, put $G=\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)$, and let \[ U=\{(u_{ij})\in G:u_{ii}=1\text{ for every }i,\quad u_{ij}=0\text{ whenever }i<j\} \] be the lower unitriangular subgroup. The source refers to a permutation module $M(\lambda)$ over a coefficient field $K$, a Specht module $S(\lambda)$, and a decomposition \[ \operatorname{Res}^{G}_{U}M(\lambda) =\bigoplus_{t\in\operatorname{RSt}(\lambda)}V_t. \] Here restriction means retaining the underlying vector space and restricting the group action to $U$. The supplied record does not specify the construction of these modules, the realization of $S(\lambda)$ inside $M(\lambda)$, the summands $V_t$, or the admissible coefficient fields. These are required source data, not additional hypotheses that this draft supplies.

Conditional on such a realization and decomposition, and on a specified total order $\prec$ on $\operatorname{RSt}(\lambda)$, write a nonzero vector $b\in S(\lambda)$ as $b=\sum_t b_t$, where $b_t\in V_t$. Its last nonzero component has index \[ \ell(b)=\max_{\prec}\{t:b_t\neq0\}. \] For a basis $B$ and $s\in\operatorname{St}(\lambda)$, put \[ B_s=\{b\in B:\ell(b)=s\}. \] The order and the meaning of a basis being adapted to the decomposition are not supplied; this notation therefore describes the intended construction only after those choices have been specified.

Question recorded in the source. For general partitions $\lambda\vdash n$, can one choose an adapted basis $B$ of $S(\lambda)$, independently of the coefficient field $K$, such that for every $s\in\operatorname{St}(\lambda)$ there is a polynomial $p_s\in\mathbb Z[x]$ satisfying \[ |B_s|=p_s(q),\qquad p_s(1)=1? \] Can this moreover be achieved with \[ p_s(x)\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}[x-1] =\left\{\sum_{j=0}^{d}a_j(x-1)^j:d\geq0,\ a_j\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\right\}? \] The intended uniform reading is that, for fixed $\lambda$ and $s$, the same polynomial works as $q$ and the admissible coefficient field vary. The record does not explicitly specify this quantifier scope, the admissible fields, or the mathematical meaning of choosing a basis independently of $K$; these qualifications remain unresolved. The equality $p_s(1)=1$ is a polynomial evaluation, not a definition of a group over a field with one element.

The supplied dated assessment calls this the James--D conjecture and its nonnegative-coefficient refinement, states that both are established for partitions with at most two parts, and labels the general partition case open because no complete proof was found in that curation. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

For a partition $\lambda$, the question asks for bases of the associated Specht modules whose vectors, grouped by their last nonzero tableau component, have cardinalities given by polynomials in $q$, with value $1$ at $q=1$. A stronger version requires nonnegative coefficients in powers of $q-1$. The bases should be independent of the coefficient field. The module constructions, admissible fields, tableau order, and precise independence requirement need clarification before the question is fully specified.
