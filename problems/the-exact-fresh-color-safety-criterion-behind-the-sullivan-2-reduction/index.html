The exact fresh-color safety criterion behind the Sullivan-2 reduction

Definitions and mathematical statement

This record is the final OCR-split fragment of a conditional argument following Conjecture 6.4, not an independent Conjecture 6.1. Companion records supply the missing definitions and preceding argument.

Let $G$ be a finite simple loopless digraph on $n\geq1$ vertices, with each arc carrying a set of old labels. A directed path or cycle is rainbow if distinct labels can be chosen for all its arcs, with no requirement that labels increase along it. Let $R_G^-(u)$ be the vertices other than $u$ from which an old-label rainbow path leads to $u$. Given a set $F$ of ordered pairs $(u,v)$ with $u\ne v$, add those arcs to the host if needed and give every pair in $F$ one common fresh label; if an arc already exists, augment its label set rather than making a parallel arc. The aid's exact safety criterion is: the augmented graph has a rainbow directed cycle if and only if $G$ already has one or some $(u,v)\in F$ closes an old-label rainbow path from $v$ to $u$.

Consequently, when $G$ has no rainbow directed cycle, the unique largest safe fresh-label set is \[ F_{\max}(G)=\{(u,v):u\ne v,\ v\notin R_G^-(u)\}. \] Every safe $F$ satisfies \[ d_F^+(u)\leq n-1-|R_G^-(u)|, \] with equality at every vertex exactly when $F=F_{\max}(G)$. The host must be enlarged when this set includes nonedges; the printed preceding declaration $G'=H$ does not support its full-complement count.

For the conditional application, let $H$ be a finite nonempty oriented graph (no loops, parallel arcs, or digons). Let $N_H^\pm(u)$ be its first neighborhoods and $N_H^{+2}(u)$, $N_H^{-2}(u)$ the vertices at exact directed distance two from, or to, $u$. Give every old arc both labels $1,2$. There is no rainbow cycle, and $R_G^-(u)=N_H^-(u)\mathbin{\dot\cup}N_H^{-2}(u)$. Adjoining $F_{\max}$ with label $3$ preserves rainbow-cycle-freeness and gives total labeled outdegree \[ \sum_{i=1}^3d_{G_i}^+(u) =n-1-|N_H^-(u)|-|N_H^{-2}(u)|+2|N_H^+(u)|. \] Assuming Seymour--Sullivan's colored Conjecture 6.1, which asserts a rainbow cycle or a vertex with rainbow out-reach at least its total labeled outdegree, one obtains a vertex $u$ with \[ |N_H^-(u)|+|N_H^{-2}(u)|\geq2|N_H^+(u)|. \] Indeed a strict reverse inequality at every vertex would make every total at least $n$, impossible for rainbow reach among at most $n-1$ other vertices. Applying this conclusion to the arc reversal of $H$ yields the intended Sullivan-2 assertion \[ \exists u:\quad |N_H^+(u)|+|N_H^{+2}(u)|\geq2|N_H^-(u)|. \] This remains a conditional implication; the safe-completion lemma does not prove the colored conjecture or Sullivan-2 unconditionally.

Short English statement

The record finishes a conditional reduction from a rainbow-reachability conjecture to Sullivan's second neighborhood inequality. A fresh label is safe exactly on arcs that close no old rainbow path. The source's host must include the required nonedges, and exact-distance neighborhoods and arc reversal must be kept explicit.
