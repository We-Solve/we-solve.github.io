Problem 5.5 — Explore ΓC for other free subgroups G< MCG (S), for example free subgroups generated by high powers of Dehn twists ab…

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the source's $G\leq\operatorname{MCG}(S)$, retain its surface-group extension \[ 1\longrightarrow\pi_1(S)\longrightarrow\Gamma_G\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow1. \] The quasi-isometry group $\operatorname{QI}(\Gamma_G)$ consists of self quasi-isometries of a word metric modulo finite uniform distance. Let $\operatorname{QI}_f(\Gamma_G)$ denote the subgroup represented by maps coarsely preserving the family of cosets of $\pi_1(S)$: the coset images, and those under a coarse inverse, lie at uniformly bounded Hausdorff distance from cosets. The exact source convention is not included.

The task is to explore or compute the source's group $\Gamma_C$ for other free subgroups $G$, including subgroups generated by high powers of twists about a pair of filling curves. Filling means every essential curve meets the pair, with the surface's boundary/puncture convention fixed. The construction of $C$ and $\Gamma_C$ is absent, so $\Gamma_C$ is not identified with an invented commensurator or extension.

The continuation explains that [Mos03a] gives a generalization of the missing Theorem 5.3 for nonfree $G$, involving $\operatorname{QI}_f$ rather than all quasi-isometries. It reports coarse algebraic-topology methods [FM00] giving $\operatorname{QI}_f=\operatorname{QI}$ in the free case, and Whyte's homological methods giving this equality for $G=\operatorname{MCG}(S)$ in Theorem 5.2. It suggests extending the latter methods to Bieri--Eckmann duality groups that are not Poincaré duality groups, with the mapping class group cited as an example [Har86]. The exact theorem hypotheses are missing.

For a Leininger--Reid subgroup, reported here to be a two-dimensional Poincaré duality group, the source says those methods fail and nevertheless asks to calculate \[ \Gamma_C=\operatorname{QI}_f(\Gamma_G). \] This final target does not assert that every quasi-isometry preserves fibers or that no other method can overcome the reported obstacle.

Short English statement

Compute the indicated quasi-isometric/extension group for further free subgroups and for Leininger--Reid surface subgroups, and investigate when all quasi-isometries preserve the surface-group cosets.
