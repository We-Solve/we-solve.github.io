Math-3198

Kirby Problem 1.75

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

Let $\mathcal U$ be the space of smooth unparametrized embedded circles in $S^3$ belonging to the unknot isotopy class, with the smooth topology; thus reparametrizations of $S^1$ are identified. Let $\mathcal G\subset\mathcal U$ be the subspace of great circles, the intersections of $S^3\subset\mathbb R^4$ with 2-dimensional linear subspaces. It is the Grassmannian $O(4)/(O(2)\times O(2))$. A knot energy is a reparametrization-invariant functional on smooth knots; a critical point has zero first variation. A deformation retraction is a continuous $H:\mathcal U\times[0,1]\to\mathcal U$ with $H(u,0)=u$, $H(u,1)\in\mathcal G$ and $H(g,s)=g$ for $g\in\mathcal G$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Construct a knot energy whose critical points in $\mathcal U$ are all great circles. Can a gradient flow for this energy be defined so that it gives a deformation retraction of $\mathcal U$ onto $\mathcal G$? The desired construction must apply to every smooth unknot, with the flow staying in its embedding class and its limiting retraction continuous. [S2]

Short English statement

Find a knot energy with no nongreat-circle unknot critical points, together with a gradient flow that explicitly retracts the entire smooth unknot space onto the great circles.
