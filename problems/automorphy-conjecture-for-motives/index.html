Math-121

Automorphy Conjecture for Motives

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a number field, $G$ a quasi-split connected reductive group over $F$, and $r$ the representation of its $L$-group specified in the report. Let $M$ be the intended motive, with local Langlands parameters $\varphi_v$ and corresponding packets $\Pi_{\varphi_v}$. The original explicitly refers to these data "as in the report"; compatibility of $G,r,M$, and the parameters is missing and cannot be replaced by an unrestricted choice of unrelated data.

The conjecture asks for an irreducible automorphic representation of $G(\mathbb A_F)$, \[ \pi=\bigotimes'_v\pi_v, \] with the usual restricted-tensor-product interpretation, satisfying all of \[ L(s,\pi,r)=L(M,s),\qquad \pi_v\in\Pi_{\varphi_v}\quad\text{for every place }v, \] \[ M\text{ absolutely irreducible}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \pi\text{ cuspidal}, \] and with $\pi$ tempered and essentially unitary. In the usual terminology, essentially unitary means unitary after an appropriate character twist; temperedness is a local condition at every place in the intended normalization. The source does not say whether a motivic-weight twist is already built into these conventions, which must therefore be recovered.

The motive category, coefficient field, purity/weight conditions, realization and local-parameter construction, and allowable $L$-group representations are not supplied. The equality of $L$-functions needs normalization, ramified and archimedean-factor conventions, and a choice of full versus partial functions. No compatibility, existence of general local correspondences, or interpolation of parameters is proved here.

The cuspidality clause is only the supplied implication, not an equivalence. The statement requests existence, not uniqueness or a multiplicity formula. The assessment mentions multiplicity assertions, but gives no such additional conjecture, so none is invented. Neither bad places nor the temperedness requirement are silently dropped to turn the fragment into a more familiar restricted automorphy statement.

Short English statement

The source asks for an automorphic representation matching a motive's $L$-function and every local packet, with cuspidality under absolute irreducibility and the stated tempered/unitary properties. The compatible motivic data and normalization, especially weight twists, are missing.
