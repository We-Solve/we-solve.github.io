Moments of L-functions — Moments of the Riemann zeta function — Problem 12

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the Riemann zeta function, and let $T\to\infty$. The Riemann hypothesis asserts that every nontrivial zero of $\zeta$ has real part $1/2$. Denote by $\mathsf{H}_{\mathrm{QAD}}$ the smoothed quaternary additive divisor conjecture invoked in the source's reference [NSW]. Its precise formulation, including smoothing conditions, parameter ranges, and error estimates, is not supplied; this notation is therefore a placeholder for an unresolved hypothesis.

The source reports that Ng, Shen, and Wong [NSW] proved, assuming the Riemann hypothesis and $\mathsf{H}_{\mathrm{QAD}}$, that \[ I_4(T)\sim C_4T(\log T)^{16},\qquad C_4>0. \] The supplied record does not define $I_4(T)$ or specify $C_4$. In particular, identifying $I_4(T)$ with a particular normalization of the eighth moment requires checking the cited source.

Conditional main-term problem. Under these same two assumptions, determine an explicit full main term $M_4(T)$, including all contributions needed to obtain \[ I_4(T)=M_4(T)+o(T). \] The source also requests a power-saving error as an alternative, interpreted as \[ I_4(T)=M_4(T)+O(T^{1-\delta}) \quad\text{for some }\delta>0. \] The main term must recover the reported leading asymptotic $M_4(T)\sim C_4T(\log T)^{16}$. Its intended explicit form is not supplied.

Shifted problem. Under the same assumptions, asymptotically evaluate \[ J_{\omega}(T;\boldsymbol a,\boldsymbol b) :=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\omega(t) \prod_{j=1}^{4} \zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+a_j+it\right) \zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+b_j-it\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t, \] where $\boldsymbol a=(a_1,\ldots,a_4)$, $\boldsymbol b=(b_1,\ldots,b_4)$, and \[ a_j,b_j\in{\mathbb{C}},\qquad |a_j|,|b_j|\ll(\log T)^{-1} \quad(1\le j\le4). \] Here $\omega$ is smooth and compactly supported on ${\mathbb{R}}$, with \[ \operatorname{supp}\omega\subseteq[c_1T,c_2T],\qquad 0<c_1<c_2. \] The shifts and weight may vary with $T$. The source does not specify bounds on the weight or its derivatives, the required uniformity in the shifts, or a precise error target for the shifted integral. Consequently, a fully quantified shifted asymptotic cannot be extracted from this record alone.

Short English statement

Assuming the Riemann hypothesis and the smoothed quaternary additive divisor conjecture used by Ng, Shen, and Wong, determine the full main term for the source's $I_4(T)$ with an $o(T)$ or power-saving error, and obtain an asymptotic for the displayed shifted integral. The supplied record leaves the definition of $I_4$, the exact divisor conjecture, and the shifted uniformity and weight conditions unresolved.
