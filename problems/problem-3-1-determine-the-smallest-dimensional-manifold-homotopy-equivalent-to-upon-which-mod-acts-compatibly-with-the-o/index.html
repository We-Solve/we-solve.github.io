Problem 3.1 — Determine the smallest dimensional manifold homotopy-equivalent to Σ upon which ModΣ acts compatibly with the outer a…

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Sigma$ be the surface in the source and let $\eta:\operatorname{Mod}_\Sigma\to\operatorname{Out}(\pi_1\Sigma)$ be its natural outer action. In the homeomorphism-action interpretation, seek a connected manifold $X$ together with a homotopy equivalence $h:X\to\Sigma$ and a homomorphism $\rho:\operatorname{Mod}_\Sigma\to\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$ such that \[ h_*\,[\rho(\varphi)_*]\,h_*^{-1}=\eta(\varphi) \quad\text{in }\operatorname{Out}(\pi_1\Sigma) \quad\text{for every }\varphi\in\operatorname{Mod}_\Sigma. \] Outer automorphisms account for the basepoint ambiguity in the induced fundamental-group maps.

Problem 3.1 asks for the smallest possible dimension of such an $X$. The surface type, manifold category, presence of boundary, compactness and action regularity are not specified in the excerpt; the displayed homeomorphism model is the customary conditional interpretation. Compact closed manifolds and noncompact or boundary manifolds cannot be interchanged here, since the required homotopy type constrains their dimensions differently.

The condition concerns an action on a manifold homotopy-equivalent to $\Sigma$, realizing its prescribed outer action. It is not the ordinary action-dimension problem of a proper action on a contractible manifold; properness, contractibility and cocompactness are not requested. Faithfulness likewise is not stated as an independent extra hypothesis, though it may follow from compatibility in cases where $\eta$ is faithful.

Short English statement

Find the least dimension of a manifold with the homotopy type of $\Sigma$ on which its mapping class group acts with the usual induced outer action on the surface group.
