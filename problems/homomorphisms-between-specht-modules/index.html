Homomorphisms between Specht Modules

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, let $F$ be an arbitrary field, and let $S_n$ denote the symmetric group on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. A partition $\lambda\vdash n$ is a finite weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers with sum $n$. Its Young diagram has $\lambda_i$ boxes in row $i$. A $\lambda$-tableau is a bijective filling of this diagram with $1,\ldots,n$; its row tabloid $\{t\}$ records the sets of entries in its respective rows. Let $M_F^\lambda$ be the permutation $F S_n$-module with these tabloids as an $F$-basis. For a tableau $t$, let $C_t\leq S_n$ be the subgroup preserving each column's set of entries, and define its polytabloid by \[ e_t=\sum_{\sigma\in C_t}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\{\sigma t\}\in M_F^\lambda, \] where the signs are interpreted in $F$. The Specht module $S_F^\lambda$ is the $F S_n$-submodule spanned by all such $e_t$.

Homomorphism-space problem. For every integer $n\geq1$, every field $F$, and every pair of partitions $\lambda,\mu\vdash n$, determine the $F$-vector space \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{F S_n}(S_F^\lambda,S_F^\mu) = \left\{f\in\operatorname{Hom}_F(S_F^\lambda,S_F^\mu): f(gv)=g f(v)\text{ for every }g\in S_n,\ v\in S_F^\lambda\right\}. \] A concrete determination would describe its dimension and a basis, or an equivalent explicit description of all its maps, uniformly in the stated data. No restriction to algebraically closed fields or to a particular characteristic is supplied.

Associated decomposition-number target and unresolved qualification. For an isomorphism class of simple $F S_n$-modules represented by $D$, write \[ d_{\lambda,D}=[S_F^\lambda:D] \] for the multiplicity of $D$ among the composition factors of $S_F^\lambda$. These multiplicities give a precise interpretation of the associated decomposition-number target. The supplied statement asks to compute the homomorphism spaces, "thereby determining" the associated decomposition-number problem. The displayed range uses positive $n$; the source does not explicitly address $n=0$.

Short English statement

Determine all module homomorphisms between any two Specht modules for the same symmetric group over every field. The supplied record also asks for the associated decomposition numbers, interpreted as simple composition-factor multiplicities, but does not justify how the requested homomorphism spaces determine them.
