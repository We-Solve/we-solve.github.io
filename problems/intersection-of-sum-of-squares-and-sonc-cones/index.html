Intersection of Sum-of-Squares and SONC Cones

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for a characterization of the intersection of the sum-of-squares and SONC cones. It does not explicitly define their ambient space or parameter range. The following gives a conventional real-polynomial interpretation; these conventions require confirmation against the cited report.

For positive integers $n,d$, let \[ V_{n,2d}=\{p\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]:\deg p\leq 2d\}, \qquad \Sigma_{n,2d}=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{m}q_j^2:m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\ q_j\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_n],\ \deg q_j\leq d\right\}. \] Empty sums are understood to be zero. For $\alpha\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{n}$, write $x^\alpha=\prod_{k=1}^{n}x_k^{\alpha_k}$ and $|\alpha|=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\alpha_k$.

Under the same convention, a circuit polynomial of degree at most $2d$ has the form \[ f(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{r}a_i x^{\alpha_i}+b x^\beta, \] where $1\leq r\leq n$, the vectors $\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_r\in(2{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0})^n$ are affinely independent, $a_i>0$, $b\in{\mathbb{R}}\setminus\{0\}$, and \[ \beta\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{n}\cap\operatorname{relint}\operatorname{conv}\{\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_r\}, \qquad |\alpha_i|\leq2d,\quad |\beta|\leq2d. \] Here $\operatorname{relint}$ denotes interior relative to the affine hull. Such a polynomial is nonnegative if $f(x)\geq0$ for every $x\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$. Let $C_{n,2d}$ be the cone of finite sums of these nonnegative circuit polynomials and nonnegative monomials $c x^{2\gamma}$, where $c\geq0$, $\gamma\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}^{n}$, and $|\gamma|\leq d$. This is the conventional sum-of-nonnegative-circuit-polynomials (SONC) interpretation used in this draft.

Problem. Characterize \[ \Sigma_{n,2d}\cap C_{n,2d}; \] that is, give necessary and sufficient conditions describing the polynomials belonging to both cones, for the parameters intended in the source.

The supplied excerpt does not settle whether the intended setting is all polynomials of total degree at most $2d$, homogeneous forms of degree $2d$, or another convention. It also does not specify parameter restrictions, treatment of degree zero, or the desired form of a characterization. The definitions above are therefore a provisional reconstruction rather than definitions quoted from the report.

Short English statement

Describe exactly which polynomials are both sums of squares and sums of nonnegative circuit polynomials. The draft uses conventional real-polynomial definitions, whose agreement with the source requires review. The supplied dated curation labels the general characterization open.
