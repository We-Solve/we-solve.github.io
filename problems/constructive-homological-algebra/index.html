Constructive Homological Algebra

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology, let $R$ be a ring with identity and let $(C_\bullet,d_\bullet)$ be a chain complex of left $R$-modules, with maps $d_n:C_n\to C_{n-1}$ satisfying $d_n\circ d_{n+1}=0$ for every $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. Its cycles, boundaries, and homology in degree $n$ are \[ Z_n(C_\bullet)=\ker(d_n),\qquad B_n(C_\bullet)=\operatorname{im}(d_{n+1}),\qquad H_n(C_\bullet)=Z_n(C_\bullet)/B_n(C_\bullet). \] This module setting supplies standard terminology only; the source does not specify an ambient category or restrict its questions to this setting.

Research questions (as supplied). Determine when homology can be computed constructively, what algebraic conditions are required for such computation, and which results and techniques of homological algebra admit constructive formulations and implementations in software or proof assistants.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The supplied passage describes a research programme rather than a single quantified conjecture. It does not specify the algebraic objects, their effective presentations, the degrees or boundedness of complexes, the permitted computational operations, or the required representation of the output homology. It also leaves unspecified the meaning of constructive computation, the intended homological results and techniques, and the software or foundational systems in which implementations are sought. Accordingly, no universal algorithmic assertion, necessary-and-sufficient criterion, or specific formalization theorem is asserted here.

Short English statement

The problem asks for algebraic settings in which homology can be computed constructively, for the hypotheses enabling those computations, and for constructive implementations of homological methods in software or proof assistants. The supplied formulation is a broad research programme whose precise computational and foundational targets remain unspecified.
