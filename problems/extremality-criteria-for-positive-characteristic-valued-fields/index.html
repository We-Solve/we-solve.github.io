Math-4198

Extremality Criteria for Positive-Characteristic Valued Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(K,v)$ be a valued field, with valuation written additively as $v:K\to\Gamma\cup\{\infty\}$, where $\Gamma=v(K^\times)$ is an ordered abelian group and $v(0)=\infty$. Write \[ \mathcal O_v=\{a\in K:v(a)\geq 0\},\qquad \mathfrak m_v=\{a\in K:v(a)>0\},\qquad Kv=\mathcal O_v/\mathfrak m_v. \] Positive residue characteristic means $\operatorname{char}(Kv)=p>0$; it does not require $\operatorname{char}(K)>0$.

The valued field $(K,v)$ is extremal if, for every integer $n\geq 1$ and every polynomial $f\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$, the set \[ \{v(f(a_1,\ldots,a_n)):(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\in\mathcal O_v^n\} \subseteq\Gamma\cup\{\infty\} \] has a largest element, with $\infty$ larger than every element of $\Gamma$.

Use the standard convention that a $\mathbb Z$-group is an ordered abelian group elementarily equivalent to $(\mathbb Z,+,0,<)$ in the language of ordered abelian groups. A group $\Gamma$ is divisible if, for every $\gamma\in\Gamma$ and every integer $m\geq 1$, there exists $\delta\in\Gamma$ such that $m\delta=\gamma$. A field $k$ is large if every smooth geometrically integral curve over $k$ having a $k$-rational point has infinitely many $k$-rational points. In characteristic $p>0$, $k$ is imperfect if $k^p=\{a^p:a\in k\}\ne k$.

An extension $(L,w)/(K,v)$ is immediate if the induced inclusions of value groups and residue fields are equalities. The valued field $(K,v)$ is maximal if it has no proper immediate valued field extension.

Question concerning an additional assumption. Consider valued fields $(K,v)$ satisfying $\operatorname{char}(Kv)=p>0$ and \[ \left[\Gamma\text{ is a }\mathbb Z\text{-group}\right] \quad\text{or}\quad \left[\Gamma\text{ is divisible and }Kv\text{ is large and imperfect}\right]. \] Find a simple, useful additional assumption under which every such valued field is extremal. The source does not specify a class of admissible additional assumptions or mathematical criteria for being simple and useful; this part is therefore a request for a sufficient-condition criterion rather than a fully specified proposition.

Question concerning maximal fields. Is every maximal valued field $(K,v)$ satisfying \[ \left[\Gamma\text{ is a }\mathbb Z\text{-group}\right] \quad\text{or}\quad \left[\Gamma\text{ is divisible and }Kv\text{ is large}\right] \] extremal? In this second question, the supplied wording omits both the imperfection requirement and an explicit repetition of positive residue characteristic. The imperfection requirement is therefore absent from the displayed question. It remains unresolved whether the quantifier in this second question ranges over all valued fields or only those with $\operatorname{char}(Kv)>0$; these are distinct formulations requiring source clarification.

Short English statement

Ask for a simple, useful sufficient condition for extremality in positive residue characteristic when the value group is a $\mathbb Z$-group, or is divisible with large imperfect residue field. Also ask whether maximality suffices for a $\mathbb Z$-group, or for a divisible value group with large residue field. The characteristic scope of the second question is unclear. Extremality means that every polynomial attains a largest valuation on tuples from the valuation ring.
