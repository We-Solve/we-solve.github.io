Stable Rationality of Generically Free Simple-Group Quotients

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ denote a ground field, whose characteristic and algebraic-closure assumptions are not specified in the supplied record. Let $G$ be a simply connected simple algebraic group in the $\operatorname{Spin}$ series or of type $E_8$. The intended range of indices in the $\operatorname{Spin}$ series and whether only split groups or other forms are included remain unspecified.

A finite-dimensional linear representation $V$ of $G$ over $k$ is called generically free if there is a nonempty $G$-invariant open subset of $V$ on which the geometric stabilizers are trivial. For a scheme-theoretic interpretation, these stabilizer group schemes must be trivial; the source does not specify a convention in positive characteristic. Interpret the rational quotient $V/G$ through its invariant function field $k(V)^G$. It is stably $k$-rational if there exist integers $m,r\geq 0$ and algebraically independent indeterminates such that \[ k(V)^G(t_1,\ldots,t_m)\cong_k k(u_1,\ldots,u_r). \]

Question, with unresolved scope. Once the ground-field assumptions, group forms, and intended $\operatorname{Spin}$ indices are fixed, is $V/G$ stably $k$-rational for every generically free finite-dimensional linear representation $V$ of each such $G$? Equivalently, does the displayed field isomorphism exist for each such pair $(G,V)$, with $m$ and $r$ allowed to depend on the pair?

This is the usual invariant-field reading of the supplied question. The universal quantification over representations is an interpretation of its plural wording. The record does not explicitly provide the missing field or group-form hypotheses, so this draft does not assert that the question ranges over all fields or all forms.

Short English statement

The question asks whether generically free linear quotients for simply connected simple groups in the $\operatorname{Spin}$ series and of type $E_8$ become rational after adjoining finitely many independent variables. The ground field, allowed group forms, and exact range of the series require clarification.
