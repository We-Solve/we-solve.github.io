Generic Properties of Random Heegaard Three-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: "What properties does a generic closed 3-manifold have when its Heegaard gluing map is chosen by a random walk on the mapping class group?" It specifies a research programme rather than a single quantified conjecture.

A genus-$g$ handlebody is a compact connected orientable three-manifold homeomorphic to a regular neighbourhood of a bouquet of $g$ circles. A Heegaard splitting expresses a closed connected orientable three-manifold as two genus-$g$ handlebodies glued along their boundaries. For a closed connected oriented surface $\Sigma_g$, let $\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g)$ denote its group of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms modulo isotopy.

The following is a conditional framework for making the question precise; its parameters and conventions are not specified by the source. Fix an integer $g\geq 0$, oriented genus-$g$ handlebodies $H_g^+$ and $H_g^-$, and boundary markings $j_\pm:\Sigma_g\to\partial H_g^\pm$, with $j_+$ orientation preserving and $j_-$ orientation reversing. Fix a probability measure $\mu$ on $\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g)$. For independent increments $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ with common law $\mu$, define \[ W_0=1,\qquad W_n=X_1X_2\cdots X_n\quad(n\geq1). \] Choosing a representative $f_n$ of $W_n$, define \[ M_n=H_g^+\mathbin{\cup_{\,j_-\circ f_n\circ j_+^{-1}}}H_g^-. \] The gluing map reverses boundary orientation, and the resulting homeomorphism type is independent of the representative within its isotopy class.

One possible meaning of a generic property $\mathcal P$ in this model is \[ \lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P_\mu\left(M_n\text{ has }\mathcal P\right)=1. \] Under this interpretation, the question asks which topological or geometric properties $\mathcal P$ satisfy this limit, with all hypotheses on $g$, the markings, and $\mu$ stated explicitly. The source does not choose these parameters, impose support or moment assumptions on $\mu$, define genericity, or identify a particular property to characterize. Thus neither this interpretation nor a universal quantifier over these parameters can be attributed to the source, and a unique precise conjecture remains unresolved.

The supplied background assessment also mentions fibred three-manifolds, but gives no construction or separate question for them; they cannot be silently identified with the Heegaard model above.

Short English statement

The question asks which properties are typical of closed three-manifolds obtained by randomly gluing two handlebodies. A precise probability statement requires a specified genus, random-walk law, meaning of typicality, and target properties; these are absent from the supplied formulation.
