Math-6802

TDLC Analogue of Finite-Subgroup Conjugacy

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A totally disconnected locally compact (TDLC) group is a Hausdorff locally compact topological group whose connected components are singletons. For a prime number $p$, a finite elementary abelian $p$-subgroup of a group $G$ is a finite subgroup isomorphic to $({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})^r$ for some finite nonnegative integer $r$. Including or excluding the trivial subgroup does not affect the finiteness question below. Define \[ \mathcal E_p(G)=\{E\leq G:E\text{ is a finite elementary abelian }p\text{-group}\}. \] The group $G$ acts on $\mathcal E_p(G)$ by conjugation, and $\mathcal E_p(G)/G$ denotes the set of its orbits. Thus the proposed conclusion is \[ \left|\mathcal E_p(G)/G\right|<\infty. \] For comparison, an abstract group $\Gamma$ has type $\mathrm{FP}_\infty({\mathbb{Z}})$ if its trivial ${\mathbb{Z}}[\Gamma]$-module ${\mathbb{Z}}$ admits a projective resolution whose modules are finitely generated in every degree. This definition alone does not specify the intended topological analogue for a TDLC group.

Formulation problem. Formulate an analogue for TDLC groups of the discrete finite-subgroup conjugacy theorem referred to in the supplied record. The proposed analogue is to give appropriate TDLC hypotheses corresponding to membership in Kropholler's hierarchy and to type $\mathrm{FP}_\infty({\mathbb{Z}})$, and ask whether these imply that $\mathcal E_p(G)/G$ is finite. A possible logical shape, rather than a fully specified conjecture, is \[ \text{for every prime }p\text{ and every TDLC group }G,\qquad \left. \begin{gathered} G\text{ satisfies the intended hierarchy hypothesis},\\ G\text{ satisfies the intended finiteness hypothesis} \end{gathered} \right\} \Longrightarrow \left|\mathcal E_p(G)/G\right|<\infty. \] The supplied record does not define either hypothesis, identify the precise version of Kropholler's hierarchy, or reproduce the discrete theorem being generalized. It also does not explicitly settle the quantification over $p$; the displayed universal quantification describes the natural per-prime target and requires confirmation. Consequently this is an unresolved formulation request, not a precise assertion for a determined class of TDLC groups. No bound uniform in $p$, and no finiteness assertion combining all primes, is specified.

Short English statement

The question seeks suitable hypotheses on a totally disconnected locally compact group under which, for a prime $p$, there are only finitely many conjugacy classes of finite elementary abelian $p$-subgroups. The required hierarchy and homological finiteness hypotheses are unspecified, so a complete conjecture cannot yet be stated. The supplied record labels the problem open; that status has not been independently verified.
