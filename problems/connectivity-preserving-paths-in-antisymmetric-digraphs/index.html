Connectivity-Preserving Paths in Antisymmetric Digraphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An antisymmetric digraph has no pair of oppositely directed arcs between distinct vertices. The supplied statement does not explicitly specify the range of the connectivity parameter $k$, whether connectivity means strong vertex connectivity, or whether digraphs are finite, loopless, and without parallel arcs. The following is a precise candidate formulation under those conventions, with $k\geq 1$; these qualifications require confirmation against the cited source.

Let $D=(V,A)$ be a finite loopless digraph without parallel arcs. Write \[ \delta^+(D)=\min_{v\in V}|\{w\in V:(v,w)\in A\}|, \qquad \delta^-(D)=\min_{v\in V}|\{w\in V:(w,v)\in A\}|. \] For $S\subseteq V$, let $D-S$ be the induced digraph on $V\setminus S$. A digraph is strongly connected if every ordered pair of vertices is joined by a directed path. For a positive integer $k$, interpret $\kappa(D)\geq k$, or $k$-connectedness, as requiring $|V(D)|\geq k+1$ and strong connectivity of $D-X$ for every $X\subseteq V(D)$ with $|X|<k$.

Under these conventions, the supplied conjectural question is: for every integer $k\geq 1$, every integer $m\geq 0$, and every $k$-connected antisymmetric digraph $D$ satisfying \[ \delta^+(D)\geq\left\lfloor\frac{3k+1}{2}\right\rfloor+m, \qquad \delta^-(D)\geq\left\lfloor\frac{3k+1}{2}\right\rfloor+m, \] does there exist a directed path $P$ with distinct vertices $v_0,\ldots,v_{2m}$, with $(v_i,v_{i+1})\in A$ for $0\leq i<2m$, such that \[ \kappa\left(D-V(P)\right)\geq k? \] Here length counts arcs, so $P$ has $2m+1$ vertices. In particular, when $m=0$, the question asks for a single vertex whose deletion preserves the required connectivity. The conventions above are an explicit interpretation of the supplied excerpt, not additional hypotheses verified from the report.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether sufficiently large minimum in-degree and out-degree force an antisymmetric digraph to contain a directed path of length $2m$ whose vertex deletion preserves $k$-connectivity. The interpretation of connectivity, the range of $k$, and the graph conventions require confirmation. The supplied curation attributes the conjecture to Mader and labels it open; those claims have not been independently verified here.
