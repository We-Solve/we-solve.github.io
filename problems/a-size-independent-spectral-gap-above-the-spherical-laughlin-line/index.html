A size-independent spectral gap above the spherical Laughlin line

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The spherical fermionic Laughlin $V_1$ model is claimed uniformly gapped at filling $1/3$, with a stronger lower bound on the square of the interaction across the entire lowest-level Fock space.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
For the lowest-Landau-level pseudopotential $H_{N,Q}=\sum_{i<j}P_{ij}^{(1)}$ on $\bigwedge^NU_Q$, with pair total-spin projector $Q-1$ and coefficient one, the source claims an integer $N_0$ such that \[ H_{N,3(N-1)}\ge\frac1{25}(I-P_{\mathrm L,N})\qquad(N\ge N_0). \] Here $P_{\mathrm L,N}$ projects onto the line spanned by $\prod_{i<j}(u_iv_j-u_jv_i)^3$. Thus the spherical Laughlin line is a unique zero-energy ground space with a size-independent positive excitation gap.

Outline of the argument

Exterior-algebra identities expand the interaction square into spin coefficients. Uniform asymptotic control of these coefficients bounds cross terms against the original interaction, giving a Fock-space inequality independent of particle number and hence the Laughlin gap.
