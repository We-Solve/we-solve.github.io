Kourovka Notebook Problem 12.1

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite group $G$, its integral group ring is \[ {\mathbb{Z}}[G]=\left\{\sum_{g\in G}a_g g:a_g\in{\mathbb{Z}}\right\}, \] with coefficientwise addition and multiplication induced by the group multiplication and distributivity. Its group of units is \[ U({\mathbb{Z}}[G])=\{u\in{\mathbb{Z}}[G]:\text{there exists }v\in{\mathbb{Z}}[G]\text{ with }uv=vu=1\}. \] For a subgroup $H\leq U({\mathbb{Z}}[G])$, the index $[U({\mathbb{Z}}[G]):H]$ is the number of left cosets of $H$. The subgroup is proper if $H\neq U({\mathbb{Z}}[G])$.

Part (a). The supplied source attributes to H. Bass an explicit construction, for a finite cyclic group $C$, of a proper subgroup $B_C$ of finite index in $U({\mathbb{Z}}[C])$. Determine the index \[ [U({\mathbb{Z}}[C]):B_C]. \] Here $B_C$ denotes the particular subgroup in the cited Bass construction. The supplied record does not give its generators or another definition identifying it, nor does it specify restrictions on the order of $C$. Thus this is a source-dependent formulation: a self-contained index problem requires the precise construction and its applicable hypotheses from the cited reference.

Part (b), attributed to A. A. Bovdi and marked archived solved in the supplied source. For each finite abelian non-cyclic group $A$, construct subgroups analogous to Bass' subgroup. In the terms of the preceding description, the requested objects are explicitly constructed subgroups $H_A$ satisfying \[ H_A<U({\mathbb{Z}}[A]),\qquad [U({\mathbb{Z}}[A]):H_A]<\infty. \] The record does not specify further properties required by the word "analogous"; these must be checked against Bass' construction and the original problem.

The supplied archive reports constructions for finite $p$-groups with $p\neq2$ by K. Hoechsmann and J. Ritter, and a general construction by R. Zh. Aleev for groups of central units of integral group rings of arbitrary finite groups, including nonabelian groups. Here the group of central units means \[ U({\mathbb{Z}}[G])\cap Z({\mathbb{Z}}[G]),\qquad Z({\mathbb{Z}}[G])=\{z\in{\mathbb{Z}}[G]:zx=xz\text{ for every }x\in{\mathbb{Z}}[G]\}. \] These are supplied archive assessments, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

Determine the index of the specific finite-index subgroup of the units of a finite cyclic integral group ring constructed by Bass. The source also records Bovdi's request for analogous constructions for finite abelian non-cyclic groups as archived solved. The precise Bass subgroup and the full meaning of "analogous" require the cited sources; the supplied registry labels the combined problem partially solved.
