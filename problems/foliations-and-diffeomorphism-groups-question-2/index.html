Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 2

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a closed, oriented $3$-manifold, let $\varphi$ be a transitive pseudo-Anosov flow on $M$, and let $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_k$ be the specified collection of closed orbits. Transitivity means that the flow has a dense orbit. The term pseudo-Anosov flow is retained from the source; its precise singularity and regularity conventions are not specified in the supplied record.

For distinct surgery orbits, remove the interiors of pairwise disjoint tubular neighborhoods $N(\gamma_i)$, obtaining a manifold with boundary tori $T_i=\partial N(\gamma_i)$. A Dehn filling slope on $T_i$ is an unoriented primitive class in $H_1(T_i;{\mathbb{Z}})$, equivalently an isotopy class of essential simple closed curves on $T_i$. Simultaneous Dehn surgery fills each $T_i$ with a solid torus whose meridian represents the specified slope. After choosing an ordered basis $(\mu_i,\lambda_i)$ for each $H_1(T_i;{\mathbb{Z}})$, the class $p_i\mu_i+q_i\lambda_i$, with coprime integers $p_i,q_i$, has coordinate $p_i/q_i\in{\mathbb{Q}}\cup\{\infty\}$. The source places multislopes in ${\mathbb{R}}^k$ but does not supply these coordinate choices or explain the treatment of infinite slopes.

Once slope coordinates and the intended surgery construction on the flow have been fixed, the finite-coordinate version of the source question is to define \[ \mathcal S=\left\{\mathbf r\in{\mathbb{Q}}^k:\begin{array}{l} \text{simultaneous Dehn surgery along the }\gamma_i\text{ with multislope }\mathbf r\\ \text{results in a pseudo-Anosov flow admitting a transverse}\\ \text{codimension-one foliation} \end{array}\right\}, \qquad Z=\overline{\mathcal S}^{\,{\mathbb{R}}^k}, \] and ask: What structure does $Z$ have? Here closure is taken in the usual Euclidean topology. A transverse codimension-one foliation is a foliation by surfaces whose tangent planes are everywhere transverse to the flow direction, subject to regularity conventions that the source does not state.

This formulation is conditional on the omitted conventions. In particular, the supplied record does not specify whether the resulting flow must be a particular flow obtained by surgery on $\varphi$, or whether existence of some suitable pseudo-Anosov flow on the filled manifold suffices. It also does not specify a proposed structural characterization of $Z$. These choices remain unresolved; no coorientability assumption on the transverse foliation is added.

Short English statement

For a transitive pseudo-Anosov flow on a closed, oriented $3$-manifold and a finite collection of closed orbits, consider the surgery multislopes that yield a pseudo-Anosov flow transverse to a surface foliation. The question asks for the structure of their closure $Z\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^k$. Slope coordinates, the intended surgery construction on the flow, and the requested kind of structure require clarification.
