Math-3987

Complete Bipartite Subgraphs and Connected Matchings at $\alpha=2$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the customary convention, let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite simple undirected graph, and put $n=|V|$. An independent set is a subset of $V$ containing no adjacent pair, and the independence number is \[ \alpha(G)=\max\{|S|:S\subseteq V\text{ is independent}\}. \] Thus $\alpha(G)=2$ requires an independent pair and excludes independent sets of size three. For positive integers $a,b$, a complete bipartite subgraph $K_{a,b}$ consists of disjoint vertex sets $A,B$ with $|A|=a$, $|B|=b$, and every edge between $A$ and $B$ present in $G$. This uses the usual, noninduced subgraph convention; edges within either part are permitted in the ambient graph. A matching $M\subseteq E$ is a set of edges with pairwise disjoint endpoints, and its size is $|M|$.

Supplied conjectural question. Does there exist an absolute real constant $c>0$ such that, for every positive integer $n$ and every $n$-vertex graph $G$ satisfying $\alpha(G)=2$, both of the following hold?

  1. $G$ contains a complete bipartite subgraph $K_{n^c,n^c}$.
  2. $G$ contains a connected matching of size $cn$.
The same constant $c$ occurs in both clauses in the supplied statement.

Unresolved qualifications. The source record does not define "connected matching." Possible meanings include: a matching whose edges lie in one connected component of $G$; a matching for which the subgraph induced by all its endpoints is connected; or a matching such that, for every two distinct matching edges, an edge of $G$ joins an endpoint of one to an endpoint of the other. These conditions differ, and the intended one must be confirmed.

The expressions $K_{n^c,n^c}$ and "size $cn$" require integer conventions. The record does not specify rounding, or whether matching size means equality or a lower bound. It also supplies no sufficiently-large-$n$ qualification or exceptional small orders. Consequently the displayed question preserves the supplied universal wording, but its integer thresholds, boundary conditions, and connectedness requirement remain unresolved; no repaired formulation is asserted here. The finite simple undirected graph convention and the noninduced interpretation of containment also require confirmation from the source.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every graph with independence number exactly two must contain both a complete bipartite subgraph with polynomially large parts and a matching of size proportional to its number of vertices, subject to an unspecified connectedness condition. The record uses one positive constant for both requirements. Connectedness, integer thresholds, and the treatment of small graphs require clarification before this becomes a fully precise conjecture.
