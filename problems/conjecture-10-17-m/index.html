Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be an oriented compact surface, and let $C,C'$ be homology cylinders over $F$ in the source's convention. Conventionally these are oriented $3$-dimensional cobordisms with two boundary markings by $F$, each inducing integral homology isomorphisms, with suitable agreement along $\partial F$. Whether equality of the induced homology markings or other extra conditions is required must be checked in the source.

Let $\sim_{Y_d}$ denote the source's degree-$d$ clasper equivalence, and let $\mathcal F^Y_\star$ denote its finite-type filtration. The precise clasper moves and indexing are not included in the record. An invariant with values in an abelian group $A$ extends additively to the free abelian group on cylinders. Under the intended indexing, degree less than $d$ means that this extension vanishes on $\mathcal F_d^Y$; that indexing must be verified.

Conjecture 10.17, attributed to M. Polyak with reference [153, "Theorem 4"], states that for every such pair and every admissible $d$, \[ C\sim_{Y_d}C' \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left[\begin{array}{l} \text{for every abelian group }A\text{ and every }A\text{-valued}\\ \text{finite-type invariant }v\text{ of }\mathcal F^Y_\star\text{-degree}<d,\\ v(C)=v(C') \end{array}\right]. \] The quantifier includes arbitrary abelian coefficient groups, not only rational or real values, and the degree bound is strict. No assumption that $F$ is closed, connected, or of a specified genus is added. The allowable $d$-range and source definitions must be supplied before treating the displayed equivalence as fully specified.

Short English statement

Two homology cylinders over a compact oriented surface are conjectured to be $Y_d$-equivalent exactly when every lower-degree finite-type invariant agrees, with values allowed in every abelian group. The filtration and cylinder conventions are missing; the supplied record also has conflicting open versus partial-progress labels.
