Schubert Classes in the Gamma Filtration

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n$ be an integer with $n\geq 8$. The supplied record uses the notation $K(X_{F(S)})$ and calls $e_n$ a special Schubert class in this group. It does not specify the field $F$, the space $S$, the variety $X$, their dependence on $n$, or the construction of $e_n$.

For clarity about the filtration convention, if the intended $K$-theory is a Grothendieck $\lambda$-ring $R$ of vector bundles equipped with a rank augmentation $\operatorname{rk}\colon R\to{\mathbb{Z}}$, put $I=\ker(\operatorname{rk})$. Define $\gamma^i(x)$ by \[ \sum_{i\geq 0}\gamma^i(x)t^i=\lambda_{t/(1-t)}(x),\qquad x\in I. \] Under this convention, for $r\geq 1$, the subgroup $\gamma^rR$ is generated additively by products \[ \gamma^{i_1}(x_1)\cdots\gamma^{i_m}(x_m),\qquad m\geq 1,\quad x_j\in I,\quad i_j\geq 1,\quad \sum_{j=1}^m i_j\geq r. \] The record does not explicitly identify its $K$-theory or augmentation with this convention.

Supplied conjectural assertion. With the intended geometric setup and special Schubert class from the cited source, the assertion is that, for every integer $n\geq 8$, \[ e_n\in K(X_{F(S)})\qquad\text{and}\qquad e_n\notin\gamma^{\,n+1-\lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor}K(X_{F(S)}). \] The omitted geometric setup and the normalization of $e_n$ must be recovered from the cited source before this can be treated as a complete standalone mathematical statement. No unrestricted quantification over fields or varieties is asserted here.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that, for every integer $n\geq 8$, a designated special Schubert class lies outside the $n+1-\lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor$ term of the gamma filtration. The underlying geometric objects and the class are not defined in the record, so the assertion remains dependent on its source context.
