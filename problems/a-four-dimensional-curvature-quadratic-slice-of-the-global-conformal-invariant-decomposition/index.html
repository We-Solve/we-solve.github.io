A four-dimensional curvature-quadratic slice of the global conformal invariant decomposition

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The exact canonical Conjecture 2 says: "Any $S$ as above may be written $\mathrm{const}\cdot Q+L+V$, where $V$ is an exact divergence." The antecedent defining $S$, the dimension, and the meanings and normalization of $Q,L$ are omitted. The supplied aid interprets this as a decomposition of natural top-degree forms with conformally invariant integrals into a constant multiple of a $Q$-curvature density, a local conformal invariant, and an exact divergence. This interpretation is explicit rather than a recovery of all missing source hypotheses. In a closed oriented setting, an exact top-degree form $V={\,\mathrm{d}}\eta$ integrates to zero; a divergence density $(\operatorname{div}_gX){\,\mathrm{d}} V_g$ has this form.

The aid gives a completely specified four-dimensional curvature-quadratic slice. Let $M$ be any nonempty closed oriented smooth four-manifold, let $a,b,c\in{\mathbb{R}}$, and define \[ S_g=(a|\operatorname{Rm}_g|^2+b|\operatorname{Ric}_g|^2+cR_g^2){\,\mathrm{d}} V_g. \] Here $R_g$ is scalar curvature, $W_g$ the Weyl tensor, and tensor norms use the full tensor contraction convention. Then the aid reports the equivalence \[ \left[\forall g\ \forall\varphi\in C^\infty(M),\quad \int_M S_{e^{2\varphi}g}=\int_M S_g\right] \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a+b+3c=0. \] The quantifier is every metric, not merely one fixed conformal class. Its witness lemma is reported to detect failure on every such manifold: if $a+b+3c\ne0$, a suitable metric and infinitesimal conformal change give a nonzero first variation. No explicit witness construction is supplied in the summary.

For $\Delta_g=\operatorname{div}_g\nabla_g$, fix \[ Q_4(g)=-\tfrac16\Delta_g R_g-\tfrac12|\operatorname{Ric}_g|^2+\tfrac16R_g^2, \qquad \beta=2a+b. \] With $a+b+3c=0$, the reported pointwise density decomposition is \[ S_g=-2\beta Q_4(g){\,\mathrm{d}} V_g +a|W_g|^2{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g -\tfrac\beta3(\Delta_g R_g){\,\mathrm{d}} V_g. \] The Weyl density is locally conformally invariant in dimension four. The last term is an exact divergence, since \[ (\Delta_g R_g){\,\mathrm{d}} V_g={\,\mathrm{d}}(\iota_{\nabla_gR_g}{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g). \] Thus this slice has the stated $Q+L+V$ form with its coefficients and Laplacian convention explicit. It concerns quadratic curvature expressions with no curvature derivatives in $S_g$, although a divergence derivative appears in the decomposition.

The aid says the standard polynomial complete-contraction version of the general conjecture follows from Alexakis, but warns that the workshop's unqualified "natural $n$-form" may be broader. No precise theorem, allowed derivative orders, parity/dimensional hypotheses, naturality class, or full citation is supplied. The four-dimensional result must not silently be identified with a classification of all such potentially broader forms.

Short English statement

The excerpt states a conformal-invariant-density decomposition but omits the preceding definition of its input. The aid makes one four-dimensional slice precise: curvature-quadratic densities have conformally invariant integrals exactly when one linear coefficient relation holds, and then admit the displayed $Q$-curvature, Weyl, and divergence decomposition. The claimed general theorem covers a specified polynomial class rather than every possible interpretation of naturality.
