Embedding Triangular 2-Complexes in 3-Space

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ denote the two-dimensional complex described in the supplied record as being formed from triangular faces by identifying edges and/or vertices, and let $|K|$ denote its underlying topological space, with the quotient topology induced by these identifications. The faces and identifications specifying the intended $K$ are not supplied.

In the topological interpretation, an embedding of $|K|$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$ is a continuous map $f:|K|\to{\mathbb{R}}^3$ that is a homeomorphism onto its image, where $f(|K|)$ has the subspace topology. The question for the intended complex is whether \[ \exists f:|K|\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^3 \quad\text{such that }f\text{ is a topological embedding}. \] The source does not specify whether a topological, piecewise-linear, or other embedding category is intended; the displayed formulation makes only the topological interpretation explicit. It also does not state finiteness or local finiteness hypotheses, specify the gluing maps, or identify the particular complex. Consequently, this is an embeddability question with an unspecified input, not a fully specified yes-or-no instance or an assertion that every such complex embeds. The supplied background refers to a pictured complex, but no picture or equivalent combinatorial description is included.

Short English statement

Can the intended triangular two-dimensional complex be embedded in ${\mathbb{R}}^3$? In the topological interpretation, this asks for a homeomorphism onto a subspace of ${\mathbb{R}}^3$. The supplied record omits the intended complex and leaves the embedding category unspecified, so the particular question requires clarification.
