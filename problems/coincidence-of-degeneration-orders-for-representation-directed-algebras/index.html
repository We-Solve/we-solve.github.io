Coincidence of Degeneration Orders for Representation-Directed Algebras

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an algebra $A$, let $\mathcal C_A$ denote the class of objects on which the two degeneration orders in the supplied source are intended to be defined. Write $\preccurlyeq_{\mathrm{geom},A}$ and $\preccurlyeq_{\mathrm{homol},A}$ for those geometric and homological degeneration relations, respectively. These symbols are placeholders for definitions missing from the supplied record; in particular, $\mathcal C_A$ is not being identified here with a category of modules or complexes.

Conjectural target (with unresolved definitions). For every representation-directed algebra $A$, the two relations coincide. Equivalently, the intended statement has the logical form \[ \forall A\text{ representation-directed},\qquad \forall X,Y\in\mathcal C_A,\qquad X\preccurlyeq_{\mathrm{geom},A}Y \ \Longleftrightarrow\ X\preccurlyeq_{\mathrm{homol},A}Y. \] Equality of these relations means that they contain precisely the same ordered pairs of admissible objects.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define representation-directed algebras, specify the base field or finiteness assumptions, identify the objects being compared, or define either degeneration relation. It also does not state any common dimension, dimension-vector, or other admissibility restriction on a pair $(X,Y)$. Consequently, the display records the conjecture's quantifier structure but is not a fully specified mathematical formulation. In particular, no identification of the homological relation with inequalities involving dimensions of $\operatorname{Hom}$ or $\operatorname{Ext}$ spaces is justified by the supplied text. The original extracted sentence contains an unresolved pronoun and corrupted diagram text, so it does not recover these missing definitions.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether geometric degeneration and homological degeneration give exactly the same comparisons for every representation-directed algebra. The record omits the definitions and underlying objects needed to make this precise. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
