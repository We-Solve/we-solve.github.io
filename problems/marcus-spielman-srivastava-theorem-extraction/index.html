Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava Theorem Extraction

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a vector $v\in{\mathbb{C}}^d$, let $v^*$ denote its conjugate transpose, so that $vv^*$ is the rank-one positive semidefinite matrix with entries $v_j\overline{v_k}$. Write $I_d$ for the identity matrix, use the Euclidean norm on vectors, and use the induced operator norm on matrices. A random vector has finite support if it takes values in a finite set with probability one.

The supplied statement asserts the following implication. Let $\varepsilon>0$, and let $v_1,\ldots,v_m$ be independent random vectors with finite support in ${\mathbb{C}}^d$, defined on a common probability space. Suppose that \[ \sum_{i=1}^{m}{\mathbb{E}}(v_i v_i^*)=I_d, \qquad {\mathbb{E}}\|v_i\|^2\leq\varepsilon \quad\text{for every }i\in\{1,\ldots,m\}. \] Then \[ \Pr\!\left( \left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m}v_i v_i^*\right\| \leq(1+\sqrt{\varepsilon})^2 \right)>0. \] The record uses $m$ and $d$ as finite family-size and dimension parameters without explicitly specifying whether zero values are permitted; no additional boundary convention is asserted here.

Short English statement

The supplied implication says that independent complex random vectors with finite support, whose expected outer products sum to the identity and whose individual expected squared lengths are at most $\varepsilon$, have positive probability of producing a sum of outer products with operator norm at most $(1+\sqrt{\varepsilon})^2$. The record identifies this as a proved Marcus--Spielman--Srivastava theorem, but its conflicting open-problem and extraction annotations require review.
