Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Let $n>=2,p>n,\ell $ different from p, and let X/Fq be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus at least two with a rational marked point x. For an arithmetic PGLn parameter on X minus x over a finite extension of $Q_\ell $ whose geometric image is Zariski dense and whose inertia at x is wild-trivial regular-unipotent tame $\exp (t_\ell $ N), must some finite constant-field extension support a nonzero Weil sheaf on SLn-bundles with Borel reduction at x, geometrically locally constructible perverse with nilpotent singular support, and coherent all-leg Hecke eigenisomorphisms as Weil sheaves?

Weil structures on regular tame eigensheaves

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 014; 2026-10-05), the asserted result is that after a finite constant-field extension, the one-point tame eigensheaf construction for $\mathrm{SL}_n$, $p>n$, can retain the prescribed arithmetic Frobenius and full multi-leg eigenstructure.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $n\ge2$, $\mathbb F_q$ have characteristic $p>n$, and $\ell\ne p$, $E=\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}$. Let $X_0/\mathbb F_q$ be smooth projective geometrically connected of genus at least two, with rational point $x_0$, and $U_0=X_0\setminus\{x_0\}$. A continuous $\rho:\pi_1^{\rm et}(U_0)\to\operatorname{PGL}_n(L)$, $L/\mathbb Q_{\ell}$ finite, is assumed geometrically Zariski dense, trivial on wild inertia, and regularly unipotent on tame inertia: \[ \rho(\gamma)=\exp(t_{\ell}(\gamma)N),\qquad N\text{ regular nilpotent}. \] The claim gives $m\ge1$ and a Weil sheaf $M_0\ne0$ with $E$-coefficients on $\operatorname{Bun}_{\operatorname{SL}_n,B,x_0}(X_0)_{\mathbb F_{q^m}}$. After geometric pullback it is perverse and locally constructible, its singular support lies in the parabolic nilpotent locus, and \[ \operatorname{Hecke}_{I,(V_i)}(M_0)\simeq M_0\boxtimes\mathop{\boxtimes}_{i\in I}(V_i)_{\rho_m} \] for all finite leg sets and dual-group representations. These are Weil isomorphisms coherent for units, convolution, permutations and all fusion diagonals; $\rho_m$ is the restriction to the extended constant field.

Outline of the argument

For manuscript 1 (family 014; 2026-10-05), an arithmetic parameter is lifted to characteristic zero with its boundary action intact and then glued across a separating node. Equivariant nearby cycles and a compact conjugator preserve Frobenius throughout specialization and descent from the enhanced flag level.
