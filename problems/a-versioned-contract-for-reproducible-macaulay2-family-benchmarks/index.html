Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$. Specialized notation and index ranges follow the cited source and the definitions below.

A computational benchmark specifies an input, a computation, and measurements such as elapsed time or memory use. A benchmark family varies stated parameters, allowing results across sizes or degrees to be compared. Reproducibility also requires software versions, machine characteristics, and a way to check output correctness.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Benchmarks

By a "benchmark" we mean an interesting calculation (e.g., one occurring in someone's research) which pushes the capabilities of M2. These are especially useful if they come in a natural family, which we can compute up to some degree but not beyond at the moment.

What "benchmark" calculations should be tracked in M2? What is the best way to keep track of these benchmarks (e.g., wiki, on the website, etc.)? [S2]
