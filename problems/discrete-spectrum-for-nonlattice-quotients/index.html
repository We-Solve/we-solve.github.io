Discrete Spectrum for Nonlattice Quotients

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the ambient group $G$, the space $X$, their relationship, or the geometric and rank hypotheses. Consequently, it does not determine a unique fully quantified problem. The following formulation is conditional on the conventional analytic setting described below; these conventions require confirmation against the source context.

Suppose $G$ is a locally compact unimodular group and $\Gamma<G$ is discrete. A lattice is such a subgroup for which the invariant quotient measure satisfies $\operatorname{vol}(\Gamma\backslash G)<\infty$. Thus, under these conventions, a nonlattice satisfies $\operatorname{vol}(\Gamma\backslash G)=\infty$. Suppose also that $X$ is a Riemannian $G$-space for which the quotient $\Gamma\backslash X$, its measure, and a nonnegative self-adjoint Laplace operator $\Delta_{\Gamma\backslash X}$ on $L^2(\Gamma\backslash X)$ are defined. The intended geometry, treatment of quotient singularities, and operator domain are not supplied.

For an ambient pair $(G,X)$ in the intended, unspecified class, the first question asks whether a nonlattice $\Gamma<G$ can admit a nonzero square-integrable Laplace eigenfunction, namely whether there can exist \[ 0\ne f\in\operatorname{Dom}(\Delta_{\Gamma\backslash X}),\qquad \lambda\in[0,\infty),\qquad \Delta_{\Gamma\backslash X}f=\lambda f. \] The nonnegative eigenvalue convention here depends on the stated choice of sign for the Laplacian.

For the second question, let $\rho_\Gamma$ denote the right regular unitary representation on $L^2(\Gamma\backslash G)$, given by \[ (\rho_\Gamma(g)F)(\Gamma h)=F(\Gamma h g). \] Let $\lambda_G$ denote the left regular representation on $L^2(G)$. In the usual weak-containment sense, $\rho_\Gamma$ is tempered if $\rho_\Gamma\prec\lambda_G$. Equivalently, for every compactly supported continuous function $\varphi$ on $G$, \[ \left\|\int_G\varphi(g)\rho_\Gamma(g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} g\right\| \le \left\|\int_G\varphi(g)\lambda_G(g)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} g\right\|. \] The second question asks whether there exists a nonlattice $\Gamma<G$ for which $\rho_\Gamma\not\prec\lambda_G$. The intended restrictions on $G$ and $\Gamma$, and whether $G$ is fixed or varies, remain unresolved. These questions concern the full displayed $L^2$ spaces; the supplied statement specifies no removal of invariant vectors or restriction to a spectral subspace.

Short English statement

For the intended group $G$ and geometric space $X$, can a discrete subgroup of infinite covolume yield a nonzero $L^2$ Laplace eigenfunction on $\Gamma\backslash X$? Can such a subgroup yield a non-tempered right regular representation on $L^2(\Gamma\backslash G)$? The ambient hypotheses are missing, so this is a conditional statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
