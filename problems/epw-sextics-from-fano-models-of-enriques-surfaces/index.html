EPW Sextics from Fano Models of Enriques Surfaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ denote the Enriques surface or its Fano model intended in the supplied record, and let $D$ denote the divisor implicit in that model. The record does not specify the base field, the precise definition of the Fano model, or the conditions defining $D$. Retain the supplied hypothesis that the complete linear system $|2D|$, namely the system of effective divisors associated with $\mathcal O_S(2D)$, gives an embedding \[ \iota_{2D}\colon S\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^{19}, \qquad \deg\iota_{2D}(S)=40. \] The qualification that the Fano model is general is part of the proposed assertion; the parameter space and exceptional locus are not supplied.

For clarity, the usual EPW construction uses a six-dimensional vector space $V$, a volume form identifying $\bigwedge^6V$ with the base field, and the resulting alternating pairing on $\bigwedge^3V$ given by wedge product. For a ten-dimensional Lagrangian subspace $A\subset\bigwedge^3V$, put \[ F_v=v\wedge\bigwedge^2V, \qquad Y_A=\left\{[v]\in\mathbb P(V):A\cap F_v\ne\{0\}\right\}. \] Here the degeneracy locus has its determinantal scheme structure. When this construction gives a sextic hypersurface, $Y_A\subset\mathbb P(V)\cong\mathbb P^5$ is an EPW sextic. Write $\operatorname{Sing}(Y_A)$ for its singular locus. This terminology does not resolve the omitted base-field hypothesis or identify the particular EPW sextic intended by the source.

Proposed statement, with unresolved qualifications. Interpreting a suitable projection as a linear projection defined everywhere on $\iota_{2D}(S)$, ask whether, for a general Fano model as above, there exist a projective linear subspace $\Lambda\cong\mathbb P^{13}\subset\mathbb P^{19}$ and an EPW sextic $Y_A\subset\mathbb P^5$ such that \[ \Lambda\cap\iota_{2D}(S)=\varnothing, \qquad T=\pi_\Lambda\left(\iota_{2D}(S)\right), \qquad \deg T=40, \qquad T\subseteq\operatorname{Sing}(Y_A), \] where $\pi_\Lambda\colon\mathbb P^{19}\dashrightarrow\mathbb P^5$ is projection from $\Lambda$, and $T$ is taken with its reduced image structure. The everywhere-defined projection and reduced-image conventions make this displayed version precise but are not explicitly specified in the record; the intended scheme-theoretic meaning of containment remains unresolved.

The supplied original wording additionally asserts that the projected surface is isomorphic to the corresponding degree-$40$ surface in the singular locus of the EPW sextic. Thus, if that wording is retained, the intended conclusion also includes \[ T\cong T_{\mathrm{corr}}, \qquad T_{\mathrm{corr}}\subseteq\operatorname{Sing}(Y_A), \qquad \deg T_{\mathrm{corr}}=40. \] Neither $T_{\mathrm{corr}}$ nor its correspondence with the Fano model is defined in the supplied record. Consequently, the existential containment question in the clean statement cannot be identified unconditionally with the original isomorphism assertion. The record does not assert that the projection restricts to an isomorphism from $S$ onto $T$.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether a general Fano model of an Enriques surface, embedded by $|2D|$ as a degree-$40$ surface in $\mathbb P^{19}$, admits a suitable projection to a degree-$40$ surface in the singular locus of an EPW sextic. The original wording also requires an isomorphism with a particular corresponding surface whose construction is missing. The base field, polarization, meaning of generality, and projection conditions require clarification.
