Numerical semiampleness of projective adjoints

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For a projective rational klt pair (X,B) over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, assume $K_X+B$ is pseudo-effective and M is a nef rational Cartier divisor. If $D=K_X+B+M$ is nef, must D be numerically equivalent to some semiample rational Cartier divisor?
