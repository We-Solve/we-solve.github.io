Math-14929

Abstract-Group Reconstruction of Countably Categorical Structures

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a first-order structure $M$, let $\operatorname{Th}(M)$ denote its complete theory. Say that $M$ is $\omega$-categorical if $\operatorname{Th}(M)$ has, up to isomorphism, exactly one countable model. Let $\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ be its automorphism group, with composition as the group operation. Define its elementary self-embedding monoid by \[ \operatorname{EEmb}(M)=\{f:M\to M : f\text{ is injective and elementary}\}. \] Here elementary means that, for every first-order formula $\varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ in the language of $M$ and every tuple $\bar a\in M^n$, \[ M\models\varphi(\bar a)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad M\models\varphi(f(\bar a)). \] The monoid operation is composition and its identity is $\operatorname{id}_M$. The topology of pointwise convergence on either invariant has basic neighborhoods \[ U(f,F)=\{g:g\vert_F=f\vert_F\},\qquad F\subseteq M\text{ finite}, \] where $g$ ranges over the relevant group or monoid.

The supplied background suggests reconstruction up to bi-interpretability. In the parameter-free reading, $M$ and $N$ are bi-interpretable if each admits a first-order interpretation in the other, without parameters, and the two composite interpretations are definably isomorphic to the respective original structures. Write $M\simeq_{\mathrm{bi}}N$ for this relation.

Reconstruction question. Determine additional hypotheses and a comparison class $\mathcal C$ of $\omega$-categorical structures under which either of the following reconstruction properties holds, considered separately: \[ \begin{aligned} &\forall M,N\in\mathcal C,\qquad \operatorname{Aut}(M)\cong_{\mathrm{grp}}\operatorname{Aut}(N) \ \Longrightarrow\ M\simeq_{\mathrm{bi}}N;\\ &\forall M,N\in\mathcal C,\qquad \operatorname{EEmb}(M)\cong_{\mathrm{mon}}\operatorname{EEmb}(N) \ \Longrightarrow\ M\simeq_{\mathrm{bi}}N. \end{aligned} \] The isomorphisms in these implications are purely algebraic; continuity for the topology of pointwise convergence is not an assumption.

Unresolved scope. These implications give a precise scheme for the supplied question, not a conjecture that either implication holds for all $\omega$-categorical structures. The record does not specify the additional hypotheses, the comparison class, whether those hypotheses must hold for both structures, or the exact reconstruction equivalence intended in the shortened statement. The displayed scheme uses the bi-interpretability target suggested by the original background and explicitly chooses its parameter-free version. The customary setting of countable structures in countable first-order languages also requires confirmation: neither the cardinality of $M$ nor the language restrictions are explicit in the shortened statement. If that setting is intended, it must be included in the specification of $\mathcal C$. Whether finite structures are included is likewise unspecified.

Short English statement

Determine conditions under which the abstract automorphism group, or separately the abstract monoid of elementary self-embeddings, determines an $\omega$-categorical structure up to bi-interpretability, without supplying the topology of pointwise convergence. The comparison class, language and cardinality conventions, and parameter convention require confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
