Math-239

Heegner transfer on a split genus-two locus

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

C. Hall asks for constructions of points on the Jacobian of a higher-genus hyperelliptic curve over $K={\mathbb{F}}_q(T)$, analogous to the source's Drinfeld--Heegner construction for elliptic analytic rank one. For a smooth projective hyperelliptic curve $X/K$ of genus $g\ge2$, its Jacobian $J_X=\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ parametrizes degree-zero divisor classes. Analytic rank means the order of vanishing of its Hasse--Weil $L$-function at the central point. The question seeks rational classes expected from that analytic information; it does not assert a general equality of analytic and Mordell--Weil ranks or specify an algorithm and independence guarantee. The record's comment is truncated after "when the analytic rank is," and cannot be completed from the supplied verification.

The attached unreviewed aid treats only a split genus-two family. Assume $K$ has odd characteristic and $F(u)\in K[u]$ is a separable cubic with $F(0)\ne0$. Let $X,E_1,E_2$ be the smooth projective models of \[ X:y^2=F(x^2),\qquad E_1:v^2=F(u),\qquad E_2:w^2=uF(u). \] The first curve has genus two. The elliptic models have the usual point at infinity $O_1$ on $E_1$ and a rational point $(0,0)$ on the quartic model of $E_2$, allowing an elliptic origin. The degree-two quotient maps are \[ \pi_1(x,y)=(x^2,y),\qquad\pi_2(x,y)=(x^2,xy). \] The aid claims these induce a $K$-isogeny $J_X\to E_1\times E_2$.

Given an already constructed non-torsion Drinfeld--Heegner input $P\in E_1(K)$, it defines \[ D_P=\pi_1^*([P-O_1])\in J_X(K),\qquad \pi_{1,*}D_P=2[P-O_1]. \] The pullback is scheme-theoretic: individual points above $P$ need not be $K$-rational, but the pulled-back divisor class is. The displayed norm relation certifies non-torsion. The aid reports $L(J_X,s)=L(E_1,s)L(E_2,s)$, so analytic ranks one and zero for $E_1,E_2$ give analytic rank one for $J_X$. Existence and nonvanishing of the required Heegner input need their own hypotheses. This transfer construction is confined to the split genus-two locus and does not solve the general higher-genus construction problem.

Short English statement

Construct rational Jacobian points suggested by analytic rank, beyond elliptic curves. The aid transfers an assumed non-torsion Heegner point through an explicit degree-two quotient on a special split genus-two family.
