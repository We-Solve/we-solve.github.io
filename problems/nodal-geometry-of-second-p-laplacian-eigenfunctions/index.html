Math-4762

Nodal Geometry of Second $p$-Laplacian Eigenfunctions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $1<p<\infty$, the $p$-Laplacian is \[ \Delta_p u=\operatorname{div}(|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u). \] The supplied record asks for the structure of second eigenfunctions on bounded domains, especially their nodal sets. It does not specify boundary conditions or the convention for the second eigenvalue, so it does not determine a unique spectral problem.

An explicitly conditional homogeneous Dirichlet formulation is as follows. Let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be a bounded open connected set. Here $W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)$ denotes the closure of $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ in the Sobolev space $W^{1,p}(\Omega)$. A Dirichlet eigenpair consists of $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$ and $0\ne u\in W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)$ such that \[ \int_\Omega |\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u\cdot\nabla\varphi\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x =\lambda\int_\Omega |u|^{p-2}u\varphi\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x \quad\text{for every }\varphi\in W_0^{1,p}(\Omega). \] Under this interpretation, set \[ \lambda_1(\Omega,p)=\inf_{0\ne v\in W_0^{1,p}(\Omega)} \frac{\int_\Omega |\nabla v|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}{\int_\Omega |v|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x}, \] and interpret $\lambda_2(\Omega,p)$ as the least Dirichlet eigenvalue strictly greater than $\lambda_1(\Omega,p)$. A second eigenfunction is an eigenfunction for $\lambda_2(\Omega,p)$; it may be normalized by $\int_\Omega |u|^p\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x=1$. For its continuous interior representative, define the interior nodal set by \[ Z_\Omega(u)=\{x\in\Omega:u(x)=0\}. \] The boundary is excluded from this definition.

Requested structural problem, conditional on the preceding spectral interpretation. Determine the possible second eigenfunctions and their interior nodal sets on bounded domains. In particular, establish or refute the following geometric descriptions in dimension two:

  • For a disk $D_R=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:|x|<R\}$, with $R>0$, and every $p\in(1,\infty)$, the nodal line is a diameter. Interpreting this as equality of the entire interior nodal set means \[ Z_{D_R}(u)=D_R\cap\{x:x\cdot e=0\} \quad\text{for some }e\in{\mathbb{R}}^2\text{ with }|e|=1. \]
  • For the square $S_a=(-a,a)^2$, with $a>0$, the nodal line is vertical or horizontal when $1<p<2$, and diagonal when $p>2$. The corresponding entire-nodal-set descriptions are \[ Z_{S_a}(u)=S_a\cap\{x_1=c\} \quad\text{or}\quad Z_{S_a}(u)=S_a\cap\{x_2=c\},\qquad c\in(-a,a), \] for $1<p<2$, and \[ Z_{S_a}(u)=S_a\cap\{x_2=x_1\} \quad\text{or}\quad Z_{S_a}(u)=S_a\cap\{x_2=-x_1\} \] for $p>2$. The source does not explicitly say whether the vertical or horizontal segment must be a central axis, which would require $c=0$.
For each geometric description, the source leaves unresolved whether it asserts existence of a second eigenfunction with that nodal set or asserts that every second eigenfunction has a nodal set of that form. These are distinct formulations and neither is selected here. Equality with the entire interior zero set is also an explicit interpretation of the source's phrase "nodal line," rather than a verified source qualification. No square claim at $p=2$ is supplied. The Dirichlet boundary condition and spectral convention above are conditional explanations, not hypotheses established by the supplied record.

Short English statement

The problem asks how second $p$-Laplacian eigenfunctions vanish: along a diameter in a disk for every $1<p<\infty$, and along a vertical or horizontal segment in a square for $1<p<2$, versus a diagonal for $p>2$. Boundary conditions, the meaning of "second," and whether these claims concern some or all second eigenfunctions need clarification. The displayed Dirichlet formulation is conditional. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
