Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 12.21

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $n\geq1$, let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$ and let \[ \operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n). \] A lattice in a Lie group $L$ is a discrete subgroup $\Gamma$ for which $\Gamma\backslash L$ has finite invariant volume; it is uniform if that quotient is compact. Real rank is the dimension of a maximal real split torus. In the usual finite-centre, no-compact-factor semisimple setting, irreducibility means that the lattice does not decompose across a nontrivial product of the noncompact factors; equivalently its projection to every proper nontrivial collection of simple factors is dense, after the standard finite central quotient.

The source question is: if $\Gamma$ is an irreducible uniform lattice in a higher-rank connected semisimple Lie group $L$, must every homomorphism $\rho:\Gamma\to\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ have finite image? With higher rank interpreted as $\operatorname{rank}_{{\mathbb{R}}}L\geq2$, the target quantifiers are \[ \forall n\geq1\ \forall\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(\Gamma,\operatorname{Out}(F_n)), \qquad |\rho(\Gamma)|<\infty. \] The source does not explicitly state finite-centre or no-compact-factor conventions for $L$; those conventions in the irreducibility explanation are not silently imposed as a correction to the source.

The supplied assessment reports the same conclusion for every irreducible lattice, uniform or nonuniform, in the stated higher-rank setting. It also reports a stronger algebraic criterion: a group $\Gamma$ such that no finite-index subgroup admits a normal subgroup surjecting to ${\mathbb{Z}}$ has only finite images in $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$. Explicitly the stated condition is \[ \forall\Gamma_0\leq\Gamma\text{ of finite index}\ \forall N\trianglelefteq\Gamma_0, \qquad \nexists\text{ surjection }N\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}. \] These are attributed resolution statements; complete theorem hypotheses must be checked against the supplied resolving reference.

Short English statement

Must an irreducible cocompact higher-rank lattice have only finite images in the outer automorphism group of a finite-rank free group? The supplied assessment answers yes, also for noncocompact lattices, citing Bridson--Wade, Corollary B. It attributes the broader normal-subgroup criterion to their Theorem A, and says this also covers hereditarily just-infinite groups that are not virtually cyclic and certain lattices in products of locally compact groups via Bader--Shalom. Those examples and the strengthened scope are reported claims, not blanket assertions about every product lattice. A hereditarily just-infinite group is an infinite group every finite-index subgroup of which has all nontrivial normal subgroups of finite index.
