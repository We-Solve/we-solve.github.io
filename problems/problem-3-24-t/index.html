Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal A_\Sigma$ be the Poisson algebra and $\mathcal M_G(\Sigma)$ the moduli space referred to in the source. The surface $\Sigma$, group $G$, algebra generators and relations, and function class in $C(\mathcal M_G(\Sigma))$ are not defined in this extract. Retain those symbols with their unresolved source meanings.

A formal deformation quantization of a complex Poisson algebra $(A,\cdot,\{\ ,\ \})$, in one common normalization, is an associative ${\mathbb{C}}[[\hbar]]$-bilinear product on $A[[\hbar]]$ of the form \[ a*b=ab+\sum_{r\ge1}\hbar^r B_r(a,b),\qquad a*b-b*a=\hbar\{a,b\}+O(\hbar^2), \] with appropriate unit and continuity conditions. A convention using $i\hbar$ instead must be fixed by the source, not inferred here.

T. Kohno's Problem 3.24 asks for a deformation quantization of $\mathcal A_\Sigma$ which descends to a deformation quantization of $C(\mathcal M_G(\Sigma))$. If descent is through a source-defined Poisson quotient map $q:\mathcal A_\Sigma\to C(\mathcal M_G(\Sigma))$, a precise formal version asks for products $*$ and $*_{\mathcal M}$ and a deformation $q_\hbar$ of $q$ satisfying \[ q_\hbar(a*b)=q_\hbar(a)*_{\mathcal M}q_\hbar(b), \qquad q_\hbar\bmod\hbar=q. \] In a quotient realization, the deformed relations must define an appropriate two-sided ideal and the required formal quotient, rather than merely a product on the larger algebra. The record supplies neither $q$ nor the quotient relations, so this is a conditional explanation of descent, not a specified new quotient. The source additionally asks to investigate the relation with geometric quantization of $\mathcal M_G(\Sigma)$; it gives no proposed comparison map or equivalence criterion.
