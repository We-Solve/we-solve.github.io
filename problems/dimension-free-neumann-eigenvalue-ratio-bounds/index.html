Math-3853

Dimension-Free Neumann Eigenvalue-Ratio Bounds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard Euclidean interpretation of the supplied statement, let $n\geq 1$ and let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be a nonempty bounded open convex set. Write \[ 0=\mu_0(\Omega)<\mu_1(\Omega)\leq\mu_2(\Omega)\leq\cdots \] for the eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, of the nonnegative Neumann Laplacian. The boundary condition is understood weakly: an eigenfunction $u\in H^1(\Omega)\setminus\{0\}$ with eigenvalue $\mu$ satisfies \[ \int_\Omega \nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x =\mu\int_\Omega uv\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x \qquad\text{for every }v\in H^1(\Omega). \] This interpretation imposes no additional smoothness assumption on the boundary.

The dimension-free adjacent-eigenvalue conjecture is the assertion \[ \exists C\in(0,\infty)\quad \forall n\geq1\quad \forall\,\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n\text{ nonempty, bounded, open and convex}\quad \forall k\geq1:\qquad \mu_{k+1}(\Omega)\leq C\mu_k(\Omega). \] Here $C$ is independent of the dimension, the domain, and the eigenvalue index. The source additionally asks for an analytic proof. The displayed indexing is an explicit convention for the positive spectrum: the source's phrase "any $k$" cannot include comparison of the first positive eigenvalue with the zero eigenvalue.

The curated statement also requests an extension to convex domains in Riemannian manifolds of nonnegative Ricci curvature. Its intended form appears to be an analogous universal bound \[ \mu_{k+1}(\Omega,g)\leq C\mu_k(\Omega,g),\qquad k\geq1, \] for Neumann eigenvalues, uniformly over an intended class of ambient manifolds and convex domains. That class is not specified sufficiently to make this extension a fully quantified conjecture: the meaning of convexity, completeness or compactness hypotheses, boundary assumptions, conditions ensuring a discrete Neumann spectrum, and permitted dependence of $C$ remain unresolved.

The verbatim extract also requests an analytic proof of an inequality labelled (4), but neither (4) nor the referenced inequality (1) is supplied. Consequently, no separate mathematical statement for those inequalities can be reconstructed from this record.

Short English statement

The main conjecture asks whether successive positive Neumann eigenvalues of every bounded convex Euclidean domain have a ratio bounded by one constant independent of dimension, domain, and index, and asks for an analytic proof. A requested extension to manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature lacks necessary formulation details. The supplied open status and verification labels are curation claims requiring human review.
