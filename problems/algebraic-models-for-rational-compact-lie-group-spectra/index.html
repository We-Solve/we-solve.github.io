Math-793

Algebraic Models for Rational Compact Lie-Group Spectra

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be an arbitrary compact Lie group and let $r=\operatorname{rank}(G)$, the dimension of a maximal torus. Use $\operatorname{Sp}^G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ for a model category of rational genuine $G$-equivariant spectra, with rational equivalences defined on equivariant stable homotopy groups. This is not silently replaced by naive spectra carrying a $G$-action.

The source's Conjecture 2 asks for an abelian category $\mathcal A(G)$, with enough injectives and injective dimension exactly $r$, and an algebraic model with a Quillen equivalence \[ \operatorname{Sp}^G_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\simeq_Q\operatorname{dg}\mathcal A(G). \] Here $\operatorname{dg}\mathcal A(G)$ denotes differential graded objects, conventionally chain complexes with a suitable model structure whose weak equivalences are quasi-isomorphisms. Injective dimension $r$ means that every object admits an injective resolution of length at most $r$, with $r$ the optimal bound. A Quillen equivalence is requested, not merely a bare equivalence of triangulated homotopy categories; the chosen models and whether a zigzag of equivalences is allowed require source specification. No symmetric monoidal equivalence is separately imposed.

The conjecture includes further structure, not only abstract existence of an algebraic category: objects of $\mathcal A(G)$ should be sheaves of modules over the intended space of closed subgroups of $G$; their fibers are assembled from the corresponding Borel-theory data using localization and inflation; and the model representing a cohomology theory is determined by its values on spheres together with limited additional structure. The topology or quotient by conjugacy on the subgroup space, coefficient sheaves, Borel fibers, transition maps, and admissible module conditions are not reproduced. In particular, no guessed Weyl-group coefficient formula or localization system is inserted. "Spheres" may mean representation spheres or another specified class, and "limited extra structure" is not defined; their exact meaning is necessary to turn the last clause into a uniqueness or reconstruction theorem.

The supplied literature assessment checked 2026-08-21 reports algebraic models for finite groups, tori, and several low-rank compact Lie groups, but not the assertion for every compact Lie group. It does not reproduce the exact ranges or which full sheaf and sphere-reconstruction clauses are satisfied in each case. The universal statement includes disconnected groups and is not restricted to the listed cases.

Short English statement

Find the proposed sheaf-based algebraic model for rational genuine spectra of every compact Lie group, with injective dimension equal to the rank and a Quillen equivalence. The fiber construction and sphere-value reconstruction requirements are additional source clauses whose exact data are missing.
