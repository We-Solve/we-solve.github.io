Math-7079

Derived Pure Braid Groups in the Solvable Filtration

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer parameter $m$, let $P_m$ denote the pure braid group on $m$ strands, equivalently \[ P_m=\pi_1\!\left(\operatorname{Conf}_m({\mathbb{C}}),b\right),\qquad \operatorname{Conf}_m({\mathbb{C}})=\{(z_1,\ldots,z_m)\in{\mathbb{C}}^m:z_i\ne z_j\text{ whenever }i\ne j\}, \] where $b$ is a fixed base configuration. For a group $G$, define its derived series by \[ G^{(0)}=G,\qquad G^{(r+1)}=[G^{(r)},G^{(r)}]\quad(r\geq0), \] where $[H,H]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ with $x,y\in H$.

The supplied corrected formulation asks whether \[ (P_m)^{(n+2)}\subseteq\mathcal F_n^m \qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq1. \] Here the superscript $(n+2)$ means the $(n+2)$-nd term of the derived series, as specified by the supplied corrected statement. The original source transcription is $ (P_m)^{n+2}\subset F_n^m$ for every $n\geq1$; its unparenthesized exponent is not independently explanatory.

The record's title identifies the intended context as the solvable filtration, but the supplied material does not define $\mathcal F_n^m$, its ambient group, or the identification or homomorphism through which a pure braid is regarded as an element of that ambient group. Consequently, the displayed inclusion records the intended target and requires these missing specifications before it becomes a fully determined mathematical assertion. The record also does not state whether $m$ is fixed or universally quantified, or whether additional restrictions on $m$ apply; no universal quantifier over $m$ is imposed here.

The supplied dated assessment describes only a base containment of the squared pure braid group in the zero-level filtration as established, and describes the higher-derived inclusion as unresolved. The meaning of squared pure braid group is not defined in the record, so this phrase is retained as a qualification rather than converted into a subgroup formula. These are supplied curation claims, not independently verified mathematical or literature conclusions.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the $(n+2)$-nd derived subgroup of the pure braid group lies in level $n$ of the intended solvable filtration for every integer $n\geq1$. The filtration, the ambient interpretation of containment, and the quantification over the strand parameter $m$ require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open.
