Given embeddings $f,g:\mathbb C^d\hookrightarrow\mathbb C^n$, does there exist an automorphism $\Psi:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n$ such that $\Psi\circ f=g$?

Manuscript 1 (family 049, 2026-10-05): The explicit corollary identifies each hypersurface fiber with A3 and states that its embedding in A4 is not rectifiable. Comparing that closed embedding with the standard coordinate hyperplane disproves the proposed ambient-automorphism equivalence.

Equivalence of Affine-Space Embeddings Under Automorphisms

OpenAI's claim

Manuscript 1 (family 049, 2026-10-05): A degree-five polynomial in four complex variables is claimed to become a coordinate after adjoining one variable while failing to be a coordinate originally, despite every fiber being affine three-space. Manuscript 2 (family 049, 2026-09-24): An explicit polynomial in four complex variables is claimed to have affine three-space as its zero fiber without being a coordinate, with higher-dimensional examples obtained by adjoining variables.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
In $R=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]$, set \[ Q=x_2^2-x_4^2+x_1x_3,\qquad f=x_1-2Q\left(Q(x_2+x_4)+x_1x_4\right). \] OpenAI claims a complex-algebra automorphism of $R[w]$ sending $f$ to $x_1$, and no such automorphism of $R$. It further claims that every hypersurface $f^{-1}(\lambda)$, $\lambda\in\mathbb C$, is isomorphic to affine three-space but its embedding in affine four-space cannot be straightened to a coordinate hyperplane by a polynomial automorphism.
Mathematical claim 2.
In $R=\mathbb C[h,u,v,w]$, define \[ x=u^3+hv,\quad y=-u^2+hw,\quad s=2u^3v+3u^4w+h(v^2-3u^2w^2)+h^2w^3, \] so $x^2+y^3=hs$. Set $p=-2s^2x+3sy^2-3s^3y$ and $F=h-p-1$. OpenAI claims \[ R/(F)\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]},\qquad\nabla F(2,0,-1/2,1/2)=0. \] Thus the zero fiber is affine three-space but $F$ is not a polynomial coordinate of $R$, since a coordinate has nowhere-vanishing gradient.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 049, 2026-10-05): Two hypersurface presentations and determinant-preserving matrix changes identify the polynomial ring and the distinguished parameter. An exponential of a locally nilpotent derivation gives the stabilized coordinates, while a parameter filtration and the rigidity of its associated bundle obstruct an unstabilized coordinate system.

Manuscript 2 (family 049, 2026-09-24): The construction perturbs the two entries of a cusp parametrization by independent parameter multiples and uses their divisibility relation. Explicit translations and inverse algebra maps identify the zero-fiber ring as a polynomial algebra; the separate ambient obstruction prevents rectifying its embedding.
