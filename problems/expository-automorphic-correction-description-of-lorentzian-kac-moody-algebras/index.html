Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify definitions of a Lorentzian Kac--Moody Lie algebra or superalgebra, a hyperbolic Kac--Moody algebra, or an automorphic correction, including the hypotheses governing these terms. Its original mathematical wording is retained verbatim:
Conjecture: Lorentzian Kac--Moody Lie or superalgebras are automorphic corrections of hyperbolic Kac--Moody algebras.
However, the supplied statement-verification note identifies this sentence in the official report as explanatory background, rather than a conjecture, and marks this record's statement as unrecoverable. Thus the displayed wording is a historical transcription, not a recovered, precisely quantified conjecture. No separate clean mathematical statement is supplied.

The background literature assessment instead discusses the assertion that all Lorentzian Kac--Moody algebras admit automorphic corrections. Being an automorphic correction of a hyperbolic algebra and admitting an automorphic correction are distinct assertions; the record does not reconcile them. Neither assertion is promoted here to a formal conjecture. According to the supplied verification note, the subsequent lattice-specific existence conjecture belongs to the separate record OWR-14605-007; its hypotheses and statement are not supplied here.

This record preserves an expository description relating Lorentzian Kac--Moody Lie algebras and superalgebras to automorphic corrections of hyperbolic Kac--Moody algebras.
