Math-885

Hermitian–Symplectic Structures on Non-Kähler Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,J)$ be a compact complex manifold. A Hermitian--symplectic structure is understood here to mean a smooth real differential two-form $\Omega$ such that \[ {\,\mathrm{d}}\Omega=0, \qquad \Omega_x(v,J_xv)>0 \quad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xX. \] The positivity condition implies that $\Omega$ is nondegenerate; thus $\Omega$ is a symplectic form taming the complex structure $J$. It is not required to have type $(1,1)$. This is the standard taming interpretation of the terminology; the supplied excerpt does not explicitly define it.

The complex manifold $(X,J)$ is Kähler if there exists a smooth real differential form $\omega$ of type $(1,1)$ satisfying \[ {\,\mathrm{d}}\omega=0, \qquad \omega_x(v,J_xv)>0 \quad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xX. \] It is non-Kähler if no such $\omega$ exists, regardless of the choice of Hermitian metric.

The supplied existence question is: \[ \begin{gathered} \text{Does there exist a compact complex manifold }(X,J) \text{ that is non-Kähler}\\ \text{and admits a Hermitian--symplectic structure }\Omega? \end{gathered} \] No complex dimension is fixed in the supplied question. Here the word "any" in that question is read existentially.

The supplied source fragment reports that no such non-Kähler examples occur among complex surfaces (references [8, 10]), nilmanifolds with invariant complex structures (reference [5]), twistor spaces (reference [11]), or Moishezon manifolds and complex manifolds of Fujiki class (references [9, 2]). These are attributed background claims, not independently verified assertions of this draft. The corresponding bibliography entries, precise hypotheses, and specification of the Fujiki class are absent from the supplied record.

The registry labels the problem open and the statement corrected_verified. These are supplied curation labels. Its dated assessment also mentions an existence and classification question, but no classification target is specified; the statement above formalizes only the supplied existence question.

Short English statement

Does there exist a compact complex manifold that admits no Kähler metric but has a closed real two-form positive on every complex tangent line? The two-form may have components other than type $(1,1)$. The supplied record labels this question open; its source qualifications and missing bibliographic details require human review.
