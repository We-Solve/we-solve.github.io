Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.102

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite solvable group. A Carter subgroup of $G$ is a nilpotent subgroup $C\leq G$ satisfying $N_G(C)=C$, where $N_G(C)=\{g\in G:g^{-1}Cg=C\}$. Write \[ |C|=\prod_{p\mid |C|}p^{a_p},\qquad \ell(C)=\sum_{p\mid |C|}a_p, \] with $\ell(1)=0$. Define the nilpotent length $h(G)$, also called the Fitting height, to be the least integer $r\geq 0$ for which there exists a series \[ 1=G_0\leq G_1\leq\cdots\leq G_r=G \] whose terms are normal in $G$ and whose factors $G_i/G_{i-1}$ are nilpotent. In particular, $h(1)=0$.

The problem asks whether there are constants $a,b\geq 0$, independent of $G$ and $C$, such that \[ \forall\,G\text{ finite solvable}\quad\forall\,C\leq G\text{ a Carter subgroup},\qquad h(G)\leq a\ell(C)+b. \] If a linear bound is unavailable, does there at least exist a polynomial $P\in{\mathbb{R}}[x]$, nonnegative on the nonnegative integers and independent of $G$ and $C$, such that \[ \forall\,G\text{ finite solvable}\quad\forall\,C\leq G\text{ a Carter subgroup},\qquad h(G)\leq P(\ell(C))? \] Here "linear" is interpreted as an affine upper bound; the supplied record does not specify a convention for the constant term.

The supplied statement attributes to E. C. Dade an exponential upper bound in $\ell(C)$. The supplied editors' comment attributes to E. Jabara a quadratic upper bound in the special case where, under the preceding hypotheses, $G=H\rtimes C$, $C$ is cyclic, and \[ C_H(C)=\{h\in H:hc=ch\text{ for every }c\in C\}=1. \] Here $G=H\rtimes C$ means that $H\trianglelefteq G$, $G=HC$, and $H\cap C=1$. These literature assertions are supplied source qualifications and have not been independently verified.

Short English statement

Is the nilpotent length of every finite solvable group bounded by a universal linear function, or at least a universal polynomial, of the number of prime factors of the order of a Carter subgroup, counted with multiplicity? The supplied record reports an exponential bound in general and a quadratic bound for a specified cyclic complement case.
