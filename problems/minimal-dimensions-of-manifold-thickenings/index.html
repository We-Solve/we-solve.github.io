Math-1029

Minimal Dimensions of Manifold Thickenings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$. An aspherical complex is a complex whose connected components have contractible universal covers. For a connected finite CW complex $X$, write \[ b_k^{(2)}(X)=\dim_{\mathcal N(\pi_1X)}\overline H_k^{(2)}(\widetilde X), \] where $\mathcal N(\pi_1X)$ is the group von Neumann algebra and $\overline H_k^{(2)}(\widetilde X)$ is reduced square-summable cellular homology. For a finite complex with multiple connected components, the usual componentwise convention sums these numbers over its components. The supplied record does not specify connectedness or the precise category of finite complexes.

The intended question is: for every finite aspherical complex $X$ and every $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$ such that $b_k^{(2)}(X)\ne0$, must every manifold thickening $M$ of $X$ satisfy \[ \dim M\geq 2k\,? \] Here manifold thickening is an unresolved source qualification. The record does not say whether it requires an embedding of $X$ as a deformation retract, a regular neighborhood, or merely a homotopy equivalence with a manifold. It also does not specify compactness, allowance of boundary, or the topological, piecewise-linear, or smooth category. These choices must be fixed before the displayed question has a unique interpretation.

More formally, once an admissible class $\mathcal T(X)$ of manifold thickenings has been specified, define \[ d_{\mathcal T}(X)=\inf\{\dim M:M\in\mathcal T(X)\}, \qquad \inf\varnothing=+\infty. \] The lower-bound question is whether \[ \forall X\ \forall k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0},\qquad b_k^{(2)}(X)\ne0\ \Longrightarrow\ d_{\mathcal T}(X)\geq2k, \] with $X$ ranging over the intended finite aspherical complexes. The record additionally asks to determine the minimum possible thickening dimension. When $\mathcal T(X)$ is nonempty, its integer-valued dimensions have a minimum; determining that minimum is a stronger request than establishing the displayed lower bound, despite the source's use of "equivalently." No equality or existence assertion is supplied.

Short English statement

For a finite aspherical complex with nonzero $k$th $L^2$-Betti number, the question asks whether every manifold thickening has dimension at least $2k$. It also asks for the minimum possible dimension. The meaning of thickening and the permitted manifold category require clarification; determining an exact minimum is stronger than proving the proposed lower bound.
