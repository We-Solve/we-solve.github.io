Semiclassical Correspondence of Primitive and Poisson Spectra

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns an associative algebra $A$ belonging to a flat family of algebras and a commutative Poisson algebra $R$ arising as its semiclassical limit. The base field, the parameter space, the deformation data defining that limit, and the meaning of a suitably generic member are not specified. Accordingly, the statement below retains these unresolved qualifications rather than asserting a conjecture for all flat families.

For terminology, write $k$ for the unspecified base field. A Poisson $k$-algebra is a commutative $k$-algebra $R$ equipped with a $k$-bilinear Lie bracket $\{-,-\}$ satisfying \[ \{r,st\}=\{r,s\}t+s\{r,t\}\qquad(r,s,t\in R). \] An ideal $I\subseteq R$ is a Poisson ideal if $\{R,I\}\subseteq I$. Under the usual commutative Poisson convention, define \[ \operatorname{PSpec}(R)=\{P\in\operatorname{Spec}(R):\{R,P\}\subseteq P\}. \] For an ideal $J\subseteq R$, its Poisson core is \[ \mathcal P(J)=\sum_{\substack{I\subseteq J\\I\text{ a Poisson ideal}}} I, \] the largest Poisson ideal contained in $J$. Under the usual maximal-ideal-core convention, the Poisson-primitive spectrum is \[ \operatorname{PPrim}(R)=\{\mathcal P(\mathfrak m):\mathfrak m\in\operatorname{MaxSpec}(R)\}. \] The supplied record does not explicitly specify these conventions or the hypotheses under which alternative definitions agree.

For $A$, let $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ denote its proper two-sided prime ideals: a proper two-sided ideal $P$ is prime if $IJ\subseteq P$ implies $I\subseteq P$ or $J\subseteq P$ for all two-sided ideals $I,J$ of $A$. Let \[ \operatorname{Prim}(A)=\{\operatorname{Ann}_A(M):M\text{ is a simple left }A\text{-module}\}. \] Use the usual Zariski, or hull-kernel, topology on each spectrum: closed sets consist of the ideals in that spectrum containing a prescribed ideal. These are the conventional topologies used to express the question; the supplied record does not define them explicitly.

Qualified source question. For $A$ a suitably generic member of the specified flat family with semiclassical limit $R$, do there exist homeomorphisms \[ h_{\mathrm{prim}}:\operatorname{Prim}(A)\longrightarrow\operatorname{PPrim}(R), \qquad h_{\mathrm{prime}}:\operatorname{Spec}(A)\longrightarrow\operatorname{PSpec}(R) \] that preserve the appropriate dimensions and group actions?

Here the admissible families, genericity condition, dimension invariants, acting groups, and compatible actions remain to be specified. If a common group $G$ and actions are intended, preservation of those actions means $h(g\cdot I)=g\cdot h(I)$ for every $g\in G$ and every ideal $I$ in the relevant domain. No particular group or action is supplied. Thus the record determines a qualified correspondence question, but does not determine a fully quantified general conjecture with explicit hypotheses.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a suitably generic quantum algebra and its semiclassical Poisson limit have homeomorphic primitive spectra and homeomorphic prime spectra, with the correspondences preserving the intended dimensions and symmetries. The allowed deformations, genericity assumptions, dimensions, and group actions are unspecified, so a precise general scope requires further source clarification.
