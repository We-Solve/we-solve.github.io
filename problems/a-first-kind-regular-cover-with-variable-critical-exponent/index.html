Math-5453

A first-kind regular cover with variable critical exponent

Definitions and mathematical statement

M. Kapovich asks for an infinitely generated Fuchsian group $\Gamma_0$ of the first kind whose critical exponent is nonconstant on its quasiconformal Teichmüller space $\mathcal T_{qc}(\Gamma_0)$. First kind means its limit set is the full ideal circle. For a discrete group acting on $\mathbb H^2$, the critical exponent is \[ \delta(\Gamma)=\inf\left\{s>0:\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma}e^{-s d(o,\gamma o)}<\infty\right\}, \] independent of $o$. Full limit set and critical exponent $1$ are not equivalent for arbitrary infinitely generated groups.

The aid's all-Fuchsian construction starts with a closed oriented surface $\Sigma_g$, $g\geq2$, and \[ \pi_1(\Sigma_g)=\langle a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g\mid\prod_i[a_i,b_i]=1\rangle. \] Let $F_g=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_g\rangle$ and let $K$ be the kernel of the epimorphism $a_i\mapsto x_i$, $b_i\mapsto1$. For each closed hyperbolic metric $m$, let $\rho_m$ be its marked uniformization and set $\Gamma_m=\rho_m(K)$. The aid asserts that these groups are infinitely generated and of the first kind. The associated regular cover $\mathbb H^2/\Gamma_m\to\Sigma_g$ has deck group $F_g$.

Write $\lambda_0(m)$ for the spectral bottom on the cover, defined by the infimum of $\int|\nabla u|^2/\int|u|^2$ over nonzero compactly supported smooth $u$. The aid invokes Brooks's theorem to obtain $\lambda_0(m)>0$, since $g\geq2$ makes the deck group nonamenable and the base is closed. The Elstrodt--Patterson--Sullivan relation is \[ \lambda_0(m)= \begin{cases} \delta(\Gamma_m)(1-\delta(\Gamma_m)),&\delta(\Gamma_m)\geq\tfrac12,\\ \tfrac14,&\delta(\Gamma_m)\leq\tfrac12. \end{cases} \] In particular $\delta(\Gamma_m)<1$ for every such metric.

For a chosen family pinching the disjoint cut-system curves $b_1,\ldots,b_g$ simultaneously to length $\ell\to0$, with the remaining geometric data controlled, the aid reports a collar-cutoff estimate \[ 0<\lambda_0(m_\ell)\leq\frac{C_g}{|\log\ell|^2}. \] Eventually $\lambda_0<1/4$, so the relevant critical exponent is on the branch above $1/2$, and \[ 0<1-\delta(\Gamma_{m_\ell})\leq2\lambda_0(m_\ell) =O(|\log\ell|^{-2}). \] Thus these exponents tend to $1$ while staying strictly below $1$. Lifting quasiconformal changes of the compact base places all these marked covers in $\mathcal T_{qc}(\Gamma_{m_0})$; the aid describes the resulting family as a pullback copy of finite-dimensional Teichmüller space. Its exponent is therefore nonconstant, giving the reported affirmative construction. The exact pinching-family and collar-test details require verification; the estimate is not asserted uniformly over every unbounded choice of remaining parameters.

Short English statement

The supplied construction uses a free-deck-group cover of a closed surface. Its spectral bottom is positive, keeping the critical exponent below one, but pinching a cut system drives the spectral bottom to zero and the exponent to one within the same quasiconformal deformation space.
