Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied background concerns trivariate $C^1$ spline spaces on bipyramid cells. Here a trivariate $C^1$ spline means a piecewise polynomial function of three variables whose pieces join with continuous first partial derivatives across their interfaces. The record does not specify the polynomial degree convention, the subdivision of the bipyramid cells, the admissible geometry, the coefficient field, or any boundary conditions.

Short English statement

For the trivariate $C^1$ spline spaces on bipyramid cells intended by the source, must the dimension attain the stated upper bound whenever the stated lower and upper bounds differ? The record omits the bounds and the precise remaining cases, so a fully specified formulation requires source clarification.
