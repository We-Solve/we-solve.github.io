Hecke-Orbit Density in Hodge-Type Newton Strata

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The intended target is the following geometric density assertion, subject to the unresolved conventions stated below. Let $(G,X)$ be a Shimura datum of Hodge type, let $p$ be any prime at which the associated Shimura variety has good reduction, and let $S$ denote its special fibre. The source does not specify the level or integral model implicit in good reduction. Put $k=\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_p$ and $S_k=S\times_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}k$, interpreting the special fibre geometrically; this interpretation requires confirmation because the supplied transcription writes ${\mathbb{F}}_p$.

A point $x\in S_k(k)$ has an associated abelian variety with the additional structures prescribed by the Hodge-type moduli interpretation. Its Newton invariant is the isomorphism class of the associated rational Dieudonné object with its prescribed structures, equivalently its isocrystal with $G$-structure. Write $N_x\subseteq S_k$ for the Newton stratum consisting of points with the same Newton invariant as $x$. Write $\mathcal I(x)$ for the geometric isogeny class of $x$, where the isogenies must respect the additional structures in the convention appropriate to this Shimura variety. Precisely which structures and which isogenies are intended is not specified by the record and remains a qualification of the assertion.

With these conventions made precise, the requested conjectural statement is \[ \text{for every such }(G,X),\ p,\ S\text{ and every }x\in S_k(k), \qquad \mathcal I(x)\subseteq N_x(k), \qquad \overline{\mathcal I(x)}^{\,N_x}=N_x. \] Here the closure is taken in the Zariski topology of the Newton stratum itself. There is to be no additional lower bound on $p$ in terms of the Shimura datum.

The explicit Siegel formulation in the supplied excerpt concerns the moduli space $\mathcal A_g$ of principally polarised abelian varieties of dimension $g$, for every integer $g\geq1$. For $x=(A_x,\lambda_x)$, it defines the Hecke orbit by points corresponding to principally polarised abelian varieties related to $x$ by symplectic isogenies, and asks for this orbit to be Zariski dense in the Newton stratum containing $x$. The excerpt does not impose a prime-to-$p$ restriction and does not specify the precise polarisation-multiplier convention for these isogenies; neither convention should be silently supplied. The geometric interpretation uses $\mathcal A_g(\overline{{\mathbb{F}}}_p)$. If the transcribed $\mathcal A_g({\mathbb{F}}_p)$ is literal, its rational-point orbit is finite and cannot be dense in a positive-dimensional Newton stratum, so that reading cannot express the general intended density assertion.

The supplied excerpt reports a proof when $p$ is sufficiently large relative to the Shimura datum, specialising to $p\geq g$ for $\mathcal A_g$. These are supplied claims about existing results, not hypotheses to retain in the unrestricted target and not independently verified facts.

Short English statement

For every prime of good reduction, the intended conjecture says that every geometric isogeny class on a Hodge-type Shimura variety is Zariski dense in its Newton stratum, with no size restriction on the prime. The ground field, integral model, and precise compatibility conditions on isogenies require confirmation. The supplied status is partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
