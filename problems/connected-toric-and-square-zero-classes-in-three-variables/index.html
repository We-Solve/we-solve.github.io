Math-1332

Connected toric and square-zero classes in three variables

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $R=k[x_1,x_2,x_3]$ be graded by a finitely generated abelian group $A$, with $\deg x_i=a_i$. For an admissible function $h:A\to{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}$, the multigraded Hilbert scheme $\operatorname{Hilb}^h_R$ parametrizes homogeneous ideals $I$ for which $\dim_k(R/I)_a=h(a)$; in families, the graded pieces are locally free of these ranks. The source asks whether every such scheme in three variables is connected. Its field, grading restrictions, admissibility convention, and connected versus geometrically connected interpretation are not specified. One precise geometric version asks connectedness of every nonempty such scheme after base change to an algebraic closure.

The source remark cites disconnected toric Hilbert schemes in $26$ variables and asks for examples with fewer variables. This is a historical example bound, not an assertion that $26$ is currently minimal. The three-variable question concerns arbitrary Hilbert functions, not only the toric function.

The attached aid reports an affirmative positive-toric subcase. Take a positive affine semigroup $S$ in a lattice, generated by the three variable degrees, and use $h(a)=1$ for $a\in S$, zero otherwise. Positivity means that the degrees lie in a strictly positive cone, in particular no nonconstant monomial has degree zero. The relation lattice $L=\ker({\mathbb{Z}}^3\to\operatorname{gp}(S))$ then has rank at most two. The aid invokes Maclagan--Thomas for smoothness and irreducibility in rank two, reports ${\mathbb{P}}^1$ in rank one, and a point in rank zero. These conclusions concern that positive toric setup and do not establish connectedness for arbitrary multigraded Hilbert functions.

The aid gives another positive-grading class in any finite number of variables. Let $S$ be the degree semigroup, and call $a\in S\setminus\{0\}$ indecomposable if it is not a sum of two nonzero semigroup elements. Assume $h(0)=1$, and that every nonzero degree with $h(a)\ne0$ is an indecomposable variable degree. Put \[ V_a=\operatorname{span}_k\{x_i:\deg x_i=a\},\qquad 0\leq h(a)\leq\dim_kV_a. \] All products of positive-degree elements in the quotient are then zero. The aid's scheme-level assertion is \[ \operatorname{Hilb}^h_R\cong\prod_a\operatorname{Gr}(h(a),V_a), \] where $\operatorname{Gr}(h(a),V_a)$ parametrizes quotients of dimension $h(a)$, and factors of quotient rank zero may be omitted. Hence this class is smooth, irreducible, and connected. Positivity and indecomposable support are part of the statement, not conclusions for every grading.

The aid describes the full arbitrary-Hilbert-function three-variable question as open in the literature it checked. Its toric and square-zero results are supplied unreviewed partial claims, and the source's fewer-variable counterexample request is not resolved here.

Short English statement

Ask whether every multigraded Hilbert scheme in three variables is connected, and seek disconnected examples with fewer variables than the historical 26-variable construction. The supplied aid settles only positive toric and specified square-zero-support classes, leaving arbitrary Hilbert functions distinct.
