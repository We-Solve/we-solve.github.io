Quadratic non-microstates pressure and weighted colored cycles

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks for a colored-planar-map interpretation of the non-microstates free-entropy pressure analogous to a Guionnet--Maurel-Segala microstates result. Its extraction damages the series indices, signs and factorials, and writes a supremum over $TS(A)$ while describing $A$ as a von Neumann algebra generated by semicircular variables. To make a variational question nontrivial, one must specify a domain of varying tracial laws of selfadjoint tuples, not silently replace it by normal traces on one fixed factor. Selfadjoint interaction polynomials, or a stated adjoint symmetrization, are also required for a real objective.

Here is the aid's normalization-safe quadratic statement. Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_m)$ vary over selfadjoint tuples in tracial probability spaces, with finite second moments and the appropriate entropy domain. Let $S$ be a standard free semicircular family of covariance $I_m$, and use the same additive normalization of $\chi$ and $\chi^*$ at $S$. Define \[ \chi^*_{\mathrm{GUE}}(X) =\chi^*(X)-\tfrac12\sum_i\tau(X_i^2)-\chi^*(S)+\tfrac m2. \] For a real symmetric positive-definite matrix $Q$, put \[ V_Q(X)=\tfrac12\sum_{i,j}(Q_{ij}-\delta_{ij})X_iX_j, \qquad p^*(Q)=\sup_X\{\chi^*_{\mathrm{GUE}}(X)-\tau(V_Q(X))\}. \] The reported exact formula is \[ p^*(Q)=-\tfrac12\log\det Q, \] attained by the centered semicircular family with covariance $Q^{-1}$. The GUE-relative subtraction is essential: unnormalized entropy pressure contains an additional constant. A fixed norm cutoff, if imposed, must allow this optimizer.

For $Q=I+T$, $\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}}<1$, the exact expansion is \[ -\tfrac12\log\det(I+T) =\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{(-1)^r}{2r}\operatorname{Tr}(T^r). \] If $T=\sum_{\ell=1}^s t_\ell B_\ell$ with symmetric matrices $B_\ell$, a multi-index $k=(k_1,\ldots,k_s)$ of total degree $r\ge1$ gives \[ \partial^kp^*(0)=\frac{(-1)^r}{2r} \left(\prod_\ell k_\ell!\right) \sum_{\substack{(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_r)\\ \#\{j:\ell_j=\ell\}=k_\ell}} \operatorname{Tr}(B_{\ell_1}\cdots B_{\ell_r}). \] Each trace is the sum over closed color walks of products of matrix entries, giving the aid's weighted cyclic, two-valent planar-diagram interpretation. Signs and weights are retained; this is not a claim that every coefficient is an unweighted positive map count.

For higher-degree selfadjoint interactions $V_t=\sum_\ell t_\ell q_\ell$, let $p(t)$ be the corresponding normalized microstates pressure on a specified common tracial-law domain, with Gibbs maximizer $\tau_t$. Equality $\chi^*(\tau_t)=\chi(\tau_t)$ only supplies the matching value at that candidate. To transfer the whole planar-map pressure series one additionally needs the global variational bound \[ \chi^*_{\mathrm{GUE}}(\tau)-\tau(V_t)\le p(t) \quad\text{for every admissible tracial law }\tau. \] Together these two inputs give $p^*(t)=p(t)$. Under justified attainment and differentiability assumptions, the envelope identity is $\partial_{t_\ell}p^*(t)=-\tau_t(q_\ell)$. Neither the global bound nor all required higher-degree existence/analyticity hypotheses are supplied in the record. Thus the quadratic calculation is a special case; entropy equality at one Gibbs law alone does not establish the requested general non-microstates combinatorial interpretation. The appended Dykema--Ricard section heading is unrelated to this question.

Short English statement

Seek a planar-map interpretation of non-microstates entropy pressure. The aid gives an exact GUE-normalized quadratic answer with weighted colored-cycle coefficients. For higher-degree potentials, transferring a microstates map series needs a global variational upper bound, not just entropy equality at one Gibbs law.
