Subpolynomial Bounds for an Additive Multiplicative Equation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer parameter $n$, define \[ SP(n):=\#\left\{(a,b,c)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}^{3}:abc+a+b+c=n\right\}. \] Here solutions are interpreted as ordered triples. The supplied source does not explicitly specify whether permutations are counted separately, nor does it explicitly state the domain of $n$; the positive integer domain and ordered-triple convention used here require confirmation against the source.

The proposed bound is \[ \forall\varepsilon\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}\ \exists C(\varepsilon)\in{\mathbb{R}}_{>0}\ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0},\qquad SP(n)<C(\varepsilon)n^{\varepsilon}. \] The constant may depend on $\varepsilon$ but must be independent of $n$. This is a question asking whether the displayed assertion holds, not an asserted theorem.

Short English statement

Does the number of positive integer triples satisfying $abc+a+b+c=n$ admit, for every $\varepsilon>0$, a uniform upper bound strictly smaller than $C(\varepsilon)n^{\varepsilon}$? The parameter domain and counting convention require source confirmation.
