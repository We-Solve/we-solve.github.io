Normed Algebra Lifts of the Motivic Tau Cofiber

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal C=\mathrm{SH}({\mathbb{C}})^{\wedge}_2$ denote the $2$-complete motivic stable homotopy category over ${\mathbb{C}}$, interpreted with its symmetric monoidal stable $\infty$-categorical enhancement when discussing commutative algebras. Write $S^0$ for the motivic sphere and \[ C_\tau=S^0/\tau=\operatorname{cofib}(\tau\colon A\longrightarrow S^0) \] for the motivic cofiber intended by the source. Here $A$ denotes the domain of the intended motivic map $\tau$; the supplied record does not specify its degree or construction. Regard $C_\tau$ as the commutative algebra specified in the source, hence as an object of $\operatorname{CAlg}(\mathcal C)$.

A normed algebra carries compatible multiplicative norm maps along the finite étale morphisms in its indexing category. In such a framework, for an allowed finite étale morphism $f\colon T\to X$, these include maps $f_\otimes E_T\to E_X$, where $f_\otimes$ is the motivic norm functor, with coherent compatibility with composition, base change, and the algebra structures. The supplied record does not specify the indexing category or how norms are to interact with $2$-completion.

Question. In the intended normed-algebra framework over ${\mathbb{C}}$, let $\mathcal N$ be its category of normed algebras and let \[ U\colon\mathcal N\longrightarrow\operatorname{CAlg}(\mathcal C) \] be the functor forgetting the norm data. Does there exist $\widetilde C_\tau\in\mathcal N$ together with an equivalence \[ U(\widetilde C_\tau)\simeq C_\tau \qquad\text{in }\operatorname{CAlg}(\mathcal C)? \] This expresses the lifting question while retaining the underlying commutative algebra structure. A fully specified formulation requires identification of $\tau$, the intended normed-algebra category, and its relationship to $2$-completion; these choices are unresolved in the supplied record.

Short English statement

Does the commutative algebra structure on the $2$-complete motivic cofiber $S^0/\tau$ over ${\mathbb{C}}$ extend to a compatible normed algebra structure? The supplied record leaves the precise definition of $\tau$ and the normed framework unspecified.
