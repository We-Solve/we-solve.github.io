Short English statement

The question asks whether the support function of the Martínez--Maure example minimizes an integral depending on its gradient while remaining Lipschitz and singular, with the integrand's singular set consisting exactly of the example's four noncoplanar cusps. The supplied record leaves essential definitions and boundary conditions unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
