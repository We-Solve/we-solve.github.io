OpenAI claim summary for Math-2510

The finite-dimensional nonprojective module Z has Ext^i(Z,Z)=Ext^i(Z,Lambda)=0 for every i>0 over the explicit finite-dimensional field algebra. Additivity gives the requested Ext^i(Z,Z plus Lambda)=0, a counterexample to the universal implication. lean/docs/199.md documents precisely this counterexample and its ancillary properties. Within these assumptions, this is a claimed negative answer to the stated question.

Projectivity from simultaneous self-Ext and algebra-Ext vanishing

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. Over $\mathbb F_2(q,H_1,H_2)$, an explicit finite algebra and nonprojective Gorenstein-projective module are claimed to have all positive self-Ext and Ext into the algebra equal to zero.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Over $k=\mathbb F_2(q,H_1,H_2)$, with algebraically independent indeterminates, the source claims a finite-dimensional algebra $\Lambda$ and nonprojective finite-dimensional left module $Z$ with \[ \operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(Z,Z)=0= \operatorname{Ext}^i_\Lambda(Z,\Lambda)\qquad(i\ge1). \] The module is Gorenstein-projective, $\Lambda/\operatorname{rad}\Lambda\cong k^8$, and $(\operatorname{rad}\Lambda)^4\ne0$. All these conclusions persist after any extension of the coefficient field.

Outline of the argument

A parameterized complete resolution starts from a small algebra and is transformed by cone and triangular-module constructions. Powers of the transcendental parameters prevent unwanted recurrence, while uniform kernel calculations give total acyclicity and remove positive self-extensions; scalar extension preserves those calculations.

Formal verification

For family 199, the documented entries cover the characteristic-two rational-function-field Auslander--Reiten example, including field-extension persistence, and a separate symmetric Tachikawa counterexample. The listed Comparator files are AuslanderReiten.lean, Tachikawa.lean. Named declarations include OAI.ArExplicit.Statement.main, OAI.Tachikawa.main_theorem. This site has not executed the Lean code.
