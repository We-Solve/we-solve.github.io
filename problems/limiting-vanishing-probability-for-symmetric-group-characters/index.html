Math-6504

Limiting Vanishing Probability for Symmetric Group Characters

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $n\geq 1$, let \[ \mathcal P(n)=\{(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r):r\geq 1,\ \lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_r\geq 1,\ \lambda_i\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ \textstyle\sum_i\lambda_i=n\}, \qquad p(n)=|\mathcal P(n)|. \] Let $S_n$ be the symmetric group on $n$ letters. Its ordinary irreducible complex characters are indexed by $\lambda\in\mathcal P(n)$; write $\chi_\lambda$ for the corresponding character. For $\mu\in\mathcal P(n)$, let $K_\mu\subseteq S_n$ be the conjugacy class of permutations with cycle type $\mu$, and define $\chi_\lambda(\mu)=\chi_\lambda(g)$ for any $g\in K_\mu$.

Under the interpretation that $\lambda$ and $\mu$ are independent and each is uniform on $\mathcal P(n)$, define \[ P_n=\Pr\left(\chi_\lambda(\mu)=0\right) =\frac{\left|\{(\lambda,\mu)\in\mathcal P(n)^2:\chi_\lambda(\mu)=0\}\right|}{p(n)^2}. \] Problem. Determine the asymptotic behavior of $P_n$ as $n\to\infty$. In particular, does $\lim_{n\to\infty}P_n$ exist, and, if so, what is its value? The supplied literature assessment specifies uniform partitions and conjugacy classes. Independence is the natural product-sampling interpretation used in this formulation, but is not expressly stated in the extracted question; this qualification requires source review. No proposed value of the limit is supplied.

Distinction from the supplied background results. Uniform choice of a conjugacy class is different from choosing an element uniformly from $S_n$. With independent uniform choices of $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(S_n)$ and $g\in S_n$, the supplied excerpt reports that $\Pr(\chi(g)=0)\to 1$. It also reports an analogous result for $\operatorname{GL}(n,q)$: if $P_{n,q}$ is the proportion of pairs $(\chi,g)\in\operatorname{Irr}(\operatorname{GL}(n,q))\times\operatorname{GL}(n,q)$ with $\chi(g)=0$, then $\inf_q P_{n,q}\to 1$ as $n\to\infty$, where $q$ ranges over prime powers. These are supplied contextual assertions, not independently verified results here, and do not answer the uniform-class question. The supplied $S_4$ example gives the uniform-class probability $4/25=0.16$; its element-sampling numerical expression is damaged or inconsistent and is left unresolved.

Short English statement

Choose two partitions $\lambda,\mu$ of $n$ independently and uniformly. Determine how often the irreducible character $\chi_\lambda$ vanishes on the conjugacy class of cycle type $\mu$ as $n$ grows. Independence needs confirmation from the source. The supplied open-status assessment concerns uniform classes; the background result about uniform group elements uses a different probability measure.
