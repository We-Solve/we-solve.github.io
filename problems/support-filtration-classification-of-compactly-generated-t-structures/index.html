Math-3866

Support-Filtration Classification of Compactly Generated t-Structures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A$ be a connective bounded commutative differential graded ring, and let $\mathcal D=D(A)$ be its unbounded derived category of differential graded modules, equipped with derived tensor product $\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}$ and tensor unit $\mathbf 1=A$. With cohomological grading, the usual connective convention is $H^i(A)=0$ for $i>0$; cohomological boundedness means that $H^i(A)=0$ outside a finite interval. The supplied record does not explicitly specify these grading and boundedness conventions. Put \[ R=\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal D}(\mathbf 1)\cong H^0(A),\qquad X=\operatorname{Spec}(R). \] Here $\operatorname{End}$ denotes degree-zero endomorphisms, and $X$ has its Zariski topology.

A $t$-structure is a pair $(\mathcal U,\mathcal V)=(\mathcal D^{\leq 0},\mathcal D^{\geq 0})$ of full subcategories such that $\mathcal U[1]\subseteq\mathcal U$, $\mathcal V[-1]\subseteq\mathcal V$, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(\mathcal U,\mathcal V[-1])=0$, and every $M\in\mathcal D$ belongs to a distinguished triangle \[ U\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow W\longrightarrow U[1], \qquad U\in\mathcal U,\quad W\in\mathcal V[-1]. \] An object $C$ is compact if $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(C,-)$ commutes with arbitrary coproducts. The $t$-structure is compactly generated if there is a set $\mathcal S$ of compact objects such that \[ \mathcal V[-1]=\left\{Y\in\mathcal D:\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(C[k],Y)=0\text{ for every }C\in\mathcal S\text{ and every }k\geq 0\right\}. \]

Source question. For every connective bounded commutative differential graded ring $A$, are the compactly generated $t$-structures on $D(A)$ whose nonpositive aisle is a $t$-$\otimes$-ideal classified by filtrations by supports \[ f:\mathbb Z\longrightarrow 2^X,\qquad X=\operatorname{Spec}\left(\operatorname{End}_{D(A)}(\mathbf 1)\right)? \] Here $2^X$ is the power set of $X$. No nondegeneracy, boundedness, or completeness condition on the $t$-structures is supplied.

Unresolved qualifications. The original statement uses the term $t$-$\otimes$-ideal, whereas the clean statement says tensor ideal. These do not specify the same condition without further conventions. Closure under $U\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}M$ for every $U\in\mathcal U$ and every $M\in D(A)$ is a stronger requirement than closure only for $M$ in a specified nonpositive aisle; the latter also requires a reference $t$-structure to be identified. The supplied record does not settle the intended condition.

The record also does not define a filtration by supports, its monotonicity, or the support construction giving the proposed classification. A customary possible interpretation is a decreasing sequence $f(n+1)\subseteq f(n)$ of Thomason subsets, where a Thomason subset is a union of closed subsets with quasi-compact open complements. Another possible support condition is specialization closure. These possibilities are recorded for clarification and are not adopted as missing hypotheses. No endpoint or exhaustiveness condition on $f$ is supplied. Thus the displayed question preserves the supplied target, but its precise parameter space and classification correspondence require clarification before it becomes a fully specified bijection statement.

Short English statement

For a connective bounded commutative differential graded ring $A$, the question asks whether support filtrations on $\operatorname{Spec}(H^0(A))$ classify compactly generated $t$-structures on $D(A)$ satisfying the intended tensor compatibility condition. The meaning of that condition, the permitted support filtrations, and the classification correspondence remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that label.
