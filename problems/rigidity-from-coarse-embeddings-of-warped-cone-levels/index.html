Rigidity from Coarse Embeddings of Warped-Cone Levels

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is: "Does a rigidity theorem hold for quasi-isometric embeddings, or even coarse embeddings, between corresponding warped-cone level sequences?" The record does not specify the actions, the correspondence between levels, or the conclusion meant by rigidity. Consequently, it does not determine a single fully quantified conjecture.

The following definitions describe the terminology conditionally; they do not supply missing hypotheses for the intended problem. In a customary warped-cone construction, let a finitely generated group $\Gamma$, with finite symmetric generating set $S$, act by homeomorphisms on a compact metric space $(X,d_X)$. For $t>0$, the warped metric $d_{\Gamma,t}$ is the largest metric on $X$ satisfying \[ d_{\Gamma,t}(x,x')\leq t\,d_X(x,x'),\qquad d_{\Gamma,t}(x,sx)\leq 1 \quad(x,x'\in X,\ s\in S). \] The metric space $(X,d_{\Gamma,t})$ is a level of the warped cone. A second action $\Lambda\curvearrowright(Y,d_Y)$ gives levels $(Y,d_{\Lambda,u})$. This is a conventional model for the terminology; the source does not specify whether this model or additional restrictions on the spaces and actions are intended.

For metric-space sequences $(X_n,d_n)$ and $(Y_n,e_n)$, a uniform family of quasi-isometric embeddings consists of maps $f_n:X_n\to Y_n$ and constants $A\geq1$, $B\geq0$, independent of $n$, such that \[ A^{-1}d_n(x,x')-B \leq e_n(f_n(x),f_n(x')) \leq A d_n(x,x')+B \quad(n\in{\mathbb{N}},\ x,x'\in X_n). \] A uniform family of coarse embeddings consists of maps $f_n:X_n\to Y_n$ and nondecreasing functions $\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$, independent of $n$, with $\lim_{r\to\infty}\rho_-(r)=\infty$, such that \[ \rho_-(d_n(x,x')) \leq e_n(f_n(x),f_n(x')) \leq \rho_+(d_n(x,x')) \quad(n\in{\mathbb{N}},\ x,x'\in X_n). \] Neither definition requires coarse surjectivity.

Under these conventions, the question asks what rigidity conclusion, if any, follows from such embeddings when \[ (X_n,d_n)=(X,d_{\Gamma,t_n}),\qquad (Y_n,e_n)=(Y,d_{\Lambda,u_n}),\qquad t_n\to\infty,\quad u_n\to\infty. \] The use of uniform controls and diverging level parameters here explains a possible sequence-level interpretation; neither is explicitly quantified in the supplied question. The relationship between $t_n$ and $u_n$, the admissible actions, and the proposed rigidity conclusion remain unresolved. No equivalence of actions, relation between groups, or other specific conclusion can be asserted from this record alone.

Short English statement

The question asks whether embeddings between sequences of warped-cone levels force a rigidity relation between the underlying geometric or dynamical data. It asks about both quasi-isometric and coarse embeddings. The supplied record leaves the necessary hypotheses, the meaning of corresponding levels, and the intended rigidity conclusion unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
