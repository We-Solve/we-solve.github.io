Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record identifies $\rho_1$ as a knot invariant and associates the proposed identity with the perturbed Alexander invariant. It does not define $\rho_1$ or the quantity $P^{(1)}$, specify their normalizations, or state their coefficient rings. The superscript $(1)$ is retained as supplied; its meaning is unspecified.

Conjectural identity, with unresolved definitions. For the knots intended by the source, the proposed relation is \[ \rho_1(t^2)=\frac{t^2}{(1-t^2)^2}P^{(1)}(t). \] Both quantities are understood to concern the same knot, with knot dependence suppressed as in the supplied statement. The intended class of knots, any orientation or framing conventions, and the precise quantification over knots are not supplied. Consequently, this is a transcription of the proposed relation rather than a self-contained conjecture with fully specified hypotheses.

The role and domain of $t$, and the algebraic setting in which the equality is asserted, are also unspecified. If the expression is interpreted pointwise over ${\mathbb{C}}$, its displayed right-hand side is undefined at $t=\pm1$ without an additional extension convention. The record does not specify such a convention or whether the intended equality is instead an identity of rational functions or formal series. No interpretation or boundary extension is imposed here.

Short English statement

The proposed formula relates the knot invariant $\rho_1$, evaluated at $t^2$, to the unspecified quantity $P^{(1)}(t)$. The supplied record labels it an open conjecture, but definitions, normalizations, the intended knot class, and the meaning of the variable must be recovered before a precise self-contained statement is possible.
