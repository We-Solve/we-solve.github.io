Invariant and Smash-Product Hochschild Cohomology

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question omits hypotheses on its algebraic data. The following gives a conventional framework for interpreting its notation; these hypotheses are not independently confirmed by the supplied record.

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$, let $G$ be a finite group with $p\mid |G|$, and let $A$ be an associative unital $k$-algebra equipped with an action of $G$ by $k$-algebra automorphisms. Define the smash product $B=A\# kG$ to have underlying vector space $A\otimes_k kG$, with multiplication \[ (a\# g)(b\# h)=a\,g(b)\# gh \qquad(a,b\in A,\ g,h\in G). \] Suppose that $M$ is a $B$-bimodule, and regard it as an $A$-bimodule by restriction along $a\mapsto a\#1$. For an algebra $C$ and a $C$-bimodule $N$, write \[ C^e=C\otimes_k C^{\mathrm{op}},\qquad \operatorname{HH}^n(C,N)=\operatorname{Ext}_{C^e}^n(C,N),\qquad \operatorname{HH}^*(C,N)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\operatorname{HH}^n(C,N). \] Writing $u_g=1_A\#g$, the action $g\cdot m=u_g m u_{g^{-1}}$, together with the action on $A$, induces the usual action on Hochschild cohomology. In cochain degree $n$, this action is represented by \[ (g\cdot f)(a_1,\ldots,a_n) =u_g f\left(g^{-1}(a_1),\ldots,g^{-1}(a_n)\right)u_{g^{-1}}. \] Define \[ \operatorname{HH}^*(A,M)^G =\{x\in\operatorname{HH}^*(A,M):g\cdot x=x\text{ for every }g\in G\}. \]

Source question, with unresolved qualifications. In the modular case $p\mid |G|$, determine the relationship between \[ \operatorname{HH}^*(A,M)^G \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{HH}^*(A\# kG,M). \] The source calls these objects graded algebras. For an arbitrary bimodule $M$, however, the definitions above supply graded vector spaces without a specified internal cup product. Suitable compatible multiplicative structure on $M$, or an intended specialization of $M$, must be supplied before the graded-algebra formulation is determined. The source does not specify this structure, the full class of admissible $k,G,A,M$, or a particular comparison map, identity, inequality, or other relationship to be established. Accordingly, this is an unresolved comparison question in a conditional framework, not a fully specified conjectural assertion.

Short English statement

For a finite group acting on an algebra over a field whose characteristic divides the group order, compare the invariant Hochschild cohomology of the algebra with the Hochschild cohomology of its smash product. The source leaves the coefficient object $M$, the structure needed to obtain graded algebras, and the precise intended comparison insufficiently specified.
