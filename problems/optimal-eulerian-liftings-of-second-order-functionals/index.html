Optimal Eulerian Liftings of Second-Order Functionals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $u\colon\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^q\to{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be a map for which the second-order energy \[ E(u)=\int_\Omega H\left(\nabla^2u(x)\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x \] is defined. Here $\nabla^2u$ denotes the Hessian tensor, with components $\partial_i\partial_j u^\alpha$ for $1\leq i,j\leq q$ and $1\leq\alpha\leq d$; the source does not specify whether derivatives are classical or weak.

The supplied question asks for the largest convex, lower-semicontinuous, subadditive lifting of $E$, described in the supplied background as an optimal Eulerian lifting. Interpreting "largest" in the pointwise order, its target has the schematic form \[ F_*\in\mathscr L(E),\qquad F(v)\leq F_*(v) \quad\text{for every }F\in\mathscr L(E) \text{ and every admissible lifted argument }v, \] where $\mathscr L(E)$ denotes the intended class of convex, lower-semicontinuous, subadditive liftings.

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not define this class: it omits the lifted domain, its topology and addition, the representation of maps as lifted objects, and the precise condition expressed by "lifts $E$." Thus $\mathscr L(E)$ is a placeholder for missing source definitions, not a fully specified admissible class. The record also gives no hypotheses on $H$, no regularity assumptions on $\Omega$, no function space for $u$, and no boundary conditions. Consequently, the displayed target records the intended extremal question but cannot determine a unique mathematical problem without further source context.

Short English statement

For an energy depending on the Hessian of a map, determine its greatest convex, lower-semicontinuous, subadditive Eulerian lifting. The supplied record leaves the lifting framework and analytic hypotheses unspecified, so this is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
