Unstable Motivic Homotopy Exact-Sequence Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for an unstable motivic homotopy exact sequence. Its base field and admissible field hypotheses are not specified. The following formulation uses the conventional Nisnevich setting to explain the notation; it does not assert that the conjecture is intended over every field.

Fix a base field $k$, subject to hypotheses that remain to be recovered from the source, and let $\mathrm{Sm}_k$ denote the category of smooth schemes of finite type over $k$, equipped with the Nisnevich topology. An abelian sheaf $F$ is strictly $\mathbb A^1$-invariant if, for every $U\in\mathrm{Sm}_k$ and every integer $i\geq 0$, projection induces an isomorphism \[ H^i_{\mathrm{Nis}}(U,F)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^i_{\mathrm{Nis}}(U\times\mathbb A^1,F). \] Its contraction is the sheaf \[ F_{-1}(U)=\ker\!\left(F(U\times\mathbb G_m)\xrightarrow{1^*}F(U)\right), \] where $1^*$ is restriction along the unit section. Define $F_{-r}=(F_{-(r-1)})_{-1}$ for $r\geq 2$.

Write $K^M_j$ for the unramified Milnor $K$-theory sheaf and $K^M_j/24$ for the cokernel sheaf of multiplication by $24$. Write $\pi_n^{\mathbb A^1}(\mathbb A^n\setminus\{0\})$ for the $n$-th $\mathbb A^1$-homotopy sheaf of punctured affine space, conventionally pointed at $(1,0,\ldots,0)$. The symbol $GW_{n+1}^{n}$ is retained from the clean statement and is interpreted as a higher Grothendieck--Witt sheaf, with lower index denoting degree and upper index denoting the duality shift. The intended indexing convention and its field hypotheses require source confirmation.

Proposed statement, with unresolved base hypotheses. For every integer $n\geq 4$, do there exist morphisms of strictly $\mathbb A^1$-invariant sheaves \[ K^M_{n+2}/24\xrightarrow{\alpha_n} \pi_n^{\mathbb A^1}(\mathbb A^n\setminus\{0\}) \xrightarrow{\beta_n}GW_{n+1}^{n} \] that are exact at the middle term and whose $n$-fold contractions form a short exact sequence \[ 0\longrightarrow (K^M_{n+2}/24)_{-n} \xrightarrow{(\alpha_n)_{-n}} \left(\pi_n^{\mathbb A^1}(\mathbb A^n\setminus\{0\})\right)_{-n} \xrightarrow{(\beta_n)_{-n}} (GW_{n+1}^{n})_{-n} \longrightarrow 0? \] Here exactness of the uncontracted displayed sequence is interpreted minimally as $\operatorname{im}(\alpha_n)=\ker(\beta_n)$, since the supplied display has no endpoint zeros. Whether surjectivity of $\beta_n$ before contraction is also intended remains unresolved. The record does not describe the intended maps.

The original statement instead uses $K^{\mathbb A^1}_{n+2}/24$ and $GW_{n+1}$. The display above follows the supplied clean statement, but the record alone does not establish that these original symbols are equivalent to its replacements. The missing base hypotheses, indexing conventions, and endpoint interpretation prevent an unconditional precise formulation.

Short English statement

For each integer $n\geq 4$, the supplied conjecture asks for an exact sequence connecting Milnor $K$-theory modulo $24$, an unstable motivic homotopy sheaf of punctured affine space, and a higher Grothendieck--Witt sheaf, becoming short exact after $n$ contractions. The base field hypotheses, intended maps, and some notation remain unresolved. The supplied partially solved label is dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
