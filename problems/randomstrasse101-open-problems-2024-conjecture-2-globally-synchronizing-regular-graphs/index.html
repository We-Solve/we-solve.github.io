Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2024) — Conjecture 2 (Globally Synchronizing Regular Graphs)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not define "globally synchronizing" or specify the random regular graph model. The following is therefore a conditional formalization, using uniform simple labelled graphs and almost-everywhere synchronization of identical attractive Kuramoto oscillators. These interpretations require confirmation against the cited source.

For each even integer $n\geq 4$, let $\mathcal G_{n,3}$ be the set of simple undirected graphs on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ in which every vertex has degree $3$, and let $G_n$ be uniformly distributed on $\mathcal G_{n,3}$. The restriction to even $n$ is necessary because the sum of the degrees is $3n$.

For a graph $G\in\mathcal G_{n,3}$, consider phases in $\mathbb T^n$, where $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/(2\pi{\mathbb{Z}})$, evolving by \[ \frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}\theta_i}{{\,\mathrm{d}} t}=\sum_{j:\{i,j\}\in E(G)}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i),\qquad i=1,\ldots,n. \] Under the interpretation used here, call $G$ globally synchronizing if, for almost every initial phase vector with respect to normalized Haar measure on $\mathbb T^n$, the resulting solution satisfies \[ \lim_{t\to\infty}\max_{1\leq i,j\leq n}\left|e^{\mathrm{i}\theta_i(t)}-e^{\mathrm{i}\theta_j(t)}\right|=0. \] The source conjecture, conditional on these definitions, is \[ \lim_{\substack{n\to\infty\\ n\text{ even}}}\Pr\!\left(G_n\text{ is globally synchronizing}\right)=1. \] The probability here concerns the random graph; the almost-everywhere quantifier over initial phases is part of the proposed definition of global synchronization.

The source may instead use a condition formulated in terms of stable equilibria or another dynamical definition. No equivalence with the definition above is asserted. Without confirmation of the intended predicate and graph model, this remains a conditional statement-only draft.

As background, the supplied record attributes to Abdalla, Bandeira, Kassabov, Souza, Strogatz, and Townsend the claims that random Erdős--Rényi graphs are globally synchronizing above the connectivity threshold with high probability, and that uniform random $d$-regular graphs have this property with high probability for $d\geq 600$. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified results here; the record does not give their detailed hypotheses.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that a uniform random $3$-regular graph is globally synchronizing with probability tending to $1$ as its number of vertices tends to infinity through even integers. The draft uses a specified Kuramoto interpretation and a uniform simple labelled graph model, both requiring source confirmation. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied metadata, not an independently verified literature assessment.
