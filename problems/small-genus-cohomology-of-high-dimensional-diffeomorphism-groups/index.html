Math-4492

Small-Genus Cohomology of High-Dimensional Diffeomorphism Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ satisfy $n\geq 3$. For a nonnegative integer $g$, let \[ W_{g,1}=D^{2n}\mathbin{\#}_g(S^n\times S^n) \] denote the smooth manifold obtained by taking the connected sum of $g$ copies of $S^n\times S^n$ and removing the interior of a smooth $2n$-disk; conventionally $W_{0,1}=D^{2n}$. Its boundary is diffeomorphic to $S^{2n-1}$.

Use the customary convention that $\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{g,1})$ is the full group of smooth diffeomorphisms of $W_{g,1}$ that agree with the identity on a neighborhood of the boundary, equipped with the $C^\infty$ topology. Here $BG$ denotes the classifying space of a topological group $G$, and \[ H^*(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{g,1});{\mathbb{Q}}) =\bigoplus_{k\geq 0}H^k(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}(W_{g,1});{\mathbb{Q}}) \] is singular cohomology with its grading and cup product.

Problem. Determine this graded rational cohomology ring at small genus, particularly determine \[ H^*\!\left(B\operatorname{Diff}_{\partial}\left((S^n\times S^n)\setminus\operatorname{int}(D^{2n})\right);{\mathbb{Q}}\right) \qquad\text{for every integer }n\geq 3. \] The requested computation concerns all cohomological degrees. The source gives no numerical meaning to "small genus," so the genus-one case is an explicit target while the extent of the remaining small-genus request is unresolved. The collar convention for boundary fixing and the $C^\infty$ topology above are customary interpretations of notation that the supplied record does not explicitly define.

Short English statement

Determine the full rational cohomology ring of the classifying space of the boundary-fixing diffeomorphism group of a punctured connected sum of $g$ copies of $S^n\times S^n$, in dimension $2n\geq 6$. The explicit focus is $g=1$; the other genera intended by "small genus" are unspecified.
