Hodge Algebras of Conjugate Varieties

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks for which subfields $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ the graded algebras of $K$-rational Hodge classes are isomorphic for every pair of conjugate varieties. The record does not specify the class of varieties or the precise meaning of algebra isomorphism. The following formulation is conditional on interpreting the varieties as smooth projective complex varieties and the isomorphisms as degree-preserving $K$-algebra isomorphisms; these conventions require source confirmation.

Under these conventions, let $X$ be a smooth projective complex variety. Using extension of coefficients to regard singular cohomology $H^{2p}(X({\mathbb{C}}),K)$ as a subspace of $H^{2p}(X({\mathbb{C}}),{\mathbb{C}})$, define \[ H^{p,p}(X,K):=H^{2p}(X({\mathbb{C}}),K)\cap H^{p,p}(X),\qquad H^{*,*}(X,K):=\bigoplus_{p=0}^{\dim X}H^{p,p}(X,K). \] Here $H^{p,p}(X)$ is the $(p,p)$-summand of the complex Hodge decomposition. Equip this algebra with the cup product and give its $p$-th summand cohomological degree $2p$. For $\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}({\mathbb{C}})$, let $X^\sigma$ denote the complex variety obtained by applying $\sigma$ to the coefficients of defining equations for $X$.

With the stated conventions, determine the set \[ \left\{K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}\ \middle|\ \begin{gathered} K\text{ is a subfield, and for every smooth projective complex variety }X\\ \text{and every }\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}({\mathbb{C}}),\text{ there exists a degree-preserving}\\ K\text{-algebra isomorphism }H^{*,*}(X,K)\cong H^{*,*}(X^\sigma,K) \end{gathered} \right\}. \] No canonical isomorphism or compatibility among these isomorphisms is requested. This conditional formulation places no restriction $\sigma(K)=K$; whether the source intends such a restriction remains unconfirmed. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved, but that label and its associated literature assessment are supplied curation rather than independently verified mathematical conclusions.

Short English statement

Determine which subfields $K\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}$ make the graded algebra of $K$-valued Hodge classes invariant, up to isomorphism, under conjugation of every variety in the intended class. The displayed formulation uses smooth projective complex varieties and degree-preserving $K$-algebra isomorphisms as explicit provisional conventions; the supplied record leaves these details unspecified.
