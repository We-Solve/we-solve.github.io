The report explicitly adopts one interpretation using the Casazza--Fickus--Mixon--Tremain two-block DFT projection. For that particular construction, it proposes that every first-block principal compression indexed by $S$, $|S|=s\geq n$, has \[ P[S]=\frac1{n+1}I+K_S,\qquad K_S\geq0,\qquad\operatorname{rank}K_S=n-1. \] Consequently its eigenvalue $1/(n+1)$ has exact multiplicity $s-n+1$. The defining DFT matrices, projection normalization, complete index range, and admissible $n$ are not supplied in the summary, so this is not a statement about arbitrary projections.

For every bipartition, the report asserts that the two complementary-projection compression spectra have together at least two eigenvalues at least $n/(n+1)$. In the natural paving interpretation these are the two block compressions of $I-P$; the full index convention is not printed. It further says one block of the associated zero-diagonal reflection has norm at least \[ \frac{n-1}{n+1}. \] A reflection is represented by $2P-I$, up to overall sign, in the constant-half-diagonal projection convention. These normalization qualifications are essential to the claimed spectral consequence.

The report additionally claims a Weyl-stability corollary and an exact even/odd replication no-amplification lemma, but gives neither their hypotheses nor formulas. This alternative reading is not identified as the original example.
