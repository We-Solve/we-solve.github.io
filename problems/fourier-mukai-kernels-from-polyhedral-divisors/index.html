Math-6950

Fourier–Mukai Kernels from Polyhedral Divisors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be algebraically closed of characteristic zero, let $T$ be a torus, let $M=\operatorname{Hom}(T,\mathbb G_m)$ and $N=\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb G_m,T)$ be its dual character and cocharacter lattices, and let $Y$ be a normal semiprojective $K$-variety. Semiprojective means projective over an affine variety. Let \[ \mathcal D=\sum_i\Delta_i\otimes D_i \] be the source's polyhedral divisor, with polyhedral coefficients in $N_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ and common tail cone $\sigma$. For $u\in\sigma^\vee\cap M$, the usual evaluation is \[ \mathcal D(u)=\sum_i\left(\min_{v\in\Delta_i}\langle u,v\rangle\right)D_i. \] Put $\Delta=\sum_i\Delta_i$ and retain the source's toric variety \[ Z=\operatorname{TV}(N(\Delta),M), \] where $N(\Delta)$ denotes the normal fan. Its cones lie in $M_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$, so in this toric construction $M$ is the lattice of one-parameter subgroups of the torus of $Z$. Each $\Delta_i$ determines the stated semiample divisor $E_i$ on $Z$, and the source also writes $\mathcal D=\sum_i E_i\otimes D_i$.

For every $u\in\sigma^\vee\cap M$, use the source's corresponding curve germ \[ u:(\mathbb A^1_K,0)\longrightarrow Z, \] and its direct-image structure sheaf $u_*\mathcal O_{\mathbb A^1_K,0}$. Define \[ \mathcal U=\bigoplus_{u\in\sigma^\vee\cap M}u_*\mathcal O_{\mathbb A^1_K,0}, \qquad \mathcal O_Y(\mathcal D)=\bigoplus_{u\in\sigma^\vee\cap M}\mathcal O_Y(\mathcal D(u)). \] For rational divisors the customary divisorial-sheaf convention uses $\mathcal O_Y(\lfloor\mathcal D(u)\rfloor)$; the extract does not state this rounding convention explicitly.

The conjecture asks whether $\mathcal D$ induces a kernel $\mathcal K_{\mathcal D}\in D^b(Y\times Z)$ for which $\mathcal U\in D^b(Z)$ and \[ \mathcal O_Y(\mathcal D)\simeq \Phi_{\mathcal K_{\mathcal D}}(\mathcal U) =R(p_Y)_*\left(\mathcal K_{\mathcal D}\otimes^{L}L(p_Z)^*\mathcal U\right), \] where $p_Y,p_Z$ are the projections, with the intended compatibility with the $M$-grading.

The bounded-derived categories must be specified: the infinite direct sums and direct images from local curve germs are not automatically coherent, so membership in $D^b(\operatorname{Coh}Z)$ cannot be assumed. Proper-support, boundedness and grading conventions for the transform are also absent. The original extraction places $\mathcal D$ in the affine $T$-variety setting, but the precise proper-polyhedral-divisor conditions and derived-category enhancement are not supplied. These are unresolved foundations of the stated comparison, not hypotheses silently added to it.

Short English statement

Does a polyhedral divisor determine a Fourier--Mukai kernel taking the specified direct sum of toric curve-germ sheaves to its graded divisorial sheaf? The exact construction is retained, while coherence, derived-category, support, rounding and grading conventions require clarification.
