Math-4221

Are there infinitely many Wilson primes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.

A Wilson prime is a prime $p$ for which $(p-1)!\equiv-1\pmod{p^2}$. Wilson's theorem makes $w_p=((p-1)!+1)/p$ an integer for every prime $p$, and the stronger condition is $p\mid w_p$.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Are there infinitely many Wilson primes? [S2]

Short English statement

Are there infinitely many primes for which Wilson’s factorial congruence holds modulo the prime square?
