Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for further pairs $(M,\mathbb{P})$, where $M$ is a model and $\mathbb{P}\in M$ is a forcing notion, satisfying a condition denoted by $(*)$. Its statement is:
Find more models $M$ and forcing notions $\mathbb{P}\in M$ such that $(*)$ is true.
The condition $(*)$ is not defined in the supplied record. Neither the theory satisfied by $M$ nor any additional hypotheses on $M$ or $\mathbb{P}$ are specified. Moreover, the examples relative to which further examples are requested are absent. Thus this is an incomplete formulation: the intended condition and the relevant background hypotheses must be recovered before a precise mathematical problem can be stated. No interpretation of $(*)$, model domain, or additional forcing hypothesis is imposed here.

Short English statement

The source requests additional models and forcing notions belonging to those models that satisfy a condition labelled $(*)$. Because that condition and the surrounding hypotheses are missing, the intended mathematical target cannot be specified precisely from this record.
