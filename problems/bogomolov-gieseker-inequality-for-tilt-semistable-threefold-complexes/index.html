Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
BG inequality conjecture: let $X$ be a smooth projective threefold over $\mathbb C$, let $B,\omega\in\operatorname{NS}(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ with $\omega$ ample, and let $E$ be tilt-semistable with $\nu_{\omega,B}(E)=0$. Then $\operatorname{ch}^{B}_{3}(E)\leq\frac{1}{18}\operatorname{ch}^{B}_{1}(E)\cdot\omega^{2}$. The conjecture is known for $X=\mathbb P^{3}$, smooth quadrics, and étale quotients of abelian threefolds.

Formulation references: [S1], [S2].

Bogomolov–Gieseker Inequality for Tilt-Semistable Threefold Complexes

OpenAI's claim

On a smooth projective threefold with trivial canonical bundle, the ordinary and Todd-corrected large-volume charges are claimed to define full numerical stability conditions uniformly in a real twist and ample-direction sector. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $X$ be a smooth projective complex threefold, $H$ ample integral and $B_0\in N^1(X)_{\mathbb Q}$. With $\operatorname{ch}^B=e^{-B}\operatorname{ch}$, define $\mathcal B_{H,B}$ by tilting coherent sheaves at slope $H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^B/(H^3\operatorname{ch}_0)$, using positive versus nonpositive Harder--Narasimhan slopes. Put \[ \nu_{a,b}(E)=\frac{H\operatorname{ch}_2^{B_0+bH}(E)-a^2H^3\operatorname{ch}_0(E)/2}{H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^{B_0+bH}(E)}. \] OpenAI claims constants $k\in\mathbb Q_{\ge0}$ and $R_*>0$, depending only on $(X,H,B_0)$, such that, simultaneously for all $a>0$ and $b\in\mathbb R$, every finite-slope $\nu_{a,b}$-semistable object of slope zero satisfies \[ \operatorname{ch}_3^{B_0+bH}(E)\le(a^2/6+k)H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^{B_0+bH}(E), \] and the same inequality with $k=0$ holds whenever $a>R_*$, uniformly in real $b$. If $K_X\simeq\mathcal O_X$, then for each $(B_*,H_*)\in N^1(X)_{\mathbb R}\times\operatorname{Amp}(X)$ it claims open neighborhoods $V$ of $B_*$ and $W$ of $H_*$, together with a single $T>0$, such that all $B\in V$, $H\in W$ and $t\ge T$ admit geometric numerical Bridgeland stability conditions supported on the full real numerical Grothendieck space, with exact charges \[ Z_{B,tH}(E)=-\int_Xe^{-B-itH}\operatorname{ch}(E),\qquad Z^{\mathrm{LV}}_{B,tH}(E)=-\int_Xe^{-B-itH}\operatorname{ch}(E)(1+c_2(X)/24). \] For the first charge the heart is the second tilt at slope $[tH\operatorname{ch}_2^B-t^3H^3\operatorname{ch}_0/6]/[t^2H^2\operatorname{ch}_1^B]$ with the same positive/nonpositive cuts. Geometric means point sheaves are stable of phase one.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 055, 2026-09-24): Product embeddings and restriction-deformation methods control the full Grothendieck group, beyond the four polarized Chern degrees. A separate fixed-volume tilt estimate bounds the third-character error in proportion to the discriminant defect, and slope truncation preserves a threshold uniform in the object and twist.
