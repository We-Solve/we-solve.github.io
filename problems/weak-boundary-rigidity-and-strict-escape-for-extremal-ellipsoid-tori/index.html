Math-1538

Weak boundary rigidity and strict escape for extremal ellipsoid tori

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use $z_j=p_j+iq_j$, $\omega_0=\sum_j{\,\mathrm{d}} p_j\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}} q_j$, and the closed unit ball $B^4=\{|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2\leq1\}$. The corrected Clifford torus is \[ L=\{|z_1|^2=|z_2|^2=1/2\}\subset\partial B^4. \] The source asks whether $L$ is weakly boundary rigid: must every Hamiltonian-isotopic torus $K$ contained in $B^4$ lie entirely in $\partial B^4$? The aid uses ambient Hamiltonian images $K=\phi_H^1(L)$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^4$, with only the endpoint containment required. Requiring $\phi_H^1$ itself to be a diffeomorphism of the closed ball would preserve the boundary automatically and is not this nontrivial interpretation. The historical fact that $K$ cannot be entirely in the open ball is weaker than the desired whole-boundary conclusion.

The aid attributes an affirmative four-dimensional answer to the peer-reviewed extremal-torus theorem of Dimitroglou Rizell. It reports a July 2026 preprint of Faisal--Li giving the analogue in every ellipsoid and dimension. Fix an action normalization \[ E(a_1,\ldots,a_n)=\left\{z:\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{\pi|z_j|^2}{a_j}\leq1\right\},\quad a_j>0, \quad d=\left(\sum_ja_j^{-1}\right)^{-1}, \] \[ T_d=\{z:\pi|z_j|^2=d\text{ for every }j\}\subset\partial E. \] The reported weak-rigidity statement is $\phi_H^1(T_d)\subset E\Rightarrow\phi_H^1(T_d)\subset\partial E$. Equivalently, any image not contained in $\partial E$ has a point satisfying $\sum_j\pi|z_j|^2/a_j>1$: strict escape, not merely failure of interior containment.

The aid further reports actual boundary rigidity when the reciprocal parameters are ${\mathbb{Q}}$-linearly independent: \[ \sum_j\frac{m_j}{a_j}=0,\quad m_j\in{\mathbb{Z}} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad m_1=\cdots=m_n=0. \] Under this nonresonance assumption, any Hamiltonian image of $T_d$ contained in $E$ equals $T_d$ setwise. It also includes endpoints of exact Lagrangian isotopies: smooth Lagrangian embeddings $\iota_t:T^n\to{\mathbb{R}}^{2n}$, beginning at the inclusion, with $[\iota_t^*\lambda_0]$ constant, where $\lambda_0=\tfrac12\sum_j(p_j{\,\mathrm{d}} q_j-q_j{\,\mathrm{d}} p_j)$. The exactness/ambient-extension conventions must be retained; ordinary Lagrangian isotopy is not substituted.

Part (b) additionally asks to investigate other ellipsoid tori and nonremovable intersections. The balanced-product result and nonresonant refinement do not classify all tori or all such intersections. The preprint date and differing publication status of the reported results are material, and no current independent literature verification is claimed.

Short English statement

Must a Hamiltonian image of the Clifford torus that fits in the closed ball stay entirely on its boundary? The supplied aid reports an affirmative published four-dimensional result, a July 2026 ellipsoid preprint, and a stronger nonresonant refinement. The wider nonremovable-intersection question remains separate.
