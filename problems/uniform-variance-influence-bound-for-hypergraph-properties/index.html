Uniform variance-influence bound for hypergraph properties

OpenAI's claim

Uniform hypergraph influence: For fixed uniformity $r\ge3$, the paper claims a variance-to-influence inequality with denominator $(\log n)^{r/(r-1)}$ for all biases and all relabeling-invariant Boolean properties. Increasing properties therefore have the corresponding sharp threshold width.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For each fixed $r\ge3$, every $n\ge r$, every $0<p<1$, and every vertex-permutation-invariant Boolean property $f$ of an $r$-uniform hypergraph on $[n]$, the manuscript claims \[\operatorname{Var}_p(f)\le\frac{C_r}{(\log n)^{r/(r-1)}}I_p(f),\qquad I_p(f)=\sum_{e\in\binom{[n]}r}\mathbb P_p\left(f(x_{e\leftarrow1})\ne f(x_{e\leftarrow0})\right).\] The product measure is Bernoulli$(p)$ on hyperedges. Influence here contains no additional factor $p(1-p)$, and monotonicity is not required.

Outline of the argument

Uniform hypergraph influence: Restrictions leave every hyperedge touching a random vertex block free, so vertex permutations still create large free-edge orbits. The restricted Fourier estimate controls low degrees uniformly in the bias. An $r$-uniform degree argument lifts this to the original spectrum, while influence handles its high-degree remainder; monotone differentiation supplies the threshold consequence.
