Math-266

Optimal vector balancing extends from zonotopes to all zonoids

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A centered zonotope is a finite Minkowski sum of segments $[-v_j,v_j]\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$; a centered zonoid is a Hausdorff limit of such zonotopes. Assume full dimension, so the body is the unit ball of its Minkowski norm $\|x\|_K=\inf\{t>0:x\in tK\}$. For $r\geq1$ define \[ \beta_r(K)=\sup_{x_1,\ldots,x_r\in K} \min_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^r}\left\|\sum_{i=1}^r\varepsilon_i x_i\right\|_K. \] G. Schechtman's question asks whether an absolute $C>0$ satisfies, for every $n\geq1$ and every full-dimensional centered zonoid $Z\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^n$, \[ \beta_n(Z)\leq C\sqrt n. \] Thus signs may depend on the $n$ chosen vectors, while $C$ is uniform in dimension and body. The source motivates this by Spencer's bound for $Z=B_\infty^n$, reports a then-known $C\sqrt n\log\log n$ substitute in the large-$n$ regime, and says removal of a logarithmic factor in its problem 4 would imply the desired result.

The attached aid attributes to Victor Reis's May 2026 preprint the zonotope theorem: for every $d$-dimensional centered zonotope $P$ and integer $1\leq r\leq d$, \[ \beta_r(P)\leq C\sqrt{r\log(2d/r)} \] with an absolute constant. Its closure-extension lemma states that for full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex bodies and $0<a\leq b$, \[ aK\subseteq L\subseteq bK \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac ab\beta_r(K)\leq\beta_r(L)\leq\frac ba\beta_r(K). \] Hausdorff approximations of a fixed full-dimensional centered zonoid by centered zonotopes have such multiplicative sandwiches with $b/a\to1$. The aid therefore reports extension of Reis's estimate to zonoids with the same theorem constant. Taking $r=d=n$ gives the requested $O(\sqrt n)$ bound, with the fixed $\sqrt{\log2}$ absorbed into the absolute constant. This affirmative answer is a supplied preprint/extension claim requiring independent mathematical and source verification.

Short English statement

Ask for a universal square-root-dimension signing bound for vectors in any centered zonoid. The supplied aid attributes the zonotope estimate to a May 2026 preprint and uses continuity under multiplicative body approximation to extend it to all zonoids.
