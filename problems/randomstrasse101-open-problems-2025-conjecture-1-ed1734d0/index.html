Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2025) — Conjecture 1

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a prime $p\equiv 1\pmod 4$, let ${\mathbb{F}}_p={\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}}$. The Paley graph $G_p$ is the simple undirected graph with vertex set ${\mathbb{F}}_p$, in which distinct vertices $x,y$ are adjacent if $x-y$ is a nonzero square in ${\mathbb{F}}_p$. The congruence condition ensures that adjacency is symmetric. Define its clique number by \[ \omega(G_p)=\max\left\{|S|:S\subseteq{\mathbb{F}}_p,\ x-y\text{ is a nonzero square for all distinct }x,y\in S\right\}. \] The independence number $\alpha(G_p)$ is the largest size of a vertex set containing no adjacent pair. As noted in the supplied source, $G_p$ is isomorphic to its complement, so $\omega(G_p)=\alpha(G_p)$.

The supplied Conjecture 1 asks whether \[ \omega(G_p)=O\left((\log p)^k\right) \] for some fixed exponent $k>0$, as $p$ tends to infinity through primes congruent to $1$ modulo $4$. Explicitly, the conjecture is that there exist constants $C>0$, $k>0$, and $p_0\geq 2$ such that for every prime $p\geq p_0$ with $p\equiv1\pmod4$, \[ \omega(G_p)\leq C(\log p)^k. \] Here $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm; the constants are independent of $p$. This interprets the source's unspecified $\mathrm{polylog}(p)$ as a fixed power of $\log p$; no particular exponent is supplied. The source motivates this conjecture by comparison with a random graph of the same degree, but does not specify the random graph model. That comparison is background motivation and imposes no additional hypothesis on the conjecture.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether the largest clique in the Paley graph on $p$ vertices is bounded by a fixed power of $\log p$, uniformly over primes $p\equiv1\pmod4$. By self-complementarity, the same bound would hold for the independence number. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied status information, not an independently verified literature assessment.
