Math-1235

Factor persistence and the limits of single-summand Arakelov equality

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the complex Hodge-theoretic interpretation, let $U=Y\setminus\Gamma$, with $Y$ smooth projective of dimension $d$ and $\Gamma$ a normal-crossing boundary, and let $L=\omega_Y(\Gamma)$ be nef and big. The original refers to a preceding problem for these objects and for the map to a moduli stack $M_H$ of abelian varieties or manifolds with local Torelli. The attached aid says that preceding setup has a full-dimensional étale-chart qualifier; that qualifier must not be replaced by a merely generically finite map to the moduli stack.

For a sheaf of positive rank, define \[ \deg_L F=c_1(F)\cdot c_1(L)^{d-1},\qquad \mu_L(F)=\deg_L(F)/\operatorname{rk}F. \] An irreducible complex weight-one subvariation has logarithmic Higgs bundle $E^{1,0}\oplus E^{0,1}$, with $\theta:E^{1,0}\to E^{0,1}\otimes\Omega_Y^1(\log\Gamma)$, under the required extension/monodromy convention. The source's extracted symbol $\pi$ is reported to be a corrupted direct sum, not a map. When both ranks are nonzero, its inequality is reconstructed as \[ \mu_L(E^{1,0})-\mu_L(E^{0,1}) \leq\frac{\deg_L\Omega_Y^1(\log\Gamma)}d =\frac{c_1(L)^d}{d}. \] Determine when equality holds. The source proposes a Shimura conclusion for $U$ in its intended setup and reports curve and small-$g$ $\mathcal A_g$ cases. Whether the conclusion concerns an image, finite cover or $U$ itself, and which equality summands are required, are not specified in the isolated record.

The aid reports a factor-persistence lemma. For log pairs $(Y_i,\Gamma_i)$, of dimensions $d_i$, with nef and big $L_i=\omega_{Y_i}(\Gamma_i)$, the product log polarization has class $L=p_1^*L_1+p_2^*L_2$. A bundle pulled from factor one has slope \[ \mu_L(p_1^*F)=\binom{d_1+d_2-1}{d_1-1} \mu_{L_1}(F)\,c_1(L_2)^{d_2}. \] Consequently the aid reports that pullback of an irreducible equality subvariation on one factor remains irreducible and attains equality on the product.

It applies this to the maximal-Higgs rank-two factor of the regular-octagon genus-two Teichmüller curve and a modular curve, reporting a surface mapping generically finitely to $\mathcal A_3$ with one equality factor but non-special image. It explicitly disproves only the weaker generically-finite, single-summand implication. That surface is not a full-dimensional étale chart of $\mathcal A_3$, so it is not reported as a counterexample to the literal source qualifier. The exact prior setup and theorem hypotheses are absent, preventing a completed source-level classification.

Short English statement

Classify equality in the logarithmic Arakelov slope bound for an irreducible Hodge subvariation and assess the intended Shimura conclusion. The attached aid shows that one equality factor persists on products and reports a diagnostic against a weaker generically-finite interpretation, explicitly preserving the stronger source chart qualifier.
