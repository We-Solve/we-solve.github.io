Math-1384

Three-way divisibility and a list strengthening for even-hole-free graphs

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A hole in a finite simple graph is an induced cycle of length at least four; a graph is even-hole-free if it has no hole of even length. Write $\omega(H)$ for clique number. A $k$-division of a graph $H$ with an edge is a partition into $k$ possibly empty sets $V_1,\ldots,V_k$ such that $\omega(H[V_i])<\omega(H)$ for every $i$. A graph is $k$-divisible if every induced subgraph with at least one edge has a $k$-division. The edge exception is essential because singleton maximum cliques cannot be nonmonochromatic.

Hoang's source conjecture is \[ \forall G:\quad G\text{ even-hole-free}\ \Longrightarrow\ G\text{ is $3$-divisible}. \] Equivalently every induced $H\subseteq G$ with an edge has a three-color assignment in which no maximum-cardinality clique is monochromatic. This concerns maximum cliques, not all inclusionwise maximal cliques.

The aid attributes to Chudnovsky--Seymour (2023) the existence of a bisimplicial vertex in every nonempty even-hole-free graph, and to Chen--Lan--Zhong a May 2026 preprint explicitly confirming the conjecture. Bisimplicial means that the neighborhood is the union of two cliques, giving $d_H(v)\leq2(\omega(H)-1)$.

Its stronger candidate is: for any integer $q\geq1$, if $G$ is a graph such that every induced subgraph $J\subseteq G$ with an edge has a vertex $v$ with \[ d_J(v)\leq q(\omega(J)-1), \] then for every induced $H$ with an edge and every assignment of lists $L(v)$ of $q+1$ distinct colors to its vertices, there is $c(v)\in L(v)$ such that no maximum clique of $H$ is monochromatic. In particular it reports hereditary three-list-colorability of the maximum-clique hypergraph for even-hole-free graphs. The lists need not share one common palette. The graph $C_5$ is even-hole-free with clique number two and cannot properly be two-colored, showing that three cannot be replaced by two for this class. These are supplied literature and research-aid conclusions requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

Every induced subgraph with an edge should admit three parts, each missing all maximum cliques. The supplied aid reports Hoang's conjecture solved and a stronger version permitting arbitrary three-element color lists; it does not assert the analogous condition for every maximal clique.
