Sharp Moments of Zeta and Character Averages

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the Riemann zeta function, let $i^2=-1$, and let $\log$ denote the natural logarithm. For $0\leq q\leq1$, define \[ M_q(T)=\frac1T\int_T^{2T}\left(\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}\left|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+it+ih\right)\right|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} h\right)^q\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] The first supplied estimate is \[ M_q(T)\ll A_q(T),\qquad A_q(T)=\left(\frac{\log T}{1+(1-q)\sqrt{\log\log T}}\right)^q. \] The natural asymptotic interpretation is $T\to\infty$; the record gives no explicit lower threshold for $T$. The displayed expression is real-valued for $T\geq e$.

For a prime $r$, let $\chi$ range over Dirichlet characters modulo $r$, and let $\chi_0$ be the principal character. For the supplied range $1\leq x\leq r$, put \[ L=\min\{x,r/x\},\qquad S_\chi(x)=\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}\\1\leq n\leq x}}\chi(n). \] For $r>2$ and $0<q\leq1$, define \[ C_q(r,x)=\frac1{r-2}\sum_{\substack{\chi\ ({\rm mod}\ r)\\\chi\ne\chi_0}}|S_\chi(x)|^{2q}. \] The second supplied estimate is \[ C_q(r,x)\ll B_q(r,x),\qquad B_q(r,x)=\left(\frac{x}{1+(1-q)\sqrt{\log\log(10L)}}\right)^q. \] Here $U\ll V$ means that $U\leq K V$ for a positive constant $K$; which parameters $K$ may depend on is not specified in the record. The source does not specify whether $x$ is real or integer; the displayed sum has a meaning in either case.

Supplied sharpness conjecture, attributed to Harper. Are both displayed upper estimates sharp for every $0\leq q\leq1$, including their displayed logarithmic factors? An order-of-magnitude interpretation would ask for corresponding lower estimates $M_q(T)\gg A_q(T)$ and $C_q(r,x)\gg B_q(r,x)$ in the intended parameter regimes. The record does not specify whether these lower estimates should hold throughout the ranges, along suitable sequences, or with constants uniform in $q$.

Unresolved qualifications. A lower estimate throughout the stated character range cannot be the literal intended assertion: at $x=r$, every nonprincipal character has $S_\chi(r)=0$, whereas $B_q(r,r)>0$ for $q>0$. No replacement range or normalization is supplied. Also, the stated allowance of every prime includes $r=2$, where the normalization $1/(r-2)$ is undefined; the definition above is consequently restricted to the meaningful case $r>2$, pending clarification. At $q=0$, vanishing character sums produce $0^0$, for which no convention is supplied. Thus the full endpoint-inclusive conjecture requires clarification before it becomes a precise two-sided assertion.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture, attributed to Harper, asks whether two moment upper bounds have the correct order, including their logarithmic factors, for every $0\leq q\leq1$. The intended meaning of sharpness and uniformity is unspecified. The character formulation also needs clarification at $r=2$, at $q=0$, and near the endpoint $x=r$, where a matching positive lower bound fails.
