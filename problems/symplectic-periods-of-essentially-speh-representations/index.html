Symplectic Periods of Essentially Speh Representations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a representation $\theta$ of a group $G$ on a vector space $V_\theta$ over a coefficient field $k$, and a subgroup $H\subseteq G$, an $H$-invariant linear functional is a map $\ell:V_\theta\to k$ satisfying \[ \ell(\theta(h)v)=\ell(v)\qquad(h\in H,\ v\in V_\theta). \] A period means a nonzero such functional, subject to any continuity requirements of the intended representation category. In the general linear group setting, the usual symplectic subgroup is \[ \operatorname{Sp}_{2n}(F)=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_{2n}(F):g^{\mathsf T}J_ng=J_n\},\qquad J_n=\begin{pmatrix}0&I_n\\-I_n&0\end{pmatrix}. \] Thus, if this is the intended ambient setting, having a symplectic period means \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Sp}_{2n}(F)}(V_\theta,k)\ne\{0\}, \] where $k$ carries the trivial action and the Hom space is interpreted in the intended category. The supplied record does not specify $F$, $k$, the ambient group, or that category; this description is conditional and does not supply those missing hypotheses.

Retain the source notation $Q(\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_{2r})$. For a nonempty list of length $2r$, the adjacent-pair condition is \[ Q(\Delta_{2i+1},\Delta_{2i+2})\text{ is essentially Speh} \qquad\text{for every }i\in\{0,\ldots,r-1\}. \] Let $\mathcal G'$ denote the source's class of representations $Q(\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_{2r})$ built from ladder representations and satisfying this condition. The question is whether \[ \forall\theta\in\mathcal G',\qquad \operatorname{Hom}_{H_{\mathrm{sp}}}(V_\theta,k)\ne\{0\}, \] where $H_{\mathrm{sp}}$ denotes the intended symplectic subgroup of the ambient group of $\theta$, and $k$ is the intended coefficient field with trivial action.

The supplied record does not define the objects $\Delta_j$, the construction $Q$, ladder representations, or the qualification "essentially Speh." Consequently, it does not determine membership in $\mathcal G'$ precisely. It also does not specify whether $r=0$ is allowed. These qualifications remain unresolved; the displayed assertion records the source's intended question rather than a complete standalone conjecture with fixed ambient data. The dated literature assessment additionally describes the representations as "generic," but the supplied mathematical statement does not explicitly impose genericity.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every representation in the specified ladder-based class, whose adjacent pairs are essentially Speh, admits a nonzero invariant functional for the relevant symplectic subgroup. The ambient representation setting and several defining terms are missing, so this remains a qualified statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
