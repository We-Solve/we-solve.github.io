Parameter Dependence of Directed-Polymer Endpoint Limits

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns a directed polymer in $(1+1)$ dimensions, meaning one temporal and one spatial dimension. The symbols $\beta$ and $\alpha$ denote parameters whose definitions and admissible ranges are not supplied.

Source question. In the $(1+1)$-dimensional directed-polymer convergence diagram, does the putative scaling limit at the right-hand end depend on the parameters $\beta$ and $\alpha$?

Unresolved qualifications. The referenced convergence diagram is absent from the supplied record. The polymer model, environment law, roles of $\beta$ and $\alpha$, limiting regime, rescaling, observable, and mode of convergence are unspecified. The phrase "right-hand end" refers to a position in that missing diagram; it does not by itself identify a polymer endpoint observable. The record also does not specify whether dependence means dependence of a limiting law, dependence up to deterministic rescaling, or another equivalence criterion. Consequently, a complete quantified statement cannot be recovered from the supplied evidence. The existence of the putative limit is not asserted.

Short English statement

The question asks whether an unspecified directed-polymer scaling limit depends on $\beta$ and $\alpha$. Its precise meaning requires the missing convergence diagram and definitions of the model, parameters, scaling, and limiting object. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
