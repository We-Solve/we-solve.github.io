Math-7483

Genus-Reducing Local-Knot Concordance in Orientable Three-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A local knot in a three-manifold $M$ is an embedded circle contained in the interior of an embedded three-ball in $M$. Identifying such a ball with a standard ball gives a corresponding classical knot in $S^3$.

The supplied question asks whether the genus-saving cobordism phenomenon reported for local knots in non-orientable three-manifolds can also occur in an orientable three-manifold. The record does not specify enough conventions to give an unconditional, uniquely determined formalization. The following gives an explicit interpretation whose conventions require confirmation.

Fix a category of embeddings, for example smooth embeddings or locally flat topological embeddings. For oriented local knots $K_0,K_1\subset M$, let $I=[0,1]$ and define \[ g_M(K_0,K_1)=\min\left\{g(\Sigma):\begin{array}{l} \Sigma\subset M\times I\text{ is a compact, connected, orientable,}\\ \text{properly embedded surface in the chosen category,}\\ \partial\Sigma=(-K_0)\times\{0\}\sqcup K_1\times\{1\} \text{ as oriented boundary} \end{array}\right\}, \] with value $+\infty$ if no such surface exists. Here $-K_0$ denotes reversal of the knot orientation. Given chosen ball identifications, let $\widehat K_0,\widehat K_1\subset S^3$ be the corresponding oriented classical knots, and define $g_{S^3}(\widehat K_0,\widehat K_1)$ using the same conventions in $S^3\times I$.

Under this orientable embedded-surface interpretation, the proposed existence question is whether there are an orientable three-manifold $M$ and two local knots $K_0,K_1\subset M$, with corresponding classical knots, such that \[ g_M(K_0,K_1)<g_{S^3}(\widehat K_0,\widehat K_1)<+\infty. \] The allowed class of three-manifolds, the embedding category, the orientation conventions, and the admissible cobordisms are unresolved in the supplied record. Thus this inequality is an explicit candidate interpretation, not an independently established equivalent formulation of the source question. In particular, concordance ordinarily means an annular cobordism, whereas positive-genus cobordisms are also suggested by the source wording; the intended restriction is not supplied.

Short English statement

Can an orientable three-manifold provide a cobordism between two knots contained in balls whose genus is smaller than the least possible genus between their corresponding classical knots in $S^3$? This expresses a candidate reading of the supplied question. The category of embeddings, permitted manifolds, and surface conventions require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; that label is not independently verified here.
