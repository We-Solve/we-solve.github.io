Local Complexity of Functional Estimation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks whether the dependence on a reference distribution $P_0$ observed in testing rates also occurs when estimating the functional $\rho(P,P_0)$. It does not specify a statistical experiment, distribution class, loss, localization, or admissible family of functionals. Consequently, it determines a research question rather than a uniquely specified minimax problem.

A conditional framework for making the estimation question precise is as follows. Let $\mathcal P$ be a specified class of probability distributions, let $P_0\in\mathcal P$, and let $\rho:\mathcal P\times\mathcal P\to T$ take values in a specified measurable target space $T$. For each sample-size index $n$ and unknown $P\in\mathcal P$, let $Q_{P,n}$ denote the law of the observed data. Specify a loss $L:T\times T\to[0,\infty]$ and a nonempty local parameter set $\mathcal U_n(P_0)\subseteq\mathcal P$. Whenever the requisite measurability conditions hold, define \[ R_n(P_0)=\inf_{\widehat\theta_n}\;\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_n(P_0)} \mathbb E_{Q_{P,n}}\!\left[L\!\left(\widehat\theta_n,\rho(P,P_0)\right)\right], \] where the infimum is over admissible measurable estimators based on the observed data. Whether those estimators may use $P_0$ must also be specified. These ingredients provide a definition template; they are not additional hypotheses established by the supplied source excerpt.

Research question. Determine for which functionals and distribution classes the local minimax estimation rate exhibits substantial dependence on $P_0$. The registry formulation additionally asks how fuzzy-testing rates transition to functional-estimation rates as the null radius increases.

Unresolved qualifications. The source does not define substantial dependence, the relevant asymptotic regime, the local parameter sets, or the null radius. It also does not specify the fuzzy-testing null and alternative hypotheses, their separation, or the permitted testing errors. Thus the second question has no uniquely defined testing rate or transition in the supplied evidence. Its explicit formulation appears in the registry statement but not in the supplied original quotation.

Short English statement

The question is whether estimating $\rho(P,P_0)$ can have a rate that depends substantially on the reference distribution $P_0$, as the supplied account says occurs in testing. The registry also asks about a transition from fuzzy testing to estimation as the null radius grows. Both questions require further statistical specifications before they become precise minimax problems.
