Flows in Signed Graphs and Binary Matroids

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A signed graph is a graph $G$ equipped with a sign map $\sigma:E(G)\to\{+1,-1\}$. A binary matroid is a matroid representable over ${\mathbb{F}}_2$. For either type of object $X$, write $E(X)$ for its edge set or matroid ground set, respectively, and write $w=(w_e)_{e\in E(X)}$ for the supplied nonnegative weights. The record does not specify the scalar domain of these weights, finiteness assumptions, or graph conventions concerning loops and parallel edges.

Let $\mathcal D$ denote the source-intended class of pairs $(X,w)$, where $X$ is a signed graph or binary matroid and $w$ is a nonnegative weight assignment. Write $C(X,w)$ for the unspecified cut condition, $H(X,w)$ for the unspecified assertion that the weights are Eulerian, and $\mathcal I(X,w)$ and $\mathcal F(X,w)$ for the unspecified sets of feasible integer and fractional flows. These symbols are placeholders for definitions missing from the supplied record; they do not supply a flow model.

In the order given by the supplied statement, the three questions have the following logical form: \[ \begin{aligned} \text{(Q1)}\quad &\forall (X,w)\in\mathcal D,\quad C(X,w)\Longrightarrow\mathcal I(X,w)\ne\varnothing,\\ \text{(Q2)}\quad &\forall (X,w)\in\mathcal D,\quad C(X,w)\Longrightarrow\mathcal F(X,w)\ne\varnothing,\\ \text{(Q3)}\quad &\forall (X,w)\in\mathcal D,\quad \left(C(X,w)\land H(X,w)\right)\Longrightarrow\mathcal I(X,w)\ne\varnothing. \end{aligned} \] The supplied wording asks for an integer flow both in (Q1) and in (Q3), with an additional Eulerian hypothesis only in (Q3). Formulation limitation. Short English statement

For signed graphs and binary matroids, the supplied record asks whether a cut condition guarantees integer or fractional flows, and whether Eulerian weights guarantee an integer flow under the same cut condition. The necessary definitions are missing, and the two integer-flow questions overlap as written. The supplied status is partially_solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
