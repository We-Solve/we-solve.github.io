Source question. For settings such as Bergman spaces and wavelet frames where Beurling density is not the correct notion, does a critical density separating sampling from interpolation exist?

The question asks whether an appropriate density threshold can distinguish sampling from interpolation in settings, exemplified by Bergman spaces and wavelet frames, where the source regards Beurling density as inadequate.
