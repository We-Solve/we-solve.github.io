Math-341

Regular Mather functions, convex duality, and integrability

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Patrick Bernard's Question 5 concerns an analytic Lagrangian $L:\mathbb T^n\times{\mathbb{R}}^n\times\mathbb T\to{\mathbb{R}}$, $L=L(x,v,t)$, satisfying Mather's conditions. In the conventional time-periodic Tonelli interpretation, $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$, $D^2_{vv}L>0$, $L$ is superlinear in $v$, and the Euler--Lagrange flow has the completeness needed for the minimizing-measure theory. "Positive definite" concerns the velocity Hessian, rather than the sign of $L$. Let $\mathcal I$ be the invariant probability measures of the extended flow with finite relevant action, and identify $c\in H^1(\mathbb T^n;{\mathbb{R}})$ with a vector in ${\mathbb{R}}^n$. Define \[ \alpha_L(c)=-\inf_{\mu\in\mathcal I}\int(L(x,v,t)-c\cdot v)\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu. \] The question is whether analyticity of $\alpha_L$ and positive definiteness of $D^2\alpha_L(c)$ for every $c\in{\mathbb{R}}^n$ force complete integrability of the Lagrangian system. The source does not specify the regularity or nonautonomous meaning of complete integrability, so a smooth Liouville conclusion and a continuous invariant-graph foliation cannot silently be identified.

The aid gives convex-duality consequences. Under the hypotheses above, \[ \nabla\alpha_L:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to{\mathbb{R}}^n \] is a global analytic diffeomorphism. Its Legendre dual \[ \beta(h)=\sup_c\{c\cdot h-\alpha_L(c)\} \] is analytic, $\nabla\beta=(\nabla\alpha_L)^{-1}$, and \[ D^2\beta(h)=\left(D^2\alpha_L(c)\right)^{-1}\quad(h=\nabla\alpha_L(c)). \] For every minimizing measure at fixed $c$, its rotation vector $\rho(\mu)=\int v\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu$ equals $h=\nabla\alpha_L(c)$, and its unmodified action equals $c\cdot h-\alpha_L(c)=\beta(h)$. Equality of rotation vectors and actions does not assert uniqueness of the measure or its support. Pointwise positive-definite Hessian does not add an unstated uniform lower bound on all of ${\mathbb{R}}^n$.

The aid reports a positive autonomous two-torus special case via Massart--Sorrentino: the dual regularity yields $C^0$ integrability, understood as a continuous foliation of the cotangent bundle by invariant Lagrangian graphs of the appropriate Lipschitz regularity. This is not asserted to prove smooth complete integrability for general time-periodic $n$-dimensional systems.

Its explicit spatially homogeneous time-periodic family is \[ L_{B,a}(v,t)=\tfrac12(v-a(t))^TB(t)^{-1}(v-a(t)), \] where $B(t)$ is an analytic periodic symmetric positive-definite matrix and $a(t)$ an analytic periodic vector. With averages over one unit period, \[ \overline B=\int_0^1B(t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t,\quad \overline a=\int_0^1a(t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t,\qquad \alpha_{L_{B,a}}(c)=\tfrac12c^T\overline Bc+\overline a\cdot c. \] Momentum $p=B(t)^{-1}(v-a(t))$ is constant, and $x(t)=x(0)+\int_0^t(B(s)p+a(s))\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s$ modulo ${\mathbb{Z}}^n$, giving the stated smooth integrability of this family. All coefficient pairs with the same two averages have identical $\alpha$ functions, although their time-resolved coefficients differ. This information loss is not a counterexample to the original implication, since every member of the exhibited family is integrable.

Short English statement

Bernard asks whether an analytic strictly convex Mather function forces complete integrability. The aid derives analytic convex duality and uniform rotation data for minimizing measures, reports a continuous-integrability theorem in the autonomous two-torus case, and gives explicitly integrable time-periodic examples whose Mather function remembers only averaged coefficients. The general target remains distinct.
