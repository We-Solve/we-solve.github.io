Math-1546

Grone--Merris majorization and a sharp complete-bipartite Laplacian-energy gap

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite simple graph with $n\ge1$ vertices, $m$ edges and degrees $d(v)$. Its combinatorial Laplacian is $L=D-A$, with eigenvalues $\lambda_1\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n\ge0$. Define the conjugate degree sequence, padded to length $n$, by \[ d_i^*=\#\{v:d(v)\ge i\},\qquad1\le i\le n. \] Both sequences sum to $2m$. Majorization $\lambda\prec d^*$ means \[ \sum_{i=1}^k\lambda_i\le\sum_{i=1}^kd_i^* =\sum_v\min(d(v),k)\quad(1\le k<n), \qquad\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i=\sum_{i=1}^nd_i^*. \] Put $\mu=2m/n$ and $E_L(G)=\sum_i|\lambda_i-\mu|$.

Question 6, Grone--Merris conjecture. Is $\lambda\prec d^*$? The source reports the workshop consequence \[ E_L(G)\le B(G):=\sum_{i=1}^n|d_i^*-\mu|. \] The supplied aid attributes an affirmative theorem for every finite simple graph to Hua Bai (2011). This is a named literature result as supplied, not a new proof by this draft.

Supplied exact energy and equality refinement. The degree-only bound can be written \[ B(G)=2\max_{0\le k\le n} \left(\sum_v\min(d(v),k)-\frac{2mk}{n}\right). \] For a decreasing sequence $x$ of mean $\mu$, define its mean-crossing index set \[ I(x)=\{k\in\{0,\ldots,n\}:\#\{i:x_i>\mu\}\le k \le\#\{i:x_i\ge\mu\}\}. \] The aid asserts that equality $E_L(G)=B(G)$ holds precisely when some $k\in I(\lambda)\cap I(d^*)$ has a tight Grone--Merris prefix, \[ \sum_{i=1}^k\lambda_i=\sum_{i=1}^kd_i^*. \] The inclusive mean-crossing convention handles repeated entries equal to $\mu$, and $k=0,n$ handles the edgeless/degenerate case without introducing undefined indices.

For the complete bipartite graph $K_{a,b}$, $1\le a\le b$, the aid gives the exact deficit \[ B(K_{a,b})-E_L(K_{a,b})=2a(a-1). \] Hence equality in this family occurs exactly for $a=1$, the stars. This is a family-specific equality conclusion, not a claim that only stars attain equality among all simple graphs.

Short English statement

The source asks whether Laplacian eigenvalues are majorized by the conjugate degree sequence; the aid cites Bai's affirmative theorem. Its refinements give a degree-only Laplacian-energy bound, an exact tight-prefix equality test, and a complete-bipartite gap showing equality there exactly for stars.
