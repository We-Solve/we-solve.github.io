Uniform Stability of Linearized Quasicontinuum Operators

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write \[ A:=\phi''(1)+4\phi''(2), \] where the source presupposes that these second derivatives exist and that the inequality $A>0$ is meaningful. The function $\phi$, its domain, and its relation to the underlying interaction model are not specified. Let $L_N$ denote the source's linearized force-based quasicontinuum (QCF) operator $L$, with its dependence on the parameter $N$ made explicit; the operator formula and the meaning of $N$ are not supplied.

The source asks whether \[ A>0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{$L_N$ inherits uniform stability or positive definiteness as $N\to\infty$.} \] This is an incompletely specified question: the reference operator from which stability is to be inherited, the admissible models, the spaces, the norms, the boundary conditions, and the treatment of possible null modes are absent.

For clarity, possible meanings can be distinguished without selecting one as the intended statement. If $L_N:V_N\to W_N$ acts between specified normed spaces, a uniform stability estimate could mean that there exist $c>0$ and $N_0$, independent of $N$, such that \[ \|L_Nu\|_{W_N}\ge c\|u\|_{V_N} \qquad(N\ge N_0,\ u\in V_N). \] The spaces, their norm scaling, and any additional requirements such as invertibility would have to be specified. If instead $L_N$ acts on a real inner product space $V_N$, quadratic-form positive definiteness means \[ \langle L_Nu,u\rangle_N>0 \qquad(0\ne u\in V_N), \] whereas uniform coercivity would require \[ \langle L_Nu,u\rangle_N\ge c\|u\|_{V_N}^{2} \qquad(N\ge N_0,\ u\in V_N) \] for an $N$-independent $c>0$. These are conditional definitions only; the source does not identify the intended criterion. It also does not specify whether positive definiteness refers to a quadratic form or whether a spectral property is intended. Consequently, the supplied scalar inequality alone does not determine a precise operator proposition to assess.

The supplied dated curation describes proved one-dimensional QCF stability and spectral results and an unresolved general inheritance question, especially for multidimensional models. This qualification is retained as a supplied assessment, not as an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion; the intended dimensional scope remains unspecified.

Short English statement

Assuming $\phi''(1)+4\phi''(2)>0$, the source asks whether linearized force-based quasicontinuum operators retain stability or positive definiteness as $N\to\infty$. The operator, model, boundary conditions, norms, and intended positivity criterion are missing, so a unique precise formulation cannot yet be given. The supplied classification is partially solved, with a dated distinction between one-dimensional results and the broader unresolved question.
