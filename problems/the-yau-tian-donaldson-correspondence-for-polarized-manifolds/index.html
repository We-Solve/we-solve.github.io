The Yau–Tian–Donaldson Correspondence for Polarized Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A polarized manifold $(X,L)$ is understood here in the usual projective sense: $X$ is a smooth complex projective manifold and $L$ is an ample holomorphic line bundle on $X$. Write $c_1(L)\in H^2(X,{\mathbb{R}})$ for its real first Chern class. A constant-scalar-curvature Kähler metric in this class is a Kähler form $\omega$ satisfying \[ [\omega]=c_1(L),\qquad \operatorname{Scal}(\omega)\equiv s \quad\text{for some }s\in{\mathbb{R}}. \] Here the scalar curvature is that of the Riemannian metric determined by $\omega$ and the complex structure.

The stability terminology requires a qualification. A test configuration of exponent $r\geq 1$ for $(X,L)$ is a flat projective family $\pi:\mathcal X\to{\mathbb{A}}^1_{{\mathbb{C}}}$, equipped with a ${\mathbb{C}}^*$-action covering the standard action on the base and a relatively ample equivariant line bundle $\mathcal L$, whose restriction over ${\mathbb{A}}^1_{{\mathbb{C}}}\setminus\{0\}$ is equivariantly isomorphic to the product family with polarization $L^{\otimes r}$. Its Donaldson--Futaki invariant is denoted by $\operatorname{DF}(\mathcal X,\mathcal L)$, with the sign convention in which stability requires positivity. Under a usual strict convention, K-stability requires this invariant to be positive for every nontrivial admissible test configuration. K-polystability instead requires nonnegativity, with equality permitted precisely for product configurations, including products induced by nontrivial ${\mathbb{C}}^*$-actions on $(X,L)$. A trivial configuration has trivial action on $X$, allowing a change of linearization on the line bundle. The admissible class of configurations, including normality and treatment of configurations trivial away from subsets of codimension at least two, must also be specified to obtain an exact definition.

Source question (Yau--Tian--Donaldson conjecture, as supplied). For every polarized manifold $(X,L)$, is the following equivalence true? \[ \left(\exists\,\omega\text{ K\"ahler on }X:\ [\omega]=c_1(L),\ \operatorname{Scal}(\omega)\text{ is constant}\right) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (X,L)\text{ is K-stable}. \] Unresolved qualification. The supplied record does not define its use of "K-stable," specify the admissible test configurations, or impose a discreteness hypothesis on automorphisms. In particular, it does not resolve whether K-polystability was intended. The displayed question preserves the source wording; it does not silently replace that wording by K-polystability, uniform stability, or a relative stability condition. These choices must be resolved before this can serve as a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether a polarized complex projective manifold admits a constant-scalar-curvature Kähler metric in its polarization class exactly when it is K-stable. The meaning of stability needs clarification, especially the distinction between strict K-stability and K-polystability when automorphisms are present. This is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
