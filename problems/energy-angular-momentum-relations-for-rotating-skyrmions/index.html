Energy-Angular Momentum Relations for Rotating Skyrmions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for an energy--angular momentum relation for skyrmions and its consequences for rotating Landau--Lifshitz motion. It does not specify the underlying spatial domain, the energy functional, the angular momentum, the admissible skyrmion class, or the evolution equation. Consequently, the following is a conditional formulation of the target, not a fully specified variational problem.

Let $\mathcal S$ denote the admissible class intended by the source, let $E$ denote its energy, and let $j(u)\geq 0$ denote the magnitude of its angular momentum. These are placeholders for definitions absent from the supplied record; in particular, no choice of angular momentum or norm is asserted. For an attainable magnitude $s\in j(\mathcal S)$, write formally \[ M(s):=\inf\{E(u):u\in\mathcal S,\ j(u)=s\}. \] This notation does not assert that the infimum is attained. The source does not specify whether $\mathcal S$ consists of configurations, stationary profiles, relative equilibria, or another class of skyrmion solutions.

Requested statement. Determine how the minimal energy $M(s)$ depends on the angular momentum magnitude $s$, and determine whether this dependence yields estimates for the rotation frequency $\omega$ of the corresponding Landau--Lifshitz solution and proves that this solution is non-static. Here non-static means that, for a solution $u$ on its time interval $I$, \[ \exists\,t_1,t_2\in I\quad u(t_1)\ne u(t_2). \] The correspondence between constrained energy minimizers and evolving solutions, the rotation action, the definition and normalization of $\omega$, and the intended range of $s$ remain unspecified. No differentiability of $M$, frequency formula, existence of minimizers, or implication from a nonzero frequency to non-static motion is asserted.

The supplied dated assessment reports that existence of localized rotating Landau--Lifshitz solutions is known, while the energy--angular momentum law and proof that the constructed motion is non-static remain open. This is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion.

Short English statement

Determine how the least skyrmion energy varies with angular momentum magnitude, and whether that relation controls the rotation frequency $\omega$ and establishes non-static Landau--Lifshitz motion. The supplied record omits the model, functionals, admissible class, and parameter range needed for a fully precise formulation. Its open-status and existence assertions are supplied dated assessments requiring independent review.
