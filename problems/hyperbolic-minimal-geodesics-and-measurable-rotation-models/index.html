Math-4644

Hyperbolic Minimal Geodesics and Measurable Rotation Models

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact manifold of the source's unspecified "hyperbolic type," equipped with an arbitrary Riemannian metric $g$, and let $SM$ be its unit tangent bundle. The set $SM_{\min}^g$ is described as the velocities of globally $g$-minimizing geodesics. A necessary convention to verify is whether minimizing means that a lift $\widetilde\gamma$ to the universal cover satisfies \[ d_{\widetilde g}(\widetilde\gamma(s),\widetilde\gamma(t))=|s-t|\quad\text{for every }s,t\in{\mathbb{R}}. \] This is distinct from global minimization in the compact manifold itself and is relevant to the possibility of closed geodesics.

The source asks:

  1. Does $SM_{\min}^g$ contain the velocity orbit of a closed hyperbolic geodesic? Hyperbolicity here ordinarily means that the transverse return map has no eigenvalue on the unit circle, not that $g$ has negative curvature.
  2. Is there an ergodic geodesic-flow-invariant probability measure with support contained in $SM_{\min}^g$, hyperbolic in the sense of having no zero transverse Lyapunov exponents? The unavoidable exponent in the flow direction is excluded in that convention.
  3. Separately, for an area-preserving surface diffeomorphism $f$, can its intended measure-preserving system be measurably conjugated to $R_\beta:S^1\to S^1$, $R_\beta(x)=x+\beta\pmod1$, where $\beta$ is Diophantine? A usual meaning of Diophantine is that $|\beta-p/q|\geq c q^{-\tau}$ for some $c>0$, finite $\tau$, and every $p\in{\mathbb{Z}},q\geq1$. Measurable conjugacy is an invertible measure-preserving intertwining map modulo null sets.
The last clause supplies neither a restriction on $f$ beyond area preservation nor the invariant probability measure, domain subset, or relation to the geodesic questions. Its quantifier is not precise enough to assert such a rotation model for every area-preserving diffeomorphism. No missing dynamics hypotheses are invented.

Short English statement

Ask for hyperbolic periodic orbits and hyperbolic invariant measures among minimizing geodesics, and retain a separate question about measurable Diophantine rotation models. The minimizing-lift convention, hyperbolic-type assumption and hypotheses of the surface-map clause need clarification.
