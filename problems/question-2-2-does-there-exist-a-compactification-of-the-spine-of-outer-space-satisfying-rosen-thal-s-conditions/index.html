Question 2.2 — Does there exist a compactification of the spine of Outer space satisfying Rosen- thal’s conditions?

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K_r$ be the spine of Outer space for the rank-$r$ free group in the source's convention, with its natural outer-automorphism action. In a standard model, Outer space parametrizes marked metric graphs and the spine is the associated forest-collapse complex. Let $A(S)$ denote the source's complex of arc systems filling a punctured surface. The rank range, surface type, arc equivalences, and filling convention are not supplied.

Question 2.2. Does $K_r$ admit a compactification satisfying Rosenthal's conditions? Ask the same question for $A(S)$. A compactification normally means a compact Hausdorff space containing the original space as a dense subspace; any required equivariance, boundary structure, and fixed-point or control properties must be specified by the actual Rosenthal conditions. Those conditions are absent from the record, so no weaker arbitrary compactification is substituted and no particular compactification is asserted to qualify.

The surrounding text says that the candidate spaces $E$ in its preceding cases have dimension equal to the respective groups' virtual cohomological dimensions. It credits G. Mislin [68] with a cocompact classifying-space model for the mapping class group of a closed surface of the larger Teichmüller-space dimension. The extracted notation "$EG$" does not preserve whether a proper-action decoration is present; for groups with torsion, a classifying space for proper actions and a free contractible $EG$ are different objects. The text then introduces a variation of Question 1.1, not supplied here. These dimension comparisons and the omitted variation are context, not a proof that either requested Rosenthal compactification exists.

Short English statement

Find compactifications satisfying Rosenthal's specific conditions for the Outer-space spine and for filling-arc systems on a punctured surface. The criterion is missing; the nearby minimal-dimension/proper-classifying-space discussion is retained without identifying an arbitrary compactification as sufficient.
