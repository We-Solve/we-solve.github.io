Definitions and mathematical statement

For a group $H$, define its lower central series by \[ \gamma_1(H)=H,\qquad \gamma_{n+1}(H)=[\gamma_n(H),H]\quad(n\geq 1), \] where $[K,H]$ is the subgroup generated by the commutators $k^{-1}h^{-1}kh$ with $k\in K$ and $h\in H$. The group $H$ is nilpotent if $\gamma_{c+1}(H)=\{1\}$ for some integer $c\geq 1$. For a set $X$, write $F(X)$ for the free group on $X$; a free nilpotent group has the form \[ N=F(X)/\gamma_{c+1}(F(X)),\qquad c\geq 1. \] The source does not specify restrictions on the rank $\lvert X\rvert$ or on $c$.

Write $T_A(N)$ provisionally for the tensor $A$-completion referred to in A. G. Myasnikov and V. N. Remeslennikov, Siberian Math. J. 35, no. 5 (1994), 986--996. The supplied record does not define this construction, the nature of $A$, or its admissibility hypotheses; this notation is a reference to that missing definition and does not specify a construction independently.

The requested assertion is that a free nilpotent group can have a non-nilpotent tensor $A$-completion. With admissibility understood to include all hypotheses of the cited construction, its provisional existential formulation is \[ \exists\,(A,X,c)\ \text{admissible},\quad c\geq 1,\qquad \forall n\geq 1,\quad \gamma_{n+1}\!\left(T_A\!\left(F(X)/\gamma_{c+1}(F(X))\right)\right)\neq\{1\}. \] This formulation requires clarification of the admissible coefficient objects and input groups. In particular, the supplied wording does not determine whether $A$ may be chosen or is intended to be fixed subject to further hypotheses.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a free nilpotent group whose tensor $A$-completion is not nilpotent. A precise unconditional formulation requires the missing definition of that completion and the hypotheses on $A$.
