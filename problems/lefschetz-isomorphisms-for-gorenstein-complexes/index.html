Math-1162

Lefschetz Isomorphisms for Gorenstein* Complexes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Lefschetz question. For every $(d-1)$-dimensional Gorenstein* complex $K$, does multiplication by a generic linear form induce isomorphisms $\omega^{d-2i}:H_i(K)\to H_{d-i}(K)$? The source omits the coefficient field, definition of $H_i(K)$, choice of parameters, and index range. The following is the usual face-ring interpretation, explicitly conditional on those conventions.

Fix a field $k$, let $K$ be a finite simplicial complex on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ of dimension $d-1$, and form its Stanley--Reisner ring $k[K]=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I_K$, where $I_K$ is generated by squarefree monomials supported on nonfaces and $\deg x_j=1$. Gorenstein* over $k$ can be expressed as the homology-sphere link condition: for every face $\sigma$, including the empty face, $\widetilde H_r(\operatorname{lk}_K\sigma;k)$ is $k$ when $r=d-|\sigma|-1$ and zero otherwise, with the usual reduced-homology conventions. Choose a linear system of parameters $\Theta=(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_d)$, so $H(K)=k[K]/(\Theta)$ is finite-dimensional, and write $H_i(K)=H(K)_i$. These $H_i(K)$ are graded pieces of an Artinian reduction, not simplicial homology groups.

The intended strong Lefschetz assertion is that for generic permitted choices of $\Theta$ and $\omega\in H(K)_1$, the multiplication maps\[\omega^{d-2i}:H(K)_i\longrightarrow H(K)_{d-i}\] are isomorphisms simultaneously for every integer $0\le i\le\lfloor d/2\rfloor$. Over an infinite field, generic can mean belonging to a nonempty Zariski-open set of parameter and linear-form coefficients. Whether the original assertion fixes a generic $\Theta$ first or quantifies over another parameter class must be checked. The coefficient characteristic and finite-field interpretation cannot be omitted or silently replaced by characteristic zero.

The supplied triage, checked 2026-08-21, labels the item open but says its extraction was merely an incomplete context sentence about the $g$-conjecture. The later curated field explicitly supplies the Lefschetz formula and reports source verification. This conflict in source layers is retained; neither the incomplete-context warning nor the open label is an independently verified present-day assessment of the formulated Lefschetz assertion.

Short English statement

The intended question asks whether every Gorenstein* face ring has a generic Artinian reduction with the strong Lefschetz isomorphisms. Its graded pieces must not be confused with topological homology. The field and parameter conventions are essential and absent from the excerpt.
