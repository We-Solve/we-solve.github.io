Math-15272

Baum–Connes Assembly for Minimal Exact Crossed Products

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A $G$-$C^*$-algebra $A$ is a $C^*$-algebra equipped with a continuous action of a topological group $G$ by $*$-automorphisms. In the usual locally compact group setting, a crossed-product functor assigns a completion $A\rtimes_E G$ of the convolution $*$-algebra $C_c(G,A)$, functorially in $A$. Exactness means that every $G$-equivariant short exact sequence of coefficient algebras induces a short exact sequence \[ 0\longrightarrow I\rtimes_E G\longrightarrow A\rtimes_E G\longrightarrow (A/I)\rtimes_E G\longrightarrow 0. \] The source calls $\rtimes_E$ the minimal exact crossed-product functor. Minimality ordinarily refers to the order given by canonical quotient maps between crossed-product completions; the precise class of functors being compared is not specified here.

Write $K_*^{\mathrm{top}}(G;A)$ for equivariant topological $K$-theory with coefficients in $A$, and $K_*(B)$ for the operator $K$-theory of a $C^*$-algebra $B$, with degree $*\in{\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. The requested investigation concerns the reformulated Baum--Connes assembly map \[ \mu^E_{G,A}\colon K_*^{\mathrm{top}}(G;A)\longrightarrow K_*(A\rtimes_E G). \] Describe its construction and concrete realization, and give concrete realizations and computations of $A\rtimes_E G$ for the intended groups and coefficient algebras.

Unresolved scope. The supplied record specifies neither the admissible class of groups and coefficient algebras nor particular cases to compute. The locally compact setting above explains the standard terminology; it is not an additional hypothesis attributed to the source. The record also does not identify the exact version of the minimal exact functor or the hypotheses ensuring the displayed assembly map is defined. Consequently this is a research objective with an incompletely specified domain, rather than a fully quantified conjecture. In particular, the request to "understand" the map does not explicitly assert that it is an isomorphism.

Short English statement

Investigate the Baum--Connes assembly map with target the $K$-theory of the minimal exact crossed product, including concrete descriptions and computations of that crossed product. The intended groups, coefficient algebras, functor convention, and precise success criterion remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; that label is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
