Math-15039

Unbounded Simplicial Volume in Surface-Bundle Families

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a closed, connected, oriented $n$-manifold $M$, its simplicial volume is \[ \|M\|=\inf\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{k}|a_j|:\ c=\sum_{j=1}^{k}a_j\sigma_j\text{ is a real singular cycle representing }[M]_{{\mathbb{R}}}\right\}, \] where $a_j\in{\mathbb{R}}$, the $\sigma_j$ are singular $n$-simplices, and $[M]_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ is the real fundamental class. This definition states the usual closed oriented manifold convention; the supplied record does not specify whether all its hypotheses hold for the total spaces in question or whether another version of simplicial volume is intended.

The source refers to surface bundles \[ p_R:E_R\longrightarrow\Sigma_2 \] "constructed with pullback simplicial volumes at least $R$." Conventionally, $\Sigma_2$ denotes a closed, connected, oriented surface of genus $2$, but this meaning is not explicitly defined in the supplied record. Neither the construction of $E_R$ nor the pullback maps and spaces whose simplicial volumes satisfy the stated lower bound are supplied. Consequently, that lower bound cannot be formulated precisely from this record alone.

Intended question, conditional on specifying the missing construction and conventions. For the particular family $(E_R)$ referred to in the source, is \[ \lim_{R\to\infty}\|E_R\|=+\infty? \] More explicitly, if $I$ is the family's parameter set, ordered by its numerical parameter $R$ and unbounded above, the requested assertion is \[ \forall A>0\ \exists R_0\ \forall R\in I, \qquad R\ge R_0\ \Longrightarrow\ \|E_R\|>A. \] Here $I$ records an unspecified parameter domain rather than an additional supplied hypothesis about a known construction. The source does not determine whether $R$ ranges over integers, real numbers, or another unbounded numerical set, or how choices of bundles affect the intended quantification. The question concerns divergence of this family, which is stronger than merely having examples with arbitrarily large simplicial volume.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the simplicial volumes of the specified surface-bundle total spaces eventually exceed every fixed bound as $R$ grows. A precise unconditional statement requires the missing construction, the meaning of the pullback-volume bound, and the family and manifold conventions. The supplied record labels the problem open, but that label is dated curation rather than an independently verified mathematical assessment.
