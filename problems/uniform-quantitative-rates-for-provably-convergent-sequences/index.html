Uniform Quantitative Rates for Provably Convergent Sequences

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks: for natural classes of provably convergent sequences, which uniform rates, moduli, or other quantitative forms of convergence can be extracted? It is a research programme whose precise hypotheses and desired conclusions remain unspecified in the supplied record.

The following gives a conditional framework for interpreting some of the requested quantitative notions; it does not impose a domain on the original problem. Suppose that a class $\mathcal C$ consists of sequences $x=(x_n)_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}$ in a metric space $(X,d)$, with ${\mathbb{N}}=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ as an indexing convention for this framework. If a limit $\ell_x\in X$ is specified, a rate of convergence for $x$ is a function $r_x:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}$ satisfying \[ \forall k\in{\mathbb{N}}\;\forall n\ge r_x(k),\qquad d(x_n,\ell_x)\le 2^{-k}. \] A Cauchy modulus for $x$ is a function $c_x:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}$ satisfying \[ \forall k\in{\mathbb{N}}\;\forall m,n\ge c_x(k),\qquad d(x_m,x_n)\le 2^{-k}. \] A uniform Cauchy modulus for $\mathcal C$ is a single function $c$ satisfying this condition for every $x\in\mathcal C$. A uniform bound on metastability for $\mathcal C$ is a functional $\Phi:{\mathbb{N}}\times{\mathbb{N}}^{{\mathbb{N}}}\to{\mathbb{N}}$ such that \[ \forall x\in\mathcal C\;\forall k\in{\mathbb{N}}\;\forall g:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}\; \exists N\le\Phi(k,g)\; \forall i,j\in[N,N+g(N)]\cap{\mathbb{N}},\qquad d(x_i,x_j)\le 2^{-k}. \] In these definitions, uniformity means independence from the particular sequence. If dependence on additional parameters, bounds, or proofs is allowed, those inputs must instead be specified explicitly.

Statement to be made precise. Specify a formal theory $T$, a class $\mathcal C$, a notion of convergence, and the representation and hypotheses under which the relevant convergence assertions are provable in $T$. Determine which quantitative convergence data can be extracted from such proofs, with what effectiveness and with what dependence on the sequence, proof, and other permitted parameters. Potential targets include rates of convergence, Cauchy moduli, bounds on metastability, and other quantitative data.

Unresolved qualifications. The record specifies neither $T$, $\mathcal C$, the ambient space or convergence notion, nor the permitted parameter dependence and meaning of extraction. It also does not distinguish individual provability for each sequence from a proof of a universally quantified convergence assertion for a class. Thus the metric definitions above illustrate possible targets, while the supplied evidence does not determine a single fully quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

The problem asks what quantitative information can be extracted from proofs that sequences converge, uniformly over suitable natural classes. Possible information includes convergence rates, Cauchy moduli, and metastability bounds. The formal theory, classes, convergence notion, and permitted dependencies must be specified before this becomes a precise individual problem. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a curation label, not an independently verified mathematical conclusion.
