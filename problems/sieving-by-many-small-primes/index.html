Math-2779

Sieving by Many Small Primes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an integer $N\geq1$, write $[N]=\{1,\ldots,N\}$. Let $p_1,\ldots,p_{1000}$ be distinct primes with \[2\leq p_1<p_2<\cdots<p_{1000}<N^{9/10}.\] If $R_i\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}/p_i{\mathbb{Z}}$ is the set of residue classes removed at the $i$th prime, the surviving set is \[S=\{n\in[N]: n\bmod p_i\notin R_i\text{ for every }1\leq i\leq1000\}.\] The source asks whether removing "half the residue classes" for each $p_i$ forces \[|S|\leq\frac{N}{10}.\] This is not yet a uniquely defined assertion: for odd $p_i$ the number $p_i/2$ is not an integer, so the sizes $|R_i|$ require a rounding or other convention. The intended quantifier over the choices of $R_i$ is also omitted. The notation above treats removed classes as distinct members of a subset; it does not choose their sizes or resolve the quantifier. The source also does not explicitly state whether its conclusion is for every admissible $N$ or only for sufficiently large $N$. No one of these variants is silently asserted here.

The supplied literature triage, checked 2026-08-17, identifies the local code with Green's Problem 44 and says that the same parity and quantifier defects occur in the maintained source. It reports Erdős's observation that the intended assertion is affirmative by the large sieve when all 1000 primes are below $N^{1/2}$, and reports no further progress for the $N^{9/10}$ version. These statements concern the intended, convention-dependent variant. The registry is labelled open and the triage partially solved / PARTIAL-PROGRESS; the review assigns formulation-dependent uncertainty to the literal wording.

Short English statement

After sieving $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ by removing about half the residue classes for each of 1000 primes below $N^{9/10}$, must at most one tenth of the integers remain? The rounding convention and quantifiers over the removed classes must first be specified.
