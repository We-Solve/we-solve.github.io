Deformation Equivalence of Categorical Hyperkähler Constructions

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Source formulation (Conjecture 6; incomplete setup). Let $S$, $v$, the pairing $\langle\ ,\ \rangle$, and the reduced category $T_S(v)_w^{\mathrm{red}}$ have the meanings intended in the source report. Those meanings and their required hypotheses are not specified in the supplied record. In particular, the phrase "$w$ coprime with $v$" is retained as a source condition; it is not defined here because the nature of $v$ is unspecified.

The proposed assertion is that, for every $g\geq 0$ in the source's intended parameter domain and every $w\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ coprime with $v$ in the source's sense, setting \[ g'=\frac{\langle v,v\rangle+2}{2}, \] one has \[ T_S(v)_w^{\mathrm{red}} \quad\text{deformation equivalent to}\quad D^b\!\left(\operatorname{Hilb}(S,g')\right). \] Here the conventional interpretation of $\operatorname{Hilb}(S,n)$ is the Hilbert scheme of zero-dimensional subschemes of $S$ of length $n$, and that of $D^b(X)$ is the bounded derived category of coherent sheaves on $X$. These interpretations require confirmation against the source's setup. In particular, the record supplies no hypotheses ensuring that $g'$ is a nonnegative integer. It also does not specify the definition of deformation equivalence for the categories in this assertion, the construction denoted by $T$, or the operation denoted by $\mathrm{red}$. The parameter $g$ does not otherwise occur in the displayed assertion; its domain beyond $g\geq0$, and its relation to $S$ or $v$, are missing. Consequently, this is a transcription of the intended conjecture with unresolved qualifications, rather than a fully specified standalone mathematical statement.

Qualifications retained from the surrounding extract. The source expresses an expectation that the categories written there as $T_S^\sigma(v)_w$ do not produce new examples of irreducible holomorphic symplectic varieties, and a belief that they are related to known examples by interesting derived equivalences, more specifically by compactifications of the Fourier--Mukai derived equivalence between a possibly relative abelian variety and its dual. It promises a more precise conjecture in future work and describes Conjecture 6 as a simplified form and a categorical version of a conjecture numbered (4), whose statement is absent.

The supplied original extract also contains adjacent clauses labelled (ii) and (iii). Clause (ii) says that the Serre functor $S_T$ of $T_S^\sigma(v)_w^{\mathrm{red}}$ is trivial $\acute{e}$tale locally on $M_S^\sigma(v)$. Clause (iii) asserts a wall-crossing equivalence between reduced categories associated with stability parameters, but its prime and superscript placement is corrupted in the supplied text. The definitions of $\sigma$, $M_S^\sigma(v)$, and local triviality of the Serre functor are absent, as are the hypotheses and precise indexing of the wall-crossing claim. These clauses are preserved as surrounding source claims; the extract does not establish that they are additional conclusions of Conjecture 6.

Short English statement

Conjecture 6 proposes that each admissible reduced category $T_S(v)_w^{\mathrm{red}}$, with $w\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ coprime with $v$ in the source's sense, deforms to the bounded derived category of the Hilbert scheme of $(\langle v,v\rangle+2)/2$ points on $S$. The supplied record omits the setup needed to define admissibility, the categories, and deformation equivalence, and does not explain the role of $g\geq0$. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
