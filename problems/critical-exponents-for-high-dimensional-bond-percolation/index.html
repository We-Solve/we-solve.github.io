Math-119

Critical Exponents for High-Dimensional Bond Percolation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix $d\geq2$ and put independent Bernoulli-$p$ open/closed states on the nearest-neighbor edges of ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$. Let $C(0)$ be the open cluster of the origin and define \[ \theta(p)=\mathbb P_p(|C(0)|=\infty),\qquad \chi(p)=\mathbb E_p|C(0)|=\sum_{x\in{\mathbb{Z}}^d}\mathbb P_p(0\leftrightarrow x), \] allowing $\chi=\infty$. Retain the source's threshold equality \[ p_c=\inf\{p\in[0,1]:\theta(p)>0\} =\sup\{p\in[0,1]:\chi(p)<\infty\}. \]

For each fixed dimension, determine critical behavior at $p_c$ and near-critical behavior as $p\uparrow p_c$ or $p\downarrow p_c$. The requested program includes existence and values of the associated critical exponents, and their universality in the intended comparison class. For example, standard logarithmic exponent conventions for the two supplied observables would be \[ \beta=\lim_{p\downarrow p_c}\frac{\log\theta(p)}{\log(p-p_c)}, \qquad \gamma=-\lim_{p\uparrow p_c}\frac{\log\chi(p)}{\log(p_c-p)}, \] when these limits exist. These formulas specify examples of the exponent question; they do not assert existence or supply predicted values. An exact power asymptotic with a finite nonzero amplitude is stronger than such a logarithmic limit.

The extract does not list all desired observables or exponents, prescribe exact-asymptotic versus logarithmic definitions, or identify the lattices, bond/site models or finite-range changes over which universality is to be tested. Thus the full general critical-behavior request is retained without inventing a numerical conjecture or a universal comparison across unspecified models. Despite the record's high-dimensional title, its mathematical scope explicitly includes every $d\geq2$.

Short English statement

Determine critical and near-critical behavior for independent nearest-neighbor bond percolation in every dimension at least two, including existence, values and universality of critical exponents. The exact exponent list, asymptotic strength and universality class are not supplied.
