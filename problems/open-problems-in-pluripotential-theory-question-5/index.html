Open Problems in Pluripotential Theory — Question 5

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt omits the ambient hypotheses and the definition of the domain of definition. The following is a conditional formulation in the standard compact Kähler setting; its agreement with the cited source requires review.

Let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold without boundary, of complex dimension $n\geq 1$, and let $\omega$ be a Kähler form. Fix a normalization of $d^c$, used consistently throughout. Write $\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)$ for the upper semicontinuous, locally integrable functions $\varphi:X\to[-\infty,\infty)$ satisfying $\omega+dd^c\varphi\geq0$ in the sense of currents. Set \[ \operatorname{Vol}_{\omega}(X)=\int_X\omega^n. \] For bounded $\omega$-plurisubharmonic functions, interpret $(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n$ using the locally bounded complex Monge--Ampère product.

Under the usual bounded-approximation interpretation, define $DMA(X,\omega)$ to consist of those $\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)$ for which there is a positive Radon measure $\mu_\varphi$ such that, for every sequence \[ \varphi_j\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)\cap L^\infty(X), \qquad \varphi_j\searrow\varphi, \] one has \[ \lim_{j\to\infty}\int_X f\,(\omega+dd^c\varphi_j)^n =\int_X f\,d\mu_\varphi \qquad\text{for every }f\in C(X,{\mathbb{R}}). \] In particular, the limiting measure must be independent of the decreasing approximating sequence. Denote it by $(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n$.

Problem (Coman--Guedj--Zeriahi, as attributed in the supplied record). Characterize $DMA(X,\omega)$: give necessary and sufficient conditions on $\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)$ for membership in this global domain of definition.

The supplied source distinguishes this global domain from $DMA_{\mathrm{loc}}(X,\omega)$, its local counterpart. In the customary interpretation, local membership is tested on coordinate neighborhoods after adding a smooth local potential $\rho$ with $dd^c\rho=\omega$, using decreasing locally bounded plurisubharmonic approximations to $\rho+\varphi$ and weak convergence on those neighborhoods. The exact local convention is not included in the excerpt.

The source supplies the following qualifications: every bounded global approximant has total Monge--Ampère mass $\operatorname{Vol}_{\omega}(X)$; this fixed-mass constraint prevents the construction attributed to Błocki, labeled [Blo04], from carrying over here; and the global domain contains functions whose gradient is not in $L^2$. The latter statement is understood relative to the metric associated with $\omega$. These are supplied contextual assertions, not conclusions established by this draft. Their dimension restrictions are absent from the excerpt: in particular, the asserted distinction between global and local domains must not be read as an independently verified assertion for every $n\geq1$.

Short English statement

Characterize the $\omega$-plurisubharmonic functions on a compact Kähler manifold whose complex Monge--Ampère measures are well defined by every decreasing bounded approximation, with a limit independent of the approximation. The ambient assumptions and precise approximation convention are reconstructed from standard notation and require source review. The supplied record labels the question open; this status is not independently verified.
