Completeness of original Pixton relations on closed moduli

OpenAI's claim

A formal tautological class at genus $10^{60}$ with $3\binom{10^{60}}3$ markings is claimed to vanish in Chow and rational cohomology while lying outside the original Pixton relation span. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Put $g=10^{60}$, $D=\binom g3$ and $n=3D$. In the formal decorated stable-graph strata algebra $\mathcal S_{g,n}$, OpenAI constructs a degree-$2D$ class $Y$ as follows. Concatenate all increasing triples of colors $1,\ldots,g$ into a color word $\mathbf i$ of length $n$, with three-slot blocks $J=(a,b,c)$. Let $\overline D_{ab}$ sum all rational-tail divisors carrying $a,b$. Let $\beta_J$ sum one-edge unmarked genus-$g$ graphs with the edge subdivided by a genus-zero vertex carrying $J$, weighted by inverse automorphism orders and pulled back by forgetting other labels. Then \[ F_J=\overline D_{ab}\overline D_{ac}-\tfrac12\beta_J,\qquad Y=\sum_{\tau\mathbf i=\mathbf i}\operatorname{sgn}(\tau)\,\tau\left(\prod_JF_J\right). \] OpenAI claims $q_{g,n}(Y)=0$ but $Y\notin\mathcal P_{g,n}$, where $q$ realizes graph classes in rational Chow of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}$ and $\mathcal P_{g,n}$ is Pixton's original relation span. Thus that span is strictly smaller than the Chow kernel; the same class also lies in the rational-cohomological kernel outside the span.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 053, 2026-09-24): Color-indexed three-label factors combine tail-divisor products with bridge corrections, followed by alternating relabelings. A faithful coefficient-algebra treatment of relative curve correspondences yields the required odd cubic identity in Chow; the high-arity construction separates that identity from the original relation system.
