Induction gives $DH_L^n=H_R^nD$; the norm-convergent exponential series then gives \[ De^{-itH_L}=e^{-itH_R}D. \] If $D^*D=P$ is a projection and $P\psi=\psi$, then $\|D\psi\|=\|\psi\|$. Neither statement requires $D$ to be an invertible symmetry on the full Hilbert space.
