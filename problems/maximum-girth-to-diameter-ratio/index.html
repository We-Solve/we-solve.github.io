Math-4155

Maximum Girth-to-Diameter Ratio

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks about graphs of minimum vertex degree at least $3$, but does not explicitly specify finiteness, connectedness, simplicity, or whether edges are undirected. The following precise formulation is conditional on the intended class being finite, connected, simple, undirected graphs; this convention requires confirmation from the source.

Let $\mathcal G$ denote that class subject to $\delta(G)\geq 3$, where $\delta(G)=\min_{v\in V(G)}\deg_G(v)$. For $G\in\mathcal G$, define $d_G(u,v)$ as the minimum number of edges in a path joining $u$ and $v$, and set \[ \operatorname{diam}(G)=\max_{u,v\in V(G)}d_G(u,v). \] Define $\operatorname{girth}(G)$ as the minimum number of edges in a cycle of $G$. Under the stated conditional conventions, these quantities are finite and the diameter is positive.

Determine \[ x=\lim_{D\to\infty}\sup\left\{\frac{\operatorname{girth}(G)}{\operatorname{diam}(G)}:G\in\mathcal G,\ \operatorname{diam}(G)\geq D\right\}. \] In particular, is $x>1$? Equivalently, does there exist $\varepsilon>0$ such that for every $D\in{\mathbb{N}}$ there exists $G\in\mathcal G$ satisfying \[ \operatorname{diam}(G)\geq D, \qquad \operatorname{girth}(G)>(1+\varepsilon)\operatorname{diam}(G)? \]

The supplied background also asks whether girth minus diameter is unbounded. Under the same conditional graph conventions, this asks whether \[ \forall M\in{\mathbb{R}}\ \exists G\in\mathcal G: \operatorname{girth}(G)-\operatorname{diam}(G)>M. \] The background describes this as a "stronger question," but does not specify the comparison intended; that description is retained as source wording rather than an asserted logical relationship.

Short English statement

Determine the asymptotic largest ratio of girth to diameter among graphs with minimum degree at least $3$, and decide whether that ratio exceeds $1$. The supplied background additionally asks whether girth minus diameter is unbounded. The precise graph conventions remain to be confirmed.
