Math-4793

Free Toral Rank versus Free $p$-Rank

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ be a finite connected complex with basepoint $x_0$. Assume that $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ is abelian and that its natural action on $\pi_n(X,x_0)$ is trivial for every $n\geq 2$. Following the finite-dimensional wording of the cleaned source record, assume \[ \dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\left(\bigoplus_{n\geq 1}\pi_n(X,x_0)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}\right)<\infty. \] The term in degree one is defined because the fundamental group is abelian. The original wording, "finitely generated rational homotopy," is not explicitly explained in the supplied record; whether it means this total finite-dimensional condition requires source review.

For a prime $p$ and an integer $r\geq 0$, write $T^r=(S^1)^r$ and $E_{p,r}=({\mathbb{Z}}/p{\mathbb{Z}})^r$, with the usual torus topology and discrete topology, respectively. Interpret an action here as a continuous action on $X$ itself. An action of a group $G$ is free if $g\cdot x=x$ implies $g=e$ for every $g\in G$ and $x\in X$. Define, using the maxima specified in the source, \[ \operatorname{rk}_T(X)=\max\{r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:T^r\text{ acts freely on }X\}, \qquad \operatorname{rk}_p(X)=\max\{r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}:E_{p,r}\text{ acts freely on }X\}. \] For $r=0$, the acting group is trivial.

Comparison problem. Determine the relationship between $\operatorname{rk}_T(X)$ and $\operatorname{rk}_p(X)$ as $X$ ranges over complexes satisfying these hypotheses and $p$ ranges over primes, including any dependence on $p$.

The supplied source poses a comparison question without specifying a single conjectured equality or inequality. Its dated curation describes an asymptotic upper bound for free $p$-rank at sufficiently large primes and counterexamples to a universal reverse inequality, but supplies neither the bound's formula nor the reverse inequality's precise quantifiers. These descriptions therefore cannot be promoted here to precise mathematical assertions. The category of complex and any additional restrictions on admissible actions also require confirmation from the source.

Short English statement

For a finite connected complex with abelian fundamental group acting trivially on higher homotopy and finite-dimensional total rational homotopy, compare the largest ranks of a torus and an elementary abelian $p$-group that can act freely on the complex. The supplied record leaves the precise comparison target unspecified and labels the problem partially solved; that status is supplied curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
