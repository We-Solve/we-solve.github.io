Math-995

The Dupont–Monod Comparison-Map Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a connected semisimple Lie group with finite center, and equip ${\mathbb{R}}$ with its usual topology and the trivial $G$-action. For every integer $n\geq 0$, define the space of continuous homogeneous cochains by \[ C^n_{\mathrm c}(G;{\mathbb{R}})=\{f:G^{n+1}\to{\mathbb{R}}\mid f\text{ is continuous and }f(hg_0,\ldots,hg_n)=f(g_0,\ldots,g_n)\text{ for all }h,g_0,\ldots,g_n\in G\}. \] Its bounded subspace is \[ C^n_{\mathrm{cb}}(G;{\mathbb{R}})=\left\{f\in C^n_{\mathrm c}(G;{\mathbb{R}}):\sup_{(g_0,\ldots,g_n)\in G^{n+1}}|f(g_0,\ldots,g_n)|<\infty\right\}. \] Both complexes have differential \[ (\delta f)(g_0,\ldots,g_{n+1})=\sum_{i=0}^{n+1}(-1)^i f(g_0,\ldots,\widehat{g_i},\ldots,g_{n+1}), \] where the hat denotes omission. Their cohomology groups are continuous cohomology $H^n_{\mathrm c}(G;{\mathbb{R}})$ and continuous bounded cohomology $H^n_{\mathrm{cb}}(G;{\mathbb{R}})$, respectively. These are ordinary cohomology groups, without taking closures of coboundary spaces. Inclusion of the bounded subcomplex induces the comparison map \[ c_G^n:H^n_{\mathrm{cb}}(G;{\mathbb{R}})\longrightarrow H^n_{\mathrm c}(G;{\mathbb{R}}). \] Dupont--Monod comparison-map conjecture, as supplied. For every connected semisimple Lie group $G$ with finite center and every integer $n\geq 0$, is $c_G^n$ an isomorphism of real vector spaces?

The supplied record imposes no further restriction on compact factors or on the degree. Its mathematical formulation is consistent across the supplied statements. The report-year discrepancy and the dated literature claims described below remain unverified source qualifications.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether, for every connected semisimple Lie group with finite center, continuous bounded cohomology with trivial real coefficients agrees with continuous cohomology through the natural comparison map in every nonnegative degree. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
