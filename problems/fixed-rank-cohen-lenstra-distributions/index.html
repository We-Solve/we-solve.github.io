Math-755

Fixed-Rank Cohen–Lenstra Distributions

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix an odd prime $p$ and an integer $g\geq 1$. The source does not explicitly define $A_K$ or specify the admissible groups $G$. The following formulation uses the conventional interpretation that $A_K$ is the $p$-primary subgroup of the ideal class group of $K$, and that $G$ ranges over finite abelian $p$-groups of $p$-rank $g$. These interpretations require source confirmation.

For an imaginary quadratic field $K$, let $\mathcal O_K$ be its ring of integers, let $\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_K)$ be its group of nonzero fractional ideals modulo principal fractional ideals, and set \[ A_K=\{a\in\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_K):p^n a=0\text{ for some integer }n\geq 0\}. \] For a finite abelian $p$-group $H$, define \[ r_p(H)=\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}(H/pH). \] Interpret the $p$-rank of $K$ as $r_p(A_K)$. Let $D_K<0$ denote the field discriminant. Interpret ordering by discriminant as ordering by $|D_K|$, and count fields up to isomorphism. For real $X>0$, define \[ \mathcal K_{p,g}(X)=\{[K]:K\text{ is imaginary quadratic},\ |D_K|\leq X,\ r_p(A_K)=g\}. \] For a finite abelian $p$-group $G$, let $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ be its group of automorphisms.

Conjectural statement under these interpretations. For every odd prime $p$, every integer $g\geq 1$, and every finite abelian $p$-group $G$ with $r_p(G)=g$, does the limiting frequency exist and satisfy \[ \lim_{X\to\infty} \frac{\#\{[K]\in\mathcal K_{p,g}(X):A_K\cong G\}} {\#\mathcal K_{p,g}(X)} = \frac{p^{-g^2}}{|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|} \prod_{k=1}^{g}\left(p^g-p^{g-k}\right)^2\,? \] The quotient is defined only when its denominator is nonzero. The source does not separately address the nonemptiness or infinitude of the conditioning family. The interpretation of discriminant ordering as ordering by absolute discriminant is also an explicit convention requiring confirmation.

Short English statement

Fix an odd prime $p$ and a positive rank $g$. Interpreting $A_K$ as the $p$-primary ideal class group, the question asks whether imaginary quadratic fields of that rank, counted by absolute discriminant, have the displayed limiting distribution among finite abelian $p$-groups of rank $g$. The group interpretation and counting conventions are explicit qualifications because the supplied statement does not fully specify them.
