Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 13

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a four-dimensional manifold endowed with a foliation $\mathcal F$ by two-dimensional leaves. In the smooth setting needed for the following definitions, its tangent distribution $T\mathcal F\subset TM$ is a smooth rank-two subbundle satisfying $[\Gamma(T\mathcal F),\Gamma(T\mathcal F)]\subseteq\Gamma(T\mathcal F)$.

A smooth rank-two distribution $\mathcal D\subset TM$ is an Engel structure if its derived distributions \[ \mathcal D^{2}=\mathcal D+[\mathcal D,\mathcal D],\qquad \mathcal D^{3}=\mathcal D^{2}+[\mathcal D,\mathcal D^{2}] \] have ranks three and four, respectively, at every point of $M$. Here brackets of distributions denote the pointwise spans of Lie brackets of their smooth local sections; in particular, $\mathcal D^{3}=TM$.

Question (Nariman--Yazdi, Question 13). Given a four-manifold $M$ with a foliation $\mathcal F$ by surfaces, can $\mathcal F$, interpreted through its tangent distribution $T\mathcal F$, be perturbed to an Engel structure?

Unresolved meaning of perturbation. The supplied statement does not specify a topology, a size of perturbation, or whether a deformation is required. For example, if perturbation means arbitrarily close approximation in the compact-open $C^{\infty}$ topology on the space $\Gamma(\operatorname{Gr}_{2}(TM))$ of smooth rank-two distributions, the question would ask, for every admissible pair $(M,\mathcal F)$, whether \[ \forall\,U\text{ a neighborhood of }T\mathcal F, \quad \exists\,\mathcal D\in U \quad\text{such that }\mathcal D\text{ is Engel}. \] This is a conditional interpretation, not a uniquely determined formulation of the supplied question. The source also leaves smoothness conventions, compactness, boundary conditions, and any orientation hypotheses unspecified; no such additional restrictions are asserted here.

Short English statement

The question asks whether a foliation of a four-manifold by surfaces can be perturbed so that its tangent planes form an Engel distribution, whose successive Lie brackets span three and then four dimensions everywhere. The meaning and size of the perturbation, and the intended manifold and regularity hypotheses, require clarification.
