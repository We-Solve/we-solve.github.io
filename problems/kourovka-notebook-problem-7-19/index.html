Kourovka Notebook Problem 7.19

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k\geq 1$ and $m\geq 0$ be integers, let $X=\{x_1,\ldots,x_k\}$, and let $F(X)$ be the free group on $X$. For relators $r_1,\ldots,r_m\in F(X)$, put \[ G=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_k\mid r_1,\ldots,r_m\rangle =F(X)/\langle\!\langle r_1,\ldots,r_m\rangle\!\rangle, \] where the denominator is the normal closure of the relators. A group $G$ is simple if $G\neq\{1\}$ and its only normal subgroups are $\{1\}$ and $G$.

Order the formal alphabet $X\cup X^{-1}$, and enumerate all finite words over this alphabet as $w_0,w_1,\ldots$, first by length and then lexicographically, including the empty word. Write $\overline{w_n}$ for the element represented by $w_n$ in $G$, and define the word-problem characteristic function by \[ \chi_G(n)= \begin{cases} 1,&\overline{w_n}=1_G,\\ 0,&\overline{w_n}\neq 1_G. \end{cases} \] Here a primitive recursive function means a function obtained from the zero, successor, and projection functions by composition and primitive recursion.

Problem (F. B. Cannonito). Construct an explicit finite presentation as above such that $G$ is simple and $\chi_G$ is not primitive recursive. Equivalently, require \[ \forall f:\mathbb{N}\to\{0,1\}\text{ primitive recursive},\quad \exists n\in\mathbb{N}\quad f(n)\neq\chi_G(n). \] The requested output is an explicit example, together with justification of these properties. The source does not prescribe a particular format for explicitness; specifying the finite presentation is the interpretation used here. The word encoding above makes the computational requirement precise.

Short English statement

Construct an explicit finitely presented, nontrivial simple group whose word problem cannot be decided by any primitive recursive function. The supplied record labels this problem open; that label is not an independently verified assessment.
