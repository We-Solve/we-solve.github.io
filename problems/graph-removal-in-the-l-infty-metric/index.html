Graph Removal in the $L_infty$ Metric

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns a local version of graph removal. To describe the distance under a conventional graph model, let $G=(V,E)$ and $H$ be finite simple undirected graphs, with $n=|V|\geq 1$. These graph conventions are provisional: the supplied record does not explicitly specify them. Say that $G$ is $H$-free if it contains no subgraph isomorphic to $H$; the source does not explicitly distinguish ordinary from induced copies.

For a set $F\subseteq E$ of deleted edges, define its removed-edge degree at $v\in V$ by \[ d_F(v)=|\{e\in F:v\in e\}|, \qquad \|d_F\|_\infty=\max_{v\in V}d_F(v). \] Under the ordinary-subgraph interpretation, the corresponding deletion distance is \[ D_{\infty,H}(G)= \min\left\{\|d_F\|_\infty: F\subseteq E,\ (V,E\setminus F)\text{ is }H\text{-free}\right\}. \] Its vertex-normalized version is $D_{\infty,H}(G)/n$. The source does not specify whether the distance is normalized or which normalization is intended.

Source question. Does an analogue of the graph removal lemma hold when distance from being $H$-free is measured by the maximum number of deleted edges incident to any one vertex, rather than by the total number of deleted edges?

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not give the copy-counting hypothesis, its normalization, the order and dependencies of the quantitative parameters, or the range of graphs $H$. In particular, it does not specify whether the hypothesis bounds the total number of copies of $H$, bounds copies through individual vertices, or imposes another local condition. Therefore the displayed distance defines a provisional interpretation of the metric, but the evidence does not determine a unique fully quantified removal conjecture. No particular copy-counting implication is asserted here.

Short English statement

Can graph removal be formulated so that making a graph $H$-free requires deleting only a small number of edges at each vertex? The supplied record leaves the necessary copy-counting hypothesis and quantitative formulation unspecified, so a precise complete conjecture requires clarification from the source.
