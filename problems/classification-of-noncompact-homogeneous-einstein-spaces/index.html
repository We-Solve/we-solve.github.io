Classification of Noncompact Homogeneous Einstein Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the usual Riemannian interpretation of the supplied statement, let $G$ be a finite-dimensional Lie group, let $K\leq G$ be a closed subgroup, and let $M=G/K$ be the corresponding smooth homogeneous space. These conventions explicate the terminology; the source does not explicitly specify the category or impose connectedness, simple connectedness, or effectiveness of the action.

A Riemannian metric $g$ on $M$ is $G$-invariant if $L_a^*g=g$ for every $a\in G$, where $L_a(bK)=abK$. It is Einstein if there exists a constant $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that $\operatorname{Ric}_g=\lambda g$, and it is nonflat if its Riemann curvature tensor $\operatorname{Rm}_g$ is not identically zero.

The requested classification, with "nonflat" interpreted as a condition on the Einstein metric being sought, is to determine all noncompact homogeneous spaces $G/K$ for which \[ \exists\,g\ \exists\,\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}:\qquad g\text{ is a }G\text{-invariant Riemannian metric},\qquad \operatorname{Ric}_g=\lambda g,\qquad \operatorname{Rm}_g\not\equiv0. \] The source supplies no dimension bound and does not restrict the Einstein constant to be negative. Its background discussion of negative scalar curvature must therefore not be read as an additional hypothesis.

The classification target remains qualified: the source does not specify whether to classify homogeneous presentations $G/K$, underlying manifolds admitting such presentations, or Einstein metrics, nor whether equivalence means equivariant diffeomorphism, diffeomorphism, isometry, or isometry up to scaling. Moreover, flatness is a property of a metric, so the source's phrase "nonflat homogeneous spaces" requires the interpretation stated above or further clarification. These choices require human mathematical review.

Short English statement

Classify noncompact homogeneous spaces that carry an invariant, nonflat Riemannian Einstein metric. The source leaves the equivalence relation and several category conventions unspecified. Its supplied status is partially_solved; the dated literature assessment concerns negative scalar curvature and is not an independently verified resolution of the full classification problem.
