Phys-133 · Math-12071

Axis and low-dimensional cases of Levitin's disk-intersection conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and notation.
An indefinite matrix pencil $A-\lambda B$ has eigenvalues where its determinant vanishes. A Nevanlinna function maps the upper half-plane to itself and the lower half-plane to itself under conjugate symmetry; meromorphic examples have real poles. A Schauder basis permits a unique norm-convergent ordered expansion. Counts in the source are zeros and poles, with a multiplicity convention requiring specification for a proof.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
The record bundles primary items 11.1--13.1.
  1. Let $n\ge1$, $0<c<2$, and let $A$ be the $2n\times2n$ tridiagonal matrix with diagonal entries $c$ and adjacent off-diagonal entries $1$. Let $B=\operatorname{diag}(I_n,-I_n)$. Prove that every non-real $\lambda\in\sigma(BA)$ satisfies both $|\lambda-c|\le2$ and $|\lambda+c|\le2$.
  2. Extend the spectral results for $(-d^2/dx^2+c/(1+|x|))\psi=\lambda\operatorname{sgn}(x)\psi$ to more general potential functions, specifying the admissible class and conclusions.
  3. Let $m_j$ be meromorphic Nevanlinna functions and $I\subset\mathbb R$ a nonempty interval such that the number of poles in every nonempty subinterval $J\subset I$ tends to infinity with $j$. If $g$ is analytic on a complex neighborhood of $I$, show for every open complex neighborhood $U$ of $J$ that the number of zeros of $m_j-g$ in $U$ tends to infinity; also show that the difference between the numbers of such zeros and poles of $m_j$ in $U$ is bounded by a constant independent of $j$. The source records the case where $\lim_jj^{-1}\#\{\text{poles in }J\}$ exists.
  4. For $V(x)=\sum_{k=-m}^m v_ke^{2ikx}$, $v_k\in\mathbb C$, classify coefficient sets for which eigenfunctions of $-d^2/dx^2+V$ on $(0,\pi)$ with periodic boundary conditions form a Schauder basis of $L^2(0,\pi)$. The source records the case $V=e^{-2ix}+be^{2ix}$ as affirmative exactly when $|b|=1$.
Scope and source qualification.
The original broad Sturm–Liouville extension and the zero/pole count conventions are preserved as questions to specify. Spectral domains and generalized eigenfunctions must be treated where needed; a statement about the named matrix alone does not settle this multi-question record. The catalogue title/status was paired with a machine-generated research attempt; that attempt is not peer-reviewed evidence of settlement. The statement covers the original source questions rather than only the title’s subcase.

Short English statement

Resolve an indefinite-pencil enclosure, generalize an indefinite Sturm–Liouville model, compare Nevanlinna zeros and poles, and classify periodic Schrödinger bases.
