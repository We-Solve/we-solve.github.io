Math-7310

Positive Ricci Curvature and String Orientation Classes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Under the standard interpretation of the notation, let $n\geq 0$ be an integer and let $M$ be a closed smooth $n$-dimensional manifold equipped with a string structure $S$. A string structure is a lift of the stable tangent classifying map from $B\operatorname{Spin}$ to $B\operatorname{String}$; equivalently, it includes a spin structure and a homotopy trivialization of its characteristic class $\tfrac12p_1(TM)$. Let $\operatorname{tmf}$ denote connective topological modular forms. Interpreting $\sigma$ as the string orientation, write \[ \sigma(M,S)\in\pi_n\operatorname{tmf} \cong\operatorname{tmf}^{-n}(\mathrm{pt}) \] for the image of the string bordism class of $(M,S)$.

The historical question in the supplied record is whether, for every such pair $(M,S)$, \[ \left(\exists\text{ a Riemannian metric }g\text{ on }M\text{ with } \operatorname{Ric}_g(v,v)>0 \text{ for every }x\in M\text{ and }0\ne v\in T_xM\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sigma(M,S)=0. \] The closed smooth domain, the meaning of $\sigma$, and universal quantification over choices of $S$ are standard interpretive conventions used to make this draft precise; the supplied statement does not explicitly specify them. It supplies no alternative domain, boundary conditions, or rule selecting $S$.

Source qualification. The supplied original statement and verification note report that this implication has a negative answer in the cited workshop report. The verification note further says that a strengthened hypothesis is subsequently proposed, but does not supply that hypothesis. Thus this draft records the historical question and its supplied negative-answer qualification; it cannot state the strengthened question. The registry's open label and dated discussion of Stolz's related Witten-genus conjecture must be distinguished from the reported negative answer to this particular $\operatorname{tmf}$-valued question.

Short English statement

The historical question asks whether positive Ricci curvature forces the string orientation class of a string manifold to vanish in $\operatorname{tmf}$. The supplied verification says this question has a negative answer and mentions an unspecified strengthened hypothesis. The record also labels the entry open while discussing Stolz's related Witten-genus conjecture. These qualifications concern different formulations and require human review.
