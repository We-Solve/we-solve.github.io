Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology only, a polytope in a finite-dimensional real vector space $V$ is the convex hull of a finite nonempty subset of $V$. Its nonempty faces are the sets on which some linear functional attains its maximum; the empty face is also included. If an ambient lattice $M\subset V$ is specified, its lattice points are $P\cap M$. Two vertices are adjacent when they are the endpoints of an edge.
