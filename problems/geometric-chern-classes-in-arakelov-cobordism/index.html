Definitions and mathematical statement

Supplied problem. Give a geometric interpretation of the Chern classes in Arakelov algebraic cobordism.

The supplied record associates this question with Rodriguez's weak Arakelov algebraic cobordism theory, but does not define that theory or identify it explicitly as the theory intended in the original question. It also does not specify the admissible base schemes or geometric objects, the bundles and any additional data carrying Chern classes, the groups or operators in which those classes occur, or the range of Chern indices.

Accordingly, the requested construction is a geometric description of the Chern classes in the intended Arakelov algebraic cobordism theory. The record does not specify what constitutes a geometric interpretation or which compatibility properties it must satisfy. These missing specifications prevent a fully quantified mathematical formulation; choosing them would add hypotheses absent from the supplied source. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.

Short English statement

The problem asks for a geometric meaning for Chern classes in Arakelov algebraic cobordism. The intended theory, its domain, and the required form of the interpretation need clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this status has not been independently verified.
