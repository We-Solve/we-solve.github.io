Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the ambient class of spaces or define its starred groupability notation. Consequently, the question below is a conditional formulation requiring those conventions to be recovered from the source.

For a topological space $X$, the standard unstarred cover notation can be described as follows. An open cover $\mathcal U$ consists of open subsets of $X$ whose union is $X$; under the usual nontrivial-cover convention, $X\notin\mathcal U$. Write $\mathcal O(X)$ for these covers. An $\omega$-cover is a member $\mathcal U\in\mathcal O(X)$ such that every finite subset of $X$ is contained in some member of $\mathcal U$; write $\Omega(X)$ for this class. A $\gamma$-cover is an infinite member $\mathcal U\in\mathcal O(X)$ such that each point of $X$ belongs to all but finitely many members; write $\Gamma(X)$ for this class. A $\tau$-cover is a member $\mathcal U\in\mathcal O(X)$ such that each point belongs to infinitely many members and, for every $x,y\in X$, at least one of \[ \{U\in\mathcal U:x\in U,\ y\notin U\},\qquad \{U\in\mathcal U:y\in U,\ x\notin U\} \] is finite. Write $\mathrm T(X)$ for this class. These descriptions explain standard notation; the supplied record does not confirm its cover conventions, including any countability restriction.

For classes $\mathcal A(X)$ and $\mathcal B(X)$ of covers, $\mathsf S_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}$ from $\mathcal A(X)$, there are finite subfamilies $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal U_n$ such that \[ \bigcup_{n\geq1}\mathcal F_n\in\mathcal B(X). \] The property $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ has the same selection quantifiers restricted to sequences for which no $\mathcal U_n$ has a finite subcover, and requires instead \[ \left\{\bigcup\mathcal F_n:n\geq1\right\}\in\mathcal B(X). \]

Short English statement

Tsaban's supplied Problem 17 asks whether $\mathsf U_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Gamma,\mathrm T)$ is equivalent to selecting finitely many sets from each $\omega$-cover so that the selected family has the source's $\tau$-groupability property.
