Short-Interval Möbius Orthogonality to Nilsequences

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied formulation requires conventions for the degree of a nilmanifold and for polynomial sequences. The following gives a precise filtered-nilmanifold interpretation; the record does not specify whether this is its intended convention.

Let $k\geq 1$ be an integer. Let $G$ be a nilpotent Lie group and $\Gamma\leq G$ a discrete cocompact subgroup, so that $G/\Gamma$ is compact. Fix a metric $d$ on $G/\Gamma$. Equip $G$ with a descending filtration by closed subgroups \[ G=G_0=G_1\supseteq G_2\supseteq\cdots\supseteq G_k\supseteq G_{k+1}=\{e\}, \qquad [G_i,G_j]\subseteq G_{i+j}, \] where $G_r=\{e\}$ for $r>k$. This is the degree-at-most-$k$ convention used here; it is additional interpretive data absent from the record. For $g:{\mathbb{Z}}\to G$, define \[ D_hg(n)=g(n+h)g(n)^{-1}. \] Write $\operatorname{Poly}({\mathbb{Z}}\to G_\bullet)$ for the sequences satisfying \[ D_{h_r}\cdots D_{h_1}g(n)\in G_r \quad\text{for every }r\geq1\text{ and all }n,h_1,\ldots,h_r\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] The source writes $\operatorname{Poly}({\mathbb{Z}}\to G)$ without specifying a filtration.

For a positive integer $n$, the Möbius function $\mu(n)$ is $0$ if a prime square divides $n$, and otherwise is $(-1)^r$ if $n$ has $r$ distinct prime factors, with $\mu(1)=1$. A map $F:G/\Gamma\to{\mathbb{C}}$ is Lipschitz if $|F(u)-F(v)|\leq Ld(u,v)$ for some finite $L$ and all $u,v$.

Under the preceding convention, the proposed assertion is as follows. For every fixed such filtered nilmanifold and every fixed Lipschitz $F$, and for every positive function $H=H(X)$ with $H(X)\to\infty$ as $X\to\infty$, define \[ A_{X,H}(x)=\sup_{g\in\operatorname{Poly}({\mathbb{Z}}\to G_\bullet)} \left|\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\\x\leq n\leq x+H(X)}}\mu(n)F(g(n)\Gamma)\right|. \] Is it true that, for every $\varepsilon>0$, \[ \lim_{X\to\infty}\frac{1}{X}\lambda\left(\{x\in[X,2X]:A_{X,H}(x)>\varepsilon H(X)\}\right)=0, \] where $\lambda$ denotes Lebesgue measure? This specifies one meaning of the source's assertion $A_{X,H}(x)=o(H)$ for almost all $x\in[X,2X]$. If integer starting points are intended, replace the measure in this display by the cardinality of the corresponding set with $x\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. The record does not choose between these interpretations. Both endpoints of the sum are included, as in the supplied statement. No upper bound on $H(X)$ is supplied, and none is imposed here. The supremum allows the polynomial sequence to vary with $X$ and $x$, while the nilmanifold, filtration, metric, and $F$ remain fixed.

Short English statement

The question asks whether Möbius sums on intervals of every diverging length have negligible correlation, relative to the interval length, uniformly over polynomial nilsequences on a fixed nilmanifold, outside a vanishing proportion of starting points. The filtration convention and the meaning of almost all starting points require confirmation from the source.
