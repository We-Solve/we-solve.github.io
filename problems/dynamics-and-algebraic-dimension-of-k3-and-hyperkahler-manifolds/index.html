Math-1168

Dynamics and Algebraic Dimension of K3 and Hyperkähler Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The record contains two separate questions. First, let $X$ be a projective complex K3 surface, meaning a smooth simply connected projective surface with trivial canonical bundle, and let $f\in\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ have positive topological entropy $h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)>0$. Does there exist $x\in X$ such that \[ \overline{\{f^m(x):m\in{\mathbb{Z}}\}}^{\mathrm{an}}=X? \] The closure is in the usual complex-analytic, or Euclidean, topology, as specified by the supplied literature assessment; Zariski density would be weaker. The display uses a two-sided automorphism orbit. The source does not specify whether only forward iterates $m\geq0$ are intended, so this convention needs source confirmation. The question asks for existence of a dense orbit for every such automorphism, not density of every point's orbit.

Second, in the usual irreducible holomorphic symplectic interpretation of hyperkähler manifold, let $M$ be a compact simply connected Kähler manifold with \[ H^0(M,\Omega_M^2)={\mathbb{C}}\sigma, \] where $\sigma$ is nondegenerate, and let $\dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}M=2m$. Define its algebraic dimension by \[ a(M)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{{\mathbb{C}}}\mathcal M(M), \] where $\mathcal M(M)$ is its field of meromorphic functions. Is \[ a(M)\in\{0,m,2m\}? \] Dimension here is complex dimension. The extracted source does not explicitly impose the irreducible holomorphic symplectic convention, compactness, or connectedness assumptions; arbitrary products of hyperkähler factors are not silently included in this interpretation.

The supplied literature triage checked 2026-08-21 says that the Euclidean dense-orbit question remains open for projective K3 surfaces, while the algebraic-dimension trichotomy is known in complex dimension two and conditionally in higher dimensions. The higher-dimensional conditions and exact theorem statements are not supplied, so no unconditional higher-dimensional trichotomy is asserted here.

Short English statement

Ask whether every positive-entropy automorphism of a projective K3 surface has an analytically dense orbit, and whether a compact irreducible hyperkähler manifold has algebraic dimension zero, half its complex dimension, or its full dimension. Orbit and manifold conventions need confirmation.
