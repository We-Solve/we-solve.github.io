Kourovka Notebook Problem 12.40

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and $G$ a finite group. Interpret an irreducible $p$-modular character $\varphi$ as the irreducible Brauer character afforded by a simple module $V$ over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p$; its degree is $\varphi(1)=\dim V$. For a positive integer $n$, define its $p$-part by \[ n_p=p^{v_p(n)},\qquad v_p(n)=\max\{a\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}:p^a\mid n\}. \] No hypothesis that $p\mid |G|$ is stated in the source.

For a fixed prime $p$, a finite-valued bound of the requested form is a function \[ f_p:\{p^a:a\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\}\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0} \] such that, for every finite group $G$ and every irreducible $p$-modular character $\varphi$ of $G$, \[ \varphi(1)_p\leq f_p(|G|_p). \] The requested problem is to determine whether such bounds exist and, where they do, determine the best possible bound, separately for \[ \text{(a) }p=2,\qquad \text{(b) }p\ne2. \] Under the pointwise interpretation of "best possible," the target for fixed $p$ is the extended-real extremal function \[ B_p(p^a)=\sup\left\{\varphi(1)_p:\ G\text{ is finite},\ |G|_p=p^a,\ \varphi\text{ is an irreducible }p\text{-modular character of }G\right\}. \] A finite bound exists precisely when these suprema are finite for every $a\geq0$.

The source writes $f$, without explicitly stating whether part (b) permits dependence on the odd prime $p$. If a single function is intended, require instead a finite-valued function on $\{p^a:p\text{ is an odd prime},\ a\geq0\}$, with the displayed inequality holding simultaneously for all odd primes. This quantifier qualification remains unresolved.

The supplied record labels the overall problem partially solved and reports part (a) as solved negatively: no estimate of the requested kind exists for $p=2$. This is a supplied source assessment, not an independently verified assertion here. Part (b) is retained as the remaining question in that record.

Short English statement

For irreducible modular characters of finite groups, seek the sharpest bound on the $p$-part of the character degree using only the $p$-part of the group order. The supplied source reports that no such bound exists for $p=2$, and retains the odd-prime case as unresolved. Whether the odd-prime bound must be uniform in $p$ is not explicit.
