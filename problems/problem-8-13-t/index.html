Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

T. Kerler's Problem 8.13 concerns homological TQFTs. The source's collections $\mathcal Q^*$ and $\mathcal Q^0$, and its meanings of homological and strictly homological, are not defined in this extract. With those source meanings retained, the four requests are:
  1. Find the irreducible components and the ring structure of $\mathcal Q^*$ under direct sum $\oplus$ and tensor product $\otimes$.
  2. Determine whether every strictly homological TQFT belongs to $\mathcal Q^*$.
  3. Identify the homological TQFTs coming from higher-rank gauge theories, for example $\mathrm{PSU}(n)$ in [129], as elements of $\mathcal Q^*$.
  4. Identify the irreducible factors of the constant-order theories $V_p^{[0]}$ in the cyclotomic-integer expansion of Reshetikhin--Turaev theory as elements of $\mathcal Q^*$.
The source describes (1) in terms of representation theory for the groups $\mathrm{Sp}(2,{\mathbb{Z}}),\mathrm{Sp}(4,{\mathbb{Z}}),\ldots,\mathrm{Sp}(2g,{\mathbb{Z}}),\ldots$, together with generators and relations from standard handle attachments. It speculates that these constraints may force a positive answer to (2). For (3), it seeks understanding and possibly closed formulas for the polynomials of [129] expressing $\mathrm{PSU}(n)$ invariants through Alexander-polynomial coefficients.

For (4), the source reports evidence suggesting $(p-3)/2$-fold symmetric products of elements of $\mathcal Q^0$. A proposed corollary for closed $M$ with first Betti number $b_1(M)\ge1$ is \[ V_p(M)=(\zeta_p-1)^{(p-3)/2} P_{(p-3)/2}\!\left(\lambda_{\rm CWL}(M)\right) +O\!\left((\zeta_p-1)^{(p-1)/2}\right),\tag{46} \] where $\lambda_{\rm CWL}$ is the Casson--Walker--Lescop invariant, $P_j\in{\mathbb{Z}}[x]$ has degree $j$, and the normalization is $V_p(S^3)=1$. The parameter $p$, root $\zeta_p$, coefficient ring, polynomial universality, and remainder convention must be recovered from preceding definitions; the half-integer expressions require an appropriate odd-index range. In a cyclotomic-adic interpretation the $O$-term means a divisibility/filtration statement, not an ordinary numerical asymptotic unless so defined. The source says [212] establishes (46) for $p=5$ and general $b_1\ge1$, and reports work in progress for general $p$ when $M$ is a torus bundle over a circle. These are source-era reports, not newly verified results.

The contextual perturbative program views homological theories as a starting point extending finite-type theory for homology $3$-spheres, and at fixed $p$ views Reshetikhin--Turaev theory as a deformation of $\mathcal Q^*$-theories. For a surface $\Sigma_g$, let $\Gamma_g$ be its mapping class group and $\mathcal I_g$ the Torelli subgroup, the kernel of the action on $H_1(\Sigma_g;{\mathbb{Z}})$. The action \[ V^{[g]}:\Gamma_g\to\mathrm{GL}_R(V(\Sigma_g)) \] extends linearly to ${\mathbb{Z}}[\Gamma_g]\to\operatorname{End}_R(V(\Sigma_g))$. Write $J_g$ for the augmentation ideal of ${\mathbb{Z}}[\mathcal I_g]$, as embedded in ${\mathbb{Z}}[\Gamma_g]$, subject to checking whether the source instead means its generated ambient ideal. The excerpt defines length as the maximal $L\in{\mathbb{N}}$ satisfying \[ V^{[g]}(J_g^{L+1})=0. \] This definition has a monotonicity defect as written: annihilation of one power implies annihilation of every higher power, so it cannot specify a finite maximal $L$. It is retained as a source issue rather than silently changed to a minimal exponent. Genus-uniformity is also unstated. The source identifies length-zero theories with homological ones and suggests length-one theories are extension objects $\operatorname{Ext}(V,W)$, $V,W\in\mathcal Q^*$. It further reports, in characteristic different from $2$, factorization of the corresponding mapping-class representations through a Johnson--Morita homomorphism displayed as \[ \Gamma_g\longrightarrow \bigwedge^3 H_1(\Sigma_g)\rtimes\mathrm{Sp}(2g,{\mathbb{Z}}), \] with explicit extensions referenced in [211]. Coefficients, closed-versus-bordered surface conventions and the precise target of this homomorphism require source review.

Short English statement

Classify the source's homological TQFTs, relate gauge-theoretic and constant cyclotomic terms to them, and develop the indicated perturbative extension theory. Preserve the speculative symmetric-power and Casson--Walker--Lescop formula separately from source-reported cases. Several defining objects are missing, and the printed maximal-length definition is internally problematic.
