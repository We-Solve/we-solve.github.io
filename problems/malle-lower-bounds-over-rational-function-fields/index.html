Math-902

Malle Lower Bounds over Rational Function Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $q=p^r$ be a prime power and let $K={\mathbb{F}}_q(t)$. Let $G\leq S_m$ be a transitive permutation group. The supplied statement asks for a lower bound of Malle type for a counting function $N_{G,K}(X)$ of degree-$m$ extensions with discriminant norm at most $X$.

A conventional interpretation of the group condition is that $L/K$ is finite separable and that the Galois closure $\widetilde L/K$, acting on the $m$ embeddings of $L$ into a separable closure of $K$, has permutation group conjugate to $G$ in $S_m$. This interpretation requires confirmation: the source says that the degree-$m$ extension has Galois group $G$, without explicitly mentioning its Galois closure. Requiring $L/K$ itself to be Galois would give a different counting problem.

Under the Galois-closure interpretation, and once the discriminant and constant-field conventions have been fixed, the counting function would be \[ N_{G,K}(X)=\#\left\{[L/K]_K:\ [L:K]=m,\ L/K\text{ finite separable},\ \operatorname{Gal}(\widetilde L/K)\cong G\text{ in the specified permutation action},\ \mathrm N\mathfrak d_{L/K}\leq X\right\}, \] where $[L/K]_K$ denotes a $K$-isomorphism class. If $\mathfrak d_{L/K}$ means the full discriminant divisor on ${\mathbb{P}}^1_{{\mathbb{F}}_q}$, then its norm is $q^{\deg\mathfrak d_{L/K}}$. Using instead a discriminant ideal over ${\mathbb{F}}_q[t]$ excludes the place at infinity; the supplied record does not choose between these conventions.

For comparison, for nontrivial $G$ the usual permutation-index exponent is defined by \[ \operatorname{ind}(g)=m-\#\{\text{orbits of }\langle g\rangle\text{ on }\{1,\ldots,m\}\}, \qquad a(G)=\left(\min_{1\ne g\in G}\operatorname{ind}(g)\right)^{-1}. \] A precise power-exponent lower-bound formulation would be \[ \forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists c=c(G,q,\varepsilon)>0\ \exists X_0=X_0(G,q,\varepsilon)>0\ \forall X\geq X_0:\quad N_{G,K}(X)\geq cX^{a(G)-\varepsilon}. \] This formula is a possible interpretation to be checked, not an identification established by the supplied extract. A lower bound retaining the conjectured logarithmic factor is a stronger possible target. The record supplies neither the exact lower-bound formula nor the logarithmic exponent.

Unresolved statement qualifications. The requested target is the conjectural lower bound in Malle's asymptotic for $N_{G,{\mathbb{F}}_q(t)}(X)$. A definitive quantified statement cannot be recovered from the supplied material without specifying the strength of that bound, the admissible $q$, any restrictions relating $p$ to $G$, whether the extensions must have constant field exactly ${\mathbb{F}}_q$, and the discriminant convention. The trivial transitive group also requires separate treatment because the minimum defining $a(G)$ is empty. No resolution of these issues is asserted here.

Short English statement

The supplied problem asks for the conjectured lower bound on the number of degree-$m$ extensions of ${\mathbb{F}}_q(t)$ with prescribed transitive Galois-closure group and bounded discriminant. The exact bound and several counting hypotheses are missing. The displayed power-exponent formulation is a possible interpretation requiring review. The supplied label is partially solved; it is not an independently verified status.
