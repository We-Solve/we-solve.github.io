Math-114

Field Reconstruction from Rationalized Milnor K-Theory

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $F$, use the conventional Kronecker dimension \[ \operatorname{Kdim}(F)= \begin{cases} \operatorname{trdeg}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(F)+1,&\operatorname{char}(F)=0,\\ \operatorname{trdeg}_{{\mathbb{F}}_p}(F),&\operatorname{char}(F)=p>0. \end{cases} \] No finite-generation hypothesis is imposed. The supplied excerpt does not explicitly define this dimension convention.

Interpret the source's inseparable closure $F^i$ as its perfect closure: in a fixed algebraic closure $\overline F$, set \[ F^i=\begin{cases} F,&\operatorname{char}(F)=0,\\ \displaystyle\bigcup_{r\geq 0}\{x\in\overline F:x^{p^r}\in F\},&\operatorname{char}(F)=p>0. \end{cases} \] Its abstract field-isomorphism type does not depend on the chosen algebraic closure. This interpretation agrees with the supplied literature assessment's terminology, but the excerpt does not separately define $F^i$.

Regard the multiplicative abelian group $F^\times$ as a ${\mathbb{Z}}$-module. Its Milnor $K$-theory ring is the graded ring \[ K_*^M(F)=T_{{\mathbb{Z}}}(F^\times)\big/\left\langle a\otimes(1-a):a\in F\setminus\{0,1\}\right\rangle, \] where the generators $F^\times$ have degree one and $K_0^M(F)={\mathbb{Z}}$. Write \[ A(F)=K_*^M(F)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}. \] An isomorphism of these graded ${\mathbb{Q}}$-algebras is understood to preserve the grading and the unit.

Conjecture (reconstruction within the stated class). For every pair of fields $F,G$ with $\operatorname{Kdim}(F)\geq 2$ and $\operatorname{Kdim}(G)\geq 2$, \[ A(F)\cong A(G)\quad\text{as graded ${\mathbb{Q}}$-algebras} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad F^i\cong G^i\quad\text{as fields}. \] This formalizes the source's assertion that the isomorphism type of $F^i$ is determined by $A(F)$, interpreting comparison fields as belonging to the same stated class. The source does not explicitly specify whether comparison with fields outside this class is also intended. No compatible lifting of a particular algebra isomorphism, uniqueness, or functorial reconstruction is asserted here.

The accompanying source discussion states that $F\to F^i$ induces an isomorphism on rationalized Milnor $K$-theory. It also discusses indeterminacy from nonzero rational scalings in degree one when $F^\times$ is divisible; these remarks explain the perfect-closure target and do not add a divisibility hypothesis to the conjecture. The supplied dated assessment reports a reconstruction result under transcendence-degree-at-least-five hypotheses, whose complete additional hypotheses are not supplied, and labels the broader conjecture partially solved. These are supplied contextual claims, not independently verified results in this draft.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether rationalized Milnor $K$-theory determines the perfect closure of every field of Kronecker dimension at least two, up to abstract field isomorphism. The draft uses conventional meanings of Kronecker dimension and inseparable closure. The supplied partial-solution assessment is dated curation and requires independent review.
