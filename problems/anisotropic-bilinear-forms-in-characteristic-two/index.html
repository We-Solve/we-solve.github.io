Anisotropic Bilinear Forms in Characteristic Two

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a field with $\operatorname{char}(F)=2$. A symmetric bilinear form on an $F$-vector space $V$ is a map $b\colon V\times V\to F$ that is linear in each argument and satisfies $b(v,w)=b(w,v)$. Its radical is \[ \operatorname{rad}(b)=\{v\in V: b(v,w)=0\text{ for every }w\in V\}. \] In the usual finite-dimensional setting, regularity means $\operatorname{rad}(b)=\{0\}$. The form is anisotropic if $b(v,v)\ne 0$ for every $v\ne 0$. The supplied record does not explicitly state the dimension convention.

For each field $F$ of characteristic $2$, each integer $h>0$, and each integer $d\geq 0$, the source asks: which anisotropic regular symmetric bilinear forms $b$ over $F$ satisfy \[ \operatorname{height}(b)=h, \qquad \operatorname{degree}(b)=d? \] Here $\operatorname{height}(b)$ and $\operatorname{degree}(b)$ denote the invariants intended in the cited report. Their definitions and conventions are absent from the supplied record, so the displayed conditions cannot be made fully explicit from this evidence alone. The record also does not specify whether the requested classification is up to isometry, similarity, or another equivalence relation. These qualifications require resolution from the source before this becomes a complete mathematical formulation.

Short English statement

For every field of characteristic $2$ and every pair of integers $h>0$, $d\geq 0$, classify anisotropic regular symmetric bilinear forms of height $h$ and degree $d$. The supplied record leaves the definitions of height and degree, the dimension convention, and the classification equivalence unspecified.
