Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Conjecture 18 (Dynamical Andr{\'e}--Oort Conjecture for 1-Dimensional Families)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T$ be a nonsingular algebraic curve, and consider the family written in the supplied source as \[ T\longrightarrow\mathcal M_d^{\mathrm{crit}},\qquad t\longmapsto(f_t,P_{1,t},\ldots,P_{2d-2,t}). \] Here $\mathcal M_d^{\mathrm{crit}}$ denotes the source's moduli space of degree-$d$ rational maps with marked critical points, and $P_{i,t}$ is the critical point bearing label $i$ in the fiber over $t$. The source does not specify the ground field, the allowed range of $d$, or the convention for repeated critical points. These qualifications remain unresolved.

A rational map $g$ is postcritically finite (PCF) if every critical point $c$ has finite forward orbit: \[ \#\{g^n(c):n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq0}\}<\infty. \] The supplied conjecture, attributed to Baker--DeMarco (2013), asserts the equivalence of the following conditions:

  1. (a) The set $\{t\in T:f_t\text{ is PCF}\}$ is infinite.
  2. (b) For every $i,j\in\{1,\ldots,2d-2\}$, the marked critical-point maps $P_i:T\to{\mathbb{P}}^1$ and $P_j:T\to{\mathbb{P}}^1$ are $f$-dynamically related.
The quantification in (b) includes $i=j$, as written in the source. The supplied record does not define "$f$-dynamically related," so condition (b) is retained as source terminology and does not yet give a self-contained mathematical condition. The field over which parameters are counted in (a) is also unspecified. Furthermore, the displayed morphism to a moduli space does not by itself specify a global representative family on a fixed ${\mathbb{P}}^1$; the source nevertheless writes the critical markings as maps to ${\mathbb{P}}^1$. The intended representative-family or lifting convention requires clarification.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that a one-dimensional family of rational maps with marked critical points has infinitely many postcritically finite members exactly when every pair of marked critical points is dynamically related. Precision requires the missing definition of dynamical relatedness, the ground field and parameter domain, and the conventions for the marked family.
