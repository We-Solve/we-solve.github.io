Isomorphism Type of the Khovanov-Complex Concordance Group

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $C(K)$ for the graded Khovanov chain complex denoted by the double-bracket notation in the supplied record, and write $[C(K)]_{\sim}$ for its class under the intended equivalence relation $\sim$. The record describes an abelian group $G$ of such classes, with multiplication induced by tensor product: \[ [C(K)]_{\sim}\cdot[C(L)]_{\sim} =[C(K)\otimes C(L)]_{\sim}. \] This formula records the intended operation; it does not supply the missing definition of the equivalence relation or establish closure, well-definedness, or the group axioms.

Problem (conditional on the intended construction of $G$). Determine the isomorphism type, as an abelian group, of $G$. The supplied original question is: "What can one say about the isomorphism type of $G$?"

Unresolved construction details. The supplied record does not specify the objects allowed as $K$, the coefficient ring, the precise Khovanov complex variant, the grading and shift conventions, the equivalence relation, or the tensor-product base ring. In particular, the title's reference to concordance does not define the equivalence relation. These omissions prevent an unconditional definition of $G$ from this record alone; they must be resolved from the relevant report passage before the problem has a fully precise formulation.

Supplied structural context. The dated curation describes $G$ as established and asserts that it admits a surjection onto an infinite-rank free abelian group whose kernel is infinitely generated, while its full isomorphism type remains undetermined. This is retained as a supplied literature assessment, not as an independently verified theorem or a substitute for the missing construction.

Short English statement

Determine the abstract abelian-group structure of the group that the source associates with equivalence classes of graded Khovanov chain complexes under tensor product. The supplied record leaves the equivalence relation and several construction conventions unspecified, so this statement remains conditional on recovering those definitions. Its partially_solved label and structural claims are supplied dated curation requiring independent review.
