Kourovka Notebook Problem 1.55

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\kappa$ denote the prescribed number of free generators, and let $F_\kappa$ be a free group with a free basis of cardinality $\kappa$. The supplied record does not specify the permitted values of $\kappa$.

A strict total order $<$ on $F_\kappa$ is a transitive, irreflexive relation such that, for distinct $g,h\in F_\kappa$, exactly one of $g<h$ and $h<g$ holds. Under the two-sided compatibility convention, a linearly ordered group satisfies \[ \forall a,b,g,h\in F_\kappa,\qquad g<h\ \Longrightarrow\ agb<ahb. \] Under the left-order convention, compatibility instead means \[ \forall a,g,h\in F_\kappa,\qquad g<h\ \Longrightarrow\ ag<ah. \] The supplied record does not explicitly specify the compatibility convention; these alternatives must not be identified without clarification.

Classification problem (A. I. Kokorin, 1965). Give an elementary classification of linearly ordered free groups with a fixed number $\kappa$ of free generators.

One possible classification target is the collection of compatible strict total orders on $F_\kappa$, up to order-preserving group isomorphism. For this target, two orders $<_1$ and $<_2$ are equivalent precisely when there exists $\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(F_\kappa)$ such that \[ \forall g,h\in F_\kappa,\qquad g<_1h\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \varphi(g)<_2\varphi(h). \] However, the source does not specify the classification equivalence, whether a chosen free basis must be preserved, or what constitutes an elementary classification. This formulation therefore records a possible interpretation rather than resolving those qualifications.

Short English statement

For a fixed number of free generators, classify the linear group orders on the corresponding free group by elementary means. The source leaves the order-compatibility convention, permitted ranks, classification equivalence, and meaning of "elementary" unspecified. Its open status is a supplied registry label, not an independently verified assessment.
