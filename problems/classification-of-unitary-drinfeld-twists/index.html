Classification of Unitary Drinfeld Twists

Definitions and mathematical statement

In a unital $*$-algebra $A$, an element $U$ is called unitary if $U^*U=UU^*=1_A$. Applying this definition to a Drinfeld twist requires specifying the algebra or completion containing the twist and its involution.

Source-level question. How can the unitary Drinfeld twists for $G_q$ be classified or otherwise described?
