Short English statement

The extracted question asks whether the modular Serre weights of $\bar r$ are exactly its Buzzard--Diamond--Jarvis predicted weights. The supplied verification says this was a theorem under omitted hypotheses, incorrectly extracted as an open problem. The missing setting and hypotheses prevent a precise standalone formulation; human mathematical review is required.
