The Q-Form Classification Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $q$ be even, and let $M$ and $M'$ be simply connected manifolds of dimension $2q$, equipped with normal $(q-1)$-smoothings \[ \bar\nu\colon M\longrightarrow B, \qquad \bar\nu'\colon M'\longrightarrow B. \] Here simply connected means path connected with trivial fundamental group. The space $B$ is the common target of the normal structures. The supplied record does not specify its normal-structure fibration, the manifold category, or the precise convention for a normal $(q-1)$-smoothing; these must be supplied to give the hypotheses their full meaning.

Write $E_q(M,\bar\nu)$ for the invariant called the Q-form in the source. Its algebraic definition and the data that an isomorphism must preserve are not supplied. Thus the following is the source's conjectural implication with an unresolved invariant, rather than a self-contained definition of that invariant: \[ \left. \begin{gathered} (M,\bar\nu)\text{ and }(M',\bar\nu')\text{ are bordant over }B,\\ E_q(M,\bar\nu)\cong E_q(M',\bar\nu') \end{gathered} \right\} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad (M,\bar\nu)\text{ and }(M',\bar\nu')\text{ are }h\text{-cobordant over }B. \] Bordism over $B$ is understood to require extension of the specified normal structures across a cobordism. An $h$-cobordism is a cobordism whose two boundary inclusions are homotopy equivalences; the qualification "over $B$" requires compatibility with the specified structures over $B$. The exact compatibility convention must be checked against the source. The record does not specify whether the manifolds are closed, compact, oriented, or allowed to have boundary, nor any relative boundary conditions or lower bound on $q$. These omissions remain unresolved hypotheses and are not silently filled in here.

Short English statement

The Q-form conjecture asserts that two simply connected $2q$-manifolds, for even $q$, with bordant normal $(q-1)$-smoothings into the same $B$ and isomorphic Q-forms are $h$-cobordant over $B$. The definition of the Q-form and several geometric conventions are missing from the supplied record, so this statement requires source-based mathematical review.
