Topological Isolation of Singularities in Witt Bordism

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns a geometric construction in rational Witt bordism. Its precise category and orientation conventions are not stated. The following gives a conditional formulation under the usual convention of compact, oriented PL stratified pseudomanifolds without codimension-one strata; these conventions require confirmation from the source.

For such an $n$-dimensional stratified pseudomanifold $X$, the rational Witt condition requires that, for every singular stratum of odd codimension $2k+1$, with link $L$ of dimension $2k$, \[ I^{\bar m}H_k(L;{\mathbb{Q}})=0, \qquad \bar m(c)=\left\lfloor\frac{c-2}{2}\right\rfloor. \] Here $I^{\bar m}H_k$ denotes intersection homology with lower middle perversity. In the absence of codimension-one strata, the relevant integers satisfy $k\geq 1$. An orientation means an orientation of the regular stratum.

Two closed $n$-dimensional rational Witt spaces $X$ and $Y$ are rational Witt bordant if there is a compact, oriented $(n+1)$-dimensional stratified pseudomanifold $W$ with collared boundary and an orientation-preserving identification \[ \partial W\cong X\sqcup(-Y), \] where the interior satisfies the rational Witt condition and the stratification near the boundary is the product of the boundary stratification with a collar. The notation $-Y$ means $Y$ with reversed orientation. A space has at worst isolated singularities when its singular locus consists of isolated points; an empty singular locus is allowed.

Construction problem. For every dimension $n\geq 0$ and every closed $n$-dimensional rational Witt space $X$ in the intended bordism category, give a purely topological construction of a closed rational Witt space $Y$ of dimension $n$, having at worst isolated singularities, together with a rational Witt bordism $W$ from $X$ to $Y$.

The supplied dated assessment says that the existence of such a bordant representative is known algebraically; the requested target is a purely topological construction. The record does not specify what methods are permitted by that phrase. It also does not settle whether the intended category is PL or topological, whether orientation is required, or whether closedness and compactness are implicit. Consequently, the conventional formulation above is conditional and requires source-level clarification.

Short English statement

Construct, by purely topological means, a rational Witt bordism from each rational Witt space to one whose singularities are all isolated, allowing a nonsingular representative. The supplied assessment distinguishes this construction problem from the algebraically known existence statement. The intended category, bordism conventions, and meaning of "purely topological" need confirmation.
