Definitions and mathematical statement

Terminology and unresolved scope. An inverse spectral problem asks for the recovery of a mathematical object from specified spectral data. In this record, the objects are called indefinite strings, but neither their admissible class nor their spectral data are defined. The record does not specify the governing equation, underlying interval, coefficient or measure hypotheses, endpoint conditions, normalization, or equivalence under which recovery is intended.

Supplied problem statement.

Find an equally concise analogue of the solution of the inverse spectral problem for indefinite strings.

The comparison implicit in "equally concise" and the particular solution to be used as a model are not identified. The supplied background says that concise complete inverse spectral results exist for important generalized string classes, including an indefinite analogue of Krein theory, while the "exact real-measure class" still lacks a comparable full characterization. That class is not defined, and its relation to the quoted request is not made explicit. Consequently, the supplied material does not determine a unique mathematical formulation with specified domains, boundary conditions, and quantifiers. These qualifications remain unresolved; no particular string model or reconstruction theorem is asserted here.
