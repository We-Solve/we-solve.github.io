Yang–Baxter Solutions from Skew Lattices

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A skew lattice is a set $S$ equipped with two associative, idempotent binary operations $\wedge$ and $\vee$ satisfying, for every $x,y\in S$, \[ x\wedge(x\vee y)=x=(y\vee x)\wedge x, \qquad x\vee(x\wedge y)=x=(y\wedge x)\vee x. \] Neither operation is required to be commutative.

For a set $X$, use the braid convention for a set-theoretic Yang--Baxter solution: a map $r\colon X\times X\to X\times X$ satisfies \[ (r\times\mathrm{id}_X)\circ(\mathrm{id}_X\times r)\circ(r\times\mathrm{id}_X) = (\mathrm{id}_X\times r)\circ(r\times\mathrm{id}_X)\circ(\mathrm{id}_X\times r) \] as maps $X^3\to X^3$. This definition alone imposes no bijectivity requirement; the supplied question does not specify whether bijectivity or other additional properties are intended.

Problem. Can skew lattices be used to describe further set-theoretic solutions of the Yang--Baxter equation, and what kinds of solutions arise from such descriptions?

The supplied formulation is an open-ended construction and characterization problem. Short English statement

Investigate constructions of set-theoretic Yang--Baxter solutions from skew lattices and characterize the solutions they produce. The source leaves both the allowed constructions and the meaning of further solutions unspecified. Its open status is a supplied dated assessment requiring independent review.
