Math-1581

Symmetry and finite-extension descent for definable valuation rings

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and $F=K(Z)$, with $Z$ transcendental over $K$. A nontrivial valuation ring of a field $M$ means a proper subring $\mathcal O\subsetneq M$ containing $1$ such that for every $x\in M^\times$, either $x\in\mathcal O$ or $x^{-1}\in\mathcal O$. Shlapentokh's question asks whether there exist such $K$ and such a valuation ring in $F$, or in a finite extension of $F$, which is definable. In the pure ring-language reading with parameters this asks, for example, for a formula $\varphi(x,\bar a)$ with $\bar a\in F$ defining a proper valuation ring of $F$. The original wording does not specify the characteristic, parameter allowance, quantifier complexity or whether the valuation must be trivial on $K$. Its phrase "or a finite extension" also leaves the ambient definability convention unstated.

The supplied aid gives the following parameter obstruction for valuations trivial on the constants. If $v$ is a nontrivial valuation on $F$ whose restriction to $K^\times$ is zero, then its valuation ring is not definable in the pure ring language using only finitely many parameters from $K$. Indeed the places of $K(Z)/K$ correspond to the points of ${\mathbb{P}}^1(K)$, and the fractional-linear action of $\operatorname{PGL}_2(K)$, by automorphisms fixing $K$ pointwise, moves every such valuation ring. A definable set with parameters from $K$ would be invariant under all these automorphisms. Thus a definition of a nontrivial $K$-trivial valuation ring must use a nonconstant parameter. This obstruction is not asserted for valuations whose restrictions to $K$ are nontrivial.

The second reported reduction is independent of that constant-triviality restriction. Let $L/F$ be finite and $\mathcal O_w$ a nontrivial valuation ring of $L$ definable in its pure field structure, allowing parameters. Then \[ \mathcal O_v=\mathcal O_w\cap F \] is a nontrivial valuation ring of $F$ definable with parameters from $F$. To formulate the transfer, choose an $F$-basis $e_1=1,e_2,\ldots,e_n$ of $L$, and name in $F$ its finitely many multiplication structure constants and the coordinates of the parameters in the definition of $\mathcal O_w$. Interpret $L$ on $F^n$ using these constants; substitute $(x,0,\ldots,0)$ for the argument of the defining formula. This gives the contraction formula in $F$. If the definition in $L$ is existential, so is its translated definition in $F$, since each quantified $L$-variable is replaced by finitely many $F$-variables. Nontriviality is preserved: a valuation trivial on $F$ has only the trivial extension to an algebraic extension. The descent need not preserve being parameter-free or definable from constants alone, because the basis constants and parameter coordinates may be nonconstant.

Accordingly the finite-extension option does not avoid the parameter-allowing base-field existence problem under this interpretation. The aid supplies these reductions, not an example solving that problem. Its statement that a positive definition must name a nonconstant element is justified by the symmetry argument only in the $K$-trivial case.

Short English statement

Seek a definable nontrivial valuation ring in a rational function field over some algebraically closed field, possibly after a finite extension. The aid excludes definitions from constants alone for valuations trivial on the constants and shows that a finite-extension example descends to the base field when parameters are allowed.
