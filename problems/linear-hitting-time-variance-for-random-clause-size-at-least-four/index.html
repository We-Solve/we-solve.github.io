Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For each fixed $k\geq 4$, let $H_n$ be the first unsatisfiable time under independent proper uniformly signed k-clauses sampled with replacement. Is $Var(H_n)=Theta_k(n)$? Ask also $Var(min(H_n,floor(Bn)))=Theta_{k,B}(n)$ for $B>\log (2)/(-\log (1-2^{-k}))$.

Linear hitting-time variance for random clause size at least four

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. The proposed first-unsatisfiable-prefix variance is $\Theta(n)$ for $k\geq4$, and between order $n$ and order $n\log n$ for $k=3$.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Sample independent clauses, with replacement, uniformly among the $2^k\binom nk$ signed clauses on $k$ distinct variables. Write $F_m=\bigwedge_{j=1}^mC_j$ and $H_n=\min\{m\ge1:F_m\text{ is unsatisfiable}\}$. For each fixed $k\ge3$, put $\ell_3(n)=\log(en)$, $\ell_k(n)=1$ for $k\ge4$, and $U_k=\log2/[-\log(1-2^{-k})]$. Constants depending only on $k$ satisfy \[ \operatorname{Var}(H_n)\le C_kn\ell_k(n)\quad(n\ge k),\qquad \operatorname{Var}(H_n)\ge c_kn\quad(n\text{ sufficiently large}). \] For fixed $B>0$, $T_{n,B}=\min(H_n,\lfloor Bn\rfloor)$ obeys $\operatorname{Var}(T_{n,B})\le C_{k,B}n\ell_k(n)$. Its matching lower bound $c_kn$ applies when $B>U_k$.

Outline of the argument

Assignment-set survival estimates and clause omission express deletion delays through independent root tests. Occupation and collision moments bound squared delays and hence variance, with matching linear lower bounds from transition-width estimates; clipping preserves the upper bounds.
