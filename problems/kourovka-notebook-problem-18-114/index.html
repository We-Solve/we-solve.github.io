Kourovka Notebook Problem 18.114

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{F}}_5$ be the field with five elements. For a nonzero finite-dimensional ${\mathbb{F}}_5$-vector space $V$, let $GL(V)$ denote its group of invertible linear maps. A subgroup $G\leq GL(V)$ is a $5'$-subgroup if $5\nmid |G|$, and it acts irreducibly if its only invariant linear subspaces are $0$ and $V$. For a finite group $H$, let $k(H)$ denote the number of its conjugacy classes. Write $V\rtimes G$ for the semidirect product of the additive group of $V$ by the given action of $G$, with multiplication \[ (v,g)(w,h)=(v+g(w),gh). \] Does there exist an integer $n\geq 1$, an $n$-dimensional ${\mathbb{F}}_5$-vector space $V$, and a subgroup $G\leq GL(V)$ such that \[ 5\nmid |G|,\qquad G\text{ acts irreducibly on }V,\qquad k(V\rtimes G)=|V|=5^n, \] while $G$ is not cyclic?

The supplied source further reports that the analogous existence question, with ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ and a $p'$-subgroup in place of ${\mathbb{F}}_5$ and a $5'$-subgroup, has an affirmative answer for $p=2,3$ and a negative answer for primes $p>5$. These are supplied background assertions attributed to the journal reference below; they have not been independently verified here. The local registry labels the question for $p=5$ as open.

Short English statement

Is there a noncyclic group of order coprime to $5$ acting faithfully and irreducibly on a nonzero finite vector space over ${\mathbb{F}}_5$, such that the resulting semidirect product has exactly as many conjugacy classes as the vector space has elements?
