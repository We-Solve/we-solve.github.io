Math-870

Determinant Approximation for $L^{2}-Torsion$

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a residually finite group, and let \[ G=G_0\supseteq G_1\supseteq G_2\supseteq\cdots, \qquad G_i\trianglelefteq G, \qquad [G:G_i]<\infty, \qquad \bigcap_{i\geq 0}G_i=\{e\}. \] Put $Q_i=G/G_i$. For a finite matrix $A$ over the rational group algebra ${\mathbb{Q}} G$, let $A_i$ be its image under the entrywise quotient map ${\mathbb{Q}} G\to{\mathbb{Q}} Q_i$.

The supplied statement leaves the determinant convention and the meaning of normalization unstated. The following definitions make a possible formulation precise while retaining that uncertainty. For an $m\times n$ matrix $B$ over ${\mathbb{Q}} H$, let $T_B:\ell^2(H)^n\to\ell^2(H)^m$ be its left convolution operator, and put $|T_B|=(T_B^*T_B)^{1/2}$. Write $\mathcal N(H)$ for the group von Neumann algebra and use its canonical trace $\tau_H(S)=\langle S\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle$. On matrices use the sum of the diagonal traces, without division by the matrix size. Define the spectral measure \[ \mu_B(E)=\sum_{j=1}^n\left\langle 1_E(|T_B|)(\delta_e\otimes e_j),\delta_e\otimes e_j\right\rangle \] for Borel sets $E\subseteq[0,\infty)$. Two conventions that must be distinguished are \[ D_H^+(B)=\exp\left(\int_{(0,\infty)}\log t\,\mathrm d\mu_B(t)\right) \] and \[ D_H^0(B)= \begin{cases} 0,&\mu_B(\{0\})>0,\\ D_H^+(B),&\mu_B(\{0\})=0. \end{cases} \] Here $\exp(-\infty)=0$; the first convention omits the kernel, and in particular gives $D_H^+(0)=1$.

For finite $H$, the canonical trace corresponds to the ordinary matrix trace divided by $|H|$. Thus, if $s_1,\ldots,s_r$ are the positive singular values of the finite linear operator $T_B$, counted with multiplicity, then \[ D_H^+(B)=\left(\prod_{j=1}^r s_j\right)^{1/|H|}, \] with empty product equal to $1$. This specifies the finite-quotient normalization without applying a second normalization to a determinant already defined using the canonical trace.

Determinant approximation question, conditional on the determinant convention. For every group, chain, and matrix as above, does \[ D_G^{\star}(A)=\lim_{i\to\infty}D_{Q_i}^{\star}(A_i) \] hold, including existence of the limit, for the convention $\star$ intended in the source? The supplied evidence does not explicitly determine whether $\star=+$ or $\star=0$, whether a kernel hypothesis is intended, or whether matrices must be square. Consequently these alternatives are not asserted to be equivalent or to be separately intended conjectures. The definitions accommodate rectangular matrices, but the intended range of matrix sizes requires source review. The original source fragment describes an analogue of Lück approximation for $\ell^2$-torsion; it supplies no chain-complex hypotheses from which a separate torsion statement can be specified.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the Fuglede--Kadison determinant of a rational group-ring matrix can be recovered from its images in every nested normal finite-quotient system with trivial intersection. The determinant convention, treatment of kernels, matrix dimensions, and finite-quotient normalization need confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
