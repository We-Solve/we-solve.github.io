Math-3856

Tate-Shafarevich Finiteness for Tori over Function Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a number field and let $C$ be a smooth curve over $k$ with function field $K=k(C)$. Here integrality of $C$ is implicit in the use of its function field; the supplied record does not explicitly require properness or geometric integrality.

A $K$-torus is an algebraic group $T$ over $K$ such that $T_{K^{\mathrm{sep}}}\cong(\mathbb G_m)^r$ for some integer $r\geq 0$. A group of multiplicative type is a commutative affine group scheme that becomes diagonalizable over a separable closure. The supplied generalization does not specify whether such groups must be of finite type.

For the usual interpretation using closed points and completions, let $C^{(1)}$ be the set of closed points of $C$, and let $K_c$ be the completion of $K$ at the discrete valuation associated with $c\in C^{(1)}$. Define \[ \operatorname{Sha}_C^1(T) :=\ker\!\left(H^1_{\mathrm{fppf}}(K,T) \longrightarrow\prod_{c\in C^{(1)}}H^1_{\mathrm{fppf}}(K_c,T)\right). \] These cohomology groups classify torsors; for a torus they agree with the usual degree-one Galois cohomology groups. The supplied record does not itself define $\operatorname{Sha}_C^1$, so this local interpretation remains a qualification requiring source confirmation.

Finiteness question. For every number field $k$, every smooth curve $C/k$ as above, and every $K$-torus $T$, is \[ \#\operatorname{Sha}_C^1(T)<\infty? \] The supplied record also asks this for every group of multiplicative type over $K$; the intended scope of that extension, particularly its finite-type hypothesis, requires clarification. The displayed formulation uses exactly the points of the given curve $C$, without replacing it by a proper model.

Supplied qualification. The original excerpt says that the conjecture is known for a stably rational $K$-torus $T$, referring to an unspecified "loc. cit." Here stable rationality means that $T\times_K\mathbb A_K^m$ is $K$-birational to $\mathbb A_K^{\dim(T)+m}$ for some integer $m\geq0$. The supplied assessment also mentions unspecified related cases and labels the problem partially solved. These are supplied literature claims, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

For a torus over the function field of a smooth curve over a number field, ask whether there are only finitely many torsor classes that become trivial over every completion attached to a closed point of the curve. The source also proposes the question for groups of multiplicative type. The local convention and the scope of this broader class require confirmation. The supplied curation reports the stably rational case as known.
