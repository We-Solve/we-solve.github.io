Math-1178

Eisenbud–Mazur Symbolic-Square Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a commutative Noetherian regular local ring of equal characteristic zero, meaning $\operatorname{char}R=\operatorname{char}(R/\mathfrak m)=0$; regularity means $\dim_{R/\mathfrak m}\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2=\dim R$. Let $P\subset R$ be a prime ideal. For $s\geq1$, define its symbolic power by \[ P^{(s)}=R\cap P^sR_P =\{f\in R:\text{there exists }u\in R\setminus P\text{ with }uf\in P^s\}. \] The contraction notation uses the natural localization map. The Eisenbud--Mazur conjecture, labelled Conjecture 8 in the supplied excerpt, asks whether \[ \forall (R,\mathfrak m)\text{ as above}\ \forall P\in\operatorname{Spec}(R), \qquad P^{(2)}\subseteq\mathfrak mP. \] Here $\mathfrak mP$ is the ordinary ideal product. No homogeneity, positive-height, completion or finite-type hypothesis is added to the conjecture.

The surrounding original excerpt contains separate remarks and positive-characteristic results, retained here as source context rather than hypotheses or substitutes for this conjecture. Remark 7 calls its Theorem 6 an improvement of Hochster--Huneke [8, Theorem 3.7] on growth of symbolic powers in singular affine algebras, and says that the exponent $n-1$ on a Jacobian ideal is sharp and cannot generally be lowered to $n-2$, citing [8, Example 3.8]. Theorem 6, its parameters and that Jacobian ideal are not reproduced. The excerpt also reports counterexamples to the symbolic-square conjecture in positive and mixed characteristic, referring to [3] and [9]. These are not counterexamples under the stated equal-characteristic-zero hypotheses.

Its Theorem 9 is printed for a regular local ring of positive characteristic and a prime of height $h>0$, with the two assertions \[ P^{(h+1)}\subseteq\mathfrak mP,\qquad R/P\text{ is }F\text{-pure}\ \Longrightarrow\ P^{(2h-1)}\subseteq P^2. \] In characteristic $p>0$, $F$-purity ordinarily means purity of the Frobenius map $a\mapsto a^p$, that is, injectivity after tensoring with every module. The second displayed assertion has a source-level problem at $h=1$: for $R=k[x]_{(x)}$ over a perfect field of characteristic $p$, $P=(x)$ has $F$-pure field quotient, but it would require $P=P^{(1)}\subseteq P^2$. Thus the printed context needs an additional qualification or correction that cannot be recovered here. It is not asserted as a valid unrestricted theorem, and its exponent is not silently changed.

The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports the equal-characteristic-zero conjecture established for homogeneous and other special primes, with only special-case verification in later work and the general case still conjectural. This is dated supplied metadata. The verification field says the conjecture was isolated from theorem and remark spillover; retaining that spillover as qualified context preserves its distinctions without incorporating the apparent error into the main question.

Short English statement

For every prime in an equal-characteristic-zero regular local ring, must the symbolic square lie in the maximal ideal times that prime? The characteristic and symbolic-power convention are explicit. Separate source remarks on singular rings and positive characteristic are retained, including a flagged height-one inconsistency in a neighboring quoted theorem.
