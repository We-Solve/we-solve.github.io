Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 34

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $T^2={\mathbb{R}}^2/{\mathbb{Z}}^2$, let $S$ be a closed oriented surface of genus $2$, and let $\pi\colon M\to S$ be a fibration with fiber $T^2$. Write $\mathcal F$ for the foliation whose leaves are the fibers $\pi^{-1}(s)$, for $s\in S$.

The question is to identify hypotheses on the monodromy of $\pi$ ensuring that every foliation $\mathcal G$ sufficiently close to $\mathcal F$ has a compact leaf close to some fiber of $\pi$. In a locally trivial bundle interpretation, monodromy describes transport around loops in $S$, up to the equivalence appropriate to the bundle category. The source does not specify that category or whether the intended hypotheses concern transport maps, their isotopy classes, or their induced action on the homology of $T^2$.

The requested implication can be displayed as a schema. Let $\mu_\pi$ denote the intended monodromy data, let $\mathscr{Fol}(M)$ denote a chosen space of foliations of the same dimension as $\mathcal F$, let $\tau$ be a chosen topology on that space, and let $C(L,\pi^{-1}(s))$ express the intended closeness of a leaf to a fiber. Seek a hypothesis $H$ on $\mu_\pi$ implying \[ \begin{gathered} \exists U\in\tau,\quad \mathcal F\in U,\\ \forall\mathcal G\in U\ \exists s\in S\ \exists L\text{ a leaf of }\mathcal G:\quad L\text{ is compact}\ \text{ and }\ C(L,\pi^{-1}(s)). \end{gathered} \] Here $\mathscr{Fol}(M)$, $\tau$, and $C$ are placeholders for missing source specifications, not additional assumptions asserted by the source. In particular, the source does not specify differentiability, the topology of perturbation, the meaning of leaf closeness, or how the required leaf closeness depends on the size of the perturbation. Consequently this schema records the intended question but does not supply a uniquely determined precise formulation.

Short English statement

For a torus fibration over a closed oriented surface of genus $2$, determine conditions on its monodromy that guarantee a compact leaf near a fiber after a sufficiently small perturbation of the fiber foliation. The source leaves the regularity, perturbation topology, leaf closeness, and intended monodromy data unspecified.
