Statement

Origin of cellular compartmentalisation

Domain. Protocell origins; integrated compartment function.   Retained status/source context. Open integrated reproduction programme. Membrane formation and internal RNA copying have partial experimental realizations; merely enclosing molecules is not a complete protocell.

Definitions and assumptions.
A protocell type $i$ is defined by membrane composition, enclosed catalyst counts, volume and permeability. A finite-type stochastic model includes uptake, catalytic reactions, leakage, membrane growth, division and death. External feeds and environmental conditions form a declared plausible envelope $E$. A viable daughter must retain catalysts above functional thresholds and admit subsequent growth. Let $K_{ij}(e)$ be the mean number of viable type-$i$ daughters produced by a type-$j$ parent in one life cycle; type definitions and assay limits are fixed.
Problem statement.
Find a chemically compatible system with \[ \inf_{e\in E}\rho(K(e))>1, \] under a validated branching approximation, and show repeated hereditary reproduction without adding modern cellular machinery. Measure membrane and internal chemistry jointly so reproduction is not inferred by combining incompatible experiments.
Additional binding assumptions and resolution criterion.
State the full offspring law and irreducibility/nonsingularity conditions behind the branching approximation. Supercriticality for every constant environment does not imply supercriticality under arbitrary environmental switching; that extension needs a specified random matrix-product growth criterion. All membrane and internal-catalyst measurements must refer to the same reproducing units. Positive survival probability is not a high-probability persistence guarantee from a small initial inoculum.
Alternative formulations.
(i) A lipid-vesicle formulation balances solute retention against nutrient permeability. (ii) A coacervate or mineral-pore formulation uses alternative compartment boundaries and asks what constitutes division and lineage continuity. These are different physical models. A weaker encapsulation target measures retention and copying for one cycle; the stronger target demands sustained daughter viability and heritable variation.
Mathematical-status qualification.
The restricted calculation, identity, obstruction or construction identified in this entry is not itself a new open conjecture. The residual target is the explicitly delimited extension, identification, implementation or independent biological validation; an already known special case does not satisfy that residual target.
Obstructions and partial progress.
Adamala and Szostak demonstrate RNA template copying inside model vesicles under compatible conditions, an important resolution of one chemical conflict. The broader review describes additional components but does not certify a complete autonomous system. Membranes can inhibit substrate access, while ions assisting copying can destabilize compartments. In finite-type branching theory the spectral criterion is standard; identifying a chemically realistic kernel and validating its approximation is the biological research target. Physical growth--division details are isolated in Problem 297.

Binding conventions for a concrete instance

Objects, quantifiers and non-vacuity.
An instance declares measurable state, input, output and parameter spaces; a fixed candidate family; admissible initial laws, interventions and environments; a finite horizon or specified asymptotic regime; observation and sampling laws; and performance metrics, positive normalization scales and positive tolerances. These are chosen before fitting or testing, not adjusted after seeing failures. Shared parameters must genuinely be shared across the declared validation domain. Finite dimension alone does not restrict arbitrary real-number encodings: specify a finite library, bounded description length, or regularity/complexity bounds. Exclude constant or no-signal solutions through a fixed informative-output or baseline-improvement requirement where appropriate. A universally quantified result cannot be established by choosing one trivial favorable instance.
Probability laws and data.
Every expectation or probability refers to the specified stochastic law, including initial uncertainty, process noise, observation noise and sampling as appropriate. A deterministic equation is not a probability model without a declared random input or initial law. "Observed" targets in population-level formulas mean population observables $g_j^*$, not noisy estimates asserted to be exact. Let $\widetilde g_j$ be independent validation estimates and suppose \[ \mathbb{P}\{\lvert\widetilde g_j-g_j^*\rvert\le r_j\text{ for every }j\}\ge1-\alpha. \] Then an uncertainty-aware sufficient certification rule for positive target tolerances $\varepsilon_j$ is \[ \max_j\frac{|\widehat g_j-\widetilde g_j|+r_j}{\varepsilon_j}\le1. \] On that simultaneous confidence event it implies every population error is at most its tolerance, by the triangle inequality. Vector/norm versions use corresponding valid simultaneous bounds. Training uncertainty must also be included when the claim averages over repeated training data. Evaluation on finitely many conditions does not establish a continuous-domain supremum without covering and regularity bounds.
Dynamics and well-posedness.
For ODEs and SDEs give coefficient domains, initial laws and existence/uniqueness conditions in the intended solution sense, or keep the entire admitted solution set. For stochastic jump models specify nonnegative off-diagonal rates, diagonal normalization, rate orientation and non-explosion. For PDEs specify spatial domains, boundary/initial conditions, conservation or source terms, and a weak or classical solution class. Moving-domain concentration equations include dilution/transport terms. A stationary law is used only for a time-homogeneous augmented model under existence assumptions; a stationary approximation to a driven system must be justified. Absorbing extinction states are retained. Survival-conditioned, quasi-stationary and unconditional laws are distinct and must not be substituted for one another.
Identification and causal meaning.
Two parameter values are observationally equivalent when they induce the same full distribution under all allowed observation/intervention designs, not merely the same likelihood value for one sample. A target is identified only if constant on the equivalence class. Gauge symmetries, label switching and reference conventions are quotiented or fixed. Finite-data compatible sets use a declared confidence, likelihood or discrepancy rule; an empty set signals model rejection. Bayesian credible sets are not automatically confidence sets. A prior or regularizer does not create data identification. Bounds intended to resolve a question must be informative at a predeclared width or decision threshold.

A causal contrast uses well-defined intervention versions and a stated causal model. Random assignment, or explicit consistency, overlap and exchangeability assumptions, is required for causal interpretation from observations. Interference and time-varying confounding are represented when present. Fixing a mediator by intervention differs from conditioning on its observed value. Marginal potential-outcome laws do not determine individual cross-world effects or an arbitrary joint coupling. Ethically admissible designs, rather than unconstrained interventions, define the experimental menu.

Metrics, units, optimization and limits.
Specify the sampling or reference measure for densities and integrals, the log base, and dimensionless arguments of logarithms. Total variation is $\sup_A|P(A)-Q(A)|$; Wasserstein distances require the declared ground metric and appropriate moment conditions; KL divergence uses its stated absolute-continuity convention. All normed readouts are scaled consistently, and error probabilities use $0<\alpha<1$. A ratio has a positive denominator or an explicit undefined/zero convention. Thermodynamic entropy production is nonnegative in expectation under the relevant hypotheses; individual stochastic realizations need not be. An infimum is not an attained minimum without compactness/coercivity and continuity or another existence argument. Fix the order of large-population, long-time and vanishing-mutation limits. Stability claims declare perturbation space and symmetries.

How the provisional difficulty order was assigned

Four editorial components are scored from 1 (lower burden) to 5 (higher burden): (a) formal or inferential difficulty, (b) access to evidence and identification burden, (c) mechanistic/computational integration, and (d) independent validation and transfer. Their unweighted total orders the entries. For selected entries in the highest bands, an explicit editorial priority breaks equal totals; all other ties are broken by original problem number. Equal totals are not evidence of reliably different true difficulties. Scores are judgments, not measurements, completion-time estimates or consensus rankings.

The object ranked is an informative, nondegenerate resolution of the scoped scientific target, with the stated budget and validation domain. A sharp useful impossibility result can qualify; an already known identity, vacuous bound, arbitrary favorable model or lookup table cannot. Rank 330 is merely the least difficult relative assessment in this catalogue, not a claim of an easy biological solution. Broad historical titles do not automatically outrank precisely formulated mechanistic tasks.
