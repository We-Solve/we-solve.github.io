Math-13740

Gowers Box Norms over Finite Fields

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be an odd prime, let $n\geq1$ be an integer, and put $G={\mathbb{F}}_p^n$. For a finite nonempty set $S$, write ${\mathbb{E}}_{s\in S}F(s)=|S|^{-1}\sum_{s\in S}F(s)$. Let $f:G\times G\to{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfy $|f(x,y)|\leq1$ for every $x,y\in G$.

Using the standard multiplicative-derivative convention, set \[ \Delta_{(h,h)}f(x,y)=f(x+h,y+h)\overline{f(x,y)}\qquad(h\in G). \] For $g:G\times G\to{\mathbb{C}}$, its box norm is defined by \[ \|g\|_{\square}^4= {\mathbb{E}}_{x,x',y,y'\in G} g(x,y)\overline{g(x',y)}\,\overline{g(x,y')}g(x',y'). \] The right-hand side is real and nonnegative. Replacing the displayed derivative by its complex conjugate leaves its box norm unchanged, so the derivative orientation does not change the hypothesis. Suppose, for a positive threshold $\delta$ (necessarily at most $1$ when the hypothesis holds), that \[ {\mathbb{E}}_{h\in G}\|\Delta_{(h,h)}f\|_{\square}^4\geq\delta. \]

Source question. Must $f$ correlate with a function of the displayed form \[ H(x,y)=a(x)b(y)c(x+y)(-1)^{q(x,y)}? \] Here $a,b,c$ are functions of the indicated single $G$-variables; their codomains and magnitude bounds are not stated in the record. If $H$ is a specified complex-valued function, normalized correlation would be measured by \[ \left|{\mathbb{E}}_{x,y\in G}f(x,y)\overline{H(x,y)}\right|. \] The source does not give the required positive lower bound, its dependence on $\delta$, or its uniformity in $n$ and $p$. In particular, writing a dimension-independent lower bound $\eta(\delta)>0$ would be an additional specification that must be checked against the intended theorem.

Most substantially, the source does not define the class or codomain of $q$, or the meaning of the phase $(-1)^{q(x,y)}$. For odd $p$, parity of a representative of an element of ${\mathbb{F}}_p$ is not an intrinsic field-valued operation. Therefore the displayed phase cannot silently be interpreted as an additive character or replaced by one; nor can $q$ silently be required to be quadratic. These unresolved choices prevent a complete precise inverse-theorem statement.

The supplied literature triage dated 2026-08-17 identifies this with Problem 55 of Ben Green's maintained collection, although its local code is catalogue entry. It reports that the inverse theorem is unknown and describes this as the simplest Gowers-type norm with an unknown inverse theorem. It also explicitly flags the same odd-characteristic phase ambiguity in the local import and the cited PDF. Those are dated source assessments, not an independently verified current status.

Short English statement

For a bounded complex function on ${\mathbb{F}}_p^n\times{\mathbb{F}}_p^n$, ask whether a large average box norm of its diagonal multiplicative derivatives forces correlation with a product depending separately on $x$, $y$, and $x+y$, multiplied by the displayed phase $(-1)^{q(x,y)}$. The source leaves $q$, that phase, and the quantitative correlation requirements undefined.
