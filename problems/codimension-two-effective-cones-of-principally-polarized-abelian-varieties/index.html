Math-15744

Codimension-Two Effective Cones of Principally Polarized Abelian Varieties

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $g$ be an integer with $g\geq 3$. The supplied record denotes by $\mathcal A_g$ the moduli space of principally polarized abelian varieties of dimension $g$. It does not specify the base field, whether the moduli stack or a coarse moduli space is intended, or whether a compactification is required.

The question recorded for permanent UnsolvedMath ID $30004621$ is whether, for every such $g$, \[ \operatorname{Eff}_2(\mathcal A_g) \stackrel{?}{=} \operatorname{Cone}\left(S_A,S_F,S_D,S_1,S_2\right). \] Here the right-hand side expresses the proposed generation by the five named classes: under a real-vector-space convention, it would mean \[ \left\{a_A S_A+a_F S_F+a_D S_D+a_1 S_1+a_2 S_2: a_A,a_F,a_D,a_1,a_2\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}\right\}. \] This interpretation is conditional: the record supplies neither definitions of these five classes nor their dependence on $g$, their ambient cycle-class space, the equivalence relation on cycles, or a convention concerning closure of the effective cone.

There is also an unresolved dimensional incompatibility. The original statement and literature assessment call the target a codimension-two effective cone, whereas the clean statement uses $\operatorname{Eff}_2$ and calls the proposed generators surface classes. For the usual moduli space, $\dim\mathcal A_g=g(g+1)/2$; hence surfaces have codimension $g(g+1)/2-2$, which is not two for any integer $g\geq3$. Thus two-dimensional cycles and codimension-two cycles cannot be identified here. The displayed equality preserves the supplied question symbolically; it cannot be made into a uniquely specified mathematical conjecture from this record without resolving this incompatibility and defining the five classes and their ambient space.

Short English statement

For every integer $g\geq3$, the supplied question asks whether five named classes generate the indicated effective cone associated with the moduli space of principally polarized abelian varieties. The record leaves the classes undefined and conflates surfaces with codimension-two cycles, so the intended conjecture requires clarification. Its open status is supplied dated curation.
