Source question. Can one determine in real time whether a given asset's price process exhibits a bubble?

Short English statement

The problem asks whether information available at the present time can reveal whether an asset-price process has a bubble. A precise detection problem requires definitions of a bubble, the observable information, the admissible models, and successful detection; the supplied record leaves these unspecified.
