Linear Systems and Eigenvalue Problems — Problem 23

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $A\in{\mathbb{C}}^{n\times n}$, and let $\mathcal V\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}^n$ be a $d$-dimensional invariant subspace, meaning $A\mathcal V\subseteq\mathcal V$. Let $\widehat{\mathcal V}\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}^n$ also have dimension $d$. For a subspace $\mathcal S$, write $P_{\mathcal S}$ for its orthogonal projector with respect to the standard Hermitian inner product. One precise measure of the approximation of $\mathcal V$ by $\widehat{\mathcal V}$ is \[ \delta=\|P_{\mathcal V}-P_{\widehat{\mathcal V}}\|_2. \] For equal positive dimensions, this is the sine of the largest principal angle. The source requires a small subspace angle but does not specify an angle convention or a numerical threshold; $\delta$ is an explicit convention for this draft.

Let $\mathcal W\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}^n$ have dimension $r$, where $0\le r<n-d$, and set \[ \mathcal Q=\widehat{\mathcal V}+\mathcal W, \qquad m=\dim\mathcal Q. \] Choose $Q\in{\mathbb{C}}^{n\times m}$ with orthonormal columns spanning $\mathcal Q$, so that $Q^*Q=I_m$, and define the compression $B=Q^*AQ$. Here $Q^*$ denotes the conjugate transpose. In general, \[ m=d+r-\dim(\widehat{\mathcal V}\cap\mathcal W), \] so $d\le m\le d+r<n$. The source describes $m=d+r$ as the generic case; it does not explicitly assume a trivial intersection.

Let $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_d$ be the eigenvalues of the restriction $A|_{\mathcal V}$, and let $\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_m$ be the eigenvalues of $B$, in both cases counted with algebraic multiplicity. To make eigenvalue approximation quantitative, one possible error measure is \[ E=\min_{\substack{\iota:\{1,\ldots,d\}\to\{1,\ldots,m\}\\ \iota\ \mathrm{injective}}} \max_{1\le j\le d}|\lambda_j-\mu_{\iota(j)}|. \] For $d=0$, take $E=0$; the substantive question concerns $d>0$.

Problem. Provide explicit sufficient conditions ensuring that $d$ eigenvalues of $Q^*AQ$ approximate the eigenvalues of $A|_{\mathcal V}$. A quantitative formulation is to give conditions, involving the subspace approximation error and any other stated data, that ensure $E\le\tau$ for a prescribed tolerance $\tau>0$. Additional assumptions on the eigenvalues of $A$ associated with $\mathcal V$ may be imposed as needed, as expressly permitted by the source.

The source supplies no particular tolerance, eigenvalue matching rule, convergence regime, or required uniformity in $\mathcal W$. The error measure above specifies a possible quantitative interpretation, rather than a uniquely prescribed source formulation. No normality, diagonalizability, simplicity, or spectral separation hypothesis is supplied; any such hypothesis used in sufficient conditions must be stated explicitly.

Short English statement

Given a subspace close to an invariant subspace of a complex matrix, enlarge it by another subspace and compress the matrix to the resulting space. Find sufficient conditions guaranteeing that the compression has $d$ eigenvalues close to those belonging to the original invariant subspace. The source permits additional assumptions on the target eigenvalues but leaves the meaning and accuracy of approximation unspecified.
