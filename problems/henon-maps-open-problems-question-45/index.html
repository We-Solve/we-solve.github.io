Henon Maps: Open Problems — Question 45

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\operatorname{Aut}({\mathbb{C}}^3)$ denote the group of polynomial maps $f\colon{\mathbb{C}}^3\to{\mathbb{C}}^3$ having polynomial inverses. For such a map, the standard first dynamical degree is \[ \lambda_1(f)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\deg(f^n)\right)^{1/n}, \] where $\deg$ is total polynomial degree. The supplied excerpt uses $\lambda(f)$ without defining it; the formulation below interprets it as $\lambda_1(f)$, subject to confirmation from the source context.

A projective compactification of ${\mathbb{C}}^3$ is a projective variety $X$ together with a dense open embedding $j\colon{\mathbb{C}}^3\hookrightarrow X$. Write $D=X\setminus j({\mathbb{C}}^3)$ for its boundary. The map $j\circ f\circ j^{-1}$ determines an induced birational self-map $\bar f\colon X\dashrightarrow X$.

Source question, with unresolved local terminology. For every $f\in\operatorname{Aut}({\mathbb{C}}^3)$ satisfying \[ \lambda_1(f)^2>\lambda_1(f^{-1}), \] prove that there exist a projective compactification $X$ of ${\mathbb{C}}^3$ and a point $p$ on its divisor at infinity such that $\bar f$ admits a super-attracting fixed point at $p$. In particular, the fixed-point assertion requires $\bar f$ to be defined and holomorphic on a neighborhood of $p$, with $\bar f(p)=p$.

The supplied excerpt does not specify regularity assumptions on $X$, the precise divisor structure of its boundary, or the meaning of "super-attracting." At a smooth point $p$, two possible local conditions are \[ \mathrm d\bar f_p=0 \qquad\text{and}\qquad \mathrm d\bar f_p\text{ is nilpotent}. \] These conditions are not equivalent in dimension three. The draft does not select between them or impose smoothness or normality on $X$; the intended convention and admissible compactifications require source-context verification.

Supplied motivation. The excerpt reports that, under the same dynamical-degree inequality, an invariant valuation on ${\mathbb{C}}[x_1,x_2,x_3]$ is known to exist by the reference labeled $[\mathrm{DF20}]$. It informally describes such a valuation as selecting an irreducible subvariety in every algebraic compactification of ${\mathbb{C}}^3$, compatibly across compactifications. The excerpt supplies neither the precise invariance convention nor the compatibility statement. It also says that extending an earlier construction to a larger class of polynomial automorphisms of ${\mathbb{C}}^3$ is unclear; that earlier construction is not included in the record.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every polynomial automorphism of ${\mathbb{C}}^3$ satisfying $\lambda(f)^2>\lambda(f^{-1})$ has a projective compactification on which its induced birational map has a super-attracting fixed point at infinity. The dynamical-degree notation, admissible compactifications, and exact local meaning of super-attraction require confirmation. The registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
