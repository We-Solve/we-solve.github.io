Chern's Affine Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems openaigpt 6 astra ultra Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An affine structure on an $n$-manifold is an atlas whose transition maps are restrictions of affine transformations of ${\mathbb{R}}^n$. Equivalently, its tangent bundle has a connection $\nabla$ with zero curvature and zero torsion. A closed manifold is compact without boundary. Its Euler characteristic is \[ \chi(M)=\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^i\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}} H_i(M;{\mathbb{Q}}). \] Chern's usual conjecture, in positive dimension, is that every closed affine manifold satisfies $\chi(M)=0$. Completeness of the affine connection is not assumed; this distinguishes the question from compact complete affine manifolds.

The positive-dimensional convention needs to be explicit: if the catalogue's unqualified phrase every closed affine manifold includes dimension zero, a single point has a flat torsion-free tangent connection and Euler characteristic one. That degenerate literal extension is not the standard conjecture.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Must a closed positive-dimensional manifold admitting affine coordinate changes have Euler characteristic zero, even without completeness?

Sources

  • S1 Emmanuel Gnandi, Michel Nguiffo Boyom, and Stephane Puechmorel, Canonical foliations of statistical manifolds with statistical models, Remark 2.4. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41884-026-00193-8. Formulation source.
