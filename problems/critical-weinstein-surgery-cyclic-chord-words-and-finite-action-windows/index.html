Math-1534

Critical Weinstein surgery, cyclic chord words, and finite action windows

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $W^{2n}$ be a Stein/Weinstein domain, $n\ge2$, with contact boundary $(Y^{2n-1},\ker\alpha)$ and a strictly plurisubharmonic Morse function giving a handle decomposition. Handles of index less than $n$ are subcritical. A critical index-$n$ handle is attached along a Legendrian sphere $L^{n-1}\subset Y$; denote the new boundary by $Y_L$. A Reeb chord of $L$ is a positive-time trajectory of $R_\alpha$ with both endpoints on $L$, with action $\mathcal A(c)=\int_c\alpha>0$.

Question 1.10. Give a surgery formula computing the change in contact homology under this critical attachment from the appropriate relative/Legendrian contact-homology data of $L$. The source motivates this by new closed Reeb trajectories formed from concatenated chords of $L$. It attributes the comparatively understood subcritical case to Mei-Lin Yau and Frédéric Bourgeois; its description as homology with sphere-contact-homology coefficients is heuristic here, with no complete formula/hypotheses. The suggested Lefschetz-fibration analogy is not a theorem.

Supplied modern scope report. The aid distinguishes a Bourgeois--Ekholm--Eliashberg cyclic-word exact triangle for filling-linearized contact homology, decorated Hochschild-type descriptions for reduced/full symplectic homology, and wrapped-Floer/cocore and categorical surgery statements under their own hypotheses. These are different invariants and formulas. The record supplies neither an exact sequence with all maps nor the analytic, augmentation, coefficient and grading hypotheses needed for a single universal theorem. In particular, the chord-word new-generator sector alone is not the final homology: its differential and its coupling to the old sector are essential.

Supplied conditional action-window lemma. Assume a quantitative thin-handle correspondence assigns a new orbit generator $\gamma_w$ to a cyclic composable chord word $w=c_1\cdots c_\ell$, $\ell\ge1$, and that \[ \left|\mathcal A(\gamma_w)-\sum_{i=1}^\ell\mathcal A(c_i)\right| \le\ell\delta, \qquad \mathcal A(c_i)\ge a_0>\delta\ge0. \] Then every such new orbit with $\mathcal A(\gamma_w)<T$ obeys the bounds asserted by the aid, \[ \ell<\frac{T}{a_0-\delta},\qquad \mathcal A(c_i)<\frac{Ta_0}{a_0-\delta}\quad\text{for every }i. \] Finite action-window enumeration additionally needs local finiteness of the relevant chord set, as in a suitable chord-generic model. These quantitative assumptions are not derived solely from existence of the Weinstein handle.

For the standard one-chord Legendrian unknot $S^{n-1}$, with chord action $A>0$ and chord degree $n-1$, the aid states a parity-sensitive count of good cyclic generators. Over characteristic-zero coefficients, with the usual cyclic signs and a nonresonant cutoff $T/A\notin{\mathbb{Z}}$, put $N=\lfloor T/A\rfloor$. In a sufficiently thin handle where the stated correspondence stabilizes below this cutoff, the count is \[ \begin{cases} N,&n\text{ odd},\\ \lceil N/2\rceil,&n\text{ even}. \end{cases} \] For even $n$ only odd powers survive the cyclic sign test. These are generator counts in the stated model, not contact or symplectic homology ranks. At resonant cutoffs $T=jA$, the strict action window and handle-action errors require a separate convention; the floor formula is not asserted there without further information. Characteristic two also changes the sign cancellation and is outside this count.

Short English statement

The source asks for a critical-handle surgery formula using Legendrian chord data. The aid separates several modern invariant-specific formulas and proves a conditional action bound making chord-word enumeration finite. Its one-chord parity count concerns generators only; a surgery homology formula still needs the differential, old-to-new coupling and precise analytic choices.
