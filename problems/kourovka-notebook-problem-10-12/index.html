Kourovka Notebook Problem 10.12

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A semigroup $S$ has cancellation if, for all $x,y,z\in S$, \[ xy=xz\Longrightarrow y=z,\qquad yx=zx\Longrightarrow y=z. \] Let $A\subseteq S$ be a finite semigroup generating set. Write $S^1$ for $S$ with an identity adjoined if necessary, and define \[ B_{S,A}(n)=\{a_1\cdots a_k:0\leq k\leq n,\ a_i\in A\}\subseteq S^1, \qquad b_{S,A}(n)=|B_{S,A}(n)|, \] where the empty product is the identity. Interpret the source's term "non-exponential growth" in the standard subexponential sense: \[ \forall c>1\ \exists n_0\in{\mathbb{N}}\ \forall n\geq n_0, \qquad b_{S,A}(n)<c^n. \] The source does not explicitly define this growth terminology.

A group of left quotients of $S$, denoted $G=S^{-1}S$, means a group $G$ together with an injective semigroup homomorphism $\iota:S\to G$ such that \[ G=\{\iota(s)^{-1}\iota(t):s,t\in S\}. \] Put $X=\iota(A)\cup\iota(A)^{-1}$. This is a finite group generating set for $G$. Define \[ B_{G,X}(n)=\{x_1\cdots x_k:0\leq k\leq n,\ x_i\in X\}, \qquad b_{G,X}(n)=|B_{G,X}(n)|. \] Exponential growth means \[ \exists c>1\ \exists n_0\in{\mathbb{N}}\ \forall n\geq n_0, \qquad b_{G,X}(n)\geq c^n. \] These growth properties are independent of the respective finite generating sets.

Question. Does there exist a finitely generated cancellative semigroup $S$ of subexponential growth whose group of left quotients $G=S^{-1}S$ has exponential growth?

The source explicitly states that the group of left quotients exists under its hypotheses; the question above retains this requirement and does not ask whether such a group exists for an arbitrary cancellative semigroup. The source also states that an affirmative answer would give a positive solution to Problem 12 in S. Wagon's The Banach--Tarski Paradox (1985). The text of that problem is not supplied, so this consequence is retained as a source attribution without further formulation or verification.

Short English statement

R. I. Grigorchuk asks whether a finitely generated semigroup with both cancellation laws can have subexponential growth while its group of left quotients has exponential growth. The source attributes to an affirmative answer a positive solution of Problem 12 in Wagon's 1985 book; that auxiliary problem is not supplied.
