Pingree open problems — Thouvenot problem

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(\Sigma,\sigma)$ be a mixing shift of finite type: $\Sigma$ is the space of bi-infinite sequences allowed by a finite transition matrix, and the shift is topologically mixing. A symbolic factor onto $(M,T)$ is a continuous surjection $\pi:\Sigma\to M$ satisfying $\pi\circ\sigma=T\circ\pi$. The supplied "good symbolic cover" further requires injectivity on a full-measure set for every invariant probability measure; the space carrying these measures and whether a common full-measure set is intended are not specified.

In the usual invertible positive-roof setting, a roof $f:M\to(0,\infty)$ defines the suspension \[ X=(M\times\mathbb R)/\left((x,s+f(x))\sim(Tx,s)\right),\qquad T_t[x,s]=[x,s+t]. \] The source assumes $f\in C^1(M)$, or smoother. It calls this a flow for $t\in\mathbb R$, but does not explicitly require a positive roof, invertibility of $T$, compactness, or another condition guaranteeing the construction and entropy convention being used.

Let $h_{\mathrm{top}}(X,T_1)$ denote the intended topological entropy of the time-one map. Is every ergodic measure-preserving system $(Y,\mathcal B,\mu,S)$ with \[ h_\mu(S)<h_{\mathrm{top}}(X,T_1) \] realized by a $T_1$-invariant probability measure on $X$? Precisely, seek a Borel probability measure $\nu$ invariant under $T_1$ and a measure-theoretic isomorphism between $(Y,\mathcal B,\mu,S)$ and $(X,\nu,T_1)$ modulo null sets. Such a realization is automatically ergodic. Invariance under the entire suspension flow is not requested. For isomorphism to a flow time map, the target class should consist of invertible systems on standard probability spaces; whether atomic systems are included is unstated. No extra nonarithmetic or mixing condition on the roof is supplied.

Short English statement

Does the time-one map of a smooth suspension with a good mixing symbolic cover realize every ergodic system of smaller entropy as an invariant measure? The precise roof, symbolic-cover, and target-system hypotheses need clarification.
