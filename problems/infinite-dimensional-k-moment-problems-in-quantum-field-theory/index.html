Infinite-Dimensional K-Moment Problems in Quantum Field Theory

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A moment functional assigns values to polynomial observables on an infinite-dimensional configuration space. For a specified subset $K$, a representing measure is a positive measure $\mu$ supported in $K$ such that $L(p)=\int_K p\,d\mu$ for the allowed polynomials $p$. Existence asks whether such a measure exists; determinacy asks whether it is unique; support criteria determine which moment or positivity conditions force concentration on $K$. Topology, cylinder-polynomial class and measure notion matter in infinite dimensions.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Develop existence, determinacy, and support criteria for representing measures in infinite-dimensional $K$-moment problems arising in quantum field theory and stochastic analysis.
Source scope and qualification.
This is a general programme. No fixed configuration space or subset $K$ is given in the extracted sentence, so neither a universal finite-dimensional criterion nor a guessed topology is substituted for the intended family of moment problems.

Short English statement

Develop criteria for existence, uniqueness and support of infinite-dimensional moment measures relevant to fields and stochastic processes.
