Math-4569

Sharp Singularity Asymptotics for Sparse Bernoulli Matrices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $n\geq 2$, let $p_n\in[0,1]$, and let $M_n=(M_{ij})_{1\leq i,j\leq n}$ have mutually independent entries with \[ \mathbb P(M_{ij}=1)=p_n,\qquad \mathbb P(M_{ij}=0)=1-p_n. \] Write $R_i$ and $C_j$ for its rows and columns, and define \[ \begin{aligned} E_{\mathrm{zero},n} &=\{\exists i:R_i=0\}\cup\{\exists j:C_j=0\},\\ E_{\mathrm{equal},n} &=\{\exists i<j:R_i=R_j\}\cup\{\exists i<j:C_i=C_j\}. \end{aligned} \] Under the usual characteristic-zero interpretation, define the singularity event by \[ S_n=\{\det M_n=0\}, \] where the determinant is evaluated over ${\mathbb{R}}$, equivalently over ${\mathbb{Q}}$. The source does not explicitly specify the field, so this interpretation requires confirmation; singularity over a finite field would be a different question.

The original supplied formulation specifies the sparse regime $p_n\to0$. The conjectural target is to determine the range of such sequences for which \[ \mathbb P(S_n) =(1+o_n(1))\mathbb P\left(E_{\mathrm{zero},n}\cup E_{\mathrm{equal},n}\right) \qquad(n\to\infty). \] Here $o_n(1)$ denotes a quantity tending to zero as $n\to\infty$; no uniformity over parameter sequences is specified.

The supplied corrected statement additionally asks whether, when $\limsup_{n\to\infty}p_n<\tfrac12$, \[ \mathbb P(S_n)=2n(1+o_n(1))(1-p_n)^n. \] Unresolved qualification. The condition $\limsup p_n<\tfrac12$, even together with $p_n\to0$, does not supply a sufficient parameter range for this second formula as written. For example, it allows $p_n=n^{-2}$, for which $2n(1-p_n)^n\to\infty$, whereas a probability is at most one. An additional restriction is therefore missing; none is imposed here. It is also unclear whether this second question is intended only within $p_n\to0$ or in a wider range satisfying the displayed upper restriction.

The original source summary reports a bound \[ \mathbb P(S_n)\leq\exp(-c n p_n) \qquad\text{for }p_n\geq C\frac{\log n}{n} \] in its sparse-regime discussion, and asks for improvement to the conjectured exponent. The constants $c,C>0$, their quantifiers, any further parameter restrictions, and the precise meaning of that exponent improvement are not supplied. This bound is retained as reported background, not asserted here as an independently verified theorem.

Short English statement

For independent Bernoulli matrices in the sparse regime $p_n\to0$, ask when singularity is asymptotically accounted for by a zero row or column, or by two equal rows or columns. The additional proposed asymptotic $2n(1-p_n)^n$ needs a missing restriction on $p_n$. The field and exact parameter ranges require review.
