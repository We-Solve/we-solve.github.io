Math-4850

Lipschitz Equivalence of Dust-Like Self-Similar Sets

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d,m$ be positive integers and let $\rho=(\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_m)\in(0,1)^m$ satisfy \[ \sum_{j=1}^{m}\rho_j^d<1. \] A self-similar realization with contraction vector $\rho$ is a nonempty compact set $E\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$ satisfying \[ E=\bigcup_{j=1}^{m}S_j(E),\qquad \|S_j(x)-S_j(y)\|=\rho_j\|x-y\|\quad(x,y\in{\mathbb{R}}^d), \] where the $S_j$ are similarities. We use the usual strong separation interpretation of dust-like: $S_i(E)\cap S_j(E)=\varnothing$ whenever $i\ne j$. This interpretation is a drafting convention because the supplied excerpt does not explicitly define "dust-like." Write $E=D(\rho)$ for a chosen such realization; the notation does not specify its defining similarities.

Let $\tau=(\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_n)\in(0,1)^n$ be another finite contraction vector and let $F=D(\tau)$ be an associated dust-like self-similar realization. Equip both sets with their induced Euclidean metrics, denoted by $d_E$ and $d_F$. The supplied statement does not explicitly specify the ambient dimension of $F$, or whether an inequality analogous to $\sum_j\rho_j^d<1$ is imposed on $\tau$; these admissibility details remain unresolved.

The sets $E$ and $F$ are Lipschitz equivalent if there exist a bijection $f:E\to F$ and a constant $L\geq1$ such that \[ L^{-1}d_E(x,y)\leq d_F(f(x),f(y))\leq Ld_E(x,y) \qquad\text{for all }x,y\in E. \] Problem. Characterize nontrivial necessary and sufficient conditions on the contraction vectors $\rho$ and $\tau$ for their associated dust-like self-similar sets $D(\rho)$ and $D(\tau)$ to be Lipschitz equivalent. The wording "any" in the supplied statement suggests that the criterion should apply to arbitrary admissible realizations. The excerpt does not explicitly settle whether this means a criterion uniform over all choices of defining similarities or a question about particular chosen realizations. This quantifier qualification must be resolved before treating the formulation as definitive. The requested word "nontrivial" is also not given a formal meaning.

Short English statement

Given two contraction vectors and associated dust-like self-similar sets, seek substantive conditions on the vectors that are equivalent to the existence of a bi-Lipschitz bijection between the sets. The precise admissibility hypotheses for the second vector, the meaning of dust-like, and the quantification over realizations require confirmation from the source.
