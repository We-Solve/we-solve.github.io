Math-3668

Determinant Bounds in Terms of Knot-Crossing Number

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret a knot or link in the conventional setting as a nonempty finite disjoint union of tamely embedded circles in the three-sphere, considered up to ambient isotopy. This ambient space and regularity convention are not explicit in the supplied statement and require confirmation. Let $c(K)$ denote the crossing number, the minimum number of crossings among regular diagrams of $K$. Interpret $\det(K)$ as the usual link determinant $\lvert\Delta_K(-1)\rvert$, where $\Delta_K(t)$ is the one-variable Alexander polynomial with its usual ambiguity by multiplication by $\pm t^m$. Interpret $\log$ as the natural logarithm and $v_8$ as the volume of a regular ideal octahedron in hyperbolic three-space of sectional curvature $-1$. These are conventional interpretations of notation left undefined in the record.

The supplied question asks whether, for every knot or link $K$, \[ \frac{2\pi\log\det(K)}{c(K)}\leq v_8. \] As a real-valued inequality, this expression requires $c(K)>0$ and $\det(K)>0$. Thus its precisely defined portion asks: \[ \text{For every knot or link }K\text{ with }c(K)>0\text{ and }\det(K)>0, \qquad \frac{2\pi\log\det(K)}{c(K)}\leq v_8\;? \] These restrictions are necessary to evaluate the displayed expression, but they are absent from the source's universal quantifier. The unknot has $c(K)=0$, and links can have zero determinant, including split links. The source supplies neither exclusions nor conventions for these cases. Consequently, the restricted question above does not resolve the intended meaning of the full universal statement.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the logarithm of the determinant of a knot or link, scaled by $2\pi$ and divided by its minimum crossing number, is at most the volume $v_8$ of a regular ideal hyperbolic octahedron. The quotient is defined over the reals only for positive crossing number and positive determinant; the intended treatment of the remaining cases is unspecified.
