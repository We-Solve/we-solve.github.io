Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 38

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $I=\{a,b,c,d\}$. The supplied question considers four local $C^r$ diffeomorphisms $f_i$ of ${\mathbb{R}}$, each defined on a neighborhood of $[-1,1]$, such that \[ f_i(0)=0\qquad(i\in I). \] For a map $f$, define \[ \operatorname{Fix}(f)=\{x\in\operatorname{dom}(f):f(x)=x\}. \] The regularity parameter $r$ is not specified in the supplied record. Write \[ h=[f_a,f_b]\circ[f_c,f_d]. \] Here brackets denote commutators, but the source does not specify a convention. For example, one convention is $[f,g]=f\circ g\circ f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}$. Local inverses and these compositions are defined sufficiently near the common fixed point; an assertion on a larger domain requires additional domain specifications.

The source asks whether there exist local $C^r$ diffeomorphisms $\tilde f_i$, $C^r$-close to the corresponding $f_i$, that coincide with them outside $[-1,1]$, and satisfy the following two displayed conditions, transcribed without repair: \[ h=[\tilde f_a\tilde f_b][\tilde f_c\tilde f_d], \] \[ \operatorname{Fix}(f_a)\cap\operatorname{Fix}(f_b)\cap \operatorname{Fix}(f_c)\cap\operatorname{Fix}(f_d)\cap(-1,1)=\varnothing. \] These conditions do not specify a coherent perturbation problem as written. Each bracket in the first condition lacks a separator between two commutator arguments. The second condition refers to the original maps, whose fixed-point sets all contain $0\in(-1,1)$.

A possible intended repair is preservation of the product of commutators together with elimination of common fixed points of the perturbed maps: \[ [\tilde f_a,\tilde f_b]\circ[\tilde f_c,\tilde f_d]=h, \qquad \bigcap_{i\in I}\operatorname{Fix}(\tilde f_i)\cap(-1,1)=\varnothing. \] This repair is an unverified interpretation, not an established transcription of the question. A precise formulation also requires the allowed values of $r$, the topology and quantifier intended by $C^r$-closeness, the domains on which the maps must agree outside $[-1,1]$, and the domain on which the commutator identity must hold. In particular, equality of germs at $0$ and equality on a specified neighborhood are distinct requirements.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether four local diffeomorphisms of ${\mathbb{R}}$ with a common fixed point can be perturbed inside $[-1,1]$, preserving their product of commutators and removing common fixed points. This interpretation requires unverified corrections to both displayed target conditions. The literal fixed-point condition concerns the original maps and conflicts with their assumed common fixed point. Regularity, closeness, and composition domains also require clarification.
