A positive-density obstruction for the prop-agator limit

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Problem 33 (Lionel Levine). What is the infinite-volume limit of "Andrea's prop-agator model [12]" at finite propagator density? The extract does not define the model, reference [12], initial state, active-set law, boundary condition, update schedule or limit topology.

Reported active-set absorbing reconstruction. The aid interprets a local propagator as an idempotent $\Pi_x=a_x^\dagger a_x$, using sandpile addition and its designated dual operation, and runs these operators on a prescribed active set $I$ to a common absorbing state. Their exact definitions are not printed, so this reconstruction is a premise rather than a definition recovered from the original question.

On a wired finite box $\Lambda\subset{\mathbb{Z}}^d$, $d\geq1$, start from the maximal stable BTW height $\eta_{\max}(x)=2d-1$ in the usual threshold-$2d$ convention. Let $\eta$ be the final stable state and \[ D=\{x\in\Lambda:\eta(x)<2d-1\} \] its defect sites. The aid proves the covering property \[ I\subseteq\bigcup_{x\in D}\{x,x\pm e_1,\ldots,x\pm e_d\}, \] so \[ |D|\geq\frac{|I|}{2d+1}. \] It also reports net wired-boundary grain loss at least $|I|/(2d+1)$; in this maximum-height convention the final height deficit $\sum_x(2d-1-\eta(x))$ is at least $|D|$. The exact accounting of addition and dual operations must be matched to the reconstructed dynamics.

For a joint translation-invariant local weak limit of the active set and state, with active density $p=\Pr(0\in I)$, the reported consequence is \[ \Pr(0\in D)\geq\frac p{2d+1}. \] This uses stationarity or a suitable uniform-root/bulk-limit interpretation; a global finite-box density alone does not specify the state at a fixed nonstationary origin.

Reported infinite-volume obstruction. If a stationary active set has positive density, the aid rules out a translation-covariant conservative infinite-volume realization of this pair-stable absorbing reconstruction with a finite odometer at every site. The odometer is the relevant site's firing count in the chosen dynamics; the exact dual-firing convention is absent. The report emphasizes that the random-walk telescoping argument does not assume odometer integrability, only sitewise finiteness.

This obstruction does not deny finite-volume absorbing states or all local weak subsequential limits. It excludes one combination of conservation, stationarity/covariance, pair stability and finite firing, and does not identify a unique limiting distribution. Other initial states or interpretations of the original propagator model remain outside the supplied result.

Short English statement

The original asks for a propagator model's infinite-volume law but leaves its rules unspecified. Under the aid's explicit active-set reconstruction, positive active density forces a positive defect density and boundary loss. A stationary conservative realization with finitely many firings per site is ruled out without requiring an integrable odometer; a complete limit law is not supplied.
