Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.54

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

All groups in this statement are finite. A formation is a nonempty class $\mathfrak F$ of groups, closed under isomorphisms, homomorphic images, and finite subdirect products. It is hereditary if every subgroup of every member of $\mathfrak F$ belongs to $\mathfrak F$. Thus non-heredity means \[ \exists A\in\mathfrak F\;\exists B\leq A:\quad B\notin\mathfrak F. \] For an explicit local-formation convention, let $f$ assign to each prime $p$ a formation or the empty class. Define $\operatorname{LF}(f)$ to consist of those groups $G$ for which \[ G/C_G(H/K)\in f(p) \] for every chief factor $H/K$ of $G$ and every prime $p\mid |H/K|$. Here a chief factor has $K,H\trianglelefteq G$, $K<H$, with no normal subgroup of $G$ strictly between them, and \[ C_G(H/K)=\{g\in G: ghg^{-1}h^{-1}\in K\text{ for every }h\in H\}. \] A formation is local if it equals $\operatorname{LF}(f)$ for some such $f$.

For $L\leq M$, put \[ \operatorname{Core}_M(L)=\bigcap_{m\in M}mLm^{-1}. \] Under the chain convention used for this draft, $H\leq G$ is $\mathfrak F$-subnormal if $H=G$, or if there is a chain \[ H=H_0<H_1<\cdots<H_n=G \] such that \[ H_i/\operatorname{Core}_{H_i}(H_{i-1})\in\mathfrak F \qquad(1\leq i\leq n). \] The supplied record does not define this terminology. In particular, it does not say whether each inclusion in the chain must be maximal. The formulation below uses the displayed convention; agreement with the intended source convention requires review, and a maximal-inclusion requirement must not be silently treated as equivalent for a non-hereditary formation.

Write \[ \mathcal S_{\mathfrak F}(G)=\{H\leq G:H\text{ is }\mathfrak F\text{-subnormal in }G\}. \] The subgroup lattice has meet $U\cap V$ and join $\langle U,V\rangle$, the subgroup generated by $U\cup V$. Subject to the terminology qualification above, the question is whether there exists a local formation $\mathfrak F$ such that \[ \begin{gathered} \exists A\in\mathfrak F\;\exists B\leq A:\ B\notin\mathfrak F,\\ \text{and for every finite group }G\text{ and all }U,V\in\mathcal S_{\mathfrak F}(G),\\ U\cap V\in\mathcal S_{\mathfrak F}(G) \quad\text{and}\quad \langle U,V\rangle\in\mathcal S_{\mathfrak F}(G). \end{gathered} \] No solubility restriction on the ambient groups is supplied. The record additionally states that a negative answer would solve Kourovka Problem 9.75, because all hereditary local formations with this lattice property are already known. This is supplied contextual information, not an independently verified classification or implication; the statement of Problem 9.75 and the classification references are not supplied.

Short English statement

Does there exist a local formation of finite groups that is not closed under taking subgroups, but whose $\mathfrak F$-subnormal subgroups are closed under intersection and generated join in every finite group? The precise convention for $\mathfrak F$-subnormality requires source review. The supplied record labels the problem open.
