Lattice Gases and Antiferromagnetic Potts Models — Conjecture 3 (Hamidoune \protect[Hamidoune_90], Stanley \protect[Stanley_98])

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not define $Z(w)$ or specify the class of graphs beyond claw-freeness. The following precise formulation is therefore conditional on interpreting $Z$ as the univariate independence polynomial of a finite simple undirected graph.

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite simple undirected graph. A set $I\subseteq V$ is independent if no two distinct vertices of $I$ are adjacent. Define \[ Z_G(w)=\sum_{\substack{I\subseteq V\\ I\text{ independent}}}w^{|I|},\qquad w\in{\mathbb{C}}. \] The empty independent set contributes $1$. The graph $G$ is claw-free if it has no induced subgraph isomorphic to $K_{1,3}$, the graph consisting of one central vertex adjacent to three pairwise nonadjacent vertices.

Under the interpretation above, the statement attributed in the supplied record to Hamidoune and Stanley, and listed as Conjecture 3 by Sokal (2000), is \[ \forall\,G\text{ finite, simple, undirected and claw-free},\qquad \forall\,w\in{\mathbb{C}},\quad Z_G(w)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ w\in{\mathbb{R}}\text{ and }w<0. \] If $V=\varnothing$, then $Z_G=1$, so the assertion about zeros is vacuous. Identification of the source's $Z(w)$ with this polynomial, including the graph hypotheses, requires confirmation. The registry labels the problem open; this is supplied metadata, not an independently verified mathematical status assessment.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly conditional interpretation of $Z(w)$ as the independence polynomial of a finite simple undirected graph, every complex zero must be strictly negative and real whenever the graph has no induced claw $K_{1,3}$. The supplied record leaves the definition of $Z$ and the graph domain unstated.
