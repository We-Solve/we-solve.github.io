Scalar Curvature Question [?35]: Bound on the Filling Radius forSc ≥σ > 0

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.
Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Let $X$ be a complete Riemannian $n$-manifold with scalar curvature bounded below by a positive constant. Does there exist a constant $C_n$, depending only on dimension, such that its filling radius satisfies \[\operatorname{FillRad}(X)\leq C_n\left(\inf_{x\in X}\operatorname{Sc}(x)\right)^{-2}?\] The filling radius measures the neighborhood size needed to fill the fundamental cycle after the metric-space embedding used in its definition. The catalogue transcription gives exponent $-2$; the cited original is needed to determine whether this exponent is correct.

Scalar Curvature Question [?35]: Bound on the Filling Radius forSc $\geq$$\sigma$ > 0

OpenAI's claim

Pointwise scalar curvature and width: The paper claims dimension-only control of the full fibers of a continuous map from any complete scalar-positive $n$-manifold to an $(n-2)$-complex, for $n\ge4$. It also gives the stated macroscopic-dimension consequence on universal covering spaces when the closed base admits a positive-scalar-curvature metric.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For every $n\ge4$ there is $C_n<\infty$ such that a connected complete smooth boundaryless $n$-manifold with $\operatorname{Scal}_g\ge1$ has a continuous map $f:M\to K$, where $K$ is a simplicial complex with the usual polyhedral topology and $\dim K\le n-2$, with \[\sup_{y\in K}\operatorname{diam}_g f^{-1}(y)\le C_n.\] Entire fibers, rather than their individual components, are bounded. For smooth Riemannian $(M,g)$ of dimension $n\ge4$, assume $M$ is closed and connected and $\operatorname{Scal}\ge\sigma^2>0$, the corollary gives $\operatorname{FillRad}(M,g)\le C_n/(2\sigma)$. A separate stated corollary covers all closed positive-scalar-curvature dimensions $n\ge2$: the universal-cover geometry obtained by lifting that metric has continuous macroscopic dimension at most $n-2$. The filling radius uses the integral fundamental class when the manifold is orientable and its mod-two fundamental class otherwise.

Outline of the argument

Pointwise scalar curvature and width: A separated net supplies labeled inner and outer balls in the original metric. Compatible walls and inserted cylinders are constructed while a nearby collapse map keeps track of those original positions. The final walls have no intersection of $n-1$ different labels, so their sign coordinates define an $(n-2)$-dimensional map; shared coordinates place the entire fiber in one uniformly bounded original ball.
