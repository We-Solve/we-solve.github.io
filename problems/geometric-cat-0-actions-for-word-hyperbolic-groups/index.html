Geometric CAT$0$ actions for word-hyperbolic groups

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a counterexample in the selected manuscript. A hyperbolic group with a finite classifying complex is claimed to admit no proper and cocompact isometric action with proper complete CAT$0$ target.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
The source constructs a finite, connected, aspherical simplicial complex $K$ of dimension two. In its universal cover, a length-$\ell$ combinatorial loop admits a triangular disk diagram with at most $C\ell$ faces, for one finite constant $C$. Nevertheless, \[ \pi_1K\text{ has no proper cocompact isometric action on a proper complete CAT}(0)\text{ space}. \] The linear filling estimate makes $\pi_1K$ word-hyperbolic. Within the homotopy class of $K$, no simplicial complex with finitely many simplices admits a geodesic length metric compatible with its topology and locally CAT$0$; the claim also excludes such a locally CAT$-1$ metric.

Outline of the argument

Nested incidence tables produce finite relations with a linear disk-filling bound. Conditional sampling creates a limiting configuration of controlled energy, and its geometric obstruction contradicts any proposed CAT$0$ action; the finite aspherical model preserves the topological finiteness claim.

Formal verification

For family 257, the documented construction gives a finite aspherical hyperbolic group model without a geometric proper-complete CAT$0$ action. The stronger dimension-two and all locally CAT$0$ model claims are excluded. The listed Comparator files are HyperbolicObstruction.lean. Named declarations include OAI.HyperbolicObstruction.main.
