Math-15851

Unconditioned Component Statistics in Branching Processes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source asks to determine the size and structure statistics of connected components when the root's age is not conditioned on. The supplied assessment places this in a branching-process setting, but the excerpt does not give the stochastic process, offspring/reproduction law, component definition, observation time, or root-sampling rule. In particular, it does not justify substituting an ordinary Galton--Watson tree.

Let $C$ denote the rooted connected component in the intended model, let $A\geq0$ denote the age of the selected root, and let $\nu$ be the distribution of $A$ under that same selection rule. If $K(a,\cdot)$ is a regular conditional law of the rooted component given $A=a$, the requested unconditioned law is \[ \mathbb P(C\in B)=\int K(a,B)\,\nu(da) \] for each measurable component event $B$. Its size marginal, with $N=|V(C)|\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\cup\{\infty\}$ when that convention applies, is \[ \mathbb P(N=m)=\int\mathbb P(N=m\mid A=a)\,\nu(da). \] The structural statistics concern the rooted component law itself, not only its size; possible age marks, orientation, temporal information and equivalence up to rooted isomorphism must be specified by the model.

These identities describe unconditioning but do not determine the statistics: both the conditional kernel and the correct root-age distribution must come from the missing process. Sampling an initial ancestor, a uniformly chosen observed individual, or a component followed by a root can produce different age and size biases. No uniform age distribution, tree structure, stationary regime or large-population limit is added. The source does not say whether a finite-time exact law, limiting distribution, moments or asymptotic tails are the intended outputs.

The supplied literature triage checked 21 August 2026 says the report labels this unconditioned problem open and that the located literature does not establish a complete resolution. It cites only the original report, so that dated statement is not an independently verified current status or a specification of the omitted model.

Short English statement

Find the rooted-component size and shape distributions without fixing the root's age. The unconditional law must average the age-conditioned component law against the correct root-age distribution. The process and sampling rule are missing, so this identity alone does not yield the requested statistics.
