Math-4726

Compression, Amenability, and Uniform Embeddability of Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record contains four questions. The following gives a precise conventional interpretation while retaining the unresolved terminology and domain qualifications.

For a finitely generated group $G$ with finite symmetric generating set $S$, let $d_S(g,h)=|g^{-1}h|_S$ be its word metric. For a metric group $(G,d)$, define its non-equivariant Hilbert-space compression exponent by \[ \alpha_2(G,d)=\sup\left\{\alpha\in[0,1]:\begin{array}{l} \text{there exist a real Hilbert space }H,\ f:G\to H,\\ c,L>0,\ C\geq0\text{ such that, for all }g,h\in G,\\ c\,d(g,h)^\alpha-C\leq\|f(g)-f(h)\|\leq Ld(g,h)+C \end{array}\right\}. \] The lower bound is required only when $d(g,h)\geq1$, avoiding any convention about $0^0$. For word metrics the exponent is independent of the finite generating set. No equivariance of $f$ is required. The supplied record does not explicitly distinguish this exponent from equivariant compression.

Here a uniform embedding, in the coarse-geometric sense, of $(G,d)$ into a Banach space $X$ means a map $f:G\to X$ and nondecreasing functions $\rho_-,\rho_+:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$, with $\rho_-(t)\to\infty$ as $t\to\infty$, such that \[ \rho_-(d(g,h))\leq\|f(g)-f(h)\|\leq\rho_+(d(g,h)) \qquad(g,h\in G). \] A group is uniformly embeddable into Hilbert space if such an embedding exists with $X$ a Hilbert space. This interpretation of uniform embeddability requires confirmation from the report.

A discrete group $G$ is amenable if there is a positive linear functional $m:\ell^\infty(G)\to{\mathbb{R}}$ on bounded real-valued functions such that $m(1)=1$ and $m(u\circ L_g)=m(u)$ for every $g\in G$, where $L_g(h)=gh$.

Under the Cowling--Haagerup interpretation, a discrete group $G$ is weakly amenable if there is a net of finitely supported functions $\varphi_i:G\to{\mathbb{C}}$ converging pointwise to $1$, whose associated Schur multipliers \[ M_{\varphi_i}\left((T_{g,h})_{g,h\in G}\right) =\left(\varphi_i(g^{-1}h)T_{g,h}\right)_{g,h\in G} \] on $B(\ell^2(G))$ have uniformly bounded completely bounded norms. Here $\|M\|_{\mathrm{cb}}=\sup_{n\geq1}\|\operatorname{id}_{M_n}\otimes M\|$. The record does not specify the intended meaning of weak amenability, so this definition is conditional.

A Banach space $X$ is uniformly convex if for every $\varepsilon\in(0,2]$ there is $\delta>0$ such that \[ \|x\|,\|y\|\leq1,\quad\|x-y\|\geq\varepsilon \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \left\|\frac{x+y}{2}\right\|\leq1-\delta. \]

Let $\mathfrak G$ denote the group called Grigorchuk's group of intermediate growth in the supplied record. The usual identification with the first Grigorchuk group is an interpretation requiring source confirmation; the record supplies no defining action or presentation. Let $\mathcal C$ denote the intended class of discrete groups equipped with metrics. The source does not determine $\mathcal C$ or its metric convention for Questions 2 and 3. Taking finitely generated groups with word metrics gives a precise restricted reading, but must not be silently substituted for the full intended scope.

Subject to these explicit qualifications, the four questions are:

  1. Determine $\alpha_2(\mathfrak G,d_S)$, where $S$ is a finite symmetric generating set of the intended Grigorchuk group.
  2. Does there exist $(G,d)\in\mathcal C$ such that $G$ is amenable and $\alpha_2(G,d)<\tfrac12$?
  3. Is it true that every $(G,d)\in\mathcal C$ which admits a uniform embedding into a Hilbert space has weakly amenable underlying group $G$?
  4. Does there exist a finitely generated group $G$, equipped with a word metric, such that for every uniformly convex Banach space $X$, there is no uniform embedding $G\to X$?
These are statement-only questions. The unspecified class, terminology, and group identification prevent this conditional formulation from being treated as a fully verified transcription of the report.

Short English statement

The record asks for the Hilbert-space compression of Grigorchuk's intermediate-growth group, whether amenable groups can have compression below $1/2$, whether Hilbert-space uniform embeddability implies weak amenability, and whether some finitely generated group fails to embed uniformly into every uniformly convex Banach space. The meanings of compression, uniform embedding, and weak amenability, and the domains of the middle two questions, require source confirmation.
