Math-7142

Stable Symbolic-Power Containments from a Single Containment

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For the following conventional formulation, let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian regular ring and let $I\subsetneq R$ be a radical ideal with finite positive big height \[ h=\max\{\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p):\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}_R(I)\}. \] Here $\operatorname{Min}_R(I)$ denotes the set of prime ideals minimal over $I$, and regularity means that every localization at a prime ideal is a regular local ring. For an integer $m\geq1$, define the ordinary and symbolic powers by \[ I^m=\underbrace{I\cdots I}_{m\text{ factors}},\qquad I^{(m)}=\bigcap_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}_R(I)}\left(I^mR_{\mathfrak p}\cap R\right), \] where the intersection with $R$ denotes contraction along localization.

The source asks whether a single containment with a fixed offset $C$ forces the corresponding containments eventually. If $C$ is interpreted as a fixed integer and only positive symbolic exponents are used, the proposed implication is \[ \begin{split} &\left[\exists k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\;\bigl(hk-C\geq1\ \text{and}\ I^{(hk-C)}\subseteq I^k\bigr)\right]\\ &\qquad\Longrightarrow \left[\exists N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\;\forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq N}\;\bigl(hn-C\geq1\ \text{and}\ I^{(hn-C)}\subseteq I^n\bigr)\right]. \end{split} \] The same $R,I,h,C$ occur throughout, and $N$ may depend on these data. This is a conditional precisification requiring review: the supplied source does not specify the domain, sign, or admissible range of $C$, any additional lower bound on $k$, or conventions for nonpositive symbolic exponents. It also does not explicitly specify the ring conventions, properness of $I$, or exclusion of big height zero. Thus the conventions above do not resolve the intended scope of the original question.

Short English statement

Does one containment $I^{(hk-C)}\subseteq I^k$, for a radical ideal of big height $h$, imply $I^{(hn-C)}\subseteq I^n$ for every sufficiently large integer $n$, with the same offset $C$? The permitted values of $C$, restrictions on the initial index $k$, and boundary conventions remain unspecified in the supplied record.
