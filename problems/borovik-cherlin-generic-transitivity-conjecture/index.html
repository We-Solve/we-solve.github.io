Math-772

Borovik–Cherlin Generic-Transitivity Conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a definable group of finite Morley rank acting definably on a definable set $X$ in a common ambient structure. Write $\operatorname{RM}(Y)$ for the Morley rank of a definable set $Y$, with $\operatorname{RM}(\varnothing)=-\infty$. The supplied finite-Morley-rank setting implicitly requires $r:=\operatorname{RM}(X)$ to be finite. For a positive integer $k$, the diagonal action on $X^k$ is \[ g\cdot(x_1,\ldots,x_k)=(g\cdot x_1,\ldots,g\cdot x_k). \] The action is generically $k$-transitive if there is an orbit $O\subseteq X^k$ such that \[ \operatorname{RM}(X^k\setminus O)<\operatorname{RM}(X^k). \] This is the definition in the supplied excerpt; it uses a complement of strictly smaller rank, rather than merely an orbit of full rank.

The supplied record attributes to Borovik and Cherlin the conjectural bound and rigidity assertion \[ k\leq r+2, \] with equality only if, for some integer $n$ and a suitable field $K$, the action is definably isomorphic to the natural action \[ \operatorname{PGL}_n(K)\curvearrowright\mathbb P^{n-1}(K). \] Here $\operatorname{PGL}_n(K)=\operatorname{GL}_n(K)/\{aI_n:a\in K^\times\}$, and $\mathbb P^{n-1}(K)$ is the set of one-dimensional $K$-linear subspaces of $K^n$. A definable isomorphism of actions means a definable group isomorphism $\phi:G\to\operatorname{PGL}_n(K)$ and a definable bijection $f:X\to\mathbb P^{n-1}(K)$ satisfying \[ f(g\cdot x)=\phi(g)\cdot f(x) \qquad(g\in G,\ x\in X). \]

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied formulation does not specify positive rank or infinitude of $X$, faithfulness of the action, connectedness or transitivity assumptions, the field $K$, its ambient definable structure, or the range of $n$. Rank-zero degeneracies obstruct the unrestricted bound, and a definable kernel acting trivially obstructs rigidity for the whole group as stated. It is therefore necessary to determine whether faithfulness is intended or whether rigidity concerns the induced faithful group $G/\ker(G\curvearrowright X)$, where \[ \ker(G\curvearrowright X)=\{g\in G:(\forall x\in X)\ g\cdot x=x\}. \] In the customary projective interpretation with an algebraically closed field $K$ of Morley rank one, one would have $r=n-1$ and hence equality would mean $k=n+1$; those field hypotheses are not supplied explicitly. The displayed assertion records the source's intended target, without selecting missing hypotheses or claiming a complete corrected conjecture.

Short English statement

The supplied Borovik--Cherlin conjecture asks whether generic transitivity is bounded by the Morley rank of the acted-on set plus two, with equality forcing the standard projective linear action. The source leaves essential hypotheses unresolved, especially nondegeneracy, faithfulness, and the field in the equality case. This is a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
