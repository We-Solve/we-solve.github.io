Convergence of Discrete to Classical Conformality

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For reference, two smooth Riemannian metrics $g$ and $\widehat g$ on the same smooth surface $S$ are classically conformally equivalent if there exists $u\in C^\infty(S,{\mathbb{R}})$ such that \[ \widehat g=e^{2u}g. \] This standard definition provides context; the supplied record does not specify whether its intended convergence concerns metrics, conformal structures, conformal maps, or uniformizations.

Source question (2014). Prove or disprove that discrete conformality converges to classical conformality as the triangulations of a surface become arbitrarily fine.

Unresolved formulation. The supplied record does not define discrete conformality, the discrete data associated with a triangulation, or their comparison with classical conformal data. It does not specify the class of surfaces, including compactness, orientability, genus, or the presence of boundary; the regularity and geometric data on the surface; the meaning of refinement or arbitrarily small mesh; admissible mesh-quality conditions; a topology of convergence; or any marking, normalization, or boundary conditions. It also does not specify whether convergence is required for every admissible sequence of triangulations or for a suitably chosen sequence. Consequently, the source question cannot be converted into a uniquely determined quantified convergence assertion from the supplied evidence alone. These choices remain unresolved rather than being imposed as additional hypotheses.

The supplied dated assessment labels the problem partially solved and reports convergence results for closed surfaces under mesh-quality hypotheses, with separate positive results in positive genus and genus zero, while describing the unrestricted conjectural formulation as not fully settled. This is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical or literature conclusion.

Short English statement

The question asks whether discrete conformality approaches classical conformality as surface triangulations become arbitrarily fine. A precise convergence claim requires definitions of the discrete model, admissible surfaces and triangulations, comparison objects, and convergence topology. These are absent from the supplied record. Its partially solved label and reported restricted results are supplied dated assessments requiring independent review.
