Math-13892

Connected Concentration Sets and Solution Counts

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record contains two revised questions about the singularly perturbed equation and energy functional of the report. Let $u_\varepsilon$ denote its solutions, let $E_\varepsilon[u_\varepsilon]$ be their energy, and let $N$ and $m$ be the dimensional parameters used there. The equation, functional, domain, boundary conditions, and admissible ranges of $N$ and $m$ are not included.
  1. Suppose a family satisfies \[ \exists C>0\ \exists\varepsilon_0>0\ \forall\varepsilon\in(0,\varepsilon_0):\qquad E_\varepsilon[u_\varepsilon]\le C\varepsilon^{N-m}, \] where $C$ is independent of $\varepsilon$, and suppose its concentration set is connected. Must the limiting concentration set have dimension $m$? The source does not define concentration set, distinguish connectedness before and after passage to a limit, prescribe the mode of convergence, or specify the notion of dimension. The statement therefore retains these terms as report-dependent data; it does not replace dimension $m$ by an upper bound or by an assertion of smooth manifold structure.
  2. What is the optimal asymptotic bound on the number of solutions as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$? If $\mathcal S_\varepsilon$ denotes the intended solution class and $\sim$ its intended notion of equivalence, the counted quantity would be $\#(\mathcal S_\varepsilon/\!\sim)$. Neither this class nor equivalence, finiteness, or the direction of the desired bound is specified. The record does not say whether the count is restricted to the energy-bounded, connected-concentration families in the first question or concerns a broader solution class.

The supplied verification says these are the two revised open questions following a negative answer to a preceding problem. That preceding problem and its counterexample are not supplied, so they cannot be reconstructed here. The displayed upper energy bound and connectedness are not presented as a self-contained universal assertion for arbitrary energy functionals: the report's missing PDE and concentration hypotheses are essential.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether a connected concentration set with the stated energy scaling must limit to an $m$-dimensional set. The second asks for the optimal growth of the number of solutions. Both require the report's missing equation, concentration definitions, and counting conventions.
