Math-1052

Short-Interval Möbius Orthogonality to Polynomial Phases

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, let $\mu(n)$ be the Möbius function: $\mu(1)=1$, $\mu(n)=0$ if a prime square divides $n$, and $\mu(n)=(-1)^r$ if $n$ is a product of $r$ distinct primes. For $u\in{\mathbb{R}}$, put $e(u)=\exp(2\pi i u)$. Let $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}$ be fixed, and let $H=H(X)$ be a positive real-valued function such that $H(X)\to\infty$ as $X\to\infty$. Define \[ M_{k,H}(x;X)=\sup_{\substack{P\in{\mathbb{R}}[t]\\ \deg P=k}}\left|\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\\ x\leq n\leq x+H(X)}}\mu(n)e(P(n))\right|. \] The summation includes both endpoints whenever they are integers. The supremum is over all real-coefficient polynomials of degree exactly $k$, and the polynomial may vary with $x$ and $X$. No upper bound on $H(X)$ is stated in the supplied record.

The supplied question asks whether, for every such fixed $k$ and every such function $H$, one has $M_{k,H}(x;X)=o(H(X))$ for almost all $x\in[X,2X]$.

The record does not specify whether $x$ ranges over real or integer starting points, or explicitly define "almost all." Under the interpretation of real starting points and an exceptional set of relative Lebesgue measure tending to zero, the precise question is whether, for every $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}$, every positive $H(X)\to\infty$, and every $\varepsilon>0$, \[ \lim_{X\to\infty}\frac{1}{X}\operatorname{meas}\left\{x\in[X,2X]:M_{k,H}(x;X)>\varepsilon H(X)\right\}=0, \] where $\operatorname{meas}$ denotes Lebesgue measure. If integer starting points are intended instead, the corresponding question replaces this displayed limit by \[ \lim_{X\to\infty}\frac{1}{X}\#\left\{x\in[X,2X]\cap{\mathbb{Z}}:M_{k,H}(x;X)>\varepsilon H(X)\right\}=0. \] These are explicit interpretations requiring source confirmation; the supplied record does not select between them. The limits are taken separately for each fixed $k$ and each admissible $H$, with no asserted uniformity over these choices.

Short English statement

Does the Möbius function exhibit cancellation, uniformly over real polynomial phases of each fixed degree $k\geq1$, on almost every interval $[x,x+H(X)]$ with $x\in[X,2X]$, for every length function $H(X)\to\infty$, however slowly? The meaning of almost every starting point requires confirmation. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
