Math-3371

Planar Coexistence in Hostile-Environment First-Passage Percolation

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $d$, let ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$ denote the integer lattice, with nearest-neighbour edges between vertices whose difference has $\ell^1$-norm one. Define its Bernoulli site-percolation threshold by \[ p_c^{\mathrm{site}}(d)=\inf\left\{q\in[0,1]:\mathbb P_q(\text{the origin belongs to an infinite open cluster})>0\right\}, \] where under $\mathbb P_q$, vertices are independently open with probability $q$.

The supplied record concerns hostile-environment first-passage percolation (FPPHE) on ${\mathbb{Z}}^d$, with type 1 growing from the origin, type 2 seeds of density $p$, and a type 2 growth parameter $\lambda$. The excerpt uses a rate-one normalization for comparison with type 1 growth. It does not supply the full stochastic construction: the seed law, passage-time distributions and dependence, activation mechanism, competition rules, and admissible parameter endpoints are absent. Consequently, a uniquely specified probability law cannot be reconstructed from this record alone.

To express the intended question conditionally, fix a completion $\mathcal M$ of these missing model specifications, let $\mathbb P^{\mathcal M}_{d,p,\lambda}$ be its law, and let $I_i(t)\subseteq{\mathbb{Z}}^d$ be the vertices occupied by type $i$ at time $t$. A candidate definition of coexistence is \[ \mathcal C=\left\{\left|\bigcup_{t\geq0}I_1(t)\right|=\infty\right\}\cap \left\{\left|\bigcup_{t\geq0}I_2(t)\right|=\infty\right\}. \] Here inactive seeds must be distinguished from occupied type 2 vertices if the intended model requires that distinction. The source does not define coexistence or survival, so this candidate must be checked against the original model before acceptance.

Requested statement, conditional on those specifications. Determine whether admissible parameters $p,\lambda$ exist such that \[ \mathbb P^{\mathcal M}_{2,p,\lambda}(\mathcal C)>0. \] Also determine a coexistence regime satisfying \[ p<p_c^{\mathrm{site}}(d),\qquad \mathbb P^{\mathcal M}_{d,p,\lambda}(\mathcal C)>0. \] The source does not specify whether the second request is for every $p$ below the threshold, for some subinterval, or for some parameters, nor whether "in any dimension" means each dimension in an intended range or some dimension. These quantifiers remain unresolved; the displayed conditions do not settle them.

Supplied contextual qualifications. The excerpt reports type 1 extinction when $p>1-p_c^{\mathrm{site}}(d)$, on the stated ground that the origin is almost surely confined to a finite cluster of vertices without type 2 seeds. It also reports extinction for $\lambda\geq1$ for all $p$, and more strongly asserts that, for some $\varepsilon=\varepsilon(d,p)>0$, type 1 growth is contained in first-passage percolation of rate $1-\varepsilon$, yielding extinction for $\lambda>1-\varepsilon$. It attributes the comparison to van den Berg and Kesten, reference [1]. These are supplied contextual assertions, not independently verified facts; their seed-density restrictions and endpoint conventions are missing. In particular, the literal phrase "for all $p$" requires clarification at $p=0$.

The excerpt says that its Theorem 2 treats small $p$, that monotonicity is not known, and that survival of type 1 when $\lambda$ is made small is naturally expected. Its subsequent purported confirmation is truncated. It additionally reports substantially different behaviour on hyperbolic and non-amenable graphs: positive-probability type 2 survival for any $p$ and $\lambda$, and an analogue of Theorem 2, citing reference [3]. No graph hypotheses, parameter restrictions, theorem statement, or full reference [3] are supplied. These remarks are background and do not specify the lattice coexistence problem.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether both types can grow indefinitely with positive probability in planar hostile-environment first-passage percolation, and seeks a coexistence regime with seed density $p<p_c^{\mathrm{site}}(d)$. The supplied record leaves the model construction, the exact coexistence definition, and the second question's quantifiers incomplete. Its open-status label and higher-dimensional results are supplied dated curation, requiring independent review.
