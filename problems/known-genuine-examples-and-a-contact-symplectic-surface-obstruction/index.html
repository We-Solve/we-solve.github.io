Math-1449

Known genuine examples and a contact-symplectic surface obstruction

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,g)$ be closed and let $\phi^t:SM\to SM$ be its geodesic flow. Hermann's question uses a continuous $D\phi^t$-invariant splitting \[ T(SM)=E^s\oplus E^c\oplus E^u \] and constants $C\geq1$, $\lambda>\mu>0$ satisfying, for $t\geq0$, \[ \|D\phi^t\xi\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|\xi\|\quad(\xi\in E^s),\qquad \|D\phi^{-t}\eta\|\leq Ce^{-\lambda t}\|\eta\|\quad(\eta\in E^u), \] and, for every $t\in\mathbb R$, \[ C^{-1}e^{-\mu|t|}\|\zeta\|\leq\|D\phi^t\zeta\| \leq Ce^{\mu|t|}\|\zeta\|\qquad(\zeta\in E^c). \] The stable and unstable inequalities missing behind the original reference "(4.1)" are supplied by its verification field. Anosov flows give the stated degenerate positive answer with center equal to the flow direction. The intended genuine examples are partially hyperbolic but not Anosov; nonzero stable and unstable bundles are required in the structural statement below.

The source cites [Car-Puj2011] and [Car-Puj2013] for genuine examples by Carneiro and Pujals. Its extracted description says they deformed a higher-rank locally symmetric space of noncompact type, whereas the accompanying research summary says compact rank-one locally symmetric metrics of nonconstant negative curvature. This discrepancy is retained explicitly: the report attributes partially hyperbolic non-Anosov examples to those authors, but the construction's precise starting geometry must be checked in the references.

The aid's additional contact-symplectic obstruction is conditional on the displayed estimates and nonzero $E^s,E^u$. With canonical contact form $\alpha$ on $SM$, it reports that $E^c\cap\ker\alpha$ is symplectic for $d\alpha$, and \[ \dim E^s=\dim E^u=r\geq1,\qquad \dim E^c=1+2q,\quad q\geq0,\qquad \dim M=1+r+q. \] Thus $q=0$ is the Anosov case. A genuine example must have $q\geq1$, center dimension at least $3$, and base dimension at least $3$; every such geodesic flow over a closed surface is Anosov. This is a necessary dimension restriction, not a construction in every permitted dimension.

The record also includes an adjacent source fragment headed "5. Negative curvature and hyperbolicity of the geodesic flow" and "5.1. Does the marked length spectrum determine the metric?" It defines, for a closed Riemannian manifold, \[ \mathcal L_g([\gamma])= \inf\{L_g(\eta):\eta\text{ lies in the free homotopy class }[\gamma]\}. \] That fragment is retained as a separate neighboring question. Its intended curvature assumptions, marking and equivalence of metrics are not fully supplied, and the solved label for Hermann's existence question is not applied to this marked-length-spectrum question.

Short English statement

The supplied source already attributes genuine partially hyperbolic non-Anosov geodesic flows to Carneiro and Pujals, although its construction description conflicts with the paired summary. The aid adds a contact dimension obstruction excluding genuine surface examples under nonzero stable and unstable bundles. A concatenated marked-length-spectrum question is preserved separately.
