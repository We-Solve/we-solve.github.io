Math-4002

Profinite Rigidity of Coxeter Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Coxeter system is specified by a set $S$ and a symmetric matrix $m_{st}$ with $m_{ss}=1$ and $m_{st}\in\{2,3,4,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}$ for distinct $s,t\in S$. Its Coxeter group is \[ W(S,m)=\left\langle S\ \middle|\ s^2=1\ (s\in S),\ (st)^{m_{st}}=1\ (s\ne t,\ m_{st}<\infty)\right\rangle. \] An entry $m_{st}=\infty$ imposes no relation on $st$. In the graph notation of the supplied excerpt, a nonedge has $m_{st}=2$, and an edge whose label is omitted has label $3$. Write $W_\Gamma$ for the group specified by such a labelled graph $\Gamma$.

For a group $G$, let \[ \mathcal N_G=\{N\trianglelefteq G:[G:N]<\infty\}. \] Its profinite completion is \[ \widehat G=\varprojlim_{N\in\mathcal N_G}G/N, \] where each finite quotient has the discrete topology and the inverse limit has the induced topology from their product. An isomorphism of profinite completions is understood here to be an isomorphism of topological groups.

Let $\mathcal W$ denote the class described in the supplied statement as all Coxeter groups. A group $W\in\mathcal W$ is $\mathcal W$-profinitely rigid if, for every $W'\in\mathcal W$, an isomorphism $\widehat W\cong\widehat{W'}$ of profinite groups implies an isomorphism $W\cong W'$ of abstract groups.

Question. Is every Coxeter group $\mathcal W$-profinitely rigid? Equivalently, is the following implication valid for every pair $W_\Gamma,W_\Lambda\in\mathcal W$? \[ \widehat{W_\Gamma}\cong\widehat{W_\Lambda} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad W_\Gamma\cong W_\Lambda. \] The conclusion concerns the groups, without requiring an isomorphism to preserve their specified Coxeter generators or labelled graphs.

Unresolved scope qualification. The supplied statement says all Coxeter groups but does not explicitly specify whether the Coxeter generating sets must be finite. The definition above permits arbitrary sets $S$, which gives the literal unrestricted reading. If the intended convention is finite rank, the question must instead quantify only over systems with finite generating sets. The supplied record does not resolve this distinction.

Short English statement

Does the profinite completion determine a Coxeter group up to group isomorphism among Coxeter groups? The supplied record labels the problem partially solved, but that status is not independently verified here. Whether the intended question is restricted to finite-rank Coxeter groups remains unclear.
