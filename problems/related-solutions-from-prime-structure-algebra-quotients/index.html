Math-15405

Related Solutions from Prime Structure-Algebra Quotients

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For notation, let $X$ be a set and let $r\colon X\times X\to X\times X$ satisfy the braid form of the set-theoretic Yang--Baxter equation \[ (r\times\operatorname{id}_X)(\operatorname{id}_X\times r)(r\times\operatorname{id}_X) = (\operatorname{id}_X\times r)(r\times\operatorname{id}_X)(\operatorname{id}_X\times r) \quad\text{on }X^3. \] Write $r(x,y)=(\lambda_x(y),\rho_y(x))$. The associated structure monoid is \[ M(X,r)=\left\langle X\ \middle|\ xy=\lambda_x(y)\rho_y(x)\text{ for all }x,y\in X\right\rangle. \] The notation $kM(X,r)$ denotes its monoid algebra: finite formal $k$-linear combinations of monoid elements, with multiplication extending the monoid multiplication. The supplied record does not specify the coefficient assumptions on $k$, including whether it must be a field. It also does not specify whether $X$ is finite or whether $r$ must be bijective, involutive, or nondegenerate. These conditions are therefore not imposed here.

Put $A=kM(X,r)$. In the usual ring-theoretic sense, a prime ideal $P$ of $A$ is a proper two-sided ideal such that, for all two-sided ideals $I,J$ of $A$, \[ IJ\subseteq P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad I\subseteq P\ \text{or}\ J\subseteq P. \] Its prime quotient is $A/P$.

Source question. For those prime ideals $P$ for which $kM(X,r)/P$ arises from a Yang--Baxter solution related to $(X,r)$, determine how the properties of those related solutions, as $P$ varies, reflect properties of $(X,r)$.

Unresolved formulation. The record supplies neither a definition of a related solution nor a construction associating such a solution to a prime quotient. In particular, it does not state whether the quotient must itself be isomorphic to a structure algebra, whether an associated solution is unique, or which properties and implications are sought. Thus the question above preserves the supplied research target but cannot be made into a uniquely specified quantified conjecture from this record alone.

Short English statement

Starting with the structure algebra of a Yang--Baxter solution $(X,r)$, consider its prime quotients that arise from related solutions. The question asks what the properties of these solutions, taken over the relevant prime ideals, reveal about $(X,r)$. The meaning of the association and the properties to be compared remain unspecified.
