Math-3677

Kühnel Conjecture for Embedded Simplicial Skeletons

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Delta_n$ denote the standard $n$-dimensional simplex, with $n+1$ vertices, and let $\Delta_n^{(k)}$ be its simplicial subcomplex consisting of all faces of dimension at most $k$. Write $|\Delta_n^{(k)}|$ for its geometric realization. For a manifold $M$, define its $k$th Betti number over $\mathbb Z_2$ by \[ b_k(M;\mathbb Z_2)=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}H_k(M;\mathbb F_2), \qquad \mathbb F_2=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z. \] For $k\geq1$, being $(k-1)$-connected means being path connected and having $\pi_i(M)=0$ for every $1\leq i\leq k-1$. A topological embedding is a continuous map that is a homeomorphism onto its image.

Kühnel's conjecture, as supplied. For every admissible pair of integers $n,k$ and every compact $(k-1)$-connected manifold $M$ of dimension $2k$, the proposed implication is \[ |\Delta_n^{(k)}|\hookrightarrow M \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \binom{n-k-1}{k+1} \leq \binom{2k+1}{k+1}\,b_k(M;\mathbb Z_2). \] Here the arrow denotes existence of an embedding.

Unresolved formulation qualifications. The source does not explicitly give the admissible parameter range, the simplex indexing convention, whether $M$ must have empty boundary, or the category of manifolds and embeddings. The definitions above use the conventional dimension indexing for $\Delta_n$ and explain the topological meaning of embedding; these conventions require confirmation against the cited report. For integers $k\geq1$ and $n\geq k+1$, the displayed binomial coefficients have nonnegative upper arguments; use $\binom{a}{r}=0$ when $0\leq a<r$. The inequality is then numerically nontrivial only when $n\geq2k+2$. This explains a meaningful parameter range without asserting that the source intended to exclude other cases. In particular, the source leaves the cases $k=0$ and $n=k$, and any required conventions for them, unresolved. No boundary or embedding-category qualification is silently imposed.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture bounds the size of a simplex whose $k$-skeleton embeds in a compact, sufficiently connected $2k$-manifold in terms of the manifold's $k$th Betti number over $\mathbb Z_2$. Parameter, boundary, and embedding-category conventions still require source confirmation.
