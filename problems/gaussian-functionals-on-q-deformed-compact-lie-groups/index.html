Gaussian Functionals on q-Deformed Compact Lie Groups

Definitions and mathematical statement

For terminology, let $A=\operatorname{Pol}(G_q)$ be the Hopf $*$-algebra of representative functions of a compact quantum group $G_q$, with counit $\varepsilon:A\to{\mathbb{C}}$, and put $K=\ker\varepsilon$. A generating functional is a linear map $L:A\to{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfying \[ L(1)=0,\qquad L(a^*)=\overline{L(a)}\quad(a\in A),\qquad L(a^*a)\geq0\quad(a\in K). \] A generating functional is called Gaussian if \[ L(abc)=0\qquad(a,b,c\in K). \] These standard definitions provide terminology; they do not determine the particular decomposition method intended by the source.

Source conjecture. Does the Lévy--Khintchine decomposition method described in the cited report for the quantum group denoted there by $U_q(n)$ extend to the $q$-deformations $G_q$ of other simple compact Lie groups $G$?
