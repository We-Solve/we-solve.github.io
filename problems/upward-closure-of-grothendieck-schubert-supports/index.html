Upward Closure of Grothendieck Schubert Supports

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks the following support question. It does not explicitly specify the version of Grothendieck polynomials or the ambient symmetric group. The formulation below uses the ordinary single type A Grothendieck polynomials and the stable symmetric group to make the notation precise; these conventions require confirmation against the source.

Let $S_\infty=\bigcup_{n\geq 1}S_n$, where the inclusions fix all additional letters. For $u\in S_\infty$, let \[ \ell(u)=\#\{(i,j):i<j,\ u(i)>u(j)\} \] be its Coxeter length. Define the left weak order by \[ u\leq_L v \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \text{there exists }a\in S_\infty\text{ with }v=au \text{ and }\ell(v)=\ell(a)+\ell(u), \] and write $u<_L v$ when additionally $u\ne v$.

Let $\mathfrak S_u$ denote the ordinary type A Schubert polynomial indexed by $u$. In the ordinary single Grothendieck polynomial convention, expand $G_w$ in the Schubert basis of ${\mathbb{Z}}[x_1,x_2,\ldots]$: \[ G_w=\sum_{u\in S_\infty}c_{w,u}\mathfrak S_u, \qquad c_{w,u}\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] The sum has finite support. Define its Schubert support and total polynomial degree by \[ \operatorname{Supp}_S(G_w)=\{u\in S_\infty:c_{w,u}\ne0\}, \qquad \deg(G_w)=\max\{|\alpha|:[x^\alpha]G_w\ne0\}, \] where $\alpha$ ranges over finitely supported sequences of nonnegative integers, $x^\alpha=\prod_i x_i^{\alpha_i}$, and $|\alpha|=\sum_i\alpha_i$.

Question (supplied Conjecture 1). With the intended conventions fixed, is it true that for every indexing permutation $w$ and every $u\in\operatorname{Supp}_S(G_w)$, \[ \ell(u)<\deg(G_w) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \exists v\in\operatorname{Supp}_S(G_w)\text{ such that }u<_L v? \] Here the displayed stable interpretation takes both indexing permutations in $S_\infty$. The supplied record does not resolve whether a fixed finite symmetric group was intended instead. The conclusion requires some strictly larger element in left weak order; it does not state that every larger permutation belongs to the support, nor does it require that $v$ cover $u$.

The excerpt also mentions an unspecified stronger statement and analogues of Grothendieck monomial support conjectures cited there as reference [3]. Neither that stronger statement nor the identity of reference [3] is supplied, so neither is incorporated into this formulation.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every permutation in the Schubert expansion of a Grothendieck polynomial whose Coxeter length is below the polynomial's total degree has a strictly larger permutation, in left weak order, in the same support. The polynomial convention and ambient symmetric group require source confirmation. The record labels the problem partially solved; that label is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified mathematical assessment.
