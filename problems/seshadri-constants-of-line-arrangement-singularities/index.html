Seshadri Constants of Line Arrangement Singularities

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns the singular points of a line configuration in ${\mathbb{P}}^2$. The base field and the meaning of "line configuration" are not specified. The following is a conditional formulation using the usual interpretation as a finite reduced union of distinct projective lines over an algebraically closed field $K$; neither this field convention nor any restriction on its characteristic is confirmed by the record.

Let $X={\mathbb{P}}^2_K$, let $\mathcal A=\bigcup_{j=1}^{d}L_j$ be such a union, and let $S=\{x_1,\ldots,x_r\}$ be its set of distinct singular points, namely the points incident with at least two component lines. Assume $r\geq 1$, as needed for the expression below. Write $H=\mathcal O_X(1)$. Define the multipoint Seshadri constant by \[ \varepsilon(H;x_1,\ldots,x_r) = \inf_{\substack{C\subset X\ \text{integral curve}\\ \sum_{i=1}^{r}\operatorname{mult}_{x_i}(C)>0}} \frac{\deg C}{\sum_{i=1}^{r}\operatorname{mult}_{x_i}(C)}, \] where $\operatorname{mult}_{x_i}(C)=0$ when $x_i\notin C$.

Reading "on one line" as allowing every projective line in $X$, define \[ k=\max_{\ell\subset X\ \text{projective line}}\#(S\cap\ell). \] Under these conventions, the supplied question asks whether, for every such configuration, \[ \varepsilon(\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^2_K}(1);x_1,\ldots,x_r)=\frac{1}{k}. \] The record does not clarify whether the maximum defining $k$ is instead restricted to the component lines $L_1,\ldots,L_d$. That alternative gives a different formulation and remains unresolved here. Configurations with no singular points are also not addressed by the source wording, since then $k=0$ and $1/k$ is undefined.

The supplied background and literature assessment discuss global-generation and very-ampleness thresholds on a surface, rather than the displayed line-configuration question. Their intended connection to this question is not supplied, so they cannot resolve its missing hypotheses or establish its current mathematical status.

Short English statement

For the singular points of a finite arrangement of projective lines, the question asks whether the multipoint Seshadri constant of $\mathcal O_{{\mathbb{P}}^2}(1)$ equals the reciprocal of the largest number of these points on a line. The base field, admissible arrangements, and whether that line must belong to the arrangement require clarification. The supplied literature assessment concerns a different formulation.
