Math-1032

Malle Conjecture for Number Field Counts

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a number field, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $G\subseteq S_n$ be a transitive permutation group, where $S_n$ is the symmetric group on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. Transitivity means that for every $i,j$ there exists $g\in G$ with $g(i)=j$. For a real parameter $X>1$, the supplied record asks for the asymptotic behavior, as $X\to\infty$, of the schematic count \[ N_k(G,X)=\#\left\{K/k:\operatorname{Gal}(K/k)\simeq G,\ \operatorname{Disc}(K/k)<X\right\}. \] It further asks whether, in the cases where Malle's original logarithmic exponent is valid, \[ N_k(G,X)\sim C(G,k)X^{1/a(G)}(\log X)^{b(G,k)-1}. \] Here $\sim$ denotes that the ratio of the two sides tends to $1$. The expression presupposes a positive constant $C(G,k)$, independent of $X$, and specified exponents $a(G)\neq 0$ and $b(G,k)$.

Unresolved formulation. These displays preserve the supplied target but do not specify a unique counting problem. The record does not explicitly require $[K:k]=n$, specify whether extensions are counted up to $k$-isomorphism, or define the discriminant bound. The relative discriminant of an extension of number fields is an ideal of the ring of integers of $k$; comparing it with a real number requires a numerical convention, such as its absolute ideal norm, which the record does not state.

The group condition also requires clarification. Requiring $K/k$ to be Galois with group isomorphic to $G$ differs from counting degree-$n$ extensions whose normal closures have Galois group $G$ in the specified permutation action on the $n$ embeddings of $K$. An abstract group isomorphism alone does not encode that permutation action. The record does not resolve these interpretations.

Finally, the record does not define $a(G)$ or $b(G,k)$, identify the cases covered by the qualification about the original logarithmic exponent, or address degenerate cases such as the trivial transitive group in degree $1$. No definitions of these exponents, additional hypotheses, or choice between the counting interpretations are imposed here. The displayed asymptotic is a qualified question attributed to Malle's conjecture, not an asserted theorem.

Short English statement

The record asks how the number of number field extensions with a prescribed permutation group grows with a discriminant bound, and whether a qualified Malle-type asymptotic holds. A precise conjecture requires clarification of the extensions being counted, the discriminant convention, the exponents, and the scope of the logarithmic-exponent qualification. The supplied open label is a dated registry assessment.
