Math-7240

Commutants of Volterra Operators on $L^p$ Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

In the usual Banach-space range $1\leq p\leq\infty$, let $L^p[0,1]$ be the Lebesgue space on the unit interval, and define the Volterra operator by \[ (Vf)(x)=\int_0^x f(s)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} s. \] Let $\mathcal B(L^p[0,1])$ be the bounded linear operators on that space. The commutant is \[ [V]_p=\{T\in\mathcal B(L^p[0,1]):TV=VT\}. \] For each $p\in(1,\infty)\setminus\{2\}$, give an explicit characterization of $[V]_p$, with necessary and sufficient conditions for a bounded operator to belong to it. Merely repeating the commutation identity is the definition, rather than the requested characterization.

The source singles out $p=1,2,\infty$ as known cases and describes the other values as unresolved. This is retained as a supplied status statement, not asserted as independently verified current knowledge. The source does not explicitly state the overall range of $p$ or the scalar field. The displayed target uses the conventional $L^p$ Banach-space interpretation; it does not silently extend the problem to quasi-Banach exponents $0<p<1$, where the given integral is not defined on every $L^p$ function. Real versus complex scalars and any desired form of operator representation should be confirmed from the report.

Short English statement

Characterize all bounded operators commuting with the Volterra integration operator on $L^p[0,1]$ for $1<p<\infty$, $p\ne2$, beyond the endpoint and Hilbert-space cases reported known.
