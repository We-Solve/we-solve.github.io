Kirby Problem 4.57

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a closed oriented smooth $4$-manifold $X$, write $\chi(X)=\sum_i(-1)^i\dim H_i(X;\mathbb Q)$ for its Euler characteristic and $\sigma(X)$ for the signature of its intersection form on $H_2(X;\mathbb R)$. Let $X,Y$ have equal Euler characteristics and equal signatures.

A torus link in $X$ is a finite disjoint union of smoothly embedded copies $T_i$ of $T^2=S^1\times S^1$. Require every component to have trivial normal bundle, and choose disjoint tubular neighborhoods $\nu T_i\cong T^2\times D^2$. Simultaneous torus surgery removes their interiors and glues back copies of $T^2\times D^2$ using chosen boundary diffeomorphisms of $T^3$, with the orientation convention giving an oriented result.

Is there always such a torus link in $X$ and a choice of gluings whose result is diffeomorphic to $Y$? If this smooth conclusion is too strong, does the same assertion hold with "homeomorphic to $Y$" in its place?

All surgery tori must already be disjoint in the original $X$. A sequence in which later tori occur only in a previously altered manifold would not automatically give the simultaneous surgery asked for. The number of components and all surgery slopes may depend on the pair. No simple-connectivity, symplecticity, or prescribed homology class for the tori is assumed. The numerical equalities are the hypotheses, not a claimed complete diffeomorphism classification.

Short English statement

Can any two closed oriented smooth four-manifolds with equal Euler characteristic and signature be related by simultaneous surgery on a torus link in the first?
