Math-15129

Transfer Matrix Eigenvalue Polynomials for Quantum Affine Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $V=V_1\otimes\cdots\otimes V_m$ be a tensor product of finite-dimensional modules over a quantum affine algebra, and let $W\subseteq V$ be a common generalized eigenspace for the transfer matrices under consideration. Here a common generalized eigenspace means a subspace associated with fixed generalized eigenvalues of the operators in the intended transfer-matrix family. The source denotes by $Q_{i,k}(z)$ the Baxter polynomials appearing in formulas for the joint eigenvalues associated with $W$, with $z$ their polynomial variable.

Requested characterization. Characterize the families of Baxter polynomials $Q_{i,k}(z)$ that occur in these joint transfer-matrix eigenvalue formulas for $W$.

Unresolved formulation. This is the mathematical target stated in the supplied record, but it is not a fully specified classification problem. The record does not specify the quantum affine algebra, its parameters, the coefficient field, the permitted modules, the transfer-matrix family or its eigenvalue formulas, the ranges and meanings of $i$ and $k$, or the definition and normalization of $Q_{i,k}(z)$. Consequently, the polynomial families to be characterized cannot be defined precisely from the supplied evidence. No particular QQ-system, recurrence, degree restriction, root condition, or normalization is imposed here.

Short English statement

The problem asks which Baxter polynomials $Q_{i,k}(z)$ appear in transfer-matrix eigenvalue formulas on a common generalized eigenspace in a tensor product of finite-dimensional quantum affine algebra modules. The supplied record leaves the polynomial family and the relevant formulas undefined, so a precise characterization problem requires further source clarification. Its open status is a supplied curation label.
