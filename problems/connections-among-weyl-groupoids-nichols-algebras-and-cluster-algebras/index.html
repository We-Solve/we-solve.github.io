Connections Among Weyl Groupoids, Nichols Algebras, and Cluster Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The objects named in the supplied question are Weyl groupoids, Nichols algebras, and cluster algebras. A groupoid is a category in which every morphism is invertible; Weyl groupoids are groupoids generated by reflections associated with Cartan data. A Nichols algebra of a braided vector space $V$ is the quotient of its braided tensor algebra $T(V)$ by the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{n\geq 2}V^{\otimes n}$. A cluster algebra is a commutative algebra generated by cluster variables obtained by iterated seed mutation. These descriptions identify the terminology without selecting the classes of objects intended by the source.

The supplied question is:

Is there a deeper connection between Weyl groupoids or Nichols algebras and cluster algebras?

The record does not define "deeper connection," specify a correspondence or construction to be sought, or give a property that such a connection must satisfy. It also does not specify the Cartan data, finiteness assumptions, ground field, braiding, coefficient system, or permitted seed data. Consequently, the question is a research direction rather than a fully specified mathematical proposition: no definite domains, quantifiers, or criterion for an affirmative answer can be recovered without additional information. In particular, the word "or" does not specify whether separate connections for the two families or a common framework involving both families is intended.

As supplied context, the dated curation reports a precise connection between rank-two reflection groupoids and type-$A$ cluster algebras, and reports that no general equivalence or deeper connection for Weyl groupoids or Nichols algebras has been established. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified mathematical or bibliographic conclusions. They do not specify the additional connection sought in this question.
