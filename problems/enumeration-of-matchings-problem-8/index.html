Enumeration of Matchings — Problem 8

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the following standard coordinate convention for an Aztec diamond. For an integer $n\geq 1$, let \[ \mathcal A_n=\left\{Q_{i,j}=[i,i+1]\times[j,j+1] : i,j\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ \left|i+\tfrac12\right|+\left|j+\tfrac12\right|\leq n\right\}. \] Here the elements of $\mathcal A_n$ are unit square cells. Two cells share an edge when their integer indices differ by one of $(1,0),(-1,0),(0,1),(0,-1)$. A domino tiling of a collection of cells is a partition of that collection into pairs of cells sharing an edge.

Let $p=Q_{a,b}$ and $q=Q_{c,d}$ be cells of $\mathcal A_n$. They are related by a knight's move if \[ (|a-c|,|b-d|)\in\{(1,2),(2,1)\}. \] For such a pair define \[ T(n;p,q)=\#\left\{\text{domino tilings of }\mathcal A_n\setminus\{p,q\}\right\}. \] Problem attributed to Propp (1998), Problem 8. Determine $T(n;p,q)$ for the two "close-to-central" cells intended in the source, which are related by a knight's move.
