Resolvent Degrees of Lines and Bitangents

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a specified ground field $k$, let $f:Y\to X$ be a dominant generically finite map of irreducible $k$-varieties. Its resolvent degree $\operatorname{RD}_k(f)$ is the least nonnegative integer $d$ for which there is a tower of dominant generically finite rational maps \[ X_r\dashrightarrow X_{r-1}\dashrightarrow\cdots\dashrightarrow X_0=X \] and a dominant rational map $X_r\dashrightarrow Y$ over $X$, such that each step of the tower is, birationally, obtained by base change from a dominant generically finite map of varieties of dimension at most $d$. This definition concerns dense open subsets; it does not prescribe an extension across degenerate fibers. The supplied record does not specify the ground field or precise models of its parameter spaces, so these data remain unresolved rather than being chosen here.

The notation $H_{3,3}$ denotes the source's space parametrizing smooth cubic surfaces, and $H_{3,3}^{(1)}$ denotes its incidence cover parametrizing pairs $(S,L)$, where $S$ is such a surface and $L$ is a line contained in $S$. The map forgets $L$ and is described in the source as a $27$-sheeted cover. Likewise, $H_{4,2}$ denotes the source's space parametrizing smooth plane quartics, and $H_{4,2}^{(1)}$ denotes its incidence cover parametrizing pairs $(C,B)$, where $B$ is a bitangent line to $C$. This map forgets $B$ and is described as a $28$-sheeted cover. On the ordinary-bitangent locus, this means that the intersection divisor is $B\cdot C=2p+2q$ for distinct geometric points $p,q$. The record does not specify its convention for hyperflexes or the precise locus on which its cover description applies.

With these unresolved choices understood, the supplied conjectural targets are \[ \operatorname{RD}\!\left(H_{3,3}^{(1)}\longrightarrow H_{3,3}\right)=3 \] and \[ \operatorname{RD}\!\left(H_{4,2}^{(1)}\longrightarrow H_{4,2}\right) =\dim H_{4,2}=6. \] Here $\operatorname{RD}$ suppresses the unspecified ground field. The asserted dimension $6$ is retained from the record; the record does not define whether these spaces are moduli spaces, stacks, or other parameter spaces. Consequently, this is a qualified transcription of the conjectures, pending specification of those data. The background literature assessment refers instead to genus-two curves, which is inconsistent with the stated smooth-plane-quartic target: smooth plane quartics have genus three. No replacement of either target is made.

Short English statement

The supplied conjectures assign resolvent degree $3$ to choosing a line on a smooth cubic surface and resolvent degree $6$ to choosing a bitangent of a smooth plane quartic. The ground field, parameter-space models, and bitangent boundary conventions require clarification. The supplied background's reference to genus-two curves conflicts with the quartic statement. These are statement-only conjectural drafts requiring mathematical review.
