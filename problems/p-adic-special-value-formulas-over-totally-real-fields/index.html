Math-862

$p$-Adic Special-Value Formulas over Totally Real Fields

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be totally real, let $n\geq2$ be odd (hence $n\geq3$), and let $p$ be any prime. The source's Conjecture 2 has three parts, all of which are retained:
  1. There exists $q_p(n,k)\in{\mathbb{Q}}^*$ such that \[ L_p(n,\omega_p^{1-n},k)D_k^{1/2,p} =q_p(n,k)\operatorname{Eul}_p(n,k)R_{n,p}(k). \]
  2. With compatible choices, $q_p(n,k)=q(n,k)$, where $q(n,k)$ is the rational factor in the source's Borel complex special-value formula.
  3. Both $L_p(n,\omega_p^{1-n},k)$ and $R_{n,p}(k)$ are nonzero.
Here $L_p$ is the specified $p$-adic $L$-function branch with the indicated cyclotomic finite-order character twist $\omega_p^{1-n}$, $D_k$ is the field discriminant, $D_k^{1/2,p}$ its chosen $p$-adic square-root realization, $\operatorname{Eul}_p(n,k)$ the prescribed Euler-factor term, and $R_{n,p}(k)$ the determinant regulator in degree $2n-1$. In the usual totally real odd-$n$ setting this regulator uses rank $[k:{\mathbb{Q}}]$ rational $K$-theory data. The source does not reproduce its basis, target embeddings, regulator normalization, exact Euler product or the complex formula defining $q(n,k)$.

Those choices are essential: rational factors and square-root signs cannot be compared after independently rescaling complex and $p$-adic regulators. The equality is in the intended $p$-adic coefficient field, with ${\mathbb{Q}}^*$ embedded there. The phrase any prime includes $p=2$, for which character/branch conventions also need specification; it is not silently excluded.

The nonvanishing part is separate. An equality with a nonzero rational factor does not rule out both the special value and regulator vanishing, so it must not be omitted or treated as an automatic consequence of the formula. Conversely, numerical agreement in examples is not a general proof of the rational-factor equality.

The literature triage checked 21 August 2026 reports substantial special cases and numerical instances, while retaining general nonvanishing and regulator-type assertions open. Exact hypotheses and parameter lists of those partial results are not supplied. This is dated source metadata, not an independent current-status determination.

Short English statement

For every totally real field, odd $n\geq3$ and prime, the source predicts a $p$-adic special-value/regulator identity with the same rational factor as Borel's complex formula, plus nonvanishing of both sides' principal factors. Compatible normalizations and the separate nonvanishing assertion are essential.
