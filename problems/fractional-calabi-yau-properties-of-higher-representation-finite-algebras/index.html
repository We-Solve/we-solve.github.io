Fractional Calabi–Yau Properties of Higher Representation-Finite Algebras

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question concerns higher representation-finite algebras and twisted fractionally Calabi--Yau algebras. The excerpt does not specify the ground field or the ambient finiteness hypotheses. The following gives a precise formulation under the usual finite-dimensional, finite-global-dimension interpretation; confirmation of the intended ambient class remains necessary.

Let $k$ be a field and let $A$ be a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra of finite global dimension. Write $\operatorname{mod}A$ for the category of finite-dimensional right $A$-modules and $\mathcal D_A=D^b(\operatorname{mod}A)$. Its Serre functor is $S_A=-\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}DA$, where $DA=\operatorname{Hom}_k(A,k)$. Write $[m]$ for the cohomological shift by $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}$. For a $k$-algebra automorphism $\phi$ of $A$, let $T_\phi$ be the induced twisting functor, obtained on right modules by replacing the action with $x\cdot a=x\phi(a)$.

In this formulation, $A$ is fractionally Calabi--Yau if there exist integers $\ell>0$ and $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ and a natural isomorphism of triangle functors \[ S_A^{\ell}\cong[m]. \] It is twisted fractionally Calabi--Yau if there additionally may be an automorphism $\phi$, so that \[ S_A^{\ell}\cong[m]\circ T_\phi \qquad\text{for some }\ell>0,\ m\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ \phi\in\operatorname{Aut}_{k\text{-alg}}(A). \] The fractional Calabi--Yau condition sought below may use different integers from those witnessing the twisted condition.

For an integer $n\geq1$, use the convention that $A$ is $n$-representation-finite if $\operatorname{gl.dim}A\leq n$ and there is a module $M\in\operatorname{mod}A$ such that \[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{add}M &=\{X\in\operatorname{mod}A:\operatorname{Ext}_A^i(M,X)=0\text{ for every }1\leq i<n\}\\ &=\{X\in\operatorname{mod}A:\operatorname{Ext}_A^i(X,M)=0\text{ for every }1\leq i<n\}. \end{aligned} \] Here $\operatorname{add}M$ consists of direct summands of finite direct sums of copies of $M$; when $n=1$, the displayed vanishing conditions are empty. Interpret higher representation-finite as $n$-representation-finite for some integer $n\geq1$, subject to confirmation of the source's convention.

Question under this interpretation. For every algebra $A$ in the intended ambient class, does being higher representation-finite imply that \[ \exists\,p\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}\ \exists\,q\in{\mathbb{Z}}:\quad S_A^p\cong[q]? \] More generally, does being twisted fractionally Calabi--Yau imply this same conclusion for every such $A$? The supplied curation reports that higher representation-finite algebras are twisted fractionally Calabi--Yau; that background assertion is not proved or independently verified here.

The surrounding excerpt discusses entropy vanishing results and imposes the additional hypothesis that $A$ is elementary for its Hochschild (co)homology entropy assertion. Short English statement

Must every higher representation-finite algebra have a positive power of its Serre functor naturally isomorphic to a shift? More generally, does the same conclusion follow whenever a positive Serre power is a shift composed with a twist by an algebra automorphism? The ground field, ambient hypotheses, and source conventions need confirmation. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified assessment.
