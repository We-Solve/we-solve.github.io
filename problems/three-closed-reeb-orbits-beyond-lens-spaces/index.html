Math-3796

Three Closed Reeb Orbits Beyond Lens Spaces

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth three-dimensional manifold and let $\alpha$ be a smooth contact form, meaning that $\alpha\wedge{\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha$ is nowhere zero. Its Reeb vector field $R_\alpha$ is characterized by \[ \alpha(R_\alpha)=1,\qquad {\,\mathrm{d}}\alpha(R_\alpha,v)=0\quad\text{for every }v\in TM. \] A closed Reeb orbit is a periodic integral curve $\gamma:{\mathbb{R}}/T{\mathbb{Z}}\to M$, for some $T>0$, satisfying $\dot\gamma(t)=R_\alpha(\gamma(t))$. Count orbits by their images in $M$, so that multiple traversals of one orbit do not count as different orbits. This geometric counting convention is an interpretation requiring confirmation from the source.

For the usual finite-quotient convention, a lens space is a manifold diffeomorphic to \[ L(p,q)=S^3/\langle g\rangle,\qquad g(z_1,z_2)=\left(e^{2\pi i/p}z_1,e^{2\pi iq/p}z_2\right), \] where $S^3\subset{\mathbb{C}}^2$, $p\geq1$, $q\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, and $\gcd(p,q)=1$. This convention includes $S^3=L(1,q)$. The record does not specify its lens-space convention, including whether $S^1\times S^2$ is included as a degenerate lens space.

Source conjecture, with unresolved scope. Let $\mathcal C$ denote the intended class of smooth three-manifolds, which the supplied record does not fully specify. The proposed assertion is \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every }M\in\mathcal C\text{ that is not a lens space, and every contact form }\alpha\text{ on }M,\\ \text{there exist }T_1,T_2,T_3>0\text{ and periodic integral curves }\gamma_i:{\mathbb{R}}/T_i{\mathbb{Z}}\to M\\ \text{of }R_\alpha\text{ such that }\operatorname{im}(\gamma_i)\neq\operatorname{im}(\gamma_j)\text{ whenever }i\neq j. \end{gathered} \] The class $\mathcal C$ remains unresolved: compactness, connectedness, absence of boundary, and any conditions at a boundary are not stated. In particular, restricting to closed connected manifolds would add hypotheses requiring source confirmation. No nondegeneracy hypothesis is supplied. The original wording says only "manifold"; the supplied cleaned statement explicitly specifies dimension three. Thus this is a parameterized statement-only draft, rather than a fully determined conjecture.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture asks whether every Reeb vector field on a three-manifold outside the lens-space class has at least three geometrically distinct closed orbits. The intended manifold hypotheses, lens-space convention, and orbit-counting convention need confirmation. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
