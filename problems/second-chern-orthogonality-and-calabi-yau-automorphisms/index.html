Second-Chern Orthogonality and Calabi-Yau Automorphisms

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal S$ denote the class called strict Calabi--Yau threefolds (SCY3) in the supplied source. The record does not define this class or specify the base field; in particular, its intended smoothness, projectivity, canonical-bundle, fundamental-group, and cohomological hypotheses require confirmation from the cited discussion.

To make the numerical notation explicit, conditionally on the intended setting being smooth projective threefolds over ${\mathbb{C}}$, write \[ N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\left(\operatorname{Div}(X)/{\equiv_{\mathrm{num}}}\right)\otimes_{{\mathbb{Z}}}{\mathbb{Q}}, \qquad N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{R}}}=N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\otimes_{{\mathbb{Q}}}{\mathbb{R}}. \] Let $\operatorname{Amp}(X)\subseteq N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{R}}}$ be the cone of ample real divisor classes and set \[ \operatorname{Amp}(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}=\operatorname{Amp}(X)\cap N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}. \] With $c_2(X)=c_2(T_X)$, define \[ c_2(X)^\perp =\{\alpha\in N^1(X)_{{\mathbb{R}}}:c_2(X)\cdot\alpha=0\}, \qquad c_2(X)\cdot[D]=\int_X c_2(T_X)\smile c_1(\mathcal O_X(D)). \] Here $\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ denotes the group of biregular automorphisms.

Question 1. Is the following assertion true? \[ \forall X\in\mathcal S,\qquad |\operatorname{Aut}(X)|=\infty \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\alpha\in\operatorname{Amp}(X)_{{\mathbb{Q}}}\setminus\{0\} \quad c_2(X)\cdot\alpha=0. \] The word "ample" and the exclusion of zero are retained literally from the supplied formulations. Whether the source intends this cone or a different cone requires verification.

Question 2. Does there exist $X\in\mathcal S$, other than the source's examples $X_3$ and $X_7$, with an automorphism $f\in\operatorname{Aut}(X)$ that is primitive and has positive entropy?

For a complex projective interpretation, positive entropy means $h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)>0$. A usual interpretation of primitive is that there are no normal projective variety $Y$, dominant rational map $\pi:X\dashrightarrow Y$, and birational self-map $g:Y\dashrightarrow Y$ such that \[ 0<\dim Y<3, \qquad \pi\circ f=g\circ\pi. \] This convention is conditional: the supplied record does not specify the intended definition of primitive, including any requirements on the fibres of $\pi$. Likewise, it supplies neither constructions of $X_3,X_7$ nor the equivalence relation intended by "other than." Thus the conventional reading $X\not\simeq X_3,X_7$ cannot be made fully explicit from this record alone. The original excerpt makes Question 2 existential; it does not ask whether every threefold satisfying Question 1's hypothesis admits such an automorphism.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether every strict Calabi--Yau threefold with infinitely many automorphisms has a nonzero rational ample divisor class pairing trivially with its second Chern class. The second asks for another strict Calabi--Yau threefold with a primitive automorphism of positive entropy, beyond the examples labelled $X_3$ and $X_7$. Definitions of the class of threefolds, primitivity, and the excluded examples require source confirmation.
