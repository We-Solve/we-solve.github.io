Kourovka Notebook Problem 1.28

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Work in the first-order language of groups $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{grp}}=\{1,\cdot,{}^{-1}\}$, without parameters. A universal sentence is a sentence $\forall x_1\cdots\forall x_n\,\varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$, where $n\geq 0$ is finite and $\varphi$ is a quantifier-free Boolean combination of equations between group terms. For a group $G$, define its universal theory by \[ \operatorname{Th}_{\forall}(G)=\{\sigma:\sigma\text{ is a universal }\mathcal L_{\mathrm{grp}}\text{-sentence and }G\models\sigma\}. \] For a set $X$, let $F_X$ be the free group on $X$. Define $\gamma_1(F_X)=F_X$ and $\gamma_{j+1}(F_X)=[\gamma_j(F_X),F_X]$, where $[H,K]$ denotes the subgroup generated by $[h,k]=h^{-1}k^{-1}hk$ with $h\in H$ and $k\in K$. For a positive integer $c$, the free nilpotent group on $X$ in the variety of groups of nilpotency class at most $c$ is \[ N_{X,c}=F_X/\gamma_{c+1}(F_X). \] Problem (M. I. Kargapolov). Describe the universal theory of a free nilpotent group; in the notation above, determine $\operatorname{Th}_{\forall}(N_{X,c})$.

Unresolved scope. The supplied statement does not specify the rank $|X|$, whether infinite ranks or the trivial group are included, or the nilpotency parameter $c$. Short English statement

Describe exactly which universally quantified, parameter-free statements in the language of groups hold in a free nilpotent group. The supplied problem leaves the rank, nilpotency class, and required form of the description unspecified.
