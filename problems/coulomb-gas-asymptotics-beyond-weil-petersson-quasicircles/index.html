Coulomb-Gas Asymptotics Beyond Weil–Petersson Quasicircles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A planar quasicircle is the image of the unit circle under a quasiconformal homeomorphism of ${\mathbb{C}}$. For a bounded planar quasicircle $\eta$, let $\Omega$ be its bounded complementary component and let $f:\mathbb D\to\Omega$ be a conformal bijection, where $\mathbb D=\{z\in{\mathbb{C}}:|z|<1\}$. A standard characterization of the Weil--Petersson property is \[ \int_{\mathbb D}\left|\frac{f''(z)}{f'(z)}\right|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A(z)<\infty, \] where ${\,\mathrm{d}} A$ denotes planar area measure. This characterization specifies the terminology for bounded planar quasicircles; the supplied excerpt does not itself specify the ambient setting or a definition of the Weil--Petersson class.

Source question. Determine the Coulomb-gas asymptotics when the boundary curve $\eta$ is not a Weil--Petersson quasicircle. In particular, the supplied dated assessment identifies arbitrary quasicircles outside the Weil--Petersson class as a remaining target.

Unresolved formulation. Consequently, the question cannot be converted into a uniquely specified asymptotic assertion from this record alone.

Short English statement

The problem asks how Coulomb-gas asymptotics extend beyond Weil--Petersson boundary curves, especially to arbitrary non-Weil--Petersson quasicircles. The supplied excerpt omits the gas model and the formula to be extended, so a precise asymptotic claim requires additional source context.
