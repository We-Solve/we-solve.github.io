Nichols Algebras over Nonabelian Simple Groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite nonabelian simple group. A left Yetter--Drinfeld module over ${\mathbb{C}} G$ is a complex vector space $V$ with a left $G$-action and a grading \[ V=\bigoplus_{g\in G}V_g, \qquad h\cdot V_g\subseteq V_{hgh^{-1}}\quad(g,h\in G). \] Its support is $\operatorname{supp}(V)=\{g\in G:V_g\ne0\}$. The associated braiding is \[ c(v\otimes w)=g\cdot w\otimes v \qquad(v\in V_g,\ w\in V). \] Write ${}_{{\mathbb{C}} G}^{{\mathbb{C}} G}\mathcal{YD}$ for this category. The Nichols algebra of $V$ is \[ \mathcal B(V)=T(V)/I(V),\qquad T(V)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}V^{\otimes n}, \] where $T(V)$ has its braided Hopf algebra structure with every element of $V$ primitive, and $I(V)$ is the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{n\ge2}V^{\otimes n}$.

Question. Is the following assertion true? \[ \text{For every finite nonabelian simple group }G \text{ and every }0\ne V\in{}_{{\mathbb{C}} G}^{{\mathbb{C}} G}\mathcal{YD}, \qquad \dim_{{\mathbb{C}}}\mathcal B(V)=\infty. \] The supplied clean statement imposes neither irreducibility nor finite dimensionality on $V$, and it imposes no condition that its support generate $G$.

Scope qualification. The original excerpt also asks a broader question about Nichols algebras of irreducible Yetter--Drinfeld modules. The displayed question follows the supplied clean statement concerning finite nonabelian simple groups; it does not formulate a classification for arbitrary groups. The excerpt includes supporting results, but its transcription of some group families is corrupted, and its prime-dimensional equivalence requires source checking for potentially missing hypotheses. These contextual assertions are not adopted as additional hypotheses or as verified theorems here.

Short English statement

For every finite nonabelian simple group $G$, must the Nichols algebra of every nonzero complex Yetter--Drinfeld module over ${\mathbb{C}} G$ be infinite-dimensional? The supplied formulation includes modules that are not irreducible. Its contextual theorem statements and supplied partial-solution status require human review.
