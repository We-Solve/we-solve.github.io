Spectral Convergence Under Lower Ricci Bounds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record asks for an extension of convergence results for noncollapsed Ricci-bounded sequences. It does not specify the individual convergence theorems or all their hypotheses, so it determines an extension question rather than a unique fully quantified conjecture.

To explain the curvature notation, suppose that the spaces in question are smooth Riemannian manifolds $(X_i,g_i)$, and write $\operatorname{Ric}_{g_i}$ for their Ricci tensors. Under this interpretation, a uniform lower Ricci bound means that there is a common constant $K\geq 0$ such that \[ \operatorname{Ric}_{g_i}(v,v)\geq -K g_i(v,v) \qquad\text{for every }i,\ x\in X_i,\ v\in T_xX_i. \] The stronger hypothesis is written in the source as $\lvert\operatorname{Ric}_{X_i}\rvert\leq K$. The tensor norm or alternative convention intended by these absolute-value signs is not specified.

The requested extension can be expressed schematically as follows. For each intended spectral, tensor, or related convergence theorem, let $H$ denote its remaining hypotheses, including its precise noncollapse and convergence assumptions, and let $C$ denote its conclusion. Given the corresponding result under the source's two-sided curvature hypothesis, \[ H\ \text{ and }\ \lvert\operatorname{Ric}_{X_i}\rvert\leq K\ \text{ for all }i \quad\Longrightarrow\quad C, \] does the analogous assertion \[ H\ \text{ and }\ \operatorname{Ric}_{g_i}\geq -K g_i\ \text{ for all }i \quad\Longrightarrow\quad C \] hold? Here $H$ and $C$ are placeholders for the particular results intended in the cited report, not independently specified hypotheses and conclusions. The quantification ranges only over those intended results; the question is not an assertion that every theorem involving a two-sided Ricci bound admits this replacement.

The supplied record does not fix the dimension, compactness or boundary assumptions, volume normalization and scale defining noncollapse, convergence topology, limiting spaces, spectral operators, tensor classes, or precise convergence conclusions. These must be recovered from the cited source before the schematic question becomes a precise individual mathematical statement.

Short English statement

Can the spectral, tensor, and related convergence results intended in the source for noncollapsed sequences with uniformly bounded Ricci curvature be extended to a uniform lower Ricci bound, retaining their other hypotheses? The supplied record leaves the particular results and several essential hypotheses unspecified.
