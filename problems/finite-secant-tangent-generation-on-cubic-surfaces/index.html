Math-3383

Finite Secant–Tangent Generation on Cubic Surfaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a field. Under the usual projective interpretation, a smooth cubic surface over $K$ is a smooth degree-three hypersurface $S\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}^3_K$. Write $S(K)$ for its set of $K$-rational points.

The supplied record does not define the permitted secant and tangent constructions completely. One precise finite-intersection convention is the following. Given $P,Q\in S(K)$, possibly equal, a construction produces $R\in S(K)$ if there is a line $\ell\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}^3_K$, defined over $K$ and not contained in $S$, whose intersection divisor satisfies \[ \ell\cdot S=P+Q+R. \] Intersection multiplicities are included. For $P\ne Q$, this is the secant construction. For $P=Q$, the line has intersection multiplicity at least two at $P$, giving the tangent construction.

For $B\subseteq S(K)$, let $\operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{st}}(B)$ be the smallest subset of $S(K)$ containing $B$ and closed under these constructions. Thus its points are obtainable by finitely many successive constructions.

Question, under this convention. Is it true that, for every field $K$ and every smooth cubic surface $S\subseteq{\mathbb{P}}^3_K$, there exists a finite subset $B\subseteq S(K)$ such that \[ \operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{st}}(B)=S(K)? \] The quantification over all fields preserves the supplied wording; no restriction to number fields, finitely generated fields, infinite fields, or particular characteristics is supplied. If $S(K)=\varnothing$, the choice $B=\varnothing$ satisfies the displayed equality.

Unresolved qualifications. The projective hypersurface interpretation and the finite-intersection convention above make the question explicit, but the supplied record does not confirm these conventions. In particular, it does not specify whether constructions using lines contained in $S$ may add points on those lines. Such lines have no residual degree-three intersection divisor. The intended construction rule and intended field hypotheses require source review before this draft can be treated as a definitive formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether finitely many rational points on every smooth cubic surface over a field generate all its rational points by repeated secant and tangent constructions. The draft specifies a residual-intersection interpretation, while flagging the unspecified treatment of lines contained in the surface and the unrestricted field domain. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
