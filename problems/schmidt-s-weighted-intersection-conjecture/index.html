Math-331

Schmidt's weighted intersection conjecture

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\|x\|_{{\mathbb{Z}}}=\min_{p\in{\mathbb{Z}}}|x-p|$. For every $u\in[0,1]$, define exactly as in the source \[ C_u=\left\{(\alpha,\beta)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2: \inf_{q\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}}\max\{q^u\|q\alpha\|_{{\mathbb{Z}}}, q^{1-u}\|q\beta\|_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\}>0\right\}. \] The constant implicit in this positive infimum may depend on the point and the weight. Conjecture 32 states \[ \forall s,t\in[0,1],\qquad C_s\cap C_t\ne\varnothing. \] The source attributes the conjecture to W. Schmidt [170], while explicitly noting that his original stated case was $s=1/3$, $t=2/3$. The OCR string "6 =" is interpreted as $\ne$, as confirmed by the supplied aid. The source reports that every $C_s$ is Lebesgue null and has Hausdorff dimension two, and that $C_s\cap C_0\cap C_1$ also has dimension two [157].

The attached aid identifies these sets with the modern weighted badly approximable sets, including the exact endpoint identities \[ \operatorname{Bad}=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}:\inf_{q\ge1}q\|qx\|_{{\mathbb{Z}}}>0\},\qquad C_0={\mathbb{R}}\times\operatorname{Bad},\quad C_1=\operatorname{Bad}\times{\mathbb{R}}. \] It attributes to Badziahin, Pollington and Velani's 2011 finite-intersection theorem the stronger statement \[ \dim_H\left(\bigcap_{j=1}^{m} C_{u_j}\right)=2 \quad\text{for every }m\ge1,\ u_1,\ldots,u_m\in[0,1], \] and reports that later hyperplane-absolute-winning results give \[ \dim_H\left(\bigcap_{j=1}^{\infty} C_{u_j}\right)=2 \quad\text{for every sequence }(u_j)_{j\ge1}\subset[0,1]. \] These are supplied literature-resolution claims, not new proofs or independent verification.

The aid's additional quantitative endpoint/interior distinction is: if $0<u<1$ and $\alpha=a/r\in{\mathbb{Q}}$ with $a\in{\mathbb{Z}}$, $r\ge1$, then for every $N\ge1$ and every $\beta\in{\mathbb{R}}$ there is $q\in r{\mathbb{Z}}$, $1\le q\le rN$, such that \[ \max\{q^u\|q\alpha\|_{{\mathbb{Z}}},q^{1-u}\|q\beta\|_{{\mathbb{Z}}}\} \le r^{1-u}N^{-u}. \] If instead $\beta=a/r$, the analogous bound is $r^uN^{-(1-u)}$. Thus every interior-weight $C_u$ excludes rational coordinates, whereas at an endpoint one coordinate is unrestricted. This distinction does not weaken the intersection theorem.

Short English statement

Schmidt's weighted intersection conjecture asks whether every two sets $C_s,C_t$ meet. The supplied literature report gives full Hausdorff dimension even for countable intersections and preserves the endpoint conventions, where one coordinate may be arbitrary.
