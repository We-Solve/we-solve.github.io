Multiplicity Reduction by Regular Equimultiple Blowups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a Noetherian local ring $(A,\mathfrak m)$ of dimension $d$, its Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity is \[ e(A)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{d!}{n^d}\operatorname{length}_A(A/\mathfrak m^{n+1}). \] Write $e_X(x)=e(\mathcal O_{X,x})$ when this definition applies. This is the usual interpretation of multiplicity adopted for explaining the question; the supplied record does not explicitly specify a multiplicity invariant. A closed center $C\subseteq X$ is regular if its local rings are regular. Under the usual multiplicity interpretation, equimultiplicity along $C$ means that $e_X(c)$ is constant for $c\in C$. Whether the intended centers must additionally lie in the locus of maximum multiplicity is not specified. If $\mathcal I_C$ is the ideal sheaf of $C$, the blowup along $C$ is \[ \operatorname{Bl}_C(X)=\operatorname{Proj}_X\!\left(\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\mathcal I_C^n\right). \] Source question. Can the multiplicity of a variety in positive characteristic be reduced by successive blowups along regular equimultiple centers? In particular, can such reduction be proved in the situation \[ X=\operatorname{Spec}(B)\longrightarrow W=\operatorname{Spec}(S), \qquad S\subset B,\qquad S\text{ regular}, \] where the inclusion $S\subset B$ is described in the source as purely inseparable?

The record does not specify the base field, the precise ring-theoretic meaning of purely inseparable, or finiteness hypotheses on $S\subset B$. It also does not specify whether reduction is local at a chosen point, concerns maximum multiplicity globally, or requires a strict decrease at every point above a chosen point after finitely many blowups. Thus the question above preserves the supplied target, but these missing qualifications prevent a unique fully quantified conjecture. The record also leaves unspecified whether the blowups are intrinsic to $X$ or are performed in an ambient space with corresponding transforms.

Short English statement

The question asks whether blowing up regular centers along which multiplicity is constant can lower multiplicity in positive characteristic, especially for a purely inseparable affine morphism to a regular affine scheme. The exact hypotheses and the required meaning of reduction remain unspecified. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
