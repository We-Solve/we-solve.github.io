Math-412

Global Moduli of G2 Metrics

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a compact smooth seven-manifold admitting the source's holonomy-$G_2$ metrics. To make the proposed cohomology map well defined, one normally works with torsion-free $G_2$-structures: positive three-forms $\varphi$ inducing a metric $g_\varphi$ and satisfying \[ \mathrm d\varphi=0,\qquad \mathrm d(*_\varphi\varphi)=0. \] An orientation and positivity convention are understood in this formulation. Whether holonomy must be exactly $G_2$ or may be a subgroup of $G_2$ is not fixed in the supplied wording.

Let $\mathcal F$ be the intended space of such forms and let $\operatorname{Diff}_0(M)$ be diffeomorphisms isotopic to the identity. The structure-moduli interpretation is \[ \mathcal M=\mathcal F/\operatorname{Diff}_0(M),\qquad \pi:\mathcal M\to H^3(M;{\mathbb{R}}),\quad \pi([\varphi])=[\varphi]_{\mathrm{dR}}. \] The cohomology class is unchanged by diffeomorphisms isotopic to the identity. The source calls this a moduli space of metrics and speaks of the metric's defining form; a metric alone does not include all the needed form/orientation choices in every holonomy convention. The form-moduli versus metric-moduli distinction must therefore be resolved.

The questions are to describe this moduli space and, assuming the stated local-diffeomorphism property, to determine whether $\pi$ is globally a diffeomorphism onto its image. In the structure-moduli formulation the proposed conclusion is that $\pi$ is injective on the whole intended $\mathcal M$, so its local smooth inverses combine to a global smooth inverse on $\pi(\mathcal M)$. Equivalently, equal degree-three cohomology classes should force two admissible structures to differ by a diffeomorphism isotopic to the identity. The question does not ask surjectivity onto all of $H^3(M;{\mathbb{R}})$.

The original wording does not restrict this injectivity to a single connected component. Componentwise results or a quotient by the full diffeomorphism group would be different statements.

Short English statement

Describe the global moduli of compact $G_2$ geometry and decide whether its degree-three period map is globally one-to-one, given that it is locally a diffeomorphism. The distinction between metrics and defining torsion-free forms is essential.

The supplied summary attributes local smoothness, harmonic-three-form tangent spaces, and the local period-map result to Joyce and Hitchin. It calls the general global question open, but also mentions an open-embedding claim on components and Calabi--Yau Torelli-type comparisons without exact hypotheses or references. Its final sentence is truncated. Those comments are not adopted as a general injectivity theorem. The partial-progress label and 2008 source are retained with current status marked for review.
