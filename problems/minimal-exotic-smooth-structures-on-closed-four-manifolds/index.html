Math-4577

Minimal Exotic Smooth Structures on Closed Four-Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed compact orientable smooth four-manifold is a compact orientable smooth manifold of dimension four with empty boundary. The supplied statement does not specify connectedness. Say that an underlying topological four-manifold admits multiple smooth structures if it supports two smooth structures whose resulting smooth manifolds are homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic. Here diffeomorphisms are not required to preserve an orientation; the source does not specify an orientation-sensitive equivalence relation.

For an orientation of such a manifold $M$, its real intersection form is \[ Q_M\colon H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})\times H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})\longrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}, \qquad Q_M(a,b)=\langle a\smile b,[M]\rangle. \] Call $M$ definite if $Q_M$ is positive definite or negative definite. Reversing all component orientations changes the sign of this form. The source does not specify whether manifolds with $H^2(M;{\mathbb{R}})=0$ are included; under the usual universal-inequality definition, definiteness on the zero vector space holds vacuously.

The supplied problem asks for the following.

  1. Determine the simplest closed compact orientable smooth four-manifold admitting multiple smooth structures. No complexity invariant, ordering, or equivalence criterion for "simplest" is supplied, so this part does not yet specify a precise minimization problem.
  2. Determine whether there exist closed compact orientable smooth four-manifolds $M_0,M_1$ such that \[ M_0\cong_{\mathrm{homeo}}M_1, \qquad M_0\not\cong_{\mathrm{diff}}M_1, \qquad Q_{M_0}\text{ is definite}. \] Orientations may be transported by the homeomorphism when comparing the forms. No restriction on the fundamental group is imposed in the supplied question.

The supplied dated curation labels the problem partially solved and reports examples of closed definite four-manifolds with multiple smooth structures and fundamental group ${\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. It also reports that the simplest example and the simply connected definite case remain unresolved. These are supplied literature assessments, not independently verified conclusions. The simply connected case is a refinement mentioned in that assessment, rather than a hypothesis of the original existence question.

Short English statement

The problem asks which closed orientable four-manifold is simplest among those supporting inequivalent smooth structures, and whether such structures occur on a definite four-manifold. The meaning of "simplest" is unspecified. Supplied dated curation reports definite examples with fundamental group ${\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$, while describing the simplest example and the simply connected definite case as unresolved; this assessment requires independent verification.
