Rank Preservation Under Singular Covariance Updates

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Sigma$ denote a singular covariance matrix in the neighborhood coordinate descent setting mentioned in the supplied record. Write $I$ for its finite index set, let $u\in I$, and write $\Sigma_{\setminus u,\setminus u}$ for the principal submatrix indexed by $I\setminus\{u\}$. Singularity means that $\det\Sigma=0$ before the update. Let $\widetilde\Sigma$ denote the matrix after the neighborhood coordinate descent update referred to in the source.

The supplied question asks whether, for every admissible singular covariance matrix, index, and update in the intended setting, \[ \det\Sigma_{\setminus u,\setminus u}=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \det\widetilde\Sigma=0. \] This is only a schematic formulation: the record supplies neither the update rule nor the definition of admissibility. It also does not specify the scalar field, matrix dimension restrictions, boundary conventions, or any additional constraints imposed by the algorithm. Consequently, the intended quantification over updates cannot be made precise from this record alone.

The original extract refers to "Conjecture 4" without reproducing it and ends with an incomplete rank expression involving the blocks $\Sigma_{uu}$, $\Sigma_{u,\setminus u}$, $\Sigma_{\setminus u,u}$, and the notation $(\Sigma_{\setminus u,\setminus u})^{-}$. The meaning of this inverse notation, the scope of the rank operator, and the hypotheses of the intended identity are not supplied. No rank identity or full rank preservation claim is asserted here.

Short English statement

The question is whether the intended neighborhood coordinate descent update preserves singularity when the principal submatrix obtained by deleting index $u$ is singular. The update and the referenced Conjecture 4 are missing, so this remains an incomplete statement requiring source clarification and human mathematical review.
