Math-4195

Northcott Property for Totally Real Integer Rings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question is whether the ring $\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{tr}}^{(d)}}$ has Property $(N)$. The record identifies $\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{tr}}^{(d)}$ as the maximal totally real subfield of $\mathbb Q^{(d)}$, but does not define $\mathbb Q^{(d)}$, specify the range or quantification of $d$, or define Property $(N)$. Consequently, the following explicit formulation is conditional on conventions that require source verification.

Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}\subset\mathbb C$. An algebraic number is totally real if every one of its conjugates over $\mathbb Q$ lies in $\mathbb R$. Write $\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{tr}}$ for the field of all totally real algebraic numbers. Under the conventional interpretation that, for a fixed positive integer $d$, $\mathbb Q^{(d)}$ is the compositum in $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ of all number fields of degree at most $d$, set \[ K_d=\mathbb Q^{(d)}\cap\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{tr}},\qquad \mathcal O_{K_d}=\{\alpha\in K_d:\alpha\text{ satisfies a monic polynomial in }\mathbb Z[x]\}. \] Thus $K_d$ is the maximal totally real subfield described in the record.

For an algebraic number $\alpha$ with primitive irreducible polynomial $a\prod_{i=1}^{n}(x-\alpha_i)\in\mathbb Z[x]$, where $a>0$, define its absolute multiplicative Weil height by \[ H(\alpha)=\left(a\prod_{i=1}^{n}\max\{1,|\alpha_i|\}\right)^{1/n}. \] If Property $(N)$ means the Northcott property for this height, the question becomes: does \[ \forall B\in\mathbb R_{>0},\qquad \#\{\alpha\in\mathcal O_{K_d}:H(\alpha)\le B\}<\infty \] hold for the intended values of $d$? No bound on the degrees of the elements $\alpha$ is imposed in this formulation.

A different possible interpretation uses the house $\max_{1\le i\le n}|\alpha_i|$. Under that interpretation the question instead asks whether \[ \forall B\in\mathbb R_{>0},\qquad \#\left\{\alpha\in\mathcal O_{K_d}:\max_{1\le i\le n}|\alpha_i|\le B\right\}<\infty. \] The supplied record does not specify which boundedness condition defines Property $(N)$; these formulations are not identified with one another here. The intended definition and quantification of $d$ remain unresolved.

Short English statement

The source asks whether the algebraic integers in the maximal totally real subfield of $\mathbb Q^{(d)}$ have Property $(N)$. A precise interpretation requires checking the definition of $\mathbb Q^{(d)}$, the intended values of $d$, and whether Property $(N)$ concerns bounded Weil height or bounded house. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
