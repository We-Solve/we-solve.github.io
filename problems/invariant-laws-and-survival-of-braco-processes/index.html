Math-3342

Invariant Laws and Survival of BRACO Processes

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Retain $(a,b,c,d)$ for the parameters of the source's branching-coalescing (BRACO) process $X$, and write $Y$ for its corresponding resampling-selection process. This distinguishes the two processes whose symbols collided in the original extraction. Let $P_t$ and $Q_t$ denote their respective Markov semigroups in the intended state spaces. An invariant probability law $\mu$ for $X$ satisfies $\mu P_t=\mu$ for every $t\geq0$, and analogously for $Y$.

The first question asks whether every invariant law of $X$ is the Poissonization of an invariant law of $Y$: \[ \forall\mu\ [\mu P_t=\mu\text{ for all }t\geq0] \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists\nu\ [\nu Q_t=\nu\text{ for all }t\geq0, \ \mu=\mathcal P\nu]. \] Here $\mathcal P$ is the specific Poissonization kernel in the report. In a usual countable-site construction, conditional on a state $y$ sampled from $\nu$, the particle counts at sites $i$ are independent Poisson variables with intensities $\lambda_i(y)$; the resulting mixture is $\mathcal P\nu$. The intensity map, including any parameter-dependent normalization, is not supplied and is not set equal to $y$ by assumption. The assertion ranges over all invariant laws in the intended state space, not merely ergodic, translation-invariant or moment-bounded laws unless the missing setup restricts that space. It is a surjectivity question and does not assert uniqueness of $\nu$.

The second question concerns the reversed jump rates \[ a^\dagger(i,j)=a(j,i). \] Keeping $b,c,d$ unchanged, does survival of the $(a,b,c,d)$ process imply survival of the $(a^\dagger,b,c,d)$ process? This is transposition of the prescribed rates, without additional stationary weights. If survival means global survival from a prescribed nonzero finite initial configuration $x$, its usual event is $\{\tau_0=\infty\}$, where $\tau_0=\inf\{t\geq0:X_t=0\}$, with positive probability. The source does not specify that initial-state quantifier or distinguish global survival from local survival at a site; no one convention is silently substituted. Admissibility and nonexplosion of both original and reversed-rate processes require the missing hypotheses on the site set and rates.

The source does not provide either generator, the state spaces, rate summability or spatial assumptions, or the parameter relation defining the corresponding diffusion. These are essential for a completely specified theorem. The literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports both invariant-law and survival questions explicitly posed and no general resolution located. The verification field reports repairing the collided process labels and defining the reversed rates. These are retained as supplied metadata without independent verification.

Short English statement

First, ask whether every stationary BRACO distribution comes from a stationary resampling-selection distribution by the source's Poisson mixture. Second, ask whether positive survival persists when migration rates are transposed. The generators, Poisson intensity normalization and survival convention are missing, while the two requests and the exact reversal formula are preserved.
