Boundary of Zero-Entropy Dissipative Systems

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal M$ denote the source's intended class of mildly dissipative disk diffeomorphisms, equipped with its intended topology $\tau$. For $f\in\mathcal M$, let $h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)$ denote its topological entropy, and write $\operatorname{IR}(f)$ for the property that $f$ is infinitely renormalizable. Define \[ \mathcal Z=\{f\in\mathcal M:h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)=0\},\qquad \mathcal I=\{f\in\mathcal Z:\operatorname{IR}(f)\}. \] The boundary relative to $(\mathcal M,\tau)$ is \[ \partial_{\mathcal M,\tau}\mathcal Z =\overline{\mathcal Z}^{\,\mathcal M,\tau} \setminus\operatorname{int}_{\mathcal M,\tau}(\mathcal Z). \] The source asks whether \[ \partial_{\mathcal M,\tau}\mathcal Z=\mathcal I, \] or, equivalently, whether every $f\in\mathcal M$ satisfies \[ f\in\partial_{\mathcal M,\tau}\mathcal Z \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)=0\ \text{and}\ \operatorname{IR}(f). \] This is a conditional formulation: the supplied record does not specify the differentiability class, topology, disk and mapping boundary conventions, or definitions of mild dissipation and infinite renormalizability. These must be recovered from the cited sources before the displayed question determines a fully specified mathematical proposition. No assertion is made here for all dissipative disk diffeomorphisms.

Short English statement

Within the intended space of mildly dissipative disk diffeomorphisms, does a map lie on the boundary of the zero-topological-entropy locus exactly when it has zero entropy and is infinitely renormalizable? The space, topology, and renormalization conventions require clarification from the sources.
