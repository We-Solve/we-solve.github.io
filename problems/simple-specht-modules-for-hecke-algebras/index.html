Simple Specht Modules for Hecke Algebras

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p$, where $p$ is a prime. A nonzero module $M$ over a $k$-algebra $H$ is called simple if its only $H$-submodules are $0$ and $M$.

The supplied source asks for a classification of the simple Specht modules for "the corresponding Hecke algebra" over $k$. The antecedent specifying that algebra is absent from the supplied record. Its type, rank, defining parameters, and the intended construction and indexing of its Specht modules are therefore unresolved. No assumption that $k$ is algebraically closed is supplied.

Statement schema, pending these specifications. For each intended Hecke algebra $H$ over such a field $k$, write $(S^\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ for its specified family of Specht modules. Determine exactly the set \[ \left\{\lambda\in\Lambda : S^\lambda\ne 0\ \text{and every }H\text{-submodule }N\subseteq S^\lambda\text{ satisfies }N=0\text{ or }N=S^\lambda\right\}. \] Here $\Lambda$ and $S^\lambda$ are placeholders for source data that have not been supplied. This schema expresses the classification target but does not specify a unique mathematical problem. In particular, neither a restriction to type $A$ nor a quantification over every family of Hecke algebras is justified by the supplied wording.

Short English statement

Determine precisely which Specht modules are nonzero and simple over fields of prime characteristic. The source does not identify the intended Hecke algebra, its parameters, or its Specht-module conventions, so a definitive classification problem requires further source clarification. The supplied status is partially solved; this is a curation label, not an independently verified conclusion.
