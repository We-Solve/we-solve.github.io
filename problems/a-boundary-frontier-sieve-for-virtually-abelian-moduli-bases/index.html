Math-1233

A boundary-frontier sieve for virtually abelian moduli bases

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Viehweg asks whether the fundamental group of a moduli stack $\mathcal M_H$ of canonically polarized manifolds can be almost abelian, and more precisely conjectures a negative answer for a positive-dimensional nonsingular variety $U$ mapping generically finitely to that stack. A group is almost abelian if it contains an abelian subgroup of finite index. In the usual complex interpretation use topological fundamental groups, with the stack/orbifold group for $\mathcal M_H$. Interpret generically finite as finite generic fibers over the image, so a corresponding smooth projective family with ample fiberwise canonical class has maximal variation. This is different from requiring the coarse moduli map to be quasi-finite everywhere. The ground field, group convention, connectedness and exact image condition are not fixed in the original.

The original reports the conjecture known for $\mathcal M_g$ and under suitable local Torelli hypotheses. It discusses $U={\mathbb{P}}^n\setminus D$ for a normal-crossings divisor with fewer than $n$ components, attributing results to Viehweg--Zuo [VZ02], and cites [VZ05] as further motivation. Its repeated component-bound sentence uses $H$ where the boundary notation is unclear; it should not be interpreted as a new strict/non-strict range.

The attached unreviewed aid treats $n\ge2$ and a nonempty reduced simple-normal-crossings divisor $D=\sum_{i=1}^r D_i\subset{\mathbb{P}}^n_{\mathbb{C}}$, with component degrees $d_i$. Attributing the group calculation to Libgober, it gives \[ \pi_1(U)\simeq{\mathbb{Z}}^r/\langle(d_1,\ldots,d_r)\rangle \simeq{\mathbb{Z}}^{r-1}\oplus{\mathbb{Z}}/\gcd(d_1,\ldots,d_r){\mathbb{Z}}. \] Thus these complements have abelian fundamental group. If such $U$ supports a maximal-variation family of canonically polarized manifolds, the aid invokes Viehweg--Zuo and log-general-type results to require \[ r\ge n,\qquad\sum_i d_i\ge n+2. \] For general-position hyperplanes, under the stronger everywhere quasi-finite coarse-moduli-map hypothesis, it reports Brody hyperbolicity and an entire-curve argument forcing $r\ge2n+1$. At the first allowed hyperplane number $r=2n+1$, its group formula yields $\pi_1(U)\simeq{\mathbb{Z}}^{2n}$. These are necessary conditions for a possible example, not a construction of a family or a counterexample to the conjecture. The all-variety question and the stack-group question remain separate from this boundary calculation.

Short English statement

Ask whether a positive-dimensional maximal-variation base for canonically polarized manifolds can have virtually abelian fundamental group. The aid gives necessary boundary conditions for projective-space complements and a stronger hyperplane bound under an everywhere quasi-finite moduli map.
