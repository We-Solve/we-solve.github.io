Math-3882

Embedding $\sigma$-Compact TDLC Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A totally disconnected locally compact (TDLC) group means a Hausdorff topological group that is locally compact and whose connected components are singletons. Such a group $G$ is $\sigma$-compact if there are compact subsets $K_n\subseteq G$, indexed by positive integers, such that $G=\bigcup_{n\geq 1}K_n$. A topological group $H$ is compactly generated if some compact subset $K\subseteq H$ generates $H$ as an abstract group, so that $H=\langle K\rangle$. A topological group embedding $\iota:G\to H$ is an injective continuous homomorphism that is a homeomorphism onto its image with the subspace topology.

Under this interpretation of embedding, the question is: for every $\sigma$-compact TDLC group $G$, do there exist a compactly generated TDLC group $H$ and a topological group embedding $\iota:G\to H$? No second countability assumption is included.

The supplied clean statement explicitly supplies the $\sigma$-compact hypothesis. The original excerpt starts with an unspecified $G$ and does not itself state that hypothesis or define the meaning of embedding. Thus the formulation above follows the supplied clean statement and uses the topological embedding convention, which requires confirmation against the full source.

The original excerpt also contains questions about a TDLC version of the Atiyah conjecture, compactly generated TDLC groups admitting lattices of irrational covolume, equality of rational cohomological dimensions for quasi-isometric compactly generated TDLC groups $G,H$ both having finite cohomological dimension over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, and a suitable TDLC analogue of right-angled Artin groups. These are neighboring questions in the supplied excerpt; they are not additional assertions or requirements of the selected embedding question.

Short English statement

Does every $\sigma$-compact totally disconnected locally compact group occur, with its given topology, as a subgroup of a compactly generated totally disconnected locally compact group? This formulation interprets embedding topologically; the supplied excerpt leaves that convention implicit.
