Math-3691

Commutator Diameters in Hamiltonian Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,\omega)$ be a closed symplectic manifold, with the symplectic form included in the notation $M$. Let $\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)$ be its group of smooth Hamiltonian time-one maps. For $\phi\in\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)$, the Hofer norm is \[ \|\phi\|_H=\rho_H(\operatorname{id},\phi) =\inf_{\phi_H^1=\phi}\int_0^1 \left(\max_M H_t-\min_M H_t\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t, \] where the infimum is over smooth time-dependent Hamiltonians generating $\phi$. Additive time-dependent constants in $H$ do not change the displayed oscillation.

With commutator convention $[f,g]=fgf^{-1}g^{-1}$, define \[ A(M,\omega)=\sup_{f,g\in\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)}\|[f,g]\|_H\in[0,\infty]. \] The source asks whether this quantity is finite or infinite for a given closed symplectic manifold, and how the answer depends on the manifold. Finiteness means \[ \exists C<\infty\quad\forall f,g\in\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega),\qquad \|[f,g]\|_H\leq C; \] infinity means that for every $R>0$ some pair has $\|[f,g]\|_H>R$. The constant may depend on $(M,\omega)$; no bound uniform across all symplectic manifolds is asked.

The supremum is over single commutators, not arbitrary products of commutators or the entire group's Hofer diameter. Nor is this a question about commutator length. The source specifies no manifold class beyond closed symplectic and gives no preferred finite or infinite answer. The supplied literature triage checked 2026-08-21 reports no authoritative resolution of the extracted general question, without a more specific theorem or example.

Short English statement

For a fixed closed symplectic manifold, are the Hofer norms of all single Hamiltonian commutators uniformly bounded, or can they be arbitrarily large? This is a commutator-subset diameter question, distinct from commutator length or the whole group's diameter.
