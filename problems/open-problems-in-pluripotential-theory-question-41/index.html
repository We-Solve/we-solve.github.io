Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X$ denote the ambient complex manifold in the DiNezza--Lu construction cited by the supplied record, and let $S_0$ denote its initial positive current. The record assumes that $S_0$ has a nonzero Lelong number: $\nu(S_0,x)>0$ for at least one $x\in X$, where $\nu(S_0,x)$ measures the local concentration of the current at $x$. The ambient hypotheses and the admissibility conditions on $S_0$ are not supplied.

According to the record, DiNezza and Lu construct a maximal family of positive currents \[ (\omega_t)_{0<t<T_{\max}} \] and Zariski open subsets $\Omega_t\subseteq X$ satisfying \[ \Omega_s\subseteq\Omega_t\qquad(0<s<t<T_{\max}), \] such that $\omega_t|_{\Omega_t}$ is a smooth Kähler form and the family satisfies the Kähler--Ricci flow equation there. Here $T_{\max}$ denotes the maximal existence time in that construction. The supplied record does not specify the flow normalization, the initial convergence condition, or the meaning of maximality.

For a closed positive $(1,1)$-current $T$, the usual local potential formulation of analytic singularities is that, near every point, one can write $T=i\partial\bar\partial u$, where \[ u=c\log\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^{m}|f_j|^2\right)+v, \qquad c>0, \] with finitely many holomorphic functions $f_j$ and a locally bounded remainder $v$. A stronger convention requires $v$ to be smooth; the record does not specify which convention is intended.

Question (DiNezza--Lu). In the setting of the cited construction, does every current $\omega_t$, for $0<t<T_{\max}$, have analytic singularities on $X$? Equivalently, under the local potential interpretation above, does each $\omega_t$ admit such a local representation near every point of $X$, including points outside $\Omega_t$? The question concerns the singularities of the currents themselves; their smoothness on $\Omega_t$ alone does not specify this property. A fully specified statement requires the omitted hypotheses of the construction and the intended regularity of the remainder $v$.

Short English statement

Starting from an initial current with at least one positive Lelong number, does every positive-time current in the cited DiNezza--Lu Kähler--Ricci flow have analytic singularities? The supplied excerpt leaves the construction's hypotheses and the precise convention for analytic singularities unresolved.
