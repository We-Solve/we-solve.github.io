Stable Reduction of Plane Quartics from Cayley Octads

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A smooth plane quartic over a field $K$ is a smooth geometrically connected curve $C\subset{\mathbb{P}}^2_K$ defined by a homogeneous polynomial of degree $4$; it has genus $3$. In the usual complete-intersection description, a Cayley octad consists of eight distinct geometric points in ${\mathbb{P}}^3$ forming the scheme-theoretic intersection of three independent quadrics. The supplied record refers to a Cayley octad associated with $C$, but does not specify the construction, its auxiliary choices, or its hypotheses.

In a discrete valuation setting, a stable model after a finite extension is a proper flat family with generic fibre the extended curve and geometric special fibre a connected nodal curve satisfying $2g_v-2+n_v>0$ on every irreducible component. Here $g_v$ is the genus of the normalization of that component and $n_v$ counts branches of nodes on it. One possible meaning of stable reduction type is the isomorphism class of the dual graph of this special fibre, with vertices weighted by $g_v$, edges representing nodes, and loops allowed. The record does not specify whether this is the intended meaning or whether additional data are retained.

Conjecture as supplied, with unresolved scope. The combinatorial configuration data of an associated Cayley octad determine the stable reduction type of a smooth plane quartic. More formally, let $\mathscr D$ denote the intended class of admissible curve--octad data, let $\mathcal P(C,O)$ denote the intended combinatorial invariant, and let $T(C)$ denote the intended stable reduction type. The asserted determination property has the form \[ \forall (C_1,O_1),(C_2,O_2)\in\mathscr D,\qquad \mathcal P(C_1,O_1)\cong\mathcal P(C_2,O_2) \ \Longrightarrow\ T(C_1)\cong T(C_2). \] This is a formulation template, not a fully specified conjecture: the source does not define $\mathscr D$, $\mathcal P$, the relevant equivalences, or $T$. In particular, it leaves the valued base field, residue characteristic restrictions, valuation-dependent configuration data, and treatment of choices of associated octad unresolved. No assertion over all fields or residue characteristics is made here.

The source motivates the conjecture by replacing the eight Weierstraß points in the hyperelliptic comparison with a Cayley octad, analogously to the role of a hyperelliptic cluster picture. The hypotheses of that comparison are also unspecified.

Short English statement

The proposed conjecture says that suitable combinatorial data attached to the eight points of an associated Cayley octad determine a smooth plane quartic's stable reduction type. The supplied record does not define those data or the precise arithmetic scope, so a complete mathematical formulation requires further source clarification.
