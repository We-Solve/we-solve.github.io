Retracted versus Intrinsic Thin Plate Splines on Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,g)$ denote the Riemannian manifold in the supplied question. Write $s_{\mathrm{ret}}$ and $s_{\mathrm{int}}$ symbolically for, respectively, a retracted thin plate spline and an intrinsic manifold thin plate spline constructed from the same data, whenever both constructions are defined. These symbols identify the objects to be compared; the record does not specify their defining variational problems or the retraction construction.

Comparison problem. Determine how $s_{\mathrm{ret}}$ differs from $s_{\mathrm{int}}$, particularly in the interpolation regime denoted in the source by $\lambda=\infty$, and describe how this comparison depends on the manifold's closeness to Euclidean space. The supplied background also identifies comparison of smoothness properties as an unresolved target.

Unresolved specifications. The record does not specify the parameter domain, interpolation sites and values, admissible maps, spline energies, boundary conditions, choice of retraction, existence or uniqueness assumptions, or a measure of difference between the splines. It also does not define closeness to Euclidean space or the smoothness properties to be compared. The notation $\lambda=\infty$ is supplied as a designation of the interpolation case; no penalized functional or limiting procedure is given. Consequently, no particular quantitative estimate, convergence statement, or universal quantification over manifolds and data can be extracted without additional information.

The supplied dated assessment says that the interpolation difference has been quantified under $\varepsilon$-flatness, while a general smoothness comparison remains unsettled. Neither $\varepsilon$-flatness nor the hypotheses and conclusion of that reported comparison are supplied, so this draft does not assert such a result.

Short English statement

Compare retracted and intrinsic thin plate splines on a Riemannian manifold, especially for interpolation, denoted by $\lambda=\infty$, and determine how their difference and smoothness depend on closeness to Euclidean geometry. Precise spline constructions, comparison criteria, and geometric hypotheses remain unspecified in the supplied record.
