Single Polynomial Description of Binary Tensor Gram Loci

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, a real binary tensor with $n$ factors is an element of \[ T_n=({\mathbb{R}}^2)^{\otimes n}. \] The supplied record does not define the associated Gram locus, its coordinates, or any normalization imposed on tensors. Consequently, its intended mathematical statement cannot be specified uniquely from this record alone.

To express the proposed property without silently resolving those omissions, let $G_n\subseteq A_n\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^{m_n}$ denote the intended Gram locus and its intended ambient domain, where the coordinate dimension $m_n$, the set $A_n$, and the construction of $G_n$ remain to be supplied. For each fixed format covered by the conjecture, the proposed single-polynomial characterization has the form \[ \exists\,p_n\in{\mathbb{R}}[x_1,\ldots,x_{m_n}]\quad \forall\,x\in A_n,\qquad x\in G_n\ \Longleftrightarrow\ p_n(x)\geq 0. \] Here real coefficients are the unrestricted interpretation of a real polynomial inequality; the record supplies no coefficient restrictions. This display is a conditional formulation of the requested characterization, not a complete definition of the conjectured locus. In particular, equality with $\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^{m_n}:p_n(x)\geq0\}$ is asserted only if the intended ambient domain is all of ${\mathbb{R}}^{m_n}$. If additional ambient constraints are intended, they must be stated explicitly.

The supplied dated assessment identifies $n\geq4$ as the remaining conjectural range, reports a characterization for three binary factors, and reports that the convex hull is known in general. These are supplied literature-status claims, not independently verified mathematical conclusions. The record does not specify whether a general characterization means existence of a separate polynomial for each fixed $n$, or a uniform construction of such polynomials.

Short English statement

For each fixed binary tensor format, the question asks whether membership in its Gram locus can be characterized by one polynomial being nonnegative. The supplied record leaves the Gram locus, normalization, coordinates, and ambient constraints undefined, so the displayed formulation is conditional. Its dated curation labels the problem partially solved and identifies $n\geq4$ as conjectural; human mathematical review is required.
