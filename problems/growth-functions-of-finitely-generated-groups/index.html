Math-82

Growth Functions of Finitely Generated Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. For a finitely generated group $G$, let $S\subseteq G$ be a finite symmetric generating set, meaning $S=S^{-1}$ and $\langle S\rangle=G$. Define \[ |g|_S=\min\{k\in\mathbb N_0:g=s_1\cdots s_k,\ s_i\in S\},\qquad v_{G,S}(n)=\left|\{g\in G:|g|_S\le n\}\right|, \] where the empty product is the identity. Thus $v_{G,S}(0)=1$. Finite groups are included; the supplied statement imposes no infinitude assumption.

For functions $f,h:\mathbb N_0\to(0,\infty)$, adopt the growth comparison \[ f\preccurlyeq h\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists C\in\mathbb Z_{\ge1}\ \forall n\in\mathbb N_0, \quad f(n)\le C h(Cn), \] and write $f\simeq h$ when both $f\preccurlyeq h$ and $h\preccurlyeq f$. Write $v_G$ for the resulting growth equivalence class of $v_{G,S}$, which is independent of the finite generating set.

Classification question. Characterize the functions $f:\mathbb N_0\to(0,\infty)$, up to $\simeq$, for which there exist a finitely generated group $G$ and a finite symmetric generating set $S$ such that \[ f\simeq v_{G,S}. \]

Infimal growth question. With the pointwise interpretation of the infimum, define \[ \underline v_G(n)=\inf\{v_{G,S}(n):S\subseteq G\text{ is a finite symmetric generating set}\}, \qquad n\in\mathbb N_0. \] Determine how $\underline v_G$ can differ, in growth, from $v_{G,S_0}$, as $G$ ranges over finitely generated groups and $S_0$ is any fixed finite symmetric generating set of $G$.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not define growth equivalence, the generating sets allowed in the infimum, or the order of taking that infimum and passing to growth equivalence classes. The definitions above give an explicit conventional reading using word balls and a pointwise infimum over all finite symmetric generating sets, without a cardinality bound. The phrase "how far" specifies no particular discrepancy invariant, inequality, or extremal target; consequently the source supplies a classification question rather than a definite quantitative conjecture.

Short English statement

Classify the possible word growth types of finitely generated groups. Also determine how their usual word growth can compare with the pointwise minimum over all finite symmetric generating sets. The conventions and the intended measure of discrepancy require source confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
