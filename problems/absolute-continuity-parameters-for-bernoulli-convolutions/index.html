Absolute Continuity Parameters for Bernoulli Convolutions

OpenAI's claim

Unbiased Bernoulli convolutions are claimed to admit an arithmetic singularity criterion for every parameter, with singular reciprocal quartic-Salem and specified degree-31 non-Pisot examples. The cited manuscript argues for that result; its relation to this problem is specified below.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
Let $\nu_\lambda$ be the law of $\sum_{j\ge0}\epsilon_j\lambda^j$ for independent unbiased signs $\epsilon_j\in\{-1,1\}$ and $0<\lambda<1$. OpenAI claims an exact arithmetic singularity criterion. Define $R(n,d)$ as the real algebraic units $r\in(1/2,1)$ of degree $dn$ whose minimal polynomial has coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$ and is irreducible modulo two; choose its sign to make the constant term $-1$. Let $\pi_n(p)$ be its first $n$ coefficient vector and \[ W_n(r;d)=\{\pi_n(p_q):q\in R(n,d),\ |q-r|\le4^{-n}\},\quad \mathcal O_u=\{w:(2u_i-1)w_i\ge0\}, \] with $u\in\{0,1\}^n$. Let $R_*(n,d,j)$ contain those $r$ for which at least $2^n/d$ orthants contain at least $j(2r)^n$ vectors of $W_n(r;d)$. It claims \[ \nu_\lambda\text{ singular}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \lambda\in(0,1/2)\cup\left((1/2,1)\cap\bigcup_{d\ge2}\bigcap_{j\ge1}\limsup_{n\to\infty}\bigcup_{r\in R_*(n,d,j)}[r-4^{-n},r)\right), \] and absolute continuity exactly on the complement; replacing limsup with liminf leaves the set unchanged. It separately claims singularity at $\lambda=1/\beta$ for every degree-four Salem number $\beta\in(1,2)$, and for the unique real root $\beta\in(1.8392,1.8393)$ of \[ t^{31}-2t^{30}+\sum_{j=0}^9(t^{3j+1}-t^{3j}), \] which it claims is non-Pisot. Pisot means an algebraic integer greater than one with all other conjugates strictly inside the unit circle; a degree-four Salem number has the other conjugates $\beta^{-1}$ and a conjugate pair on that circle.

Outline of the argument

Manuscript 1 (family 153, 2026-10-03): Measure concentration supplies finite bit-word differences whose initial coefficients can be completed to height-one polynomials with roots just above the parameter. Uniform finite-order nonvanishing retains the sign change, and irreducibility over the two-element field is imposed without changing the initial data, converting overlap concentration into one-sided algebraic-unit approximation.
