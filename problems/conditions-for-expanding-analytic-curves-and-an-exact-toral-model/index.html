Conditions for expanding analytic curves and an exact toral model

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The missing setup is supplied by the preceding local record, permanent ID $20003284$, at the start of section 4.3. Let $L$ be a Lie group, $G<L$ a closed subgroup, $\Lambda<L$ a lattice, $a\in G$ semisimple, and $x_0\in L/\Lambda$ with $\overline{Gx_0}=L/\Lambda$. Define the expanding horospherical subgroup \[ U=\{u\in G:a^{-n}ua^n\longrightarrow e\text{ as }n\to\infty\}. \] For a real-analytic curve $\gamma:[0,1]\to U$, let $\nu$ be the image of Lebesgue probability measure under $t\mapsto\gamma(t)x_0$, and let $\lambda$ be Haar probability on $L/\Lambda$. N. Shah's Question 17 asks for conditions on $\gamma$ ensuring \[ (a^n)_*\nu\overset{w^*}{\longrightarrow}\lambda, \qquad\text{that is,}\qquad \int_0^1 f\left(a^n\gamma(t)x_0\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t \longrightarrow\int_{L/\Lambda}f\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\lambda \quad(f\in C_c(L/\Lambda)). \] The preceding source notes convergence for measures arising from probabilities on $U$ absolutely continuous with respect to its Haar measure, citing [177]; the curve question is a refinement for potentially singular measures. The current extract's trailing "Recently" is an incomplete continuation, not an extra condition. The aid reports that a following rank-one ambient-group conjecture has been proved, without claiming a general classification in this full setting.

An obstruction reported by the aid is containment of $\gamma([0,1])x_0$ in a closed set $F\subset L/\Lambda$ with $aF\subset F$ and $\lambda(F)<1$. Then all translated measures are supported on $F$ and cannot converge to $\lambda$.

The supplied exact model is separate from the homogeneous problem. Let $d\ge1$, $q>1$, $\eta:[0,1]\to{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be real analytic, $t_0\in[0,1]$, and $w\in L^1([0,1])$ satisfy $w\ge0$ and $\int_0^1w=1$. On $\mathbb T^d={\mathbb{R}}^d/{\mathbb{Z}}^d$, put \[ \sigma_n=(t\mapsto q^n\eta(t)\pmod{{\mathbb{Z}}^d})_*(w(t)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t), \] \[ R_\eta=\{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}^d:k\cdot(\eta(t)-\eta(t_0))=0\text{ for all }t\in[0,1]\}, \] \[ H_\eta=\{x\in\mathbb T^d:k\cdot x=0\pmod1\text{ for all }k\in R_\eta\}. \] The aid reports that $H_\eta$ is a connected subtorus and that the weak cluster measures of $\sigma_n$ are exactly normalized Haar measures on the cosets $z+H_\eta$ whose classes are cluster points of \[ q^n\eta(t_0)+H_\eta\quad\text{in }\mathbb T^d/H_\eta. \] Every such quotient cluster point gives the corresponding measure limit along a subsequence. Consequently $\sigma_n$ converges to full Haar measure on $\mathbb T^d$ exactly when $R_\eta=\{0\}$. Here magnification is applied to the specified real lift $\eta$; no noninteger torus endomorphism is presumed. This analytic toral model and the closed-set obstruction are reported partial results, not a solution for general $L,G,\Lambda,a$.

Short English statement

Which analytic curves in an expanding horospherical subgroup become Haar distributed under repeated translation by $a$? A preceding record supplies the missing homogeneous setup. The attached toral model classifies all cluster measures by integral resonances, while the full homogeneous question remains broader.
