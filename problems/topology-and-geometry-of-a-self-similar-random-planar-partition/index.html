Math-13164

Topology and geometry of a self-similar random planar partition

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathcal P=\{C_i\}$ denote Aldous's source-specific self-similar random partition of ${\mathbb{R}}^2$, with designated capital points $c_i$. Its construction and probability law are not supplied. The task has several parts:
  1. Determine topological properties of the regions and their boundaries, in particular whether boundary fractal dimension is greater than one. The supplied summary uses Hausdorff dimension, suggesting $\dim_{\mathrm H}\partial C_i>1$, but the excerpt does not state a typical-region versus every-region quantifier or a dimension convention.
  2. Determine the region area distribution. A region chosen by a typical capital and the region containing a typical location in the plane need not have the same law; sampling and scale normalization must be specified.
  3. Determine the structural law of the capital-city adjacency network: vertices are the capitals and edges correspond to the source's region-adjacency relation. Is the abstract graph planar? Does its intended geometric drawing have crossings? Abstract planarity and absence of crossings in the straight-segment drawing between capitals are distinct questions.
  4. Determine whether analogues of the Lewis and Aboav--Weaire laws hold. Ordinarily these concern area versus number of neighbors and neighbor-degree correlations, respectively. If $A$ is a sampled region's area, $D$ its degree, and $M$ the average degree of its neighbors, familiar candidate relationships concern ${\mathbb{E}}[A\mid D=d]$ as a linear function of $d$, and ${\mathbb{E}}[M\mid D=d]$ as a constant plus a reciprocal function of $d$. The source gives no exact formulas, coefficients, error criterion, or sampling convention for its requested analogues.
The adjacency rule, existence and finiteness of areas/degrees, dimension quantifiers, self-similar scale and law definitions require the omitted model. None is supplied by replacing the partition with a standard tessellation.

The registry reports that boundary Lebesgue measure zero and a strict Hausdorff dimension bound between one and two have been proved in related cited work, while the other laws remain open. These are supplied progress claims whose exact model and quantifiers must be checked, not new results asserted here.

Short English statement

Analyze the boundaries, region-area distribution, and capital adjacency graph of Aldous's particular random planar partition, including crossings and analogues of the Lewis and Aboav--Weaire correlations. The model and sampling conventions are missing; reported boundary results remain to be checked.
