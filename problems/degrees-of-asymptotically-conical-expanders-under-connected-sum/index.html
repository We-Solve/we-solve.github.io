It does not define this degree, the geometric equation defining the expanders, the relevant moduli space, or the hypotheses under which the degree is defined. Short English statement

The problem asks how the degree associated with asymptotically conical expanders behaves under boundary connected sum, and asks for its value on $D^3\times S^1$. The supplied extract omits the definition of this degree and the required manifold hypotheses, so both questions remain conditional on recovering that context.
