Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.49

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Use the conventional normalized univalent class $S$ of injective holomorphic functions on $\mathbb D$ with $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$. For a subset $\mathcal C$ of the real vector space of holomorphic functions, $f\in\mathcal C$ is an extreme point if $f=tg+(1-t)h$, with $g,h\in\mathcal C$ and $0<t<1$, implies $g=h=f$. The source does not specify whether it instead asks for extreme points of the closed convex hull of each class; taking that hull is an additional operation, conventionally with locally uniform convergence.

Problem (D. A. Brannan). Determine the extreme points for all four listed classes:

  • Basilevič functions, referenced to Problem 6.48. That local record, permanent ID $2306048$, gives the one-parameter family $B(\alpha)$, $\alpha>0$, by the extracted formula \[ f(z)=\left[\int_0^z p(t)s(t)^{\alpha}t^{-1}\,dt\right]^{1/\alpha}, \] where $p(0)=1$, $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, and $s(z)=z+\cdots$ is starlike. The formula and normalization need checking: formally its leading coefficient is $\alpha^{-1/\alpha}$, rather than $1$, absent an additional normalization factor. No factor or branch convention is silently corrected.
  • $S^*(\alpha)$ as defined in that same referenced record: the strongly starlike family, $0<\alpha<1$, satisfying \[ \left|\arg\frac{zf'(z)}{f(z)}\right|<\frac{\alpha\pi}{2}\qquad(z\in\mathbb D), \] with the removable value at $0$ and the argument near $1$ understood. This is not the different condition $\operatorname{Re}(zf'/f)>\alpha$.
  • Close-to-convex functions of order $\alpha$, $0<\alpha<1$. The source supplies no definition of this order convention; no angular or real-part condition is selected in its place.
  • Functions of boundary rotation $k\pi$, $2<k<4$. For context, the usual bounded-boundary-rotation class of normalized locally univalent functions is described by \[ \sup_{0<r<1}\int_0^{2\pi}\left|\operatorname{Re}\left(1+\frac{zf''(z)}{f'(z)}\right)\right|\,d\theta\leq k\pi, \qquad z=re^{i\theta}. \] The source does not state whether its rotation phrase means this upper bound or exact equality.
The supplied summary reports partial results but no complete resolution located as of its 2018-based triage.

Short English statement

Classify extreme functions in the four specified univalent-function families. The local cross-reference identifies strongly starlike functions, but normalization, close-to-convex order, boundary-rotation, and convex-hull conventions remain incomplete.
