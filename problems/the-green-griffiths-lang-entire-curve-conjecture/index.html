The Green–Griffiths–Lang Entire-Curve Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An entire curve in a complex projective variety $X$ means a holomorphic map $f\colon{\mathbb{C}}\to X^{\mathrm{an}}$, where $X^{\mathrm{an}}$ denotes the associated complex analytic space. For a smooth complex projective variety of dimension $n$, being of general type means that its canonical bundle $K_X$ is big, equivalently that its Kodaira dimension satisfies $\kappa(X)=n$. The supplied record does not specify smoothness or a convention for general type when $X$ is singular; the preceding definition therefore only explains the smooth case and does not resolve the singular case.

The supplied Green--Griffiths--Lang entire-curve conjecture asks whether, for every complex projective variety $X$ of general type, there exists a proper closed algebraic subset $Y\subsetneq X$ such that \[ \forall f\colon{\mathbb{C}}\to X^{\mathrm{an}}\text{ holomorphic and nonconstant}, \qquad f({\mathbb{C}})\subseteq Y^{\mathrm{an}}. \] Here one subset $Y$, depending on $X$, must contain the images of all such curves simultaneously. The displayed formulation uses "closed algebraic subset" to express the proposed exceptional locus; whether the source intends any further restrictions on its use of "subvariety" is unspecified. The cleaned statement explicitly requires $Y$ to be proper, whereas the supplied original wording says only that $Y$ is contained in $X$. Properness is essential to the question and is retained from the cleaned statement. The ground field ${\mathbb{C}}$ is inferred from the complex entire-curve setting; it is not separately stated in the record.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every complex projective variety of general type has a single proper algebraic exceptional locus containing every nonconstant entire curve. The supplied formulation leaves the treatment of singular varieties unspecified.
