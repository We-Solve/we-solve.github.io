Math-4582

Character Fields of Odd-Degree Representations

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ denote the set of characters of irreducible finite-dimensional complex representations of $G$. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, its degree is $\chi(1)$. Define \[ \operatorname{Irr}_{2'}(G)=\{\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G):2\nmid\chi(1)\}, \qquad {\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)={\mathbb{Q}}\left(\chi(g):g\in G\right)\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}. \] Thus ${\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)$ is the field generated over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ by all values of $\chi$.

Determine the collection of field extensions \[ F_2=\left\{{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)/{\mathbb{Q}}:\ G\text{ is a finite group},\ \chi\in\operatorname{Irr}_{2'}(G)\right\}. \] Here ${\mathbb{Q}}(\chi)/{\mathbb{Q}}$ denotes a field extension, not a quotient. The quantifiers range over all finite groups and all their irreducible complex characters of odd degree.

The supplied dated curation states that the classification for $p=2$ has been obtained, although the registry labels this record partially solved. Its further discussion of conjectural classifications for odd primes concerns a broader problem and does not change the target $F_2$. No classification theorem is supplied in the record, so none is asserted here.

Short English statement

Determine exactly which fields generated by character values occur for irreducible complex characters of odd degree, allowing every finite group. The supplied curation reports that this classification is known for $p=2$; that literature assessment has not been independently verified.
