A parametric audit of a refuted logarithmic outdegree condition

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The source's Conjecture 6.25 asserts that a digraph $D$ on $n$ vertices satisfying \[ \sum_{v\in V(D)}\log\left(1+\frac1{d_D^+(v)}\right) \geq n\log\left(1+\frac1r\right) \] has a directed cycle of length at most $\lceil n/r\rceil$. The supplied verification confirms a ceiling on page 10 of Blair D. Sullivan's 2006 survey. The inequality direction is retained literally. The logarithms are undefined at outdegree zero, so a finite-valued formulation must require positive outdegrees; treating a zero-degree term as infinity would give an immediate defect for acyclic graphs. The aid's counterexamples have all outdegrees positive.

The source itself prints a counterexample: start with a transitive tournament on $n-1$ vertices, remove its arc from the maximum-outdegree vertex to the sink, and replace it by an oppositely directed path of length two. The aid verifies, for every $n\geq4$, the exact product \[ P(D):=\prod_v\left(1+\frac1{d_D^+(v)}\right) =\frac{4(n-2)^2}{n-3}, \] and the complete directed cycle-length spectrum $\{4,5,\ldots,n\}$. It reports that this graph refutes the proposed implication exactly for positive integer parameters $r\geq\lceil n/3\rceil$.

More generally, let $T_{m,q}$ be obtained from a transitive tournament on ordered vertices $1,\ldots,m$, $m\geq3$, by removing $1\to m$ and inserting a reversed $q$-arc path from $m$ to $1$, with new internal vertices, $q\geq2$. Then $N=m+q-1$ and the aid's formulas are \[ P(T_{m,q})=\frac{2^q(m-1)^2}{m-2},\qquad \{\text{directed cycle lengths}\}=\{q+2,\ldots,N\}. \] Writing $P=2^q(m-1)^2/(m-2)$, the exact two-threshold counterexample criterion is \[ r\geq\max\left\{ \left\lceil\frac N{q+1}\right\rceil, \left\lceil\frac1{P^{1/N}-1}\right\rceil \right\},\qquad r\in{\mathbb{N}}. \] The second threshold is precisely the displayed logarithmic hypothesis; the first makes its ceiling conclusion strictly shorter than the actual girth. Failure of one threshold alone is not a counterexample to the implication.

The summary additionally reports a valid factor-two logarithmic replacement derived from a 2023 nonuniform-degree theorem. Its exact inequality, hypotheses, rounding convention and bibliographic reference are absent. No replacement theorem is guessed from that description, and the source's sign is not silently reversed. The literal conjecture is refuted independently by the explicit positive-outdegree families above.

Short English statement

The source already includes a counterexample. The aid computes its full cycle spectrum and exactly when it violates the ceiling bound, then generalizes the construction using a longer reverse path. It also records a zero-outdegree domain problem; an incompletely specified factor-two replacement is not reconstructed as a theorem.
