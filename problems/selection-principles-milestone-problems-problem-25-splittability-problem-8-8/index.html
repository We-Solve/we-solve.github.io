Selection Principles Milestone Problems — Problem 25 ([splittability, Problem 8.8])

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied question asks about three splitting properties. Its statement does not specify the ambient class of spaces or define the cover classes. The following is an explicit conventional interpretation, conditional on confirming those conventions in the source.

Let $X$ be a topological space. For this interpretation, a cover is a countably infinite family $\mathcal U$ of open subsets of $X$ such that $\bigcup\mathcal U=X$ and $X\notin\mathcal U$. Let $\Omega(X)$ consist of those covers $\mathcal U$ for which \[ \forall F\subseteq X\;\left(F\text{ finite}\Longrightarrow \exists U\in\mathcal U\;(F\subseteq U)\right). \] A cover $\mathcal U$ is large if every point belongs to infinitely many members of $\mathcal U$. Let $\mathrm T(X)$ consist of the large covers satisfying, for every $x,y\in X$, \[ \left|\{U\in\mathcal U:x\in U,\ y\notin U\}\right|<\infty \quad\text{or}\quad \left|\{U\in\mathcal U:y\in U,\ x\notin U\}\right|<\infty. \] For cover classes $\mathcal A(X)$ and $\mathcal B(X)$, say that $X$ satisfies $\mathsf{Split}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)$ if every $\mathcal U\in\mathcal A(X)$ admits a partition \[ \mathcal U=\mathcal U_0\mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathcal U_1, \qquad \mathcal U_0,\mathcal U_1\in\mathcal B(X). \]

For each of the three properties \[ P\in\{\mathsf{Split}(\Omega,\Omega),\ \mathsf{Split}(\Omega,\mathrm T),\ \mathsf{Split}(\mathrm T,\mathrm T)\}, \] the question is whether the following preservation assertion holds: \[ \forall X\in\mathcal C\;\forall n\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 1}, \qquad X\models P\ \Longrightarrow\ X^n\models P, \] where $X^n$ carries the product topology and $\mathcal C$ denotes the intended ambient class of spaces. The supplied record leaves $\mathcal C$ unspecified and does not establish the countability and openness conventions used above. Consequently, this is a conditional formalization rather than a fully source-verified formulation. Each of the three preservation assertions is a separate target; the wording does not assert that any one of them holds.

Short English statement

For each of the three stated splitting properties, ask whether every positive finite Cartesian power of a space with that property also has it. The intended class of spaces and the precise cover conventions require source verification. The registry labels the problem open; that label is supplied metadata, not an independently verified status assessment.
