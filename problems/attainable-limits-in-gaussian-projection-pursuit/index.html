Math-15234

Attainable Limits in Gaussian Projection Pursuit

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\gamma$ denote the dimension-to-sample ratio in the supplied record, with $0\leq\gamma<1$. Write $\mathcal P({\mathbb{R}})$ for the Borel probability measures on ${\mathbb{R}}$, and let $\mathcal A_\gamma\subseteq\mathcal P({\mathbb{R}})$ denote the intended class of attainable limiting empirical distributions of projections of structureless Gaussian data. The source record does not define this class precisely.

For $q>0$, $N(0,q)$ denotes the centered Gaussian probability law with variance $q$. The supplied conjecture asks whether every $\mu\in\mathcal A_\gamma$ admits a decomposition of the form \[ \mu=(1-\eta)N(0,q)+\eta\nu, \qquad q<(1+\sqrt\gamma)^2, \qquad 0\leq\eta\leq1, \qquad \nu\in\mathcal P({\mathbb{R}}), \] where $\nu$ is described as a non-Gaussian component when it has positive weight, and $\eta$ is described as possibly small. Equality here means equality of probability measures: for every Borel set $B\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$, \[ \mu(B)=(1-\eta)N(0,q)(B)+\eta\nu(B). \] The absent component corresponds to $\eta=0$.

Unresolved qualifications. This displays the intended decomposition, but the supplied record does not specify a complete quantified conjecture. It gives no numerical meaning to "small," no requirement excluding $\eta=1$, and no rule governing dependence of the component weight on $\gamma$ or $\mu$. Allowing $\eta=1$ makes the decomposition automatic for any non-Gaussian $\mu$, by taking $\nu=\mu$. It is also unclear whether variance zero is permitted; if it is, $N(0,0)$ would mean the point mass at zero. The data normalization, asymptotic regime, admissible projection directions, dependence of directions on the observations, and mode of convergence defining $\mathcal A_\gamma$ must be specified before the conjecture has a unique mathematical meaning. The strict upper bound on $q$ is retained exactly as supplied.

Short English statement

For Gaussian data with dimension-to-sample ratio $\gamma<1$, the supplied conjecture proposes that every attainable limiting projection distribution consists of a centered Gaussian component of variance below $(1+\sqrt\gamma)^2$, together with a possibly small non-Gaussian component. The attainable class and the permitted component weights are unspecified, so this remains a statement-only draft requiring mathematical review.
