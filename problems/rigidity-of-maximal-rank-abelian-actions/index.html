Rigidity of Maximal Rank Abelian Actions

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not determine a self-contained conjecture. It concerns abelian actions described as having maximal rank and Lyapunov exponents in general position, but does not specify the acting group, the manifold, the invariant measure, the regularity, or the meanings of these two hypotheses.

For terminology only, an action of an abelian group $G$ on a manifold $M$ is a homomorphism $\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Diff}(M)$, when a differentiable action is intended. Write $d=\dim M$. If a probability measure $\mu$ on $M$ is invariant under every $\alpha(g)$, then $h_\mu(\alpha(g))$ denotes the measure-theoretic entropy of the transformation $\alpha(g)$. When the derivative and integrability hypotheses needed for Lyapunov theory hold, Lyapunov exponents describe asymptotic exponential growth rates of tangent vectors under derivatives of the action. These conventions explain possible terminology; the source does not supply the hypotheses needed to adopt this framework as its intended setting. It also does not specify whether its entropies are measure-theoretic or topological.

The retained source formulation asks, for maximal-rank abelian actions with Lyapunov exponents in general position:

  1. Which values can the entropies and Lyapunov exponents take?
  2. Are these quantities logarithms of algebraic numbers, with algebraic degrees related to $d$? The algebraic numbers, the meaning of the degree relation, and the normalization of the quantities are unspecified.
  3. Are there finitely many ergodic components? The invariant measure and whether this concerns the full action or individual transformations are unspecified.
  4. Are the actions measurably conjugate to algebraic actions? For specified probability-preserving $G$-actions $\alpha$ and $\beta$, measurable conjugacy means an isomorphism of their probability spaces modulo null sets that intertwines $\alpha(g)$ and $\beta(g)$ almost everywhere for every $g\in G$. The source does not specify the algebraic models or their measures.

These questions are retained as an incomplete source formulation, not asserted as a universal conjecture. The supplied verification field says that the report immediately states a theorem resolving the questions in its particular setting, and marks the statement as unrecoverable, with no separate clean statement promoted. The supplied open label and literature assessment do not resolve this qualification. Recovering the theorem's exact hypotheses and conclusions is necessary before a precise independent problem can be stated.

Short English statement

The record asks about possible entropy and Lyapunov values, algebraicity, finitely many ergodic components, and measurable algebraic models for maximal-rank abelian actions. Essential hypotheses are missing. Although the supplied status is open, the supplied verification says that a theorem immediately resolves the questions in the report's setting.
