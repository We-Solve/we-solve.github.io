Quantization of Quasi-Hamiltonian Moduli Spaces

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write \[ M:=M_G(\Sigma;C_1,\ldots,C_r) \] for the quasi-Hamiltonian $G$-space named in the supplied record. A quasi-Hamiltonian structure involves a $G$-action, an invariant two-form, and a group-valued moment map $\Phi:M\to G$, subject to the quasi-Hamiltonian axioms. Here pre-quantized means that a compatible prequantization has been supplied; it does not assert that every such space admits one. Write $Q(M)$ for the quantization requested by the source.

Problem as recorded. Given a pre-quantized quasi-Hamiltonian $G$-space $M_G(\Sigma;C_1,\ldots,C_r)$, compute \[ Q\left(M_G(\Sigma;C_1,\ldots,C_r)\right). \] The request particularly concerns non-simply connected $G$, for which the source notes obstructions to prequantization. The computation is requested only for data equipped with a prequantization; the record does not assert that those obstructions always vanish.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not specify the admissible class of groups $G$, the topology, orientation, compactness, or boundary conditions of $\Sigma$, the domain of $r$, or the meaning and admissibility conditions of the parameters $C_i$. In particular, interpreting the $C_i$ as conjugacy classes prescribing boundary holonomy would require information absent from this record. It also does not define the construction of $M_G(\Sigma;C_1,\ldots,C_r)$, the normalization and level of its prequantization, or the definition and target of $Q$. Thus the displayed request preserves the source formulation, but a fully specified mathematical computation requires these additional data. No particular moduli-space construction, quantization convention, or range of boundary cases is selected here.

Short English statement

Compute the quantization of the source-designated pre-quantized quasi-Hamiltonian moduli space, particularly when $G$ is not simply connected. The group, surface, boundary data, prequantization conventions, and meaning of quantization require clarification before this becomes a fully specified problem.
