Math-4994

The Weak Gordon–Luecke Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed oriented $3$-manifold is a compact oriented $3$-manifold without boundary. Irreducibility means that every embedded $2$-sphere, in the manifold category intended by the source, bounds an embedded $3$-ball. A knot $K\subset Y$ is an embedded circle. Its complement $Y\setminus K$ carries the orientation induced from $Y$. Two knots are equivalent here if some orientation-preserving self-homeomorphism of $Y$ takes one onto the other; no orientation on the knots themselves is specified.

The supplied standalone formulation of the weak Gordon--Luecke conjecture is the following. For every closed oriented irreducible $3$-manifold $Y$ and every pair of knots $K_1,K_2\subset Y$, neither of which is a rational unknot in a lens space, assert that \[ \begin{aligned} &\exists\text{ an orientation-preserving homeomorphism } h:Y\setminus K_1\longrightarrow Y\setminus K_2\\ &\qquad\Longleftrightarrow \exists\text{ an orientation-preserving homeomorphism } F:Y\longrightarrow Y\text{ with }F(K_1)=K_2. \end{aligned} \] This is an existence statement: it does not require every given complement homeomorphism $h$ to extend over $Y$. The complements displayed here are the complements of the embedded circles, as written in the supplied statement; no boundary parametrization or peripheral marking is specified.

The exceptional phrase "rational unknot in a lens space" is retained verbatim because the supplied record does not define that class or specify its lens-space conventions. The original formulation also refers to "the usual nonexceptional hypotheses" without enumerating them. The standalone version supplies the displayed exception, but the record alone does not establish that it exhausts those hypotheses. Consequently, the intended exceptional class and the complete hypothesis list require source review before this can be regarded as a fully specified mathematical formulation.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that, subject to its exceptional-knot restriction, two knots in the same closed oriented irreducible $3$-manifold have orientation-preservingly homeomorphic complements exactly when an orientation-preserving homeomorphism of the ambient manifold carries one knot to the other. The exceptional class and the original reference to further nonexceptional hypotheses remain insufficiently specified.
