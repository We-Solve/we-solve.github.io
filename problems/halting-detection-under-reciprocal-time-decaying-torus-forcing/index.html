Halting detection under reciprocal-time decaying torus forcing

OpenAI's claim

Area-changing prefix instructions: The manuscript claims complete-domain realization of finite prefix instructions by smooth incompressible motions that retain discarded information; on the flat torus, the resulting mean-zero force detects halting from rest and repeats after loading.

Scalar potentials and slow-clock computation: The paper claims compactly supported forced fluid computation with a fixed particle and half-space detector, with time derivatives decaying at the stated inverse-time rates; other realizations include invariant-plane instructions and velocity-field detection under a companion diffusion statement.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Mathematical claim 1.
For the incompressible system $\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta u+f$, $\nabla\cdot u=0$, $u(0)=0$, choose viscosity $\nu>0$, whose value is computable. An effectively computable smooth general body force on $\mathbb T^3$, with spatial mean zero, satisfies \[\|\partial_t^kD_x^\alpha f(t)\|_\infty\le C_{k,\alpha}(1+t)^{-1}\quad(k\ge0).\] The velocity starts from rest and exists smoothly for all times; it is the unique classical periodic solution, its kinetic energy stays bounded and its pressure has spatial mean zero. For the label $y_*=(1/2,1/2,1/4)$ and region $O=\{1/2<x_1<1,\ 0<x_3<1/8\}$, particle entry at some time is equivalent to halting of the machine. The construction records every computation step through the first halt, or forever if it never halts.
Mathematical claim 2.
For the incompressible system $\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta u+f$, $\nabla\cdot u=0$, $u(0)=0$, choose viscosity $\nu>0$, whose value is computable. The force is smooth and effectively constructed on $\mathbb T^3$, with spatial mean zero. Its corresponding velocity is unique and globally smooth, and both fields satisfy \[\|\partial_t^jD_x^\alpha f(t)\|_\infty+\|\partial_t^jD_x^\alpha u(t)\|_\infty\le C_{j,\alpha}(1+t)^{-1-j}\quad(j\ge0).\] For initial particle position $(1/4,1/2,1/2)$, the event of entering $\{1/2<x_1<1\}$ is equivalent to halting of the supplied machine. A first force choice has zero pressure; its solenoidal projection gives a divergence-free choice with unchanged velocity and altered normalized pressure. Only loading depends on the input for a fixed machine.

Outline of the argument

Area-changing prefix instructions: Gapped radix codes turn prefix cylinders into separated closed rectangles. Local curl motions and private heights implement the planar instruction, while reciprocal normal strain compensates its area change. A history invariant and initialization segment identify the simulated state, and horizontal interpolation keeps the detector equivalence valid during the entire instruction period.

Scalar potentials and slow-clock computation: Three scalar potentials successively lift a coded rectangle, apply its rule, and lower the image. A logarithmic time change remains onto an unbounded computational-time interval while reducing all time derivatives. Area-changing prefix maps are balanced by a normal component, and the alternative planar processor provides the stated stationary, periodic, and diffusion-based detector variants.
