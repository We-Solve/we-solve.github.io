Open Problems on Central Simple Algebras — Problem 16

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F$ be a field, let $n\geq 1$ be an integer, and let $p$ be a prime. Write $\operatorname{PGL}_n=\operatorname{GL}_n/\mathbb{G}_m$ over $F$. For every field extension $K/F$, its torsors are classified by central simple $K$-algebras of degree $n$. Here a central simple $K$-algebra is a finite-dimensional associative unital algebra with center $K$ and no nonzero proper two-sided ideals; its degree is $\sqrt{\dim_K A}$.

For such an algebra $A$, define \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(A)=\min\left\{\operatorname{trdeg}_F E:\ F\subseteq E\subseteq K,\quad A\simeq B\otimes_E K\text{ for a central simple }E\text{-algebra }B\text{ of degree }n\right\}. \] Define its essential $p$-dimension by \[ \operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(A)=\min_{\substack{K'/K\text{ finite}\\p\nmid[K':K]}}\operatorname{ed}_F(A\otimes_K K'). \] The essential dimension and essential $p$-dimension of $\operatorname{PGL}_n$ are \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{PGL}_n)=\sup_{\substack{K/F\text{ a field extension}\\A\text{ central simple over }K\\\deg A=n}}\operatorname{ed}_F(A), \qquad \operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(\operatorname{PGL}_n)=\sup_{\substack{K/F\text{ a field extension}\\A\text{ central simple over }K\\\deg A=n}}\operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(A). \] Problem. Determine $\operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{PGL}_n)$ and $\operatorname{ed}_{F,p}(\operatorname{PGL}_n)$ as functions of the base field $F$, the integer $n$, and, for the latter invariant, the prime $p$. In particular, determine \[ \operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{PGL}_4)\quad\text{when }\operatorname{char}F=2, \qquad\text{and}\qquad \operatorname{ed}_F(\operatorname{PGL}_5). \] The supplied statement gives no general restrictions on $F$, on its characteristic, or on $p$; in particular, it does not specify a characteristic for the degree-five question. These parameters are therefore retained explicitly, without an assertion that the answers are independent of them. The supplied registry labels this problem open; that label is not an independently verified assessment of its present status.

Short English statement

Determine how many algebraically independent parameters over a base field $F$ are needed to define arbitrary central simple algebras of degree $n$, both directly and after allowing finite extensions of degree prime to $p$. The source specifically asks for degree $4$ in characteristic $2$ and degree $5$, with no characteristic restriction stated for the latter.
