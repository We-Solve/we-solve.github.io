Math-1895

Isoperimetric profile and curvature at infinity

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a metric space $(X,d)$ with a Markov kernel $P$, let $P_x=P(x,\cdot)$ have finite first moment. Its coarse Ricci curvature is \[ \kappa(x,y)=1-\frac{W_1(P_x,P_y)}{d(x,y)}\qquad(x\ne y), \] where $W_1$ is the infimum of $\int d(u,v)\,d\pi(u,v)$ over couplings of the two measures. Fix $o\in X$ and set \[ \kappa_R=\inf\{\kappa(x,y):x,y\in B(o,R),\ x\ne y\}. \] The source hypotheses are \[ \inf_{x\ne y}\kappa(x,y)=0,\qquad \kappa_R>0\text{ for every finite }R, \] with an empty pair set treated separately. Thus positive lower bounds on bounded balls deteriorate toward the global zero infimum as radius grows.

Question (Yann Ollivier). Is there a systematic relation between the rate of this deterioration at infinity and the isoperimetric profile? Analyze the $M/M/k$ queue as an example. The source supplies no particular comparison formula or asserted decay law.

The isoperimetric profile needs additional measure and boundary conventions. For example, if $P$ has an invariant probability $\nu$, one Markov-boundary profile is \[ I(v)=\inf_{\nu(A)=v}\int_A P(x,X\setminus A)\,d\nu(x), \] for attainable masses $v$. Metric boundary measure, conductance profiles, and counting-measure profiles are different possible choices. This formula is explanatory, since invariance, reversibility, the reference measure, and the intended profile are absent from the excerpt.

An $M/M/k$ queue customarily has state $q\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, arrival rate $\lambda>0$, $k\geq1$ servers, and service rate $\mu>0$ per busy server. Its generator has birth rate $\lambda$ and death rate $\mu\min(q,k)$, with no death from state $0$. If stationary probability is needed, the usual stability condition is $\lambda<k\mu$. To turn this example into coarse Ricci data one must also specify a metric and a transition kernel or semigroup time scale; these choices and parameters are not given by the source.

The supplied summary says qualitative curvature/volume/isoperimetry intuition has support from some comparison results but reports no established general curvature-decay/profile theorem. No particular result is cited, and that status remains an unverified supplied assessment.

Short English statement

When coarse Ricci curvature has positive lower bounds on every bounded ball but global infimum zero, relate its decay with radius to isoperimetry, using the $M/M/k$ queue as a test case. The metric, Markov time scale, reference measure, and precise profile or comparison bound are unspecified.
