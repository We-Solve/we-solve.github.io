Coupling and Learning Strategies for Climate Supermodels

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
A climate supermodel couples several imperfect dynamical models through selected state-variable connections and learns or chooses those connections from observations. Its joint resolved trajectory is intended to improve agreement with the observed climate. The design choices include graph structure, coupling terms, observation frequency, training data, and treatment of slow and fast processes. Physical balances and conserved quantities restrict which couplings are admissible.
Source scope and qualifications.
Retain all source design questions: connection form and count, variables and frequency, data needs and learning method, slow ocean scales, reality outside the model class, changing-climate prediction, conservation constraints, and choosing connections from physical insight. The report's low-dimensional numerical demonstrations do not answer this whole program.
Catalogue mathematical target.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For climate supermodels, which connections and variables should be used, how much data and what learning strategies are needed, how should slow oceanic scales and changing climates be handled, and what restrictions follow from model class, balances, conservation laws, or physical insight?
Saved literature context.
The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: Supermodeling and synchronization-based learning answer several questions experimentally, including learned connections and climate response, but no general theory settles the listed design, conservation, data, and changing-climate questions. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.

Short English statement

Determine how to connect and train imperfect climate models while respecting physical laws and coping with slow scales and climate change.
