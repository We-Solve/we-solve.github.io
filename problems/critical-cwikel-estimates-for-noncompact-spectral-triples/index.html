Math-13925

Critical Cwikel Estimates for Noncompact Spectral Triples

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(A,H,D)$ be a locally compact spectral triple, with $*$-representation $\pi:A\to\mathcal B(H)$ and self-adjoint operator $D$. For a compact operator $T$ on $H$, let $\mu_n(T)$, $n\geq 1$, denote its singular values in nonincreasing order. For $p>0$, define the weak Schatten class by \[ \mathcal L_{p,\infty}(H)=\left\{T\text{ compact}:\sup_{n\geq1}n^{1/p}\mu_n(T)<\infty\right\}. \] Thus membership expresses the singular-value bound $\mu_n(T)=O(n^{-1/p})$.

The source's dimension terminology appears to mean that, for a candidate parameter $d>0$, a compact spectral triple is called $d$-dimensional when \[ (1+D^2)^{-d/(2p)}\in\mathcal L_{p,\infty}(H) \qquad\text{for every }p>0, \] and a locally compact spectral triple is called $d$-dimensional when \[ \pi(a)(1+D^2)^{-d/(2p)}\in\mathcal L_{p,\infty}(H) \qquad\text{for every }p>0\text{ and every }a\in A. \] The exponent $-d/(2p)$ is a reconstruction of damaged mathematical typography in the supplied original statement and requires comparison with the report. These conditions are recorded as the source's terminology; the record does not specify a minimality or sharpness condition that would single out an exact dimension.

Requested endpoint problem. Establish critical Cwikel-type estimates at $p=2$ for noncompact spectral triples, including estimates for the Euclidean operator \[ T_f=M_f(1-\Delta)^{-d/4}. \] Here, in the Euclidean interpretation, $d$ is a positive integer, $H=L^2({\mathbb{R}}^d)$, $M_f$ denotes multiplication by a measurable function $f$, and $\Delta=\sum_{j=1}^d\partial_j^2$ is taken with the nonpositive self-adjoint convention. The power of $1-\Delta$ is defined by spectral calculus. Initially, the product is defined on the natural domain \[ \left\{u\in L^2({\mathbb{R}}^d):f(1-\Delta)^{-d/4}u\in L^2({\mathbb{R}}^d)\right\}; \] its boundedness or compactness requires hypotheses on $f$. The Euclidean space and Laplacian sign convention make the displayed expression explicit; the supplied record does not itself state them in this detail.

Unresolved formulation. The record does not specify the admissible class of noncompact spectral triples, the function space and norm for $f$, the endpoint operator ideal or singular-value bound, or the allowed dependence of constants. Consequently, it does not determine a single quantified endpoint inequality. In particular, no estimate for every measurable $f$, or for every locally compact spectral triple, is asserted here. The weak Schatten conditions above explain the dimension motivation but do not supply the missing endpoint hypotheses or conclusion.

The original discussion asks how to compute spectral-triple dimension, describes Cwikel-type estimates as a tool, and distinguishes the pre-critical range $p>2$, the post-critical range $p<2$, and the difficult critical case $p=2$. It also connects the Euclidean operators to Cwikel--Lieb--Rozenblum inequalities for numbers of bound states, described there as eigenvalues outside the essential spectrum, of Schrödinger operators. No specific Schrödinger operator or counting inequality is supplied.

Short English statement

The requested problem concerns critical $p=2$ Cwikel-type estimates used to study the dimension of noncompact spectral triples, especially estimates for $M_f(1-\Delta)^{-d/4}$ in Euclidean space. The supplied record omits the function spaces, endpoint bound, and structural hypotheses needed for a precise theorem. Its open status and verification claims are supplied curation, not independently verified facts.
