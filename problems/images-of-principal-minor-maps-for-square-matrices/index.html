Math-6638

Images of Principal Minor Maps for Square Matrices

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a positive integer $n$, let $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$. For $A\in{\mathbb{C}}^{n\times n}$ and $S\subseteq[n]$, let $A[S,S]$ denote the principal submatrix indexed by $S$, and put $p_S(A)=\det A[S,S]$, with $p_{\varnothing}(A)=1$. Define the principal-minor map by \[ \Phi_n:{\mathbb{C}}^{n\times n}\longrightarrow H_n:=\{(x_S)_{S\subseteq[n]}\in{\mathbb{C}}^{2^n}:x_{\varnothing}=1\},\qquad A\longmapsto(p_S(A))_{S\subseteq[n]}. \] Including the empty principal minor is a coordinate convention; deleting that fixed coordinate identifies $H_n$ with ${\mathbb{C}}^{2^n-1}$. The matrices here are arbitrary complex square matrices, with no symmetry or Hermitian assumption.

The first question is to determine equations describing $\Phi_5({\mathbb{C}}^{5\times5})$. Under the literal interpretation as an exact description by polynomial equations, this asks for a family \[ \mathcal F_5\subseteq{\mathbb{C}}[x_S:\varnothing\ne S\subseteq[5]] \] such that, for every $x\in H_5$, \[ \left(\exists A\in{\mathbb{C}}^{5\times5}\;\forall S\subseteq[5],\ x_S=\det A[S,S]\right) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \left(\forall f\in\mathcal F_5,\ f((x_S)_{S\ne\varnothing})=0\right). \] The existence of such an exact description is not assumed. The supplied record does not specify whether "image" instead means its Zariski closure; equations defining that closure would be a different target unless equality with the image is established.

The second question asks whether, for general positive integers $n$, equations describing the principal-minor image can be obtained using \[ G_n=\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})^n\rtimes S_n, \] where $S_n$ permutes the $n$ factors, together with what the original passage calls "some increasing combinatorial structures." The cleaned statement calls these "compatible combinatorial structures." The record supplies neither the relevant representation or ambient space for the group action, nor the initial equations, nor a definition of these structures or their compatibility. Thus this part remains a qualified research question, rather than a fully specified equation-generation assertion. In particular, an affine, homogeneous, or projective interpretation has not been selected.

The original passage also asks "Does the converse hold?" Its antecedent is absent from the supplied record, so that converse cannot be formulated precisely here.

Short English statement

Determine equations describing the collections of principal minors of arbitrary complex $5\times5$ matrices, and investigate a construction for general matrix size using $\operatorname{SL}_2({\mathbb{C}})^n\rtimes S_n$ and additional combinatorial structures. The record leaves unresolved whether the intended target is the exact image or its Zariski closure, how the group acts, which combinatorial structures are intended, and which converse is being asked about.
