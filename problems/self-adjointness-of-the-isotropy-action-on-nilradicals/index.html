Math-14441

Self-Adjointness of the Isotropy Action on Nilradicals

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Put $\mathfrak n=\operatorname{Lie}(N)$, and write \[ \rho(u)=\operatorname{Ad}(u)|_{\mathfrak n},\qquad H=\rho(U). \] This notation presupposes an ambient Lie group in which the adjoint action is defined and satisfies $\operatorname{Ad}(u)\mathfrak n=\mathfrak n$ for every $u\in U$. The supplied record does not specify that ambient group or the hypotheses on $U$ and $N$.

To interpret transpose in the usual real Riemannian setting, one must also specify a positive-definite inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ on the finite-dimensional real vector space $\mathfrak n$. For such a specified inner product, the transpose $A^{\mathsf T}$ of an endomorphism $A$ is characterized by \[ \langle Ax,y\rangle=\langle x,A^{\mathsf T}y\rangle \qquad(x,y\in\mathfrak n). \] The choice of real setting and inner product here explains the usual meaning of the question; neither is explicitly specified in the supplied statement.

Question, with unresolved source hypotheses. For the groups and inner product intended by the source, is the restricted adjoint image invariant under transpose, that is, \[ H^{\mathsf T}:=\{A^{\mathsf T}:A\in H\}=H? \] Equivalently, is it true that \[ \forall u\in U\;\exists v\in U:\qquad \left(\operatorname{Ad}(u)|_{\mathfrak n}\right)^{\mathsf T} =\operatorname{Ad}(v)|_{\mathfrak n}? \] Here "self-adjoint action" means invariance of the image under transpose. It does not require each image element to equal its own transpose, and "closed under transpose" does not assert topological closedness.

The supplied background describes $\mathfrak n$ as a nilradical and places this question in work toward the strong Alekseevskii conjecture. It does not identify the Lie algebra whose nilradical is intended, the relevant geometric assumptions, or the metric determining transpose. Thus the displayed condition gives the precise property being asked about, but the intended class of objects over which it is to hold cannot be recovered from this record. In particular, the record does not determine whether the inner product is fixed by the geometry or is to be chosen existentially. The supplied status is open; this draft does not independently verify that status.

Short English statement

Does transposing any transformation in the restricted adjoint image of $U$ on $\operatorname{Lie}(N)$ produce another transformation in the same image? The ambient groups, geometric hypotheses, and inner product defining transpose are missing, so the intended scope remains unresolved. The supplied background presents this as a structural question toward the strong Alekseevskii conjecture.
