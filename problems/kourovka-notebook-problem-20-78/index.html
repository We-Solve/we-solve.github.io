Kourovka Notebook Problem 20.78

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ denote its set of irreducible complex characters. For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$, its degree is $\chi(1)$, and its kernel is \[ \ker\chi=\{g\in G:\chi(g)=\chi(1)\}. \] Define its codegree and the set of character codegrees of $G$ by \[ \operatorname{cod}(\chi)=\frac{|G:\ker\chi|}{\chi(1)}, \qquad \operatorname{Cod}(G)=\{\operatorname{cod}(\chi):\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)\}. \] Conjecture (G. Qian). For every finite group $G$ and every positive integer $m$, if $G$ contains an element of order $m$, then there exists $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ such that \[ m\mid\operatorname{cod}(\chi). \] Equivalently, under this hypothesis there exist $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ and a positive integer $k$ such that \[ |G:\ker\chi|=km\chi(1). \] Supplied qualifications. The record reports that the conjecture has been proved when $m$ is a prime power (G. Qian, 2011), when $m$ is square-free (I. M. Isaacs, 2011), when $G$ is solvable (G. Qian, 2021), when $G$ is a symmetric or alternating group (E. Giannelli, 2024), when $G$ is almost simple (S. Y. Madanha, 2023), and when $\mathbf F(G)=1$ (Z. Akhlaghi, E. Pacifici, and L. Sanus, 2024). Here $\mathbf F(G)$ is the Fitting subgroup, the largest nilpotent normal subgroup of $G$; an almost simple group satisfies $S\leq G\leq\operatorname{Aut}(S)$ for some nonabelian finite simple group $S$. These special-case results and the general conjecture's open status are supplied source assessments, not independently verified conclusions.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every element order in a finite group divides the codegree of some irreducible complex character of that group. The supplied record labels the general problem open and lists several proved special cases; those literature assessments require verification.
