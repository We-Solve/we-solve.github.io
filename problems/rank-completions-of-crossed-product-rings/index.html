Math-15771

Rank Completions of Crossed-Product Rings

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\Gamma$ be a countable group. A measure-preserving action $\alpha$ on a measure space $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ assigns to each $g\in\Gamma$ an invertible measurable transformation $\alpha_g$, with measurable inverse, such that $\alpha_e=\operatorname{id}_X$, $\alpha_{gh}=\alpha_g\circ\alpha_h$, and $\mu(\alpha_g^{-1}(E))=\mu(E)$ for every $E\in\Sigma$. The supplied record does not specify the measure spaces allowed, whether transformations are identified modulo null sets, or whether the actions must satisfy additional hypotheses.

For each admissible action, write $R_\alpha$ for the crossed-product ring intended by the source and $S_\alpha$ for its regular closure in the intended ambient ring. A ring $S$ is von Neumann regular if \[ \forall s\in S\;\exists t\in S:\quad s=sts. \] This definition alone does not specify the regular-closure construction: the coefficient ring, ambient ring, and any involution or additional closure requirements must also be given. If $\rho_\alpha$ is the intended rank function on $S_\alpha$, inducing the rank pseudometric \[ d_\alpha(s,t)=\rho_\alpha(s-t), \] then $A_\alpha$ denotes the corresponding completion, after identifying elements at distance zero when necessary. The record does not specify $\rho_\alpha$ or its normalization.

Question, conditional on specifying these constructions. Fix a nonempty class $\mathcal C_\Gamma$ of admissible measure-preserving actions and a specified notion $\mathcal E$ of isomorphism for their completed algebras. Which invariants $I$ of actions are determined by the isomorphism class of $A_\alpha$? Explicitly, for which such invariants does \[ \forall\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal C_\Gamma,\qquad A_\alpha\cong_{\mathcal E}A_\beta \ \Longrightarrow\ I(\alpha)=I(\beta) \] hold? Further, characterize the countable nonamenable groups $\Gamma$ for which the completion is independent of the action, in the sense that \[ \exists B\;\forall\alpha\in\mathcal C_\Gamma,\qquad A_\alpha\cong_{\mathcal E}B. \] In particular, does any countable nonamenable group have this property?

The choices of $\mathcal C_\Gamma$, $R_\alpha$, $S_\alpha$, $\rho_\alpha$, and $\mathcal E$ remain unresolved in the supplied record. Thus these formulas describe the quantifier structure of the supplied question, rather than a uniquely specified mathematical conjecture. In particular, "the same algebra" is not specified to mean an abstract algebra isomorphism, an involution-preserving isomorphism, or an isomorphism preserving rank or other distinguished structure.

Short English statement

The question asks what information about a measure-preserving action of a countable group can be recovered from the rank completion of the regular closure of its crossed-product ring, and which countable nonamenable groups could give isomorphic completions for every admissible action. The admissible actions, ring construction, rank, and meaning of isomorphism require clarification before this becomes a fully specified problem.
