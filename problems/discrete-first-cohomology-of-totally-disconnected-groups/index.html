Math-6801

Discrete First Cohomology of Totally Disconnected Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $E/F$ be a field extension such that $\operatorname{char}(F)=0$ and $\operatorname{trdeg}_F(E)<\infty$. No positive lower bound on the transcendence degree is supplied. Define \[ G=\operatorname{Aut}_F(E) =\{g:E\longrightarrow E\mid g\text{ is a field automorphism and }g|_F=\mathrm{id}_F\}. \] The multiplicative abelian group $E^\times=E\setminus\{0\}$ has the natural $G$-action $g\cdot a=g(a)$.

The supplied problem asks: determine the discrete first cohomology group \[ dH^1(G,E^\times). \] The supplied record does not explicitly specify the topology on $G$ or the coefficient category and convention defining $dH^1$. These qualifications must be resolved before the target is fully specified.

For clarity, one possible interpretation is the following, conditional on the intended conventions. Endow $G$ with the topology of pointwise convergence on the discrete set $E$, so that the subgroups fixing finite subsets of $E$ pointwise form a neighbourhood basis at the identity, and give $E^\times$ the discrete topology. Its natural action is then continuous. If $dH^1$ means continuous first group cohomology with discrete abelian coefficients, its multiplicative cocycle description is \[ Z^1_{\mathrm{cont}}(G,E^\times) =\{c:G\longrightarrow E^\times\mid c\text{ is continuous and } c(gh)=c(g)\,g(c(h))\text{ for all }g,h\in G\}, \] \[ B^1(G,E^\times) =\{c_a\mid a\in E^\times,\quad c_a(g)=a^{-1}g(a)\text{ for all }g\in G\}, \qquad dH^1(G,E^\times)=Z^1_{\mathrm{cont}}(G,E^\times)/B^1(G,E^\times). \] This conditional description identifies a conventional target; it is not a computation or confirmation of the source's intended definition. In particular, no algebraic closedness hypotheses on either field, no local compactness hypothesis on $G$, and no rational vector-space structure on $E^\times$ are supplied, and none is imposed here.

Short English statement

For a characteristic-zero field extension $E/F$ of finite transcendence degree, determine $dH^1(\operatorname{Aut}_F(E),E^\times)$, with the natural automorphism action on nonzero field elements. The topology and precise discrete-cohomology convention require confirmation. The supplied registry labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
