Unconditional Uniqueness for Critical Equivariant Wave Maps

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns rotationally symmetric manifolds $M^n$ and $N^\ell$, with metrics written as \[ {\,\mathrm{d}} r^2+h(r)^2{\,\mathrm{d}}\omega^2, \qquad {\,\mathrm{d}}\phi^2+g(\phi)^2{\,\mathrm{d}}\chi^2, \] where $n\geq 3$, $k\geq 1$, and $\ell=k(k+n-2)$. Here $r$ and $\phi$ denote the respective radial coordinates, and the angular metric factors are denoted by ${\,\mathrm{d}}\omega^2$ and ${\,\mathrm{d}}\chi^2$. The same symbol $\phi$ denotes the scalar profile of an equivariant wave map. Primes denote differentiation of $h$ and $g$ with respect to their arguments.

For a source manifold $M$ that is admissible in the sense of the cited report, the displayed profile equation is \[ \partial_t^2\phi-\partial_r^2\phi -(n-1)\frac{h'(r)}{h(r)}\partial_r\phi +\frac{\ell}{h(r)^2}g(\phi)g'(\phi)=0, \] with Cauchy data \[ \phi(0,r)=\phi_0(r),\qquad \partial_t\phi(0,r)=\phi_1(r). \] The record designates $H^{n/2}\times H^{n/2-1}$ as the critical data space.

Question (unconditional uniqueness). For the admissible geometries and equivariance classes intended by the report, do any two solutions of this Cauchy problem with the same critical Sobolev data coincide throughout their common interval of existence, assuming only the intended critical Sobolev solution regularity and imposing no additional Strichartz-space condition?

More explicitly, if $I$ is a common time interval containing $0$, and $\phi$ and $\psi$ are solutions in the intended critical solution class with \[ \left(\phi,\partial_t\phi\right)(0) = \left(\psi,\partial_t\psi\right)(0) =(\phi_0,\phi_1) \in H^{n/2}\times H^{n/2-1}, \] is it necessarily the case that $\phi=\psi$ on their common spacetime domain, without separately assuming membership in a Strichartz space?

Unresolved qualifications. This is a conditional formulation of the supplied question. The record does not define admissibility, the radial coordinate domains, the hypotheses on $h$ and $g$, the angular geometry or equivariant ansatz, or the required pole, endpoint, and spatial-infinity conditions. It also does not specify the underlying measure and geometric realization of the Sobolev spaces, the time regularity of the solution class, or the sense in which the equation is satisfied. Consequently, the phrase "intended critical solution class" cannot be replaced by a fully specified function space from this record alone. The notation $N^\ell$ and the simultaneous use of $\ell=k(k+n-2)$ as the equation coefficient are retained exactly as supplied; the relationship between target dimension and angular spectral data requires clarification. These missing qualifications prevent a fully determined uniqueness conjecture.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether two equivariant wave-map solutions with the same data in $H^{n/2}\times H^{n/2-1}$ must agree using critical Sobolev regularity alone, without an extra Strichartz-space assumption. The admissible geometries and precise solution class are not defined in the supplied record, so the statement remains conditional on those missing specifications.
