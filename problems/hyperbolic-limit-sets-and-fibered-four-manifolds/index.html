Math-3560

Hyperbolic Limit Sets and Fibered Four-Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb H^n$ denote real hyperbolic $n$-space of sectional curvature $-1$, and identify its ideal boundary with $S^{n-1}$. For a subgroup $\Gamma<\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^4)$, define its limit set by \[ \Lambda(\Gamma)=\overline{\Gamma x}\cap\partial_\infty\mathbb H^4, \qquad x\in\mathbb H^4, \] where the closure is taken in the ideal compactification. Write $\dim_H\Lambda(\Gamma)$ for its Hausdorff dimension in the standard round metric on $\partial_\infty\mathbb H^4\cong S^3$. A hyperbolic manifold is a Riemannian manifold of constant sectional curvature $-1$. The source does not specify completeness, compactness, or volume restrictions for the manifolds below.

Limit-set question. Interpreting a hyperbolic $3$-manifold group as a group isomorphic to the fundamental group of a connected hyperbolic $3$-manifold, and provisionally interpreting the displayed action as discrete, is the following assertion true? \[ \begin{gathered} \text{For every }\varepsilon>0\text{, there exist a connected hyperbolic }3\text{-manifold }N \text{ and a discrete subgroup }\Gamma<\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb H^4)\\ \text{such that }\Gamma\cong\pi_1(N) \quad\text{and}\quad 3-\varepsilon<\dim_H\Lambda(\Gamma)\leq 3. \end{gathered} \] The subgroup may depend on $\varepsilon$. Discreteness and the intended geometric class of $N$ require confirmation from the original problem context. This formulation allows dimension exactly $3$; the source does not say whether dimensions strictly below $3$ are required.

Fibration question. Does there exist a connected real hyperbolic $4$-manifold $M$, a connected surface $B$, and a surface $F$, together with a locally trivial fiber bundle \[ F\hookrightarrow M\xrightarrow{\ p\ }B? \] Here interpreting "fibering" as a locally trivial fiber bundle is provisional: the source does not specify the smooth or topological category. It also does not specify completeness or finite volume of $M$, compactness, orientability, genus, or boundary conditions for the manifolds and surfaces. These missing qualifications remain unresolved and may affect the intended existence question.

The source asks about the full limit set $\Lambda(\Gamma)$, whereas its supplied literature assessment refers to a conical limit set. These are distinct notions, so that assessment does not justify replacing the limit set in the question. The two existence questions are retained separately; no equivalence or implication between them is asserted.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether hyperbolic $3$-manifold groups acting on $\mathbb H^4$ can have limit-set Hausdorff dimensions arbitrarily close to $3$. The second asks whether a real hyperbolic $4$-manifold can be a surface bundle over a surface. The intended geometric hypotheses and bundle category need clarification. Both questions are labeled open in the supplied record; this draft does not independently verify that status.
