Dual Hamiltonian Geometry of Equivariant Sheaf Categories

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record specifies a conjectural template whose full hypotheses and categorical conventions are not supplied. Let $(G,X)$ denote the hyperspherical pair mentioned in the record, with $G$ acting on $X$, and let $G^\vee$ and $M^\vee$ denote its dual group and dual Hamiltonian space. The definition of a hyperspherical pair, the permitted base fields, and the construction of this dual data are not included in the record and remain prerequisites for a complete statement.

Write $\mathcal D$ for the formal disk and $\mathring{\mathcal D}$ for its punctured disk, in the unspecified geometric setting of the source. The notation $G(\mathcal D)$ denotes the corresponding group of disk-valued points, acting on $X(\mathring{\mathcal D})$. Write $\operatorname{Shv}_{G(\mathcal D)}(X(\mathring{\mathcal D}))$ for the source's category of equivariant sheaves, whose sheaf theory, coefficients, and derived conventions are unspecified. Write $\operatorname{QCoh}(M^\vee)^{G^\vee}$ for the category of $G^\vee$-equivariant quasi-coherent sheaves on the dual Hamiltonian space, subject to the unresolved categorical decorations in the original extraction. Let $\mathcal H_G$ denote the spherical Hecke category referred to by the source.

Conjectural statement, with unresolved qualifications. For every hyperspherical pair satisfying the additional conditions referenced by the source as $[3,\text{ Conjecture }7.5.1]$, there should exist an equivalence \[ \Phi:\operatorname{Shv}_{G(\mathcal D)}\!\left(X(\mathring{\mathcal D})\right) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \operatorname{QCoh}(M^\vee)^{G^\vee} \] of $\mathcal H_G$-module categories. Thus, for every $A\in\mathcal H_G$ and every object $\mathcal F$ of the left-hand category, the equivalence should intertwine the specified module actions through coherent isomorphisms \[ \Phi(A\star\mathcal F)\simeq A\cdot\Phi(\mathcal F). \] The actions themselves and the coherence conventions must be taken from the intended source framework; they are not defined by the supplied record. The original extraction also indicates a spectral description of $\mathcal H_G$ involving $\operatorname{QCoh}(\check{\mathfrak g}^{*})^{G^\vee}$, but contains unreadable decorations. No precise graded, shifted, or otherwise modified version of that description can be recovered from this record alone.

The source's accompanying remark introduces a vector space $V_X$ and a subgroup $G_X^\vee\subseteq G^\vee$, and describes \[ M^\vee=G^\vee\times_{G_X^\vee}V_X. \] Here the associated-space notation requires the intended $G_X^\vee$-action on $V_X$. In a setting where this presentation and the relevant quotient stacks are defined, the remark gives \[ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{QCoh}(M^\vee)^{G^\vee} &\simeq\operatorname{QCoh}([M^\vee/G^\vee])\\ &\simeq\operatorname{QCoh}([V_X/G_X^\vee])\\ &\simeq\operatorname{QCoh}(V_X)^{G_X^\vee}. \end{aligned} \] Consequently the source also proposes this last category as an equivalent target, carrying the transported Hecke-module structure and all the same unresolved qualifications. The missing conditions in $[3,\text{ Conjecture }7.5.1]$, categorical conventions, and definitions prevent this template from being a fully specified standalone conjecture.

Short English statement

The proposed local relative Langlands equivalence identifies equivariant sheaves on punctured-disk points of a hyperspherical space with equivariant quasi-coherent sheaves on its dual Hamiltonian space, compatibly with the spherical Hecke action. The source also expresses the target using a vector space $V_X$ and a subgroup $G_X^\vee$. Essential hypotheses, sheaf conventions, and conditions referenced elsewhere remain unspecified, so this is a qualified statement-only draft.
