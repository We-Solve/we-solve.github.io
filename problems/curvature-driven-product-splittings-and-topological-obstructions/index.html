Math-15756

Curvature-Driven Product Splittings and Topological Obstructions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A closed manifold is a compact manifold without boundary. Write $T^k=(S^1)^k$ for the $k$-dimensional torus, where $k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ and $T^0$ is a point. Define the first rational Betti number by \[ b_1(X)=\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H^1(X;{\mathbb{Q}}). \] For a nonempty closed manifold $X$, its rational cuplength is \[ \operatorname{cup}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(X)=\max\left(\{0\}\cup\left\{r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 1}:\text{there exist }d_1,\ldots,d_r\geq 1\text{ and }\alpha_i\in H^{d_i}(X;{\mathbb{Q}})\text{ with }\alpha_1\smile\cdots\smile\alpha_r\neq 0\right\}\right). \] The product-splitting property requested in the supplied record is the existence of a finite-sheeted covering $p:\widetilde M\to M$, an integer $k\geq 0$, and a simply connected manifold $N$ such that \[ \widetilde M\cong T^k\times N, \qquad b_1(M)\leq k\leq\operatorname{cup}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(\widetilde M). \] Here $\cong$ is a placeholder for the intended type of product splitting: the supplied extraction does not specify whether it means a homeomorphism, a diffeomorphism, or an isometry with a product metric. Simply connected means path connected with trivial fundamental group.

Question. Which curvature hypotheses on closed manifolds ensure that every manifold satisfying those hypotheses has the displayed finite-cover product-splitting property? The original formulation additionally asks for hypotheses beyond the known cases and for further topological obstructions to the requested conclusion.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not identify the known cases, specify the curvature hypotheses or metric quantifiers, state connectedness and regularity assumptions, or fix the category of splitting. Curvature suggests a Riemannian setting, but that interpretation and its precise scope require confirmation. The intended target of the additional topological-obstruction question is also unspecified. Consequently, the displayed conclusion records the explicit target supplied by the extraction; it does not supply a complete conjecture with fixed hypotheses.

Short English statement

Identify curvature hypotheses forcing a closed manifold to have a finite cover that is a product of a torus and a simply connected manifold, with the torus dimension bounded below by $b_1(M)$ and above by the cover's rational cuplength. The source also asks for additional topological obstructions. The known cases, precise curvature assumptions, metric quantifiers, and meaning of splitting remain unspecified.
