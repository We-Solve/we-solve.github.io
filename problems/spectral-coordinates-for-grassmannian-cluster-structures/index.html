Spectral Coordinates for Grassmannian Cluster Structures

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a field $K$ and integers $0\leq k\leq n$, let $\operatorname{Gr}_K(k,n)$ denote the Grassmannian of $k$-dimensional subspaces of $K^n$. Its Plücker homogeneous coordinate ring and affine cone are denoted by \[ R_{k,n,K}=K[p_I: I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\},\ |I|=k]/I_{\mathrm{Pl}}, \qquad X_{k,n,K}=\operatorname{Spec}R_{k,n,K}, \] where $I_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ is the ideal of Plücker relations. These definitions supply notation only: the source record does not specify the intended ground field or the range of $k,n$ for the question.

Source question. For the affine cone over $\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)$, viewed through decorated $\operatorname{GL}(k)$-connections, do spectral coordinates recover the cluster structure on its homogeneous coordinate ring? In addition, does every cluster in that structure correspond to a spectral network?

More explicitly, if $\mathscr A$ denotes the intended Grassmannian cluster structure, the second requirement has the schematic quantifiers \[ \forall\,\mathcal C\in\operatorname{Clust}(\mathscr A), \quad \exists\,\mathcal W\text{ a spectral network realizing }\mathcal C. \] Here $\operatorname{Clust}(\mathscr A)$ denotes the set of clusters of $\mathscr A$. The word "realizing" records the requested correspondence; it is not a defined mathematical relation in the supplied record.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not specify the underlying surface, connection moduli space, decorations, spectral data, admissible networks, or the construction of their spectral coordinates. It also does not specify the identification with the Grassmannian cone, the domains of the coordinate charts, the frozen variables and any localizations, or the equivalence of coordinates intended by "recover." Consequently, the supplied evidence determines this conjectural target but does not determine a fully specified universal proposition. These choices must be recovered from the cited sources before asserting a precise coordinate identification or a correspondence for every cluster.

Short English statement

The question asks whether spectral coordinates associated with decorated $\operatorname{GL}(k)$-connections reproduce the Grassmannian cluster structure, and whether every cluster can be realized by a spectral network. The supplied record leaves essential geometric and coordinate conventions unspecified. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
