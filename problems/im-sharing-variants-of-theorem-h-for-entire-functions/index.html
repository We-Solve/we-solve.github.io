Math-6969

IM-Sharing Variants of Theorem H for Entire Functions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $f:{\mathbb{C}}\to{\mathbb{C}}$ be nonconstant and entire, let $k\geq2$ be an integer, and let $a,b\in{\mathbb{C}}$ satisfy $b\ne0$ and $b\ne a$. Sharing $a$ ignoring multiplicity (IM) means equality of the level sets: \[ \forall z\in{\mathbb{C}},\qquad f(z)=a\iff f'(z)=a, \] without requiring equal zero multiplicities of $f-a$ and $f'-a$. Sharing counting multiplicity (CM) additionally requires those multiplicities to agree. The separate hypothesis on $b$ is only the implication \[ \forall z\in{\mathbb{C}},\qquad f'(z)=b\Longrightarrow f^{(k)}(z)=b. \] The requested conclusion is retained exactly: \[ \exists c,d\in{\mathbb{C}}^\times,\quad c^{k-1}=1,\qquad f(z)=d e^{cz}+c^{-1}a\quad\text{for every }z\in{\mathbb{C}}. \] The original record attributes this conclusion under CM sharing to Theorem H and asks whether it remains true under IM sharing.

There is a material consistency problem in the supplied formula: with $k=2$, $a=1$, $b=2$ and $f(z)=e^z$, both displayed hypotheses hold, even with CM sharing, because $f=f'=f''$. But the proposed conclusion forces $c=1$ and $e^z=d e^z+1$, which cannot hold identically. Thus the printed CM theorem and proposed IM conclusion cannot have exactly these hypotheses and this formula simultaneously. The source must be checked for a missing condition or a different formula; no correction, such as deleting or changing the additive constant, is guessed here.

Short English statement

The source asks whether its CM-sharing uniqueness conclusion survives IM sharing, while retaining a separate one-way condition at $b$. The exact formula is preserved, but it already conflicts with $f=e^z$, $k=2$, $a=1$, $b=2$, so the intended theorem requires source correction.
