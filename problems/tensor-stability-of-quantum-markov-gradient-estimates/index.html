Tensor Stability of Quantum Markov Gradient Estimates

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A quantum Markov semigroup is a family $P=(P_t)_{t\geq 0}$ of unital completely positive maps on an operator algebra, satisfying $P_0=\mathrm{id}$ and $P_{s+t}=P_sP_t$. The relevant algebra class, continuity requirements, and any normality, invariant-state, traciality, or symmetry assumptions must be specified to determine the intended setting; the supplied record does not specify them.

For two such semigroups $P^{(1)}$ and $P^{(2)}$, their tensor-product semigroup is formally given by \[ (P^{(1)}\otimes P^{(2)})_t(a\otimes b) =P^{(1)}_t(a)\otimes P^{(2)}_t(b),\qquad t\geq0. \] The operator-algebra tensor product and the extension of these maps to it are part of the unspecified setting.

Write $P\models\operatorname{GE}(K,\infty)$ for satisfaction of the ordinary quantum Markov gradient estimate denoted by this notation in the cited report. Its defining inequality, gradient structure, auxiliary choices, and domains are absent from the supplied record, so this notation remains an unresolved predicate here.

Source question, in schematic form. Let $\mathcal C$ denote the intended class of quantum Markov semigroups and $\mathcal K$ the intended range of curvature parameters. Is it true that, for every $K\in\mathcal K$ and every $P^{(1)},P^{(2)}\in\mathcal C$, \[ \left[ P^{(1)}\models\operatorname{GE}(K,\infty) \ \text{and}\ P^{(2)}\models\operatorname{GE}(K,\infty) \right] \Longrightarrow P^{(1)}\otimes P^{(2)}\models\operatorname{GE}(K,\infty)? \] This displays the common-$K$, two-factor interpretation of tensor stability. The record does not explicitly specify this quantifier convention, the classes $\mathcal C$ and $\mathcal K$, or the definition of $\operatorname{GE}$; consequently it does not determine a fully self-contained mathematical proposition. The target is the ordinary estimate $\operatorname{GE}$. The supplied literature assessment distinguishes it from the stronger complete estimate $\operatorname{CGE}$.

Short English statement

The question asks whether tensoring quantum Markov semigroups preserves the ordinary gradient estimate $\operatorname{GE}(K,\infty)$, with the same curvature parameter under the displayed interpretation. The supplied record omits the estimate's definition and essential hypotheses, so the statement remains schematic and requires mathematical review. Its open status is supplied dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
