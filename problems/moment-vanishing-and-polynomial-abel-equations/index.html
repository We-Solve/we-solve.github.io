Moment Vanishing and Polynomial Abel Equations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $n\geq 1$ be an integer and let $\Omega\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be a bounded domain. For polynomial functions $P$ and $q$ on ${\mathbb{R}}^n$, define \[ m_k(P,q;\Omega)=\int_{\Omega}P(x)^kq(x)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x, \qquad k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}, \] where ${\,\mathrm{d}} x$ denotes Lebesgue measure and $P^0$ denotes the constant polynomial $1$. These integrals are finite because $\Omega$ is bounded and polynomial functions are bounded on its closure. The source does not specify whether the polynomial coefficients are real or complex; the expression makes sense in either case, but the intended coefficient class remains unresolved.

The stated moment problem is to determine necessary and sufficient conditions on $(\Omega,P,q)$ for \[ \forall k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0},\qquad m_k(P,q;\Omega)=0. \] No additional regularity or algebraic hypotheses on $\Omega$ are supplied.

The record also requests corresponding closed-solution conditions for the polynomial Abel equation \[ \frac{{\,\mathrm{d}} y}{{\,\mathrm{d}} x}=p(x)y(x)^2+q(x)y(x)^3, \qquad P'(x)=p(x). \] This derivative and ordinary differential equation require a one-variable interpretation. The source does not specify an interval, endpoint conditions, the meaning of a closed solution, the class of solutions being quantified over, or how this one-variable setting is related to the preceding $n$-dimensional moment problem. Consequently, the Abel-equation request is an unresolved part of the formulation: no precise boundary condition or equivalence with moment vanishing can be asserted from the supplied record.

Short English statement

Characterize when every integral of $P^kq$ over a bounded domain vanishes. The record also asks for related closed-solution conditions for a polynomial Abel equation, but omits the interval, closure condition, solution quantifiers, and precise connection to the moments. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
