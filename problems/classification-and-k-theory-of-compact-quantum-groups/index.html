Math-47

Classification and K-Theory of Compact Quantum Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A compact quantum group is a pair $\mathbb G=(A,\Delta)$, where $A$ is a nonzero unital $C^*$-algebra and $\Delta:A\to A\otimes_{\min}A$ is a unital $*$-homomorphism satisfying \[ (\Delta\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\Delta)\Delta, \] with both linear spans $\Delta(A)(1\otimes A)$ and $\Delta(A)(A\otimes1)$ dense in $A\otimes_{\min}A$. Let $h$ be its Haar state, characterized by \[ (h\otimes\mathrm{id})\Delta(a)=(\mathrm{id}\otimes h)\Delta(a)=h(a)1\qquad(a\in A). \] If $\pi_h$ denotes the Gelfand--Naimark--Segal representation associated with $h$, the reduced $C^*$-algebra is \[ C_r(\mathbb G)=\overline{\pi_h(A)}^{\|\cdot\|}. \] A nonzero $C^*$-algebra $B$ is simple if every norm-closed two-sided ideal $I\subseteq B$ satisfies $I=\{0\}$ or $I=B$. For unital $B$, write $K_0(B)$ for the Grothendieck group of stable Murray--von Neumann equivalence classes of projections in matrix algebras over $B$, and write \[ K_1(B)=\varinjlim_n\pi_0\left(U(M_n(B))\right), \] where $\pi_0$ uses norm-continuous paths and stabilization is $u\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(u,1)$.

Research tasks extracted from the supplied statement. Determine conditions on a compact quantum group $\mathbb G$ that are necessary and sufficient for $C_r(\mathbb G)$ to be simple, and compute the groups \[ K_0\left(C_r(\mathbb G)\right),\qquad K_1\left(C_r(\mathbb G)\right). \] The source emphasizes that these questions remain open even for some basic examples, but supplies neither those examples nor a specified family of compact quantum groups. Thus these are broad research tasks, not a conjecture asserting simplicity or particular $K$-groups for every compact quantum group. The supplied wording does not determine a more specific quantified problem.

The original excerpt additionally asks whether some basic examples of compact quantum groups can be classified up to isomorphism. No family or intended notion of isomorphism is specified, and this question is absent from the cleaned statement. It is retained here as an unresolved additional research direction rather than a definite classification assertion. The original talk places these questions in an overview of compact quantum groups, finite-index subfactors, rigid $C^*$-tensor categories, and their interactions; it supplies no additional hypotheses restricting the tasks above.

Short English statement

Determine when the reduced $C^*$-algebra of a compact quantum group has no nonzero proper closed two-sided ideals, and calculate its two topological $K$-theory groups. The source leaves the relevant basic examples unspecified and also raises an unspecified classification question. Its open-status label is supplied curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
