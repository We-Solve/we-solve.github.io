Math-516

Halperin conjecture

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F\to E\to B$ be a fibration of simply connected spaces, interpreted in the usual CW-type rational-homotopy setting. The fiber $F$ is rationally elliptic if \[ \dim_{\mathbb{Q}} H^*(F;{\mathbb{Q}})<\infty, \qquad\dim_{\mathbb{Q}}\left(\pi_*(F)\otimes{\mathbb{Q}}\right)<\infty. \] Its Euler characteristic is $\chi(F)=\sum_j(-1)^j\dim_{\mathbb{Q}} H^j(F;{\mathbb{Q}})$.

Halperin conjecture (Stephen Halperin). For every such fibration with $F$ rationally elliptic and $\chi(F)\neq0$, the rational cohomological Serre spectral sequence collapses at $E_2$. Since $B$ is simply connected, its $E_2$-page is \[ E_2^{p,q}=H^p(B;{\mathbb{Q}})\otimes H^q(F;{\mathbb{Q}}), \] and collapse means $d_r=0$ for every $r\geq2$. This concerns the spectral sequence; it does not by itself specify a canonical tensor-product ring isomorphism for $H^*(E;{\mathbb{Q}})$ or resolve multiplicative extensions. Finite-type and convergence conventions for the general space class should be checked, rather than adding a finite-dimensional base assumption.

The supplied summary gives an equivalent algebraic formulation for the intended elliptic fibers: the rational cohomology algebra has no nonzero degree-lowering derivations. In explicit sign notation, for each integer $r>0$, every linear map $D:H^*(F;{\mathbb{Q}})\to H^{*-r}(F;{\mathbb{Q}})$ satisfying \[ D(ab)=D(a)b+(-1)^{r|a|}aD(b) \] is zero. This retains the source's degree-lowering convention despite its notation $\operatorname{Der}_{>0}$.

The summary reports many confirmed cases: truncated-polynomial cohomology examples such as even spheres and complex projective spaces, flag spaces $G/T$, equal-rank homogeneous spaces, few-generator cohomology algebras, and closure under suitable products/fibrations. These are supplied progress claims with incomplete hypotheses and references, not an unrestricted claim about all homogeneous spaces or all fibrations.

Short English statement

Every fibration of simply connected spaces with rationally elliptic fiber of nonzero Euler characteristic should have a rational Serre spectral sequence with no differentials after $E_2$. The claimed equivalent formulation rules out all negative-degree derivations of the fiber's rational cohomology algebra.
