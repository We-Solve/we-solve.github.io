Quasiminimal Excellence Above Aleph-Two

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A subset of a set $M$ is cocountable if its complement in $M$ has cardinality at most $\aleph_0$. Under the usual definability convention, a structure is quasiminimal if every subset of its universe definable by a formula in one free variable, allowing parameters from the structure, is countable or cocountable. The supplied record does not specify the permitted language, its cardinality, or the precise conventions underlying its terminology.

Write $\operatorname{QM}(M)$ and $\operatorname{QME}(M)$ for the source's assertions that $M$ is quasiminimal and quasiminimally excellent, respectively. The record supplies no axiomatization of quasiminimal excellence, no associated closure operator, and no definition of its additional abbreviation $\operatorname{QMAut}$. These predicates therefore retain their source meanings; no alternative axiomatization is imposed here.

The question, identified as Question 5 in the supplied excerpt, is whether \[ \forall M\;\left(\left(|M|\geq\aleph_2\ \land\ \operatorname{QM}(M)\right) \Longrightarrow \operatorname{QME}(M)\right), \] where the quantifier ranges over structures in the source's intended setting. Thus the cardinality bound includes $\aleph_2$ itself and every larger cardinal. The missing specifications of that setting and of $\operatorname{QME}$ prevent this draft from being a self-contained axiomatization of the question.

The supplied curation records the implication $\operatorname{QME}\Rightarrow\operatorname{QM}$. The excerpt also presents an $\aleph_1$-sized counterexample below the proposed bound: the dense linear order \[ D=\omega_1\times_{\mathrm{lex}}{\mathbb{Q}}, \qquad (\alpha,q)<(\beta,r) \iff \alpha<\beta\ \lor\ (\alpha=\beta\ \land\ q<r), \] which it describes as $\operatorname{QMAut}$ but not $\operatorname{QME}$. The excerpt contrasts approximation of this order by countable substructures along $\omega_1$ with approximation in the excellent case along the directed partial order of all countable subsets of an uncountable basis, ordered by inclusion. It suggests that work of Pillay and Tanovic, cited there as [PT11], may be relevant. These are supplied contextual claims, not additional hypotheses or independently verified results.

As further context, the excerpt attributes the following statement to Gallinaro and Kirby as Theorem 2, marked "forthcoming" at the time of that source. For each $\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}$, put \[ \Gamma_\lambda=\{(\exp(z),\exp(\lambda z)):z\in{\mathbb{C}}\}, \] the graph of the multivalued complex power map $w\mapsto w^\lambda$. The excerpt states that \[ \langle{\mathbb{C}};+,\cdot,-,0,1,(\Gamma_\lambda)_{\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}}\rangle \] is quasiminimal. This attributed contextual statement is separate from the question above; its historical publication qualification is retained without a current-status assertion.

Short English statement

Must every quasiminimal structure of cardinality at least $\aleph_2$ be quasiminimally excellent? The supplied record labels this question open and describes a counterexample at cardinality $\aleph_1$, but leaves the precise excellence axioms and ambient language conventions unspecified.
