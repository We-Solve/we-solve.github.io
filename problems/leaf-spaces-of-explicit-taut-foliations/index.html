Math-14798

Leaf Spaces of Explicit Taut Foliations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a connected component of the unspecified $3$-manifold supporting one of the explicit taut codimension-one foliations $\mathcal F$ intended by the source. Let $p:\widetilde M\to M$ be its universal covering map and let $\widetilde{\mathcal F}$ be the lifted foliation. Define its leaf space by \[ L=\widetilde M/\!\sim,\qquad x\sim y\iff x\text{ and }y\text{ belong to the same leaf of }\widetilde{\mathcal F}, \] with the quotient topology. Deck transformations preserve the lifted foliation and therefore induce an action of $G=\pi_1(M)$ on $L$. The leaf space is not assumed to be Hausdorff. The foliation is called $\mathbb R$-covered when $L$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbb R$; the supplied background particularly concerns examples that are not $\mathbb R$-covered.

For a closed manifold, the usual closed-transversal convention calls a foliation taut if every leaf meets a closed curve transverse to the foliation. The record does not specify whether the intended manifolds are closed or have boundary, and does not supply a boundary version of tautness, regularity assumptions, or a coorientation hypothesis.

Problem. Understand the leaf spaces of some explicit taut foliations constructed in the literature, including the examples referred to as reference [12] in the report, sufficiently to apply the report's intended technique. The supplied edited statement describes the examples as constructed from knot diagrams and asks whether the induced fundamental-group action descends to a nontrivial orientation-preserving action on $\mathbb R$.

If "descends" means a topological quotient of the leaf-space action, the corresponding precise test for a selected example is whether there exist a surjective quotient map $q:L\to\mathbb R$ and a homomorphism \[ \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}(\mathbb R) \] satisfying \[ q(g\cdot\ell)=\rho(g)(q(\ell))\quad(g\in G,\ \ell\in L), \qquad \exists g\in G\ \exists t\in\mathbb R:\ \rho(g)(t)\ne t. \] Here $\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}(\mathbb R)$ is the group of increasing self-homeomorphisms of $\mathbb R$. This quotient interpretation is conditional: the supplied excerpt does not specify the required quotient map or the technique. It also does not identify the example family sufficiently to state a universal assertion, and its request concerning "some" examples must not be replaced by a claim that every explicit taut foliation admits such a quotient.

Short English statement

Analyze the lifted leaf spaces of some explicit taut foliations and determine whether their fundamental-group actions yield nontrivial actions on $\mathbb R$ by increasing homeomorphisms. The intended examples, ambient manifolds, boundary conventions, and meaning of descent require clarification; the displayed equivariant quotient condition is a conditional formulation.
