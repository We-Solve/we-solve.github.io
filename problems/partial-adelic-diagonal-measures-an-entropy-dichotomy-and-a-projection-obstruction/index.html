Math-323

Partial-adelic diagonal measures: an entropy dichotomy and a projection obstruction

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be a nonempty subset of the places of ${\mathbb{Q}}$. A natural precise meaning of the source's partial adele ring is \[ \mathbb A_S=\prod_{v\in S}'{\mathbb{Q}}_v, \] with restricted product taken relative to ${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ at the nonarchimedean places; ${\mathbb{Q}}_\infty={\mathbb{R}}$ when $\infty\in S$. Put $G_S=\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb A_S)$ and \[ D_S=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix}:a\in\mathbb A_S^\times\right\}. \] For a lattice $\Gamma<G_S$, E. Lindenstrauss's Question 13 asks for a classification of nonzero finite ergodic $D_S$-invariant measures on $G_S/\Gamma$, equivalently invariant ergodic probability measures after normalization. The source puts "irreducible" in quotation marks but does not define it, specify $S$, or specify whether the full diagonal or an identity-component subgroup is intended. For a finite product, a common irreducibility condition is dense projection to every proper nonempty subproduct; its intended extension to arbitrary $S$ needs clarification.

The supplied aid reports the following finite-place partial statements. Suppose $S$ is finite, contains $\infty$, has $|S|\ge2$, and $\Gamma$ is $S$-arithmetic, commensurable with $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathcal O_S)$, where \[ \mathcal O_S={\mathbb{Z}}[p^{-1}:p\in S\setminus\{\infty\}]. \] It attributes to the Einsiedler--Lindenstrauss torus theorem the dichotomy that every full-$D_S$ ergodic probability $\mu$ is Haar or satisfies \[ h_\mu(a)=0\quad\text{for every }a\in D_S. \] This leaves the zero-entropy measures unclassified and is not a theorem for all subsets of places and all lattices in the original question.

For a finite product $G_S$, if a coordinate projection $\Lambda_v=\pi_v(\Gamma)$ is discrete, the aid reports that it is a lattice and that the coordinate map \[ G_S/\Gamma\longrightarrow \mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{Q}}_v)/\Lambda_v, \qquad g\Gamma\longmapsto\pi_v(g)\Lambda_v \] sends a full-diagonal ergodic probability to a diagonal ergodic probability on the lower-place quotient. Dense coordinate projections rule out this particular discrete-projection descent, not every possible measurable factor.

For a closed diagonal orbit $D_Sg\Gamma$, its invariant orbit measure is finite exactly when the stabilizer $D_S\cap g\Gamma g^{-1}$ is a lattice in $D_S$. A cocompact such stabilizer gives a compact orbit and a finite ergodic orbit measure. The aid illustrates non-Haar measures in the one-real-place case $\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{R}})/\mathrm{SL}_2({\mathbb{Z}})$ using \[ \gamma=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \operatorname{spec}(\gamma)=\left\{\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2},\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\right\}. \] Diagonalizing this hyperbolic element gives a periodic diagonal orbit and its normalized orbit measure. Thus Haar uniqueness already fails in this reading; the question asks for classification rather than blanket uniqueness. The appended section 3.6 sentence about algebraic actions of "large" groups is a transition, not an added hypothesis. These partial conclusions are supplied machine-generated research claims requiring independent verification.

Short English statement

Classify finite ergodic diagonal-invariant measures on partial-adelic $\mathrm{SL}_2$ quotients. The supplied finite $S$ arithmetic result separates Haar measure from zero-entropy possibilities; coordinate descent and periodic orbits explain why the choice of places and irreducibility matter.
