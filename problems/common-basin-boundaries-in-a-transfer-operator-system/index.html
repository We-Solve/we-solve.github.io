Common Basin Boundaries in a Transfer-Operator System

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a particular transfer-operator dynamical system in the parameter regime $6<B\leq16$, with fixed points denoted by $u_{-r_*},u_0,u_{r_*}$. It does not specify the evolution operator, its state space, the topology, the definition of $r_*$, or the fixed-point functions. Consequently, the following expresses the intended conjecture conditionally on those missing source-specific data; it does not define the underlying system.

For each admissible $B$, write $T_B:X_B\to X_B$ for the unspecified evolution on its unspecified topological state space. Suppress any dependence of the fixed points and $r_*$ on $B$. The stated fixed-point conditions are \[ T_Bu_{-r_*}=u_{-r_*},\qquad T_Bu_0=u_0,\qquad T_Bu_{r_*}=u_{r_*}. \] Under the interpretation that a basin consists of states whose forward iterates converge to the indicated fixed point, define \[ \mathcal B_B(u)=\left\{x\in X_B:T_B^{\,n}(x)\longrightarrow u\text{ as }n\to\infty\right\}, \] where $T_B^{\,0}$ is the identity and convergence must be understood in the topology intended by the source. For $A\subseteq X_B$, its boundary relative to $X_B$ is \[ \partial_{X_B}A=\overline{A}^{\,X_B}\setminus\operatorname{int}_{X_B}(A). \] Interpreting "the common boundary" as equality with both complete basin boundaries, the intended assertion is \[ \text{for every }B\in(6,16],\qquad \mathcal B_B(u_0) =\partial_{X_B}\mathcal B_B(u_{-r_*}) =\partial_{X_B}\mathcal B_B(u_{r_*}). \] The endpoint $B=6$ is excluded and $B=16$ is included. The record does not resolve whether "common boundary" instead means only the intersection of the two boundaries. The definitions of the system, admissible states, convergence, and ambient boundary topology must be recovered from the cited report before this can serve as a complete mathematical formulation.

Short English statement

For the specified transfer-operator system and every $6<B\leq16$, the question asks whether the basin of $u_0$ equals the boundary of each of the basins of $u_{-r_*}$ and $u_{r_*}$. The system, topology, and meaning of $r_*$ are missing from the supplied record, so this is a conditional formulation requiring source clarification and human mathematical review.
