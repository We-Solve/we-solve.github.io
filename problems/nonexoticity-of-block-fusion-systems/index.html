Math-886

Nonexoticity of Block Fusion Systems

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\ell$ be a prime, let $G$ be a finite group, and, under the usual modular representation theory convention, let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $\ell$. An $\ell$-block $B$ of $kG$ is a direct summand $kGb$, where $b$ is a primitive central idempotent of $kG$. The supplied statement does not specify the hypotheses on $k$; the convention just stated makes the intended block fusion system precise and requires confirmation against the source.

Choose a maximal $B$-Brauer pair $(D,e_D)$; its underlying $\ell$-subgroup $D$ is a defect group of $B$. Here a $B$-Brauer pair is a pair $(Q,e_Q)$, where $Q\leq G$ is an $\ell$-subgroup, $e_Q$ is a block idempotent of $kC_G(Q)$, and $\operatorname{Br}_Q(b)e_Q\neq 0$, with $\operatorname{Br}_Q$ the Brauer homomorphism. For each $Q\leq D$, let $(Q,e_Q)$ be the unique $B$-Brauer pair contained in $(D,e_D)$, using the usual partial order on Brauer pairs.

The block fusion system $\mathcal F_D(B)$ has all subgroups of $D$ as objects and morphism sets \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F_D(B)}(Q,R) =\left\{c_g|_Q:Q\longrightarrow R\;\bigm|\; g\in G,\ {}^g(Q,e_Q)\leq(R,e_R)\right\}, \qquad Q,R\leq D, \] where $c_g(x)=gxg^{-1}$ and ${}^g(Q,e_Q)=(gQg^{-1},ge_Qg^{-1})$. The supplied source states that this fusion system is saturated.

For a finite group $H$ and a Sylow $\ell$-subgroup $S\leq H$, the group fusion system $\mathcal F_S(H)$ has subgroups of $S$ as objects and \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F_S(H)}(U,V) =\{c_h|_U:U\longrightarrow V\mid h\in H,\ hUh^{-1}\leq V\}. \] A saturated fusion system is called nonexotic if it is isomorphic to $\mathcal F_S(H)$ for some such finite group $H$ and Sylow subgroup $S$.

Statement to be proved. For every prime $\ell$, every finite group $G$, and every $\ell$-block $B$ as above, the saturated fusion system $\mathcal F_D(B)$ is nonexotic. Explicitly, there exist a finite group $H$, a Sylow $\ell$-subgroup $S$ of $H$, and an isomorphism $\alpha:D\longrightarrow S$ such that, for every $Q,R\leq D$, \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F_S(H)}(\alpha(Q),\alpha(R)) =\left\{\alpha\circ\varphi\circ(\alpha|_Q)^{-1} \mid \varphi\in\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F_D(B)}(Q,R)\right\}. \] The realizing group $H$ may depend on $B$. The supplied registry labels this problem open; that label is supplied curation, not an independently verified mathematical or literature assessment.

Short English statement

Every block of a finite group determines a saturated fusion system on a defect group. The problem asks whether this fusion can always be realized by conjugation in some finite group in which the defect group is a Sylow subgroup. The coefficient field convention needs source confirmation, and the supplied open status requires independent review.
