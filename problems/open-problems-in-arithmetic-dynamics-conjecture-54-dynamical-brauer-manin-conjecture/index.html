Open Problems in Arithmetic Dynamics — Conjecture 54 (Dynamical Brauer--Manin Conjecture)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K$ be a number field, and let $M_K$ denote its set of places. For $v\in M_K$, write $K_v$ for the completion of $K$. Let $\mathcal A_K$ be the adele ring of $K$, namely the restricted product of the fields $K_v$ with respect to their rings of integers at the nonarchimedean places. For a variety $X/K$, write $X(\mathcal A_K)$ for its adelic points, equipped with the usual adelic topology, and view $X(K)$ diagonally inside this space. For $S\subseteq X(K)$, interpret \[ \mathcal C(S):=\overline{S}^{\,X(\mathcal A_K)}. \] Let $f:X\to X$ be a morphism defined over $K$, let $Y\subset X$ be a subvariety defined over $K$, and let $P\in X(K)$. Define the forward orbit by \[ \mathcal O_f(P):=\{f^n(P):n\in\mathbb Z_{\geq 0}\},\qquad f^0=\operatorname{id}_X. \] Dynamical Brauer--Manin Conjecture, as supplied (Hsia--Silverman, 2009, [MR2537714]). For every such $K,X,f,Y,P$, \[ \mathcal C\left(Y(K)\cap\mathcal O_f(P)\right) = Y(\mathcal A_K)\cap\mathcal C\left(\mathcal O_f(P)\right). \] Here the source expression $Y\cap\mathcal O_f(P)$ is interpreted as $Y(K)\cap\mathcal O_f(P)$, and both closures are taken in the same ambient space $X(\mathcal A_K)$.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not specify whether "subvariety" means a closed subvariety, nor does it detail its adelic topology convention. The displayed formulation uses the usual adelic topology and a common ambient closure. The closedness convention requires confirmation, since it affects the relationship between closure and membership in $Y(\mathcal A_K)$. No properness hypothesis or additional restriction on the dynamics is supplied; none has been added. This is a statement-only transcription requiring human mathematical review.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture says that the adelic closure of the orbit points lying on $Y$ equals the intersection of $Y$'s adelic points with the adelic closure of the entire forward orbit. The registry labels this problem open; that label is supplied metadata, not an independently verified assessment. The meaning of "subvariety" and the adelic closure convention require review.
