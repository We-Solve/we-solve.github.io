Math-994

Flag-Complex Realization of Gamma Vectors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A finite simplicial complex is flag if every set of pairwise adjacent vertices is a face. The coefficient convention for homology sphere is not specified in the supplied record. To make the terminology explicit, for a fixed coefficient field $k$, a $d$-dimensional homology sphere over $k$ means a finite pure simplicial complex $\Delta$ such that, for every face $\sigma$, including the empty face, \[ \widetilde H_j(\operatorname{lk}_{\Delta}\sigma;k)\cong \begin{cases} k,&j=d-|\sigma|,\\ 0,&j\ne d-|\sigma|. \end{cases} \] Here $\operatorname{lk}_{\Delta}\sigma=\{\tau\in\Delta:\tau\cap\sigma=\varnothing,\ \tau\cup\sigma\in\Delta\}$, with the usual reduced homology convention in degree $-1$. This is an explicit field-coefficient interpretation for the draft; whether the source intends this convention, integral homology spheres, or a specified coefficient field remains unresolved.

For $d\geq0$, let $f_j(\Delta)$ count the $j$-dimensional faces and set $f_{-1}(\Delta)=1$. Define \[ h_{\Delta}(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{d+1}f_{i-1}(\Delta)t^i(1-t)^{d+1-i}. \] The symmetry of this polynomial for a homology sphere gives its unique expansion \[ h_{\Delta}(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{r}\gamma_i(\Delta)t^i(1+t)^{d+1-2i}, \qquad r=\left\lfloor\frac{d+1}{2}\right\rfloor. \] The tuple $(\gamma_0(\Delta),\ldots,\gamma_r(\Delta))$, with $\gamma_0(\Delta)=1$, is its gamma-vector.

Proposed statement. For every finite flag homology sphere $\Delta$, does there exist a finite flag simplicial complex $K$ such that \[ f_{i-1}(K)=\gamma_i(\Delta)\quad(0\leq i\leq r), \qquad f_j(K)=0\quad(j\geq r)? \] Thus "face vector" is interpreted as the augmented face vector, including the empty face and allowing trailing zeros. The complex $\{\varnothing\}$, having no vertices, is allowed; it realizes the vector $(1)$ when $r=0$.

The supplied original statement also includes the proposed bounds \[ 0\leq\gamma_i(\Delta)\leq\binom{\ell}{i}\qquad(2\leq i\leq r), \qquad \ell=\gamma_1(\Delta). \] Here $r\geq1$ is needed to define $\ell$; the realization would identify $\ell$ with the nonnegative integer $f_0(K)$. The original wording $i\geq2$ may be read by extending $\gamma_i=0$ for $i>r$, with $\binom{\ell}{i}=0$ for $i>\ell$. These bounds are retained as part of the supplied conjectural formulation, not asserted as established results. The clean statement emphasizes realization, while the supplied background reports a truncated extraction; the exact source formulation requires review.

Short English statement

The question asks whether the gamma-vector of every flag homology sphere counts the faces of some flag simplicial complex, including its empty face. The supplied original formulation also proposes bounds on the gamma entries in terms of $\gamma_1$. The homology coefficient convention and the precise source wording remain unresolved.
