Math-4191

Intrinsic Diophantine Exponents of Submanifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M\subset{\mathbb{R}}^d$ be a $k$-dimensional submanifold satisfying the source's condition of being nondegenerate. Write $\mathcal M_{k,d}$ for the intended class of such submanifolds. The source supplies the expressions \[ E_M(x)=\limsup_{p/q\in M}\frac{-\log|x-p/q|}{\log q}, \qquad \omega(M)=\inf_{x\in M}E_M(x), \qquad \omega(k,d)=\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_{k,d}}\omega(M). \] Here $p/q$ denotes a rational point in the ambient space. The displayed limit superior is reproduced from the source: the record does not specify its limiting regime, the normalization of rational representations, or its treatment of exact coincidences and insufficient rational approximants. Thus these expressions require additional conventions before they constitute fully specified extended-real quantities.

Questions supplied by the source.

  1. Can the available upper bound for $\omega(k,d)$ be improved?
  2. Can $\omega(k,d)$ be determined exactly for additional pairs $(k,d)$?
  3. Does the essential-infimum variant satisfy \[ \omega'(k,d)=\omega(k,d)? \]
The first two questions refer to an existing upper bound and previously determined cases, neither of which is given in the record.

For the third question, an essential infimum requires a measure on each $M$. If the intended construction is to replace the pointwise infimum by an essential infimum and then take the same supremum over manifolds, its explicit form, after specifying measures $\mu_M$, would be \[ \omega'_{\mu}(k,d) =\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_{k,d}} \operatorname*{ess\,inf}_{x\in M}^{\mu_M}E_M(x). \] This is a conditional clarification of the phrase "essential-infimum variant," not an additional definition supplied by the source. The measure and the intended construction of $\omega'(k,d)$ require confirmation.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define nondegeneracy, the regularity required of $M$, the admissible integer pairs $(k,d)$, the norm denoted by $|\cdot|$, or conventions for empty approximation families or an empty manifold class. These qualifications must be resolved before asserting a fully precise formulation. No answer to the questions is asserted.

Short English statement

The source asks how large the intrinsic rational-approximation exponent can be among nondegenerate manifolds of a fixed dimension and ambient dimension. It seeks improved upper bounds, further exact values, and equality with an essential-infimum version. The definition of the admissible manifolds, approximation limit, and essential-infimum measure needs clarification. The supplied open status is dated curation, not an independently verified conclusion.
