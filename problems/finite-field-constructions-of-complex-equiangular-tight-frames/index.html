Math-15303

Finite-Field Constructions of Complex Equiangular Tight Frames

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d,n$ be positive integers with $n\ge d$. A complex equiangular tight frame of $n$ unit vectors in ${\mathbb{C}}^d$ is a family $(\varphi_i)_{i=1}^n$ such that, for some real number $c\ge0$, \[ \varphi_i^*\varphi_i=1\quad(1\le i\le n),\qquad |\varphi_i^*\varphi_j|^2=c\quad(1\le i\ne j\le n),\qquad \sum_{i=1}^n\varphi_i\varphi_i^*=\frac nd I_d. \] Here $^*$ denotes conjugate transpose and $I_d$ is the identity operator on ${\mathbb{C}}^d$. Write $\mathcal E_{{\mathbb{C}}}(d,n)$ for the set of such families. When $n=1$, necessarily $d=1$, and the pairwise equiangularity condition is vacuous. These definitions do not specify the parameter ranges of the conjectures named below.

Problem as supplied. Develop finite-field constructions that resolve difficult existence questions for complex equiangular tight frames, including the questions identified in the record as the Fickus and Zauner conjectures, and determine whether the resulting finite-field solutions can be transferred to complex equiangular tight frames.

More precisely, a fully specified version must identify the target pairs $(d,n)$, any additional structure required by each conjecture, and a finite-field notion of equiangular tight frame. For each intended target, the requested programme is to obtain the corresponding finite-field solutions and establish a transfer result, under explicitly stated hypotheses, that yields an element of $\mathcal E_{{\mathbb{C}}}(d,n)$ with any required additional structure.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not state either named conjecture, its parameter range, or its possible symmetry requirements. It also does not specify the finite fields, bilinear or Hermitian forms, finite-field equiangularity and tightness constants, allowable characteristics, or the meaning and hypotheses of lifting. In particular, the complex normalization $n/d$ cannot simply be read as a finite-field scalar when the characteristic divides $d$. No unconditional implication from existence over an arbitrary finite field to complex existence is asserted here. The record therefore specifies a research programme rather than a uniquely determined quantified conjecture.

Short English statement

The problem asks whether finite-field constructions can settle specified complex equiangular tight frame existence questions, including the named Fickus and Zauner conjectures, and whether those constructions can be transferred to ${\mathbb{C}}$. The exact conjectures, finite-field models, and transfer hypotheses are missing, so the statement remains a qualified research programme requiring mathematical review.
