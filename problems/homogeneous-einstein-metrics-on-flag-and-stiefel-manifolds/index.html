Math-867

Homogeneous Einstein Metrics on Flag and Stiefel Manifolds

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Riemannian metric $g$ on a smooth manifold $M$ is Einstein if there exists a constant $\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}$ such that \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g=\lambda g. \] It is homogeneous if a Lie group acts transitively on $M$ by isometries. For a specified transitive action of a Lie group $H$ on $M=H/L$, with $L$ closed, write \[ \mathcal E_H(H/L)=\{g:\ g\text{ is an }H\text{-invariant Riemannian metric on }H/L,\ \operatorname{Ric}_g=\lambda g\text{ for some constant }\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}\}. \] Such invariant metrics correspond to positive-definite inner products on $T_{eL}(H/L)\cong\mathfrak h/\mathfrak l$ preserved by the isotropy representation of $L$.

Under the usual compact convention, a generalized flag manifold is a quotient $G/K$, where $G$ is a compact connected semisimple Lie group and $K=C_G(T)$ is the centralizer of a torus $T\subseteq G$. The record does not explicitly state its convention for generalized flag manifolds. The Stiefel spaces are presented in the record as \[ V_k{\mathbb{R}}^n=\operatorname{SO}(n)/\operatorname{SO}(n-k), \] where the denominator has its standard block embedding. The intended integer range of $n,k$ is not supplied. In the usual range $1\leq k<n$, this quotient parametrizes ordered orthonormal $k$-frames in ${\mathbb{R}}^n$. At $k=n$, the displayed quotient gives $\operatorname{SO}(n)$, whereas the space of all ordered orthonormal $n$-frames is $\operatorname{O}(n)$; the intended endpoint convention therefore requires clarification.

An isotropy representation has pairwise nonequivalent irreducible components if, as a real representation of the isotropy group $L$, it admits a decomposition \[ \mathfrak h/\mathfrak l=\mathfrak m_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathfrak m_r, \qquad \mathfrak m_i\text{ irreducible},\qquad \mathfrak m_i\not\cong\mathfrak m_j\quad(i\ne j). \] This uses real irreducibility; the source does not explicitly specify the representation-field convention.

Requested classification. For every generalized flag manifold $G/K$ and every Stiefel manifold in the intended parameter range, classify all homogeneous Einstein metrics. For the displayed transitive actions, the corresponding invariant-metric formulation is to determine $\mathcal E_G(G/K)$ and $\mathcal E_{\operatorname{SO}(n)}(V_k{\mathbb{R}}^n)$. The record does not resolve whether these fixed-action invariant metrics exhaust the intended classification target, or whether metrics homogeneous under other transitive actions must also be included.

Requested finiteness questions. Determine, for each homogeneous space whose isotropy representation has pairwise nonequivalent irreducible components, whether its homogeneous Einstein metrics form a finite set of classes under the intended equivalence relation. Determine the analogous finiteness question for each compact semisimple Lie group. If the group itself is regarded as a homogeneous space under left translations, its invariant metrics are precisely its left-invariant metrics; the record does not explicitly restrict the group question to this action.

The equivalence relation in both classification and counting is unspecified. In particular, a finiteness question must address constant rescaling, since $g\mapsto cg$, $c>0$, preserves the Einstein property. One possible convention is to identify $g_1,g_2$ when $g_2=c\phi^*g_1$ for a diffeomorphism $\phi$ and $c>0$, but the record does not choose this convention, an equivariant version, or a normalization. Compactness is not explicitly imposed on the general homogeneous spaces in the finiteness question. These unresolved choices prevent a unique fully specified counting statement.

Short English statement

Classify homogeneous Einstein metrics on generalized flag manifolds and the displayed real Stiefel quotients, and determine finiteness for spaces with pairwise inequivalent irreducible isotropy components and for compact semisimple Lie groups. The acting groups, equivalence relation, scaling convention, general compactness assumptions, and Stiefel parameter boundaries require clarification.
