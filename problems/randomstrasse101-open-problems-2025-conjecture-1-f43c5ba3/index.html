Randomstrasse101 Open Problems (2025) — Conjecture 1

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb{T}=\{\lambda\in\mathbb{C}:|\lambda|=1\}$. In the standard complex phase-retrieval interpretation of the source, the signal space is $\mathbb{C}^{M}$, and $x\sim y$ means that $y=\lambda x$ for some $\lambda\in\mathbb{T}$. The quotient includes the class of the zero vector.

For an integer $M\geq 2$, put $N=4M-5$. Let $A\in\mathbb{C}^{N\times M}$ have rank $M$, with its column space distributed according to the unitary-invariant probability measure on the Grassmannian $\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(M,N)$ of $M$-dimensional complex linear subspaces of $\mathbb{C}^{N}$. Define \[ \Phi_A:\mathbb{C}^{M}/\mathbb{T}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R}_{\geq0}^{N},\qquad \Phi_A([x])=\left(|(Ax)_1|^2,\ldots,|(Ax)_N|^2\right), \] and \[ p_M=\mathbb{P}\left(\Phi_A\text{ is injective}\right). \] Thus injectivity means \[ \forall x,y\in\mathbb{C}^{M},\qquad \left(\forall j\in\{1,\ldots,N\},\ |(Ax)_j|^2=|(Ay)_j|^2\right) \Longrightarrow \left(\exists\lambda\in\mathbb{T},\ y=\lambda x\right). \] This event depends only on the column space of $A$, so the specified Grassmannian distribution determines $p_M$. The source mentions independent identically distributed Gaussian entries as an example; independent standard circular complex Gaussian entries realize the specified distribution.

Conjecture 1 (Bandeira et al., as attributed in the supplied record).

  1. (a) For every integer $M\geq2$, $p_M<1$.
  2. (b) $\displaystyle\lim_{M\to\infty}p_M=0$, with $M$ ranging over integers.
The source states part (a) for "all $M$" without specifying a lower bound. The range $M\geq2$ above is the positive-integer range in which the stated Grassmannian exists: $4M-5\geq M$. In particular, the source construction does not define $p_1$. The signal domain and meaning of $\mathbb{T}$ are made explicit here using the standard complex phase-retrieval interpretation; they are not separately defined in the supplied source statement.

Short English statement

For a random $M$-dimensional complex subspace of $\mathbb{C}^{4M-5}$, consider whether coordinatewise squared magnitudes determine every vector up to a common complex phase. The supplied conjecture says that this succeeds with probability strictly less than one for every admissible $M$, and that this probability tends to zero as $M\to\infty$. The unspecified small-dimension boundary and the interpretation of the signal quotient require review.
