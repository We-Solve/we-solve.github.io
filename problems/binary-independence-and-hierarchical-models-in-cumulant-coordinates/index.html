Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.

Moments are expectations of products or powers of random variables. Cumulants are coefficients of the formal logarithm of the moment-generating series, so moment and cumulant coordinates are related polynomially at each finite order. An independence or hierarchical model imposes specified relations on the joint law; the task is to express its constraints in cumulant coordinates.

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
Problem 14. Use the transformations from probabilities to moments to cumulants to de-scribe independence models/ hierarchical models in terms of polynomial functions in the cumulants. [S2]
