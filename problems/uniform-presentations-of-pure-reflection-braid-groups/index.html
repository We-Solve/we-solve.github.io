Math-7113

Uniform Presentations of Pure Reflection Braid Groups

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem asks whether, for every finite reflection group $W$, its pure braid group admits the presentation \[ P_c(W)\stackrel{?}{\cong}\langle T:\operatorname{Red}_T(\Delta)\rangle, \] where $T$ consists of squares of reflection generators and $\Delta$ is the full twist. The source does not specify the class of reflection groups, the meaning of the subscript $c$, the reflection generators and their braid lifts, or the relations encoded by $\operatorname{Red}_T(\Delta)$. These choices must be resolved before this is a uniquely determined mathematical assertion.

One possible precise interpretation of the presentation notation is the following, conditional on the intended choices. Write $P=P_c(W)$, and suppose that specified braid lifts $\widetilde r$ of specified reflection generators give elements \[ T=\{\widetilde r^{\,2}\}\subseteq P. \] Let $\Delta\in P$ be the specified full twist. Assume that $\Delta$ has a positive factorization over $T$, and define \[ \ell_T^+(\Delta)=\min\{k\geq 0:\Delta=t_1\cdots t_k,\ t_i\in T\}. \] Under the interpretation that reduction means shortest positive factorization, put \[ \mathcal R_T(\Delta)=\{(t_1,\ldots,t_k):k=\ell_T^+(\Delta),\ t_i\in T,\ t_1\cdots t_k=\Delta\text{ in }P\}. \] Introduce a formal generator $x_t$ for each $t\in T$, and define \[ G(T,\Delta)=\left\langle x_t\ (t\in T)\ \middle|\ x_{t_1}\cdots x_{t_k}=x_{u_1}\cdots x_{u_k}\ \text{for all }(t_1,\ldots,t_k),(u_1,\ldots,u_k)\in\mathcal R_T(\Delta)\right\rangle. \] The evaluation map $x_t\mapsto t$ defines a homomorphism $G(T,\Delta)\to P$. On this interpretation, the question is whether this homomorphism is an isomorphism for every $W$ in the intended class, with the intended choices. This asks both whether $T$ generates $P$ and whether the displayed relations give all relations among those generators.

This conditional expansion is not asserted to be the source's definition of $\operatorname{Red}_T(\Delta)$. In particular, positive factorization, the choice of lifts, and the pairwise equality interpretation of the relations require confirmation. The supplied unexpanded question remains the target until these qualifications are resolved.

Short English statement

The question asks whether every intended finite reflection group has a pure braid group presented by squared reflection generators, with all defining relations coming from reduced factorizations of the full twist. The source leaves essential choices and the relation notation unspecified, so the explicit presentation above is conditional. The supplied record labels the universal question open; that label is not independently verified here.
