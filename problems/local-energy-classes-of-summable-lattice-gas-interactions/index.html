Local-Energy Classes of Summable Lattice-Gas Interactions

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d$ denote the lattice dimension and let $X=\mathbb{N}^{\mathbb{Z}^d}$ be the configuration space. The source does not explicitly specify the range of $d$ or the convention for $\mathbb{N}$. Write $\Phi=(\Phi_\Lambda)_\Lambda$ for a translation-invariant lattice-gas interaction on $X$. Under the conventional interpretation that the interaction indices $\Lambda$ are finite subsets of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, the supplied summability class is \[ S=\left\{\Phi:\sum_{\substack{\Lambda\subseteq\mathbb{Z}^d\text{ finite}\\0\in\Lambda}}\lVert\Phi_\Lambda\rVert_\infty<\infty\right\}, \] where $\lVert\Phi_\Lambda\rVert_\infty$ denotes the supremum of the absolute value of the interaction term over its configurations. The finite-subset indexing convention is implicit in the terminology and requires confirmation against the report.

Let $\mathcal{A}_S$ denote the class of local energy functions generated by interactions in $S$, as in the supplied record. For the two exponents needed below, write \[ \mathcal{R}_k=\left\{f:\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^k\delta_n(f)<\infty\right\},\qquad k\in\{d-1,d\}. \] Here $\delta_n(f)$ is the source's unspecified modulus-of-continuity quantity. The record does not define it, the common ambient class of functions $f$, or the rule assigning a local energy function to an interaction. In particular, it does not specify the neighborhoods used to measure continuity, the normalization or allocation of interaction terms, or whether energy functions are identified under any equivalence relation. These conventions must be supplied before the classes above determine a fully precise mathematical problem.

Question, conditional on those conventions. Taking the inclusions \[ \mathcal{R}_d\subseteq\mathcal{A}_S\subseteq\mathcal{R}_{d-1} \] as supplied premises, determine whether each inclusion is an equality or is strict. If both inclusions are strict, give a necessary and sufficient characterization of membership in $\mathcal{A}_S$ in terms of the modulus of continuity of the local energy function. The source does not specify the intended form of that characterization. The displayed inclusions are reported by the supplied record and are not independently established here.

Short English statement

The question asks which continuity conditions characterize local energies arising from absolutely summable, translation-invariant lattice-gas interactions on $\mathbb{N}^{\mathbb{Z}^d}$. It asks whether either of two supplied inclusions is an equality and, if both are strict, requests an exact continuity criterion. Definitions of the continuity quantities and the interaction-to-energy correspondence are missing, so the formulation remains conditional.
