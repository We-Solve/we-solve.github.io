Spectral Rigidity of Unit-Area Riemannian Two-Spheres

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(M,g)$ be a closed Riemannian surface homeomorphic to $S^2$, with $\operatorname{Area}_g(M)=1$. Write $\lambda_1(g)$ for the first positive eigenvalue of the nonnegative Laplace--Beltrami operator $-\Delta_g$ on functions. The symbol $g_0$ denotes the reference metric on $S^2$ used in the supplied statement; its definition and normalization are not supplied.

For a specified metric $h$ on $S^2$, one standard inhomogeneous Sobolev convention is \[ \|u\|_{H^1(S^2,h)}^2=\int_{S^2}\left(|u|^2+|\nabla_hu|_h^2\right)\,\mathrm dV_h, \qquad H^{-1}(S^2,h)=\left(H^1(S^2,h)\right)^*. \] Under this convention, a smooth function $s$ defines a functional by $u\mapsto\int_{S^2}su\,\mathrm dV_h$, with dual norm \[ \|s\|_{H^{-1}(S^2,h)}=\sup_{0\ne u\in H^1(S^2,h)}\frac{\left|\int_{S^2}su\,\mathrm dV_h\right|}{\|u\|_{H^1(S^2,h)}}. \] These definitions describe a possible convention; the supplied record does not fix the Sobolev norm or pairing convention.

Proposed assertion, retaining the unresolved source notation. Does there exist a universal constant $C>0$ such that, for every unit-area Riemannian topological two-sphere $(M,g)$, there are a diffeomorphism $\Phi:S^2\to M$ and a positive conformal factor $s:S^2\to(0,\infty)$ satisfying \[ \Phi^*g=s g_0, \qquad \|s\|_{H^{-1}(S^2,g)}\le C\lambda_1(g)? \] Here $C$ must be independent of $(M,g)$. The metric identity implies $\int_{S^2}s\,\mathrm dV_{g_0}=1$. The displayed bound contains no additional spectral-defect term.

The notation $H^{-1}(S^2,g)$ needs clarification because $g$ is initially a metric on $M$. Transporting it by $\Phi$, so that the metric in this norm is $\Phi^*g=s g_0$, is a possible reading, but the supplied record does not explicitly settle the intended identification or norm convention. Consequently, the assertion remains a qualified formulation pending clarification of these choices and of $g_0$.

The original statement additionally describes this as asking whether the conformal factors are uniformly bounded in the dual of $H^1$. That claimed equivalence is retained as source wording: a uniform bound and the displayed eigenvalue-dependent estimate are not identified here without further justification.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks whether every unit-area Riemannian two-sphere admits a conformal parametrization whose positive conformal factor has $H^{-1}$ norm at most a universal constant times its first positive Laplace eigenvalue. The reference metric, Sobolev conventions, and claimed equivalence with a uniform dual bound require clarification. The supplied literature assessment concerns a different problem about plane domains.
