Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns a compact, connected Riemannian manifold $(\Omega,g)$ of dimension $n\geq 2$ with boundary $\partial\Omega$. In the standard smooth setting with nonempty boundary, the Steklov eigenvalue problem is \[ \Delta_g u=0\quad\text{in }\Omega,\qquad \partial_{\nu_g}u=\sigma u\quad\text{on }\partial\Omega, \] where $u\not\equiv0$, $\Delta_g$ is the Laplace--Beltrami operator, and $\nu_g$ is the outward unit normal. Smoothness and nonemptiness of the boundary are standard conditions for this background formulation; the supplied excerpt does not explicitly specify them. The eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity, are written \[ 0=\sigma_0<\sigma_1\leq\sigma_2\leq\cdots\longrightarrow+\infty. \] Writing ${\,\mathrm{d}} V_g$ and ${\,\mathrm{d}} A_g$ for the Riemannian volume and boundary measures, respectively, the standard interpretation of the corrupted variational formula in the excerpt is \[ \sigma_1(\Omega,g)= \min_{\substack{f\in W^{1,2}(\Omega),\ \operatorname{Tr}f\not\equiv0\\ \int_{\partial\Omega}\operatorname{Tr}f\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A_g=0}} \frac{\displaystyle\int_\Omega |\nabla_g f|_g^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} V_g} {\displaystyle\int_{\partial\Omega}|\operatorname{Tr}f|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A_g}, \] where $W^{1,2}(\Omega)$ is the space of square-integrable functions with square-integrable weak first derivatives, and $\operatorname{Tr}f$ is the boundary trace. The nonzero-trace restriction ensures a positive denominator.

The extracted question specializes to a surface, hence to $n=2$, and refers to its genus $\gamma$. Its recoverable wording is:

Can the dependence on the genus $\gamma$ of the surface $\Omega$ be removed from inequality (3)?
Inequality (3) is absent from the supplied record. The supplied background describes the target as a bound for normalized Steklov eigenvalues, while the excerpt discusses the spectral gap $\sigma_1$; this does not identify the missing inequality.
