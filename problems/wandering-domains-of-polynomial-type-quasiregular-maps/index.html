Wandering Domains of Polynomial-Type Quasiregular Maps

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record contains two questions. Its cleaned statement retains only the first; both are preserved here.

For the first question, identify ${\mathbb{R}}^2$ with ${\mathbb{C}}$. A nonconstant sense-preserving map $p\colon{\mathbb{R}}^2\to{\mathbb{R}}^2$ is $K$-quasiregular if $p\in W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ and \[ \|Dp(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2\leq K J_p(x) \quad\text{for almost every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2, \] where $K\geq1$, $Dp$ is the weak derivative, and $J_p=\det Dp$. It is of polynomial type if \[ |p(x)|\longrightarrow\infty\qquad\text{as }|x|\longrightarrow\infty. \] Such a map extends continuously to $\widehat{{\mathbb{R}}^2}={\mathbb{R}}^2\cup\{\infty\}$ by fixing infinity; write $d=\deg p$ for its positive topological degree. Define $p^0=\operatorname{id}$ and $p^{n+1}=p\circ p^n$.

The record does not define the Fatou set for these quasiregular maps. To display the question without silently choosing a convention, let $\mathcal F(p)$ denote the intended Fatou set. Under a convention in which forward images of its connected components lie in connected components of $\mathcal F(p)$, a component $U$ is wandering if there are pairwise distinct connected components $U_n$ of $\mathcal F(p)$, for $n\geq0$, such that \[ U_0=U,\qquad p^n(U)\subseteq U_n\quad(n\geq0). \] Question 1. Can there exist a $K$-quasiregular map $p\colon{\mathbb{R}}^2\to{\mathbb{R}}^2$ of polynomial type, with $K<d=\deg p$, having a wandering Fatou component? The intended quasiregular Fatou-set convention must be confirmed before this becomes an unambiguous statement; in particular, no uniform bound on the dilatations of all iterates is supplied or assumed.

For the additional question in the original statement, let $X$ and $Y$ be compact connected Riemann surfaces, with $X$ hyperbolic, equivalently of genus at least two, and let $W\subseteq Y$ be a domain, meaning a nonempty connected open subset. A simple island of a holomorphic map $f\colon W\to X$ over a Jordan domain $V\subset X$ is a domain $D$ with compact closure contained in $W$ such that $f|_D\colon D\to V$ is a conformal isomorphism.

One usual interpretation of the Ahlfors islands property is the following: $f$ is nonconstant and holomorphic, and there exists an integer $k\geq1$ such that, for every collection of Jordan domains $V_1,\ldots,V_k\subset X$ with pairwise disjoint closures, every connected open set $O\subseteq Y$ meeting $\partial_Y W$, and every connected component $O_0$ of $O\cap W$, there are $j\in\{1,\ldots,k\}$ and a domain $D$ with compact closure contained in $O_0$ for which $f|_D\colon D\to V_j$ is a conformal isomorphism. The supplied record does not give its definition of an Ahlfors islands map, so this interpretation requires confirmation against the source.

Question 2. For which triples $(X,Y,W)$ satisfying the preceding surface hypotheses does an Ahlfors islands map $f\colon W\to X$ exist? How does existence depend on the choice of the hyperbolic target $X$? The record does not specify whether $W$ must be proper or what precise comparison of targets is intended. If $W=Y$, the displayed boundary condition is vacuous, so this boundary case must be resolved explicitly.

Short English statement

The main question asks whether a polynomial-type quasiregular plane map whose degree exceeds its distortion bound can have a wandering Fatou component. The original record also asks when Ahlfors islands maps to compact hyperbolic Riemann surfaces exist and how existence depends on the target. The Fatou-set convention, the precise islands-map definition, and the allowed boundary cases need confirmation.
