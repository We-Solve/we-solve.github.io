Kostka–Shoji Formula for Graded Modules

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)$ for the space of global sections of a sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$, and $\operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet}\mathcal E=\bigoplus_{d\geq 0}\operatorname{Sym}^{d}\mathcal E$ for the graded symmetric algebra of a specified sheaf $\mathcal E$. The notation $GL_N$ denotes a general linear group, but the source record does not specify its base field or the admissible values of $N$ and $r$.

The supplied question asks whether the conjectural Kostka--Shoji formula yields the following graded $GL_N$-module identity: \[ \begin{aligned} \Gamma\left(T_r^*B_N^r,O(\mu)\right) &=\Gamma\left(B_N^r,\operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet}T_rB_N^r\otimes O(\mu)\right)\\ &=\bigoplus_{\lambda\geq\mu}K_{\lambda\mu}^{-,\bullet}\otimes V_\lambda. \end{aligned} \] Here the symbols $B_N^r$, $T_rB_N^r$, $T_r^*B_N^r$, $O(\mu)$, $K_{\lambda\mu}^{-,\bullet}$, and $V_\lambda$ are retained exactly as source notation. Their constructions, the allowed indices $\lambda,\mu$, the relation $\lambda\geq\mu$, the tensor product's base field, and the group actions and gradings are not defined in the supplied record. In particular, the record does not specify whether $K_{\lambda\mu}^{-,\bullet}$ denotes a graded multiplicity space or a polynomial encoding one, or how $O(\mu)$ on the first space relates to its namesake on the second. The displayed equalities reproduce the source's proposed identity; whether they mean canonical identifications, graded module isomorphisms, or character identities requires clarification.
