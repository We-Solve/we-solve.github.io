Math-3407

Definability Complexity of Well-Orderings of the Reals

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Definability-complexity question. What is the least Lévy-hierarchy complexity of a parameter-free formula $\Phi(x,y)$ for which there is a set-forcing extension in which $\Phi$ defines a well-order of the reals over $\langle H(\omega_2),\in\rangle$, or over a specified natural expansion such as $\langle H(\omega_2),\in,\mathrm{NS}_{\omega_1}\rangle$? The base structure and expanded structure are separate optimization problems; adding a predicate changes definability complexity.

In a forcing extension $V[G]$, let $H(\omega_2)=\{a:|\operatorname{tc}(\{a\})|<\omega_2\}$, with hereditary size and the cardinals computed in that extension. Reals may be coded as subsets of $\omega$. The unary predicate $\mathrm{NS}_{\omega_1}$ names the nonstationary subsets of $\omega_1$: $A\subseteq\omega_1$ is nonstationary if it is disjoint from a closed unbounded subset of $\omega_1$. This is a predicate on the structure, not an assumption that the ideal itself belongs to $H(\omega_2)$. Parameter-free means no set, real, or ordinal parameters are allowed in $\Phi$, beyond its displayed free variables; the fixed language predicates remain available.

For a fixed permitted ground model and forcing, the relation is\[x\prec y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\langle H(\omega_2)^{V[G]},\in\rangle\models\Phi(x,y),\] with the additional predicate interpreted in the expansion when appropriate. It must be a well-order of all reals in $V[G]$. The Lévy hierarchy starts with bounded formulas $\Delta_0$ and counts unbounded quantifier alternations to obtain $\Sigma_n$ and $\Pi_n$, relative to the chosen language; a $\Delta_n$ definability claim requires equivalent definitions of both kinds. Thus optimizing $\Sigma_n$, $\Pi_n$, or $\Delta_n$ must be distinguished, and no claim about projective complexity is silently substituted.

The source does not fix the starting theory or ground-model hypotheses, permitted forcings and preservation conditions, or whether one formula must work uniformly over all allowed ground models. Existence over some ground model, existence over each model in a class, and existence over a specified model are different assertions. It also does not enumerate the allowed natural expansions; arbitrary added predicates coding a chosen well-order would change the task. These missing quantifiers prevent a unique numerical optimum from being extracted.

The supplied triage checked 2026-08-21 labels the item partially solved. It reports forcing parameter-free definable well-orders of $H(\omega_2)$ under explicit cardinal-arithmetic hypotheses, while describing the general optimal complexity for well-ordering the reals in the listed structures as unsettled. Those hypotheses and exact complexity levels are not reproduced, so the result for all of $H(\omega_2)$ is not silently used as an optimality theorem for this question. The dated assessment requires independent verification.

Short English statement

Find the lowest definability level at which forcing can produce a parameter-free well-order of all reals, separately in the membership structure and its specified expansions. The ground-model and forcing quantifiers, preservation requirements, and choice among $\Sigma$, $\Pi$, and $\Delta$ complexity are essential missing inputs.
