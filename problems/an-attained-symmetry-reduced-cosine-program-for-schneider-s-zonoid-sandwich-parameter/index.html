Math-1409

An attained symmetry-reduced cosine program for Schneider's zonoid-sandwich parameter

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $K\subset{\mathbb{R}}^n$ be an origin-symmetric convex body with nonempty interior. A zonoid is a Hausdorff limit of finite Minkowski sums of line segments. Define \[ \lambda(K)=\inf\{\lambda\ge1:\exists\text{ zonoid }Z, \ K\subseteq Z\subseteq\lambda K\}. \] For $C_n=\{x:\sum_i|x_i|\le1\}$, the supplied aid recovers the source's split display as \[ \lambda(C_n)=\frac n{2^{n-1}} \binom{n-1}{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor} \sim\sqrt{\frac{2n}{\pi}}. \] The original OCR loses the fraction/root layout and appends page number 12. R. Schneider's question is whether, in each dimension, \[ \lambda(K)\le\lambda(C_n)\qquad\text{for every such }K. \] It asks for maximality, not uniqueness of maximizers.

The supplied aid reports an attained minimax formulation. Let $h_K(u)=\sup_{x\in K}\langle u,x\rangle$ and, for a finite even positive Borel measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb S^{n-1}$, set \[ (C\mu)(u)=\int_{\mathbb S^{n-1}}|\langle u,v\rangle|\,{\,\mathrm{d}}\mu(v), \quad m_\mu=\min_u\frac{C\mu(u)}{h_K(u)},\quad M_\mu=\max_u\frac{C\mu(u)}{h_K(u)}. \] Restrict to $m_\mu>0$. The reported exact formula is \[ \lambda(K)=\min_\mu M_\mu/m_\mu, \] with an optimizer and, after scaling, $m_\mu=1$. The measure convention absorbs any usual factor in the cosine transform. It reports that an optimal sandwich zonoid can be centered, that $\lambda(TK)=\lambda(K)$ for invertible linear $T$, and that general affine-invariance language is interpreted with recentering at the symmetry center.

For every compact linear symmetry group of $K$, an optimal zonoid is reported to be choosable invariant by Minkowski averaging. After coordinates are changed so the group is orthogonal, its generating measure may also be chosen invariant. This is a symmetry reduction, not a solution of the global maximum problem.

Additional supplied benchmarks are $\lambda(K)\le\sqrt n$ and $\lambda(K)=1$ for $n\le2$. The Rademacher zonotope with support function \[ h_Z(u)=2^{-n}\sum_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^n}|\langle u,\varepsilon\rangle| \] provides the cross-polytope sandwich, while Schneider's sharp sign-sum inequality is reported to give its optimality and the displayed exact value. Full statements and citations are absent. The aid says the cross-polytope extremality question remains open in the literature it checked; that is a supplied assessment, not a fresh current-status claim.

Short English statement

The source asks whether the cross-polytope is hardest to sandwich by a zonoid up to dilation. The supplied aid gives an attained cosine-transform optimization, symmetry reductions, and known benchmarks, while leaving the general maximality question unresolved.
