Kourovka Notebook Problem 19.91

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime, let $G$ be a finite group, and let $A$ be an abelian Sylow $p$-subgroup of $G$. A subgroup $B\leq A$ is elementary abelian if it is abelian and every nonidentity element has order $p$. Here strong closure of $B$ in $A$ with respect to $G$ means \[ \forall b\in B\;\forall g\in G,\qquad g^{-1}bg\in A\ \Longrightarrow\ g^{-1}bg\in B. \] For an abelian $p$-group $P$, define \[ \Omega_1(P)=\langle x\in P:x^p=1\rangle=\{x\in P:x^p=1\}. \] Requested statement and proof requirement. For every prime $p$, every finite group $G$, every abelian Sylow $p$-subgroup $A$ of $G$, and every strongly closed elementary abelian subgroup $B\leq A$, prove, without invoking the Classification Theorem for Finite Simple Groups (CFSG), that \[ \exists N\trianglelefteq G\qquad B=\Omega_1(A\cap N). \] The CFSG-free requirement concerns the proof method and is essential to the supplied problem.

The source states that the case $p=2$ is a corollary of a theorem of Goldschmidt, and that Flores and Foote proved the assertion for odd $p$, using CFSG. These are supplied literature assertions, not independently verified here.

The source also singles out a CFSG-free proof under the additional hypotheses \[ p=3,\qquad \operatorname{rank}_3(A)=3, \] where $\operatorname{rank}_3(A)=\dim_{{\mathbb{F}}_3}\Omega_1(A)$, equivalently the largest dimension of an elementary abelian subgroup of $A$. It describes this special case as already quite interesting because it arises in Aschbacher's treatment of the $e(G)=3$ problem and would provide an alternative to part of his argument. For that application, the source permits assuming that all proper simple sections of $G$ are known. A simple section means a simple quotient $H/K$ with $K\trianglelefteq H\leq G$. The source does not specify the meaning of $e(G)$, which sections are intended by "proper," or the precise information meant by "known"; this application-specific allowance is therefore retained as an unresolved qualification, not added as a formal hypothesis to the general statement.

Short English statement

Given an abelian Sylow $p$-subgroup $A$ of a finite group $G$ and a strongly closed elementary abelian subgroup $B\leq A$, find a normal subgroup $N$ whose elements of order dividing $p$ in $A\cap N$ are exactly $B$. The requested proof must avoid CFSG. The source particularly highlights $p=3$ with $A$ of $3$-rank $3$.
