Constant-factor hardness of directed feedback vertex set

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents the following claims relevant to this question, with manuscript numbers following paper order in Sources. In manuscript 1 (family 102; 2026-09-23), the asserted result is that directed feedback vertex set is NP-hard to approximate within any prescribed constant, even without weights.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Manuscript 1.
Let $\operatorname{DFVS}(G)$ be the minimum number of vertices whose removal leaves a directed graph acyclic. For each fixed real $A\ge1$, the manuscript claims that an NP-hard promise problem can be mapped in deterministic polynomial time to an unweighted digraph $G$ and positive integer $k$, with the two resulting cases \[ \operatorname{DFVS}(G)\le k\qquad\hbox{from}\qquad\operatorname{DFVS}(G)>Ak. \] The polynomial may depend on $A$. This implies NP-hardness of every fixed-factor approximation for directed feedback vertex set.
