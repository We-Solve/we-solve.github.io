Research attempt

Literature, source context and scope

The primary report's contraction is a ratio of divergences to the current target, not a Lipschitz constant of the map. Its scheduler result assumes that a realizable transport step has this ratio below one. We construct and analyze a restricted Gaussian translation family where both parts can be specified exactly, then test the identity-only map class.

A joint schedule and map construction

Fix $\sigma>0$, $M\in\mathbb R$, integer $L\ge1$, and $0<\eta\le1$. Use target bridges $\pi_\ell=N(m_\ell,\sigma^2)$, $m_\ell=\ell M/L$, starting reference $N(0,\sigma^2)$. Let the current approximation be $N(c_{\ell-1},\sigma^2)$ and apply the translation \[ Q_\ell(x)=x+\eta(m_\ell-c_{\ell-1}),\qquad c_\ell=(1-\eta)c_{\ell-1}+\eta m_\ell, \quad c_0=0. \] These maps are diffeomorphisms and their composition sends the reference to $N(c_\ell,\sigma^2)$.
Proposition: For Kullback--Leibler divergence, each step has contraction ratio $\omega=(1-\eta)^2<1$. Equal increments minimize the sum of successive bridge divergences among all real mean paths from zero to $M$. The terminal error satisfies \[ D_{\rm KL}(\pi_L\|N(c_L,\sigma^2)) \le \frac{(1-\eta)^2M^2}{2\sigma^2\eta^2L^2}. \]
Proof: For equal-variance normal densities, integrating their log-density ratio yields $D_{\rm KL}(N(u,\sigma^2)\|N(v,\sigma^2))=(u-v)^2/(2\sigma^2)$: under the first density $E(X-v)^2=\sigma^2+(u-v)^2$ and $E(X-u)^2=\sigma^2$. The update multiplies $m_\ell-c_{\ell-1}$ by $1-\eta$, proving the contraction inequality, including the case of zero denominator where both divergences vanish.

For an arbitrary mean path with increments $\Delta_\ell$ summing to $M$, the sum of successive divergences is $\sum_\ell\Delta_\ell^2/(2\sigma^2)\ge M^2/(2\sigma^2L)$, since $\sum_\ell(\Delta_\ell-M/L)^2\ge0$. Equality holds for our schedule.

Put $r=1-\eta$ and $e_\ell=c_\ell-m_\ell$. Then $e_\ell=r(e_{\ell-1}-M/L)$, so by summing a geometric series $e_L=-r(M/L)(1-r^L)/\eta$. Its square divided by $2\sigma^2$ gives the exact terminal divergence and the displayed upper bound. ∎

Why scheduler optimization alone is insufficient

If only the identity map is permitted, no approximation changes. Whenever its before-step divergence is positive, its before and after divergences coincide, so no $\omega<1$ inequality is possible, however small the displacement is. This is a concrete failure of the attempted inference from close successive densities to an automatically attainable contraction. Conversely, the translation family succeeds because it contains an explicit corrective direction; arbitrary nonlinear target densities need not share that property.

Verification and remaining gaps

Checks reproduce the center recursion, divergence ratios and terminal $L^{-2}$ bound. This standard Gaussian subcase does not solve joint design for general $\alpha$-divergences, sample-based density approximations or the source's sum-of-squares parametrizations.
