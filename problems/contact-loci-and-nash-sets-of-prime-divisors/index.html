Contact Loci and Nash Sets of Prime Divisors

Definitions and mathematical statement

A prime divisor is an irreducible codimension-one subvariety of a model. The supplied record considers a prime divisor $E$, its minimal model, and a free infinitely near point $p_{i_0}$ used to obtain that model. It does not specify the ambient space, the base field, the construction of the minimal model, or the convention for a free infinitely near point.

The notation $E_{p_{i_0}}$ denotes the divisor associated in the source with $p_{i_0}$; its construction is not supplied. The notation $\mathcal N_{E'}$ denotes the Nash set associated with a prime divisor $E'$, and $E'\equiv_{i_0}E$ is the source's relation indexing the divisors in the union. Neither the Nash-set convention nor this relation is defined in the supplied record. Likewise, $\operatorname{Cont}_q(E_{p_{i_0}})$ denotes the source's contact locus, whose ambient arc space and contact-order convention are unspecified.

For a prime divisor $E$ and a free infinitely near point $p_{i_0}$ used to obtain its minimal model, the question is whether there exists a contact-order parameter $q$ such that \[ \bigcup_{E'\equiv_{i_0}E}\mathcal N_{E'} = \operatorname{Cont}_q\left(E_{p_{i_0}}\right). \] The parameter $q$ is existentially quantified for the given data; its allowed domain is not stated. The left-hand side is the union displayed in the source, with no additional closure. This is a schematic transcription of the proposed equality: the missing definitions and ambient identifications must be recovered before it constitutes a fully specified mathematical statement.

Short English statement

Given a prime divisor $E$ and a free infinitely near point $p_{i_0}$ in the construction of its minimal model, ask whether the union of the Nash sets indexed by $E'\equiv_{i_0}E$ is a single contact locus of $E_{p_{i_0}}$, for some order $q$. The supplied record leaves essential notation and ambient assumptions undefined.
