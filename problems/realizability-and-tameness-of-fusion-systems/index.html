Math-4010

Realizability and Tameness of Fusion Systems

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $p$ be a prime and let $S$ be a finite $p$-group. A fusion system $\mathcal F$ on $S$ is a category whose objects are the subgroups of $S$, whose morphisms are injective group homomorphisms, which contains all homomorphisms induced by conjugation in $S$, and in which every morphism factors as an isomorphism in the category followed by an inclusion.

Write $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathcal F}(P)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F}(P,P)$, and let $\operatorname{Aut}_S(P)$ consist of automorphisms induced by $N_S(P)$. A subgroup $P\leq S$ is fully normalized, respectively fully centralized, if $|N_S(P)|$, respectively $|C_S(P)|$, is maximal among the corresponding orders for subgroups isomorphic to $P$ in $\mathcal F$. The fusion system is saturated if the following hold:

  1. Every fully normalized $P\leq S$ is fully centralized and satisfies $\operatorname{Aut}_S(P)\in\operatorname{Syl}_p(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathcal F}(P))$.
  2. If $\varphi\in\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F}(P,S)$ and $\varphi(P)$ is fully centralized, then $\varphi$ extends to a morphism $N_\varphi\to S$ in $\mathcal F$, where, writing $c_s(x)=sxs^{-1}$, \[ N_\varphi=\{s\in N_S(P):\varphi\circ(c_s|_P)\circ\varphi^{-1}\in\operatorname{Aut}_S(\varphi(P))\}. \]

For a finite group $G$ containing $S$, define $\mathcal F_S(G)$ by \[ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal F_S(G)}(P,Q) =\{c_g|_P:P\to Q\mid g\in G,\ gPg^{-1}\leq Q\} \qquad(P,Q\leq S). \] The system $\mathcal F$ is realizable if there is such a finite group $G$ with $S\in\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$ and $\mathcal F=\mathcal F_S(G)$.

Recoverable characterization problem. For arbitrary primes $p$, finite $p$-groups $S$, and saturated fusion systems $\mathcal F$ on $S$, determine necessary and sufficient conditions for realizability: \[ \exists\,G\text{ finite}\quad S\leq G,\qquad S\in\operatorname{Syl}_p(G),\qquad \mathcal F=\mathcal F_S(G). \]

Short English statement

Characterize the saturated fusion systems arising from conjugation in finite groups with the given Sylow $p$-subgroup. The source also requires a normal subgroup $H$, but does not specify its role, and asks about tameness without defining the intended remaining target. Its partially solved label and reported results are supplied dated curation requiring mathematical review.
