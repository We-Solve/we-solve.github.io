OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. Every integrable zero-temperature Parisi minimizer for pure zero-field SK is claimed to have full relative Stieltjes support below overlap one.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Let $\mathcal U$ contain nonnegative, nondecreasing right-continuous $\gamma:[0,1)\to[0,\infty)$ with $\int_0^1\gamma<\infty$, and define $\mu_\gamma([0,t])=\gamma(t)$. For covariance $\xi(t)=t^2/2$, let \[ \Phi_t=-\tfrac12(\Phi_{xx}+\gamma(t)\Phi_x^2),\quad\Phi(1,x)=|x|, \qquad\mathcal P(\gamma)=\Phi_\gamma(0,0)-\tfrac12\int_0^1t\gamma(t)\,dt. \] Every minimizing $\gamma$ is claimed to satisfy \[ \operatorname{supp}\mu_\gamma=[0,1),\qquad \gamma(b)>\gamma(a)\quad(0\le a<b<1). \] For $dX_t=\gamma(t)\Phi_{\gamma,x}(t,X_t)dt+dB_t$, $X_0=0$, the source also gives $\mathbb E\Phi_{\gamma,x}(t,X_t)^2=t$ and $\mathbb E\Phi_{\gamma,xx}(t,X_t)^2=1$ for $t<1$. The optimization corollary, for $H_n(\sigma)=n^{-1/2}\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j$, gives a randomized $C(\varepsilon)n^2$-operation algorithm whose value is at least $\max H_n-\varepsilon n$ with probability tending to one. This operation model charges one unit for real sums/products and uses prescribed scalar functions; no separate finite-precision preprocessing bound is claimed.

Outline of the argument

Controlled diffusion treats integrable coefficients beyond finite step laws. Backward shape inequalities are proved first for step functions and extended by approximation; variational support conditions exclude any interval gap in the minimizing order parameter.
