Regularity of Polynomial Laws in Free Probability

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record poses a research question about the regularity of polynomial distributions, rather than a fully specified conjecture. Its stated question is: what regularity properties does the distribution $\mu_Y$ of $Y=P(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ have under various assumptions on self-adjoint $X_1,\ldots,X_n$? The ambient probability space, admissible polynomials, additional assumptions, and requested regularity properties are not specified.

One conventional framework, stated conditionally rather than attributed as an explicit source hypothesis, is the following. Let $n\geq 1$, let $(\mathcal M,\tau)$ be a von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal tracial state, and let $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathcal M$ satisfy $X_j=X_j^*$. A noncommutative polynomial $P\in{\mathbb{C}}\langle t_1,\ldots,t_n\rangle$ is a finite complex linear combination of words in the formal self-adjoint variables $t_1,\ldots,t_n$. Set $Y=P(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$. If $Y=Y^*$, its spectral distribution is the Borel probability measure on ${\mathbb{R}}$ defined by \[ \mu_Y(B)=\tau\left(E_Y(B)\right)\qquad\text{for every Borel set }B\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}, \] where $E_Y$ is the spectral measure of $Y$. Equivalently, \[ \int_{{\mathbb{R}}}f(t)\,\mathrm d\mu_Y(t)=\tau(f(Y)) \] for every bounded Borel function $f:{\mathbb{R}}\to{\mathbb{C}}$. Self-adjointness of the inputs alone does not ensure self-adjointness of $Y$; the record does not impose a corresponding condition on $P$.

In this conditional framework, the question asks for hypotheses on the tuple and the polynomial that imply specified regularity properties of $\mu_Y$. For example, non-atomicity means $\mu_Y(\{a\})=0$ for every $a\in{\mathbb{R}}$, and absolute continuity means $\mu_Y\ll\lambda$, where $\lambda$ denotes Lebesgue measure on ${\mathbb{R}}$. These definitions clarify possible regularity targets; they do not assert that either property holds under the assumptions displayed above.

The supplied background additionally describes a report conjecture that finite free entropy is preserved for general polynomials, and describes results about non-atomicity and other regularity under strong free-entropy-dimension hypotheses. It supplies neither an exact entropy conjecture nor the hypotheses of those results. In particular, it does not specify the entropy convention, the entropy assumption on the input tuple, the admissible output polynomials, or exclusions of constant evaluations. These omissions prevent a precise entropy implication from being stated without adding unsupported hypotheses. No freeness assumption on the inputs is explicitly supplied.

Short English statement

Determine which assumptions on self-adjoint noncommutative random variables and a polynomial ensure regularity of the polynomial's distribution. The supplied question leaves the assumptions and regularity targets open; interpreting the output as a probability measure on ${\mathbb{R}}$ requires self-adjointness of the evaluated polynomial. The background also mentions an incompletely specified finite-free-entropy conjecture. The supplied label is partially solved, a dated curation assessment requiring independent review.
