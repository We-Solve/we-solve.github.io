Prismatic Cohomology from Topological Cyclic Homology

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record concerns topological periodic cyclic homology, denoted by $TP$, with $TP_k$ denoting its degree-$k$ group. It uses relative expressions $TP_k(R/S_n)$ and $TP_k(R/S_n^{\otimes 2})$, but does not define $S_n$, the parameter $n$, or the precise relative construction.

Let $R$ be an $S_n$-algebra in the intended setup. The proposed identification concerns the groups \[ \operatorname{Ext}_{TP_0(R/S_n^{\otimes 2})}^{j} \left(TP_0(R/S_n),TP_i(R/S_n)\right). \] Here $\operatorname{Ext}_A^j(M,N)$ denotes an Ext group over the indicated coefficient ring $A$, once the intended module category and module structures on $M$ and $N$ have been specified. The question is whether these groups identify with the appropriate prismatic cohomology groups of $R$, and whether the associated Adams-type spectral sequence displayed in the source, \[ \operatorname{Ext}_{TP_0(R/S_n^{\otimes 2})}^{j} \left(TP_0(R/S_n),TP_i(R/S_n)\right) \Longrightarrow TP_{i-j}(R), \] is the Bhatt--Morrow--Scholze spectral sequence.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied record does not specify the allowed $S_n$-algebras, the ranges of $n,i,j$, the tensor-product base or whether the tensor product is derived, or the coefficient actions needed to form the Ext groups. It also does not specify a prime, completion conventions, a prism or other base for prismatic cohomology, the cohomological grading and possible twists in the proposed identification, or the page, filtration, and convergence conditions of the displayed spectral sequence. Thus the display preserves the supplied target, but does not by itself determine a fully specified conjecture or assert the existence or convergence of a spectral sequence. These missing choices require clarification from the underlying setup.

Short English statement

For an $S_n$-algebra $R$, the supplied conjectural question asks whether the displayed Ext groups compute prismatic cohomology and whether the associated Adams-type spectral sequence is the Bhatt--Morrow--Scholze spectral sequence. The base, admissible algebras, coefficient structures, gradings, and convergence conventions remain unspecified in the supplied record.
