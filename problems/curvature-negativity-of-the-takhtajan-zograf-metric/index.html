These are placeholders for data that must be recovered from the cited source; they are not definitions of a particular space or metric. For any specified Riemannian metric $g$, use the curvature convention \[ R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_Y Z-\nabla_Y\nabla_X Z-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z, \] where $\nabla$ is its Levi--Civita connection. For linearly independent $u,v\in T_pM$, define sectional curvature by \[ K_g(p;u,v)=\frac{g_p(R(u,v)v,u)}{g_p(u,u)g_p(v,v)-g_p(u,v)^2}. \] With this convention a round sphere has positive sectional curvature.
