Math-14600

Generic Closedness of the Canonical $\Gamma$-Field

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be either $\mathbb G_m$ or an abelian variety over a field $k_0$, and put $O=\operatorname{End}(G)$. Write $LG$ for the Lie algebra of $G$, viewed as an additive algebraic group. The supplied record describes a canonical $\Gamma$-field $C$, with exponential map $\exp_G$, by \[ \Gamma(C)=\{(x,\exp_G(x)):x\in LG(C)\},\qquad \Gamma(k_0)=\{0\},\qquad C_0=\Gamma\operatorname{cl}_C(k_0). \] Here $0$ denotes the identity of the product group, whose second component is the identity of $G$. The notation $\Gamma\operatorname{cl}_C$, the precise notion of a $\Gamma$-field, and the required properties of $k_0$ are not defined in the supplied record. Its background identifies the intended object as the complex $\Gamma$-field.

The requested assertion is that $C$ is generically $\Gamma$-closed. The supplied expanded formulation asks whether the following implication holds for every number $n$ of factors and every finitely generated $\Gamma$-field $k$ containing $C_0$. To interpret $V(C)$ and independence over $\Gamma(k)$, one needs a compatible embedding $k\subseteq C$; this is implicit rather than explicitly stated. Let \[ V\subseteq (LG)^n\times G^n \] be an irreducible variety defined over $k$, with $\dim V=n$. Assume the following conditions from the record:

  1. $V$ is not contained in a translate by $\Gamma(k)$ of a proper algebraic subgroup defined over $C_0$.
  2. For every connected proper algebraic subgroup $H<G^n$, \[ \dim\left(V/(LH\times H)\right)>n-\dim H, \] and both quotient projections have positive-dimensional image.
In the quotient expression, $LH$ is the Lie algebra of $H$, and $V/(LH\times H)$ is read as the Zariski closure of the image of $V$ under the quotient map by $LH\times H$. The natural reading of the two quotient projections is the projection of this image to $(LG)^n/LH$ and to $G^n/H$. The record does not explicitly specify these conventions. Likewise, condition (1) does not specify the ambient subgroup class or explain whether its translating element is a tuple in $\Gamma(k)^n$; its wording is retained pending clarification.

The proposed conclusion is the existence of \[ \boldsymbol\gamma=(\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_n) \in V(C)\cap\Gamma(C)^n \] whose coordinates are $O$-linearly independent over $\Gamma(k)$. Under the usual module interpretation, an endomorphism $a\in O$ acts on a pair by its differential on the Lie algebra component and by $a$ on the group component. Provided $\Gamma(k)$ is an $O$-submodule, independence means \[ \left(a_1,\ldots,a_n\in O\ \text{and}\ \sum_{i=1}^n a_i\gamma_i\in\Gamma(k)\right) \Longrightarrow a_1=\cdots=a_n=0. \] The record does not specify whether independence instead uses a torsion quotient or a scalar extension, so this interpretation requires review.

Unresolved qualifications. As written, condition (2) includes the trivial connected subgroup $H=\{e\}$, which is proper whenever $G^n$ is nontrivial. Then $LH=\{0\}$, the quotient image has dimension $n$, and the displayed strict inequality requires $n>n$. Consequently the literal hypotheses admit no such $V$ in that case. The intended exclusion of subgroups, or a possible correction to the inequality, cannot be recovered from the supplied record. Furthermore, the record allows abelian varieties of unspecified dimension while retaining $\dim V=n$ and the bound $n-\dim H$; no dimension restriction or replacement by a multiple of $\dim G$ is supplied. These issues prevent an unqualified precise formulation of the intended conjecture. No correction is imposed here.

Short English statement

The intended question asks whether every suitably constrained variety over a finitely generated $\Gamma$-field above $C_0$ contains a point on the exponential graph with coordinates independent over the base $\Gamma$-field. The supplied formulation needs clarification: its subgroup condition includes the trivial subgroup and then demands $n>n$. The closure operation, base-field conventions, subgroup class, and independence convention also require specification. The supplied open-status assessment is dated curation, not independent verification.
