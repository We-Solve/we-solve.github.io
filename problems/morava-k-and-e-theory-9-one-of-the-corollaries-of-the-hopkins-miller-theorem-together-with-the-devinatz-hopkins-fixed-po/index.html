Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Fix a prime $p$ and height $n$. Let $S$ denote the source's Morava stabilizer group and $E_n$ its intended Morava-$E$ theory. In the usual homotopy-fixed-point framework the relevant spectral sequence has the form \[ H_c^s(S;(E_n)_t)\Longrightarrow\pi_{t-s}(E_n^{hS}). \] Whether $S$ is the ordinary or extended stabilizer, and hence the precise identification of the target with a localized sphere, is not stated in the excerpt. Continuous cohomology with trivial coefficients must also be distinguished from the displayed coefficient action; constants may map into $(E_n)_0$, but the induced cohomology map need not be assumed injective.

The source reports that the degree-one class $\zeta$ in continuous stabilizer cohomology survives to a homotopy class of degree $-1$, using Hopkins--Miller and Devinatz--Hopkins fixed-point theory. It says large-prime collapse made this easy in that range but small primes had required these developments; no numerical prime bound or complete spectral-sequence hypothesis is supplied.

Morava K- and E-theory item 9 asks to find analogous classes in continuous degrees \[ 3,5,\ldots,2n-1 \] with the intended trivial coefficients, and determine whether they survive to nonzero homotopy classes in degrees $-3,-5,\ldots,-2n+1$ at all primes. Under the customary trivial-${\mathbb{Z}}_p$ reading these would be classes $\zeta_{2k-1}\in H_c^{2k-1}(S;{\mathbb{Z}}_p)$ for $2\leq k\leq n$, with nonzero permanent-cycle images in coefficient degree $t=0$. The source presents their expected existence/survival as part of the chromatic-splitting picture; it does not ask for, or establish, all products or a full splitting theorem.

The registry reports the $\zeta$ case settled and the higher odd-degree survival not generally known. Its additional references to the image of $J$, level structures, cardinality arguments and height-two computations are not precise constructions or proofs, and its final progress sentence is truncated.

Short English statement

Find the higher odd continuous-cohomology classes predicted to produce degrees $-3,-5,\ldots,-2n+1$ in chromatic homotopy, and determine whether they are nonzero permanent cycles at every prime.
