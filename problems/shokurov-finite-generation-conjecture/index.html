Shokurov Finite-Generation Conjecture

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

This is Shokurov's finite-generation conjecture in the supplied pl-flipping context. Let $(X,B)$ be a klt pair: in the usual normal-variety convention $K_X+B$ is ${\mathbb{R}}$-Cartier and all discrepancies are greater than $-1$. For a proper birational model $\pi:Y\to X$, set \[ A(X,B)_Y=K_Y-\pi^*(K_X+B). \] These traces form the discrepancy b-divisor $A=A(X,B)$; this is the discrepancy convention, not the log-discrepancy convention obtained by adding one to coefficients. A b-divisor has compatible divisor traces on all proper birational models, with pushforward compatibility. Let $D_\bullet=(D_i)_{i\geq1}$ be the characteristic system in the source.

Its stated asymptotic canonical saturation is precisely \[ \forall i,j\geq1\ \exists\,Y(i,j)\to X\ \forall\,Y\to Y(i,j):\quad \operatorname{Mob}\!\left(\left\lceil(jD_i+A)_Y\right\rceil\right) \leq j(D_j)_Y. \] Rounding up is coefficientwise, the comparison is coefficientwise, and $\operatorname{Mob}$ denotes the movable part of the indicated linear system in the source's birational convention. The model may depend on both $i$ and $j$, and the condition must then hold on every dominating model. The discrepancy in this formula is $A(X,B)$, not silently $A(X,S+B)$.

Let $f:X\to Z$ be a pl flipping contraction for $K_X+S+B$, with $Z$ affine. The source's original wording explicitly assumes a birational contraction and that $K_X+S+B$ is anti-ample on its fibers; the corrected statement places this in the more specific pl context. Here $S$ is the distinguished boundary divisor of that context, whose detailed hypotheses and the full pl/plt conditions are not reproduced in the record. Assume $D_\bullet$ is positive, convex and bounded as a characteristic system in the source's sense and satisfies the saturation condition above. The conjecture asks whether \[ R(X,D_\bullet)\quad\text{is finitely generated}. \] The technical definitions of positive, convex, bounded and "pbd-algebra" are not supplied and cannot be dropped.

For interpreting the algebra only, a usual characteristic-system convention writes $M_m=mD_m$ for integral mobile b-divisor data and forms \[ R(X,D_\bullet)=\bigoplus_{m\geq0} H^0(X,\mathcal O_X((M_m)_X)), \] with multiplication provided by the corresponding superadditivity condition on the $M_m$. This display is conditional on that convention; integrality, rounding, mobile traces and the exact source definition need confirmation. Finite generation means finitely many homogeneous elements generate over the degree-zero ring, usually $H^0(Z,\mathcal O_Z)$ in the relative affine setting.

The original passage reports the dimension-two conjecture as holding and says this implies existence of threefold klt flips. The literature assessment checked 21 August 2026 reports further MMP finite-generation/flip results under important hypotheses but says the full stated saturated-pbd conjecture is not settled generally. These qualified historical and dated literature claims are preserved separately from an independently verified general theorem.

Short English statement

In Shokurov's pl-flipping setup, does a positive, convex, bounded characteristic system satisfying the exact birational saturation inequalities produce a finitely generated pbd-algebra? The quantifiers over indices and all higher models are explicit, while the omitted technical system/algebra conventions require source review. The supplied assessment reports dimension-two and important MMP partial results.
