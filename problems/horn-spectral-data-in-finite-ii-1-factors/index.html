Math-1081

Horn Spectral Data in Finite $\mathrm{II}_1$ Factors

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with normalized faithful normal trace $\tau$, so $\tau(1)=1$. For a bounded self-adjoint element $a\in M$, its spectral distribution is the compactly supported probability measure $\mu_a$ on ${\mathbb{R}}$ defined by \[ \mu_a(B)=\tau(1_B(a))\qquad(B\subset{\mathbb{R}}\text{ Borel}), \] using spectral projections. Equivalent spectral data may be expressed by a decreasing quantile, or eigenvalue, function; equality of such functions is understood almost everywhere.

For this given factor $M$, and prescribed distributions $\mu,\nu$, characterize \[ \{\mu_{a+b}:a=a^*,\ b=b^*\in M,\ \mu_a=\mu,\ \mu_b=\nu\}. \] Here the operators vary over all pairs having the prescribed marginal spectral distributions; they are not required to commute or be free. Equivalently, characterize all triples $(\mu_a,\mu_b,\mu_c)$ realized by self-adjoint $a,b,c\in M$ satisfying $a+b+c=0$. In passing between the two forms, $c=-(a+b)$, so $\mu_c$ is the pushforward of $\mu_{a+b}$ under $x\mapsto-x$.

The requested characterization is for an arbitrary $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor as stated. Embeddability is not a hypothesis of the question, and no specific complete system of Horn-type inequalities or sufficiency theorem is supplied in the extraction.

Short English statement

For two prescribed self-adjoint spectral distributions in a fixed $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor, determine all distributions of their sums. The zero-sum triple version uses reflection of the third distribution and imposes no commutativity or freeness.
