Convex Geometry of General Polytope Face Vectors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each integer $d\geq4$, interpret $\mathcal P^d$ conventionally as the class of convex polytopes of dimension $d$. For $P\in\mathcal P^d$, let $f_i(P)$ denote the number of its $i$-dimensional faces, and set \[ f(P)=(f_0(P),\ldots,f_{d-1}(P))\in{\mathbb{R}}^d, \qquad S_d=\{f(P):P\in\mathcal P^d\}. \] Let $\sigma^d$ be a $d$-dimensional simplex and write \[ a_d=f(\sigma^d),\qquad (a_d)_i=\binom{d+1}{i+1}\quad(0\leq i\leq d-1). \] These are conventional interpretations of notation that the supplied record does not explicitly define.

For $S\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}^d$, define its convex hull by finite convex combinations: \[ \operatorname{Conv}(S)=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^m\lambda_js_j:m\geq1,\ s_j\in S,\ \lambda_j\geq0,\ \sum_{j=1}^m\lambda_j=1\right\}. \] One conventional interpretation of the cone with apex $a_d$ is \[ C_d=\operatorname{Cone}_{a_d}(S_d) =a_d+\left\{\sum_{j=1}^m t_j(s_j-a_d):m\geq1,\ s_j\in S_d,\ t_j\geq0\right\}. \] No topological closure is included in this displayed definition. The source does not specify whether its cone notation includes closure, so the following formulation is conditional on this convention.

Questions. For every integer $d\geq4$, does \[ \operatorname{Conv}(S_d)=C_d \] hold? For each such $d$, is $C_d$ finitely generated, meaning that there exist an integer $r\geq1$ and vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_r\in{\mathbb{R}}^d$ such that \[ C_d=a_d+\left\{\sum_{j=1}^r t_jv_j:t_j\geq0\right\}? \] The source additionally asks whether the corresponding rays are dense and which polytopes determine, or lie on, the boundary of this cone. These two requests do not yet specify unique mathematical questions. In particular, the source does not identify the family of rays, the space in which density is intended, or its topology. Possible face-vector rays would have the form \[ a_d+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}\left(f(P)-a_d\right),\qquad f(P)\neq a_d, \] but this family and any density target require confirmation. Likewise, a boundary question must specify whether boundary is taken in ${\mathbb{R}}^d$, relative to $\operatorname{aff}(C_d)$, or in a space of directions. It must also distinguish characterizing polytopes with boundary face vectors from characterizing generators or supporting faces of the cone. No choice among these meanings is asserted here.

Short English statement

For each dimension $d\geq4$, ask whether the convex hull of polytope face vectors equals the cone based at the simplex face vector, and whether that cone has finitely many generators. The source also asks about density of rays and boundary polytopes, but leaves the relevant rays, topology, and boundary convention unspecified. The definitions above therefore give a conditional statement requiring human mathematical review.
