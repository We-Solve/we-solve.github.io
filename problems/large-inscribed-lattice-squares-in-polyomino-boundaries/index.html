Math-7383

Large Inscribed Lattice Squares in Polyomino Boundaries

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A polyomino is understood here in the usual unit-grid sense: a nonempty finite union of closed squares $[i,i+1]\times[j,j+1]$, with $(i,j)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2$, connected through shared edges. Let $P$ be such a polyomino whose boundary $J=\partial P$ is a Jordan curve, and let $\Omega$ be the bounded component of ${\mathbb{R}}^2\setminus J$. Define the greatest side length of an axis-parallel open square in $\Omega$ by \[ s=\max\left\{t>0:\text{there exist }x,y\in{\mathbb{R}}\text{ with }(x,x+t)\times(y,y+t)\subseteq\Omega\right\}. \] The supplied conjecture asks whether, for every such $P$, there exist $v,a,b\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2$ satisfying \[ a\cdot b=0,\qquad \|a\|_2=\|b\|_2=\ell>0, \qquad v,\ v+a,\ v+a+b,\ v+b\in J, \] with \[ \ell\ge \begin{cases} s/\sqrt{2},&s\in 2{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0},\\[2pt] \sqrt{m^2+(m+1)^2},&s=2m+1,\quad m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}. \end{cases} \] Thus the four points must be the vertices of a nondegenerate square; its orientation is unrestricted. Only the vertices are required to lie on $J$; the statement imposes no additional containment condition on its sides or interior.

The source's even/odd formulation presupposes that $s$ is integral, but does not separately state or justify this property. The lattice is interpreted above as the standard square lattice associated with the unit cells. These conventions should be confirmed during mathematical review.

Short English statement

For every polyomino with a simple closed boundary, must that boundary contain four lattice points forming a square whose side satisfies the stated parity-dependent lower bound in terms of the largest axis-parallel open square inside the boundary? The source implicitly treats that largest side length as an integer.
