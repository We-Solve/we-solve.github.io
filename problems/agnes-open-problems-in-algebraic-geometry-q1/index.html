AGNES Open Problems in Algebraic Geometry — Q1

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record does not specify the hypotheses on $X$, the domain of $\beta$, the genus range, or the coefficient rings. The following gives a conventional setting in which its displayed questions are defined; this setting requires confirmation against the source.

Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over ${\mathbb{C}}$, let $g\geq 2$, and let $\beta\in H_2(X,{\mathbb{Z}})$ be a curve class. Let $\overline{M}_g(X,\beta)$ be the moduli stack of connected, unmarked, genus-$g$ stable maps to $X$ representing $\beta$, with virtual fundamental class $[\overline{M}_g(X,\beta)]^{\operatorname{vir}}$. Let \[ \pi:\overline{M}_g(X,\beta)\longrightarrow\overline{M}_g \] forget the map and stabilize the domain, where $\overline{M}_g$ is the moduli stack of stable curves of genus $g$. Use rational Chow groups and rational cohomology, identifying the homological pushforward of the virtual class with its corresponding cohomology class on $\overline{M}_g$.

For stable pairs $(h,n)$, the tautological Chow rings $R^*(\overline{M}_{h,n})\subseteq A^*(\overline{M}_{h,n};{\mathbb{Q}})$ form the smallest system of unital ${\mathbb{Q}}$-subalgebras closed under pushforward by the standard marking-forgetting and boundary-gluing morphisms, including gluing from products. Write $RH^*(\overline{M}_{h,n})$ for their images under the cycle-class map.

Q1 (the primary source item). Is it true, for every $(X,g,\beta)$ in the intended admissible range, that \[ \operatorname{cl}\!\left(\pi_*[\overline{M}_g(X,\beta)]^{\operatorname{vir}}\right) \in RH^*(\overline{M}_g)? \] The supplied excerpt does not determine whether the conventional range stated above is exactly the intended range.

Q2 (also included in the supplied record). Determine when \[ \pi_*[\overline{M}_g(X,\beta)]^{\operatorname{vir}} \in R^*(\overline{M}_g) \subseteq A^*(\overline{M}_g;{\mathbb{Q}}). \]

The source expresses an expectation that Q1 has an affirmative answer. It states that Q1 is affirmative when $X$ is a curve, citing the unresolved reference label [fabp], and that Q2 has counterexamples even when $X$ is a curve. These are supplied source assertions, not independently verified conclusions here. The source further presents as a wild speculation, motivated by the Bloch--Beilinson conjecture, that the Chow membership in Q2 holds whenever $X$ is defined over $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$. In the conventional complex setting, this means that $X$ admits a model over $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$. The excerpt supplies no additional arithmetic condition on $\beta$.

Short English statement

Ask whether pushing the virtual class of the stable-map space to the moduli space of stable curves always gives a tautological cohomology class, and determine when it gives a tautological Chow class. The source expects an affirmative cohomological answer and speculates about a Chow answer for targets defined over $\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$. The ambient hypotheses and the cited curve results require source review.
