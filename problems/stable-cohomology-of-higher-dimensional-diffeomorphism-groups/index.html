Stable Cohomology of Higher-Dimensional Diffeomorphism Groups

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $d$ denote the manifold dimension; the supplied record does not specify its permitted range. Let $\gamma_d\to B\operatorname{SO}(d)$ be the universal oriented real $d$-plane bundle, and let \[ MTSO(d)=\operatorname{Th}(-\gamma_d),\qquad X_d=\Omega^\infty_0MTSO(d), \] where $X_d$ is the path component of the basepoint in the infinite loop space of this Thom spectrum. All cohomology below has coefficients in ${\mathbb{Q}}$. Write \[ L(\gamma_d)=1+L_1(\gamma_d)+L_2(\gamma_d)+\cdots, \qquad L_k(\gamma_d)\in H^{4k}(B\operatorname{SO}(d);{\mathbb{Q}}), \] for the Hirzebruch $L$-class. Under the usual Thom and cohomology-suspension interpretation of the source notation, put \[ \mathrm{th}:H^{4k}(B\operatorname{SO}(d);{\mathbb{Q}})\longrightarrow H^{4k-d}(MTSO(d);{\mathbb{Q}}), \qquad s:H^*(MTSO(d);{\mathbb{Q}})\longrightarrow H^*(X_d;{\mathbb{Q}}), \] and write $s\,\mathrm{th}(L_k)$ for $s(\mathrm{th}(L_k(\gamma_d)))$. These maps explain the notation conventionally; their conventions are not defined in the supplied record.

A homotopy-commutative topological monoid $M$ has a multiplication $\mu:M\times M\to M$ homotopic to its composite with interchange of the factors. Its path components form a monoid $\pi_0(M)$. A natural interpretation of the superscript in the source is invariance under translation by components: for $a\in\pi_0(M)$, choose $m_a\in M$ representing $a$, let $T_a(x)=\mu(m_a,x)$, and set \[ H^*(M;{\mathbb{Q}})^{\pi_0(M)} =\{u\in H^*(M;{\mathbb{Q}}):T_a^*u=u\text{ for every }a\in\pi_0(M)\}. \] This interpretation must be checked against the original report, since the supplied record does not specify the action.

The supplied existence question asks for such a monoid $M$, constructed from classifying spaces of diffeomorphism groups of oriented $d$-manifolds with boundary, with multiplication induced by gluing, and for the intended geometric comparison homomorphism \[ \Phi_d:H^*(X_d;{\mathbb{Q}})\longrightarrow H^*(M;{\mathbb{Q}})^{\pi_0(M)} \] to be surjective with \[ \ker\Phi_d= \begin{cases} 0,&d\text{ even},\\[2pt] \langle s\,\mathrm{th}(L_k):4k\ge d\rangle,&d\text{ odd}. \end{cases} \] Here the angle brackets denote the ideal in $H^*(X_d;{\mathbb{Q}})$ generated by the displayed classes. The inequality $4k\ge d$, including its equality case, is retained exactly from the supplied formulation. The original statement attributes the conjectured kernel to J. Ebert.

The record does not specify the allowable manifolds, compactness or connectedness assumptions, boundary identifications, boundary conditions on diffeomorphisms, the precise gluing construction, or the construction of $\Phi_d$. Consequently, this is a conditional formulation of the recorded target, not a fully specified geometric existence statement. The intended comparison map cannot be replaced without justification by an arbitrary algebra homomorphism.

Short English statement

The question asks whether gluing oriented $d$-manifolds with boundary can produce a homotopy-commutative monoid whose translation-invariant rational cohomology is a quotient of $H^*(\Omega^\infty_0MTSO(d);{\mathbb{Q}})$, with the stated parity-dependent kernel attributed to J. Ebert. The manifold class, gluing data, comparison map, and dimension range remain unspecified. The supplied dated assessment labels the problem partially solved and says that Madsen--Weiss settles the surface case; this assessment has not been independently verified.
