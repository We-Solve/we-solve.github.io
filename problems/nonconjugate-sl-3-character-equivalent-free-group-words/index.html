Math-6577

Nonconjugate $\operatorname{SL}_3$-Character-Equivalent Free-Group Words

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ be the free group on two generators. Define \[ \operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{C}})=\{A\in M_3({\mathbb{C}}):\det A=1\}. \] For a group homomorphism $\rho:F_2\to\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{C}})$, its character is the function $\chi_\rho(w)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(w))$, where $\operatorname{tr}$ is the sum of the diagonal entries. Elements $g,g'\in F_2$ are $\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{C}})$-character equivalent if \[ \operatorname{tr}(\rho(g))=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(g')) \quad\text{for every group homomorphism }\rho:F_2\to\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{C}}). \] They are conjugate in $F_2$ if $g'=hgh^{-1}$ for some $h\in F_2$.

The question is whether \[ \exists g,g'\in F_2\quad \left[ \begin{gathered} \forall\rho\in\operatorname{Hom}(F_2,\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{C}})),\quad \operatorname{tr}(\rho(g))=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(g')),\\ \forall h\in F_2,\quad g'\ne hgh^{-1}. \end{gathered} \right] \] The quantifier ranges over all such representations, with no additional restriction. The supplied background mentions an analogous trace characterization for two generators without specifying that characterization; it is not used as an additional hypothesis here. The supplied open status and source-verification labels are curation claims, not independently verified conclusions of this draft.

Short English statement

Do there exist two nonconjugate elements of the free group on two generators whose traces agree under every representation into $\operatorname{SL}_3({\mathbb{C}})$?
