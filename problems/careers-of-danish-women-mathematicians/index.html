For women mathematicians in twentieth-century Denmark, investigate the following questions:

  1. How did they enter university positions?
  2. What motivated their mathematical interests and their choice of research area?
  3. What was their daily departmental life like?
  4. How did they balance work and family?
  5. What reactions did they experience as some of the few women?
