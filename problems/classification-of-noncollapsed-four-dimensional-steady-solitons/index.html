Math-3850

Classification of Noncollapsed Four-Dimensional Steady Solitons

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A four-dimensional steady gradient Ricci soliton is a triple $(M^4,g,f)$, where $g$ is a smooth Riemannian metric and $f\colon M\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is smooth, satisfying \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g+\nabla_g^2 f=0. \] This fixes the sign convention for the potential; replacing $f$ by $-f$ gives the alternative convention. The curvature operator is the self-adjoint map $\mathcal R_g\colon\Lambda^2T_xM\to\Lambda^2T_xM$ induced by the Riemann curvature tensor. Nonnegative curvature operator means \[ \langle\mathcal R_g\omega,\omega\rangle_g\geq0 \qquad(x\in M,\ \omega\in\Lambda^2T_xM). \]

The source also requires the soliton to be noncollapsed, but does not specify its definition or scale range. One common metric interpretation is that there exists $\kappa>0$ such that, for every $x\in M$ and $r>0$, \[ \sup_{B_g(x,r)}|\operatorname{Rm}_g|\leq r^{-2} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Vol}_g(B_g(x,r))\geq\kappa r^4. \] This formula is a possible convention for review, not an additional hypothesis established by the supplied record; an interpretation using the associated Ricci flow and parabolic neighborhoods would require its own specification.

Let $B^4$ denote the four-dimensional Bryant steady gradient Ricci soliton. Let $\mathcal F_{[5]}$ provisionally denote the particular family described in the original excerpt as the ${\mathbb{Z}}_2\times O(3)$-symmetric solitons constructed in reference [5]. Here ${\mathbb{Z}}_2$ is the cyclic group of order two, and $O(3)=\{A\in{\mathbb{R}}^{3\times3}:A^{\mathsf T}A=I\}$. Symmetry refers to an isometric group action, but the intended action, its effectiveness, its action on the potential, and the defining construction and parameters of $\mathcal F_{[5]}$ are not supplied. In particular, $\mathcal F_{[5]}$ cannot be defined merely as all solitons having this symmetry.

Source conjecture, with unresolved scope. Every noncollapsed four-dimensional steady gradient Ricci soliton with nonnegative curvature operator belongs to the Bryant model or to $\mathcal F_{[5]}$. The source does not specify whether membership is understood up to isometry, constant metric rescaling, or another equivalence of soliton triples. It also does not explicitly impose completeness, noncompactness, nonflatness, simple connectivity, or exclusion of product solitons and quotients. These omissions prevent an unqualified precise classification statement; none of these hypotheses is inserted here.

The second assertion is that every member of $\mathcal F_{[5]}$ has a ray as its blow-down. A ray means the metric space $([0,\infty),d_{\mathrm{Euc}})$. For a metric interpretation, a pointed blow-down at $p\in M$ would be a pointed Gromov--Hausdorff limit \[ (M,\lambda_j g,p)\longrightarrow(X,d_X,p_X), \qquad \lambda_j>0,\quad\lambda_j\longrightarrow0. \] The intended conclusion would identify the limit with \[ (X,d_X,p_X)\cong([0,\infty),d_{\mathrm{Euc}},0). \] However, the supplied record does not specify the convergence topology, basepoint convention, existence of limits, or whether the assertion concerns one subsequential limit, every subsequential limit, or convergence along every sequence of scales. Thus this display records a candidate interpretation of the claimed ray limit, not a resolved quantification of the source assertion.

Additional questions in the original excerpt. The excerpt also asks about $O(2)$-symmetry for three-dimensional ancient Ricci flows and for higher-dimensional steady gradient solitons with positive curvature operator, and about $O(3)$-symmetry for all noncollapsed four-dimensional steady gradient solitons. An ancient Ricci flow satisfies $\partial_tg(t)=-2\operatorname{Ric}_{g(t)}$ on a time interval extending to $-\infty$; positive curvature operator means strict positivity on every nonzero two-form. The excerpt supplies no further hypotheses or precise meaning of these symmetry assertions. The four-dimensional symmetry question does not explicitly include the nonnegative-curvature-operator assumption of the main conjecture. These questions are retained as contextual questions, not silently incorporated as established statements or additional hypotheses.

Short English statement

The supplied conjecture seeks to classify noncollapsed four-dimensional steady gradient Ricci solitons with nonnegative curvature operator as the Bryant soliton or members of a particular ${\mathbb{Z}}_2\times O(3)$-symmetric family, and asserts that members of that family have a ray as their blow-down. The family, admissible geometric hypotheses, equivalence relation, noncollapse convention, and blow-down quantifiers require clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
