Math-6934

Optimization over Nonzero Littlewood–Richardson Coefficients

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A partition $\alpha$ is a finite weakly decreasing sequence of positive integers. Write $|\alpha|=\sum_i\alpha_i$ for its size and $\ell(\alpha)$ for its length. For a nonempty partition $\alpha$, put $m=|\alpha|$, $r=\ell(\alpha)$, and define \[ \eta(\alpha)=m^2+\frac{r(r-1)(2r-1)}{6}-\sum_{i=1}^{r}\left(\alpha_i-(i-1)\right)^2. \] Thus, in evaluating $\eta(\mu)$ and $\eta(\nu)$, the size and length are those of the respective partition. The usual empty-partition and empty-sum conventions would extend this definition by $\eta(\varnothing)=0$; the supplied record does not explicitly specify whether empty partitions are admitted.

For partitions $\lambda,\mu,\nu$, let $c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}$ denote the Littlewood--Richardson coefficient, defined by the Schur-function expansion \[ s_\mu s_\nu=\sum_\lambda c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}s_\lambda. \] Define \[ \Delta(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=\eta(\lambda)-\eta(\mu)-\eta(\nu). \] The supplied problem asks for the exact maximum of $\Delta$, given $n,k,d$ with $k\le n/2$, subject to \[ |\lambda|=n,\qquad |\mu|=k,\qquad |\nu|=n-k,\qquad c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\ne0. \] The size conditions require nonnegative integer sizes, and $d$, as a partition length, must be an integer. However, the source does not explicitly give the parameter ranges or boundary conventions.

Unresolved length qualification. The introductory definition describes $\lambda$ as having length $d$, but the subsequent list of optimization constraints does not explicitly repeat a length condition. Under the reading that this introductory condition remains in force, the requested quantity is \[ M(n,k,d)=\max\left\{\Delta(\lambda,\mu,\nu): \begin{array}{l} \lambda,\mu,\nu\text{ are partitions},\quad \ell(\lambda)=d,\\ |\lambda|=n,\quad |\mu|=k,\quad |\nu|=n-k,\\ c^{\lambda}_{\mu,\nu}\ne0 \end{array}\right\}, \] for parameters with a nonempty feasible set. This is a conditional formulation requiring review: the supplied record does not explicitly resolve whether the intended restriction is exactly $\ell(\lambda)=d$, a bound on lengths, or only the displayed size and nonvanishing constraints. With only the latter constraints, the maximum has no dependence on $d$. No value is specified for an empty feasible set.

Short English statement

Determine the largest value of $\eta(\lambda)-\eta(\mu)-\eta(\nu)$ among partitions of the prescribed sizes whose Littlewood--Richardson coefficient is nonzero. The role of the given length parameter $d$, the permitted boundary cases, and empty feasible sets require clarification. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status.
