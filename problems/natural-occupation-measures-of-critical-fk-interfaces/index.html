Statement recorded in the supplied source.

Natural occupation measures of critical FK interfaces

OpenAI's claim

OpenAI presents a proof in the selected manuscript. For $1\leq q<4$, the rescaled edge-counting measure of the critical square FK interface is claimed to converge with the curve to its SLE Minkowski-content measure.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Fix $q\in[1,4)$, $p_q=\sqrt q/(1+\sqrt q)$, $\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)$, and $d=1+\kappa/8$. On mesh $1/n$ in $D=(0,1)^2$, use critical FK weights and wired/free boundary arcs between $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$. Let $\eta_n$ be the oriented Dobrushin strand, and $z_{n,j}$ its medial-edge traversal midpoints, counting boundary traversals but not terminal half-edges. With $\Pi$ the coordinatewise projection onto $[0,1]^2$, the source claims a constant $c(q)>0$ such that \[ ([\eta_n],c(q)n^{-d}\sum_{j=1}^{N_n}\delta_{\Pi(z_{n,j})}) \Longrightarrow([\eta],\mu_\eta). \] Here $\eta$ is chordal $\operatorname{SLE}_\kappa$, curves retain order modulo increasing reparametrization, and measures converge weakly on $\overline D$. The limiting content measure is the weak limit of $r^{d-2}\Pi_*[\mathbf1_{\{\operatorname{dist}(z,\eta)<r\}}dA(z)]$, using whole-plane area. The two limit coordinates belong to the same random curve.

Outline of the argument

Row slicing and angular electric spectra determine Coulomb correlations with quantitative remainders. A two-arm outward coupling fixes the single-edge amplitude, and integrated correlation bounds identify the occupation measure and its total mass with one deterministic normalization.
