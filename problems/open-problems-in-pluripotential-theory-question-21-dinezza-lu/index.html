Open Problems in Pluripotential Theory — Question 21 (DiNezza-Lu)

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $(X,\omega)$ be a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension $n$, and let $D\subset X$ be a divisor, with $X\setminus D$ understood as the complement of its support. Fix the convention $dd^c=\frac{i}{\pi}\partial\bar\partial$. A function $u\colon X\to[-\infty,\infty)$ is $\omega$-plurisubharmonic if it is locally the sum of a plurisubharmonic function and a smooth function, is locally integrable, and satisfies $\omega+dd^c u\geq 0$ as a current. Its non-pluripolar Monge--Ampère measure is \[ \left\langle(\omega+dd^c u)^n\right\rangle =\lim_{j\to\infty}\mathbf{1}_{\{u>-j\}} \left(\omega+dd^c\max\{u,-j\}\right)^n, \] where the measures for the bounded truncations are taken in the Bedford--Taylor sense. Interpret the source notation $\mathcal E(X,\omega)$ as the full Monge--Ampère mass class \[ \mathcal E(X,\omega)=\left\{u\text{ is }\omega\text{-plurisubharmonic}:\ \int_X\left\langle(\omega+dd^c u)^n\right\rangle =\int_X\omega^n\right\}. \] Question (DiNezza--Lu, as attributed in the supplied record). For every such $(X,\omega)$ and $D$, every integrable density $f\in L^1(X,\omega^n)$ whose restriction to $X\setminus D$ is smooth, and every $\varphi\in\mathcal E(X,\omega)$ satisfying \[ \left\langle(\omega+dd^c\varphi)^n\right\rangle=f\,\omega^n \] as an equality of measures on $X$, must one have \[ \varphi|_{X\setminus D}\in C^\infty(X\setminus D)? \] The equation and the full mass condition imply $f\geq0$ almost everywhere and $\int_X f\,\omega^n=\int_X\omega^n$. The supplied statement imposes no strict positivity condition on $f$, even on $X\setminus D$; this absence is retained. The full mass interpretation and the normalization of $dd^c$ above are explicit conventions, since the supplied excerpt does not define them.

Short English statement

On a compact Kähler manifold, suppose a full mass $\omega$-plurisubharmonic potential solves the complex Monge--Ampère equation with an integrable density that is smooth away from a divisor. Must the potential also be smooth away from that divisor? The supplied wording includes densities that may vanish there.
