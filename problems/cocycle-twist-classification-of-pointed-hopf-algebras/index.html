Cocycle-Twist Classification of Pointed Hopf Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Work over ${\mathbb{C}}$. A Hopf algebra $H$ is pointed if every simple subcoalgebra is one-dimensional. Its group of group-like elements is \[ G=G(H)=\{g\in H:\Delta(g)=g\otimes g,\ \varepsilon(g)=1\}. \] For finite-dimensional pointed $H$, the group $G$ is finite and the coradical is $H_0={\mathbb{C}} G$. Let \[ \operatorname{gr}H=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}H_n/H_{n-1},\qquad H_{-1}=0, \] be the associated graded Hopf algebra for the coradical filtration. Write $\pi:\operatorname{gr}H\to{\mathbb{C}} G$ for its degree-zero projection and define its diagram by \[ R=(\operatorname{gr}H)^{\operatorname{co}\pi} =\{x\in\operatorname{gr}H:(\operatorname{id}\otimes\pi)\Delta(x)=x\otimes1\}. \] Then $R$ is a graded braided Hopf algebra in the category of left Yetter--Drinfeld modules over ${\mathbb{C}} G$, and $\operatorname{gr}H\cong R\#{\mathbb{C}} G$. The infinitesimal braiding of $H$ is $V=R_1$.

A left Yetter--Drinfeld module over ${\mathbb{C}} G$ is a $G$-graded vector space $V=\bigoplus_{g\in G}V_g$, equipped with a left $G$-action satisfying $h\cdot V_g\subseteq V_{hgh^{-1}}$. Its braiding is \[ c(v\otimes w)=g\cdot w\otimes v\qquad(v\in V_g). \] The Nichols algebra $\mathcal B(V)$ is the quotient of the braided tensor Hopf algebra $T(V)$, with $V$ primitive, by the largest homogeneous braided Hopf ideal contained in $\bigoplus_{n\geq2}T^n(V)$.

The bosonization $A=\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G$ has vector space $\mathcal B(V)\otimes{\mathbb{C}} G$. Writing $r\#g$ for $r\otimes g$, its multiplication and coproduct are \[ (r\#g)(s\#h)=r(g\cdot s)\#gh, \] \[ \Delta_A(r\#g)=\sum \left(r_{(1)}\#(r_{(2)})_{(-1)}g\right) \otimes\left((r_{(2)})_{(0)}\#g\right), \] where $\Delta_{\mathcal B(V)}(r)=\sum r_{(1)}\otimes r_{(2)}$ is the braided coproduct and $\delta(s)=\sum s_{(-1)}\otimes s_{(0)}$ is the ${\mathbb{C}} G$-coaction.

A normalized convolution-invertible Hopf $2$-cocycle on $A$ is a linear map $\sigma:A\otimes A\to{\mathbb{C}}$ such that, for all $a,b,c\in A$, \[ \sigma(1,a)=\sigma(a,1)=\varepsilon(a), \] \[ \sum\sigma(a_{(1)},b_{(1)})\sigma(a_{(2)}b_{(2)},c) =\sum\sigma(b_{(1)},c_{(1)})\sigma(a,b_{(2)}c_{(2)}). \] Convolution is defined by $(f*g)(a,b)=\sum f(a_{(1)},b_{(1)})g(a_{(2)},b_{(2)})$, with unit $(a,b)\mapsto\varepsilon(a)\varepsilon(b)$. The cocycle deformation $A_\sigma$ has the coalgebra of $A$ and multiplication \[ a\cdot_\sigma b=\sum\sigma(a_{(1)},b_{(1)})a_{(2)}b_{(2)}\sigma^{-1}(a_{(3)},b_{(3)}). \] Thus "cocycle twist" here means this multiplication deformation by a functional cocycle.

Conjecture 1 (supplied source). For every finite-dimensional pointed Hopf algebra $H$ over ${\mathbb{C}}$, with $G=G(H)$ and infinitesimal braiding $V$, there exists a normalized convolution-invertible Hopf $2$-cocycle $\sigma$ on $\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G$ and an isomorphism of Hopf algebras \[ H\cong\left(\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G\right)_\sigma. \] In particular, $\operatorname{gr}H\cong\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G$ is not an additional hypothesis of this conjecture; the source separates that issue from the deformation question below.

Two constituent questions in the source. Independently, let $G$ be a group and let $V$ be a Yetter--Drinfeld module over ${\mathbb{C}} G$ with $\dim\mathcal B(V)<\infty$.

  1. Is $\mathcal B(V)$ the only finite-dimensional post-Nichols algebra of $V$? Using the usual coradically graded convention, a post-Nichols algebra here is a connected graded braided Hopf algebra $S$ in this Yetter--Drinfeld category, with $S_1\cong V$, whose grading is its coradical grading. The question asks whether every such finite-dimensional $S$ is isomorphic to $\mathcal B(V)$, compatibly with its specified degree-one part; equivalently, whether it is generated in degree one. The supplied excerpt does not itself define "post-Nichols algebra", so this convention requires source review.
  2. For every finite-dimensional pointed Hopf algebra $H$ satisfying $\operatorname{gr}H\cong\mathcal B(V)\#{\mathbb{C}} G$, is $H$ a cocycle deformation of this bosonization as above? Such an $H$ is called a lifting. The finite-dimensional lifting setting forces the relevant group $G$ to be finite.

Supplied qualifications. The excerpt reports affirmative answers to both questions when $G$ is abelian and for every $V$ then known to the source; the latter phrase does not specify a quantified class. It reports a negative answer to the first question in positive characteristic, which lies outside the present ground field ${\mathbb{C}}$. For the first question, it cites report reference [7] for the abelian case and a strategy in [5, Proposition 5.4] requiring defining relations of $\mathcal B(V)$; it says alternative proofs [13] and [10] still require some relations and asks whether a proof avoiding this information exists. For the second question, it cites [8] for the abelian case, says known proofs again depend on defining relations, and requests a more abstract proof. It also reports that when $G$ is finite abelian and $\gcd(|G|,210)=1$, relations of every lifting can be described explicitly [6]; beyond this case, several liftings had been computed but their relations were described as complicated. These are supplied source qualifications, not independently verified results or a new literature assessment.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every finite-dimensional pointed Hopf algebra over ${\mathbb{C}}$ is obtained from the Nichols algebra of its infinitesimal braiding, combined with its group algebra, by a convolution-invertible Hopf $2$-cocycle deformation. The source separates generation in degree one from deformation of liftings and also asks for proofs avoiding explicit defining relations. Its open-status label and reported special cases are supplied assessments requiring human review.
