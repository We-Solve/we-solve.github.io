Math-6521

Shift Chain Colorings and Disk Covering Paths

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shift-chain coloring. Let $k\geq 1$ be an integer and let $(V,<)$ be a linearly ordered vertex set. A $k$-uniform hypergraph is a pair $H=(V,E)$, where every $e\in E$ is a $k$-element subset of $V$. Write each edge uniquely as $e=\{e_1<\cdots<e_k\}$. The hypergraph is a shift-chain if, for every $e,f\in E$, \[ \left(e_i\leq f_i\text{ for every }1\leq i\leq k\right) \quad\text{or}\quad \left(f_i\leq e_i\text{ for every }1\leq i\leq k\right). \] A proper two-coloring is a map $\chi:V\to\{0,1\}$ such that every edge contains vertices of both colors.

The question asks whether there exists an integer $k_0\geq 1$ such that, for every integer $k\geq k_0$, every $k$-uniform shift-chain has a proper two-coloring. The supplied record does not specify whether only finite vertex sets are intended or whether arbitrary linearly ordered vertex sets are allowed. These are distinct scope choices, and neither is selected here as the definitive formulation.

Paths in a doubly covered region. Let $\mathcal D$ be a family of closed Euclidean unit discs covering ${\mathbb{R}}^2$; thus every member has the form \[ \overline B(a,1)=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:\|x-a\|_2\leq 1\}, \qquad a\in{\mathbb{R}}^2, \] and $\bigcup_{D\in\mathcal D}D={\mathbb{R}}^2$. Define its doubly covered region by \[ T(\mathcal D)=\{x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:x\text{ belongs to at least two members of }\mathcal D\}. \] The literal corrected question asks whether there exists a universal constant $c\geq 0$ such that, for every such covering $\mathcal D$ and every $p,q\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$, there is a rectifiable continuous path $\gamma:[0,1]\to{\mathbb{R}}^2$ satisfying \[ \gamma(0)=p,\qquad \gamma(1)=q,\qquad \gamma([0,1])\subseteq T(\mathcal D),\qquad \operatorname{length}(\gamma)\leq\sqrt{2}\,\|p-q\|_2+c. \] Here the same $c$ must work for all coverings and all endpoint pairs, including coincident endpoints. Containment of the entire path forces both endpoints to belong to $T(\mathcal D)$, whereas the supplied statement says any two points without imposing that restriction. It is unresolved whether the intended quantifier is instead $p,q\in T(\mathcal D)$, or whether another endpoint convention or covering hypothesis is missing. No local finiteness assumption is supplied.

The original statement gives the coefficient $2$ in place of $\sqrt{2}$. The supplied corrected statement and its verification note explicitly identify $\sqrt{2}$ as the restored coefficient; that is the coefficient displayed above. This records the supplied correction without independently validating it.

Short English statement

The first question asks whether all shift-chains admit a coloring with no monochromatic edge once the edge size is sufficiently large; the intended size class of vertex sets is unspecified. The second asks for a universal additive constant in a $\sqrt{2}\,d+c$ bound for paths through the doubly covered part of a closed-unit-disc covering. Its endpoint quantifiers require clarification. The supplied record labels the combined problem partially solved; this draft does not independently verify that assessment.
