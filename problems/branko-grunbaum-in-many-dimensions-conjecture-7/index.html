Branko Grunbaum in Many Dimensions — Conjecture 7

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a finite simplicial complex $S$, let $f_i(S)$ denote the number of its $i$-dimensional simplices, and let $\dim S=\max\{i:f_i(S)>0\}$. Write $|S|$ for its geometric realization. A topological embedding into ${\mathbb{R}}^{2d}$ is a continuous map $e:|S|\to{\mathbb{R}}^{2d}$ that is a homeomorphism onto its image.

The supplied statement uses the terms "$d$-complex" and "embeddable" without specifying their conventions. Under the interpretation of finite simplicial complexes and topological embeddings, the conjecture attributed in the source to Grünbaum--Kalai--Sarkaria is: \[ \forall d\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}\ \exists C_d\in{\mathbb{R}}_{\ge0}\ \forall S,\qquad \left(\dim S=d\ \text{and } |S|\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{R}}^{2d}\right) \Longrightarrow f_d(S)\le C_d f_{d-1}(S), \] where $S$ ranges over finite simplicial complexes and $C_d$ depends only on $d$. The finiteness, simplicial-complex, and topological-embedding conventions require confirmation against the intended source; they are not explicit in the supplied excerpt.

Short English statement

For each integer $d\ge1$, is there a constant depending only on $d$ that bounds the number of $d$-faces by that constant times the number of $(d-1)$-faces for every $d$-complex embeddable in ${\mathbb{R}}^{2d}$? The displayed formulation uses finite simplicial complexes and topological embeddings, subject to confirmation of the source conventions.
