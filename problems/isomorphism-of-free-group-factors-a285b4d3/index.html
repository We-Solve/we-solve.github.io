Math-913

Isomorphism of Free Group Factors

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For $n\in\{2,3\}$, let $\mathbb F_n$ be the free group on $n$ generators, and let $\ell^2(\mathbb F_n)$ be the complex Hilbert space of square-summable functions on $\mathbb F_n$. Its left regular representation is given by \[ (\lambda_n(g)\xi)(h)=\xi(g^{-1}h) \qquad(g,h\in\mathbb F_n,\ \xi\in\ell^2(\mathbb F_n)). \] Define the free group von Neumann algebra, called the free group factor, by \[ L(\mathbb F_n)=\overline{\operatorname{span}_{{\mathbb{C}}}\{\lambda_n(g):g\in\mathbb F_n\}}^{\mathrm{WOT}} \subseteq B(\ell^2(\mathbb F_n)), \] where $B(H)$ denotes the bounded linear operators on $H$, and $\mathrm{WOT}$ denotes the weak operator topology, determined by the matrix coefficients $T\mapsto\langle T\xi,\eta\rangle$.

Question. Does there exist a normal unital complex-linear $*$-isomorphism \[ \Theta:L(\mathbb F_2)\longrightarrow L(\mathbb F_3)? \] Here an isomorphism is a bijective map preserving multiplication, adjoints, and the identity; normality means preservation of suprema of bounded increasing nets of positive operators. This expresses isomorphism as von Neumann algebras, the standard interpretation of the supplied question about free group factors.

The original statement also appends \[ \begin{aligned} &\phi(a_1b_1)\phi(a_2b_2)-\phi(a_1a_2b_1b_2)\\ &\qquad=\left(\phi(a_1b_1)-\phi(a_1)\phi(b_1)\right) \left(\phi(a_2b_2)-\phi(a_2)\phi(b_2)\right). \end{aligned} \] The record supplies neither definitions nor hypotheses for $\phi,a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2$, and gives no connection between this formula and the isomorphism question. Short English statement

The question asks whether the complex von Neumann algebras generated by the left regular representations of the free groups on two and three generators are isomorphic. The supplied record labels the problem open; this draft does not independently verify that status. An appended formula in the original extraction lacks definitions and hypotheses.
