For each fixed FK weight $0<q<4$, do canonical flag-triangulation conformal embeddings of spherical n-edge FK maps admit deterministic distance rescalings $a_n\to 0$ so that the area measure, the entire distance graph and every macroscopic nested loop converge jointly, through all n, to the corresponding Liouville quantum sphere and CLE decoration? Conformal metric and loop convergence of FK maps below four

OpenAI's claim

The linked manuscripts present the following claims by OpenAI: For each $0<q<4$, flag-triangle embeddings of finite FK maps are claimed to converge with area, graph metric, and all nested interfaces to a Liouville sphere with an independent conformal loop ensemble; Subcritical FK-decorated spherical maps and tree-weighted maps are claimed to have their corresponding ordinary Liouville quantum spheres as metric-measure limits through all edge counts. Together they give a claimed affirmative answer in the matching scope.

Mathematical statement of the claimed result

Result 1.
Fix $0<q<4$ and set $q=2+2\cos(\pi\gamma^2/2)$, $\kappa=16/\gamma^2$, $\sqrt2<\gamma<2$. Rooted $n$-edge FK map/subset pairs have weight $q^{\ell/2}$. Their $4n$ equilateral flag triangles carry normalized area $m_n$, and three independent area marks fix the uniformization $\phi_n$ to $0,1,\infty$. With all-edge distance $d_n$, define \[ K_n(a)=\{(\phi_n(u),\phi_n(v),a d_n(u,v)):u,v\in V(M_n)\}. \] The source claims a deterministic $a_n\to0$ giving \[ ((\phi_n)_*m_n,K_n(a_n),\Gamma_n) \Longrightarrow(\mu_h,\{(z,w,D_h(z,w))\},\Gamma), \] for the similarly marked ordinary unit-area $\gamma$-LQG sphere and a nested $\operatorname{CLE}_\kappa$ independent of the embedded surface. Area, distance graphs, and curves use weak, Hausdorff, and uniform loop-matching topologies, respectively. Maximum embedded flag-triangle diameter vanishes in probability.
Result 2.
For each fixed $q\in(0,4)$, sample rooted $n$-edge planar map/subset pairs $(M,A)$ with weight $q^{\ell(M,A)/2}$, $\ell=2k(A)+|A|-|V(M)|$. Separately, sample rooted map/spanning-tree pairs uniformly. Distances $d_n$ use every primal edge and $\mu_n(v)=\deg(v)/(2n)$. The source claims deterministic $a_n>0$, $a_n\to0$, such that \[ (V(M_n),a_nd_n,\mu_n)\Longrightarrow(S,D_h,\mu_h) \] in Gromov--Hausdorff--Prokhorov law, through all integer sizes. The limiting sphere has the ordinary LQG law normalized to area one: $q=2+2\cos(\pi\gamma^2/2)$, $\sqrt2<\gamma<2$, for FK, and $\gamma=\sqrt2$ for spanning trees. Root and decoration are forgotten.
