Zeta Moments from Shifted Divisor Correlations

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the meromorphic continuation of the Riemann zeta function, initially defined by $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$. For a positive integer $k$ and a real number $T>0$, define the critical-line moment \[ M_k(T)=\int_0^T\left|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)\right|^{2k}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t. \] The critical line, integration interval, and positive-integer range of $k$ are conventional interpretations used here to display the question; the supplied record does not explicitly specify them.

For such $k$, the ordinary $k$-fold divisor function is \[ d_k(n)=\#\{(n_1,\ldots,n_k)\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}^{\,k}:n_1\cdots n_k=n\},\qquad n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}. \] A standard example of a shifted-divisor correlation is \[ D_k(X;h)=\sum_{\substack{n\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1}\\n\leq X,\ n+h\geq1}}d_k(n)d_k(n+h), \qquad X\geq1,\quad h\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] This example fixes notation only: the source does not identify the precise shifted divisor sums required, and it does not specify whether ordinary divisor coefficients or coefficients carrying additional complex shifts are intended.

Requested derivation. Obtain an arithmetic derivation of the conjectured $2k$th moments of the Riemann zeta function from shifted-divisor problems, including the required $k$-fold convolutions of shifted divisor sums. Under the conventional moment interpretation above, this asks for a derivation of the intended conjectural asymptotics of $M_k(T)$ as $T\to\infty$.

Unresolved specifications. The supplied record gives neither the exact moment asymptotic nor its constants, lower-order terms, or error term. It also gives no definition of the required $k$-fold convolution, no shift ranges, and no uniformity or error estimates for the shifted-divisor problems. Consequently, the requested derivation cannot be made into a uniquely specified theorem from this record alone. In particular, the displayed example $D_k(X;h)$ must not be treated as a complete specification of the required arithmetic input.

Short English statement

The problem asks for an arithmetic derivation of conjectured zeta-function moments using shifted divisor sums and their required $k$-fold convolutions. The exact moment conjecture, convolution operation, parameter ranges, and necessary estimates are unspecified.
