Holomorphic Symplectic Geometry — Question 5

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied excerpt does not state the hypotheses on $X$ or the list of cases referred to by "some more cases." The following formulation is conditional on the intended setting being irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifolds of complex dimension four; this setting requires verification against the surrounding source text.

In this conditional formulation, an irreducible holomorphic symplectic fourfold is a simply connected compact Kähler manifold $X$ of complex dimension four such that \[ H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)={\mathbb{C}}\sigma, \] where $\sigma$ is an everywhere nondegenerate holomorphic two-form. Write \[ b_2(X)=\dim_{{\mathbb{Q}}}H^2(X,{\mathbb{Q}}). \] With the usual interpretation of the source notation, let $S$ be a K3 surface and let $T$ be a complex torus of complex dimension two. The space $S^{[2]}$ is the Hilbert scheme of length-two zero-dimensional subschemes of $S$. The generalized Kummer fourfold $K_2(T)$ is the fiber over $0\in T$ of the composite \[ T^{[3]}\longrightarrow \operatorname{Sym}^3(T)\xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{sum}\;}T, \] where the first map sends a length-three subscheme to its associated cycle. Two compact complex manifolds are deformation equivalent if they are connected by a finite chain of smooth proper holomorphic families over connected bases, with consecutive manifolds occurring as fibers of the same family.

The supplied source states the comparison values \[ b_2(S^{[2]})=23,\qquad b_2(K_2(T))=7, \] and refers to its Section 1.2 and to a reference labeled [G] for further information on Betti numbers.

Question, conditional on the scope above. Which additional cases for $b_2(X)$ can be excluded from the source's preceding list? That list is absent from the supplied record. In particular, determine whether \[ \text{for every irreducible holomorphic symplectic fourfold }X,\qquad b_2(X)\ne 3. \] Also determine whether, for every such $X$, \[ b_2(X)=23\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{there exists a K3 surface }S\text{ such that }X\text{ is deformation equivalent to }S^{[2]}. \] These are questions, not asserted conclusions. Without confirmation of the omitted hypotheses on $X$, this conditional formulation does not constitute a fully determined transcription of the source question.

Short English statement

Under the explicitly provisional interpretation that $X$ is an irreducible holomorphic symplectic fourfold, ask whether its second Betti number can equal $3$, and whether second Betti number $23$ forces deformation equivalence to the Hilbert square of a K3 surface. The broader request to exclude additional cases cannot be specified because the preceding list and hypotheses are missing.
