Math-4675

Bott–Chern and Aeppli Cohomology for Almost-Complex Manifolds

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $M$ be a smooth real manifold of dimension $2n$ with an almost-complex structure $J$, meaning a smooth bundle endomorphism $J:TM\to TM$ satisfying $J^2=-\mathrm{id}_{TM}$. Write $\Omega^{p,q}(M,J)$ for the smooth complex-valued forms of type $(p,q)$, where $0\leq p,q\leq n$. The exterior derivative decomposes according to type as \[ d=\mu+\partial+\bar\partial+\bar\mu, \] with respective bidegrees $(2,-1)$, $(1,0)$, $(0,1)$, and $(-1,2)$. Without integrability, $\partial^2$ and $\bar\partial^2$ need not vanish, so the classical formulas below cannot simply be assumed to define cohomology groups.

For comparison, when $J$ is integrable, one has $d=\partial+\bar\partial$, $\partial^2=\bar\partial^2=0$, and $\partial\bar\partial+\bar\partial\partial=0$. The classical groups are \[ H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\ker(\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q}\to\Omega^{p,q+1})}{\bar\partial\Omega^{p,q-1}}, \] \[ H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\{\alpha\in\Omega^{p,q}:\partial\alpha=0,\ \bar\partial\alpha=0\}}{\partial\bar\partial\Omega^{p-1,q-1}}, \qquad H_A^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\ker(\partial\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q}\to\Omega^{p+1,q+1})}{\partial\Omega^{p-1,q}+\bar\partial\Omega^{p,q-1}}. \] Here the form spaces in these formulas are on $M$, and form spaces with indices outside $0,\ldots,n$ are zero. Complex de Rham cohomology is \[ H_{\mathrm{dR}}^k(M;{\mathbb{C}})=\frac{\ker(d:\Omega^k(M;{\mathbb{C}})\to\Omega^{k+1}(M;{\mathbb{C}}))}{d\Omega^{k-1}(M;{\mathbb{C}})}. \] In the integrable case, representatives induce natural maps \[ H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)\longrightarrow H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(M)\longrightarrow H_A^{p,q}(M), \] and a $d$-closed representative gives a map $H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)\to H_{\mathrm{dR}}^{p+q}(M;{\mathbb{C}})$. Complex conjugation exchanges bidegrees $(p,q)$ and $(q,p)$ on Bott--Chern and Aeppli cohomology and squares to the identity. Wedge product makes Bott--Chern cohomology a bigraded algebra and Aeppli cohomology a bigraded module over it.

Construction problem. For an almost-complex manifold $(M,J)$, find suitable Bott--Chern-type and Aeppli-type cohomology groups which recover these classical groups when $J$ is integrable, complete the source's requested "vertical row" of the Dolbeault--de Rham diagram, respect the intended natural maps and spectral sequences, carry a real structure, and possess the expected algebra and module structures.

Unresolved qualifications. It also does not specify the grading or precise axioms for the proposed groups, real structure, algebra, and module. The classical structures above give reference meanings, but do not determine a unique extension to the nonintegrable setting. No compactness, boundary, or auxiliary metric hypothesis is supplied. Consequently this is a construction request with unresolved specifications, rather than a fully determined existence assertion. The supplied literature assessment also mentions a canonical almost-complex $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma, but states no version of that lemma or requirement that it hold.

Short English statement

The problem asks for analogues of Bott--Chern and Aeppli cohomology on almost-complex manifolds that recover the integrable theory and fit the intended comparison maps, spectral sequences, real structure, and multiplication structures. The source omits the comparison diagram and several required axioms, so the precise construction target remains partly unspecified.
