Verbatim supplied catalogue question

For the remaining considered cases of the structured nonconvex subset of the pooling problem, is the conjectured full description of its convex hull correct?

Source limitation and later primary model

The OWR passage, printed pages 2715--2716, does not list the parameters, remaining cases, or proposed inequalities. The source record therefore stays qualified as unrecoverable. A primary-source search found the later paper by Luedtke, D'Ambrosio, Linderoth and Schweiger, arXiv:1803.02955, published in SIAM Journal on Optimization in 2020. Its introduction and Sections 2--3 were inspected. It describes a five-variable aggregate model and reports full hull descriptions in three parameter cases. The complete proofs of those three cases and their exact correspondence to the unnamed 2015 remaining cases have not been audited. No full solution is inferred from the abstract.

We can nevertheless test a source-specific mathematical route on an explicitly specified specialization of its displayed model. In the later paper take capacity $C=1$, pool-quality bounds $0\le t\le1$, and bypass-quality bounds $-1\le y/z\le0$, interpreted linearly at $z=0$. The set is \[ T=\{(x,u,y,z,t):0\le x\le1,\ 0\le t\le1,\ z\ge0,\ x+z\le1, -z\le y\le0,\ y+u\le0,\ u=xt\}. \] This specialization is an auxiliary test, not a silently substituted statement of the missing original conjecture.

The isolated bilinear hull and the failed intersection step

For the graph $G=\{(x,t,u):0\le x,t\le1,u=xt\}$, its convex hull is exactly \[ 0\le x,t\le1,\qquad u\ge0,\quad u\ge x+t-1,\quad u\le x,\quad u\le t. \] Validity on the graph follows from the bounds. Conversely any point satisfying these inequalities is the convex combination of the four graph corner points $(0,0,0),(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(1,1,1)$ with respective weights \[ 1-x-t+u,\quad x-u,\quad t-u,\quad u. \] They are nonnegative, sum to one, and reproduce all three coordinates. This proves the isolated hull with no external envelope theorem.

An attempted hull description for $T$ replaces only $u=xt$ by those four inequalities and retains the other linear constraints. It fails at \[ (x,u,y,z,t)=(1/2,1/2,-1/2,1/2,1/2). \] All linear side constraints and all four bilinear-hull inequalities hold. We next derive a convex inequality valid on $T$ that excludes this point, so it is not even in $\operatorname{conv}T$.

A complete nonlinear certificate

For a point of $T$, $u=xt\ge0$ and $u\le-y\le z\le1-x$, so $u+x\le1$. Multiplication by $u\ge0$ gives \[ u(u+x)\le u=xt,\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad u^2\le x(t-u). \] Here $x\ge0$ and $t-u=t(1-x)\ge0$. The set $\{(x,t,u):x\ge0,t-u\ge0,u^2\le x(t-u)\}$ is convex. Indeed for any finite convex combination of feasible triples, write $a_i=x_i,b_i=t_i-u_i$ and weights $\theta_i$. Then \[ \left|\sum_i\theta_i u_i\right| \le\sum_i\theta_i\sqrt{a_i b_i} \le\sqrt{\left(\sum_i\theta_i a_i\right) \left(\sum_i\theta_i b_i\right)} \] by Cauchy--Schwarz, establishing the quadratic inequality and the required nonnegative sides for the combination. Thus it is valid on the whole hull. At the proposed point, $u^2=1/4$ but $x(t-u)=0$, so the inequality fails. This is the corresponding simple specialization of the later paper's nonlinear cut, rederived here with a full direct proof.

The mathematical error isolated by the example is $\operatorname{conv}(G\cap L)=\operatorname{conv}(G)\cap L$ for an arbitrary linear side-constraint set $L$: the four-corner representation may use components that violate $L$, even when their average meets $L$. Exact convexification of each product separately therefore does not prove a full pooling hull. The one additional cut is shown valid and necessary for this example; its sufficiency for this or every other parameter case is not claimed or proved.
