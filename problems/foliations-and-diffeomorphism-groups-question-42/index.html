Foliations and Diffeomorphism Groups — Question 42

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a group and let $\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}({\mathbb{R}}^{2})$ denote the group of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of the plane. Consider a homomorphism \[ \Phi\colon G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Homeo}^{+}({\mathbb{R}}^{2}) \] preserving two foliations $\mathcal F^{+}$ and $\mathcal F^{-}$. The source allows possible prong singularities and describes the foliations as transverse; their precise admissible singularities and the meaning of transversality at singular points are not specified.

The source states that this action extends to an associated circle at infinity $S^{1}_{\infty}(\mathcal F^{+},\mathcal F^{-})$, producing an induced action \[ \varphi\colon G\longrightarrow \operatorname{Homeo}^{+}\!\left(S^{1}_{\infty}(\mathcal F^{+},\mathcal F^{-})\right). \] This extension assertion is supplied background attributed to the reference key bonatti2024action; its precise hypotheses and the circle construction are not included in the record.

Question. Can smoothability of the plane action $\Phi$ be detected from its induced circle action $\varphi$? In particular, what condition on the induced action characterizes smoothability of the original plane action?

For a specified differentiability class $C^{r}$, a possible precise interpretation of smoothability is the existence of a homeomorphism $h\colon{\mathbb{R}}^{2}\to{\mathbb{R}}^{2}$ such that \[ h\circ\Phi(g)\circ h^{-1}\in\operatorname{Diff}^{r}_{+}({\mathbb{R}}^{2}) \qquad\text{for every }g\in G, \] where $\operatorname{Diff}^{r}_{+}({\mathbb{R}}^{2})$ denotes the orientation-preserving $C^{r}$ diffeomorphisms of the plane. Here the same $h$ must work for every group element. This interpretation is conditional: the source does not specify the differentiability class, the intended notion of smoothability, whether the foliations must also become smooth, or what equivalence and additional data are allowed when inspecting $\varphi$. These choices must be resolved before the question has a unique fully precise formulation.

Short English statement

An orientation-preserving action on the plane preserves two transverse foliations, possibly with prong singularities, and the source assigns it an induced action on a circle at infinity. The question asks whether that circle action reveals whether the original plane action can be made smooth. The meaning of smoothability and the exact hypotheses for the boundary construction remain unspecified.
