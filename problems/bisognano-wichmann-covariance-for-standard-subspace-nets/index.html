Bisognano–Wichmann Covariance for Standard-Subspace Nets

Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.
Definitions and conventions.
For a standard real subspace $H$, the closed antilinear operator $S(h+ik)=h-ik$ has polar decomposition $S=J\Delta^{1/2}$; $\Delta^{it}$ is its modular group. Covariance of a net means $U(g)H(O)=H(gO)$. Positive energy means the translation spectrum lies in the closed forward light cone. The Bisognano--Wichmann property identifies the modular group of a wedge subspace with the corresponding Lorentz boosts, with the standard $2\pi$ normalization and a convention-dependent sign.
Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.
Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
Given a local net of standard subspaces $H(O)$ on double cones $O\subset\mathbb R^4$, covariant under a positive-energy unitary Poincaré representation $U$, does another positive-energy representation $U'$ on the same Hilbert space make $H$ covariant and satisfy the Bisognano–Wichmann property?
Source scope and qualification.
The new representation must act on the same Hilbert space and preserve covariance of the same net. Wedge subspaces are obtained from the double-cone net by the source's closure convention; replacing the net is not an answer.

Short English statement

Can a local standard-subspace net be given another positive-energy Poincaré action whose boosts agree with its wedge modular groups?

Modular data transform covariantly, but are not fixed by covariance.
Let $H$ be standard and $V$ unitary. On $VH+iVH$, the Tomita operator satisfies $S_{VH}=VS_HV^*$, directly from $S_H(h+ik)=h-ik$. Therefore $\Delta_{VH}=V\Delta_HV^*$. In particular, any unitary preserving $H$ commutes with its modular group, but need not equal that group at a rescaled parameter.
An explicit one-subspace test.
Take $H=\mathbb R^2\subset\mathbb C^2$. Its Tomita operator is complex conjugation, so $\Delta_H=I$. The nontrivial real rotations $V_t=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\cos t&-\sin t\\\sin t&\cos t\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ preserve $H$ and are unitary on $\mathbb C^2$, yet cannot coincide with the trivial modular group. Thus covariance of a subspace under a one-parameter action gives no modular identification by itself.
The net-level obstruction.
A prospective $U'$ is constrained on every wedge boost by $U'(\Lambda_W(-2\pi t))=\Delta_{H(W)}^{it}$ in one sign convention. These separately defined groups must satisfy all joint Lorentz and translation relations, preserve the same double-cone net and have positive translation energy. The toy example is not a four-dimensional local net and does not disprove existence of such a replacement representation. The first gap is proving those compatibility relations for the actual family of wedge modular operators. Replacing the net or Hilbert space would evade the source target and is not done.
