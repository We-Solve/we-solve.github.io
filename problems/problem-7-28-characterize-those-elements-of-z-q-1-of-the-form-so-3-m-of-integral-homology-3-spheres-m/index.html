Problem 7.28 — Characterize those elements of Z[[q−1]] of the form τ SO(3)(M ) of integral homology 3-spheres M.

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An integral homology 3-sphere is, in the usual closed connected oriented convention, a 3-manifold $M$ with $H_i(M;{\mathbb{Z}})\cong H_i(S^3;{\mathbb{Z}})$ for every $i$. Set $t=q-1$, and let ${\mathbb{Z}}[[t]]$ be the ring of formal power series with integer coefficients.

The source uses the formal series invariant \[ \tau^{\operatorname{SO}(3)}(M)=\sum_{k\geq0}\lambda_k(M)(q-1)^k\in{\mathbb{Z}}[[q-1]]. \] Problem 7.28 asks to characterize its image: give necessary and sufficient conditions on an integer sequence $(a_k)_{k\geq0}$ for there to exist a single integral homology 3-sphere $M$ with \[ \lambda_k(M)=a_k\qquad\text{for every }k\geq0. \] Equivalently, determine exactly the subset $\{\tau^{\operatorname{SO}(3)}(M):M\text{ an integral homology 3-sphere}\}$ of the formal series ring.

This asks for full-series realizability, not only necessary congruences, a finite list of coefficients, or separate manifolds realizing each truncation. No analytic convergence of the series is required. The excerpt does not define the series normalization, orientation conventions, or its construction; it must not be replaced by a finite-level quantum invariant evaluated at a root of unity. No particular constant coefficient or coefficient congruence is asserted without those definitions.

Short English statement

Which integer formal series occur as the source's $\operatorname{SO}(3)$ series invariant of an integral homology 3-sphere? The desired characterization must realize all coefficients simultaneously on one manifold.
