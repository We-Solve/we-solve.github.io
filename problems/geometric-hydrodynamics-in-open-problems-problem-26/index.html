Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied problem concerns the three-dimensional Euler evolution in spaces denoted by $C^k$ and restrictions on $k$ arising from applications of Kolmogorov--Arnold--Moser (KAM) theory. The spatial domain, boundary conditions, phase space, topology, admissible indices $k$, and existing restrictions on $k$ are not specified in the record.

To explain a possible topological interpretation, let $X^k$ denote an intended phase space of $C^k$ Euler velocity fields, equipped with a specified topology. Suppose that the Euler evolution defines a continuous global semiflow \[ \Phi\colon [0,\infty)\times X^k\longrightarrow X^k, \qquad (t,u_0)\longmapsto\Phi_t(u_0). \] Here a global semiflow means that $\Phi_0$ is the identity and $\Phi_{t+s}=\Phi_t\circ\Phi_s$ for every $s,t\geq0$. This global evolution is a conditional framework for interpreting the question, not a hypothesis established or explicitly supplied by the source record.

Under this interpretation, topological non-transitivity means that there exist nonempty open sets $U,V\subseteq X^k$ such that \[ \Phi_t(U)\cap V=\varnothing \qquad\text{for every }t\geq0. \] Failure of topological mixing means that there exist nonempty open sets $U,V\subseteq X^k$ such that \[ \text{for every }T\geq0\text{ there exists }t\geq T \text{ with }\Phi_t(U)\cap V=\varnothing. \] These definitions do not settle the source's intended meaning: measure-theoretic mixing would instead require a specified invariant measure and an appropriate measurable evolution.

Requested statement, with unresolved scope. Relax the restrictions on the smoothness index $k$ in the $C^k$ spaces that arise from the application of KAM theory, and establish non-transitivity and non-mixing of the three-dimensional Euler evolution in the source's intended full generality. The supplied record does not identify the current restrictions, the desired replacement range, or the meaning of full generality. Consequently, it does not determine a fully quantified conjecture. In particular, the conditional topological formulation above must not be treated as a resolution of the missing hypotheses or as an assertion of global well-posedness.

Short English statement

Problem 26 asks for weaker regularity requirements in KAM-based arguments and for non-transitivity and non-mixing results for three-dimensional Euler evolution in full generality. The supplied record leaves the regularity range, spatial setting, phase space, evolution time domain, and meanings of these dynamical properties unresolved.
