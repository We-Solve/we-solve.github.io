Math-3676

Uniform Cover Decomposition Bounds for Convex Polygons

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a precise provisional formulation, regard a convex $n$-gon $Q\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2$ as a compact convex polygon with nonempty interior and exactly $n$ vertices, including its boundary. These conventions are not explicitly specified in the supplied record and require confirmation.

An indexed family of translates of $Q$ is a family $\mathcal T=(Q+t_i)_{i\in I}$, where $t_i\in{\mathbb{R}}^2$. Distinct indices may represent the same translate. For a positive integer $m$, call $\mathcal T$ an $m$-fold covering of the plane if \[ \#\{i\in I:x\in Q+t_i\}\ge m \qquad\text{for every }x\in{\mathbb{R}}^2. \] A decomposition into two coverings is a partition $I=I_0\mathbin{\dot\cup}I_1$ such that \[ \bigcup_{i\in I_0}(Q+t_i)={\mathbb{R}}^2 \qquad\text{and}\qquad \bigcup_{i\in I_1}(Q+t_i)={\mathbb{R}}^2. \] Define \[ m(Q)=\min\left\{m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}:\text{every }m\text{-fold covering by translates of }Q \text{ decomposes into two coverings}\right\}, \] with $m(Q)=+\infty$ if this set is empty. The value $+\infty$ is an explicit bookkeeping convention for possible nonexistence of the least multiplicity.

Question. Is it true that \[ \forall n\in{\mathbb{Z}},\ n>3,\quad \exists M\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge1}\quad \forall\text{ convex }n\text{-gons }Q\subset{\mathbb{R}}^2, \qquad m(Q)\le M? \] Thus the bound $M=m(n)$ may depend on the number of vertices but must be independent of the shape and size of $Q$.

Unresolved scope qualification. The displayed definition provisionally quantifies over arbitrary indexed families, allows repeated translates, and imposes no local finiteness restriction. The supplied record does not specify these choices, whether polygon boundaries are included, or whether degenerate polygons or redundant collinear vertices are allowed. Consequently this explicit interpretation requires source confirmation before being treated as the definitive intended formulation.

Short English statement

For each integer $n>3$, is there a finite multiplicity depending only on $n$ such that every covering of the plane at that multiplicity by translates of any convex $n$-gon can be partitioned into two coverings of the whole plane? The admissible covering families and polygon conventions require confirmation. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
