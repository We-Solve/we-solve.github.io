Math-222

Exact line-arrangement operation formulas and a rich-line construction barrier

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The original asks whether $h(C)$ is uniformly bounded below in characteristic zero, but its extracted item omits the preceding definition of $h$. Under the proper-singularity interpretation highlighted by the report, for a reduced plane curve $C$ of degree $d$ with nonempty finite proper singular set of size $s$, the conventional quantity is \[ N(C)=d^2-\sum_{P\in\operatorname{Sing}C}m_P(C)^2, \qquad h(C)=N(C)/s. \] This convention excludes infinitely near points and differs from evaluating at a prescribed finite point set. The original curve class, field scope, and zero-singularity case require confirmation. In this interpretation the question is whether one constant $B\in{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfies $h(C)\geq B$ for every admissible characteristic-zero curve.

The source reports that an affirmative answer would imply Bounded Negativity for rational surfaces in characteristic zero, conventionally a lower self-intersection bound depending on the fixed surface for all reduced irreducible curves. It says $h$ is unbounded below in positive characteristic, asks what a useful replacement would be there, and reports $h(C)>-4$ for line unions with a source-era best example approximately $-3.36$. These are supplied assessments, not a current audit.

For an arrangement of $d$ distinct projective lines evaluated on its changing full proper singular locus, the research report gives an addition rule. If a new line passes through $q$ old singular points of multiplicities $m_j$ and produces $u=d-\sum_jm_j$ distinct new nodes, it claims \[ h'=\frac{s h+1-q-2u}{s+u}. \] Its arbitrary deletion formula is reported as \[ h'=\frac{s h+R+v-q}{s-\delta}, \] with corrections for destroyed singularities; $R,v,q,\delta$ for that operation are not defined in the supplied summary, and no meanings are guessed.

The candidate invariant is $\Phi_t=N_t-h_0s_t$. The report claims that from any seed $h_0\leq-2$, iterative additions each meeting at most one current singular point never yield $h<h_0$. If current $h\leq-3$, any improving addition must satisfy $q\geq u+2$; for the Wiman seed it reports the exact improvement test $67q-91u>67$. All formulas require their singular-count denominators to be nonzero. This operation calculus is claimed characteristic-independent, while the main uniform-bound problem is reported open in characteristic zero.

Short English statement

Is the proper-singularity negativity ratio uniformly bounded below in characteristic zero? The report supplies exact line-addition/deletion rules and restrictions on certain improvement strategies, while retaining the main problem as open. The omitted definition of $h$, its singular-locus convention, and deletion corrections need source review.
