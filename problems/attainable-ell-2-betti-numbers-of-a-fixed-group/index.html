Attainable $\ell^2$-Betti Numbers of a Fixed Group

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be an arbitrary countable discrete group. No residual finiteness assumption is imposed. Write ${\mathbb{Q}} G$ for the rational group algebra, whose elements are finite sums $\sum_{g\in G}a_g g$ with $a_g\in{\mathbb{Q}}$. Let $\ell^2(G)$ be the Hilbert space of square-summable complex-valued functions on $G$, and let $\lambda(g)$ act by left translation: \[ (\lambda(g)\xi)(h)=\xi(g^{-1}h). \] Let $\mathcal N(G)$ be the group von Neumann algebra generated by these operators, with canonical trace \[ \tau_G(T)=\langle T\delta_e,\delta_e\rangle, \] where $e$ is the identity of $G$.

For positive integers $m,n$ and $A=(a_{ij})\in M_{m,n}({\mathbb{Q}} G)$, let \[ T_A:\ell^2(G)^n\longrightarrow\ell^2(G)^m, \qquad (T_A\xi)_i=\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda(a_{ij})\xi_j. \] Its kernel is closed. If $P_A\in M_n(\mathcal N(G))$ is the orthogonal projection onto this kernel, define its von Neumann dimension using the unnormalized matrix trace: \[ \dim_{\mathcal N(G)}\ker T_A :=\sum_{j=1}^n\tau_G\left((P_A)_{jj}\right). \] Thus the ambient space $\ell^2(G)^n$ has dimension $n$. Define \[ C(G):=\left\{ \dim_{\mathcal N(G)}\ker T_A: m,n\geq 1,\ A\in M_{m,n}({\mathbb{Q}} G) \right\}\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq0}. \] Here the source's phrase "$\ell^2$-Betti numbers" refers to these matrix-kernel dimensions. Using square matrices gives the same set, since $A^*A\in M_n({\mathbb{Q}} G)$ and $\ker T_A=\ker T_{A^*A}$.

Problem. For each fixed countable discrete group $G$, determine the set $C(G)$.

The supplied record labels this problem open and describes it as an Atiyah problem. Its literature assessment is dated curation, not an independently verified assertion of current status. The bibliographic year qualifications described in the Sources remain unresolved.

Short English statement

For a fixed countable discrete group $G$, characterize all real numbers obtained as von Neumann dimensions of kernels of finite matrices with entries in ${\mathbb{Q}} G$, acting on finite direct sums of $\ell^2(G)$. The group is not assumed to be residually finite. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
