Answered Survey of Zero-Entropy Disk Dynamics

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let \[ D=\{(x,y)\in{\mathbb{R}}^2:x^2+y^2\leq 1\},\qquad \mu(A)=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_A{\,\mathrm{d}} x\,{\,\mathrm{d}} y \] for Lebesgue-measurable subsets $A\subseteq D$. Consider the class \[ \mathcal F_0=\left\{f:D\longrightarrow D:\ f\text{ is a }C^\infty\text{ diffeomorphism},\ f_*\mu=\mu,\ h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)=0\right\}. \] Here smoothness is understood up to the boundary, with a smooth inverse; $h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)$ denotes topological entropy. Such a diffeomorphism maps $\partial D$ onto itself. The supplied statement imposes no pointwise boundary-fixing condition or specified boundary dynamics. Area preservation is expressed above as preservation of standard area measure; the source record does not explicitly distinguish this convention from preservation of the oriented area form.

To spell out some possible notions of complexity, $f$ has a dense forward orbit if \[ \exists x\in D\quad \overline{\{f^n(x):n\geq 0\}}=D. \] It is ergodic with respect to $\mu$ if every measurable set $A$ satisfying $\mu(f^{-1}(A)\mathbin{\triangle}A)=0$ has $\mu(A)\in\{0,1\}$. One standard measure-theoretic definition of weak mixing is \[ \forall A,B\subseteq D\text{ measurable},\qquad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} \left|\mu(f^{-n}(A)\cap B)-\mu(A)\mu(B)\right|=0. \] Strong mixing with respect to $\mu$ means \[ \forall A,B\subseteq D\text{ measurable},\qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}\mu(f^{-n}(A)\cap B)=\mu(A)\mu(B). \] These definitions clarify possible readings of mixing; the supplied prompt does not select a particular definition of mixing or a complete list of complexity properties.

Retained survey question. How mixing, or otherwise dynamically complex, can a map $f\in\mathcal F_0$ be?

Short English statement

This record asks broadly what dynamical complexity is possible for smooth area-preserving diffeomorphisms of the closed disk with zero topological entropy. The supplied verification treats it as a survey with reported positive constructions, rather than a separate precise open conjecture. Its partially_solved label and literature assessment are supplied curation, not independently verified mathematical conclusions.
