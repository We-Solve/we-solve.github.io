Research attempt: A positive observed distribution can have only a nonstochastic complex fit

Choose, explicitly as an auxiliary interpretation, a binary hidden root $H$ with three conditionally independent observed spins $Y_i\in\{-1,1\}$. For a stochastic root probability $\pi$, let $m_{ih}=E(Y_i\mid H=h)$ and $d_i=m_{i1}-m_{i0}$. Conditional independence gives \[ \operatorname{Cov}(Y_i,Y_j)=\pi(1-\pi)d_id_j. \] This follows by expanding $E(Y_iY_j)$ and subtracting the product of means. Consequently the product of the three pairwise covariances is nonnegative, including root or edge degeneracies.

Now define an observed distribution by \[ p(y_1,y_2,y_3)=\tfrac18\left(1+\tfrac14y_1y_2+ \tfrac14y_1y_3-\tfrac14y_2y_3\right). \] It is strictly positive: the bracket is either $5/4$ or $1/4$. Summing spin monomials over the cube shows total mass one, means zero, and covariances $1/4,1/4,-1/4$. Their product is negative, so this observed distribution is not in the chosen stochastic tripod image.

It is nevertheless in the complex parameterized image, not just its closure. Take uniform hidden root and conditional spin means $t_i$ in one root state and $-t_i$ in the other, where \[ t_1=i/2,\qquad t_2=t_3=-i/2. \] Use conditional entries $(1\pm t_i)/2$, which sum to one but are complex. Averaging the two product distributions cancels the odd spin monomials and leaves pair coefficients $t_1t_2=1/4$, $t_1t_3=1/4$, $t_2t_3=-1/4$. This is exactly the displayed real positive distribution.

Thus adding positivity of observed cell probabilities to algebraic membership does not force stochastic parameters. The attempted elimination-only route needs parameter-side inequalities or an equivalent semialgebraic criterion. The example is for the declared general binary latent-star model; the source does not specify that model, so it is not a full characterization of its unstated stochastic map.
