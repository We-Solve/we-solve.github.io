Math-4106

Connectivity of Cluster-Tilting Exchange Graphs

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

A Frobenius category is an exact category $\mathcal C$ with enough projective objects and enough injective objects, in which the projective and injective objects coincide. Its stable category $\underline{\mathcal C}$ is the quotient by morphisms factoring through projective-injective objects; it is triangulated, with suspension denoted by $\Sigma$. Under the usual additional convention that this stable category is linear over a field $k$ and has finite-dimensional morphism spaces, the stably $2$-Calabi--Yau condition means that there are bifunctorial isomorphisms \[ \operatorname{Hom}_k\!\left(\operatorname{Hom}_{\underline{\mathcal C}}(X,Y),k\right) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\underline{\mathcal C}}(Y,\Sigma^2X) \qquad (X,Y\in\underline{\mathcal C}). \] The supplied record does not specify the field, finiteness hypotheses, or precise convention for this condition; the displayed interpretation is conditional on that usual setting.

For an object $T$, let $\operatorname{add}(T)$ consist of the objects isomorphic to direct summands of finite direct sums of copies of $T$. A standard cluster-tilting convention requires \[ \operatorname{add}(T) =\{X\in\mathcal C:\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathcal C}(T,X)=0\} =\{X\in\mathcal C:\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathcal C}(X,T)=0\}, \] with $\operatorname{add}(T)$ functorially finite: every object admits both a right and a left $\operatorname{add}(T)$-approximation. A right approximation $A\to X$, with $A\in\operatorname{add}(T)$, is a morphism through which every morphism from an object of $\operatorname{add}(T)$ to $X$ factors; a left approximation is defined dually. The exact cluster-tilting convention used in the report is not included in the supplied excerpt.

In a Krull--Schmidt setting, a basic object is a finite direct sum of pairwise nonisomorphic indecomposable objects. Under the usual basic-object interpretation, the full exchange graph $\mathcal E(\mathcal C)$ has as vertices the isomorphism classes of all basic cluster-tilting objects. An undirected edge represents one admissible cluster-tilting mutation, replacing a single indecomposable summand while retaining the other summands. The supplied record does not give the exchange-sequence or approximation conditions defining admissible mutation, so this description does not assert that every replacement or every summand admits mutation.

Question (under these explicitly stated conventions). For every stably $2$-Calabi--Yau Frobenius category $\mathcal C$ in the intended class, is the full graph $\mathcal E(\mathcal C)$ connected? Equivalently, for every pair of its vertices represented by $T,T'$, do there exist $m\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 0}$ and cluster-tilting objects \[ T=T_0,T_1,\ldots,T_m\cong T' \] such that each consecutive pair is related by one mutation?

Here the full graph includes all cluster-tilting objects under the chosen vertex convention, without restricting to objects already reachable from a fixed object. The intended ambient hypotheses, vertex convention, and mutation convention require confirmation from the report. Existence of a cluster-tilting object is also not explicitly assumed in the supplied statement: the pairwise-path formulation is vacuous for an empty vertex set, whereas a convention requiring a connected graph to be nonempty would require a separate existence hypothesis.

Short English statement

The question asks whether any two cluster-tilting objects in a stably $2$-Calabi--Yau Frobenius category can be joined by finitely many mutations in the full exchange graph. The supplied record labels this problem open. Its precise ambient assumptions, basic-object convention, mutation rules, and treatment of categories without cluster-tilting objects remain to be confirmed.
