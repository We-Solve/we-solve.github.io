Simple Groups Acting on Nonspherical Phan Geometries

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a building or twin building of Coxeter type $(W,S)$, the type is called nonspherical when the Coxeter group $W$ is infinite. A group $H$ is simple if $H\neq\{1\}$ and its only normal subgroups are $\{1\}$ and $H$.

The supplied source asks about groups denoted by $G_\sigma$ acting on Phan geometries associated with good flips $\sigma$. It does not define the ambient group $G$, the admissible buildings, a good flip, the associated Phan geometry, or the construction of $G_\sigma$. The supplied background describes the question as a centralizer question. If $\sigma$ is an involutive automorphism of $G$, its fixed subgroup is \[ G^\sigma=\{g\in G:\sigma(g)=g\}, \] which can also be identified with the centralizer of $\sigma$ in the copy of $G$ inside $G\rtimes\langle\sigma\rangle$. Identifying the source notation $G_\sigma$ with this subgroup requires confirmation from the report; the record does not exclude a quotient, an action image, or a related subgroup.

Problem (source formulation, with unresolved scope). In the nonspherical setting intended by the source, determine the groups $G_\sigma$ acting on the Phan geometries associated with good flips $\sigma$. In particular, determine whether these groups are simple. If the simplicity question is intended universally over the admissible pairs $(G,\sigma)$, its assertion would be \[ \text{for every admissible }(G,\sigma),\qquad G_\sigma\neq\{1\},\qquad \left(N\trianglelefteq G_\sigma\Longrightarrow N=\{1\}\ \text{or}\ N=G_\sigma\right). \] The admissible pairs and the precise meaning of $G_\sigma$ remain unspecified. The record also does not state whether simplicity is abstract or topological, or whether a central quotient is intended. Thus this is a faithful schematic statement requiring source clarification before a fully quantified formulation is possible.

Short English statement

Determine the groups acting on nonspherical Phan geometries arising from good flips, and decide whether they are simple. The ambient groups, good-flip hypotheses, acting-group construction, and intended notion of simplicity need clarification from the source.
