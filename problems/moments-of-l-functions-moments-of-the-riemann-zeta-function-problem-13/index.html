Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\zeta(s)$ denote the meromorphic continuation of the Riemann zeta function, initially defined by $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}$ for $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$. The supplied record does not define $I_k(T)$. Under the conventional unweighted interpretation, put \[ I_k(T):=\int_0^T\left|\zeta\!\left(\tfrac12+it\right)\right|^{2k}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t, \qquad k\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq1},\quad T\geq2. \] This normalization and these integration limits require confirmation against the source.

The problem attributed in the supplied excerpt to Nathan Ng (Lethbridge) asks whether the conjectural method called the "recipe" in reference [CFKRS] can be made rigorous for the fourth moment $I_2(T)$. Under the interpretation above, the requested result is a proof following that recipe of an asymptotic formula \[ I_2(T)=T P_4(\log T)+O\!\left(T^{1-\delta}\right) \qquad (T\to\infty), \] for some constant $\delta>0$, where $P_4\in{\mathbb{R}}[X]$ is the fixed degree-four polynomial giving the full fourth-moment main term predicted by the recipe. The implied constant must be independent of $T$. All coefficients of the main-term polynomial must be accounted for, and the error must save a positive power of $T$.

The record presents this as a request for a new proof of an existing asymptotic formula. It does not supply the coefficients of $P_4$, a definition of the recipe, or the full bibliographic identification of [CFKRS]. Consequently, the displayed formulation is conditional on confirming the normalization of $I_k(T)$, and the precise main term and required methodological steps remain to be checked against the cited source.

Short English statement

Make the [CFKRS] recipe rigorous for the fourth moment of the Riemann zeta function, obtaining its complete polynomial main term with an error saving a positive power of $T$. The supplied excerpt leaves the moment normalization, polynomial coefficients, and precise recipe unspecified.
