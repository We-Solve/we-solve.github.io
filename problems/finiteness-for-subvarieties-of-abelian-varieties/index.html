Finiteness for Subvarieties of Abelian Varieties

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The supplied record describes an extension problem, but does not specify enough hypotheses or conclusions to determine a single conjecture.

For notation, let $A$ denote an abelian variety over a base field that remains unspecified in the source, and put $g=\dim A$. For a closed subvariety $X\subseteq A$, put $d=\dim X$ and $\operatorname{codim}_A X=g-d$. When the closed immersion is smooth, its normal bundle is \[ N_{X/A}=\left(T_A|_X\right)/T_X. \] A vector bundle $E$ is ample if the tautological line bundle $\mathcal O_{\mathbf P(E)}(1)$ is ample, where $\mathbf P(E)$ parametrizes one-dimensional quotients. A morphism $f\colon Y\to A$ is finite if, for every affine open $\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq A$, its inverse image is $\operatorname{Spec}B$ with $B$ a finite $R$-module. Finiteness alone does not require surjectivity onto $A$.

Extension problem as supplied. Extend arithmetic-finiteness and big-monodromy results for high-codimension subvarieties of abelian varieties with ample normal bundle to subvarieties of smaller codimension, and more generally to varieties $Y$ admitting a finite morphism $Y\to A$ to an abelian variety. The record does not state the arithmetic-finiteness or monodromy theorems to be extended. In particular, it specifies neither the arithmetic base and fixed data for a finiteness assertion nor the family, representation, or target group for a monodromy assertion. It also does not specify a smaller-codimension range or which smoothness, irreducibility, and positivity hypotheses should remain in the proposed extension. These are unresolved parts of the formulation; no universal theorem over all base fields or all finite morphisms is asserted here.

Quoted contextual assertion. The original excerpt also contains a statement labelled Theorem 4. In the notation above, it states that if $X\subset A$ is smooth and irreducible, has ample normal bundle, and satisfies $d<g/2$, then the following are equivalent:

  1. (a) $X\subset A$ is nondivisible and its Tannaka group satisfies $G^{*}_{X,\omega}\simeq E_6$.
  2. (b) $X$ is isomorphic to the Fano surface of lines on a smooth cubic threefold, and the canonical morphism $\operatorname{Alb}(X)\to A$ is an isogeny.
Here the Fano surface parametrizes lines contained in the cubic threefold; $\operatorname{Alb}(X)$ denotes the Albanese variety appearing in the excerpt, and an isogeny of abelian varieties is a surjective homomorphism with finite kernel. The source excerpt does not define nondivisibility, the Tannakian construction, $\omega$, the superscript $*$, or the precise form of the group denoted by $E_6$. It also omits the base-field hypotheses and choices underlying the stated canonical Albanese morphism. Consequently, this equivalence is retained as a contextual source assertion with unresolved qualifications, not as a fully specified theorem verified in this draft. The record does not establish whether an extension of this equivalence itself is part of the intended question.

Short English statement

The supplied question asks how arithmetic-finiteness and big-monodromy results extend to smaller-codimension subvarieties of abelian varieties and to varieties admitting finite maps to abelian varieties. The precise existing results, desired conclusions, base field, and retained hypotheses are missing. An adjacent $E_6$ classification theorem is preserved as context, with its undefined notation and missing qualifications explicitly recorded.
