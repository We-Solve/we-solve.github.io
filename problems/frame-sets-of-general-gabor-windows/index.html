Math-3468

Frame Sets of General Gabor Windows

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $L^2({\mathbb{R}})$ denote the Hilbert space of complex-valued measurable functions on ${\mathbb{R}}$, modulo equality almost everywhere, with $\|f\|_2^2=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}}|f(t)|^2\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t<\infty$ and inner product $\langle f,h\rangle=\int_{{\mathbb{R}}}f(t)\overline{h(t)}\,{\,\mathrm{d}} t$. For $g\in L^2({\mathbb{R}})$ and $\alpha,\beta>0$, define the indexed Gabor family \[ \mathcal G(g,\alpha,\beta)=\left(g_{k,l}\right)_{(k,l)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2},\qquad g_{k,l}(t)=e^{2\pi i\beta lt}g(t-\alpha k). \] This family is a frame for $L^2({\mathbb{R}})$ if there exist real constants $0<A\leq B<\infty$ such that, for every $f\in L^2({\mathbb{R}})$, \[ A\|f\|_2^2\leq \sum_{(k,l)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^2}|\langle f,g_{k,l}\rangle|^2 \leq B\|f\|_2^2. \] The constants may depend on $(g,\alpha,\beta)$, but not on $f$. Define the frame set of $g$ by \[ \mathcal F(g)=\{(\alpha,\beta)\in(0,\infty)^2:\mathcal G(g,\alpha,\beta)\text{ is a frame for }L^2({\mathbb{R}})\}. \] Problem. Characterize all triples $(g,\alpha,\beta)\in L^2({\mathbb{R}})\times(0,\infty)^2$ for which the displayed frame inequalities hold; equivalently, determine $\mathcal F(g)$ for every $g\in L^2({\mathbb{R}})$. No additional regularity, support, or nonvanishing hypothesis on $g$ is supplied.

The supplied record labels the general characterization problem open and reports that frame sets are known for several important windows, with unresolved cases remaining among standard windows. These are supplied dated literature assessments, not independently verified conclusions. The bibliographic year discrepancy described in Sources remains unresolved.

Short English statement

Determine exactly which square-integrable windows $g$ and positive translation and modulation spacings $\alpha,\beta$ generate a Gabor frame: every $f\in L^2({\mathbb{R}})$ must satisfy the same positive lower and finite upper frame bounds for that triple. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
