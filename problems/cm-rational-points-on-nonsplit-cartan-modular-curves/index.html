Math-6512

CM Rational Points on Nonsplit Cartan Modular Curves

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a prime $p$, a nonsplit Cartan subgroup $C_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{F}}_p)$ is the image of ${\mathbb{F}}_{p^2}^{\times}$ acting by multiplication on the two-dimensional ${\mathbb{F}}_p$-vector space ${\mathbb{F}}_{p^2}$, after choosing a basis. Different choices of basis give conjugate subgroups. Under the usual convention, $X_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)$ is the smooth projective modular curve over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ associated with this subgroup, obtained by compactifying the moduli curve of elliptic curves with full level-$p$ structure modulo $C_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)$.

A noncuspidal geometric point of this curve is a CM point if its underlying elliptic curve $E/\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ has complex multiplication, meaning that $\operatorname{End}_{\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}}(E)$ strictly contains ${\mathbb{Z}}$, or equivalently is an order in an imaginary quadratic field. Define \[ \operatorname{CM}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(X_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)) =\{x\in X_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)({\mathbb{Q}}):x\text{ is noncuspidal and its underlying elliptic curve has CM}\}. \] The question in the supplied record is whether \[ \exists\,c\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\geq 2}\quad \forall\,p\text{ prime},\qquad p>c\ \Longrightarrow\ X_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)({\mathbb{Q}})=\operatorname{CM}_{{\mathbb{Q}}}(X_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)). \] Taking an integer bound at least $2$ does not change the existence question. The bound is uniform over all primes, and the assertion does not claim that any rational CM points exist.

Unresolved source qualifications. The supplied record does not define its modular-curve notation. The definition above uses the nonsplit Cartan subgroup itself; replacing it by its normalizer gives the curve commonly denoted $X_{\mathrm{ns}}^{+}(p)$, which is a different formulation. The intended convention requires confirmation against the source. Moreover, the statement as supplied says all rational points on $X_{\mathrm{ns}}(p)$, without excluding cusps. On the projective convention above, the assertion therefore also requires that there be no rational cusps for primes exceeding the bound. If the intended target concerns only noncuspidal rational points, that restriction must be confirmed rather than silently inserted.

Short English statement

Is there a single bound beyond which every rational point on the indicated nonsplit Cartan modular curve is a noncuspidal point representing an elliptic curve with complex multiplication? The precise Cartan versus normalizer convention, and whether cusps are included, require source confirmation. This is a statement-only draft; the supplied open status is a dated curation claim.
