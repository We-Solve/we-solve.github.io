A half-measure Fourier frame with no two-Riesz partition

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Interpret the damaged source formula on the unit torus $[0,1)$ as the restricted exponentials $e_n(t)=e^{2\pi int}\chi_E(t)$, equivalently $e^{2\pi int}$ in $L^2(E)$, for $n\in{\mathbb{Z}}$ and measurable $E$ of Lebesgue measure $1/2$. A family $(f_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ is a Riesz sequence if constants $0<A\le B<\infty$ satisfy \[A\sum|c_\lambda|^2\le\left\|\sum c_\lambda f_\lambda\right\|^2\le B\sum|c_\lambda|^2\] for every finitely supported coefficient family. Completeness is not part of this definition.

Original question (Darrin Speegle, supplied attribution). Does there exist such an $E$ for which no partition ${\mathbb{Z}}=A\sqcup B$ makes both $(e_n)_{n\in A}$ and $(e_n)_{n\in B}$ Riesz sequences? The source formula has lost variable and exponential syntax, and does not explicitly state the ambient torus or Hilbert space; those conventions are the aid's natural reconstruction.

Attached unreviewed affirmative claim. The aid asserts existence of a compact set $E\subset[0,1)$, $|E|=1/2$, with the stated no-two-partition property. More strongly, it says this $E$ supports no exponential Riesz sequence with arbitrary real frequency set $\Lambda$ having upper Beurling density $D^+(\Lambda)=1/2$, where \[D^+(\Lambda)=\limsup_{R\to\infty}\sup_{a\in{\mathbb{R}}}\frac{\#(\Lambda\cap[a,a+R])}{R}.\] The frequency sets are understood in the separated/locally finite regime appropriate to the reported density theorem.

Its construction adapts the Kozma--Nitzan--Olevskii no-exponential-Riesz-basis example by moving its recursively refined component from $[2,3]$ to $[3/2,5/2]$. It claims the no-basis property survives this component shift and that the resulting compact set has twice its measure larger than its diameter. An affine normalization then puts it in one torus period with measure $1/2$. The full recursion and proof that moving only one component preserves the obstruction are absent; this is not merely an automatic translation of the whole example.

The aid invokes an Enstad--van Velthoven critical-density theorem, Landau's density inequality, and complement counting to obtain the stronger no-critical-density-sequence property and the desired partition obstruction. In the integer partition setting the intended counting mechanism is that two Riesz subsets each have upper density at most $1/2$, while complementarity forces density $1/2$ for each; the stronger exclusion would then apply. The exact critical-density theorem, all its hypotheses and publication locators are not supplied. The aid's existence and adaptation assertions require independent mathematical and bibliographic review.

Short English statement

Speegle asks for a half-measure torus set whose restricted integer exponentials cannot be split into two Riesz sequences. The aid claims a compact example and a stronger exclusion of every critical-density exponential Riesz sequence, using a component-shifted no-basis construction and density theorems whose full hypotheses are not provided.
