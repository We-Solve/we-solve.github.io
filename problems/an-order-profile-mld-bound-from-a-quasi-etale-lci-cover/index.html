Math-228

An order-profile mld bound from a quasi-etale lci cover

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

The question attributed to Mustaţă recalls Shokurov's prediction $\operatorname{mld}(P;X)\leq\dim X$ for ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Gorenstein varieties, and asks the weaker dimension-only boundedness question: for each fixed $n$, does there exist a finite constant $B(n)$ such that \[ \operatorname{mld}_x(X)\leq B(n) \] for every intended $n$-dimensional $X$ and point $x$? In the standard characteristic-zero normal setting, for a divisor $E$ on a birational model $h:Z\to X$, its log discrepancy is $a(E;X)=1+\operatorname{ord}_E(K_Z-h^*K_X)$, and the minimal log discrepancy at a closed point is the infimum over divisors centered there, with the usual $-\infty$ convention in the non-log-canonical case. The original states "all $P\in X$" but does not specify field or closed-point conventions; these must be fixed, rather than silently interpreting every scheme point identically.

The attached report says the general dimension-only problem remains open already in dimension four. This is its literature-status claim. Its candidate partial theorem concerns a finite crepant quasi-étale map of pointed varieties \[ q:(Y,y)\to(X,x), \] where $Y$ is normal and locally a complete intersection of dimension $n$. Quasi-étale means étale in codimension one, and crepant means $K_Y=q^*K_X$. Suppose a minimal smooth local presentation cuts out $Y$ by a regular sequence $f_1,\ldots,f_c$ in a smooth ambient space of dimension $n+c$, and put $s_i=\operatorname{ord}_y(f_i)$. The report asserts \[ \operatorname{mld}_x(X)\leq\operatorname{mld}_y(Y) \leq n-\sum_{i=1}^c(s_i-1). \] If the right-hand side is negative, it asserts both minimal log discrepancies are $-\infty$, not finite negative values. The stated bound is independent of the degree of $q$. It is a bound for this crepant quasi-étale lci-cover class, not for arbitrary ${\mathbb{Q}}$-Gorenstein varieties.

The report calls the order-profile bound sharp on homogeneous normal complete-intersection cones whose projective defining hypersurfaces form a simple-normal-crossings divisor and whose ordinary blowup is log smooth. In that stated setting its asserted finite value is $n-\sum_i(s_i-1)$, with the preceding $-\infty$ rule in the negative case. The precise field, local presentation, and log-smooth cone hypotheses and the full supporting discrepancy theorems are not included in the summary.

Short English statement

Ask for any universal upper bound on minimal log discrepancies in fixed dimension. The report proposes an explicit bound for varieties with a crepant quasi-étale local-complete-intersection cover, including a sharp cone family, while leaving the general problem separate.
