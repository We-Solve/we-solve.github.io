Forms and Multiloop Algebras in Nullity Two

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For each positive integer $n$, put \[ R_n={\mathbb{C}}[t_1^{\pm1},\ldots,t_n^{\pm1}], \] and let $\mathfrak g$ be a finite-dimensional semisimple complex Lie algebra. Here the nullity parameter is the number $n$ of Laurent variables.

An $R_n$-form of $\mathfrak g\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}}R_n$ means an $R_n$-Lie algebra $\mathcal L$ for which there exists a faithfully flat commutative $R_n$-algebra $S$ and an isomorphism of $S$-Lie algebras \[ \mathcal L\otimes_{R_n}S\simeq\mathfrak g\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}}S. \] The classification below is interpreted as classification up to $R_n$-linear Lie algebra isomorphism. The supplied record does not explicitly specify the descent topology or equivalence relation; this is a conventional interpretation requiring review against the source.

To define a multiloop algebra, choose positive integers $m_1,\ldots,m_n$, primitive $m_i$-th roots of unity $\zeta_i\in{\mathbb{C}}$, and pairwise commuting automorphisms $\sigma_i\in\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathbb{C}}}(\mathfrak g)$ satisfying $\sigma_i^{m_i}=1$. For $\mathbf k=(k_1,\ldots,k_n)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n$, set \[ \mathfrak g_{\overline{\mathbf k}} =\{x\in\mathfrak g: \sigma_i(x)=\zeta_i^{k_i}x\text{ for every }i\}. \] The corresponding multiloop algebra is \[ M(\mathfrak g;\boldsymbol\sigma,\mathbf m) =\bigoplus_{\mathbf k\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n} \mathfrak g_{\overline{\mathbf k}}\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}} {\mathbb{C}} z_1^{k_1}\cdots z_n^{k_n} \subseteq\mathfrak g\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}}{\mathbb{C}}[z_1^{\pm1},\ldots,z_n^{\pm1}], \] with bracket inherited from the ambient Lie algebra and $R_n$-action specified by $t_i=z_i^{m_i}$. In this draft, a form is called multiloop if it is $R_n$-isomorphic to such an algebra.

Classification problem. For every positive integer $n$ and every finite-dimensional complex semisimple $\mathfrak g$, classify all $R_n$-forms of $\mathfrak g\otimes_{{\mathbb{C}}}R_n$, and characterize precisely those forms that are multiloop algebras.

Nullity-two question. For $n=2$, the supplied record additionally asks whether every form that is not multiloop is of "Margaux type." No definition of "Margaux type" or "Margaux-like" is supplied, so this additional question cannot yet be expressed as a precise quantified mathematical assertion. Moreover, the original wording refers to counterexamples to being "loop algebras," whereas the cleaned wording refers to non-multiloop forms. Their intended agreement requires source review; no equivalence of those terms is asserted here.

Short English statement

Classify forms of a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra over a Laurent polynomial ring, and determine which arise from commuting finite-order automorphisms by the multiloop construction. In two variables, the source also asks whether all exceptions are of Margaux type, but that term and the original use of "loop" need clarification. This is a statement-only draft requiring human mathematical review.
