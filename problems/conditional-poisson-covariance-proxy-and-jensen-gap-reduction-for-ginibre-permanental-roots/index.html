Math-1307

Conditional-Poisson covariance proxy and Jensen-gap reduction for Ginibre permanental roots

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For an $N\times N$ matrix $A$, its permanent is $\operatorname{Per}(A)=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathfrak S_N}\prod_{i=1}^NA_{i,\sigma(i)}$. A permanental polynomial is $p_N(z)=\operatorname{Per}(zI_N-Z)$. Its normalized mean zero measure, counting multiplicities, is \[ \overline\nu_N=\frac1N{\mathbb{E}}\sum_{p_N(\zeta)=0}\delta_\zeta. \] A mean planar density, when it exists, is the density of this measure with respect to planar area.

Question 2 (Yan Fyodorov). What is the mean density of permanental polynomials? The 2006 workshop text says it is unknown for random matrices larger than $5\times5$. It does not specify the matrix ensemble, entry normalization, finite-size versus limiting target, or whether the density is normalized by degree; that historical sentence is not a verified current status claim.

Reported complex-Ginibre reduction. The attached aid specializes to independent centered circular complex Gaussian entries with ${\mathbb{E}}|Z_{ij}|^2=1/N$. Let $S_N(x)=\sum_{k=0}^Nx^k/k!$. It reports the covariance kernel \[ K_N(z,w)={\mathbb{E}}[p_N(z)\overline{p_N(w)}]=\frac{N!}{N^N}S_N(Nz\overline w). \] Define a covariance proxy, rather than the actual zero measure, by \[ \nu_N^{\mathrm{cov}}=\frac1{4\pi N}\Delta_z\log K_N(z,z)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} A(z), \] where $\Delta_z$ is the ordinary two-dimensional Laplacian. For $x=Nr^2$ let $Y_x$ have the Poisson$(x)$ distribution conditioned on $Y_x\leq N$: \[ \Pr(Y_x=k)=\frac{x^k}{k!S_N(x)}\quad(0\leq k\leq N). \] The aid gives the exact radial formulas \[ \nu_N^{\mathrm{cov}}(\{|z|\leq r\})=\frac{{\mathbb{E}} Y_x}{N},\qquad \frac{{\,\mathrm{d}}\nu_N^{\mathrm{cov}}}{{\,\mathrm{d}} A}(z)=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(Y_x)}{\pi x},\quad x=N|z|^2>0, \] with the density at zero interpreted by its limit. This proxy converges weakly to $\pi^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{|z|<1\}}{\,\mathrm{d}} A$; the reported pointwise density convergence excludes the edge $|z|=1$.

To specify the reported Jensen-gap normalization, put \[ J_N(r)=\log{\mathbb{E}}|p_N(r)|^2-{\mathbb{E}}\log|p_N(r)|^2. \] Rotational invariance makes this radial, and Jensen's inequality gives $J_N\geq0$ wherever the expectations are defined. The aid reports the exact disk-count identity \[ \overline\nu_N(\{|z|\leq r\})- \nu_N^{\mathrm{cov}}(\{|z|\leq r\}) =-\frac{rJ_N'(r)}{2N} \] for almost every $r$, together with its radial distributional formulation. Equivalently, in potential form the difference is $-(4\pi N)^{-1}\Delta J_N$. Thus appropriate control $J_N'(r)=o(N/r)$, at the radii needed for passage to a limiting measure, would transfer the proxy's disk-law limit to the actual mean zero measure. Such a derivative bound is a remaining target, not a proved consequence of the covariance calculation.

The aid explicitly checks that the proxy is not the actual finite-$N$ density. At $N=1$, the sole root is a standard circular complex Gaussian, of actual density $\pi^{-1}e^{-|z|^2}$, whereas the covariance proxy has density $\pi^{-1}(1+|z|^2)^{-2}$. The reported result is therefore a reduction for the specified ensemble, not a computation of the original general mean density.

Short English statement

Compute the expected zero distribution of a random matrix's permanental polynomial. For complex Ginibre matrices, the aid gives exact conditional-Poisson formulas for a covariance-based proxy and an exact Jensen-gap correction to the true mean disk count. Controlling that correction remains necessary; covariance alone gives the wrong finite-size density even at $N=1$.
