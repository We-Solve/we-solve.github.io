Hochschild-Cohomology Lie Structure of a Stratified Algebra

Definitions and mathematical statement

Write $C_2={\mathbb{Z}}/2{\mathbb{Z}}$. For a commutative unital base ring $K$ of characteristic $2$, put $A=K[C_2\times C_2]$ and let $M$ have underlying $K$-module $K[C_2]$. To define the displayed triangular algebra, one must specify an $(A,K)$-bimodule structure on $M$, compatible with its $K$-module structure. With such a choice, define \[ B=\begin{pmatrix}A&M\\0&K\end{pmatrix},\qquad \begin{pmatrix}a&m\\0&\lambda\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}a'&m'\\0&\lambda'\end{pmatrix} =\begin{pmatrix}aa'&am'+m\lambda'\\0&\lambda\lambda'\end{pmatrix}, \qquad e=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}. \] The source specifies only $\operatorname{char}K=2$; it does not explicitly state that $K$ is a field or give the left $A$-action on $M$. The commutative-base convention above explains the notation and requires confirmation for the intended problem. In particular, no action induced by a particular homomorphism $C_2\times C_2\to C_2$ is selected here.

The source assumes that $e$ is stratifying: multiplication induces an isomorphism \[ Be\otimes_{eBe}eB\longrightarrow BeB, \] and $\operatorname{Tor}^{eBe}_i(Be,eB)=0$ for every $i>0$. Here $BeB$ is the two-sided ideal generated by $e$.

Let $\operatorname{HH}^n(B)=H^n(C^\bullet(B,B))$, where $C^n(B,B)=\operatorname{Hom}_K(B^{\otimes_K n},B)$ carries the Hochschild differential. The standard Gerstenhaber bracket has degree $-1$: \[ [-,-]\colon\operatorname{HH}^p(B)\times\operatorname{HH}^q(B)\longrightarrow\operatorname{HH}^{p+q-1}(B). \] Thus its graded Lie interpretation uses shifted degree $p-1$ on $\operatorname{HH}^p(B)$.

Requested statement, subject to the unresolved specifications. For the intended base $K$ and intended bimodule $M$, determine the graded Lie structure of $\operatorname{HH}^*(B)$ with its Gerstenhaber bracket, for example by a complete description of its graded components and brackets. Decide whether the intended long exact sequence associated with the short exact sequence of $B$-bimodules \[ 0\longrightarrow BeB\longrightarrow B\longrightarrow B/BeB\longrightarrow0 \] determines that Lie structure. The source does not display the long exact sequence or specify its construction, terms, maps, or bracket compatibility; these must be identified before the second request is precise. The Gerstenhaber interpretation is the standard interpretation of the requested Lie structure, but the source does not explicitly specify a grading convention or request an additional restricted Lie operation. This draft records the problem and its unresolved qualifications without asserting a computation.

Short English statement

The problem asks for the Hochschild-cohomology Lie bracket of a triangular algebra in characteristic $2$, and whether a long exact sequence determines that bracket. The base assumptions, the action defining the off-diagonal bimodule, and the intended long exact sequence need clarification.
