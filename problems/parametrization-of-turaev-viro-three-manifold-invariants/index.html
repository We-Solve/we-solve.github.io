Parametrization of Turaev–Viro Three-Manifold Invariants

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let ${\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. For each $N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}$, write $\mathcal C_{N,1,0}$ for the fusion category designated by this notation in the supplied source record. Write $\operatorname{TV}_N$ for the three-manifold invariant associated with its Turaev--Viro topological quantum field theory, subject to the construction and conventions intended by that source.

Source conjecture and requested task. The original formulation conjectures that the family \[ \left(\operatorname{TV}_N\right)_{N\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{>0}} \] admits a parametrization as $N$ varies. The supplied edited formulation asks for a uniform parametrization of this family.

Unresolved qualifications. The record does not define $\mathcal C_{N,1,0}$, specify its base field or the categorical structure and normalization used for its Turaev--Viro construction, or state the admissible class of three-manifolds, including orientation and boundary conventions. It also supplies neither the proposed parameters nor a definition of what constitutes a uniform parametrization. Thus the displayed family names the intended objects but does not specify a parametrization theorem with a definite mathematical conclusion. These omissions prevent a fully precise formulation from the supplied evidence alone.

Short English statement

For every positive integer $N$, consider the Turaev--Viro three-manifold invariant associated with the source-designated fusion category $\mathcal C_{N,1,0}$. The task is to describe these invariants uniformly as $N$ varies. The categories, manifold conventions, and intended meaning of parametrization require clarification before this becomes a precise conjecture.
