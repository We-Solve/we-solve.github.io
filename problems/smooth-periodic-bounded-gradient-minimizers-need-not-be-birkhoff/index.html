Math-1637

Smooth periodic bounded-gradient minimizers need not be Birkhoff

Status Solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Solved from source status

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $\mathbb T={\mathbb{R}}/{\mathbb{Z}}$ and let $L:\mathbb T^n\times\mathbb T\times{\mathbb{R}}^n\to{\mathbb{R}}$ be smooth and convex in its gradient variable, with the further usual coercivity assumptions required by the chosen variational theory. A typical source example is $L(x,s,p)=\tfrac12|p|^2+F(x,s)$ with $F$ smooth and periodic. A function $u:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to{\mathbb{R}}$ is a class-A minimizer if, for every bounded variation domain $\Omega$ and every admissible compactly supported perturbation $\varphi$, \[ \int_\Omega L(x,u,Du)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x \le\int_\Omega L(x,u+\varphi,Du+D\varphi)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x. \] For the quadratic example one may take $\varphi\in W^{1,2}_0(\Omega)$ whenever the integrals are defined.

The source's "without self-intersections" property of the projected graph in $\mathbb T^{n+1}$ is the Birkhoff ordering condition: every integer translate \[ (\tau_{k,j}u)(x)=u(x-k)+j,\qquad(k,j)\in{\mathbb{Z}}^n\times{\mathbb{Z}}, \] is everywhere above, everywhere below, or identical to $u$. It is not injectivity of the map from the noncompact graph to its torus image. The source recalls that such minimizers have a rotation covector $c\in({\mathbb{R}}^n)^*$ with $|u(x)-u(0)-c\cdot x|$ bounded by a constant depending only on $L$, and that each rotation covector has a corresponding minimizer with $|u(x)-c\cdot x|$ bounded. Its broad question seeks analytic conditions ensuring this ordering. Its concrete question asks whether class-A minimality together with $\|Du\|_\infty<\infty$ suffices.

The supplied assessment reports that the concrete implication is false in every dimension $n\ge8$. It describes a smooth nonnegative one-periodic potential $P$ and a smooth class-A minimizer $v$ for \[ \int\left(\tfrac12|Dv|^2+P(v)\right)\,{\,\mathrm{d}} x \] with $1/4<v<3/4$ and bounded gradient, whose graph fails the Birkhoff ordering. The input is a nonflat Liu--Wang--Wei global Allen--Cahn minimizer with Lawson-cone asymptotics, rescaled to the Junginger--Gestrich--Valdinoci normalization. Birkhoff ordering of that bounded minimizer would force planar level sets, contradicting the nonflat asymptotics. This is a supplied literature-based counterexample account, not an independently checked construction.

The aid isolates the following transfer. Let $h:{\mathbb{R}}^n\to(0,1)$ be a class-A minimizer of $\int(|Dh|^2+W(h))$, for a normalized double-well potential $W$ with $W(0)=W(1)=0$. Set \[ v=\tfrac14+\tfrac12h, \qquad P(r)=\tfrac18W(2r-\tfrac12) \quad(\tfrac14\le r\le\tfrac34), \] and choose a nonnegative smooth one-periodic extension $P$ agreeing on that interval, as available for the double-well input used by the aid. The energies on the compressed interval differ by the factor $1/8$. For arbitrary compact competitors, clipping to $[1/4,3/4]$ decreases the gradient contribution and replaces exterior potential values by zero endpoint values, preserving minimality. Interior elliptic bounds supply bounded gradient for this bounded smooth-potential solution. Adding passive spatial variables preserves class-A minimality and the ordering failure, giving higher-dimensional examples. A compatible smooth nonnegative extension is part of this construction; it is not asserted for an arbitrary unspecified double-well jet.

The reported counterexamples refute bounded gradient as a universal sufficient condition. They do not settle all lower-dimensional cases or the broader request for other analytic criteria.

Short English statement

Ask whether bounded-gradient entire minimizers of a periodic scalar variational problem must have ordered integer translates. The supplied literature assessment reports counterexamples in every dimension at least eight, using nonflat Allen--Cahn minimizers and a smooth periodic-potential transfer.
