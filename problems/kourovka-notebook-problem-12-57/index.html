Kourovka Notebook Problem 12.57

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a finite group and let $A\leq G$ be cyclic of order $4$. For $g\in G$, write $A^g=g^{-1}Ag$. Say that $A$ is a $TI$-subgroup if \[ \forall g\in G,\qquad A\cap A^g\in\{\{1\},A\}. \] In particular, no assumption that $A$ is nonnormal is imposed. A component of $G$ is a subnormal quasisimple subgroup $L\leq G$: thus there is a chain $L=L_0\trianglelefteq L_1\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq L_r=G$, and $L=[L,L]$ with $L/Z(L)$ a nonabelian simple group. Here $Z(L)$ denotes the center of $L$. Write \[ C_G(L)=\{g\in G: g\ell=\ell g\text{ for every }\ell\in L\}. \] The source notation $C(L)$ is interpreted as $C_G(L)$; the ambient centralizer is not explicitly defined in the supplied record. The product $LC_G(L)$ is a subgroup of $G$.
  1. (a) Is it true that, for every such pair $(G,A)$ and every component $L$ of $G$, \[ A\cap LC_G(L)=\{1\}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A\leq C_G(L)? \] No additional hypothesis that $A$ normalizes $L$ is supplied.
  2. (b) Assuming that $A\leq C_G(L)$ for every component $L$ of $G$, determine the structure of the normal closure \[ \langle A^G\rangle=\langle A^g:g\in G\rangle. \] The assumption includes groups with no components, for which it is vacuous. The source does not specify the desired form of the structural description or classification.

Short English statement

For a cyclic $TI$-subgroup $A$ of order $4$ in a finite group $G$, ask whether trivial intersection with $LC_G(L)$ forces $A$ to centralize a component $L$. Also determine the structure of the normal closure of $A$ when $A$ centralizes every component of $G$.
