Math-1072

Semidegree Thresholds for Directed Cycles

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An oriented graph is a finite simple directed graph with no loops and at most one directed edge between each unordered pair of distinct vertices, so antiparallel pairs are excluded. For a vertex $v$ let $d^+(v)$ and $d^-(v)$ be its outdegree and indegree, and define the minimum semidegree by \[ \delta^0(G)=\min\{\min_vd^+(v),\min_vd^-(v)\}. \] For every fixed positive integer $\ell$ divisible by three (hence $\ell\ge3$), determine the minimum semidegree forcing a directed cycle of length exactly $\ell$ in every sufficiently large $n$-vertex oriented graph.

A precise threshold function for $n\ge\ell$ is \[ q_\ell(n)=\min\{q\in{\mathbb{N}}_0:\text{every oriented }G\text{ on }n\text{ vertices with } \delta^0(G)\ge q\text{ contains a directed }C_\ell\}. \] The requested task is to determine this forcing threshold in the intended eventual/asymptotic sense as $n\to\infty$ with $\ell$ fixed. The source supplies no formula and does not distinguish an exact eventual integer threshold from its asymptotic leading term; that precision must be checked. Any sufficiently-large cutoff may depend on $\ell$.

A directed $C_\ell$ uses $\ell$ distinct vertices with cyclically oriented consecutive edges. It need not pass through any prescribed vertex and need not be spanning. The problem is not changed to a vertex-covering cycle condition, an outdegree-only hypothesis, a minimum total-degree hypothesis, or general digraphs allowing opposite edges.

The supplied dated assessment says the $3\mid\ell$, $\ell\ne3$ cases are settled and singles out the directed triangle case as the Caccetta--Häggkvist exception. It does not supply the threshold formula or a theorem equating this semidegree question with every formulation of that named conjecture. These distinctions and the partial-solution status remain source claims requiring verification.

Short English statement

For each fixed cycle length divisible by three, find the minimum of indegree and outdegree that forces one directed cycle of that length in a large oriented graph. The cycle is unprescribed, and the supplied assessment separates the triangle case from later settled lengths.
