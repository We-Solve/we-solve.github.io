Math-2016

Ricci pinching on solvable Lie groups

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $G$ be a solvable Lie group with Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$. Left-invariant Riemannian metrics are identified with positive definite inner products on $\mathfrak g$, given their usual finite dimensional topology. Let $\operatorname{Ric}_g$ be the Ricci endomorphism, defined by $g(\operatorname{Ric}_g u,v)=\operatorname{Ricci}_g(u,v)$, and set \[ \operatorname{Scal}(g)=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{Ric}_g),\qquad |\operatorname{Ric}(g)|^2=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{Ric}_g^2),\qquad F(g)=\frac{\operatorname{Scal}(g)^2}{\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{Ric}_g^2)}. \] The formula is defined only where $\operatorname{Ric}_g\ne0$. On this domain, a local maximum is a metric $g$ with a neighborhood $U$ in which $F(h)\leq F(g)$ for every admissible $h\in U$. This is a nonstrict local maximum; no quotient by scaling or automorphisms is necessary for that definition.

A solvsoliton, in the usual algebraic sense, is a left-invariant metric satisfying \[ \operatorname{Ric}_g=cI+D\qquad(c\in{\mathbb{R}},\ D\in\operatorname{Der}(\mathfrak g)), \] where $D[u,v]=[Du,v]+[u,Dv]$ for all $u,v\in\mathfrak g$. The question is whether every local maximum of $F$ on the left-invariant metrics on a fixed solvable group is a solvsoliton. In particular, the requested implication is \[ g\text{ locally maximizes }F\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Ric}_g=cI+D. \] It is not a request to classify only global maximizers. The source does not specify how to treat Ricci-flat metrics, including the identically undefined functional on an abelian group. Restricting to its nonzero-denominator domain is necessary for the displayed formula and is marked for review.

Short English statement

Must every local maximizer of scalar curvature squared divided by squared Ricci norm, among left-invariant metrics on a solvable Lie group, be an algebraic Ricci soliton? The functional's undefined Ricci-flat locus needs a convention.
