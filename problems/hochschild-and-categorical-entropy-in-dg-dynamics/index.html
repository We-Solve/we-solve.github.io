Hochschild and Categorical Entropy in dg Dynamics

Problem. Determine hypotheses on a dg categorical dynamical system under which its Hochschild homology entropy, its Hochschild cohomology entropy, or both coincide with the entropy of the corresponding categorical dynamical system. Schematically, characterize the systems for which \[ h_{HH_*}(\widetilde F)=h_{\mathrm{cat}}(F), \qquad\text{or}\qquad h_{HH^*}(\widetilde F)=h_{\mathrm{cat}}(F), \] with both equalities requested if "(co)homology" is intended simultaneously. This is a request for conditions ensuring equality, not an assertion of equality for every system. The excerpt does not determine whether $h_{\mathrm{cat}}$ means the value $h_0$ of a parameterized categorical entropy $h_t$; no equality for all $t$ is asserted here. A complete mathematical formulation requires the omitted entropy definitions and domain hypotheses.

Short English statement

Determine when the growth measured by Hochschild homology or cohomology agrees with categorical entropy for a dg dynamical system. The supplied record does not specify enough definitions or hypotheses to turn this into a unique fully quantified conjecture.
