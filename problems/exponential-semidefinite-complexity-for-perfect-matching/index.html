CS-157 · Math-10375

Exponential semidefinite complexity for perfect matching

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems unknownunknown Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Statement recorded in the supplied source.
For every fixed shift $\rho \in (0,1)$, do the matrices indexed by odd vertex sets U and perfect matchings M, with entry $|M\cap \delta (U)|-1+\rho $, where $\delta (U)$ is the set of edges crossing U, of complete graphs on even n vertices require real positive-semidefinite factorization rank at least $2^{cn}$, for one $c>0$ independent of $\rho $ and all sufficiently large n depending on $\rho $? This also asks for the resulting exponential exact semidefinite-lift lower bound.
