Descent of Freeness for Orthogonal Modular Form Algebras

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

For a subgroup $H$ of the orthogonal group appearing in the source, let $M_*(H)$ denote its graded algebra of modular forms, with multiplication given by multiplication of forms and grading by weight. Under the polynomial-algebra interpretation of "free," a graded algebra $A$ over a coefficient field $K$ is free if there are homogeneous elements $f_i$, indexed by a set $I$, such that the graded homomorphism \[ K[X_i\mid i\in I]\longrightarrow A,\qquad X_i\longmapsto f_i, \] is an isomorphism, where $\deg X_i=\deg f_i$. The source does not specify the coefficient field, the allowed weights, whether finitely many generators are required, or the precise modular-form conventions.

The object $M$ and the notation $O^+(M)$ are also not defined in the supplied record: in particular, the lattice hypotheses, signature, modular domain, and meaning of the superscript $+$ remain unspecified. For fixed compatible choices of these data, the supplied question is as follows.

Let $\Gamma\leq O^+(M)$ and $\Gamma'\leq\Gamma$ satisfy \[ [O^+(M):\Gamma]<\infty, \qquad [\Gamma:\Gamma']<\infty, \] and suppose that $\Gamma$ is generated by reflections. Must the implication \[ M_*(\Gamma')\text{ is a free graded algebra} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad M_*(\Gamma)\text{ is a free graded algebra} \] hold for every such pair? With compatible modular-form conventions, the subgroup inclusion gives $M_*(\Gamma)\subseteq M_*(\Gamma')$. No normality assumption on $\Gamma'$, and no assumption that $\Gamma'$ is generated by reflections, appears in the supplied statement. The missing conventions above must be resolved before this becomes a fully specified conjecture.

Short English statement

Does polynomial freeness of the modular-form algebra of a finite-index subgroup $\Gamma'$ imply polynomial freeness for its containing group $\Gamma$, when $\Gamma$ is a finite-index reflection-generated subgroup of $O^+(M)$? The supplied record leaves the underlying orthogonal and modular-form conventions unspecified.
