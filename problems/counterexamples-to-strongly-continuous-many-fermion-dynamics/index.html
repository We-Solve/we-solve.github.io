Definitions and mathematical statement

Shared notation.
$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.

For a one-particle test function $f\in L^2(\mathbb R^d)$, $a(f)$ is a bounded fermionic annihilation operator. The source's strong continuity means norm continuity of the CAR-algebra automorphism group: $\|\tau_t(a(f))-a(f)\|\to0$ as $t\to0$. A counterexample must give a nonzero lower bound along a sequence of times approaching zero. Suitable states can witness such a bound through expectations or vector norms.

Catalogue wording retained for traceability.
For the unregularized continuum fermion Hamiltonian, find an interaction $V$ and states for which the Heisenberg evolution of $a(f)$ fails to be strongly continuous.
Scope and source qualification.
The primary report explicitly distinguishes operator-norm continuity from strong operator continuity. Self-adjointness of a represented Hamiltonian supplies the latter and does not automatically settle the former. Related record 30006190 asks about cutoff removal.

Short English statement

Find an unregularized fermion interaction and states exhibiting the requested failure of strong continuity.

An exact represented norm-discontinuity mechanism.
On fermionic Fock space over countably many orthonormal modes let $N$ be total particle number and set the toy Hamiltonian $H=N^2$, defined diagonally with eigenvalue $n^2$ on the $n$-particle sector. It is self-adjoint on vectors with $\sum_n n^4\|\psi_n\|^2<\infty$. Fix the annihilator $a_1$. On any normalized occupation vector $\Psi_n$ with $n$ occupied modes including mode one, \[ e^{itH}a_1e^{-itH}\Psi_n =e^{-it(2n-1)}a_1\Psi_n, \qquad\|a_1\Psi_n\|=1. \] Taking $t_n=\pi/(2n-1)\to0$ gives \[ \|e^{it_nH}a_1e^{-it_nH}-a_1\|\geq2. \] The opposite inequality is the triangle inequality, so the norm is exactly two along this sequence.
Continuity topology countercheck.
On each fixed finite-sector vector the phase varies continuously. For a general vector, approximate by finitely many sectors and use the uniform bound $\|a_1\|=1$; this proves strong operator continuity. The moving witnesses $\Psi_n$ account for the failure of operator-norm continuity. There is no contradiction with self-adjointness.
Why this is not the requested counterexample.
The all-to-all number-square interaction is an auxiliary represented model, not an identified admissible unregularized continuum pair potential $V$ of [S2]. Nor has invariance of the abstract quasi-local CAR algebra under this conjugation been proved. The first source gap is realizing an analogous diverging occupation-dependent frequency using the actual allowed continuum interactions and states, with a well-defined limiting Heisenberg evolution. Without those properties the example only demonstrates the topology mechanism, not a solution to the source problem.
