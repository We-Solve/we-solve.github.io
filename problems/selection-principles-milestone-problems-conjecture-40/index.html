Selection Principles Milestone Problems — Conjecture 40

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $X\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$ have the subspace topology. Use the following standard definitions, which are not explicitly supplied in the record and require confirmation against the cited source. A sequence $(g_k)_{k\geq1}$ of real-valued functions on $X$ converges quasi-normally to zero if there exists a sequence $(\varepsilon_k)_{k\geq1}$ of positive real numbers converging to zero such that \[ \forall x\in X\ \exists K\geq1\ \forall k\geq K,\qquad |g_k(x)|<\varepsilon_k. \] The sequence $(\varepsilon_k)$ is independent of $x$. The space $X$ is a $\mathsf{wQN}$-space if every sequence $(f_n)_{n\geq1}$ of continuous functions $f_n:X\to{\mathbb{R}}$ satisfying $f_n(x)\to0$ for every $x\in X$ has a subsequence $(f_{n_k})_{k\geq1}$, with $n_1<n_2<\cdots$, that converges quasi-normally to zero.

An open $\gamma$-cover of $X$ is an infinite family $\mathcal U$ of open subsets of $X$, with $X\notin\mathcal U$, such that \[ \forall x\in X,\qquad \{U\in\mathcal U:x\notin U\}\text{ is finite}. \] Write $\Gamma(X)$ for the family of these covers. The property $\mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\Gamma)$ means that for every sequence $(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}$ in $\Gamma(X)$, there are choices $U_n\in\mathcal U_n$ such that $\{U_n:n\geq1\}\in\Gamma(X)$. In particular, the selected family must be infinite. These cover conventions are explicit here but are not specified in the supplied excerpt.

Conjecture (Scheepers, as attributed in the supplied record). For every set $X\subseteq{\mathbb{R}}$, \[ X\text{ is a }\mathsf{wQN}\text{-space} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\text{ satisfies }\mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\Gamma). \] The record reports that Scheepers proved the reverse implication in the reference labeled $[\mathrm{wqn}]$. It also reports, citing $[\mathrm{wqn},\mathrm{FrwQN}]$, equivalence of the properties labeled $s_1$ for $X$, $\mathsf{wQN}$ for $X$, and SSP for $C_p(X)$, where $C_p(X)$ is the space of continuous real-valued functions on $X$ with the topology of pointwise convergence. Definitions of $s_1$ and SSP, and the precise scope of that background equivalence, are absent from the supplied record; these assertions are retained as source-reported context. The registry labels this problem open; that label is not an independently verified present-day mathematical assessment.

Short English statement

The conjecture asks whether every set of reals with the weak quasi-normal subsequence property satisfies the selection principle $\mathsf S_1(\Gamma,\Gamma)$. The supplied source attributes the conjecture to Scheepers and reports the converse implication as known. The definitions and source conventions require human review.
