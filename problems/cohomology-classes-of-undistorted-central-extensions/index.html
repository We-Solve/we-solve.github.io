Math-4724

Cohomology Classes of Undistorted Central Extensions

Status Partially solved · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedPartially solvedOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Consider a central extension \[ 0\longrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}}\xrightarrow{\iota}E\xrightarrow{\pi}G\longrightarrow1, \qquad \ker\pi=\iota({\mathbb{Z}})\subseteq Z(E). \] For the abstract-group interpretation, give ${\mathbb{Z}}$ the trivial $G$-action. Choose a set-theoretic section $s:G\to E$ with $s(1)=1$. Its defining cocycle $c:G\times G\to{\mathbb{Z}}$ is determined by \[ s(g)s(h)=\iota(c(g,h))s(gh). \] It satisfies \[ c(g,h)+c(gh,k)=c(h,k)+c(g,hk) \qquad(g,h,k\in G), \] and $c(1,g)=c(g,1)=0$. Changing the section replaces $c$ by $c+\delta b$, where \[ (\delta b)(g,h)=b(g)+b(h)-b(gh), \] so the extension determines a class $[c]\in H^2(G;{\mathbb{Z}})$.

The source does not specify the metric framework for the word "undistorted." Under the usual interpretation when $E$ is a finitely generated discrete group, choose a finite generating set $S$ and let $|\cdot|_S$ be its word length. The central subgroup is undistorted if its inclusion is a quasi-isometric embedding: there exist $A\geq1$ and $B\geq0$ such that \[ A^{-1}|n|-B\leq |\iota(n)|_S\leq A|n|+B \qquad\text{for every }n\in{\mathbb{Z}}. \] This interpretation is conditional; finite generation and discreteness are not stated in the supplied question.

Source question. For a central ${\mathbb{Z}}$-extension whose central subgroup is undistorted, determine what restrictions this imposes on the cohomology class of its defining two-cocycle, interpreted as $[c]\in H^2(G;{\mathbb{Z}})$ in the abstract-group setting above.

Unresolved qualifications. The supplied formulation does not specify a class of groups, a metric or length function outside the finitely generated setting, or a cohomology theory for topological groups. It also does not state a particular proposed restriction on $[c]$. Thus it supplies a classification question rather than a definite conjectural implication. These omissions prevent a unique fully specified general formulation. The supplied literature assessment concerns connected Lie groups, but does not provide the hypotheses or conclusion of the claimed restricted result.

Short English statement

The question asks how an undistorted central copy of ${\mathbb{Z}}$ constrains the cohomology class defining the extension. The group category, metric assumptions, and intended cohomological restriction remain unspecified. The supplied record labels the problem partially solved and reports a complete answer for connected Lie groups; this is dated curation requiring independent verification.
