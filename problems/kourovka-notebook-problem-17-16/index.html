Kourovka Notebook Problem 17.16

Status Open · unsolvedmath.comLLM status Open unverified

Top Open Problems Not reported cataloguedOpenOpen

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

Let $S$ be a finite set and let $M=(m_{st})_{s,t\in S}$ be a symmetric Coxeter matrix, with $m_{ss}=1$ and $m_{st}\in\{2,3,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}$ for distinct $s,t$. For a positive integer $m$, let $w_m(s,t)$ denote the alternating word of length $m$ beginning with $s$. Define the Artin group \[ A(M)=\left\langle S\ \middle|\ w_{m_{st}}(s,t)=w_{m_{st}}(t,s)\quad(s\ne t,\ m_{st}<\infty)\right\rangle. \] Its corresponding Coxeter group is \[ W(M)=A(M)/\langle\!\langle s^2:s\in S\rangle\!\rangle, \] where the denominator denotes the normal subgroup generated by the indicated squares. The Artin group is of finite type when $W(M)$ is finite. No irreducibility assumption is imposed.

Write $\operatorname{Aut}(A(M))$ for the group of all automorphisms of $A(M)$, under composition. A group $G$ is called linear here if there exist a field $K$, a positive integer $d$, and an injective homomorphism $G\to\operatorname{GL}_d(K)$.

Question (V. G. Bardakov, Kourovka Notebook 17.16). For every such matrix $M$ with $W(M)$ finite, do there exist a field $K_M$, an integer $d_M\geq1$, and an injective homomorphism \[ \operatorname{Aut}(A(M))\longrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{d_M}(K_M)? \] The field and dimension may depend on $M$. The source does not specify a coefficient field or characteristic; the formulation above uses linearity over some field and does not assert a fixed-field or characteristic-zero formulation.

The supplied source states that finite-type Artin groups themselves are linear, citing Bigelow, Krammer, Digne, and Cohen--Wales. It also reports an affirmative answer for the braid group $A=B_n$ on $n$ strands for every $n\geq2$, attributed to Bardakov and Bryukhanov (2007). These are supplied background assertions, not results independently verified for this draft. The local registry labels the general question open.

Short English statement

Bardakov asks whether the automorphism group of every Artin group whose corresponding Coxeter group is finite has a faithful finite-dimensional representation over some field. The supplied record reports an affirmative answer for braid groups $B_n$, $n\geq2$, and labels the general question open; neither assessment has been independently verified here.
