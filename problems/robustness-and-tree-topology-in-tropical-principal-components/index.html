Robustness and Tree Topology in Tropical Principal Components

Definitions and mathematical statement

Scope and unresolved definitions. The supplied record concerns tropical principal component analysis (tropical PCA), tropical line segments in a space of trees, and possible connections between tropical geometry and deep learning. It does not specify the tree space, tropical arithmetic convention, metric, PCA objective, or meaning of robustness. Consequently, it specifies a research programme rather than a fully quantified conjecture.

For purposes of stating the requested tasks schematically, write $\mathcal T$ for the intended tree space, $D$ for an input collection of trees, and $\operatorname{Top}(T)$ for the combinatorial topology of a tree $T\in\mathcal T$. Requested mathematical investigation.

  1. For tropical PCA applied to $D$, quantify how its fitted principal object and associated output change under outliers and perturbations. The input distance, output distance, outlier model, perturbation model, principal-object dimension, and treatment of nonunique fits must be specified before a sensitivity bound can be formulated. The record does not distinguish perturbations of observations from perturbations of the fitting procedure.
  2. Describe the tree topologies occurring along $\operatorname{tseg}(T_0,T_1)$ and how transitions between them occur. A precise formulation must specify the tree model, segment construction, and treatment of endpoints, degeneracies, and simultaneous transitions; these choices are absent from the record.
  3. Determine whether tropical geometry yields meaningful mathematical connections to deep learning. The record supplies neither a class of network architectures nor a proposed correspondence, performance criterion, or theorem expressing what constitutes a meaningful or useful connection.
No numerical robustness bound, particular topology classification, or deep-learning equivalence is asserted here. The supplied information does not determine such a statement or its quantifiers.

Short English statement

Investigate the sensitivity of tropical PCA and tropical tree segments to outliers and perturbations, describe topology changes along those segments, and seek precise connections with deep learning. The source leaves the mathematical models and success criteria unspecified, so this remains a statement-only research programme requiring clarification and human mathematical review.
