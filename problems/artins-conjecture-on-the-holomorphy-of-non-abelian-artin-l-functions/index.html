Math-32

Artins Conjecture on the Holomorphy of Non-Abelian Artin L-Functions

Status OpenLLM status Open unverified

Statement

Definitions and mathematical statement

An Artin representation is a continuous complex representation $\rho:G_K\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ with finite image. Its Euler product has local factors \[ L_v(s,\rho)=\det(1-\rho(\operatorname{Frob}_v)(Nv)^{-s}\mid V^{I_v})^{-1}, \] with compatible Frobenius convention and inertia invariants. For every irreducible nontrivial such $\rho$, the conjecture says that the resulting meromorphic continuation $L(s,\rho)$ has no poles anywhere in ${\mathbb{C}}$. The trivial representation is excluded, since its Dedekind zeta function has a pole.

Formulation and concept references: [S1].

Short English statement

Are all nontrivial irreducible Artin $L$-functions holomorphic throughout the complex plane?
