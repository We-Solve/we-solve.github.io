\section{Multicolour Erdős--Hajnal Conjecture}\label{3289}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 3289 · Graph Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For an integer $r\geq1$, $K_r$ denotes the complete graph on $r$ vertices. Fix $k\geq2$ and $m\geq2$, and a pattern $\chi:E(K_k)\to\{1,\ldots,m\}$. A colour-preserving copy in $c:E(K_n)\to\{1,\ldots,m\}$ is an injection $\phi:V(K_k)\to V(K_n)$ with $c(\phi(u)\phi(v))=\chi(uv)$ for every $uv\in E(K_k)$. A vertex subset omits a colour if its induced edges use at most $m-1$ colours.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every fixed pattern $\chi$, is there an $\varepsilon>0$ such that, for every integer $n\geq1$, every $m$-colouring of $E(K_n)$ either contains a colour-preserving copy of $\chi$ or has a subset $S\subseteq V(K_n)$ with $|S|\geq n^\varepsilon$ whose induced edges omit a colour? \sref{3289}{2} \sref{3289}{3}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Must avoiding a fixed coloured clique force a polynomially large vertex set missing at least one of the available colours?
