\section*{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.43}\label{2305043}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2305043 · AMR-022-5043 · Analysis · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix $0<r<1$ and let $A_r=\{z\in\mathbb C:r<|z|<1\}$.
Let $\mathcal H_r$ be the class of analytic functions
$f:A_r\to\mathbb C$ with $|f(z)|\leq1$ throughout the annulus.
Every such function has a Laurent expansion
$f(z)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}c_kz^k$, converging on $A_r$, where
\[
 c_k=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|z|=\rho}
                      \frac{f(z)}{z^{k+1}}\,dz
 \quad(r<\rho<1).
\]
The integral is independent of the chosen intermediate radius.

For arbitrary nonnegative integers $m,n$, determine the coefficient
body
\[
 \mathcal C_{m,n}(r)=
       \{(c_{-m},\ldots,c_n):f\in\mathcal H_r\}
       \subset\mathbb C^{m+n+1}.
\]
Thus one seeks necessary and sufficient joint restrictions on
these finitely many coefficients, not only separate bounds for
each coefficient. The remaining Laurent coefficients are free
to vary in a realization.

Equivalently, determine the sharp support value
$\sup_{f\in\mathcal H_r}\operatorname{Re}
\sum_{k=-m}^{n}\lambda_kc_k$
for every complex coefficient vector $(\lambda_{-m},\ldots,\lambda_n)$,
and characterize all functions attaining it. These support values
specify the compact convex coefficient body completely. The
problem includes its dependence on the annulus modulus $r$,
all finite coefficient ranges, and the corresponding extremal
functions. Functions need not extend analytically across either
boundary circle; boundedness on the open annulus is the
only boundary restriction.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Describe all finite collections of Laurent coefficients possible for bounded analytic functions on an annulus, and their extremizers.
