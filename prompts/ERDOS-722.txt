## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For fixed $k>r$, do the divisibility conditions
\[
 \binom{k-i}{r-i}\mid\binom{n-i}{r-i}\quad(0\leq i<r)
\]
suffice, for large $n$, for an $r$-$(n,k,1)$ design? Keevash proved that they do. Here we prove the conditions and construct several exact families, including every Steiner triple system order $n\equiv3\pmod6$. The general absorption theorem is not reconstructed.
>>>
