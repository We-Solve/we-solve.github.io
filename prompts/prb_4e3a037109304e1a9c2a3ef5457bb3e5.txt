\section*{Kirby Problem 3.29}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a closed connected hyperbolic three-manifold, given by a finite
triangulation with $t$ tetrahedra. A connected finite-sheeted covering
$p:N\to M$ has degree $d=[\pi_1(M):p_*\pi_1(N)]$. Its first Betti number is
$b_1(N)=\dim_{\mathbb Q}H_1(N;\mathbb Q)$.
Define
\[
 d_b(M)=\min\{\deg(p):b_1(N)>0\},\qquad
 d_f(M)=\min\{\deg(p):N\text{ fibers over }S^1\}.
\]
Fibering means that $N$ is a mapping torus of a homeomorphism of a closed
surface. These minima are finite by virtual fibering, and
$d_b(M)\leq d_f(M)$.

Find explicit upper bounds for these quantities from finite data for $M$.
In particular, do constants $C,k>0$, independent of $M$ and its triangulation,
exist such that $d_b(M)\leq Ct^k$ whenever $M$ is triangulated with $t$
tetrahedra? Ask separately for a useful explicit bound on $d_f(M)$; the source
does not automatically demand that this second bound be polynomial.

The existence of some suitable cover is already part of the setup. An
unrestricted enumeration of finite covers that eventually finds one does not
supply the requested a priori quantitative degree estimate.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Give explicit bounds on the degrees of covers of closed hyperbolic three-manifolds with positive first Betti number or a surface fibration, and test a polynomial bound for the former.
