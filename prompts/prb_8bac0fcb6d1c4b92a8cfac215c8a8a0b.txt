\section*{Periodic rational difference equations}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an integer $k\ge1$, consider the recurrence
\[
 x_{j+k}=\frac{A_0+\sum_{i=1}^k A_i x_{j+i-1}}
                  {B_0+\sum_{i=1}^k B_i x_{j+i-1}},
\]
where all coefficients are nonnegative, each coefficient sum is positive,
and $A_1^2+B_1^2>0$. Positive initial values give positive subsequent terms.
Let $F:(0,\infty)^k\to(0,\infty)^k$ shift the state one place and append
the displayed quotient. The recurrence is globally periodic if
$F^p=\operatorname{id}$ for some positive integer $p$; its global period is
the least such $p$. Individual initial states may have smaller periods.

The problem asks whether every globally periodic recurrence of this form
comes from one of the following five models by positive rescaling of all
terms and by index dilation:
\[
 x_{j+1}=x_j,\quad x_{j+1}=1/x_j,\quad
 x_{j+2}=x_{j+1}/x_j,\quad x_{j+2}=(1+x_{j+1})/x_j,
\]
\[
 x_{j+3}=(1+x_{j+1}+x_{j+2})/x_j.
\]
Index dilation replaces each lag by a common positive integer multiple,
interlacing independent copies of the original recurrence. Rescaling means
substituting $y_j=a x_j$ with $a>0$ and simplifying the quotient.

Thus the requested classification concerns identities valid for every
positive initial state, not just the occurrence of some periodic orbits.
Coefficient nonnegativity and genuine dependence on the earliest term are
essential restrictions: dropping them admits additional globally periodic
rational maps. Exceptional states of smaller period do not invalidate
global periodicity in this formulation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are the five classical models, their rescalings, and their interlacings all globally periodic recurrences in this family?
