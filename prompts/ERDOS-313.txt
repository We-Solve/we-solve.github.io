Question: Are there infinitely many integers $m\ge 2$ for which there exist distinct primes $p_1<\cdots<p_k$ satisfying
\[\sum_{i=1}^k \frac1{p_i}=1-\frac1m?\tag{$\ast$}\]

\[\sum_{p\mid m}\frac1p + \frac1m = 1?\tag{$\ast\ast$}\]
