\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be any bounded domain and put $H^\infty(D)=\{h\in\mathcal O(D):\sup_D|h|<\infty\}$. Define
\[N(D)=\{a/b:a,b\in H^\infty(D),\ b(z)\ne0\text{ for every }z\in D\}.
\]
This is the source's bounded-characteristic ring. To generate its full ring means to have coefficients $u_j\in N(D)$ with $\sum_j u_jf_j=1$. In either ring, the generated ideal is formed from finite sums of generator multiples. Nonvanishing means no zeros anywhere in $D$, without a uniform positive lower bound.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $f_1,\ldots,f_N\in N(D)$ with no common zero, find necessary and sufficient conditions for them to generate all of $N(D)$. Equivalently, for $g_1,\ldots,g_N\in H^\infty(D)$, characterize when their ideal in $H^\infty(D)$, or in $N(D)$, contains a nonvanishing function. Seek in particular a corona-type pointwise lower estimate on $\sum_j|g_j|$ for $D=\mathbb D$. The source update records a solution in the disc, leaving the stated programme on general bounded domains. \sref{2308003}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize when holomorphic generators produce the whole bounded-characteristic ring, or a zero-free element in their ideals, for general bounded plane domains.
