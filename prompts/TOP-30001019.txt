\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $X$ be a hyperkähler manifold of complex dimension $2n$. Is its algebraic dimension necessarily in $\{0,n,2n\}$? If $a(X)=n$, does the algebraic reduction admit a holomorphic model $f:X\to B$, where $B$ is a normal projective variety of dimension $n$, and hence define a Lagrangian fibration? Does parabolic $\operatorname{NS}(X)$ imply $a(X)=n$?
