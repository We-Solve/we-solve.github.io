\section{The Moving Sofa Problem}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


In $\mathbb R^2$, set
\[
 H=(-\infty,1]\times[0,1],\qquad
 V=[0,1]\times(-\infty,1],\qquad L=H\cup V.
\]
Thus $L$ is a right-angled hallway with both arms of width one. Write $\lambda_2$ for planar Lebesgue measure. A moving sofa is a nonempty connected closed set $S\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ for which there are continuous functions $\theta:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ and $b:[0,1]\to\mathbb R^2$ such that $\theta(0)=0$ and, with
\[
 R_\theta=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad g_t(x)=R_{\theta(t)}x+b(t),
\]
one has $g_0(S)\subseteq H$, $g_t(S)\subseteq L$ for all $t\in[0,1]$, and $g_1(S)\subseteq V$. These are continuous orientation-preserving rigid motions; the sofa cannot be deformed or reflected during the motion. Closed sets are Borel measurable. \sref{statement}{2}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the moving sofa constant
\[
 \alpha_{\max}=\sup\{\lambda_2(S): S\text{ is a moving sofa as defined above}\},
\]
and describe a sofa of maximum area. The optimization allows every such connected shape and every continuous rigid-motion path through the unit-width right angle. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the greatest area of a single rigid planar shape that can be carried continuously around a right angle in a hallway of width one, and identify a shape attaining it.
