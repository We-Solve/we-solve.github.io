\section{High-intensity hyperbolic Voronoi crossing limits}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


In the Poincare disc $\mathbb D$ with hyperbolic metric, sample a Poisson point process of intensity $\lambda>0$ per unit hyperbolic area. Partition $\mathbb D$ into its hyperbolic Voronoi cells and color each cell black with probability $1/2$, independently. Four distinct ideal-boundary points $a,b,c,d$ in cyclic order determine open arcs $A=(a,b)$ and $C=(c,d)$. Let $q_\lambda(a,b,c,d)$ be the annealed probability, averaging points and colors, that a connected black crossing joins these two arcs, meaning that a connected black set has closure in $\overline{\mathbb D}$ meeting both arcs.
Let $x\in(0,1)$ be their conformal cross-ratio, characterized by a conformal map from the disc to the upper half-plane taking $(a,b,c,d)$ to $(0,x,1,\infty)$. Cardy's crossing function is
\[F(x)=\frac{\Gamma(2/3)}{\Gamma(1/3)\Gamma(4/3)}x^{1/3}\,{}_2F_1(1/3,2/3;4/3;x),\]
where $\Gamma$ and ${}_2F_1$ are the gamma and Gauss hypergeometric functions.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Prove that $q_\lambda(a,b,c,d)\to F(x)$ as $\lambda\to\infty$. In particular, prove existence and conformal invariance of the limit and that for each nondegenerate choice of arcs it lies strictly between $0$ and $1$. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Show that high-density hyperbolic Voronoi crossings converge to Cardy's conformally invariant probability.
