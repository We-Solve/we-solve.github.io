\section*{Triangular minimum of planar Coulomb renormalized energy}\label{statement}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $\Lambda\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ be locally finite, with no multiplicities and bounded centered-disk point density. Let $E$ be locally integrable, with $\operatorname{div}E=2\pi(\sum_{p\in\Lambda}\delta_p-1)$ and $\operatorname{curl}E=0$ in the distributional sense. For each $R$, choose a smooth cutoff $\chi_R$ supported on $[-R,R]^2$, equal to one on $[-R+1,R-1]^2$, taking values between zero and one, and with gradients uniformly bounded over $R$. Define
\[W(E)=\limsup_{R\to\infty}\frac{1}{4R^2}\lim_{\eta\downarrow0}\left[\frac12\int_{\mathbb R^2\setminus\bigcup_{p\in\Lambda}B(p,\eta)}\chi_R|E|^2+\pi\log\eta\sum_{p\in\Lambda}\chi_R(p)\right].\]
Must $W(E)$ be at least the finite value for the covolume-one triangular periodic field, for every such field and every such cutoff family?
