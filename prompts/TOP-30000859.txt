\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
In critical anisotropic bond percolation on the square lattice, let horizontal and vertical edge probabilities $p_h,p_v$ satisfy $p_h+p_v=1$, and let $H(a,b)$ be the event of an open horizontal crossing of $[0,a]\times[0,b]$. Does an RSW theorem hold for general $p_h,p_v$, namely a uniform positive a priori bound for square crossings and a conditional extension bound for $H(kn,n)$ for every $k$? More generally, is there a class of shapes for which both properties hold?
