\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.56}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $A$ be a finite nilpotent group acting by automorphisms on a finite
soluble group $G$. Nilpotent means that the lower central series
terminates, and soluble means the same for the derived series.
For a finite group $X$, let $\ell(X)$ be its composition length, the
number of simple factors in a composition series, with $\ell(1)=0$.

An $A$-composition series of $G$ is an $A$-invariant subnormal series
\[
 1=G_0\trianglelefteq G_1\trianglelefteq\cdots
                          \trianglelefteq G_t=G
\]
with no proper refinement by $A$-invariant subgroups.
Its factors are elementary abelian groups viewed as irreducible modules
for $A$ over the corresponding prime fields. Call a factor trivial
as an $A$-module if every member of $A$ acts as the identity on it.
Let $c(G,A)$ count these trivial factors, with repetitions counted,
in the specified series.

The Fitting height $h(G)$ is the least length of a normal series of
$G$ with nilpotent factors; set $h(1)=0$.
The conjecture asks for absolute constants $C_1,C_2\ge0$ such that
\[
 h(G)\le C_1\ell(A)+C_2c(G,A)
\]
for every such group action. The constants may not depend on the groups,
their orders, the primes involved, or the action.

The action is not assumed coprime, meaning $|A|$ and $|G|$ may share
prime factors, and faithfulness is not required. The count concerns
trivial composition factors of the action, rather than the number of
fixed elements in $G$ or the total number of composition factors.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can Fitting height under a nilpotent automorphism action be bounded linearly by the acting group’s composition length and the number of trivial action factors?
