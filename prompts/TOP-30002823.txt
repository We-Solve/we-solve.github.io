\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For the $p$-local sphere, let $L_nS$ be the chromatic localizations, let $L_{K(n)}S$ be Morava-$K(n)$ localization, and let $F_n$ be the common fiber in the chromatic fracture square. Does the natural map include $L_{n-1}S$ as a wedge summand of $L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S$, so that
\[L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S\simeq L_{n-1}S\vee\Sigma F_n,\]
with the complementary spectrum admitting the predicted wedge decomposition into lower chromatic localizations?
