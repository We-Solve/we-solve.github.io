\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let \(G\) be a locally compact, second-countable Hausdorff group, and let \(A\) be a \(G\)-\(C^*\)-algebra, meaning a \(C^*\)-algebra with a strongly continuous action \(\alpha:G\to\operatorname{Aut}(A)\) by \(*\)-automorphisms. With a left Haar measure, the Banach convolution algebra \(L^1(G,A)\) is the \(L^1\)-norm completion of \(C_c(G,A)\), with
\[
\|f\|_1=\int_G\|f(s)\|\,\dd s,
\qquad
(f*h)(t)=\int_G f(s)\,\alpha_s(h(s^{-1}t))\,\dd s.
\]
The coefficient version of the Bost conjecture asks whether the assembly map
\[
\mu^{G,A}_{L^1}:K_*^{G,\mathrm{top}}(EG;A)
\longrightarrow K_*(L^1(G,A))
\]
is an isomorphism in each degree \(*=0,1\), for every such group \(G\) and every coefficient algebra \(A\) in the intended category. The target is the topological \(K\)-theory of this Banach algebra.

The usual assembly-map domain is equivariant topological \(K\)-homology with coefficients, formed from the universal proper \(G\)-space, commonly denoted \(\underline EG\), by passage over \(G\)-compact proper subspaces. The extraction writes \(EG\) without specifying whether it means this proper classifying space or a universal free space. That convention must be checked; replacing one by the other can change the domain. It also does not explicitly state separability or other admissibility restrictions on \(A\), and the words ``every coefficient algebra'' are therefore retained without silently narrowing that quantifier.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the Bost assembly map an isomorphism in both \(K\)-theory degrees for every locally compact second-countable Hausdorff group and every allowed \(G\)-\(C^*\)-coefficient algebra? The proper-space and coefficient conventions require confirmation.
