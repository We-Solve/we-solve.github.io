\section{Turán Problem for $10$-Cycles in the Hypercube}\label{3056}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 3056 · Combinatorics}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $d\geq1$, the hypercube $Q_d$ has vertices $\{0,1\}^d$ and edges joining strings that differ in one coordinate. A copy of $C_{10}$ is an ordinary, not necessarily induced, cycle on ten distinct vertices. Define
\[\operatorname{ex}(Q_d,C_{10})=\max\{|E(F)|:F\subseteq Q_d\text{ is a spanning subgraph with no }C_{10}\}.\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find sharp upper and lower bounds on $\operatorname{ex}(Q_d,C_{10})$, in particular its asymptotic size compared with $|E(Q_d)|=d2^{d-1}$. The historical qualitative question asks whether $\operatorname{ex}(Q_d,C_{10})=o(d2^{d-1})$ as $d\to\infty$. \sref{3056}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine how many edges a hypercube subgraph can retain while avoiding every ten-cycle, including the historical question of whether its edge density must tend to zero.
