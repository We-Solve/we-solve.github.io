\section{Hilbert's Tenth Problem for Number Fields}\label{1222}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 1222 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a number field $K$, does an algorithm exist that takes an arbitrary multivariable polynomial with coefficients in $K$ and decides whether it has a zero in $K$? Determine for which number fields such a decision procedure exists. Solutions are sought in the field $K$ itself.
\subsection{Short English statement}
