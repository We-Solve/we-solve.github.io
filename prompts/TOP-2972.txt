\section*{Kirby Problem 4.96}\label{2972}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2972 · KP-4.96 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A closed symplectic $4$-manifold $(X,\omega)$ is a compact
boundaryless smooth $4$-manifold with a closed nondegenerate real
$2$-form. An $\omega$-compatible almost complex structure is an
endomorphism $J:TX\to TX$ with $J^2=-1$, preserving $\omega$
and satisfying $\omega(v,Jv)>0$ for nonzero $v$.
The first Chern class $c_1(X,\omega)\in H^2(X;\mathbb Z)$
is the first Chern class of the complex tangent bundle $(TX,J)$;
it is independent of the compatible choice of $J$.
Write $[\omega]\in H^2(X;\mathbb R)$ for the de Rham class.

Do there exist two closed symplectic $4$-manifolds
$(X_1,\omega_1),(X_2,\omega_2)$ and a diffeomorphism
$f:X_1\to X_2$ such that
\[
 f^*c_1(X_2,\omega_2)=c_1(X_1,\omega_1),\qquad
 f^*[\omega_2]=[\omega_1],
\]
but no symplectomorphism between the two manifolds exists?
A symplectomorphism would be some diffeomorphism $g:X_1\to X_2$
with $g^*\omega_2=\omega_1$ as differential forms.

The map excluded in the conclusion is every possible $g$, not
just the particular $f$ witnessing the topological and cohomological
agreements. Thus showing $f^*\omega_2\ne\omega_1$ is insufficient.
Equivalently, after transporting forms along $f$, the problem seeks
cohomologous symplectic forms on one smooth manifold with the same
first Chern class but different symplectomorphism types. No deformation
or path connecting those forms is assumed.
All the displayed equalities are required for the same witnessing
diffeomorphism, and the nonexistence conclusion allows every
possible alternative diffeomorphism.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can two closed symplectic four-manifolds have matching smooth type, symplectic cohomology class and first Chern class, yet fail to be symplectomorphic?
