## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
We show that $22r^2n$ edges suffice to force a subdivision of $K_r$ in a finite simple graph on $n$ vertices. The constant is deliberately unoptimized.
The explicit external input is the Bollob\'as--Thomason linkage theorem: every $22t$-connected graph is $t$-linked, meaning that any prescribed pairing of $2t$ distinct vertices can be joined by $t$ pairwise vertex-disjoint paths. Its substantial proof is not reproduced. We prove the density-to-connectivity lemma and the subsequent construction of the subdivision.
