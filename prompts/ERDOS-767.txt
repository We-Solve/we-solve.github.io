## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let \(g_k(n)\) be the maximum number of edges in an \(n\)-vertex simple graph with no cycle having \(k\) chords incident to one vertex of that cycle. For fixed positive \(k\), is
\[
g_k(n)=(k+1)n-(k+1)^2
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\)?
Jiang proved that the equality holds for \(n\geq3k+3\). This attempt proves the exact proposed lower bound and an upper bound differing from it by only a constant depending on \(k\):
\[
(k+1)n-(k+1)^2\leq g_k(n)
\leq(k+1)n-\frac{(k+1)(k+2)}2 \qquad(n\geq2k+2). \tag{1}
\]
Attempt status: unresolved. The additive constant in the upper bound is the remaining issue.
