\section*{\texorpdfstring{Hyperlinearity of Every Group}{Hyperlinearity of Every Group}}\label{30006756}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $U(d)=\{U\in M_d({\mathbb{C}}):U^*U=I\}$ and
$\|A\|_{2,d}=(d^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(A^*A))^{1/2}$. A group $G$ is hyperlinear in the selected convention if, for every finite $F\subseteq G$ and ${\varepsilon}>0$, there is a map $\phi:F\to U(d)$ for some $d$ such that
\[
 \|\phi(g)\phi(h)-\phi(gh)\|_{2,d}<{\varepsilon},
 \qquad \|\phi(g)-I\|_{2,d}>\sqrt2-{\varepsilon}\quad(g\ne e).
\]
The first condition applies only when $g,h,gh\in F$, and the second only to elements of $F$. Dimension may depend on $F$ and ${\varepsilon}$; exact homomorphisms are not required. Determine whether every group admits these finite approximate matrix models.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2211.10492v3}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every finite portion of every group be modeled by unitary matrices that nearly preserve multiplication while keeping nonidentity elements detectably away from the identity?
\subsection{Sources}
\begin{itemize}
\item[S1]A. Dogon, Flexible Hilbert-Schmidt Stability versus Hyperlinearity for Property (T) Groups, arXiv:2211.10492v3 (25 August 2023).
\url{https://arxiv.org/html/2211.10492v3}.
