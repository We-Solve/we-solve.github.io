## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
A $k$-tuple of integers $0\leq a_1<\cdots<a_k$ is admissible when its residues omit some class modulo each prime. Minimise, over these tuples, the quantities
\[A(k)=\min a_k,\qquad B(k)=\min\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^k a_i.\]
Estimate both functions; in particular, is $A(k)\sim k\log k$? The accompanying remarks also ask about the growth of the greedy admissible sequence beginning at $0$.
