The current page [1] labels the question NOT PROVABLE. Komj\'ath [2] proves consistency of an $\aleph_2$-chromatic graph with no $\aleph_1$-chromatic subgraph. An unconditional proof would conflict with the known consistency obstruction. This attempt therefore proves the countable-target case and examines, with complete proofs, why an induced-subgraph construction does not settle the ordinary-subgraph question.
