\section*{Finite étale covers with a prescribed exterior sheet and p-divisible disk degrees}\label{30007291}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a prime p, a finite extension $k/Q_p$, its algebraic closure and completed algebraic closure Omega. Let X over the algebraic closure be a smooth proper connected curve of genus at least two. Choose finitely many disjoint open disks $V_i$ of positive value-group radii strictly inside rational parameter charts, so that $U=X^an$ minus their union is nonempty and contains an Omega-rational point. Does one smooth proper connected curve Y admit a finite etale surjective morphism to X with a component over U mapping isomorphically to U and every component over every prescribed $V_i$ having degree divisible by p, without shrinking the disks?
