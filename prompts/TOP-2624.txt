{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2624 · Group Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $n\geq1$ and $G$ be a finite group. A left coset of a subgroup $H$ is $gH=\{gh:h\in H\}$ and a right coset is $Hg=\{hg:h\in H\}$. Each $C_i$ below may be a left or right coset of its own subgroup, with no common subgroup, distinctness, or normality requirement. Put $U=\bigcup_{i=1}^n C_i$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Whenever $U\ne G$, must
\[
 |G\setminus U|\geq\frac{|G|}{2^n}?
\]
The proposed lower bound is uniform over all finite groups and every selection of the $n$ cosets. \sref{2624}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
If n cosets fail to cover a finite group, is at least a fraction two-to-the-minus-n of the group left uncovered?
