\section*{Multiple ergodic averages — Problem 4}\label{4800004}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4800004 · AMR-047-0004 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix $\ell\ge1$ and an integer sequence $a(n)$, $n\ge1$.
A connected $\ell$-step nilmanifold is a compact connected
quotient $G/\Gamma$, where $G$ is a nilpotent Lie group of
nilpotency class at most $\ell$ and $\Gamma$ is a discrete
cocompact subgroup. Equip it with its invariant probability $m$.
Call $b\in G$ irrational when $(b^n\Gamma)$ is equidistributed:
the averages of every continuous function along that orbit
converge to its integral against $m$.

Assume two conditions. For every such nilmanifold and every
irrational $b$, the sequence $(b^{a(n)}\Gamma)$ is also
equidistributed. Moreover, for every integer $r\ge1$,
\[
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N
          \#\{1\le n\le N:r\mid a(n)\}>0.
\]
Prove that for every probability space $(X,\mathcal B,\mu)$,
every commuting family of invertible measure-preserving maps
$T_1,\ldots,T_\ell$, and every measurable $A$ with $\mu(A)>0$,
some $n\ge1$ with $a(n)\ne0$ satisfies
\[
          \mu\bigl(A\cap T_1^{a(n)}A\cap\cdots
                         \cap T_\ell^{a(n)}A\bigr)>0.
\]
Here $T^kA$ is the image of $A$ under the invertible iterate.
The systems need not be ergodic. The nilmanifold tests concern
all continuous observables and all eligible rotations; the
positive divisibility densities may depend on $r$. Their role
is a sufficient criterion for recurrence in arbitrary commuting
systems, not only the nilsystems used as tests.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do equidistribution on all relevant nilmanifolds and positive divisibility densities force multiple recurrence for commuting transformations?
