## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let \(P\) lie strictly inside a nondegenerate triangle \(ABC\).
Write \(R_A=PA,R_B=PB,R_C=PC\), and let \(r_A,r_B,r_C\)
be the perpendicular distances from \(P\) to the supporting
lines \(BC,CA,AB\), respectively. The assertion is
\[
 R_A+R_B+R_C\geq2(r_A+r_B+r_C).
\]
Distances to the side lines are intended even when the foot
lies on an extension of a side.
>>>
