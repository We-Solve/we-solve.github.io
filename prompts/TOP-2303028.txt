\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $n\ge2$ and let $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^n$ be a nonempty connected open set, without any finite-volume or boundedness assumption. Write
\[\mathcal H^1(\Omega)=\{h\in C^2(\Omega):\Delta h=0,\ \int_\Omega|h(x)|\,dx<\infty\}.
\]
Lebesgue integration is used.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Classify all domains $\Omega$ such that
\[\int_\Omega h(x)\,dx=0\qquad\text{for every }h\in\mathcal H^1(\Omega).
\]
Include every dimension $n\ge2$; the source records an existing planar classification. \sref{2303028}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify all domains on which every integrable harmonic function has integral zero, including the higher-dimensional cases.
