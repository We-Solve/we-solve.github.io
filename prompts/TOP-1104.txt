\section{Connes Embedding Problem}\label{1104}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1104 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A tracial von Neumann algebra $(M,\tau)$ is a weak-operator closed unital $*$-algebra of bounded operators together with a faithful normal tracial state $\tau$ ($\tau(1)=1$ and $\tau(xy)=\tau(yx)$). Let $R$ denote the hyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with its normalized trace $\tau_R$: the infinite-dimensional factor obtained as the weak closure of an increasing union of finite-dimensional matrix algebras. A factor has center $\mathbb C1$. For an index set $I$ and ultrafilter $\mathcal U$ on $I$, set
\[
 R^{\mathcal U}=\ell^\infty(I,R)/\mathcal I_{\mathcal U},\qquad
 \mathcal I_{\mathcal U}=\{(x_i):\lim_{i\to\mathcal U}\|x_i\|_2=0\},
 \qquad\|x\|_2=\tau_R(x^*x)^{1/2},
\]
where $\ell^\infty(I,R)$ consists of operator-norm bounded families. Its trace is $\tau_{\mathcal U}([(x_i)])=\lim_{i\to\mathcal U}\tau_R(x_i)$. An embedding is a unital injective $*$-homomorphism preserving the specified trace.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every tracial von Neumann algebra $(M,\tau)$ admit a trace-preserving embedding $M\hookrightarrow R^{\mathcal U}$ into some tracial ultrapower of $R$? The index set $I$ may depend on $M$. Equivalently, for every separable tracial von Neumann algebra $M$ and any nonprincipal ultrafilter $\mathcal U$ on $\mathbb N$, does such an embedding into $R^{\mathcal U}$ exist? Here separability means that the predual $M_*$ is separable. \sref{1104}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every finite tracial von Neumann algebra be represented, without changing its trace, inside an ultrapower of the hyperfinite type II-one factor?
