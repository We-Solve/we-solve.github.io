{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306104 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $S_H$ consist of injective, sense-preserving harmonic functions on $\mathbb D$ with the expansion
\[f(z)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_nz^n+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{-n}\overline z^{\,n},\qquad a_1=1.\]
Harmonicity means that the real and imaginary parts satisfy the planar Laplace equation. Writing $f=h+\overline g$ with $h,g$ holomorphic, sense preservation is $|h'(z)|^2-|g'(z)|^2>0$ throughout $\mathbb D$. The subclass $S_H^0$ is defined by $a_{-1}=0$.
Define the covering constant and second-coefficient extremum by
\[d_H=\sup\{d\geq0:\{w:|w|<d\}\subseteq f(\mathbb D)\text{ for all }f\in S_H^0\},
\qquad \alpha_H=\sup_{f\in S_H}|a_2|.\]
The harmonic Koebe map is
\[k_0(z)=\operatorname{Re}\frac{z+z^3/3}{(1-z)^3}
+i\operatorname{Im}\frac{z}{(1-z)^2}.\]
The source places $k_0$ in $S_H^0$ and states that its image omits the real ray $(-\infty,-1/6]$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Prove or disprove $d_H=1/6$, and determine the exact value of $\alpha_H$. Both the covering problem for $S_H^0$ and the coefficient problem for the larger class $S_H$ are requested. \sref{2306104}{2} \sref{2306104}{3}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the harmonic Koebe covering constant and, independently, the largest possible second analytic coefficient in the full normalized harmonic class.
