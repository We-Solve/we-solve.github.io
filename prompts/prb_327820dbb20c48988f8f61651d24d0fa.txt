\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} The gravity--capillary water-wave problem describes irrotational incompressible fluid motion with a free surface subject to gravity and surface tension. The source uses surface elevation $\eta$ and surface velocity potential $\xi$, an energy functional, and a conserved momentum or impulse constraint to obtain solitary-wave minimizers. Global well-posedness means existence for all time, uniqueness, and continuous dependence in its variational energy space. Uniqueness up to translations means that any two minimizers in the specified family differ only by a spatial shift.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} These are the report's two missing hypotheses for the standard orbital-stability argument. Its energy space, constrained minimum, and water-wave parameter regime must be inherited from that contribution; the brief extract does not justify an assertion for all depths, surface tensions, or wave sizes.
