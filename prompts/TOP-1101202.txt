\section*{Questions in Geometric Group Theory — Q 12.2}\label{1101202}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 1101202 · AMR-010-1202 · Group Theory · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $F_n$ be the free group of finite rank $n\geq2$, and let
$\operatorname{Out}(F_n)=\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)/\operatorname{Inn}(F_n)$, where inner automorphisms are conjugations by elements of $F_n$. A subgroup $K\leq F_n$ is characteristic if every automorphism of $F_n$ preserves it.

Whenever $K$ is characteristic and has finite index, each automorphism descends to the finite quotient $F_n/K$. Inner automorphisms descend to inner automorphisms, giving a well-defined homomorphism
\[
 \rho_K:\operatorname{Out}(F_n)\longrightarrow
                     \operatorname{Out}(F_n/K).
\]
Its kernel is a principal congruence subgroup in the sense of this problem. The congruence subgroup property asks whether every finite-index subgroup $U\leq\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ contains $\ker\rho_K$ for some such $K$.

Does this hold for every finite rank $n$? Equivalently, do these kernels form a neighborhood basis for the identity in the full profinite topology, whose basic neighborhoods are finite-index normal subgroups? The subgroup $U$ need not be normal, since it contains a finite-index normal core.

The quotient $F_n/K$ may be any finite characteristic quotient; it is not restricted to abelian quotients or reductions of the homology representation to matrices modulo an integer. Replacing all finite characteristic quotients by only those abelian ones would ask a stronger and different question. The chosen quotient may depend on $U$, with no uniform bound on its size required.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every finite-index subgroup of $\operatorname{Out}(F_n)$ contain the kernel of its action on some finite characteristic quotient of $F_n$?
