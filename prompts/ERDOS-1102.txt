## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let $\mathrm{SF}$ be the positive squarefree integers, of density $\delta=6/\pi^2$. Property $P$ means that $(n+A)\cap\mathrm{SF}$ is finite for every positive $n$. Property $Q$ means that infinitely many $n$ satisfy $n+a\in\mathrm{SF}$ for every $a\in A$ with $a<n$; the membership $a\in A$ is implicit in the source's wording.

We prove that $P$ forces density zero, with arbitrarily slow decay, and that the optimal density for $Q$ is $\delta$. The $P$ argument and the $Q$ upper bound are reconstructed fully. The sharp $Q$ example is reconstructed from an explicitly stated finite construction lemma of van Doorn--Tao, Proposition 12 in \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01087v2}. That sieve lemma is an external input. This is an exposition of their known resolution.
>>>
