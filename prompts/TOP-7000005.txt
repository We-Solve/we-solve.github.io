\section*{Geometry of Curves and Surfaces — Problem 1.5}\label{7000005}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 7000005 · AMR-069-0005 · Geometry · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D$ be a compact smooth two-dimensional disk with a smooth
Riemannian metric $g$ of positive Gauss curvature. Let
$\Gamma\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a smooth embedded closed curve.
An isometric spanning disk is a smooth embedding
$f:D\to\mathbb R^3$ satisfying
\[
                      f^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle=g,
                      \qquad f(\partial D)=\Gamma.
\]
The pullback condition means that for all tangent vectors $v,w$
at every point, $\langle df(v),df(w)\rangle=g(v,w)$. No boundary
parametrization is prescribed in the unmarked version; a marked
version supplies one and requires $f$ to extend it.

Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $g$ and $\Gamma$ for
such an embedding to exist. Conditions must describe the given
data and be more informative than restating that a solution
exists. Matching the curve length with the $g$-length of
$\partial D$ is necessary, but is not by itself the requested
complete criterion.

The entire intrinsic metric is prescribed, not merely a positive
curvature sign or a total area. The disk must be embedded, so an
immersed spanning surface with self-intersections does not solve
the problem. Its boundary is the specified space curve, which
need not be planar.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize which space curves can bound a positively curved disk
with a specified intrinsic metric, without stretching or
self-intersection.
