\section*{Kirby Problem 2.15}\label{2763}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2763 · KP-2.15 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix the standard genus-$g$ Heegaard splitting
$S^3=H_g\cup_{S_g}H'_g$, where both $H_g$ and $H'_g$ are handlebodies
(regular neighborhoods of bouquets of $g$ circles). The Goeritz group
$\mathcal G_g$ consists of isotopy classes of orientation-preserving
homeomorphisms of $S^3$ that preserve $H_g$ setwise. Isotopies must also preserve
that handlebody at every time. Equivalently, it is the subgroup of
$\operatorname{Mod}(S_g)$ whose classes extend over both handlebodies.

For every integer $g\geq4$, is $\mathcal G_g$ finitely generated? If so, give
an explicit finite collection of homeomorphism classes
$\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_k$ and prove
\[
\mathcal G_g=\langle\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_k\rangle.
\]
The number and description of the generators may depend on $g$.
The chosen side of the splitting must be preserved, so elements exchanging
the two handlebodies are outside this convention. Likewise, isotopy as arbitrary
maps of $S^3$ would erase the information being studied; the isotopy is an
isotopy of the Heegaard pair. An infinite generating family alone does not
settle finite generation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is the symmetry group of a standard Heegaard splitting of the three-sphere finitely generated in every genus at least four?
