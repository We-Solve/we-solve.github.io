## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Fix $N\ge 1$.
For subsets $A,B\subseteq\{1,2,\dots,N\}$ define, for each integer $m\ge 1$,
\[
 r_{A,B}(m):=\#\{(a,b)\in A\times B: ab=m\}.
\]
Define
\[
 F(A,B):=\#\{m\in\mathbb{N}: r_{A,B}(m)=1\}.
\]
Question: estimate
\[ M(N):=\max_{A,B\subseteq\{1,\dots,N\}} F(A,B)\]
as $N\to\infty$.
>>>
