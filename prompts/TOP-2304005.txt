\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $P\in\mathbb C[z]$ have degree $n\ge2$ and zeros $z_1,\ldots,z_n\in\overline{\mathbb D}$, listed with multiplicity. A critical point is a zero of the derivative $P'$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every zero $z_j$ of $P$, must there be a critical point $w$ with $|w-z_j|\le1$? Equivalently,
\[\forall j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\ \exists w\in\mathbb C:\ P'(w)=0,\quad|w-z_j|\le1.
\]
The quantifier includes every chosen zero, not only a zero of maximum modulus. \sref{2304005}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every zero of a polynomial whose zeros lie in the closed unit disc have a critical point within distance one?
