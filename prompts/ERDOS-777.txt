## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For \(\mathcal F\subseteq2^{[n]}\), let \(m=|\mathcal F|\) and \(e(\mathcal F)\) count unordered pairs of distinct comparable members. The questions ask:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does \(m\leq(2-\varepsilon)2^{n/2}\) force \(e(\mathcal F)<2^n\) for large \(n\)?
\item Does \(e(\mathcal F)\geq c m^2\) force \(m=O_c(2^{n/2})\)?
\item For every \(\varepsilon>0\), is there a \(\delta>0\) such that \(e(\mathcal F)>m^{2-\delta}\) forces \(m<(2+\varepsilon)^{n/2}\)?
\end{enumerate}
The answers are yes, no, yes. We give a complete explicit counterexample to the second, and deductions for the first and third from precisely stated published inputs. Attempt status: solved relative to those inputs.
