A $3$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices has no three distinct edges whose union has at most six vertices. We prove that it has $o(n^2)$ edges. This is equivalent, for $n\geq6$, to avoiding the stated family of all six-vertex three-edge $3$-graphs: an unused vertex may be added to pad a smaller union. The substantial external input is the triangle removal lemma, stated below.
