\section*{Margulis–Platonov normal subgroups over global function fields}\label{30007292}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every global function field k, and every absolutely almost simple simply connected algebraic k-group G, let A consist of the places v where G has $k_v-\operatorname{rank}$ zero, set $H_A=product_(v$ in $A)G(k_v)$, and let $\delta _A$ be the diagonal map. Is every abstract normal subgroup N of G(k) not contained in its center equal to $\delta _A^{-1}(W)$ for an open normal subgroup W of $H_A$? Include all positive characteristics and anisotropic G.
