\section*{\texorpdfstring{Regular Inverse Galois Problem over Q}{Regular Inverse Galois Problem over Q}}\label{30006738}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A finite extension $F/{\mathbb{Q}}(T)$ is regular over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ when $F\cap\overline{{\mathbb{Q}}}={\mathbb{Q}}$ inside an algebraic closure, so there are no new algebraic constants. The question is
\[
 \forall G\text{ finite}\ \exists F/{\mathbb{Q}}(T)\text{ finite Galois and regular}:\quad
 \operatorname{Gal}(F/{\mathbb{Q}}(T))\simeq G.
\]
Geometrically, this asks for a geometrically integral Galois cover of ${\mathbb{P}}^1_{{\mathbb{Q}}}$ with group $G$. A realization only over ${\mathbb{Q}}$ or with a nontrivial constant-field extension does not meet the regular target.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23615v1}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every finite group be realized by a regular Galois cover of the projective line defined over the rationals?
\subsection{Sources}

\item[S1]Demeio and Gvirtz-Chen, Galois Realisations of PSL2(Fp\textasciicircum{}2).
