\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $A$ be a finite alphabet and $x\in A^{\mathbb Z^2}$. For $n_1,n_2\in\mathbb N$ write $R=\{0,\ldots,n_1-1\}\times\{0,\ldots,n_2-1\}$ and \[N_x(n_1,n_2)=\left|\{(x_{u+v})_{v\in R}:u\in\mathbb Z^2\}\right|.\] A configuration is periodic if there exists one nonzero vector $p\in\mathbb Z^2$ with $x_{u+p}=x_u$ for every $u$. Two linearly independent period vectors are not required.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
If $N_x(n_1,n_2)\le n_1n_2$ for at least one pair of positive integers $(n_1,n_2)$, must $x$ be periodic? \sref{4600054}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does having no more rectangular patterns than positions in one rectangle force a nonzero lattice period?
