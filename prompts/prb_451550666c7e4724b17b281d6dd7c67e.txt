\section*{Kirby Problem 2.27}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
The braid group $B_n$ is generated by $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$, with
relations $\sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i$ for $|i-j|\geq2$ and
$\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}$.
For a finite simple graph $\Gamma$ with vertex set $V$ and edge set $E$, its
right-angled Artin group is
\[
A(\Gamma)=\langle v\ (v\in V)\mid [v,w]=1\text{ when }\{v,w\}\in E\rangle,
\qquad [v,w]=vwv^{-1}w^{-1}.
\]
There are no further defining relations.

For every integer $n\geq4$, does there exist a subgroup $H\leq B_n$ of finite
index, a finite graph $\Gamma$, and an injective homomorphism
$H\hookrightarrow A(\Gamma)$? Finite index means that $B_n$ has finitely many
cosets of $H$. Both $H$ and $\Gamma$ may depend on $n$; no uniform graph or
bound on the index is demanded.
The subgroup need not equal the full braid group. Conversely, finding a
RAAG-embeddable subgroup of infinite index would not establish the required
virtual embedding. Injectivity must hold for all elements of the chosen
finite-index subgroup.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does each braid group on at least four strands have a finite-index subgroup that embeds in a right-angled Artin group?
