\section*{Uniform duality for convex-body covering entropy}\label{30007182}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For convex bodies $K,L$ symmetric about zero, let $N(K,L)$ be the least number of translates of $L$ covering $K$, and let
\[
K^\circ=\{y:\sup_{x\in K}x\cdot y\leq1\}
\]
be the polar of $K$, with $L^\circ$ defined similarly. Do constants $a,b\geq1$, independent of the dimension and the bodies, exist such that
\[
\log N(K,L)\leq b\log N(L^\circ,a^{-1}K^\circ)
\]
always holds?
\subsection{Short English statement}
Are there constants $a,b\geq1$, independent of dimension and of the convex bodies $K,L$ symmetric about zero, such that $\log N(K,L)\leq b\log N(L^\circ,a^{-1}K^\circ)$ always holds, where $N(K,L)$ is the least number of translates of $L$ covering $K$ and $K^\circ=\{y:\sup_{x\in K}x\cdot y\leq1\}$ is the polar, defined similarly for $L$?
