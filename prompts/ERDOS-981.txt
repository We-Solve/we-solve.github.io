## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For an odd prime $p$, write $S_N(p)=\sum_{n\le N}(n/p)$. The problem asks about
\[
 f_\varepsilon(p)=\min\{m\ge1:S_N(p)<\varepsilon N\text{ for every }N\ge m\},
\]
not the first $m$ at which that inequality happens to hold. The current statement and discussion, \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/981} and \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/981}, explicitly correct an earlier first-passage formulation. Elliott's 1969 paper \emph{A conjecture of Erd\H{o}s concerning character sums}, \url{https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/1385725869900067}, is credited with the affirmative eventual-time result. Tang--Zhang, \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24631}, discuss the different first-passage question.

We prove that the natural eventual-time random model has finite positive mean, prove convergence for every finite observation window, and deduce the lower bound for the desired mean. We identify the missing uniformity required for the upper bound. The prime $2$ can be assigned any fixed finite value, with no effect on an asymptotic over primes.
