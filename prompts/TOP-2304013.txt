For $P(z)=\sum_{k=1}^n\varepsilon_kz^k$ with $\varepsilon_k\in\{-1,1\}$, is there an absolute $A>0$ such that
\[\max_{|z|=1}|P(z)|>(1+A)\sqrt n\]
