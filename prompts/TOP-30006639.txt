\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

A nondegenerate braided fusion category has no nontrivial transparent objects. Its Witt class records it modulo the source equivalence involving centers of fusion categories; the pointed part comes from categories whose simple objects are all invertible. Higher-dimensional quantum cellular automata are bounded-range automorphisms of local observable algebras.

Construct higher-dimensional quantum cellular automata corresponding to every element of the Witt group of nondegenerate braided fusion categories, extending the existing constructions for its pointed part.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The requested realization must identify the Witt correspondence and the automaton equivalence class. The source also flags unresolved order and dimension questions for existing examples.
