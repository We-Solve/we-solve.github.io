\section{Weak Limits of Discrete Wave-Turbulence Spectra}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
The torus $\mathbb T_L^d$ has a period proportional to $L$ under the source convention, so its Fourier lattice becomes dense as $L\to\infty$. The Fourier variables $v_k^{\nu,L}$ are the slow-time modes of the damped, forced Schrödinger evolution. Their averaged actions $n_k^{\nu,L}$ are second moments. Weak convergence to a continuous spectrum is tested against functions of the wave vector after placing the discrete actions in the source\textquotesingle s rescaled measure. The iterated limit sends $\nu$ to zero first at fixed $L$, then sends $L$ to infinity.

\paragraph{Target retained from the canonical source catalogue.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For the damped, randomly forced nonlinear Schrödinger equation on the torus $\mathbb T_L^d$, impose $\nu=\varepsilon^2$, $\rho=L^{1/2}\rho_0$, and $b_k=L^{-d/2}b_k^0$. Under the iterated limit $\lim_{L\to\infty}\lim_{\nu\to0}$, do the averaged actions

$$n_k^{\nu,L}(\tau)=\frac12\mathbb E\lvert v_k^{\nu,L}(\tau)\rvert^2$$

converge weakly to a function $n_k^0(\tau)$ satisfying the corresponding damped-driven wave kinetic equation?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The extraction omits the original nonlinear Schrödinger equation, collision integral and test-function topology. Those are source-defined parts of the target; no alternative kinetic equation is supplied here.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Under the stated damping, forcing and box scalings, does the discrete averaged wave spectrum converge to the damped-driven kinetic equation?
