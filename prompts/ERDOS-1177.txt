## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For a finite $3$-uniform hypergraph $G$, write $F_G(\kappa)$ for the class of $G$-free $3$-uniform hypergraphs whose chromatic number is exactly $\kappa$. Determine whether all three assertions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F_G(\aleph_1)\ne\varnothing$ implies the existence of a member with at most $2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$ vertices.
\item For finite $G,H$, nonemptiness of both $F_G(\aleph_1)$ and $F_H(\aleph_1)$ implies $F_G(\aleph_1)\cap F_H(\aleph_1)\ne\varnothing$.
\item For uncountable cardinals $\kappa,\lambda$, nonemptiness of $F_G(\kappa)$ implies nonemptiness of $F_G(\lambda)$.
\end{enumerate}
>>>
