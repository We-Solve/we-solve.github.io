## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let $G$ be any finite abelian group of order $N$. For a uniformly chosen $k$-element subset $A$, let $F_A(g)$ count subsets of $A$ with sum $g$, including the empty subset. For every fixed $0<\epsilon<1$ we prove that, uniformly over $G$, with probability tending to one,
\[
 |F_A(g)-2^k/N|\le\epsilon 2^k/N\quad\hbox{for all }g\in G
\]
provided
\[
 k=\log_2N+O_\epsilon\left(\frac{\log N\log\log\log N}{\log\log N}\right).
\tag{1}
\]
The proof reconstructs the moment bound and repeated exceptional-set reduction of Erd\H{o}s--Hall, \emph{Probabilistic methods in group theory II}, \url{https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1976-34.pdf}. The constants below are deliberately coarse. Only elementary finite-group character orthogonality and probability inequalities are used as standard inputs; all group-uniform estimates are proved.
>>>
