## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Given any fixed $C>0$, we construct infinitely many $a,b,n$ with
$a+b>n+C\log n$ for which the reduced denominator of $n!/(a!b!)$ has no prime divisor exceeding a constant depending only on $C$. Our choices also satisfy $a/n,b/n\to1/2$.
Put $D=C+1$, and for a large integer $M$ write
\[
 L=\log M,\qquad K=\lceil DL\rceil,\qquad M\leq m<2M.
\]
We will take $n=2m$, $a=m+K$, $b=m$.
>>>
