## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let \(F(n,s,t)\) be the minimum possible independence number
of an \(n\)-vertex graph in which every \(s\)-vertex subset
contains an independent \(t\)-set.
Writing \(L=\log n\), with integer roundings understood,
we reconstruct the bounds
\[
 \frac{L^2}{\log L}\ll F(n,L^2,L)\ll L^2,\qquad
 F(n,L^3,L)\asymp\frac{L^2}{\log L}.                            \tag{1}
\]
They disprove both proposed larger lower bounds.
The arguments follow the counting and random-graph methods
of Alon--Sudakov,
\emph{On graphs with subgraphs having large independence numbers},
\url{https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/erdoshajnal4.pdf}.
See \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/804}.
We supply the needed estimates below rather than use (1)
as an unexplained external theorem. All logarithms are natural.
>>>
