## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let \(v=p^2+p+1\), where \(p\geq2\) is a prime power.
A pairwise balanced design consists of blocks of size
at least two, with each pair of distinct points in exactly
one block. If its number \(b\) of blocks exceeds \(v\),
then \(b\geq v+p\).
The usual size-at-least-two convention matters: freely
adjoining singleton blocks would make the displayed
assertion false.
>>>
