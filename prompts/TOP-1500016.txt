\section{Algebraic Stories — Symbolic powers vs. ordinary powers.}\label{1500016}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1500016 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, with $n\geq2$, and let $P_1,\ldots,P_s$ be general distinct points of $\mathbb P^{n-1}$. Write $\mathfrak p_j$ for their homogeneous prime ideals, $I=\bigcap_{j=1}^s\mathfrak p_j$, and, for $m\geq1$,
\[I^{(m)}=\bigcap_{j=1}^s\mathfrak p_j^m,\qquad I^m=\underbrace{I\cdots I}_{m\text{ factors}}.
\]
For a graded ideal $J$, use $\operatorname{Hilb}_J(t)=\sum_{e\geq0}\dim_{\mathbb C}J_e\,t^e$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine, for every $n,s,m$, the entire series
\[\operatorname{Hilb}_{I^{(m)}}(t)-\operatorname{Hilb}_{I^m}(t)
 =\sum_{e\geq0}\dim_{\mathbb C}(I^{(m)}/I^m)_e\,t^e.
\] \sref{1500016}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine in every degree how symbolic powers of an ideal of general points differ from its ordinary powers.
