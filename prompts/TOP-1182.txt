\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let $R$ be a regular local ring and $P,Q$ prime ideals for which $R/(P+Q)$ has finite length and $\dim(R/P)+\dim(R/Q)=\dim R$. Must Serre's intersection multiplicity
\[\chi^R(R/P,R/Q)=\sum_{i\geq0}(-1)^i\operatorname{length}_R\operatorname{Tor}_i^R(R/P,R/Q)\]
be strictly positive?
