\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. All Taylor coefficients below are taken at $0$. A normalized holomorphic function has $f(0)=0$ and $f'(0)=1$.Let $\ell(f)=\sup\{\rho>0:B(w,\rho)\subset f(\mathbb D)\text{ for some }w\in\mathbb C\}$. Define Landau's constant $L=\inf_f\ell(f)$, with $f$ ranging over all normalized holomorphic functions on $\mathbb D$. Here the full image must contain the disc; no univalent inverse branch is required.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the exact value of $L$. \sref{2305009}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the sharp radius of a disc that must lie in the image of every normalized holomorphic disc function.
