\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.119}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A classifying space $K(H,1)$ for a group $H$ is a connected CW-complex with fundamental group isomorphic to $H$ and contractible universal cover. Equivalently, its higher homotopy groups vanish. A group has type $F_n$ if it admits such a space with finite $n$-skeleton, meaning finitely many cells in dimensions at most $n$. In particular type $F_1$ is finite generation and type $F_2$ is finite presentability.

Does there exist one group $G$ with the following simultaneous property? For every positive integer $n$, there are a finite-index subgroup $G_n\le G$ and a homomorphism to the additive infinite cyclic group,
\[
 f_n:G_n\longrightarrow\mathbb Z,
 \qquad K_n=\ker f_n,
\]
such that $K_n$ is of type $F_n$ but is not of type $F_{n+1}$.

The negated condition means that no classifying space for $K_n$ has a finite $(n+1)$-skeleton, even though some classifying space has a finite $n$-skeleton. All the subgroups $G_n$ must belong to the same ambient group $G$; independently constructing a different ambient group for each $n$ does not meet this requirement. The subgroups need not form a nested family and need not be normal. No relation between the homomorphisms for different indices is stipulated, and the source does not require them to be surjective.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can one group have finite-index subgroups with integer-valued homomorphisms whose kernels realize every successive gap between types $F_n$ and $F_{n+1}$?
