\section{Higher-Categorical Models of Continuous and Spacetime Symmetries}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


Higher-categorical symmetry includes objects, morphisms and higher compositions encoding defects, their fusion and junctions. Continuous internal symmetries act on field degrees of freedom, while spacetime symmetries act on geometry. Recovering standard symmetries requires a precise comparison between the higher data and these usual actions.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Develop precise higher-categorical data and constructions that recover standard continuous internal symmetries and spacetime symmetries of quantum field theories, and establish their functorial and physical properties.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}This is a construction programme. Which higher category, topology on continuous data and functorial properties are used must be specified in a proposed framework.

Build precise higher-categorical descriptions recovering continuous internal and spacetime symmetries of field theories.
