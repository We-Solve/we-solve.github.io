\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
A polaron is a quantum particle coupled to a phonon field, with coupling parameter $\alpha$. The Landau--Pekar ansatz approximates the joint state by a particle wave function coupled to a coherent field whose amplitudes evolve by effective equations. Strong coupling means $\alpha\to\infty$. A time window longer than order $\alpha^2$ requires a comparison bound on intervals whose size exceeds that scaling in the source\textquotesingle s time convention, with the error tending to zero in its specified norm.
