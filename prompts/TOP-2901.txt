\section*{Kirby Problem 4.25}\label{2901}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2901 · KP-4.25 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a closed connected orientable smooth $3$-manifold, and give
$S^4$ its standard smooth structure. A smooth embedding $e:M\to S^4$
is a smooth injective immersion which is a homeomorphism onto its
image. Since $M$ is compact, the image is a closed embedded
hypersurface. No preferred position or parameterization of that image
is prescribed.

Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $M$ for such an embedding
to exist. The classification should distinguish exactly the manifolds
admitting smooth embeddings in the standard $4$-sphere, rather than
only supplying necessary obstructions or sufficient constructions for
special families.

The algorithmic part asks whether a single algorithm can decide this
property from a finite triangulation of $M$. The input is promised
to triangulate a closed orientable $3$-manifold. The algorithm must
halt on every valid input and return yes precisely when the associated
smooth $3$-manifold embeds smoothly in $S^4$. There is no bound
specified on its running time.

The target is smooth embeddability; a locally flat topological embedding
alone does not suffice. Similarly, embeddability in another homology
$4$-sphere, or in a connected sum of copies of $S^2\times S^2$,
is a different question. The criterion and decision procedure must
work for all closed orientable $M$, without assuming that $M$ is a
homology sphere, prime, or given with a special geometric structure.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize the closed orientable three-manifolds that smoothly embed in the standard four-sphere, and decide whether this property is algorithmically decidable.
