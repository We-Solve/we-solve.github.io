\section*{Outer automorphisms of oligomorphic groups in finite languages}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

Let $L$ be a finite first-order signature and let $M$ be a countably infinite
$L$-structure. The structure is $\omega$-categorical if all countably infinite
models of its complete theory are isomorphic to $M$.

Write $G=\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ and give $G$ the topology of pointwise
convergence on the discrete underlying set of $M$. A basic neighborhood of
$g\in G$ consists of all automorphisms agreeing with $g$ on a specified finite
subset of $M$. Let $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{top}}(G)$ be the group of
topological group automorphisms of $G$, and put
\[
 \operatorname{Inn}(G)=\{h\mapsto ghg^{-1}:g\in G\},\qquad
 \operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{top}}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G).
\]

For every such $\omega$-categorical structure $M$, is
$\operatorname{Out}(\operatorname{Aut}(M))$ finite?
The requested finiteness is for each structure separately; no uniform bound
on its size is imposed.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the automorphism group of every countably infinite omega-categorical
structure in a finite language have only finitely many outer topological
automorphisms?

\subsection{Sources}
Andre Nies's section, Problem 2.1, in G. Barmpalias, S. Gao, J. He, T. Kihara,
A. Nies, T. Slaman, C.-M. Tran, D. Turetsky, P. Welch, L. Yu and H. Zhang,
\emph{Open Problems in Mathematical Logic}, arXiv:2608.26628v2 (31 August 2026),
\url{https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26628v2#S2}.
The source lists the question as open; source checked on 24 October 2026.
