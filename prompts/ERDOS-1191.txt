## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Write $A(x)=|A\cap[1,x]|$ for an infinite Sidon subset $A$ of the positive integers; Sidon means that $a+b=c+d$ forces equality of the unordered pairs. Must every such $A$ satisfy
\[\liminf_{x\to\infty}\frac{A(x)\sqrt{\log x}}{\sqrt x}=0?\]
Conversely, is there an infinite Sidon set $A$ and a constant $c>0$ such that
\[\liminf_{x\to\infty}\frac{A(x)(\log x)^c}{\sqrt x}>0?\]
>>>
