\section{Sidon Set Size Bounds}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A finite set $A\subset\mathbb Z$ is Sidon if $a+b=c+d$ with $a,b,c,d\in A$ implies equality of the unordered pairs $\{a,b\}=\{c,d\}$, with multiplicity retained when a pair repeats an element. Equivalently, each nonzero ordered difference has at most one representation. For $[N]=\{1,\ldots,N\}$, put
\[
 F(N)=\max\{|A|:A\subset[N]\text{ is Sidon}\}.
\]
The notation $O(1)$ denotes an absolute bounded term independent of $N$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Improve lower or upper estimates for $F(N)$, at least along infinitely many integers $N\to\infty$, in comparison with the square-root benchmarks
\[
 \sqrt N+O(1)\leq F(N)\leq\sqrt N+N^{1/4}+O(1).
\]
The lower benchmark is realized along the prime-power construction sequence $N=q^2+q+1$ with $F(N)\geq q+1$; it is not being asserted uniformly for every integer $N$. The request includes improvements of the second-order term, not merely a proof of $F(N)=N^{1/2+o(1)}$. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Sharpen the known size estimates for Sidon subsets of an interval, even if the improvement holds only for an infinite sequence of interval lengths.

\item[\hypertarget{source:statement:2}{S2}] Daniel Carter, Zach Hunter and Kevin O’Bryant, \emph{On the Diameter of Finite Sidon Sets}, arXiv:2310.20032v1 (2023), §1. It states the equivalent interval extremal problem and Singer and Erdős--Turán benchmarks, and gives the improved upper bound $F(N)\leq\sqrt N+0.98183N^{1/4}+O(1)$. \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.20032}.
