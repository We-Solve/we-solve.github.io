\section*{Logarithmic Sobolev inequality from subgaussian marginals}\label{30007120}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does an absolute C exist such that each centered log-concave probability measure $\mu $ with a Lebesgue density on $R^{n}$ and each $a>0$ satisfying $sup_{|\theta |=1}\int \exp (\langle x,\theta \rangle ^{2}/a^{2})d\mu \leq 2$ obeys $Ent_\mu (f^{2})\leq Ca^{2}\int |\nabla f|^{2}d\mu $ for every compactly supported smooth f? $Ent_\mu (g)=\int g$ $logg-(\int g)\log (\int g)$. The same C must work for all $n,a,\mu $.
