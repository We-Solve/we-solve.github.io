## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For a $4$-chromatic graph list its different cycle lengths as $\ell_1<\ell_2<\cdots$. Can the least consecutive difference be arbitrarily large, even when the girth is large? No: there are always two cycle lengths differing by one or two, and hence some consecutive difference is at most two.
The general minimum-degree theorem is due to Bondy and Vince. We reconstruct the induced-cycle argument needed for a $4$-critical subgraph, including its structural lemma, so no external cycle theorem is assumed.
>>>
