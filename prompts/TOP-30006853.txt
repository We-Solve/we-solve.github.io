\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A CAT(0) space is a geodesic metric space whose geodesic triangles are no thicker than Euclidean comparison triangles. It is proper if closed bounded sets are compact. A group action by isometries is proper if only finitely many translates of a compact set meet that set, and cocompact if the quotient is compact. Suppose $G$ admits such an action on a proper CAT(0) space. The target is the alternative
\[
 H\le G,\ H\text{ finitely generated}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 [H\text{ virtually solvable}\ \text{or}\ F_2\hookrightarrow H].
\]
Here virtually solvable means having a solvable subgroup of finite index, and $F_2$ is the free group on two generators. No cubical structure, finite-dimensionality, or geodesic completeness of the space is assumed in the recorded question.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/forum-of-mathematics-pi/article/tits-alternative-for-2dimensional-mathrm-cat0-complexes/E1B5B96E5D4324F2F3707B4A05B2D86C}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Must every finitely generated subgroup of a CAT(0) group either be solvable up to finite index or contain a nonabelian free group?
