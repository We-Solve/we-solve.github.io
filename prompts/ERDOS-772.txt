## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For fixed \(k\), suppose \(|A|=n\), \(A\subset\mathbb N\), and the ordered representation function \(r_A(s)=1_A*1_A(s)\) is at most \(k\) for every \(s\). Must \(A\) contain a Sidon subset of size larger than \(n^{1/2+c}\) for some \(c>0\)?
We prove the stronger bound
\[
\max_{\substack{B\subseteq A\\B\ {\rm Sidon}}}|B|
\geq \frac{3}{4(4k)^{1/3}}n^{2/3}-\frac14. \tag{1}
\]
Thus for every fixed \(k\), any fixed \(c<1/6\) works for sufficiently large \(n\). Attempt status: solved for both questions. A Sidon set here requires equality of two unordered pairs, allowing a repeated element, to be trivial.
>>>
