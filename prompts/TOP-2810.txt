\section*{Kirby Problem 3.12}\label{2810}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2810 · KP-3.12 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a hyperbolic three-manifold with curvature $-1$. For a smooth
immersion $f:S\to M$ of a closed connected orientable surface of genus at
least two, define its area using the pullback metric:
\[
 A(f)=\int_S dA_{f^*g_M}.
\]
Self-overlaps are counted with multiplicity. Require $f_*:\pi_1(S)\to\pi_1(M)$
to be injective. Taking the infimum over immersions in a homotopy class is
allowed; one is not required to choose a totally geodesic representative.

Determine the following universal infima: (a) $A(f)$ over all such immersions
in all closed hyperbolic three-manifolds; (b) the same infimum with the genus
fixed at a specified $g\geq2$; (c) the infima obtained by requiring $f$ to be
an embedding; and (d) the analogous question for closed orientable essential
surfaces when the ambient hyperbolic manifold is allowed to be nonclosed.
For (d), essential excludes spheres and peripheral surfaces, including
surfaces homotopic into a cusp, and retains $\pi_1$-injectivity.

The ambient manifold varies in these optimizations; a lower bound depending
on one fixed $M$ is not a universal answer. The question asks for the actual
infimum, so one must pair a proposed universal bound with examples approaching
it, without assuming that a minimum is attained.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find the smallest possible areas of essential orientable surfaces in hyperbolic three-manifolds, with variants fixing genus or requiring an embedding.
