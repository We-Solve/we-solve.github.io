## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For $x>0$, let $R_n(x)$ be the largest sum of $n$ distinct positive unit fractions strictly below $x$. Is it true for almost every $x$ that the optimal underapproximations are eventually obtained by successively appending the largest permissible unused unit fraction? Equivalently, after a finite initial stage there is one increasing denominator sequence whose $n$-term partial sums equal $R_n(x)$ for every subsequent $n$.
