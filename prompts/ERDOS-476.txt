## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For $A\subseteq\mathbb F_p$, put $A\hat+ A=\{a+b:a,b\in A,\ a\ne b\}$. We prove
\[|A\hat+ A|\ge\min(p,2|A|-3).\]
The original theorem is due to Dias da Silva and Hamidoune. This proof reconstructs the elementary method of Alon, Nathanson and Ruzsa, including the coefficient identity it needs.
