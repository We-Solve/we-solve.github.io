\section{Erdős–Hajnal Conjecture}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Graphs are finite, simple and undirected. For a graph $G$, let $\omega(G)$ be its largest clique size and $\alpha(G)$ its largest independent-set size. A clique has every pair of distinct vertices adjacent; an independent set has no adjacent pair. A graph is $H$-free if no induced subgraph is isomorphic to $H$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every finite simple graph $H$, is there a real number $\delta_H>0$ such that every nonempty $H$-free graph $G$ satisfies
\[
 \max\{\alpha(G),\omega(G)\}\ge |V(G)|^{\delta_H}?
\]
The exponent depends on $H$ and must be uniform over all orders and all $H$-free graphs $G$. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
For each forbidden induced graph, must every graph avoiding it have a clique or independent set whose size is a fixed positive power of its order?
