\section{Book Thickness of Subdivisions}\label{3105}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 3105 · Graph Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A $k$-page book embedding of a finite undirected simple graph $G$ is a linear order $\prec$ of its vertices and an assignment of edges to $k$ pages such that no two same-page edges $vw,xy$ have endpoints ordered $v\prec x\prec w\prec y$. The book thickness $\operatorname{bt}(G)$ is the least nonnegative number of pages required. Let $G'$ replace each edge $uv$ by $u w_{uv},w_{uv}v$, using a distinct new vertex for each edge.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is there a function $f:\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\to\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ such that
\[\operatorname{bt}(G)\leq f\bigl(\operatorname{bt}(G')\bigr)\]
for every finite undirected simple graph $G$? \sref{3105}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can the book thickness of a graph be bounded solely in terms of the book thickness after every edge is subdivided exactly once?
