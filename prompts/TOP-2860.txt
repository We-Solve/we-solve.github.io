\section{Kirby Problem 3.62}\label{2860}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2860 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For closed oriented $3$-manifolds, a continuous map $f:Y\to Z$ has degree $d$ if $f_*[Y]=d[Z]$ in $H_3(Z;\mathbb Z)$. Write $\widehat{\operatorname{HF}}(Y)$ for integral hat Heegaard Floer homology with the canonical coherent orientation system and
\[
\operatorname{HF}_{\rm red}(Y)=\operatorname{coker}\bigl(\operatorname{HF}^{\infty}(Y)\to\operatorname{HF}^{+}(Y)\bigr).
\]
Here $\operatorname{rk}A=\dim_{\mathbb Q}(A\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q)$, summed over all spin$^c$ structures, as in the integral convention of the source's section introduction. For knots in $S^3$, $\widehat{\operatorname{HFK}}(S^3,K;i)$ denotes the summand in Alexander grading $i\in\mathbb Z$.
For a nullhomotopic knot $K\subset Z$, use its meridian and Seifert longitude to define $p/q$ Dehn surgery $Z_{p/q}(K)$, where $\gcd(p,q)=1$ and $q>0$. A proper degree-one map of knot exteriors maps boundary to boundary and sends their relative fundamental classes to one another.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine how Floer invariants behave under maps of nonzero degree. In particular, for every degree-one map of closed oriented manifolds $f:Y\to Z$, are both inequalities
\[
\operatorname{rk}\widehat{\operatorname{HF}}(Y)\ge\operatorname{rk}\widehat{\operatorname{HF}}(Z),\qquad
\operatorname{rk}\operatorname{HF}_{\rm red}(Y)\ge\operatorname{rk}\operatorname{HF}_{\rm red}(Z)
\]
valid? The source also asks the refinements: if $Y=Z_{p/q}(K)$ for a nullhomotopic knot, must $\operatorname{rk}\widehat{\operatorname{HF}}(Y)\ge p\operatorname{rk}\widehat{\operatorname{HF}}(Z)$? If there is a proper degree-one map from the exterior of $K\subset S^3$ to that of $L\subset S^3$, must
\[
\operatorname{rk}\widehat{\operatorname{HFK}}(S^3,K)\ge\operatorname{rk}\widehat{\operatorname{HFK}}(S^3,L),
\]
and must this inequality hold in every Alexander grading? \sref{2860}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find rank inequalities under nonzero-degree maps, especially degree-one monotonicity for hat and reduced Heegaard Floer homology, the stronger surgery bound, and total and Alexander-graded knot Floer bounds under degree-one maps of exteriors.
