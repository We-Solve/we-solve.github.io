\section{Are there any Wieferich primes in base 47}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For an integer base $a\ge1$, a prime $p$ is Wieferich to base $a$ if $p\nmid a$ and $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod{p^2}$. Equivalently $p$ divides the integer Fermat quotient $(a^{p-1}-1)/p$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does there exist a prime $p\ne47$ such that $47^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod{p^2}$? \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does any prime satisfy the square-modulus Fermat congruence for base forty-seven?
