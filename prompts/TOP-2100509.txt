\section{Open Problems in Integrable Systems — Nijenhuis operators: singular points and global properties}\label{2100509}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2100509 · Dynamical Systems}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A smooth endomorphism field $L:TM\to TM$ is Nijenhuis if, for all smooth vector fields $X,Y$, its torsion
\[N_L(X,Y)=L^2[X,Y]+[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]\]
vanishes identically.\par The Segre characteristic of a real endomorphism records its Jordan block sizes, the grouping of blocks by equal eigenvalues, and which eigenvalues are real or occur in complex-conjugate pairs. Numerical eigenvalues may vary while this characteristic stays fixed. An endomorphism field on $\mathbb R^n$ has compact support if it vanishes outside a compact subset.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For an arbitrary prescribed Segre characteristic in dimension $n$, does there exist a smooth compactly supported Nijenhuis field $L$ on $\mathbb R^n$ and a nonempty open domain $U\subset\mathbb R^n$ such that $L_x$ has that characteristic for every $x\in U$? More generally, find necessary and sufficient conditions on the characteristic for such an $L$ and $U$ to exist. \sref{2100509}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which fixed real Jordan types can occur on an open region of a compactly supported Nijenhuis tensor? Give a necessary and sufficient criterion.
