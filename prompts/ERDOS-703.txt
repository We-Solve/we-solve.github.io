## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let \(T(n,r)\) be the largest size of a family
\(\mathcal F\subseteq2^{[n]}\) for which \(|A\cap B|\ne r\).
We give the known exact answer for fixed \(r\geq2\) and
sufficiently large \(n\), and the exponential upper bound
when \(r\) is bounded away from \(0\) and \(n/2\).
The upper bounds are established external theorems of
Frankl--F\"uredi and Frankl--R\"odl. We reconstruct the matching
families, check parity and quantifiers, and derive their
asymptotic consequences. No new solution is claimed.
See \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/703}.
>>>
