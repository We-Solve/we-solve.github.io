## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
If $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ has positive density, must it contain three distinct elements $a,b,c$ with $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=c$? Yes. We prove that an infinite set avoiding such triples has upper logarithmic density zero:
\[\limsup_{X\to\infty}\frac1{\log X}\sum_{n\le X,\ n\in A}\frac1n=0.\tag{1}\]
Positive natural density, and even positive lower natural density, implies positive lower logarithmic density by partial summation, so (1) suffices for the stated problem.
>>>
