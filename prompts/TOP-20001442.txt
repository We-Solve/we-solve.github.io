\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For origin-symmetric convex bodies $K,L\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ with support functions $h_K,h_L$, define
\[tK+_0(1-t)L=\bigcap_{u\in S^{n-1}}\{x:\langle x,u\rangle\leq h_K(u)^th_L(u)^{1-t}\}.\]
Must its volume be at least $|K|^t|L|^{1-t}$ for $0\leq t\leq1$? The stated equality question asks whether equality occurs only for $K=L$ or for parallelograms with parallel sides.
