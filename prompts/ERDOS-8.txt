## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#8; reconstruction of a known counterexample, 2026-09-23.}
Given a finite colouring of the positive integers, must there be a distinct covering system whose moduli all have the same colour? A distinct covering system consists of finitely many residue classes $a_d+d\mathbb Z$, with different moduli $d>1$, whose union is $\mathbb Z$. The question concerns the colours of the moduli, not the colours of the integers covered by their residue classes.
