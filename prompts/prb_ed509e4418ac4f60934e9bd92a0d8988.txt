\section{Vertex Coloring of graph fractional powers}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


All graphs here are finite and simple. For $n\ge1$, let $S_n(G)$ replace every edge of $G$ by a path of length $n$, with distinct new internal vertices. For a graph $H$, let $H^m$ join distinct vertices at graph distance at most $m$. Define $G^{m/n}=(S_n(G))^m$. Let $\Delta$, $\chi$ and $\omega$ denote maximum degree, chromatic number and largest clique size.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
The recorded conjecture asks whether, for every connected graph $G$ with $\Delta(G)\ge3$ and all integers $n>m>1$, \[\chi(G^{m/n})=\omega(G^{m/n}).\] \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Do the chromatic and clique numbers agree for every fractional graph power with numerator greater than one and denominator larger than the numerator?
