\section*{Kourovka Notebook Problem 21.17}\label{2526}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2526 · KOU-21.17 · Group Theory · Kourovka Notebook - New Problems, Issue 21}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a class $\mathcal X$ of groups, define four classes up to isomorphism.
$H(\mathcal X)$ consists of all quotients of members of $\mathcal X$;
$S(\mathcal X)$ consists of all their subgroups; $P(\mathcal X)$ consists
of direct products indexed by arbitrary sets; and $P_f(\mathcal X)$
consists of products with finitely many factors. The arbitrary products
are unrestricted Cartesian products, not direct sums. Operators are
applied from the inside outward, so $S(H(\mathcal X))$ means subgroups
of quotients, not quotients of subgroups.

A group $G$ is metabelian if its commutator subgroup $G'$ is abelian,
or equivalently $G''=1$. For every class $\mathcal M$ consisting of
metabelian groups, must the following inclusion hold?
\[
 H(S(P_f(\mathcal M)))\ \subseteq\ S(H(P(S(\mathcal M)))).
\]
More concretely, take finitely many groups $M_1,\ldots,M_t\in\mathcal M$,
a subgroup $U\le\prod_{i=1}^tM_i$, and a normal subgroup $N\trianglelefteq U$.
The question asks whether $U/N$ embeds into a quotient of a possibly
infinite direct product of groups each isomorphic to a subgroup of a
member of $\mathcal M$.

All choices of $U$ and $N$ are allowed, and the representing product
and quotient may depend on them. The class is not assumed closed under
any of these operations, and its groups need not be finite or finitely
generated. The proposed inclusion is the target; no equality of the
two operator expressions is being demanded.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For metabelian groups, can every quotient of a subgroup of a finite product embed in a quotient of an arbitrary product of subgroups?
