\section*{Indicators of linear combinations}\label{3700012}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3700012 · AMR-036-0012 · Analysis · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix $\rho>0$ and $n\geq1$. Let $A\subset\mathbb C^n$ be
a set such that every choice of $n$ distinct vectors is
linearly independent. For each $a\in A$, prescribe a
continuous $2\pi$-periodic real function $h_a$ satisfying
$h_a''+\rho^2h_a\geq0$ in the sense of distributions.
This is the meaning of $\rho$-trigonometric convexity.

An entire function $f$ has order $\rho$ and normal type
when, with $M(r,f)=\max_{|z|=r}|f(z)|$,
$0<\limsup_{r\to\infty}r^{-\rho}\log M(r,f)<\infty$.
Its indicator at this order is
\[
 h_f(\theta)=\limsup_{r\to\infty}
                 r^{-\rho}\log|f(re^{i\theta})|.
\]

Characterize the prescribed data for which there are entire
functions $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ of order $\rho$ and normal type
such that, for every $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\in A$, the
nonzero combination $g_a=\sum_ja_jf_j$ has indicator $h_a$.
All combinations use the same finite collection of functions;
separately realizing each indicator is insufficient.

Also characterize realization with completely regular growth.
For a function $g$ with indicator $h_g$, this means
\[
 t^{-\rho}\log|g(tz)|\longrightarrow
             |z|^\rho h_g(\arg z)\quad(t\to\infty)
\]
in the distribution space $\mathcal D'(\mathbb C)$.
In this version require that the realizing functions and
the specified combinations have this regular growth.
The target is necessary and sufficient compatibility
conditions, for arbitrary admissible vector sets and
indicator assignments.
\subsection{Short English statement}
When can the indicators of many linear combinations of one finite family of entire functions be prescribed simultaneously?
