For a finite graph $H$, let $t_H(G)$ be the number of graph homomorphisms $H\to G$ divided by $|V(G)|^{|V(H)|}$. Characterize those $H$ for which, for each fixed density $p$, the two conditions $t_{K_2}(G_n)\to p$ and $t_H(G_n)\to p^{|E(H)|}$ force every finite graph homomorphism density to tend to its value in a random graph of density $p$.
