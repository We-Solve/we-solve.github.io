\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For $N\ge1$, define
\[
 r_3(N)=\max\{|A|:A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\},\
 a+c=2b,\ a,b,c\in A\Rightarrow a=b=c\}.
\]
The problem asks for its asymptotic order: an explicit scale $g(N)$ with matching upper and lower bounds $c\,g(N)\le r_3(N)\le C\,g(N)$ for all sufficiently large $N$, or the corresponding precise asymptotic determination intended by the source. Merely improving one bound without a matching other bound does not identify the order.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2309.02353}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
What is the true asymptotic size of the largest subset of the first $N$ integers containing no nonconstant three-term arithmetic progression?
