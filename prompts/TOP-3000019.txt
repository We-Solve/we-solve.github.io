\section*{Conforti-Cornuéjols conjecture on the MFMC property}\label{3000019}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3000019 · AMR-029-0019 · Combinatorics · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A finite clutter $\mathcal C$ is a family of nonempty subsets of a finite
ground set $V$, none containing another. Its incidence matrix $A$ has
rows indexed by $V$ and columns by the members of $\mathcal C$.
For each $w\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^V$, consider the dual pair
\[
 \min\{w^Tx:A^Tx\ge\mathbf1,\ x\ge0\},\qquad
 \max\{\mathbf1^Ty:Ay\le w,\ y\ge0\}.
\]
The max-flow min-cut (MFMC) property says that both programs have
integral optimal solutions for every such $w$.

Deleting an element removes all clutter members containing it.
Contracting an element removes it from every member and retains only
inclusion-minimal resulting sets. Repeated operations produce minors.
The packing property requires $\tau(\mathcal D)=\nu(\mathcal D)$ in every
minor $\mathcal D$ with no empty member. Here $\tau$ is the least size
of a set meeting every member, and $\nu$ is the largest number of
pairwise disjoint members; both are zero for an empty family.

The Conforti--Cornu\'{e}jols conjecture asserts that the packing property
implies MFMC. The reverse implication is part of the standard equivalent
formulation. Unit-weight packing equality in every nontrivial minor
must therefore force integral optimal certificates for arbitrary
nonnegative integral weights on the original clutter, not merely
unweighted equality there.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does matching-cover equality in every clutter minor force the
full integral max-flow min-cut property for every integral weight vector?
