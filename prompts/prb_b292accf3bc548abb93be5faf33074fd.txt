\section{Quantum Variance Conjectures for Matrix Elements}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} Let $Hu_j=E_ju_j$ and $A_{jk}=\langle u_j,Au_k\rangle$ for a selfadjoint observable with classical symbol $a$. $\{A\}$ is the classical mean on the energy surface, and $C(t)=\{a\circ\Phi_t\,a\}$ is its autocorrelation. The regularized power spectrum is $S(\omega)=\int_{\mathbb R}(C(t)-\{A\}^2)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt$ when the chosen regularization makes this meaningful. For $d$ configuration dimensions the source uses the Weyl density $\rho(E)=(2\pi\hbar)^{-d}\int\delta(E-H_{\rm cl}(p,q))\,dp\,dq$. A quantum variance averages squared matrix elements over the appropriate near-energy ensemble; it is not the value of one chosen entry.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} The catalogue formula suppresses the averaging bar and can be misread as exact equality for each matrix entry. The primary source calls this a distributional variance conjecture in the semiclassical near-diagonal regime, with j distinct from k and frequency (E\_j-E\_k)/hbar tending to zero. The Gaussian assertion is an asymptotic distribution claim under the source's chaotic-dynamics setting, not an identity for every observable and Hamiltonian.

\paragraph{Primary-source variance meaning.} With the report's averaging conventions, the proposed off-diagonal variance is $\overline{|A_{jk}|^2}\sim S(0)/(2\pi\hbar\rho(E))$ and the diagonal variance is $\overline{|A_{jj}-\{A\}|^2}\sim gS(0)/(2\pi\hbar\rho(E))$ as $\hbar\downarrow0$. The source's Gaussian and symmetry-factor statements remain part of the target.

\paragraph{Catalogue mathematical target.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For an observable $A$, do the off-diagonal matrix elements satisfy $|A_{jk}|^2=S(0)/(2\pi\hbar\rho(E))$, hence scale as $\hbar^{d-1}$? Are the diagonal matrix elements Gaussian with mean $\{A\}$ and variance $gS(0)/(2\pi\hbar\rho(E))$, where $g=2$ in the presence of time-reversal symmetry and $g=1$ otherwise?

\paragraph{Saved literature context.} The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The Feingold--Peres-type variance predictions remain beyond general proofs; rigorous work gives upper bounds and special arithmetic cases, so the stated conjectures are only partially supported. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.
