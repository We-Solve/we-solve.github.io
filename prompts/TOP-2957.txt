\section*{Kirby Problem 4.81}\label{2957}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2957 · KP-4.81 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a closed simply connected smooth $4$-manifold $X$, give
$\operatorname{Diff}(X)$ the $C^\infty$ topology and
$\operatorname{Homeo}(X)$ the compact-open topology. Composition
makes each set a topological group. Their component groups are
the corresponding mapping class groups, and forgetting smoothness
induces
\[
 q_X:\pi_0\operatorname{Diff}(X)\longrightarrow
                         \pi_0\operatorname{Homeo}(X).
\]
Write $K_X=\ker q_X$. A class belongs to $K_X$ precisely when
its representative diffeomorphism is topologically isotopic to the
identity, although it may fail to be smoothly isotopic to it.

Does there exist such an $X$ for which $K_X$ is finitely generated?
Equivalently, can one find a finite list of smooth mapping classes
$[f_1],\ldots,[f_r]\in K_X$ so that every class in $K_X$ is a
finite product of these classes and their inverses? The empty list
is allowed if the kernel is trivial.

The question concerns the whole kernel as an abstract group,
not merely its abelianization, a subgroup of detected classes, or
the number of distinct numerical invariants. The manifold may be
chosen, but must be closed and simply connected. No bound on
$r$ is prescribed. The conclusion does not require actual finite
generation of the subgroup of all topologically trivial diffeomorphisms
before quotienting by smooth isotopy.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can the full group of topologically trivial smooth mapping classes of a closed simply connected four-manifold be finitely generated?
