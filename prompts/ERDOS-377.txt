## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Define for $n\ge 1$
\[
  f(n)\;:=\;\sum_{\substack{p\le n\\ p\text{ prime}}} \mathbf{1}_{p\nmid \binom{2n}{n}}\,\frac{1}{p}.
\]
Is there an absolute constant $C$ such that $f(n)\le C$ for all $n$?
