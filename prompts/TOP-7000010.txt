\section*{Geometry of Curves and Surfaces — Problem 2.1}\label{7000010}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 7000010 · AMR-069-0010 · Geometry · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Fix a closed oriented smooth $n$-manifold $M$, where closed means
compact and without boundary. For a $C^1$ immersion
$f:M\to\mathbb R^{n+1}$, the derivative has rank $n$ everywhere.
The orientations determine a continuous unit normal map
$N_f:M\to S^n$, called the Gauss map. Its image describes the
normal directions attained by the immersion.

Given a subset $A\subset S^n$, an $A$-directed immersion is one
with $N_f(M)\subset A$. Characterize the pairs $(M,A)$ for which
such an immersion exists, or equivalently characterize the
admissible sets $A$ for each specified $M$.

The inclusion need not be equality: every direction in $A$ need
not occur. The immersion may have self-intersections; replacing
it by an embedding is a stronger problem. No openness assumption
on $A$ is built into the general question, although open sets
are an important subcase for existence criteria.

An elementary necessary condition is $A\cup(-A)=S^n$. Indeed,
for any direction $u$, the height function
$x\mapsto\langle f(x),u\rangle$ attains a maximum on the compact
manifold, and its normal there is $u$ or $-u$. The problem seeks
a complete criterion, including topological restrictions on $M$,
beyond this necessary condition.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which prescribed sets of normal directions can contain the Gauss
image of an immersion of a given closed oriented manifold?
