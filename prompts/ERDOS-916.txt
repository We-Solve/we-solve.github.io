## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Does every finite simple graph with \(n\geq3\) vertices and at least \(2n-2\) edges contain a cycle and a vertex outside it adjacent to at least three vertices on it?
Call this configuration a wheel in this attempt; the rim may be long, but its three spokes must be edges.
We reconstruct Thomassen's stronger theorem: a wheel-free graph of order \(n\geq3\) has at most \(2n-3\) edges, and equality holds exactly for the graphs obtained by repeatedly gluing triangles and \(K_{3,3}\)'s along edges. Call these graphs cockades.
>>>
