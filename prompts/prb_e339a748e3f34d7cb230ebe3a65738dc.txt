\section*{Kirby Problem 3.75}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $Y$ be a closed oriented rational homology three-sphere equipped with a smooth orientation-preserving action $\rho$ of a finite cyclic group $C_p=\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$, $p\ge2$. An extension over a compact oriented smooth four-manifold $W$ with $\partial W=Y$ is an action $\widetilde\rho:C_p\to\operatorname{Diff}^+(W)$ restricting to $\rho$ on the boundary.

Let $Q_W$ denote the intersection pairing on $H_2(W;\mathbb Z)$ modulo torsion, evaluated over $\mathbb R$ when discussing its signs. It is definite if it is positive or negative definite; include the rank-zero case. It is indefinite if it has both positive and negative directions. The filling is non-spin when $w_2(W)\ne0$.

(a) Determine constraints on $Q_W$ imposed by the extension of the given cyclic action. (b) Is there an integral homology sphere $Y$ bounding a smooth integral homology ball and a cyclic action on $Y$ that extends over no definite smooth filling of either sign? (c) Obtain methods imposing constraints also on indefinite non-spin fillings.

The extension must satisfy the group relation: if a generator acts by $f$ on $Y$, its extension $F$ must obey $F^p=\operatorname{id}_W$. Extending $f$ merely as a diffeomorphism without this relation does not answer the equivariant problem. The order, filling, and action on its homology are otherwise unrestricted, so a proposed obstruction must state any additional hypotheses it uses.
\subsection{Short English statement}
What intersection forms can bound a rational homology sphere while extending a specified cyclic group action?
