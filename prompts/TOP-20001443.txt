\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Log-Minkowski inequality

Show that if $K$ and $L$ origin-symmetric convex bodies with nonempty interior in $\R^n$, then
$$
\int\limits_{S^{n-1}} \log{\frac{h_K(u)}{h_L(u)}}\,d\bar{V}_L(u) \geq \frac1n \log{\frac{|K|}{|L|}},
$$
where $\bar{V}_L$ is the cone-volume probability measure of $L$.
