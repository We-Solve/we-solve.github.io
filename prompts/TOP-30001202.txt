For a discrete random variable $X$, entropy is $H(X)=-\sum_xp(x)\log p(x)$, and mutual information is $I(X;Y)=H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y)$. Coding rates, capacities, and exponents depend on the stated channel or source model and error criterion.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For $x(t+1)=f(x(t))$ and observed quantization regions $P(0),\ldots,P(T)$, define $Q^+(0)=P(0)$, $Q^+(t)=f(Q^+(t-1))\cap P(t)$, $Q^-(T)=P(T)$, $Q^-(t-1)=f^{-1}(Q^-(t))\cap P(t-1)$, and $Q(t)=Q^+(t)\cap Q^-(t)$. Under what conditions on $f$ are some $Q(t)$ small, and is $Q(t)$ the finest information about $x(t)$ obtainable from the quantized observations? \sref{30001202}{2}
