## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let infinite \(A,B\subseteq\mathbb N\) satisfy
\(A+B\supseteq[N_0,\infty)\cap\mathbb N\) and
\(A(x)B(x)\sim x\). Then \(A(x)B(x)-x\longrightarrow\infty\).
We reconstruct Ruzsa's stronger bound
\[
 A(x)B(x)-x\geq(1-o(1))\frac{a^*(x)}{A(x)},\qquad
 a^*(x)=\max(A\cap[1,x]),
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
after interchanging \(A,B\) if necessary.
Source: I. Z. Ruzsa, \emph{Exact additive complements},
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.00812}, Section 2.
See \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/785}.
The external input is Narkiewicz's dichotomy, stated as
Theorem 1.1 of that paper:
\[
 A(2x)/A(x)\to1,\qquad B(2x)/B(x)\to2
                                                               \tag{2}
\]
in one fixed ordering of the two sets.
>>>
