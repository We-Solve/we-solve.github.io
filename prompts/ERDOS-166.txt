The requested lower bound is $R(4,k)\gg k^3/(\log k)^{O(1)}$.

\[
 R(4,k)\gg\frac{k^3}{(\log k)^4}.
\]
Here $R(4,k)$ is the least order forcing a $K_4$ or an independent
$k$-set. Mattheus and Verstraete proved this bound. We reconstruct the
sampling and alteration step, including its quantifier for every large
$k$, from their finite-geometric graph and independent-set estimate.
