\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.23}\label{2306023}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2306023 · Analysis}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$.For a nonconstant holomorphic function $f$, let $n_f(w)$ be the number of solutions of $f(z)=w$ in $\mathbb D$, counted with multiplicity and allowed to be infinite. It is areally mean $p$-valent, for $p>0$, if
\[\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^R n_f(\rho e^{it})\rho\,d\rho\,dt\le\pi pR^2\qquad(R>0).\]
Constants in coefficient asymptotics may depend on the particular function. Coefficients are not normalized unless stated.For $f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n$, define $\Delta_n=\big||a_{n+1}|-|a_n|\big|$. For symmetric series use $f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}b_nz^{An+B}$, with integers $A\ge1$, $B\ge0$, and measure differences of successive $b_n$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Find the sharp power exponents in bounds $\Delta_n=O(n^s)$ for mean $p$-valent functions, especially for $1/4<p<1$, where the source gives $s=2p-2\sqrt p$. Determine the corresponding sharp exponents for the symmetric series with parameters $A,B$. In particular, sharpen the bound $\big||b_{n+1}|-|b_n|\big|=O(n^{1-\sqrt2})$ for odd mean-univalent functions $f(z)=z+\sum_{n\ge1}b_nz^{2n+1}$. \sref{2306023}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find optimal decay or growth powers for successive coefficient-modulus differences of mean-valent functions, including symmetric series and the odd mean-univalent case.
