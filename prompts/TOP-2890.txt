\section*{Kirby Problem 4.14}\label{2890}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2890 · KP-4.14 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Use the source's broad cork convention. A cork $(C,f)$ consists of a
compact contractible smooth $4$-manifold $C$ and a boundary
diffeomorphism $f:\partial C\to\partial C$ which does not extend to
a diffeomorphism of $C$. No finite-order condition on $f$, and no
Stein structure on $C$, is imposed. Contractible means homotopy
equivalent to a point.

Given a smooth embedding $e:C\hookrightarrow W$ into the interior
of a closed smooth $4$-manifold, define the cork twist
\[
 W_{e,f}=(W\setminus\operatorname{int}(e(C)))\cup_{e|_{\partial C}\circ f}C.
\]
The boundary identification replaces the original attachment by the
specified diffeomorphism $f$; smooth collars are used for the gluing.

Does there exist one cork $(C,f)$ with the property that for every
pair $W,W'$ of closed simply connected smooth $4$-manifolds which
are homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic, some embedding $e:C\hookrightarrow W$
satisfies $W_{e,f}\cong W'$ by a diffeomorphism?
The cork and boundary map must be fixed before the pair is chosen.
Only their embedding in $W$ may vary with the pair.

Universality asks for a single twist to realize each requested change,
not a sequence of different corks or arbitrary powers of $f$.
The question concerns closed ambient manifolds; a version requiring
universality for manifolds with boundary is a different assertion.
No fixed homeomorphism between $W$ and $W'$ is required to be
realized by the surgery.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there one fixed contractible cork and boundary twist capable of relating every exotic pair of closed simply connected four-manifolds?
