\section{Quadratic bound in Linnik's least-prime problem}\label{9400132}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 9400132 · Number Theory}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For integers $d\ge2$ and $1\le a<d$ with $\gcd(a,d)=1$, define
\[p(a,d)=\min\{p:p\text{ is a positive prime and }p\equiv a\pmod d\}.\]
Dirichlet's theorem guarantees that this set is nonempty. The minimum is taken over the entire residue class; in particular $p=a$ is allowed when $a$ is prime.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every integer $d\ge2$ and every $a$ with $1\le a<d$ and $\gcd(a,d)=1$, is it true that
\[p(a,d)<d^2?\] \sref{9400132}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every reduced residue class modulo d contain a prime strictly below the square of d?
