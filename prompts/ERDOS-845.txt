## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Call $2^a3^b$, $a,b\ge0$, a 3-smooth integer. The question asks whether, for each fixed $C$, integers expressible as sums of distinct 3-smooth terms with largest term at most $C$ times the smallest have density zero. The answer is no: van Doorn and Everts prove that every positive integer has such a representation with ratio strictly less than six. We reconstruct their carrying procedure in this special case, using the multiset $\{2,2,3\}$ explicitly.
