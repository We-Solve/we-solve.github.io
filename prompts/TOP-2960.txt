\section*{Kirby Problem 4.84}\label{2960}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2960 · KP-4.84 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a closed oriented smooth $4$-manifold. Its smooth
mapping class group is
$\mathcal M(X)=\pi_0\operatorname{Diff}^+(X)$, where
$\operatorname{Diff}^+(X)$ has the $C^\infty$ topology and consists
of orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms. Let
$q:\operatorname{Diff}^+(X)\to\mathcal M(X)$ send a diffeomorphism
to its smooth-isotopy class.

A finite subgroup $G\le\mathcal M(X)$ is realized by a smooth
finite group action if there is a homomorphism
\[
 \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Diff}^+(X)
       \quad\text{such that}\quad q\circ\rho:G\hookrightarrow\mathcal M(X)
\]
is the given inclusion. This equality makes $\rho$ injective,
so the action is effective. Each group relation must hold exactly
for the chosen diffeomorphisms, not just up to isotopy.

Does there exist a closed oriented smooth $X$ for which every
finite subgroup of $\mathcal M(X)$ is realized in this sense?
The choice of action may vary with the finite subgroup; one
section of $q$ on the entire mapping class group is not required.

A finite-order mapping class need not a priori have a representative
of that same finite order. The problem asks for simultaneous
representatives of all elements of each finite subgroup respecting
multiplication. The quantifier on $X$ is existential, while the
quantifier on finite subgroups of its mapping class group is
universal. There is no simple-connectivity assumption.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Is there a closed oriented smooth four-manifold on which every finite subgroup of the smooth mapping class group is realized by an actual smooth group action?
