\section*{Kirby Problem 4.44}\label{2920}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2920 · KP-4.44 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $X$ be a closed oriented simply connected four-manifold and $x\in H_2(X;\mathbb Z)$. For integers $g,d\ge0$, ask whether $x$ has a representative $f:\Sigma_g\to X$ by a generic immersion of a connected closed oriented genus-$g$ surface with exactly $d$ double points. Generic means that the map is locally an embedding except for transverse intersections of two sheets, with no triple points or other singularities; double points are counted without signs. Require $f_*[\Sigma_g]=x$.

Treat smooth immersions in a smooth $X$ and locally flat topological immersions in a topological $X$ separately, using the corresponding transverse local models. The problem is to determine existence for the prescribed data, not to assert that every triple $(x,g,d)$ is realizable.

For $d=0$, define the genus function as the minimum genus of a connected embedded representative of $x$, using the same category. Compute the locally flat genus function for manifolds other than $S^4$, and compute smooth genus functions for further four-manifolds.

The specified test is whether $(3,2)\in H_2(\mathbb{CP}^2\#\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)$ is represented by a sphere. The coordinates refer to the standard positive generators in the two summands, so the square of the class is $13$. Ask the smooth and locally flat versions explicitly. An immersed sphere with double points would not settle this embedded-sphere question, and an answer in one category need not determine the other.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine which homology classes admit surfaces of prescribed genus and double-point count, including the sphere test in two positive projective planes.
