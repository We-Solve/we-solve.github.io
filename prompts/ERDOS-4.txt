\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#4; reconstruction of a known implication, 2026-09-23.}
Write $\log_j x$ for the $j$-fold iterated natural logarithm, with $x$ sufficiently large that all expressions below are positive. Is it true that for every $C>0$ there are infinitely many indices $n$ such that
\[
 p_{n+1}-p_n>C\frac{\log n\,\log_2n\,\log_4n}{(\log_3n)^2}?
\]
We reconstruct the affirmative answer from the stronger interval-covering theorem of Ford, Green, Konyagin, Maynard, and Tao. That deep theorem is stated explicitly as an external input; the passage to actual consecutive primes and then to the prime index is proved below.
