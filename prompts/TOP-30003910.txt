\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.}
Asymptotic flatness describes an isolated gravitating system whose far-field geometry approaches Minkowski space in the prescribed sense. Future null infinity is the asymptotic endpoint of outgoing null rays. Weak cosmic censorship asserts, in an appropriate generic initial-data class, that singularities formed by evolution are hidden from distant observers; completeness of future null infinity is a standard precise ingredient. The black-hole region is the complement of the causal past of future null infinity.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Does weak cosmic censorship hold for black-hole spacetimes in asymptotically flat settings, and under which geometric and dimensional hypotheses can it be proved or disproved?

\paragraph{Source scope and qualification.}
The question asks to specify the hypotheses, and does not assert censorship for every data set or every matter model. Genericity, asymptotic structure and the precise singularity/completeness formulation are not fixed in the catalogue extraction.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Establish or refute weak cosmic censorship in suitable asymptotically flat settings and identify its geometric and dimensional hypotheses.
