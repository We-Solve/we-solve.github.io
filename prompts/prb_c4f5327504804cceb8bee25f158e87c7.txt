Does there exist $c>0$ such that every $S\subseteq(\mathbb Z_3^n)^3$ with $|S|\geq3^{n(1-c)}$ and $x+y+z=0$ for every $(x,y,z)\in S$ contains three elements $(x_i,y_i,z_i)$ satisfying $x_1+y_2+z_3=0$? \sref{statement}{2}

Find a cross-summing triple among three-coloured zero-sum triples once their set exceeds the stated exponential threshold.
