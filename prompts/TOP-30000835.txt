\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


\paragraph{Definitions and conventions.} For oscillator coordinates $q_x$ and momenta $p_x$, the Hamiltonian dynamics has Liouville generator $A$. Define $D_x=p_{x+1}\partial_{p_x}-p_x\partial_{p_{x+1}}$; the source's asymmetric momentum-noise generator is $S=\sum_x(D_x^2+p_xp_{x+1}D_x)$. The combined dynamics adds this stochastic generator to the harmonic Hamiltonian generator. Thermal conductivity is the linear-response coefficient relating energy current to a small temperature gradient, or its source Green--Kubo thermodynamic-limit formulation.

\paragraph{Source scope and qualifications.} This noise does not conserve total momentum, as the report explicitly says. The catalogue literature assessment quotes results for a different energy-and-momentum-conserving noise. Those results do not answer this asymmetric-noise question; pinning, dimension, equilibrium, and limit conventions must remain those of the source model.

\paragraph{Catalogue mathematical target.}
\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
For harmonic oscillators, add to the Hamiltonian dynamics the asymmetric momentum noise $S=\sum_x[(p_{x+1}\partial_{p_x}-p_x\partial_{p_{x+1}})^2+p_xp_{x+1}(p_{x+1}\partial_{p_x}-p_x\partial_{p_{x+1}})]$. Is the resulting thermal conductivity finite?

\paragraph{Saved literature context.} The supplied catalogue's assessment dated 2026-08-21 records: The harmonic energy-momentum conserving model has an explicit answer: conductivity diverges in dimensions one and two and is finite in dimension at least three or with pinning; for general anharmonic interactions and disorder the question remains open. The assessment is retained as dated provenance; it is not a new comprehensive literature or open-status certification.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine whether the specified asymmetric momentum noise makes the harmonic chain's thermal conductivity finite.
