\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
A Cartan--Hadamard manifold is complete, simply connected, and has nonpositive sectional curvature. In dimension $n$, must every bounded sufficiently regular domain $\Omega$ in such a manifold satisfy the sharp Euclidean isoperimetric bound
\[|\partial\Omega|\geq n\omega_n^{1/n}|\Omega|^{(n-1)/n},\]
where $\omega_n$ is the volume of the Euclidean unit ball?
