\section*{Kirby Problem 1.41}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a smooth oriented knot $K\subset S^3=\partial B^4$, let $g_4(K)$ be the least
genus of a smooth connected oriented surface properly embedded in $B^4$ with
boundary $K$. Write $nK$ for the connected sum of $n$ copies. Subadditivity of
$g_4$ makes the following limit exist:
\[
g_s(K)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{g_4(nK)}n
=\inf_{n\geq1}\frac{g_4(nK)}n.
\]
The smooth concordance group identifies knots cobounding smooth annuli in
$S^3\times[0,1]$; its operation is connected sum. A class $[K]$ is torsion if
$mK$ bounds a smooth disk in $B^4$ for some $m\geq1$.

The four questions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can $g_s(K)$ be rational but not an integer?
\item Can $g_s(K)$ be irrational?
\item Can $0<g_s(K)<1/2$?
\item Is $g_s(K)=0$ equivalent to $[K]$ being torsion?
\end{enumerate}
All genera and concordances here are smooth. Torsion already forces stable
genus zero by subadditivity; the reverse implication in (d) asks whether
sublinear growth can occur for a class of infinite order.
\subsection{Short English statement}
What values can stable smooth four-genus take, and does it vanish exactly on torsion concordance classes?
