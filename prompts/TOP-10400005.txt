{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 10400005 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


A knot or link is a smooth oriented embedding of one or more circles in the oriented $3$-sphere, considered up to orientation-preserving ambient isotopy. Write $O$ for the unknot, which bounds an embedded disk. The Jones polynomial is normalized by $V_O(t)=1$ and
\[t^{-1}V_{L_+}(t)-tV_{L_-}(t)=(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})V_{L_0}(t),\]
where the three oriented links agree outside a ball and inside it have respectively a positive crossing, a negative crossing, and the oriented smoothing. For knots $V_K(t)\in\mathbb Z[t,t^{-1}]$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For a knot $K$, let $M_n(K)$ be the $n$-fold cyclic cover of $S^3$ branched over $K$, obtained from the cyclic cover of its exterior determined by a meridian mapping to $1\in\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ and then filling over the branch locus. Find a relationship between values of $V_K$ at roots of unity and the topology of the manifolds $M_n(K)$. Specify which values, cover invariants and relationships are valid, rather than assuming a formula for all $n$. \sref{10400005}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Relate root-of-unity values of the Jones polynomial to cyclic branched covers of the knot.
