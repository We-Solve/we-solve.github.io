\section*{Exact structure inside a two-fold sumset}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For subsets of $\mathbb F_2^n$, sums are taken coordinatewise modulo two, and density means cardinality divided by $2^n$.
Given finite nonempty sets $S_1,\ldots,S_\ell\subseteq\mathbb F_2^n$, write
\[
 S_1+\cdots+S_\ell
 =\{s_1+\cdots+s_\ell:s_i\in S_i\text{ for every }i\}.
\]
Repeated sums are counted only once.
The sets $S_i$ are otherwise arbitrary and are not required to be subspaces or to have the same cardinality.

The problem asks whether there exist absolute constants $c>0$, $C\geq1$, and $D\geq1$ such that for every $n\geq1$ and every $A\subseteq\mathbb F_2^n$ of density at least $0.01$, one can find $B$ and $S_1,\ldots,S_\ell$ with
\[
 |B|\geq c2^n,\qquad B+B\subseteq A+A,\qquad
 B=S_1+\cdots+S_\ell,
\]
where $1\leq\ell\leq Dn^C$ and $1\leq|S_i|\leq Dn^C$ for all $i$.
All constants must be uniform over the dimension and over $A$.
The containment is exact, not a high-density or approximate containment.
The source's fixed density is interpreted as a lower density threshold, since exact density $1/100$ is impossible in a vector space of cardinality $2^n$.
The desired conclusion supplies a dense iterated sum of sets of polynomial size inside the additive structure already forced by $A$.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the sumset of every fixed-density binary set contain the double sumset of a dense set built from polynomially many small summands?
