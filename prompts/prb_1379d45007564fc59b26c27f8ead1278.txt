\section{Show that every (1/2+ľ)|E(ő\_(n))| edges of the n-cube ő\_(n) contains a C\_(4) when n is sufficiently large}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


The hypercube graph $Q_n$ has vertices $\{0,1\}^n$ and edges between binary strings differing in exactly one coordinate; $|E(Q_n)|=n2^{n-1}$. A subgraph may select any subset of these edges. A $4$-cycle means a simple cycle of length four. Write $\operatorname{ex}(Q_n,C_4)=\max\{|E(G)|:G\subseteq Q_n\text{ contains no }4\text{-cycle}\}$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $\varepsilon>0$, does there exist $n_0(\varepsilon)$ such that for every $n\geq n_0(\varepsilon)$, each subgraph $G\subseteq Q_n$ with $|E(G)|\geq(\tfrac12+\varepsilon)|E(Q_n)|$ contains a $4$-cycle? Equivalently, is
\[\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{ex}(Q_n,C_4)}{|E(Q_n)|}=\frac12?\] \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every fixed edge density above one half force a four-cycle in a sufficiently large hypercube?
