\section*{Kirby Problem 4.50}\label{2926}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2926 · KP-4.50 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A closed topological $4$-manifold is a compact boundaryless Hausdorff
second-countable space locally homeomorphic to $\mathbb R^4$.
Consider connected such manifolds $X$ with finite fundamental group
$\pi_1(X)$. Both orientable and nonorientable manifolds are included,
and equivalence means homeomorphism of the underlying manifolds.

Classify these manifolds up to homeomorphism by algebraic invariants.
In particular, identify a collection of algebraic data associated to
$X$ and the precise equivalence on that data which detects when two
manifolds in the specified class are homeomorphic. Merely identifying
their homotopy types or their homology groups would not by itself
complete the requested classification.

The source emphasizes three families of possible fundamental groups:
dihedral groups, quaternionic groups, and finite abelian groups
requiring at most two or three generators. For clarity, the dihedral
family has presentation
$\langle r,s\mid r^m=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^{-1}\rangle$.
The quaternionic family includes the generalized quaternion groups,
such as $Q_8=\{\pm1,\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}$; its $2$-power-order
members have presentation
$\langle a,b\mid a^{2^{r-1}}=1,\ b^2=a^{2^{r-2}},\ bab^{-1}=a^{-1}\rangle$
for $r\ge3$.

An initial concrete target is the full classification when
$|\pi_1(X)|\le8$. These highlighted subfamilies do not restrict the
main question, which includes every finite fundamental group.
No smooth structure, triangulation, or simple-connectivity assumption
is imposed. The source regards reduction to complete algebraic
invariants as the intended form of a solution.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Classify closed topological four-manifolds with finite fundamental group, including the dihedral, quaternionic and small-generator abelian families.
