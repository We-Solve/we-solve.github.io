\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Fix $p\in\mathbb N$ and a slit length $\ell\in(0,1)$. For distinct angles $\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_p$ modulo $2\pi$, set
\[D_\theta=\mathbb D\setminus\bigcup_{j=1}^p\{te^{i\theta_j}:1-\ell\le t<1\}.\]
Let $u_\theta(z)$ be the harmonic measure at $z\in D_\theta$ of the outer circle $\partial\mathbb D$; the two sides of each slit are assigned boundary value zero. In particular $u_\theta(0)$ is the probability that planar Brownian motion starting at zero exits through the outer circle rather than a slit.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each fixed $p$ and $\ell$, determine the configurations of the $p$ radial slits that minimise $u_\theta(0)$. \sref{2307045}{2}
Update7.45 reports Dubinin's proof that equally spaced slits minimise the quantity. \sref{2307045}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Arrange a fixed number of equal radial slits to minimise the harmonic measure of the outer boundary seen from the centre.
