## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let $g(N)$ be the largest size of $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ such that $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\le N$ for every $a,b\in A$. Determine $g(N)$. Is it attained by
\[B_N=\{a\in\mathbb N:a\le\sqrt{N/2}\}
\ \cup\ \{a\in2\mathbb N:\sqrt{N/2}\le a\le\sqrt{2N}\}?\]
We give a valid construction, a universal upper bound of the correct order, and a counterexample to the literal assertion that this construction is always optimal. The sharp asymptotic and arbitrarily large counterexamples are not proved here.
>>>
