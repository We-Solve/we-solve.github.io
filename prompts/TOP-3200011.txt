\section*{\texorpdfstring{Combinatorial interpretation of Kronecker coefficients}{Combinatorial interpretation of Kronecker coefficients}}\label{3200011}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A partition $\lambda\vdash n$ is a nonincreasing sequence of positive integers summing to $n$, and $S^\lambda$ denotes the corresponding irreducible complex representation of the symmetric group $S_n$. The Kronecker coefficients are defined by
\[
 S^\lambda\otimes S^\mu\cong
 \bigoplus_{\nu\vdash n}(S^\nu)^{\oplus g_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}},
 \qquad
 g_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}=\dim\operatorname{Hom}_{S_n}
 (S^\nu,S^\lambda\otimes S^\mu).
\]
Find a uniform positive combinatorial rule, for example explicit finite combinatorial sets $\mathcal C_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}$ whose cardinalities give these coefficients without cancellation. The catalogue does not impose a runtime bound or a formal class of allowed combinatorial rules. Merely naming a basis of the above Hom space, or declaring a set of that unknown cardinality, is a tautology rather than the intended interpretation.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23286}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every Kronecker coefficient be computed by directly counting explicitly described combinatorial objects, without subtracting cancelling contributions?
\subsection{Sources}
\begin{itemize}
\item[S1]John M. Campbell, Kronecker coefficients via the Giambelli identity for Schur functions, arXiv:2604.23286, 2026.
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23286}.

\end{itemize}
