\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For a nonempty compact $K\subset\mathbb C^n$, let
\[\widehat K=\{z\in\mathbb C^n:|p(z)|\le\sup_{w\in K}|p(w)|\text{ for every complex polynomial }p\}\]
be its polynomial hull. Let $P(K)$ be the uniform closure in $C(K)$ of the restrictions of complex polynomials. Its Šilov boundary $\operatorname{Sh}(P(K))$ is the smallest closed subset of $K$ on which every member of $P(K)$ attains its maximum modulus. Write $\partial\widehat K$ for the Euclidean topological boundary of the hull in $\mathbb C^n$.

\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For each $n>1$, characterise all compact sets $K\subset\mathbb C^n$ for which
\[\operatorname{Sh}(P(K))=\partial\widehat K.\]
The empty set can be included with both boundaries empty. \sref{2307052}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterise compact sets in several complex variables whose polynomial algebra has Šilov boundary equal to the entire topological boundary of the polynomial hull.
