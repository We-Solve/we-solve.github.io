\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


For a finite system, susceptibility measures the integrated connected two-point correlation, equivalently the linear response to a uniform field. The hierarchical four-dimensional $|\phi|^4$ model organizes interactions by nested blocks. The Euclidean counterpart uses the ordinary periodic lattice. Finite-size scaling concerns the joint dependence on the system size and distance to criticality.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Extend the universal finite-size susceptibility-scaling asymptotics for the four-dimensional hierarchical $|\phi|^4$ model to the Euclidean model with periodic boundary conditions.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}The scaling window, normalization and asymptotic function are those of the hierarchical result in the cited report; changing those quantities would give a different comparison.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Prove the Euclidean periodic-model analogue of the hierarchical model's universal finite-size susceptibility asymptotics.
