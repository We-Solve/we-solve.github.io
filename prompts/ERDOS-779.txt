## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Let $n\ge 2$ and let $p_1<p_2<\cdots<p_n$ be the first $n$ primes. Define
\[
P:=\prod_{i=1}^n p_i.
\]
Question: does there always exist a prime $p$ such that
\[
p_n < p < P\quad\text{and}\quad P+p\text{ is prime}?
\]
