\section*{\texorpdfstring{Modularity of Elliptic Curves over Number Fields (End\_K(E)=Z)}{Modularity of Elliptic Curves over Number Fields (End\_K(E)=Z)}}\label{30006690}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For $E/K$ with $\operatorname{End}_K(E)={\mathbb{Z}}$, let $(r_v,N_v)$ be its finite-place Weil--Deligne representations. Use Thorne's geometric-Frobenius convention to form
\[
 \pi(E)=\bigotimes_{v\nmid\infty}'
 \operatorname{rec}_{K_v}^{-1}(r_v\otimes|\cdot|^{1/2},N_v),\qquad
 A=\varinjlim_U H^*(Y_U,{\mathbb{Q}}).
\]
Here $Y_U$ are the adelic locally symmetric spaces in the source, and the transition maps give the finite-adelic group action. The assertion is the existence of a $\operatorname{GL}_2({\mathbb{A}}_K^\infty)$-equivariant injection $\pi(E)\hookrightarrow A\otimes_{{\mathbb{Q}}}{\mathbb{C}}$. The condition concerns endomorphisms \emph{over $K$}; replacing it by $\operatorname{End}_{\bar K}(E)={\mathbb{Z}}$ would narrow the target.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/28276}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every elliptic curve in the specified number-field class occur in the automorphic cohomology predicted by Thorne's modularity formulation?
