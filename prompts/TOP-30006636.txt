\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A trisection decomposes a smooth four-manifold into three handlebody pieces with controlled pairwise and triple intersections. A four-dimensional topological quantum field theory assigns vector-space or categorical data to boundaries and compatible maps to bordisms, obeying gluing and disjoint-union axioms. A closed-manifold invariant alone does not provide those boundary assignments.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Extend the trisection invariants of smooth four-manifolds to a four-dimensional topological quantum field theory, or identify an obstruction to such an extension.

Extend the source trisection invariants to a gluing-compatible four-dimensional topological field theory, or find an obstruction.
