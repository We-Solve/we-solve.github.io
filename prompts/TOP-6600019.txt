\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $d\geq1$, let $\mathbf{Cl}(\mathbb R^d)$ be the space of all closed subsets of $\mathbb R^d$, including $\varnothing$ and $\mathbb R^d$. Its Chabauty--Fell topology is generated by the conditions that a closed set meet a specified open set and avoid a specified compact set. Let $\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb R)$ be the real determinant-one linear maps and $\mathrm{ASL}_d(\mathbb R)=\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb R)\ltimes\mathbb R^d$ their affine extensions. They act by $F\mapsto gF$. A Borel probability measure $\mu$ is invariant if $\mu(g^{-1}A)=\mu(A)$ for every Borel $A$ and every group element $g$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Classify all $\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb R)$-invariant Borel probability measures on $\mathbf{Cl}(\mathbb R^d)$, and likewise all $\mathrm{ASL}_d(\mathbb R)$-invariant Borel probability measures. \sref{6600019}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Describe every invariant random closed set for the linear and affine volume-preserving groups.
