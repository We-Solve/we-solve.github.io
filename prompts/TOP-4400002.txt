\section*{Pingree open problems — Hochman problem 2}\label{4400002}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 4400002 · AMR-043-0002 · Dynamical Systems · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D:[0,1)\to[0,1)$ be $D(x)=2x-\lfloor2x\rfloor$. Fix a Borel probability
$\mu$ with $D_*\mu=\mu$ which is ergodic: every measurable set invariant
modulo $\mu$ has measure zero or one. Assume
$0<h_\mu(D)<1$, with Kolmogorov--Sinai entropy measured using logarithms
to base two. Regard $\mu$ also as a measure on $\mathbb R$ supported on $[0,1)$.

For real $a,b$, let $F_{a,b}(x)=ax+b$ and define its pushforward by
$(F_{a,b*}\mu)(E)=\mu(F_{a,b}^{-1}E)$. In this question ``non-singular''
means that $\mu$ and $F_{a,b*}\mu$ are not mutually singular. Equivalently,
there is a nonzero finite positive measure dominated by both; it does not
mean that the two measures are equivalent on all of $\mathbb R$.
The target is to characterize, in terms of the given invariant measure,
\[
 \mathcal A(\mu)=\{(a,b)\in\mathbb R^2:
                   \mu\not\perp F_{a,b*}\mu\}.
\]
The description must address both the allowed slopes and the allowed
translations. Coordinates are transformed in $\mathbb R$, without reducing
$ax+b$ modulo one. The positive entropy hypothesis excludes atomic ergodic
measures, so a constant affine map cannot qualify. A classification is sought
uniformly over this class of measures, allowing the answer to depend on $\mu$;
the question does not prescribe a particular Bernoulli digit distribution.
\subsection{Short English statement}
For an intermediate-entropy doubling-invariant measure, determine which affine changes of coordinates retain a nonzero overlapping measure component.
