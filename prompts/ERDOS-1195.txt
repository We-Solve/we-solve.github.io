## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
How quickly can $M_S(x)=|S\cap(0,x)|$ grow if $S\subset(0,\infty)$ is measurable, has infinite measure, and no quotient of two distinct elements is an integer? We reconstruct the known sharp answer: for a finite-valued non-decreasing $F:[1,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ tending to infinity, such a set with $M_S(x)\ge F(x)$ for all sufficiently large $x$ exists exactly when
\[
 \int_1^\infty\frac{F(x)}{x^2}\,dx<\infty. \tag{1}
\]
The eventual qualifier matters: an arbitrary target may exceed $x$ on a bounded initial interval. The sharp criterion is due to Boon Suan Ho, whose paper discloses substantial GPT-5.4 Pro assistance. The logarithmic construction below follows the streamlined argument in Terence Tao's discussion of that work, with the estimates supplied explicitly.
>>>
