\section*{Upper bound on common independent set cover}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For a finite loopless matroid $M=(S,r)$ on a nonempty ground set,
$r(X)$ is the maximum size of an independent subset of $X$.
Independence is hereditary and satisfies the exchange axiom.
Looplessness ensures $r(X)>0$ for every nonempty $X$.
Define the density parameter
\[
                              \Delta(M)=
                     \max_{\varnothing\ne X\subseteq S}\frac{|X|}{r(X)}.
\]
The empty set is omitted to avoid the meaningless quotient $0/0$.

Let $M_1=(S,r_1)$ and $M_2=(S,r_2)$ be two loopless matroids
on the same ground set, and put
$q=\lceil\max\{\Delta(M_1),\Delta(M_2)\}\rceil+1$.
Must there be a partition $S=I_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup I_q$ with
\[
                             r_1(I_j)=r_2(I_j)=|I_j|
                                      \quad(1\le j\le q)?
\]
Empty classes are permitted, so this means a cover or partition
using at most $q$ common independent sets.

Each density supplies a necessary lower bound for the number
of independent classes for its own matroid. The conjecture asks
whether compatibility of the two requirements costs at most one
additional class above the larger rounded density. Separate
partitions for the two matroids do not provide the requested
common partition. The assertion places no representability or
special structural restriction on either matroid.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can two loopless matroids always be simultaneously coloured
using at most one more colour than their larger rounded density requires?
