Establish the Fourier restriction conjecture and Kakeya dimension conjecture in their full dimensional ranges. For restriction, determine the expected bounds for restricting Fourier transforms to curved hypersurfaces. For Kakeya, must a set in $\mathbb R^n$ containing a unit line segment in each direction have dimension $n$?
