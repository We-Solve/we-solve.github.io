\section*{Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups — Problem 52}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A finitely generated group $G$ is word-hyperbolic if its Cayley graph has uniformly thin geodesic triangles. For an integer $n\geq2$, a homeomorphism of the round sphere $S^n$ is quasiconformal if, in conformal coordinate charts, it belongs locally to $W^{1,n}$ and has bounded distortion
\[
 \lVert Df\rVert^n\leq K\,|\det Df|
\]
almost everywhere. The constant $K$ can be chosen independent of the charts.

Does every word-hyperbolic group admit a faithful action on some sphere $S^n$ by quasiconformal homeomorphisms with one common finite distortion bound for all group elements, and with discrete image in $\operatorname{Homeo}(S^n)$? More explicitly, seek an injective homomorphism
\[
 \rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Homeo}(S^n),\qquad
 \sup_{g\in G}K(\rho(g))<\infty,
\]
whose image has the identity isolated in the topology of uniform convergence of maps and their inverses.

The dimension and uniform distortion bound may depend on $G$. Uniformity is essential: it is not enough that each group element be quasiconformal with unrelated constants that grow without bound. Discreteness of the action also does not mean a properly discontinuous action on the entire compact sphere; limit sets can prevent that. The question asks for an action on a full sphere, not just the standard convergence action on the group's own possibly nonspherical Gromov boundary.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can every hyperbolic group act discretely on some sphere with a uniform quasiconformal distortion bound?
