\section{The values of the Hermite constants for dimensions other than 1–8 and 24}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $d\geq1$, a full-rank lattice is $L=B\mathbb Z^d\subset\mathbb R^d$ for an invertible real $d\times d$ matrix $B$. Let $\operatorname{covol}(L)=|\det B|$ and $\lambda_1(L)=\min\{\|v\|:v\in L\setminus\{0\}\}$, using Euclidean length. The Hermite constant is
\[\gamma_d=\sup_L\frac{\lambda_1(L)^2}{\operatorname{covol}(L)^{2/d}}.\]
The historically listed question concerns dimensions other than $1,\ldots,8$ and $24$. The cited 2026 version reports $\gamma_9=2$, addressing the nine-dimensional instance of that historical request.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Determine the exact values of $\gamma_d$ in the dimensions $d\notin\{1,2,\ldots,8,24\}$, with the source-supported value $\gamma_9=2$ already available. \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Determine the exact Hermite constants outside dimensions one through eight and twenty-four. The cited version supplies the value two in dimension nine.
