{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2200013 · Algebra}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Let $n\geq1$ and $f(z)=\sum_{j=0}^n a_jz^j$ with $a_j>0$ for every $j$. Define the weighted tropical polynomial on $\mathbb R$ by
\[T_f(t)=\max_{0\leq j\leq n}\left(\log a_j+jt+\log\binom nj\right).\]
Its corners are the distinct points where this continuous piecewise-linear function is not differentiable. Let $v_T(f)$ be the number of corners and $N_{\mathbb R}(f)$ the number of real roots, counted with multiplicity.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Does every such positive-coefficient polynomial satisfy
\[N_{\mathbb R}(f)\leq v_T(f)?\] \sref{2200013}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the number of real roots never exceed the number of corners of the binomially weighted tropical coefficient graph?
