\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Let M carry a faithful normal state $\varphi $ with $M_\varphi =C1$, put $\xi =\varphi ^{1/2}$ and $D=\log $ $\Delta _\varphi $ on $L^{2}(M)$. For fixed $T\in B(L^{2}(M))$ and $s\in R$, if unit vectors in intervals of D shrinking to s have a positive limsup for the fixed symmetric translation-invariant orbit mean of ||T $\exp (itD)h||^{2}$, can a subsequence be replaced by $v_j\xi $ for uniformly operator-norm-bounded $v_j\in M$, with $||Tv_j\xi ||$ bounded below and spectral widths at most four times the original widths?
