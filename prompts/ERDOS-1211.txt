## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For a partition $\mathbb N=A\sqcup B$, let $S(A)$ and $S(B)$ be the sets of nonempty sums of distinct elements of the corresponding part. How small can
\[
 \max\bigl(\overline\delta_{\log}(S(A)),
           \overline\delta_{\log}(S(B))\bigr)
\]
be? Conlon--Fox--Pham proved that the optimum is $(2+\sqrt3)/4$.
We reconstruct the sharp upper-bound construction, including its logarithmic-density calculation, and prove the elementary universal lower bound $1/2$. The stronger universal lower bound matching the construction remains unresolved in this attempt.
>>>
