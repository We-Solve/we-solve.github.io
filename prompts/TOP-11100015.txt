\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Fix a prime $p$ and a positive chromatic height $n\ge1$. In the category of dualizable $K(n)$-local spectra at $p$, classify thick tensor ideals. A thick tensor ideal is closed under cofibers, retracts, and tensoring with any dualizable object. Does their inclusion order give the expected chromatic filtration analogous to the thick-subcategory theorem for finite spectra?
