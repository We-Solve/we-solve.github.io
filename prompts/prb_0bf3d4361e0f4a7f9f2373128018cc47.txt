\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
For an elliptic curve over ${\mathbb{Q}}$, write $E({\mathbb{Q}})\simeq{\mathbb{Z}}^{r(E)}\oplus T$ with $T$ finite. The target is
\[
 \forall r\in{\mathbb{Z}}_{\ge0}\ \exists E/{\mathbb{Q}}:\qquad r(E)\ge r,
\]
or $\sup_{E/{\mathbb{Q}}}r(E)=\infty$. The rank is that of the actual rational-point group. Analytic ranks, Selmer upper bounds, and ranks after enlarging the base field are not interchangeable with this integer. No algorithm producing the curves, or prescribed rate of growth in their heights, is required.
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation and concept references:} \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.01329v2}{[S1]}.

\subsection{Short English statement}
Can elliptic curves over the rationals have arbitrarily many independent rational points of infinite order?
\subsection{Sources}
\begin{itemize}
\item[S1]Harris B. Daniels and Hannah Goodwillie, On the ranks of elliptic curves with isogenies, arXiv1611.01329v2,11December2024,10pages.
\url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.01329v2}.
