\section{Yau's Conjecture on First Eigenvalues}\label{1212}
{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 1212 · Geometry}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


For $n\geq1$, let $S^{n+1}(1)=\{x\in\mathbb R^{n+2}:\lVert x\rVert=1\}$ with its standard round metric. Let $M^n\subset S^{n+1}(1)$ be a connected smooth closed embedded hypersurface, with the induced metric, whose mean-curvature vector is identically zero. Let $\Delta_M=\operatorname{div}_M\nabla_M$; $\lambda_1(M)$ denotes the least positive eigenvalue of the nonnegative operator $-\Delta_M$, equivalently
\[
\lambda_1(M)=\inf_{\substack{f\in C^\infty(M,\mathbb R),\ f\not\equiv0\\\int_M f\,dV=0}}
\frac{\int_M\lVert\nabla_Mf\rVert^2\,dV}{\int_M f^2\,dV}.
\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Is $\lambda_1(M)=n$ for every connected closed embedded minimal hypersurface $M^n\subset S^{n+1}(1)$? \sref{1212}{2}

\subsection{Short English statement}
Does every closed embedded minimal hypersurface of the unit sphere have first nonzero Laplace eigenvalue equal to its dimension?
