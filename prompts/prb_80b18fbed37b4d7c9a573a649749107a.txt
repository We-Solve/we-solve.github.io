\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
A profinite group is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite groups endowed with the discrete topology. For a field \(K\), choose a separable closure \(K^{\mathrm{sep}}\). Its absolute Galois group is
\[
G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K),
\]
endowed with the Krull topology: the subgroups \(\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/L)\), where \(L/K\) ranges over finite Galois extensions contained in \(K^{\mathrm{sep}}\), form a neighborhood basis of the identity. The topological isomorphism class of \(G_K\) is independent of the choice of separable closure.

\textbf{Characterization problem.} Give necessary and sufficient conditions on a profinite group \(G\) for
\[
\exists\,\text{a field }K\quad G\cong_{\mathrm{top}}G_K,
\]
where \(\cong_{\mathrm{top}}\) denotes an isomorphism of topological groups. Equivalently, characterize the class
\[
\left\{G\text{ profinite}:\exists\,\text{a field }K\text{ with }G\cong_{\mathrm{top}}\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K)\right\}.
\]
The field ranges over all fields; the supplied statement imposes no restriction on characteristic, cardinality, or arithmetic type. The target is the full absolute Galois group, with its profinite topology.

The supplied record labels this problem open. Its dated assessment says that later work gives obstructions and characterizes important quotients, while the broad characterization remains open. These are supplied curation claims, not independently verified conclusions. The source citation's year and its precise problem location remain unresolved as described in Sources.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Characterize exactly which profinite groups are topologically isomorphic to the absolute Galois group of some field. All fields are allowed. The supplied open-status assessment requires independent verification.
