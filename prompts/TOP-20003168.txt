\section*{Explicit stable homogeneous representatives over S\textasciicircum{}2 x S\textasciicircum{}2}\label{20003168}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 20003168 · AIM-TOPOLOGY-0256 · Topology · AIM Workshop Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a closed connected simply connected smooth manifold that admits a Riemannian metric of nonnegative sectional curvature.
Let $\xi\to M$ be an arbitrary smooth real vector bundle of finite rank $r$.
For an integer $k\geq0$, its stabilization is the fiberwise direct sum
\[
 \xi\oplus\underline{\mathbb R}^{k},
 \qquad \underline{\mathbb R}^{k}=M\times\mathbb R^k.
\]
The total space of this bundle is naturally diffeomorphic to $E(\xi)\times\mathbb R^k$.

The stable converse soul question asks whether for every pair $(M,\xi)$ as above there exists some $k\geq0$ for which this total space admits a complete Riemannian metric with sectional curvature at least zero on every tangent two-plane.
The integer $k$ may depend on the bundle and base; no uniform rank bound is required.
The sought metric need not be a product metric, need not restrict to the originally given metric on the zero section, and need not make the bundle projection a Riemannian submersion.
Completeness on the noncompact total space is part of the conclusion.
Once a single stabilization works, all larger stabilizations work by taking products with Euclidean space, so existence of one $k$ is equivalent to existence for all sufficiently large $k$.
The underlying converse question concerns which vector bundles can arise geometrically as normal bundles of souls; this version permits trivial additional directions before seeking the curvature realization.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does adding sufficiently many trivial directions make every real vector bundle over a simply connected nonnegatively curved closed manifold admit a complete nonnegative-curvature metric?
