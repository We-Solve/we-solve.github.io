For a set $A\subseteq\mathbb N$, let $P(A)$ be the sums of finite subsets. The source asks whether $A(N)\gg\sqrt N$ forces $P(A)$ to contain an infinite arithmetic progression. The primary theorem makes the coefficient quantifier precise: there exists a sufficiently large absolute $C$ for which $A(N)\ge C\sqrt N$ is sufficient. It does not assert this for every positive coefficient. Below we prove bounded gaps when $C>\sqrt2$ and construct an obstruction at a small positive coefficient. The general infinite-progression conclusion at the published large threshold is not reconstructed.
