## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Every graph of maximum degree \(d\geq1\) has a proper colouring
with at most \(\lceil100d^{4/3}\rceil\) colours in which no cycle
uses only two colours. In particular \(f(d)=o(d^2)\).
We reconstruct the four-event argument of Alon, McDiarmid,
and Reed, \emph{Acyclic coloring of graphs}, Section 2:
\url{https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/Publications2/Acyclic%20colouring%20of%20graphs.pdf}.
See \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/797}.
The probabilistic external input is the asymmetric
Lov\'asz local lemma.
>>>
