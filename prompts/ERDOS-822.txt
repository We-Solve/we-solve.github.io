## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
Does $\{n+\varphi(n):n\ge1\}$ have positive lower density? Gabdullin, Iudelevich and Luca proved that it does. This attempt proves a lower bound of order $X/\log X$, constructs a positive-density family of structured inputs, and derives its exact collision equation with two degenerate cases excluded. The remaining averaged collision bound is not proved here. Thus this is a partial reconstruction of an already settled problem, not a claim that the problem itself lacks a solution.
We use the prime number theorem, including its standard uniform consequence for prime counts on intervals $[T/2,T]$ when $T$ tends to infinity, and Mertens' fixed-power-interval estimate $\sum_{X^a<p\le X^b}1/p\to\log(b/a)$ for fixed $0<a<b$.
