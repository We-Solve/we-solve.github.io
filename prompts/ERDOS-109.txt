\textbf{Erd\H{o}s \#109; partial reconstruction of a known theorem, 2026-09-23.}
If $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ has positive upper asymptotic density, prove that there are infinite $B,C\subseteq\mathbb N$ with $B+C\subseteq A$. We use $\overline d(A)=\limsup_{N\to\infty}|A\cap[1,N]|/N$. The problem is known to be solved; the independent argument here establishes finite-by-infinite sumsets of arbitrarily large finite width, the full density-one case, and a precise sufficient condition for the infinite-by-infinite step.
