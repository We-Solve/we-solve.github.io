\section{Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 6.111}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


Write $\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathbb T=\partial\mathbb D$. Let $S$ be the class of holomorphic injective functions $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ with $f(0)=0$ and $f'(0)=1$. Let $\mathcal A=\{f\in\operatorname{Hol}(\mathbb D):f(0)=0,f'(0)=1\}$. If $f(z)=z+\sum_{k\geq2}a_kz^k$, $g(z)=z+\sum_{k\geq2}b_kz^k$, define
\[N_\delta(f)=\{g\in\mathcal A:\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}k|a_k-b_k|\leq\delta\}.\]
More generally a weight sequence $T_k\geq0$ gives $TN_\delta(f)$ by replacing $k$ with $T_k$. Let $St\subset S$ be the maps whose images are starlike about zero, meaning that every segment from zero to an image point stays in the image. For holomorphic $u,v$, $u\prec v$ means $u=v\circ\eta$ for a holomorphic $\eta:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D$ with $\eta(0)=0$. For real $-1\leq B<A\leq1$, define
\[K[A,B]=\left\{f\in S:1+\frac{zf''(z)}{f'(z)}\prec\frac{1+Az}{1+Bz}\right\},\qquad
\delta(A,B)=\begin{cases}(1-B)^{(A-B)/B},&B\ne0,\\e^{-A},&B=0.\end{cases}\]
Define the largest uniform allowable radius by
\[\delta_*(A,B)=\sup\{d\geq0:N_d(f)\subseteq St\text{ for every }f\in K[A,B]\}.\]
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
For every $-1\leq B<A\leq1$, do both $N_{\delta(A,B)}(f)\subseteq St$ for every $f\in K[A,B]$ and $\delta_*(A,B)=\delta(A,B)$ hold, without restricting to either
\[-\tfrac14(\sqrt3+2)\leq B<A\leq1\quad\text{or}\quad -1\leq-A\leq B<A\leq1?\] \sref{statement}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Does the known sharp neighborhood radius remain valid for every admissible Janowski parameter pair?
