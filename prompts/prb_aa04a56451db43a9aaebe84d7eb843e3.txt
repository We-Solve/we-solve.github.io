\section{Componentwise entropy rigidity and a reduced search for a non-multilinear matroid}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}Standard number systems are denoted by $\mathbb N,\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$; index ranges, computational models and specialized notation follow the cited problem source and the definitions below.


A matroid is a finite ground set with an integer-valued rank function satisfying monotonicity, submodularity, and $0\le r(A)\le |A|$. Its CI structure consists of disjoint triples satisfying $r(A\cup C)+r(B\cup C)=r(A\cup B\cup C)+r(C)$. This structure is probabilistically representable when a random vector indexed by the ground set has exactly these conditional-independence relations $X_A\perp X_B\mid X_C$. A multilinear representation realizes a fixed positive integer multiple of this rank function by dimensions of sums of equal-dimensional linear subspaces.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Conjecture 22. There exists a connected matroid whose CI-structure is p-representable but not multilinear. \sref{statement}{2}
\paragraph{Scope qualification.}
The supplied extraction includes adjacent workshop exposition; the numbered target passage is retained verbatim and that surrounding exposition is omitted. Connected means that the matroid is not a nontrivial direct sum. The question concerns the CI structure, not a prescribed entropy value for every subset.
