\section*{Kirby Problem 2.9}\label{2757}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 2757 · KP-2.9 · Topology · Kirby's Problems in Low-Dimensional Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $D$ be an oriented disk with $n\geq2$ distinguished interior points $P$.
The braid group $B_n$ is the mapping class group of $D$ preserving $P$ setwise
and fixing $\partial D$ pointwise. For each isotopy class of embedded arc $a$
joining two points of $P$, with interior disjoint from $P$, let $h_a$ be the
positive half-twist supported near $a$. It interchanges the endpoints by a
positive half-turn. These elements are precisely the conjugates of a standard
positive Artin generator.

Seek a useful presentation of $B_n$ whose generators are \emph{all} the $h_a$,
with relations expressed by simple, explicit rules for configurations of arcs.
In particular, the source asks for homogeneous positive relations: each should
be an equality
\[
 h_{a_1}\cdots h_{a_r}=h_{b_1}\cdots h_{b_r}
\]
without inverse letters and with the same number of factors on the two sides.
The rules must present the whole braid group, so both validity and completeness
of the relations are required. Ideally a finite collection of local geometric
rules describes this infinite generating set. Merely giving the usual Artin
presentation on adjacent half-twists does not specify relations among all these
generators. Likewise, restricting to a particular finite family of band generators
changes the requested presentation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Find a transparent presentation of the braid group using every positive elementary half-twist, preferably with a small set of homogeneous geometric relation rules.
