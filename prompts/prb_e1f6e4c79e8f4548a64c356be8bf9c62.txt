\section*{Few subsequence sums in $\mathbb Z_n\times\mathbb Z_n$}\label{statement}

\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $n\ge2$ and write $G=(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^2$ additively.
A sequence $S=(s_1,\ldots,s_\ell)$ in $G$ may have repeated terms.
Its set of nonempty subsequence sums is
\[
 \Sigma(S)=\left\{\sum_{i\in I}s_i:
                  \varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,\ell\}\right\}.
\]
It is zero-sum-free if $0\notin\Sigma(S)$. Distinctness here concerns
the resulting group elements, not the index subsets producing them.

For $0\le t\le n-1$, the proposed extremal bound is
\[
 |S|=n-1+t,\quad 0\notin\Sigma(S)
           \quad\Longrightarrow\quad |\Sigma(S)|\ge n(t+1)-1.
\]
This is precisely the source's comparison with the sequence having
$n-1$ copies of $e_1=(1,0)$ and $t$ copies of $e_2=(0,1)$.
For that sequence, the sums are all pairs $(a,b)$ with
$0\le a\le n-1$, $0\le b\le t$, except $(0,0)$; hence it is
zero-sum-free and has exactly the displayed number of sums.
The empty subsequence is excluded consistently on both sides.
Prove this bound for every $n,t$ and every sequence, or produce a
zero-sum-free sequence with fewer sums. Classification of every
equality case would be additional information, not part of the stated
minimum-value claim.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Among zero-sum-free sequences of the specified length in a two-dimensional modular group, do repeated coordinate vectors minimize the number of distinct nonempty sums?
