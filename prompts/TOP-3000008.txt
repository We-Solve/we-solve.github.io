\section*{Bounded degree matroid basis}\label{3000008}
{\small UnsolvedMath ID 3000008 · AMR-029-0008 · Combinatorics · AMR Open Problem Lists}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $M$ be a matroid on a finite set $V$, given by an independence oracle:
on a queried subset it reports whether that subset is independent.
Independent sets are closed under taking subsets and satisfy the exchange
axiom; their maximal members are bases. Let $\mathcal H$ be an explicitly
listed family of subsets of $V$, and set
\[
       \Delta=\max_{v\in V}|\{H\in\mathcal H:v\in H\}|\ge1.
\]
Assign rational costs $c_v$ and integer bounds $\ell_H\le u_H$.
Assume at least one basis satisfies every bound, and let $\mathrm{OPT}$
be the least cost of such a basis, with $c(B)=\sum_{v\in B}c_v$.

Is there an algorithm using polynomially many oracle calls and polynomial
time in the input encoding that returns a basis $B$ satisfying
\[
 c(B)\le\mathrm{OPT},\qquad
 \ell_H-\Delta+1\le |B\cap H|\le u_H+\Delta-1
                       \quad(H\in\mathcal H)?
\]
Both lower and upper bounds must be handled simultaneously. The output
may violate the original degree constraints, but only by the displayed
additive amount; its cost is compared with the optimum under the original
constraints. The number $\Delta$ counts constraints involving one ground
element, not the largest size of a constraint set. The empty-family case
reduces to ordinary minimum-cost basis computation and needs no relaxation.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can one find a basis costing no more than the constrained optimum
while violating every lower and upper degree bound by at most $\Delta-1$?
