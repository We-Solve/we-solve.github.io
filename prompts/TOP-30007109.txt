\section*{Triangular-cylinder approximation of aligned radial sweeps}\label{30007109}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}
Let $P\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a two-dimensional vector plane with unit normal $e$, and take linearly independent $a,b\in P$. Let $J=[\alpha,\beta]$ with $\alpha<\beta$, let $R:J\to[0,\infty)$ be continuous, let $w:J\to P$ be continuously differentiable, and fix $p_0\in P$ and $L>0$. Suppose $w'(t)=\lambda(t)(a+tb)$ for a continuous real function $\lambda$. Define
\[
 S=\{p_0+\rho(a+tb)+s(e+w(t)):t\in J,\ 0\le\rho\le R(t),\ |s|\le L\}.
\]
For every finite tagged partition of $J$, can one cover the part of $S$ over each partition interval by a compact cylinder having a nondegenerate triangular base and axis $e+w(\text{tag})$, with the sum of the base areas perpendicular to their respective axes converging to
\[
 |\det_P(a,b)|\int_\alpha^\beta
 \frac{R(t)^2}{2\sqrt{1+\lVert w(t)\rVert^2}}\,dt
\]
uniformly over all partitions and tags as the mesh tends to zero?

\subsection{Short English statement}
Do aligned radial sweeps admit these finite triangular-cylinder covers, with the prescribed uniform limit of total perpendicular base area?
