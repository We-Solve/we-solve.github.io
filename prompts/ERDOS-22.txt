## Problem statement (quoted material)
<<<
For every $\epsilon>0$ and all sufficiently large $N$, construct a $K_4$-free graph with at least $N^2/8$ edges and independence number at most $\epsilon N$. The problem page records the affirmative theorem of Fox, Loh, and Zhao. Their paper addresses the exact critical density; a construction with only $(1/8-o(1))N^2$ edges does not automatically reach the requested threshold. We examine a natural elementary construction and prove that it cannot solve the question when $\epsilon<1/12$.
