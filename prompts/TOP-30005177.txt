\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$; the standard number fields have their usual meanings. All additional conventions are stated locally.


A renormalization-group fixed point is a theory unchanged, up to its prescribed rescaling, by coarse-graining. Non-Gaussian means that its correlations are not those of a free Gaussian theory. Long-range fermionic models here use the source's nonlocal covariance and symplectic internal symmetry. Conformal invariance requires the appropriate covariance of correlations under conformal transformations.

\paragraph{Catalogue wording retained for traceability.}
Prove conformal invariance of the non-Gaussian renormalization-group fixed point for long-range fermionic models with symplectic symmetry.

\paragraph{Scope and source qualification.}Scale invariance at a fixed point does not by itself establish full conformal invariance; the source model and correlation-function conventions must be retained.
