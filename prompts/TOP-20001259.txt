\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N$, $\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb C$ denote the natural numbers, integers, rationals, reals, and complex numbers, respectively. All additional parameters and hypotheses are specified in the question.


\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Can one compute the zeta function of $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)$,
\[\zeta_n(s)=\prod_{p\mid n}(1-p^{-s})^{-1},\]
without first factoring $n$? For a polynomial $f\in\mathbb F_q[x]$, consider the analogous product over its monic irreducible factors. From this zeta data, can one recover the factorization of $f$ in deterministic polynomial time?
\par\smallskip\textit{Formulation references:} \sref{20001259}{1}, \sref{20001259}{2}.
\subsection{Short English statement}
Can one compute the zeta function of $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)$,
\[\zeta_n(s)=\prod_{p\mid n}(1-p^{-s})^{-1},\]
without first factoring $n$? For a polynomial $f\in\mathbb F_q[x]$, consider the analogous product over its monic irreducible factors. From this zeta data, can one recover the factorization of $f$ in deterministic polynomial time?
