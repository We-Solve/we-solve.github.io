{\small\sffamily UnsolvedMath ID 2997 · Topology}
\subsection{Definitions and mathematical statement}

\paragraph{Shared notation.}$\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Q,\mathbb R,\mathbb C$ denote the integers, rationals, reals and complex numbers. Any other conventions are specified locally.


An open book on a closed smooth four-manifold $X$ consists of an embedded closed surface $L\subset X$ with trivial normal bundle and a smooth locally trivial fibration $f:X\setminus L\to S^1$. In a tubular neighborhood $L\times D^2$, it has the polar-coordinate form $f(x,re^{i\theta})=e^{i\theta}$ for $r>0$. The closures of fibers are the pages and $L$ is the binding. For an oriented $X$, $\sigma(X)$ is the signature of the intersection form on $H_2(X;\mathbb R)$.
\paragraph{Statement recorded in the supplied source.}
Characterize the closed smooth four-manifolds admitting an open book. In particular, if $X$ is closed, simply connected and smooth with $\sigma(X)=0$, must $X$ admit an open book? \sref{2997}{2}
\subsection{Short English statement}
Which closed smooth four-manifolds have open books, and is zero signature sufficient when the manifold is simply connected?
