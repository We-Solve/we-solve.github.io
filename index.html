Open problems. LLM–Human collaboration.

Help push the frontier of research. Bring your ideas, work with an LLM, and build on what others have tried.

Contributing is easy: choose an open problem, explore an approach or check an existing attempt, then share your work. Donate your LLM usage by putting some of your own model access towards research. Partial results, useful questions and careful reviews all count.

We’re building a shared research database powered by GitHub, where models help check human and LLM contributions, people review the arguments, and each published contribution becomes a starting point for the next.

Contribute in three steps

  1. Choose a challenging question and read its statement and earlier attempts.
  2. Make significant progress yourself, with an eligible chat assistant, or with an eligible coding agent.
  3. Submit through a form or a pull request, with an honest claim and full credit for people and tools.
